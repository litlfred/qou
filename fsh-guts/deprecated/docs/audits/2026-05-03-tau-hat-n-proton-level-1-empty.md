# Mass tower at proton scale is structurally level-0-only

**Date:** 2026-05-03
**Branch:** `claude/tau-hat-n-proton-probe`
**Goal:** apply the proton-scale τ̂_0 realisation (PR #533) to compute
the level-1 mass contribution per the pentagon-uv-commutation formula
— the original target of Phase A.3 (issue #522, PR #531).

## Verdict: **at A=1, the tower is empty beyond level 0.**

The proton residual (~500 ppm vs CODATA, per `9b66f7571` audit) is
**not** a tower-truncation effect. Phase A.3's "extend `N`" plan
applies to A ≥ 3 atoms only.

## Reasoning

The mass-tower jet form
([`pentagon-uv-commutation.md` lines 86–106](../../content/quantum-observable-universe/braids-and-knots/pentagon-uv-commutation.md))

$$
\mathfrak{m} = |\mathrm{tr}_M(W)| + \sum_{n=0}^{N}\Bigl(\sum_{\xi \in \mathcal{S}^{(n)}_1}\Phi_q(|\mathrm{tr}_M(\xi)|) + z^{2}\sum_{(\xi,\xi') \in \mathcal{S}^{(n)}_2}\Phi_q(|\mathrm{tr}_M(\xi\xi')|)\Bigr) + R_N
$$

has the **inter-level ascension rule** (line 56–60): "every triple
at level $n$ absorbs into a pair plus singleton at level $n$ plus a
residual that lives at level $n+1$." Level-$(n+1)$ sub-braids are
produced solely by triples at level $n$ getting absorbed.

At proton scale:

- **A = 1** ⇒ exactly 1 nucleon ⇒ 0 pairs ⇒ 0 triples ⇒
  $\mathcal{S}^{(n)}_2 = \emptyset$ for all $n$.
- $\mathcal{S}^{(0)}_1 = \{W\}$ (the single atom braid).
- For $n \geq 1$, $\mathcal{S}^{(n)}_1$ is generated by triple
  absorption from $\mathcal{S}^{(n-1)}_2 = \emptyset$ ⇒
  $\mathcal{S}^{(n)}_1 = \emptyset$ for $n \geq 1$.

Therefore the entire $\sum_{n=0}^{N}$ contribution at A=1 reduces to
$\Phi_q(|\mathrm{tr}_M(W)|)$ alone, plus a tail $R_N$ that is
provably zero (the inter-level ascension input is empty). The mass
formula at A=1 is exactly
$\mathfrak{m}_{\rm proton} = |\mathrm{tr}_M(W_{\rm proton})| + \Phi_q(|\mathrm{tr}_M(W_{\rm proton})|)$,
with no tower contribution.

## Cross-check via τ̂_0 (PR #533)

For consistency, lift any level-0 element $W \in A_0$ to $A_1$ via
$\ell_0$, then project to the transient block via $(1 - \hat\tau_0)$:

$$(1 - \hat\tau_0)(\ell_0(W)) = \ell_0(W) - \ell_0(\mathfrak{d}_1(\ell_0(W))) = \ell_0(W) - \ell_0(W) = 0,$$

using the splitting $\mathfrak{d}_1 \circ \ell_0 = \mathrm{id}_{A_0}$
verified numerically in PR #533.

So **any lifted level-0 element has zero transient component**.
Non-zero level-1 contributions can only come from genuinely new
sub-braids that don't descend from level 0 — the "transient
creation modes" per
[`descent-eigenspaces.md` lines 19–21](../../content/quantum-observable-universe/lifting-and-descent/descent-eigenspaces.md).
At A=1 there are no such modes (no triples at level 0 to source
them).

## Where Phase A.3 actually applies

The mass-tower truncation extension matters at **A ≥ 3**:

| A | atoms | triples in $\mathcal{S}^{(0)}_2$ | level-1 reachable? |
|--:|---|---:|---|
| 1 | proton, neutron | 0 | **NO** |
| 2 | D | 0 | **NO** |
| 3 | T, ³He | 1 | yes |
| 4 | ⁴He | 4 | yes |
| 6 | ⁶Li | 20 | yes |

So `tritium` (3200 ppm residual) and `³He` (3100 ppm residual) are
the smallest atoms where level-1 contributions can be non-zero —
exactly the atoms where the per-atom precision is worst. This is
suggestive (though not conclusive) that the level-1 contribution
at A=3 may explain part of the 3000 ppm gap.

## What's still needed for A ≥ 3

The probe at A=1 confirms the structural prediction. Computing the
**actual level-1 numerical contribution at A ≥ 3** requires:

1. **Generalise τ̂_0** from A=1 (proton, $H_3(q_0)$) to A=3 (tritium,
   $H_9(q_0)$) — extends `tau_hat_n_proton_realisation.py` from
   `DIM_A0 = 6` (3 strands) to the appropriate H_9 dimension. The
   construction is the same, but the linear algebra is larger
   (dim H_9 = 9! = 362 880, so dim End(A_1) blows up; would need
   to restrict to a small principal block).
2. **Identify the transient creation modes** at A ≥ 3 — which
   level-1 sub-braids are produced by triple absorption. This
   requires the explicit form of the pentagon q-factor identity
   on the H_9 transient block.
3. **Choose a connection** — universal vs q₀-aware Markov-derived;
   the audit (PR #531) flagged this as research-grade open.

All three are out of scope for this memo; they're the substantive
research-grade work.
