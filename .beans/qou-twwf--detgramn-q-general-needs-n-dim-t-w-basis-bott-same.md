### Why the concrete type-A route

Abstractly the braid case needs the strong exchange property, parabolic
subgroups and minimal coset representatives; Mathlib's `GroupTheory/Coxeter/`
is four files and has none of them, and lists Matsumoto's theorem as an open
TODO (`Coxeter/Basic.lean:78`). The Bourbaki free-module route avoids
Matsumoto entirely, and `permCoxeterSystem` (this same PR) put `cs.length`
within reach of `Equiv.Perm` so the branch could be made concrete.

## 2026-08-30 (later) — spanning + independence both land; the wall is Matsumoto

Added on the same branch, all sorry-free, `lean`-direct exit 0, axioms clean:

* `TwRep.lean` — `toEnd : H_n(q) →ₐ[K] Module.End K (S_{n+1} →₀ K)`, and
  `orbitMap_surjective`: the module is **cyclic**, generated by `T_1`.
* `TwSpanning.lean` — `single_mem_of_closed`, `eq_top_of_singles`. No type-A
  specialisation needed; Mathlib's right descents are exactly the induction.
* `TwIndependent.lean` — `linearIndependent_Tw`: **the `T_w` are linearly
  independent**, so `dim_K H_n(q) ≥ (n+1)!`. Plus `basisTw`, conditional.

### The move that got independence without Matsumoto

`T_w` is built from *some* reduced word (`Classical.choose`). That is a priori
not well-defined — ruling it out **is** Matsumoto. But `applyWord_single` shows
that along **any** reduced word the operators merely walk the basis, so
whichever word was chosen, `orbitMap (T_w) = single w 1`. Distinct basis
vectors ⇒ independent. Well-definedness is never used.

### The remaining gap, stated exactly

`orbitMap` is surjective. So the full basis theorem is **exactly** injectivity
of `orbitMap`, equivalently `dim_K H_n(q) ≤ (n+1)!`, equivalently that the
`T_w` also span. That is where Matsumoto is genuinely needed: two reduced words
for the same `w` give the same element of `H` **iff** they are connected by
braid moves (invariance under a braid move is immediate from `σ_braid` /
`σ_commute`; the combinatorial statement that reduced words are braid-connected
is Matsumoto). `basisTw` carries that hypothesis explicitly and its docstring
says it is not established.

### Two routes to close it, neither started

1. **Prove Matsumoto** (Mathlib TODO, `Coxeter/Basic.lean:78`). Needs the
   strong exchange property first; Mathlib's `Coxeter/Inversion.lean` has the
   ingredients (`wordProd_mul_getD_rightInvSeq`, `IsReduced.nodup_rightInvSeq`)
   but not the property. Upstreamable; a multi-session piece.
2. **Type-A tower spanning.** Span `H_{n+1}` over `H_n` by the `n+1` staircase
   elements `1, σ_n, σ_n σ_{n-1}, …` and induct — the Hecke analogue of the
   `InCosets` / `card_An_le` argument already in `SymmetricCoxeter.lean`.
   Avoids Matsumoto entirely, but is type-A only and not upstreamable as such.
