## Addendum (owner asked): the drive, the fit, and the one literature lead

**What closing c_3 means.** Move it from tier `cal-anchored` to `derived`.
Cal-anchored = extracted by inverting c_3 = (Q_beta^exp/Q_N2 - 1)/u^3, so it
carries no content beyond the measurement. Derived = the framework produces it
and Q_beta becomes a prediction to CHECK. Motivating near-match:
c_3 = -2 = J_Hopf(q=1), 3.47% off.

**The fit this audit prevents**, concretely and in ~20 minutes: B has two
values; posit B(q) = alpha + beta*g(q) for any plausible categorical g; solve
the 2x2. Fits both anchors EXACTLY, sign change included, produces a clean
formula, and is worth NOTHING -- two parameters against two points leaves zero
degrees of freedom, so there is nothing left to be right or wrong about. The
subtler form: one free normalisation tuned to the proton anchor, muon presented
as an "independent cross-check" that is not independent because the
normalisation absorbed the freedom.

**Budget, so it can be checked not argued: at most ONE free parameter**, and
one is generous.
