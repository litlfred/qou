SUCCESSOR to qou-ahu3 question (b), which was VACUOUS as posed.

WHY THE OLD QUESTION DIED. I asked "is there ANY relation between the 8x8
ordering-indexed entries and the LP cone condition?" Measured: the entries
include M33 = q(q^2+1)/4, M44 = (q^2+1)/(4q^3), M00, M77, and

    M33/M44 = q^4 ,  M77/M00 = q^6 ,  hence q^2 = (M77/M00)/(M33/M44) ,
    and  q = 4 M33 / (q^2 + 1) .

So q itself is a rational expression in four entries: they generate ALL of
Q(q). Every rational function of q is then expressible in them, the cone
numerator included, trivially and with no content. Probe:
computations/probes/toffoli_8x8_to_cone_relation.py, 5/5.

MY OVER-CLAIM, corrected in the same probe. I first concluded the OPPOSITE --
that no rational expression could work -- from the (true) fact that the cone
numerator divides no entry's numerator. That inference is invalid: e1 - e2 can
vanish where neither does, so a rational expression's zero set is NOT confined
to the union of its constituents' zeros. What the divisibility actually
supports is narrower and is what the probe now claims:

    NO MONOMIAL (product or quotient) of the entries equals the cone condition.

The earlier zero-set refutation of prop:toffoli-lp-stability is UNAFFECTED,
because the proof's form is a monomial times a sign -- exactly the case the
divisibility argument covers.

THE RE-POSED QUESTION. A relation is only informative if its FORM is
restricted. Candidates, roughly in increasing generosity:

  (i)   monomial in the entries               -- ANSWERED NO (proved)
  (ii)  Z-linear (or Q-linear) combination of the entries and their products
        up to some bounded degree
  (iii) expressible WITHOUT first reconstructing q -- i.e. is the cone
        condition in the subring generated by the entries, as opposed to the
        field?  The field is everything; the subring is not, and that is where
        any real content lives.

(iii) is the sharpest and is the recommended next step: the entries have
denominators that are powers of q and (q^2+1), so the subring question is
concrete and decidable by linear algebra over a bounded degree window.

NOTE ON WHY THIS MATTERS AT ALL. The arc has already REFUTED the reduction
prop:toffoli-lp-stability asserts. This successor is not needed to close that;
it asks the constructive follow-up -- whether the ordering-indexed data can
express the cone condition in ANY disciplined way. A negative answer would say
the 8x8 and the NF cone are genuinely different objects; a positive one would
supply the map the proposition always lacked.

## ANSWERED 2026-08-31 — item (iii) is POSITIVE, and the codimension is what makes it a result

Probe: `computations/probes/toffoli_cone_in_entry_subring.py`, **8/8**, exact
sympy over Q(q); the one numeric step is an mpmath-60dps agreement check of the
symbolic Gram against `pyhecke.gram.gram_matrix_mp` (max |diff| 2.77e-54 over
q = 0.7, the proton q0, 1.7). No float64.

**THE MEASUREMENT.** Over the common denominator D = q^6 (q^2+1)^4 every element
is a numerator polynomial of degree <= 20, so the ambient space is 21-dimensional.

    {1} u {8 distinct entries} u {36 pairwise products}   45 generators, rank 15
    the cone coordinate appended                          rank 15  -> IN SPAN

**Rank 15 of 21 is a PROPER subspace, codimension 6.** That is the whole content:
a generic rational function with this denominator is NOT in the span, so
membership is a real coincidence rather than the forced answer question (b) got.

**Two sharpenings.**

  * **Squares alone are NOT enough.** {1} u entries u squares spans rank 14, and
    the cone coordinate raises it to 15 -- OUTSIDE. The cross terms M_ij M_kl are
    load-bearing; this is not "any quadratic will do".
  * **Four entries suffice.** {M00, M33, M44, M77} at degree 2 spans rank 14
    (codim 7, strictly inside the rank-15 span) and still contains the target.
    Explicit 10-term witness, verified symbolically to exact zero:

        1*(-4/9) + M00^2*(5/36) + M00*M33*(-20/3) + M00*M44*(-10/9)
        + M00*M77*(5/18) + M33^2*(-28/9) + M33*M44*(20/3) + M33*M77*(38/9)
        + M44^2*(20/9) + M77^2*(5/36)   =   (G t)_5

**The cone coordinate, exactly** (also re-derived here from the symbolic Gram):

        (G t)_5 = (q^10 + q^6 - q^4 + q^2 - 1) / (q^2 + 1)^2

**WHAT THIS DOES NOT DO — it does not revive `prop:toffoli-lp-stability`.** That
block reduces the cone condition to "P_77 . W_std with the sign of P_76", a
MONOMIAL times a sign, and the zero-set refutation of that stands untouched (the
probe re-checks the divisibility fact: no entry's numerator is divisible by the
cone numerator). A degree-2 polynomial is a strictly larger class than a
monomial. So the result **explains why** the published reduction had to fail:
the relation exists but needs cancellation BETWEEN products, which is exactly
how the cone acquires its extra root at q_c ~ 0.8945, and which no single
product can supply.

**Items (i) and (ii) of the list above are now subsumed or answered:** (i)
Z-linear in the entries alone is refuted a fortiori by the squares-alone
measurement (rank 14 without the target); (ii) is what was measured.

**Remaining, and genuinely open:** the 10-term witness is A solution, not THE
solution -- the solution space has dimension |generators| - rank = 15 - 14 = 1
free parameter for the four-entry span, and 45 - 15 = 30 for the eight-entry
span. Whether any solution has a structural reading (e.g. coefficients that are
Hecke-theoretic rather than arbitrary rationals) is unmeasured, and is the only
part of this question left. Denominators 9, 36, 18, 3 are suggestive of nothing
so far.

## RE-SCOPED 2026-08-31 — the open item was posed in the WRONG BASIS, and is now answered

Probe: `computations/probes/toffoli_cone_in_markov_hecke_parameters.py`, **13/13**,
exact sympy over Q(q). Lean: `cone_eq_markov_hecke_polynomial`,
`toffoliDiagonal_eq_markov`, both sorry-free, axioms
`[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]`; file now 34 theorems / 0 sorries.

**MY OWN FRAMING WAS THE DEFECT.** The entry above left open "whether any member
of the solution family has a Hecke-theoretic reading", over the ten RATIONAL
coefficients (-4/9, 5/36, -20/3, ...) in the 8x8-amplitude basis. Those
coefficients have no structural reading because THE BASIS has none. In the
natural coordinates the answer is immediate:

    z    = q^3/(q^2+1)     Markov parameter, convention D
    hbar = q - 1/q         Hecke deformation, sigma^2 = hbar sigma + 1

    (G t)_5  =  z^2 + 2 hbar z + 3 hbar^2 z^2 + hbar^3 z + hbar^4 z^2

Integer coefficients **(1, 2, 3, 1, 1)**, no denominators, and **degree 6 is
MINIMAL** (measured: no polynomial in (z, hbar) of total degree <= 5 equals it).

**FORCED, NOT FITTED — and half of it is PRIOR ART, which the corpus-grep
caught.** Every Markov trace of a Hecke word lies in Z[z, hbar] (Markov property
removes the top generator; the quadratic relation reduces the rest), and the
corpus ALREADY CARRIES that as a table: `QOU.Machinery.MarkovTraceH4.TRM` lists
all 24 H_4 normal-form trace values in `z` and `h`, its entries 9 and 13 being
`h*z^2 + z` = tr(sigma_0 sigma_1 sigma_0). The probe now READS that file and
checks the six H_3 values against it rather than claiming the derivation. What
is new is only the downstream assembly, which nothing in the corpus had written:
t = (0, 0, 1, hbar, 0, 0), i.e. T_q = sigma_1 + hbar sigma_0 sigma_1, so
(G t)_5 = tr(W sigma_1) + hbar tr(W sigma_0 sigma_1) is in Z[z, hbar] before any
computation.

**Two clean negatives**, by resultant (minimal polynomial over each subfield):

    over Q(z)     degree 3   -> NOT a function of the Markov parameter alone
    over Q(hbar)  degree 2   -> NOR of the Hecke deformation alone

Q(z, hbar) = Q(q) since 3 and 2 are coprime (q exhibited explicitly), so the
content is the SUBRING claim, not a field claim.

**One structural exclusion, scope-(b).** N = q^10+q^6-q^4+q^2-1 is neither
reciprocal nor anti-reciprocal -- q^10 N(1/q) has coefficients
(-1,0,1,0,-1,0,1,0,0,0,1) against N's (1,0,0,0,1,0,-1,0,1,0,-1). Alexander
polynomials of knots ARE reciprocal, so N is the Alexander polynomial of no
knot. This rests on the reciprocity invariant, not on a numerical near-miss.

**REMAINING, and now small.** Does the coefficient string (1, 2, 3, 1, 1) count
something in the H_3 reduction tree? That is a well-posed question about five
small integers. The predecessor's version -- ten rationals in a structureless
basis -- is retired, not answered.

## CORRECTION 2026-08-31, ~1h after the entry above — the question I left open was ILL-POSED

Probe (corrected in place, not superseded):
`computations/probes/toffoli_cone_in_markov_hecke_parameters.py`, now **18/18**.
Lean: `markov_hecke_relation`, sorry-free; file now 35 theorems / 0 sorries.

**WHAT I ASSERTED.** That the constraint's expression in (z, hbar) has integer
coefficients **(1, 2, 3, 1, 1)**, and that the surviving open item was "does
(1, 2, 3, 1, 1) count something in the H_3 reduction tree? A well-posed question
about five small integers."

**WHAT IS ACTUALLY TRUE.** z and hbar are ALGEBRAICALLY DEPENDENT:

    R(z, hbar) = hbar^3 z - hbar^2 z^2 + 4 hbar z - 4 z^2 + 1 = 0

irreducible over Q, total degree 4. So Q[z, hbar] is NOT a polynomial ring but
the coordinate ring Q[Z,H]/(R) of an affine rational curve, and a polynomial
representative is unique only MODULO R. Measured: the degree-<=6 representatives
form a coset of a **SIX-dimensional** space. A second, equally integral,
equally degree-6 representative is

    -1 + 4 z^2 - 3 hbar z + 4 z^4 + hbar^2 z^4

differing from the first by exactly (hbar z + z^2 + 1) * R. Six free parameters
is far too much room for five small integers to carry information. The question
is WITHDRAWN, not answered.

**HOW IT WAS CAUGHT.** Not by a gate — by expressing all six components of G.t
in (z, hbar) as a sanity check before starting the H_4 generalisation. The
solver returned a DIFFERENT degree-6 integer string for row 5 than the one I had
just merged. Two integral representatives of one quantity is the signal; the
relation was then one resultant away.

**WHAT SURVIVES, and it is most of the result.**

  * The IDENTITY is true and Lean-proved (`cone_eq_markov_hecke_polynomial`).
    Nothing proved is affected -- the defect was in the interpretation offered
    beside it.
  * MINIMAL DEGREE 6 stands, and is a genuine invariant. That measurement was
    made over the span of the EVALUATED monomials, so it already accounted for
    the relation: no polynomial in (z, hbar) of total degree <= 5 equals the
    constraint, R-multiples included.
  * Integrality stands and is not a one-representative fluke: the grevlex
    normal form modulo R is integral too.
  * The (z, hbar) framing itself stands -- forced by the Markov trace, with
    `QOU.Machinery.MarkovTraceH4.TRM` as the prior art.

**CANONICAL-ISH REPRESENTATIVE.** Reducing modulo a Groebner basis of (R) under
a STATED order (grevlex, Z > H) gives a well-defined element, and both
representatives reduce to it:

    hbar^5 z + 4 hbar^3 z + hbar^2 - 2 hbar z + 5 z^2 - 1

Reproducible, not intrinsic -- another term order gives another normal form.
