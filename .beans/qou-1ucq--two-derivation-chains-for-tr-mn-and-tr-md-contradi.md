Found 2026-08-30 while scoping the six documents of `qou-rm57`. This is a
**numerical contradiction between two live chains**, not a labelling problem,
and it reaches the paper.

## The contradiction

Two independent derivation chains exist per atom. Both declare
`target_tolerance: 1e-09`. They disagree:

| atom | `*-tr-M-q0` | `markov-trace-assembly-*` | rel diff | vs own tolerance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| p | 1.0653062285923893 | 1.0653062285923893 | **0.0** | — |
| n | 0.9232332971695838 | 0.9232332299116371 | 7.285e-8 | **73×** |
| D | 0.3882864715477134 | 0.3882864240747888 | 1.223e-7 | **122×** |

The proton pair agrees **exactly**, so this is not a systematic offset between
the two methods. It is specific to n and D.

## 🛑 It reaches the paper, and the quoted uncertainty is off by ~1e8

[`content/quantum-observable-universe/mass-theory/markov-trace-frobenius-selection.md`](../content/quantum-observable-universe/mass-theory/markov-trace-frobenius-selection.md)
lines 111–113 tabulate these values **with error bars**:

| row | value | quoted bound | sibling chain differs by | ratio |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| proton | 1.0653061442544499 | ≤ 2.57e-16 | — | — |
| neutron | 0.9232332299116371 | ≤ 2.03e-16 | 7.285e-8 | **3.6e8×** |
| deuteron | 0.3882864240747888 | ≤ 8.16e-16 | 1.223e-7 | **1.5e8×** |

A quoted bound of 2e-16 against an independent-chain disagreement of 7e-8 is
**eight orders of magnitude** apart. Whatever those bounds measure, it is not
the uncertainty on the quantity.

## And the table straddles two pins in three rows

Measured against the chains:

- **neutron and deuteron** match the `markov-trace-assembly-*` chain exactly —
  which evaluates at the **CODATA-2018 muon pin** `1.1099785955541805`.
- **proton** matches *neither* chain. It is identical to 17 digits with
  `markov-peel-atomic-tr-m.derivation.json` step 3's **proton-pin** C value
  `1.0653061442544499756…`.

So one three-row table carries **row 1 at the proton pin and rows 2–3 at a
muon pin** — a layer straddle inside a published table, of exactly the kind
`qou-y313` was opened for one artifact type down.

## What is already known, and what is not

The block **already carries a banner** (flagged 2026-08-09) saying the table is
convention-unlabelled, that convention C produced it, and that *"the table must
be regenerated by its producer, not hand-edited."* That much is not new.

**New here:** (a) the two chains contradict each other, (b) by 73×/122× their
own declared tolerance, (c) the quoted error bars understate that by ~1e8, and
(d) the table mixes two pins across its three rows. The existing banner
addresses convention only, and would be fully discharged by a C→D regeneration
that left every one of (a)–(d) in place.

## What is NOT established

**Which of each pair is right.** I did not guess and did not pick one. Both
values are recorded in each document's scoping block (PR #6274) so the next
reader meets the discrepancy rather than inheriting a coin-flip.

## Why the convention audit cannot find this

[`derivation_convention_frame_audit.py`](../computations/audits/derivation_convention_frame_audit.py)
compares each value to **canon** and fires above `REL_TOL = 1e-6`. Both of these
values are equally far from canon (~6.5 % and ~32 %), so it flags them
identically and never notices they disagree with **each other** — and the
disagreement itself, at 1e-7, is *below its threshold anyway*. Two blind spots
at once, in an audit merged the same day. This came from reading the files.

## The work

Re-derive **one** of each contradicting pair under convention D at the proton
pin and compare. The proton agreeing exactly is the lever: whatever differs for
n and D does not differ for p, so the fault is in something the proton path does
not exercise (the n/D braid words, or the 3A-strand handling those two share).

**Default if nothing is done:** the paper keeps a three-row table with mixed
pins, values from one of two contradicting chains, and error bars understating
the real spread by ~1e8. That is worse than the convention issue the existing
banner describes, and the banner does not mention it.
