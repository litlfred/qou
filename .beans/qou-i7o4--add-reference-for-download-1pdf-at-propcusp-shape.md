The figure-eight complement is the corpus's most-used hyperbolic object and its deformation space has an explicit arithmetic classification that the corpus does not cite anywhere.

**From the source**
- Sec. 1 (Introduction), with the curve C at (1)-(2) and the Weierstrass model E at (4) — The deformation curve of incomplete hyperbolic structures on the figure-eight knot complement has genus one and is birational to an elliptic curve E over Q; degenerations correspond to points of finite order on E, and the Haken cases correspond exactly to the rational points of E, a cyclic group of order four.
  Use here: Supplies a finite arithmetic classification of what the corpus's muon-anchor knot complement can degenerate to, which bounds any attempt to deform that anchor and gives the cusp/Reeb blocks an arithmetic handle they currently lack.
