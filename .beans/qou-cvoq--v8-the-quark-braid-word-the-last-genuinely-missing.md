The only carrier still genuinely missing. chi=-6 gives the LEPTON words
(T(2,n) = closure of sigma_1^n, n odd <= 7) and the corpus already has the hadron
words (gluon unit sigma_0 sigma_1^-1, B_3 = its cube). No quark word exists.
Every quark row in Y = B - L currently ASSUMES writhe(quark) = 0, motivated only
by the gluon unit being writhe-balanced. If quarks have nonzero writhe, L(quark)
= +-1 != 0 and B - L breaks on the quark rows.
Candidate route: a quark should be a single strand / sub-braid of
B_3 = (sigma_0 sigma_1^-1)^3. Read its writhe off that decomposition rather than
assuming it.
