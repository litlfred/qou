- Thm 4.9 + Thm 4.10 — For a cusped orientable 3-manifold M and any rho: pi_1(M) -> Isom^+(H^3), all pseudo-developing maps give the same volume (so vol(rho) is well-defined), and vol(rho) equals the straightened volume — i.e. it can be computed by straightening ANY ideal triangulation of M and summing tetrahedron volumes.
  Use here: prop:thurston-volume-additivity asserts triangulation-independent additivity of volume over an ideal triangulation; Thm 4.10 is the theorem that licenses 'any triangulation' in the cusped, representation-valued setting rather than only for the complete structure.
