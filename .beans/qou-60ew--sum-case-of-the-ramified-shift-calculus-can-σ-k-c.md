Continues `qou-4g01` (#6474, merged `e9c390ff7d`). The W1 prune there is for
SINGLE-shift operators; its audit's "Honest scope" leaves the sum case open.

Branch `claude/shift-calculus-sum-case-2026-09-03`.

Plan:
1. Lean: extend `QOU.Mathlib.RamifiedShiftCalculus` with finite sums of shift
   operators (`Σ_k c_k σ^k`): residue slot of a sum = sum of slots; a sum of
   derivative-like terms of shifts 1 and 2 still has a dead residue slot at
   every jet level on a power-series seed (no cancellation can revive what is
   termwise zero); decompose a general element by shift parity; what a
   non-zero residue forces (some term with `c_k(-k) ≠ 0`), and what the (-1)^n
   residual forces on the odd/even parts.
2. Probe: exact sympy check of the sum case, including a NON-vacuity witness
   where two non-derivative terms cancel each other's residue.
3. Audit doc + one scope sentence on the conjecture block.

RESULT (2026-09-03, PR #6498). The plan's guess "no cancellation can revive
what is termwise zero" was WRONG. Measured exactly (Lean sorry-free, probe
27/27):

- twoOp_iterate_residue: shift 2 + shift 1, BOTH annihilating constants, has
  D^{n+2} f(-2) = c1(-2)·c2(-1)·(D^n f)(1). The word (1 then 2) jumps over the
  constant slot; weight -1 classical and Jackson+q-d/dz alike. So the #6474
  clause "a residue needs a non-derivative component" is single-shift only.
- residual_tower_is_odd_part: a tower with the full (-1)^n residual is
  generated by the odd part; the even part annihilates every level.
- twoOp_residual_kills_residue: residue and residual are mutually exclusive
  for derivative-like sums of shifts <= 2. The #6474 conclusion (odd shift AND
  non-derivative component) survives for sums by this second route.

Honest gap: a residual weaker than full alternation at every level (asymptotic,
or on a sub-tower) is not covered. Shifts >= 3 still unpruned. D_q not built.
