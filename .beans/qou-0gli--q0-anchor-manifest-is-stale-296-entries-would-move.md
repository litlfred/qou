Found by exploration 2026-08-30 while working `qou-mjcd`. **Measured, not
applied** — regenerating the manifest is a corpus-wide declaration change and is
the owner's call.

## What it is

`computations/q0_anchor_manifest.json` is GENERATED by
`gen_q0_anchor_manifest.py`, which classifies each committed witness by the q₀
signature in its text: muon-only → `ideal_muon`, proton-only →
`physical_proton`, both → `ambiguous`, neither → **omitted**.

Replaying that classification read-only against the current corpus (no write,
no producer run) gives:

| move | count |
|---|---|
| absent → `ideal_muon` | **150** |
| absent → `physical_proton` | 102 |
| `physical_proton` → `ambiguous` | **34** |
| absent → `ambiguous` | 4 |
| `ambiguous` → `physical_proton` | 3 |
| `ideal_muon` → `physical_proton` | 2 |
| `ideal_muon` → `ambiguous` | 1 |
| **total moving** | **296** (manifest holds 751) |

## Why it matters

**Absent is not neutral.** `resolve_q0_anchor` returns the runtime **proton**
default for any stem not in the manifest. So 150 producers whose committed
witnesses carry a muon-only signature are being handed proton by the resolver —
a declaration that contradicts their own recorded output.

The 34 `physical_proton → ambiguous` are deliberate two-anchor probes declared
single-layer. Confirmed on three by reading them: e.g.
`flavor-split-alpha-dilog-expansion.witness.json` describes itself as "both
anchors" and its payload cleanly separates `proton_anchor.q0` from
`muon_anchor.q0`, yet it is declared `physical_proton` and stamped
`q0Value` = proton. `witness.py` names this exact failure — *"a witness
asserting a pin its contents do not carry"* — and the manifest already has the
`ambiguous` category for it (16 entries today).

## Two DIFFERENT causes, and the split matters

- **18 of the 150** carry vintage **C**. These were invisible before
  `gen_q0_anchor_manifest.MUON_VINTAGES` was widened to recognise C this
  session: a producer migrated to the current muon pin matched no vintage,
  scored neither signature, and was omitted → defaulted to proton. My fix makes
  them detectable; the manifest has not been regenerated since.
- **132 of the 150** carry vintage **A**, which `MUON_VINTAGES` always matched.
  Those are not a detection failure at all — the committed manifest is simply
  **stale**, never regenerated as witnesses were added.

Do not attribute all 150 to the detector fix. It accounts for 18.
