## Reasons for Scrapping

A2 was scoped as *"assemble the joint second-order Plancherel structure of the
`R_k` entering `Cov(Sigma_h, R_4)`"*. There are no `R_k` entering
`Cov(Sigma_h, R_4)` by way of `Sigma_h`: bean `qou-470y` shows `Sigma_h` is not
a polynomial in the free cumulants at all, so the object A2 would assemble does
not exist.

This is not "A2 walled" in the sense the workplan anticipated — it is A2 being
**premised on a step that cannot be taken**. The workplan rated A1 low-risk and
A2 the crux; the risk was in fact concentrated entirely in A1, and inverted.

Scrapped rather than left open so the work plan does not carry a task whose
input is now known not to exist. If a future route makes some *other*
polynomial function stand in for `Sigma_h`, that is a new bean with a new
premise, not this one resumed.
