## WHY THIS IS BIGGER THAN THE H_4 QUESTION

The metric blocker's repair -- "quotient by the radical" -- looked like an ad hoc
amputation. IT IS NOT. The radical is the Temperley-Lieb ideal, generated by the
three-strand q-antisymmetriser, and the quotient is a canonical, named,
well-studied algebra with a diagrammatic calculus and a standard form.
"Quotient by the radical" and "work in TL_n" are THE SAME INSTRUCTION.

So the degeneracy is not a defect convention D introduced. IT IS WHAT
CONVENTION D MEANS. Under C the framework was doing full-Hecke; under D it has
been doing Temperley-Lieb all along without saying so. That belongs in the
registry and in the paper.

## THE CLOSED FORM, AND THE ONE IDENTITY BEHIND ALL OF IT

  y_(n-k,k) = [n - 2k + 1]_q / [2]_q^n ,     y_lambda = 0 for >= 3 rows.

Verified against y_lambda_su_n for EVERY partition of n = 2..7.

Everything reduces to the three-term q-integer recursion

  [m+2] + [m] = [2] * [m+1],

proved in Lean as `qint_three_term_pair` in ONE linear_combination, stated on a
formal inverse pair q*r = 1 with X = q^m, Y = r^m abstracted -- no natural
subtraction, no q^-1, no division. That single identity IS:

  * the branching coherence of the Markov trace at EVERY level of the tower
    (`markov_branching_general`);
  * hence mu_+ = y_(3) + y_(2,1) and mu_- = y_(2,1) + y_(1^3) -- this morning's
    qou-4g4a result is the n=3 instance;
  * hence, one level up, y_(3)[H_3] = y_(4) + y_(3,1) and
    y_(2,1)[H_3] = y_(3,1) + y_(2,2), i.e. [5]+[3] = [2][4] and [3]+[1] = [2]^2.

    TWO-STRAND WEDDERBURN WEIGHTS. The two numbers the whole NN sector runs on

## THE RISK -- stated first, not discovered later

Three extra parameters is NEITHER "more accurate to experiment" NOR "backed by
canonical derivation" by itself. qou-4g4a proved an IMPOSSIBILITY -- the
interface band lies strictly between the two isospin weights, missing by 63 %
and 23 %. The danger of C5 is that it converts a clean impossibility into a fit
with room to hide. A 5-parameter interface that reaches the deuteron proves
NOTHING unless the 5 parameters are themselves derived.

What C5 has actually delivered so far is STRUCTURAL: the arena is now a named
algebra with a known form. It is not yet a prediction, and the Lean module says
so in its own docstring.
