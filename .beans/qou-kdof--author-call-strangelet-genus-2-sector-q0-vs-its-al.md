`content/quantum-observable-universe/observations/strangelet-double-beta-interpretation.md`
states that the interaction products are "born in the genus-2 sector where
electromagnetism is absent (Q = 0)" and cascade to "the genus-1 sector where
electromagnetism is active (Q != 0)".

But the knots it names for those sectors are written as mirrors —
`T_{2,5}^sigma`, `T_{2,3}^sigma` — i.e. all-negative crossings. Under
`def:coulomb-charge` (`Q = (1/2)(n+ - n-)`) an all-negative 5-crossing knot has
`Q = -5/2`, not 0. So as written both sectors have `Q != 0`, and the stated
`Q = 0`/`Q != 0` contrast between them does not hold.

Possible resolutions, all author calls: the sector `Q` may be a composite charge
over the whole Borromean unit rather than the individual knot's; the mirror
superscripts may be notational rather than chirality-bearing; or the sector
assignment may need restating. Not guessed at during the sweep.

Note this bean is adjacent to but distinct from the defect that *was* fixed in
that block (it cited `prop:crossing-energy-decomposition` for an "EM/strong
ratio" in the `p = m` sector, where the channel-total ratio has a zero
denominator).
