Added 2026-08-02 from library/arxiv-1707.02991v1/1707.02991v1.pdf (authors verified: Piotr Kucharski, Markus Reineke, Marko Stošić, Piotr Sułkowski). The knots–quivers correspondence: to a knot K one associates a quiver whose vertices are the generators of the (open-BPS) HOMFLY-PT homology, and whose motivic Donaldson–Thomas / LMOV invariants express the colored HOMFLY invariants. Worked example: the trefoil $3_1$ → 3-node quiver (3 = det $3_1$ = # HOMFLY generators). For QOU: gives the concrete OPEN-BPS count of $4_1$ used in the $A_\omega$ non-degeneracy check — $\det(4_1)=5$ nodes, which is NOT the GGM resurgent-Stokes $\{3,4\}$ (the two BPS sectors are distinct).
