Note
NOT READ — metadata only, reconciled from three agreeing bibliographies (see comment above). TWO SCOPE CAVEATS, both established from other documents rather than from this one. (1) COXETER GROUPS, NOT H_n(q): every document that cites it describes it as constructing GS bases for Coxeter groups W = sgp⟨Σ | (ss′)^m = 1⟩ at q = 1; none mentions the Iwahori–Hecke deformation. The q-deformed type-A statement is [kangleeleeoh2002] §4, Prop. 4.3 (pp. 275–276), held and read 2026-11-23. (2) NOT GENERAL COXETER: [chenliu2009] Example 3.2 refutes the Bokut–Shiao conjecture that a GS basis of an arbitrary Coxeter group follows from S = {(1),(2),(3′)} with commuting relations; the positive results are for finite Coxeter groups, which does cover type A_{n-1}. It is cited in this folio for the COMPLETION family $\sigma_i\sigma_{i-1}\cdots\sigma_j\sigma_i \to \sigma_{i-1}\sigma_i\sigma_{i-1}\cdots\sigma_j$ that HeckeGS.lean §4e names as missing from `heckeRewritingSystem`; the axiom `hecke_is_gs_basis` that formerly cited 'Bokut & Shiao 2001, Thm 3.5' was deleted as FALSE for the uncompleted system (NotAGSBasis.hecke_not_gs_basis_four).
