Note
arXiv:2506.10206v2 (2025), MSC 33B30/33B15. A beta-integral technique giving analytic proofs of two-term Li₂ relations and ladder identities with arguments in ℚ(√2), ℚ(√3), ℚ(√5), ℚ(√−7), plus single-term dilogarithm evaluations. Recalls (§1.1) the duplication, Euler reflection and Landen equations used to reduce the 4₁ real-q Habiro-tail action A₀ = π²/5 to Li₂(φ⁻²); it does NOT state the value Li₂(φ⁻²) = π²/15 − ln²φ itself (that is zagier-dilogarithm §1). Its own golden-ratio result is π²/100 = Re Li₂(r₀), r₀ a root of r⁴ − 3r³ + 4r² − 2r + 1 (§6). Corrected 2026-11-21, bean qou-hrq0. PDF read in-sandbox 2026-07-18 (library/arxiv-2506.10206v2/2506.10206v2.pdf); the Rogers L-function (eq 4), Euler reflection (6), Landen (7) match the corpus derivation.
