YEAR CAVEAT: the arXiv banner rendered on p. 1 reads '8 Nov 2014' but the 1502.* identifier is a February 2015 posting; recorded as 2015 with the printed banner date noted here. No DOI or journal line on the preprint; no published version sought. Read 2026-08-09 for §3.7 (p. 6), verified in the PDF: Shirshov 1953 and Cartier 1958 give counterexamples to PBW for Lie algebras over a commutative algebra, and Cohn's 1963 family L_p = Lie_K(x₁,x₂,x₃ | y₃x₃ = y₂x₂ + y₁x₁) over K = k[y₁,y₂,y₃ | yᵢᵖ = 0] is proved not to embed in its universal enveloping algebra for p = 2, 3, 5 (Bokut–Chen–Chen, J. Algebra 337 (2011) 82-102). That paragraph is why cor:pbw / cor:ugb-pbw cannot drop the 'free as an R-module' hypothesis their proofs use (bean qou-pwd4). §3.7 REPORTS those counterexamples and proves none of them — the primaries have not been read and are not in this file, so cite this entry as the collecting source, not as the proof.
