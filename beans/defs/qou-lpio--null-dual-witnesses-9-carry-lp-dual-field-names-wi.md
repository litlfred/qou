## What this is

Nine witnesses carry one or more of the six LP-dual field names
(`y0_star`, `y_star`, `active_set`, `primal_obj`, `dual_obj`,
`duality_gap`) with the value empty — `None`, `[]`, `{}` or `""`. The names
are published; nothing is behind them. AGENTS.md makes the dual block
first-class because those variables *are* the binding-energy shadow prices,
so a name with no value satisfies the rule's letter without meeting it.

Reported by `computations/witness_hollowness_audit.py` as offence kind
`null-dual` (PR #5668 gave it the deep occurrence walk that finds all nine).
Advisory: exit 0 by default, 1 under `--strict`.

**Five all-empty:**
`audits/delta-n2-placeholder-audit`, `audits/deuteron-delta-i-audit`,
`binding-lp-delta-audit`, `compound-binding-via-differences`,
`confinement-operator-selection`.

**Four partial (one field each):**
`e-0-lp-dual-h3` (`y0_star`, above eight per-target blocks whose other five
fields are real), `option-b-proton-mass-full-chain` (`active_set`),
`r5-full-canonical-knot-solver` (`active_set`),
`wedderburn-lp-ew-wiring` (`active_set`).

## Why no sweep

**The population drains on its own.** Three of the eight measured on
2026-08-23 — `shadow-price-c-321-q0`, `toffoli-lp-stability`,
`sweeps/paper-six-term-shadow-price-sweep` — carried NO dual fields at all
within two hours, regenerated by unrelated sibling precision work
(`migrate(q0) batch 4`, and two `fix(precision)` commits). Nobody decided
anything; routine work removed them.

## What to check, and when to act

Re-run the audit. **If the null-dual count is still 9 or has grown after a
few weeks of ordinary churn, the drain hypothesis is wrong and this needs a
deliberate pass.** Record the count and the date each time it is checked, so
the trend is visible rather than re-derived.

If a deliberate pass IS needed, the option is **drop the names**, and it is
already measured safe: `witness_hollowness_audit`'s drop-safety
co-occurrence scan finds nothing that reads a dual field from any of these.
The three apparent hits on `confinement-operator-selection` all read their
duals from a *different, populated* witness —
`onepb_status_dashboard.py` from `lp-confinement-nf-forcing`,
`deuteron_from_scratch_ledger.py` from `deuteron-real-sdp`, and
`lp_dual_solver.py` merely names it in prose. Re-run the scan rather than
trusting that sentence; it ages with the tree.

The other two options are worse. **Populate** needs an actual linear program
defined per producer — a modelling decision, not plumbing, and
`confinement-operator-selection` has no LP at all (its three `RANKING`
passes are `sorted()` calls). **Label** is the zero-cost interim if a drop
is unwanted.

## 2026-09-13 — this list is BLOCKING a convention-D refresh, which the "drains on its own" framing does not anticipate

Found while screening the raw-float64 queue. **Count re-check requested by this
bean's own "record the count and the date each time" instruction is NOT done
here** — I measured one member, not the population.

`confinement-operator-selection` (an all-empty member) does not merely *carry*
nine hollow names: those names are what **stops its producer from ever
regenerating it.** The producer emits a downgrade guard —

    Witness NOT written -- refusing a downgrade.
      the committed witness holds 12 key(s) this producer cannot emit ...
      (9 of those are None/STUB placeholders, 3 are real provenance.)

— and it fires on exactly the nine. The witness is consequently frozen at
**convention C, CODATA-2018 muon** values (`z = 0.4993203340335982`,
`q0 = 1.1099785955541805`, `gram_det = -0.1892`) while the producer's live
imports are **convention D at the proton pin** (`z = 0.6126880371464652`,
`q0 = 1.1099785827513808`, `gram_det = 3.998e-17`, i.e. singular). `z` is
**22.70 %** off; `max|ΔG| = 0.1722`; `max|ΔG⁻¹| = 2.0965`. ~20 content blocks
read those C/muon numbers, including `mba-step10-confinement-sdp`.

**Consequence for this bean's decision.** The "population drains on its own"
hypothesis assumes ordinary churn regenerates these witnesses. For this member
the causation runs the other way: the hollow names are why churn *cannot* reach
it. It will not drain on its own, however long it is left.

**And that makes the already-measured-safe option load-bearing.** This bean
records that dropping the names is safe here (the drop-safety scan finds nothing
reading a dual field from this witness; the three apparent hits read
`lp-confinement-nf-forcing` instead). Dropping them is therefore not just tidying
— it is the unblocker for the convention-D refresh tracked under `qou-cds2` /
`qou-8iru`. Cross-referenced there.

**Not acted on.** The refresh itself moves ~20 content blocks from C to D and
zeroes a published Gram determinant; that is `qou-cds2` scope and an owner call,
not an idle drain.
