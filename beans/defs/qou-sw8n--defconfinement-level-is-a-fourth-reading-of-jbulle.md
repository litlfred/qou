## The finding

`folio/quantum-observable-universe/knots-particles-confinement/confinement-level.md`
defines a filtration it calls `J^\bullet(q)`:

    J^ℓ(q) := subalgebra generated by {λ : E(λ, q) ≤ E_ℓ(q)}

indexed by Wedderburn-block **energy**, generated by the **blocks**.  And
`prop:gb-spectral-sequence`
(`folio/quantum-observable-universe/mass-endomorphism/gb-spectral-sequence.md`)
— a step-7 carrier the canonical-derivation cheat sheet names — cites **that
one** as its filtration.  Not Coxeter length, not an ideal power, not a
rewrite depth.

Found by walking the canonical chain (`qou-u96j`), not by grepping the topic —
the walk reached `prop:gb-spectral-sequence` and read what it actually cites.

## Why it matters: it evades BOTH exclusion routes, structurally

- **Route A (`unit_in_degree_zero_forces_constant_grading`) does not apply.**
  `J^ℓ` is the subalgebra generated by a **proper subset** of blocks, so its
  own unit is `Σ_{λ∈S} e_λ`, not `1 = Σ_{all λ} e_λ`.  The unit enters only at
  the **top** level — exactly the shape the 2026-08-26 audit §5 says any
  realising filtration must have (*"never in degree 0"*).
- **Route B (`monomial_filtration_forces_constant_grading`) does not apply.**
  It is indexed by blocks, not words, so it is not spanned by a subset of the
  normal-form basis at all.

So it sits precisely in the gap those audits left open — the one the
2026-08-26 audit §3 names as *"what could still escape"*.

## 🛑 It does NOT supply the identification either — scope (a), not (b)

It grades by block energy `E(λ, q)` — the **LP shadow prices**
(`confinement-level-unique-local-constancy` says so in as many words) — not by
`gbDepth`.  A generic-`q` necessary condition already fails: distinct blocks
generically have distinct energies, giving `p(n)` strata against `gbDepth`'s
`n − m_n + 1`.  Measured differing at every `n = 2..7` (p(n) = 2,3,5,7,11,15
against strata 1,1,2,2,3,3).

**That is generic-`q` ONLY and is NOT a proved exclusion.**  The corpus sits
at one specific `q₀`; the definition explicitly permits ties (*"tied operators
enter the filtration together"*); and `confinement-level-properties` states
the ordering **shifts with `q`**.  A tie pattern at `q₀` matching the `gbDepth`
strata exactly is a coincidence of high codimension, not an impossibility.

## The decisive test — NOT run

Compute `E(λ, q₀)` (the LP duals) and compare its strata to `gbDepth`'s.  That
needs the **confinement LP**, which is **step 10** of the canonical chain — so
this fourth reading ties step 7 to step 10 in a way nothing in the corpus
currently records.  That connection is itself the more interesting half.
