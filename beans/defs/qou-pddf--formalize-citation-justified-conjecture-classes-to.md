A conjecture class justified ONLY by a literature citation (e.g. a published theorem cited in its docstring/audit) is a valid *interim* justification per §3b — but a citation is NOT a Lean proof, and not all published math/physics results are fully correct or fully applicable as stated. STANDING POLICY: every conjecture class whose sole justification is a citation should be tracked here for EVENTUAL Lean formalization of the cited theorem itself. Until then it stays an instance-less conjecture class (honest open state), never axiomatized as proved (§'Axiomatization is NOT a Proof'). Child tasks track specific citation-only conjectures.
