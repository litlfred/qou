HYPOTHESIS, wired to nothing. Named test from PR #5229 §4.

The chain is proved, not numerological:
  colour antisymmetry needs a three-row block
    (FibreDimensionForcesTL.lean:68-71, verbatim in the corpus already)
  => r >= 3, by the proved vanishing lemma s_lam = 0 <=> l(lam) > r
     (QOU.QBeta.WenzlWeight.schurPrincipal_eq_zero_iff, sorry-free)
  => fibre of quantum dimension [3]_q
  => z is DETERMINED, by ocneanu_param_eq_of_fibre_qdim (z*[N]_q = q^N,
     sorry-free): z_3 = q^5/(q^4 + q^2 + 1) = 0.4493048091 at the proton pin.

What is missing is any measurement. No QOU channel sits at r >= 3 — all four
registered channels that are in the family at all are at r = 2 (PR #5229 W10).

THE TEST. Build the level-3 channel explicitly — Markov trace at
z_3 = q^5/(q^4+q^2+1), weights s_lam(1, Q, Q^2) at Q = q^2, quotient
H_n/<E^(4)> — and check whether the three-row blocks it KEEPS (exactly the ones
r = 2 annihilates) reproduce a known colour observable WITHOUT a new
calibration.
  PASS = a colour quantity falls out of the l(lam) = 3 sector at the existing
         three calibrations.
  FAIL = it needs a fourth input, in which case 'level 3 = SU(3) = colour' is
         a name and nothing else.

Level-3 scaffolding already computed (PR #5229 W6): row cap l(lam) <= 3;
quotient dim 1,2,6,23,103,513,2761,15767 for n=1..8; corank 0,0,0,1,17,207,
2279,24553; the radical first appears at n = 4 with corank exactly 1,
generated by the one-column diagram (1^4).

ANTI-FIT WARNING for whoever picks this up. Do not tune anything to land a
colour number — that would be a fourth calibration laundered through a level
choice, and the three-calibration discipline review will catch it. The whole
value of this test is that z_3 is FORCED by the fibre, with zero freedom.
Also do not read anything into '2.5995 is near 2.6' or 'near 3': the r in
(2.5,2.7) band is 0.029 wide in z and the two off-family spellings differ by
0.002, so that proximity carries no information (PR #5229 §4).
