Riera-Rodriguez (pjm-v154-n1-p09-s, PDF p.5) gives the S5 action on H1(B,Z) as twice a 4-dim irreducible. Check that the probe's 8 harmonic 1-cochains carry the same representation, by characters on the dart action. Also decompose the Delta_0 and Delta_2 eigenspaces (multiplicities 1,11,5,4,5,4 and 1,5,5,1,6,6) into S5 irreducibles. Compare qualitatively with Fortier Bourque-Petri §9.2 (m1 >= 4) and Cook's FreeFEM multiplicities; do not equate the continuum and combinatorial lambda_1. Lean option: RR's integer identities A^5 = B^2 = (AB)^4 = I, A^T C A = C.
