## RESOLVED NEGATIVELY 2026-08-30 — Sigma_h is not a polynomial in the R_k

`computations/deltalambda/wlambda_covariance_epic_A1_sigmah_not_polynomial.py`,
11/11 assertions, exact over every partition of `n <= 10`.

The workplan scopes A1 as *"Effort: low-med. Risk: low"* and records the premise
that makes Track A go: *"Genus applicability established (both legs
polynomial-on-Young-diagrams)"*. **The `Sigma_h` leg is not, and cannot be.**

### The obstruction

Each ingredient verified exactly, not assumed:

| | verified |
|---|---|
| `Sigma_h(lam') = Sigma_h(lam)` | conjugation-INVARIANT |
| `R_k(lam') = (-1)^k R_k(lam)` | k = 2..8, all partitions n <= 10 |
| `f^lam = f^lam'` | Plancherel is conjugation-invariant |
| `E_Planch[Sigma_h] / n^{3/2} -> ~0.97`, `/n^2 -> 0` | half-integer order |

Suppose `Sigma_h = P(R_2, R_3, ...)`. Conjugation-invariance plus cumulant
parity force `P` to be unchanged when the odd-index `R`'s flip sign, so `P` lies
in the subalgebra generated by the even-index `R`'s and EVEN monomials in the
odd ones. Every such generator has **integer** Plancherel order: `R_even` gives
`k/2` with `k` even, and a pair `R_j R_k` with `j, k` odd gives `(j+k)/2` with
`j+k` even. So `E[P]` grows like an integer power of `n` — but
`E[Sigma_h] ~ c n^{3/2}`. Contradiction.

The argument needs only the **expectation's** growth, not a degree theory, which
is why it is checkable rather than asymptotic hand-waving.

### What survives and is useful

* **`Sigma_c = R_3 / 2`** exactly. The *content* sum IS in `Lambda*`; the hook
  sum is not. Both sides conjugation-odd, consistent with the parity law.
* **`R_4 = 3 omega_3 - n`** — the Lean `r4Value` normalisation is right; the
  naive `Sigma_3 = R_4 + R_2` reading (`R_4 = omega_3 - n`) is wrong.
* **`M_1 = Cov(p_2, omega_3) = Cov(Sigma_h, omega_3)`.** Since
  `p_2 = Sigma_h + R_3/2` and `Cov(R_3, omega_3) = 0` — `R_3` odd, `omega_3`
  even, Plancherel conjugation-invariant — the `p_2` / `Sigma_h` distinction is
  immaterial to `M_1`. It does not rescue polynomiality: `p_2` is not in
  `Lambda*` either.

### Consequence

Track A is blocked at its **first** step, not at A2. Track D (Fulman
exchangeable-pair / Stein) requires neither leg to be polynomial and is
