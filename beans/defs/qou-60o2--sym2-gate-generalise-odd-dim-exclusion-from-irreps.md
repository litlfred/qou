THE GAP: the corpus excluded odd-dimensional IRREP summands
(Sym2GateParity.gate_fails_iff_odd_dim) and the 2026-08-07 collapse audit routed
three beans (qou-8p1i, qou-y7vg, the Ocneanu thread) onto that. But the N=3
question asks for a 3-dimensional OBJECT, and W = V_{1/2} + V_0 has dim 3, sits
in the tensor category generated by the fibre, and is NOT an irrep — so the
criterion did not reach it. 'No 3-dim object passes' was an extrapolation.

PROVED (Frobenius-Schur multiplicity count):
  sym2SingletCount_eq_zero_iff  gate passes iff every orthogonal multiplicity
    is 0 and every symplectic one <= 1, i.e. W is a sum of pairwise DISTINCT
    even-dimensional irreps.
  even_dimOfMult_of_gate        => dim W is EVEN. The invariant.
  dimOfMult_ne_three_of_gate    => no dim-3 object passes, reducible or not.
  gate_fails_of_odd_dim         contrapositive, any odd dimension.
Plus single_irrep_gate_iff_not_even (reproduces the companion criterion on one
irrep — extends, does not compete), reducible_three_fails_gate (the witness that
the content is new), two_copies_fails_gate (dim 4, even, still fails => even is
NECESSARY NOT SUFFICIENT; the invariant prunes, it does not characterise).

SCOPE (b), invariant named: the FS indicator (-1)^{2j} as parity of dim W.
Excludes the single-fibre tensor category and NOTHING BEYOND IT. qou-8pl3 Route A
is untouched and in fact sharpened: 'not a subobject of any V^{tensor n}' now
holds for arbitrary subobjects, not just summands.
