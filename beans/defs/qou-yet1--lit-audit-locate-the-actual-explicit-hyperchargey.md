2026-08-02 result.

Resolved the source confusion.

- arXiv:1806.09450 (DV/Todorov) gives the group-level `U(1)` slot and the `Z6` quotient geometry, but not the explicit hypercharge operator.
- The explicit algebraic weak-hypercharge generator is in C. Furey, arXiv:1806.00612, *SU(3)_C × SU(2)_L × U(1)_Y (×U(1)_X) as a symmetry of division algebraic ladder operators*.
- In the exact paper text, the twelfth non-mixing generator is written as
  `(1/3) Σ_j A†_j A_j - (1/2) Σ_k B†_k B_k` (eq. 43), and the paper states that this assigns charges coinciding with hypercharge `Y`.
- Supporting precursor in the longer thesis route: arXiv:1611.09182 gives `N' = -2Y` on the leptonic subspace (around lines 2168-2171 of the extracted text).

Consequence: the stale corpus claim should be corrected from 'fetch 1806.09450' to 'fetch / cite Furey 1806.00612 (with 1611.09182 as the longer derivation background)'.
