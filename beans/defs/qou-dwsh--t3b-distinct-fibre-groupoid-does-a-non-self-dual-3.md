The ONLY honest remaining direction for flavour SU(3), and the one the corpus itself names but has not built. Single-fibre V^{tensor n} is structurally capped at gl_2 (Descartes chi(A)=-6 -> SU(2) -> dim V = 2 -> partitions <= 2 rows; the (1^3) block DROPS at n=3). So the representation must NOT be a tensor power of one V. Need: a groupoid with inequivalent fibre objects carrying a 3-dim W whose fundamental is not self-dual. Gated on T3a.
