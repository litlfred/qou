Answers 'does hyperbolic geometry on the complement give SU(3)?' -- NO: the holonomy of a hyperbolic structure is rho: pi_1 -> PSL(2,C), RANK 1. Hyperbolic geometry supplies MORE SL(2), reinforcing the dim V = 2 cap. QOU is an SL_2 theory throughout (SU(2) gauge, SL(2,C) holonomy, U_q(sl_2), Jones = the sl_2 specialisation of HOMFLY). The right object is instead the PGL(3,C) analogue: Garoufalidis-Thurston-Zickert gluing equations for PGL(n), Fock-Goncharov coordinates, higher Teichmuller. Generalises the corpus's existing Neumann-Zagier / A-polynomial machinery from PGL(2) to PGL(3). This is the geometric route to a rank-2 structure on the complement.
