    UGB.BuchbergerPBW.ugb_pbw_corollary #

    Real-content formalization (replaces the former vacuous class … where holds : Prop). The PBW corollary U𝔤 ≅ S𝔤 is stated on the concrete U_q(su(2)) instance: the PBW monomials E_+^a K^b E_-^c (PBWMonomial) are exactly the normal forms of the q-Serre rewriting system, and reduction gives each element a unique normal form. The conclusion is a concrete isNormalForm / ReducesToNF claim over real SU2Gen types — not a bare Prop.

    Content block cor:ugb-pbw: Poincaré–Birkhoff–Witt theorem over a commutative ring.

    Manuscript statement #

    For a Lie algebra $\mathfrak g$ over a commutative ring $R$, $U\mathfrak g = T\mathfrak g/J$ is $R$-module isomorphic to $S\mathfrak g$; the PBW basis arises as the Gröbner–Shirshov normal forms of the commutator relations $G = \{x_ix_j - x_jx_i - [x_i,x_j]\}$.

    What is weakened relative to the manuscript #

    The abstract isomorphism is instantiated on the U_q(su(2)) realisation carried by NormalForms.lean, where the GS basis is the q-Serre system qSerreRewritingSystem and the PBW basis is the divided-power basis {E_+^a K^b E_-^c} (PBWMonomial). We state two concrete consequences:

    • every PBW monomial m is in normal form under the q-Serre system — withdrawn: this is false as it was stated, see below; and
    • every element reduces to a unique normal form (normal_form_unique against qSerre_is_gs_basis).

    The R-linear bijection U𝔤 ≅ S𝔤 itself is not assembled (raw AlgElement data type).

    The PBW-normality half was withdrawn (bean qou-ix9e) #

    It rested on the axiom pbw_monomials_are_normal_forms, which became checkable once qSerreRewritingSystem was built rather than axiomatised — and it fails. PBWMonomial.toMonomial lays the letters out as E_+^a K^{b₊} K⁻¹^{b₋} E_-^c, and three of its joins are leading monomials of the system: [K,K⁻¹], [K,E_-], [E_+,E_-]. So those words are reducible, not normal. NormalForms.pbw_monomials_are_not_all_normal_forms proves the negation at ⟨0,1,1,0⟩, which is the word [K,K⁻¹] — rule 1's leading monomial exactly.

    The letter order E_+ … K … E_- is simply not this system's normal-form order: reading the orientations off the rules, reduction moves E_- left past E_+ and moves K right past both, so normal words are E_-^c E_+^a K^b with K and K⁻¹ never mixed. Establishing that is the genuine remaining content of Bokut–Malcolmson Thm 3.2 and is open, so this theorem no longer claims it rather than importing it from a false axiom.

    The existence/termination half is proved (normal_form_exists, bean qou-lzoo), and its unit-leading-coefficient hypothesis is now discharged by qSerre_rules_monic — the thing that was left hanging when the system was opaque.

    PBW corollary — U_q(su(2)) instance.

    With qs the q-Serre data: every element reduces to a unique normal form under the q-Serre rewriting system. Existence is normal_form_exists (termination + monic rules, both discharged here); uniqueness is normal_form_unique against qSerre_is_gs_basis.

    The former first conjunct — "every PBW monomial is in normal form" — is gone, because it is false; see the module docstring and NormalForms.pbw_monomials_are_not_all_normal_forms. What survives is a statement about ReducesToNF over real SU2Gen types, and it no longer depends on any refuted claim.
