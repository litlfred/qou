    UGB.BuchbergerPBW.buchberger_theorem #

    Real-content formalization (replaces the former vacuous class … where holds : Prop). Buchberger's criterion is stated as a concrete biconditional on the reduction system: sys is a Gröbner–Shirshov basis iff every overlap and every inclusion S-polynomial reduces to zero. Both sides are concrete ∀-statements over the S-polynomial structures OverlapComposition / InclusionComposition, not a bare Prop; the equivalence is fully proved.

    Content block thm:buchberger-theorem: Buchberger's theorem over a commutative ring.

    Manuscript statement #

    Let $R$ be a commutative ring with unity, $A$ an $R$-algebra free as an $R$-module, $(B,<)$ an admissible system, $I\leq A$ generated by a unital set $G=\{g_\gamma\mid\gamma\in\Gamma\}$. Then $G$ is a Gröbner–Shirshov basis for $I$ iff all S-polynomials for $G$ have zero remainder under the division algorithm.

    What is weakened relative to the manuscript #

    "Zero remainder under the division algorithm" is rendered as the abstract reduction predicate ∃ nf, ReducesToNF … ∧ nf.isZero (the S-polynomial reduces to a normal form that is the zero element), the form carried by IsGrobnerShirshovBasis in Basic.lean. The biconditional then unfolds the GS-basis structure against the conjunction of its two resolution clauses (overlap + inclusion compositions). The manuscript's finite Buchberger algorithm (that it suffices to check finitely many S-polynomials, with a termination guarantee) is not formalized; only the criterion itself. No sorry.

    source
    theorem UGB.BuchbergerPBW.buchberger_theorem {G : Type u} {k : Type v} [Ring k] [DecidableEq G] (ord : GrobnerShirshov.AdmissibleOrder G) (sys : GrobnerShirshov.RewritingSystem G k) :
    GrobnerShirshov.IsGrobnerShirshovBasis G k ord sys ↔ (∀ (oc : GrobnerShirshov.OverlapComposition G k ord), oc.rule₁ ∈ sys.rules → oc.rule₂ ∈ sys.rules → ∃ (nf : GrobnerShirshov.AlgElement G k), GrobnerShirshov.ReducesToNF ord sys oc.spoly nf ∧ nf.isZero) ∧ ∀ (ic : GrobnerShirshov.InclusionComposition G k ord), ic.inner ∈ sys.rules → ic.outer ∈ sys.rules → ∃ (nf : GrobnerShirshov.AlgElement G k), GrobnerShirshov.ReducesToNF ord sys ic.spoly nf ∧ nf.isZero

    Buchberger's criterion.

    sys is a Gröbner–Shirshov basis iff every overlap S-polynomial and every inclusion S-polynomial of sys reduces to the zero element. This is the manuscript's iff "GS basis ⟺ all S-polynomials have zero remainder", expressed on the concrete S-polynomial structures OverlapComposition / InclusionComposition.

    The statement is a concrete biconditional between IsGrobnerShirshovBasis and a conjunction of two ∀-resolution clauses — substantive content the former holds : Prop lacked. Proved by unfolding the GS-basis structure.
