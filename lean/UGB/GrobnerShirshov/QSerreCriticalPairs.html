    UGB.GrobnerShirshov.QSerreCriticalPairs — discharging the q-Serre GS obligation #

    qSerre_is_gs_basis is an axiom. Bean qou-18s7 verified it by an exact symbolic critical-pair computation (computations/qserre_gs_basis_check.py) but left it verified, not formalised. This file formalises what can be formalised from the shape of the system alone, and isolates exactly what is left.

    The structural observation everything here rests on #

    Every one of the seven q-Serre leading monomials is a two-letter word (qSerre_lm_length), and the seven are pairwise distinct, so a rule of the system is determined by its leading monomial (qSerre_rule_eq_of_lm_eq).

    Those two facts collapse most of the obligation:

    • Inclusion compositions are entirely degenerate. outer.lm = left ++ inner.lm ++ right with both leading monomials of length 2 forces left = right = [], hence outer.lm = inner.lm, hence outer = inner — and then the S-polynomial is a difference of an element with itself. qSerre_inclusion_resolves discharges inclusion_resolves outright.

    • Overlap compositions split by overlap length. overlap ≠ [] together with the length-2 leading monomials leaves only |overlap| ∈ {1, 2} (qSerre_overlap_shape). The |overlap| = 2 case is again degenerate — left = right = [], so rule₁ = rule₂ and the S-polynomial vanishes (qSerre_overlap_resolves_of_length_two).

    What is left #

    Only the |overlap| = 1 case: lm₁ = [a,b], lm₂ = [b,c], and the S-polynomial reduces to a·tail₂ − tail₁·c (the ambient word [a,b,c] cancels because the rules are monic — qSerre_rules_monic). Bean qou-18s7 found exactly 8 such ambiguities among the seven rules and checked that all 8 resolve; each needs an explicit two-step reduction chain, which is what remains to formalise.

    So the axiom's remaining content is 8 concrete reduction chains, not an open mathematical question — a materially smaller and better-understood gap than "is this a Gröbner–Shirshov basis".

    §4 The axiom, reduced to exactly the genuine critical pairs #

    source
    theorem UGB.GrobnerShirshov.qSerre_is_gs_basis_of_critical_pairs {k : Type v} [Field k] (qs : QSerreRelations k) (hcp : ∀ (oc : OverlapComposition SU2Gen k degLexOrder), oc.rule₁ ∈ (qSerreRewritingSystem k qs).rules → oc.rule₂ ∈ (qSerreRewritingSystem k qs).rules → List.length oc.overlap = 1 → ∃ (nf : AlgElement SU2Gen k), ReducesToNF degLexOrder (qSerreRewritingSystem k qs) oc.spoly nf ∧ nf.isZero) :
    IsGrobnerShirshovBasis SU2Gen k degLexOrder (qSerreRewritingSystem k qs)

    qSerre_is_gs_basis follows from the length-1 critical pairs alone.

    This is the point of the file. IsGrobnerShirshovBasis quantifies over every overlap and inclusion composition, which is an unbounded-looking obligation; §§2–3 show that all of it is degenerate except the overlaps whose overlap is a single letter. So the axiom's entire content is the hypothesis hcp below — and bean qou-18s7's computation says there are exactly 8 such ambiguities and all 8 resolve.

    Discharging hcp therefore discharges qSerre_is_gs_basis outright, and hcp is 8 explicit two-step reduction chains rather than an open question about the algebra.

    Exactly eight ordered rule pairs admit a length-1 overlap, and here they are.

    Of the 49 ordered pairs, 41 admit none: a length-1 overlap forces lm₁ = [a,b] and lm₂ = [b,c] sharing the letter b, and for those 41 the second letter of lm₁ differs from the first of lm₂, so the decomposition equations are contradictory.

    This reproduces, inside Lean, the enumeration computations/qserre_gs_basis_check.py arrived at symbolically — the two agree that there are 8, and on which 8. Bean qou-18s7 verified that all 8 resolve; the reduction chains witnessing that are what remains (§6).

    §7 What remains #

    The |overlap| = 1 case. By §3 that means left = [a], overlap = [b], right = [c] with lm₁ = [a,b] and lm₂ = [b,c], and since every rule is monic (qSerre_rules_monic) the S-polynomial simplifies: the ambient word [a,b,c] appears with coefficient 1 in both halves and cancels, leaving

    spoly = a · tail₂ − tail₁ · c

    where tailᵢ is the right-hand side of rule i. Bean qou-18s7 enumerated the ambiguities of this shape — there are 8 — and verified by exact computation over ℚ(q) that every one reduces to zero, each in two steps.

    Formalising those 8 chains is the whole of the remaining gap in qSerre_is_gs_basis. Each needs an explicit MultiStepReduction, and the cost per chain is dominated by the CoeffEq obligation in SingleStepReduction, which is an equality of coefficient functions at every monomial.

    §6 Bridge: contextMul of an explicit element is an explicit element #

    The cost driver in every critical-pair chain is contextMul at a general monomial: its coefficient is stated through excise, so contextMul_coeff_middle (which matches only the literal shape a ++ w ++ b) does not fire. These two lemmas evaluate it once and for all, turning a · (explicit element) · b back into an explicit element, after which a chain step is ordinary arithmetic.
