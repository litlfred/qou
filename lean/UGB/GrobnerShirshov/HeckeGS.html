    The Hecke relations, and why they are not a Gröbner–Shirshov basis #

    Instantiation of the abstract GS framework for the Hecke algebra H_n(q).

    The headline #

    hecke_is_gs_basis — the axiom that used to sit at §4, asserting that heckeRewritingSystem is a GS basis and citing Bokut & Shiao 2001, Thm 3.5 — is false, and has been deleted. NotAGSBasis.hecke_not_gs_basis_four refutes it at n = 4 with a machine-checked counterexample: the overlap of the Yang–Baxter rule σ₃σ₂σ₃ → σ₂σ₃σ₂ with the far-commutation rule σ₃σ₁ → σ₁σ₃ has S-polynomial σ₂σ₃σ₂σ₁ − σ₃σ₂σ₁σ₃, and both of those words are irreducible for the system. So the system is not confluent.

    This is the known situation for Coxeter presentations: Bokut & Shiao's theorem produces a GS basis by completing the presentation with the family σᵢσᵢ₋₁⋯σⱼσᵢ → σᵢ₋₁σᵢσᵢ₋₁⋯σⱼ; heckeRewritingSystem contains only the defining relations, so the cited theorem never applied to it. The axiom was previously vacuous (the unrepaired SingleStepReduction let everything reduce to zero) rather than false; repairing the definitions turned it into a falsehood, which is why it had to go rather than stay as an assumption.

    Everything downstream that used it — heckeCost_well_defined, threading_cost_unique — now takes the GS property as an explicit hypothesis.

    The completion exists and is proved (2026-08-24, bean qou-auq3). HeckeCompletion.lean adjoins the Bokut–Shiao family to this system and proves heckeCompleted_isGrobnerShirshovBasis for every n and every HeckeParams, with no hypothesis and no sorry. So the two theorems below are not merely conditional-and-parked: their completed-system counterparts, heckeCompleted_normalForm_unique and heckeCompleted_threading_cost_unique (§4bx there), hold outright — modulo the two Diamond-Lemma axioms local_confluence and reduction_well_founded, which normal_form_unique carries and which are not this file's to discharge.

                        Why there is no sentinel rule #

                        The composition lemma is stated in terms of leading monomials, and a rule whose leading monomial is the empty word destroys it. isNormalForm asks that no rule's leading monomial be an infix of any monomial of the element; [] <:+: m holds for every m; so with such a rule present nothing non-zero is ever in normal form, and every element reduces to zero. The consequence is proved below, in general rather than for this system: gs_basis_of_empty_lm.

                        That is why heckeRewritingSystem requires 0 < n and drops the sentinel.

                        §4a Leading monomials of the three rule families #

                        With leadingMonomial repaired (it is now the ord-greatest support element, not an arbitrary one) the leading monomials are computable facts rather than intentions, and one of them is not what the docstrings said:

                        • heckeRelation i has leading monomial [σᵢ, σᵢ] — as intended;
                        • farCommRelation i j (j < i) has leading monomial [σᵢ, σⱼ] — as intended;
                        • yangBaxterRelation i j (i < j) has leading monomial [σⱼ, σᵢ, σⱼ], not [σᵢ, σⱼ, σᵢ]. Deg-lex ranks σⱼ above σᵢ, so of the two words of length 3 the larger is the one starting with the larger generator. The rule therefore rewrites σᵢ₊₁σᵢσᵢ₊₁ → σᵢσᵢ₊₁σᵢ, the reverse of the orientation stated in its docstring. The orientation is still admissible (the leading monomial dominates the tail); only the prose was wrong.

                        §4b Classification of the system's rules, and of their leading monomials #

                        Membership in heckeRewritingSystem is three list operations deep: a map for the quadratic family, and for each of the other two a flatMap over a filterMap whose if carries that family's side condition. Every proof that begins from r ∈ rules has to unpick that, and until now each one did it inline.

                        mem_heckeRewritingSystem_rules does it once, and inverts to the rule — r = heckeRelation i hp, not merely r.lm = [σᵢ, σᵢ]. The distinction is not cosmetic. OverlapComposition.spoly is computed from rule.relation and rule.lc, so an inversion that stops at the leading monomial cannot reach it; and the completed system carries two distinct rules on one leading monomial (HeckeCompletion §4j puts yangBaxterRelation i' i and bokutShiaoRelation i' i i' on the same word), so there the lm does not determine the rule at all. The leading-monomial classification is recovered below as a corollary.

                        theorem UGB.GrobnerShirshov.Hecke.gs_basis_of_empty_lm {G : Type u_1} [DecidableEq G] (ord : AdmissibleOrder G) (sys : RewritingSystem G ℝ) (r₀ : ReductionRule G ℝ) (hr₀ : r₀ ∈ sys.rules) (hlm : r₀.lm ord = []) (hlc : r₀.lc ord ≠ 0) :
                        IsGrobnerShirshovBasis G ℝ ord sys

                        A rule that is a non-zero scalar collapses the GS property.

                        If some rule's leading monomial is [] and its constant term is invertible — which is exactly what the removed sentinel ⟨ofTerms [([], q)], _⟩ was — then every element of the free algebra reduces to zero and IsGrobnerShirshovBasis holds for any generators, any order, any rules. The predicate then distinguishes nothing.

                        Two remarks on the statement, relative to the version proved against the unrepaired definitions:

                        • the hypothesis lc ≠ 0 is new and is needed. Previously SingleStepReduction imposed no algebraic condition, so a rule with empty leading monomial rewrote anything to anything; now a step must subtract an actual multiple of the relation, and a relation with vanishing constant term subtracts nothing. The removed sentinel satisfies it (q > 0).
                        • the support hypothesis one might expect — that the relation is supported on [] alone — is not needed: AdmissibleOrder.not_lt_nil shows the empty word is the minimum of every admissible order, so a relation whose leading monomial is [] has no other monomials at all.

                        §4e The Hecke relations are not a Gröbner–Shirshov basis #

                        hecke_is_gs_basis used to be an axiom here, citing Bokut & Shiao 2001, Theorem 3.5. With the definitions repaired the statement is not merely unproved — it is false, and the axiom has been removed rather than left in place. What follows is the machine-checked refutation at n = 4.

                        The obstruction is the overlap between the Yang–Baxter rule for (σ₂, σ₃), whose leading monomial is σ₃σ₂σ₃, and the far-commutation rule for (σ₃, σ₁), whose leading monomial is σ₃σ₁; they share the letter σ₃, over the ambient word σ₃σ₂σ₃σ₁. Its S-polynomial is

                        σ₂σ₃σ₂σ₁ − σ₃σ₂σ₁σ₃,

                        and both words are irreducible for the system: no σᵢσᵢ, no σᵢ₊₁σᵢσᵢ₊₁, and no σᵢσⱼ with i > j+1 occurs as a factor of either. So the S-polynomial is a non-zero normal form, the system is not confluent, and it is not a GS basis.

                        This is not a defect of the Hecke deformation: the same overlap obstructs the Coxeter presentation of S₄. Bokut & Shiao's theorem produces a GS basis by completing the presentation — the missing family is exactly σᵢσᵢ₋₁⋯σⱼσᵢ → σᵢ₋₁σᵢσᵢ₋₁⋯σⱼ, of which σ₃σ₂σ₁σ₃ → σ₂σ₃σ₂σ₁ is the first instance. heckeRewritingSystem contains only the defining relations, so the theorem does not apply to it. Completing the system is the work that bean qou-auq3 actually needs; see the bean for the analysis.

                                              Normal forms of a Hecke algebra element are unique — conditionally on the system being a GS basis, which is now an explicit hypothesis rather than the deleted axiom. NotAGSBasis.hecke_not_gs_basis_four shows the hypothesis is unsatisfiable at n = 4 for heckeRewritingSystem as it stands, so this statement is vacuous as it applies to this system.

                                              The Bokut–Shiao completion has since been added and proved: HeckeCompletion.heckeCompleted_normalForm_unique is this statement against heckeCompletedRewritingSystem, with the hypothesis discharged. Use that one; this stays because it is stated at the level the abstract framework works at, and because deleting it would erase the record of why the uncompleted system cannot support it.
