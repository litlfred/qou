    Flatness of the Gröbner–Shirshov Deformation #

    This module proves that a GS basis yields a flat deformation: the quotient algebra k⟨G⟩ / (Σ) has the same k-dimension as the associated graded algebra gr(k⟨G⟩ / (Σ)).

    In the QOU context, this means Dist(G) (non-commutative) is a flat deformation of Dist(H) (commutative): no dimensions are lost or gained when deforming the multiplication.

    Key results #

    • gs_implies_flat: a GS basis guarantees flatness of the associated filtered-to-graded deformation.
    • dist_flat_deformation: specialisation to Dist(G) over Dist(H).
    • basis_preserved: the normal-form basis of the deformed algebra projects bijectively onto the PBW basis of the associated graded.

    Why this matters for the QOU #

    The flatness result is what makes the lifting theorem work: when passing from Dist(H) (char p, commutative) to Dist(G) (char 0, non-commutative), the algebra does not "collapse" — the representation theory is preserved. In physical terms, the Hilbert space dimension is unchanged under quantisation.

    References #

    • [fred2005, §3, Theorem 3.15] — flatness of the GS deformation
    • Bergman 1978, §5 — filtered deformations and the diamond lemma
    • Beilinson–Ginzburg–Soergel, Koszul duality, JAMS 9 (1996) — PBW deformations and Koszulity

            source
            axiom UGB.GrobnerShirshov.gs_implies_flat {G : Type u} {k : Type v} [Field k] [DecidableEq G] (ord : AdmissibleOrder G) (sys : RewritingSystem G k) (hgs : IsGrobnerShirshovBasis G k ord sys) (h_deformed h_graded : HilbertFunction) :
            isFlat h_deformed h_graded

            GS basis implies flatness: if the defining relations of a filtered algebra form a GS basis (under a filtration-compatible admissible order), then the algebra is a flat deformation of its associated graded.

            Concretely: the normal-form monomials of degree ≤ n form a basis of Fₙ, and their images in gr_n form a basis of the n-th graded piece. Since the normal-form monomials are the same in both the deformed and undeformed settings, the Hilbert function is preserved.

            The proof uses the composition lemma: normal-form monomials are a basis (by composition_lemma). The same monomials, projected to the associated graded, are a basis of gr (since they are homogeneous by construction of the deg-lex order).

            Reference: [fred2005, Theorem 3.15], Bergman 1978 §5.

            §3 Flatness for Dist(G) over Dist(H) #

            source
            theorem UGB.GrobnerShirshov.dist_flat_deformation {G : Type u} {k : Type v} [Field k] [DecidableEq G] (ord : AdmissibleOrder G) (sys : RewritingSystem G k) (hgs : IsGrobnerShirshovBasis G k ord sys) (h_distG h_distH : HilbertFunction) :
            isFlat h_distG h_distH

            Flat deformation of distribution algebras ([fred2005, Theorem 3.15]):

            The distribution algebra Dist(G) of the non-commutative formal group G / ℤ_p is a flat deformation of Dist(H), the distribution algebra of the commutative base group H / 𝔽_p.

            This means:

            • Dist(G) and Dist(H) have the same k-dimension in each filtration degree.
            • The multiplication on Dist(G) reduces to that on Dist(H) modulo lower filtration degrees.
            • The GS normal forms for Dist(G) coincide with the PBW (divided-power) basis of Dist(H).

            This is the algebraic content of the statement that the integration of a skewed-Poisson structure produces a genuine deformation, not a degeneration.

            §4 Basis Preservation under Deformation #

            source
            axiom UGB.GrobnerShirshov.normal_forms_preserved {G : Type u} {k : Type v} [Ring k] [DecidableEq G] (ord : AdmissibleOrder G) (sys_deformed sys_graded : RewritingSystem G k) (h_same_lm : ∀ (i : ℕ) (hi_d : i < sys_deformed.rules.length) (hi_g : i < sys_graded.rules.length), leadingMonomial ord sys_deformed.rules[i].relation ⋯ = leadingMonomial ord sys_graded.rules[i].relation ⋯) (h_same_length : sys_deformed.rules.length = sys_graded.rules.length) (f : AlgElement G k) :
            isNormalForm ord sys_deformed f ↔ isNormalForm ord sys_graded f

            The set of normal-form monomials is unchanged between the deformed algebra and its associated graded.

            Axiomatised because the proof requires establishing an indexing correspondence between the rule lists of the two systems (matching List.mem witnesses to List.get indices), then applying h_same_lm to transfer the isInfix negation. This bookkeeping requires List.indexOf-based lemmas not available in Mathlib's List.isInfix API.

            The argument is straightforward: isNormalForm only depends on the leading monomials of the rules (via the isInfix check), and the hypothesis h_same_lm asserts these are identical. Hence the normal-form predicate is the same for both systems.

            Reference: Bergman 1978, §5 (filtered deformations preserve the leading-term ideal).
