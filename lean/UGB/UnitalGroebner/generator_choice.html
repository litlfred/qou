    UGB.UnitalGroebner.generator_choice #

    Real-content formalization (replaces the former vacuous class … where holds : Prop). The generator-choice lemma — "in the division algorithm we may take hᵢ = g_{γᵢ} for a basis member g_{γᵢ} ∈ G" — is stated as a concrete claim about single-step reduction: every reduction step f → g is witnessed by an actual rule r ∈ sys.rules (the chosen generator). The conclusion is a concrete existential over ReductionRule / sys.rules, not a bare Prop; and here it is in fact fully proved (no sorry).

    Content block lem:generator-choice-lemma: Generator choice in the division algorithm.

    Manuscript statement #

    Let $R$ be a commutative ring with unity, $A$ an $R$-algebra free as an $R$-module, $(B,<)$ an admissible system, and $I \trianglelefteq A$ generated by a unital Gröbner–Shirshov basis $G = \{g_\gamma \mid \gamma\in\Gamma\}$. Then in the division algorithm we may choose $h_i \in I$ with $h_i = g_{\gamma_i}$ for some $\gamma_i \in \Gamma$.

    What is weakened relative to the manuscript #

    The division algorithm is modelled by the SingleStepReduction relation of Basic.lean. The manuscript's "we may choose $h_i = g_{\gamma_i}$" is exactly the statement that each reduction step is realised by a rule of the system (a generator g_γ ∈ G), together with the subword-occurrence data. This is the SingleStepReduction.exists_witness field, so the statement is proved outright. Not formalized here: that the reductions terminate and that the cumulative h_i exhaust the ideal (those are the freeness/Buchberger theorems, formalized in unital_groebner_decomposition and buchberger).

    theorem UGB.UnitalGroebner.generator_choice {G : Type u} {k : Type v} [Ring k] [DecidableEq G] (ord : GrobnerShirshov.AdmissibleOrder G) (sys : GrobnerShirshov.RewritingSystem G k) (f g : GrobnerShirshov.AlgElement G k) (hstep : GrobnerShirshov.SingleStepReduction ord sys f g) :
    ∃ r ∈ sys.rules, ∃ m ∈ f.support, GrobnerShirshov.leadingMonomial ord r.relation ⋯ <:+: m

    Generator choice — single division step.

    Whenever the division algorithm performs a reduction step f → g (a SingleStepReduction), that step is realised by an actual rule r ∈ sys.rules — the chosen generator g_γ ∈ G — whose leading monomial occurs as a subword of some monomial in the support of f. This is precisely "we may take hᵢ = g_{γᵢ}".

    The conclusion is a concrete nested existential over ReductionRule/Monomial (membership in sys.rules, an isInfix occurrence), not a bare Prop. Proved directly from the reduction witness.
