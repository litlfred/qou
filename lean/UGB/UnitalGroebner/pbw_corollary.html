    UGB.UnitalGroebner.pbw_corollary #

    Real-content formalization (replaces the former vacuous class … where holds : Prop). The PBW corollary U𝔤 ≅ S𝔤 is stated as its Gröbner–Shirshov core: the canonical-form map U𝔤 → S𝔤 is class-injective (normal forms — the PBW monomials — are unique) and total (every element reduces to a PBW normal form). The conclusion is a concrete claim over AlgElement, not a bare Prop.

    Content block cor:pbw-corollary: Poincaré–Birkhoff–Witt theorem over commutative rings.

    Manuscript statement #

    For a Lie algebra $\mathfrak{g}$ over a commutative ring $R$ with unity, the universal enveloping algebra $U\mathfrak{g} = T\mathfrak{g}/J$ (with $J = (xy - yx - [x,y]_{\mathfrak g})$) is isomorphic as an $R$-module to the symmetric algebra $S\mathfrak{g}$. (When $R$ is a $\mathbb Q$-algebra this is the GS-basis route to the Lie-algebra / smooth-formal-group equivalence.)

    What is weakened relative to the manuscript #

    The manuscript asserts a full $R$-module isomorphism $U\mathfrak{g}\cong S\mathfrak{g}$. We state the PBW-basis core at the level of the canonical-form map: with the Serre/commutator relations of $U\mathfrak g$ presented as a GS basis sys, the normal-form monomials are the PBW monomials $S\mathfrak g$, every element reduces to one (ReducesToNF), and that normal form is unique (normal_form_unique). Together these say each U𝔤-class has a unique $S\mathfrak g$-representative — the bijective PBW correspondence. The R-linearity of the bijection is not formalized (raw AlgElement data type, AlgOps.lean §6). Both halves are now proved: existence by normal_form_exists (from termination plus the unit-leading-coefficient hypothesis hlc — bean qou-lzoo; the axiom it replaced was false), and uniqueness by normal_form_unique.

    theorem UGB.UnitalGroebner.pbw_corollary {G : Type u} {k : Type v} [Ring k] [DecidableEq G] (ord : GrobnerShirshov.AdmissibleOrder G) (sys : GrobnerShirshov.RewritingSystem G k) (hgs : GrobnerShirshov.IsGrobnerShirshovBasis G k ord sys) (hnqz : GrobnerShirshov.NoQuasiZerosFree G k) (hlc : ∀ r ∈ sys.rules, IsUnit (r.lc ord)) (f : GrobnerShirshov.AlgElement G k) :
    (∃ (nf : GrobnerShirshov.AlgElement G k), GrobnerShirshov.ReducesToNF ord sys f nf) ∧ ∀ (nf₁ nf₂ : GrobnerShirshov.AlgElement G k), GrobnerShirshov.ReducesToNF ord sys f nf₁ → GrobnerShirshov.ReducesToNF ord sys f nf₂ → nf₁ = nf₂

    PBW corollary — canonical-form core.

    Presenting U𝔤 by a Gröbner–Shirshov basis sys, every element reduces to a normal form (a PBW monomial / element of S𝔤), and any two normal forms of the same element coincide. This is the bijective PBW correspondence U𝔤 ↔ S𝔤 at the canonical-form level.

    The conclusion is a concrete conjunction over AlgElement — a ReducesToNF existential plus a ∀-uniqueness clause — the substantive content the former holds : Prop lacked.
