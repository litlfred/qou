    UGB.UnitalGroebner.unital_groebner_decomposition #

    Real-content formalization (replaces the former vacuous class … where holds : Prop). The decomposition A = I ⊕ Õ(G) is stated as a concrete claim about the reduction system: for a Gröbner–Shirshov basis, every algebra element f decomposes as a multi-step reduction f →* nf to a normal form nf (its Õ(G)-component), and the deformed and graded algebras share the same Hilbert function (flatness). Neither conjunct is instantiable with True.

    Content block thm:main-theorem: Free module decomposition via unital Gröbner–Shirshov bases.

    Manuscript statement #

    Let $R$ be a commutative ring with unity, $A$ an $R$-algebra free as an $R$-module without quasi-zeros, $(B,<)$ an admissible system, and $I \trianglelefteq A$ with a unital Gröbner–Shirshov basis $G$. With $\widetilde{O}(G)$ the free $R$-module on the non-leading monomials, (1) $A/I \cong \widetilde{O}(G)$ and (2) $A = I \oplus \widetilde{O}(G)$.

    What is weakened relative to the manuscript #

    The internal-direct-sum statement A = I ⊕ Õ(G) is rendered as its two algorithmic consequences over the concrete AlgElement / RewritingSystem types:

    • Õ(G)-component existence — every f reduces (MultiStepReduction) to a normal form, i.e. an element supported on non-leading monomials; f minus this normal form lies in the ideal I generated by the rules. This is the Õ(G) summand of the decomposition.
    • Flatness — isFlat h_deformed h_graded: the Hilbert function of the deformed algebra equals that of its associated graded, which is the dimension-count form of "no quasi-zeros / direct sum".

    The R-module structure of the summands and the explicit I-component are not formalized (raw AlgElement data type; see AlgOps.lean §6). The existence/termination half is proved (bean qou-lzoo): it is normal_form_exists, from reduction_well_founded plus the hypothesis hlc that every rule has a unit leading coefficient. It used to invoke an axiom of the same name that was not merely unproved but false — see NormalFormExistsRefutation. The flatness half is still supplied by the gs_implies_flat axiom of Flatness.lean.

    theorem UGB.UnitalGroebner.unital_groebner_decomposition {G : Type u} {k : Type v} [Field k] [DecidableEq G] (ord : GrobnerShirshov.AdmissibleOrder G) (sys : GrobnerShirshov.RewritingSystem G k) (hgs : GrobnerShirshov.IsGrobnerShirshovBasis G k ord sys) (hlc : ∀ r ∈ sys.rules, IsUnit (r.lc ord)) (h_deformed h_graded : GrobnerShirshov.HilbertFunction) :
    (∀ (f : GrobnerShirshov.AlgElement G k), ∃ (nf : GrobnerShirshov.AlgElement G k), GrobnerShirshov.ReducesToNF ord sys f nf) ∧ GrobnerShirshov.isFlat h_deformed h_graded

    Free-module decomposition — Õ(G)-component + flatness.

    For a Gröbner–Shirshov basis sys, every element f reduces to a normal form (the Õ(G)-summand of f in A = I ⊕ Õ(G)), and the deformed/graded Hilbert functions agree (isFlat).

    The conclusion is a concrete conjunction: a ReducesToNF existential over AlgElement together with an isFlat equality of Hilbert functions — substantive content the former holds : Prop lacked.
