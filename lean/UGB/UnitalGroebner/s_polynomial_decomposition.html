    Content block prop:s-polynomial-decomposition: $S$-polynomial decomposition of cancelling sums.

    Manuscript statement #

    Let $R$ be a commutative ring with unity, $A$ an $R$-algebra free as an $R$-module, $(B,<)$ an admissible system. Let $\alpha \in B$ and suppose $f_1,\dots,f_n \in A$ satisfy $\mathrm{LM}(f_i)=\alpha$ and $\mathrm{LC}(f_i)\in R^\times$. If $f := \sum_i c_i f_i$ (with $c_i\in R$) satisfies $\mathrm{LM}(f) < \alpha$, then $f = \sum_{i\neq j} d_{i,j}\,S_{i,j}$ where $S_{i,j} := a_i^{-1} f_i - a_j^{-1} f_j$, $a_i := \mathrm{LC}(f_i)$.

      S-polynomial decomposition — base (two-rule) case.

      The two rules f₁, f₂ share a leading monomial α with unit leading coefficients a₁, a₂; the combination c₁f₁ + c₂f₂ cancels at α (c₁a₁ + c₂a₂ = 0, i.e. its leading monomial drops strictly below α). Then the combination is a scalar multiple of the S-polynomial.

      The conclusion is a concrete AlgElement equation — the real content the former holds : Prop lacked. The expected witness is d = c₁·a₁. A complete proof needs coefficient extensionality for AlgElement (which is a raw data structure, not a quotient — see the AlgOps.lean §6 design note); supplying that ext principle is the remaining manuscript step.
