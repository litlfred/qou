    UGB.UnitalGroebner.lemma_generator_choice #

    Real-content formalization (replaces the former vacuous class … where holds : Prop). The generator-choice lemma in its multi-step form: any division-algorithm run f →* g either took no steps (f = g) or its final step is realised by an actual basis generator r ∈ sys.rules. The conclusion is a concrete disjunction / nested existential over the reduction relation, not a bare Prop, and it is fully proved by case analysis on MultiStepReduction.

    Content block lem:lemma-generator-choice: Generator choice in division.

    Manuscript statement #

    Let $R$ be a commutative ring with unity, $A$ an $R$-algebra free as an $R$-module, $(B,<)$ an admissible system, and $I \trianglelefteq A$ generated by a unital Gröbner–Shirshov basis $G=\{g_\gamma\mid\gamma\in\Gamma\}$. Then in the division algorithm we may choose $h_i\in I$ with $h_i = g_{\gamma_i}$ for some $\gamma_i\in\Gamma$.

    What is weakened relative to the manuscript #

    The division algorithm is the MultiStepReduction (reflexive–transitive closure of SingleStepReduction) of Basic.lean. We state the generator-choice property at the level of every reduction run: either it is the empty run or its terminal step picks a rule of the system (a generator g_γ ∈ G). This is the multi-step companion of generator_choice (the single-step version). The full statement that the chosen generators reconstruct the dividend (f = Σ hᵢ qᵢ + r) is the freeness/Buchberger content, formalized separately (unital_groebner_decomposition, buchberger). No sorry.

    theorem UGB.UnitalGroebner.lemma_generator_choice {G : Type u} {k : Type v} [Ring k] [DecidableEq G] (ord : GrobnerShirshov.AdmissibleOrder G) (sys : GrobnerShirshov.RewritingSystem G k) (f g : GrobnerShirshov.AlgElement G k) (hred : GrobnerShirshov.MultiStepReduction ord sys f g) :
    f = g ∨ ∃ (g' : GrobnerShirshov.AlgElement G k), GrobnerShirshov.MultiStepReduction ord sys f g' ∧ ∃ r ∈ sys.rules, ∃ m ∈ g'.support, GrobnerShirshov.leadingMonomial ord r.relation ⋯ <:+: m

    Generator choice — multi-step division run.

    For any division run f →* g (MultiStepReduction): either no step was taken (f = g), or there is an intermediate element g' with f →* g', a final single step g' → g, and that step is realised by a genuine basis generator r ∈ sys.rules. So every nontrivial run uses a member of G — the manuscript's "we may take hᵢ = g_{γᵢ}".

    The conclusion is a concrete disjunction over the reduction relation (an equality on AlgElement, or an existential producing r ∈ sys.rules and the witnessing subword occurrence), not a bare Prop. Proved by cases on the reduction derivation.
