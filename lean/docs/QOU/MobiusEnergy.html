    QOU.MobiusEnergy — Categorical Möbius Energy and Self-Avoidance #

    Formalization of the categorical Möbius energy functional and the non-self-intersection corollary from Chapter 4, §9 of the QOU manuscript.

    Structure #

    1. Categorical Möbius energy — the functional E_cat(γ) built from the Frobenius–Hopf comultiplication and q-Hodge star.
    2. Classical limit — in the limit q → 1, E_cat recovers the classical Möbius energy up to topological normalisation.
    3. Non-self-intersection — the non-cocommutativity of the Hopf structure at q ≠ 1 provides an energy barrier preventing self-intersection.

    References #

    • [freedman1994] M. Freedman, Z.-X. He, Z. Wang, "Möbius energy of knots and unknots", Annals of Mathematics 139 (1994), 1–50.
    • [ohara1991] J. O'Hara, "Energy of a knot", Topology 30 (1991), 241–247.

        structure QOU.CategoricalMobiusEnergy (M : Type u_1) :
        Type u_1

        Categorical Möbius energy data for a knot γ in a quantum observable universe (C, A, ω).

        The energy functional is $$E_{\mathrm{cat}}(\gamma) = \lambda \circ \mathrm{tr}_A \iint_{S^1 \times S^1 \setminus \mathrm{diag}} (\Delta(\gamma(s)) \otimes \Delta(\gamma(t))) \star_q \|d\gamma/ds \wedge_q d\gamma/dt\|\,ds\,dt$$

        where Δ is the Hopf comultiplication, λ is the Frobenius form, and ⋆_q is the q-Hodge star.

              §3 Möbius Energy Recovery (Proposition) #

              source
              theorem QOU.MobiusEnergy {M : Type u_1} (cme : CategoricalMobiusEnergy M) :
              cme.energy = 1 / 4 * cme.classicalEnergy.value + cme.classicalLimitConst

              Proposition: Categorical recovery of Möbius energy.

              In the classical limit q → 1, the categorical Möbius energy recovers the classical Möbius energy up to a topological normalisation: $$\lim_{q \to 1} E_{\mathrm{cat}}(\gamma) = \frac{1}{4} E(\gamma) + \mathrm{const}.$$

              The proof proceeds in three steps:

              1. Gauge fixing — the categorical volume constraint fixes G = SU(2), ensuring E_cat is Möbius-invariant.
              2. Frobenius–Hopf kernel expansion — at q = 1, the comultiplication becomes cocommutative and the Frobenius pairing reduces to the Killing form, yielding the 1/r² repulsive kernel.
              3. Self-energy subtraction — the diagonal regularisation matches the arc-length subtraction via the antipode S.

              LaTeX: \label{prop:mobius-energy}

                structure QOU.NonSelfIntersection (M : Type u_1) :
                Type u_1

                Corollary: Obstruction to self-intersection.

                For q ≠ 1, the non-cocommutativity of the Hopf structure provides a categorical obstruction to knot self-intersection: the energy diverges whenever two non-adjacent strands approach within distance ℏ_q.

                The mechanism:

                • The universal R-matrix R ∈ U_q(su(2)) ⊗̂ U_q(su(2)) controls the failure of cocommutativity: σ ∘ Δ ≠ Δ.
                • The q-deformed commutator [X̂, P̂]_q = iℏ_q replaces the 1/r² kernel with [2]_q / (r² − ℏ_q²).
                • This kernel has a hard singularity at r = ℏ_q, creating an energy barrier of infinite height — the "quantum exclusion zone".

                Physically, the vortex filament γ cannot pass through itself because doing so would require infinite categorical energy. This is the knot-theoretic analogue of the Pauli exclusion principle.

                LaTeX: \label{cor:non-self-intersection}

                • cme : CategoricalMobiusEnergy M

                  The categorical Möbius energy data.

                • exclusionRadius_pos : 0 < hbar_q self.cme.q

                  The exclusion radius is positive: 0 < ℏ_q.

                • energyDivergence (E₀ : ℝ) : ∃ ε > 0, ∀ (_s _t : ℝ), self.cme.energy > E₀

                  Energy divergence: when two non-adjacent strands approach within distance ℏ_q, the categorical energy diverges. Encoded as: for any ε > 0, there exists an energy bound E₀ such that if any two non-adjacent strand points are within exclusionRadius + ε, the energy exceeds E₀.
