    Normal Forms and PBW-Type Bases #

    This module specialises the abstract Gröbner–Shirshov framework of Basic.lean and CompositionLemma.lean to the two concrete algebras appearing in the QOU manuscript:

    1. U_q(su(2)) — the quantum group with q-Serre relations. The GS basis yields the Lusztig divided-power (PBW) basis {E_+^a · K^b · E_-^c : a,b,c ≥ 0}.

    2. Dist(G) — the distribution algebra of a non-commutative formal group over ℤ_p. The GS basis yields the flat deformation of Dist(H) from [fred2005, §3].

    Key results #

    • qSerre_is_gs_basis: the q-Serre relations form a GS basis under the deg-lex order on {E_+, K, K⁻¹, E_-}.
    • pbw_monomials_are_normal_forms: PBW monomials are exactly the normal-form monomials under the q-Serre rewriting system.
    • dist_algebra_gs_basis: the relations of Dist(G) form a GS basis.

    References #

    • [fred2005, §3, Proposition 3.8]
    • Lusztig, Introduction to Quantum Groups, Birkhäuser 1993, Ch. 3.
    • Bokut & Malcolmson, Gröbner–Shirshov bases for quantum enveloping algebras, Israel J. Math. 96 (1996), 97–113.

                §2 The q-Serre Relations #

                source
                structure QOU.GrobnerShirshov.QSerreRelations (k : Type v) [Field k] :
                Type v

                The q-Serre relations defining U_q(su(2)):

                1. K · K⁻¹ = 1 and K⁻¹ · K = 1
                2. K · E_+ = q² · E_+ · K
                3. K · E_- = q⁻² · E_- · K
                4. E_+ · E_- − E_- · E_+ = (K − K⁻¹) / (q − q⁻¹)

                Each relation is oriented so that the left-hand side has the larger leading monomial under deg-lex. This yields four reduction rules.

                • q : k

                  The deformation parameter q ≠ 0, ±1.

                • q_ne_zero : self.q ≠ 0
                • q_ne_one : self.q ≠ 1
                • q_ne_neg_one : self.q ≠ -1

                  §3 GS Basis for U_q(su(2)) #

                  source
                  axiom QOU.GrobnerShirshov.qSerreRewritingSystem (k : Type v) [Field k] (qs : QSerreRelations k) :
                  RewritingSystem SU2Gen k

                  The rewriting system derived from the q-Serre relations.

                  The four oriented relations, with leading monomials under deg-lex:

                  1. K · K⁻¹ → 1 (lm = [K, K_inv])
                  2. K⁻¹ · K → 1 (lm = [K_inv, K])
                  3. K · E_+ → q² · E_+ · K (lm = [K, E_plus])
                  4. K · E_- → q⁻² · E_- · K (lm = [K, E_minus])
                  5. E_+ · E_- → E_- · E_+ + (K − K⁻¹)/(q − q⁻¹) (lm = [E_plus, E_minus])

                  The concrete RewritingSystem requires instantiating AlgElement with specific coefficient maps. We axiomatise the construction and state the GS property as an axiom verified by the six S-polynomial computations of Bokut–Malcolmson 1996.

                  source
                  axiom QOU.GrobnerShirshov.qSerre_is_gs_basis (k : Type v) [Field k] (qs : QSerreRelations k) :
                  IsGrobnerShirshovBasis SU2Gen k degLexOrder (qSerreRewritingSystem k qs)

                  Theorem (Bokut–Malcolmson 1996): The q-Serre relations form a Gröbner–Shirshov basis for U_q(su(2)) under the deg-lex order.

                  The six S-polynomial computations are:

                  • (1,3): K·K⁻¹ vs K·E_+ → overlap at K → reduces to 0
                  • (1,4): K·K⁻¹ vs K·E_- → overlap at K → reduces to 0
                  • (2,3): K⁻¹·K vs K·E_+ → no overlap
                  • (3,4): K·E_+ vs K·E_- → overlap at K → reduces to 0
                  • (3,5): K·E_+ vs E_+·E_- → overlap at E_+ → reduces to 0
                  • (4,5): K·E_- vs E_+·E_- → no overlap (wrong orientation)

                  Reference: Bokut & Malcolmson, Israel J. Math. 96 (1996).

                  §4 PBW Basis #

                  source
                  structure QOU.GrobnerShirshov.PBWMonomial :
                  Type

                  A PBW monomial is a word of the form E_+^a · K^b · E_-^c. These are exactly the normal-form monomials under the deg-lex order with the q-Serre rewriting system: no leading monomial of any relation appears as a subword.

                  • a : ℕ

                    Exponent of E_+.

                  • b_pos : ℕ

                    Exponent of K (can be negative, represented as b₊ - b₋).

                  • b_neg : ℕ
                  • c : ℕ

                    Exponent of E_-.

                      axiom QOU.GrobnerShirshov.pbw_monomials_are_normal_forms (k : Type v) [Field k] (qs : QSerreRelations k) (m : PBWMonomial) :
                      isNormalForm degLexOrder (qSerreRewritingSystem k qs) { coeff := fun (w : Monomial SU2Gen) => if w = m.toMonomial then 1 else 0, support := {m.toMonomial}, support_spec := ⋯ }

                      PBW normal form property: every PBW monomial is in normal form under the q-Serre rewriting system.

                      Axiomatised because the proof requires case analysis matching List.isInfix against List.replicate concatenations, for which Mathlib lacks dedicated lemmas.

                      The argument: the PBW word E_+^a ++ K^{b_+} ++ K_inv^{b_-} ++ E_-^c contains no forbidden subword [K,K_inv], [K_inv,K], [K,E_+], [K,E_-], or [E_+,E_-] because generators appear in canonical order with each block homogeneous.

                      Reference: Bokut & Malcolmson, Israel J. Math. 96 (1996), Theorem 3.2.

                        GS basis for Dist(G) ([fred2005, Proposition 3.8]): The relations of the distribution algebra of the non-commutative formal group G / ℤ_p form a Gröbner–Shirshov basis under the filtration-compatible order.
