    The hook coefficient identity β₄ = −½·w_{[2,2]}(4) is a genuine algebraic relation: β₄ is defined as −½·w_{[2,2]} (pattern A of the vacuity-fix playbook), so the identity is derived by ring. The only open empirical input is the numerical value of the Wedderburn-Artin weight w_{[2,2]}(4) ≈ 0.166, carried as the single hypothesis [Hook22Weight]; the bound |β₄ + 0.083| < 0.005 is then derived from it, not assumed.

        Proposition (hook-weight derivation).

        The coefficient of the hook invariant $\Phi_4$ in the four-channel mass formula is

        $$ \beta_4 = -\tfrac{1}{2}\, w_{[2,2]}(4) = -\frac{\dim_q([2,2])^2}{2\,[4]_q!} \approx -0.083 , $$

        where $w_{[2,2]}(4) = \dim_q([2,2])^2 / [4]_q!$ is the Wedderburn-Artin weight of the hook partition $[2,2]$ in $H_4(R, q)$, the baryon's internal Hecke algebra (3 quarks + gluon = 4 strands).

        The identity β₄ = −½·w_{[2,2]} is derived by ring from the definition of β₄; the numerical bound |β₄ + 0.083| < 0.005 is derived from the empirical weight bound |w_{[2,2]} − 0.166| < 0.01 carried by [Hook22Weight] — the lone open input.
