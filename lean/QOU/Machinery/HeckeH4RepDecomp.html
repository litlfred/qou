    The five H₄(q) Wedderburn blocks as a BilinearTrace representation family #

    BilinearTraceRepBasis.gram_symm_of_rep_decomp_of_basis turns a basis-level trace decomposition into Gram symmetry, hence (via cyclic_of_gram_symm) into cyclicity of the Markov trace. To apply it at H₄ one needs the five Wedderburn blocks packaged as a single family

    ρ : ∀ l, (Fin 24 → K) → Matrix (dim l) (dim l) K

    that is multiplicative on mulNF₄ and linear in the coordinate vector.

    Both come for free from wedderburnHom₄, and that is the point of this file. It is an AlgHom out of H4A K (q - q⁻¹), whose Add/SMul/Mul instances are coordinatewise on the .v field and whose components are exactly chiNF₄ q, chiNF₄ (-q⁻¹), blockOf22 q, blockOf31 q, blockOf211 q by rfl (wedderburnHom₄_apply). So map_mul / map_add / map_smul discharge every structural obligation with no fresh computation on the 24-word normal form.

    This route also sidesteps a real obstacle: the per-block linearity lemmas blockOf22_add, blockOf22_smul (and their 31 / 211 counterparts) are private, hence unusable from any other file. Re-deriving them here would mean editing the block-map modules — measured here at 941 s / 5.5 GB and 916 s / 6.5 GB, so the expensive thing to touch.

    What is NOT here #

    The 24 scalar identities trM4 z (q - q⁻¹) (e i) = ∑_λ w_λ · tr (ρ_λ (e i)) at the closed-form Markov weights. That is the arithmetic content of the decomposition and it is genuinely open in Lean; it is carried below as an explicit hypothesis (hbasis) rather than assumed, so the conditional result is honest about what it rests on.

    This is not the route that retired the ASCII chain. QOU/Machinery/ H4Cyclicity.lean proved trM4_cyclic from the generator actions, the chain has since been deleted, and its cone is under 10 minutes. What this file offers is a second, structurally different route to the same Gram symmetry — via the Wedderburn blocks rather than the generators — which is worth having only on its own merits, not because anything depends on it.

    Base ring (§7c): generic [Field K] — inherited from wedderburnHom₄, which needs inverses for -q⁻¹. No ordering and no archimedean construct.

    -- Ref: [wenzl1988] https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01239526 -- Ref: [manuscript] bean qou-x64l, step 4.

            The conditional cyclicity statement #

            Everything structural is discharged above. What remains is arithmetic: the 24 scalar identities pinning TRM4 against the block traces at the Markov weights. Those are carried as hbasis — an explicit hypothesis, not an assumption dressed up as a proof — so the statement below is a genuine conditional result and names exactly what qou-x64l step 4 still owes.

            source
            theorem QOU.Machinery.H4.trM4_gram_symm_of_markov_basis {K : Type u_1} [Field K] (q : K) (hq : q ≠ 0) (h2 : q ^ 2 + 1 ≠ 0) (h3 : q ^ 4 + q ^ 2 + 1 ≠ 0) (z : K) (w : Block → K) (hbasis : ∀ (i : Fin 24), trM4 z (q - q⁻¹) (BilinearTrace.e i) = ∑ l : Block, w l * (rho q l (BilinearTrace.e i)).trace) (i j : Fin 24) :
            trM4 z (q - q⁻¹) (mulNF₄ (q - q⁻¹) (BilinearTrace.e i) (BilinearTrace.e j)) = trM4 z (q - q⁻¹) (mulNF₄ (q - q⁻¹) (BilinearTrace.e j) (BilinearTrace.e i))

            Gram symmetry of trM4 from the Wedderburn blocks, conditional on the basis-level Markov decomposition.

            Given the 24 scalar identities hbasis, the level-4 Markov trace is Gram-symmetric on the subscript normal-form basis — with no reference to the ASCII presentation and no 576-entry closure.
