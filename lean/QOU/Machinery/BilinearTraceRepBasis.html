    Representation decomposition from the basis alone #

    BilinearTraceCyclicity.gram_symm_of_rep_decomp derives Gram symmetry — hence, via cyclic_of_gram_symm, full cyclicity — from a decomposition

    τ x = ∑_λ w_λ · tr (ρ_λ x)

    required for every x. That hypothesis is stronger than the mathematics needs, and the gap is exactly what makes the bridge awkward to use at a concrete level.

    Both sides of that identity are linear in x. So it is determined by its values on the ι-indexed basis e i = Pi.single i 1, and a concrete level should only ever have to discharge #ι scalar equations rather than a statement quantified over the whole module. At H₄(q) that is the difference between 24 equations in the coordinate vector's entries and one identity in a free Fin 24 → K.

    rep_decomp_of_basis closes that gap: given additivity and homogeneity of τ and of each ρ_λ, the basis-level decomposition upgrades to the ∀ x form. gram_symm_of_rep_decomp_of_basis then chains it into the existing bridge, so the concrete obligation becomes:

    • the ρ_λ are multiplicative on the bilinear product (at H₄ this is map_mul for the Wedderburn AlgHom, whose components are the block maps by rfl), linear (likewise map_add / map_smul), and
    • #ι scalar identities τ (e i) = ∑_λ w_λ · tr (ρ_λ (e i)).

    Nothing here is specific to H₄; it serves H₃, H₅, and any level whose Markov trace is a coefficient vector against a basis.

    Base ring (§7c): generic [CommRing R]. No archimedean construct, no ordering, no ℝ.

    A linear map's trace-decomposition is determined on the basis.

    If τ and every ρ l are additive and homogeneous, then the decomposition τ x = ∑_{l ∈ s} w l · tr (ρ l x) holding on the basis vectors e i forces it for every x.

    This is the rung that lets a concrete level discharge #ι scalar equations in place of a statement quantified over ι → R.
