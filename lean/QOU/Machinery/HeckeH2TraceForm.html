    QOU.Machinery.HeckeH2TraceForm — the regular trace of H₂(q) #

    The trace capstone for the rank-two Hecke algebra H2A R h (HeckeH2AlgebraInstance). Two statements, both sorry-free over a generic [CommRing R] (§7c — nothing archimedean is used):

    • regTraceForm_apply — the regular trace (trace of left multiplication, i.e. Mathlib's Algebra.trace) is the explicit linear form $$\mathrm{tr}_{\mathrm{reg}}(a\,\gamma + b\,\sigma) = 2a + h\,b.$$ The 2 is $\dim_R H_2 = 2$ (the trace of the identity); the h is the trace of the generator $\sigma$ (its regular matrix $\bigl(\begin{smallmatrix}0&1\\1&h\end{smallmatrix}\bigr)$).

    • regTrace_eq_sum_characters — the Wedderburn reading: for the two distinct roots $l_+ , l_-$ of the Hecke quadratic $l^2 = h\,l + 1$, the regular trace is the sum of the two irreducible characters character l± · of HeckeH2Wedderburn, $$\mathrm{tr}_{\mathrm{reg}} = \chi_{l_+} + \chi_{l_-},$$ because the regular representation $H_2 \cong R \times R$ decomposes as the direct sum of the two one-dimensional blocks. The only ring fact used is Vieta's $l_+ + l_- = h$, forced by the two root equations and $l_+ \neq l_-$.

    This is the trace-level companion to HeckeH2Wedderburn.wedderburn: that file split the algebra; this one reads its regular trace off the same two characters. Higher ranks stay open (bean qou-vndn).

    -- Ref: [wenzl1988] https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01404457
