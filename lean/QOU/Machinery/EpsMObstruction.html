    The Frobenius shift law #

    source
    theorem QOU.Machinery.EpsMObstruction.frobenius_shift {G : Type u_1} [CommGroup G] (ζ : G) (e : ℕ) :
    (ζ ^ e) ^ 25 = (ζ ^ 24) ^ e * ζ ^ e

    The shift law. With σ acting by g ↦ g ^ 25 (the Galois generator φ₅² on roots of unity at the prime 5), an element written as ζ ^ e is moved by exactly (ζ ^ 24) ^ e:

    σ(ζ ^ e) = (ζ ^ 24) ^ e * (ζ ^ e).

    Generic CommGroup; no arithmetic hypotheses are needed, the identity is pure exponent bookkeeping.

    theorem QOU.Machinery.EpsMObstruction.orderOf_shift_factor {G : Type u_1} [CommGroup G] (ζ : G) (m : ℕ) (hord : orderOf ζ = 24 * m) :
    orderOf (ζ ^ 24) = m

    The shift factor ζ ^ 24 has order exactly m when ζ has order 24 * m — so the shift really does land in μ_m, which is what makes the shift exponent well defined mod m. (No m ≠ 0 hypothesis is needed: the infinite-order case m = 0 holds too.)

    The obstruction form #

    source
    def QOU.Machinery.EpsMObstruction.qSymbol (ew e1w : ZMod 24) :
    ZMod 24

    The obstruction contribution of one Bloch symbol [w]: the product of the Teichmüller discrete logarithms of w and of 1 - w, taken in ZMod 24 (the base Teichmüller group at the prime 5 is μ₂₄).

      theorem QOU.Machinery.EpsMObstruction.reflection_even (a b : ZMod 24) :
      qSymbol a b + qSymbol b a = 2 * (a * b)

      The reflection relation is even. [x] + [1 - x] contributes 2 * (a * b), where a = e(x) and b = e(1 - x): the two symbols carry the same pair of logarithms in opposite order.

      theorem QOU.Machinery.EpsMObstruction.inversion_closed_form (a b einv e1inv : ZMod 24) (hinv : einv = -a) (h1inv : e1inv = 12 + b - a) :
      qSymbol a b + qSymbol einv e1inv = a ^ 2 - 12 * a

      The inversion relation's closed form. With e(1/x) = -a and e(1 - 1/x) = 12 + b - a (the second from 1 - 1/x = -(1-x)/x together with e(-1) = 12), the pair [x] + [1/x] contributes exactly a ^ 2 - 12 * a.

      theorem QOU.Machinery.EpsMObstruction.inversion_even_iff (a : ZMod 24) :
      (∃ (k : ZMod 24), a ^ 2 - 12 * a = 2 * k) ↔ ∃ (j : ZMod 24), a = 2 * j

      The inversion contribution is even exactly when a is. This is why the parity of the obstruction form is not preserved by the derived inversion relation, and hence why the parity statement is confined to the defining five-term relation: inversion holds in the Bloch group only modulo torsion, and ZMod 24 sees precisely that torsion.

      theorem QOU.Machinery.EpsMObstruction.clearable_of_even (q : ZMod 24) (hq : ∃ (j : ZMod 24), q = 2 * j) :
      ∃ (s : ZMod 24), (∃ (k : ZMod 24), s = 2 * k) ∧ q + s = 0

      Clearability. An even obstruction can always be cancelled by an even shift — the last step of the representative-change argument, which uses only shifts realised by the defining five-term relation (all of which are even).
