    QOU.Machinery.HeckeH3GramNondegeneracy — Gram nondegeneracy of the H₃(q) Markov trace #

    A3-s1 Schur-trace bridge at rank 3 (bean qou-c7e3, approach qou-ulrx, milestone qou-lv5t). The Gram matrix of the Markov-trace pairing $\langle x, y\rangle = \mathrm{tr}_M(x\cdot y)$ on the GB normal-form basis $[\gamma, \sigma_0, \sigma_1, L_+, L_-, e^-]$ of $H_3(q)$, its determinant in closed form, and the concrete nondegeneracy statement.

    Provenance (sympy, verified before formalization) #

    The 6×6 entry table and the factored determinant were derived symbolically in computations/h3_gram/hecke_h3_markov_gram_derive.py and pinned in computations/h3_gram/hecke-h3-markov-gram.data.json; every closed form below was cross-checked numerically (e.g. at z = 3, h = 2 both sides of gramMatrix_det equal -736) before the Lean proofs were attempted. Nothing is trusted from the computation — gramMatrix_eq re-derives every entry from the trM/mulNF substrate of MarkovTraceOnHecke.lean inside Lean.

    The determinant and its degeneracy locus #

    $$ \det G \;=\; -\bigl(z^2 - hz - 1\bigr)^5\, \bigl((h^2+4)z^2 - (h^3+4h)z - 1\bigr). $$

    At the Hecke specialization $h = q - q^{-1}$ the two factors split:

    • $z^2 - hz - 1 = (z - q)(z + q^{-1})$ — the quintic factor vanishes exactly at the character weights $z \in \{q, -q^{-1}\}$ (the two 1-dimensional Wedderburn blocks of $H_3(q)$ swallowing the trace);
    • $(h^2+4)z^2 - (h^3+4h)z - 1 = \bigl((q^2+1)z - q^3\bigr)\bigl((q^3+q)z + 1\bigr)/q^3$ — the quadratic factor vanishes at the two $(2,1)$-block Markov-weight zeros $z = q^3/(q^2+1)$ and $z = -1/(q(q^2+1))$.

    Away from these four points the trace pairing is nondegenerate: exists_pairing_ne_zero produces, for every nonzero y, a witness w with $\mathrm{tr}_M(y\cdot w) \neq 0$. This realizes the trace-nondegeneracy hypothesis of QOU.Machinery.HeckeTower.WenzlTowerStep (semisimple_ascend) concretely at rank 3 — the hypothesis is no longer an abstract input but a proved fact of the explicit H₃ Markov trace on its degeneracy-free locus.

    Base ring (§7c): generic [CommRing R] throughout; [Field K] only where the inverse Gram matrix is needed (exists_pairing_ne_zero, gramMatrix_det_ne_zero). No archimedean construct.

      theorem QOU.Machinery.H3.exists_pairing_ne_zero {K : Type u_2} [Field K] (z h : K) (hdet : (gramMatrix z h).det ≠ 0) (y : Fin 6 → K) (hy : y ≠ 0) :
      ∃ (w : Fin 6 → K), trM z h (mulNF h y w) ≠ 0

      Trace nondegeneracy from the determinant — the Wenzl-hypothesis realization at rank 3: whenever det G ≠ 0, every nonzero y ∈ H₃(q) pairs nontrivially with some w under the Markov trace. This exact shape is the semisimple_ascend trace-nondegeneracy hypothesis of QOU.Machinery.HeckeTower.WenzlTowerStep, here discharged concretely for the explicit H₃ trace. The witness is a basis vector: pick j with (y ᵥ* G) j ≠ 0 (possible since G is invertible) and take w = Pi.single j 1.

      theorem QOU.Machinery.H3.gramMatrix_det_ne_zero {K : Type u_2} [Field K] (q z : K) (hq : q ≠ 0) (h1 : z ≠ q) (h2 : z ≠ -q⁻¹) (h3 : (q ^ 2 + 1) * z ≠ q ^ 3) (h4 : (q ^ 3 + q) * z ≠ -1) :
      (gramMatrix z (q - q⁻¹)).det ≠ 0

      Nondegeneracy at the Hecke specialization h = q - q⁻¹: the Gram determinant is nonzero away from the four degeneracy points.

      • Quintic factor, via z² - hz - 1 = (z - q)(z + q⁻¹): the eigenvalue channels z = q and z = -q⁻¹. Each is a corank-5 collapse — the Gram drops to rank 1 there, not to rank 5.

      • Quadratic factor, via q³·((h²+4)z² - (h³+4h)z - 1) = ((q²+1)z - q³)((q³+q)z + 1): the Markov-weight zeros z = q³/(q²+1) and z = -1/(q(q²+1)). Each is a corank-1 collapse, and the block that drops out is one-dimensional in both cases: the sign representation (1³) at z = q³/(q²+1), whose radical is the q-antisymmetriser (-q³, q², q², -q, -q, 1), and the trivial representation (3) at z = -1/(q(q²+1)), whose radical is the q-symmetriser.
