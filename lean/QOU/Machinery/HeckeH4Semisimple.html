    QOU.Machinery.HeckeH4Semisimple — Prop-level semisimplicity + trace nondegeneracy of H₄(q) #

    T2-b capstone (beans qou-308a / qou-5lbe; route fixed by [docs/audits/2026-07-19-t2b-rank34-ascent-routes.md §2]): the two honest reachable rank-4 statements, both det-free — the 24×24 Gram determinant is sympy-verified but Lean-out-of-reach (det_fin_24 would carry 24! terms), so nothing here touches it.

    • (i) isSemisimpleRing_H4A — transfer of IsSemisimpleRing along the explicit rank-4 Wedderburn isomorphism wedderburn₄ of HeckeH4Wedderburn, with the matrix factors supplied by QOU.MathlibExt.MatrixSemisimple — the rank-4 semisimplicity statement the cited WenzlCriterion / SchurElementCriterion classes carry, now proved rather than cited at this rank (mirrors HeckeH3Semisimple).
    • (ii) exists_pairing_ne_zero₄ — the trace-nondegeneracy half of the Wenzl hypothesis pair at the 3→4 rung: whenever the five per-block Markov weights are nonzero, every nonzero y ∈ H₄(q) pairs nontrivially with some w under the level-4 Markov trace trM4. Proved from the block decomposition trM4_eq_blocks (the Wedderburn expansion $\mathrm{tr}_M = \sum_\lambda y_\lambda \chi_\lambda$) plus a block-projection witness — w is the wedderburnInvV₄ preimage of the dual elementary matrix in a block where y's image is nonzero.

    The five Markov weights are sympy-solved from the 24-equation linear system TRM4 = Σ_λ y_λ · (block-trace rows of A) (unique solution, verified exactly), and trM4_eq_blocks re-verifies them in Lean by linear_combination — nothing is trusted from the computation. Their numerators factor into the Jones degeneracy linear forms in z (see each weight's docstring); the five nonvanishing hypotheses of (ii) are exactly the rank-4 analogue of the factor-by-factor hypotheses of HeckeH3GramNondegeneracy.gramMatrix_det_ne_zero.

    HONESTY (STRICT, §"Axiomatization is NOT a Proof"): every lemma is discharged by computation — no axiom, no sorry, no conditional class, no native_decide.

    Base ring (§7c): generic [Field K]; no archimedean construct.
