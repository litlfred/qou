    QOU.Machinery.HeckeH4WedderburnCoord — the 24×24 coordinate matrix of the rank-4 Wedderburn map #

    Wedderburn tranche W3 (part 1 of 3) (bean qou-308a; design provenance: the H₄ Wedderburn design doc §4, session scratchpad h4_wedderburn_iso/design.md): the matrix-literal layer of the inverse side of $H_4(q) \cong K \times K \times M_2(K) \times M_3(K) \times M_3(K)$.

    • coordM — the 24×24 coordinate matrix $A$ of the assembled map $(\chi_+, \chi_-, \rho_{22}, \rho_{31}, \rho_{211})$ on the subscript NF basis (rows chi_plus, chi_minus, rho22_{00..11}, rho31_{00..22}, rho211_{00..22}, columns the 24 basis words), authored per-row (coordMRow0 … coordMRow23) in atom-separated form — only q⁻¹, (q ^ 2 + 1)⁻¹, (q ^ 4 + q ^ 2 + 1)⁻¹, (q ^ 4 + 1)⁻¹ powers;
    • coordMInv — its symbolic inverse $A^{-1}$ (sympy, 24×24 inversion over $\mathbb{Q}(q)$, from computations/h4_wedderburn/hecke-h4-wedderburn-seminormal.data.json). Nothing is trusted from the computation — the product identities are proved entry-by-entry in HeckeH4WedderburnMul*.lean;
    • wvec / homVec — the stacked component vector and the stacked coordinates of the assembled map;
    • wedderburnInvV₄ — the inverse coordinate map, defined as the matrix action (coordMInv q).mulVec (wvec …);
    • homVec_eq_mulVec — the glue identifying the assembled map's stacked coordinates with (coordM q).mulVec v (24 coordinate goals, each closed by ring after unfolding — both sides are the same $A$-row applied to v).

    HONESTY (STRICT, §"Axiomatization is NOT a Proof"): every lemma is discharged by computation — no axiom, no sorry, no conditional class, no native_decide; the sympy cofactors are payloads, re-verified by Lean's linear_combination kernel check.

    Base ring (§7c): generic [Field K] — inverses are genuinely used; no archimedean construct.

    -- Ref: [wenzl1988] https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01239526 -- Ref: [jones1987] https://doi.org/10.2307/1971403
