    QOU.Machinery.H4 — the H₄(q) Markov trace on the Fin-24 GB normal-form module #

    Extends the H3 block of MarkovTraceOnHecke.lean (Fin 6) to H₄(q) (Fin 24 = |S₄|), the base case for the general-n Markov stabilization (τ-functor open item A, docs/audits/2026-07-18-tau-functor-construction-reduction.md).

    All data (trace values TRM, generator right-actions mulσ0/1/2, the reduced words behind mulNF) is generated and cross-validated by computations/markov/hecke_regular_nf_table.py (Hecke + braid + commutation relations, trace cyclicity, and the Markov reduction — all machine-checked; reproduces the hardcoded H3 block exactly). Convention: T_{s_i}² = 1 + h·T_{s_i} (h = q − q⁻¹), Ocneanu/Markov trace tr(1)=1, tr(x·T_{top}) = z·tr(x).

    Proof status #

    • trM_add, trM_smul — linearity (M1), sorry-free.

    • trM_markov_σ2 — the Markov reduction (M3) tr(x·σ₂)=z·tr(x) for x ∈ H₃ (parabolic NF indices {0,2,3,7,8,13}), sorry-free. This is the base case for the general-n Markov stabilization (τ-functor open item A).

    • trM_cyclic — cyclicity (M2): sorry-free, now in QOU.Machinery.H4BasisBridge. It used to be proved in QOU.Machinery.MarkovTraceH4Gram from gram_symm over the 576-entry MarkovTraceH4Gram0..7 closure; that whole chain (6604 generated lines, the tree's build pole) was deleted once QOU.Machinery.H4.trM4_cyclic (H4Cyclicity) proved cyclicity directly on the subscript basis from the three generators. trM_cyclic is now a three-rewrite corollary of it across the basis relabelling, under the same fully-qualified name. Verified: #print axioms QOU.Machinery.H4.trM_cyclic → [propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound], no sorryAx.

      trM_cyclic_of_gram_symm below is kept: it is the sorry-free reduction "Gram symmetry ⇒ cyclicity", still true and still the Fin 24 instance of BilinearTraceCyclicity.cyclic_of_gram_symm. What went away is its former input, the 576 computed entries — not the reduction itself.

                Bilinearity machinery for the cyclicity reduction #

                The Fin-24-free half of trace cyclicity. Each generator right-action mulσk is R-linear (depth-1, proved by fin_cases over the 24 entries); mulNF is then R-bilinear with the mulσk kept opaque — only their linearity lemmas are used, so the depth-6 whnf wall is never touched. This lifts to the Gram expansion tr_M(u·v) = ∑_{i,j} u_i v_j · G_{i,j} (G_{i,j} = tr_M(b_i·b_j)), from which cyclicity reduces to symmetry of G (trM_cyclic_of_gram_symm).
