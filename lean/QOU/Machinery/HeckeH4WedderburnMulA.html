    QOU.Machinery.HeckeH4WedderburnMulA — entry-by-entry verification of $A\cdot A^{-1} = 1$ (rows 0–7) #

    Wedderburn tranche W3 (part 2 of 3) (bean qou-308a): rows 0–7 of the product identity coordM q * coordMInv q = 1 — one public row lemma per row, 24 scalar goals each, every goal closed by linear_combination against mul_inv_cancel₀ with sympy-derived, exactly-verified cofactors (the matinv_right payloads of the design doc §4).

    HONESTY (STRICT, §"Axiomatization is NOT a Proof"): every lemma is discharged by computation — no axiom, no sorry, no conditional class, no native_decide; the sympy cofactors are payloads, re-verified by Lean's linear_combination kernel check.

    Base ring (§7c): generic [Field K] — inverses are genuinely used; no archimedean construct.
