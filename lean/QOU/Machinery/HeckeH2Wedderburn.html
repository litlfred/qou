    QOU.Machinery.HeckeH2Wedderburn — explicit Wedderburn decomposition of H₂(q) #

    Wenzl-bridge stage 2b (bean qou-101j, epic qou-otbb): the first proved semisimplicity content behind the cited criterion classes (QOU.Information.PNeqNpEquivalence.WenzlCriterion, QOU.MathlibExt.WenzlSchurElement.SchurElementCriterion), at rank $n = 2$, on the concrete Hecke algebra H2A of HeckeH2AlgebraInstance.

    Given the two roots $\ell_\pm$ of the Hecke quadratic $x^2 = h\,x + 1$ (over $\mathbb{R}$: $\ell_\pm = (h \pm \sqrt{h^2+4})/2$, i.e. $q$ and $-q^{-1}$) with invertible difference, the two characters $a + b\,\sigma \mapsto a + \ell_\pm b$ assemble into an explicit algebra isomorphism

    $$ H_2(q) \;\cong\; R \times R $$

    — the rank-2 Wedderburn decomposition, whose two one-dimensional blocks are the partitions $(2)$ and $(1,1)$. Everything here is sorry-free over a generic commutative ring per §7c; the archimedean instantiation (real roots via Real.sqrt, unconditional semisimplicity) lives in HeckeH2WedderburnReal.
