    QOU.Machinery.HeckeH4BlockMap31 — the $(3,1)$ coordinate block map of $H_4(q)$ #

    Wedderburn tranche W2b (bean qou-308a; design provenance: the H₄ Wedderburn design doc §1/§3, session scratchpad h4_wedderburn_iso/design.md): the $(3,1)$ block map of the rank-4 Wedderburn picture $H_4(q) \cong K \times K \times M_2(K) \times M_3(K) \times M_3(K)$, on the subscript 24-word NF basis of HeckeH4StabilizationRung (H₃ Lean basis as prefix, then (length, canonical word) order).

    blockOf31 is defined by its explicit entries — the rho31_* rows of the verified 24×24 coordinate matrix $A$ of computations/h4_wedderburn/hecke-h4-wedderburn-seminormal.data.json (sympy-exact over $\mathbb{Q}(q)$; row/entry provenance re-verified against the seminormal word products in the session cofactor derivation) — rather than as a $v$-weighted sum of seminormal word products. At rank 4 the longest basis word is $\sigma_0\sigma_1\sigma_2\sigma_0\sigma_1\sigma_0$; unfolding 6-fold matrix products inside every lemma invites elaboration blowups, while the explicit-entry definition keeps every per-entry goal a small scalar identity. The word-product decomposition is recovered as a theorem inside blockOf31_mulNF₄ (expansion + intertwiners + unit — nothing is trusted from the symbolic computation).

    Proved here (sorry-free):

    • blockOf31_unit — the unit $\gamma$ maps to $\mathbb{1}$ (column 0 of $A$);
    • the three right-action intertwiners blockOf31_mulσ₀/₁/₂ — blockOf31 q (u·σₖ) = blockOf31 q u * Sₖ against the proved seminormal matrices S0_31/S1_31/S2_31 of HeckeH4BlockRep (27 scalar goals, each closed by linear_combination against mul_inv_cancel₀ with sympy-derived, exactly-verified cofactors — the SET-E payloads of the design doc);
    • blockOf31_mulNF₄ — multiplicativity on the full NF product, by the structured route (right-basis expansion + intertwiner peeling — no 6-fold word product is ever elaborated);
    • H4A.blockRep31 — the induced algebra homomorphism H4A K (q - q⁻¹) →ₐ[K] M_3(K).

    HONESTY (STRICT, §"Axiomatization is NOT a Proof"): every lemma below is discharged by computation — no axiom, no conditional class, no native_decide; the sympy cofactors are payloads, re-verified by Lean's linear_combination kernel check. Nothing wired; 1/(8π) stays phase-4.

    Base ring (§7c): generic [Field K] — inverses are genuinely used; no archimedean construct. Genericity hypotheses per theorem, read off the cofactor atoms: q ≠ 0, q ^ 2 + 1 ≠ 0, q ^ 4 + q ^ 2 + 1 ≠ 0.
