    The 72 generator identities #

    tr(b_g · b_j) = tr(b_j · b_g) for each word j and generator g.

    Diagonal entries are absent by design (removed 2026-08-21; vacuity ledger, proof-no-trivial-true / reflexive-tautology). At j = g the cyclicity instance degenerates to tr(b_g · b_g) = tr(b_g · b_g) — the two sides are syntactically identical, so trM4_cyc_1_1, trM4_cyc_2_2 and trM4_cyc_6_6 were X = X := rfl, asserting nothing falsifiable while reading, from their names, like three more entries of a proved table. The traceCentral_e₄_* case splits that consumed them now discharge those branches with rfl inline, where the triviality is visible at the point of use. The table's real content is the off-diagonal cases.
