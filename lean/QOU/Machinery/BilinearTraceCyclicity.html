    Abstract bilinear-trace cyclicity: cyclic ⟺ Gram-symmetric #

    The Fin-index-agnostic core of trace cyclicity on a finite free module.

    Every concrete Markov-trace level (H₃ on Fin 6, H₄ on Fin 24, …) is a linear functional τ on ι → R together with a bilinear "product" B. The whnf-free half of tr_M(u·v) = tr_M(v·u) — the reduction to the finite Gram base — is entirely formal (bilinear expansion + a Finset.sum_comm swap) and does not depend on the level. This file isolates it once, over an arbitrary Fintype index:

    cyclicity for all u, v ⟺ symmetry of the Gram matrix G i j = τ (B (e i) (e j)).

    QOU.Machinery.H4.trM_cyclic_of_gram_symm (PR #4046) is the Fin 24 instance of cyclic_of_gram_symm below; the same lemma serves H₃, H₅, and any future level. The residual — Gram symmetry itself — is what a per-level computation or the abstract Ocneanu-induction / character-decomposition argument supplies; this file does not close it, it reduces to it (honestly).

      Character route to Gram symmetry (bricks toward the all-n closure) #

      The whnf-free reduction above leaves the finite Gram symmetry itself. The light, level-agnostic way to discharge it: a Markov/Ocneanu trace on a semisimple algebra is a nonnegative combination of irreducible characters tr_M = ∑_λ w_λ · χ_λ, and every character χ_λ = trace ∘ ρ_λ is cyclic for free via Matrix.trace_mul_comm. These bricks build that argument abstractly; the remaining keystone is the concrete decomposition (the ρ_λ, e.g. Hoefsmit / seminormal reps of H_n(q), and the weights w_λ), which supplies the ∑.

        Bridge — Gram symmetry from a representation decomposition #

        The keystone brick that connects the character route back to the concrete whnf-free reduction. It needs no ring/associativity structure on the NF module (so the Fin 24 associativity whnf wall is avoided): only that the trace τ decomposes as a finite weighted sum of representation-traces, each representation being multiplicative on the bilinear product B. Then the finite Gram symmetry — the residual left by cyclic_of_gram_symm — holds. What remains for a concrete level is exactly the content: the representations ρ_λ (e.g. Hoefsmit / seminormal at H_n(q)) and the Markov weights w_λ.
