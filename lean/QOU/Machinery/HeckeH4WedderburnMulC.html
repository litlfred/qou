    QOU.Machinery.HeckeH4WedderburnMulC — entry-by-entry verification of $A\cdot A^{-1} = 1$ (rows 16–23) #

    Wedderburn tranche W3 (part 3 of 3) (bean qou-308a): rows 16–23 of the product identity coordM q * coordMInv q = 1 — one public row lemma per row, 24 scalar goals each, every goal closed by linear_combination against mul_inv_cancel₀ with sympy-derived, exactly-verified cofactors (the matinv_right payloads of the design doc §4).

    This part also assembles the two matrix identities: coordM_mul_coordMInv : A·A⁻¹ = 1 from the 24 row lemmas, and coordMInv_mul_coordM : A⁻¹·A = 1 by Matrix.mul_eq_one_comm (square matrices over a commutative ring — one verified side suffices for both, cheaper than a second 576-goal sweep and equally explicit).

    HONESTY (STRICT, §"Axiomatization is NOT a Proof"): every lemma is discharged by computation — no axiom, no sorry, no conditional class, no native_decide; the sympy cofactors are payloads, re-verified by Lean's linear_combination kernel check.

    Base ring (§7c): generic [Field K] — inverses are genuinely used; no archimedean construct.

    -- Ref: [wenzl1988] https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01239526 -- Ref: [jones1987] https://doi.org/10.2307/1971403
