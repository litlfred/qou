    QOU.Machinery.QuantumTorusSymbol — principal symbol calculus (QDM-1 / QDM-2) #

    Bricks QDM-1 and QDM-2 of the q-D-module symbol-calculus epic (qou-dap4). This is the algebraic, verifiable-here core, built directly on the existing QOU.Machinery.QuantumTorus q-Weyl ring (NT-T2). The deep geometric application (QDM-3/QDM-4 — a characteristic-variety / holonomicity statement) is out of scope: see docs/audits/2026-07-20-qdm-symbol-calculus.md.

    The order filtration and the principal symbol #

    The quantum torus T = A[L^±; σ] (SkewMonoidAlgebra A (Multiplicative ℤ)) carries the order filtration by top L-degree topDeg : T → WithBot ℤ (defined in QuantumTorus.lean). The principal symbol of P is its leading L-monomial:

    symbol P = L^(leadDeg P) · leadCoeff P = monomial (leadDeg P) (leadCoeff P),

    where leadDeg P = (topDeg P).unbotD 0 ∈ ℤ (junk 0 for P = 0, which is harmless because leadCoeff 0 = 0) and leadCoeff P = coeffAt P (leadDeg P).

    What is proved here (sorry-free) #

    • QDM-1 — symbol, leadDeg, leadCoeff and their values on the generators: symbol_monomial, symbol_emb, symbol_Lgen, topDeg_emb, topDeg_Lgen, plus leadCoeff_ne_zero (a nonzero element has a nonzero leading coefficient).
    • QDM-2 — the leading-coefficient product law coeffAt_mul_add : coeffAt (P·Q) (m+n) = coeffAt P m · (σᵐ (coeffAt Q n)) (proved over any CommRing, no domain hypothesis — the twist σᵐ survives here as the graded structure constant), the vanishing lemma coeffAt_mul_eq_zero_of_lt, and hence, over a domain, topDeg_mul : topDeg (P·Q) = topDeg P + topDeg Q and symbol_mul : symbol (P·Q) = symbol P · symbol Q.

    The associated graded — what commutes in gr vs the ambient twist #

    The quantum torus is already ℤ-graded by L-degree, so its associated graded ring gr T for the order filtration is T itself. The symbol map is therefore multiplicative into gr T = T with the twisted product law L^m a · L^n b = L^{m+n}(a · σᵐ b) (monomial_mul_monomial). In particular the classical-Weyl "symbols commute" phenomenon does not hold here: the commutator [L, M] = (σ − id)(M)·L = (q²−1) M L has the same order as ML (the q-Weyl twist is a same-order deformation, not a lower-order one), so the principal symbol of a commutator is not forced to vanish. What symbol_mul records is the honest statement — the leading term of a product is the twisted product of the leading terms — and the twist σ^{leadDeg P} is exactly the graded structure constant that survives into gr.

    Base ring (§7c) #

    Generic {A : Type*} [CommRing A] [MulSemiringAction Ldeg A] throughout, plus [IsDomain A] for the two multiplicativity results that genuinely need the leading coefficient to stay nonzero. No ℝ, no ordering, no archimedean construct: the whole file is generic-ring algebra and lean-direct-verifies against the package oleans.

          QDM-2 leading-coefficient product law (holds over any CommRing). The coefficient of P·Q at the sum of top degrees is the twisted product of the leading coefficients: coeffAt (P·Q) (m+n) = coeffAt P m · σᵐ(coeffAt Q n). The twist σᵐ = ofAdd m • · is the graded structure constant that survives into the associated graded ring.

          theorem QOU.Machinery.QuantumTorus.symbol_mul {A : Type u_1} [CommRing A] [MulSemiringAction Ldeg A] [IsDomain A] {P Q : QuantumTorus A} (hP : P ≠ 0) (hQ : Q ≠ 0) :

          QDM-2 principal symbol is multiplicative (over a domain). symbol (P·Q) = symbol P · symbol Q. This is the associated-graded ring-hom property of the principal-symbol map — the leading term of a product is the (twisted) product of the leading terms. The [IsDomain A] hypothesis is exactly what keeps the leading coefficient nonzero (so the top degree is leadDeg P + leadDeg Q, not lower).
