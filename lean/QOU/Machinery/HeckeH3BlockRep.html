    QOU.Machinery.HeckeH3BlockRep — the (2,1) two-dimensional block of H₃(q) #

    Wenzl-bridge stage 2c-2 (bean qou-vndn, epic qou-otbb): the two-dimensional Hoefsmit seminormal representation of H3A, completing the block data of the rank-3 Wedderburn picture $H_3(q) \cong K \times K \times M_2(K)$ (the two characters are HeckeH3Characters; this file supplies the $M_2$ block).

    Convention: base field $K$, $q \neq 0$, Hecke parameter $h = q - q^{-1}$ (eigenvalues $q$ and $-q^{-1}$). The genericity denominator is $[2]_q$-type: $q^2 + 1 \neq 0$ (equivalently the two eigenvalues are distinct, $q \neq -q^{-1}$). The seminormal matrices

    $$ S_0 = \begin{pmatrix} q & 0 \\ 0 & -q^{-1} \end{pmatrix}, \qquad S_1 = \begin{pmatrix} -\dfrac{1}{q(q^2+1)} & 1 \\[2mm] \dfrac{q^4+q^2+1}{(q^2+1)^2} & \dfrac{q^3}{q^2+1} \end{pmatrix} $$

    satisfy the Hecke quadratic and the braid relation (proved below as matrix identities), and the induced map on NF coordinates is an algebra homomorphism blockRep : H3A K (q - q⁻¹) →ₐ[K] M₂(K) — multiplicativity by the same structured route as the characters (right-action intertwiners + right-linearity of mulNF).

    Over $\mathbb{R}$ the hypotheses hold for every $q \neq 0$ ($q^2 + 1 > 0$), so the real block is unconditional — same pattern as HeckeH2WedderburnReal. What remains for the full rank-3 Wedderburn isomorphism is bijectivity of the assembled map $(\chi_+, \chi_-, \rho)$ — the six-dimensional linear inversion — tracked in bean qou-vndn.

    Base ring (§7c): generic [Field K] — inverses are genuinely used; no archimedean construct.

    -- Ref: [wenzl1988] https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01404457 -- Ref: [jones1987] https://doi.org/10.2307/1971403
