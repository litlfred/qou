    QOU.Machinery.HeckeH2WedderburnReal — H₂(q) over ℝ is semisimple, unconditionally #

    Archimedean instantiation of HeckeH2Wedderburn (§7c split: this file owns the Real.sqrt construction). The Hecke quadratic $x^2 = h\,x + 1$ has discriminant $h^2 + 4 > 0$ for every real $h$, so the two roots $(h \pm \sqrt{h^2+4})/2$ (i.e. $q$ and $-q^{-1}$) are always real and distinct:

    • wedderburnReal — the explicit decomposition $H_2(q) \cong \mathbb{R} \times \mathbb{R}$ for every real $h$;
    • isSemisimpleRing_H2A_real — H₂(q) over ℝ is a semisimple ring for every $h$, sorry-free.

    This is the rank-2 slice of Wenzl semisimplicity [wenzl1988] with an actual proof — the first inhabited rung behind the cited hypothesis classes (WenzlCriterion, SchurElementCriterion), and stronger than the genericity-conditional statement at this rank: the real substrate line never meets the rank-2 degeneracy locus at all (the first degeneration $[2]_q = 0$ requires complex $q$, a root of unity). Higher ranks ($n \geq 3$, where hook lengths $\geq 2$ bring genuine root-of-unity loci) remain the open stage-2 frontier (bean qou-vndn).

    -- Ref: [wenzl1988] https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01404457
