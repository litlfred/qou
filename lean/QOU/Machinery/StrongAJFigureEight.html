    Principal generation from division + minimality (PROVED — the Euclidean core). Let I be a left ideal of the quantum torus containing a. If right-division by a always leaves a strictly-smaller-topDeg remainder, and a is the minimal-degree nonzero element of I (every element of I of smaller topDeg vanishes), then I is the principal left ideal span {a}.

    This is the genuine mathematical content of "the recurrence ideal is principal": any P ∈ I reduces mod a to a remainder r ∈ I of smaller degree, which minimality kills, so P = q·a. It derives the conclusion by composing the two hypotheses — it does not assume it.
