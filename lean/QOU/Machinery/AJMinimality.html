    QOU.Machinery.AJMinimality — the honest formalizable fragment of NT-T4 minimality #

    Bean qou-jshd (TL-minimality). The minimality field of QOU.Machinery.StrongAJFigureEight.TransferLemmas asks that the order-3 AJ operator Â_hom be the minimal-order nonzero annihilator of the 4₁ colored-Jones tower, so that principal_of_division_minimality makes the recurrence ideal principal (I = span {Â_hom}). The Nguyen–Tran figure-eight paper obtains this by CITING Tran's transfer machinery. This file records what is honestly formalizable now, and — in the companion audit docs/audits/2026-07-20-tl-minimality-wall.md — what is a genuine wall.

    What [Tr4] = arXiv:1404.0331 Lemmas 3.1–3.3 actually prove (honest reading) #

    Tran, "The strong AJ conjecture for cables of torus knots". §3 (case s odd, q > 2):

    • Lemma 3.1 — for the torus knot T(p,q): (L^{2m} − t^{-4pqm²}M^{-2pqm}) J_T ∈ R[M^{±1}] for all m > 0. A power-of-the-base annihilation: powers of the order-1 base recurrence still send J_T into the coefficient ring.
    • Proposition 3.2 — builds explicit σ-invariant operators P₁, P₂ with Pᵢ Gᵢ ∈ R[M^{±1}] for the cable pieces G₁, G₂.
    • Assembled with Lemma 2.3 (an existence-of-annihilator construction) into an element R ∈ A^σ_C with ε(R) = M^{k}L^{l}(A_C)^{2k}, which feeds Lemma 2.2 (∃ P ∈ A^σ with ε(P) = M^kL^l A^{2m} ⟹ p ⊂ √ε(A^σ)).

    Two honest facts follow, both material to this bean:

    1. [Tr4] L3.1–3.3 are an annihilator construction (existence), NOT a minimality theorem. They exhibit a specific (high-degree, non-minimal) σ-invariant element to feed the radical-inclusion Lemma 2.2. Nowhere is any operator shown to be minimal-degree. Tran's whole strong-AJ route works at the level of radicals of σ-invariant ideals (√ε(A^σ_K) = p); it never proves the recurrence ideal is principal. Principality/minimality is the content of the plain AJ conjecture (Garoufalidis's recurrence polynomial = minimal-order generator), which Tran assumes as a hypothesis (Lemma 2.1: "Suppose the AJ conjecture holds for K").
    2. [Tr4] §3 is about cables of torus knots, not 4₁. The figure eight is hyperbolic — neither a torus knot nor a cable of one — so these lemmas do not literally apply to it. The figure-eight paper reuses the method (build a σ-invariant annihilator, feed Lemma 2.2), not these statements.

    So TransferLemmas.minimality is not discharged by [Tr4] L3.1–3.3. The minimal-order-generator fact is the plain-AJ input, and its Lean-honest status is a wall (needs the character-variety A-polynomial / holonomic-rank machinery, absent from Mathlib 4.24 + corpus). See the audit doc for the wall analysis.

    What IS formalizable now (sorry-free, this file) #

    Minimality of the order-3 operator among homogeneous annihilators is NoLowOrderHomogAnnihilator below: no nonzero order-≤2 homogeneous operator annihilates the tower. Two genuine ingredients of it are corpus-provable and proved here (they are NECESSARY, not sufficient — full minimality remains the wall):

    • Order-exactness (hc3_ne_zero). The homogenised operator ajHomElement genuinely has order 3: its L³ coefficient hc3 = −feRhs·feCoeff₂ is nonzero at every colour where feRhs and the top figure-eight coefficient feCoeff₂ are nonzero (the generic-q condition). So Â_hom is not a disguised lower-order operator — topDeg Â_hom = 3, the reference degree minimality is stated against.
    • Order-2 obstruction (figureEight_not_homogAnnihilator). The order-2 figure-eight coefficient triple (feCoeff₀, feCoeff₁, feCoeff₂) is not a homogeneous annihilator: it sends the tower to feRhs ≠ 0, not 0. This rules out exactly the naive order-2 candidate — the concrete reason the homogenisation to order 3 is forced.

    The remaining NoLowOrderHomogAnnihilator (rule out all order-≤2 triples) is the wall. No TransferLemmas instance is provided and minimality is not axiomatised — per the anti-laundering rule this is the honest "open" state.
