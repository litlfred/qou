    QOU.Machinery.HeckeH3Characters — the two 1-dim Wedderburn blocks of H₃(q) #

    Wenzl-bridge stage 2c-1 (bean qou-vndn, epic qou-otbb): the character route of HeckeH2Wedderburn generalised to the six-dimensional H3A of HeckeH3AlgebraInstance.

    At each root $\ell$ of the Hecke quadratic $x^2 = h\,x + 1$ (over $\mathbb{R}$: $q$ and $-q^{-1}$), sending both generators $\sigma_0, \sigma_1 \mapsto \ell$ extends to a ring homomorphism $H_3(q) \to R$. On the NF basis $[\gamma, \sigma_0, \sigma_1, L_+, L_-, e^-]$ (words of lengths $0,1,1,2,2,3$) the character is

    $$ \chi_\ell(v) = v_0 + \ell,(v_1 + v_2) + \ell^2 (v_3 + v_4)

    • \ell^3 v_5 . $$

    These are the two one-dimensional Wedderburn blocks of $H_3(q) \cong R \times R \times M_2(R)$ — the partitions $(3)$ and $(1,1,1)$. Everything here is sorry-free over a generic [CommRing R] per §7c, including the stabilization statement: the H₃ characters restrict along the tower inclusion iotaAlg to the H₂ characters of HeckeH2Wedderburn.

    What stays open at this rank (bean qou-vndn): the two-dimensional block — the Hoefsmit seminormal representation $H_3(q) \to M_2(R)$ for the partition $(2,1)$ (it needs the invertible content denominator $[2]_q = \ell_+ - \ell_-$) — and the assembled six-dimensional Wedderburn isomorphism $H_3(q) \cong R \times R \times M_2(R)$.
