    Module.End R (W → R)

    The braiding morphism σ as an honest R-linear endomorphism. The Iwahori–Hecke length-rule action mulStep D h (proved additive and R-homogeneous) packaged as an element of the endomorphism ring Module.End R (W → R). This is the object the HeckeBraidedPresentation class lives on: composition in Module.End is the ring multiplication in which the F2 relations below are genuine ring identities.
