    QOU.Machinery.HeckeH3Wedderburn — the full rank-3 Wedderburn isomorphism #

    Wenzl-bridge stage 2c-3 (bean qou-vndn, epic qou-otbb): the assembled explicit isomorphism

    $$ H_3(q) \;\cong\; K \times K \times M_2(K) $$

    from the two characters (HeckeH3Characters) and the $(2,1)$ block (HeckeH3BlockRep). The inverse was computed symbolically (sympy, 6×6 inversion over $\mathbb{Q}(q)$) and is verified here by Lean — nothing is trusted from the computation; the determinant of the coordinate map is

    $$ \det = \frac{(q^2+1)\,(q^2-q+1)^3\,(q^2+q+1)^3}{q^7}, $$

    so the degeneracy locus is exactly the Wenzl genericity condition at rank 3: the hypotheses are $q \neq 0$, $q^2 + 1 \neq 0$ ($[2]$-type) and $q^4 + q^2 + 1 = (q^2+q+1)(q^2-q+1) \neq 0$ ($[3]$-type). Over $\mathbb{R}$ all three hold for every $q \notin \{0, \pm 1\}$… indeed $q^2+1 > 0$ and $q^4+q^2+1 > 0$ always, so only $q \neq 0$ remains.

    Semisimplicity note (honest): Mathlib currently lists IsSemisimpleRing (Matrix ι ι R) as proof_wanted (RingTheory/SimpleModule/Basic.lean), so the IsSemisimpleRing Prop for $H_3(q)$ cannot yet be obtained by transfer. The explicit isomorphism below is the Wedderburn decomposition — strictly more informative — and the Prop-level corollary lands the day the Mathlib gap closes.

    Base ring (§7c): generic [Field K]; no archimedean construct.
