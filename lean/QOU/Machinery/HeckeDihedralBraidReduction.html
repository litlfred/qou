    structure QOU.Machinery.HeckeGeneral.DihedralCosetStructure (W : Type u_3) :
    Type (max u_3 (u_4 + 1))

    Dihedral (order-3 pair) coset structure: the parabolic factorization datum for an adjacent generator pair. W factors as C × Fin 6 (coset space × the S₃ cell in the merged NF order [γ, σ₀, σ₁, L₊, L₋, e⁻]), lengths are additive along the cell, and the two step maps act cell-wise by the proved n = 3 tables (descentH3σ₀.t, descentH3σ₁.t). Every Coxeter system carries one for each adjacent pair by the standard parabolic coset decomposition — that fact is the isolated open combinatorial input.

    • C : Type u_4

      The coset space (minimal-coset-representative labels).

    • equiv : W ≃ self.C × Fin 6

      The parabolic factorization W ≃ C × (S₃ cell).

    • len : W → ℕ

      The global length.

    • len0 : self.C → ℕ

      The length of the minimal coset representative.

    • ti : W → W

      Step map of the first generator.

    • tj : W → W

      Step map of the second generator.

    • len_add (w : W) : self.len w = self.len0 (self.equiv w).1 + descentH3σ₀.len (self.equiv w).2

      Length additivity along the cell: ℓ(u₀·d) = ℓ(u₀) + ℓ_D(d).

    • ti_eq (w : W) : self.equiv (self.ti w) = ((self.equiv w).1, descentH3σ₀.t (self.equiv w).2)

      The first generator acts cell-wise by the proved σ₀ table.

    • tj_eq (w : W) : self.equiv (self.tj w) = ((self.equiv w).1, descentH3σ₁.t (self.equiv w).2)

      The second generator acts cell-wise by the proved σ₁ table.

              star (mulStep D h v u) = mulStep D (star h) (fun (x : W) => star (v x)) u

              Conjugation intertwines the generator action with h ↦ star h: star ∘ σ(h) = σ(star h) ∘ star, entrywise, at every rank. The descent test is real (a length comparison), so conjugation passes straight through the length rule.

              ρ-equivariance of the e₊ channel (B1 dictionary, module level): conjugation carries the e₊ channel vector at q to the e₊ channel vector at star q of the conjugated state — at every rank.
