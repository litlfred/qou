    QOU.Machinery.HeckeH4Wedderburn — the full rank-4 Wedderburn isomorphism #

    Wedderburn tranche W4 (bean qou-308a; design provenance: the H₄ Wedderburn design doc §4, session scratchpad h4_wedderburn_iso/design.md): the assembled explicit isomorphism

    $$ H_4(q) \;\cong\; K \times K \times M_2(K) \times M_3(K) \times M_3(K) $$

    from the two characters (HeckeH4Characters), the three block representations (HeckeH4BlockMap22/31/211), and the Lean-verified inverse coordinate map (HeckeH4WedderburnCoord → HeckeH4WedderburnMul* → HeckeH4WedderburnInv), mirroring the merged rank-3 pattern HeckeH3Wedderburn.wedderburn₃.

    The inverse was computed symbolically (sympy, 24×24 inversion over $\mathbb{Q}(q)$) and is verified by Lean — nothing is trusted from the computation; the determinant of the coordinate map is

    $$ \det A = -\frac{(q^2+1)^{24}\,(q^4+1)^{10}\,(q^2-q+1)^3\,(q^2+q+1)^3} {q^{50}}, $$

    so the degeneracy locus is exactly the Wenzl genericity condition at rank 4: the hypotheses are q ≠ 0, q ^ 2 + 1 ≠ 0 ($[2]$-type), q ^ 4 + q ^ 2 + 1 = (q^2+q+1)(q^2-q+1) ≠ 0 ($[3]$-type) and the new $[4]$-type condition q ^ 4 + 1 ≠ 0 absent at rank 3. Over $\mathbb{R}$ all four hold for every q ≠ 0 — the denominators are strictly positive — so only q ≠ 0 remains.

    Semisimplicity note: the Prop-level corollary IsSemisimpleRing (H4A K (q - q⁻¹)) is derived in HeckeH4Semisimple by transfer along this isomorphism, with the matrix factors supplied by QOU.MathlibExt.MatrixSemisimple. The explicit isomorphism below is the stronger statement.

    HONESTY (STRICT, §"Axiomatization is NOT a Proof"): every declaration below is discharged by computation — no axiom, no sorry, no conditional class, no native_decide.

    Base ring (§7c): generic [Field K]; no archimedean construct.
