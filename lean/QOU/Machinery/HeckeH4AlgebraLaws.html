            The structured associativity route #

            Per the design (§2): (1) the three generator/product commutation identities mulσₖ (u·v) = u·(v·σₖ) — each a Fin 24 computation whose left side only ever applies one generator table on top of the mulNF₄ expansion (the sparse trick that makes the MarkovTraceH4 Gram closure feasible); (2) the word induction over the 24 basis composites; (3) mulNF₄_assoc by right-linearity. No 24³ brute force is ever elaborated.
