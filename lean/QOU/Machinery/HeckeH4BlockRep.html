    QOU.Machinery.HeckeH4BlockRep — the three matrix blocks of H₄(q) #

    Wenzl-bridge A2-s2 (bean qou-xzaq, approach qou-pk7l, milestone qou-lv5t): the Hoefsmit seminormal matrix data for the three higher-dimensional Wedderburn blocks of the rank-4 Iwahori–Hecke algebra $H_4(q)$ — partitions $(2,2)$ (2-dimensional), $(3,1)$ (3-dimensional) and $(2,1,1)$ (3-dimensional). Together with the two characters $\chi_+ : \sigma_i \mapsto q$ (partition $(4)$) and $\chi_- : \sigma_i \mapsto -q^{-1}$ (partition $(1^4)$) these are the block data of $H_4(q) \cong K \times K \times M_2 \times M_3 \times M_3$.

    Convention: Hecke quadratic $S^2 = (q - q^{-1})\,S + 1$, eigenvalues $q$ and $-q^{-1}$; each $2\times 2$ mixing block is b-normalized ($b$-entry $= 1$, $\det = -1$, trace $= q - q^{-1}$), the HeckeH3BlockRep.S1 pattern. Two mixing blocks occur:

    • the axial-distance $-2$ block — identical to QOU.Machinery.H3.S1 (HeckeH3BlockRep), it is the $\sigma_1$-edge mixing block of all three partitions here; genericity denominator $q^2 + 1$;
    • the axial-distance $-3$ block — the $\sigma_2$-edge of $(3,1)$ and $(2,1,1)$, with $a = -1/(q(q^4+q^2+1))$, $c = (q^2+1)^2(q^4+1)/(q^4+q^2+1)^2$, $d = q^5/(q^4+q^2+1)$; this is where the $[4]$-type factor $q^4 + 1$ enters the rank-4 picture (matching its appearance in the determinant of the 24×24 coordinate matrix).

    For each block we prove, as sorry-free matrix identities: the Hecke quadratic for $S_0, S_1, S_2$, both adjacent braid relations $S_0 S_1 S_0 = S_1 S_0 S_1$ and $S_1 S_2 S_1 = S_2 S_1 S_2$, and the far commutation $S_0 S_2 = S_2 S_0$.

    Data provenance: derived and verified symbolically (sympy, exact over $\mathbb{Q}(q)$) in computations/h4_wedderburn/ (derive.py → hecke-h4-wedderburn-seminormal.data.json; human summary docs/audits/2026-07-19-h4-wedderburn-seminormal-data.md). Every matrix entry below is transcribed from that data file.

    Base ring (§7c): generic [Field R] — inverses are genuinely used; no archimedean construct. Genericity hypotheses as needed per theorem: q ≠ 0, q ^ 2 + 1 ≠ 0, q ^ 4 + q ^ 2 + 1 ≠ 0.
