    theorem QOU.Machinery.CoxeterParabolic.monodromyN_diagram_equivariant {B : Type u_1} {W : Type u_2} [Group W] {M : CoxeterMatrix B} (cs : CoxeterSystem M W) (e : B ≃ B) (he : ∀ (a b : B), M.M (e a) (e b) = M.M a b) (l : B) (v : W → ℂ) (u : W) :
    HeckeGeneral.monodromyN (descentRight cs (e l)) v ((diagramHom cs e he) u) = HeckeGeneral.monodromyN (descentRight cs l) (fun (x : W) => v ((diagramHom cs e he) x)) u

    Monodromy is diagram-equivariant: the q = i Jordan nilpotent at the image generator pulls back along a diagram symmetry to the nilpotent at the source generator. Conjugation edges are NOT covered — inner automorphisms need not preserve length; that case is open.
