    structure QOU.Machinery.KLRQuiver :
    Type (u_1 + 1)

    A quiver Γ for the KLR algebra: a type of vertices I together with an adjacency map adj : I → I → ℕ (multiplicity of edges). For the standard simply-laced case all multiplicities are 0 or 1.

    • I : Type u_1

      The vertex type.

    • adj : self.I → self.I → ℕ

      Adjacency multiplicity.

          Conditional class for the U⁻_q categorification #

          The deep claim: a KLR algebra R(Γ) categorifies the negative half of the quantum group U_q(g) associated to Γ. This is the Khovanov-Lauda-Rouquier theorem (2008-2010); not currently in Mathlib.

          Captured here as a conditional class.
