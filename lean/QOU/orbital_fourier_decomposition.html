      Proposition. Let $\varrho: S^1 \hookrightarrow \Sigma_4$ be the trefoil $T_{2,3}$ embedded in the Bring's surface, and let $N = N_{\varrho/\Sigma_4}$ be the normal bundle. The four quantum numbers $(n, \ell, m, s)$ arise from the Fourier decomposition of sections $\sigma \in \Gamma(N)$ as follows.

      The normal bundle.

      The trefoil $\varrho(S^1)$ is a smooth closed curve in the $3$-dimensional Bring's surface $\Sigma_4$ (viewed locally as a real $3$-manifold via the spatial realization functor). The normal bundle $N$ is a rank-$2$ real vector bundle over $S^1$:

      $$ N_p = T_p \Sigma_4 / T_p \varrho \qquad\text{for } p \in \varrho(S^1). $$

      At each point of the trefoil, $N_p \cong \mathbb{R}^2$ — two independent transverse directions. A section $\sigma \in \Gamma(N)$ is a smooth choice of transverse displacement at each point of the trefoil.
