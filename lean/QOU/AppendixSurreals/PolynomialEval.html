    QOU.AppendixSurreals.PolynomialEval — surreal evaluation of real polynomials #

    This module provides the polynomial-evaluation infrastructure that turns real polynomials into surreal-valued functions:

    polySurrealEval p x := p.eval₂ SurrealField.ofReal x

    with two key compatibility lemmas:

    • polySurrealEval_ofReal — agreement with classical evaluation on the real subfield (so the surreal lift is conservative).
    • polySurrealEval_isInfinitesimal_diff — the surreal lift sends infinitesimal perturbations of finite arguments to infinitesimal perturbations of values.

    The second lemma is the automatic-witness lemma for ClassicalLimitIsStandardPart: it shows that for any polynomial f : ℝ[X], the polynomial-induced surreal extension F satisfies hypothesis (2) of the conjecture (F (1 + ε) - ofReal (f 1) infinitesimal) without further assumptions.

    This is a baby Berarducci–Mantova-style theorem: BM-continuity holds unconditionally for polynomials. Extending to formal power series requires the surreal convergence framework (not yet in Mathlib).

      theorem QOU.AppendixSurreals.polySurrealEval_isInfinitesimal_diff {K : Type u} [SurrealField K] (p : Polynomial ℝ) {a δ : K} (ha : SurrealField.IsFinite a) (hδ : SurrealField.IsInfinitesimal δ) :
      SurrealField.IsInfinitesimal (polySurrealEval p (a + δ) - polySurrealEval p a)

      Polynomial infinitesimal-stability (the main lemma).

      For any polynomial p : ℝ[X], finite a : K, and infinitesimal δ : K, the difference

      polySurrealEval p (a + δ) - polySurrealEval p a

      is infinitesimal. In particular, the surreal evaluation of a real polynomial is "BM-continuous at every finite point".

      This is the automatic-witness lemma for ClassicalLimitIsStandardPart: applied with a := ofReal r and δ := ε, it shows that polynomial extensions of real polynomials automatically satisfy hypothesis (2) of the conjecture.

      Application: automatic witness for ClassicalLimitIsStandardPart #

      source
      theorem QOU.AppendixSurreals.polySurrealEval_witness {K : Type u} [SurrealField K] (f : Polynomial ℝ) {δ : K} (hδ : SurrealField.IsInfinitesimal δ) :
      SurrealField.IsInfinitesimal (polySurrealEval f (1 + δ) - SurrealField.ofReal (Polynomial.eval 1 f))

      Automatic-witness corollary. For any real polynomial f : ℝ[X] and any infinitesimal δ : K, the surreal lift evaluated at 1 + δ differs from ofReal (f.eval 1) by an infinitesimal — i.e., hypothesis (2) of ClassicalLimitIsStandardPart is automatic for the polynomial surreal lift polySurrealEval f.

      Combined with the unconditional continuity of polynomials at every real point (hypothesis (1) of the conjecture), this gives ClassicalLimitIsStandardPart for every polynomial real function, with no further assumptions.
