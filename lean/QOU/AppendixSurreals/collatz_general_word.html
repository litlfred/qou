    General-word cycle forcing for q-Collatz (Front B, B2 — the arbitrary parity word) #

    Every cycle-exclusion result already in the corpus is stated either for one named parity word or for one shape of word:

    • period 2/3/4 word-by-word (qcollatz_period2_*, qcollatz_period3_*, qcollatz_period4_*);
    • the adjacent-tail shape Oᵐ E^{k-m} — an m-step odd chain closed by an even tail — in collatz_one_odd_family.lean.

    Nothing states the forcing equation for an arbitrary parity word. This file supplies it, together with the q = 1 height bounds every word obeys, and derives a cycle-exclusion family that strictly contains the adjacent-tail one.

    The uniform step #

    Both branches of the Skein-Collatz map divide by [2]_q = q + 1, so a step is uniformly affine:

    [2]_q · P_{i+1} = a_i · P_i + b_i,    (a_i, b_i) = (1, 0)     even
                                      (a_i, b_i) = ([3]_q, 1) odd

    with [3]_q = q² + q + 1. A parity word is therefore just a w : ℕ → Bool (w i = true ⟺ step i is odd), and iterating k steps gives word_collapse:

    (q+1)^n · Pₙ = wordHol · P₀ + wordNum.

    A period-k cycle (P k = P 0) then forces (qcollatz_word_forces)

    ((q+1)^k − [3]_q^m) · P₀ = n_w,      m = #{i < k : w i}.

    The height bounds #

    Applying a q = 1 evaluation φ : R →+* ℤ — the engine no_cycle_of_q_one_height of collatz_one_odd_family.lean — the denominator has height 2^k − 3^m, and the numerator obeys, at n steps with m odd ones,

    3^m − 2^m  ≤  φ(n_w)  ≤  3^m·2^{n−m} − 2^n.

    Both are proved here by induction on the word, in the multiplied forms 3^m ≤ φ(n_w) + 2^m and 2^m·(φ(n_w) + 2^n) ≤ 3^m·2^n, which avoids truncated ℕ-subtraction inside an exponent. The lower bound is attained exactly by the adjacent-tail word — 3^m − 2^m is precisely the height that file computes for its oddTailSum — so the landed family reappears here as the extreme case.

    Scope — what this does and does not do (AGENTS.md (a)/(b)) #

    This is a scope-(a) result. The upper bound is sharp, and on its own it is too weak to exclude a general word: 3^m·2^{k−m} − 2^k < 2^k − 3^m forces m ≤ 1. That is not a defect of the argument — the general cycle problem is open classically, and this is its q-analog. What the bound does buy is an exclusion whenever the odd steps are front-loaded: if every odd step occurs before position D, the numerator stops growing at D, and the height obstruction fires under a contractivity hypothesis relating D, m and k (qcollatz_no_front_loaded_cycle). D = m recovers the adjacent-tail case; every D > m is new, and front_loaded_beyond_adjacent_tail exhibits a concrete such word (O E O then evens) that no existing lemma covers.

    No invariant is fabricated, and no word is excluded that the height does not exclude.

    Base ring: generic [CommRing R] (§7c — no ordering, no radical, no Real.*).

              theorem QOU.AppendixSurreals.CollatzGeneralWord.qcollatz_no_front_loaded_cycle {R : Type u_1} [CommRing R] (φ : R →+* ℤ) (q P0 : R) (w : ℕ → Bool) (hq : φ q = 1) (k D : ℕ) (hDk : D ≤ k) (hfront : ∀ (i : ℕ), D ≤ i → w i = false) (hm : 1 ≤ oddCount w D) (hcontract : 3 ^ oddCount w D * 2 ^ D < 2 ^ oddCount w D * 2 ^ k - 2 ^ oddCount w D * 3 ^ oddCount w D + 2 ^ oddCount w D * 2 ^ D) (hforce : ((q + 1) ^ k - wordHol q w k) * P0 = wordNum q w k) :
              False

              No front-loaded cycle under the contractivity bound. Suppose every odd step of the word occurs before position D, there is at least one, and the period-k cycle equation is forced. Then the q = 1 height obstruction fires whenever

              3^m·2^D  <  2^m·2^k − 2^m·3^m + 2^m·2^D        (`m = oddCount w D`),

              and no such cycle exists.

              Taking D = m (all odd steps first, then evens) the hypothesis reduces to 2·3^m − 2^m < 2^k, so this subsumes qcollatz_no_adj_tail_cycle, whose hypothesis is the very slightly stronger 2·3^m < 2^k; every D > m is a word shape no existing lemma reaches.

              Scope (a). This excludes the front-loaded words, not all words: the general upper bound alone forces m ≤ 1, and the general cycle problem is open classically.

              A front-loaded word outside the adjacent-tail family #

It is front-loaded with D = 3, m = 2, and the contractivity hypothesis holds at k = 5: 3²·2³ = 72 < 2²·2⁵ − 2²·3² + 2²·2³ = 128 − 36 + 32 = 124.

                The exact height, and a computable exclusion criterion for any word #

                The two bounds above are enough for the front-loaded family but throw information away. The numerator's q = 1 height has an exact closed form: each odd step at position i contributes 2^i, carried through the [3]_q of every later odd step. Writing m_i for the number of odd steps after i,

                φ(n_w) = Σ_{i < k, w i} 3^{m_i} · 2^i.

                With that, the height engine gives an exclusion criterion computable directly from the word — no shape hypothesis at all. wordNum_map_lower and wordNum_map_upper are then the two extreme cases of this sum (all odd steps as early, resp. as late, as possible).

                Where the criterion stops — a proved limitation #

                qcollatz_no_cycle_of_height_lt is evaluable for every word, and computations/qcollatz_height_criterion_coverage.py evaluates it over all 2^k words for k ≤ 18. Two facts come out of that sweep, and the second is provable rather than merely observed.

                Measured. The excluded fraction does not grow with k — it declines, 0.375 at k = 3 down to 0.319 at k = 18, monotonically from k = 5 on. So the criterion does not asymptotically cover the word space.

                Proved, below. More sharply, the criterion cannot fire at all unless 2^k > 2·3^m − 2^m. The reason is the lower height bound: every word has φ(n_w) ≥ 3^m − 2^m, so once the denominator height 2^k − 3^m drops to that level there is no room left.

                The consequence is the useful part. A genuine cycle needs its holonomy near one, i.e. 2^k/3^m ≈ 1; the criterion needs 2^k/3^m > 2 − (2/3)^m, i.e. roughly 2^k > 2·3^m. Those two regimes are disjoint. The criterion is strong away from the critical ratio m/k ≈ log 2 / log 3 and empty at it — which the sweep sees directly: at k = 18 exclusion reaches m ≤ 10 while the denominator stays positive to m = 11, and that critical band of 31824 words has no exclusions.

                This bounds the whole height approach, qcollatz_no_adj_tail_cycle and qcollatz_no_front_loaded_cycle included: pushing the criterion further cannot reach cycle candidates. Closing the cycle question needs an invariant that survives at 2^k ≈ 3^m, which the q = 1 height provably does not.
