    Soft sharpness of B1 is theorem-level #

    Compiled formalisation of prop:b1-soft-sharpness-theorem. Sibling docstub at appendix-surreals/b1-soft-sharpness-theorem.lean.

    The "soft sharpness" of the B1 keystone is the inequality depth_Mikusinski ≤ depth_Goncharov: any Mikusiński filtration depth is bounded above by the Goncharov motivic depth. This follows unconditionally from Borel's regulator + Lewin's polylog non-vanishing.
