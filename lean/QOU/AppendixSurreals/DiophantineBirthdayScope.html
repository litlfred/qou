    §1 — every real sits at birthday ≤ ω, from the axioms #

    A restatement, but the one worth having in front of you: whatever separation is proposed, it has to fit inside [0, ω] for both classes of number.

    source
    theorem QOU.AppendixSurreals.birthday_ofReal_le_omega' {K : Type u} [SurrealField K] (r : ℝ) :
    SurrealField.birthday (SurrealField.ofReal r) ≤ Ordinal.omega0

    Every real embeds with birthday at most ω — algebraic and transcendental alike. Directly the birthday_ofReal_le_omega axiom, named here because it is the constraint the conjecture has to live inside.

    §2 — equal birthdays are indistinguishable by any birthday-only criterion #

    Trivial to prove and the entire point: if a proposed separation is a predicate applied to birthday (ofReal ·), then two reals with equal birthday fall on the same side of it, no matter how the predicate is chosen.

    source
    theorem QOU.AppendixSurreals.no_birthday_only_separation {K : Type u} [SurrealField K] {a b : ℝ} (h : SurrealField.birthday (SurrealField.ofReal a) = SurrealField.birthday (SurrealField.ofReal b)) (P : Ordinal.{u_1} → Prop) :
    P (SurrealField.birthday (SurrealField.ofReal a)) ↔ P (SurrealField.birthday (SurrealField.ofReal b))

    No birthday-only criterion separates two reals of equal birthday.

    P ranges over every predicate on ordinals, so this covers any separation criterion phrased in terms of the birthday alone — thresholds, limit/successor tests, anything.

    Combined with Conway's birthday(√2) = ω = birthday(π) (cited in the header, not derivable from these axioms), this is why conj:diophantine-birthday cannot be a statement about birthday ∘ ofReal.

    The same statement in the form the conjecture would need to contradict: there is no predicate on ordinals witnessing a separation between two reals of equal birthday.

    §3 — what this does and does not settle #

    Settled. A Diophantine separation cannot be a predicate on birthday (ofReal α), because in the intended model the birthdays coincide.

    Not settled. Whether a separation exists at the level of the sign-expansion trajectory — the prefixes of gonshor, compared against the Liouville exponent. That is the statement the conjecture's own gloss describes, and it remains open and unformalised.

    Also not settled. That the corpus's axioms are consistent with Conway's birthday on irrationals at all: nothing in SurrealField pins it, which is the same gap BirthdayFaithful closes for the naturals.
