    One-odd-step Skein-Collatz cycles are excluded at every period (Front B, B2) #

    Period-1/2/3/4 exclusion (elsewhere) proceeds word-by-word and does not scale. This file proves the first infinite family by a genuine height obstruction — the q-analog of the Steiner cycle-value bound, made rigorous for the one-odd-step words E^{k-1}O.

    A period-k cycle with exactly one odd step (the last) satisfies an even chain Pᵢ₊₁·(q+1) = Pᵢ (i < k-1) then one odd step P₀·(q+1) = (q²+q+1)·P_{k-1} + 1. The chain collapses (even_chain_collapse) to P₀ = (q+1)^{k-1}·P_{k-1}, and the odd step then forces

    ((q+1)^k − (q²+q+1)) · P_{k-1} = 1.

    Over the Skein ring ℤ[q^{±1}] — or any ring carrying a q = 1 evaluation φ : R →+* ℤ — apply φ: the denominator becomes 2^k − 3, so (2^k − 3)·φ(P) = 1, i.e. 2^k − 3 is a unit of ℤ. But 2^k − 3 ∉ {±1} for k ≥ 3. Contradiction — no one-odd-step cycle of period k ≥ 3. (k = 2 is EO, the fundamental {q⁻¹, 1+q⁻¹}, where (q+1)² − [3] = q is a unit — the one admissible case.)

    This is the honest q-Steiner obstruction: the cycle denominator D_w = (q+1)^k − (q²+q+1) has q = 1 height 2^k − 3, which the numerator n_w = 1 cannot match once k ≥ 3.

    The general adjacent-tail family E^{k-m}O^m #

    Both families above are the m = 1, 2 cases of the tail of m consecutive odd steps. In general it forces ((q+1)^k − (q²+q+1)^m)·P = Sₘ, where Sₘ = Σ_{j<m} (q²+q+1)^j (q+1)^{m-1-j} has q=1 height 3^m − 2^m. The denominator height is 2^k − 3^m; since |3^m − 2^m| < 3^m < 2^k − 3^m once 2^k > 2·3^m (enough even steps to be contractive), the divisibility fails — the whole family E^{k-m}O^m is excluded when 2^k > 2·3^m.

      Three-odd words E^a O E^b O E^c O #

      Three odd steps separated by even runs a, b, c. Chaining the three odd steps through the even runs (via the same collapse mechanism) forces

      ((q+1)^{a+b+c+3} − (q²+q+1)³)·x
    = (q²+q+1)² + (q²+q+1)·(q+1)^{b+1} + (q+1)^{b+c+2}.

      At q = 1 (s = q+1 ↦ 2, t = q²+q+1 ↦ 3) the denominator height is 2^k − 27 (k = a+b+c+3) and the numerator height is 9 + 3·2^{b+1} + 2^{b+c+2}.

      Honest wall (why three-odd is not a uniform one-shot like one/two-odd). For the one- and two-odd families the numerator height stayed uniformly below the denominator height for every arrangement once the period cleared a small threshold, so a single k ≥ k₀ excluded the whole family. That fails here: the worst arrangement a = c = 0 gives numerator 9 + 3·2^{k-2} + 2^{k-1} = 9 + 5·2^{k-2}, which exceeds the denominator 2^k − 27 = 4·2^{k-2} − 27. Those worst cases are exactly the clustered words — O E^{k-3} O O and its kin are rotations of the adjacent tail E^{k-3}O³, already excluded by qcollatz_no_adj_tail_cycle (a cycle is a cyclic word: D_w is rotation-invariant, so one representative per rotation class suffices). What the q = 1 height obstruction adds here is the spread arrangements: a leading even run a ≥ 3 makes the denominator dominate 16× the top numerator term, so the divisibility fails and the word is excluded. The two lemmas below give the general forced equation and the honest exclusion (conditional height, then the clean a ≥ 3 corollary).

      Uniform q-Steiner forced equation (any number of odd steps) #

      The rungs above (m = 1, 2, 3, 4) are instances of one statement. Encoding the even runs as a function r : ℕ → ℕ and the cycle values as v : ℕ → R, the same telescoping proves the forced equation for every m at once: a cyclic word with m odd steps and runs r 0, …, r (m-1) pins its value by ((q+1)^{S+m} − [3]^m)·v₀ = N_m, where S = runSum m = Σ_{i<m} r i and N_m is the q-Steiner numerator with recursion N_{m+1} = [3]·N_m + (q+1)^{S_m + m}. This subsumes qcollatz_{one,two,three,four}_odd_forces (e.g. m = 2 gives [3] + (q+1)^{r₀+1}, matching qcollatz_two_odd_forces). Only the arithmetic height bound on N_m stays arrangement-dependent — the algebra is uniform.
