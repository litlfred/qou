    QOU.AppendixSurreals.KComplex — Complex extension of a SurrealField #

    This module provides the complex extension KComplex K := K × K of a SurrealField K, modelled as ordered pairs (a, b) representing a + b · i. We provide:

    • the Manhattan modulus |a| + |b| (placeholder for √(a²+b²));
    • the multiplicative unit KComplex.one := (1, 0);
    • the imaginary unit KComplex.I := (0, 1);
    • complex multiplication KComplex.mul (a,b) (c,d) := (ac − bd, ad + bc) as a named operation (we deliberately do not override Prod's componentwise * instance, since KComplex K := K × K is an abbreviation);
    • the conjugate KComplex.conj (a,b) := (a, -b);
    • the algebraic-norm KComplex.normSq (a,b) := a² + b² valued in K.

    The named operations satisfy the standard ring identities (one, commutativity, associativity, i² = -1), the conjugation identities (involution, z · conj z = (normSq z, 0)), and the algebraic norm is multiplicative and faithful (normSq_mul, normSq_eq_zero).

    This unblocks Lean-level reasoning about the coloured-Jones placeholder ColoredJones : Knot → KComplex K → K → KComplex K (for example, the KashaevSurreal deep proof sketch needs KComplex K to be a ring).
