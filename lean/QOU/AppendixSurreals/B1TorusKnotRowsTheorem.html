    B1 keystone is theorem-level at torus-knot rows #

    Compiled formalisation of prop:b1-torus-knot-rows-theorem. Sibling docstub at appendix-surreals/b1-torus-knot-rows-theorem.lean.

    The B1 keystone holds unconditionally for every torus-knot row T(r, s) (gcd(r, s) = 1, r, s ≥ 2) via Bloch-Suslin plus Garoufalidis-Lê (Tate-rationality of the Chern-Simons invariant of torus knots).
