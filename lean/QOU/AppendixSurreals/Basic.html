    QOU.AppendixSurreals.Basic — Axiomatic surreal-coefficient field #

    We introduce a surreal-coefficient field K as a typeclass extending LinearOrderedField and equipped with:

    • a birthday : K → Ordinal function (Conway's recursive birthday);
    • a real-coefficient inclusion (↑) : ℝ → K making K an ℝ-algebra;
    • bounds linking ring operations to ordinal natural-sum.

    We do not construct K here. Mathlib's surreal-numbers development (SetTheory.Surreal.Basic) does not yet expose a real-closed-field witness; the actual K = 𝐍𝐨 carrier is axiomatised pending that development.

    This module is the foundation for everything else in AppendixSurreals/.

      Conway's birthday function.

    • birthday_zero : birthday 0 = 0

      The birthday of zero is zero.

    • birthday_one : birthday 1 = 1

      The birthday of one is one.

    • birthday_neg (x : K) : birthday (-x) = birthday x

      Birthday is symmetric: b(-x) = b(x).

    • birthday_add_le (x y : K) : birthday (x + y) ≤ (birthday x).nadd (birthday y)

      Birthday is bounded under sum by ordinal natural-sum.

    • birthday_avg_le (x y : K) : birthday ((x + y) / 2) ≤ (birthday x).nadd (birthday y) + 1

      Birthday of a dyadic midpoint is bounded by the natural-sum of its parents' birthdays plus one.

      This is exactly Conway's {L | R} recipe: the midpoint (x + y) / 2 is born one day after the deepest of x, y. The bound is genuinely independent of birthday_add_le: the additive bound gives only upper bounds on birthday (x + y) (hence lower bounds on summands), whereas this asks for an upper bound on a strictly earlier surreal.

      Narrow kernel rationale. Every caller in the surreals appendix that reasons about halving does so in the form (parent₁ + parent₂) / 2 (Gonshor sign-sequence step, dyadic refinement). A standalone birthday (x / 2) axiom would assert more than callers need; this combined form is exactly the Conway midpoint recipe and nothing more.

      Reserved for future induction proofs of the sharper birthday (gonshorIn K s) ≤ s.length bound; the current file proves the universally-supported ≤ ω form (see SignSequence.birthday_gonshorIn_le_omega) and the s.length ≤ 1 cases unconditionally.

    • birthday_ofReal_le_omega (r : ℝ) : birthday (ofReal r) ≤ Ordinal.omega0

      Real numbers have birthday at most ω.

    • omegaSurreal : K

      The first infinite surreal ω as an element of K.

      Conway's construction: ω = {0, 1, 2, … | }. Its existence in any SurrealField K follows from K extending the ordinals via the canonical inclusion (Ehrlich 2012, Thm 12.5).

    • omegaSurreal_gt_natCast (n : ℕ) : ↑n < omegaSurreal

      omegaSurreal is strictly greater than every natural number.

          Closure of IsFinite under the field operations #

          The finite surreals form a sub-(unital sub-)ring of K. The following closure lemmas (zero, one, neg, sub, plus the already-proved add, mul) provide the predicate-level closure needed to bundle {x | IsFinite x} as a Subring K.
