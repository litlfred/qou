    §3b class axiomatisation for content block conj:q-birch-swinnerton-dyer: The q-deformed Birch and Swinnerton-Dyer conjecture.

    For a QOU with substrate parameter q and Descartes elliptic curve 𝓔_q, with categorical Mordell–Weil group 𝓔_q(𝟏) of rank r_q, the class carries the conjecture's content, mirroring classical BSD:

    • Weak q-BSD — the order of vanishing of the q-deformed L-function at the central point equals the categorical rank: ord_{s=1} L(𝓔_q, s) = r_q.
    • Full q-BSD (rank-0 collapse) — when r_q = 0 (regulator and torsion trivial) the leading value is L(𝓔_q, 1) = Ω_q · |Ш_q(𝓔_q)| · ∏_𝔪 c_𝔪(q).
