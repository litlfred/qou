    QOU.AppendixSurreals.CasimirZetaPrefactor — the π²/720 prefactor's arithmetic core #

    Stage B of the N_⊙-construction workplan (docs/workplans/2026-06-12-n-odot-construction-scoping.md) for conj:casimir-epsilon-vacuum.

    The leading parallel-plate Casimir energy per unit area is −(π²ℏc/720)·d⁻³. Under the free-field ↔ disjoint-unknot identification (audit 2026-06-01-casimir-free-field-disjoint-grounding.md), the mode-count functional is the bare disjoint-unknot count (the unique n-stable functional, CasimirNStability.lean), carrying no y_λ Hecke weighting, so the prefactor is q-free and reduces to

    (π²/6) · |ζ(−3)| = (π²/6) · (1/120) = π²/720,

    where π²/6 is the transverse (k_⊥) geometric factor and ζ(−3) = 1/120 is the regularised cubic mode-sum.

    This file formalises the number-theoretic core of that prefactor:

    • riemannZeta_neg_three : riemannZeta (−3) = 1/120, from Mathlib's riemannZeta_neg_nat_eq_bernoulli' together with bernoulli' 4 = −1/30. Previously the value entered the witness only as a 40-digit numerical mp.zeta(-3) check; it is now a Mathlib theorem.
    • casimir_prefactor_factorization : (π²/6)·(1/120) = π²/720.

    Honest scope. What is machine-checked here is (i) the exact value ζ(−3) = 1/120 and (ii) its combination with the geometric factor into π²/720. What is not formalised — and is not elementary — is the ζ-regularisation step itself (the identification of the divergent cubic mode-sum Σ n³ with ζ(−3)); the regulator-independence of that identification is checked numerically in computations/casimir_n_unknot_construction.py (the exponential-cutoff route gives the same 1/120 via the same B₄ = −1/30). And the categorical mode-count object N_⊙(A, d) — the genuine blocker of the conjecture (Stage C) — is not constructed here. This file pins one input; it does not close the conjecture.

    Ref: [casimir1948] https://dwc.knaw.nl/DL/publications/PU00018547.pdf

    theorem QOU.AppendixSurreals.riemannZeta_neg_three :
    riemannZeta (-3) = 1 / 120

    ζ(−3) = 1/120. The Riemann zeta value at −3, from Mathlib's negative-integer Bernoulli formula riemannZeta (−k) = −B'_{k+1}/(k+1) with B'_4 = −1/30. This is the regularised value of the cubic mode tower Σ_{n≥1} n³ that sets the Casimir prefactor.

    theorem QOU.AppendixSurreals.riemannZeta_neg_three_re :
    (riemannZeta (-3)).re = 1 / 120

    The real part of ζ(−3) is the rational 1/120 used downstream in the (real-valued) energy prefactor.

    theorem QOU.AppendixSurreals.casimir_prefactor_factorization :
    Real.pi ^ 2 / 6 * (1 / 120) = Real.pi ^ 2 / 720

    Prefactor factorisation. (π²/6) · |ζ(−3)| = π²/720: the transverse geometric factor π²/6 times the regularised cubic mode-sum |ζ(−3)| = 1/120 is the textbook Casimir energy prefactor. The right-hand side is q-free — no Hecke y_λ enters, because the leading-order modes are disjoint unknots.

    theorem QOU.AppendixSurreals.casimir_prefactor_pos :
    0 < Real.pi ^ 2 / 720

    The prefactor is strictly positive (its sign as a force is fixed separately by the ∂_d of the energy; here we record the magnitude is nonzero, so the leading Casimir term does not vanish).
