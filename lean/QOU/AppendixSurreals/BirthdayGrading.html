    QOU.AppendixSurreals.BirthdayGrading — Birthday-graded hom-spaces #

    A birthday grading on a K-linear hom-space H is an ordinal-indexed filtration filter α : Submodule K H with filter monotone in α and exhaustive.

    This module provides:

    • the BirthdayGrading structure (filter family + monotonicity + exhaustiveness fields);
    • helper definitions archimedeanPart (= filter ω) and firstOrderPart (= filter (ω + 1));
    • a few lattice lemmas (archimedeanPart ≤ firstOrderPart, filter α ≤ ⊤, ⊥ ≤ filter α, exhaustiveness extraction).

    Closure of filter under sum / scalar action is not proved here — these would require the additional arithmetic-on-birthdays lemmas from the surrounding SurrealField axioms and are deferred to a future expansion of this module.

    References #

    • [conway1976] Conway, On Numbers and Games, Academic Press.
    • [gonshor1986] Gonshor, An Introduction to the Theory of Surreal Numbers, Cambridge University Press.

    A birthday grading on a K-linear hom-space H is an ordinal-indexed filtration of H by K-submodules.

    • filter : Ordinal.{u_2} → Submodule K H

      The birthday-≤ α submodule.

    • mono {α β : Ordinal.{u_2}} : α ≤ β → self.filter α ≤ self.filter β

      Filtration is monotone in α.

    • exhaustive : ⨆ (α : Ordinal.{u_2}), self.filter α = ⊤

      Filtration exhausts H.
