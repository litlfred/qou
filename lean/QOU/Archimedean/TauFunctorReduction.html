    The observation frame τ_∞. Modelling the two composable halves of the observation side of τ: the Markov trace tr : H → R (the Frobenius pairing tr_M, pre-wall, generic R) followed by the archimedean evaluation ev : R →+* ℝ (the wall F4 — "specialize q to the substrate q₀", a ring hom). τ_∞ = ev ∘ tr_M. This is the observation half; the full τ additionally carries the monoidal/braided structure (F1/F2) and the reality→Hodge lift (F5), none of which this composition supplies.
