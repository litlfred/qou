    theorem QOU.Archimedean.cLiTwo_ofReal (x : ℝ) :
    MathlibExt.cLiTwo ↑x = ↑(QPochhammer.LiTwo x)

    The bridge. cLiTwo at a real argument is the real LiTwo.

    Unconditional: both series are the same termwise after casting, and where neither converges both tsums are 0 by Mathlib's junk-value convention, so no disc hypothesis is needed.
