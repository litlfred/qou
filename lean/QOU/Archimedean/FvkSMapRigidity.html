    def QOU.Archimedean.FvkSMapRigidity.SFixed {R : Type u_1} [CommRing R] (m : ℕ → R) (c : R) :
    Prop

    S-fixedness. The moment sequence m is fixed by the s-multiplication map with ratio c: each moment is the previous one times c^k · m 1. This is exactly the condition c^{-k} · m (k+1) / m 1 = m k written without division.

      theorem QOU.Archimedean.FvkSMapRigidity.sFixed_closed_form {R : Type u_1} [CommRing R] {m : ℕ → R} {c : R} (h1 : m 0 = 1) (h : SFixed m c) (k : ℕ) :
      m k = m 1 ^ k * c ^ k.choose 2

      Rigidity, forward direction. An S-fixed normalised moment sequence is log-quadratic: m k = (m 1)^k · c^{k(k−1)/2}, the Stieltjes–Wigert shape.

      This is the sense in which F = S(K) is an SW-only phenomenon: the S-map has a fixed point exactly at the log-quadratic sequences.

      theorem QOU.Archimedean.FvkSMapRigidity.sFixed_second_ratio {R : Type u_1} [CommRing R] {m : ℕ → R} {c : R} (h : SFixed m c) (k : ℕ) :
      c * m (k + 1) ^ 2 = m (k + 2) * m k

      The division-free detector. S-fixedness forces the second-ratio identity c · m(k+1)² = m(k+2) · m k — a constant-in-k relation.

      This is what fails for the balanced family: its exact moment ratio m(k+1)/m(k) = q̃^k · E · (1 − p^{k+1}) makes the corresponding second ratio q̃ · (1 − p^{k+2})/(1 − p^{k+1}) depend on k. So the balanced moment problem admits no exact S-map, and F = S(K) holds for it only asymptotically.
