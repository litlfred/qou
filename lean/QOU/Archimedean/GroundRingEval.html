    QOU.Archimedean.GroundRingEval — the specialisation ι : R_q → ℝ, q ↦ q₀ #

    Bean qou-d8m1, the other half of qou-mt7r.

    The §7c split (qou-6ome) left the library with two layers and nothing between them:

    layer field
    QOU.SubstrateParameter M field : M → R_q (categorical)
    QOU.ArchimedeanSubstrateField M field : M → ℝ (order axioms)

    ArchimedeanSubstrateField's own docstring said it was "obtained by specialising the categorical field through ι : R_q → ℝ, q ↦ q₀" — and the type said no such thing. Its field was independent, so an instance could pair a real field with a completely unrelated categorical one and every archimedean proposition downstream still held. The prose asserted a tie the structure did not carry.

    ι is constructed here, not assumed #

    This is the difference from QOU/Shared/ArchimedeanEval.lean, and it is worth stating because the two files look alike. There the coefficient ring is an opaque CommRing R, so eval : R →+* ℝ has to be a hypothesis. Here the ground ring is concrete — R_q = LaurentPolynomial ℤ — and evaluation at a real unit is an ordinary ring homomorphism:

    • evalAt q₀ : R_q →+* ℝ for q₀ : ℝˣ, built from AddMonoidAlgebra.lift applied to zpowersHom at q₀;
    • evalAt_T : evalAt q₀ (T n) = q₀ ^ n, so it really is "substitute q₀ for the indeterminate" and not some other map;
    • evalAt_q_param / evalAt_q_inv_param pin the two named generators.

    evalAt_q_param_mul_inv checks the constructed hom respects q_param * q_inv_param = 1 — the relation GroundRingCore proves — so the image of the formal inverse really is the inverse of the image.

    q₀ : ℝˣ rather than q₀ : ℝ with a side condition. R_q is ℤ[q, q⁻¹]: the indeterminate is invertible, so any evaluation must send it somewhere invertible. Taking a unit is not a convenience — a plain real with q₀ ≠ 0 would need the inverse rebuilt at every use, and over a general codomain nonzero would not even suffice. This is the same distinction CategoricalQExtDeriv ran into (bean qou-u87j): over a ring, invertible is not implied by nonzero.

    What the tie does and does not settle #

    ArchimedeanRealisation bundles a categorical SubstrateParameter, a choice of q₀, and the two archimedean axioms stated about the realised values. toArchimedeanSubstrateField then produces the archimedean layer rather than letting it be posited independently.

    The order axioms are still genuinely assumed, and that is correct: 1 ≤ q₀^n and "not a root of unity" are archimedean facts about the chosen q₀, not consequences of the categorical layer. What is no longer assumed is the field itself.

    This is not the archimedean τ-functor. ι is an evaluation of the ground ring; τ is a functor from the braided category (QOU/Archimedean/TauFunctorTargetSpec.lean, still open). That ι turns out to be constructible says nothing about τ, and evalAt should not be cited as progress on it.

      structure QOU.Archimedean.ArchimedeanRealisation (M : Type u_1) :
      Type u_1

      An archimedean realisation of a categorical substrate parameter.

      The archimedean layer is produced from the categorical one through ι, rather than posited beside it. The two order axioms remain assumptions — they are archimedean facts about the chosen q₀ and do not follow from the categorical layer — but they are now stated about the realised values.

      • sp : SubstrateParameter M

        The categorical substrate parameter being realised.

      • q0 : ℝˣ

        The chosen specialisation point, a unit because q is invertible in R_q.

      • ge_one (p : M) : 1 ≤ (evalAt self.q0) (self.sp.field p)

        Classicality bound, stated about the realised value.

      • not_root_of_unity (p : M) (k : ℕ) : k ≥ 1 → (evalAt self.q0) (self.sp.field p) ^ k ≠ 1

        Not a root of unity, stated about the realised value.

            theorem QOU.Archimedean.dq_witness_realisable :
            ∃ (r : ArchimedeanRealisation PUnit.{u_1 + 1}), ∀ (p : PUnit.{u_1 + 1}), r.toArchimedeanSubstrateField.field p = 2

            The d_q² counterexample witness is realisable.

            QOU/Elaboration.lean's dq_squared_nonvanishing refutes a statement quantified over arbitrary ArchimedeanSubstrateFields, using a witness with field ≡ 2 on PUnit. Because that structure's field is independent of any categorical parameter, the refuted statement could in principle have been a strawman — a real-valued function with no substrate behind it.

            It is not. The witness arises from a genuine ArchimedeanRealisation: the categorical parameter is the indeterminate q, specialised at q₀ = 2. So d_q² fails to vanish on a substrate the framework can actually produce.

            This is the first thing the tie buys beyond hygiene, and it is why qou-w7sc concluded the migration it proposed was the wrong shape: there was exactly one construction site of a bare ArchimedeanSubstrateField in the library, and it is this witness, which should keep its concrete form.
