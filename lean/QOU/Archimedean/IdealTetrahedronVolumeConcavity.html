                theorem QOU.Archimedean.IdealTetrahedronConcavity.strictConcaveOn_volChart :
                StrictConcaveOn ℝ angleSimplex volChart

                T3 — the Lobachevsky volume sum is strictly concave on the open angle simplex.

                Proved by restricting to an arbitrary segment and applying Mathlib's strictConcaveOn_of_deriv2_neg to the restriction, whose second derivative is the hessian_neg_definite quadratic form.

                Non-vacuity #

                volChart_maximiser_unique below would hold vacuously on an empty simplex, and strictConcaveOn_volChart would be content-free. Both anchor shapes the corpus actually uses are exhibited as genuine points, so neither is.
