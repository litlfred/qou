    Dilog Part-B W-P1 — the a.e. Borel–Cantelli assembly (final) #

    Assembles the a.e.-convergence positive from the proved endpoints: branch_gap_lower_of_fract (#4437), volume_window_le_geom (#4440), and the schedule + majorant bricks (#4444). For the canonical Faddeev schedule q_m = 1 + 1/(m+1), ε_m = 1/(m+1)², we prove that for a.e. x ∈ (0,1) the branch-index boundary term (q_m − 1)·(−log(1 − x·q_m^{⌊s_m⌋})) → 0.

    Two pieces:

    • bdry_tendsto_of_eventually_no_resonance — the analytic squeeze: if x is eventually no-resonant (ε_m ≤ frac(s_m)), the boundary term is squeezed between 0 and the vanishing majorant tendsto_boundary_majorant, so → 0.
    • the measure side (ENNReal summability of Σ volume(W_m) + measure_limsup_eq_zero) gives a.e. eventual no-resonance — remaining, bean qou-a0li.
