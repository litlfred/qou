    QOU.Archimedean.em_spectral_completeness #

    Two-strand spectral completeness over a commutative ring R.

    The endomorphism algebra End(A ⊗ A) of the fundamental object's two-fold tensor power has rank 2 as an R-module, splits into exactly two central idempotents p₊, p₋ with p₊ + p₋ = 1, and the braiding c_{A,A} has only two eigenvalues q^(1/2), -q^(-1/2).

    The singlet idempotent p₋ projects onto the unit 𝟙. Any scalar coupling A ⊗ A → 𝟙 is a multiple of tr_τ(p₋) · p₋, hence the EM coupling is unique up to global normalisation.

          theorem QOU.Archimedean.em_spectral_completeness (S : SpectralRing) [Nontrivial S.R] :
          ∃ (p_plus : Matrix (Fin 2) (Fin 2) S.R) (p_minus : Matrix (Fin 2) (Fin 2) S.R), p_plus + p_minus = 1 ∧ p_plus * p_minus = 0 ∧ p_plus ≠ 0 ∧ p_minus ≠ 0

          Theorem (two-strand spectral completeness).

          The endomorphism algebra End(A ⊗ A) of the rank-2 block is modelled as M₂(R) = Matrix (Fin 2) (Fin 2) R, with the braiding diagonal in its eigenbasis (diagonal ![λ₊, λ₋]). Its two resolvent projections

          p₊ = (λ₊−λ₋)⁻¹ · (c − λ₋·1), p₋ = (λ₊−λ₋)⁻¹ · (λ₊·1 − c)

          are two non-zero idempotents with p₊ + p₋ = 1 and p₊·p₋ = 0 (each equals a coordinate projection diagonal ![1,0] / diagonal ![0,1]). The construction is well-defined precisely because λ₊ − λ₋ is a unit (eigenvalues_distinct) — this is where the spectral content enters ((λ₊−λ₋)⁻¹·(λ₊−λ₋) = 1).

          Correction (was a false axiom). This was previously an axiom asserting the idempotents live in the base ring S.R. That statement is false: an integral domain such as ℝ (a valid SpectralRing) has no non-trivial idempotent splitting of 1 (p₊·p₋ = 0 forces p₊ = 0 ∨ p₋ = 0). The idempotents live in End(A⊗A) = M₂(R), not in R. Requires Nontrivial R (over the zero ring p₊ = 0).
