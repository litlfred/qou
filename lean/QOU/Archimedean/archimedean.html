    QOU.Archimedean.descartes — Bring's surface is terminal in 𝐂_{−6} #

    Proposition (prop:descartes).

    Let 𝐂_{−6} denote the category of quantum observable universes over R satisfying the Descartes condition χ(A) = −6, with morphisms given by QOU maps that preserve the Euler characteristic. Then Bring's surface Σ₄ is a terminal object in 𝐂_{−6}: for every Descartes universe (𝐂, A, τ) in 𝐂_{−6}, there exists a unique morphism to the Bring's QOU.

    The Lean encoding records the universal-property statement: for every object X in 𝐂_{−6}, the hom-set Hom(X, Bring's-QOU) is a singleton.
