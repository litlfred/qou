          The sign relation.

          The three edge functionals w·v_t, w·v_{t+2γ}, w·v_{t+2γ+2α} are three linear functionals on a two-dimensional space, so they satisfy one relation; α+β+γ = π makes its coefficients sin 2α, sin 2β, sin 2γ — all POSITIVE for acute angles.             9. The anchor: the definition really is the triangle with those vertices #

            inscribedTriangle is DEFINED as an intersection of three half-planes. This is what keeps that from quietly assuming the geometry: the three points of the circle at angles t, t+2γ, t+2γ+2α sit on the boundary in the pattern that characterises a triangle's vertices — each on exactly two of the three chord lines, and strictly inside the third half-plane.

            That, plus convexity of a half-plane intersection, is what "the triangle with those vertices" means. A full interior (convexHull ℝ {P₁, P₂, P₃}) identification is not attempted here.
