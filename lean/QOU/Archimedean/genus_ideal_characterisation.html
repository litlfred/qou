    QOU.Archimedean.genus_ideal_characterisation — genus-filtration ideal as (u⁵) #

    Lemma (lem:genus-ideal-characterisation).

    At genus g = 4, the kernel of the truncation π_4 : ℤ[u] → ℤ[u]/(u⁵) is the genus-filtration ideal I_4 = (u⁵), consisting of those terms in the Chebyshev expansion of a_p whose Seifert cycles have no target in H_1(Σ_4).

    The Lean statement below records the abstract ring-theoretic side — the kernel of the quotient map by (u⁵) equals (u⁵) — which is the standard universal-property identity. The categorical identification of this ideal with the genus-filtration kernel of the Seifert-cycle map is recorded in the manuscript and discharged separately (the manuscript proof relies on prop:hecke-completeness which is downstream of this lemma).

    theorem QOU.Archimedean.genus_ideal_characterisation :
    RingHom.ker (Ideal.Quotient.mk (Ideal.span {Polynomial.X ^ 5})) = Ideal.span {Polynomial.X ^ 5}

    Genus-filtration ideal characterisation at g = 4.

    The kernel of the canonical quotient map ℤ[u] → ℤ[u] / (u⁵) is the principal ideal (u⁵). This is the abstract ring-theoretic core of the genus-filtration content described in lem:genus-ideal-characterisation; the categorical Seifert-cycle interpretation of the same ideal is recorded in the manuscript.

    Discharged by the standard Ideal.mk_ker identity (kernel of the quotient projection equals the ideal we quotient by).
