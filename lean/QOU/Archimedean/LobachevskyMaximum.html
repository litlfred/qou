    theorem QOU.Archimedean.IdealTetrahedronConcavity.Lob_pi_sub (t : ℝ) :
    Lob (Real.pi - t) = -Lob t

    Λ(π - t) = -Λ(t) -- antisymmetry about π/2, from Cl₂'s antisymmetry about π. This is what makes the volume sum vanish on every degenerate face of the simplex.

    The two identities Milnor's volume proof actually consumes #

    Sourced from the Appendix of [milnor1982] (full text now in library/), which states Λ is "odd, periodic of period π" and, in the orthoscheme computation, substitutes Λ(2a) = 2Λ(a) + 2Λ(a + π/2) to reduce the value to Λ(a)/2. Those are H4-4's inputs; both fall out of Clausen lemmas already in the tree.

    Milnor's general multiplication identity Λ(nθ) = n Σ_{k mod n} Λ(θ + kπ/n) is NOT proved here -- only the n = 2 case below, which is what the volume proof uses. The general case is left open (qou-w3eg).

    source
    theorem QOU.Archimedean.IdealTetrahedronConcavity.Lob_add_pi (t : ℝ) :
    Lob (t + Real.pi) = Lob t

    Λ has period π -- Milnor's Appendix, Lemma 1. Immediate from Cl₂'s 2π-periodicity, since Λ(t) = Cl₂(2t)/2 doubles the argument.

    source
    theorem QOU.Archimedean.IdealTetrahedronConcavity.Lob_duplication (a : ℝ) :
    Lob (2 * a) = 2 * Lob a + 2 * Lob (a + Real.pi / 2)

    The duplication identity Λ(2a) = 2Λ(a) + 2Λ(a + π/2).

    This is the n = 2 case of Milnor's multiplication identity, and the exact substitution his orthoscheme computation makes to collapse (Λ(π/2 + a) + Λ(2a) + Λ(π/2 - a) - Λ(0))/4 to Λ(a)/2.

    Proof: Cl_2_duplication at θ = 2a gives Cl₂(4a) = 2Cl₂(2a) - 2Cl₂(π - 2a), and Cl_2_two_pi_sub at π - 2a gives Cl₂(π + 2a) = -Cl₂(π - 2a), which is precisely the term needed to match.
