    QOU.Archimedean.OcneanuTwoThreeSubstrate — the two ratios really do differ over ℝ #

    Bean qou-bhfg. The archimedean half of QOU.Eightfold.OcneanuTwoThreeNotProportional, split per AGENTS.md §7c: the generic file states the gate over an arbitrary field and mentions no ordering, and ℝ enters only here, to discharge the gate's right-hand side.

    What it discharges #

    The generic file proves the N=2 and N=3 Ocneanu weight ratios are equal iff [3]_q = 0, and that [3]_q = q² + 1 + q⁻². Over an ordered field that is a sum of positive terms, so it never vanishes and the ratios always differ.

    This is what upgrades the exclusion's stated reason from "differs at every q tested" to a proof: the discrepancy is exactly [3]_q, and [3]_q > 0.

    Scope #

    Unchanged from the generic file: this concerns the quoted ratio formula y_{(2,1)}/y_{(3)} = q [2]_q [N−1]_q / [N+2]_q, which is consumed from docs/audits/2026-08-07-two-row-projection-is-the-pairwise-cap.md and not re-derived. It says the N=2 and N=3 weightings differ; it says nothing about whether N = 3 is otherwise available.

    The exclusion, proved. At any real q > 1 — the substrate sits at q₀ ≈ 1.10998 — the N = 2 and N = 3 Ocneanu weight ratios are different.

    So moving to N = 3 does not merely add the singlet channel; it re-weights the channels already in use. That is the content the exclusion row asserts, and it no longer rests on a sample.

    The exclusion at full strength. For every N, the Ocneanu weight ratios at N and at N + 1 differ — and by qb_shift_identity the discrepancy is the same [3]_q at every N.

    So the recorded reason for the route-3 exclusion — "the N=2 and N=3 traces are not proportional" — is the N = 2 case of a fact that holds all the way up: no re-tuning to an adjacent N reproduces the weighting.
