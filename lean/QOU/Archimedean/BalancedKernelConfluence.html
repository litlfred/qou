    The balanced-kernel weak-convergence theorem (MAIN, conditional). Let K n be the balanced q-Borel kernels along any sequence q_n → 1⁺ — probability measures on [0,∞) with all moments finite — and suppose the k-th moments converge to k! (the audit-§2 balanced moment confluence, exact at every order in the merged probe, here an explicit hypothesis pending its own Lean tranche). Then, conditional on the two classical Mathlib-gap theorems (Fréchet–Shohat + Carleman determinacy), the kernels converge WEAKLY to the unit Schwinger measure e^{−s}·ds. Positivity of the limit — the archimedean (POS) — is free: the limit object is a ProbabilityMeasure.
