    The image of the braid representation ρ₃ : B₃ → Aut_C(V⊗³) in SU(2) is the binary dihedral (dicyclic) group Dic₃ of order 12. Here we formalise the Dic₃ structure (Mathlib's QuaternionGroup 3, order 4·3 = 12) and derive the order claim from the single genuine categorical input — the group isomorphism image ≃* Dic₃ — rather than projecting it from an opaque context field.

      12 ≤ Fintype.card G ∧ Fintype.card G = 12

      Descartes braid structure (claims i–ii).

      Let (C, A, τ) be a Descartes universe. The braiding of C = Rep(SU_q(2)) induces a representation ρ₃ : B₃ → Aut_C(V⊗³) whose image in SU(2) (via the fibre functor τ) is the binary dihedral group Dic₃ of order 12 ([manuscript claims (i) faithful image of order ≥ 12, (ii) image is Dic₃ of order 12]).

      The single conjectural input is the group isomorphism himg : G ≃* Dic₃ identifying the braid image G with Dic₃ — the open categorical fact. Both numerical claims are then derived: the order is 12 by transport along himg and dic3_card, and faithfulness (order ≥ 12) follows. (Claims (iii) three S₃ colour-sectors and (iv) p-adic universality concern the ambient S₅ and base ring and are tracked separately.)
