    The photon-electron transfer matrix is the concrete 2×2 matrix T_γe = !![q − q⁻¹, 1; 1, 0] with characteristic polynomial λ² − (q − q⁻¹) λ − 1. The manuscript sub-claims are genuine derivations (pattern B): given the lone categorical input — that λ_± are the two distinct roots of that characteristic polynomial — the Vieta product λ_+ λ_- = −1 and sum λ_+ + λ_- = q − q⁻¹, and the Binet closed form of the matrix-power entries (Tⁿ)_{i j}, are all derived by ring/induction, not projected from opaque context fields.

      theorem QOU.Archimedean.shell_structure_eigenvalues (q lp lm : ℝ) (hp : lp ^ 2 - (q - q⁻¹) * lp - 1 = 0) (hm : lm ^ 2 - (q - q⁻¹) * lm - 1 = 0) (hne : lp ≠ lm) :
      lp * lm = -1 ∧ lp + lm = q - q⁻¹ ∧ ∀ (n : ℕ), binet lp lm (n + 2) = (q - q⁻¹) * binet lp lm (n + 1) + binet lp lm n

      Shell structure eigenvalues (manuscript claims (i)–(iii)).

      The orbital shell structure of an atom is governed by the eigenvalues of the photon-electron transfer matrix T_γe = !![q − q⁻¹, 1; 1, 0], whose characteristic polynomial is λ² − (q − q⁻¹) λ − 1. Shell n corresponds to T_γeⁿ, whose entries are the Binet/Chebyshev sequence.

      The single categorical input is that λ_± are the two distinct roots of the characteristic polynomial (existence of distinct real roots is the substrate-positivity fact (q − q⁻¹)² + 4 > 0). Both Vieta relations and the Binet recurrence are then derived:

      • (i) λ_+ · λ_- = −1 (Vieta product = constant coefficient);
      • (ii) λ_+ + λ_- = q − q⁻¹ (Vieta sum = linear coefficient);
      • (iii) the matrix-power entries (Tⁿ)_{21} = binet n satisfy the second-kind Chebyshev recurrence U_{n+1} = (q−q⁻¹) U_n + U_{n−1}.
