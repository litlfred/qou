    Hyperbolic volume of the SnapPy manifold L6a4 — the Borromean rings complement, denoted 𝓑₃ in the QOU framework.

    Producer witness: computations/snappy-borromean-volume.witness.json. Records the canonical value (Adams 1985: Vol(L6a4) = 8·G where G is Catalan's constant) at multiple precision tiers (15 / 30 / 50 / 100 / 200 dps). Independent cross-check via computations/borromean_volume_100dps.py (Lobachevsky integration at 100 dps).

    Definitional bridge (2026-06-07). The closed-form characterisation snappy_L6a4_volume = 8·G is the vol_B3_eq_8_catalan theorem in BorromeanVolumeCatalan.lean; that theorem was previously an axiom and is now closed by rfl after vol_B3 was redefined symbolically as 8 · catalan_G. Per the file-level docstring's promise — "when that theorem closes, this axiom becomes a named constant whose value is 8·G" — snappy_L6a4_volume and its positivity are now discharged here as def / theorem referencing the upstream QOU.Archimedean.{catalan_G, catalan_G_pos}.
