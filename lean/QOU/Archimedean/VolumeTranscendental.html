    Vol(4₁) is transcendental — the corollary HyperbolicVolumeIndependence already implies #

    QOU/Archimedean/HyperbolicVolumeTranscendence.lean carries three conjecture classes and draws one implication between them:

    HyperbolicVolumeIndependence  →  SingleVolumeIrrational        (`implies_single_irrational`)
HyperbolicVolumeIndependence  →  BorelZagierQOU                (`implies_borel_zagier`)

    Both land in the rational register — Vol ∉ ℚ, and no vanishing ℚ-linear combination. Neither is the statement downstream consumers actually need, and the stronger one is already in the class: no_relation quantifies over arbitrary finitely-supported f : ℕ × ℕ × ℕ → ℚ, so restricting to the (i, 0, 0) slice says exactly that no nonzero rational polynomial annihilates Vol(4₁) — transcendence, not merely irrationality.

    The file's own module docstring lists the chain as "irrationality ⟸ ℚ-linear independence ⟸ algebraic independence" and stops there. The middle rung — transcendence — was never drawn, and Transcendental appears nowhere in it.

    Why the distinction is load-bearing here #

    computations/qbeta/qbeta_n_question_arithmetic_reduction_probe.py reduces the route-5 N question to a trichotomy on the arithmetic nature of q₀, closing the rational branch (Niven) and the transcendental branch (2cos(rπ) is an algebraic integer), and leaving one survivor: q₀ algebraic irrational, which by the pinning identity requires Vol(4₁) to be algebraic.

    SingleVolumeIrrational does not touch that survivor — an irrational number may be algebraic. volTranscendental below does: it is precisely the hypothesis that kills the surviving branch, so conditional on HyperbolicVolumeIndependence the Kashaev root-of-unity sublocus is empty for every N.

    🛑 What this is NOT #

    This does not prove Vol(4₁) transcendental. HyperbolicVolumeIndependence is a conjecture class with no instance, and this file adds none — it draws an implication between an existing conjecture and a statement, in the §3b-cond pattern. Every result here is conditional on [HyperbolicVolumeIndependence], and that binder is the dependency edge.

    Bean qou-82w7 records that "transcendental" is frequently an interpretation of a near-singularity rather than a proved classification, and must not be used to prune algebraic candidates. This file is compliant by construction: nothing here asserts transcendence unconditionally, and the antecedent stays open.
