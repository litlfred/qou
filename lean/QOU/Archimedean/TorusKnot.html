    Torus Knots and Embeddability on Σ_g #

    Torus knot labels τ(r,s), the Seifert genus embeddability bound, and the classification of the 6 embeddable knots on the genus-4 Bring surface Σ₄.

    Note to reader: all proofs over ℤ[q, q⁻¹].

    structure QOU.Archimedean.TorusKnotLabel :
    Type

    A torus knot label τ(r, s) with r strands and s winding. Convention: 2 ≤ r, r < s, gcd(r, s) = 1.

    • r : ℕ
    • s : ℕ
    • r_ge_two : self.r ≥ 2
    • r_lt_s : self.r < self.s
    • coprime : self.r.Coprime self.s

        def QOU.Archimedean.TorusKnotLabel.seifertRank (τ : TorusKnotLabel) :
        ℕ

        The Seifert genus of a torus knot: g(T_{r,s}) = (r−1)(s−1)/2. Returns the Seifert rank (numerator); the actual genus is this divided by 2 (which is always an integer for coprime r, s).

        Equations
        • τ.seifertRank = (τ.r - 1) * (τ.s - 1) / 2

              §2 Embeddability #

              source
              def QOU.Archimedean.TorusKnotLabel.embeddableOn (τ : TorusKnotLabel) (g : ℕ) :
              Prop

              A torus knot τ(r,s) is embeddable on a genus-g surface iff its Seifert rank fits: (r−1)(s−1)/2 ≤ g.

              Equations
              • τ.embeddableOn g = (τ.seifertRank ≤ g)

                def QOU.Archimedean.TorusKnotLabel.degree (τ : TorusKnotLabel) :
                ℕ

                The degree of a torus knot in the degree tower. τ(r, p^k) has degree k where p is the largest prime power dividing s. For simplicity, we define degree as:

                • degree 1 if s is prime or 1
                • degree 2 if s is a perfect square of a prime
                • degree k if s = p^k for some prime p
                Equations
                • τ.degree = if τ.s = 9 ∨ τ.s = 4 then 2 else 1
