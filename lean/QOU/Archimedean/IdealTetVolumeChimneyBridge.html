    The Bloch–Wigner ideal-tetrahedron volume D(z) equals the genuine hyperbolic measure of the normalized chimney over its inscribed triangle.

    For z in the upper half plane with acute dihedral angles, BlochWigner_lob z (= IdealTetrahedron.volume for the ideal tetrahedron of shape z) is the dx dy dz / z³ measure of the concrete vertical chimney over the inscribed triangle at radius 1, in Milnor's normalized position.

    Unconditional and sorry-free; it uses no MostowIdealTetVolume instance. It is an equality of volumes, not of regions — see the module header: it does NOT prove the ideal tetrahedron IS the chimney (T2 / region_eq_chimney stays open), and it does NOT discharge qou-krvm (iv).
