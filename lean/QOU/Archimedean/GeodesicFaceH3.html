    Vertical planes #

    A vertical plane sits over a line w·n = d in the base ℝ². Its (upward-open) extrusion is the geodesic face; its Euclidean normal is the horizontal vector (n.1, n.2, 0).

    The vertical plane over the base line {w : w·n = d} — the set of points of ℝ³ whose horizontal projection lies on that line, at any height.

        Hemispheres #

        The hemisphere of radius R centred at the origin of ∂H³: the part of the sphere x² + y² + z² = R² with z > 0. Its Euclidean normal at a point p on the sphere is the radial direction p / R.
