    QOU.Archimedean.nuclear_groebner_reduction — Unique nuclear normal form via Gröbner reduction #

    The Hecke algebra H_A(q) = R[B_A] / Skein has an admissible system (positive braid monomials, length-lex ordering) with Gröbner basis G = {Hecke, Yang-Baxter, far commutativity}. The division algorithm gives the unique normal form N̄(p,n).
