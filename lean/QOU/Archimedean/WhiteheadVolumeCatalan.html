    Whitehead link volume — Catalan-constant identity Vol(L5a1) = 4·G #

    The Step-2 geometric input for the Whitehead row of conj:b1-hyperbolic-rows-theorem. Companion to BorromeanVolumeCatalan.lean:

    • the Borromean rings complement 𝓑₃ (L6a4) decomposes into two regular ideal octahedra ⇒ Vol(𝓑₃) = 8·G;
    • the Whitehead link complement (L5a1) is a single regular ideal octahedron ⇒ Vol(L5a1) = 4·G = ½·Vol(𝓑₃) (Thurston; Wielenberg 1978).

    Here G is Catalan's constant (catalan_G, upstream in HyperbolicVolumeTranscendence) and each regular ideal octahedron has volume 8·Λ(π/4) = 4·G (Lobachevsky–Catalan identity, lobachevsky / lobachevsky_pi_div_four_eq_half_catalan, both from BorromeanVolumeCatalan).

    What is / isn't Lean here (honesty, per AGENTS.md §Axiomatization-is-NOT-a-Proof) #

    • Proved sorry-free below: Vol(L5a1) = 4·G, the exact half-Borromean relation Vol(L5a1) = Vol(𝓑₃)/2, the octahedral form Vol(L5a1) = 8·Λ(π/4), positivity, and the numerical bracket 3.66 < Vol(L5a1) < 3.67. These are algebraic consequences of the same catalan_G / Lobachevsky infrastructure the Borromean file uses.
    • NOT proved here (and deliberately not laundered into a theorem): the Volume Conjecture for the Whitehead link — that the reduced colored Jones evaluated at q = e^{2πi/N} grows at rate Vol(L5a1). That asymptotic is verified only numerically in computations/whitehead_volume_search.py (growth → 4G, formula-free) and computations/whitehead_c0_run.py (the sub-leading factor). It is the Step-3 gap of conj:b1-hyperbolic-rows-theorem and remains a conjecture-class; the numerics are evidence for its instance, not a proof.

    References #

    • [adams1985hyperbolic] Adams, C. C. (1985), Proc. Amer. Math. Soc. 100, 601-606.
    • [wielenberg1978] Wielenberg, N. (1978), "The structure of certain subgroups of the Picard group", Math. Proc. Cambridge Philos. Soc. 84, 427-436 (Whitehead link complement = regular ideal octahedron).
    • [lewin1981] Lewin, L. (1981), Polylogarithms and Associated Functions.
