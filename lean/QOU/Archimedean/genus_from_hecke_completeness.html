    QOU.Archimedean.genus_from_hecke_completeness — the embeddable-count half #

    Lemma (lem:genus-from-hecke-completeness).

    Let N g be the number of torus knots T_{r,s} with r ∈ {2, 3} whose Seifert genus is at most g. Then g = 4 is the least genus at which N g ≥ 5, the number of irreducibles of H₂(q) ⊕ H₃(q).

    What changed, 2026-09-15 #

    This file previously carried torusKnotEmbeddingCount as a hardcoded table with | 4 => 5, and a theorem concluding torusKnotEmbeddingCount 4 = 5. Both were wrong, in the same direction:

    • the content block's own table gives 6 at g = 4, not 5;
    • the table's comment attributed T_{3,5} to g = 3, while its Seifert genus (3-1)(5-1)/2 = 4 puts it at g = 4;
    • the understated value was exactly the one that made the = 5 hold.

    The count is now computed from the Seifert-genus formula over a Finset rather than tabulated, so it cannot be bent to a theorem's shape again, and the theorem states the inequality the lemma actually claims (5 ≤ N 4).

    What this file does NOT establish #

    The lemma's companion claim — that the N 4 = 6 evaluations available at g = 4 span the 5-dimensional representation — is false, and nothing here should be read as supporting it. A T_{r,s} is the closure of a braid word on r strands, so it evaluates only in the H_r(q) columns; the character matrix is block-diagonal and its rank is bounded by min (#r=2 rows) 2 + min (#r=3 rows) 3. The two row counts are proved below (strandCountTwo_at_four, strandCountThree_at_four), and they give the bound 2 + 2 = 4 < 5 independently of q. The exact symbolic rank over ℚ(q) is in computations/audits/genus_completeness_rank_probe.py; the scope statement is rem:torus-evaluation-block-rank.

          theorem QOU.Archimedean.blockRankBound_at_four_lt_five :
          min {p ∈ embeddableTorusKnots 4 | p.1 = 2}.card 2 + min {p ∈ embeddableTorusKnots 4 | p.1 = 3}.card 3 < 5

          The block bound at g = 4 is four, so the spanning criterion fails.

          Rank is additive over the blocks of a block-diagonal matrix and each block is capped by the smaller of its dimensions, so the rank of the character matrix is at most min r₂ 2 + min r₃ 3 where rᵢ is the number of i-strand rows. Derived from the two counts above rather than asserted: min 4 2 + min 2 3 = 2 + 2 = 4.
