    QOU.Archimedean.force_carrier_bring_uniqueness #

    Bring's surface Σ_4 has genus 4. By the genus-4 torus-knot uniqueness ([torus_knot_genus_four_unique]), the force-carrier sector of any Bring-supported QOU is exactly {T_{2,9}, T_{3,5}}.

    The categorical complexities are [9]_q and [10]_q, with product [9]_q · [10]_q ∈ R — the denominator of α_EM.
