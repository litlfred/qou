    QOU.Archimedean.HeckeBraidingSubstrateNonSymmetric — the substrate braiding is not symmetric #

    Bean qou-tzfm. The archimedean half of QOU.Eightfold.HeckeBraidingNonSymmetric, split off per AGENTS.md §7c: the generic gate is stated over an arbitrary field and an arbitrary algebra over it and mentions no ordering; this file is where ℝ and 1 < q enter, and it does nothing but instantiate.

    Why the framework needs it #

    docs/audits/2026-08-25-4a-question-imports-symmetric-theory.md observes that the eightfold surface's surviving question — "can the gerbe's band be SU(3)?" — is stated in symmetric-category machinery, while QOU's category is not symmetric. The corpus previously carried that non-symmetry as prose (qou-definition-intro.md). Here it is a consequence: the Hecke relation makes the double braiding trivial only at q² = 1, and the substrate sits at q₀ ≈ 1.10998 > 1.

    What is not claimed: that this excludes SU(3) from the framework. It establishes the hypothesis under which the symmetric machinery does not apply, and nothing further. See the audit's §4.

    At any real q > 1 the double braiding is not the identity. The substrate sits at q₀ ≈ 1.10998, so the QOU braiding is non-symmetric — which is what the audit needs, and it is a consequence of the Hecke relation rather than a stipulation.

    The trace tower is not sign-flip invariant at the substrate.

    rem:isospin-mass-split-em-localisation lists the trace tower among the functionals that "respect these structural involutions", and uses that to localise the mirror-isobar mass split entirely into the Coulomb sector. At any real q > 1 — the substrate included — this says the premise is false for that sector: flipping a generator's sign changes the trace.

    The remark's cited support, prop:crystal-isospin-degeneracy, is a crystal limit q → 0 statement, and remains true there. Both facts hold; what does not hold is carrying the q → 0 invariance across to the functional at q₀. The size of the failure is (q − q⁻¹) · tr 1, about 0.209 · tr 1 at q₀ ≈ 1.10998, so it is not a negligible-correction argument either.

    Scope-(a), and narrow: this refutes one premise for one sector at one regime. It does not touch the co-Casimir or chirality sectors, and it does not refute the remark's conclusion — the split may still be entirely Coulomb-sourced.
