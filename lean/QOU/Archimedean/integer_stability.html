    QOU.Archimedean.integer_stability — convex-quadratic binding E(p, A) #

    Proposition (prop:integer-stability).

    At fixed mass number A = Z + N, the nuclear binding energy is a convex quadratic in the proton number p:

      E(p, A) = α(A) · p² − γ · p + δ(A),     where
  α(A) = a_C / A^{1/3} + a_A / A
  γ    = a_A
  a_C  = (q − q⁻¹) · λ_C        (Coulomb coefficient, `q`-deformed)
  a_A  = Pauli asymmetry coefficient.

    The Coulomb coefficient a_C is positive at q > 1, so α(A) > 0 and the parabola is convex; the unique integer optimum p*(A) is the closest integer to the real minimiser.

    The Lean encoding records the quadratic shape + the convexity claim (α(A) > 0) as a single proposition. Concretising a_C and a_A to the substrate-derived Coulomb/Pauli scales is downstream work.
