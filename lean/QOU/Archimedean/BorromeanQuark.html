    QOU.Archimedean.BorromeanBaryon — Borromean quark structure #

    Quarks as Borromean-linked unknots in three color tubes. The Borromean link B_3 has the property that no two components are linked, but all three together are inseparable. Vol(S³ \ B_3) = 7.32772...

    References #

    • [manuscript] Ch. 6, §3
    • [thurston1978] Geometry and topology of three-manifolds

    Ground ring: ℝ (archimedean realisation). Evaluates formal results at q₀ ≈ 1.1098 for comparison with experimental data. NOT part of the formal proof chain. See GroundRingAnnotations.lean.

              The mass calibration, stated as an identity rather than as a discharged hypothesis (bean qou-znut, resolved 2026-08-24).

              This was a class BorromeanQuark with a topological_mass : ℝ → ℝ field and a volume_calibrates_mass field asserting that field equals borromean_volume / (1 - 1/q)², plus an unguarded instance canonical supplying the right-hand side as the field's value so the assertion closed by rfl.

              The reading recorded here is that this is definitional and that is correct: topological_mass never was an independent functional that had to be shown to agree with a volume formula. It is the volume formula. The instance's docstring said so — "the mass-calibration identity holds definitionally" — and the class docstring already conceded the part that is genuinely open, that exhibiting a Borromean configuration "does not establish that the physical nucleon is one" and that identification "stays an open conjectural interpretation in the narrative".

              What the typeclass added was the appearance of a hypothesis. A reader meeting theorem baryon_mass_from_volume [BorromeanQuark] takes it for a derivation of baryon mass from hyperbolic volume, discharged by an instance; it is a rewriting of a definition, and baryon_mass below was already that definition. Since the reading is that there was nothing to discharge, the honest shape is the one without the binder — a def and a theorem … := rfl. Dropping the class changes no proof: baryon_mass_from_volume's content is unchanged, and it now says as much in its type.

              What is NOT claimed here, and was not claimed before. That the physical nucleon mass is borromean_volume / (1 - 1/q)². That is conj:borromean-quark, it lives in the narrative, and no Lean declaration in this file asserts it.
