    The conformal height-scaling data of a boundary inversion. On H³ (where p ≠ c), the inversion's Fréchet derivative is (R/‖p-c‖)²·(orthogonal reflection) — a positive scalar times a linear isometry — so it scales every tangent norm by lam(p) = (R/‖p-c‖)², and (because c_z = 0) it scales the height by the SAME factor.
