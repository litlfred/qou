    The bound #

    source
    theorem QOU.Archimedean.GammaGammaTraceFloor.pairing_ge_weight_of_psdFeasible {W : Type u_1} {ι : Type u_2} {d : ι → ℕ} (B : Mass.GammaGammaWordSelection.WedderburnBlocks ℝ W ι d) (y : ι → ℝ) (s : Finset ι) (hy : ∀ l ∈ s, 0 ≤ y l) {w : W} (hw : B.PSDFeasible w) {l₀ : ι} (hl₀ : l₀ ∈ s) (hchi : (B.proj w l₀).trace = 1) :
    y l₀ ≤ ∑ l ∈ s, y l * (B.proj w l).trace

    A PSD-feasible candidate's Frobenius pairing is bounded below by the weight of any block whose character it pins at 1.

    This is the SDP half of the ruling doing work: PSDFeasible forces every (B.proj w l).trace to be non-negative, so with non-negative weights every term of Σ_l y l * χ_l(w) is non-negative and the sum dominates the l₀ term, which is y l₀ * 1.

    The hypotheses are exactly the three ingredients and no more: weight non-negativity, PSD feasibility, and the distinguished character value.

    theorem QOU.Archimedean.GammaGammaTraceFloor.pairing_ge_one_of_psdFeasible {W : Type u_1} {ι : Type u_2} {d : ι → ℕ} (B : Mass.GammaGammaWordSelection.WedderburnBlocks ℝ W ι d) (y : ι → ℝ) (s : Finset ι) (hy : ∀ l ∈ s, 0 ≤ y l) (hnorm : ∑ l ∈ s, y l * ↑(d l) = 1) {w : W} (hw : B.PSDFeasible w) (hdet : ∀ (l : ι), (B.proj w l).det = 1) :
    1 ≤ ∑ l ∈ s, y l * (B.proj w l).trace

    The bound that supersedes the floor below: the pairing is at least 1.

    pairing_ge_weight_of_psdFeasible keeps one term of the pairing and discards the rest as merely non-negative. They are not merely non-negative: on a determinant-one block a PSD matrix has trace at least its dimension (PsdDetOneTrace.card_le_trace_of_det_one), so each term is at least y l * d l — and those sum to 1, because that sum IS the Markov normalisation tr_M(1) = 1.

    The determinant hypothesis is not an assumption about the candidate but a property of the γγ construction: σᵢ and σᵢ₊₁ are conjugate in the braid group, so their block images share a determinant and every vertex — hence every word — has determinant 1 in every block. Measured to 1e-39 in the companion probe; it enters here as a hypothesis per §3b.

    The bound is tight: the empty word is the identity in every block, so every trace equals its dimension and the sum is exactly 1.

    The floor and its decay #

    🛑 Everything below is superseded in strength by pairing_ge_one_of_psdFeasible above — the floor [n+1]_q/[2]_q^n is below 1 at every n ≥ 2. It is kept because it is what the argument gives if you stop at the trivial block, which is an easy place to stop, and because its decay lemma is the reason the weaker bound's exclusion is one-sided rather than scattered.
