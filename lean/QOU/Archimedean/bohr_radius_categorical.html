    Proposition (transport sketch) (prop:bohr-radius-categorical).

    The dressed atomic transfer matrix T^dressed ∈ H_n(q) admits a candidate categorical length scale

      a_0^cat = ℏ / (m_e · c · α_EM)

    at which postulated kinetic and Coulomb Markov-trace contributions balance: Φ_1^kin(a_0^cat) = Φ_1^Coul(a_0^cat).

    Caveat: NOT yet derived from QOU/Hecke structure; transported from the standard QED virial argument by analogy. Listed as a research-grade direction.
