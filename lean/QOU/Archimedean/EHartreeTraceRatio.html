    QOU.Archimedean.EHartreeTraceRatio #

    Round-20 algebraic core of conj:e-hartree-categorical-derivation.

    Round 19 (e_hartree_round19_dressed_scalar_block.py) computed the dressed scalar (2,2)-block character at K-shell dressing m = 5: χ₂₂ = h·([4]_q/[2]_q)² with h = q − q⁻¹. Round 20 (e_hartree_round20_trace_ratio.py) formed the trace-ratio ρ₂₂ := χ₂₂ / (q·[9]_q·[10]_q)² and found ρ₂₂ = C(q)·α_EM² with C(q) = h·([4]_q/[2]_q)² = h·(q² + q⁻²)² an O(1) element of R and α_EM = 1/(q·[9]_q·[10]_q).

    This file proves, sorry-free over ℝ (archimedean, 0 < q; AGENTS.md §7c), the two purely-algebraic facts underpinning round 20:

    • qbracket_four_div_two — the bracket reduction [4]_q / [2]_q = q² + q⁻² (the dressed (2,2) q-dimension dim_q V_(2,2)).
    • trace_ratio_factorisation — χ₂₂ / cplx² = χ₂₂ · (1/cplx)², the α²-scaling step (pure algebra, α = 1/cplx).

    The deep input — that the dressed seminormal character equals h·([4]_q/[2]_q)² — is verified numerically in the round-19 witness and is the character-theory content; it is the cited/granted ingredient here (the seminormal-trace formalisation is the standing open part). These lemmas discharge the algebra that turns that character value into the α_EM² shape.

    Round 24 (e_hartree_round24_molecular_reuse.py) adds the LEG-1 discharge of the round-23 "molecular-reuse" assumption: reusing the proven genus-4 uniqueness (QOU.Archimedean.torus_knot_genus_four_unique), the force-carrier crossing numbers are forced to {9, 10}, so the complexity cplx = q·[9]_q·[10]_q (= 1/α_EM, em-coupling-projection) is context-free — there is no molecular-vs-free version to assume. LEG 2 (the (2,2)=EM identification) stays conditional and is not formalised as discharged.

    NOTE (sandbox): authored against the restored partial lake-cache, which lacks the ring/field_simp/linarith oleans, so this file (including the round-24 additions) is hand-audited but compile-verified by CI (lean_ci.yml) rather than locally.

          theorem QOU.Archimedean.EHartreeTraceRatio.em_channel_forced_to_two_two :
          List.filter (fun (l : List ℕ) => conjPart l == l) [[2, 2], [2, 1, 1], [1, 1, 1, 1]] = [[2, 2]]

          Round 25 (the EM channel is forced to (2,2)).

          The 2-strand EM coupling is the sign rep of S₂ (the singlet e₋ = Λ²A, prop:em-coupling-projection); a covalent bond is two such singlets (Pauli-2), so the pair sits in sgn ⊠ sgn on S₂ × S₂. Inducing to the 4-strand molecular braid gives — witnessed (multiplicity 1 each, Frobenius reciprocity) in e-hartree-round25-em-channel-induction.witness.json —

          Ind_{S₂×S₂}^{S₄}(sgn ⊠ sgn) = (2,2) ⊕ (2,1,1) ⊕ (1,1,1,1).

          A parity-symmetric covalent bound state is closed under the Hopf antipode S : V_λ ↦ V_{λᵀ}, so its channel must be self-transpose. Among the three induced summands (2,2) is the unique self-conjugate one (proved here), so the EM bound-state channel is forced to (2,2); (2,1,1) is the force-carrier channel and (1,1,1,1) the decoupled channel.

          This grounds the (2,2)=EM identification in the covalent-pair structure; it does not close the conjecture — the sgn ⊠ sgn physical input and the round-20 α² algebra remain (and prop:force-carrier-intertwining-correspondence is e-hartree-conditional, so the (2,1,1) coincidence is corroboration only).
