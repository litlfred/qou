    ℕ → K

    The carrier moments S_q(n): the q-involution numerators of the Lean-proved binding recurrence (S(0) = S(1) = 1, S(n+2) = S(n+1) + [n+1]_q·S(n)) — exactly the sequence hypothesis of QOU.Mass.KeystoneBridgeC1Binding.keystone_bridge_binding_qlift, made concrete. Read as a Hamburger moment sequence (KSel-2 carrier frame). At q = 1 these are the involution numbers I(n), the moments of the Gaussian N(1,1) (carrierMoment_q_one).
