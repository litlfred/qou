    Additive Bring zero-mode form for 1/α_EM (conditional) #

    The candidate closed form

    1/α_EM = q · [9]_q · [10]_q + 2g/(4π(g-1)) + α·H·(1 - h²α) + Δ₂

    at g = 4 (Bring's surface, χ = -6), with the additive correction 2/(3π) = 2g/(4π(g-1)) identified as the τ_arch-image of a specific Bloch-group class assembled from the cosimplicial / Yekutieli triple data of the genus-4 force-carrier pair {T(2,9), T(3,5)}.

    This file axiomatises the missing structural input as a Lean typeclass BringZeroModeBlochClass. The class field asserts that there exists a class [Z] ∈ B(ℂ) whose Bloch-Wigner value D[Z] equals 2/(3π) under the chosen normalisation. Combined with the sibling typeclass QOU.Archimedean.BlochSuslinUniqueness from prop:archimedean-functor-uniqueness, the resulting conditional proposition is:

    Theorem (conditional). 1/α_EM - q[9]_q[10]_q = 2/(3π) + α·H·(1 - h²α) + Δ₂.

    On the correction term, and why it is not written O(α). The α-dependent part is not an unspecified asymptotic remainder: to the order currently derived it is the explicit Habiro/Schwinger self-coupling

    α·H·(1 - h²α), H = [2]_q/(8π), h = q - q⁻¹,

    with h the Hecke structure constant (the denominator of the balanced [k]_q, forced by the σ² = (q-q⁻¹)σ + 1 relation) — not the mass-chain ℏ_q = 1 - q⁻¹, from which it differs by a factor ≈ 2.11. Since α appears on both sides, the relation is solved as a fixed point rather than evaluated as a series; truncating after α·H gives the quadratic form and retaining the cubic (1 - h²α) factor gives the better one.

    Δ₂ is the genuine remainder: the uncomputed archimedean two-loop term. It is not the -h²Hα² piece already included above — that piece is weight 1 (a q-rational over π), whereas the two-loop object is weight 3 (ζ(3)-type), about 40× larger, and lives on the archimedean side of the wall rather than in the fibre. Writing the whole α-dependence as O(α) hid both the derived terms and that distinction.

    The Lean theorem bring_zero_mode_additive_form takes the class as an [Instance] hypothesis and is proved without sorry — exactly in the §3b-cond pattern.

    Closure of the class instance itself reduces to the explicit identification of the Bloch-group class on Bring's surface; that identification is the remaining open computational sub-problem documented in em-coupling-bring-zero-mode.md.

                  The section above still inputs g = 4 (and b₁ = 2g).                   V = 240/4 + 240/8 + 240/10 = 60 + 30 + 24 = 114, E = 3·240/2 = 360, χ = V − E + F = −6 ⇒ g = 4 ⇒ b₁ = 2 − χ = 8.

                  Together with bringSurfaceArea_eq_gaussBonnet (the tiling area = 12π), every ingredient of the ratio b₁/Area = 2/(3π) is now derived from the tiling — numerator and denominator both; the mathematics is closed.

                  Epistemic posture (owner, 2026-07-12): comparison to observed, no ontological claim. The framework does not assert a mechanism by which this ratio enters 1/α_EM; it reports a parameter-free structural value whose assembly with the tree term matches observed 1/α (near-match tier). Under that posture the remaining outside-view question (per docs/audits/2026-07-12-alpha-em-heat-kernel-coupling-outside-view.md) is assembly-selection — why this combination of Bring invariants, answered by the documented attempt ledger — and the stated cost is that the O(α²) coefficient is permanently observational (never computable without a mechanism), so the +0.313 ppb residual is a floor, not a waypoint.
