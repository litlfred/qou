      The Gröbner normal form in $H_8(q)$ gives $F_{\mathrm{Pauli}}^{(8)} \approx 0.368$. The pre-reduction term count is $8!/8 = 7! = 5040$, collapsing to $8$ terms after the quotient ideals. Structural counts are discharged by computation; the empirical Pauli factor is the single conjectural input carried by [Witness8BePauli].
