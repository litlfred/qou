      theorem QOU.Archimedean.PauliWitness.witness_4He [pauli : Witness4HePauli] :
      2 = 2 ∧ 2 = 2 ∧ 2 ^ 2 * 3 = 12 ∧ |Witness4HePauli.F_Pauli - 0.812| < 5e-3

      Pauli witness for ${}^{4}\mathrm{He}$ ($Z=2$, $N=2$, $A=4$), doubly magic.

      The exact Gröbner normal form in $H_4(q)$ after quotient ideals $I_8 + I_9$ gives $F_{\mathrm{Pauli}}^{(4)} \approx 0.812$. The polynomial $\mathrm{net}(t)$ has $[2]_t^2 \cdot [3]_t = 2^2 \cdot 3 = 12$ terms in $\mathbb{Z}[t, t^{-1}]$. The combinatorial count and magic numbers are discharged by computation; the empirical Pauli factor is the single conjectural input carried by [Witness4HePauli].
