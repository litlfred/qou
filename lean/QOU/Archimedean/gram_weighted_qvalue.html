      Manuscript statement (full body):

      Proposition. Let $\mathcal{G}_{\mathrm{par}} = \mathcal{G}(t_0, \ldots, t_{A-1})$ be the typed Garside element (typed Garside element) of the parent nucleus in $H_A(q)$, and let $G$ be the Gram matrix $G_{ij} = \mathrm{tr}_M(b_i \cdot b_j)$ restricted to the NF subspace spanned by $\mathcal{G}_{\mathrm{par}}$ and all conversion daughters. Then the $Q$-value for $\beta^-$ decay is:

      \begin{equation}\label{eq:gram-weighted-qvalue}
  Q(Z, N)
  \;=\;
  \biggl(
    \sum_{k=0}^{N-1}
    w_k^G \;\cdot\; \Delta_k
  \biggr)
  \,\times\, N \,\times\, m_e
\end{equation}

      where:

      1. The sum runs over all $N$ neutrons in the parent (all possible conversion channels).

      2. $\mathcal{G}_{\mathrm{dau}}^{(k)}$ is the typed Garside element of the daughter obtained by converting neutron $k$ ($t_k = n \mapsto p$), and

      \begin{equation}\label{eq:nf-reduction-cost}
  \Delta_k
  \;=\;
  \lVert \mathcal{G}_{\mathrm{par}} \rVert_1
  \;-\;
  \lVert \mathcal{G}_{\mathrm{dau}}^{(k)} \rVert_1
\end{equation}

      is the NF reduction cost (change in $L^1$ norm of the Gröbner--Shirshov normal form).

      1. The Gram weight for neutron $k$ is:
      \begin{equation}\label{eq:gram-weight}
  w_k^G
  \;=\;
  \frac{
    \lVert \delta_k \rVert_F
  }{
    \sum_{j=0}^{N-1} \lVert \delta_j \rVert_F
  },
  \qquad
  \delta_k
  \;=\;
  \mathcal{G}_{\mathrm{par}} - \mathcal{G}_{\mathrm{dau}}^{(k)},
  \qquad
  \lVert \delta_k \rVert_F
  \;=\;
  \sqrt{|\delta_k \cdot G \cdot \delta_k|}
\end{equation}

      where $\lVert \cdot \rVert_F$ is the Frobenius norm induced by the Gram matrix.

      Inputs. The substrate parameter $q$ (from $\mathrm{Vol}(4_1)$ and $m_\mu/m_e$) and the electron mass $m_e$. No other parameters.
