                n = i + m → swVec v n i = v ^ (n.choose 2 + 2 * i.choose 2) * vRise v m i

                THE MAIN INDUCTION (general n, i; sorry-free). The closed form for the iterated Jacobi vector of the SW data:

                (Mⁿe₀)_i = v^{C(n,2) + 2·C(i,2)} · ∏_{j=1}^{i}(v^{m+j} − 1),  n = i + m.

                Proved by induction on n; the four cases are i = 0, and i+1 with m ∈ {0, 1, ≥2} (the last two need one and two steps of bottom extraction respectively). Conjectured from the audit's symbolic sweep (depth ≤ 8); established here at every order.
