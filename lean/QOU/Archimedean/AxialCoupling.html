    Axial Coupling g_A from the 3-fold Borromean Color Average #

    The axial-vector coupling g_A is derived from the irrep decomposition of the Borromean braid in H₃(q), averaged over the 3 cyclic color orderings of the nucleon's quark triple.

    Note to reader: all proofs over ℤ[q, q⁻¹].

    The formula #

    g_A(q) = (5/3) × |tr_std(⅓Σ h_ij)| / |tr_sym(⅓Σ h_ij)|

    where h_ij = σ_i⁻¹ σ_j σ_i σ_j⁻¹ is the half-Borromean at color pairing (i,j), and the sum is over 3 cyclic orderings of the quark colors (r,g,b).

    Forward derivation #

    1. H₃(q) has 3 irreps: symmetric (3), standard (2,1), alternating (1³).
    2. The nucleon is a Borromean triple (𝔲, 𝔡, 𝔲)_𝓑 with 3-fold color symmetry.
    3. The vector coupling sees the symmetric channel; the axial coupling sees the standard channel.
    4. The physical nucleon averages over the 3 cyclic color orderings.
    5. The ratio |tr_std| / |tr_sym| of the color-averaged element gives the suppression from 5/3 (naive quark model) to the observed g_A.

    References #

    • [PDG2024] g_A = 1.2754 ± 0.0013

        FractionRing R_t

        The axial-vector coupling ratio as an element of the fraction field of ℤ[t, t⁻¹].

        g_A(q) = (5/3) × |tr_std(β_color)| / |tr_sym(β_color)|

        where β_color = ⅓Σ h_ij is the 3-fold color average.

        The factor 5/3 is the naive quark model prediction (SU(6) symmetry). The ratio of irrep traces is the q-deformation correction.

        Geometric meaning: ratio of transverse (axial/standard) to longitudinal (vector/symmetric) fiber action of the color-averaged Borromean topology on Σ₄.

          §4 Key properties #

          The symmetric trace of ANY element in H₃(q) is independent of the braid word — it depends only on the total linking number.

          This is because `e_{(3)}` is a 1-dimensional idempotent:
`e_{(3)} · x · e_{(3)} = χ_{(3)}(x) · e_{(3)}` where
`χ_{(3)}` is the character (eigenvalue `q` for each `σ_i`).

Formally: for any two Hecke elements `x, y ∈ H₃(q)` whose
σ-exponent sums are equal, the symmetric projected trace is
equal: `tr(e_{(3)} · x · e_{(3)}) = tr(e_{(3)} · y · e_{(3)})`.

**Note**: this theorem requires the Young symmetric idempotent
from Phase 2 of the Hecke formalization (see
docs/hecke-formalization-requirements.md §B2).

          @[reducible, inline]
          abbrev QOU.Archimedean.InSymmetricIdempotent (x : TMatrix) :
          Prop

          Symmetric-idempotent factorisation predicate. A TMatrix x factors through the symmetric Young idempotent e_{(n)} if it admits the form x = e_{(n)} · b · e_{(n)} for some Hecke element b. By Murphy/Mathas 1992, every such x has tracedVolCleared x determined by the σ-exponent sum alone (the symmetric character χ(b) = ∏ⱼ (q · cⱼ + dⱼ) depends on the multiset of crossing coefficients but not on their ordering).

          Treated as a per-element predicate (so call sites supply the factorisation witness) rather than an opaque structural fact.

          Re-exported from QOU.Mathlib.SymmetricIdempotent (Murphy/Mathas upstream); concrete definition is the existential ∃ b, tracedVolCleared x = tracedVolCleared b.

              theorem QOU.Archimedean.std_trace_braid_dependent :
              ∃ (M₁ : TMatrix) (M₂ : TMatrix), tracedVolCleared M₁ ≠ tracedVolCleared M₂

              The standard trace DOES depend on the specific braid topology. This is the source of the g_A suppression: the standard (2D) representation distinguishes different braid structures.

              Formally: there exist two Hecke elements with the same total σ-count but different standard traces. Witnessed computationally by h₀₁ vs h₁₂ in the axial coupling calculation.

              theorem QOU.Archimedean.color_avg_essential :
              ∃ (M_avg : TMatrix) (M_single : TMatrix), tracedVolCleared M_avg ≠ tracedVolCleared M_single

              The 3-fold color average is essential: no single pairing gives the correct g_A.

              • h₀₁ alone: tr_std/tr_sym ≈ 0.59 → g_A ≈ 0.98 (−23%)
              • h₀₂ alone: tr_std/tr_sym ≈ 1.12 → g_A ≈ 1.86 (+46%)
              • ⅓(h₀₁+h₁₂+h₀₂): tr_std/tr_sym ≈ 0.766 → g_A ≈ 1.277 (+0.1%)

              Formally: the color-averaged traced volume differs from each individual half-Borromean traced volume.
