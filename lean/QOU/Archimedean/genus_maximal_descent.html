    Genus from maximal descent (manuscript claims (i)–(ii)).

    Let (C, A, τ) be a quantum observable universe satisfying the Descartes condition χ(A) = −6. If geometric descent produces a compact one-complex-dimensional base M₀, then Riemann–Roch χ = 2(1 − g) forces the genus g = 4 — the maximal genus consistent with the Hurwitz bound |Aut(Σ_g)| ≤ 84(g − 1) = 252.

    • (i) the genus is 4 — derived from χ = −6 via Riemann–Roch;
    • (ii) the Hurwitz bound aut_order ≤ 84 (g − 1) holds (the standard Hurwitz theorem, carried as the hypothesis hhurwitz).

    χ, the categorical Euler characteristic, and the Riemann–Roch identity are the genuine inputs; the genus value is computed, not assumed.
