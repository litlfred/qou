    QOU.Archimedean.BreitWheelerPairProduction #

    Canonical declaration for content block at quantum-observable-universe/predicted-spectra/breit-wheeler-pair-production-derivation.ts.

    Formalises the manuscript bare photon pair step of Breit–Wheeler pair production: two identical photons give γγ = σ₀², and the Hecke relation σ₀² = (q − q⁻¹)σ₀ + 1 puts it in normal form (q − q⁻¹)σ₀ + γ with net weight net(γγ) = 1 + (q − q⁻¹) (§7c: archimedean over ℝ for the q > 1 sign argument).

    structure QOU.Archimedean.BreitWheelerPairProduction :
    Type

    Data for the bare Breit–Wheeler photon pair γγ = σ₀².

    A definition carrier: the substrate q > 1, the Hecke normal-form coefficients of σ₀² (σ₀-part q − q⁻¹, constant 1), and the net weight net = 1 + (q − q⁻¹), the sum of the normal-form coefficients.

    • q : ℝ

      Substrate parameter q.

    • hq : 1 < self.q

      QOU regime q > 1.

    • nfSigma : ℝ

      The σ₀-coefficient of NF(σ₀²).

    • nfConst : ℝ

      The constant coefficient of NF(σ₀²).

    • net : ℝ

      The net weight of γγ.

    • nfSigma_def : self.nfSigma = self.q - self.q⁻¹

      σ₀-coefficient is q − q⁻¹ (from σ₀² = (q−q⁻¹)σ₀ + 1).

    • nfConst_def : self.nfConst = 1

      Constant coefficient is 1.

    • net_def : self.net = self.nfConst + self.nfSigma

      net(γγ) = nfConst + nfSigma = 1 + (q − q⁻¹).

      BreitWheelerPairProduction

      De-axiomatisation (bean h2yz). The bare-pair NF coefficients are not free: they are the Hecke reduction of σ₀·σ₀ computed by a TransferMatrixOperator T at the same q. T.reduce_sigma_squared proves reduceA 1 0 1 0 = q − q⁻¹ and reduceB 1 0 1 0 = 1, so γγ = σ₀² genuinely reduces to (q−q⁻¹)σ₀ + 1 — the coefficients are derived, not asserted.
