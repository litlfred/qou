    Prokhorov/Helly selection (classical theorem, Mathlib gap). A tight sequence of Borel probability measures on ℝ has a subsequence converging in the topology of weak convergence. Direct half of Prokhorov's theorem (Prokhorov 1956, Thm 1.12; Billingsley, Convergence of Probability Measures, 2nd ed., Thm 5.1). The pinned Mathlib carries the tightness definitions (IsTightMeasureSet) and the Lévy–Prokhorov metrizability of weak convergence, but not the compactness theorem.

      class QOU.Archimedean.BalancedKernelTightness.MomentPassageStieltjes :
      Prop

      Moment passage to weak limits (classical theorem, Mathlib gap). If probability measures on [0,∞), with all moments finite and each k-th moment converging to M k, converge weakly to μ, then μ realizes the moment data M. Classical proof: for each k the bounded 2k-th moments give uniform integrability of s ↦ s^k, so the moment functional passes to the weak limit (Billingsley, Probability and Measure, 3rd ed., Thm 25.12, and the proof of Thm 30.2). This is the "moments of the limit" half of Fréchet–Shohat, separated from selection.

        Subsequential-limit identification (conditional). Let μs be probability measures on [0,∞) with all moments finite and k-th moments converging to k!. Then — conditional on moment passage and the classical Carleman theorem (whose divergence hypothesis for {k!} is Lean-PROVED, carlemanCondition_factorial) — every weakly convergent subsequence of μs has limit exactly the unit Schwinger measure e^{−s}·ds. Fibre-level uniqueness is never invoked: determinacy enters only for the archimedean limit sequence {k!}.
