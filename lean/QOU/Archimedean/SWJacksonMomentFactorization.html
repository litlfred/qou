      theorem QOU.Archimedean.SWJacksonMomentFactorization.balancedNormalizer_mul_one_sub_pow {K : Type u_1} [Field K] (v : K) (hv : v ≠ 0) (k : ℕ) :
      BalancedMomentConfluence.balancedNormalizer v (k + 1) * (1 - (v ^ 2)⁻¹) ^ k = v ^ k.choose 2 * pPoch (v ^ 2)⁻¹ k

      The SW ⊛ Jackson factorization at moment level (division-free): N^bal(k+1) · (1−p)^k = v^{C(k,2)} · (p;p)_k with p = (v²)⁻¹. The factor v^{C(k,2)} = q̃^{k(k−1)/2} is the moment sequence G_k of the log-normal LN(−L/2, L) — Stieltjes' 1894 indeterminate example at base q̃ = √q, the KSel-3 "one Gaussian" — and (p;p)_k/(1−p)^k is the Jackson moment sequence J_k. So m_k = G_k · J_k exactly: the balanced family's indeterminacy core IS the classical SW log-normal.
