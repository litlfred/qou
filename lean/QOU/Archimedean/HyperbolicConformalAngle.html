    ℝ

    The hyperbolic angle between tangent vectors u, v at height z in the upper half-space model. The conformal factor z⁻² multiplies the Euclidean inner product; the induced norms carry the same factor under a square root.

      theorem QOU.Archimedean.hypAngle_eq_angle {V : Type u_1} [NormedAddCommGroup V] [InnerProductSpace ℝ V] {z : ℝ} (hz : 0 < z) (u v : V) :
      hypAngle z u v = InnerProductGeometry.angle u v

      CONFORMALITY (the linchpin). For every z > 0, the hyperbolic angle at height z equals the Euclidean angle between the same tangent vectors — the z⁻² conformal factor cancels between numerator and denominator.

      In particular the right-hand side does not mention z, so the hyperbolic angle is height-independent: the same two tangent directions subtend the same hyperbolic angle at every height.

      Height-independence, stated on its own (immediate from hypAngle_eq_angle): the hyperbolic angle at any two positive heights agrees. This is what lets a dihedral angle along a vertical edge be measured at any convenient height.
