      ∃ (c : k), G₂ = c • G₁

      Schur proportionality of invariant forms. If the commutant of ρ is scalar (the absolute-irreducibility hypothesis — every matrix commuting with all ρ s is c • 1), and G₁ is a unit, then any invariant form G₂ is a scalar multiple of G₁. This is the Option-B core: two invariant symmetric forms on an irreducible representation are proportional.
