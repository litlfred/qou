    T2 — the totally-geodesic classification, made UNCONDITIONAL (zero citation). #

    This capstone closes the T2 development. The classification "the vertical planes and hemispheres of the upper half-space model H³ are totally geodesic" was originally cited (Thurston, GT3M Ch. 2 §2.3). Over the T2 arc it was reduced to a proof conditional on a single named conjecture, HypMinimizerUnique (existence

    • uniqueness of constant-speed length-minimisers in H³), and then that conjecture was itself derived, sorry-free:
    • WALL-2 (HyperbolicNormalization.exists_normalization / exists_normalization_isom) — any two points of H³ are carried to the z-axis by a dH-isometry with a dH-isometry left inverse;
    • WALL-4 (HyperbolicGeodesicZAxisUniqueness.zaxis_minimizer_eq_vseg) — a constant-speed minimiser between two z-axis points is the vertical segment;
    • WALL-5 (HyperbolicGeodesicUniqueness.hypMinimizerUnique_derived) — assembles the two into a genuine witness of HypMinimizerUnique.

    hypMinimizerUnique_derived was deliberately kept a def so that promoting it to a global instance — which auto-discharges every [HypMinimizerUnique] hypothesis across the corpus — was a separate, explicit step. This file takes that step (owner-authorised), and restates the two corpus-face classification theorems in their now-unconditional form.

    Everything here is ℝ/archimedean (real analysis) — §7c. #print axioms on the unconditional theorems shows only [propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]: no sorryAx, no new axiom, no remaining conjecture. T2 is at zero citation.
