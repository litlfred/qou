            Beta-decay transfer matrices (manuscript claims (i)–(iii)).

            Beta decay is mediated by the T_{3,5} torus knot, whose insertion at the converting quark produces the universal 2×2 matrix-valued polynomial Δ_β(q). The three crossing transfer matrices T_pp, T_nn, T_mix satisfy:

            • (i) tr T_pp = q − q⁻¹, tr T_nn = −(q − q⁻¹), tr T_mix = 0;
            • (ii) det T_pp = det T_nn = −1 (Hecke unimodularity, forced by the eigenvalues q · (−q⁻¹) = −1);
            • (iii) the two elementary decay matrices coincide, T_mix − T_nn = T_pp − T_mix (the single universal Δ_β).

            All three are derived from the explicit entries — the lone input is the substrate parameter q : ℝ.
