                        Helium-4 N(2,2) = [p, p, n, n] ★ DOUBLY MAGIC #

                        12 terms. Word-length histogram: {0:1, 1:3, 2:4, 3:3, 4:1} PALINDROMIC — confirms shell closure at (Z=2, N=2).

                        This is the Poincaré polynomial of S₄ evaluated on the NF support.

                        Beta decay Q-value: tritium → helium-3 #

                        The Q-value is determined by the DIFFERENCE in NF coefficients.

                        Key observation: tritium has (q+1)²(q²−q+1) factors while helium-3 has (q²+1) factors. The Q-value comes from the algebraic difference between these cyclotomic polynomials.

                        Δ(coeff σ₁) = q²(q+1)²(q²−q+1)/4 − (q²+1)²/4 = [q²(q+1)²(q²−q+1) − (q²+1)²] / 4

                        This is an EXACT polynomial in q. The Q-value is obtained by evaluating at q = q₀ and multiplying by the crossing energy scale κ = m_e/3.
