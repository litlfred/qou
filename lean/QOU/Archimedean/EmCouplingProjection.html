    QOU.Archimedean.em_coupling_projection — Electromagnetic coupling from braiding #

    The electromagnetic fine structure constant is determined by three finite combinatorial ingredients: the antisymmetric eigenvalue of the R-matrix (q⁻¹), the q-deformed crossing numbers of the two force-carrier generators T_{2,9} and T_{3,5}, and the substrate parameter q.

    α_EM = q⁻¹ / ([9]_q · [10]_q)

    Stated division-free over ℤ[q, q⁻¹] as the defining multiplicative relation (see IsEmCoupling): the denominator [9]_q · [10]_q is not a unit of the Laurent ring, so α_EM itself is a fraction-field element — but its defining content is the single relation

    [9]_q · [10]_q · α = q⁻¹,

    which lives in ℤ[q, q⁻¹] without any division. This mirrors the 2 · E_cross = HA convention in QOU.Archimedean.GroundRing, where a presentation factor that would leave the ground ring is replaced by the cleared-denominator relation.

    LaTeX label: prop:em-coupling-projection

    References #

    • [manuscript] §5/em-coupling-projection
    • [jones1987] https://doi.org/10.2307/1971403

    Ground ring: R_q = ℤ[q, q⁻¹] (Laurent polynomials over ℤ). The q-integers reuse the division-free QOU.Archimedean.qInt; q⁻¹ is the genuine ring element q_inv = T(-1), never a field inverse. The classical limit q → 1 ⇒ α_EM → 1/90 is an archimedean statement (it needs an order and |·| on the codomain) and therefore belongs to a ℝ-specialisation under descartes-universe/, not to this fibre-level file — see notation-collisions.md §Base ring convention (§7c).

          def QOU.Archimedean.IsEmCoupling (α : R_q) :
          Prop

          Electromagnetic coupling projection (Proposition).

          The fine-structure constant is the τ-trace of the antisymmetric braiding component normalised by the force-carrier crossing complexities,

          α_EM = q⁻¹ / ([9]_q · [10]_q),

          stated division-free over ℤ[q, q⁻¹] as the cleared-denominator relation α satisfies:

          [9]_q · [10]_q · α = q⁻¹.

          The formula factorises into three layers:

          1. Charge (discrete, from writhe): Q_q(K) = [w(K)]_q.
          2. Coupling strength (R-matrix): |tr_τ(σ_anti)|² = q⁻¹.
          3. Normalisation by force-carrier complexity: [9]_q · [10]_q.
