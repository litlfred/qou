    noncomputable def QOU.Archimedean.IdealTetrahedronConcavity.AngleTriple.ofShape (z : ℂ) (hz : 0 < z.im) :
    AngleTriple

    The shape parameter of an ideal tetrahedron is an AngleTriple.

    For Im z > 0, the dihedral angles (arg z, arg(1/(1−z)), arg(1−1/z)) are positive and sum to π — exactly the AngleTriple data. Every hypothesis is discharged by a theorem that already existed; what was missing was the observation that the two files describe the same object.
