    QOU.Archimedean.lepton_continuant_generation_exclusion — why the ladder stops at three #

    Interpretive derivation for the three-generation gap (prop:lepton-meson-torus-knot-dictionary, bean qou-x4qm).

    The gap, made precise #

    Two corpus statements bracket the lepton ladder:

    • torus-embeddable-complete-proof proves the embeddability bound g(T(2,n)) = (n-1)/2 ≤ g(Σ₄) = 4, so for m = 2 the odd coprime windings are n ∈ {3, 5, 7, 9} — four admissible knots.
    • lepton-continuant-recurrence assigns T(2,n) for n = 3, 5, 7 to e/μ/τ — three generations.

    So "why three generations" reduces to the single question what excludes T(2,9)? The dictionary's opening line asserts the answer — "n = 3, 5, 7, … (odd primes)" — but the primality condition appears exactly once in the whole corpus and is asserted, never derived.

    What this file derives #

    The candidate structural reason was stated in the dictionary author-note as a hypothesis: σ⁹ = (σ³)³ is a harmonic of generation 1 — the braid word factors through a smaller winding — while 3, 5, 7 admit no such factorisation. Made precise on the normal-form data:

    The σ-coefficient bₙ of the Gröbner–Shirshov normal form of T(2,n) = σ₀ⁿ (the Morgan–Voyce continuant of lepton_continuant_recurrence) satisfies the Fibonacci-polynomial divisibility b_a ∣ b_n ⟺ a ∣ n.

    Consequences on the genus-admissible windings {3, 5, 7, 9}, all proved below over Polynomial ℤ (X = the Hecke parameter h = q − q⁻¹):

    • b₃ ∣ b₉ with the explicit harmonic cofactor b₉ = b₃ · (X⁶ + 6X⁴ + 9X² + 1) — winding 9's normal-form coefficient is a multiple of winding 3's (t29_harmonic_of_first_generation).
    • ¬ (b₃ ∣ b₅) and ¬ (b₃ ∣ b₇) — the remainder is the unit −1, so b₃ would have to be a unit of Polynomial ℤ; it is not (degree 2).

    Since 3, 5, 7 are prime they have no proper odd divisor > 1, so b₃, b₅, b₇ are mutually non-divisible; 9 = 3·3 is the unique composite in the window, and b₉ factors through b₃. This turns the asserted "(odd primes)" into a derived statement at the normal-form level: the generation windings are exactly the odd windings whose continuant coefficient does not factor through a smaller one, i.e. the odd primes.

    Honest scope (scope-(a), not scope-(b)) #

    This is a derivation of the harmonic / factorisation distinction on the normal-form coefficient, not a proof that T(2,9) cannot be a physical state: T(2,9) is a genuine prime knot of genus 4, and the map from the continuant net to a physical mass is still open (the net ratios are O(1) while the observed lepton ratios are O(10²) — recorded as a failed validation in generation-count-t29-truncation.witness.json, not patched with a fitted scale). What is closed here is the corpus's asserted-not-derived primality parenthetical, replaced by the continuant-divisibility theorem. The remaining bridge — "normal-form-independent winding ⟹ physical generation", plus the mass map — stays open (bean qou-x4qm).

    The harmonic factorisation: b₃ ∣ b₉ #

    source
    theorem QOU.Archimedean.t29_harmonic_of_first_generation :
    morganVoyceB 9 = morganVoyceB 3 * (Polynomial.X ^ 6 + 6 * Polynomial.X ^ 4 + 9 * Polynomial.X ^ 2 + 1)

    Winding 9 is a harmonic of generation 1. The normal-form σ-coefficient of T(2,9) is the product of T(2,3)'s coefficient and the explicit cofactor X⁶ + 6X⁴ + 9X² + 1, so b₃ ∣ b₉. This is the precise form of σ⁹ = (σ³)³ factors through generation 1 on the Gröbner–Shirshov normal-form data.

    Non-divisibility for the prime windings 5 and 7 #

    source
    theorem QOU.Archimedean.not_b3_dvd_b5 :
    ¬morganVoyceB 3 ∣ morganVoyceB 5

    Winding 5 is not a harmonic of generation 1. b₅ leaves the unit remainder −1 modulo b₃ (b₅ = b₃·(X²+2) − 1), so a common divisor would divide 1; but b₃ is not a unit. Hence ¬ (b₃ ∣ b₅).

    source
    theorem QOU.Archimedean.not_b3_dvd_b7 :
    ¬morganVoyceB 3 ∣ morganVoyceB 7

    Winding 7 is not a harmonic of generation 1. b₇ = b₃·(X⁴+4X²+2) − 1, the same unit-remainder argument as not_b3_dvd_b5.

    theorem QOU.Archimedean.three_generation_windings_are_the_nonharmonic_ones :
    morganVoyceB 3 ∣ morganVoyceB 9 ∧ ¬morganVoyceB 3 ∣ morganVoyceB 5 ∧ ¬morganVoyceB 3 ∣ morganVoyceB 7

    Generation-exclusion summary. Among the four genus-admissible odd windings {3, 5, 7, 9}, winding 9 is the unique one whose continuant coefficient factors through a smaller winding's (b₃ ∣ b₉), while the prime windings 3, 5, 7 are mutually non-divisible. This is the derived content of the dictionary's asserted "(odd primes)": the generations are the odd windings whose normal-form coefficient does not factor through a smaller one.
