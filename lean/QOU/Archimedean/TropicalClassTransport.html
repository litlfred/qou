    The SW core: tropical class exactly 0 #

    source
    theorem QOU.Archimedean.TropicalClassTransport.swMoment_ratio {K : Type u_1} [Field K] (v : K) (n : ℕ) :
    SWCoreJacobi.swMoment v (n + 1) = SWCoreJacobi.swMoment v n * v ^ n

    The SW core's moment ratio is exactly v^n. Since v^n is an integer power of the lattice step, the core's tropical class is exactly 0 at every index — an exact statement, with no limit involved.

    theorem QOU.Archimedean.TropicalClassTransport.balancedNormalizer_ratio {K : Type u_1} [Field K] (v : K) (hv : v ≠ 0) (hp : 1 - (v ^ 2)⁻¹ ≠ 0) (k : ℕ) :
    BalancedMomentConfluence.balancedNormalizer v (k + 2) * (1 - (v ^ 2)⁻¹) = BalancedMomentConfluence.balancedNormalizer v (k + 1) * v ^ k * (1 - (v ^ 2)⁻¹ ^ (k + 1))

    THE TRANSPORT (exact, division-free). With p = (v²)⁻¹ the balanced normalizer's ratio is the SW ratio v^k scaled by E = 1/(1−p) and corrected by the Jackson factor (1 − p^{k+1}):

    N^bal(k+2)·(1 − p) = N^bal(k+1)·v^k·(1 − p^{k+1}).

    Derived from the merged exact factorization; no perturbation argument, no asymptotics.

    theorem QOU.Archimedean.TropicalClassTransport.balancedNormalizer_ratio_div {K : Type u_1} [Field K] (v : K) (hv : v ≠ 0) (hp : 1 - (v ^ 2)⁻¹ ≠ 0) (k : ℕ) (hn : BalancedMomentConfluence.balancedNormalizer v (k + 1) ≠ 0) :
    BalancedMomentConfluence.balancedNormalizer v (k + 2) / BalancedMomentConfluence.balancedNormalizer v (k + 1) = v ^ k * (1 - (v ^ 2)⁻¹ ^ (k + 1)) / (1 - (v ^ 2)⁻¹)

    Quotient form of the transport: the balanced ratio equals the SW ratio v^k times E = 1/(1−p) times the Jackson correction.

    The archimedean limit: the Jackson correction dies #

    source
    theorem QOU.Archimedean.TropicalClassTransport.tendsto_jacksonLogCorrection (q : ℝ) (hq : 1 < q) :
    Filter.Tendsto (fun (k : ℕ) => Real.log (1 - q⁻¹ ^ (k + 1))) Filter.atTop (nhds 0)

    The Jackson correction contributes nothing to the class in the limit. For 1 < q, log(1 − q^{-(k+1)}) → 0; dividing by the fixed log q̃ gives the log_q̃-error term of the tropical class, so the balanced tropical class converges to frac(log_q̃ E) — the u ≡ 1 class — at a geometric rate.
