            theorem QOU.Archimedean.tetVol_strictConcaveOn :
            StrictConcaveOn ℝ tetSimplex tetVol

            The Lobachevsky ideal-tetrahedron volume is strictly concave on the open angle simplex. This is the analytic content of qou-krvm hypothesis (iv) for the single-tetrahedron case: the volume functional is strictly concave, hence has a unique maximiser, on the angle-structure polytope. Symmetry-invariance is tetVol_symm₁₂ (and its S₃ companions).
