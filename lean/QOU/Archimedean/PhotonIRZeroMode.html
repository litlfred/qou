    QOU.Archimedean.PhotonIRZeroMode #

    From-the-Lagrangian derivation of the α_EM Bring zero-mode 2/(3π).

    prop:em-coupling-bring-zero-mode derives 1/α_EM = q[9]_q[10]_q + 2g/(4π(g-1)) + αH(1 - h²α) + Δ₂ at g = 4, where H = [2]_q/(8π), h = q - q⁻¹ is the Hecke structure constant, and Δ₂ is the uncomputed archimedean two-loop term (see EmCouplingBringZeroMode); its Status section left open why the photon IR contribution equals dim H¹ / Area, recording a failed naive attempt on the 3-manifold M_Bring × S¹.

    This file proves the geometric core of the resolution: the photon IR zero-mode lives on the 2-surface M_g itself, as the harmonic 1-form sector b₁(M_g) = 2g, normalised by the Gauss–Bonnet area Area(M_g) = -2πχ = 4π(g-1). The heat-kernel zero-mode contributes b₁/Area (standard), so the IR shift is 2g/(4π(g-1)) = 2/(3π) at g = 4 — and the naive M × S¹ attempt is strictly smaller (9/(24π²)), explaining the recorded failure.

    The topological/geometric facts here are unconditional; the identification "photon IR shift = b₁/Vol" is the standard heat-kernel zero-mode result (cited in the narrative), and the upstream τ_arch uniqueness remains the named conditional input of prop:em-coupling-bring-zero-mode.
