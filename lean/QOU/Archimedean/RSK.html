    Robinson–Schensted–Knuth Correspondence #

    Bijection: permutations of {1,...,k} ↔ pairs (P, Q) of SYT of the same shape λ ⊢ k. Implies Burnside's identity k! = Σ dim(λ)².

    References #

    • [knuth1970] D. Knuth, Permutations, matrices, and generalized Young tableaux.
    • [sagan2001] B. Sagan, The Symmetric Group, §3.1–3.3.
