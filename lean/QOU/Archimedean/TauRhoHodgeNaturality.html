    theorem QOU.Archimedean.TauRhoHodgeNaturality.star_mulVec_map_star {g : ℕ} (M : Matrix (Fin g) (Fin g) ℂ) (v : Fin g → ℂ) :
    star (M.mulVec v) = (M.map ⇑(starRingEnd ℂ)).mulVec (star v)

    The ρ-naturality square (forward), general form. For any matrix W and its entrywise conjugate W.map (starRingEnd ℂ), conjugation intertwines the two weight actions: star (W.mulVec v) = (W.map conj).mulVec (star v). This is the matrix-level naturality of ρ = star; it is star-linearity of mulVec and needs no hypothesis on W.
