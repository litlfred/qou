This identity is genuinely abstract over the opaque realisation functor ρ_∞ (no faithful Mathlib structure for ρ_∞ exists), so per the vacuity-fix playbook pattern C it is named as a def … : Prop over the categorical context — honestly recording the conditional conjecture rather than faking a proof by projecting a context field. The context class carries the genuine inputs (the substrate parameter, the CODATA constants and the categorical Φ₁ data); a Lean instance would be the conjecture (none = honest open state).

      Proposition statement (from manuscript):

      Let $\rho_{\infty} : \widehat{\mathbb{Z}[q]} \to \mathbb{R}$ denote the archimedean realization functor (archimedean realization). On molecular-binding $\Phi_{1}$ outputs, $\rho_{\infty}$ acts as*

      $$ \boxed{\;\; \rho_{\infty}\bigl(\Phi_{1}(\mathcal{M})\bigr) \;=\; \Phi_{1}(\mathcal{M}) \cdot \underbrace{m_e c^{2}\,\alpha_{EM}^{2}}_{E_{\mathrm{Hartree}}} \cdot \underbrace{(q + q^{-1})^{-2}}_{\text{molecular factor}} \;\;} $$

      with the leading factor $E_{\mathrm{Hartree}} = m_e c^{2}\,\alpha_{EM}^{2}$ the Hartree energy.
