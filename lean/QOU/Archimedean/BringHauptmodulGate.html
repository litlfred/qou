    The Γ₀(5) hauptmodul gate: t₅(τ₀) = −10 (exact rational, machine-checked arithmetic) #

    Bring's curve has modulus τ₀ fixed by j(τ₀) = −121945/32 and j(5τ₀) = −25/2 (Braden–Disney-Hogg arXiv:2208.13692 §2.2; the sibling record QOU/Archimedean/BringEllipticFactor.lean carries these literature values). By Riera–Rodríguez Thm 1 the residual gauge on Bring's modulus inside Sp(8, ℤ) is Γ₀(5) — τ is unique only mod Γ₀(5) — so the invariant coordinate of the modulus is the genus-0 hauptmodul of X₀(5),

    t₅(τ) = (η(τ)/η(5τ))⁶,

    related to j by the classical level-5 modular relation j(τ) = (t² + 250t + 3125)³ / t⁵. At j(τ₀) = −121945/32 this gives the sextic (t² + 250t + 3125)³ + (121945/32)·t⁵ = 0, whose relevant real root is the exact rational t = −10 (uploaded-papers audit idea A2, docs/audits/2026-07-18-uploaded-papers-binding-mass-gap-ideas.md §"New exact facts"; η-quotient numerics at τ₀ ≈ −0.5 + 0.186676i confirm, and the companion probe computations/probes/bring_hauptmodul_gate_probe.py re-verifies both exactly and numerically). The Fricke partner is 5³/t₅(τ₀) = −25/2 = j(5τ₀).

    The gate. Any q₀ ↦ τ dictionary (the open input named in docs/audits/2026-07-18-alpha-em-tau-correspondence.md) is physical only through Γ₀(5)-invariants, and must therefore land on the Γ₀(5)-class with t₅ = −10 — a discrete, parameter-free pass/fail in the style of the keystone 144·C(n) gate: a candidate dictionary missing t₅ = −10 is refuted with no precision search. Note τ₀ itself is transcendental (Schneider); only its invariants are exact — conflating the two re-imports the precision trap (audit idea A2, "Risk").

    This file machine-checks the arithmetic content of the gate over ℚ (§7c: no ℝ needed): that t = −10 is a root of the sextic (equivalently of its ℤ-cleared form), and the Fricke-companion arithmetic. The modular statements (that t₅ is the hauptmodul, that j = (t²+250t+3125)³/t⁵, RR92 Thm 1's Γ₀(5) gauge) are cited literature, not formalized here — this module asserts only the exact arithmetic they reduce to at τ₀.

    theorem QOU.Archimedean.BringHauptmodulGate.fricke_companion :
    5 ^ 3 / -10 = -25 / 2

    Fricke companion. The Fricke involution sends t₅ ↦ 5³/t₅; at t₅(τ₀) = −10 this gives 5³/(−10) = −25/2, which is exactly the literature value j(5τ₀) = −25/2 — an internal consistency check tying the two published j-invariants through the one rational hauptmodul value.
