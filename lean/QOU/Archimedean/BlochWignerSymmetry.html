    QOU.Archimedean.BlochWignerSymmetry — the cyclic symmetry of D #

    BlochWigner_lob is defined in the sibling module as a sum of Lobachevsky values at the three dihedral angles of the ideal tetrahedron of shape z:

    D(z) = Λ(arg z) + Λ(arg (1/(1−z))) + Λ(arg (1 − 1/z))

    The three shape parameters z, 1/(1−z), 1 − 1/z describe the same tetrahedron viewed from its three pairs of opposite edges. So D is invariant under the order-3 Möbius rotation z ↦ 1 − 1/z.

    This file proves that. The point is how cheap it is: the invariance is combinatorial, not analytic. Under w = 1 − 1/z the three angle-arguments are literally permuted, because of three field identities

    1 − w = 1/z          1/(1 − w) = z          1 − 1/w = 1/(1 − z)

    so the Lobachevsky sum is reordered and nothing is computed. No dilogarithm, no polylogarithm, no analysis — which matters, because Mathlib has none of those (bean mlgp, Gaps 1 and 4).

    Why this was worth isolating #

    Bean mlgp recorded that upgrading the literature-cited volume-conjecture instances would need the method of steepest descent, which Mathlib lacks entirely. That is true of proving Ohtsuki's asymptotic expansion. It is not true of the identity that carries the volume out of that expansion.

    QOU/Archimedean/FiveTwoOhtsukiBridge.lean reduces the 5₂ volume conjecture, given Ohtsuki's expansion, to

    2π · Re ζ = 3 · D(y₀)

    and that in turn reduces to exactly two facts: a cancellation of arg–log correction terms, and D(x₀) = D(y₀) where x₀ = 1 − 1/y₀. The second is this file's BlochWigner_lob_mobius_cycle — an instance of the cyclic symmetry, needing nothing Mathlib does not already have.

    Archimedean (ℂ → ℝ).

    -- Ref: [thurston1978] ideal tetrahedra and their three shape parameters -- Ref: [zagier1986] the Bloch–Wigner dilogarithm

    The three field identities #

    Everything below rests on these. They are field_simp/ring facts about the order-3 Möbius map z ↦ 1 − 1/z, with no complex analysis in sight.

    source
    theorem QOU.Archimedean.one_sub_mobius (z : ℂ) :
    1 - (1 - 1 / z) = 1 / z

    Under w = 1 − 1/z, the "one minus" slot returns 1/z.

    source
    theorem QOU.Archimedean.inv_one_sub_mobius (z : ℂ) (hz : z ≠ 0) :
    1 / (1 - (1 - 1 / z)) = z

    Under w = 1 − 1/z, the second shape parameter returns z itself. This is the step that closes the 3-cycle.

    source
    theorem QOU.Archimedean.one_sub_inv_mobius (z : ℂ) (hz : z ≠ 0) (hz1 : z ≠ 1) :
    1 - 1 / (1 - 1 / z) = 1 / (1 - z)

    Under w = 1 − 1/z, the third shape parameter returns 1/(1−z).

    Note the shape of the proof: rewrite 1 − 1/z as (z−1)/z first, so the nested division becomes a single quotient and field_simp has the non-vanishing facts it needs already in hand. Trying to field_simp the nested form directly asks it to discharge 1 − 1/z ≠ 0 on its own.

    Both orientations of the non-vanishing fact have to be in context. field_simp matches hypotheses syntactically, so z − 1 ≠ 0 does not discharge the (1 − z)⁻¹ that the right-hand side contributes: with only that one, the goal reduces to -1 = z * (1 - z)⁻¹ - (1 - z)⁻¹ and stalls there.

    The symmetry #

    source
    theorem QOU.Archimedean.BlochWigner_lob_mobius_cycle (z : ℂ) (hz : z ≠ 0) (hz1 : z ≠ 1) :
    BlochWigner_lob (1 - 1 / z) = BlochWigner_lob z

    D is invariant under the order-3 Möbius rotation z ↦ 1 − 1/z.

    The proof is a reordering: the three Lobachevsky summands are permuted cyclically, so the sum is unchanged. Nothing is evaluated.

    source
    theorem QOU.Archimedean.blochWigner_xOf_eq (y : ℂ) (hy : y ≠ 0) (hy1 : y ≠ 1) :
    BlochWigner_lob (1 - 1 / y) = BlochWigner_lob y

    Lemma (i) of the 5₂ bridge. With x₀ = 1 − 1/y₀, the two shapes have the same Bloch–Wigner value. Verified numerically at 40 digits (D(x₀) − D(y₀) = 1.1e-41) before it was proved; here it is a corollary of the cyclic symmetry, and needs no 5₂-specific input at all.

    This is one of the facts FiveTwoOhtsukiBridge.ohtsuki_sum_im_eq_three_blochWigner rests on. The others are BlochWigner_lob_inv below and an arg–log cancellation which, once the cubic is used, is three arg evaluations.

    The other symmetry: inversion #

    z ↦ 1/z swaps the orientation of the ideal tetrahedron. The three angles are again permuted, but each one is also negated, so D picks up a sign. Like the Möbius 3-cycle this is a reordering rather than a computation — the only analytic input is that arg is odd off the branch cut, which is why the hypothesis is z.im ≠ 0 rather than anything about D.

    source
    theorem QOU.Archimedean.arg_ne_pi_of_im_ne_zero {z : ℂ} (hz : z.im ≠ 0) :
    z.arg ≠ Real.pi

    arg z ≠ π off the real axis. Mathlib's Complex.arg_inv and arg_conj both carry an if arg z = π guard for the branch cut; this is the side condition that discharges it, and it is the only thing z.im ≠ 0 is used for below.

    source
    theorem QOU.Archimedean.arg_one_div_of_im_ne_zero {z : ℂ} (hz : z.im ≠ 0) :
    (1 / z).arg = -z.arg

    arg (1/z) = −arg z off the real axis.

    source
    theorem QOU.Archimedean.one_div_im_ne_zero {z : ℂ} (hz : z.im ≠ 0) :
    (1 / z).im ≠ 0

    1/z is off the real axis when z is: Im z⁻¹ = −Im z / |z|². Needed three times below, and again in the 5₂ bridge, because every hbw-style hypothesis about a dilogarithm has to be applied at both z and 1/z.

    source
    theorem QOU.Archimedean.BlochWigner_lob_inv {z : ℂ} (hz : z.im ≠ 0) :
    BlochWigner_lob (1 / z) = -BlochWigner_lob z

    D is odd under inversion: D(1/z) = −D(z) for z off the real axis.

    Under z ↦ 1/z the three shape parameters go to their own reciprocals, but shuffled: 1/z's first angle is minus z's first, its second is minus z's third, and its third is minus z's second. Concretely 1 − 1/(1/z) = 1 − z and 1/(1 − 1/z) is the reciprocal of z's third shape. So the sum is again a reordering, this time of three negated terms.

    The hypothesis z.im ≠ 0 is exactly what keeps all three reciprocals off the arg branch cut; it cannot be dropped, since at a real z the identity reads 0 = −0 only because both sides vanish for a different reason (BlochWigner_lob_real_eq_zero).

    And the third: conjugation #

    z ↦ z̄ is the other orientation reversal. Unlike inversion it does not permute the three angles at all — it negates each one where it stands — so the proof is one line shorter and the sign is the same. Both are needed: the 5₂ bridge reaches the corpus's triangulation shape y₊ from Ohtsuki's y₀ by composing them, y₊ = conj (1/y₀).

    source
    theorem QOU.Archimedean.arg_conj_of_im_ne_zero {z : ℂ} (hz : z.im ≠ 0) :
    ((starRingEnd ℂ) z).arg = -z.arg

    arg z̄ = −arg z off the real axis, discharging the same branch-cut if.

    source
    theorem QOU.Archimedean.BlochWigner_lob_conj {z : ℂ} (hz : z.im ≠ 0) :
    BlochWigner_lob ((starRingEnd ℂ) z) = -BlochWigner_lob z

    D is odd under conjugation: D(z̄) = −D(z) for z off the real axis.

    Conjugation commutes with all three Möbius maps (1 − z̄ = conj (1 − z), 1/(1 − z̄) = conj (1/(1 − z)), 1 − 1/z̄ = conj (1 − 1/z)), because they have real coefficients. So each angle is negated in place and Λ is odd.

    This is the formal content of the remark in FiveTwoOhtsukiBridge that the conjugation half of the two-cubic reconciliation is D(z̄) = −D(z).

    source
    theorem QOU.Archimedean.BlochWigner_lob_conj_inv {z : ℂ} (hz : z.im ≠ 0) :
    BlochWigner_lob ((starRingEnd ℂ) (1 / z)) = BlochWigner_lob z

    D(conj (1/z)) = D(z) — the two orientation reversals compose back to the identity on D.

    This is the step that connects Ohtsuki's shape y₀ (a root of y³ − 3y² + 2y − 1 in the upper half plane) to the corpus's triangulation shape y₊ (a root of the reciprocal cubic y³ − 2y² + 3y − 1, also upper): the reciprocal drops into the lower half plane, and conjugation lifts it back.
