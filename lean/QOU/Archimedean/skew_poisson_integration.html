      Let $H/\mathbb{F}_\pp$ be a smooth commutative formal group and $\pi_<$ a skewed-Poisson structure on $H$. Then there exists a (generally non-commutative) formal group $G/\mathbb{Z}_\pp$ such that:

      1. $G \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}_\pp} \mathbb{F}_\pp \cong H$ (reduction modulo $\pp$ recovers $H$);

      2. $\mathrm{Dist}(G)$ is a deformation of $\mathrm{Dist}(H)$ computed via a Grobner--Shirshov basis for the relations of the enveloping algebra;

      3. the comultiplication on $\mathrm{Dist}(G)$ satisfies the strong multiplicativity condition: the Frobenius relation $(\mathrm{Id}\otimes m)\circ(\Delta\otimes\mathrm{Id}) = \Delta\circ m$ is preserved under the deformation.
