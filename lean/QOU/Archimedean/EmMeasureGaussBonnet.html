    The α_EM per-loop measure as a Gauss–Bonnet invariant #

    Lean formalisation of the three identities established numerically in computations/alpha_em/alpha_em_gauss_bonnet_measure_probe.py (audit docs/audits/2026-07-18-alpha-em-gauss-bonnet-measure.md):

    • the "gauge loop unit" 1/(4π) is the geometric ratio |χ|/(2·Area);
    • the α_EM per-loop measure 1/(8π) is the Gauss–Bonnet object |χ|/(4·Area), which is genus-independent (it equals 1/(8π) for every g ≠ 1);
    • the Betti reading 1/(π·b₁) coincides with the measure only at g = 4.

    Here Area = 4π(g-1) is the hyperbolic Gauss–Bonnet area -2π·χ, |χ| = 2g-2, and b₁ = 2g for a genus-g surface. Everything is an ℝ-identity (archimedean: π, division), so the base ring is ℝ per the §7c wall.
