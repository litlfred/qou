    What is proved (all sorry-free, real content) #

    1. partBHolonomy_eq_prod — Φ_n IS an ordered holonomy. The Part-B partial product ∏_{k<n}(1 − x qᵏ)⁻¹ equals the discrete Yekutieli holonomy (ordered List.prod, QOU.MathlibExt.YekutieliIntegration.holonomy) of its scalar segment transports. This is the τ-shadow identification half of qou-zbcj: the recognisable Part-B observable literally is an ordered product of segment transports.
    2. partBHolonomy_reorder_free — abelian collapse / τ-shadow order-freedom. In the commutative τ-shadow (ℝ) the holonomy value is invariant under any reordering of its segments (holonomy_perm); so the value of the multiplicative integral is independent of the refinement order.
    3. holonomy_growth_order_dependent — fibre-level obstruction. An explicit non-abelian group and two single-segment paths with holonomy (p ++ q) ≠ holonomy (q ++ p): on the non-commutative fibre 𝒫exp the joint/symmetric limit is genuinely illegal. This is the structural reason na-Stokes selects the iterated (not joint) ordering for Part B — the joint limit would scramble the path-ordering of a holonomy, which is only harmless in the abelian degeneration realised by the scalar τ-shadow (fact 2).

    What this does NOT claim (anti-laundering, AGENTS.md "Axiomatization is NOT a Proof") #

    It does not discharge the physics bridge that the specific Φ_n is the Yekutieli multiplicative integral of the specific 𝐂-connection with the (ρ then q) refinement order — that identification remains the honest open conjectural input of qou-zbcj. This file proves the abstract ordering dichotomy the bridge invokes, plus the concrete "Φ_n is an ordered product" τ-shadow fact. It also does not claim −π²/3 is a curvature/commutator term (that is a no-match; the audit above). Nothing is wired to production; the landed NaStokesPartBOrdering class is untouched.
