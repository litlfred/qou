          K.m = 2 ∧ K.n = 9 ∨ K.m = 3 ∧ K.n = 5

          Proposition (genus-4 torus knot uniqueness).

          The only torus knots of genus 4 (under the canonical m ≤ n, gcd(m,n) = 1 ordering) are T_{2,9} and T_{3,5}.

          Proof: genus = (m-1)(n-1)/2 = 4 (floor division) forces (m-1)(n-1) ∈ {8, 9}. Since m-1 ≥ 1 and n-1 ≥ 1, both factors are ≤ 9, bounding m, n ≤ 10; a finite case split (interval_cases) with the coprimality hypothesis leaves exactly (2,9) and (3,5) — the cofactor cases (2,10) and (4,4) are eliminated by gcd ≠ 1.
