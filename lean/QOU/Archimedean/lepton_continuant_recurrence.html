    QOU.Archimedean.lepton_continuant_recurrence — Morgan–Voyce continuant NF #

    Proposition (prop:lepton-continuant-recurrence).

    Let h = q − q⁻¹ be the Hecke parameter and T_{2,n} = σ₀ⁿ the (2, n)-torus knot braid in B₂. Its Gröbner–Shirshov normal form in H₂(q) is NF(σ₀ⁿ) = a_n · 1 + b_n · σ₀, where the sequences {a_n} and {b_n} satisfy the Morgan–Voyce continuant recurrence

      a_{n+1} = b_n
  b_{n+1} = h · b_n + b_{n-1}
  b_0     = 0
  b_1     = 1

    The net coefficient is net(T_{2,n}) = a_n + b_n.

    The recurrence is encoded below over Polynomial ℤ with the indeterminate X playing the role of the Hecke parameter h = q − q⁻¹ (identical convention to the Chebyshev-degree sibling lemma chebyshev_degree_genus). The statement records the recurrence itself; downstream specialisation to specific n ∈ {3, 5, 7} recovers the e⁻/μ⁻/τ⁻ lepton-tower NF entries.
