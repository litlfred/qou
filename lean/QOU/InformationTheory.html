    Chapter 9 — Information-Theoretic Perspective #

    Formalization of the information-theoretic perspective on the QOU from Chapter 9 of the manuscript.

    LaTeX source: chapters/information-theory.tex

    Structure #

    1. Local Planck length — minimal information unit ℓ_P ∼ ℏ_q
    2. Information transfer rate — time as information transfer
    3. Cosmological regime conditions — local conditions at a point; deep-quantum (q → ∞) and classical (q → 1) regimes. Existence of global initial/terminal states is NOT assumed.

      Information transfer rate: quantifies the rate of information propagation at a point, proportional to ℏ_q. Time slows at the classical regime (q → 1, rate → 0) and runs fast in the deep-quantum regime (q → ∞, rate → 1).

        §3 Cosmological Regime Conditions #

        These are **local** conditions on the substrate parameter at a
single point p in an affine chart.  They describe what happens
IF such a regime holds at p — not that it holds globally or that
the universe reaches such a state.  Whether the sequence of
configurations S_k terminates or extends infinitely in either
direction is not assumed.
        source
        structure QOU.InformationTheory.DeepQuantumRegime (M : Type u_1) :
        Type u_1

        Deep-quantum regime at a point: q(p) is large, corresponding to high torsion pressure and near-maximal information transfer rate (ℏ_q → 1). This is the regime near a cosmological initial state (if one exists in the given affine chart).

        LaTeX: \label{sec:big-bang-info}

        • sp : ArchimedeanSubstrateField M

          The substrate parameter.

        • point : M

          The point where the regime holds.

        • threshold : ℝ

          The deep-quantum threshold Q > 1 (e.g. Q = φ² ≈ 2.618).

        • threshold_gt_one : self.threshold > 1

          The threshold exceeds classicality.

        • deep_quantum : self.sp.field self.point ≥ self.threshold

          q(p) ≥ Q at this point: genuinely deep-quantum.
