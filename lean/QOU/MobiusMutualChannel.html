            8. The realisable-triples restriction — §7's open sub-item, CLOSED #

            §7 proved that Möbius invariance forces f d = f 1 / d ^ 2, and its docstring flags one honest gap: MobiusInvariantKernel quantifies over unconstrained positive triples (d, da, db), while a geometrically realisable triple is three distances among three points of ℝ³ and must satisfy the triangle inequalities. The witness §7 uses, (1, 1/d, 1), needs d ≥ 1/2; the natural alternative da = db = 1/√d covers only d ≤ 4. Neither family reaches all of (0, ∞), so §7 left open whether the characterisation survives the restriction.

            It does. The mistake in §7's framing was looking for a single witness family. The realisable hypothesis is a two-parameter family, and that is enough room to bootstrap.

            Take the isosceles configuration da = db = t with chord c. Of the three triangle inequalities only c ≤ 2t binds — t ≤ c + t holds for free — and invariance reads

            f (c / t²) = t⁴ · f c        whenever  0 < c,  0 < t,  c ≤ 2t.

            That is isoscelesStep, and the rest is two applications of it:

            • x ≤ 4: take c = 1, t = 1/√x. Realisable exactly when √x ≤ 2, i.e. x ≤ 4, which is the hypothesis; the argument lands on c/t² = x.
            • x > 4: take c = 1/x, t = 1/x. Realisable because 1/x ≤ 2/x, and again c/t² = x. Now c = 1/x < 1/4, so the first case applies to f c, and substituting gives the result.

            So the restricted hypothesis is not weaker in its consequence, and §7's result may now be cited without the caveat. Bean qou-zfs6.

            What this does not do. It does not make the triples geometric: Realisable is the triangle-inequality predicate on three reals, which is exactly the condition for three points of ℝ³ to realise them, but no points are constructed here. And it says nothing about the FHW crossing bound or the mutual channel's magnitude — it is purely about the kernel's functional form.

                  §4 The structural gate: the mutual channel is ORIENTATION-BLIND #

                  qou-o6ml's constraint 2 prunes route (1)'s first ansatz. Two proved facts compose: for the Borromean link every pairwise lk_ij = 0, so a nonzero wᵢⱼ must be mediated by the triple invariant μ̄₁₂₃; and QOU.BraidsAndKnots.MilnorTripleLinkingMagnus.odd_symmetric_functional_vanishes (sorry-free) shows an exchange-symmetric functional that is ODD in μ̄₁₂₃ vanishes identically, because μ̄₁₂₃ flips sign under that exchange (mubar_borromean_eq_one / mubar_swapped_eq_neg_one). So an admissible wᵢⱼ must be EVEN in μ̄₁₂₃, and the linear ansatz is dead (linear_coefficient_must_vanish).

                  The Möbius mutual channel clears that gate, and not narrowly. Its integrand is a function of the separation alone — no orientation, no sign, no ordering of the strands enters it. So the exchange that flips μ̄₁₂₃ leaves the energy pointwise unchanged: the channel is not merely even in μ̄₁₂₃, it is blind to it, which is strictly stronger than the constraint demands.

                  This section proves that blindness as an honest invariance theorem rather than asserting it. reverse reparametrises every component backwards — the operation under which an oriented invariant changes sign — and the energy comes through unchanged. The proof is a genuine change of variables, not an unfolding.

                  WHAT IS AND IS NOT ESTABLISHED. This shows the functional survives the constraint that killed the linear-in-μ̄ ansatz. It does NOT show the functional is correct, and it is not evidence for route (2) over any other orientation-blind candidate. κ(A) cannot adjudicate that (PR #7080).

                        §5 The O2 gate is NOT cleared by construction — a correction #

                        I recorded on PR #7284 that this third gate "is the one the O'Hara subtraction satisfies by construction". That is wrong for this channel, and the qou-o6ml ROAST it came from is wrong the same way. Both reason from the literature O'Hara integrand 1/|γ(s)−γ(t)|² − 1/D(s,t)², which is subtracted by construction — but that subtraction lives in the self term, which by qou-o6ml's own split is qou-bn8e's object, not this bean's.

                        The channel this bean needs is the mutual one, and QOU.MobiusEnergy's own mutualIntegrand_eq_ohara_without_subtraction states plainly that it is the O'Hara integrand with the subtraction DROPPED (1/D² = 0). So the mutual channel stands in precisely coth's position and inherits precisely coth's obligation.

Here it forces the same shape — subtract the mean — and innerPairEnergy IS that mean over the unit parameter interval. So the subtraction is determined by the geometry rather than chosen, and §3's bracket already shows it is strictly positive: the subtraction is not optional, and not zero.

                        What is proved below is the gate itself: the subtracted kernel satisfies O2, and it is genuinely sign-changing rather than trivially so.

                          §6 The measure was an unstated assumption — removed, not documented #

                          §5's O2 theorem integrates against the flat parameter measure ds. The literature O'Hara energy does not: its integrand carries the arclength weights |γ'(s)||γ'(t)|, which is exactly what makes it reparametrisation-invariant. The two coincide only for unit-speed parametrisation, and LinkEmbedding carries no derivative data with which to say so — so §5 silently assumed constant speed.

                          "Zero mean" is a statement ABOUT A MEASURE, so that assumption is not cosmetic here: the physically intended one is the charge/arclength density, not ds.

                          The fix is to state O2 against an ARBITRARY density w rather than to write the constant-speed caveat into a docstring. Arclength is then one instance, w = 1 recovers §5 exactly, and nothing is assumed that is not named in the hypotheses. w is a bare function, so its integrability and non-degeneracy are carried explicitly — they cannot be derived from anything LinkEmbedding holds.

                              7. The discriminator: Möbius invariance FORCES the inverse square #

                              §§1-6 are admissibility: the mutual channel is integrable, blind to orientation, bounded away from zero, and (after subtracting its own mean) satisfies O2 against any density.                               This section supplies one, and it is the property that singles out the Möbius/O'Hara energy among knot energies in the first place: invariance under Möbius transformations of the ambient space.

                              What makes it statable here. A Möbius transformation is a map of ℝ³ ∪ {∞}, and LinkEmbedding carries no ambient geometry — only sep, a real-valued separation. But the classical transformation law under an inversion centred at a point a is itself a statement about distances:

                              |I(x) − I(y)| = |x − y| / (|x − a| · |y − a|)          (chord)
|I(γ(s))′|    = 1 / |γ(s) − a|²                        (arclength element)

                              so the invariance condition can be written entirely in the language sep already speaks. Those two laws are classical and are assumed here with a citation, not derived; what is proved is what they force.

                              What they force, and it is the whole point. Not merely that 1/d² happens to be invariant — that the exponent is the ONLY one that can be. A functional f of separation, carried against the arclength density, is Möbius invariant iff f(d) = f(1)/d². So the inverse square is not an ansatz and not a choice: it is the unique orientation-blind kernel compatible with the symmetry.

                                9. The LINK version of Theorem D — it EXISTS, it is ELEMENTARY, #

                                   and it is VACUOUS exactly where this framework needs it

                                qou-dux3 records the gap this section closes and re-opens one level down: BFHW's Theorem D, 2π c([γ]) + 4 ≤ E(γ), bounds the self energy of a knot by its crossing number, and §7's triage recorded that it is not statable here because LinkEmbedding carries no crossing data.                                       §9d says the Möbius mutual channel has no positive topological floor. That is a negative. This subsection turns it into a localisation, by composing with the corpus's own pair-locality theorem.

                                      QOU.Mass.CrossingModelPairLocality.PairLocal is the hypothesis that a pair's mutual energy is the same measured in isolation, inside a triple, and inside the full assembly; b3_eq_zero_of_pairLocal shows it forces the connected 3-body cumulant to vanish identically, and AME2020 measures b₃ = +1.4262225 MeV (QOU.MassTheory.AmeBindingNotPairwise.ame_b3_eq), so the physical binding is not pair-local.

                                      The instinct to check first, because it is wrong. "The Möbius mutual energy is a two-body functional, therefore the channel is pair-local, therefore its b₃ is zero, therefore route (2) is dead." The first clause is right and the second does not follow. PairLocal compares the pair's energy across assemblies, and a two-body functional still moves when the assembly moves the pair. Being two-body is a statement about the integrand; pair-locality is a statement about the configurations.

                                      What is true instead. mobius_b3_eq_zero_of_sep_eq: if the assemblies do not move the pair — the separations agree pointwise — then the channel's b₃ vanishes identically. Contrapositive (mobius_b3_ne_zero_forces_moved_sep): any nonzero b₃ from this channel is entirely the assembly's effect on the pair's separations.

                                      So route (2)'s many-body content is exactly the configuration change, and nothing else. Read with §9d — no topological floor — the two say the same thing from opposite ends: every number route (2) can supply is a derived geometry. That is not a refutation of the route; it is a specification of what it owes.

                                      Not claimed: that the assemblies do move the separations in this framework, nor by how much. That is a geometric measurement this file does not make, and it is the open thing. Nor is this a claim about the true arclength- weighted energy — see §9f on what innerPairEnergy does and does not model.

                                        9h. The charge-ANTISYMMETRIC content is the configuration difference too #

                                        §9g localises the channel's many-body content in the configuration change. This localises its charge-antisymmetric content in the same place, and the argument is one observation about the signature rather than anything new:

                                        mobiusCrossingModel takes no charge argument. Its only inputs are link embeddings and the arclength s; CrossingModel carries w2, w3, w4 and nothing else; toBindingMap is built from those three; and BindingMap.b3 is triple i j k - (pair i j + pair i k + pair j k). So there is no point in the chain at which a proton differs from a neutron. Two nuclei are two instantiations of one functional.

                                        Hence if two instantiations agree on the pair and triple separations, their b₃ agree — and the contrapositive is the statement this channel owes: any nonzero difference in b₃ between two instantiations forces their separations to differ.

                                        Why this matters to the EM arc. qou-9v7t splits the measured A = 3 cumulant by isospin and names the halves: b3_sym = 1.4262225 MeV, "the strong channel's obligation", against b3_asym = 0.3818775 MeV, "the EM channel's". The corpus's own summary of that split is that the framework has no EM term in any per-bond factor (f_pp = z, f_nn = z - h, f_pn = z - h(1/2 - δ/4) — charge-labelled but not charge-coupled). The result below says route (2) does not need one: whatever charge-antisymmetric number this channel can supply is a derived geometry, exactly as §9g found for the many-body number. One obligation, not two.

                                        Two scope notes, both load-bearing.

                                        Arity. CrossingModel is Fin 4-indexed, so b3 i j k is a triple's cumulant inside a four-component model, while the mirror pair ³H/³He is A = 3. The theorem below is therefore stated for two arbitrary instantiations, which is what is proved; reading them as ³H and ³He is an interpretation, and it carries that spare fourth component with it.

                                        Not claimed. That the mirror partners' separations do differ in this framework, nor by how much — the same open item as §9g (qou-a2w5), now reached from the charge side as well. In particular this is not a prediction of b3_asym; it is a statement about where any such prediction would have to come from.
