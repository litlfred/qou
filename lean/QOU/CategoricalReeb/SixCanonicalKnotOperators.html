    QOU.CategoricalReeb.SixCanonicalKnotOperators #

    The six canonical knot operators as $q$-Reeb-flow trajectories on the color sectors. Conditional on a QReebFlowOnColorCoords q instance, the boundary permutation induced by each canonical braid word equals the corresponding element of $S_3$, realising $\rho: B_3 \to S_3$ explicitly as a flow-trajectory computation.

    LaTeX label: prop:six-canonical-knot-operators

    The argument is a finite case check on the six elements of $S_3$: each row of the proposition's table is one constructor of CanonicalBraid below, and its boundaryPerm is a finite-cases discharge.

            theorem QOU.CategoricalReeb.boundaryPerm_involution_iff_transposition (β : CanonicalBraid) :
            (∀ (s : ColorSector), β.boundaryPerm (β.boundaryPerm s) = s) ↔ β = CanonicalBraid.identity ∨ β = CanonicalBraid.sigma1 ∨ β = CanonicalBraid.sigma2 ∨ β = CanonicalBraid.garside

            The boundary permutation is an involution exactly for the identity element and the three transpositions ($\sigma_1$, $\sigma_2$, garside).

            theorem QOU.CategoricalReeb.threeCycles_are_inverse (s : ColorSector) :
            CanonicalBraid.threeCycleNeg.boundaryPerm (CanonicalBraid.threeCyclePos.boundaryPerm s) = s

            The two 3-cycles are inverses of each other on the boundary disc: applying threeCyclePos then threeCycleNeg returns the identity.

            theorem QOU.CategoricalReeb.canonical_braid_count_six :
            CanonicalBraid.all.length = 6 ∧ CanonicalBraid.all.Nodup

            The number of distinct boundary permutations realised by the six canonical braids is exactly $6 = |S_3|$. Together with boundaryPerm_bijective, this shows the canonical braids realise all six elements of the symmetric group on three color sectors.

            theorem QOU.CategoricalReeb.boundary_perm_realises_S3 :
            Function.Injective CanonicalBraid.boundaryPerm ∧ (∀ (β : CanonicalBraid), Function.Bijective β.boundaryPerm) ∧ CanonicalBraid.all.length = 6 ∧ CanonicalBraid.all.Nodup

            Six canonical knot operators realise $S_3$.

            The six canonical braid words map to six distinct bijections of ColorSector, exhausting the six elements of the symmetric group on three sectors. This is the combinatorial boundary-permutation content of prop:six-canonical-knot-operators; the geometric identification of these permutations with the boundary footprints of the $q$-Reeb flow trajectories (which DOES consume the QReebFlowOnColorCoords q class) lives in the manuscript prose, not in this theorem.
