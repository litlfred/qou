    QOU.CategoricalReeb.QReebFlowOnColorCoords #

    Axiomatisation of the $q$-Reeb flow action on the color-sector coordinates over the exceptional divisor.

    The class encodes three pieces of structure:

    1. Additive composition in the flow parameter.
    2. Per-sector signed rate: each sector advances at angular rate $k\,q$, where $k \in \mathbb{Z}$ is a signed multiplier determined by the braid generator and the sector's role in the current two-strand subsystem. Concretely: $k = 0$ for spectator sectors; $k = +1$ for the "leading" active sector under a positively-oriented generator (e.g. red under $\sigma_1$); $k = -1$ for the "partner" active sector whose angular coordinate counter-rotates so the two-strand centre-of-mass is preserved (e.g. green under $\sigma_1$); $k = +1$ uniformly under the positive 3-cycle $\sigma_1\sigma_2$; $k = -1$ uniformly under the negative 3-cycle $\sigma_2\sigma_1$.

    The signed-rate dictionary between braid generators and per-sector multipliers is supplied by the consumer (see SixCanonicalKnotOperators).

        class QOU.CategoricalReeb.QReebFlowOnColorCoords (q : ℝ) :
        Type

        The $q$-Reeb flow on color-sector coordinates.

        flow q t k ψ is the angular position $\psi'$ of a sector after applying the flow $\phi^q_t$ to the starting position $\psi$, given the sector's signed rate multiplier k : ℤ under the current braid generator.

        Sectors with rate k advance at angular rate k * q; in particular k = 0 gives the spectator rule and k = ±1 the centre-of-mass-preserving two-strand action. Composition is additive in $t$.

        • flow : ℝ → ℤ → ℝ → ℝ

          The flow on the angular coordinate. k is the signed rate multiplier under the current braid generator.

        • flow_zero (k : ℤ) (ψ : ℝ) : flow q 0 k ψ = ψ

          Identity at $t = 0$ regardless of rate.

        • flow_add (t₁ t₂ : ℝ) (k : ℤ) (ψ : ℝ) : flow q (t₁ + t₂) k ψ = flow q t₂ k (flow q t₁ k ψ)

          Additive composition in the flow parameter (rate preserved across the composition step).

        • flow_rate (t ψ : ℝ) (k : ℤ) : flow q t k ψ = ψ + ↑k * q * t

          A sector with rate k advances at angular rate $k\,q$.
