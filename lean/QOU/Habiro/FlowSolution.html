    QOU.Habiro.FlowSolution #

    Axiomatisation of the substrate-level "three flow solutions" synthesis from rem:generating-functions-solve-reeb-flow: the $q$-exponential solves the base Reeb flow, the categorical Fourier transform diagonalises the $q$-heat equation, and the $q$-dilogarithm $\mathrm{Li}_2^q$ evaluates the volumetric expansion of the Reeb-heat flow. All three solutions live in the Habiro ring $\widehat{\mathbb{Z}[q]}$ and admit archimedean shadows under the realisation functor $\tau_\infty$.

    This class is the synthesis-level placeholder. The per-function existence/uniqueness content is already supplied by the existing Lean developments QOU.QExponential (prop:q-exponential-exists, prop:q-exponential-unique), QOU.QDilogarithm (prop:q-dilogarithm-exists, prop:q-dilogarithm-unique), QOU.CategoricalFourier (prop:categorical-fourier-exists). Those are structures (not typeclasses), so HabiroFlowSolution does not extend them — it bundles their proven content into a single joint-existence claim: the three are simultaneously the unique algebraic objects in the Habiro completion solving their respective Reeb-flow equations.

    class QOU.Habiro.HabiroFlowSolution :
    Type

    The synthesis-level Habiro flow-solution class. Conjectural: the individual fixed-point properties are supplied by the existing QOU.QExponential, QOU.QDilogarithm, and QOU.CategoricalFourier developments (structures, not typeclasses — HabiroFlowSolution does NOT extend them). This class records that the three hold simultaneously in a common Habiro completion and that their archimedean shadows under $\tau_\infty$ are mutually compatible (the $\tau_\infty$ of $\mathrm{Li}_2^q$ gives the Bloch–Wigner volume function used by prop:thurston-volume-additivity, while $\tau_\infty(\exp_q)$ gives the analytic exponential and $\tau_\infty(\mathcal{F}_q)$ gives the classical Fourier transform on the limit cocompact lattice).

    • expQ_solves_base_reeb : Prop

      $\exp_q$ is the unique Habiro-grouplike satisfying $D_q \exp_q = \exp_q$. Encoded as a proposition the consumer must supply.

    • fourier_diagonalises_qheat : Prop

      Categorical Fourier diagonalises $\Delta_q$ on the Peter–Weyl decomposition of $\mathbf{Rep}(\mathrm{SU}_q(2))$.

    • liTwoQ_volumetric : Prop

      $\mathrm{Li}_2^q$ is the formal Habiro antiderivative of $\log_q(1+t)/t$, giving the volumetric expansion of the Reeb-heat flow.

    • all_hold : expQ_solves_base_reeb ∧ fourier_diagonalises_qheat ∧ liTwoQ_volumetric

      The three propositions hold in the Habiro completion.
