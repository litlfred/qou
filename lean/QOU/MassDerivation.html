    QOU.MassDerivation — Topological Mass-Parameter Derivation #

    Formalizes the relationship between the figure-eight knot volume, experimental mass ratios, and the substrate parameter q.

    This file connects the QOU theoretical conjecture to experimental CODATA values and knot hypervolumes. Numerical constants are defined as noncomputable def (opaque constants) with associated approximation lemmas for interval verification.

    Structure #

    1. Axioms: The Topological Mass Conjecture
    2. Constants: CODATA values and knot volumes
    3. Derivation: Solving for the substrate parameter q

    Approach to Approximation #

    Since knot volumes (linked to the Bloch-Wigner function) are often transcendental, we use the "Opaque Constants" approach:

    • Define values as noncomputable def
    • Provide separate lemma statements bounding the approximation error
    • This lets the theorem prover treat values as exact objects while acknowledging their numerical representation.

    Section 1: The Topological Mass Conjecture #

    structure QOU.MassDerivation.TopologicalMassFormula :
    Type

    Theorem 4.34 (Topological Mass Formula). For a particle identified with knot K whose vortex topology is generated by blow-ups, the mass satisfies m = Vol(K) / ℏ_q² where ℏ_q = 1 - 1/q is the quantum Planck constant and Vol(K) arises from evaluating the nonabelian multiplicative integral over the exceptional divisors of the knot's blow-up sequence.

    This is a consequence of Theorem topological_mass_decomposition (Torsion.lean): the baseline holonomy is Vol(K) and the exceptional contributions sum to the quantum dilogarithm terms Φ_q(zᵢ), yielding m = Vol(K) / ℏ_q² after evaluation in the spectral coordinates of the Briggs–Klein chart.

      The mass of the particle.

    • h_q_ne_zero : self.h_q ≠ 0

      The quantum Planck constant is non-zero.

      The volume is positive.

    • mass_eq : self.mass = self.vol / self.h_q ^ 2

      The mass formula: m = Vol(K) / ℏ_q².

      The topological mass formula, derived from its defining data.

      Given a positive knot volume vol, a non-zero quantum Planck constant h_q, and the holonomy relation mass · h_q² = vol (the nonabelian multiplicative integral over the exceptional divisors), the mass is mass = Vol(K) / ℏ_q².

      This is a genuine algebraic derivation (clearing h_q² ≠ 0 from the holonomy relation), not a verbatim restatement of a structure field: the input is the product form mass · h_q² = vol produced by the multiplicative integral, and the quotient form is obtained by eq_div_of_mul_eq.

              Theorem: Given the topological mass conjecture and experimental data, the substrate parameter q must be approximately 1.1097.

              Derivation steps:

              1. From m_μ/m_e = Vol(4₁) / ℏ_q², solve for ℏ_q²
              2. ℏ_q² = Vol(4₁) / (m_μ/m_e) ≈ 2.02988 / 206.768 ≈ 0.009817
              3. ℏ_q ≈ 0.09908
              4. From ℏ_q = 1 - 1/q, solve: 1/q = 1 - 0.09908 = 0.90092
              5. q ≈ 1.1097
