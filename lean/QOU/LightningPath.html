    Lightning Path — Terminal Observational Synthesis #

    A single concluding remark that depends on all key observational results in the QOU formalization. Its purpose is twofold:

    1. Dependency graph: this declaration is the unique sink node. Tracing its transitive imports backwards yields the "lightning path" — the minimal subgraph of the proof forest connecting categorical axioms to CODATA observables.

    2. Evidence summary: the remark collects the five pillars of observational support proved elsewhere in the formalization:

      Pillar Source Key declaration
      Gauge group emergence VolumeForm SU2Emergence.det_one
      Self-dual instantons Instanton categorical_self_duality
      Topological mass decomposition TopologicalMass topological_mass_decomposition
      CODATA end-to-end chain CODATAChain end_to_end
      Descartes terminality ArchimedeanUniverse descartes_terminal

    Canonical-accuracy note. This synthesis node bundles the proved pillars, but the CODATA leg (end_to_end) rests on CODATA-pinned mass constants (HadronicMass) and the deprecated Vol(K)/ℏ_q² mass shape, not the canonical muon-anchored Hecke energy (computations/canonical_mass.py), and its binding row is a consistency identity rather than a prediction. Read pillars 3–4 below with those caveats — per-isotope binding is the still-open Δ_λ gate. Canonical sources: docs/canonical-mass-formula-guardrail.md, docs/canonical-derivation-cheat-sheet.md §9.

    Pillar 1 carries less than its heading (bean qou-szd6). Pillar 1 is stated below as "the volume constraint forces Aut⊗(ω) ≅ SU(2)". The conjunct actually proved is emg.ext_sq_action g = 𝟙 exteriorSquare, discharged by projecting the det_one field of QOU.SU2Emergence (VolumeForm.lean). That structure has three fields — an abstract gaugeGroupType, an action on exteriorSquare, and det_one — and exteriorSquare is an unconstrained parameter. It mentions neither SU(2) nor the rank of the fundamental object, so what this synthesis node checks is that a group acts trivially on some object, not that the gauge group is SU(2). The two supporting lemmas (SU2EmergenceVolumeDeterminant, SU2EmergenceDaggerUnitarity) are single-field classes restating their own conclusions, with docstrings recording the witness as pending a categorical-determinant formalisation in Mathlib.

    This is a gap in the formalisation, not a claim that the theorem is wrong — prf:su2-emergence is a real argument, but it takes the rank as an input to both of its lemmas rather than deriving it. QOU.BraidsAndKnots.VolumeConstraintForcesFibreTwo re-sources that rank from the categorical volume constraint of def:quantum-observable-universe; see bean qou-n1dj for what remains open.

    Remark (Lightning Path — Observational Evidence for QOU).

    The Quantum Observable Universe framework, starting from three categorical axioms (Frobenius self-duality, adjoint vanishing, volume constraint), produces a chain of predictions that match experiment:

    1. Gauge group: the volume constraint forces Aut⊗(ω) ≅ SU(2) (Theorem thm:su2-emergence).

    2. Self-dual instantons: every Yang–Mills minimiser on the QOU is an instanton with ⋆_q F = F (Proposition categorical_self_duality).

    3. Topological mass: a stable vortex's mass decomposes categorically as m = baseline + Σᵢ exceptional via the descent involution (Theorem topological_mass_decomposition). This categorical decomposition is not the canonical absolute-mass form: per the owner ruling (2026-06-24) the absolute nucleon mass is the muon-anchored Hecke energy m_p = 2·(m_μ·F_Bor − 6·m_e) with m_μ = m_e·Vol(4₁)/ℏ_q² and F_Bor = |tr_M(β_Bor)|·[3]_q!/q³, routed through computations/canonical_mass.py. The Vol(K)/ℏ_q² shape carried by ArchimedeanMassFormula is deprecated for absolute mass (read with K = 𝓑₃ it gives m_p ≈ 966 MeV, +2.96%; the λ_A linking weight is a fit). See docs/canonical-mass-formula-guardrail.md.

    4. CODATA agreement: the substrate parameter q ≈ 1.1097 is derived (not assumed) from Vol(4₁) and the muon/electron ratio, with |q − 1.1097| < 0.001 proved by interval arithmetic — this is the one genuinely-derived observable on this leg. The proton and neutron rows of end_to_end are CODATA-pinned reference constants (proton_topological := proton_mass_MeV; see the HadronicMass honesty note), so row1/row2_matches_CODATA are equalities-by- construction, not predictions. The deuteron and binding rows are a mass-deficit consistency identity m_p + m_n − B_exp ≈ m_D with B_exp = binding_energy_QOU the experimental deficit rounded to 4 s.f. — not an independent binding prediction. Canonically the per-isotope binding B(K) = m_e·Σ_λ y_λ χ^λ Δ_λ is the OPEN Δ_λ gate: derived only to ≈ −9% on the deuteron and off 44–72% for A ≥ 3 (docs/canonical-derivation-cheat-sheet.md §9b).

    5. Descartes terminality: the Descartes universe with χ = −6 is the unique rigid completion (descartes_terminal).

    This remark carries no new mathematical content — it exists solely as the terminal node of the dependency graph, enabling the "lightning path" visualization from axioms to observables.
