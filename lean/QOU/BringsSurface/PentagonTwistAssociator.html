    QOU.BringsSurface.PentagonTwistAssociator #

    Non-associativity as a pentagon (3-cocycle) deformation — the categorical route to octonion multiplication.

    Two independent deformations of a monoidal category, from its two coherence laws:

    deform constraint coherence law deformation datum algebraic home
    commutative → non-commutative braiding c hexagon (Yang–Baxter) R-matrix quantum groups (QOU's Hecke)
    associative → non-associative associator α pentagon (Mac Lane) 3-cocycle / Drinfeld Φ quasi-Hopf algebras (Drinfeld)

    The octonions realise the second row: 𝕆 = ℝ_F[(ℤ/2)³], the group algebra of (ℤ/2)³ (its 8 elements index e₀,…,e₇) twisted by a sign 2-cochain F that is not a 2-cocycle — its associator α = dF is a nonzero 3-cochain (a genuine 3-cocycle, though a coboundary, so cohomologically trivial; see OctonionCocycle) (Albuquerque–Majid 1999). So "the non-associative doubling ℍ → 𝕆" is precisely "turn on a twist F with dF ≠ 0" — a pentagon-cochain deformation, the associativity-side analogue of deforming the braiding (hexagon) to turn commutative into non-commutative.

    This file formalises the mechanism: for a graded twist e_a·e_b = F(a,b)·e_{a+b}, the associator is α = dF (the coboundary of F), and it automatically satisfies the pentagon because d² = 0. That is why a graded twist keeps Mac Lane coherence while breaking strict associativity. The specific octonion cochain F — whose associator α = dF is cohomologically trivial (a coboundary) yet nonzero as a cochain (non-associativity) — is realised concretely in OctonionCocycle (det3 on (ℤ/2)³, decide-verified).

    The grading group G and phase group k are written additively (cohomology is naturally additive; the octonion signs live in k = ℤ/2). Generic ([AddCommGroup G], [AddCommGroup k]; §7c), sorry-free.

      Pentagon holds automatically for a twist associator (d² = 0). For any 2-cochain twist, the associator α = dF satisfies the pentagon / 3-cocycle identity. This is the mechanism by which turning on a graded twist (the octonion (ℤ/2)³ cochain) breaks strict associativity while preserving Mac Lane coherence — the categorical form of "deform the pentagon."
