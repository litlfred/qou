    def QOU.BringsSurface.ColourCurveModulus.weierstrassR27 {R : Type u_1} [CommRing R] (z : R) :
    R

    The second Weierstrass coefficient of the colour elliptic fibre, as a polynomial in the spacetime ratio z. This is the form given in t-duality-elliptic.md: q_W(z) = 5(4z³ + 3z² + 3z + 4)/27, here cleared of its denominator so the statement lives over any CommRing.

      theorem QOU.BringsSurface.ColourCurveModulus.z_odd_inv {R : Type u_1} [CommRing R] (s s' : R) :
      s' - s = -(s - s')

      z = s - s⁻¹ is odd under s ↦ s⁻¹, the sign-preserving lift of q ↦ q⁻¹. Since weierstrassR27 is not even (bar_defect below), that lift is not a symmetry of the curve.

      theorem QOU.BringsSurface.ColourCurveModulus.bar_defect {R : Type u_1} [CommRing R] (z : R) :
      weierstrassR27 z - weierstrassR27 (-z) = 10 * z * (4 * z ^ 2 + 3)

      The bar defect. q ↦ q⁻¹ sends z ↦ -z, and the Weierstrass coefficient fails to be even by exactly 10 z (4z² + 3). This is the framework's matter/antimatter asymmetry of the colour fibration, and it is derived rather than posited.

      theorem QOU.BringsSurface.ColourCurveModulus.degenerate_iff (z : ℝ) :
      weierstrassR27 z = 0 ↔ z = -1

      The degeneration locus is the single point z = -1. The golden ratio enters only when this one condition is pulled back along z = s - s⁻¹ and q = s², where it acquires the two preimages q = φ⁻² and q = φ². rem:golden-ratio-degeneration records only the first.

      theorem QOU.BringsSurface.ColourCurveModulus.bar_symmetric_iff (z : ℝ) :
      weierstrassR27 z = weierstrassR27 (-z) ↔ z = 0

      The bar involution is a symmetry of the colour fibre exactly at z = 0. Together with z = s - s⁻¹, that is s = s⁻¹, i.e. q = 1: the matter and antimatter colour curves coincide only at the interface.
