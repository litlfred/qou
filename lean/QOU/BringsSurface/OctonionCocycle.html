    QOU.BringsSurface.OctonionCocycle #

    The concrete octonion associator on (ℤ/2)³ — a genuine pentagon (3-)cocycle.

    Completing the categorical route (PentagonTwistAssociator): the octonions are 𝕆 = ℝ_F[(ℤ/2)³], the (ℤ/2)³ group algebra twisted by a sign 2-cochain F. Its multiplication associator is the 𝔽₂ determinant (trilinear alternating form) φ(a,b,c) = det₂[a b c] — the sign rule "(e_a e_b)e_c = (-1)^φ · e_a(e_b e_c), i.e. − exactly on 𝔽₂-independent triples."

    Verified against the real octonions. Building 𝕆 by Cayley–Dickson (ℝ → ℂ → ℍ → 𝕆) and reading off the basis signs, the index law is XOR (the group is (ℤ/2)³) and the associativity defect equals det₂ exactly as a cochain; 168 of the 512 triples fail to associate.

    Written additively (values in ℤ/2; sign (-1)^φ), the pentagon / 3-cocycle condition is φ(b,c,d) + φ(a,b+c,d) + φ(a,b,c) = φ(a+b,c,d) + φ(a,b,c+d).

    • det3_is_3cocycle — φ satisfies the pentagon, verified exhaustively over all 8⁴ tuples (decide). Trilinearity telescopes the coboundary to 0, so the octonion associator is pentagon-coherent — a bona fide 3-cocycle.
    • det3_nonzero — the deformation genuinely fires: on the independent triple e₁, e₂, e₄ the associator is 1 ≠ 0, i.e. (e₁e₂)e₄ = −e₁(e₂e₄) (non-associativity).

    Cohomology (correcting an earlier over-claim). φ is a coboundary φ = dF, so [φ] = 0 in H³((ℤ/2)³; ℤ/2) — the octonion multiplication associator is cohomologically trivial (verified: φ equals the coboundary of the Cayley–Dickson sign cochain, exactly). There is therefore no H³-nontriviality to prove: non-associativity is φ ≠ 0 as a cochain (F is not a 2-cocycle), not an H³ phenomenon. The gauge e_a ↦ λ_a e_a changes F by dλ and leaves φ = dF fixed, so 𝕆 is non-associative under every basis rescaling even though [φ] = 0. (The nontrivial H³ 3-cocycle of Albuquerque–Majid 1999 is the category associator making 𝕆 associative in Vect_{(ℤ/2)³}^ω, a distinct object from this multiplication associator; not claimed here.) Generic ZMod 2 (§7c), sorry-free.

          theorem QOU.BringsSurface.OctonionCocycle.det3_cyclic_invariant (a b c : V) :
          det3 (cyc a) (cyc b) (cyc c) = det3 a b c

          Fano / PSL(2,7) symmetry of the octonion associator (witness). det3 is invariant under the coordinate 3-cycle cyc ∈ GL(3, 𝔽₂). More generally det3 (g·a) (g·b) (g·c) = det(g) · det3 a b c = det3 a b c for every g ∈ GL(3, 𝔽₂) ≅ PSL(2,7) (order 168 — the Fano-plane / Klein-quartic symmetry), since det g = 1 over 𝔽₂. This is the concrete tie between the (ℤ/2)³ octonion sign structure and the genus-3 (Klein-quartic, PSL(2,7) = Fano) home of the octonion product. Here the cyc generator is machine-checked; the full GL(3,𝔽₂)-invariance is determinant multiplicativity.
