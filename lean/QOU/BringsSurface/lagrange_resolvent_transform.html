    QOU.BringsSurface.lagrange_resolvent_transform — discrete Fourier on C_n #

    Proposition (prop:lagrange-resolvent-transform).

    Let C_n act on a vector space V of dimension n with coordinates x_0, …, x_{n-1}, and let ζ = exp(2πi/n). The Lagrange resolvent transform (discrete Fourier transform on C_n) is

      w_k = (1/√n) · Σ_{j=0}^{n-1} ζ^{kj} · x_j,    k ∈ {0, …, n-1}.

    Then:

    (i) The cyclic permutation σ : x_j ↦ x_{j+1} acts diagonally: σ(w_k) = ζ^{−k} · w_k — the C_n symmetry is manifest as a phase rotation of the Fourier modes.

    (ii) Any C_n-invariant polynomial constraint on the x_j transforms to a constraint on C_n-equivariant combinations of the w_k (pairings w_k · w_{n-k} of conjugate modes).

    The Lean encoding gives the definition of the transform on Fin n → ℂ and discharges item (i) as the σ-equivariance claim; item (ii) is recorded abstractly.
