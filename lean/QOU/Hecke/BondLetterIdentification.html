    Interior bond factors for the σ, σ⁻¹ and symmetric letters #

    Bean qou-qqm4. The bond product (tr_bondWord_of_braided, and Mass.ProductionChainBondProduct for the production layout) carries an arbitrary scalar d per bond. This file pins d for three specific letters, in the interior position a bond actually occupies.

    What is proved #

    tr_bond_interp — the headline. For the colour superposition σ_t := (1−t)·σ + t·σ⁻¹, the interior bond factor is z − h·t, for every t : R. Every letter in the corpus is an instance: t = 0 is σ (p-p), t = 1 is σ⁻¹ (n-n), t = 1/2 is averaged, and t = 1/2 − δ/4 is averaged_weighted, the production p-n letter.

    This is the trace-level counterpart of MassTheory.DeuteronKernel.interfaceFactor, which defines f(q,t) = z_D − h·t as a scalar. Here it is a theorem about the Markov trace on the tower, with material on both sides of the bond.

    So the factor law is settled for the production letter as well, and what qou-qqm4 leaves measured about the bond model reduces to the single value t = 1/2 − δ/4.

    tr_bond_gen — the σ letter carries d = 0: factor z. InteriorMarkov restated at the bond; here so the three sit together.

    tr_bond_ginv — the content. The σ⁻¹ letter carries d = −h: factor z − h. The skein relation σ⁻¹ = σ − h (gen_inv_eq_of_quadratic) fed through tr_bond_of_interiorMarkov at d = −h.

    tr_bond_gen_add_ginv — the unnormalised symmetric letter σ + σ⁻¹ carries 2z − h, i.e. z − h/2 per letter wherever 2 is invertible. Stated unnormalised so it needs no 2⁻¹ in R.

    bond_factors_of_skein_letters — the three collected.

    All are stated over a MarkovTrace carrying [BraidedTower], so InteriorMarkov is discharged by interiorMarkov_of_braided rather than assumed: the hypotheses are the tower's defining relations, the Markov axioms, and the quadratic scalar h.

    Why these are not already in the corpus #

    The corpus has both halves of the σ⁻¹ fact but never composes them, and it has the composition only in right-multiplication form:

    • MathlibExt.OcneanuTrace.tr_mul_ginv_of_quadratic — tr (a * ginv n) = (z − h) * tr a. The b = 1 case.
    • MathlibExt.DisjointBlockTraceLaw.tr_mul_gen_comb_of_quadratic — the general combination tr (a * (c • g n + c' • ginv n)) = tr a * ((c+c')·z − c'·h). Also b = 1.
    • mass-theory/interface-knot-markov-z.lean — over a different structure (MarkovHeckeSkeinAlgebra), and takes trace-linearity as a hypothesis.

    A bond carries material on both sides, so the bond product needs the interior form with b ∈ upper g. The structural reason it is missing is that MarkovInteriorBond works in MarkovTower, which has no ginv field at all.

    🛑 THE pn LETTER IS NOT THE SYMMETRIC ONE — corrected before merge #

    An earlier version of this file, and the first body of its PR, identified the production pn interface with the symmetric letter, i.e. d_pn = −h/2. That is wrong, and the corpus had already ruled it out on 2026-09-07. The production letter is averaged_weighted:

    d_pn = −h · (1/2 − δ/4),   δ = (q−1)²(1 − q⁻⁴)/3

    carried by Hecke.ClosedFormInterfaceFactors.bondD, MathlibExt.MarkovBondRule.dIface, computations/canonical_braid_crossings.py and computations/markov_peel.py. −h/2 is the corpus's averaged letter — a different ctype, and not the production one.

    The two agree through order ε³ and separate at ε⁴:

    ε⁰ ε¹ ε² ε³ ε⁴
    z − h/2 (symmetric) 1/2 0 3/4 −3/4 5/8
    z + d_pn (production) 1/2 0 3/4 −3/4 31/24
    measured pn bond 1/2 0 3/4 −3/4 31/24

    computations/probes/bond_pn_kind_order_four_discriminator_probe.py is the discriminator, on two objects plus a held-out ³He word. The error is instructive and is why this block exists: an ε² check passes both, so agreeing to the order you happened to expand to is not evidence of identity.

    tr_bond_gen_add_ginv is therefore a theorem about the averaged letter, and is not a statement about the production pn interface.

    🛑 What is NOT proved #

    1. The value t = 1/2 − δ/4 is not derived here. tr_bond_interp settles the factor law for every letter including the production one, so what remains is precisely the colour weight's value — not the form of its contribution. δ = (q−1)²(1−q⁻⁴)/3 = ℏ_q²·h_q·[2]_q/3 is the substrate flavour split (prop:flavor-split-substrate, i.e. Δm/M_N), so it is assembled from corpus quantities rather than fitted; why the weight is 1/2 − δ/4 specifically is qou-qqm4's live open item.
    2. h is a hypothesis, not a value. That h = q − q⁻¹ for H_n(q), and that the substrate's z is z_D = q³/(q²+1), are elsewhere. BraidedTower.quad is existential (∀ m, ∃ h, …) and cannot supply a uniform h, which is why it is an explicit argument.
    3. No c_k, no binding coefficient, no absolute trace. The bond model is about the ratio tr(atom)/tr(free).
    4. Nothing here reaches the production Ocneanu trace. The only MarkovTrace on the presented tower factors through a rank-2 quotient; qou-0fmb is the bridge.

    What the production weight t = 1/2 − δ/4 equals, and why that is not #

    yet a derivation

    observations/flavor-split-substrate defines the substrate flavour split as

    δ = (m_n − m_p) / M_N,      M_N = (m_p + m_n)/2

    — the mean nucleon mass in the denominator. Under that normalisation the production weight is not an opaque offset: it is exactly the proton's share of the pair's mass. The two theorems below record the identity and the reason it does not promote to a rule.

    source
    theorem QOU.Hecke.BondLetterIdentification.tPN_eq_proton_mass_fraction (mp mn : ℝ) (hpos : mp + mn ≠ 0) :
    1 / 2 - (mn - mp) / ((mp + mn) / 2) / 4 = mp / (mp + mn)

    The production p-n weight is the proton's mass fraction.

    With δ = (m_n − m_p)/M_N at M_N = (m_p + m_n)/2 — the corpus's own normalisation — the canonical colour weight satisfies

    t = 1/2 − δ/4 = m_p / (m_p + m_n)

    identically. Dually 1 − t = m_n/(m_p + m_n), so the σ branch carries the neutron's fraction and the σ⁻¹ branch the proton's: the split is cross-weighted, the shape a barycentric lever arm takes.

    🛑 This is an algebraic identity, not a derivation. It re-expresses δ/4 in the corpus's own quantities and supplies no mechanism; what it buys is that the open coefficient now has a name and a candidate reading that can be tested. The test is massFraction_like_pair, immediately below, and it fails.

    source
    theorem QOU.Hecke.BondLetterIdentification.massFraction_like_pair (m : ℝ) (hm : m ≠ 0) :
    m / (m + m) = 1 / 2

    …and the mass-fraction reading does not extend to the like pairs, which is what stops the identity above from being a derivation.

    A mass-fraction rule assigns any like pair the weight m/(m+m) = 1/2. The corpus assigns t = 0 to pp (the letter is σ) and t = 1 to nn (σ⁻¹) — see tr_bond_gen and tr_bond_ginv. So the rule contradicts both like-pair letters, and cannot be the mechanism fixing t in general.

    A consistent reading survives and is not proved here: the like pairs are fixed by exchange symmetry (identical particles admit no interpolation), and only the unlike pair has a free weight, displaced from the symmetric 1/2 by the flavour asymmetry. That is a story about why δ appears, not a derivation of the factor 1/4. Bean qou-qqm4.

    The like-pair bond factors, and where 1 − q⁻⁴ in δ comes from #

    At the canonical z_D = q³/(q²+1) and h = q − q⁻¹ the two like-pair factors are not independent numbers: the nn one is q⁻⁴ times the pp one. That single identity accounts for the (1 − q⁻⁴) factor of the substrate flavour split, which is otherwise an unexplained substrate expression.

    The definitions are inlined as hypotheses rather than imported so this section costs no new dependency; they are MassTheory.DeuteronKernel.zD, .hq and .flavorDelta verbatim.

    source
    theorem QOU.Hecke.BondLetterIdentification.nnFactor_eq_ppFactor_div_q_pow_four (q : ℝ) (hq : 0 < q) :
    q ^ 3 / (q ^ 2 + 1) - (q - q⁻¹) = q ^ 3 / (q ^ 2 + 1) / q ^ 4

    The nn bond factor is q⁻⁴ times the pp one.

    z_D − h = 1/(q(q²+1)) against z_D = q³/(q²+1), so their ratio is exactly q⁻⁴. Each pp → nn substitution multiplies a bond product by q⁻⁴.

    🛑 This is a FORMALISATION of an identity the corpus already had, not a new one — credit upstream. computations/qbeta/qbeta_bond_factors_derived_probe.py records b_pp/b_nn − q⁴ = 0 as its identity 2, established symbolically, and b_pp − z = 0 as its identity 1 under an assertion named b_pp_IS_the_markov_parameter. Both predate this file. What is added here is that the two are the same law at t = 0 and t = 1 of tr_bond_interp — identity 2 is tr_bond_ginv divided by tr_bond_gen — and that it holds for a bond carrying material on both sides, which that probe does not address.

    All three of that file's bond constants are b(t) = z − h·t, exactly, as rational functions of q: b_pp = q³/(q²+1) at t = 0, its degree-10 b_pn at t = 1/2 − δ/4, and b_nn = 1/(q³+q) at t = 1. Checked in computations/qbeta/qbeta_bond_factors_are_one_interpolation_probe.py (4/4). That also separates the production pn ctype from averaged symbolically (b(1/2) − b_pn ≠ 0), which is a stronger form of this file's correction 1 than the ε⁴ discriminator it was resting on.

    theorem QOU.Hecke.BondLetterIdentification.flavorSplit_eq_bondGap (q : ℝ) (hq : 0 < q) :
    (q - 1) ^ 2 * (1 - (q ^ 4)⁻¹) / 3 = (q - 1) ^ 2 * ((q - q⁻¹) / (q ^ 3 / (q ^ 2 + 1))) / 3

    1 − q⁻⁴ is the relative gap between the like-pair bond factors, i.e. h / z_D exactly.

    So the substrate flavour split factorises through the bond model's own quantities:

    δ = (q−1)² · (h / z_D) / 3

    🛑 An identity, not a derivation — the same class as tPN_eq_proton_mass_fraction. It says the (1 − q⁻⁴) in δ is not an independent substrate expression but the like-pair bond gap; it does not explain the remaining (q−1)²/3, and it says nothing at all about the 1/4 in t = 1/2 − δ/4, which is what qou-qqm4 leaves open.

    theorem QOU.Hecke.BondLetterIdentification.likePairBondProduct_eq_z_pow {R : Type u_3} [CommRing R] (zpp znn c : R) (hrel : znn * c = zpp) (a b : ℕ) :
    zpp ^ a * znn ^ b * c ^ b = zpp ^ (a + b)

    The like-pair bond product collapses to a power of z alone.

    With a proton-proton bonds and b neutron-neutron bonds, z^a·(z−h)^b·q^(4b) = z^(a+b): every nn bond contributes the same z as a pp bond, up to one factor q⁻⁴. Division-free, so it needs no nonvanishing side conditions beyond 0 < q.

    The mirror-pair law is the immediate corollary. A mirror pair (Z,N) and (N,Z) in canonical pppnnn ordering has (a,b) = (Z−1, N−1) and (N−1, Z−1), and one pn bond each — common, hence cancelling. So

    tr(Z,N) / tr(N,Z)  =  q^(−4(N−Z))

    exactly, independent of the pn convention: δ does not appear. Verified symbolically at (Z,N) = (1,2), (2,3), (1,3), (2,4), (3,5), (4,6) and at the self-mirror (1,1), (2,2), where it gives 1.

    Swapping the two like-pair letters swaps a and b, which inverts the ratio. Nothing else in the bond model distinguishes a proton from a neutron — that scope is exact and load-bearing; see the correction below.

What is new here is only the closed form q^(4(Z−N)) for the bare bond-product ratio, and its generalisation off A = 3; the cancellation itself is not.
