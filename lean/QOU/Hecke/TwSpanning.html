    QOU.Hecke.TwSpanning — the orbit of T_1 is everything #

    The spanning half of the Bourbaki route to the T_w basis (bean qou-twwf). QOU.Hecke.TwBasis builds the right-multiplication operators Rs i on W →₀ R; this file shows that starting from the single basis vector T_1 and applying them repeatedly reaches every T_w.

    Unlike the braid relation, this half needs no type-A specialisation: Mathlib has right descents (CoxeterSystem.exists_rightDescent_of_ne_one, isRightDescent_iff), which is exactly what the induction on length wants.

    What is proved #

    • single_mem_of_closed — any submodule containing T_1 and closed under every Rs i contains every T_w.
    • span_orbit_eq_top — the smallest such submodule is ⊤.

    Together with the algebra map of QOU.Hecke.TwRep this says the module is a cyclic H_n(q)-module generated by T_1; the independence of the T_w is the other half and is not proved here.

    Every T_w is reachable from T_1. A submodule holding T_1 and closed under every Rs i holds every basis vector.
