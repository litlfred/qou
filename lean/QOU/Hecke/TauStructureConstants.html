    QOU.Hecke.TauStructureConstants — τ(T_v T_w) = δ_{w,v⁻¹} q^{ℓ(v)} #

    The hypothesis that QOU.Hecke.TauSymmetrizing carried, discharged. With it the Gram determinant of the canonical trace becomes unconditional, and H_n(q) is a symmetric Frobenius algebra at every q ≠ 0.

    Ref: [geckpfeiffer2000] §8.1. The citation is attribution — Geck and Pfeiffer proved this; what follows is a Lean proof of their statement.

    The induction is on ℓ(v) alone, and that matters #

    8.1.1 is usually run on ℓ(v) + ℓ(w), choosing a simple reflection that is a descent of both factors. Producing such a common descent needs the exchange condition, and Mathlib has no exchange condition — measured, there is no occurrence of xchange anywhere in Mathlib/GroupTheory/Coxeter/.

    It is not needed. Induct on ℓ(v), take any left descent i of v (CoxeterSystem.exists_leftDescent_of_ne_one), and split on whether i happens to be a right descent of w. Both branches close because of one observation:

    a left descent of v and a right descent of w at the same i are contradictory once w is pinned to v⁻¹ or to v⁻¹ s_i, since ℓ(s_i v) = ℓ(v⁻¹ s_i) (CoxeterSystem.length_inv).

    In the ascent branch that kills the only surviving delta, so both sides vanish; in the descent branch it kills the (q-1) term, leaving exactly q times the inductive value. The (q-1) term never contributes at all — which is precisely why no common descent is required.

    Handedness #

    This corpus's T_a · T_b = T_{ba} (see QOU.Hecke.TwMul; it is a fact about the orbit representation, not a typo), so the decompositions read T_v = T_{s v} · T_s at a left descent and T_w = T_s · T_{w s} at a right one. Both are Tw_mul_Tw under the length hypothesis the descent supplies.

    What is proved #

    • permCoxeterSystem_simple / Tw_simple — the bridges to σ and to the concrete transposition.
    • Tw_simple_mul_self — T_s² = (q-1) T_s + q, the v = s case of σ_mul_Tw.
    • three decomposition lemmas at a descent / an ascent.
    • tau_Tw_mul_Tw — the identity.
    • tauGram_det / tauGram_det_ne_zero — the conditional results of TauSymmetrizing, now unconditional.

    What this still does NOT give #

    det(τ-Gram) ≠ 0 for every q ≠ 0 is not semisimplicity, which fails at roots of unity. It is Frobenius-ness. The Schur-element criterion needs, in addition, that τ's Wedderburn weights are 1/s_λ; that identification is where q enters and is not here. SchurElementCriterion remains a cited class with no instance.

    The identity #

    theorem QOU.Hecke.TwBasis.tau_Tw_mul_Tw (K : Type u_1) [Field K] (q : K) (n : ℕ) (v w : Equiv.Perm (Fin (n + 1))) :
    (tau K q n) (Tw K q n v * Tw K q n w) = if w = v⁻¹ then q ^ (SymmetricCoxeter.permCoxeterSystem n).length v else 0

    τ(T_v · T_w) = δ_{w, v⁻¹} · q^{ℓ(v)} — Geck–Pfeiffer §8.1.

    Ref: [geckpfeiffer2000]. Proved by induction on ℓ(v) with an arbitrary left descent; no exchange condition is used. See the module docstring.

    τ is a trace #

    Immediate from the identity, and not available before it: swapping v and w in δ_{w,v⁻¹} q^{ℓ(v)} gives δ_{v,w⁻¹} q^{ℓ(w)}, and those agree because w = v⁻¹ ↔ v = w⁻¹ and ℓ(v⁻¹) = ℓ(v). This is what makes τ a symmetrizing trace rather than merely a linear functional, and it is what makes the block weights of a Wedderburn decomposition well defined.
