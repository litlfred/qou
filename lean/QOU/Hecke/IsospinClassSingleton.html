            theorem QOU.Hecke.IsospinClassSingleton.two_two_survives_row_cap :
            [2, 2].length ≤ 2

            (2,2) also has at most two rows, so it survives convention D's row cap — the one self-conjugate shadow price QOU/Hecke/SelfConjugateRowCap.lean leaves standing.

            The charge-type class under the row cap is also a singleton #

            The same question for c(λ) < 0. For a two-row shape (a,b) with b ≤ a, summing contents row by row gives 2·c(a,b) = a(a-1) + b(b-3), and that is negative for exactly one pair.

            (1,1) at n = 2 is therefore the unique charge-type shape the row cap admits — so "charge-type at its level" forces a partition just as "isospin-type at the quartet level" does. The selection principle transfers.

            What does not transfer is the number. m_e·H_q((1,1)) = m_e[2]_q = 1.0276 MeV against a_C ≈ 0.711 — 44 % off — while the corpus uses λ_C = h[6]_q·m_e = 0.68244 (−4 %), which is not m_e·H_q(λ) for any partition with n ≤ 14. Measured in computations/qbeta/qbeta_charge_class_singleton_probe.py, where the same sweep finds that six of the seven coefficients in mass-formula-regime-switching-table1-data are not hook products at all: they carry integer prefactors, a bare h = q - 1/q, or a quotient, none of which a product of [h]_q yields. Only δ_S = [2]_q²[3]_q = H_q((2,2)) matches.

            That is worth stating carefully. unified-iterative-correction-proof.md asserts C_λ = m_e·H_q(λ) as "the universal constant determined by the partition algebra", without restriction to a family — yet its own (2,1) ⊢ 3 clause says the volume/surface/Coulomb coefficients arise "through their image under the Wedderburn embedding", an unspecified transformation. So the two cannot both be read at face value, and the bridge between them is unspecified rather than contradicted. Recorded as a gap, not a refutation. Bean qou-orjl.
