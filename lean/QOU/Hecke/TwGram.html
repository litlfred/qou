    QOU.Hecke.TwGram — the regular-trace Gram matrix of H_n(q) in the T_w basis #

    First brick of bean qou-0y6h (Geck–Pfeiffer det(Gram) = ∏ Schur), and the immediate consequence of the T_w basis landing.

    QOU.MathlibExt.RegTraceGram.isSemisimpleRing_of_regTraceGram_det_ne_zero has been waiting for a basis since it was written; its own docstring says so:

    "This is the exact hook a T_w-basis Gram computation plugs into: supply the basis b (brick qou-lrz1) and the determinant value det = [n]_q! (brick qou-twwf), and semisimplicity of H_n(q) follows — no further Hecke input."

    QOU.Hecke.TwBasis.basisTwOfField is that basis. This file plugs it in.

    What is proved #

    • twGram — the Gram matrix of the regular trace form in the T_w basis, indexed by Equiv.Perm (Fin (n+1)) × Equiv.Perm (Fin (n+1)).
    • twGram_apply — its (v, w) entry is tr(L_{T_v T_w}), an observable.
    • isSemisimpleRing_of_twGram_det_ne_zero — **det ≠ 0 ⟹ H_n(q) is semisimple**, at every n`.

    What this does NOT do #

    It does not compute the determinant. That is the remaining content of qou-0y6h — Geck–Pfeiffer 7.2.6, det(Gram) = ∏_λ (Schur_λ)^{f^λ} — and it is the hard half. What changed is only that the statement can now be made: before the basis there was no finite index set to take a determinant over, which is why the 2026-08-29 gap inventory recorded det(Gram) as not even stateable.

    Nothing here bears on the P≠NP wall; semisimplicity is a magnitude-axis fact.
