    QOU.Hecke.InstanceOnH3A — the HeckeAlgebra class, on the tree's own H₃(q) #

    QOU.Machinery.HeckeH3AlgebraInstance.H3A is the tree's three-strand Hecke algebra: a Ring and Algebra on the six-dimensional normal-form module, with the presentation proved in QOU.Machinery.HeckeH3AlgebraLaws (mulσ₀_hecke, mulσ₁_hecke, braid_operator) and mulNF_assoc for associativity. What it never had is an instance of the class QOU.Hecke.HeckeAlgebra, so every [HeckeAlgebra R n q A] hypothesis in the corpus stayed unwitnessed at n = 3.

    This file supplies it, on that carrier rather than on a new one. Together with QOU.Hecke.isHeckeOfFullRank_H3A (rank 6 = 3!) it gives the pair that actually pins the algebra down: the relations plus the dimension. Bean qou-ti7p.

    The basis, and where the generators sit #

    H3A R h wraps v : Fin 6 → R on the normal-form basis [γ, σ₀, σ₁, L₊, L₋, e⁻], and mulNF decomposes its right argument, applying the composite generator action of each basis element on the left one. So

    σ₀ = e₁,  σ₁ = e₂,  σ₁σ₀ = e₃,  σ₀σ₁ = e₄,  σ₀σ₁σ₀ = e₅,

    which is what sigma_mul_* below record and what makes spans a finite check rather than an argument.

    Far-commutativity is vacuous here, and that is a fact about n = 3 #

    Generator 3 carries idx : Fin 2, so no two generator indices differ by ≥ 2 and Generator.farCommuting is never satisfied — exactly as HeckeH3AlgebraLaws records ("far commutation is vacuous with two generators"). The first level where it has content is n = 4.
