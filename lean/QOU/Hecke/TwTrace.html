    QOU.Hecke.TwTrace — the regular trace in the T_w basis #

    Fourth brick of bean qou-0y6h, and the first step of the route the 2026-08-30 scoping audit redirected to.

    QOU.Hecke.TwGram reduces semisimplicity of H_n(q) at every rank to det (twGram) ≠ 0. The entries of that matrix are tr(L_{T_v T_w}), so the determinant computation starts by making tr(L_x) explicit in the basis.

    What is proved #

    • repr_Tw — the basis coordinates of a basis vector, δ_{v,w}.
    • trace_lmul_eq_sum — tr(L_x) = Σ_w ⟨x · T_w⟩_w, the diagonal coefficients.
    • repr_σ_mul_Tw_diag — that coefficient for x = σ_i is 0 when the length goes up and q - 1 when it goes down. Only descents contribute.
    • trace_lmul_σ — hence tr(L_{σ_i}) = (q-1) · #{w : w has a descent at i}.

    The last is a genuine value, not a restatement: the trace of a generator is counted by descents, and the count is a purely combinatorial quantity.

    The closed form #

    • card_descent_eq_card_ascent, two_mul_card_descent — exactly half the permutations have a descent at i, by the involution w ↦ w · s_i.
    • two_mul_trace_lmul_σ — hence 2·tr(L_{σ_i}) = (q-1)·(n+1)!, in closed form. Stated doubled so no division is needed and it holds in any characteristic.

    What is NOT here #

    The general tr(L_{T_u}) for u beyond a single generator, and the determinant itself.

    The regular trace is the sum of the diagonal coefficients of left multiplication, read in the T_w basis.

    Only descents contribute. The diagonal coefficient of σ_i · T_w is 0 when the length goes up and q - 1 when it goes down — the q-term of the down case lands on T_{w s_i}, off the diagonal.
