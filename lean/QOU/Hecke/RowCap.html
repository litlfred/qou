    1. A column filling #

    The only structure the cap uses: a strictly increasing assignment of colours from Fin N to the r boxes of one column.

    source
    @[reducible, inline]
    abbrev QOU.Hecke.RowCap.ColumnFilling (r N : ℕ) :
    Type

    A strictly increasing filling of a column of r boxes by colours in Fin N. This is the restriction to the first column of a semistandard Young tableau of shape λ with entries in {1, …, N}, where r = ℓ(λ).

      The cap as a biconditional: a column of r boxes is fillable from N colours exactly when r ≤ N. The forward direction is the pigeonhole above; the reverse exhibits Fin.castLE as a witness.
