    InteriorMarkov is a theorem of every braided tower — the qou-gr3t gap closes onto qou-301d #

    Bean qou-gr3t. QOU.Hecke.MarkovInteriorBond proves the general-A bond product for the canonical non-monotone atom word conditionally on InteriorMarkov — the rule tr (a σ_g b) = z · tr (a b) for a below the bond g and b generated by the letters strictly above it — and shows by counter-model (cmTower) that InteriorMarkov does not follow from the MarkovTower axioms alone. The bean's stated next step was to prove InteriorMarkov for a tower that carries the Hecke relations.

    Meanwhile qou-301d landed the chain

    BraidedTower T  ⟹  SandwichReduces  ⟹  TowerSpan  ⟹  DisjointBlockMultiplicative T

    (QOU.MathlibExt.HeckeSandwichReduces.disjointBlock_of_braided), where BraidedTower is the three defining relations of the Iwahori–Hecke algebra — quadratic, far-commutation, braid — and nothing about the trace. This file closes the loop:

    • interiorMarkov_of_disjointBlock — InteriorMarkov follows from the disjoint-block law (plus tr 1 = 1). With b above the bond, σ_g · b is generated by the letters at level ≥ g, so tr (a σ_g b) = tr a · tr (σ_g b); σ_g ∈ H (g+1) and b is at level ≥ g+1, so tr (σ_g b) = tr σ_g · tr b = z · tr b; and tr (a b) = tr a · tr b. Three applications of the block law and the top-of-tower Markov axiom at a = 1.
    • toTower — an Ocneanu MarkovTrace with far-commuting generators is a MarkovTower (forget ginv, zinv, markov_inv; keep everything else); toTowerB is the same for a BraidedTower, whose far field supplies the commutation.
    • interiorMarkov_of_braided — InteriorMarkov holds on every braided tower. The bespoke hypothesis of qou-gr3t is a theorem of the Hecke relations.
    • tr_bondWord_of_braided — hence the general-A bond product tr (bondWord) = (∏ (z + d_k)) · tr (freeWord) holds on every braided tower, with no trace hypothesis beyond the Markov axioms; in particular on the presented Hecke tower heckeTowerMarkovTrace h z hz with h ≠ 0 (tr_bondWord_heckeTower) and on the symmetric-group tower (tr_bondWord_symTrace), the two BraidedTower instances the corpus carries.

    🛑 What is NOT established — the remaining distance is a bridge, not a theorem #

    The bond product is now a theorem for every Markov trace on every braided tower. The trace the corpus computes with — the Ocneanu trace of H_n(q) with z = q³/(q²+1) — is constructed and proved unique in SchurWeyl/MarkovTraceInstance.lean / MarkovTraceUnique.lean (ocneanuMarkovTrace, markovTrace_unique), but as a HeckeCoset.MarkovTraceFamily, a different abstraction from the OcneanuTrace.MarkovTrace this file and the qou-301d chain consume; nothing in the tree yet packages that family as an OcneanuTrace.MarkovTrace on the presented tower (PR #7258's correction of qou-301d; bean qou-0fmb). The only OcneanuTrace.MarkovTrace on the presented tower in the tree, heckeTowerMarkovTrace, factors through the rank-2 quotient and is not faithful. So: the bond product holds for the production trace once that bridge is built, and the bridge is a packaging statement, not new mathematics. This file removes the bespoke hypothesis qou-gr3t carried; what is left is the one packaging gap the corpus already names. The InteriorMarkov counter-model cmTower is unaffected: it is not braided (σ_n = 1 for n ≥ 3 and X 2 * X 1 * X 2 ≠ X 1 * X 2 * X 1), which is why it could refute the rule.
