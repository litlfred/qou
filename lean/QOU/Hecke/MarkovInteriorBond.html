    The interior-bond rule for NON-monotone block words: what the Markov axioms #

    do and do not give #

    QOU.MathlibExt.MarkovUnusedGenerator.tr_wordProd_mul_sigma derives the bond rule tr (W * σ_g) = z * tr W from the Markov-tower axioms for a monotone word W omitting g. QOU.Hecke.MarkovBondFactor records that the general A-bond factorisation "needs the cross-level Markov axiom (or a word-induction principle) that the class does not yet carry". Bean qou-gr3t asks for the statement the compute actually measured (route C step 3, n = 9, exact in q): with a a word on the strands below an interior bond generator σ_g and b an arbitrary — in particular non-monotone — word on the strands above it,

    tr (a * σ_g * b) = z * tr (a * b). (S1)

    This file settles what the tower axioms can and cannot say about (S1).

    Results #

    • InteriorMarkov T — (S1) as a Prop about a tower, with a ∈ H g and b in the subalgebra generated by σ_{g+1}, σ_{g+2}, … (the block above the bond starts at σ_{g+1}, adjacent to the bond — this is the nucleon geometry of the probe, where nucleon k uses σ_{3k}, σ_{3k+1} and the bond is σ_{3k+2}). It is a hypothesis, never a field and never an axiom.
    • tr_bond_of_interiorMarkov — (S2): the (z + d) bond letter, conditional on InteriorMarkov. Pure linearity on top of (S1).
    • tr_bondWord_of_interiorMarkov — (S3): the A-bond product tr (x₀ (σ_{g₀}+d₀) x₁ (σ_{g₁}+d₁) ⋯ x_A) = ∏ (z + d_k) · tr (x₀ x₁ ⋯ x_A) for strictly increasing bonds with blocks in between, conditional on InteriorMarkov, by induction on the number of bonds. This is the identity the probe measured, at full generality of the blocks.
    • interiorMarkov_ascending — the axioms do give (S1) when b is an ascending word σ_{g+1} σ_{g+2} ⋯ σ_{g+k} (iterated stabilisation), for an arbitrary a ∈ H g. This is the monotone case of qou-g8i1 in the a * σ_g * b shape.
    • exists_not_interiorMarkov — the axioms do NOT give (S1) in general. A Markov tower is exhibited in which tr (σ_0 * σ_2 ^ 2) = 0 while z * tr (σ_2 ^ 2) = z = 1. The witness b = σ_2² even lies in ⟨σ_{g+2}, …⟩, i.e. commutes with σ_g by far-commutation, so the stronger "commuting-b" form of (S1) fails too (exists_not_interiorMarkovFar).

    What the counter-model shows #

    The interior-bond rule for non-monotone b is not a consequence of the trace axioms (tower, far-commutation, cyclicity, Markov at the top). Every model in which it holds — the Iwahori–Hecke tower first among them — holds it because of structure the tower axioms do not carry: the quadratic relation σ² = h σ + 1 (which is exactly what kills the monomial σ_2² in the counter-model) together with the braid relation and the resulting spanning H_{n+1} = H_n + H_n σ_n H_n, i.e. Ocneanu's uniqueness argument. The "cross-level Markov axiom" named in MarkovBondFactor is therefore not derivable at the trace-axiom level: it is either an additional hypothesis (as InteriorMarkov is stated here) or a theorem of the Hecke algebra proved through its basis. That theorem is not in this file and is not claimed.

    The counter-model is commutative and degenerate as a model of braids — the point is only that the axioms admit it. Its trace is the monomial functional f(μ) = z^{|μ|} on squarefree monomials, f(X₂²) = 1, and 0 otherwise; the Markov property at every level is checked by cases on the level.

    Base ring #

    Generic [CommRing R], generic R-algebra A; the counter-model needs [Nontrivial R] only so that 1 ≠ 0. No ℝ, no ordering, no archimedean content. z is an abstract ring element throughout, so nothing here is a convention-C or convention-D statement.
