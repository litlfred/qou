    QOU.Hecke.MarkovTraceProduct — Product factorization of the Markov trace #

    The Markov trace of a braid β ∈ B_n factors as a product over generators:

    tr_M(ρ(β)) = ∏_{i=0}^{n-2} V̂_i(q)

    where V̂_i(q) = z · a_i + b_i is the vertex volume at generator σ_i. This follows from the Markov property applied recursively to the rightmost active generator.

    Pre-Archimedean (formal-chain) version. Lives under lean/QOU/ and is compiled by lake build. Polymorphic in the ground ring R_q = ℤ[q^±1] (Laurent polynomials) per QOU.GroundRingAnnotations. The co-located documentation stub at braids-and-knots/markov-trace-product.lean mirrors this content.
