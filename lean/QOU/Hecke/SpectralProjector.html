    QOU.Hecke.SpectralProjector — the rank (generator-level) Hecke spectral projectors #

    The Iwahori–Hecke generator σ satisfies (σ − q)(σ + q⁻¹) = 0; clearing q gives the polynomial form q·σ² = (q² − 1)·σ + q, so σ carries exactly the two eigenvalues {q, −q⁻¹}. Written over the common denominator q² + 1, the two spectral projectors are

    • e₊ = (q·σ + 1)/(q² + 1) — the symmetric (eigenvalue q) block,
    • e₋ = (q² − q·σ)/(q² + 1) — the antisymmetric (eigenvalue −q⁻¹) block.

    They are idempotent, orthogonal, and complete (e₊ + e₋ = 1). These are the q-Young (anti)symmetrizers at the GENERATOR level — the n = 2 case of e_λ = P(q)⁻¹ Σ_w q^{ℓ(w)} T_w (see rem:h3-hecke-idempotents), and the algebraic origin of the sign-alternating Bethe–Weizsäcker pairing term (the two Hecke eigenvalues, bw-mass-decomposition-dictionary.md).

    Why this is only the rank case (the owner's "have full matrices"). A block-swap braid β = σ_{i₁}⋯σ_{i₉} is a word, not a generator, so it does not satisfy the Hecke quadratic: on a reducible module its R-matrix carries far more than two eigenvalues (22 distinct at H₆, block_swap_spectrum.py). The exact q-correct projector for β is the full Lagrange spectral projector Π_{μ≠λ}(β−μ)/(λ−μ) (verified idempotent numerically), of which the e₊/e₋ proved here are the rank-1 building blocks — so reducing the symmetrizer to a scalar combination of {id, β} (degree ≤ 1) is provably insufficient.

    All four facts close by field_simp + linear_combination against the Hecke quadratic (sympy-cross-checked). Self-contained over any Field with q² + 1 ≠ 0.
