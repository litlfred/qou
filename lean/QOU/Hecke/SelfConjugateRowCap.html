    def QOU.Hecke.SelfConjugateRowCap.conjTwoRow (a b : ℕ) :
    List ℕ

    Column heights of the two-row shape (a, b) with b ≤ a: b columns of height 2, then a - b columns of height 1. This is the conjugate partition, written as a list of parts.
