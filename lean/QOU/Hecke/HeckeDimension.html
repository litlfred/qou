    QOU.Hecke.HeckeDimension — H_n(q) is spanned by (n+1)! elements #

    Fourth brick of the type-A route to the T_w basis (bean qou-twwf). QOU.Hecke.HeckeSpanning proved H_{n+1}(q) = Σ_{k ≤ n+1} incl(H_n(q)) · c_k; inducting on that gives an explicit spanning family of the right size.

    What is proved #

    • exists_spanning_finset — for every m there is a Finset of at most (m+1)! elements spanning H_m(q) over K.

    This is the ≤ half of the basis theorem in the form that does not mention dimension, so it holds over any commutative ring. Turning it into dim_K H_n(q) = (n+1)! — and hence into injectivity of QOU.Hecke.TwBasis.orbitMap — is a separate step that wants a field, and is not done here.

    The base case is m = 0: H_0(q) has an empty generating set, so Algebra.adjoin K ∅ = ⊤ collapses to span K {1} = ⊤. That is why adjoin_range_σ is stated at every m rather than only at n+1.

    theorem QOU.Hecke.IwahoriHecke.exists_spanning_finset (K : Type u_1) [CommRing K] (q : K) (m : ℕ) :
    ∃ (s : Finset (IwahoriHecke K q m)), s.card ≤ (m + 1).factorial ∧ Submodule.span K ↑s = ⊤

    H_m(q) is spanned by at most (m+1)! elements. By induction on the tower: each step multiplies the bound by the m+2 descending runs.
