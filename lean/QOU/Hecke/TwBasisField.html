    QOU.Hecke.TwBasisField — over a field, the T_w ARE a basis #

    The end of the type-A route (bean qou-twwf). Two independent halves meet:

    • QOU.Hecke.HeckeDimension.exists_spanning_finset — H_n(q) is spanned by at most (n+1)! elements, so dim_K H_n(q) ≤ (n+1)!;
    • QOU.Hecke.TwBasis.orbitMap_surjective — the action on T_1 is onto the free module of rank (n+1)!, so dim_K H_n(q) ≥ (n+1)!.

    Equal finite dimension turns the surjection into an isomorphism, which is exactly the hypothesis QOU.Hecke.TwBasis.basisTw was left carrying.

    What is proved #

    • finrank_eq_factorial — dim_K H_n(q) = (n+1)!.
    • orbitMap_injective — the hypothesis of basisTw, discharged.
    • basisTwOfField — the T_w are a basis of H_n(q), unconditionally.

    Why a field #

    Only here. Everything upstream — the Coxeter bridge, the Rs relations, the representation, the spanning bound, the independence — holds over an arbitrary commutative ring. The dimension squeeze is the one step that uses invertibility, so if the basis is ever wanted over ℤ[q,q⁻¹] this is the file to redo, and nothing before it.

    Matsumoto's theorem is never used; see QOU.Hecke.TwIndependent for why the choice of reduced word does not matter.
