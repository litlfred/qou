    QOU.Hecke.TwGramRankOne — the T_w Gram determinant at rank one #

    Fifth brick of bean qou-0y6h. TwGram reduced semisimplicity of H_n(q) to det (twGram) ≠ 0 at every rank; TwTrace made the trace of a generator explicit. This file closes the first rank end to end:

    det (twGram K q 1) = (1 + q)² = [2]_q²,

    hence H_1(q) — the honest IwahoriHecke K q 1, a RingQuot of a free algebra, not a presentation chosen for convenience — is semisimple whenever 1 + q ≠ 0, with no cited class and no sorry.

    Why this is not a duplicate of H2TraceForm #

    QOU.Hecke.H2TraceForm.h2_regTraceGram_det proves the same determinant [2]_q² for AdjoinRoot (X² - (q-1)X - q), which is a model of the rank-one Hecke algebra reached through a power basis. The T_w route reaches IwahoriHecke K q 1 itself, and reaches it by machinery that is stated at every rank — nothing here is special to n = 1 except the enumeration of S₂. The two determinants agreeing is therefore a genuine cross-check of the whole TwRep → TwIndependent → TwBasisField → TwGram → TwTrace stack against an independently derived value, not a restatement of it.

    What is proved #

    • Tw_swap — T_{s_i} = σ_i, at every rank.
    • trace_lmul_one — tr(L_1) = (n+1)!, at every rank.
    • trace_lmul_σ_sq — tr(L_{σ_i²}) from the quadratic relation, at every rank.
    • perm_two_cases — S₂ = {1, s}.
    • twGram_det_rank_one — det (twGram K q 1) = (1 + q)².
    • isSemisimpleRing_rank_one — the discharge.

    What is NOT here #

    The determinant at rank ≥ 2. That needs tr(L_{T_u}) for a general u, which is not a corollary of the generator case: the diagonal coefficient [T_u T_w : T_w] has no closed form in the corpus yet.
