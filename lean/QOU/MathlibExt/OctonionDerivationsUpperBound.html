    QOU.MathlibExt.OctonionDerivationsUpperBound — dim Der(𝕆) ≤ 14, closing dim = 14 #

    The residual upper bound from OctonionDerivationsBound: finrank Der𝕆 ≤ 14, i.e. the Leibniz-defect operator L has rank ≥ 50. We exhibit 50 endomorphisms Hfam 0 .. 49 (integer matrices; rational combinations of the 50 pivot matrix-units, scaled by 2) whose defect images L (Hfam s), projected onto 50 fixed output coordinates (rowProj), are 2 · (standard basis of ℚ⁵⁰) (hrows, machine-checked by decide over ℤ). Hence rowProj ∘ L is surjective, so rank L ≥ 50 and finrank Der𝕆 ≤ 14; with the lower bound octDer_finrank_ge this gives octonion_derivation_dim : dim Der(𝕆) = 14, sorry-free.

    The row-projection identities are proved over ℤ (which kernel-reduces under decide, unlike ℚ) via a cast bridge key; the 50×50 certificate was produced by exact rational Gaussian elimination on the 512×64 defect matrix. Reference: [baez2002] §4.1.
