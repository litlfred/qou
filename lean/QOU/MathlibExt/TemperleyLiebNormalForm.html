    Jones normal forms for TL_n(δ) — the general index type #

    Bean qou-cds1. The Temperley–Lieb arc reaches the planar diagram basis from four directions — spanning sets, lower bounds, Wedderburn decompositions and semisimplicity all stop at it. computations/tl_normal_form_basis_probe.py measured that object before this file was written, and two of its findings are what this file is shaped by.

    First: which family. The Jones normal forms — words

    (e_{i₁} e_{i₁-1} ⋯ e_{j₁}) (e_{i₂} ⋯ e_{j₂}) ⋯ (e_{i_k} ⋯ e_{j_k})
with  i₁ < ⋯ < i_k,  j₁ < ⋯ < j_k,  j_r ≤ i_r

    — number exactly catalan n, are distinct as words, and map bijectively onto all planar diagrams. So they are the right family, and this file names it.

    Second, and it is a negative: word length is not a termination measure. Left multiplication by a generator does close in the normal forms, but the word length changes by +1, 0, or a NEGATIVE ODD amount, reaching −5 by n = 7. A "rewrite until shorter" induction — the first thing one reaches for — cannot exist. The index must be the combinatorial data (I, J), and the multiplication rule must be proved on that. This file therefore indexes by a pair of subsets and never by a word.

    The encoding, and why it is subsets rather than sorted lists #

    Pointwise domination j_r ≤ i_r on equal-size subsets of a linear order is equivalent to a threshold count:

    ∀ x,  |I ∩ [0,x]|  ≤  |J ∩ [0,x]|

    (verified against the pointwise form for every pair at m ≤ 7 before being adopted). The threshold form needs no sorting, is decidable, and makes NormalIdx m a subtype of a Fintype — so Fintype.card is settled by decide rather than by a bijection argument.

    elems likewise filters List.finRange, which is already sorted, instead of calling Finset.sort. That is not a style choice: Finset.sort is merge sort and does not reduce in the kernel, so every decide below fails if it is used. Measured, not assumed — it is how the first draft failed.

    What is proved here #

    • NormalIdx m is a Fintype with card = catalan (m+1) for m ≤ 6 (1, 2, 5, 14, 42, 132, 429), bridged to Mathlib's Nat.catalan rather than to a hand-typed table. Each is one decide; m = 6 costs 17 s, m = 5 6 s, and the rest are instant.
    • Its elt map reproduces, exactly, the two bases this corpus has already proved: {1, e₀} for TL₂ and {1, e₀, e₁, e₀e₁, e₁e₀} for TL₃.

    That second point is the reason to trust the general definition: at the only two places where a proved basis exists, the general construction lands on it.

    🛑 What is NOT proved #

    The general theorem is not here, and nothing in this file stands in for it.

    • Fintype.card (NormalIdx m) = catalan (m+1) is checked at m ≤ 6 by decide — it is not proved for general m.

      The route is identified, and it is not "a bijection to Dyck paths". A pair (I, J) is exactly a word of length m over four letters — I-only, J-only, both, neither — and the domination condition says every prefix has #(I-only) ≤ #(J-only), with equality overall. Those are the bicoloured Motzkin words of length m: two step letters and two neutral letters, with a ballot condition. So the general count is the classical bicoloured-Motzkin-to-Catalan bijection, not a Dyck-path one, and the two neutral letters are exactly what makes it Motzkin rather than Dyck.

      Equivalently, in closed form,

      catalan (m+1)  =  ∑_k  C(m, 2k) · catalan k · 2^(m-2k)

      Both formulations were checked against catalan (m+1) for m ≤ 10 before being written down here.

      Mathlib has DyckWord, DyckWord.semilength and catalan_succ. It has no Motzkin words at all — checked by enumerating Mathlib/Combinatorics/Enumerative/ (Bell, Catalan, Composition, DoubleCounting, DyckWord, IncidenceAlgebra, InclusionExclusion, Partition, Stirling) and by searching the whole library for *motzkin*, whose only hit is Tactic/Linarith/Oracle/FourierMotzkin, an unrelated linear-arithmetic oracle. So the bijection is the work, and it starts from nothing.

      That is stated from a search rather than from the shape of the difficulty — the distinction a sibling branch had to correct on main the same day (0e18f7b954, "I wrote a 'Mathlib lacks X' claim from the shape of the difficulty — Mathlib has it"), and the same class as this file's own Matrix.ext_iff_trace_mul_right correction, where the lemma existed under the shape it was used for rather than the name it was known by.

    • elt is not proved to span TL_{m+1}(δ) for general m, and that is the real content of dim TL_n = catalan n. It needs the closure lemma, on the (I, J) index and not on word length. span_range_elt_two is the m = 2 instance only, and it is proved by citing the existing TL₃ result, not by any general argument.

    • elt is not proved injective at general m either.

    No class, axiom or sorry stands in for any of these. They are open.

    def QOU.MathlibExt.TemperleyLiebNormalForm.NormalIdx (m : ℕ) :
    Type

    A Jones normal form for TL_{m+1}(δ), as a pair of equal-size subsets of the m generators with J dominating I.

    The domination is stated as a threshold count rather than pointwise on sorted lists, which is what keeps the whole type decidable.
