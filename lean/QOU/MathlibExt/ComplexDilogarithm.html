    noncomputable def QOU.MathlibExt.cLiTwo (z : ℂ) :
    ℂ

    The complex dilogarithm as its defining power series, Li₂ z = ∑_{k ≥ 0} z^(k+1)/(k+1)².

    Indexed from k = 0 with numerator z^(k+1) so the k-th term is z^(k+1)/(k+1)², matching QOU.QPochhammer.LiTwo term by term. Outside the closed unit disc the tsum is 0 by Mathlib's junk-value convention; the lemmas below are all guarded by a disc hypothesis.

      Standard power-series indexing #

      cLiTwo is written ∑' k, z^(k+1)/(k+1)² so that it matches QOU.QPochhammer.LiTwo term by term (which is what makes cLiTwo_ofReal unconditional). Mathlib's power-series machinery — FormalMultilinearSeries. ofScalars, whose sum is ∑' n, cₙ • xⁿ — wants the other indexing.

      Rather than redefine cLiTwo and break the bridge, the two indexings are reconciled here. This is step 1 of the analyticity route recorded in bean qou-gkco; steps 2–3 (radius = 1, hence AnalyticOnNhd on the disc) are not done.

        The natural domain of the reflection identity: ball(½, ½) #

        The reflection identity Li₂ z + Li₂(1−z) = π²/6 − log z·log(1−z) is not well-posed on the unit ball: cLiTwo (1−z) is not given by its series there (|1−z| reaches 2), and Complex.log z has its branch cut through it.

        Metric.ball (1/2) (1/2) is the natural domain — the disc on the segment [0,1] as diameter. On it all four ingredients are simultaneously good, and it contains the real segment (0,1) where the identity is already proved (QOU.Archimedean.cLiTwo_reflection_ofReal), so it is also the connected open set an identity-theorem continuation needs.

        The integral representation Li₂(y) = −∫₀¹ log(1 − yz)/z dz #

        This is the identity Route 5 of the 5₂ saddle sum rule needs (bean qou-0yqu), and it is also the door out of the unit disc: the integral converges for every y ∉ [1,∞) regardless of ‖y‖, unlike the defining series, so it is the analytic continuation.

        Proved here on the disc, by termwise integration. Writing k = n − 1, the k-th term is y^(k+1)·z^k/(k+1), whose integral over [0,1] is y^(k+1)/(k+1)² — exactly the k-th term of cLiTwo's defining series, with no reindexing needed. The swap is intervalIntegral.hasSum_integral_of_dominated_convergence with the constant dominating bound ‖y‖^(k+1) (using z^k ≤ 1 and 1/(k+1) ≤ 1 on [0,1]), which is geometric and hence summable.

        The y with ‖y‖ ≥ 1 case — needed for the 5₂ root y₁, ‖y₁‖ = 1.52 — is not here: it requires taking the right-hand side as the definition past the disc and is tracked in qou-gkco.

        Past the disc: the integral as the definition #

        cLiTwo is a tsum, so outside the closed unit disc it is junk-valued 0 — the inversion formula Li₂ z + Li₂(1/z) = −π²/6 − ½Log(−z)² is not merely unproved for it, it is false. Anything that needs Li₂ at ‖y‖ > 1 (the 5₂ saddle root y₁, ‖y₁‖ = 1.52) has to use a different object.

        cLiTwoExt below is that object: the standard integral definition, which makes sense for every y such that 1 − yz ≠ 0 on [0,1] — in particular for all y ∉ [1,∞) — with no reference to convergence of the series.

        Labelling — updated 2026-08-08. cLiTwoExt agrees with cLiTwo on the open disc (cLiTwoExt_eq_cLiTwo), and it is now proved analytic on ℂ ∖ [1,∞) in QOU/MathlibExt/ComplexDilogarithmDeriv.lean (analyticOnNhd_cLiTwoExt), by differentiation under the integral sign. So "the analytic continuation" is a theorem, not a name.

        This paragraph previously said analyticity was "not proved here … not a theorem in this file". That is no longer true and the sentence would now be a false claim in the corpus, which is why it is rewritten rather than left standing.           Integrability of the cLiTwoExt integrand #

          log(1 − yz)/z looks singular at z = 0 and is not: log(1 − yz) ∼ −yz, so the quotient extends continuously with value −y. Formally that value is the slope of z ↦ log(1 − yz) at 0, whose derivative there is −y.

          The hypothesis Re(1 − yz) > 0 on [0,1] is not cosmetic: it keeps 1 − yz inside slitPlane, without which Complex.log is discontinuous and the integral representation is simply wrong. It holds for the 5₂ saddle roots — Re(1 − y₁z) = 1 − 0.785z and Re(1 − y₃z) = 1 − 0.430z, both positive on [0,1].

          Conjugation symmetry — Schwarz reflection, for free #

          cLiTwoExt is an integral over a real interval of log(1 − yz)/z, and every piece of that integrand commutes with conjugation: z̄ = z on the reals, conj(1 − yz) = 1 − ȳz, and Complex.log commutes with conj off the cut. So Li₂(ȳ) = conj (Li₂ y) needs no analytic continuation at all — it is the integral definition read twice.

          The hypothesis y.im ≠ 0 cannot be dropped, and this is not a technicality of the proof: on the cut the statement is false. At y = 5, conj y = y, so it would assert Li₂(5) ∈ ℝ, and mpmath gives Li₂(5) = 1.78371916127 − 5.05619832211i. Measured off the axis it holds exactly — residual 0.0 at 40 dps at the five 5₂ arguments, and still 0.0 at 1e−7 from the cut on either side of Re = 5.

          This is what QOU.Archimedean.FiveTwoOhtsukiBridge.IsKummerAtOhtsukiArgs.of_upper_half_plane consumes to reach the three lower-half-plane arguments of Ohtsuki's sum from QOU.Knot.KummerRadial.kummer, which is proved only for Im z > 0.

          theorem QOU.MathlibExt.cLiTwoExt_one_im :
          (cLiTwoExt 1).im = 0

          Li₂(1) is real. cLiTwoExt_conj cannot reach y = 1 — it is on the axis, where the conjugation identity is exactly what fails in general — so this is proved the same way but from the stronger local fact: on [0,1] the integrand log(1 − z)/z is real-valued outright, because 1 − z ≥ 0 there.

          Stated as .im = 0 rather than = π²/6: that is all the 5₂ bridge's hone hypothesis needs, and it avoids importing an evaluation this file does not prove.
