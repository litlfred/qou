    QOU.MathlibExt.MolienFakeDegree — the correct q-analogue of Σ_λ (f^λ)² = n! #

    Lean side of docs/audits/2026-08-21-molien-mining-the-correct-q-analogue-of-rsk.md, and the companion to QOU.MathlibExt.QPlancherel.

    What this establishes #

    For n = 2, 3, 4, in the balanced convention this corpus already uses (qBracket q m = (q^m − q^{-m})/(q − q^{-1})):

      Σ_{λ ⊢ n}  f^λ · q^{−κ_λ} · qDim q λ  =  qFactorial q n

    f^λ is the classical number of standard Young tableaux, κ_λ = Σ_x c(x) is the content statistic, and qDim is the corpus's own q-hook-length dimension. In the standard convention this is the textbook identity Σ_λ f^λ R_λ(q) = [n]_q! with R_λ(q) = q^{n(λ)} d_q(λ) the fake degree — the graded multiplicity of V_λ in the coinvariant algebra, whose Poincaré polynomial is [n]_q! for S_n.

    The contrast is proved too. sum_qDim_sq_ne_qFactorial_n3_at_two shows Σ_λ qDim q λ ² ≠ qFactorial q n, which is the whole of the Z(n) defect measured in docs/audits/2026-06-20-keystone-wlambda-Z-defect-n-scaling.md. The classical identity has TWO factors of f^λ; the graded identity deforms exactly ONE of them into the graded multiplicity and leaves the other classical, whereas Σ qDim² deforms both and squares. They agree only at q = 1. Having both proved here — same n, same convention, same hook data — is what makes the identities non-vacuous.

    Deliberately reusing the corpus's definitions #

    Everything is stated in terms of QPartitionCombinatorics.qDim, qFactorial, qBracket and Partition.hookLength. An earlier draft of this file introduced its own balQInt/balQFact; that was discarded on finding qBracket is definitionally the same object. A second q-bracket in the corpus would be exactly the divergence surface bean qou-m7py removed from the Rust engine.

    The qDim_* bridge lemmas hold by rfl: qDim on a concrete partition reduces definitionally to qFactorial / ∏_cells qBracket (hookLength …).

    Scope — read before extending #

    These are the concrete cases n ≤ 4, kernel-checked. The general-n statement is not here. Mathlib as of v4.24.0 has YoungDiagram and Nat.Partition but no hook lengths, tableaux, or hook-length formula (probed by name, all absent). The corpus supplies all three of the combinatorial ingredients, which an earlier draft of this note wrongly listed as missing: Partition.hookLength and qDim in QPartitionCombinatorics, and #SYT in QOU.Mathlib.SYTEnum — numSYTenum is kernel-computable (the numSYT_* lemmas below are by decide) and is bridged to the structure-level count by numSYTenum_eq_numStandardYoungTableaux.

    So the genuinely missing piece for a general-n proof is narrower than "hook-length theory": it is the graded decomposition of the coinvariant algebra (Chevalley/Steinberg) plus an induction over Finset-many partitions. Tracked as bean qou-3quc rather than faked with a sorry.

    The identity is standard (Chevalley/Steinberg + fake degrees); what is corpus-new is the diagnosis that Σ qDim² is the wrong q-analogue.

    Note qDim is ℝ-specialised upstream although it is purely algebraic (AGENTS.md §7c); these statements inherit that and are over ℝ for that reason alone.

    The contrast: the corpus's Σ qDim² is NOT the q-factorial #

    source
    theorem QOU.MathlibExt.MolienFakeDegree.sum_qDim_sq_ne_qFactorial_n3_at_two :
    QPartitionCombinatorics.qDim 2 [3] ^ 2 + QPartitionCombinatorics.qDim 2 [2, 1] ^ 2 + QPartitionCombinatorics.qDim 2 [1, 1, 1] ^ 2 ≠ QPartitionCombinatorics.qFactorial 2 3

    The Z(n) ≠ 1 defect, in its smallest exact form. At n = 3, q = 2:

    Σ_λ qDim q λ ² = 33/4 while qFactorial q 3 = 105/8.

    Together with molien_qanalogue_n3 — same n, same convention, same hook data — this shows the two sums are genuinely different objects, so the identities above are not vacuous.
