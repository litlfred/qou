        The ellipticity source (why the Birkhoff matrix is theta-valued). If f and g are two q-characters with the same nonzero multiplier c, their ratio f/g is q-invariant (elliptic). The connection matrix P = (X^∞)⁻¹X^0 compares two solutions with matching exponents, so its entries are such ratios — elliptic, hence theta quotients, not constants.
