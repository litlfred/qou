    A TowerSpan witness with a growing filtration AND a non-multiplicative trace #

    Bean qou-fq1i (child of qou-301d). InvolutionTowerWitness removed the constant-filtration objection to DisjointBlockTraceLaw.towerSpan_unit, but its trace is an algebra homomorphism, so it says nothing about the trace content of TowerSpan: DisjointBlockMultiplicative holds there for every pair, disjointness playing no part. This file supplies the cheaper of the two towers Mathlib lacks — the symmetric-group algebra tower — with a trace that is not a homomorphism, and proves SandwichReduces there.

    The construction #

    A = MonoidAlgebra R (Equiv.Perm ℕ); the generators are the adjacent transpositions t m = of (swap m (m+1)), with g m = ginv m = t m; the tower is H n = adjoin {t m | m < n} (the group algebra of the permutations of {0, …, n}); and the trace is the coefficient of the identity, tr x = x 1, with z = z' = 0.

    • idCoeff_mul_comm, idCoeff_one — tr is a normalised trace.
    • symTrace — the MarkovTrace axioms hold with z = z' = 0: for a ∈ H n, tr (a * t n) = a (s n), and s n moves n+1, which nothing in H n does. The quadratic relation holds with h = 0, so z' = z - h is consistent.
    • idCoeff_not_mul_hom — the trace is not multiplicative: tr (t m * t m) = 1 ≠ 0 = tr (t m) * tr (t m).
    • sandwichReduces_sym — SandwichReduces holds at every level, and this is the substantive part of the file. By linearity it reduces to the group-level double-coset decomposition doubleCoset: every permutation supported on [n, M+2] lies in W ∪ W · s (M+1) · W, where W is generated by s n, …, s M. That in turn needs mem_closure_of_suppIn: the adjacent transpositions of an interval generate every permutation supported on it — proved by a double induction (gen_aux) that pushes the preimage of the top point upward one transposition at a time.
    • towerSpan_sym, disjointBlock_sym — hence TowerSpan, via towerSpan_of_sandwichReduces, and the disjoint-block law, via disjointBlock_of_sandwichReduces.
    • disjointBlock_sym_direct — the same law proved directly, without the tower machinery, from W_{<n} ∩ W_{≥n} = {1}: a permutation supported on {0..n} and on {n, n+1, …} at once is supported on the single point n, hence trivial. This is an independent check that the reduction's conclusion is true in this model.
    • t_not_mem_shiftedUpTo — the filtration grows: t N ∉ shiftedUpTo n M for M < N, because every element of that stage has zero coefficient at s N (which moves N+1), while t N has coefficient 1 there.
    • pair_law_disjoint / pair_law_same_block_fails — the law is content here, not a restatement of multiplicativity: with a = 1 + t n ∈ H (n+1) and b = 1 + t (n+1) ∈ shifted (n+1), tr (a * b) = 1 = tr a * tr b — the cross term t n * t (n+1) is a 3-cycle and contributes nothing — whereas tr (a * a) = 2 ≠ 1 = tr a * tr a, because the cross term t n * t n = 1 contributes 1.

    🛑 What this witness still does NOT exercise #

    q = 1, z = 0. The quadratic relation here is t m * t m = 1, i.e. h = 0, and the Markov parameter is z = 0. The Iwahori–Hecke tower at q ≠ 1 has h = q - q⁻¹ ≠ 0 and a non-zero Markov parameter, and the double-coset decomposition for it must be carried out by rewriting with the braid relation and the quadratic relation. Here the decomposition is proved in the permutation model — by support and point-pushing arguments — and the braid relation is never written down. So SandwichReduces for a Hecke tower with q ≠ 1 remains unproved, and the disjoint-block law for the production atom word — hence its bond-product reading — remains measured, not derived.

    What is established is narrower and stated exactly: TowerSpan's trace content is now exercised by a witness in which the trace is not a homomorphism, the filtration grows, and the disjoint-block law is true for a reason (W_{<n} ∩ W_{≥n} = {1}) that is specific to a trace supported at the identity. A Hecke trace with z ≠ 0 does not have that shape.

    Base ring #

    Generic [CommRing R]; no ordering, no ℝ, no archimedean content. The three statements needing 1 ≠ 0 carry [Nontrivial R].
