    QOU.MathlibExt.IdempotentBaseChange — base change preserves the image of an idempotent #

    For an idempotent e : Module.End R M and an R-algebra A,

    A ⊗[R] (LinearMap.range e) ≃ₗ[A] LinearMap.range (e.baseChange A).

    No finiteness, freeness, or flatness hypothesis is needed: the image of an idempotent is a direct summand, and the argument below is purely formal — e splits as subtype ∘ₗ proj, base change is functorial, and a split injection stays a split injection.

    Why this file exists #

    It is the second rung toward the k[S_n] division-ring collapse, residual item 1 of SnWedderburnPartitionBridge.sn_isotypic_specht_split, scoped in docs/audits/2026-08-10-sn-division-ring-collapse-scoping.md (bean qou-dr1c).

    The collapse needs the equivariant base-change statement K ⊗_k End_{k[G]}(V) ≅ End_{K[G]}(K ⊗_k V), which does not follow from the module-level A ⊗[R] End R M ≃ End A (A ⊗[R] M) of EndBaseChange alone — it additionally needs G-invariants to commute with the base change. For a finite group in characteristic zero the invariants are the image of the averaging idempotent f ↦ |G|⁻¹ • Σ_σ ρ σ ∘ₗ f ∘ₗ ρ σ⁻¹ on End_k V, so this file supplies the step that carries invariants across.

    What is still missing for the equivariant statement, and hence for item 1:

    1. the identification range (averaging idempotent) = End_{k[G]}(V);
    2. the compatibility — that under EndBaseChange.endBaseChangeEquiv the averaging idempotent for V corresponds to the averaging idempotent for K ⊗_k V.

    Neither is proved here. This file is a rung, not the item.

    Construction #

    e idempotent splits through its image: with proj e : M →ₗ[R] range e the corestriction and (range e).subtype the inclusion,

    • subtype ∘ₗ proj e = e (definitionally),
    • proj e ∘ₗ subtype = id (needs idempotence),
    • e ∘ₗ subtype = subtype (needs idempotence).

    Applying LinearMap.baseChange — which preserves composition and the identity — carries all three to A, making subtype.baseChange A injective with image range (e.baseChange A).
