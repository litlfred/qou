    QOU.MathlibExt.OctonionSU3BracketClosure — su3 e is a Lie subalgebra #

    Two wirings for the colour stabilizer su3 e = ker (evOct e) ⊂ Der𝕆:

    1. Bracket closure (bean qou-51st): the commutator of two derivations is a derivation (bracket_mem_ker_leibnizDefect, generic over any bilinear multiplication), and the commutator of two derivations killing e kills e (bracketDer_mem_su3) — so su3 e is closed under the commutator bracket inside Der𝕆 = 𝔤₂, i.e. a Lie subalgebra. Both are short structural proofs: the first is the classical cross-term cancellation, the second is D₁ (D₂ e) − D₂ (D₁ e) = D₁ 0 − D₂ 0 = 0.

    2. Concrete conjecture wiring (bean qou-iibg): the appendix conjecture class ColourStabilizerAction specialised to the corpus's concrete stabilizer, ColourStabilizerActionOnSU3 e V. This is a statement-level wiring only — no instance is provided; producing one is the open derivation (conj:sm-colour-stabilizer-action). The specialisation makes the open problem precise on the corpus's own objects instead of an abstract colour-algebra slot.

    Base ring ℚ; exact rational linear algebra, nothing archimedean.

Together with bracket_mem_Der𝕆 this exhibits su3 e as a Lie subalgebra of Der(𝕆) = 𝔤₂, as the Günaydin–Gürsey colour picture requires.
