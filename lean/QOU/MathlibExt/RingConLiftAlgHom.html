    An algebra hom out of a RingCon quotient — the first-isomorphism lift Mathlib lacks #

    The gap, measured against the pinned Mathlib #

    Mathlib/GroupTheory/Congruence/Basic.lean has Con.lift, for monoid congruences. There is no ring analogue: Mathlib/RingTheory/Congruence/ has no lift, and Mathlib/RingTheory/TwoSidedIdeal/ carries ker, mem_ker and ker_eq_bot and no lift or quotientKerEquivOfSurjective. So the two halves of the first isomorphism theorem can be in hand — a surjection and an identification of its kernel with the congruence — and there is still no way to turn them into an ≃ₐ.

    This file supplies the missing map, over a generic CommRing R.

    Why it lives here rather than beside its application #

    Split out of QOU.SchurWeyl.WtraceRadicalQuotient under §7c: that file mixes this generic content with an ℝ-only application, and scripts/check-sidecars.sh flags the mix. Nothing here mentions ℝ, needs an ordering, or takes a square root.

    🛑 traceQuotEquiv does NOT go through this file #

    Stated here as well as there, because the temptation to assume otherwise is exactly what a reader does with a general-purpose lemma sitting next to its apparent use. WtraceRadicalQuotient.twoRowQuotHom is inlined against the corpus's own algebra instances, because two Algebra ℝ instances on the quotient type do not unify (Mathlib's RingCon.instAlgebraQuotient against the corpus's QBeta.MarkovTraceQuotient.instAlgebraTraceQuot). So this is the general-purpose piece — the one that would go upstream — and it is not on that result's critical path.
