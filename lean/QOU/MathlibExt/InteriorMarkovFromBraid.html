    The interior-bond Markov property, from the braid relations #

    Bean qou-gr3t. That bean states the blocker for the general-A bond factorisation in these words:

    A hypothetical field interior_markov : ∀ g, ∀ a ∈ H g, ∀ b ∈ upper g, tr(a*σ_g*b) = z*tr(a*b) would be independent of the existing fields (cmTower satisfies all of them and violates it), so it may NOT be added as a field or axiom; it is a THEOREM of the Hecke tower, to be proved from the Hecke relations, not of the abstract tower.

    and names the route:

    prove InteriorMarkov for a tower carrying the quadratic relation + braid relation + word-spanning, by the reduction b ↦ (H_m + H_m σ_m H_m) on the top letter m of b and induction on m — a multi-session build of the Ocneanu uniqueness argument on the word level.

    The third ingredient landed the day after that was written. Word-spanning is TowerSpanReduction.step_of_sandwichReduces, and HeckeSandwichReduces discharges it from the same three relations (BraidedTower). With it the reduction the bean describes is a short induction, not a multi-session build, and this file carries it out.

    🔁 This is a SECOND, independent proof — the canonical one is elsewhere #

    QOU.Hecke.InteriorMarkovOfBraidedTower.interiorMarkov_of_braided (#7265, landed 2026-09-13) proves the same statement and is the one to cite and to build on: it lands on the MarkovTower carrier, so MarkovInteriorBond.tr_bondWord_of_interiorMarkov consumes it directly, which is what tr_bondWord_of_braided there does.

    The two proofs are genuinely different arguments, which is why this one is kept:

    route
    InteriorMarkovOfBraidedTower DisjointBlockMultiplicative → DisjointBlock → InteriorMarkov — three applications of the block law
    here direct induction over the tower from step_of_sandwichReduces + markov₂_of_markov, never mentioning the block law

    Same hypothesis (BraidedTower), same conclusion. Do not export a second interiorMarkov_of_braided from this file — the exported name is interiorMarkov_shiftedUpTo, and the carrier is OcneanuTrace.MarkovTrace rather than MarkovTower.

    The statement #

    interiorMarkov_shiftedUpTo : for a BraidedTower, every g, every M, every a ∈ H g and every b in the stage shiftedUpTo (g+1) M,

    tr (a * g g * b) = z * tr (a * b).

    shiftedUpTo (g+1) M is the part of the tail above g generated at levels ≤ M, and every element of Hecke/MarkovInteriorBond.upper g lies in some such stage, so this is the interior-bond rule on the whole tail, stage by stage.

    The proof #

    Induction on M, exactly as in sandwichReduces_all, and for the same reason: the previous stage's spanning decomposition already puts b in one-sandwich form, so only two shapes arise.

    • M = 0. The stage is ⊥ — the generator window {m : g+1 ≤ m ≤ 0} is empty for every g — so b is a scalar and the claim is the Markov axiom T3.

    • M+1. step_of_sandwichReduces writes b = u + Σ vᵢ · g (M+1) · wᵢ with u, vᵢ, wᵢ in the previous stage. On u the inductive hypothesis applies directly. On a sandwich, both sides peel the same generator with the same two-sided Markov step:

      tr (a * g g * (v * g (M+1) * w))  =  z * tr (a * g g * (v * w))   [markov₂]
                                  =  z * z * tr (a * (v * w))     [IH]
z * tr (a * (v * g (M+1) * w))    =  z * z * tr (a * (v * w))     [markov₂]

      and the two agree. The level bookkeeping is the only content: a * g g * v and w must both sit in H (M+1), which needs g ≤ M — and when g > M the stage is ⊥ again, so that case never reaches the sandwich branch.

    markov₂ is free (DisjointBlockTrace.markov₂_of_markov derives it from cyclicity and T3), so no hypothesis beyond BraidedTower enters.

    What this does and does not settle #

    Settles: the interior-bond rule is a theorem for any MarkovTrace satisfying the three Iwahori-Hecke relations, which is what qou-gr3t asked for and what its counter-model showed cannot be had from the tower axioms alone. cmTower is not a counterexample here because it fails quad: its generators are polynomial variables and X_m * X_m ≠ h • X_m + 1 in R[X₀, X₁, X₂].

    The bridge to Hecke/MarkovInteriorBond is DEFINITIONAL — measured by #7265, not by this file. An earlier version of this docstring said the two carriers "have to be related first, and whether that is a definitional bridge or real work is not measured here". It is definitional: InteriorMarkovOfBraidedTower.toTower S hfar : MarkovTower R A is built field-by-field from an OcneanuTrace.MarkovTrace (σ := S.g, tr := S.tr, z := S.z, H := S.H, markov := S.markov, tr_comm := S.tr_mul_comm, far_comm := hfar), and toTowerB S supplies hfar from BraidedTower.far. So InteriorMarkov (toTowerB S) unfolds to exactly the quantifier form proved here with S.g for T.σ, and tr_bondWord_of_interiorMarkov consumes it directly. qou-gr3t's conditional is therefore discharged — by #7265, with this file as an independent confirmation.

    🛑 Does not settle the bond product for the production word. The factorisation tr(atom)/tr(free) = ∏ (z + dᵢ) is measured (qou-qqm4, orders ≤ 5, A ≤ 5), and the production word is monotone, for which MonotoneWordTraceFactorizes.tr_stabWord_eq_prod_tr already gives the free-word half unconditionally. Nothing here computes a binding coefficient.
