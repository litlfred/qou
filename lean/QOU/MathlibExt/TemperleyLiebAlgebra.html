    The Temperley–Lieb algebra as a presented algebra #

    Mathlib-style local infrastructure. Mathlib has no Temperley–Lieb algebra of any kind — a case-insensitive word search for Temperley, Wenzl, Kauffman and skein over the vendored Mathlib tree returns nothing — so this file builds the object from FreeAlgebra and RingQuot, exactly as QOU.MathlibExt.IwahoriHecke builds the Hecke algebra.

    The presentation #

    For an index type B carrying an adjacency relation Adj : B → B → Prop (read Adj i j as "|i − j| = 1") and a loop parameter δ : R, the algebra TemperleyLiebAlgebra R δ Adj is the free R-algebra on B modulo

    • e i * e i = δ * e i for every i,
    • e i * e j * e i = e i whenever Adj i j,
    • e i * e j = e j * e i whenever ¬ Adj i j.

    Specialising B := Fin n and Adj := adjFin (§The classical indexing) recovers the classical TL_{n+1}(δ): n generators e_0, …, e_{n-1} — the literature's e_1, …, e_n — on n + 1 strands. The offset follows the house convention of QOU.MathlibExt.BraidGroup, which indexes n generators on n + 1 strands to avoid truncated ℕ-subtraction.

    What is a THEOREM here, and what is not #

    Theorems (all proved below; no sorry, no new axiom):

    • the three defining relations hold in the quotient — e_sq, e_adjacent, e_farCommute — together with their Fin-indexed restatements;
    • the universal property, as lift / lift_e / hom_ext and packaged as a single ∃! (exists_unique_lift);
    • idempotency up to scale: isIdempotentElem_smul_of_sq in an arbitrary R-algebra, specialised to the generators by isIdempotentElem_e;
    • non-vacuity, in three strengths (below).

    Definitions, not theorems. Adj is an arbitrary relation; nothing here proves it is the adjacency of a strand diagram, because no strand diagram is defined in this file.

    Not claimed, and deliberately absent. No Catalan-dimension theorem, no diagrammatic basis, no Jones–Wenzl projectors, no Markov trace, and no isomorphism with any concrete algebra. §Non-vacuity exhibits representations of TL₂ and TL₃; it does not claim either is injective, and this file proves no upper bound on the size of TemperleyLiebAlgebra R δ Adj.

    Non-vacuity #

    A presented algebra whose relations force 0 = 1 makes every theorem above vacuously true. Three witnesses, in increasing strength:

    1. nontrivial — the augmentation e i ↦ 0 splits R → TL, so 0 ≠ 1 in the quotient for every B, Adj and δ.
    2. tl2_linearIndependent — in the one-generator case 1 and e are R-linearly independent, i.e. TL₂(δ) is at least as large as the expected {1, e}. Witnessed by the representation e ↦ (δ, 0) into R × R.
    3. tl3_e_zero_ne_zero, tl3_e_one_ne_zero, tl3_e_zero_ne_e_one — in the two-generator case, where the relation e i e j e i = e i is non-vacuous for the first time, both generators are nonzero and distinct. Witnessed by an explicit 2 × 2 matrix representation.

    What a commutative model can and cannot do. In a commutative algebra the loop relation gives e₀e₁e₀ = e₀²e₁ = δe₀e₁ and e₁e₀e₁ = e₁²e₀ = δe₀e₁, so adjacency forces e₀ = e₁, and then δ²e₀ = e₀. Only the second consequence below is a genuine limitation of commutative models:

    • Nonzero-ness alone does not require a matrix model. When δ² = 1 the constraint δ²e₀ = e₀ is vacuous, and e₀ = e₁ = 1 in R itself is a commutative model of every relation with both generators nonzero. At δ = 1 — which is exactly where part 4 discharges the hypotheses over ℤ — tl3_e_zero_ne_zero and tl3_e_one_ne_zero are therefore obtainable commutatively.
    • Distinctness does. No commutative model separates e₀ from e₁, at any δ. So tl3_e_zero_ne_e_one is the statement that genuinely needs the matrix representation, and it is the one carried into part 4.

    Design notes #

    • The relation is an inductive, not a Set of ideal generators. RingQuot quotients by the ring congruence generated by an arbitrary relation, so the three families are three constructors and no ideal-membership reasoning is needed downstream.
    • Adj is a bare B → B → Prop — not assumed symmetric or irreflexive, since neither the construction nor the universal property needs either. adjFin is proved to have both properties separately, for callers who want them. Note that for an irreflexive Adj the third family does fire at i = j, where it asserts e i * e i = e i * e i and so adds nothing.
    • Base ring is a generic [CommRing R] (AGENTS.md §7c). No ordering, no ℝ, no archimedean construct appears; δ is a formal parameter. Where invertibility of δ is needed it is taken as an explicit c * δ = 1 hypothesis rather than through IsUnit, so no Units juggling reaches the arithmetic.

                  Non-vacuity, part 2: TL₂(δ) has the shape {1, e} #

                  One generator, no adjacent pair. The representation e ↦ (δ, 0) into R × R separates 1 from e, which is the content of the classical statement that TL₂(δ) is free of rank two on {1, e}.

                  This does not claim TL₂(δ) ≅ R × R: no upper bound on TL₂(δ) is proved anywhere in this file.

                  The upper bound now exists, one file over. QOU.MathlibExt.TemperleyLiebSpanning.tl2_span_eq_top proves TL₂(δ) = span_R {1, e₀}, and tl2Basis combines it with tl2_linearIndependent below into an actual basis — so TL₂(δ) is free of rank two, and dim TL₂(δ) = 2 = catalan 2. It lives there rather than here because the proof routes through adjoin_range_e_eq_top, which is in QOU.MathlibExt.HeckeToTemperleyLieb and imports this file.

                  The sentence above is still correct as written — it is scoped to this file — and ≅ R × R is still not claimed, for a reason freeness does not settle: the basis {1, e₀} maps to {(1,1), (δ,0)} in R × R, whose determinant is −δ, so that particular map is an isomorphism only when δ is a unit.

                        Non-vacuity, part 3: both generators of TL₃(δ) are nonzero #

                        TL₃(δ) has two generators e_0, e_1, and they are adjacent, so the relation e_i e_j e_i = e_i is non-vacuous here for the first time.
