    QOU.MathlibExt.SphericalCategory — the spherical condition, and every ribbon category satisfies it #

    QOU/MathlibExt/PivotalCategory.lean supplies the two entries of Mathlib's rigid-category "Future work" list that concern dualisation as a functor and pivotal categories, and closes with a section headed "What is still not proved" whose entire content is:

    Sphericality — that the left and right quantum dimensions agree — is a separate condition and is not touched here. A pivotal structure does not imply it.

    This file supplies it.

    Main definitions #

    • qDimPivotalRight X, qDimPivotalLeft X — the two quantum dimensions of an object in a pivotal category, closing the loop through the pivotor in the two possible directions. Neither mentions a braiding, which is what makes the class below the right shape for Mathlib and distinguishes it from QOU.BraidKnot.SphericalStructure (see the comparison below).
    • SphericalCategory C — a pivotal category in which the two agree, for every object.

    Main statements #

    • qDimPivotalRight_eq_qDimTwistedWith, qDimPivotalLeft_eq_qDimTwistedLeftWith — under pivotalOfRibbon, the two pivotal dimensions are the twisted braided closures qDimTwistedWith X Xᘁ and qDimTwistedLeftWith X Xᘁ of TwistedQuantumDimension.lean. These are the bridge: the pivotal side is braiding-free by construction, the twisted side is where the geometry was done.
    • sphericalOfRibbon — every ribbon right-rigid category is spherical. Barrett–Westbury's classical statement, and the reason the pivotal structure of a ribbon category is the one worth having. Derived, not assumed: the field is discharged by qDimTwistedLeftWith_eq.

    Relation to QOU.BraidKnot.KeystonePivotalSpherical.SphericalStructure #

    That class is a different object and both should stay. It is stated over the twistless braided closure η ≫ β ≫ ε, and its leftQDim_eq is an asserted hypothesis: bean qou-djk4 established that no axiom of a braided rigid category forces the two twistless closures together, because they differ by the monodromy (β_ B A).inv against (β_ A B).hom. So SphericalStructure is the right object exactly where there is no twist available, and it is not derivable from ribbon at any twist, including the trivial one.

    SphericalCategory below is the opposite trade: it asks for a pivotal structure, and in exchange the condition it names is derivable — that is sphericalOfRibbon.

    Non-vacuity #

    qDimPivotalLeft X = qDimPivotalRight X := rfl is rejected by the elaborator, so the class field relates two genuinely distinct morphisms rather than restating one. (Checked, not assumed — the same check qou-djk4 applied to qDimTwistedLeftWith.)

          The right pivotal dimension is the twisted braided closure.

          pivotalOfRibbon's pivotor is θ_X ≫ drinfeldIso X, and drinfeldIso is by definition the comparison of X and Xᘁᘁ as right duals of Xᘁ; rightDualIso_hom_evaluation is exactly the statement that it converts the evaluation of Xᘁ ⊣ Xᘁᘁ into the swapped evaluation β ≫ ε, which is the closure qDimTwistedWith uses.

          The left pivotal dimension is the twisted braided closure taken the other way.

          The evaluation form does not reach this one — the pivotor appears whiskered on the left, against a coevaluation — which is what coev_comp_rightDualIso is for. After it, the swapped coevaluation η ≫ β⁻¹ appears literally, and braiding naturality moves the inverse twist from one side of it to the other.
