    QOU.MathlibExt.PivotalCategory — the dual functors and pivotal categories #

    Mathlib's Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Rigid/Basic.lean carries a "Future work" list, and two of its five entries are exactly this file's subject, quoted verbatim:

    • Show that ᘁ gives an equivalence of categories C ≅ (Cᵒᵖ)ᴹᵒᵖ.
    • Define pivotal categories (rigid categories equipped with a natural isomorphism ᘁᘁ ≅ 𝟙 C).

    Neither exists yet: grep -n "Functor" Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Rigid/Basic.lean returns nothing, so right-dualisation is not even a functor in Mathlib, and [Pp]ivotal occurs in the whole of Mathlib/CategoryTheory/ only in that one TODO line. So the pivotal class cannot be stated in Mathlib's own requested form until the functor exists. This file supplies both, in that order.

    It imports only Mathlib and is stated over Mathlib's RightRigidCategory / BraidedCategory, so it can be lifted upstream verbatim. Companion module: QOU/MathlibExt/Ribbon.lean (the balancing twist, the other missing half).

    Main definitions #

    • rightDualFunctor C : Cᵒᵖ ⥤ C — right-dualisation as a functor. It is contravariant, which is why the source is Cᵒᵖ; functoriality is rightAdjointMate_id and comp_rightAdjointMate.
    • doubleDualFunctor C : C ⥤ C — X ↦ Xᘁᘁ, f ↦ (fᘁ)ᘁ. Covariant, being dualisation twice.
    • PivotalCategory C — Mathlib's requested shape: a natural isomorphism 𝟭 C ≅ ᘁᘁ.

    Main statements #

    • PivotalCategory — the class, carrying both the pivotor and its monoidality (pivotor_tensor). The instance satisfying it is QOU.MathlibExt.pivotalOfRibbon, which lives downstream of the twist; there is deliberately no braided-only instance, and the note further down says why.
    • rightAdjointMate_congr_dual — the mate is independent of the choice of dual, up to rightDualIso. Mathlib has no naturality lemma for rightDualIso.
    • rightDualIso_hom_unique / rightDualIso_hom_trans — the comparison of two right duals is uniquely determined by commuting with the evaluations, and such comparisons compose. Mathlib carries rightDualIso and rightDualIso_id and nothing else about it; uniqueness is what makes the comparison usable, since it turns "construct the map" into "check one identity".
    • swap_mate_mate — the double mate against swapped pairings is the identity; the braided form of Mathlib's "left adjoint mate of the right adjoint mate is the morphism itself" entry.
    • PivotalCategory.ofPointwise / pivotor_naturality — the functorial form and the pointwise form (an X ≅ Xᘁᘁ for each X, plus a naturality square against the double mate) carry the same data. The pointwise form is what a string-diagram argument produces; the functorial form is what Mathlib asked for. Having both means a proof in either style lands.
    • drinfeldIso — in a braided right-rigid category every object is isomorphic to its double dual, X ≅ Xᘁᘁ. Both X and Xᘁᘁ are right duals of Xᘁ — the first because BraidedCategory.exactPairing_swap turns the pairing X ⊣ Xᘁ around — and right duals are unique up to canonical isomorphism (rightDualIso). Sorry-free and unconditional.

    The naturality, and a correction #

    drinfeldIso is pointwise; a PivotalCategory instance needs its naturality, f ≫ drinfeldIso Y = drinfeldIso X ≫ (fᘁ)ᘁ. An earlier revision of this file recorded that as open, on the strength of a probe in which simp [drinfeldIso, rightDualIso, rightAdjointMate] unfolded the goal to a ~20-term identity resembling Mathlib's coevaluation_evaluation_braided'.

    That assessment was wrong, and it is retracted. The error was attacking the definition of the mate instead of its algebra. Nothing here needs a Whitney move; the proof is two general lemmas Mathlib is missing, each of which follows from lemmas Mathlib already has:

    • rightAdjointMate_congr_dual — the mate does not depend on the choice of dual, up to rightDualIso. Both sides rewrite, via comp_rightAdjointMate with explicit instance arguments, to the mixed mate taken with one object's first dual and the other's second. Mathlib has no naturality lemma for rightDualIso at all — rightDualIso_id is the only lemma about it.
    • swap_mate_mate — taking the mate twice, the second time against the swapped pairings, is the identity: (fᘁ)ᘁ = f when X is read as the right dual of Xᘁ via BraidedCategory.exactPairing_swap. This is the braided form of Mathlib's "Future work" entry "Show that the left adjoint mate of the right adjoint mate of a morphism is the morphism itself." Proved by evaluating both sides against ε and cancelling: the map g ↦ (g ▷ Yᘁ) ≫ ε is injective because tensorRightHomEquiv carries it to g ≫ (λ_ Y).inv.

    Composing them gives drinfeldIso_naturality — the Drinfeld isomorphism is a natural isomorphism 𝟭 C ≅ ᘁᘁ on every braided right-rigid category, unconditionally and sorry-free.

    That is naturality only. An earlier revision promoted it to an instance pivotalOfBraided : PivotalCategory C and claimed every braided right-rigid category pivotal; with the monoidality field present that instance is unconstructible, because the claim is false. What survives, and is enough for FGModuleCat K, is pivotalOfRibbon: symmetric ⇒ balanced ⇒ ribbon, so the symmetric rigid categories are still pivotal with no linear algebra involved.

    Sphericality — no longer open #

    Sphericality — that the left and right quantum dimensions agree — is a separate condition and is still not implied by a pivotal structure, which is why it is a class of its own rather than a theorem here. It is, however, no longer unproved: QOU.MathlibExt.SphericalCategory states it in the braiding-free pivotal form, and QOU.MathlibExt.sphericalOfRibbon derives it for every ribbon right-rigid category (Barrett–Westbury), via the two bridges identifying the pivotal dimensions of pivotalOfRibbon with the twisted braided closures of QOU.MathlibExt.TwistedQuantumDimension.

    This section previously read "not touched here" with nothing further, and that was accurate when written. It is corrected rather than deleted so the reader can see that the gap was named before it was closed.

    PivotalCategory carries the monoidality field — and drinfeldIso cannot satisfy it #

    A pivotal structure in the literature is a monoidal natural isomorphism: on top of naturality it must satisfy

    pivotor (X ⊗ Y) ≫ (tensorDoubleDualIso X Y).hom = pivotor X ⊗ₘ pivotor Y

    against the comparison (X ⊗ Y)ᘁᘁ ≅ Xᘁᘁ ⊗ Yᘁᘁ (QOU.MathlibExt.tensorDoubleDualIso). The class below carries that as pivotor_tensor, so [PivotalCategory C] now means what the literature means and Barrett–Westbury applies to it with no side condition.

    Earlier revisions of this file omitted the field, and said so with a warning not to add it while pivotalOfBraided existed. Both are resolved: the field is here and that instance is gone.

    drinfeldIso is natural but NOT monoidal. A braided right-rigid category is therefore not pivotal in the monoidal sense — braiding alone is not enough. Two independent arguments:

    • Hopf-algebraic. For a quasitriangular Hopf algebra the Drinfeld element u implements S² and satisfies Δ(u) = (u ⊗ u)(R₂₁R)⁻¹ (Kassel, Quantum Groups XIV.6). u is grouplike — which is what monoidality of the induced X ⟶ Xᘁᘁ means — exactly when the monodromy R₂₁R is trivial, i.e. essentially only in the symmetric case. In Rep U_q(sl₂) it is not.
    • Diagrammatic. Monoidality reduces (see rightDualIso_tensorExactPairing) to comparing two exact pairings on Yᘁ ⊗ Xᘁ: exactPairing_swap of tensorExactPairing, against tensorExactPairing of the two exactPairing_swaps. Expanding the block braiding β_{X ⊗ Y, Yᘁ ⊗ Xᘁ} by the two hexagons gives four crossings. Two of them pair off with the two caps and reproduce the componentwise comparisons; the surviving two are X over Yᘁ and Y over Xᘁ. Closing the caps joins X to Xᘁ and Y to Yᘁ into two loops, and — counting with the orientation reversal at each cap, which is the step that makes this come out non-trivial — those two crossings have the same sign. The loops are Hopf-linked, not unlinked: the discrepancy is the monodromy (β_ X Y).hom ≫ (β_ Y X).hom, not the identity.

    This retracts the scoping of the monoidality step, which had been recorded as "the last hard step" of a proof that was expected to go through. It does not affect anything already proved here: drinfeldIso and drinfeldIso_naturality are statements about naturality only and stand unchanged. What it did cost was the instance built from them — see the note where pivotalOfBraided used to be.

    The corrected statement: ribbon, not merely braided #

    The monodromy is precisely what a balancing cancels. With θ satisfying θ_{X ⊗ Y} = β_{X,Y} ≫ β_{Y,X} ≫ (θ_X ⊗ θ_Y) (QOU.MathlibExt.BalancedCategory.twist_tensor), and using that θ_X ⊗ θ_Y commutes with the monodromy by naturality of the braiding, the twisted map θ_X ≫ drinfeldIso X is monoidal. The direction was open when this was written and is now settled: it is θ, not θ⁻¹. The instance is QOU.MathlibExt.pivotalOfRibbon — a ribbon category is pivotal, which is the classical statement (a ribbon element v makes uv⁻¹ grouplike).

    The diagrammatic argument above is proved, in QOU.MathlibExt.BraidedTensorDual: rightDualIso_swap_tensor states that the two pairings differ by exactly the inverse monodromy, and twist_drinfeld_monoidal that the balancing axiom cancels it.

    An earlier revision of this paragraph predicted the wrong route, and the prediction is retracted. It said the formal content would be a lemma Mathlib also lacks — that in a braided rigid category the braiding against a dual leg, β_{X, Yᘁ}, is the bent-round inverse braiding β_{Y,X}⁻¹. No such lemma was needed. What the proof actually turns on is weaker and more general: a strand crossing any cap c : A ⊗ B ⟶ 𝟙 can be pushed off it at the cost of braiding past each leg separately (QOU.MathlibExt.braiding_slide_cap_right), with nothing assumed of c beyond its type — no rigidity, no exact pairing. Applying that once per leg of a dual pair leaves two inverse braidings, and those are the monodromy.

                  There is deliberately no pivotalOfBraided #

                  An earlier revision of this file carried an instance pivotalOfBraided : PivotalCategory C, with the Drinfeld isomorphism as pivotor, claiming every braided right-rigid category is pivotal.

                  It has been removed, because with the monoidality field it is unconstructible — and it is unconstructible because it is false. drinfeldIso is natural but not monoidal: it misses by exactly the inverse monodromy ((β_ X Y).hom ≫ (β_ Y X).hom)⁻¹ (QOU.MathlibExt.rightDualIso_swap_tensor). Braiding alone does not make a rigid category pivotal in the literature's sense.

                  The replacement is QOU.MathlibExt.pivotalOfRibbon, which asks for a balancing and uses θ ≫ drinfeldIso. It lives in QOU.MathlibExt.BraidedTensorDual, downstream of the twist. Note this costs less than it appears: Ribbon.lean makes every symmetric category balanced, so FGModuleCat K and friends still get a pivotal structure by instance search.

                  The fact underlying the old instance is untouched and still here: drinfeldIso together with drinfeldIso_naturality is a natural isomorphism 𝟭 C ≅ ᘁᘁ on any braided right-rigid category. Only its promotion to a PivotalCategory instance was wrong.
