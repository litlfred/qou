    Yekutieli Non-Abelian Integration — discrete holonomy layer #

    This file builds the discrete / lattice core of Yekutieli's non-abelian multiplicative integration: the path-ordered product (holonomy) of a path presented as an ordered list of segment transports, and the plaquette holonomy / discrete curvature of a square loop.

    What is and isn't formalised here #

    • Formalised, sorry-free (algebraic layer). The path-ordered product is a non-commutative List.prod in a monoid/group; its structural laws — concatenation multiplicativity (holonomy_append, the composition law of the non-abelian line integral), the empty/singleton evaluations, and the reverse-path inverse (holonomy_reverse_map_inv) — are proved outright. The plaquette holonomy of a square loop and the flatness of a pure-gauge (exact) connection (loopHolonomyPureGauge_eq_one) — the discrete non-abelian analogue of "the curvature of an exact connection vanishes", i.e. d² = 0 at lattice level — are proved by group telescoping.

    • NOT formalised (the continuum, genuinely not in Mathlib). The path-ordered exponential P exp ∫_γ A as the mesh→0 limit of these ordered products, the smooth curvature 2-form F = dA + A ∧ A, and the continuum non-abelian Stokes theorem on surfaces. These are the analytic statements the yekutieli_leading_coefficient lemma still carries as the axiomatised YekutieliStokesNormalisation class. This file is the foundational algebraic layer below that class, not a discharge of it (AGENTS.md "Axiomatization is NOT a Proof").

    References #

    • Ref: [yekutieli2015] https://doi.org/10.1142/9537 — non-abelian multiplicative integration and Stokes' theorem on surfaces.
    • Ref: [manuscript] quantum-observable-universe/brings-surface/yekutieli-leading-coefficient.md

    Base ring (§7c). Purely algebraic: a generic non-commutative Monoid / Group G. No ℝ, no ordering, no Real.* — the discrete holonomy is an ordered product, independent of any archimedean structure.

    Path-ordered product (discrete non-abelian line integral).

    The holonomy of a path presented as an ordered list of segment transports [g₀, g₁, …, g_{n-1}] is their ordered product g₀ * g₁ * … * g_{n-1} — segments composed in path order. This is the discrete / lattice realisation of the path-ordered exponential P exp ∫_γ A; the continuum limit (mesh → 0) is analytic and is not formalised here.

      Plaquette holonomy of a pure-gauge (exact) connection.

      Assign group elements h₀, h₁, h₂, h₃ to the four vertices of a square and take the connection to be exact / pure gauge: the transport along the edge from vertex i to vertex j is hᵢ * hⱼ⁻¹ (a discrete gradient). The boundary holonomy of the square loop v₀ → v₁ → v₂ → v₃ → v₀ is the ordered product of the four edge transports, listed in path order (the first edge v₀ → v₁ leftmost, per the holonomy convention).

          Discrete non-abelian Stokes for a plaquette strip #

          Model a strip of plaquettes by the parallel transport g : ℕ → G from a common basepoint to each successive rung of the strip. The based curvature of plaquette i is the increment Fᵢ = g_{i+1} · gᵢ⁻¹ (discrete Stokes: curvature = increment of transport). The theorem below shows the ordered product of these curvatures over the strip equals the boundary holonomy — the internal transports cancel pairwise. This is the one-dimensional non-abelian Stokes theorem, of which loopHolonomyPureGauge_eq_one is the closed-loop special case.

              Gluing law — subdivision additivity of discrete Stokes #

              The strip theorem above is the boundary of the segment 0..n. For a tiled region built from many segments, the discrete Stokes theorem is additive under gluing: adjacent tiles sharing a cut compose, so the boundary of a subdivided region is the ordered product of the tile boundaries. This is the algebraic principle that lets a 2D tiling be assembled from plaquettes; combined with stripHolonomy_telescope it expresses any tiled boundary as an ordered product of based curvatures.
