    From an Artin–Wedderburn equivalence to the central idempotent system.

    An algebra equivalence A ≃ₐ M₁ × ⋯ × M_N — which IsSemisimpleRing.exists_algEquiv_pi_matrix_divisionRing_finite supplies for any finite-dimensional semisimple algebra — transports the standard idempotents of the product back to A. The result is exactly the data QOU.Hecke.WedderburnDecomp asks for: idempotent, pairwise orthogonal, central, summing to 1.

    The first three of those four come straight from Mathlib (CompleteOrthogonalIdempotents.single, transported by .map); centrality is supplied here.

    What this does not give, and it is the whole remaining gap: WedderburnDecomp indexes its idempotents by Partition n, while Artin–Wedderburn produces an index of unconstrained size. Matching the two — that the blocks of H_n(q) are indexed by the partitions of n, and there are no others — is Mathas (1999) §3.43, which is not in Mathlib and not in this tree. So this closes the construction of the idempotents and leaves the indexing open.
