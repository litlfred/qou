    QOU.MathlibExt.OfModuleAsModule — the ofModule / asModule round-trip #

    Closes bean qou-ltzf.

    Mathlib turns a k[G]-module M into a representation (Representation.ofModule M, on the type synonym RestrictScalars k (MonoidAlgebra k G) M) and a representation back into a k[G]-module (Representation.asModule). What it does not provide at this pin is the round-trip: that (ofModule M).asModule is M as a k[G]-module. Measured, not assumed — exact? cannot close

    example [IsSimpleModule (MonoidAlgebra k G) M] :
    IsSimpleModule (MonoidAlgebra k G) (Representation.ofModule (k := k) (G := G) M).asModule

    The ingredients are all there; only the assembly is missing. Representation.asModuleEquiv and RestrictScalars.addEquiv compose to an additive equivalence (ofModule M).asModule ≃+ M, and Representation.smul_ofModule_asModule says that composite is k[G]-linear — which is exactly the hypothesis AddEquiv.toLinearEquiv wants.

    Why it is wanted #

    Any argument that takes a simple submodule of k[G] and feeds it to a theorem stated about representations needs this. Concretely it is what stands between the covariance-kernel epic's W1+W3 bricks and the injectivity of the Specht action map k[S_n] →ₐ[k] ∏_μ End_k(S^μ): simple submodules of k[S_n] arrive as IsSimpleModule (MonoidAlgebra k G) ↥N, while SpechtClassification.specht_exhausts_irreducibles wants IsSimpleModule … ρ.asModule.

    Base ring (§7c) #

    Generic: [CommSemiring k] / [Monoid G] for the equivalence, strengthened to [Ring]-level typeclasses only where IsSimpleModule requires them. No ℝ, no ordering.
