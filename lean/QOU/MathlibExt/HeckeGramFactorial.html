    QOU.MathlibExt.HeckeGramFactorial — the end-to-end Gram=[n]_q! hook #

    Brick qou-twwf interface (epic qou-yi7h). Closes the gap between the abstract Gram-determinant criterion (RegTraceGram) and the quantum arithmetic (QIntegerFactorial) into a single consumable theorem:

    Hook. Let A be a finite-dimensional K-algebra, b a finite basis, q ≠ 1 Wenzl-generic, and c a unit. If the regular-trace Gram determinant in b equals c · [n]_q!, then A is semisimple.

    This is the exact shape a T_w-basis computation for H_n(q) plugs into: supply (b, c, proof det = c·[n]_q!) — the Schur/Poincaré identity det(Gram) = [n]_q! up to a unit (bricks qou-lrz1 + qou-twwf) — and semisimplicity follows with no further Hecke input. Everything on the Lean side of that identity is now proven sorry-free:

    • det(Gram) ≠ 0 ⇒ semisimple — isSemisimpleRing_of_regTraceGram_det_ne_zero (via nondegenerate_iff_det_ne_zero + the B3d keystone);
    • [n]_q! ≠ 0 ⇔ q generic — qFactorial_ne_zero_iff (B1);
    • the n = 2 determinant value — QOU.Hecke.H2CompanionRep.h2GramFromRep_det (det = [2]_q², computed from the regular representation).

    The one remaining open input is the general determinant value in the n!-dimensional T_w basis — the deep classical Schur-element product formula — which no fabricated instance may stand in for.
