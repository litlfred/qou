    QOU.MathlibExt.CarrierReindexBridge — the Nat.Partition ↔ YoungDiagram reindex facts #

    The two facts that let the Nat.Partition n-carrier fake-degree sum be reindexed to the {ν : YoungDiagram // card = n}-carrier telescope sum, via partitionShapeEquiv:

    • numStandardYoungTableaux of the equiv-image is the partition's dimSYT (definitional);
    • the equiv-image's rowLens is the partition's toList.

    Both import only already-built corpus modules. Used by NormalizedSumClosure to discharge the reindex step of the telescope closure of normalizedSum n q = 1.

    theorem QOU.MathlibExt.CarrierReindexBridge.numSTY_partitionShapeEquiv {n : ℕ} (p : n.Partition) :
    Mathlib.numStandardYoungTableaux ↑((Mathlib.partitionShapeEquiv n) p) = Mathlib.dimSYT p

    The SYT count of the partitionShapeEquiv-image is the partition's dimSYT (definitional: dimSYT p := numStandardYoungTableaux (partitionShape p)).

    (↑((Mathlib.partitionShapeEquiv n) p)).rowLens = Mathlib.PartitionListBridge.toList p
