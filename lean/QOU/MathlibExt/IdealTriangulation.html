    Ideal tetrahedra #

    source
    structure QOU.MathlibExt.IdealTetrahedron :
    Type

    An ideal tetrahedron, as its shape parameter.

    Four ideal vertices on the sphere at infinity, normalised to 0, 1, ∞, z. The condition 0 < Im z says the tetrahedron is nondegenerate and positively oriented; degenerate (flat) tetrahedra are exactly the real shapes, and are excluded.

    • shape : ℂ

      The shape parameter.

    • im_pos : 0 < self.shape.im

      Nondegenerate and positively oriented.

                Relabelling, and volume invariance #

                z ↦ 1/(1−z) does not change the tetrahedron — it changes which edge-pair is called "first". The three dihedral angles are cyclically permuted, so the volume is unchanged. This is not a coincidence to be checked numerically; it is Lob a + Lob b + Lob c being symmetric under cycling its arguments.

                source
                theorem QOU.MathlibExt.IdealTetrahedron.shape_cycle₂ (T : IdealTetrahedron) (hz0 : T.shape ≠ 0) (h1z : 1 - T.shape ≠ 0) :
                1 / (1 - 1 / (1 - T.shape)) = 1 - 1 / T.shape

                1/(1 − 1/(1−z)) = 1 − 1/z: the second edge parameter of the rotated tetrahedron is the third of the original.

                source
                theorem QOU.MathlibExt.IdealTetrahedron.shape_cycle₃ (T : IdealTetrahedron) :
                1 - 1 / (1 / (1 - T.shape)) = T.shape

                1 − 1/(1/(1−z)) = z: the third edge parameter of the rotated tetrahedron is the first of the original.

                Needs no non-vanishing hypothesis, which the unused-variable linter caught: Lean's 1/0 = 0 makes both sides 1 in the degenerate case 1 − z = 0, so the identity holds unconditionally.

                        Gluing equations #

                        The combinatorics of how the tetrahedra are glued, expressed as exponent data: for each edge class k, each tetrahedron i and each edge-pair j, how many times that edge-pair is glued around k.

                        Two conditions are recorded, and they are not equivalent:

                        • SatisfiesEdgeEquations — the full gluing equation, ∏ param^exp = 1. This constrains modulus and argument.
                        • SatisfiesAngleEquations — the dihedral angles around each edge sum to 2π. This is the angle-structure condition (Casson–Rivin, and what the corpus's m004/L6a4 polytopes encode). It is the argument half only.

                        ⚠ Neither condition, alone or together, certifies that a triangulation is a triangulation of a given manifold. That is topology, and is not modelled here — see the module header.
