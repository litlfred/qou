    QOU.MathlibExt.BraidedQuantumDimension — the braided quantum dimension, and its duality symmetry #

    The braided quantum dimension of an object A with a chosen right dual B is the scalar

    qDimWith A B = η_ A B ≫ (β_ A B).hom ≫ ε_ A B  :  𝟙_C ⟶ 𝟙_C

    — the braided, twistless closure of the identity. Mathlib has the coevaluation, the evaluation and the braiding, but no name for their composite and no lemma about it.

    This is not the ribbon trace, and calling it that is a category error. The ribbon trace carries the balancing twist, η ≫ (θ_A ▷ B) ≫ β ≫ ε; no θ appears above and none can, because BalancedCategory is not in scope here. The two agree exactly when θ = 𝟙. The genuine twisted trace is QOU.MathlibExt.qDimTwistedWith, and the difference is not cosmetic: sphericality (left trace = right trace) is provable for the twisted one from the ribbon axiom and is structurally unavailable for this one, since the two twistless closures differ by the monodromy, which no braided-rigid axiom kills.

    What the twistless closure does have, and keeps: qDimWith_congr_dual and qDimWith_rightDual below, both on a plain braided (right-rigid) category with no balanced or ribbon structure — the latter is strictly cheaper here than its twisted counterpart, which needs the ribbon axiom.

    Main statements #

    • qDimWith_congr_dual — the quantum dimension does not depend on the choice of dual. For any two exact pairings A ⊣ B₁ and A ⊣ B₂, the two scalars agree. The proof transports η and ε across rightDualIso (Mathlib's coevaluation_comp_rightAdjointMate and rightAdjointMate_comp_evaluation at f = 𝟙) and cancels the comparison map by braiding naturality. This is the quantum-dimension analogue of rightAdjointMate_congr_dual in PivotalCategory.lean.
    • qDimWith_swap — read against the swapped pairing, which makes X a right dual of Xᘁ, the dimension of Xᘁ collapses to that of X: the swapped coevaluation carries a β⁻¹ that cancels the trace's own β on the nose.
    • qDimWith_rightDual — the braided quantum dimension is conjugation-symmetric, qDim(Xᘁ) = qDim(X), unconditionally in any braided right-rigid category.

    Why this is not the statement the corpus expected to need #

    KeystonePivotalSpherical.lean factors this symmetry into two hypotheses — pivotal transport (qDimObj (Xᘁ) = leftQDim X) and sphericality — and KeystoneRibbonCategory.lean recorded the general-object case as blocked, on the reasoning that qDimObj (Xᘁ) traverses the double dual Xᘁᘁ, which a braided right-rigid category does not identify with X, so the statement was "not merely unproved but not expressible as a computation on the existing data".

    The premise is right and the conclusion does not follow. Xᘁᘁ genuinely is not identified with X by that data — but the dimension never needs the identification. It needs only that the dimension of a fixed object is the same whichever dual is used to compute it, and X is also a right dual of Xᘁ (BraidedCategory.exactPairing_swap). Dual-independence is strictly weaker than double-dual identification, and it is what the trace actually consumes.

    The conditional theorems in KeystonePivotalSpherical.lean remain valid; they are simply no longer the only route, and neither hypothesis is needed for this conclusion. Sphericality is untouched and is a genuinely separate condition.

    Name collision (§notation-collisions) #

    QOU.BraidKnot.C2QDimTranspose.qDim_conj is a different theorem: d_q(λ) = d_q(λ') for a partition λ over ℝ, the transpose-invariance of the combinatorial hook-length q-dimension. Nothing here refers to it. The declaration below is named qDimWith_rightDual to keep the two apart.

      The braided quantum dimension is conjugation-symmetric: qDim(Xᘁ) = qDim(X), in any braided right-rigid category, with no pivotal, spherical or transport hypothesis.

      Both duals of Xᘁ — the canonical Xᘁᘁ and the swapped X — give the same dimension by qDimWith_congr_dual; against the swapped one it is qDim(X) by qDimWith_swap.

      Distinct from QOU.BraidKnot.C2QDimTranspose.qDim_conj, which is the partition-transpose statement d_q(λ) = d_q(λ') over ℝ.
