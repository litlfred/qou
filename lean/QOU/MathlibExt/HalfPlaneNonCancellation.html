    QOU.MathlibExt.HalfPlaneNonCancellation — an oscillatory sum that cannot cancel #

    Bean qou-mlgp Gap 5.

    The asymmetry this addresses #

    For a sum of complex numbers Mathlib gives the upper bound for free — norm_sum_le is the triangle inequality — and nothing at all in the other direction. That asymmetry is exactly the corpus's blocker. Every volume-conjecture instance needs

    (2π/N) · log‖⟨K⟩_N‖  →  Vol(K),

    the upper half of which follows from ‖Σ zₖ‖ ≤ Σ‖zₖ‖ plus a majorant estimate, and the lower half of which needs to know the phases did not conspire to cancel. The corpus has the upper halves (norm_whiteheadLinkKashaev_le, the W6 majorant rate) and is stuck on the lower ones (WhiteheadLinkLowerRate.lower, VdVWhiteheadLemma4.phase_cost).

    What is actually provable, cheaply #

    Full non-cancellation for an arbitrary oscillatory sum is false, and proving it in the cases that matter is the method of steepest descent — the expensive thing this bean's Gap 5 is about. But there is a large, elementary special case:

    terms confined to a half-plane cannot cancel.

    If every term lies in the closed cone of half-angle α < π/2 about some direction, the sum keeps at least cos α of its mass. No analysis, no asymptotics — one rotation and the triangle inequality in the other direction (Re w ≤ ‖w‖).

    Measured on the Whitehead-chain sums at q = e^{2πi/N}, the inner clasp sum is exactly phase-free (arc 0.00°) and at the neighbouring root e^{2πi/(N+½)} it spans ≈ 90° at the dominant index — comfortably inside a half-plane, giving a clean factor 1/√2.

    ⚠ What this lemma does NOT reach, and why — measured 2026-08-31 #

    An earlier revision of this header claimed the lemma "settles the inner sums outright and isolates the outer one as the real obstruction". Neither half of that is a gain, and the file should not advertise one.

    • The inner sums are already settled without it. In the corpus's own transcription (QOU/Knot/WhiteheadLinkScaffold.lean) the inner sum Σ_k S_{n,k} is a sum of real positive terms — whiteheadTerm_pos, whiteheadLink_inner_pos — so ‖Σ‖ = Σ‖·‖ holds on the nose and there is nothing for a cos α factor to buy.
    • The outer sum is not merely "not confined"; it is confined nowhere, and the failure gets worse with N. The Fresnel phase is θ_n = −n(n+1−N)π/(2N) (whiteheadLinkPhase), whose total sweep over n ∈ [0, N) is (N−1)²π/(8N) ≈ Nπ/8 radians — unbounded. Measured arc covered mod 2π: 135.0° at N = 8, 303.8° at N = 32, 340.3° at N = 128, 349.5° at N = 256, i.e. → 360°. A half-plane hypothesis here would need α ≳ 175°, and cos α < 0 makes the conclusion vacuous.

    So the only sum in the corpus whose lower bound is open (VdVWhiteheadLemma4 / WhiteheadLinkLowerRate) is exactly the one this lemma cannot touch, and a conditional theorem "phases half-plane-confined ⟹ Lemma 4" was deliberately not written: its hypothesis is measured false, so it would be vacuous content dressed as progress. What is recorded instead is the measurement, which closes the elementary route.

    The lemma remains correct, upstreamable, and the right tool wherever a genuinely localised (windowed) sum appears — but it has no consumer in this corpus today, and saying so is more useful than a forced one.

    Scope #

    Pure complex analysis — no knots, no Kashaev invariant, no volume. Upstreamable unchanged.

    Archimedean (ℂ).

    theorem QOU.MathlibExt.norm_sum_ge_of_rotated_re {ι : Type u_1} (s : Finset ι) (z : ι → ℂ) (u : ℂ) (hu : ‖u‖ = 1) (c : ℝ) (h : ∀ i ∈ s, c * ‖z i‖ ≤ (u * z i).re) :
    c * ∑ i ∈ s, ‖z i‖ ≤ ‖∑ i ∈ s, z i‖

    No cancellation after a rotation, in terms of real parts.

    If some unit u rotates every term so that its real part is at least c times its norm, then the sum keeps at least the fraction c of the total mass.

    This is the whole content; the arg-based statement below is a repackaging. The proof is three steps — sum the pointwise bound, pull the rotation out of the sum, and use Re w ≤ ‖w‖, which is the reverse-direction triangle inequality that norm_sum_le does not provide.

    c is unconstrained: it may be negative, in which case the conclusion is weak but true, and c = 0 recovers the trivial bound.

    theorem QOU.MathlibExt.cos_mul_sum_norm_le_norm_sum {ι : Type u_1} (s : Finset ι) (z : ι → ℂ) (θ : ℝ) {α : ℝ} (hα0 : 0 ≤ α) (hα : α ≤ Real.pi / 2) (h : ∀ i ∈ s, |(Complex.exp (-↑θ * Complex.I) * z i).arg| ≤ α) :
    Real.cos α * ∑ i ∈ s, ‖z i‖ ≤ ‖∑ i ∈ s, z i‖

    Terms in a half-plane keep a cos α share of their mass.

    If, after rotating by e^{-iθ}, every term has argument within α ≤ π/2 of the real axis — i.e. the terms lie in a closed cone of half-angle α about direction θ — then

    `cos α · Σ‖zᵢ‖ ≤ ‖Σ zᵢ‖`.

    At α = π/2 this degenerates to 0 ≤ ‖Σ zᵢ‖, which is the honest boundary: a closed half-plane permits 1 + (−1) · i·i-style cancellation only in the limit, and the bound must vanish there. Strictly inside, the constant is strictly positive and independent of the number of terms — which is what makes it useful against a sum whose length grows with N.

    Zero terms are allowed and need no hypothesis: arg 0 = 0, so they satisfy the bound automatically.
