    QOU.MathlibExt.OctonionSU3Stabilizer — colour 𝔰𝔲(3) ⊂ 𝔤₂ = Der(𝕆) #

    The stabilizer of a unit imaginary octonion inside the derivation algebra 𝔤₂ = Der(𝕆) (dim = 14, octonion_derivation_dim). Fix e : 𝕆 and evaluate a derivation there:

    evOct e : Der(𝕆) →ₗ[ℚ] 𝕆, D ↦ D(e), su3 e := ker (evOct e).

    su3 e is the subalgebra of derivations fixing e. For a unit imaginary e this is the Günaydin–Gürsey colour SU(3) ⊂ G₂ = Aut(𝕆) (the "fix an imaginary direction → Im 𝕆 = 1 ⊕ 3 ⊕ 3̄" subgroup), with

    dim 𝔰𝔲(3) = 14 − rank(ev_e) = 14 − 6 = 8 = dim SU(3), so 14 − 8 = 6 = dim S⁶ = dim G₂/SU(3).

    This file lands the rank–nullity reduction finrank su3 = 14 − rank(ev_e) and the gate finrank su3 = 8 ⟺ rank(ev_e) = 6 — sorry-free, on the corpus's own Der𝕆 — mirroring the sibling dim = 14 ⟺ rank = 50 gate (DerivationRankNullity.octCarrier_finrank_ker_eq_14_iff). The value rank = 6 is established by exact rational linear algebra (computations/octonion_su3_stabilizer_probe.py, witness octonion-su3-stabilizer.witness.json, merged), and its explicit Lean proof (a 6×6 image minor + the ⟨e₀,e⟩^⊥ bound from derivation_annihilates_one and norm-preservation) is the follow-up. Base ring ℚ; nothing archimedean.
