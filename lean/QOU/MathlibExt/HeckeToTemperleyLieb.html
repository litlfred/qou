    H_n(q) ↠ TL_n(δ) — the Temperley–Lieb quotient map, as an algebra hom #

    Bean qou-cds1. rem:hecke-radical-is-temperley-lieb says, and has said throughout, that no formalised statement of H_n(q)/rad ≅ TL_n(q) exists. This file does not supply the isomorphism. It supplies the surjection, which is the half that needs no semisimplicity input:

    there is a surjective R-algebra map H_n(q) ↠ TL_n(δ),

    reducing the isomorphism to a single question — is its kernel the trace radical? That question is stated at the end, and not answered.

    Why this needed no new infrastructure #

    Both algebras were already presented in this corpus as RingQuots of a free algebra with their universal properties proved (lift / hom_ext / exists_unique_lift, in IwahoriHecke and in TemperleyLieb). So the map is built by lift, and the entire content is checking that one family in TL satisfies the two Hecke relations.

    The normalisation, and why v rather than √q #

    IwahoriHecke is in the Iwahori normalisation (T_i − q)(T_i + 1) = 0; TemperleyLieb carries the balanced relations e_i² = δ e_i, e_i e_j e_i = e_i. Those do not match under any shift T_i ↦ a − e_i: the quadratic relation then forces δ = q + 1 and the braid relation forces δ = q + q⁻¹, and the two agree only at q = 1. The fix is the standard one, carrying the square root as primitive datum:

    T_i  ↦  v·e_i − 1 ,      with   v·δ = v² + 1 ,   q = v² .

    Read at v = q^{1/2} that is δ = v + v⁻¹, the usual loop parameter. But no inverse and no square root appears anywhere below. The hypothesis is the polynomial identity v * δ = v ^ 2 + 1, meaningful over any commutative ring — so §7c holds without effort: nothing is stated over ℝ, nothing needs 0 < q, and v is not required to be a unit until surjectivity, where it genuinely is.

    That is not mere convenience. Real.sqrt q would drag a purely algebraic statement across the substrate→archimedean wall for no reason, and the √q-vs-q distinction is exactly the convention-C/D confusion this corpus has spent months draining.

    The shape of the proof, which is smaller than expected #

    The two relation checks are ring identities about two elements, with no reference to algebraMap, centrality, or the ambient algebra. They are proved that way in §Abstract below and then instantiated once. The braid check is the surprise: writing u_i = v·e_i, the hypotheses are u² = Q·u + u and u_i u_j u_i = Q·u_i, and both sides of the braid relation reduce to

    −u_i u_j − u_j u_i + u_i + u_j − 1 ,

    in which Q does not appear at all. So the braid relation needs no compatibility between q and δ beyond what the loop relation already forces, and the proof needs no commutation hypothesis on Q. The v·δ = v² + 1 condition enters only through the loop relation.

    -- Ref: [jones1985] https://doi.org/10.2307/1971403 — the Temperley–Lieb -- quotient of the Hecke algebra and the Markov trace that detects it.

    The diagram hypothesis #

    source
    structure QOU.MathlibExt.HeckeToTemperleyLieb.IsTypeADiagram {B : Type u_1} (M : B → B → ℕ) (Adj : B → B → Prop) :
    Prop

    Type-A compatibility between a Coxeter matrix and an adjacency predicate.

    IwahoriHecke is indexed by a Coxeter matrix M; TemperleyLieb by an adjacency predicate Adj. This says they describe the same diagram: 3 on adjacent pairs (the braid relation), 2 on distinct non-adjacent ones (commutation), 1 on the diagonal.

    A structure rather than four loose hypotheses because all of them are needed together at every use, and because adj_irrefl is the one that is easy to forget — without it M i i would have to be both 1 and 3.

    • diag (i : B) : M i i = 1

      M i i = 1: a generator braids trivially with itself.

    • adj (i j : B) : Adj i j → M i j = 3

      Adjacent generators satisfy the length-3 braid relation.

    • far (i j : B) : i ≠ j → ¬Adj i j → M i j = 2

      Distinct non-adjacent generators commute.

    • adj_irrefl (i : B) : ¬Adj i i

      Adjacency is irreflexive.

    • adj_symm (i j : B) : Adj i j → Adj j i

      Adjacency is symmetric.

      The length-3 braid relation, abstractly.

      Given the loop relation for each of u₁, u₂ and the two adjacency relations u₁u₂u₁ = Q·u₁, u₂u₁u₂ = Q·u₂, the shifted elements u − 1 braid.

      Instantiation in TL #

      Written with the scalar action • rather than algebraMap _ * _. That is not cosmetic: the three relations all need the scalar moved past a generator, and algebraMap R A v * e i * (algebraMap R A v * e j) cannot be reassociated into algebraMap R A v * algebraMap R A v * (e i * e j) by noncomm_ring — the second scalar is trapped between two generators and only centrality frees it. In • form that step is smul_mul_smul_comm, a single rewrite.

            TL as an explicit quotient of H — the presentation-level isomorphism #

            The engineering obstruction noted below (no first isomorphism theorem for noncommutative rings in Mathlib) is routed around rather than waited on, and the result is stronger than what it would have given. Instead of quotienting by the kernel — an ideal named but not described — we quotient by the named relators:

            TL_n(δ)  ≅  H_n(q) / ⟨ U_i U_j U_i − q·U_i : i ~ j ⟩ ,      U_i := T_i + 1

            which is the classical statement, and says which relations you add. The quotient is a RingQuot, so every instance comes for free and no Ideal / TwoSidedIdeal / RingCon machinery is needed.

                            🛑 What remains open, stated precisely #

                            An isomorphism IS proved here — heckeTLEquiv:

                            TL_n(δ)  ≅  H_n(v²) / ⟨ U_i U_j U_i − v²·U_i : i ~ j ⟩ ,   U_i = T_i + 1

                            sorry-free, at general index type. What is not proved is that the ideal on the right is the trace radical, which is what rem:hecke-radical-is-temperley-lieb asserts.

                            Open. ⟨U_i U_j U_i − v²·U_i⟩ = traceRadical tr_M, for the convention-D Markov trace on H_n(q) — equivalently tlKernel hδ hd = traceRadical tr_M.

                            ⊆ is a computation: the trace kills each relator. ⊇ says the trace sees everything outside the ideal, which is semisimplicity of TL_n(δ) at the relevant δ — exactly the Wedderburn input this construction was built to avoid needing. The corpus has the positivity half of that (TemperleyLiebTower.yw_pos, terms_zero_of_weighted_sum_zero) and does not have the block decomposition it would be applied to. That is genuine mathematics, and it is the whole of what is left.

                            An engineering obstruction that was routed around, not waited on #

                            The first version of this file stopped at surjectivity and recorded a second blocker: turning "surjective with kernel I" into "H/I ≅ TL" needs a first isomorphism theorem for noncommutative rings, and Mathlib has none usable here — Ideal.quotientKerAlgEquivOfSurjective is stated for Ideal, which is Submodule R R and therefore the commutative notion, while TwoSidedIdeal has ker and no quotient-equivalence under any quotientKerEquivOfSurjective-shaped name (checked across TwoSidedIdeal/ and RingTheory/Congruence/).

                            That is still true, and it no longer matters: quotienting by the named relators via RingQuot sidesteps it entirely, and gives a stronger statement than the kernel version would have — the ideal is presented by generators rather than merely named. Recorded because the obstruction is real and the next agent who wants H/ker ≅ … for some other map in this corpus will hit it.

                            Nothing above is asserted by a class, an axiom or a sorry. tlKernel is a definition, tlRelator_mem_ker a theorem exhibiting one relator inside it, and the open item appears only in this comment. A conditional class asserting the ideal equality would satisfy the propagation audit while adding no mathematics, which §"Axiomatization is NOT a Proof" forbids.

                            What this file does NOT claim #

                            • Not that H_n(q)/rad ≅ TL_n(q). The isomorphism proved here is modulo the relator ideal; identifying that ideal with the trace radical is the open item above.
                            • Not that the relators generate tlKernel — heckeTLEquiv says the relator quotient is TL, and heckeToTL_surjective says H ↠ TL, but the equality tlKernel = ⟨relators⟩ is not separately stated. It follows from heckeTLEquiv and is simply not written down.
                            • Not anything about braid groups, diagram bases, or dim TL_n = catalan n. B here is an arbitrary index type with an adjacency predicate; specialising to Fin (n-1) with TemperleyLieb.adjFin is immediate but is not done, because nothing above needs it.
                            • Not that heckeToTL is injective anywhere. It is not, whenever the kernel is nonzero — which is the point of its being a quotient map.
