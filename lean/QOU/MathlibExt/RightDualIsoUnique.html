    QOU.MathlibExt.RightDualIsoUnique — uniqueness of the right-dual comparison #

    Mathlib defines CategoryTheory.rightDualIso, the canonical comparison between two right duals of the same object, and then proves exactly one thing about it: rightDualIso_id. Anything that needs to identify a map as the comparison, or to compose two comparisons, has to re-derive the characterisation first.

    This file supplies that characterisation and its two immediate consequences:

    • rightDualIso_hom_evaluation — the comparison is compatible with the evaluations;
    • rightDualIso_hom_unique — and it is the only map that is, so exhibiting any map with that property identifies it;
    • rightDualIso_hom_trans — comparisons compose.

    The engine is whiskerRight_evaluation_injective: pairing against an evaluation is injective, because tensorRightHomEquiv carries g ↦ (g ▷ Y') ≫ ε to g ≫ (λ_ Y).inv, and a unitor is a mono. That is the snake identity doing its job, with the morphism carried out of the way by the adjunction rather than by a string-diagram argument.

    Nothing here needs a braiding, and nothing needs rigidity beyond the exact pairings named in each statement — which is why these live in their own file rather than alongside the pivotal structures that first motivated them.

    theorem QOU.MathlibExt.rightDualIso_hom_unique {C : Type u_1} [CategoryTheory.Category.{u_2, u_1} C] [CategoryTheory.MonoidalCategory C] {A B₁ B₂ : C} (p₁ : CategoryTheory.ExactPairing A B₁) (p₂ : CategoryTheory.ExactPairing A B₂) (f : B₁ ⟶ B₂) (h : CategoryTheory.CategoryStruct.comp (CategoryTheory.MonoidalCategoryStruct.whiskerRight f A) (ε_ A B₂) = ε_ A B₁) :
    f = (CategoryTheory.rightDualIso p₁ p₂).hom

    The comparison of two right duals is unique. Any map commuting with the evaluations is rightDualIso. Mathlib has rightDualIso and rightDualIso_id and nothing else about it, so this is the missing characterisation — and it is what makes the comparison usable in proofs, since it turns "construct the map" into "check one identity".

    theorem QOU.MathlibExt.coev_comp_rightDualIso {C : Type u_1} [CategoryTheory.Category.{u_2, u_1} C] [CategoryTheory.MonoidalCategory C] {A B₁ B₂ : C} (p₁ : CategoryTheory.ExactPairing A B₁) (p₂ : CategoryTheory.ExactPairing A B₂) :
    CategoryTheory.CategoryStruct.comp (η_ A B₁) (CategoryTheory.MonoidalCategoryStruct.whiskerLeft A (CategoryTheory.rightDualIso p₁ p₂).hom) = η_ A B₂

    The coevaluation identity that characterises rightDualIso — the mirror of rightDualIso_hom_evaluation.

    Where the evaluation form says the comparison of two right duals commutes with the two evaluations, this says it carries one coevaluation to the other. Proved by transporting both sides through tensorLeftHomEquiv for the pairing p₁ — the choice matters: the p₂ equivalence leaves a mixed η_{p₂} against ε_{p₁} that is no snake, while the p₁ one reduces each side to (ρ_ B₁).hom ≫ (rightDualIso p₁ p₂).hom after one use of the evaluation form.
