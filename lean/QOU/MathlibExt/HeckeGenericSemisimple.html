    QOU.MathlibExt.HeckeGenericSemisimple — the Wenzl-criterion bridge #

    The composition brick (epic qou-hnhg) that joins the abstract semisimplicity engine (track 3, TraceFormSemisimple) to the quantum arithmetic (track 5/B1, QIntegerFactorial). It reduces the entire remaining Hecke problem to a single Gram-determinant identity:

    Bridge. Let A be a finite-dimensional K-algebra and q ≠ 1. If the regular trace form of A is nondegenerate exactly when the quantum factorial [n]_q! ≠ 0, then genericity of q (QGeneric q n) implies A is semisimple.

    Applied to A = H_n(q) with its Markov trace, the hypothesis hgram is exactly the Schur-element / Gram-determinant computation det (Gram) = [n]_q! (up to a unit) — the one thing the concrete Iwahori–Hecke construction (track 2, beans qou-w8hn … qou-8irr) must still supply. Everything else — genericity ⇔ [n]_q! ≠ 0 (B1) and nondegenerate-trace-form ⇒ semisimple (B3d) — is already proven sorry-free. This is the crispest possible statement of what "discharge WenzlCriterion" now requires.
