    TowerSpan reduced to one local rewriting rule #

    Bean qou-301d. Continuation of QOU.MathlibExt.DisjointBlockTraceLaw, which derives the disjoint-block trace law from TowerSpan — the hypothesis that each step of the level filtration is spanned by the previous stage together with single sandwiches v * g (M+1) * w.

    TowerSpan is a claim about every element of a filtration step, which is an awkward thing either to prove or to check. This file replaces it with a claim about one element shape:

    SandwichReduces B m  :  ∀ c ∈ B,  g m * c * g m  ∈  B + span (sandwich B m)

    i.e. a doubled generator with base material between it collapses back down. towerSpan_of_sandwichReduces shows the two are related in the useful direction: the local rule implies the global decomposition.

    Why this is the right reduction #

    DisjointBlockTraceLaw identifies the obstruction to the unconditional argument as a repeated maximal-level generator — a word g M * x * g M that no rotation can peel. SandwichReduces is precisely the statement that such a word rewrites, and nothing more. So the hypothesis has been moved from "the whole filtration decomposes" to "the one blocking shape reduces", which is

    • smaller — one element shape rather than a submodule containment,
    • local — it mentions a single level m, not the tower, and
    • where the braid relation lives — in the Iwahori-Hecke algebra this is the content of g_i g_{i±1} g_i = g_{i±1} g_i g_{i±1} together with the quadratic relation, which is what lets a doubled generator be pushed down.

    The proof #

    P B m is the candidate submodule B + span (sandwich B m). The work is showing it is closed under multiplication, which needs exactly one non-trivial case:

    (v * g * w) * (v' * g * w')  =  v * (g * (w * v') * g) * w'

    whose middle factor is SandwichReduces applied to w * v' ∈ B. Everything else is mul_base_left / mul_base_right. With multiplication closure, P becomes a subalgebra via Submodule.toSubalgebra and Algebra.adjoin_le finishes.

    🛑 What is NOT established #

    SandwichReduces is not proved for any tower with a growing filtration. This file moves the hypothesis; it does not discharge it. Per AGENTS.md §"Axiomatization is NOT a Proof", a reduction between two unproved statements is progress in shape only, and the honest summary of qou-301d remains: the disjoint-block law, and hence the bond-product reading of the atom word, is measured, not derived.

    The inhabitation caveat from DisjointBlockTraceLaw.towerSpan_unit carries over unchanged — the only witness has a constant filtration, and towerSpan_of_constant shows such a witness satisfies TowerSpan for reasons that have nothing to do with any of this.
