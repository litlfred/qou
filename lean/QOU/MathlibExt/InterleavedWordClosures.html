    The two n = 4 words are two different links: the cross term is a difference of closures #

    Route A, step 1, of the 2026-09-05 binding-gap survey (bean qou-amw3) asks whether the n = 4 interleaving cross term Δ = tr(g₀g₁g₀g₂g₁) − tr(g₀²g₁²g₂) = z(z² − hz − 1) is an Euler characteristic — i.e. whether the categorified trace (HOMFLY-PT homology, whose graded Euler characteristic is the two-variable Ocneanu trace) sees it as one object.

    The first thing to settle is what the two words are, and the Markov axioms answer it exactly. From (T1) cyclicity, (T3) stabilisation and the Hecke quadratic alone:

    tr(g₀ g₁ g₀ g₂ g₁) = z² · tr(g₀³)          (`interleaved_eq_stab_sq_cube`)
tr(g₀² g₁² g₂)     = z  · tr(g₀²)²         (`sequential_eq_stab_sq_sq`)

    The interleaved word is σ³ ∈ B₂ after two Markov stabilisations — its closure is the (positive) trefoil, one component; the sequential word is a stabilised product of two σ² — its closure is the three-component chain of two Hopf links. So the "cross term" is the difference of two link invariants of two different links:

    Δ = z² · tr(σ³) − z · tr(σ²)²             (`crossTerm_eq_closures`)

    At the level of the polynomial both terms are Euler characteristics of Khovanov–Rozansky homology (the trefoil's and the chain's), so a categorification of Δ always exists as a shifted direct sum; what does not follow is a natural chain map between the two whose cone carries Δ, because the two words are not related by any braid move (their closures have different component counts). That negative is recorded in the probe and the survey §7d, not here.

    Everything in this file is over the generic MarkovTrace R A structure of QOU.MathlibExt.OcneanuTrace (base ring §7c: CommRing R), sorry-free, with the same hypotheses as interleaved_sub_sequential.
