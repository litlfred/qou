    QOU.MathlibExt.QSelfSquareCapacity — the q-deformed shell-capacity identity #

    The self-square identity for the q-bracket:

    [n]_q² = Σ_{ℓ=0}^{n-1} [2ℓ+1]_q.

    At q = 1 this is the classical n² = Σ_{ℓ<n} (2ℓ+1) — the arithmetic behind the 2n² = Σ_{ℓ<n} 2(2ℓ+1) electron-shell capacity (Gap 1 of the periodic-table derivation; content predicted-spectra/shell-eigenvalue-properties.md, where each sub-shell ℓ contributes 2(2ℓ+1) and the shell sums to 2n²). Here it is the genuine q-deformed identity over [m]_q = (q^m − q^{-m})/(q − q⁻¹).

    The proof is the telescoping of the one-line difference identity [k+1]_q² − [k]_q² = [2k+1]_q, which itself reduces to (q·q⁻¹ − 1)(a−b)² = 0 (needs only q ≠ 0).

    Sorry-free; unconditional over ℝ with q ≠ 0 and q − q⁻¹ ≠ 0.

    Refs:

    • [macdonald1995] Symmetric Functions and Hall Polynomials, I.3
    • [manuscript] predicted-spectra/shell-eigenvalue-properties (the 2n² capacity)
