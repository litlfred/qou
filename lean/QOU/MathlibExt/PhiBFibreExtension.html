    The def↔contour identification as an honest conjecture class (G1 remainder) #

    The last analytic gap of the fibre-branch Φ_b construction: the constructed real-integral phiB (QOU.MathlibExt.FaddeevPhiBRealIntegral) is numerically pinned to the Faddeev contour integral to 8e-29, but the identification has had no Lean shape — the contour integral itself is outside Mathlib's reach.

    This file gives the identification its honest §3b form without the contour: the open claim is that phiB b is the restriction to the real axis of a full Φ : ℂ → ℂ satisfying the two modular-double shift equations (FaddeevPhi). Those functional equations characterize the contour integral up to the pentagon/Faddeev–Volkov rigidity (see QOU.QBeta.CyclicDilogPentagon.faddeevPhi_unique_of_pentagon for the uniqueness lever on the finite fibre analog), so the class carries exactly the def↔contour content that IS statable. No instance — inhabiting it requires the analytic continuation of the real integral, which is the open input; the 8e-29 pin stays numerical evidence in computations/qbeta/qbeta_phi_b_real_form_pin.py, never a Lean claim.

    The derived theorems show the class is rigid rather than decorative: any extension is pinned by the constructed phiB on the real axis AND on the ib-shifted lattice lines (via quasi-periodicity), and it can never vanish on the real axis (unimodularity of the constructed phase) — so the zero lattice of a genuine extension is strictly off-axis, matching the known analytic structure of the Faddeev integral.

    class QOU.MathlibExt.PhiBFibreExtension (b : ℝ) :
    Type

    The def↔contour identification (§3b conjecture class). The constructed real-integral phiB b extends to a full Faddeev Φ_b on the fibre branch: a function Φ : ℂ → ℂ that (i) restricts to phiB b on the real axis and (ii) satisfies the two modular-double shift equations. The open content is the analytic continuation; no instance is fabricated. (0 < b puts the real b in FaddeevPhi's Re b > 0 scope.)

    • Φ : ℂ → ℂ

      The extension.

    • extends_phiB (x : ℝ) : Φ b ↑x = FaddeevPhiBRealIntegral.phiB b x

      (i) It restricts to the constructed real integral on the real axis.

    • faddeev : FaddeevQuantumDilogarithm.FaddeevPhi (↑b) (Φ b)

      (ii) It satisfies the Faddeev modular-double shift equations.
