    The converse half of Artin–Wedderburn: a finite product of matrix rings over division rings is semisimple.

    Mathlib proves the forward half (IsSemisimpleRing.exists_ringEquiv_pi_matrix_divisionRing) but states the iff only as proof_wanted; this is the direction it is missing.
