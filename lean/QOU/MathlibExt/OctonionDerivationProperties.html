    QOU.MathlibExt.OctonionDerivationProperties — derivations preserve 𝕆 = ℝ·1 ⊕ Im 𝕆 #

    Structural properties of the octonion derivation algebra Der𝕆 (from QOU.MathlibExt.OctonionDerivations), connecting dim Der(𝕆) = 14 = dim 𝔤₂ to the G₂ = Aut(𝕆) mechanism used in the genus-4 octonion-recovery analysis (docs/audits/2026-07-12-octonion-recovery-from-genus4-analysis.md).

    • octMul_one_left / octMul_one_right — e₀ = Pi.single 0 1 is the two-sided unit.
    • derivation_annihilates_one — every octonion derivation kills the identity, D 1 = 0. This is the infinitesimal shadow of no_fixed_unit (QOU.BringsSurface.OctonionObstruction, "an algebra automorphism fixes 1"): a derivation is the tangent to a one-parameter family of automorphisms, and differentiating φ_t(1) = 1 at t = 0 gives D 1 = 0. Hence Der(𝕆) preserves the splitting 𝕆 = ℝ·1 ⊕ Im 𝕆, acting trivially on ℝ·1 and (as the 14-dimensional 𝔤₂) on the 7-dimensional Im 𝕆 where G₂ acts irreducibly.

    Base ring ℚ (the derivation-dimension count needs char ≠ 2, 3); nothing archimedean.
