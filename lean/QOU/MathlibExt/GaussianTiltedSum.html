    The tilted sum equals the tilted integral, up to the same exponentially small relative error as the untilted one.

    e^{L²/a}·√(π/a) is exactly ∫_ℝ e^{−ax² + 2Lx} dx, so this says the tilted Laplace sum is its tilted Laplace integral — which is the statement the constant c_σ is read off from.
