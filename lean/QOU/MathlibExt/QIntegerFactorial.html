    QOU.MathlibExt.QIntegerFactorial — quantum integers, q-factorial, genericity #

    Mathlib has Coxeter groups and IsSemisimpleRing but no quantum integers and no Iwahori–Hecke algebra. This file supplies the first infrastructure brick of the Hecke-semisimplicity attack (bean qou-6566, epic qou-hnhg): the quantum integer [n]_q, the quantum factorial [n]_q! (the Poincaré polynomial of H_n(q)), and the genericity equivalence

    [n]_q! ≠ 0 ⟺ q is not a k-th root of unity for any 2 ≤ k ≤ n,

    for q ≠ 1 over a field. This is exactly the parameter-side condition of Wenzl's semisimplicity criterion (WenzlSchurElement.WenzlGeneric, [wenzl1988]): H_n(q) is semisimple iff [n]_q! ≠ 0. The hard half — [n]_q! ≠ 0 ⟹ H_n(q) semisimple — needs the Hecke algebra + Gram/Schur machinery (beans qou-phir/qou-0y6h/qou-q5bp); this brick discharges the arithmetic side, sorry-free, over a generic field (§7c: no archimedean input until the real 1 < q corollary).

          The genericity equivalence (sorry-free). For q ≠ 1 over a field, the quantum factorial is nonzero iff q is generic at rank n:

          [n]_q! ≠ 0 ⟺ ∀ k ∈ [2, n], q^k ≠ 1.

          This is the arithmetic (parameter) half of Wenzl's semisimplicity criterion for H_n(q).

Any real q > 1 — in particular the substrate q₀ ≈ 1.10998 — has [n]_q! ≠ 0 for every n (indeed > 0 by qFactorial_pos): no quantum integer vanishes, so the parameter-side hypothesis of Wenzl's criterion is discharged.
