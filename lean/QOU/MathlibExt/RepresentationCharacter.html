    Characters of a Representation, without the category layer #

    Mathlib's character theory (FDRep.character, char_linHom, average_char_eq_finrank_invariants, scalar_product_char_eq_finrank_equivariant) is stated for FDRep k G, the category of finite-dimensional representations. Code that carries a bare Representation k G V — as the Specht cluster does — cannot use any of it without first building a bridge through Rep k G ≌ ModuleCat (MonoidAlgebra k G) and the full subcategory inclusion FDRep ⥤ Rep, and Mathlib has no lemma relating CategoryTheory.Simple in FDRep k G to IsSimpleModule (MonoidAlgebra k G).

    This file redevelops the part of the theory that is needed, directly on Representation. Every proof is the FDRep one with the category layer removed; the ingredients (Representation.dualTensorHom_comm, Representation.isProj_averageMap, LinearMap.trace_conj', trace_tensorProduct', trace_transpose') are all already Representation- or LinearMap-level.

    Why it is wanted #

    For the completeness half of the Specht classification the chain is

    #{pairwise non-isomorphic simples} ≤ #conj classes ≤ #partitions,

    whose left step is linear independence of the characters. That rests on

    ⅟|G| • Σ_g χ_V(g⁻¹) · χ_W(g) = finrank_k Hom_G(V, W)

    — charPairing_eq_finrank_homG below. Note it needs no algebraically closed field: only Invertible (|G| : k), which characteristic zero supplies. FDRep.char_orthonormal does carry [IsAlgClosed k], but only to pin the diagonal to 1; a bound does not need that, and the diagonal being merely nonzero is enough for independence.

      1. The character of Hom_k(V, W) #

      linHom ρV ρW is conjugate to dual ρV ⊗ ρW through dualTensorHomEquiv, so its trace is the product of the two traces. This is FDRep.char_linHom with the monoidal-category step replaced by Representation.dualTensorHom_comm.

      2. Averaging: the mean of the character is the dimension of the invariants #

      Representation.isProj_averageMap says the group average is a projection onto invariants; the trace of a projection is the rank of its image.

        4. Schur, at the Representation level #

        5. The count #

        Pairwise non-isomorphic simples have orthogonal characters, characters are class functions, and class functions are at most #conj classes-dimensional.

        Stated for a family of characters as functions rather than a dependent family of carrier types: the caller usually wants to mix representations living in different modules (here, Specht modules of various shapes plus one unknown), and a ι → Type* family with per-index instances is far more painful to assemble than a ι → (G → k).
