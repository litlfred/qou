    The δ rule, proved at the normal-form index #

    Bean qou-cds1. computations/tl_closure_rule_probe.py measured, on all 3456 products at n = 2..7:

    loops = 1   ⟺   k ∈ I  ∧  k-1 ∉ I

    TemperleyLiebClosure proved the mechanism — e_mul_prod_eq_smul: on a word pre ++ k :: post whose prefix is far from k, left multiplication by e_k returns the same word scaled by δ. What was missing was the step from the measured condition to that hypothesis. This file supplies it.

    e_mul_elt_eq_smul :  k ∈ I  →  (∀ j ∈ I, j+1 ≠ k)  →
                     e_k * elt(I,J)  =  δ • elt(I,J)

    So the δ half of the closure rule is now proved as stated, not merely observed on 3456 rows and explained by a sketch.

    Why the condition is what it is #

    k ∈ I puts k at the top of some block, so the word has k in it. k-1 ∉ I forces every earlier block's top to be ≤ k-2 — an earlier top is < k by sortedness, and cannot be k-1 by hypothesis — and descRun_le carries that bound to every letter of those blocks. Two or more below k is exactly "far from k" in e_farCommute's sense. That is the whole content of word_split.

    Every step of the chain is now closed: the bound is descRun_le (TemperleyLiebWordBlocks), the block-top ordering is elems_sorted, and the j_r ≤ i_r that descRun_eq_cons needs is threshold_to_pointwise (TemperleyLiebThresholdBridge) — the gap recorded two tranches ago.

    🛑 What is NOT proved #

    • The loops = 0 branch remains open — 2378 of the 3456 products, no candidate formula fits, and |I'| - |I| moves in both directions. Nothing here touches it, and it is what still blocks dim TL_n = catalan n.
    • card (NormalIdx m) = catalan (m+1) is still decide at m ≤ 6, not a theorem at general m.
    • The converse of threshold_to_pointwise is still unproved and still unneeded.

    No class, axiom or sorry stands in for any of this.

    A note on subst #

    word_split substitutes k := A[r] rather than rewriting with A[r] = k. That is not cosmetic: A[r] carries its bounds proof, so rw finds no syntactic occurrence and silently fails to fire. Recorded because the failure looks like a missing lemma and is not one.
