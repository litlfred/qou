    The modular-double product phiBProd — a FaddeevPhi inhabitant (Im b² > 0) #

    Discharges the FaddeevPhi conjecture-class on the product-supported substrate branch: for Im b² > 0 (in particular the substrate b-map b = √(ln q₀ / 2π)·e^{iπ/4}, PR #3540) the Faddeev Φ_b equals the modular-double product

    $$\Phi_b(z) \;=\; \frac{\prod_{k\ge 0}\bigl(1 + e^{i\pi b^2(2k+1)}e^{2\pi bz}\bigr)} {\prod_{k\ge 0}\bigl(1 + e^{-i\pi b^{-2}(2k+1)}e^{2\pi z/b}\bigr)},$$

    verified against the contour integral to 1e-39 at both anchors (computations/qbeta/phi-b-substrate-regime.witness.json, battery S5). Here the product is formalized as a tprod and both modular-double shift equations are proved by index re-shifting — the infinite-product limit of the qPochProd_add cocycle argument of the finite backbone (PR #3500) — yielding FaddeevPhi b (phiBProd b) under explicit Multipliable hypotheses.

    Hypothesis discipline (§3b-cond style). The two Multipliable inputs are the analytic convergence facts (geometric bounds |q|^k, |q̃|^k for Im b² > 0). Their Mathlib discharge needs the norm→Multipliable bridge (Mathlib.Analysis.SpecialFunctions.Complex.LogBounds cone), which is outside the current sandbox olean cone — they are therefore carried as explicit named hypotheses on faddeevPhi_phiBProd, not axiomatized and not sorry'd. The algebraic content (factor shift identities + telescoping + the guarded dual shift) is proved unconditionally. Route decision + follow-up: docs/audits/2026-07-11-phib-lean-route-memo.md.

                The substrate b-map lands in the product-supported branch #

                The physical b-map pinned by the numerical battery (PR #3540, computations/qbeta/phi-b-substrate-regime.witness.json) is b(t) = √(t/2π)·e^{iπ/4} with t = ln q₀ > 0. Here we prove the two facts that place it in scope: Re b > 0 (the FaddeevPhi.hb precondition) and Im b² > 0 (the branch on which the modular-double product converges — the regime of faddeevPhi_phiBProd). With the pending Multipliable discharge, faddeevPhi_phiBProd (substrateB t) … becomes the physical instance.
