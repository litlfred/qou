    Compact quantum dilogarithm — the genuine infinite object (bi-formal R[[q]][[x]]) #

    The analytic layer deferred by QOU.MathlibExt.QuantumDilogarithm (finite q-Pochhammer backbone). Target T1: treat both q and x as formal variables, so the reciprocal quantum dilogarithm $$\Phi_q(x)^{-1} \;=\; \prod_{n=0}^{\infty}\bigl(1 + q^{2n+1} x\bigr)$$ is an element of R[[q]][[x]] — a power series in x whose coefficients are power series in q. This is well-defined with no analytic convergence: the x^k coefficient is the elementary symmetric series e_k(q, q³, q⁵, …), whose lowest q-term is q^{k²} and whose every q-power has finite multiplicity. (The naive analytic product diverges at the physical q₀ ≈ 1.11 > 1; see docs/workplans/2026-07-11-q-dilog-infinite-product.md.)

    We do not formalise the product via an infinite-Multipliable argument. Instead we take the equivalent q-difference characterisation: Euler's identity gives the coefficient recurrence $$(1 - q^{2k})\,a_k \;=\; -\,q\,a_{k-1}, \qquad a_0 = 1,$$ each 1 - q^{2k} being a unit in R[[q]] (constant term 1). We define Φ_q^{-1} by this recurrence (qDilogInv) and prove the defining functional equation Φ_q(q²x)^{-1} = (1 + q x)·Φ_q(x)^{-1} coefficient-wise (qDilogInv_functional).
