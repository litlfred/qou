    QOU.MathlibExt.OctonionComplexStructure — the ℚ-form of 𝕆 = ℂ ⊕ ℂ³ #

    The Dubois-Violette–Todorov splitting (dubois-violette-todorov2018 Sec. 2) fixes a unit imaginary octonion and reads 𝕆 as a 4-dimensional complex vector space; the colour SU(3) is then the complex-linear symmetry. This file lands the ℚ-form of that structure on the corpus's own concrete octonions (octMul, Baez convention), for the standard imaginary unit e₁:

    • Le : 𝕆 →ₗ 𝕆 — left multiplication by e₁;
    • Le_sq_eq_neg_id — Le² = −id: Le is a complex structure on the full 8-dimensional ℚ-space (the alternative-algebra identity u(ux) = u²x = −x, machine-checked componentwise on the concrete multiplication table);
    • su3_comp_Le_eq_Le_comp — the colour stabilizer acts ℂ-linearly: every derivation D ∈ su3 e₁ commutes with Le. The proof is pure Leibniz — D(e₁·x) = D(e₁)·x + e₁·D(x) = e₁·D(x) since D(e₁) = 0 — so the stabilizer's action on the octonion base module is not merely ℚ-linear (the tautological instance of OctonionColourActionBaseModule) but linear over the complex structure the fixed unit defines. This is the algebraic heart of the 𝟏 ⊕ 𝟑 colour decomposition, in exact rational arithmetic.

    Together these are the first brick of the M₃(ℂ)-route (bean qou-1gix, feeding GAP bean qou-ni4b): the remaining steps are the Le-stable splitting span(1, e₁) ⊕ span(e₂, …, e₇) with its 𝟏 ⊕ 𝟑 block structure, and — the open physics — transport to the three-strand Hecke state space. Base ring ℚ; nothing archimedean.
