    Boundary values: cLiTwoExt = cLiTwo on the closed disc #

    cLiTwoExt_eq_cLiTwo gives agreement on the open unit disc. Every base point worth using for a dilogarithm identity sits on the boundary — z = −1 for Landen, z = 1 for reflection — so the open-disc statement is exactly one point short wherever it is needed.

    This file closes that gap on the part of the boundary that is off the cut:

    ‖y‖ ≤ 1  and  y ∉ [1,∞)   ⟹   cLiTwoExt y = cLiTwo y

    How #

    Two continuity facts and a limit, with no new estimates:

    • cLiTwo is continuous on the closed disc. Its series is dominated there by 1/(k+1)², which is summable (summable_one_div_succ_sq), so continuousOn_tsum applies — the Weierstrass M-test, and the only place the boundary is touched.
    • cLiTwoExt is continuous at y because it is analytic on ℂ ∖ [1,∞) (analyticOnNhd_cLiTwoExt), which is the theorem ComplexDilogarithm.lean flags as its own missing piece and ComplexDilogarithmDeriv.lean supplies.
    • The two agree on the open disc, and y is in its closure, so the punctured neighbourhood filter is NeBot and the limits must agree.

    Why it is infrastructure and not a one-off #

    Stated for every boundary point off the cut, not for −1. The Landen base point is the immediate consumer (cLiTwoExt_neg_one below, giving −π²/12), but reflection at z = 1 and any other boundary evaluation get it for free.

    ⚠ It does not reach [1,∞), which is where the inversion leg needs values — that boundary is on the cut, cLiTwoExt is not defined there, and no continuity argument in this file applies to it. Route 33 G5 records that as the sharper obstacle, and it stays open.

    The 5₂ sum rule is PROVED: QOU.QBeta.FiveTwoComplexPentagon.fiveTwo_saddle_sum_rule and fiveTwoSaddleSumRule_holds, sorry-free, standard axioms.
