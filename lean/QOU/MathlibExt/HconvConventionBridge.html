    QOU.MathlibExt.HconvConventionBridge — the per-λ convention bridge hconv #

    The last unproved input of the telescope closure of normalizedSum n q = 1 (qou-3quc, assembly NormalizedSumClosure.normalizedSum_eq_one_of_convBridge, #6011): the per-λ convention bridge

    q^{−κ_λ} / hookProd_q(λ) = schurElement(q², 0, λ) · (1 − q²)^{|λ|}

    (route verified exact n≤5 in computations/qbeta/fake_degree_via_telescope_probe.py; audit docs/audits/2026-08-29-normalizedsum-closes-via-telescope.md).

    The proof is the sign/monomial foldl aggregation. Per cell, 1 − q^{2h} = −q^{h}(q−q⁻¹)·[h]_q (NormalizedSumTelescopeBridge.one_sub_qsq_pow_eq), so ∏_x (1 − q^{2 h_x}) = (−1)^{|λ|} q^{Σh} (q−q⁻¹)^{|λ|} hookProd_q(λ). With schurElement(q²,0,λ) = q^{2n(λ)} · ∏_x (1 − q^{2 h_x})⁻¹, (1 − q²) = −q(q−q⁻¹), and the n-statistic identity κ_λ = Σ_x h_x − |λ| − 2 n(λ) (ContentSumNStatistic.contentSum_eq_hookSum_sub), the sign and (q−q⁻¹) powers cancel and the exponent collapses to −κ_λ.

    Base field ℝ (schurElement is archimedean); regime 1 < q (the substrate regime).
