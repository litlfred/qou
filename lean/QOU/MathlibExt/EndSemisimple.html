    QOU.MathlibExt.EndSemisimple — endomorphism rings of finite-dimensional #

    vector spaces are semisimple #

    Fills the field + Module.Finite case of Mathlib's proof_wanted IsSemisimpleRing.module_end (RingTheory/SimpleModule/Basic.lean), companion to QOU.MathlibExt.MatrixSemisimple (bean qou-ciun / epic qou-otbb infrastructure).

    Route: a finite module over a field is free with a finite chosen basis; algEquivMatrix identifies Module.End K V with the matrix algebra over that basis, and the matrix instance of MatrixSemisimple transfers back along the ring equivalence.

    Scope note (honest): Mathlib's proof_wanted is stated for a finite semisimple module over an arbitrary ring; that generality needs the isotypic decomposition and is not covered here — this file covers the vector-space case, which is what the QOU Wedderburn ladder consumes. Stated Mathlib-conventionally for upstreaming.
