    QOU.MathlibExt.RegTraceGram — the Gram-determinant criterion #

    Brick B2f-core (epic qou-hnhg). Packages the regular trace form regTraceForm as a genuine LinearMap.BilinForm, so all of Mathlib's Gram-determinant machinery applies to it. In particular LinearMap.BilinForm.nondegenerate_iff_det_ne_zero gives, for any finite basis b,

    (regTraceBilin).Nondegenerate ↔ det (Gram matrix of regTraceBilin in b) ≠ 0,

    so the abstract semisimplicity keystone (B3d) becomes a determinant condition:

    H is semisimple ⟸ the Gram matrix of the regular trace form has nonzero determinant.

    For the Iwahori–Hecke algebra H_n(q) in its T_w basis that determinant is [n]_q! (up to a unit) — the Schur-element / Poincaré identity. Supplying that value (which needs the n!-dim T_w basis, brick qou-lrz1) is the only remaining step to discharge WenzlCriterion; the packaging + the nondegenerate ⇒ semisimple direction are proven here, sorry-free.
