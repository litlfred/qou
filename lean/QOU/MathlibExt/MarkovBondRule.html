    The bond rule and the mirror cancellation (convention D) #

    The canonical color-weighted atom word carries, between its A nucleons, exactly A - 1 bond letters of the form σ_g + d · I:

    • Z - 1 proton-proton bonds, d = 0;
    • N - 1 neutron-neutron bonds, d = -h with h = q - q⁻¹;
    • exactly one proton-neutron interface, d = dIface.

    Measured over 16 nuclei (A = 2 … 10, worst relative deviation 4.45e-31, the compute path's own floor) and symbolically at six nuclei (n = 6, 9, 12), the free-to-atom Markov-trace ratio is the product

    tr_M^free / tr_M^atom = ∏_bonds 1 / (z + d_b), z = q³/(q²+1).

    This file proves the algebra that product form implies — all of it unconditionally over a generic field — and states the one trace-level input as an explicit hypothesis rather than asserting it.

    What is proved here, unconditionally #

    • markovZ_sub_h — z - h = z̄, where z̄ = z(1/q). The neutron-neutron bond letter lands exactly on the bar image of the Markov parameter. This is the non-obvious one: it is what makes q ↦ q⁻¹ act as proton↔neutron conjugation on these words.
    • mirrorP_eq — P = 12 q⁵ (q²+1) (z + dIface): the degree-10 polynomial that governs the mirror law is exactly the interface's Markov weight with denominators cleared. In particular the leading 12 is P evaluated at q = 1, not a free constant.
    • mirrorP_bar_sub — q¹² P(1/q) - P(q) = (q-1)⁵ (q+1)³ (q²+1)², so the mirror offset vanishes to order exactly 5 at the classical point.
    • bondFactor_mirror_cancel — the cancellation itself: for any exponents a b : ℕ, the bond factors z^a and z̄^b cancel out of the mirror-crossed ratio, leaving (z̄ + d̄) / (z + d). This is why the mirror offset is independent of the nucleus.

    What is NOT proved here #

    The trace-level rule

    tr(x · (σ_g + d·I)) = (z + d) · tr(x) for g unused by x

    is measured, not proved (bean qou-g8i1). It is carried as the single field of BondRule below — a content-bearing hypothesis class in the §3b-cond sense, never discharged here. Everything above is independent of it: markovZ_sub_h, mirrorP_eq and mirrorP_bar_sub are identities of rational functions, and bondFactor_mirror_cancel is stated about arbitrary elements, so it holds whatever the traces turn out to be.

    Nothing in this file computes or asserts a physical value.

    Base ring #

    Generic [Field R] [CharZero R] per the §7c convention — this is q-deformation combinatorics with no archimedean content, and ℝ appears nowhere. CharZero is required by mirrorP_eq alone, and is carried there rather than on the section variable: that statement clears denominators of 2, 3, 4 and 12, none of which is invertible in characteristic 2 or 3. Every other result here holds over any field.

    Convention #

    Convention D throughout (z = q³/(q²+1)), per the owner ruling of 2026-08-15. QOU.Braiding.HnMarkovTrace.MarkovTraceTower still carries the convention-C parameter 1/(√q + 1/√q) and must not be used as the base for these statements (bean qou-um5h).

              The neutron-neutron bond lands on the bar image of z.

              z - h = z(1/q) exactly. Under the bar involution q ↦ q⁻¹ the Markov parameter z and the neutron-neutron bond factor z - h are exchanged, which is precisely the statement that bar acts as proton↔neutron conjugation on the bond letters. Nothing about this is forced by the shapes involved; it is an identity special to convention D.

              theorem QOU.MathlibExt.MarkovBondRule.mirrorP_bar_sub {R : Type u_1} [Field R] (q : R) (hq0 : q ≠ 0) :
              q ^ 12 * mirrorP q⁻¹ - mirrorP q = (q - 1) ^ 5 * (q + 1) ^ 3 * (q ^ 2 + 1) ^ 2

              The mirror offset vanishes to order exactly 5.

              q¹² P(1/q) - P(q) = (q-1)⁵ (q+1)³ (q²+1)². The right side is a product of powers of q-1, q+1 and q²+1, with (q-1) appearing to the fifth power — so the offset is O(h⁵) at the classical point, and that order is derived rather than observed.

              theorem QOU.MathlibExt.MarkovBondRule.bondFactor_mirror_cancel {R : Type u_1} [Field R] (zz zb w wb : R) (a b : ℕ) (hz : zz ≠ 0) (hzb : zb ≠ 0) (hw : w ≠ 0) (hwb : wb ≠ 0) :
              1 / (zz ^ a * zb ^ b * w) / (1 / (zb ^ b * zz ^ a * wb)) = wb / w

              The bond factors cancel out of the mirror-crossed ratio.

              Given the product form, a nucleus (Z,N) contributes z^(Z-1) from its proton-proton bonds and z̄^(N-1) from its neutron-neutron bonds, and its mirror (N,Z) evaluated at 1/q contributes the same two factors with the roles exchanged. They cancel identically, for any exponents — which is why the mirror offset does not depend on the nucleus.

              Stated about arbitrary ring elements so that it is independent of everything else in this file: zz and zb stand for the two bond factors and w, wb for the interface factor and its bar image.

              class QOU.MathlibExt.MarkovBondRule.BondRule (R : Type u_2) [Field R] (A : Type u_3) (q : R) :
              Type (max u_2 u_3)

              The one trace-level input, carried as a hypothesis and never discharged.

              tr is the Jones–Markov trace, mul the algebra multiplication, and sigmaPlus g d the bond letter σ_g + d·I. The field says that appending a bond letter at a generator the element does not use multiplies the trace by z + d.

              This is measured, not proved — 16 nuclei numerically to 4.45e-31, six symbolically. The mechanism is multiplicativity of the trace across an unused generator (the two sides then lie in commuting subalgebras) followed by the Markov property; formalising that chain is bean qou-g8i1.

              It is a genuine hypothesis in the §3b-cond sense: the field is a parameter-quantified equation in the shape of the claim, not a marker, and no instance is provided.

              • tr : A → R

                The Jones–Markov trace.

              • mul : A → A → A

                Algebra multiplication.

              • sigmaPlus : ℕ → R → A

                The bond letter σ_g + d·I at generator g.

              • Unused : ℕ → A → Prop

                g is not used by x.

              • tr_mul_sigmaPlus (x : A) (g : ℕ) (d : R) : Unused q g x → tr q (mul q x (sigmaPlus q g d)) = (markovZ q + d) * tr q x
