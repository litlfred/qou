    The multiplicativity engine #

    source
    theorem QOU.MathlibExt.MarkovTraceMultiplicative.trace_kron {R : Type u_1} [CommRing R] {m : Type u_2} {n : Type u_3} [Fintype m] [Fintype n] (A : Matrix m m R) (B : Matrix n n R) :
    (Matrix.kroneckerMap (fun (x1 x2 : R) => x1 * x2) A B).trace = A.trace * B.trace

    The trace is multiplicative under the Kronecker product. Restatement of Matrix.trace_kronecker in the form the Markov-trace application uses: the amplitude of a disjoint (non-interacting) union is the product of the amplitudes.

    def QOU.MathlibExt.MarkovTraceMultiplicative.IsJuxtaposition {R : Type u_1} [CommRing R] {m : Type u_2} {n : Type u_3} (A : Matrix m m R) (B : Matrix n n R) (C : Matrix (m × n) (m × n) R) :
    Prop

    A juxtaposition (disjoint-strand product) of two block evaluations: the composite matrix C is the Kronecker product of the blocks. This is the algebraic content of "the two sub-braids share no strand", i.e. of the connected sum / distant union in the Hecke tower.

      C.trace = A.trace * B.trace

      Markov-trace multiplicativity under juxtaposition. If the composite evaluation is the juxtaposition of two blocks, its trace factorises as the product of the block traces. This is the algebraic form of J(K₁ # K₂) = J(K₁) · J(K₂) (Jones 1987) — and it is the hypothesis that QOU.Mass.ArchimedeanTraceBridge carries, here discharged.

      C.trace ≠ 0

      Non-vanishing is preserved by juxtaposition — the composite amplitude of two non-vanishing components is non-vanishing (needed for the archimedean log, where the amplitude must be nonzero before the free energy is defined).

      Positivity is preserved by juxtaposition (over a linearly ordered ring): if both component amplitudes are positive so is the composite. Together with markovTrace_juxtapose this supplies BOTH hypotheses of QOU.Mass.ArchimedeanTraceBridge.freeEnergy_connectedSum — multiplicativity and positivity — from the juxtaposition structure alone.
