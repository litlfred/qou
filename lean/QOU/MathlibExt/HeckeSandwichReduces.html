    The braid relations discharge SandwichReduces — and hence TowerSpan #

    Bean qou-301d. DisjointBlockTraceLaw derives the disjoint-block trace law from TowerSpan; TowerSpanReduction replaces TowerSpan by the local rewriting rule SandwichReduces. Both files close with the same 🛑: the local rule is not proved for any tower with a growing filtration.

    This file proves it, from the defining relations of a braid tower.

    What is assumed #

    BraidedTower T carries exactly three equations, all of them relations the Iwahori-Hecke algebra H_n(q) satisfies by presentation:

    • quad — g m * g m = h • g m + 1 for some h : R (in H_n(q), h = q - q⁻¹);
    • far — g m * g k = g k * g m when k + 1 < m (distant generators commute);
    • braid — g (m+1) * g m * g (m+1) = g m * g (m+1) * g m.

    Nothing else: no basis, no dimension count, no faithful representation, no Markov property. In particular the trace fields of MarkovTrace play no part in anything proved here — this is a statement about the filtration, which is what TowerSpan was always about.

    The argument #

    sandwichReduces_all is an induction on the level M. Writing g₁ = g (M+1) and g₂ = g (M+2), and using the previous level's decomposition c = u + Σ vᵢ g₁ wᵢ with u, vᵢ, wᵢ at level ≤ M:

    • every element at level ≤ M commutes with g₂ (far, since M + 1 < M + 2);
    • the base part collapses by the quadratic relation, g₂ u g₂ = u g₂² = h • (u g₂) + u;
    • each sandwich part is turned over by the braid relation, g₂ (v g₁ w) g₂ = v (g₂ g₁ g₂) w = v (g₁ g₂ g₁) w = (v g₁) g₂ (g₁ w), and v g₁, g₁ w are at level ≤ M + 1.

    The base case is the quadratic relation together with one application of braid at m = 0; below the bottom of the shift window the stage is ⊥ and only the quadratic relation is needed.

    What this does and does not settle #

    Settled. TowerSpan is no longer a hypothesis for any tower whose generators satisfy the braid relations — towerSpan_of_braided. Composing with DisjointBlockTraceLaw.disjointBlock_of_towerSpan gives disjointBlock_of_braided: the disjoint-block trace law holds for every MarkovTrace on a braided tower.

    🛑 NOT settled here — but see MathlibExt.BraidedTowerInstances, which supplies the instances this file does not. BraidedTower is a hypothesis on a MarkovTrace, and this file constructs no instance of it. The existing witnesses do not qualify, which is a fact about them worth stating rather than leaving implicit: InvolutionTowerWitness has commuting involutions, and braid_fails_for_commuting_involutions below shows the braid relation there would force g m = g (m+1), so that witness satisfies SandwichReduces for a reason that has nothing to do with this file's argument; DisjointBlockTraceLaw.towerSpan_unit is degenerate for the reasons its own docstring gives. So the honest status of the corpus claim is unchanged in one respect — nothing here exhibits a Hecke tower in Lean — while the hypothesis has moved from an unproved structure theorem to a list of defining relations.

    What is supplied is a bracket on the class, in two halves that no single witness combines:

    • braidedTower_unit — an instance in which the quadratic scalar h = u - u⁻¹ is genuinely non-zero (unit_quad_scalar_ne_zero), i.e. the relations are consistent with the Hecke case h = q - q⁻¹; its filtration is constant.
    • the symmetric-group algebra tower (bean qou-fq1i) — a growing filtration and a non-multiplicative trace, but h = 0, and its SandwichReduces is proved by support arguments in the permutation model rather than by rewriting with the relations, so it does not reach q ≠ 1.

    Prop

    The braid-tower relations. Three equations on the generator family of a MarkovTrace, each of them a defining relation of the Iwahori-Hecke algebra. None of them mentions the trace.

    • quad (m : ℕ) : ∃ (h : R), T.g m * T.g m = h • T.g m + 1

      The quadratic relation. In H_n(q) the scalar is h = q - q⁻¹.

    • far (m k : ℕ) : k + 1 < m → T.g m * T.g k = T.g k * T.g m

      Distant generators commute.

    • braid (m : ℕ) : T.g (m + 1) * T.g m * T.g (m + 1) = T.g m * T.g (m + 1) * T.g m

      The braid relation on adjacent generators.

      The hypothesis is not free #

      InvolutionTowerWitness satisfies quad and far (its generators commute) but not braid: for commuting involutions the braid relation collapses to g m = g (m+1). Stated here for a generic tower so that no reader mistakes that witness for an instance of BraidedTower.

      source
      theorem QOU.MathlibExt.HeckeSandwichReduces.braid_forces_eq_of_commuting_involutions {R : Type u_1} [CommRing R] {A : Type u_2} [Ring A] [Algebra R A] (T : OcneanuTrace.MarkovTrace R A) (m : ℕ) (hcomm : T.g (m + 1) * T.g m = T.g m * T.g (m + 1)) (hi : T.g m * T.g m = 1) (hi' : T.g (m + 1) * T.g (m + 1) = 1) (hbraid : T.g (m + 1) * T.g m * T.g (m + 1) = T.g m * T.g (m + 1) * T.g m) :
      T.g m = T.g (m + 1)

      If two adjacent generators commute and both square to 1, the braid relation forces them equal. So a commuting-involution tower is braided only if it is constant — which is exactly why InvolutionTowerWitness is not an instance.

      An instance, with the quadratic scalar genuinely non-zero #

      OcneanuTrace.unitMarkovTrace u — the one-point tower on R with every generator equal to a fixed unit u — satisfies all three relations, with h = u - u⁻¹. Its filtration is constant (H = ⊤), so it witnesses the relations and not the tower; but it does establish that BraidedTower does not secretly force h = 0, which is the one thing a reader might suspect after braid_forces_eq_of_commuting_involutions.
