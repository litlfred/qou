    The composite rule driven by oscillation, for monotone g″ #

    Same induction, but with abs_corrected_trapezoid_sub_integral_le_osc in place of the g⁗ bound. When g″ is ANTITONE the per-cell oscillation on [a+jh, a+(j+1)h] is exactly g″(a+jh) − g″(a+(j+1)h), and the bounds TELESCOPE in the induction's arithmetic — no separate summation lemma is needed, the h³/12·(g″(a) − g″(A)) of the inductive step plus the h³/12·(g″(A) − g″(B)) of the new cell add to h³/12·(g″(a) − g″(B)).

    Hypotheses are C² ONLY — no g‴, no g⁗. That is because integral_eq_corrected_trapezoid_sub_deriv2 derives the order-2 identity from the order-1 one by rewriting the kernel, rather than from the quartic kernel.

    The payoff: k cells give a bound with NO factor of k. Against the g⁗ version's k·h⁵·sup|g⁗|/720, this is h³·TV(g″)/12 — and on the sum (divide by h) that is O(h²) = O(1/N²), below the O(1/N) term qou-mlgp (A) extracts, while needing only logSinc″ (bounded, and monotone by QOU.MathlibExt.mul_cube_cos_lt_sin_cube) instead of logSinc⁗.
