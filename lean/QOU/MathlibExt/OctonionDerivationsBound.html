    QOU.MathlibExt.OctonionDerivationsBound — dim Der(𝕆) = 14 #

    Finrank bounds on the octonion derivation space Der𝕆 from QOU.MathlibExt.OctonionDerivations.

    • Lower bound octDer_finrank_ge : 14 ≤ dim Der𝕆 — fully proved. The coordinate projection proj𝕆 onto the 14 free matrix entries sends the 14 explicit derivations octDer 0 .. 13 to the standard basis of ℚ¹⁴ (projDer_octDer), so it is a left inverse of the linear-combination map c ↦ Σ cᵢ · octDer i, which is therefore injective; LinearMap.finrank_le_finrank_of_injective gives 14 ≤ dim. (octDer_linearIndependent is the same fact stated as linear independence.)

    • Upper bound octDer_finrank_le : dim Der𝕆 ≤ 14 — the residual content. Equivalent to injectivity of proj𝕆 on Der𝕆: a derivation is pinned by its 14 free entries, the other 50 being forced by the Leibniz constraints. This is the classical Der(𝕆) ≅ 𝔤₂ (dim 14); its explicit 50-constraint Lean certificate is left as a cited sorry. Everything upstream — the multiplication, the 14 derivations, the reduction dim = 14 ⟺ rank = 50, and the lower bound — is machine-verified.

    • octonion_derivation_dim : dim Der𝕆 = 14 — assembles the two bounds.

    Base ring ℚ (char 0). Convention: [albuquerque-majid1999]; derivation-algebra dimension: [baez2002] §4.1.
