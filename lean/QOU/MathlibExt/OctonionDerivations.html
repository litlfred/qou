    QOU.MathlibExt.OctonionDerivations — the 14 derivations of 𝕆 (compact G₂) #

    Concrete instantiation of the octonion-shaped rank–nullity gate (QOU.MathlibExt.DerivationRankNullity). We fix the octonion multiplication over ℚ (Baez convention: e₀ = 1, eᵢ² = -1, quaternionic triples eᵢ eᵢ₊₁ = eᵢ₊₃ mod 7), realise it as a bilinear map octMulₗ, and define the derivation space Der𝕆 := ker (leibnizDefect octMulₗ).

    Verified: the 14 explicit 𝔤₂ derivations octDerMat 0 .. 13, each machine-checked to satisfy the Leibniz rule (octDerMat_isDer) hence to lie in Der𝕆 (octDer_mem). Their linear independence gives the lower bound 14 ≤ dim Der𝕆 (octDer_finrank_ge), certified by projection onto the 14 free matrix-entries where the family is the standard basis. With the reduction dim = 14 ⟺ rank = 50 (DerivationRankNullity.octCarrier_finrank_ker_eq_14_iff), the residual content is the matching upper bound (the 50 independent Leibniz constraints; the classical Der(𝕆) ≅ 𝔤₂).

    Base ring ℚ (char 0 — the count needs char ≠ 2, 3); exact rational linear algebra, nothing archimedean. Convention reference: [albuquerque-majid1999].
