    theorem QOU.MathlibExt.RpowAlgebra.rpow_five_half (q : ℝ) (hq : 0 ≤ q) :
    q ^ (5 / 2) = √q ^ 5

    q^{5/2} = (sqrt q)^5 for q ≥ 0.

    Proof: q^{5/2} = q^{2 + 1/2} = q^2 · q^{1/2} = (sqrt q)^4 · sqrt q = (sqrt q)^5.
