    Proposition: Archimedean Topological Mass Formula #

    Formalizes Proposition prop:topological-mass-formula: in the archimedean (ℂ) realisation, the general topological mass decomposition (Theorem thm:topological-mass) specialises to the closed-form mass formula m(K) = Vol(K) / ℏ_q².

    Logical structure #

    The proposition derives the closed form from the categorical decomposition via two evaluation identities on top of the proved TopologicalMassDecomposition:

    1. Baseline = Volume: the holonomy term evaluates to Vol(K) via the Chern–Simons/volume correspondence.
    2. Exceptional sum = dilogarithm: the sum over blow-up centres evaluates to Vol(K) · (1/ℏ_q² − 1) via Briggs–Klein spectral coordinates.

    Given these, the closed-form formula is a one-line algebraic consequence.

    Proposition (Topological Mass Formula — Archimedean).

    In the ℂ-quantum observable universe, the mass of a particle associated with a hyperbolic knot K satisfies m(K) = Vol(K) / ℏ_q², where Vol(K) is the hyperbolic volume of S³ \ K and ℏ_q = 1 − 1/q is the quantum Planck constant.

    This structure extends TopologicalMassDecomposition with the two evaluation identities (Chern–Simons/volume correspondence and Briggs–Klein dilogarithm evaluation) that specialise the general decomposition to the archimedean closed form.

    • h_q_def : self.h_q = 1 - 1 / self.q

      The quantum Planck constant satisfies its defining equation. This constraint ensures h_q cannot be overridden to an inconsistent value.

    • baseline_eq_volume : self.baselineHolonomy = self.knotVolume

      Hypothesis 1 (Chern–Simons/Volume correspondence). The baseline holonomy of the QOU connection evaluates to the hyperbolic volume of the knot complement.

    • exceptional_sum_eq : ∑ i : Fin self.numBlowups, self.exceptionalContribution i = self.knotVolume * (1 / self.h_q ^ 2 - 1)

      Hypothesis 2 (Dilogarithm evaluation). The sum of exceptional divisor contributions, evaluated in Briggs–Klein spectral coordinates, yields Vol(K) · (1/ℏ_q² − 1).
