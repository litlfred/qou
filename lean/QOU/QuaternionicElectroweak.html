    Quaternionic electroweak alignment — SU(2)_L × SU(2)_R on the flavor doublet #

    An honest formalization of the Morita quaternionic-electroweak picture over the Bring tetraplectic boundary. The flavor doublet is modelled as a single quaternion (its 4 real d.o.f. = the 2 complex d.o.f. of a standard SU(2) doublet). The gauge group SU(2) is realized as the unit quaternions (normSq = 1), not the full quaternion algebra — this is the correction to the earlier draft, whose actions took arbitrary quaternions and so modelled the whole algebra, overclaiming the gauge structure.

    What is actually proved here is exactly the group-action content:

    • IsSU2.mul / IsSU2.one — the unit quaternions are closed under multiplication and contain the identity (the SU(2) ≅ S³ group);
    • su2L_normSq / su2R_normSq — the left and right actions are norm-preserving (isometric / unitary): the defining property of a genuine gauge action, and the substance the arbitrary-algebra version could not state;
    • su2L_su2R_commute — the two factors SU(2)_L and SU(2)_R act independently (they commute);
    • u1Y_isSU2 — the hypercharge phase U(1)_Y sits inside SU(2) as the i-plane circle subgroup.

    Per the §7c base-ring convention the algebraic group/action content is stated over a generic {R : Type*} [CommRing R]; only the hypercharge phase u1Y, which uses Real.cos/Real.sin, specializes to ℝ.

    The identification of this algebraic structure with the physical electroweak sector is interpretive — see rem:tetraplectic-su2-gauge-reading and mass-theory/hoefsmit-ew-cell-content.md (Morita 1982; Cederwall–Preitschopf). No physical claim beyond the group-action content is asserted by this file.
