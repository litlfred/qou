    The Skein-observation bridge: q-Collatz halting ↔ the P-vs-NP horizon #

    Purpose. Make explicit, in Lean, the shared substrate linking two QOU-internal reformulations of Millennium-class open problems:

    • q-Collatz — does every domain-stable positive Skein element reach the fundamental cycle? (QOU.AppendixSurreals.QCollatz.reaches_cycle.)
    • P-vs-NP — is the degree-0 observation of a braid computation extractable in time polynomial in the crossing count, rather than the naive 3^c resolution-tree cost? (QOU.ComputationalHorizon.)

    Both live on the same Skein module 𝒮(q) ≅ ℤ[q^{±1}], both read their output through the same observation map — the Markov trace tr_M — and both ask a reachability question about a discrete dynamical system graded by a ℕ-valued length/complexity filtration.

    The structural point (not an ideal) #

    One might hope to "classify the ideal of Skein relations specific to halting." There is no such ideal. The Skein relations give a single, fixed ideal cutting ℤ⟨braids⟩ down to 𝒮(q), and that ideal yields a decidable invariant (the Markov trace / Jones polynomial is computable). The halting / hardness content is not algebraic: it lives in the length filtration of the reduction dynamics (crossing count c; braid length; the [2]_q-adic valuation). By Reeb- / Skein-Turing-completeness (Cardona–Miranda–Peralta-Salas– Presas) the halting set is undecidable, hence not cut out by any ideal.

    So the two problems share the ideal (⟹ share the decidable observable, haltsObserved_of_halts below) and differ only in the filtration-reachability question they ask. This file packages that shared structure as SkeinObservationSystem, instantiates it for q-Collatz, links its filtration to the horizon's 3^c leaf count, and records the conditional proof-by- contradiction skeleton for a residue-based separation.

    Honesty / anti-laundering #

    Nothing here proves either conjecture. The q-Collatz instance is conditional on a [QCollatz Skein] hypothesis (no fabricated instance); the separation skeleton separation_of_residue_nonzero is a modus-tollens by design whose entire content sits in two named open hypotheses — the faithfulness-leg equivalence (QOU.Information.PNeqNpEquivalence, still sorry) and the χ(A) residue non-vanishing. It is the machine-checked shape of the argument, not a resolution of P vs NP (which additionally must clear the algebrization barrier, Aaronson–Wigderson 2008 — the residue must be a genuinely non-algebraic cohomological invariant, not polynomial data).

    -- Ref: [cardona2021] https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2026818118

    structure QOU.Information.SkeinObservationSystem (S : Type u) (V : Type v) :
    Type (max u v)

    An abstract Skein-observation system: a discrete dynamical system on a state space S, with an observation map obs : S → V (the Markov trace), a normal-form target (the attractor / unknot), and a ℕ-valued length / complexity filtration len (crossing count / braid length / [2]_q-adic valuation). Both the q-Collatz halting problem and the P-vs-NP computational horizon are instances.

    • step : S → S

      One reduction step of the dynamical system (C_q; one crossing resolution).

    • obs : S → V

      The observation map: the Markov trace / Jones value the observer reads.

    • normalForm : S

      The normal-form target — the fundamental cycle / the unknot.

    • len : S → ℕ

      The ℕ-valued length/complexity filtration grading the reduction.

              The q-Navier–Stokes leg: regularity vs. finite-time blow-up #

              The third filtration question. A vortex flow of the brane fluid is a braid word β(t) ∈ H_n(q) evolving in time; its closure is a knotted vortex tube, its conserved helicity is the Markov-trace observation (obs), and its crossing number is the length filtration len (see fluid-dynamics/helicity-conservation, braid-fluid, TwoVortexChannelAlgebra). q-NS smoothness asks whether len stays bounded along the orbit — no finite-time singularity — the q-analog of Beale–Kato–Majda (QOU.FluidDynamics.q_bkm_criterion). This is the same filtration that q-Collatz asks to descend and P-vs-NP asks to be polynomial.

                  P-vs-NP rate leg: the 3^c horizon cost separates PolyObservable #

                  from HaltsObserved

                  HaltsObserved asks only that the observation is eventually reached; PolyObservable asks that it is reached within a polynomial budget. The gap is the P-vs-NP question. When a system's observation is reachable only at the naive resolution-tree cost 3^{\text{len}} — the exponential horizon (horizon_numLeaves_eq_three_pow_filtrationLevel) — it is observed-halting but beats every budget the horizon exceeds.

                  Proof-by-contradiction skeleton for a residue-based separation #

                  source
                  theorem QOU.Information.separation_of_residue_nonzero {PolyCollapse resZero : Prop} (hequiv : PolyCollapse ↔ resZero) (hres : ¬resZero) :
                  ¬PolyCollapse

                  The lift-and-contradict skeleton. Suppose the framework's central equivalence holds — the P=NP horizon collapse is EQUIVALENT to the vanishing of the obstruction residue (hequiv : PolyCollapse ↔ resZero) — and the residue is provably non-zero (hres : ¬ resZero, a topological computation of χ(A)). Then the collapse cannot occur (¬ PolyCollapse): the separation.

                  Contrapositive reading — exactly the user's "lift an example P=NP and contradict": if the collapse held, then (through the equivalence) the residue would vanish, contradicting hres.

                  Both hypotheses are open inputs. hequiv is the faithfulness leg (QOU.Information.PNeqNpEquivalence, still sorry); hres is the χ(A) non-vanishing. This is a modus-tollens by design — the machine-checked SHAPE of the argument, proving nothing about P vs NP unconditionally, and it does NOT by itself clear the algebrization barrier (the residue must be a genuinely non-algebraic cohomological invariant for the argument to have content).

                      A concrete crossing-reduction vortex flow, and the corpus q-BKM link #

                      The q-NS leg above is abstract; here it is grounded two ways. First, a concrete crossing-reduction flow — the vortex-knot complexity is n : ℕ, each step resolves one crossing (n ↦ n − 1), the observation is the complexity itself, and the normal form is the unknot 0. It is unconditionally Halts and Regular, so the q-NS predicates are non-vacuous (as qCollatzSystem grounds the q-Collatz leg). Second, a conditional transport of the corpus q-BKM criterion (QOU.FluidDynamics.q_bkm_criterion) onto the bridge's no-blow-up / bounded-filtration predicates.
