    QOU.Information.SkeinSurrealGrade — the surreal-level (ω-grade) brick #

    The honest core of "complexity ↔ surreal birthday/length", formalized sorry-free as a conditional whose hypothesis is the growth bound (formalize-first: the growth bound is the honest input, the grade gap is derived).

    What this is — and is NOT #

    This module does not prove P ≠ NP, and adds nothing to the open counting→decision transfer. It formalizes the strictly weaker, genuinely-true sub-fact that the QOU horizon actually supports: exponential-in-length observation cost occupies a strictly higher transseries level (ω-grade) than any polynomial budget. See docs/audits/2026-08-09-pneqnp-robustness-mathlib-infra.md.

    The mathematics #

    • Transseries level = ω-grade. Berarducci–Mantova: the surreals are the universal transseries field; a growth function's level orders it against iterated exp/log (poly = level 0, exp = level 1). In the coarser ω-monomial grade the corpus uses, nᵏ has magnitude ωᵏ (finite) while 2ⁿ exceeds every ωᵏ. That is exactly SuperPolynomial vs PolyBounded (QOU.Information.SurrealBudgetGrade): ord_ω(2ⁿ) > ord_ω(nᵏ) for all k. The P ⊊ EXP band-gap reads as this strict ω-grade inequality — proved sorry-free as not_polyBounded_of_superPoly (the "surreal squeeze").

    • len is the finite birthday shadow. A finite braid word's surreal sign-expansion length is literally its ℕ length, and the birthday of a finite ordinal n is n. So SkeinObservationSystem.len is the finite (birthday < ω, archimedean) shadow of the surreal length. The exponential blow-up lives in the cost as a function of this level (3^{len}), never in the level of any single word (birthdayShadow_lt_omega0).

    The bricks (all sorry-free) #

    level_gap (the squeeze), levelGeOne_of_exp_growth (the conditional — hypothesis = growth bound), the horizon-cost family horizonCostOfLen/horizonCostOfLen_levelGeOne, and the corpus cross-check horizonCostOfLen_boundary_eq_physicalCost recovering physicalCost_superPoly.

    Base ring not applicable (ℕ-valued costs; Ordinal for the birthday shadow).
