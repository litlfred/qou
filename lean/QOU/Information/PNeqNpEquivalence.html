    QOU.Information.PNeqNpEquivalence — chipping the conjectured equivalence #

    Structural companion to QouPNeqNp / conj:qou-p-neq-np. The conjecture asserts an equivalence $\mathrm{P} \neq \mathrm{NP} \iff \mathrm{res}_{\mathcal{R}}(\chi(A)) \neq 0$ at the non-torsion substrate point $q_0$. This module decomposes the equivalence into its two directions and proves — sorry-free — everything that is not the irreducible open kernel:

    • equivalence_collapse — pure propositional logic: once the non-vanishing leg is established, asserting the equivalence is exactly asserting $\mathrm{P} \neq \mathrm{NP}$. Since the corpus derives $\chi(A) = -6$ (ex:chi-minus-six-derivation, prf:qou-obstruction-reduction) with no computational-complexity input, this is the machine-checked form of the collapse observation in the .md.
    • ResidueFaithfulness.sound_of_ne_zero — the soundness direction ($\mathrm{P} \neq \mathrm{NP} \Rightarrow \mathrm{res} \neq 0$) is automatic given the derived non-vanishing: it carries no content.
    • conclusion_of_faithful — faithfulness alone, plus the derived non-vanishing, already yields the conclusion; the equivalence adds nothing beyond its faithful direction.
    • chi_mod_vanishes_iff — the Kummer filtration arithmetic of the derived value: $-6 \bmod n = 0 \iff n \mid 6$, i.e. the classically transparent torsion levels are exactly the Jaeger--Vertigan--Welsh tractable set $n \mid 6$.
    • nontorsion_of_one_lt — any real $q > 1$ (in particular the substrate $q_0 \approx 1.10998$) is non-torsion in $\mathbb{G}_m(\mathbb{R})$: no positive power returns to $1$, so no finite Kummer level applies.

    What deliberately remains open: the faithfulness direction $\mathrm{res} \neq 0 \Rightarrow \mathrm{P} \neq \mathrm{NP}$ — that is the conjecture (the instance-free faithful field of QouPNeqNp; no instance is provided, so it is assumed only where written). $\mathrm{P}$ and $\mathrm{NP}$ themselves are kept as an opaque Prop parameter: Mathlib has no standard P/NP formalisation, and axiomatising one here would add fake precision without content (per the anti-laundering rule, AGENTS.md §Axiomatization is NOT a Proof).

      The cited complexity layer and the counting–decision gap #

      Everything citable about hardness at $q_0$ concerns counting: Jaeger--Vertigan--Welsh give $\#\mathrm{P}$-hardness of exact Jones evaluation away from the lattice roots of unity, and the textbook inclusion $\mathrm{NP} \subseteq \mathrm{P}^{\#\mathrm{P}}$ makes $\mathrm{FP} = \#\mathrm{P} \Rightarrow \mathrm{P} = \mathrm{NP}$. Lean has no complexity-theory library, so these are carried as a cited hypothesis class — never proved, never instantiated (a fabricated instance would launder literature into a Lean proof; per AGENTS.md §Axiomatization is NOT a Proof). The theorems below are sorry-free given the class, and isolate exactly what a proof of the conjecture must add.

        Ref: [arora2009] https://doi.org/10.1017/CBO9780511804090

        The Wenzl bridge (stage 1, bean qou-f0p8) #

        The residue–semisimplicity bridge, parameter side. Wenzl's criterion [wenzl1988] characterises semisimplicity of the Iwahori--Hecke algebra $H_n(q)$ by genericity of the parameter: $H_n(q)$ is semisimple iff $q^k \neq 1$ for every $2 \leq k \leq n$ (equivalently $[n]_q! \neq 0$). Stage 1 formalises the parameter-side condition (WenzlGeneric, over a generic monoid per §7c), proves — sorry-free — that any real $q > 1$ satisfies it at every rank, carries the criterion itself as a cited hypothesis class (WenzlCriterion, no instance: the Hecke radical theory is stage 2, bean qou-vndn), and composes end-to-end: for the substrate $q_0 > 1$ the semisimplicity hypothesis of counting_faithfulness is discharged, so the companion faithfulness needs only the two cited classes plus $\mathrm{FP} \neq \#\mathrm{P}$. Notably the residual drops out: the reachable counting statement is purely about genericity of $q_0$.
