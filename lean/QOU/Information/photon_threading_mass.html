    QOU.Information.photon_threading_mass — mass as photon-threading cost #

    Proposition (prop:photon-threading-mass).

    Let 𝔋𝔋 be a knot composite with braid word β_𝔋 ∈ B_n𝔋 ∈ B_n. The categorical mass m_𝔋𝔋 equals the total Gröbner–Shirshov reduction cost of threading the photon strand γ through the hadron braid:

    m_𝔋 = (q − 1)² · |q − q⁻¹| · Σ_w |c_w|𝔋 = (q − 1)² · |q − q⁻¹| · Σ_w |c_w|,

    the sum running over the Hecke reductions σ_i² → (q − q⁻¹)σ_i + 1 that move γ from above β_𝔋𝔋 to below it.

    Formalisation notes #

    • Non-degenerate conditional (§3b-cond). The mass equation is derived, not assumed. The two genuine inputs are carried as fields:

      1. crossing_energy_eq — the manuscript's definition of the crossing energy E_𝔋 = |q − q⁻¹| · Σ_w |c_w|𝔋 = |q − q⁻¹| · Σ_w |c_w| (each Hecke step costs |q − q⁻¹| in Skein units, weighted by |c_w|);
      2. mass_energy_equiv — mass–energy equivalence m_𝔋 = E_𝔋 · (q − 1)²𝔋 = E_𝔋 · (q − 1)²𝔋 · (q − 1)² (the factor (q − 1)² = 1/c_q² converts Skein/spatial units to mass/temporal units), from [thm:mass-energy-equivalence].

      The headline equation is obtained by substitution + ring; it is not a field of the context, so the projection-vacuity test is not triggered (§3b-cond item 5).

    class QOU.Information.PhotonThreadingMassContext :
    Type

    Inputs for the photon-threading mass identity.

    The two manuscript facts that compose into the mass equation are carried as explicit fields (§3b-cond): the definition of the crossing energy as the summed Hecke cost, and mass–energy equivalence. The headline equation is derived from them.

    • q : ℝ

      Substrate parameter q : ℝ.

    • m_H : ℝ

      Categorical mass m_𝔋𝔋 of the hadron.

    • E_H : ℝ

      Crossing energy E_𝔋𝔋 of the hadron braid (Skein units).

    • c_w_abs : ℕ → ℝ

      The Hecke-reduction step coefficients |c_w| indexed by step.

    • n_steps : ℕ

      Number of Hecke reduction steps required for photon threading.

    • crossing_energy_eq : E_H = |q - q⁻¹| * ∑ i ∈ Finset.range n_steps, c_w_abs i

      Crossing-energy definition (input 1): each of the n_steps Hecke reductions costs |q − q⁻¹| in Skein units, weighted by the coefficient |c_w|, so E_𝔋 = |q − q⁻¹| · Σ_w |c_w|𝔋 = |q − q⁻¹| · Σ_w |c_w|.

    • mass_energy_equiv : m_H = E_H * (q - 1) ^ 2

      Mass–energy equivalence (input 2): the crossing energy converts to mass through the factor (q − 1)² = 1/c_q².
