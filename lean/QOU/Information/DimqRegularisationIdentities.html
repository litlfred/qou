    The exact identities behind the two regularisations of dim_q(𝒪_q(SU(2))) #

    computations/audits/dimq_regularisation_schemes_probe.py records what the two standard regularisations of the divergent Peter–Weyl sum

    dim_q(A) = Σ_{m ≥ 1} [m]_q² = (1/u²) Σ_{m ≥ 1} (q^{2m} + q^{-2m} − 2), u = q − q⁻¹,

    give. Each scheme reduces to an exact rational identity in q, and those identities are what this file proves — over an arbitrary field, with the non-vanishing of every denominator as an explicit hypothesis.

    • ζ-scheme. Continuing the two geometric series, Σ q^{2m} ↦ q²/(1 − q²) and Σ q^{−2m} ↦ q⁻²/(1 − q⁻²), the two pieces sum to −1 (zeta_geometric_pair_eq_neg_one), so with Σ 1 ↦ ζ(0) = −1/2 the numerator is −1 − 2ζ(0) = 0 for every q. The analytic continuation itself (that these are the ζ-regularised values) is the cited convention; only the algebra is proved here.
    • Hadamard finite part. Dropping the pole −2t/(1 − t) at t = 1 leaves −1/u². hadamard_eq_neg_six_iff says this equals −6 exactly when u² = 1/6, i.e. never at the substrate parameter (u(q₀)² ≈ 0.0437, probe).
    • The fundamental object is finite. qint_two is [2]_q = q + q⁻¹, the value tr(id_V) = dim_q(V) takes on the fundamental V.

    Nothing here selects a scheme, and nothing here derives −6.

    theorem QOU.Information.DimqRegularisation.zeta_geometric_pair_eq_neg_one {K : Type u_1} [Field K] (q : K) (hq : q ≠ 0) (h₁ : q ^ 2 - 1 ≠ 0) :
    q ^ 2 / (1 - q ^ 2) + (q ^ 2)⁻¹ / (1 - (q ^ 2)⁻¹) = -1

    The two continued geometric series sum to −1: q²/(1 − q²) + q⁻²/(1 − q⁻²) = −1 whenever q ≠ 0 and q² ≠ 1.

    theorem QOU.Information.DimqRegularisation.zeta_numerator_eq_zero {K : Type u_1} [Field K] [CharZero K] (q : K) (hq : q ≠ 0) (h₁ : q ^ 2 - 1 ≠ 0) :
    q ^ 2 / (1 - q ^ 2) + (q ^ 2)⁻¹ / (1 - (q ^ 2)⁻¹) - 2 * (-1 / 2) = 0

    Hence the ζ-regularised numerator (−1) − 2·ζ(0) vanishes once ζ(0) is taken as −1/2: the scheme gives dim_q^ζ(A) = 0 for every admissible q. (CharZero is needed only to know that 2 ≠ 0.)

    theorem QOU.Information.DimqRegularisation.hadamard_eq_neg_six_iff {K : Type u_1} [Field K] [CharZero K] (u : K) (hu : u ≠ 0) :
    -1 / u ^ 2 = -6 ↔ u ^ 2 = 1 / 6

    The Hadamard finite part −1/u² equals −6 iff u² = 1/6.

    theorem QOU.Information.DimqRegularisation.qint_two {K : Type u_1} [Field K] (q : K) (hq : q ≠ 0) (h₁ : q ^ 2 - 1 ≠ 0) :
    (q ^ 2 - (q ^ 2)⁻¹) / (q - q⁻¹) = q + q⁻¹

    [2]_q = (q² − q⁻²)/(q − q⁻¹) = q + q⁻¹ — the quantum dimension of the fundamental object, finite for every q ≠ 0 with q² ≠ 1 (equivalently q − q⁻¹ ≠ 0).
