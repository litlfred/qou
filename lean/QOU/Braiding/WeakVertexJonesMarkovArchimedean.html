    Numerical envelopes for the Jones–Markov derivation, at the proton pin #

    WeakVertexJonesMarkov is entirely generic-field algebra: the normalisation, the factorisation J − 1 = −h₄h₆/q¹⁰, and the exact ratio couplingRatio q = −G₈(q)G₁₂(q)/q¹⁹ need no ordering and no square root. What genuinely needs ℝ is evaluating those at the substrate anchor, and per AGENTS.md §7c that lives here rather than in the generic file.

    Anchor: the experimental (proton) pin q₀ = 1.1099785827513808… (qou_substrate.constants.Q), the default for all production compute.

    Two envelopes, both from the producer's numbers:

    • couplingRatio_near_q0 — the ratio has run from −96 to −37.09 by the time it reaches q₀. This is the archimedean face of couplingRatio_not_constant: the framing q^{-19} and the size of the Jones exponents suppress the ratio away from q = 1, which is why no rescaling constant exists.

    • 🛑 substitutionRateFactor_overshoot_of_leading_square is WITHDRAWN (2026-09-05, bean qou-xql6) and is replaced by substitutionRateFactor_has_no_classical_leading_value. It claimed the order of the substitution factor was derived, bracketing |W(q₀)|²/s(q₀)⁴ against 96². Two things were wrong with it, and the retraction is not a re-scoping.

      It compared two different ratios. −96 is the classical value of (J − 1)/s² (couplingRatio_one), where the numerator vanishes — J(1) = 1 because V_K(1) = 1 for every knot. The substitution factor's numerator does not vanish: W(1) = 1/4 is the Markov rank factor. So |W|²/s^k diverges as q → 1 for every k ≥ 1 and has no leading constant at all; −96 was never its leading value, and there was nothing to derive to order.

      And the power was wrong. sSq is s², so the merged substitutionRateFactor = (W)²/(sSq)² was |W|²/s⁴, while the rate carries s² once (prf:trace-ratio-observable). The factor is 129.405107404, not 11875.44.

      Every exact identity of this arc survives untouched — the Jones–Markov relation, J − 1 = −h₄h₆/q¹⁰, (J−1)/s² = −G₈G₁₂/q¹⁹, couplingRatio_one. Only the bridge from −96 to the rate is gone. Producer beta_rate_skein_power_audit.py (9/9).
