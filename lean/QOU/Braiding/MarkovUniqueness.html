    structure QOU.Braiding.MarkovUniqueness.IsMarkov {R : Type u_1} {A : Type u_2} [CommRing R] [Ring A] (inH : A → Prop) (σ : A) (z : R) (tr : A → R) :
    Prop

    Markov-functional data, relative to a level predicate inH (read: "lies in H_n"), a stabiliser σ (read: σ_n), and the Markov parameter z.

    Only the two properties the uniqueness argument actually consumes are recorded. Normalisation tr 1 = 1 is deliberately not a field: it fixes the base case of the induction, not the step, and including it here would suggest the step needs it.

    • tr_comm (a b : A) : tr (a * b) = tr (b * a)

      Cyclicity: tr (a * b) = tr (b * a).

    • markov (x : A) : inH x → tr (x * σ) = z * tr x

      The Markov relation: stabilising an element of H_n multiplies its trace by z.

      tr (a * σ * b) = tr' (a * σ * b)

      The inductive step of Markov-trace uniqueness.

      Two functionals that are cyclic, share the same Markov relation, and already agree on level n agree on every stabilised element a * σ * b with a, b at level n.

      This is the whole content of the induction: cyclicity moves b to the front so that b * a is again at level n, the Markov relation then peels σ off, and the level-n agreement finishes it. Nothing here is specific to Hecke algebras — it holds for any ring and any level predicate closed under the product.

      tr y = tr' y

      Markov-trace uniqueness, given spanning.

      If every element of the next level is either at level n or stabilised from level n, then two Markov functionals agreeing on level n agree there too — so the Markov trace is determined by its defining properties.

      🛑 hspan is the classical spanning fact H_{n+1} = H_n + H_n · σ_n · H_n (Jones 1985, Wenzl 1988) and is an explicit hypothesis: this corpus does not prove it, and markov-trace-inductive-limit.lean records its closure as open. Do not cite this theorem as though the spanning fact were established.

      The ∨ form is the single-term case; the general statement needs additivity of tr across the spanning decomposition, which is why this is stated for a decomposition that has already been taken.

      What contact_is_markov reduces to.

      Stated as a theorem rather than prose so the reduction is machine-checked: if the contact coefficient is itself a Markov functional agreeing with the Markov trace at level n, then on the spanned level it simply is the Markov trace — no separate identification is needed.

      This is the payoff of the file. JonesMobiusDiagonal.contact_is_markov assumes an equation; what actually has to be checked is that the diagonal residue satisfies cyclicity and the Markov relation.
