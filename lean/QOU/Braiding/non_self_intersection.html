    QOU.Braiding.non_self_intersection — categorical Möbius energy diverges at self-intersection #

    Proposition (prop:non-self-intersection).

    Let ϱ be a knot embedding with categorical Möbius energy E_cat(ϱ), and let q ≠ 1 be the substrate parameter. The non-cocommutativity of the Hopf structure at q ≠ 1 provides a categorical obstruction to self-intersection: the energy 𝔪(ϱ) diverges whenever two non-adjacent strands of ϱ approach within the fibre τ(A).

    The Lean encoding records the divergence claim as a ∀ M, ∃ ε, distance < ε → energy > M statement against an abstract NonSelfIntersectionContext.
