    The s² → |W|² substitution is not a small correction #

    prf:hadron-half-life (item 1) and rem:hadron-half-life-fermi (open computation 2) prescribe replacing the single-crossing coupling s² = (q^{1/2} − q^{-1/2})² = (q − 1)²/q in the decay rate by the full weak-vertex form factor |W(q)|², with W(q) = tr_M((σ₀σ₁)⁵) = q²(q¹² + q⁸ − 1)/(q² + 1)² (WeakVertexT35TraceClosedForm). The rate scales by |W|²/s² under that substitution, so the half-lives scale by s²/|W|².

    This file records, as a checked inequality at the proton pin q₀ ≈ 1.1099785, that the factor lies between 129 and 130 — the substitution shortens every predicted half-life by two orders of magnitude. It therefore cannot be the 10–17 % correction the corpus attributed to it. This is a scope-(a) negative about the prescribed substitution, not about W: the closed form is correct, the coupling it was to replace is a different normalisation. Bean qou-ix74.

    🛑 THE POWER WAS WRONG UNTIL 2026-09-05 — this file said s⁴ and 10⁴ (bean qou-xql6). substitutionRateFactor was defined as (weakVertexTrace q)^2 / (sSq q)^2, and sSq is already s² (sSq q = (√q − 1/√q)², and sSq_eq says q + q⁻¹ − 2), so squaring it gave s⁴. A name reading "s, squared" was consumed as "s".

    The rate carries s² exactly once. prf:trace-ratio-observable states it: |ℳ_q|² = |skein_amp|²·|tr(par)/tr(dau)|² with skein_amp = q^{1/2} − q^{−1/2}, "the Skein amplitude at the beta-decay vertex". Nothing else in the rate carries an s — [2]_q is full-scale, the pair-Skein denominator is the half-scale sum q^{1/2} + q^{−1/2}, and Q ∝ q − q⁻¹ is full-scale.

    So 11875.4418937 becomes 129.405107404, high by 1/s² = 91.77×. The scope-(a) negative SURVIVES — 129× still cannot be a 10–17 % correction — but "four orders of magnitude" is withdrawn in favour of two. Producer beta_rate_skein_power_audit.py (9/9); witness beta-rate-skein-power-audit.witness.json.
