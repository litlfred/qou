      Categorical Möbius energy E_cat(ρ).

      These three energies stay at ℝ on purpose (§7c): the Möbius energy is a classical real-analytic functional of a curve, and this claim is the archimedean statement that the categorical energy reproduces it in the classical limit. That is content on the far side of the substrate→archimedean wall. What does not belong at ℝ is the substrate parameter — see below.

    • claim_mobius_energy : Shared.MarkovTraceContext.q = 1 → E_cat = c_topo * E_class

      Manuscript identity: in the classical limit q → 1, the categorical Möbius energy recovers the classical Möbius energy up to a topological normalisation.

      The hypothesis is q = 1 on the inherited substrate parameter q : R. It previously read q_real = 1 for a private q_real : ℝ that was tied to the context's own q by nothing at all, so the antecedent constrained an unrelated free real and the "classical limit" was not a limit of this universe's substrate.
