    QOU.Braiding.hecke_mass_trace — 𝔪(ϱ_β) = |tr_M(Φ_mass(β))| #

    Proposition (prop:hecke-mass-trace).

    Let (𝐂, A, τ) be a QOU, let ϱ : 𝟙 → A be a vortex with braid representative β ∈ B_n, and let Φ_mass(β) ∈ H_n(R, q) be its Hecke mass element. The categorical mass equals the norm of the Markov trace of the Hecke mass element:

      𝔪(ϱ_β) = | tr_M(Φ_mass(β)) |

    Moreover the topological decomposition descends:

      tr_M(Φ_mass(β)) = tr_M(Φ^desc(β)) + Σ_i tr_M(Φ^trans_i(β)).

    The Lean encoding uses the existing QOU.Hecke.MarkovTrace infrastructure for the Markov trace, with abstract fields for the Hecke mass element and the topological decomposition components.

    • claim_cat_mass_eq_norm_trace : cat_mass = norm ((Hecke.MarkovTrace.trace n q) Phi_mass)

      Manuscript claim (i): the categorical mass equals the norm of the Markov trace of the Hecke mass element. Carried as a class field per AGENTS.md §3b-cond — downstream consumers take [HeckeMassTraceContext] as an explicit hypothesis to obtain this identity without a naked sorry; the identity is conditional on the class instance, not an unconditional theorem.

    • claim_trace_decomposition : (Hecke.MarkovTrace.trace n q) Phi_mass = (Hecke.MarkovTrace.trace n q) Phi_desc + List.foldr (fun (a : A) (acc : R) => (Hecke.MarkovTrace.trace n q) a + acc) 0 Phi_trans

      Manuscript claim (ii): the Markov trace decomposes additively over the descended + transient pieces. The fibre functor τ is exact and monoidal, so the mass identity descends to H_n(R, q); tr_M is R-linear in its argument. §3b-cond class field.
