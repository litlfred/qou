    QOU.Braiding.fermion_boson_knot_link — intentionally empty; the formalisation lives in the content-block sibling #

    This module used to hold FermionBosonKnotLinkContext together with a projection that discharged the manuscript claim from that class's own fields. Both are removed (2026-08-31).

    Why the declaration was empty. The class carried the claim as its own axioms and the accompanying declaration handed them straight back, so a [FermionBosonKnotLinkContext] binder assumed nothing and the declaration asserted nothing. The file's own docstring said as much: "the substantive mathematical content is carried by the context-class axioms". It is the proof-no-self-assuming-projection shape of AGENTS.md §3b-cond item 5, and the class was inhabited by Unit and zero (bean qou-z9ws).

    Why removal costs nothing readable. Three facts, all measured:

    1. The content block's lean.ref is qou:QOU.Braiding.fermion_boson_knot_link, and per AGENTS.md §0a resolution checks the sibling first — so the block already resolves to knots-particles-confinement/fermion-boson-knot-link.lean, never to this file.
    2. That sibling carries the real formalisation: TorusKnotLink, fermion_iff_odd, boson_iff_even.
    3. Nothing imports this module and nothing else in content/ names the class (measured 2026-08-31 across every .lean in the tree).

    So the declaration was unreachable from its own block, unreachable by import, and vacuous where it stood.

    The file is kept rather than deleted so that the lean.ref's second resolution candidate — <lakeRoot>/QOU/Braiding/fermion_boson_knot_link.lean — still exists as a real path.
