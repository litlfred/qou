    QOU.Braiding.jones_mobius_diagonal — Jones polynomial as the Möbius contact term #

    The fundamental ($N = 2$) Jones polynomial is the diagonal / contact datum of the categorical Möbius two-point kernel, and the categorical Möbius energy is the off-diagonal (renormalized) remainder. Hence the fundamental Jones data embeds into the Möbius two-point data as its self-energy / contact term.

    LaTeX label: lem:jones-mobius-diagonal

    Structure #

    The two categorical facts are recorded as class fields:

    1. contact_is_markov — the kernel's diagonal contact coefficient is the fundamental Markov trace (lem:mobius-energy-frobenius-kernel restricted to the diagonal, identified via thm:jones-markov-trace);
    2. renormalization — the Möbius energy is the regularized two-point value minus the diagonal contact (Hadamard finite part).

    The embedding conclusion markov_eq_mobius_contact is then proved (no sorry): the fundamental Jones polynomial equals the term the Möbius renormalization subtracts. The base ring is generic (AddCommGroup) since the renormalization is an additive identity (AGENTS.md §7c base-ring convention); it specialises to R_q = ℤ[q, q⁻¹] and to ℝ.

    class QOU.Braiding.JonesMobiusDiagonal (R : Type u_1) [AddCommGroup R] :
    Type u_1

    Abstract data for the Jones–Möbius diagonal identity over a base ring R. R is generic (the renormalization is an additive identity, §7c).

    • markovTrace : R

      Fundamental ($N = 2$) Markov trace tr_M(ρ(β_K)); its value is the Jones polynomial J_K.

    • contact : R

      Diagonal contact coefficient of the categorical Möbius kernel tr(Δ(ϱ(s)) ⊗ Δ(ϱ(t))) — the regularized residue on s = t.

    • mobiusEnergy : R

      Off-diagonal (renormalized) categorical Möbius energy E_cat(ϱ).

    • twoPointReg : R

      Regularized two-point value: the full kernel integral over S¹ × S¹ ∖ diag with the universal singular part removed (finite part).

    • contact_is_markov : contact = markovTrace

      (Diagonal = Jones). The kernel's contact coefficient is the fundamental Markov trace — the coincidence restriction of lem:mobius-energy-frobenius-kernel, identified via thm:jones-markov-trace.

    • renormalization : mobiusEnergy = twoPointReg - contact

      (Renormalization). The Möbius energy is the regularized two-point value minus the diagonal contact (Hadamard finite part).

      markovTrace = twoPointReg - mobiusEnergy

      Lemma (Jones polynomial = Möbius contact term). The fundamental Jones polynomial (Markov trace) is exactly the diagonal self-energy subtracted by the Möbius renormalization: J_K = twoPointReg − E_cat. The fundamental Jones data therefore embeds into the Möbius two-point data as its contact / diagonal term; the converse map loses the off-diagonal fusion content Δ(e_λ) = Σ N^λ_{μν} e_μ ⊗ e_ν.
