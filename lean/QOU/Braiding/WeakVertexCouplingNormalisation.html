    What the decay-rate coupling s² actually is, and why W cannot be rescaled into it #

    WeakVertexSubstitutionScale showed that substituting the weak-vertex trace W(q) = tr_M((σ₀σ₁)⁵) for the single-crossing coupling s² = (q^{1/2} − q^{-1/2})² in the decay rate multiplies every rate by ≈ 129.4 (the figure read ≈ 1.19·10⁴ until 2026-09-05 — sSq is already s² and was squared again, while the rate carries s² once; bean qou-xql6). That left one question: what normalisation does relate them. Nothing in this file depends on the magnitude: the exclusion below is about the candidate family's functional form.

    This file answers the first half and prunes the obvious family for the second.

    s² is a classical-value deviation, not an arbitrary amplitude #

    sSq_eq_twoQ_sub_two: s² = [2]_q − 2 exactly, where [2]_q = q + q⁻¹ is the SU_q(2) fundamental dimension and 2 = [2]_1 is its classical value. So the coupling the rate uses is the q-dimension minus its q = 1 value, which is what makes the rate vanish classically. That identity — not a convention — is what any W-based replacement has to imitate, and it motivates the candidate family c · (W(q) − W(1)) · (bridge), with W(1) = 1/4 (weakVertexTrace_one).

    The candidate family is excluded, and not by its order #

    s² vanishes to second order at q = 1 (sSq_one, hasDerivAt_sSq giving s²′(1) = 0), while W − W(1) vanishes to first order (hasDerivAt_weakVertexTrace_one: W′(1) = 5 ≠ 0). One bridge factor vanishing like q − 1 — the corpus has h = q − q⁻¹, ℏ_q = 1 − q⁻¹, s, q − 1 — repairs the order, so order alone does not decide.

    What decides is no_constant_rescaling: for the order-matched candidate (W − 1/4)·ℏ_q there is no constant c with c · (W(q) − 1/4) · (1 − q⁻¹) = s²(q) for all q > 1. The required c is 100/69591 at q = 2 and 86528/5647625 at q = 3/2. Equivalently the ratio ((W − 1/4)·ℏ_q)/s² runs from 5 (as q → 1) through 8.524 at the proton pin to 695.91 at q = 2: it is a function, not a constant, so the mismatch is in the functional form and no normalisation constant absorbs it.

    This is a structural exclusion of that family, proved at two rational points; it says nothing against W itself, whose closed form is weakVertexTrace_eq. What the right coupling is remains open — bean qou-pfh7.

    Ground ring: generic field K throughout this file (AGENTS.md §7c). The archimedean half — the vanishing-order derivatives and the numeric exclusion — lives in WeakVertexCouplingNormalisationArchimedean, which consumes these definitions at K := ℝ. Verified lean-direct, sorry-free.
