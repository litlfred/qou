    Closed form for the weak-vertex form factor W(q) = tr_M((σ₀σ₁)⁵) in H₃(q) #

    rem:hadron-half-life-fermi lists the weak-vertex form factor — the canonical Markov trace of the T(3,5) torus braid (σ₀σ₁)⁵ ∈ H₃(q) — as an open computation, and prop:hadron-half-life's Lean (BetaRateData.W2, PR #6631) carries it as positive data with no closed form. This file supplies the closed form, derived through the Wedderburn decomposition of H₃(q):

    W(q) = tr_M((σ₀σ₁)⁵) = q² (q¹² + q⁸ − 1) / (q² + 1)²          (convention D)

    The derivation, and what each theorem carries #

    H₃(q) ≅ K ⊕ M₂(K) ⊕ K (Wedderburn), with the trivial block σᵢ ↦ q, the sign block σᵢ ↦ −q⁻¹, and the two-dimensional block in Young seminormal form — the same matrices as QOU.Archimedean.HeckeH3.stdRep3_gen0/1, re-stated here over a generic field because the convention-D Markov parameter z = q³/(q² + 1) needs (q² + 1)⁻¹, which the Laurent ring ℤ[t, t⁻¹] of that file does not contain.

    The canonical Markov trace (Ocneanu; convention D, owner ruling R1 2026-08-15) is the linear functional on H₃(q) whose values on the six reduced-word basis elements are

    tr_M(1) = 1,  tr_M(σ₀) = tr_M(σ₁) = z,  tr_M(σ₀σ₁) = tr_M(σ₁σ₀) = z²,  tr_M(σ₀σ₁σ₀) = h z² + z

    with h = q − q⁻¹, z = q³/(q² + 1) — exactly the table pyhecke.gram.TR_M and computations/markov/markov_trace_canonical.py implement. Being a trace, it is a linear combination of the three irreducible characters, tr_M = Σ_λ y_λ χ_λ. Solving the three equations tr_M(1) = 1, tr_M(σ₀) = z, tr_M(σ₀σ₁) = z² for the weights gives

    y₍₃₎ = (q⁴ + 1)/(q² + 1)²,   y₍₂₁₎ = q²/(q² + 1)²,   y₍₁₁₁₎ = 0,

    and y₍₁₁₁₎ = 0 is the statement that convention D is the SU(2) (Temperley–Lieb) trace. ocneanuD_basis_* below verifies that the functional built from these weights reproduces all six basis values — three more than were used to solve for it, which is the check that the weights are right. A linear functional on the six-dimensional H₃(q) is determined by its values on a basis, so that functional is tr_M; that last (standard) step — that the six reduced words are a basis — is not formalised here and is the one thing the reader supplies.

    On the T(3,5) braid: (σ₀σ₁)³ = Δ² is the full twist, which acts on the (2,1) block as the identity (stdGen_M_cubed_eq_one, the generic-field form of QOU.Archimedean.HeckeH3.stdRep3_M_cubed_eq_one), so (σ₀σ₁)⁵ acts there as (σ₀σ₁)², whose trace is −1 (chi21_T35); on the trivial block it is q¹⁰. Hence

    W(q) = y₍₃₎ q¹⁰ − y₍₂₁₎ = q²(q¹² + q⁸ − 1)/(q² + 1)²                (`weakVertexTrace_eq`).

    Cross-check against the Jones polynomial #

    T(3,5) is the knot 10₁₂₄, V(t) = t⁴ + t⁶ − t¹⁰. The numerator of W is q²² · V(q⁻²) (weakVertexTrace_numerator_eq_jones), the mirror/writhe normalisation of the Ocneanu trace of a positive braid on three strands. This is an identity of Laurent polynomials, independent of the Wedderburn route, and pins the result to a published invariant.

    Value at the substrate #

    At the proton pin q₀ ≈ 1.10998, W(q₀) ≈ 1.18748 (weakVertexTrace_near_q0). The value ≈ 0.7036 published in obs:hadron-half-life-verification is reproduced by neither convention's Ocneanu trace (D: 1.1875; C, z = √q/(q+1): 0.9063). Its source — identified 2026-09-05, bean qou-ix74, after this header first attributed it to the deprecated t35-full-calculation.py, which was wrong — is the live hecke_division engine's former trace rule: the Markov rotation applied without the Hecke relation, which collapses to Σ_w c_w z^{|w|} over the reduced word and gives 0.70355 at z_C and 0.96844 at z_D (the latter is what t35-weak-vertex.witness.json carried from 2026-08-29). The engine now delegates to the canonical recursion and returns this file's value. The content was corrected in PR #6689 (bean qou-4x7c).
