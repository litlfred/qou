    QOU.Braiding.skein_crossing_asymmetry — crossing asymmetry ⇒ Coulomb repulsion #

    Proposition (prop:skein-crossing-asymmetry).

    The R-matrix braiding weights positive crossings by q and negative crossings by q⁻¹, so the Skein relation

    q · L₊ − q⁻¹ · L₋ = (q^{1/2} − q^{-1/2}) · L₀

    is asymmetric between L₊ and L₋. The physical content the manuscript extracts is the per-crossing Coulomb correction

    δ_Coulomb = q − ½(q + q⁻¹) = ½(q − q⁻¹),

    which is > 0 for q > 1 (repulsion) and vanishes at q = 1. Both the correction identity and its positivity are derived sorry-free.

    The development is over ℝ (the correction is a physical real quantity and the positivity is archimedean — uses 1 < q; §7c). The Skein relation is carried as an explicit hypothesis (h_skein) encoding the R-matrix eigenvalue structure; the asymmetry/positivity is derived from it together with q > 1 — no hypothesis field is the conclusion.

    NOTE: the content block prop:skein-crossing-asymmetry resolves to its sibling climax-volume-mass/skein-crossing-asymmetry-prop.lean, which carries the same (sorry-free) development; this library copy is kept in sync.

      theorem QOU.Braiding.skein_crossing_asymmetry (q : ℝ) (hq : 1 < q) :
      q⁻¹ < q ∧ q - 1 / 2 * (q + q⁻¹) = coulombCorrection q ∧ 0 < coulombCorrection q

      Skein crossing asymmetry ⇒ Coulomb repulsion.

      In the substrate regime q > 1 the R-matrix weights the positive crossing (q) strictly more than the negative crossing (q⁻¹): q⁻¹ < q. This crossing-weight asymmetry is exactly the per-crossing Coulomb correction δ = ½(q − q⁻¹) > 0 (relative to the mixed-charge baseline ½(q + q⁻¹)). All three conjuncts are derived from q > 1 (no unused hypothesis: the weight asymmetry, the correction identity, and the positivity each follow from the single premise).
