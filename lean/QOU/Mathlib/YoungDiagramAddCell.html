    QOU.Mathlib.YoungDiagramAddCell — adjoining a corner, the inverse of eraseCorner #

    YoungDiagramCorner supplies IsCornerCell and eraseCorner. This file supplies the other direction — IsAddableCell and addCell — together with the two round-trips.
    IsAddableCell μ s s ∉ μ, with its up- and left-neighbours present or absent by boundary
    addCell μ s hs μ with s adjoined; still a Young diagram
    card_addCell \|μ + s\| = \|μ\| + 1
    isCornerCell_addCell the added cell is a removable corner of the result
    isAddableCell_eraseCorner a removable corner is addable to the erased diagram
    eraseCorner_addCell, addCell_eraseCorner the two round-trips

    Why #

    The Markov assembly (bean qou-uer7, step 2b) needs the reindexing

    {(λ, removable corner of λ) : |λ| = n+1} ≃ {(μ, addable corner of μ) : |μ| = n},

    and SYTBranchingRule.branchInv supplies only the inner half — it fixes λ and varies the corner. The outer exchange needs to build λ from (μ, s), which eraseCorner alone cannot do. These lemmas are that construction and its round-trips, and cornerAddEquiv below is the exchange itself.

    Pure combinatorics; no ring, nothing archimedean.

    LaTeX label: def:young-diagram-add-cell

    IsAddableCell, addCell, mem_addCell and card_addCell are not redeclared here — they come from QOU.Mathlib.YoungDiagramAddable, which this module now imports.

    They used to be defined in both files, with character-identical bodies, and the duplication made the two modules MUTUALLY EXCLUSIVE: no module could import both, since Lean rejects the second declaration of a name already in the environment. That is a real constraint, not a tidiness point — it is what stopped YoungLatticeCornerCount from reusing YoungDiagramAddable's corner/addable API while needing cornerAddEquiv from here (bean qou-dup1).

    This file is now a proper EXTENSION of YoungDiagramAddable: it adds the eraseCorner round-trips and the outer exchange, and redeclares nothing. Measured before the change: card_addCell and mem_addCell have zero consumers outside this file, so the argument-order difference between the two former copies breaks nothing.

    The outer branching exchange #

    (λ, removable corner) ↔ (λ ∖ c, that corner, now addable).

    Phrasing note, and it is the whole trick. The natural spelling is a Sigma over the diagram, Σ L : {μ // |μ| = n+1}, {c // IsCornerCell L.1 c}. Don't: the second component's type then depends on the first, so left_inv lands on a HEq between corner-proofs over two diagrams that are equal but not syntactically so, and discharging it needs a transport whose motive does not typecheck (the card-proof inside the diagram depends on the diagram). Phrasing both sides as subtypes of one product type removes the dependency: the round-trips are then plain Subtype.ext on a pair.
