    QOU.Mathlib.SYTEraseMax — deleting the max-value cell (forward branching map) #

    The forward half of the Greene–Nijenhuis–Wilf branching bijection: given a standard tableau T of μ and the removable corner c carrying the top value |μ|, blank that cell to obtain a standard tableau of μ ∖ c. Its entry map is T's, restricted to the cells of μ ∖ c (and 0 elsewhere); the strict row/column laws are inherited, and the entry bijection lands on {1, …, |μ|−1} = {1, …, |μ ∖ c|} because the only cell that carried |μ| was the deleted corner.

    Pure combinatorics; nothing archimedean; no fit.
