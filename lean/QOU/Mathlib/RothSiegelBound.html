    Siegel's exponent, and why the counting threshold needs a factor of two #

    siegelHeight is (#box · ‖A‖)^ρ with ρ = #E/(#box − #E). The two size estimates above make the base well behaved — both factors are C^{Σr} — but that is worth nothing while ρ is unbounded, and the counting condition proved so far only gives #E < #box, hence #box − #E ≥ 1 and ρ ≤ #E. With #E itself exponential in Σr, that bound makes siegelHeight doubly exponential and the whole argument fails.

    The fix is a factor of two in the threshold: asking 2·deg α < 4ε²m instead of deg α < 4ε²m gives 2·#E ≤ #box, hence ρ ≤ 1 and siegelHeight ≤ #box·‖A‖. "Take m large" becomes m > deg α/(2ε²) rather than m > deg α/(4ε²) — a factor of two in m, bought to avoid a double exponential. Worth stating because the weaker threshold is the one the variance argument naturally produces, and it is not enough.

    The obligation, without real logarithms #

    The remaining condition reads naturally in logs — log H₀ ≳ m²q·log(4B)/p — but that form is painful to formalise and, it turns out, unnecessary. Exponentiating throughout leaves a purely integer inequality:

    C₀^{mq} · W^{2m²q}  ≤  H₀^p

    with C₀ any integer above c K and W any integer above 4B. Both are constants of K and α; neither involves r, N or the heights. So "choose H₀ large" is a single arithmetic condition on H₀ that a parameter family can be checked against directly, with no analysis.

    ceil_siegelHeight_le turns the height bound into ⌈siegelHeight⌉ ≤ C₀·W^{Σr}, and pow_le_pow_of_height is the arithmetic: Σr ≤ 2m·r₀ (from sum_rothDegrees_le once r₀ ≥ 1) makes the exponent Σr·mq at most 2m²q·r₀, which the condition on H₀ then dominates once raised to r₀.

    All seven clauses of Γ_m, at the auxiliary polynomial's own height. Not an implication: the height clause is discharged here, so what comes out is RothGammaFull itself, instantiated at b = ⌈siegelHeight r α μ⌉ — the very bound Siegel's lemma hands step 1.

    This is where the near-circularity finally closes. hbound is a statement about the produced H and N, and b is a function of them, so neither can be a hypothesis of the existential. The resolution is that the threshold C₀^{mq}·W^{2m²q} refers to none of the produced data — it is a constant of K, α, m, p, q alone — so it can be handed to the selection as its floor parameter T and recovered afterwards from T ≤ H j. T is taken as a parameter above that threshold rather than equal to it, because later stages impose floors of their own and the selection can only be given one.

    The hypotheses that remain are each a fact about one fixed object:

    • hfloorExp pins F against m, p, q (the Γ_m exponent clause, unchanged);
    • hm2 : 2·[K:ℚ] < 4ε²m is the doubled unknowns-beat-conditions threshold, i.e. m > [K:ℚ]/2ε² — doubled because Siegel's exponent must be ≤ 1, not merely finite, or the height is doubly exponential in Σ r;
    • hα bounds the houses of the α h by B, and hC₀/hW name integers above c K and 4B.

    Nothing here mentions N, r, or the polynomial.
