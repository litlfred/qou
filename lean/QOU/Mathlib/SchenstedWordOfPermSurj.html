    QOU.Mathlib.SchenstedWordOfPermSurj — every permutation word comes from a permutation #

    Brick 36 of epic qou-nrex (RSK inverse).

    rskPairWord accepts any word whose content is {1, …, n}; rskPair accepts a permutation and reaches rskPairWord through wordOfPerm. Surjectivity of rskPair therefore needs the two entry points to see the same words:

    exists_wordOfPerm_eq — a word of content {1, …, n} is wordOfPerm σ for some σ.

    The permutation is read straight off the word: σ i = w[i] - 1. It is injective because the word is duplicate-free, and on a finite type injective is bijective — which is the only reason no inverse has to be built by hand.

    A word of content {1, …, n} has length n, no repeats, and entries in [1, n].

    theorem QOU.Mathlib.Schensted.exists_wordOfPerm_eq {w : List ℕ} {n : ℕ} (hw : ↑w = Multiset.map (fun (x : ℕ) => x + 1) ↑(List.range n)) :
    ∃ (σ : Equiv.Perm (Fin n)), wordOfPerm σ = w

    Every word of content {1, …, n} is a permutation word. The permutation is i ↦ w[i] - 1; duplicate-freeness makes it injective, and injective is bijective on a finite type.
