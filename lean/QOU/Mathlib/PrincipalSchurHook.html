    QOU.Mathlib.PrincipalSchurHook — the branching Schur polynomial at x_i = Qⁱ #

    Bialternant.alt_add_stair gives a_{ν+δ} = a_δ · s_ν for the branching Schur polynomial, and RSSYTSchur.bsum_eq_schur identifies s_ν with the sum over reverse tableaux with entries < N. This file evaluates at the principal point x_i = Qⁱ. There the alternant is a Vandermonde, and the corpus Frame–Robinson–Thrall identity SchurWeyl.hookSide_betaR turns it into hooks:
    alt_pow a_α(1, Q, …, Q^{N−1}) = ∏_{a<b} (Q^{α_b} − Q^{α_a})
    prod_Ioi_eq_prod_pairs a Fin N pair product as a product over {(i,j) : i < j < N}
    alt_principal a_{α^μ}(Qⁱ) = M · Q^{e(μ)} · ∏_{i<j<N} (1 − Q^{β_i − β_j}), M independent of μ
    hook_mul_schur_principal ∏_c (1 − Q^{h(c)}) · s_μ(1, …, Q^{N−1}) = Q^{e(μ)} · ∏_{i<N} ∏_{t<μ_i} (1 − Q^{N−i+t})

    Here α^μ_j = μ_{N−1−j} + j, β_i = μ_i + (N−1−i) (so α^μ_j = β_{N−1−j}), and e(μ) = ∑_{j<N} j · μ_j.

    The last row is the hook-content formula for the principal specialisation, division-free. Its only hypotheses are that Q is not zero and no 1 − Q^k (k ≥ 1) vanishes, in a domain — they are used to cancel the μ-independent factor M · ∏_{i<N} (Q;Q)_{N−1−i} that the empty diagram contributes.

    Generic [CommRing R]; the cancellation step adds [IsDomain R]. No ℝ, no ordering.
