    QOU.Mathlib.SchenstedRSKSurjective — every standard pair is hit #

    Brick 32 of epic qou-nrex (RSK inverse), and the point the P and Q sides meet.

    insertWordRec_uninsertSteps_of_standard — for a pair (P, Q) of the right shape, the word the reverse cascade recovers from P at Q's positions has insertWordRec w = (P, Q): not merely the right insertion tableau, but the right recording tableau too.

    The three inputs, each proved elsewhere:

    • insertWord_uninsertSteps_of_standard (brick 27) — the recovered word's insertion tableau is P;
    • rowIndices_uninsertSteps (brick 29) — the recovered word's row-index record is the position list it was recovered from;
    • qAppendSteps_qPositions_self (brick 31) — replaying that position list rebuilds Q,

    joined by brick 4's identification of the recording tableau with the record of rowIndices. The second is the one that was missing: without it the recovered word's recording tableau is unconstrained, and "the insertion tableau came out right" says nothing about Q.

    What the hypotheses say #

    hrange and hlt together say Q's entries are exactly 0, …, n-1; with hQstrict, hQc and hQnd that is standardness. hshape says P has the shape those positions build — the pair is a pair, not two unrelated tableaux.

    Scope #

    Surjectivity of rskPair onto standard pairs of a common shape, at the level of this representation. It is not bijectivity: injectivity is SchenstedRSKInjective, and combining them into a bijection — with the shape and standardness conditions matched to whatever rskPair's codomain is — is the next brick. RSKBurnsideHolds for general n remains open; it is proved only for n ≤ 12, by the unrelated rskBurnsideHolds_le12 route with no RSK correspondence in it.

    Pure combinatorics on ℕ; nothing archimedean; no fit.

    Scope note superseded (2026-08-23). The scope paragraph above records the state of the arc when this brick landed.     theorem QOU.Mathlib.Schensted.insertWordRec_uninsertSteps_of_standard {P Q : List (List ℕ)} (hPs : RowsSorted P) (hPc : ColStrict P) (hPn : NoEmptyRows P) (hQc : ColStrict Q) (hQstrict : ∀ row ∈ Q, List.Sorted (fun (x1 x2 : ℕ) => x1 < x2) row) (hQnd : Q.flatten.Nodup) (hQn : NoEmptyRows Q) {n : ℕ} (hrange : ∀ t < n, t ∈ Q.flatten) (hlt : ∀ a ∈ Q.flatten, a < n) (hshape : tableauShape P = addCellSteps [] (qPositions Q n)) :
    insertWordRec (uninsertSteps P (qPositions Q n)).2 = (P, Q)

    Every standard pair of a common shape is an insertion pair.

    The word is the one the reverse cascade recovers from P at the positions Q records. Its insertion tableau is P by brick 27; its recording tableau is Q because its row-index record is Q's position list (brick 29) and replaying that list rebuilds Q (brick 31).
