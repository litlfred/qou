    QOU.Mathlib.SYTBranchingRecursion — step 1 of closing dimAitkenIdentity #

    The general-n hook-length formula numSYTenum = dimHLF is isolated to the single cited sorry SYTHookFormulaGeneral.dimAitkenIdentity. The route to closing it (scoped in docs/workplans/2026-06-28-syt-hlf-branching-bijection-scope.md) is the corner-removal branching recursion: both the brute-force count and the closed form satisfy

    F(λ)  =  Σ_{i a removable corner of λ}  F(λ⁻ⁱ),        F ∈ {numSYTenum, dimHLF}.

    This file lands step 1: the corner-removal combinators and the exact, sorry-free, decide-verified branching identities for small n. They serve two purposes:

    • they pin the precise statements the eventual general lemmas (numSYTenum_branching (B) — the hard bijection — and dimHLF_branching (A) — pure β-algebra) must prove, removing any ambiguity in the off-by-one corner bookkeeping; and
    • they are a regression backstop: any future general proof must agree with these on n ≤ 5.

    No new sorry is introduced — per the net-sorry discipline in the scope doc, the general statements (A)/(B) are added only when (A) is provable sorry-free. Pure combinatorics on ℕ; nothing archimedean.
