    QOU.Mathlib.RamifiedShiftCalculusSum — the sum case of the ramified shift calculus #

    QOU.Mathlib.RamifiedShiftCalculus (#6474) pruned single-shift derivative-like prolongations of shift 1 or 2 at a ramified pole z = z_c + t²: they annihilate constants, so the residue slot t^{−2} is dead at every jet level. Its audit left the general q-D-module element — a finite sum Σ_k c_k σ^k — explicitly open: "its residue slot is the sum of the slots and could in principle cancel or survive".

    This module settles that case, and the answer is not the one the single-shift result suggests.

    1. Mixed shifts revive the residue — without any cancellation #

    For the two-term operator twoOp c₁ c₂ = shiftOp 2 c₂ + shiftOp 1 c₁ with both terms annihilating constants (c₁(−1) = 0, c₂(−2) = 0), the residue slot at jet level n + 2 is

    (twoOp c₁ c₂)^[n+2] f (−2) = c₁(−2) · c₂(−1) · (twoOp c₁ c₂)^[n] f (1)

    (twoOp_iterate_residue). The mechanism is a jump, not a cancellation: the word "shift 1 then shift 2" reads t^{−2} from t^{−1} and t^{−1} from t^{+1}, never landing on the constant slot t⁰ where every derivative's multiplier vanishes. Every other word of length 2 does land there and dies. So the residue at level n + 2 is the t¹ coefficient of the level-n jet — the leading square-root coefficient of the branch, which is nonzero for a genuine branch.

    This is not a q-phenomenon: for the classical d/dt (shift 1, e+1) and (1/t)d/dt (shift 2, e+2) the weight is (−1)·(1) = −1 (classicalTwo_iterate_residue), and the Jackson [e+1]_q + q-deformed [e+2]_q pair gives the same −1 (jacksonPlusQddz_iterate_residue).

    Consequence for the single-shift prune. Its clause "a residue needs a non-derivative component" was proved for single-shift operators and does not extend to sums: two derivatives of different shifts suffice. What the residue does need, among shifts ≤ 2, is the odd (shift-1) component — a pure shift-2 sum is a single shift-2 operator and stays dead.

    2. The (−1)^n residual kills it again #

    An operator D = E + O whose even part E commutes with the covering monodromy t ↦ −t and whose odd part O anti-commutes with it satisfies the parity twist mono (D f) = E (mono f) − O (mono f). If a jet tower from a fixed-parity seed X carries the full Door-v residual mono (Dⁿ X) = (−1)ⁿ ε · Dⁿ X for all n, then (char ≠ 2, no zero divisors) the even part annihilates every level of the tower and the tower is generated by O alone (residual_tower_is_odd_part).

    For the two-term derivative-like operator this closes the loop: the residual forces Dⁿ X = (shiftOp 1 c₁)ⁿ X, and the single-shift prune then kills the residue slot (twoOp_residual_kills_residue). The residue and the residual are mutually exclusive for derivative-like sums of shifts ≤ 2 — the single-shift conclusion "odd shift and a non-derivative component" survives for sums, but by a different mechanism: the residue admits mixed derivatives, the residual does not.

    Ground ring #

    Generic [CommRing R]; [CharZero R] [NoZeroDivisors R] only for the residual theorem (the step 2·y = 0 ⇒ y = 0); [Field K] only where q-integers are instantiated, with the hypothesis q − q⁻¹ ≠ 0 that [1]_q = 1 needs.

          The residual kills the residue. For the two-term derivative-like operator (shift-1 component annihilating constants) on a power-series fixed-parity seed, a tower carrying the full (−1)^n residual is the pure shift-1 tower, and its residue slot is dead at every level. Residue and residual are mutually exclusive.

          The residual demanded only from a level n₀ on #

          The corpus's Door-v residual is asymptotic: the sign alternation of the GGM carrier's growing solution sets in only once its recurrence coefficient 2 − q^{m+1} turns negative (conj:c1-ggm-bar-parity-faces, "for m+1 < log 2 / log q … no sign change occurs"). So the hypothesis that matters is alternation for n ≥ n₀, not for all n. The theorem survives with the same proof, applied to the level-n₀ jet as the seed: from n₀ on the tower is the odd part's, and — because a shift-1 iterate of length ≥ 2 reads t^{−2} through t^{−1} with the dead weight c₁(−1) — the residue slot is dead at every level ≥ n₀ + 2, with no hypothesis on the seed. Levels 2 … n₀ + 1 are untouched: there the residue formula twoOp_iterate_residue still applies and the slot is alive.
