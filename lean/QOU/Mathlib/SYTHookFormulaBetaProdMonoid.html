    QOU.Mathlib.SYTHookFormulaBetaProdMonoid — the full FRT hook product in any commutative monoid #

    SYTHookFormulaBetaProd assembles the per-row identity into the full divisor-free Frame–Robinson–Thrall hook product over ℕ. SYTHookFormulaBetaMonoid showed the per-row half is arithmetic-free in shape. The assembly is too: colLen_eq_succ_add_cnt_drop, drop_sorted and drop_le_getD are facts about ℕ indices and are reused verbatim, so only the two product statements need restating.
    prodHooksF f l ∏_{c ∈ λ} f(h_λ(c)) — the corpus prodHooks, pushed through f
    prodHooksF_eq_prod_rowHookProdF it is the product of the per-row rowHookProdF
    prodHooksF_mul_betaGapProdF prodHooksF f l · ∏_i betaGapProdF f (l[i]) (drop) = ∏_i descFactF f β_i
    prodHooksF_one_sub_pow the same at f n = 1 − Qⁿ

    The last row is the q-analogue of ∏_c h_λ(c) = (∏_i β_i!) / ∏_{i<j}(β_i − β_j), cleared of its denominator:

    ∏_c (1 − Q^{h_λ(c)}) · ∏_i (β-gaps) = ∏_i ∏_{k=1}^{β_i} (1 − Q^k).

    What this is not yet. It is the hook-length identity in β-gap form, not in Vandermonde form. Recognising ∏_i betaGapProdF (fun n => 1 − Qⁿ) … as ∏_{i<j}(1 − Q^{β_i − β_j}) — equivalently as ∏_{i<j}(x_i − x_j) up to a monomial, with x_i = Q^{β_i} — is the remaining step, and it is a reindexing of a nested recursion into a pair product rather than a new identity. Bean qou-j7ln; the consumer is QOU.Mathlib.LagrangeCoeffSum.sum_eq_bracket_mul_of_qratio_scaling.
