    QOU.Mathlib.FibrationExactness — exactness at the middle spot #

    Bean qou-de7r. For p : E → B with p e = b and F = p⁻¹(b), the sequence

    π_N(F, e) → π_N(E, e) → π_N(B, b)

    is exact at π_N(E, e). That is the theorem this file proves, and it is the only spot of the homotopy long exact sequence that can be reached without the connecting map.

    What is proved #

    • fibre_comp_eq_const — the composite is trivial. No hypothesis at all: p ∘ ι is the constant map b, because that is what it means to be the fibre, so homotopyGroupMap_const finishes it.
    • exists_preimage_of_map_eq_const — the converse, from the stationary relative homotopy lifting property. A class in π_N(E, e) killed by p_* comes from the fibre.
    • homotopyGroupMap_eq_const_iff — the two together, as an iff, at the level of pointed sets: no group structure, hence no Nonempty N, is needed to state or prove it.
    • range_homotopyGroupHom_eq_ker — the same statement as range = ker of MonoidHoms, once N is nonempty with decidable equality so that Mathlib's group structure exists.
    • injective_homotopyGroupHom_of_subsingleton_fibre — a vanishing π_N(F) makes p_* injective. The topological instance of LongExactSequence.injective_iff_prev_eq_zero, and the reason one exact spot is already worth something.
    • pi_range_eq_ker — the same, spelled at Fin (n + 1) so that it is visibly about π_{n+1}; pi_eq_homotopyGroup records that Mathlib's HomotopyGroup.Pi is that group definitionally.
    • Prod.homotopyGroupMap_fst_eq_const_iff — the theorem instantiated at a trivial bundle B × F → B, where the lifting hypothesis is discharged outright by Prod.hasHomotopyLiftingPropertyRel_fst. This is what keeps the main theorem from being a statement about the empty set of fibrations.

    What is assumed, and why exactly that #

    The hypothesis is HasHomotopyLiftingPropertyRel p (N → I) (Cube.boundary N): homotopies that do not move on the boundary of the cube lift to homotopies that do not move on the boundary of the cube.

    The plain homotopy lifting property is not enough, and the reason is worth stating because it is the whole difficulty. Lifting a nullhomotopy of p ∘ g does produce a map Iᴺ → E landing in F. But the lifted homotopy is unconstrained on ∂Iᴺ, so the endpoint need not send ∂Iᴺ to the basepoint — it is not an element of Ω^N e at all, and there is no class to exhibit. Pinning the boundary is the content, not the bookkeeping.

    Classically the two properties agree for cubes, via the pair homeomorphism (Iⁿ × I, Iⁿ × {0} ∪ ∂Iⁿ × I) ≅ (Iⁿ × I, Iⁿ × {0}). That homeomorphism is not proved in this development; it is named SerreCubePairBridge in QOU.Mathlib.HomotopyLifting and is used nowhere. So a reader converting a Serre fibration into the hypothesis below still has that step to do.

    What is missing #

    The connecting map δ : π_n(B, b) → π_{n-1}(F, e) and exactness at the two other spots. δ is where the degree shift enters, and Mathlib's GenLoop gives no ready handle on it: it would need the identification of π_n(E, F, e) with π_n(B, b), hence relative homotopy groups, which Mathlib does not have either. Nothing here is a step towards it beyond the lifting predicate, and nothing here should be read as one.

    Consequently this does not yet feed QOU.Mathlib.LongExactSequence: that structure wants R-modules and ℤ-indexed maps exact at every spot, and what is proved here is one spot of a sequence of (generally non-abelian) groups.

        The other half, and the place the fibration hypothesis is spent.

        If p_* ⟦g⟧ is trivial, then g is homotopic rel ∂Iᴺ to a cube that lands in the fibre. Lift the nullhomotopy of p ∘ g; its endpoint lands in F because the homotopy ended at b, and it is based because the lift was chosen not to move on ∂Iᴺ. The lifted homotopy is itself the homotopy exhibiting the two classes as equal — no second construction is needed.

        The hypothesis is not vacuous. For the projection of a trivial bundle the relative lifting property is a theorem, so exactness at the middle spot holds unconditionally there.

        A trivial bundle is the case where nothing needs lifting theory: the lift is written down. Recorded not because the conclusion is interesting there but because it is the witness that the hypothesis of the general theorem is satisfiable at all.
