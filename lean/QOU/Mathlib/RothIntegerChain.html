    Steps 1 → 2 → 3 → 4, with the coefficient rings agreeing throughout.

    Step 1 supplies the polynomial (exists_rothPolySet_auxiliary), step 2 turns the approximation quality into a lower bound on the index at the rational point, step 3 (Roth's Lemma) caps that index from above, and step 4 collides them.

    What is left over is exactly what the earlier stages isolated and nothing new: Γ_m's clauses (hg), the approximation quality (hclose, hT), and the two numeric comparisons (hlog, hgap). Choosing parameters that satisfy them is the remaining work.
