    QOU.Mathlib.HookProductMonoidBridge — the list↔diagram hook bridge, in any commutative monoid #

    HookLengthFormula.hookLengthProduct_eq_prodHooks identifies the geometric cell product ∏_{c∈μ} h(c) with the corpus list product prodHooks μ.rowLens, over ℕ. Its proof is reindexing — prod_biUnion over rows, then columns — and its only content is hookLength_eq_of_mem plus ColLenRowLensBridge.colLen_rowLens, both statements about the hook's argument. So it generalises the same way as the rest of the SYTBeta chain:

    ∏_{c ∈ μ.cells} f (h_μ(c)) = prodHooksF f μ.rowLens.

    This is the encoding bridge the general-r branching route needs: everything proved in SYTBeta is List-encoded, and QBeta.WenzlWeight.schurPrincipal is YoungDiagram-encoded.

    The three reindexing lemmas are re-proved here rather than reused because the corpus versions are private, and row_prod is ℕ-valued. They are left public here — unlike the corpus versions — because the content side (ContentProductRows) needs exactly the same reindexing.

    The third hook notion #

    QBeta.WenzlWeight.hook — the one schurPrincipal is actually defined with — is a third spelling, (rowLen i − j) + (colLen j − i) − 1. On a cell that is exactly hookLength_eq_of_mem's closed form, so the two agree definitionally once that rewrite is made. Recorded because a route that stopped at YoungDiagram.hookLength would still not reach schurPrincipal.
