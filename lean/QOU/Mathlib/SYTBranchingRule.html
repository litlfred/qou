    QOU.Mathlib.SYTBranchingRule — the structural GNW branching count #

    Packaging the deletion/insertion bijection (SYTBranchingBijection) as a cardinality identity: for a non-empty Young diagram μ,

    numStandardYoungTableaux μ = ∑_{removable corners c} numStandardYoungTableaux (μ ∖ c).

    The bijection ⨆_{corners c} SYT(μ ∖ c) → SYT μ, (c, S) ↦ insertMax c S, is shown bijective (surjective by re-inserting the max value; injective because the inserted corner is recovered as the unique max-value cell, then the roundtrip pins S). Equiv.ofBijective + Nat.card_sigma give the sum.

    Pure combinatorics; nothing archimedean; no fit.
