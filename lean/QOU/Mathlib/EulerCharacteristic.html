    QOU.Mathlib.EulerCharacteristic — why Δ_λ is proved and its magnitude is not #

    Bean qou-fngs, LES/spectral arc. PagedSpectralSequence.lean made collapse mean something. This file proves the one thing a spectral sequence gives you without collapse, and thereby explains a standing asymmetry in the corpus.

    The asymmetry #

    Δ_λ = (−1)^{n(λ)} · dim V_λ is settled, while the magnitude it is built from — the r* ≈ 1.585 scaling of dim V_λ itself — is open. That is not an accident of effort. It is the difference between an Euler characteristic and a graded piece, and this file is that difference stated as two theorems about the same object:

    • eulerChar_bump_eq — the alternating sum of page ranks over a window is unchanged by page-turning. No collapse hypothesis, no filtration analysis; the correction terms cancel against each other.
    • finrank_bump_eq_iff — an individual page rank is unchanged exactly when the differentials into and out of that cell both vanish.

    So an index of Δ_λ's shape is page-independent for free, and can therefore be computed on whichever page is accessible; a single dim V_λ is page-independent only under a vanishing statement nobody has, which is precisely the missing collapse. Δ_λ being available while dim V_λ is not is the expected situation, not a gap in the argument for Δ_λ.

    The mechanism #

    Total degree p + q grades each page, and d_r : E_r^{p,q} → E_r^{p+r,q-r+1} raises it by exactly one (deg_next) — so every page is a cochain complex in this grading and page-turning is passage to cohomology. Rank–nullity plus quotient additivity give the local accounting (finrank_bump_add):

    rk E_{r+1}^c + rk (im d_c) + rk (im d_{prev c}) = rk E_r^c

    The two correction terms sit in adjacent total degrees, so under the sign (−1)^{p+q} they enter with opposite signs. Reindexing the second along the bijection prev (sum_sgn_imRank_prev) turns it into minus the first, and they cancel. That cancellation is the whole of Euler–Poincaré.

    Why a field, and why that is not a §7c violation #

    Ranks are Module.finrank over a field K with finite-dimensional pages. That is a genuine restriction — R_q = ℤ[q, q⁻¹] is not a field and its modules have no such dimension — and it is deliberate rather than sloppy: the statement being explained, Δ_λ = (−1)^{n(λ)} dim V_λ, is a dimension of a representation, so a field is where it lives. K is an arbitrary field, not ℝ, so nothing archimedean is smuggled in.

    What this does not do #

    It does not iterate to E_∞. Iterating needs the support hypothesis at every page, which is the boundedness condition of a convergent spectral sequence; supplying that for the Gröbner filtration is the same missing work BarGradedCollapse leaves open. One step is proved here, and one step is what the asymmetry above needs.

            Euler characteristic is page-independent.

            The alternating sum of page ranks over a window is unchanged by turning the page — with no collapse hypothesis. The window hypothesis hsupp is about where the differential is nonzero, not about it vanishing.

            This is the other half of the asymmetry. An index of the shape Δ_λ = (−1)^{n(λ)} dim V_λ is a term of such a sum, so it can be evaluated on whichever page is accessible; the individual dim V_λ cannot (finrank_bump_eq_iff).
