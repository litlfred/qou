    QOU.Mathlib.SchenstedRSKBurnside — ∑_{λ ⊢ n} (#SYT λ)² = n! for every n #

    Brick 42 of epic qou-nrex (RSK inverse), and the end of the epic.

    rskBurnsideHolds — Identity-I axiom (i), for general n, from the correspondence rather than from enumeration. Until now it was proved only for n ≤ 12, by rskBurnsideHolds_le12, a decide-verified route with no RSK in it.

    The three steps are all cardinality algebra; the mathematics is behind them:

    • Nat.card (Equiv.Perm (Fin n)) = n!;
    • the correspondence (brick 40) matches permutations with pairs of standard tableaux on a diagram of n cells;
    • card_pairs_eq_sum decomposes that subtype — Equiv.subtypeSigmaEquiv turns a subtype of a Σ into a Σ over a subtype, brick 41 re-indexes it by Nat.Partition n, and Nat.card_sigma splits the sum. Each fibre is SYT(λ) × SYT(λ), whose count is dimSYT².

    The finiteness Nat.card_sigma needs is already in place: Nat.Partition n is a Fintype, and StandardYoungTableau μ carries the Finite instance its own file proves by encoding entries on the cells.

    What this closes #

    RSKBurnsideHolds n was stated in RSKBurnsideTarget as a well-typed Prop awaiting exactly this proof, with the comment "General n still awaits RSK". It no longer does. rskBurnside_imp_substrate therefore discharges RSKBridge.RSK_substrate_axiom n for every n, and SpechtWedderburnCriterion.spechtEquiv_of_sum_sq converts the identity into the general Wedderburn split.

    Pure combinatorics on ℕ; nothing archimedean; no fit.
