    QOU.Mathlib.SYTVandermondeKeystone — the divided-difference keystone via det_vandermonde #

    The β-Lagrange identity behind the general SYT hook-length formula (obligation (A), bean sy7b) rests on the divided-difference leading-coefficient keystone

    ∑ i, f.eval (β i) / vWeight β i  =  f.coeff (k − 1)      (`f.natDegree < k`)

    where vWeight β i = ∏_{j ≠ i} (β i − β j). This file proves it the determinant way — by Laplace-expanding Matrix.det_vandermonde — rather than via Lagrange interpolation (owner preference: exact determinant algebra over interpolation; and det_vandermonde is in the build cache while Mathlib.LinearAlgebra.Lagrange is not). This is the drop-in replacement for the hand-rolled SYTLagrangeInterp.interp_leadingCoeff (coordinated on PR #2986).

    Proof skeleton #

    Let V = vandermonde β (V i j = β i ^ j), cofactor i = (−1)^(i+(k−1)) · det(V.submatrix i.succAbove (Fin.last).succAbove). Laplace along the last column (det_succ_column) gives, for the matrix A_d = V with the last column replaced by β i ^ d,

    det A_d = ∑ i, cofactor i · (β i)^d.
    • d < k−1: A_d has two equal columns (d and k−1) ⇒ det A_d = 0.
    • d = k−1: A_d = V ⇒ det A_d = det V.

    The crux (cofactor_mul_vWeight): cofactor i · vWeight β i = det V, from det_vandermonde on V and on the sub-vandermonde V.submatrix i.succAbove (Fin.last).succAbove = vandermonde (β ∘ i.succAbove), with the sign (−1)^(i+k−1) = (−1)^(k−1−i). Then for injective β (so det V ≠ 0):

    ∑ i, (β i)^d / vWeight β i  =  (∑ i, cofactor i (β i)^d) / det V  =  det A_d / det V  =  [d = k−1].

    Linearity over the coefficients of f lifts the power-sum form to the polynomial keystone.

    Pure exact ℚ/CommRing determinant algebra; no interpolation, no ℝ.

          The crux: the Vandermonde cofactor product-split #

          cofᵢ · vWeightᵢ = det V, proved by splitting det_vandermonde's pair product at the pivot row i via Fin.prod_univ_succAbove. The four helper lemmas below carry the reindexing; the crux assembles them.

          theorem QOU.Mathlib.SYTVandermonde.pivot_contribution {k : ℕ} (β : Fin (k + 1) → ℚ) (i : Fin (k + 1)) :
          ((∏ q ∈ Finset.Ioi i, (β q - β i)) * ∏ a : Fin k, if i.succAbove a < i then β i - β (i.succAbove a) else 1) = (-1) ^ (k - ↑i) * vWeight β i

          The pivot-row contribution: (∏_{q>i}(β q − β i)) · (∏_{a : σa<i}(β i − β σa)) = (−1)^(k−i) · vWeightᵢ. The above-pivot product flips sign on each of its k − i factors, and the two half-products reassemble vWeightᵢ = ∏_{j≠i}(β i − β j).

          theorem QOU.Mathlib.SYTVandermonde.cof_mul_vWeight {k : ℕ} (β : Fin (k + 1) → ℚ) (i : Fin (k + 1)) :
          cof β i * vWeight β i = (Matrix.vandermonde β).det

          The Vandermonde cofactor product-split (crux). cofᵢ · vWeightᵢ = det V.

          The cofactor along the last column is (−1)^(i+k) · det(V.submatrix iᵒ lastᵒ), and that submatrix is vandermonde (β ∘ i.succAbove) (nodes with βᵢ deleted). Combine with det_vandermonde_pivot_split; the signs match because (−1)^(i+k) = (−1)^(k−i) (i ≤ k).

          This is the heart of the determinant route; it replaces the sibling's hand-rolled SYTLagrangeInterp.interp_leadingCoeff (PR #2986).
