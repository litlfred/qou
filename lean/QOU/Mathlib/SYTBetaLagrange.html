    QOU.Mathlib.SYTBetaLagrange — the β-Lagrange identity #

    The Greene–Nijenhuis–Wilf hook-summation identity reduces, in β-number coordinates, to the self-contained ℚ β-Lagrange identity

    ∑ᵢ βᵢ · ∏_{j≠i} (βᵢ − 1 − βⱼ)/(βᵢ − βⱼ)  =  (∑ᵢ βᵢ) − C(k,2).

    This file assembles it on the leading-coefficient keystone (SYTVandermonde.interp_leadingCoeff, the Matrix.det_vandermonde route — owner-preferred over polynomial interpolation).

    Route. Let Q(X) = ∏ⱼ (X − βⱼ) (vanishes at every node βᵢ, since the j=i factor is 0) and G(X) = X · ∏ⱼ (X − (βⱼ+1)), so G(βᵢ) = βᵢ ∏ⱼ(βᵢ − 1 − βⱼ). Removing the j=i factor flips a sign, ∏_{j≠i}(βᵢ−1−βⱼ) = −∏ⱼ(βᵢ−1−βⱼ), so the left side is −∑ᵢ G(βᵢ)/Dᵢ with Dᵢ = ∏_{j≠i}(βᵢ−βⱼ). Writing G = R + (X − C k)·Q with deg R < k (the top two coefficients cancel by Vieta), the keystone gives ∑ᵢ G(βᵢ)/Dᵢ = R.coeff (k−1) = C(k,2) − ∑βᵢ, whence the identity.

    Pure ℚ polynomial algebra; nothing archimedean; no fit.
