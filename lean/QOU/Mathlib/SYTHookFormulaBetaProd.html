    QOU.Mathlib.SYTHookFormulaBetaProd — wiring the per-row beta product into the corpus prodHooks #

    SYTHookFormulaBeta proved, self-contained, the per-row Frame–Robinson–Thrall hook product rowHookProd a t · betaGapProd a t = (a + |t|)!. This file connects rowHookProd to the corpus QOU.BraidKnot.IdentityI.prodHooks and assembles the full divisor-free Frame–Robinson–Thrall hook product:

    prodHooks l · (∏_i betaGapProd (l[i]) (l.drop (i+1))) = ∏_i (l[i] + (l.drop (i+1)).length)!
                                                      = ∏_i β_i!

    (β_i = l[i] + (k−1−i)), i.e. ∏_c h_λ(c) = (∏_i β_i!) / ∏_{i<j}(β_i − β_j) cleared of the Vandermonde denominator. This is the hook-side identity (i) of the beta-number route to the general HLF, sorry-free for every weakly-decreasing positive partition.

    Pure ℕ combinatorics; nothing archimedean; no fit.
