    QOU.Mathlib.YoungDiagramEraseCornerSurjective — every diagram is an erased corner #

    Bean qou-ejde records, as the one gap in its route to heckeIncl injectivity:

    Step 3 quantifies over partitions of m+1 obtained as eraseCorner of a partition of m+2. That IS all of them (add a corner cell to any partition of m+1), but the surjectivity of eraseCorner onto partitions of m+1 must be STATED and proved, not assumed.

    This file states and proves it. Two lines of new mathematics, both of which reduce to pieces already on main:
    isAddableCell_rowLen_zero the cell just past the end of row 0 is addable to any diagram
    exists_eraseCorner_eq every diagram is μ ∖ c for some μ and removable corner c of it

    Why row 0 and not a new bottom row #

    addable_iff characterises an addable cell as one sitting just past the end of its row, in a row that is either the first or strictly shorter than the row above. At i = 0 the second conjunct is 0 = 0, discharged by rfl — so row 0 needs no side condition at all, whereas a new bottom row would need rowLen strict-decrease bookkeeping. The empty diagram is covered without a case split: rowLen 0 = 0 there, and (0, 0) is addable to ⊥.

    What this does NOT claim #

    Only that some (μ, c) erases to ν — not that it is unique (it is not; a diagram generally has several addable cells), and nothing about cardinalities beyond what card_eraseCorner already gives. cornerAddEquiv in YoungDiagramAddCell is the sharper statement, a bijection of pairs; this file is the bare surjectivity, which is what a ∀ ν quantifier needs to be discharged by subst.

    Nothing archimedean; no ring; no fit.

    -- Ref: [manuscript] bean qou-ejde, step 3's stated gap.

    theorem QOU.Mathlib.exists_eraseCorner_eq (ν : YoungDiagram) :
    ∃ (μ : YoungDiagram) (c : ℕ × ℕ) (hc : IsCornerCell μ c), eraseCorner μ c hc = ν

    Corner removal is surjective. Every Young diagram ν is obtained from some strictly larger diagram by deleting a removable corner — take ν with the cell past the end of its first row adjoined. Together with card_eraseCorner this is what lets a ∀ ν statement about diagrams of size m+1 be proved by working upstairs at m+2.
