    Cells-content bridge for row-length Young diagrams #

    Brick 9b crux. To realise the recording tableau as a QOU.Mathlib.StandardYoungTableau we must discharge its bijOn obligation — the entry map bijects the diagram cells onto {1, …, n}. The single fact all four BijOn components (card, InjOn, MapsTo, SurjOn) reduce to is:

    (ofRowLens (tableauShape Q) _).cells.val.map (fun c => (Q.getD c.1 []).getD c.2 0)
  = content Q

    i.e. the multiset of entries read off the cells of the row-length diagram equals the flattened list content. The proof decomposes μ.cells by rows (cells_biUnion, built from Finset.ext + mem_row_iff), rewrites each row as a product (row_eq_prod), and matches it to the list content (map_getD_range, content_eq_sum_range).

    Supporting general-purpose lemmas about YoungDiagram.ofRowLens (rowLen_getD, cells_biUnion, rows_pairwiseDisjoint) are stated for an arbitrary sorted row-length list, independent of the RSK development.
