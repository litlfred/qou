    Standard-labelling shift of the recording tableau #

    Brick 9a. QOU's StandardYoungTableau μ ties its bijOn obligation to toSsyt.entry mapping the cells onto Set.Icc 1 μ.cells.card — a 1-based labelling. The recording tableau Q(w) produced by insertWordRec is 0-based (its timestamps are 0, …, |w|-1, Brick 8). A naive "+1 to every entry" breaks the SSYT zeros' obligation off the shape (0 + 1 ≠ 0), so the relabelling is applied to the underlying list tableau Q̃ = shiftT Q = Q.map (·.map (·+1)), whose out-of-shape lookups are still 0.

    This file supplies the shift-invariance the realisation needs:

    • shiftT_shape — the shape is unchanged;
    • shiftT_rowsSorted / shiftT_colStrict — the SSYT order invariants survive the shift (+1 is strictly monotone);
    • content_shiftT — content commutes with the shift, so the shifted recording content is {1, …, |w|} (content_shiftT_recording), still Nodup;
    • recordingSSYT — the shifted recording tableau Q̃(w) as a Mathlib SemistandardYoungTableau, its entries 1, …, |w|.

    The remaining bijOn bridge (Q̃'s entry map bijects the cells onto Icc 1 |w|), yielding insertWordSYT w : StandardYoungTableau _, is Brick 9b.

    Pure combinatorics on ℕ; nothing archimedean.
