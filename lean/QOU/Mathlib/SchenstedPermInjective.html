    QOU.Mathlib.SchenstedPermInjective — RSK separates permutations (brick 8) #

    Brick 7 proved the RSK map injective on words of a fixed length. A permutation reaches that statement through wordOfPerm, so this file supplies the missing half — wordOfPerm is itself injective, and it has the length the brick-7 hypothesis wants — and composes the two:

    distinct permutations of Fin n have distinct (P, Q) pairs.

    That is the injectivity half of the Robinson–Schensted correspondence, at the raw List (List ℕ) level where the combinatorics lives.

    Why this rather than the typed rskPair #

    SchenstedPairingMap.rskPair is typed in StandardYoungTableau, built from the raw tableaux by listStandardYT. Lifting brick 7 through that wrapper needs a typed→raw retraction, which the corpus does not currently carry; and the content would be unchanged, since the wrapper is a repackaging of the same data. So the honest statement to prove now is the raw one, and the typed corollary can follow once a retraction exists. Nothing here claims rskPair itself is injective.

    Scope #

    Injectivity only. Surjectivity — the genuinely remaining half — and the cardinality identity RSKBurnsideHolds are the rest of epic qou-nrex.

    Pure combinatorics on ℕ; nothing archimedean.
