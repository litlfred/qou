    QOU.Mathlib.ConcentratedColumnCollapse — the collapse the mass formula actually uses #

    Bean qou-fngs. QOU/Mathlib/SpectralCollapseCriteria.lean proves that bounded support forces collapse, and legacyOfPaged turns a collapsing PagedSS into the [GBFiltrationCollapsesAtE2 R] hypothesis downstream theorems take. Neither had ever been applied: measured on origin/main, every occurrence of PagedSS in the corpus is a hypothesis S : PagedSS … and not one declaration constructs one, so the only discharge of GBFiltrationCollapsesAtE2 remained canonicalTrivial, whose witness is PUnit.

    This module supplies the missing side: the page family the corpus's own mass-formula proof describes, and the discharge that follows from it.

    What prop:phi-q-categorical-truncation asks for #

    Step 1 of that proof (mass-theory/phi-q-categorical-truncation-proof.md) reads

    E_∞^{p,*} = Z(A) = ⊕_{λ ⊢ n} R · e_λ   for p = 0,
            0                            for p ≥ 1.

    That is concentration in the p = 0 column, and it is a statement about the filtration degree p, not about the page index r. So it is exactly the N = 0 case of collapses_of_bounded_support, and collapses_two_of_bounded_support already covers N ≤ 1. The E₂ collapse the mass formula needs is therefore derivable from the concentration hypothesis it already states, for any differential the family admits.

    The k in k⋆ is a different filtration, and this is worth stating plainly #

    The bean records the conclusion "the mass formula needs the specific k⋆, so it needs E₂, not merely 'some page'", with E₂ said to be "what fixes k⋆ to be the max GB-depth rather than something larger". That reads the two indices as one, and they are not:

    • p is the Hochschild / filtration degree — the column index of the spectral sequence, and the index CellIndex.p carries here. Collapse is a statement about differentials between columns.
    • k is the GB-depth of a partition, which Step 3 uses to stratify Z(A) = ⊕_{k = 0}^{k⋆} Z^{(k)}(A). That is a decomposition inside the single surviving column E_∞^{0,*}; it indexes no page and no column.

    k⋆ is finite for the reason Step 3 gives — k⋆ := max_{λ ∈ supp(T_w)} depth_GB(λ) is a maximum over a finite support — and the spectral sequence plays no part in fixing it. So collapse at E₂ neither implies nor is implied by the value of k⋆; what collapse buys the proof is Step 1's E_∞^{p,*} = 0 for p ≥ 1, and nothing more. Keeping the two apart is what makes the E₂ claim reachable at all: as a statement about p it is the N = 0 window, which is provable.

    What is still assumed, and it is the whole of it #

    The concentration itself. Nothing here shows that the Gröbner-filtration spectral sequence of H_n(R, q) is concentrated in the p = 0 column; that is the content of S-GB-spectral-collapse and it remains an assertion. What is no longer assumed is the step from concentration to collapse, and the discharge below takes the real page family rather than PUnit.

          Inhabitation, as a theorem rather than a named model #

          `QOU/Mathlib/PagedSpectralSequence.lean` declines to ship a trivial witness, and
gives the reason: a named trivial model is a thing downstream code reaches for,
and in `QOU/Mathlib/SpectralSequence.lean` one was reached for and then cited as
though the substrate axiom had been discharged.

The same reasoning applies here, but the opposite question also needs an answer:
a structure nothing can inhabit makes every theorem about it vacuous, and that is
precisely the defect this module was written to measure.  So inhabitation is
recorded as an existence **theorem** — enough to show the tower is not empty,
with no name for a downstream citation to point at.
