    The RSK pairing map Sym n → Σ_λ SYT(λ)² #

    Brick 11. Packages the two standard tableaux of a permutation word — the insertion tableau P(w) (insertWordPSYT, Brick 10) and the recording tableau Q̃(w) (insertWordSYT, Brick 9b) — into a single dependent pair living in

    Σ μ : YoungDiagram, StandardYoungTableau μ × StandardYoungTableau μ,

    the target Σ_λ SYT(λ)² of the Robinson–Schensted correspondence. The pairing is well-typed because P(w) and Q̃(w) share a Young diagram, not merely a row-length list: recordingShape_eq_pShape lifts the row-length agreement insertWordRec_shape_eq_P (through shiftT_shape) to an equality of YoungDiagrams, so the recording tableau casts onto P(w)'s shape.

    Two entry points:

    • rskPairWord — for a word w whose content is {1,…,n} (a permutation word);
    • rskPair — for a permutation σ : Equiv.Perm (Fin n), via wordOfPerm.

    This is the last structural brick before the RSK inverse (bumping reversal) and the cardinality identity Σ_{λ⊢n} (f^λ)² = n! (RSKBurnsideHolds n) it feeds.

    Pure combinatorics on ℕ; nothing archimedean.
