    The translate matrix is small when the translation is. f(binom(n,i)·γ^{n−i}) ≤ 2^{|n|}·G^{|n|−|i|} for f ∘ γ ≤ G ≤ 1 — the same peeling as seminorm_translateMatrix_le, but tracking the power of γ that the entry actually carries instead of discarding it.

    That difference is the whole of step 2: γ = β − α is tiny, and a bound of the form (2G)^{|n|} would be true and useless.
