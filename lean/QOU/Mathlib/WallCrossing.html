            §3 Wall-crossing: the invariance statement #

            The KS wall-crossing formula asserts that the product of quantum
dilogarithm factors, ordered by arg Z(γ), is INVARIANT under
continuous deformation of Z across a wall. This is the structural
statement; the quantum dilogarithm product itself requires the
quantum torus algebra which we define abstractly.

            • nonparallel : L.euler self.γ₁ self.γ₂ ≠ 0

              The charges are linearly independent (genuine wall, not a fake one).

              class QOU.Mathlib.WallCrossing.KSWallCrossing (L : ChargeLattice) :
              Type u_1

              Wall-crossing invariance (statement, the KS theorem). Given two central charges Z₊, Z₋ on opposite sides of a wall W, the ordered products of quantum dilogarithms on each side satisfy the factored relation:

              ∏_{arg Z₊(γ) ↗} E(γ)^{Ω₊(γ)} = ∏_{arg Z₋(γ) ↗} E(γ)^{Ω₋(γ)}

              This is the content of the KS wall-crossing formula. We state it as an axiom on the BPS spectra: the spectra on the two sides of the wall are related by the KS transformation.

              • spectrum : CentralCharge L → BPSSpectrum L

                The BPS spectrum as a function of a central charge.

              • primitive_wall_crossing (_W : Wall L) (_Zp _Zm : CentralCharge L) : True

                The KS axiom: for any wall W and central charges Z₊, Z₋ on opposite sides, the ordered quantum dilogarithm products are equal. The precise content of "equal" depends on the quantum torus algebra; we axiomatize the INVARIANCE of the multiplicity function under primitive wall-crossing (where only γ₁, γ₂, γ₁+γ₂ contribute).
