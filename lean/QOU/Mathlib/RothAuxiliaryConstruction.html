      A solution of the system is an auxiliary polynomial.

      Everything the construction has to deliver, from a coefficient vector that (a) is not identically zero on the box and (b) kills every vanishing condition of weight below μ. Siegel's lemma supplies exactly such a vector; this theorem is what turns it into a polynomial.
