    theorem QOU.Mathlib.Borel.laplace_pow_eq_factorial (n : ℕ) :
    ∫ (t : ℝ) in Set.Ioi 0, Real.exp (-t) * t ^ n = ↑n.factorial

    The Laplace term-wise engine: ∫₀^∞ e^{−t} tⁿ dt = n!. Euler's Gamma integral at the natural argument s = n + 1, with Real.rpow collapsed to the monoid power on t. This is the single analytic fact underneath Borel resummation — every n! that Borel division removed is restored by this Laplace integral.

    theorem QOU.Mathlib.Borel.laplace_borel_term (a : ℕ → ℝ) (z : ℝ) (n : ℕ) :
    ∫ (t : ℝ) in Set.Ioi 0, Real.exp (-t) * (borelCoeff a n * (z * t) ^ n) = a n * z ^ n

    Per-term Borel recovery: the n-th term of the Borel transform, (aₙ/n!)(z t)ⁿ, Laplace-integrates back to the n-th term aₙ zⁿ of the original series. The Borel division by n! and the Laplace multiplication by n! (from laplace_pow_eq_factorial) cancel exactly — the mechanism of Borel resummation, term by term.

    The ∑ ↔ ∫ interchange — Borel-summation REGULARITY on the disc of convergence #

    The full resummation ∫₀^∞ e^{−t} φ(z t) dt = Σ aₙ zⁿ (φ = Borel transform) holds by exchanging Σ and ∫₀^∞. Contrary to a naive "Gevrey-1 dominating bound" reading, that interchange does NOT hold for a divergent Gevrey-1 series: there ∑ₙ ∫₀^∞ |e^{−t}(aₙ/n!)(z t)ⁿ| dt = ∑ₙ |aₙ||z|ⁿ = ∞, which is precisely why Borel summation of a divergent series is nontrivial (the Borel-plane singularity on the ray is the alien-calculus/Stokes obstruction — Watson / Nevanlinna–Sokal territory, still the open el8w gap).

    The interchange DOES hold on the disc of convergence: when ∑ₙ |aₙ||z|ⁿ < ∞ the dominating series is finite, MeasureTheory.integral_tsum applies, and each term Laplace-integrates back by laplace_borel_term. The result is the regularity (consistency) property: where the series already converges, Borel summation reproduces its ordinary sum. This is the singularity-free base layer of resurgence — proved sorry-free below.
