    QOU.Mathlib.LatticeGaugeShadow — the algebraic core of the lattice-QCD-from-QOU shadow #

    This file formalises the structural / categorical core of the "lattice QCD as the q → 1 shadow of the q-deformed surface state-sum" correspondence recorded narratively in the content block rem:lattice-qcd-from-qou (folio/quantum-observable-universe/brings-surface/lattice-qcd-from-qou.md, PR #4622).

    Scope discipline (STRICT — read first) #

    The physical / numerical correspondence — "the q-deformed surface state-sum reproduces lattice-gauge results", any numerical agreement — is evidence-tier only (owner ruling): it is an empirical cross-check of the same evidential tier as agreement with an experimental datum, strictly weaker than a formal derivation, and it is NOT proved here (or anywhere in Lean). The remark itself states: "This correspondence is a structural observation, not a theorem. … A proof of the state-sum / lattice-gauge correspondence remains open."

    What is provable, and is proved sorry-free below, is the pure algebra that underlies the structural observation. Three targets:

    1. Null scalar channel (CyclicCharacter.annihilates_orderingDefect, matrix_trace_annihilates_orderingDefect). The surface-ordering correction of two cell holonomies is the ordering defect A·B − B·A (identical to the q-curvature transport defect F_q of QOU.LocalQ.ObservationParallelogram.Fq). A block-trace character χ^λ — a cyclic additive functional — annihilates it: χ(A·B − B·A) = 0. This is the rigorous version of the σ₁³-agent's numerical tr[A,B] = 0 observation. The concrete matrix trace realises the abstract cyclic character.

    2. q → 1 degeneration → Wilson plaquette product (qSurfaceOrderedProduct_at_one, qCellMul_sub_classical). The q-deformed surface-ordered cell product carries the surface-ordering (non-abelian) correction, weighted by (q − 1); at q = 1 the correction switches off and the ordered product degenerates to the ordinary (Wilson-action) plaquette product ∏ U_p.

    3. Refinement invariance of the surface state-sum, single move (wilsonPlaquetteProduct_refine_invariant, holonomy_R2_move_invariant, qCellMul_associator_at_one_eq_zero). The classical (undeformed / q = 1) state-sum is invariant under the two elementary refinement moves — the 1 → 2 cell subdivision and the Reidemeister-II cancelling-pair insertion — because the ordered product is an associative monoid product. The obstruction to invariance is the associator of the q-deformed cell product, which is proved to vanish at q = 1. Away from q = 1 the associator is the pentagon / Pachner-2–3 obstruction, which is open and carried by the existing conjecture-class QOU.BraidsAndKnots.PachnerPentagonBorromean (conj:pachner-pentagon-borromean); the general q-deformed triangulation-invariance is beaned, not claimed.

    Base ring (§7c) #

    Everything here is pure algebra over a generic noncommutative Ring R (holonomies) with a value ring / group for the character. No ℝ, no ordering, no Real.*, no archimedean construct — the substrate→archimedean wall is not crossed.

    Ref: [manuscript] rem:lattice-qcd-from-qou, conj:pachner-pentagon-borromean, def:q-flat-connection, rem:two-observer-parallelogram

      Type (max u_3 u_4)

      A block-trace character χ^λ: a cyclic additive functional on the holonomy ring. The two fields are the genuine defining properties of a class function / trace: additivity (toHom : R →+ S) and cyclicity (χ(a·b) = χ(b·a)). The concrete matrix trace Matrix.trace is such a character (see matrix_trace_annihilates_orderingDefect), so the notion is inhabited by a real object, not a stub.
