    QOU.Mathlib.SchenstedRecordingOfSYT — a standard Young tableau as a recording tableau #

    Brick 34 of epic qou-nrex (RSK inverse).

    Brick 33 read a standard Young tableau off its cells as a list tableau and proved every structural invariant brick 32 asks of Q — except the one that is not structural: content. Brick 32's Q holds the timestamps 0, …, n-1, because insertWordRec starts counting at 0; a StandardYoungTableau holds 1, …, |μ|, because its bijection is onto Set.Icc 1 |μ|. The two conventions differ by one, and insertWordSYT already pays that difference in the other direction via shiftT.

    • mem_flatten_toListTableau — the entries are the image of the cells;
    • mem_flatten_toListTableau_iff_Icc — hence exactly 1, …, |μ|, from bijOn (mapsTo one way, surjOn the other);
    • unshiftT — the down-shift, with the structural invariants transported across it. It is the inverse of the existing shiftT on a tableau whose entries are positive, which is exactly what the content fact supplies.
    • toRecordingTableau and its bundle — the 0-based tableau, with every hypothesis brick 32 takes: column-strict, rows strictly increasing, entries distinct, no empty rows, shape μ.rowLens, and content {0, …, |μ|-1}.

    Scope #

    The Q argument of brick 32, assembled. It does not yet run brick 32 on it: that also needs the P side (hshape : tableauShape P = addCellSteps [] (qPositions Q n), i.e. that the two tableaux of the pair share a shape) and, for rskPair proper, the round trip listStandardYT (toListTableau S) = S in the other direction. Neither is claimed here, rskPair bijectivity is not claimed, and RSKBurnsideHolds for general n remains open.

    Pure combinatorics on ℕ; nothing archimedean; no fit.
