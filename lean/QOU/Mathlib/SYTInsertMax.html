    QOU.Mathlib.SYTInsertMax — re-inserting the max value at a corner (inverse branching map) #

    The inverse half of the Greene–Nijenhuis–Wilf branching bijection: given a removable corner c of μ and a standard tableau S of μ ∖ c, put the top value |μ| back into cell c to recover a standard tableau of μ. Strict rows/columns hold because |μ| exceeds every value of S, and c has no cell to its right or below (it is a corner); the entry bijection lands on {1, …, |μ|} since S already bijects μ ∖ c onto {1, …, |μ|−1} and c ↦ |μ|.

    Pure combinatorics; nothing archimedean; no fit.
