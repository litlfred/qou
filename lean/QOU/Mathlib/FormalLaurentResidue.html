    Formal Laurent residues: the residue of an exact form vanishes #

    Library home of the invariant behind prop:sw-classical-jet-exactness (the content-block sibling brings-surface/sw-classical-jet-exactness.lean carries a self-contained copy per the sibling convention): a local Laurent expansion is modelled by its coefficient function f : ℤ → R, the residue is the coefficient of t⁻¹, and the termwise derivative shifts every exponent down by one — so the residue of a derivative is ((−1)+1)·f(0) = 0. Iterating gives the jet form: every positive-order classical jet is exact, hence residue-free at every point, hence period-free on every closed cycle (the residue-sum shape of prop:sw-period-residue-sum).

    The model is deliberately coefficient-level: it does not encode convergence or the curve, only the algebraic identity that kills the residues — which is the load-bearing (b)-invariant. Everything is over a generic CommRing R (§7c wall-safe; no archimedean content).

    Main results #

    • fresidue_fderiv — the residue of a derivative vanishes.
    • fresidue_jet_succ — every positive-order jet has vanishing residue.
    • fderiv_add, fderiv_smul, fresidue_add, fresidue_sum — (bi)linearity, so the vanishing extends to finite residue sums of exact families (fresidue_sum_jet_succ).
