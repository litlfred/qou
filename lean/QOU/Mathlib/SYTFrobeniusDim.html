    QOU.Mathlib.SYTFrobeniusDim — the manifestly-integer (Frobenius/Aitken) form of dimHLF #

    The two-row hook dimension is the ballot number C(a+b,b) − C(a+b,b−1) (SYTDimHLFPair.dimHLF_pair), a manifest ℤ with no floor-division — that is what made the two-row corner-removal recursion (A) provable by Pascal's rule. For m ≥ 3 there is no single-binomial closed form; the manifestly- integer generalisation is the Frobenius/Aitken determinant in signed-multinomial form:

    dimHLF λ  =  Σ_{σ ∈ S_m}  sgn(σ) · multinomial(n ; (λ_i − i + σ(i))_i)

    (n!·det(1/(λ_i − i + j)!) with the determinant expanded over S_m). Each term is an integer multinomial — the indices sum to n since Σ(λ_i − i + σ(i)) = Σλ_i = n — and terms with a negative index vanish. Integrality of #SYT is therefore global (the signed sum), not entry-wise.

    This file is step 2a of closing the general-n hook-length formula (bean sy7b): it pins the formula in Lean and decide-verifies dimHLF = frobeniusDim for n ≤ 5 — the regression backstop for the general identity dimHLF = frobeniusDim (step 2b, via Matrix.det_vandermonde + the sorry-free hook side). The two-row specialisation is exactly C(n,b) − C(n,b−1); (3,2,1) gives 60 − 20 − 30 + 6 = 16. Pure ℕ/ℤ combinatorics; nothing archimedean.
