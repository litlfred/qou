    Content of the recording tableau — the RSK timestamps are 0, 1, …, |w|-1 #

    Brick 8 keystone. The recording tableau Q(w) = (insertWordRec w).2 fills its cells with the step indices 0, 1, …, |w|-1 at which each box was added. This file proves that its multiset content is exactly List.range |w| — every timestamp occurs exactly once — and derives the Nodup consequence.

    This is the combinatorial core the standard-tableau bijOn obligation rests on: distinctness of the entries (injectivity of the filling) is Nodup, and coverage of {0, …, |w|-1} is the range identity. Both fall out of the single branch lemma

    content (insertRec P Q x s).2 = content Q + {s}      (shape-aligned P, Q),

    which says each insertion step contributes precisely its own index s to the recording content, in every branch (new row, no-bump append, or bump-recursion).

    Pure combinatorics on ℕ; nothing archimedean.
