    Watson uniqueness on the sector-at-0 (final wiring). If f and g on the sector {z ≠ 0 : |arg z| < π/(2t)} are the pullbacks f = F ∘ Φ_t, g = G ∘ Φ_t of half-plane functions F, G that agree on {re > 0}, then f = g on the sector. The carrier Φ_t(z) = (z^t)⁻¹ sends the sector into {re > 0} (sectorTransport_mapsTo), so each sector point's value is an agreed half-plane value. Supply hFG from watson_uniqueness_rhp_open to obtain the full sector-at-0 Watson uniqueness.
