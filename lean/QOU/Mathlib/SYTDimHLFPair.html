    QOU.Mathlib.SYTDimHLFPair — the two-row hook-length dimension is the ballot number #

    dimHLF is the Frame–Robinson–Thrall dimension (∑l)! / ∏h_c as a floor ℕ-division, so it cannot be manipulated algebraically until its exactness is pinned. This file pins it on two rows:

    dimHLF [a, b]  =  C(a+b, b) − C(a+b, b−1)          (`dimHLF_pair`,  a ≥ b ≥ 1)

    the classical ballot number — a manifest ℕ (a difference of binomial coefficients), with no division. This is the integrality infrastructure the two-row case of the corner-removal recursion (A) dimHLF_branching needs: once the dimension is a binomial difference, the branching dimHLF[a,b] = dimHLF[a−1,b] + dimHLF[a,b−1] is just Pascal's rule (no ℕ-division to fight).

    Proof route: from the two-row hook product prodHooks[a,b]·(a+1−b)! = (a+1)!·(a−b)!·b! (SYTHookRows.prodHooks_pair_mul) and Nat.choose_mul_factorial_mul_factorial, show (C(a+b,b) − C(a+b,b−1)) · prodHooks[a,b] = (a+b)!, then read off the floor-division via Nat.div_eq_of_eq_mul_left. Pure ℕ; nothing archimedean.
