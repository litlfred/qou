    QOU.Mathlib.RSSYTSchur — reverse tableaux with bounded entries are counted by schur #

    For a Young diagram μ with at most N rows, the weighted sum over reverse semistandard tableaux with entries in {0, …, N − 1}

    Σ_T ∏_{c ∈ μ} x_{T(c)}

    equals Bialternant.schur N ν x with ν_j = μ.rowLen (N − 1 − j) (the row lengths, smallest first). The proof peels off the cells holding 0: they form a horizontal strip at the outer boundary, and removing them and lowering the remaining entries by one gives a tableau of the smaller shape with entries below N − 1. That is exactly the branching recursion schur is defined by.
