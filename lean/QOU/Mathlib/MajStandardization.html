    QOU.Mathlib.MajStandardization — Stanley's standardization bijection #

    First half of the maj / fake-degree formula Σ_{S ∈ SYT(λ)} q^{maj S} = q^{n(λ)} [n]_q! / ∏_{x ∈ λ} [h(x)]_q (bean qou-q3q2).

    A reverse semistandard tableau of shape μ is a filling by naturals with rows weakly decreasing and columns strictly decreasing. This file proves, sorry-free:

    • stdEquivSigma μ : (Σ S : SYTe μ, DecSeq n) ≃ RSSYT μ — reverse semistandard tableaux are in bijection with pairs (standard tableau S, weakly decreasing sequence b₀ ≥ ⋯ ≥ b_{n−1} ≥ 0);
    • sum_stdEquivSigma — under it |T| = maj S + |b|.

    As generating functions this is Σ_{T ∈ RSSYT(λ)} q^{|T|} = (Σ_{S ∈ SYT(λ)} q^{maj S}) / ∏_{i=1}^{n} (1 − q^i), since weakly decreasing length-n sequences are partitions with at most n parts.

    Construction #

    Φ(S, b) writes b_k + D_k in the cell carrying entry k, where D_k is the number of descents j ≥ k of S (a descent: entry j + 1 lies in a strictly lower row than j). Rows decrease because b and D do; columns decrease strictly because a cell strictly below another forces a descent in between (run_no_des: a descent-free run of entries moves weakly up and strictly right). The inverse standardizes T: order cells by value, larger first, ties left to right (stdSYT), and subtract D. Strictness at descents (aN_step) is where the column-strict condition is used.

    Orientation: the reverse convention makes the weight shift exactly maj S = Σ_{descents} j (1-indexed); the ordinary semistandard orientation gives Σ_{descents} (n − j) instead.

    The second half #

    The second half — Σ_{T ∈ RSSYT(λ)} q^{|T|} = q^{n(λ)} / ∏_x (1 − q^{h(x)}) — and the maj formula itself are proved in QOU.Mathlib.MajFormula (fmaj_mul_hook). SYTe is an ordering-based presentation of standard tableaux, equivalent to QOU.Mathlib.StandardYoungTableau by QOU.Mathlib.MajSYTBridge.sytEquiv.

    Pure finite combinatorics on ℕ; nothing archimedean.
