    QOU.Mathlib.SYTBetaGapVandermonde — the β-gap product is the Vandermonde pair product #

    SYTHookFormulaBetaProdMonoid proves the divisor-free Frame–Robinson–Thrall hook product with the denominator carried as a nested recursion, ∏_i betaGapProdF f (l[i]) (l.drop (i+1)). The consumer wants it as a pair product ∏_{i<j} f(β_i − β_j) — the Vandermonde shape, which at f n = 1 − Qⁿ and x_i = Q^{β_i} is ∏_{i<j}(x_i − x_j) up to a monomial.

    This file is that reindexing. It is bookkeeping, not a new identity: betaGapProdF's recursion advances a by one per step precisely because β decreases by one per row beyond the part, so the k-th factor of row i is already f(β_i − β_{i+1+k}).
    betaNum l i β_i = l[i] + (|l| − 1 − i)
    betaGapProdF_eq_prod_range the recursion as a Finset.range product
    betaGap_eq_betaNum_sub the k-th factor's argument is β_i − β_{i+1+k}
    prod_betaGapProdF_eq_pair_prod ∏_i betaGapProdF … = ∏_{i<j} f(β_i − β_j)
    prodHooksF_mul_pair_prod the FRT hook product with the Vandermonde denominator
    prodHooksF_one_sub_pow_pair the same at f n = 1 − Qⁿ

    The last row completes sub-brick (a) of the general-r branching route (bean qou-j7ln): the q-analogue of ∏_c h_λ(c) = (∏_i β_i!)/∏_{i<j}(β_i − β_j), cleared of its denominator and with that denominator in pair form.

      The k-th β-gap factor of row i is β_i − β_{i+1+k}.

      β_i − β_j = (l[i] − l[j]) + (j − i) for j > i, and the recursion's a + (k+1) − t[k] is exactly that with j = i+1+k. Needs l[j] ≤ l[i], i.e. that l is weakly decreasing — without it the two truncated subtractions part company.
