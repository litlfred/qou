    The RSK recording tableau as a standard Young tableau #

    Brick 9b (final assembly). Assembles the pieces — the shifted recording SSYT recordingSSYT (Brick 9a), the cells↔content bridge content_eq_cells_map (Brick 9b crux), and content_shiftT_recording (content Q̃ = {1,…,|w|}, Nodup) — into the standard-tableau bijOn, yielding

    insertWordSYT w : QOU.Mathlib.StandardYoungTableau (recordingShape w)

    the recording tableau Q̃(w) of a word as a genuine standard Young tableau. Its entry map bijects the cells of the recording shape onto Set.Icc 1 |w|: injectivity is the Nodup of the bridge multiset (nodup_map_iff_injOn), and the image is {1,…,|w|} because the bridge multiset is (range |w|).map (·+1).

    This is the first bridge from the QOU RSK program into Mathlib's SYT world at the level of a concrete StandardYoungTableau object; the Sym n → Σ_λ SYT(λ)² map and Identity-I cardinality (Σ_{λ⊢n} (f^λ)² = n!) build on it.

    Pure combinatorics on ℕ; nothing archimedean.
