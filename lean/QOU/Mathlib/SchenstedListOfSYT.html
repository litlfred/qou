    QOU.Mathlib.SchenstedListOfSYT — a standard Young tableau as a list tableau #

    Brick 33 of epic qou-nrex (RSK inverse).

    Brick 32 proved surjectivity onto standard pairs in the List (List ℕ) representation. rskPair lands in Σ μ : YoungDiagram, StandardYoungTableau μ × StandardYoungTableau μ, so what stands between the two is a representation bridge. listStandardYT (brick 10) crosses it one way; this file starts the other.

    toListTableau S reads a standard Young tableau off its cells, row by row, and this file proves it lands in the class brick 32 consumes:

    • tableauShape_toListTableau — its shape is μ.rowLens, on the nose;
    • rows_strict_toListTableau / rowsSorted_toListTableau — rows strictly (hence weakly) increase;
    • colStrict_toListTableau — columns are StrictBelow, both halves: the length condition is rowLen_anti and the entry condition is the SSYT's col_strict;
    • noEmptyRows_toListTableau, nodup_flatten_toListTableau.

    Column strictness is where the two notions differ in shape rather than in content: StrictBelow bundles the Young-shape length condition with the entry comparison, while a SemistandardYoungTableau keeps them apart — the lengths in the diagram, the entries in col_strict. The bridge has to supply both, and rowLen_anti is the half that has no counterpart in the tableau's own data.

    def QOU.Mathlib.Schensted.toListTableau {μ : YoungDiagram} (S : StandardYoungTableau μ) :
    List (List ℕ)

    A standard Young tableau read off row by row. Row i is the entries at the cells (i, 0), …, (i, rowLen i - 1); there are as many rows as μ.rowLens has entries.
