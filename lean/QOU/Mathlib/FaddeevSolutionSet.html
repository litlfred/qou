    The solution set absorbs any doubly-periodic factor, with no regularity asked of it. Both shift equations carry a prefactor that does not see E, and the two half-shifts ±ib/2 differ by exactly the period ib.

      The witness. Multiplying any FaddeevPhi b solution by the lattice indicator gives another FaddeevPhi b solution. Since the indicator takes only the values 0 and 1, the two solutions do NOT have a differentiable ratio wherever the indicator is discontinuous — so hratio (bean qou-cons) is not derivable from the class.
