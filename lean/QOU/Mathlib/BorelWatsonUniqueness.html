    Asymptotics.SuperpolynomialDecay Filter.atTop Real.exp fun (x : ℝ) => |f x|

    Stretched-exponential ⇒ superexponential decay (the >π-sector amplification). If ‖f x‖ ≤ C·exp(−b·x^s) eventually with b > 0 and s > 1, then ‖f‖ decays superexponentially: exp(n x)·‖f x‖ → 0 for every n. The stretched exponent s > 1 is precisely what a Watson sector of opening > π produces, and precisely what upgrades the single-sector exponential bound to the strength Phragmén–Lindelöf requires.

    Watson uniqueness on the right half-plane. Two functions analytic on the open right half-plane, continuous on its closure, O(exp(B‖z‖^c)) with c < 2, and bounded on the imaginary axis, that agree up to a stretched- exponential ≤ C·exp(−b·x^s) (b>0, s>1) along the positive real axis, are equal on the closed right half-plane. A Borel/Watson wrapper of PhragmenLindelof.eqOn_right_half_plane_of_superexponential_decay, with the superexponential hypothesis supplied by stretchedExp_superpolynomialDecay. This is the uniqueness half of Watson / Nevanlinna–Sokal, realized.
