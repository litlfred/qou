    QOU.Mathlib.SYTHookFormulaBetaMonoid — the per-row FRT identity in any commutative monoid #

    SYTHookFormulaBeta proves the divisor-free per-row Frame–Robinson–Thrall identity over ℕ:

    rowHookProd a t · betaGapProd a t = (a + |t|)!.

    Read the proof and the arithmetic turns out to be incidental. Every step is a reindexing of the multiset of hook lengths against the multiset {1, …, a + |t|}; the only genuinely numerical moves are omega facts about the arguments, never about the values. So the identity holds with ℕ multiplication replaced by any commutative monoid and each factor n replaced by f n.
    rowHookProdF f a t ∏_{j<a} f((a−j) + cnt t j) — the row's hook lengths, pushed through f
    betaGapProdF f a t the β-gap product, pushed through f
    descFactF f n f(n)·f(n−1)⋯f(1), the f-analogue of n!
    rowHookProdF_mul_betaGapProdF rowHookProdF f a t · betaGapProdF f a t = descFactF f (a + |t|)

    Why this generality is the point. At f = id over (ℕ, ×) it is the corpus statement (rowHookProdF_id, proved below, so the generalisation is checked against its own special case rather than merely asserted to contain it). At f n = 1 − Q^n over a field it is the q-analogue — the per-row half of the hook-length identity that general-r branching needs, relating ∏_c (1 − Q^{h(c)}) to the shifted parts β_i. See bean qou-j7ln and QOU.Mathlib.LagrangeCoeffSum.sum_eq_bracket_mul_of_qratio_scaling, which is waiting on exactly that.

          The per-row Frame–Robinson–Thrall identity, in any commutative monoid.

          For a weakly-decreasing tail t with every part ≤ a,

          rowHookProdF f a t · betaGapProdF f a t = descFactF f (a + |t|).

          At f = id over (ℕ, ×) this is SYTBeta.rowHookProd_mul_betaGapProd; at f n = 1 − Q^n over a field it is the q-analogue.

          The content side #

          The hook side above is the denominator of the level-r principal specialisation. Its numerator ∏_c f(r + content c) splits row by row into a contiguous block f(b), f(b+1), …, f(b+m−1), and such a block is a ratio of two descFactFs. This section proves that, division-free.

          A contiguous block of f-factors is a ratio of descFactFs, division-free:

          f(b)·f(b+1)⋯f(b+m−1) · descFactF f (b−1) = descFactF f (b+m−1).

          Row i of the level-r numerator is exactly such a block: its cells have content j − i for j < μ_i, so its factors are f(r − i + j), a block starting at b = r − i of length m = μ_i. The top index b + m − 1 = μ_i + r − 1 − i is the β-number β_i, which is why the ∏_i descFactF f β_i produced here cancels against the one the hook side produces.

          The instance f n = 1 − Qⁿ, which is what general-r branching needs.

          The per-row hook identity, q-analogue.

          ∏_{j<a} (1 − Q^{h(j)}) · ∏ (β-gaps as 1 − Q^{gap}) = ∏_{k=1}^{a+|t|} (1 − Q^k),

          where h(j) = (a − j) + cnt t j runs over the hook lengths of the row. This is rowHookProdF_mul_betaGapProdF at f n = 1 − Qⁿ; it needs no separate proof because the original argument never touched the values, only the exponents.
