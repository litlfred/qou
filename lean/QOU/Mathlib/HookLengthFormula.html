    QOU.Mathlib.HookLengthFormula — arm / leg / hook lengths and the HLF target #

    Foundation for the hook-length formula (Frame–Robinson–Thrall 1954), the Mathlib-gap infra that computes #SYT(μ) in closed form — the unlock for the tabulated character coefficients c_λ ∈ {1,3,8,10} of symbolic_character_table (bean qou-tqab; see QOU.BraidKnot.SytSmallShapeCounts for the hand-enumerated c_λ = 1 case that this generalises).

    This file supplies the definitional layer — arm, leg, and hook lengths of a cell, and the hook-length product over a diagram — plus the geometric hook-length formula itself, sorry-free:

    #SYT(μ) · ∏_{c ∈ μ} hook(c) = |μ|!.

    The proof does not re-derive the FRT/RSK bijection: the corpus already proves the list-form HLF QOU.Mathlib.SYTEnum.numSYTenum_mul_prodHooks (via the GNW corner recursion; axiom-clean). This file identifies the geometric cell-hook product with the corpus list prodHooks (hookLengthProduct_eq_prodHooks — a pure-ℕ reindex of the cell product by rows and columns, matching per-cell hooks via hookLength_eq_of_mem + ColLenRowLensBridge.colLen_rowLens), wires through the count bridge (numSYTenum_eq_numStandardYoungTableaux) and SpechtClassification.card_eq_rowLens_sum, and thereby discharges the geometric HLF on the genuine numStandardYoungTableaux count.

    Main definitions #

    • YoungDiagram.armLength / legLength / hookLength — of a cell (i,j).
    • YoungDiagram.hookLengthProduct — ∏_{c ∈ μ} hook(c).

    Main statements #

    • hookLength_pos, hookLengthProduct_pos — positivity.
    • hookLength_eq_of_mem — the closed (rowLen−j)+(colLen−i)−1 form on cells.
    • hookLengthProduct_eq_prodHooks — geometric cell product = corpus list prodHooks.
    • numStandardYoungTableaux_mul_hookLengthProduct — the geometric HLF, sorry-free.

    Reference: the Frame–Robinson–Thrall hook-length formula (1954); proved here via the corpus GNW list-form HLF rather than re-deriving the bijection.

          theorem YoungDiagram.hookLength_eq_of_mem (μ : YoungDiagram) {i j : ℕ} (h : (i, j) ∈ μ) :
          μ.hookLength i j = μ.rowLen i - j + (μ.colLen j - i) - 1

          On a cell (i, j) ∈ μ, the hook length is the closed form (rowLen i − j) + (colLen j − i) − 1. On a cell j < rowLen i and i < colLen j, so arm = rowLen i − j − 1 and leg = colLen j − i − 1 are the honest right/below counts.

            theorem QOU.Mathlib.hookLengthProduct_eq_prodHooks (μ : YoungDiagram) :
            μ.hookLengthProduct = BraidKnot.IdentityI.prodHooks μ.rowLens

            The geometric↔list hook bridge ∏_{c∈μ} hook(c) = prodHooks (rowLens μ) (sorry-free). Reindexes the cell product by rows (cells_biUnion') and columns (row_prod), converts the corpus list prodHooks product to a Finset product (list_prod_map_range), and identifies the per-cell hook (rowLen i − j) + (colLen j − i) − 1 (hookLength_eq_of_mem + ColLenRowLensBridge.colLen_rowLens) with the corpus list term. Together with the proved list-form HLF (QOU.Mathlib.SYTEnum.numSYTenum_mul_prodHooks) this fully discharges the geometric Frame–Robinson–Thrall HLF below.

            theorem QOU.Mathlib.numStandardYoungTableaux_mul_hookLengthProduct (μ : YoungDiagram) :
            numStandardYoungTableaux μ * μ.hookLengthProduct = μ.cells.card.factorial

            The hook-length formula (target): for every Young diagram μ with n = |μ| cells, the number of standard Young tableaux times the hook-length product equals n!:

            #SYT(μ) · ∏_{c ∈ μ} hook(c) = n!.

            Equivalently #SYT(μ) = n! / ∏ hook(c). This is the closed form that pins the per-shape counts (e.g. the hook (2,1): hooks 3·1·1 = 3, so #SYT = 3!/3 = 2, matching SytSmallShapeCounts.card_SYT_mu21).

            Stated as the infra target; the proof (RSK, or the Frame–Robinson–Thrall / Novelli–Pak–Stoyanovskii probabilistic bijection) is the tracked next step — a Mathlib-upstream-scale contribution.
