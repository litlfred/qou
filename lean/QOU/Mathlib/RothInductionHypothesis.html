    Non-vacuity. The largest hypothesis stack in this arc, shown jointly satisfiable.

    One variable on each side, everything constant: n = r = 1, d₁ ≡ 1, P = 1, φ = ψ ≡ 1, p/q = 1/2, B = 1, E = 0. Then the auxiliary polynomial's determinant is 1, whose coefficients are 0 and 1, so B = 1 works; E = 0 makes B ≤ q^E read 1 ≤ 1; and the induction hypothesis is rothLemmaUniform_one, which is proved.

    ⚠ This certifies joint satisfiability and nothing more — and the arc has been explicit that finite witnesses test vacuity, not usefulness (correction 9). What it rules out is the failure mode that a stack this large invites: two side conditions that quietly contradict each other, leaving a theorem that is true because it is empty.

    §12's own form — Θ rather than the E-encoded schema #

    Correction nineteen: §12 invokes the induction hypothesis as

    J(U; ρ₁,…,ρ_{m−1}; κ₁,…,κ_{m−1}) ≤ Θ_{m−1}(b; ρ₁,…,ρ_{m−1}; H₁,…,H_{m−1})

    which is the definition of Θ — U ∈ R(b;ρ) and κ ∈ Q(H), nothing else. There is no height exponent at this step; Γ enters separately, as what bounds Θ_{m−1} afterwards.

    The two theorems below are that step. They consume IsRothTheta's upper-bound clause directly and are shorter than the RothLemmaUniform versions above, which fuse set membership with Γ and share a single E that Γ cannot supply.

    ⚠ The RothLemmaUniform forms above are superseded, not wrong. They are proved and their statements are true; they simply carry a hypothesis stack the induction cannot produce. Kept until the recursion is written and confirms nothing needs them.

    ⚠⚠ Correction twenty-six — the two halves above take the wrong b #

    hB in both is a bound on the determinant's coefficients. Correction 21 established that §12's budget covers the divided Wronskian W, with det = C c · W and c a product of factorials of size 2^{Θ(r² log r)}; WronskianHeight.exists_divided_wronskian proves exactly that, and §11's clause b ≤ 2^{c'}·a^r has no room for the extra factor.

    So the two halves above and the only available height bound do not compose, and both were green. The correction landed in WronskianHeight and was never propagated here.

    The _div forms below take W and the factorisation det = C c · W instead, and are otherwise identical — SeparatedGauss.exists_integral_wronskian_factors_div parks the c⁻¹ on the y side, and index_C_mul is indifferent to which constant it divides out.

    ⚠ The originals are kept: they are true, and a caller who does have a determinant bound (a small r, say) can still use them. What they cannot be is the induction's hres.
