    The argument principle — scaffold (PI-3, bean qou-bmyx) #

    For f meromorphic on a closed disc \overline{B}(c,R) with no zeros or poles on the bounding circle, the contour integral of the logarithmic derivative counts zeros minus poles with multiplicity:

    $$\frac{1}{2\pi i}\oint_{|z-c|=R} \frac{f'(z)}{f(z)}\,dz \;=\; \sum_{w\in B(c,R)} \operatorname{ord}_w f \;=\; \#\{\text{zeros}\} - \#\{\text{poles}\}\quad(\text{with multiplicity}).$$

    This is the argument principle. It is the least-Analysis-blocked of the three PentagonRigidity infra walls (PI-1 Hadamard factorization, PI-2 elliptic divisor-degree, PI-3 here): PI-2's degree-zero theorem and PI-1's canonical-product zero-counting BOTH reduce to it (elliptic_divisor_degree_zero names the argument principle as its explicit open core; a canonical product's zero count is this integral applied to an entire f). The f'/f-integrand carries the analytic content; the RHS is the purely-combinatorial divisor sum.

    MOTIVATION (epic qou-lbth). The residual analytic leg of PentagonRigidity.uniqueUpToScalar needs to pin the canonical minimal Faddeev solution phiBProd among its elliptic multiples by counting zeros; the argument principle is the tool that turns "a contour integral of f'/f" into "an integer count of zeros", and is the shared engine under both PI-1 and PI-2.

    What is authored here — bricks vs. open core #

    Elementary bricks (full proofs, Mathlib-only cone — see the verification caveat):

    • toNat_sub_toNat_neg — the integer identity n.toNat - (-n).toNat = n.

    • inv_two_pi_I_mul_integral_nat_sub_center_inv — brick 2: the Cauchy simple-pole term (2πi)⁻¹ ∮_{C(w,r)} n·(z-w)⁻¹ = n.
    • circleIntegral_logDeriv_eq_zero_of_analyticOnNhd — brick 3: the Cauchy–Goursat vanishing ∮_{C(w,r)} logDeriv g = 0 for g analytic + nonvanishing on the disc.

    Now PROVED sorry-free (the full local argument principle):

    Now PROVED sorry-free (2026-07-20 — the global statements, via factorization):

    • argument_principle — the global statement (contour integral = divisor sum), proved fully sorry-free via the global Weierstrass factorization MeromorphicOn.extract_zeros_poles: on the closed disc, f = (∏ᶠ_w (·-w)^{ord_w f}) · g with g analytic and nonvanishing, so logDeriv f = Σ_w (ord_w f)·(·-w)⁻¹ + logDeriv g on the bounding circle C(c,R). The hypotheses ∀ u, ord_u f ≠ ⊤ and divisor.support.Finite (needed by extract_zeros_poles) are discharged from hf + a boundary witness + connectedness (exists_meromorphicOrderAt_ne_top_iff_forall) and disc compactness (locallyFinsuppWithin.finiteSupport).
    • entire_zero_count — the PI-3 canonical-product application, proved sorry-free by specializing argument_principle to entire f: no poles ⇒ divisor ≥ 0 (AnalyticAt.meromorphicOrderAt_nonneg), so Σ ord = zeroCount.

    Why the contour-deformation gap was dissolved, not closed #

    An earlier revision isolated a single sorry — residue_theorem_deformation, the multiply-connected (off-centre / multi-hole homology) contour deformation ∮_{C(c,R)} h = Σ_w ∮_{C(w,ρ w)} h for general analytic h. That deformation is a genuine Mathlib v4.24.0 gap (below), and it is not needed for the argument principle: logDeriv f is not an arbitrary h, and the global factorization route above computes ∮_{C(c,R)} logDeriv f directly. The deformation lemma was therefore removed in favour of the factorization proof, and the file is now sorry-free. (The general-h residue-sum deformation remains genuinely absent from Mathlib and would still require a winding-number / homology engine to state and prove; it is simply unnecessary here. The same factorization route is boundary-shape-agnostic: a rectangular outer contour argument principle follows identically via the rectangle Cauchy integral formula

    • integral_boundary_rect_eq_zero_of_differentiable_on_off_countable, with no crosscut deformation.)

        The local argument principle (deep core — stated open) #

        The single missing Mathlib brick: the residue of the logarithmic derivative at a point equals the meromorphic order there. Everything global is this, summed.

        The three lemmas below are the sorry-free analytic bricks the local argument principle assembles from — the log-derivative split, the Cauchy simple-pole term, and the Cauchy–Goursat vanishing of the analytic part. Only the global factorization on the closed disc (extending the germ meromorphicOrderAt_eq_int_iff gives to a nonvanishing analytic g on a neighborhood of the disc) remains open.

        The residue-SUM engine — via global zero/pole extraction (no contour deformation) #

        The argument principle is assembled here without any multiply-connected contour deformation. The classical "deform the big contour onto small circles around each puncture" route needs a homology/winding engine that Mathlib v4.24.0 lacks (Complex.residue, windingNumber, and every off-centre / keyhole contour-deformation lemma are absent — verified 2026-07-20 by #check; only the concentric annulus deformation circleIntegral_eq_of_differentiable_on_annulus_off_countable and the on-disc Cauchy integral formula are present). Instead we use the global Weierstrass factorization MeromorphicOn.extract_zeros_poles: on the closed disc, f = (∏ᶠ_w (·-w)^{ord_w f}) · g with g analytic and nonvanishing, so logDeriv f = Σ_w (ord_w f)·(·-w)⁻¹ + logDeriv g on the bounding circle. The off-centre difficulty dissolves because the Cauchy integral of a bare simple pole ∮_{C(c,R)} (z-w)⁻¹ = 2πi holds for every interior w ∈ B(c,R) (circleIntegral.integral_sub_center_inv's off-centre companion circleIntegral.integral_sub_inv_of_mem_ball), and the analytic part integrates to 0 by Cauchy–Goursat. Summing over the finite divisor support gives the argument principle directly — fully sorry-free.
