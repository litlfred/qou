    QOU.Mathlib.QHookVandermonde — the q-hook product against a genuine Vandermonde #

    SYTBetaGapVandermonde.prodHooksF_one_sub_pow_pair proves the q-analogue of Frame–Robinson–Thrall in (1 − Q^{β_i − β_j}) form:

    ∏_c (1 − Q^{h(c)}) · ∏_{i<j} (1 − Q^{β_i − β_j})  =  ∏_i ∏_{m=1}^{β_i} (1 − Q^m).

    Its own docstring names what is left: "with x_i = Q^{β_i} the middle factor is ∏_{i<j}(x_i − x_j) up to the monomial ∏_{i<j} x_j." This file does that conversion, so the identity is available against a genuine Vandermonde in the node coordinates x_i = Q^{β_i} — which is the shape every consumer in the LagrangeCoeffSum family takes, sum_prod_qratio_corner included.

    The conversion is termwise and needs no sign or exponent bookkeeping:

    x_i − x_j  =  (−x_j) · (1 − Q^{β_i − β_j})        (β_j ≤ β_i)

    so the whole monomial-and-sign correction is just ∏_{i<j} (−x_j), left in product form rather than evaluated to (−1)^{\binom k 2} Q^{\sum_j j·β_j}. Leaving it unevaluated is deliberate: the evaluated form is what a hand derivation reaches for and it is where the sign errors live, and no consumer needs it — the correction cancels against itself on both sides of every ratio.

    The only mathematical input is that β is strictly decreasing on a weakly-decreasing shape, which is getD_antitone plus the (length − 1 − i) staircase.

    Base ring (§7c) #

    Generic [CommRing R]. No ordering, no ℝ, nothing archimedean; the arguments stay in ℕ.
