    Generic list tableau → standard Young tableau, and P(w) standardness #

    Brick 10. Factors the insertWordSYT construction (Brick 9b) into a reusable primitive: any list tableau T with weakly-increasing rows (RowsSorted), strictly-increasing columns (ColStrict), and content the full set {1, …, n} is a QOU.Mathlib.StandardYoungTableau on its row-length shape (listStandardYT). The bijOn proof is exactly the Brick 9b assembly with the content hypothesis abstracted.

    Two immediate instances:

    • the recording tableau Q̃(w) — content {1,…,|w|} unconditionally (Brick 8/9a);
    • the insertion tableau P(w) — content ↑w (insertWord_content), which is {1,…,n} exactly when w is a permutation word (insertWordPSYT).

    So for a permutation word both P(w) and Q(w) are standard tableaux of the same shape — the input to the RSK map Sym n → Σ_λ SYT(λ)² toward Identity-I.

    Pure combinatorics on ℕ; nothing archimedean.
