    QOU.Mathlib.YoungDiagramCorner — removable corners and corner removal of Young diagrams #

    Mathlib's YoungDiagram carries ofRowLens / rowLens / mem_cells / up_left_mem but no corner / erase / SDiff API. This file supplies the missing structural primitive needed for the Greene–Nijenhuis–Wilf branching rule numStandardYoungTableaux μ = ∑_{removable corners c} numStandardYoungTableaux (μ ∖ c):

    • IsCornerCell μ c — c is a removable corner: a cell of μ with no cell to its right or below.
    • eraseCorner μ c hc — the Young diagram μ ∖ c (delete the corner cell; the result is still a lower set, hence a genuine YoungDiagram), with its membership and cardinality laws.

    Removing a corner (a maximal cell) is exactly what keeps cells.erase c downward closed: any cell strictly above-or-right of c that stayed in μ would force one of c's missing neighbours back in.

    Pure combinatorics on ℕ × ℕ; nothing archimedean; no fit.

    def QOU.Mathlib.IsCornerCell (μ : YoungDiagram) (c : ℕ × ℕ) :
    Prop

    A removable corner of μ: a cell c ∈ μ whose right neighbour (i, j+1) and lower neighbour (i+1, j) are both outside μ — i.e. c is an outer corner whose deletion leaves a Young diagram.
