      theorem MvPolynomial.log_taylorConst {m : ℕ} (r : Fin m → ℕ) {H B : ℝ} (hH : 0 < H) (hB : 0 < B) :
      Real.log (taylorConst r H B) = 2 * Real.log ↑(∏ h : Fin m, (r h + 1)) + Real.log H + ↑(∑ h : Fin m, r h) * Real.log (2 * B) + ↑(∑ h : Fin m, r h) * Real.log 2

      What a parameter choice has to beat, term by term. log(taylorConst) splits into four named costs: the box count (twice — once for P's support, once for its translate), the height of P, the translation cost (2B)^{Σr}, and the peeling cost 2^{Σr}.

      Writing it out is the point of the exercise. In the additive form of Roth's inequality the whole left side is 2·log(box) + log H + Σr·log(4B) + Σ_h r_h·log q_h, and every one of those terms scales with Σ_h r_h except log H. So the normalisation cannot be one-sided: r_h·log q_h ≥ L alone bounds the right side from below but leaves Σ_h r_h·log q_h on the left unbounded above, and a parameter record that carries only the lower bound cannot discharge this. That is a constraint on the record, discovered by writing the decomposition rather than by assuming it.

      theorem MvPolynomial.roth_contradiction_at_witness (hlog : Real.log (taylorConst (fun (x : Fin 2) => 1) 1 1024) + ∑ x : Fin 2, ↑1 * Real.log ↑1 < 8 * (↑0 - ↑0)) (hgap : ↑(rothConstant 2) ^ 2 ^ (2 - 1) * (↑1 / ↑1) < 0 ^ 2 ^ (2 - 1)) :
      False

      The structural hypotheses are jointly satisfiable. roth_contradiction concludes False, so its hypotheses are supposed to be jointly unsatisfiable — which means vacuity is not the risk. The risk is that some structural subset is already inconsistent, making the theorem true for a reason having nothing to do with Roth. This rules that out by supplying every structural argument at the parameters of index_le_rothBound_witness (m = 2, p = q = 1, F = 10, a = 1, r = (1,1), H = (1024,1024), P = 1, κ = 1024, α = 1024, G = 0), leaving exactly the two numeric conditions as hypotheses.

      ⚠ It does not show the collision fires. At μ = ν = 0 both remaining hypotheses are false — hlog because taylorConst > 1 makes its left side positive while its right side is 0, and hgap because c₂² · 1 = 25 is not below 0. They must be false somewhere, or False would be derivable from satisfiable hypotheses. Both holding at once needs μ ≈ m(½−ε) from step 1 and K ≈ (2+δ)L from an approximation exponent above 2, which is Roth's inequality and is what a parameter record has to deliver — not something a small witness can exhibit.
