    QOU.Mathlib.SYTBranching2Row — the corner-removal recursion (A) on two rows #

    The general hook-length formula is reduced (in SYTBranchingRecursion) to the two corner-removal recursions (A) dimHLF_branching and (B) numSYTenum_branching. This file proves (A) on two rows, sorry-free:

    dimHLF [a, b] = branchSum dimHLF [a, b]          (`dimHLF_branching_pair`,  a ≥ b ≥ 1)

    i.e. dimHLF[a,b] = dimHLF[a−1,b] + dimHLF[a,b−1] (and = dimHLF[a,b−1] when a = b, the single corner). With the ballot-number form dimHLF[a,b] = C(a+b,b) − C(a+b,b−1) (dimHLF_pair) this is pure Pascal's rule plus binomial monotonicity/symmetry — no floor-division. Pure ℕ.

    (A) corner-removal recursion on two rows (sorry-free). dimHLF [a, b] = branchSum dimHLF [a, b] for a ≥ b ≥ 1: the brute-force hook-length dimension of a two-row shape equals the sum over removable corners of the dimensions of the corner-removed shapes — the two-row instance of obligation (A) for the general hook-length formula.
