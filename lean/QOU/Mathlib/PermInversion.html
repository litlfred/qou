    Inversion count of a permutation of Fin n and its decrease under an adjacent descent swap #

    A small Mathlib-gap kernel for the Okounkov–Vershik exchange argument (QOU.BraidKnot.JucysMurphy.syt_all_connect_to_some): the well-founded measure that drives the walk of any standard Young tableau toward the row-superstandard base.

    invCount π counts inversions {(i, j) : i < j, π j < π i}. The key fact is that composing on the right with the adjacent transposition swap a (a+1) at a descent (π (a+1) < π a) strictly decreases the count (invCount_mul_swap_lt): the pair (a, a+1) is the only pair whose order is reversed by an adjacent swap, so the inversion set of π ∘ swap injects into that of π while missing the (now-resolved) inversion (a, a+1).

    Pure combinatorics on Fin n; §7c-clean; no fit.
