    QOU.Mathlib.MajSYTBridge — SYTe is the corpus's standard Young tableau #

    QOU.Mathlib.MajStandardization.SYTe μ presents a standard tableau as an ordering of the cells (e : Cell μ ≃ Fin |μ|, increasing along rows and down columns, entries 0, …, |μ|−1). QOU.Mathlib.StandardYoungTableau μ presents it as a semistandard tableau whose entry map is a bijection of the cells onto {1, …, |μ|}.

    Theorem (sytEquiv). The two types are equivalent: shift entries by one.

    Consequences:

    • card_SYTe : Fintype.card (SYTe μ) = numStandardYoungTableaux μ;
    • fmaj_eval_one : (fmaj μ).eval 1 = numStandardYoungTableaux μ — the maj generating function ∑_{T ∈ SYT(μ)} q^{maj T} of QOU.Mathlib.MajFormula specialises at q = 1 to #SYT(μ).

    Pure finite combinatorics on ℕ; nothing archimedean.
