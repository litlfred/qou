    • comm (a b : self.carrier) : self.mul a b = self.mul b a

      Commutativity of the Frobenius algebra.

                  Jacobiator of the q-deformed Lie bracket #

                  noncomputable def QOU.Elaboration.q_jacobiator {M : Type u_1} (sp : ArchimedeanSubstrateField M) (dtheta_VW dtheta_WU dtheta_UV LxiU LxiV LxiW : M → ℝ) (p : M) :
                  ℝ

                  The Jacobiator of the q-deformed Lie bracket applied to three horizontal vector fields. For horizontal U, V, W (i.e. θ(U) = θ(V) = θ(W) = 0), the bracket reduces to [V,W]_q = q⁻¹[V,W], and the Jacobiator is Jac_q(U,V,W) = −q⁻¹(1−q⁻¹) Σ_cyc dθ(V,W) L_ξ U.

                    theorem QOU.Elaboration.jacobi_identity_violation {M : Type u_1} (sp : ArchimedeanSubstrateField M) (dtheta_VW dtheta_WU dtheta_UV LxiU LxiV LxiW : M → ℝ) (p : M) (hq_ne : sp.field p ≠ 1) (hq_ne_zero : sp.field p ≠ 0) (h_cyc_ne_zero : dtheta_VW p * LxiU p + dtheta_WU p * LxiV p + dtheta_UV p * LxiW p ≠ 0) :
                    q_jacobiator sp dtheta_VW dtheta_WU dtheta_UV LxiU LxiV LxiW p ≠ 0

                    When dθ|_{H×H} is non-degenerate and q ≠ 1, the Jacobiator is generically non-zero. This witnesses that (𝔛(M), [·,·]_q) violates the Jacobi identity for all q ≠ 1.

                    More precisely: given a point where the cyclic sum Σ_cyc dθ(V,W) L_ξ U ≠ 0 (which is generically true by non-degeneracy of the Levi form), the Jacobiator is non-zero.

                    LaTeX: Proposition in qou-elaboration.tex, lines 551–572.

                    Leibniz algebra structure #

                    The q-deformed Lie bracket satisfies the Leibniz identity (left Leibniz rule) even though it fails the Jacobi identity. A left Leibniz algebra requires: [U, [V, W]_q]_q = [[U, V]_q, W]_q + [V, [U, W]_q]_q.

                    This is a strictly weaker condition than the Jacobi identity and is the correct algebraic classification for (𝔛(M), [·,·]_q) when q ≠ 1.

                    source
                    structure QOU.Elaboration.IsLeibnizAlgebra {M : Type u_1} (sp : ArchimedeanSubstrateField M) (bracket : (M → ℝ) → (M → ℝ) → M → ℝ) :
                    Prop

                    Marker structure: a Leibniz algebra is a module with a bilinear bracket satisfying the left Leibniz identity. We record this as a Prop-valued structure witnessing that the q-bracket on horizontal fields forms a Leibniz algebra.

                    LaTeX: \label{prop:q-jacobiator}, conclusion at line 571.

                    • left_leibniz (U V W : M → ℝ) (p : M) : bracket U (bracket V W) p = bracket (bracket U V) W p + bracket V (bracket U W) p

                      Left Leibniz identity: [U, [V, W]] = [[U, V], W] + [V, [U, W]] pointwise.
