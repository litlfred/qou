    QOU.FlavorDressing — Flavor-dressed quark mass functor #

    Categorical bridge from the discrete category Flavor of quark flavors to the positive reals carrying dressed-quark mass values. Substrate-pure derivation of the down–up splitting from prop:flavor-split-substrate lives here; the splitting-only substrate bound theorem is included.

    Members #

    • FlavorDressingFunctor — typeclass with μ : Flavor → ℝ and positivity.
    • dressed_mass_from_substrate_split — closed-form derivation of μ_d − μ_u = M_N · substrate_split_factor q, conditional on the axiomatised Skein-side closed form.
    • flavor_mass_splitting_bound — two-sided substrate bound 0 < μ_d − μ_u < M_N · ℏ_q² · h_q · [2]_q.
