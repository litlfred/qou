    QOU Atomic Mass Predictions — CODATA 2022 vs AME2020 Audit

    Two independent mass sources are compared for each element:

    • CODATA 2022 constituent masses (m_p, m_n, m_e)
    • AME2020 atomic mass unit (u) and mass excesses (Δ_H, Δ_n)

    For an element with Z protons and N neutrons (A = Z + N) the CODATA path is Z·m_p + N·m_n + Z·m_e − A·(B/A) and the AME path is A·u + Z·Δ_H + N·Δ_n − A·(B/A). Their difference is the structural offset

      codataMass − ameMass = Z·offset_Z + N·offset_N            (1)

    with

    • offset_Z = (m_p + m_e) − (u + Δ_H) ≈ 1.546 × 10⁻⁵ MeV — the H-atom electron binding energy, absent from the free m_p + m_e but present in M_H; and
    • offset_N = m_n − (u + Δ_n) ≈ 1.46 × 10⁻⁶ MeV — the residual CODATA-2022 vs AME2020 neutron-mass discrepancy.

    Both offsets are tiny. Identity (1) (codata_minus_ame_offset) and the resulting per-element bound (deviation_uniform) are proved generically for every ElementDatum, subsuming what were 118 copy-pasted per-element theorems. The curated 118-element AME2020 dataset (Z = 1…118) is retained as elements.

    Correction note: the historical per-Z-only form … = Z·offset_Z is false — the N·offset_N term does not cancel against the B/A binding term (that would require m_n = u + Δ_n, which fails by ≈ 1.46 eV). Identity (1) is the corrected statement.

                              theorem QOU.AtomicMass.ElementDatum.codata_minus_ame_offset (e : ElementDatum) :
                              e.codataMass - e.ameMass = ↑e.Z * codata_ame_offset_per_Z + ↑e.N * codata_ame_offset_per_N

                              Structural identity. For any element datum the CODATA−AME2020 mass difference is Z·offset_Z + N·offset_N. The A·(B/A) binding terms cancel; the proton and neutron offsets do not.

                              theorem QOU.AtomicMass.deviation_uniform (e : ElementDatum) :
                              |e.codataMass - e.ameMass| ≤ ↑e.Z * |codata_ame_offset_per_Z| + ↑e.N * |codata_ame_offset_per_N|

                              Uniform per-element deviation bound. For any element datum, |codataMass − ameMass| ≤ Z·|offset_Z| + N·|offset_N|. This single generic statement subsumes the per-element numerical checks (the offsets are bounded by codata_ame_offset_per_{Z,N}_bound).
