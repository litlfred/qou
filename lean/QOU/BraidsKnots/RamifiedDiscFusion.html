    QOU.BraidsKnots.RamifiedDiscFusion #

    Compiled formalisation of prop:ramified-disc-fusion. Sibling docstub at braids-and-knots/ramified-disc-fusion.md.

    At the foundry boundary latitudes $\phi \in \{0, \pi\}$ of the Reeb bundle, the three color-sector preimages of a small disc on the exceptional divisor fuse into a single topological disc with degree-3 ramification at the centre. The monodromy of this branched cover around the boundary circle realises the $S_3$ Galois action on the three sectors.

          Proposition (prop:ramified-disc-fusion). The cyclic monodromy generator $(rgb)$ acts on the Lagrange resolvent $w_k$ by multiplication by $\zeta^{-1}$. This exhibits the explicit geometric agreement of the $S_3$ action with the Lagrange-resolvent action, satisfying the third claim of the narrative.
