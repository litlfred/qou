    QOU.BraidsKnots.ReebBundleComplementEqualsVolume #

    Proposition (prop:reeb-bundle-complement-equals-volume), conditional on conj:mass-volume-factorization.

    Let 𝓡_p ≅ S³ be the Reeb bundle and K_β the closure of the colour-tube trajectory under β ∈ B₃. The categorical Markov-trace observable equals the JSJ-graded sum over the hyperbolic pieces P_i of the knot complement 𝓡_p \ ν(K_β):

    tr_M(ρ(β)) = Σ_{P_i hyperbolic} ρ_F(P_i) · V_hyp(P_i).

    2026-09-03 — a second class of the same name, and the JSJ sum collapsed #

    This file declared QOU.BraidsKnots.MassVolumeFactorization with three fields — markov_trace : ℝ, jsj_volume_sum : ℝ, and factorization_identity : markov_trace = jsj_volume_sum — and ReebBundleComplementEqualsVolume returned that field. It cited AGENTS.md §3b-cond for the pattern, and it is the case §3b-cond item 5 forbids: the class carries the block's own conclusion, so the "conditional theorem" derives nothing and a [MassVolumeFactorization] binder assumes nothing (both reals are chosen by the instance).

    The conjecture already has a carrier, and this was a second one under the same name. QOU.ParticleInteractions.MassVolumeFactorization is the real one, and it has the shape §3b-cond actually describes: parameterised over the link configuration K and strand count n, with density, volume and mass as functions on K and factorization : ∀ k : K, mass k = (density k).value * volume k. That field quantifies over the caller's K, so the binder constrains caller data. The two classes share only a name and live in different namespaces; nothing imports this one (grep across content/ matches the other declaration).

    And the degenerate re-declaration erased the proposition's content. Collapsing Σ_{P_i hyperbolic} ρ_F(P_i)·V_hyp(P_i) into a single opaque jsj_volume_sum removes the JSJ grading — which is the whole of what "JSJ-graded volume sum" means, and the reason the block is a distinct proposition rather than a restatement of the conjecture.

    Disposition: name it, assert nothing (AGENTS.md §1a, disposition 3) #

    ReebBundleComplementEqualsVolume now states the identity with the sum explicit, over caller-supplied data, and asserts none of it. Two consequences the .md itself asserts are derived from it:

    • markovTrace_eq_zero_of_no_hyperbolic_pieces — when no piece is hyperbolic the sum is empty and tr_M(ρ(β)) = 0. This is the .md's "pure-rotation 3-cycles trace Heegaard torus trajectories, leaving a Seifert-fibered complement where V_hyp = 0".
    • markovTrace_eq_zero_of_volumes_vanish — the same conclusion from vanishing volumes rather than an empty piece set, which is the other reading of that sentence. Both are now theorems, so the JSJ grading is doing work instead of being a name.

    What is still open #

    The conjecture itself, and everything geometric: nothing here constructs the Reeb bundle 𝓡_p, the colour-tube closure K_β, the JSJ decomposition of 𝓡_p \ ν(K_β), the Thurston volume, the Frobenius packing density ρ_F, or the Markov trace. They are a finite index set and two functions the caller supplies. The substantive bridge lives in QOU.PhiQJSJ.bridge.
