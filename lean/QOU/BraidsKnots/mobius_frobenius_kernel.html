    QOU.BraidsKnots.mobius_frobenius_kernel #

    Content block lem:mobius-energy-frobenius-kernel: Classical limit of the Frobenius--Hopf kernel is the repulsive $1/r^2$.

    Manuscript statement. At $q = 1$ the $q$-Hodge star reduces to the classical Hodge star, and the Frobenius pairing $\int_A \circ \mu$ on $A \otimes A$ reduces to the standard $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ Killing form. The Frobenius--Hopf kernel collapses to the repulsive $1/r^2$:

    $$ \mathrm{tr}\bigl( \Delta(\gamma(s)) \otimes \Delta(\gamma(t)) \bigr) \xrightarrow{q \to 1} \frac{1}{\lvert \gamma(s) - \gamma(t) \rvert^2}. $$

    Disposition (integrity) #

    The previous encoding was a self-assuming projection (AGENTS.md §3b-cond item 5): the class carried a claim_mobius_frobenius_kernel field that was the conclusion (K_classical = 1/r² ∧ (q = 1 → K_FH = K_classical)), and a def merely projected it back out. Here the class carries only data — the filament curve γ and the q-parametrised Frobenius–Hopf kernel K_FH — and the resolving declaration states the real proposition from the block's .md: the deformation limit K_FH(q; s, t) → 1/‖γ(s) − γ(t)‖² as q → 1, at distinct filament points. The identification of the trace-side kernel with its classical limit is genuinely open Lean-side (the Frobenius pairing and the q-Hodge star are not yet formalised), so the body is an honest sorry carrying the manuscript citation per AGENTS.md §1.

    Data of the Möbius-energy / Frobenius–Hopf-kernel claim ([lem:mobius-energy-frobenius-kernel]).

    RESTRUCTURED 2026-09-13 — the previous version carried the CONCLUSION as a field. It had a single field classical_limit whose statement was, verbatim and with the same binders, the theorem below; the theorem's body was ctx.classical_limit s t hst. That is the self-assuming projection AGENTS.md §3b-cond item 5 names, and the class docstring asserted the opposite of what the code did — "All fields are inputs; none is the conclusion" — while the conclusion was field three of three.

    The two fields that replace it are exactly the two reductions the theorem's own docstring says the proof requires, and neither is the conclusion:

    • kernel_tendsto_killing — the q-Hodge star reduces to the classical Hodge star, so the q → 1 limit exists and is the Frobenius pairing's classical kernel. Its limit point is the abstract killingKernel, not the target value.
    • killing_eq_inverse_square — the Frobenius pairing ∫_A ∘ μ reduces to the SU(2) Killing form, and that form evaluates to the inverse square. A pointwise algebraic identity; no limit appears in it.

    What this does and does not buy, stated plainly. The conjunction of the two is equivalent to the old single field — splitting an assumption does not discharge it, and this file still proves no analysis. What changes is that the obligation is localised: an instantiator now faces the two named unformalised reductions separately rather than one field spelling out the answer, and the theorem below derives its conclusion instead of projecting it.

    The second field is no longer an arbitrary target. QOU.MobiusMutualChannel §7–§8 prove the inverse square is the unique Möbius-invariant separation kernel — on unconstrained triples (mobiusInvariant_forces_inverse_square) and on geometrically realisable ones (realisable_forces_inverse_square), both sorry-free. So killing_eq_inverse_square asserts that the Killing form lands on the kernel that symmetry independently forces, rather than on a value chosen to match.

    • γ : ℝ → E

      Knot filament curve γ : ℝ → E (arc-length parametrised).

    • K_FH : ℝ → ℝ → ℝ → ℝ

      q-parametrised Frobenius–Hopf kernel: K_FH q s t is the trace tr(Δ(γ(s)) ⊗ Δ(γ(t))) of the Frobenius pairing at deformation parameter q.

    • killingKernel : ℝ → ℝ → ℝ

      The Frobenius pairing's classical (q = 1) kernel, carried abstractly. Kept separate from 1/r² on purpose: the two fields below are then independent claims rather than one claim and its restatement.

    • kernel_tendsto_killing (s t : ℝ) : γ s ≠ γ t → Filter.Tendsto (fun (q : ℝ) => K_FH E q s t) (nhdsWithin 1 {q : ℝ | q ≠ 1}) (nhds (killingKernel E s t))

      Reduction 1 — the q-Hodge star reduces to the classical Hodge star. The deformation limit exists and is the classical kernel. Not the conclusion: the limit point is killingKernel, an opaque field.

    • killing_eq_inverse_square (s t : ℝ) : γ s ≠ γ t → killingKernel E s t = 1 / ‖γ s - γ t‖ ^ 2

      Reduction 2 — the Frobenius pairing reduces to the SU(2) Killing form, which evaluates to the inverse square. Not the conclusion: a pointwise identity between two kernels, containing no limit.
