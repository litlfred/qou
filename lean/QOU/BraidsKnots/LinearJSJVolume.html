    The theorem and the conjecture, kept apart #

    The linear volume law Vol(𝒦(Z,N)) = Z·Vol_p + N·Vol_n used to be a single axiom JSJ_volume_additivity carrying a Thurston citation. That conflated two very different inputs, and the citation covered only one of them.

    The content block conj:jsj-decomposition-linearity already draws the line correctly, and this file now matches it:

    "Additivity of hyperbolic volume over the pieces of the geometric decomposition along essential tori is a theorem of 3-manifold topology (Thurston). The conjectural content therefore lies entirely in the decomposition hypothesis: that the atomic knot complement JSJ-decomposes along the inter-nucleon tori into exactly Z proton and N neutron hyperbolic pieces, each carrying an isotope-independent volume quantum."

    Thurston gives additivity over whatever the JSJ pieces happen to be. He does not say the pieces are Z copies of one block and N copies of another, nor that their volumes are isotope-independent constants — that is the QOU claim, and it is open. Labelling the combined statement [thurston1978] made an open conjecture of this framework read as a cited external theorem. Bean qou-t7zg.

    So: AtomicJSJDecomposition is the conjecture (a §3b-cond class, no instance — the honest open state per §3b-tax), and the three consequences below now take it as an explicit [Instance] hypothesis. They are unchanged in content and still fully verified — conditional on the named hypothesis rather than on a mislabelled axiom.

    class QOU.BraidsKnots.AtomicJSJDecomposition :
    Prop

    The decomposition hypothesis (conjectural; conj:jsj-decomposition-linearity).

    The atomic knot complement 𝒦(Z,N) JSJ-decomposes along the inter-nucleon essential tori into exactly Z proton pieces and N neutron pieces, each carrying an isotope-independent volume quantum. Given Thurston's additivity over the pieces, this yields the linear law.

    This is the open part. It is a primary conjecture carrier: it takes no other conjecture class as a hypothesis, and no instance is provided.

    • volume_linear (Z N : ℕ) : Vol (AtomicKnot Z N) = ↑Z * Vol_p + ↑N * Vol_n

      The linear volume law that the decomposition hypothesis delivers, via Thurston additivity over the Z + N pieces.

      theorem QOU.BraidsKnots.atomic_volume_additive [AtomicJSJDecomposition] (Z N Z' N' : ℕ) :
      Vol (AtomicKnot (Z + Z') (N + N')) = Vol (AtomicKnot Z N) + Vol (AtomicKnot Z' N')

      Cluster additivity. Combining two nucleon clusters adds their volumes (hence, under the mass functor, the leading m_qCLT_approx masses). A genuine consequence of the decomposition hypothesis — this is the structural content the m_qCLT_approx channel relies on, not a restatement of the hypothesis.
