    QOU.BraidKnot.ZeqNRhoCorrection #

    Z=N substrate-q correction to the Kashaev law (axiomatised as a typeclass) #

    For light atoms on the doubly-symmetric line Z = N = k, the integer Kashaev binding-energy law B = ρ_struct · m_e · Vol_hyp(K_A) acquires a substrate-q correction at finite q of the form

    B(q) = m_e · Vol_hyp(K_A) · (ρ_struct + A − [A]_q)

    where [A]_q = (q^A − q^(−A))/(q − q^(−1)) is the q-integer and A = 2k. At q = 1: [A]_q = A, recovering the classical integer Kashaev law.

    This is conjecture-grade: the empirical regularity holds at 10⁻³ precision for ²H, ⁴He, ¹²C, ¹⁶O (per propagation through α-cluster). The Lean class below axiomatises the conjecture; any downstream theorem that depends on it takes [ZeqNRhoCorrection A K] as a typeclass hypothesis (matches the declaration order class ZeqNRhoCorrection (A : ℕ) (K : KnotAssignment A)) and is then fully verified by Lean — the only unproved input is the class instance.

        class QOU.BraidKnot.ZeqNRhoCorrection (A : ℕ) (K : KnotAssignment A) :
        Type

        Z=N ρ-correction conjecture (axiomatised).

        Encodes the assertion that for Z = N = k light atoms, the binding-energy formula at finite substrate q takes the form

        B(q) = m_e · Vol_hyp(K_A) · (ρ_struct + A − [A]_q).

        The class is parametrised by the atom mass number A (the conjecture's empirical regime is the doubly-symmetric A = 2k line; the class itself does not enforce evenness — downstream callers should restrict to even A or supply a separate Even A hypothesis) and the canonical knot assignment K_A. Instances supply:

        • bindingEnergy q — the binding energy at substrate q.
        • classical_limit — recovery of the integer Kashaev law at q = 1.
        • q_correction — the conjectural finite-q form.

        The q_correction field is the conjectural input: providing it is the role of mathematicians (not Lean). Downstream theorems consuming [ZeqNRhoCorrection A K] are conditional on it.

        • m_e : ℝ

          Mass of the electron in MeV — a numerical constant.

        • bindingEnergy (q : ℝ) : ℝ

          The binding energy at substrate q, predicted by the formula.

        • classical_limit : bindingEnergy K 1 = ↑K.rho_struct * m_e K * K.vol_hyp

          Classical limit: at q = 1 recovers the integer Kashaev law.

        • q_correction (q : ℝ) : bindingEnergy K q = m_e K * K.vol_hyp * (↑K.rho_struct + ↑A - qIntBal q A)

          The conjecture itself: the binding energy at finite q equals the Z=N substrate-q-corrected form. This is the field that plays the role of sorry for the conjecture.
