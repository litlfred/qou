    Identity I, content decomposition — Lean formalization (partial) #

    Companion to docs/audits/2026-06-23-keystone-identity-I-content-decomposition.md and WLambdaDefectClosedForm.lean. Identity I — the one carried representation- theoretic input to the keystone W_λ defect cubic 144·C(n) = −n(n−1)(2n−13) — is

    M(n) := (1/n!) Σ_{λ⊢n} dim(λ)² Σ_{c∈λ} h_c²  =  n(3n−1)/2 ,

    and it factors without the Frame–Robinson–Thrall hook-length formula as M(n) = binom(n,2) + n² via

    (A) per-partition Σ_c h_c² = Σ_c c(c)² + n² (a single-Young-diagram fact), and (B) the Jucys–Murphy / Plancherel content-square mean E_Planch[Σ c²] = binom(n,2).

    Mathlib (as of the pinned toolchain) carries no hook-length, content, or Jucys–Murphy infrastructure, so this file builds the needed combinatorics from scratch. Every declaration below is sorry-free:

    • (A) is given a self-contained computable model (partition = weakly-decreasing List ℕ), machine-checked on all partitions n ≤ 9 (kernel decide), and the general statement is proved outright (sumSqHooks_eq_sumSqContents_add_sq). The proof expands the hook square h² = P²+Q²+1+2PQ−2P−2Q (P = λ_i−j the arm+1, Q = colLen j−i the leg+1), then discharges each of the nine cell sums by a row form: the P terms by row reflection, the Q terms by the Fubini cell-transpose (cell_fubini) then the K-moment row forms, and the PQ cross term by the λ-weighted min-sum (cellAC_eq, source of the + n²) and the j-weighted K2 (cellBC_eq). The nine rows then collapse to the content cubic by ring.
    • (B) is faithfully stated and machine-checked. Its identity-coefficient core Σ_{k<n} k = binom(n,2) — the coefficient of 1 in the central Jucys–Murphy element Σ_k X_k² — is proved outright (jm_identity_coeff_two); and the full Plancherel-mean identity Σ_{λ⊢n} dim(λ)² Σ_c c(c)² = n!·binom(n,2), stated combinatorially via the Frame–Robinson–Thrall hook-length dimension dim(λ) = n!/∏h_c (needing no ℂ[S_n] Wedderburn / Jucys–Murphy infrastructure), is verified by kernel decide for all partitions n ≤ 12 (idB_verified_le12), the dimension itself certified by the RSK sum-of-squares Σ_λ dim(λ)² = n! (dim_validated_le12).

    Base ring ℤ throughout; nothing archimedean (no q₀, no 210 ppb). No fit.

                      Hook side of (A): arm + leg pieces, closed form (sorry-free) #

                      Writing the hook as h(c) = a(c) + l(c) + 1 expands the hook square into the six terms h² = a² + l² + 1 + 2·a·l + 2·a + 2·l.
