    The braided quantum dimension of the unit collapses to the identity. For the exact pairing 𝟙_C ⊣ Y of the monoidal unit with any right dual Y, the braided (twistless) trace η_{𝟙,Y} ≫ β_{𝟙,Y} ≫ ε_{𝟙,Y} equals 𝟙_{𝟙_C}. The braiding of the unit is a pair of unitors (braiding_tensorUnit_left), and the snake identity (ExactPairing.evaluation_coevaluation'), whiskered by 𝟙_C (conjugation by the right unitor), forces the composite to be the identity. (RightRigidCategory is omitted — only the unit's exact pairing is needed.)

    theorem QOU.BraidKnot.Ribbon.qDim_unit {C : Type u_1} [CategoryTheory.Category.{u_2, u_1} C] [CategoryTheory.MonoidalCategory C] [CategoryTheory.BraidedCategory C] [CategoryTheory.RightRigidCategory C] :
    RibbonMTC.qDimObj (CategoryTheory.MonoidalCategoryStruct.tensorUnit C) = CategoryTheory.CategoryStruct.id (CategoryTheory.MonoidalCategoryStruct.tensorUnit C)

    The categorical quantum dimension of the monoidal unit (the vacuum object) is 1. qDimObj (𝟙_C) = 𝟙 (𝟙_C). This is the categorical fact behind the RT-input normalization RibbonMTCInput.vac_dim_one: when the vacuum label's simple object is the unit, its dimension is genuinely the identity endomorphism of 𝟙_C, not merely a posited scalar. It is also the trivial case of conjugation symmetry — dim(𝟙) = 1 = dim(𝟙ᘁ).
