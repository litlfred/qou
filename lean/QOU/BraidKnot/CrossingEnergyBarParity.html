    QOU.BraidKnot.CrossingEnergyBarParity #

    Which involution the words bar-even and bar-odd are computed against, for the two crossing-energy channels.

    Context #

    def:bar-involution is the ring anti-involution on H_n(q) given by bar σᵢ = σᵢ⁻¹ and bar q = q⁻¹. It has two independently-applicable halves, and different corpus blocks silently apply different ones:

    • the coefficient half q ↦ q⁻¹, holding the diagram fixed;
    • the diagram half σᵢ ↦ σᵢ⁻¹ — geometrically the mirror, exchanging positive and negative crossings, i.e. p ↔ m.

    obs:crossing-energy-decomposition splits E_cross = p·q - m·q⁻¹ into E_sym = (p - m)·cosh λ and E_asym = (p + m)·sinh λ. Its weights are described by their parity under the coefficient half (cosh even, sinh odd), while its item 2 describes the channel totals by their behaviour under the mirror. Those two descriptions disagree, which has read as an error in one of them.

    What is proved here #

    Each channel, under each half and under the composite:

    coefficient half diagram half (mirror) full involution
    eSym even (eSym_coef) odd (eSym_mirror) odd (eSym_bar)
    eAsym odd (eAsym_coef) even (eAsym_mirror) odd (eAsym_bar)

    The two consequences that matter:

    • eSym_mirror together with eSym_coef shows the disagreement is not an error: the same summand is coefficient-even and mirror-odd, because the mirror flips the multiplicity p - m as well as the weight.
    • bar_neg_of_both — under the full involution, the one def:bar-involution actually defines, both channels are negated. So the parity words do not distinguish the channels there; they distinguish them only against a named half. This is why the notation register must name the half rather than say "bar" unqualified.

    Multiplicities are taken in the coefficient ring rather than in ℕ, which is both more general and avoids natural-number subtraction in p - m.

    Base ring: a general Field R per AGENTS §7c — the content is purely algebraic, needing only inverses and 2 ≠ 0 for the halving. No ordering, no Real.sqrt, no archimedean hypothesis; λ = log q and the reading of the weights as cosh/sinh belong past the substrate wall and play no role in any statement below.

    Companion compute: computations/bar_parity_involution_atlas_probe.py (witness bar-parity-involution-atlas.witness.json), which additionally places the spin-involution sectors (grading D) on the coefficient half and checks that the full involution fixes both Hecke idempotents (grading B).

              theorem QOU.BraidKnot.bar_neg_of_both {R : Type u_1} [Field R] (p m q : R) :
              eSym m p q⁻¹ = -eSym p m q ∧ eAsym m p q⁻¹ = -eAsym p m q

              The full bar involution does not separate the two channels: it negates both.

              Hence "bar-even" and "bar-odd" cannot be read against def:bar-involution itself — against that involution the two channels have the same parity. The words are meaningful only relative to a named half, which is what the notation-register disambiguation records.
