    QOU.BraidKnot.AJFigureEightAnnihilation #

    The general-N annihilation, proved — StrongAJFigureEight is now sorry-free (bean udmw, assembly tranche).

    This file discharges the one remaining sorry of the AJ-in-QOU tranche: the explicit order-2 operator (figureEightOperator) annihilates the colored Jones tower of 4₁ at every q ≠ 0 — including roots of unity. The proof is the qZeilberger certificate telescope (computations/aj_figure_eight_certificate.py), reassembled division-free: the rational certificate's (q^k − q^N)(q^k − q^{N+1}) denominators vanish at the tower boundary and at roots of unity, so the telescoping sequence certW is defined through the split products of AJFigureEightTelescope with divisions only by powers of q, x = q^N and the nonvanishing clearing product clearD. The B-side shift denominators are consumed exactly by factors the operator itself supplies (a₂ ∋ (xq²−1), a₁ ∋ (xq−1)², the A-shift ∋ (qx−1)) — the structural reason the telescope closes without genericity hypotheses.

    Every identity transcribed here is pre-verified exactly in computations/aj_figure_eight_telescope_assembly_check.py (master local identity [K3], boundary W(0) = −rhs [W0], the N = 0 instance [N0], and a full simulation of this file's certW telescope for N = 1..6 [SIM]).

    • figureEightOperator_annihilates — the summed telescope, all N, all q ≠ 0.
