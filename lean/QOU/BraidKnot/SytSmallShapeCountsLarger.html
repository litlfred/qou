    Tabulated c_λ for the larger self-conjugate shapes → {3, 8, 10} #

    Completes the tabulated c_λ of prop:symbolic-character-table (bean qou-tqab) begun in SytSmallShapeCounts (which pinned [2,1] → c = 1). Now that the hook-length / enumeration count (numSYTenum_eq_numStandardYoungTableaux, all n, sorry-free) has landed, the remaining three self-conjugate shapes are pinned by the forward route — no explicit tableau construction:

    • #SYT(μ) via the computable enumeration numSYTenum (decide) + the bridge to the structure count numStandardYoungTableaux = Nat.card (StandardYoungTableau);
    • Fintype.card (StandardYoungTableau μ) = #SYT(μ) (Nat.card_eq_fintype_card);
    • c_λ = |R_row| = #SYT(μ)/2 via two_mul_card_rowClass_eq_card (self-conjugacy).
    shape #SYT c_λ = #SYT/2
    [3,1,1] 6 3
    [3,2,1] 16 8
    [4,1,1,1] 20 10

    matching the manuscript's [3,1,1] → 3, [3,2,1] → 8, [4,1,1,1] → 10.

    -- Ref: [sagan2001] The Symmetric Group, §3.10–3.11 (hook length formula)
