    QOU.BraidKnot.RootOfUnityMTCTruncation — root-of-unity MTC (conjecture) #

    Class axiomatisation of [conj:root-of-unity-mtc-truncation]: at q = e^{2πi/k}, the cyclotomic-KLR quotient becomes finite-dimensional, the Kashiwara crystal truncates to partitions with at most k - 1 columns, and the resulting category of modules is a Modular Tensor Category MTC_k whose simple objects are non-abelian anyons.

    Takes [HeckeAnyonicSubstrate R q] and [CrystalGraphRealization R q] as upstream inputs. Specialised to a fixed truncationLevel k ≥ 2 encoding the chosen primitive root of unity.

    LaTeX: \label{conj:root-of-unity-mtc-truncation}

    References:

    • Kitaev, A. (2006).
    • Nayak, C. et al. (2008).
    • Bakalov, B. & Kirillov, A. A. (2001). Lectures on Tensor Categories and Modular Functors. AMS University Lecture Series 21.
    • Khovanov, M. & Lauda, A. D. (2009).
    • Kashiwara, M. (1991).

    Type u

    Root-of-unity MTC truncation (conjecture).

    At q = e^{2πi/k}, the KLR algebra truncates to an MTC MTC_k whose simple objects (non-abelian anyons) are labelled by the truncated crystal nodes (partitions with at most k - 1 columns).

    • k_ge_two : 2 ≤ k

      Truncation level k ≥ 2.

    • inTruncation : CrystalGraphRealization.CrystalNode q → Prop

      Predicate: a crystal node is supported on a partition with at most k - 1 columns. These are the simple MTC_k objects.

    • columnCount : CrystalGraphRealization.CrystalNode q → ℕ

      Number of columns of the partition labelling a crystal node.

    • inTruncation_iff (v : CrystalGraphRealization.CrystalNode q) : inTruncation k v ↔ columnCount k v < k

      A node is in the truncation iff its column count is < k.

    • truncation_holds (T : CrystalGraphRealization.NormalForm q) : inTruncation k (CrystalGraphRealization.nodeOf T)

      The conjecture itself: every GS normal form at q = e^{2πi/k} has nodeOf T in the truncated component. Plays the role of sorry.

      def QOU.BraidKnot.RootOfUnityMTCTruncation.canonical (R : Type u) [CommRing R] (q : R) [C : CrystalGraphRealization R q] :
      RootOfUnityMTCTruncation R q 2

      The trivial model — a def, not an instance, and not "faithful".

      Its docstring said "Faithful instance. Carries the explicit research-grade conjecture as a sorry." Neither half was true. There is no sorry: it sets columnCount _ := 0 and inTruncation _ := (0 < 2), a constantly-true predicate that does not mention its argument, so inTruncation_iff is (0 < 2) ↔ (0 < 2) by decide and truncation_holds is 0 < 2. Every node is in the truncation because the truncation is everything and every partition was declared to have zero columns. A sorry would at least be visible to #print axioms; this was not.

      As an unconditional instance it made columnCount_nodeOf_lt_k — the bound the MTC truncation pipeline is supposed to consume — an unconditional theorem reading 0 < 2.

      Note it only ever covered k = 2; general k has no model at all, so the rank of the truncated MTC is not exhibited anywhere in this corpus.

      Demoted the same way as ConfinementGradingCorrespondence.canonical (bean qou-fngs). Nothing consumed it: the class has no users outside this file.

      Still open: conj:root-of-unity-mtc-truncation — that at q = e^{2πi/k} the KLR algebra truncates to MTC_k with simple objects labelled by partitions of at most k − 1 columns.
