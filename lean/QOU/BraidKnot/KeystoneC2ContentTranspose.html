    Keystone C2 — transpose-invariance of the content & hook sums (Σ-core of W_λ = W_{λ'}) #

    The s5-so3 keystone's necessary condition C2 is the conjugation symmetry of the q-Plancherel weight W_λ = d_q(λ)²/[n]_q!, i.e. W_λ = W_{λ'} under transpose λ ↦ λ'. On the QOU side this is the chirality flip (content c(i,j) = j − i negates under transpose), so C2 says the weight is chirality-blind.

    KeystoneChiralityInvolution.lean machine-checks C2 at the cell / example level (the single-cell cellContent_transpose_neg, the self-conjugate electron (3,1,1)). The global statement is the transpose-invariance of the symmetric content / hook data — which this file establishes at the Σ-level for both the squared-content and squared-hook sums, in the same self-contained List ℕ model as IdentityIContentDecomposition (no Mathlib.YoungDiagram), reusing that file's column-length duality and cell_fubini transpose.

    • conj — the transpose of a partition in the List ℕ model: row j of the conjugate is the j-th column length colLen l j. (For a weakly-decreasing l this is exactly MathlibExt.QPartitionCombinatorics.Partition.conjugate, whose mapping function (l.filter (· > j)).length is definitionally colLen l j.)
    • sumSqContents_conj — the content-square sum is transpose-invariant: Σ_c c(c)² (λ') = Σ_c c(c)² (λ). Since content negates under transpose and squaring kills the sign, the per-cell summand is preserved and cell_fubini matches the cell sets. This is the chirality-blindness of Σ c² — the Σ-core of C2 for the content moment that the keystone defect cubic and the Plancherel mean are built from.
    • colLen_conj + sumSqHooks_conj — the hook-square sum is transpose-invariant: Σ_c h_c² (λ') = Σ_c h_c² (λ). The hook is transpose-symmetric (h_λ(i,j) = h_{λ'}(j,i)), via the column-length involution colLen (conj l) c = λ_c. This is the moment that feeds d_q(λ) and hence W_λ = W_{λ'} directly, so the two results together give the full Σ-core of C2.
