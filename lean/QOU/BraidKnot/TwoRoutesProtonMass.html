                        theorem QOU.BraidKnot.TwoRoutes.averaged_crossing_squared_scalar (q : ℝ) (hq : q ≠ 0) (σ : ℝ) (hecke : σ ^ 2 = (q - 1 / q) * σ + 1) :
                        (σ - (q - 1 / q) / 2) ^ 2 = 1 + (q - 1 / q) ^ 2 / 4

                        The averaged crossing operator σ_avg = σ − h/2 satisfies σ_avg² = 1 + h²/4 = [2]_q²/4 (SCALAR — identity matrix).

                        Proof: from the Hecke relation σ² = hσ + 1: (σ − h/2)² = σ² − hσ + h²/4 = (hσ+1) − hσ + h²/4 = 1 + h²/4. QED

                        Consequence: even powers of the averaged crossing are SCALAR (no operator content). Odd powers are linear in σ. For n interface crossings: n even: (σ−h/2)ⁿ = (1+h²/4)^{n/2} · I (pure scalar) n odd: (σ−h/2)ⁿ = (1+h²/4)^{(n−1)/2} · (σ−h/2) (linear)

                        This means the interface's quadratic terms factor out as a universal scalar, and only ONE effective crossing carries the operator-valued binding information.
