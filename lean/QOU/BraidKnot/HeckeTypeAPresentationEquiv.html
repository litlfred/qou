    Two of the presentations are the same algebra #

    The tree holds several Iwahori–Hecke presentations (bean qou-img9). This file removes one from the pile by proving two of them equal rather than documenting that they coexist:

    • QOU.IwahoriHecke.TypeA.IwahoriHeckeOfSn K q (n+1) — HeckeAlgebra at the type-A Coxeter matrix, i.e. RingQuot (FreeAlgebra K (Fin n)) modulo a relation carrying the quadratic family and one altProd family covering braid and far-commutation together;
    • QOU.Hecke.IwahoriHecke K q n — RingQuot (FreeAlgebra K (Fin n)) modulo a relation with the three families written separately.

    Same carrier, same generators, same relations in a different syntactic shape. The equivalence is built from the two universal properties, so neither construction is privileged.

    Why this is worth having #

    QOU/Hecke/TwBasisField.lean proves finrank_eq_factorial — dim H = (n+1)! — against the second presentation. Its author flagged, correctly, that transporting it to the first was unproved and not claimed. algEquiv below is that transport: an AlgEquiv carries a basis and a finrank, so the dimension result reaches IwahoriHeckeOfSn and everything built on it.

    Index bookkeeping #

    IwahoriHecke K q n has Fin n generators and is H_{n+1}(q); IwahoriHeckeOfSn K q m has Fin (m-1) generators. So the match is at m = n+1, where Fin (m-1) reduces to Fin n definitionally.

          The transport #

          QOU/Hecke/TwBasisField.lean proves finrank_eq_factorial against the sibling presentation, and its author flagged — correctly — that carrying it to this one was unproved. algEquiv is the mechanism; these two are the carry. An AlgEquiv is in particular a linear equivalence, so it moves both the dimension and the basis.
