    Keystone gate is EXACT on the self-conjugate sector — no archimedean continuation #

    BWVacuumRowConcrete.lean de-opaques the keystone gate bwS0_eq_hecke into the concrete RT/Seifert vacuum row bwS0Concrete = S₀·θ^f and shows (bwS0Concrete_eq_S0_forces_trivial_twist) that the gate bwS0Concrete = S₀ holds iff the framing twist is trivial, θ_λ^f = 1. At real q₀ > 1 that is false off the self-conjugate blocks (θ_λ = q₀^{c(λ)} is not a root of unity for c(λ) ≠ 0), leaving the residue as a fibre root-of-unity continuation problem — the standing open piece of bwS0_eq_hecke (ledger docs/coordination/s5-so3-keystone.md; the 2026-07-11 general Subgoal-2 reversal makes it open-for-all, no owner gate).

    This file closes that residue on the self-conjugate sector, exactly and without any continuation.

    The observation #

    KeystoneModularTwist.contentSum_conjugate proves the content sum is transpose-antisymmetric, c(λ′) = − c(λ). On a self-conjugate block (λ′ = λ) this forces c(λ) = − c(λ), i.e. c(λ) = 0 over ℤ. Hence the twist θ_λ = q^{c(λ)} = q^0 = 1 for every q (no q₀ > 1, no root-of-unity fibre needed), so θ_λ^f = 1 for every framing f, and bwS0Concrete_of_twist_pow_one gives the gate exactly:

    bwS0Concrete (heckeVerlindeData …) (theta q) f λ = S₀_λ for all f, all q, self-conjugate λ.

    The framing obstruction that refutes the gate off-diagonal is structurally absent on the self-conjugate sector — there is nothing to continue. The physical electron block λ = [3,1,1] (c = 0+1+2−1−2 = 0, self-conjugate) is the flagship instance: its keystone value bwS0 = S₀ = d_q/D is exact at real q₀ with no archimedean continuation, d_q([3,1,1]) = [3]_q[4]_q/[2]_q (KeystoneMcKayValueMap.qFactorial_shape_311).

    Scope. This is a genuine, no-fit advance on the remaining open archimedean piece of bwS0_eq_hecke — it Lean-izes the "self-conjugate continuation is exact" ledger observation (#3701, previously only a Python probe). It does not close the gate on the non-self-conjugate blocks (where c(λ) ≠ 0, θ^f ≠ 1 at q₀ > 1): that fibre root-of-unity continuation stays open.

    Base ring ℝ (§7c): bwS0Concrete/S₀ live over ℝ (D = √(Σ d_q²)); the content-sum algebra is over ℤ exponents.
