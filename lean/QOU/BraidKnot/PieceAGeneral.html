    Piece A of Identity I — general-n (Mathlib YoungDiagram, HLF-free) #

    The per-partition hook–content identity

    Σ_{c∈μ} hook(c)² = Σ_{c∈μ} content(c)² + n²,   n = |μ|,

    for every Young diagram μ (docs/audits/2026-08-01-keystone-identity-I-piece-A-general-proof.md, bean qou-o5xj). This is the HLF-free half of Identity I 2M(n)=n(3n−1), the carried input to the keystone defect closed form 144·C(n)=−n(n−1)(2n−13) (WLambdaDefectClosedForm). No hook-length formula, no dimensions, no RSK.

    Stage 1 (this commit): definitions + the hook decomposition hook(i,j) = rowLen i + colLen j − i − j − 1 and cell non-negativity.

    Base ring (§7c) #

    All sums over ℤ (contents are signed); no ℝ, no archimedean input.

              Stage 5 — Piece A from the pair-counting identity #

              The remaining combinatorial core is the pair-counting identity 2Σ(arm·leg) + 2Σ(i·j) + 2A + 2L = card² − card (bean qou-o5xj): the four terms count, respectively, ordered UR/LL pairs, ordered UL/LR pairs, same-row and same-column ordered pairs — together every ordered pair of distinct cells. Given it, Piece A is immediate from the reduction.
