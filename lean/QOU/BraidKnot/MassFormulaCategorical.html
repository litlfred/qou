    QOU.BraidKnot.MassFormulaCategorical #

    The QOU mass formula M(K) = ρ(K) · m_e · |tr_M(T_w(K))| for the canonical Hecke element T_w(K) of knot/nucleus K.

    Mathematical content #

    Per prop:atom-knot-mass-derivation in the manuscript:

    M(K) = ρ(K) · m_e · |tr_M(T_w(K))|     (cat units)

    where:

    • ρ(K) ∈ ℕ_{≥1} is the SDP-multiplicity (per Wedderburn decomposition, ρ = ⌊dim_q(λ⋆)⌉ per conj:rho-prefactor-structural)
    • m_e is the electron mass (calibration anchor; cat units)
    • |tr_M(T_w)| is the absolute Markov trace at q_0

    Conversion to MeV:

    M(K)_MeV = M(K) · M_E_MEV_CODATA

    where M_E_MEV_CODATA = 0.51099895069 MeV (the only CAL input in the chain; everything else is derived).

    What this module provides #

    • categoricalMass — the M = ρ · m_e · |tr_M| formula at the abstract HeckeElement n level
    • categoricalMass_pos — the formula gives a strictly positive mass when ρ > 0 and tr_M ≠ 0
    • categoricalMassMeV — conversion to MeV

    Open Lean port #

    The Markov trace markovTrace n is currently axiomatised (QOU.HeckeAlgebra.Common) — there's no concrete T_w element to apply it to in Lean yet. This module's content sits at the schematic level: the FORMULA is type-correct, but plugging in real T_w values awaits the Hoefsmit-machinery port.
