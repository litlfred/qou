    The anti-involution — Graham–Lehrer axiom (C2) #

    A cellular algebra carries an anti-automorphism * with C^λ_{s,t}* = C^λ_{t,s}. For a Hecke algebra it is the map fixing every generator, and it exists because the defining relations are palindromic: reversing a word takes each relation to a relation.

    It is not an ornament. It is what makes the bilinear form on a cell module symmetric, and hence what lets the radical of that form cut out the simple head — so it is a prerequisite for Specht theory, not a corollary of it.

    Why type A specifically. For a general Coxeter matrix the reversal of altProd a b m is altProd a b m when m is odd and altProd b a m when m is even, so the general statement needs a parity lemma plus a chain of auxiliary word identities. In type A the matrix takes only the values 1, 2, 3, and at each the commutation the reversal needs is supplied by the relation itself:

    m reversal needs supplied by
    1 (i = j) nothing both sides are altProd _ _ 1
    2 (far) a·b = b·a the far-commutation relation
    3 (adjacent) a·b·a = b·a·b the braid relation

    So type A is a three-way case split with no parity argument — and type A is where Specht modules live anyway.

          Toward the Murphy basis: the rank-one x_λ #

          Murphy's basis is m_{st} = T_{d(s)}^* · x_λ · T_{d(t)}, where x_λ = Σ_{w ∈ S_λ} T_w is the sum over the Young subgroup. Its defining property is that it is a q-eigenvector for every generator of that subgroup: σ_i · x_λ = q · x_λ for s_i ∈ S_λ. Everything else in the construction is conjugation by coset representatives.

          The rank-one case — S_λ = ⟨s_i⟩, so x_λ = 1 + σ_i — is proved here, and it needs nothing but the quadratic relation: σ_i + σ_i² = q(1 + σ_i) is that relation rearranged. It is the base case of the whole construction and the one place where no basis machinery is required.

          The general case is DONE, and is not here — it is in QOU.BraidKnot.HeckeMurphy (corrected 2026-09-04). This paragraph used to read "that pairing argument over Equiv.Perm (Fin (n+1)) is the open work". It was superseded the same day it was written, by 5f38f5286b, and never updated.

          What is actually on main, sorry-free:

          • HeckeMurphy.σ_mul_Tw_pair — the pair identity σᵢ(T_w + T_{w sᵢ}) = q(T_w + T_{w sᵢ}), which is the sentence this paragraph called open;
          • HeckeMurphy.σ_mul_murphySum — σᵢ · x_S = q · x_S for any Finset of permutations closed under w ↦ w sᵢ, by Finset.sum_involution;
          • HeckeMurphy.σ_mul_murphySum_subgroup and murphySum_mul_σ_subgroup — the same on an arbitrary subgroup, both sides;
          • HeckeMurphyYoung.σ_mul_murphySum_young — the same for the row stabiliser of an actual tableau, i.e. the genuine x_λ.

          So the rank-one case below is the base case kept for its independent proof (the quadratic relation alone, no basis machinery), not the frontier.

          The real open input is Mathas' spanning lemma — see HeckeMurphyYoung.basisOfSpanning and the §9 note there, which reduces spanning to Lemma 3.14's straightening of row-standard pairs, and names the missing row-standard tableau type as the blocker.

          Why this correction is here. A stale "open work" pointer is worse than no pointer: it sends an agent to re-derive a merged result. This one did, twice in one session (PR #6626, closed as duplicate). Bean qou-phir.
