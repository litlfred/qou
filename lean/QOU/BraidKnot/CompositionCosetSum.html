    x_μ · T_e is the sum over a coset — the algebraic core of Mathas' Corollary 3.4 #

    Mathas 3.4 says M^μ = x_μ H is R-free on {x_μ T_d : d ∈ D_μ}. The identity that carries all of its content is the one proved here:

    `x_μ · T_e = Σ_{v ∈ S_μ} T_{e·v}`

    i.e. multiplying the Murphy element by a single basis element of the algebra produces the sum over one whole coset of the Young subgroup, with every coefficient 1. No q appears. Freeness is then a statement about disjoint cosets in the T_w basis, and Prop 3.3 (CompositionCosetExists) is what says the cosets partition S_n.

    Handedness — why e⁻¹ and not e #

    Two conventions have to be reconciled and neither is negotiable.

    • TwBasis.Tw_mul_Tw is flipped: T_v · T_w = T_{w·v} when the lengths add. That is forced by the handedness of orbitMap, not a typo (see its module docstring). So x_μ · T_e = Σ_v T_v · T_e = Σ_v T_{e·v} lands on the left coset e·S_μ.
    • CompositionMinimal.IsDistinguished is the right-coset notion: it gives ℓ(v·d) = ℓ(v) + ℓ(d) for v ∈ S_μ, which is the hypothesis for S_μ·d.

    The length hypothesis needed above is ℓ(e·v) = ℓ(e) + ℓ(v), and inverting (e·v)⁻¹ = v⁻¹·e⁻¹ converts one into the other, because ℓ is inverse-invariant and S_μ is a subgroup (so v⁻¹ ranges over it too). Hence the correct hypothesis on e is that e⁻¹ is distinguished — recorded as IsDistinguishedL. This is not a weakening: IsDistinguishedL c and IsDistinguished c are carried onto each other by e ↦ e⁻¹, so Prop 3.3 transports verbatim (existsUnique_isDistinguishedL).
