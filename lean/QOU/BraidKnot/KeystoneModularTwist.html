    Keystone modular data (ii): the ribbon twist / modular T-matrix θ_λ = q^{c(λ)} #

    KeystoneVerlindeSMatrixBridge.lean equips the keystone (Beasley–Witten localization) modular tensor category with the first genuine piece of modular data: the vacuum S-matrix row S_{0λ} (VerlindeModularData, weighting the Verlinde measure μ(λ) = S_{0λ}²). A modular tensor category carries two pieces of modular data: the S-matrix and the diagonal modular T-matrix / ribbon twist θ_λ. This file adds the second: the twist on a simple object.

    In a ribbon / modular category the twist on the simple V_λ is the topological framing anomaly θ_λ = q^{κ_λ}, and for the Hecke MTC the exponent is (up to convention) the content sum κ_λ = c(λ) = Σ_{(i,j)∈λ} (j − i) — the quadratic-Casimir eigenvalue on V_λ. We build, sorry-free:

    • theta q λ := q ^ (c(λ)) — the twist, as a zpow over a generic {R : Type*} [GroupWithZero R] (§7c: purely algebraic — no ordering, no Real.sqrt, no ℝ; strictly weaker base ring than the S-matrix side, which needed ℝ for D = √(Σ d_q²)).
    • theta_conj — the C2-analogue for the twist: θ_{λ'} = θ_λ⁻¹, the twist's chirality / bar-parity behaviour, complementing the S-matrix's qDim_conj (d_q(λ') = d_q(λ)). It follows from the signed content flip c(λ') = − c(λ) (transpose negates content), which this file proves in general — the corpus previously carried only the squared content invariance (KeystoneC2ContentTranspose.sumSqContents_conj) and the single [2] ↔ [1,1] instance (KeystoneChiralityInvolution.conjugate_two_content_flip), never the signed general statement.
    • ModularTwistData — a structure carrying the abstract twist datum theta + theta_conj, and heckeTwistData, the Hecke instance θ_λ = q^{c(λ)} mirroring heckeVerlindeData (note: needs no positivity hypothesis 1 < q, unlike the S-matrix data — another sign the twist is the purely-algebraic half of the modular data).
    • Consistency lemmas: the twist is trivial on the vacuum (theta q [] = 1, c(∅) = 0), and on the trivial / one-row rep [n] it is q^{c([n])} with c([n]) = Σ_{j<n} j = \binom{n}{2} (two_mul_contentSum_single_row, the triangular number — the symmetric-rep framing anomaly).

    Honest scope. This is a genuine formalize-first extension of the Verlinde MTC modular data — it makes the T-matrix explicit and proves its transpose law from the content-sum combinatorics. It does not prove the physics that the BW localization of S³/2I yields this modular category: that category identification is still owner-gated (bwS0_eq_hecke in KeystoneVerlindeSMatrixBridge.lean; narrow Subgoal-2 reversal #2503/#2524). No fit, no physics claim — only the algebra of the twist and its chirality behaviour.

    • [bakalovkirillov2001] Lectures on Tensor Categories and Modular Functors (S, T modular data).
    • [macdonald1995] Symmetric Functions and Hall Polynomials, I.1 (content, conjugate).

    The signed content sum is transpose-antisymmetric: c(λ') = − c(λ) #

    The corpus proves the squared content sum is transpose-INVARIANT (sumSqContents_conj); the twist needs the signed content sum, which is transpose-ANTISYMMETRIC. Both use the same cell_fubini row/column transpose of IdentityIContentDecomposition; here squaring is dropped, so the per-cell content sign survives and the sum negates.
