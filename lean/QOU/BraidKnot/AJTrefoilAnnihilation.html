    QOU.BraidKnot.AJTrefoilAnnihilation #

    The general-N trefoil annihilation, assembled from two strictly smaller cited cores (beans 6q9g + rz8b).

    Unlike 4₁ (order 2, genuine qZeilberger certificate), the trefoil annihilator is order 1 and the Rosso–Jones tower extends by a SINGLE term per color, so no telescoping certificate is needed: with P(n) = ∑_{j≤n} (−1)^j w^{6j(j+1)} [2j+1]_v the tower is J(n+1) = (−1)^n w^{−6n(n+2)} P(n)/[n+1]_v (coloredJonesTrefoil_eq_P_form, proved — all quadratic exponents cancel in the operator's ratios), and the annihilation reduces to two rational identities in (w, Y = (w²)^N) ([C1] coefficient cancellation, [C2] boundary), pre-verified exactly in computations/aj_trefoil_telescope_assembly_check.py and proved here inside the generic case.

    The degenerate colors — an honest finding #

    coloredJonesTrefoil divides by qInt (w²) N, which vanishes exactly when x = (w⁴)^N = 1 — there the Lean value is the division-by-zero convention 0, not the analytic value. The class field quantifies over ALL w ≠ 0, and it remains TRUE there because the operator's own coefficients vanish exactly at the junk slots (a₀ at x = 1, a₁ at xq = 1); the surviving term then needs the root-of-unity evaluation J = 1 at the neighboring color. The case split:

    • w⁴ = 1 — every qInt collapses and both sides vanish (proved);
    • generic (x ≠ 1, xq ≠ 1) — the [C1]/[C2] computation (proved);
    • x = 1 / xq = 1 — reduces to the two evaluation cores trefoil_eval_at_root_prev / trefoil_eval_at_root_next (J(N+1) = 1 when (w⁴)^N = 1, resp. J(N) = 1 when (w⁴)^{N+1} = 1) — root-of-unity periodicity of the Rosso–Jones sum, proved (sorry-free) via the genuine sum evaluation in QOU.BraidKnot.AJTrefoilRootEval (reflection + shift reindexings and a Gauss-sum block-cancellation collapse), and numerically pre-verified to 40 dps in the pre-verification script.
