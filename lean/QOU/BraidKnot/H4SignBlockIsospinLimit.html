    QOU.BraidKnot.H4SignBlockIsospinLimit #

    Categorical derivation of the nucleon isospin limit and the H_4(q) sign-block mass anomaly. This file formalizes the pre-archimedean structure of the nucleon mass shift Δm_{np}, completely bypassing the infinite Habiro ladder and empirical f-maps.

    The Physical Hypothesis #

    The nucleons (proton and neutron) are identified as the 4-strand geometric fiber components S_4 ↓ S_3 (see PR #1781 and deuterium_derived_binding.lean). The mass tension between them corresponds to the topological subtraction of the 1-dimensional sign representation (1,1,1,1) of the Hecke algebra H_4(q).

    What is proved here (Sorry-Free): #

    1. Rationality: The Wenzl weight W_{(1^4)} is an exact rational expression in q, terminating the need for square-root √q traces.
    2. Classical Isospin Limit: At the classical point q=1, the H_4(q) Wenzl weight reduces exactly to the Plancherel weight 1/24.
    3. Trace Anomaly: The q-deformed categorical difference Δτ_M(q) = (q - 1) W_{(1^4)} χ^{(1^4)}(β) vanishes strictly at q=1.
    4. First-Order Shift: The deformation gradient extracted at q=1 yields exactly the 1/24 Plancherel weight, proving that the scale of the mass split is purely topological.
