    The algebraic recast #

    The empirical (CAL-anchored) c₃ extractor (qbeta_utils.c3_target_at) is

    c₃(q) = (Q_codata / Q_N2(q) − 1) / u(q)³, Q_N2(q) = m_e · Q_A_over_me(q) · cos(α(q) − b(q)·ℏ_q(q)²/2) · (1 − u(q)²).

    Setting the S-channel-zero condition c₃(q*) = −Vol(4₁) and clearing denominators gives the closed-form defining equation

    Φ(q*) = Q_codata / m_e, where Φ(q) = Q_A_over_me(q) · cos(α(q) − b(q)·ℏ_q(q)²/2) · (1 − u(q)²) · (1 − Vol41·u(q)³).

    Φ is fully elementary in q (rational · √ · cos); the only transcendental constant it carries is the figure-eight hyperbolic volume Vol(4₁) = 2·Cl₂(π/3) = 2·Im Li₂(e^{iπ/3}), a Bloch–Wigner dilog value. No Faddeev / Habiro q-series enters the q-dependence.

    qstarShape below records this closed form (documenting the POSITIVE partial result: the shape is closed form). Vol41 is carried as a real parameter because the Clausen constant is not in Mathlib; it is the external dilog value above.

    The exclusion invariant (scope-(b)) #

    q* is the root of Φ(q) = R with R = Q_codata / m_e a ratio of two measured CODATA inputs. Numerically Φ'(q*) = −3.63 ≠ 0, sign-definite on a neighbourhood of q* (verified to 50 dps in the probe), so Φ is strictly monotone there. qstarRootStrictMonoInAnchor proves the mathematical consequence: the root strictly tracks the anchor (a larger measured R gives a strictly larger q*). A constant does not vary with its argument; therefore q* is not an experiment-independent constant — it moves with the neutron β-decay Q-value, with dq*/dQ_β = 1/(m_e·Φ'(q*)) = −0.539 (a falsifiable lever: a more precise Q_β sharpens q*).

    StrictMonoOn Φ I is the numerically-verified hypothesis (Φ' ≠ 0, sign-definite near q*); it is NOT proved in Lean here (that needs the full derivative computation). The theorem is the sorry-free implication "if Φ is strictly monotone then the root tracks the anchor" — the conditional-proved ≠ premise-established discipline applies: this proves the invariant's consequence, not the monotonicity premise (which the probe establishes numerically).
