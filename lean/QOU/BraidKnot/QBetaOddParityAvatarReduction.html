    QOU.BraidKnot.QBetaOddParityAvatarReduction #

    The odd-parity generating-function avatar for the Q_β c_k ladder reduces, at order u³, to a single scalar — the algebraic core of the qbeta_odd_parity_avatar_reduction_probe.py finding.

    The Q_β precision ladder F(u) = Π_{k≥2}(1 + c_k u^k) (u = b·ℏ_q²) has, as a power series at u = 0, two hard structural constraints:

    • c_1 = 0 — the ladder starts at k = 2 (no u¹ term);
    • c_2 = -1 — Lean-proved exact (q_beta_categorical_precision_ladder N=2 rung, Kashiwara f̃₁²), q-independent, and EVEN.

    A generating-function "avatar" of the cosh/cos family is EVEN in u, so its Taylor series has only even powers — the companion compute probe qbeta_cosh_avatar_crossanchor_multicoeff_probe.py used this to refute the entire even-parity slice (predicted c_3 = 0 ≠ cal-anchored ≈ −2.07).

    The natural fix is an ODD-parity extension. Writing such an avatar as F(u) = A(u) · G(u) with A the even baseline (cos(√2·u), whose truncation through u³ is 1 − u², giving c_2 = −1) and G the odd extension, the lowest odd term G can contribute while preserving c_1 = 0 is u³: G(u) = 1 + g·u³ + O(u⁴).

    This file proves the algebraic consequence: through order u³ the product carries c_2 = −1 independently of g, and c_3 = g exactly. Hence an odd-parity closure of c_3 requires a closed-form g(q) matching the cal-anchored c_3 target — which is exactly the (already saturated) single-formula c_3 search. The generating-function reframing opens no new c_3 search space.

    These are the EXACT (truncated-polynomial) coefficients: cos(√2·u) contributes nothing at u³ (it is even), so the through-u³ truncation 1 − u² of the baseline is faithful for c_2 and c_3. The baseline's higher even terms (u⁴, …) affect c_4 and up, not c_2 / c_3, so they are correctly outside the scope of this reduction.

    Proof: one-line ring identities over any CommRing.

    theorem QOU.BraidKnot.qbeta_odd_parity_avatar_product {R : Type u_1} [CommRing R] (a2 g u : R) :
    (1 + a2 * u ^ 2) * (1 + g * u ^ 3) = 1 + a2 * u ^ 2 + g * u ^ 3 + a2 * g * u ^ 5

    Odd-parity avatar product (general even baseline). The product of an even baseline 1 + a₂·u² (carrying the u² coefficient a₂, and no u¹/u³ term — the through-u³ truncation of an even function) with the minimal odd extension 1 + g·u³ expands exactly as

    (1 + a₂u²)(1 + g u³) = 1 + a₂u² + g u³ + a₂ g u⁵.

    Reading coefficients: the u² coefficient is a₂ (contains no g), and the u³ coefficient is g. There is no u⁴ term; the only higher term is a₂ g u⁵.

    theorem QOU.BraidKnot.qbeta_odd_parity_c3_content_eq_g {R : Type u_1} [CommRing R] (a2 g u : R) :
    (1 + a2 * u ^ 2) * (1 + g * u ^ 3) - (1 + a2 * u ^ 2) - a2 * g * u ^ 5 = g * u ^ 3

    The odd-parity content collapses to g·u³. Subtracting the even baseline 1 + a₂u² and the residual a₂ g u⁵ from the product leaves exactly g·u³: the entire order-u³ (c_3) content is the single scalar g, independent of the baseline coefficient a₂.

    theorem QOU.BraidKnot.qbeta_odd_parity_avatar_reduction {R : Type u_1} [CommRing R] (g u : R) :
    (1 - u ^ 2) * (1 + g * u ^ 3) = 1 - u ^ 2 + g * u ^ 3 - g * u ^ 5

    Reduction at the c_2 = −1 baseline. With a₂ = −1 (the cos(√2·u) baseline truncated through u³), the avatar product is

    (1 − u²)(1 + g u³) = 1 − u² + g u³ − g u⁵,

    so the u² coefficient is −1 (independent of g, matching the proved N=2 rung) and the u³ coefficient is exactly g. A non-fitted closure of c_3 therefore demands a closed-form g(q) reproducing the cal-anchored c_3 target — the saturated single-formula search.
