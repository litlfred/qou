    QOU.BraidKnot.SpechtCentralC2Scalar — C₂ acts on every Specht block by a scalar #

    Toward the SnIsotypicSpechtSplit instance (epic qou-nrex, follow-on to brick 42).

    spechtProduct — the map the Wedderburn split is built from — sends C₂ to the family of scalars the keystone names:

    spechtProduct_centralC2 — spechtProduct n (C₂ n) i = (contentSqSum i.diagram) • 1.

    Three steps, each closing a gap between how the eigenvalue lemma is stated and how the split uses it:

    • asAlgebraHom_comp — a representation precomposed with a group hom acts through the induced map on the monoid algebra. Proved by MonoidAlgebra.algHom_ext: two algebra homs agreeing on the group elements agree.
    • mapDomain_centralC2 (previous file) — so the induced map carries C₂ n to C₂ (nOf μ), which is the index the eigenvalue lemma lives at.
    • spechtRep_asAlgebraHom_coe — the Specht representation is the restriction of the ambient Mtab one, and restriction commutes with the algebra hom. MonoidAlgebra.induction_on does it: the claim is additive and k-homogeneous, so it suffices on group elements, where it is LinearMap.restrict_apply.

    centralC2_acts_by_contentSqSum then applies on the nose.

    Pure algebra over a characteristic-zero field; nothing archimedean; no fit.
