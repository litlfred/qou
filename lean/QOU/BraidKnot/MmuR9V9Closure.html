                              theorem QOU.BraidKnot.universal_habiro_closure (q m_e vol_4_1 : ℝ) :
                              m_mu_R9_V9 q m_e vol_4_1 = m_e * vol_4_1 / hbarQ q ^ 2 * (1 - delta_total_mmu q) ∧ qbetaC3Closure q = qbetaC3Base q * (1 + qbetaC3Rung1 q + qbetaC3Rung2 q)

                              Theorem (conditional on [UniversalHabiroStructure]).

                              If the universal Habiro structure holds at q, then both the m_μ R9+V9 closure and the Q_β c_3 3-rung closure represent genuine categorical derivations of their respective L2 observables at q.

                              Corrected 2026-08-21 (vacuity ledger, proof-no-unused-hypothesis-rfl, verdict genuine). This was billed as "the §3b-cond conditional-class theorem: its conclusion is the L2 closure of both observables, conditional on the class instance." It is not, and never was:

                              • both conjuncts are closed by rfl, so the statement is two definitional unfoldings joined by ∧ — it says that m_mu_R9_V9 and qbetaC3Closure unfold to their own definitions;
                              • neither hq : 0 < q nor the [UniversalHabiroStructure q] instance was used, and the instance in particular was advertised as the structure supplying the closure while contributing nothing. Both unused binders have been dropped, which is what makes the remaining content honest: unfolding lemmas that do not pretend to be conditional on an open structure.

                              The instance could not have contributed: UniversalHabiroStructure's two fields are markov_trace_origin : True and cinquefoil_framing_t25_origin : True, both self-described opaque markers, so the class constrains nothing.

                              A universal Habiro closure is therefore NOT established here. It remains one of the framework's open targets (Theorems 4 + 5); the empirical position is unchanged — witnesses show the closures hold to within their L2 100× gates at proton-pinned q₀, the class instance has NOT been constructed, NEAR-MATCH tier, production wiring untouched.
