    General λ: the Jucys–Murphy route to θ_λ = q^{c(λ)} #

    KeystoneHeckeRootsForceTwist.lean discharged the KeystoneModularTwist header's "(up to convention)" hedge for the one-dimensional blocks [n] and (1ⁿ), by the two roots of the Hecke relation. A character argument reaches no further: for dim V_λ > 1 the generator is not a scalar, so its twist cannot be read off a root.

    This file builds the infrastructure for the general case and carries it as far as is honest.

    What is PROVED here, unconditionally #

    The combinatorial heart, and the reason the twist is well defined on a block at all:

    • cells — the Young diagram of λ as a Finset (ℕ × ℕ), with contentSum_eq_cells_sum identifying the corpus's nested-foldl contentSum with a single sum over that set.
    • sum_content_of_equiv — tableau-independence. A standard tableau of shape λ is, at minimum, a bijection between the n entries and the cells of λ. Summing the content of each entry's cell gives c(λ) for every such bijection. This is a reindexing along the bijection, so it holds with no hypothesis about Hecke algebras at all.

    Tableau-independence is exactly what makes the Jucys–Murphy product a scalar on the block rather than a per-tableau quantity: the JM elements act diagonally on the seminormal basis, and a diagonal operator is a scalar precisely when its eigenvalues agree across basis vectors.

    What is ASSUMED, and why it is not proved here #

    JucysMurphySpectrum carries one hypothesis: that the central full twist's scalar on V_λ is the product over entries of q^{2·content} — i.e. that the JM elements act on the seminormal basis with eigenvalue q^{2·content} and that their product is the full twist.

    That is a genuine theorem of Hecke-algebra representation theory, and it is not proved in this corpus. Proving it needs the seminormal form of H_n(q) — Young's orthogonal/seminormal basis, the JM recursion L_{i+1} = q^{-1} σ_i L_i σ_i, their commutativity, and the identification of ∏ᵢ Lᵢ with Δ² — none of which Mathlib or this corpus carries. Per §"Axiomatization is NOT a Proof", this class does not make the general case proved. It makes it conditional, in the §3b-cond sense: the class supplies a hypothesis, and fullTwistScalar_eq_theta_sq derives the conclusion from it using the unconditional combinatorics above.

    The honest reading: the hedge in KeystoneModularTwist.lean remains open for dim V_λ > 1. What this file removes is the combinatorial part of the gap, leaving the representation-theoretic part named, isolated in one class field, and cited.

    The falsifiability check #

    fullTwistScalar_single_row specialises the conditional general result to λ = [n] and lands on q^{n(n−1)} — which theta_single_row_sq proves unconditionally from the Hecke root. So the assumed spectrum is not free to be anything: on the blocks where the answer is already known without it, it reproduces that answer.

    Refs:

    • [macdonald1995] Symmetric Functions and Hall Polynomials, I.1 (content, conjugate).
    • [wenzl1988] Hecke algebra representations (seminormal form).

      The Jucys–Murphy hypothesis (§3b-cond) and the conditional general theorem #

      source
      class QOU.BraidKnot.Keystone.JucysMurphySpectrum (R : Type u_2) [Field R] (q : R) (l : MathlibExt.QPartitionCombinatorics.Partition) (n : ℕ) (fullTwistScalar : R) :
      Prop

      The Jucys–Murphy spectrum hypothesis for the block V_λ.

      fullTwistScalar is the scalar by which the central full twist Δ² acts on V_λ (Schur: it is a scalar because Δ² is central). tableau is a standard tableau, as a bijection between the n entries and the cells of λ.

      The single assumed field is fullTwistScalar_eq: that this scalar is the product over entries of q^{2·content}. This is the deep input and it is NOT proved in this corpus — it packages the seminormal-form facts (JM elements act diagonally with eigenvalue q^{2·content}; their product is Δ²). See the module docstring.

      Non-degeneracy (§3b-cond item 5). fullTwistScalar is a parameter, not a field: it is supplied by whatever exhibits the twist action, and the class constrains it. An earlier draft of this file made it a field, which was degenerate — the class could then be satisfied for any λ by simply defining the scalar to be the product, so it constrained nothing. As a parameter, an instance exists only when the supplied scalar really is the JM product.

      fullTwist_eq_theta_sq derives θ_λ² from that hypothesis via the unconditional tableau-independence result — the content-sum identification is not assumed anywhere.

        The general-λ twist, conditional on the Jucys–Murphy spectrum.

        Given the assumed JM spectrum, the full twist acts on V_λ by θ_λ² = q^{2c(λ)} — for every partition, not only the one-dimensional blocks.

        The derivation is the unconditional part: the product of q^{2·content} over entries collapses to q raised to the sum of contents (prod_zpow_eq_zpow_sum), and that sum is c(λ) for any tableau (sum_content_of_equiv). Neither step assumes anything about Hecke algebras.

        This does not prove the general case — it reduces it to the one named, cited hypothesis.
