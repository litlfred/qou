    QOU.BraidKnot.SpechtActionInjective — the Specht action map is injective #

    Brick 4c of W1+W3 of the Wedderburn epic (qou-bxw4), after #5139 (W2), #5151 (Schur orthonormality), #5156 (Hom_G(k[G], V) ≃ V), #5162 (both sides of the dimension count), #5167 (per-partition multiplicity) and #5174 (the ofModule/asModule round-trip).

    The product of the Specht actions

    Φ : k[S_n] →ₐ[k] ∏_{μ ⊢ n} End_k(S^μ)

    is injective. With the target's dimension from SpechtBlockDimension this gives

    n! ≤ Σ_μ (dim_k S^μ)².

    Honest scope #

    One inequality, not the RSK identity. n! = Σ_μ (dim S^μ)² additionally needs Σ_μ (dim S^μ)² ≤ n!, i.e. that the blocks do not overlap — the isotypic independence of the multiplicity spaces that bricks 1–4b count. That is not proved here, and sn_isotypic_specht_split remains open, as do the two keystones that inherit sorryAx from it.

    The argument #

    An x in the kernel acts as zero on every Specht module, hence — via SpechtClassification.specht_exhausts_irreducibles, which says the Specht modules exhaust the simples — as zero on every simple k[S_n]-module. k[S_n] is semisimple (Maschke), so as a module over itself it is the supremum of its simple submodules; an element of a supremum of submodules is a finite sum of elements of them, so x acts as zero on all of k[S_n]. Then x = x • 1 = 0.

    Two general lemmas carry the weight and are stated for an arbitrary ring / group: eq_zero_of_smul_simples_eq_zero (the semisimple sweep) and asAlgebraHom_of_intertwiner (transport of the algebra action along a rep-iso, which the intertwining hypothesis gives only on group elements).

    Base ring (§7c) #

    variable {k : Type} [Field k] [CharZero k] for the Specht layer; the two general lemmas are over [Ring R] / [Field k] [Monoid G] respectively. No ℝ.

    x • y = 0

    The semisimple sweep. In a semisimple module, an element of the ring that kills every simple submodule kills everything.

    The supremum of a family of submodules consists of finite sums of their elements (Submodule.iSup_induction — note it has no smul case, which is what makes this work: y ↦ x • y = 0 is closed under addition but not under multiplication by the ring on the right, since the ring need not be commutative).
