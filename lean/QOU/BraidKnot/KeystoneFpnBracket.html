    Keystone target-3 — the deuteron f_pn bracket gate (archimedean) #

    The full-Hecke geometric-index program's falsifiable target (3) (see docs/scope/2026-06-24-keystone-fullhecke-geometric-index-program.md): any index reproducing the W_λ family must land the deuteron proton/neutron interface fraction f_pn in the bracket

    f_pn ∈ (0.39479…, 0.66067…),

    which strictly contains the AME-exact f_pn ≈ 0.58776 (#2503 / #2528 consumer).

    This file formalizes that target as a machine-checkable gate, parallel to the order-h² defect gate (KeystoneG2Gate, target 1) and the conjugation symmetry (qPlancherelWeight_conj, target 4) — but at the archimedean / deuteron-observable level rather than the pure-ℤ structural level.

    Honest scope. Unlike the defect cubic (a proved ℤ identity), the bracket bounds are q₀-derived real numbers from the algebraic W_(2,1) (the n=3 standard-rep weight) evaluated at the substrate: the lower bound is the LP-dual a_K/R ratio ≈ 0.39479017 (witness computations/a-K-via-lp-dual.witness.json); the upper bound ≈ 0.66067 and the bracket framing are the #2503/#2528 consumer of W_(2,1). Those derivations live in the q₀ compute layer; they are cited here, not re-derived in Lean. The Lean content is the gate predicate (f_pn lands the bracket), the strict containment of the experimental value, and the bracket's non-degeneracy — the falsifiable check the geometric W_λ index (#2524) must pass for the deuteron. No fit.
