          Z-dependent correction — the bar-antisymmetric companion #

          The leading exponent above lives in the bar-EVEN sum σ+σ⁻¹ (A-only). The binding data, however, shows a Z-dependent deviation from q₀^{10A−5} — sharp on the proton-rich ³He (≈ −1.4 q₀-exponent units) and slight on its neutron-rich mirror T. That deviation is bar-ODD, so it cannot come from the symmetric sum; it lives in the atomWrithe σ−σ⁻¹, derived below to be proton-number-only (4Z). The coefficient (≈ −1.4 / excess proton) is measured, not fitted (Δ_λ gate): only the arithmetic skeleton is proved here; the bridge to the coefficient is a named-but-unasserted predicate.

                      Mirror-pair cancellation — the bar-even baseline drops out #

                      A mirror pair swaps (Z, N) ↦ (N, Z) (proton↔neutron counts) at fixed A = Z + N. The bar-EVEN crossing statistics are Z↔N-symmetric (A-only), so they are identical across a mirror pair; only the bar-ODD atomWrithe distinguishes them. Hence any binding deviation that separates into an A-only baseline plus an interface-imbalance term has the baseline cancel exactly in the mirror difference — isolating the Z-correction. This is why the ³He/T (−1.34) and ⁷Be/⁷Li (−1.77) mirror splits are clean even though the absolute deviations are large and A-dependent (and even though the leading 10A−5 law has collapsed by A=7). See delta_lambda_z_coefficient_and_slope.py.

                      theorem QOU.BraidKnot.mirror_baseline_cancels {K : Type u_1} [AddCommGroup K] (baseline : ℕ → K) (zCorr : ℤ → K) (f : ℕ → ℕ → K) (hf : ∀ (a b : ℕ), f a b = baseline (a + b) + zCorr (↑a - ↑b)) (Z N : ℕ) :
                      f Z N - f N Z = zCorr (↑Z - ↑N) - zCorr (↑N - ↑Z)

                      The mirror cancellation (proved; the separation is the hypothesis). Model the Δ_λ deviation as f Z N = baseline (Z+N) + zCorr (Z − N) — an A-only baseline plus a term in the interface imbalance Z − N, the additive form justified by the mirror-invariance of the two bar-even statistics above. Then the mirror-pair difference cancels the baseline exactly (since Z + N = N + Z) and equals zCorr (Z−N) − zCorr (N−Z): the observable mirror split is the bar-odd Z-correction alone, independent of the (A-dependent, collapsing) baseline.

                      The cancellation given the additive form is unconditional; the separation hypothesis hf itself is the additive-channel ansatz (supplied, not proved — the data confirms it but the corpus gates fitting its magnitude).
