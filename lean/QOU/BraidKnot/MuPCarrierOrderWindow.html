    1. The generic substrate agrees with the production one on ℝ #

    source
    theorem QOU.BraidKnot.MuPCarrierOrderWindow.hbarGen_eq_hbarQ (q : ℝ) (hq : q ≠ 0) :
    MuPCarrier.hbarGen q = hbarQ q

    The generic ℏ of MuPCarrierBarParity agrees with the production hbarQ on ℝ, so the parity results there apply verbatim to the definitions below.

    2. The corpus's own m_μ rungs are bar-mixed #

    delta_R9 = ℏ_q⁶·q/5 has exponent pattern (k, m) = (6, 1); bar-evenness needs m = k/2 = 3. delta_V9 = ℏ_q⁸·[3]_q/25 has a bar-even [3]_q factor, so its ℏ_q⁸ prefactor is left undressed by the required q⁴. Both therefore fail the selection rule of MuPCarrierBarParity, and the witnesses below make that concrete.

    source
    theorem QOU.BraidKnot.MuPCarrierOrderWindow.delta_R9_not_bar_even :
    delta_R9 2⁻¹ ≠ delta_R9 2

    delta_R9 is not bar-even. Witness q = 2: δ_R9(1/2) = 1/10 but δ_R9(2) = 1/160.

    At the substrate pin the violation is not marginal — the probe measures δ_R9(1/q₀)/δ_R9(q₀) = 1.5180.

    3. Order window #

    source
    theorem QOU.BraidKnot.MuPCarrierOrderWindow.order_window (h c t : ℝ) (k : ℕ) (hh0 : 98 / 1000 ≤ h) (hh1 : h ≤ 1 / 10) (hc0 : 1 / 5 ≤ |c|) (hc1 : |c| ≤ 5) (ht : |c| * h ^ k = t) (htlo : 18 / 10 ^ 8 ≤ t) (hthi : t ≤ 24 / 10 ^ 8) :
    k = 6 ∨ k = 7

    Order window. Suppose the μ–p correction has the monomial form t = |c| · h^k, where

    • h = ℏ_q ∈ [0.098, 0.100] (the substrate at any pin in the legitimate μ–p window; the probe measures ℏ_{q₀} = 0.09908171604),
    • |c| ∈ [1/5, 5] is a small-height coefficient, and
    • t ∈ [1.8e-7, 2.4e-7] is the measured residual 209.755 ppb, a window that comfortably contains ±1σ_exp = ±22 ppb.

    Then k ∈ {6, 7}.

    Orders k ≤ 5 give at least 0.098⁵/5 ≈ 1.81e-6, over 7.5× the top of the window; orders k ≥ 8 give at most 5·0.1⁸ = 5e-8, under 0.28× the bottom. Both are contradictions.
