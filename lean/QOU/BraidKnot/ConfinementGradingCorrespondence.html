    QOU.BraidKnot.ConfinementGradingCorrespondence — confinement = KLR grading shift (conjecture) #

    Class axiomatisation of [conj:confinement-grading-correspondence]: under the Brundan–Kleshchev isomorphism, the confinement filtration J^•(q) on H_n(R, q) coincides with the filtration induced by the natural ℤ-grading on the KLR algebra. A normal form T_w has confinement level ℓ iff its KLR-module lift has top non-zero graded piece in degree ℓ.

    Takes [CrystalGraphRealization R q] as upstream input.

    LaTeX: \label{conj:confinement-grading-correspondence}

    References:

    • Brundan, J. & Kleshchev, A. (2009).
    • Khovanov, M. & Lauda, A. D. (2009).

    Type u

    Confinement / KLR-grading correspondence (conjecture).

    Encodes the equality of two filtrations on H_n(R, q): the LP shadow-price confinement filtration and the KLR ℤ-grading top- degree filtration.

    • confLevel : CrystalGraphRealization.NormalForm q → ℕ

      Confinement level ℓ(K, q) of a normal form (LP shadow-price filtration).

    • klrTopDegree : CrystalGraphRealization.NormalForm q → ℕ

      Top non-zero graded degree of the KLR-module lift M_K.

    • filtrations_agree (T : CrystalGraphRealization.NormalForm q) : confLevel T = klrTopDegree T

      The conjecture itself: the two filtrations agree on the nose. Plays the role of sorry.

      The trivial model — a def, not an instance, and not "faithful".

      Its docstring said "Faithful instance. Carries the explicit research-grade conjecture as a sorry." Neither half was true. There is no sorry: it sets confLevel _ := 0 and klrTopDegree _ := 0, so filtrations_agree is discharged by rfl on 0 = 0. That is strictly worse than a sorry, because a sorry is visible to #print axioms and this is not.

      As an unconditional instance it was supplied by typeclass resolution for every R and q, which made confLevel_le_klrTopDegree and klrTopDegree_le_confLevel unconditional theorems about a filtration pair that is identically zero — conj:confinement-grading-correspondence asserted nothing.

      Same shape as SpectralSequence.canonicalTrivial and demoted the same way (bean qou-fngs, checker check-self-discharging-instances.ts). The model stays available and must now be named, so a reader of a downstream theorem can see whether the hypothesis was discharged by real filtrations or by zero. Nothing consumed it: the class had no users outside this file.
