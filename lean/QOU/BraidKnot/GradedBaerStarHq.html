    Type u_1

    Graded Baer- on $\mathcal{H}_q$ across the depth filtration.*

    Hq is the substrate convolution Hopf algebra with its bar involution $*\colon q \mapsto q^{-1}$ (a real InvolutiveStar); depth is the iterated-integral depth grading $G^{(0)} \subseteq G^{(1)} \subseteq \cdots$.

    Conjectural class — see conj:graded-baer-star-hq.

    • depth : Hq → ℕ

      Iterated-integral depth grading $G^{(n)}(\mathcal{H}_q)$.

    • IsProjection : Hq → Prop

      The orthogonal projections.

    • proj_selfadjoint (p : Hq) : IsProjection p → star p = p

      The conjecture (Baer-* property): every projection is self-adjoint under the bar involution — i.e. each depth piece $G^{(n)}$ is a Baer--ring and the inclusions preserve projections. (The $$-involution law star_star itself is now free from InvolutiveStar.)
