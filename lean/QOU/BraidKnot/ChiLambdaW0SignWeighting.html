    QOU.BraidKnot.ChiLambdaW0SignWeighting #

    Canonical Lean for content block prop:chi-lambda-w0-sign-weighting.

    The substantive, derivable core of the proposition is the long-element character-weighting identity at S₃: with the standard irreducible-character values at the longest element w₀ ∈ S₃, namely χ_{(3)}(w₀) = 1, χ_{(2,1)}(w₀) = 0, χ_{(1,1,1)}(w₀) = −1, the character-weighted sum collapses to

    S_k^{(w₀)} = c_k^{(3)} − c_k^{(1,1,1)}.

    This file derives that identity directly (ring), and discharges the two witnessed sign facts at k = 5, 6 by norm_num on the witness-pinned c_k^{(λ)} values. The previous canonical was an empty structure ChiLambdaW0SignWeighting where, which stated nothing (proof-statement-integrity failure); it is replaced by the genuine statements below.

    The general sign-agreement claim (empirical c_k matches S_k^{(w₀)} in sign for all k) is not provable from S₃ character theory alone — it depends on the Wenzl-bridge Markov-trace values — and remains open in the manuscript; only the k = 5, 6 witnessed instances are discharged here.

    -- Ref: [manuscript] quantum-observable-universe/mass-theory/chi-lambda-w0-sign-weighting.md
