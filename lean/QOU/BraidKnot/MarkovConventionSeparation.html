    Conventions C and D are separated by the range of the Markov kernel #

    Bean qou-cxjq step 3. Before migrating any convention-C definition, this file settles the structural question that governs how the migration must be done.

    The two conventions share one rational kernel #

    Write g u = u / (u ^ 2 + 1). Then, exactly,

    • convention C z_C q = √q / (q + 1) = g (√q),
    • convention D z_D q = q ^ 3 / (q ^ 2 + 1) = q ^ 2 * g q.

    So D is not C with a different letter in it: C is g evaluated at a square root, D is g evaluated at q and then rescaled by q ^ 2.

    The separation #

    g is bounded: g u ≤ 1 / 2 for every real u, with equality exactly at u = 1 (immediately from 0 ≤ (u - 1) ^ 2). Meanwhile z_D q - 1 / 2 = (q - 1) * (2 * q ^ 2 + q + 1) / (2 * (q ^ 2 + 1)), whose second factor is positive for every real q, so

    1 < q  →  1 / 2 < z_D q .

    Hence for every q > 1 there is no real u at all with g u = z_D q (no_kernel_instantiation_is_conventionD). At q = 1 the two coincide, both equal to 1 / 2 — the classical coincidence, and the only point of contact.

    Why this is the gate on the migration, not a remark #

    Three consequences, in increasing order of how much work they save:

    1. "Never substitute s ^ 2 = q" is now a theorem, not a caution. The substitution cannot work, because it asks for a u that provably does not exist above q = 1. The substrate sits at q₀ ≈ 1.11 > 1.

    2. The Tier-2 migration baked convention C in structurally. Replacing Real.sqrt q by a generic markovZ (u : R) := u / (u ^ 2 + 1) was carried out to remove the square root, and it did. But the shape it migrated to has a real range of [-1/2, 1/2], which excludes z_D q for every q > 1. So the generic form is not convention-neutral: no choice of u makes it D. It removed the square root and made the convention harder to see, which is the opposite of what a convention migration should do.

    3. Therefore the 9 C-sqrtvar sites cannot be repaired by re-instantiating. The definition shape has to change — from g to q ^ 2 * g q. A site that merely re-parameterises stays in C no matter what is passed to it.

    Everything here is archimedean (an ordering and a Real.sqrt), so it lives on the ℝ side of the AGENTS.md §7c wall by necessity, not by accident: the separation is an inequality, and there is nothing to state over a generic commutative ring.

    The rational kernel both conventions are built from: g u = u / (u² + 1). This is the generic Tier-2 markovZ, stated here over ℝ because the whole point of this file is an inequality.

    This is a convention-C object, deliberately, and it is here for comparison only — it is the thing convention D is separated from, and every theorem below is about the gap between it and markovZD. Do not consume it as a Markov parameter; markovZD is canonical (owner ruling R1, 2026-08-15).
