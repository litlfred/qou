    The additive Jucys–Murphy family on the bounded presentation #

    Tranche 1 of porting jmRaw_comm — the one item left on qou-t1kv.

    Why the additive family is the route #

    jmRaw_comm is not proved directly. HeckeTranspositionExpansion proves it by identifying the multiplicative and additive families, L_k = 1 + A_k at d = q − q⁻¹, and doing the real work on the additive side, where the corresponding statement addJM_comm holds for every d. So the port has to carry addJM first.

    The bounds here are uniform #

    Every relation-using lemma in this file bottoms out in hecke_smul at index k, so all of them carry the same hypothesis k + 1 < n — "σ_k is a generator of H_n(q)". That is the cheapest case; the transposition machinery in the next tranche is where the bounds stop being uniform.

    addJM H d k itself is a word in σ₀ … σ_{k−1}, so it is well-formed for k < n; the lemmas need one more because they multiply by σ_k.
