    QOU.BraidKnot.QBetaPerBlockChiClosedForms #

    Pre-archimedean closed-form identities for the per-Wedderburn-block characters $\chi_\lambda(\Delta_{T_\beta})(q)$ over $H_3(q)$, plus the antipode pairing $(3) \leftrightarrow (1,1,1)$ under $q \mapsto 1/q$.

    This file proves the per-block closed forms from qbeta-per-block-paper-closed-forms.witness.json as rational identities in $q$, plus a structural antipode-pairing identity. These are the PRE-ARCHIMEDEAN inputs to the Q$_\beta$ precision-ladder derivation.

    Honest scope (after correction). A first version of this file attempted to prove a closed form for the y_λ-weighted Markov-trace sum $\Delta\tau_M(q) = \sum_\lambda y_\lambda(q) \cdot \chi_\lambda(q)$. That version was retracted because the canonical Wenzl–Markov weights $y_\lambda(q)$ from solve_y_lambda(3) involve $\sqrt q$ (via the Markov parameter $z = 1/(\sqrt q + 1/\sqrt q)$), so $\Delta\tau_M(q)$ is a rational function of $\sqrt q$, NOT of $q$ alone. Proving its sum form in Lean requires square-root handling beyond ring tactics — out of scope here.

    What IS provable in pure ring arithmetic, and what this file proves:

    1. The three per-block characters $\chi_{(3)}, \chi_{(2,1)}, \chi_{(1,1,1)}$ as named rational functions of $q$.
    2. The antipode pairing: $\chi_{(1,1,1)}(q) = q^{-4} \cdot \chi_{(3)}(q)$ for $q > 0$, reflecting the standard $V_{(3)} \otimes \text{sign} = V_{(1,1,1)}$ relation.
    3. The vanishing at q = 1: each $\chi_\lambda(q)$ vanishes when $q = 1$ (from the $(q^2 - 1)$ factor), confirming $\Delta\tau_M(1) = 0$ regardless of $y_\lambda$.

    These are rigorous, sorry-free, and do NOT depend on any y_λ form.

    LaTeX label: prop:qbeta-per-block-chi-closed-forms

    What this file does NOT claim #

    • A closed form for $\Delta\tau_M(q)$ (open — needs $\sqrt q$ handling).
    • The empirical $c_3 = -2$ identity (still NEAR-MATCH per docs/coordination/1ppq-qbeta.md).
    • The f-map identification (open).

            Theorem (antipode pairing). Under the substitution $q \mapsto 1/q$ the trivial-irrep character $\chi_{(3)}$ maps to the sign-irrep character $\chi_{(1,1,1)}$:

            $\chi_{(1,1,1)}(q) = q^{-4} \cdot \chi_{(3)}(q)$.

            This reflects the standard Hecke-algebra identity $V_{(3)} \otimes V_{\text{sign}} = V_{(1,1,1)}$, with the $q^{-4}$ prefactor coming from the framing weight (degree-$4$ shift between the $q^{-1}$- and $q^{-5}$-denominators).

            Proof: direct rational arithmetic; clear denominators and apply ring.

            theorem QOU.BraidKnot.chi_blocks_have_q2_minus_1_factor (q : ℝ) (hq : 0 < q) :
            ∃ (P3 : ℝ → ℝ) (P21 : ℝ → ℝ) (P111 : ℝ → ℝ), chi_block_3 q = (q ^ 2 - 1) * P3 q ∧ chi_block_21 q = (q ^ 2 - 1) * P21 q ∧ chi_block_111 q = (q ^ 2 - 1) * P111 q

            Theorem (q²−1 factor in all three blocks). Every per-block character $\chi_\lambda(\Delta_{T_\beta})$ factors as $(q^2 - 1)$ times a rational function of $q$ on the physical regime $q > 0$ (so the classical limit $q \to 1^+$ is the structural source of the vanishing — see chi_blocks_vanish_at_one).

            The identity $q^2 - 1 = (q-1)(q+1)$ would algebraically extend the vanishing to $q = -1$ as well, but this theorem is stated under $q > 0$ to match the physical regime of $\chi_\lambda$ (where the $1/q^k$ denominators in the closed forms are well-defined and positive); the $q = -1$ root is outside scope.

            This is the structural source of the order-$w$ leading term in the Taylor expansion around $q = 1$: $q^2 - 1 = 2w + w^2$ where $w = q - 1$. Each block contributes at first order in $w$, with the per-block ε¹-order coefficients $\{-2, -1, -2\}$ from the witness's taylor_coefficients_per_block_ε_order field.

            Extensions (added after the per-block χ_λ theorems above) #

            The four sections below extend the per-block χ_λ formalization in the directions named in the README's "What this proposition does NOT establish" list — three are tractable in pure ring/field_simp, one (the canonical √q-bearing Δτ_M) is conditional on a class axiomatising the canonical Wenzl–Markov weights per AGENTS.md §3b-cond.

            The fifth extension (z-recursion as a class per §3b-cond) follows in the next section.

            Skipped extension (vacuous in Lean): the script's witness assertion delta_tau_M_rational_in_q (Δτ_M has no √q atoms) is structurally vacuous at the Lean type level — the surrogate delta_tau_M_surrogate below is defined as a quotient of polynomials in q : ℝ, so no half-integer power of q can appear by construction. The check is only meaningful at the Python/SymPy AST level (where Δτ_M is built from chi_λ and y_λ that COULD in principle carry √q atoms but don't here).

                        Orders 4–7 Taylor coefficients — formalized via an iterated-Leibniz recurrence #

                        The order-3 explicit-closed-form template does not extend (order-4 would need the degree-≈57 third-derivative closed form). Instead we build a general iterated Leibniz lemma (iteratedDeriv_mul_eq_sum, missing from Mathlib v4.24) and apply the recurrence on the polynomial identity f·D = N (f = delta_tau_M_surrogate, D = 4q⁸(q²+q+1), N the numerator):

                        Σ_{k=0}^{n} C(n,k)·f⁽ᵏ⁾(1)·D⁽ⁿ⁻ᵏ⁾(1) = N⁽ⁿ⁾(1),

                        solving the k=n term against D(1)=12 to recover f⁽ⁿ⁾(1) from the lower derivatives. This yields c₄ = 77/18, c₅ = 1349/108, c₆ = -191/4, c₇ = 26285/162, sorry-free.
