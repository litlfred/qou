    Bring W-plane Gauss–Manin monodromy operator (§(d) sub-lead, numerology-guarded) #

    The full-Hecke geometric-index program's Bring/Jacobian sub-lead (program §(d), see docs/scope/2026-06-24-keystone-fullhecke-geometric-index-program.md): the rank-2 W-plane is the elliptic curve E = Bring/S₄ (Jac(Bring) ≅ E⁴, #2710), and the depth-2 selection-rule recurrence kernel R(x) = x² − τ·x + 1 (#2488, τ(q₀) ≈ 2.6424) is conjectured to come from the Gauss–Manin monodromy of E along the S³/2I Seifert fibration.

    Numerology guardrail (STRICT). τ = 2.6424 is a single number; matching one geometric scalar to one algebraic scalar is not a derivation (the W_λ target is a λ-family). So this file formalizes the monodromy operator and the functional form of the recurrence kernel — never the trace value. The genuine structural content is:

    The recurrence kernel x² − τ·x + 1 is the Cayley–Hamilton relation of a unimodular 2×2 monodromy M, with τ = tr M. Its monic, unit-constant-term shape is forced by det M = 1 (the symplectic structure of H¹ of an elliptic curve) — not fitted.

    unimodular_two_recurrence proves this sorry-free. The geometric identification — that M is the actual Seifert / Gauss–Manin monodromy of E = Bring/S₄ with tr M = τ — is the open §3b conjecture (BringWPlaneMonodromy), and the falsifiable functional target (reproduce the whole (n−1,1) recurrence family, not the one number τ) is stated, not closed.

    Base ring ℝ (the monodromy acts on the real H¹); the structural lemma is pure linear algebra. No fit; τ appears only as the symbol tr M.

    theorem QOU.BraidKnot.unimodular_two_recurrence (M : Matrix (Fin 2) (Fin 2) ℝ) (hdet : M.det = 1) :
    M * M = M.trace • M - 1

    The recurrence kernel is the Cayley–Hamilton relation of a unimodular 2×2 monodromy. For any M ∈ Mat₂(ℝ) with det M = 1 (a symplectic / SL₂ monodromy on the rank-2 W-plane H¹(E), E = Bring/S₄), M·M = (tr M)·M − I — i.e. M satisfies the depth-2 recurrence kernel R(x) = x² − τ·x + 1 with τ = tr M. The monic, unit-constant-term form of the kernel (#2488) is thus forced by unimodularity; this is the operator-level (functional) content, not the trace value τ ≈ 2.6424 (which the numerology guardrail forbids fitting).

    class QOU.BraidKnot.BringWPlaneMonodromy (is_seifert_gauss_manin : Prop) :
    Type

    Bring W-plane Gauss–Manin monodromy — §3b conjecture class (numerology-guarded).

    The conjecture: there is a unimodular 2×2 operator M that is the actual Gauss–Manin / Seifert monodromy of the W-plane elliptic fibre E = Bring/S₄ along the S³/2I fibration, whose trace is the depth-2 recurrence kernel coefficient τ (#2488). By unimodular_two_recurrence, such an M automatically satisfies the recurrence kernel x² − τ·x + 1 (Cayley–Hamilton) — so the conjecture's content is the geometric identification of M, not the kernel's form (which is forced) nor the value of τ (which is never fitted). The falsifiable functional target is that the same M reproduces the whole (n−1,1) recurrence family, not the single scalar τ.

    • M : Matrix (Fin 2) (Fin 2) ℝ

      The Gauss–Manin monodromy operator on the rank-2 W-plane H¹(E).

    • unimodular : (M is_seifert_gauss_manin).det = 1

      Unimodular: det M = 1 (the symplectic structure of H¹ of an elliptic curve).

    • seifert_holds : is_seifert_gauss_manin

      The conjecture: the geometric identification holds — M is the genuine Seifert / Gauss–Manin monodromy of E = Bring/S₄ along the S³/2I fibration (the §3b conjectural input).

      Parameterised 2026-09-13. is_seifert_gauss_manin was a field typed with the universe Prop alongside this witness, so the instance chose the proposition and ⟨True, trivial⟩ discharged the pair — the geometric identification, which the class docstring calls the conjecture's whole content, cost nothing to assume. As a parameter it is fixed by the binder and visible in every consumer's type.

      It is a parameter rather than an encoding deliberately: identifying M with the genuine Seifert monodromy is the open geometric claim itself, so writing a proposition for it here would be guessing at the conjecture rather than carrying it. monodromy_satisfies_recurrence below is unaffected — it uses unimodular only, and never this field.
