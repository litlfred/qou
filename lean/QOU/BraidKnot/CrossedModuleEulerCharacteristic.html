    Hecke crossed module h² trace = dim(V) · χ(Bring) #

    The Q₈ crossed module for the Borromean braid (σ₁σ₂⁻¹)³ has h² curvature trace = −12 = 2 · (−6) = dim(V) · χ(Σ₄), where Σ₄ is Bring's surface (genus 4, Euler characteristic −6).

    The proof is finite combinatorics: enumerate 15 pairwise products of 6 quaternion matrices, sum, trace.

    -- Ref: [kassel1995] https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4612-0783-2 -- Ref: [yekutieli2015] https://doi.org/10.1142/9537
