    The SYT-encoding bridge, half two: the 0-indexed count embeds into the 1-indexed one #

    SYTEncodingBridge embedded QOU.Mathlib.StandardYoungTableau (1-indexed, BijOn-packaged) into QOU.BraidKnot.StandardYoungTableau (0-indexed, raw predicates), giving numStandardYoungTableaux μ ≤ numSYT μ. This file supplies the reverse embedding, and hence the equality of the two counts.

    The reverse direction needs two things the 0-indexed encoding does not carry directly:

    • general monotonicity from adjacent strictness — the raw structure states row_strict / col_strict only for adjacent cells, while SemistandardYoungTableau demands the general j1 < j2 / i1 < i2 forms (entry_lt_row, entry_lt_col below, by induction on the gap);
    • the pigeonhole — the 1-indexed side demands Set.BijOn onto Set.Icc 1 n, i.e. surjectivity, which the raw structure never states. It follows from inj_on plus lt_card: an injection from the n-cell set into an n-element target is onto (Finset.surj_on_of_inj_on_of_card_le).

    -- Ref: [sagan2001] Sagan, The Symmetric Group §3 (standard tableaux; both entry conventions)
