    QOU.BraidKnot.jet_filtered_knot_factor — conjectural class (§3b) #

    §3b class axiomatisation for content block conj:jet-filtered-knot-factor: The Bethe–Weizsäcker knot factor $a(Z, N)$ is the Jones polynomial of the nuclear quotient braid $\bar\beta$ (Part 4; Parts 1–3 established in the manuscript).

    This is a conjecture: the class carries the conjecture's actual content as the factor_is_jones field; the noncomputable instance's sorry represents the open conjecture itself (per AGENTS.md §3b), NOT a missing proof.

    Mathlib-infra pass (2026-06-25, Tier 1). The established Part-1 per-level invariant is the $q$-Plancherel-weighted character average $a_k = \frac{\sum_\lambda w_\lambda \chi_\lambda}{\sum_\lambda w_\lambda \dim}$ with $w_\lambda = d_q(\lambda)^2 / Z_q(k)$. That Wedderburn weight is now the real QOU.MathlibExt.QPlancherel.measure (built over the QDimension / HasPartitionFunction typeclasses); jetWedderburnWeight below is that measure, and jetWedderburnWeight_nonneg is a sorry-free proof of its positivity (from QPlancherel.measure_nonneg). Only the characters $\chi_\lambda$ and the open Part-4 Jones identification stay abstract.
