    Epistemic status (honest framing — same as D(n)) #

    E(n) is NOT derived here from a symbolic moment-collapse. The companion probe computations/keystone_wlambda_defect_coeff_ladder.py extracted E(n) numerically as the h⁶ Taylor coefficient of 1 − Z(n) via mp.taylor (method-validated: the h² coefficient reproduces the proved-exact C(n) to 0.0), proved its degree-9 polynomiality by constant 9th finite difference, interpolated the unique degree-9 polynomial, and validated it on held-out n = 12, 13, 14 (PASS, exact). The deg c_{2k} = 3k law itself is a conjecture on k=1,2,3 evidence (expected from Kerov–Olshanski / Kerov-polynomial theory — Plancherel moments are polynomial on Young diagrams — the QOU-specific content being the sharp 3k degree + 144^k denominator).

    What is Lean-checked below (sorry-free, pure ℤ, q₀ never used — the h→0 limit): the closed-form polynomial's algebra (factored ↔ the witness's expanded int_coeffs_low_to_high), the shared n(n−1) vanishing at n=0,1, and the value table cross-checking 21946982400·E(n) for n=2..14 (held-out 12,13,14 included). The derivation of E(n) from the defect stays deferred — parallel to C(n)'s general-n Identity I. The Lean file formalizes the closed form's algebra and witness cross-check, not a derivation.
