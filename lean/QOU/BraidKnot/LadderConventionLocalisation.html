    The Markov-parameter convention enters the categorical Q_β ladder ONLY at N=1 #

    Bean qou-cds1 (convention C → D re-derivation of the Q_β precision ladder).

    Reading the ladder construction (QBetaCategoricalPrecisionLadder.Q_beta_categorical) factor by factor:

    • N1_factor = cos(alpha − b·ℏ_q²/2) with alpha = (markovZ' − 1)·h/2 — the only factor that reads the Markov parameter markovZ';
    • N2_factor = 1 − b²·ℏ_q⁴ (c₂ = −1), N3_factor = 1 − 2·b³·ℏ_q⁶ (c₃ = J_Hopf(1) = −2), N4_factor, N5_factor — closed forms in q through hoefsmitB q and hbarQ' q alone, with no markovZ' and no alpha.

    So the whole Markov-parameter-convention dependence of the categorical ladder is the single N=1 eigenphase. This file states that as an identity: the ladder is the φ = alpha q instance of a phase-parameterised family whose N=2..5 tail (ckTail) is a common factor independent of φ.

    Consequence for qou-cds1. A convention change C → D moves the Markov parameter z_C ↦ z_D; on this construction that acts only by replacing the phase alpha q with the D-frame mirror-covariant phase alpha_D − h²/4 (QOU.QBeta.phaseTwiceD_sub_hsq_bar_odd). The categorical c_k for k ≥ 2 are the z-convention-immune Kashiwara/Hopf tails b^k·ℏ_q^{2k} and are unchanged. The S1 sensitivity figure c₃ ≈ −974 under a naive z-swap is the extractor mis-attributing this single N=1 phase change to the higher rungs — it is not a change in the categorical c_k.

    Scope (honest). This is a statement about the construction as coded: it localises the z-dependence to N=1 and thereby explains the −974 artifact. It does not claim the physical D re-derivation leaves the Kashiwara tails untouched — whether N2..N5 should themselves change under D (if the Kashiwara operators see the trace normalisation) is the open question recorded in docs/audits/2026-08-21-cds1-d-frame-ladder-state-synthesis.md. All proofs are ring/unfold, sorry-free.

          theorem QOU.BraidKnot.hoefsmitB_sq_eq_one_sub_stdWeightD (q : ℝ) :
          hoefsmitB q ^ 2 = 1 - stdWeightD q

          The Hoefsmit coupling squared is the complement of the D standard-block weight: b² = 1 − y_(2,1)^D.

So b, hence N2..N5, is naturally a D-frame object, a positive structural reason the categorical tails are correctly framed in D (bean qou-cds1).

            The D-frame N=2 rung is forced — b² = [3]_q / [2]_q² #

            Bean qou-cds1 asks, as its own named next step, to "state the invariant a forced D c₂ must satisfy (D analogue of c₂ = −1 = 1 − b²ℏ⁴) and check if exact weights + α_D − h²/4 determine it".

            The answer is that the phase leg does not enter at all, and the weight leg determines the rung completely. Q_beta_categorical_ofPhase_tail_common (above) already localises every z-dependence to the N=1 phase, so α_D − h²/4 is irrelevant to N2_factor. And stdWeightD_eq_jones pins y_(2,1)^D = 1/[2]_q², so with hoefsmitB_sq_eq_one_sub_stdWeightD the coupling collapses to a bare quantum-integer ratio, via the identity [2]_q² − [3]_q = 1.

            So under D there is no free parameter in the N=2 rung: b is not an independent Hoefsmit input but a function of the D weights, which are themselves forced. This is the contrast with C, where b² ≠ 1 − y_(2,1)^C and the coupling is genuinely separate data.

            Scope. This states and settles the invariant for c₂ ONLY. It says nothing about c₃/c₄/c₅, whose values remain fitted near-matches (see dWeights_at_one), and it closes no part of the numerical gap — the 41.94×C residual recorded in qou-cds1 is untouched. What it removes is the possibility that a D-frame c₂ could be chosen.
