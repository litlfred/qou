    QOU.BraidKnot.InterfaceOperator — block-diagonality of the interface operator #

    The operator-valued Wenzl branching combiner for per-isotope binding peels one nucleon at a time through the interface operator A^λ_{ξν}(I), defined as the compression of the bridge generator I = c·σ_{n-4} + d·𝟙 to the (ξ,ν)-isotypic subspace of V_λ:

    A^λ_{ξν}(I) = P_{ξν} · ρ_λ(I) · P_{ξν}.

    The closure-(b) design (docs/audits/2026-06-16-categorical-interface-operator-closure-b-design.md, §"Why this is the right object") establishes — in prose — the structural reason this object is universal by construction (no test-word dependence):

    Because W acts within ξ-blocks and β_block within ν-blocks (no mixing) and the trace is cyclic, only the diagonal (ξ,ν)→(ξ,ν) isotypic block of ρ_λ(I) survives the closed trace. Off-diagonal blocks would carry ρ_{ξ'}(W) ⊗ ρ_{ν'}(β) between different irreps and vanish under the closed trace.

    This file makes that the content of a sorry-free theorem. The decomposition

    χ^λ(W·I·β) = Σ_{ξν} Tr( A^λ_{ξν}(I) · (ρ_ξ(W) ⊗ ρ_ν(β)) )

    reduces — at the algebra/trace level — to the statement that a cyclic trace τ of W·I·β, where W and β each commute with every projector in an orthogonal resolution of unity {P i}, collapses onto the diagonal compressions P i · I · P i:

    τ(W·I·β) = Σ_i τ( W · (P i · I · P i) · β ).

    This is precisely the "no off-diagonal survives the trace" invariant. It is what turns the categorical extraction of A^λ_{ξν}(I) into a deterministic compression rather than a least-squares fit (which was rank-deficient at n ≥ 9 and produced the 21–42 % error on T / ³He). The downstream H₉ universality extraction (Rust-gated) inherits this guarantee.

    Scope and base ring (§7c) #

    Stated over a generic commutative ring R as the trace codomain and a generic (possibly noncommutative) ring A as the operator algebra — no archimedean structure, no specialisation to ℝ, no fitted constant. The hypotheses are exactly the categorical facts the design doc cites:

    • {P i} is an orthogonal resolution of unity (P i · P j = 0 for i ≠ j, Σ P i = 1) — the isotypic projectors P_{ξν};
    • W and β each commute with every P i — W lies in the inner subalgebra H_{n-3} and β in the block subalgebra H_3, each acting block-diagonally on the isotypic decomposition;
    • τ is additive and cyclic — the Markov trace τ_M.

    Note the identity holds for any orthogonal resolution of unity, not only idempotent projectors — idempotency of the P i is not needed and is therefore not assumed (the isotypic projectors are the intended special case).

      Block-diagonality of the interface operator under a cyclic trace.

      Let {P i} be an orthogonal resolution of unity in A (P i · P j = 0 for i ≠ j, and Σ i, P i = 1). Let W, β ∈ A each commute with every P i, and let τ : A →+ R be cyclic (τ (x*y) = τ (y*x)). Then the trace of W · I · β collapses onto the diagonal compressions of I:

      τ (W · I · β) = Σ i, τ ( W · (P i · I · P i) · β ).

      This is the "only the diagonal isotypic block survives the closed trace" invariant from the closure-(b) design — the structural reason the interface operator A^λ_{ξν}(I) = P_{ξν} ρ_λ(I) P_{ξν} is universal by construction.
