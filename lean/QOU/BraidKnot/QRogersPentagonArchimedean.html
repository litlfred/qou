          Mathlib-backed log structure of the Rogers correction #

          Coverage finding (2026-06-25). Mathlib has no dilogarithm / polylogarithm Li₂, so the classical q = 1 Rogers–Abel FIVE-TERM identity (intrinsically a statement about Li₂) cannot yet be a Mathlib filler — it stays class-axiomatised in QRogersAnalyticContinuation below. What IS provable now with Mathlib is the log²-correction skeleton of the five-term relation (Real.log_div, Real.log_one): the additive-log form of the correction term and its degenerate vertex. These are the real, machine-checked building blocks the eventual Li₂ five-term proof composes.

          At q = 1, the classical Rogers identity holds (provable via real-log arithmetic + polylogarithm formalisation pending in Mathlib). At q ≠ 1, the q-log² Rogers correction is the proposed extension; the conjectural content is encoded in the class field.

          Per AGENTS.md §3b-cond, downstream theorems consume the class via [Instance].

          • q_rogers_holds_classical (u v : ℝ) : q_rogers_holds_at u v 1

            At q = 1, the classical Rogers identity holds for all convergent (u, v).

                T3 scaffold: Rogers correction's leading order #

                Per docs/audits/2026-05-24-derivation-chain-gb-jet-orders-and-pentagon.md §3 (Post-τ pentagon, jet grades 2 and 4): the post-τ Pentagon picks up a $\log_q^2$ Rogers correction. Since $\log_q(x) \approx (x-1)/h + O(h)$ near $q=1$, the bilinear $\log_q \cdot \log_q$ product is $O(h^{-2}) \cdot O(h^{-2}) \cdot O(h^4) = O(h^0)$ after multiplication by the Pentagon residual's $h^4$ factor, giving a net $h^4$ contribution.

                The conditional-class wrapper records the categorical statement that the Rogers correction's first non-trivial $h$-order is $h^4$ (not $h^2$, not $h^6$). Empirical confirmation: the Borromean Markov-trace closed form's $h^4 = -3/4$ (see QOU.BraidsAndKnots.BorromeanMarkovTrace.h4_leading_coefficient_eq_neg_three_quarters).
