    QOU.BraidKnot.InterfaceComposition #

    Canonical declaration for content block at quantum-observable-universe/mass-theory/canonical-braid-interface-composition.ts.

    Formalises the manuscript interface-composition table: the pp/pn/nn adjacent-interface counts for the three nucleon orderings — canonical (pppnnn), alternating (pnpn), paired (ppnn). The canonical and paired orderings share the profile (1,1,1); the alternating ordering is the outlier (0,3,0).
