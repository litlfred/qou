    QOU.BraidKnot.ColSuperstandardEigen — the OV eigen-equation, reduced to the annihilation #

    Assembles the Okounkov–Vershik eigen-equation for the column-superstandard tableau T₀, reducing JucysMurphy.seminormalVector_base_exists (bean sv8b) to the single remaining debt: the per-column Garnir annihilation.

    OVBaseSeminormalVector.jm_polytabloid_split writes the JM action as

    X_s · e_t = (same-column count) · (−e_t) + (off-column Garnir sum).

    Both summands are settled here for T₀:

    • −i half — ColSuperstandardTableau.sameColumn_count: the same-column count is the row index of s's cell.
    • +j half — offColumn_sum (this file): the off-column sum is the column index of s's cell times e_{T₀}.

    giving X_s · e_{T₀} = (col − row) · e_{T₀} — and col − row is exactly the content of s's cell, which is the OV eigenvalue.

    What the +j half rests on #

    The off-column labels {j < s : (j s) ∉ colStab T₀} are, under column-major order, exactly the labels of the columns strictly left of s's (offColumn_filter_eq). For each such column c, OVBaseSeminormalVector.colGarnir_single_column_of_annihilation collapses the sum over that column's labels to a single e_{T₀} — given the full-group annihilation ∑_{σ ∈ S_{C∪{s}}} sgn(σ)·σ·e_{T₀} = 0. Summing over the col(s) columns gives col(s) · e_{T₀}.

    Two side conditions that the Garnir lemma needs are discharged outright here: s ∉ colLabels μ c (its column differs), and permFixingOutside_colLabels_le_colStab (permutations supported on one column preserve the column pattern).

    The remaining debt — stated honestly #

    offColumn_sum and jm_eigen_colSuperstandard carry the annihilation as an explicit hypothesis; they are conditional theorems, not closures. Nothing here proves the annihilation. Per bean sv8b that reduces to a row collision: for every ρ ∈ colStab T₀, two distinct labels of C ∪ {s} share a row in ρ • T₀. The route is sum_sign_rep_polytabloid_eq_zero_of_family + mtab_sum_sign_smul_of_eq_zero, and it is being worked on PR #4702.

    What this file buys: the annihilation is now the only thing between the corpus and the base seminormal vector — the counting, the column decomposition, the stabiliser side conditions and the assembly are all discharged.

    -- Ref: [vershik-kerov1977] Vershik–Kerov, Young's seminormal form -- Ref: [sagan2001] Sagan, The Symmetric Group §2.6 (Garnir relations), §3.1

      The Okounkov–Vershik eigen-equation for the column-superstandard tableau, conditional only on the per-column Garnir annihilation:

      X_s · e_{T₀} = col(s) · e_{T₀} − row(s) · e_{T₀}

      Since the content of cell (i, j) is j − i, the right-hand side is content · e_{T₀} — the OV eigenvalue. Stated as a difference of ℕ-smuls rather than a ℤ-smul because the content is negative below the diagonal and the ℕ subtraction would truncate.

      The per-column Garnir annihilation — the last debt #

      Everything above carries the annihilation as an explicit hypothesis. This section discharges it, so the eigen-equation becomes unconditional.

      The route is the one recorded on bean sv8b: reduce to a row collision via sum_sign_rep_polytabloid_eq_zero_of_family, produce the colliding label with OVBaseSeminormalVector.exists_collision_earlier_col, and kill the tabloid sum with mtab_sum_sign_smul_of_eq_zero at the transposition swap x s.

      The step that makes it work is that the collision is needed in ρ • T₀ for every ρ ∈ colStab T₀, not just in T₀ — and colPattern_smul_of_mem_colStab says the column pattern is colStab-invariant, so the collision lemma applies to ρ • T₀ with the same column data. That is why the generic-in-t form of the collision (rather than a colSuperstandard-specialised one) is what the assembly needs.

      The per-column Garnir annihilation for T₀ (sorry-free).

      For a column c strictly left of s's column, the signed sum over S_{{s} ∪ C_c} annihilates the polytabloid of the column-superstandard tableau.

      Proof: sum_sign_rep_polytabloid_eq_zero_of_family reduces the goal to a tabloid-level sum over ρ • T₀ for each ρ ∈ colStab T₀. Since ρ preserves the column pattern, s still sits in column c_s of ρ • T₀, so exists_collision_earlier_col supplies a label x ≠ s in column c sharing s's row. Then swap x s is a sign-reversing involution lying in rowStab (ρ • T₀), and the group is closed under right-multiplication by it because both x and s lie in {s} ∪ C_c — so mtab_sum_sign_smul_of_eq_zero applies.
