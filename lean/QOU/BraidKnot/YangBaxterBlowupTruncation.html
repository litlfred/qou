    QOU.BraidKnot.YangBaxterBlowupTruncation #

    THEOREM (uniqueness C2). The Yang-Baxter relation σ_i σ_{i+1} σ_i = σ_{i+1} σ_i σ_{i+1} implies that triple generator subsets |S| ≥ 3 do NOT generate independent blow-up centres in the mass-endomorphism tower. They reduce to pair subsets via YB.

    Consequence: the mass formula has at most quadratic terms in the per-generator spectral coordinates (descended + single + pair).

    Ref: [kassel1995] Kassel, "Quantum Groups" (Springer GTM 155) Ref: [manuscript] prop:mass-endomorphism-tower — |S| ≤ 2 truncation

        σ i * σ (i + 1) * σ i = σ (i + 1) * σ i * σ (i + 1)

        THEOREM. Any triple product σ_i σ_j σ_k with |i-j| = 1 and |j-k| = 1 reduces via Yang-Baxter to a product on a 2-element spectral support.

        Specifically: σ_i σ_{i+1} σ_i has the same spectral support as σ_{i+1} σ_i σ_{i+1}, which is the pair {i, i+1}. The triple does NOT generate an independent blow-up centre beyond the pair.

        theorem QOU.BraidKnot.blowup_truncation_adjacent_triple {R : Type u_1} [Monoid R] (σ : ℕ → R) [BraidRelation R σ] (i : ℕ) :
        σ i * σ (i + 1) * σ i = σ (i + 1) * σ i * σ (i + 1)

        COROLLARY (|S| ≤ 2 truncation). In the mass-endomorphism tower, the independent blow-up centres are enumerated by subsets S with |S| ≤ 2. Subsets |S| ≥ 3 reduce:

        • Adjacent triples {i, i+1, i+2}: σ_i σ_{i+1} σ_i reduces via YB to the pair support {i, i+1}.
        • Non-adjacent triples: factorize via far-commutativity into a pair + singleton, both already counted.

        This is the mass-formula uniqueness constraint C2.
