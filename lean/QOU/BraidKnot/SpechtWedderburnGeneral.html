    QOU.BraidKnot.SpechtWedderburnGeneral — the Wedderburn split, unconditionally, for every n #

    SpechtWedderburnLe12 proved

    k[S_n] ≃ₐ[k] ∏_{μ ⊢ n} End_k(S^μ)

    for n ≤ 12, and named its own limit precisely: "The general case is exactly RSKBurnsideHolds n for general n, which is the RSK correspondence and is the subject of the separate multi-session Schensted programme." That programme has landed (QOU.Mathlib.rskBurnsideHolds, epic qou-nrex brick 42), so the hypothesis is discharged and the n ≤ 12 bound comes off.

    Nothing else changes. The reduction, the reindexing, the two encoding bridges and the criterion are all exactly as SpechtWedderburnLe12 left them; this file substitutes one argument. That the substitution is all it takes is the point — the split was never waiting on representation theory, only on the count.

    Honest scope #

    The split itself, over a characteristic-zero field, for every n. This file does not address SnWedderburnPartitionBridge.sn_isotypic_specht_split or the keystones that inherit sorryAx from it; whether the general split discharges those is a separate question about how they are stated, and is not claimed here.

    -- Ref: [sagan2001] §1.10, §2.4; [james1984representation].
