    class QOU.BraidKnot.ClassicalRiemannHypothesis :
    Prop

    Conjecture (Riemann, 1859). Every non-trivial zero s of the Riemann zeta function lies on the critical line Re s = 1/2. "Non-trivial" excludes the trivial zeros at the negative even integers s = -2(n+1), n : ℕ.

[`conj:classical-riemann-hypothesis`](../../../quantum-observable-universe/stochastic-mechanics/classical-riemann-hypothesis.md).
