    QOU.BraidKnot.QBetaPreArchHeatDepthOneKernel #

    Pre-archimedean depth-1 q-difference backbone of the q-heat flow — the formalize-first layer under the heat-route candidate family for the open c_k-recursion kernel R(q, ξ) of prop:q-beta-c-k-recursion-from-habiro-q-difference.

    The corpus's q-exponential is the abstract grouplike exp_q ∈ G(ℋ_q) over ℤ[q, q⁻¹] (def:q-exponential), characterised by the Jackson equation D_q exp_q = exp_q (prop:q-exponential-taylor is its archimedean realisation). Cleared of denominators, that defining equation IS a depth-1 q-difference equation

    exp_q (q·x) = (1 + (q-1)·x) · exp_q x,

    so every Peter–Weyl heat mode t ↦ exp_q (-(μ_λ·t)) (content-Casimir eigenvalue μ_λ(q), prop:q-heat-kernel-peter-weyl) carries the exact per-mode kernel 1 - (q-1)·μ_λ·t, and finite sums of modes (heat-trace truncations Z_q) satisfy explicit division-free depth-2 compositions.

    Everything here is generic-R commutative algebra (AGENTS.md §7c): no ordering, no Real.*, no archimedean constructs — the statements live at the fibre level where the Habiro kernel hunt operates. The candidate family these lemmas back is UNGATED: per QOU.BraidKnot.QBetaCKRecursionKernelConstraints it must still produce a bar-odd leading coefficient a₁ = -c₃ and survive the constant-depth-2 c₄ refutation. Gate evaluation happens in the Stage-B probe (computations/qbeta/), not here.

    theorem QOU.BraidKnot.jackson_fixed_shift_kernel {R : Type u_1} [CommRing R] (f : R → R) (q x : R) (h : f (q * x) - f x = (q - 1) * x * f x) :
    f (q * x) = (1 + (q - 1) * x) * f x

    The Jackson fixed-point equation is a depth-1 q-difference equation. Denominator-free form of D_q f = f: if f (q·x) - f x = (q-1)·x·f x (the Jackson derivative equation cleared of its (q-1)·x denominator), then f satisfies the depth-1 shift equation with kernel 1 + (q-1)·x. This is the pre-archimedean form of the q-exponential's defining equation — the prototype cyclotomic kernel.

    theorem QOU.BraidKnot.two_mode_depth_two_composition {R : Type u_1} [CommRing R] (F G A B : R → R) (q : R) (hF : ∀ (t : R), F (q * t) = A t * F t) (hG : ∀ (t : R), G (q * t) = B t * G t) (t : R) :
    (B t - A t) * (F (q * (q * t)) + G (q * (q * t)) - A (q * t) * (F (q * t) + G (q * t))) = (B (q * t) - A (q * t)) * B t * (F (q * t) + G (q * t) - A t * (F t + G t))

    Division-free two-mode composition (depth 2). If F, G satisfy depth-1 equations with kernels A, B, their sum S = F + G satisfies the explicit depth-2 relation

    (B t - A t)·(S (q²t) - A (qt)·S (qt)) = (B (qt) - A (qt))·B t·(S (qt) - A t·S t).

    This is the assembly step from per-mode kernels toward heat-trace kernels ∏_λ (1 - (q-1)·μ_λ·t): no division by the Casoratian is needed, so the identity holds over any commutative ring — fully fibre-level.

    (1 - (q - 1) * (μ₂ * t) - (1 - (q - 1) * (μ₁ * t))) * (F (q * (q * t)) + G (q * (q * t)) - (1 - (q - 1) * (μ₁ * (q * t))) * (F (q * t) + G (q * t))) = (1 - (q - 1) * (μ₂ * (q * t)) - (1 - (q - 1) * (μ₁ * (q * t)))) * (1 - (q - 1) * (μ₂ * t)) * (F (q * t) + G (q * t) - (1 - (q - 1) * (μ₁ * t)) * (F t + G t))

    Heat-mode specialization. The two-mode composition with the concrete per-mode heat kernels A t = 1 - (q-1)·(μ₁·t), B t = 1 - (q-1)·(μ₂·t) (content-Casimir eigenvalues μ₁ = μ_λ, μ₂ = μ_λ'): the rank-2 truncation of the heat trace Z_q satisfies an explicit depth-2 q-difference relation whose coefficients are polynomials in q, μ₁, μ₂, t.
