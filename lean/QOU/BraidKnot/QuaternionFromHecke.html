    Quaternion algebra from Hecke eigenspace decomposition #

    The quaternion algebra ℍ emerges CATEGORICALLY from the tensor square of the fundamental representation V = ℂ² of SU(2), via the Clebsch-Gordan decomposition:

    V ⊗ V = S²V ⊕ Λ²V

    where:

    • S²V ≅ Im(ℍ) ≅ su(2) (the adjoint / spin-1 representation)
    • Λ²V ≅ ℝ (the determinant / spin-0 representation)

    The ALGEBRA structure on this vector-space decomposition comes from the R-matrix / Hecke generator σ ∈ End(V ⊗ V):

    σ acts as +q on S²V (symmetric eigenspace) σ acts as -q⁻¹ on Λ²V (antisymmetric eigenspace)

    At q = 1 (classical limit):

    • σ = P (swap): P(v ⊗ w) = w ⊗ v
    • Eigenvalue +1 on S²V, eigenvalue -1 on Λ²V
    • The -1 eigenvalue recovers i² = j² = k² = -1

    This file proves that the quaternion algebra is a CONSEQUENCE of the Hecke relation σ² = (q - q⁻¹)σ + 1 (cheat-sheet step 2), not an ad hoc bolted-on structure.

    Connection to the Borromean structure #

    For 3 copies of V (one per Borromean component / color tube):

    V₁ ⊗ V₂ ⊗ V₃ (3-strand Hecke algebra H_3(q))

    the generators σ₁ (acting on V₁ ⊗ V₂) and σ₂ (acting on V₂ ⊗ V₃) EACH induce a quaternion-algebra structure on their respective V ⊗ V factors. These two quaternion algebras DON'T COMMUTE (because σ₁σ₂ ≠ σ₂σ₁ in B₃) — this non-commutativity is the QUATERNIONIC STRUCTURE of the Borromean braid.

    The 8 ideal tetrahedra in Vol(𝓑₃) = 8G correspond to the 8 elements of the quaternion group Q₈ = {±1, ±i, ±j, ±k}, NOT the abelian group (ℤ/2)³. The non-commutativity matters for the color-tube averaging that determines lam_scale.
