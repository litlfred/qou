    x_μ · T_v = q^{ℓ(v)} · x_μ for v in the Young subgroup #

    CompositionMurphy.xComp_mul_σ is the defining eigenvector property of the Murphy element at a single generator lying in a block: x_μ · σᵢ = q · x_μ. Mathas' Lemma 3.9 needs it for an arbitrary v ∈ S_μ, which is what this file supplies.

    `x_μ · T_v = q^{ℓ(v)} · x_μ`   for every `v ∈ S_μ`

    Why this is not just "iterate the generator case" #

    The tempting proof — write v as a product of simple reflections and iterate — needs every letter of some reduced word for v to lie inside the block set. That is the standard parabolic fact (a parabolic subgroup is Coxeter on the simple reflections it contains), and the corpus does not have it: CoxeterParabolicCoset.parabolic is defined as Subgroup.closure (cs.simple '' J), and only the easy direction (wordProd_mem_parabolic) is proved.

    The route taken here avoids it. The observation is that

    an element of a Young subgroup can only descend within a block (descent_index_eq).

    If v descended across a block boundary at i, then c.index (v i.castSucc) < c.index (v i.succ) — because v preserves blocks — and CompositionMinimal.lt_of_index_lt turns that straight back into v i.castSucc < v i.succ, contradicting the descent. So every descent of v is automatically at a generator of S_μ, and Mathlib's exists_rightDescent_of_ne_one supplies one whenever v ≠ 1. The induction on ℓ(v) then never has to ask which letters a reduced word uses.
