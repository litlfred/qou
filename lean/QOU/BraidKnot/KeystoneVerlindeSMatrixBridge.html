    Keystone residual (i): the DG bridge — Beasley–Witten localization ⇒ Verlinde S-matrix measure #

    WLambdaMTCLocalizationObligation.lean carries the residual-(i) obligation as a single raw field loc_matches : loc λ = d_q(λ)²/D². That field is opaque: it asserts the gauge-theory output equals the Hecke Plancherel measure, without exhibiting the gauge object that produces it. This file does the formalize-first DG step: it factors loc_matches through the actual differential-geometric output of the construction — the modular S-matrix of the Beasley–Witten Chern–Simons localization on the Seifert manifold S³/2I (the Poincaré homology sphere; Reeb = Seifert S¹).

    What the bridge buys (and what it does NOT) #

    The Beasley–Witten non-abelian localization of Chern–Simons theory on a Seifert 3-manifold outputs a modular tensor category 𝒞 whose vacuum S-matrix row S_{0λ} weights the Verlinde measure μ(λ) = S_{0λ}². In any modular tensor category the vacuum row is the published structural identity S_{0λ} = d_λ / D, D² = Σ_λ d_λ² (Bakalov–Kirillov / EGNO; not a fit). So:

    • DG side (gauge output): the localization measure is μ(λ) = S_{0λ}² — the squared vacuum S-matrix row, the genuine TQFT/DG datum.
    • Bridge (this file, sorry-free): μ(λ) = S_{0λ}² = (d_λ/D)² = d_λ²/D² — so μ is the Hecke Plancherel measure, discharging BeasleyWittenLocalization.loc_matches (toBeasleyWitten).

    This reduces residual (i)'s open content from the raw measure assertion to the precise, citable modular-data inputs VerlindeModularData carries. It does not close residual (i): the genuine open content is a category-identification / physics claim — that the BW localization of S³/2I, on the full-Hecke side, is a modular category whose simple objects are the Hecke blocks with quantum dimension the Hecke d_q(λ). That identification is owner-gated (narrow Subgoal-2 reversal, #2503/#2524) and is foreclosed on the SU(2)_k sub-route (Su2SchurWeylForeclosure.lean, #2717/#2727: [2j+1]_q ≠ d_q(λ) off the standard rep, electron block outside the GL₂ image). So constructing a VerlindeModularData whose qDim is the Hecke d_q remains the open, multi-session research step — the bridge states it in gauge-theoretic language and proves everything downstream of it. Honest scope: a formalization cannot prove the physics that the localization yields this category; it can only factor the assertion and verify its arithmetic (the n=5 witness + heckeVerlindeData below show the Hecke side realizes the bridge once the identification is granted).

    Base ring: generic {R : Type*} [Field R] for the bridge (§7c — the S-matrix identity is algebraic); the non-vacuity model and heckeVerlindeData specialise to ℝ (the substrate q₀, Real.sqrt for D).
