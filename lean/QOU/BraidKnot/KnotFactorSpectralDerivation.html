    QOU.BraidKnot.knot_factor_spectral_derivation #

    The knot factor a_K in the unified mass formula is the chirality Gram pairing in the Wedderburn (central-idempotent) basis.

    Base ring (AGENTS.md §7c) #

    Pre-archimedean. The narrative states the pairing over the Hecke base ring — "we work over A = H_n(R, q)… the identity holds exactly in R" — and the Lean now matches: everything below is stated over a generic {R : Type*} [Field R]. Nothing here uses an ordering, Real.*, or any archimedean input; the previous ℝ was a gratuitous specialisation.

    Why a field and not CommRing (the wall-base-ring-minimal adjudication): the Gram pairing is normalised by χ_λ(𝟙)², and χ_λ(𝟙) = dim_q V_λ is a Laurent polynomial in q, not a unit of ℤ[q^{±1}]. So the quotient genuinely leaves the Laurent ring and lands in its fraction field — R := ℚ(q) is the intended base. The division is essential, not a restatable Laurent identity, and [Field R] is the weakest §7c class admitting it. R := ℝ remains an instance for archimedean evaluation at the substrate anchor.

        theorem QOU.BraidKnot.knotFactor_self {R : Type u_1} [Field R] (n : ℕ) (β : HeckeExpansion R n) :
        knotFactor n β β = ∑ lam ∈ β.partitions, β.weight lam * (β.c lam / β.trivial lam) ^ 2

        Self-pairing. Pairing a braid with itself gives the Plancherel sum of squared character ratios,

        a_K(β, β) = Σ_λ w_λ · (χ_λ(β) / χ_λ(𝟙))² ,

        the "sum of positive squares" form. Over an ordered field every summand with w_λ ≥ 0 is non-negative, which is what makes the amphicheiral corollary's sign flip on the alternating block observable rather than a convention.
