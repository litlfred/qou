          FrobeniusSelfDualityForcesLPDualExtraction (Matrix (Fin n) (Fin n) R) R

          De-axiomatisation (concrete model, bean cy3v). The n×n matrix algebra over a field, with the matrix trace, is a genuine instance of the Frobenius carrier — the three axioms are theorems, not assumptions: cyclicity is Matrix.trace_mul_comm (a non-trivial fact in a non-commutative algebra), additivity is Matrix.trace_add, and non-degeneracy is witnessed by pairing a nonzero entry A i j ≠ 0 against the elementary matrix E_{j,i}, which gives tr(A · E_{j,i}) = A i j. So the abstract carrier — hence the Frobenius self-duality — is realisable, not vacuous.
