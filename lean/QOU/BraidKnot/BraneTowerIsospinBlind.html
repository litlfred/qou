    Brane-tower (R-side) is isospin-blind — a structural (b)-exclusion #

    The six-term mass formula (folio/quantum-observable-universe/mass-theory/knot-mass-formula-unified.md) requires every term to be a Markov-invariant trace. Terms 2 and 3 (the brane tower) are trace-invariant in the R-side algebraic form ∑_S z^{|S|} Vol_q^{(S)}(β), where each Vol_q^{(S)} is a Markov trace over the transfer-matrix chain supported on S.

    This file formalises the (b) structural exclusion flagged in docs/audits/2026-06-26-t2t3-trace-invariance-status-synthesis.md:

    In the trace-invariant R-side form, the single-generator stratum T₂ depends only on the strand count n = 3A (one z² per adjacent generator position, by the Markov axiom tr_M(σᵢ) = z, which is reduced-word independent). Hence two isobars (same A, same n) receive identical T₂ — the brane tower carries zero mirror-nucleus splitting.

    The cited invariant is the Markov axiom (prop:markov-pair-z2): the trace of a single generator is the constant z, independent of which generator and of the surrounding braid word. That word-independence is exactly what makes the closed form a function of n alone, and it is carried here as the explicit hypothesis field markov_gen, from which the closed form is derived (not assumed).

    Consequence for the open question. Any trace reformulation of T₂/T₃ puts the entire T/³He mirror split in T₁ (descended tr_M) and T₅ (chirality), never in the brane tower. This is a search-pruning negative, not a refutation of the formula; the production Φ_q(|V̂ᵢ|) specialisation does carry a split, but at the cost of Markov-invariance (and with inverted sign, docs/audits/2026-06-18-he3-brane-tower-structural-gap.md).

    Base ring: generic [CommRing R] per AGENTS.md §7c — no archimedean construct appears (the z, z² values are purely algebraic; the substrate specialisation z ≈ 0.499 lives downstream over ℝ).

    The pair stratum T₃ = (C(n-1,2)) • z⁴ is isospin-blind by the identical argument (constant z² per position pair); only T₂ is formalised here to keep the witness tight, and the corollary transfers verbatim.

    structure QOU.BraidKnot.MarkovTowerT2 (n : ℕ) (R : Type u_2) [CommRing R] :
    Type u_2

    R-side single-generator brane-tower datum on an n-strand braid over a generic base ring R. trGen i is the Markov trace of the i-th adjacent generator; the field markov_gen records the Markov axiom tr_M(σᵢ) = z (word-independent), the cited invariant of the (b)-exclusion.

    • z : R

      Markov parameter z = 1/(q^{1/2}+q^{-1/2}).

    • trGen : Fin (n - 1) → R

      Markov trace of each adjacent generator.

    • markov_gen (i : Fin (n - 1)) : self.trGen i = self.z

      Markov axiom: every generator trace equals z, independent of the word.
