    Placement of knot-indexed convolutions in the bi-filtration grid #

    Compiled formalisation of prop:knot-indexed-convolution-grid. Sibling docstub at braids-and-knots/knot-indexed-convolution-grid.lean.

    This proposition records the row-placement of the paper's knot- indexed convolutions in the bar-class × depth bi-filtration grid. Each knot-indexed convolution lands at a unique (bar_class, depth) cell — the placement function is well-defined.
