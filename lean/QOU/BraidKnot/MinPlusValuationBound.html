        minVal val l ≤ val l.sum

        The bound. For a non-archimedean valuation, the valuation of a sum is at least the minimum of the valuations of its terms. This is what makes the min-plus matrix product a valid lower bound on val(χ), hence a valid UPPER bound on the pole order — and it is only a bound.

            theorem QOU.BraidKnot.exists_strict_of_cancel :
            ∃ (val : ℤ → Val), (∀ (a b : ℤ), min (val a) (val b) ≤ val (a + b)) ∧ min (val 1) (val (-1)) < val (1 + -1)

            Non-vacuity: the bound can be STRICT. Two terms of equal finite valuation can cancel, sending the sum's valuation to ⊤. This is the measured failure mode — two of forty-six (word, λ) pairs, both on channels of dimension > 1, where the minimal paths cancel and the min-plus value under-states the true valuation.
