    Discharging RSKBurnsideHolds #

    QOU.Mathlib.RSKBurnsideHolds n — ∑_{p ⊢ n} dimSYT p ² = n! — was introduced as a target: a well-typed Prop whose docstring records it as "not yet a theorem", its proof "the RSK correspondence … which the pinned Mathlib lacks and which is the multi-session next step".

    It is now a theorem. Two ingredients that landed independently make it a reindexing:

    • OVTopAssembly.sum_numSYT_sq_eq_factorial — ∑ i : PartitionIndex n, numSYT i.diagram ² = n!, obtained by specialising the Wedderburn block-trace identity to the assembled snSpectralData ℚ n;
    • SYTEncodingBridge.numSYT_eq_numStandardYoungTableaux — the bridge between the corpus' two standard-tableau encodings, which is what let the RSK statement (phrased with numSYT) reach dimSYT (phrased with numStandardYoungTableaux).

    The remaining glue is partitionEquivYoungIndex, whose forward map sends p to the diagram YoungDiagram.ofRowLens (p.parts.sort (· ≥ ·)) _ — definitionally RSKBurnsideTarget.partitionShape p, the shape dimSYT counts. So Equiv.sum_comp transports the PartitionIndex sum to the Nat.Partition sum.

    Note this does not by itself discharge DeltaLambdaSignRule.burnside (∑_λ (dimIrrep λ)² = n! over the custom Partition encoding). The difference is exactly the encoding: dimSYT is defined as an SYT count, while dimIrrep is defined by hook-length division. That gap is the roadmap's bridge (1), dimIrrep = numSYT, which is now proved as QOU.BraidKnot.dimIrrep_eq_numSYT (PartitionHookProductBridge). What remains for burnside is bridge (2) — the reindexing List (Partition n) (complete + Nodup) ↔ Finset.univ (PartitionIndex n) — and then wiring the two together.
