    QOU.BraidKnot.knot_operator_transition — knot-type hierarchy of nucleon additions #

    Proposition (prop:knot-operator-transition).

    The operator that adds the (A+1)-th nucleon to a nucleus of mass number A has a definite knot type, following the hierarchy

      1 nucleon: unknot  →  2: σ  →  3: Hopf  →  4: trefoil

    The 4th nucleon completes the trefoil topology (T_{2,3} = electron), explaining why ⁴He has the strongest binding per nucleon among light nuclei.
