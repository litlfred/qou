    The staircase's hook lengths, in closed form #

    The staircase δ_k = (k, k−1, …, 1) is the minimiser of the hook sum at n = T_k, and the REV_n arc (bean qou-hok7) measures every other shape against it. Its value there is a two-constant series, and that series rests on exactly one combinatorial fact:

    the hook length `2j − 1` occurs `k + 1 − j` times, and no even hook length occurs.

    Until now that fact was enumerated — verified for k = 1..40 by rev_staircase_closed_form_and_constant_term_probe.py. This file proves it for every k, and the proof is short because the right presentation of the staircase makes it a one-liner: its cells are {(i, j) : i + j < k}, which is visibly symmetric in i and j, so

    rowLen i = k − i,    colLen j = k − j,    hook (i, j) = 2(k − i − j) − 1 .

    The hook depends on the antidiagonal index s = i + j alone, and the antidiagonal s has s + 1 cells. That is the multiset statement, and staircase_hookSum is the telescoped sum identity the arc actually consumes.
    mem_staircaseYD (i, j) ∈ δ_k ↔ i + j < k
    staircaseYD_rowLen / staircaseYD_colLen k − i / k − j
    staircase_hook hook (i, j) = 2(k − i − j) − 1 on a cell
    staircase_hookSum Σ_{c} g(h(c)) = Σ_{s<k} (s+1) • g(2(k−s)−1) for any g into an AddCommMonoid

    Relation to what the corpus already had #

    QOU.StaircaseCornerHook.staircase_corner_hook_eq_two_k_sub_one proves the corner hook is 2k − 1. That is the s = 0 case of staircase_hook and nothing more; the rest of that file is the EW-residual conjecture, a different subject. QOU.Hecke.staircasePartition is a Nat.Partition, so the arc's YoungDiagram-indexed hook machinery cannot reach it — hence the diagram here rather than a bridge.

    Why the {(i,j) : i+j < k} presentation and not ofRowLens #

    YoungDiagram.ofRowLens would need the row-length list, its sortedness, and then a separate argument for colLen (the transpose). The sublevel-set presentation gets colLen for free: i + j < k is symmetric, so the diagram is its own transpose by inspection, and the two mem_iff_lt_* characterisations then give both lengths by the same omega.

    Base ring (§7c) #

    No ring at all: hooks are ℕ, and the sum identity is stated for an arbitrary AddCommMonoid target exactly as (I') is in HookSumBetaCoordinates. The ε-valued specialisation the arc uses lives downstream, past the archimedean wall.

      theorem QOU.BraidKnot.HookSum.staircase_hookSum {N : Type u_1} [AddCommMonoid N] (g : ℕ → N) (k : ℕ) :
      ∑ c ∈ (staircaseYD k).cells, g (QBeta.WenzlWeight.hook (staircaseYD k) c) = ∑ s ∈ Finset.range k, (s + 1) • g (2 * (k - s) - 1)

      The telescoped hook sum of the staircase. For any g into an additive commutative monoid,

      Σ_{c ∈ δ_k} g(h(c))  =  Σ_{s<k} (s+1) • g(2(k−s) − 1) .

      This is the identity the REV_n arc consumes. Reindexing s = k − j turns the right-hand side into Σ_{j=1}^{k} (k+1−j) g(2j−1), which at g = ε is the staircase's β-side — and with betaSide_eq_hookSum that is the whole of V(δ_k) bar the L-free constant.

      The proof is the antidiagonal fibration: the hook is constant on each antidiagonal (staircase_hook) and antidiagonal s has s+1 cells (staircaseYD_antidiag_card).
