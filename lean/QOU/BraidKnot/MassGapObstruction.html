    QOU.BraidKnot.MassGapObstruction — Step 3, the mass-gap arm #

    Implements the mass-gap arm of Step 3 of the three-step program (docs/workplans/2026-06-11-mass-gap-three-step-program.md §3): tie the mass-gap classical limit to the χ(A) = −6 Kummer obstruction of thm:qou-obstruction-reduction, in §3b-cond conditional form.

    The obstruction-reduction context class (QOU.AlgebraicSubstrate.QouObstructionReductionContext) carries the genuine inputs — Van den Bergh CY-3 duality + Drinfeld rigidity + the Kummer datum κ₀ — from which conclusion (a) dim HH² = 1 is derived. The χ(A)-identification κ₀ = χ(A) (conclusions (b),(c)) is the single honest domain gap obstruction_trace_gap (sorry, cited), packaged as the derived lemma kappa_vanishes_iff_chi. The NS corollaries are wired in QOU.FluidDynamics.ns_obstruction_*. The mass-gap corollary landed here is conditional on that one gap (it was previously "sorry-free" only because κ₀ = χ(A) was an assumed class field — the self-projection now removed):

    • kummer_vanishes_at_two — at χ(A) = −6 the level-2 obstruction vanishes: the Kummer shadow of the μ₂-vanishing of the gap (massGapBound_at_one, the gap function vanishing on μ₂ ⊂ 𝔾ₘ).
    • kummer_persists_above_three — at χ(A) = −6 the obstruction persists at every prime > 3 (the workplan's headline claim, now an actual theorem: p ∤ 6 for prime p > 3).
    • massgap_classical_limit_obstruction — the combined Step-3 statement: the categorical gap bound dies at the classical point (unconditional, from QOU.Torsion.massGapBound_at_one) AND the Kummer obstruction persists at all primes > 3 (conditional on the context). Both phenomena reduce to the divisibility structure of χ(A) = −6, which is the workplan's "Route 1 executed honestly at the limit point computes the obstruction".

    Universality caveat (mandatory per workplan §3). These theorems prove that the QOU framework's classical limit fails — NOT that standard q = 1 Yang–Mills (Wightman-axiomatized, on ℝ⁴) does not exist. Promotion to a Millennium-relevant counterexample would additionally require a universality theorem ("any axiomatized YM with a gap yields a QOU-type structure"), which does not exist. Every statement here is framework-conditional; publication, if any, is per-problem and conditional.

    Kummer persistence above 3 at χ(A) = −6 — the obstruction does NOT vanish at any prime p > 3: such a prime does not divide 6. This is the workplan's "the classical-limit obstruction persists at all primes > 3" as a theorem.

    theorem QOU.BraidKnot.massgap_classical_limit_obstruction [ctx : AlgebraicSubstrate.QouObstructionReductionContext] (hchi : AlgebraicSubstrate.QouObstructionReductionContext.chi_A = -6) :
    Torsion.massGapBound 1 = 0 ∧ ∀ (p : ℕ), Nat.Prime p → 3 < p → ¬AlgebraicSubstrate.QouObstructionReductionContext.kappa_vanishes p

    Step 3, mass-gap arm (combined statement). Conditional on the obstruction-reduction context with χ(A) = −6:

    1. the categorical gap bound vanishes at the classical point q = 1 (unconditional — QOU.Torsion.massGapBound_at_one);
    2. the Kummer obstruction persists at every prime p > 3.

    Together: the mass-gap classical limit and the classical-limit obstruction both reduce to the divisibility structure of χ(A) = −6 in the Kummer tower — the gap's death at q = 1 is the μ₂ face (2 ∣ 6, kummer_vanishes_at_two) of the same class whose primes-above-3 faces obstruct the classical limit.

    See the module docstring for the mandatory universality caveat: this is a statement about the QOU framework's classical limit, conditional on QouObstructionReductionContext.
