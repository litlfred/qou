    QOU.BraidKnot.AppliedOxygen16Uniqueness — concrete ¹⁶O application #

    Thin wrapper specialising NucleusEmptySDPUniqueness to ¹⁶O's strand count n = 3·A = 3·16 = 48 (Z=8, N=8, A=16).

    ¹⁶O is the most abundant heavy-element nucleus in the universe (by mass), and the fourth alpha-cluster product (4·⁴He = ¹⁶O via the ¹²C + ⁴He fusion channel). Doubly magic (Z=8 closed shell, N=8 closed shell).

    Status #

    Sorry-free closure for ¹⁶O's empty-SDP uniqueness via the parameterised nucleus_uniqueness_closed at n := 48. No abstract-axiom dependency. R5-full witness not yet provisioned; this module pre-provisions the uniqueness scaffolding.
