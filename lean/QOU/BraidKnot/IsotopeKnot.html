    QOU.BraidKnot.IsotopeKnot #

    Canonical declaration for content block at quantum-observable-universe/particle-interactions/isotope-knot-equivalence-proof.ts.

    Formalises the manuscript proof that isotope equivalence — K₁ ∼ K₂ iff their proton-block invariants agree, w_{q^Z}(K₁) = w_{q^Z}(K₂) — is an equivalence relation, so the quotient at fixed Z is the element class (element = isotope class). Pure algebra — generic over the invariant target (§7c-clean).
