    QOU.BraidKnot.QuantumDilogarithm #

    — Disambiguation of Φ_q (product form) and Li_2^q (integral form)

    Two distinct functions appear in the QOU mass formulae:

    1. Φ_q(z) = ∏_{k=1}^∞ (1 - q^{-k}z)^{-1} — the quantum dilogarithm product, unbounded as |z| → 1.

    2. -Li_2^q(-z) = ∫0^z (ln_q(1+t) / t) dt = Σ{n ≥ 1} z^n / (n · [n]_q) — the symmetric q-dilogarithm (Kashaev integration limit), bounded in [0.1, 0.82] for z ∈ (0, 1) at q_0.

    These functions are not equal: their values differ by 6 orders of magnitude at z = 0.989 (the typical per-generator volume scale in atomic braid words at q_0). The historical paper-level identity claim Φ_q(z) = ∫_0^z (ln_q(1+t)/t) dt is false.

    The corrected canonical form of the mass-endomorphism tower (per prop:mass-endomorphism-tower, resolution note 2026-05-17) uses -Li_2^q(-|V|) — NOT Φ_q(|V|) — at every site that evaluates the tower on per-generator amplitudes.

    opaque QOU.BraidKnot.phi_q_residual :
    ℝ → ℝ → ℝ

    The quantum dilogarithm product at substrate q and argument z. Defined formally as the infinite product

    Φ_q(z) = ∏_{k=1}^∞ (1 - q^{-k} z)^{-1}.

    At z = 0 the product is empty, giving Φ_q(q, 0) = 1. Unbounded near |z| = 1 (concretely, Φ_q(0.989) ≈ 6.7 × 10^5 at q_0 ≈ 1.110). Used in mass_endomorphism.py and brane_tower_isospin_diagnosis.py (the latter as a diagnostic that exposes the −737 MeV mirror-nucleus pathology when the archimedean specialisation is taken to be Φ_q instead of the corrected −Li_2^q(−z)).

    Axiomatised here pending a Lean infinite-product implementation; the value at z = 0 is fixed by the empty-product convention.

    noncomputable def QOU.BraidKnot.phi_q (q z : ℝ) :
    ℝ

    Quantum dilogarithm encoded as the empty-product baseline 1 plus a z-multiplied residual factor. The phi_q_residual axiom abstracts everything beyond the empty-product term (so phi_q_residual q 0 is the linear-in-z coefficient, with phi_q_residual q z for z ≠ 0 carrying all higher-order behaviour). This is a strict improvement over a free phi_q_at_zero axiom: the empty-product convention is now a theorem — only Φ_q's variation away from z = 0 stays opaque.

      opaque QOU.BraidKnot.li2_q_residual :
      ℝ → ℝ → ℝ

      The symmetric q-dilogarithm evaluated at z. Defined as

      Li_2^q(z) = Σ_{n ≥ 1} z^n / (n · [n]_q)

      where [n]_q = (q^n − q^{-n}) / (q − q^{-1}) is the q-bracket.

      Equivalently (Kashaev integration limit identity):

      −Li_2^q(−z) = ∫_0^z (ln_q(1+t) / t) dt.

      Bounded in [0.1, 0.82] for z ∈ (0, 1) at q_0 — this is the correct archimedean specialisation of Vol_q^(S) in the mass-endomorphism tower. The series has empty first term at z = 0, giving Li_2^q(q, 0) = 0.

The li2_q_residual axiom abstracts everything beyond the empty-sum term (li2_q_residual q 0 is the linear-in-z coefficient; the residual for z ≠ 0 carries all higher-order behaviour).         Canonical tower form (corrected). The mass-endomorphism tower uses -Li_2^q(-|V|) on per-generator amplitudes, NOT Φ_q(|V|). The corrected per-generator term is:

        towerTerm q V = -li2_q q (-|V|)

                z-Habiro Rogers saddle identity (conjecture — formalize-first) #

                The compact-q-dilog face phi_q (root-of-unity quantum dilogarithm; the archimedean realisation of the merged QOU.MassEndomorphism.ZHabiroCauchy eulerCoeff limit) and the Rogers face towerTerm = -li2_q(-|·|) are, per prop:phi-q-aj-machinery-identification, two archimedean saddles of ONE algebraic object (the AJ quantum A-polynomial Â_K): phi_q at q = e^{2πi/N} (root of unity, unbounded), the Rogers form at real q (bounded). The QOU substrate evaluates masses at REAL q₀ (Im q₀ = 0 at the observable level), so the production tower's use of towerTerm rather than phi_q is exactly the claim that the correct real-q archimedean saddle of the Faddeev face IS the Rogers form.

                This identity is OPEN for 𝓑₃ (the AJ-saddle identification is unproven there — see phi-q-aj-machinery-identification.md). We therefore axiomatise it as a conjecture class — the saddle_phi_q_eq_towerTerm field IS the conjectural input (AGENTS.md §3b "Lean formalization of conjectures") — and prove NON-VACUOUS downstream corollaries that compose the conjecture with the proven towerTerm_at_zero / phi_q_at_zero lemmas to derive new facts (they do not project the conclusion; AGENTS.md §3b-cond rule 5).

                Ref: docs/audits/2026-06-22-zhabiro-bounded-rogers-continuation-direction.md §4
