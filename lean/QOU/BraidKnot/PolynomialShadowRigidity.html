    Rigidity of the polynomial / rational shadow from its root-of-unity values #

    QOU/BraidKnot/BWVacuumRowHabiroRigidity.lean states the keystone's "rigidity ⇒ no fit" content as a §3b class HabiroRigidity, whose content field root_injective is Habiro's injectivity theorem, carried as an unproved hypothesis. This file discharges that hypothesis — not for the Habiro ring, but for the carrier the keystone's actual candidate lives on, where the same conclusion is elementary and provable.

    Why the carrier matters (the dichotomy this file resolves) #

    The candidate boundary datum of computations/habiro_interpolation_wlambda.py is

    F_λ(q) = [mark λ]²_q / D(q)²,     D(q)² = Σ_{9 McKay nodes} [mark μ]²_q

    Since [n]_q = (qⁿ − q⁻ⁿ)/(q − q⁻¹), the (q − q⁻¹) factors cancel and F_λ is a ratio of Laurent polynomials with integer coefficients — as bean qou-2v7k already recorded ("d_q = [mark]_q is a global Laurent polynomial, Z(q) is rational; continuation is trivial"). That splits the two carriers cleanly:

    carrier rigidity from root values evaluable at q₀ ≈ 1.10998
    Habiro ring Ẑ[q] yes (Habiro injectivity, unproved here) no — HabiroDomainVanishing.lean
    polynomial / rational shadow yes — proved below yes (it is a rational function)

    The Habiro ring is not q₀-evaluable: its (q;q)_k-adic completion diverges at real q > 1, which is the content of qPochSelf_ne_zero_of_no_root_of_unity in HabiroDomainVanishing.lean. So the evalQ0 field of HabiroRigidity is not innocuous on that carrier — it postulates a map that does not exist there. On the rational shadow, by contrast, both columns hold, and the rigidity needs no miracle: it is Polynomial.eq_of_infinite_eval_eq plus the infinitude of the roots of unity.

    What is proved here (sorry-free) #

    • Complex.setOf_isRootOfUnity_infinite — the roots of unity in ℂ form an infinite set. Proved by injecting ℕ, n ↦ exp(2πi/(n+1)), injective because a primitive k-th root has multiplicative order exactly k.
    • eq_of_eval_eq_on_rootsOfUnity — two polynomials agreeing at every root of unity are equal as polynomials.
    • eval_eq_of_agree_on_rootsOfUnity — hence they agree at every point, in particular at the substrate anchor q₀. This is the honest, unconditional analogue of q0_value_unique.
    • mul_eq_of_ratio_agree_on_rootsOfUnity / ratio_eval_eq_of_mul_eq — the same for a ratio N/D, which is the shape F_λ actually has: agreement at the roots of unity forces N₁·D₂ = N₂·D₁, hence equal values wherever both denominators are nonzero.

    What this does NOT do — the gate stays open, and by a measured margin #

    This proves rigidity from agreement at all roots of unity, of which there are infinitely many. The Σ(2,3,5) fibre geometry supplies five values and cannot supply more: the Poincaré sphere has exactly three exceptional fibres (p₁,p₂,p₃) = (2,3,5) and exactly two irreducible SU(2) flat connections (D(2,3,5) = 2, Fintushel–Stern), and since ℓ₂ = ℓ₃ = 1 for both connections that is 1 + 1 + 2 = 4 nontrivial roots, plus q = 1.

    Five is not close to enough, and the shortfall is quantitative rather than a matter of computing harder. Reducing F_λ = (q^m − q^{−m})² / Σ_μ (q^{m_μ} − q^{−m_μ})² to lowest terms (marks 1,2,3,4,5,6,4,2,3) gives, per mark m:

    m:            1    2    3    4    5    6
deg numer:   10   12   14   16   18   20
deg denom:   20   20   20   20   20   20
values to pin (deg N + deg D + 1):  31   33   35   37   39   41

    So elementary rigidity on the rational shadow needs 31–41 values where the geometry offers 5. The interpolation probe's own docstring says the roots "CONSTRAIN but do not UNIQUELY PIN F_λ"; the table above is that statement made numerical. Closing the gate therefore requires either a source of ~26+ further independent values that is not the three exceptional fibres, or a bridge showing the WRT Habiro element and the rational shadow agree at all roots of unity — at which point the theorems below, not Habiro's theorem, supply the rigidity.

    Scope-(a) throughout: this constrains what the fibre data can pin. It excludes no ingredient and refutes no route.
