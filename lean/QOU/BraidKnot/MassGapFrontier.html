    QOU.BraidKnot.MassGapFrontier — the two research gaps, narrowed to typed form #

    After S1a/S1b/Step-2/Step-3-arm/S1c-constructive (all merged), the mass-gap three-step program has exactly two research-grade residues. This module narrows each from a prose hypothesis to a typed, machine-checkable interface with a verified consuming pipeline, so that discharging either becomes a matter of constructing one instance — with the precise field signature pinned in Lean rather than in prose.

    Gap 1 — S1c physical spectral hypothesis, narrowed three ways #

    The open statement: every non-vacuum excitation of the physical 𝒯_q on End(Aₙ) carries at least one crossing's energy.

    1. Spectral anchor (proved, sorry-free). The per-crossing energy E_×(q) is not an ad-hoc constant: it is the mean of the two Hecke crossing eigenvalues {q, −q⁻¹} (crossingEnergy_eq_eigenvalue_mean), and the eigenvalue separation is [2]_q = q + q⁻¹ (crossing_eigenvalue_separation). This anchors the filtration hypothesis in the categorical source of q (the per-crossing weights of def:crossing-energy are the eigenvalues of the braiding T on S²V ⊕ Λ²V).

    2. Induction decomposition (proved, sorry-free). Strategy (i) of the workplan — Wenzl/Jones-tower positivity transport — has the shape base case + transport step. crossingFiltration_of_base_and_transport proves: filtration at the tower base + a TowerTransport instance (the inclusion H_n ⊂ H_{n+1} preserves the per-crossing bound) ⟹ the full CrossingFiltration. The universally-quantified hypothesis is thereby replaced by two strictly smaller named ones.

    3. What remains is constructing FiltrationBase + TowerTransport for the physical tower. Honest scope note (the base case is NOT a near-term native_decide win): the per-crossing Hecke spectrum is the signed pair {q, −q⁻¹}, and for 1 < q the antisymmetric eigenvalue is negative — −q⁻¹ ∈ (−1, 0) < 0 < E_×(q). The Lean CategoricalTransferMatrix.eigenvalues, by contrast, is a non-negative, monotone, vacuum-0 energy spectrum (its structure axioms), so the physical energy spectrum is not the raw signed Hecke spectrum: an energy functional E : (signed Hecke / vertex-volume) → ℝ≥0 sits in between, and FiltrationBase is a claim about that spectrum. The finite-rank check (H₂/H₃) is well-posed only after that functional is fixed — and fixing it is itself open content (the crossing-graded model in CrossingGradedModel sidesteps it by postulating energy = k·E_×). The transport step is where the Wenzl conditional-expectation positivity argument lives.

    Gap 2 — Step-3 universality, narrowed to an interface class #

    The open statement: any axiomatized Yang–Mills theory with a gap yields a QOU-type structure (the Millennium-relevance gate; the workplan notes it "does not exist").

    AxiomatizedYMWithGap pins the minimal abstract data the transfer corollary needs (an excitation spectrum and a uniform positive gap — deliberately free of any QOU-specific structure), and YMUniversality is the gate itself: a map from such theories into obstruction- reduction contexts with χ(A) = −6. The corollary universality_transfers_obstruction (proved, sorry-free) shows exactly what one instance would buy: the Kummer persistence at all primes > 3 transfers to every axiomatized gapped YM theory.

    No instance of YMUniversality is provided or claimed — constructing one is precisely the open research, and per the workplan's mandatory caveat, until it exists every obstruction statement is QOU-framework-conditional.

    Gap 1a — the spectral anchor of E_×(q) #

    source
    theorem QOU.BraidKnot.crossingEnergy_eq_eigenvalue_mean (q : ℝ) :
    Torsion.minCrossingEnergy q = (q + -q⁻¹) / 2

    The per-crossing energy is the mean of the two Hecke crossing eigenvalues. The braiding eigenvalues on S²V ⊕ Λ²V are q (symmetric channel) and −q⁻¹ (antisymmetric channel); their mean is exactly E_×(q) = (q − q⁻¹)/2.

    This anchors the CrossingFiltration hypothesis in the categorical source of q: the filtration asserts that every non-vacuum excitation carries at least the mean spectral weight of one crossing.

    source
    theorem QOU.BraidKnot.crossing_eigenvalue_separation (q : ℝ) :
    q - -q⁻¹ = q + q⁻¹

    The crossing eigenvalue separation is [2]_q. The distance between the symmetric and antisymmetric channel eigenvalues is q − (−q⁻¹) = q + q⁻¹ = [2]_q — the quantum integer that normalises the Markov trace (tr_M = V/[2]_q^{c−1}).
