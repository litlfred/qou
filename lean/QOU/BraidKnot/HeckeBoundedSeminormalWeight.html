    IsJMWeight on the bounded presentation — and a genuine dim > 1 witness #

    The last piece of the port begun in HeckeBoundedJucysMurphy.lean, and the one qou-t1kv was actually after: a Jucys–Murphy weight vector on a module of dimension greater than one.

    Why this needed the bounded class #

    HeckeSeminormalWeight.IsJMWeight is stated for a HeckeGenerators, and HeckeStableScalarCascade.stdTwo_no_stable_extension proves no irreducible representation is one. So every witness for the unbounded predicate is forced into the one-dimensional scalarGenerators. IsJMWeightB lifts that restriction.

    Transport #

    HeckeGeneratorsBelow.map pushes a bounded presentation along an A ≃ₐ[R] B. That is what lets the explicit 2×2 matrices — which carry no Module (Matrix …) (Fin 2 → K) instance in Mathlib — act as endomorphisms, where Module.End K V supplies Module, IsScalarTower and Algebra together. Matrix.toLinAlgEquiv' is the transport.

    The witness #

    stdTwoBelow3_isJMWeight : on the 2-dimensional (2,1) representation of H₃(q), the vector e₀ = (1,0) is a JM weight vector with eigenvalues q^{2c}, c = 0, 1, −1 — the contents of the standard tableau [[1,2],[3]].

    This is the first IsJMWeight-style witness in the corpus on a module of dimension > 1.
