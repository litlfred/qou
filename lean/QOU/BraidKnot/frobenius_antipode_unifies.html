    QOU.BraidKnot.frobenius_antipode_unifies — S(σᵢ) = σᵢ⁻¹ unifies Term-5 + neutron tower #

    Canonical Lean for content block thm:frobenius-antipode-unifies-chirality-and-tower.

    The Term-5 chirality factor and the neutron-tower closure both use the Frobenius antipode S : σ ↦ σ⁻¹ on H_n(R, q). S is an anti-homomorphism: S(ab) = S(b) S(a).

    The structural primitive the manuscript turns on is exactly the group-inverse map on the unit group of the Hecke algebra: the braid generators σᵢ are units, the antipode restricts to g ↦ g⁻¹ on them, and the anti-homomorphism law is the standard (a·b)⁻¹ = b⁻¹·a⁻¹. This file derives those facts directly for the concrete model S = (·)⁻¹ on any Group G — no axiomatisation of the conclusion. The previous encoding posited claim_antipode_generator and claim_antipode_antihom as class fields and projected them (:= ⟨…⟩); that was a vacuous self-assuming projection and is removed.
