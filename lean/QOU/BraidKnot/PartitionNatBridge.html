    QOU.BraidKnot.PartitionNatBridge — custom Partition n ≃ Nat.Partition n #

    DeltaLambdaSignRule.Partition n (a sorted list of positive parts summing to n) and Mathlib's Nat.Partition n (a multiset of positive parts summing to n) carry the same data: a (· ≥ ·)-sorted list is determined by its underlying multiset, and any multiset sorts to a unique weakly-decreasing list. partitionEquivNatPartition makes this an Equiv.

    This is the missing encoding bridge for the Burnside reindexing (DeltaLambdaSignRule.burnside, Σ_λ (f^λ)² = n!). Composed with SpechtClassification.partitionEquivYoungIndex (Nat.Partition n ≃ PartitionIndex n) it yields Partition n ≃ PartitionIndex n, the reindexing over which the SYT sum-of-squares OVTopAssembly.sum_numSYT_sq_eq_factorial (Σ_i (#SYT i.diagram)² = n!) is stated. The remaining steps to discharge burnside are the List-complete/Nodup ↔ Finset.univ sum transport along this equiv, and the hook-length bridge dimIrrep = numSYT (see the burnside roadmap comment).

    Construction mirrors SpechtClassification.partitionEquivYoungIndex (same Multiset.sort round-trip: Multiset.sort_eq, List.mergeSort_eq_self on an already-sorted list).
