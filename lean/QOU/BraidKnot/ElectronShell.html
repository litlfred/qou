    Electron Shell #

    An electron shell of multiplicity Z around a nucleon-level atom is a cabling H_A(q) ↪ H_{A+Z}(q) plus an SDP-selected binding subword W_bind ∈ BraidWord (A+Z). Binding multiplicity μ(S, q) counts the active dual blocks of the confinement SDP; μ ≥ 2 at magic Z ∈ {2, 10, 18, 36, 54, 86} (noble gas) is the multi-operator regime.

    structure QOU.BraidKnot.ElectronShell :
    Type

    Electron shell. Cabled-strand representation: Z electrons added as extra strands in H_{A+Z}(q) with binding subword drawn from the confinement-SDP active support.

    The bindingWord lives in BraidWord nStrandsTotal where nStrandsTotal = A + nElectrons.

    • nucleus : ConfinedParticle

      The nucleus particle the shell is attached to.

    • nElectrons : ℕ

      Number Z of electrons in the shell.

    • nStrandsTotal : ℕ

      Total strand count A + Z after cabling.

    • cabling : self.nStrandsTotal = self.nucleus.coarseStrands + self.nElectrons

      Cabling consistency: nStrandsTotal = nucleus.coarseStrands + nElectrons.

    • bindingWord : Archimedean.BraidWord self.nStrandsTotal

      Canonical binding subword as a formal Hecke word in the cabled algebra.

            class QOU.BraidKnot.NobleGasMultiOperator (S : ElectronShell) [HasBindingMultiplicity S] (q : Archimedean.R_q) (_h : S.nElectrons ∈ nobleGasMagic) :
            Prop

            Noble-gas multi-operator regime — typeclass (Pattern 4).

            Witnesses μ(S, q) ≥ 2 for a specific shell S whose electron count lies in nobleGasMagic. The computational content is supplied by the electron-shell-ilp witnesses (alternating-σ_0⁻¹/σ_1⁻¹ packings); a class lets each caller bind to the witness for the specific (S, q) it cares about, rather than asserting a universal SDP property.

            • multi : bindingMultiplicity S q ≥ 2

              Binding multiplicity at noble-gas Z is at least 2.
