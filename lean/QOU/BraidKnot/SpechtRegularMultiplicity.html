    QOU.BraidKnot.SpechtRegularMultiplicity — the multiplicity of S^μ in k[S_n] #

    Brick 4b of W1+W3 of the Wedderburn epic (qou-bxw4), after #5139 (W2), #5151 (Schur orthonormality), #5156 (Hom_G(k[G], V) ≃ V) and #5162 (both sides of the dimension count).

    States, in the epic's own vocabulary, the multiplicity fact those bricks add up to:

    dim_k Hom_G(k[S_n], S^μ) = dim_k S^μ.

    Hom_G(k[G], V) is the multiplicity space of V in the regular representation — a k[G]-map out of k[G] is its value at [1], which is #5156 — and W2 is what makes that space measure multiplicity on the nose rather than in units of dim End_G(S^μ). Together with the orthonormality of #5151 this is the classical "each irreducible occurs in the regular representation with multiplicity equal to its dimension", for Specht modules over any characteristic-zero field.

    Honest scope #

    This is the per-partition statement. It does not sum: concluding n! = Σ_μ (dim S^μ)² needs the multiplicity spaces for distinct μ to exhaust and to be independent, which is the decomposition of the regular module and is not proved here. sn_isotypic_specht_split remains open, and the two keystones that inherit sorryAx from it keep their labels.

    The multiplicity of S^μ in the regular representation is dim_k S^μ.

    Immediate from RegularHom.finrank_homG_regular once the Specht module is viewed as a representation of Perm (Fin n). It is stated separately because Hom_G(k[G], −) is only a multiplicity space because the commutant is the base field, which is W2 (SpechtAbsolutelyIrreducible.specht_end_scalar, via the orthonormality of SpechtSchurDiagonal.finrank_homG_specht); over a base where the commutant were a larger division ring this dimension would count multiplicity in units of dim 𝔇, not on the nose.
