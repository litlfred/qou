    The consecutive-label cell-swap constructor for standard Young tableaux #

    The Okounkov–Vershik exchange argument (JucysMurphy.lean: syt_all_connect_to_some) walks every standard tableau toward the row-superstandard base by repeatedly swapping the cells of two consecutive labels a, a+1 when those cells lie in different rows and different columns. This file builds the elementary object that walk consumes: given a QOU.BraidKnot.StandardYoungTableau μ and two cells p, q ∈ μ with entry q = entry p + 1 (consecutive labels) in different rows and columns, the tableau obtained by exchanging their entries is again a standard Young tableau (swapCellsSYT).

    The clean idea (why no cell geometry is needed). Realise the exchange as a value-level involution swapSucc a that swaps the two integers a and a+1 and fixes everything else. It is order-reversing only on the single pair (a, a+1); on every other strictly-increasing pair it is order-preserving (swapSucc_lt). The one bad pair is exactly the values of p, q, and — since entry determines the cell (inj_on) — the different-row / different-column hypotheses guarantee no row- or column-adjacent pair of cells carries the values (a, a+1). So row/column strictness transfers verbatim, with the two hypotheses discharging the single exceptional case.

    Scope: pure combinatorics on ℕ; §7c-clean (no ℝ, no ordering on q); no fit.
