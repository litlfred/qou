    QOU.BraidKnot.sorting_color_energy — Hecke eigendecomposition + sorting projection #

    Proposition (prop:sorting-color-energy).

    Let σᵢ be the Hecke generator on V ⊗ V. Then:

    (1) The R-matrix decomposes V ⊗ V = Sym²(V) ⊕ Λ²(V) with eigenvalues q (symmetric) and −q⁻¹ (antisymmetric).

    (2) Sorting is the projection from Λ²(V) to Sym²(V) at σᵢ.

    2026-09-03 — the projection, and five decorative fields #

    SortingColorEnergyContext carried V, V_sq, Sym2, Wedge2, sigma, q, sym_eigenvalue and wedge_eigenvalue as data, plus one claim field claim_eigenvalues stating item (1) verbatim; sorting_color_energy was a def whose body was that field. AGENTS.md §3b-cond item 5.

    Worth noting separately: five of the data fields — V, V_sq, Sym2, Wedge2, sigma — never appear in the claim. They read as the objects the proposition is about, and carry none of its content: the field claim_eigenvalues mentions only sym_eigenvalue, wedge_eigenvalue and q. So the class looked like it axiomatised a representation and axiomatised two ring elements.

    The manuscript's item (1) is derivable, so this file does not stop at naming #

    With sym_eigenvalue a free element, sym_eigenvalue = q is a definition of sym_eigenvalue, not a proposition about the Hecke algebra — the same emptiness as QOU.BraidKnot.six_term_mass_formula_complete. But unlike that case there is real content within reach, because the eigenvalues are not arbitrary: they are exactly the roots of the Hecke relation (σ − q)(σ + q⁻¹) = 0, which is the manuscript's own ground relation.

    So, sorry-free below:

    • heckeRelation_q and heckeRelation_neg_qInv — q and −q⁻¹ are roots.
    • eq_or_eq_of_heckeRelation — over a ring without zero divisors they are the only roots. This is the substance of item (1): a scalar by which σ acts is forced to be one of the two.
    • sorting_color_energy_of_heckeRelation — derives the block's claim from the relation plus the identification of which channel is which.

    sorting_color_energy itself is the named claim over caller-supplied data (§1a disposition 3), so it is something the derivation concludes to rather than a field handed back.

    NoZeroDivisors is required and is not free: over a general CommRing a scalar can satisfy the relation without being either root, so the classification genuinely needs it. R_q = ℤ[q, q⁻¹] has it.

    What is still unencoded #

    Everything in item (2), and the representation theory behind item (1): nothing here constructs V, the tensor square, the decomposition V ⊗ V = Sym²(V) ⊕ Λ²(V), the operator σᵢ, or a proof that σᵢ satisfies the Hecke relation and acts by a scalar on each summand. What is proved is that given it acts by scalars satisfying that relation, the eigenvalues are the manuscript's — which is the half that does not need the missing machinery.

    Base ring (§7c). q is carried as a unit q : Rˣ over a generic [CommRing R] rather than an element of a field: the only thing inverted is q itself. This is the manuscript's own ground ring — the Hecke relation has coefficients in R_q = ℤ[q, q⁻¹] (LaurentPolynomial ℤ, where q = T 1 is a unit) — and no ordering or Real.* appears anywhere.
