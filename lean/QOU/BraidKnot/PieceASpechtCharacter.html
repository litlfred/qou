    Piece A: Specht Module Character Evaluation #

    This file scaffolds the representation-theoretic route for Piece A (Σ_c h_c² = Σ_c c(c)² + n²) using Specht module characters.

    By evaluating the character of central elements (like the sum of Jucys-Murphy squares) on the Specht module $S^\lambda$, one can extract the sum of squared contents and relate it to the hook lengths via the dimension formula.

    source
    theorem QOU.BraidKnot.PieceA.centralC2_mapsTo_specht {k : Type} [Field k] [CharZero k] (μ : YoungDiagram) (x : Specht.Mtab μ) :
    x ∈ Specht.SpechtSubmodule μ → ((Specht.Mtab.rep μ).asAlgebraHom (JucysMurphy.centralC2 (Specht.nOf μ))) x ∈ Specht.SpechtSubmodule μ

    The endomorphism ρ(C₂) = (Mtab.rep μ).asAlgebraHom (centralC2 …) maps the Specht submodule S^μ into itself: on S^μ it acts by the scalar contentSqSum μ (centralC2_acts_by_contentSqSum), and S^μ is closed under that scalar. This is the invariance hypothesis needed to LinearMap.restrict ρ(C₂) to S^μ.

    theorem QOU.BraidKnot.PieceA.trace_centralC2_specht_module {k : Type} [Field k] [CharZero k] (μ : YoungDiagram) :
    (LinearMap.trace k ↥(Specht.SpechtSubmodule μ)) (LinearMap.restrict ((Specht.Mtab.rep μ).asAlgebraHom (JucysMurphy.centralC2 (Specht.nOf μ))) ⋯) = ↑(JucysMurphy.contentSqSum μ) * ↑(Module.finrank k ↥(Specht.SpechtSubmodule μ))

    The trace of centralC2 on the Specht module S^μ. Since centralC2 acts by the scalar contentSqSum μ on the Specht module, the restricted operator is (contentSqSum μ) • id, so its trace evaluates to contentSqSum μ * dim(S^μ).

    (LinearMap.trace k (MonoidAlgebra k (Equiv.Perm (Fin n)))) (LinearMap.mulLeft k (JucysMurphy.centralC2 n)) = ∑ μ : SpechtClassification.PartitionIndex n, ↑(JucysMurphy.contentSqSum ↑μ) * ↑(Module.finrank k ↥(Specht.SpechtSubmodule ↑μ)) ^ 2

    The central element C_2 evaluated on the full group algebra is given by a sum of dimensions and squared contents, bridging representation theory to hooks.
