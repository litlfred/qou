    QOU.BraidKnot.CrossingGradedModel — S1c, the crossing-graded model tower #

    Implements the constructive half of S1c strategy (iii) ("min-energy-per-crossing counting") of the mass-gap three-step program: the crossing-graded model transfer matrix, whose level-k excitation carries exactly k crossings, each contributing the minimum crossing energy — i.e. the additive energy accounting of strategy (iii) taken as the definition of the spectrum:

    eigenvalues k = k · E_×(q).

    What this module proves (all sorry-free):

    1. The model is a well-formed CategoricalTransferMatrix (crossingGradedTM): vacuum eigenvalue 0, non-negative, monotone.
    2. The model satisfies CrossingFiltration (crossingGradedTM_filtration): every non-vacuum excitation dominates one crossing's energy — by construction, which is exactly the point of strategy (iii).
    3. The full reduction pipeline composes end-to-end: the model tower has a uniform SubstrateGap (crossingGraded_substrateGap), each level is a YangMillsMassGap witness (crossingGradedYMG), and the millennium-conjecture class HasYangMillsMassGap receives its first in-framework instance (instHasYangMillsMassGapCrossingGraded) — for the model, on the substrate window 1 < q ≤ 2.

    What this module does NOT prove (the honest residue of S1c): that the physical brane-tower transfer matrix on End(Aₙ) has a spectrum dominating the crossing-graded model. That single comparison — "every non-vacuum excitation of the physical 𝒯_q carries at least one crossing's energy" — is the entire remaining content of S1c; it enters as the per_crossing field of CrossingFiltration for the physical tower (the class is a one-field constructor, so no further machinery is needed once that spectral fact is established). Strategies (i) (Wenzl/Jones-tower positivity transport) and (ii) (Perron–Frobenius) of the workplan are candidate routes to it; both are research-grade.

    This module therefore converts S1c from "research-grade step" to "research-grade single hypothesis plus a verified pipeline that consumes it."
