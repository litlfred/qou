    W_λ partition-function defect — sub-leading coefficient D(n) (keystone, h⁴ order) #

    Companion to WLambdaDefectClosedForm.lean (the leading C(n)). The q-Plancherel partition function Z(n) = Σ_{λ⊢n} d_q(λ)²/[n]_q! (balanced [m]_q, h = q − q⁻¹) has the even-in-h defect expansion

    1 − Z(n) = C(n)·h² + D(n)·h⁴ + O(h⁶),

    with the leading C(n) = −n(n−1)(2n−13)/144 proved + cross-checked in the companion file/probe. This file formalizes the sub-leading coefficient

    D(n) = n(n−1)(−100n⁴+1472n³−5273n²−203n−828) / 1036800,   1036800 = 50·144².

    Epistemic status (honest framing — read before extending) #

    D(n) is NOT derived here from a closed symbolic collapse the way C(n) is. The companion probe computations/keystone_wlambda_defect_dn_subleading.py extracted D(n) numerically — as the h⁴ Taylor coefficient of 1 − Z(n) via mp.taylor (method-validated: the h² coefficient reproduces the proved-exact C(n) to 0.0; tight 1e-25 rational tolerance), proved its degree-6 polynomiality by constant 6th finite difference, interpolated the unique degree-6 polynomial through n=2..8, and validated it on held-out n=9,10 (PASS, exact). The symbolic expansion of the h⁴ coefficient into named Plancherel moments — the h⁴ analogue of the h² Identity I M(n)=n(3n−1)/2 that gives C(n) — is not yet in hand, so this file does not claim to derive D(n) from the defect.

    What is genuinely Lean-checked below (sorry-free, pure ℤ, no archimedean input — q₀ never appears, this is the h→0 limit): the closed-form polynomial's algebra (factored ↔ expanded), the shared n(n−1) vanishing, the 50·144² denominator and the 144^k ladder of denominators, and the value table cross-checking the witness's 1036800·D(n) integers (held-out n=9,10 included). The derivation of D(n) from 1 − Z(n) stays deferred — exactly parallel to how the companion file defers the general-n Identity I (which needs the hook-length formula, absent from Mathlib). Ref: [manuscript] docs/audits/2026-06-24-wlambda-defect-cn-closedform-crosscheck.md
