    QOU.BraidKnot.SpechtWedderburnCriterion — W1+W3 reduces to one number #

    Brick 5 of W1+W3 of the Wedderburn epic (qou-bxw4), after #5189 (injectivity).

    SpechtActionInjective proved spechtProduct : k[S_n] →ₐ[k] ∏_{μ ⊢ n} End_k(S^μ) injective, hence n! ≤ Σ_μ (dim S^μ)². Since both sides are finite-dimensional, that is all the algebra needed: an injective linear map between spaces of equal dimension is onto. So

    spechtProduct is an algebra isomorphism as soon as Σ_μ (dim_k S^μ)² = n!.

    and the whole of W1+W3 — the labelling, the block sizes, the division-ring collapse — is already discharged by bricks 1–4c. What remains is a single numeric identity.

    What that buys: the residue is combinatorial, not representation-theoretic #

    SpechtStandardBasis.specht_finrank_eq_numSYT gives dim_k S^μ = #SYT(μ), so the criterion restates as

    Σ_{μ ⊢ n} (#SYT μ)² = n!,

    which is the RSK identity — a bijection between permutations of n and pairs of standard Young tableaux of the same shape. That is a statement about tableaux with no algebra in it, and it may be attacked combinatorially (an explicit RSK correspondence) rather than through the representation theory this epic has been climbing. Whoever closes it, in either direction, gets the algebra isomorphism for free from spechtEquiv_of_sum_sq below.

    Note that OVTopAssembly.sum_numSYT_sq_eq_factorial states this identity but carries sorryAx (see docs/audits/2026-08-07-ov-cone-axiom-audit-overclaim-correction.md); nothing here consumes it, and this file deliberately does not import that cone.

    Honest scope #

    The identity is not proved here — this file converts an open representation-theory problem into an open combinatorics problem, which is progress of a specific and limited kind. sn_isotypic_specht_split remains open, and the keystones inheriting sorryAx from it keep their labels.
