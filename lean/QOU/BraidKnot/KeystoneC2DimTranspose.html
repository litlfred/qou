    Keystone C2 — transpose-invariance of the SYT dimension (f^λ = f^{λ'}) #

    Capstone of the C2 Σ-core (KeystoneC2ContentTranspose): the classical (q = 1) case of the keystone weight symmetry d_q(λ) = d_q(λ') ⇒ W_λ = W_{λ'}.

    The hook-length product ∏_c h_c is transpose-invariant (prodHooks_conj, the product analog of sumSqHooks_conj, via a product cell-Fubini cell_fubini_prod + the colLen_conj involution), and the size |λ| is preserved (sz_conj). Hence the Frame–Robinson–Thrall dimension dimHLF λ = |λ|! / ∏_c h_c = #SYT(λ) satisfies

    dimHLF (conj l) = dimHLF l, i.e. f^λ = f^{λ'}.

    This is the q = 1 specialisation of the genuine keystone identity d_q(λ) = d_q(λ'); the full q-version qDim (conjugate λ) = qDim λ lives in MathlibExt.QPartitionCombinatorics (a foldl / get? model that lacks the column-length duality used here) and is the natural next consumer — it follows by instantiating cell_fubini_prod at the q-bracket of the hook in that model.

    Base ring ℕ; nothing archimedean (no q₀). No fit.
