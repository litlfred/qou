    QOU.BraidKnot.SpechtFinrankWired — brick-4 finrank = numSYT, re-routed onto proved independence #

    This module rebuilds the standard-basis dimension identity dim_k S^μ = #SYT(μ) of brick 4, but sources its linear-independence input from the proved triangular core SpechtTriangularCore.standardPolytabloid_triangular' (independence — fully sorry-free apart from Sagan's relation-level dominance core) rather than from the opaque brick-2 stub Specht.specht_finrank_eq_numSYT (which carries an opaque sorry : finrank Sᵘ = numSYT μ).

    Concretely:

    • standardBasis_wired mirrors SpechtStandardBasis.standardBasis — a Module.Basis.mk of the corestricted standard polytabloids — but its independence half comes from SpechtTriangularCore.standardPolytabloid_triangular' (obtain ⟨_, htri⟩ := …; htri.linearIndependent, corestricted via SpechtStandardBasis.linearIndependent_corestrict_iff), and its spanning half is the existing SpechtStandardBasis.standardPolytabloid_span.
    • specht_finrank_eq_numSYT_wired mirrors SpechtStandardBasis.specht_finrank_eq_numSYT but reads off standardBasis_wired.

    The net effect on the OV capstone (OVTopAssembly.snSpectralData's rank field): after the capstone's rank rewrites with specht_finrank_eq_numSYT_wired instead of the brick-2 stub, the rank sorry-path no longer contains the independence stub SpechtStandardBasis.standardPolytabloid_triangular nor the opaque brick-2 finrank stub — its only remaining sorry is the spanning / straightening stub SpechtStandardBasis.standardPolytabloid_straightening (via standardPolytabloid_span), plus the relation-level dominance core SpechtDominanceCore.dominance_relation_exists that the proved triangular' legitimately consumes.
