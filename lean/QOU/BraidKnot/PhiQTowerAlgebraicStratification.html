    Algebraic stratification of the Φ_q tower #

    Narrative anchor for the Hecke-tower / GS-filtration reformulation of the Φ_q tower. The JSJ identification is a separate τ-image conjecture (see phi-q-tower-jsj-bridge); the algebraic stratification stands unconditionally.

    The mass formula sums over algebraic strata indexed by subsets S ⊆ {0, …, n-2}, computed entirely within H_n(R, q):

    𝔪(ϱ) = ‖tr_M(ρ(β_∅))‖F + Σ{|S|=1} Φ_q(‖V̂_S‖F) + Σ{|S|=2} z² Φ_q(‖V̂_S‖_F).

    No 3-manifold language; everything is a Laurent polynomial in q^{±1/2} (or, generically, an element of R = ℚ(q^{1/2})). The norm ‖·‖_F is the Frobenius norm valued in R — not the Archimedean absolute value.

    This stub upgrades the previous : True := trivial placeholder to a class-axiomatised statement, so the proposition has actual Lean content (the four-point identification of the mass formula with a sum over GS-filtration strata) rather than being vacuous.

    class QOU.BraidKnot.PhiQTowerAlgebraicStratification (R : Type u) [CommRing R] :
    Type u

    Algebraic stratification of the Φ_q tower.

    A (R, q)-valued formal datum recording the four-piece decomposition of the mass formula by GS-filtration depth. Caller supplies:

    • R — ground ring (typically R ⊇ ℤ[q^{±1/2}]),
    • q v : R with v² = q,
    • frobeniusNorm : R → R — the categorical Frobenius norm on the Hecke algebra,
    • the four sums (level0, level1, level2_adj, level2_non_adj) that appear on the right-hand side of the mass formula,
    • the equality mass = level0 + level1 + level2_adj + level2_non_adj,
    • the audit hypothesis pentagon_ascension : level≥3 = 0 saying that no level-3 contribution survives at fixed jet level n.

    The class field level_zero_descended records that the level-0 summand is ‖tr_M(ρ(β_∅))‖_F (the descended carrier); the field levels_in_R records that every level lives in the ground ring (no Archimedean leakage). These witness the algebraic-side discipline.

    This statement is unconditional: it does not depend on [VolumeConjecture q] or any τ_arch hypothesis. Compare QOU.PhiQJSJ.bridge (the conjectural Archimedean bridge), which does depend on [VolumeConjecture q].

    • q : R
    • v : R
    • v_sq : v * v = q
    • frobeniusNorm : R → R
    • mass : R

      Categorical mass 𝔪(ϱ) of the vortex.

    • level0 : R

      Level-0 (descended) summand ‖tr_M(ρ(β_∅))‖_F.

    • level1 : R

      Level-1 summand Σ_{|S|=1} Φ_q(‖V̂_S‖_F).

    • level2_adj : R

      Level-2 adjacent-pair summand z² · Σ_{adj} Φ_q(‖V̂_i V̂_j‖_F).

    • level2_non_adj : R

      Level-2 non-adjacent-pair summand.

    • mass_sum : mass = level0 + level1 + level2_adj + level2_non_adj

      The mass formula sums over the four levels.

    • level3plus : R

      The cumulative level-≥3 GS-filtration contribution to the mass. Pentagon ascension says this vanishes at fixed jet level n.

    • pentagon_ascension : level3plus = 0

      Pentagon ascension: the level-≥3 contribution vanishes. This is the categorical content of prop:mass-endomorphism-tower §"Pentagon ascension". Cf. gemini-code-assist review on PR #535.
