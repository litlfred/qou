    The word filtration is not the ideal-power filtration #

    prop:curvature-rank-gb-stratification supplies the word side of the word-depth ↔ partition-depth bridge (bean wpdb):

    J^r(q) / J^{r+1}(q) = { T_w : r(T_w) = r }

    and four content blocks plus one compute script read the partition side — Z^{(k)}(A) = span{ e_λ : gbDepth λ = k } — off it. The audit 2026-08-16-word-depth-vs-partition-depth-bridge-is-unstated established that nothing in the corpus relates the two indexings.

    This file rules out one of the three inequivalent things the corpus calls J^• — and it is one the corpus asserts, not one nobody wrote down.

    Corrected framing (2026-08-16, same day as first landing) #

    This docstring originally called the ideal-power reading "the tempting reading" and "the one reading that would make them relate for free", as though it were a hypothetical the corpus had never committed to. That was an under-claim, and correcting it upward matters more than the retractions elsewhere in this PR. Step 2 of prf:curvature-rank-gb-stratification asserts it outright:

    "The filtration J^ℓ(q) collects elements requiring at least ℓ applications of these relations to reach normal form. Equivalently, J^ℓ is the image of I^ℓ (the ℓ-th power of the rewrite ideal) under the quotient map."

    The two halves of that sentence are not equivalent, and the second is what the theorems below kill. I had read the statement block and not the proof block.

    The reading, and why it is the tempting one #

    H_n(R, q) is presented as R⟨σ_1, …, σ_{n-1}⟩ / I with I generated by the braid and Hecke relations. Given that presentation the ideal-power candidate is canonical, is a genuine filtration by two-sided ideals, and needs no extra data. If it worked, the bridge would be a computation rather than a conjecture — which is presumably why the "equivalently" got written.

    Why it cannot be #

    π(I) = 0, so every positive power vanishes and the filtration collapses to two strata: everything in degree 0, nothing above. A grading with at most one nonzero graded piece cannot reproduce gbDepth, which already takes two values on the partitions of 4 (gbDepth [4] = 4, gbDepth [2,2] = 3).

    Scope #

    Scope-(b), narrowly. This is a structural exclusion — a proved invariant forbids the construction — but of one clause, not of the proposition. What is excluded is the "equivalently" half of Step 2. The other two objects the corpus calls J^• are untouched:

    • rewrite depth — Step 2's first sentence, "elements requiring at least ℓ applications of R_H / R_YB". Not a filtration by ideals, so nothing here reaches it. Its own well-definedness is the open question: the phrase describes a word, and the minimum over words representing a given element is 0 for every element, since every element has a normal-form representative. So it needs a chosen normal form as part of the data, not just a rank function.
    • Coxeter length — F_ℓ = span{T_w : ℓ(w) ≤ ℓ}, the independent definition in alg:mba-step07-gb-filtration. This one is well defined (Matsumoto), and is excluded for a completely different reason: a dimension count. It fails at n ≤ 3 and holds from n = 4. See 2026-08-16-three-gb-filtrations-one-name.

    This file is not evidence that the bridge is false, and none of the above bears on any mass result. Step 2's conclusion may well survive on the rewrite reading alone; what is dead is the equivalence, and with it Step 3 as written, since Step 3 chains through both.

    theorem QOU.BraidKnot.WordFiltration.map_defining_ideal_eq_bot {A : Type u_1} [Ring A] (I : Ideal A) [I.IsTwoSided] :
    Ideal.map (Ideal.Quotient.mk I) I = ⊥

    The image of the defining ideal in the quotient is zero.

    Not deep — it is what "quotient by I" means — but it is the whole obstruction, and stating it is what stops the free route being taken by accident.

    source
    theorem QOU.BraidKnot.WordFiltration.map_pow_eq_bot {A : Type u_1} [Ring A] (I : Ideal A) [I.IsTwoSided] {k : ℕ} (hk : k ≠ 0) :
    Ideal.map (Ideal.Quotient.mk I) I ^ k = ⊥

    Every positive power of the image vanishes. So the filtration J^k := π(I)^k has J^0 = A/I and J^k = 0 for all k ≥ 1.

    source
    theorem QOU.BraidKnot.WordFiltration.graded_piece_trivial {A : Type u_1} [Ring A] (I : Ideal A) [I.IsTwoSided] {k : ℕ} (hk : k ≠ 0) :
    Ideal.map (Ideal.Quotient.mk I) I ^ k = Ideal.map (Ideal.Quotient.mk I) I ^ (k + 1)

    The associated graded is concentrated in degree 0. Every graded piece J^k / J^{k+1} with k ≥ 1 is the zero quotient, since both terms are ⊥.

    The contradiction with the partition side #

    source
    theorem QOU.BraidKnot.WordFiltration.gbDepth_not_from_single_stratum (d : YoungDiagram → ℕ) (hconst : ∀ (μ ν : YoungDiagram), d μ = d ν) :
    d ≠ gbDepth

    The ideal-power filtration cannot carry the partition grading.

    Formally: any function d on Young diagrams that is read off a filtration whose graded pieces above degree 0 all vanish is constant on the diagrams that appear — but gbDepth is not constant on the partitions of 4.

    Stated as the implication rather than as a bare ≠ so that the hypothesis is visible: what a filtration with one nonzero stratum can supply is a constant, and gbDepth is not one.

    The step from map_pow_eq_bot to this theorem's hconst is prose, not Lean. Closing it in Lean would need Z(H_n(q)) and the central idempotents e_λ as actual Lean objects, so that "the grading Z^{(k)} read off the filtration" is a definition rather than a description; neither exists in the tree. So the two halves are each proved and the join between them is an argument — which is exactly the epistemic status the audit assigns to the bridge itself, and it would be dishonest to present this file as more. The real content is gbDepth_ne_on_H4; this theorem is the connector, and it is cheap.
