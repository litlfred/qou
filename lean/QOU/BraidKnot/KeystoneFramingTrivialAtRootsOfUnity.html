    Matching at every root of unity forces the Seifert framing to be trivial #

    This composes two things the corpus already had into a conclusion neither carried alone, and it closes — for the concrete RT/Seifert row — the object BWVacuumRowConcrete.lean named as surviving.

    The two inputs #

    1. The concrete row (BWVacuumRowConcrete.lean, de-opaquing bwS0_eq_hecke): the RT/Seifert vacuum row of the framed S³/2I is bwS0Concrete(λ) = S_{0λ} · θ_λ^f, with S_{0λ} = d_q(λ)/D and the ribbon twist θ_λ = q^{c(λ)} (KeystoneModularTwist.theta, a zpow with integer exponent c(λ) = contentSum λ), f : ℤ the forced Seifert framing. Its capstone bwS0Concrete_eq_hecke_forces_trivial_twist shows the gate forces θ_λ^f = 1 on every block — and stops there.

      That file records the f ≠ 0 case as REFUTED at the archimedean anchor (real q₀ > 1 makes q₀^{c(λ)} not a root of unity), while explicitly leaving open "the fibre root-of-unity form (|q| = 1, hence |θ_λ| = 1) at the pre-τ categorical level, archimedean-continued to q₀".

    2. The reduction (KeystoneRootsOfUnityReduction.lean): for rational functions, agreement at all roots of unity already forces agreement at q₀ — no continuation step. The BW side qualifies: since f·c(λ) ∈ ℤ, the twist θ_λ^f = q^{f·c(λ)} is a Laurent monomial, and it multiplies the same S_{0λ} = d_q/D that the Hecke side carries — so the irrational D cancels from the comparison and what remains is rational. (This answers bean qou-zngd affirmatively for this construction.)

    What is proved here #

    zpow_eq_one_on_rootsOfUnity_iff — q^k = 1 at every root of unity iff k = 0. Elementary, via eq_of_eval_eq_on_rootsOfUnity: the polynomials X^k and 1 agree on an infinite set, hence are equal, hence k = 0 by degree.

    framing_trivial_of_gate_on_rootsOfUnity — therefore, if the de-opaqued gate holds at every root of unity, then f · c(λ) = 0; and if any block has c(λ) ≠ 0, then f = 0.

    Why that closes the route rather than advancing it #

    BWVacuumRowConcrete.lean states in its own terms that "the framing f = 0 carries zero Σ(2,3,5) data (reduces to the compact datum — a tautology)". So the two horns are now both pinned:

    demand consequence
    match at all roots of unity f = 0 — tautological framing, no Σ(2,3,5) content
    match at finitely many roots does not pin: the n=5 vacuum row needs 25..33 values, Σ(2,3,5) supplies 5 (probe wlambda_rational_shadow_degree_probe.py)

    There is no third option that reaches q₀, because the reduction needs all roots of unity to transport a value there, and the fibre geometry cannot supply more than five.

    The load-bearing reading, stated rather than left implicit #

    "Closes the surviving object" depends on one interpretation, which is asserted here so a reader can reject it rather than have to reconstruct it:

    A fibre-level identity of two objects means they agree at every root of unity, not at one chosen level. That is what it means for two Habiro / unified- WRT elements to be the same element — the corpus's own framing, since a Habiro element "can be considered as a function whose domain is the set of roots of unity" (Beliakova–Le, quoted in HabiroDomainVanishing.lean). Under that reading the theorem below applies and forces f = 0.

    Under the weaker reading — the gate holds at one root of unity ζ of order n — the conclusion does not follow, and should not be claimed: ζ^{f·c(λ)} = 1 merely needs n ∣ f·c(λ), which is satisfiable with f ≠ 0 (e.g. f = n). What such a match does not come with is any route to q₀: the reduction needs the full root-of-unity family, and the finite-data horn above shows five values do not pin the function either. So the weaker reading leaves the framing unconstrained but leaves the keystone exactly where it was — with the archimedean step unbridged.

    Nothing here excludes some other transport from a single level to q₀. It says only that for this concrete row, both sides are explicit rational functions, so whatever "continuation" means it has no freedom left: the two either agree as rational functions (⟺ f·c(λ) = 0) or they disagree at q₀.

    Scope — this is a (b), and here is the invariant #

    Scope-(b) structural exclusion, per AGENTS.md §"Before declaring refuted", citing a proved invariant rather than a magnitude probe: the exponent f · c(λ) is an integer, and a nonzero integer power of the indeterminate is not the constant 1 in ℂ[X] — a degree argument, discharged below.

    The exclusion is narrow and must not be over-read. It says: the concrete RT/Seifert row S_{0λ}·θ_λ^f, with the twist entering as an integer power, cannot match the compact Hecke datum at every root of unity unless the framing is trivial. It does not exclude:

    • other constructions of the BW / path-integral vacuum row (a non-monomial twist, a λ-dependent framing, a correction term, an f varying with the root of unity);
    • bwS0_eq_hecke itself under any such other construction;
    • the Beasley–Witten localization program, which is a separate open construction (bwse, PR #4969) and is untouched by this.

    It closes one named route, with its invariant on the table.

    (∀ (z : ℂ), IsRootOfUnity z → z ^ k = 1) ↔ k = 0

    q ^ k = 1 at every root of unity iff the natural exponent is 0.

    The forward direction is the rigidity of the polynomial shadow: X ^ k and 1 agree on an infinite set, so they are equal in ℂ[X], so k = 0 by degree.

    The keystone conclusion. If the de-opaqued gate holds at every root of unity — i.e. the twist contribution q ^ (f · c(λ)) is trivial there — then the integer f · c(λ) vanishes.
