    W_λ keystone — the localization obligation at the physical blocks (n = 4, 5) #

    WLambdaMTCLocalizationObligation.lean states the owner-gated residual-(i) matching obligation as a §3b-cond class BeasleyWittenLocalization, derives the keystone's necessary conditions C1 (normalization), C2 (chirality-blindness), C6 (full support), and gives heckeLocalizationOfPos — the construction of the genuine Hecke q-Plancherel localization loc = d_q²/D² on any conjugate-closed sorted block set at q > 1. Its only worked model is the trivial n = 2 case where every d_q = 1 (so loc ≡ 1/2); that file flags "a stronger n=4 witness with the genuine non-trivial d_q values is future work."

    This file supplies that, at the two physically relevant sizes:

    • n = 5 — the s5-so3 keystone proper (S₅): the seven Hecke blocks λ ⊢ 5, including the electron block (3,1,1) (self-conjugate) and the muon block (3,2) (transpose (2,2,1)). These are the blocks whose q-Plancherel weights W_λ = d_q(λ)²/D² the gauge datum on S³/2I must reproduce.
    • n = 4 — the file's named warm-up, with genuinely non-trivial d_q (the blocks (3,1) / (2,1,1) have d_q ≠ 1).

    For each size we instantiate heckeLocalizationOfPos on the actual partition combinatorics (conjugate-closure + sortedness discharged by decide) and specialise the derived keystone necessary conditions to the physical blocks:

    • C1 keystone{Four,Five}_normalized — Σ_λ W_λ = 1 over the real blocks.
    • C2 keystone_muon_transpose_eq — the muon block and its transpose carry equal weight, W_{(3,2)} = W_{(2,2,1)} (chirality-blindness on a genuine non-self-conjugate pair).
    • C6 keystone_{electron,muon}_pos — the electron and muon blocks carry strictly positive Plancherel weight (no physical block is dropped).

    Nothing here is fitted or owner-gated: it is the formalize-first specialisation of already-proved machinery to the physical partition blocks, making the keystone's necessary conditions concrete at the electron/muon weights the geometric realization must hit.

    Base ring. ℝ is genuine here (§7c): the construction needs 1 < q (positivity of d_q, qDim_pos/sum_qDimSq_pos), an archimedean fact. No q₀ literal; q stays a free real > 1.
