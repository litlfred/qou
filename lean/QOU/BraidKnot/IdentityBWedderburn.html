    Identity (B) for ℂ[Sₙ] via Artin–Wedderburn — fully sorry-free #

    The keystone's second carried representation-theory input — the Plancherel content-square mean

    $$ \mathbb{E}_{\mathrm{Planch}}\Big[\textstyle\sum_{c\in\lambda} c(c)^2\Big] \;=\; \binom{n}{2}, \qquad\text{i.e.}\qquad \sum_{\lambda\vdash n} (f^\lambda)^2 \Big(\textstyle\sum_{c\in\lambda} c(c)^2\Big) \;=\; n!\,\binom{n}{2}, $$

    is proved here for ℂ[Sₙ] without any sorry: the Wedderburn/Schur side is the generic semisimple trace identity below, and the one classical input — centrality of the second Jucys–Murphy power sum C₂ = ∑ₖ Xₖ² — is proved in QOU.BraidKnot.IdentityBCentrality (Jucys' theorem: at the coefficient level C₂ = binom(n,2)·1 + class-sum of 3-cycles).

    What is proved sorry-free here #

    The entire Wedderburn/Schur "side 2" is discharged without any Sₙ-specific Okounkov–Vershik data by the generic semisimple trace identity:

    • AbsTrace.trace_piMapEndo — trace of a block-diagonal endomorphism of ∀ i, Mᵢ is the sum of block traces (Fintype.induction_empty_option).
    • AbsTrace.trace_mulLeft_pi_matrix — for a central element of ∏ᵢ Matₐᵢ(F) whose i-th component is the scalar matrix ωᵢ·1, the regular-representation trace of mulLeft is ∑ᵢ dᵢ²·ωᵢ.
    • AbsTrace.trace_mulLeft_central_eq_block_sum — the abstract discharge. For a central z in a finite-dimensional semisimple algebra A over an algebraically closed field F, there are block data (d, ω) with ∑ dᵢ² = finrank_F A and trace_F(mulLeft z) = ∑ dᵢ²·ωᵢ, via Mathlib's IsSemisimpleRing.exists_algEquiv_pi_matrix_of_isAlgClosed (Artin–Wedderburn) + Matrix.center_eq_range (concrete Schur) + the Pi-trace decomposition.

    Specialising to A = ℂ[Sₙ] (semisimple by Maschke, alg-closed by IsAlgClosed ℂ) and z = C₂, and reading the easy-side trace(mulLeft C₂) = n!·binom(n,2) off RegularRepTrace.trace_centralC2_eq_factorial_mul_choose_two, gives:

    • identityB_via_wedderburn — Identity (B) given the hypothesis C₂ ∈ Set.center ℂ[Sₙ]. The quadratic weighting and ∑ dᵢ² = n! are produced, not assumed.
    • identityB_via_wedderburn_unconditional — fully sorry-free: the centrality hypothesis is discharged by IdentityBCentrality.centralC2_mem_center. Axioms: [propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound].

    Why this is more atomic than the carried OV bundle #

    RegularRepBlockTrace.identityB_conditional carries the full SnWedderburnSpectralData bundle (the labelled block decomposition: partition ↔ irreducible, dim = #SYT, JM-eigenvalue = contents). Here the conditional form identityB_via_wedderburn assumes only the centrality of C₂ — the block structure, the quadratic dimension weighting, and ∑ dᵢ² = n! are machine-derived from Artin–Wedderburn — and the unconditional theorem carries no remaining Sₙ-specific hypothesis: that centrality is proved in IdentityBCentrality.

    No native_decide, no : True, no statement-weakening; the base ring is ℂ exactly where the representation theory needs alg-closedness + characteristic zero.

    Ref: [vershik-kerov1977] (Okounkov–Vershik / Jucys central elements).

      Specialisation to ℂ[S_n]: Identity (B) modulo only the centrality of C₂ #

      The abstract trace_mulLeft_central_eq_block_sum discharges the entire Wedderburn/Schur side 2 without any S_n-specific Okounkov–Vershik input. Specialising to A = ℂ[S_n] (semisimple by Maschke, alg-closed by IsAlgClosed ℂ) and z = C₂ yields Identity (B) — ∑ dᵢ²·ωᵢ = n!·binom(n,2) with ∑ dᵢ² = n! — with the single residual hypothesis that C₂ ∈ Set.center ℂ[S_n].

      This is a much smaller input than the full SnWedderburnSpectralData bundle carried by RegularRepBlockTrace.identityB_conditional: that bundle assumes the existence of the labelled block decomposition (partition ↔ irreducible, dim = #SYT, JM-eigenvalue = contents). Here ONLY the centrality of C₂ is assumed; the block structure, the quadratic dimension weighting, and the sum-of-squares ∑ dᵢ² = n! are all produced by Artin–Wedderburn + the Pi-trace decomposition.
