    QOU.BraidKnot.PerPartitionKLambda — per-partition $k_\lambda$ rule #

    The surviving open form of the level-selection rule n_⋆ after the refutation chain of PR #638 (scalar-eigenvalue + per-eigenvalue operator-valued forms refuted at uniform k) and the partial closures of PR #639 (Type-B fold-depth fits D + T, fails ³He; 4 simple 2-input rules also fail).

    The empirical finding (per computations/probe_phiq_per_partition_klambda.py): re-assigning k_λ = 1 for the maximum eigenvalue magnitude partition per atom, with k_λ = 2 for all other Wedderburn blocks, collapses all three atoms' brane-tower magnitudes to |tower| < 2 (vs uniform-k towers of 5–11).

    This file encodes the per-partition k_λ predicate as a Lean structure. The conjectural claim (that the substrate-derivable selector matches the empirical max-|μ| pattern) is the open form.

    LaTeX label: rem:per-partition-k-lambda-rule

    What this encodes #

    • KLambdaRule — a partition-indexed assignment λ → ℕ_≥1.
    • PerPartitionRecovery — the propositional content: there exists a KLambdaRule derivable from substrate data alone that closes the AME light-isotope binding to within eps.

    Conjectural status #

    Per AGENTS.md §3b: this is a structural axiom. The OPEN content is the structural existence of the rule (substrate-derivable, not per-atom retuned). This file does not close the conjecture; it makes the propositional content checkable.

                          def QOU.BraidKnot.PerPartitionKLambda.OpenPerPartitionConjecture :
                          Prop

                          The open structural form. The conjecture that there exists a substrate-derivable isMaxEigSpectrum predicate such that the candidateRule based on it closes the AME binding for all atoms {D, T, ³He} within eps = 0.05 (= 5%).

                          This is the surviving open form of conj:brane-tower-z-habiro after PR #638 refuted scalar and per-eigenvalue forms and PR #639 showed the per-partition relaxation is sufficient.

                          "Substrate-derivable" excludes per-atom retuning: the same isMaxEigSpectrum predicate must apply to all atoms.

                            theorem QOU.BraidKnot.PerPartitionKLambda.open_per_partition_witnessed_empirically :
                            OpenPerPartitionConjecture

                            The OpenPerPartitionConjecture is CONDITIONALLY witnessed by the empirical max-|μ| selector — but the predicate isMaxEigSpectrum is itself decidable only via eigenvalue evaluation, not via a closed-form substrate rule. Closing the structural identification of isMaxEigSpectrum is the open R&D part flagged in rem:per-partition-k-lambda-rule.

                            What's CLOSED vs OPEN #

                            CLOSED (this file):

                            • The propositional STRUCTURE of the per-partition rule (KLambdaRule, candidateRule, PerPartitionRecovery).
                            • Per-atom numerical brackets |tower| < 2 for all three atoms under the empirically-derived constants from PR #639's witness.
                            • Existence (OpenPerPartitionConjecture) — trivially, with a placeholder selector. The CONTENT (substrate-derivability) is NOT closed.

                            OPEN:

                            • Structural identification of isMaxEigSpectrum as a closed-form substrate rule. Candidate routes per rem:per-partition-k-lambda-rule:
                              • Hoefsmit hook-content asymptotic for max eigenvalue magnitude
                              • Type-B-folded-crystal weight asymptotic
                              • Kashiwara-crystal action of the braid word
                            • Generalisation beyond {D, T, ³He} to ⁴He and heavier isotopes (where multiplicity profiles may be richer than the n = 6, 9 cases tested here).
                            • The structural lift formula carrying Φ_q^{(N-1)} → Φ_q^{(N)} consistent with the per-partition rule.
