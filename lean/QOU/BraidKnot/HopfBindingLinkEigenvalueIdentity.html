    QOU.BraidKnot.HopfBindingLinkEigenvalueIdentity #

    Categorical identification of the Hopf-link Hoefsmit-eigenvalue structure — supporting lemma for conj:canonical-knot-id-sdp's binding-link uniqueness for the deuteron.

    Background (PR #1712, 2026-06-02) #

    The deuteron's canonical binding link is L_H = σ_1² (Hopf link) per the structural decomposition in conj-canonical-knot-id-sdp.md. The empirical SDP-feasibility probe binding_link_psd_feasibility_probe.py ran at H_3 and found L_H = σ_1² in Hoefsmit standard-(2,1) at q_0:

    min eigenvalue = +0.811653754... = q_0⁻² (10+ digit match) max eigenvalue = +1.232052454... = q_0²

    Both eigenvalues strictly positive ⇒ L_H is PSD-feasible in the standard block at q_0. Together with the trivial and sign blocks (also positive), this discharges the single-term SDP-feasibility leg of PSD-feasibility for CanonicalKnotIdSDP .deuterium in conj-canonical-knot-id-sdp.lean.

    It does not discharge a field, and no longer names one. This used to cite bindingKPSDFeasible_holds, a Prop-universe field deleted on 2026-09-07: being a proposition held as data, it was whatever the instance chose, and every instance chose the decidable proxy evenPower. So the citation pointed at a field that carried none of the eigenvalue content proved here. What the class requires is bindingKEvenPower, a necessary condition; the positivity below is the stronger, semantic fact, and it remains outside the class.

    What this file adds (sorry-free) #

    The categorical identification

    σ_k² in Hoefsmit standard-(2,1) basis ↦ diag(q², q⁻²)

    — eigenvalues are EXACTLY (q², q⁻²) for ANY q > 0, not just numerically at q_0. Combined with q > 0 ⇒ q² > 0 and (q²)⁻¹ > 0, this gives PSD-feasibility of σ_k² in the standard block at every substrate value, not just q_0.

    This strengthens the existing evenPower decidable proxy (which only knows σ²ⁿ has the SHAPE of a Hopf-link-power) to a categorical guarantee: σ_k² ⪰ 0 in Hoefsmit standard-(2,1) for all q > 0.

    §3b-cond status #

    This is not a §3b-cond conjectural class — it's an unconditional structural lemma about the Hoefsmit seminormal representation. The eigenvalues of σ_k in the standard-(2,1) rep are (+q, −q⁻¹) per Hoefsmit (1979); squaring gives (q², q⁻²). No sorry.

    The downstream uniqueness statement (CanonicalKnotIdSDP_deuterium instance is unique modulo conjugation) remains a §3b-cond conditional theorem proved elsewhere modulo a strict-complementarity sorry.

    noncomputable def QOU.BraidKnot.hoefsmitStandardEig (q : ℝ) :
    ℝ × ℝ

    The two eigenvalues of σ_k in the Hoefsmit standard-(2,1) representation at parameter q: +q and −q⁻¹.

    Per Hoefsmit (1979) §3 / Geck-Pfeiffer (2000) §5: the seminormal generators of H_n(q) in the (n−1, 1) standard rep are diagonal on the basis indexed by reverse standard Young tableaux of shape (n−1, 1). For n=3 this gives a 2×2 diagonal matrix with diagonal (+q, −q⁻¹).
