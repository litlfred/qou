    QOU.BraidKnot.AJTrefoilRootEval #

    Root-of-unity evaluation of the trefoil Rosso–Jones cyclotomic sum — the two lemmas that discharge the cited evaluation cores trefoil_eval_at_root_prev / trefoil_eval_at_root_next in QOU.BraidKnot.AJTrefoilAnnihilation:

    • trP_sum_at_root_self — under (w⁴)^M = 1, Σ_{j≤M} (−1)^j w^{6j(j+1)} [2j+1]_{w²} = (−1)^M w^{6M(M+2)} [M+1]_{w²};
    • trP_sum_at_root_offset — under (w⁴)^{n+2} = 1, the same closed form holds for the sum truncated at n.

    Proof architecture #

    Multiplying by w²(w² − w⁻²) turns the quantum-integer sum into a difference of two signed pure-power sums S₁ − S₂ (sum_bridge), and the closed form into a two-term power difference (rhs_expand) — both exact. Then three lemmas close the evaluation:

    1. Shift (s1_eq_shift, exact): re-indexing j ↦ j+1 maps the S₁ summand onto the negated summand of the Gauss-type sum G(m) = Σ_{j≤m} (−1)^j w^{6j²−2j}, so S₁(m) = 1 − G(m+1).
    2. Reflection (s2_eq_reflect, uses the root): j ↦ m−j maps the S₂ summand onto the G summand times w^{12mj}, and w^{12mj} = ((w⁴)^m)^{3j} = 1, so S₂(m) = (−1)^m w^{6m²+2m} G(m).
    3. Dichotomy (gauss_dichotomy, uses the root): (G(m) − 1)(1 + β) = 0 with β = (−1)^m w^{6m²+2m}. Since β² = 1, either β = −1 (automatic for m odd) and the bracket vanishes, or β = 1, which forces m even and (w²)^m = 1, and then the residual Gauss-type sum collapses to its last term (gauss_sum_eq_one): with d = orderOf (w²), the summand is antiperiodic (d odd) or pairwise cancelling over each period (d even, where (w²)^{d/2} = −1 is forced), so the first m terms cancel in blocks and G(m) = 1.

    The offset variant reflects the extended sum S₂(n+2) (whose range matches the root (w⁴)^{n+2} = 1), peels the top two terms of each sum, and the same dichotomy at m = n+2 closes it — the peeled top term of S₂ cancels β exactly, and the two survivors match the target under two multiplications by (w⁴)^{n+2} = 1.

    All identities were pre-verified exactly (sympy, symbolic w) and at roots of unity (mpmath, 40 dps) before formalisation; see the session scratch checks accompanying PR #3434.

    theorem QOU.BraidKnot.AJTrefoil.trP_sum_at_root_self {K : Type u_1} [Field K] {w : K} (hw : w ≠ 0) (hv : w ^ 2 - (w ^ 2)⁻¹ ≠ 0) {M : ℕ} (hroot : (w ^ 4) ^ M = 1) :
    ∑ j ∈ Finset.range (M + 1), (-1) ^ j * w ^ (6 * (j * (j + 1))) * qInt (w ^ 2) (2 * j + 1) = (-1) ^ M * w ^ (6 * (M * (M + 2))) * qInt (w ^ 2) (M + 1)

    Self-matched root evaluation — under (w⁴)^M = 1 and w⁴ ≠ 1, the trefoil Rosso–Jones cyclotomic sum has the closed form (−1)^M w^{6M(M+2)} [M+1]_{w²}. (Stated with the explicit sum so that trP in AJTrefoilAnnihilation unfolds to it definitionally.)

    ∑ j ∈ Finset.range (n + 1), (-1) ^ j * w ^ (6 * (j * (j + 1))) * qInt (w ^ 2) (2 * j + 1) = (-1) ^ n * w ^ (6 * (n * (n + 2))) * qInt (w ^ 2) (n + 1)

    Offset root evaluation — under (w⁴)^{n+2} = 1 and w⁴ ≠ 1, the trefoil Rosso–Jones cyclotomic sum truncated at n has the same closed form (−1)^n w^{6n(n+2)} [n+1]_{w²}.
