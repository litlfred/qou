    QOU.BraidKnot.ColoredJonesRecurrence — the order-(N+1) colored-Jones recurrence #

    The general form of KnotOperatorRecurrence (the twist-knot 2×2 case): a knot operator that is a power of a fixed braiding element acts on a finite-dimensional module — for colored Jones at colour N this module is (N+1)-dimensional — so it satisfies a monic degree-d polynomial relation (Cayley–Hamilton, d = N+1), and its powers therefore obey an order-d linear recurrence. Applying the Markov trace / closure (a linear functional) carries the recurrence to the knot invariants: the resulting recurrence operator is the quantum A-polynomial (the AJ structure).

    This file proves the inheritance step in full generality, decoupled from any particular Cayley–Hamilton coefficient formula: whatever monic relation M^d = ∑_{k<d} c_k · M^k the operator satisfies, the power sequence and its trace obey the same order-d recurrence. The d = 2 instance is KnotOperatorRecurrence.trace_two_step (the twist-knot Jones recurrence); d = N+1 is colour N.

    • pow_recurrence_of_relation — M^{n+d} = ∑_{k<d} c_k · M^{n+k};
    • trace_recurrence_of_relation — tr(M^{n+d}) = ∑_{k<d} c_k · tr(M^{n+k}) (the closure / colored-Jones recurrence).

    Generic CommRing (§7c); the existence of such a relation for a colour-N knot operator is Cayley–Hamilton on its (N+1)-dimensional module (cited, not re-proved here). The bar-odd Q_β D-ladder has no such finite recurrence — proven non-q-holonomic (KC22) — which is exactly its escape from this machinery (audit docs/audits/2026-07-18-knot-operator-gram-recurrence.md).

    Power recurrence from a monic relation. If a knot operator M satisfies M^d = ∑_{k<d} c_k · M^k (its degree-d Cayley–Hamilton relation), then its powers obey the order-d linear recurrence M^{n+d} = ∑_{k<d} c_k · M^{n+k}.

    Colored-Jones / closure recurrence. Applying the (linear) Markov trace — the closure of the tangle, i.e. the knot invariant — to pow_recurrence_of_relation gives the invariant's order-d recurrence: tr(M^{n+d}) = ∑_{k<d} c_k · tr(M^{n+k}). The recurrence operator is the quantum A-polynomial (AJ).
