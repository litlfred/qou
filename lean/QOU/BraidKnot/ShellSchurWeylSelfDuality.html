    QOU.BraidKnot.ShellSchurWeylSelfDuality #

    Canonical declaration for content block at quantum-observable-universe/particle-interactions/shell-schur-weyl-self-duality-proof.ts.

    Formalises the manuscript proof's structural core: Schur–Weyl duality indexes GLₖ- and Sₙ-irreps by the same partition λ ⊢ n, and GLₖ-duality is implemented by the partition transpose λ ↦ λ*. The transpose is an involution, so it is a bijection of the index set, and its fixed points are exactly the self-conjugate partitions (the self-dual shells).
