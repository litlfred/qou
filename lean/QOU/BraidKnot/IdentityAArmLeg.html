    QOU.BraidKnot.IdentityAArmLeg — toward the general (all-n) proof of Identity (A) #

    Status: COMPLETE — statistics_combine and identityA are proved sorry-free (all-n, every Young diagram), on Mathlib's YoungDiagram:

    (A) Σ_cells hook(c)² = Σ_cells content(c)² + n².

    #print axioms for both shows only [propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound] (no sorryAx, no native_decide/decide). It complements IdentityIContentDecomposition.lean, whose list-model defs are tuned for native_decide (exhaustive n ≤ 22, all 4507 partitions) and are hostile to symbolic proof; this is the symbolic, genuinely-general counterpart.

    Foundation. Built directly on Mathlib.Combinatorics.Young.YoungDiagram (μ.cells, μ.rowLen, μ.colLen, μ.transpose). The transpose API (rowLen_transpose : μ.transpose.rowLen i = μ.colLen i, and colLen_transpose) is exactly the conjugation relation the heart's leg-sums need — leg μ over μ.cells matches arm μ.transpose over μ.transpose.cells under the cell swap (i,j) ↦ (j,i). Hook/arm/leg/content are ℤ-valued to avoid ℕ-subtraction.

    Proof architecture (all sorry-free) #

    identityA = sumSq_hook_expand ∘ statistics_combine. The expansion is pure ring; the heart statistics_combine is discharged by reducing each of its six statistic sums and the cross term:

    • Squares are reflections. sum_arm_sq_eq_sum_jsq (Σ arm² = Σ (col)², per-row reflection j ↦ rowLen i − 1 − j) and sum_leg_sq_eq_sum_isq (Σ leg² = Σ (row)², its transpose via sum_transpose_swap); likewise the linear sum_arm_eq_sum_col, sum_leg_eq_sum_row.
    • The cross term Σ arm·leg is a cell-pair cardinality. arm_mul_leg_card writes arm·leg = card(Ioo j (rowLen i) ×ˢ Ioo i (colLen j)); sum_armleg_eq_card_bigNE then identifies Σ arm·leg = card(bigNE) (cells R strictly NE of S) by fiber_card over the corner map (card_eq_sum_card_fiberwise).
    • Counting bigNE. card_product_partition is the 9-class trichotomy of cells ×ˢ cells (split_ifs <;> omega); with the swap symmetries (card_bigSW_eq, card_bigSE_eq), the NW-rectangle count (card_bigNW_eq : card(bigNW) = Σ row·col — left-justification fills the rectangle), the axis cards (card_rowEq_eq, card_colEq_eq) and the diagonal (card_diag_eq), this yields the closed form two_sum_armleg: 2·Σ arm·leg = n² + n − Σ rowLen(c.1) − Σ colLen(c.2) − 2·Σ(c.1·c.2).
    • Final ring/linarith. sum_rowLen_cells/sum_colLen_cells (rowLen = arm + col + 1) feed two_sum_armleg into the reflections; linarith closes.

    This is the symbolic realisation of the arm-leg / co-arm-co-leg decomposition of §"Proof of (A)" (A := arm+1, L := leg+1), with the genuinely-2D cross term handled by the SE-pair count #{R SE of S} = Σ c.1·c.2.
