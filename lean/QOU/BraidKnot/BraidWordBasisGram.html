    QOU.BraidKnot.BraidWordBasisGram — non-diagonal Gram in σ-basis #

    Encodes the structural finding from PR #639 cycle 7 (probe_braid_word_basis_gram.py): the Gram matrix G_wv = tr_M(σ_w · σ_v) in the paper-canonical reduced-braid-word basis of H_n(q) (per AGENTS.md §7a Hecke–Skein convention) is not diagonal.

    This is in contrast to the per-partition basis, where Wedderburn orthogonality forces the off-diagonal Gram to be identically zero (PR #639 cycle 6: probe_off_diagonal_gram_wedderburn.py confirmed 110/110 partition pairs of H_6 give zero off-diagonal).

    Empirical content (from witness) #

    At H_3(q_0) in the 6-element reduced-word basis {e, σ_0, σ_1, σ_0σ_1, σ_1σ_0, σ_0σ_1σ_0}:

    • 15/15 off-diagonal entries non-zero (numerical, |·| > 1e-9)
    • Max off-diagonal: G_{σ_0σ_1, σ_1σ_0} ≈ 1.22 — larger than the diagonal G_{σ_0σ_1, σ_0σ_1} ≈ 0.36
    • Symmetric: G_wv = G_vw (Markov-trace cyclicity)
    • Substrate-only: each entry is a Z[q^{±1}] polynomial in q evaluated at q_0 via the Wenzl bridge

    What this Lean file proves vs leaves open #

    CLOSED (this file):

    • The structural claim that the Gram is non-diagonal at H_3(q_0). Encoded via witness-recorded numerical bracket on the explicit off-diagonal entry G_{σ_0σ_1, σ_1σ_0} > 1.0.
    • Symmetry of the Gram (Markov-trace cyclicity).

    OPEN (deferred):

    • The closed-form symbolic expressions for the 6×6 Gram entries as Z[q^{±1}] polynomials in q. Tractable: each is a sum of Wenzl bridge contributions over partitions of 3.
    • The full σ-basis Gram for higher n (H_6 at deuteron, H_9 at T / ³He).

    LaTeX label: rem:per-partition-k-lambda-rule (the appended "Paper-canonical σ_w basis" section).

                Open: closed-form σ-basis Gram as Z[q,q⁻¹] polynomials #

                The full 6×6 Gram at H_3(q) can be computed in closed form as polynomials in q, q⁻¹ via the Wenzl bridge. Each entry is a sum over three partitions

                G_{wv}(q) = y_3 · χ^[3](σ_w σ_v) + y_2,1 · χ^[2,1](σ_w σ_v) + y_1,1,1 · χ^[1,1,1](σ_w σ_v)

                with y_λ from Wenzl Schur principal specialization and χ^λ from Hoefsmit seminormal matrix entries. Each summand lives in Z[q, q⁻¹], so the Gram matrix is entry-wise in Z[q, q⁻¹] — over the same parameter ring as the Habiro completion / brane-tower bicompletion.

                The closed-form derivation is a mechanical Hecke-algebra calculation; deferred to a downstream proof-builder pass.
