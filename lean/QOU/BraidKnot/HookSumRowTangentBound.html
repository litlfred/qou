    The row-indexed tangent lower bound on the hook sum #

    For a Young diagram μ with n cells and ℓ = μ.colLen 0 rows, the hook sum has the row form

    2 · Σ_{c∈μ} hook(c) = Σ_{i<ℓ} λ_i (λ_i + 2i + 1),        λ_i = μ.rowLen i   (rows 0-indexed),

    obtained from sum_hookZ_eq, two_mul_sum_snd and sum_fst_eq_bStat in HookSumClosedForm. Each row summand is a convex function of λ_i, so a tangent line of slope 2j + 1 at λ_i = j − i (or at λ_i = 1 when j ≤ i) bounds it from below. Summing:

    2 Σ_c hook(c)  ≥  ℓ(ℓ+1) + (n − ℓ)(2j + 1) − Σ_{i<ℓ} ((j − (i+1))₊)²          for every j ≥ 0,

    two_mul_sum_hookZ_ge_tangent. The correction sum is written with ℕ-truncated subtraction, so no hypothesis relating j to ℓ is needed; for j ≤ ℓ it equals Σ_{d<j} d² (sum_trunc_sq_eq_of_le), and for every ℓ it is at most Σ_{d<j} d² (sum_trunc_sq_le).

    The bound that settles REV_n #

    Writing n = T_k + r with T_k = k(k+1)/2, the min-hook shape is the staircase δ_k with its first r rows extended by one cell, whose hook sum is H⁺ = Σ_{i≤k} i² + r(k+1). Taking j = k and the closed form 6 Σ_{d<m} d² = m(m−1)(2m−1) turns the tangent bound into the polynomial identity

    2 ( Σ_c hook(c) − H⁺ )  ≥  (ℓ − k)² − r,                                 `two_mul_sum_hookZ_sub_hminClosed_ge`

    for every diagram with n cells and every row count ℓ — no k ≤ ℓ hypothesis. With R_λ ≤ (ℓ−1)·C (hooks strictly decrease along a row, HookSumThicknessBound), this is what makes the shape free energy REV_n of qou-hok7 grow like √n and not n^{3/4}: the energy of an ℓ-row shape grows quadratically in ℓ − √(2n) while its entropy grows only linearly in ℓ. The archimedean maximisation max_x (xC − x²L/2) = C²/(2L) is quad_max_le, the one statement here over ℝ (it needs the order).

    What is and is not proved here #

    • Proved: the row form, the tangent bound for every j, its j = k specialisation, and the quadratic maximum. All sorry-free.
    • Not proved here: that H⁺ is the minimum hook sum over all diagrams with n cells (the closed form H_min(n) of hmin_closed_form_and_rev_sublinearity_probe.py). The inequality above is stated against the defined quantity hminClosed k r; the probe rev_sqrt_n_upper_bound_probe.py verifies H⁺ = H_min(n) exactly for n ≤ 30, and the upper-bound argument only needs H⁺ ≥ H_min(n), which is attainment by the shape λ⁺.

    Base ring #

    ℤ throughout, matching HookSumClosedForm; ℝ only in quad_max_le.
