    QOU.BraidKnot.geom_universality — Geom(K) is the unique additive/functorial volume #

    Proposition (prop:geom-universality).

    The categorical volume form, evaluated on the knot complement S³ \ K through the fibre functor τ, yields Vol(K) for hyperbolic knots and CS(K) for torus knots. Geom(K) is the unique functional on knot complements satisfying:

    (i) Additivity: Geom(K₁ # K₂) = Geom(K₁) + Geom(K₂).

    (ii) Functoriality: Geom(K) = τ(tr_M(ρ(β_K))) for some fibre functor τ.

    (iii) Normalisation: Geom(○) = 0 (unknot) and Geom(4₁) = Vol(4₁)/ℏ_q² (fixes q_0).

    The Lean encoding records uniqueness as a ∀ G', (additive G' ∧ functorial G' ∧ normalised G') → G' = Geom claim.
