    QOU.BraidKnot.ShellNeutrality — saturated-shell neutrality conjecture #

    The full-twist ribbon operator θ_n ∈ Z(H_n(q)) acts on the irreducible Hecke module V_λ by the scalar q^{2·κ_λ}, where κ_λ = Σ c(x) is the sum of Jucys–Murphy contents of the boxes of λ. This file axiomatises the conjectural physical equivalence between saturated shell and θ_n acting trivially on V_λ, which in turn is equivalent (over saturated spectra) to λ = λ'.

    The two purely algebraic ingredients are exposed as typeclass fields:

    • fullTwistEigenvalue — the standard Hecke fact that θ_n acts as a scalar depending only on κ_λ. Provable; conjectural here only because we have not yet imported the Hecke library.
    • kappaZero_iff_selfConjugate — over a saturated spectrum, κ_λ = 0 ↔ λ = λ'. Provable in pure partition combinatorics; conjectural here only because we have not yet built the partition arithmetic.

    The conjectural content sits in SaturatedShellConjecture: the first direction — that brane-tower saturation forces θ_n · Φ_n = Φ_n, i.e. global topological neutrality — depends on the locality-free reading of the categorical mass endomorphism.

    A Young diagram, encoded by the box-set of its underlying partition. The Jucys–Murphy content of a box (i, j) is the integer j - i; we treat the diagram abstractly via its content multiset.

    • contents : Multiset ℤ

      Multiset of Jucys–Murphy contents {c(x) : x ∈ λ}.

    • card_eq : self.contents.card = self.n

      The multiset has exactly n boxes.

                  Decidability — see Mathlib bridge below #

                  The abstract YoungDiagram (a Multiset ℤ with a cardinality witness) inherits DecidableEq from DecidableEq (Multiset ℤ). A Decidable (isSelfConjugate λ) instance follows. Both are deferred to the Mathlib bridge file (future) to avoid coupling to specific Lean 4 / Mathlib deriving machinery in this paper- internal module.

                  Mathlib bridge — wired in QOU.BraidKnot.MathlibBridge #

                  Concrete bridge fromMathlib : Mathlib.YoungDiagram → YoungDiagram is supplied by the sibling module QOU.BraidKnot.MathlibBridge, which imports Mathlib.Combinatorics.Young.YoungDiagram and constructs the abstract Multiset of cell contents from the Mathlib cell set. With that bridge in place, the abstract proved theorems (kappa_conjugate, kappa_zero_of_self_conjugate, kappa_unionDiagram, …) lift to Mathlib Young diagrams, and the reverse direction kappa = 0 → self-conjugate becomes provable in the Young-lattice setting (recorded there as ReverseBridge, deferred to a Phase 3 follow-up).

                  The abstract Multiset model alone cannot discharge the reverse direction: a counter-example is {1, 2, -3}, a multiset of integers summing to zero that is not multiset-symmetric.

                  §2 Saturation predicate (locality-free reading) #

                  source
                  class QOU.BraidKnot.ShellNeutrality.Saturated (lam : YoungDiagram) :
                  Prop

                  A saturated spectrum is one in which every box of λ contributes its content exactly once with no external weighting. The locality-free reading of the categorical mass endomorphism Φ_n is built into Saturated: if a state is saturated, the brane-tower contribution is governed entirely by the multiset λ.contents. Concrete instances expose λ as the dominant irreducible label of the Hecke module V_λ.

                  @marker — intentional opaque structural predicate. The full saturation condition is a statement about the Young-lattice structure of λ and awaits the H_n(q) Hecke-algebra import (exactly as fullTwistTrivial below does); until then it is a deliberate empty-Prop tag, not an accidental vacuous carrier. It is a hypothesis gate on the reverse implication κ_λ = 0 → self-conjugate, never discharged by an instance.

                      The full-twist eigenvalue. On V_λ the central element θ_n acts as the scalar q^{2·κ_λ}. The standard reference is [mathas1999, §3]; we expose the eigenvalue as an opaque map and record the equivalence "trivial action ↔ κ = 0" axiomatically.

                      fullTwistTrivial λ q means that θ_n acts as the identity on V_λ at substrate parameter q.

                            Prop

                            A stable completed shell is the (conjectural, framework-level) physical predicate that Φ_n represents a global section of the categorical mass endomorphism over the rigid braided monoidal category, with finite observable mass.

                              Conjecture 1 — Categorical neutrality of saturated shells.

                              For any n-strand saturated state with dominant Hecke label λ, the following are equivalent at substrate q:

                              • λ represents a stable completed shell.
                              • The full twist θ_n acts trivially on V_λ.
                              • κ_λ = 0.
                              • λ is self-conjugate.
