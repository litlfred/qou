    Base change for M^μ — the Specht module is defined over ℤ #

    Everything in M^μ that the Specht construction produces has integer coordinates: a polytabloid is a signed sum of basis tabloids, so its coefficient at each tabloid is a sum of ±1. This file makes that precise as a functorial statement — a ring hom φ : R →+* S acts on M^μ coefficient-wise and carries polytabloid to polytabloid — and derives the one consequence the nondegeneracy argument needs: a linear relation proved over one ring survives into another.

    Why it is wanted #

    specht_form_nondegenerate has to reach every characteristic-zero field, and the only place the Specht form is easy to control is ℚ, where it is positive definite (specht_form_nondegenerate_ordered). The bridge is integrality:

    • the coordinate matrix of the polytabloids is an integer matrix (coordMatrix_intCast below);
    • a spanning relation established over ℚ transfers along ℚ →+* k (mem_span_map below), so the same finite sub-family spans over k.

    Neither statement mentions the bilinear form, so both live here rather than in SpechtIrreducible, which is where they are consumed.

    What is not here #

    No claim that the polytabloids are independent, and no use of the standard basis theorem. The sub-family that ends up spanning is extracted over ℚ by generic linear algebra at the point of use; this file only says that whatever ℚ-relation you find keeps holding after base change.

        4. Relations survive base change #

        The one consequence the nondegeneracy proof consumes: if x lies in the span of a finite family over R, then its base change lies in the span of the base changed family over S. Applied with R = ℚ, S = k and the family a sub-family of the polytabloids, this is what says a spanning set found over ℚ still spans over k.
