    QOU.BraidKnot.HabiroNewtonLadder #

    Habiro–Newton ladder of the figure-eight colored Jones — Lean proof and generalization of the cp4i probe identity.

    Formalizes, sorry-free, the structural identity verified exactly by computations/aj_operator_aw_shape_probe.py (bean cp4i; audit: docs/audits/2026-07-03-aj-conjecture-dialog-assessment.md §"Probe result"):

    J_N(4₁; q) = ∑_{k=0}^{N-1} ∏_{j=1}^{k} (Λ_N − λ_j), Λ_N = q^N + q^{−N}, λ_j = q^j + q^{−j},

    i.e. the Habiro–Lê double-Pochhammer form of the figure-eight colored Jones is a Newton-interpolation ladder on the bar-even variable Λ with all cyclotomic coefficients equal to 1 (the 4₁ signature of Habiro's cyclotomic expansion — Ref: [habiro2004cyclotomic]; strong AJ proved for 4₁ — Ref: [nguyen-tran-2021]). The identity is TERMWISE (habiroTerm_eq_newtonLadder), which is strictly stronger than the summed statement the probe checked per-N.

    What this file proves (all sorry-free) #

    • newtonLadder_succ — the ladder is two-term / first-order (g_{k+1} = (x − a_{k+1}) · g_k, lower coefficient B_k ≡ 0): the degenerate boundary of the Askey–Wilson three-term shape.
    • casoratian_eq_zero — generalized: over any commutative ring, any two solutions of a first-order ladder have identically vanishing Casoratian (discrete Wronskian) — the reason the proved knot side supplies no Casoratian sign for the 1jrh test (docs/audits/2026-07-02-1jrh-aw-one-recurrence-formalize-first-prune.md).
    • lam_bar_even / multiplier_bar_even — the ladder nodes and multiplier are bar-even (q ↦ q⁻¹ invariant), consistent with the bar-even multiplier the 1jrh one-recurrence hypothesis requires.
    • newtonLadder_eq_zero / newtonLadder_sum_stable — generalized termination: the sum truncates at any node hit (x = a m), the 4₁ mechanism Λ_N = λ_N.
    • habiroTerm_eq_newtonLadder — the termwise Habiro↔Newton identity.
    • coloredJones41_eq_newton_sum / coloredJones41_sum_stable / coloredJones41_bar_symmetric — the probe's checks (0), termination, and bar symmetry, now structural theorems for ALL N (the probe verified N ≤ 9).
    • coloredJones41_two — anchor: J_2 is the Jones polynomial of 4₁.

    Base ring (§7c) #

    The Newton-ladder section is generic {R : Type*} [CommRing R] (no inverses). Nodes λ_j, the Habiro term, and the colored Jones need q⁻¹, hence {K : Type*} [Field K] — still fully algebraic, no ℝ, no ordering, no archimedean construct. Bar involution is the literal map q ↦ q⁻¹.

    What this file does NOT prove #

    That coloredJones41 agrees with the quantum-group / skein-theoretic colored Jones of 4₁ — the Habiro–Lê formula is taken as the definition here (it is the form the corpus's compute uses and the literature proves; Ref: [habiro2002], [nguyen-tran-2021]). No claim about the AJ conjecture itself is made or needed.
