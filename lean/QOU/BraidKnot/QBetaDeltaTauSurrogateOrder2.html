    QOU.BraidKnot.QBetaDeltaTauSurrogateOrder2 #

    Order-2 (second-derivative) Taylor coefficient of delta_tau_M_surrogate at q = 1, via the quotient-rule chain extended one level past the order-1 result.

    Status (corrected 2026-10-10, bean qou-eddc). This header used to describe the order-2 proof as living here, over the grouped-frame closed form N = -q¹¹+q¹⁰-2q⁹+q⁸+q⁶-q⁵-q³+2q²-q+1, D = 4q⁵(q²+q+1). Both are out of date:

    • delta_tau_M_surrogate is now the canonical (borromean) closed form, with numerator of degree 17 and denominator 4q⁸(q²+q+1).
    • The derivative proof delta_tau_M_surrogate_deriv_hasDerivAt_one : HasDerivAt delta_tau_M_surrogate_deriv (5/3) 1 and the coefficient theorem delta_tau_M_surrogate_taylor_coeff_2 live in QBetaPerBlockChiClosedForms.lean.

    What this file proves is the two numerical identities below: g'(1) = -5/6 evaluated from delta_tau_M_surrogate_deriv, and (5/3)/2! = 5/6. The order-2 coefficient c₂ = 5/6 is the same in both frames; the frames first differ at order 3.

    Corollary (order-2 Taylor coefficient = 5/6). Since g''(1) = 5/3, the second Taylor coefficient at q = 1 is g''(1) / 2! = 5/6, matching the witnessed catalogue value delta_tau_M_surrogate_taylor_witness 2.
