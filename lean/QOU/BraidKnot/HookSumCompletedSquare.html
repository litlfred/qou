    The hook sum is a positive-definite quadratic in the β-numbers #

    HookSumBetaCoordinates puts the hook sum in β-coordinates. Completing the square there turns it into a sum of squares, which is what makes the REV_n energy term rigid:

    8 · Σ_{c∈μ} h(c)  =  Σ_{i<L} (2β_i − a_i)²  −  Σ_{i<L} a_i²,      a_i := 2L − 3 − 4i

    The a_i depend only on L, so the whole μ-dependence sits in one sum of squares. Two consequences follow immediately and are the point of this file:
    eight_mul_sum_hookZ_completedSquare the identity above
    hookDev_sub_succ d_i − d_{i+1} = 2(β_i − β_{i+1}) − 4 for d_i := 2β_i − a_i
    eight_mul_card_mul_sum_hookZ_ge Cauchy–Schwarz: (Σ d_i)² − L·Σ a_i² ≤ 8L·Σ_c h(c)

    hookDev_sub_succ is the rigidity, exactly. The deviations d_i are constant precisely when every β-gap is 2 — the staircase — and Cauchy–Schwarz says Σ d_i² is minimised, at fixed Σ d_i, exactly when the d_i are all equal. Since Σ d_i is determined by (n, L) alone (identity (III)), the hook sum's floor at fixed (n, L) is attained at gap 2 and the cost of deviating is quadratic in the gap's departure from 2. No asymptotics enter.

    Why this matters for qou-hok7 #

    The Θ(√n) coefficient's open item is a lower bound on the pair term P = Σ_{i<j} ε(β_i − β_j). P is small exactly when the β-numbers are widely separated, and this file is the exact statement of what that costs: separation is deviation of d from constant, and the energy E = (H − H_min)·ln q₀ pays for it as a sum of squares. A numerical version of this was measured in PR #7286 (computations/deltalambda/rev_energy_gap_rigidity_probe.py), where it appeared only as the asymptotic E ≈ ln q₀ (2n)^{3/2}(ḡ−2)²/24; the identity below is that statement without the asymptotics, and it is where a proof should start rather than the fit.

    The Cauchy–Schwarz floor is tight, not merely valid: measured against H_min(n) at every triangular n tested with L = k, the gap is exactly 0.

    Base ring (§7c) #

    All sums over ℤ, matching HookSumBetaCoordinates and PieceAGeneral. No ℝ, no archimedean input — the ln q₀ that turns H into an energy lives strictly downstream.
