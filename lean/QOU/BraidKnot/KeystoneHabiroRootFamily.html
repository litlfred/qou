      bwPoly = valPoly

      The root family triggers rigidity. If the gauge-row polynomial bwPoly and the value-map polynomial valPoly agree at every level-N fold value and both have degree < N − 1, then they are equal — hence agree at the substrate t₀. The antecedent is the concrete root-of-unity agreement (the WRT invariant equals the modular datum at each fibre root); by KeystoneGaugeRowRigidity this pins the continuation.
