    The qZeilberger certificate — mathematical content Lean-verified #

    The general-N annihilation proof is a creative-telescoping argument. Its certificate was computed and verified exactly corpus-side (computations/aj_figure_eight_certificate.py, witness aj-figure-eight-certificate.witness.json): Cert(q,x,y) = −y(qx+1)(qx²−1)(q³x²−1)(1−x)(1−qx)/(q·x⁷·(y−x)(y−qx)), with the Habiro–Lê shift ratios (single-fraction forms) s = (qxy−1)(x−qy)/(qxy) and r₁ = (qxy−1)(x−1)/((qx−1)(x−y)). The two theorems below carry the certificate's mathematical content in denominator-cleared polynomial form (pure ring identities, no hypotheses): with L = a₂·r₁(x)·r₁(qx) + a₁·r₁(x) + a₀ = nL/dL and R = Cert(x,qy)·s − Cert(x,y) = nR/dR (the together-combined numerators/denominators), the identity L = R away from denominator zeros is exactly nL·dR = nR·dL. (The boundary identity rhs = −Cert(x,1) lives in AJFigureEight.feCert_boundary — its cleared form is a syntactic tautology, so only the fraction form carries content.) What remains of the general-N sorry after these is bookkeeping: the shift-ratio lemmas for the Habiro products, the division-free reformulation across the termination zeros, and the Finset.sum telescope.
