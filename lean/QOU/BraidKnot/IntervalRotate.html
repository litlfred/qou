    Rotating an interval of Fin n #

    intervalRotate a m k rotates the block [a, a+m) of Fin n by k and fixes everything outside it.

    Why this exists #

    The Garnir tableau g_{ij} of Mathas §3 is defined procedurally — "place a, a+1, …, b in increasing order into the remaining cells, first along row i+1, then along row i" — and formalising a procedure means discharging a surjectivity obligation for a bespoke map.

    It is not a procedure. Measured in computations/hecke/garnir_permutation_is_rotation_probe.py (108 Garnir tableaux, all partitions of n = 3..7, zero mismatches), the induced permutation of labels is exactly a cyclic rotation of the interval [a,b] by j+1. A rotation is invertible by construction, so building g_{ij} on this gives bijectivity for free.

    Nothing here mentions tableaux; it is ordinary arithmetic on Fin n.
