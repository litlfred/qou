    QOU.BraidKnot.eigenvalue_product_expansion — Λ_sym(Z, N) = q^{(Z−N)(A−1)/2} · ([2]_q/2)^{ZN} #

    Proposition (prop:eigenvalue-product-expansion).

    For a nucleus with Z protons and N neutrons (A = Z + N), the symmetric-channel eigenvalue product of the nuclear braid in the [2,1] representation of H_3(R, q) is

      Λ_sym = q^((Z−N)(A−1)/2) · ([2]_q/2)^{Z·N}.

    The Lean encoding records the closed form as a real-valued function of (Z, N, q).
