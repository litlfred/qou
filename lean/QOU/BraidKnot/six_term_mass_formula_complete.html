    QOU.BraidKnot.six_term_mass_formula_complete — six-term unified mass formula #

    Proposition (prop:six-term-mass-formula-complete).

    The .md states that the categorical mass of a vortex ϱ is the sum of six named, concrete terms:

      𝔪(ϱ) = |tr_M(T_w)|                                            (Term 1)
       + Σ_{i=0}^{n-2} Φ_q(|V̂_i^full|)                          (Term 2)
       + z² Σ_{i<j} Φ_q(|V̂_i^full V̂_j^full|)                    (Term 3)
       + m_e · τ(C_{dim_q})                                     (Term 4)
       + a_K · 𝒞_K                                              (Term 5)
       + Σ_{ℓ≥2} Res_{q=ζ_ℓ}[·] / (q₀ − ζ_ℓ)                    (Term 6)

    and adds that "every term evaluates uniquely from the canonical braid word and substrate parameter."

    2026-09-02 — this file encodes the SHAPE, not the formula #

    SixTermMassFormulaContext carried term1 … term6 and cat_mass as seven opaque elements of R plus a field claim_six_term_sum asserting the sum identity, and six_term_mass_formula_complete returned that field. Removing the projection is not the interesting part; the interesting part is what is left when you do.

    None of the six terms is encoded. Nothing in this file mentions the Markov trace, Φ_q, the V̂_i^full, z, m_e, τ(C_{dim_q}), a_K, 𝒞_K, or a cyclotomic residue. With the terms opaque, cat_mass = term1 + ⋯ + term6 is not the six-term mass formula — it is the statement that some element is a sum of six elements, which is the definition of a six-way additive split and holds of every quantity that can be written as one.

    So this is not a case where naming the claim over parameters (AGENTS.md §1a disposition 3) would recover the content, as it did for QOU.Archimedean.knot_operator_selection. There, the parameters were the objects the claim is about. Here, naming sixTermSplit m t₁ … t₆ names a schema, not the proposition.

    Both facts about that schema are proved below rather than asserted:

    • sixTermSplit_sum — for any six terms a mass satisfying it exists (take the sum). So the schema constrains nothing whatever about the terms.
    • not_sixTermSplit_of_independent — it is refutable when cat_mass is chosen independently (1 ≠ 0 + ⋯ + 0). So it constrains cat_mass, and only cat_mass.

    Together: the schema pins one element to five additions and says nothing about the mathematics the .md describes. That is also why a sorry here would be wrong — it would read as "true, not yet proved" over a statement that is refutable at free data.

    What is unencoded, term by term #

    term .md content what encoding it needs
    1 \|tr_M(T_w)\| the Markov trace on the canonical Hecke normal form
    2 Σ_i Φ_q(\|V̂_i^full\|) Φ_q (Faddeev) and the full vertex operators
    3 z² Σ_{i<j} Φ_q(\|V̂_i V̂_j\|) the same, plus the Markov parameter z
    4 m_e · τ(C_{dim_q}) the fibre functor τ on the dim_q Casimir
    5 a_K · 𝒞_K the chirality coefficient and knot invariant
    6 Σ_{ℓ≥2} Res_{q=ζ_ℓ}[·]/(q₀−ζ_ℓ) the cyclotomic pole expansion

    The .md's further claims — that every term evaluates uniquely from the braid word, that Markov-trace invariance is enforced, and that the breakdown locates the origin of isospin and parity violation — have no encoding here either.

    Prop

    A six-way additive split: m = t₁ + t₂ + t₃ + t₄ + t₅ + t₆.

    This is the shape of the manuscript's six-term formula with all six terms left opaque. It is named here so the distance between the shape and the claim is visible in the Lean rather than only in prose. See sixTermSplit_sum and not_sixTermSplit_of_independent for exactly how much it constrains.
