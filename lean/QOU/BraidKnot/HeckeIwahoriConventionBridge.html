    Iwahori normalisation → the corpus's σ convention #

    The corpus states the Hecke relation as σ² = (q − q⁻¹)σ + 1 (AGENTS.md §7a), so σ has eigenvalues q and −q⁻¹. The Iwahori–Hecke literature — and QOU/IwahoriHecke/ — states it as (T − Q)(T + 1) = 0, so T has eigenvalues Q and −1. Bridging the two is the one obstacle between the Jucys–Murphy arc (HeckeGeneratorsBelow and everything over it) and the constructed algebra.

    The rescaling is exact, and needs no square root #

    Write σ = c · T. From T² = (Q − 1)T + Q,

    σ² = c²T² = c(Q − 1)·(cT) + c²Q = c(Q − 1)·σ + c²Q,

    so matching σ² = (q − q⁻¹)σ + 1 requires both

    c²Q = 1        and        c(Q − 1) = q − q⁻¹.

    Taking Q = q² and c = q⁻¹ satisfies both identically: q⁻² · q² = 1, and q⁻¹(q² − 1) = q − q⁻¹.

    So the parameter, not the generator, is what moves. One does not adjoin √q to the base ring and rescale T; one instantiates the Iwahori algebra at Q = q² — the corpus's q is already the square root of the Iwahori parameter — and rescales by q⁻¹, which is in the field whenever q ≠ 0.

    This matters for §7c: the "adjoin a square root" reading forces a base-ring extension (and, if done carelessly, ℝ). Nothing here leaves a generic [Field R].

    Scale-invariance of the other two relations #

    The braid and far-commutation relations are homogeneous of the same degree on both sides, so a scalar rescaling cannot disturb them:

    • braid — degree 3 each side, c³ cancels (braid_of_scaled);
    • far — degree 2 each side, c² cancels (far_of_scaled).

    Together with hecke_of_iwahori this gives heckeGeneratorsBelow_of_iwahori: any family satisfying the Iwahori relations at Q = q² is a HeckeGeneratorsBelow at q. That is the whole convention half of the bridge (bean qou-0o6j); what it still needs from QOU/IwahoriHecke/ is a family whose braid relations actually hold, which today they do not — heckeRel imposes the quadratic relation only (bean qou-ayyi).

    The bridge, assembled #

    Given a family T : ℕ → A satisfying the Iwahori relations at parameter Q = q² — quadratic, braid and far — the rescaled family σ i := q⁻¹ • T i satisfies the corpus relations at q, i.e. it is a HeckeGeneratorsBelow R A q n.

    This is the convention half of bean qou-0o6j. It is stated against a hypothesised T-family rather than against QOU.IwahoriHecke because that construction does not yet impose the braid relations (bean qou-ayyi / qou-phir); the moment it does, this is the instance.

      The bridge runs both ways, and here is a concrete instance #

      Everything above is stated against a hypothesised Iwahori family. A bridge nobody can feed is worth little, so this section closes the loop:

      • iwahori_of_hecke — the inverse rescaling. A corpus-convention σ gives an Iwahori-convention T = q·σ satisfying (T − q²)(T + 1) = 0.
      • stdTwoIw — the explicit 2×2 Young seminormal matrices of HeckeStableScalarCascade, pushed into Iwahori normalisation.
      • stdTwoIw_isHeckeGeneratorsBelow — they satisfy the three Iwahori relations at n = 3, so the bridge applies to them.
      • stdTwoIw_roundTrip — and what comes back out is stdTwo again, on the nose.

      The round trip is the falsifiability check: a bridge with the scaling inverted, or with Q = q instead of q², would fail it.
