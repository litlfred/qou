    The per-cell hook lengths agree #

    source
    theorem QOU.BraidKnot.hookLength_eq_term {n : ℕ} (Λ : DeltaLambdaSignRule.Partition n) (i j : ℕ) (hj : j < Λ.parts.getD i 0) :
    Λ.hookLength i j = Λ.parts.getD i 0 - j + (IdentityI.colLen Λ.parts j - i) - 1

    For a genuine cell (i,j) the custom hook length equals the corpus list term (rowLen − j) + (colLen j − i) − 1. Both rowLen − j and colLen j − i are ≥ 1 on a cell, so the ℕ subtractions are exact.
