    QOU.BraidKnot.StructuralRank — Hecke panel structural rank decomposition (conditional on Mackey-filtration nesting) #

    Backs the audit docs/audits/2026-05-09-A-operator-rank-structure.md.

    Encodes — at the level of ℕ-valued ranks — the structural rank identity for the operator-valued least-squares supply matrix M_λ arising from the Hecke branching H_{n_1+n_2}(q) ⊃ H_{n_1}(q) ⊗ H_{n_2}(q):

    rank(M_λ) = Σ_{μ ∈ supp_λ} max_{ν : c^λ_{μν} > 0} rank(X_μ) · rank(Y_ν)

    where X_μ, Y_ν are the test-panel image matrices for the Hoefsmit reps V_μ, V_ν, and c^λ_{μν} are LR coefficients.

    Two structural ingredients enter as class hypotheses (per §3b-cond conditional-class pattern):

    1. Within-μ nesting (max over ν). For fixed μ, the column spaces of the (μ, ν)-blocks vary with ν as a nested chain, not a direct sum. Joint column-span rank = max_ν rank(block μ ν). Closest published precedent: the Mackey filtration on induced modules for cyclotomic Hecke algebras (Mathas 2004) and its graded refinement (Hu–Mathas 2012). Neither states the column-span / fitting-matrix rank consequence explicitly, but both establish the underlying nested-filtration structure.

    2. Across-μ independence (sum over μ). Different μ-blocks contribute orthogonal column spaces, so total rank = Σ_μ of per-μ joint ranks. This is Wedderburn block-diagonality for H_n(q) semisimple — folklore, freely used in Geck–Pfeiffer (2000), §8–9.

    Given both hypotheses, the rank formula is an unconditional ℕ arithmetic consequence (proved here without sorry). Numerical sanity at λ = [7, 2] matches the empirical extract (A_operator_rank_probe.witness.json v1.1, A=3): 22 + 64 = 86.

    class QOU.BraidKnot.PanelRankDecomposition (μIdx : Type) [Fintype μIdx] (νIdx : μIdx → Type) [(μ : μIdx) → Fintype (νIdx μ)] (blockRank : (μ : μIdx) → νIdx μ → ℕ) (jointRankMu : μIdx → ℕ) (totalRank : ℕ) :
    Prop

    Structural rank decomposition class for a Hecke panel matrix indexed by (μ, ν)-blocks.

    Class hypotheses encode the two structural facts from the audit: (a) within-μ nesting (joint = max), and (b) across-μ independence (total = sum of joints).

    Conjectural status: §3b-cond. The within-μ nesting field is the Mackey-filtration / Specht-filtration nesting (Mathas 2004; Hu–Mathas 2012), specialized to test-panel column spaces. Not explicitly stated in the published literature for the fitting-matrix / column-rank shape used here, but mechanically a corollary of those filtration results once the column spaces are identified with Specht-filtration layers.

    • joint_eq_max (μ : μIdx) : jointRankMu μ = Finset.univ.sup (blockRank μ)

      Within-μ nesting (Mackey/Specht filtration; cf. Mathas 2004, Hu–Mathas 2012). The joint column-span rank across all ν-blocks for a fixed μ equals the maximum of the individual ν-block ranks — column spaces vary as a nested chain in ν, not as a direct sum.

    • total_eq_sum : totalRank = ∑ μ : μIdx, jointRankMu μ

      Across-μ independence (Wedderburn block-diagonality for H_n(q) semisimple; standard). Total rank decomposes additively across μ-blocks.

          Submodule-level reformulation #

          Lifts the ℕ-arithmetic PanelRankDecomposition to actual sub-spaces: the panel-image of each (μ, ν)-block is a Submodule K V for a finite-dimensional ambient V, the within-μ nesting becomes a chain (totally ordered by ≤) of sub-spaces, and across-μ independence becomes iSupIndep of the per-μ joint sub-spaces.

          Both parts fully proved (no sorry):

          Lemma Statement
          iSup_eq_νStar ⨆ ν, W μ ν = W μ (νStar μ)
          finrank_iSup_eq_νStar finrank of joint span = finrank of νStar block
          finrank_blockRank_le each block's finrank ≤ νStar block's finrank
          finrank_iSup_eq_finset_sup finrank of joint span = max-over-ν of block finranks
          finrank_iSup_eq_sum_of_iSupIndep (general) iSupIndep p → finrank (⨆ i, p i) = ∑ i, finrank (p i)
          toRankDecomposition PanelImageStructure → PanelRankDecomposition (instance)

          The general lemma finrank_iSup_eq_sum_of_iSupIndep factors through DirectSum.coeLinearMap: its range is ⨆ i, p i (DirectSum.range_coeLinearMap), and iSupIndep makes it injective (iSupIndep.dfinsupp_lsum_injective), so we get (⨁ i, p i) ≃ₗ[K] (⨆ i, p i) and then close with Module.finrank_directSum.

          class QOU.BraidKnot.PanelImageStructure {K : Type u} [Field K] {V : Type v} [AddCommGroup V] [Module K V] [FiniteDimensional K V] (μIdx : Type) [Fintype μIdx] [DecidableEq μIdx] (νIdx : μIdx → Type) [(μ : μIdx) → Fintype (νIdx μ)] (W : (μ : μIdx) → νIdx μ → Submodule K V) :
          Type

          Panel-image Submodule structure.

          A finite-dimensional ambient space V over a field K, together with a (μ, ν)-indexed family of sub-spaces W : (μ : μIdx) → νIdx μ → Submodule K V carrying the two structural hypotheses of the audit:

          1. For each μ, there is a maximizing νStar μ such that W μ ν ≤ W μ (νStar μ) for all ν (within-μ chain).
          2. The per-μ joint sub-spaces W μ (νStar μ) are complete-lattice-independent (across-μ orthogonality).
          • νStar (μ : μIdx) : νIdx μ

            A maximizing ν for each μ (witness of the chain's top element).

          • νStar_max (μ : μIdx) (ν : νIdx μ) : W μ ν ≤ W μ (νStar W μ)

            Within-μ nesting: νStar μ dominates every ν in the inclusion order.

          • independent : iSupIndep fun (μ : μIdx) => W μ (νStar W μ)

            Across-μ independence of the per-μ joint sub-spaces.

            Finrank of an independent supremum decomposes as a sum.
