    Proposition (prop:three-parameters-wedderburn).

    The number of calibration inputs equals the number of irreducible representations of H₃(R, q), which is the number of partitions of 3.

      H₃(R, q) ≅ M₁ ⊕ M₂ ⊕ M₁    (for λ = (3), (2,1), (1,1,1))

    The Lean encoding records the counting identity #(calibration inputs) = 3 = #(Partition 3).
