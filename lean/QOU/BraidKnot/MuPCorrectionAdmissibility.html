    theorem QOU.BraidKnot.MuPCorrectionAdmissibility.bar_even_monomial_iff (q : ℝ) (hq : 1 < q) (k m : ℕ) :
    MuPCarrier.hbarGen q⁻¹ ^ k * q⁻¹ ^ m = MuPCarrier.hbarGen q ^ k * q ^ m ↔ k = 2 * m

    Selection rule, both halves. Over ℝ with 1 < q, the monomial ℏ_q^k · q^m is bar-even — ℏ_{q⁻¹}^k · (q⁻¹)^m = ℏ_q^k · q^m — iff k = 2m.

    Forward direction: hbarGen_bar turns the left side into (−q)^k · ℏ_q^k · (q⁻¹)^m; cancelling ℏ_q^k ≠ 0 and clearing (q⁻¹)^m leaves (−q)^k = q^{2m}; taking absolute values gives q^k = q^{2m}, and n ↦ q^n is injective for q > 1. Converse: hbarGen_monomial_bar_even.

    3. The w-tower admits no order with a small-height coefficient #

    source
    theorem QOU.BraidKnot.MuPCorrectionAdmissibility.no_small_height_w_monomial (q c t : ℝ) (j : ℕ) (hq0 : 11099 / 10000 ≤ q) (hq1 : q ≤ 111 / 100) (hc0 : 1 / 5 ≤ |c|) (hc1 : |c| ≤ 5) (htlo : 18 / 10 ^ 8 ≤ t) (hthi : t ≤ 24 / 10 ^ 8) :
    |c| * MuPCarrier.wGen q ^ j ≠ t

    The w-tower carries no small-height residual (Lean form of the probe's measured t/w³ = 0.1621 < 1/5, t/w⁴ = 14.88 > 5 at the CODATA-2018 ratio; 0.1632 and 14.98 at CODATA-2022 — see the module docstring). For every order j, a monomial |c|·w^j with |c| ∈ [1/5, 5] and q in the pin window misses the residual window t ∈ [1.8e-7, 2.4e-7].

    Orders j ≤ 3 give at least (1/5)·(108/10000)³ ≈ 2.52e-7, above the top of the window; orders j ≥ 4 give at most 5·(11/1000)⁴ ≈ 7.32e-8, below the bottom. Both are contradictions; the proof mirrors order_window.

    No bar-even small-height monomial carries the μ–p residual. If ℏ_q^k · q^m is bar-even (hbar), q lies in the pin window, |c| ∈ [1/5, 5] and t in the residual window, then |c|·ℏ_q^k·q^m ≠ t.

    Bar-evenness forces k = 2m (bar_even_monomial_iff), the positive half of the selection rule rewrites the monomial as w^m (hbarGen_pow_two_mul_eq_wGen_pow), and no_small_height_w_monomial excludes every m.

    5. The admissibility class, and the monomial family's absence from it #

    source
    structure QOU.BraidKnot.MuPCorrectionAdmissibility.GapA4CorrectionAdmissible (F : ℝ → ℝ) (q₀ : ℝ) :
    Prop

    Admissible GAP-A-4 correction factor. A function F : ℝ → ℝ of the substrate modulus is an admissible carrier of the μ–p residual at the pin q₀ when

    • pin_window — the pin lies in the window [1.1099, 1.11] (which contains the proton pin 1.10997858275138);
    • bar_even_at_pin — F is fixed by the bar involution at the pin, F q₀⁻¹ = F q₀. The corpus's precondition is bar-evenness as a function of q; that implies this field, so every function bar-even everywhere is in the class, and the exclusion below therefore covers the larger class. (This is also exactly the instance the proof uses.)
    • carries_residual — evaluated at the pin, F lands in the residual window [1.8e-7, 2.4e-7] around the measured t ≈ 2.1e-7.

    Every field is content-bearing. The structure is a filter, not a certificate. It is trivially inhabited — the constant function fun _ => 2/10⁷ at q₀ = 1.1099 satisfies all three fields — so membership encodes no derivation whatever, and F ∈ GapA4CorrectionAdmissible must never be cited as evidence that a correction exists. Its only content is which functions are excluded from it: monomial_not_admissible below.

    • pin_window : 11099 / 10000 ≤ q₀ ∧ q₀ ≤ 111 / 100
    • bar_even_at_pin : F q₀⁻¹ = F q₀
    • carries_residual : 18 / 10 ^ 8 ≤ F q₀ ∧ F q₀ ≤ 24 / 10 ^ 8
