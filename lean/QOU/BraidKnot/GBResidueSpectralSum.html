    QOU.BraidKnot.GBResidueSpectralSum — Gröbner–Shirshov residue spectral sum #

    The categorical mass of a closed braid is the sum of its descended Markov trace (E⁰ term) and a finite sum of residues at root-of-unity poles of the quantum dilogarithm, one per Gröbner–Shirshov filtration stratum that the braid's Hecke normal form occupies.

    Conditional-class axiomatisation (AGENTS.md §3b). The gradedMarkovTrace and residueAtRoot opaques are now class fields of GBResidueSpectralSum; concrete instances supply the graded trace and cyclotomic residue values.

    Formal-chain version. Lives under lean/QOU/ and is compiled by lake build. The co-located documentation stub at braids-and-knots/gb-residue-spectral-sum.lean mirrors this.

    Base ring (AGENTS.md §7c) #

    Pre-archimedean. The decomposition 𝔪 = |tr_M| + Σ_ℓ Res_ℓ, the classical limit, and the Fin levelBound cardinality statement are identities in any linearly ordered commutative ring R ([CommRing R] [LinearOrder R]); only the order is used, and that only to give |·| a meaning. No completeness, no Real.*, no archimedean axiom appears. The archimedean content — the actual MeV mass, the descended Markov-trace value, and the cyclotomic residues of Φ_q at ζ_ℓ — is carried entirely by an instance, built at R := ℝ from the crystal-basis witness (see fromCrystalData below). This is the §7c prescription: the generic construction stays here, the ℝ realisation consumes it via (R := ℝ).

      Type u_2

      GB residue spectral sum.

      The mass of a closed braid K with Hecke normal form T_w is

      𝔪(K) = |tr_M(T_w)| + Σ_{ℓ=0}^{levelBound-1} Res_{q→ζ_ℓ}(Φ_q · tr_{M,ℓ})(T_w)

      The class field structure replaces the previous opaque gradedMarkovTrace and opaque residueAtRoot declarations: each instance must supply the graded trace and cyclotomic residue values explicitly.

      • descended : R

        The descended (E⁰) Markov trace of the braid word.

      • levelBound : ℕ

        The confinement-level cutoff.

      • q : R

        The substrate parameter.

      • roots : Fin (levelBound R) → RootOfUnity R

        The roots of unity indexed by filtration level.

      • gradedTrace : Fin (levelBound R) → R

        The graded Markov trace at level ℓ, supplied by the instance. Per prop:graded-markov-trace-from-gs-filtration.

      • residue : Fin (levelBound R) → R

        The cyclotomic residue at level ℓ, supplied by the instance. Per prop:residue-at-root.

      • mass : R

        The spectral sum: 𝔪 = |descended| + Σ_ℓ residue ℓ.

      • spectral_decomposition : mass = |descended| + ∑ ℓ : Fin (levelBound R), residue ℓ

        Consistency: the mass is the descended term plus the sum of residues over filtration levels.

            theorem QOU.BraidKnot.spectral_sum_finite {R : Type u_1} [CommRing R] [LinearOrder R] (S : GBResidueSpectralSum R) :
            Finset.univ.card = GBResidueSpectralSum.levelBound R

            Finiteness (cardinality form).

            The spectral sum ∑ ℓ : Fin S.levelBound, S.residue ℓ is a sum over a Finset of cardinality S.levelBound, hence finite by Mathlib's standard finite-sum machinery.

            Note: this is the algebraic finiteness — the index set Fin S.levelBound has cardinality S.levelBound by construction, so the sum has at most S.levelBound non-zero terms. The substantive claim of conj:gs-spectral-sum-finite — that S.levelBound truly equals the confinement level ℓ(K, q_0) of the underlying braid, so that any analytic extension of S.residue to ℕ vanishes above the cutoff — is the conjectural part and is not formalised here; see the .md narrative under "Why a conjecture and not a theorem".

            Constructing instances from crystal-basis data #

            A concrete GBResidueSpectralSum is determined by the data (descended, levelBound, q, roots, gradedTrace, residue); the mass and spectral_decomposition fields are then forced. The constructor below packages this fact and is the post-class analogue of "discharging" the previous opaque declarations: any caller that supplies the data gets a concrete instance with no residual abstraction.

            The intended source of the data is the crystal-basis derivation witness term7-alpha5-crystal-derivation.witness.json (per prop:term7-shell-correction), which supplies per-atom and per-level structural values from Schur–Weyl + Wedderburn + categorical R-matrix action evaluated at the crystal limit q → 0. The corresponding compute-side derivation chain is term7-s-crystal-4he.derivation.json.

            Construct a GBResidueSpectralSum from explicit per-level data. The mass field is forced to the canonical decomposition |descended| + Σ_ℓ residue ℓ, and spectral_decomposition holds by rfl.

            This is the §7c realisation point: instantiating at R := ℝ with the crystal-basis witness values is what puts the (otherwise generic) decomposition on the archimedean side.
