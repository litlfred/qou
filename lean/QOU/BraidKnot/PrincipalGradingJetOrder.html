    def QOU.BraidKnot.PrincipalGradingJetOrder.principalGrading (l : List ℕ) :
    ℕ

    The principal (ρ) grading n(λ) = Σ_i (i)·λ_{i} (0-indexed rows) = Σ_box row(box) — the co-content / principal-specialisation exponent (s_λ(1,q,q²,…)=q^{n(λ)}·∏…). Equivalently (classical) the fake-degree valuation min_{T∈SYT(λ)} maj(T); see the module docstring and -- Ref: [macdonald1995].

        deltaSign l = (-1) ^ principalGrading l

        Bar-parity of the sign carrier. deltaSign lands in {+1, −1}; combined with zeta4_eval_principalGrading this identifies it as the ζ₄ evaluation of the jet-order (principal) grading — the bar-odd / ζ₄ fibre datum of the Δ_λ closure.

        Assembly: Δ_λ = tr(g_ρ) = (−1)^{n(λ)}·dim(λ) #

        Combining the magnitude dim(λ) — the ordinary dimension of V_λ (#SYT(λ)), established as the whole magnitude of Δ_λ in the magnitude reduction (PR #3537) — with the sign carrier (−1)^{n(λ)} derived here gives the assembled per-partition value Δ_λ = tr(g_ρ). The dimension enters as the abstract input d : ℕ; its value dim(λ) is the classical #3537 result and is not re-derived here (taking it as a Lean hypothesis would be a self-assuming projection). The lemmas below verify only that the assembly decomposes correctly: its magnitude is exactly d and its sign is deltaSign. Honest scope: this formalises the assembly identity, not the physical binding closure B(K) = m_e·Σ_λ y_λ χ^λ Δ_λ (still open — see the audit doc).

            Bar-parity selection rule #

            The sign carrier deltaSign λ = (−1)^{n(λ)} is a ℤ/2 (bar-parity) selection rule keyed on the parity of the fake-degree valuation n(λ): V_λ sits in the bar-EVEN (symmetric, +) sector iff n(λ) is even, the bar-ODD (antisymmetric, −) sector iff n(λ) is odd. This is the Lean form of the bar-parity Δ_λ selection rule.
