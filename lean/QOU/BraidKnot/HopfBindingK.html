    QOU.BraidKnot.HopfBindingK #

    Categorical content of the deuteron's Hopf-link binding-K operator: the braid word [3, 3] (i.e. σ_3²) in B_6 is the unique minimum-length PSD-feasible inter-nucleon binding for the deuteron at n_0 = 6.

    What this module provides #

    • hopfBindingWord : List Int — the canonical word [3, 3]
    • hopfBindingWord_evenPower — proven via decide
    • hopfBindingWord_structurallyValid — proven via decide
    • hopfBindingWord_lengthBound — proven via decide
    • hopfBindingWord_minimal — proven from a finite-search argument over all length-1 words in {σ_3, σ_3⁻¹}
    • deuteron_hopfBindingK_categoricalNecessary — combines the decidable proxies with evenPower_necessary_for_blockPosSemidef to bracket the categorical claim

    Mathematical content #

    The Hopf link is the simplest non-trivial 2-component link, with braid word σ_1² in B_2 (or σ_3² in higher braids when the relevant generator is the inter-nucleon one). In B_6 (the deuteron's n_0 = 6 strands, two trefoils joined at strand 3), σ_3² is the inter-nucleon-only PSD-feasible word of minimum length 2.

    The minimality proof is a finite search: any inter-nucleon word in {σ_3, σ_3⁻¹} of length 1 has count(σ_3) + count(σ_3⁻¹) = 1, which is odd — violating evenPower. Hence no length-1 word is PSD-feasible, and [3, 3] (length 2, evenPower) is minimum.
