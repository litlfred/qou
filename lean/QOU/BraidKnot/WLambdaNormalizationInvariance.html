    W_λ normalization invariance — the geometric d_q²/D² vs keystone d_q²/[n]_q! fork #

    Formalize-first companion to the C1-normalization question raised on PR #2524 (Beasley–Witten Seifert–CS localization gives W_λ = d_q²/D², the true RT/Verlinde Plancherel measure, whereas the keystone consumer uses d_q²/[n]_q!). The two families differ only by the single, λ-independent global factor F/D² (F = [n]_q!, D² = Σ_λ d_q²). This file proves that structural fact before the numerical consumer probe is run, so the probe's verdict is bounded in advance:

    • wPlanch_eq_global_factor — the geometric weight is the keystone weight times the λ-independent scalar F/D². (The normalization change is a global rescale, not a per-block reshuffle.)
    • wPlanch_sum_one — the geometric weight is a genuine probability measure (Σ_λ = 1), by construction D² = Σ_λ d_q².
    • wKey_sum_eq_Z — the keystone weight sums to Z = D²/F, not 1 in general (the Z(n) defect; keystone_wlambda_Z_defect_n_scaling.py).
    • ratio_invariant — the pruning result. Any same-block ratio of weights (e.g. muon (3,2) vs electron (3,1,1), both ⊢ 5) is identical under the two normalizations: the F/D² factor cancels. Hence the C1 choice is invisible to any generation observable expressed as a same-n ratio / normalized-within-n weight; it can shift a prediction only through a cross-n or absolute (un-normalized) use of W_λ.

    Everything is a pure field identity over a generic {R : Type*} [Field R] (§7c: no archimedean input; q₀ and the 210 ppb target are never used). The q-dimensions d : ι → R and the factor F are abstract; the result holds for the Hecke q-dimensions specialized at any q.
