    QOU.BraidKnot.PartitionReindex — Burnside reindexing (bridge 2, complete) #

    Completes bridge (2) of the DeltaLambdaSignRule.burnside roadmap. The encoding equiv PartitionNatBridge.partitionEquivNatPartition (Partition n ≃ Nat.Partition n) composes with SpechtClassification.partitionEquivYoungIndex (Nat.Partition n ≃ PartitionIndex n) to give partitionEquivIndex : Partition n ≃ PartitionIndex n. Over it, a complete, parts-deduplicated list of custom partitions transports its sum to the Finset.univ sum over PartitionIndex n (sum_map_eq_sum_partitionIndex) — exactly the reindexing burnside needs to consume OVTopAssembly.sum_numSYT_sq_eq_factorial (Σ_i (#SYT i.diagram)² = n!).

    Bridge (1), the hook-length identity dimIrrep = numSYT, is now proved as QOU.BraidKnot.dimIrrep_eq_numSYT (PartitionHookProductBridge, from HookProductHLF.numSYT_mul_hookProduct + the custom↔YoungDiagram hookProduct bridge). Both ingredients burnside needs are therefore available; what remains is assembling them into the burnside statement itself.

    -- Ref: [sagan2001] Sagan, The Symmetric Group
