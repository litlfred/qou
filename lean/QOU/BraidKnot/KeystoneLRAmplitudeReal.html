    The colored amplitude at the remaining Ê₈ nodes (first flat connection) #

    The colored Lawrence–Rozansky amplitude at node λ (mark α) is A_m(α) = (2/√5)·sin(αmπ/5), factoring as |A_m(α)| = |A_m(1)|·|[α]_{ζ^m}| with the color character [α]_{ζ¹} = sin(απ/5)/sin(π/5) (= the Chebyshev-folded quantum integer qi φ α, φ = (1+√5)/2). At m = 1 the node's sin²(απ/5) reduces by the reflection sin(π − x) = sin x to one of the two vacuum values: α ∈ {1,4} (marks with [α] = 1) → (10−2√5)/16; α ∈ {2,3} (marks with [α] = φ) → (10+2√5)/16; α = 5 → 0. So the full colored amplitude closes at every Ê₈ node from the two sin² values already proved.
