    The intertwining T_d · x_μ = x_ν · T_d #

    Mathas' Lemma 3.9 turns m_{st} for a composition μ into a combination of terms for its reordering ν. The engine is this identity: the Murphy element of one composition, conjugated across by d, is the Murphy element of the other.

    Every input is now proved rather than assumed:

    • CompositionYoung.youngOfComp_conj_of_blockMatch — S_ν = d⁻¹ S_μ d, so the two index sets correspond;
    • CompositionMinimal.length_mul_sortPerm_eq_add and length_sortPerm_mul_eq_add — the length hypothesis Tw_mul_Tw needs, on both sides, because that lemma is handed;
    • TwBasis.Tw_mul_Tw — T_v · T_w = T_{w·v} when the lengths add.

    What is left is the reindexing, and the bijection is conjugation by d.

    The intertwining. T_d · x_μ = x_ν · T_d.
