        The pairing identity g_ρ + g_{−ρ} = 1.

        2 * (↑Rrow.card * q + ↑Rcol.card * (h - q) + ∑ t ∈ Soff, seminormalWeight q h (ρ t)) = ↑(2 * Rrow.card + Soff.card) * h

        Full single-generator character χ_λ(σ) = c_λ·h (all three tableau classes). The seminormal character of σ on S^λ splits the standard tableaux into three classes by where 1, 2 sit: same-row (diagonal eigenvalue q), same-column (diagonal eigenvalue −q⁻¹), and off-diagonal (weight a_ρ). The two Hecke eigenvalues satisfy q + (−q⁻¹) = q − q⁻¹ = h, so the same-column eigenvalue is −q⁻¹ = h − q (stated that way here to keep the identity inverse-free). For a self-conjugate λ the same-row and same-column counts are equal (Rrow.card = Rcol.card, conjugation symmetry), so the diagonal contribution r·q + r·(h − q) = r·h is an integer multiple of h; the off-diagonal part is (|Soff|/2)·h by two_mul_characterSum_eq. Hence

        2·χ_λ(σ) = (2·|Rrow| + |Soff|)·h, i.e. χ_λ(σ) = c_λ·h, c_λ = |Rrow| + |Soff|/2 ∈ ℤ.
