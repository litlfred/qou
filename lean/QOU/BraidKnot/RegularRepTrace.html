    QOU.BraidKnot.RegularRepTrace — the regular-representation trace bridge for Identity (B) #

    This file supplies the general-n bridge that the n ≤ 12 partition-form check of Identity (B) (IdentityIContentDecomposition.idB_verified_le12) flagged as future work in its status note: "the central Jucys–Murphy element Σ_k X_k² acts on the block V_λ by the scalar Σ_{c∈λ} c(c)², so its Plancherel average is its identity coefficient Σ_{k<n} k = binom(n,2)."

    That note names the two ways to compute the trace of the central element C₂ = Σ_k X_k² acting by left multiplication on the regular representation R[S_n]:

    1. Coefficient side (this file, general n, ℂ-free): tr_reg(C₂) = |S_n| · coeff_1(C₂) = n! · binom(n,2). The general fact — tr of left-multiplication by z on R[G] is |G|·z(1) — is trace_mulLeft_eq_card_mul_coeff_one; specialised to C₂ (whose identity coefficient is binom(n,2), the merged JucysMurphyCoeff.centralC2_coeff_one) it gives trace_centralC2_eq_factorial_mul_choose_two.

    2. Block side (Wedderburn + Okounkov–Vershik): tr_reg(C₂) = Σ_λ dim(λ) · tr_{V_λ}(C₂) = Σ_λ dim(λ)² · Σ_{c∈λ} c(c)², using R[S_n] ≅ ⊕_λ dim(λ)·V_λ and the JM eigenvalue ω_λ(C₂) = Σ_{c∈λ} c(c)².

    Equating the two sides is exactly Identity (B): Σ_λ dim(λ)² · Σ_{c∈λ} c(c)² = n! · binom(n,2). This file discharges side 1 in full generality over any CommRing R (§7c base-ring discipline — no ℂ, no representation theory, no dim(λ)/SYT/RSK). Side 2 is formalised conditionally in RegularRepBlockTrace: its existence is semisimplicity (Maschke, in Mathlib), but the S_n Okounkov–Vershik identification — which blocks (partitions), dim(λ)=#SYT(λ), and the JM-eigenvalue = contents — is not in Mathlib, so the general-n equality of the two sides stays conditional on that named input; the n ≤ 12 machine check (IdentityIContentDecomposition.idB_verified_le12) stands unconditionally in the meantime.

    The general lemma trace_mulLeft_eq_card_mul_coeff_one is the standard "trace of the left regular representation reads off the identity coefficient" fact, proved here directly from the basis-of-singles matrix of LinearMap.mulLeft: the (i,j) entry is z(i·j⁻¹), so the diagonal is the constant z(1), and the trace is |G|·z(1).

    theorem QOU.BraidKnot.RegularRepTrace.trace_mulLeft_eq_card_mul_coeff_one {R : Type u_1} [CommRing R] {G : Type u_2} [Group G] [Fintype G] [DecidableEq G] (z : MonoidAlgebra R G) :
    (LinearMap.trace R (MonoidAlgebra R G)) (LinearMap.mulLeft R z) = ↑(Fintype.card G) * z 1

    Trace of the left regular representation reads off the identity coefficient. For a finite group G and z ∈ R[G] = MonoidAlgebra R G over any CommRing R, the trace of left-multiplication by z on the regular representation is |G| · z(1). Proof: in the basis of single g 1 (Finsupp.basisSingleOne), the matrix of LinearMap.mulLeft R z has (i,j) entry (z * single j 1) i = z(i · j⁻¹) (MonoidAlgebra.mul_single_apply), so every diagonal entry is z(j · j⁻¹) = z(1); summing the constant diagonal over the |G| basis vectors gives |G|·z(1). No field, no representation theory — pure group-algebra/linear-algebra over generic R.

      theorem QOU.BraidKnot.RegularRepTrace.trace_centralC2_eq_factorial_mul_choose_two {R : Type u_1} [CommRing R] (n : ℕ) :
      (LinearMap.trace R (MonoidAlgebra R (Equiv.Perm (Fin n)))) (LinearMap.mulLeft R (centralC2 n)) = ↑n.factorial * ↑(n.choose 2)

      Identity (B), coefficient side, general n, ℂ-free. The trace of the Jucys–Murphy central element C₂ acting by left multiplication on the regular representation R[S_n] is n! · binom(n,2), over any CommRing R. Combines the general trace formula (trace_mulLeft_eq_card_mul_coeff_one: tr_reg(z) = |S_n|·z(1)), the order |S_n| = n! (Fintype.card_perm + Fintype.card_fin), and the identity coefficient C₂(1) = binom(n,2) (centralC2_coeff_one). This is side 1 of the Identity-(B) trace equation; equating it with the Wedderburn block side Σ_λ dim(λ)²·Σ_{c∈λ} c(c)² (formalised conditionally in RegularRepBlockTrace, the residual input being the S_n Okounkov–Vershik identification) yields (B).
