    QOU.BraidKnot — Catalogue of Taylor coefficients #

    Catalogue of Taylor coefficients of delta_tau_M_surrogate at q = 1, orders 0..7, sourced from qbeta-c3-multiblock-recursion-attack.witness.json (taylor_coeffs_w_expansion field).

    The values are the canonical (borromean) coefficients, the frame of delta_tau_M_surrogate in QBetaPerBlockChiClosedForms.lean and of the witness's taylor_coeffs_w_expansion field. The table held the superseded grouped-frame values for orders 3..7 (-41/9, 149/18, -1729/108, 111/4, -3665/81) until 2026-10-10 (bean qou-eddc); orders 0..2 are the same in both frames.

    This file is a CATALOGUE: each theorem below is rfl on the table and proves nothing about the function. Every order is proved separately, as a statement about delta_tau_M_surrogate itself, in QBetaPerBlockChiClosedForms.lean: delta_tau_M_surrogate_vanish_at_one, delta_tau_M_surrogate_hasDerivAt_one, and delta_tau_M_surrogate_taylor_coeff_2 … _7.
