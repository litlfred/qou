    QOU.BraidKnot.SpechtBlockDimension — the target side of the Wedderburn count #

    Fourth brick of W1+W3 of the Wedderburn epic (qou-bxw4), after #5139 (W2), #5151 (Schur orthonormality) and #5156 (Hom_G(k[G], V) ≃ V).

    The Wedderburn statement sn_isotypic_specht_split asserts an algebra isomorphism

    k[S_n] ≃ₐ[k] ∏_{μ ⊢ n} Matrix (Fin (dim S^μ)) (Fin (dim S^μ)) k.

    Its target has k-dimension Σ_μ (dim S^μ)², and its source has dimension n!. So the identity n! = Σ_μ (dim S^μ)² — the RSK sum-of-squares, which OVTopAssembly currently carries as a sorryAx — is a dimension count of that isomorphism.

    This file computes the two dimensions and nothing else. It is pure linear algebra; no representation theory enters, and it claims no isomorphism.

    What is not here #

    The isomorphism itself, and hence the identity. Establishing either needs the decomposition of the regular module, which is the next brick. Recording the two sides separately keeps that brick honest: a later n! = Σ_μ (dim S^μ)² must come from an actual map, not from these two computations sitting next to each other.
