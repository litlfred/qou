    Dressed Particle #

    A dressed particle is the codomain of a dressing morphism dress_S : P_bare → P_dressed in the confinement category. Dressings compose via shell concatenation; stripping is not in general invertible.

    structure QOU.BraidKnot.DressedParticle :
    Type

    Dressed particle. Codomain of a dressing morphism; carries a bare-particle core, an electron shell, and the composite confined particle whose Markov class is that of ι(K_bare) · W_bind in H_{A+Z}(q).

    • bare : ConfinedParticle

      Bare nuclear confined particle.

    • shell : ElectronShell

      Electron shell attached to bare.

    • composite : ConfinedParticle

      The composite confined particle obtained after dressing.

    • shell_on_bare : self.shell.nucleus = self.bare

      Consistency: the shell's nucleus is the bare particle.

    • level_preserved : self.composite.level = self.bare.level

      Level preservation: dressing does not change the level.
