    Keystone G2 — the full heat-kernel ladder gate (C / D / E) #

    Strengthens the single-order G2 gate (KeystoneG2Gate: the 2nd heat-kernel coefficient a₂(n) must equal the proved cubic n144C) to the full proved ladder: a candidate equivariant index reproduces the W_λ defect only if its cleared heat-kernel coefficient at every derived order k equals the proved value —

    order k h-power cleared coefficient clearing factor
    1 h² n144C (= 144·C) 1·144¹
    2 h⁴ n1036800D (= 1036800·D) 50·144²
    3 h⁶ n21946982400E (= 21946982400·E) 7350·144³

    The teeth: the gate is unique on the derived orders, subsumes the single-order G2 gate, and forces the universal n(n−1) vanishing at every order (every heat-kernel coefficient vanishes at the single box, since Z(0) = Z(1) = 1 exactly). The clearing factors follow the proved m_k·144^k ladder (WLambdaDefect.denom_ladder, denom_E).

    Base ring ℤ; no q₀, no fit. Distinct from the sibling z2bn target a_D = lw (#2800): this is target-1, the defect-cubic ladder, not the prolongation period.
