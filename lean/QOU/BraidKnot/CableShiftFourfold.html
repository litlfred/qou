    def QOU.BraidKnot.cableBlock (g : ℤ) :
    Word

    The four letters the 2-parallel substitution writes for one letter g.

    For g = i > 0 this is σ_{2i} σ_{2i−1} σ_{2i+1} σ_{2i}, the standard 2-parallel image. For g = −i < 0 the image is the block reversed with every letter inverted, σ_{2i}⁻¹ σ_{2i+1}⁻¹ σ_{2i−1}⁻¹ σ_{2i}⁻¹ — which is the SAME list of expressions in g, since −(2i+1) = 2g − 1 and −(2i−1) = 2g + 1. That coincidence is why the sign bookkeeping below needs no case split on the letter's own sign beyond g ≠ 0.

    • QOU.BraidKnot.cableBlock g = [2 * g, 2 * g - 1, 2 * g + 1, 2 * g]

        What the law actually rides on: multiplicativity, not cyclicity #

        A sibling session raised this on PR #5863 and it is a real sharpening, verified here rather than taken on trust. The framing above says "a one-dimensional channel sees only the writhe". True, but it names the channel rather than the property. Two properties are in play and only one of them does the work:

        • Cyclicity (χ(xy) = χ(yx)) is shared — every character is a class function, the multi-dimensional χ^λ included. So cyclicity cannot be what separates the channels.
        • Multiplicativity (χ(xy) = χ(x)·χ(y)) is what a one-dimensional channel has and a higher-dimensional one does not: a trace of a product is not a product of traces once the dimension exceeds one.

        So the migration the deficit measures is from a multiplicative readout to a merely cyclic one. That is why the writhe stops controlling the valuation: the writhe is exactly what a homomorphism can see, and once the maximizer is a sum over standard-tableau paths there is no homomorphism left for the factor of four to ride on.

        fourfold_of_multiplicative makes the dependence explicit — ANY readout that is multiplicative on concatenation and sends each letter to s^{±1} quadruples, whatever channel it came from. sqWritheReadout is the converse guard: a readout that is cyclic but NOT multiplicative, on which the law fails outright (it scales by sixteen, not four). Without that guard the theorem above could be read as though cyclicity sufficed.
