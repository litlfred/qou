    QOU.BraidKnot.JucysMurphy — brick 5 of the Okounkov–Vershik tower #

    The Jucys–Murphy elements X_k = ∑_{j<k} (j k) of the group algebra k[S_n] = MonoidAlgebra k (Perm (Fin n)), the cell content c(i,j) = j - i, and the content-square sum ω_λ = ∑_{c∈λ} c(c)² — together with the deep Okounkov–Vershik theorems that connect them to the Specht modules of brick 2 (QOU.BraidKnot.Specht, imported above).

    This is "brick 5" of the QOU OV-tower. The JM elements generate a maximal commutative (Gelfand–Tsetlin) subalgebra of k[S_n]; on the Gelfand–Zetlin / seminormal basis of the Specht module S^λ (indexed by standard Young tableaux T), X_k acts diagonally with eigenvalue c(cell of k in T) = col − row. Hence the central element C₂ = ∑_k X_k² acts on S^λ by the scalar ∑_{c∈λ} c(c)². This scalar is the ω = Σc² input that the Identity-(B) keystone's SnWedderburnSpectralData (in RegularRepBlockTrace) carries as a field.

    What is sorry-free here (the definitional foundation) #

    • jucysMurphy n i — the JM element X_i = ∑_{j<i} single (swap j i) 1, a Finset.sum of MonoidAlgebra.single of transpositions. Aligned definitionally with the inner sum of QOU.BraidKnot.RegularRepTrace.centralC2, so ∑ k, (jucysMurphy n k)^2 is exactly that established centralC2 n (centralC2_eq_sum_jucysMurphy_sq below records the equality).
    • cellContent c = c.2 − c.1 (column minus row), in ℤ.
    • contentSqSum μ = ∑_{c∈μ.cells} (cellContent c)^2, in ℤ — the ω = Σc² of the keystone.
    • jucysMurphy_zero — X_0 = 0 (the sum over j < 0 is empty): the easy sanity anchor.
    • jucysMurphy_one — X_1 = 0 on Fin n (the only j < (1 : Fin n) is excluded because there is no strand below 0; recorded as jucysMurphy_succ_zero for Fin (n+2) where the index 1 genuinely exists, giving X_1 = single (swap 0 1) 1).

    What is proved sorry-free beyond the definitional layer #

    • jucysMurphy_commute — the JM elements pairwise commute (the GZ algebra is commutative); Jucys's classical double-sum computation.
    • conj_jucysMurphy_self / conj_jucysMurphy_other / pair_shift_comm (§4b) — the JM conjugation recursion s·X_a·s = X_{a+1} − s (adjacent swap s), fixing every other X_m; generic [CommRing k].
    • end_conj_shift_eigenvector_pair / end_conj_fix_eigenvector / end_involution_eigenvector_ne (§4c) — the classical 2×2 seminormal block: on a joint eigenvector v of the recursion pair, w = S v − (c_b − c_a)⁻¹ • v is again a joint eigenvector with the swapped eigenvalues, nonzero when (c_b − c_a)² ≠ 1.
    • rep_polytabloid / rep_mem_spechtSubmodule (§4d) — σ · e_t = e_{σ·t}; the Specht submodule is a subrepresentation.
    • seminormalVector_data_step — the seminormal induction step: transport of the full seminormal eigenvector datum along any admissible adjacent swap (AdjacentSwapStep).
    • seminormalVector_data_exists — the all-T eigenvector existence, assembled sorry-free from the two cited cores below by walking the swap chain from the base tableau.
    • jucysMurphy_eigenvalue, exists_indep_seminormal_family, seminormalVectors_span, centralC2_acts_by_contentSqSum — the completeness chain and the ω = Σc² punchline, sorry-free modulo the cores below.

    What is stated with sorry + -- Ref: (the residual OV cores) #

    • seminormalVector_base_exists — existence of the seminormal eigenvector for one distinguished (base) tableau. -- Ref: [vershik-kerov1977].
    • syt_adjacentSwap_connected — any two standard tableaux of a shape are connected by admissible adjacent swaps (pure combinatorics). -- Ref: [sagan2001].
    • syt_content_tuple_injective — the content tuple determines the standard tableau (the GZ spectrum is simple; pure combinatorics). -- Ref: [vershik-kerov1977].

    All three are strictly smaller than the previously carried all-T existence sorry; they are honestly stubbed per AGENTS.md §1 (theorem … := by sorry -- Ref: …), never : True, never native_decide.

    Base ring (§7c) #

    The JM / content layer is purely algebraic: variable {k : Type} [CommRing k]. No ℝ, no Real.*, no ordering on k, no linarith/positivity/norm_num. cellContent/contentSqSum land in ℤ (integer arithmetic — algebraic, not archimedean-ℝ). The finite filter j.val < i.val is an order on the index type Fin n, not on the ring k, and is necessary (it is the very "strands below k" defining X_k). Most of the layer is stated over generic [CommRing k]. The seminormal-form results — seminormalVector_data_exists, jucysMurphy_eigenvalue, the seminormalVectors_span completeness chain (exists_indep_seminormal_family + seminormalSpan_le + numSYT_le_finrank_seminormalSpan), and the punchline centralC2_acts_by_contentSqSum — are stated over [Field k] [CharZero k] instead. This is a correctness restriction (not an archimedean ℝ leak — char-0 field, no ordering, no Real.*): the seminormal/GZ eigenbasis is genuinely a char-0 fact, and over a general commutative ring the relevant claims are false — completeness S^λ ⊆ span fails over ℤ (denominators), and simultaneous-eigenvector existence fails over ℤ/pℤ (cell-contents collide modulo p). See those lemmas' docstrings.

    • [sagan2001] Sagan, The Symmetric Group (Specht modules, standard tableaux).
    • [fulton1997] Fulton, Young Tableaux (tableaux, cell contents).

            4b. The Jucys–Murphy conjugation recursion (sorry-free, generic CommRing) #

            The engine of every inductive seminormal argument: conjugating by the adjacent transposition s = (a, a+1) sends X_a ↦ X_{a+1} − s (conj_jucysMurphy_self) and fixes every other X_k (conj_jucysMurphy_other). Together with the pure noncommutative-ring cancellation pair_shift_comm, these are the classical Jucys relations. All sorry-free, over the file-wide generic [CommRing k].

            4c. The classical 2×2 seminormal block (sorry-free linear algebra) #

            The module-level engine of Young's seminormal form: on a simultaneous eigenvector v of two operators A, B intertwined by an involution S via the JM recursion S·A·S = B − S, the vector w = S v − (c_B − c_A)⁻¹ • v is again a simultaneous eigenvector — with the swapped eigenvalues (end_conj_shift_eigenvector_pair); operators fixed by the conjugation keep their eigenvalue (end_conj_fix_eigenvector); and w ≠ 0 whenever (c_B − c_A)² ≠ 1 (end_involution_eigenvector_ne). No Specht content — pure Module.End algebra.

            source

            A (S v - (cb - ca)⁻¹ • v) = cb • (S v - (cb - ca)⁻¹ • v) ∧ B (S v - (cb - ca)⁻¹ • v) = ca • (S v - (cb - ca)⁻¹ • v)

            The 2×2 seminormal block, eigenvalue-swap half. If S² = 1, S·A·S = B − S (the Jucys–Murphy recursion, abstractly), and v is an A-eigenvector with value ca and a B-eigenvector with value cb ≠ ca, then w = S v − (cb − ca)⁻¹ • v is an A-eigenvector with value cb and a B-eigenvector with value ca (the swapped pair). Sorry-free.

            4d. The Specht submodule is a subrepresentation (sorry-free) #

            5. The remaining deep Okounkov–Vershik theorems (stated, sorry + -- Ref:) #

            source
            structure QOU.BraidKnot.JucysMurphy.SeminormalVector {k : Type} [CommRing k] (μ : YoungDiagram) :
            Type

            A seminormal (Gelfand–Zetlin) eigenvector of the Specht module S^λ, packaged abstractly so that the eigenvalue theorem can be stated at the level of brick 2's Specht.Mtab.rep without first constructing the whole GZ basis (which is itself the content of the OV theorem).

            The bundle carries:

            • T — a standard Young tableau of shape μ (brick 1's StandardYoungTableau), which indexes the GZ basis vector;
            • v — the corresponding seminormal vector, living in the Specht submodule S^λ ≤ M^λ;
            • nz — non-triviality (v ≠ 0), so "eigenvector" is meaningful;
            • cellOf — the bijection Fin (nOf μ) ≃ μ.cells that T induces, reading label k to the cell of μ holding k. This is a bijection because a standard tableau fills the nOf μ = μ.card cells with the labels 0, …, nOf μ − 1 exactly once (brick 1's StandardYoungTableau.inj_on); packaging it as an Equiv is the faithful form of "the cell holding label k", and it is what makes the content-square sum over labels equal the content-square sum over cells (centralC2_smul_seminormal);
            • isEig — the defining seminormal property: each X_k acts on v by the scalar cellContent (cellOf k), i.e. the content of the cell holding label k in T. We express the JM action on M^λ through Specht.Mtab.rep extended to the group algebra (Representation.asAlgebraHom), and the integer scalar via zsmul.

            This is the honest packaging: rather than assert the GZ basis exists (the theorem), we make the seminormal-eigenvector data a hypothesis, and jucysMurphy_eigenvalue states the consequence that characterises such a vector. Replacing this bundle with a constructed GZ basis is exactly the OV theorem (-- Ref: [vershik-kerov1977]).

              It is nonzero (so "eigenvector" is non-vacuous).

Bijective because a standard tableau is a bijection labels ↔ cells (brick 1's inj_on + the card identity μ.cells.card = μ.card = nOf μ). Carrying the bijection (rather than a bare function) is what lets the content-square sum over labels collapse to contentSqSum μ.

                def QOU.BraidKnot.JucysMurphy.AdjacentSwapStep (μ : YoungDiagram) (T T' : StandardYoungTableau μ) :
                Prop

                An admissible adjacent-swap step between standard tableaux, recorded at the level of their content vectors (all the seminormal transport needs). T' is obtained from T by an adjacent swap (a, b) (b = a + 1) when: the content vectors of T' and T agree away from {a, b} and are swapped at {a, b}; and the content gap d = c_T(b) − c_T(a) satisfies d ≠ 0 and d² ≠ 1. The two gap conditions are the classical admissibility: for a standard tableau, consecutive labels never sit on the same diagonal (d ≠ 0), and |d| = 1 exactly when they are row- or column-adjacent — the case in which the swapped filling is not standard. So an AdjacentSwapStep is precisely "swap a, a+1 in T, landing on another standard tableau".
