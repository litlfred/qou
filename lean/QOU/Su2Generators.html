    QOU.Su2Generators #

    Canonical declaration for content block at quantum-observable-universe/brings-surface/su2-generators.ts.

    Formalises the manuscript definition of the binary dihedral group Dic₃ ⊂ SU(2) (order 12) — the SU(2)-lift of S₃ ≅ D₃ — by its two generators α (a lift of the 3-cycle) and β (a lift of a transposition), with α³ = β² = -I, the dihedral relation βαβ⁻¹ = α⁻¹, and the derived commutator [β,α] = α⁻². Stated over an ambient group G with the central element z = -I (pure group theory — §7c-clean, no archimedean structure).

    structure QOU.Su2Generators (G : Type u_1) [Group G] :
    Type u_1

    The binary dihedral group Dic₃ presented inside an ambient group G.

    A definition carrier: generators α, β, the central element z = -I, and the defining relations α³ = z, β² = z, z² = 1, z central, and the dihedral relation βαβ⁻¹ = α⁻¹.

    • α : G

      Lift of the 3-cycle.

    • β : G

      Lift of a transposition.

    • z : G

      The central element -I.

    • z_central (g : G) : self.z * g = g * self.z

      z is central.

    • z_sq : self.z ^ 2 = 1

      (-I)² = I.

    • α_cubed : self.α ^ 3 = self.z

      α³ = -I.

    • β_squared : self.β ^ 2 = self.z

      β² = -I.

    • dihedral : self.β * self.α * self.β⁻¹ = self.α⁻¹

      Dihedral relation βαβ⁻¹ = α⁻¹.
