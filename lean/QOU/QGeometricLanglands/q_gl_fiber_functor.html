    §3b class axiomatisation for content block conj:q-gl-fiber-functor: Fibre-functor mediation of the $q$-Langlands correspondence.

    This is a conjecture: the class carries the conjecture's actual content as the uniqueness field ($\tau$-rigidity forces $\mathbb{L}_q$ unique up to natural isomorphism); the noncomputable instance's sorry represents the open conjecture itself (per AGENTS.md §3b), NOT a missing proof.
