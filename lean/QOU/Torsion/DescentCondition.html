    QOU.Torsion.DescentCondition — Twisted 2-cocycle condition #

    The global consistency of the distributional connection A_∞ is equivalent to the satisfaction of the twisted 2-cocycle condition.

    Pre-Archimedean (formal-chain) version. Lives under lean/QOU/ and is compiled by lake build. The co-located documentation stub at lifting-and-descent/descent-condition.lean mirrors this content.
