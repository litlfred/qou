              theorem QOU.Torsion.vacuum_state_state_eq (dla : DirectLimitAlgebra) (v₁ v₂ : VacuumState dla) (n : ℕ) (a₀ : dla.sectionAlgebra n) (a : dla.limitAlgebra) :
              v₁.state a = v₂.state a

              Uniqueness of the vacuum state: any two vacuum states on the same direct limit algebra agree on all elements.

              Proof strategy: Both states are constant (by invariant), and they agree on embedded elements (by extends_haar + Haar uniqueness). So they are equal everywhere.

              This is the categorical analogue of the Schur uniqueness in QOU.Torsion.invariant_functional_unique (vacuum-state.lean).
