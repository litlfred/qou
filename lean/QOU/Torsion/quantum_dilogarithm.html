    QOU.Torsion.quantum_dilogarithm — tr_trans(ϱ, p_i) = Φ_q(z_i) #

    Definition / Proposition (def:quantum-dilogarithm).

    In the archimedean realisation, the transient trace contribution tr_trans(ϱ, p_i) from the topological decomposition of mass evaluates to the quantum dilogarithm Φ_q(z_i), where z_i is the spectral coordinate of p_i in the Briggs–Klein chart.

    The Lean encoding records the equation as a class field of an abstract QuantumDilogarithmContext.

    Base ring (AGENTS.md §7c) — ℝ here is required, not a gratuitous specialisation #

    The statement above is explicitly the archimedean realisation ("In the archimedean realisation, …"), and the object it names forces that: Φ_q is the Faddeev quantum dilogarithm, a transcendental analytic function (an infinite product / integral representation), not a rational or polynomial construct. It has no meaning over a generic {R : Type*} [CommRing R], and neither does the spectralCoord chart coordinate it is evaluated at. This block therefore sits on the observation side of the substrate→archimedean wall by construction.

    Consistent with docs/audits/2026-06-25-lean-library-tree-7c-coverage-gap.md, which already records the dilogarithm files as legitimately archimedean — they are named there among the files a directory-based heuristic wrongly flagged.

    Recorded here so §7c sweeps stop re-flagging the module: a bare grep sees a Torsion module whose every field is ℝ-typed and scores a base-ring candidate, which it is not.
