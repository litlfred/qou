    QOU.Torsion.neutron_proton_splitting — neutron-proton mass splitting #

    Proposition (prop:neutron-proton-splitting).

    The neutron–proton mass difference is

      Δm = m_n − m_p = α · |Φ(udd) − Φ(uud)| ≈ 1.302 MeV

    where Φ(uud) and Φ(udd) are the quantum-dilogarithm volumes of the proton and neutron quark orderings respectively, and α = m_e · 8G is the same binding scale as the atomic mass formula. CODATA value: Δm = 1.293 MeV (error 0.63 %).

    This is a quantitative physical prediction: the splitting equals the binding scale times the absolute difference of two quantum-dilogarithm volumes Φ(udd), Φ(uud), and the resulting number matches CODATA to under 1 %. Since the qou-octr fix (2026-08-14) the file is sorry-free: the corpus values of α, Φ(uud), Φ(udd) are pinned as provenance-cited hypothesis fields (NeutronProtonSplittingNumerics), the CODATA agreement is derived from them by arithmetic, and the earlier exact-equality conjunct — refutable as written, and a §7ab approximation-convention violation (the manuscript claims ≈, never =) — is replaced by the prediction + tolerance form. Porting the exact closed forms of the traced-volumes themselves stays open.

    The two Φ volumes (Φ(uud) ≈ -0.8785, Φ(udd) ≈ -1.2261) are derived: they are the canonical Hecke traced-volume of the quark orderings (uud = [1,1,0], udd = [1,0,0]), Φ(K) = Σᵢ log|V̂ᵢ(K)| with V̂ᵢ = z·M₁₀ + M₁₁ the transfer-matrix product over the quark crossings (computations/level1-qvalues.py, HeckeRational.traced_volume). This has an exact closed form — log of a rational function of q — e.g. Φ(udd) = log|(q²+1)²(10000q² − 4993q − 10000) / (40000 q³)| (with the NF-basis constant z = 4993/10000). Provenance + symbolic derivation: docs/audits/2026-07-11-quark-volume-provenance.md. (This transfer-matrix V̂ᵢ is a different object from the Φ_q q-Pochhammer of QOU.MathlibExt.QuantumDilogarithm.) They remain opaque NeutronProtonSplittingContext fields (§3b-cond) here only because the traced_volume construction is not yet ported to Lean (AGENTS.md anti-laundering rule).

            theorem QOU.Torsion.neutron_proton_splitting [cm : NeutronProtonSplittingNumerics] :
            predictedNeutronProtonSplitting = NeutronProtonSplittingContext.α * |NeutronProtonSplittingContext.Φ_udd - NeutronProtonSplittingContext.Φ_uud| ∧ |predictedNeutronProtonSplitting - codataNeutronProtonSplittingMeV| ≤ 1e-2 * codataNeutronProtonSplittingMeV

            Neutron-proton mass splitting ([prop:neutron-proton-splitting]), conditional on the corpus-pinned numerics — now sorry-free.

            The framework prediction is α · |Φ(udd) − Φ(uud)| (predictedNeutronProtonSplitting); with the pinned values it equals 3.7449 · 0.3476 ≈ 1.3017 MeV and agrees with the CODATA splitting 1.293 MeV within the 1 % manuscript tolerance (measured error 0.68 %).

            The earlier flat form carried an exact-equality conjunct m_n − m_p = α·|Φ(udd) − Φ(uud)|, which over-claimed on two counts: it was refutable as written (neutron_proton_splitting_unconstrained_false below), and exact = between a measured mass difference and a derived value violates the §7ab approximation convention — the manuscript claims ≈ with a stated tolerance, which is exactly conjunct (2) here. The prediction-formula conjunct (1) holds definitionally.
