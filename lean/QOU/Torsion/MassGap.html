    Yang–Mills Existence and Mass Gap (Categorical Formulation) #

    Formal statement of the Yang–Mills mass gap conjecture in the QOU framework. The conjecture translates the classical millennium problem into categorical language:

    • Existence: a critical point of the q-weighted Yang–Mills–Levi functional exists and the vacuum state extends to a Hilbert space.
    • Mass gap: every non-vacuum excitation has categorical mass bounded below by Δ_q > 0.

    Key provable ingredients #

    1. The minimum crossing energy ½(q − q⁻¹) > 0 for q > 1.
    2. The mass gap lower bound Δ_q ≥ E_cross · (q−1)² > 0.
    3. The two-point function decays exponentially with rate Δ_q.

    References #

    • [manuscript] Chapter 5, Conjecture: Yang–Mills existence and mass gap
    • [jaffe2006] A. Jaffe & E. Witten, "Quantum Yang–Mills Theory", Clay Mathematics Institute Millennium Problem.

      Mass gap lower bound #

      source
      noncomputable def QOU.Torsion.massGapBound (q : ℝ) :
      ℝ

      The mass gap lower bound Δ_q: the minimum categorical mass any non-trivial vortex can have.

      Δ_q = E_×(q) · (q − 1)² = ½(q − q⁻¹)(q − 1)²

      Equations
      • QOU.Torsion.massGapBound q = QOU.Torsion.minCrossingEnergy q * (q - 1) ^ 2

        theorem QOU.Torsion.massGapBound_at_one :

        source
        theorem QOU.Torsion.massGapBound_mono {q₁ q₂ : ℝ} (hq₁ : 1 < q₁) (hq₂ : q₁ ≤ q₂) :
        massGapBound q₁ ≤ massGapBound q₂

        The mass gap bound is monotonically increasing for q > 1.

        Δ_q = E_×(q) · (q − 1)² where E_×(q) = (q − q⁻¹)/2. Both factors are positive and increasing for q > 1, so their product is increasing.
