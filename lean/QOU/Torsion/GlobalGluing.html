    QOU.Torsion.GlobalGluing — Global gluing of twisted deformations #

    The direct limit algebra A_∞ is a well-defined global section of a sheaf of twisted deformation algebras on the underlying site 𝒮.

    Pre-Archimedean (formal-chain) version. Lives under lean/QOU/ and is compiled by lake build. The co-located documentation stub at lifting-and-descent/global-gluing.lean mirrors this content.
