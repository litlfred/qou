    QOU.Torsion.algebraic_filter_classification — fermion / boson via [F_A, α] #

    Proposition (prop:algebraic-filter-classification).

    Let F_A be the substrate curvature and α be a harmonic 1-form in the q-harmonic basis on branes. The commutator [F_A, α] classifies α into exactly one of two types:

    (i) Fermionic (matter): [F_A, α] ≠ 0.

    (ii) Bosonic (radiation): [F_A, α] = 0.

    The Lean encoding records the dichotomy as a ∀ α, fermionic α ∨ bosonic α statement against an abstract context.

    • IsBosonic : F_A → OneForm → Prop

    • zero : OneForm

      Zero 1-form (for the bosonic case).

      The two predicates' definitions: fermionic ⟺ commutator ≠ 0; bosonic ⟺ commutator = 0.

    • bosonic_iff (F : F_A) (α : OneForm) : IsBosonic F α ↔ commutator F α = zero
