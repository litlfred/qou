    The mass trace as a functional of the multiplicative integral #

    def:mass-identity defines the inertial mass of a closed cycle ϱ through the composite Ψ = (∫_A ⊗ id) ∘ Δ and the categorical trace on End(A), and its proof block for the archimedean model (mass-identity-C) identifies the same mass with a non-abelian multiplicative integral, i.e. a holonomy. As written the block applies tr to Ψ ∘ ϱ : 𝟙 → A, which is not an endomorphism (bean qou-puwe, defect 1). This file states the identity in a form that typechecks and proves it, over an arbitrary braided monoidal category, from the axioms of FrobeniusHopfData alone.

    Statement #

    A cycle enters through its left-multiplication endomorphism L_x = λ_A⁻¹ ≫ (x ▷ A) ≫ μ. The discrete multiplicative integral of a word w = [g₁, …, g_k] of transports gᵢ : 𝟙 ⟶ A is their ordered product hol w = g₁ * ⋯ * g_k in the convolution monoid Hom(𝟙, A); this is the List.prod holonomy of QOU.MathlibExt.YekutieliIntegration.holonomy, read in A. The theorems are:

    1. massIntegrand_eq_leftMul_density: Ψ = L_u with u = Ψ ∘ η. The mass integrand is left multiplication by one fixed element, the density u of the Frobenius form ∫_A relative to the coalgebra. Only the unit law and the Frobenius relation are used.

    2. density_eq_unit_of_frobeniusForm_eq_counit: if ∫_A is the counit, then u = η and Ψ = id. This is the correct reason for the step "Ψ = id" in prop:categorical-mass-markov; it is the counit law, not the Nakayama automorphism or S² = id.

    3. leftTrace_leftMul: tr(L_y) = ∫_A(y · c) with c = μ ∘ coev, the Casimir element of the self-duality.

    4. massTrace_eq_functional_hol (the mass identity): for every word w,

      tr(L_{hol w} ≫ Ψ) = ∫_A(u · hol w · c).

      The trace of the mass endomorphism of a cycle is a fixed linear functional, x ↦ ∫_A(u · x · c), evaluated on the multiplicative integral of the cycle.

    What this does not say #

    The functional x ↦ ∫_A(u · x · c) is the categorical trace of A acting on itself. Under a fibre functor to free R-modules it is the regular trace x ↦ Tr_A(L_{u x}), not the Jones–Markov trace: for A = H_n(q) the normalised regular trace fails the Markov property at n = 3 (tr(σ₁σ₂) = h²/6 against (tr σ₁)² = h²/4, h = q − q⁻¹). The Markov trace is the quantum trace of the module V^{⊗n}, not of A (see QOU.MassEndomorphism.MassLeftMulEndomorphism). This file makes no claim about that identification.

    Base ring (§7c). No ring, order or norm appears: everything is a morphism identity in a monoidal category. No archimedean structure is used.

    References #

    • Ref: [kock2004] https://doi.org/10.1017/CBO9780511615443 — Frobenius objects, the Frobenius relation, and the self-duality ev = ∫_A ∘ μ.
    • Ref: [yekutieli2015] https://doi.org/10.1142/9537 — multiplicative integration; only its discrete (ordered-product) layer is used here.
