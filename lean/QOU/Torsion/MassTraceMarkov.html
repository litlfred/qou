    The Markov trace of a braid is a trace of its multiplicative integral #

    QOU.Torsion.MassTraceIdentity proved the mass identity with the categorical trace of the Frobenius algebra A on itself, and recorded that this functional is the regular trace, which is not a Markov trace (tr(σ₁σ₂) = h²/6 against (tr σ₁)² = h²/4 on H₃(q)). This file states the identity with the trace that is a Markov trace: the site-weighted (quantum) trace on V^{⊗(m+1)}, V = R², through Jimbo's R-matrix.

    Statement #

    A braid word is a list of letters (a, ε), a : Fin m a strand and ε the sign of the crossing. Its transport is the R-matrix acting at strands a, a+1 of V^{⊗(m+1)}:

    T(a, +) = Ř_a,        T(a, −) = Ř_a − (q − q⁻¹) = Ř_a⁻¹.

    The discrete multiplicative integral of the word is the ordered product of its transports, Hol(ℓ₁ ⋯ ℓ_k) = T(ℓ₁) ⋯ T(ℓ_k). The theorems are:

    1. braidTrace_wordBraid (the mass identity, Markov form):

      tr_M(β_ℓ) = wi^{m+1} · Tr(K^{⊗(m+1)} · Hol(ℓ)),

      where β_ℓ is the braid the word spells, tr_M is the normalised Markov trace QOU.IwahoriHecke.BraidGroup.braidTrace, K = diag(w₀, w₁) is the site weight and wi is the inverse of w₀ + w₁.

    2. jonesFn_wordBraid: the framing-corrected trace of QOU.IwahoriHecke.JonesNormalisation is the same functional of Hol(ℓ), times A^{m+1} · B^{writhe}, with the writhe read off the word as the signed letter count.

    The weight is not chosen here. QOU.IwahoriHecke.MarkovPeel.peel_forces_physical_weight shows the peel exists only for w = (1, q²) up to scale, and MarkovNormalized shows the normalised peel scalar is then q³/(q²+1) (convention D). The two theorems hold for every weight; the Markov property holds only for that one.

    What this does not say #

    • Not a link invariant. jonesFn is invariant under both Markov moves and under conjugation, but descending it to links needs Markov's theorem, which this corpus does not prove (JonesNormalisation, §"One gap remains").
    • Not the categorical trace of A. That is MassTraceIdentity, and it is the regular trace. This file replaces it; it does not identify the two.
    • Not the continuous holonomy. Hol is the ordered product of finitely many transports. That a KZ / Drinfeld–Kohno holonomy along a path of configurations equals such a product is a cited theorem, not proved here.
    • Not the abstract Markov trace. The Ocneanu-weight trace on H_n(q) being this functional is bean qou-0fmb.

    Base ring (§7c). R is any commutative ring; q⁻¹ and (w₀ + w₁)⁻¹ are carried as qi, wi with witnessing equations. No order, norm or root is used.

    References #

    • Ref: [turaev2010] https://doi.org/10.1515/9783110221831 — enhanced Yang–Baxter operators; the functional α^{-w} β^{-n} Tr(ρ(b) ∘ μ^{⊗n}) is this one with μ = K.
    • Ref: [jones1987] https://doi.org/10.2307/1971403 — Hecke algebra representations of braid groups and link polynomials.
    • Ref: [jimbo1986] https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00704588 — the R-matrix.
