    §7 Mass Identity — archimedean realisation #

    The observable-side layer of def:mass-identity: the substrate parameter and the non-negativity of the integrand.

    Base ring (§7c) — this is the post-wall half of a deliberate split #

    QOU/Torsion/MassIdentity.lean holds the generic core, over an arbitrary [CommRing R]. This file holds everything that needs the wall, and it needs its own module rather than a section of the core because check-wall-side treats a real-field type coexisting with generic-R markers in one file as a split-me defect — correctly, and it caught a first attempt that kept both here.

    Two things live on this side, and only two:

    • sp : ArchimedeanSubstrateField M — irreducibly archimedean. Its field : M → ℝ comes with field_pos, field_ge_one and not_root_of_unity: order conditions and a root-of-unity condition, none of which a generic ring can state.
    • integrand_nonneg — an order on the codomain.

    Everything else — the cycle, its non-emptiness, and the two multiplicative-integration factors — is ring-free or generic and stays in the core.

    Why the split cost nothing #

    MassIdentity.sp was inert: nothing anywhere in the tree read it (grep-verified; the .sp hits are Symplectic.sp, cr.sp and eigenvalue.sp on unrelated structures). So the deeper of the two archimedean anchors came out for free. And the only consumer, TopologicalMassDecomposition, is ℝ throughout already — it wanted this layer all along and simply was not labelled as one.

    Bean qou-puwe, option 1. Fixes the wall-side defect only; the block's prose still defines mass as the Frobenius–Hopf categorical trace while the core formalises nonabelian multiplicative integration, which is option 3 and remains open.

    Archimedean realisation of the mass identity.

    The generic core plus exactly what needs the wall: the substrate parameter, and the non-negativity of the integrand on the cycle.

    An ℝ-instantiation of MassIdentity — the core's ring is fixed here and nowhere earlier.

    • connectionGradient : M → ℝ
    • leviCurvatureCoupling : M → ℝ
    • vortexCycle : Set M
    • cycle_nonempty : self.vortexCycle.Nonempty
    • sp : ArchimedeanSubstrateField M

      The substrate parameter q : M → ℝ. Archimedean by construction: its field is ordered and bounded below by 1.

    • integrand_nonneg (p : M) : p ∈ self.vortexCycle → 0 ≤ self.connectionGradient p * self.leviCurvatureCoupling p

      The integrand is non-negative on the cycle. An order condition, and the reason this structure cannot live in the generic core.
