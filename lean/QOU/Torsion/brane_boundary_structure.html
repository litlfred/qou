    QOU.Torsion.brane_boundary_structure — four properties of ∂A_n #

    Proposition (prop:brane-boundary-structure).

    The defining boundary ∂A_n has the following properties:

    (1) Codimension: ∂A_n is a closed subset of real codimension ≥ 2 in 𝓢_n.

    (2) Exceptional object: at each degeneration locus of r_n, ker(r_n|_S) ≅ a simple object of 𝐂 (archimedean: ℙ¹(ℂ) ≅ S²).

    (3) Involution symmetry: the descent involution τ̂_n restricts to an endomorphism of ∂A_n, whose fixed subobjects coincide with the monodromy fixed points of the quantum characteristic classes.

    (4) Chirality interface (archimedean, n = 1): ∂𝓢_1 separates macroscopic spacetime coordinates from internal chiral modes; the Reeb involution ξ ↦ −ξ reverses orientation across it.

    The Lean encoding records the four properties as fields of an abstract BraneBoundaryContext.
