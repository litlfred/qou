    QOU.FluidDynamics.leray_profile — finite-dimensional Leray profile #

    Proposition (prop:leray-profile).

    Near the blowup time T*, the singular NS solution approaches a self-similar Leray profile V(ξ) on the rescaled coordinate ξ = (x − x₀)/√(T* − t). QOU representation theory constrains V to a finite-dimensional ansatz: a sum of weight-j matrix entries of SU_q(2) representations; in particular V(0) = Σ_n V_n.

    class QOU.FluidDynamics.LerayProfileContext (R : Type u_1) [CommRing R] :
    Type u_1

    Hypotheses for the finite-dimensional Leray profile (§7c: generic CommRing, no ℝ). basis n are the weight-n matrix entries of the SU_q(2) ansatz, normalised to 1 at the origin of the rescaled coordinate; profile_expansion is the finite ansatz itself.

    • V : R → R

      The Leray profile V on the rescaled coordinate.

    • j_max : ℕ

      Maximum spin level j_max in the finite-dimensional ansatz.

    • V_coeff : Fin (j_max R + 1) → R

      Ansatz coefficients, fixed by the singular gerbe data (Σ, n*, κ_{n*}).

    • basis : Fin (j_max R + 1) → R → R

      Weight-n matrix-entry basis functions of the ansatz.

    • basis_at_zero (n : Fin (j_max R + 1)) : basis n 0 = 1

      Normalisation: each basis entry equals 1 at the origin.

    • profile_expansion (x : R) : V x = ∑ n : Fin (j_max R + 1), V_coeff n * basis n x

      Finite ansatz (the representation-theoretic input): V is the finite sum of its weight components.
