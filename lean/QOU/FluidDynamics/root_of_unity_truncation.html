      1 < RootOfUnityTruncationContext.q ∧ 2 ≤ RootOfUnityTruncationContext.κ ∧ 2 * RootOfUnityTruncationContext.j_max + 2 ≤ RootOfUnityTruncationContext.κ ∧ RootOfUnityTruncationContext.κ_t ≤ 1 + RootOfUnityTruncationContext.C * (↑RootOfUnityTruncationContext.κ - 1) ∧ (RootOfUnityTruncationContext.nsBlowsUp ↔ RootOfUnityTruncationContext.leviNonDeg)

      Content statement (from manuscript):

      The QOU axiom requires that $q$ is not a root of unity. But the limit $q \to 1$ passes through a dense set of roots of unity, and at each root the representation theory undergoes a dramatic truncation that strengthens regularity. The blowup at $q = 1$ is the accumulation of infinitely many such truncations.
