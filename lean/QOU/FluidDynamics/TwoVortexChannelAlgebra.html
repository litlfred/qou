    QOU.FluidDynamics.TwoVortexChannelAlgebra — the two-vortex interaction channel algebra #

    The algebraic content of the soliton reading of two interacting vortex loops (2026-07-03 audit docs/audits/2026-07-03-parabolic-cylinder-channel-interpolation.md §5): two geodesic vortices interact through the braiding σ of the 2-strand Hecke algebra H_2(q), whose commutant is generated by the symmetric/antisymmetric channel projectors e₊, e₋ (the Wedderburn pair of van-der-waals-hopf-channel). This file works in that commutative subalgebra, modelled abstractly: a commutative ring R carrying elements e₊, e₋ (partition of unity, orthogonal), the braiding σ with the two channel eigenvalues q and −u (where u is a ring-level witness of q⁻¹, q·u = 1 — kept generic per §7c instead of specialising to a field).

    Everything is proved sorry-free from the class fields:

    • idem_plus, idem_minus — idempotency is derived from partition + orthogonality;
    • braiding_sq — the Hopf-link interaction σ² = q²·e₊ + u²·e₋: the two-crossing spectral decomposition (needs no invertibility);
    • hecke_relation — the model satisfies σ² = (q − u)·σ + 1, i.e. the abstract channel data reproduces the Hecke/skein relation of AGENTS.md §7a with u in the q⁻¹ slot;
    • eigenvalue_ratio — the per-crossing suppression of the antisymmetric channel: (−q⁻¹)/q = −(q²)⁻¹, stated division-free as −u²·q = −u, the algebraic decay rate q⁻² (at q₀ > 1) by which a crossing disturbance is damped — the fibre-level content of "the disturbance decays fast enough not to disrupt the soliton";
    • trivial_model — non-vacuity witness.

    No archimedean claim is made here: that q⁻² < 1 requires q > 1 is an ordering statement, deliberately left on the archimedean side of the wall (§7c).

    class QOU.FluidDynamics.TwoVortexChannelAlgebra.TwoVortexChannels {R : Type u_1} [CommRing R] (σ e_plus e_minus q u : R) :
    Prop

    Two-vortex channel data, as a class. σ is the braiding of the two-vortex interaction; e₊/e₋ the symmetric/antisymmetric channel projectors (partition of unity, orthogonal); q and −u the two channel eigenvalues, with u a ring-level inverse witness for q. The intended model is the commutant of σ₁ in H_2(q) with e₊ = (σ + q⁻¹)/(q + q⁻¹), e₋ = (q − σ)/(q + q⁻¹).

    • partition : e_plus + e_minus = 1

      The two channels partition unity: e_plus + e_minus = 1.

    • orthogonal : e_plus * e_minus = 0

      The channels are orthogonal: e_plus·e_minus = 0.

    • inverse : q * u = 1

      u witnesses q⁻¹ at ring level: q·u = 1.

    • eigen : σ = q * e_plus - u * e_minus

      Spectral form of the braiding: σ = q·e_plus − u·e_minus (eigenvalues q, −q⁻¹; AGENTS.md §7a).

      theorem QOU.FluidDynamics.TwoVortexChannelAlgebra.eigenvalue_ratio {R : Type u_1} [CommRing R] (q u : R) (h : q * u = 1) :
      -u ^ 2 * q = -u

      Per-crossing suppression ratio. The ratio of the antisymmetric to the symmetric channel eigenvalue is (−q⁻¹)/q = −(q²)⁻¹: each crossing damps the antisymmetric disturbance by q⁻² relative to the symmetric carrier.

      Stated division-free (§7c, the R[h] pattern) in the file's own idiom, with u the ring-level witness of q⁻¹: the ratio r = −u² is characterised by r · q = −u, which is the cleared form of (−q⁻¹)/q = −(q²)⁻¹. No field is needed — q · u = 1 is the only input, so the statement lives over the same CommRing as the rest of the file rather than importing a Field (and its 0⁻¹ = 0 junk convention) for one lemma. That the ratio is a decay — q⁻² < 1 — is the archimedean statement q > 1, made only past the wall, §7c.

      theorem QOU.FluidDynamics.TwoVortexChannelAlgebra.channel_algebra {R : Type u_1} [CommRing R] (σ e_plus e_minus q u : R) [inst : TwoVortexChannels σ e_plus e_minus q u] :
      e_plus * e_plus = e_plus ∧ e_minus * e_minus = e_minus ∧ σ ^ 2 = q ^ 2 * e_plus + u ^ 2 * e_minus ∧ σ ^ 2 = (q - u) * σ + 1

      The bundled channel-algebra proposition — the Lean face of prop:two-vortex-channel-algebra: from the four channel hypotheses (partition, orthogonality, inverse witness, spectral form), all ring-level conclusions follow — derived idempotency of both channels, the spectral square, and the Hecke relation. (The eigenvalue-ratio identity is the companion lemma eigenvalue_ratio above, kept separate because it needs only the inverse witness, no channel data.)

      theorem QOU.FluidDynamics.TwoVortexChannelAlgebra.trivial_model {R : Type u_1} [CommRing R] (q u : R) (h : q * u = 1) :
      TwoVortexChannels q 1 0 q u

      Non-vacuity. The one-channel degenerate model (e_plus = 1, e_minus = 0, σ = q) inhabits the class for any invertible q, so the axiomatisation is consistent. The faithful two-channel model lives in the commutant of σ₁ in H_2(q).
