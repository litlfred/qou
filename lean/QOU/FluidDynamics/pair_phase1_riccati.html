    QOU.FluidDynamics.pair_phase1_riccati #

    Phase-1 closed-form calculus of the two-regime pair-collision model (the separated / Biot–Savart phase of the anti-parallel vortex pair, rem:townsend-biot-savart-strain), theorem-grade and derivative-free.

    The corpus two-regime ODE model has, in phase 1 (separation d² > V₁): (d²)' = −Γ/π and Ω' = (Γ/(π d²))·Ω. Its exact solution is d²(t) = d₀² − r·t (with r := Γ/π) and Ω(t) = Ω₀·d₀²/d²(t). The probe computations/qns_pair_riccati_probe.py (witness qns-pair-riccati-probe.witness.json) found numerically that d²·Ω is an exact invariant and the Riccati coefficient c′ is exactly uniform (= 1/π at unit parameters) throughout phase 1. This file proves that finding as algebra — no deriv, no limits: every statement is an exact identity or inequality about the closed-form flow, over a generic linearly ordered field (§7c: only field algebra and order are used).

    Main results (all sorry-free):

    • d2_mul_Om_invariant — d²(t)·Ω(t) = d₀²·Ω₀ (the probe's exact invariant).
    • Om_closed_form — Ω(t) = Ω₀ / (1 − c′·Ω₀·t) with c′ = r/(d₀²·Ω₀): the exact solution of the uniform-coefficient Riccati equation Ω' = c′·Ω² — phase 1 IS a uniform Riccati flow.
    • one_div_Om_sub — 1/Ω(t) − 1/Ω(t+s) = c′·s: the derivative-free form of uniformity (the reciprocal decreases at exactly rate c′).
    • Om_mono — Ω is monotone on the pre-collision interval.
    • discrete_riccati_lower — Ω(t+δ) ≥ Ω(t) + c′·δ·Ω(t)²: the pair channel satisfies the hypothesis of the GP5 blow-up lemma riccati_lower_growth (surreal_enstrophy_finite_modes.lean) with the uniform constant c′ = Γ/(π d₀² Ω₀), at every step size, on all of phase 1.
    • Om_hits_every_level — for every M > Ω₀ there is t ∈ (0, d₀²/r) with Ω(t) = M: the phase-1 flow is unbounded on approach to the inviscid collision time t* = d₀²/r = π d₀²/Γ.

    Scope (honest): these are statements about the corpus ODE model's phase-1 closed form — the in-QOU two-regime collision model — not about the Navier–Stokes PDE. The model-to-PDE step (uniformity of the overlap engagement, Γ constancy during approach) is the named open item; the sub-leading strain correction vanishing on approach is the owner-proved kappa_squared_tends_to_zero (fluid-dynamics/townsend-biot-savart-strain.lean). Phase 2 (overlap saturation) is exactly where this calculus stops being valid — the saturation destroys the uniform c′, which is the regularizing cutoff measured by the probe and the analytic race overlap_race_strict.

          Γ-robustness of the overlap race (the lock-uniformity caveat) #

          The campaign ledger names one candidate flaw in the pair-channel regularisation: "Γ growth during collision" — if the circulation strengthens as the pair approaches, could the approach outrun the stretching and reach d = 0 before the overlap cutoff engages?

          No — in the two-regime model the race is Γ(t)-free. Reparametrize time by the consumed circulation τ with dτ = Γ(t)·dt/π (monotone for any positive circulation history). Then phase 1 reads d(d²)/dτ = −1 and dV₁/dτ = V₁/(2d²), so W := V₁² satisfies dW/dτ = W/d² — the same closed form as Ω above: W(τ) = W₀·d₀²/(d₀² − τ) (take Om W₀ d₀² 1 τ with rate r = 1), and W·d² is an exact invariant. Γ has been eliminated: every positive circulation history traverses the same phase-plane curve, only at a different speed. The overlap condition V₁ ≥ d² (W ≥ (d²)²) therefore engages exactly when (d²)³ ≤ W₀·d₀², i.e. at separation d² = (V₁₀·d₀)^{2/3} > 0 — strictly before collision, for any Γ(t) > 0.
