    QOU.FluidDynamics.qou_ns_surreal_bridge — factoring Bridge B2 through the #

    standard-part functor

    Scope discipline (owner directive 2026-07-24). This factors the open categorical→classical bridge (B2, bean qou-innq) through the surreal-analysis infrastructure already in the corpus (surreal-blowup-calculus, standard-part-classical-limits). It does not close the bridge: the analytic content is isolated into one named hypothesis and left open. Everything is given a QOU.

    The idea (from the surreal infra) #

    surreal-blowup-calculus renormalises a divergent expression X(Λ) by evaluating at the infinite surreal ω = 1/ε and taking a standard part X_ren := st(X(ω) − C(ω)) ∈ ℝ. The q → 1 limit of a QOU is q = 1 − ε with ε = 1/ω infinitesimal, so the QOU-internal q-NS observable at q = 1 is naturally surreal-valued (internalObs : S), and its standard part is the would-be classical (archimedean, L²(ℝ³)) value.

    This lets us split the opaque B2 identification into two named pieces:

    • blowup_iff_infinite — categorical / surreal side, in the corpus: the QOU's internal q-NS singularity is exactly the surreal observable being infinite (no finite standard part). This is the surreal-blowup-calculus reading of ns-obstruction-tower.
    • classical_regular_iff_finite — the analytic wall (open): classical Leray–Hopf regularity of the standard-part flow in L²(ℝ³) holds iff the surreal observable is finite. This is where all the analysis lives after factoring through st; it is carried as a hypothesis, never proved here.

    With the split, the transport is a one-line logical consequence (classical_singular_of_internal_blowup): given a QOU whose internal q-NS is singular, and given the surreal bridge, the standard-part classical flow is not regular. The whole Clay-level difficulty is now localised in the single biconditional classical_regular_iff_finite.

    Honest scope (anti-laundering) #

    QOUNSSurrealBridge has no instance. classical_regular_iff_finite is the open analytic hypothesis (surreal-finiteness ⟺ L² Leray–Hopf regularity); it is not a theorem. Per the 2026-06-08 surreal-narrowing ledger, the surreal layer is a language for stating the bridge, not a proof of it.

    Finite-mode half proved (surreal_enstrophy_finite_modes). At every finite truncation N, the surreal enstrophy Ω_N(1−ε) deviates from the classical Ω_N(1) = Σ_{k<N} k² a_k by an infinitesimal (enstrophy_perturbation_infinitesimal, sorry-free) — so the standard part of the finite-mode observable is the classical value, and the open content of classical_regular_iff_finite is localised entirely in the N → ∞ (UV) limit (N_eff ~ log_q Re → ∞ as q → 1⁺): whether the standard part commutes with the mode limit. That commutation question is the wall; it remains open.

    Wall halved (classical_unbounded_implies_surreal_infinite). One direction of classical_regular_iff_finite is now proved under tower domination: classical enstrophy unbounded ⇒ the surreal observable is infinite (contrapositive: surreal-finite ⇒ classically bounded).

    Converse half proved under UV exhaustion (surreal_finite_of_classical_bounded). With the tower UV-exhausting the total (some finite truncation captures it up to a finite constant) and the classical enstrophy bounded, the surreal observable is finite.

    Sub-classical CLOSURE (surreal_finite_of_classical_bounded_subclassical). On the approach q = 1 − ε — which sits inside the corpus golden window for every positive infinitesimal ε (golden_window_mem) — the termwise domination [k]_{1−ε} ≤ k (qInt_le_self) makes the exhaustion hypothesis a theorem: every truncation is dominated by its classical value, so bounded classical enstrophy forces a finite surreal observable with no spectral hypothesis at all. Together with classical_unbounded_implies_surreal_infinite, the biconditional closes on the sub-classical approach, given only the definitional limit pair (truncations below the total; the total a lub) and non-negative amplitudes.

    Super-classical side (q = 1 + ε) — forward direction proved, blow-up grade-capped. The reversed domination [k]_{1+ε} ≥ k (qInt_ge_self) makes the forward direction immediate (superclassical_infinite_of_classical_unbounded: classical unbounded ⇒ surreal infinite, no infinitesimality needed), and the dissipation closure caps the blow-up at ω-grade 1 (enstrophy_grade_one_of_dissipation_closure: ε·Ω finite — the surreal reading of the critical exponent α = 1).

    Honest remaining open content: on the physically-preferred q → 1⁺ direction (the side of thm:q-ns-wellposedness), the gap is now grade 1 vs grade 0: whether the flow saturates the closure's ε⁻¹ cap (blow-up, α = 1) or stays at grade 0 (regular) — the critical-exponent question of p2-critical-exponent. By the grade trichotomy (grade_trichotomy, saturated_implies_infinite) this reduces to a single surreal criterion: is ε · Ω(1+ε) infinitesimal? — infinitesimal with finite Ω ⟺ regular; non-infinitesimal ⟺ saturated blow-up; infinitesimal with infinite Ω ⟺ intermediate 0 < α < 1. Only that criterion is still carried by this hypothesis; it remains open and is not asserted.

    Exploratory in-model evidence (computations/qns_enstrophy_grade_probe.py, witness qns-enstrophy-grade-probe.witness.json): on the cascade-cutoff spectral model, sustained Kolmogorov amplitudes measure α ≈ 1.335 ≈ 4/3 — exceeding the grade-1 cap, so the dissipation closure fails for sustained spectra (the Finding-2 α tension resolves toward the cascade value in-model) — while Gevrey (smooth-data) amplitudes measure α ≈ 0 (grade-0 regular). This is model evidence only; the true flow's regime remains the open content.

    structure QOU.FluidDynamics.QOUNSSurrealBridge (S : Type u_1) :
    Type u_1

    Bridge B2, factored through the standard-part functor st : S → ℝ.

    internalObs : S is the surreal-valued QOU-internal q-NS observable at q = 1 (e.g. the enstrophy / obstruction magnitude); isFinite marks the surreals with a well-defined standard part. The two biconditionals are the two halves of B2; the second is the open analytic wall.

    • internalObs : S

      The surreal-valued internal q-NS observable at q = 1.

    • isFinite : S → Prop

      "x is a finite surreal" — has a standard part st x ∈ ℝ.

    • internalBlowup : Prop

      The QOU's internal q-NS singularity observable (ns-obstruction-tower).

    • classicalRegular : Prop

      Leray–Hopf regularity of the standard-part flow in L²(ℝ³) (the classical object — Object 2).

    • blowup_iff_infinite : self.internalBlowup ↔ ¬self.isFinite self.internalObs

      Surreal side (surreal-blowup-calculus, in-corpus): internal blow-up ⟺ the observable is an infinite surreal (no finite standard part).

    • classical_regular_iff_finite : self.classicalRegular ↔ self.isFinite self.internalObs

      The analytic wall (OPEN): classical L² Leray–Hopf regularity ⟺ the surreal observable is finite. All the analysis is here.
