    QOU.FluidDynamics.surreal_enstrophy_finite_modes — the surreal enstrophy at #

    finite truncation (S1–S3 of the surreal-enstrophy campaign)

    Scope discipline (owner directive 2026-07-24). Given a QOU, working in the surreal-bridge picture (qou_ns_surreal_bridge): the q → 1 limit is q = 1 − ε with ε a positive infinitesimal on a non-archimedean line. This file builds the finite-mode surreal enstrophy concretely and proves that at every finite truncation N its deviation from the classical value is infinitesimal — so the standard part of Ω_N(1−ε) is the classical enstrophy. This closes the finite-mode half of the bridge field blowup_iff_infinite and localises the open wall entirely in the N → ∞ (UV) limit: whether st ∘ lim_N = lim_N ∘ st on the full tower is exactly the classical_regular_iff_finite analytic hypothesis, deliberately not asserted. No claim about the classical ℝ³ PDE is made.

    Contents #

    • S1 — infinitesimal calculus over a generic linearly ordered field K (Infinitesimal, IsFinite; closure under +, ·-by-finite). No archimedean axiom is assumed — K may be a surreal line; for archimedean K (e.g. ℝ) the only infinitesimal is 0 and all statements degenerate correctly.
    • S2 — q-integer perturbation: [k]_{1−ε} − k is infinitesimal for every fixed k (qInt_one_sub_infinitesimal), where [k]_q = Σ_{j<k} q^j (so [k]_1 = k, the q → 1 degeneration of the spectral tower — regularity-breakdown Obstruction 1).
    • S3 — finite-mode enstrophy: with mode amplitudes a k finite, Ω_N(1−ε) − Ω_N(1) is infinitesimal (enstrophy_perturbation_infinitesimal) — the standard part of the surreal enstrophy at truncation N equals the classical enstrophy Σ_{k<N} k² a_k.

    Honest scope (anti-laundering) #

    Everything here is proved sorry-free; nothing open is asserted. The open analytic content (UV limit N → ∞, where N_eff ~ log_q Re → ∞ as q → 1⁺) is named in the module docstring and carried by qou_ns_surreal_bridge's classical_regular_iff_finite hypothesis — it is not discharged here, and the finite-mode results must not be cited as if they were the full bridge.

    Base ring (§7c) #

    Generic [Field K] [LinearOrder K] [IsStrictOrderedRing K] — deliberately NOT ℝ: the intended instance is a non-archimedean (surreal) line, and no archimedean construct is used. ℝ remains an instance (degenerate case).

            G1 — half the UV wall: classical-unbounded ⇒ surreal-infinite #

The wall is thereby halved, not closed.

            V — sub-classical closure: [k]_{1−ε} ≤ k kills the tail question #

            On the sub-classical approach q = 1 − ε (inside the golden window, golden_window_mem) the q-integers are dominated termwise by their classical values, so the surreal enstrophy is dominated by the classical one at every truncation — and the uniform tail bound of surreal_finite_of_uniform_tail becomes a theorem rather than a hypothesis. The bridge biconditional closes on this side with no spectral hypothesis at all (only the definitional limit properties). The super-classical approach q = 1 + ε, where [k]_{1+ε} ≥ k and the tail question is genuine, remains open.
