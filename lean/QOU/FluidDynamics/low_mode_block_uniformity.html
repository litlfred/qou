    QOU.FluidDynamics.low_mode_block_uniformity #

    The ε-uniformity reduction — the millennium remainder of epic qou-a3n3 reduced to one named spectral hypothesis (bean qou-habm; formalize-first: this file states and proves the invariant that any future probe or estimate must target, before any wide search runs).

    low_mode_block_global.lean proved large-data global persistence at the fixed block with the bound Ω ≤ kmax²·E₀, non-uniform in ε because the block's top rung kmax = [m]_{1+ε} diverges as q → 1⁺. This file splits the enstrophy at a fixed band B (independent of ε; K is reserved for the coefficient field) and proves:

    Ω = Ω_{≤B} + Ω_{>B} ≤ kB²·E + Ω_{>B},

    so the ε-uniform large-data bound holds iff the above-band tail enstrophy Ω_{>B} is uniformly bounded — "no enstrophy pile-up at the receding damping threshold." The fixed-band part is proved (banded_enstrophy_le); the tail is the named hypothesis (uniformity_reduction), and the trivial converse (tail_le_total) closes the equivalence. Everything is finite-sum algebra over a generic ordered field (§7c) on a spectral energy distribution e : ℕ → K with ladder k : ℕ → K.

    Structural observation recorded alongside (vanishing viscosity). The q-ladder viscosity is ν_q = (q−1)/(2q) = ε/(2(1+ε)), which vanishes in the classical limit — so the discrete Leray bound of low_mode_block_global degrades at the explicit rate ∑Ω ≤ 2(1+ε)·E₀/(δ·ε) (leray_bound_scaling below), and the in-QOU classical limit is a vanishing-viscosity (Euler-type) limit, not a fixed-ν Navier–Stokes limit. Mapping the in-QOU statement onto the fixed-ν millennium formulation therefore requires the substrate-to-physical rescaling of time/length units — a modeling step that must be chosen explicitly, and is flagged (not chosen) here.

    What this file does not do: prove or refute the tail hypothesis. It is the honest open frontier; the reduction tells the next agent exactly what to measure (the above-fixed-band enstrophy fraction as ε → 0 — a candidate SF5 shell probe) or what estimate to attempt (a flux bound localizing dissipation below a fixed band).

          theorem QOU.FluidDynamics.LowModeUniformity.uniformity_reduction {K : Type u_1} [Field K] [LinearOrder K] [IsStrictOrderedRing K] (k e : ℕ → K) (B m : ℕ) (kB : K) (he : ∀ (n : ℕ), 0 ≤ e n) (hk : ∀ n ≤ B, k n ^ 2 ≤ kB ^ 2) :
          blockEnstrophy k e m ≤ kB ^ 2 * blockEnergy e m + tailEnstrophy k e B m

          The ε-uniformity reduction: block enstrophy is at most kB²·E + tail. Since kB and (via energy monotonicity, low_mode_block_global.energy_le_initial) E ≤ E₀ are uniform in the block width, an ε-uniform enstrophy bound holds iff the above-band tail enstrophy is uniformly bounded. The tail is the named open hypothesis — "no enstrophy pile-up at the receding threshold"; nothing in the corpus proves or refutes it.

          The trivial converse: the tail never exceeds the total (spectral energies nonnegative), so unbounded tail forces unbounded total — the reduction is an equivalence, not just an upper bound.
