    QOU.FluidDynamics.q_ns_wellposedness #

    Content block thm:q-ns-wellposedness: Global regularity of the $q$-deformed Navier--Stokes equation.

    The block's claim: for a divergence-free initial velocity 1-form v₀ ∈ ker(δ_q|_{Ω¹(A)}) with finite Frobenius norm, the q-deformed Navier--Stokes system admits a unique global solution (v, p) : [0, ∞) → ker(δ_q|_{Ω¹(A)}) × A such that

    1. smoothness: t ↦ v(t) is smooth in the internal hom of 𝐂;
    2. energy inequality: ‖v(t)‖_A² + 2ν_q ∫₀ᵗ ‖d_q v(s)‖_A² ds ≤ ‖v₀‖_A² for all t ≥ 0;
    3. spectral decay: the Δ_q-eigencomponents satisfy |v̂_k(t)| ≤ |v̂_k(0)| · exp(−ν_q [k]_q² t).

    Disposition (integrity) — what is assumed, and what is proved #

    Previously the class carried a single field wellposedness_holds whose type was verbatim the conclusion below, and the theorem was ctx.wellposedness_holds: a self-assuming projection (AGENTS.md §3b-cond item 5). The file's own docstring asserted the opposite — that it was sorry'd and that nothing in the class assumed any part of the conclusion — which was false on all three counts. Recorded in docs/audits/2026-08-29-q-ns-conjecture-statement-strength.md; the declaration is in lean-vacuity-review-ledger.json under proof-no-self-assuming-projection, verdict genuine.

    Now the four claims are separated by what each actually needs, and only the genuinely open ones are hypotheses:

    block property here
    existence + uniqueness existence_uniqueness — assumed (Millennium-adjacent; bean qou-t28w, tier B)
    (i) smoothness smooth_of_isSolution — assumed (regularity; the same open problem)
    (ii) energy inequality q_ns_energy_inequality — proved here
    (iii) spectral decay q_ns_spectral_decay — proved here

    (ii) and (iii) are discharged by q_ns_energy_and_decay, from the equations — the pointwise energy balance E' + 2ν_q D ≤ 0 and the modal inequality d/dt|v̂_k| ≤ −ν_q [k]_q² |v̂_k| — rather than from any assumption about the integrated bounds themselves. No field of the class is, or implies, either conclusion.

    What remains assumed is exactly the open problem, no more: that a solution exists, is unique, and is smooth.

    Type 1

    Carrier data and hypotheses for [thm:q-ns-wellposedness].

    The fields split into three kinds, and the split is the point:

    • data — carriers, the initial datum, the norm / dissipation / eigencomponent functionals, the rate functionals, and the opaque Smooth / IsSolution predicates (which fix what the words mean, and assert nothing);
    • the equations — energy_balance and modal_inequality, the pointwise differential statements the q-NS system makes, plus the analytic regularity needed to integrate them;
    • the open inputs — existence_uniqueness and smooth_of_isSolution, and nothing else.

    Neither the energy inequality nor the spectral-decay bound appears as a field; both are theorems below.
