    QOU.FluidDynamics.low_mode_block_global #

    Large-data global persistence at the fixed low-mode block — and the precise localization of the millennium difficulty (epic qou-a3n3 remainder; opening brick).

    The GP arc (surreal_enstrophy_finite_modes.lean) left one gap: data with c·Ω₀ > νμ (large data) past the bootstrap horizon. This file proves that for the fixed finite block the gap closes, with no smallness hypothesis, from two structural facts of a Galerkin low-mode block:

    1. Energy monotonicity (E (n+1) ≤ E n): the quadratic transfer term of a truncated Leray system is energy-conserving (it redistributes, never creates), so unforced dissipative dynamics can only lower the block energy.
    2. Spectral norm equivalence (Ω n ≤ kmax²·E n): on a finite block the enstrophy is controlled by the energy through the top wavenumber — all norms are equivalent in finite dimension.

    Consequences, all sorry-free and valid for arbitrarily large data:

    • energy_le_initial — E n ≤ E 0 for all n.
    • block_global_enstrophy_bound — Ω n ≤ kmax²·E 0 for all n: the block's enstrophy is globally bounded forever. This is large-data global persistence at the fixed block.
    • cumulative_enstrophy_bound — the discrete Leray energy inequality: with quantitative dissipation E (n+1) ≤ E n − δν·Ω n, the cumulative enstrophy satisfies δν·∑_{i<n} Ω i ≤ E 0 — enstrophy is not just bounded but summable-in-time against the dissipation rate.

    Where the millennium difficulty actually lives (honest localization). Every constant above is uniform in the data but NOT in the block size: on the q-ladder the top wavenumber of the active block is kmax = [m]_{1+ε} and the damping threshold pushes m ~ ε⁻¹, so the global bound kmax²·E 0 diverges as q → 1⁺. The open millennium content is therefore not "does the finite block persist for large data" (it does — this file), but "are the bounds uniform as the block widens into the classical limit" — equivalently, whether the energy flux through the receding damping threshold keeps the low-mode enstrophy bounded independently of ε. That uniformity question is the honest remainder of epic qou-a3n3; nothing in this file (or the corpus) resolves it.

    Hypothesis-carrying discrete style as in the GP bricks (§7c: generic linearly ordered field; sequences with step inequalities as explicit hypotheses — the derivation of those inequalities from a concrete ODE discretization is the consumer's obligation, not smuggled in here).

    δν * ∑ i ∈ Finset.range n, Ω i ≤ E 0

    The discrete Leray energy inequality: with quantitative dissipation E (n+1) ≤ E n − δν·Ω n and nonnegative energy, the cumulative enstrophy is bounded by the initial energy: δν·∑_{i<n} Ω i ≤ E 0 for every n. Enstrophy is summable-in-time against the dissipation rate — the discrete counterpart of the classical Leray a-priori bound, again with no smallness hypothesis.
