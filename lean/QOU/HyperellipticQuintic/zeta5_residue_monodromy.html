    QOU.HyperellipticQuintic.zeta5_residue_monodromy #

    The exact $\zeta_5$-residue Picard-Lefschetz monodromy of the chiral $3p_z$ lone-pair over the embedded hyperelliptic quintic $y^2 = x^5 - 1$.

    This is the formal specification of conj:h2s-angle-s3-braid-holonomy, which evaluates the analytic Picard-Lefschetz monodromy of the $\zeta_5$ residue to derive the $1/\sqrt{5}$ Bring scaling factor and the $+2.1^\circ$ phase angle.
