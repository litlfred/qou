    QOU.MobiusEnergy — Categorical Möbius Energy and Self-Avoidance #

    Formalization of the categorical Möbius energy functional and the non-self-intersection corollary from Chapter 4, §9 of the QOU manuscript.

    Structure #

    1. Categorical Möbius energy — the functional E_cat(γ) built from the Frobenius–Hopf comultiplication and q-Hodge star.
    2. Classical limit — in the limit q → 1, E_cat recovers the classical Möbius energy up to topological normalisation.
    3. Non-self-intersection — the non-cocommutativity of the Hopf structure at q ≠ 1 provides an energy barrier preventing self-intersection.

    References #

    • [freedman1994] M. Freedman, Z.-X. He, Z. Wang, "Möbius energy of knots and unknots", Annals of Mathematics 139 (1994), 1–50.
    • [ohara1991] J. O'Hara, "Energy of a knot", Topology 30 (1991), 241–247.

        The O'Hara / Möbius integrand at parameters (s, t):

          The MUTUAL term — what a per-pair EM weight actually needs #

          oharaIntegrand above is the self-energy form: one curve, and the subtraction is what tames the diagonal singularity as t → s. Bean qou-o6ml route (2) does not want that. It wants the mutual energy between two distinct components of a link, and that is a different and simpler object.

          source
          noncomputable def QOU.mutualIntegrand (sep : ℝ → ℝ → ℝ) (s t : ℝ) :
          ℝ

          The mutual (cross) integrand between points on two distinct components:

          $$\frac{1}{|\gamma_i(s) - \gamma_j(t)|^2}.$$

          No subtraction. The subtracted term in oharaIntegrand is 1/D(s,t)² with D the arc-length distance along the curve; for points on different components there is no path along the curve at all, so D = ∞ and the term vanishes identically. The mutual energy is the unsubtracted integral, and the regularisation question does not arise because distinct components are disjoint — there is no diagonal to be singular on.

          Modelled here as a bare separation function rather than through KnotEmbedding, because that structure carries a single curve : ℝ → M and cannot express a multi-component link at all. That is the real gap on route (2), and naming it is the point of this block.

            A multi-component carrier #

            mutualIntegrand above is stated over a bare separation function because KnotEmbedding carries a single curve : ℝ → M and cannot express a link. This is that structure. With it the mutual term is stated through the encoding, and — the point of the exercise — its positivity needs no side hypothesis at all, because disjointness is part of what it means to be a link.

            structure QOU.LinkEmbedding (M : Type u_1) (n : ℕ) :
            Type u_1

            An n-component link embedding: n parametrised components in M, together with the ambient separation between a point on any component and a point on any other.

            The separation is carried at link level rather than assembled from the components, because KnotEmbedding.ambientDist relates two points on the same curve and there is nothing in that structure relating two curves.

            • component : Fin n → KnotEmbedding M

              The components.

            • sep : Fin n → ℝ → Fin n → ℝ → ℝ

              Ambient distance from component i at parameter s to component j at parameter t.

            • sep_symm (i : Fin n) (s : ℝ) (j : Fin n) (t : ℝ) : self.sep i s j t = self.sep j t i s

              Separation is symmetric under exchanging the two (component, parameter) pairs.

            • sep_nonneg (i : Fin n) (s : ℝ) (j : Fin n) (t : ℝ) : 0 ≤ self.sep i s j t

              Separation is non-negative.

            • disjoint (i j : Fin n) : i ≠ j → ∀ (s t : ℝ), 0 < self.sep i s j t

              Distinct components never meet. This is what makes the configuration a link rather than a singular map, and it is exactly the hypothesis that makes the mutual integrand unconditionally positive below.

            • sep_self (i : Fin n) (s t : ℝ) : self.sep i s i t = (self.component i).ambientDist s t

              Restricted to one component, the link-level separation is that component's own ambient distance — so the two notions agree where they overlap rather than sitting side by side.

                  structure QOU.CategoricalMobiusEnergy (M : Type u_1) :
                  Type u_1

                  Categorical Möbius energy data for a knot γ in a quantum observable universe (C, A, ω).

                  The energy functional is $$E_{\mathrm{cat}}(\gamma) = \lambda \circ \mathrm{tr}_A \iint_{S^1 \times S^1 \setminus \mathrm{diag}} (\Delta(\gamma(s)) \otimes \Delta(\gamma(t))) \star_q \|d\gamma/ds \wedge_q d\gamma/dt\|\,ds\,dt$$

                  where Δ is the Hopf comultiplication, λ is the Frobenius form, and ⋆_q is the q-Hodge star.

                  LaTeX: \label{prop:mobius-energy}

                    The substrate parameter governing the q-deformation.

                  • basepoint : M

                    A chosen base point for evaluating the substrate field.

                  • q : ℝ

                    The q-parameter value at the base point.

                  • q_gt_one : self.q > 1

                    q > 1 (non-classical regime).

                  • knot : KnotEmbedding M

                    The knot embedding in the fibre space.

                  • energy : ℝ

                    The categorical energy value E_cat(γ).

                  • classicalEnergy : ClassicalMobiusEnergy

                    The classical Möbius energy of the same knot.

                  • energyUnderMobius : ℝ → ℝ

                    The categorical energy of the knot after applying the Möbius transform with parameter g ∈ PSL(2, ℂ) (parametrised here by a real coordinate on the group, acting on the ℂP¹ fibre via the gauge group fixed by the categorical volume constraint).

                  • mobiusInvariant (g : ℝ) : self.energyUnderMobius g = self.energy

                    Möbius invariance: E_cat(γ) is invariant under the full Möbius group PSL(2, ℂ). Encoded as: the transformed energy equals the base energy for every group element g — the energy functional is constant along every Möbius orbit (categorical: invariance under Aut⊗(ω)).

                  • classicalLimitConst : ℝ

                    Classical limit: as q → 1, the categorical energy recovers the classical Möbius energy up to topological normalisation. Encoded as: E_cat = (1/4) · E_classical + const.

                  • classicalLimit : self.energy = 1 / 4 * self.classicalEnergy.value + self.classicalLimitConst

                        §3 Möbius Energy Recovery (Proposition) #

                        source
                        theorem QOU.MobiusEnergy {M : Type u_1} (cme : CategoricalMobiusEnergy M) (g : ℝ) :
                        cme.energyUnderMobius g = 1 / 4 * cme.classicalEnergy.value + cme.classicalLimitConst

                        Proposition: Categorical recovery of Möbius energy.

                        In the classical limit q → 1, the categorical Möbius energy recovers the classical Möbius energy up to a topological normalisation: $$\lim_{q \to 1} E_{\mathrm{cat}}(\gamma) = \frac{1}{4} E(\gamma) + \mathrm{const}.$$

                        The proof proceeds in three steps:

                        1. Gauge fixing — the categorical volume constraint fixes G = SU(2), ensuring E_cat is Möbius-invariant.
                        2. Frobenius–Hopf kernel expansion — at q = 1, the comultiplication becomes cocommutative and the Frobenius pairing reduces to the Killing form, yielding the 1/r² repulsive kernel.
                        3. Self-energy subtraction — the diagonal regularisation matches the arc-length subtraction via the antipode S.

                        Stated along the whole Möbius orbit rather than at the base energy alone. In that form it is derived from two fields — invariance carries the transformed energy back to E_cat, and the classical limit then applies — rather than returned verbatim.

                        The base case is cme.classicalLimit itself, which is what this declaration used to restate: theorem MobiusEnergy … := cme.classicalLimit, the structure field under a second name. Use the field directly when the orbit plays no part.

                        LaTeX: \label{prop:mobius-energy}

                          structure QOU.NonSelfIntersection (M : Type u_1) :
                          Type u_1

                          Corollary: Obstruction to self-intersection.

                          For q ≠ 1, the non-cocommutativity of the Hopf structure provides a categorical obstruction to knot self-intersection: the energy diverges whenever two non-adjacent strands approach within distance ℏ_q.

                          The mechanism:

                          • The universal R-matrix R ∈ U_q(su(2)) ⊗̂ U_q(su(2)) controls the failure of cocommutativity: σ ∘ Δ ≠ Δ.
                          • The q-deformed commutator [X̂, P̂]_q = iℏ_q replaces the 1/r² kernel with [2]_q / (r² − ℏ_q²).
                          • This kernel has a hard singularity at r = ℏ_q, creating an energy barrier of infinite height — the "quantum exclusion zone".

                          Physically, the vortex filament γ cannot pass through itself because doing so would require infinite categorical energy. This is the knot-theoretic analogue of the Pauli exclusion principle.

                          LaTeX: \label{cor:non-self-intersection}

                          • cme : CategoricalMobiusEnergy M

                            The categorical Möbius energy data.

                          • separation : ℝ → ℝ → ℝ

                            Ambient separation between the strand points at parameters s and t.

                            KnotEmbedding carries only arcLengthDist — an intrinsic distance along the curve — so the ambient separation that the 1/r² kernel is a function of does not exist anywhere upstream. It is carried here because the barrier cannot be stated without it.

                          • energyBarrier (s t : ℝ) : self.separation s t < hbarQ self.cme.q → ∀ (E₀ : ℝ), self.cme.energy > E₀

                            Energy barrier. If some pair of strand points came within the exclusion radius ℏ_q, the categorical energy would exceed every bound.                               §5 The classical-limit field constrains nothing — measured 2026-09-14 #

                              CategoricalMobiusEnergy.classicalLimit reads energy = (1/4) * classicalEnergy.value + classicalLimitConst, which looks like the bridge from the categorical energy to the classical one. It is not a constraint on that pair at all, because classicalLimitConst is a free field of the same structure, appearing once and linearly. Given any energy and any classicalEnergy whatever, exactly one constant satisfies the equation — so the field defines classicalLimitConst rather than relating the two energies.

                              The mathematical statement the docstring quotes, lim_{q→1} E_cat(γ) = ¼ E(γ) + const, means a universal constant: the same for every knot. Encoded per instance, that content is gone.

                              This is the same class as the incident the ClassicalMobiusEnergy.unknot_iff docstring records — "no sorry, no axiom and no tautological conclusion, so no vacuity detector saw it" — one field over, and it survived that repair. It is recorded, not repaired: making the constant universal changes what the structure asserts, which is an authoring decision for the owner, not a fix an agent should make unasked. Bean qou-pzsi.

                              Is a UNIVERSAL constant available? No — measured, not assumed. #

                              classicalLimit_unconstraining above shows the field constrains nothing per instance. The natural repair is to ask for one k shared by every instance — that would be a real, falsifiable claim about the normalisation rather than a per-instance bookkeeping slot.

                              It is not available as the structure is written, and this is a theorem rather than a doubt. shiftEnergy takes any instance to another with the same classicalEnergy and the constant moved by an arbitrary d: shift energy and energyUnderMobius together, and both mobiusInvariant and classicalLimit survive by ring. Nothing else in the structure — sp, basepoint, q, q_gt_one, knot — relates energy to classicalEnergy, so nothing obstructs the shift.

                              Consequence: given even ONE instance, classicalLimitConst is surjective onto ℝ at fixed classical energy, so ∃ k, ∀ c, c.classicalLimitConst = k is false. Making the constant universal is therefore not a quantifier change — it requires adding a constraint to the structure that ties energy to classicalEnergy, which is new mathematics and is exactly what the framework does not currently have.
