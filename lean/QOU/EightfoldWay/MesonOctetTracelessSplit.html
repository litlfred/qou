    The meson octet–singlet split is the traceless/scalar decomposition of End(V) #

    prop:meson-octet-decomposition states 𝟑 ⊗ 𝟑̄ = 𝟖 ⊕ 𝟏 — a quark–antiquark pair decomposes into the flavour octet plus the singlet.

    What is actually being formalised (and what is not) #

    The temptation here is to "formalise" the proposition as 3 * 3 = 8 + 1. That is arithmetic wearing a theorem's clothes: it says nothing falsifiable about V ⊗ V*, and it would satisfy a coverage checker while proving nothing. Per the strict Lean discipline, it is not a theorem.

    The genuine content is that the identification V ⊗ V* ≅ End(V) carries a canonical splitting

    End(V)  =  {traceless}  ⊕  {scalars}

    with the traceless part of dimension n² − 1 (the octet at n = 3) and the scalar part of dimension 1 (the singlet). That splitting is what 𝟖 ⊕ 𝟏 names, and it is a real statement: it asserts existence and uniqueness of the decomposition, both of which can fail — over a field of characteristic dividing n the scalar matrices are themselves traceless, the two summands intersect, and the splitting does not exist. The hypothesis (n : K) ≠ 0 below is exactly that obstruction, and it is why this is not a triviality.

    Proved here, for a general n (the octet is n = 3):

    • traceless_add_scalar — every matrix splits as traceless + scalar;
    • trace_tracelessPart — the first summand really is traceless;
    • scalar_coeff_unique — the scalar coefficient is forced, so the splitting is unique;
    • traceless_scalar_disjoint — the two summands meet only in 0, i.e. the sum is direct;
    • meson_split_dims — the dimension bookkeeping n² = (n² − 1) + 1 stated as a consequence, not as the content.

    Honest scope #

    This is the decomposition as vector spaces. The SU(3)-equivariance of the split — that the traceless part is the adjoint representation and the scalar part the trivial one, rather than merely a complement of the right dimension — is not formalised here. Formalising that needs the representation theory of SU(3), which Mathlib does not currently carry in usable form. So this discharges the linear-algebraic core of the proposition and leaves the equivariance as narrative.

        The dimension bookkeeping, as a consequence #

        theorem QOU.EightfoldWay.MesonOctetTracelessSplit.meson_split_dims {n : ℕ} (hn : 1 ≤ n) :
        n ^ 2 = n ^ 2 - 1 + 1

        n² = (n² − 1) + 1 — the octet/singlet dimension count at general n (n = 3 gives 9 = 8 + 1). Stated after the splitting and derived from 1 ≤ n², to make explicit that the arithmetic is a consequence of the decomposition and not a substitute for it.
