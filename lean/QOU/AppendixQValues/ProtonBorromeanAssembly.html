    QOU.AppendixQValues.ProtonBorromeanAssembly #

    §3b-cond conjecture class for the proton's Borromean mass assembly, and the proton-mass substrate identity derived from it.

    The proposition prop:proton-mass-substrate-identity states m_p = 2 · m_μ · F_Bor(q₀) − 12 · m_e (0.24 ppm vs CODATA, 50-dps MPFR verified). The numerics are compute-verified; the OPEN categorical content (block "Open work §1") is why the structural integers enter linearly:

    • the constituent multiplicity is dim V_(2,1) = 2 (the trace-dominant Hecke irrep dimension), and
    • the per-constituent electron-binding count is |S₃| = 6 (the Ocneanu −1/12 leading-pole inverse; 12 = 2 · 6),

    assembled as m_p = multiplicity · (m_μ · F_Bor − bindingCount · m_e).

    We axiomatise these as a §3b-cond class (the conjectural inputs) and DERIVE the published flat identity 2·m_μ·F_Bor − 12·m_e from them — the concrete 12 emerging as multiplicity · bindingCount = 2 · 6. This is the honest formalisation of the stated gap: the class carries the assembly hypotheses, the theorem composes them into the observed linear factor. Generic [CommRing R] (§7c, no archimedean structure); sorry-free modulo the class instance.

    class QOU.AppendixQValues.ProtonBorromeanAssembly (R : Type u_1) [CommRing R] :
    Type u_1

    Conjecture: the proton's Borromean mass assembly (conj:proton-borromean-assembly).

    The fields are the conjectural categorical hypotheses (the two structural-integer identifications and the assembly law in factored form); the substrate identity is derived from them (proton_mass_substrate_identity), so this is a genuine §3b-cond conditional input, not a self-assuming projection of the conclusion.

    • mMu : R

      muon mass (CAL-2 derived), electron mass (CAL-1), Borromean observable value F_Bor(q₀), and the proton mass.

    • mE : R
    • fBor : R
    • protonMass : R
    • multiplicity : R

      Conjectural input 1 — the constituent multiplicity is dim V_(2,1) = 2 (the trace-dominant Hecke irrep dimension).

    • multiplicity_eq_two : multiplicity = 2
    • bindingCount : R

      Conjectural input 2 — the per-constituent electron-binding count is |S₃| = 6 (the Ocneanu −1/12 leading-pole inverse).

    • bindingCount_eq_six : bindingCount = 6
    • assembly : protonMass = multiplicity * (mMu * fBor - bindingCount * mE)

      Conjectural assembly law — the proton mass is the multiplicity times the per-constituent (muon-dressed minus electron-bound) mass.
