    QOU.KnotRegistry — Formal Knot Registry with Alexander-Briggs Indexing #

    A structured registry of all knots relevant to the Quantum Observable Universe and Bring's Surface. Each entry carries:

    1. Alexander-Briggs index (primary key, e.g. "4_1")
    2. Braid word (from QOU.KnotTheory, Markov closure to recover the link)
    3. Hyperbolic volume with SnapPy-grade precision
    4. QOU physical identity (the particle the knot encodes)
    5. Knot Atlas URL for machine-readable external linkage

    Data Sources #

    • Braid words: The Knot Atlas / KnotInfo (converted from PD / Gauss code)
    • Hyperbolic volumes: SnapPy census (Culler et al.)
    • QOU identities: Manuscript Conjecture 4.25 and §5

    Convention #

    Every def or theorem involving a registered knot includes a docstring with a persistent link to http://katlas.org/wiki/<knot>.

        A fully-specified knot registry entry.

        Each entry is the "settled type" for one knot in the QOU formalization: it pins the Alexander-Briggs name, braid word, computed volume, physical identity, and external reference URL.

          Hyperbolic volume of the knot complement (SnapPy).

        • identity : QOUIdentity

          The QOU particle this knot represents.

        • atlas_url : String

          Persistent URL to The Knot Atlas wiki page.

                noncomputable def QOU.KnotRegistry.photonEntry :
                KnotEntry

                Empty Knot (photon background) — the identity braid $\mathbf{1}$ with zero crossings. QOU identity: Photon. Represents the background flux on the exceptional divisor. The closure of the identity braid is a disjoint union of unknots; the entry is distinguished from the single-component unknot ($0_1$, the neutrino) by carrying no strand at all in its bare presentation. Both have Jones polynomial $1$, so transfer-matrix arithmetic coincides, but the physical roles differ: photon (empty knot, $\varnothing$) vs. neutrino (unknot, $0_1$). This entry records only the bare photon; the genuine photon is the four-term Hecke element PhotonHeckeElement defined below.

                Knot Atlas (closest entry, single-strand limit): http://katlas.org/wiki/0_1

                  The Photon as a Four-Term Hecke Element #

                  The registry's photonEntry records the photon's bare limit — the identity braid 𝟙 — because KnotEntry carries a single BraidWord, not a Hecke-algebra superposition. The genuine photon is a four-term element of H₃(q), γ = 𝟙 + φ₀·σ₀ + φ₁·σ₁ + φ₀₁·σ₀σ₁, over the basis words {𝟙, σ₀, σ₁, σ₀σ₁} with real colour weights. It is therefore not the unknot 0₁ (which is the neutrino): its support has four words, and its two-photon products generate the full operator basis (see the gamma-gamma-generates-particles proposition).

                          Mathematical value (exact, transcendental): 8 · G where G = Catalan's constant (OEIS A006752). The volume.value field below is the 50-dps decimal approximation to that exact value — the EXACT symbol lives in QOU.Archimedean.vol_B3 (defined as 8 · catalan_G).

                          Knot Atlas: http://katlas.org/wiki/L6a4 Hyperbolic volume (catalog literal): 50 dps, 7.32772475341775212043682811945907288619319499425336 ± 1×10⁻⁵⁰. Bumped 2026-06-07 from the previous 15-dps literal 7.32772475341775 ± 1×10⁻¹⁴ to match the project's standard working serialization precision (50 dps per computations/_precision.py). Source: mpmath mp.catalan at mp.dps = 50, evaluated as 8 * mp.catalan — a closed-form-derived decimal (computed from the exact identity Vol(L6a4) = 8·G), NOT a SnapPy numerical measurement. The numerical value agrees with SnapPy's M = SnapPy.Manifold("L6a4"); M.volume() to 15+ digits (and with the proof of Adams (1985) that the two are equal), but the literal stored here is computed from the closed form so its precision is bounded by mp.dps, not by SnapPy's f64 convergence floor. (Copilot review #1978: previous docstring conflated the two provenances.) Components: 3

                          Note: this value field is a data-catalog decimal kept for downstream consumers that want a literal rather than the symbolic form. The categorical QOU.Archimedean.vol_B3 is the EXACT transcendental (8 · catalan_G) — use it in proofs and as the canonical Borromean volume reference.
