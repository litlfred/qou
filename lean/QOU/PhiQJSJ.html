    QOU.PhiQJSJ — Φ_q tower equals ρ_q · Σ_S Vol(K_S) (archimedean bridge) #

    The Φ_q–JSJ archimedean bridge: under the archimedean realisation, each summand Φ_q(V_S) of the dilogarithm form of the mass tower equals ρ_q · Vol_hyp(K_S), where K_S is the Φ_q-piece of the braid. Summed over S, this identifies the dilog form of the tower with the JSJ piecewise sum Σ_P ρ_F(P) · Vol_hyp(P).

    Formal-chain version. This file lives under lean/QOU/ and is compiled by lake build. The co-located documentation stub at folio/quantum-observable-universe/braids-and-knots/phi-q-tower-jsj-bridge.lean mirrors this content (per QOU.GroundRingAnnotations).

    LaTeX label: conj:phi-q-tower-jsj-bridge

    The Lean statement is conditional on Kashaev.VolumeConjecture q, the typeclass axiomatising KVC. Modulo that typeclass instance the bridge theorem is fully verified by Lean. This is Workplan B from AGENTS.md §3b ("Lean formalization of conjectures").

      class QOU.PhiQJSJ.TowerSummandRealization (q : ℝ) :
      Type

      Archimedean realisation of the dilogarithm form of the per-S summand of the tower. This is the target of the bridge: it is the real number that Φ_q(V_S) (resp. z² · Φ_q(V_i V_j)) realises to under τ_arch = π_∞ ∘ τ.

      Axiomatised here so the bridge theorem can be stated without importing the full archimedean-realisation infrastructure; populated by any concrete instance of the realisation functor.

      • realisation (β : PhiQPiece.Braid) : Finset (Fin (β.n - 1)) → ℝ

        Archimedean realisation of the per-S dilog summand of the tower (with the appropriate Markov weight z^|S| already absorbed).

      • realisation_nonneg (β : PhiQPiece.Braid) (S : Finset (Fin (β.n - 1))) : 0 ≤ realisation q β S

        The realisation is non-negative (real volumes are non-negative).

          Master conditional bridge theorem.

          For every braid β, the archimedean realisation of the per-S dilog summand equals ρ_q · Vol_hyp(K_S). This is the statement of conj:phi-q-tower-jsj-bridge, fully verified by Lean modulo the typeclass instance [PerPieceBridge q], which in turn depends on [VolumeConjecture q] (Kashaev) and [TowerSummandRealization q] (realisation infrastructure).

          Non-negativity of the bridge value.

          Both sides of the bridge equation are non-negative: the archimedean realisation is non-negative by hypothesis, and the right-hand side is the product of ρ q > 0 (when 1 < q) and the non-negative piece volume.
