            theorem QOU.OneDim.GammaGammaForcesTheLayout.bonded_iff_base_succ {a b : ℕ} (hd : Disjoint (triple a) (triple b)) :
            Bonded (triple a) (triple b) ↔ b = a + 3 ∨ a = b + 3

            Two disjoint triples are bonded iff their bases differ by exactly three.

            A block's only possible neighbours are the triples immediately to its left and right — which is why the coordination graph is a path.

            theorem QOU.OneDim.GammaGammaForcesTheLayout.no_triangle_of_triples {a b c : ℕ} (hab : Disjoint (triple a) (triple b)) (hbc : Disjoint (triple b) (triple c)) (hac : Disjoint (triple a) (triple c)) (bab : Bonded (triple a) (triple b)) (bbc : Bonded (triple b) (triple c)) (bac : Bonded (triple a) (triple c)) :
            False

            No triangle. Three pairwise-disjoint triples cannot be pairwise bonded: bonding forces each pair of bases to differ by exactly 3, and three distinct naturals cannot pairwise differ by 3.

            This is the Finset ℕ analogue of NoThreeCellOnTheChain.no_triangle, reached from the γγ vertex instead of from a LinearLayout hypothesis. The two are independent routes to the same conclusion; neither is derived from the other (see the 🛑 note at the top).

            theorem QOU.OneDim.GammaGammaForcesTheLayout.degree_le_two {a b c d : ℕ} (hb : Disjoint (triple a) (triple b)) (hc : Disjoint (triple a) (triple c)) (hd : Disjoint (triple a) (triple d)) (bb : Bonded (triple a) (triple b)) (bc : Bonded (triple a) (triple c)) (bd : Bonded (triple a) (triple d)) (hbc : b ≠ c) (hbd : b ≠ d) (hcd : c ≠ d) :
            False

            A block's neighbours among disjoint triples number at most two — the bases a - 3 and a + 3. Stated as: three pairwise-distinct triples all bonded to triple a and pairwise disjoint from it cannot exist.

            The exclusion has content: the interleaved layout IS a triangle #

            Without this section no_triangle_of_triples would be a theorem about an option space that might be empty. It is not: the interleaved layout's blocks are pairwise bonded — its coordination graph is a triangle — and the braid group permits it. What excludes it is precisely that no block of it admits an internal γγ vertex.
