    QOU.ObservableSubcategory #

    Canonical declaration for content block at quantum-observable-universe/information-theory/observable-subcategory.ts.

    Formalises the manuscript definition of the observable subcategory Bun°(ℙ(V)) = { 𝒮 ∈ Bun(ℙ(V)) : Γ(𝒮, A₀) ≠ 0 } — the full subcategory of objects whose degree-0 algebra is non-trivial (the regime where the Frobenius pairing is non-degenerate and degree-0 measurements are defined).

    structure QOU.ObservableSubcategory :
    Type 1

    Data for the observable subcategory Bun°(ℙ(V)).

    A definition carrier: Obj are the objects of the ambient bundle category, A0section 𝒮 is the degree-0 section Γ(𝒮, A₀) (its non-vanishing is the observability criterion), and isObservable is the membership predicate, pinned to A0section 𝒮 ≠ 0.

    • Obj : Type

      Objects of the ambient bundle category Bun(ℙ(V)).

    • A0section : self.Obj → ℝ

      The degree-0 section Γ(𝒮, A₀) (scalar proxy; observability turns on its non-vanishing).

    • isObservable : self.Obj → Prop

      Membership in the observable subcategory Bun°.

    • observable_iff (S : self.Obj) : self.isObservable S ↔ self.A0section S ≠ 0

      Defining identity: 𝒮 ∈ Bun° iff its degree-0 section is non-zero.

      def QOU.ObservableSubcategory.ofSection {C : Type} (A0 : C → ℝ) :
      ObservableSubcategory

      De-axiomatisation (bean tps9). The observability predicate is not a free axiom: every degree-0 section A₀ : C → ℝ canonically induces the observable subcategory, with membership defined as its non-vanishing. The carrier's observable_iff is then Iff.rfl, so Bun° is genuinely "the non-vanishing locus of Γ(−, A₀)", not an assumed criterion.
