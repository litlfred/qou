    QOU.substrate_radial_coordinate — Reeb rotation 𝓛_ξ Z = J·ℏ_q·Z #

    Proposition (prop:substrate-radial-coordinate).

    Within the contact affine chart U_ξ, the substrate parameter q governs the radial and phase coordinates of the state. Let Z be the affine coordinate of a state. Then Z → 0 as q → ∞ (deep-quantum origin) and Z → ∞ as q → 1 (classical boundary). The q-deformed Reeb flow rotates the phase of Z:

      𝓛_ξ Z = J · ℏ_q · Z

    where ℏ_q = 1 − q⁻¹ is the quantum Planck constant and J is the complex structure on the affine chart.

    The Lean encoding records the eigenvalue-equation form of the Reeb-flow generator against an abstract SubstrateRadialContext.
