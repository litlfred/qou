    QOU.Intent #

    Canonical declaration for content block at quantum-observable-universe/quantum-observable-universes/observation.ts.

    Formalises the manuscript observation structure: an observation morphism 𝔒 : A → A acting fibrewise on the state bundle, and the two-observer parallelogram — two independent observations produce a discrepancy (their commutator) controlled by the local Planck constant ℏ_q, vanishing exactly when the observations commute. Generic ring (§7c-clean).
