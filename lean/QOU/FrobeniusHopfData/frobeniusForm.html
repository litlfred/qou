    The manuscript claim — that the Frobenius pairing ∫_A ∘ μ is non-degenerate (induces an iso A ≅ A^∨) iff the snake identities hold — is irreducibly abstract over the opaque categorical objects A, A^∨ and the opaque iso predicate is_iso. No faithful Mathlib structure reduces it (it would require the full rigid-monoidal snake- identity apparatus on the specific quantum universe), so this is pattern C of the vacuity-fix playbook: the context class carries the pairing data and FrobeniusForm names the conditional conjecture as a Prop (honestly not asserted — no theorem … := ctx.claim_* self-assuming projection; an instance discharging it is the open claim).

      Non-degeneracy of the Frobenius pairing, at the level this model can state it.

      Let (𝐂, A, τ) be a quantum universe with multiplication μ : A ⊗ A → A and Frobenius form ∫_A : A → 𝟏. The manuscript claim is that the Frobenius pairing ∫_A ∘ μ is non-degenerate — inducing an iso A ≅ A^∨ — iff it is bijective as the underlying map (the snake identities, with a coevaluation η̃ : 𝟏 → A ⊗ A whose two snake composites are id_A).

      Only the bijectivity half is stated here, and the loss is deliberate. FrobeniusFormContext models A and A_dual as plain Type and the pairing as a plain function, so there is no categorical iso in scope to put on the other side of the ↔. Stating bijectivity alone is weaker than the manuscript claim and is what this model supports.

      The categorical half is now BUILT — see QOU/FrobeniusHopfData/FrobeniusPairingCategorical.lean, added 2026-09-07. It states non-degeneracy over a monoidal category as the existence of a coevaluation satisfying both snake identities against the pairing the data carries, and exactPairing_of_nondegenerate turns that into Mathlib's ExactPairing. #print axioms on it reports no axioms at all — not even propext. The manuscript's non-degeneracy condition simply is Mathlib's ExactPairing; what was missing was the right model, not infrastructure.

      That file does not assert the manuscript's equivalence with bijectivity: that needs a faithful fibre functor τ reflecting isomorphisms, and is deliberately left unstated.

      Why this docstring first said otherwise (corrected 2026-09-07; this paragraph first said it "needs the rigid-monoidal apparatus", which implies a Mathlib gap that does not exist). Mathlib carries CategoryTheory.Monoidal.Rigid at the pinned revision — ExactPairing, rightDualIso (Rigid/Basic.lean:541) and three further files, verified on disk rather than recalled. What is missing is local: this class does not model a category at all. Restating the two-sided claim means re-modelling FrobeniusFormContext over a MonoidalCategory with an ExactPairing, which Mathlib supports. Cf. docs/audits/2026-09-04-mathlib-infrastructure-inventory.md §U6, which records that Monoidal/Rigid/ and Monoidal/Braided/ are present while pivotal / spherical / ribbon are not.

      What was here until 2026-09-07, and why it went. The class carried is_iso : Prop — a field typed with the universe Prop, so it held a proposition as data — and this def read ctx.is_iso ↔ Function.Bijective ctx.pairing. The docstring called that the §3b pattern-C disposition, resting on "the open state is honestly represented by the absence of an instance". That mechanism did not hold: the instance is one line, because the instance chooses is_iso. Measured, lean-direct exit 0:

      example (X Y : Type) (p : X → Y) : ∃ ctx : FrobeniusFormContext,
    FrobeniusForm ctx :=
  ⟨{ A := X, A_dual := Y, pairing := p,
     is_iso := Function.Bijective p }, Iff.rfl⟩

      So the old FrobeniusForm held for every pairing datum and asserted nothing. The example below shows the replacement is refutable, which is the property the old form could not offer.
