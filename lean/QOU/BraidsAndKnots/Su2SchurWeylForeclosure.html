    SU(2) / WRT(S³/2I) Schur–Weyl foreclosure — the geometric W_λ index cannot be the naive SU(2)-side modular data #

    The s5-so3 keystone asks whether a geometric equivariant index (Beasley–Witten Seifert–Chern–Simons on the Poincaré sphere S³/2I) reproduces the q-Plancherel weight W_λ = d_q(λ)²/[n]_q!. WRT(S³/2I) carries SU(2)-side modular data; under classical Schur–Weyl (ℂ²)^{⊗n} = ⊕_{λ : ≤2 rows} S^λ ⊗ V_λ, the GL₂(=spinor ℂ²) image is exactly the partitions with at most two rows. The Step-1 gate (docs/audits/2026-06-24-keystone-step1-gate-wrt-vs-wlambda.md, PR #2717) ran this at n=3,4,5 and found the SU(2) side omits every ≥3-row block, including the electron (3,1,1).

    This file machine-checks that structural foreclosure (previously prose / Python only in #2406 / #2608 / #2717): the GL₂ Schur–Weyl image among the partitions of 5 is exactly {(5),(4,1),(3,2)}, the electron (3,1,1) is not in it, and therefore any weight system supported on the GL₂ image differs from any W_λ that is nonzero on the electron block (su2_index_ne_wlambda). Since the actual W_λ(3,1,1) = d_q(3,1,1)²/[5]_q! > 0 at the substrate (d_q(3,1,1) ≈ 6.2204, witnessed in the Step-1 gate), the naive SU(2)/WRT(S³/2I) index cannot equal W_λ — the geometric realization must live on the full-Hecke side (S_n, d→∞), as the corpus arena correction (#2406) concluded.

    The partition type here mirrors QOU.BraidsAndKnots.PartitionOf5 (the keystone scaffold in S5SO3FiberAction.lean); it is restated self-contained — under the dedicated Su2Foreclosure namespace, importing only Mathlib.Tactic — to keep this foreclosure free of the scaffold's heavier import surface. The row-counts and the electron block (3,1,1) (which carries the central A₅ rep T₁ ⊕ T₂) coincide with the scaffold's.

    This is a (b) structural exclusion (refutation-scope discipline): it rests on a proved invariant — the row-count bound of the GL_d Schur–Weyl image — and excludes only the SU(2) sub-route, not the full-Hecke geometric programme (the open frontier). No fit; nothing archimedean here (the capstone is abstract over the weight codomain; positivity of W_λ(3,1,1) enters only as the cited hypothesis hW).
