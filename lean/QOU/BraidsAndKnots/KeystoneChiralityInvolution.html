    QOU.BraidsAndKnots.KeystoneChiralityInvolution #

    — C2 of the keystone necessary conditions: the forced transpose / chirality involution

    The s5-so3 keystone's geometric index must reproduce the q-Plancherel weights W_λ = d_q(λ)²/[n]_q!. Necessary condition C2 (2026-06-16-keystone-index-necessary-conditions.md) is conjugation symmetry W_λ = W_{λ'}, which holds exactly at q₀ because the hook-length multiset is transpose-invariant (h_λ(i,j) = h_{λ'}(j,i)), so d_q(λ) = d_q(λ'). C2 is the strongest pruning lever: any construction must carry the involution inducing transpose λ ↔ λ' — which on the QOU side is precisely the chirality flip T₁ ↔ T₂. A construction without it is dead on arrival.

    This file machine-checks the structural facets of C2 over the corpus partition type:

    • cellContent_transpose_neg — the chirality atom: a cell's content c(i,j) = j − i negates under transpose (i,j) ↦ (j,i). Chirality = sign of content, so transpose flips chirality.
    • electron_self_conjugate + electron_content_zero — the electron block (3,1,1) is self-conjugate (a transpose fixed point) and hence chirality-neutral (c(λ) = 0). This is the keystone's central A₅ rep ((3,1,1) = T₁ ⊕ T₂ self-conjugate; ledger #2424).
    • conjugate_two_content_flip — a concrete transpose pair [2] ↔ [1,1] exhibits the chirality flip c(λ') = − c(λ).

    The global W_λ = W_{λ'} (transpose-invariance of d_q) is the cited + numerically-witnessed necessary condition (keystone_index_necessary_conditions.py, max diff 0.0 at q₀); these are its formalized facets.
