    R[h] layer — the standard-irrep character as a polynomial in h #

    The faithful base of the standard-irrep character is the polynomial χ^{(2,1)} = 2 − 3h⁴ − h⁶ ∈ R[h] — pure ring, no division, no field. Instantiating R = LaurentPolynomial ℤ, h = T − T⁻¹ gives the genuine ℤ[q, q⁻¹] character. Only the q-rational form (… / q⁶) needs q inverted, and lives in the ℚ section below.

      Rˣ layer — the published q-rational form over a commutative ring #

      The published character χ^{(2,1)}(β_Bor) = (−q¹² + 3q¹⁰ − 3q⁸ + 4q⁶ − 3q⁴ + 3q² − 1)/q⁶ divides by q⁶. Demanding only that q be a unit of a commutative ring R (q : Rˣ, so ↑q⁻¹ is its ring inverse and division by q⁶ is multiplication by (↑q⁻¹)⁶) keeps the form division-ring-free: it now instantiates over R = LaurentPolynomial ℤ, q = T (a unit, inverse T(-1)), giving the genuine ℤ[q, q⁻¹] character. This is strictly more general than the [Field R] form below (every non-zero q in a field is a unit).

      The bridge chi21_borromean_qform_unit_eq_poly shows this published form is exactly the faithful R[h] polynomial character chi21_borromean at h = ↑q − ↑q⁻¹; the only input is the unit law ↑q · ↑q⁻¹ = 1 (no division, no field, no ordering).

            Payoff — instantiation over ℤ[q, q⁻¹] (no field) #

            LaurentPolynomial ℤ is the universal home of the character: q = T is a unit (inverse T(-1)), so chi21_borromean_qform_unit evaluates there and the published /q⁶ form is the genuine Laurent polynomial 2 − 3h⁴ − h⁶, h = T − T⁻¹. Witnesses that the Rˣ layer is not vacuous over a non-field.
