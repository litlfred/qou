    Unit-modulus eigenvalues from det = 1 and small real trace.

    The genuine structural content the figure-eight needs: if a complex number λ is a root of the real characteristic polynomial x² - t·x + 1 of a 2×2 real matrix with determinant 1 and real trace t satisfying t² < 4, then ‖λ‖ = 1 (unit modulus).

    Proof: take real and imaginary parts of the root equation. The imaginary part gives λ.im·(2λ.re - t) = 0; with t² < 4 the root is non-real (λ.im ≠ 0), so λ.re = t/2. The real part then pins λ.im² = 1 - λ.re², hence normSq λ = λ.re² + λ.im² = 1, i.e. ‖λ‖ = 1.
