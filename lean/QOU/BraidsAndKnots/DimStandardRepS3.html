    Dimension of the Standard Representation V_{(2,1)} of S₃ #

    The irreducible representation V_{(2,1)} of the symmetric group S₃ has classical dimension 2, computed via the Frame–Robinson–Thrall hook-length formula:

    dim V_λ = n! / ∏_{(i,j) ∈ λ} hook(i,j)

    For λ = (2,1) ⊢ 3:

    • Cells: (0,0), (0,1), (1,0)
    • Hook lengths: h(0,0) = 3, h(0,1) = 1, h(1,0) = 1
    • Hook product = 3 × 1 × 1 = 3
    • dim = 3! / 3 = 6 / 3 = 2

    Significance for the proton mass identity #

    Per docs/audits/2026-05-25-proton-mass-identity-12-me-ocneanu.md, the factor "2" in the substrate identity

    m_p = 2 · m_μ · F_Bor(q₀) − 12 · m_e

    equals dim V_{(2,1)}: the classical dimension of the standard (trace-dominant) S₃ irrep that contributes 49% of the proton's Wedderburn T1 trustable trace.

    References #

    -- Ref: [manuscript] Frame–Robinson–Thrall hook-length formula (1954) -- Ref: [manuscript] James–Kerber, The Representation Theory of the Symmetric Group (1981)
