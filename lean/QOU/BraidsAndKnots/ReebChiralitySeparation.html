            theorem QOU.BraidsAndKnots.reebChiralityKey_injective :
            Function.Injective A5Irrep.reebChiralityKey

            Reeb level ⊕ chirality SEPARATES the five A₅ irreps (the composite key is injective). The chirality ±1 breaks the T₂/G Casimir-12 degeneracy that the level alone leaves; all other pairs are already separated by the level. This realises the five_distinct_classes separation from on-main canonical data, with no geometric angle input.
