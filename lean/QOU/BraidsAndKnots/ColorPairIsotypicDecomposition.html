    QOU.BraidsAndKnots.ColorPairIsotypicDecomposition — the color-pair space #

    Bean qou-ctkw / open question 1 of conj:inter-nucleon-channel-color-tube.

    The three color sheets are the T₁ irrep of A₅ ≅ I (InterNucleonChannelColorTube.color_sheets_carry_T1), and BringFiberAction is discharged by the icosahedral Reeb instance. So the space of ordered color-sheet pairs carries T₁ ⊗ T₁, a 9-dimensional representation of the icosahedral group.

    The exact character computation (computations/color_tube_t1_tensor_decomposition.py, witness color-tube-t1-tensor-decomposition.witness.json; inner products over ℚ(√5) on the five classes of A₅, after asserting table orthonormality) gives

    T₁ ⊗ T₁ = A ⊕ T₁ ⊕ H (dims 1 + 3 + 5 = 9) Sym² T₁ = A ⊕ H (dim 6), Alt² T₁ = T₁ (dim 3).

    What this file proves. Not a restatement of that output — the consequences that are forced, and can fail:

    1. tensor_dim_consistent — the summand dims sum to dim(T₁)² = 9. This would fail for a wrong decomposition (A ⊕ T₁ ⊕ G gives 8 ≠ 9).
    2. sym_alt_assignment_forced — the Sym²/Alt² assignment is forced by dimension alone: given the summand dims {1, 3, 5} and dim Sym²(V₃) = 6, the only sub-collection summing to 6 is {1, 5} = {A, H}, leaving {3} = {T₁} for Alt². So the Z/2 grading of the color pairs is not an extra choice — it is determined.
    3. uniform_is_isotypy_blind_barycentre — the three isotypic projector diagonals sum to the identity's, and dividing by 9 returns exactly 1/9 on every pair: the uniform-flavor projection is the isotypy-blind average. This is the structural reading of the projection conj:inter-nucleon-channel-color-tube identifies as lossy.

    Scope (honest). This narrows open question 1 — from an arbitrary nonnegative normalised 9-vector per flavor pair (8 free parameters) to a convex combination of three canonical weight systems (2 free parameters) — and supplies the canonical Z/2 for the ε rule. It does not close it: the flavor dependence (F₁, F₂) is not supplied by the color-side decomposition. No instance of InterNucleonChannelColorTube is provided here, and nothing is wired.

              theorem QOU.BraidsAndKnots.ColorPairIsotypicDecomposition.uniform_is_isotypy_blind_barycentre :
              diagSame.sum / 9 = 1 / 9 ∧ diagDiff.sum / 9 = 1 / 9

              The uniform projection W = 1/9 is the isotypy-blind barycentre. The three projectors sum to the identity, whose diagonal is 1 on every pair; so dividing the summed diagonal by 9 returns exactly 1/9 on both pair classes. The "flavor-only projection" the conjecture identifies as lossy is precisely the average that forgets which isotypic summand a pair sits in.

              diagSame[0]! / ↑1 ≠ diagSame[1]! / 3 ∧ diagSame[1]! / ↑3 ≠ diagSame[2]! / 5 ∧ diagSame[0]! / ↑1 ≠ diagSame[2]! / 5

              The three canonical weight systems are distinct. Normalising each projector diagonal by its trace gives three different normalised weight systems on the color pairs (A: all mass on same-color; T₁: all on different-color; H: split). So the isotypic decomposition genuinely narrows W — it is not three names for one weighting.
