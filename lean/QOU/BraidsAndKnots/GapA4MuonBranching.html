    GAP-A-4: Operator-Valued Muon Generation Branching

    This module formalizes the structural scaffold for resolving the 210 ppb muon mass tension. The bare-categorical evaluation m_e * Vol(4_1) / hbar_q^2 assumes a scalar invariant. Under GAP-A-4, the generation tower is operator-valued: the mass is evaluated through a Wedderburn trace over the 5-strand fiber space associated with Bring's surface.

    • Electron (ℓ = 1): corresponds to the dim-3 icosahedral irrep T₁, which lives in the (3,1,1) block of H_5.
    • Muon (ℓ = 2): corresponds to the dim-5 icosahedral irrep H, which lives in the (3,2) (and (2,2,1)) block of H_5.
