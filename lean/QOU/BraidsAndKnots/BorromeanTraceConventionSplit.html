                theorem QOU.BraidsAndKnots.trD_t_eq_trM_borromean {t : ℚ} (ht : t ≠ 0) :
                trD_borromean t = trM_borromean (t ^ 2 - (t ^ 2)⁻¹)

                Bridge to the corpus closed form. For t ≠ 0, the t-form of convention D equals trM_borromean (t² − t⁻²) — the Lean-proved BorromeanMarkovTrace quotient form at h = q − q⁻¹ = t² − t⁻². So trD_borromean here IS the corpus pipeline object, re-parameterised.

                trD_borromean 2 ≠ trC_borromean 2

                The split witness point. At t = 2 the two conventions give different exact rationals: tr_C(2) = −236413/512 while tr_D(2) = −13746641/73984.

                source
                theorem QOU.BraidsAndKnots.conventionD_ne_conventionC :
                ¬∀ (t : ℚ), t ≠ 0 → trD_borromean t = trC_borromean t

                Convention D ≠ convention C. The pipeline (TL/Jones) trace and the canonical (Wenzl z-trace) Markov trace of β_Bor are NOT the same function of t = √q: they differ at t = 2 (where both denominators are nonzero), hence differ as rational functions. The trace-convention split registered in the 2026-06-09 audit addendum is a theorem.
