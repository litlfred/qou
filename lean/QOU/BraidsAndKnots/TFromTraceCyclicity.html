    QOU.BraidsAndKnots.TFromTraceCyclicity — time reversal as word reversal #

    Formalisation of [conj:t-from-trace-cyclicity]: time reversal T acts on braid words by word reversal,

    T : σ_{i₁}^{ε₁} ⋯ σ_{i_ℓ}^{ε_ℓ} ↦ σ_{i_ℓ}^{ε_ℓ} ⋯ σ_{i₁}^{ε₁},

    and the Markov trace is invariant under it.

    What is proved here, and what is assumed #

    The block's structural claims about T itself — that it is an involution, that it preserves q, the colour-sheet flavours, the Hecke generator signs, and acts purely on the ordering of crossings — are unconditional theorems below (timeReversal_involutive, timeReversal_crossings, timeReversal_perm). They need no hypothesis: on BraidWord n = List (Fin (n-1) × Bool) reversal is List.reverse, which keeps every (generator, sign) pair and only permutes their order.

    The trace invariance tr_M(β) = tr_M(β^T) is the genuinely conjectural content and is carried by the class field trace_reversal_invariant.

    It does not follow from cyclicity #

    The .md derives trace invariance "by the Markov trace cyclicity axiom tr_M(ab) = tr_M(ba)". That step does not go through. Cyclicity — available here as QOU.markovTrace_append_comm — gives invariance under rotation of a word, tr(vw) = tr(wv); reversal is a different operation, and for non-commuting matrices tr(ABC) ≠ tr(CBA) in general. The claim is plausible for Hecke characters by a different route (the anti-automorphism fixing each generator, under which characters are invariant), but that route is not formalised here and the corpus does not carry it. Hence the class field rather than a proof.

    -- Ref: [manuscript] conj:t-from-trace-cyclicity -- Ref: [jones1987] Hecke Algebra Representations of Braid Groups

    Symbol-collision note #

    BraidWord is overloaded in this corpus: QOU.BraidWord is the structure in QOU/PhotonThreading.lean, while the braid word carried by the Hecke representation machinery is the indexed abbreviation QOU.Archimedean.BraidWord (n : ℕ) := List (Fin (n-1) × Bool). A bare open QOU resolves the name to the former and this file will not elaborate. Everything below is therefore fully qualified.

      class QOU.BraidsAndKnots.TFromTraceCyclicity (n d : ℕ) :
      Prop

      Time-reversal closure (conjecture).

      The one unproved input: the Markov trace does not see word reversal.

      Axiomatised as a class per AGENTS.md §3b — providing an instance asserts the conjecture. The field is parameter-quantified over every representation and every braid word, i.e. it is stated in the shape of the claim rather than as an opaque Prop.

        The conjecture: the Markov trace of a braid word equals that of its time reversal, for every representation and every word.
