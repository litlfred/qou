    R[h] layer — multiplicative identities over a generic commutative ring #

    Everything in this section holds over {R : Type*} [CommRing R] (h : R), i.e. in the polynomial ring R[h]. No division, no field, no ordering — the tr_M closed form is encoded as the multiplicative relation [2]_q² · t = numerator (see IsBorromeanMarkovTrace).

        def QOU.BraidsAndKnots.IsBorromeanMarkovTrace {R : Type u_1} [CommRing R] (h t : R) :
        Prop

        The defining multiplicative relation for the Borromean Markov trace. A value t : R is the Borromean Markov trace at h iff

        [2]_q² · t = numerator, i.e. (h² + 4)·t = 4 + h² − 3h⁴ − h⁶.

              theorem QOU.BraidsAndKnots.borromean_markov_trace_closed_form_mul {R : Type u_1} [CommRing R] (h t : R) (ht : IsBorromeanMarkovTrace h t) :
              trM_borromean_den h * (t - 1) = h ^ 4 * (-3 - h ^ 2)

              Closed form, multiplicative (R[h]). Any t satisfying the defining relation IsBorromeanMarkovTrace h t satisfies the exact multiplicative identity

              [2]_q² · (t − 1) = −3h⁴ − h⁶ = h⁴·(−3 − h²).

              This is the division-free statement of tr_M = 1 + (−3h⁴ − h⁶)/[2]_q²: after clearing [2]_q², the correction [2]_q²·(tr_M − 1) is a pure multiple of h⁴, with no h⁰…h³ term (the structural h² = 0 fact). (No Dvd claim is made: over a generic ring this identity need not yield a quotient witness for t − 1.)

              Bar-(anti-)symmetric structure, over R[h] #

              The bar-involution sends q ↦ q⁻¹, equivalently h = q − q⁻¹ ↦ −h. The numerator 4 + h² − 3h⁴ − h⁶ and denominator h² + 4 are even polynomials in h, so the defining relation is invariant under h ↦ −h: the SAME trace value t solves the relation at h and at −h. Hence the bar-anti-symmetric companion vanishes structurally (the ℚ/field statement Q_borromean_eq_zero below is the archimedean shadow of this).

              Archimedean (ℚ) layer — quotient form and positivity #

              The genuine division form tr_M := num/den and the ordering fact [2]_q² > 0 are archimedean: they live over an ordered field, here ℚ. Each result below is recovered from the R[h] multiplicative core above by clearing [2]_q² (which is a unit since [2]_q² = h² + 4 > 0).

                  Linking-weight bridge formula — explicit inconsistency with Family A #

                  PR #1272 audit (2026-05-25): the framework's linking_weight(𝓑₃, q) has been computed via two independent routes — both Lean-proved — that turn out to give different values. This section makes the inconsistency explicit at the Lean level so future work can address it categorically rather than by silent normalisation choices.

                  The +5/2 leading constants and the /2 in the family-A weight are archimedean (rational coefficients), so this section also lives over ℚ.

                  Route 1 — Family A (Wedderburn dim sum on H_4): λ_A(q) := (1/4) · Σ_{λ ⊢ 4} dim_q V_λ = 5/2 + (3/4)·h² (Lean-proved in lambda-scale-substrate-form.lean §lambdaA_qForm_eq_hForm.)

                  Route 2 — Jones bridge (H_3 Markov trace of β_Bor, rescaled): linking_weight_bridge(h) := 5/2 + 4·(trM_borromean(h) − 1) (The +5/2 leading and the factor 4 force linking_weight(q=1) = 5/2 to match Family A's leading at h=0; the formula is a bridge between the H_3 Markov trace and Family A's H_4 Wedderburn count, not a derivation.)

                  The two routes disagree by an explicit rational function in h, derived below. Identifying which is the categorically-correct linking_weight is the open Reading-A research direction.

                  Ref: PR #1272 — gamma-gamma-pair-production-decomposition.witness.json Ref: docs/audits/2026-05-25-non-abelian-stokes-reading-a-exploration.md
