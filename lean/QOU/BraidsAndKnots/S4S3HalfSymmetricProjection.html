                    theorem QOU.BraidsAndKnots.s4_s3_half_symmetric_projection :
                    effectiveCellCount = 1 ∧ effectiveCellCount / 8 = 1 / 8

                    Main result: the half-symmetric H_4 projection, restricted to the S_3-trivial subspace, extracts exactly ONE of the 8 ideal octahedral cells in the Borromean complement tessellation.

                    Scope (2026-05-24, post-purge): combinatorial bijection only. The proof states the standard S_n branching arithmetic; it makes NO claim about per-cell curvature κ. The earlier Yekutieli per-cell reading (κ = −G) was purged from production on 2026-05-24 (commit 71d674da1) as non-canonical: PR #985 found the natural-reading bijection gives κ = +0.5, not −G, and no categorical derivation supplies −G·h².

                    Per the absorbed-into-VolDensity framing (PR #1080), the canonical mass formula is m(K) = m_e · VolDensity(K, q_0) with no separate λ_scale constant. This bijection is a structural input that constrains VolDensity(𝓑₃)'s categorical refinement at h² order; it is NOT a per-cell additive correction.
