    Strict trace bound: tr_M(β_Bor) < 1 away from h = 0 #

    source
    theorem QOU.BraidsAndKnots.trM_borromean_lt_one (h : ℚ) (hh : h ≠ 0) :
    trM_borromean h < 1

    The Borromean trace is strictly below 1 for every h ≠ 0. From the closed form, 1 − t = (3h⁴ + h⁶)/(h² + 4) with a positive numerator when h ≠ 0. At h = 0 the trace is exactly 1 (isBorromeanMarkovTrace_one_at_zero), so the hypothesis is sharp.

    source
    theorem QOU.BraidsAndKnots.abs_trM_borromean_lt_one (h : ℚ) (hh : h ≠ 0) (hsmall : h ^ 2 ≤ 1) :
    |trM_borromean h| < 1

    Two-sided bound on the physical domain. For h ≠ 0 with h² ≤ 1 (the substrate has h₀² ≈ 0.0437), the trace also stays above −1: the numerator plus denominator is 8 + 2h² − 3h⁴ − h⁶ ≥ 8 − 2h² ≥ 6 > 0. Combined with trM_borromean_lt_one, |t| < 1.

    theorem QOU.BraidsAndKnots.borromean_pair_within_radius (h q : ℚ) (hh : h ≠ 0) (hsmall : h ^ 2 ≤ 1) (hq : 1 < q) :

    The composite convergence bound (the TRUE bound of the formalize-first plan): on the physical domain (h ≠ 0, h² ≤ 1) the Borromean free-pair argument is inside the Li₂^q radius for every q > 1. Together with pair_trace_dressed_lt_radius this gives convergence of every pair term of the brane tower over the Borromean nucleon — for ALL Z, with no per-isotope hypothesis.
