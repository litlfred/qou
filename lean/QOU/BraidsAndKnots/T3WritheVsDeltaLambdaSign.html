    QOU.BraidsAndKnots.T3WritheVsDeltaLambdaSign #

    Does the derived weak-isospin T₃ writhe-sign reproduce the mixed-sign Δ_λ? #

    Serves bean qou-buxs (child of qou-20sx). Formalises the decisive structural finding of computations/t3_writhe_vs_delta_lambda_sign.py.

    The weak-isospin grading of WeakIsospinT3Grading.lean (PR #4618) is T₃ = sign(κ)/2, κ = signed writhe: the per-quark datum is sign(κ), the sign of the net writhe. The Option-E deuteron closure needs the per-partition chirality sign sgn(w₀^{S_{λ'}}) = ∏_j (-1)^{C(c_j,2)} (c_j = column heights of λ), a mixed-sign pattern the PROBE-4 KL-positivity prune (2026-06-16) proved must be supplied exogenously from the braid images.

    Aggregating the crossing sign over the Young/Wedderburn column antisymmetrizer (guided by the chiral-EFT τ₁·τ₂ Pauli/exchange-symmetry template, Hammer–König–van Kolck arXiv:1906.12122 p.12) admits two readings that differ, and the difference is the whole result:

    • crossing-COUNT parity crossingCountParity c = (-1)^{C(c,2)} — the sign character / Slater-determinant sign. Its product over column heights is sgn(w₀^{S_{λ'}}) itself (the target's own definition).
    • net-writhe SIGN netWritheSign c — the literal T₃ statistic sign(κ): a column of height c ≥ 2 is a negative half-twist with net writhe -C(c,2), so sign(net) = -1 for every c ≥ 2 and +1 for c ≤ 1.

    These two per-column factors coincide iff C(c,2) is odd (c ≡ 2,3 mod 4) and both are +1 at c ≤ 1; they diverge at c = 4,5 — first realised by a partition of 6. So the literal T₃ sign-of-net-writhe reproduces Δ_λ for n = 3 (all column heights ≤ 3) but fails at n = 6.

    What is proved below (decide; genuine finite decidable checks, no fit) #

    • heightFourStatisticsDisagree — the falsifiable root: at column height 4 the two sign statistics disagree (crossingCountParity 4 = 1, netWritheSign 4 = -1). This is why the maps part ways; had they agreed the whole negative would vanish.
    • n3_literalT3_reproduces_target — for every partition of 3 the literal-T₃ aggregation equals the target sign (3/3, coincidental: heights ≤ 3).
    • n6_literalT3_fails_at_3111 — for the partition (3,1,1,1) ⊢ 6 the literal-T₃ aggregation differs from the target: the honest negative. (Its conjugate has a height-4 column.)

    This file is deliberately import-free (pure core Lean 4, Int-valued, decidable) so the arithmetic is self-verifiable without Mathlib; it makes no archimedean (ℝ) or categorical claim, only the finite sign comparison.

                  The falsifiable root: the two statistics disagree at height 4 #

                  theorem QOU.BraidsAndKnots.T3WritheVsDeltaLambda.heightFourStatisticsDisagree :
                  crossingCountParity 4 = 1 ∧ netWritheSign 4 = -1 ∧ crossingCountParity 4 ≠ netWritheSign 4

                  At a column of height 4 the two sign statistics disagree. The crossing-count parity is (-1)^6 = +1; the net-writhe sign is -1. This is the concrete reason the literal T₃ grading cannot be the Δ_λ sign: Δ_λ reads a crossing-COUNT parity, T₃ reads a net-orientation sign, and they differ exactly when C(c,2) is even (c ≡ 0,1 mod 4, first at c=4).
