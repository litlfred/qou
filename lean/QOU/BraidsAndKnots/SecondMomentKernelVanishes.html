    The dim²-weighted content-spread total vanishes, at every k #

    secondMomentKernel k = Σ_{λ⊢k} (f^λ)²·ω₃(λ) with ω₃(λ) = Σ_□ c(□)² − binom(k,2). It is zero, and the corpus said so — but only by decide, over 3 ≤ k ≤ 20 (YoungDiagramKernel.secondMomentKernel_eq_zero_of_le_twenty).

    That lemma's own docstring names what a general-k proof needs and why it was not available:

    what the decide supplies is a certified floor pending the general character-orthogonality proof, which needs the JM-eigenvalue ⇒ Plancherel-mean bridge (Sₙ representation-theory infrastructure, not yet in the corpus).

    It is in the corpus now. That bridge is Identity (ii) — 2·Σ_λ dim(λ)²·(Σ_□ c(□)²) = k(k−1)·k! — which identityB_general proves from the Jucys–Murphy central element acting on the Wedderburn blocks of ℚ[S_k], exactly the three inputs the docstring lists (JM elements with their content eigenvalues, centrality of Σ_i X_i², and the regular-representation trace that carries the column-orthogonality content). PlancherelSnDataGeneral states it over the row-length list model.

    So the identity is arithmetic:

    secondMomentKernel k = Σ_λ f²·Σc²  −  binom(k,2)·Σ_λ f²
                     = k!·binom(k,2)  −  binom(k,2)·k!  =  0.

    What this unblocks #

    ω₃'s mean-zero leg is what carried the k ≤ 20 cap through the pincer: tiltedMean_zero_of_le_twenty is the only consumer of that bound in YoungDiagramTailFirstInstance.nuV_top_closed_form and nuV_top_nonneg. Both are restated here without it. The kernel conjecture itself is untouched — kernelValue k ≤ 0 is the dim³-weighted total, which lies outside the polynomial-function algebra and remains open; this is the dim² total, which was never conjectural, only uncertified past k = 20.

    Pure arithmetic on top of the two transported identities; nothing archimedean; no fit.
