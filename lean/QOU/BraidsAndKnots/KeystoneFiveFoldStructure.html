    The keystone five-fold: 1 + 1 + 1 + 2 = 5, and the electron as the fifth element #

    The s5-so3 keystone (bean s5bb) rests on a five-fold: the W_λ index runs over the five A₅ irreps {A, T₁, T₂, G, H} (A5Irrep, dims 1,3,3,4,5). This file formalizes the structure of that five — how the seven S₅ partitions of 5 produce exactly five A₅ irreps — and records the resonance with the confinement-SDP operator basis surfaced 2026-06-29.

    The decomposition. Conjugation pairs the seven partitions of 5 into three conjugate pairs plus one self-conjugate:

    • (5) ↔ (1⁵), (4,1) ↔ (2,1³), (3,2) ↔ (2,2,1) — three pairs, each collapsing to ONE A₅ irrep (A, G, H resp., via PartitionOf5.toA5Irrep; the sign character is trivial on A₅);
    • (3,1,1) self-conjugate — the electron block — which does NOT collapse but splits into the chirality pair T₁ ⊕ T₂.

    So toA5Irrep (the partition restriction) hits only FOUR irreps {A, T₁, G, H}; the fifth, T₂, is supplied only by the chirality split of the self-conjugate electron block. The count is

    1 (A) + 1 (G) + 1 (H) + 2 (T₁ ⊕ T₂ from the electron block)  =  5  =  #A₅ irreps.

    The electron resonance (2026-06-29). This 4 + 1-by-the-electron structure matches the nucleus confinement SDP operator basis (computations/nucleus_operator_selection_sdp.py): the basis is the six Bring generators (γ, σ₀, σ₁, L₊, L₋, e⁻) (χ(Bring) = −6), of which five are strong-sector (all but the electron e⁻). In both places the electron is the distinguishing fifth element: the A₅ five-fold completes only through the electron-block chirality split T₁ ⊕ T₂, and the strong confinement five is the six Bring generators minus the electron. This is the candidate structural origin of the per-unit 5 in the binding residual 10A − 5 = 5(2A−1) (QOU/Mass/BindingResidualExponent.lean, the 5 open there). The identification of the binding 5 with this A₅ five-fold is near-match tier (not asserted as a theorem here); what is proved below is the partition→irrep five-fold structure itself.

    Base ring: pure finite combinatorics over the inductive PartitionOf5 / A5Irrep (DecidableEq); no archimedean construct, no fit.
