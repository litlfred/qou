    QOU.BraidsAndKnots.WstdSuperExpBound — the pigeonhole cube–square lower bound #

    The rigorous core (C) of the super-exponential lower bound on the GAP-A-3 tower weight w_std(k) = E_Planch[f^{λ/(2,1)}] (Δ_λ magnitude leg). The full argument (compute probe computations/deltalambda/wlambda_wstd_superexp_lower_bound.py, audit docs/audits/2026-07-18-wstd-superexp-lower-bound.md) reduces the growth bound to this clean inequality applied with aᵢ = f^λ, S = ∑(f^λ)² = k!, N = p(k):

    S³ ≤ N³ · (∑ aᵢ³)²      ⇒     ∑(f^λ)³ ≥ (k!)^{3/2} / p(k)^{3/2},

    whence Σ(f^λ)³/k! ≥ √(k!)/p(k)^{3/2} → ∞ super-exponentially (Stirling).

    Proof: pigeonhole (some square aⱼ² is ≥ the mean S/N), then ∑ aᵢ³ ≥ aⱼ³ (single nonneg term ≤ sum) and cube/square monotonicity give S³ ≤ (N·aⱼ²)³ = N³(aⱼ³)² ≤ N³(∑aᵢ³)².

    Base ring: ℝ (archimedean — ordering, a real square-sum bound; genuinely post-wall). Sorry-free.

    Algebraic transfer (B)+(D). From the exact Plancherel identity cube = 2·w_std + 2·e (B, with e = E_Planch[f^{λ/(3)}]) together with 0 ≤ e and the dominance e ≤ w_std (D), the tower weight carries at least a quarter of the cube moment: cube ≤ 4·w_std. Combined with a lower bound L ≤ cube (the pigeonhole cube_sum_sq_ge, applied with L = √(k!)/p(k)^{3/2}), this gives L/4 ≤ w_std — the super-exponential lower bound on w_std, modulo the (SYT-combinatorial) identity cube = 2w+2e and the inequality e ≤ w.
