    The (TAIL) first instance: ν_n ≥ 0, and its closed form #

    Assembly of the five derivation steps for the extreme member of the (TAIL) conjecture — the top hook statistic's Plancherel covariance with the 3-cycle eigenvalue,

    ν_n = Cov_Planch(N_n, ω₃) = n(n−2)(n−3)·C(2n−4, n−2) / (3·n!)

    (audit docs/audits/2026-07-26-covariance-kernel-A2-tail-first-instance.md). The ingredients all landed separately; this file closes the chain:

    step ingredient
    1 carrier confinement hookShape_of_hookLen_eq_sum, hookCount_sum_of_hookShape/_not_
    2 hook-shape data sytDim_hookShape (f^{(a,1^c)} = C(n−1,a−1)), contentSq_hookShape
    3 Plancherel restriction top_mixedMoment_eq_hookShapes_sum, six_mul_omega3_hookShape
    4 moment sums BinomialSquareSums (zeroth/first/second C(m,k)² moments)
    5 collapse BinomialSquareSums.bracket_sum_collapse
    mean-zero SecondMomentKernelVanishes.tiltedMean_zero (discharged, every k; was 3 ≤ k ≤ 20 via tiltedMean_zero_of_le_twenty)

    The sign needs no normalisation. ν_n ≥ 0 follows from the numerator's closed form together with tiltNorm > 0; the Plancherel normalisation Σ_λ (f^λ)² = k! enters only in the exact value. So nuV_top_nonneg is unconditional on the range where mean_zero is discharged, while nuV_top_closed_form carries the normalisation as one explicit, named premise.

    The one open premise. tiltNorm k 0 = k! is the RSK/Burnside identity in this file's encoding. The corpus proves the RSK sum-of-squares as OVTopAssembly.sum_numSYT_sq_eq_factorial : ∑ i : PartitionIndex n, numSYT i.diagram ^ 2 = n!, but with numSYT μ = Nat.card (QOU.BraidKnot.StandardYoungTableau μ), whereas the hook-length chain available here (sytDim_eq_numSYTenum, numSYTenum_eq_numStandardYoungTableaux) lands on Nat.card (QOU.Mathlib.StandardYoungTableau μ) — a different structure (0- vs 1-indexed entries; raw inj_on/strictness vs a SemistandardYoungTableau wrapper with Set.BijOn). The missing brick is therefore an equiv between the two SYT encodings; it is not the burnside roadmap's bridge (1) (numSYT_mul_hookProduct, the hook-length formula in the custom-Partition encoding). That same bridge would also discharge RSKBurnsideTarget.RSKBurnsideHolds.
