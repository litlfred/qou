    The lean.ref URI qou:QOU.HadronicMass.nucleon_borromean_topology resolves to nucleon_borromean_topology below.

    The lemma's content is that the Hopf link, being a torus link, has hyperbolic volume Vol(L_Hopf) = 0, so the nucleon mass contribution arises entirely from the Skein functional, not the volume term. The genuine geometric inputs are:

    • h_vol_hopf : Vol_Hopf = 0 — the Hopf link is a torus link (open manuscript fact for this abstract carrier);
    • h_mass_volume : mass_volume = Vol_Hopf * c — the volume mass term is the volume scaled by the (Skein) coefficient c;
    • h_mass_total : mass_total = mass_volume + mass_skein — the total mass is the sum of the two contributions.

    From these the conclusion is derived: the volume mass vanishes (mass_volume = 0) and the total reduces to the Skein mass (mass_total = mass_skein). The empirical inputs are isolated as hypotheses (pattern A of the vacuity-fix playbook); the previous encoding projected the whole conjunction from a self-asserting class field — a vacuous self-assuming projection — and is removed.

    Nucleon Borromean topology / Hopf-link binding ([lem:nucleon-borromean-topology]).

    The proton and neutron are each a Borromean configuration (6₂³) of three quark vortices, bound by Hopf links L_Hopf (color exchange). The Hopf link is a torus link, so Vol(L_Hopf) = 0 (h_vol_hopf); its volume mass term mass_volume = Vol_Hopf · c then vanishes, and the total mass mass_total = mass_volume + mass_skein collapses to the Skein mass. Both numerical clauses are derived from the geometric hypotheses, not projected.
