    QOU.frobenius_dagger_positive_definite — f† ∘ f > 0 for all f ≠ 0 #

    Proposition (conditional on a formally-real pivotal structure) (prop:positive-definite-dagger).

    Let (𝐂, A, τ) be a quantum universe whose endomorphism ring End(𝟙) ≅ R is a formally-real ordered field, with Frobenius pairing ⟨a, b⟩ = ∫_A(μ(a ⊗ b)) and a pivotal self-adjoint antipode S. Define the Frobenius dagger by f† = ∫_A ∘ μ(S(f) ⊗ −). Then for every non-zero f : 𝟙 → X, the composite f† ∘ f = ⟨f, f⟩ is a positive element of R.

    We verify positive-definiteness concretely in Rep(SU(2)) on the fundamental representation V = ℂ². Schur's lemma pins an endomorphism down to f = z • id. The dagger is the conjugate transpose, so f† ∘ f = |z|² • id.
