    QOU.CategoricalMassMarkov #

    Canonical declaration for content block at quantum-observable-universe/mass-endomorphism/categorical-mass-markov-trace-endomorphism-intro.ts.

    Formalises the introductory structural datum of the Markov-trace mass- endomorphism arc: the categorical mass is pinned by a Markov-trace endomorphism Φ on the substrate algebra, together with the global sections φ_n that close the foundations arc. This is an overview block; the carrier names the endomorphism as an additive map (its content is developed in the downstream blocks). Generic ring (§7c-clean).

    De-axiomatisation (bean 2g2u) #

    The carrier's two fields Φ and φ are no longer free data. Given the real HeckeMassElement Φ_mass(β) (QOU.Torsion.HeckeMassElement, which already carries the genuine Φ_mass = Φ^desc + Φ^trans decomposition and its Markov-trace mass), ofHecke derives both:

    • Φ is left multiplication by the mass element a ↦ Φ_mass · a — a genuine AddMonoidHom (additivity is mul_add, not a posited field), so the "mass endomorphism is additive" is a theorem about a concrete map, not an axiom;
    • φ_n is the Φ-orbit of the mass element Φ^[n](Φ_mass) — the level-n global sections realised as brane-level iterates, with φ_0 = Φ_mass and φ_{n+1} = Φ(φ_n) both proved.

    This is an honest cross-wire to the existing formalisation, not illustrative inhabitation (a dummy Φ := id, φ := 0 would compile but carry no content). The open closed-form of Φ_mass itself is the separate research conjecture conj:mass-endomorphism (see QOU.MassEndomorphism.VolumeBridge), not this intro carrier.
