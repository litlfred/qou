    QOU.BraidTwistFlip #

    Canonical declaration for content block at quantum-observable-universe/braids-and-knots/braid-twist-flip.ts.

    Formalises the manuscript definition of the twist and flip generators σ₁, σ₂ of the trefoil monodromy group S₃ ≅ D₃, acting on the three branches 𝐛 = (b₀, b₁, b₂) of the cubic constraint (its Galois group). The two generators are the reflections of D₃; their product σ₁σ₂ is the order-3 rotation α, and they satisfy the dihedral relation σ₁ α σ₁ = α⁻¹, exhibiting D₃ ≅ S₃.

    The S₃ acts on the three branches, not on the four spectral resolvents: S₃ has no faithful action on four points. The four resolvents carry the conjugate pairs (w₁,w₄), (w₂,w₃) and the ratios η₁ = w₁/w₂, η₂ = w₃/w₄, on which the reflection swaps the pairs and the rotation cycles the branches (def:braid-group-action; owner ruling, bean qou-htv4 — the earlier four-resolvent-with-one-fixed model is superseded). Pure combinatorics — generic over any branch-coordinate type α (§7c: no archimedean structure needed).
