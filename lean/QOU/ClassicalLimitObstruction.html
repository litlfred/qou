    Classical Limit Obstruction #

    The classical limit obstruction of a quantum universe is HH²(𝒰_q, Ad) — the second Hochschild cohomology of the quantized enveloping algebra with adjoint coefficients.

    In a QOU, the observability axiom HH⁰(Ad) = 0 combined with Frobenius–Poincaré duality HH^n ≅ (HH^{d-n})∨ collapses this to a one-dimensional space HH² ≅ k, controlled by the single scalar χ(A).

    🛑 The sentence above is RETIRED as a derivation (2026-09-15). The collapse to a line is NOT a consequence of the QOU axioms: the duality is cohomology-to-homology with a Nakayama twist (Brown–Zhang), the "unique deformation direction" is a class in HH¹ not HH², and at q = 1 Whitehead's lemma gives HH²(U(𝔤), M) = 0. One-dimensionality is now the hypothesis (W1) of the Wigner axiom (QOU.AlgebraicSubstrate.WignerAxiom, conj:wigner-axiom), and the scalar is χ_eu(M₀) = 2 − 2g, the Euler characteristic of the BASE CURVE (formerly written χ(A); the trace of the identity of A is a quantum dimension, dim_q, a different object). ofQOU below sets dim := 1 BY CONSTRUCTION and must be read as assuming (W1). See docs/audits/2026-09-15-chi-as-a-component-of-the-chern-character.md.

    structure QOU.ClassicalLimitObstruction (k : Type u_1) [CommRing k] :
    Type

    The classical limit obstruction space of a quantum universe.

    For a general QU, this is HH²(𝒰_q, Ad) — potentially multi-dimensional. For a QOU, the observability axiom and Frobenius duality reduce it to a one-dimensional space.

    The generator maps to χ(A) ∈ ℤ under the trace map.

    • dim : ℕ

      The dimension of the obstruction space HH²(Ad).

    • eulerChar : ℤ

      The Euler characteristic of the base curve, χ_eu(M₀) = 2 − 2g, to which the generator of the obstruction line pairs under the Wigner axiom. (This field read "χ(A) = tr(id_A)" until 2026-09-15; tr(id_A) is the quantum dimension of A, a different object.)

      def QOU.ClassicalLimitObstruction.ofQOU {k : Type u_1} [CommRing k] (_QOU : QuantumObservableUniverse k) (eulerChar : ℤ) :
      ClassicalLimitObstruction k

      Construct the classical limit obstruction for a QOU.

      In a QOU, the obstruction space is one-dimensional:

      • Observability gives HH⁰(Ad) = 0.
      • Frobenius–Poincaré duality gives HH² ≅ (HH¹)∨.
      • Drinfeld uniqueness gives dim HH¹ = 1.
      • Therefore dim HH² = 1.

      The generator maps to χ(A) under the trace map.

      Ref: [yekutieli2010] https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aim.2010.03.006

        theorem QOU.kummer_obstruction_divisibility (obs : ClassicalLimitObstruction ℝ) (n : ℕ) :
        obs.eulerChar % ↑n = 0 ↔ ↑n ∣ obs.eulerChar

        The obstruction vanishes at Kummer level n iff n ∣ χ(A).

        For the Descartes universe with χ(A) = -6, the obstruction vanishes only at n ∈ {1, 2, 3, 6} and persists at all other levels — in particular at all primes > 3.

        Ref: [yekutieli2010] https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aim.2010.03.006
