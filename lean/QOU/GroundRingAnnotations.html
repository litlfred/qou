    Ground Ring Annotations #

    Each Lean module in the QOU formalization works over a specific ring. The formal proof chain (the results that constitute the paper's theorems) uses only ℤ[q, q⁻¹]. Modules over ℝ or ℂ provide the archimedean realisation — they specialise the formal results to numerical computations but are NOT part of the formal chain.

    The formal chain #

    The paper's formal results form a chain over ℤ[q, q⁻¹]:

    ℤ[q, q⁻¹] (GroundRing)
  → Hecke algebra H_A(q) (GrobnerShirshov)
  → Torus knots τ(r,s) (TorusKnot)
  → Transfer matrices T(q) (TransferMatrix)
  → Skein polynomial P(q) (SkeinPolynomial)
  → β-decay operator T_{β-decay}(q) (BetaDecay)
  → Spectral decomposition Q_q (SpectralDecomp)

    Results over ℝ (archimedean realisation) are obtained by specialising q ↦ q₀ ≈ 1.1098 and are confined to Chapter 7.

    Bridge: specialisation map #

    The specialisation ι : ℤ[q, q⁻¹] → ℝ sends q ↦ q₀ where q₀ = 1 / (1 - √(Vol(4₁) / (m_μ/m_e))). Every formal result over ℤ[q, q⁻¹] descends to a numerical result via ι.

    Note to reader: Lean proofs in the formal chain work exclusively over ℤ[q, q⁻¹]. Numerical evaluations (Chapter 7) use the archimedean specialisation and are NOT part of the formal chain.

    Co-located stub annotations #

    Co-located .lean stubs live alongside .ts/.md content blocks in chapter directories (NOT under lean/QOU/). They are documentation stubs — not compiled by lake build — that mirror the paper's mathematical content in Lean syntax.

    Each stub carries a ground-ring annotation in its module docstring. Stubs in the formal chain use R_q = ℤ[q, q⁻¹]; stubs in the archimedean realisation use ℝ.

    Formal chain stubs (R_q) #

    Stubs for definitions and theorems that belong to the formal proof chain declare a local R_q := LaurentPolynomial ℤ and work over the Laurent polynomial ring. The parameter q is the canonical indeterminate of R_q. Ordering constraints (q > 1, 0 < q) are commented out — they are archimedean and not meaningful over R_q.

    Directories: quantum-observable-universes/, quantum-universes/, models-of-qous/, lifting-and-descent/, braids-and-knots/, archimedean-universe/ (algebraic stubs), algebraic-substrate/, information-theory/ (bounds).

    Archimedean stubs (ℝ) #

    Stubs that evaluate formal results at q₀ ≈ 1.1098 for comparison with experimental data. These use ℝ and are NOT part of the formal proof chain.

    Directories: observations/, glossary/, archimedean-universe/ (mass predictions), some information-theory/ files.

    Geometric/analytic stubs (ℝ) #

    Stubs using ℝ-specific analysis functions (trig, norms, real powers) that cannot be expressed over R_q.

    Includes: exceptional-divisor, descartes-elliptic-curve, mass-identity, some models-of-qous/ files.
