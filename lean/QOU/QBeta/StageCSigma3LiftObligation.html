      class QOU.QBeta.StageC.StageCSigma3Lift (R : Type u_1) [CommRing R] (σ : R →+* R) :
      Type u_1

      Conjecture carrier (OPEN — the Stage-C σ₃-lift obligation; bean 1jrh handoff): the categorical lift Λ ∈ H₄(q) of the free-neutron-decay operator.

      A lift supplies its two Markov-trace values and the level-4 rung they induce:

      • trH4 — tr_M^{H₄}(Λ), evaluable by the shipped Stage-C instrumented base (computations/habiro/qbeta_h4_stagec_markov_infra.py) once Λ is specified.
      • shadowH3 — tr_M^{H₃} of Λ's σ₃-free H₃ shadow.
      • c4even_eq — the level-4 rung reads directly as the difference: the H₃→H₄ stabilization constant is exactly 1 in the Wenzl normalization (Stage-C infra audit, gate C12 at 1e-50 across 5 q-points), so no normalization constant intervenes.
      • sigma3_gating — non-degeneracy: a σ₃-free element's H₄ data is fully determined by its H₃ data (branching preflight), hence carries zero level-4 content; a valid lift must have c4even ≠ 0.
      • bar_even / shadow_bar_even — both traces are fixed by the bar-involution σ (the scope's bar-evenness invariant; the ladder's even channel).

      NO instance is provided: the open state is the honest state (§3b-tax). The scope's provenance rule (Λ must be derived from the decay categorical content, never back-solved from a c₄ target) and the anchor-stability side-condition are meta-level selection rules on constructions, not fields of this carrier — see the module docstring.

      • shadow_bar_even : σ (shadowH3 σ) = shadowH3 σ

        Bar-evenness of the H₃ shadow trace.
