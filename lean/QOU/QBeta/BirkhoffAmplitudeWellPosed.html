    QOU.QBeta.BirkhoffAmplitudeWellPosed — is the A=3 amplitude E a gauge-invariant of the Birkhoff connection matrix, or does it require the full (frame-dependent) monodromy? A WELL-POSEDNESS result. #

    Scope (read this first). Two merged results pinned the A=3 anti-self-adjoint amplitude E to the GLOBAL 0↔∞ Birkhoff connection matrix P = (X^∞)⁻¹ X^0 of the DM q-cubic carrier C1:

    • QOU.QBeta.A3AntiSelfAdjointLift (#4611) bracketed the A=3 lift r* = μ₃/c₃ ≈ 1.585 to one free real DOF — the anti-self-adjoint amplitude E := C + σ B — and proved E ↦ factor is an affine bijection (amplitude_surjective/amplitude_injective), with any genuine lift forcing E ≠ 0 (amplitude_nonzero).
    • QOU.QBeta.StokesAmplitudeE (#4811) walled the LOCAL Stokes route: a single-slope carrier gives E = 0, a multi-slope carrier leaves E free — its value living in the global Birkhoff matrix, not local Stokes data (stokesAmplitude_surjective_of_dim_pos + the §3b GlobalBirkhoffSuppliesAmplitude carrier).

    Before anyone reads a value off P, this file answers the well-posedness question — the mirror of the μ₃-target-robustness audits (2026-08-01-mu3-target-{frame-robustness,robustness}): is E a single entry of P, or does the anti-self-adjoint amplitude require the FULL monodromy representation (a gauge-dependent combination)?     The residual gauge freedom (a CITED structural fact, not a fit). By QOU.MathlibExt.QCharacter, the canonical local solutions X^{(0)} = M^{(0)} e_{q,A(0)}, X^{(∞)} = M^{(∞)} e_{q,A(∞)} are built from q-characters e_{q,c} — and a q-character is unique only up to multiplication by a q-invariant (elliptic / theta) unit (IsQChar.ratio_qInvariant: two solutions with the same multiplier differ by a q-invariant ratio). Choosing the normalisation of the two local bases is therefore a residual gauge freedom: X^{(0)} ↦ X^{(0)} D_0, X^{(∞)} ↦ X^{(∞)} D_∞ with D_0, D_∞ q-invariant diagonal matrices, under which the connection matrix transforms

    P ↦ D_∞⁻¹ · P · D_0, entrywise P_{ij} ↦ (d_∞,i)⁻¹ · P_{ij} · (d_0,j).

    C1 is rank 2 (its two exponents λ₊, λ₋ at 0, C1ModuleExponents), so P is 2×2. This file models that 2×2 gauge action over a generic field and proves exactly what is, and is not, frame-free.

    Net verdict — MIXED, both branches proved sorry-free.

    • (a) There IS a specific gauge-invariant functional — the cross-ratio. The functional crossRatio P := P₀₁·P₁₀ / (P₀₀·P₁₁) is invariant under the full residual gauge action (crossRatio_gauge_invariant). Moreover it is essentially the unique invariant: the gauge group (K^×)⁴ fixes a generic P only on its 1-parameter global-scale subgroup (gauge_fixes_all_iff ⇒ stabiliser ≅ K^×), leaving 4 − 3 = 1 independent invariant on the four entries. This is what the sibling must compute — a frame-free scalar.

    • (b) A single entry is NOT gauge-invariant — it carries a residual elliptic-unit freedom. Any off-diagonal entry P₀₁ is only defined up to multiplication by an arbitrary q-invariant unit u = (d_∞,0)⁻¹ d_0,1 (entry_gauge_orbit_surjective: every u·P₀₁ is reachable; entry_not_gauge_invariant: a nontrivial gauge changes it). So reading E off a single entry is ill-posed: it needs an extra normalisation datum to pin the gauge — a scope-(a) well-posedness caveat. The residual freedom is the full multiplicative group of q-invariant (elliptic) units, i.e. the two diagonal gauges D_0, D_∞ modulo their common scale: dim = 2·2 − 1 = 3 scalar gauges on the matrix, of which a single entry sees the quotient 1-parameter elliptic unit as pure ambiguity.
