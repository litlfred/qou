    Wenzl–Pochhammer boundaries — quantum integers vanishing at roots of unity #

    Fibre-side infrastructure for the z→c_k evaluation-map program (PR #3458). The Wenzl–Pochhammer / Habiro-tower annihilator boundaries are the roots of unity at which the quantum integer [n]_q = (q^n − 1)/(q − 1) vanishes: at a primitive m-th root of unity ζ (with m > 1), [n]_ζ = 0 exactly when m ∣ n. In particular [4m]_q = 0 at ζ_{4m} are the boundaries at which the Habiro tower truncates ([4]_q=0 at ζ₄, [8]_q=0 at ζ₈ — the second boundary that the c_8 pattern-break aligns with; see docs/scope/2026-07-07-qbeta-ck-special-function-candidate-landscape.md).

    These are the phase-channel structure identified in the candidate triage: the c_k live at root-of-unity boundaries, not on the smooth real-q axis (the excluded magnitude channel).

    The f-map is now established (main a5bb64f3) to be a resurgence connection, not a coefficient map: the colored-Jones / Ohtsuki geometric-connection series is divergent (essential singularity at q=1, Kashaev ~ e^{N·Vol/2π}) while the Q_β = ∏(1+c_k u^k) trivial-connection series is regular, so no analytic change of variables links them — the c_k ↔ κ_k relation is Stokes data (Garoufalidis–Gu–Mariño). The boundary fact below is a pure algebraic truth that is agnostic to that: it pins where the geometric-connection singularities sit (the roots of unity [4m]_q=0), which is the input to the Stokes/resurgence analysis, independent of the connection being resurgent rather than a coefficient re-basing.

    source
    theorem QOU.QBeta.qInt_root_vanishes_iff_dvd {ζ : ℂ} {m : ℕ} (hm : 1 < m) (hζ : IsPrimitiveRoot ζ m) (n : ℕ) :
    (ζ ^ n - 1) / (ζ - 1) = 0 ↔ m ∣ n

    The quantum integer [n]_q = (q^n − 1)/(q − 1) vanishes at a primitive m-th root of unity ζ (with m > 1) exactly when m ∣ n. This is the Wenzl–Pochhammer boundary structure: the roots of unity at which the Habiro tower truncates.

    source
    theorem QOU.QBeta.qInt_fourm_vanishes_at_zeta_fourm {ζ : ℂ} {m : ℕ} (hm : 0 < m) (hζ : IsPrimitiveRoot ζ (4 * m)) :
    (ζ ^ (4 * m) - 1) / (ζ - 1) = 0

    The Habiro-tower boundaries [4m]_q = 0 at ζ_{4m} (the [4]_q=0 / [8]_q=0 / … annihilators): a primitive 4m-th root of unity kills [4m]_q.
