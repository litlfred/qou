          theorem like_nucleon_rates_coincide {q : ℝ} (_hq : 1 < q) :

          — literally X = X, closed by rfl, with the hypothesis bound under a _ because nothing used it. It named the surviving claim and asserted none of it: 1/q² = 1/q² holds for every real q and says nothing about nucleon bonds. That is the tautological conclusion AGENTS.md §5 bans, and it was found by proof-no-unused-hypothesis-rfl in the repo's vacuity audit.

          The electromagnetic ratio #

          source
          theorem QOU.QBeta.tanh_log_eq {q : ℝ} (hq : 0 < q) :
          Real.tanh (Real.log q) = (q ^ 2 - 1) / (q ^ 2 + 1)

          tanh(ln q) = (q² − 1)/(q² + 1) for q > 0. This is the coefficient that turns the colour weight δ into the p–n bond's departure from −1, and at the substrate pin it is 0.103963710 — the odd-to-even ratio interaction-sector-taxonomy.md derives for the electromagnetic coupling.
