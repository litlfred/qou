    S(π) = −π²/2, assembled — bean qou-asem #

    Four proved pieces, composed:

    piece statement
    extended_sum Σₖ (2·extend L(wₖ) + extend L(−wₖ)) = π²/2
    extend_eq_re_cRogersL extend = Re of the analytic Rogers L (qou-rstq item 4)
    levelSeven_orbit_data the wₖ are the level-7 roots, negated
    corr_sum_eq_neg_pi_sq Σⱼ corr yⱼ = −π²

    giving Σⱼ Re V(−1, yⱼ) = π²/2 − π² = −π²/2.

    Why this could not be written before today #

    extended_sum is about the corpus's piecewise extend; deformedV is built from the analytic cLiTwoExt. Until extend_eq_re_cRogersL (item 4) those were different objects and the composition did not typecheck, let alone hold. Route 32 G9 existed precisely to assert that gap, and is now rewritten.

    ⚠ What this is NOT #

    S(0) = −3π²/2 is the 5₂ sum rule; S(π) = −π²/2 is its value at the other endpoint, and S(0) = 3·S(π). So this assembles one endpoint of a transport that reformulates the sum rule rather than approaching it. It is a composition of proved parts, and the parts were the work.

    Still open and untouched here: Gap A (qou-glbl), the globally differentiable root labelling — the one remaining hypothesis in the transport that would connect S(π) to S(0).

    The 5₂ sum rule is PROVED: QOU.QBeta.FiveTwoComplexPentagon.fiveTwo_saddle_sum_rule and fiveTwoSaddleSumRule_holds, sorry-free, standard axioms.
