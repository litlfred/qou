    Faddeev–Volkov rigidity reduces to holomorphicity of the ratio (FaddeevPhi-specific) #

    The FaddeevPhi-specific bridge into the generic doubly-periodic Liouville result QOU.MathlibExt.DoublyPeriodicLiouville.ratio_const_of_doublyPeriodic (which proves: two entire functions with nowhere-zero denominator and a ratio periodic under an ℝ-basis are proportional). That generic lemma takes both period identities as input; this file supplies the extra FaddeevPhi-specific content — the compact ib-period of the ratio is not an assumption, it is derived from nowhere-zero via the quasi-period prefactor (phi_b_quasiperiod, ratio_ib_periodic). So a caller need only supply the dual i/b-period, the entireness, and the basis.

    Convergence note. The generic elliptic-Liouville step (const_of_doublyPeriodic) and its ratio bridge were landed independently on origin/main (DoublyPeriodicLiouville.lean) while this branch developed a parallel EllipticLiouville.lean; per the reuse-and-simplify discipline the duplicate was removed and this file now consumes the upstream lemma. The two independent proofs of the same Liouville step are a confirmation, not waste.

    What remains open (anti-laundering, explicit). Unchanged: (i) Φ_b holomorphic + divisor cancellation so the solutions are entire (the true Φ_b is meromorphic — this is the idealized nowhere-zero-entire setting), (ii) the basis non-degeneracy b² ∉ ℝ (substrate arg b = π/4; fails for real b), (iii) the i/b-period everywhere (identity theorem off the dual pole lattice). Nothing here is axiomatized.

    Verified lean-direct against pinned Mathlib (exit 0).

    ∃ (c : ℂ), ∀ (z : ℂ), Ψ z = c * Φ z

    Reduction of Faddeev–Volkov rigidity to entireness of the two solutions. For two FaddeevPhi solutions Ψ, Φ at the same b with Φ nowhere zero and both entire, ∃ c, Ψ = c·Φ follows from a basis bas of ℂ realizing the two periods ⟨ib, i/b⟩ plus the dual i/b-periodicity of the ratio. The compact ib-periodicity is derived here (nowhere-zero forces the quasi-period prefactor nonzero), then upstream ratio_const_of_doublyPeriodic finishes.
