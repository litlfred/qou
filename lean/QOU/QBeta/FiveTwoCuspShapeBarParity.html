    The 5₂ cusp shapes in bar-parity coordinates — two cubic classes, and Φ₆ reappears #

    The 5₂ analogue of QOU.QBeta.CuspShapeBarParity, which does the same job for the figure-eight. That file records the 4₁ complete structure — both ideal tetrahedra at the sixth root of unity ω, Φ₆(ω) = ω² − ω + 1 = 0, trace field ℚ(√−3) — and reads its bar-parity split barEven ω = 1, (barOdd ω)² = −3 as the 𝔙 = Vol + i·CS split.

    5₂ is not that. Measured from its complete hyperbolic structure (snappy, 300-bit shapes, verified below to ~1e-61):

    • it has three ideal tetrahedra, and two of them share a shape: z₁ = z₃ ≠ z₂ — exactly, not approximately;

    • neither shape is a root of unity. Both are cubic, with different minimal polynomials over ℚ:

      A-shape (z₁ = z₃):   z³ − 2z² + 3z − 1 = 0
B-shape (z₂):        z³ − 2z² +  z − 1 = 0

    • both cubics have discriminant −23 and signature (r₁,r₂) = (1,1), so both generate the cubic field of discriminant −23 — the same field, by two different generators. The signature independently reproduces the (1,1) that dt_d1b_trace_field_bar_grading_probe measured for 5₂ by Sturm counting.

    This file states the bar-parity coordinates of both shapes over a generic field — these are polynomial identities in the shape relation, with no archimedean content, so §7c keeps them off ℝ.

    What the file establishes, and the one thing worth noticing #

    Reducing z⁻¹ against each cubic (the constant term is −1, so z is a unit with a polynomial inverse):

    z⁻¹ barEven z = z + z⁻¹ barOdd z = z − z⁻¹
    A z² − 2z + 3 z² − z + 3 −z² + 3z − 3
    B (z − 1)² z² − z + 1 −z² + 3z − 1

    The B-shape's bar-even coordinate is z² − z + 1 = Φ₆(z) — the very polynomial whose vanishing defines the 4₁ cusp shape. For 4₁, Φ₆(ω) = 0; for 5₂'s B-shape, Φ₆ is not zero but is the bar-even coordinate. Both statements are proved here (shape52B_barEven, and cyclotomic_six for the naming).

    Stated as an identity, NOT as an interpretation. It is a two-line consequence of z⁻¹ = (z−1)², which is itself a two-line consequence of the B-cubic. Whether the reappearance of Φ₆ across the two knots means anything is not addressed here and is not claimed; recording it is cheap, and reading significance into an algebraic coincidence is the failure mode this arc has hit repeatedly.

    And a genuine contrast with 4₁, which is the substantive difference. For the figure-eight barEven ω = 1 — rational, which is what makes the bar-even coordinate the anchor-stable −Vol(4₁) part. For 5₂ neither shape has rational barEven: both cubics are irreducible over ℚ (verified in the probe by the rational-root test), so each barEven lies in the cubic field. So the 4₁ story does not transcribe, and the bar-even/bar-odd split for 5₂ lands in a cubic field rather than in ℚ plus √−3.

          theorem QOU.QBeta.FiveTwoCuspShapeBarParity.shape52A_not_shape52B {R : Type u_1} [Field R] {z : R} (htwo : 2 ≠ 0) (hA : IsShape52A z) :

          The two 5₂ shapes are distinct away from characteristic 2: subtracting the cubics forces 2z = 0, and with 2 ≠ 0 that gives z = 0, which shape52A_ne_zero excludes. This is the algebraic form of the measured z₁ = z₃ ≠ z₂. The 2 ≠ 0 hypothesis is NOT decoration — see the next theorem.

          source
          theorem QOU.QBeta.FiveTwoCuspShapeBarParity.shape52_cubics_agree_in_char_two {S : Type u_2} [Field S] (h : 2 = 0) (z : S) :
          z ^ 3 - 2 * z ^ 2 + 3 * z - 1 = z ^ 3 - 2 * z ^ 2 + z - 1

          The hypothesis is necessary, and Lean is what caught that. In characteristic 2 the two cubics COINCIDE — −2z² = 0 and 3z = z — so both read z³ + z + 1 = 0 and a single element satisfies both. The distinctness of the 5₂ shape classes is a characteristic-0 fact about this knot, not a formal consequence of the two polynomials being different as symbols.
