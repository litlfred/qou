    The Faddeev quasi-period lattice ⟨ib, i/b⟩ is non-degenerate iff b² ∉ ℝ #

    Discharges the hspan input of QOU.QBeta.FaddeevRigidityReduction: the two quasi-periods of the Φ_b ratio, I·b (compact) and I/b (modular-dual), form a genuine ℝ-basis of ℂ exactly when b² ∉ ℝ — i.e. (b^2).im ≠ 0. This is the substrate non-degeneracy condition: it holds on the physical branch arg b = π/4 (where b² is purely imaginary) and fails for real b (the |q| = 1 regime), where both periods lie on the imaginary axis and the lattice collapses — so Faddeev–Volkov rigidity genuinely requires the substrate branch, not just any b.
