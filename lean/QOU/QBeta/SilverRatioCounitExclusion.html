            theorem QOU.QBeta.epsNP_silver :
            epsNP silver = 0

            epsNP vanishes at the silver ratio. This is what makes the exclusion run: the target of the search is zero at a point where every candidate is a product of units.

            theorem QOU.QBeta.bracket2half_silver :
            bracket2half silver = √2

            [2]_q/2 = √2 exactly at the silver ratio. The second evaluation the argument turns on: it is what makes the right-hand side of the forced equation a power of √2 rather than of an arbitrary real.

            theorem QOU.QBeta.chiBlock3_silver_ne_zero :
            chiBlock3 silver ≠ 0

            CONTROL — chiBlock3 does NOT vanish at the silver ratio.

            chiBlock3 is a difference of two typed-word counits (namely q¹·([2]_q/2)² − q³·([2]_q/2)²), so if the exclusion argument also excluded it the argument would be proving too much. It does not: the value here is −12 − 8√2 ≠ 0.

            source
            theorem QOU.QBeta.silver_separates_target_from_control :
            epsNP silver = 0 ∧ chiBlock3 silver ≠ 0

            The two evaluations together, as the single statement the exclusion cites: at the silver ratio the target vanishes while the control does not.
