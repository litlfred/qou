    theorem QOU.QBeta.CharacterDoubling.char_doubling_of_constant_residue (c q : ℝ) :
    Real.cosh (c * Real.log (q ^ 2)) = 2 * Real.cosh (c * Real.log q) ^ 2 - 1

    Every constant-residue character satisfies the doubling identity, for every c. This is the hyperbolic analogue of ln(q²) = 2 ln q.

    The contrapositive, quantified over all c at once: a function failing the doubling identity at a single positive point is the character of no constant-residue holonomy.

    The second survivor is vacuous #

    Excluding the constant-residue case leaves "a q-dependent residue" as a survivor. It carries no content: cosh is onto [1, ∞), so wherever the requirement satisfies 1 ≤ κ a residue reproducing it exists by construction — one defines a(q) := arcosh(κ(q)) from the answer. Measured, κ on the z_D base is ≥ 1 throughout q ∈ (1, 10] (1.5333 at q → 1⁺, rising monotonically to 8.0243 at q = 10), so the vacuity is not conditional in the physical domain.

    This is the same disposition EigenphaseCarrierVacuity gives the elliptic trichotomy's middle branch ("M arbitrary — CIRCULAR"), reached independently on the hyperbolic side. Only the third branch — a residue whose q-dependence is derived rather than read off the requirement — can carry content.
