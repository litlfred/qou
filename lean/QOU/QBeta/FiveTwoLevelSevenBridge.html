    The level-7 bridge: the correction is −π², and it is one arg #

    Bean qou-trns. The transport gives S(π) − S(0) = π², and S(0) is the 5₂ sum rule (−3π²/2, Route 32). So the sum rule reduces to evaluating S(π), which sits at x = −1 where the curve's cubic is

    unitCubic (−1) y = y³ + 2y² − y − 1 ,

    the level-7 cubic. There V(−1, y) = Li₂(y) + 2·Li₂(−y) + log(−y)², and the already-proved cyclotomic identity extended_sum reads Σⱼ Re[2L(−yⱼ) + L(yⱼ)] = +π²/2 once wₖ = −yⱼ — which it does, exactly.

    What separates the two is a log-bilinear correction with no dilogarithms in it:

    corr y = Re[ log(−y)² − log(−y)·log(1+y) − ½·log(y)·log(1−y) ] .

    Route 32 measures Σⱼ corr yⱼ = −π² to 2e-50. This file proves it.

    Why it collapses #

    Per root, the log-times-log part factors as

    log|y| · ( log|y| − log|1+y| − ½·log(1−y) )

    and the second factor vanishes because

    2·log|y| = 2·log|1+y| + log(1−y)   ⟺   y² = (1+y)²(1−y)
                                    ⟺   y³ + 2y² − y − 1 = 0 ,

    which is the cubic itself (sq_eq_of_root). So every root contributes nothing from the logarithms.

    What is left is arguments. At a negative root every factor's argument cancels out of the real part, giving 0. At the unique positive root t ∈ (0,1), −t is a negative real, log(−t) = log t + iπ, and Re[log(−t)²] = (log t)² − π² contributes a bare −π².

    So the entire correction is one argument at one root — the same −t-on-the- cut that produced corrections 5 and 6 in this branch. Third appearance, and this time it is not a defect but the answer.

    What this does and does not close #

    It closes the arithmetic between S(π) and extended_sum. It does not close the sum rule: extended_sum is stated for the corpus's piecewise extend, and identifying that with Re of the analytic Rogers L is qou-rstq item 4 — open, on paper only, and sitting directly on this bridge.

    The 5₂ sum rule is PROVED: QOU.QBeta.FiveTwoComplexPentagon.fiveTwo_saddle_sum_rule and fiveTwoSaddleSumRule_holds, sorry-free, standard axioms.

      3b. The orbit: extended_sum's arguments ARE the level-7 roots, negated #

      extended_sum is stated for the orbit {1/(1−x), x, (x−1)/x} of a root x of x³ − 2x² − x + 1. The bridge is about roots of y³ + 2y² − y − 1. These are the same configuration: y ↦ −y carries one cubic to the other, so the six arguments extended_sum uses are the level-7 roots and their negatives.

      Proved here rather than assumed, because "the same up to sign" is exactly the kind of step that turns out to be off by a reciprocal.
