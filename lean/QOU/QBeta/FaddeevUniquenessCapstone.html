    A_ω uniqueness leg — the conditional capstone (same divisor ⇒ proportional) #

    Composes the three proved pieces of the Faddeev–Volkov uniqueness leg into one statement, discharging the hr_entire input via brick (c):

    • brick (c) RatioEntire.entire_toMeromorphicNFOn_div_of_same_order — same divisor ⇒ the normal-form ratio r = toMeromorphicNFOn (Φ₁/Φ₂) is entire;
    • the concrete-Φ_b reduction FaddeevPhiUniquenessAssembly.faddeevPhi_proportional_of_ratio_entire — entire ratio + the two FaddeevPhi quasi-period periodicities ⇒ proportional.

    The result: two FaddeevPhi solutions with the same divisor are proportional on Φ₂'s nonvanishing locus. The hr_entire wall is gone (brick (c)); what remains are the honestly-named residual analytic inputs, carried as explicit hypotheses, none axiomatised:

    1. hsame — the same-divisor premise. Not derivable from the FaddeevPhi class (FaddeevPhiEllipticFreedom.faddeevPhi_mul_elliptic: an elliptic factor gives a solution with a different divisor); it needs a strip-growth strengthening or the PhiBProd divisor.
    2. hr_ratio — the normal-form representative agrees with Φ₁/Φ₂ pointwise on Φ₂'s nonvanishing locus. This needs the solutions to be in meromorphic normal form (no 0/0 junk at removable points) — a second property the total-function class does not encode.
    3. hS₀/hS₁ + hmem₀/hmem₁ — the periodicity loci are dense and lie off Φ₂'s zeros (the Φ_b zero-lattice discreteness, PhiBProd-gated).

    So the leg is reduced to exactly these three named analytic facts about the concrete Φ_b — the maximal reduction achievable at the class level. Verified lean-direct against the Meromorphic cone (exit 0).
