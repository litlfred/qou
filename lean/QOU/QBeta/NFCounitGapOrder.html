        theorem QOU.QBeta.NFCounitGap.gap_at_one :
        epsTyped 1 - epsSingleWrap 1 = 3 / 2

        The gap at h = 1 is exactly 3/2 — 1 − (−1/2), matching the closed form's cofactor (1+2+2+2−1)/4 = 3/2. (Not −1/4: that is the cofactor's value at h = 0, a different quantity, and Lean caught the conflation.)

        theorem QOU.QBeta.NFCounitGap.gap_dvd_h_sq {K : Type u_1} [Field K] (h2 : 2 ≠ 0) (h : K) :
        ∃ (c : K), epsTyped h - epsSingleWrap h = h ^ 2 * c

        The consequence for the acceptance test. Any quantity of the form h³ · c vanishes to third order, so for the purposes of a Taylor comparison pinned at first order and predicting second order, the gap is invisible: substituting h = 0 kills it, as does its first and second derivative data. Stated concretely as: the gap is a multiple of h³, hence of h², hence of h — each of which is the algebraic content of "contributes nothing below order three".
