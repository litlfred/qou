    theorem QOU.QBeta.trace_conj_of_mul_eq_one {n : Type u_1} [Fintype n] [DecidableEq n] {R : Type u_2} [CommRing R] (P Q M : Matrix n n R) (h : Q * P = 1) :
    (P * M * Q).trace = M.trace

    Conjugation preserves the trace. Stated with a one-sided inverse so it needs no Invertible instance: Q * P = 1 is all the gauge equivalence supplies, and all the argument uses.

    This is the whole of why branch 3's monodromy reading closes. A connection whose monodromy reproduces the Hecke braiding has holonomies of the form P * ρ(w) * Q with Q * P = 1; their traces are therefore exactly tr ρ(w), which the probe measures to be Laurent polynomials in q. The carrier needs a transcendental trace, so no such monodromy is a carrier.

    theorem QOU.QBeta.det_even_word (k : ℕ) :
    (-1) ^ (2 * k) = 1

    Every generator of the braid image has det = −1 in the (2,1) block (measured in the probe), so an even-length word has det = +1 exactly. The carrier's M.det = 1 field is automatic on the braid image — it was never the obstruction; the trace field is.

    theorem QOU.QBeta.det_odd_word (k : ℕ) :
    (-1) ^ (2 * k + 1) = -1

    And an odd-length word has det = −1, so it cannot meet the det = 1 field at all — the braid image splits cleanly by parity.
