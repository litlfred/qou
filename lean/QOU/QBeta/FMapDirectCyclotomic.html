    QOU.QBeta.FMapDirectCyclotomic #

    This file formalizes the Direct Cyclotomic Map (Option 3) which maps the Habiro integer coefficients $a_m(u)$ to the $c_k$ precision ladder.

    Because the total trace difference $\Delta\text{tr}_M(q)$ introduces an $\mathcal{O}(w^{-1})$ pole relative to the geometric net-winding term, the f-map cannot act by scalar division. Instead, it acts functorially on the cyclotomic expansion: $\Delta\text{tr}_M = \sum a_m(u) (u; u)_m$ to produce the infinite product $\prod (1 + c_k u^k)$.

    Crucially, the map must respect the index-alternating bar-parity of the Habiro basis proved in ZBarParity.lean to preserve the $x=1$ Kashaev double shadow.

      The bar-parity descent dichotomy (KC17): fibre → c_k only at ζ₄ #

      The cyclotomicLadderExtract above is a placeholder; the substantive question it stands in for is the dichotomy proved below — does the u-side cyclotomic re-basing carry the fibre bar-parity to the c_k rungs, or is the odd channel purely archimedean? The two proved twists (ZBarParity.lean) are:

      • q-side Habiro basis (q;q)_k: the index-alternating parity (−1)^k (pochNum_bar_twist, KC16);
      • u-side ladder monomial u^k: the degree-dependent scaling twist (q²)^k (uPow_bar_twist, KC17; bar(u) = q²·u).

      "The re-basing transports the parity" means these agree at every rung: (q²)^k = (−1)^k. The theorems below show this holds iff q² = −1 — the ζ₄ point where the odd Hopf-cubic carrier lives (KC20 / parity–chirality dictionary) — and that at any real substrate q₀ it never holds. That is the KC17 obstruction as a proved dichotomy: the odd channel enters through the archimedean evaluation, not a fibre basis change. Division-free, generic CommRing; derives no c_k value, wires nothing, changes no tier.
