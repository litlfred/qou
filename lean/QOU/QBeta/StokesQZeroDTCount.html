    QOU.QBeta.StokesQZeroDTCount — the q→0 Stokes/DT constant of 4₁ is exactly 3 #

    This is the base-case (q→0) brick of the G3 Stokes keystone (bean qou-ujmw): it proves, by a decidable integer-matrix computation, that the figure-eight Stokes constant A_ω read at the semiclassical limit q → 0 is the integer 3 — the Donaldson–Thomas / rotated-3d-index invariant of T[4₁], not an assumed value.

    What is proved here (and what is NOT) #

    The Garoufalidis–Gu–Mariño ("Peacock patterns…", 2012.00062) Stokes matrix of the figure-eight knot is the eq-(59a) sandwich S⁺ = ±½·A·W₋₁·B·W₋₁ᵀ·C, where W₋₁(q) is the fundamental-solution matrix of the GGM q-difference equation (Theorem 9) and A,B,C are small integer matrices whose glyph-verified magnitudes are |A|=[[0,1],[1,1]], |B|=[[0,1],[1,0]], |C|=[[0,1],[1,2]]. At the semiclassical limit the q-series W₋₁(q) collapses to the exact integer matrix W₋₁(0) = [[0,-2],[1,1]] (its determinant 2 is GGM Theorem 9 at q→0, det W_m = 2), and the whole sandwich evaluates to -2·S⁺(0) with S⁺(0) = [[1,3],[0,1]] (GGM eq 60). The off-diagonal entry — the q→0 Stokes / DT constant — is exactly 3.

    • detW0 — det W₋₁(0) = 2. Falsifiable base of GGM Theorem 9 at q→0.
    • ggm_sandwich_qzero — every entry of the eq-(59a) sandwich A·W₋₁·B·W₋₁ᵀ·C at q→0 equals -2·S⁺(0) with S⁺(0)=[[1,3],[0,1]].
    • sandwich_offdiagonal_qzero — its (0,1) entry is -6 = (-2)·3; with the -½ normalization the Stokes/DT constant is 3.

    Scope (honest, anti-laundering). This brick verifies GGM eq 60 as a decidable computation from the DT building blocks — it reduces "A_ω = 3 at q→0" to the single concrete input W₋₁(0) = [[0,-2],[1,1]] (a certified q→0 evaluation of the GGM/DGG q-series, computations/probes/qbeta_g3_ggm_stokes_41.py, det = 2). It does NOT derive 3 from the 13 QOU categorical drivers — that internal derivation stays open (qou-qnnq; docs/audits/2026-08-01-3bp8-… §4). It also does not carry the q-dependent extension: that the full q-series symmetry constant (S₁₂+S₂₁)/S₁₁ = 3 holds at every q is the companion conditional lemma QOU.QBeta.StokesSymmetryConstant.symmetryConstant_const; this file supplies its unconditional q→0 base value. The value→observable normalization (Im 𝔙_q → N_odd, magnitude A′ ≈ 2.37) is separately open (qou-2cvs).

    Matrices are hand-rolled Fin 2 → Fin 2 → ℤ (elementary, decide-checkable); Mathlib.Data.Matrix.Notation is deliberately avoided so the file verifies on the minimal restored olean set.
