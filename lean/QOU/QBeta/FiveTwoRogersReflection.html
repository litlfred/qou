    Rogers reflection, from the corpus's real LiTwo #

    Bean qou-rhyp, item 1 — discharging four of the nine hypotheses that FiveTwoLevelSevenLadder.lean takes abstractly.

    FiveTwoLevelSevenLadder.numerators_of_five_term assumes L (1 − x) = L1 − L x at four points. With Rogers L built from the corpus's real dilogarithm this is not a new analytic fact: it follows from LiTwo_reflection', which is already sorry-free in QOU/Archimedean/DilogReflection.lean.

    The reason it is free is a cancellation worth stating. Euler reflection carries a correction term,

    LiTwo x + LiTwo (1 − x) = π²/6 − log x · log (1 − x),

    and Rogers' two half log·log terms add to a whole one:

    L x + L (1 − x)
  = LiTwo x + LiTwo (1 − x) + ½·log x·log(1−x) + ½·log(1−x)·log x
  = LiTwo x + LiTwo (1 − x) + log x·log(1−x)
  = π²/6.

    The correction cancels exactly. That is the whole content, and it is why the Rogers normalisation is the one in which the level-7 ladder has rational values.

    Namespaces, since they differ and this cost a build. LiTwo is declared in QOU.QPochhammer (DilogarithmArchimedeanLimit.lean), but LiTwo_reflection' and the hasDerivAt_* lemmas are in QOU.Archimedean (DilogReflection.lean, which itself does open QOU.QPochhammer). Both opens are needed here.

    Scope. This discharges reflection only. The inversion and Landen extensions and the five-term relation — the other five hypotheses — remain open; see qou-rhyp. The 5₂ sum rule is PROVED: QOU.QBeta.FiveTwoComplexPentagon.fiveTwo_saddle_sum_rule and fiveTwoSaddleSumRule_holds, sorry-free, standard axioms.
