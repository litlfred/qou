    theorem QOU.QBeta.LadderUniformBound.prod_one_add_mul_le_one (l : List ℝ) (h : (List.map abs l).sum < 1) :
    (List.map (fun (x : ℝ) => 1 + x) l).prod * (1 - (List.map abs l).sum) ≤ 1

    Weierstrass product bound, division-free form. A finite product ∏(1 + xᵢ) whose terms have total absolute weight S < 1 satisfies ∏(1 + xᵢ) · (1 - S) ≤ 1. No sign hypothesis on the xᵢ, and no division anywhere — the 1/(1-S) reading is the immediate consequence and is stated in the docstring of ladder_lt_of_sum_bound rather than in a type.

    The contrapositive an agent actually wants: a ladder that DOES reach F forces a total absolute rung weight above S. Reading the corpus numbers into it is the probe's job, not this file's.

    The same exclusion by an independent route, via Mathlib #

    The corpus-grep checklist turned up Finset.norm_prod_one_add_sub_one_le, which bounds ‖∏(1 + fᵢ) - 1‖ by exp(∑‖fᵢ‖) - 1 with no hypotheses — neither smallness nor sign. Composing it with the same |cos| ≤ 1 cap gives the exclusion again, from a bound this file did not prove.

    Two properties are worth noting. It needs no S < 1 hypothesis, so it still says something where the Weierstrass form goes vacuous. And it routes through |·| rather than through positivity of the product, so it never needs prod_one_add_pos — which is what lets it drop the smallness assumption.
