        Prop

        Conjecture (primary, OPEN). The Masbaum cyclotomic grading of the 5₂ tail is the image of the GGM block grading under a level-mixing map that is not identically zero.

        No instance is provided: the conjecture is open, and a fabricated instance would assert exactly what is unknown. The non-degeneracy field is what makes the statement have content — without it the zero map satisfies the intertwining whenever mas ≡ 0.

        • exists_nondegenerate_map : ∃ (L : LevelMap R), L.Intertwines mas blk ∧ ∃ (n : ℕ) (m : ℕ), L.coeff n m ≠ 0
