    The P hypothesis, discharged for an arbitrary truncation order #

    ladder_lt_of_one_le takes P ∈ (0, 1] as a hypothesis, and rung_lt_one shows this corpus's rungs are each below 1 — which left the join between the two to be made numerically, per truncation order. #7276 asked whether the product bound was formalized or discharged by measurement; it was the latter. It is now the former.

    prod_pos_le_one is the induction: a finite list of reals each in (0, 1] has its product in (0, 1]. ladder_lt_of_rungs composes it with the cap, so the exclusion holds at every truncation order with no numerical step at all — the measured half of the argument is now only about which c_k the corpus records, never about what their product does.
