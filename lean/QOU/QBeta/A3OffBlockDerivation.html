    QOU.QBeta.A3OffBlockDerivation — deriving the A=3 super-matrix off-blocks from the Hecke/Wedderburn branching, and the structural bound that EXCLUDES the observed lift #

    Scope (read this first). This file attacks the wall that ROUTE 2 (QOU.QBeta.SuperBerezinianA3Magnitude, PR #4544) relocated: "derive the q-cubic off-diagonal blocks B, C of the graded super-matrix from first principles." Route 2 proved that treating B as a free scalar makes the even-face correction (A − B·D⁻¹·C)/A surjective, so hitting r* ≈ 1.585 that way is a fit. Here we do NOT treat B as free: we derive the off-blocks from the actual A=3 categorical data (the H₃(q) Wedderburn branching + the super-sector grading) and prove what that derivation actually yields.

    The categorical input (the A=3 branching data — all established corpus structure).

    • H₃(q) is semisimple with three Wedderburn blocks, the partitions of 3: (3) (trivial rep, dim 1), (2,1) (standard, dim 2), (1,1,1) (sign, dim 1); 1 + 4 + 1 = 6 = 3!.
    • The bar-involution q ↦ q⁻¹ acts on the blocks as the transpose (conjugate partition, = the sign-twist / keystone T₁↔T₂ parity, option-e.md 2026-06-16): it fixes the self-conjugate (2,1) and swaps (3) ↔ (1,1,1).
    • The super-sector grading is that transpose involution's ±1 eigendecomposition: the bar-EVEN (mass / C1 / cosh) face is the +1 eigenspace (e_{(2,1)} and e₊ = (e_{(3)}+e_{(1,1,1)})/√2); the bar-ODD (GGM / decay / sinh) face is the −1 eigenspace (e₋ = (e_{(3)}−e_{(1,1,1)})/√2).
    • The mass endomorphism 𝓜 (Re 𝔙 = binding) is bar-self-adjoint (BerezinianSelfAdjointCrossTerm) — diagonal 𝓜 = diag(a, c, b) in the Wedderburn basis, with a = m_{(3)}, c = m_{(2,1)}, b = m_{(1,1,1)} its block contributions.

    The derivation (Part 2). Rotating the self-adjoint 𝓜 into the transpose eigenbasis is a fixed orthogonal change of basis — it does not leave B free. On the (3)↔(1,1,1) 2-cycle the symmetric-under-swap rotation e₊,e₋ gives, with no remaining freedom,

    even (2,2)-entry = (a+b)/2, odd block D = (a+b)/2, off-blocks B = C = (a−b)/2 (the (2,1) block decouples).

    So the even-face Berezinian correction ((A − B·D⁻¹·C)/A on the e₊ mass face) is the derived quantity

    r(a,b) = 1 − ((a−b)/(a+b))² (magnitudeFactor_eq).

    The result (Part 3 — scope-(b) structural exclusion, invariant cited).

    magnitudeFactor_le_one (sorry-free): for ALL real diagonal blocks a, b (a+b ≠ 0), r(a,b) = 1 − ((a−b)/(a+b))² ≤ 1. The self-adjoint Hecke/Wedderburn branching Schur complement is a REDUCTION, never a lift.

    The observed A=3 factor is a genuine LIFT r* = μ₃/c₃ ≈ 1.585 > 1 (the single Markov trace c₃ = 0.297 is bracketed short of the AME moment μ₃ = 0.4708). By observed_lift_unreachable, the branching-derived factor never equals any r > 1 — in particular r* and the whole Route-5 acceptance band [1.50, 1.71] are structurally unreachable. The cited invariant is square non-negativity (sq_nonneg): r > 1 would force a real square to be negative.

    Why (Part 5 — the parity certificate). The branching off-blocks are equal (B = C, symmetric real rotation) and, since the two 1-dim blocks are bar-conjugate (b = σ a: trivial/sign Markov weights are q↔q⁻¹ conjugate — the equal Borromean characters, Part 1), bar-ODD (σB = −B). Hence C = −σ B: the assembly is graded self-adjoint (BerezinianSelfAdjointCrossTerm.selfadjoint_crossTerm_bar_even ⇒ its cross-term is bar-EVEN, the correct parity for the bar-even ratio r*, yet provably bounded ≤ 1). A r* > 1 lift therefore requires anti-self-adjoint (C ≠ −σ B, two INDEPENDENT amplitudes) off-blocks — precisely the GGM / Stokes datum the branching does NOT determine. This is the honest under-determination: the categorical branching fixes the bar-even, self-adjoint sector (bounded ≤ 1); the bar-odd anti-self-adjoint amplitude that the observed lift needs is left FREE.

    Net verdict — scope-(b) on the self-adjoint branching route; the derivation of B,C from branching does NOT close r*. It sharpens Route 2: the branching data is not merely silent on B (Route 2), it positively supplies the wrong-sign (reduction, ≤ 1) self-adjoint off-block; the lift is carried entirely by the anti-self-adjoint off-block (conj:qdifference-kernel-prolongation-symbol, bean qou-jqof), recorded as the §3b dependent class A3LiftFromDerivedOffBlock with NO instance.     Part 1 — the branching data: the H₃(q) Borromean characters on the transpose #
