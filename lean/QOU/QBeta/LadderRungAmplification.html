    The ladder's extracted third rung as a function of the N=1 cosine.

    Mirrors c3 = (Q_codata / Q_N2 − 1) / u³ with Q_N2 = Q_A · cs · (1 − u²), which is the extraction in qbeta_ck_cache.py. cs stands for cos(α_eff): it is the only place a Markov convention enters, since Q_A, b and u are built from the Hoefsmit couplings.

      The amplification identity.

      The gap between the rungs extracted under two conventions is the gap between their cosine reciprocals, scaled by Q_c/(Q_A(1−u²)) and divided by u³. Nothing else contributes.

      The amplification factor is exactly u⁻³.

      Multiplying the rung gap by u³ leaves a quantity with no u-scaling in it at all beyond the (1 − u²) prefactor — so the 1/u³ = 1.6×10⁶ at the substrate anchor is the entire magnification, not a coincidence of scale.

      A rung gap is nonzero exactly when the cosines differ (given a nonvanishing overall scale). So an enormous c₃ gap carries no information beyond "the cosines differ" — the size of it is u⁻³, not evidence about the conventions.
