    theorem QOU.QBeta.reeb_tower_sinh_three_term (a η : ℝ) :
    Real.sinh (a + η) + Real.sinh (a - η) = 2 * Real.cosh η * Real.sinh a

    Bar-ODD tower three-term identity. sinh(a+η) + sinh(a−η) = 2 cosh η · sinh a. At a = kη this is the recursion for the odd Reeb tower sinh(kη) (the c_3 sector).

    theorem QOU.QBeta.reeb_rotation_sin_three_term (a θ : ℝ) :
    Real.sin (a + θ) + Real.sin (a - θ) = 2 * Real.cos θ * Real.sin a

    Rotation (J_ν) analogue. The same three-term recurrence holds for sin with multiplier cos θ: sin(a+θ) + sin(a−θ) = 2 cos θ · sin a. The rotation tower sin(kθ) and the growth tower sinh(kη) solve one operator p_{k+1} = 2t·p_k − p_{k−1}, with t = cos θ (|t| ≤ 1) vs t = cosh η (t ≥ 1).

    Growth regime (I_ν): the multiplier is ≥ 1. 1 ≤ cosh η always, so the growth-tower recurrence multiplier t = cosh η lies in the unbounded (I_ν, arccosh) regime.

    theorem QOU.QBeta.reeb_growth_hyperbola (η : ℝ) :
    Real.cosh η ^ 2 - Real.sinh η ^ 2 = 1

    The conic distinction. The growth tower lives on the hyperbola cosh²η − sinh²η = 1 (the I_ν world); the rotation tower lives on the circle cos²θ + sin²θ = 1 (the J_ν world). Same recurrence, two conics — the elementary shadow of I_ν(x) = i^{−ν} J_ν(i x).
