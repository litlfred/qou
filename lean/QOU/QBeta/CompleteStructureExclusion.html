    0 < (PhiBSArgumentIdentification.SDeformedVolumeChannel.u S₁ q₀).re ^ 2 - (PhiBSArgumentIdentification.SDeformedVolumeChannel.u S₁ q₀).im ^ 2 ∧ (PhiBSArgumentIdentification.SDeformedVolumeChannel.u S₂ q₀).re ^ 2 - (PhiBSArgumentIdentification.SDeformedVolumeChannel.u S₂ q₀).im ^ 2 < 0

    The sign flip needs more than nonvanishing. Im V₄₁ is positive inside the cone |Im u| < |Re u| and negative outside it, so two anchors of OPPOSITE leak sign must lie on opposite sides of the diagonal |Re u| = |Im u| — not merely away from 0.

    Recorded as the sharper A_ω-free filter that qou-8vt2 AV4 and qou-qmnb AV1 tell candidates to apply BEFORE computing any magnitude.

    Statement note. An earlier version of this claimed the stronger ordering Im₂² − Re₂² < Re₁² − Im₁², and linarith rejected it — correctly. Both quantities are positive, but opposite SIGNS of the leak give no ORDERING between their magnitudes. What is true, and all that is true, is the sidedness below.
