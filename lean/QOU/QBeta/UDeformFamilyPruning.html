    u(q₀) family pruning — the corrected harmonic-form kill-test laws #

    Formalization of the structural laws behind computations/qbeta/qbeta_udeform_map_constraint_probe.py and computations/probes/sdeformed_channel_signflip_probe.py (the u(q₀) constraint probe of the conj:binding-qbeta-complex-volume S-channel, SDeformedVolumeChannel).

    The leading form (corrected). The figure-eight (4₁) A-polynomial cusp modulus is τ = −i√3 (purely imaginary, ℚ(√−3)), so the leading Neumann–Zagier complex-volume coefficient is a₂ = i·√3/4 (purely imaginary) and the leading imaginary part of the deformed complex volume is the HARMONIC quadratic form

    Im 𝔙(u) = (√3/4)·(Re²u − Im²u)      (NOT the earlier `√3·Re u·Im u`).

    The concrete 𝔙 is QOU.Knot.FigureEightComplexVolume.V₄₁; this file feeds the family's real/imaginary components into it via familyIm x y := (V₄₁ ⟨x, y⟩).im = (√3/4)(x² − y²) (familyIm_eq) and reuses imV₄₁ / imV₄₁_pos_iff / imV₄₁_neg_iff / signflip_forces_diagonal_cross / f3_re_linear_same_sign. It does not re-define a standalone √3·x·y leading form (that was the wrong-a₂ artefact, refuted by τ = −i√3).

    All three u(q₀) kill-test families are REFUTED — each is even in d = q − q*, hence same-signed on both sides of q*, so none can produce the measured bar-odd flip S(muon) ≈ +0.00968 > 0 > S(proton) ≈ −0.03946:

    • F1 — fixed-phase (Re = cx·d, Im = cy·d): familyIm = (√3/4)(cx² − cy²)d², EVEN in d. Its product across any two substrate points is a perfect square (fixed_phase_product_nonneg), so the sign is the SAME on both sides of q* and the family CANNOT sign-flip (fixed_phase_family_excluded). A scope-(b) structural sign exclusion (invariant proved), per AGENTS.md §refuted.
    • F2 — phase-crossing (Re = a·d, Im = b·d²): familyIm = (√3/4)(a²d² − b²d⁴), EVEN in d with leading ∝ d² (the old cubic_ratio_law/∝d³ was a wrong-form artefact). Within the small excursion b²d² ≤ a² the value is ≥ 0 (phase_crossing_nonneg), its cross-anchor product is ≥ 0 (phase_crossing_product_nonneg), so the family CANNOT sign-flip near q* (phase_crossing_family_excluded).
    • F3 — transverse-constant (Re = a·d, Im = c₀): familyIm = (√3/4)((a·d)² − c₀²), EVEN in d. Within (a·d)² < c₀² the value is < 0 (transverse_const_neg, reusing f3_re_linear_same_sign), its cross-anchor product is > 0 (transverse_const_product_nonneg), so both anchors are NEGATIVE and the family CANNOT sign-flip (transverse_const_family_excluded). This REPLACES the earlier linear_family_sign_flip/linear_family_eq/hasDerivAt_linear_family, which claimed F3 sign-flips/survives — the CORE ERROR of the √3·x·y form.

    Surviving necessary condition. S = Im 𝔙 sign-flips only where Re²u − Im²u changes sign, i.e. u(q₀) must CROSS the diagonal |Re u| = |Im u| between the anchors (signflip_requires_diagonal_cross, via signflip_forces_diagonal_cross). No family with both anchors on the same side of that diagonal survives.

    Honesty boundary. What is NOT formalized: any SDeformedVolumeChannel instance. The identification of a concrete u(q₀) (and of the measured product-slope k) would wire an anchor-inverted map as canonical — a fit, forbidden by the anti-laundering rule. u(q₀) stays OPEN. These theorems are the kill-test laws over the corrected harmonic form; the measured numbers stay in computations/qbeta/qbeta-udeform-map-constraint.witness.json.
