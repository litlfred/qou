            What actually pins z_D, and what bar-parity does NOT do #

            Three conditions are in play and they do different jobs. Keeping them apart is the point of this section, because conflating them is how "bar-parity closes the gap" would get written down, and that is false.

            • the two-row cap y_(1³) = 0 — narrows to {q, z_D} (AdmissibleRoots). For the DECAY channel this is a RULING, not a derivation.
            • faithfulness y_(2,1) ≠ 0 — the corpus's own hfaith hypothesis. y_(2,1)(q) = 0 exactly, so the augmentation is the trivial-block-only trace and is not faithful. Within the admissible pair this picks z_D.
            • full-bar — derived above. Excludes z_C and the CONSTANT z ≡ 1.

            The limitation, stated so it cannot be quoted away. fullBar_add_barEven plus the fact that every constant is bar-even means z_D + c is full-bar for ANY constant c. So a full-bar parameter can take ANY value at the substrate pin, and bar-parity therefore constrains the FUNCTIONAL FORM of z(q), not its value. one_not_fullBar excludes z ≡ 1 as a choice of parameter; it does NOT exclude the required VALUE (z_required ≈ 0.99850), which a full-bar function could attain.

            Consequently the narrowing to {q, z_D} — and hence the exclusion of what the data wants — still rests on the cap, which for this channel is the ruling.

              Why the cap had to be a ruling: the decay channel is BLIND to it #

              DecayBlockCarrier proved tr(Δ_S)(z) = (y_(3) − y_(1³)) · χ^(3)(Δ_S). The decay pairing therefore depends on the block weights ONLY through the difference y_(3) − y_(1³). Together with normalisation that is two constraints on three unknowns, so a one-parameter family of normalised weight triples produces the SAME Q-value — and the family sweeps every value of y_(1³).

              So the two-row cap y_(1³) = 0 is not merely un-derived for this channel: it is undeterminable by it. No decay measurement, at any precision, can establish or refute it. It selects one member of a family the channel cannot tell apart.

              That is why it arrived as a ruling, and it says where a derivation would have to come from: another channel, or a structural argument — never from Q_β itself.
