    The ruling DECIDES the braid ordering the corpus owed #

    qou-99qc / #7188 measured that under the ruled functional at z_D the braid ORDER of the nucleon word is visible and unconstrained, and concluded that the corpus owes a derivation of it. The γγ ruling supplies one, and the lemmas below are what make it a derivation rather than a preference.

    The census carries two orderings of the same eleven-letter multiset (qbeta_delta_candidate_census_probe.py). Their confinement cores are:

    • interleaved — nuc9's for _ in range(3): σ₀ ; (σ₁ − h), and σ₁ − h = σ₁⁻¹ by the Hecke relation (QOU.QBeta.NucleonFlavour.skein_letter_inv), so the core is (σ₀σ₁⁻¹)³ — which is vertex 0 three times, i.e. γγ-produced;
    • gen-grouped — _GEN_CHAINS, all σ₀ letters first, so the core is σ₀σ₀σ₀σ₁⁻¹σ₁⁻¹σ₁⁻¹, whose first two letters are both positive.

    not_gammaGamma_of_snd_pos excludes the second. So the ruling selects the interleaved order, and that is a structural exclusion, not a scope-(a) miss.

    🛑 The ruled order is the LESS accurate one, and that is the correct outcome under AGENTS.md §NO FITTING ("rank by derivational cleanliness first, and treat accuracy as a diagnostic"). Measured at the proton pin, convention D: interleaved −17.4959 % against gen-grouped −15.4514 %. The ruling costs 2.04 points of residual and buys the one thing the residual cannot: the order is no longer an unstated modelling choice.

        How far the ruling can reach — a parity bound #

        The ordering result above settles the core. This settles how much of the nucleon word the γγ principle can settle at all, and the answer is bounded by parity rather than by effort.

        Every vertex contributes exactly two letters, so a γγ-word has even length (gammaGamma_length_even). The nucleon crossing budget is 11 (qou-1qsw), which is odd. So no 11-crossing word is γγ-produced — not this one, not any reordering of it, not any other candidate with that budget (eleven_not_gammaGamma). At least one letter necessarily lies outside the principle's reach, and that is a structural fact about the budget, not a gap in the construction.

        Concretely, for the census word core(6) + quark(3) + gluon(2):

        block letters γγ-produced?
        core (σ₀σ₁⁻¹)³ 6 yes — interleavedCore_isGammaGamma
        quark 3 no — odd length
        gluon σ₀σ₀ 2 no — second letter positive

        So the ruling reaches 6 of 11 letters. The remaining five are the freedom that survives it, and the two gluon letters are excluded twice over: by gluonPair_not_gammaGamma directly, and by the parity of the quark block they follow.

        Scope-(b) throughout — each exclusion cites a proved invariant (vertex length, or the vertex sign pattern), never a measured miss.
