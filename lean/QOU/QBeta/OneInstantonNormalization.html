    One-instanton normalization: the geometric realization of the bar-odd ladder #

    BarOddLadderShape (the c₃ bar-odd S = A/u³ kit) records that its geometric instance — realizing the numerator A as Im 𝔙_q of a categorical meridian assignment — is the single named open problem (conj:algebraic-geometric-dilog-equivalence). A literature pass on the fibre-Stokes → archimedean map (Écalle bridge equation; Garoufalidis–Gu–Mariño resurgence of complex Chern–Simons) identifies the shape that realization must take: the one-instanton imaginary part of the median-resummed knot state integral,

    A(q) = Im 𝔙_q(q) = 𝒜_K · e^{ − Re 𝔙_K(q)/(π ℏ_q(q)) } · sin(Im 𝔙_K(q)/(π ℏ_q(q))) ≕ M(q) · Z(q),

    with 𝒜_K the integer (b = 0 weight branch, GZWeightSelection/GZWeightGeneral) Stokes amplitude, M the smooth nonzero amplitude 𝒜_K·e^{−Re𝔙/πℏ}·(subexponential prefactor), and Z the Im-phase factor whose simple zero at q* is the substrate point where 𝔙_K becomes real (Im 𝔙 = 0) — an amphichiral-type locus, matching A(q*) = 0 geometrically rather than by construction.

    The structural payoff (this file). The committed exactness BarOddLadderShape.hasDerivAt_S_at_qstar — the S-slope at q* has zero log-derivative correction — is not an extra assumption the one-instanton form must satisfy: it is an automatic consequence of the product shape A = M·Z at a simple zero of Z. The product rule gives A′(q*) = M′(q*)·Z(q*) + M(q*)·Z′(q*), and Z(q*) = 0 kills the first term, so A′(q*) = M(q*)·Z′(q*) — the amplitude times the phase-slope, with no M′/M (log-derivative of the exponential amplitude) term. That is exactly the log-derivative-free static slope the ladder kit proves. So the one-instanton normalization map is structurally consistent with the committed N_odd shape, and the capstone reduces to a magnitude identification: M(q*)·Z′(q*) = A′ ≈ +2.37.
