    Route-B assembly for concrete Φ_b: proportionality modulo the entire ratio #

    Instantiates the abstract meromorphic reduction FaddeevRigidityMeromorphic.uniqueUpToScalar_of_ratio_dense_periodic for two concrete FaddeevPhi solutions. The two dense-set periodicities of the ratio are derived from CyclicDilogPentagon.ratio_ib_periodic / ratio_ibinv_periodic (valid off the two prefactor lattices) together with the {ib, i/b} ℝ-basis PeriodLatticeBasis.exists_period_basis (needs (b²).im ≠ 0).

    What is discharged. The two prefactor-lattice complements are dense (Common.dense_primaryPole_compl, Common.dense_dualPole_compl — the two discreteness facts). The double periodicity of the ratio is no longer an input: it is composed here from the FaddeevPhi quasi-period ratio lemmas and the period basis.

    What remains open (honest, not axiomatized).

    • hr_entire — the ratio extends to an entire function. This is the divisor-rigidity wall (bricks b/c); it is not derivable from the difference equations (the elliptic ratio may be nonconstant — Weierstrass ℘), so the "same divisor" step is exactly this input, not a consequence of double periodicity.
    • The loci S₀, S₁ are dense — carried as hypotheses. To use r = Φ₁/Φ₂ on them, they must avoid Φ₂'s zeros as well as the prefactor lattice; that Φ_b-zero discreteness is gated on the PhiBProd construction and folded into the Dense Sᵢ + membership hypotheses, not axiomatized.

    So this is the §3b-cond reduction: proportionality is derived from hr_entire plus the (gated, non-axiomatized) locus density; the conjecture conj:algebraic-geometric-dilog-equivalence stays open until hr_entire is proved for Φ_b. Verified lean-direct against pinned Mathlib (exit 0).

    theorem QOU.QBeta.FaddeevPhiUniquenessAssembly.faddeevPhi_proportional_of_ratio_entire {b : ℂ} (hb2 : (b ^ 2).im ≠ 0) {Φ₁ Φ₂ r : ℂ → ℂ} [MathlibExt.FaddeevQuantumDilogarithm.FaddeevPhi b Φ₁] [MathlibExt.FaddeevQuantumDilogarithm.FaddeevPhi b Φ₂] (hr_entire : Differentiable ℂ r) (hr_ratio : ∀ (z : ℂ), Φ₂ z ≠ 0 → r z = Φ₁ z / Φ₂ z) {S₀ S₁ : Set ℂ} (hS₀ : Dense S₀) (hS₁ : Dense S₁) (hmem₀ : ∀ z ∈ S₀, 1 + Complex.exp (Complex.I * ↑Real.pi * b ^ 2) * Complex.exp (2 * ↑Real.pi * b * z) ≠ 0 ∧ Φ₂ z ≠ 0 ∧ Φ₂ (z + Complex.I * b) ≠ 0) (hmem₁ : ∀ z ∈ S₁, 1 + Complex.exp (2 * ↑Real.pi * (z + Complex.I / (2 * b)) / b) ≠ 0 ∧ Φ₂ z ≠ 0 ∧ Φ₂ (z + Complex.I / b) ≠ 0) :
    ∃ (c : ℂ), ∀ (z : ℂ), Φ₂ z ≠ 0 → Φ₁ z = c * Φ₂ z

    Route-B assembly (concrete Φ_b). For two FaddeevPhi solutions at b with nondegenerate period lattice ((b²).im ≠ 0): if the ratio r is entire (hr_entire, the open bricks-b/c input) and equals Φ₁/Φ₂ off Φ₂'s zeros (hr_ratio), and the loci S₀, S₁ — where the respective prefactor is nonzero and Φ₂ is nonzero at z and at its shift — are dense, then the two solutions are proportional on Φ₂'s nonvanishing locus. The double periodicity of r is derived from ratio_ib_periodic / ratio_ibinv_periodic; only hr_entire and the locus density remain inputs.
