    The A_ω monodromy datum is a pentagon-rigidity invariant #

    Continuation of the L4 Faddeev–Volkov rigidity lever (QOU.QBeta.CyclicDilogPentagon): the keystone gap of the c₃ bar-odd S program is G3, the argument-identification / monodromy datum z(q₀) → S — no FaddeevPhi argument has yet been exhibited whose archimedean evaluation reproduces the anchor targets S(muon) = +0.00968298, S(proton) = −0.03945936 (docs/audits/2026-07-13-phib-proof-status-and-monodromy-datum-gap-audit.md §G3, "the load-bearing gap").

    Two independent results sharpen what kind of object that datum is, and together they say the datum is a scale-invariant monodromy, not a resummation constant:

    • It is non-perturbative. The deformed Ohtsuki tower at the substrate meridian obeys arg κ̃_n = −n·arctan(t/π) exactly (phase law, main commit c821747763): the perturbative tower carries no intrinsic odd phase, so any genuine odd monodromy phase — the bar-odd S seat — is non-perturbative. A non-perturbative jump across a Stokes line is a ratio of analytic continuations: intrinsically scale-invariant (the overall normalisation of Φ_b cancels in the monodromy).

    • It is rigidity-determined. L4 (CyclicDilogPentagon) proved, modulo the honestly-carried open PentagonRigidity input (§3b-cond, the Liouville doubly-periodic-⇒-constant step absent from Mathlib), that any two nowhere-zero FaddeevPhi solutions are equal once normalised at a point.

    This file composes the two into the structural statement the lane exists to make: every scale-invariant readout of a FaddeevPhi solution is the same on all solutions (monodromy_datum_rigidity). Hence the bar-odd monodromy datum A_ω extracted from any Φ_b representative is one and the same pentagon-forced invariant — it carries no fitting freedom, which is precisely what "the datum is forced, not fitted" means for the G3 keystone.

    Honest scope #

    This does not close G3: it does not compute A_ω, and it inherits the open PentagonRigidity hypothesis (the pentagon's Liouville content). What it removes is the "which solution / which normalisation" degree of freedom — proving that the readout is well-posed as an invariant of b alone. The concrete qpRatio readout is proved scale-invariant unconditionally as a non-vacuous witness; by phi_b_quasiperiod it is even solution-independent without the pentagon (it equals the fixed quasi-period factor), so the pentagon input is the load-bearing ingredient only for the dual / large-argument monodromy that the local ib-quasi-period does not pin. No c_k is produced or wired; registry untouched; scope-(a) throughout.

    def QOU.QBeta.MonodromyDatumRigidity.ScaleInvariant {X : Type u_1} (D : (ℂ → ℂ) → X) :
