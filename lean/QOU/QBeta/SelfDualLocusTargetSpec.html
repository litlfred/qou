    The SHAPE target a fibre derivation of the c₃ bar-odd channel must supply.         Simple-zero refinement (the "slope ≠ 0 at q*" shape, algebraically) #

        The 2026-07-18 frontier fixes the SHAPE of the bar-odd numerator N_odd as a simple zero at q* (slope ≈ +2.37). A numeric slope is archimedean; the algebraic content of "simple zero" — valid over a generic CommRing with no ℝ, no derivative (§7c) — is that N factors as (x − qStar) · cofactor with the cofactor a unit at qStar (nonzero there). That is the typed shape requirement a fibre derivation must meet.
