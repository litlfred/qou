    QOU.QBeta.SixOneShapeDiscriminant — the four-shape discriminant of 6₁ (Stevedore) #

    The 6-crossing continuation of CubicSaddleDiscriminant (5₂, three saddles → −23). The GGM state-integral saddle curve is worked explicitly only for 4₁ and 5₂ (library/arxiv-2012.00062v3/2012.00062v3.pdf §§5–6); the 6-crossing knots appear there only as q-series. So the faithful 6-crossing datum is taken from the hyperbolic geometry instead of GGM: the shape parameter z of the Stevedore knot 6₁ (4-tetrahedron triangulation) generates a degree-4 field with minimal polynomial

    x⁴ − 3x³ + 6x² − 5x + 2 (SnapPy shape, PARI algdep-verified: |p(z)| < 10⁻⁶⁰ at 100-digit precision),

    whose four roots are the Galois conjugates of z — the four geometric shapes (the 6₁ analogue of the three 5₂ saddles). Their discriminant is 257 (prime), the 6₁ arithmetic fingerprint (the analogue of −23 for 5₂). This file proves, over a generic commutative ring:

    • disc_eq_root_diff_sq_quartic — the quartic discriminant as the square of the product of the six pairwise root differences, written in the elementary symmetric functions e₁,…,e₄ (ring);
    • six_one_shape_diff_sq — for the four roots of the 6₁ shape polynomial (Vieta e₁=3, e₂=6, e₃=5, e₄=2) the product of the squared pairwise differences is 257: the geometric (shape-difference) form of the 6₁ field discriminant.

    (For the record, algdep also gives the 6₂ shape field x⁵−3x⁴+5x³−5x²+4x−1, discriminant 1777 (prime); 6₃, being amphichiral, has a larger shape field.)

    Generic CommRing (§7c); the transcendental hyperbolic data (Vol(6₁) ≈ 3.16396) is archimedean and lives in the witness. Source: SnapPy Manifold('6_1') (the shape) + PARI/GP algdep (the minimal polynomial); the discriminant identity is a ring identity independent of the source.

    Quartic discriminant as the product of squared root differences. For any four ring elements (the four shapes), the square of the product of their six pairwise differences equals the quartic discriminant written in the elementary symmetric functions e₁ = Σyᵢ, e₂ = Σyᵢyⱼ, e₃ = Σyᵢyⱼyₖ, e₄ = ∏yᵢ.

    theorem QOU.QBeta.SixOneShapeDiscriminant.six_one_shape_diff_sq {R : Type u_1} [CommRing R] (y₁ y₂ y₃ y₄ : R) (h1 : y₁ + y₂ + y₃ + y₄ = 3) (h2 : y₁ * y₂ + y₁ * y₃ + y₁ * y₄ + y₂ * y₃ + y₂ * y₄ + y₃ * y₄ = 6) (h3 : y₁ * y₂ * y₃ + y₁ * y₂ * y₄ + y₁ * y₃ * y₄ + y₂ * y₃ * y₄ = 5) (h4 : y₁ * y₂ * y₃ * y₄ = 2) :
    ((y₁ - y₂) * (y₁ - y₃) * (y₁ - y₄) * (y₂ - y₃) * (y₂ - y₄) * (y₃ - y₄)) ^ 2 = 257

    The four 6₁ shape differences square to 257. For the four roots of the 6₁ shape polynomial x⁴ − 3x³ + 6x² − 5x + 2 (Vieta Σy = 3, Σyᵢyⱼ = 6, Σyᵢyⱼyₖ = 5, ∏y = 2), the product of their squared pairwise differences is the field discriminant 257 — the geometric (shape-difference) form of the 6₁ arithmetic fingerprint, the 6-crossing analogue of five_two_saddle_diff_sq = −23.
