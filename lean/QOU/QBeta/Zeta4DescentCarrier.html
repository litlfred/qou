    QOU.QBeta.Zeta4DescentCarrier #

    The bridge between the KC17 bar-parity descent dichotomy (FMapDirectCyclotomic.lean, merged PR #3556) and the bar-odd Hopf-cubic carrier (HopfCubicOddCarrier.lean, KC20).

    The dichotomy uside_parity_descent_iff_zeta4 shows the u-side cyclotomic re-basing transports the fibre bar-parity to the c_k rungs iff q² = −1, and uside_parity_never_descends_real shows this fails at every real substrate. This file identifies that descent point with the fibre root of unity the odd carrier lives on:

    • descent_iff_order_four — over ℂ, q² = −1 ↔ (q⁴ = 1 ∧ q² ≠ 1): the descent condition is exactly "q is a primitive 4th root of unity" (order-4 root, i.e. q = ζ₄ = ±i). So the parity descends precisely at ζ₄ — the fibre point, off the real substrate axis.

    • carrier_var_at_descent — at the descent point the bar-even loop-trace variable y = q² + q⁻² (the argument of the Hopf-cubic carrier cTotal) equals −2, the antipode of its q = 1 value y = +2 (cHopf_at_two).

    • cTotal_at_descent_image — the bar-odd carrier cTotal (KC20) is non-degenerate at that image (cTotal(−2) = −32 ≠ 0): the odd channel is genuinely present at ζ₄, consistent with ζ₄ being where the fibre parity and the odd Hopf-cubic carrier meet.

    Net structural picture: the odd (bar-odd, Q_β / S) channel's fibre carrier connects to the c_k parity only at q = ζ₄ (this file) and never on the real substrate axis (uside_parity_never_descends_real) — so at q₀ the odd channel is irreducibly archimedean. Derives no c_k value; wires nothing; changes no tier (flip-flop guard §8). All sorry-free, lean-direct verified.

    theorem QOU.QBeta.descent_iff_order_four (q : ℂ) :
    q ^ 2 = -1 ↔ q ^ 4 = 1 ∧ q ^ 2 ≠ 1

    The descent point is a primitive 4th root of unity (ζ₄). Over ℂ, q² = −1 ↔ (q⁴ = 1 ∧ q² ≠ 1) — the right-hand side is exactly the order-4-root condition. Combined with uside_parity_descent_iff_zeta4 (FMapDirectCyclotomic), the fibre bar-parity descends to the c_k rungs precisely when q = ζ₄.
