    phiBProd strip bounds from the product (growth-estimate epic, bricks G1 + G3) #

    Route-B bricks of the Faddeev keystone growth-estimate epic (qou-mz4f; scope). The scoping survey established that phiBProd's strip growth needs no contour integral: input 1's product form gives it by elementary product-norm estimates already in Mathlib (Multipliable.norm_tprod, Real.rexp_tsum_eq_tprod, Real.log_le_sub_one_of_pos).

    • G1 (norm_tprod_numFactor_le / norm_tprod_denFactor_le) — each infinite product's norm is bounded above by exp of the summed factor-log-norms, i.e. ‖∏' k, (1 + gₖ)‖ ≤ exp(∑' ‖gₖ‖) (the general norm_tprod_one_add_le), applied off the zero lattices where all factors are nonzero. This is the numerator (and denominator) upper bound.
    • G3 (norm_phiBProd_le_of_den_lower) — the phiBProd = num/den strip bound, given a denominator lower bound δ (the G2 obligation, carried here as an explicit hypothesis): ‖phiBProd b z‖ ≤ exp(∑' ‖numFactorₖ − 1‖)/δ.

    G2 (the denominator lower bound that supplies δ) is the one remaining estimate; G3 is stated conditionally on it so both G1 and the G3 assembly land now on the merged product infrastructure.

    Verified lean-direct against the pinned Mathlib Log.Summable + InfiniteSum.Field cones + PhiBProd. Sorry-free.

    theorem QOU.QBeta.PhiBProdStripBound.norm_tprod_one_add_le {g : ℕ → ℂ} (hg : Summable fun (k : ℕ) => ‖g k‖) (hnz : ∀ (k : ℕ), 1 + g k ≠ 0) :
    ‖∏' (k : ℕ), (1 + g k)‖ ≤ Real.exp (∑' (k : ℕ), ‖g k‖)

    General product-norm upper bound. For a norm-summable complex family g with no vanishing factor 1 + gₖ, ‖∏' k, (1 + gₖ)‖ ≤ exp(∑' k, ‖gₖ‖). Proof: ‖∏‖ = ∏‖·‖ (Multipliable.norm_tprod); with all factors positive, ∏‖1+gₖ‖ = exp(∑ log‖1+gₖ‖) (rexp_tsum_eq_tprod); and log‖1+gₖ‖ ≤ ‖1+gₖ‖ − 1 ≤ ‖gₖ‖.
