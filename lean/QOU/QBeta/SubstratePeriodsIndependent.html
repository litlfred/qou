    LinearIndependent ℝ ![Complex.I * b, Complex.I / b]

    The two Faddeev–Volkov periods are ℝ-linearly independent whenever Im(b²) ≠ 0. Proof: an ℝ-relation s•(ib) + t•(i/b) = 0 factors through I and, after clearing b, gives s·b² + t = 0; taking imaginary parts (t real) yields s·Im(b²) = 0, so s = 0, whence t = 0.

    Fintype.card (Fin 2) = Module.finrank ℝ ℂ

    The two periods span ℂ over ℝ: being ℝ-independent and of cardinality 2 = finrank ℝ ℂ, they form a Basis (Fin 2) ℝ ℂ — the exact input DoublyPeriodicLiouville.ratio_const_of_doublyPeriodic consumes. The Basis follows from periods_linearIndependent by the standard "independent family of size finrank is a basis" constructor; only the independence content is proved here (it is the load-bearing fact — the basis is its mechanical corollary).
