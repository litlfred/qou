    QOU.QBeta.WindingGFTransform — the winding generating-function transform #

    of the 4₁ index annihilator (bean qou-3bp8, route 2b of qou-qnnq)

    #4433 established that the winding sum F_m(w) = Σ_e I₄₁(m,e)(q̃)·wᵉ has radius of convergence exactly 1, while the corpus sheet w = q₀²e^{2iϑ} lies outside the annulus: the physical effective Stokes coefficient requires analytic continuation. The continuation vehicle is the DGG quantum A-polynomial annihilator (eq C.7 of [dimofte-gaiotto-gukov2013], glyph-decoded and grid-certified in-corpus by qbeta_g3_annihilator_certification.py, re-certified at the substrate point by the qou-3bp8 W1 probe), whose expanded scalar form is carried here as the conditional class FourOneIndexAnnihilated (§3b-cond: a cited literature input, NOT proved here — everything downstream is conditional on it and says so in its hypotheses).

    The theorem gf_three_term is the load-bearing derived step: given the annihilator and HasSum hypotheses for the three winding rows at their respective arguments, the generating functions satisfy an exact three-term functional equation relating F_{m−1}(w/s), F_m(w), and F_{m+1}(s·w) (with s = √q̃). Because |1/s| = q₀^{1/2} > 1, solving for the F_{m−1}(w/s) term expresses an OUTER-argument value through values at smaller radius — the exact outward continuation ladder used by the W2 stepping probe. The numerical A2 certification of the W1 probe checks the same relation inside the annulus; this file replaces "numerically certified algebra" with a machine-checked proof.

    Also here:

    • pairedWindowSum_unit_sheet_im_zero — the unit-sheet bar-even lemma of #4433: a real winding family in paired form evaluated at w = e^{iθ} has vanishing imaginary part (amphichirality ⇒ no bar-odd part without radial deformation).
    • SDeformedVolumeChannelWinding — the bean-qou-awpk schema item: the Stokes coefficient carried per winding class, tied to the constant A_ω of the base channel as its w = 1 total.

      The winding generating-function three-term functional equation (conditional on the annihilator class). For s > 0, w ≠ 0, and HasSum hypotheses for the three rows at their arguments — row m at w, row m−1 at w/s, row m+1 at s·w — the sums satisfy

      c₋ · F_{m−1}(w/s) + P_m(w) · F_m(w) + c₊ · F_{m+1}(s·w) = 0

        Entirety of square-dominated winding families #

        The qou-3bp8 decay finding: the leading q̃-power of I(0,e) grows quadratically (lead(e) = T(⌈e/2⌉) ≈ e²/8), which forces the substrate values to decay SUPER-exponentially in e once past the pre-asymptotic hump (measured onset ≈ e ≈ 30–40: I(0,28) ~ 0.09, I(0,40) ~ 2·10⁻⁵). The #4433 "radius of convergence exactly 1" diagnostic sampled only e ≤ 28 — the pre-asymptotic plateau. Consequence, PROVED here: a two-sided family dominated by A·ρ^{e²} is absolutely summable against w^e for EVERY w ≠ 0 — the winding generating function is entire on ℂ*, no analytic continuation is needed, and the gf_three_term hypotheses are dischargeable at every point (capstone below). The decay bound itself enters as a hypothesis (computational/literature input); the entirety consequence and the discharge are machine-checked.
