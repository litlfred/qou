    A - t - σ (A - t) = -(t - σ t)

    Bar-odd isolation. For the mass endomorphism 𝓜 = A − t with bar-even diagonal A (σA = A), the bar-odd part 𝓜 − σ𝓜 equals −(t − σt) — minus the cross-term's bar-odd part, with no contribution from the diagonal mass A. So c₃ (the bar-odd face) is carried entirely by the cross-term t = B·D⁻¹·C.

    Even diagonal drops out of the bar-odd face (sum form). For any bar-even A, adding A to a cross-term t leaves the bar-odd part exactly unchanged: (A + t) − σ(A + t) = t − σt (an exact equality, no sign convention). The bar-even mass is invisible to the decay (bar-odd) channel.
