    The algebraic pole half of conj:algebraic-geometric-dilog-equivalence #

    conj:algebraic-geometric-dilog-equivalence (mass-theory) is downstream of the Kashaev–Murakami–Murakami volume conjecture and is therefore, per AGENTS.md §3b and the anti-laundering rule, a genuine conjecture — not provable by axiomatisation. Its asymptotic/geometric content (partial product → Bloch–Wigner volume; poles ↔ ideal-tetrahedron cusps) is the volume conjecture and stays open in general (the figure-eight case is the known theorem of Andersen–Hansen 2006 / Ohtsuki).

    Its algebraic half is unconditionally a theorem, and this file proves it sorry-free: the q-Pochhammer partial denominator (x;q)_n = ∏_{k<n}(1 − x qᵏ) vanishes exactly at the poles x = q^{-k}, k < n. This is the rigorous "pole" side of the pole-to-cusp bijection — the seed data that thurston-relaxation.md feeds to the Newton solve. It does not establish the cusp correspondence (geometric, conjectural).

    Base ring: a generic [Field K] (the negative powers q^{-k} need inverses; q ≠ 0). No ℝ — the pole structure is algebraic, not archimedean (§7c).

      The pole set {q^{-k} : k < n} is contained in the zero set of (x;q)_n; stated as the roots exist as the explicit function k ↦ q^{-k} and each is a genuine root (the content the bare ∃ z, z k = q^{-k} field of the conjecture class omits).
