    class QOU.QBeta.BarOddLadderShape (S A u : ℝ → ℝ) (qstar A' : ℝ) :
    Prop

    Bar-odd ladder shape (hypothesis carrier, §3b): S = A/u³ with a positive lever u, a simple zero of the numerator at q*, and positive numerator slope A′ there. All fields are the measured/derived inputs of the c₃ bar-odd chain; the theorems below are consequences.

    • ladder (q : ℝ) : S q = A q / u q ^ 3

      The ladder shape S = A/u³ (the N=3 rung lever).

    • u_pos (q : ℝ) : 0 < u q

      The lever is positive (substrate u = b·ℏ_q² > 0 for q > 1).

    • zero_at_qstar : A qstar = 0

      The numerator vanishes at the decay-sector determination q*.

    • deriv_at_qstar : HasDerivAt A A' qstar

      The numerator is differentiable at q* with derivative A′.

    • slope_pos : 0 < A'

      The numerator slope at q* is positive (measured A′ ≈ +2.37).
