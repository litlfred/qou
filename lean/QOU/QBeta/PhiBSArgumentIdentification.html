    The S argument-identification keystone (bar-odd face of conj:algebraic-geometric-dilog-equivalence) #

    The c₃ bar-odd derivation converged (every route, 2026-07-11) on a single open link: identify the Faddeev Φ_b argument z(q₀) whose fibre-branch evaluation at each substrate anchor reproduces the anchor-dependent S-leak S = c₃ + Vol(4₁) (S(muon) ≈ +0.00968, S(proton) ≈ −0.03946). This is the keystone conjecture — the bar-odd companion of the pole↔cusp matching of conj:algebraic-geometric-dilog-equivalence (whose bar-even face is the −Vol(4₁) datum).

    Where physics lives. The physical regime is the fibre branch: real b, hence |q_b| = |e^{iπb²}| = 1 (norm_fibreQParam). There the modular-double product diverges and the construction is the real single integral QOU.MathlibExt.FaddeevPhiBRealIntegral.phiB — a genuine noncomputable def, unimodular (norm_phiB) and σ-unitary (phiB_mul_conj), hence an intrinsically bar-odd carrier (prop:phi-b-unitary-bar-odd). The fibre b-map is b(q₀) = √(ln q₀ / 2π), so q_b = e^{i·ln q₀ / 2} (fibreQParam_fibreB).

    Honesty discipline. The conjecture is stated relative to a given argument map zArg — NOT as a bare existence claim. A pointwise existence claim (∀ q₀ ∃ z, phase (fibreB q₀) z = S q₀) would be near-vacuous (choice-function selection against an unbounded continuous phase); the open mathematical content is that the structural map — the substrate → meridian-holonomy map supplied by the pole↔cusp matching of conj:algebraic-geometric-dilog-equivalence / conj:shared-denominator-cusp-holonomy — is such a zArg. No instance is fabricated (the class is the open state); no c_k is produced or wired.

    Physical channel caveat (decay = binding). The identification of the bar-odd face with the decay observable 𝔔_β = Im 𝔙 — and hence the "decay = binding" reading of the Gram-ghost mode — is conditional on conj:binding-qbeta-complex-volume (ComplexVolumeMatch). Φ_b touches binding ONLY through that unproven channel identity; nothing in this file assumes it. The class below is about the numerical S-map (a function of the substrate modulus), not about the decay observable.
