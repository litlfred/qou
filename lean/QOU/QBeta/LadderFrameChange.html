    theorem QOU.QBeta.hbar_mul_succ_eq_h {R : Type u_1} [Field R] (q : R) (hq : q ≠ 0) :
    (q - 1) / q * (q + 1) = q - 1 / q

    ℏ_q · (q+1) = h.

    The substrate's two first-order quantities differ by exactly the factor q + 1: ℏ_q = (q−1)/q and h = q − 1/q = (q²−1)/q. This is what makes the frame change exact rather than asymptotic.

    theorem QOU.QBeta.u_eq_b_mul_h_sq_div {R : Type u_1} [Field R] (q b : R) (hq : q ≠ 0) (hq1 : q + 1 ≠ 0) :
    b * ((q - 1) / q) ^ 2 = b * (q - 1 / q) ^ 2 / (q + 1) ^ 2

    u = b·h² / (q+1)² — the exact frame-change identity.

    u ∼ h² is not an approximation near q = 1; it holds at every q, with conversion factor b/(q+1)². At the proton anchor that factor is ≈ 0.1949, which is why u/h² measures 0.194875 rather than the √3/8 ≈ 0.2165 its q → 1 limit would suggest.

    theorem QOU.QBeta.u_pow_eq_even_h_pow {R : Type u_1} [Field R] (q b : R) (hq : q ≠ 0) (hq1 : q + 1 ≠ 0) (k : ℕ) :
    (b * ((q - 1) / q) ^ 2) ^ k = (b / (q + 1) ^ 2) ^ k * (q - 1 / q) ^ (2 * k)

    Every u-rung is an even h-rung: uᵏ = (b/(q+1)²)ᵏ · h²ᵏ.

    A u-coefficient at order k contributes at h-order exactly 2k. So the u-frame ladder places weight only on even h-orders, and odd ones are unreachable — not unpopulated by choice, but outside the span of any u-ladder whatsoever.

    This is the exact statement behind "the u¹ rung is an artifact of expanding in a squared variable", and behind the half-integer powers seen when the u-expansion is pushed past its analytic range.

    theorem QOU.QBeta.u_rung_order_is_even (k : ℕ) :
    ∃ (m : ℕ), m = 2 * k ∧ Even m

    An odd h-order cannot be produced by a u-rung.

    Contrapositive form of the above, stated so it can be cited directly: if a u-rung of order k is written as a multiple of hᵐ, then m is even. Nothing about b or q enters — it is forced by the exponent arithmetic.
