    The coincidence locus, as a ring identity #

    source
    theorem QOU.QBeta.coincidenceLocus_factor {R : Type u_1} [CommRing R] (s : R) :
    s ^ 7 + s ^ 5 - s ^ 4 - 1 = (s - 1) * (s ^ 6 + s ^ 5 + 2 * s ^ 4 + s ^ 3 + s ^ 2 + s + 1)

    The denominator-cleared coincidence locus of z_C and z_D, factored.

    This is the identity behind decayLadder_zC_not_jones's s⁷ + s⁵ = s⁴ + 1: the polynomial has s = 1 as a root, and the cofactor has all-positive coefficients. Pure CommRing content — no ordering, no archimedean structure (AGENTS.md §7c).

        The classical-limit coincidence. The two conventions define the same Markov parameter at q = 1. This is why a ladder coefficient obtained as a q = 1 evaluation of a Markov-trace invariant — c₃ = J_Hopf(1) = −2 — is unmoved by the C→D ruling.

        source
        theorem QOU.QBeta.zCsub_eq_zDsub_iff {s : ℝ} (hs : 0 < s) :
        zCsub s = zDsub s ↔ s = 1

        The coincidence is isolated. For s > 0, the two Markov parameters agree exactly at s = 1 (q = 1) and nowhere else.

        The forward direction is the sharp form of decayLadder_zC_not_jones: that theorem rules out s > 1, while this rules out every positive s ≠ 1, including the interval 0 < s < 1.
