    The transport's hypotheses, discharged at the concrete labelling — qou-glbl #

    FiveTwoRootLabelling builds Y₁ Y₂ Y₃ and proves them differentiable and curve-solving. FiveTwoTransport states hasDerivAt_envelopeS, whose hypotheses are about an arbitrary labelling. This file joins them: every hypothesis that can be discharged pointwise from the construction, discharged.

    Why a separate module #

    VSide is declared in FiveTwoTransport, and Yreal/Ypair in FiveTwoRootLabelling; neither imports the other, and neither should — the labelling is about the real cubic and the transport is about V. This is the join, and it is the first place the two can be spoken of together.

    What is discharged here #

    hypothesis of hasDerivAt_envelopeS supplied by
    hY₁ hY₂ hY₃ — differentiability hasDerivAt_Yreal, hasDerivAt_Ypair_fst/snd
    hs₁ hs₂ hs₃ — VSide vSide_Yreal, vSide_Ypair_fst/snd (below)
    hl₁ — log(−Y₁)′, route 2 hasDerivAt_log_neg_Yreal (below)
    hl₂ hl₃ — log(−Y₂,₃)′, route 1 hasDerivAt_log_neg_Ypair_* (below)
    hA — Σ log(1 − x·Yⱼ) = log x logSumA_concrete (below)
    hB — Σ log(1 − Yⱼ/x) = −log x logSumB_concrete (below)

    and the routing is not a choice: log_neg_route says −y ∈ slitPlane fails precisely at a positive real root, Y₁ is that root at every φ (Route 36 G8 measures exactly one per arc point), and Y₂ Y₃ are non-real wherever pairDisc φ < 0. So Y₁ must take route 2 and the pair must take route 1 — one lemma each, no case analysis.

    ⚠ Two hypotheses, two different arcs #

    • sin φ ≠ 0 — the open arc. VSide is FALSE at φ = π (Route 36 G7), so this is not a convenience; it is the largest set on which these statements are true at all.
    • pairDisc φ < 0 — off the collision. This is the exceptional point envelopeS_total tolerates, and Route 36 G12 measures it holding at 153 of 199 arc points, so the pair statements are non-vacuous rather than merely typed.

    ⚠ hA and hB are not symmetric, and Lean found it #

    logSumA_concrete needs no off-collision hypothesis; logSumB_concrete does. pairDisc φ < 0 was written into both and the linter flagged it unused in the first. That is structure, not a slip: logSumA's signs are Im(x·y) facts proved straight from the root structure, while logSumB's come through the conjugacy Im(y/x) = −Im(x·conj y), and the pair is conjugate only where pairDisc φ < 0. Past the discriminant flip they are two distinct reals — each its own conjugate, neither the other's. So hA survives the collision and hB does not.

    What is NOT here #

    heⱼ for the pair — it needs k = 0, and Route 36 G13 shows no term-by-term bound on the four args reaches it (they sum to 11.857 > 2π). And hcont at φ = π (Route 36 G11) — envelopeS_total wants continuity on the closed interval, the Li₂ argument sits on the cut there, and nothing has discharged it.

    The 5₂ sum rule is PROVED: QOU.QBeta.FiveTwoComplexPentagon.fiveTwo_saddle_sum_rule and fiveTwoSaddleSumRule_holds, sorry-free, standard axioms.

    heⱼ at the pair — the four regime bounds, instantiated #

    FiveTwoPairSaddle reduces heⱼ at the pair to |argSum x y| < 2π and proves that bound in four regimes, for an abstract (x, y) with Im y > 0. This section supplies the regime hypotheses from the concrete labelling.

    The dispatch is on Re y = cos φ − t/2 (re_Ypair_eq) and the sign of cos φ, and it is exhaustive by construction rather than by inspection: t > 0 makes Re y > 0 force cos φ > 0, which is what collapses what looks like four cases into three.

    ⚠ sin φ ≥ 0 is not enough for the divisor sign fact. im_div_Ypair_fst_pos carries 0 < x.im, i.e. strictly sin φ > 0, and envelopeS_total needs the derivative on Ico 0 π, which contains φ = 0. The endpoint is handled separately: sin φ = 0 with 0 ≤ φ < π forces φ = 0, where x = 1 and the fact is Im y > 0 outright. Dropping the Ico hypothesis and working on the open interval would have left a hole exactly at the point the transport starts from.
