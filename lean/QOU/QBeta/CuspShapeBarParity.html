    The figure-eight cusp shape in bar-parity coordinates — barEven = 1, barOdd² = −3 #

    The cusp side of the pole→cusp story (conj:algebraic-geometric-dilog-equivalence; the pole side is proved in QOU.QBeta.QPochhammerPoles). The complete hyperbolic structure on the figure-eight complement S³∖4₁ has both ideal tetrahedra at the shape ω = e^{iπ/3}, the primitive sixth root of unity — the root of the cyclotomic Φ₆(z) = z² − z + 1 whose splitting field is the figure-eight trace field ℚ(√−3).

    This file makes the shape explicit in the bar-parity coordinates of QOU.QBeta.BarParityZeta4Swap (reusing barEven/barOdd), and proves — over a generic field, no archimedean structure — that the shape's bar-parity split is exactly the 𝔙 = Vol + i·CS split:

    • barEven ω = ω + ω⁻¹ = 1 — the bar-even / mass / Re 𝔙 = Vol coordinate (rational, = 2cos(π/3); the amphichiral, anchor-stable part −Vol(4₁));
    • barOdd ω = ω − ω⁻¹ = 2ω − 1 with (barOdd ω)² = −3 — the bar-odd / decay=binding / Im 𝔙 = CS coordinate, whose square is the cusp modulus τ ∝ √−3 (= 2i·sin(π/3) = √−3 archimedean).

    So the two faces of the complex volume 𝔙 (binding-qbeta-complex-volume.lean, 𝔈_bind = Re 𝔙, 𝔔_β = Im 𝔙) are the two bar-parity coordinates of the ONE cusp shape ω, and the √−3 that drives the Neumann–Zagier deformation CVol(u) = Vol − (τ/4)u² + … is barOdd(ω). The q₀ ↦ u meridian-holonomy map (the open link) is what deforms ω off this complete point; everything here is the complete-point (u = 0) coordinate data, proved exactly.

    Also proved: ω is a primitive sixth root of unity (ω³ = −1, ω⁶ = 1) — the root-of-unity structure the bar-odd phase channel accumulates in.

    Generic field, §7c. No c_k wired; no archimedean evaluation.

      The degenerate (golden-ratio) branch — why a real pole cannot reach the cusp #

      The complete figure-eight gluing equation (z(z−1))² = 1 factors into two branches, z(z−1) = ±1:

      • z² − z + 1 = 0 — the cusp shape e^{iπ/3} (IsCuspShape), complex, discriminant −3: the hyperbolic structure, barOdd² = −3 (√−3 = CS).
      • z² − z − 1 = 0 — the golden ratio φ = (1+√5)/2 (IsGoldenShape), real, discriminant +5: the degenerate structure (Vol = 0, no hyperbolic geometry).

      The two branches swap bar-parity roles, and the swap is exactly a sign flip in the discriminant −3 ↦ +5:

      branch poly barEven barOdd special coord
      cusp (hyperbolic) z²−z+1 = 1 (rational) ² = −3 barOdd = √−3 (imaginary ⇒ CS)
      golden (degenerate) z²−z−1 ² = +5 = 1 (rational) barEven = √5 (real ⇒ no CS)

      This is the algebraic shadow of the pole→cusp phase obstruction: seeding Thurston's Newton with the real substrate pole q₀^{-k} flows to the golden (real, √5) branch — Vol = 0, CS = 0 — never the cusp. Only complexifying the seed (the phase φ(q₀)) moves the special coordinate from the real √5 to the imaginary √−3, i.e. turns on the CS = bar-odd S. The phase is precisely the branch selector, and no real datum can supply it.
