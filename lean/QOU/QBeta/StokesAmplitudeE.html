    QOU.QBeta.StokesAmplitudeE — can the Stokes / resurgence route supply the A=3 amplitude E? A WALL. #

    Scope. QOU.QBeta.A3AntiSelfAdjointLift (merged #4611) bracketed the A=3 Δ_λ magnitude lift r* = μ₃/c₃ ≈ 1.585 to EXACTLY ONE free real degree of freedom: the anti-self-adjoint off-block amplitude E. Its amplitude_nonzero proved any genuine lift forces E ≠ 0; its amplitude_surjective/amplitude_injective proved E ↦ factor is a bijection, so pinning E to hit r* without an independent derivation is a FIT. Deriving E from the substrate is the open keystone (qou-jqof, epic qou-x631).

    This file asks the assigned question — can the local Stokes-constant / resurgence / Écalle-Borel route supply E? — and answers it with a rigorous WALL, extending the retraction PR #4790 (c1_infinity_isoformalDim_eq_zero: C1's ∞ is pure isoclinic ⇒ no local Stokes data) to the amplitude E specifically.

    The bridge (MODEL, cited — NOT a fit, NOT a smuggled decomposition). The amplitude a local q-Stokes datum can supply is the strictly-off-block part of the q-Stokes matrix. By RSZ (Astérisque 355, §5.5.2 / Thm 5.5.7) the q-Stokes matrices are unipotent automorphisms inducing the identity on gr M₀: St = I + N with N strictly off-block nilpotent. N carries exactly one coordinate per slope-pairing modulus, and the number of those moduli is RSZ Thm 3.3.5's isoformal dimension d = rszIsoformalDim k r μ = Σ_{i<j} rᵢrⱼ(μⱼ−μᵢ) (RSZIsoformalInterface). So the local-Stokes-supplied amplitude is the coordinate sum ∑_{i<d} coeffᵢ·vᵢ (stokesAmplitude): a linear form with no constant term — the zero constant term is the unipotency (identity diagonal), an RSZ import, stated as the model, never claimed as derived (per §"Defined residual ≠ derived decomposition"). We do NOT assert E is this; we assert the honest conditional: IF E is supplied by local Stokes data, THEN it has this form, and derive the walls.

    The WALL — two branches, both block the local Stokes route.

    • Pure-isoclinic branch (scope-(b), proved invariant rszIsoformalDim_one). When the carrier module has a single Newton slope (k = 1 — exactly C1's ∞, #4790), the isoformal dimension is 0 (rszIsoformalDim_one), so the local-Stokes amplitude is forced to 0 (stokesAmplitude_eq_zero_of_dim_zero). Composed with A3AntiSelfAdjointLift.amplitude_nonzero, a single-slope module's local Stokes data cannot produce the lift (pureIsoclinic_local_stokes_cannot_lift). The cited invariant is the emptiness of the slope-pairing index set at k = 1 — a proved dimension bound, licensing scope-(b).

    • Multi-slope branch (scope-(a)). A local-Stokes lift therefore forces ≥ 2 distinct slopes (local_stokes_lift_forces_positive_dim: 1 ≤ d). But when d ≥ 1 the local amplitude is surjective onto R (stokesAmplitude_surjective_of_dim_pos): the nonempty isoformal moduli leaves E entirely free — local Stokes data does not select its value. Selecting the point in the moduli is the job of the global (0↔∞) Birkhoff connection matrix — theta-valued, un-started, and NOT local Stokes data (#4790's successor W2, docs/audits/2026-08-01-branch-selection-birkhoff-connection-matrix.md).

    Net verdict — WALL (scope-(a) with a scope-(b) sub-exclusion). The local Stokes-constant / resurgence / Écalle-Borel route provably cannot supply the numeric E: either the carrier is single-slope and local Stokes data gives E = 0 (no lift, scope-(b)), or it is multi-slope and local Stokes data leaves E free — its value living in the global Birkhoff matrix, a distinct open object. This unifies the sibling Stokes negatives — the naive large-order amplitude gives 2πP ≈ 1 not the GGM 3 (2026-08-01-stokes3-largeorder-amplitude-41.md); the bar-odd ladder resums but is anchor-blind on the real meridian, its Stokes datum exp-suppressed and fibre-borne (2026-08-01-qborel-ecalle-resum-barodd.md); the ±i phase supplies the Δ_λ sign neither (2026-08-01-stokes-phase-sign-delta-lambda.md) — into a single structural statement at the amplitude level: the carrier module's LOCAL Stokes moduli is too small to be E.

Nothing fitted, nothing wired; μ₃/c₃/AME/q₀ never inverted; q₀-derivation not routed through.
