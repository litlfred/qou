    QOU.QBeta.FloorRemainderFromDerivedShapeFields — H_floor's remainder for 4₁ and 5₂, from the corpus's own shape fields #

    Where this sits (Route C, milestone M3, bean qou-m3ad Part 4) #

    AdjointIndexSetM3 records the rival hypothesis H_floor: the knot-independent core of the DT index set is the triple {trivial, geometric, conjugate-geometric} and the knot-dependent remainder is the number of real embeddings of the trace field. Its inputs — realEmbeddings and complexPairs — were transcribed from Garoufalidis–Zagier §2.1 (library/arxiv-2111.06645v3), i.e. from a table.

    This file derives both numbers for 4₁ and 5₂ from the shape fields the corpus's own two-bridge triangulations produce (two_bridge_layered_nz_probe.py, #6985; Farey word R for 4₁, RL for 5₂):

    • 4₁: both shapes are e^{iπ/3}, minimal polynomial x² − x + 1 (discriminant −3) — no real root, so the signature is (0, 1) and the remainder is 0;
    • 5₂: shapes θ, θ, θ² − 1, θ² − 1 with θ³ − θ + 1 = 0 (discriminant −23, FiveTwoGluingFromFareyWord.shape_cubic_discriminant) — exactly one real root, so the signature is (1, 1) and the remainder is 1.

    The real-root statements are proved outright over ℝ (this is archimedean content, §7c: the ordering is the whole point). The link theorems at the end say the transcribed realEmbeddings values are the derived real-root counts, so forced_remainder_is_the_real_embedding_count now rests on a derivation rather than on a table for the two knots the corpus derives.

    The companion probe computations/probes/aomega_m3_floor_remainder_from_derived_shape_fields_probe.py (13/13) computes the same signatures by an exact Sturm count and, in addition, pushes every tetrahedron shape through every embedding of the field and sums the Bloch–Wigner dilogarithm: the real embedding of 5₂ gives real shapes and volume exactly 0, the complex pair gives ±Vol, i.e. the Chern–Simons value sets {+V, −V} and {0, +V, −V} that H_floor grades by. That part is measured, not formalised here (BlochWignerLobachevsky.BlochWigner_lob_real_eq_zero covers real shapes in (0,1) only; the 5₂ real embedding has a shape θ ≈ −1.3247).

    Scope. This does not separate H_Ad from H_floor (the core is 3 under both, core_is_three_on_all_three), and it does not reach (−2,3,7), whose field the corpus does not derive. It upgrades the provenance of Part 4's rule on two knots; it derives nothing about A_ω.

        The link to H_floor's transcribed inputs #

        realEmbeddings in AdjointIndexSetM3 is a table. The two theorems below say its 4₁ and 5₂ entries are the real-root counts of the derived shape polynomials: 0 because x² − x + 1 has none, 1 because θ³ − θ + 1 has exactly one. (The complex-pair count 1 is then forced by the degrees 2 and 3.) So on these two knots forced_remainder_is_the_real_embedding_count rests on the corpus's own triangulations, not on Garoufalidis–Zagier's table.
