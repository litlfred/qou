    K

    The Q_beta ladder's rung-1 phase, alpha = (z-1)*h/2.

    Stated over a general field with z and h free — unlike EigenphaseCarrierVacuity.eigenphase, which is pinned to the excised convention C via markovZC, and unlike BraidImageEigenphaseExclusion.alphaOf, which is ℝ- and function-valued. A claim holding "identically in q and z" needs the free form.

      2 * rungOnePhase z h = Interactions.QBetaCounitTrace.epsNP h - Interactions.QBetaCounitTrace.trMNP h w ↔ w = z

      The falsifiable content. Leave the Ocneanu weight on the sigma- generators as an unknown w; then (D1) holds iff w = z.

      So the rung-1 phase does not merely happen to equal the gap — it PINS the Markov parameter. This is the precise form of the claim that z enters the five-rung ladder in exactly one place, and it is what stops rungOnePhase_eq_counit_sub_trace from being arithmetic on chosen constants.

      h ≠ 0 is necessary and its sharpness is recorded below.

      Sharpness of h ≠ 0: at the classical point h = 0 the weight is genuinely unpinned — (D1) holds for EVERY w.
