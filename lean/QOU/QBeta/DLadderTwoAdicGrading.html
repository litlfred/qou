    The bar-odd D-ladder 2-adic parity grading — verified anchor + open target #

    The bar-odd S = h·D(ũ) ladder has exact coefficients d_k = a_k + b_k√3 ∈ ℚ(√3) (the ser_sqrt/ser_div construction in computations/qbeta/qbeta_dladder_generating_function_structure_probe.py; witness qbeta-barodd-d-ladder-ord17). Result 2 of docs/audits/2026-07-13-dladder-coupled-recurrence-and-2adic-parity-grading.md found an exact 2-adic parity grading of the √3-component:

    the 2-adic valuation of den(b_k) vanishes iff k is odd

    — equivalently the reduced denominator den(b_k) is odd iff k is odd — the arithmetic shadow of the sinh/cosh (bar-odd/bar-even) split (QOU.QBeta.bar_parity_decomposition). Structural origin (audit §"Structural origin"): the √3 is the Hoefsmit b = √(q⁴+q²+1)/(q²+1) (q⁴+q²+1 = 3 at the branch point), the 2-powers come from (q²+1), the ½ in α, and 2q³ in Q_A; the bar-odd projection φ : ũ ↦ −ũ sorts them by ũ-parity.

    This file provides, per the formalize-first discipline:

    1. a sorry-free verified anchor — the grading holds for the first exact coefficients, machine-checked by decide on their exact reduced denominators (as ℕ — ℚ-division does not kernel-reduce under decide);
    2. the open target as a §3b conjecture class carrying the real content (the grading law for the full sequence) — not proved: the general proof needs the ser_sqrt/ser_div construction formalized, which is not yet in Lean. Per the anti-laundering rule, axiomatizing the class is not a proof of the law.

        def QOU.QBeta.DLadderTwoAdicGrading.TwoAdicParityGraded (den : ℕ → ℕ) (K : ℕ) :

          theorem QOU.QBeta.DLadderTwoAdicGrading.three_not_parity_graded :
          3 ∣ bDen 2 ∧ 3 ∣ bDen 3

          Contrast: p = 3 is NOT graded (sorry-free). 3 divides den(b_k) at both an even index (k=2, den 288 = 2⁵·3²) and an odd index (k=3, den 27 = 3³), so no parity rule 3 ∣ den(b_k) ⟺ (parity of k) can hold — unlike 2, whose grading is bDen_two_adic_graded. Together they witness that 2 is the unique graded prime: only the prime of the ℤ/2 bar-involution sees the parity grading (p=3 is factorial-smooth growth on both parities).

            theorem QOU.QBeta.DLadderTwoAdicGrading.gap_invariant_under_scalar_transform (p : ℕ) [Fact (Nat.Prime p)] (a b f : ℚ) (ha : a ≠ 0) (hb : b ≠ 0) (hf : f ≠ 0) :
            padicValRat p (a / f) - padicValRat p (b / f) = padicValRat p a - padicValRat p b

            q-Borel scalar-transport invariance of the grading gap (sorry-free). Every member of the scalar acceleration family — classical Borel ÷k!, Borel–Leroy ÷(k!)^s (integer s), Jackson q-Borel ÷[k]_Q! / ÷(Q;Q)_k — acts diagonally on the ℚ(√3) components of d_k = a_k + b_k√3: both are divided by the same scalar f ∈ ℚ^×. Since padicValRat p is a valuation, the signed component gap v_p(a_k) − v_p(b_k) is exactly invariant under the whole family: the ℤ/2 bar-parity grading (gap form) survives into every accelerated plane — the invariant is permissive, it obstructs no resummation shape. Compute twin (exact, k ≤ 21, six bases): computations/qbeta/qbeta_dladder_qborel_2adic_transport_probe.py (witness qbeta-dladder-qborel-2adic-transport).
