    Convention-D admissible base: the (1³)-weight has EXACTLY two roots {q, z_D} #

    qou-cds1 (S1, the Q_β c_k ladder under convention D) rests on a base-admissibility fact established by qou-99qc in sympy (computations/qbeta/qbeta_admissible_functionals_bracket_probe.py, 15/15) but not yet Lean-proved:

    Under convention D a functional on H₃(q) descends to the two-row (Temperley–Lieb) quotient iff it annihilates the (1³) ideal, i.e. iff its Markov parameter z is a root of the (1³)-block Ocneanu weight y_(1³)(z). That weight, solved rather than sampled, is quadratic in z with exactly two roots z = q and z = z_D = q³/(q²+1); at z = q the Markov trace is the augmentation, so the admissible set is closed at two elements. The coordinate sum net the ansatz evaluates is tr_M at z = 1, and z = 1 is neither root — so net is structurally excluded.

    This file makes the completeness (exactly two roots; z = 1 excluded) a theorem. It complements WenzlWeightVanishing.lean, which proves the row criterion at the fixed level r = 2 (z = z_D ⇒ the ≥3-row blocks die); here z is a variable and both roots are exhibited.

    The exact weight (cited input, from the Markov-trace character solve on H₃) #

    The (1³)-block Ocneanu weight at n = 3, as a function of the Markov parameter z, is

      y_(1³)(z) = q² · N(q, z) / (q⁴ + q² + 1),   N(q, z) = (q²+1)z² − (2q³+q)z + q⁴,

    with denominator q⁴+q²+1 > 0 over ℝ (never vanishing). The roots are the roots of the numerator N, and this file proves N factors — over any commutative ring — as

      N(q, z) = (z − q) · ((q²+1)·z − q³),

    so the two admissible Markov parameters {q, z_D} are manifest and z = 1 is excluded on the substrate window 1 < q < (supergolden ratio ≈ 1.4656, the root of x³ = x² + 1; NOT the plastic number ≈ 1.32472, which is the root of x³ = x + 1).

    What is cited vs. what is proved. That N is the (1³)-weight numerator is the external input (the exact Markov-character solve; the sympy probe). The algebra — the factorisation, the exactly-two-roots equivalence, and the z = 1 exclusion — is what is proved here, unconditionally. No c_k, no base choice, nothing wired; this hardens the foundation the D re-derivation stands on, it does not pick the base.

    Base ring (§7c): the factorisation and the root iff are generic (CommRing / Field); only the z = 1 exclusion is archimedean (ℝ, ordering), and it lives below the wall as a separate lemma.

On the substrate window (1 < q, q below the supergolden ratio), the (1³)-weight numerator vanishes at exactly z = q and z = z_D, and not at z = 1. This is the Lean form of qou-99qc's measured completeness — the base functional of the convention-D Q_β ladder is one of exactly two (augmentation z = q, or Markov trace at z_D), and the ansatz's coordinate-sum base is structurally excluded.
