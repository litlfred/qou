    G2 form-clash — the proved affine transport vs the axiomatised cosine (qou-avt1) #

    QOU.QBeta.CrossingWeightAffine.evalMap_affine proves the counit→trace transport factor is affine in the crossing weight w, hence — reparametrised by the counit-minus-trace gap α := (z−1)h/2 (so that ε − tr_M = evalMap h 1 − evalMap h z = −h(z−1) = −2α, the exact qou-avt1 identity) — the transport factor

    T(α) = evalMap h z / evalMap h 1 = 1 − 4α / (h(h−2))

    is linear in α. Its even part (T(α)+T(−α))/2 is therefore the constant 1: the affine transport carries no even-order content.

    QOU.Interactions.QBetaCosResummation (axiomatised via the class TransferMatrixEigenphase) asserts the Q_β correction is cos α, whose even part is cos α itself, = 1 − α²/2 + O(α⁴) — nonconstant.

    Consequence (G2). The two even parts agree only where cos α = 1, i.e. α ∈ 2πℤ (α = 0 at q = 1). So the entire O(α²) content of the cosine resummation is carried by the axiom alone; nothing in the proved (KC19) affine transport sources it — sharpening qou-avt1's "the cosine is the thing to drop" into "the cosine is the sole carrier of the even-order correction", and explaining the ~300× overshoot (a transport linear in α cannot source a quadratic α² term).

    This is a statement about functional form in α, independent of the Markov-z convention (C vs D): evalMap is over a generic field and α is a free parameter, so the form-clash is upstream of the C/D question that gates the numerical w* (qou-cds1). Base ring: generic field for the affine half (§7c), ℝ only for the cosine half (archimedean).

    Companion: computations/qbeta/qbeta_counit_trace_transport_overshoot_probe.py (G1–G5, 6/6). Verified T even-part = 1 symbolically (sympy) two ways before formalising.

    def QOU.QBeta.LadderCosClash.transportFactor {R : Type u_1} [Ring R] (k α : R) :
    R

    The affine counit→trace transport factor as a function of the gap α, with a fixed kernel k: T(α) = 1 − k·α. Linear in α.
