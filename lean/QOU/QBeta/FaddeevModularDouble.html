    Faddeev modular-double quantum dilogarithm — structural axiomatisation #

    The Faddeev non-compact quantum dilogarithm Φ_b — the correct decay-face object (bar-odd / root-of-unity / Chern–Simons), not the compact q-Pochhammer ∏(1−q^{-k}z)^{-1} used in the mass tower — is a transcendental function not available in Mathlib. This file provides:

    1. the bar-parity decomposition (the sinh/cosh = bar-odd/bar-even split) over any commutative ring with a ring involution σ — the algebraic core of "modular double b ↔ b^{-1} = bar involution q ↔ q^{-1}; symmetric part = cosh (mass/Rogers face), antisymmetric part = sinh (decay/CS face)";
    2. a class axiomatising Φ_b's defining relations (modular invariance Φ_b = Φ_{b^{-1}}; Faddeev inversion Φ(x)Φ(-x) = c) with a derived consequence — the §3b pattern (the transcendental function is the open object; its structural identities are formalised).

    Scope: this does not construct Φ_b, and does not claim Φ_b is the canonical archimedean realisation of the decay step — that is the open f-map (a §3b-cond conditional). See docs/scope/2026-07-06-qbeta-fmap-torus-knot-cs-phase-geometry.md and docs/audits/2026-07-06-qbeta-faddeev-phi-b-modular-double-accuracy.md.

    2 * f = f + σ f + (f - σ f) ∧ σ (f + σ f) = f + σ f ∧ σ (f - σ f) = -(f - σ f)

    Bar-parity decomposition (the sinh/cosh split under the modular involution). Over any commutative ring, for a ring involution σ (the bar q ↦ q^{-1} / modular double b ↦ b^{-1}), the doubled element 2·f splits as a bar-even (cosh; mass/Rogers face) part f + σ f, fixed by σ, plus a bar-odd (sinh; decay/CS face) part f − σ f, negated by σ.

      σ-unitarity — the algebraic form of |u| = 1 (the fibre-saddle phase) #

      Numerically witnessed on the validated evaluator (computations/qbeta/qbeta_fmap_phi_b_root_of_unity_reference.py): at real b (root-of-unity q = e^{2πib²}, the fibre/decay saddle) and real z, |Φ_b(z)| = 1 — Φ_b is a pure phase. The analytic reason is the reflection w ↦ −w̄ of the Faddeev contour integral (Hikami math/0604094 eq (2.1)), which sends log Φ_b to −(log Φ_b)‾ for real b, z.

      The lemmas below isolate the algebraic core over any commutative ring with a ring involution σ (archimedean instance: complex conjugation): σ-unitaries form a multiplicative monoid, and — the arc-closing rigidity — a bar-even σ-unitary is an involution (u² = 1), so any genuine phase (u² ≠ 1) necessarily carries bar-odd content. This is the algebraic reason the unimodular fibre-saddle Φ_b is a bar-ODD (decay/sinh) carrier and the Q_β/Q_A = cos Φ(u) phase reframe is the natural bar-odd form.

        IsSigmaUnitary σ (σ u₁ * u₂) ∧ u₁ * (σ u₁ * u₂) = u₂

        Cross-anchor connecting factor is a pure phase. For two σ-unitaries u₁, u₂ (the fibre-saddle Φ_b at the proton and muon anchors), the connecting factor w = σ(u₁)·u₂ is itself σ-unitary and satisfies u₁·w = u₂. Hence all cross-anchor variation between σ-unitaries is carried by a σ-unitary (pure-phase) factor — the magnitude channel is flat, and the Δc_3 swing is a phase-channel (bar-odd) quantity.
