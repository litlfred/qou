    QOU.QBeta.AomegaM2BPSCohomology — Route C, Milestone M2: the closed-sector BPS spectrum would be ABELIAN (conditional characterization, not a construction) #

    Route C milestone ladder (docs/audits/2026-08-11-route-c-bps-cohomology-scoping.md §4): M0 (bar-eigenspace reframe) and M1 (the eq-(98) DT dictionary + eq-200 q¹ erratum) are landed; M2 asks: "for that small (Q,W) compute BPS_d (Davison–Meinhardt) and check dim reproduces {3,4} with the bar/chirality grading."

    This file records the finite M2 result on the certified eq-(98) datum (StokesMatrixProvenance). The one structural fact M2 was meant to decide:

    Is the "4 = s(1)+1" extra state a motivic multiplicity on a repeated charge (a higher BPS cohomology carrying q-corrections), or a genuine extra BPS state at a distinct Borel ray (an abelian spectrum with trivial plethystic)?

    Answer: distinct ray ⇒ abelian. Every eq-(98) Stokes constant sits at its own Borel singularity ι_{i,j} (splus_rays_nodup / sminus_rays_nodup). The Kontsevich–Soibelman spectrum generator is therefore a product of elementary factors at pairwise-distinct rays, the Davison–Meinhardt plethystic log is trivial, and dim BPS_d = |Ω_d| with no motivic/q-correction. The closed-sector BPS cohomology is a direct sum of 1-dimensional spaces, one per (charge, chirality), and needs no superpotential W to realize {3,4}. That is why A_ω and the whole Stokes tower 3, 9, 75, 642, … are integers, never Poincaré polynomials.

    DERIVED (certified integer algebra, this file + StokesMatrixProvenance): the eq-(98) reconstruction, the {3,4} chirality spectrum, the (7,1) bar split, s(1)=3 for 4₁, and the pairwise-distinctness of the Borel rays that makes the spectrum abelian.

    CONJECTURAL (NOT claimed here): that the closed-sector 3d–3d category of T[K] IS this (Q,W). That identification is the M3 bridge (categorified AJ / Hecke→BPS), literature-open, and gated by the anti-laundering rule. Nothing here derives A_ω; A_ω = 3 remains certified-as-GGM-DT-count, conditionally adopted.
