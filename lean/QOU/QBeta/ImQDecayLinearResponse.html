    Bar-odd ⇒ odd in ε: the structural root of the c₃ S sign-flip #

    The bar-odd part S = Q_β/E₀ of the β-decay observable is the decay projection Im(q) (conj:im-q-physical-role candidate (iii); merged #3600). Writing the substrate parameter with a small imaginary part q = q₀ + iε, the bar involution σ : q ↦ q⁻¹ acts on ε and forces the bar-odd observable to be an odd function of ε. Consequently sign S = sign ε, so the measured S(muon) > 0 > S(proton) is a parity consequence — the sign-flip is structural, not a fit (and no real q₀, where ε = 0, can produce it).

    This file formalizes the clean algebraic root of that statement for the figure-eight operator trace T (the merged #3600 closed form), whose real integer coefficients make complex conjugation commute with evaluation: T(\overline z) = \overline{T(z)}. Taking z = q₀ + iε (so \overline z = q₀ − iε) gives Im T(q₀ − iε) = − Im T(q₀ + iε) — the bar-odd oddness in ε.

    Base ring is ℂ by necessity (§7c archimedean side): complex conjugation and the Im projection are the content. No sorry.
