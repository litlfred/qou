    QOU.QBeta.FMapStructuralPaths #

    This file scaffolds the three categorical pathways (drivers) for constructing the qBetaFMap operator, which maps the $z$-recursion coefficients ($a_n$) into the Q_β precision ladder ($c_k$).

    As demonstrated by the exact Habiro trace difference $\Delta\text{tr}_M(q)$, the scalar ratio $\Delta(\text{net}) / \Delta\text{tr}_M(q)$ has a pole at $q=1$ of order $\mathcal{O}(w^{-1})$. Therefore, the f-map cannot be a simple scalar multiplication; it must be a structured functor acting on the operator spectrum.

    Scaffolded Pathways #

    1. Faddeev Recursion (Modular Limit): Bridges the trace via $\Phi_q$.
    2. Hexagon Equations: Extracts $c_k$ from the $V_{(2,1)}$ braiding eigenvalues.
    3. Direct Cyclotomic Map: Maps the integer coefficients $a_m \in \mathbb{Z}[u]$ directly to the ladder via algebraic reconstruction.
