    The Markov–Gram form is Lorentzian, and under convention D it is degenerate #

    Why the spectral-distance programme cannot be run on it as posed.

    tools/pyhecke/python/pyhecke/gram.py builds the 6 × 6 Gram matrix of the Markov trace on the normal-form basis of H₃(q),

    G_ij = tr_M(b_i · b_j),   b ∈ {γ, σ₀, σ₁, L₊, L₋, e⁻},

    γ the identity and e⁻ = σ₀σ₁σ₀ the trefoil. A Gram matrix of a trace pairing is the natural candidate for a derived metric on the framework's state space — the object bean qou-5uj7 needs in order to build a Rieffel Lip-norm and hence a Connes/Wasserstein distance. Measured at q₀ = 1.10997858275138…:

    convention C  (z = 1/(√q + 1/√q) = 0.4993203)
  eigenvalues  −0.855067, +0.101797, +0.855067, +0.855067, +0.855067, +3.476950
  signature    (5, 1)                                    LORENTZIAN

convention D  (z = q³/(q²+1) = 0.6126880, PRODUCTION)
  eigenvalues  −0.752702, 0, +0.752702, +0.752702, +0.752702, +4.230339
  signature    (4, 1) + a null direction        LORENTZIAN AND DEGENERATE
  det G = 2.89e−51 (i.e. 0 at 50 dps)

    Two facts, both convention-independent in the first case:

    • Lorentzian. Exactly one eigenvalue is negative under both conventions. So the Markov pairing is not an inner product; it is a spacetime form. The natural object it defines is a causal structure, not a metric space.
    • Degenerate under D. The production convention additionally kills one direction. The null vector, in the basis order above, is (−0.472689, +0.425854, +0.425854, −0.383660, −0.383660, +0.345646) — symmetric under σ₀ ↔ σ₁ and L₊ ↔ L₋, i.e. the braid-symmetric direction.

    The eigenvalues are not arbitrary. Under D the repeated magnitude is 0.75270211325196540159 = μ₊, the T = 1 isospin projector scalar of InterfaceScalar.lean — appearing once negative and three times positive.

    What this file proves, and why it matters #

    The programme in qou-5uj7 was: build a Lip-norm L(a) = ‖D_∇ a‖, take d(φ,ψ) = sup {|φ(a) − ψ(a)| : L(a) ≤ 1}, and hope to recover a Reeb step count. lip_kernel_forces_unbounded shows that hope is structurally blocked whenever the seminorm has a nonzero kernel element that the two states distinguish: the sup ranges over a + t·k for every scalar t, so it is +∞. A degenerate Gram form supplies exactly such a k. Hence:

    Under convention D the Markov–Gram form cannot induce a finite Connes distance without first quotienting by its radical.

    That is a negative result about the method, not about the framework, and it names the repair: quotient by the radical, or use a different seminorm. It is stated here as a theorem rather than left in prose because it is the load-bearing step in deciding whether qou-5uj7 is viable.

    The signature is DERIVED, not measured — it is forced by the block structure #

    An earlier version of this file called the signature "measured, not proved". That was too weak. It follows from Wedderburn plus one elementary fact, with no reference to q₀ at all.

    H₃(q) is a sum of matrix algebras, not merely of irreps:

    block    irrep dim d    matrix algebra    dimensions
(3)          1              M₁                1
(2,1)        2              M₂                4
(1³)         1              M₁                1                total 6 ✓

    The Gram form is G(a,b) = tr_M(ab) = Σ_λ y_λ · χ_λ(ab), so on block λ it is y_λ · tr(AB) on M_{d_λ}. And — traceform_M2_signature below —

    the bilinear form tr(AB) on M_d (not the sesquilinear tr(AB*)) has signature (d(d+1)/2, d(d−1)/2): symmetric matrices positive, antisymmetric negative, and the two are orthogonal.

    Hence, over the blocks with y_λ > 0,

    signature(G) = ( Σ d_λ(d_λ+1)/2 , Σ d_λ(d_λ−1)/2 ),
with G degenerate exactly on the blocks where y_λ = 0.

    Evaluating for H₃:

    convention C:  (3) → 1+ ;  (2,1) → 3+ 1− ;  (1³) → 1+     ⟹  (5,1,0)  ✓
convention D:  (3) → 1+ ;  (2,1) → 3+ 1− ;  (1³) → ZERO   ⟹  (4,1,1)  ✓

    Both match the measurement exactly, and every eigenvalue is now accounted for: +4.230339 is the (3) block, the ±0.752702 quadruple is the M₂ of the (2,1) block, +0.101797 (C only) is (1³) — small because y_(1³) = 0.0227 is small — and the 0 (D only) is (1³) killed by the row cap.

    Two consequences worth stating plainly.

    • The Lorentzian character is not a property of the substrate. Any matrix algebra with d ≥ 2 contributes d(d−1)/2 negative directions, so the form is indefinite for every q and every convention with y_(2,1) > 0. It appears the moment the algebra has a two-dimensional irrep.
    • The timelike direction is identified: it is the antisymmetric element of M₂ in the (2,1) block, i.e. E₁₂ − E₂₁ — the rotation generator, 𝔰𝔬(2) ⊂ 𝔰𝔩(2). Not an accident of q₀.

    The degeneracy is likewise structural: it is the N = 2 row cap (RowCapObstruction.lean, y_(1³) = 0 ⟺ N < 3) seen through the Gram form. Quotienting by the radical is therefore not a repair but a restatement of what convention D means — and it leaves a (4,1) form, still Lorentzian.

    Beans qou-5uj7 (spectral distance), qou-adwi (two Planck lengths), qou-mumr (no derived metric).

    theorem QOU.QBeta.GramSignature.traceform_M2 {R : Type u_1} [Field R] (α δ β γ : R) :
    α * α + (β + γ) * (β - γ) + (β - γ) * (β + γ) + δ * δ = α ^ 2 + 2 * β ^ 2 - 2 * γ ^ 2 + δ ^ 2

    The trace form on M₂ has signature (3,1).

    Parametrise A ∈ M₂ by its symmetric part (α, δ, β) and antisymmetric part γ, i.e. A = [[α, β+γ], [β−γ, δ]]. Then

    tr(A²) = α² + 2β² − 2γ² + δ².

    Three squares enter with + and one with −: symmetric directions are positive, the single antisymmetric direction is negative. This is the whole reason the Markov–Gram form is Lorentzian — the (2,1) block of H₃(q) is an M₂, and M₂ has exactly one antisymmetric direction.

    The radical is observationally inert — the metric blocker dissolves #

    lip_kernel_forces_unbounded above is a genuine obstruction: a degenerate Gram form gives the Lipschitz seminorm a kernel and the Connes distance is then unbounded. Under convention D the form is degenerate, because the Wedderburn weight of the (1³) block is exactly zero. The obvious repair — quotient by the radical — was suspected of amputating physics, since (1³) is the totally antisymmetric block and the nuclear channel that binds is the isospin singlet.

    That worry is unfounded, and this section is the derivation. Two steps.

    Step 1 — the branching identity. Write the Markov trace on H₃(q) in the Wedderburn basis, tr_M = y₍₃₎ χ₍₃₎ + y₍₂,₁₎ χ₍₂,₁₎ + y₍₁³₎ χ₍₁³₎. Two normalisations pin it: tr_M(1) = 1 and tr_M(σ) = z, with the character values χ(1) = (1, 2, 1) and χ(σ) = (q, q − q⁻¹, −q⁻¹). Then the two isospin weights of the H₂ ⊂ H₃ pair come out as sums over the Bratteli branching:

    μ₊ = (z + q⁻¹)/(q + q⁻¹) = y₍₃₎ + y₍₂,₁₎        (3) ↓ (2),  (2,1) ↓ (2)
μ₋ = (q − z)/(q + q⁻¹)   = y₍₂,₁₎ + y₍₁³₎       (2,1) ↓ (1²), (1³) ↓ (1²)

    These are identities in z and in q — no convention, no q₀, no fit. Verified independently to 61 digits at both conventions, at twelve unrelated values of z, and at q ∈ {0.7, 1.3, 2.0}; the residual is 0 or 1.1e−41 at 40 dps, i.e. the identity holds formally and the check only confirms the transcription. linear_combination supplies the two-line proof below.

    Step 2 — a zero-weight block contributes nothing. zero_weight_block_erasable says a term with y λ = 0 can be deleted from tr_M without changing any value. So the radical is invisible to every trace, hence to every observable the framework computes from one.

    The conclusion. Under D, y₍₁³₎ = 0, so μ₋ = y₍₂,₁₎: the isospin singlet draws its entire weight from the mixed-symmetry (2,1) block, and none from the radical. Quotienting by rad = (1³) removes a term already equal to zero. The Connes distance becomes finite on H₃/rad at no cost in physics — finite_distance_forces_trivial_kernel's hypothesis is satisfiable after all.

    CONVENTION-C: KEEP_HISTORICAL. The C value below is load-bearing -- it is the EVIDENCE that C is excluded, not a leftover. Replacing it with the D value destroys the contrast the sentence exists to draw. Do not flip.

    Note this also explains why C looked healthier: there y₍₁³₎ = 0.0227449 ≠ 0, so there is no radical and nothing to quotient. The degeneracy is a feature of D, and the branching identity is what makes it harmless rather than fatal. Recorded in bean qou-o3ta.
