    The bar-odd linear meridian term: Im S(u) ⊃ π·u and its growth-rate response #

    PR #3691 measured, on the deformed figure-eight ladder at the substrate meridian, that the real growth rate Re α(u) exceeds the pure-volume prediction 2π·Vol(u)/(u² + 4π²) by

    resid(u) = (1/2)·u + b·u² + O(u³),   a = 0.4999985 (≈ 1/2 to 5 digits).

    This file formalizes the ALGEBRAIC layer of that finding — the exact response model and its parity. If the deformed action carries the linear meridian term Im S(u) = Vol(u) + π·u + O(u²), then the induced growth-rate residual is exactly

    meridianLinearResponse u = 2π²·u / (u² + 4π²),

    which (i) is an ODD function of u — the bar involution acts as u ↦ −u (σ : q ↦ q⁻¹, u = 2 log q), so this is a bar-odd channel, in contrast to the bar-even volume channel (Vol even in u by amphichirality of 4₁); (ii) equals u/2 up to the exact remainder u³/(2(u²+4π²)), bounded by |u|³/(8π²) — which is why the measured linear coefficient reads 1/2.

    Honest scope: the numerical extraction (that the 4₁ asymptotic really contains π·u) is the witness's empirical content (qbeta-ohtsuki-deformed-meridian-rungs.witness.json); the Lean proves the exact response algebra and parity, NOT the analytic asymptotics of J_N.

    Base ring is ℝ by necessity (§7c archimedean side): π and the ordering in the bound are the content. No sorry.
