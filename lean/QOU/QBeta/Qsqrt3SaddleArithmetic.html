    QOU.QBeta.Qsqrt3SaddleArithmetic #

    The even-channel resurgence action's arithmetic home is ℚ(√3).

    The 4₁ (figure-eight) Kashaev sum Σ_k exp(2 Σ_{j≤k} log(2 sin(πj/N))) localises, in the N → ∞ volume-conjecture limit, at the saddle x* = 5/6 (the maximum of G(x) = ∫₀ˣ log(2 sin πs) ds, where G'(x*) = log(2 sin(5π/6)) = log 1 = 0). The perturbative expansion around that saddle is built from the Taylor derivatives of log(2 sin πx) at x*, and those derivatives are exact elements of ℚ(√3)·π^k — real quadratic data. The invariant trace field of 4₁ is the imaginary quadratic field ℚ(√−3) (Eisenstein / hexagonal); it is a distinct number field from the totally real ℚ(√3) that carries the certified saddle data. Both sit inside the cyclotomic field ℚ(ζ₁₂), and √−3 = i·√3 relates the two generators, but the fields themselves are not equal (one totally real, one imaginary quadratic). This file certifies the real ℚ(√3) saddle arithmetic; the ℚ(√−3) trace field is the ambient classical fact it refracts.

    This file certifies that arithmetic, sorry-free:

    • sin_saddle / grad_saddle_zero — x* = 5/6 is a critical point.
    • cot_saddle / hessian_eq — the Hessian G''(x*) = π·cot(5π/6) = −√3·π ∈ ℚ(√3)·π.
    • laplaceB1_at_saddle — the leading saddle-Laplace coefficient b₁ = g₄/(8 g₂²) + 5 g₃²/(24 |g₂|³) evaluated at the exact exponent Taylor data g_k = 2·G^{(k)}(x*) (the sum is exp(2N·G), so the Laplace exponent is 2G), i.e. (g₂, g₃, g₄) = (−2√3π, −8π², −16√3π³), equals √3·π/54 ∈ ℚ(√3)·π — an exact arithmetic object, replacing the entropy-blocked numerical PSLQ of #3571.

    noncomputable def QOU.QBeta.Qsqrt3Saddle.laplaceB1 (g2 g3 g4 : ℝ) :
    ℝ

    The leading saddle-Laplace 1/N coefficient as a function of the first three saddle Taylor data (g₂, g₃, g₄): b₁ = g₄/(8 g₂²) + 5 g₃²/(24 |g₂|³).

    Domain: g₂ < 0. A Laplace 1/N expansion exists only at a maximum of the exponent, where the second derivative g₂ = 2·G''(x*) is strictly negative; there |g₂| = −g₂, which is what the (−g2)^3 denominator encodes. This is the natural (non-misuse-prone) domain — the coefficient is meaningless at g₂ ≥ 0. The sole call site (laplaceB1_at_saddle) instantiates g₂ = −2√3π < 0 concretely, so no separate hypothesis is threaded.
