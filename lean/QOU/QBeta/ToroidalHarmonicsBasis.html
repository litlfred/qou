    Toroidal Harmonics Basis for the Q_beta Ladder #

    This module scaffolds the proof that the continuous analytic extension of the discrete Habiro trace over the (2,3) torus knot projects exactly onto Toroidal Harmonics of half-integer degree. We formalise the Liouville-Green transformation mapping the exact Toroidal Harmonic ODE to the local Parabolic Cylinder (Weber) ODE near its turning points, and define the change of variables linking the toroidal Legendre operator to a second-order operator in w.

    Main definitions #

    • ToroidalOperator / ToroidalMarkovWOperator: exact transformation between x and w limits.
    • ToroidalHarmonicODE: The exact ODE in the toroidal coordinate $\eta$.
    • WeberODE: The local Parabolic Cylinder ODE limit in the substrate coordinate $u$.
    • f_map_projection: The mode-selective transformation isolating $P_{n-1/2}^m$.

    Tags #

      noncomputable def QOU.QBeta.x_of_w (w : ℝ) :
      ℝ

      The change of variables x(w) = -1 + 1/(2w). The definition itself is convention-neutral: it holds for whatever w is later identified with.

      Reading under convention D (canonical; z = q³/(q²+1), q = e^η): with the negative loop value z₋ = z − (q − q⁻¹) = 1/(q(q²+1)), the bar-invariant product w = z·z₋ = 1/[2]_q² = 1/(2(cosh 2η + 1)) gives x = cosh 2η. The former convention-C reading w = z² = 1/(2(cosh η + 1)), x = cosh η (with z = √q/(q+1)) is excised; under D, w = z² gives x ≈ 0.332 < 1 at the substrate and has no toroidal reading.

      Open: whether w = z·z₋ is the right variable is not established. The proved Markov-parameter recursion is written in q − 1, a different variable, and no link from it to this w is proved here (bean qou-nxjw).

            The Toroidal Harmonic Operator intertwines with ToroidalMarkovWOperator in the setting of differential algebra. We formalize this by taking a commutative algebra K over ℝ that is a field, equipped with a derivation D (representing d/dw). This bypasses the analytic limits and DifferentiableAt side conditions by working in the formal algebraic geometry of the differential operators.

            Prop

            Opaque stub, not the ODE. Intended: the toroidal-harmonic ODE of degree n and order m, _F'' - (n² - 1/4 + (m² - 1/4)/sinh²η) _F = 0. Actually: True, ignoring every argument — the body computes the potential V and then discards it, because the differential operator has no Mathlib formalism here yet.

            So this is a name reserved for a claim, not the claim. It is Prop, it is inhabited by trivial, and it is equal to WeberODE and to every other tautology. Any statement quantified over it says nothing until the body is replaced. qou-p3pl tracks the real formalisation.

                The Liouville-Green limit maps the global Toroidal Harmonic ODE to the local Parabolic Cylinder ODE. The phenomenological constants map to A=2, B~12.13, C=-22.

                Not proved, and not provable as stated. ToroidalHarmonicODE and WeberODE are both the constant True (see their docstrings), so the equivalence below is True ↔ True, closed by Iff.rfl with T := id. It holds for any T, any n, m, and any A, B, C — the hypotheses hA : A = 2 and hC : C = -22 are unused, which is the cheapest way to see that no asymptotics are being established. The statement is a placeholder recording the intended theorem; qou-p3pl carries the obligation to prove it once the two operators are real.
