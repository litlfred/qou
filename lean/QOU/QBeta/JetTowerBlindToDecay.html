    ∑ i ∈ s, W i * m₁ i = ∑ i ∈ s, W i * m₂ i

    The reindexing that makes the tower blind to decay. On a finite index set closed under an involution t, if the weight W is t-invariant and the two multiplicities swap under t, the two weighted sums coincide — so their difference, which is what the decay channel sees, vanishes identically.

    Instantiated at ι = partitions of k, t = transpose, W = the q-Plancherel weight (a function of the hook multiset, which transpose permutes) and m₁, m₂ = the trivial and sign branching multiplicities.
