    phiBProd is meromorphic ⇐ its two products are analytic (brick toward qou-7xvy) #

    The divisor route to the Faddeev–Volkov uniqueness hr_entire input (QOU.QBeta.RatioEntireOfSameDivisor.entire_toMeromorphicNFOn_div_of_same_order) needs, for the concrete Φ_b = phiBProd, three facts: MeromorphicOn (phiBProd b), meromorphicOrderAt (phiBProd b) ≠ ⊤, and the same-divisor premise. This file discharges the first of the three, reducing it to a single, honest, Mathlib-shaped analytic input.

    phiBProd b = (fun z ↦ ∏' k, numFactor b z k) / (fun z ↦ ∏' k, denFactor b z k) is a quotient of two infinite products (definitionally). So

    if each product is analytic on ℂ (AnalyticOnNhd), then phiBProd b is meromorphic on ℂ — an analytic function divided by an analytic function is meromorphic (AnalyticOnNhd.meromorphicOn + MeromorphicOn.div).

    The remaining open input is exactly analyticity of the two tprods — i.e. a locally-uniformly-convergent infinite product of entire functions is entire. PhiBProd already proves the products are Multipliable (pointwise convergence, geometric bounds for Im b² > 0); upgrading Multipliable to AnalyticOnNhd is the infinite-product-analyticity fact that Mathlib does not yet package, and it is the honest §3b-cond hypothesis carried here — not axiomatised, and strictly weaker than the conclusion (analyticity of the factors, not proportionality of solutions).

    Verified lean-direct against the pinned Meromorphic cone (AnalyticOnNhd.meromorphicOn, MeromorphicOn.div) + PhiBProd. Sorry-free.

This discharges the MeromorphicOn input of the divisor-route brick (c) for the concrete Φ_b, leaving only AnalyticOnNhd of the products (the infinite-product-analyticity input) open.
