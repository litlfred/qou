                The consequence for the resummation #

                theorem QOU.QBeta.DFrame.bridgePhase_C_bar_odd {K : Type u_1} [Field K] (u : K) (hu0 : u ≠ 0) (h2 : u ^ 2 + 1 ≠ 0) :
