          theorem QOU.QBeta.HopfCubic.cHopf_discriminant_eq :
          18 * -6 * 10 * -6 - 4 * (-6) ^ 3 * -6 + (-6) ^ 2 * 10 ^ 2 - 4 * 10 ^ 3 - 27 * (-6) ^ 2 = -76

          The Hopf cubic has negative discriminant −76 = −2²·19. A depressed real cubic with negative discriminant has exactly one real root and a complex-conjugate pair — so the odd channel's arithmetic is genuinely complex (the chiral realization), not real like the amphichiral even channel. The prime 19 is the arithmetic fingerprint shared with the total C (disc −152 = −2³·19). Stated as the exact integer discriminant of y³ − 6y² + 10y − 6 (a = −6, b = 10, c = −6).
