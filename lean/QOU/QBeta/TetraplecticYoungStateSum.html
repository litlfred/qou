                      Tetraplectic → Young correspondence (§3b-cond conditional class) #

                      The deep content — that the S₅-equivariant Nekrasov localization of L²(M,Ω) organizes the finite state-sum into exactly the partition-indexed irrep sectors — is conjectural and is carried as hypothesis fields, not proved. No instance is constructed (the correspondence stays open); the derived theorems below are genuine consequences of the hypotheses.

                      source
                      structure QOU.QBeta.TetraplecticYoungStateSum.YoungOrganization :
                      Type

                      Tetraplectic → Young organization data for the N = 5 finite state-sum.

                      The two hypothesis fields axiomatize the conjectural physics inputs (per AGENTS.md §3b-cond) — they are NOT theorems of this file:

                      • nekrasov_occupied — Nekrasov localization leaves no empty fixed-point stratum: every partition sector carries positive multiplicity in L²(M,Ω);
                      • plancherel — the seminormal dimensions f^λ (numbers of standard Young tableaux of shape λ) satisfy the Burnside/Plancherel identity Σ_λ (f^λ)² = 120 = |S₅|.

                      mult and dim are the multiplicity and seminormal-dimension data; the file derives occupied_card and plancherel_eq_perm from the two hypotheses.

                      • mult : Nat.Partition 5 → ℕ

                        Multiplicity of the irrep V_λ in L²(M,Ω), indexed by partitions of 5.

                      • dim : Nat.Partition 5 → ℕ

                        Seminormal dimension f^λ — the number of standard Young tableaux of shape λ.

                      • nekrasov_occupied (p : Nat.Partition 5) : 0 < self.mult p

                        HYPOTHESIS (Nekrasov localization, conjectural): every partition sector is occupied.

                      • plancherel : ∑ p : Nat.Partition 5, self.dim p ^ 2 = 120

                        HYPOTHESIS (Burnside / Plancherel, conjectural): Σ_λ (f^λ)² = 120.
