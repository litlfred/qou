    ℝ

    The square of the canonical Hoefsmit ladder base, b² = (q⁴ + q² + 1)/(q² + 1)².

      source
      theorem QOU.QBeta.hoefsmitBSq_eq_h (q : ℝ) (hq : q ≠ 0) :
      hoefsmitBSq q = ((q - q⁻¹) ^ 2 + 3) / ((q - q⁻¹) ^ 2 + 4)

      b² written through the Hecke parameter h = q − q⁻¹: b² = (h² + 3)/(h² + 4) (the form used by the mainstream c_k probes). This makes bar-evenness manifest, since h² is bar-even.

      source
      theorem QOU.QBeta.hoefsmitBSq_bar_even (q : ℝ) (hq : q ≠ 0) :
      hoefsmitBSq q⁻¹ = hoefsmitBSq q

      b² is bar-even: b²(q⁻¹) = b²(q). The Hoefsmit ladder base is invariant under the bar involution q ↦ q⁻¹.

          theorem QOU.QBeta.uSq_bar_covariance (q : ℝ) (hq : q ≠ 0) :
          uSq q⁻¹ = q ^ 4 * uSq q

          The corrected ladder covariance: u²(q⁻¹) = q⁴·u²(q), i.e. u ↦ +q²·u under the bar involution — u is bar-EVEN-covariant.

          This is the load-bearing correction: the criterion-2b prune asserted u ↦ −q²·u (bar-odd-covariant) from a non-canonical b; the canonical b (bar-even) gives +q² with no sign, so the bar-even-kernel law on the coefficients is c_k(q⁻¹) = q^{−2k}·c_k(q) with no (−1)^k.
