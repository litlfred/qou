                §3 det = 1 is genuinely necessary #

                A 2×2 matrix has spectrum {e^{+iα}, e^{−iα}} exactly when its characteristic polynomial is X² − 2cos α · X + 1. The predicate below is the one QOU.QBeta.EigenphaseCarrier.TransferMatrixSpectrum already states; it is restated here so the exclusions can be phrased against it directly.
