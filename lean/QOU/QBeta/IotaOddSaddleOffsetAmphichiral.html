    QOU.QBeta.IotaOddSaddleOffsetAmphichiral — the saddle offset vanishes iff amphichiral #

    Formalize-first structural GATE for the owner's saddle-vector picture. A knot-particle is pictured as a vector (angle + magnitude) attached to a saddle point of Bring's surface. At q = 1 the bar-involution q ↔ q⁻¹ acts trivially, the two chirality-conjugate saddle contributions coincide, and the vector is origin-centred. For q > 1 the bar-involution is nontrivial, the two contributions split, and the vector acquires an offset — and the claim is that the offset is precisely the ι-odd (bar-odd, q ↔ q⁻¹ antisymmetric) component of the saddle vector.

    This file proves the honest gate for that picture: the ι-odd saddle offset vanishes iff the knot is (ι-fixed) amphichiral. It is deliberately not a True-placeholder; the discriminating content is threefold.

    The involution ι and the offset #

    Let M be a module over a commutative ring R in which 2 is invertible, and let ι : M →ₗ[R] M be an R-linear involution (ι ∘ ι = id) — the chirality / mirror involution, i.e. the bar-involution q ↔ q⁻¹ acting at the observable (saddle-vector) level. Decompose any v into its ι-even and ι-odd parts $$\mathrm{even}(v) = \tfrac12\,(v + ι v),\qquad \mathrm{offset}(v) = \mathrm{odd}(v) = \tfrac12\,(v - ι v).$$

    What is proved (non-vacuous — three certified facts) #

    • even_add_odd — even(v) + offset(v) = v: a genuine direct-sum decomposition (uses Invertible 2; false without the ½).
    • iota_offset_odd — ι(offset v) = -offset v: the offset genuinely lands in the (-1)-eigenspace of ι, i.e. it really is the ι-odd component (uses ι linear + ι∘ι = id; false for a non-involution).
    • offset_eq_zero_iff_iotaFixed — the headline gate: offset v = 0 ↔ ι v = v. The offset vanishes exactly on the ι-fixed (amphichiral) locus.

    Grounding in the certified 5₂ / 4₁ Stokes data (the anti-vacuity anchor) #

    Modelling the saddle vector as the ordered pair of chirality-conjugate leading Stokes constants v_K = (S⁺-const, S⁻-const) with ι the chirality swap (a,b) ↦ (b,a) (the amphichiral involution of BarOddAntisymOffDiagonal, here acting on the two chirality labels), the certified merged data of FiveTwoDTSpectrumSplit gives:

    • offset_fourone_zero — 4₁ is amphichiral: its two chirality constants coincide (Splus0_41 0 1 = 3, one member), so v_{4₁} = (3,3) is ι-fixed and offset(4₁) = 0.
    • offset_fivetwo_ne_zero — 5₂ is chiral: its constants split (Splus0 0 1 = 4, Sminus0 0 2 = 3), so v_{5₂} = (4,3) is not ι-fixed and offset(5₂) ≠ 0.
    • fivetwo_offset_numerator_eq_chiralityOdd — the offset's antisymmetric numerator is exactly the certified ι-odd datum chiralityOdd 4 3 = 1 (cast to ℚ): the offset is sourced by the merged 5₂ chirality-split datum, not a freshly-invented number.

    Hence the picture's structural premise holds: the ι-odd offset is a genuine eigencomponent of a real decomposition, it vanishes exactly on the ι-fixed locus, and on the certified knot data that locus is realised by the amphichiral 4₁ (offset 0) and missed by the chiral 5₂ (offset 1/2, numerator 1).

    Scope (honest) #

    This is a structural gate, scope-(a), not a bridge or derivation. The identification amphichiral ⟺ ι-fixed saddle vector is the modelling convention — backed by the certified 4₁ collapse and 5₂ split — not an independently re-proved knot-theory theorem. The file derives no physical number from the 13 categorical drivers, wires no c_k, and inverts no Q_β/CODATA value.
