              theorem QOU.QBeta.Seminormal.trace_sigma1_mul_sigma2 {q : ℝ} (hq : 0 < q) :
              (sigma1 q * sigma2 q).trace = -1

              trace (σ₁σ₂) = −1, identically in q. The q-dependence cancels exactly — this is the sharpest instance of the integrality obstruction, and it pins the eigenphase of σ₁σ₂ to the constant 2π/3, not to (z−1)h/2.
