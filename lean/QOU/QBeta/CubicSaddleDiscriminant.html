    QOU.QBeta.CubicSaddleDiscriminant — the three-saddle discriminant of the 5₂ cubic #

    Extends the 5₂ saddle-curve algebra (FiveTwoSaddleCurve.lean, the #3892 g3 5₂ port) to the full three-saddle discriminant. The 4₁ curve is a quadratic in y (two saddles) and DeformedSaddleCurve.saddle_separation gives its single squared saddle difference; the 5₂ curve is a cubic (three saddles), and this file supplies the missing cubic analogue: the product of the three squared saddle differences equals the cubic discriminant, which at the u = 0 saddle cubic evaluates to the printed trace-field discriminant −23.

    This ties FiveTwoSaddleCurve.u0_cubic_discriminant (the value −23 via the coefficient formula) to the geometry of the three saddles y₁,y₂,y₃: the exact pairwise singularities ι̃_ij = (V_i − V_j)/(2πi) of the deformed 5₂ state integral live on these differences, and the product of their squares is the arithmetic fingerprint −23 (the trace-field discriminant of 5₂).

    Generic commutative ring (§7c); the transcendental saddle values V_i (Li₂-values, Vol(5₂), CS(5₂) ≠ 0) are archimedean and live in the witness. GGM state-integral data: library/arxiv-2012.00062v3/2012.00062v3.pdf (eqs. 73, 230–233).

    theorem QOU.QBeta.CubicSaddleDiscriminant.disc_eq_root_diff_sq {R : Type u_1} [CommRing R] (y₁ y₂ y₃ : R) :
    ((y₁ - y₂) * (y₂ - y₃) * (y₃ - y₁)) ^ 2 = (y₁ + y₂ + y₃) ^ 2 * (y₁ * y₂ + y₂ * y₃ + y₃ * y₁) ^ 2 - 4 * (y₁ * y₂ + y₂ * y₃ + y₃ * y₁) ^ 3 - 4 * (y₁ + y₂ + y₃) ^ 3 * (y₁ * y₂ * y₃) + 18 * (y₁ + y₂ + y₃) * (y₁ * y₂ + y₂ * y₃ + y₃ * y₁) * (y₁ * y₂ * y₃) - 27 * (y₁ * y₂ * y₃) ^ 2

    Cubic discriminant as the product of squared root differences. For any three ring elements (the three saddles), the square of the product of their pairwise differences equals the discriminant written in the elementary symmetric functions e₁ = Σyᵢ, e₂ = Σyᵢyⱼ, e₃ = ∏yᵢ: ((y₁−y₂)(y₂−y₃)(y₃−y₁))² = e₁²e₂² − 4e₂³ − 4e₁³e₃ + 18e₁e₂e₃ − 27e₃².

    theorem QOU.QBeta.CubicSaddleDiscriminant.five_two_saddle_diff_sq {R : Type u_1} [CommRing R] (y₁ y₂ y₃ : R) (hs : y₁ + y₂ + y₃ = 2) (hq : y₁ * y₂ + y₂ * y₃ + y₃ * y₁ = 3) (hp : y₁ * y₂ * y₃ = 1) :
    ((y₁ - y₂) * (y₂ - y₃) * (y₃ - y₁)) ^ 2 = -23

    The three 5₂ saddle differences square to −23. For three saddles of the u = 0 cubic y³ − 2y² + 3y − 1 (Vieta: Σy = 2, Σyᵢyⱼ = 3, ∏y = 1), the product of their squared pairwise differences is the trace-field discriminant −23 — the geometric (root-difference) form of FiveTwoSaddleCurve.u0_cubic_discriminant, exhibiting −23 as ∏_{i<j}(V_i − V_j)-square of the exact saddle singularities.
