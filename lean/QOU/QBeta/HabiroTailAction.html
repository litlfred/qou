    4₁ Habiro-tail resurgent action = π²/5, via the golden-ratio dilogarithm #

    The 4₁ Kashaev/Habiro cyclotomic tail, at real q = eᵗ (the archimedean ρ_∞ face), is S(q) = Σ_n ∏_{j=1}^n (1 - q^j)(1 - q^{-j}); each factor is -4 sinh²(jt/2), so the series is alternating with a minimal term at 2 sinh(n*t/2) = 1, i.e. arcsinh(1/2) = ln φ (golden ratio). Its optimal-truncation resurgent action is A₀ = -4 ∫₀^{ln φ} ln(2 sinh s) ds = π²/5, which reduces to the golden-ratio Landen dilogarithm value Li₂(φ⁻²) = π²/15 − ln²φ.

    What is formalized here (sorry-free) #

    1. habiroFactor_bar_even — the tail factor (hence the whole tail) is q ↔ q⁻¹ invariant: the 4₁ Habiro tail is bar-even (no bar-odd datum; the #3923 odd-phase null). Pure algebra.

    2. li2_inv_phi_sq — the classically intricate value Li₂(φ⁻²) = π²/15 − ln²φ is DERIVED (not axiomatized) from the three standard dilogarithm functional equations — Euler reflection, duplication, Landen — packaged in [ClassicalDilog], by linear elimination at the golden points ψ + ψ² = 1, 1 + ψ = φ, ψ/(1+ψ) = ψ². Only the cited functional equations are axiomatized; the special value is proved from them.

    3. habiroAction_eq_pi_sq_div_five — A₀ = π²/5, derived from li2_inv_phi_sq plus the cited log(2 sinh) integral representation (the ln²φ cancels).

    Template note (owner: "use as an analogy for other open derivation gaps") #

    ClassicalDilog + li2_inv_phi_sq is a reusable pattern: axiomatize the standard, cited functional equations of a special function, then DERIVE a particular special value by linear elimination at a distinguished (algebraic) point. The same shape can attack other corpus gaps where an unknown constant must follow from known functional/structural relations at a special substrate point (e.g. an A_ω Stokes datum from GGM relations, a Δ_λ value from Frobenius relations) — provided the axiomatized fields are genuine cited relations and the target is derived, not fitted.

    References:

    • [hakimoglu-brown2025] Hakimoglu-Brown, Analytic dilogarithm identities, arXiv:2506.10206v2 (2025) — analytic (beta-integral) proofs of two-term Li₂ relations and ladder identities over ℚ(√5); supplies the analytic proofs of the reflection/duplication/Landen relations and the ℚ(√5) golden-ratio value.
    • Lewin (1991), Polylogarithms and Associated Functions, §1.20 (the two classical golden-ratio dilogarithm values, Landen 1780).

        • reflection (x : ℝ) : Li2 x + Li2 (1 - x) = Real.pi ^ 2 / 6 - Real.log x * Real.log (1 - x)

        • duplication (x : ℝ) : Li2 x + Li2 (-x) = Li2 (x ^ 2) / 2

          Duplication: Li₂(x) + Li₂(-x) = ½ Li₂(x²).

          Landen: Li₂(-x) = − Li₂(x/(1+x)) − ½ ln²(1+x).
