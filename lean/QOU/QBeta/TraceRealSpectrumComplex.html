    def QOU.QBeta.IsElliptic (tr det : ℝ) :
    Prop

    A real 2 × 2 matrix is elliptic — complex-conjugate spectrum — exactly when its characteristic discriminant tr² − 4·det is negative. Stated on the scalars so it needs no matrix machinery: the eigenvalues solve x² − (tr)x + det = 0.

    Equations
    • QOU.QBeta.IsElliptic tr det = (tr ^ 2 - 4 * det < 0)
