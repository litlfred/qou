      c k = c 0 * ρ ^ k

      C-finite degeneration. When the ratio is a constant ρ (the rational / q → 1 limit), the sequence is geometric c_k = c_0 · ρ^k — C-finite of order 1 with characteristic root ρ. The genuine q-Bessel case has the q^k-dependent ratio qbesselRatio above and is therefore NOT of this form: it is q-holonomic, which is why the canonical c_k admit no low-order constant-coefficient recurrence (#1839).

      theorem QOU.QBeta.qbessel_second_order_factor {K : Type u_1} [Field K] (q a : K) (k : ℕ) :
      a * q ^ (2 * k + 1) - q ^ (k + 1) - a * q ^ k + 1 = (1 - q ^ (k + 1)) * (1 - a * q ^ k)

      Factorisation behind the second-order q-difference equation ☆. The middle coefficient of the q-Bessel q-difference equation factors into the ★ denominator: with a = q^{ν+1} (so q^ν · q = a), a·q^{2k+1} − q^{k+1} − a·q^k + 1 = (1 − q^{k+1})(1 − a·q^k). This is the purely algebraic reason the second-order operator reduces to the first-order (★).

      (a * q ^ (2 * k + 1) - q ^ (k + 1) - a * q ^ k + 1) * c (k + 1) + q ^ k * c k = 0

      Second-order q-difference equation ☆, coefficient form. The Hahn–Exton q-Bessel generating function g(w) = Σ c_k w^k satisfies q^ν·g(q²w) − (1+q^ν−w)·g(qw) + g(w) = 0 (the function-level q-Bessel equation; verified to 60 dps in computations/qbeta/qbessel_second_order_qdifference_equation.py). Its w^{k+1} coefficient is the relation below, which follows from the first-order ★ via qbessel_second_order_factor. The "second order" is the q-difference order (three q-shifts w, qw, q²w); on the coefficients it collapses to the consecutive-c_k relation, so this and ★ are equivalent.

          def QOU.QBeta.lever1HabiroRatio {K : Type u_1} [Field K] (q a : K) (k : ℕ) :
          K

          Lever-1 Fibre Kernel — Hahn-Exton contiguity ratio (shared Habiro-ring class). The correction to the binding period S(m) relative to the order-2 q-involution skeleton is the cyclotomic Habiro sequence D(m). This alias names the Hahn-Exton q-Bessel ratio qbesselRatio for use in the conditional product form below.

          SCOPE (per docs/audits/2026-06-28-lever1-binding-correction-not-single-qhypergeometric.md): D(m) shares the Hahn-Exton / Habiro-ring class with the Q_β c_k ladder — both are cyclotomic-completion objects with ζ₄ residues — but this is a shared-CLASS analogy, NOT a literal contiguity identity. D(m) does not satisfy the single-parameter Hahn-Exton recurrence: the literal contiguity template was tested with held-out-m falsification and FAILS (no single a fit on m=4,5 predicts m=6,7,8), and D(m) factors as a product of pairwise-coprime irreducible bar-symmetric cores of growing degree (Stages 7-9), not a single q-hypergeometric element. The bare Hahn-Exton ratio is likewise structurally excluded for the parallel c_k (2026-06-20 audit, r₂ = +2 in the forbidden gap). So lever1HabiroRatio is the q-Bessel ratio re-exported as the class representative, not a claim that D(m) is governed by it.
