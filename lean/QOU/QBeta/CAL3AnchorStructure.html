      σ (κ * S) = -(κ * S)

      The odd-channel factorization sits in the bar-odd part. If the c_3 odd channel is κ·S with κ bar-odd (σ κ = −κ, the carrier) and S bar-fixed (σ S = S, an archimedean constant), then it is bar-odd. The parity is inherited from the carrier κ, NOT from the archimedean scalar S — so anchoring S cannot change the channel's parity (consistent with the standing reading KC6/KC10: parity gates test the odd part).

      κa * Sa / (κb * Sb) = κa / κb * (Sa / Sb)

      The cross-anchor swing is isolated in S. For nonzero carriers, the ratio of the factorized odd channel at two anchors factors into the pure-q carrier ratio times the scalar ratio: (κa·Sa)/(κb·Sb) = (κa/κb)·(Sa/Sb). Since κ is a pure-q function (anchor-rigid to the ppb the anchors differ by), the entire c_3 cross-anchor swing lives in Sa/Sb.

      S is the single archimedean leak (not derived). The identity S = c_3^odd / κ_(2,1)(q_0) merely defines S; it carries no content until S is fixed independently. The open keystone is the identification of S with a CAL-3-class quantity g(P_avg^π) — deliberately not axiomatised (a class S = g P_avg^π is vacuously satisfiable; the real content is cross-anchor rigidity of a single categorical g, which is unproved). This lemma records only the trivial well-definedness of the defining ratio.
