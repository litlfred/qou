    The Jones–Markov weights are Webster–Williamson Molien series (n = 3, exact) #

    qou-sf1t / qou-3quc. The 2026-08-21 Molien lead (docs/audits/2026-08-21-webster-williamson-molien-lead-for-equivariant-index.md) observed that Webster–Williamson (The geometry of Markov traces, arxiv-0911.4494v2, bib key webster-williamson-2014-markov) realise the Jones–Ocneanu trace weights as Molien series M_ν(u,t) of the reflection representation, and named four verification items. The 2026-08-22 note refuted the guessed closed form y_λ = f^λ q^{−κ_λ}/[n]_q! — the wrong normaliser.

    This file supplies the correct identification for n = 3, exact-symbolic: under the forced specialisation

    u = q², t = q z / (q² − q z − 1)

    the S₃ Molien series M_ν(u,t) equal the Jones–Markov Hecke weights y_λ(z) up to a single common normaliser Z = (q² − q z − 1)² — a perfect square, the Poincaré/Molien shape the lead predicted, NOT [n]_q!:

    y_(3) = M_triv · Z, y_(2,1) = M_std · Z, y_(1³) = M_sign · Z.

    The specialisation is not fitted: eliminating t gives a Molien conic in the weight ratios, and requiring the three y_λ-ratios to lie on it is an overdetermined system (three z-coefficients, one unknown u) whose only solution is the monomial u = q² (and its bar u = q⁻²). This resolves the lead's items 4 (weight family = Jones–Markov y_λ), 2 (specialisation), and the weight-level half of item 1 (the geometric-trace weights are tr_M's) — for n = 3. General n (qou-3quc) stays open; this is one (strong) data point.

    What is proved here #

    Each theorem is the denominator-cleared form of y_λ = M_ν(q², tSpec) · Z, a pure polynomial identity over any commutative ring (ring). Writing N_ν := molNum_ν(q², tSpec) · (q²−qz−1)² (the Molien numerator with the weight variable specialised and the normaliser cleared — a polynomial), the identity y_λ = M_ν · Z is exactly

    y_λ^num · molDen_ν(q²) = y_λ^den · N_ν.

    The N_ν are the polynomials named nThree/nSign/nStd below; that each equals molNum_ν(q², q z/(q²−qz−1))·(q²−qz−1)² is a one-line rational computation (done in the companion probe, exact ℚ(q,z)), and the polynomial identities are what the corpus's checker can re-verify. The S₃ Molien series and the H₃(q) Jones–Markov weights are computed by unrelated routes, so the match is an identity, not a tautology.

    Base ring (§7c): a generic CommRing K — a polynomial identity, no ordering, no archimedean structure, no division.
