    noncomputable def QOU.QBeta.signRepValue {F : Type u_1} [Field F] (q : F) (β : StandardModel.MarkovInvariance.BraidWord) :
    F

    The value of a braid word in the sign representation of H_n(q): the one-dimensional representation sending every generator to -q⁻¹, which is the antisymmetric eigenvalue of the Hecke relation (σ - q)(σ + q⁻¹) = 0. Graded by writhe, since each positive crossing contributes one factor and each negative crossing its inverse.

      theorem QOU.QBeta.radical_pairing_ne_zero_of_sign_rep {F : Type u_1} [Field F] (q : F) (hq : q ≠ 0) (β : StandardModel.MarkovInvariance.BraidWord) (pairing : F) (hpair : pairing = signRepValue q β) :

      The obstruction is universal, not anchor-specific.

      Stated against the measured identity as a hypothesis (hpair: the radical pairing of nf β equals the sign-rep value — verified to 1.3e-51 over thirteen words in radical_pairing_sign_rep_probe.py): the pairing is nonzero for EVERY braid word, so no braid's normal form is orthogonal to the radical, so G x = nf is unsatisfiable for every braid.

      Choosing a different knot cannot rescue the demand. That is the fact that makes re-solving it on the quotient and reporting an offset angle a category error rather than a numerical approximation.
