    QOU.QBeta.FiveTwoStokesMatrixIdentities #

    Algebraic consistency of the concrete 5₂ Stokes pair (S⁺, S⁻).

    This file promotes to sorry-free theorems the numerical checks that were run in the 5₂ state-integral / Stokes-sandwich campaign (PR #4487's V1/V2, and the exact-arithmetic checks K1/K2/K3 in computations/probes/qbeta_g3_5two_stokes_sandwich.py). Every statement here is a finite, decidable algebraic identity about concrete integer / rational matrices — genuinely Lean-provable, no analysis.

    What is (and is not) proved #

    The 5₂ Stokes sandwiches S±(q) are 3×3 matrices of integer q-series (Stokes integrality; the K2 check of the sandwich probe). Write S±(q) = Σ_k S±[k] · qᵏ with each S±[k] ∈ Mat₃(ℤ). The coefficient matrices S±[0..3] below are the exact rational-series output of the derived sandwich compute (glyph-decoded (95a/b), assembled from the fully-pinned tower layer). We prove:

    • det S⁺[0] = 1, det S⁻[0] = 1 — the leading (q→0) Stokes matrices are unimodular (the printed (96/97) factorization constants 3, 4). Promotes the det S±(q) = 1 check (K3) at leading order.
    • det W₋₁[0] = -2 — the q→0 W₋₁ block has determinant of magnitude 2 (the W₋₁ "det ±2" phenomenon). Here W₋₁[0] is rational (denominators of 6, from the derived c-slot subtraction), so the honest statement is over ℚ and the value is -2, not the fitted-integer +2 an earlier framing assumed.
    • Inversion, leading order: (S⁺[0])ᵀ · S⁻[0] = I. Promotes PR #4487's numerical inversion V1 at q→0.
    • Inversion, truncated to order q³: the convolution coefficient matrices of (S⁺(q))ᵀ · S⁻(q) vanish for k = 1, 2, 3 (and equal I at k = 0). This is the algebraic promotion of V1 as a polynomial identity to order q³ — the untruncated (S⁺(q))ᵀ · S⁻(q) = I for all orders is beaned (qou-9dvz follow-up) since it needs closed-form series entries not available symbolically here.

    Scope (honesty, per AGENTS.md) #

    These are fibre-side, purely algebraic facts about the concrete Stokes matrices. They do not prove FW Theorem 1.4, do not show the analytic integrals I_{j,k} satisfy these relations, and — per the program roast (qou-l7vf) — do not bridge to the archimedean c₃. No ℝ/ℂ; no ordering, no Real.*; §7c base-ring rule satisfied (concrete ℤ[q^±]-algebra data over ℤ/ℚ).

    Source of the matrix data: computations/probes/qbeta_g3_5two_stokes_sandwich.py (unflipped W-convention, the K1/K2-passing orientation).

          Inversion identity — leading order #

          The exact-arithmetic sandwich compute finds (S⁺(q))ᵀ · S⁻(q) = I (verified to O(q⁸) in the probe cross-check). At q→0 this is the concrete integer-matrix identity below — PR #4487's numerical V1 at leading order.

          Inversion identity — truncated to order q³ #

          The full q-series coefficient matrices S±[k] (k = 0,1,2,3), as produced by the derived sandwich compute, are all integer (Stokes integrality). We prove the order-by-order inversion: the convolution Pₖ = Σ_{a+b=k} (S⁺[a])ᵀ · S⁻[b] is I at k = 0 and 0 at k = 1, 2, 3. Hence (S⁺(q))ᵀ · S⁻(q) = I + O(q⁴) as a polynomial matrix identity. The untruncated all-order statement is beaned (qou-9dvz follow-up).
