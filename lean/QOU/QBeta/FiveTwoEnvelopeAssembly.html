    The envelope, assembled: the three remaining joins #

    Bean qou-trns. The pieces existed separately; this file joins them. Each of the three sections is one of the tasks that bean listed as outstanding.

    1. The envelope theorem itself #

    hasDerivAt_of_partial_y_eq_zero: if ∂F/∂y = 0 at the point, then along any differentiable path the total derivative collapses to the x-part:

    d/dt F(x t, y t) = (∂F/∂x)·x′(t)

    with y′ never appearing. This is why the envelope argument needs only that the roots are differentiable, not what their derivatives are — and deformed_saddle_iff_curve already gives ∂V/∂y = 0 for free, because the saddle condition is the curve.

    Stated for a general F, since nothing about the dilogarithm enters.

    2. The branch pinning, reduced to hcont #

    exp_logSumA_sub_log_eq_one and exp_logSumB_add_log_eq_one discharge the exp(·) = 1 hypothesis of the pinning lemma from the Vieta products alone; branchDiff_eq_zero_of_continuousOn then pins the branch difference to 0 given continuity and the base point. So all the algebra is done and what remains is exactly hcont.

    3. The gluing #

    envelope_integrate: from S′ = 2φ on [0,b) off one point, plus continuity on [0,b], conclude S φ = S 0 + φ² on all of [0,b] — so S(π) − S(0) = π². That one exceptional point is φ₀, where the discriminant vanishes and no differentiable root labelling exists (exists_unit_circle_disc_zero).

    ⚠ What is still not proved #

    hcont — continuity of the branch-difference along the arc. Every other input to the envelope step is now a theorem; this is the single remaining analytic hypothesis, and it is stated as a hypothesis everywhere it is used rather than assumed silently.

    Route 26 G8 also measured that the section-2 identities hold on [0,π) and fail at the endpoint φ = π, forced by 1 − x·y and 1 − y/x coinciding at x = −1. Harmless — π is an endpoint and section 3 needs the derivative only on the half-open interval — but it is why the statements below are on Ico.

    The 5₂ sum rule is PROVED: QOU.QBeta.FiveTwoComplexPentagon.fiveTwo_saddle_sum_rule and fiveTwoSaddleSumRule_holds, sorry-free, standard axioms.
