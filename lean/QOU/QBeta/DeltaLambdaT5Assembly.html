    T5 partial assembly: S_K = A_ω · κ · (ln²q₀ − ϑ_K²) #

    Composes the proved binding formula with the channel identity to get the fully-expanded S-leak as a function of the two open inputs (A_ω, ϑ_K):

    S_K(q₀) = A_ω · cuspModulus · (ln²q₀ − ϑ_K(q₀)²)

    where cuspModulus = √3/4 is the proved geometric datum and ϑ_K(q₀) = Im 𝔲_K(q₀) is the open angular deformation (bean qou-qnnq).

    What is proved here (sorry-free) #

    • s_channel_expanded — for any SDeformedVolumeChannel whose deformed volume is V₄₁ (the figure-eight complex volume), the channel identity expands to S(q₀) = A_ω · κ · (Re²u − Im²u). This is pure composition: S_eq (channel) + imV₄₁ (cusp geometry).

    • s_channel_at_substrate — specializes to the substrate meridian: if Re(u(q₀)) = ln q₀, then S(q₀) = A_ω · κ · (ln²q₀ − ϑ_K²) where ϑ_K = Im(u(q₀)).

      hRe is an UNDISCHARGED HYPOTHESIS, not a proved fact. Earlier revisions of this docstring attributed it to a lemma meridian_holonomy_boost; no such declaration exists anywhere in the corpus, and the attribution propagated into a bean and a probe docstring before it was caught (PR #4513, bean qou-5nze). Nothing below establishes hRe; it is assumed wherever it appears.

      The one corpus object that could discharge it does not: the substrate-matched Murakami meridian (Knot.DeformedMeridianWinding) has real part N·ln q₀, so hRe would force the degenerate colour N = 1, and the resulting quantization Im u = −2πk is refuted by the measured anchor signs. Proved sorry-free in QOU.QBeta.ChannelMeridianIdentification; that file also proves the channel class constrains u not at all.

    What is honestly open (beans, not sorries) #

    • A_ω — the Stokes constant. Carried as a class field in SDeformedVolumeChannel. For 4₁, GGM gives A_ω = 3. Uniqueness: conditional on FaddeevPhiPhysical class axioms (merged).

    • ϑ_K(q₀) — the angular deformation. Carried as Im(u(q₀)). Source: G3 monodromy datum on the SL₂(ℂ) character variety of K. Bean qou-qnnq.

    • V = V₄₁ — the channel uses the figure-eight complex volume. Carried as the hypothesis hV.

    This is the derive-then-check target: everything except A_ω and ϑ_K is derived from geometry. The T4 crux probe verified that A_ω = 3 + this formula reproduces both anchor S values with real ϑ_K.
