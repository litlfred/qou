                theorem QOU.QBeta.E0_mul_counit_eq_QA {F : Type u_1} [Field F] [CharZero F] (me q : F) (hq : q ≠ 0) (hq1 : q - 1 ≠ 0) :
                E0 me q * -counitDelta q = QA me q

                The bridge. 𝔈₀ · (−ε(Δ_S)) = Q_A, identically in q and m_e.

                So the ladder's leading term is the trace functional at z = 1 with the scale K = 𝔈₀. This is prop:neutron-qvalue-symbolic stated as an identity: the two decay readings do not merely agree numerically at leading order, they are the same expression.

                theorem QOU.QBeta.traceDelta_cross_mul {F : Type u_1} [Field F] [CharZero F] (q z : F) (hq : q ≠ 0) :
                traceDelta q z * (2 * q - q ^ 2 + 1) = counitDelta q * (2 * q * z - q ^ 2 + 1)

                The correction factor is affine in z, in cross-multiplied form so no non-vanishing hypothesis on the counit is needed: tr_M^z(Δ_S)·(2q − q² + 1) = ε(Δ_S)·(2qz − q² + 1).

                theorem QOU.QBeta.traceNum_sub_denom {F : Type u_1} [Field F] (q z : F) :
                2 * q * z - q ^ 2 + 1 - (2 * q - q ^ 2 + 1) = (z - 1) * (2 * q)

                The affine correction departs from 1 linearly in z − 1 with slope 2q, stated on the numerators so no non-vanishing hypothesis is needed. Non-zero slope (q ≠ 0) is why the trace inversion for z is exact and unique rather than a fit — and, since the ladder's cosine has a different slope at z = 1, why the two routes report different z for one measured correction.
