    The bar-odd channel is the ι-odd antisymmetric off-diagonal contraction #

    Bar-grade selection rule for the 5₂ state-integral chirality split.

    The Andersen–Kashaev state integral for 5₂, frame-reduced, splits (A1, qbeta-5two-chirality-state-integral, derived two independent ways) into a bar-even index channel and a bar-odd chirality channel: $$\hat Z \;=\; R^{1/8}\,\underbrace{H^{+}_{1}H^{-}_{1}}_{\text{bar-even}} \;+\; i\Bigl(-\tfrac{R^{1/8}}{2}\Bigr)\, \underbrace{\bigl(\beta\,H^{+}_{2}H^{-}_{0} - \beta^{-1}H^{+}_{0}H^{-}_{2}\bigr)} _{\text{bar-odd}} .$$

    Writing p := H⁺₂H⁻₀ and q := H⁺₀H⁻₂, the bar-odd home is the β-graded antisymmetric off-diagonal contraction $$\omega(\beta,p,q) \;=\; \beta\,p - \beta^{-1}q .$$

    The amphichiral involution ι : (β,p,q) ↦ (β⁻¹, q, p) swaps the two off-diagonal products and inverts the modulus (β ↔ β⁻¹ is the bar action on the cusp modulus, τ ↦ -τ̄). This file proves the structural facts that localise the whole 5₂ bar-odd frontier:

    • omega_iota_odd — ω is ι-odd: ω(ι(β,p,q)) = -ω(β,p,q).
    • sEven_iota_invariant — the companion s(β,p,q) = β p + β⁻¹q is ι-even.
    • omega_of_iotaFixed / omega_zero_on_iotaFixed — on the ι-fixed locus (β²=1, p=q) the contraction vanishes. This locus is simultaneously (i) the amphichiral locus — reproducing the corpus fact V(4₁)_odd = 0 (4₁ amphichiral ⇒ u=0, CS=0 ⇒ ι-fixed) — and (ii) the search grade of the B-tranche walls B1.2 (the symmetric GK bilinear) and B1.3 (a single span vector), both ι-even / ι-fixed objects. Hence those searches were structurally on ω's zero locus: an ι-even object cannot carry the ι-odd bar-odd datum.
    • omega_ne_zero_imp_chiral — contrapositive: a nonzero bar-odd datum forces a chiral (non-ι-fixed) knot. This is the structural reason the frontier needed 5₂ (chiral) and not 4₁ (amphichiral).

    Scope. This is a selection rule, not a bridge. It says which algebraic object carries the bar-odd datum (the ι-odd antisymmetric off-diagonal), and therefore why the ι-even searches returned nothing. It does not derive the anchor-dependent c₃ swing: the numerical companion (qbeta_5two_barodd_antisym_offdiagonal_probe.py) shows the ι-odd home is anchor-smooth (≈ 4.6 ppm across the muon↔proton anchors) while c₃ swings ≈ 3.47 %, so ω alone cannot realise the swing — the residual is the anchor-dependent meridian-holonomy deformation q₀ ↦ u, not a smoother object choice. Comparison to observed only; no c_k is wired; nothing here inverts Q_β/CODATA.

    Generic field throughout (§7c base-ring): the substrate→archimedean wall is not crossed — no ordering, no Real.*, no numerical q₀.
