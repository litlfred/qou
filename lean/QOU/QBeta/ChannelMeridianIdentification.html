    1. The channel imposes nothing on u #

    source
    def QOU.QBeta.ChannelMeridianIdentification.channelFree (u : ℝ → ℂ) (V : ℂ → ℂ) (A : ℝ) :
    PhiBSArgumentIdentification.SDeformedVolumeChannel fun (q₀ : ℝ) => A * (V (u q₀)).im

    The channel is conservative over u (branch (a), proved). For an arbitrary deformation map u, an arbitrary deformed volume V and an arbitrary Stokes constant A, the S-channel class is inhabited — witnessed by the S that the class identity itself names. Hence SDeformedVolumeChannel places NO condition whatsoever on u, and in particular does not impose the Murakami substrate matching u = N·t − 2πi·k. Any quantization of Im u must therefore come from OUTSIDE the class.

    This is deliberately a def, never an instance. Registering it for typeclass resolution would let every [SDeformedVolumeChannel S] theorem discharge itself against a tautologically-defined S and silently trivialise the downstream chain. It also does NOT fabricate the open conjecture: the conjecture concerns the MEASURED S_K, whereas the S built here is defined to satisfy the identity.
