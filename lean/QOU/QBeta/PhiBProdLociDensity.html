    The periodicity loci are dense (G5 specialization: discharges the loci-density inputs) #

    Discharges the last carried inputs of PhiBProdUniquenessComplete.phiBProd_uniqueUpToScalar — the density of the two periodicity loci S₀, S₁ — for the concrete substrate phiBProd, giving a phiBProd uniqueness statement whose only remaining hypothesis is the honest regularity MeromorphicNFOn Φ₁.

    The loci are taken as the explicit membership sets, so hmem₀/hmem₁ are definitional. Their density is dense_compl_of_countable applied to the total bad set — a finite union of the prefactor lattice (Common.dense_*Pole_compl's pattern) and phiBProd's zeros (and one shift), each countable: phiBProd's zeros inject into {num = 0} ∪ {den = 0}, and each product's zeros are a countable union over k of the factor ℤ-lattices.

    Verified lean-direct against the merged phiBProd chain + Common.PeriodicDense. Sorry-free.
