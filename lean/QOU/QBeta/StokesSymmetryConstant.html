    QOU.QBeta.StokesSymmetryConstant — A_ω = 3 is a structure constant #

    The W3 probe (qbeta_3bp8_w3_stokes_symmetry_constant.py) certifies that the GGM Stokes matrix of 4₁ satisfies, as an exact integer q-series identity (all coefficients through q⁴⁰⁰, GGM 2012.00062 symmetry eq 65):

    S⁺₁₂(q) + S⁺₂₁(q) = 3 · S⁺₁₁(q),      det S⁺(q) = 1.

    Three candidate readings of "the integer 3" of the T4/T5 chain coincide at q → 0 (where S⁺(0) = [[1,3],[0,1]]) and separate at the substrate:

    R1 raw entry S⁺₁₂ → 1.5852 at q̃_sub R2 value-ratio S⁺₁₂ / S⁺₁₁ → 3.7067 at q̃_sub R3 symmetry constant (S⁺₁₂ + S⁺₂₁)/S⁺₁₁ → exactly 3, at every q

    This file proves the two structural facts that make R3 the distinguished reading, over an arbitrary field and an arbitrary index type (so nothing depends on the specific series):

    • symmetryConstant_const — if a family satisfies the symmetry with constant c, then its symmetry constant is c at every index. So q-independence of R3 is a consequence of the symmetry, not a numerical accident. (Empirically this shows up as truncation-invariance: R3 = 3 already at a pre-asymptotic q-order 400 where R1 and R2 are meaningless.)
    • readings_coincide_at_base — at a base point with S₁₁ = 1, S₂₁ = 0 all three readings equal S₁₂. This is why the q → 0 data of GGM eq (60) cannot distinguish them, and why the substrate evaluation was needed to separate them.
    • rawEntry_eq_symmetryConstant_iff / symmetryConstant_sub_valueRatio — separation criteria: R1 equals R3 exactly when S₁₂ + S₂₁ = S₁₂·S₁₁, and R3 − R2 = S₂₁/S₁₁, so R2 and R3 agree iff the off-diagonal vanishes. Away from the base point the three are genuinely different objects.

    Scope. Nothing here derives the number 3 from the categorical drivers — that remains open (qou-qnnq lineage). What is established is that if A_ω is the Stokes symmetry constant, then it is exact and q-independent, so the corpus's constant A_ω = 3 needs no substrate correction; and the raw-entry reading is separately excluded by QOU.QBeta.ThetaRealityBound.thetaSq_nonneg_iff (A ≥ 2.054).
