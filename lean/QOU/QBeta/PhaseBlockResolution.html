    Two exact facts about the Q_β ladder phase, block-resolved #

    Companion to computations/qbeta/qbeta_phase_block_resolution_probe.py (18/18) and computations/qbeta/qbeta_alpha_commutator_floor_probe.py (22/22), on bean qou-cds1 (re-derive the Q_β ladder under convention D).

    Background, in one paragraph. The ladder's eigenphase satisfies the exact identity ε(Δ) − tr_M^z(Δ) = h(z−1) = 2α, so α is half the gap between two functionals on the neutron−proton normal-form difference

    Δ = (h²/2)·γ − (h/2)·σ₀ − (h/2)·σ₁ in the Gröbner–Shirshov basis [γ, σ₀, σ₁, L₊, L₋, e⁻] of H₃(q).

    Resolving that gap per Wedderburn block gives

    2α(z) = Σ_λ [a_λ − y_λ(z)] · χ^λ(Δ) + net(d_C),

    in which only y_λ(z) depends on the Markov parameter. This file pins the two statements from that resolution that are ring identities rather than computations in a specific representation.

    Base ring (AGENTS.md §7c) #

    Generic [CommRing R]. Nothing archimedean appears: no ordering, no Real.*. The divisions the narrative writes are carried as invertible data — ⅟(2 : R) for the halves in Δ, and q : Rˣ for the bar involution q ↦ q⁻¹ — which is the CommRing analogue of the nonzero side conditions, and recovers the field statements via invertibleOfNonzero / Units.mk0.

        theorem QOU.QBeta.PhaseBlockResolution.chi_two_one_delta_eq_zero {R : Type u_1} [CommRing R] [Invertible 2] (h : R) (chi : Fin 6 → R) (hdim : chi 0 = 2) (hs0 : chi 1 = h) (hs1 : chi 2 = h) :
        pair chi (deltaCoords h) = 0

        The 2-dimensional block character annihilates Δ.

        The λ = (2,1) block of H₃(q) is two-dimensional and each generator σᵢ acts there with eigenvalues q and −q⁻¹, hence with trace h = q − q⁻¹. Those two facts alone force χ^{(2,1)}(Δ) = 0:

        (h²/2)·2 − (h/2)·h − (h/2)·h = h² − h²/2 − h²/2 = 0.

        Note what the statement does not need: the character's values on the three longer basis elements are left completely free, because Δ has zero coordinate there. So the vanishing is a property of Δ's support together with the block's dimension and generator trace — not of the block's full character table.

        Consequence for the ladder: the eigenphase α = (ε − tr_M)/2 is blind to the 2-dimensional block in every convention, since no Markov parameter enters this identity.

            theorem QOU.QBeta.PhaseBlockResolution.floor_bar_even {R : Type u_1} [CommRing R] (u v : R) (huv : u * v = 1) :
            floorNum v * floorDen u = floorNum u * floorDen v

            The commutator floor is bar-even.

            Stated division-free by cross-multiplication, and with the bar involution q ↦ q⁻¹ carried by the single hypothesis u · v = 1, so the statement needs nothing beyond CommRing:

            floorNum v · floorDen u = floorNum u · floorDen v.

            The identity is not a tautology of the shape — over a general commutative ring floorNum v · floorDen u − floorNum u · floorDen v = 3(u²−v²)((uv)²−1), which is nonzero in general and vanishes exactly on the bar-involution locus uv = 1.

            Why it matters on qou-cds1. The sibling prune MarkovWeightsConventionD shows both surviving convention-D Markov weights are bar-even, hence that no bar-odd part can be assembled from the weights. The floor is not built from weights — it is a non-trace functional evaluated on the very component every weight annihilates — so that prune does not reach it. This says the trace-invisible sector supplies no bar-odd material either, and the prune therefore extends rather than breaking.
