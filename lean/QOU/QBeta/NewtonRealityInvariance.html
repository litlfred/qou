    QOU.QBeta.NewtonRealityInvariance — real seeds cannot reach the cusp #

    The structural invariant behind the pole→cusp negative of computations/qbeta/qbeta_c3_barodd_pole_seed_newton_probe.py (finding A: a literal real Φ_q-pole seed flows to the golden branch, never the cusp) and computations/qbeta/qbeta_c3_pole_sign_complexification_probe.py (the pole's own sign, phase π, does not help): a Newton iteration whose numerator and denominator commute with complex conjugation, started at a real seed of either sign, has all-real iterates; a convergent all-real sequence has a real limit; so it can never converge to the cusp shape e^{iπ/3} (imaginary part √3/2 ≠ 0).

    This upgrades the computed per-k negatives to a scope-(b) exclusion of the whole real-seed class, per AGENTS.md §Before declaring "refuted" — the proved invariant is conjugation equivariance plus closedness of the real axis.

    Honesty (hypothesis-carrying, conditional-proved ≠ premise-established) #

    ConjEquivariant is an explicit hypothesis. Real-coefficient rational maps — in particular the 4₁ gluing polynomial z(z−1)·w(w−1) − 1 at real meridian and its derivative — satisfy it, and the concrete polynomial instance is provided (conjEquivariant_realPolynomial). Nothing here claims which map the substrate assigns; the open link u(q₀) is untouched.

        Real seeds cannot reach a non-real target. If a Newton iteration with conjugation-equivariant data starts at a real seed (either sign) and converges, its limit is real — in particular it is not any point with non-zero imaginary part, such as the 4₁ cusp shape e^{iπ/3} (Im = √3/2).
