    The odd-channel Habiro datum lives in the cyclotomic coefficient's bar-asymmetry #

    A knot's Kashaev/Habiro cyclotomic tail has the general form S_K(q) = Σ_n C_{K,n}(q) · ∏_{j=1}^n (1 - q^j)(1 - q^{-j}), with C_{K,n} the cyclotomic coefficient (C_n = 1 for 4_1; nontrivial and q-dependent for other knots, e.g. the twist knots). The tail factor ∏ (1 - q^j)(1 - q^{-j}) is bar-even (habiroFactor_bar_even, QOU.QBeta.HabiroTailAction). Therefore the entire bar-odd (q ↔ q⁻¹ antisymmetric) content of the tail is carried by the bar-asymmetry of the coefficient C_{K,n} alone:

    C_n(q)·f_n(q) − C_n(q⁻¹)·f_n(q⁻¹) = (C_n(q) − C_n(q⁻¹))·f_n(q).

    Consequences, all sorry-free:

    • 4_1 has C_n = 1, bar-symmetric ⇒ zero bar-odd datum (bar-even tail; the π²/5 action is entirely bar-even) — consistent with 4_1 amphichirality and the #3923 odd-phase null.
    • For a chiral knot with a bar-asymmetric C_{K,n}(q) ≠ C_{K,n}(q⁻¹), the bar-odd Habiro datum is nonzero and equals (C_n(q) − C_n(q⁻¹)) times the (bar-even) factor. So the Q_β odd-channel resurgence (b3ix) is located precisely in the cyclotomic-coefficient asymmetry — for 5_2 this is the Masbaum twist-knot coefficient, the concrete next input.

    This is the general structural locator; it does not require the explicit 5_2 coefficient (which must come from Masbaum's twist-knot formula, not be invented).

    C q · f_j(q) − C q⁻¹ · f_j(q⁻¹) = (C q − C q⁻¹) · f_j(q).

    Concrete chiral instance: the 5_2 cyclotomic coefficients (Masbaum) #

    5_2 = K_2 is the twist knot with two full twists. Its cyclotomic coefficients are given AUTHORITATIVELY by Masbaum's twist-knot formula (Theorem 4.5 + 5.1 + Remark 5.2 of masbaum2003, "Skein-theoretical derivation of some formulas of Habiro", Algebr. Geom. Topol. 3 (2003) 537–556, DOI 10.2140/agt.2003.3.537): for p = 2, c'_{n,2} = (-1)^n a^{(5n²+7n)/2} Σ_{k=0}^n a^{k²-2k-3nk} [n,k]_a, f̃_{K2,n} = (-1)^n q^{-n(n+1)/2} a^{n(n+3)/2} c'_{n,2} (q = a²).

    Basis bridge (derived, verified on 4_1). Masbaum's basis is the color-dependent (q^{1-N})_n(q^{1+N})_n, which at the Kashaev point q^N = 1 becomes (q;q)_n²; the framework's tail basis is the bar-even f_n(q) = (q;q)_n (q^{-1};q^{-1})_n. Since (q^{-1};q^{-1})_n / (q;q)_n = (-1)^n q^{-n(n+1)/2}, the two bases give the same tail with C_{K,n}(q) = (-1)^n q^{n(n+1)/2} · f̃_{K,n}(q). On 4_1 = K_{-1}, f̃_{4_1,n} = (-1)^n q^{-n(n+1)/2} ⇒ C_n = 1 (Kashaev's figure-eight), so the bridge is exact. This gives the framework coefficients in q: C_{5_2,1}(q) = -(q⁴+q²), C_{5_2,2}(q) = q¹¹+q⁸+q⁷+q⁵, C_{5_2,3}(q) = -(q²¹+q¹⁷+q¹⁶+q¹⁵+q¹³+q¹²+q¹¹+q⁹).

    Below, t is the ordinary Jones variable (t = q^{-1}, the mirror of the substrate q), so C_{5_2,n} is written with q = t^{-1}; e.g. C_{5_2,1}(t) = -(t^{-2} + t^{-4}), matching -(q⁴+q²) at t = q^{-1}. The leading C_{5_2,1} was first extracted self-calibrating as (V_{5_2}-1)/ (V_{4_1}-1); probe qbeta_5two_masbaum_cyclotomic CROSS-CHECKS that the heuristic equals Masbaum's authoritative value EXACTLY (and that Masbaum reproduces the standard Jones polynomial V_{5_2} at N = 2). Unlike 4_1's C_n = 1, every C_{5_2,n} (n ≥ 1) is bar-asymmetric — the genuine 5_2 bar-odd data.
