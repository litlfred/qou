    Q_β crossing-weight affine narrowing — the ladder-factor bar-parity split #

    Sharpens QOU.Interactions.QBetaCounitTrace (registry KC18): KC18 decomposed the numerator of the evaluation map E(w) = h²/2 − h·w into a bar-even identity term h²/2 and a bar-odd crossing/loop term −h·w. Here we analyse the object where the c_k actually live — the ladder factor L(w) = E(w)/E(w₀) — and show its bar-odd (decay) part is linear in the crossing-weight departure (w − w₀) times a fixed bar-odd kernel 2h/(4w₀² − h²).

    Bar involution: q ↦ q⁻¹ acts as h ↦ −h (h = q − q⁻¹ is bar-ODD); the crossing weights w, w₀ are bar-EVEN (z is bar-even, QOU.QBeta.z_bar_invariant, KC13). So the bar image of L(w) is L̄(w) = E₋(w)/E₋(w₀) with E₋(w) = h²/2 + h·w, and the even/odd parts are (L ± L̄)/2.

    All statements hold over an arbitrary field of characteristic ≠ 2 (the substrate→archimedean wall is not crossed: no ordering, no Real.sqrt). See AGENTS.md §7c. Companion probe: computations/qbeta/qbeta_crossing_weight_affine_probe.py (12/12, sympy-exact).

    No c_k value is produced; nothing is wired (registry §8 flip-flop guard).

        The crossing-weight departure w − 1 (counit reference) #

        At the counit reference w₀ = 1, the ladder factor is L(w) = (h − 2w)/(h − 2), and the c_k ladder is L = 1 + Σ_{k≥2} c_k u^k. Inverting recovers the crossing weight from the ladder tail: w = 1 − (h−2)/2·(L − 1). So the departure w − 1 is the ladder tail scaled by the fixed factor −(h−2)/2; with the derived c_2 = −1 its leading term is +(h−2)/2·u².
