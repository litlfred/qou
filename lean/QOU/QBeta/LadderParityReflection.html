    Why the S parity is convention-immune: it never reads the Markov parameter #

    ImQDecayLinearResponse.lean proves the ε-parity for one concrete Laurent expression (fig8TraceC), by the route

    real coefficients ⇒ T(conj z) = conj (T z) ⇒ Im T odd in ε.

    The registry's KC21 row claims that parity statement is convention-immune — unaffected by the 2026-08-09 Markov-trace flip C → D (z_C = √q/(1+q) ↦ z_D = q³/(q²+1)). That claim was asserted, not derived: c₃ is defined by an extractor whose N=1 rung consumes z (α = (z−1)h/2), and under D the extractor is a numerically and structurally different function (c₃ ≈ −974 rather than ≈ −2.07). Parity of f_C does not transfer to f_D just because both are written c₃.

    This file supplies the missing reduction. The observation is that the middle step above needs nothing about the formula for T: it needs only that conjugation commutes with it. Isolate that as Reflective, prove the parity from it alone, and prove the class is closed under every operation the ladder performs. The Markov parameter then enters as one reflective ingredient among many, and which z it is never appears in the argument — which is exactly what "convention-immune" should mean.

    The concrete instances confirming z_D (rational, proved here) and z_C (needs the principal √ branch, so measured rather than proved — probe assertion A8) sit in computations/qbeta/qbeta_kc21_parity_convention_immunity_probe.py.

    Two scope pins, both load-bearing:

    • This is complex conjugation, not the bar involution. σ : q ↦ q⁻¹ is a different involution. Nothing here says S is bar-odd, and nothing here bears on bar(z_D) = q⁻⁴·z_D or on the z_C full-bar-condition failure.
    • Reflective does not single out the bar-odd channel. Every reflective function has Im odd in ε — bar-even ones included (Q_A is bar-even and has it; probe assertion A11). So the parity is a consequence of real-analyticity, and is not evidence that the channel carrying it is bar-odd. KC21 states the derivation as "bar-odd ⇒ odd in ε"; the honest statement is "reflective ⇒ Im odd in ε".

    Base ring is ℂ by necessity (§7c archimedean side): conjugation and the Im projection are the content. No sorry.

    def QOU.QBeta.LadderParityReflection.Reflective (f : ℂ → ℂ) :
    Prop

    A map is reflective when complex conjugation commutes with it: f(conj z) = conj (f z). Every function built from real constants by the field operations and composition is reflective (see the closure lemmas below); fig8TraceC_conj is the concrete instance for the figure-eight trace.

        The convention-immunity statement. For every reflective Markov parameter, the rung phase is reflective.

        This quantifies over conventions: it is not a fact about z_C, nor about z_D, but about the whole class. A future convention change cannot break it unless the new z is non-reflective — i.e. unless it fails to be real-valued on the real q-axis.
