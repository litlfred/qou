    Bar-parity exclusion of a bar-odd derivation of c₃ = J_Hopf(1) = −2 #

    Bean qou-cds1 (f-map attack). Three feasibility probes tried to DERIVE the c₃ identification c₃ = J_Hopf(1) = −2 from the convention-D two-row Markov trace: route A (the bar-odd crossing term), route B (the Z3 carrier G·D, bar-even functional × bar-odd carrier), and the bar-even redirect. Routes A and B both fail for the SAME reason, and it is a proved parity invariant, not a numeric near-miss.

    c₃ = J_Hopf(1) = −2 is the value of the Hopf-link Jones polynomial −(q²+q⁻²) at q = 1 — a bar-EVEN quantity (q ↦ q⁻¹ fixes q²+q⁻²), value −2 ≠ 0. But every bar-odd construction vanishes at the bar fixed point q = 1. So no bar-odd object — crossing term or Z3 carrier — can produce the bar-even value −2 there. This file formalises that invariant.

    Symbolic cross-check (all 19 assertions, exact ℚ(q)): computations/qbeta/fmap_c3_parity_genericity_exclusion.py. The bar-EVEN channel fails separately, by genericity (scope-(a)) — −2 at q=1 is hit by many bar-even combinations and none equals the full (non-bar-even) Hopf polynomial — recorded in the probe and the audit, not here (it is not a proved invariant). Sorry-free.

      theorem QOU.QBeta.barOdd_ne_hopf_value_at_one (f : ℝ → ℝ) :
      barOdd f 1 ≠ -2

      Parity exclusion of the bar-odd routes to c₃. The bar-odd content of any construction is 0 at q = 1, but the c₃ target — the Hopf-link Jones value at q = 1 — is −2 ≠ 0. So c₃ = J_Hopf(1) = −2 cannot be the bar-odd content of any object: it is a bar-EVEN q=1 shadow. This is the single invariant behind the refutation of both route A (crossing term) and route B (Z3 carrier); both are bar-odd, hence vanish here.
