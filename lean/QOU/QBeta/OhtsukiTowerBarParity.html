    Bar-parity of the Ohtsuki κ-tower at the imaginary substrate #

    The finite-b complex volume of the figure-eight complement expands as an Ohtsuki tower 𝔙_b = 𝔙_0 + Σ_{n≥1} κ_n (2πb²)ⁿ with real coefficients κ_n ∈ ℚ(√3)·π^{⌈n/2⌉} (κ₁ = 11√3π/108, κ₂ = 2π²/27, … ; #3628, qbeta_c3_phib_kappa_tower_scale_probe.py, qbeta_c3_borel_pade_resummation_probe.py).

    At the substrate the fluctuation variable is imaginary, 2πb² = i·t with t = ln q₀ ∈ ℝ (b = √(ln q₀ / 2π)·e^{iπ/4}, so 2πb² = i·ln q₀). This file proves the bar-parity selection rule that organizes the tower into the two faces of 𝔙 = Vol + i·CS:

    • Even rungs feed Re 𝔙 = Vol (bar-even / mass). (i·t)^{2m}·κ is real: Im = 0 (even_rung_real). Since (i·t)² = −t² is real, every even power is real, so the even sub-tower is the volume/mass channel.
    • Odd rungs feed Im 𝔙 = CS (bar-odd / decay = the c₃ S). (i·t)^{2m+1}·κ is purely imaginary: Re = 0 (odd_rung_imaginary). The single factor of i left after pairing makes every odd power imaginary, so the odd sub-tower is the Chern–Simons / bar-odd S channel.

    This is the Lean root of the "even Ohtsuki rungs → Vol, odd rungs → the bar-odd S" split used across the merged c₃-wall thread (#3600/#3636/#3654/#3675/#3682): the sign-flip lives entirely in the odd (imaginary) sub-tower, and the Borel–Padé resummation (qbeta_c3_borel_pade_resummation_probe.py) resums that odd sub-tower to the exact smooth Im 𝔙 with no Stokes ambiguity on the imaginary ray.

    Base ring is ℂ by necessity (§7c archimedean side): the Re/Im projections against the imaginary substrate direction are the content. No sorry; no c_k wired.

    theorem QOU.QBeta.OhtsukiTowerBarParity.imaginary_sub_sq (t : ℝ) :
    (Complex.I * ↑t) ^ 2 = ↑(-t ^ 2)

    The paired imaginary factor is real: (i·t)² = −t², cast of a real. The single squaring that turns the imaginary substrate direction into a real scalar — the seed of the even/odd parity split.

    theorem QOU.QBeta.OhtsukiTowerBarParity.even_rung_real (t κ : ℝ) (m : ℕ) :
    ((Complex.I * ↑t) ^ (2 * m) * ↑κ).im = 0

    Even rung ⇒ real ⇒ feeds Re 𝔙 = Vol (bar-even / mass channel). For a real coefficient κ, the 2m-th tower rung at the imaginary substrate x = i·t has vanishing imaginary part: Im((i·t)^{2m}·κ) = 0. Pairing the imaginary factors via (i·t)² = −t² (real) leaves (−t²)^m·κ, a real number. So every even Ohtsuki rung contributes to the volume/mass face of 𝔙, never to the bar-odd S.

    theorem QOU.QBeta.OhtsukiTowerBarParity.odd_rung_imaginary (t κ : ℝ) (m : ℕ) :
    ((Complex.I * ↑t) ^ (2 * m + 1) * ↑κ).re = 0

    Odd rung ⇒ imaginary ⇒ feeds Im 𝔙 = CS (bar-odd / decay = the c₃ S). For a real coefficient κ, the 2m+1-th tower rung at the imaginary substrate x = i·t has vanishing real part: Re((i·t)^{2m+1}·κ) = 0. Pairing the imaginary factors leaves one factor of i, so the rung is ((−t²)^m·t·κ)·i, purely imaginary. So every odd Ohtsuki rung contributes to the Chern–Simons / bar-odd S face of 𝔙, never to the volume/mass. This is where the c₃ bar-odd sign-flip lives; the Borel resummation of exactly this odd sub-tower gives the exact smooth Im 𝔙.
