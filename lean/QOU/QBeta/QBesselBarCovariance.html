      theorem QOU.QBeta.heckeCoeffRatio_bar_covariance {K : Type u_1} [Field K] (k : ℕ) (q a : K) (hq : q ≠ 0) (ha : a ≠ 0) (h1 : 1 - q ^ (k + 1) ≠ 0) (h2 : 1 - a * q ^ k ≠ 0) :
      heckeCoeffRatio k q⁻¹ a⁻¹ = a * q * heckeCoeffRatio k q a

      Bar-covariance of the q-Bessel coefficient ratio.

      Under the bar involution q ↦ q⁻¹, a ↦ a⁻¹, the ratio ρ_k transforms by the k-independent twist a q (= q^{ν+2}): ρ_k(q⁻¹, a⁻¹) = (a q) · ρ_k(q, a).

      The twist q^{ν+2} ≠ 1 is the structural obstruction to bar-evenness of the c_k ladder: bar-evenness would require twist = 1. This corroborates the sibling bar-parity prune (criterion 2(b) retired — bar-oddness is irreducible).
