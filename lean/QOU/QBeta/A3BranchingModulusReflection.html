          The reduction never exceeds 1 for a real branching (sorry-free) — the amplitude form of A3OffBlockDerivation.magnitudeFactor_le_one: factor_sa = 1 − x² ≤ 1, so the self-adjoint branching is a reduction. The lift 2 − factor_sa ≥ 1 is therefore the reflected value, and requires the transverse leg 2x — which the branching's own small x cannot make large enough (scope-(a), see the audit).
