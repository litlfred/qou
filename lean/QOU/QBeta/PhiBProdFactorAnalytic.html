    The phiBProd factors are entire; finite partial products are entire (input-1 infra) #

    PhiBProdMeromorphic.phiBProd_meromorphicOn_of_analytic (input 1, merged #4223) discharges MeromorphicOn (phiBProd b) given that the two infinite products are analytic on ℂ. This file builds the infrastructure floor of that hypothesis: each individual factor is entire (a composite 1 + exp(affine in z)), and every finite partial product is entire (Finset.analyticOnNhd_prod).

    The remaining gap to the infinite product's analyticity is the analytic-limit step — a locally-uniformly-convergent product of entire functions is entire (Mathlib.Analysis.Complex.LocallyUniformLimit) — plus the partial-products → tprod local-uniform convergence estimate (available from PhiBProd's geometric Multipliable bounds for Im b² > 0). This file lands the factor/partial-product half unconditionally; the convergence half is the named next infra brick.
