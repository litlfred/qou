    QOU.QBeta.DTChiralityBarInvolutionLink — the chirality split IS q ↦ q⁻¹ (D4, conditional) #

    The qou-rtec attack ledger's D4 lever, formalized conditionally: the {S⁺, S⁻} DT chirality split and the corpus bar involution q ↦ q⁻¹ are the same ℤ/2 action, transported along knot mirroring. The corpus already carries the two halves separately:

    • the pair side — ChiralityGradedCount: the mirror swap on an ordered split (a, b), its faithful bar-grading (a+b, a−b), and the proved no-symmetric-scalar obstruction;
    • the q side — the Jones mirror rule J_{m(K)}(q) = J_K(q⁻¹) (the convention noted in HabiroCyclotomicOddLocus, t = q⁻¹ = mirror) and the amphichiral σ-fixedness used by FigureEightSelfDuality.

    This file ties them: a class DTBarInvolutionLink whose fields are the two transformation hypotheses (the DT pair transforms under mirroring by the swap; the carrying invariant transforms by bar), and theorems deriving the consequences — the bar-even part of the DT datum is mirror-invariant, the bar-odd part is a chirality obstruction, amphichirality kills it and forces the invariant σ-fixed, and the two involutions compose coherently.

    Honesty (§3b-cond; anti-laundering) #

    The class axiomatises hypotheses, not conclusions (§3b-tax non-degeneracy): neither transformation law is proved here — dt_mirror_swap is the GGM-style chirality-split reading and jones_mirror_bar is the Jones mirror rule, both literature facts about objects the corpus does not construct internally. Every theorem is conditional on the instance; no instance for a real knot is declared (a structural consistency instance on Split itself shows the class is satisfiable — it carries no knot data). Nothing here derives A_ω, the DT integers, or the split; the 5₂/4₁ numbers enter only through ChiralityGradedCount's literature defs, quoted in conditional corollaries.

    class QOU.QBeta.DTChiralityBarInvolutionLink.DTBarInvolutionLink (K : Type u) (R : Type v) [GroupWithZero R] :
    Type (max u v)

    The D4 link, as an explicit conditional hypothesis class. K is a type of knot data with a mirror involution, dt the chirality-split DT reading, and jones the carrying q-invariant. The two transformation fields assert that mirroring acts on the DT pair by the swap and on the invariant by the bar involution q ↦ q⁻¹ — making the chirality split and bar parity the same ℤ/2 action. Both are hypotheses (literature transformation laws), not corpus theorems; see the module docstring.

    • mirrorK : K → K

      Knot mirroring.

    • mirrorK_involutive (k : K) : mirrorK R (mirrorK R k) = k

      Mirroring is an involution.

    • dt : K → ChiralityGradedCount.Split

      The chirality-split DT reading (S⁺, S⁻).

    • jones : K → R → R

      The carrying q-invariant (Jones-type).

    • dt_mirror_swap (k : K) : dt R (mirrorK R k) = ChiralityGradedCount.mirror (dt R k)

      Hypothesis 1: mirroring swaps the split.

    • jones_mirror_bar (k : K) (q : R) : q ≠ 0 → jones (mirrorK R k) q = jones k q⁻¹

      Hypothesis 2 (the Jones mirror rule): mirroring is the bar involution on the invariant.
