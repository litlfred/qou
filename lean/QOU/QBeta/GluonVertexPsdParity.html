      The alternating (1,1) block sends σ₀ ↦ -q⁻¹, so it sends σ₀^k ↦ (-q⁻¹)^k. Both projections are ring homomorphisms H₂(q) → R, which is why a power of the generator maps to the power of its eigenvalue.
