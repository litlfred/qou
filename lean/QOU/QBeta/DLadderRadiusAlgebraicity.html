    QOU.QBeta.DLadderRadiusAlgebraicity — the algebraicity backbone of the D-ladder radius #

    The D-ladder D(w) = Σ_k d_k w^k is the Taylor series at w = 0 of D(w) = [log R(q) − log R(1/q)] / (2(q − 1/q)) in the bar-even coordinate w = ũ(q), and it is convergent with radius ρ = ũ(q*), where q* is the real root > 1 of the irreducible degree-12 factor of the Δ_net numerator (a zero of Q_b, hence a logarithmic branch point of log R).

    This file formalises the algebraic skeleton of that statement — the part that is exact polynomial algebra and real analysis, with no transcendental input:

    1. The clean coordinate. With t = q + q⁻¹ the two identities (q−1)²/q = t − 2 and √(q⁴+q²+1)/(q²+1) = √(t²−1)/t are exact (sq_sub_one_div_eq_tOf_sub_two, sqrt_quartic_div_eq). They are the reason ũ is a function of t alone — i.e. bar-even — and hence the structural reason the split log R = E(ũ) + h·D(ũ) into bar-even and bar-odd parts exists at all (h = q − q⁻¹ is bar-odd).

    2. The fold. In the clean coordinate ũ(t) = (t−2)·√(t²−1)/t has dũ/dt = (t³−2)/(t²√(t²−1)) (hasDerivAt_utilde), so the only critical point on t > 1 is t³ = 2 (deriv_utilde_eq_zero_iff), with critical value −(t²−1)^{3/2} = −(2^{2/3}−1)^{3/2} (utilde_at_fold, utilde_at_cbrt_two). That is the |ũ| = 0.4502 entry of the singularity inventory — the fold the earlier search did find, and which is not the radius.

    3. Degree-12 algebraicity. ρ² is a root of the degree-12 Q(y) = 54289y¹² − 3911138y¹¹ + … because t* is a root of P_t(t) = t¹² − 24t¹⁰ + … + 3728. The transfer is an exact identity in ℤ[t] (QyHom_utilde_eq_Pt_mul_Ct): Z¹²·Q(Y/Z) = P_t(t)·C(t) at Y = (t−2)²(t²−1), Z = t², with an explicit degree-36 cofactor C. Consequently every root of P_t is carried to a root of Q by t ↦ ũ(t)² (Qy_utilde_sq_eq_zero), and P_t has a real root in (2.16108, 2.16109) by the intermediate value theorem (exists_root_Pt), at which 0.1427 < ũ < 0.1429 (utilde_bracket) — the audit's ρ = 0.142799996644… to four digits, and strictly below every other entry of the |ũ| singularity inventory. Together: exists_real_root_Qy_of_utilde_sq. The two degree-12 polynomials of the audit are themselves linked by q¹²·P_t(q + q⁻¹) = P_q(q)·P_q(−q) (Pt_tOf_mul_pow_eq).

    What is NOT claimed here. Nothing in this file derives c₃, computes any d_k, or asserts that the radius of convergence of Σ d_k w^k equals the root located below — that identification is the analytic content of the audit (a residue computation on log R), not algebra, and is not formalised. Nor is irreducibility of P_t, P_q or Q over ℚ claimed (it is true — checked in sympy — but is not proved here). What is proved is the exact algebraic backbone: the coordinate identities, the fold, and the polynomial transfer P_t(t) = 0 ⇒ Q(ũ(t)²) = 0 together with the existence of the real root.

    Base ring (§7c): the polynomial content is stated over a generic [CommRing R] / [Field K]; only the √, ordering and IVT statements specialise to ℝ.
