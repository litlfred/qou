    QOU.Calculations — Skein-Deformed Residue Proofs and Substrate Derivation #

    This module formalizes the complete chain from the Knot Registry's Alexander-Briggs-indexed volumes through to the substrate parameter q.

    Structure #

    1. Bridge lemmas: Connect the KnotRegistry volume to MassDerivation constants
    2. Skein-deformed residue: Formalize m_μ/m_e ≈ Vol(4₁) / ℏ_q²
    3. Substrate derivation: Prove q ≈ 1.1097 with error bounds
    4. CODATA consistency: Cross-check against NIST experimental values

    Data Sources #

    • Vol(4₁): SnapPy census, Bloch-Wigner dilogarithm, 2·Cl₂(π/3) = 6·Λ(π/3) (OEIS A091518) Ref: [turaev2010] https://doi.org/10.1515/9783110221831
    • m_μ/m_e: NIST CODATA 2022 recommended value 206.7682827(46) Ref: [codata2022] https://physics.nist.gov/cgi-bin/cuu/Value?mmusme
    • q derivation: Manuscript §4, Conjecture 4.25

    Section 1: Registry-to-MassDerivation Bridge #

    These lemmas verify that the volume constant used in mass calculations is pulled directly from the Alexander-Briggs registry entry for 4₁.

    source
    theorem QOU.Calculations.registry_volume_eq_mass_derivation :
    KnotRegistry.figureEightEntry.volume.value = MassDerivation.vol_figure_eight

    The figure-eight knot's registry volume equals the MassDerivation volume constant. This ensures the mass derivation is sourced from the Alexander-Briggs-indexed registry, not a free-floating constant.

    Knot Atlas: http://katlas.org/wiki/4_1

    source
    theorem QOU.Calculations.higgs_is_figure_eight :
    KnotRegistry.lookupByIdentity KnotRegistry.QOUIdentity.higgsField = some KnotRegistry.figureEightEntry

    The registry entry for the Higgs field is the figure-eight knot. By construction: unfold the registry list, the find? skips unknotEntry (.neutrino) and trefoilEntry (.electron), then matches figureEightEntry (.higgsField).

    Note: .muon has no registry entry (the muon generation-2 identity would correspond to a cinquefoil entry not yet added to the registry). lookupByIdentity .muon = none.

    Section 2: Skein-Deformed Mass Residue #

    The QOU mass ratio formula: m_μ / m_e = Vol(4₁) / ℏ_q² where ℏ_q = 1 - 1/q is the quantum Planck constant.

    source
    theorem QOU.Calculations.skein_mass_residue (q : ℝ) (_hq : q > 1) :
    have h_q := MassDerivation.quantum_planck q; h_q ≠ 0 → MassDerivation.experimental_mu_e_ratio = MassDerivation.vol_figure_eight / h_q ^ 2 → MassDerivation.experimental_mu_e_ratio = KnotRegistry.figureEightEntry.volume.value / h_q ^ 2

    The skein-deformed mass residue formula. Given the topological mass conjecture, the muon-electron mass ratio equals the figure-eight volume divided by the squared quantum Planck constant.

    This is the central equation of the QOU mass derivation. Ref: Manuscript Conjecture 4.25.

    Section 3: Substrate Parameter Derivation #

    Full derivation chain:

    1. Vol(4₁) / (m_μ/m_e) = ℏ_q²
    2. ℏ_q = √(Vol(4₁) / (m_μ/m_e)) ≈ 0.09908
    3. q = 1 / (1 - ℏ_q) ≈ 1.1097
