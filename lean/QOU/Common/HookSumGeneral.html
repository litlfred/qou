    QOU.Common.HookSumGeneral — the hook-sum identity for ALL partitions #

    W11 (HookSumIdentity.lean) discharged the balanced_quantum_twist hook-sum hypothesis Σ_c h(c) = b(λ) + b(λ') + n by decide for the hook and staircase families. This file proves the general engine behind it — the arm-sum identity, for every partition — sorry-free:

    Σ_i C(λ_i, 2) = Σ_i Σ_{j<λ_i} j = Σ_j j · (colLen λ j) = b(λ'), (armSum)

    i.e. the diagram cells weighted by column index j, summed rows-first vs columns-first (a Fubini double-count: armSum_eq_colSum), identified with b of the conjugate (armSum_eq_bStat_conj). Here b(λ) = Σ_i i · λ_i (0-indexed) and colLen λ j = #{i : λ_i > j}.

    The full hook-sum identity assembles from this as

    Σ_c h(c) = Σ_c arm + Σ_c leg + Σ_c 1 = b(λ') + b(λ) + n,

    using Σ_c arm = armSum λ = b(λ') (proved here) and Σ_c leg = armSum λ' = b(λ) (the leg sum of λ is the arm sum of its transpose). The remaining step — the h = arm + leg + 1 cell decomposition and the leg/transpose identity over the List ℕ encoding — is mechanical conjugation bookkeeping, not yet formalized here; HookSumIdentity.lean covers the hook/staircase families concretely by decide in the meantime.

    Ground ring: none — a ℕ-valued combinatorial identity feeding the Field twist in BalancedQuantumTwist.
