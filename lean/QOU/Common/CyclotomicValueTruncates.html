    Cyclotomic value truncation — the residue ↔ Habiro-tail change of basis is finite #

    Structural core supporting prop:habiro-residue-sum-equivalence (content/.../mass-theory/habiro-residue-sum-equivalence.md) and the audit docs/audits/2026-07-18-residue-habiro-tail-algebraic-not-analytic.md.

    The identity between the Habiro cyclotomic expansion Σ_m a_m (q;q)_m and the Gröbner–Shirshov residue spectral sum is often labelled an analytic identity. It is not: both sides are algebraic operations on one element of the Habiro ring Ẑ[q], related by the unipotent-triangular evaluation-at-roots-of-unity map. The load-bearing algebraic fact is that at a root of unity ζ where (ζ;ζ)_N = 0, the cyclotomic sum truncates to its degree-< N partial sum — every higher term vanishes because the vanishing Pochhammer factor persists. There is nothing to resum; the change of basis is finite.

    Generic [CommRing R], §7c wall-safe: no ℝ, no ordering, no analysis. The ζ is an arbitrary ring element with (ζ;ζ)_N = 0 (a root of unity in the intended reading); no root-of-unity structure is needed for the truncation.

    Verified lean-direct against the pinned Mathlib (exit 0), sorry-free.

    Ref: [habiro2004] https://doi.org/10.2140/gt.2004.8.1127

      Cyclotomic value truncates. At a root of unity ζ with (ζ;ζ)_N = 0, a cyclotomic sum Σ_{m ≤ M} a_m (ζ;ζ)_m (M ≥ N) equals its partial sum up to N: every term with m ≥ N vanishes.

      This is the finite, unipotent-triangular change of basis between the Habiro cyclotomic coefficients a_m and the root-of-unity value — the algebraic fact that makes the residue ↔ cyclotomic-tail equivalence a finite algebraic identity, not an analytic one.
