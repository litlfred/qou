    QOU.Common.HookSumIdentity — the hook-sum identity, decided for hook & staircase shapes #

    balanced_quantum_twist (QOU.Common.BalancedQuantumTwist, its sibling module) reduces the balanced quantum dimension to the monomial exponent Σ hookSz − Σ cellSz, collapsing to content(λ) − n(n−1)/2 given the hook-sum identity

    Σ_{c ∈ λ} h(c) = b(λ) + b(λ') + n, b(λ) = Σ_i i·λ_i (0-indexed),

    the one piece of partition combinatorics balanced_quantum_twist leaves as a hypothesis. This file discharges that hypothesis for two shape families — hooks (a, 1^b) and staircases (k, k−1, …, 1) — by kernel decide on a computable List ℕ encoding of the partition (row lengths, weakly decreasing). Each decide is an honest finite verification: the whole identity is evaluated by the kernel, no sorry, no native_decide.

    The general identity (all λ) is a natural follow-up requiring an induction on partitions with a conjugation-double-counting lemma; here the finite families are exactly what the owner queued ("via finite decide"), and they cover the shapes the low-body-order binding terms actually use.

    Ground ring: none — this is a ℕ-valued combinatorial identity (the twist it feeds is the Field statement in BalancedQuantumTwist).
