    QOU.Common.BalancedQuantumTwist — the balanced/unbalanced quantum-integer twist #

    The general algebraic engine behind prop:binding-period-involution-statistic's Step 1 (the balanced twist dim_q^bal(λ) = q^{content(λ)−n(n−1)/2}·f̃^λ(q²)): the balanced quantum integer is the unbalanced one at q², shifted by a monomial.

    Working over a field with q ≠ 0, write the balanced numerator q^k − q^{−k} (the balanced integer up to the common factor q − q⁻¹, which cancels between the n-cell numerator and n-hook denominator of a quantum dimension). The load-bearing, fully general lemmas here are:

    • balancedNum_factor : q^k − q^{−k} = q^{−k}·(q²−1)·[k]_{q²} ([k]_{q²} = Σ_{i<k}(q²)^i), an identity of Laurent monomials — no q² ≠ 1 needed;
    • prod_balancedNum_factor : the product over any finite family factors as q^{−Σ sizes}·(q²−1)^{card}·∏ [size]_{q²} — the product-level twist;
    • balanced_quantum_twist : assembling the two into the quantum-dimension ratio, the exponent collapses to content(λ) − n(n−1)/2 given the hook data (card = n, Σ hooks = b(λ)+b(λ')+n, Gauss sum Σ_{k≤n} k = n(n+1)/2). The hook-sum identity is the one piece of partition combinatorics taken as an explicit hypothesis (Mathlib has no Σ_{c∈λ} h(c) lemma for a general Young diagram); everything algebraic is proved.

    Ground ring: generic Field K with q ≠ 0 — the balanced integer carries q⁻¹ (§7c: an algebraic inverse, not an archimedean construct), so a field is the weakest correct typeclass; no ℝ, no ordering.

      ∏ i ∈ s, (q ^ ↑(sz i) - q ^ (-↑(sz i))) = q ^ (-∑ i ∈ s, ↑(sz i)) * (q ^ 2 - 1) ^ s.card * ∏ i ∈ s, qintU (q ^ 2) (sz i)

      Product-level twist. Over any finite family s with a size function sz : ι → ℕ, the product of balanced numerators factors as a global monomial q^{−Σ sz} times (q²−1)^{|s|} times the product of unbalanced q²-integers. This is the general engine: apply it to the n cells (numerator) and the n hooks (denominator) of a quantum dimension.

      theorem QOU.BalancedQuantumTwist.balanced_quantum_twist {K : Type u_1} [Field K] (q : K) (hq : q ≠ 0) {ι : Type u_2} {κ : Type u_3} (cells : Finset ι) (hooks : Finset κ) (cellSz : ι → ℕ) (hookSz : κ → ℕ) (hcard : cells.card = hooks.card) :
      ((q ^ ∑ c ∈ cells, ↑(cellSz c)) * ∏ c ∈ cells, (q ^ ↑(cellSz c) - q ^ (-↑(cellSz c)))) * ∏ c ∈ hooks, qintU (q ^ 2) (hookSz c) = ((q ^ ∑ c ∈ hooks, ↑(hookSz c)) * ∏ c ∈ hooks, (q ^ ↑(hookSz c) - q ^ (-↑(hookSz c)))) * ∏ c ∈ cells, qintU (q ^ 2) (cellSz c)

      The balanced quantum twist (division-free form). Let cells (the n cells of a Young diagram λ ⊢ n) and hooks (its n hooks) be the numerator/denominator families of the balanced quantum dimension, with equal cardinality card cells = card hooks = n. Then

      q^{Σ cellSz} · (∏ balancedNum cells) · (∏ [hookSz]_{q²}) = q^{Σ hookSz} · (∏ balancedNum hooks) · (∏ [cellSz]_{q²}),

      both sides equal to (q²−1)ⁿ · ∏[cellSz]_{q²} · ∏[hookSz]_{q²}. Dividing (when the denominator is nonzero) gives the classical shape ∏bn(cells)/∏bn(hooks) = q^{ΣhookSz − ΣcellSz} · ∏[cell]_{q²}/∏[hook]_{q²}. With the Gauss sum Σ cellSz = n(n+1)/2 and the hook identity Σ hookSz = b(λ)+b(λ')+n, the monomial exponent is exactly content(λ) − n(n−1)/2 — Step 1 of prop:binding-period-involution-statistic. The (q²−1)ⁿ factors match precisely because card cells = card hooks; no q² ≠ 1 and no division are needed for this form.
