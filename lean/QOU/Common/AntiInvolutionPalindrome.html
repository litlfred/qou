    QOU.Common.AntiInvolutionPalindrome — palindromy & first-moment vanishing #

    The general algebra behind two of the content-monomial separation facts (computations/lever1_content_monomial_separation.py, lever-1 W12): the content-only binding object

    C_n(q) = Σ_{λ⊢n} f^λ · q^{content(λ)}

    is (P2) palindromic C_n(q) = C_n(q⁻¹) and (P4) its deviation from the classical value is bar-even O((q−1)²) — the same q-rigidity the level-1/2 alien datum Δ_{ω₀} measured (bar-even, O((q−1)²); the W5 bridge datum, lever1-bridge-alien-datum.witness.json). Both are instances of a single abstract fact, proved here generally and sorry-free: a weighted exponential sum over a finite set carrying an anti-invariant involution is palindromic, and its first moment vanishes.

    The anti-invariant involution for C_n is partition conjugation λ ↦ λ': f^{λ'} = f^λ (weight invariant) and content(λ') = −content(λ) (exponent anti-invariant). Feeding those two facts into the abstract lemmas gives P2 and P4 for every n at once — no decide, no RSK. The first-moment lemma is the exact algebraic form of "P'(1) = 0", i.e. the bar-even O((q−1)²) alien rigidity: the linear term of the q-deformation is forced to vanish.

    Ground ring: Field K with q ≠ 0 for the palindromy (the exponent is a ℤ power, q⁻¹ is algebraic — §7c wall-safe); the first-moment identity is ℤ-valued.

    ∑ i ∈ s, ↑(w i) * q ^ e i = ∑ i ∈ s, ↑(w i) * q ^ (-e i)

    Palindromy from an anti-invariant involution. If σ is an involution of the finite set s, the weight w is σ-invariant, and the exponent e is σ-anti-invariant (e (σ i) = − e i), then the weighted exponential sum is invariant under q ↦ q⁻¹: Σ_{i∈s} w i · q^{e i} = Σ_{i∈s} w i · q^{−e i}.
