    QOU.Common.FormalResidue — residue of an exact form, library version #

    Library home for the formal-residue calculus first proved inline in the content-block sibling brings-surface/sw-classical-jet-exactness.lean (prop:sw-classical-jet-exactness). A local Laurent expansion is modelled by its coefficient function f : ℤ → R; the residue is the coefficient of t⁻¹; the termwise derivative shifts every exponent down by one. The load-bearing invariant is fresidue_fderiv: the residue of an exact form vanishes — d(anything) has no residue, anywhere, ever.

    Beyond the block-level statement, this module adds the library API:

    • linearity of fderiv and fresidue (fderiv_add, fresidue_add, …);
    • vanishing residues for all positive-order jets (fresidue_jet_succ);
    • the converse over a characteristic-zero field (exists_antideriv_iff_fresidue_eq_zero): a formal Laurent expansion has a termwise antiderivative iff its residue vanishes — the residue is the complete obstruction to exactness in the formal model.

    The core stays over a generic CommRing R (§7c wall-safe); the converse needs division by exponents, hence Field K + CharZero K — an algebraic hypothesis, not an archimedean one.
