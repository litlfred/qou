    The link of the Bring cone: a circle bundle over the curve, Euler number −6 #

    Bean qou-rydq. The affine cone over Bring's curve B ⊂ ℙ³ (the canonical model, a complete intersection of a quadric and a cubic) is a quasi-homogeneous surface singularity, and its link is the Seifert-fibred 3-manifold given by the unit circle bundle of the embedding line bundle restricted to the curve (Orlik–Wagreich). For a curve of genus g embedded by a line bundle of degree d the Seifert Euler number of that circle bundle is −d. The canonical embedding has d = deg K_B = 2g − 2, so the link has Euler number −(2g − 2) = 2 − 2g = χ_eu(B); at g = 4 this is −6, and the total space is the unit tangent bundle UT(Σ₄) (the circle bundle of K⁻¹ = T_B, orientation reversed), whose fundamental group is infinite.

    Two consequences recorded here as the arithmetic they rest on:

    • canonical_link_euler_eq_chi: for a canonically embedded curve the link's Euler number is the topological Euler characteristic — the −6 of lem:brings-euler-characteristic has a third face, as a Seifert invariant.
    • hopf_link_euler: the model QOU.Tetraplectic.BringCone R = Quaternion R is the cone over the Hopf circle bundle S³ → S² = ℙ¹, degree 1, Euler number −1, finite (trivial) π₁. The two links differ in Euler number by the factor 6 and in π₁ by finite-versus-infinite, which is why the quaternionic (3-Sasakian) description of the Bring cone is a local model of the fibre and not a global statement about the link. The content change that rewords the corpus's lemma accordingly is owner-gated (bean qou-rydq); the arithmetic is not.

    Integers only; no sorry.

    -- Ref: [orlik-wagreich1971] https://doi.org/10.2307/1970772 -- Ref: [milnor1968] Singular points of complex hypersurfaces (link of an isolated singularity)
