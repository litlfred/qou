      Content statement (from manuscript).

      Bring's surface $\Sigma_4$ (Bring's surface) gives rise to a quantum observable universe over $R$ (with $30 \in R^{\times}$). The five sub-claims:

      (i) Quantum universe — the realisation is $\mathbf{C} = \mathrm{Rep}(\mathrm{SU}_q(2))$, i.e. quantum-group arity $2$; (ii) Observability — $\dim \mathrm{Hom}(\mathbf 1, \mathrm{Ad}\,A) = 0$ (quantum Schur lemma); (iii) Yekutieli lifting — perpetual obstruction $\forall n,\ \kappa_n \ne 0$ (the lone open categorical input, carried by [BringsObstruction]); (iv) Euler characteristic — $g = 4$ and $\chi(A) = 2 - 2g = -6$ (derived arithmetic); (v) Geometric descent (Riemann–Hurwitz) — $6$ branch points, total ramification $14$.

      Only sub-claim (iii) is assumed; (i), (ii), (iv), (v) are discharged by norm_num / rfl. In particular χ(A) = -6 is derived from χ(A) = 2 - 2·4, not asserted independently.
