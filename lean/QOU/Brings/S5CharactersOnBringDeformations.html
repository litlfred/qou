    S_5 on the deformations of Bring's curve — the character arithmetic #

    The first-order deformation space H¹(T_C) of Bring's curve C = Σ₄ is a representation of its automorphism group S₅. Two content blocks state its decomposition, H¹(T_C) ≅ [4,1] ⊕ [3,2], and draw consequences from it: no S₅-invariant deformation (rigidity), no invariant symplectic form on the deformation space, and a one-dimensional space of equivariant "actions" V₄ ⊗ [3,2] → [3,2].

    This file proves the arithmetic half of those statements by kernel evaluation, from the character table of S₅ taken as input:

    • charTable — the seven irreducible characters of S₅ on its seven conjugacy classes, in the order 1⁵, 2·1³, 2²·1, 3·1², 3·2, 4·1, 5, with class sizes 1, 10, 15, 20, 20, 30, 24 (Fulton–Harris, Representation Theory, §2.3; James–Kerber, The Representation Theory of the Symmetric Group). It is a definition with a citation, not a theorem: nothing in this file derives the table. charTable_orthonormal checks that the input is at least an orthonormal system for the class-weighted inner product, which any character table must be.
    • The symmetric and exterior powers are computed by the power-sum formulas 2·χ_{Sym²}(g) = χ(g)² + χ(g²), 2·χ_{Λ²}(g) = χ(g)² − χ(g²), 6·χ_{Sym³}(g) = χ(g)³ + 3χ(g)χ(g²) + 2χ(g³). To stay in ℤ and avoid division, the file works with the scaled characters 2·χ_{Sym²}, 2·χ_{Λ²}, 6·χ_{Sym³} throughout, and every multiplicity statement is scaled accordingly: inner (2·χ) ψ = 240 means ⟨χ, ψ⟩ = 1.
    • h1TangentSix is 6·χ_{H¹(T_C)} computed by the complete-intersection route, H¹(T_C) = H⁰(N_C) − 𝔰𝔩(V₄) with N_C = 𝒪_C(2) ⊕ 𝒪_C(3); the geometric inputs of that route (the normal-bundle splitting, H⁰(T_C) = 0, H¹(T_{ℙ³}|_C) = 0) are theorems of algebraic geometry cited in the content block, not formalised here.

    Every theorem below is a closed equality in ℤ and is discharged by decide; this is a genuinely finite computation, not a laundering of a structural claim (AGENTS.md §Strict Lean Discipline, item 6).

    Companion probe (same numbers, exact Fraction arithmetic, plus a brute-force check of the power-sum formulas over all 120 permutations): computations/audits/bring_h1_tangent_ci_route_and_v4_on_32_probe.py.
