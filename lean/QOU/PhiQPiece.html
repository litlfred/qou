    QOU.PhiQPiece — Φ_q-piece sub-link of a braid closure #

    For a braid β ∈ B_n and a subset S ⊆ {0, …, n-2} of its crossing indices, the Φ_q-piece K_S ⊆ S³ is the link obtained by smoothing (L₀-resolution) every crossing of β outside S and keeping the crossings indexed by S intact.

          noncomputable def QOU.PhiQPiece.subLink (β : Braid) (S : Finset (Fin (β.n - 1))) :
          SubLink

          The Φ_q-piece K_S of a braid β: the closure of the sub-braid obtained by deleting (= replacing by the identity strand) every crossing whose index is not in S.

          This is the abstract specification; the geometric construction (Kauffman L₀-smoothing of the diagram) is recorded in the accompanying .md file.
