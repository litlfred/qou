    QOU.HeckeAlgebra.Wedderburn — block decomposition #

    The Wedderburn structure theorem applied to the semisimple Iwahori-Hecke algebra H_n(q) at generic q: a direct sum of matrix algebras indexed by partitions λ ⊢ n, H_n(q) ≅ ⊕_{λ ⊢ n} M_{f^λ}(ℝ), where f^λ = #{standard Young tableaux of shape λ} is the seminormal dimension from QOU.HeckeAlgebra.Hoefsmit.

    What this module provides #

    • WedderburnBlock λ — the matrix algebra for partition λ.
    • WedderburnDecomposition n — the indexed family Π λ ⊢ n, WedderburnBlock λ representing an element of H_n(q) in block-diagonal form. The embedding itself is not in this module. It lives in QOU/HeckeAlgebra/WedderburnConstruction.lean as blockDiagonal (unconditional, injective) and wedderburnEmbedOf (the same map reindexed to Fin (heckeDim n), given RSK as a hypothesis). An opaque wedderburnEmbed stood here until 2026-09-05 and is gone; see the note where it was declared for why deletion and not deprecation.

    (This list previously also advertised wedderburnEmbed_posSemidef — "block-diagonal-of-PSD-blocks is PSD on the whole". No such theorem exists in this file; the claim is removed rather than left standing.)

    Categorical content #

    For the QOU SDP-uniqueness arguments, the key fact is: "PSD-feasibility on H_n(q) decomposes block-by-block", i.e. an element x ∈ H_n(q) is PSD iff each Wedderburn block x_λ ∈ M_{f^λ}(ℝ) is PSD. This makes the SDP problem fully diagonal across partitions and reduces uniqueness to per-block arguments.

    Open Lean port #

    Block-diagonal embedding requires careful index arithmetic (Fin n block-aware composition); the proof of dimension agreement Σ_λ (f^λ)² = n! is the Robinson-Schensted-Knuth correspondence (a non-trivial Mathlib import). Both are deferred — this module captures the type-level interface.

    Superseded in part (2026-09-05, beans qou-usol, qou-kp2z). The first of those two is not in fact a blocker: on the natural index type Σ λ, Fin (f^λ) × Fin (f^λ) the assembly is an ordinary definition and is injective. QOU/HeckeAlgebra/WedderburnConstruction.lean builds it (blockDiagonal, blockDiagonal_injective) with axiom footprint [propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]. Only landing in Fin (n!) needs RSK, and that step is isolated there as the explicit hypothesis RSKDimIdentity. The opaque that used to stand below has been deleted, so what remains open here is deleting it in favour of wedderburnEmbedOf is the clean follow-up (its two remaining references are doc comments in MarkovTrace.lean).

    • [james-kerber-1981] James-Kerber, The Representation Theory of the Symmetric Group, §1.1 (RSK correspondence).

          The embedding: retired from this file (2026-09-05, bean qou-kp2z) #

          opaque wedderburnEmbed : ∀ {n}, WedderburnDecomposition n → Matrix (Fin (heckeDim n)) (Fin (heckeDim n)) ℝ stood here. It is deleted; QOU/HeckeAlgebra/WedderburnConstruction.lean carries the real thing (blockDiagonal, and wedderburnEmbedOf for the Fin (heckeDim n) landing).

          Why it is a deletion rather than a deprecation: opaque is not a free notation. It is irreducible and it reports in #print axioms, so every declaration routed through it read as axiom-bearing by this corpus's own standard. Measured on merged main at 7e75622 before removal, nothing routed through it at all: the sole term-level occurrence of the name in the whole Lean tree was the declaration itself, and wenzlBridge — whose module doc described the identity in terms of it — is in fact stated over markovTraceOnWedderburn. So the axiom was being paid for by nobody, which is the cheapest possible case and also the easiest one to leave standing forever.

          Its docstring carried an argument worth answering rather than discarding. Per a Gemini review it held that the embedding could not simply be defined, because a stub returning 0 would contradict the Markov-trace structure (non-zero traces for non-zero blocks via the Wenzl bridge) together with markovTrace_zero. That objection is met, not dodged: blockDiagonal is not a stub, and blockDiagonal_injective shows it is not the zero map — a non-zero decomposition has a non-zero image. The objection was to a stub; the answer is a construction.

          The dimension identity Σ_λ (f^λ)² = n! (RSK) is still not available here, and is not silently assumed: it is the named hypothesis RSKDimIdentity in the construction module, discharged by nothing in this corpus.
