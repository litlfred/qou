          The complement: three block-reading functionals at (2,1) ARE independent #

          WedderburnTrace.three_supported_traceFunctionals_dependent says any three trace-factoring functionals at one partition are linearly dependent — one coefficient against one number. This is the other half, and it is what makes the pair sharp rather than merely discouraging: read the block instead of its trace and three independent functionals exist at (2,1), exhibited explicitly and witnessed by matrix units.

          Together the two say precisely where a derivation of the (2,1) clause has to live: not in the Markov trace, which is one-dimensional there, but in the f^{(2,1)} = 2-dimensional block, of which the trace reads one dimension.

          What this does not say, and the distinction is the whole point. It does not identify these three functionals with volume, surface and Coulomb. It says three independent functionals exist at (2,1) once you read the block — an existence statement about the linear algebra, nothing more. Matching them to physical quantities is exactly the open obligation qou-qb7p recorded, and three independent functionals existing is a necessary condition for such a match, not evidence of one. A count agreeing with a count is a target to check a future derivation against, never a criterion to search on.
