    QOU.Descent.descended_level_zero #

    Canonical Lean declaration for content block prop:descended-level-zero: QOU.Descent.descended_level_zero.

    The jet-tower decomposition asserts that the descended (holonomy) contribution to the mass appears once only, at jet level 0; levels n ≥ 1 carry only singleton and quadratic transient contributions. The derivation: the descended projector stabilises under composition, so iterated descent collapses to the base-level holonomy regardless of the jet level at which it is read off.

    The vanishing-at-higher-levels claim is irreducibly abstract over the opaque holonomy contribution function; no Mathlib structure makes it tautologous. We therefore name the conditional conjecture as a Prop (pattern C of the vacuity-fix playbook): for a contribution function desc_contrib : ℕ → ℝ, descended_level_zero is the proposition that the contribution vanishes at every jet level ≥ 1. The self-assuming claim_descended_level_zero carrier field of the old encoding is removed; inhabiting the proposition is the open conditional claim, not faked by a projection.

    def QOU.Descent.descended_level_zero (desc_contrib : ℕ → ℝ) :
    Prop

    Descended-level-zero collapse (named conditional conjecture).

    For a descended (holonomy) contribution function desc_contrib : ℕ → ℝ indexed by jet level, descended_level_zero desc_contrib is the proposition that the descended contribution appears only at jet level 0:

    ∀ n, 1 ≤ n → desc_contrib n = 0.

    This names the conditional claim of the jet-tower descent proposition (pattern C); it is not asserted — an inhabitant witnesses the stabilisation of the descended projector under iterated descent.
