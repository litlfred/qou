    QOU.dq_squared — archimedean specialisation of d_q² α #

    Proposition (prop:categorical-dq-squared-archimedean specialisation).

    For the q-deformed exterior derivative acting on Ω^k(M),

      d_q² α = q⁻¹ · (1 − q⁻¹) · dΘ ∧ 𝓛_ξ α

    where Θ is the connection form and ξ is the Reeb vector field. In particular, d_q² = 0 identically when q = 1 (recovering the classical de Rham d² = 0).

    The Lean encoding records the formula against an abstract DqSquaredContext carrying the differential-form data (α, dΘ ∧ 𝓛_ξ α) and the substrate parameter q. Concretising to a real Ω^k(M) exterior algebra is downstream work.
