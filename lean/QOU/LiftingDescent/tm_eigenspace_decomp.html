    QOU.LiftingDescent.tm_eigenspace_decomp #

    Canonical declaration for content block lem:topological-mass-eigenspace-decomp: Eigenspace decomposition of the mass integrand (descent involution).

    The manuscript lemma says: the mass-identity integrand α = (∇_A q, L ∧ F⁺) splits under the descent involution τ̂ₙ into a descended and a transient part, those two parts are Killing-orthogonal, and the multiplicative-integral norm is additive over Killing-orthogonal sums — whence ‖MI(α)‖ = ‖MI(α_desc)‖ + ‖MI(α_trans)‖.

    What is proved here versus what is assumed. The splitting is not assumed: it is QOU.LiftingDescent.JetChain.decomposition, proved in JetProlongation from the retraction identity. What the context class carries is the two genuinely-open inputs — Killing-orthogonality of the two eigenspaces at the integrand, and the additivity law for MI over Killing-orthogonal sums. Neither is a bare Prop: each is an equation in the class's own data, so no instance can discharge it with trivial, and the conclusion is derived from them rather than carried as a field (AGENTS.md §3b-cond item 5).

    This replaces an earlier encoding in which killing_orthogonal and mi_additive were opaque Prop fields with companion witnesses and the conclusion was a third field projected out — a shape under which an instance could take both hypotheses to be True and still be forced to supply the conclusion outright.

    Type (max u v)

    Context for the tm_eigenspace_decomp claim: a jet-chain level, a Killing form and a multiplicative-integral norm on that level, the mass integrand, and the two manuscript hypotheses relating them.

    • level : ℕ

      The brane-tower level at which the decomposition is taken.

    • killing : EndAlg (level R EndAlg + 1) → EndAlg (level R EndAlg + 1) → R

      The Killing form on the level-level + 1 endomorphism algebra.

    • MI : EndAlg (level R EndAlg + 1) → ℝ

      The multiplicative-integral norm functional x ↦ ‖MI(x)‖.

    • alpha : EndAlg (level R EndAlg + 1)

      The mass-identity integrand α = (∇_A q, L ∧ F⁺).

    • killing_orthogonal : killing (JetChain.descendedPart (level R EndAlg) alpha) (JetChain.transientPart (level R EndAlg) alpha) = 0

      Killing-form orthogonality of 𝒜^desc and 𝒜^trans at the integrand: the descended and transient parts of α pair to zero.

    • mi_additive (x y : EndAlg (level R EndAlg + 1)) : killing x y = 0 → MI (x + y) = MI x + MI y

      Multiplicative-integral additivity over Killing-orthogonal sums: MI is additive on any pair that pairs to zero.

      Lemma (manuscript lem:topological-mass-eigenspace-decomp).

      Let α be the integrand of the mass identity for a vortex ϱ and let τ̂ₙ be the descent involution. Killing-form orthogonality of 𝒜_{n+1}^desc ⊕ 𝒜_{n+1}^trans together with multiplicative-integral additivity over orthogonal direct sums gives

      ‖MI(α)‖ = ‖MI(α_desc)‖ + ‖MI(α_trans)‖.

      The proof rewrites α by the proved jet-chain decomposition and then applies the additivity law at the orthogonality hypothesis.
