    QOU.LiftingDescent.SpatialDimensionBinder — d as an actual binder #

    The gap this closes. The spatial dimension d is named throughout the corpus and is a binder nowhere. q-navier-stokes.lean carries dim : Nat := 3 as a structure field with a default that no theorem varies; the descent rungs rem:descent-reduction-{three,two,…} are prose. Nothing in the Lean tree takes d and proves something whose content changes with it.

    What d is allowed to bind to here. Only one thing in the descent ladder is both d-dependent and already pinned by proved corpus results: whether the mass identity's surface Σ can carry a boundary. The mass identity integrates a two-dimensional multiplicative integral, so Σ is 2-dimensional; it can be a proper submanifold — and so can have non-empty boundary — exactly when the ambient dimension strictly exceeds 2. Below that it is forced to be the base itself (closed), or to degenerate.

    That threshold is what separates the rungs, and it is where the physics sits: with no boundary the feedback image of the surface holonomy is trivial, so by CrossedModuleSurfaceHolonomy.ker_le_center (sorry-free) the holonomy is central — the mass is abelian. With a boundary it need not be.

    The honest shape, and what is assumed #

    DescentStratum carries the 2-dimensional Stokes theorem as an explicit hypothesis field, because the corpus cites it (Yekutieli Thm 0.4.2) and does not prove it. This is the §3b-cond conditional-class pattern, and it is used in the non-degenerate direction required there: the class supplies the input (Φ h = 1 at a boundaryless stratum) and mass_central_of_closed derives the conclusion through ker_le_center, rather than carrying the conclusion as a field and projecting it.

    Why this is not decoration — d is load-bearing, and that is PROVED #

    A d-indexed statement is worthless if the same conclusion holds at every d. stratumThree exhibits a stratum at d = 3 whose realised holonomies include a non-central element, so mass_central_of_closed genuinely fails there: exists_realised_not_central_at_three. Its hypothesis is therefore not removable, and the family really does vary across the threshold. The witness is the adjoint crossed module of Equiv.Perm (Fin 3), whose centre is trivial.

    What this does NOT do #

    It does not formalise differential geometry, manifolds, or forms. d : ℕ is a label and the geometric content — that a 2-dimensional Σ admits a boundary iff 2 < d — enters as the definition BoundaryAvailable, not as a theorem. So this makes d a real binder over the consequence (centrality), not over the geometry itself. Formalising the geometry is a separate and much larger task.

    Base ring §7c: none — group theory and ℕ, no ℝ, nothing archimedean.
