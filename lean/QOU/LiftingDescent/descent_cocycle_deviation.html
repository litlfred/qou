    QOU.LiftingDescent.descent_cocycle_deviation — twisted Čech 2-cocycle equation #

    Lemma (lem:descent-cocycle-deviation).

    Under the central extension 𝔊_{n+1} → 𝔊_n with local lift data a_{ij}^{(n)} ∈ 𝓞_M^×, the cocycle deviation at level n+1 is

      g_{ijk}^{(n+1)} = a_{ij}^{(n)} · a_{jk}^{(n)} · (a_{ik}^{(n)})⁻¹ · g_{ijk}^{(n)}

    so that the twisted Čech 2-cocycle equation

      d(g_{ijk}^{(n)}) = a_{ij}^{(n)} · g_{ijk}^{(n)} · (a_{jk}^{(n)})⁻¹

    holds, with cohomology class in H²(M, 𝓞_M^×).

    • g_cocycle : ℕ → ℕ → ℕ → ℕ → M

    • d_cocycle : ℕ → ℕ → ℕ → ℕ → M

    • claim_cocycle_deviation (n i j k : ℕ) : g_cocycle (n + 1) i j k = mul (mul (a_local n i j) (a_local n j k)) (mul (inv (a_local n i k)) (g_cocycle n i j k))
