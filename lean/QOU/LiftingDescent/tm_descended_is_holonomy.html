    QOU.LiftingDescent.tm_descended_is_holonomy #

    Lemma (lem:topological-mass-holonomy).

    Under the topological blow-up decomposition Σ = Σ₀ ∪ ⋃_{i=1}^N D_i, where Σ₀ is a disc spanning the unknot core ϱ₀ and each D_i is the exceptional disc at the i-th blow-up, the descended term equals the unknot-core holonomy:

    MI∫_Σ τ̂ₙ(α) = Hol_{∇A}(ϱ₀; 𝔊).

    Structure (hypotheses vs. derivation) #

    Everything is a parameter; nothing is a named constant. The multiplicative integral takes values in an arbitrary (not necessarily commutative) monoid G — the structure group in which holonomies live — so the blow-up product is an ordered List.prod. Surfaces and integrands are arbitrary types, and the multiplicative integral is an arbitrary map MI : Form → Surf → G.

    The descended term is modelled as what the proof block says it is: a level-n section β pulled back along the blow-down, τ̂ₙ(α) = pullback β (the ascent embedding ℓₙ). The hypotheses are the standard properties of the multiplicative integral the proof uses, each strictly more primitive than the conclusion:

    1. hfact — multiplicativity over the blow-up decomposition (Yekutieli): MI∫_Σ = MI∫_{Σ₀} · Π_i MI∫_{D_i} (ordered product).
    2. hnat — naturality under the blow-down on the exceptional discs: MI∫_{D_i} (pullback β) = MI∫_{blowdown D_i} β.
    3. hcollapse — each exceptional disc collapses to a point under the blow-down: blowdown D_i = pt.
    4. hpt — the multiplicative integral over a degenerate (point) surface is the identity.
    5. hstokes — non-abelian Stokes on the spanning disc: MI∫_{Σ₀} (pullback β) = Hol_{∇A}(ϱ₀; 𝔊).

    Derived: (2)+(3)+(4) give that every exceptional-disc contribution is 1 (descended_exceptional_disc_trivial, formerly an assumed class field); (1) then collapses the ordered product and (5) closes.

    An earlier revision modelled the five quantities as opaque reals and assumed disc triviality directly as a Prop-class field; one earlier still defined descendedMIdisc := 1, collapsing the lemma to rfl. Bean tmvac.

    MI (pullback β) (D i) = 1

    Exceptional discs contribute trivially (derived). A pulled-back (descended) integrand has trivial multiplicative integral over every exceptional disc: naturality moves the integral to the blow-down image, which is a point, where the multiplicative integral is the identity.

    Descended term = unknot-core holonomy ([lem:topological-mass-holonomy]).
