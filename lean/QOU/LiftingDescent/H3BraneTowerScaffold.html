    QOU.LiftingDescent.H3BraneTowerScaffold #

    Scaffold for a concrete RecursiveBraneTower instance at the H_3(q_0) (proton-scale) Hecke algebra. The numerical realisation is in computations/tau_hat_n_proton_realisation.py (PR #533): a 6-dim level 0 = H_3(q_0), 30-dim Ω¹(H_3(q_0)), 180-dim level 1 = H_3(q_0) ⊗ Ω¹(H_3(q_0)), and a 180×180 idempotent τ̂_0 = ℓ_0 ∘ 𝔡_1 with rank 6 (descended) and nullity 174 in End(A_0) sense (rank 36 / nullity 32 364 in End(A_1) sense).

    This Lean file declares the shape of the instance, sorry-free: the level modules A_0/Ω¹/A_1, their dimensions, and the ascent/descent maps ℓ_0/𝔡_1 as concrete placeholder linear maps (embed / project the first 6 of 180 coordinates) satisfying the retraction identity descent_ascent_id and the idempotence tau_hat_0_idem. The physical matrix entries (the explicit H_3(q_0) regular representation, the Kähler-differential basis, the 180×6 ascent/descent matrices of PR #533) are not yet bound — a future refactor rebinds the defs to the Python realisation without changing any type or consumer-side proof, a routine but multi-day linear-algebra discharge using Mathlib.LinearAlgebra and the existing QOU.FrobeniusHopfData infra.

    This scaffold is not required by the abstract τ̂_n stack (which is sorry-free in recursive-brane-tower.lean, descent-involution.lean, jet-prolongation.lean per PR #532's audit). Its purpose is to provide a Lean-level point of entry for the level-1 mass-tower computation at A ≥ 3 — the next step after the proton-scale numerical probe.
