    QOU.LiftingDescent.DirectLimitAlgebra — Direct limit of the endomorphism algebras #

    The direct limit 𝒜_∞ = colim End_𝒞(Aₙ) is the algebra of observables at infinite brane level.

    Formal-chain version. Lives under lean/QOU/ and is compiled by lake build. The co-located documentation stub at lifting-and-descent/direct-limit-algebra.lean mirrors this content.

    structure QOU.LiftingDescent.DirectLimitAlgebra (R : Type u_2) [CommRing R] :
    Type (max u_2 (u_3 + 1))

    The direct limit algebra of a brane tower: the colimit of the sequence End_𝒞(A₀) → End_𝒞(A₁) → End_𝒞(A₂) → ⋯ with connecting maps given by jet prolongation.

    Polymorphic in the ground ring R (per QOU.GroundRingAnnotations, the formal-chain stub uses R = ℤ[q^±1] or generic R).
