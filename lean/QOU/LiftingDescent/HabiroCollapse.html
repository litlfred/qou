    The Habiro collapse at a 2p-th root of unity — and what it does not give #

    MotivicPerpetualObstructionFromTrace.lean proves the QOU Frobenius is a non-unit conditional on a hypothesis h_collapse : reduce p (frob p) = C. Bean qou-lrnd recorded that discharging h_collapse "needs cyclotomic ring theory over ℤ[ζ_2p]". This file does two things to that.

    One: the collapse is not cyclotomic at all. It factors into a general ring-theoretic statement plus a single arithmetic input. If φ : R →+* S sends q ↦ -1, then φ ((q;q)_m) = 0 for every m ≥ 2 — because the i = 2 factor is 1 - q² and 1 - (-1)² = 0. So any Habiro expansion Σ_{m<N} a m (q;q)_m has image φ (a 0) + 2 φ (a 1), with no roots of unity, no ramification, and no ring of integers anywhere in the proof (map_habiro_expansion). The cyclotomic content of h_collapse is exactly one fact, φ ζ_{2p} = -1, and everything else was already ordinary algebra.

    Two: the collapse does not imply what it is used for. Downstream, h_collapse feeds not_isUnit_of_image: ¬IsUnit (φ x) → ¬IsUnit x. That needs the residue to be a non-unit. The residue here is C = 4 in O/𝔭 ≅ 𝔽_p, and 4 is a unit there for every p ≠ 2 (isUnit_four_zmod). So the transfer is inapplicable at every odd prime — the direction of not_isUnit_of_image is what breaks, not its statement.

    This is a structural exclusion, not a failed search: there is an explicit unit with the required residue. The cyclotomic unit u₄ = 1 + ζ + ζ² + ζ³ = (1 - ζ⁴)/(1 - ζ) is a unit of ℤ[ζ_p] — its inverse 1 + ζ⁴ + ζ⁸ + ⋯ + ζ^{4(b-1)}, where 4b ≡ 1 (mod p), is integral by inspection — and ζ ≡ 1 (mod 𝔭) gives u₄ ≡ 4 (mod 𝔭). Verified exactly (integer arithmetic, no floats) for p ∈ {3,…,31, 101, 1009} in computations/probes/cyclotomic_residue_four_unit_probe.py.

    So Frob_p ≡ 4 (mod 𝔭) is compatible with Frob_p being a unit, and cannot on its own establish the perpetual obstruction.

    Ref: [manuscript] bean qou-lrnd

      Habiro collapse. Under any ring map sending q ↦ -1, a Habiro expansion collapses to its two lowest coefficients: φ (Σ_{m<N} a m · (q;q)_m) = φ (a 0) + 2 · φ (a 1).

      This is the whole content of h_collapse in MotivicPerpetualObstructionFromTrace.lean, minus the single arithmetic input φ q = -1. The 2 is φ ((q;q)_1) = 1 - (-1).

      theorem QOU.LiftingDescent.isUnit_four_zmod {p : ℕ} (hp : Nat.Prime p) (hp2 : p ≠ 2) :
      IsUnit 4

      4 is a unit of 𝔽_p for every odd prime — so a residue of 4 is not an obstruction, and not_isUnit_of_image cannot be applied to it.

      O/𝔭 ≅ 𝔽_p is where the collapse of the QOU Frobenius lands, which is why this blocks the intended argument at every prime except 2.
