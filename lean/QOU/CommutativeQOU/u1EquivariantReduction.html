    QOU.CommutativeQOU.u1EquivariantReduction #

    Canonical Lean declaration for content block at quantum-observable-universe/braids-and-knots/u1-equivariant-reduction.ts.

    For a commutative-limit QOU with exceptional divisor S_p ≅ P¹(ℂ) ≅ S², the U(1) ⊂ SU(2) rotation about the polar axis has exactly two fixed points (the poles φ = 0, π), and the orbit space S_p / U(1) ≅ [0, π] is an interval of length π.

    Both quantities are concrete and derived (pattern A of the vacuity-fix playbook), not assumed. The pole count is the literal 2; the orbit-interval length is the length π − 0 = π of the closed interval [0, π]. The self-assuming claim_u1EquivariantReduction carrier field of the old encoding is removed.

        U(1)-equivariant reduction (claims, derived).

        Let (C, Θ, G, S) be a commutative-limit QOU and S_p ≅ P¹(ℂ) the exceptional divisor at a brane point p. Fix the U(1) ⊂ SU(2) rotation about the polar axis. Then:

        1. the U(1) action has exactly 2 fixed points (the poles φ = 0, π);
        2. the orbit space S_p / U(1) ≅ [0, π] has length π.

        Both are derived: the pole count is rfl on the literal 2, and the interval length is π − 0 = π by sub_zero. These are concrete facts, not self-assuming projections of a carrier field.
