    theorem QOU.Knot.Kummer.one_sub_exp_mul_I (θ : ℝ) :
    1 - Complex.exp (↑θ * Complex.I) = ↑(2 * Real.sin (θ / 2)) * Complex.exp (↑((θ - Real.pi) / 2) * Complex.I)

    The half-angle factorisation. 1 − e^{iθ} = 2 sin(θ/2) · e^{i(θ−π)/2}.

    This is the whole geometric content: the modulus 2 sin(θ/2) is positive on (0, 2π) and the phase (θ−π)/2 lands in (−π/2, 0), so the argument of 1 − e^{iθ} is (θ−π)/2 on the nose, with no branch ambiguity.

    theorem QOU.Knot.Kummer.Lob_add_two_Lob (θ : ℝ) :
    Archimedean.Lob θ + 2 * Archimedean.Lob ((Real.pi - θ) / 2) = Archimedean.Cl_2 θ

    The Λ-identity behind it: Λ(θ) + 2Λ((π−θ)/2) = Cl₂(θ).

    Immediate from Cl_2_duplication, since Λ(t) = Cl₂(2t)/2: Cl₂(2θ)/2 + Cl₂(π−θ) = Cl₂(θ) is the duplication formula halved.

    theorem QOU.Knot.Kummer.blochWigner_lob_exp {θ : ℝ} (h0 : 0 < θ) (h1 : θ < Real.pi) :
    Archimedean.BlochWigner_lob (Complex.exp (↑θ * Complex.I)) = Archimedean.Cl_2 θ

    KUMMER'S IDENTITY ON THE UNIT CIRCLE.

    For θ ∈ (0, π), the Bloch–Wigner Λ-sum at z = e^{iθ} is Cl₂(θ).

    The tetrahedron is isosceles — angles (θ, (π−θ)/2, (π−θ)/2) — and the identity is exactly Clausen duplication.

    ⚠ This is the Λ-side only, and only on |z| = 1. It makes no claim about Im Li₂ — for that, and for the two halves combined, see QOU/Knot/KummerDilogHalf.lean. Neither reaches the 5₂ shape, which has |y₁| = 1.52.
