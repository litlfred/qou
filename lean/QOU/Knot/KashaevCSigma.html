      What hτ still asks for #

      hc is derived above. hτ is NOT the same kind of step, and it is worth being precise about why.

      c_σ is defined by the asymptotics, so hc was an identity about a shape those asymptotics produce. τ_DG is defined by an entirely different construction — the Dimofte–Garoufalidis 1-loop invariant, gluing equations and the Neumann–Zagier matrix — and QOU.OneLoopEdgeLattice formalises only the algebra that construction runs on, saying in its own header that nothing there "proves that the multi-cusp construction computes the adjoint torsion". In this corpus |τ(4₁)| is a SnapPy computation (computations/one_loop_torsion.py), matching DG's worked example.

      So hτ : |τ_DG| = |cot θ*|/2 is a bridge between two independent constructions, and the τ side of it has no Lean definition to bridge to. It is not one more step of this arc; it needs the 1-loop invariant formalised first. What CAN be pinned here is the target:
