        theorem QOU.Knot.JetGradeLengthShift.length_mul_sTop {n : ℕ} {w : Equiv.Perm (Fin (n + 2))} (hw : w JetGradePromotion.top = JetGradePromotion.top) :
        JetGradePromotion.length (w * sTop) = JetGradePromotion.length w + 1

        The promotion theorem, as a count. Multiplying on the right by the fresh generator raises the Coxeter length -- the Grobner-Shirshov filtration grade -- by exactly one, for every permutation fixing the top point.

        This is what GradedGeneratingFunction.gf_shift consumes: a degree function shifted by one with its coefficients untouched.

        The length always moves, for every permutation #

        length_mul_sTop needs w to fix the top point. That hypothesis is not needed for the weaker statement that the length changes, and the weaker statement is what the Hecke relation requires -- if the length could stay put, the descending branch of the right-action rule would be taken twice and T_s^2 = A T_s + 1 would fail.

        The bijection phi never used any hypothesis on w (see sTop_lt_sTop), so it generalises verbatim. Only the special pair needs care, and there the two alternatives are exhaustive: (sub, top) is an inversion of v * sTop exactly when it is not one of v, because right multiplication by sTop exchanges the values at those two points and a permutation cannot take the same value twice. So the count moves by exactly one, in one direction or the other.

        The length always moves. For every permutation -- no hypothesis at all -- right multiplication by the fresh generator changes the Coxeter length. It rises by one when (sub, top) was not an inversion and falls by one when it was, so in particular it is never left where it was.
