    The saddle index, concretely #

    k* = ⌊5N/6⌋ sits at angle θ_{k*} = k*π/N = 5π/6 − rπ/(6N) with r = 5N mod 6 ∈ [0, 5]. Three facts follow, and all three are what log_kashaevProd_saddle asks for.

    Taking logs of the sandwich #

    kashaev_le_argmax_mul and le_kashaev bracket ⟨4₁⟩_N between two multiples of the peak. Logging turns the multiples into additive constants.
