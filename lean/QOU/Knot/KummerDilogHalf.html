    For θ ∈ (0, π),

    `BlochWigner_lob(e^{iθ}) = Im cLiTwoExt(e^{iθ}) + log‖e^{iθ}‖ · arg(1 − e^{iθ})`

    — the second term vanishing because ‖e^{iθ}‖ = 1. This is Route 72's G3 discharged: the corpus's angle-built BlochWigner_lob is the Bloch–Wigner dilogarithm on the unit circle.
