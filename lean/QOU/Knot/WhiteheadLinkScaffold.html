            Conditional volume limit for the Whitehead link (derived, not axiomatised). Given the Lemma-4 class AND the majorant volume rate (the open 2D brick W6, bean qou-oiv6), the Whitehead-link Kashaev modulus obeys the volume conjecture: (2π/N)·log ‖J_N‖ → 4·Cl₂(π/2) = 4G = Vol(L5a1).

            The squeeze is no longer hand-rolled. It is QOU.MathlibExt.tendsto_log_div_of_sandwich — a sub-exponential sandwich does not move an exponential growth rate — instantiated at

            m N = majorant N / N^p ,   S N = ‖J_N‖ ,   c N = N^p ,

            for which m ≤ S is exactly VdVWhiteheadLemma4.phase_cost and S ≤ c·m is exactly the sorry-free triangle bound norm_whiteheadLinkKashaev_le. That is the whole content of the conditional: the class supplies one inequality, the triangle bound the other, and the shared lemma does the analysis.

            Note the direction. The shared lemma is stated knowing the small side's rate and concluding about the large one; here it is the large side (majorant) whose rate is known. Dividing the known side by N^p is what puts it in the lemma's m slot — and it costs nothing precisely because log(N^p)/N → 0, which is the same sub-exponentiality the lemma's c slot wants. Getting this backwards (trying m = ‖J_N‖) asks for the rate that is open.
