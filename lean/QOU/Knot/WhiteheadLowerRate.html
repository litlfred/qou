    class QOU.Knot.FigureEightVolume.WhiteheadLinkLowerRate :

    The one-sided rate the volume conjecture actually needs.

    Strictly weaker than VdVWhiteheadLemma4 (see lowerRate_of_lemma4): it mentions neither the majorant nor a polynomial exponent, only that the scaled log-modulus is not asymptotically below the volume.
