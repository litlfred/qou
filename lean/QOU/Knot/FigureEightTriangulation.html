          The gluing equations #

          SnapPy's two 4₁ edge equations are [2,1,0, 2,1,0] and [0,1,2, 0,1,2]. With both shapes w and parameters (w, 1/(1−w), 1 − 1/w), they read w⁴/(1−w)² = 1 and ((1−w)/w²)² = 1, and both reduce to w² − w + 1 = 0 — the shape's minimal polynomial, exactly as 5₂'s reduce to its cubic.
