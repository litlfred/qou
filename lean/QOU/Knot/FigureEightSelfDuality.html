        theorem QOU.Knot.SelfDuality.higgs_complex_volume_real {R : Type u_1} [CommRing R] (σ : R →+* R) (V : R) (hSelfDual : σ V = V) :
        barOdd σ V = 0 ∧ barEven σ V = 2 * V

        Keystone corollary. For the self-dual Higgs knot, the complex volume is purely real: its bar-odd (CS / Q_β / Im) face is 0 and its bar-even (Vol / mass / Re) face is 2V. Hence Q_β cannot be the figure-eight's own imaginary face — it must live in the many-body binding defect (see the CAL-123 reanalysis audit).
