      Winding lower bound (squared-modulus form). |u|² ≥ (2πk)²: the imaginary winding part alone forces it, independent of the real offset a = N·t. Since normSq = |u|² this is exactly |u| ≥ 2πk.

      The datum is never in the small-|u| disk. For every winding k ≥ 1, |u|² ≥ (2π)² — the substrate winding datum sits OUTSIDE any disk |u| < 2π, in particular outside Murakami's controlled small-|u| neighborhood of the geometric structure. Algebraic core of the branch-B3 scope-(a) obstruction to an anchor-free q₀.
