    abbrev QOU.Knot.L6a4AngleStructure.Pt :
    Type

    The 24 tetrahedron angles of the L6a4 (Borromean rings) triangulation, in pi-units: 8 ideal tetrahedra, 3 angles each. Indexed by Nat rather than Fin 24 so the permutation generators reduce definitionally at literal indices; coordinates past 23 are simply unconstrained and never referenced.

                theorem QOU.Knot.L6a4AngleStructure.unique_symmetric_point {x : Pt} (h : GA x) (hf : Fixed x) :
                x 0 = 1 / 4 ∧ x 2 = 1 / 2

                The unique symmetric point (bean qou-krvm hypothesis (ii), TRUE here). A feasible point fixed by the symmetry group is exactly the regular ideal octahedron: 1/4 on the size-16 orbit, 1/2 on the size-8 orbit.

                Contrast M004AngleStructure.fixed_iff_line, where the same hypothesis is FALSE and the fixed locus is one-dimensional.

                Non-vacuity: the point exists #

                unique_symmetric_point says AT MOST one symmetric feasible point. On its own that would also hold if there were NONE — the theorem would be vacuously true. These two witnesses close that hole by exhibiting the point.
