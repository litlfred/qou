    theorem QOU.Knot.FlatTet.Shape.param_pow_prod (S : Shape) (a b c : ℕ) :
    S.param 0 ^ a * S.param 1 ^ b * S.param 2 ^ c = S.z ^ a * (S.z - 1) ^ c / ((1 - S.z) ^ b * S.z ^ c)

    One tetrahedron's contribution, as a single quotient.

    z^a · (1/(1−z))^b · (1 − 1/z)^c = z^a (z−1)^c / ((1−z)^b z^c).

    This is the whole bridge: it turns the three edge parameters — two of which are quotients — into one numerator over one denominator, in a form that composes across tetrahedra.

    The general n-tetrahedron composition #

    satisfies_of_cleared_two written for arbitrary n, which is what the 19 manifolds in GeneratedGluing need. Finset.prod_div_distrib does the work: each tetrahedron becomes one quotient by param_pow_prod, the quotients compose into a single quotient of products, and the cleared hypothesis says numerator equals denominator.
