    def QOU.Knot.BlockGeneratingFunction.ofFin {m A : ℕ} (f : Fin A → Equiv.Perm (Fin m)) :
    ℕ → Equiv.Perm (Fin m)

    Extend an A-indexed family of blocks to all of ℕ by the identity, so it can be fed to blockProd. Blocks past A contribute nothing.

      The block factorisation. Grading by the Coxeter length of the assembled A-nucleon word, the generating function is the product of the per-nucleon generating functions.

      The left-hand degree is ℓ of a single permutation in S_m; the right-hand side is a product over A independent blocks. They agree because length is additive across an order-separated split, which is the parabolic length formula of BlockLengthAdditive.
