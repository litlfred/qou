    §1 — A tighter lower bracket on the real root #

    realRoot_gt : 2/5 < realRoot is not enough: the quadratic below is negative there. 3/7 is, with room on both sides — g(3/7) = −0.0029 makes it provable and −3(3/7)² + 6(3/7) − 2 = +0.0204 makes it work.

      §7 — The angle bound #

      arg (z⁹) = 9 · arg z in Real.Angle, i.e. modulo 2π. The window of §6 puts 9 · arg z in (3π/2, 9π/4), strictly inside (π, 3π), so the winding number is forced to be exactly one and arg (z⁹) = 9·arg z − 2π. Positivity of the left side is then arg z > 2π/9.

      2π/9 < arg z — the smallest dihedral angle of the 5₂ tetrahedron exceeds 2π/9.

      §9 — The other two angles, and hence the minimum #

      The chord bound wants the SMALLEST angle to exceed 2π/9. §8 gives that for angle₃; the other two clear it with room, and by a cheaper route — both exceed π/4, and π/4 > 2π/9 because 1/4 > 2/9. So no ordering of the three needs proving.
