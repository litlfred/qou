    QOU.Knot.HomflySkeinReference — the 2-strand skein reference, and a mirror #

    Bean qou-7ue5. Two places in computations/ disagree about the HOMFLY of the same braid closure, and this file settles what kind of disagreement it is.

    • homflypt_reference_qa._torus_homfly derives closure(σ₁ᵏ) from the skein relation by hand and gives P(σ₁³) = 2v² − v⁴ + v²z² for the right trefoil.
    • homflypt_engine.homflypt_2strand is a hardcoded table returning −v⁻⁴ + 2v⁻² + v⁻²z², under the label "Right-hand trefoil".

    What is proved here #

    skein is the reference recurrence. skein_two and skein_three give its closed forms, and engine_k2_is_mirror/engine_k3_is_mirror show that the engine's two table entries are exactly the reference evaluated at the HOMFLY mirror substitution (v, z) ↦ (v⁻¹, −z).

    So the engine is not wrong about the polynomial — it computes the left trefoil and the negative Hopf link, while labelling them "Right-hand" and "(positive)". The discrepancy is a crossing-sign convention, nothing deeper.

    The z-sign is the part that is easy to get wrong, and this file exists partly because I did. At k = 3 the two agree under plain v ↦ v⁻¹, because z occurs there only as z²; that coincidence does not survive to k = 2, where the reference's +vz must become −v⁻¹z. plain_inversion_ne_mirror exhibits a concrete rational point at which the naive substitution and the true mirror differ, so the accident cannot be mistaken for a rule.

    What is NOT proved #

    Everything geometric. That switching a crossing of σ₁ᵏ yields σ₁ᵏ⁻² and smoothing it yields σ₁ᵏ⁻¹; that closure(σ₁⁰) is the 2-component unlink and closure(σ₁¹) the unknot; and that HOMFLY exists and is an ambient-isotopy invariant at all — all of these are built into the definition of skein rather than derived. This file proves the algebra downstream of the skein relation, which is precisely the layer the two computations/ files disagree on. It says nothing about which convention the corpus should adopt; that remains an owner call on qou-7ue5.

    Base ring is a generic field per §7c — the content is algebraic and no archimedean construct appears, so nothing here mentions ℝ. The concrete counterexample is over ℚ.

    noncomputable def QOU.Knot.HomflySkeinReference.skein {R : Type u_1} [Field R] (v z : R) :
    ℕ → R

    The 2-strand skein recurrence for P(closure(σ₁ⁿ)), in the Lickorish convention v⁻¹P(L₊) − vP(L₋) = zP(L₀). Resolving one positive crossing gives P(L₊) = v²P(L₋) + vzP(L₀); on σ₁ⁿ the switched diagram is σ₁ⁿ⁻² and the smoothed one is σ₁ⁿ⁻¹. Seeds are the 2-component unlink and the unknot.

      theorem QOU.Knot.HomflySkeinReference.plain_inversion_ne_mirror :
      skein 2⁻¹ 1 2 ≠ skein 2⁻¹ (-1) 2

      The z-sign is not optional. Inverting v alone is not the mirror: at v = 2, z = 1 the naive substitution and the true mirror disagree already at k = 2. The agreement one sees at k = 3 is an artefact of z occurring there only as z², and must not be read as a rule.
