    Total volume of a finite family of ideal tetrahedra indexed by ι, with angle coordinates x i ∈ ℝ × ℝ (third angle π − (x i).1 − (x i).2 implicit): totalVol x = Σ_i tetVol (x i).

      StrictConcaveOn ℝ K totalVol

      The total volume is strictly concave on any convex, block-in-simplex coupled polytope.

      For a convex feasible set K ⊆ ι → ℝ × ℝ on which every tetrahedron's angle block lands in tetSimplex, totalVol is StrictConcaveOn ℝ K. This is the key new lemma of bean qou-7425: a block-separated sum of strictly concave functions is strictly concave. Two distinct feasible points differ in some block i₀, where single-tet strict concavity gives a strict inequality; every other block contributes ≤; Finset.sum_lt_sum makes the total strict.

      Generic in the number of tetrahedra — no assumption on ι beyond finiteness, and no assumption on K beyond convexity and block-membership, so the coupling among tetrahedra (the edge equations) is carried entirely by K.

      Concrete instantiation: the m004 (figure-eight) coupled polytope #

      Two ideal tetrahedra, indexed by Fin 2. The m004 gluing data (M004AngleStructure.GA, SnapPy, π-units) has two edge equations on the six angles (α₁,β₁,γ₁,α₂,β₂,γ₂). Eliminating γⱼ = π − αⱼ − βⱼ (the per-tet sum), edge₁ : 2α₁+β₁+α₂+2γ₂ = 2 becomes 2α₁+β₁−α₂−2β₂ = 0, and edge₂ becomes its negation — a single independent constraint, matching the rank-3 feasible set. The constraint is homogeneous, hence identical in π-units and radians, so it reads off directly on the radian coordinates x : Fin 2 → ℝ × ℝ.
