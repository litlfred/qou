      theorem QOU.Knot.KummerRadial.kummer {z : ℂ} (hz : 0 < z.im) :
      Archimedean.BlochWigner_lob z = (MathlibExt.cLiTwoExt z).im + Real.log ‖z‖ * (1 - z).arg

      KUMMER'S IDENTITY, general z.

      For every z with Im z > 0,

      `BlochWigner_lob z = Im cLiTwoExt z + log‖z‖ · arg(1 − z)`

      — the corpus's angle-built Bloch–Wigner function is the dilogarithm one.

      Proof: along the ray through z, the defect has vanishing derivative (hasDerivAt_defect_zero, which is the law of sines in disguise), so it is constant; at r = 1 it vanishes by KummerDilogHalf.kummer_unit_circle.
