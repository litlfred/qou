    source
    theorem QOU.Knot.IdealTet.norm_eq_sin_div_sin {z : ℂ} (hz : 0 < z.im) :
    ‖z‖ = Real.sin (Archimedean.tetAngle₂ z) / Real.sin (Archimedean.tetAngle₃ z)

    THE LAW OF SINES FOR AN IDEAL TETRAHEDRON.

    For Im z > 0,

    `‖z‖ = sin θ₂ / sin θ₃`

    with θ₂ = arg(1/(1−z)), θ₃ = arg(1 − 1/z).

    This is what makes the radial reduction of Kummer's identity work: along a ray of constant arg z, it is exactly the condition under which the Λ-side and the dilogarithm side have the same derivative.

    theorem QOU.Knot.IdealTet.log_sin_sub_log_sin {z : ℂ} (hz : 0 < z.im) :
    Real.log (2 * Real.sin (Archimedean.tetAngle₃ z)) - Real.log (2 * Real.sin (Archimedean.tetAngle₂ z)) = -Real.log ‖z‖

    The log form, which is the shape the Λ-derivative produces: log(2 sin θ₃) − log(2 sin θ₂) = −log ‖z‖.

    This is precisely the identity that makes the two sides of Kummer agree along a ray, so it is worth having stated separately.
