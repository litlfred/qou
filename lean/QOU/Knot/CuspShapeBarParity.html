    Cusp-shape bar-parity: the figure-eight modulus split 𝔙 = Vol + i·CS #

    The cusp shape of the figure-eight knot 4₁ is ω = e^{iπ/3}, a primitive sixth root of unity — a root of the cyclotomic polynomial Φ₆(z) = z² − z + 1, with trace field ℚ(√−3). Splitting ω along the bar involution x ↦ x⁻¹ (QOU.QBeta.BarParityZeta4Swap) recovers the complex-volume grading 𝔙 = Vol + i·CS:

    • bar-even (cosh-like, real / Vol side): barEven ω = ω + ω⁻¹ = 1 — the rational part, 2cos(π/3) = 1;
    • bar-odd (sinh-like, imaginary / CS side): barOdd ω = ω − ω⁻¹ = 2ω − 1 with barOdd ω ^ 2 = −3 — the √−3 cusp modulus, (2i·sin(π/3))² = −3.

    So the exceptional divisor / cusp shape is reached along the bar-odd direction (the Chern–Simons / Im 𝔙 axis), while its bar-even shadow is the rational Volume normalisation. This is the algebraic core of "the meridian is at the exceptional divisor": the meridian is the bar-odd (boost) cycle.

    Boost / rapidity structure — "one meridian turn = one knot operation" #

    The meridian holonomy is multiplication by the substrate q (one turn = one R-matrix application = one crossing). Under the bar split:

    • q sits on the boost hyperbola barEven q ² − barOdd q ² = 4 (the cosh² − sinh² = 1 identity, ×4) — one turn is a hyperbolic rotation, not a compact spin;
    • composing turns multiplies the holonomies q ↦ q·q', and this adds rapidity: the bar-even/bar-odd shapes obey the cosh/sinh addition laws 2·barEven(qq') = barEven q · barEven q' + barOdd q · barOdd q' (and the odd companion). Additive rapidity ⇔ multiplicative holonomy.

    At a real substrate q₀ > 1 the turn is a genuine boost of rapidity ln q₀ (≈ 0.104); only at a root of unity (|q| = 1) is it a compact U(1) spin. That real-vs-compact split is the fibre-vs-observable Im q wall — the bar-odd sinh axis carries the boost, exactly the axis along which the cusp shape sits (barOdd ω ^ 2 = −3). The archimedean rapidity ln q₀ lives on the ℝ specialisation side; everything here is generic-field (§7c), no c_k wired.

    Core cusp-shape lemmas — canonical home is QOU.QBeta.CuspShapeBarParity #

    The four core figure-eight cusp lemmas (inv, barEven = 1, barOdd ² = −3, barOdd = 2ω − 1) are proved canonically over the IsCuspShape predicate in QOU.QBeta.CuspShapeBarParity (cuspShape_inv / cuspShape_barEven / cuspShape_barOdd_sq / cuspShape_barOdd), which additionally proves cuspShape_cube / _sixth / _reconstruct and the golden shape. This module previously duplicated those four proofs; they are removed and the one downstream use below (cusp_barParity_split) delegates to the canonical lemmas. The generic bar-involution multiplicativity lemmas barEven_mul / barOdd_mul have been moved to their natural home, the base module QOU.QBeta.BarParityZeta4Swap (where barEven / barOdd are defined); what remains genuinely new here is the meridian boost holonomy_hyperbola.

    QBeta.BarParityZeta4Swap.barEven q ^ 2 - QBeta.BarParityZeta4Swap.barOdd q ^ 2 = 4

    Boost hyperbola. The meridian holonomy q (any nonzero field element) satisfies barEven q ² − barOdd q ² = 4 — the cosh² − sinh² = 1 identity. One meridian turn is a hyperbolic rotation.

    §7c note: the difference is 4·q·q⁻¹, so the proof is a single explicit unit witness (q · q⁻¹ = 1) rather than a field_simp denominator clear. The [Field R] on the section variable is not removable here — it is inherited from barEven / barOdd, which are defined with the ring inverse x⁻¹ in QOU.QBeta.BarParityZeta4Swap.
