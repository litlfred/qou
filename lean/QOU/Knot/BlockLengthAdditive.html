        JetGradePromotion.length (u * v) = JetGradePromotion.length u + JetGradePromotion.length v

        Coxeter length is additive across an order-separated block split.

        This is the content half of the block factorisation: it is what makes the index set of the filtration a product, and hence what lets BodyOrderExpansion.gf_pow apply to the atomic braid rather than to an abstract product.

        A blocks, not two #

        length_mul is the two-block statement and carries the content. The atomic braid has A nucleon blocks, so the composition law needs the A-fold form. I previously declined this as "index plumbing that adds no insight" — the insight claim was right, the declining was not: two is not A, and gf_pow's A-fold factorisation has nothing to stand on without it.

          theorem QOU.Knot.BlockLengthAdditive.length_blockProd {m : ℕ} {b : ℕ → ℕ} (hb : Monotone b) {w : ℕ → Equiv.Perm (Fin m)} (hlow : ∀ (i : ℕ), LowerOnly (b (i + 1)) (w i)) (hup : ∀ (i : ℕ), UpperOnly (b i) (w i)) (A : ℕ) :
          JetGradePromotion.length (blockProd w A) = ∑ i ∈ Finset.range A, JetGradePromotion.length (w i)

          Length is additive across A order-separated blocks. Block i occupies [b i, b (i+1)); the Coxeter length of the whole is the sum of the blocks'.

          This is what BodyOrderExpansion.gf_pow needs in order to apply to the atomic braid: it makes the filtration's index set an honest A-fold product, not a two-fold one.

          The hypotheses are exactly "each block acts inside its own block" #

          length_blockProd takes hlow and hup separately, which leaves open whether anything satisfies both at once. It does, and the condition is the natural one: a permutation supported in the half-open interval [a, b) is lower-only at b and upper-only at a simultaneously.

          Stating this matters for the same reason the Hecke-relation instantiation did. A theorem whose hypotheses no interesting family satisfies proves nothing about the object it names, and the two-sided form is what a nucleon block actually is: block i moves strands 3i, 3i+1, 3i+2 and nothing else.
