    theorem QOU.Knot.FigureEightVolume.sum_log_two_sin_reflect {N k : ℕ} (hN : N ≠ 0) (hk : k < N) :
    ∑ j ∈ Finset.Ico 1 (k + 1), Real.log (2 * Real.sin (↑j * Real.pi / ↑N)) + ∑ j ∈ Finset.Ico 1 (N - k), Real.log (2 * Real.sin (↑j * Real.pi / ↑N)) = Real.log ↑N

    The exact reflection s_k + s_{N−1−k} = −log N, for every k < N.

    Written on L_k := ∑_{j=1}^{k} log(2 sin(jπ/N)) = −s_k, so the statement below is L_k + L_{N−1−k} = log N. Splitting Ico 1 N at k+1 and reflecting the upper block by j ↦ N − j turns it into the lower block for N−1−k; the endpoint value log N is sum_log_two_sin_endpoint.

    WHY THIS IS THE STRUCTURAL HALF OF qou-mlgp (A). Unlike the endpoint, this is exact for every k as well as every N, so it constrains the whole expansion rather than one value. Substituting the conjectured form s_k = (N/π)Λ(θ) − ½log(2N sin θ) + ε_k with θ = kπ/N, h = π/N, and using Λ(π − x) = −Λ(x), everything cancels and the reflection collapses to

    ε_k + ε_{N−1−k} = ½[f(θ) + f(θ+h)] − (1/h)·∫_θ^{θ+h} f,     f = log(2 sin),

    which is the TRAPEZOID remainder on a single cell — an identity, not an estimate. Hence the SYMMETRIC half of (A)'s error is a trapezoid remainder, O(h² csc²θ) in the interior, already sharper than the O(1/N) being claimed, and the entire difficulty of (A) sits in the ANTISYMMETRIC half.

    Measured (computations/probes/kashaev_reflection_probe.py, 40 digits): the identity holds exactly, the symmetric part is O(1/N²), and the antisymmetric 1/N term is the Euler–Maclaurin B₂ term −(π/12N)·cot θ — vanishing identically at θ = π/2, as the antisymmetry forces.
