    QOU.volume_from_rigidity — rank(V) = 2 from symplectic self-duality #

    Proposition (prop:volume-from-rigidity).

    Let (𝐂, ⊗, 𝟙) be an R-linear rigid monoidal category, and let V be a simple object that is symplectically self-dual: there exists an isomorphism φ : V ≅ V^∨ with φ^∨ = −φ (alternating). The alternating condition forces rank(V) = 2.

    The Lean encoding records the rank claim against an abstract SymplecticSelfDualContext carrying the categorical data (V, V^∨, φ, φ^∨) and the rank function.

    • neg : Vdual → Vdual

      Negation on Vdual (an R-linear structure).

    • alternating (v : V) : φDual v = neg (φ v)

      The alternating condition φ^∨ = −φ.

    • rank : Type → ℕ

    • simple : Prop

      Hypothesis: V is simple (irreducible).

    • claim_rank_eq_two : simple → rank V = 2

      Manuscript rank-2 claim: under simplicity, the alternating φ^∨ = −φ forces rank(V) = 2.
