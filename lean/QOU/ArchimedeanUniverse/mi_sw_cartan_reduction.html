    QOU.ArchimedeanUniverse.mi_sw_cartan_reduction #

    Content block lem:mi-sw-abelianisation-cartan: Cartan reduction recovers the Seiberg--Witten differential.

    Lean structure (§3b-cond: hypotheses in the class, conclusion derived) #

    The narrative lemma has three clauses on the exceptional divisor 𝒮_c ≅ ℙ¹(ℂ):

    1. the SU(2)-bundle trivialises, so ∇_A reduces to an 𝔰𝔲(2)-valued 1-form A|_{𝒮_c};
    2. under the weight-space decomposition A|_{𝒮_c} = A⁰ ⊗ H + A⁺ ⊗ E₊ + A⁻ ⊗ E₋, and the off-diagonal weight components vanish (abelianisation);
    3. the Cartan component is the Seiberg–Witten differential: A⁰ = X·dz = λ_SW.

    WeightDecomposedForm models clause 2's decomposition (the three weight components of the reduced form); cartanEmbed is the abelian embedding ω ↦ ω ⊗ H. The class MiSwCartanReductionHypotheses carries the genuine geometric inputs — the vanishing of the off-diagonal components on the exceptional divisor, the local Higgs-eigenvalue computation A⁰ = X • dz, and the definitional λ_SW = X • dz of the Seiberg–Witten differential on the spectral curve. The theorem mi_sw_cartan_reduction then derives (not projects) the conclusion: the reduced connection is the Cartan embedding of X • dz (via the proved helper eq_cartanEmbed_of_offdiag_zero), and its Cartan component is λ_SW (by composing the two local identifications). No class field is conclusion-typed; no instance is fabricated — the open geometric content is precisely the existence of an instance for the actual Bring-surface data.

    Stated over a generic module (R, Ω) per AGENTS.md §7c: the identification is purely algebraic (equalities in a module of scalar 1-forms); no archimedean construct is required.

        §3b-cond hypothesis class for [lem:mi-sw-abelianisation-cartan]: the geometric inputs of the Cartan-reduction lemma on the exceptional divisor 𝒮_c ≅ ℙ¹(ℂ).

        Fields are genuine hypotheses (never the conclusion):

        • abelianised_plus / abelianised_minus — the off-diagonal weight components of the reduced connection vanish on 𝒮_c (weight-space abelianisation; narrative clause 2);
        • cartan_local_form — the local Higgs-eigenvalue computation: in the coordinate chart the Cartan component is X • dz, where X is the color coordinate cut out on the single spectral-curve sheet over 𝒮_c (open geometric input; not in Mathlib);
        • sw_eq_local_form — the Seiberg–Witten differential is the tautological form X • dz on the spectral curve (its defining property, recorded as a hypothesis on the opaque lambdaSW).

        The conclusion A⁰ = X·dz = λ_SW plus the structural trivialisation statement is derived in mi_sw_cartan_reduction below. No instance is declared — fabricating one would be the §3b-tax violation; the open content is the existence of an instance for the Bring-surface geometry.

        • X : R

          The color coordinate X (Higgs-field eigenvalue on the single sheet of the spectral curve over 𝒮_c).

          Lemma (conditional on the geometric inputs above) ([lem:mi-sw-abelianisation-cartan]): Cartan reduction recovers the Seiberg–Witten differential.

          Under the weight-space abelianisation and the two local identifications carried by MiSwCartanReductionHypotheses, the reduced connection is the Cartan embedding of X • dz (trivialisation + abelianisation, clauses 1–2), and its Cartan component is the Seiberg–Witten differential (clause 3, A⁰ = X·dz = λ_SW).

          Derived, not projected: the first conjunct composes the proved helper eq_cartanEmbed_of_offdiag_zero with the local form; the second composes the two local identifications by transitivity. Neither conjunct is a class field (§3b-cond item 5).
