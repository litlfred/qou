    S₅-Hodge depth of Bring's curve — representation-theory core #

    Formalizes the character-theoretic assertions computed by computations/substrate/s5_bring_hodge_depth.py (keystone / strand-b1).

    Cited geometric input (NOT proved here). Riera–Rodríguez 1992, The period matrix of Bring's curve: the space of holomorphic differentials H⁰(Ω) of Bring's genus-4 curve is the irreducible 4-dimensional S₅ standard representation W (the partition (4,1), or equivalently its sign-twist (2,1,1,1)). The numerical period matrix τ itself lives in that reference; it is not reconstructed here.

    What this file proves (unconditional finite ℚ arithmetic). The S₅ class-function inner products over the 7 conjugacy classes:

    • W is irreducible: ⟨χ_(4,1), χ_(4,1)⟩ = 1;
    • the sign-twist (2,1,1,1) is also irreducible and distinct from (4,1): ⟨χ_(2,1,1,1), χ_(2,1,1,1)⟩ = 1, ⟨χ_(4,1), χ_(2,1,1,1)⟩ = 0;
    • W occurs in H¹ = H^{1,0} ⊕ H^{0,1} = W ⊕ conj W = 2·W with multiplicity 2: ⟨χ_{H¹}, χ_(4,1)⟩ = 2.

    Consequence (interpretation, modulo the cited input). Because the Gauss–Manin operator M on the prolonged periods is S₅-equivariant, Schur's lemma forces M = id_W ⊗ B with B a 2×2 matrix on the multiplicity space, so the period recurrence has depth ≤ 2.

    Explicitly NOT proved. Depth = 2 (not merely ≤ 2) needs Griffiths transversality (∇F^p ⊂ F^{p-1} ⊗ Ω ⇒ B non-scalar) — a further geometric input, stated in the workplan, not formalized. And the identification H⁰(Ω) ≅ W is the Riera–Rodríguez input above, not a theorem of this file.
