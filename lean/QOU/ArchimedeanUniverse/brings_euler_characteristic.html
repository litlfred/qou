    QOU.ArchimedeanUniverse.brings_euler_characteristic #

    Content block lem:brings-euler-characteristic: The categorical Euler characteristic χ(A) = tr(id_A) for A = 𝒪_q(SU(2)) on Bring's surface Σ₄ equals χ(Σ₄) = 2 − 2g(Σ₄) = −6.

    Lean structure #

    The genuine geometric input is the genus of Bring's surface, g(Σ₄) = 4 (a classical fact about the Bring curve / the S₅-quotient Σ₄). The integer Euler characteristic χ(A) is defined by the Euler formula χ = 2 − 2g, and the value χ(A) = −6 is derived by norm_num from g = 4 (brings_euler_characteristic).

    Trace of the identity via the q-family — χ(A) = tr(id_A) #

    The .md's central claim is that this Euler characteristic is the categorical trace of the identity, χ(A) = tr(id_A), "recovered as the quantum dimension specialised at the Descartes substrate parameter q₀". We compute that trace as an algebraic super-trace, rather than assuming the value.

    Scope of what is formalised. This file does not construct the category Rep(SU_q(2)) or its objects; it models the single q-datum the recovery needs — the quantum dimension of the monoidal unit, dim_q 𝟙 = tr_q(id_𝟙) = 1 (qdimUnit, the constant function 1) — and computes the super-trace from it and the (integer) Betti numbers. The categorical reading below (cohomology H^i as trivial Rep(SU_q(2))-modules) is the motivation for qdimUnit = 1, not something proved in Lean.

    tr(id_A) is the super-trace (categorical Euler characteristic) of the identity over the cohomology of the surface,

      tr(id_A)(q)  =  Σ_{i=0}^{2} (−1)^i · dim_q H^i(Σ_g)
               =  b₀·dim_q𝟙 − b₁·dim_q𝟙 + b₂·dim_q𝟙,

    with the Betti numbers (b₀, b₁, b₂) = (1, 2g, 1) of a closed orientable genus-g surface. The only q-datum this needs is the quantum dimension of the monoidal unit, dim_q 𝟙 = tr_q(id_𝟙) = 1, which is the constant function 1 of the q-family (the cohomology H^i(Σ₄) sit in Rep(SU_q(2)) as trivial, quantum-dimension-1 modules — Σ₄ enters the state bundle as a classical base). Hence

      tr(id_A)(q)  =  b₀ − b₁ + b₂  =  1 − 2g + 1  =  2 − 2g   for every q,

    so tr(id_A) = −6 at g = 4 and is q-independent (categorical_trace_q_independent) — the substrate evaluation at q₀ is not a fit; the q-dependence cancels because the relevant modules are quantum-trivial and the alternating sum returns the classical Euler characteristic.

    Honesty on the object. The naive Peter–Weyl quantum dimension of the full coordinate Hopf algebra, dim_q 𝒪_q(SU(2)) = Σ_n [n+1]_q², diverges; the finite value −6 is the categorical Euler characteristic / super-dimension, not that divergent sum. The structural inputs are exactly the classical topology of Σ₄ (the Betti numbers (1, 2g, 1)) and the quantum-triviality of its cohomology; there is no upstream Lean theorem asserting tr(id_A) = 2 − 2g — QOU.Torsion.euler_char_constant (prop:euler-char-stability) proves only stability of the Euler characteristic across brane-tower levels, not its value, so it is not cited here as establishing the value.

    Base ring (§7c) #

    Everything here is the algebraic super-trace computation — a commutative-ring identity b₀ − b₁ + b₂ = 2 − 2g = −6 with no ordering, Real.*, or CODATA literal — so it is stated over a generic {R : Type*} [CommRing R], not ℝ. The archimedean evaluation (the substrate value q₀ ≈ 1.1097 ∈ ℝ) is the downstream R := ℝ specialisation and is not performed here; indeed the value is q-independent, so no archimedean input is used at all.

    • genus_is_four : g R = 4
