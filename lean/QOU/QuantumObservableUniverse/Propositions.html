        theorem QOU.main_commutative_diagram (cqou : CQuantumObservableUniverse) (sp : ArchimedeanSubstrateField cqou.M) (inst : InstantonConnection cqou.toCRManifoldData) (diag : MainCommutativeDiagram cqou sp inst) :
        diag.substrate_coupling ∘ diag.euler_lagrange = diag.parallel_transport ∘ diag.jet_eval

        The main diagram commutes — as a diagram, not merely pointwise.

        κ ∘ E[S_q] = ∇^A ∘ j, which is what the commutes field's own docstring claims. The field itself states the pointwise version, ∀ x, κ (E[S_q] x) = ∇^A (j x); the two are different propositions and the composition form is the one the square asserts.

        Derived from the field by funext. This declaration previously read := diag.commutes — the pointwise field returned verbatim, under a docstring promising the composition. Nothing else in MainCommutativeDiagram constrains the four maps, so the pointwise form cannot be derived at all: it is the axiom.

        What this does NOT show: that any concrete quantum observable universe satisfies commutes. That is an assumption of the data bundle and stays one until the four maps are given definitions rather than carried as opaque functions.
