    QOU.QuantumObservableUniverse.SelfDualityDegree — self-duality of 2-forms #

    exists in exactly one dimension, and the corpus asserts it in three

    Bean qou-xlw1, epic qou-8lxd. Companion to QOU.Eightfold.ContactDistributionRank.

    The one fact everything below turns on #

    The Hodge star on an oriented m-dimensional space maps Λ^k → Λ^{m−k}. It is an endomorphism of Λ^k — the only way it can have ±1 eigenspaces, hence the only way a self-dual/anti-self-dual splitting can exist — precisely when m − k = k, i.e. m = 2k.

    For k = 2 that is m = 4, and no other dimension:

    rank_ℝ H = 2n n dim M = 2n+1 dim Λ²H* ⋆ : Λ² → splitting?
    2 1 3 1 Λ⁰ no
    4 2 5 6 Λ² yes
    6 3 7 15 Λ⁴ no
    8 4 9 28 Λ⁶ no

    Why this matters here: the corpus asserts the splitting three times, at three #

    incompatible dimensions

    (1) Categorically. self-dual-ses.md opens "The q-Hodge star ⋆_q is an involution on Ω²(A)", and defines Ω^±(A) = ker(id ∓ ⋆_q). But categorical-hodge-star.md defines ⋆ : Ω^k(A) → Ω^{n−k}(A), so ⋆_q is an endomorphism of Ω² only at calculus dimension n = 4 — while brings-is-qou-verification.md:3 and brings-is-qou-proof.md:10 declare the calculus to be the Woronowicz left-covariant 3-dimensional one, n = 3, where ⋆ : Ω² → Ω¹. No manifold is involved in this one; it is a typing mismatch inside the categorical layer.

    (2) Archimedeanly, in general. self-dual-2group.md, emergence-to-quantum-flat-proof.md, glossary-quantum-flatness.md and self-dual-ses.md all write the simplicity constraint as B ∈ Γ(Λ^+H^* ⊗ Ad(P)) — the splitting is of Λ²H^*, the exterior square of the contact distribution, not of the manifold's 2-forms. By the table that forces rank_ℝ H = 4, hence n = 2, hence dim M = 5. This is what archimedean-realisations.md:3's "the rank-4 distribution H = ker Θ" is recording, and it is derived, not asserted — it is the hypothesis under which Λ^±H^* is well-formed at all.

    (3) In the worked example. brings-qou-4tuple.md:12 builds M_S as the unit circle bundle in T*S over Bring's surface, a Riemann surface of real dimension 2 — so M_S is 3-dimensional, rank_ℝ H = 2, and Λ²H^* is one-dimensional. There is no splitting to have. Yet nineteen lines later the same block defines the substrate parameter as q(p) = 1 + |⟨L_Θ, F_A^+⟩|(p).

    So the framework's single substrate modulus is defined, in its own defining block, by a quantity that does not exist at the dimension that block assigns.

    The three-way collision, stated plainly #

    Three requirements, three dimensions, pairwise exclusive (self_duality_and_eightfold_exclusive, bring_dim_admits_no_splitting):

    • self-duality (instanton, quantum flatness, F_A^+, q(p)) → dim M = 5;
    • the eightfold way (Λ³_ℂ ξ ≅ 𝟙, ContactDistributionRank) → dim M = 7;
    • Bring's worked example (T*S circle bundle) → dim M = 3.

    No single contact manifold satisfies two of these. In particular fixing the rank-3-vs-rank-4 disagreement in favour of either side does not open the eightfold route — 5 and 7 are as incompatible as 3 and 5.

    What is proved here, and what is not #

    Proved (unconditional integer arithmetic): the endomorphism criterion and every row of the table; that the splitting exists at rank_ℝ H = 4 and nowhere else; that Bring's dimension admits none; that the calculus dimension must be 4 for ⋆_q on Ω²; and the pairwise exclusions.

    Not proved, and asserted nowhere below:

    • that Λ^±H^* should be what the corpus means. This file takes the corpus's own notation at face value and reports what it requires. If the intended object were the 2-forms of the ambient T*S (a real 4-manifold, where the splitting does exist) rather than Λ²H^*, the general-theory blocks would need rewriting but the arithmetic below would be untouched.
    • which resolution the author wants. Both readings cost real content and the choice is not a measurement.
    • anything about Woronowicz's classification. That 𝒪_q(SU(2)) also admits 4-dimensional bicovariant calculi is standard and cited, not formalised — library/ holds no source on it, and the corpus declares only the 3D left-covariant one.

    Base ring: none — integer arithmetic (§7c: no ℝ, no ring at all).
