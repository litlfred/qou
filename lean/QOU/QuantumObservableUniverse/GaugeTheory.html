    §8 Gauge Theory Structures #

    Projective line object, fibre–adjoint involution, categorical connection, module action, categorical action functional, variational equations, and observations.

    Rank of the Einstein slot — retyped 2026-09-15 (owner-authorised, qou-h0ou) #

    CategoricalVariationalData.einsteinTensor and .stressTensor, and the CategoricalAction.gravity term they are summed against, were typed (𝟙_ C) ⟶ (𝟙_ C) — the scalars End(𝟙_C). Einstein's equation is a rank-2 identity, and a scalar cannot state one; the three were tensors in name only.

    They are now PairingSpace C fh.obj fh.obj = fh.obj ⊗ fh.obj ⟶ 𝟙_ C, the field space of QOU.MathlibExt.MonoidalCalcVar. The co-pairing needed to contract back to a scalar is not invented for this change — it is fh.coevaluation, already a field of FrobeniusHopfData, and the field space already has a canonical inhabitant, the Frobenius pairing fh.mul ≫ fh.frobeniusForm (frobeniusPairing below). A being self-dual is what makes fh.obj ⊗ fh.obj the right pair of slots.

    gauge, gaugeCurrent and matter are unchanged scalars: only the terms entering gravity = einsteinTensor + stressTensor had to move, since that equation's three terms must inhabit one additive group. This is a typing consequence, not a physical claim about which currents "are" tensors.

    variational_einstein_contracted derives the old scalar Einstein equation from the new rank-2 one against fh.coevaluation, so nothing previously statable is lost; MonoidalCalcVar.subsingleton_of_contract_reflects is why the converse fails. No metric, curvature, stress-energy realisation or numerical constant is introduced.

              Variational equations of the categorical action #

              The equations of motion for the categorical action are:

              • Einstein: R_λ − ½ tr(R_λ) id = T_q
              • Yang–Mills: ∇ ★_q F + q[F, ★_q F] = J_ψ
              • Transport: D_∇ ψ = 0

              A full derivation requires a categorical calculus of variations (Euler–Lagrange machinery for monoidal categories), which is not yet available in Lean/Mathlib. The morphisms gravity, gauge, matter in CategoricalAction represent the action terms; the equations of motion relate them to the external current densities and tensors. These are stated axiomatically below, pending formalization.

                Variational equations as a typeclass (Pattern 4) #

                The three variational equations of motion (Einstein, Yang–Mills, transport) require a categorical calculus of variations not yet available in Mathlib. Rather than asserting them universally for every (C, fh, ca, vd), we package them as a typeclass HasCategoricalEOM. Callers must supply an instance to use the identities, making the conjectural content visible at every use site.

                The classical (set-theoretic) versions of these equations follow from Wald, Bleecker, and Kobayashi–Nomizu; the typeclass instances will be discharged once the categorical Euler–Lagrange machinery is formalised.

                  The rank-2 field space and the bridge back to the scalars #

                  Added 2026-09-15 with the retype (qou-h0ou). Two things keep the retype honest. The field space is inhabited by something the substrate already carries, so the new slots are not empty by construction; and the scalar equation this file stated before the retype is recoverable from the rank-2 one, so the change is a refactor rather than a replacement.

                  The converse direction fails — MonoidalCalcVar.subsingleton_of_contract_reflects shows that a contraction which reflected equality uniformly in the co-pairing would force the field space to be a subsingleton — so the rank-2 form is strictly stronger. coevaluation_contract_eq_iff gives the exact residue for the particular co-pairing used here.

                    theorem QOU.coevaluation_contract_eq_iff {C : Type u} [CategoryTheory.Category.{v, u} C] [CategoryTheory.MonoidalCategory C] [CategoryTheory.BraidedCategory C] [CategoryTheory.Preadditive C] (fh : FrobeniusHopfData C) (G T : MathlibExt.MonoidalCalcVar.PairingSpace C fh.obj fh.obj) :
                    CategoryTheory.CategoryStruct.comp fh.coevaluation G = CategoryTheory.CategoryStruct.comp fh.coevaluation T ↔ CategoryTheory.CategoryStruct.comp fh.coevaluation (G - T) = 0

                    What the bridge costs, exactly. Two rank-2 fields have the same contraction against fh.coevaluation precisely when their difference is killed by it. So the scalar Einstein equation determines the rank-2 one only modulo that kernel, and FrobeniusHopfData carries no hypothesis making the kernel trivial — there is no epi condition on coevaluation anywhere in the structure.

                    Stated as the residue rather than as a claim about fh, because which of the two holds is a property of the particular category and is not asserted here.
