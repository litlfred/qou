    structure QOU.VolumeFromRigidity (C : Type u) [CategoryTheory.Category.{v, u} C] [CategoryTheory.MonoidalCategory C] :
    Type (max u v)

    Volume form from rigidity and symplectic self-duality.

    Aligned with condition (iii) of QuantumObservableUniverse under the iso reading (bean qou-n1dj, 2026-08-22). The change is dictated by this lemma's own prose, which is where the corpus settles what "non-degenerate" means for this ε:

    producing an epimorphism ε : Λ²V → 𝟙. For ε to be non-degenerate, we must have rank(Λ²V) = 1.

    rank(Λ²V) = 1 is C(N,2) = 1 is N = 2 (exteriorSquareDim_eq_one_iff), so the author's own non-degeneracy condition is the strong reading. Previously projection and volumeForm were bare morphisms, and neither the ↠ of the docstring nor the non-degeneracy of the prose was formalised at all.

    One thing the prose overstates, which this structure deliberately does not repeat: "this alternating condition forces rank(V) = 2". An alternating form exists on every even rank; what forces 2 is the next sentence's rank(Λ²V) = 1. So phi is recorded as the symplectic isomorphism it is, and the rank comes from volumeIso.

    LaTeX: \label{lem:volume-from-rigidity}

    • V : C

      The fundamental object V of categorical dimension 2.

    • V_dual : C

      The dual object V∨.

    • phi : self.V ≅ self.V_dual

      The symplectic isomorphism φ : V ≅ V∨. The prose says ≅; the field used to say ⟶.

    • exteriorSquare : C

      The exterior square Λ²V.

    • projection : CategoryTheory.MonoidalCategoryStruct.tensorObj self.V self.V ⟶ self.exteriorSquare

      The projection π : V ⊗ V ↠ Λ²V.

    • projection_epi : CategoryTheory.Epi self.projection

      The ↠ of the field above, formalised.

    • volumeIso : self.exteriorSquare ≅ CategoryTheory.MonoidalCategoryStruct.tensorUnit C

      The volume form ε : Λ²V ≅ 𝟙 — non-degenerate in the sense this lemma's own prose gives that word, rank(Λ²V) = 1.
