      Prop

      Axiom 3 — vanishing categorical Chern classes (pattern C — named, not asserted).

      Lemma statement (from manuscript): under the proposition's hypothesis $c_{1}(\mathrm{Ad}(P)) = c_{2}(\mathrm{Ad}(P)) = 0$, the categorical Chern classes $c_{i}(\mathrm{Ad}(A)) := c_{i}(\tau(\mathrm{Ad}(A)))$ vanish, verifying axiom~(3) of quantum observable universe. For $c_{2}(\mathrm{Ad}(P)) \neq 0$ the construction is replaced by the modular tensor category $\mathbf{Rep}_{k}(\widehat{\mathfrak{g}})$ at level $k = \langle c_{2}, [M] \rangle$, with the level absorbed into the ribbon twist.

      We name the vanishing as a Prop over the context's primitive Chern-class data, rather than projecting it from self-assuming c_1_eq_zero/c_2_eq_zero fields. An instance of this proposition is the open vanishing claim.
