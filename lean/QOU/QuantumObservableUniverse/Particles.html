    structure QOU.ReebDecomposition (M : Type u_1) :
    Type u_1

    Reeb decomposition of a differential form on a CR manifold. LaTeX: \label{def:reeb-decomposition}

    • total : M → ℝ

      The total form (abstract representative).

    • horizontal : M → ℝ

      Horizontal component α_H satisfying ι_ξ α_H = 0.

    • vertical : M → ℝ

      Vertical component α_V = θ ∧ ι_ξ α.

    • decomp (p : M) : self.total p = self.horizontal p + self.vertical p

      Decomposition identity: total = horizontal + vertical.
