      Axiom 1 — Haar-state Frobenius pairing (pattern C — named, not asserted).

      Lemma statement (from manuscript): the Haar state $h = \int_{A}$ on $A = \mathcal{O}_{q}(G)$ is faithful; equivalently, the pairing $(a, b) \mapsto h(ab)$ is non-degenerate and induces an isomorphism $A \xrightarrow{\sim} A^{\vee}$, verifying axiom~(1) of quantum observable universe.

      We name non-degeneracy as the bijectivity of the induced pairing map — a Prop over the context's primitive data — rather than projecting it from self-assuming pairing_inj/pairing_surj fields. An instance of this proposition is the open faithfulness claim.
