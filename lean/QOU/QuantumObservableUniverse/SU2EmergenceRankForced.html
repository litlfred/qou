    theorem QOU.su2_emergence_rank_forced {N : ℕ} (hvol : BraidsAndKnots.exteriorSquareDim N = 1) (G : Set (Matrix (Fin N) (Fin N) ℂ)) (h_dag : ∀ g ∈ G, g * star g = 1) (h_det : ∀ g ∈ G, g.det = 1) :
    N = 2 ∧ ∀ g ∈ G, g ∈ Matrix.specialUnitaryGroup (Fin N) ℂ

    thm:su2-emergence with the rank derived.

    Given the volume constraint in its strong form — dim Λ²V = 1, i.e. Λ²V ≅ 𝟙 — and a set G of automorphisms of the fibre each preserving the volume form (det g = 1) and the Frobenius dagger (g · g† = 1), BOTH conclusions follow:

    • N = 2, from the volume constraint alone; and
    • G ⊆ SU(N), from the two constraints alone.

    The conjunction is the statement prf:su2-emergence intended. Neither conjunct implies the other, which is why they are stated together.

    theorem QOU.su2_emergence_rank_hypothesis_is_load_bearing :
    BraidsAndKnots.exteriorSquareDim 3 ≠ 1 ∧ BraidsAndKnots.exteriorSquareDim 2 = 1

    The rank conjunct is load-bearing, not decorative.

    If it followed from the unitarity half it would hold at every N. It does not: at N = 3 the volume hypothesis exteriorSquareDim N = 1 is false (dim Λ²V = 3), so su2_emergence_rank_forced has no N = 3 instance — while mem_specialUnitary_of_unitary_of_det_one applies at N = 3 perfectly well. That contrast is the anti-vacuity check: the two halves are independent, and only the volume constraint distinguishes rank 2.

    Contrapositive form: a fibre whose gauge group is not special unitary at rank 2 cannot satisfy the volume constraint. Recorded because this is the direction a falsification would come from — exhibit a fibre automorphism violating det = 1 or g·g† = 1, and the volume form is what breaks.

    theorem QOU.no_three_dimensional_fundamental_in_one_fibre {N : ℕ} (hvol : BraidsAndKnots.exteriorSquareDim N = 1) :
    N ≠ 3

    A single fibre satisfying the volume constraint has no three-dimensional fundamental.

    Immediate from exteriorSquareDim_eq_one_iff, and recorded separately because it is the form the flavour question needs: the eightfold-way sector requires a three-dimensional W, and this says one fibre cannot supply it.

    This is a SECOND route to a confinement the corpus already has, not a new result. eightfold-open-problems states — correctly — that "the search is confined to the groupoid level is now a theorem, not an extrapolation", on the strength of the Sym² gate (Sym2Gate.lean, Sym2GateParity.lean): a three-dimensional W must have Sym²(W) irreducible, and the gate decides that for an irreducible summand.

    The two arguments are independent. The Sym² route constrains W by the representation theory of its symmetric square; this one constrains the fibre by the non-degeneracy of its volume form, and never mentions W's symmetric square at all. Two independent derivations of the same negative is worth having; neither supplies the construction that is actually open, which is a three-dimensional W in the multi-fibre groupoid. Nothing here bears on that, and nothing here should be read as narrowing it.
