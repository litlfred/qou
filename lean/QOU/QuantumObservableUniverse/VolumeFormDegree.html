    QOU.QuantumObservableUniverse.VolumeFormDegree — self-duality is a degree-2 #

    accident, not a property of volume-form objects

    Bean qou-j3w6 (the condition-5 decision), epic qou-8lxd.

    Why this file exists #

    def:fundamental-object does not postulate a two-dimensional object. It postulates an object V carrying a volume form — "the categorical determinant" — together with an adjoint coform satisfying the snake identities, and then observes that "under a fibre functor the volume constraint forces rank(V) = 2". Rank two is the conclusion; the volume form is the input.

    The degree, however, is written into the definition by hand: the volume form is declared on Λ²V, and that is what makes the rank two. This file separates the two, by working with the volume form at an unspecified degree N and asking what each degree forces. The answer is a dichotomy that the corpus has been reading as two unrelated facts:

    degree N of the volume form Λ^N W ≅ 𝟙 forced rank complementary power Λ^{N-1}W ≅ W^∨ duality
    2 2 Λ¹W = W W^∨ ≅ W — self-dual, forced
    3 3 Λ²W ≠ W W^∨ ≅ Λ²W — self-duality not forced

    So the self-duality of the fundamental object — the datum the corpus reads as "symplectic self-dual, which is what distinguishes SL₂/SU(2) from GL₂" — is not an extra axiom and not a general feature of volume-form objects. It is what a volume form of degree exactly two says, because two is the only degree at which the complementary exterior power Λ^{N-1} is the object itself.

    That matters for the eightfold way. QOU.Eightfold.Sym2GateFSTrichotomy (gate_forces_complex_of_dim_three, sorry-free) proves that a three-dimensional object passing the Sym² gate must be complex type — not self-dual. The table above says a degree-3 volume form is exactly the structure that does not force self-duality, and whose duality relation W^∨ ≅ Λ²W is the SU(3) relation 𝟑̄ ≅ Λ²𝟑. The missing flavour object and the fundamental object are therefore the same construction at different degrees, not one derived datum and one imported one.

    What is proved here, and what is not #

    Proved (unconditional, no framework hypothesis): the whole dimension bookkeeping of the table — that a non-trivial volume form of degree N forces rank N, that the complementary power has the same dimension as the object, that the complementary degree equals 1 exactly at N = 2, and that the alternating cube is one-dimensional exactly at rank 3.

    Not proved, and deliberately not asserted anywhere below:

    • that a degree-3 volume-form object exists in a quantum observable universe. Nothing here constructs one. This file says what such an object would have to look like and why degree 2 cannot be bent into it; it is a specification, in the sense of bean qou-dwsh, not a construction.
    • that W^∨ ≅ Λ^{N-1}W at the level of objects. What is proved here is the dimension shadow of that isomorphism. The object-level statement is the standard consequence of a non-degenerate pairing Λ^{N-1}W ⊗ W → Λ^N W ≅ 𝟙 in a rigid monoidal category, cited and not formalised — Mathlib has no exterior-power-of-an-object API to state it against. Every declaration below is about Nat.choose, and none of them silently upgrades to an object-level claim.
    • anything about which degree the axioms supply. Condition 5 of def:quantum-observable-universe names Λ². Whether axioms 1–4 supply a second volume form of another degree — the natural candidate being the measurability axiom's c₁(Ad(A)) = 0, which in the usual reading is the statement that the top exterior power of Ad(A) is trivial, i.e. a volume form of degree rank Ad(A) — is open, and is the question bean qou-j3w6 puts to the author. That reading of c₁ is an interpretation of a categorical Chern class the corpus has no Lean carrier for; it is recorded here as a lead, and is asserted nowhere below.

    Base ring: none — this is integer combinatorics (§7c: no ℝ, no ring at all).

    Companion results already in the corpus, which this generalises rather than duplicates: QOU.BraidsAndKnots.VolumeConstraintForcesFibreTwo (exteriorSquareDim_eq_one_iff, the N = 2 row) and QOU.Eightfold.RowCapObstruction (weyl_dim_alt_eq_zero_iff, the vanishing of the alternating cube below rank 3).

      theorem QOU.QuantumObservableUniverse.VolumeFormDegree.complementary_dim {N : ℕ} (hN : 1 ≤ N) :
      extPowerDim N (N - 1) = N

      The complementary power has the same dimension as the object. For a rank-N object, dim Λ^{N-1}W = N = dim W. This is the dimension shadow of W^∨ ≅ Λ^{N-1}W, the isomorphism the volume form induces; the object-level statement is cited, not formalised (see the module docstring).

      theorem QOU.QuantumObservableUniverse.VolumeFormDegree.degrees_two_and_three_exclusive {n : ℕ} (h2 : extPowerDim n 2 = 1) (h3 : extPowerDim n 3 = 1) :
      False

      The two degrees are mutually exclusive on one object: an object cannot carry a volume form of degree 2 and one of degree 3, since each forces its own rank. So a second fundamental object is genuinely a second object, not a re-reading of the first.
