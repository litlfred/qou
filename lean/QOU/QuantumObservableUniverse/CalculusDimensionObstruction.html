    QOU.QuantumObservableUniverse.CalculusDimensionObstruction — the categorical #

    route gives the volume form and the distribution, and it kills self-duality without ever mentioning a manifold

    Bean qou-xlw1, epic qou-8lxd. Fourth companion to QOU.QuantumObservableUniverse.SelfDualityDegree, QOU.Eightfold.ContactDistributionRank, QOU.Eightfold.HyperbolicVolumeFormType.

    The question #

    Asked by the owner: "Is there perhaps a category-theoretic approach that might give the needed volume forms and distributions?"

    Yes, and the corpus already has both. They are not archimedean objects that have to be transported into the category — they are objects of the differential calculus on A = 𝒪_q(SU(2)), and no manifold appears anywhere in their construction.

    Where they already are #

    quantum-observable-universes/categorical-reeb-brings.md gives the whole package, from the Woronowicz left-covariant 3-dimensional calculus:

    • Ω¹(A) is free of rank 3 over A, with left-invariant basis ω₊, ω₋, ω₃;
    • the contact form is Θ_A = ω₃, normalised by ω₃(e₃) = 1;
    • the structure equation is dΘ_A = −q⁻¹ ω₊ ∧ ω₋;
    • hence the distribution H_A := ker Θ_A = span_A{ω₊, ω₋}, free of rank 2;
    • hence the volume form Θ_A ∧ dΘ_A = −q⁻¹ ω₃ ∧ ω₊ ∧ ω₋, which is the top form of Ω^•(A).

    So the categorical layer supplies a contact form, a rank-2 distribution and a degree-3 volume form outright. What it does not supply is a complex rank-3 object — that remains Λ³W ≅ 𝟙, i.e. condition 5 at degree 3, which is bean qou-j3w6 and the author's.

    The obstruction this file proves, and it is stronger than the geometric one #

    Write d for the dimension of the calculus — the rank of Ω¹(A) over A. Then ker Θ has rank d − 1, and two requirements the corpus places on the same calculus pull in opposite directions:

    • A contact form needs d ODD. dΘ restricted to ker Θ must be non-degenerate and alternating, so ker Θ has even rank; d − 1 even means d odd (contact_forces_odd).
    • Self-duality of 2-forms needs d = 4. ⋆ : Ω^k → Ω^{d−k} is an endomorphism of Ω² — the only way Ω^± = ker(id ∓ ⋆) can be defined — iff d = 4 (selfDual_forces_four, and SelfDualityDegree for the same fact one layer down).

    Four is even. So no differential calculus carries both (no_calculus_has_both). self-dual-ses.md asserts ⋆_q is an involution on Ω²(A) and categorical-reeb-brings.md builds a contact form on the same A; those two blocks cannot both be describing one calculus.

    This is the categorical form of what SelfDualityDegree proves archimedeanly — and it is stronger, because it needs no manifold, no metric, no H = ker Θ ⊂ TM, and no resolution of the corpus's three-way S³ ambiguity. It is a statement about Ω^•(A) alone.

    The dimension dictionary, and what it says about the rank dispute #

    Matching the two layers, rank_ℝ(ker Θ) = d − 1 categorically and = 2n archimedeanly, so d = 2n + 1 = dim M: the calculus dimension is the manifold dimension (calculusDim_eq_crDim). The declared Woronowicz calculus has d = 3, giving n = 1, rank H = 2, dim M = 3.

    That is a third independent vote for the 3-dimensional reading, after Bring's T*S construction and the nine dim M_S = 3 sites — and it is the only one of the three with no geometry in it at all. The rank-4 reading would need d = 5 (rank-4 distribution, still odd d, still contact) — not the 4-dimensional bicovariant calculus, which by the above is not contact at all.

    What is proved here, and what is not #

    Proved (unconditional integer arithmetic): the parity argument, that d = 4 is forced by self-duality and excluded by contactness, the exterior-algebra dimensions of a rank-3 calculus, that Θ ∧ dΘ sits in top degree there, and the calculus/manifold dimension dictionary.

    Not proved, and asserted nowhere below:

    • the Woronowicz structure equations, or that Ω¹(𝒪_q(SU(2))) is free of rank 3 in the left-covariant calculus. Cited — categorical-reeb-brings.md and brings-is-qou-verification.md:3 state them; Mathlib has no covariant- differential-calculus API.
    • that 𝒪_q(SU(2)) admits 4-dimensional bicovariant calculi. Standard (Woronowicz's classification), cited, and library/ holds no source on it.
    • that a contact distribution must carry a compatible complex structure, or anything about Frobenius–Schur type. That is ContactDistributionRank.
    • which degree condition 5 should carry. qou-j3w6, the author's.

    Base ring: none — integer arithmetic (§7c: no ℝ, no ring at all).
