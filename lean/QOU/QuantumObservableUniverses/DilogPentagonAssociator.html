    QOU.QuantumObservableUniverses.DilogPentagonAssociator #

    "QOU axioms → q-dilogarithm naturally": the Mac Lane pentagon coherence of the monoidal associator is equivalent to being a q-dilogarithm evaluation map.

    The substrate category 𝐂 is rigid monoidal, so its associator α_{X,Y,Z} satisfies the Mac Lane pentagon coherence. Passing to the shuffle Hopf algebra ℋ_q of the braiding, that coherence descends to a functional equation on the level-2 primitive: the five reassociations of a fourfold tensor collapse to the dilogarithm five-term relation. This file formalizes the payoff — the pentagon names the dilogarithm:

    FiveTerm A f ↔ ∃ q-dilogarithm datum with arguments A and potential f

    i.e. a potential satisfies the Mac Lane pentagon iff it is a q-dilogarithm evaluation map. Sorry-free, generic base ([Add H]; §7c — categorical content, no archimedean construct).

    The two guises of the one pentagon (both already in the corpus) #

    This is a consolidation: the same Mac Lane coherence appears in QOU in two already-formalized guises, and this file records that they are one law.

    • Cochain / 3-cocycle guise — QOU.BringsSurface.PentagonTwist.assoc_pentagon proves (sorry-free, via d² = 0) that the additive twist associator α = dF satisfies the pentagon.
    • Dilogarithm five-term guise — the pentagon field of the content-block QOU.QDilogarithm structure, whose unique level-2 solution is Li₂^q (prop:q-dilogarithm-exists), realized in operator form by the Faddeev–Kashaev pentagon (conj:pentagon-uv-commutation).
    • Analytic realization — QOU.BraidKnot.QRogersPentagonArchimedean proves the classical q = 1 Rogers–Abel five-term skeleton; the q ≠ 1 analytic Li₂ five-term stays an honest conjecture class (QRogersAnalyticContinuation) because Mathlib lacks the polylogarithm five-term.

    Honest scope boundary (anti-laundering) #

    What is proved here is the structural equivalence at the level of the scalar pentagon potential — that the five-term relation and "being a q-dilogarithm" are the same condition. What is NOT closed here, and is not laundered:

    1. the categorical → scalar descent (that the morphism-level Mac Lane pentagon on α_{X,Y,Z} reduces to this scalar five-term equation) is the classical shuffle–associator identification (Faddeev–Kashaev), whose Lean formalization needs shuffle-Hopf infrastructure absent from Mathlib;
    2. the analytic q ≠ 1 Li₂ five-term (the QRogers class above);
    3. the bar-odd resurgent monodromy datum (keystone G3 of the Φ_b gap audit) — genuinely open, and not touched by this algebraic equivalence.
