    QOU.QuantumObservableUniverses.QAnalysisUniquenessCounterexamples #

    Refutations of six axioms that stood in the q-analysis display siblings until 2026-09-06. Five asserted uniqueness of a distinguished element from agreement of the ambient maps; one asserted existence of a QDilogarithm over every additive carrier. All six are false.

    The one defect, six times #

    Each of QDilogarithm, QExponential and QLogarithm carries its ambient structure -- the coproduct, the counit, the Jackson derivative, the pentagon arguments -- as free function fields, and its axioms as proof fields about those functions. So the functions can be chosen to make the axioms hold for every value of the distinguished element:

    • coprod := fun x => (x, x) makes grouplike, primitive_left and primitive_right hold whatever e_q or log_q is;
    • a constant counit makes counit_eq_one / counit_eq_zero hold whatever it is;
    • D_q := id makes flow_invariant hold whatever it is.

    Two records then agree on every hypothesised field and differ in the concluded one. The QDilogarithm case is starker still: Li2, the field the conclusion is about, is not mentioned by the pentagon field at all -- the pentagon constrains Li2_eval, a different field, and nothing ties the two. Li2 is free data.

    The witnesses below are not exotic. They are the same shape as the records the corpus's own *-exists.lean siblings use to prove these types inhabited (QExponential.exists_in_shuffle_hopf and QLogarithm.exists_in_shuffle_hopf, both of which say in a comment that the witness is trivial and the intended Kassel construction is sharper). The existence witness and the uniqueness counterexample are the same degenerate record.

    The existence axiom (not_qDilogarithm_exists_over_every_add_carrier) #

    QDilogarithm.exists_axiom : ∀ (H : Type*) [Add H], Nonempty (QDilogarithm H) quantified over every carrier with an Add, including empty ones. A QDilogarithm Empty would have to supply log_q : Empty. Unlike the five uniqueness axioms this one is repairable rather than wrong in substance: over an AddZeroClass the type really is inhabited, and QDilogarithm.nonempty_of_addZeroClass below proves it with no axiom -- while being explicit that inhabiting the type is not the Kassel construction the proposition's prose describes.

    What the citations actually say #

    [kassel1995] §III.3 proves grouplikes are linearly independent, and §IV.3 identifies primitives with a Lie algebra -- neither is a uniqueness statement, and a Lie algebra of primitives is exactly a statement that primitives are not unique. A corrected uniqueness claim needs a normalisation the structures do not carry, and is deliberately not supplied here: guessing the missing normalisation is how these six arose.

    • [kassel1995] Quantum Groups (Springer GTM 155), §III.3, §IV.3

          theorem QOU.QAnalysisCounterexamples.QDilogarithm.nonempty_of_addZeroClass (H : Type u_1) [AddZeroClass H] :
          Nonempty (QDilogarithm H)

          The repaired statement, with no axiom: over an AddZeroClass the type is inhabited.

          This is what the Lean existence claim always said, and it is worth being plain that it is much weaker than the proposition's prose: the witness is the all-zero record, so this establishes inhabitation of the structure, not the shuffle-recursion construction of the q-dilogarithm. The same caveat applies to the two exists_in_shuffle_hopf theorems, which say so themselves.
