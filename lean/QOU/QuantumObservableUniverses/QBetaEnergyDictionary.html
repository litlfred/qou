    QOU.Thermo — the β = ln q dictionary (partition-function unification, R1) #

    The QOU Frobenius/Markov trace is tr_M = ∑_{λ⊢n} y_λ(q) χ_λ, a weighted sum over Young diagrams (Wedderburn blocks). Read as a statistical- mechanics partition function Z = ∑_states e^{-β E}, the Wedderburn blocks are the microstates and the inverse temperature is β = ln q: the positive Frobenius weight y_λ(q) is the Boltzmann weight of block λ, whose energy is read off as E_λ = -ln y_λ / ln q.

    This file proves that dictionary is well posed and physically meaningful:

    • boltzmann_energyOf — the read-off energy reproduces the weight (e^{-β E_λ} = y_λ); derive-then-check, not asserted.
    • energyOf_unique — the energy is the unique solution (Boltzmann map is injective).
    • energyOf_le_iff — the correspondence is order-reversing: a larger Frobenius weight is a lower energy, so the dominant Wedderburn block is the ground state. This is the falsifiable physical content.
    • partition_sum_eq — the stat-mech partition function written in energy variables equals the Frobenius weighted sum: ∑ d_λ e^{-β E_λ} = ∑ d_λ y_λ.
    • logPos_of_one_lt — for the substrate regime q > 1, β = ln q > 0.

    Base ring ℝ: genuinely archimedean (Real.exp, Real.log, ordering), correct per §7c.
