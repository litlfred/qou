    QOU.ParticleInteractions.AtomKnot — atom as a closed braid via NF block decomposition #

    Atom-knot of type (Z, N): closed braid with NF T_w = T_{w_q^Z} · T_{w_qbar^N} · T_{w_inter} on n(K) = 3(Z+N) strands.

    structure QOU.BraidKnot.AtomKnot :
    Type

    Atom-knot of type (Z, N).

    Records the proton-quark NF block, the neutron-quark NF block, and the inter-nucleon connector permutation. The total NF is the product of the three blocks; the closed braid is the Markov closure of any braid representative realising this NF.

    • Z : ℕ

      Proton count Z.

    • N : ℕ

      Neutron count N.

    • protonBlock : List ℕ

      Proton-quark NF block (encoded as a list of generator indices).

    • neutronBlock : List ℕ

      Neutron-quark NF block.

    • interBlock : List ℕ

      Inter-nucleon connector permutation.
