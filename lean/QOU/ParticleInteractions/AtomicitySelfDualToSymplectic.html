    theorem QOU.Atomicity.sp_fin_one_eq_sl (R : Type u_1) [CommRing R] :
    LieAlgebra.Symplectic.sp (Fin 1) R = LieAlgebra.SpecialLinear.sl (Fin 1 ⊕ Fin 1) R

    Low-rank coincidence 𝔰𝔭(2) = 𝔰𝔩(2) (C₁ = A₁).

    The symplectic Lie subalgebra of Matrix (Fin 1 ⊕ Fin 1) (Fin 1 ⊕ Fin 1) R, defined as the skew-adjoint matrices for the rank-one symplectic form J = fromBlocks 0 (-1) 1 0, coincides exactly with the special-linear Lie subalgebra of trace-zero matrices.

    Proof: membership in Symplectic.sp (Fin 1) R unfolds (via mem_skewAdjointMatricesLieSubalgebra and mem_skewAdjointMatricesSubmodule) to J.IsSkewAdjoint A, i.e. Aᵀ * J = J * (-A); membership in SpecialLinear.sl (Fin 1 ⊕ Fin 1) R is definitionally trace A = 0. Reducing the matrix equation entrywise over Fin 1 ⊕ Fin 1 shows both are equivalent to A (inl 0) (inl 0) + A (inr 0) (inr 0) = 0, the trace-zero condition.
