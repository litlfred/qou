    QOU.ParticleInteractions.four_term_pair_nucleon #

    Proposition for content block prop:four-term-pair-nucleon: The proton and neutron are derived — not asserted — from three independent γγ pair productions using the full 4-term photon color content, confined by the Borromean braid 𝓑₃.

    The substantive claim is that the nucleon normal-form (NF) net coefficients

    net(𝔭) ≈ +0.999 (quarks u₁, u₂, d₃), net(𝔫) ≈ +0.811 (quarks u₁, d₂, d₃),

    are the Gröbner–Shirshov normal forms of the Borromean-confined product 𝓑₃ · C(u,u;σ₀) · C(u,d;σ₀) · C(u,d;σ₁) evaluated at the substrate q₀, and that this NF is independent of the photon polarization (φ₀, φ₁, φ₀₁) (confinement universality).

    These are genuine categorical NF claims requiring the Hecke / Gröbner–Shirshov reduction engine and the Markov-trace evaluation at q₀ (compute: computations/, witness four-term-photon-to-atoms.witness.json). That machinery is not yet in Lean, so the substantive equalities are stated faithfully here and discharged by sorry; only the definitional construction counts (3 pair productions, 4-term photon content) are proved.

              theorem QOU.ParticleInteractions.confinement_universality {d : ℕ} (confined g gInv : Matrix (Fin d) (Fin d) Archimedean.R_t) (hg : gInv * g = 1) :
              (g * confined * gInv).trace = confined.trace

              Confinement universality — the mechanism, proved.

              The Borromean-confined normal-form net is the Markov trace of the confined braid matrix. A change of photon polarization (φ₀, φ₁, φ₀₁) acts on the confined braid by conjugation by an invertible color operator g (two-sided inverse gInv, gInv · g = 1). Because the Markov trace is a class function, conjugation leaves it unchanged:

              tr(g · confined · gInv) = tr(confined).

              Hence the nucleon NF net is independent of the photon polarization — the categorical content of the .md "confinement universality" claim ("sweeping φ₀₁ leaves the NF unchanged"). This is the trace conjugation-invariance MarkovTrace.trace_conj_of_mul_eq_one applied to the confinement setting.

              (The identification of a polarization change with a specific conjugator g is the physics model; the specific NF values ≈ 0.999 / 0.811 are the witness-pinned q₀ evaluations discharged separately below.)
