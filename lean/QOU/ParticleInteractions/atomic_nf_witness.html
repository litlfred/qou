    QOU.ParticleInteractions.atomic_nf_witness #

    Canonical declaration for content block prop:atomic-nf-witness: Atomic braid normal form witness.

    2026-09-03 — a five-way conjunction carried as one field #

    AtomicNfWitnessContext carried thirteen data fields and, as atomic_nf_witness_field, the theorem's entire conclusion verbatim — all five sub-claims of the .md conjoined — and atomic_nf_witness returned it. AGENTS.md §3b-cond item 5, and the largest carrier in this drain.

    The field's own docstring was candid: "External theorem / missing input: atomic NF certificate facts." It is right that these are inputs. What made it a defect is that the inputs were fields, so the instance chose Phi_1, pi_star, F_Pauli, trAlt, net and the rest, and then asserted the five claims about its own choices — a ctx : AtomicNfWitnessContext R argument constrains nothing a caller brought.

    Two decorative fields. Braid : Type and W_max : ℕ → ℕ appear in no sub-claim. W_max is the word-count cap for the A > 9 stripping regime, which is exactly the half of claim 4 that is not encoded (only a ≤ 9 → NF_no_strip a is) — so the field that would have carried the missing case was present and unused, which reads as coverage rather than as the gap it is.

    Disposition: name it, assert nothing (AGENTS.md §1a, disposition 3) #

    atomic_nf_witness is a Prop-valued definition over caller-supplied data, so the five sub-claims constrain the caller's construction. Two consequences are derived from it rather than assumed:

    • fPauli_nonneg — F_Pauli = |tr_alt/net| forces 0 ≤ F_Pauli. A Pauli character magnitude cannot be negative, and now it is a theorem instead of a property nobody checked.
    • groundState_value_unique — sub-claim 1 says π* is a global minimum of Φ₁, so any two minimisers agree in value. This is the content of "global minimum" as opposed to "a minimum found by descent", and it is what makes the exact/steepest-descent distinction in the .md meaningful.

    What is unencoded, and one part that cannot be #

    Claims 1–4 are named but unproved: nothing here constructs H_{A+Z}(q), the atomic braid, Φ₁, the Gröbner–Shirshov reduction, or the Diamond-Lemma convergence argument claim 3 cites. The A > 9 stripping branch of claim 4 is absent entirely, as above.

    Claim 5 is not a mathematical statement. "Certificates (JSON) are committed to the repository for each computed atom" is a fact about this repository, not about H_{A+Z}(q); it is unencodable in Lean by nature, not by missing infrastructure. Saying so is the honest disposition — it should never appear as a conjunct that some future instance is expected to discharge.

    Base ring (§7c) #

    The nucleon/electron indices and mass numbers are ℕ. The scalars are Hecke-trace quantities — the Φ₁ cost, and the certificate's tr_alt(q₀), net(q₀) and F_Pauli = |tr_alt / net|. They are compared, divided and taken in absolute value, which needs an ordered field but nothing archimedean; so they stay a generic R and the substrate certificate is the instantiation R := ℝ.

    Bean qou-z9ws.

    The claim of prop:atomic-nf-witness, as a proposition over caller-supplied data — sub-claims 1–4 of the .md:

    1. π* globally minimises the nucleon-ordering cost Φ₁;
    2. e* minimises Φ₁ over electron positions (the greedy step);
    3. the Gröbner–Shirshov reduction converges at every construction step;
    4. the NF element accumulates without stripping for A ≤ 9; and the certificate records F_Pauli = |tr_alt/net|.

    Named, not asserted. Claim 5 of the .md is deliberately absent — see the module docstring: it is a statement about this repository, not about H_{A+Z}(q).
