    QOU.ParticleInteractions.even_tower_continuant_harmonics — the boson tower is uniformly harmonic #

    Interpretive derivation for the electroweak/boson even-winding tower (conj:electroweak-higgs-torus-link-triplet), the boson-side mirror of the fermion-side generation-exclusion result (the odd-prime windings 3,5,7; landing separately in PR #4456).

    Setup #

    The Gröbner–Shirshov normal form of T(2,n) = σ₀ⁿ in H₂(q) is a_n · 𝟏 + b_n · σ₀ with b_n = morganVoyceB n the Morgan–Voyce (Fibonacci) continuant over Polynomial ℤ (X = the Hecke parameter h = q − q⁻¹), b₀ = 0, b₁ = 1, b_{n+2} = X·b_{n+1} + b_n (lepton_continuant_recurrence).

    The bosons are the even windings n = 2k (two-component links, boson_iff_even); the electroweak carriers are W ↔ T(2,4), Z ↔ T(2,6), H ↔ T(2,8), with the Hopf link T(2,2) the trivial fundamental.

    What this file derives #

    The Morgan–Voyce continuant is a strong divisibility sequence: its polynomial coefficient satisfies a ∣ n ⇒ b_a ∣ b_n. This is the general theorem the fermion sibling's docstring asserts but only instantiates on {3,5,7,9}; here it is proved in general from the addition formula b_{m+n+1} = b_{m+1}·b_{n+1} + b_m·b_n, then applied to the even tower.

    Consequences on the boson windings, all Polynomial ℤ (derived, not asserted):

    • Every even winding is a harmonic of the fundamental Hopf continuant b₂ = X: b₂ ∣ b_{2k} for all k (b2_dvd_even). The whole boson tower descends from the single fundamental b₂.
    • The concrete carriers, with explicit cofactors: b₂ ∣ b₄, b₂ ∣ b₆, b₂ ∣ b₈ (all of W,Z,H are b₂-harmonics), and b₄ ∣ b₈ (H's coefficient factors through W's).

    Honest scope (scope-(a), not scope-(b)) #

    This sharpens the fermion/boson class distinction (C1 selection) at the normal-form level with a derived reason:

    • fermion tower — the odd prime windings 3,5,7 are mutually non-divisible (the fermion generation-exclusion result, PR #4456): three independent generations;
    • boson tower — every even winding factors through b₂ (b2_dvd_even below): no even winding is a "prime generation", the tower is uniformly non-generational — exactly the gauge/link (boson_iff_even) character.

    It does not derive the specific W↔4 / Z↔6 / H↔8 mass labels: the observed m_W < m_Z < m_H ordering still maps onto 4 < 6 < 8 only numerically (the paper's mass ordering is necessary, not sufficient; the C_sector / C_top identification stays conjectural, and the continuant net ratios are O(1) while the observed boson-mass ratios are not). What is closed is the class-level "why the even tower is non-generational", not the per-boson identification.

    The addition formula for the Morgan–Voyce continuant #

    b_{m+n+1} = b_{m+1}·b_{n+1} + b_m·b_n — the Fibonacci-polynomial addition law, proved by two-step induction on m from the recurrence.

    source
    theorem QOU.ParticleInteractions.morganVoyceB_add (m n : ℕ) :
    Archimedean.morganVoyceB (m + n + 1) = Archimedean.morganVoyceB (m + 1) * Archimedean.morganVoyceB (n + 1) + Archimedean.morganVoyceB m * Archimedean.morganVoyceB n

    Addition formula for the Morgan–Voyce continuant b_n: b_{m+n+1} = b_{m+1}·b_{n+1} + b_m·b_n.

    Derived by two-step induction on m from morganVoyceB_recurrence; it is the polynomial Fibonacci addition law specialised to b₁ = 1, b₂ = X.

    The boson tower: every even winding is a b₂-harmonic #

    source
    theorem QOU.ParticleInteractions.b2_dvd_even (k : ℕ) :
    Archimedean.morganVoyceB 2 ∣ Archimedean.morganVoyceB (2 * k)

    Every even winding is a harmonic of the fundamental Hopf continuant b₂. b₂ ∣ b_{2k} for all k: the entire boson (even-winding) tower descends from the single fundamental b₂ = X. This is the boson-side statement dual to the fermion generation-exclusion: there is no even "prime generation" — every even winding factors through b₂, matching the non-generational gauge/link character of bosons (boson_iff_even).

    Boson-tower harmonic summary. All three electroweak carriers are harmonics of the fundamental Hopf continuant b₂, and H's coefficient additionally factors through W's. Contrast the fermion tower, whose prime windings 3,5,7 are mutually non-divisible (the fermion generation-exclusion result, PR #4456): the boson tower is uniformly non-generational.
