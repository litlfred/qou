    QOU.ParticleInteractions.SilverRatioBetaDecayNull #

    Lake-built mirror of the sibling-stub folio/quantum-observable-universe/particle-interactions/silver-ratio-beta-decay-null.lean.

    Silver-ratio β-decay null point: at $q = 1 \pm \sqrt{2}$ the categorical β-decay vanishes, the Hecke generator $\sigma$ has eigenvalues equal to the silver-ratio conjugates (self-referential fixed point), and the proton/neutron Markov-traces become categorically indistinguishable.

    Derivation: from prop:neutron-qvalue-symbolic, $\Delta(\text{net}) = h(h-2)/2$ where $h = q - q^{-1}$. Setting $h = 2$ gives $q^2 - 2q - 1 = 0$, roots $q = 1 \pm \sqrt 2$.

    The key algebraic identity at $q = 1 + \sqrt 2$:

      (q^4 + q^2 + 1) / (q^2 + 1)^2 = 7/8.

    This file provides the kernel-verified proof.

        At silver ratio, $b^2$ (Hoefsmit coupling squared) = $7/8$ exactly.

        Derivation: at $q = 1 + \sqrt 2$, $q^2 = 3 + 2\sqrt 2$, $q^4 = 17 + 12\sqrt 2$.

        • Numerator: $q^4 + q^2 + 1 = 21 + 14\sqrt 2 = 7 (3 + 2\sqrt 2)$.

        • Ratio: $7/8$.

        The null, restated in crossing energy — and why it is not the VC limit #

        The corpus writes the per-crossing energy as $\mathfrak{E}_\times(q) = \tfrac12 (q - q^{-1})$ (binding-knots-visual-inventory), which is exactly $h/2$. So the two roots of beta_decay_null_iff_h_zero_or_two are zero and unit crossing energy — a readable condition, where $h \in \{0, 2\}$ reads as an algebraic coincidence. Same theorem, restated.

        This section also records what the restatement does not buy. The volume-conjecture limit is taken at roots of unity $q = e^{2\pi i/N}$, for which $|q| = 1$, whereas $\mathfrak{E}_\times = 1$ forces $q = 1 + \sqrt 2 > 2$ (crossing_energy_one_not_on_unit_circle). The limit in which the mass formula's volume term equals the hyperbolic volume is $q \to \infty$, where $\hbar_q \to 1$ and $\mathrm{Vol}/\hbar_q^2 \to \mathrm{Vol}$ (tendsto_volTerm_atTop). At the other null root, $q = 1$, that term's denominator is zero (hbarQ_eq_zero_iff).

        Provenance note. Every proposition below is verified numerically at q ∈ {0.5, 1.11, 2, 3, 1+√2, 10} and at q = 10^4, 10^8 for the limit. The tactic scripts were not compiled when written: the container had no Mathlib oleans, and its Lean toolchain ships no libLean.a/libleancpp.a, so lake could not link the cache executable to fetch them. CI is the first type-checker for this section.
