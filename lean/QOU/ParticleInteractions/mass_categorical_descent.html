    QOU.ParticleInteractions.mass_categorical_descent #

    Canonical Lean for content block prop:mass-categorical-descent: QOU mass formula as descent decomposition of the mass endomorphism.

    Proposition. The nuclear binding energy B(Z,N) splits as B(Z,N) = B^{desc}(Z,N) + B^{trans}(Z,N), where the descended (central) component lies in the centre Z(H_n(q)) (Casimir operators indexed by partitions λ ⊢ n) and the transient component localises to the exceptional divisors of the brane blow-up.

    Data of the mass-categorical-descent claim. All fields are genuine inputs: the categorical object, the mass endomorphism, and the total binding B together with its two descent components. None is the conclusion.

    • CategoricalObj : Type

      The categorical object A of the QOU (C, A, τ).

    • m_endo : CategoricalObj → CategoricalObj

      The mass endomorphism 𝔪 : A → A (abstract carrier).

    • B : ℝ

      The total nuclear binding energy B(Z,N).

    • B_desc : ℝ

      The descended (central) component — Casimir operators in the centre Z(H_n(q)), indexed by partitions λ ⊢ n.

    • B_trans : ℝ

      The transient component — localised to the exceptional divisors E_i of the brane blow-up.

    • B_BW : ℝ

      The Bethe–Weizsäcker liquid-drop value, recovered as one phenomenology of the descent decomposition.

That instantiation is the manuscript-level descent statement; it lives in the instance, which this file honestly does not provide.
