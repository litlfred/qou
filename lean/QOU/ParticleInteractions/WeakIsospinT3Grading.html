    QOU.ParticleInteractions.WeakIsospinT3Grading #

    A weak-isospin (up/down-type) grading T₃ orthogonal to the generation winding #

    Serves bean qou-tdrd (root of the convergence epic qou-20sx) and the honest negative of docs/audits/2026-08-02-meson-fluxtube-junction-reduction.md (bean qou-qpj9, PR #4614). That audit proved (MesonFluxTubeReduction.lean, winding_content_not_injective) that the framework's generation winding n ∈ {3,5,7} is a symmetric function of the quark content and therefore cannot separate up-type from down-type at a fixed generation: charm and strange both carry n = 5, so the T(2,n) dictionary collides the D-meson {5,3} with the kaon {3,5}. The precise missing datum it named is a weak-isospin T₃ grading orthogonal to n.

    The derivation (what is proved below, sorry-free) #

    The framework already carries two independent facts about a quark's canonical torus braid T(2,n) = σ₁^{±n}:

    • its crossing NUMBER n — the generation winding (lepton-meson-torus-knot-dictionary.lean, leptonTorusKnotIndex);
    • its crossing SIGN — already a physical grading in the corpus: the strong-isospin interface uses σ_g (positive) for the pp channel and σ_g⁻¹ (negative) for the nn channel (crystal-isospin-degeneracy.md, isotope_record_schema.py:176-178; audit docs/audits/2026-05-24-isospin-categorical-source.md), and the weak generator T(3,5) maximises the q≠1 Skein sign-asymmetry that drives parity violation and up↔down transmutation (force-knot-identification.md).

    The signed writhe κ = ±n of the oriented braid therefore factors canonically as

          κ  =  sign(κ) · |κ|
           └──┬───┘  └┬┘
            T₃ grading  generation winding

    The magnitude |κ| = n is the generation; the sign sign(κ) ∈ {+1,−1} is an intrinsic ℤ/2 grading orthogonal to the magnitude by construction (the sign and magnitude of an integer are independent data). Normalised as a fundamental SU(2)_weak weight, T₃ = sign(κ)/2 ∈ {+½, −½}.

    With the sign convention up-type = positive crossing inherited from the strong-isospin ⋈_K orientation (pp = σ_g positive; the proton is the more-up nucleon), charm = σ₁^{+5} gets T₃ = +½ and strange = σ₁^{−5} gets T₃ = −½: same generation n = 5, opposite T₃ — the degeneracy winding_content_not_injective is resolved by refining the unsigned winding to the signed writhe.

    Derive-then-check (not fit). T₃ is defined from the topology (signedWrithe), and the Gell-Mann–Nishijima relation Q = T₃ + Y/2 is then checked against the independently-known electric charges Q and the left-doublet hypercharge Y = 1/3. It is a theorem that could have failed (a wrong sign, or a wrong ½, breaks it); it does not (gellMannNishijima). No continuous parameter was tuned — the grading is a discrete ℤ/2.

    But report its WIDTH honestly. The relation is quantified over six quarks and collapses to two distinct arithmetic rows: Y is constant and both Q and T₃ are functions of the up/down-type bit alone (gmn_data_factors_through_weakType, gmn_check_has_two_distinct_rows). So the check is one sign correlation plus one normalisation, verified twice — not six independent confirmations. The generation index never enters it (genWinding_finer_than_gmn_data).

    What is NOT proved (the honest open remainder) #

    signedWrithe is entered as data over a finite toy Quark type, so every statement below is decidable; the arithmetic being provable does not make the physics closed. Two things remain genuinely open, packaged as the content-bearing conjecture class CanonicalWeakIsospinFromBowtie with no instance (§3b-tax honest open state):

    1. that the up-type ↔ positive-writhe identification is forced by the ⋈_K framework orientation rather than chosen consistently with it; and
    2. that this ℤ/2 lifts to the full SU(2)_L gauge action on the H_n(q) representations and, combined with the generation tower, reproduces the quark masses / CKM mixing.

    Both reduce to the open SU(3)-flavour / CKM sector; this file derives the orthogonal grading and its GMN closure, not that sector.

                    How WIDE the GMN check actually is — two rows, not six #

                    gellMannNishijima is quantified over all six quarks, and it is tempting to report it as six independent confirmations. It is not, and the theorems here make the collapse machine-checked so that no summary can widen it by accident.

                    hyperchargeLeft is a constant function, and both emCharge and T3 are functions of weakType alone. Every one of the three quantities entering Q = T₃ + Y/2 therefore depends only on the up/down-type bit, so the six cases supply exactly two distinct arithmetic rows:

                    up-type:    2/3 = +1/2 + 1/6
down-type: −1/3 = −1/2 + 1/6

                    That is one sign correlation plus one normalisation, checked twice — a genuine derive-then-check (a wrong sign or a wrong ½ still breaks it), but a two-row one. The generation index n ∈ {3,5,7} never enters GMN at all; it is exactly the datum the relation is blind to.

A trivial κ := signedWrithe, Y := hyperchargeLeft would fabricate closure over this finite toy and is forbidden (§3b-tax: do not fabricate an instance to close an open primary; the openness is physical, not formal).
