    QOU.ParticleInteractions.positron_correction #

    Proposition for content block prop:positron-correction: The character sum Σ_λ χ_λ(Δ) subtracts the categorical action of both fermions produced in pair creation (2 units of m_e), but the Q-value subtracts only the emitted electron (1 unit). The difference is the positron action:

    Q = Σ_char · m_e + 1 · m_e, the +1 being m_{e⁺}/m_e = 1.

    The structural Q-value form Q = (Σ_char + r)·m_e and the positron mass-ratio fact r = 1 are carried as categorical/physical inputs; the boxed identity Q = Σ_char·m_e + m_e is derived from them by ring algebra. The regime-applicability case split is encoded by an if-defined applied_correction and both branches are derived, not projected.

    • positron_ratio_one : r = 1

      Physical input: m_{e⁺}/m_e = 1.

      Structural Q-value form: Q = (Σ_char + r)·m_e — the character sum plus the positron-action units, scaled by m_e.
