      def QOU.ParticleInteractions.deuterium_derived_binding (ctx : DeuteriumDerivedBindingContext) :
      Prop

      Proposition statement (from manuscript):

      Let $\mathrm{NF}(\mathfrak{p})$ and $\mathrm{NF}(\mathfrak{n})$ be the proton and neutron normal forms in $H_3(q)$, each constructed as $\mathcal{B}_3 \cdot \mathrm{crossings}(quarks)$ (confinement selection) with $\mathcal{B}_3 = \sigma_0\sigma_1\sigma_2\sigma_0\sigma_1\sigma_2$. The full canonical deuteron braid word $\beta_D = \mathtt{atom\_braid\_word\_3A}(1, 1) \in H_6(q)$ (mass_at_3A_proper.atom_braid_word_3A) factors as $\mathrm{NF}(\mathfrak{p}) \cdot T_{pn} \cdot \mathrm{NF}(\mathfrak{n})$ under the H_3 → H_6 Hecke embedding, where $T_{pn} = \frac{1}{2}(\sigma_0 + \sigma_0^{-1})$ is the time-reversal symmetric crossing operator at the inter-nucleon site (the unique generator of $\beta_D$ that crosses a proton strand with a neutron strand). Then the deuterium binding energy is*

      $$ B(d) = \bigl[\mathrm{tr}_M(\mathfrak{p}) + \mathrm{tr}_M(\mathfrak{n}) - \mathrm{tr}_M(T_{pn} \cdot \mathfrak{n})\bigr] \times m_e \cdot \mathrm{Vol}(\mathcal{B}_3) \times (1 - \alpha_{\mathrm{EM}}/2). $$

        Mass–binding endomorphism analogy (2026-05-28) #

        The binding-energy formula is the structural analogue of the mass endomorphism. Where the nucleon mass is

        m(K) = m_e · (Vol(𝓑_3) / ℏ_q²) · lw(K, q_0),

        the binding energy of a composite nucleus is

        B(K) = m_e · Vol(K_bind) · Δtr,    Δtr = |tr_free| − |tr_atom|,

        with K_bind the inter-nucleon binding link (Hopf for the deuteron, 8₁₁-class for ³H, L6a4 for ³He — identified by the binding-K SDP solver). The deuteron uses Vol(𝓑_3) = 8G because both nucleons are Borromean and the Hopf inter-nucleon link is Seifert-fibred (volume 0).

        The analogy is recorded as a structural correspondence between the two endomorphism shapes.

          theorem QOU.ParticleInteractions.mass_binding_share_prefactor [a : BindingEndomorphismAnalogy] :
          BindingEndomorphismAnalogy.m_pred / (BindingEndomorphismAnalogy.lw / BindingEndomorphismAnalogy.hbar_q_sq) = BindingEndomorphismAnalogy.m_e * BindingEndomorphismAnalogy.Vol_B3 ∧ BindingEndomorphismAnalogy.B_pred / BindingEndomorphismAnalogy.delta_tr = BindingEndomorphismAnalogy.m_e * BindingEndomorphismAnalogy.Vol_Kbind

          The mass and binding endomorphisms share the same structural template: stripping the structural factor (lw/ℏ_q² for mass, Δtr for binding) leaves m_e · Vol(·) — the Borromean volume on the mass side, the binding-link volume on the binding side.

          For the deuteron these coincide (Vol(K_bind) = Vol(𝓑_3) = 8G, the Hopf inter-nucleon link being Seifert-fibred), so both prefactors equal m_e · Vol(𝓑_3); for A≥3 they differ by the binding-link volume ratio.
