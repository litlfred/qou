    Atomicity / rank-one from the jet-tower ordering — the order-theoretic separation #

    Bean qou-ngwo (atomicity upgrade route), rank-2 independence test — a different angle. The atomicity upgrade asks whether the rank-one premise (the Tannaka dual G of (𝐂, τ) is a rank-one group — a single fundamental object V, [def:fundamental-object], every simple a power of V) can be derived from the jet-tower ordering axioms rather than assumed. The decisive independence test: does a rank-2 model satisfy the jet-tower ordering axioms?

    This file isolates the order-theoretic core of that test — a route independent of any Tannakian reconstruction or character computation. The jet tower's simple objects are the powers of the fundamental generator(s), ordered by tensor generation (divisibility in the Grothendieck monoid of simples). For a category freely generated by r fundamental objects, that monoid is the free commutative monoid ℕ^r, and the tensor-generation order is divisibility, i.e. the componentwise (product) order (genLe below, at r = 2).

    The separation is then exact:

    • Rank one (single fundamental V; simples Sym^k V ↔ k ∈ ℕ): the tensor-generation order is ≤ on ℕ, a total order — the jet tower is a chain (rankOne_generation_isTotal).
    • Rank two (two independent fundamental objects; simples ↔ (i, j) ∈ ℕ²): the tensor-generation order is the componentwise order on ℕ², which is not total — the two fundamental weights (1,0) and (0,1) are incomparable (rankTwo_fundamental_incomparable, rankTwo_generation_not_isTotal).

    Hence the jet-tower chain axiom — totality of the tensor-generation order — admits the rank-one tower and excludes the free rank-2 tower. The rank-2 independence test resolves (for the free / generic dominance model) as exclusion, and the excluding invariant is precisely the incomparable fundamental pair. This identifies totality of the jet-tower order as the exact order-theoretic form of the atomicity (rank-one) content (atomicity_is_jet_tower_totality, no_rankTwo_jetTowerChain).

    Honest scope (scope-(a), conditional). This proves the separation for the free tensor-generation (divisibility) order. It does not prove the corpus jet-tower axiom set entails totality (that entailment is the remaining premise of bean qou-ngwo), and it does not exclude non-free rank-2 models (a rank-2 lattice can be given some other total order — e.g. lexicographic — that is not the tensor-generation order). What it delivers is a formalize-first narrowing: the atomicity question reduces to the single sharp premise "do the jet-tower ordering axioms force totality of the tensor-generation order?", and the generic rank-2 model is refuted against it by a two-line incomparability witness. Nothing is wired; no instance is fabricated; the rank-one premise remains a premise.

        def QOU.Atomicity.piGenLe {S : Type u_1} (f g : S → ℕ) :
        Prop

        General free tensor-generation order. The componentwise (divisibility) order on the free commutative weight monoid S → ℕ on a generating set S of |S| fundamental objects: piGenLe f g says every fundamental multiplicity of f is ≤ that of g, i.e. f is a tensor factor of g.             Categorical → order bridge — the atomicity derivation, modulo one named premise #

            Reconnaissance (2026-07-25): the corpus does not isolate a "jet-tower ordering axiom" as a Lean object. FundamentalObject (fundamental-object.lean) carries no rank field — its dimension-2 is narrative (Deligne–Milne), not formalised; the jet / KeystoneBridgeC1Binding Lean concerns the normalizer recurrence N(n) = (n−1)!, not an ordering on simples; JetOrderIndependence is a different object (the Jones-series jet-Jacobian rank). So the atomicity premise is genuinely unformalised, and the order-theoretic content above meets the categorical side through one named, honestly-conjectural bridge premise: that the tensor-generation order on the simple-object index is that of the free commutative monoid on the fundamental objects. Given that premise, the chain axiom proves rank ≤ 1.
