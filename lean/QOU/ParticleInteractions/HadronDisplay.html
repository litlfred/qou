    structure QOU.Hadron :
    Type

    A hadron: a common base point on S² (spherical coordinates θ, φ) together with its constituent knot types (valence quarks in the knot picture), of which there must be at least two.

    • theta : Archimedean.R_q

      Spherical coordinate θ of the common base point.

    • phi : Archimedean.R_q

      Spherical coordinate φ of the common base point.

    • constituents : List KnotTheory.BraidWord

      The constituent knot types.

    • constituents_nonempty : self.constituents.length ≥ 2

      A hadron must have at least 2 constituents.
