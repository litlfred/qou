    Tetraplectic geometry of the Bring cone — the Reeb quaternionic twist #

    The affine cone of Bring's curve is a real 4-manifold; its 3-dimensional link carries a 3-Sasakian (degenerate quaternionic-contact) structure whose Reeb triad is modelled here on the quaternion algebra Quaternion R.

    We take the three Reeb operators to be right-multiplication by the imaginary units i, j, k. Right-multiplication is used (rather than left) so that the operators generate the correct 𝔰𝔲(2) bracket with a +2 structure constant:

    [M_i, M_j] p = (p·i)·j − (p·j)·i = p·(ij) − p·(ji) = p·k − p·(−k) = 2·(p·k).

    The three reeb_twist_* lemmas below discharge exactly this bracket ([M_i,M_j] = 2·M_k and cyclic). These are pure quaternion-algebra identities — no ordering, radical, or other archimedean construct is used — so per the §7c base-ring convention they are stated over a generic {R : Type*} [CommRing R], with BringCone naming the geometric object. The archimedean specialization Quaternion ℝ for the actual quaternionic-contact geometry is left to the consumer; nothing here forces Mathlib.Data.Real.Basic into the dependency cone.

    Reference for the geometric interpretation (Reeb quaternionic twist on the quaternionic-contact link): [manuscript] lem:bring-cone-reeb-quaternionic-twist (brings-surface §Spectral resolvents and gauge structures), with the SU(2) reading and its scope limits in rem:tetraplectic-su2-gauge-reading.
