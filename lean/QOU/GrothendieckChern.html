    The Grothendieck group of a quantum universe and its Chern character #

    Carrier for def:grothendieck-group-of-a-qou and def:chern-character, and the Lean half of the factorisation recorded in docs/audits/2026-09-15-chi-as-a-component-of-the-chern-character.md §2.1:

    K₀(𝐂)  ──ch──▶  HH₀(𝐂)  ──ev──▶  R,        dim_q = ev ∘ ch.

    Axiom 4 of a quantum universe (QuantumUniverse.centralIdem_*) makes the Frobenius–Hopf object a finite orthogonal sum of blocks, so the split Grothendieck group is the free abelian group on the block set and the degree-zero Hochschild homology of the (semisimple) category is the free R-module on the central idempotents. In that presentation:

    • K0 U = U.blocks →₀ ℤ, with blockClass U b = [b];
    • HH0 U = U.blocks → R, the coordinates along the central idempotents;
    • chernCharacter U : K0 U → HH0 U sends Σ n_b [b] to Σ n_b [p_b] (Beliakova–Guliyev–Habiro–Lauda §3.5.1: [x] ↦ [1_x]);
    • evDimQ U : HH0 U → R evaluates a class against the quantum dimensions, φ ↦ Σ_b φ(b) · dim_q(b);
    • dimQK0 U : K0 U → R is the quantum dimension extended additively.

    Theorem dimQK0_eq_evDimQ_chernCharacter: dim_q = ev ∘ ch on K₀. This is the precise sense in which the quantum dimension of an object is one evaluation of its character, and it is the item "K₀ → HH₀ → k factorisation" of bean qou-mjt3. The identification of HH₀ of the category with R^{blocks} is the semisimplicity of axiom 4, used as the presentation; nothing here is an axiom beyond the structure QuantumUniverse.

    Base ring generic {R} [CommRing R] (§7c). No sorry.

    -- Ref: [bghl-2017] https://doi.org/10.4171/QT/92
