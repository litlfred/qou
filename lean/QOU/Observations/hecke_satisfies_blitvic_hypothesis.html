    QOU.Observations.hecke_satisfies_blitvic_hypothesis #

    Content block prop:hecke-satisfies-blitvic-hypothesis: The Hecke generators σᵢ ∈ H_∞(q) under the Markov trace tr_M satisfy the pair-partition combinatorial hypothesis of Blitvić's two-parameter non-commutative central limit theorem.

    Lean structure #

    The HeckeSatisfiesBlitvicHypothesisContext class records the genuine structural hypotheses that define the setting — the Hecke quadratic relation and the Markov property of tr_M — together with the Markov scalar z realised inside the algebra (zElem = z·1 ∈ A). These are inputs, not the conclusion.

    From them the centred generator σ̃ᵢ := σᵢ − z·1 and its joint moments centredMoment w := tr_M(∏ⱼ σ̃_{wⱼ}) are defined concretely. The proposition's content — odd-length centred moments vanish (the verifiable face of the pair-partition decomposition; an odd word cannot be matched into pairs) — is then stated as a genuine claim about that defined object.

    The full pair-partition enumeration with the crossing-weighted kernel W(π;q) (Blitvić Thm 1.1) requires the non-commutative-probability pair-partition infrastructure that is not yet in Mathlib. Rather than a bare sorry, its structural face — the first-element pairing (Wick) recursion — is carried as a §3b-cond conditional-class hypothesis (HeckeBlitvicWickRecursion), and the odd-length vanishing is then derived sorry-free by strong induction on the word length. It is not a self-assuming projection: the conclusion is about the concretely-defined centredMoment, the recursion is strictly richer than the odd-vanishing claim, and the vanishing is derived from it.

    References #

    • [blitvic2012] Blitvić, The (q,t)-Gaussian process, J. Funct. Anal. DOI:10.1016/j.jfa.2012.08.006.

    Structural context for the Hecke–Blitvić proposition.

    The fields are the genuine hypotheses of the setting: the Hecke quadratic relation (hecke_quadratic), the Markov property of the trace (markov), and the Markov scalar z realised inside the algebra (zElem). The conclusion (odd-length vanishing) is not stored here — it is derived for the concretely-defined centred moment below.

      The Hecke-algebra carrier A.

    • ringA : Ring A

      Ring structure on A.

    • σ : ℕ → A

      The Hecke generators σᵢ : A indexed by ℕ.

    • h_A : A

      The scalar (q − q⁻¹) as an element of A.

    • hecke_quadratic (i : ℕ) : σ i * σ i = h_A * σ i + 1

      Structural hypothesis (i) — the Hecke quadratic relation σᵢ² = (q − q⁻¹)·σᵢ + 1. This defines the algebra; it is an input, not the proposition's claim.

    • tr_M : A → ℝ

      Markov trace functional tr_M : A → ℝ.

    • z_real : ℝ

      Markov parameter z = 1 / (q^{1/2} + q^{-1/2}).

    • zElem : A

      The Markov scalar z·1 realised as an element of A, so the centred generator σᵢ − z·1 lives in A.

    • markov (x : A) (n : ℕ) : tr_M (x * σ n) = z_real * tr_M x

      Structural hypothesis (ii) — the Markov property tr_M(x · σₙ) = z · tr_M(x). An input, not the claim.

          Blitvić's first-element pairing (Wick) recursion — the §3b-cond conditional-class hypothesis carrying the deep, not-in-Mathlib input.

            theorem QOU.Observations.hecke_satisfies_blitvic_hypothesis [ctx : HeckeSatisfiesBlitvicHypothesisContext] [wc : HeckeBlitvicWickRecursion] (w : List ℕ) :
            w.length % 2 = 1 → HeckeSatisfiesBlitvicHypothesisContext.centredMoment w = 0

            Proposition ([prop:hecke-satisfies-blitvic-hypothesis]): odd-length centred moments vanish — conditional on Blitvić's first-element pairing (Wick) recursion.

            Blitvić's Thm 1.1 expands each centred moment as a crossing-weighted sum over pair partitions. Its structural face — the recursion that pairs the first letter of the word with each later letter and drops both, reducing the length by two (wick, with pairWeight the crossing-kernel W(π;q) contribution) — is the genuinely deep, not-in-Mathlib input, carried by the §3b-cond conditional class HeckeBlitvicWickRecursion. From it the odd-length vanishing is derived sorry-free by strong induction on the word length: a length-1 word has no later letter to pair with, so its expansion is the empty sum 0; each pairing term drops the length by two, preserving oddness, and vanishes by the inductive hypothesis.

            This is not a self-assuming projection: the conclusion is about the concretely-defined centredMoment (built from the structural Hecke + Markov hypotheses), the wick recursion is strictly richer than the odd-vanishing statement, and the vanishing is derived from it, not handed back unchanged. A full formalisation would discharge wick itself by the Markov-peeling / pair-partition machinery once that lands in Mathlib.
