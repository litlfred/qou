          theorem QOU.Observations.BarParityRankOne.isBarOdd_iff {K : Type u_1} [Field K] (h2 : 2 ≠ 0) (f : K → K) :
          IsBarOdd f ↔ ∃ (g : K → K), IsBarEven g ∧ ∀ (q : K), q ≠ 0 → f q = skein q * g q

          The bar-odd channel is free of rank one over the bar-even part, with the skein coefficient q − q⁻¹ as generator.

          A function is bar-odd iff it is the generator times a bar-even function. So there is no choice of bar-odd object: every one factors through the same q − q⁻¹. This is what turns qou-fi93's qⁿ − q⁻ⁿ from an exhibited candidate into the only shape available.
