    QOU.Observations.QPlancherelAsymptoticSkeleton #

    Proposition (prop:q-plancherel-asymptotic-skeleton).

    The q-Plancherel measure M_{n,q} pushes forward to measures on the q-deformed Thoma simplex. The q = 1 weak limit (clause 1) and the generic-q asymptotics (clause 2) are literature; the operator-algebra realisation of the generic-q limit (clause 3) is conjectural.

    The weight was corrected on 2026-09-05 (bean qou-j7ef). This module previously said the manuscript pushed forward the Wedderburn weight w̃_λ^{(n)} = dim_q²(λ)/[n]_q! and called clause 2 established for it. It is not: the cited asymptotics are proved for M_{n,q}, which differs from dim_q²/[n]_q! by a factor of 978 on some λ at n = 12 at the substrate — not by a normalisation, so no rescaling moves the citation across. The manuscript block now states the proposition over M_{n,q} itself, which the corpus already holds as the Molien / fake-degree weight (MolienFakeDegreeGeneralN.molienSum) under the substitution Q = q²; that identification is exact and is verified in exact rational arithmetic in computations/probes/molien_feray_meliot_bridge_probe.py.

    The 1/√(n − 3) majorant below is UNSOURCED, and the field is named accordingly. It was attributed to Féray–Méliot; that expression does not occur anywhere in their q-Plancherel paper, whose fluctuation result is a central limit theorem at scale √n. The theorem proved here is unaffected — it is a squeeze, valid for any non-negative sequence dominated by a majorant tending to 0, whoever supplies it — but the hypothesis is carried as an assumption of the context class and must not be read as a cited theorem until someone sources it.

    Disposition (derived, not projected) #

    The previous encoding returned ctx.constant_eq_one ▸ ctx.char_variance_bound — i.e. it re-emitted the class field that is the variance-bound conclusion (after substituting C = 1); no derivation.

    Here the class carries the genuine input: the majorant char_variance n ≤ 1/√(n − 3) — an ASSUMPTION, not a cited theorem; it was described here as "an established external theorem, cited [feray2010]" and neither half survived checking (see above) — together with non-negativity of the variance. The theorem then derives the actual asymptotic content the manuscript needs — the character fluctuations vanish in the large-n limit, char_variance n → 0 — by squeezing between 0 and the majorant 1/√(n − 3) → 0. This vanishing is precisely what makes the pushforward measures concentrate (the measure-theoretic half of the convergence in clauses 1–2); it is a consequence of the hypothesis, not a restatement of a field. That the hypothesis is unsourced weakens what the module can be cited FOR; it does not weaken the derivation, which needs only that the majorant tends to 0.

    The full measure-valued statement (existence of Ω_q, the pushforward maps ι_n, weak convergence, and the conjectural operator-algebra realisation 3a/3b) is not formalised here — those are the open literature/conjectural parts of the manuscript, honestly left unencoded rather than smuggled into a True/projection field.

    class QOU.Observations.QPlancherelAsymptoticSkeletonContext :
    Type

    Inputs for the q-Plancherel asymptotic skeleton. The variance majorant is an assumption (its 1/√(n − 3) form is unsourced — see the module docstring); the theorem QPlancherelAsymptoticSkeleton derives the asymptotic vanishing of the fluctuations from it, rather than re-emitting it.

    • char_variance : ℕ → ℝ

      Variance of the character fluctuations χ_λ − 𝔼_q[χ] at strand count n, measured under the pushforward measure μ_n^{(q)}.

    • variance_nonneg (n : ℕ) : 0 ≤ char_variance n

      The variance is non-negative (it is a variance).

    • variance_majorant (n : ℕ) : 4 ≤ n → char_variance n ≤ 1 / √(↑n - 3)

      Assumed majorant: the character fluctuations have variance O(1/√(n − 3)). This is an ASSUMPTION of the context, not a cited theorem — see the module docstring: the 1/√(n − 3) form does not occur in Féray–Méliot, and no source for it has been identified. Renamed from feray_meliot_bound on 2026-09-05 so the name stops asserting a provenance the corpus cannot produce.

      The character fluctuations vanish in the large-n limit: char_variance n → 0.

      Derived by squeezing the assumed majorant 1/√(n − 3) (which itself tends to 0, proved here via Real.sqrt continuity at 0) against the non-negative variance. This is the measure-concentration content the manuscript's clauses 1–2 rest on — a consequence of the hypothesis, not a projection of it. The squeeze needs only that the majorant tends to 0; it does not depend on where the majorant came from, which is why the unsourced provenance does not touch the theorem.
