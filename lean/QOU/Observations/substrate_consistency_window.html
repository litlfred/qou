        Content statement (from manuscript):

        The QOU framework has one free parameter: the substrate parameter $q$. Every observable quantity --- masses, couplings, decay rates, causal structure --- is a function of $q$ alone. The parameter is overdetermined: independent classes of measurements each fix $q$ to a narrow range, and these ranges overlap.
