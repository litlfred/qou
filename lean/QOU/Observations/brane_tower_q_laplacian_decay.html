    QOU.Observations.brane_tower_q_laplacian_decay #

    Proposition (prop:brane-tower-q-laplacian-decay).

    Dropping the locality assumption for composite physical observables requires summing over the brane tower — an infinite jet prolongation T(ρ) = ∑_{n ≥ 1} ℏ_q^{2n} Δ⁽ⁿ⁾(ρ), where Δ⁽ⁿ⁾ is the n-th iterated q-Laplacian on the Reeb bundle. The remainder past the single-jet term decays geometrically with ratio q⁻² < 1 for q > 1.

    Disposition (derived, not projected) #

    The previous encoding postulated the boxed closed-form bound |T(ρ) − ℏ_q²Δ⁽¹⁾(ρ)| ≤ ℏ_q⁴‖ρ‖·q⁻²/(1 − q⁻²) directly as a class field remainder_bound; the "theorem" just rewrote it via ratio_eq and re-emitted it (pure self-assuming projection).

    Here the class carries the genuine inputs the physics supplies:

    • 0 ≤ r, r < 1 — the geometric ratio r = q⁻² is < 1 in the regime q > 1 (the exponential q-Laplacian mode damping);
    • remainder_le_geom — the tail T(ρ) − ℏ_q²Δ⁽¹⁾(ρ) is dominated, level by level, by the geometric majorant ∑' k, ℏ_q⁴‖ρ‖·r^{k+1} (the .md's displayed geometric-series line: each iterated Laplacian Δ⁽ⁿ⁾ on a fixed mode contributes ≲ ℏ_q⁴‖ρ‖·r^{k+1}).

    The theorem then derives the manuscript's closed form by actually summing the geometric series — ∑' k, M·r^{k+1} = M·r/(1 − r) (Mathlib tsum_geometric_of_lt_one), the exact = step of the .md's "Geometric-series tail" paragraph. The closed form is a consequence, not a hypothesis.

    class QOU.Observations.BraneTowerQLaplacianDecayContext :
    Type

    Dual-feasibility / decay inputs for the brane-tower bound. Every field is a genuine hypothesis supplied by the q-Laplacian spectrum; the manuscript's closed-form remainder bound is derived in brane_tower_q_laplacian_decay, not carried here.

    • hbar_q : ℝ

      Substrate q-Planck constant ℏ_q = (q − 1)/q.

    • rho_L2 : ℝ

      L² norm of the Reeb-bundle density ρ.

    • q_real : ℝ

      Substrate parameter q (archimedean, over ℝ: the bound is a real-valued norm inequality, §7c).

    • T_rho : ℝ

      The brane-tower sum T(ρ) = ∑_{n ≥ 1} ℏ_q^{2n} Δ⁽ⁿ⁾(ρ).

    • delta1_rho : ℝ

      The single-jet (n = 1) local Laplacian contribution Δ⁽¹⁾(ρ).

    • r : ℝ

      The geometric ratio of the tail decay, r = q⁻².

    • ratio_eq : r = q_real ^ (-2)

      The ratio is the substrate q⁻².

    • r_nonneg : 0 ≤ r

      Positivity: 0 ≤ r.

    • r_lt_one : r < 1

      Contraction: r < 1 (i.e. q > 1).

    • remainder_le_geom : |T_rho - hbar_q ^ 2 * delta1_rho| ≤ ∑' (k : ℕ), hbar_q ^ 4 * rho_L2 * r ^ (k + 1)

      Termwise exponential-decay input. The tail past the single-jet term is majorised, level by level, by the geometric series with coefficient M = ℏ_q⁴‖ρ‖ — the .md's displayed ∑ ℏ_q^{2n}·q^{-2(n-1)} bound. This is the genuine physical hypothesis (bounded iterated-Laplacian modes), NOT the closed form.
