    QOU.Observations.TraceLocalityMeetsGsNonLocality #

    The locality / non-locality reconciliation, encoded as a typeclass packaging the three structural ingredients of prop:trace-locality-meets-gs-non-locality:

    1. Markov-property locality — tr_M(x · σ_n) = z · tr_M(x).
    2. GS non-locality — normal-form depends on the full braid word.
    3. Product factorisation bridge — tr_M(NF(β)) = Π_i V̂_i(q), local in the normal-form word.

    The class fields carry concrete content (a Markov parameter z, a normal-form function, a vertex-volume sequence, and the three identities relating them).

    Resolves issue #649 §3.

    Type (max u v)

    Locality–non-locality reconciliation class.

    Given a Hecke-algebra-like carrier A with trace trM, normal-form function nf, and per-vertex local volume vertexVolume, packages the three identities of the reconciliation.

    • trM : A → R

      The Markov trace on A.

    • markovParameter : R

      The Markov parameter z = 1/(q^{1/2} + q^{-1/2}).

    • generator : ℕ → A

      A distinguished family of "single generators" (analogue of σ_i) producing the Markov-locality identity.

    • nfLength : A → ℕ

      A function returning the GS normal-form word length.

    • nfSymbolAt : A → ℕ → ℕ

      The normal-form symbol at position i of x (the $i$-th generator index of the GS normal-form word, or a sentinel when i ≥ nfLength x). Carrying this as a class field forces an instance to supply the actual normal-form data.

    • vertexVolume : ℕ → R

      Per-position vertex volume. Defined in terms of the normal-form symbol at that position, encoding the locality requirement at the type level.

    • markovLocality (x : A) (n : ℕ) : trM (x * generator R n) = markovParameter A * trM x

      (1) Markov locality: appending a single generator multiplies the trace by markovParameter.

    • vertexVolume_local (x y : A) (i : ℕ) : i < nfLength R x → i < nfLength R y → nfSymbolAt R x i = nfSymbolAt R y i → vertexVolume A (nfSymbolAt R x i) = vertexVolume A (nfSymbolAt R y i)

      (2) Locality of vertex volumes: the per-position vertex volume depends only on the normal-form symbol at that position — i.e. on nfSymbolAt x i, not on the rest of x. This is now a real property: any two elements x, y sharing a normal-form symbol at position i produce the same per-position contribution.

    • productFactorisation (x : A) : trM x = ∏ i ∈ Finset.range (nfLength R x), vertexVolume A (nfSymbolAt R x i)

      (3) Product factorisation: the Markov trace of the normal form equals the product of per-position vertex volumes, each depending only on its local normal-form symbol.
