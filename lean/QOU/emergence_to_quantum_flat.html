    QOU.emergence_to_quantum_flat — INN(G_q) uniqueness from F_A = B #

    Proposition (prop:emergence-to-quantum-flat).

    Let (𝐂, A, τ) be a quantum observable universe satisfying the categorical volume constraint, so that G_q = Aut^⊗(τ) ≅ SU_q(2) ([thm:su2-emergence]). Then the inner-automorphism 2-group INN(G_q) is the unique strict 2-group whose quantum-flatness condition F_A = B reproduces the instanton equation F_A⁻ = 0 under the simplicity constraint.

    The Lean encoding records the uniqueness as a ∀ G', QuantumFlat G' → G' ≅ INN(G_q) claim against an abstract EmergenceContext carrying the relevant categorical data.
