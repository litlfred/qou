    Weight systems #

    The reduced chord-diagram space 𝒜 R n is the free module on chord diagrams modulo the one- and four-term relations (FourTermRelation.lean):

    𝒜 R n := CDModule R n ⧸ relationsSubmodule R.

    A weight system of degree n is an R-linear functional on 𝒜 R n, equivalently a functional on CDModule R n that vanishes on both the 1T and 4T relators. Weight systems are the targets of the symbol map of a finite-type invariant (FiniteTypeInvariant.lean); the fundamental theorem that the symbol of a finite-type invariant is a weight system, and Kontsevich's converse, are stated as honest conjecture classes there / in MilnorVassilievBridge.lean.

    All results here hold for an arbitrary relations submodule, so they are independent of the exact 4T encoding (module docstring of FourTermRelation.lean).

    References #

    • Bar-Natan, On the Vassiliev knot invariants, Topology 34 (1995) 423–472.

            Weight systems as functionals on the free module #

            Precomposing a weight system with the projection gives a functional on the free chord-diagram module that vanishes on the relations; conversely any such functional descends to a weight system. This is the "vanishes on 1T and 4T" characterization.
