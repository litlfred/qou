    The one- and four-term relations on chord diagrams #

    Weight systems are linear functionals on the free module spanned by chord diagrams that vanish on the one-term (1T, framing-independence) and four-term (4T) relators. This file builds:

    • CDModule R n — the free R-module on ChordDiagram n.
    • oneTermSubmodule — spanned by diagrams carrying an isolated (adjacent- endpoint) chord; the 1T / framing-independence relation.
    • fourTermSubmodule — the four-term relation, encoded in commutator form through the marked-point relabelling action (ChordDiagram.conj), i.e. as the image of the infinitesimal-braid (Kohno–Drinfeld) relation [tᵢⱼ, tᵢₖ + tⱼₖ] = 0.
    • relationsSubmodule — their join 1T ⊔ 4T; the reduced diagram space 𝒜 (WeightSystem.lean) is the quotient by it.

    Honest scope of the 4T encoding #

    The 1T submodule is a faithful, elementary encoding of framing independence (a chord with adjacent endpoints is genuinely isolated). The 4T submodule is a specific combinatorial encoding of the four-term relation via endpoint slides; its exact faithfulness to Bar-Natan's Figure-3 4T (up to sign / indexing conventions) is a design choice, not a theorem, and is filed for cross-check. Crucially, no lemma in this scaffold depends on the exact form of fourTermSubmodule — every structural result (WeightSystem.lean) is proved for an arbitrary relations submodule, so nothing false is asserted by the encoding.

    References #

    • Bar-Natan, On the Vassiliev knot invariants, Topology 34 (1995) 423–472.
    • Chmutov–Duzhin–Mostovoy, Introduction to Vassiliev Knot Invariants, CUP 2012.
    • Kohno, Monodromy representations of braid groups and Yang–Baxter equations, Ann. Inst. Fourier 37 (1987) 139–160.

        The one-term (framing-independence) relation #

        A chord whose two endpoints are cyclically adjacent (pairing i = i + 1) is isolated: no other chord crosses it. Framing independence sends any diagram carrying such a chord to zero.

              The four-term relation (commutator form) #

              The adjacent transposition sᵢ swaps marked points i and i+1. Relabelling a chord diagram by sᵢ (ChordDiagram.conj) slides the endpoint at i across its neighbour. The four-term relator generated by D and a pair of adjacent transpositions sᵢ, sⱼ is the commutator-shaped alternating sum

              ⟨D⟩ − ⟨D·sᵢ⟩ − ⟨D·sⱼ⟩ + ⟨D·sᵢsⱼ⟩,

              the image of the Kohno–Drinfeld relation [tᵢⱼ, tᵢₖ+tⱼₖ]=0. (See the honest- scope note in the module docstring: this is a specific encoding; no theorem depends on its exact form.)
