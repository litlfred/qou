    Finite-type (Vassiliev) invariants #

    A knot invariant v : K → R extends to singular knots (immersions with transverse double points) by the Vassiliev skein relation

    v(⨉) = v(over-crossing) − v(under-crossing),

    resolving each double point into the signed difference of its two smoothings. v has order ≤ m when this extension vanishes on every singular knot with more than m double points.

    This file formalizes the notion abstractly, over a VassilievSetup: the datum of a type of k-fold singular objects together with their resolution into a signed sum of knots and the one-double-point desingularization realizing the skein relation. We prove the genuinely definitional structural facts:

    • vExt is R-linear in the invariant v;
    • the extension of a (k+1)-singular object is the difference of the extensions of its two k-singular smoothings (vExt_desingularize);
    • order-≤ m invariants form a submodule finiteTypeSubmodule m of K → R, and these are filtered: finiteTypeSubmodule m ≤ finiteTypeSubmodule m' for m ≤ m'.

    The symbol of an order-≤ m invariant is its value on the m-singular objects; the fundamental theorem that the symbol descends to a weight system on chord diagrams (WeightSystem.lean), and Kontsevich's converse, are stated as honest conjecture classes in MilnorVassilievBridge.lean — they are not proved here and not axiomatized.

    References #

    • Bar-Natan, On the Vassiliev knot invariants, Topology 34 (1995) 423–472, https://doi.org/10.1016/0040-9383(95)93237-2.
    • Vassiliev, Cohomology of knot spaces, in Theory of Singularities and its Applications, Adv. Soviet Math. 1 (1990) 23–69.

    structure QOU.KnotTheory.Vassiliev.VassilievSetup (K : Type u) :
    Type (max u (v + 1))

    Data presenting the singular-knot resolution underlying finite-type theory for a type K of knots.

    • Sing k — objects with exactly k transverse double points (Sing 0 = K morally).
    • resolve s — the full resolution of every double point into a signed sum of honest knots (the Vassiliev skein, applied at all k double points).
    • desingularize s — resolve one distinguished double point of a (k+1)-singular object into its positive and negative smoothings, each a k-singular object.
    • resolve_step — the skein relation at the distinguished double point: resolve s = resolve s₊ − resolve s₋.
    • Sing : ℕ → Type v

      k-fold singular objects.

    • resolve {k : ℕ} : self.Sing k → K →₀ ℤ

      Full resolution into a signed formal sum of knots.

    • desingularize {k : ℕ} : self.Sing (k + 1) → self.Sing k × self.Sing k

      Resolve one distinguished double point into (positive, negative) smoothings.

    • resolve_step {k : ℕ} (s : self.Sing (k + 1)) : self.resolve s = self.resolve (self.desingularize s).1 - self.resolve (self.desingularize s).2

      The Vassiliev skein relation at the distinguished double point.

            The symbol map (degree-m part) #

            The symbol of an invariant records its value on the m-singular objects. For an order-≤ m invariant the symbol is the top-degree data; the fundamental theorem that it descends to a weight system on ChordDiagram m (via the chord diagram of the double-point pattern) is the honest conjecture class in MilnorVassilievBridge.lean.
