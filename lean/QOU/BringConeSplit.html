    The split model of the Bring-cone Reeb triad — 𝔰𝔩₂(ℝ), one sign away from 𝔰𝔲(2) #

    QOU.BringCone models "the Reeb triad of the Bring cone link" by right-multiplication by the units of the quaternions and proves [M_i, M_j] = 2 M_k and cyclic — three +2's, the compact form 𝔰𝔲(2). That is the triad of the Hopf link S³ of the cone over ℙ¹.

    The link of the Bring cone is not S³. Bézout (1·2·3 = 6) and adjunction (K = 𝒪(1)) make the embedding canonical, so the link — the unit circle bundle of 𝒪(-1)|_C = K⁻¹ = TC — is the unit tangent bundle UT(Σ₄), an ~SL₂ manifold (docs/audits/2026-09-03-bring-cone-link-is-the-unit-tangent-bundle.md). A 3-Sasakian 3-manifold is a spherical space form, and a closed aspherical 3-manifold admits no non-trivial SU(2)-action at all, so no triad of Reeb vector fields on UT(Σ₄) can close into 𝔰𝔲(2). What UT(Σ₄) does carry is three genuine contact forms — the connection form (Reeb field: fibre rotation V), the Liouville form (Reeb field: geodesic flow X) and its rotation by a quarter turn in the fibre (Reeb field: X⊥) — whose Reeb fields are the left-invariant frame of PSL(2,ℝ) and close into 𝔰𝔩₂(ℝ):

    [X, X⊥] = V,     [X⊥, V] = X,     [V, X] = −X⊥ .

    Exactly one of the three cyclic brackets carries the opposite sign to 𝔰𝔲(2). That is the whole difference between the compact and the split real form, and it is what the word "degenerate" in "degenerate quaternionic-contact" has to mean if the lemma is to describe this link at all.

    This file makes the point in the lemma's own terms. Replace the quaternions by the split quaternions — i² = −1, j² = +1, so k² = +1; the algebra M₂(R), whose unit-norm elements are SL(2, R) and whose pure-imaginary part is 𝔰𝔩₂(R) — and run the same right-multiplication construction. The same proof script gives

    [M_i, M_j] = 2 M_k,     [M_j, M_k] = −2 M_i,     [M_k, M_i] = 2 M_j ,

    one sign flipped, and split_ne_compact records that the flipped bracket is not the compact one in any ring of characteristic zero. So the conciliation works only as the split form: the lemma's identities are true, and they are the identities of the wrong real form for UT(Σ₄). The 𝔰𝔲(2) survives on this link only as the structure group of the ℍ-valued resolvent bundle — an internal symmetry, which is how rem:tetraplectic-su2-gauge-reading already reads it — not as a triad of Reeb fields.

    Pure algebra over a generic CommRing, exactly like BringCone.lean; nothing archimedean enters. Bean qou-m3sh.

            The structure constants of the two models, side by side: compact (2, 2, 2), split (2, −2, 2). Read as a Lie algebra on the imaginary units, the first is 𝔰𝔲(2) and the second is 𝔰𝔩₂(R); a real rescaling of the basis cannot move between them.
