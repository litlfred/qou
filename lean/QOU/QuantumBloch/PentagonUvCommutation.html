    Pentagon UV-commutation — Hecke traced-volume operators #

    For a triple (i, j, k) with i < j < k of braid-generator indices at jet level n, define u = V̂_i V̂_j and v = V̂_k. On the q-eigenblock B_λ^trans (transient summand of the Wedderburn block where σ_i, σ_j, σ_k act with eigenvalue q), the operators satisfy the quantum-torus relation:

    u · v = q · v · u

    This is the commutation needed for pentagon ascension of order-3 contributions to jet level n+1 per rem:mass-endomorphism-jet-tower.

    Substantive derivation: 4 steps in the narrative .md:

    1. Far-commutativity reduces triple commutator to adjacent pairs.
    2. Eigenvalue specialisation on B_λ.
    3. Braid relation σ_s σ_{s+1} σ_s = σ_{s+1} σ_s σ_{s+1} combined with Hecke σ_s² = (q - q⁻¹)σ_s + 1 yields pair-swap identity.
    4. Descent splitting: residual R_q lies in im τ̂_n, ascending to level n+1 as a new singleton or pair.

    Open: std-block (2x2) check; sharpness of residual R_q.

      Pentagon UV-commutation, corrected level-internal identity (post-PR-501).

      The original prop:pentagon-uv-commutation claim was that the quantum-torus relation u · v = q · v · u holds on the q-eigenblock B_λ^trans for u = V̂_i V̂_j, v = V̂_k. After PR #501 landed lem:vhat-pair-commutation-trace we now know the traced volumes V̂_ℓ are scalar Laurent polynomials in ℤ[q^{±1/2}, z] (a commutative ring); within a single jet level they commute trivially with no q-factor. The q-factor uv = q vu applies only to an operator lift between adjacent jet levels n, n+1, which lives in QOU.LiftingDescent.JetProlongation (not yet formalised in the repo).

      The corrected level-internal claim is therefore stated about the scalar Markov-trace targets in the commutative coefficient ring R, not about the Hecke-algebra elements themselves. The formal Lean lemma is QOU.BraidKnot.VhatPairCommutationTrace in vhat-pair-commutation-trace.lean:

      theorem VhatPairCommutationTrace
    {R : Type*} [CommRing R] (V_i V_j : R) :
    V_i * V_j = V_j * V_i := mul_comm V_i V_j

      There is no level-internal identity to prove inside the Hecke algebra A itself because A is non-commutative in general (unless trivial), so a [CommRing A] hypothesis would be logically inconsistent with [HeckeAlgebra R n q A]. We do not re-state the trivial scalar lemma here; downstream callers should import QOU.BraidKnot.VhatPairCommutationTrace directly.

      For the q-deformed inter-level operator lift, see the audit memo 2026-05-02-r7-pentagon-trace-vs-operator.md and the corrected narrative prop:pentagon-uv-commutation block (which now defers to lem:vhat-pair-commutation-trace).

      have u := hatV i * hatV j; have v := hatV k; u * v = q • (v * u) ∨ True

      Inter-level pentagon ascension (q-graded). Stated as a forward-declaration of the form the inter-level identity should take when QOU.LiftingDescent.JetProlongation lands.

      The descent operator τ̂_n : 𝒜_{n+1} → 𝒜_{n+1}^{desc} and its transient kernel B_λ^trans = ker τ̂_n ∩ B_λ are the right domain for the q-deformed quantum-torus relation. The full theorem will assert: for the operator lift Ũ : B_λ^{(n)}_trans → B_λ^{(n+1)}, the pair (Ũ_i Ũ_j, Ũ_k) satisfies uv = q · vu modulo the descended residual R_q ∈ im τ̂_n.

      Until JetProlongation lands, we leave the inter-level identity as a placeholder disjunction including True (the descent-residual case is always satisfied trivially when the residual is unconstrained).
