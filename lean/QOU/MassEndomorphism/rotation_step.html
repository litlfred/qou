        theorem QOU.MassEndomorphism.rotation_step {R : Type u_1} [CommRing R] (σ : R →+* R) {ι q x h0 h1 h2 : R} (hι : ι * ι = -1) (hq : σ q * q = 1) (n : ℕ) :
        evenStep (σ q) (ι * x) n (ι ^ n * h0) (ι ^ (n + 1) * h1) (ι ^ (n + 2) * h2) ↔ oddStep q x n h0 h1 h2

        The channel rotation, categorical (the x ↦ ιx, q ↦ q̄ rotation). A sequence h satisfies the odd-channel (non-compact, 𝔔_β) step at (q, x) iff its ι-rotation n ↦ ι^n·h_n satisfies the even-channel (compact, binding) step at the bar-conjugated data (σq, ιx) — where ι is a formal square root of −1 in the fibre ring and σ q * q = 1 is the division-free bar involution.

        This is the generic form of Askey (1989) / Ismail–Masson h_n(x|q) = i^{-n}·H_n(ix|q^{-1}): same eigenfunctions, rotated; the two channels of 𝔙 are one recurrence read through the rotation. No ℂ: the archimedean instance ι = Complex.I is downstream of τ.
