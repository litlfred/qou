    z-Habiro Cauchy-limit: the descending-chain / increment-coherence core #

    This file advances the algebraic Cauchy-limit half of conj:brane-tower-z-habiro (folio/quantum-observable-universe/mass-theory/brane-tower-z-habiro.md §"Statement", §"What survives the closure" item 1).

    The convergence prerequisite — that the analytic infinite product Φ_q(z) = ∏_{k≥1} (1 - q^{-k} z)^{-1} is Multipliable for 1 < q, |z| < q — is already proved sorry-free in QOU.MassEndomorphism.PhiQConvergence.quantumDilog_multipliable.

    The remaining open half is algebraic: the truncated partial sums

    Φ̃^(N)(β) := Σ_{i=1}^N Trunc_{<n}[Φ_q(V̂_i)] ∈ ℤ[q^±1][z] / (P_n(q,z))

    are claimed to be Cauchy in the z-Habiro topology — the cyclotomic completion of ℤ[q^±1][z] at the descending chain of ideals I_n = (P_n(q,z)), where

    P_n(q,z) := ∏_{k=1}^{n} (1 - q^{-k} z) (def:z-habiro-ring)

    and the limit exists as an algebraic element of the inverse limit varprojlim_n ℤ[q^±1][z] / (P_n).
