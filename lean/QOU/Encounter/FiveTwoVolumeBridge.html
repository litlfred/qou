    theorem QOU.Encounter.FiveTwoVolumeBridge.vol_lt_iff_of_realises {M : Type u_1} [MathlibExt.HyperbolicVolumeBridge M] {N : Type u_2} [MathlibExt.HyperbolicVolumeBridge N] (m : M) (n : N) (hm : MathlibExt.HyperbolicVolumeBridge.Realises Knot.FigureEightTri.figureEightTriangulation m) (hn : MathlibExt.HyperbolicVolumeBridge.Realises Knot.FiveTwoVolume.fiveTwoTriangulation n) :
    MathlibExt.HyperbolicVolumeBridge.vol m < MathlibExt.HyperbolicVolumeBridge.vol n ↔ Knot.FigureEightVolume.VOL_4_1 < Knot.FiveTwoVolume.VOL_5_2

    The 4₁ and 5₂ volumes are comparable through the bridge.

    If one manifold is realised by the 4₁ triangulation and another by the 5₂ triangulation, their volumes stand in the same relation as the two exact constants. Stated as an equivalence because it is the transport statement: it says the bridge carries the comparison faithfully, whatever the comparison turns out to be. The comparison itself is now proved — vol_lt_of_realises below discharges the right-hand side — but the two are worth keeping separate, because MostowRigidity's version of the same comparison discharges by norm_num on the two truncated literals, which is a statement about the decimals and not about the constants.
