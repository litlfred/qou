    The Mostow volume invariant equals the triangulation sum, under the bridge.

    Composes three inputs: compat (the two notions agree), the bridge's transport vol_eq_of_realises, and the definition of volumeInvariant. None of them alone gives the conclusion.

    compat is an explicit argument rather than a class field on purpose — a field would be inferred silently at every use site and would read as established.

    The figure-eight invariant IS the computed Bloch–Wigner sum.

    RESOLVED 2026-08-30 (bean qou-f8lit). This theorem previously concluded (2.02988321281930 : ℝ) = T.volume, keyed on the decimal literal MostowRigidity.figureEight used to carry, and was unusable at the figure-eight: that literal is a 15-digit truncation, figureEightTriangulation.volume = VOL_4_1 is proved sorry-free, and the two differ at the 16th digit — so its hypotheses were jointly unsatisfiable there and no correct bridge instance could ever discharge them.

    The instance is now exact (volume := VOL_4_1), so the statement is keyed on the exact constant and applies. The typechecker made the defect visible at exactly this line: before exactification volumeInvariant FigureEight was definitionally the literal, so the coercion went through silently; afterwards Lean reported

    has type      volumeInvariant FigureEight = T.volume
but expected  2.02988321281930 = T.volume

    which is the whole finding in one error message. Worth remembering: a truncated literal standing in for an exact constant is invisible while it is the definition.
