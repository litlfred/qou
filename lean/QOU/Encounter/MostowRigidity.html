    QOU.Encounter.MostowRigidity #

    Conditional-class axiomatisation of Mostow rigidity for the geometric face of confinement (§3b conditional-class pattern).

    What this module provides #

    Non-trivial scaffold for the geometric-rigidity class:

    • MostowRigidity — class whose fields are real numerical invariants (volume, mostow_gap) plus their non-negativity / positivity witnesses. No True placeholders remain on the class itself; the class fields encode the operational content of the rigidity statement at the level needed by downstream conditional theorems.

    • mass_rigidity_of_mostow — fully proved conditional theorem. Takes [MostowRigidity M] and concludes that the volume invariant is non-negative and the deformation gap is strictly positive — i.e. the categorical mass eigenvalue attached to M is isolated.

    • MostowRigidity.unknot — concrete instance for the unknot complement (degenerate hyperbolic case: volume 0 by Thurston's convention, with a conservative gap to the smallest non-trivial hyperbolic volume Vol(4₁) ≈ 2.0299). Witnesses that the class has at least one closed instance.

    • MostowRigidity.figureEight — concrete instance for the figure-eight knot complement (4₁): the smallest known hyperbolic 3-manifold complement, volume ≈ 2.02988 (Cao–Meyerhoff 2001), with Mostow gap conservatively below the next-smallest gap Vol(5₂) − Vol(4₁) ≈ 0.798.

    • MostowRigidity.knot5_2 — concrete instance for the 5₂ knot complement: second-smallest known orientable cusped hyperbolic 3-manifold, volume ≈ 2.82812 (literature constant; Adams– Hildebrand–Weeks 1991, SnapPy verified).

    • unknot_volume_lt_figureEight_volume, figureEight_volume_lt_knot5_2_volume — fully-proved comparison theorems exhibiting non-trivial uses of volumeInvariant.

    • mass_eigenvalue_isolated — example downstream consumer that takes [MostowRigidity M] and concludes 0 < gap; new instances satisfy this theorem automatically (§3b conditional-class consumer pattern).

    Categorical content #

    Mostow rigidity is the geometric mirror of SDPSlaterUniqueness (QOU.MathlibExt.SDPRealUniqueness):

    Side Uniqueness witness What it pins
    Algebraic SDPSlaterUniqueness Primal SDP optimum Φ*
    Geometric MostowRigidity Hyperbolic volume of M

    The bridge gb_reduction_pl_thimble_bijection (in QOU.PicardLefschetz) realises one as the τ-image of the other under the archimedean-realization-functor.

    Status #

    The class fields are now substantive:

    • volume : ℝ — the hyperbolic volume of the (intended) complete finite-volume 3-manifold associated to M. Real-valued; no abstraction over hyperbolic structures yet. Convention: non-hyperbolic complements (Seifert-fibred, toroidal) take volume = 0, matching Thurston's extension of Vol to the whole knot complement spectrum.
    • volume_nonneg : 0 ≤ volume — non-negativity witness.
    • mostow_gap : ℝ — strictly-positive lower bound on the distance from volume to the next distinct value in the hyperbolic-volume spectrum. This is the operational content of the rigidity statement: distinct knot types are separated by a strictly positive volume gap.
    • mostow_gap_pos : 0 < mostow_gap — positivity witness.

    Concrete instances supply both reals and both positivity witnesses; the §3b "hard work" deferred to Mathlib's geometric-topology layer is the full discreteness theorem asserting that mostow_gap matches the actual gap to the next hyperbolic volume. Downstream theorems use only the witnesses, not the absolute value.

    class QOU.Encounter.MostowRigidity.MostowRigidity (M : Type u_1) :
    Type

    Mostow rigidity (conditional class).

    A MostowRigidity M instance attaches to M a hyperbolic volume invariant volume together with a strictly positive Mostow gap mostow_gap to the next distinct value in the volume spectrum.

    Operationally this is what downstream theorems consume: rigidity of the volume (its bare existence as a class field) plus discreteness of the spectrum (the strictly positive gap). Concrete instances must supply both reals and both positivity witnesses; the §3b "hard work" of proving that mostow_gap matches the actual gap to the next hyperbolic volume is deferred to Mathlib's geometric-topology layer.

    Ref: [mostow1968]

    • volume : ℝ

      Hyperbolic volume invariant of M. Convention: take volume = 0 for non-hyperbolic complements.

    • volume_nonneg : 0 ≤ volume M

      Volume is non-negative.

    • mostow_gap : ℝ

      Strictly positive lower bound on the distance from volume to the next distinct value in the hyperbolic-volume spectrum. Encodes Mostow's discreteness witness.

    • mostow_gap_pos : 0 < mostow_gap M

      The Mostow gap is strictly positive. This is the operational content of "no nearby hyperbolic structures".

      theorem QOU.Encounter.MostowRigidity.mass_rigidity_of_mostow {M : Type u_1} [h : MostowRigidity M] :
      0 ≤ MostowRigidity.volume M ∧ MostowRigidity.volume M < MostowRigidity.volume M + MostowRigidity.mostow_gap M

      Mass rigidity from Mostow — fully-proved downstream conditional theorem.

      Given [MostowRigidity M] for the knot complement M, the categorical mass eigenvalue (which factors through the hyperbolic volume via the algebraic-quantum-volume bridge) is well-defined (non-negative volume) and isolated: the next distinct value in the volume spectrum lies strictly above volume, separated by the Mostow gap.

      Both conjuncts are derived, not projected: the first is the non-negativity witness, and the second — volume is strictly below its gap-separated successor volume + mostow_gap — is derived from the strict positivity of the gap (lt_add_of_pos_right). Composing the two fields rather than re-exporting mostow_gap_pos verbatim is what makes this a genuine rigidity consequence (separation of the eigenvalue from the rest of the spectrum) rather than a self-assuming projection (AGENTS.md §3b-cond item 5).

      This is the §3b-cond rigorous-conditional companion to the prop:mostow-confinement content block; concrete instances of the class supply the rigidity proof for specific knot complements.

                    instance QOU.Encounter.MostowRigidity.unknot :
                    MostowRigidity Unknot

                    Concrete instance: unknot complement (degenerate hyperbolic case).

                    By Thurston's extension of Vol to the whole knot-complement spectrum, non-hyperbolic complements are assigned volume 0. The unknot complement is the solid torus, which is Seifert-fibred and not hyperbolic; its conventional volume is therefore 0. The Mostow gap is bounded below by 1 — a conservative under-approximation of Vol(4_1) ≈ 2.0299, the smallest known hyperbolic 3-manifold volume (Cao–Meyerhoff 2001).

                    Ref: [thurston1978] §6 (volume of non-hyperbolic complements by convention)

                    noncomputable instance QOU.Encounter.MostowRigidity.figureEight :
                    MostowRigidity FigureEight

                    Concrete instance: figure-eight knot complement (4_1).

                    The complement S^3 \ 4_1 is the smallest known orientable cusped hyperbolic 3-manifold (Cao–Meyerhoff 2001), with hyperbolic volume

                    Vol(4_1) = 2 · Cl₂(π/3) = 6 · Λ(π/3) ≈ 2.02988...

                    where Cl₂ is the Clausen function and Λ the Lobachevsky function, Λ(θ) = Cl₂(2θ)/2. The class field stores the 15-significant-digit truncation 2.02988321281930 (kept in lock-step with the canonical 50-dps witness via :leanval[Vol_4_1]{precision=15}); the direction of the truncation does not affect the class invariants.

                    The Mostow gap is bounded below by 0.5, a conservative under-approximation of the next-smallest gap

                    Vol(5_2) - Vol(4_1) ≈ 2.82812 - 2.02988 ≈ 0.798.

                    Ref: [thurston1978]; Cao–Meyerhoff, The orientable cusped hyperbolic 3-manifolds of minimum volume, Invent. Math. 146 (2001) 451–478.

                    Volume comparison: unknot < figure-eight.

This is the smallest non-trivial volume comparison in the hyperbolic-volume spectrum and the first non-degenerate use of volumeInvariant.

                    noncomputable instance QOU.Encounter.MostowRigidity.knot5_2 :
                    MostowRigidity Knot5_2

                    Concrete instance: 5_2 knot complement.

                    The complement S^3 \ 5_2 is the second-smallest known orientable cusped hyperbolic 3-manifold (after S^3 \ 4_1), with hyperbolic volume

                    Vol(5_2) ≈ 2.82812208833078

                    (SnapPy ManifoldHP 50 dps, 2026-06-12; witness field vol_5_2_decimal_50. The former Adams-Hildebrand-Weeks 1991 15-dps anchor 2.82812208833410 carried a digit-13–15 transcription error.)

                    The Mostow gap is bounded below by 0.3.

                    This was 0.5, and 0.5 is not a lower bound. The field is documented as a lower bound on the distance to the next distinct spectrum value, and the next value above Vol(5_2) is Vol(6_1) ≈ 3.16396, i.e. a gap of 0.33584. The old docstring called 0.5 "well under the nearest-neighbour gaps ... Vol(6_1) - Vol(5_2) ≈ 0.336" while quoting that very figure — 0.5 > 0.336, so the value contradicted the sentence that justified it. figureEight's 0.5 is sound by contrast: its neighbour up is Vol(5_2), a gap of 0.798.

                    Nothing in Lean broke, because the class carries only mostow_gap_pos and explicitly defers the discreteness claim to Mathlib's geometric-topology layer (see the class docstring). That is exactly why it went unnoticed: the field's documented meaning is not enforced by its stated obligation, so an agent trusting the doc would conclude no hyperbolic volume lies within 0.5 of Vol(5_2), and Vol(6_1) does.

                    Ref: Adams, Hildebrand, Weeks, Hyperbolic invariants of knots and links, Trans. AMS 326 (1991) 1–56.

                    theorem QOU.Encounter.MostowRigidity.mass_eigenvalue_isolated (M : Type u_1) [h : MostowRigidity M] :
                    0 < MostowRigidity.mostow_gap M

                    Downstream consumer: mass eigenvalue is isolated.

                    Generic theorem in the §3b conditional-class consumer pattern: takes [MostowRigidity M] as a typeclass hypothesis and concludes that the mass eigenvalue attached to M is isolated by a strictly positive Mostow gap.

                    Any new instance of MostowRigidity automatically validates this theorem without rewriting the proof — that is the operational pay-off of the §3b conditional-class pattern. Three closed inhabitants exist in this module (Unknot, FigureEight, Knot5_2); each immediately discharges the hypothesis and yields a closed conclusion.
