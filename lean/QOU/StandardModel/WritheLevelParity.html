        1. The writhe has the parity of the word length #

        source
        theorem QOU.StandardModel.LevelParity.writhe_sub_length_even (β : BraidWord) (h : ∀ g ∈ β, g ≠ 0) :
        Even (writhe β - ↑(List.length β))

        writhe β ≡ length β (mod 2).

        Every letter of a braid word is a nonzero generator, so contributes ±1 to the writhe and 1 to the length; ±1 − 1 ∈ {0, −2} is even. This is the one framework-side step of the bridge to component parity, and it is unconditional.

          4. The bridge to component parity #

          source
          theorem QOU.StandardModel.LevelParity.component_parity_of_permSign (β : BraidWord) (hne : ∀ g ∈ β, g ≠ 0) (n c : ℤ) (hcycle : Even (↑(List.length β) - (n - c))) :
          Even (writhe β - n + c)

          (w − n) mod 2 is the component parity of the closure.

          Conditional on the classical cycle-sign identity, carried as hcycle: the underlying permutation of β ∈ B_n is a product of length β transpositions (one per letter), and a permutation of n letters with c cycles has sign (−1)^{n−c}; equating the two exponents gives length β ≡ n − c (mod 2). Combined with writhe_sub_length_even this yields c ≡ w − n (mod 2).

          The hypothesis is the only unproved input, and it is textbook. -- Ref: [kassel2008] https://doi.org/10.1007/978-0-387-68548-9 (§1.3, braid -- group to symmetric group; §1.1, parity of permutations)

                          6. REFUTATION of the statistics reading #

                          source
                          theorem QOU.StandardModel.LevelParity.statistics_reading_fails_on_gluon :
                          components 3 borromean % 2 = 1 ∧ components 2 (sigma1Pow 3) % 2 = 1 ∧ components 3 gluonUnit % 2 = 1

                          Component parity is not fermion/boson statistics.

                          The tempting physical reading — odd component count ↔ fermion — matches the leptons (σ₁, σ₁³: one component, odd) and the Borromean baryon (three components, odd), all fermions. It fails on the gluon unit: σ₁σ₂⁻¹ ∈ B₃ has underlying permutation a 3-cycle, so its closure is a single component, hence odd, hence "fermion" — but the gluon is a boson.

                          So carrier_component_parity is a theorem about permutation cycles with no framework content, and the statistics reading of it is refuted. Scope-(a): this refutes this reading of component parity, not the possibility that some other level-independent datum tracks statistics.

                          7. The minimal-level ruling closes the lepton-number escape #

                          source
                          theorem QOU.StandardModel.LevelParity.leptonNumber_collapses_at_minimal_level (NF : BraidWord → BraidWord) (f : BraidWord → ℤ) (hnu : NF [1] = []) (hanu : NF [-1] = []) (hparticle : f (NF [1]) = 1) (hanti : f (NF [-1]) = -1) :
                          False

                          L = sign(writhe) does not survive the minimal-level ruling either.

                          ChiralFermionBraidWords.lean rescued L from LeptonNumberCarrierChirality.lean's amphichirality obstruction by taking the carrier to be the braid word rather than the knot type: νₑ = σ₁ and ν̄ₑ = σ₁⁻¹ are distinct words, so the mirror acts freely on them even though both close to the amphichiral unknot.

                          The owner ruling in WritheMarkovInvariance.lean — states are Gröbner–Shirshov normal forms, and the observable is evaluated at the lowest level of the tower — closes that escape. The corpus assigns νₑ = K_{0₁} = T(2,1), the unknot, whose braid index is 1; its minimal-level normal form is the empty word, and so is its mirror's. At the level where the framework says observables live, the neutrino and the antineutrino are the same state, and no function of the state can give them +1 and −1.

                          The two hypotheses are exactly the two braid-index facts, carried explicitly. The result is that L must come from something other than the writhe — the Markov-II failure (leptonNumber_not_markov_invariant) and this one leave no reading in which sign(w) is lepton number.

                          Scope-(a): L = sign(writhe) is refuted under both readings of the gauge freedom. Lepton number itself, and the Y = B − L route, are untouched.
