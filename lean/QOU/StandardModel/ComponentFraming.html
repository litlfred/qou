    Per-strand twists are per-component framings, and that constrains the multiset #

    Bean qou-kebu (T2b). Companion to TwistSeparationContent.lean.

    Why the bean's proposed route cannot work as written #

    qou-kebu proposes deriving the per-strand twist multisets from the ribbon structure:

    1. The twist θ_V = q^{c_V} acts on each strand by the Casimir
    2. For T(2,p) = σ₁^p, the total twist is p·θ_V
    3. The per-strand multiset is determined by the braid monodromy

    Step 1 cannot deliver step 3. The corpus's own probe states the reason: "On a simple object of a ribbon category the twist theta acts by a scalar; for Hecke / quantum-sl_N the exponent is the content sum of the partition" (ribbon_twist_charge_quantum_probe.py:13-17). A scalar attached to an object is one number. The battery's answers are non-constant multisets — u = {0,1,1}, d = {-1,-1,1} — and no scalar produces a non-constant multiset over identical strands. That probe tested Q = c(λ)/3 directly and it failed; this is the structural reason it had to.

    θ is the weight per unit framing (q^{c_V t_i}), not the framing t_i. Deriving the multiset needs something that fixes the integers, and θ is not it.

    What does fix them: the closure map #

    The mechanism the bean's step 3 gestures at is real, but it is the permutation, not the twist. In a framed link the framing is one integer per component, not per strand. Closing a braid β ∈ B_n joins strand i to strand π(β)(i), so the strands in one cycle of π(β) become one component — and a framing that is well defined on the link must take the same value on all of them.

    That is IsComponentFraming below: t ∘ π = t. It is not an extra assumption about ribbons; it is what "the framing of a framed link" means, transported to strand indices.

    The falsifiable content #

    Component-constancy forces the multiset's shape to be built from cycle lengths, which turns each battery answer into a statement about the fermion's braid word:

    • constant_of_transitive — if π is transitive (a single n-cycle, i.e. the closure is a knot), the framing is constant, so the multiset is {a,…,a}.
    • eq_one_of_injective — if the multiset has all entries distinct, then π = 1: the closure is a totally split n-component link.

    Applied to the four battery survivors (strand-twist-y-battery.witness.json):

    fermion multiset forced
    u {0,1,1} π is not transitive — u's closure is not a knot
    d {-1,-1,1}, {-1,0,0} same
    ν {-1,0,1} π = 1 — totally split
    ν {0,0,0} unconstrained
    e {-1,-1,-1} unconstrained (constant fits any π)

    These are predictions about the braid words, checkable the moment those words exist. They are also the first constraints in this arc that are derived rather than enumerated: the battery searched 10 000 assignments and reported which survive; this says which are possible given a braid, before any search.

    Scope #

    Scope-(a) on the route, scope-(b) on the one step that a proved invariant excludes. Excluded, by the scalar/multiset mismatch above: θ alone determining a non-constant per-strand multiset. Not excluded: the twist entering as the weight q^{c_V t_i} (its actual role), the hypercharge programme, or qou-kebu itself — this reformulates its step 3 rather than closing it.

    The blocking input is unchanged and is recorded in the battery's own witness: the corpus carries no per-chiral-fermion framed braid words (htw2). Until those exist the table above cannot be evaluated — which is precisely why it is worth stating now, as the test those words will face.

    def QOU.StandardModel.ComponentFraming.IsComponentFraming {n : ℕ} (π : Equiv.Perm (Fin n)) (t : Fin n → ℤ) :
    Prop

    A component framing: a per-strand integer that is constant along the closure, i.e. invariant under the braid's underlying permutation.

    Strands i and π i are joined by the closure, so they lie on one component of the resulting link; a framing of that link assigns them the same integer.

      Against the 1·2·2·1 residual of htw2 #

      htw2 reduces the battery's four survivors to a sharp statement: the SM spectrum pins each fermion's total twist ((u,d,ν,e) = (2,−1,0,−3) = 3Q), and the four survivors are just the {−1,0,1}-multisets realising those totals — 1·2·2·1. It then records what the residual needs:

      "A separating constraint must see the per-strand assignment … That is the whole gap."

      Component-constancy is such a constraint: it is sensitive to the multiset's shape, where Y, the ℤ₆ condition and bar-negation are all total-only. So it is worth testing against the two ambiguous pairs. It separates one and is blind to the other, and both halves are recorded.

      The d residual is NOT separated — an honest negative.

      Both d-survivors have shape (2,1), so at a π whose orbits are {0,1} and {2} both are realisable as component framings: {−1,−1,1} with the doubled value on the 2-orbit, and {−1,0,0} with the doubled value 0 there.

      Component-constancy therefore cannot narrow d's ambiguity. Separating it needs something finer than orbit shape — which value sits on which orbit — and that is not supplied by the framing condition alone. Recorded so this constraint is not over-sold as closing the whole residual.

      The ν residual, closed #

      knot-particle-dictionary.md:4 supplies the missing input:

      "Fermions (n odd) are knots (1 component, half-integer spin) … Quarks are Borromean-linked unknots in the exceptional divisor."

      so leptons close to one component and quarks sit in the three-component Borromean braid. Checked on the words themselves:

      word in B₃ permutation components
      σ₁σ₂ 3-cycle 1 — the unknot
      (σ₁σ₂)² 3-cycle 1 — the trefoil
      (σ₁σ₂⁻¹)³ identity 3 — Borromean

      A one-component closure has transitive π, so constant_of_transitive applies to every lepton — and combined with the pinned total it fixes the multiset outright. Quarks are exempt: identity π gives three orbits and a free value on each, which is what leaves u = {0,1,1} available.
