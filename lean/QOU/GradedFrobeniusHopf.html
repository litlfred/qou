    Graded Frobenius--Hopf Structure via Hecke Filtration #

    The Frobenius--Hopf object A carries a natural grading induced by the Iwahori--Hecke algebra filtration. The Young symmetric idempotent e_{(n)} decomposes A into:

    • Degree 0 (symmetric channel): the image of e_{(n)}, isomorphic to the 1-dimensional trivial representation.
    • Degree ≥ 1 (standard + alternating channels): the kernel of the symmetrizer projection, which decomposes into the standard (dim 2) and alternating (dim 1) irreducible representations of H_3(q).

    Connection to adjoint vanishing #

    The QOU observability axiom Hom(𝟙, Ad(A)) = 0 is equivalent to:

    The adjoint representation Ad(A) has no degree-0 component.

    Since Ad(A) ≅ V_2 (the spin-1 irreducible, dimension 3), and the trivial representation (spin-0, dimension 1) does not appear in this decomposition, the adjoint has no component in the symmetric channel.

    This is the categorical channel separation: the symmetric (vector coupling, degree 0) channel is invisible to the adjoint, while the standard (axial coupling, degree ≥ 1) channel carries all the non-trivial structure.

    • Degree 0: image of the Young symmetric idempotent e_{(n)}

    In Rep(SU_q(2)):

    • Degree 0 ↔ trivial representation 𝟙 (dim 1, spin 0)
    • Degree 1 ↔ standard representation V₂ (dim 2, spin ½)
    • Top degree ↔ alternating representation (dim 1, spin equivalent)

        §3 Hecke grading and irrep decomposition #

        source
        structure QOU.HeckeIrrepDecomposition :
        Type

        The Hecke irrep decomposition of H₃(q).

        The regular representation of H₃(q) decomposes as: H₃(q) ≅ V_sym ⊕ V_std ⊕ V_std ⊕ V_alt with dimensions 1 + 2 + 2 + 1 = 6 = |S₃|.

        • V_sym: the 1D symmetric representation (degree 0)
        • V_std: the 2D standard representation (degree 1), multiplicity 2
        • V_alt: the 1D alternating representation (top degree)

        The graded Frobenius--Hopf decomposition restricts to:

        • Degree 0: V_sym (1D) — the vector coupling channel
        • Degree ≥ 1: V_std ⊕ V_std ⊕ V_alt (4D + 1D) — the axial coupling channel
        • dim_sym : ℕ

          Dimension of symmetric irrep.

        • dim_std : ℕ

          Dimension of standard irrep.

        • std_multiplicity : ℕ

          Multiplicity of standard irrep in the regular representation.

        • dim_alt : ℕ

          Dimension of alternating irrep.

        • total_dim : self.dim_sym + self.dim_std * self.std_multiplicity + self.dim_alt = 6

          Total dimension equals |S₃| = 6.
