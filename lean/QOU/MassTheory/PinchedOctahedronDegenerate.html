                The octahedral e₂ (posited carrier of g₂) vanishes identically at the standard pinch. So the four-shape octahedron has no four-distinct-shape content at the pinched trefoil, and g₂ cannot be this e₂.

                The octahedral gluing constraint (holds off the pinch too) #

                Independently of the pinch, the four MSR shape-logs satisfy the octahedron edge-gluing ζ⁰_r + ζ⁰_l = ζ⁰_t + ζ⁰_b identically (equivalently z_r z_l = z_t z_b). This is the structural reason the collapse is simultaneous: the four are not independent.
