          theorem QOU.MassTheory.brunnian_channelClosureMul_eq_triple {R : Type u_1} [CommRing R] (τ123 : R) :
          channelClosureMul 1 1 1 τ123 = deficit τ123

          Brunnian collapse (multiplicative) — the decisive identity. With the three pairwise deficits = 0, every cross term of 1 − ∏(1 − fᵢ) vanishes (each factor 1 − fᵢ = 1), so the multiplicative closure also equals the single 3-body deficit. This is why the interface-product's "beyond-pairwise content" supplies nothing here: the pairwise factors are identically the unlink.

          theorem QOU.MassTheory.brunnian_channel_no_magnitude {R : Type u_1} [CommRing R] [IsDomain R] (τ123 rstar : R) (hr : rstar ≠ 1) (h : channelClosureMul 1 1 1 τ123 = rstar * deficit τ123) :
          deficit τ123 = 0

          No magnitude (the refutation). The Brunnian sub-link channel closure equals the single Borromean deficit, so it reproduces a target r*·c₃ (c₃ = deficit τ₁₂₃) with r* ≠ 1 only in the degenerate c₃ = 0. For the derived A=3 factor r* = μ₃/c₃ ≈ 1.585 ≠ 1 and any nonzero deficit, the channel decomposition cannot supply it: it contributes magnitude factor exactly 1.

          theorem QOU.MassTheory.borromean_channelClosure_eq_c3 (h : ℝ) :
          channelClosureMul 1 1 1 ((4 + h ^ 2 - 3 * h ^ 4 - h ^ 6) / (h ^ 2 + 4)) = h ^ 4 * (3 + h ^ 2) / (h ^ 2 + 4)

          Concrete: the channel closure over the 𝓑₃ sub-links equals exactly c₃. Plugging the derived Borromean triple trace τ_Borr(h) = (4 + h² − 3h⁴ − h⁶)/(h² + 4) (GAP-A-4 closed form; BorromeanThreeBodyCluster.tauBorromean) into the three-unlink-pairwise + triple channel closure reproduces the #4364 near-miss deficit 1 − τ_Borr = h⁴(3 + h²)/(h² + 4) — the 0.297 MeV, 0.63× μ₃ value — with no magnitude factor.

          theorem QOU.MassTheory.borromean_channel_ne_r_star {h rstar : ℝ} (hh : h ≠ 0) (hr : rstar ≠ 1) :
          channelClosureMul 1 1 1 ((4 + h ^ 2 - 3 * h ^ 4 - h ^ 6) / (h ^ 2 + 4)) ≠ rstar * (h ^ 4 * (3 + h ^ 2) / (h ^ 2 + 4))

          Concrete refutation. In the physical regime (h ≠ 0, so c₃ > 0) the Brunnian channel closure = c₃ never equals r*·c₃ for the derived r* ≈ 1.585 ≠ 1. The missing A=3 magnitude is not in the sub-link decomposition.
