          Insufficiency at A=2: one interface factor cannot supply the 5.85× deficit #

          Master-Σ deuteron binding B_cat = m_e·|S(D)| = 0.380 MeV; B_AME(²H) = 2.2246 MeV; the fitted deficit is C_knot = B_AME/B_cat = 5.853. Integers in units of 10⁻³ MeV: B_cat = 380, B_AME = 2225.
