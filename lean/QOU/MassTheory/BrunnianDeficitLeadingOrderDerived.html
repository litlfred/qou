    QOU.MassTheory.BrunnianDeficitLeadingOrderDerived — the A=4 leg, derived #

    Companion to QOU.MassTheory.MilnorParitySignLaw (#4389), which formalised the A-indexed Brunnian cluster law

    leading Milnor order = 2(A−1), deficit sign = (−1)^(A−1)

    with a deliberate epistemic split: A=3 PROVED (closed form 1 − τ_Borr = h⁴(3+h²)/(h²+4)), A=4 only MEASURED (tauL4Measured = 1.000145898, slope-estimated order ≈ 6.06, recorded as a def precisely so no theorem would restate a measured input as a premise).

    This file records the derived replacement for that A=4 leg.

    What was computed (exactly, not measured) #

    computations/deltalambda/a4_brunnian_deficit_closed_form.py (witness a4-brunnian-deficit-closed-form.witness.json) derives the closed form of the perblock deficit 1 − τ_A for the genuine Brunnian words — the band commutator w₃ = [A₀₁, A₁₂] and the iterated w₄ = [[A₀₁, A₁₂], A₂₃] (the same words the measuring probe used; the band factors are asserted against that probe's recorded witness) — by exact symbolic algebra over ℚ(s), s = √q:

    • χ_λ from the symbolic Hoefsmit seminormal form (exact in q);
    • y_λ the symbolic Jones–Markov weights;
    • |χ_λ| resolved by exact-rational sign verification on a q-grid spanning (1, q₀] — every χ_λ is strictly positive there, as the pure-braid argument predicts (χ_λ(q=1) = dim_λ > 0);
    • leading order by numerator valuation at s = 1 (exact multiplicity of the root, no floating-point series);
    • deficit sign by exact rational arithmetic at a rational pin s₀ (so q = s₀² is an exact rational agreeing with q₀ to ~20 digits).

    Results — both legs through the identical pipeline:

    A leading (q−1)-order leading (q−1) coeff deficit sign at q₀ τ_A(q₀)
    3 4 = 2(3−1) +12 + (exact) 0.998366623
    4 6 = 2(4−1) −96 − (exact) 1.000145898

    The A=4 value τ_L₄(q₀) = 1.000145898 reproduces the independent f64 pyhecke_native measurement to the recorded precision, and the A=3 leg reproduces the corpus's proved Borromean behaviour — two independent checks that the pipeline is sound.

    The epistemic upgrade (what this file may and may not claim) #

    • The order law 2(A−1) is now exact at both known A — at A=4 it was a log-log slope estimate ≈ 6.06; it is now the exact valuation 6.
    • The A=4 sign is now derived: the leading coefficient is −96 < 0 at even order 6, so 1 − τ₄ < 0 on a punctured neighbourhood of q = 1 ((q−1)⁶ > 0 there), and the sign is exactly negative at the substrate pin. Previously the sign entered only as the measured tauL4Measured > 1.
    • Not claimed: the closed form itself is not re-derived inside Lean — it is a degree-34 rational function in s produced by the witness-backed symbolic computation. This file records the derived leading data and proves the arithmetic of the law from it. The general-A law remains a conjecture (no third data point); MilnorParitySignLaw.MilnorParitySignConjecture still carries it, and no instance is provided here.

    Two INDEPENDENT sides: witnessed data vs the law's formula #

    The declarations below are split deliberately, so that the theorems relating them are falsifiable rather than definitional.

    • witnessedOrder / witnessedLeadingCoeff are DATA — transcribed from the witness a4-brunnian-deficit-closed-form.witness.json. They assert nothing; they are a Lean-side record of what the symbolic computation produced.
    • leadingOrder / paritySign are THE LAW — the closed formulas 2(A−1) and (−1)^(A−1) posited by MilnorParitySignLaw, defined without reference to the data.

    Because the two sides are defined independently, a theorem asserting that they agree can fail: had the derivation returned order 5, or a positive A=4 coefficient, law_reproduces_witnessed_order / law_matches_witnessed_sign would not compile. That is the content — and it is the actual scientific claim (the law predicts what the computation found).

    Anti-pattern deliberately avoided (AGENTS.md §8): a statement like leadingCoeff 4 < 0 := by decide is not a theorem — leadingCoeff 4 is defined to be −96, so it is true by unfolding and says nothing about the physics. Such statements are notation, not proofs, and are not written here.
