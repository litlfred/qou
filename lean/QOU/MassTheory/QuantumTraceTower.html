      qtr (Matrix.kroneckerMap (fun (x1 x2 : K) => x1 * x2) D₁ D₂) (Matrix.kroneckerMap (fun (x1 x2 : K) => x1 * x2) X₁ X₂) = qtr D₁ X₁ * qtr D₂ X₂

      Kronecker multiplicativity of the quantum trace. Disjoint strand blocks factor: qtr_{D₁⊗D₂}(X₁⊗X₂) = qtr_{D₁}(X₁)·qtr_{D₂}(X₂).

      The μ-weighted case reduces to the canonical plain-trace multiplicativity QOU.MathlibExt.MarkovTraceMultiplicative.trace_kron: pull the two products through Matrix.mul_kronecker_mul ((D₁⊗ₖD₂)·(X₁⊗ₖX₂) = (D₁·X₁)⊗ₖ(D₂·X₂)), then apply trace_kron. One trace-multiplicativity lemma serves both this tower and the mass/binding Markov-trace compute.
