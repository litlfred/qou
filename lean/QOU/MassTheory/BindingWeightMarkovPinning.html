      theorem QOU.MassTheory.BindingWeightMarkovPinning.asymmetric_weight_ne_symmetric {R : Type u_1} {ι : Type u_2} {W y : ι → R} (a b : ι) (hWsym : W a = W b) (hyasym : y a ≠ y b) :
      ¬∀ (i : ι), y i = W i

      A conjugation-symmetric weight cannot be the (asymmetric) Markov weight. If a candidate weight W is symmetric on a conjugate pair (W a = W b) while the Markov weight y is asymmetric there (y a ≠ y b), then y ≠ W as weight systems (¬ ∀ i, y i = W i) — so W is not the Markov weight. This is the structural exclusion of W_λ.
