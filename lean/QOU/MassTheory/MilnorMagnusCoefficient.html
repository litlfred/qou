      Magnus generators in Mₙ(R): magnusGen N i = E_{i, i+1} (the elementary matrix single ⟨i⟩ ⟨i+1⟩ 1) when i+1 < N, else 0. These realise the free 2-step-nilpotent / faithful strictly-upper-triangular representation underlying Milnor's Magnus expansion x_i ↦ 1 + X_i.

          The cyclic trace of the Magnus-realised tower vanishes — the exact positive-side witness of #4480's cyclic_trace_kills_iterCommTower.
