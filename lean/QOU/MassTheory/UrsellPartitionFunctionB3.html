    The corpus b₃ is minus the third Ursell function of a lattice gas — and the sign is Mayer's #

    Bean qou-rwhv asked for the partition function Ξ whose cluster (Mayer) expansion has the corpus's connected three-body cumulant as its third Ursell coefficient. This file records the answer at the level a generic commutative ring can state it, and the one ordered-field fact that settles the sign. Audit: docs/audits/2026-09-12-ursell-partition-function-for-b3.md; probe: computations/probes/ursell_partition_function_for_b3_probe.py.

    The objects #

    • The canonical partition function of a cluster S is the product of its bond factors, Z_S = ∏_{e ⊆ S} f_e. In the framework f_e = μ_e ∈ (0,1) is the trace of the interface letter, and Z_S = tr_M(W_atom(S)) / tr_M(W_free(S)) by connected-sum multiplicativity of the Markov trace (QOU.MathlibExt.MonotoneWordFactorizes.tr_stabWord_eq_prod_tr; re-measured on the live alternating-core word in the probe, to 1e-40).
    • The Ursell functions φᵀ_S are defined by the cluster property Z_S = Σ_{π ∈ Π(S)} ∏_{B ∈ π} φᵀ_B (Jansen–Kolesnikov, library/s10955-022-02992-6/, p. 5), equivalently as the sum over connected spanning graphs of ∏ g_e with the Mayer variable g_e = f_e − 1. cluster3 / phi4_eq_connected_sum are those two descriptions agreeing.
    • The grand-canonical partition function of the same cluster is Ξ_S(z) = Σ_{T ⊆ S} z^T Z_T — a hard-core lattice gas on the nucleon sites, activity z_i per site, pair weight μ_e. Its multilinear log is ln Ξ_S = Σ_T z^T φᵀ_T (the exponential formula; verified symbolically in the probe, ∂³ ln Ξ / ∂z₁∂z₂∂z₃ |₀ = φᵀ₃). That Ξ is the partition function the bean asked for: polymers = nucleons, weight g = μ − 1, compatibility = hard core on sites, ensemble = grand canonical over the sub-clusters of a fixed nucleus.
    • The corpus's multiplicative assembly is W(S) = 1 − Z_S (MayerThreeBodySign), and its cumulants are Boolean-lattice (inclusion–exclusion) inversions of W (QOU.Mass.PairwiseCumulantVanishing.b3, b4).

    What is proved #

    1. b3corpus_eq_neg_phi3 — b₃^corpus = −φᵀ₃, exactly, as a ring identity (the 400/400 of the 2026-08-30 probe, in closed form).
    2. boolean3_eq_phi3 / b4corpus_eq — why it holds at n = 3 and why it fails at n = 4: the Boolean inversion of a bond-multiplicative Z is the sum over spanning edge sets with no isolated vertex, the Ursell function is the sum over connected ones, and the smallest isolated-vertex-free disconnected graph is two disjoint edges on four vertices. So b₄^corpus = −φᵀ₄ − (g₁₂g₃₄ + g₁₃g₂₄ + g₁₄g₂₃), and the defect is nonzero (matching_defect).
    3. phi3_pos / b3corpus_neg — on f ∈ (0,1) the third Ursell function is strictly positive, so b₃^corpus < 0 for every bond triple, non-uniform included. There is no threshold: MayerThreeBodySign.lt_of_b3_pos's fpn > 2/3 was the bound obtained by leaving P₃ ≥ 0 free; once P₃ is the bond product it can never be met (b3corpus_uniform_closed_form: b₃ = −(1−f)²(f+2)).
    4. restored_eq_neg_activity — the 2026-08-30 "restore the alternation" prescription b_n = (−1)^{n−1} φᵀ_n is the coefficient of −ln(Ξ/Ξ_free) at negative activity z ↦ −z. The corpus's uniform b_n = −φᵀ_n is that coefficient at positive activity, i.e. the Mayer expansion's own sign. Nothing in the classical theory supplies +φᵀ₃ for a gas of pair factors below 1.

    What this says about the 2026-08-30 reading #

    docs/audits/2026-08-30-ursell-alternating-sign-and-the-b3-obstruction.md §3 concluded that MayerThreeBodySign's negative b₃ is "a missing (−1)^{n−1}, not a physical obstruction". Items 3–4 reverse that: the alternation of φᵀ_n is already what makes the corpus b₃ negative, and restoring it a second time names no ensemble. Within the Mayer vocabulary a positive connected three-body coefficient at positive activity needs a bond factor above 1 (an attractive Mayer channel, g > 0), which no letter of the production word has — scope (a) for that last clause, measured in the probe. The magnitude 2.2511 MeV recorded there is +φᵀ₃ at the deuteron-pinned scale and inherits the negative-activity reading: it is not a postdiction of a derived quantity.

    Base ring (§7c) #

    Everything through item 2 and item 4 is a polynomial identity over a generic CommRing R. Item 3 needs an ordering and is stated over [Field K] [LinearOrder K] [IsStrictOrderedRing K], mirroring MayerThreeBodySign, which it imports so that b3corpus_uniform_eq_mayerSign can tie the two definitions of b₃ together on the nose.
