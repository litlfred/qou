    def QOU.MassTheory.multBinding {R : Type u_1} [CommRing R] (τ f : R) :
    ℕ → R

    The symmetric-cluster multiplicative binding table B(A) = τ^A · (1 − f^{C(A,2)}) for A = 0,…,4, with pair counts C(A,2) = 0,0,1,3,6. (0/1-body: no pairs ⇒ 1 − f^0 = 0 ⇒ B = 0.) This is the symmetric idealisation of the framework binding E₀ · τ_vac · (1 − ∏ f); the overall E₀ scale is dropped (it cancels in the fraction-of-target headline the probe reports).

      theorem QOU.MassTheory.multBinding_b4 {R : Type u_1} [CommRing R] (τ f : R) :
      fourthDiff (multBinding τ f) = τ ^ 4 * (1 - f ^ 6) - 4 * (τ ^ 3 * (1 - f ^ 3)) + 6 * (τ ^ 2 * (1 - f))

      Closed form of the multiplicative connected 4-body cumulant. The 4th forward finite difference of the multiplicative binding sequence is τ⁴(1−f⁶) − 4τ³(1−f³) + 6τ²(1−f). This is the quantity the probe evaluates 0-fit; the finite-difference extractor is the same fourthDiff proved in BodyOrderCumulant to annihilate ≤2-body content, so this is genuinely the connected 4-body part.

      theorem QOU.MassTheory.multBinding_b4_at_f_one {R : Type u_1} [CommRing R] (τ : R) :
      fourthDiff (multBinding τ 1) = 0

      No coupling ⇒ no connected cumulant. At f = 1 every saturation factor 1 − f^k vanishes, so the multiplicative 4-body cumulant is 0. The connected content is generated entirely by the coupling f ≠ 1; there is no spurious 4-body term from the extensive τ^A growth alone.

      theorem QOU.MassTheory.satBinding_b4 {R : Type u_1} [CommRing R] (f : R) :
      fourthDiff (multBinding 1 f) = 3 - 6 * f + 4 * f ^ 3 - f ^ 6

      Pure-saturation cumulant (drop the extensive τ^A growth, τ = 1). The connected 4-body part of Bsat(A) = 1 − f^{C(A,2)} is the explicit polynomial 3 − 6f + 4f³ − f⁶. This is the "saturation ceiling" the probe's headline sits against: it isolates how much connected 4-body content the pair-count exponent C(A,2) alone supplies, independent of the (weak) τ^A growth.

      theorem QOU.MassTheory.multBinding_b4_nonvacuous :
      fourthDiff (multBinding 1 0) = 3

      Non-vacuity / contrast with the additive route. Concretely at τ = 1, f = 0 the multiplicative 4-body cumulant is 3 ≠ 0, whereas every additive form (fourthDiff_linear, fourthDiff_pairwise in BodyOrderCumulant) gives 0 identically. So the multiplicative binding is the structurally correct place to look for the missing ⁴He b₄ — it is not orthogonal to the 4-body channel.

      Bar-parity of the pn interface factor (the I-par refinement) #

      b4_connected_bar_parity_probe.py establishes that the Markov z is bar-even (z(q) = z(q⁻¹)) and the Hecke h is bar-odd (h(q) = −h(q⁻¹)). Under the bar involution q ↦ q⁻¹ acting as z ↦ z, h ↦ −h, the pn pair factor z − h/2 is therefore bar-mixed — the original I-par phrasing "prove b₄ is bar-even" is too strong (it is FALSE for the multiplicative pn route, unlike the writhe-determinant route of B4CumulantBarEven which is genuinely bar-even).

      We record the split over a generic CommRing, using 2·(pn factor) = 2z − h to avoid division. The bar-even projection of the pn factor is z (the pp/nn Markov weight) and the bar-odd part is −h/2 (the radiated Q_β-like channel).
