    LinearMap.trace of the factor permutation on a tensor power #

    Bean qou-mex9. The last packaging step of the qou-uinl arc.

    TensorTraceCharacter computed the q = 1 character as a matrix trace in the monomial model, and PiTensorMonomialBasis identified that model with the genuine tensor power. Both files said the same thing was still missing: a statement phrased with LinearMap.trace over ⨂ itself, which needs Module.Free/Module.Finite instances for the tensor power and the trace-to-matrix-trace identification. This file supplies both:

    LinearMap.trace R (⨂[R] _ : α, (κ → R)) (tensorPerm σ) = (card κ) ^ orbitCount σ

    and trace_tensorPerm_two is the card κ = 2 case — the 2^{c(σ)} that ClassicalTraceFloor states as its step-1 hypothesis, now over the real object.

    How #

    • Free and finite are transported along monoEquiv (Module.Free.of_equiv, Module.Finite.equiv). This matters rather than being bookkeeping: LinearMap.trace is defined on any module but is junk without a basis, so without these instances the statement above would be vacuous rather than false.
    • Conjugation. monoEquiv_comp_tensorPerm says permuting the tensor factors is conjugate to precomposition on index tuples by indexPerm σ⁻¹, and LinearMap.trace_comp_comm' moves the trace across that conjugation.
    • orbitCount_inv closes the σ⁻¹: orbits are inversion-invariant (support_inv, cycleType_inv), so the σ⁻¹ introduced by the variance of precomposition costs nothing.

    🛑 Scope #

    This completes the q = 1 leg only. Quantum Schur–Weyl (q ≠ 1) is untouched — bean qou-mpq5 scoped that as a multi-week gap (the Jimbo double-centralizer plus Hoefsmit matrix units) and deliberately refused to fake it. That assessment stands. Nothing here bears on it.
