    Reeb-flow pn-bond magnitude — structural identities #

    Formalizes the exact algebraic content behind the landed pn-bond magnitude g = 1/√(5 + (q₀−1)²/q₀) (main, 2026-07-06; ²H "reeb-flow" tier of categorical_q_decay_ba_low_isotopes.py), per docs/audits/2026-07-06-reeb-flow-pn-magnitude-provenance-and-1four-extension.md §2b and computations/probes/reeb_g_structural_identity_probe.py.

    Three statements, all generic over the base ring per AGENTS.md §7c (no ℝ, no Real.sqrt, no field — the square-root-free forms carry the full content; the one quantity that has to be inverted, v, is carried as a unit v : Rˣ, so ↑v⁻¹ is a Units coercion over the same [CommRing R] as the rest of the file):

    • deformation_eq_ceiling (I1): with v² = q, the deformation identity 5 + (v − v⁻¹)² = 1 + (v + v⁻¹)² — i.e. (a+b)² − (a−b)² = 4ab at ab = 1. The "mystery" term (q−1)²/q is the classical √5-ceiling identity rewritten; both sides are 1 + [2]_v².
    • ss_matrix_sq (I2, Cayley–Hamilton form): the traceless ss-sector exchange matrix [[1, b], [b, −1]] squares to (1 + b²)·1. With b = [2]_v this is the q-deformation of the classical [[1,2],[2,−1]] (whose square is 5·1 — the √5 ceiling); the landed g satisfies g²·(1 + [2]_v²) = 1, i.e. it sits at criticality of the deformed ss-sector.
    • pn_sign_conjugation (pn-sign isospectrality): conjugating the uniform-magnitude [1⁴] complete-graph exchange matrix by D = diag(1,1,−1,−1) flips the sign of every pn entry while fixing the pp/nn entries. Hence det(1 + S(a, w)) = det(1 + S(a, −w)) (pn_sign_det_invariant): no determinant-class observable at uniform per-pair magnitude can distinguish the two mixed pn signs — the "open pn sign" of PR #3426 is unobservable in this class. (Scope: uniform magnitude, complete graph; per-pair non-uniform framings can break it.)

The landed g_reeb is the marginal-stability point of the q-deformed exchange — the algebraic counterpart of the deuteron being marginally bound. (Below criticality the determinant is positive; the AME value sits on the bound side, B_AME < 5g, consistent in sign.)

      theorem QOU.MassTheory.ReebPnMagnitude.ss_det_no_linear_term {R : Type u_1} [CommRing R] (g b : R) :
      (1 + g • ssMatrix b).det = 1 + g ^ 2 * (ssMatrix b).det

      Open-bond vs closed-loop dichotomy — the closed-loop (determinant) binding of a single exchange kills the linear term. For a 2×2 matrix the determinant expands as det(1 + g·M) = 1 + g·tr M + g²·det M; since the ss-sector M(b) is traceless (ssMatrix_trace_zero), the linear-in-g coefficient vanishes and the loop binding is O(g²): det(1 + g·M(b)) = 1 + g²·det M(b).

      This is the structural reason the determinant (closed-loop) form under-binds the deuteron (−E₀·ln(1−g²) ≈ 0.93 vs AME 2.22): a single open pn bond contributes to the binding at first order in the crossing amplitude g, but the trace-free loop object has no first-order term. The deuteron is one open pn bond, so its binding is the linear O(g) object (the reeb-flow 5g law), not the quadratic loop determinant — which is why the linear form is the physically correct one for the single pair. (The residual open question is the MeV unit of that linear object, not its g-order: see the audit §2h.)

          (1 + exchangeMatrix a w).det = (1 + exchangeMatrix a (-w)).det

          pn-sign isospectrality for determinant-class observables — det(1 + S(a, w)) = det(1 + S(a, −w)). Since D² = 1 and det D = 1, 1 + S(a, −w) = D (1 + S(a, w)) D and the determinant is conjugation-invariant. Consequence: the "open pn sign" (PR #3426) is unobservable in any −ln det(I + G)-class binding form at uniform per-pair magnitude.

          theorem QOU.MassTheory.ReebPnMagnitude.pn_sign_trace_blind {R : Type u_1} [CommRing R] (a w : R) (k : ℕ) (T : Matrix (Fin 4) (Fin 4) R) (hT : T * parityD = parityD * T) :
          (T * exchangeMatrix a w ^ k).trace = (T * exchangeMatrix a (-w) ^ k).trace

          pn-sign blindness of charge-diagonal observables — for any operator T commuting with the p/n grading D (every charge-diagonal / isospin- diagonal observable does), all mixed traces are pn-sign invariant: tr(T · S(a, w)^k) = tr(T · S(a, −w)^k).

          Consequence: the [1⁴] pn writhe sign is invisible not just to the determinant (pn_sign_det_invariant) but to the ENTIRE spectral/mixed-trace observable class built from grading-commuting operators. Sign-sensitivity requires an operator that fails to commute with D — i.e. one that mixes the p and n blocks, which in the framework is the weak/β sector (isospin raising/lowering). The "open pn sign" of PR #3426 is therefore a weak-sector observable, not a binding-sector one.
