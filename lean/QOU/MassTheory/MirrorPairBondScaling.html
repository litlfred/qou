    Mirror-pair bond scaling — the isospin split generalizes to (a/b)^{d(A−1)} #

    The general law behind the just-landed A=3 identity triton_helium3_split : T/³He = (a/b)² (BondWeightIsospinSplit.lean, PR #3841, bean qou-r1sr). Same Mayer/Ursell pairwise (b₂) setting: each nucleon–nucleon bond (x,y) carries the inverse-product ideal-gas weight w(x,y) = 1/(τx·τy), a := τ(proton), b := τ(neutron); a nucleus' aggregate is the product of its bond weights.

    For a nucleus with Z protons and N neutrons the pairwise bonds are C(Z,2) pp-bonds (weight a⁻² each), C(N,2) nn-bonds (b⁻²), and Z·N pn-bonds ((ab)⁻¹). The total a- and b-exponents of the bond-trace product ∏ τx·τy collapse to a single factor A−1:

    2·C(Z,2) + Z·N = Z(Z−1) + ZN = Z(A−1), likewise N(A−1), A = Z+N.

    So the whole pairwise cluster level is governed by one exponent A−1. Two consequences, both proved here (with m := A−1 kept as a free ℕ so the statements are clean CommMonoid / Field identities — no ℕ-subtraction; the physical m = A−1 is instantiated in the corollaries):

    • scaling law bondTraceProduct Z N m = (a^Z · b^N)^m — the pairwise level is a single m-th power of the "nucleon content" monomial a^Z b^N;
    • mirror-pair split for the pair (Z, Z+d) ↔ (Z+d, Z) (isospin gap d, shared A), the aggregate ratio is exactly (a/b)^{d·m} — the degeneracy break a type-blind bond model gives as 1.

    Recoveries / predictions (instantiating m = A−1): (Z,d,A) = (1,1,3) ⇒ (a/b)² — recovers triton_helium3_split (T/³He); (3,1,7) ⇒ (a/b)⁶ — the ⁷Li/⁷Be pairwise prediction (new).

    Scope (§7c + the sibling's own note): this is a generic-field algebraic identity (no ℝ, no ordering); the archimedean a,b := tr_M at q₀ lives in the witness. It isolates the pairwise (b₂) isospin-scaling law; it does not close the extensivity gap (the open magnitude is the connected b₄ term, orthogonal to this pairwise object). The exponent d(A−1) growing with A makes the pairwise model's over-scaling for heavier mirrors explicit — a feature, not a fit.

    Background: the Mayer–Ursell cluster (b₂/pairwise) expansion of statistical mechanics; see BondWeightIsospinSplit.lean for the A=3 special case and the qou-r1sr grounding witness.
