    The hook column of the gauge-invariant curvature reduces to word-traces (qou-o6ov) #

    Context (PR #6445, #6464). The projector curvature Ω₂^P = (4/[2]_q²) · Σ_a [σ_a, σ_{a+1}] is a genuine element of the Hecke algebra, unlike the representation-specific germ q·∂σ·σ⁻¹. Because it is an algebra element, the reduced-Burau hook identity of MagnusHookExteriorPower (numerically exact to 139 digits, for every word w)

    tr_{(k,1ⁿ⁻ᵏ)}(w) = q^{len(w)(1−j)} · e_j(eig w^V),   j = n − k,

    extends from the plain character to the curvature, giving the closed form verified to the dps floor on every hook in computations/deltalambda/magnus_gauge_hook_closed_form_probe.py:

    tr_{hook}(ρ·Ω₂^P) = (4/[2]_q²) · q^{(L+2)(1−j)} · Σ_a [ e_j(eig ρσ_aσ_{a+1}) − e_j(eig ρσ_{a+1}σ_a) ].

    What is formalized here is the step that makes this a one-line consequence. The seminormal Hecke construction, the specific atom word, and the q-twist are outside Mathlib (as in MagnusHookExteriorPower). The algebraic content is two facts, general over any CommRing R and any R-algebra A with an R-linear functional tr (a trace):

    • trace_mul_curvature_eq_sum_word_traces — linearity. tr (ρ · Σ_a [σ_a, σ_{a+1}]) equals Σ_a (tr (ρσ_aσ_{a+1}) − tr (ρσ_{a+1}σ_a)): the curvature trace is a signed sum of traces of words, each two generators longer than ρ. Sorry-free, unconditional.
    • trace_mul_curvature_eq_of_word_traces — the closed form, conditional on the hook identity. If every such word-trace is c · (its elementary-symmetric value) for a single scalar c — the common twist q^{(L+2)(1−j)}, common precisely because both words in each commutator have the same length L+2 — then tr (ρ · Σ_a [σ_a,σ_{a+1}]) = c · Σ_a (E_a − F_a).

    The single c is not a convenience: the probe's first pass used the twist of ρ (length L) instead of that of ρσσ (length L+2) and matched only at j = 0, 1 and the two endpoints, where the discrepancy is invisible. Requiring one c for both members of every commutator is that correction stated structurally.

    Hypothesis-carrying, per AGENTS.md §"Conditional-proved ≠ premise-established": the theorem proves the implication; the per-word hook identity is the numerically-established premise, not proved here.

    A

    The second-order (projector) curvature as a sum of nearest-neighbour commutators of the generators σ, over the interior pairs a = 0, …, m−1: Σ_a (σ_a σ_{a+1} − σ_{a+1} σ_a). The overall scalar 4/[2]_q² of Ω₂^P is omitted — it is a constant that commutes with everything below and is restored at the end.
