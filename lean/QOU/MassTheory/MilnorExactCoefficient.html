          theorem QOU.MassTheory.MilnorExactCoefficient.cyclic_trace_group_commutator_rearrange {R : Type u_1} [CommRing R] {A : Type u_2} [Ring A] [Algebra R A] (τ : CyclicTrace R A) (g h : Aˣ) :
          τ.toLin (↑g * ↑h * ↑g⁻¹ * ↑h⁻¹) = τ.toLin (↑h * ↑g⁻¹ * ↑h⁻¹ * ↑g)

          The group commutator is NOT collapsed to τ(1) by cyclicity — the positive counterpart that locates where the real coefficient lives. For a group commutator g·h·g⁻¹·h⁻¹ (built from ring units g h : Aˣ, as the Hecke image of a pure-braid band-commutator word is), cyclicity gives only the conjugation rearrangement τ(g·h·g⁻¹·h⁻¹) = τ(h·g⁻¹·h⁻¹·g) — it does not reduce to τ(1). Contrast CyclicTrace.commutator, where the ring commutator collapses to 0. This is why the physical Jones-trace deficit τ(1) − τ(g·h·g⁻¹·h⁻¹) is nonzero at order 2(A−1) (the Milnor content) while the Lie tower's trace vanishes identically.
