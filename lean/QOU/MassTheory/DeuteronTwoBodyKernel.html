    QOU.MassTheory.DeuteronTwoBodyKernel #

    The two-body Markov-trace kernel over-binds the deuteron, and no admissible colour weight in the derived neighbourhood rescues it.

    What this file is about #

    The production binding form B_sym (computations/mass_binding_dashboard.py, _bond_type_L) computes the deuteron — the A = 2, Z = N = 1 nucleus, a single p–n bond with no coordination topology, no cluster structure and no Coulomb term — as

    B_sym(²H) = E₀ · L_pn, E₀ = mₑ / (q₀(q₀−1)), L_pn = −log(z_D(q₀) − h(q₀)·t) − (t − ½)·log(1 − h(q₀)),

    where z_D(q) = q³/(q²+1) is the convention-D Markov parameter (owner ruling R1, 2026-08-15), h(q) = q − q⁻¹ is the Hecke skein parameter, the second term is the Lean-proved writhe normalisation (QOU.QBeta.WritheNormalisation, (1−h)^(Δe/2)), and t is the weight of the σ⁻¹ branch in the p–n colour superposition (1−t)·σ + t·σ⁻¹. The bring_color_weighted convention (owner ruling R3) fixes

    t = ½ − δ(q)/4, δ(q) = (q−1)²(1−q⁻⁴)/3

    — the bar-symmetric weight ½, split by the leading flavour asymmetry δ = Δm/M_N proved in prop:flavor-split-substrate.

    Numerically (mpmath, 60 dps, no float64), at the canonical proton pin q₀ = 1.109978582751380807… and CODATA mₑ = 0.51099895069 MeV:

    quantity value
    E₀ 4.185981363294475896945 MeV
    z_D(q₀) 0.6126880371464652515958
    h(q₀) 0.2090602987956393154308
    δ(q₀) 0.001375710807027096009
    t = ½ − δ/4 0.4996560722982432259979
    E₀·L_pn 2.832824919934573065733 MeV
    B_AME(²H) 2.2245662 MeV
    overshoot +27.34 %

    ²H is the only negative residual in the low-isotope table (R(²H) = −0.61; every other isotope has R > 0), which is why it is logically prior to the additive-term programme: a correction that is non-negative for all A cannot simultaneously repair ⁴He (which under-binds) and ²H (which over-binds).

    What is proved here, sorry-free #

    • interfaceFactor_half — the exact algebraic identity z_D(q) − h(q)/2 = (q⁴+1)/(2q(q²+1)): the bar-symmetric p–n interface factor is a rational function of q with no free constant in it.
    • bondL_strictMono — t ↦ L(q,t) is strictly increasing on the admissible weight range. Hence the whole one-parameter interface family is swept monotonically by t, and a single evaluation at either endpoint bounds every weight on that side.
    • overshoot_core / deuteron_overbinds — for q in the proton-pin interval, mₑ in the CODATA interval, and any near-balanced weight t ∈ [0.499, ½], E₀·L(q,t) > 1.25 · B_AME(²H). A strict, falsifiable numerical inequality: the two-body kernel over-binds the deuteron by more than 25 %.
    • required_weight_far_from_balanced — conversely, any t reproducing a deuteron binding ≤ 2.4 MeV satisfies t ≤ 0.4.
    • derived_weight_near_balanced — the derived weight satisfies t = ½ − δ/4 ≥ 0.4996.
    • no_flavour_split_rescue — combining the last two: the departure from balance that the deuteron demands exceeds 250 × the derived flavour split δ/4. The colour weighting is a 10⁻³-scale correction; the deuteron needs a 10⁻¹-scale one.

    Scope of the negative — scope (a), NOT scope (b) #

    Per AGENTS.md §"Before declaring refuted / ruled out / dead end", the result proved here is a scope-(a) scope-limited negative:

    The two-body Markov-trace kernel B_sym, with the convention-D Markov parameter and the derived bring_color_weighted interface, does not reproduce B_AME(²H); it over-binds by +27 %, and no interface weight within 250 × the derived flavour split closes the gap.

    It is not a scope-(b) structural exclusion of the B_sym construction. Two invariants would be needed for that and neither is in hand:

    1. The admissible interface family is d ∈ [0, −h], i.e. t ∈ [0, 1], and B_AME(²H) is inside the band — interfaceFactor reaches it at t ≈ 0.268 (required_weight_far_from_balanced bounds this by 0.4). So no reachability invariant excludes the target; what is excluded is reaching it at the derived weight.
    2. t enters only through the interface factor. A term entering additively (rather than by re-weighting the interface) is untouched by everything proved here.

    Under the excised convention C (z = √q/(q+1)) the band was [2.907, 5.178] MeV and B_AME(²H) = 2.225 sat below all of it — under C the deuteron was genuinely unreachable. The convention-D ruling reversed that. Do not carry the older impossibility claim forward.

    Convention-C staleness note (measured 2026-08-26) #

    QOU.Hecke.ClosedFormInterfaceFactors — the only other Lean formalisation of the three named bond factors f_pp, f_nn, f_pn — defines its Markov parameter as markovZ s = s/(s²+1) in the square-root variable q = s², i.e. z = √q/(q+1): convention C, which owner ruling R1 excised. That spelling is invisible to every convention-C detector in the tree (none of them scan a square-root-variable rational form), which is why it survived the 2026-08-10 drain that AGENTS.md records as having cleared .lean to zero. This module therefore does not import it, and states zD afresh; the two are not interchangeable. Tracked separately — do not "reconcile" them by substitution.

                    §2. Two elementary logarithm bounds #

                    Both are consequences of Real.log_le_sub_one_of_pos; they are what turns the numerical claims below into rational arithmetic.

                    §3. The bar-symmetric interface factor is an exact rational function #

                    source
                    theorem QOU.MassTheory.DeuteronKernel.interfaceFactor_half {q : ℝ} (hq0 : q ≠ 0) (hq2 : q ^ 2 + 1 ≠ 0) :
                    interfaceFactor q (1 / 2) = (q ^ 4 + 1) / (2 * q * (q ^ 2 + 1))

                    The bar-symmetric p–n interface factor has a closed rational form.

                    At the bar-symmetric weight t = ½ the writhe exponent Δe = 2t − 1 vanishes, so the writhe factor is exactly 1 and the entire bond energy is E₀ · log(1 / f) with

                    f = z_D(q) − h(q)/2 = (q⁴+1) / (2q(q²+1)).

                    Every constant on the right-hand side is fixed: there is no scale, no normalisation and no fitted coefficient anywhere in it.

                    source
                    theorem QOU.MassTheory.DeuteronKernel.bondL_half (q : ℝ) :
                    bondL q (1 / 2) = -Real.log (interfaceFactor q (1 / 2))

                    The writhe factor is exactly trivial at the bar-symmetric weight.

                    §4. Monotonicity of the bond energy in the colour weight #

                    source
                    theorem QOU.MassTheory.DeuteronKernel.bondL_strictMono {q : ℝ} (hh0 : 0 < hq q) (hh1 : hq q < 1) {t₁ t₂ : ℝ} (hlt : t₁ < t₂) (hpos : 0 < interfaceFactor q t₂) :
                    bondL q t₁ < bondL q t₂

                    The bond energy is strictly increasing in the colour weight t.

                    Both terms increase with t: the interface factor z − h·t decreases (so −log of it increases), and −(t − ½)·log(1 − h) increases because log(1 − h) < 0. Consequently the admissible interface family is swept monotonically, t = 0 (p–p) is its minimum and t = 1 (n–n) its maximum, and a bound at one weight bounds every weight on that side.

                    §5. The overshoot #

                    The numerical core is stated on abstract reals with interval hypotheses, so that the arithmetic is separated from the substrate instantiation and each half is checkable on its own.

                    source
                    theorem QOU.MassTheory.DeuteronKernel.overshoot_core {z hh E t B : ℝ} (hzlo : 0.6126879 ≤ z) (hzhi : z ≤ 0.6126881) (hhlo : 0.2090601 ≤ hh) (hhhi : hh ≤ 0.2090604) (hE : 4.185980 ≤ E) (htlo : 0.499 ≤ t) (hthi : t ≤ 1 / 2) (hB : B ≤ 2.2245666) :
                    1.25 * B < E * (-Real.log (z - hh * t) - (t - 1 / 2) * Real.log (1 - hh))

                    Numerical core of the deuteron overshoot.

                    For a Markov parameter, skein parameter and energy unit inside the intervals that the proton-pinned substrate and CODATA mₑ actually determine, and for any near-balanced colour weight t ∈ [0.499, ½], the two-body bond energy exceeds 1.25 × the experimental deuteron binding.

                    The bound is proved through the rational chain

                    f ≤ 0.5083671, −log f ≥ log 2 + 1 − 2f ≥ 0.6764130, (½ − t)·log(1 − h) ≥ 0.001 · (1 − (1 − h)⁻¹) ≥ −0.0002644, E · L ≥ 4.185980 × 0.6761486 = 2.83034 > 2.78071 = 1.25 × 2.2245666.

                    source
                    theorem QOU.MassTheory.DeuteronKernel.deuteron_overbinds {me q t B : ℝ} (hzlo : 0.6126879 ≤ zD q) (hzhi : zD q ≤ 0.6126881) (hhlo : 0.2090601 ≤ hq q) (hhhi : hq q ≤ 0.2090604) (hE : 4.185980 ≤ E0 me q) (htlo : 0.499 ≤ t) (hthi : t ≤ 1 / 2) (hB : B ≤ 2.2245666) :

                    The two-body Markov-trace kernel over-binds the deuteron by more than 25 %.

                    Stated at the canonical proton pin q₀ ∈ [1.1099785, 1.1099786] and CODATA mₑ ∈ [0.5109989, 0.5109990], for any near-balanced colour weight. The measured value is +27.34 %; 1.25 is the margin that survives the rational bounding chain above.

                    theorem QOU.MassTheory.DeuteronKernel.no_flavour_split_rescue {me q t : ℝ} (hq1 : 1.1099785 ≤ q) (hq2 : q ≤ 1.1099786) (hzlo : 0.6126879 ≤ zD q) (hzhi : zD q ≤ 0.6126881) (hhlo : 0.2090601 ≤ hq q) (hhhi : hq q ≤ 0.2090604) (hE : 4.185980 ≤ E0 me q) (hthi : t ≤ 1 / 2) (hbind : BSymDeuteron me q t ≤ 2.4) :

                    The flavour split cannot rescue the deuteron.

                    The derived colour weight sits within δ/4 < 3.5 × 10⁻⁴ of the bar-symmetric value ½; any weight reproducing a deuteron binding of at most 2.4 MeV lies at or below 0.4, i.e. at least 0.0996 below balance. The departure the deuteron demands therefore exceeds 250 × the entire derived flavour split.

                    This is the sharp form of the negative. The p–n colour weighting is a 10⁻³-scale structure and the deuteron gap is a 10⁻¹-scale one, so no refinement of δ — subleading corrections included — reaches it.
