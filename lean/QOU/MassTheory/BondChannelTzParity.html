    The bond channel is odd in T_z; the multiplet channel is even #

    What is decided here. The framework assigns each nucleon pair an interface factor f = z_D(q) − h(q)·t, where t is the weight of the σ⁻¹ branch: t = 0 for a p–p pair, t = 1 for n–n, t ≈ ½ for p–n (QOU.MassTheory.DeuteronKernel.interfaceFactor). Reading t against the pair isospin projection T_z — +1 for p–p, 0 for p–n, −1 for n–n — gives t = (1 − T_z)/2, so the bond factor is affine in T_z.

    This file proves the consequence: the T_z-odd part of the bond factor is exactly (h/2)·T_z, so the bond layer is charge-symmetric iff h(q) = 0, iff q = 1 — the classical limit. For every q > 1 the bond channel violates charge symmetry, and no choice of q repairs it.

    The contrast is the point. The two-row isospin multiplet weight [2T+1]_q with T = |T_z| is even in T_z for every q, trivially, because it sees only |T_z|. So the two channels carry opposite T_z parity and the bond channel is the sole carrier of the violation.

    Why charge symmetry is the right demand. It is the statement that the strong interaction does not distinguish p–p from n–n; the observed p–p / n–n difference is attributed to the electromagnetic channel plus a small charge-symmetry-breaking residual. A strong-channel object is therefore required to be even in T_z. This is a symmetry demand, not a fitted constraint: no measured number enters any statement below.

    What this does NOT prove. It does not show that an even-in-T_z bond factor exists in the Hecke algebra, nor that [2T+1]_q is the right even object, nor anything about magnitudes. It is a scope-(b) structural exclusion of one family — every f affine in the pair's neutron count — with the invariant cited: the T_z-odd part of an affine function is its slope, and the slope here is h(q)/2, which vanishes only at q = 1.

    PROVENANCE — most of §2 is a RE-DERIVATION, and saying so is the point. An earlier version of this docstring claimed the defect "was not formalised". That is false, and a corpus-grep found the prior art after the file was written:

    • QOU.MassTheory.BondChargeRule.sumRule_like_pairs_differ already proves the like-pair difference, in an abstract charge parameterisation (sumRule c qi qj = (qi+qj−1)·c, giving 2c), and its own docstring cites bean qou-1pig and writes f_pp − f_nn = h = 2·(h/2). So bondOfTz_pp_sub_nn and the magnitude h/2 of bondOfTz_oddPart are not new; this file restates them in the T_z variable.
    • computations/probes/pair-layer-isospin-parity.witness.json already records the same thing as a measurement: "the charge-antisymmetric (isovector) part of the like-pair channel is h/2, forced exactly by the Hecke skein relation", together with a number this file does not produce — the isovector term is 1454× the isoscalar one — and the observation that bring_color_weighted cannot repair it, since it touches only the self-conjugate p–n factor.

    What is NOT in that prior art, and is what this file contributes:

    1. The iff, quantified over q — bondOfTz_even_iff / bondOfTz_even_iff_classical. The prior work computes the difference at the substrate; this characterises when it vanishes at all, and the answer is only q = 1. That turns a measured defect into a structural exclusion.
    2. The energy level, including the writhe term — writheExponent_odd shows the writhe normalisation is itself purely T_z-odd, so it adds a second odd term rather than cancelling the first; bondL_pp_ne_nn then follows from bondL_strictMono, already proved in the imported file and used rather than re-proved.
    3. The parity separation — multipletWeight_even and channels_opposite_parity, against bean qou-xwur's two-row channel.

    Related prior work that prices the obvious repair, and it LOSES. Bean qou-nial proposes replacing the centred sum rule with a charge product rule, under which prodRule_like_pairs_are_strong_baseline makes the strong channel exactly charge-symmetric. Measured over 12 nuclei ²H–⁵⁶Fe against AME2020 (bond_rule_dashboard_comparison_probe.py), mean |ln(model/AME)| goes 0.808 → 0.940: worse. Its A=3 mirror agreement is "a flat line crossing a rising curve" — flat in A, identical at A = 7 where the observed splitting has doubled. Its own recommendation is do not switch. So evenness in T_z is not by itself sufficient, and this file should not be read as arguing for that substitution.

          §7. A charge-blind multiplier cannot change the T_z parity ratio #

          Added 2026-09-14 (bean qou-vwu3), and it generalises a measurement rather than restating one. The extensive-term arc's one surviving lead is the Markov trace's failure of bond-multiplicativity: on an interleaved word class the trace of a product of bond letters is not the product of their traces (docs/audits/2026-09-03-markov-trace-not-bond-multiplicative-n4.md, bean qou-kru4). That defect reads the braid word beyond its linking data, which is exactly what bean qou-brng leaves open after closing the Ocneanu trace family, the magnitude, μ̄₁₂₃ and the whole linking-data class.

          Measured exactly this session with charge-dressed letters b_i = σ_i + d_i·1 (computations/probes/dressed_interleaved_cross_term_charge_parity_probe.py, 11/11, sympy over ℚ(q,d₀,d₁,d₂), with the bare limit reproducing the recorded closed form as its control):

          Δ(d₀,d₁,d₂) = (z + d₂) · (z² − h z − 1)          exactly,

          with d₀ and d₁ absent — the two interleaved bonds drop out, and only the Markov-peeled bond survives. So the defect is charge-sensitive, and its entire charge dependence is one bond factor times the charge-blind scalar z² − hz − 1.

          This section proves the consequence, and it is the general statement rather than the instance: multiplying by anything charge-blind cannot move a mirror odd/even ratio. Any proposal of the shape "keep the bond factor, multiply it by a new scalar" therefore inherits §3's exclusion verbatim, whatever the scalar is.

          🛑 SCOPE — the n = 4 factorisation does NOT generalise, measured the same day. parity_ratio_const_mul is a theorem about a charge-blind multiplier and is unconditional. What is word-specific is the premise that the cross term HAS that shape. The same probe extended to n = 5, with two re-crossings (b₀b₁b₀b₂b₁b₃b₂ against b₀²b₁²b₂²b₃) and symbolic q, gives

          Δ₅ = f₃ · (z² − hz − 1) · ( f₀² + f₂² + (q⁴+1)/(q²+1)² )

          — the peeled bond still factors out linearly, but the two interleaved bonds now enter quadratically (additively, with no f₀f₂ cross term), so the mirror-even part becomes charge-dependent and the parity ratio is no longer forced. Measured: Δ₅ odd/even ≠ f odd/even.

          So do not cite §7 as closing the word-class route. It closes the n = 4 cross term, and the mechanism it closes — "bond factor × charge-blind scalar" — whenever a construction actually has that shape. At n = 5 the route is open, and the interesting object is the sum of squares. Bean qou-vwu3.

                  §8. The parity ratio is EXACTLY linear in T_z, with slope (q⁴−1)/(q⁴+1) #

                  Added 2026-09-14 (bean qou-vwu3, second tranche). §7 shows the n = 4 cross term inherits the bond factor's parity ratio; this computes that ratio in closed form, and the answer is tighter than "inherits":

                  oddPart / evenPart  =  ((q⁴ − 1)/(q⁴ + 1)) · T_z        exactly.

                  So on the physical range |T_z| ≤ 1 the ratio is capped at (q⁴−1)/(q⁴+1), attained only at the pure like-pairs T_z = ±1. Nothing in the interleaved n = 4 word can exceed it, for any bond assignment.

                  Only algebra appears below. The comparison against a measured mirror splitting is deliberately NOT formalised — it belongs in the probe and the bean, not in a proof. What Lean carries is the cap; what the cap is worth is an empirical question asked elsewhere.

                  And the cap is a statement about n = 4 alone. Measured the same day, the achievable |ratio| at the proton anchor grows with word length — 0.2057 at n = 4, 0.3928 at n = 5, 0.4935 at n = 6 — because more dressings enter the kernel quadratically and the mirror image can be driven closer to zero. ratio = (a − b)/(a + b) with a, b the value and its mirror, so |ratio| < 1 exactly while a and b share a sign, and the envelope approaches 1 as the slot count grows. A longer word can therefore be made to bracket any target below 1, which is why reaching one by choosing n is selection against the target and not a derivation.
