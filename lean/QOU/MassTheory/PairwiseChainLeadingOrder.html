    The pairwise chain's leading-order error, exactly #

    Bean qou-zrx2, re-derived under qou-j1e5. Probes: computations/probes/binding_chain_floor_corrected_probe.py, computations/probes/c1_leading_coefficient_derived_probe.py, computations/probes/c1_letter_coefficients_are_ordering_dependent_probe.py.

    What this certifies #

    derive_binding_chain_cooperative models nuclear binding as a sum over the (A−1) bonds of a chain, each bond a 2-nucleon sub-braid, and compares that sum against the exact H_{3A} trace. How good is the pairwise approximation?

    Once the classical floor is removed (QOU.MassTheory.ClassicalTraceFloor), the corrected observable Δtr − c₀(A) is ℏ_q^1, and its leading coefficient c₁ = lim (Δtr − c₀)/ℏ_q is an exact dyadic rational in every measured case.

    🛑 RE-DERIVED UNDER THE BORROMEAN RULING (2026-09-06) #

    7db032fb48 (owner ruling, bean qou-hu1f) changed atom_braid_word_3A's default intra-nucleon ordering from grouped to borromean. The six values below are the Borromean ones. The previous grouped values are superseded but recorded here, because two of the three ratios move and a reader coming from the older audit documents needs to see both:

    ordering    pp      pn      nn     T        ³He     ⁴He
grouped     19/8    37/16   9/4    163/64   85/32   259/128   (SUPERSEDED)
borromean   1/8     1/16    0      1/64     1/8     7/128     (current)

    All six Borromean values are measured, and ⁴He twice over — the probe computes it by exact series and by the permutation count, and both give 7/128.

    c1_nn = 0 is a vanishing coefficient, not a small one. The neutron–neutron leading coefficient is exactly zero under the Borromean word, which is why chain_T below is c1_pn alone.

    The three ratios #

    T    (1/16 + 0)          / (1/64)    = 4
³He  (1/8  + 1/16)       / (1/8)     = 3/2
⁴He  (1/8  + 1/16 + 0)   / (7/128)   = 24/7

    The ⁴He 24/7 is STRUCTURAL — and that is the substantive change here #

    An earlier version of this file called 24/7 "the striking one — 259 = 7·37 and 888 = 24·37, so the 37 cancels", i.e. read it as a coincidence among six measured rationals. It is not a coincidence. The 37 is a + b, the sum of the two per-letter weights in the closed form

    c₁(Z,N) = (a·Z + b·N)(2^-(A+1) − 2^-2A) − 4(i_pp − i_nn)·2^-2A

    and chain_over_atom_He4_of_letters below proves that the ratio is 24/7 for every (a, b) with a + b ≠ 0, the (a+b) cancelling identically. So 259 = 7·37 held by construction, and 24/7 is invariant under the ordering change — it is the same number under (21, 16) and under (3, −2).

    The T and ³He ratios are not invariant (292/163 → 4, 30/17 → 3/2), which is what makes the ⁴He cancellation worth naming rather than a general fact about all three.

    ⚠ What the numbers say, and it is not flattering — unchanged #

    At leading order the three ratios are 4, 1.5, 3.429. The chain over-counts the exact trace by 24/7 at ⁴He as ℏ_q → 0.

    So pairwise additivity of the signed trace functor is not a controlled approximation. Its good behaviour at q₀ comes from higher-order terms partially cancelling a large leading-order error, not from a structural property that would survive to other q. chain_over_atom_He4_gt_three below is the formal version of that: the leading-order ratio exceeds 3. That conclusion survives the ordering change intact, and now with a stronger warrant, since the ⁴He ratio is proved rather than measured.

    This concerns the signed functor tr_free − tr_atom. The exact-additivity result in docs/audits/2026-06-16-b-sym-linear-chain-closed-form.md is about the log functor α·ln(tr_free/tr_atom) — a different object, not contradicted here, but easy to conflate.

                The closed form, and why 24/7 is not a coincidence #

                The six coefficients are generated by one formula with two per-letter weights (a, b) — see QOU.MassTheory.LeadingOrderTraceCoefficient for the derivation of its shape from the permutation count. The ordering ruling moves (a, b) from (21, 16) to (3, −2) and changes nothing else.

                source
                def QOU.MassTheory.c1Closed (a b : ℚ) (Z N : ℕ) :
                ℚ

                The closed form of the leading coefficient at letter weights (a, b):

                c₁(Z,N) = (a·Z + b·N)(2^-(A+1) − 2^-2A) − 4(i_pp − i_nn)·2^-2A

                with A = Z + N, i_pp = max(Z−1, 0), i_nn = max(N−1, 0). Natural subtraction already truncates at zero, so Z - 1 is i_pp.

                Equations
                • QOU.MassTheory.c1Closed a b Z N = (a * ↑Z + b * ↑N) * (2⁻¹ ^ (Z + N + 1) - 2⁻¹ ^ (2 * (Z + N))) - 4 * (↑(Z - 1) - ↑(N - 1)) * 2⁻¹ ^ (2 * (Z + N))

                  theorem QOU.MassTheory.he4_of_letters (a b : ℚ) :

                  The ⁴He atom term is 7(a+b)/128. This is where the 7 in 259 = 7·37 comes from — it is 2^-5 − 2^-8 times two, not a property of 37.

                  theorem QOU.MassTheory.chain_over_atom_He4_of_letters (a b : ℚ) (hab : a + b ≠ 0) :
                  (c1Closed a b 2 0 + c1Closed a b 1 1 + c1Closed a b 0 2) / c1Closed a b 2 2 = 24 / 7

                  The ⁴He chain-over-atom ratio is 24/7 for every (a, b).

                  The (a + b) cancels identically, so the ratio does not depend on the letter weights at all — hence it is the same under the grouped weights (21, 16) and the Borromean weights (3, −2), and the 37 that appeared to cancel "strikingly" in the older reading is simply a + b.

                  theorem QOU.MassTheory.chain_ratios_T_He3_not_invariant :
                  (c1Closed 3 (-2) 1 1 + c1Closed 3 (-2) 0 2) / c1Closed 3 (-2) 1 2 ≠ (c1Closed 21 16 1 1 + c1Closed 21 16 0 2) / c1Closed 21 16 1 2 ∧ (c1Closed 3 (-2) 2 0 + c1Closed 3 (-2) 1 1) / c1Closed 3 (-2) 2 1 ≠ (c1Closed 21 16 2 0 + c1Closed 21 16 1 1) / c1Closed 21 16 2 1

                  The T and ³He chain-over-atom ratios really do depend on the ordering.

                  Stated as a disequality so it cannot be read as a claim about which value is right — both are correct, for their own word.

                  T     292/163 ≈ 1.7914  (grouped)  →  4      (Borromean)
³He    30/17  ≈ 1.7647  (grouped)  →  3/2    (Borromean)

                  This is the counterpoint that gives chain_over_atom_He4_of_letters its force. There the (a + b) cancels identically, so ⁴He's 24/7 holds for every letter weighting and survives any intra-nucleon reordering. T and ³He have Z ≠ N, no such cancellation, and move — so the ⁴He invariance is a property of that combination, not a general feature of the chain ratios.

                  Added on top of the qou-j1e5 derivation rather than alongside it: a parallel branch (qou-pdg9, PR #6920) reached the same generic 24/7 independently and deferred to this file, and this disequality is the one statement that branch had which this file did not.
