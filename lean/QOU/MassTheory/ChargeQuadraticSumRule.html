    QOU.MassTheory.ChargeQuadraticSumRule — what a quadratic-in-charge EM term can and cannot say #

    Bean qou-bn8e. The corpus derives the charge structure of the neutron–proton electromagnetic self-energy difference (QOU.MassTheory.NeutronProtonEMSign), using the interaction invariant Σ_{i<j} qᵢqⱼ alone and leaving one coupling free. This file asks what the general quadratic-in-charge functional on a three-strand carrier permits, and finds two things the single-invariant treatment cannot see.

    Two invariants, not one #

    The symmetric quadratics in (q₁, q₂, q₃) form a two-dimensional space. A basis is the self term Σ qᵢ² and the interaction term Σ_{i<j} qᵢqⱼ; the total charge enters as Q² = Σqᵢ² + 2Σqᵢqⱼ, not independently. So the general functional is

    E = A · Σqᵢ² + B · Σ_{i<j} qᵢqⱼ.

    Using Σqᵢqⱼ alone is the choice A = 0 — the standard renormalisation convention, in which each quark's own field energy is absorbed into its mass. It is a choice, it is not derived, and NeutronProtonEMSign does not flag it.

    1. The nucleon datum cannot separate A from B #

    chargeSelf and chargeInter differ between proton and neutron by the same amount, 1/3 each (pn_self_gap, pn_inter_gap). Hence E(p) − E(n) = (A + B)/3 (pn_energy_gap): the nucleon splitting constrains only the sum. Bean qou-bn8e previously recorded that "one scalar remains"; that was wrong — two remain, and one datum fixes one combination of them.

    The corpus's sign result is unaffected and in fact generalises: the gap is positive whenever A + B > 0, a weaker hypothesis than B > 0, and both couplings are positive for a Coulomb-like energy.

    2. Coleman–Glashow is implied — but it is NOT new, and NOT a prediction here #

    Retracted claim (2026-08-29). An earlier version of this file presented the Coleman–Glashow relation as "the first parameter-free consequence the EM sector yields on this branch" and as "a genuine prediction". Both are wrong, and the corpus already said so.

    QOU/Eightfold/ColemanGlashowIdentity.lean proves the relation sorry-free (coleman_glashow_identity) from a weaker premise than this file's: EM self-energy constant on a U-spin multiplet, with three free shifts a, b, c at charge +1, 0, −1. The block eightfold-way/u-spin-coleman-glashow.md records the same measured residual this file quotes — −0.064 MeV, 0.8 % of the largest splitting — and states the epistemics plainly: "Reproducing Coleman–Glashow would therefore be no evidence for this framework, or for any other." The quadratic-charge ansatz here is a specialisation of that three-parameter family, so CG follows a fortiori and adds nothing.

    2a. What the ansatz actually does on the octet: it saturates, so it predicts nothing #

    The sharper statement, and the one that is this file's own, is negative. The octet's observable EM content is two-dimensional: the three splittings p−n, Σ⁺−Σ⁻, Ξ⁰−Ξ⁻ obey CG, leaving two independent, and an overall shift of a, b, c moves all eight masses together and is absorbed into the strong parameters. In terms of (A, B) those two observables are

    a − b = (A + B)/3, b − c = (A − 2B)/3,

    a linear map of determinant −1/3 ≠ 0 (octet_em_map_det_ne_zero), hence a bijection onto the whole two-dimensional space (octet_em_pattern_surjective, which exhibits A = 2u + v, B = u − v).

    Two consequences, both unwelcome and both worth stating:

    • The ansatz excludes no octet EM pattern. Whatever the three splittings had come out to, some (A, B) reproduces them. So it makes no falsifiable octet prediction — not CG, not anything else.
    • A and B are exactly determined by the octet data, i.e. fitted. The bijection is the obstruction, not a route: any derivation of the couplings must come from outside the octet.

    What this does NOT do #

    It does not derive either coupling, so qou-bn8e's target — the magnitude — is untouched. It says the magnitude question needs a second measured splitting to separate A from B, and that the Σ triplet supplies one (its configuration difference is (2/3, −1/3), not proportional to the nucleon's (1/3, 1/3)) — but supplying one fits the couplings rather than deriving them, which is exactly the move qou-bn8e refuses.

    It also does not claim the EM self-energy is quadratic in the charges, and — correcting this file's earlier wording — Coleman–Glashow holding is not evidence for that hypothesis. By octet_em_pattern_surjective the ansatz reproduces every octet EM pattern, so CG could not have come out otherwise; a test no candidate can fail discriminates nothing. u-spin-coleman-glashow.md reaches the same verdict from the weaker U-spin premise. The hypothesis is untested by the octet, and testing it needs data the octet does not contain.

    Everything is over ℚ — charges and couplings are rational, no archimedean construct appears (§7c).

                theorem QOU.MassTheory.ChargeQuadraticSumRule.coleman_glashow (A B : ℚ) :
                energy A B qU qD qD - energy A B qU qU qD - (energy A B qD qD qS - energy A B qU qU qS) + (energy A B qD qS qS - energy A B qU qS qS) = 0

                Coleman–Glashow holds for the ansatz — as an implication, not as a result of this file. For every pair of couplings (A, B),

                (E(n) − E(p)) − (E(Σ⁻) − E(Σ⁺)) + (E(Ξ⁻) − E(Ξ⁰)) = 0.

                Kept only so the specialisation is visibly consistent with the corpus.                 theorem QOU.MassTheory.ChargeQuadraticSumRule.sigma_gap_independent :
                chargeSelf qU qU qS - chargeSelf qD qD qS = 2 / 3 ∧ chargeInter qU qU qS - chargeInter qD qD qS = -1 / 3

                The Σ triplet is not degenerate the way the nucleon is: its configuration difference is (2/3, −1/3), which is not proportional to the nucleon's (1/3, 1/3). So Σ⁺ − Σ⁻ together with p − n does separate A from B.

                source
                theorem QOU.MassTheory.ChargeQuadraticSumRule.pn_sigma_determinant_ne_zero :
                (chargeSelf qU qU qD - chargeSelf qU qD qD) * (chargeInter qU qU qS - chargeInter qD qD qS) - (chargeInter qU qU qD - chargeInter qU qD qD) * (chargeSelf qU qU qS - chargeSelf qD qD qS) ≠ 0

                Made precise: the two configuration-difference vectors are linearly independent, so the two splittings determine A and B separately. The determinant (1/3)(−1/3) − (1/3)(2/3) = −1/3 is nonzero.
