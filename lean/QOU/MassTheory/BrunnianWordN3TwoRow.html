    QOU.MassTheory.BrunnianWordN3TwoRow — the concrete A=3 Brunnian word in the two-row sector #

    Instance brick 3, minimal wedge (qou-v7ro). The ∀A convention-D deficit recursion D_{A+1} = −h²(h²+2)D_A is proved mathematically and certified (computations/deltalambda/milnor_recursion_forall_A_proof_probe.py) and its field-algebra glue is Lean (QOU.MassTheory.DeficitRecursionReduction). The R2 crux deferred by the brick-3 roast (docs/audits/2026-08-31-milnor-brick3-roast.md, re-roast 2026-09-03-milnor-brick3-reroast.md) is a concrete evaluable ρ(w_n) for the Brunnian word w_n. This file supplies it at the base case n = 3 in the surviving two-row sector, and closes the loop to the certified deficit there.

    What is the object #

    The A=3 Brunnian word is w₃ = [A₀₁, A₁₂], the band-commutator of the two adjacent pure-braid bands A₀₁ = σ₁², A₁₂ = σ₂² in B₃ (the W3_BAND = band_comm(A01, A12) of computations/deltalambda/a4_brunnian_deficit_closed_form.py; b = A_{n-1,n} = σ²_{n-1}). By Schur–Weyl for U_q(sl₂) on V^{⊗3} (V = K²), only the ≤ 2-row partitions of 3 survive the two-row cap (QOU.MassTheory.TwoRowCapDerived): (3) and (2,1). The (2,1) two-row irrep of H₃(q) is the 2-dim reduced-Burau representation whose generators are the sorry-free QOU.MassTheory.GermRootVectorGauge.heckeGen1 / heckeGen2. So brunnianW3 below is the genuine, concrete ρ_{(2,1)}(w₃).

    What is PROVED here (sorry-free, generic Field) #

    • heckeGen2_mul_inv / heckeGen2Inv_mul — the missing second generator inverse (the first is in GermRootVectorGaugeFixing).
    • brunnianW3_eq — the concrete 2×2 matrix of the word, entrywise.
    • brunnianW3_det = 1 — a commutator of det = ±1 bands (the (2,1) word is SL₂).
    • brunnianW3_trace — the exact (2,1) two-row character χ^{(2,1)}(w₃) as a rational in q.
    • deficitA3_eq_closed_form — the payoff: feeding this concrete (2,1) trace through the Schur–Weyl reconstruction qtr(ρ(w₃)) = [4]·χ^{(3)}(w₃) + [2]·χ^{(2,1)}(w₃) (the two surviving rows) and the deficit D₃ = 1 − qtr/[2]³ yields exactly the certified closed form D_A = (−1)^{A−3} h^{2(A−1)} (h²+2)^{A−3} (h²+3)/(h²+4) at A = 3, i.e. D₃ = h⁴(h²+3)/(h²+4).

    What is CITED, not proved (the honest seam) #

    The Schur–Weyl decomposition itself — that qtr on V^{⊗3} factors as Σ_λ qdim(V_λ)·χ^λ(w) with the ≤2-row cap, the sl₂ quantum dimensions qdim(V_{(3)}) = [4], qdim(V_{(2,1)}) = [2], and the fact χ^{(3)}(w₃) = 1 (a commutator in the 1-dim symmetric channel σ_i ↦ q) — is standard representation theory, used here as the definition of survivingQTrace. Lean does not prove Schur–Weyl duality. What Lean proves is that given that reconstruction, the concretely-computed (2,1) trace reproduces the certified deficit at A = 3. This is the base-case verification of the sharpened route (reduced Burau + two-row cap), not a proof of the ∀n instance — the position-indexed e_{n-1} on the reduced space ∀n and the Brunnian-word spectrum recursion remain brick 3's general-n body (still open, qou-v7ro; the BrunnianDeficitRecursion instance stays unfabricated).
