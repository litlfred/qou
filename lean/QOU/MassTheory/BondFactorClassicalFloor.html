    The body-number expansion has a classical floor: no power counting in ε = q − 1 #

    Bean qou-olxi, attack option 1 (owner: "do as suggested", 2026-09-15). The question was whether the many-body (body-number) expansion of the binding is ordered in the deformation parameter ε = q − 1 — the ingredient a Weinberg-style power counting over body number would need. The answer is no, and the reason is an identity rather than a measurement.

    The interface (bond) factors of the multiplicative assembly are z + d_kind (bond_factors_are_z_plus_d_probe), with z = z_D = q³/(q² + 1) the convention-D Markov parameter and d_kind the kind-dependent correction, which is O(ε) for all three interface kinds:

    kind                z + d at q = 1 + ε          d
pp   (σ)            1/2 +  ε   + ε²/4           0
pn   (averaged)     1/2 +  0   + 3ε²/4         −ε   + ε²/2
nn   (σ⁻¹)          1/2 −  ε   + 5ε²/4         −2ε  + ε²

    So at the classical point every bond factor is z_D(1) = 1/2 (zD_one, bondFactor_classical), its Mayer variable is −1/2, and the connected three-body cumulant of the assembly — b₃ = −φᵀ₃ by b3corpus_eq_neg_phi3 — is

    b₃(1/2, 1/2, 1/2) = −(1 − 1/2)²·(1/2 + 2) = −5/8 ≠ 0     (`b3corpus_classical`).

    Its ε-valuation is therefore 0: the three-body term is not suppressed relative to the two-body term by any power of ε, and the same holds for b₄. What is ordered in the corpus is the jet tower, T(ρ) = Σ_{n ≥ 1} ℏ_q^{2n} Δ^{(n)}(ρ) with remainder ratio q⁻² (rem:brane-tower-q-laplacian-decay-interpretation): the expansion parameter of the framework counts jet level, not body number. The body-number expansion at fixed q is a lattice-gas cluster expansion with O(1) weights, and its convergence is a Mayer–Ursell question, not a power-counting one.

    Scope: this is a scope-(b) exclusion — the invariant is the identity z_D(1) = 1/2 — of the ordering in ε; it says nothing against orderings in another parameter. The d_kind series are the probe's measured coefficients, carried here as truncations at second order and used only through their vanishing at ε = 0. Generic field of characteristic zero (§7c); no numerics; no sorry.
