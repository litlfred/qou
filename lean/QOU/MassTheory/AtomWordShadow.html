    The q = 1 shadow of the atom word, and both of ClassicalTraceFloor's counts #

    Bean qou-tjys. Probe: computations/probes/atom_word_merge_count_probe.py. Successor to qou-qro1 (OrbitCount.lean), which proved the general transposition step; this file applies it to the specific word.

    What this closes #

    ClassicalTraceFloor states its step 2 as two hypotheses, both "verified for A = 2 … 20 directly from the words":

    hfree  : cFree = 2 * A
hmerge : cFree = cAtom + (A - 1)

    Both follow from one lemma here, orbitDefect_freshProd.

    Why the word has a permutation shadow at all #

    atom_braid_word_3A is not a braid word: every letter is the Hecke element c·σ_g + d·1, and 2A+1 of them are averaged, so the object lies outside the image of ρ : B_n → H_n(q) and carries no link. At q = 1, though, h = q − q⁻¹ is 0, so every offset template {0, −h, −h/2, −h(½−δ/4)} vanishes at once and each letter degenerates to the transposition s_g. The q = 1 shadow is therefore fixed by the g-index sequence alone.

    The shape, measured rather than assumed #

    The probe reports (A = 2 … 20, all (Z,N), 228 pairs) that each nucleon block's net shadow is a single transposition — swap (3k) (3k+1), the same one for every k, proton or neutron, its eleven letters collapsing to one — and that the whole word's shadow is, in word order,

    swap 0 1,  then for k = 1 … A−1:   swap (3k−1) (3k),  swap (3k) (3k+1)

    i.e. 2A − 1 transpositions. The free word drops the swap (3k−1) (3k) interfaces and is the A disjoint blocks.

    The one property that matters is that each transposition's right endpoint is fresh — never an endpoint of any earlier letter. The right endpoints run 1, 3, 4, 6, 7, 9, 10, …, strictly increasing, so this is immediate. A fresh endpoint is a fixed point of the partial product, and two distinct points one of which is fixed cannot share a cycle — so orbitDefect_swap_mul_of_not_sameCycle applies at every step with its hypothesis met rather than assumed.

    Scope #

    Fresh/freshProd and orbitDefect_freshProd are general: any list of transpositions with fresh right endpoints. Nothing in them mentions nucleons, and they are the reusable content. What is not in this file is a Lean definition of atom_braid_word_3A itself — the shadow's index pattern is taken from the probe, which is why the concrete corollaries below name it as their source. Formalising the producer would be a separate and much larger undertaking.

        The concrete shadow, and both of ClassicalTraceFloor's counts #

        The index patterns below are taken from the probe, which measures them on the live producer; Lean does not know what atom_braid_word_3A returns. So what these give is "if the shadow is this list, then the counts are 2A and A+1" — the same epistemic shape as ClassicalTraceFloor's own hypotheses, but with the counting now proved instead of computed. That is the strengthening: two numerical hypotheses (hfree, hmerge) are replaced by one structural one about the word's letters, which is far more directly checkable against the producer.

        Indices are handled in ℕ and transported through an injection, so no Fin arithmetic appears.
