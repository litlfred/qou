            Writhe / framing is ruled out too #

            The Markov trace is a framed-link invariant; the ambient (unframed) Jones/HOMFLY invariant differs by a writhe-dependent framing factor ~ q₀^{c·w}. If the master formula omits it, the deficit could carry that factor. But the canonical words give writhe(D) = 11−7 = 4 = 16−12 = writhe(T) (averaged interface crossings are writhe-neutral), and writhe(³He) = writhe(⁴He) = 8 — yet C_knot differs in BOTH equal-writhe pairs (6.068 vs 6.706; 4.905 vs 290.01). So no writhe-only framing factor f(w) closes the deficit. The pair argument is what carries the exclusion — it needs only that the two members of an equal-writhe pair differ, so it is untouched by the convention refresh below. (Framing hypothesis contributed by a parallel session, tested + excluded here.)

                A structural REASON for non-universality: the chirality regime (−1)^{C(3A,2)} #

                Beyond the mirror inequality (which shows per-knot content within a level), there is a clean level-parity reason the deuteron cannot share a C_knot regime with the heavier isotopes. Under conjugation the n-statistic satisfies the Macdonald identity n(λ) + n(λ') = C(n,2) (verified numerically at n = 6: all five conjugate pairs flip the binding sign, the self-conjugate (3,2,1) is fixed). Hence the binding sign sgn(λ) = (−1)^{n(λ)} obeys sgn(λ') = (−1)^{C(n,2)} · sgn(λ): it antisymmetrizes conjugate pairs iff C(n,2) is odd.

                theorem QOU.MassTheory.CKnotNonUniversal.sgn_conj (nl nl' : ℕ) :
                (-1) ^ nl' = (-1) ^ (nl + nl') * (-1) ^ nl

                The conjugation law for the binding sign (pure exponent arithmetic, no hypothesis needed): (−1)^{n(λ')} = (−1)^{n(λ) + n(λ')} · (−1)^{n(λ)}. With the Macdonald identity n(λ) + n(λ') = C(n,2) this is sgn(λ') = (−1)^{C(n,2)} · sgn(λ) — the chirality regime is fixed by the parity of C(n,2).

                theorem QOU.MassTheory.CKnotNonUniversal.deuteron_chirality_regime_differs :
                Nat.choose 6 2 % 2 = 1 ∧ Nat.choose 9 2 % 2 = 0 ∧ Nat.choose 12 2 % 2 = 0

                The deuteron sits in a DIFFERENT chirality regime than the heavier light isotopes. With n = 3A: C(6,2) = 15 is ODD (A=2, deuteron → sgn antisymmetrizes conjugate pairs, bar-odd-projected) while C(9,2) = 36 (A=3, T/³He) and C(12,2) = 66 (A=4, ⁴He) are EVEN (sgn preserved). So the deuteron's binding-sign structure is qualitatively unlike A ≥ 3 — a structural reason C_knot(D) cannot lie on the same universal curve as the heavier isotopes, independent of the mirror inequality above.
