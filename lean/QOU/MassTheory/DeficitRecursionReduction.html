      deficit b G (n + 1) = -h ^ 2 * (h ^ 2 + 2) * deficit b G n

      The algebraic reduction (sorry-free). From the Temperley–Lieb identity (I) G_k = b^k(1+h²) − h²·Q_k (at n and n+1) and the 2-term Q-recursion Q_{n+1} = b^{n+1}(1 + (h²+2)h²) − (h²+2)h²·b·Q_n, the deficit obeys D_{n+1} = −h²(h²+2)·D_n. This is the field-algebra core of the ∀A Milnor deficit recursion; the two hypotheses are exactly the TL facts the accompanying probe establishes.
