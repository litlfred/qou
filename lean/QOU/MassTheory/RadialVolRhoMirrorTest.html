    False

    Mirror test refutes ρ = tr_M under equal Vol. Assume the radial ansatz with a common volume: C_knot(T) = Vol·tr_M(T)/ℏ_q² and C_knot(³He) = Vol·tr_M(³He)/ℏ_q² at the witnessed values. Then the ratio identity forces C_knot(T)/C_knot(³He) = tr_M(T)/tr_M(³He), but the former is > 1 and the latter < 1 — contradiction. Hence no common Vol, ℏ_q² reproduce C_knot from ρ = tr_M.
