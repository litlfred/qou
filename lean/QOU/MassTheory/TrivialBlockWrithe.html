    QOU.MassTheory.TrivialBlockWrithe — the 1-dim Hecke block characters are writhe monomials #

    Formalize-first prune for the charm-mass Frobenius/Wedderburn block-projection route (docs/audits/2026-08-02-charm-mass-frobenius-block-projection-negative.md, PR #4594). The generation hierarchy was tested as the ratio of $H_3$ Wedderburn blocks $y_\lambda\chi_\lambda$ on the Borromean baryon braid and came out $O(1)$ (1.35), not the observed $O(100)$ charm/light hierarchy.

    This file proves the structural reason: the two 1-dimensional block characters of $H_n(q)$ — the trivial $[n]$ block ($\sigma_i\mapsto q$) and the sign $[1^n]$ block ($\sigma_i\mapsto -q^{-1}$) — are pure writhe monomials:

    χ_triv(β) = q ^ writhe(β), χ_sign(β) = (-q⁻¹) ^ writhe(β).

    Consequently, on a writhe-balanced braid (writhe $= 0$, e.g. the Borromean baryon $(\sigma_1\sigma_2^{-1})^3$) both 1-dim block characters collapse to 1 independent of q — they carry no winding / generation information, so their mutual ratio is pinned to 1 and any hierarchy would have to come entirely from the remaining 2-dimensional [2,1] block. That last step is not proved here (no bound on the [2,1] character is claimed), so this is a scope-(a) sharpening of the block-ratio negative, not a structural exclusion of the complex-mass functional: it says do not look for the generation hierarchy in the 1-dim blocks at balanced writhe, and it measures how much the 2-dim block would have to supply.

    A braid word is modelled as List ℤ: each letter is a signed generator (nonzero integer; the sign is the crossing sign $\pm$, the magnitude is the generator index, irrelevant to a 1-dim representation where every $\sigma_i$ maps to the same scalar). The writhe is the sum of the crossing signs.

    Base ring (§7c): generic [DivisionRing R] — the identities are pure algebra (zpow over a nonzero scalar, plus the ring negation for the sign block's -q⁻¹), no archimedean construct. q ≠ 0 is the only hypothesis (a Hecke eigenvalue is a unit).

    This file is sorry-free and self-contained: it imports only Mathlib and depends on no QOU block. The route it prunes is scoped against conj:complex-mass-constituent-functional, which is not yet on main (open draft PR #4584) — that is a forward reference for the reader, not a dependency.
