              theorem QOU.MassTheory.transferFactor_comm {R : Type u_1} [CommRing R] (h d₁ d₂ : R) :
              (transferFactor h d₁).mul (transferFactor h d₂) = (transferFactor h d₂).mul (transferFactor h d₁)

              Any two transfer factors of the same chain commute.

              Not a triviality: the carrier is non-commutative (M2.mulNotComm) and the readout phiReadout is not symmetric in the entries. Commutation holds because every factor is S + d · 1 for one and the same S.

                  theorem QOU.MassTheory.he3_ppn_pnp_readout_eq {R : Type u_1} [CommRing R] (h z a b : R) :
                  phiReadout z (chainProd h [a, b]) = phiReadout z (chainProd h [b, a])

                  The ³He nucleon-level degeneracy, as a theorem.

                  For a proton at position 0 the later strands of ppn contribute d(p,p) = a then d(p,n) = b, while those of pnp contribute them in the opposite order. The objective cannot tell the two arrangements apart.
