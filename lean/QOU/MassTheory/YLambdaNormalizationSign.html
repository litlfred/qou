    sign(Z) = +1, and hence sign(y_λ) = (−1)^{P(λ)} unconditionally #

    YLambdaSignThreshold (#4662) proved the Wenzl principal specialisation has sign(s_λ) = (−1)^{P(λ)}, P(λ) the number of cells below the content threshold. Since y_λ = s_λ / Z, that determined sign(y_λ) only up to the single global sign of Z per n — the probe verified sign(Z) = +1 for n = 2..12, but nothing derived it. That was the honest residual hole; this file closes it.

    The closed form for Z #

    Z = Σ_{λ ⊢ n} f^λ · s_λ is the normalisation enforcing Σ_λ y_λ f^λ = tr_M(1) = 1. The classical Schur-basis expansion of the first power sum,

    Σ_{λ ⊢ n} f^λ s_λ  =  p₁ⁿ                     (Macdonald I.7; Stanley EC2 §7)

    applies verbatim, because the Wenzl weight is the principal specialisation s_λ(1, Q, …, Q^{r−1}). There p₁ = 1 + Q + ⋯ + Q^{r−1} = (1 − Qʳ)/(1 − Q), so

    Z = ((1 − Qʳ)/(1 − Q))ⁿ                       (`normalizationClosedForm`)

    Verified against the direct sum to ~1e-51 for every n = 2..12 (computations/deltalambda/ylambda_normalization_closed_form_probe.py).

    Why the sign is forced #

    In the substrate regime Q > 1, and Qʳ > 1 as well (at q₀, Qʳ ≈ 1.720). So both 1 − Qʳ and 1 − Q are negative, their ratio is positive, and Z is a positive number raised to a natural power:

    0 < Z                                          (`normalization_pos`)

    for every n — including n = 0, where the empty product is 1. Note this is not obvious from the summands: Z is a sum whose terms f^λ s_λ have mixed signs (that is exactly the content of #4662). Positivity comes from the closed form, not from term-by-term positivity.

    Combining with YLambdaSignThreshold.wenzl_sign:

    sign(y_λ) = sign(s_λ)/sign(Z) = (−1)^{P(λ)}    (`y_sign_of_closedForm`)

    with no residual global-sign ambiguity.

    Honest scope #

    The identity Σ f^λ s_λ = p₁ⁿ is a classical theorem of symmetric-function theory, cited rather than re-proved here — formalising the Schur-basis expansion is a substantial Mathlib project of its own. It enters the Lean statements as an explicit hypothesis (hZ : Z = …), so every theorem below is a rigorous conditional on a published, numerically re-verified identity — not on a conjecture. Per AGENTS.md §"Conditional-proved ≠ premise-established": the premise here is established literature, and the hZ binder is where it is discharged by citation.

    What is genuinely proved sorry-free: that the closed form is positive, and that sign(y_λ) follows.
