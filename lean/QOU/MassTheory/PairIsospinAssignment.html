              4. What it costs the bond factors #

              5. Level 3 versus level 4, as a theorem rather than as prose #

              shell-schur-weyl-self-duality-interpretation asserts that Schur–Weyl self-duality is exact at level 4 (the shell) while conjugate symmetry breaks at level 3 (the inter-nucleon pair). Sections 1–2 give the level-3 half: no_selfDual says the pair has no self-conjugate partition at all. This section supplies the level-4 half at the first shell, n = 4, where (2,2) is self-conjugate — so the asymmetry between the two levels is not a stipulation about them, it is a fact about partitions of 2 and of 4.
