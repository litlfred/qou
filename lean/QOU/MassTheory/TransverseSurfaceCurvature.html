    QOU.MassTheory.TransverseSurfaceCurvature #

    Formalize-first (forward, geometric) structure of the transverse non-abelian-Stokes surface-curvature — the named surviving carrier of the deuteron binding magnitude after braid-word, scale, level, edge, and DT-count are all excluded (bean qou-prjo; docs/audits/2026-07-24-eunit-magnus-order2.md §2, docs/audits/2026-08-02-deltalambda-value-proved-downstream-and-additive-term.md §4).

    What this file IS (and is NOT) #

    The order-2 Magnus term of the surface-ordered exponential is the transverse surface-curvature pairing Ω₂ = ½ P ∮∮ [𝓕, 𝓕], quadratic in the germ commutator q-curvature F_q = [δq₁, δq₂] (germ-commutator-q-curvature.md), where 𝓕(x) = U(x,O)⁻¹ F_q U(x,O) is the path-dependent (parallel- transported) curvature. It lives transverse to the strand ordering — the two-observer-parallelogram direction — so it survives even on the single-generator deuteron channel σ₁³ where every inter-crossing (path) Magnus commutator vanishes identically ([a,a]=0; audit §2).

    This file formalises, sorry-free and forward, the algebraic structure of that transverse pairing plus the Seifert-surface combinatorics it is carried on. It does NOT derive the deuteron magnitude:

    sorry-free ≠ derived; axiomatising the curvature is NOT deriving its magnitude (AGENTS.md). The numerical magnitude requires the actual position-dependent transport U(x,O) across the Seifert surface — the "canonical discretisation of the Yekutieli surface 2-form" that bean qou-prjo names as the missing corpus data. The wall is formalised below (transverse_nonzero_needs_noncommuting_curvature): a nonzero transverse magnitude forces the local curvatures to differ across the surface, i.e. requires exactly that transport-twist data.

    No q₀, no AME target, zero free parameters, nothing inverted.

    Part A — Seifert-surface combinatorics of a braid diagram #

    Seifert's algorithm on a connected positive-braid diagram β on s strands with c crossing letters gives a spanning surface Σ with s Seifert disks (2-cells) glued by c twisted bands (1-cells). Its Euler characteristic is χ(Σ) = s − c, so the first Betti number (twice the genus, for a one-boundary knot surface) is b₁(Σ) = 1 − χ = c − s + 1 = 2g. This integer counts the independent transverse 2-cycles (handles) available to carry surface curvature.

This is the forward geometric statement that the transverse surface curvature can carry an ∝ A (extensive) binding term — the shape the corpus's missing additive term must have.

      Part B — the transverse (surface-ordered) curvature pairing #

      Discretise the Seifert surface into 2-cells x₀, x₁, … in surface order, and record at each cell the parallel-transported germ curvature 𝓕ᵢ = U(xᵢ,O)⁻¹ F_q U(xᵢ,O) ∈ R (an element of the Hecke-image ring R). The order-2 Magnus / transverse term ½ P ∮∮ [𝓕,𝓕] discretises to the ordered double sum over cell pairs (later, earlier) of the curvature commutator. We work over a generic (non-commutative) Ring R.

            Trace/character annihilation — the transverse term is invisible to the #

            scalar Markov trace

            Any cyclic linear functional τ (τ(a·b)=τ(b·a) — the Markov trace / a Wedderburn block character χ^λ) annihilates every curvature commutator, hence the whole transverse pairing. This is the formal reason the transverse surface curvature cannot contribute to binding through the scalar trace channel (why the scalar-trace binding B = E₀·Δtr_M saturates); any surviving contribution must be operator-valued (eigenphase-weighted / non-trace) — the channel independently named in the sibling probes #4619 and 2026-07-24-eunit-magnus-order2.md §2.

            Part C — scalar magnitude envelope (diagonal / additive reading) #

            The diagonal (per-handle) envelope of the transverse magnitude assigns one unit κ² = ‖F_q‖² per independent handle, i.e. ½ · b₁ · κ². This is the additive reading; by seifertBettiOne_link_additive it is exactly linear in the crossing count, hence pairwise-capped and unable to supply the ≥3-body enhancement. The genuine (off-diagonal) content is listPairing (Part B), which needs the transport data. This wrapper exists only to state the additive envelope explicitly and its trefoil/flat values.
