    QOU.MassTheory.GermRootVectorGaugeFixing #

    The transverse non-abelian-Stokes surface pairing is gauge-FIXED on a single-generator braid channel, and the determined value is zero.

    This closes the question left open by the trefoil discretization (docs/audits/2026-08-08-nastokes-surface-curvature-trefoil-negative.md): that probe measured c₂ = ‖Ω₂^⊥‖/h² ranging over 0 … 897 across "natural" germ / handle-holonomy choices and read the spread as O(100×) gauge freedom. It is not freedom. Two conventions the corpus already pins collapse the orbit to a point, and the point is 0.

    The two conventions #

    1. Germ. def:germ-commutator-q-curvature defines δq_i as a germ-level increment of the substrate parameter q — the logarithmic q-derivative q·(∂_q σ_i)·σ_i⁻¹, not log σ_i (which is the braid-direction connection 1-form, whose commutator is the inter-crossing Magnus object, already known to vanish identically on a single-generator word). In the 2-dim Hecke representation the two germs are q-independent constant matrices (germOne_eq, germTwo_eq below), so the germ q-curvature F_q = ⁅δq₁, δq₂⁆ is the pure root vector -4E (germCurvature_eq), nilpotent (germCurvature_sq).

    2. Transport. Yekutieli path-ordering (QOU.MathlibExt.YekutieliIntegration) makes the transport to a band the ordered partial product along the word, and Seifert's algorithm on a braid closure puts bands ↔ letters (discs ↔ strands; seifertBettiOne = c - s + 1 in TransverseSurfaceCurvature). On the deuteron word σ₁³ every partial product is a power of ρ(σ₁); the generator σ₂ never occurs, so transports mentioning it are inadmissible.

    What is proved here (sorry-free) #

    • Abstract (generic CommRing scalars, generic Ring algebra). transported_eq_smul: if F is an Ad u-eigenvector (root vector) with eigenvalue c, then conjugation by uᵏ returns cᵏ • F — every transported curvature is a scalar multiple of one and the same element. comm_smul_smul: two scalar multiples of a common element commute. transversePairing_eq_zero: therefore the intersection-form-weighted transverse pairing Σᵢⱼ Jᵢⱼ • ⁅𝓕ᵢ, 𝓕ⱼ⁆ vanishes for every intersection form J, every H₁ basis / spanning tree, and every handle-exponent assignment. The vanishing is gauge-independent, not gauge-fixed by fiat.

    • Concrete (2-dim Hecke rep over a generic Field). The generators satisfy the braid relation (heckeGen_braid) and the Hecke relation (heckeGen1_hecke), certifying the representation; the germ curvature is the root vector -4E with Ad(σ₁) F = (-q²) • F (germCurvature_conj_heckeGen1); and the deuteron pairing is 0 (deuteron_transversePairing_eq_zero).

    Scope — read this before citing #

    This is a scope-(b) structural exclusion restricted to single-generator (2, L)-torus channels (the deuteron pn channel σ₁³). The cited invariant is the Ad(ρ(σ₁))-eigenvector property of the germ curvature. It does not exclude the transverse pairing for multi-generator words (A ≥ 3 nuclei), where transports involve both generators and the eigenvector property fails.

    "sorry-free ≠ derived" seam. Lean proves the algebra. The step ∂_q σ₁ = dHeckeGen1 (entrywise differentiation of a 2×2 matrix of rational functions in q) is done by hand — it is checked here only in the sense that germOne_eq derives germ₁ from the supplied dHeckeGen1 by Lean algebra (heckeGen1_logDeriv). Cite as "the germs are the constant matrices H - 2E, -H, and Lean proves their commutator is the root vector -4E", never "Lean proves the germ is the q-derivative".

    Base ring (§7c). The abstract layer is a generic CommRing acting on a generic Ring; the concrete layer is a generic Field (needed only for q⁻¹). No ℝ, no ordering, no Real.*, no archimedean evaluation anywhere.

          THE GAUGE-FIXING THEOREM.

          Let the curvature F be an Ad u-eigenvector and let every handle transport be a power of the single unit u — the situation forced by a single-generator braid word. Then the intersection-form-weighted transverse surface pairing

          Ω₂^⊥ = Σᵢ Σⱼ Jᵢⱼ • ⁅𝓕ᵢ, 𝓕ⱼ⁆

          vanishes identically, for every intersection form J (so every Seifert matrix, every H₁ basis, every spanning tree) and every assignment k of handles to word positions.

          So the surface→scalar map is not merely gauge-fixable here: its value does not depend on the gauge at all, and that value is 0.
