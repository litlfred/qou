    Brane geometric resummation — the ℏ²-damped jet-brane tower is an affine fixed point #

    Formalizes the algebraic core of computations/canonical_plus_multibrane.py::brane_geometric_sum (eq ★). Under Markov-stabilization jet lift V̂^{(n)} = z^n·V̂^{(0)}, the per-level brane tower scales as tower^{(n)} = (z²)^{n-1}·tower^{(1)}, and with the built-in ℏ_q^{2n} jet-brane damping the tower is geometric in the ratio r = ℏ_q²·z²:

    Σ_{n=1}^∞ ℏ_q^{2n} · tower^{(n)}  =  a · Σ_{k=0}^∞ r^k ,   a := ℏ_q²·tower^{(1)}.

    The un-damped raw sum diverges at the archimedean substrate q₀ > 1 (the tower_MeV ≈ 2×10⁶ blow-up in 4he-binding-full-pipeline.witness.json); the ℏ²-damping is exactly what makes the archimedean evaluation converge — the fibre-level (Habiro-style) resummation evaluated at q₀.

    What is proved here (cache-verifiable, lean-direct, no analysis / no Finset / no ring): the resummed value S is the unique fixed point of the ℏ²-damped affine brane self-map S ↦ a + r·S — i.e. it satisfies the self-consistency S = a + r·S, equivalently S − r·S = a. This is the defining recurrence the geometric tower obeys (level-1 term a plus the damped-lifted remainder r·S).

    Standard consequences (NOT re-proved here — their Mathlib oleans, ring/field_simp/ tsum_geometric, are outside the sandbox cache): from S − r·S = a one gets S·(1 − r) = a, hence for r ≠ 1 the closed form S = a/(1 − r) = ℏ_q²·tower^{(1)}/(1 − ℏ_q²z²); and the partial sums Σ_{k<n} r^k = (1 − rⁿ)/(1 − r) converge to 1/(1 − r) as n → ∞ when |r| < 1 (tsum_geometric_of_norm_lt_one). At q₀ ≈ 1.11, r = ℏ_q²z² has |r| < 1, so the archimedean sum converges.

    Base ring ℝ (§7c): the archimedean substrate evaluation at q₀ is the intended use.
