    Bond-weight isospin split — the T/³He degeneracy break is exactly (a/b)² #

    Formalization of the structural core of the per-knot p ≠ n extensivity LEAD (PR #3841, bean qou-r1sr; witness computations/deltalambda/perknot-pn-bond-mayer-grounding.witness.json).

    In the Mayer/Ursell pairwise (b₂) cluster level, each nucleon–nucleon bond (x,y) carries the ideal-gas normalization weight w(x,y) = 1 / (τ x · τ y), where τ is the per-knot Markov trace tr_M (here a := τ(proton), b := τ(neutron)). The product aggregate over a nucleus' bonds is the product of its bond weights.

    T = (p,n,n) has bonds {pn, pn, nn}; ³He = (p,p,n) has bonds {pp, pn, pn}. The two share A = 3 and the same bond count 3, and differ in exactly one bond: T carries an nn where ³He carries a pp (both carry two pn). Every symmetric / type-blind bond model therefore gives their ratio as 1. The inverse-product weight instead gives their ratio exactly (a/b)² — the empirical μ_ext split (T/³He = 4.44/3.29 ≈ 1.3501) that the type-blind model cannot produce (numerically (a/b)² ≈ 1.3315, −1.38%).

    This file proves the exact algebraic identity behind that reduction. It is a generic-field statement (§7c: no ℝ, no ordering — a pure GroupWithZero identity); the archimedean evaluation a,b := tr_M(p), tr_M(n) at the substrate q₀ lives in the witness, not here. The lemma does not assert the split closes the extensivity gap (a b₂/pairwise object, orthogonal to the open connected-b₄ magnitude — see the coordination note); it isolates the exact factor (a/b)² supplied by the p ≠ n bond resolution.
