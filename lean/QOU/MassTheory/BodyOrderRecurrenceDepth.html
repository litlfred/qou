    The body-order recurrence: depth ≥ 3 is forced, and three terms cannot test it #

    thm:binding-closure-conditional writes the per-isotope binding as a body-order series B(K) = m_e ∑_n a_D^(n), and prop:prolonged-periods-fixed-depth proves — by Cayley–Hamilton on the jet-prolongation operator M acting on the Gauss–Manin period space V of the SW family over Bring's surface — that the periods satisfy a depth-d linear recurrence with n-independent kernel,

    a^(n+d) = ∑_{j<d} c_j · a^(n+j)   for every n,     d ≤ rank V ≤ 2g = 8.

    The identification half (that d and c are the Bring Picard–Fuchs operator) is open, and bean qou-i5yp proposes to test it against the measured body-order ratio

    a₂ : a₃ : a₄ = 1 : −0.519787 : +4.45038

    (convention D, proton pin, he4_body_order_shortfall_location_probe.py, 2026-08-29). This file establishes what such a test can and cannot decide, before the Picard–Fuchs computation is attempted.

    Results #

    Write B_n = m_e · a^(n) for the n-body term (obligation (B) of thm:binding-closure-conditional). A system of zero or one nucleon has no binding, so B₀ = B₁ = 0, hence a⁰ = a¹ = 0; the deuteron binds, so a² ≠ 0.

    • eq_zero_of_initial_eq_zero — a depth-d recurrence whose first d terms vanish is identically zero. (Strong induction; no hypotheses on c.)
    • three_le_depth — therefore d ≥ 3 is forced. The depth is not merely bounded above by the rank; it is bounded below by the vanishing of the one-body term.
    • depth_three_kernel_reaches — at d = 3, the map (c₁, c₂) ↦ (a³, a⁴) is surjective: for a nonzero a², every pair of 3- and 4-body values is realised by some kernel. So the three-term ratio above cannot falsify the identification; it can only solve for c₁ and c₂. Using it as a target is an anchor inversion, i.e. a fit.
    • a_five_free / a_six — c₀ is still unconstrained after a³ and a⁴, so a⁵ is free too, and a⁶ is the first value the kernel determines. At depth d the first genuine prediction sits at body order 2d, hence at body order ≥ 6 here — requiring A ≥ 6 (⁶Li), not the A ≤ 4 the measurement covers.

    The dichotomy with S₅-equivariance #

    QOU/ArchimedeanUniverse/s5_bring_hodge_depth.lean proves (sorry-free) that χ_{H¹} = 2·χ_{W₍₄,₁₎} with W₍₄,₁₎ irreducible, i.e. H¹(Bring) ≅ W₍₄,₁₎^{⊕2} as an S₅-representation. By Schur, an S₅-equivariant endomorphism of W^{⊕2} is an element of the 2 × 2 matrix algebra over the multiplicity space, so it satisfies a degree-≤ 2 characteristic polynomial — i.e. d ≤ 2.

    no_equivariant_prolongation records the consequence: d ≤ 2 is inconsistent with the vanishing one-body term. So at least one of the following fails: the prolongation operator M is S₅-equivariant; the operator-power index is the body order (obligation (B)); or the one-body binding term vanishes. This is a scope-(b) structural exclusion of the conjunction, citing Schur's lemma and eq_zero_of_initial_eq_zero — it is not a refutation of the prolongation-symbol conjecture, which survives with a non-equivariant M.

    Everything here is stated over a generic ring or field per AGENTS.md §7c; no archimedean construct appears, and the measured ratio enters only as an explicit rational in measured_target_reachable, never as a hypothesis.

    Prop

    A depth-d linear recurrence with n-independent kernel c: a (n + d) = ∑_{j < d} c j * a (n + j) for every n. This is the shape prop:prolonged-periods-fixed-depth derives from Cayley–Hamilton on the prolongation operator.
