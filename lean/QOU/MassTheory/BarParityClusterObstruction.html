    QOU.MassTheory.BarParityClusterObstruction — why raw-trace cumulants miss the bar-even scale #

    Formalizes the sharpest structural finding of the k(A) additive-cluster negative (2026-07-18, bean qou-7uxa, PR #3875, docs/coordination/2026-07-18-ka-additive-cluster-negative-structural-findings.md): the raw per-knot trace tr_M(K) at q₀ is bar-odd, so any Mayer/Ursell cluster cumulant built from raw traces carries the wrong bar-parity for the bar-even absolute mass scale — and the fix the negative identified is to symmetrize the generators to bar-even first.

    The bar involution is abstracted as an arbitrary ring endomorphism bar : R →+* R (with Function.Involutive bar where needed), so the results are agnostic to whether the physical involution is q ↦ q⁻¹ or the channel-swap q ↦ −q⁻¹ (α_EM #3936). BarEven t := bar t = t, BarOdd t := bar t = -t.

    Proved sorry-free over a generic CommRing:

    • sym_barEven — the symmetrization t + bar t is always bar-even;
    • barEven_mul — a product of bar-even elements is bar-even (clusters built from symmetrized traces stay bar-even);
    • barOdd_mul_barOdd — a product of two bar-odd elements is bar-even (parity (−1)² = 1): the pairwise b₂ channel is bar-even by count;
    • cumulant_barParity_mixed — a connected cumulant κ = j − p with j bar-odd (the joint 2-trace) and p bar-even (product of two 1-traces) satisfies bar κ = −j − p: it is bar-parity mixed, not bar-even;
    • cumulant_not_barEven — such a κ is bar-even only in the degenerate case 2·j = 0 (the I-par obstruction the negative reported: the raw cumulant is not bar-even because the joint-trace part j is bar-odd and nonzero);
    • cumulant_barEven_of_symmetrized — with the generators bar-even-symmetrized (j, p both bar-even) the cumulant is bar-even: the productive lead.

        The I-par obstruction. A raw-trace cumulant κ = j − p (j bar-odd joint trace, p bar-even product) is bar-even only if 2·j = 0 — i.e. never, when the bar-odd joint-trace part is nonzero (and R is 2-torsion-free). This is the bar-parity FAIL the additive-cluster negative reported.
