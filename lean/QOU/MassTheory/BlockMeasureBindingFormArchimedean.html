      D.bindingForm α atom free = 0

      No shared block, no binding. If the bound word and its free constituent product agree on every block character, the logarithmic binding vanishes. The contrapositive: a nonzero binding forces a block on which the two characters differ — the "entangled" block set is non-empty.

      B.D.bindingForm α atom (B.tensor a b) = -α * Real.log (1 + (∑ l : Λ, B.D.y l * B.deviation atom a b l) / (B.D₁.trM a * B.D₂.trM b))

      The binding form is a function of the y-weighted entangled deviation alone. Against the free product of two marginals with non-vanishing traces, B = −α · log(1 + Σ_λ y_λ δ_λ / (tr_M(a) tr_M(b))). Nothing else about the composite enters: two composites with the same weighted deviation bind identically.
