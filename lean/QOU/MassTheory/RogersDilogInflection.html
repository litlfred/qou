    noncomputable def QOU.MassTheory.rogersL2 (z : ℝ) :
    ℝ

    The elementary closed form of the Rogers-dilogarithm second derivative L''(z) = −½·( log z / (1−z)² − log(1−z) / z² ). (Only the elementary derivative Li₂'(z) = −log(1−z)/z is used to obtain this form; no Li₂ value is needed.) -- Ref: [hakimoglu-brown2025] https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.10206 (Rogers L-function, eq. 4)

        theorem QOU.MassTheory.rogersL2Bracket_reflect (z : ℝ) :
        rogersL2Bracket (1 - z) = -rogersL2Bracket z

        Abel-reflection antisymmetry. The bracket flips sign under z ↦ 1−z. This is the structural reason for the inflection: z = 1/2 is the reflection's fixed point.

        theorem QOU.MassTheory.rogersL2_half_eq_zero :
        rogersL2 (1 / 2) = 0

        The flat trefoil shape z = 1/2 is an inflection point of the Rogers dilogarithm: L''(1/2) = 0. Hence the single-tetrahedron bar-even (second-order) shape correction vanishes; the μ₀ closure's g₂ (order h²) cannot come from the single modulus and must be the octahedral e₂ pairwise term.

        theorem QOU.MassTheory.rogersL2Bracket_half_eq_zero :
        rogersL2Bracket (1 / 2) = 0

        The inflection is forced by the reflection symmetry. The bracket vanishes at the fixed point z = 1/2 of z ↦ 1−z because it is antisymmetric there (B(1/2) = B(1 − 1/2) = −B(1/2)).
