    Type (max u_1 u_2)

    Relative Reeb datum — primary conjecture class; no instance is provided. Data: the common entanglement light cone of two particles, the knot convolution, and the shared-history predicate. Clauses: the convolution is the free product twisted by the light cone; no shared history ⇒ the light cone is the unit; the light cone's trace lies in [−1, 1].

    • lightCone : A₁ → A₁ → A

      The common entanglement light cone of two particles, in the composite algebra.

    • compose : A₁ → A₁ → A

      The knot convolution a ⋈ b.

    • Entangled : A₁ → A₁ → Prop

      The two particles share prior (γγ) history.

    • compose_eq (a b : A₁) : compose B a b = B.tensor a b * lightCone B a b

      The convolution is the free product twisted by the light cone.

    • lightCone_of_not_entangled (a b : A₁) : ¬Entangled B a b → lightCone B a b = 1

      No shared prior history, no contribution: the light cone is the unit.

    • trM_lightCone_mem (a b : A₁) : B.D.trM (lightCone B a b) ∈ Set.Icc (-1) 1

      The trace evaluation of the light cone lies in the domain of arccos.
