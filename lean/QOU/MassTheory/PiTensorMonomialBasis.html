    The monomial basis of a tensor power, and the factor permutation on it #

    Bean qou-uinl, stage 2. Supplies the piece TensorTraceCharacter records as missing, and it is missing from Mathlib, not merely from this repo.

    What Mathlib lacks, measured #

    Mathlib/LinearAlgebra/PiTensorProduct.lean contains zero occurrences of Basis (checked 2026-09-05), and there is no finrank lemma for ⨂[R] i : ι, M either. Mathlib has the action — PiTensorProduct.reindex — but nothing that lets you compute in coordinates, which is why TensorTraceCharacter had to work in the concrete model ((α → κ) → R) instead of over ⨂.

    This file closes that, in the case the character computation needs: for V = κ → R free with basis indexed by κ,

    monoEquiv : (⨂[R] _ : α, (κ → R)) ≃ₗ[R] ((α → κ) → R)

    the monomial basis — a pure tensor ⨂ᵢ vᵢ goes to f ↦ ∏ᵢ vᵢ (f i), and the inverse sends g to ∑_f g f • ⨂ᵢ single (f i) 1. The forward round trip is a Kronecker-delta computation; the backward one is the monomial expansion vᵢ = ∑ₖ vᵢ k • single k 1 pushed through MultilinearMap.map_sum and map_smul_univ.

    The correspondence #

    monoEquiv_tensorPerm is the payoff: under this basis, permuting the tensor factors (PiTensorProduct.reindex) is precomposition on index tuples,

    monoEquiv (tensorPerm σ x) g = monoEquiv x (g ∘ σ)

    which is exactly the indexPerm of TensorTraceCharacter. So the q = 1 character (card κ)^{c(σ)} proved there in the concrete model is a statement about the genuine tensor power, transported along monoEquiv.

    DONE, same day: QOU.MassTheory.TensorPowerTrace supplies exactly that — the Module.Free/Module.Finite instances transported along monoEquiv, and trace_tensorPerm : LinearMap.trace R (⨂[R] _ : α, (κ → R)) (tensorPerm σ) = (card κ) ^ orbitCount σ. So the character is now stated with LinearMap.trace over the tensor power itself.

    🛑 What is still not here, and is a different order of problem: quantum Schur–Weyl at q ≠ 1. Bean qou-mpq5 scoped that as a multi-week gap and refused to fake it; nothing in this arc bears on it.
