    QOU.MassTheory.ReebLinkingCWF — deriving the Reeb linking amplitude ℓ(z) #

    Closes the physical-identification gap of the CWF linking-normalization conjecture (reeb-linking-cwf-normalization.md): the Reeb pn-bond linking amplitude equals ℓ(z) = z/√(1+z²), derived — not assumed — from three ingredients, two carried by the corpus as theorems and the third derived here.

    1. Channel direction (Markov, theorem). In the two-channel Temperley–Lieb decomposition the bond strand's tangent is the direction (1, z): the direct/identity channel has amplitude 1, the exchange channel (the TL generator eᵢ, the cup-cap) carries the Markov conditional-expectation weight z = 1/[2]_v = δ⁻¹.

    2. Unit tangent (CWF, theorem). The Gauss–Călugăreanu–White–Fuller linking integrand reads a direction through unit tangents per unit arc length, so the operative object is the normalized tangent t̂ = (1, z)/√(1+z²) = (cos θ, sin θ), θ = arctan z.

    3. Transverse selection (derived here). The Gauss linking integrand is generated by the transverse (exchange) component of the tangent: its antisymmetric cross-product structure ṙ₁ × ṙ₂ vanishes on the parallel (direct/identity) component — parallel strands do not link — and the crossing that carries all linking content is the exchange channel. This is structural in the Kauffman resolution σ = A·1 + A⁻¹·eᵢ: the identity term 1 is the no-crossing smoothing and eᵢ (cup-cap) is the crossing. Hence the linking amplitude is the exchange (second) component of the unit tangent:

      `ℓ(z) = t̂_exchange = sin θ = z / √(1+z²)`.

    Steps 1–2 fix the object (the unit channel direction); step 3 selects the component (transverse/exchange). Unit-normalization (2) kills the raw-weight tan θ = z option; transverse-selection (3) kills the direct-component cos θ option — so ℓ(z) = z/√(1+z²) is forced, not chosen. The one residual structural bridge is the identification of the TL exchange channel with the CWF transverse direction, grounded in the Kauffman resolution above.

    This file proves — sorry-free over ℝ — the algebraic content: ℓ reads a unit tangent, satisfies the CWF normalization ℓ(z)²(1+z²) = z², and at the Markov value z = 1/w lands on the deformed criticality ℓ(1/w)²(1+w²) = 1 — the hypothesis of ReebPnMagnitude.ss_det_zero_at_criticality.

    Honest scope. This closes the amplitude identification (the "Reeb amplitude = CWF functional" conjecture), ℓ derived from Markov + CWF + Kauffman transverse-selection with no fit. It does not supply the absolute MeV scale: the deuteron law B(²H) = 5g remains a near-match with its unit anchor open (reeb-linking-cwf-normalization.md §Scope). Base ring ℝ (§7c): genuinely archimedean (Real.sqrt).
