          The third escape is NOT loose — measured, then proved #

          Two of qou-q8vl's hypotheses turned out stronger than needed: aggregator convexity weakens to a two-point secant condition (qou-2ss0), and interface affineness weakens to monotonicity (above). It is tempting to expect the third — that the pn letter is a strict convex combination — to weaken to mere betweenness in the same way.

          It does not. Over an ordered field the two are the same condition, so the averaging hypothesis is already tight and there is no third gain to take.

          Affine is blocked BOTH ways — and what the escape must therefore do #

          Two gaps in the sections above, closed here.

          First, a real gap in the corpus application. pn_not_strict_max_of_monotone and signMixed_impossible_of_monotone assume Monotone. A decreasing interface law was therefore never covered, even though not_strict_max_of_antitone sits in OrderCore ready for it. Closed below.

          Second, the structural reason the corpus's own law is blocked. The interface factor is a Markov trace ratio, μ(x) = tr_M(W·x) / tr_M(W) at fixed W. The numerator is linear in x, so along any affine path in the algebra, x(t) = x₀ + t·x₁, the law is affine in t — and an affine law is monotone or antitone according to the sign of its slope, hence blocked either way.

          That is why μ = z + d cannot escape: it is the affine path. And it says exactly what an escape must do — leave the affine family, not merely leave the affine formula.

          The framework does admit such a family, and the corpus has already measured it. The file header above says "whether the framework admits such a thing is open and is not addressed here"; it is addressed now. The stacked family m ↦ σ^m is not an affine path (it is a product, not a sum), and its interface factor is measured non-monotone in m: μ > 1 at m = 2, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8 and not at m = 3 (stacked_interface_writhe_normalised_probe.py, reported in docs/audits/2026-09-12-b3-sign-one-root-and-the-insufficient-escape.md §6(b)). A function exceeding a threshold at 2 and 4 but not at 3 is not monotone.

          This selects nothing. Naming the family that occupies the door is a structural observation; choosing a particular m because it yields b₃ > 0 would be selection against the target, which AGENTS.md §"NO FITTING" forbids and which bean qou-kj4v already records going backwards for σ². Under the owner ruling of 2026-09-08 (qou-ales) the braid word is selected by γγ pair-production plus the confinement SDP, and that derivation is what remains open — not the question of whether a non-monotone law exists.

          Nothing here is numerical: no q, no Markov parameter, no anchor. The σ^m measurement is cited, not recomputed, and no value from it enters any statement.
