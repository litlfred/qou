    The AME binding is not pairwise-additive — the instantiation, discharged #

    QOU.Mass.PairwiseCumulantVanishing (landed in #6101) proves the general fact: a pairwise-additive binding map has identically vanishing connected three- and four-body cumulants, for every bond functional. Its falsifiable form, not_isPairwise_of_b3_ne_zero, says a binding map with any nonzero connected b₃ is not of that shape.

    Its docstring then instantiates that against measurement — "Since AME2020 gives b₃ = +1.426222 ≠ 0, the physical binding is not of this shape" — but in prose. No BindingMap is constructed from AME numbers anywhere on main, so the step from the general theorem to the physical conclusion is asserted rather than checked. This file supplies exactly that step and nothing else.

    What is added #

    • ameMap — a BindingMap ℚ built from AME2020: every pair is the deuteron binding 2.224566, every triple the isospin average of ³H and ³He, (8.481798 + 7.718043)/2.
    • ame_b3_eq — its connected three-body cumulant is exactly 2852445/2000000 = 1.4262225, the figure the Ursell inversion reports as +1.426222.
    • ame_b3_ne_zero, ame_not_isPairwise — hence, via the merged general theorem, the AME binding is not pairwise-additive. Machine-checked, not quoted.

    Why this and not the rest #

    An earlier revision of this branch carried two further files. Both are now redundant against main and were deleted rather than merged:

    • PairwiseAdditiveCumulants.lean — its pairwise_b3_eq_zero assumed a common bond value; b3_eq_zero_ofBondFunctional covers every bond functional, so the merged version strictly subsumes it.
    • FixedDepthGrowthBound.lean — its abs_le_geometric / not_factorial_growth are subsumed by ConstantKernelGrowthBound.exists_exponential_bound and not_isConstKernelRec_of_superExponential, which quantify over a SuperExponential predicate instead of the factorial instance and prove both directions.

    Landing either would have put a second copy of a general result into the corpus behind a weaker hypothesis — the duplication class #3594 records.

    Scope #

    This changes nothing about the magnitude gate, which stays OPEN. It closes a prose-vs-proof seam in an argument already made. The b₄ leg is deliberately not instantiated: the ⁴He literal is recorded as 28.295673 in he4_body_order_shortfall_location_probe.py and as 28.2956624 in #6101's writeup, and b₃ does not depend on it. That discrepancy is noted in bean qou-edt7 rather than silently resolved here.

    Base ring: ℚ — the AME data are exact decimal literals and no archimedean construct is needed (AGENTS.md §7c).
