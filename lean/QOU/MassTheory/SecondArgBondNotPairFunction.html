    A bond that reads only the second nucleon is not a pair function at all #

    Bean qou-ix0x, PR #7287. This is the Lean form of one measurement from computations/probes/gamma_gamma_bond_is_pair_independent_probe.py, and it upgrades that measurement from a number to a shape statement.

    What was measured #

    Under the owner's γγ ruling (bean qou-ales) the selected interface bond is G³ = (σ₂σ₃⁻¹)³. Its bond factor μ was measured on all four ORDERED nucleon pairs at the proton anchor, 60 dps:

    μ(p,p) = 1.00105775254515984      μ(n,p) = 1.00105775254515984
μ(p,n) = 1.00061119140688078      μ(n,n) = 1.00061119140688078

    so μ a b depends only on b — the SECOND nucleon — to 1.6e-61, while μ(p,n) ≠ μ(n,p) by 4.47e-4.

    What is proved here, and why it is stronger than "order-dependent" #

    ChainBondOrderDependence.bondOf is the corpus's bond scalar,

    bondOf Lpp Lpn Lnn a b = if a = b then (if a then Lpp else Lnn) else Lpn

    and it is symmetric in a and b by construction (bondOf_comm below): a = b is a symmetric condition and both unlike orders take the same Lpn.

    So a second-argument-only bond is not merely arrangement-dependent in the sense orderIndependent_iff analyses — secondOnly_eq_bondOf_iff shows it is not of the bondOf form at all unless it is constant. The corpus's whole chain analysis (bondSum, orderIndependent_iff, not_orderIndependent_of_ne) is stated over bondOf, so a non-constant one falls outside its domain rather than violating its conclusion. That is the difference between "this bond breaks arrangement-invariance" and "this bond is not the kind of object the framework's bond calculus is about".

    This does NOT say the γγ bond is wrong, and it does not touch the γγ word selection, which is qou-ales's. It says that IF a bond factor reads only one endpoint, THEN the chain machinery does not apply to it unmodified. Whether the measured one-sidedness is a placement artefact or a real feature is open; the probe records the mechanism as measured, not proved.

    Anti-vacuity #

    secondOnly_const_is_bondOf exhibits the constant case inside the family, so the iff is not vacuously true on one side, and bondOf_ne_secondOnly_witness gives an explicit two-element witness that the exclusion has content.

    Base ring (§7c) #

    Fully generic: bondOf carries no typeclass, and nothing here needs an order, a Real, or any archimedean construct. The decomposition section asks only for CommRing.

      A sibling arc proves the alternating character is order-blind: a 1-dimensional representation sends a word to a product of scalars, so it sees only the multiset of letter shifts. This probe measured a Markov-trace ratio that IS order-dependent. The two are consistent, and this says exactly why: whenever every 1-dimensional block agrees on the two words, the whole difference of a y-weighted character sum is carried by the blocks of dimension ≠ 1.
