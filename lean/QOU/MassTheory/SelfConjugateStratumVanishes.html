    The self-conjugate stratum of the mass channel is empty past four cells #

    What this explains #

    computations/probes/gram_norm_binding_probe.py decomposes the operator-space Gram norm G(W) = ∑_λ y_λ · tr(ρ_λ(W) ρ_λ(W^♯)) into per-block contributions and reports the self-conjugate share — the fraction of G carried by the bar-fixed blocks λ = λ', since bar sends e_λ ↦ e_λ' (prop:hecke-baer-star-generic).

    That share prints as 0.0000 for every isotope the probe runs: ²H (n = 6), ³H and ³He (n = 9), ⁴He (n = 12). Read from a float64 probe alone that is an observation, and a weak one — a share could be small for numerical reasons, or zero by an accident of the particular braid words chosen. It is neither. It is forced, for every n ≥ 5, by the two-row cap, and this file proves it.

    The argument #

    Convention D caps the mass channel at two rows: a nonzero Wenzl/Ocneanu weight forces ℓ(λ) ≤ 2 (TwoRowCap.colLen_le_two_of_massWeight_ne_zero_of_wenzl, itself derived from schurPrincipal_eq_zero_iff rather than posited).

    A self-conjugate diagram has rowLen 0 = colLen 0. Capping the column length at two therefore caps the row length at two as well, so the whole diagram is confined to the 2 × 2 box and cannot hold more than four cells.

    So for n ≥ 5 there is no diagram that is both self-conjugate and carries a nonzero weight, and the self-conjugate stratum of any weighted Wedderburn sum is empty — not small, empty. The probe's 0.0000 is that emptiness.

    Sharpness #

    The bound is attained, so none of this is vacuous: (2,2) is self-conjugate, has column length two, and has exactly four cells — the diagram the corpus already singles out as the unique self-conjugate partition of four (QOU.Archimedean.EHartreeTraceRatio.unique_self_conjugate_partition_of_four). selfConjugate_two_two below exhibits it, so card_le_four_… is a statement about a nonempty class with an extremal member, not an empty one.

    Scope #

    This constrains the self-conjugate share only. The probe's companion chiral_anti_share = 0.5000 is a different claim — it needs every nonzero c_λ to share a sign — and is not proved here. Do not read this file as covering it.
