    Neutron–proton splitting: the sign of the missing L2 term (formalize-first prune) #

    The leading traced-volume prediction Δm = α·|ΔΦ| (namespace QOU.NeutronProtonSplitting in folio/quantum-observable-universe/observations/neutron-proton-mass-splitting.lean; docs/audits/2026-07-11-quark-volume-provenance.md) over-predicts the neutron–proton mass difference by +0.63% (docs/audits/2026-07-11-np-splitting-residual-not-q0-knob.md). Before scanning candidate closures for that residual, this file formalizes the structural invariant the missing term must satisfy (formalize-first, AGENTS.md §"Formalize-first probe discipline").

    The next correction is the electromagnetic self-energy. Its sign is fixed, parameter-free, by the quark charge structure: the pairwise Coulomb factor Σ_{i<j} q_i q_j is 0 for the proton (uud) and -1/3 for the neutron (udd), so the proton carries the larger Coulomb self-energy and the EM correction to Δm = m_n − m_p is negative — exactly the sign of the observed residual.

    This is a prune, not a closure: it proves the missing term is charge-odd and reduces Δm (ruling out a charge-blind ℏ_q-tower of the wrong sign); it does not derive the EM coupling from the 13 categorical drivers, so nothing here is wired into production (anti-laundering rule). Everything is over ℚ (charges are rational; no archimedean structure — §7c).

    The EM self-energy sign of m_p − m_n is the standard PDG result.

          Sign of the missing L2 term (conditional on the EM-Coulomb model). If the electromagnetic correction to Δm = m_n − m_p is δ = -k·(Σqq(proton) − Σqq(neutron)) for any positive self-energy coupling k > 0 (the standard Coulomb self-energy, higher energy ⇒ heavier ⇒ subtracts from m_n − m_p), then δ < 0.

          Combined with the traced-volume leading piece over-predicting Δm (residual < 0), the EM correction has the right sign to close the gap. This constrains any candidate closure: it must be charge-odd and reduce Δm.
