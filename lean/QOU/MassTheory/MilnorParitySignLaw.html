                class QOU.MassTheory.MilnorParitySignLaw.MilnorParitySignConjecture (τ : ℕ → ℝ → ℝ) :

                (CONJECTURE, primary — OPEN.) The general Milnor-parity sign law, stated for a candidate A-indexed Brunnian level-trace family τ : ℕ → ℝ → ℝ (τ A h = level trace of the length-A iterated commutator at h = q − q⁻¹): the parity crossing pattern holds at every Brunnian order A ≥ 2 and every nonzero h. For the actual Brunnian Markov-trace family this asserts the universal alternation sign(1 − τ_A) = (−1)^(A−1).

Fabricating an instance would launder the two-point pattern into a universal claim (forbidden by the anti-laundering discipline). The field is content-bearing (a parameter-quantified inequality in the shape of the claim), not an empty marker.
