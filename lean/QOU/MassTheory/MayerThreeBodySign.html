    The multiplicative assembly cannot produce a positive 3-body cumulant here #

    he4_body_order_shortfall_location_probe.py (2026-08-29) measured the model's connected 3-body Ursell cumulant as wrong-signed against AME2020 — −2.744 against +1.426 — under all four convention × bond-typing combinations it tried. That is four data points, and the obvious readings were that the bond typing is wrong, or that the arrangement freedom (QOU.MassTheory.ChainBondOrderDependence) is contaminating it.

    Neither. The sign is forced, and this file says by what.

    Setup #

    The model is B(K) = B_AME(D) · W(K)/W(D) with the multiplicative assembly W(K) = 1 − ∏_{i<j} f_ij (multiplicative_W), and the Ursell inversion b₂ = B(D), b₃ = B̄(3) − 3·b₂. The normalisation B_AME(D)/W(D) is positive, so it does not affect signs; work in W-units. Writing fpn for the deuteron's bond factor and P₃ for the product of bond factors over whatever edge set the three-body system carries,

    b₂ = 1 − fpn,     b₃ = (1 − P₃) − 3·(1 − fpn) = 3·fpn − 2 − P₃.

    Result #

    b3_le_of_nonneg — since bond factors are positive, P₃ ≥ 0, hence

    b₃ ≤ 3·fpn − 2

    and therefore lt_of_b3_pos: a positive 3-body cumulant requires fpn > 2/3.

    Three things this does not depend on, which is the whole point:

    • the edge set. P₃ enters only through P₃ ≥ 0. Whether the three-body system is a chain, a triangle, or any derived coordination graph, the bound is the same. So deriving the bond set — the repair ChainBondOrderDependence calls for, and the SU(4)-partition-shape route of bean qou-h7mh — cannot fix the 3-body sign while the assembly stays 1 − ∏f.
    • the like-pair factors. f_pp and f_nn appear only inside P₃.
    • the overall energy scale. b₃'s sign is scale-free, so the α question (bean qou-302y, the interface scale being ~2.70× too large) is a magnitude question and cannot bear on this sign either.

    What it excludes — re-scoped 2026-09-12 (bean qou-rwhv) #

    Scope (b), invariant named: the Ursell alternation (−1)^{n−1} φᵀ_n ≥ 0 on bond factors in (0,1), proved at n = 3 in strict form as QOU.MassTheory.UrsellPartitionFunctionB3.phi3_pos. It excludes the multiplicative assembly W = 1 − ∏f as the source of a positive three-body term, for every bond triple in (0,1)³ — every edge set, every like-pair typing, every convention, every energy scale, and no threshold.

    This file's own bound is weaker than that, and the reason is worth keeping on record. b3_le_of_nonneg uses only P₃ ≥ 0, which leaves P₃ free and yields the escape fpn > 2/3 of lt_of_b3_pos. Once P₃ is the bond product the escape closes: b₃(f,f,f) = −(1−f)²(f+2) (b3corpus_uniform_closed_form, b3corpus_uniform_eq_mayerSign ties the two b₃s on the nose), and for non-uniform bonds b₃ = −φᵀ₃ < 0 (b3corpus_neg). The threshold reading — "clear fpn > 2/3 or abandon 1 − ∏f" — is therefore withdrawn: there is no fpn that clears it. chi_typing_below_threshold stays as the recorded comparison; it is no longer the operative bound.

    What the sign is: b₃ = −φᵀ₃ is the third Mayer coefficient of −ln(Ξ/Ξ_free) for the hard-core lattice gas on the nucleon sites with the interface-letter traces as pair weights (UrsellPartitionFunctionB3, docs/audits/2026-09-12-ursell-partition-function-for-b3.md). That expansion carries −φᵀ_T at every order, so the negative sign here is the classical one. The 2026-08-30 reading of it as "a missing (−1)^{n−1}" is withdrawn: restoring that factor is the same expansion at negative activity (restored_eq_neg_activity), and names no ensemble.

    So the defect is still located upstream of any weight — in the assembly, not in the body-order coefficients a_D^(n) — but the falsifiable target for a replacement is now sharper: within the Mayer vocabulary a positive connected three-body term at positive activity needs a bond factor above 1 (an attractive channel, g > 0), which no letter of the production word has (scope (a), measured); outside it, a non-Mayer assembly.

    Stated over an ordered field per AGENTS.md §7c — the ordering is genuinely needed, the content is the inequality. Nothing else archimedean appears.
