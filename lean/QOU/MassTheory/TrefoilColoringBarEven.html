    noncomputable def QOU.MassTheory.trefoilColoring {F : Type u_1} [Field F] (m : F) :
    F

    The trefoil boundary-parabolic coloring c(m) = −(m⁴ − m² + 1)/m² — the off-diagonal entry of ρ(v) forced by the braid relation uvu = vuv, as a function of the meridian eigenvalue m.

      theorem QOU.MassTheory.trefoilColoring_add_one {F : Type u_1} [Field F] (m : F) (hm : m ≠ 0) :
      trefoilColoring m + 1 = -(m - m⁻¹) ^ 2

      The coloring's deviation from flat is an exact bar-even square: c(m) + 1 = −(m − m⁻¹)². Since m − m⁻¹ is bar-odd (m ↦ m⁻¹ negates it), the deviation is bar-even — the parity of the g₂ correction; at m = q₀ it equals −(q₀ − q₀⁻¹)² = −h².
