    ∃ (m : ℝ), 0 < m ∧ 5 * m ^ 2 < 1 ∧ A4InterfaceMagnitude.b4 m = t ∧ c_lo < m ∧ m < c_hi

    The required magnitude is strictly bracketed by any two straddling constants. Given a positive target cumulant t and two physical magnitudes c_lo < c_hi (0 < c_lo, 5 c_hi² < 1) whose b₄ values straddle t, the unique m with b₄ m = t (existence from magnitude_form_surjective) satisfies c_lo < m < c_hi. Consequence: matching the target requires a magnitude strictly between the two constants — neither endpoint reproduces it. This is the structural core of the qou-j16x scope-(a) finding: the derived constants z−h/2 (below) and 2h (above) bracket the required m* but supply it themselves nowhere.
