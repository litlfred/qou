      ¬(z + dpp < z + combo w dpp dnn ∧ z + dnn < z + combo w dpp dnn)

      The exclusion. If the pn offset is a strict convex combination of the like-pair offsets and the interface law is affine, then the pn bond factor cannot be the strict maximum of the three — so the sign-mixed pattern that qou-q8vl shows is required (pn above one, both like pairs below) is unreachable, for every z, every weight, and every offset pair.
