    The hook column of the Magnus tr-decay is the elementary-symmetric tower (qou-o6ov) #

    Context (numerically established, not formalizable here). For the atom braid word ρ, the hook Hecke characters obey, exact to 139 digits (A=2,3; computations/deltalambda/magnus_hook_exterior_power_probe.py):

    tr_{(k,1ⁿ⁻ᵏ)}(ρ) = q^{L(1−j)} · e_j( eigenvalues of ρ in the reduced-Burau rep (n−1,1) ),
                   j = n − k,

    i.e. the hooks are the (q-)exterior powers Λʲ(V) of the reduced Burau rep V, and the whole hook column is the tower of elementary symmetric functions e_j of the Burau spectrum (e_j = [tʲ] det(1 + t·ρ^V), the characteristic / Alexander polynomial). The seminormal Hecke construction, the specific word ρ, and the q-twist are outside Mathlib, so what is formalized here is the algebraic core that packages all orders j at once: the elementary-symmetric generating function. It is general over any CommRing.

    prod_one_add_mul_eq_sum_powerset is the all-orders identity ∏ᵢ (1 + t·μᵢ) = Σ_{S} t^{|S|} ∏_{i∈S} μᵢ; prod_one_add_mul_eq_sum_esymm regrades it by degree into Σ_j tʲ · e_j(μ) with e_j the j-th elementary symmetric sum — the generating function whose j-th coefficient is (up to the numerically-verified twist) the hook-j character.

    theorem QOU.MassTheory.prod_one_add_mul_eq_sum_powerset {ι : Type u_1} [Fintype ι] [DecidableEq ι] {R : Type u_2} [CommRing R] (t : R) (μ : ι → R) :
    ∏ i : ι, (1 + t * μ i) = ∑ S ∈ Finset.univ.powerset, t ^ S.card * ∏ i ∈ S, μ i

    All-orders generating function (subset form). ∏ᵢ (1 + t·μᵢ) = Σ_S t^{|S|} ∏_{i∈S} μᵢ, the sum over all subsets S. This packages every exterior-power degree j = |S| into one identity: the coefficient of t^j is the j-th elementary symmetric sum of μ, which the probe identifies (up to the q-twist) with the hook-(n−j,1ʲ) character of the reduced Burau. General CommRing.
