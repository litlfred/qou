    The leading-order mirror asymmetry, and its sign #

    Bean qou-z4ba. Probe: computations/probes/c1_mirror_asymmetry_sign_probe.py.

    LeadingOrderTraceCoefficient derives the leading ℏ_q coefficient of the trace difference as

    c₁(Z,N) = (3Z − 2N)(2^{−A−1} − 4^{−A}) − 4(I_pp − I_nn)·4^{−A},   A = Z + N,

    with I_pp = Z−1, I_nn = N−1 truncated in ℕ. This file extracts its isospin content: the difference between a nucleus and its mirror collapses to a single bracket,

    c₁(Z,N) − c₁(N,Z) = (Z − N)·(5·2^{−A−1} − 13·4^{−A})
                  = (Z − N)·4^{−A}·(5·2^{A−1} − 13)                        [★]

    so the whole (Z,N)-dependence of the mirror asymmetry is 5·2^{A−1} − 13. That is negative at A = 2 (10 < 13) and positive for every A ≥ 3 (20, 40, 80, …).

    Why the sign is the interesting part #

    The framework does not derive the energy scale that turns c₁ into MeV (bean qou-59j5; log Z is an entropy with no temperature, qou-ssz6), so every magnitude claim in this lane is a ratio against a chosen unit. A sign is not. Any positive scale E maps c₁ ↦ E·c₁ and preserves order, so mirror_pos below is a scale-free prediction: for A ≥ 3 the trace channel says the proton-rich member of a mirror pair binds more.

    Measured against AME 2020, over all 230 mirror pairs with Z > N and A ≥ 3, the observed sign is the opposite in every case — Coulomb repulsion makes the proton-rich mirror the less bound one.

    Scope (a), stated as such #

    This is a scope-limited negative: the trace channel alone, at leading order in ℏ_q, gets the mirror asymmetry backwards. No invariant is cited and none is claimed, so it is not a structural exclusion of the channel or of anything in it. What it localises is that the missing term must be charge-dependent and repulsive, and large enough to overturn [★] rather than merely to supply the observed gap — i.e. the Coulomb term that CoulombFromCrossingCounts records as "a_C is nowhere" and SaturationPeakStructure as "Nothing here derives a_V, a_S or a_C".

    Nothing about c₂ or higher is claimed: those have exact values for six words only and no closed form in (Z,N).

    noncomputable def QOU.MassTheory.mirrorBracket (A : ℕ) :
    ℚ

    The mirror bracket 5·2^{−A−1} − 13·4^{−A}, which carries the entire (Z,N)-dependence of the leading-order mirror asymmetry.

    This file survived the 2026-09-06 c1Closed repair unchanged, and that is not luck. Owner ruling qou-hu1f reordered the atom word and c₁'s per-nucleon coefficients moved from (21, 16) to (3, −2) — every one of the six recorded values changed, by up to 163×. Nothing here moved, because the mirror difference cancels everything except the coefficient gap:

    c₁(Z,N) − c₁(N,Z) = (Z − N)·[ (P − Q)·(2^(−A−1) − 2^(−2A)) − 8·2^(−2A) ]

    so mirrorBracket depends on P − Q alone, and P − Q = (S_p − S_n)/2 is 5 under both orderings — (42 − 32)/2 and (6 − (−4))/2. The 13 below is (P − Q) + 8, likewise fixed by it. That equality of gaps is measured on the two words, not derived: nothing shown here forces a third ordering to give 5 as well, so treat this file's invariance as verified-for-these-two rather than structural, and re-check P − Q before quoting it for any new word. Contrast PairwiseChainLeadingOrder.chain_over_atom_He4_of_letters, whose 24/7 is structural — there the letter weights cancel identically, for every (a, b).
