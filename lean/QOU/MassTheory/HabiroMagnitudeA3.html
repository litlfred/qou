    1. The Habiro cyclotomic basis magnitude |(q;q)_n| at real q > 1 #

    For real q > 1 we have 1 − q^k < 0, so |(q;q)_n| = ∏_{k=1}^n (q^k − 1). We work with this magnitude directly.

Therefore no well-defined archimedean value exists at the substrate q₀ > 1.
