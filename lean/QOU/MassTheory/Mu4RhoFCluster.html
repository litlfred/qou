      (WALL) μ₃ is unreachable by any pairwise ρ_F cluster. The residual 3-body simplex coefficient μ₃ is positive (+0.4708), but the beyond-pairwise 3-body content of any pairwise interface product ∏ fᵢ, fᵢ ∈ [0,1], is ≤ 0. Hence μ₃ ≠ that cluster content — the multiplicative ρ_F cluster route cannot supply μ₃. (The only pairwise escape fᵢ > 1 is foreclosed by markovParam_le_half: z ≤ 1/2 < 1 for every q > 0.)
