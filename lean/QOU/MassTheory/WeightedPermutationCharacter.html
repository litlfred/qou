    The q-deformed permutation character: one q-factor per orbit #

    Bean qou-uyb4. Continues the q = 1 arc (qou-uinl, qou-mex9) into q ≠ 1.

    🛑 Read this first: WHICH q ≠ 1 this is #

    "Quantum Schur–Weyl" is two independent deformations of the classical picture, and this file does exactly one of them.

    deformation what changes status
    trace weight the flat trace becomes the K-weighted (quantum) trace, K = diag(1, q²)^{⊗n} proved here
    braiding the permutation operator P_σ becomes the R-matrix Ř(q), and H_n(q) replaces S_n NOT here

    Full quantum Schur–Weyl — the U_q(gl_N) × H_n(q) double centralizer — needs both, and bean qou-mpq5 scoped it as a multi-week build. That assessment stands. Measured 2026-09-05, Mathlib has zero files matching QuantumGroup, HeckeAlgebra, BraidGroup, RMatrix, YangBaxter or TemperleyLieb — only Coxeter groups — so the braiding half is a from-scratch development, not a gap that can be closed by finding the right lemma.

    Do not cite this file as quantum Schur–Weyl. It is the weighted character of the permutation action; the braiding is untouched.

    What is proved #

    ∑_{f invariant} ∏ᵢ w (f i)  =  ∏_{orbits O} ∑_k w(k) ^ |O|

    weighted_permutation_character. The deformation lives entirely in the orbit sizes, which the q = 1 count discards — that is the whole content, and it is why the undeformed statement could get away with counting orbits rather than measuring them.

    Three checks pin it down:

    • sum_orbitSize — orbit sizes sum to card α (the orbits really do partition).

    • weighted_permutation_character_one — at w ≡ 1 the product collapses to (card κ) ^ orbitCount σ, the character proved in OrbitQuotientCount. This is the check that matters: the deformation is not a new object, it is the same character with the sizes retained.

    • weighted_character_two — at κ = Fin 2 with K = diag(1, q²), the weight the corpus's own Schur–Weyl normalisation uses, the character is

      ∏_{orbits O} (1 + q^{2|O|})

      one q-binomial per orbit. At q = 1 each factor is 2 and this is 2^{c(σ)}.

        The q-deformed permutation character. With a weight w on the fibre basis, the weighted trace of σ permuting the factors of V^{⊗α} is a product of one factor per orbit, each factor a weight-sum raised to that orbit's size:

        ∑_{f invariant} ∏ᵢ w (f i)  =  ∏_{orbits O} ∑_k w(k) ^ |O|

        At w ≡ 1 every factor is card κ and this collapses to (card κ)^{orbitCount σ} — the q = 1 character. The deformation lives entirely in the orbit SIZES, which the undeformed count discards.
