    Gauge Field Fluid Dynamics #

    Formalization of the brane-fluid framework from §Fluid Dynamics of the Brane in the QOU manuscript. Every theorem is either fully proved or marked as a conjecture with a bibliographic reference. No sorry without citation, no trivial placeholders.

                                    structure QOU.GaugeFieldFluidDynamics.LeviDegeneracyConjecture :
                                    Type

                                    Levi degeneracy conjecture: the core aspect ratio at root of unity q = e^{iπ/κ} grows at most linearly in κ.
