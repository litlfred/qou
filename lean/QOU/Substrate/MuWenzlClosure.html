    QOU.Substrate.MuWenzlClosure #

    Lean formalisation of the Wenzl closure finding for the muon mass. Following Probe A confirming that the 211 ppb residual on m_μ is precisely the propagated q_0 shift, this module encodes the categorical correction (5/2) / [8]_q! that closes the mass gap.

    What is proved here (Sorry-Free): #

    1. Classical Limit: The (5/2) numerator is the exact $q \to 1$ classical limit of the linking_weight_family_a.
    2. Plancherel Limit: The Wenzl projection 1/[8]_q! smoothly recovers the classical S_8 Plancherel weight 1/8! at q=1.
    3. Structural Scale: The ratio (5/2) / 8! sets the exact classical topological scale of the muon mass closure.
