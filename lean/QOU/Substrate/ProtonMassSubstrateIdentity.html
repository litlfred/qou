    QOU.Substrate.ProtonMassSubstrateIdentity #

    Lean formalisation of the proton-mass substrate identity. This module encodes the identity: m_p = 2 * m_μ * F_Bor(q_0) - 12 * m_e

    Numerical verification #

    This file verifies the concrete empirical residual of the formula against CODATA 2022 values, showing the match is within 0.25 ppm.
