    QOU.QuantumUniverse.CategoricalCoordinateForced — the categorical structure does force R[i] #

    Bean qou-ywh9, epic qou-8lxd; discharges the standing prose assertion in rem:commutative-crossing-energy.

    The assertion this discharges #

    rem:commutative-crossing-energy states, of the commutative limit q = 1:

    the eigenvalues collapse to (+1, −1) … We recover the categorical structure of the commutative limit by extracting the square root of the antisymmetric eigenvalue λ₋ = −1: i = √λ₋, i² + 1 = 0 in R[i]. The categorical structure forces the ring extension R[i] … The construction is intrinsic to R; we assume no real or complex numbers.

    That was prose. The one Lean file in the arc, categorical_coordinate_ring_universal, takes iS : S and hiS : iS ^ 2 = -1 as hypotheses — it is the universal property of R[q,q⁻¹][i]/(i²+1) given a square root of −1 in the target, and structurally cannot produce one. i_coord_sq_eq_neg_one proves i² = −1 by construction (AdjoinRoot.isRoot_root), a fact about the adjunction rather than about a quantum observable universe.

    This file supplies the missing implication, and its content is Cayley–Hamilton at rank two. The mechanism is exactly the volume form:

    • the degree-2 volume form Λ²V ≅ 𝟙 is what defines det — det f is the scalar by which Λ²f acts on Λ²V ≅ 𝟙;
    • at rank 2, Cayley–Hamilton reads f² = (tr f)·f − (det f)·1;
    • so on the subgroup the volume form cuts out — det = 1, which is precisely SL(V) = Sp(V), the group def:fundamental-object says the volume form selects over GL₂ — every trace-free element squares to −1.

    No square root is assumed. i² = −1 is the Cayley–Hamilton identity read on the trace-free part of the group the volume form already selects, and the two hypotheses are exactly the two data the framework already has: the volume form (giving det = 1) and trace-freeness (the Lie algebra of that group).

    What is proved, and what is not #

    Proved, sorry-free, over an arbitrary CommRing R — no ℝ, no ℂ, matching the block's "we assume no real or complex numbers":

    • sq_eq_trace_smul_sub_det — Cayley–Hamilton at rank 2, by computation.
    • sq_eq_neg_one_of_traceFree_det_one — the forcing: tr f = 0 and det f = 1 give f * f = −1.
    • J_trace, J_det, J_sq — such an f exists, exhibited, so the statement is not vacuous.
    • one_J_linearIndependent — {1, J} is R-linearly independent, so the subalgebra R·1 ⊕ R·J it spans is a genuine rank-2 extension and not a collapsed copy of R. This is what upgrades "there is a square root of −1" to "R[i] embeds".
    • sq_eq_neg_one_iff_traceFree — the converse direction on the volume-form subgroup: within det = 1, squaring to −1 is equivalent to being trace-free. So the trace-free condition is not an extra choice made to reach the answer; it is what squaring to −1 means there. No 2-cancellation hypothesis is needed: the determinant cancels.

    Not proved, and asserted nowhere below:

    • that R itself contains a square root of −1. It does not, and the block does not claim it does — what is forced is the extension, i.e. an embedding R[i] ↪ End(V). R = ℝ gives ℂ ⊆ End(ℝ²) and ℝ still has no i.
    • that the braiding forces this. The braiding enters only in fixing λ₋ = −1 at q = 1; neg_one_eq_lambdaMinus_at_q_one records that link as an arithmetic identity and nothing more. The forcing above is a statement about the volume form, which is the stronger and cleaner reading.
    • anything about q ≠ 1. def:commutative-qou is the q → 1 corner and that is where the block's claim lives.

    A matrix model is used deliberately, and it is not a weakening. End(V) for a rank-2 free module is Matrix (Fin 2) (Fin 2) R once a basis is chosen, and every statement below is basis-independent in content: trace and det are conjugation-invariant, so sq_eq_neg_one_of_traceFree_det_one transports to any basis. Mathlib has no exterior-power-of-an-object API, so det is used in its matrix form; that det is the action on Λ², which is the framework's volume form, is the one cited step (§7c: no ℝ anywhere — everything is over a bare CommRing).

    The CRT defect in the same arc, also settled #

    categorical-coordinate-ring-proof.md rows 1 and 3 state ℂ[i]/(i²+1) ≅ ℂ. That is false: over a ring that already has a square root of −1, the polynomial splits and the quotient acquires a nontrivial idempotent, so it is ℂ × ℂ. idempotent_of_sqrt_neg_one proves the idempotent exists, by computation, over any commutative ring with 2 invertible — which is the whole content of the CRT splitting and is what makes the stated isomorphism impossible (ℂ is a field and has no idempotent but 0 and 1). Reported in that block's .qa.json as an unedited math finding; this is the proof of the correction.

    source
    theorem QOU.QuantumUniverse.CategoricalCoordinateForced.sq_eq_trace_smul_sub_det {R : Type u_1} [CommRing R] (f : Matrix (Fin 2) (Fin 2) R) :
    f * f = f.trace • f - f.det • 1

    Cayley–Hamilton at rank two. f² = (tr f)·f − (det f)·1.

    This is the whole mechanism: det is the scalar by which Λ²f acts on Λ²V ≅ 𝟙, so the volume form is exactly what makes the second coefficient available.

    f * f = -1

    No square root is assumed anywhere: i² = −1 is Cayley–Hamilton read on the trace-free part of the group the volume form already gives.

      theorem QOU.QuantumUniverse.CategoricalCoordinateForced.idempotent_of_sqrt_neg_one {R : Type u_1} [CommRing R] {A : Type u_2} [CommRing A] [Algebra R A] (u x : A) (hu : u * u = -1) (hx : x * x = -1) (half : A) (hhalf : half * 2 = 1) :
      half * (1 + u * x) * (half * (1 + u * x)) = half * (1 + u * x)

      The CRT correction. If R already contains a square root u of −1 and 2 is invertible, then any R-algebra containing an x with x² = −1 carries the nontrivial idempotent e = 2⁻¹(1 + u·x).

      This is the whole content of the splitting R[X]/(X²+1) ≅ R × R, and it is why categorical-coordinate-ring-proof.md rows 1 and 3 — which state ℂ[i]/(i²+1) ≅ ℂ — assert a non-isomorphism: ℂ is a field and has no idempotent other than 0 and 1.
