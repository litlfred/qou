    QOU.Resurgence.WardFiltrationCounting — Ward↔filtration counting probe + carriers #

    Owner's insight (formalize-first scaffold): gauge-invariance order-by-order (Slavnov–Taylor / Ward) gives LINEAR relations among the Stokes constants, and these align with the jet / Gröbner–Shirshov length filtration better than bare perturbative order — because the GS length carries the Hecke word-structure. The test that decides whether this CLOSES a derivation gap (Δ_λ, c_k):

    • (B.2) Filtration-coincidence carrier — the OPEN conjecture that the three ℕ-filtrations (d_GB, jet/iterated-integral dII, perturbative order) coincide, threaded conditionally on [TwoFiltrationBridge] (§3b-cond). Content-bearing (an equality of ℕ-maps), no instance — genuinely open.

    • (B.3) The counting probe — a fully decidable, sorry-free Nat computation: at each body/jet order n, #(Ward-free bar-invariant Stokes constants) = barOrbits n vs #(Δ_λ to fix) = deltaCount n = p(n). The verdict barOrbits n < deltaCount n is proved (decide), and the deficit is proved to equal chiralPairs n — the SIGN degrees of freedom the bar-invariant trace structurally cannot see.

    • (Sign source, PROBE-4) Stokes-phase sign carrier — the mixed-sign chirality (KL-positivity PROBE-4 prune: KL weights give only the magnitude) supplied by the imaginary Stokes phase (±i discontinuity ⇒ real sign that flips under the bar-involution). Content-bearing carrier + a proved cancellation lemma showing the bar-invariant trace cannot see the sign.

    This closes NO derivation gap. The counting shows the viewpoint forces the magnitude relations |Δ_λ| = |Δ_{λ'}| (over-determined), and RELOCATES the residual gap to two categoricity questions (sign / orbit-magnitude), both open.

                Stokes-phase sign source (OPEN carrier, over generic [CommRing R]). The mixed-sign chirality of Δ_λ (n=3: 1+/2−) is exactly what the KL-positivity PROBE-4 prune shows the (non-negative) KL weights cannot supply — they give only the MAGNITUDE. The resurgent route supplies the sign via the imaginary Stokes phase: the ±i discontinuity across the Stokes line, whose real shadow is a sign sign μ — encoded by sq_one (it squares to 1; not literal ∈ {±1}, which is strictly stronger over a generic [CommRing R]) — that flips under the bar-involution λ ↦ λ'. (PR #4540 proves the concrete sign is the a-function parity (-1)^{n(λ)}, genuinely ±1.) This carrier states that structure as content (not True); whether the physics FORCES sign to be exactly the ±i phase sign is the open categoricity question (bean'd, not asserted).
