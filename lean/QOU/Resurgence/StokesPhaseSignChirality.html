    Part 1 — the ±i imaginary Stokes discontinuity (proved sorry-free over ℂ) #

    The alien derivative Δ_ω across a Stokes line is the discontinuity of the Laplace transform around the Borel singularity ξ = A. For a simple singularity the discontinuity is the contour residue ∮ dξ/(ξ−A)·ρ = 2πi·ρ (ρ the real residue) — intrinsically imaginary. We encode the discontinuity value and prove its imaginary-ness structurally (no fit, no free parameter: 2πi is forced by the monodromy).
