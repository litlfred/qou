    QOU.Resurgence.StokesConstantQDimCategoricity #

    Horn 2 (bean qou-nkm1, epic qou-r2nh): are the descent-scaffold bar-orbit magnitudes categorical q-dimensions? Companion to QOU.Resurgence.StokesMatrixDescent / WardFiltrationCounting (PR #4525), whose Ward↔filtration counting forced |Δ_λ| = |Δ_{λ'}| and relocated the residual gap to two categoricity questions — the sign (horn 1) and the magnitude (horn 2, this file).

    The established datum is the GGM state-integral Stokes constant of the figure-eight 4₁: the integer 3 (off-diagonal of S⁺(q→0) = [[1,3],[0,1]]), with descendant q-series S⁺_{σ₁σ₁}(1;q̃) = 1 − 8q̃ − 9q̃² + 18q̃³ + 46q̃⁴ + … (GGM 2012.00062 eq 21, proven for 4₁; the per-winding integer family of 2026-07-31-awpk-perwinding-stokes-41.md).

    Result — honest NEGATIVE. The direct "orbit magnitudes = q-dimensions" identification fails, for two proved reasons:

    1. The 3 = [3]_{q=1} match is vacuous. [n]_q|_{q=1} = n (qInt_at_one), so the q=1 quantum-integer spectrum is all of ℕ; every integer k is [k]_1. A q=1 match carries no information.

    2. At q ≠ 1 the Stokes q-series is structurally excluded from being a categorical q-dimension: a q-dimension lies in ℕ[q,q⁻¹] (non-negative integer coefficients — the KL/crystal positivity, the same invariant the PROBE-4 prune used on the sign), while the Stokes descendant series has coeff₁ = −8 < 0. Hence no non-negative-coefficient polynomial — no q-dimension, no ℕ-combination of them — equals it.

    All statements are proved sorry-free over a generic [Field K] (the q-integer facts) or Polynomial ℤ (the coefficient-positivity exclusion); no archimedean input, no c_k, no Δ_λ wired, nothing to production (§7c).

    Ref: manuscript, mass-theory/stokes-orbit-magnitude-qdim; GGM arXiv:2012.00062.
