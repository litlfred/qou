    QOU.su2_emergence_dagger_unitarity — gauge-group containment G ⊆ U(2) #

    Lemma (lem:su2-emergence-dagger-unitarity).

    Under the QOU hypotheses of emergence of the gauge group, every tensor automorphism g ∈ G preserves the Frobenius dagger on τ(V); equivalently, G ⊆ U(2).

    Archimedean realisation (this file) #

    After the substrate→archimedean realisation τ, the fibre τ(V) is the standard 2-dimensional Hermitian space ℂ², the Frobenius dagger is the conjugate-transpose g ↦ gᴴ, and "g preserves the Frobenius dagger" is the concrete identity g · gᴴ = 1. The lemma's content at this level is the (non-tautological) implication

    g · gᴴ = 1 ⟹ g ∈ U(2),

    which we derive from Matrix.mem_unitaryGroup_iff: each gauge element that preserves the dagger on the fibre lands in Matrix.unitaryGroup (Fin 2) ℂ, hence G ⊆ U(2). This is the archimedean face required by §7c (Hermitian pairing ⇒ ℂ). The pointwise statement is the full mathematical content — the "lift to the whole group" is the universal statement over all g ∈ G below.

    Genuine derivation: the hypothesis g * star g = 1 is the full content of Matrix.mem_unitaryGroup_iff, and the conclusion is membership in Matrix.unitaryGroup (Fin 2) ℂ.
