    QOU.Conjectures.KnotFactorFrobeniusPairing — binding as Markov-trace defect #

    Conjectural axiomatisation of conj:knot-factor-frobenius-pairing (folio/quantum-observable-universe/mass-theory/knot-factor-frobenius-pairing.md).

    The categorical mass of a braid β equals the absolute value of its Jones–Markov trace, and the bulk binding energy of an atom (Z, N) is the mass defect

    B_bulk(Z, N) = E₀ · (|tr_M(W_free)| − |tr_M(W_atom)|)

    with a single archimedean calibration constant E₀ (the fibre functor τ : R → ℝ) fixed by one reference experiment.

    The conjecture is encoded as a class (AGENTS.md §3b / §3b-tax); its fields carry the genuine binding-defect identity, not a bare True. An unproved primary conjecture is honestly represented by a content-bearing class with no instance. The richer braid-level axiomatisation lives in QOU/BraidKnot/KnotFactorFrobeniusPairing.lean; this carrier records the mass-defect form of the claim.

    class QOU.Conjectures.KnotFactorFrobeniusPairing :
    Type

    Conjecture class (conj:knot-factor-frobenius-pairing).

    An instance supplies a Markov-trace magnitude traceMag on closed braids (the |tr_M(·)| of the categorical mass identity) and the single calibration constant E₀, and asserts that the bulk binding energy binding of an atom is the calibrated Markov-trace defect between the free-nucleon braid wFree and the bound atomic braid wAtom.

    The field binding_eq_defect carries the conjecture's genuine content (the mass-defect factorisation), not a bare True. This is a primary conjecture (no other conjecture class in its signature).

    • traceMag : ℕ → ℝ

      Magnitude of the Jones–Markov trace |tr_M(W)| of a closed braid, indexed abstractly by a braid identifier. Real-valued because the absolute value is the archimedean realisation.

    • E0 : ℝ

      The single archimedean calibration constant E₀ (fibre functor τ : R → ℝ), fixed by one reference experiment; positive.

    • E0_pos : 0 < E0

      E₀ is a positive energy scale.

    • binding : ℕ → ℕ → ℝ

      The bulk binding energy of an atom, as a function of its free-nucleon braid wFree and bound atomic braid wAtom.

    • binding_eq_defect (wFree wAtom : ℕ) : binding wFree wAtom = E0 * (traceMag wFree - traceMag wAtom)

      Conjectural mass-defect factorisation. The bulk binding energy is E₀ times the Markov-trace defect between the free and the bound braid: binding wFree wAtom = E₀ · (|tr_M(W_free)| − |tr_M(W_atom)|).
