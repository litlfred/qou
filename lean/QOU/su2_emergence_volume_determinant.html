    QOU.su2_emergence_volume_determinant — gauge-group containment G ⊆ SL(2) #

    Lemma (lem:su2-emergence-volume-determinant).

    Under the QOU hypotheses of emergence of the gauge group, every tensor automorphism g ∈ G of the fibre functor τ satisfies det(g_V) = 1; equivalently, G ⊆ SL(2).

    Archimedean realisation (this file) #

    After the substrate→archimedean realisation τ, the fibre τ(V) is the standard 2-dimensional Hermitian space ℂ². The volume constraint "g preserves the volume form on τ(V)" is the concrete identity g.det = 1, and the lemma's content at this level is the containment

    g.det = 1 ⟹ g ∈ SL(2),

    which we derive by exhibiting the lift of g into Matrix.SpecialLinearGroup (Fin 2) ℂ (the subtype of matrices of unit determinant): each gauge element with unit determinant is the image of an SL(2) element, hence G ⊆ SL(2) (i.e. G is contained in the range of the coercion SL(2) → M₂(ℂ)). Combined with the dagger sibling this puts the gauge group inside SU(2) = U(2) ∩ SL(2) (su2_emergence_combined, membership in Matrix.specialUnitaryGroup (Fin 2) ℂ derived through Matrix.mem_specialUnitaryGroup_iff).

    Previous encodings were vacuous — first a context class carrying the whole implication as the field claim_pointwise_det_lifts_to_SL2 (self-assuming projection, AGENTS.md §3b-cond item 5), then a hypothesis-restating tautology g.det = 1 ⊢ g.det = 1. The conclusions now genuinely differ from the hypotheses (subgroup membership, not the determinant equation itself).
