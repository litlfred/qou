      Proposition (balanced-holonomy cycle exclusion, within a QOU). Let $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ be a quantum observable universe with substrate parameter $q$ and Skein module $\mathcal{S}(q) \cong \mathbb{Z}[q^{\pm 1}]$. The Skein-Collatz map $\mathcal{C}_q$ has no periodic orbit of balanced multiplicative holonomy ($A = 1$) other than the degenerate fixed point; equivalently $[3]_q^{k_{\mathrm{odd}}} = [2]_q^{k}$ has no solution with $k_{\mathrm{odd}} \geq 1$, by coprimality of $[2]_q$ and $[3]_q$.

      Proof. A periodic orbit of length $k$ with $k_{\mathrm{odd}}$ odd steps and $k_{\mathrm{even}}$ even steps satisfies $\mathcal{C}_q^k(P) = P$. The odd steps multiply by $[3]_q / [2]_q$ and the even steps multiply by $1/[2]_q$, so the return condition requires:

      $$ \frac{[3]_q^{k_{\mathrm{odd}}}}{[2]_q^{k_{\mathrm{odd}} + k_{\mathrm{even}}}} = 1, \qquad\text{i.e.,}\qquad [3]_q^{k_{\mathrm{odd}}} = [2]_q^k \quad (k = k_{\mathrm{odd}} + k_{\mathrm{even}}). $$

      But $[2]_q$ and $[3]_q$ are coprime in $\mathbb{Z}[q]$. The Skein divisibility identity gives the proof in one line:

      $$ [3]_q - q[2]_q = 1, $$

      so $\gcd([2]_q, [3]_q) = 1$ by Bézout. Since $[3]_q$ is a unit modulo $[2]_q$:

      $$ [3]_q^{k_{\mathrm{odd}}} \equiv 1^{k_{\mathrm{odd}}} \equiv 1 \pmod{[2]_q}, $$

      while $[2]_q^k \equiv 0 \pmod{[2]_q}$ for $k \geq 1$. These are never equal for $k_{\mathrm{odd}} \geq 1$. A cycle with $k_{\mathrm{odd}} = 0$ (all even steps) would be $P \mapsto P / [2]_q^k = P$, forcing $P = 0$.

      Therefore the only periodic orbit is $P = 1$ (the unknot). $\square$

      Balanced-holonomy cycle exclusion (proved, sorry-free).

      Given a QOU whose Skein q-integers satisfy the Skein divisibility relation [3]_q = q·[2]_q + 1 with [2]_q a non-unit (as holds for [2]_q = q + 1 in the Skein module ℤ[q^{±1}]), the equation [3]_q^{k_odd} = [2]_q^{k} has no solution with k ≥ 1.

      A Skein-Collatz periodic orbit of balanced multiplicative holonomy (A = [3]_q^{k_odd}/[2]_q^{k} = 1) would force exactly this equation, so this lemma discharges the A = 1 sub-case of no-cycles by coprimality — unconditionally, over a generic commutative ring. It does not settle the affine (A ≠ 1) case, which is part of the open conj:q-collatz.

      Proof: [3]_q ≡ 1 (mod [2]_q) from the divisibility relation, so [2]_q ∣ [3]_q^{k_odd} − 1; and [2]_q ∣ [2]_q^{k} for k ≥ 1. Equality would give [2]_q ∣ 1, i.e. [2]_q a unit — contradiction.
