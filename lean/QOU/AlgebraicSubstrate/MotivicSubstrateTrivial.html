    Trivial witness for MotivicSubstrate #

    A minimal MotivicSubstrate ℤ ℤ trivialTarget instance, where every realisation target is just ℤ and every field is the obvious choice. The instance witnesses inhabitability of the class — guarding against accidental over-constraint — but is not the QOU substrate at q₀.

    The principal open item (GAP 6a in the 2026-05-02 forward-derivation audit) is to produce a concrete MotivicSubstrate for the QOU motivic substrate over ℚ(q₀). See 2026-05-04 GAP 6a + 18a scoping for the precise outstanding mathematical work.
