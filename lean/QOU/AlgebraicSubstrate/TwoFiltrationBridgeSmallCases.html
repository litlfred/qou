    Two-filtration bridge verified at small partitions #

    Compiled formalisation of prop:two-filtration-bridge-small-cases. Sibling docstub at algebraic-substrate/two-filtration-bridge-small-cases.lean.

    Small-case (n ≤ 4) verification of the bridge identity already stated in full generality at prop:two-filtration-bridge-general: self-conjugate cells go to depth 2, chiral pairs go to depth 3, restricted to partitions of size at most 4.
