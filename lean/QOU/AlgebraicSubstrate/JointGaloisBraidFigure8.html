    Joint Galois × braid action on the figure-8 cell #

    Honest §3b-cond conditional formalisation of prop:joint-galois-braid-figure-8-verified.

    The block claims: on the self-conjugate bi-filtration cell of the figure-8 knot 4_1 (dominant cell λ_K = (2,2) in H_4(q)), the motivic-Galois action (bar involution q ↦ q⁻¹) commutes with the braid-group action.

    Restructure rationale. The previous shell axiomatised the CONCLUSION: a class field galois_4_1_central asserted the Galois map commutes with every endomorphism f, and the theorem merely instantiated f := braid. That is a vacuous self-assuming projection (fails proof-statement-integrity). Here the class instead carries the two GENUINE structural inputs of the direct computation, and the theorem DERIVES the commutation by composing them:

    • A — bar-equivariance / naturality (bar_intertwines): the bar operator intertwines the Hoefsmit representation with its bar-conjugate ρ̄ (matrix entries q ↦ q⁻¹).
    • B — self-conjugacy of the (2,2) cell (self_conjugate_cell): because λ = λ' = (2,2), the bar-conjugate representation coincides with the original.

    Composing A then B gives bar ∘ ρ(s) = ρ(s) ∘ bar on each generator, which extends to arbitrary braid words by List.foldr induction — all sorry-free. The residual manuscript-level content (the disjoint tensor decomposition H₂ ⊗ H₂ → H₄ realising the (2,2) cell, and the vanishing of the Hochschild HH² obstruction to bar–coupling compatibility) is the one honest sorry in DisjointTensorCell.kappa_commutes_bar; it is a genuine, non-false claim not derivable from the diagonal structure alone.
