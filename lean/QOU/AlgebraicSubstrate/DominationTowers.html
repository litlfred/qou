    QOU.AlgebraicSubstrate.DominationTowers — infinite towers, Ohtsuki, and the sweep #

    Three things DominationSqueeze left open, per beans qou-w1bi, qou-7jbz, qou-syf4.

    §1 Filtration completeness (bean qou-w1bi) #

    Domination.val_add_sum_eq covers a finite tail (List.sum). A brane tower is infinite, so it needs a convergence notion. DeepTail supplies one without any topology: the tail T is deeper than grade γ when (a) every term is, and (b) every finite truncation approximates T to arbitrary depth. Then lt_val_of_deepTail gives γ < val T, and leading_survives_infinite_tail concludes val (a₀ + T) = val a₀ — the infinite tower does not collapse onto its leading term.

    §2 The Ohtsuki tower (bean qou-7jbz, route to qou-7jok) #

    The Ohtsuki tower 𝔙_b = 𝔙₀ + Σ_{n≥1} κₙ (2πb²)ⁿ (rem-ohtsuki-tower-bar-parity) is a coefficient sequence in ℏ = 2πb² with the complex volume at grade 0. That is exactly jetDomination's home, so no new structure is needed — ohtsukiVal := jetVal on the coefficient sequence.

    The payoff is grade_pos_iff_leading_vanishes: for a tower κ,

    0 < jetVal κ ↔ κ 0 = 0.

    Read on a polynomial combination of three towers, that is precisely the criterion the 2026-08-09 audit identified as the productive route: ord_ω(P(𝔙)) > 0 exactly when P(Vol) = 0. The grade genuinely separates relation from non-relation at the lifted level — unlike on ℝ, where QOU.Archimedean.SurrealGradingPrune shows every non-relation shares one grade.

    relation_forces_positive_grade and no_relation_of_grade_zero are the two directions, in the shape qou-7jok consumes.

    §3 The millennium sweep (bean qou-syf4) #

    rem:collatz-surreal-bound, rem:p-vs-np-surreal-bound and rem:ns-surreal-bound all instantiate one inequality — δ²·ε⁻¹ > ε³, times a positive scalar (2ⁿ in the P-vs-NP case, which that remark handles by "multiplying through by the standard-real factor 2ⁿ preserves the inequality"). squeeze_scaled is that step; the three named corollaries below are the instantiations.

    These are restatements, not proofs of the millennium problems. Each corollary proves the inference step the corresponding remark invokes, from hypotheses that remark supplies. The premises — Reeb-period budget accounting, BQP_q → P non-compressibility, the std-of-the-skein assumption — are untouched and remain exactly as conjectural as before.

    What is still open #

    The Newton-polyhedron generalisation: Domination.val lands in a linearly ordered Γ, but a q-difference equation's natural invariant is its Newton polygon, valued in a lattice-ordered group (ℤⁿ/ℚⁿ), where a linear order appears only after choosing a supporting functional. In that setting an archimedean inequality becomes a face-membership / LP-feasibility test against the polyhedron. The corpus already scaffolds the one-dimensional shadow of this in QOU.Mathlib.QGevreyOrder (slopes, -- Ref: [ramis-sauloy-zhang2013] q-difference Newton-polygon slopes / q-Gevrey), whose own docstring lists the Newton-polygon convolution law as out of scope — and that law is exactly the abstract le_val_add axiom here. Tracked as bean qou-filh; not built in this file.

          theorem QOU.AlgebraicSubstrate.grade_pos_iff_leading_vanishes (κ : ℕ → ℝ) :
          0 < ohtsukiVal κ ↔ κ 0 = 0

          The grade sees the leading coefficient — and nothing else does.

          0 < ohtsukiVal κ ↔ κ 0 = 0. On a polynomial combination of towers this reads: the combination has strictly positive grade exactly when the relation holds among the grade-0 parts (the complex volumes).

          This is the separation the surreal prune shows is unavailable on ℝ, where every non-relation shares grade 0. Lifting to the tower restores it.

          Contrapositive — grade 0 certifies no relation. A tower combination of grade 0 has a non-vanishing leading part, i.e. P(Vol) ≠ 0. This is the usable test: compute the grade, read off non-vanishing.
