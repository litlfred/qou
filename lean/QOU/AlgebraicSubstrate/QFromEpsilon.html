    QOU.AlgebraicSubstrate.QFromEpsilon — q := 1 + ε #

    Fixes the main-text convention that the substrate parameter q is the element 1 + ε of the ordered field generated by the Reeb infinitesimal ε over ℝ, and equivalently the element 1 + 1/ω of Conway's surreal field 𝐍𝐨 under the identification of QOU.AppendixSurreals.StandardPart.

    Morphisms in a plain category have no additive structure, so the identity q = 1 + ε cannot be stated at the morphism level. It is stated here at the surreal-valued level, via an abstract carrier field that downstream StandardPart-based modules will specialise to 𝐍𝐨 or a Hahn completion.

    structure QOU.AlgebraicSubstrate.QFromEpsilon :
    Type 1

    QFromEpsilon: an ordered-field carrier F together with distinguished elements oneValue, epsilonValue, and qSubstrate satisfying qSubstrate = oneValue + epsilonValue.

    The field F is abstract. Canonical instantiations are F = ℝ(ε) (rational functions of a formal infinitesimal) and F = 𝐍𝐨 (Conway's surreal field); both are available via QOU.AppendixSurreals. The carrier is left abstract here so the structure stays usable without pulling in the surreal formalism.

    LaTeX: \label{def:q-from-epsilon}

    • F : Type

      Ordered-field carrier containing both a standard-real subring and a positive infinitesimal.

    • add : self.F → self.F → self.F

      Addition on F (left abstract to avoid committing to a specific algebraic structure here).

    • oneValue : self.F

      Image of 1 ∈ ℝ in F.

    • epsilonValue : self.F

      Image of the Reeb infinitesimal ε ∈ Der(A) in F via the surreal embedding.

    • qSubstrate : self.F

      The substrate parameter q.

    • q_eq_one_plus_epsilon : self.qSubstrate = self.add self.oneValue self.epsilonValue

      Definitional identity q = 1 + ε.
