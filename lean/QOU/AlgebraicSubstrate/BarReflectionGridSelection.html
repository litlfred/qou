        theorem QOU.AlgebraicSubstrate.BarReflectionGridSelection.grid_barInvariant_classification {G : Type u_1} [CommGroup G] (u v w : G) (hw : w ^ 2 = v) (htf : ∀ (x : G), x ^ 2 = 1 → x = 1) :
        grid u⁻¹ v = grid u v ↔ grid u v = grid 1 v ∨ grid u v = grid w v

        The two-point classification. Suppose the step has a half-step w (w² = v) and G is 2-torsion-free (both hold in ℝ_{>0}). Then a grid class is bar-invariant iff it is the unit class grid 1 v or the half-step class grid w v — the two named candidates of the extension-circle question, separated by exactly half a q̃-step (the KSel-1 discriminant).
