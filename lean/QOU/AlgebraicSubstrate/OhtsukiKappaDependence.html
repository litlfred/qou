    QOU.AlgebraicSubstrate.OhtsukiKappaDependence — real κ data on the graded tower #

    Bean qou-7jok, partial. DominationTowers.grade_pos_iff_leading_vanishes says the Ohtsuki grade separates relation from non-relation. This file feeds it the actual 4₁ coefficients and records what they show.

    The data #

    From qbeta-ohtsuki-kappa-vs-ck-direct-comparison.witness.json (tier: numerical-exact, closed forms independently reproduced there):

    level value closed form
    κ₀ 2.029883… Vol(4₁) = 2·Cl₂(π/3) = 6Λ(π/3)
    κ₁ 0.554216472404899… 11√3·π/108
    κ₂ 0.731081807488100… 2π²/27

    The finding: the tail is algebraically DEPENDENT, the head is not #

    kappa_4_1_relation proves, sorry-free,

    288·κ₁² = 121·κ₂,

    an exact relation over ℚ. This is not a coincidence to be explained — it is forced. Every κₙ lies in ℚ(√3)·π^⌈n/2⌉ ⊂ ℚ(√3)(π), a field of transcendence degree 1 over ℚ (√3 is algebraic; only π is transcendental). So any two tail coefficients are algebraically dependent, by the same transcendence-degree argument that forces the tr_M jets to be dependent (TowerHeightAttack §4). 288x² − 121y is simply the minimal witness at levels 1, 2.

    And that is exactly why the route is viable rather than dead. The asymmetry is the point:

    • the tail κ₁, κ₂, … is arithmetic — a known field, computable, and algebraically dependent;
    • the head κ₀ = Vol(4₁) is not known to lie in ℚ(√3)(π) at all. Its irrationality is open (conj:single-volume-irrationality).

    The grading reads the head; the tail is where the computable constraints live. ohtsuki_4_1_grade_zero records the first consequence: the 4₁ tower sits at grade 0, because Vol(4₁) ≠ 0.

    What this does NOT do #

    qou-7jok asks for the joint tower over 4₁, 5₂, 𝓑₃. Only the 4₁ coefficients exist in the corpus — the witness above is 4₁-specific and no κₙ for 5₂ or 𝓑₃ was found. A single-knot tower cannot exhibit a relation among the three volumes, so the joint construction remains blocked on data, not on machinery. Nothing here is evidence for or against conj:hyperbolic-volume-transcendence.

    Two further caveats, both real:

    1. 𝔙₀ is the complex volume Vol + i·CS; the real-volume statement needs the real part extracted, which the ℝ-valued tower here elides.
    2. Each knot's Ohtsuki expansion is taken at its own root of unity, so the three towers are not a priori coefficients in one common ℏ.

    §1 The relation, formally #

    source
    theorem QOU.AlgebraicSubstrate.kappa_relation_formal {R : Type u_1} [CommRing R] (s p : R) (hs : s ^ 2 = 3) :
    288 * (11 * s * p) ^ 2 * 27 = 121 * (2 * p ^ 2) * 108 ^ 2

    The relation is formal. For any s with s² = 3 and any p, the pair (11sp/108, 2p²/27) satisfies a rational relation — nothing about π or √3 beyond √3² = 3 is used. Stated denominator-cleared so it holds over any commutative ring.

        §3 The head sits at grade zero #

        source
        theorem QOU.AlgebraicSubstrate.ohtsuki_4_1_grade_zero {κ : ℕ → ℝ} (h : κ 0 = Archimedean.vol_4_1) :
        ohtsukiVal κ = 0

        The 4₁ tower has grade 0. Its leading coefficient is Vol(4₁) ≠ 0, so by grade_pos_iff_leading_vanishes the grade cannot be positive — and grade 0 is exactly the "no relation" reading.

        This is the machinery of DominationTowers running on real data: the head is read by the grade, the (dependent, arithmetic) tail is not.

        source
        theorem QOU.AlgebraicSubstrate.relation_would_lift_4_1_grade {κ : ℕ → ℝ} (h : ohtsukiVal κ = 0) :
        κ 0 ≠ 0

        Contrapositive on real data: were a polynomial relation to hold among the leading parts, the combined tower would have to leave grade 0. With only the 4₁ tower available this is a statement about one knot, not the three-knot relation qou-7jok needs.
