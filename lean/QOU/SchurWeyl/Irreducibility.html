    QOU.SchurWeyl — irreducibility of the seminormal representation (SW-5) #

    The shape-(2,1) seminormal representation seminormalRep21 : H_3(q) →ₐ[ℝ] M_2(ℝ) (HoefsmitRep.lean) is irreducible for every q ≠ 0. For a 2-dimensional representation, irreducibility is exactly the absence of a 1-dimensional invariant subspace, i.e. the absence of a common eigenvector of the two generator images S0, S1. We prove that directly:

    • an S0-eigenvector is forced to be axis-aligned, because S0 = diag(q, -q⁻¹) has distinct eigenvalues (q ≠ -q⁻¹ over ℝ, since q + q⁻¹ ≠ 0); but
    • S1 fixes neither axis — its off-diagonal entries c₀₁ = 1 and c₁₀ = (q⁴+q²+1)/(q²+1)² > 0 are nonzero — so no common eigenvector exists.

    This is the concrete d_λ = 2 case of SW-5 (bean qou-jfr8); the general-λ irreducibility depends on the general seminormal construction (residual bean qou-im9l) and is not proved here.

    Base ring ℝ (archimedean: the seminormal matrices live over ℝ, §7c).

    theorem QOU.SchurWeyl.seminormal21_no_common_eigenvector (q : ℝ) (hq : q ≠ 0) :
    ¬∃ (v : Fin 2 → ℝ), v ≠ 0 ∧ (∃ (a : ℝ), (S0 q).mulVec v = a • v) ∧ ∃ (b : ℝ), (S1 q).mulVec v = b • v

    The seminormal (2,1) representation is irreducible (q ≠ 0): S0 and S1 have no common eigenvector, hence no common 1-dimensional invariant subspace, hence the 2-dimensional representation is irreducible.
