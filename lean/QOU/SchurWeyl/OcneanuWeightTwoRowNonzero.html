    The Ocneanu weight is NONZERO on the at-most-two-row shapes #

    ocneanuWeight_conventionD_eq_zero_of_three_rows kills every shape with a third row. This is the complementary half: at convention D the weight of a shape with at most two rows is nonzero, under exactly the genericity wtrace already carries.

    🛑 This was recorded as blocked on Wenzl's theorem. It is not. #

    QOU/SchurWeyl/WtraceRadicalTwoRow.lean and bean qou-s7z2 both state that the ⊆ half of "the radical is the ℓ(λ) ≥ 3 blocks" needs the ≤2-row weights to be nonzero, and that the corpus has that only through WenzlWeightVanishing.conventionD_ocneanuWeight_eq_zero_iff_of_wenzl, which is conditional on the class IsOcneanuWeightSystem — a class whose own docstring says an instance "is exactly Wenzl's theorem, and is exactly what remains unformalised."

    That reasoning went through the wrong object. The class exists to relate an opaque candidate weight y to schurPrincipal; ocneanuWeight is not opaque — it is a closed form, and the non-vanishing follows from it directly.

    The computation #

    At z = q³/(q²+1) the per-cell numerator factor collapses:

    q^{2i}(q² − 1 − q·z) + q^{2j+1}·z = (q^{2j+4} − q^{2i}) / (q² + 1)

    because q² − 1 − q⁴/(q²+1) = −1/(q²+1). So the factor vanishes iff q^{2(c+2)} = 1 where c = j − i is the cell's content — which recovers ocneanuNumFactor_eq_zero_of_content_neg_two as the case c = −2, and shows there is nothing else to worry about: for a shape with at most two rows every cell has i ≤ 1, hence content c ≥ −1, hence c + 2 ≥ 1, and (q²)^k ≠ 1 for k ≥ 1 is hgen.

    The denominator needs the same hypothesis and nothing more: hookLength is positive on a cell of the diagram, because a cell's own arm contains it.

    What this unblocks #

    The ⊆ direction of the radical identification at the wtrace carrier. The only remaining inputs are a standard matrix-pairing fact and the block-isolation construction — neither of which is Wenzl's theorem.
