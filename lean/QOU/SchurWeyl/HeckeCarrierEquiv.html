    QOU.SchurWeyl.HeckeCarrierEquiv — the two RingQuot presentations are one algebra #

    Bean qou-img9 (the presentation pile) and the carrier item of qou-kwoa.

    QOU/SchurWeyl/HeckeSemisimpleGeneral.lean proves IsSemisimpleRing at every rank — but for QOU.SchurWeyl.Hecke, whereas the T_w basis and twGram are built on QOU.Hecke.IwahoriHecke. That gap was recorded, not closed. This file closes it.

    The two relations differ in exactly one clause #

    SchurWeyl.HeckeRel K q qi n :  σ² = (q − qi)·σ + (q·qi)·1
QOU.Hecke.HeckeRel  K Q  n  :  σ² = (Q − 1)·σ + Q·1

    The braid and far-commutation clauses are verbatim identical, on the same FreeAlgebra K (Fin n). So the whole content is the quadratic, and it moves under a generator rescaling. Write σ for the skein generator (source) and T for the Iwahori generator at Q (target), and send σ i ↦ c • T i. Squaring the target and using the Iwahori relation on T gives

    (c • T)² = c²(Q − 1) • T + (c² Q) • 1,

    while the skein relation demands

    (c • T)² = (q − qi) • (c • T) + (q · qi) • 1 = c(q − qi) • T + (q · qi) • 1.

    Matching the two coefficients gives c²(Q − 1) = c(q − qi) and c² Q = q · qi. We take the first in the division-free, slightly stronger form c(Q − 1) = q − qi, which implies it by multiplying through by c. Nothing here asks c to be a unit: toIwahori is built over a bare [CommRing K] from those two identities alone. An inverse enters only for the map back — fromIwahori takes a separate d with d · c = 1 — and only there because the two directions must compose to the identity.

    Those two identities are the hypotheses hc₁ and hc₂ below. The braid and commutation clauses are homogeneous of the same degree in the generators, so the rescaling cannot disturb them — proved here rather than asserted.

    The specialisation that matters #

    At the skein normalisation qi = q⁻¹ (so q·qi = 1), taking

    Q = q²   and   c = q⁻¹

    satisfies both identically: c²Q = q⁻²q² = 1 = q·qi, and c(Q−1) = q⁻¹(q²−1) = q − q⁻¹ = q − qi. The parameter moves, not the ring — no square root is adjoined, and nothing leaves a generic [Field K]. This is the same rescaling QOU/BraidKnot/HeckeIwahoriConventionBridge.lean derives for generator families; what is new here is that it is upgraded to an AlgEquiv of the two RingQuot carriers, which is what transports a ring-theoretic property.

    Where the ℝ corollary went #

    isSemisimpleRing_iwahoriHecke — the payoff this equivalence exists to carry — is not in this file. It needs Real.sqrt (to name the q whose square is the Iwahori Q), and §7c forbids mixing a generic-ring development with its archimedean specialisation in one file. It lives in QOU/SchurWeyl/HeckeCarrierSemisimpleReal.lean, which imports this one and consumes skeinIwahoriEquiv.

    What this is not #

    It is not the IwahoriHeckeOfSn bridge — QOU/BraidKnot/HeckeTypeAPresentationEquiv.lean already crosses that pair, and this is a different pair. Between them, three of the presentations qou-img9 counts are now provably one algebra.
