    QOU.SchurWeyl.GenericParameterReal — over ℝ the generic hypothesis is free #

    Beans qou-j377, qou-kwoa.

    QOU/SchurWeyl/GenericParameter.lean carries NotRootOfUnity over an arbitrary field. This file carries the one statement that is specific to ℝ — and specific in the strong sense: it is false over a general field, since a primitive cube root of unity has q ≠ 0 and q ^ 2 ≠ 1 and q ^ 3 = 1.

    That is exactly why the Wedderburn cone is ℝ-only. SeminormalRep.zpow_ne_one gets "q is not a root of unity" for free from q ≠ 0 ∧ q ^ 2 ≠ 1 because the only real roots of unity are ±1; a general field affords no such shortcut and the criterion has to be carried. Keeping the two apart, per §7c, makes the boundary visible instead of leaving it buried in a Real.log step three files away.

    Because this direction is an implication into NotRootOfUnity, every ℝ statement in the cone is the specialisation of a generic one — so generalising those files strengthens no hypothesis and invalidates no downstream use.
