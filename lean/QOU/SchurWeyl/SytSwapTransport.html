    QOU.SchurWeyl.SytSwapTransport — admissible-swap connectivity, transported #

    The connectivity half of SW-5 (bean qou-jfr8), carried across the same bridge SytContentTuple used for separation.

    QOU.BraidKnot.JucysMurphy.syt_adjacentSwap_connected proves, sorry-free, that any two standard tableaux of one shape are joined by a chain of admissible adjacent swaps. Reaching it from the SchurWeyl side looked like it would need the two swap constructions matched — swapCellsSYT against swapTableau — which is a much larger job than matching the label conventions was.

    It does not, and the reason is worth stating. AdjacentSwapStep is defined purely in terms of content vectors: T' is a step from T when their contents agree away from {a, a+1}, are exchanged at {a, a+1}, and the gap d satisfies d ≠ 0, d² ≠ 1. It never mentions how T' is built. So the transport needs only that swapTableau T k has that content profile — which cellOf_swapTableau_self / _succ / _of_ne give — plus separation (syt_eq_of_cellContent_eq), which says a content profile determines the tableau at most one way. Separation, landed for the eigenvalue argument, turns out to do the structural matching too.

    The admissibility conditions line up on the nose: SwapAdmissible T k asks that the cells of k and k+1 share neither row nor column, and in a standard tableau sharing a row forces d = 1 (axialDist_of_sameRow) while sharing a column forces d = −1 (axialDist_of_sameCol) — so d² ≠ 1 is exactly admissibility.
