    Radical descent on the Hecke colimit, with no conditional expectation #

    QBeta.TLTowerRadicalDescent (brick 3 of qou-s7z2) relates the radicals at consecutive ranks — conditionally on HeckeConditionalExpectation, an open §3b class with no instance. Its forward direction, map_mem_traceRadical, does not need it.

    What is narrowed #

    Brick 3's file argues that a bare inclusion cannot do better:

    A bare inclusion ι : Hn →ₐ Hn1 says nothing about the two radicals: membership in rad τn1 quantifies over all of Hn1, not over ι '' Hn, so no amount of compatibility between τn and τn1 on the subalgebra constrains it.

    Correct about a bare inclusion; too strong about a tower. A test element of the next stage is not arbitrary — TowerSpan says each stage is spanned by the previous one together with single sandwiches v * g * w — so cyclicity moves the outer factor to the front, Markov strips the generator, and the product lands back in the stage below. MarkovTraceRadical.tr_mul_eq_zero_of_step_span is that step in the abstract, and mem_rad_of_orthogonal_H below is the Hecke colimit's instance of the conclusion, unconditionally.

    What is NOT narrowed #

    • The converse, mem_traceRadical_of_map. That direction lifts a test element of Hn to ι b and then needs ε (ι b) = b — it spends inst.retract, and nothing here replaces it. So the class is still required for map_mem_traceRadical_iff, hence for TL_n ↪ TL_{n+1} and for the tower's injectivity.
    • HeckeGramFactorial's open input, the general T_w-basis determinant, which bricks 1–3 never touched.
    • A WenzlTowerStep instance at the Markov trace. This is one hypothesis removed from one direction, not the bean closed.

    Scope #

    Convention D, ℝ, and the four genericity hypotheses wtrace already carries. The abstract half (MarkovTraceRadical) is generic [CommRing R] [Ring A] [Algebra R A] and carries no genericity at all.

    Orthogonality at one level is radical membership — unconditionally.

    If a sits at level k ≥ 1 of the Hecke colimit and the Ocneanu trace form cannot see it within that level, then it cannot see it anywhere: a is in the radical of trLim.

    This is the conclusion of QBeta.TLTowerRadicalDescent.map_mem_traceRadical without its HeckeConditionalExpectation hypothesis. TowerSpan does the work instead, and the Hecke colimit has a TowerSpan instance (towerSpan_heckeColimit, proved from the braid relations).
