    The Ocneanu trace is NOT faithful on H_n(q) — at the carrier, not in the abstract #

    The corpus already holds every ingredient of this statement, sorry-free on main, and had never composed them at the carrier the tower results actually use. What was missing is assembled here: a single element of Hecke ℝ q q⁻¹ 2 = H_3(q) that is nonzero and that the trace form ⟨x, b⟩ = wtrace (x * b) cannot see.

    Why this is an assembly and not a derivation #

    Two facts do all the work, and both are on main:

    • wtrace is defined as the Frobenius sum Σ_ν y_ν · tr ρ_ν (SeminormalRep.wtrace, wtrace_apply is rfl) — so no character-theoretic decomposition has to be proved here.
    • ocneanuWeight_conventionD_eq_zero_of_three_rows says the weight y_ν vanishes at convention D for every shape with a third row, unconditionally over any field with q² + 1 ≠ 0.

    So the trace form factors through the blocks with at most two rows, and anything supported on a three-row block is invisible to it. heckeProduct_surjective (Wedderburn) produces such an element and heckeProduct_injective shows it is nonzero.

    Scope — this is a structural exclusion, and the invariant is named #

    Per AGENTS.md §"Before declaring refuted", this is scope (b): not "this construction alone did not work", but "the form provably annihilates a nonzero element". The invariant is the weight vanishing above. It is a statement about convention D (z = q³/(q²+1), canonical per the owner ruling of 2026-08-15) and inverts under the excised convention C, where the three-row weight is nonzero and the form is nondegenerate.

    What this does NOT say #

    • It does not say the framework loses anything. The radical is the Temperley–Lieb ideal, and the form is nondegenerate on the quotient H_n/rad ≅ TL_n (QBeta.MarkovTraceQuotient.quot_pairing_nondegenerate). This file supplies the negative half at the wtrace carrier; that file supplies the positive half abstractly.
    • It does not identify the radical. That the radical is exactly the ℓ(λ) ≥ 3 blocks, of dimension n! − Catalan n, is QBeta.MarkovGramDegenerate. Here only one direction is needed and only one element is exhibited.
    • It says nothing about heckeIncl or ofLim injectivity, which is a different question about the tower inclusions and is proved affirmatively in HeckeInclInjective / HeckeColimitFaithful. "Faithful" is a homonym in this corpus and these two answers point opposite ways.

      The invisible element #

      theorem QOU.SchurWeyl.OcneanuTraceNotFaithful.exists_nonzero_wtrace_orthogonal (q : ℝ) (hq0 : q ≠ 0) (hq2 : GenericParameter.NotRootOfUnity q) (hq21 : q ^ 2 + 1 ≠ 0) :
      ∃ (x : Hecke ℝ q q⁻¹ 2), x ≠ 0 ∧ ∀ (b : Hecke ℝ q q⁻¹ 2), (SeminormalRep.wtrace q hq0 hq2 2) (x * b) = 0

      The Ocneanu trace form on H_3(q) is degenerate.

      There is a nonzero x ∈ Hecke ℝ q q⁻¹ 2 with wtrace (x * b) = 0 for every b — so the form ⟨x, b⟩ = wtrace (x * b) has nonzero radical and wtrace is not faithful.

      x is the Wedderburn preimage of the tuple that is the identity on the three-row block and zero on the other two. Nonzero because heckeProduct is injective; invisible because the three-row weight is zero and every other block kills it.
