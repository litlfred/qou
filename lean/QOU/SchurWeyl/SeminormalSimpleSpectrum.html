    QOU.SchurWeyl.SeminormalSimpleSpectrum — the q-Jucys–Murphy spectrum is simple #

    The separation half of general irreducibility (SW-5, bean qou-jfr8).

    Two facts combine here, and neither suffices alone:

    • Ram (1997) Thm 4.1 (SeminormalRep.seminormalRep_qJM, proved in QJucysMurphyEigen): each L_k acts diagonally on the SYT basis, with entry q^{2·cellContent (cellOf T k)}. Diagonal is not separating — a diagonal matrix with a repeated entry has a whole plane of eigenvectors, and any subspace of that plane would be invariant.
    • Separation (syt_eq_of_cellContent_eq, transported from the Okounkov–Vershik stack in SytContentTuple): distinct tableaux of one shape have distinct content tuples.

    Together they give what irreducibility actually needs: the map sending a tableau to its tuple of L_k-eigenvalues is injective, so the joint eigenspaces of the commuting family {L_0, …, L_n} are the individual coordinate lines. Any subspace invariant under the whole family is then a span of a subset of the SYT basis — which is the step that reduces irreducibility to connectivity under admissible swaps.

    Where the genericity hypothesis earns its keep. Passing from equal eigenvalues q^{2c} = q^{2c'} to equal contents c = c' is exactly where q ^ 2 ≠ 1 is used (SeminormalRep.zpow_ne_one): at a root of unity distinct contents can share an eigenvalue, the spectrum stops being simple, and this argument — not merely this proof — fails. That regime is tracked as bean qou-4lft.

    Refs:

    • [ram1997] Ram, Seminormal representations of Weyl groups and Iwahori–Hecke algebras, https://doi.org/10.1112/S0024611597000373
    • [okounkov1996] Okounkov–Vershik — the Gelfand–Tsetlin/content-tuple separation at q = 1.

    The q-Jucys–Murphy spectrum is simple. A tableau is determined by its tuple of L_k-eigenvalues in the seminormal representation.

    This is what makes the diagonalisation of seminormalRep_qJM useful: the joint eigenspaces of the commuting family {L_0, …, L_n} are single coordinate lines, so every subspace invariant under the family is spanned by a subset of the SYT basis.
