    The seminormal block family is faithful #

    Bean qou-ejde. heckeProduct = ⊕_λ ρ_λ is injective, so the seminormal representations separate points of H_{n+1}(q).

    Why this is not circular, and why it is one line #

    HeckeProductSurjective.heckeProduct_surjective proves the map is onto, and HeckeWedderburnGeneral computes both dimensions as (n+1)! — finrank_hecke_eq_factorial upstairs and finrank_blocks_eq_factorial downstairs. A surjection between spaces of equal finite dimension is bijective (LinearMap.injective_iff_surjective_of_finrank_eq_finrank), so injectivity is the free half of an equality that was already proved for its other half.

    Note the direction of use: finrank_hecke_eq_factorial obtains the lower bound (n+1)! ≤ dim H from that same surjection, and the upper bound from HeckeCoset.finrank_le_factorial. So the dimension equality is not derived from injectivity, and using it here to get injectivity is not circular.

    What this unlocks #

    Bean qou-ejde records the route: with faithfulness, heckeIncl is injective — compose with SeminormalBlockEquality.compress_seminormalRep_heckeIncl, which identifies the upstairs block of heckeIncl y with the downstairs block of y. heckeIncl injectivity is in turn the single input to Mathlib's Ring.DirectLimit.of_injective, which yields the Hecke colimit with injective canonical maps — and with it the bridge from the level-indexed HeckeCoset.MarkovTraceFamily (which carries the genuine Ocneanu trace, proved in MarkovTraceInstance) to the single-algebra OcneanuTrace.MarkovTrace that TowerSpanReduction and DisjointBlockTraceLaw consume.

    🛑 Only the faithfulness step is proved here. The heckeIncl corollary and the colimit are not in this file; they are the remainder of qou-ejde, and the eraseCorner-surjectivity gap that the composition needs is recorded there.

    Scope #

    Generic q over a characteristic-zero field: [Field K], [CharZero K], q ≠ 0, NotRootOfUnity q — inherited from finrank_hecke_eq_factorial, which needs semisimplicity. At a root of unity H_n(q) is not semisimple and the partitions stop indexing the irreducibles, so the statement would need a different proof, not merely weaker hypotheses.
