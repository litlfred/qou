    QOU.SchurWeyl.SeminormalBlockEquality — a generator's matrix on a corner block #

    SytInsertMaxTransport supplied the four tableau-level compatibilities that genMat's entry rules read. This file assembles them into the statement they were for:

    genMat q k (insertMax c hc S'') (insertMax c hc S') = genMat q k S'' S'

    for any k with k + 1 ≤ |μ ∖ c| — a generator that avoids the top entry has, on the block of the corner c, exactly the matrix it has downstairs on μ ∖ c. It is a case analysis over genMat's three branches: diagonal, swap partner, zero.

    Also here, and needed next: the block of c is exactly the image of insertMax (cellOf_insertMax_top and exists_insertMax_of_cellOf_top, the latter a repackaging of QOU.Mathlib.insertMax_eraseMax).

    Where this sits #

    Step 1 of the three the Markov assembly needs (bean qou-uer7):

    1. this file — the block equality at a generator;
    2. lifting it from generators to all of H_{|μ|-1}(q) — also here, compress_seminormalRep_heckeIncl — then the outer reindexing {(λ, corner)} ↔ {(μ, addable corner)}, now in QOU.Mathlib.YoungDiagramCornerReindex;
    3. the λ-sum with weights, where OcneanuWeightMarkovCorner's corner identities are consumed, now in MarkovAssembly.

    All three are written. What remains outside Lean is the corner identity itself at general n.

    The image characterisation is what makes step 2's first half work: lifting to a product needs the matrix-multiplication sum over the block to be a sum over SYT (μ ∖ c), which is exactly the block and that image coinciding.

    From a generator to every element #

    Compression to the block is multiplicative on block-preserving matrices — the sum over SYT μ collapses to the block, and the block is exactly insertMax's image. With genMat_insertMax for the generators and seminormalRep_heckeIncl_mem_blockSubalgebra for the block-preservation, an adjoin-induction lifts the block equality to every element of H_{m+1}(q).
