    QOU.SchurWeyl.HeckeStandardBasis — the coset family indexed by permutations, and τ(T_w) = δ_{w,1} #

    HeckeCoset.cosetFam m : cosetIndex m → Hecke K q qi m is the coset spanning family of H_{m+1}(q), indexed abstractly by cosetIndex m ((m+1)! elements). This file indexes it by permutations w ∈ S_{m+1} = Perm (Fin (m+1)), so that it becomes the standard family T_w, and restates the symmetrising-trace theorem as τ(T_w) = δ_{w,1}.

    The dictionary #

    • adjT i = (i i+1) — the adjacent transposition matching the generator σ_i.
    • permIncl m : S_{m+1} →* S_{m+2} — the inclusion fixing the last point, matching heckeIncl (permIncl_adjT).
    • cosetWord m p : List (Fin m) — the word of generators whose product is cosetFam m p (cosetFam_eq_prod_word), of length cosetLen m p (cosetWord_length).
    • permOf m p — the product of the same word in S_{m+1} (permOf_eq_prod_word).

    permOf m is a bijection cosetIndex m ≃ S_{m+1} (permOfEquiv): injective by reading off w⁻¹(last) (which fixes the top coset representative) and cancelling, then bijective by counting.

    So T_w := cosetFam m (permOfEquiv.symm w) (stdElt) is the product of σ along a word whose image in S_{m+1} is w (stdElt_eq_prod_word, stdWord_prod_eq).

    The trace #

    MarkovTraceFamily.tr_stdElt_of_zero: a Markov trace with z = 0 satisfies

    T.tr m (T_w) = if w = 1 then 1 else 0.

    Reduced words #

    stdWord m w is a reduced word for w: its length is the inversion number ninv w (stdWord_length), and every word for w has length ≥ ninv w (ninv_le_length), so no word for w is shorter (stdWord_reduced). The ingredients are ninv (w s_i) = ninv w + 1 at an ascent (ninv_mul_adjT_of_lt), permIncl preserving ninv, and each coset-representative letter being an ascent.

    Scope #

    T_w is the product along this particular reduced word. That every reduced word for w gives the same product (Matsumoto's theorem) is not reproved here; it is not needed for the trace statement, which is about this family.

    Base ring (§7c) #

    Generic [CommRing K].
