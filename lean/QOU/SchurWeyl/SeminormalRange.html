    QOU.SchurWeyl.SeminormalRange — matrix units inside the seminormal image #

    Toward SW-5 in the range form AlgHom.range (seminormalRep …) = ⊤, which is what the Wedderburn assembly (SW-7, qou-0h5k) actually consumes: it needs surjectivity onto the matrix block, not an abstract "no proper submodule" statement, and it avoids standing up a module-over-the-algebra structure just to say so.

    Two of the three steps are here, and neither needs connectivity:

    • Diagonal units (single_self_mem_range). Each ρ(L_k) is its own diagonal (diagonal_seminormalRep_qJM), the tuples separate the tableaux (seminormalRep_qJM_spectrum_injective), so DiagonalSeparation interpolates every single T T 1 out of the family.
    • Off-diagonal units from a nonzero entry (single_mem_range_of_entry_ne_zero). Sandwiching any element of the image between two diagonal units extracts a single scaled matrix unit (Matrix.single_mul_mul_single), and the scalar is invertible exactly when the entry is nonzero.

    What is not here is the step that supplies those nonzero entries for every pair: an invariant-subspace-free way of saying that the SYT of one shape are connected by admissible swaps, along each of which genMat's off-diagonal entry offDiag is nonzero. syt_adjacentSwap_connected proves exactly that on the BraidKnot side, sorry-free; transporting it is the remaining work (bean qou-jfr8), and it is a strictly larger translation than SytContentTuple was, because the swap notions have to be matched, not just the label conventions.

    Every diagonal matrix unit lies in the image of the seminormal representation.

    The q-Jucys–Murphy images are diagonal and separating, which is exactly the hypothesis pair of single_self_mem_of_separating; the interpolation there does the rest.

    theorem QOU.SchurWeyl.single_mem_range_of_entry_ne_zero {μ : YoungDiagram} {K : Type u_1} [Field K] (q : K) (hq0 : q ≠ 0) (hq2 : GenericParameter.NotRootOfUnity q) (n : ℕ) (hN : μ.cells.card = n + 1) {x : Hecke K q q⁻¹ n} {T T' : Mathlib.StandardYoungTableau μ} (hx : (SeminormalRep.seminormalRep q hq0 hq2 n hN) x T' T ≠ 0) :
    Matrix.single T' T 1 ∈ (SeminormalRep.seminormalRep q hq0 hq2 n hN).range

    A nonzero matrix entry promotes to an off-diagonal matrix unit. If some element of the image has a nonzero (T', T) entry, then single T' T 1 is itself in the image: sandwich between the two diagonal units and divide by the entry.
