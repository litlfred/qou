    heckeIncl is injective — the composition bean qou-ejde mapped #

    The tower inclusion H_m(q) → H_{m+1}(q) is injective. This is the third and last step of the route recorded on bean qou-ejde, and the file that composes it; the other two were already on main.

    The three steps, and which file owns each #

    step statement where
    1 the seminormal family is faithful: heckeProduct is injective HeckeProductInjective
    2 the upstairs block of heckeIncl y, compressed to a corner, IS the downstairs block of y SeminormalBlockEquality.compress_seminormalRep_heckeIncl
    3 compose them this file

    Step 3 is two lemmas. seminormalRep_eq_of_heckeIncl_eq says that if heckeIncl identifies x and y then every downstairs seminormal block does too — for an arbitrary diagram ν of the right size, which is where the surjectivity of corner removal is consumed. heckeIncl_injective then feeds that to step 1's separation form eq_of_seminormalRep_eq.

    Why ν is a variable and not partitionShape lam #

    Step 2 is stated upstairs: its conclusion mentions seminormalRep at the diagram eraseCorner μ c hc, while the goal mentions it at ν. seminormalRep's output type depends on the diagram (it is a matrix indexed by that diagram's standard tableaux), so relating the two is a dependent rewrite and transporting along an equation of diagrams would land in HEq.

    Quantifying over ν as a variable avoids the transport entirely: exists_eraseCorner_eq returns an equation eraseCorner μ c hc = ν whose right side is that variable, so subst replaces ν — carrying hν with it — and step 2 then applies on the nose, with hν itself as its hNs. No transport, no HEq, no congr. The call site instantiates ν := partitionShape lam, which is an ordinary application of a ∀-statement and needs nothing.

    Scope — inherited, and to be stated downstream #

    ℝ, q ≠ 0, NotRootOfUnity q: generic q. Step 1 needs [Field K] [CharZero K] and genericity because finrank_hecke_eq_factorial needs semisimplicity; step 2 is stated over ℝ with qi = q⁻¹. At a root of unity H_n(q) is not semisimple, the partitions stop indexing the irreducibles, and this proof does not merely weaken — it fails. Every downstream consequence inherits that hypothesis.

    What this does NOT give #

    Injectivity of the tower inclusion, not of anything about a colimit. In particular it does not prove that ofLim n is injective for heckeColimitMarkovTrace, and it does not make trLim faithful. qou-ejde's own later note measures that the Hecke colimit and its canonical maps exist without any injectivity hypothesis (@DirectLimit / @DirectLimit.Ring.of carry none), so what this unlocks is the strict-growth question (bean qou-p8yg), not the bridge. Nor is Ring.DirectLimit.of_injective the lemma to reach for: under the pinned Mathlib v4.24.0 the namespace is unified and no lemma of that name exists — qou-ejde records this correction.

    -- Ref: [manuscript] bean qou-ejde, steps 1-3; Geck-Pfeiffer, Characters of Finite Coxeter -- Groups and Iwahori-Hecke Algebras, §8.1 (generic semisimplicity, seminormal form).
