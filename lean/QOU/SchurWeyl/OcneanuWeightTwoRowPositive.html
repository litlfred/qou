    The Ocneanu weight is POSITIVE on the at-most-two-row shapes, for every q > 0, q ≠ 1 #

    ocneanuWeight_conventionD_eq_zero_of_three_rows kills every shape with a third row at convention D (z = q³/(q²+1)), and ocneanuWeight_conventionD_ne_zero_of_two_rows shows the remaining weights are nonzero. This file upgrades "nonzero" to strictly positive over an ordered field, for every q > 0 with q ≠ 1.

    The computation #

    By ocneanuNumFactor_conventionD the numerator factor of a cell (i, j) is q^{2i}·((q²)^{j+2−i} − 1)/(q²+1), and the denominator factor is (q²)^{h} − 1 with h ≥ 1 the hook length. On a shape with at most two rows i ≤ 1, so the exponent j + 2 − i is at least 1. Both (q²)^m − 1 and (q²)^h − 1 then have the sign of q² − 1: positive for q > 1, negative for 0 < q < 1. Each cell therefore contributes a positive ratio, and so does their product.

    Scope #

    • q = 1 is excluded, and it must be. There every denominator factor is 0, so the closed form ocneanuWeight is x / 0, which Lean evaluates to 0. The classical weight at q = 1 is the limit of this formula, not its value, and nothing here says anything about it.
    • This is positivity of the weights, not of a state. That the Markov trace on H_n(q) is a positive functional for the *-structure σᵢ* = σᵢ is Jones's Temperley–Lieb positivity for loop value [2]_q = q + q⁻¹ ≥ 2 (Jones 1983/1985, [jones1985]), which is cited, not proved, here. The weights being positive is the per-block shadow of that statement: on each surviving Wedderburn block the trace is a positive multiple of the matrix trace.
    • The general-n Markov property of ocneanuWeight is still open (see OcneanuWeight). This file proves a sign, not that the weights are the Markov trace at general n.

    Base ring (§7c) #

    The statement needs an ordering, so it is over [Field R] [LinearOrder R] [IsStrictOrderedRing R], not ℝ; it mentions no archimedean construct.

    theorem QOU.SchurWeyl.ocneanuWeight_conventionD_pos_of_two_rows {R : Type u_1} [Field R] [LinearOrder R] [IsStrictOrderedRing R] (q : R) (hq : 0 < q) (hq1 : q ≠ 1) (mu : YoungDiagram) (h : mu.rowLen 2 = 0) :
    0 < ocneanuWeight q (q ^ 3 / (q ^ 2 + 1)) mu

    The Ocneanu weight is strictly positive on the at-most-two-row shapes at convention D, for every q > 0 with q ≠ 1.

    Together with ocneanuWeight_conventionD_eq_zero_of_three_rows, the convention-D weights are nonnegative everywhere on q > 0, q ≠ 1, with support exactly the shapes of at most two rows.
