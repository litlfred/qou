    QOU.SchurWeyl.HeckeStandardBasisIndep — the T_w form a basis at generic q #

    Over a field K of characteristic zero, with q ≠ 0 not a root of unity and qi = q⁻¹, the elements T_w = stdElt m w for w ∈ S_{m+1} are linearly independent, so they form a basis of H_{m+1}(q) (stdBasis).

    Proof #

    • The T_w span: they are the coset family reindexed by permutations (HeckeCoset.span_cosetFam_eq_top).
    • There are (m+1)! of them, and dim H_{m+1}(q) = (m+1)! at generic q (finrank_hecke_eq_factorial, via the Wedderburn surjection).
    • A spanning family whose size is the dimension is a basis.

    Scope #

    This is the generic-parameter statement only. Over an arbitrary commutative ring (for example ℤ[q, q⁻¹], or q a root of unity) linear independence is not proved here; that needs the regular representation, and is not done.

    Base ring (§7c) #

    [Field K] [CharZero K], the hypotheses of finrank_hecke_eq_factorial. Nothing archimedean.
