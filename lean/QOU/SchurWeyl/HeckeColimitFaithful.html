    The Hecke colimit is faithful, and its filtration grows strictly #

    HeckeColimit's own docstrings say, three times, that H n is "the image, not an isomorphic copy of Hecke K q qi n — those coincide exactly when ofLim n is injective, which is the open question of bean qou-ejde and is not assumed anywhere here."

    That question is settled here. ofLim n is injective at generic q, so the colimit contains each level of the tower faithfully and the range filtration H is strictly increasing.

    The route, and why it is short now #

    Mathlib.DirectLimit.mk_injective says the canonical map into a direct limit is injective as soon as every transition map is. So the whole content is lifting one-step injectivity to all steps:

    1. heckeIncl_injective (bean qou-ejde, one step) — already proved;
    2. heckeInclLE_injective (this file) — every step, by Nat.le_induction over heckeInclLE_succ, since a composition of injections is an injection;
    3. ofLim_injective (this file) — DirectLimit.mk_injective applied to 2.

    🛑 Ring.DirectLimit.of_injective does not exist, under that name, in the pinned Mathlib v4.24.0 — bean qou-ejde's original route cites it and that citation is dead. The live lemma is DirectLimit.mk_injective, whose hypothesis is exactly "all transitions injective"; the two injectivity lemmas that do exist under the old spelling are lift_injective and mk_injective.

    What this closes, and what it does not #

    Closes: ofLim faithfulness, hence finrank (H n) = (n+1)! and H n < H (n+1) — strict growth of the colimit's filtration, which is the level HeckeTowerWitness could not reach and which qou-p8yg asks for.

    Does not close: trLim faithfulness. That is a different statement — whether the trace on the colimit separates points — and nothing here bears on it. HeckeColimitMarkovTrace's "What is NOT proved here" lists both; only the first is discharged.

    Nor does this transfer to twH in MathlibExt/HeckeTowerWitness, whose separator is a rank-2 collapse. This is the colimit carrier, not that one.

    Scope #

    ℝ, q ≠ 0, NotRootOfUnity q — generic q, inherited from heckeIncl_injective (whose step 2 is stated over ℝ) and from finrank_hecke_eq_factorial (which needs semisimplicity). At a root of unity every step of this fails, not merely weakens.
