    QOU.SchurWeyl.HeckeMultiplicationRule — T_w σ_i in the standard basis #

    With T_w := stdElt m w (well defined on reduced words by HeckeMatsumoto.matsumoto), the right action of a generator is

    T_w σ_i = T_{w s_i}                              if w(i) < w(i+1)   (ascent),
T_w σ_i = (q − qi) T_w + (q·qi) T_{w s_i}        if w(i) > w(i+1)   (descent),

    and on the left (σ_mul_stdElt) the same rule with s_i w and the test w⁻¹(i) < w⁻¹(i+1).

    This is the defining multiplication rule of the Iwahori–Hecke algebra in the T_w basis.

    Proof #

    The ascent case is HeckeMatsumoto.stdElt_mul_σ_of_ascent together with ninv_mul_adjT_of_lt. At a descent, v := w s_i has v(i) < v(i+1), so T_w = T_v σ_i by the ascent case, and the quadratic relation σ_i² = (q − qi) σ_i + q·qi (Hecke.σ_quadratic) gives T_w σ_i = T_v σ_i² = (q − qi) T_w + q·qi · T_v.

    On the left, Matsumoto does the work: if s_i w is longer than w, then i followed by the word of w is a reduced word for s_i w, so σ_i T_w = T_{s_i w} (prod_σ_of_reduced). The length test is moved to w⁻¹ by ninv_inv.

    Base ring (§7c) #

    Generic [CommRing K], with q, qi : K free. Nothing archimedean.
