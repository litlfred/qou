    QOU.SchurWeyl.HeckeWedderburn3 — the Wedderburn iso H_3(q) ≅ ℝ × ℝ × M₂(ℝ) (SW-7, R2-inst at n=3) #

    The three irreducible representations of H_3(q) = Hecke ℝ q q⁻¹ 2 (q ≠ 0) — the trivial rhoTriv, the sign rhoSign, and the (2,1) seminormal seminormalRep21 — assemble into a single algebra map

    ρ = (rhoTriv, rhoSign, seminormalRep21) : H_3(q) →ₐ[ℝ] ℝ × ℝ × M₂(ℝ).

    • injective (rho_injective): ρ x = 0 gives the three representation images of x are zero; expanding x in the six-word basis reduces to the component core sixCoeffs_zero, so every coordinate — hence x — vanishes;
    • bijective (rho_bijective): both sides have finrank = 6 (finrank_hecke3; 1 + 1 + 2·2 on the right), so an injective ℝ-linear map between them is surjective.

    Hence wedderburn3 : H_3(q) ≃ₐ[ℝ] ℝ × ℝ × M₂(ℝ) — the Artin–Wedderburn decomposition of the semisimple algebra H_3(q) at n = 3, matching 1² + 1² + 2² = 6 = 3!. This is R2-inst at n = 3.

    Base ring ℝ (the seminormal rep is archimedean, §7c).
