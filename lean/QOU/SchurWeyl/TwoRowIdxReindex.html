    Reindexing the at-most-two-row partitions by second-row length #

    WtraceRadicalSemisimple.finrank_traceQuot sums over TwoRowIdx n, the at-most-two-row partitions of n + 1. QOU.Mathlib.TwoRowDimCatalan.dimHLF_two_row_sq_sum sums over Finset.range (n / 2 + 1), indexed by the second-row length. This file supplies the bijection between the two index sets, so the two sums can be compared.

    Why this is worth its own file #

    It is bean qou-7vht item 2, and its point is subtractive: with it, the only thing standing between finrank_traceQuot and catalan (n+1) is the hook length formula at general n — absent from Mathlib, and proved in this corpus only for n ≤ 4 (shapesLE4, by decide) plus n = 5. Before it there were two gaps and it was easy to mistake the bounded one for the hard one; after it there is one, and it is named.

    Nothing here closes that gap #

    🛑 The bijection is finite combinatorics and says nothing about tableau counts. It does not prove finrank_traceQuot = catalan (n+1), and the sum it produces is still over Fintype.card (StandardYoungTableau …), not over dimHLF.

    -- Ref: [manuscript] beans qou-7vht, qou-7wf6, qou-8iru.
