    QOU.SchurWeyl.MolienSymmetrisingTrace — the Molien weight is the symmetrising trace #

    The Molien weight of a Young diagram μ, with Q = q², is

    M_μ(Q) = Q^{n(μ)} · ∏_{x ∈ μ} (1 − Q) / (1 − Q^{h(x)}),

    n(μ) = Σ_{(i,j) ∈ μ} i and h(x) the hook length. In the balanced variable it is q^{−κ(μ)} / ∏_x [h(x)]_q (κ = content sum) — proved in MolienBalancedForm.molienWeight_eq_balanced, and f^μ · d_q(μ) · M_μ-type products of it are the Féray–Méliot q-Plancherel measure. This file proves, in Lean and without citing the literature, that

    the seminormal trace weighted by M is a Markov trace with parameter z = 0, and hence is the symmetrising trace τ(T_w) = δ_{w,1}.

    Main results #

    • ocneanuWeight_zero_eq_molienWeight — M is the Ocneanu weight at z = 0 (generic field).
    • sum_dim_molienWeight_eq_one, sum_addable_molienWeight — normalisation and branching (generic field, Q not a root of unity).
    • SeminormalRep.markov_molienWeight — the Markov corner identity at z = 0.
    • SeminormalRep.molienMarkovTrace : MarkovTraceFamily K q q⁻¹ 0, over any field K.
    • SeminormalRep.molienMarkovTrace_symmetrising — on the coset basis it is 1 at the identity and 0 elsewhere; SeminormalRep.eq_molien_of_zero — every z = 0 Markov trace equals it.

    Method: interpolation from the Wenzl levels #

    Put W_μ(a) = Q^{n(μ)} ∏_x (1 − Q)(1 − a·Q^{c(x)}) / (1 − Q^{h(x)}), a polynomial in a (wenzlPoly). Then

    • W_μ(Q^r) = (1 − Q^r)^{|μ|} · wenzlWeight r Q μ — the level-r Wenzl weight, rescaled;
    • W_μ(0) = M_μ.

    Each of the three weight identities the level-r chain proves (sum_dim_wenzlWeight_eq_one, sum_addable_wenzlWeight_all, markov_wenzlWeight) becomes, after clearing the (1 − Q^r) powers, an identity between two polynomials in a that holds at every a = Q^r, r ≥ 1. Those are infinitely many distinct points when Q is not a root of unity, so the polynomials are equal, and evaluating at a = 0 gives the identity for M. The Markov parameter z_r = Q^r(1 − Q)/(q(1 − Q^r)) becomes the polynomial a(1 − Q)/q and vanishes at a = 0.

    This is the r → "∞ with a = Q^r → 0" degeneration of Wenzl's family, made exact. It avoids the general-z Ocneanu corner identity, which is not proved in the corpus.

    Base ring (§7c) #

    Generic [Field F] throughout; the seminormal representations, and hence the trace, are built over any field with q ≠ 0 not a root of unity. No ordering, no ℝ.
