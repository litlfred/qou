    QOU.SchurWeyl — Burnside spanning for the (2,1) seminormal rep (SW-6, concrete) #

    For q ≠ 0 the four matrices 1, S0, S1, S0·S1 admit no nontrivial linear relation over ℝ (seminormal21_no_linear_relation) — i.e. they are linearly independent. Since dim_ℝ M₂(ℝ) = 4, they therefore span M₂(ℝ), so the ℝ-subalgebra generated by the generator images S0, S1 is the whole matrix algebra: the Burnside surjectivity of the (2,1) seminormal representation, obtained by an explicit entry computation rather than a general density theorem.

    This is the concrete d_λ = 2 half of SW-6 (bean qou-jfr8/qou-j377): the surjective side of the Wedderburn block map for the (2,1) factor. The full H_3(q) ≅ ℝ × ℝ × M₂(ℝ) isomorphism additionally needs dim H_3(q) = 6 (the Iwahori–Hecke basis theorem {T_w : w ∈ S_3}), which is not proved here.

    Base ring ℝ (§7c). LaTeX label: prop:seminormal21-spans-M2.
