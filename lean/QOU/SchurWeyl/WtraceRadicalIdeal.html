    The trace radical IS the kernel of the two-row projection #

    WtraceRadicalTwoRowSubset.mem_rad_iff_twoRow_blocks_vanish identifies the radical of wtrace pointwise: a given x is invisible to the Ocneanu trace form exactly when every at-most-two-row block kills it. That is a statement about elements. This file packages it as a statement about objects — an equality of two-sided ideals,

    traceRadicalIdeal (wtrace …) = TwoSidedIdeal.ker (twoRowProduct …),

    together with surjectivity of twoRowProduct.

    Why the packaging is not bookkeeping #

    The phrase "H_n(q)/\mathrm{rad} is the Temperley–Lieb algebra" needs rad to be an ideal and needs a map whose kernel it is. A pointwise iff over x supplies neither: mem_rad_iff_twoRow_blocks_vanish characterises membership in a Set, and says nothing about that set being closed under anything. The closure properties live in QBeta.MarkovTraceQuotient (three additive, two multiplicative, cyclicity spent in exactly one) and are what traceRadicalIdeal is assembled from; what was missing is the identification of that ideal with a kernel.

    Together, traceRadicalIdeal_eq_ker and twoRowProduct_surjective are exactly the data of the first isomorphism theorem for this pair.

    🛑 What this file does NOT claim #

    It does not claim H_n(q)/\mathrm{rad} \cong \prod_{\ell(\lambda)\le 2} M_{f^\lambda}. The two theorems here are the input to that isomorphism, not the isomorphism. Producing the ≃ₐ needs a lift out of TwoSidedIdeal.ringCon.Quotient, and the pinned Mathlib has no first-isomorphism lemma at that generality: Mathlib/RingTheory/Congruence/ and Mathlib/RingTheory/TwoSidedIdeal/ contain ker, mem_ker and ker_eq_bot, and no lift or quotientKerEquivOfSurjective. So the remaining step is a construction, and it is not done here. Three artifacts record the isomorphism as open — mass-theory/hecke-radical-is-temperley-lieb.md, MathlibExt/HeckeToTemperleyLieb.lean's §"What this file does NOT claim", and bean qou-0cyu item G3 — and they stay correct.

    Nor does it re-prove the dimension count. Mathlib/TwoRowDimCatalan.dimHLF_two_row_sq_sum already gives ∑_{ℓ(λ)≤2} (f^λ)² = catalan n, sorry-free and for every n. That is a statement about the target of twoRowProduct; nothing here connects it to the finrank of a quotient type, because that connection is the missing isomorphism.

    Scope #

    Convention D (z = q³/(q²+1)) throughout — the vanishing that makes the two-row part the whole story is ocneanuWeight_conventionD_eq_zero_of_three_rows. Base ring ℝ, inherited from wtrace; heckeProduct itself is generic over [Field K] [CharZero K], so the pin enters only through the trace. Genericity hypotheses are not uniform across the inputs: hgen : ∀ k, 0 < k → (q²)^k ≠ 1 is strictly stronger than hq2 : NotRootOfUnity q as used, and is needed for the ⊆ half, so the ideal identity inherits it.

    The at-most-two-row partitions of n+1. "At most two rows" is spelled rowLen 2 = 0 — row index 2 is empty — matching the hypothesis shape of mem_rad_iff_twoRow_blocks_vanish. Note the index shift: Hecke ℝ q q⁻¹ n is the algebra whose blocks are partitions of n + 1.
