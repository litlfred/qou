    QOU.SchurWeyl.HeckeInclusion — the tower inclusion H_n(q) ↪ H_{n+1}(q) (SW-7-gen infra) #

    The Iwahori–Hecke algebras form a tower H_1(q) ⊂ H_2(q) ⊂ ⋯, with H_n(q) sitting inside H_{n+1}(q) as the subalgebra generated by the first n-1 generators. Here H_n(q) = Hecke K q qi n (n generators σ_0,…,σ_{n-1}), so the inclusion Hecke K q qi n →ₐ[K] Hecke K q qi (n+1) sends σ_i ↦ σ_i (i : Fin n ↪ Fin (n+1) by Fin.castSucc).

    This is the structural map underlying the coset-decomposition proof of the Iwahori–Hecke basis theorem dim H_n(q) = n! (the general dim ≤ n! route, bean qou-uoal): H_{n+1}(q) = ⊕_{k} H_n(q)·r_k over the n+1 coset representatives r_k = σ_n σ_{n-1} ⋯ σ_k, giving the recursion dim H_{n+1} ≤ (n+1)·dim H_n.

    The images σ_i (i : Fin n, embedded) satisfy the same quadratic, braid, and far-commutation relations they satisfy in H_{n+1}(q), so the inclusion is a well-defined algebra map by the universal property (Hecke.lift).

    Base ring: generic [CommRing K] (§7c — purely algebraic).
