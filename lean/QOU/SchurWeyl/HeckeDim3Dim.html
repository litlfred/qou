    QOU.SchurWeyl.HeckeDim3Dim — finrank_ℝ H_3(q) = 6 (SW-7 capstone, dimension) #

    Packages the two bounds of the Iwahori–Hecke basis theorem at n = 3 into the single dimension statement. The six reduced words {1, σ₀, σ₁, σ₀σ₁, σ₁σ₀, σ₀σ₁σ₀} form a basis of H_3(q) = Hecke ℝ q q⁻¹ 2 for q ≠ 0:

    • span ⊤ — HeckeDim3.span_sixWords_eq_top (dim ≤ 6, on main),
    • linear independence — HeckeDim3Indep.sixWords_indep (dim ≥ 6, on main),

    so Module.finrank ℝ (H_3(q)) = 6 = 3!. This is the dimension half of R2-inst at n = 3; the matching Wedderburn isomorphism H_3(q) ≅ ℝ × ℝ × M₂(ℝ) is assembled separately from the same three irreps.

    Base ring ℝ (the seminormal rep used for independence is archimedean, §7c).
