    QOU.SchurWeyl.HeckeCarrierSemisimple — the Iwahori carrier is semisimple over any field #

    Bean qou-j377, the last ℝ on the semisimplicity arc.

    QOU/SchurWeyl/HeckeSemisimpleGeneral.lean proves IsSemisimpleRing (Hecke K q q⁻¹ n) over any [Field K] [CharZero K] with q not a root of unity. skeinIwahoriEquiv carries that to QOU.Hecke.IwahoriHecke — the T_w / twGram carrier — and is itself already generic. So this file is one transport.

    The statement is parameterised by the square root, and that is not a defect #

    skeinIwahoriEquiv relates the skein algebra at q to the Iwahori algebra at q ^ 2. The ℝ form (HeckeCarrierSemisimpleReal.lean) takes the Iwahori parameter Q directly and produces its own q as Real.sqrt Q, which is available because every Q > 1 in ℝ is a square. A general field affords no such step — ℚ does not contain √2 — so the honest general statement takes the square root as the parameter and reads "the Iwahori algebra at a square is semisimple".

    That is a genuine mathematical narrowing, not a limitation of the proof, and it is the whole residue of the ℝ that the general-field tranche left on this arc: the ℝ was never carrying algebra here, only the existence of a square root. Where a caller does have a square root — over ℝ, or over any field with Q a square — nothing is lost.

    QOU.Hecke.IwahoriHecke K (q ^ 2) n is semisimple, over any field of characteristic zero with q not a root of unity, at every rank.

    The T_w / twGram carrier, general-field. Transported from isSemisimpleRing_hecke_generic along skeinIwahoriEquiv.

    Stated at q ^ 2 rather than at a general Iwahori parameter Q because naming a q with q ^ 2 = Q is exactly what a general field cannot do; see the module docstring.
