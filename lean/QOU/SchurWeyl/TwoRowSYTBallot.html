    The two-row standard-tableau count is the ballot number #

    Bean qou-7vht item 1, on its narrowed target. The goal is

    numStandardYoungTableaux (twoRow m k) = ballot (m + k) k

    i.e. the two-row case of the hook length formula, not the general theorem.

    Why the two-row case suffices, and why it is much smaller #

    QOU.Mathlib.TwoRowDimCatalan.dimHLF_two_row_eq_ballot already identifies the hook-length-formula value dimHLF [n-k, k] with ballot n k. So identifying the genuine tableau count with the same ballot number closes the gap between WtraceRadicalSemisimple.finrank_traceQuot (a count) and dimHLF_two_row_sq_sum (formula values) — without proving Frame–Robinson–Thrall in general, which is absent from Mathlib and proved in this corpus only for n ≤ 4 (by decide) plus n = 5.

    The route: a recursion, not a bijection #

    The textbook argument is a bijection with ballot sequences. That is not the cheap route in Lean, because the corpus already has the branching rule as a Finset sum (QOU.Mathlib.YoungLatticeUpOperator.branching_finset) and ballot already has the matching recursion (ballot_succ_succ). So the whole content is:

    1. evaluate cornerCells (twoRow m k) — two cells, one cell, or none;
    2. identify the two corner-erased shapes as twoRow (m-1) k and twoRow m (k-1);
    3. induct, with ballot_succ_succ on the generic step.

    The k = m boundary is where it could have gone wrong and does not: there (0, m-1) is not a corner (the cell below it is present), so only one term survives — and on the ballot side the term that must vanish is ballot (2j+1) (j+1), which is exactly ballot_boundary_zero, already proved for a different purpose. Bean qou-7wf6 noted that one lemma resolves both; this is the second use.

      Successor forms #

      Everything below avoids m - 1 and k - 1. Truncated subtraction is the whole difficulty here: rw needs a syntactic match, Lean normalises n + 1 - 1 to n in the goal but not inside a lemma instance, and the k ≤ m proof carried by twoRow makes the motive ill-typed when the index is rewritten. Restating in successor form removes all three at once.

      The identification #

      source
      theorem QOU.SchurWeyl.TwoRowSYTBallot.numSYT_twoRow_eq_ballot (m k : ℕ) (h : k ≤ m) :
      ↑(Mathlib.numStandardYoungTableaux (twoRow m k h)) = Mathlib.ballot (m + k) k

      The two-row standard-tableau count IS the ballot number, for every k ≤ m.

      #SYT (m, k) = ballot (m + k) k

      Outer induction on k, inner induction on m from k upward (Nat.le_induction): the k < m step needs the result at the same k with a smaller m, which an induction on k alone cannot supply.

      The two ballot facts consumed are exactly the two the corpus already had: ballot_succ_succ for the generic step, and ballot_boundary_zero for the k = m boundary, where the term that must vanish is ballot (2j+1) (j+1).
