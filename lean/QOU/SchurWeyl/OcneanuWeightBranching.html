    QOU.SchurWeyl.OcneanuWeightBranching — the branching identity at convention D #

    The Markov trace on the tower H_1 ⊂ H_2 ⊂ ⋯ is consistent iff its weights satisfy

    w_μ^{(n)} = ∑_{λ = μ + □} w_λ^{(n+1)},

    the sum running over the addable corners of μ. This file proves that identity for ocneanuWeight at convention D's Markov parameter z = q³/(q²+1).

    Why convention D makes this tractable #

    At convention D every diagram with three or more rows carries weight zero (ocneanuWeight_conventionD_eq_zero_iff). So the whole trace lives on the diagrams of at most two rows, twoRow m k with k ≤ m, and such a diagram has at most three addable corners — (0,m), (1,k), and (2,0) — of which the third contributes zero. The branching identity is therefore a two-term identity, not a sum over all addable corners of an arbitrary diagram.

    Via ocneanuWeight_conventionD_eq_schurPrincipal it reduces further. The two-row weight has the closed form

    schurPrincipal 2 Q (twoRow m k) = Q^k · (1 + Q + ⋯ + Q^{m−k})

    (schurPrincipal_twoRow) — the hook-content product telescopes completely — and branching becomes the single arithmetic identity

    (1 + Q + ⋯ + Q^d)(1 + Q) = (1 + Q + ⋯ + Q^{d+1}) + Q(1 + Q + ⋯ + Q^{d−1})

    (qBracket_pieri), which is the Pieri rule s_μ · e₁ = ∑_{λ = μ+□} s_λ in two variables. The d = 0 case needs no special handling in the arithmetic — the empty sum kills the last term — but it does at the level of diagrams, since twoRow m (m+1) is not a partition; hence the separate _diag statements.

    What this is and is not #

    It is the consistency of the trace tower at convention D: the weight family is coherent under restriction H_{n+1} → H_n. It is not the Markov condition tr(x·σ_n) = z·tr(x), which remains open — see OcneanuWeight's docstring and bean qou-uer7. Consistency is one of the two halves; it does not imply the other.

    Base ring (§7c) #

    Generic [Field R] throughout; the combinatorial lemmas are pure ℕ. No ℝ, no ordering.
