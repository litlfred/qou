    QOU.SchurWeyl.SeminormalCornerTrace — the block trace as a sum over corners #

    SeminormalBlockTrace reduced tr(ρ_μ(ι y) · σ_{|μ|-1}) to a diagonal sum over SYT μ:

    ∑_T ρ_μ(ι y)_{TT} · a(axialDist T (|μ|-1)).

    SeminormalBlockEquality then showed that on the block of a corner c that matrix is the downstairs matrix ρ_{μ ∖ c}(y). This file puts the two together and splits the T-sum by the cell carrying the top entry, which by SYTBranchingRule is exactly a sum over the removable corners of μ:

    tr(ρ_μ(ι y) · σ_{|μ|-1}) = ∑_{c corner of μ} ∑_{S : SYT (μ ∖ c)} ρ_{μ∖c}(y)_{SS} · a(c(c) − c(r_S))

    where r_S is the cell of S carrying its top entry — a removable corner of μ ∖ c.

    That right-hand side is the shape the Markov condition is stated in: the inner factor depends on the downstairs tableau only through r_S, so summing over μ with weights and reindexing (YoungDiagramCornerReindex) turns the whole thing into OcneanuWeightMarkovCorner's per-corner identity ∑_{s addable} w_{μ+s}·a(c(s) − c(r)) = z·w_μ.
    branchEquiv Σ_{c corner} SYT (μ ∖ c) ≃ SYT μ, from branchInv's two bijectivity halves
    axialDist_insertMax_top the last axial distance of a re-inserted tableau is c(c) − c(r_S)
    trace_heckeIncl_mul_lastGen_corner the decomposition itself
