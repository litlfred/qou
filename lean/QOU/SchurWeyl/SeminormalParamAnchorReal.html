    QOU.SchurWeyl.SeminormalParamAnchorReal — the S1 anchor, at ℝ #

    Split out of QOU/SchurWeyl/SeminormalParam.lean (bean qou-j377).

    That file's five other results are now generic over [Field K]. This one cannot be: it identifies the hand-built (2,1) matrix S1 from HoefsmitRep.lean, which is ℝ-valued, with seminormalBlock at the predicted parameter — and its proof of 1 - q ^ 4 ≠ 0 runs through positivity on q ^ 2 + 1, an order step. §7c therefore puts it in its own file.

    It is off the general-field path, which is why generalising it is not on the critical list: scripts/lean-general-field-tranche-scope.py reports HoefsmitRep's twelve ℝ-carrying declarations as unreachable from heckeWedderburn, reaching the chain only through QJucysMurphy.qJM_seminormalRep21_one, a rank-2 sanity check with zero consumers. The anchor is a genuine cross-check of the axial-distance formula against a hand-built matrix and is kept as such; it is simply not load-bearing for the Wedderburn theorem.

    Were it wanted over a general field, the order step is not the obstacle it looks like: GenericParameter.sq_add_one_ne_zero supplies q ^ 2 + 1 ≠ 0 from NotRootOfUnity, and under that hypothesis the 1 - q ^ 4 ≠ 0 case split disappears rather than being replaced.

    theorem QOU.SchurWeyl.Seminormal.S1_eq_seminormalParam (q : ℝ) (hq : q ≠ 0) (hq2 : q ^ 2 ≠ 1) :
    S1 q = seminormalBlock q (seminormalParam q (-2))

    Anchor. The hand-built (2,1) matrix S1 — constructed in HoefsmitRep.lean to satisfy the braid relation with S0, independently of any axial-distance formula — is the generic block at the parameter this file predicts. In [[1,2],[3]] the entry 2 has content 1 and 3 has content -1, so σ_1 acts with axial distance -2.

    The hypothesis q² ≠ 1 is necessary, not cosmetic: at q = ±1 the numerator q - q⁻¹ and the denominator 1 - q⁴ both vanish, so seminormalParam q (-2) evaluates to 0 (Lean's 0 / 0), while S1's (0,0) entry is -1 / (2q) ≠ 0. That is the Hecke algebra degenerating to the group algebra, where the seminormal form is not given by this formula.
