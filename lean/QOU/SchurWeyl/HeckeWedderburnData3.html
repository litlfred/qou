    QOU.SchurWeyl.HeckeWedderburnData3 — the HeckeWedderburnData instance at n = 3 #

    (bean qou-tpl5, part B)

    QSchurWeyl.lean states the Wedderburn form of the Markov trace against an abstract bundle HeckeWedderburnData K Part H: block dimensions d_λ, Markov weights w_λ, a Wedderburn isomorphism H ≃ₐ ∏_λ M_{d_λ}(K), a trace functional, and the identity tr_M = ∑_λ w_λ χ_λ. Every result there (markov_one_eq, markov_add, markov_smul) is therefore conditional on such a bundle existing. This file discharges that condition at n = 3, so those results become unconditional statements about H_3(q).

    What is delivered #

    • heckeDim3 = ![1, 1, 2] — the block dimensions of H_3(q), one per partition of 3 ((3), (1,1,1), (2,1)), satisfying 1² + 1² + 2² = 6 = 3!.
    • piReshape3 — the algebra isomorphism ℝ × ℝ × M₂(ℝ) ≃ₐ[ℝ] ∏ l : Fin 3, M_{d_l}(ℝ), i.e. the reshape of the binary-product form produced by wedderburn3 into the indexed-product form the bundle requires. The two 1-blocks use M₁(ℝ) ≅ ℝ.
    • heckeWedderburnData3 — the bundle itself, assembled from wedderburn3 (SW-7), the canonical Ocneanu/HOMFLY weights markovWeight0/1/2 (SW-8 part A) and markovTrace3. Its markov_eq field is proved, not assumed: the weighted-character definition of markovTrace3 is shown to agree termwise with the block traces of the reshaped isomorphism.
    • markov_one_eq₃ — the specialisation of markov_one_eq, recorded to demonstrate that the abstract results now apply concretely.

    Base ring: ℝ throughout, as HeckeWedderburnData requires a Field and the n = 3 Wedderburn iso (wedderburn3) is stated over ℝ. The archimedean specialisation is inherited, not introduced here (§7c).

    Refs:

    • [jones1987] Jones, Hecke algebra representations of braid groups and link polynomials, https://doi.org/10.2307/1971403
    • [wenzl1988] Wenzl, Hecke algebras of type A_n and subfactors, https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01393520

              The bundle #

              source
              noncomputable def QOU.SchurWeyl.WedderburnData3.heckeWedderburnData3 (q : ℝ) (hq : q ≠ 0) (z : ℝ) :
              HeckeWedderburnData ℝ (Fin 3) (Hecke ℝ q q⁻¹ 2)

              The HeckeWedderburnData bundle for H_3(q) (q ≠ 0, Ocneanu parameter z). This discharges the hypothesis of every conditional result in QSchurWeyl.lean at n = 3.

              The markov_eq field is the substance: markovTrace3 is defined as the weighted character sum w₀·χ_triv + w₁·χ_sign + w₂·χ_{(2,1)}, and the bundle demands ∑_λ w_λ · tr((iso h) λ). These agree because the trace of a 1 × 1 block is its single entry, so the two scalar blocks contribute their characters directly, while the (2,1) block contributes its matrix trace.
