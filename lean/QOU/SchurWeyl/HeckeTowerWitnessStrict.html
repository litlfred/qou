    The presented tower's filtration grows strictly — the twH bridge #

    MathlibExt/HeckeTowerWitness presents the infinite Hecke tower by generators and relations and proves twH_zero_lt_one (H 0 < H 1) only. Its own module docstring warns not to read twH_mono as strict growth: it is ≤. This file closes that gap at the Hecke parameter, for every level.

    Why the rank-2 collapse cannot do it, and why that is not a small obstacle #

    collapse : HeckeTower R h →ₐ Hecke2 R h sends every generator to the same sigma, so it cannot separate twH n from twH (n+1). The tempting repair — map into a product of rank-2 algebras and send one generator somewhere else — fails for a structural reason worth recording. In a commutative target the braid relation

    f(m+1) * f(m) * f(m+1) = f(m) * f(m+1) * f(m)

    becomes f(m+1)² f(m) = f(m)² f(m+1); substituting the quadratic on each side gives h·f(m)f(m+1) + f(m) versus h·f(m)f(m+1) + f(m+1), i.e. f(m) = f(m+1). So the braid relation forces adjacent generators to be equal in any commutative target, hence all of them by chaining. No commutative algebra — of any rank, product or not — can separate two stages of this filtration.

    The target that works #

    The separator has to be genuinely non-commutative, and the corpus has one: the colimit HeckeLim ℝ q q⁻¹ of the honest Iwahori–Hecke tower. All three relations hold there with matching indices — gLim_quadratic (with q · q⁻¹ = 1), gLim_braid, gLim_far — so the presented tower maps into it generator-for-generator, and the separation is inherited from HeckeColimit.H_lt_succ.

    The two new facts #

    • H_le_adjoin_gLim — level n+1 of the colimit filtration is generated by the colimit generators g 0 … g n. This is adjoin_range_σ_eq_top transported along gLim_eq; it was implicit in HeckeColimitNotFaithful and is extracted here because it is what turns a dimension count into a statement about a generator.
    • gLim_notMem_H — g n ∉ H n. H_lt_succ says the inclusion H n ⊆ H (n+1) is strict but names no witness; with the generation fact it does: if g n were already in H n, then H (n+1), being generated by g 0 … g n, would collapse into H n.

    Scope #

    ℝ, convention D's genericity (q ≠ 0, NotRootOfUnity q) — inherited from finrank_hecke_eq_factorial, which needs semisimplicity — and the Hecke parameter h = q - q⁻¹. The generic-R, generic-h case is not settled here and the commutativity argument above says why it is not merely unproved: any separator for it must be non-commutative, and HeckeTowerWitness has no non-commutative model at generic h.

    Why no commutative target can separate two stages #

    The obstruction above is stated in the module docstring; this makes it machine-checked rather than prose.

    a = b

    In a commutative ring the braid relation forces adjacent generators to be equal.

    b·a·b = a·b·a becomes a·b² = b·a²; substituting the quadratic on each side turns it into h·a·b + a = h·a·b + b. So any commutative target of the presented tower identifies σ_m with σ_{m+1}, hence all generators by chaining, hence maps every stage of the filtration onto the same subalgebra. No commutative algebra separates twH n from twH (n+1) — of any rank, product or not. This is why collapse into the rank-2 Hecke2 cannot be repaired by enlarging the target, and why the separator below is the non-commutative colimit.
