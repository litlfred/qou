    QOU.SchurWeyl.TwoRowCorners — the corners of a ≤2-row diagram, and what adding one does #

    MarkovAssembly reduces the Markov condition to a per-corner identity quantified over every diagram ν and every removable corner of it. OcneanuWeightMarkovCorner proves that identity at convention D in the form the arithmetic wants — as a two-term statement about twoRow. Between the two sits pure combinatorics: which cells are addable, which are removable, and what diagram adding one produces. This file is that layer.
    isAddableCell_twoRow_iff addable ⟺ (0,m), or (1,k) with k < m, or (2,0) with 0 < k
    isCornerCell_twoRow_iff removable ⟺ (0,m−1) with k < m, or (1,k−1) with 0 < k
    addableTwoRow, sum_addable_twoRow, sum_addableTwoRow_expand the addable-cell sum, as at most three explicit terms
    addCell_twoRow_row0, addCell_twoRow_row1 adding at the end of a row gives the next twoRow
    rowLen_two_addCell_row2 adding (2,0) gives a 3-row diagram — hence weight zero at convention D

    Why the third addable cell does not need arithmetic #

    A ≤2-row diagram has up to three addable cells, but OcneanuWeightMarkovCorner is a two-term identity. The gap is closed not by an arithmetic cancellation but by the convention-D vanishing: (2,0) produces a 3-row diagram, whose Ocneanu weight is 0. rowLen_two_addCell_row2 is the combinatorial half of that argument.

    Base ring (§7c) #

    None — pure ℕ × ℕ combinatorics. Nothing archimedean, no ring.
