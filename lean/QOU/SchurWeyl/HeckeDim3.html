    QOU.SchurWeyl.HeckeDim3 — H_3(q) is spanned by the six T_w words (SW-7 infra) #

    H_3(q) = Hecke K q qi 2 (two generators σ₀, σ₁) is spanned as a K-module by the six reduced words

    1, σ₀, σ₁, σ₀σ₁, σ₁σ₀, σ₀σ₁σ₀ (= {T_w : w ∈ S_3}),

    so dim_K H_3(q) ≤ 6. This is the spanning half of the Iwahori–Hecke basis theorem dim H_n(q) = n! at n = 3 — the missing gate to the Wedderburn isomorphism (SW-7) and hence to R2-inst. The matching lower bound dim ≥ 6 (linear independence, via the three irreducible representations) is a separate result.

    The proof: the K-span S of the six words contains 1 and is closed under left multiplication by each generator (12 explicit reductions using only the quadratic relation σ_i² = (q-qi)σ_i + (q·qi)·1 and the braid relation σ₀σ₁σ₀ = σ₁σ₀σ₁), so S contains the submonoid generated by {σ₀,σ₁}; since that submonoid's span is adjoin{σ₀,σ₁} = ⊤, S = ⊤.

    Base ring: generic [CommRing K] (§7c — purely algebraic; no archimedean input).
