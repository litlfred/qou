    The Hecke tower as a directed system over ℕ #

    Bean qou-0fmb, step 1 of the colimit bridge HeckeCoset.MarkovTraceFamily → OcneanuTrace.MarkovTrace.

    SchurWeyl.heckeIncl gives one step, H_n → H_{n+1}. A direct limit needs the whole family f i j : i ≤ j → (H_i →ₐ H_j) together with the two DirectedSystem laws. This file builds it by Nat.leRec and nothing else.

    What is here #

    • heckeInclLE i j h — the iterated inclusion, AlgHom.id at h : i ≤ i and heckeIncl ∘ … one step up.
    • heckeInclLE_self, heckeInclLE_succ — the two computation rules.
    • heckeInclLE_trans — composition, by induction on the upper bound.
    • the DirectedSystem instance.

    What is NOT here #

    No colimit, no trace, no MarkovTrace. In particular nothing in this file needs heckeIncl to be injective — that is qou-ejde, and it gates strict growth of the filtration (qou-p8yg), not the existence of the limit.

    -- Ref: [manuscript] beans qou-0fmb / qou-ejde / qou-301d.
