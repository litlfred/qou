    QOU.SchurWeyl.HeckeCarrierSemisimpleReal — the ℝ specialisation of the carrier equivalence #

    Beans qou-img9, qou-kwoa.

    QOU/SchurWeyl/HeckeCarrierEquiv.lean is generic: carrierEquiv over a CommRing, skeinIwahoriEquiv over any Field. Its payoff is not, and that is why it is here rather than there.

    skeinIwahoriEquiv relates the skein algebra at q to the Iwahori algebra at q². Consumers of twGram hold the Iwahori parameter Q, so using it means naming a q with q² = Q — i.e. Real.sqrt, an archimedean construct. Per §7c the specialisation lives in a separate file and consumes the generic result; splitting it out is what keeps HeckeCarrierEquiv free of ℝ.

    The ℝ here is not an extra restriction on top of heckeWedderburn's: that theorem is already ℝ-only, for the reason measured in QOU/SchurWeyl/GenericParameter.lean.
