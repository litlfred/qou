    QOU.SchurWeyl.HeckeDim3Indep — the six T_w words are linearly independent (SW-7, dim ≥ 6) #

    Lower bound dim_ℝ H_3(q) ≥ 6: the six words {1,σ₀,σ₁,σ₀σ₁,σ₁σ₀,σ₀σ₁σ₀} are linearly independent over ℝ (q ≠ 0), proved by applying the three irreducible representations rhoTriv, rhoSign, seminormalRep21 to a linear relation and solving the resulting 6×6 system. Combined with HeckeDim3.span_sixWords_eq_top (dim ≤ 6) this gives dim H_3(q) = 6 and, since the three reps then jointly biject, the Wedderburn isomorphism H_3(q) ≅ ℝ × ℝ × M₂(ℝ) (R2-inst at n=3).

    The solving is factored into a component core sixCoeffs_zero, whose hypotheses are the three representation images of Σ cᵢ wᵢ being zero. This is exactly what is available both from a genuine linear relation Σ cᵢ wᵢ = 0 (⇒ sixWords_indep) and from ρ (Σ cᵢ wᵢ) = 0 for the product map ρ = (rhoTriv, rhoSign, seminormalRep21) (⇒ injectivity of ρ, used for the Wedderburn iso). Factoring avoids re-deriving the 6×6 solve.

    Base ring ℝ (seminormal rep is archimedean, §7c).

    Component core. Given that the three representations rhoTriv, rhoSign, seminormalRep21 each send c₁·1 + c₂σ₀ + c₃σ₁ + c₄σ₀σ₁ + c₅σ₁σ₀ + c₆σ₀σ₁σ₀ to zero, all six coefficients vanish (q ≠ 0). This is the 6×6 solve; both the linear-independence statement and the injectivity of the product representation reduce to it.
