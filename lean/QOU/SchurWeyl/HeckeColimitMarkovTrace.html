    The Ocneanu trace on the colimit: closing the bridge #

    Steps 4–6 of bean qou-0fmb. Steps 1–3 built the colimit HeckeLim, its K-algebra structure, the range filtration H, and the generators — and showed the three braid relations hold there. What was missing was the trace, and with it the four remaining OcneanuTrace.MarkovTrace fields.

    What is proved here #

    • wtrace_inclLE — the level-wise trace is compatible with the iterated inclusion, not merely with one step. DirectLimit.Module.lift needs the former; MarkovTraceInstance.wtrace_incl supplies the latter, and this is its Nat.le_induction iterate.
    • trLim — the Ocneanu trace on the colimit, as a linear map HeckeLim →ₗ[ℝ] ℝ. It is DirectLimit.Module.lift, not Ring.lift: wtrace is linear and is not a ring map (it is not multiplicative), so the ring lift does not apply and the module lift is what the MarkovTrace.tr field wants anyway.
    • trLim_cyclic, trLim_one, trLim_markov, trLim_markov_inv — the four trace axioms, each obtained by DirectLimit.induction down to a single level where the corresponding wtrace lemma already holds.
    • heckeColimitMarkovTrace — the MarkovTrace ℝ (HeckeLim ℝ q q⁻¹) instance. This is the bridge: HeckeCoset.MarkovTraceFamily, which is indexed by level, now has a counterpart on a single algebra, which is the interface every MathlibExt tower result is stated against.

    The two Markov parameters #

    z = q³/(q²+1) is convention D, canonical per the owner ruling of 2026-08-15, and it is inherited from wtrace_markov, not chosen here.

    zinv is derived, not posited: σ⁻¹ = σ - (q - q⁻¹)·1 by definition, so tr (a σ⁻¹) = tr (a σ) - (q - q⁻¹) tr a = (z - (q - q⁻¹)) tr a, giving zinv = q³/(q²+1) - (q - q⁻¹). This is the markovParam_inv_eq_sub shape of OcneanuTrace realised concretely, and nothing about it is fitted.

    Hypotheses, and what they are #

    The construction carries exactly the hypotheses that MarkovTraceInstance's wtrace already carries — hq0 : q ≠ 0, hq2 : NotRootOfUnity q, hq21 : q² + 1 ≠ 0, hgen : ∀ i > 0, (q²)^i ≠ 1 — and adds none. They are genericity conditions on the parameter under which the seminormal form exists; they are not assumptions about the trace.

    Note qi = q⁻¹ throughout, so the q * qi = 1 hypothesis of step 3's gLim_mul_ginvLim is discharged by hq0 and never surfaces.

    The payoff #

    braidedTower_heckeColimit records that the colimit satisfies the three MathlibExt.BraidedTower fields — the relations of step 3, unchanged. That instance is what carries towerSpan_heckeColimit and disjointBlock_heckeColimit: TowerSpan (H_{n+1} = H_n + H_n g_n H_n) and the disjoint-block trace law now hold for the genuine Iwahori–Hecke tower, not only for the free HeckeTower carrier they were first witnessed on.

    What is NOT proved here #

    No faithfulness. H n remains the image of level n, and whether ofLim n is injective is bean qou-ejde, assumed nowhere. Nor is trLim claimed faithful — the HeckeTower witness of MathlibExt is explicitly not, and nothing here changes that.
