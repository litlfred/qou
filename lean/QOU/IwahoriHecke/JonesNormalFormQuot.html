    QOU.IwahoriHecke.JonesNormalFormQuot — Markov uniqueness on the presented H_{N+1}(q) #

    Instantiates QOU.IwahoriHecke.JonesNormalForm on the presented Iwahori–Hecke algebra QOU.Hecke.IwahoriHecke K q N (RingQuot of the free algebra on N generators, quadratic relation σᵢ² = (q − 1)σᵢ + q). The generators σ₀, …, σ_{N-1} satisfy the type-A relations (heckeFamily) and generate the algebra (adjoin_gen_eq_top), so:

    • markov_unique_quot — two normalised, cyclic K-linear functionals on H_{N+1}(q) that satisfy the Markov property t (x · σ_k) = z · t x (for x generated by σ₀, …, σ_{k-1}, every k < N) with the same z are equal.

    Sorry-free; no axioms beyond propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound. The cross-level Markov hypothesis is essential: the former QOU.Hecke.markov_trace_unique_axiom, which omitted it, was false (QOU.Hecke.MarkovTraceClassNotUnique.markov_trace_unique_axiom_false) and has been replaced by the theorem QOU.Hecke.markov_trace_unique (bean qou-q425).
