    The Markov peel, and why the physical weight is forced #

    QOU.IwahoriHecke.QTraceCyclic made the site-weighted trace tr_w a genuine trace — cyclic, hence invariant under conjugation, which is Markov move I. What a Markov trace needs beyond that is move II, the peel:

    tr(ι(x) · σ_n) = z · tr(x)   for x in the smaller algebra,

    relating the trace on H_{n+1}(q) to the one on H_n(q). This file proves it, and computes z.

    The tower #

    Two maps, one on each side of the action:

    • tower — the algebra map H_{m+1}(q) → H_{m+2}(q), σ_a ↦ σ_{a.castSucc}. The only input is coxA_castSucc, that the type-A Coxeter matrix restricts along castSucc.
    • extendSiteHom — the matrix-level counterpart End(V^{⊗(m+1)}) → End(V^{⊗(m+2)}), X ↦ X ⊗ 1, built as idTensorHom reindexed along Fin.snocEquiv. It is an algebra map because both factors are.

    heckeActionN_tower says the square commutes, by hom_ext_sigma — the two composites agree on generators because extendSite_strandBraid does.

    The peel, and the weight it forces #

    The computation is trace_peel. Writing P u v := ∑ₜ w t · Ř_{(u,t),(v,t)} for the weighted partial trace of the braiding over the new site, the whole content is:

    • P is diagonal — Ř's swap entry needs u = t and t = v at once, so it never survives the partial trace;
    • if P is the scalar z, the peel holds with that z (trace_peel, qTrace_peel).

    The interesting direction is the converse, and it is what makes the corpus's choice of weight non-arbitrary:

    peel_forces_physical_weight — if the peel holds at all, then w 1 = q² · w 0. With the normalisation w 0 = 1 that is w = (1, q²) on the nose, and then z = q³.

    So the weight K = diag(1, q²) that QOU.MassTheory.WeightedPermutationCharacter and the rest of the corpus use is not a convention chosen to make things work — it is the unique weight (up to overall scale) for which the braiding admits a Markov peel at all, and q³ is not fitted either: it is w 1 · q, forced by the same two equations.

    🛑 What this is still not #

    A Markov trace in the sense the Jones polynomial consumes also wants normalisation tr(1) = 1; qTrace_one gives (1 + q²)^{m+1}, so the normalised functional is tr_w / (1 + q²)^{m+1} and the corresponding z acquires the same factor. That rescaling is not done here. Nor is the link invariant: QOU.IwahoriHecke.MarkovTrace.markovTrace is still noncomputable opaque, and nothing here constructs it.
