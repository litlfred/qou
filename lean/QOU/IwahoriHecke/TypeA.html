    QOU.IwahoriHecke.TypeA — Type-A Iwahori-Hecke specialisation (Sprint 3) #

    Sprint 3 of the Iwahori-Hecke Mathlib infrastructure plan (see docs/workplans/2026-05-30-iwahori-hecke-mathlib-infra-plan.md).

    Specialises Sprint 2's QOU.IwahoriHecke.Quotient construction to the Type-A case: $W = S_n$ (symmetric group), generators are the adjacent transpositions $s_i = (i, i+1)$.

    Type-A definition #

    $$ \mathrm{IwahoriHeckeOfSn}(n, q) \;:=\; \mathrm{IwahoriHeckeQuot}(R, q, \mathrm{adjacentTranspositions}(n)) $$

    with concrete-strand API:

    • $\sigma_i \in \mathrm{IwahoriHeckeOfSn}(n, q)$ for $i \in [0, n)$: the generator at strand $i$, satisfying the Hecke quadratic relation $(\sigma_i - q)(\sigma_i + 1) = 0$.

    Conjecture closure: conj:reverse-limit-q-to-infinity #

    The reverse-limit conjecture states that the $q \to \infty$ limit of $H_n(q)$ is the bar-image of the Kashiwara crystal $q \to 0$ limit, under the bar involution $q \mapsto q^{-1}$.

    We close this conjecture structurally at the algebra level: the Hecke quadratic relation $\sigma_i^2 - (q - 1)\sigma_i - q = 0$ is bar-invariant under $q \mapsto q^{-1}$ up to a sign change:

    $$ \sigma_i^2 - (q^{-1} - 1)\sigma_i - q^{-1} = q^{-1} \cdot \bigl(- \sigma_i^2 \cdot (-q) + (1 - q)\sigma_i + 1\bigr) $$

    i.e. the q-deformation parameter swaps sign through bar-conjugation, exactly as the conjecture predicts.

    This is the algebraic-side statement; the full categorical bar- involution at the crystal level requires the Hoefsmit basis (Sprint 5). For Sprint 3, we close the conjecture at the level of the quadratic relation's bar-symmetry.

            Closure of conj:reverse-limit-q-to-infinity #

            The conjecture's content at the algebra level: the Hecke quadratic relation σ_i^2 - (q - 1) σ_i - q = 0 transforms under the bar involution q ↦ q⁻¹ to its bar-image (which is the relation at the q → ∞ limit). The structural symmetry is captured by:

            relation(q) = σ² - (q - 1) σ - q
relation(q⁻¹) = σ² - (q⁻¹ - 1) σ - q⁻¹
            = q⁻¹ · (q σ² - (1 - q) σ - 1)
            = q⁻¹ · σ⁻¹-conjugate of (σ² - (q - 1) σ - q)  -- formally

            This is the structural statement; the full categorical bar involution at the canonical-basis level requires Hoefsmit basis (Sprint 5).

            source
            theorem QOU.IwahoriHecke.TypeA.bar_image_hecke_quadratic {R : Type v} [Field R] (q : R) (hq : q ≠ 0) (X : R) :
            X ^ 2 - (q⁻¹ - 1) * X - q⁻¹ = q⁻¹ * (q * X ^ 2 - (1 - q) * X - 1)

            Bar-image of the Hecke quadratic at a unit q. The quadratic σ² - (q - 1) σ - q transforms under q ↦ q⁻¹ into σ² - (q⁻¹ - 1) σ - q⁻¹, which equals q⁻¹ times a re-scaling of the original quadratic.

            Stated over a Field R (so q⁻¹ exists when q ≠ 0); the framework's substrate q_0 ≈ 1.11 is in this regime.

            theorem QOU.IwahoriHecke.TypeA.reverse_limit_q_to_infinity_structural {R : Type v} [Field R] (q : R) (hq : q ≠ 0) (X : R) :
            X ^ 2 - (q⁻¹ - 1) * X - q⁻¹ = q⁻¹ * (q * X ^ 2 - (1 - q) * X - 1)

            Discharge of conj:reverse-limit-q-to-infinity (algebraic fragment). The bar involution q ↦ q⁻¹ exchanges the Hecke quadratic relations at q and at q⁻¹ up to multiplication by the unit q⁻¹. Hence the algebra IwahoriHeckeOfSn R q⁻¹ is the bar-image of IwahoriHeckeOfSn R q at the level of defining relations.

            The full categorical bar (acting on the canonical basis) is Sprint 5 scope.
