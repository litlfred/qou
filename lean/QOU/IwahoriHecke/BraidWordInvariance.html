    Word products in the Hecke algebra, and what Matsumoto's theorem is actually for #

    The T_w basis of an Iwahori–Hecke algebra is defined by picking a reduced expression w = s_{i₁} ⋯ s_{i_k} and setting T_w := T_{i₁} ⋯ T_{i_k}. Whether that is well defined is the standard hard point, and the corpus has repeatedly deferred it to "Matsumoto's theorem". This file separates the two halves of that deferral, because only one of them is hard and the corpus was treating both as unavailable.

    The algebra half is easy and is proved here. Two words related by a braid move — replacing an alternating subword i j i ⋯ (M i j letters) by j i j ⋯ (M j i letters) — have the same product in H(M, q), immediately from the defining braid relation (heckeWord_eq_of_braidMove, and its closure heckeWord_eq_of_braidEquiv).

    The group half is the real Matsumoto theorem and is about the Coxeter group, not the algebra: any two reduced expressions for the same w ∈ W are related by a chain of braid moves. Mathlib does not have it: Mathlib/GroupTheory/Coxeter/ has IsReduced, the length function, descents and inversion sequences, but no exchange property and no Matsumoto.

    🛑 The claim that "nothing in this corpus proves it either" was wrong about the exchange property, and it was mine (corrected 2026-09-06). QOU.Machinery.CoxeterReflectionCocycle.rightExchange proves RightExchangeProp cs for every Coxeter system, sorry-free, off strong_exchange (Björner–Brenti Thm 1.4.3). What remains open is deletion → Matsumoto; the exchange property is not part of the gap.

    ✅ And that remainder is now closed too (2026-09-06). QOU.Machinery.CoxeterDeletion supplies deletion and QOU.IwahoriHecke.Matsumoto supplies Matsumoto — for every Coxeter system, with no hypothesis, since CoxeterDihedralOrder.dihedralExact_full settles the order of σᵢσⱼ and bond_ne_zero_of_pow_eq_one the infinite-bond case. So the sentence below reading "not proved anywhere in this corpus or in Mathlib" is stale for this corpus; it remains true of Mathlib.

    exists_Tw_of_braidEquiv below is a conditional theorem naming its hypothesis rather than a class asserting the conclusion.

    🛑 But do not read it as making the dependency exact — corrected 2026-09-06, and the earlier wording here was mine. Its hypothesis quantifies over all words, and at the only instantiation anyone wants — π := cs.wordProd for a Coxeter system — that hypothesis is false, so the theorem is vacuous there. Braid moves preserve word length (a Coxeter matrix is symmetric), while π [i,i] = π [] because s_i² = 1. Refuted in QOU.IwahoriHecke.TwMatsumoto.not_braidEquiv_hypothesis; the satisfiable replacement, stated against reduced words, is TwMatsumoto.exists_Tw_of_matsumoto.

    Relation to QOU.IwahoriHecke.Tw #

    That file's wordProduct is built over QOU.IwahoriHecke.Quotient.IwahoriHeckeQuot, which by its own scope note imposes the quadratic relation only and no braid relations. Nothing of this file's content can hold there: with no braid relation, a braid move changes the product. The word product belongs over QOU.MathlibExt.IwahoriHecke.HeckeAlgebra, which imposes both families, and that is where it is built here.

            What the missing group-theoretic half buys #

            The statement below is the whole content of the deferral. π is any way of reading a word as a group element — for a Coxeter system, CoxeterSystem.wordProd. The hypothesis hmat is Matsumoto's theorem, which is a fact about W and is not proved anywhere in this corpus or in Mathlib. Everything else is discharged.

            Note the hypothesis is stated for all pairs of words with the same image, which is stronger than Matsumoto (that speaks only of reduced expressions).

            🛑 That extra strength is not a feature; it is fatal, and the sentence that used to stand here calling it "what a total Tw : W → H needs" was wrong. On a Coxeter group the all-words hypothesis is refuted outright — see QOU.IwahoriHecke.TwMatsumoto.not_braidEquiv_hypothesis. The statement one can actually discharge is TwMatsumoto.exists_Tw_of_matsumoto, whose hypothesis is Matsumoto verbatim and whose Tw agrees with the word product on every reduced word. This theorem remains true as a statement about an abstract π; it is simply not applicable to the case it was written for.

            ∃ (Tw : W → MathlibExt.IwahoriHecke.HeckeAlgebra R q M), ∀ (l : List B), Tw (π l) = heckeWord q M l

            The T_w map exists as soon as the group half does. If words with the same image in W are braid-equivalent, the word product descends to a well-defined function on W agreeing with it on every word.

            This is a conditional theorem naming its hypothesis, which is the honest form of the deferral — as opposed to a class that asserts the conclusion, or an instance that supplies a placeholder.
