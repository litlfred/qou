    The framing correction, and why its two constants are forced #

    BraidTower and NegativePeel give both Markov moves for the braid trace, with different scalars: z⁺ = q³·wi and z⁻ = qi·wi at the weight the peel forces. Their product is q²·wi², not 1, so the raw trace is an invariant of a framed closure and nothing more.

    This file removes the framing, and the point is that there is no freedom in how.

    The writhe #

    Mathlib has no braid group, hence no writhe. writhe : Art(M) →* Multiplicative ℤ sends every generator to 1; the Artin relations are satisfied because a relation equates two alternating words of lengths M i j and M j i, which a symmetric M makes equal. So the exponent sum is well defined on the presented group — that symmetry is the whole content, and it is the same hypothesis heckeRep and liftBalanced already take.

    braidWritheZ is its integer form; braidWritheZ_braidTower says adding an inert strand does not change it.

    The two constants, and their uniqueness #

    Correct by J(β) := A^{n} · B^{writhe β} · tr(β) with A, B units. The two moves give two equations, and they determine A and B up to sign:

    move strands writhe condition
    II⁺ n → n+1 w → w+1 A · B · z⁺ = 1
    II⁻ n → n+1 w → w−1 A · B⁻¹ · z⁻ = 1

    Multiplying: A² = (z⁺z⁻)⁻¹ = q⁻²(1+q²)². Dividing: B² = z⁻/z⁺ = q⁻⁴. So

    A = (1 + q²)·q⁻¹,   B = q⁻²

    is a solution, and strandFactor/writheFactor are exactly those — written division-free, as units built from q·qi = 1 and (1+q²)·wi = 1, so R stays a bare CommRing.

    jonesFn_peel and jonesFn_peel_neg verify both moves; jonesFn_conj is move I, which the correction leaves alone because the writhe and the strand count are both conjugation-invariant. framing_constants_unique states exactly what the two equations pin, and no more: for any a, b, c with a·b·z⁺ = 1, a·c·z⁻ = 1 and b·c = 1, they force a²·(q³·qi·wi²) = 1 and b²·q³ = qi. That is a determination of the squares — the sign is genuinely free, which is why the standard Jones normalisation carries a (−1)-type factor and why this file does not claim uniqueness of A and B themselves.

    🛑 One gap remains, and it is the topological one #

    jonesFn is now invariant under both Markov moves and under conjugation. It is still not a link invariant, because Markov's theorem — that closure-equivalence of braids is generated by exactly these moves — is a theorem of low-dimensional topology that Mathlib does not have and this corpus does not prove. Nothing here hypothesises it: with it, jonesFn descends to links; without it, jonesFn is a function on braids that happens to be constant on the orbits of the two moves.

    That is the honest state, and it is a strictly smaller gap than the one this branch started with.

    -- Ref: [jones1987] Hecke algebra representations of braid groups and link polynomials -- Ref: [markov1936] the theorem, not proved here -- Ref: [manuscript] bean qou-k4oq
