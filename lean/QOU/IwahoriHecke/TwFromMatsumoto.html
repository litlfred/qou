    🛑 exists_Tw_of_braidEquiv cannot be applied to a Coxeter group — and the statement that can #

    QOU.IwahoriHecke.BraidWordInvariance.exists_Tw_of_braidEquiv presents itself as the honest form of the T_w deferral: a conditional theorem naming Matsumoto as a hypothesis. Its own docstring notes the hypothesis is "stronger than Matsumoto (that speaks only of reduced expressions)", and offers that strength as a feature — "what a total Tw : W → H needs".

    It is not merely stronger. For the only instantiation anyone would want, it is false, so the theorem is vacuous there and reduces nothing.

    The refutation, in one line of arithmetic #

    A braid move replaces an alternating block of length M i j by one of length M j i. For a Coxeter matrix those are equal, so braid-equivalence preserves word length (braidEquiv_length_eq). But in a Coxeter group s_i² = 1, so π [i, i] = π [] while the two words have lengths 2 and 0. Hence

    not_braidEquiv_hypothesis — for any Coxeter system with at least one generator, the hypothesis of exists_Tw_of_braidEquiv at π := cs.wordProd is false.

    Nothing is wrong with exists_Tw_of_braidEquiv as a statement: it quantifies over an abstract π : List B → W and is a true conditional. What is wrong is the claim that it "makes the remaining gap exact". It makes a gap that cannot be filled.

    The statement that does work #

    exists_Tw_of_matsumoto takes the real Matsumoto hypothesis — braid-equivalence of two reduced words with the same image — and delivers Tw agreeing with the word product on every reduced word. That is satisfiable (it is exactly Matsumoto's theorem) and it is what the T_w basis is actually stated about; a basis is indexed by W through reduced expressions, not through arbitrary words.

    The construction needs a choice of reduced word per element, which Mathlib supplies (CoxeterSystem.exists_reduced_word'); redWord packages it.

    🛑 The all-words hypothesis is false for cs.wordProd.

    π [i,i] = π [] because s_i² = 1, but braid-equivalence preserves length and 2 ≠ 0. So exists_Tw_of_braidEquiv, instantiated at the word product of a Coxeter system — the only instantiation it exists for — has a hypothesis nothing can discharge, and proves nothing.

    Stated for the Coxeter matrix's own entries, which are symmetric by CoxeterMatrix.symmetric.
