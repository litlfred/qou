    The sl₂ R-matrix is a §7a Hecke generator #

    The braiding half of quantum Schur–Weyl. QOU.MassTheory.WeightedPermutationCharacter supplied the trace-weight half of the q ≠ 1 deformation — one q-factor per orbit, with the fibre κ = Fin 2 carrying K = diag(1, q²). The other half is the braiding: at q ≠ 1 the symmetric group no longer acts by permuting tensor factors, and its replacement is the Iwahori–Hecke algebra acting through Jimbo's R-matrix.

    This file supplies the generator of that action for the fundamental of U_q(sl₂) and proves the one fact that makes it a Hecke action:

    Ř² = (q − q⁻¹) Ř + 1,

    i.e. IsHeckeGenerator q qi Ř in the sense of QOU.MathlibExt.SkeinRelation, which is the symmetric normalisation AGENTS.md §7a mandates. So Ř has eigenvalues q (symmetric channel) and −q⁻¹ (antisymmetric channel) — the q-deformation of the ±1 eigenvalues of the transposition it degenerates to.

    The degeneration is proved, not asserted #

    rCheck_one shows Ř at q = qi = 1 is exactly the flip e_i ⊗ e_j ↦ e_j ⊗ e_i — the permutation matrix of the transposition. That is the concrete link to the q = 1 character of QOU.MassTheory.TensorPowerTrace: the classical 2^{c(σ)} trace is the q → 1 limit of this braiding, and the limit is checked here rather than assumed.

    Scope #

    • Fibre Fin 2 only. The general gl_n R-matrix Ř = q Σ_i E_ii⊗E_ii + Σ_{i≠j} E_ji⊗E_ij + (q − q⁻¹) Σ_{i<j} E_ii⊗E_jj has the same quadratic for any linearly ordered index type; only Fin 2 is done here, because Fin 2 is the fibre every consumer in this corpus uses (weighted_character_two, trace_permMatrixOf_two, ClassicalTraceFloor's 2^{c(σ)}).
    • n = 3 only. Both relations are proved — the quadratic (isHeckeGenerator_rCheck) and Yang–Baxter (yangBaxter) — and heckeAction feeds them to QOU.IwahoriHecke.Balanced.liftBalanced to produce an actual algebra map H₃(q) → End(V^{⊗3}). General n needs the embedding of a two-site operator at each position i of V^{⊗n}, which is not built here.
    • Nothing is claimed about the image. heckeAction is an algebra map; that it is surjective onto the commutant of the quantum-group action — the Schur–Weyl statement proper — is not proved and does not follow from anything here.

    -- Ref: [jimbo1986] Jimbo, "A q-difference analogue of U(g) and the Yang-Baxter equation" -- Ref: [manuscript] AGENTS.md §7a; bean qou-k4oq

    def QOU.IwahoriHecke.RMatrix.rCheck {R : Type u_1} [CommRing R] (q qi : R) :
    Matrix (Fin 2 × Fin 2) (Fin 2 × Fin 2) R

    Jimbo's Ř-matrix for the fundamental of U_q(sl₂), on V ⊗ V with V = Fin 2 → R, indexed by Fin 2 × Fin 2.

    On basis vectors:

    • Ř (eᵢ ⊗ eᵢ) = q · eᵢ ⊗ eᵢ — the symmetric channel, eigenvalue q;
    • Ř (e₀ ⊗ e₁) = e₁ ⊗ e₀ + (q − qi) · e₀ ⊗ e₁;
    • Ř (e₁ ⊗ e₀) = e₀ ⊗ e₁.

    At q = qi = 1 the middle coefficient vanishes and this is the flip (rCheck_one).

            rc1 q qi * rc2 q qi * rc1 q qi = rc2 q qi * rc1 q qi * rc2 q qi

            The Yang-Baxter equation Ř₁Ř₂Ř₁ = Ř₂Ř₁Ř₂ on V^{⊗3} — the braid relation for the sl₂ R-matrix.

            Together with isHeckeGenerator_rCheck this is exactly what QOU.IwahoriHecke.Balanced.liftBalanced consumes, so the two of them are the whole input to a Hecke action on a tensor power.

            q * qi = 1 is genuinely used, and only here: all 64 entries but two are ring identities in q and qi separately, and the two survivors differ by (q·qi − 1)(q − qi). So the braid relation holds for the deformed braiding because qi is q⁻¹, not for formal reasons.

            The Hecke action on V^{⊗3} #

            With both relations in hand, QOU.IwahoriHecke.Balanced.liftBalanced turns them into an actual algebra map out of H₃(q). This is the point of the whole file: IsHeckeGenerator and yangBaxter are statements about operators; what quantum Schur-Weyl needs is an action, and this is it.
