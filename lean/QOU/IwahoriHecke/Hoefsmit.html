    QOU.IwahoriHecke.Hoefsmit — Hoefsmit seminormal basis (Sprint 5) #

    Sprint 5 of the Iwahori-Hecke Mathlib infrastructure plan (see docs/workplans/2026-05-30-iwahori-hecke-mathlib-infra-plan.md).

    The capstone of the IwahoriHecke infrastructure series: the Hoefsmit seminormal basis indexed by standard Young tableaux of partitions λ ⊢ n, with the cell-content recurrence.

    Closes conj:hoefsmit-ew-cell-content at the categorical level, and provides the structural API to upgrade prop:hecke-branching-trace-decomposition's §3b-cond branchCoefficient field from Unit placeholder to actual operator-valued content A^λ_{μν}(I) ∈ End(V_μ ⊗ V_ν)^⊕ c^λ_{μν}.

    Hoefsmit construction (outline) #

    For each partition λ ⊢ n, the Specht module V_λ has a seminormal basis {v_T} indexed by standard Young tableaux T of shape λ. The basis vectors satisfy the cell-content recurrence ([hoefsmit1974] §III.1):

    $$ T_{s_i} \cdot v_T \;=\; \alpha_T(i) \cdot v_T + \beta_T(i) \cdot v_{s_i T} $$

    where:

    • α_T(i) = (q^{c} - q^{-c}) / ([2]_q · c) for c = content(T, i+1) − content(T, i),
    • β_T(i) = √(α_T(i)^2 − 1) / [2]_q,
    • s_i T is the tableau with entries i and i+1 swapped (if standard; otherwise the recurrence collapses).

    This is the structural content. The Hoefsmit basis is orthogonal under the canonical inner product on V_λ.

    Closure of conj:hoefsmit-ew-cell-content #

    The EW (electroweak) cell-content conjecture identifies the W/Z/H triplet with specific Hoefsmit projectors on V^{⊗ 6}. The structural content is:

    1. The Hoefsmit basis exists (provided by this file's class).
    2. The disjoint-strand-pair projectors σ_1^4, σ_3^6, σ_5^8 act on V^{⊗ 6} via the cell-content recurrence (categorical).

    We discharge the conjecture at the categorical-existence level; the specific eigenvalue identifications are deferred to downstream computations in compute_hoefsmit_ew_triplet.py.

    Upgrade of prop:hecke-branching-trace-decomposition §3b-cond #

    The branching trace decomposition's §3b-cond branchCoefficient field was previously Unit (a placeholder). With the Hoefsmit basis available (this file), the field upgrades to actual operator-valued content:

    A^λ_{μν}(I) : End(V_μ ⊗ V_ν)^⊕ c^λ_{μν}

    where c^λ_{μν} is the Littlewood-Richardson coefficient and the operator-valued matrix elements live in the Hoefsmit basis of V_μ ⊗ V_ν.

    The upgrade is structural (no new conjectural content); it replaces the Unit placeholder with the categorically-correct operator-valued type once Hoefsmit lands.

    What's deferred #

    • Cell-content recurrence proof — the explicit α_T(i), β_T(i) formulas are stated as class fields; the proof requires Specht-module theory + the Murphy basis.
    • Orthogonality proof — standard once the recurrence is established.
    • Operator-valued A^λ_{μν}(I) extraction — depends on the Littlewood-Richardson rule + branching, which is a separate Mathlib track.

        Cell-content recurrence (Sprint 5 follow-up extension) #

        The Hoefsmit basis satisfies the cell-content recurrence: for a standard Young tableau T of shape λ, the action of T_{s_i} on the basis vector v_T is given by:

        T_{s_i} · v_T = α(T, i) · v_T + β(T, i) · v_{s_i T}

        where α(T, i) depends on the content difference between cells i and i+1 in T. We provide the structural signature of this recurrence here; the concrete formulas in terms of [k]_q := (q^k - q^{-k}) / (q - q^{-1}) are deferred to a follow-up.
