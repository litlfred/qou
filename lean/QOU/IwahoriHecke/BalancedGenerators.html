    The §7a balanced generators of the Iwahori–Hecke algebra #

    Two pieces of Hecke infrastructure already live in this corpus and nothing connected them. This file is that connection.

    • QOU.MathlibExt.IwahoriHecke constructs the algebra H(M, Q) by generators and relations, in the Iwahori normalisation (T_i − Q)(T_i + 1) = 0, with its universal property and a proof that it does not collapse.
    • QOU.MathlibExt.SkeinRelation develops the whole skein calculus — explicit inverse, unit, T − T⁻¹ = q − q⁻¹, eigenvalue factorisation — for an abstract element satisfying the symmetric normalisation σ² = (q − q⁻¹)σ + 1 that AGENTS.md §7a mandates.

    The gap: the constructed algebra's own generators are not IsHeckeGenerators. In the Iwahori normalisation the eigenvalues are Q and −1, not q and −q⁻¹, so the skein calculus was floating free of the only algebra the corpus actually builds. Neither file mentions the other.

    The bridge #

    Take the Iwahori parameter to be Q = q² for scalars q, qi with q·qi = 1, and rescale:

    $$\sigma_i \;:=\; qi \cdot T_i .$$

    Then σ_i² = qi²((q²−1)T_i + q²) = (1 − qi²)T_i + 1 = (q − qi)σ_i + 1, which is exactly IsHeckeGenerator q qi σ_i (isHeckeGenerator_sigma). From that single theorem the entire SkeinRelation API transfers onto the generators of the constructed algebra, and this file spells out the consequences AGENTS.md §7a names by hand: the eigenvalue factorisation (σ_i − q)(σ_i + qi) = 0, the negative crossing σ_i⁻¹ = σ_i − (q − qi), and the skein relation L₊ − L₋ = (q − qi) L₀.

    The rescaling is a change of generators, not of algebra: T_i = q σ_i (T_eq_sigma), so the σ_i generate, uniqueness of maps out of the algebra can be tested on them (hom_ext_sigma), and a family satisfying the balanced relations lifts (liftBalanced). Nothing is lost and nothing new is assumed.

    Following SkeinFromHecke's own design, qi is carried as a separate scalar with an explicit q * qi = 1 hypothesis rather than written q⁻¹. That keeps the base ring a bare CommRing — no Field, no Inv, no ordering, nothing archimedean (AGENTS.md §7c).

    What this does NOT do #

    It does not construct the T_w basis, prove H free of rank |W|, or construct the Markov trace. Those remain open — see QOU.IwahoriHecke.Tw.

    theorem QOU.IwahoriHecke.Balanced.altProd_algebraMap_mul {R : Type u_1} [CommRing R] {A : Type u_2} [Ring A] [Algebra R A] (r : R) (n : ℕ) (a b : A) :
    MathlibExt.IwahoriHecke.altProd ((algebraMap R A) r * a) ((algebraMap R A) r * b) n = (algebraMap R A) (r ^ n) * MathlibExt.IwahoriHecke.altProd a b n

    Rescaling by a central scalar multiplies altProd by its power. altProd (r·a) (r·b) n = rⁿ · altProd a b n. The alternation is invisible to a central scalar: each of the n factors contributes exactly one r, whichever of a, b it is.

    This is what carries the braid relation across the rescaling.

            The q = 1 degeneration #

            At q = qi = 1 the deformation parameter q − qi vanishes and the quadratic collapses to σ_i² = 1: the balanced generators become involutions, which is the group-algebra presentation of the Coxeter group itself. This is the algebraic shadow of the q = 1 classical limit used in QOU.MassTheory.ClassicalTraceFloor, and it is the reason the balanced normalisation is the right one to degenerate — in the Iwahori normalisation the same limit gives T² = 1 only after the rescaling performed here.
