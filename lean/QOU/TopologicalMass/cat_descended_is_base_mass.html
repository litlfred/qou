    QOU.TopologicalMass.cat_descended_is_base_mass — descended term equals base mass #

    Lemma (lem:topological-mass-cat-descended).

    Let α = (∫_A ⊗ id) ∘ Δ ∘ ϱ be the categorical mass integrand of a closed cycle ϱ at level n + 1, and ϱ₀ its base-level descent. Then

    ‖tr(τ̂ₙ(α))‖ = 𝔪(ϱ₀).

    Structure (hypotheses vs. derivation) #

    The proof narrative has two genuine sub-claims, each stated as its own named proposition (honest sorry + citation per AGENTS.md §1, §1a item 2), and the main theorem derives the .md identity by composing them (congrArg + trans) — it does not assume its conclusion:

    1. descended_trace_is_base_trace — descent compatibility of the Frobenius trace: the trace of the descended integrand τ̂ₙ(α) at level n + 1 equals the base-level trace tr₀(α₀) of the descent of the cycle.
    2. base_mass_is_norm_of_base_trace — the base-level mass is the mass norm of the base-level trace: ‖tr₀(α₀)‖ = 𝔪(ϱ₀).

    The intermediate object tr₀(α₀) (the base-level trace) is a genuine quantity of the proof narrative, not a laundering device: both steps are visible sorried gaps, and the conclusion is obtained only by composing them — per the lean-proof-vacuity-audit fix recipe and AGENTS.md §3b-cond item 5. (An earlier revision carried both halves as fields of a data-only context class and universally quantified over arbitrary contexts, which made the statement false as stated; that carrier is removed.)

    Model / base ring (§7c) #

    The operators of the claim — the integrand α, the descent involution τ̂ₙ, the Frobenius trace tr, and the mass norm ‖·‖ — are carried as named opaque constants on ℝ (an opaque constant needs a concrete inhabited carrier; the mass norm is an archimedean real norm, so ℝ is the honest carrier here).
