            theorem QOU.Eightfold.FlavourColourDiscriminator.colour_eq_flavour_data :
            colourTripleData = flavourTripleData

            The two triples carry identical object-level data. The dimensions arrive by genuinely different routes — (7-1)/2 from the octonion decomposition, 2·1·3/2 from the Weyl dimension formula — and agree at 3.

            theorem QOU.Eightfold.FlavourColourDiscriminator.no_object_gate_separates (gate : ObjectData → Bool) :
            gate colourTripleData = gate flavourTripleData

            The class exclusion. A gate is a verdict-valued function of what it can read. No gate that reads only object-level data separates the colour triple from the flavour triple — not the two already tried, and not any third.

            This is why the Sym² and ℤ/3 failures were not repairable: they were not failures of those two constructions, but of the class.

                theorem QOU.Eightfold.FlavourColourDiscriminator.tensor_cube_gates_also_blind :
                sym3Dim colourTripleData.dim = sym3Dim flavourTripleData.dim ∧ alt3Dim colourTripleData.dim = alt3Dim flavourTripleData.dim

                The next moves are already dead. After Sym² fails, the natural instinct is to try the next tensor functor — Sym³, or Λ³ (whose singlet is the colour ε_{abc}, which makes it look especially promising). Both dimensions are functions of dim V alone, so a gate reading either is a gate reading ObjectData, and no_object_gate_separates already covers it.

                Checked concretely rather than argued: Sym³ gives 10 for both triples and Λ³ gives 1 for both.

                This is what makes the class exclusion worth having. It does not merely record two past failures — it forecloses the entire "try another tensor construction on V" family without running any of them.

                inductive QOU.Eightfold.FlavourColourDiscriminator.Channel3 :
                Type

                The Young-symmetry channel of V ⊗ V ⊗ V, indexed by the partitions of 3 — equivalently by the irreps of S₃.

                • symmetric : Channel3

                  λ = (3), the trivial S₃-irrep: the decuplet's flavour channel.

                • mixed : Channel3

                  λ = (2,1), the standard irrep: the octet's flavour channel.

                • antisymmetric : Channel3

                  λ = (1³), the sign irrep: the colour channel, ε_{abc}.
