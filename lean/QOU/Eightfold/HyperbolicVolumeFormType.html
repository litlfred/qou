    QOU.Eightfold.HyperbolicVolumeFormType — the hyperbolic volume form is a #

    third kind of degree-3 form, on a fourth three-manifold, and it is real

    Bean qou-xlw1, epic qou-8lxd. Third companion to QOU.Eightfold.ContactDistributionRank and QOU.QuantumObservableUniverse.SelfDualityDegree.

    The question this answers #

    Raised by the owner: "when we get to the knot complement there's an S³ there and we need a hyperbolic volume … so we need a volume form that is that hyperbolic metric on the knot complement, which is outside the exceptional divisor. On the exceptional divisor, with the Reeb flow, it should not necessarily be [hyperbolic]."

    Both halves are right, and the second half is provable.

    Four three-manifolds, three geometries #

    The prior audit (2026-09-04-where-the-ambient-s3-comes-from) separated three objects the corpus writes as S³. The hyperbolic volume lives on a fourth, which that audit missed because it is not called S³ — it is S³ with something removed:

    object geometry curvature compact π₁ its volume form
    S³_AM — Alexander–Markov closure arena topological only — yes trivial none stated
    S³_Reeb — Hopf bundle over 𝒮_p ≅ S² round / spherical +1 yes trivial Θ ∧ dΘ
    M_S — circle bundle in T*S contact, genus-4 base — yes infinite (b₁ ≥ 8) Θ ∧ dΘ
    S³ ∖ K — the knot complement hyperbolic −1 no, cusped infinite Vol(K)

    Why S³_Reeb cannot be the hyperbolic one — cited, and the reason is π₁ #

    virtual-particles-exceptional-divisor.md:7 puts the Reeb bundle over the exceptional divisor as the Hopf fibration U(1) ↪ S³ → 𝒮_p. That total space is S³: compact and simply connected.

    A complete finite-volume hyperbolic 3-manifold is ℍ³/Γ with Γ ⊂ PSL(2,ℂ) discrete and torsion-free, so π₁ = Γ; and Γ must be infinite, since ℍ³ itself has infinite volume. Hence no simply connected 3-manifold carries a complete finite-volume hyperbolic metric, and S³_Reeb is not hyperbolic.

    That is standard hyperbolic geometry, cited and not formalised — Mathlib has no hyperbolic-manifold API. It is recorded here because it is what makes the owner's second half correct: on the exceptional divisor, with the Reeb flow, the geometry is the round Hopf one, and it is not hyperbolic, provably rather than incidentally.

    What is formalised: the third pair of "different threes" #

    The hyperbolic volume form is a top form on a real 3-manifold, so the object it is a volume form of is the real rank-3 tangent bundle. Λ³ of that is a line, so it is a genuine degree-3 volume form — and the object is self-conjugate, i.e. real Frobenius–Schur type, which QOU.Eightfold.Sym2GateFSTrichotomy.real_triple_fails_gate excludes.

    What the eightfold way needs is a complex rank-3 object, whose underlying real rank is 6, not 3 (realRank_of_complexRank, three_ne_six).

    So this is the third instance of one trap, and they should be read together:

    file the two threes
    ContactDistributionRank.degree_three_but_rank_one at dim M = 3 the contact volume form has degree 3 while ξ has complex rank 1
    ContactDistributionRank.dim_seven_two_distributions at dim M = 7 the Sasakian distribution has complex rank 3 while the quaternionic-contact one has 2
    here the hyperbolic volume form has degree 3 while its object has real rank 3, not complex rank 3

    Each time, a 3 that is a degree has been read as a 3 that is a rank.

    Consequences for the framework, stated plainly #

    1. Vol(K) is currently an input, which is a fact about the corpus's present state and NOT a claim that it must stay one. It is computed by SnapPy (knot-volumes-verification.md) and enters q₀ alongside CODATA m_μ/m_e (local-substrate-q-proof.md:14). By Mostow rigidity the complete hyperbolic metric on a finite-volume 3-manifold is unique up to isometry, so Vol(K) is a topological invariant of the knot — there is no metric to choose. The corpus leans on exactly this: "Mostow rigidity (geometric confinement) makes the resulting topology — and hence the mass — discrete" (approach-to-exceptional-divisor.md).

    🛑 An earlier draft of this file said the hyperbolic volume form "is not a QOU structure — it is imported", and that is RETRACTED. Rigidity means the metric is not chosen; it does not mean the volume is not derivable. The corpus in fact asserts the opposite of what I wrote: brings-surface/thurston-relaxation.md states that the q-Pochhammer poles are in bijection with the n ideal tetrahedra of a hyperbolic triangulation, that Newton iteration on Thurston's gluing equations seeded by those poles converges to the shape parameters realising the complete hyperbolic structure, and that "the same algebraic-residue data feeds both the SDP and the gluing solver; they are different evaluations of one categorical object" (conj:algebraic-geometric-dilog-equivalence). The volume-conjecture leg points the same way: the Kashaev invariant / coloured Jones is a categorical object the framework already computes, and its root-of-unity asymptotics are Vol(S³∖K). Whether those links are proved, conjectured or merely asserted is a separate question and is being measured — but "not a QOU structure" was an impossibility claim I never established, against a route the corpus names.

    2. It is not a fourth calibration. Vol(4₁) = 2·Cl₂(π/3) is a closed form, known exactly; SnapPy verifies it numerically rather than supplying it. Its status is that of π, not that of a measured input. (For a general knot no closed form is known and the SnapPy value is the only source — that dependency is real, and is separate from the 4₁ case.)

    3. The two volume forms are on different manifolds and neither yields the other. Θ ∧ dΘ is a contact volume form on a compact simply connected S³; the hyperbolic volume form is the Riemannian volume of a cusped, non-compact, infinite-π₁ manifold. No restriction, deformation or limit of one is the other, because they are not forms on the same space.

    4. The hyperbolic volume form does not supply W either. Fifth independent route to land on real type, after 𝔰𝔲(2) = Im ℍ, the tetraplectic Sp(1)ⁿ, the affine-W compactification, and Ad(A).

    What is proved here, and what is not #

    Proved (unconditional integer arithmetic): that a complex rank-n object has real rank 2n; that complex rank 3 means real rank 6; that real rank 3 has a one-dimensional top exterior power (so the hyperbolic volume form is a genuine volume form) while its object is not complex rank 3; and the three-way table above as arithmetic.

    Not proved, and asserted nowhere below:

    • that S³ admits no complete finite-volume hyperbolic metric. Cited standard hyperbolic geometry (π₁ of such a manifold is infinite).

      🛑 The provenance note attached to this used to read "Mathlib has no hyperbolic-manifold API and library/ holds no source on it". BOTH HALVES ARE FALSE, measured. Mathlib carries Analysis/Complex/UpperHalfPlane/{Metric,Basic,Manifold,MoebiusAction,Topology} — the hyperbolic metric on ℍ² and the Möbius action — plus the whole NumberTheory/ModularForms/ tree; what it lacks is a dilogarithm and a 3-dimensional hyperbolic-manifold API. And library/ is saturated with this material: section-file hits are dilogarithm 849, Kashaev 548, volume conjecture 381, Thurston 321, gluing equation 215, ideal tetrahedra 178, Bloch–Wigner 135, Neumann–Zagier 96, Lobachevsky 46.

      The error was a change-of-vocabulary blind spot of exactly the kind this corpus keeps recording: the search that produced "holds nothing" used the contact/CR vocabulary (Sasakian, CR manifold, 7-manifold), while this layer is covered under the knot-theory / dilogarithm vocabulary. Note also Bloch-Wigner (hyphen) returns 135 and Bloch–Wigner (en-dash) returns 19 — one spelling of one name changes the count sevenfold.

    • Mostow rigidity, or that Vol(4₁) = 2·Cl₂(π/3). Both cited.

    • that the corpus's S³_Reeb and S³ ∖ K are the manifolds the corpus intends — the three-way S³ ambiguity is unresolved and is the author's.

    • anything about which knot is meant anywhere. The corpus's own torus-vs-hyperbolic treatment (pion-mass-hyperbolic-threshold.md) is careful and is not disturbed by anything here.

    Base ring: none — integer arithmetic (§7c: no ℝ, no ring at all).
