    QOU.Eightfold.AmphichiralHolonomyIsSelfDual — route E, narrowed to one knot #

    Route E (qou-960k, qou-g1m8) proposes a PGL(3,ℂ) decoration of the knot complement as the source of the flavour triple. AmbientInvolutionDoesNotDecideType established what it must now exhibit: a three-dimensional object of complex Frobenius–Schur type, because Sym2GateFSTrichotomy.gate_forces_complex_of_dim_three excludes real and quaternionic constructions as a class.

    This file prunes that obligation using a standard geometric fact together with a property the corpus already assigns to its own knots, and the result is that route E survives on exactly one of the particle dictionary's three knots.

    The argument #

    For a hyperbolic 3-manifold, the complex-conjugate of a holonomy representation is the holonomy of the mirror manifold: ρ̄_K ≅ ρ_{mirror K} (carried here as an explicit hypothesis, MirrorConjugate, so the theorem names what it uses). An amphichiral knot satisfies mirror K = K. Composing, ρ̄_K ≅ ρ_K — the holonomy is self-dual, hence not complex type, hence selfDual_holonomy_not_flavour_triple says it cannot be the missing three-dimensional object.

    Applied to the three knots of the dictionary #

    knot role status route E
    4₁ figure-eight Higgs / mass mechanism amphichiral — the corpus says so in five places (self-dual-higgs-real-volume.md:1, knot-particle-dictionary.md:24, binding-knots-visual-inventory.md:60, cohomological-embedding-table2-data.md:12, figure-eight-reeb-theta-value.md:9) pruned by the theorem below
    3₁ trefoil electron chiral, but a torus knot: Vol = 0, complement Seifert-fibred, so no hyperbolic holonomy to conjugate (knot-particle-dictionary.md:24; the 2026-08-04 PGL(3) assessment §2d flags the same) no holonomy of this kind exists

    So route E's whole remaining surface is the Borromean link — which is, and this is not a coincidence worth passing over, the carrier the eightfold decuplet actually needs.

    🛑 One tension, flagged rather than silently resolved #

    The classical literature calls the Borromean rings amphichiral. The corpus asserts the opposite for its 𝓑₃ via μ̄(123). Both can be true of different objects — μ̄(123) is an invariant of an ordered, oriented link and changes sign under mirroring, while the classical amphichirality is realised by a homeomorphism that may permute components — but the corpus does not say which it means, and this file does not adjudicate it.

    That question is now the whole of route E: if the classical reading wins, the theorem below prunes 𝓑₃ too and route E is closed on every knot in the dictionary; if the corpus's reading wins, 𝓑₃ is the one place left to look. It is a small, sharp, checkable question, and it is cheaper than the PGL(3,ℂ) computation it would replace.

    Scope #

    Scope-(a), and narrower than it may read. This prunes the holonomy route — a representation arising as the holonomy of a hyperbolic structure. Route E could in principle use a PGL(3,ℂ) representation that is not a holonomy, and nothing here touches that. What is excluded is the specific construction qou-52fu proposed.

    Base ring (§7c) #

    No ring, no ordering, no archimedean content: the argument is an involution and an equivalence relation. The FSType half is inherited from the companion.

        The route-E conclusion for an amphichiral knot. If the holonomy's occurring irreps are self-dual — which §2 delivers from amphichirality — then the object it carries is not the three-dimensional flavour candidate.

        The hsd hypothesis is the bridge from the geometric self-duality of §2 to the representation-theoretic FSType of the companion. It is stated rather than derived on purpose: the two self-dualities are different notions (one is ρ̄ ≅ ρ, the other is a Frobenius–Schur type), and collapsing them silently would be exactly the ambient-vs-instance conflation this arc has been correcting. A user of this theorem must supply the identification.

        4. The two self-dualities are NOT identified here #

        Recorded as a theorem so that a later reader cannot mistake §3's hypothesis for a derivation. selfDual_of_amphichiral produces iso (dual (hol K)) (hol K), an assertion about a representation and its dual. FSType.SelfDual is an assertion about a Frobenius–Schur label. Nothing in this file maps one to the other, and the statement below is what is actually available: the geometric half, alone.
