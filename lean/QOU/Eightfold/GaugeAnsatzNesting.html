    QOU.Eightfold.GaugeAnsatzNesting — a coarser gauge ansatz is a special case of a finer one #

    Issue #7092 asks whether the duality/σ ratio on the Ptolemy coordinates is a coboundary for a gauge action, and it is answered on the exact L6a4 class-1 point with three verdicts (computations/ptolemy/ptolemy_duality_sigma_coboundary_test.py):

    • the scalar ansatz λ_v^{t_i} — the issue as written — is not a coboundary, for either σ;
    • the PGL(3) torus (snappy's 128 × 6 decoration-change action) is one for σ_b and is not for σ_a;
    • the resulting torus element does not lift to a diagonal SL(3) matrix over K.

    Read as three independent measurements, the first two look like they are in tension. They are not, and this file is why: the scalar ansatz is a sub-torus of the PGL(3) torus, so the two verdicts are one chain rather than two results.

    The arithmetic behind it #

    Writing the two exponent matrices as snappy and the probe build them, over the 128 non-vertex coordinates,

    • A_torus i (lev, k) = #{ v : cusp v = k, t v ≥ lev + 1 } for lev ∈ {0, 1},
    • A_scalar i k = ∑ over v with cusp v = k of t v,

    and on those rows every t v ≤ 2 (the value 3 occurs only on the 32 vertex coordinates, which the action matrix excludes), so #{lev ∈ {0,1} : t v ≥ lev+1} = t v and

    A_scalar[:, k] = A_torus[:, (0,k)] + A_torus[:, (1,k)]

    exactly over ℤ — measured 128/128, and failing on 32/32 vertex rows, where min 3 2 = 2 ≠ 3, which is what makes the restriction load-bearing rather than incidental (computations/ptolemy/ptolemy_gauge_ansatz_nesting.py).

    What is proved here #

    The general fact, of which that identity is one instance: if a coarse exponent row B is obtained from a fine row A by summing over the fibres of a map φ on the columns, then every coarse gauge value is a fine gauge value — take z c := lam (φ c). Hence

    coboundary for the coarse ansatz → coboundary for the fine one,

    so a fine "no" would force a coarse "no", and the observed (fine yes, coarse no) is the only informative pattern; the reverse is excluded outright.

    The consequence for #7092 is stated as strictly_stronger: the issue as written asks a strictly stronger question than the one that admits the affirmative answer, so its negative is not evidence against the torus positive.

    Scope #

    This is the group theory of the two ansätze, machine-checked, and nothing more. It does not say that the ratio is a coboundary for either — that is the measured content of the probe above — and it does not bear on whether ρ ↦ ρ⁻ᵀ induces σ_b as a map on the variety, which is the open conjecture of bean qou-qjhc.
