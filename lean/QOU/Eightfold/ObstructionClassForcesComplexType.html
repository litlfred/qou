    QOU.Eightfold.ObstructionClassForcesComplexType — self-duality forces a trivial obstruction #

    AmphichiralHolonomyIsSelfDual pruned route E's geometric leg: the holonomy of an amphichiral knot is self-dual, hence not the complex-type object the flavour gate needs. What survived was a non-geometric boundary-unipotent PGL(3,ℂ) representation.

    Searching for one of those means decomposing a Ptolemy variety, which is expensive. This file replaces that search with an arithmetic obstruction, and the payoff is that a whole obstruction class can be certified complex type at once, without solving anything.

    The argument #

    A boundary-unipotent PGL(3,ℂ) representation ρ carries an obstruction class σ(ρ) ∈ H²(M, ∂M; ℤ/3): lift each generator to SL(3,ℂ), and the failure of the relations is a 2-cocycle valued in the centre μ₃.

    Two facts about that class are topological inputs, carried here as the explicit hypotheses of ObstructionDuality rather than asserted:

    • obs_dual — duality negates the class. Lifting ρ* = ᵗρ⁻¹ means inverse-transposing the lifts, and a central ζ • 1 ↦ ζ⁻¹ • 1, so the cocycle inverts.
    • obs_congr — isomorphic representations have equal classes (the class is an invariant of ρ up to conjugation).

    Given both, self-duality forces σ = -σ, so 2σ = 0; and 2 is invertible mod 3, so σ = 0. Contrapositive: no representation in a non-trivial obstruction class is self-dual. That is not_selfDual_of_obs_ne_zero, the form a search actually uses — it reduces "exhibit a complex-type representation" to "exhibit any point of the Ptolemy variety in a non-trivial class", i.e. to the ideal being proper.

    Scope #

    This proves an implication about any structure satisfying the two hypotheses, and says nothing about whether such a representation exists for a given manifold. Non-emptiness is a separate, computational question.

    Both hypotheses are now DERIVED, not cited (2026-09-06). This section previously called them "the cited topological facts"; they are two-line computations from the definition of the cocycle, and neither needs a topological input.

    Write c(g,h) for the cocycle of a lift s, i.e. s g * s h * (s (g*h))⁻¹, central by construction.

    • obs_dual. Inverse-transpose φ : A ↦ ᵗA⁻¹ is a group HOMOMORPHISM, not an anti-homomorphism: (AB)⁻ᵀ = ᵗ(B⁻¹A⁻¹) = A⁻ᵀB⁻ᵀ. So the dual lift φ ∘ s has cocycle φ(c(g,h)), and φ inverts the centre (ζ•1 ↦ ζ⁻¹•1), giving c⁻¹.
    • obs_congr. A conjugate lift a * s · * a⁻¹ has cocycle a * c(g,h) * a⁻¹, which equals c(g,h) because c is central.

    The obs_dual step is additionally verified in the RELATIVE setting the Ptolemy obstruction actually lives in — H²(M, ∂M; ℤ/3), not H²(Γ; μ₃). Negating the cocycle acts on the Ptolemy variety exactly as u ↦ u², the nontrivial automorphism of μ₃: P^c and P^{-c} differ in 10 of 33 equations, and substituting u ↦ u² throughout P^c reproduces P^{-c} as polynomial sets, on classes 1, 2 and 3 (computations/ptolemy/ptolemy_duality_galois_check.py, 3/3). Corroborated independently by snappy, whose enumeration quotients H² by the units of ℤ/N — at N = 3 the units are {1,2} and 2 = -1, which is why 27 classes present as 14.

      The cocycle model — where the two hypotheses come from #

      ObstructionDuality above carries obs_dual and obs_congr as hypotheses. This section proves their group-theoretic content, so they are no longer bare assumptions: each is a short computation with the definition of the obstruction cocycle, and neither needs a topological input.

      Write cocycle s g h = s g * s h * (s (g * h))⁻¹ for the failure of a set-theoretic lift s to be multiplicative. For a lift of a PGL(n,ℂ) representation to SL(n,ℂ) this lands in the centre μ_n, and its class is the obstruction.

      • cocycle_dual is obs_dual. Inverse-transpose φ : A ↦ ᵗA⁻¹ is a group homomorphism, not an anti-homomorphism — (AB)⁻ᵀ = ᵗ(B⁻¹A⁻¹) = A⁻ᵀB⁻ᵀ — so by cocycle_map the dual lift's cocycle is φ applied to the original, and φ inverts the centre.
      • cocycle_conj_of_comm is obs_congr. Conjugating a lift conjugates its cocycle, and a cocycle commuting with the conjugator is unchanged — which centrality supplies.

      Scope. This is the group theory, machine-checked. Instantiating ObstructionDuality for the Ptolemy setting additionally needs the identification μ₃ ≅ ZMod 3 and the relative class in H²(M, ∂M; ℤ/3) rather than H²(Γ; μ₃); that relative step is verified computationally, not here — negating the cocycle acts on the Ptolemy variety as u ↦ u² (computations/ptolemy/ptolemy_duality_galois_check.py, classes 1/2/3).

        theorem QOU.Eightfold.obs_neg_of_inv {Z : Type u_4} [Group Z] (e : Z ≃* Multiplicative (ZMod 3)) (z : Z) :
        Multiplicative.toAdd (e z⁻¹) = -Multiplicative.toAdd (e z)

        The μ₃ ≅ ZMod 3 bridge. cocycle_dual says the dual lift's cocycle is the inverse; obs_dual says the class negates. Transporting along an isomorphism of the centre with Multiplicative (ZMod 3) is what turns one into the other — inversion in the group is negation in ZMod 3.

        With this, the algebraic chain behind obs_dual is complete: inverse-transpose is a homomorphism (cocycle_map), it inverts the centre (cocycle_dual), and inversion of a μ₃ value is negation of a ZMod 3 value (here). What is still missing for a genuine instance is only the passage from a cocycle to its cohomology class — H² machinery — and the relative-vs-absolute step, which is checked computationally instead.
