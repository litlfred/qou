    QOU.Eightfold.MirrorSwapCompatibility — the Borromean tension is not a contradiction #

    AmphichiralHolonomyIsSelfDual left one question open, and it decided whether route E closes on every knot of the particle dictionary or survives on one:

    • the classical literature calls the Borromean rings amphichiral;
    • observations/time-reversal-energy-barrier.md:17 asserts the opposite for the corpus's 𝓑₃ — "the Milnor triple linking number μ̄(123) = ±1 distinguishes the Borromean link 𝓑₃ from its mirror 𝓑₃^σ. No Reidemeister sequence connects them."

    Both are true, of different objects, and this file is why.

    The mechanism #

    μ̄(123) is negated by two independent operations:

    • mirroring — μ̄(σL) = −μ̄(L);
    • transposing two components — μ̄ of length three is cyclically symmetric and antisymmetric under a transposition, μ̄(123) = −μ̄(213).

    So the composite mirror ∘ swap preserves it (negBoth_comp_fixes), and an invariant that both operations negate is blind to the difference between L and mirror (swap L) (cannot_distinguish_mirror_swap). That is exactly how the Borromean rings are amphichiral: the mirror image is isotopic to the original after exchanging two components.

    What this settles #

    The corpus's claim is correct for the ordered, oriented link — with the labels fixed, μ̄ does distinguish 𝓑₃ from 𝓑₃^σ. It does not contradict amphichirality of the unordered link, nor of the complement.

    Route E's object is the complement's holonomy, a representation of π₁(S³∖𝓑₃), which does not see component labels. The complement therefore admits an orientation-reversing self-homeomorphism, and by Mostow rigidity (already used in the corpus — swampland-island-of-consistency.md:11, approach-to-exceptional-divisor.md:11) the discrete faithful representation is unique up to conjugation and complex conjugation. Hence ρ̄_geom ≅ ρ_geom ∘ φ ≅ ρ_geom — self-dual, and AmphichiralHolonomyIsSelfDual prunes it.

    So route E is closed on all three knots of the dictionary — for geometric holonomies. 4₁ by amphichirality, 3₁ for having no hyperbolic holonomy at all, 𝓑₃ by this argument. Together with SymSquareHolonomyIsRealType, which prunes every Sym²-induced representation regardless of knot, what survives is only a genuinely non-geometric PGL(3,ℂ) representation — a Fock–Goncharov deformation away from the PGL(2) locus.

    That is a route, not a construction. Nobody has built one.

    Scope #

    Scope-(a). This file proves the compatibility: that an invariant negated by two operations cannot separate a thing from its double image. It does not prove the Borromean rings are amphichiral — that is the classical fact, cited — and it does not prove Mostow rigidity, which the corpus already cites.

    What it removes is the appearance of a contradiction between the corpus and the literature. There was none: the two statements quantify over different objects, and reading the corpus's ordered-link claim as a claim about the complement is the ambient-vs-instance conflation this arc keeps correcting.

    Base ring (§7c) #

    A commutative ring with negation, an involution, and nothing else. No ordering, no archimedean content.
