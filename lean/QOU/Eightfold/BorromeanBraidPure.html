    QOU.Eightfold.BorromeanBraidPure — the Borromean braid is pure, and no half-twist is #

    Bean qou-xhih (Front 1 of qou-icj2), companion to computations/probes/bring_monodromy_full_twist_probe.py and to PR #6449's bring_monodromy_braid_lift_probe.py.

    What is formalised #

    Words in the signed Artin generators σ₁^{±1}, σ₂^{±1} of B₃, and two B₃-invariants of such words:

    • permOfWord — the image in S₃ = Equiv.Perm (Fin 3); a generator acts as the adjacent transposition, whatever its sign (a transposition is its own inverse, so the sign is invisible in S₃).
    • expSum — the exponent sum, +1 per positive letter, −1 per negative.

    Both are shown to be invariant under the defining relations of B₃ (BraidStep: free cancellation g g⁻¹ → 1 and the braid relation σ₁σ₂σ₁ = σ₂σ₁σ₂, in any context), hence constant on equivalence classes Relation.EqvGen BraidStep. That is exactly what makes a statement about a word a statement about the braid it represents.

    Then, for the Borromean word (σ₁ σ₂⁻¹)³:

    • borromean_perm_eq_one — its S₃ image is the identity, so the Borromean braid is pure: every word equivalent to it has trivial image (borromean_equiv_perm_eq_one).
    • borromean_expSum — its exponent sum is 0, while any conjugate of a single generator has exponent sum ±1 (expSum_conj_gen); therefore no half-twist (conjugate of a generator) is equivalent to the Borromean word (borromean_not_halfTwist). In particular none of the six local monodromy lifts of the Bring colour cubic — each a half-twist — is the Borromean braid; the Borromean braid arises only as a product of them.

    What is NOT formalised #

    B₃ itself is not constructed as a group here; the results are about words modulo the presentation's relations, which is the same information. Nothing here is about the Bring fibre: the six lifts being half-twists is the probe's numerical result, consumed by the reader, not proved in Lean. No sorry.
