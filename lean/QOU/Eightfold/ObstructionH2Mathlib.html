    Agreement with Mathlib's groupCohomology #

    ObstructionH2 defines Z2/B2/H2 by transcribing Mathlib's IsCocycle₂ / IsCoboundary₂ and specialising to the trivial action, because importing Mathlib.RepresentationTheory.Homological.GroupCohomology.LowDegree needed 79 further oleans the restored cache did not carry. Those 79 are now built (scripts/build-missing-mathlib-oleans.sh, 79/79, no failures), so the transcription is discharged here: the two objects are literally EQUAL, and nothing downstream rests on "agrees by transcription" any more.

    What the proofs came to #

    Almost nothing, which is the good outcome — the definitions line up without translation. mem_cocycles₂_iff reduces membership to f (g*h, j) + f (g, h) = A.ρ g (f (h, j)) + f (g, h*j), and Rep.ofDistribMulAction_ρ_apply_apply rewrites A.ρ g a to g • a, which the trivial action collapses to a. That is the carrier of Z2 verbatim, so the cocycle half closes by Iff.rfl. The coboundary half is the same fact one level down: coboundaries₂ is the range of d₁₂, which under a trivial ρ is x g.2 - x (g.1 * g.2) + x g.1 — B2's existential, transposed between a pointwise statement and a funext.

    Two things the elaborator required, neither mathematical:

    • Γ : Type, not Type*. Rep k G puts k and G in the same universe, and ZMod 3 is in Type 0. Harmless for the application — the fundamental group of a 3-manifold is in Type — but it does mean these two lemmas are less general than the ObstructionH2 results they connect, which stay universe-polymorphic.
    • noncomputable, because Rep.ofDistribMulAction is.

    Mathlib's bridges are stated for Rep.ofDistribMulAction, a representation induced from a DistribMulAction, rather than for Rep.trivial. That is the better fit here: Z2 is IsCocycle₂ under a trivial action, so using the induced representation makes the two sides definitionally the same object instead of merely isomorphic ones.
