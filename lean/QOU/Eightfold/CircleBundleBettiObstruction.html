    QOU.Eightfold.CircleBundleBettiObstruction — the QOU contact 3-manifold is not S³ #

    The question this answers #

    The framework uses an ambient S³ in two distinct places and calls both "the arena":

    • def:reeb-bundle (mass-theory/reeb-bundle.md) — the link of the exceptional divisor of a blow-up: a circle bundle over 𝒮_p ≅ ℂP¹ ≅ S², i.e. the Hopf fibration, i.e. the coset projection SU(2) → SU(2)/U(1);
    • rem:brings-qou-4tuple (brings-surface/brings-qou-4tuple.md, item 1) — the QOU's own CR manifold M_S = {(p,η) ∈ T*S : |η| = 1}, the unit circle bundle over Bring's surface, carrying the contact form Θ = λ|_{M_S} restricted from the Liouville form. This is the manifold on which Θ ∧ dΘ — the natural degree-3 volume form the owner's question asks after — actually lives.

    Both are circle bundles over a closed orientable surface. They are not the same 3-manifold, and this file proves it from the framework's own Descartes axiom χ(𝒮) = −6 — no extra input.

    The theorem #

    descartes_forces_b1_pos — a circle bundle whose base has Euler characteristic −6 has total space with first Betti number at least 8, whatever its Euler number. Since b₁(S³) = 0, not_sphere_of_descartes follows: the QOU contact manifold is not S³.

    The consequence for the corpus: the Alexander–Markov ambient S³, the Reeb-bundle S³ over the exceptional divisor, and the QOU contact manifold M_S are three objects, not one. Only the first two can be S³; M_S provably cannot. Any identification of "the arena" with "the manifold carrying Θ ∧ dΘ" is therefore false as stated, and must be read locally (at a blow-up point) rather than globally.

    The model, and what is cited rather than derived #

    For an oriented circle bundle S¹ → M → Σ_g over a closed orientable surface of genus g with Euler number e, the Gysin sequence with ℚ coefficients gives

    0 → H¹(Σ_g;ℚ) → H¹(M;ℚ) → H⁰(Σ_g;ℚ) --∪e--> H²(Σ_g;ℚ)

    so b₁(M) = 2g when e ≠ 0 and b₁(M) = 2g + 1 when e = 0 (then M ≅ Σ_g × S¹). That is totalB1 below: the count is the model, cited standard algebraic topology, exactly as Sym2GateFSTrichotomy.sym2Singlets carries the Frobenius–Schur singlet count. Mathlib has no circle-bundle Gysin sequence to derive it from.

    What is not cited is the conclusion: the case analysis, the recovery of the genus from the Euler characteristic, and the strict positivity are proved here. Note in particular that the e = 0 branch is not a special case to be waved away — it gives a larger b₁, so the obstruction holds without any hypothesis on the Euler number at all.

    Base ring (§7c) #

    Integer arithmetic only. No ring parameter, no ordering beyond ℤ/ℕ, no archimedean content.

          The exclusion. The QOU contact manifold — the unit circle bundle over Bring's surface, whose Euler characteristic the Descartes condition fixes at −6 — is not S³.

          5. The two bundles are different — one can be S³, the other cannot #

          def:reeb-bundle sits over the exceptional divisor 𝒮_p ≅ ℂP¹, genus 0; the link of a (−1)-curve has Euler number −1. rem:brings-qou-4tuple sits over Bring's surface, genus 4. The first is Betti-compatible with S³; the second is not. So the corpus's two "arenas" are provably distinct 3-manifolds, and the recorded contradiction between rem:markov-trace-ambient-s3 and docs/audits/2026-06-28-ed-ambient-s3-gauge-embedding-wlambda-scope.md §(i) is a conflation of two objects under one glyph, not a disagreement about one.
