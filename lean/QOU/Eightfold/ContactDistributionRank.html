    QOU.Eightfold.ContactDistributionRank — the contact volume form gives a #

    complex rank-n object, and rank 3 forces dim M = 7

    Bean qou-xlw1, epic qou-8lxd. Companion to QOU.QuantumObservableUniverse.VolumeFormDegree (the categorical side) and QOU.Eightfold.CircleBundleBettiObstruction (which rules the corpus's contact manifold out of being S³).

    The question #

    The owner asked whether the ambient S³ of the Markov closure "has a natural volume form" — whether a restriction of a tetraplectic or contact structure supplies the degree-3 form the eightfold way needs. The corpus does carry such a form: Θ ∧ dΘ appears by name in four blocks, and brings-qou-4tuple builds it from the canonical Liouville form on T*S, so it is natural rather than postulated. It is nonetheless prose tier — zero definition, zero Lean, and no block connects it to Λ³ (2026-09-04-where-the-ambient-s3-comes-from).

    This file supplies the arithmetic that decides the question, and the answer is sharper than a yes or a no.

    Why the contact route is not the tangent-bundle route #

    Every previous S³ attempt died on Frobenius–Schur type: the tangent bundle of a real 3-manifold is a real rank-3 bundle, so it is self-dual, and QOU.Eightfold.Sym2GateFSTrichotomy.real_triple_fails_gate excludes it. Four independent routes have now landed there — 𝔰𝔲(2) = Im ℍ, the tetraplectic Sp(1)ⁿ, the affine-W compactification, and Ad(A).

    The contact distribution is a different object and it escapes that argument. On a contact M^{2n+1} with contact form α, the distribution ξ = ker α has real rank 2n, dα|_ξ is symplectic, and a compatible J makes ξ a complex bundle of complex rank n. A complex bundle is not self-conjugate by construction, so the type obstruction does not apply to it. This is the first S³-adjacent route that survives it.

    What it does not survive is the arithmetic below.

    The arithmetic, and what it decides #

    ξ is complex rank n = (dim M − 1)/2. So the eightfold's Λ³_ℂ ξ ≅ 𝟙 — the trivial-canonical-bundle / Sasaki–Einstein condition — needs n = 3, hence

    dim M = 7, and no other dimension.

    Read against the corpus's own candidates that is decisive, and negative for every one of them:

    manifold dim n complex rank of ξ supplies W?
    any S³ — S³_AM, S³_Reeb, SU(2) 3 1 1 (a complex line) no
    M_S under the 3-dimensional reading 3 1 1 no
    M_S under the rank-4 ker Θ reading 5 2 2 no
    what would be needed 7 3 3 yes

    So the contact structure on any three-dimensional manifold gives a complex line, not a three-dimensional object — and that is a statement about the dimension, independent of which S³ is meant, which is what makes it robust to the three-way S³ ambiguity the corpus carries.

    The degree-1 accident, again. At n = 1 the canonical bundle Λ¹_ℂ ξ is ξ itself, exactly as Λ^{N-1}W = W iff N = 2 in VolumeFormDegree. Both files are recording the same shape: the low case is degenerate because the exterior power collapses onto its argument, and it is that collapse — not a property of volume forms — that the corpus has been reading as structure.

    Where it points. dim M = 7 is where qou-8p1f's three-forms-with-an-open- orbit case lives (G₂), and qou-uq5x independently found S⁶ = G₂/SU(3) viable with complex 𝟑 isotropy. Two legs reached from opposite directions — contact/CR and octonionic — arrive at 7 and at a complex 3-dimensional object. That convergence is a lead, and is asserted nowhere below.

    What is proved here, and what is not #

    Proved (unconditional integer arithmetic): the whole table — the odd-dimension parametrisation, that the complex rank determines the dimension and conversely, that complex rank 3 holds exactly at dim M = 7, that no 3-dimensional contact manifold gives complex rank 3, and the degree-1 collapse.

    Not proved, and asserted nowhere below:

    • that a compatible J exists on ker Θ for the corpus's Θ. A contact distribution admits one (the space of compatible J is contractible and non-empty), but that is a cited fact about contact geometry, not something stated here, and nothing in the corpus attaches a J to ker Θ.
    • that Λ^n_ℂ ξ ≅ 𝟙 holds for any manifold in the corpus. Triviality of the canonical bundle is a genuine geometric condition (Sasaki–Einstein), not a consequence of being contact. This file says which n it would require.
    • the identification of ξ with a fundamental object of the braided category. Mathlib has no exterior-power-of-an-object API and no differential forms on manifolds at all, so every declaration below is about ℕ.
    • anything about dim M for the corpus's M_S, which the corpus states inconsistently — 3-dimensional in one block, ker Θ of rank 4 (hence 5) in two others. The table above is stated for both readings precisely because that contradiction is unresolved and is the author's to settle.

    Base ring: none — integer arithmetic (§7c: no ℝ, no ring at all).

            The 3-Sasakian rung, and a second pair of "different threes" #

            The corpus carries a Bring-cone 3-Sasakian structure, which it itself calls "degenerate quaternionic-contact" (bring-cone-reeb-quaternionic-twist.md:4) — 4n + 3 at n = 0. Since dim M = 7 is 4n + 3 at n = 1, it is worth saying exactly how far that is from what the eightfold needs, and exactly which distribution the question is about. Both are one line of arithmetic, and the second is a trap of the same shape as degree_three_but_rank_one.
