    QOU.Eightfold.ObstructionH2 — the H² step, so the obstruction class is well defined #

    ObstructionClassForcesComplexType proves that a self-dual representation has trivial obstruction class, given ObstructionDuality's two hypotheses, and its CocycleModel section proves their content at the level of cocycles. One gap remained: a cocycle depends on the chosen lift, so "the obstruction class of ρ" is not yet well defined.

    This file closes that gap by constructing H²(Γ; ℤ/3) for the trivial action — the centre μ₃ is fixed under conjugation, so the action is trivial — and proving

    • twist_mem_B2 — changing the lift by t shifts the cocycle by t g + t h - t (g*h), which is a coboundary. So the class does not depend on the lift. This is the H² step.
    • obs_dual_holds — negating a cocycle negates its class.
    • obs_congr_holds — cohomologous cocycles have equal classes.
    • cls_eq_zero_of_cohomologous_neg — unconditionally: a cocycle cohomologous to its own negation has trivial class. No hypotheses, no instance to supply.

    eq_zero_of_eq_neg_module generalises the ZMod 3 arithmetic to any ZMod 3-module, which is needed because H²(M, ∂M; ℤ/3) is (ZMod 3)³ for L6a4 — not ZMod 3.

    Why this is not Mathlib's groupCohomology.H2 #

    Mathlib has the general machinery, and the definitions here are transcribed from it — IsCocycle₂ and IsCoboundary₂ in Mathlib/RepresentationTheory/Homological/GroupCohomology/LowDegree.lean, specialised to the trivial action (g • a = a). It is not imported: that module's transitive cone needs 79 further Mathlib oleans (homological algebra and category theory) which the restored cache does not carry, and building them is hours of compute against 2.6 GB of free disk. The trivial-action case needs none of it — cocycles and coboundaries are submodules of Γ × Γ → ZMod 3 and the quotient is a Submodule quotient.

    That gap is now CLOSED. QOU.Eightfold.ObstructionH2Mathlib proves Z2 Γ = cocycles₂ (triv3 Γ) and B2 Γ = coboundaries₂ (triv3 Γ) — literal equality, not isomorphism — so these are no longer merely "our" objects agreeing by transcription. The 79 oleans were built (scripts/build-missing-mathlib-oleans.sh, 79/79, no failures); the measured cost was ~40 minutes and 53 MB, not the "hours against 2.6 GB of free disk" this file first claimed. The disk half of that claim was simply wrong.

    The agreement lemmas need Γ : Type rather than Type*, because Rep k G puts k and G in the same universe and ZMod 3 is in Type 0. The results in THIS file stay universe-polymorphic; only the bridge to Mathlib is specialised.

    Scope #

    This is group cohomology of Γ. The Ptolemy obstruction is the relative class in H²(M, ∂M; ℤ/3); that step is verified computationally, not here — negating the cocycle acts on the Ptolemy variety as u ↦ u² (computations/ptolemy/ptolemy_duality_galois_check.py, classes 1/2/3).
