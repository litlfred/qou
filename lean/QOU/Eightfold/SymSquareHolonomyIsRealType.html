    QOU.Eightfold.SymSquareHolonomyIsRealType — the induced PGL(3) rep is real type #

    Route E asks for a three-dimensional object of complex Frobenius–Schur type (Sym2GateFSTrichotomy.gate_forces_complex_of_dim_three). There is one obvious way to get a three-dimensional representation out of a hyperbolic knot complement, and this file shows it is the wrong type, for every knot at once.

    The construction, and why it fails #

    A hyperbolic structure supplies ρ₂ : π₁(S³∖K) → SL(2,ℂ). The free way to reach three dimensions is to post-compose with the symmetric square, Sym² : SL(2,ℂ) → SL(3,ℂ) — the adjoint representation, dim Sym²(V₂) = 3.

    That composite is self-dual, hence real or quaternionic, hence excluded:

    • every SL(2) representation is self-dual, because the invariant alternating form gives V ≅ V* (carried as the hypothesis SelfDualSource);
    • Sym² preserves self-duality: V ≅ V* gives Sym² V ≅ Sym²(V*) ≅ (Sym² V)* (symSquare_selfDual_of_selfDual);
    • a self-dual three-dimensional candidate fails the Sym² gate (Sym2GateFSTrichotomy, via the even-dimension argument).

    So the induced object is real type and real_triple_fails_gate disposes of it in one line. This does not depend on the knot — not on hyperbolicity, not on amphichirality, not on the Milnor invariant. It is a property of the map SL(2) → SL(3).

    What this leaves of route E #

    Route E is not refuted; its cheap construction is. A surviving ρ₃ must lie in the PGL(3,ℂ) character variety away from the image of PGL(2,ℂ) — a genuine higher-Teichmüller deformation (Fock–Goncharov coordinates, the Garoufalidis–Goerner–Zickert gluing equations of library/arxiv-1207.6711v1), not the composite above. That is a sharper obligation than "exhibit ρ₃ with ρ₃ ≇ ρ̄₃": it says where to look and, more usefully, where not to.

    It also subsumes the companion AmphichiralHolonomyIsSelfDual for induced representations: that file prunes 4₁ by amphichirality; this one prunes the Sym²-induced representation of every knot, 4₁, 3₁ and 𝓑₃ alike. The companion still does work the moment a non-induced ρ₃ appears, because the mirror argument does not care how ρ₃ was built.

    Honest scope #

    Scope-(a). Two hypotheses are carried rather than derived, and named so a reader can see the borrow: SelfDualSource (every source-group representation is self-dual — the SL(2) symplectic form) and the FSType identification of the resulting self-duality. Neither is proved here; both are standard, and the second is deliberately not collapsed into the first, since ρ ≅ ρ* and a Frobenius–Schur label are different assertions.

    Base ring (§7c) #

    No ring and no archimedean content: an abstract duality and an equivalence.
