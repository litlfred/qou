    Products of degree-1 elements vanish in the indecomposable quotient #

    Bean qou-ox7v, settled — and it kills route B (qou-wnhk) of epic qou-2q85.

    The question this settles #

    Route B proposed: build a depth-1 stratum ℋ_q^(1,K), then use the depth-additivity of the q-shuffle (ℋ_q^(n₁) *_q ℋ_q^(n₂) ⊆ ℋ_q^(n₁+n₂)) to square it into ℋ_q^(2,K), where the hyperbolic volume lives.

    I then raised a worry (roast R1) that the shuffle filtration and the K-theory weight tower "cannot match as graded algebras", on a rank count. That worry was wrong and is retracted. They match. The mismatch was mine: I compared the whole weight-2 graded piece against K_3^ind, which is only one summand of it.

    The corpus states the resolution itself, at appendix-surreals/k-theory-period-rosetta.md:22:

    K_*^ind denotes the indecomposable part of higher K-theory, modulo the image of products of lower K-groups.

    So the weight-2 piece splits into a decomposable part — products of weight-1 elements, log · log — and an indecomposable part, which is what K_3^ind, hence Bloch–Wigner, hence the volume, is.

    The shuffle-square of a depth-1 object lands in the decomposable part, which is exactly what the indecomposable quotient discards. Route B cannot reach the volume by squaring — not because the gradings disagree, but because squaring produces precisely the summand the target is defined to exclude.

    What is proved here #

    The invariant behind that sentence, in the generality it actually holds: for any bilinear mul : A₁ →ₗ A₁ →ₗ A₂, every product mul a b dies in A₂ ⧸ decomposable mul. Nothing about knots, periods or q is used — which is the point. The obstruction is not special to this corpus; it is what "indecomposable" means.

    Scope #

    (b), a structural exclusion, with the invariant exhibited rather than asserted — but the exclusion is of squaring as a route to an indecomposable target, not of route B's ingredients. Building ℋ_q^(1,K) may still be worth doing for its own sake; what is excluded is reaching the volume from it by multiplication.

    It says nothing about the coproduct route (A, qou-jopk): Δ_q's 2 = 2+0 split is not a product of two degree-1 elements, so this theorem does not touch it.

      Route A (qou-jopk): the degree-0 action descends — and is trivial #

      The surviving route of epic qou-2q85 proposed that the depth-0 interface layer acts on the depth-2 volume stratum rather than mapping into it. Two results settle it, and they point opposite ways.

      First, a correction to my own framing of the route. I described the mechanism as the coproduct's 2 = 2 + 0 split. That is wrong: a coproduct decomposes an element, it does not let a degree-0 element act on a degree-2 one. The action comes from the product, via 0 + 2 = 2 — the same shuffle-additivity as everywhere else. ℋ^(0) is a subalgebra and ℋ^(2) is a module over it, automatically, in any filtered algebra.

      Second, the positive half. That action is well defined on the volume stratum: if degree-0 times degree-1 stays degree-1, then multiplying a decomposable by a degree-0 element leaves it decomposable, so the action descends to the indecomposable quotient. smul_mem_decomposable and mkQ_smul_comm below.

      Third, the negative half, from the corpus's own table. def:hq-bi-filtration's low-(n,K) grid gives ℋ_q^(0,K) explicitly: e_{(2,2)} for the figure-eight, e_{(3)} ± e_{(1,1,1)} for the trefoil, scalars for the unknot and the Bring input. So at each tabulated cell the depth-0 stratum is spanned by the central idempotent — which is the unit of the localized algebra e_K ℋ_q e_K, and ℋ_q^(2,K) is by definition the e_K-isotypic piece. The unit acts as the identity (unit_smul_eq_self). The action exists, descends, and does nothing.

      What escapes. ⋈_K is depth 0 but sits at a different cell — the grid row gives it in H_6(q) on an EW braid in B_6, not at the cell of the knot whose volume is in question. A non-trivial action would therefore have to come from the coproduct's cell-merging K₁ · K₂ = K, which moves the vertical axis. That is a different structure from the one route A proposed, and this file does not touch it.
