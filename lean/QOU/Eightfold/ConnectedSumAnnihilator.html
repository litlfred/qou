    Connected sum does not give a map of recurrence ideals #

    Bean qou-zhep, the connected-sum probe, formalize-first.

    The proposed first target was a map

    A_{K₁} × A_{K₂} → A_{K₁ # K₂}

    on recurrence ideals in the quantum torus, justified by the classical fact that the coloured Jones is multiplicative under connected sum, J_{K₁ # K₂}(N) = J_{K₁}(N) · J_{K₂}(N). I called this "the known-easy prototype". This file establishes that the naive form of that map does not exist, before any search was run — which is what the formalize-first rule asks for.

    Why it fails, in one line #

    The quantum torus acts by two operators. On a pointwise product of colour towers they behave completely differently:

    • M (diagonal multiplication by q^n) passes to one factor: M(f·g) = (M f)·g. It is "half" multiplicative.
    • L (the colour shift) is fully multiplicative: L(f·g) = (L f)·(L g). It shifts both factors at once.

    So an operator built from L does not see the two towers as a tensor product — it sees their pointwise product, and a relation satisfied by one factor is destroyed by the other. annihilator_not_monotone exhibits this with an explicit witness.

    What this does and does not exclude #

    Scope-(b) for the naive map (an AGENTS.md structural exclusion, with the invariant exhibited rather than asserted): there is no map sending an annihilator of f to an annihilator of f·g, because the containment A_f ⊆ A_{f·g} already fails on a first-order operator over ℚ.

    Scope-(a) at most for the programme. It does not exclude a connected-sum construction. The classical route is the Hadamard product / creative telescoping closure property: the pointwise product of two holonomic sequences is holonomic, with an annihilator of order at most n₁ · n₂. That is a construction on operators, not a map of ideals, and it raises the order multiplicatively — which is the honest shape of the connected-sum case and the reason it is not the cheap prototype I called it. -- Ref: [manuscript] bean qou-zhep -- Ref: [manuscript] QOU.Machinery.QuantumTorusAJ (the act2 function model)

          source
          theorem QOU.Eightfold.ConnectedSumProbe.annihilator_not_monotone :
          ∃ (f : ℕ → ℚ) (g : ℕ → ℚ) (a₀ : ℚ) (a₁ : ℚ), annihilates a₀ a₁ f ∧ ¬annihilates a₀ a₁ fun (n : ℕ) => f n * g n

          The recurrence ideal is not monotone under pointwise multiplication.

          There are towers f, g and a first-order operator P with P ∈ A_f and P ∉ A_{f·g}. Concretely f ≡ 1, g = 2^n, P = L − 1: the residual is 2^n, nonzero at every colour.

          Since J_{K₁ # K₂} = J_{K₁} · J_{K₂} pointwise, this is exactly the statement that a map A_{K₁} × A_{K₂} → A_{K₁ # K₂} cannot be "carry the annihilator across".
