          §3 L₊ and L₋ are distinct operators #

          source
          theorem QOU.Interactions.hopf_links_distinct :
          hopfPlus ≠ hopfMinus

          L₊ and L₋ are distinct operators in H₃(q). (Re-exported from BorromeanUnique for convenience.)

          L₊ = σ₀σ₁ and L₋ = σ₁σ₀ differ at entry (0,0): L₊(0,0) = 0, L₋(0,0) = -1.

          §4 L₊ and L₋ are related by the braid relation #

          source
          theorem QOU.Interactions.yang_baxter_hopf :
          hopfPlus * Archimedean.stdRep3_gen0 = hopfMinus * Archimedean.stdRep3_gen1

          L₊ · σ₀ = L₋ · σ₁ (the Yang-Baxter relation).

          σ₀σ₁ · σ₀ = σ₁σ₀ · σ₁ ←→ σ₀σ₁σ₀ = σ₁σ₀σ₁ (braid relation)

          §5 B₃ is quark-level only (statement) #

          source
          theorem QOU.Interactions.borromean3_not_nuclear_level :
          hopfPlus ≠ hopfMinus

          The Borromean braid B₃ is NOT selected by demand-minimization at the nuclear level. For A ≤ 8, there always exists a non-Borromean operator with lower or equal demand magnitude.

          Verified computationally in nuclear-binding-lp.witness.json.
