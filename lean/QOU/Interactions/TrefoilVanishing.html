    The e⁻ (trefoil) NF coefficient of B₃ is exactly zero #

    Proved by direct Hecke algebra computation over ℤ[q, q⁻¹].

    The computation: B₃ = (σ₀σ₁⁻¹)³ = (σ₀σ₁ − HA·σ₀)³

    Step 1: σ₀σ₁⁻¹ = σ₀σ₁ − HA·σ₀ (using σ₁⁻¹ = σ₁ − HA) NF: −HA·σ₀ + L₊ (no e⁻ term)

    Step 2: (σ₀σ₁⁻¹)² — expand and reduce via Hecke NF: (HA²−2)·γ + (−HA³+3HA)·σ₀ − HA·σ₁ + (2−HA²)·L₊ + L₋ (no e⁻ term)

    Step 3: (σ₀σ₁⁻¹)³ — multiply step 2 by step 1 and reduce NF: c_γ·γ + c_σ₀·σ₀ + c_σ₁·σ₁ + c_L₊·L₊ + c_L₋·L₋ + 0·e⁻ (e⁻ = 0)

    The vanishing is EXACT in ℤ[q, q⁻¹] — not numerical.

    Strategy: express B₃ as a specific linear combination of NF basis elements, then observe c_{e⁻} = 0. The key reductions that eliminate e⁻:

    • Each time σ₀σ₁σ₀ (= e⁻) appears, it's from the braid relation σ₁σ₀σ₁ → σ₀σ₁σ₀. But the anti-symmetric structure of σ₀σ₁⁻¹ = σ₀σ₁ − HA·σ₀ causes these contributions to cancel in pairs.

          Cross-term: −2HA·σ₀·L₊ = −2HA·σ₀²·σ₁ = −2HA·(HA·σ₀+1)·σ₁ = −2HA²·σ₀σ₁ − 2HA·σ₁ = −2HA²·L₊ − 2HA·σ₁ This doesn't produce e⁻.
