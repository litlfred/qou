    Bar parity of the electromagnetic coupling object #

    The EM coupling object is α_EM⁻¹ = q·[9]_q·[10]_q (bare; CS-tower dressed downstream). This file proves, over any field and sorry-free, the exact bar (q ↦ q⁻¹) decomposition that fills the coupling-level cell of the interaction-sector taxonomy (def:interaction-sector-taxonomy):

    • the quantum integer [n]_q is bar-even (invariant), with no nondegeneracy hypotheses — numerator and denominator flip sign together, and the degenerate cases agree on both sides under the x/0 = 0 convention;
    • therefore the bare coupling C(q) = q·[9]_q·[10]_q satisfies C(q) ± C(q⁻¹) = (q ± q⁻¹)·[9]_q·[10]_q — its bar-even part is the cosh channel (strong side) and its bar-odd part the sinh channel (weak side) of the same invariant factor B = [9]_q·[10]_q. The EM coupling is the full exponential mix e^λ·B, not a pure channel.

    Observable level (added same day, bean qou-z2xz): α = C⁻¹ carries the conjugate exponential e^{−λ} and the Hartree scale α² the doubled e^{−2λ}; and under nondegeneracy (q² ≠ ±1, B ≠ 0 — true at the real anchor) the coupling is provably NEITHER bar-even NOR bar-odd. So the electromagnetic taxonomy cell is not merely unassigned — no pure-parity assignment exists. Numeric twin (proton pin, 60 dps): computations/probes/alpha_em_bar_parity_probe.py.

          The electromagnetic objects are provably NOT pure-parity #

          Under nondegeneracy — q² ≠ 1, q² ≠ −1, B ≠ 0, all of which hold at the real substrate anchor q₀ ≈ 1.11 — the coupling is neither bar-even nor bar-odd. So the electromagnetic cell of the taxonomy is not merely unassigned: no pure-parity assignment exists, unlike binding (pure even) and Q_β (pure odd).
