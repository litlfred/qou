    Q_β counit — internal bar-parity decomposition (NOT a binding double-count) #

    Correction (2026-06-16). An earlier version of this file claimed the bar-even part of the counit was "the channel #2346 books as binding," i.e. a binding/Q_β double-count. That was wrong. The #2346 witness habiro-exact-qbeta reports Q_A = Im 𝔙 = 0.78364580… MeV, which equals the full counit |ε(Δ)|·𝔈₀ to 2·10⁻¹⁶ (branch A): their Im 𝔙 is the whole counit value, not the bar-odd part alone. So there is no double-count — the two framings agree, and the split below is an internal decomposition of the counit, distinct from #2346's binding/Q_β (Re/Im of the single-knot confinement defect) split.

    The counit on the nucleon difference, ε(Δ) = h²/2 − h (h = q − q⁻¹), decomposes under the bar-involution h ↦ −h into a bar-even h²/2 and a bar-odd −h part. These lemmas record that algebra. They do not assign the bar-even part to any other observable.
