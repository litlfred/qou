    Verified Hecke NF Engine for H₃(q) #

    Complete multiplication table for H₃(q) in the NF basis {γ, σ₀, σ₁, L₊, L₋, e⁻}, encoded as structure constants.

    Each product bᵢ · bⱼ = Σ c_{ij}^k · bₖ is a specific linear combination with coefficients in ℤ[q, q⁻¹].

    The structure constants are verified against the 2×2 standard representation (nfToMatrix) plus trivial and alternating characters.

    Usage #

    To verify an NF computation:

    1. Express each step as hecke_mul a b where a, b are NF vectors
    2. Chain the multiplications
    3. Compare the result with the claimed NF

    • [jones1987] https://doi.org/10.2307/1971403

                        §5 Proton NF via Hecke engine #

                        The proton NF: B₃ · σ₀ · (σ₀ − ½HA) · (σ₁ − ½HA).

                        Since ½HA is not in ℤ[q,q⁻¹], we compute 4×proton: 4×proton = B₃ · σ₀ · (2σ₀ − HA) · (2σ₁ − HA).
