    QOU.Interactions.nucleon_ordering_factorization #

    Proposition for content block prop:nucleon-ordering-factorization: For a nuclear braid 𝒩(Z, N) in H_A(q) with nucleon ordering π = (t₁, …, t_A), the Markov trace decomposes by Wedderburn channel:

    tr_M(𝒩) = W_sym · S(π) + W_std · (z · a_π + b_π) + W_alt · A(π),

    with S(π) = ∏ₖ(q + d_k) and A(π) = ∏ₖ(−q⁻¹ + d_k) products of commuting scalars (hence ordering-independent), and (a_π, b_π) a 2 × 2 matrix product over the standard representation.

    Why the repair is neither of the two obvious options #

    Restoring the sorry undoes what that commit was for — reducing sorries — and the projection is AGENTS.md §3b-cond item 5. Disposition 3 (name the claim over parameters, assert nothing) is neither: no sorry, no projection, and no class whose instance chooses the objects the claim is about.

    With trM_N and the nine channel quantities as class fields the instance picked all of them, so a [NucleonOrderingFactorizationContext R] binder assumed nothing about anything a caller brought. As parameters the claim constrains the caller's data, and it becomes something other results can be proved from — which is what trace_ordering_independent below does.

    What is proved here, and what is not #

    • scalar_channel_ordering_independent — sub-claim (i), sorry-free: a product of commuting scalars is permutation-invariant, so the symmetric and alternating channel values do not depend on the nucleon ordering.
    • trace_ordering_independent — derived: two orderings related by a permutation, agreeing on the standard channel, give the same Markov trace. This is the manuscript's ordering-independence conclusion, and it is obtained from the named decomposition rather than assumed.
    • not_nucleonOrderingFactorization_of_free_data — the decomposition is refutable at freely-chosen data, which is why a sorry over it would read as "true, not yet proved" about something false.

    Unencoded, stated rather than implied: the master factorisation itself — the Wedderburn block decomposition of H_3(q) (sym ⊕ std ⊕ alt) evaluated on the nuclear braid word — needs the Hecke representation-theory and Markov-trace machinery, which is in computations/ (GS reduction + Hoefsmit) and not in Lean. Nothing here constructs tr_M, the Wedderburn weights, or the standard-representation matrix product; S(π) = ∏ₖ(q + d_k) and A(π) = ∏ₖ(−q⁻¹ + d_k) are not tied to q either.

    Base ring (§7c). Generic [CommRing R]: the identity is a sum of products with no ordering, radical or Real.* construct anywhere, so ℝ buys nothing. Same family as bean qou-mtcx.

Here ds/ds' are the per-nucleon channel data and f is the scalar map (f d = q + d for S, f d = −q⁻¹ + d for A).

    Proved by List.Perm.prod_eq — real content, not a projection.

    §7c: stated over a generic commutative monoid. Permutation-invariance of a product uses no ordering, no radical and no Real.* construct, so ℝ bought nothing here; the channel data and the scalar map may even live in different types. The archimedean instance is recovered at α := ℝ, M := ℝ.

    Prop

    tr_M(𝒩) = W_sym · S(π) + W_std · (z · a_π + b_π) + W_alt · A(π).
