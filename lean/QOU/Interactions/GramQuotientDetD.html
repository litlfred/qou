    QOU.Interactions.GramQuotientDetD #

    The convention-D successor to gramMatrix_det_ne_zero: the canonical H_3(q) Gram (the bilinear pairing tr_M(ab)) has rank EXACTLY 5 — corank exactly 1, radical exactly span(gramRadicalD q) — for every real q, with an explicit, strictly negative quotient determinant.

    Stated as "the induced form on H_3(q)/rad is nondegenerate" this would be a tautology — a form is always nondegenerate on the quotient by its own radical. The content is that the leading 5×5 minor is nonzero for every real q, so the rank never drops below 5 (the complex roots of the numerator are where it would); together with gramMatrixD_det_eq_zero that pins the radical to the single direction gramRadicalD q at every real q.

    The gap this closes #

    GramMatrixH3Symbolic.gramMatrix_det_ne_zero is a convention-C result (z = √q/(q+1), excised 2026-08-09 / re-affirmed 2026-08-15). In the canonical convention D (z = q³/(q²+1)) the 6×6 Gram is exactly singular for every q — gramMatrixD_det_eq_zero — because z_D is a root of the determinant factor (q²+1)z − q³ (markovWeightD_root). So the C theorem has no D analogue at the 6×6 level, and the positive-definiteness reading built on it fails outright (gramMatrixD_not_posDef). Bean qou-cds2 names the replacement as its last open Lean item: what survives is the form on the quotient by the radical.

    The statement #

    Under D the three-strand q-antisymmetriser gramRadicalD q = (−q³, q², q², −q, −q, 1) lies in the radical (gramMatrixD_mulVec_radical). Its last coordinate is 1 (gramRadicalD_last), nonzero for every q including q = 0, so the basis vectors e_0, …, e_4 project to a basis of the quotient by that direction, and the form in that complement basis is the leading 5×5 principal minor of the Gram — gramQuotientD q, defined here as a Matrix.submatrix of gramMatrixD q along Fin.castSucc (never re-typed). Its determinant is

    det( G_D | H_3/rad ) = −(q⁴+1)(q²−q+1)⁴(q²+q+1)⁴ / (q²+1)¹⁰

    (gramQuotientD_det). Every factor is real-rootless — q⁴+1 > 0, q²−q+1 = (q−½)²+¾ > 0, q²+q+1 = (q+½)²+¾ > 0, (q²+1)¹⁰ > 0 — so the determinant is strictly negative for every real q (gramQuotientD_det_neg), hence nonzero (gramQuotientD_det_ne_zero), with no hypothesis on q at all. This is the same strength as the C theorem (1 < u there), stated about the quotient instead of the whole algebra: rank exactly 5, radical exactly span(gramRadicalD q).

    The negative sign is not incidental: the quotient of the bilinear pairing tr_M(ab) is indefinite (signature (4,1) at q₀), consistent with the negative eigenvalue the D-frame spectrum carries there (gram-matrix-dual.witness.json, and the docstring of gramMatrixD_not_posDef). Any D-frame successor to the C-frame positive-definiteness reading of THIS pairing must therefore say "nondegenerate", never "positive". That is a statement about the bilinear form only: the corpus's *-pairing tr_M(ab*) (L₊ ↔ L₋ in one slot) has a positive-definite quotient under D (QOU.QBeta.GramSignature, post-retraction: C (6,0,0), D (5,0,1)) — a different form on the same algebra, not a contradiction.

    What is basis-dependent and what is not #

    The VALUE above is stated in the complement basis e_0, …, e_4. A different complement of the radical changes it by the square of a change-of-basis determinant; the NONVANISHING is basis-independent, and that — not the value — is what replaces gramMatrix_det_ne_zero. Quote the closed form only together with its basis.

    Proof route #

    Matrix.det_fin_five (QOU.Shared.DetFin, the explicit 120-term Leibniz formula) expands the determinant; the entries are unfolded from gramEntryD by simp (every leading-block denominator divides (q²+1)²); field_simp clears the (q²+1)-powers against the polynomial right-hand side, and ring closes the degree-20 identity — within the default heartbeat budget (measured: the file compiles at maxHeartbeats 200000; no raise is needed) —

    (q²+1)¹⁰ · det = −q²⁰ − 4q¹⁸ − 11q¹⁶ − 20q¹⁴ − 29q¹² − 32q¹⁰ − 29q⁸ − 20q⁶ − 11q⁴ − 4q² − 1 = −(q⁴+1)(q⁴+q²+1)⁴ .

    The same closed form, the factorisation, the absence of real roots and the value at q₀ are reproduced independently, exactly over ℚ(q), by the probe computations/probes/gram_h3_quotient_nondegeneracy_probe.py (witness computations/probes/gram-h3-quotient-nondegeneracy-probe.witness.json, fields quotient_form.det, numerator_factored, denominator_factored, det_at_q0).

    Base ring (AGENTS.md §7c) #

    The file inherits ℝ from gramMatrixD. That is acceptable here because the two corollaries (gramQuotientD_det_neg, gramQuotientD_det_ne_zero) genuinely use the order: real-rootlessness of the numerator is the archimedean content. The closed-form identity itself is a ℚ(q) fact; it would state over any field of characteristic zero, and does so the moment gramMatrixD does.
