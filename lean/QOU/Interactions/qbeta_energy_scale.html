    Q_β energy scale: per-Hecke-step energy and the counit closed form #

    Companion to QOU/Interactions/qbeta_counit_vs_trace.lean and the audit docs/audits/2026-06-16-q-beta-path-a-counit-transition-resolution.md.

    The β-decay Q-value is the framework's relation Q_β = ε(Δ) · 𝔈₀, where

    • ε(Δ) is the Frobenius counit of the neutron−proton normal-form difference (the net reduction content — see the companion file), and
    • 𝔈₀ is the energy of one Hecke reduction step σ² → hσ + 𝟙 (content/.../particle-interactions/energy-scale-planck.md): 𝔈₀ = m_e · c_q² · ℏ_q^E with information-speed-squared c_q² = 1/(q−1)² and action-per-tick ℏ_q^E = (q−1)/q.

    So Q_β = (net reduction content) × (energy per Hecke step) — a counit (transition) observable, categorically distinct from the Markov-trace (static loop / partition-function) mass. These lemmas pin the two algebraic facts the resolution rests on:

    1. 𝔈₀ factorises to m_e / (q(q−1));
    2. ε(Δ) · 𝔈₀ is the closed form m_e (q+1)(q²−2q−1) / (2q³).

    ℏ_q^E = (q−1)/q (energy-scale action-per-tick) is distinct from the Hecke parameter h = q − q⁻¹; they are kept separate here.

    Base ring (AGENTS.md §7c) #

    Generic [CommRing R] — not a field. Nothing here is archimedean (no ordering, no Real.*), and every division the narrative writes is a division by a quantity the old statements already had to assume nonzero. Each is therefore carried as an invertible datum instead, which is the exact CommRing analogue of that side condition:

    old field hypothesis §7c replacement
    q ≠ 0 q : Rˣ, so 1/q is the ring element ↑q⁻¹
    q - 1 ≠ 0 d : Rˣ with hd : ↑d = q − 1
    (2 : R) ≠ 0 [Invertible (2 : R)], so 1/2 is ⅟(2 : R)

    Over a field "nonzero" and "invertible" coincide, so no generality is lost and the old statements are recovered with Units.mk0 / invertibleOfNonzero.
