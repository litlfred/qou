    Proton and neutron NF witness verification #

    Proves the proton and neutron NF coefficients are correct by verifying them against the 2×2 matrix product in the faithful standard representation.

    Proton = B₃ · σ₀ · (σ₀ − ½HA) · (σ₁ − ½HA) Neutron = B₃ · (σ₀ − ½HA) · (σ₀ − ½HA) · (σ₁ − HA)

    Since ½ is not in ℤ[t,t⁻¹], we work with 4× the nucleon matrix (multiply each mixed crossing by 2).
