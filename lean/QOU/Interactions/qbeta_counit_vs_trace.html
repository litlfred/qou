    Q_β net-ansatz: counit vs Markov trace (algebraic core) #

    Formalises the algebraic identities behind the audit docs/audits/2026-06-16-q-beta-net-ansatz-path-a-counit-vs-trace.md.

    The N=0 neutron−proton Hecke normal-form difference is, in the basis [γ, σ₀, σ₁, L₊, L₋, e⁻], (n − p) = [h²/2, −h/2, −h/2, 0, 0, 0], with h = q − q⁻¹ the Hecke parameter. Two canonical functionals act on it:

    • the Frobenius counit (augmentation) ε, which weights every basis element by 1 — giving the unweighted sum ε(n−p) = h²/2 − h;
    • the Markov trace tr_M, which by axiom M1 weights the identity γ = 𝟙 by tr_M(𝟙) = 1 and each crossing σ by the loop value z — giving tr_M(n−p) = h²/2 − h·z.

    The per-braid mass tower is built on tr_M; the Q_β ansatz uses ε. These lemmas pin the precise relation between the two: they coincide exactly at the trivial loop z = 1, and differ by h·(z − 1) otherwise.

    All statements hold over an arbitrary field (the substrate→archimedean wall is not crossed: no ordering, no Real.sqrt). See AGENTS.md §7c.

        Bar-decomposition of the evaluation map (the standing reading, realized) #

        The Hecke parameter h = q − q⁻¹ is the fundamental bar-odd object: under the fibre bar involution q ↦ q⁻¹ it flips sign, h ↦ −h. The Markov loop parameter z = √q/(1+q) is bar-even (QOU.QBeta.z_bar_invariant, fixed under q ↦ q⁻¹, i.e. under h ↦ −h). So the evaluation map splits cleanly, and the split IS the standing reading (owner-granted 2026-07-07, registry KC6/KC10): the bar-even part is the identity/mass term h²/2 (carries the shadow), the bar-odd part is the crossing/loop term (−h for the counit, −h·z for the Markov trace) — the odd channel.

        The lemmas below realize this over any field by expressing the bar involution on h as the sign flip h ↦ −h (z held fixed, being bar-even).

        theorem QOU.Interactions.QBetaCounitTrace.epsNP_decomp {R : Type u_1} [Field R] (h : R) :
        epsNP h = h ^ 2 / 2 + -h

        Counit bar-decomposition. ε = h²/2 + (−h): an even part h²/2 plus an odd part −h. Under h ↦ −h the even part is invariant and the odd part flips sign, so the odd channel of Q_β (built on ε) is exactly the unweighted crossing term −h.
