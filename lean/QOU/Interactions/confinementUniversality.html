Let $\mathfrak{N}(\varphi_0, \varphi_1, \varphi_{01})$ denote the nucleon NF obtained by the derivation chain (4-term pair nucleon): $\gamma\gamma \to q\bar{q} \to \mathcal{B}_3 \cdot \mathrm{crossings} \to \mathfrak{N}$.*

        Then for all $\varphi_0, \varphi_1, \varphi_{01} \in \mathbb{R}$:

        $$ \mathfrak{N}(\varphi_0, \varphi_1, \varphi_{01}) #

        \mathfrak{N}(0, 0, 0) #

        \mathrm{NF}(\mathcal{B}_3 \cdot \mathrm{crossings}). $$

        The nucleon NF depends only on $q$ and the quark flavor assignment.
