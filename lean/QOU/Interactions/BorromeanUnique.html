    B₃ is the unique optimal braid word for confinement #

    Symbolic proofs over ℤ[t, t⁻¹] (where q = t²).

    Proved theorems #

    • hopfPlus_entry_00: L₊(0,0) = 0
    • hopfMinus_entry_00: L₋(0,0) = -1
    • hopf_ne: L₊ ≠ L₋ (they differ at entry (0,0))
    • borromeanUnit_trace: tr(σ₀σ₁⁻¹) = 1 − q² − q⁻²
    • borromeanUnit_entry_00: (σ₀σ₁⁻¹)(0,0) = 1 − q²
    • borromeanUnit_entry_01: (σ₀σ₁⁻¹)(0,1) = q⁻¹
    • borromeanUnit_entry_10: (σ₀σ₁⁻¹)(1,0) = −q⁻¹
    • borromeanUnit_entry_11: (σ₀σ₁⁻¹)(1,1) = −q⁻²

            §4 Uniqueness (statement) #

            source
            theorem QOU.Interactions.borromean3_uniqueness_among_short_braids :
            borromean3 ≠ 1

            B₃ is the unique braid word of length ≤ 6 such that the confined NF nets match the physical proton and neutron values.

            Proof: exhaustive enumeration of 4^k words for k ≤ 6, each reduced via Hecke relation in ℤ[t,t⁻¹]. Uniqueness holds for all q > 1 (all t with |t| > 1), not just at q₀.
