    QOU.Shared.ArchimedeanEval — tying q_real to the inherited q : R #

    Bean qou-mt7r. Three MarkovTraceContext descendants live past the §7c wall and declare their own q_real : ℝ:

    • QOU/Information/reeb_operations_per_planck.lean
    • QOU/Information/toffoli_transfer_matrix.lean
    • QOU/Observations/strangelet_crossing_energy.lean

    Each is genuinely archimedean — 1 < q_real, Real.sqrt q_real, inv_pos, linarith — so ℝ is the right type. The defect was that nothing related q_real to the inherited q : R. The docstrings called it "the specialisation of the generic shared q : R"; the type said no such thing. q_real was an independent field, so a context could set q_real := 37 while carrying a Hecke algebra over an entirely unrelated q, and every proposition downstream still held. The claims were archimedean statements about a real number, not about this universe's substrate parameter.

    What this fixes, and what it does not #

    ArchimedeanEval carries a ring homomorphism eval : R →+* ℝ and derives q_real := eval q rather than letting it be posited. The independent field is gone, so the two can no longer disagree by construction.

    q_real_one_of_q_one is the payoff and the test of whether the tie is worth anything: q = 1 → q_real = 1. It is a one-line consequence of eval being a ring hom, and it was not provable at all before — with q_real independent, the classical limit of the substrate parameter said nothing about the classical limit of the real one.

    This is not the archimedean τ-functor, and inhabiting it is not progress on that wall. QOU/Archimedean/TauFunctorTargetSpec.lean makes the distinction machine-checked for its own interface — interface_alone_is_trivially_inhabitable shows a bare diagonal matrix inhabits it — and the same caution applies here: ofRingHom below builds an ArchimedeanEval from any ring hom at all, with no functoriality, no naturality, and no reference to the braided category. So an ArchimedeanEval instance is not evidence that the functor exists. What the mixin buys is exactly one thing — q_real is some ring-homomorphic image of q rather than an unrelated real — and that is enough to make the three propositions statements about the substrate parameter.

    The genuine τ-functor would supply this eval with provenance. Until it exists, eval is a hypothesis, and it is labelled as one.

    Not a second parallel mechanism #

    Bean qou-mt7r warns that whatever τ gets introduced "should be the one qou-mt7r needs, not a second parallel mechanism", and qou-6ome left the same debt on the other side of the corpus: SubstrateParameter (categorical, M → R_q) and ArchimedeanSubstrateField (M → ℝ) are also untied.

    They are the same need, and the shared mechanism is not a new abstraction — it is RingHom _ ℝ, which Mathlib already has. ArchimedeanEval invents nothing; it is MarkovTraceContext plus a R →+* ℝ. The qou-6ome side should carry an R_q →+* ℝ in the same way rather than growing its own notion, and there eval is genuinely constructible — R_q = LaurentPolynomial ℤ, so evaluation at a nonzero real is an ordinary ring hom, not a hypothesis. That asymmetry is worth knowing before copying this file: here R is an opaque CommRing, so eval has to be assumed; there it does not.

    Why a sibling mixin rather than extending the parent #

    Bean qou-mt7r route 1 proposed adding the evaluation to MarkovTraceContext itself. That parent has 15 descendants, 12 of which are correctly pre-archimedean and have no business carrying a map to ℝ. Extending it would put an archimedean field on every one of them — the §7c wall in reverse. A mixin costs the three archimedean descendants one word in their extends clause and leaves the other twelve untouched.

    A MarkovTraceContext together with an assumed archimedean evaluation R →+* ℝ.

    The ring-homomorphism requirement is what carries the content: it forces eval 1 = 1 and eval to respect the ring operations, so q_real moves with q instead of floating free.
