    QOU.FeynmanPathIntegrals.MarkovIdentificationInstance — the branching identity, derived #

    markov_identification.lean carries the Wenzl-bridge / branching decomposition

    tr_M = ∑_λ w_λ · tr_{S_λ}

    as the named proposition markovTrace_branching nPart w trS trM — "named, not asserted" — and its module docstring lists the identity itself as Open: nothing there constructs the Hecke algebra, the Specht modules, or the quantum dimensions in w_λ; they are reals the caller supplies, and markov_identification derives clause (ii) from the identity as a hypothesis.

    At convention D — z_D = q³/(q²+1), the canonical Markov parameter per the 2026-08-15 owner ruling — the hypothesis is a theorem of this repo. QOU.SchurWeyl.MarkovTraceInstance proves, for every n, that every Markov trace on the Hecke tower equals the Ocneanu-weighted seminormal trace (eq_wtrace_of_markov), and wtrace_apply unfolds that trace to exactly the weighted sum of per-block traces. That is the branching decomposition, so this file supplies the hypothesis from it:

    • diagIndex n enumerates the Young diagrams of size n + 1 by a natural index (Fintype.equivFin), because the proposition indexes blocks by ℕ and sums over Finset.range;
    • indexed n f transports a function on diagrams to ℕ → ℝ (zero off-range), and sum_indexed shows the range sum is the Fintype sum;
    • markovTrace_branching_of_markovTrace T x proves markovTrace_branching at the data (number of blocks, Ocneanu weights, seminormal block traces of x, T.tr n x) for any Markov trace T at z_D and any Hecke element x;
    • markov_identification_of_markovTrace is lem:feynman-markov-identification at that data — the eigenbasis amplitude equals T.tr n x — with no assumption beyond the hypotheses of MarkovTraceInstance (q ≠ 0, q not a root of unity, q² + 1 ≠ 0, q² not a root of unity).

    What this does and does not claim #

    • It closes the decomposition gap at the data the lemma is about: the weights are the Ocneanu weights and the partial traces are the seminormal-representation traces, both concrete, and the identity is a theorem. not_markovTrace_branching_of_free_data in the base file is unaffected — the identity is false at free data and true at this data; the two are different claims.
    • It is convention D only. For an arbitrary Markov parameter z the per-corner identity is still open (MarkovConventionD module docstring); nothing here bears on that.

    Base ring (§7c) #

    ℝ, forced by seminormalRep and by the base file (whose data are ℝ-valued). Nothing here is archimedean beyond that inheritance.
