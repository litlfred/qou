    QOU.FeynmanPathIntegrals.markov_identification #

    Canonical Lean for content block lem:feynman-markov-identification: Markov-weighted eigenbasis sum equals the Markov trace.

    Lemma. For β ∈ Bₙ acting on V^{⊗n} via ρₙ, the eigenbasis transition amplitude — defined as the Markov-weighted sum of partial traces

    ⟨ψ_f | ψ_i⟩_eig := Σ_{λ ⊢ n, ℓ(λ) ≤ 2} w_λ · tr_{S_λ}(ρₙ(β))

    — equals the Jones–Markov trace tr_M(ρ(β)) on H_n(R, q), where w_λ = dim_q(V_λ)·dim(S_λ)/dim_q(V^{⊗n}).

    2026-09-03 — a correction that reinstated the defect it documents #

    The module docstring said the branching identity was

    "stated as the standalone lemma markovTrace_branching about the named opaque objects markovTrace, markovWeight, spechtPartialTrace, discharged by sorry with the manuscript citation"

    and the declaration docstring repeated "so the body is sorry with the manuscript citation". There were no opaque objects — all three were fields of MarkovIdentificationContext — and there was no sorry anywhere in the file; every one of its four occurrences was prose. The body was ctx.markovTrace_branching: the projection, AGENTS.md §3b-cond item 5.

    What makes this the sharpest instance in the drain is the paragraph that followed, which is correct about its own predecessor:

    "A previous revision took the branching identity as a hypothesis h_branching : trM = Σ w·trS on the main theorem — definitionally the conclusion — so the substantive identity was assumed rather than stated; that encoding (and its docstring's claim of a sorry that did not exist) is corrected here."

    The correction moved the hypothesis from a theorem argument into a class field. That is the same assumption under a different binder — and it carried forward, verbatim, the false sorry claim it had just diagnosed. A documented fix that reinstates the documented defect, in the same commit that names it.

    Independently corroborated on main #

    Commit 78988da5c92 — "lean: nine docstrings claim their body is a sorry; it is a projection" — singled this file out as one of two that were doubly wrong: the docstring said the Wenzl-bridge infrastructure "cannot be added as a simple class field, so the body is sorry", and "it was added as exactly such a field, of the same name, and is projected."

    That is the same finding reached here from the other direction, and it deliberately stopped at the prose, recording the projection as owned by bean qou-z9ws / PR #6422 — this change. The rebase conflicted because its correction describes the theorem this commit deletes.

    Disposition: name it, assert nothing (AGENTS.md §1a, disposition 3) #

    The class is gone. markovTrace_branching is a Prop-valued definition over caller-supplied data, and markov_identification derives the lemma's conclusion from it — which is what the file was trying to express all along, and could not while the identity was a field the instance chose.

    A sorry would not have been sound either: with markovTrace free, the branching identity is refutable (not_markovTrace_branching_of_free_data), so a sorry would read as "true, not yet proved" about something false.

    What is proved, and what is open #

    • amplitudeEigenbasis_eq_sum — clause (i), by rfl. It is the definition of the eigenbasis amplitude and is labelled as such: it carries no content about Hecke algebras.
    • markov_identification — clause (ii), derived from the branching identity taken as a hypothesis.

    Open in this file — and closed at convention D one module over (2026-09-03). QOU.FeynmanPathIntegrals.MarkovIdentificationInstance.markovTrace_branching_of_markovTrace proves markovTrace_branching at the data the lemma is about — block count, Ocneanu weights, seminormal block traces, T.tr n x — for every Markov trace T at z_D = q³/(q²+1) and every n, from QOU.SchurWeyl.MarkovTraceInstance.eq_wtrace_of_markov; and markov_identification_of_markovTrace is the lemma at that data. The paragraph below is the status of this file, which constructs none of those objects and remains correct as written.

    Open (here): the branching identity itself — the Wenzl-bridge decomposition tr_M = Σ_λ w_λ tr_{S_λ} of the Jones–Markov trace into Specht-module partial traces. Nothing here constructs ρₙ, H_n(R, q), the Specht modules, or the quantum dimensions in w_λ; they are reals the caller supplies. The in-repo RSK infrastructure (QOU.Mathlib.RSK_substrate_proved) proves the classical Burnside identity for symmetric groups, not this branching.
