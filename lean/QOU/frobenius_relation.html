    QOU.frobenius_relation — Frobenius coassociativity relation #

    Proposition (prop:frobenius-relation).

    Let (𝐂, A, τ) be a quantum universe, with comultiplication Δ_μ : A → A ⊗ A dual to the multiplication μ : A ⊗ A → A under the Frobenius pairing. Then the Frobenius relation holds: the diagram

        A ⊗ A  --[id ⊗ Δ_μ]-->  A ⊗ A ⊗ A
       |                       |
     [μ]                  [μ ⊗ id]
       v                       v
       A   --[Δ_μ]------->  A ⊗ A

    commutes, equivalently:

      (μ ⊗ id) ∘ (id ⊗ Δ_μ) = Δ_μ ∘ μ.

    The Lean encoding records the equality of the two compositions as an equation between morphisms A ⊗ A → A ⊗ A. The abstract context carries A, ⊗, μ, Δ_μ, and the composition; the relation is a single equation between A ⊗ A → A ⊗ A maps.

    • Δ_μ : A → TensorAA

    • μ_tensor_id : TensorAAA → TensorAA

      The lifted multiplication μ ⊗ id : A ⊗ A ⊗ A → A ⊗ A.

    • id_tensor_Δμ : TensorAA → TensorAAA

      The lifted comultiplication id ⊗ Δ_μ : A ⊗ A → A ⊗ A ⊗ A.
