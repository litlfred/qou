    QOU.Molecular.co2_lp_dual_binding #

    Proposition (conditional on [conj:q-integer-mass-formula]) (prop:co2-lp-dual-binding): The LP dual of the CO₂ molecular braid selects the symmetric linear arrangement O=C=O with two double bonds (k₁, k₂) = (2, 2) as the ground state, subject to carbon valence k₁ + k₂ ≤ 4; the molecule uses 66 strands.

    Disposition (integrity) #

    All three conjuncts of the resolving declaration are now derived; none is a projected conclusion field (a previous revision carried an opaque co2_lp_dual_bound and impl_… fields handing back the conclusions, and a later revision still returned the carbon_valence and n_strands_def fields verbatim in the conjunction — both the self-assuming-projection shape forbidden by AGENTS.md §3b-cond item 5):

    • (1) Carbon-valence feasibility. The bond orders of the symmetric candidate are definitional geometry inputs (k1_def : k1 = 2, k2_def : k2 = 2 — a double bond to each oxygen). The valence bound k₁ + k₂ ≤ 4 is derived as the arithmetic fact 2 + 2 ≤ 4 (co2_carbon_valence_feasible) — the LP primal-feasibility check of the candidate, mirroring how computations/lp_dual_solver.py separates feasibility (checkable) from optimality (the dual bound).
    • (2) Strand count. The per-atom strand counts are definitional composition inputs (strands_C = 6 + 6 + 6 protons/neutrons/ electrons of carbon, strands_O = 8 + 8 + 8 of oxygen) and the molecular total is defined as strands_C + 2·strands_O. The claim n_strands = 66 is derived by evaluating 18 + 2·24 (co2_strand_count).
    • (3) Symmetric optimum beats both asymmetric alternatives. Instantiating the §3b-cond ground-state-minimisation hypothesis at (3,1) and (1,3) yields Φ₁(2,2) ≤ Φ₁(3,1) and Φ₁(2,2) ≤ Φ₁(1,3) (co2_symmetric_beats_asymmetric) — the .md's "wins over asymmetric alternatives (3,1) or (1,3)", obtained by using the quantified hypothesis at two points, not restating it.

    The single irreducibly-conjectural input is the LP-dual ground-state selection — the symmetric (2,2) configuration minimises Φ₁ over the carbon-valence-feasible configurations. It is carried by the §3b-cond class Co2SymmetricSelection as a quantified hypothesis the theorem instantiates, never restated verbatim in the conclusion.

    Base ring (§7c) #

    Φ₁ is the LP objective functional on bond configurations and the only thing done to its values is ≤; the strand counts and valences are ℕ. Nothing is measured and nothing is archimedean, so the Φ₁ values live in a generic ordered R (contrast ch4_lp_dual_binding, whose E_bind_CH is a measured bond energy and is ℝ by meaning). The substrate evaluation is R := ℝ.

      class QOU.Molecular.Co2LpDualBindingContext (R : Type u_2) [Preorder R] :
      Type u_2

      Data of the CO₂ LP-dual-binding claim. All fields are inputs (the symmetric candidate's bond orders, the molecular composition, and the Φ₁-functional); none is a conclusion.

      • k1 : ℕ

        Bond order to the first oxygen atom in the symmetric candidate.

      • k2 : ℕ

        Bond order to the second oxygen atom in the symmetric candidate.

      • k1_def : k1 R = 2

        The symmetric candidate places a double bond to the first oxygen (definitional geometry input).

      • k2_def : k2 R = 2

        The symmetric candidate places a double bond to the second oxygen (definitional geometry input).

      • strands_C : ℕ

        Strand count contributed by the carbon atom: 6 protons + 6 neutrons + 6 electrons (definitional composition input).

      • strands_C_def : strands_C R = 6 + 6 + 6

        Carbon contributes 6 + 6 + 6 = 18 strands.

      • strands_O : ℕ

        Strand count contributed by each oxygen atom: 8 protons + 8 neutrons + 8 electrons (definitional composition input).

      • strands_O_def : strands_O R = 8 + 8 + 8

        Each oxygen contributes 8 + 8 + 8 = 24 strands.

      • n_strands : ℕ

        The number of strands in the CO₂ molecular braid.

      • n_strands_def : n_strands R = strands_C R + 2 * strands_O R

        The molecular strand count is the carbon contribution plus two oxygen contributions (definitional composition input).

      • Phi1_config : Co2Config → R

        Φ₁ of each carbon-valence-feasible bond configuration.
