    QOU.Molecular.ch4_lp_dual_binding #

    Proposition (conditional on [conj:q-integer-mass-formula]) (prop:ch4-lp-dual-binding): The LP dual of the CH₄ molecular braid in H₂₆(q) selects the S₄-symmetric strand ordering as the ground state. The four C–H bond energies are equal by symmetry, and the total binding energy is exactly 4 · E_bind^{CH}.

    Disposition (integrity) #

    The class carries only genuine data: the per-bond C–H binding energies, the single-bond reference E_bind^{CH}, and the total binding energy defined as the sum of the four per-bond energies. It does not carry the conclusion as a projected field (the previous version carried a Prop-valued ch4_lp_dual_bound and impl_… fields handing back the conclusions — the self-assuming projection forbidden by AGENTS.md §3b-cond item 5).

    The §3b-cond class carries the S₄-symmetry hypothesis (each C–H bond has the same binding energy E_bind^{CH}). ch4_lp_dual_binding (the resolving decl) derives the total-binding identity E_bind_total = 4 · E_bind^{CH} from that symmetry hypothesis composed with the definitional E_bind_total = Σ E_bind_per_bond: rewriting each summand to the common value E_bind^{CH} and evaluating the Fin 4 sum (Finset.sum_const, card). This genuinely uses the hypothesis to compute a numeric identity — it does not project the conclusion.

      Total binding energy of the CH₄ molecule.

    • E_bind_total_def : E_bind_total = ∑ i : Fin 4, E_bind_per_bond i

      Total binding is the sum of the four per-bond energies (definitional input).
