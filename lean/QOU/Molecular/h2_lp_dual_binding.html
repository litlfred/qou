    QOU.Molecular.h2_lp_dual_binding #

    Proposition (conditional on [conj:q-integer-mass-formula]) (prop:h2-lp-dual-binding): The LP dual of the H₂ molecular braid selects the interleaved ordering [𝔭₁, e₂, 𝔭₂, e₁] as the ground state, with molecular volume

    Φ₁^{H₂} = Σ_{i=0..2} ln |V̂ᵢ^{H₂}|,

    and a non-negative binding shadow price y_cov* = Φ₁^sep − Φ₁^{H₂} ≥ 0.

    Disposition (integrity) #

    The class carries only genuine data: the three traced volumes V̂ᵢ^{H₂}, the molecular Φ₁ defined as the sum of their log-magnitudes, the Φ₁-functional on orderings, and the separated-atom ordering / reference. It does not carry the conclusion as a projected field (the previous version carried a Prop-valued h2_lp_dual_bound and an impl_… field handing back the conclusion — the self-assuming projection forbidden by AGENTS.md §3b-cond item 5).

    • h2_molecular_volume_decomposition is derived sorry-free: the molecular volume is the sum of the three log-magnitudes, from the defining equation + add_assoc. Genuine algebra, no projection.
    • h2_binding_nonneg is derived sorry-free from the §3b-cond hypothesis: it instantiates the ground-state-minimisation hypothesis at the separated ordering and rearranges, deriving the binding shadow price Φ₁^sep − Φ₁^{H₂} ≥ 0. This genuinely uses the hypothesis (it does not restate it): the conclusion is a numeric inequality on Φ₁ values obtained by specialising ∀ π to one π and chaining the two definitional links.

    class QOU.Molecular.H2LpDualBindingContext :
    Type

    Data of the H₂ LP-dual-binding claim. All fields are inputs; none is the conclusion.

    • V_hat_H2 : Fin 3 → ℝ

      Traced volume V̂ᵢ^{H₂} of the three crossings (indices 0..2).

    • Phi1_H2 : ℝ

      The molecular Φ₁ for H₂, defined as the sum of the log-magnitudes of the traced volumes.

    • Phi1_H2_def : Phi1_H2 = Real.log |V_hat_H2 0| + (Real.log |V_hat_H2 1| + Real.log |V_hat_H2 2|)

      Φ₁^{H₂} is the sum of the three log-magnitudes (definitional input — the molecular volume's defining formula).

    • Phi1_of_ordering : (Fin 4 → Fin 4) → ℝ

      Φ₁ of every strand ordering π ∈ S₄.

    • interleaved : Fin 4 → Fin 4

      The interleaved ground-state ordering [𝔭₁, e₂, 𝔭₂, e₁].

    • interleaved_is_H2 : Phi1_of_ordering interleaved = Phi1_H2

      Its Φ₁ is the molecular volume Φ₁^{H₂} (definitional link).

    • separated : Fin 4 → Fin 4

      The separated (two-isolated-atoms) ordering.

    • Phi1_sep : ℝ

      The separated-atom reference value Φ₁^sep (definitional link).

    • separated_is_sep : Phi1_of_ordering separated = Phi1_sep

      Φ₁ of the separated ordering equals Φ₁^sep (definitional link).
