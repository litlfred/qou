    QOU.Molecular.co_lp_dual_binding #

    Proposition (conditional on [conj:q-integer-mass-formula]) (prop:co-lp-dual-binding): The LP dual of the CO molecular braid selects the triple-bond configuration (k* = 3) as optimal over single (k = 1) and double (k = 2) bonds.

    Disposition (integrity) #

    The class carries only genuine data: the selected bond order k* (definitional input), the Φ₁-functional on bond orders, and the three candidate bond orders. It does not carry the conclusion as a projected field (the previous version carried a Prop-valued co_lp_dual_bound and an impl_… field handing back the conclusion — the self-assuming projection forbidden by AGENTS.md §3b-cond item 5).

    • co_triple_beats_single and co_triple_beats_double are derived from the §3b-cond hypothesis: instantiating the ground-state-minimisation hypothesis at k = 1 and k = 2 yields Φ₁(3) ≤ Φ₁(1) and Φ₁(3) ≤ Φ₁(2) — genuine new inequalities (use the hypothesis, do not restate it).
    • co_lp_dual_binding (the resolving decl) conjoins the definitional selection k* = 3 with the two derived minimality inequalities. Neither inequality projects a conclusion field.

    Base ring (§7c) #

    Φ₁ here is the LP objective functional on bond orders, and the only thing done to its values is ≤. Nothing is measured and nothing is archimedean, so the values live in a generic ordered R (contrast ch4_lp_dual_binding, whose E_bind_CH is a measured bond energy and is therefore ℝ by meaning). The substrate evaluation is the instantiation R := ℝ.
