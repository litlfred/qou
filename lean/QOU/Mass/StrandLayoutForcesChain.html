    QOU.Mass.StrandLayoutForcesChain — the chain bond set is forced by the strand layout #

    Arc bk-crossing-mass-binding, bean qou-brng; companion to QOU.Mass.CoordinationSaturation and QOU.MassTheory.ChainBondOrderDependence.

    What this settles #

    The corpus carries two inter-nucleon bond sets and they have been compared as though they were two choices of the same kind of object:

    • mass_at_3A_proper.atom_braid_word_3A wires A − 1 bonds (the chain);
    • hecke_core_symbolic.build_atom_nf_sym wires C(A,2) (the complete graph).

    They are not two choices. Measured on the live producer, atom_braid_word_3A places its pp/nn/pn bridge at generator 3k+2 for k = 0 … A−2 — verified directly by diffing the include_inter=False and include_inter=True words at A = 4, 5, 6, which added generators {2, 5, 8}, {2, 5, 8, 11} and {2, 5, 8, 11, 14}.

    This file proves why those are the only ones available. Nucleon k occupies strands 3k, 3k+1, 3k+2 of a 3A-strand braid, and the generator σ_m swaps strands m and m+1. So:

    • crossesBlocks_iff — σ_m joins two different nucleons iff m % 3 = 2;
    • adjacent_of_crosses — and when it does, the two nucleons are consecutive;
    • no_single_generator_joins_nonadjacent — hence no single generator joins non-adjacent nucleons, at any A.

    The chain is therefore forced by the layout, not selected among bond sets. A non-adjacent bond is not a different edge set one could equally have written; it is not a single crossing at all, and would need a word transporting strands past each other — which atom_braid_word_3A does not write and which would not sit at a Markov-removable position in the same way.

    The consequence worth carrying #

    The layout is a linear order on nucleons. So the chain bond set and the nucleon ordering convention are the same unlicensed choice, not two independent ones — which merges this arc's bond-set question into the ordering gap already tracked as qou-48is (there the ordering moves tr_M by O(10 %) at A ≥ 3).

    Scope #

    Pure ℕ arithmetic about the block map m ↦ m / 3; §7c does not apply (no ring, no archimedean construct). It says nothing about which bond set the physics wants, and nothing about build_atom_nf_sym, whose inter-nucleon factors are all at generator 0 and therefore carry no strand assignment at all (measured separately: that product is invariant under every permutation of its bond multiset, so it encodes a bond count rather than a graph).

      theorem QOU.Mass.StrandLayoutForcesChain.crossesBlocks_iff (m : ℕ) :
      block m ≠ block (m + 1) ↔ m % 3 = 2

      A generator crosses a nucleon boundary exactly at m ≡ 2 (mod 3). σ_m swaps strands m and m+1; those lie in different nucleons iff m % 3 = 2.

      theorem QOU.Mass.StrandLayoutForcesChain.adjacent_of_crosses (m : ℕ) (h : m % 3 = 2) :
      block (m + 1) = block m + 1

      And the two nucleons it joins are consecutive. This is the half that forces the chain: a boundary-crossing generator never reaches past its neighbour.

      theorem QOU.Mass.StrandLayoutForcesChain.no_single_generator_joins_nonadjacent (m i j : ℕ) (hi : block m = i) (hj : block (m + 1) = j) (hne : i ≠ j) :
      j = i + 1

      No single generator joins non-adjacent nucleons, at any A. So the edges expressible as one crossing are exactly the A − 1 consecutive pairs, and the complete graph is not among the bond sets this layout can write.
