    QOU.Mass.QWeberInterpolation — parabolic-cylinder-type even/odd channel interpolation #

    The fibre-level skeleton of the parabolic cylinder function D_ν(z) transported to the q-Hermite / Rogers–Szegő channel towers (2026-07-03 audit docs/audits/2026-07-03-parabolic-cylinder-channel-interpolation.md). Classically

    D_ν = 2^{ν/2}√π [ y_even/Γ((1−ν)/2) − √2·y_odd/Γ(−ν/2) ],

    one continuous-index family containing both parity towers, with parity selected at integer ν by Γ-pole vanishing: the even-solution coefficient vanishes at odd integers, the odd-solution coefficient at even integers. The Γ_q-pole lattice is identical, so the mechanism q-deforms verbatim.

    QWeberFamily carries exactly that structure as a class: a combination field and the two pole-vanishing fields (the analytic content, taken as hypotheses — §3b). Everything downstream is proved:

    • parity collapse — at even (resp. odd) integer index the family is a pure even (resp. odd) channel (pure_even_channel, pure_odd_channel);
    • recurrence closure — if both towers satisfy the Rogers–Szegő three-term recurrence (FollowsRogersSzego, the q-oscillator bound-state recurrence), then so does the interpolant at every index ν (interp_follows_rogers_szego) — the fibre-level face of Weber's equation having a two-dimensional solution space;
    • two-step decoupling transfer at t = −1 and odd-term vanishing transfer (interp_two_step_at_neg_one, interp_odd_vanishes_at_neg_one) — the proved parity split of BindingQHermiteOscillator lifts through the interpolation;
    • non-vacuity — an explicit (classical-choice) model demoA/demoB/demoD inhabits the class over any field (demo_family), so the axiomatisation is consistent.

    Ground ring: a generic field K (§7c — fibre-level / q-formal; no archimedean evaluation, no ℝ). The index ν ranges over K, with the integer lattice embedded by Nat.cast.

    Ref: [dlmf-ch12] https://dlmf.nist.gov/12 (classical D_ν and the Γ-pole connection formula); [atakishiyev-suslov1990] https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01017247 (q-oscillator).

    class QOU.Mass.QWeberInterpolation.QWeberFamily {K : Type u_1} [Field K] (D : K → ℕ → K) (E O : ℕ → K) (A B : K → K) :
    Prop

    The q-Weber interpolating family, as a class. D ν is the member of the family at continuous index ν : K; E/O are the even/odd channel towers; A/B are the channel coefficients. The two vanishing fields are the fibre-level shadow of the Γ_q-pole mechanism: 1/Γ_q(−ν/2) kills the odd part at even integers and 1/Γ_q((1−ν)/2) kills the even part at odd integers.

    • combination (ν : K) (m : ℕ) : D ν m = A ν * E m + B ν * O m

      The family is everywhere the two-channel combination A(ν)·E + B(ν)·O.

    • A_vanishes_at_odd (j : ℕ) : A ↑(2 * j + 1) = 0

      The even-channel coefficient vanishes on the odd integer lattice (Γ_q((1−ν)/2) pole).

    • B_vanishes_at_even (j : ℕ) : B ↑(2 * j) = 0

      The odd-channel coefficient vanishes on the even integer lattice (Γ_q(−ν/2) pole).
