    def QOU.Mass.QShiftColorCoefficientLadder.gradedZInt {R : Type u_1} [CommRing R] (q z : R) (n : ℕ) :
    R

    The q-graded z-integer ∑_{k<n} [k+1]_q · z^k.

    It is the Jackson q-derivative of [n+1]_z (jackson_zInt_numerator), and it is the q-deformation of the classical d/dz [n+1]_z = ∑_{k<n} (k+1)·z^k: the coefficient of z^k is [k+1]_q where the classical one is k+1 (gradedZInt_one). Written with the bare geometric sum ∑_{j<k+1} q^j rather than qInt, so the definition lives over a CommRing; see the predecessor files for the [·]_q naming over a Field.

      theorem QOU.Mass.QShiftColorCoefficientLadder.qshift_colorSpectralCubic {R : Type u_1} [CommRing R] (q z X : R) :
      ColorCurveQIntegerForm.colorSpectralCubic (q * z) X - ColorCurveQIntegerForm.colorSpectralCubic z X = (q - 1) * z * (gradedZInt q z 1 * X ^ 2 + gradedZInt q z 2 * X + gradedZInt q z 3)

      The q-shift of the colour spectral cubic, in closed form.

      C_{q·z}(X) − C_z(X) = (q−1)·z·( g₁·X² + g₂·X + g₃ ) with gᵢ = gradedZInt q z i.

      Dividing by (q−1)z, the Jackson q-derivative of the colour cubic is

      D_q C(X) = gradedZInt q z 1 · X² + gradedZInt q z 2 · X + gradedZInt q z 3

      against C_z(X) = [1]_z·X³ + [2]_z·X² + [3]_z·X + [4]_z: the same shape, ladder index dropped by one, [k]_z replaced by gradedZInt q z (k−1). The operator preserves the q-integer-curve class.

      Branch-free by construction: the coefficients are symmetric functions of the sheets, so a_k(q·z) involves no choice of sheet — which is where obstruction (ii) of the W1 bean lives, and why it is absent here.
