    QOU.Mass.BindingJacobiAngerBridge — the Layer-1 ↔ Layer-2 bridge #

    Sibling PR #2795 left two recurrence layers for the binding prolongation a_D^(n): Layer 1 (BindingHyperbolicRecurrence) the hyperbolic cosh/sinh tower (depth-2 SL₂, n ≤ 4 fit), and Layer 2 (BindingQBesselKernel) the super-exponential q-Bessel tower. This file records the bridge between them — the elementary Jacobi–Anger structure already formalised in QOU.QBeta.ReebTowerJacobiAnger — and the binding-specific payoff it implies: a confinement criterion.

    One operator, two conics (Jacobi–Anger) #

    Both towers solve the same three-term operator p_{k+1} = 2t·p_k − p_{k−1} (reeb_tower_cosh_step, reeb_rotation_sin_three_term), with the multiplier t selecting the branch:

    t regime tower conic Bessel
    t = cosh η ≥ 1 (reeb_growth_one_le_cosh) growth binding Layer-1 cosh/sinh(nθ) hyperbola cosh²−sinh²=1 I_ν
    t = cos θ, |t| ≤ 1 (reeb_rotation_abs_cos_le_one) rotation sin(nθ) circle cos²+sin²=1 J_ν

    These are the two faces of I_ν(x) = i^{−ν} J_ν(i x) — the Jacobi–Anger shadow (reeb_growth_hyperbola vs reeb_rotation_circle). Layer 2 (the q-Bessel kernel, BindingQBesselKernel) is the q-deformation of the radial J_ν side; the binding multiplier t = τ/2 = cosh θ_binding ≥ 1 puts binding on the growth / I_ν branch — which is exactly why the binding tower grows (super-exp at the q-Bessel level, exp at the SL₂ level) rather than oscillating.

    Confinement criterion (the binding payoff) #

    The binding transfer kernel R(q,x) = x² − τx + 1 (BindingHyperbolicRecurrence) has real, reciprocal roots — a confined (bound) hyperbolic SL₂(ℝ) state — iff its discriminant τ² − 4 ≥ 0, i.e. |τ| ≥ 2. With τ = 2 cosh θ (binding_trace_two_cosh) this holds identically, with discriminant 4 sinh²θ: the cosh-form of τ guarantees confinement (the opposite, |τ| < 2, is the elliptic / J_ν-rotation / scattering regime).

    theorem QOU.Mass.BindingJacobiAngerBridge.binding_trace_ge_two (θ : ℝ) :
    2 ≤ 2 * Real.cosh θ

    Binding trace lies in the |τ| ≥ 2 confined regime. τ = 2 cosh θ ≥ 2, since 1 ≤ cosh θ (reeb_growth_one_le_cosh). The depth-2 binding transfer is therefore always hyperbolic SL₂(ℝ) (a real binding angle θ exists), never elliptic.

    theorem QOU.Mass.BindingJacobiAngerBridge.binding_discriminant_eq_four_sinh_sq (θ : ℝ) :
    (2 * Real.cosh θ) ^ 2 - 4 = 4 * Real.sinh θ ^ 2

    The binding transfer discriminant is 4 sinh²θ. For τ = 2 cosh θ, τ² − 4 = 4 cosh²θ − 4 = 4 sinh²θ (via the growth hyperbola cosh²θ − sinh²θ = 1, reeb_growth_hyperbola). The discriminant of R(q,x) = x² − τx + 1.

    theorem QOU.Mass.BindingJacobiAngerBridge.binding_discriminant_nonneg (θ : ℝ) :
    0 ≤ (2 * Real.cosh θ) ^ 2 - 4

    Confinement: the binding discriminant is non-negative. τ² − 4 = 4 sinh²θ ≥ 0, so the binding kernel x² − τx + 1 has real reciprocal roots e^{±θ} — a confined hyperbolic bound state — for every real binding angle θ. This is the structural reason the cosh-form of τ (built from the bar-even gap [2]_q, NaStokesKeystone) describes a bound nucleus.

    theorem QOU.Mass.BindingJacobiAngerBridge.binding_on_growth_hyperbola (θ : ℝ) :
    Real.cosh θ ^ 2 - Real.sinh θ ^ 2 = 1

    The binding growth tower lives on the I_ν hyperbola (not the J_ν circle): cosh²θ − sinh²θ = 1. The binding-context restatement of reeb_growth_hyperbola; together with binding_discriminant_nonneg it places binding firmly on the growth side of the Jacobi–Anger bridge I_ν(x) = i^{−ν} J_ν(i x), whose q-deformation is the Layer-2 q-Bessel kernel.
