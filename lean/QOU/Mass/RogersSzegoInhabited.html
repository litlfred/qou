    QOU.Mass.RogersSzegoInhabited — FollowsRogersSzego is non-vacuous #

    QOU.Mass.BindingQHermiteOscillator.FollowsRogersSzego (#2800) is the §3b conjecture class encoding the standard Rogers–Szegő / continuous q-Hermite three-term recurrence. Its downstream consequences (q→1 collapse, even/odd vanishing) were proved abstractly, but the class had no exhibited model — so those theorems were conditional on inhabitation.

    This file closes that: the sequence defined by the recurrence is a concrete FollowsRogersSzego instance, so the class is satisfiable (the conjecture is not vacuously unsatisfiable), and the abstract consequences now apply to a real witness.

    Sibling PR #2822 independently identified a_D^(n)=lw(n−1) as the q-deformed involution number — a q-Hermite moment — pointing the z2bn binding kernel R(q,x) at exactly this family; the inhabited recurrence here is the Lean home of that kernel.

    Ground ring ℝ. Self-contained (imports only BindingQHermiteOscillator).

      @[simp]
      theorem QOU.Mass.BindingQHermiteOscillator.rsSeq_q_one (t : ℝ) (n : ℕ) :
      rsSeq 1 t n = (1 + t) ^ n

      q→1: the Rogers–Szegő sequence is the binomial (1+t)^n. At q = 1 the coefficient 1 − q^{m+1} vanishes, collapsing the recurrence to H_{n+1} = (1+t)·H_n; with H_0 = 1 this gives rsSeq 1 t n = (1+t)^n, the classical (un-deformed) Rogers–Szegő. Marked @[simp] so the simplifier reduces rsSeq 1 t n to its closed form downstream (per Gemini review, #2842).

      theorem QOU.Mass.BindingQHermiteOscillator.rsSeq_two (q t : ℝ) :
      rsSeq q t 2 = 1 + (1 + q) * t + t ^ 2

      H_2(t;q) = 1 + (1+q)·t + t² for the standard Rogers–Szegő — the middle coefficient is the standard quantum integer [2]_q = 1+q (contrast the balanced q+q⁻¹ of QGaussianBinomialPascal.rogersSzego_two; this is the normalization that obeys the FollowsRogersSzego recurrence).

      theorem QOU.Mass.BindingQHermiteOscillator.rsSeq_neg_one_step (q : ℝ) (n : ℕ) :
      rsSeq q (-1) (n + 2) = (1 - q ^ (n + 1)) * rsSeq q (-1) n

      Two-step recurrence at t = −1. Setting t = −1 kills the (1+t) head of the three-term recurrence, leaving the telescoping two-step H_{n+2} = (1 − q^{n+1})·H_n.

      theorem QOU.Mass.BindingQHermiteOscillator.rsSeq_even_at_neg_one (q : ℝ) (j : ℕ) :
      rsSeq q (-1) (2 * j) = ∏ i ∈ Finset.range j, (1 - q ^ (2 * i + 1))

      The even terms of the inhabited model at t = −1 are the q-shifted factorial (q; q²)_j — rsSeq q (−1) (2j) = ∏_{i<j} (1 − q^{2i+1}). Unrolling the two-step recurrence rsSeq_neg_one_step on the even subsequence gives the explicit finite product (1−q)(1−q³)⋯(1−q^{2j−1}), the q-Pochhammer (q; q²)_j, written self-contained as a Finset.prod (no dependency on Mathlib's QPochhammer). Together with rsSeq_odd_vanishes_at_neg_one this is the full closed form of the inhabited Rogers–Szegő model at the binding anchor t = −1.
