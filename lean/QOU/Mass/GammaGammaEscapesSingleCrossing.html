    Why the γγ-selected bond can unbind: the single-crossing obstruction is vacuous on γγ words #

    Bean qou-ales, step 8 — a reconciliation, not a new derivation. The corpus holds two sorry-free bodies of work that look like they collide, and this file proves they do not.

    One side. QOU/QBeta/SingleCrossingAlwaysBinds.lean proves that a single-crossing interface can never unbind: its scalar is μ = z + d with d ∈ [-h, 0], which lies in (0,1) at the substrate anchor, so B = −α·log μ > 0 (single_crossing_binds). Its t3_grading_cannot_unbind_pp adds that the T₃ / writhe grading cannot rescue the sign, and its docstring states the conclusion in strong terms — the framework "picked a sign that makes unbinding impossible, whatever the magnitude".

    The other side. Step 3 (#7192) measured that the γγ-selected interface bond G³ = (σ₂σ₃⁻¹)³ does unbind the diproton (B_pp = −4.47 keV, ratio tr_atom/tr_free = 1.001058), and steps 6–7 proved G³ is what reading 3 selects.

    Both are correct. Two structural facts separate them, and both are proved here.

    1. No non-empty γγ word is a single crossing #

    length_even (step 1) gives every γγ word an even number of crossings; a single crossing has one. So interfaceBond m for m ≥ 1 is never in the obstruction's domain (gammaGamma_ne_singleCrossing, interfaceBond_ne_singleCrossing). The obstruction is a statement about words of length one; the selected bond has length six.

    2. The writhe / T₃ grading is IDENTICALLY TRIVIAL on a γγ word #

    This is the sharper half. The grading multiplies the channel scalar by c^(writhe w / 2), and writhe_eq_zero (step 1) gives every γγ word writhe 0. So the factor is c⁰ = 1 exactly (writhe_normalisation_eq_one_of_gammaGamma): on γγ words the grading neither binds harder nor unbinds (t3_grading_vacuous_on_gammaGamma). The owner's ruling of 2026-09-08 — select every braid word by γγ pair production — therefore does not beat the T₃ obstruction; it makes it silent.

    3. What is still measured, and what is now proved #

    The sign law itself is proved (unbound_iff_ratio_gt_one): with α > 0 and μ > 0, −α·log μ < 0 iff μ > 1. And obstruction_conclusion_false_of_ratio_gt_one shows the obstruction's μ < 1 hypothesis is essential — for a ratio above one its conclusion is false, not merely unproved. So of the step-3 diproton result, exactly one number remains a measurement: the ratio 1.001058. Everything else about the sign is now algebra.

    Nothing here reads AME2020, a CODATA binding value, or a census, and nothing here asserts the ratio — it is carried as a hypothesis wherever it is used.
