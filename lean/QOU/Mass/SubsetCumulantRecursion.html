    Subset cumulants at general A: the algebra the binding-gap arc measured #

    computations/probes/the_binding_arc_was_measured_in_float64_probe.py (bean qou-wx83) re-measured, at 200 bits, the identities the t7qv binding-gap arc had read off the float64 floor. Every one of them held to ~1e-60. This file proves the ones that are algebra — true for every set function, every ring, every q — so that the probe's role is reduced to what a numerical check can honestly be: a consistency test on the code, not the warrant for the claim.

    The arc's object is a set function L : Finset ι → R on subsets of the A nucleons — L S = log(tr_free / tr_S) with bonds placed among the nucleons in S — and its Möbius cumulants over the Boolean lattice,

    cum L S = L S − Σ_{T ⊊ S} cum L T .

    Three facts carry the arc's cumulant reading:

    • Reconstruction (sum_powerset_cum): Σ_{T ⊆ S} cum L T = L S. Immediate from the definition; the probe's "reconstruction residual 2.49e-60" is an instance.

    • The size-only recursion (cum_eq_sizeCum): if L S depends only on #S — the exchangeable, homonuclear case — then so does cum L S, and the scalar sequence obeys

      c_k = f_k − Σ_{j<k} C(k,j) · c_j ,

      which is the c_k-only recursion the owner asked for. It replaces 2^A − A − 1 traces by A − 1. The probe's "size-only vs full Möbius 4.98e-60" is an instance.

    • Cumulants vanish across an additive split (cum_eq_zero_of_additive_split): if the ground set splits as A ⊔ B and L S = L (S ∩ A) + L (S ∩ B), then cum L S = 0 for every S meeting both sides. This is what makes the Möbius coefficients cumulants in the standard sense — independence kills every mixed cumulant — and it is exactly the additivity the Markov trace supplies on a split link. The probe's "additivity residual 4.98e-60" is an instance, and QOU.Mass.PairwiseCumulantVanishing (hard-wired to Fin 4, orders 3 and 4) is the special case where every pair is its own block.

    Two smaller facts from the same probe, about the seminormal form and the Hecke relation:

    • seminormal_diag_product: the two diagonal entries of a branching 2×2 seminormal block, a = h/(1 − u⁻²) and a' = h/(1 − u²) with u = q^ρ, multiply to −(h/(u − u⁻¹))², i.e. −1/[ρ]_q². So the off-diagonal radicand a·a' + 1 is 1 − 1/[ρ]², and the abs the corpus's builder wraps around it (sqrt(|a a' + 1|)) is a no-op whenever |[ρ]| ≥ 1 (seminormal_radicand_nonneg).
    • hecke_quadratic_factor / hecke_root_product: x² − h x − 1 = (x − q)(x + q⁻¹) and q · (−q⁻¹) = −1. The det T = −1 the arc leans on is this, read on a block.

    Nothing here is numerical. The numbers live in the probe; the reasons live here.

    Ground ring: any CommRing for the cumulant layer, any Field for the seminormal layer. Bean qou-wx83.
