    QOU.Mass.CoordinationSurfaceDeficit — the boundary deficit of a bounded-degree bond count #

    QOU.Mass.CoordinationSaturation proves the structural dichotomy: complete-graph coordination gives unbounded B/A (refuted by saturation), bounded-degree coordination gives constant B/A = ε·k/2. That constant is a pure volume term: perNucleonBounded counts k·A/2 bonds, i.e. it gives every nucleon k neighbours.

    A finite cluster cannot do that. Nucleons on the boundary have fewer neighbours than interior ones, so the true bond count is smaller than k·A/2 by a boundary deficit. This file is the exact bookkeeping of that deficit and what it does to B/A.

    The result #

    Split A = A_int + A_surf with interior degree k and surface degree k' ≤ k. Handshake gives

    M(A) = (k·A_int + k'·A_surf)/2 = k·A/2 − (k − k')·A_surf/2         (`bondCount_split`)

    so, with a single uniform per-bond energy ε (the only arrangement-invariant regime, by QOU.MassTheory.ChainBondOrderDependence.orderIndependent_iff),

    B(A)/A = ε·k/2 − (ε(k − k')/2)·(A_surf/A)                          (`perNucleon_split`)

    The first term is the SEMF volume term and the second is a deficit with the SEMF sign. Its A-dependence is carried entirely by the surface fraction A_surf/A, so the shape of the correction is a question about the geometry of the cluster, not about the algebra.

    Feeding in the compact-packing surface law A_surf = c·A^{2/3} — stated as a hypothesis, see the scope note — gives exactly

    B(A)/A = ε·k/2 − (ε·c·(k − k')/2)·A^(−1/3)                         (`perNucleon_surfaceLaw`)

    which is the volume-minus-surface form of the semi-empirical mass formula, with no constant beyond the packing data (k, k', c). Two corollaries worth having:

    • perNucleon_lt_plateau — the deficit is strictly negative when k' < k and A_surf > 0, so the plateau is approached from below: B/A < ε·k/2 at every finite A.
    • perNucleon_tendsto_plateau — B/A → ε·k/2 as A → ∞ under the surface law, so CoordinationSaturation.bounded_degree_saturates is recovered as the limit. This file refines that lemma, it does not contradict it.

    And the ratio the data can be checked against is scale-free:

    a_S / a_V = c·(k − k')/k                                            (`surfaceToVolume_ratio`)

    ε cancels, so this is a pure-geometry prediction testable against the measured residual shape.

    Scope — what is proved and what is assumed #

    • Proved: the handshake decomposition, the per-nucleon split, the SEMF shape under the surface law, approach from below, the A → ∞ limit, and the scale-free ratio. All exact, no fit, ε, k, k', c free.
    • Assumed (hypothesis, not proved here): that the coordination graph is a compact 3-D packing whose surface count obeys A_surf = c·A^{2/3}. That is standard packing geometry and is stated as a hypothesis everywhere it is used; proving an isoperimetric bound for sphere packings is not attempted.
    • NOT derived, and this is the honest gap: that the substrate emits such a coordination graph. #6611's decision frame §5.2 asks for an edge set derived from the braid geometry; this file assumes a packing instead, which is a weaker input. The linking-vs-self-energy amplitude assignment (qou-brng) remains the open input, exactly as CoordinationSaturation records.
    • Not a fit. No coefficient here is chosen against data. The probe computations/probes/coordination_surface_deficit_probe.py measures what ratio the AME data want and compares; it does not search on it.

    Ground ring ℝ (§7c: archimedean — real powers A^(2/3) and an order claim).
