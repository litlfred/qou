            Running the accumulator through a concatenation: the second block starts with whatever p-signs the first block contributed.

            theorem QOU.Mass.EpsEqFromMonotonicity.epsCoeff_append_of_blocks {ι : Type u_1} [DecidableEq ι] {A B : List (Letter ι)} {x y : ι} (hxy : x ≠ y) (hA : ∀ l ∈ A, l.1 ≠ y) (hB : ∀ l ∈ B, l.1 ≠ x) :
            epsCoeff (A ++ B) x y = signSum A x * signSum B y ∧ epsCoeff (A ++ B) y x = 0

            The arithmetic core. When the two far components occupy disjoint, ordered blocks of the hub's longitude — no y in the first, no x in the second — the degree-2 Magnus coefficient is exactly the product of the blocks' signed sums, and the reverse ordering vanishes.

            This is where the u * v of ChainTriple.eps_eq comes from. It is a fact about lists; no topology enters.

            theorem QOU.Mass.EpsEqFromMonotonicity.underSum_eq_linking {m s lk : ℤ} (h : 2 * lk = 2 * m * s) :
            lk = m * s

            The other half. A run of 2 m crossings between two strands alternates which passes under, so the hub records exactly m of them, every one carrying the run's sign s. Its signed contribution is therefore m * s — and the pair's total signed crossing count is 2 * lk by the definition of the linking number. The two agree.

            Stated without division, as the identity it is: the measured inputs are that the run has even length and that its length is 2|lk|.
