    No real branch point — the archimedean consequence of the sum of squares #

    Chip on conj:hecke-monodromy-step-two. The probe computations/probes/colour_cubic_branch_monodromy_probe.py reports, as its fourth assertion, "its six roots are complex, none real" — measured numerically at 40 dps. This file proves it: by QOU.Mass.ColourCubicDiscriminant.colourDiscriminant_sos, D(z) = (4z³ + 3z² + 3z + 4)² + 2z²(z² + 1), and over ℝ both summands are non-negative with the second strictly positive unless z = 0, where D(0) = 16. So D never vanishes on ℝ: every branch point of the colour cubic is non-real, and the monodromy the conjecture speaks of is genuinely a statement about loops in ℂ ∖ Δ, not about real intervals.

    Archimedean (ordering on ℝ), hence its own file per §7c; the identity it rests on is generic.
