    Torus-knot pages: the algebra behind total_curvature_is_the_computable_half_probe #

    computations/probes/total_curvature_is_the_computable_half_probe.py (bean qou-m3sh) found that every multi-strand component of the canonical atom closure is a (2, odd)-torus knot — T(2,7) on the sigma branch, T(2,5) / T(2,3) on the sigma^-1 branch — and read three algebraic facts off that by exact integer arithmetic. This file proves the ones that are algebra, so the probe's role on them is reduced to a consistency check.

    • The Seifert form of T(2, n) is unimodular. From the 2-strand positive braid the Seifert matrix is (n-1) × (n-1), -1 on the diagonal and +1 on the superdiagonal. It is upper triangular, so its determinant is (-1)^(n-1), which is 1 for odd n (seifertT2_det, seifertT2_det_eq_one_of_even). That is the self-duality of H_1(page) under the Seifert form — the sense in which the vanishing cycles are a frame with a dual frame — and it holds for every n, not only the three the probe checked.

    • The curvature floor is bookkeeping once Milnor is granted. Milnor: the infimum of total curvature over an isotopy class is 2π · b(L), and b(L) ≥ μ + #knotted. With μ = A + 1 (QOU/Mass/SLq2LabelParity.lean) and #knotted = A (the probe, both branches, A = 2, 3, 4, 6) the floor is 2π(2A + 1) (curvature_floor). The geometry — Milnor's theorem, and the identification of the components as torus knots — is NOT formalised here; this records only that the arithmetic chain is what it says.

    • The pages embed on Bring's surface by the corpus's own criterion. The corpus derives Bring's genus from torus-knot embeddability (EulerCharacteristicSix: T(2,9) must embed, T(2,11) must not, so g = 4). The pages found by the probe are T(2,3), T(2,5), T(2,7), of Seifert rank 1, 2, 3, and they embed at the forced genus (pages_embed_on_bring); T(2,9) is the last that does.

    • Bennequin arithmetic. Both sub-braid shapes that occur, 2 strands with 7 crossings and 3 strands with 8, give χ = k - c = -5 and Bennequin genus 3 (bennequin_chi, bennequin_genus). On the sigma branch that equals g(T(2,7)), so the braid's own surface has minimal genus and — fibered knots having a unique minimal-genus Seifert surface — is the page. The uniqueness theorem is not formalised.

    What is checked by the probe but NOT proved here: the Alexander polynomial det(V - t Vᵀ) = (tⁿ + 1)/(t + 1) and the monodromy order 2n, both verified in exact integer arithmetic at n = 3, 5, 7 and left as that.

      The Alexander polynomial, as far as the algebra goes #

      det(V - X·Vᵀ) for the bidiagonal Seifert matrix above is a tridiagonal determinant, diagonal X - 1, superdiagonal 1, subdiagonal -X. Expanding along the last row gives the continuant recurrence D_m = (X - 1) D_{m-1} + X D_{m-2} with D_0 = 1, D_1 = X - 1. That expansion is the one step NOT carried out in Lean here; what is proved is the closed form the recurrence forces, (X + 1) D_m = X^(m+1) + (-1)^m, so that for T(2, n) with n = 2g + 1 the Alexander polynomial is (X^n + 1)/(X + 1). The probe checks the determinant itself, in exact integer arithmetic, at n = 3, 5, 7.
