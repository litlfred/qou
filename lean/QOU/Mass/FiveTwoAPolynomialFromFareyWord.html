    The 5₂ A-polynomial recovered from the derived triangulation — exact identities #

    Companion to computations/probes/two_bridge_cusp_rows_apoly_probe.py (bean qou-yvyd). From the two-bridge triangulation of S³ ∖ 5₂ built from the Farey word RL (no SnapPy input), that probe takes λ as the primitive cusp class dying in H₁(M), pins the meridian among μ₀ + kλ by the Dehn-filling test (a nondegenerate solution of the gluing equations with H(μ₀+kλ) = 1 and H(λ) ≠ 1 factors through the filling, whose π₁ is then nontrivial; the meridian filling is S³, so only the meridian's frame carries no such point — Property P, [kronheimer2004]), samples the deformation curve and fits, with integer coefficients verified on fresh samples to 1e-41,

    A(L, M) = L³ + L²(M¹⁰ − M⁸ + 2M⁴ + 2M² − 1) + L(−M¹⁴ + 2M¹² + 2M¹⁰ − M⁶ + M⁴) + M¹⁴.

    This file states the two exact identities the probe checks numerically and that a reader can check by hand, as theorems over any commutative ring:

    • meridian filling: A(L, ±1) = (L + 1)³, so every point of the curve over M² = 1 has L = −1 — no representation factors through the meridian filling, the Property-P consistency the frame identification rests on;
    • the involution: L³ M¹⁴ · A(L⁻¹, M⁻¹) = A(L, M), the symmetry every knot A-polynomial has under reversing the orientation of the boundary torus (over a field, L, M ≠ 0).

    Nothing geometric is asserted here; the Newton-polygon slopes {−10, −4, 0} (the literature's {0, 4, 10} for the mirror) are read off the polynomial in the probe.

    -- Ref: [manuscript] computations/probes/two_bridge_cusp_rows_apoly_probe.py -- Ref: [kronheimer2004] Kronheimer–Mrowka, Witten's conjecture and Property P.

      The chirality, pinned against a held table (added 2026-09-07, bean qou-lrvc) #

      Culler's census A-polynomial for K3_2 (= SnapPy's Rolfsen-table 5_2 = m015), read from library/A_census.tgz in SnapPy's (μ, λ) framing, is

      1 + L(M¹⁰ − M⁸ + 2M⁴ + 2M² − 1) + L²(−M¹⁴ + 2M¹² + 2M¹⁰ − M⁶ + M⁴) + L³M¹⁴.

      The mirror image of a knot has A_{K̄}(L, M) = A_K(L⁻¹, M) up to a unit, so the identity below says the derived triangulation is the mirror of the census 5_2 — the same conclusion the cusp shape (−conj τ to 38 digits) and the slopes ({0,4,10} against {−10,−4,0}) give in fivetwo_chirality_frame_vs_snappy_probe.py.
