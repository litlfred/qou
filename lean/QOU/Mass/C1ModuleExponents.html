    def QOU.Mass.C1ModuleExponents.c1ExponentPoly {R : Type u_1} [CommRing R] (q t : R) :
    R

    The C1 exponent polynomial at 0 t² + (q−1)t + (q−1) — the characteristic polynomial of the companion A(0) = [[0,1],[1−q,1−q]]. Its roots are the local exponents.
