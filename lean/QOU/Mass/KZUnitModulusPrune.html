    A unit-modulus monodromy eigenvalue is never the real pin q₀ (Step-2 option C, the prune) #

    Option C-prune of docs/audits/2026-09-06-step-2-plan-hecke-monodromy.md. The corpus's prose says the substrate connection is Knizhnik–Zamolodchikov, "flat at q₀", with monodromy the U_q(su₂) R-matrix and q = e^{2πi/(k+2)} (mass-theory/lifted-crossing-locality.md:15, vertex-operator-dictionary.md:1,3). Kohno–Drinfeld monodromy of a KZ connection at a real level has eigenvalues of the form exp(iθ), θ ∈ ℝ, hence of modulus one; the substrate pin q₀ ≈ 1.11 is real and greater than one. So no such eigenvalue equals q₀: either the level is imaginary (a non-unitary KZ connection) or the substrate connection is not KZ. The corpus wording must name a non-unitary level or be reworded; that edit is the owner's call and is not made here.

    Only the one-line analytic fact is proved (Complex.norm_exp_ofReal_mul_I); the identification of the framework's connection with KZ is not asserted. Archimedean by nature (ℂ, moduli, 1 < q), hence its own file per §7c; the generic-field shadow lemmas are in QOU/Mass/HeckeQuadraticClassicalShadow.lean.

    ‖Complex.exp (↑θ * Complex.I)‖ ≠ q

    C-prune. A monodromy eigenvalue of the form exp(iθ) with θ real has modulus one, so it is never a real number greater than one — in particular never the substrate pin q₀ ≈ 1.11.
