    KZPullbackGenerators — the named hypothesis of Step-2 option C #

    Bean qou-d0qq, plan step 5 of docs/audits/2026-09-07-route-c-step3-and-kz-level-plans.md §B. The Step-2 conjecture QOU.Mass.HeckeMonodromy says the substrate connection's monodromy generators satisfy the Hecke quadratic; option C names which connection. This file carries that name as a dependent conjecture class (§3b-tax): its content is the hypothesis, and the quadratic is derived from it (quad_of_kz), so the class does not restate the conclusion.

    The hypothesis. The substrate connection is the 𝔰𝔩₂ Knizhnik–Zamolodchikov connection on Conf₃(ℂ), pulled back along the root map z ↦ {X₁(z), X₂(z), X₃(z)} of the colour cubic, at the level fixed by the substrate parameter: k + 2 = iπ / ln q in Kohno's convention q = e^{iπ/(k+2)} (q₀ is an input here; nothing derives it). Concretely, for every lasso i around a branch point the substrate generator T.g i is conjugate in the tower to a KZ monodromy generator k that satisfies the Hecke quadratic with the level's q:

    • the conjugacy is what computations/probes/colour_cubic_braid_monodromy_probe.py measured (13/13): every lasso lifts to a positive half-twist σ_j^{+1} up to an explicit conjugator;
    • the quadratic on k is Kohno–Drinfeld — the monodromy of the 𝔰𝔩₂ KZ connection on V^{⊗n} is the R-matrix with eigenvalues q, −q⁻¹ — cited, not formalised.

    What is derived. quad_of_kz: conjugation preserves the quadratic, so every T.g i satisfies it. heckeMonodromy_of_kz then assembles a HeckeMonodromy instance from this class plus the braid, far-commutation and shadow hypotheses — which are not consequences of per-generator conjugacy (the measured conjugators differ from lasso to lasso), so they stay explicit.

    What is not claimed. That the substrate connection is the pulled-back KZ connection is the open hypothesis this class names; no instance is provided, and none should be until the connection is constructed (option C's remaining work). Kohno 1987 and Drinfeld 1985 are cited but not held in library/.

    -- Ref: [kohno1987] Kohno, Ann. Inst. Fourier 37 (1987) — KZ monodromy factors through H_n(q). -- Ref: [drinfeld1985] Drinfeld — quasi-Hopf / R-matrix side of the Kohno–Drinfeld theorem. -- Ref: [manuscript] computations/probes/colour_cubic_braid_monodromy_probe.py

    Prop

    Dependent conjecture (Step-2 option C). Each substrate generator is conjugate, in the tower, to a Knizhnik–Zamolodchikov monodromy generator satisfying the Hecke quadratic with the level's q. The class carries the hypothesis; the quadratic on T.g i is derived below.

    • conj_kz (i : ℕ) : ∃ (k : A), k * k = (q - q⁻¹) • k + 1 ∧ ∃ (u : Aˣ), T.g i = ↑u * k * ↑u⁻¹

      For every lasso there is a KZ generator k with the Hecke quadratic (Kohno–Drinfeld) and a unit u conjugating it to the substrate generator (the measured half-twist lift).
