                      theorem QOU.Mass.HeckeTowerMackey.mackey_level2 :
                      mulT R32 R32 = matadd (mulT [[1], [1]] [[1], [1]]) Id2

                      Mackey / oscillator relation at level 2 (the lepton level): Res∘Ind = Ind∘Res + Id on K₀(H_2): R32·R32ᵀ = [[2,1],[1,2]] = R21ᵀ·R21 + Id₂ = [[1,1],[1,1]] + Id₂. The off-diagonal 1 is the meson bridge: inducing a lepton channel to H_3 and restricting reaches the OTHER lepton channel exactly once — through the entangled (2,1) Hoefsmit block, never through (3) or (1,1,1).
