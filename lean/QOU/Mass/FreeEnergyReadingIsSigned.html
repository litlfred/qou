    The free-energy reading has no energy spectrum #

    rem:bond-log-ratio-is-a-free-energy establishes that L = -log tr_2(sigma + d) is a free-energy difference over the level-2 Wenzl state sum, and records as open the separate claim that B = alpha L is the nuclear binding energy. The evidence already on the books is three misses. This file proves something prior to all of them, and it is not a measurement at q0 but an identity in q.

    A free energy is F = -kT log Z with Z = sum_i exp(-E_i/kT). Every summand of a partition function is a positive Boltzmann factor, and that positivity is what makes F a Legendre transform — hence what gives it an internal energy and an entropy at all. The H_2 state sum here has two summands, carrying the sigma-eigenvalues q on (2) and -q⁻¹ on (1,1):

    tr_2(sigma + d) = w_+ (q + d) + w_- (-q⁻¹ + d).

    The second one is negative for every q > 0 and every d ≤ 0 (minusSummand_neg), and the corpus's interface letters are exactly the non-positive ones — 0, -h/2, d_pn, -h (admissible_letters_nonpos). Positivity would need d > q⁻¹ > 0, so the required offset does not merely lie outside the available range, it lies on the other side of zero from all of it (positivity_would_need_d_gt_inv_q). No q rescues the sign, because q⁻¹ > 0 for every q > 0.

    Consequently no real E_- satisfies exp(-E_-) = -q⁻¹ + d (no_real_energy_for_minus_block): exp is positive and the factor is not. The (1,1) block has no energy, so there is no U and no S for alpha L to be or to differ from, and the step "this free energy is the binding energy" has nothing to stand on.

    What is NOT retracted, and it is most of the arc: the total stays positive (stateSum_pos_but_signed) — the (2) block outweighs the (1,1) block — so L is real, exact, convention-D canonical, and every number computed from it is untouched. The free-energy description is also correct: signed free energies over quasi-probabilities are legitimate objects, and the block names this one correctly when it says "a free energy of a signed observable over a quasi-probability, not of a positive one". What this file removes is the sentence after that.

    Measured companion, with the q0 figures and the pn near-cancellation: the_free_energy_reading_has_no_energy_spectrum_probe.py. Bean qou-qnqf.
