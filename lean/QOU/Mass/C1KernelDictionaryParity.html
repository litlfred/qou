    QOU.Mass.C1KernelDictionaryParity — the C1 kernel cannot carry the Door-v residual #

    C1KernelAsShiftSum (#6582) read the binding kernel

    L_K = (q²x − 1)σ² − (q − 1)σ − (q − 1)

    in the ramified shift calculus under the two natural σ ↔ t-shift dictionaries and found that they disagree on parity: under (a) (σ = unit t-shift) the kernel has an odd component, under (b) (σ = t-shift 2 on the double cover) it commutes with the covering monodromy t ↦ −t. The question this module answers is whether the Door-v (−1)^m residual — the target property #6474 demanded of the prolongation operator's tower — decides between them.

    It does not decide; it kills both. A tower whose level-n jet carries the monodromy character (−1)^n ε from some level n₀ on is zero from that level on, in either dictionary:

    • (b), by monodromy commutation. A monodromy-commuting homogeneous operator T transports the character of level n to level n + 1 unchanged, so alternation at two consecutive levels forces 2 · (−1)^n ε · Tⁿ⁺¹X = 0, hence Tⁿ⁺¹X = 0 (comm_mono_flip_kills, no seed hypothesis). Applied to c1KernelOpCover: c1KernelOpCover_residual_kills_tower.
    • (a), by the e-dependence of the shift-2 weight. #6498's residual_from_level_tower_is_odd_part forces the even part E to kill both the level-n₀ jet Y and O Y. Written out, E Y = 0 is (q^{e+2} − 1) Y(e+2) = (q − 1) Y(e) and E (O Y) = 0 is the same recurrence with q^{e+1} in place of q^{e+2}. Their difference is q^{e+1}(q − 1) Y(e+2) = 0, so Y = 0 for q ≠ 0, 1 (c1E_zero_c1E_O_zero_imp), and the tower is zero from n₀ on (c1KernelOp_residual_kills_tower). Under (a) the jet dies at the level where the alternation starts; under (b) one level later.

    The corpus's Door-v residual is the asymptotic sign alternation of the growing GGM solution — nonzero at every level. So the C1 kernel, read as a shift operator on the jet tower, is not a carrier of the Door-v residual in either dictionary. If the conjecture's identification "symbol = L_K" holds and the prolongation operator is its symbol, the (−1)^m twist is not produced by the prolongation's own iteration; it must sit in the seed or in the pairing (C1 ⊗ C1 vs GGM), not in D_q. And the dictionary question W2 is not settled by the residual — both readings fail it the same way.

    Two invariants, both named: monodromy commutation for (b); for (a), that the shift-2 weight q^{e+2} − 1 is not translation-invariant in e while the zeroth-order weight −(q − 1) is. Nothing here constructs D_q, and an operator whose symbol is L_K but which differs from it by lower-order terms is not excluded.
