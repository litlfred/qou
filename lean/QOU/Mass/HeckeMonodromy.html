    HeckeMonodromy — Step 2 of the canonical chain as a §3b conjecture class #

    Option A of docs/audits/2026-09-06-step-2-plan-hecke-monodromy.md. Cheat-sheet §2a records that Step 2 (geometry → H_n(q)) is stipulated: the curvature 2-form is defined to be the Hecke quadratic F(σ) = σ² − (q − q⁻¹)σ − 1, so "flatness derives the Hecke relation" is a tautology, and the geometry it cites (prop:hecke-spectral-sequence) has no declaration in content/.

    This class is the honest restatement §2a asks for. The conjecture (primary, §3b-tax — no other conjecture class among its binders): the framework's flat connection on the complement of the discriminant locus of the colour cubic has braid-group monodromy landing in the Hecke tower that carries the Markov trace, its generators satisfy the Hecke quadratic and the braid relations, and its classical shadow — under a ring map ε to the q = 1 target — is the Picard–Lefschetz involution measured in colour_cubic_branch_monodromy_probe.py. The fields carry exactly that content; nothing is True, nothing is empty.

    What the file derives from the class (sorry-free, unconditional given the instance):

    • hecke_factor — the generator factors as (g − q)(g + q⁻¹) = 0 in the algebra: the two Stokes/Hecke eigenvalues q, −q⁻¹ of QOU.Mass.HeckeStokes.stokes_curvature_factors are now a consequence of the monodromy relation rather than the definition of a curvature;
    • shadow_hecke_at_one — the shadow satisfies the q = 1 Hecke quadratic (X − 1)(X + 1) = 0;
    • shadow_kills_h — applying ε to the Hecke quadratic and using the involution forces ε(h • g) = 0: the classical shadow is the h = q − q⁻¹ = 0 specialisation, so the deformation parameter is exactly what the shadow forgets. This is the sense in which what remains stipulated is the deformation 1 ↦ q and nothing else.

    What this does NOT do. It does not construct the connection (option C) and it does not define the forms (α, β) route D2 needs. NaStokesKeystone.lean's docstring claim "has curvature 2-form F(σ)" is, from here on, to be read as an instance of this class, not as a fact.

    Base ring §7c: generic Field K for the eigenvalue algebra (division by q), generic Ring A/Ring B for the tower and its shadow; nothing archimedean.

    class QOU.Mass.HeckeMonodromy {K : Type u_1} [Field K] {A : Type u_2} [Ring A] [Algebra K A] (T : MathlibExt.OcneanuTrace.MarkovTrace K A) (q : K) {B : Type u_3} [Ring B] (ε : A →+* B) :
    Prop

    Conjecture (Step 2 as monodromy). For a Markov-trace tower T : MarkovTrace K A with generators T.g i, a parameter q ≠ 0, and a ring map ε : A →+* B to the classical target: the generators are the monodromies of the substrate connection around the branch points of the colour cubic, so they satisfy the Hecke quadratic with h = q − q⁻¹, the braid and far-commutation relations of the braid group, and under ε each becomes an involution (the Picard–Lefschetz transposition on the two colliding roots).

    • quad (i : ℕ) : T.g i * T.g i = (q - q⁻¹) • T.g i + 1

      The Hecke quadratic on every generator, with h = q − q⁻¹.

    • braid (i : ℕ) : T.g i * T.g (i + 1) * T.g i = T.g (i + 1) * T.g i * T.g (i + 1)

      The braid relation between neighbours.

    • far_comm (i j : ℕ) : i + 2 ≤ j → T.g i * T.g j = T.g j * T.g i

      Far commutation.

    • shadow_involution (i : ℕ) : ε (T.g i) * ε (T.g i) = 1

      The classical shadow of every generator is an involution: the branch-point transposition.

      The shadow is a Coxeter representation #

      The probe finds the classical monodromy group to be S₃, generated by transpositions. Pushing the three relation fields through ε shows why that is forced by the class: the shadow generators satisfy the Coxeter presentation of the symmetric group — involutions, braid relation, far commutation — so ε ∘ ϱ factors through S_n and is a permutation representation.
