    Binding residual exponent — chain-topology derivation of 10A − 5 #

    The canonical trace-binding B_pred(Z,N) = E₀·(tr_M(W_free) − tr_M(W_atom)) undershoots AME by a near-integer q₀-power: Δ_λ(K) = B_AME / B_pred ≈ q₀^(10A−5) (deuteron exact to 0.01 exponent units; audit docs/audits/2026-06-09-delta-lambda-canonical-residual.md). This file formalizes the derived part of that exponent — its decomposition over the chain topology of the W_atom braid word — reducing the open question from "why 10A−5" to "why 5 per topological unit".

    The W_atom braid (atom_braid_word_3A) is a chain: A = Z+N nucleons, each a 3-strand 𝓑₃ on g0(7) + g1(4) = 11 crossings, linked by A−1 interface crossings (bonds). Hence

    • len(W_free) = 11·A (constituents, interfaces dropped)
    • len(W_atom) = 11·A + (A−1) (chain: one extra crossing per bond)

    so W_atom − W_free = A−1 is exactly the bond count. The residual exponent factorizes over the chain's 2A−1 topological units — A nucleons plus A−1 bonds — each carrying the same per-unit weight w:

    exponent(w, A) = w·A + w·(A−1) = w·(2A−1).

    At the measured per-unit weight w = 5 this is exactly the observed 10A − 5. The 2A−1 = nucleons + bonds decomposition is derived (chain topology, proved below); the per-unit w = 5 is the reduced open constant (candidate structural origin: the Bring/S₅ five-fold). The per-bond reading (not "10 per nucleon − 5 global") is what carries the Z-dependent correction: same-A isotopes with different bond types (³He {pp,pn} vs T {pn,nn}, since the interface factors f_pp = z ≠ f_nn = z − h) deviate (³He by −1.40 exponent units, the only proton-rich A = 3 case).

    Base ring: this is pure combinatorial bookkeeping over ℕ (crossing/bond counts) — no archimedean construct, no q₀. No fit: the per-unit weight 5 is carried as a parameter; only the chain decomposition is asserted.
