    The H₆ interface: the pp bond (σ₂σ₃⁻¹)^m is selected by the H₃ sign table through branching #

    Bean qou-ales, step 6 (owner order 2026-09-12: after merging step 5, "3" — the H₆ pp bond in Lean). Step 3 (#7192) measured on all eleven Wedderburn blocks of H₆ that, among the γγ bond candidates G^m = (σ₂σ₃⁻¹)^m on the interface between two nucleon blocks, the minimal non-zero survivor under the Frobenius reading is G³, with the (3,3) block the worst offender for every other m. This file explains that table by the branching rule and proves it on an interval.

    The mechanism #

    The bond word lives in the parabolic subalgebra ⟨σ₂, σ₃⟩ ≅ H₃. Restricted to it, each H₆ irreducible V_λ decomposes into H₃ irreducibles with multiplicities (a_λ, b_λ, c_λ) for ((3), (2,1), (1³)), and on a γγ word of that subalgebra the (3) and (1³) characters are 1 (trivChar_eq_one, altChar_eq_one), so

    χ_λ(G^m) = a_λ + b_λ · χ_(2,1)(V^m) + c_λ            (`branchedChar`).

    By the branching rule (Hoefsmit 1974; for the middle-strand embedding, conjugation by the distinguished coset representative T_d, Dipper–James) the multiplicities are path counts in Young's lattice from μ ⊢ 3 to λ ⊢ 6; this file computes them by decide (branchOf_*) and checks f^λ = a + 2b + c on all eleven blocks (dim_eq_branch) and Σ (f^λ)² = 720 (fDim_sq_sum).

    Convention D (the two-row truncation, owner ruling 2026-08-15 / 2026-09-08) puts the Markov weight on the two-row partitions (6), (5,1), (4,2), (3,3) only; their branching data are (1,0,0), (3,1,0), (3,3,0), (1,2,0). So under reading 3 the bond G^m is feasible iff 1 + 2χ_m ≥ 0 at (3,3) (the other three are weaker), and the H₃ sign table decides it.

    What is proved (all sorry-free) #

    • branchOf_six … branchOf_threeThree, dim_eq_branch, fDim_sq_sum — the path counts.
    • interfaceBond_gammaGamma — (σ₂σ₃⁻¹)^m is γγ-produced.
    • InterfaceBranching q B — the hypothesis that the block data B takes the branched values on the interface bonds (the branching rule, stated, not derived here; verified at the proton pin by gamma_gamma_h6_interface_branching_probe.py, 24 values to 1e−59 against the step-3 witness).
    • interface_frobeniusFeasible_iff — under it, reading-3 feasibility of G^m is the sign condition on the branched characters of the positive-weight blocks.
    • h6_signs_of_small — for every 0 < x ≤ 1/12, 1 + 2χ_m(x) < 0 for m = 1, 2, 4, 5. The interval is narrower than step 4's 1/10: 1 + 2χ_5 changes sign near x ≈ 0.0924, so the H₆ selection is not a theorem on x ≤ 1/10; 1 < q ≤ 23/20 gives h² ≤ 0.0787 ≤ 1/12 (h_sq_le_twelfth_of_q_le) and still contains the pin.
    • interface_frobenius_table — for every 1 < q ≤ 23/20, every two-row-supported weight with y_(3,3) > 0: G¹, G², G⁴, G⁵ infeasible, G³ feasible.
    • interfaceSelected_three — the minimal-crossing feasible non-zero bond with m ≤ 5 is G³.

    What is NOT proved #

    • The branching rule itself (the InterfaceBranching hypothesis).
    • The diproton sign B_pp = −4.47 keV: it needs the full 11-letter nucleon words in H₆, and stays a step-3 measurement.
    • m > 5; non-γγ letters.

    Nothing here reads AME2020, a CODATA binding value, or a census.

    Base ring (§7c) #

    The Young-lattice section is pure ℕ. branchedChar and the interval lemmas are on ℝ, as the step-4/5 sign lemmas they extend.
