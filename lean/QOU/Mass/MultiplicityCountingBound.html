    theorem QOU.Mass.MultiplicityCountingBound.abs_sum_le_card_mul {R : Type u_1} [Ring R] [LinearOrder R] [IsStrictOrderedRing R] {ι : Type u_2} (s : Finset ι) (a : ι → R) (M : R) (hM : ∀ i ∈ s, |a i| ≤ M) :
    |∑ i ∈ s, a i| ≤ ↑s.card * M

    Many summands buy at most a factor of their number. A signed sum whose summands are bounded by M is bounded by card · M. This is the entire multiplicity channel: the triangle inequality, and no more is available.
