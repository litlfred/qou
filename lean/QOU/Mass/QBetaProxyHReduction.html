    QOU.Mass.QBetaProxyHReduction — the CAL-2 proxy 1/h-scale exact reduction #

    Formalize-first result behind the deuteron "1/h scale" probe (computations/probes/probe_d_qbeta_h_invariant.py, part (i); audit docs/audits/2026-07-03-deuteron-from-scratch-error-ledger.md).

    With the bandwidth h = q − q⁻¹, the categorical scale E₀ = 1/(q(q−1)) (in m_e units, cheat-sheet step 11), and the CAL-2 proxy closed form Q_β = m_e·|q²−2q−1|(q+1)/(2q³) — whose numerator resolves to 2q − q² + 1 on the substrate branch 1 < q < 1+√2 — the following is an algebraic identity in the fraction field (sympy-proved in ℚ(q); here proved over a generic [Field R] per AGENTS.md §7c, no ℝ-specific construct):

      Q_β_proxy = E₀ · h · (2 − h) / 2        ⟺        Q_β_proxy / h = E₀ · (2 − h)/2.

    Structural reading (observation tier, not wired — Δ_λ gate): the mysterious 1/h binding scale is exactly E₀ dressed by (1 − h/2), the bar-average of the smoothed crossing pair {1, 1−h} (from σ⁻¹ = σ − h) at the deuteron's single p–n interface.
