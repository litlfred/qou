    The classical shadow of the Hecke quadratic is a reflection #

    Step-2 option B of docs/audits/2026-09-06-step-2-plan-hecke-monodromy.md (cheat-sheet §2a: the curvature 2-form F(σ) = σ² − (q − q⁻¹)σ − 1 is stipulated, not computed).

    B — the order-zero shadow is derived. The probe computations/probes/colour_cubic_branch_monodromy_probe.py continues the three roots of the colour cubic around each of its six simple branch points and finds a transposition every time (Picard–Lefschetz), the six generating S₃ with identity product. On the two colliding roots a transposition is the swap S = [[0,1],[1,0]], an involution with eigenvalues ±1. This file records the algebra that makes that the q = 1 shadow of Step 2:

    • hecke_quadratic_at_one — X² − (1 − 1⁻¹)X − 1 = (X − 1)(X + 1): the Hecke quadratic at q = 1 is the minimal polynomial of a reflection;
    • swap_sq, swap_satisfies_hecke_at_one — the swap is an involution and satisfies that quadratic; swap_eigen_one, swap_eigen_neg_one — its eigenvectors, so the two Stokes/Hecke eigenvalues q, −q⁻¹ of stokes_curvature_factors specialise to +1, −1 on the swap;
    • Equiv.swap on Fin 3 squares to the identity (transposition_sq) — the monodromy element itself, as a permutation of the three roots.

    So the relation Step 2 stipulates is, at q = 1, a theorem about branch-point monodromy; what remains stipulated is exactly the deformation 1 ↦ q.

    The archimedean companion (option C's prune: a unit-modulus KZ eigenvalue is never the real pin q₀) lives in QOU/Mass/KZUnitModulusPrune.lean, split off per §7c.

    Base ring §7c: everything here is over a generic field K (matrix and polynomial identities).

    theorem QOU.Mass.HeckeQuadraticClassicalShadow.hecke_quadratic_at_one {K : Type u_1} [Field K] (X : K) :
    X ^ 2 - (1 - 1⁻¹) * X - 1 = (X - 1) * (X + 1)

    The Hecke quadratic X² − (q − q⁻¹)X − 1 at q = 1 is (X − 1)(X + 1): the minimal polynomial of a reflection. (The q ≠ 0 factorisation stokes_curvature_factors specialised.)
