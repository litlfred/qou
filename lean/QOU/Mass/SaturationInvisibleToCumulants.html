        theorem QOU.Mass.SaturationInvisibleToCumulants.ratios_are_scale_free {R : Type u_1} [CommRing R] (γ f₂ f₃ f₄ : R) :
        b3Contrib (γ * f₂) (γ * f₃) = γ * b3Contrib f₂ f₃ ∧ b4Contrib (γ * f₂) (γ * f₃) (γ * f₄) = γ * b4Contrib f₂ f₃ f₄

        The single-term consequence. With the saturating half inert, one term carries all three cumulants, so its b₃/b₂ and b₄/b₂ are fixed by its shape alone — no coefficient can move them. Stated as: scaling a term by γ leaves both ratios unchanged.
