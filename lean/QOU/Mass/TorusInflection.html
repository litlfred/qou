    The Frenet framing of T(2, q) on the torus jumps at r = 4 / (q² + 4) #

    computations/probes/the_torsion_half_is_not_an_invariant_probe.py (bean qou-m3sh) measures total curvature, total torsion, writhe and the Frenet self-linking Lk_F = Wr + (1/2π)∮τ ds along the family of torus embeddings

    γ(t) = ((1 + r cos qt) cos 2t, (1 + r cos qt) sin 2t, r sin qt),   0 < r < 1,

    of the (2, q)-torus knot, q odd. It found Lk_F integral at every r but NOT constant: -2q on the fat side and -q on the thin side, the jump sitting where the sampled curvature minimum touched zero. So even the integer half of Călugăreanu is not an invariant of the knot type — the Frenet framing is a framing of the curve, and it is discontinuous across an inflection.

    This file proves the algebra of where that happens. At t = π/q one has cos qt = -1, sin qt = 0, and with ρ = 1 - r, c = cos(2π/q), s = sin(2π/q) the derivatives are

    γ'(π/q)  = (-2ρ s, 2ρ c, -q r),
γ''(π/q) = ((q² r - 4ρ) c, (q² r - 4ρ) s, 0)

    (the differentiation is done symbolically by the probe and NOT formalised here; the two vectors are taken as the definitions vel and acc). Their cross product is

    γ' × γ'' = (q² r - 4ρ) · (q r s, -q r c, -2ρ (c² + s²))          (`cross_vel_acc`)

    and the second factor is non-zero for r < 1, so the curvature vanishes at t = π/q exactly when q² r = 4(1 - r), i.e. r = 4 / (q² + 4) (inflection_iff). For the three component types of the canonical closure that is r* = 4/13, 4/29, 4/53 (inflection_radius_three_five_seven) — the trefoil value matches the probe's golden-section estimate 0.307692 — and cos(q t) = -1 at the q distinct parameters t_k = (2k + 1)π/q, k < q (cos_q_mul_inflection, inflection_params_injective), which is why the jump in Lk_F is by q.

    Not formalised: that γ' and γ'' are the derivatives of γ, that these are the only inflections of the family, and the jump itself (a statement about framings). Bean qou-m3sh.
