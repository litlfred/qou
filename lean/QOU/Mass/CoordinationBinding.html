    theorem QOU.Mass.CoordinationBinding.coordination_degree {A : ℕ} (v : Fin A) :
    ⊤.degree v = A - 1

    Per-nucleon coordination number z(A) = A − 1. In the fully-coordinated cluster of A nucleons — the complete graph ⊤ : SimpleGraph (Fin A) — every nucleon is bonded to the other A − 1, i.e. its degree is A − 1. This is the topological integer the arc identifies with the open-shell multiplier M(open shell).

    theorem QOU.Mass.CoordinationBinding.coordination_handshake {A : ℕ} :
    ∑ v : Fin A, ⊤.degree v = 2 * A.choose 2

    Handshake identity. Summing the per-nucleon coordination degree over all A nucleons double-counts every bond: ∑_v deg v = 2 · C(A,2). Ties the two counts above together.

    structure QOU.Mass.CoordinationBinding.BondExtensiveBinding (A : ℕ) :
    Type

    Conditional extensive-binding class (the OPEN input, not derived). Asserts that the many-body binding energy of the A-nucleon cluster is ε per nucleon–nucleon bond times the bond count C(A,2), with a non-negative per-bond energy ε. The field extensive is the arc's open hypothesis (b_αα-type per-bond constant is empirical, not from the 13 drivers); this class carries it so downstream facts are verified conditional on it, never asserting it holds.

    • epsilon : ℝ

      per-bond binding energy (archimedean, ℝ)

    • binding : ℝ

      the many-body binding energy of the A-nucleon cluster

    • epsilon_nonneg : 0 ≤ self.epsilon

      per-bond energy is non-negative

    • extensive : self.binding = self.epsilon * ↑(A.choose 2)

      OPEN hypothesis: binding is extensive in the bond count C(A,2)

      theorem QOU.Mass.CoordinationBinding.two_mul_binding_eq_epsilon_sum_degrees {A : ℕ} (h : BondExtensiveBinding A) :
      2 * h.binding = h.epsilon * ↑(∑ v : Fin A, ⊤.degree v)

      Coordination bridge: 2·B = ε · ∑_v z(A). Twice the (conditional) extensive binding energy equals the per-bond energy times the summed coordination degrees ∑_v deg v. This is the machine-checked combinatorial certificate the file's purpose calls for: it routes the binding law's bond count through the proved handshake identity (coordination_handshake) rather than through the bare A.choose 2, so the extensivity is tied to the actual per-nucleon coordination degrees. Still fully conditional on BondExtensiveBinding — it certifies the shape of the law against the graph, it does not assert the law holds.
