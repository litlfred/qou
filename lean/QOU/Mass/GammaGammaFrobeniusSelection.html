    The confinement SDP on braid words, reading 3: Frobenius positivity selects β_Bor #

    Bean qou-ales, step 5. Owner ruling 2026-09-12 (chat): "try PSD confinement 3 for now, but keep 1 2 for later." Three readings of "the confinement SDP at q₀ selects a braid word" were on the table after steps 3 and 4:

    1. literal — every Wedderburn projection π_λ(w) is Matrix.PosSemidef (step 1's SDPSelected; on H₃ this admits a non-trivial γγ word iff some vertex power is ±1, vtx_pow_isHermitian_iff_scalar, and for 1 < q ≤ 23/20 no k ≤ 5 survives, hoefsmit_vtx_pow_not_isHermitian);
    2. symmetric part — π_λ(w) + π_λ(w)ᵀ is PSD (SymPSDFeasible below);
    3. Frobenius positivity — on every block carrying positive Markov weight y_λ > 0, the character χ_λ(w) = tr π_λ(w) is non-negative (FrobeniusFeasible below). This is the reading adopted for now.

    This file formalises reading 3 and proves what it selects on H₃, and keeps readings 1 and 2 on record by proving the only relation between them that is free: 1 ⇒ 3 and 2 ⇒ 3 (a PSD matrix has non-negative trace; the symmetric part has the same trace). Hence whatever readings 1 or 2 select later has cost at least the reading-3 minimum (FrobeniusSelected.cost_le_of_psdFeasible, …_of_symPSDFeasible).

    What is proved (all sorry-free) #

    • frobeniusFeasible_iff_std — for a γγ-word on H₃, reading-3 feasibility is decided by the (2,1) block alone: (3) and (1³) have character 1.
    • vertexPow_frobeniusFeasible_iff — for the vertex power (σ₀σ₁⁻¹)^k it is 0 < y_(2,1) → 0 ≤ tr V^k, with V the vertex image of step 4.
    • hoefsmit_vertexPow_frobenius_table — at the concrete Hoefsmit data, for every 1 < q ≤ 23/20 and every y with y_(2,1) > 0: k = 1, 2, 4, 5 are infeasible and k = 3 is feasible (the sign table of step 4, chi_signs_of_small, read as feasibility).
    • frobeniusSelected_borromean — the minimal non-zero Frobenius-feasible vertex power with k ≤ 5, cost = crossing count, is k = 3, i.e. β_Bor, for every 1 < q ≤ 23/20 (the proton pin q₀ ≈ 1.10998 is inside) and every positive weight on the (2,1) block. Under convention D at the pin the step-3 witness has y_(3) = 0.5054, y_(2,1) = 0.2473, y_(1³) = 0; only the sign of y_(2,1) enters here.

    What is NOT proved #

    • Nothing about k > 5 (the candidate set is vertexPowCands 5, the γγ words of H₃ with at most ten crossings); chi_signs_of_small stops at five.
    • Nothing about non-γγ letters (step-3 §4.2) or the H₆ interface.
    • Reading 3 is the owner's provisional choice, not a derivation of which reading the confinement SDP means; readings 1 and 2 stay recorded above.
    • No AME, CODATA binding value or census is read anywhere in the cone.

    Base ring (§7c) #

    FrobeniusFeasible / FrobeniusSelected are over a generic [Ring R] [PartialOrder R]. The comparisons with readings 1 and 2 use Matrix.PosSemidef.trace_nonneg, which Mathlib states for RCLike, so they are over ℝ; the H₃ results are over ℝ as in steps 3 and 4.
