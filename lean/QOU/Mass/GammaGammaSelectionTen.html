    Candidates up to ten vertices: the reading-3 survivors are the powers of the q-deformed 3-cycle #

    Bean qou-ales, step 7 (owner order 2026-09-12 "1 3 2", item 2: extend the H₃ candidates to k ≤ 10). Steps 4–6 stopped at five vertices because the sign lemmas did. This file carries the Chebyshev closed forms to k = 10 and extends both selection theorems.

    What is proved (all sorry-free) #

    • lin_six … lin_ten, trace_vtx_pow_six … ten — V^k = α_k V + β_k 1 and χ_k = tr V^k in closed form, x = h²: χ₆ = x⁶ + 6x⁵ + 9x⁴ − 4x³ − 12x² + 2, χ₇ = −x⁷ − 7x⁶ − 14x⁵ + 21x³ + 7x² − 7x − 1, χ₈ = x⁸ + 8x⁷ + 20x⁶ + 8x⁵ − 30x⁴ − 24x³ + 12x² + 8x − 1, χ₉ = −x⁹ − 9x⁸ − 27x⁷ − 21x⁶ + 36x⁵ + 54x⁴ − 9x³ − 27x² + 2, χ₁₀ = x¹⁰ + 10x⁹ + 35x⁸ + 40x⁷ − 35x⁶ − 98x⁵ − 15x⁴ + 60x³ + 15x² − 10x − 1.
    • chi_signs_six_to_ten — on 0 < x ≤ 1/10 the signs are (+, −, −, +, −), so with step 4 the pattern for k = 1..10 is − − + − − + − − + −: χ_k > 0 ⇔ 3 ∣ k on the whole interval (vertexPow_frobeniusFeasible_iff_three_dvd). The survivors are the powers of V³, the q-deformed identity of step 4.
    • frobeniusSelected_borromean_ten — on H₃, with candidates 1 ≤ k ≤ 10, reading 3 still selects k = 3, for every 1 < q ≤ 23/20: the new survivors k = 6, 9 cost 12 and 18.
    • h6_signs_six_to_ten — the H₆ (3,3) condition 1 + 2χ_m for m = 6..10 has signs (+, −, −, +, −) on 0 < x ≤ 1/18. The interval narrows again: 1 + 2χ₈ changes sign at x ≈ 0.0581 (inside step 6's 1/12), so on (0, 1/12] the m = 8 row is not sign-definite. 1 < q ≤ 10/9 gives h² ≤ 0.0446 ≤ 1/18 (h_sq_le_eighteenth_of_q_le); the pin h² = 0.0437 is inside, with little room.
    • interface_frobenius_table_ten — on 1 < q ≤ 10/9, under the branching hypothesis and a two-row weight with y_(3,3) > 0: G⁶, G⁹ feasible, G⁷, G⁸, G¹⁰ infeasible; hence interface_frobeniusFeasible_iff_three_dvd for 1 ≤ m ≤ 10.
    • interfaceSelected_three_ten — the selection of G³ among m ≤ 10 needs only G¹, G² infeasible and G³ feasible, so it holds on the wide interval 1 < q ≤ 23/20.

    What is NOT proved #

    k > 10; the branching rule (still the hypothesis InterfaceBranching); and whether the 3 ∣ k pattern persists for all k — it is a statement about cos(kθ) with 2 cos θ = −1 − h², i.e. about θ/π, which step 4 left open. Nothing here reads AME2020, a CODATA binding value, or a census.
