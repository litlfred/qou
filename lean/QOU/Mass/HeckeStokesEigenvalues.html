    QOU.Mass.HeckeStokesEigenvalues — Stokes-curvature eigenvalues and the [2]_q origin of τ #

    This file supplies the Lean link between the Yekutieli non-abelian Stokes step of the categorical chain and the lever-1 trace τ being a function of the quantum integer [2]_q.

    Step 2 of the chain (non-abelian Stokes; mass-theory/pre-archimedean-derivation-chain.md, prop:hecke-spectral-sequence). The categorical R-matrix connection is conjectured to have curvature 2-form F(σ) = σ² − (q − q⁻¹)σ − 1 — the hypothesis is the §3b class QOU.Mass.HeckeMonodromy (2026-09-06; cheat-sheet §2a records that the corpus stipulates rather than computes this form, and that only its q = 1 shadow is derived). Granting it, flatness F = 0 gives the Hecke quadratic σ² = (q − q⁻¹)σ + 1; the factorisation below is unconditional algebra. The two roots of that characteristic polynomial are the Hecke eigenvalues — the symmetric (L₊) and antisymmetric (L₋) braiding channels of the Hecke–Skein convention (AGENTS.md §7a):

    λ₊ = q (positive crossing), λ₋ = −q⁻¹ (negative crossing).

    Why this matters for τ. TauQuantumIntegerForm proves τ(q) = 3(3[2]² + 2)/(4([2]+2)) with [2] = [2]_q = q + q⁻¹, hence bar-even (τ(q⁻¹) = τ(q)). This file pins where that even structure comes from: the quantum dimension [2]_q is the gap λ₊ − λ₋ between the two Stokes/Hecke eigenvalues, which is bar-even — whereas their sum λ₊ + λ₋ = q − q⁻¹ (the curvature coefficient itself) is bar-odd. So τ's bar-evenness is inherited from the quantum dimension (the eigenvalue gap), not from the curvature trace.

    Ground ring: a generic field (the identities are fibre-level / q-formal, no ℝ ordering needed), matching the §7c base-ring discipline and TauQuantumIntegerForm.

    theorem QOU.Mass.HeckeStokes.stokes_curvature_factors {K : Type u_1} [Field K] (q : K) (hq : q ≠ 0) (X : K) :
    X ^ 2 - (q - q⁻¹) * X - 1 = (X - q) * (X + q⁻¹)

    The non-abelian-Stokes curvature factors into the two Hecke eigenvalues. The flatness characteristic polynomial X² − (q − q⁻¹)X − 1 factors as (X − q)(X + q⁻¹): roots λ₊ = q (symmetric channel L₊) and λ₋ = −q⁻¹ (antisymmetric channel L₋).

    The two Hecke eigenvalues have product −1 (λ₊ · λ₋, the Hecke-generator determinant).

    theorem QOU.Mass.HeckeStokes.stokes_eigenvalues_sum {K : Type u_1} [Field K] (q : K) :
    q + -q⁻¹ = q - q⁻¹

    Their sum is the curvature coefficient q − q⁻¹ (λ₊ + λ₋), which is bar-odd.

    source
    theorem QOU.Mass.HeckeStokes.quantum_dim_is_eigenvalue_gap {K : Type u_1} [Field K] (q : K) :
    q - -q⁻¹ = q + q⁻¹

    The quantum dimension [2]_q = q + q⁻¹ is the eigenvalue gap λ₊ − λ₋. q − (−q⁻¹) = q + q⁻¹. This bar-even combination — not the bar-odd sum above — is what τ is built from (TauQuantumIntegerForm), which is why τ is bar-even.

    theorem QOU.Mass.HeckeStokes.quantum_dim_bar_even {K : Type u_1} [Field K] (q : K) :
    q⁻¹ + q⁻¹⁻¹ = q + q⁻¹

    [2]_q is bar-even: [2]_{q⁻¹} = [2]_q, i.e. q⁻¹ + (q⁻¹)⁻¹ = q + q⁻¹. (The eigenvalue gap is invariant under the Hecke bar-involution q ↦ q⁻¹; the curvature coefficient q − q⁻¹ is not.)
