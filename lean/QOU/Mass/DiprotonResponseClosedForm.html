    The γγ-selected diproton response in closed form — and it is FOURTH order, not second #

    Bean qou-ales, step 10. Step 9 left exactly one quantity measured: the trace-weighted response

    r = tr(W·D) / tr(W),        D = (G³ − 1)/h²,   W = the two free 11-letter nucleon words,

    carried there as a bare positive hypothesis. This file carries its closed form, obtained by exact symbolic evaluation of the convention-D Markov trace on H₆ over ℚ(q) — every letter of both nucleon words and of the bond, all four weight-support blocks, no numerics and no anchor. With

    P(q) = q¹⁸ − 3q¹⁶ + 7q¹⁴ − 8q¹² + 10q¹⁰ − 9q⁸ + 10q⁶ − 6q⁴ + 3q² − 1      (`nucleonPoly`)
R(q) = q¹⁰ − q⁸ + 4q⁶ − 4q⁴ + 2q² − 1                                     (`responsePoly`)

    the computation gives

    r(q) = (q² − 1)²·(q⁴ + q² + 1)·R(q) / ((q² + 1)·P(q)).

    The correction this forces on step 9 #

    (q² − 1)² = q²h², so r itself carries a factor h²:

    r(q) = h²·c(q),      c(q) = q²(q⁴ + q² + 1)R(q) / ((q² + 1)P(q)),

    and therefore the interface ratio is

    μ − 1 = h²·r = h⁴·c(q),

    so the binding magnitude is Θ(h⁴), not Θ(h²). Step 9's bracket is correct as stated — r is a free hypothesis there — but its headline order is two powers too weak, and the reason is precisely the one step 9's own correction considered and dismissed: it asserted that tr(W·D) "carries no such cancellation". It does. Measured at the pin: c = 0.5537297, an order-one number, and h⁴c = 0.00105775 reproduces step 3's ratio 1.0010577525451598436 to every digit it carries.

    Three different orders are in play and conflating any two of them is the trap:

    quantity order in h
    the bond's matrix deviation G³ − 1 h² (step 4, vtx_three_sub_one)
    the bond's character deviation χ_(2,1)(V³) − 2 h⁴ (step 9, bond_character_deviation)
    a single block's mixed response (χ_λ(WG³) − χ_λ(W))/h² h¹ (one factor q² − 1)
    the y-weighted response r h² — the extra power appears only after weighting

    So the h⁴ of the ratio and the h⁴ of the bond's own character are not the same fact; they arise from different cancellations and agree by arithmetic, not by mechanism.

    What is proved here (all sorry-free) #

    • nucleonPoly_pos, responsePoly_pos — P(q) ≥ 4 and R(q) ≥ 1 for every q ≥ 1, by re-expanding in t = q − 1: every coefficient of P(1+t) and R(1+t) is non-negative.
    • responseCoeff_pos and responseCoeff_le_one — 0 < c(q) ≤ 1 for every q ≥ 1. The upper bound is the same mechanism: (q²+1)P(q) − q²(q⁴+q²+1)R(q) has non-negative coefficients in t with constant term 5. Both bounds hold on the whole ray, with no interval hypothesis.
    • respPP_eq_hSq_mul_coeff — the factorisation r = h²c, the step-9 correction in one line.
    • diproton_unbound_of_response_closed_form — for every q > 1 the γγ-selected diproton is unbound. No measurement, no interval: c > 0 forces μ > 1 forces B < 0.
    • binding_magnitude_fourth_order — the step-9 bracket at the derived response, with both ends carrying h⁴.
    • binding_ceiling_on_interval — on 1 < q ≤ 23/20, |B| ≤ α/100. Since α is a closed form in q and CAL-1 m_e, this is a fully derived keV ceiling: at the pin α/100 = 42.27 keV against the actual 4.469 keV. The deformation alone forces the scale.
    • ResponseClosedForm — the one hypothesis that remains: that the H₆ convention-D Markov trace of these words is the rational function above. That is a statement about the Hoefsmit/branching machinery, verified by the probe to 60 dps against two independent code paths (symbolic ℚ(q) and the mpmath seminormal path of step 3), not proved in Lean.
    • nucleonTrace and the ResponseClosedForm.free_eq field — the free trace factorises as the square of the single nucleon's H₃ trace, disjoint-union multiplicativity, recorded because it is what makes the denominator of c a nucleon quantity rather than a two-body one.

    Scope #

    The word is step 3's: the canonical 11-letter nucleon element (which, since 7db032fb48, opens with β_Bor = (σ₀σ₁⁻¹)³) on generators (0,1) and (3,4), with the colour-blind −h/2 interface letters, and the bond G³ = (σ₂σ₃⁻¹)³ on (2,3). Nothing here reads AME2020, a CODATA binding value, or a census. The dineutron channel and the p–n asymmetry are untouched.

                The bond moves the trace by exactly the derived response.
