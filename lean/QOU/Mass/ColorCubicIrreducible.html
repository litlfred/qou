    QOU.Mass.ColorCubicIrreducible — the colour cubic has no polynomial root in z #

    The colour spectral cubic of the Lever-1 binding kernel (QOU.Mass.ColorSpectralCubicGeometry) is

    C_z(X) := X³ + (z+1)X² + (z²+z+1)X + (z³+z²+z+1).

    This file proves, for every field K of characteristic zero, that no polynomial r ∈ K[z] is a root: C_z(r) ≠ 0 in K[z]. Since C_z is monic over K[z] and K[z] is integrally closed, a root in K(z) would already lie in K[z] (Gauss); a cubic with no root over a field is irreducible. So C_z(X) is irreducible over K(z), i.e. the three colour sheets X_i(z) are genuinely algebraic of degree 3 and none of them is a rational function of z.

    Why this matters for the prolongation-symbol keystone (bean qou-1z28) #

    The W1 wall (qou-d8de) asks for a q-difference prolongation D_q on the curve, and its first named route is a q-shift on the base, z ↦ q²z, with a q-connection lift. A rational rank-3 q-difference system in z ↦ λz whose solutions are the three sheets would give each sheet a scalar linear q-difference equation with rational coefficients (four shifts of one sheet in a 3-dimensional K(z)-span). Each sheet is algebraic, hence D-finite (the classical prolongation, proved exact in prop:sw-classical-jet-exactness). A power series that is both D-finite and q-holonomic with λ not a root of unity is rational (Bézivin 1994; Ramis 1992; unified in Schäfke–Singer, Consistent systems of linear differential and difference equations, JEMS 21 (2019)). Irreducibility — this file — is exactly what makes that conclusion contradictory. The analytic theorem is cited, not formalised; what is formalised here is the algebraic half the citation needs.

    Proof shape #

    r ∈ K[z] of degree d ≥ 2: the leading term r³ has degree 3d, strictly above the other three terms (1+2d, 2+d, 3), so C_z(r) has degree 3d ≠ 0. Degree ≤ 1, r = a z + b: the four z-coefficients of C_z(az+b) are

    z³ : (a+1)(a²+1), z² : 3a²b + a² + 2ab + a + b + 1, z¹ : 3ab² + 2ab + a + b² + b + 1, z⁰ : (b+1)(b²+1),

    and each of the two branches a = −1, a² = −1 of the leading coefficient reduces the z² / z⁰ pair to an inconsistent system, with an explicit integer Bézout certificate (5 = 0, resp. 65 = 0). Characteristic zero is used exactly there. The certificates were computed by exact sympy in computations/probes/w1_base_qshift_rationality_prune_probe.py and are checked here by linear_combination.

    Ground ring: a generic [Field K], [CharZero K] only where a nonzero integer must be nonzero (§7c). Nothing archimedean.
