    QOU.Mass.BindingHyperbolicRecurrence — Layer 1: the hyperbolic (cosh/sinh) recurrence #

    The n ≤ 4 effective layer of the binding prolongation a_D^(n). Sibling PR #2795 settled that the global a_D^(n) is super-exponential (lw/#1765, a q-Bessel-class object — see the companion BindingQBesselKernel), while the depth-2 SL₂ recurrence

    a_D^(n+2) = τ · a_D^(n+1) − a_D^(n), det = 1 (sl2-prolongation-recurrence)

    is the geometric effective reading fit to the three binding anchors n = 2, 3, 4 (deuteron n=2, ⁴He magic-shell n=4). NaStokesKeystone.tau_in_stokes_eigenvalue_gap proved τ = 3(3[2]²+2)/(4([2]+2)), a bar-even number ≥ 2.

    The binding Layer-1 tower is the Reeb growth tower #

    The cosh/sinh recurrence is already formalised generally in QOU.QBeta.ReebTowerJacobiAnger (reeb_tower_cosh_step / reeb_tower_sinh_step, the bar-even / bar-odd Reeb towers). This file specialises that machinery to the binding index tower n ↦ a_D^(n) and adds the two binding-specific facts not in the Reeb file: the det = +1 transfer factorisation and the τ = 2 cosh θ trace.

    Even/odd = cosh/sinh — the answer to "what comes out naturally" #

    With τ = 2 cosh θ, the two independent solutions of the depth-2 recurrence are the two hyperbolic functions of nθ:

    a_D^(n) = cosh(nθ) (even in θ — the bar-even / [2]_q / m_π channel; τ lives here) a_D^(n) = sinh(nθ) (odd in θ — the bar-odd / curvature-coefficient / Q_β channel)

    The bar-involution q ↦ q⁻¹ is θ ↦ −θ, so this is the same even/odd split as NaStokesKeystone.tau_closed_form_bar_even (cosh) vs NaStokesKeystone.curvature_coeff_bar_odd (sinh). τ, built from the even gap [2]_q alone, lands on the cosh branch ⟹ τ = 2 cosh θ.

    Ground ring ℝ (archimedean: Real.cosh/Real.sinh/Real.exp), matching §7c — the hyperbolic angle θ is a post-τ archimedean datum.

    theorem QOU.Mass.BindingHyperbolicRecurrence.binding_kernel_factors (lam x : ℝ) (hlam : lam ≠ 0) :
    x ^ 2 - (lam + lam⁻¹) * x + 1 = (x - lam) * (x - lam⁻¹)

    The binding transfer kernel factors into a det = +1 hyperbolic eigenvalue pair. R(q,x) = x² − (λ + λ⁻¹)x + 1 = (x − λ)(x − λ⁻¹) for λ ≠ 0. The two roots λ, λ⁻¹ multiply to +1 — the SL₂(ℝ) hyperbolic pair e^{±θ} of the depth-2 binding recurrence. This is the det = +1 companion of the Hecke curvature kernel x²−(q−q⁻¹)x−1 = (x−q)(x+q⁻¹) (det = −1, QOU.Mass.HeckeStokes.stokes_curvature_factors): binding is the symmetric hyperbolic face. (Not in the Reeb file — the binding-transfer determinant is the genuinely new datum here.)

    theorem QOU.Mass.BindingHyperbolicRecurrence.cosh_satisfies_binding_recurrence (θ : ℝ) (n : ℕ) :
    Real.cosh ((↑n + 2) * θ) = 2 * Real.cosh θ * Real.cosh ((↑n + 1) * θ) - Real.cosh (↑n * θ)

    cosh(nθ) is the EVEN solution of the depth-2 binding recurrence with τ = 2 cosh θ: cosh((n+2)θ) = 2 cosh θ · cosh((n+1)θ) − cosh(nθ). The binding-index specialisation of the bar-even Reeb tower reeb_tower_cosh_step (at a = (n+1)θ, η = θ).

    theorem QOU.Mass.BindingHyperbolicRecurrence.sinh_satisfies_binding_recurrence (θ : ℝ) (n : ℕ) :
    Real.sinh ((↑n + 2) * θ) = 2 * Real.cosh θ * Real.sinh ((↑n + 1) * θ) - Real.sinh (↑n * θ)

    sinh(nθ) is the ODD solution of the depth-2 binding recurrence with τ = 2 cosh θ: sinh((n+2)θ) = 2 cosh θ · sinh((n+1)θ) − sinh(nθ). The binding-index specialisation of the bar-odd Reeb tower reeb_tower_sinh_step. Same recurrence as cosh — the recurrence is blind to the even/odd boundary data; that is exactly the cosh/sinh degeneracy of a det = +1 hyperbolic transfer.

    theorem QOU.Mass.BindingHyperbolicRecurrence.binding_trace_two_cosh (θ : ℝ) :
    Real.exp θ + (Real.exp θ)⁻¹ = 2 * Real.cosh θ

    The binding trace is 2 cosh θ when the eigenvalue is e^θ. e^θ + (e^θ)⁻¹ = 2 cosh θ, so the det = +1 pair λ = e^θ, λ⁻¹ = e^{−θ} has trace τ = 2 cosh θ — the hyperbolic-angle form of the depth-2 binding trace τ = 2 cosh θ_binding ≈ 2.6424 (θ_binding ≈ 0.78145).
