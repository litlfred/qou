    The γγ vertex is the q-deformed 3-cycle: powers, symmetry, and the Chebyshev table #

    Bean qou-ales, step 4 — the k ≥ 2 non-symmetry left open in §4.3 of the step-3 record (docs/audits/2026-09-11-gamma-gamma-word-selection-step3.md). Step 3 proved that the (2,1)-block image V of one pair-production vertex σ₀σ₁⁻¹ is not symmetric, and measured (to k ≤ 5, at the proton q₀) that no power V^k is either. This file replaces the measurement by algebra.

    What is proved (all sorry-free) #

    Let h = q − q⁻¹ and let V = ρ_(2,1)(σ₀σ₁⁻¹) be the Hoefsmit image of the vertex (stdImage_vertex_entries). Everything below is over a generic field unless an order is used.

    • vtx_det, vtx_trace — from the Hoefsmit relations alone, det V = 1 and tr V = −1 − h².
    • sq_eq_of_det_one — Cayley–Hamilton for a 2×2 matrix of determinant one, M² = (tr M)·M − 1; hence pow_eq_lin: M^k = α_k M + β_k 1 with α_{k+1} = t α_k + β_k, β_{k+1} = −α_k (lin).
    • vtx_pow_isHermitian_iff — on ℝ with b ≠ 0, V^k is symmetric ⇔ α_k = 0 ⇔ V^k = ±1 (vtx_pow_isHermitian_iff_scalar). So the literal Matrix.PosSemidef reading admits a non-trivial γγ word in H₃ iff some vertex power is a scalar — iff θ/π is rational, where 2 cos θ = tr V.
    • vtx_three — V³ = (2h² + h⁴)·V + (1 + h²)·1, i.e. V³ − 1 = h²·((2 + h²)·V + 1); hence vtx_three_eq_one_iff: V³ = 1 ⇔ h = 0. At q = 1 the vertex is the 3-cycle s₀s₁ ∈ S₃, so the k = 3 survivor of step 3 (β_Bor) is its q-deformation — which is why it lies within 0.11 of the identity at q₀ and why the selected pp bond moves the diproton by only 4.5 keV.
    • trace_vtx_pow_* — the probe's χ_(2,1) column is the Chebyshev recurrence χ_{k+1} = (tr V)·χ_k − χ_{k−1} in closed form: with x = h², χ₁ = −1 − x, χ₂ = x² + 2x − 1, χ₃ = 2 − 3x² − x³, χ₄ = x⁴ + 4x³ + 2x² − 4x − 1, χ₅ = −x⁵ − 5x⁴ − 5x³ + 5x² + 5x − 1.
    • chi_signs_of_small — for every 0 < x ≤ 1/10 the signs are (−, −, +, −, −) for k = 1..5, and lin_ne_zero_of_small — α_k ≠ 0 for k = 1..5. The proton pin has h² ≈ 0.0437 (h_sq_le_of_q_le), so the step-3 table is a theorem on an interval, not a point measurement: under the Frobenius reading the minimal non-zero survivor is k = 3 for every such q, and under the literal reading no k ≤ 5 survives for any such q.

    What is NOT proved #

    Whether some V^k = ±1 for some k at the proton pin — i.e. whether θ/π ∈ ℚ — is not decided here; it is the arithmetic of cos θ = −(1+h²)/2 at a transcendental-looking q₀, and lin_ne_zero_of_small covers k ≤ 5 only. Nothing here reads AME, CODATA, or a census.

    Base ring (§7c) #

    lin, Cayley–Hamilton, pow_eq_lin, the trace/determinant identities and the closed-form traces are over a generic [Field R] (or [CommRing R]). Only the symmetry criterion (Matrix.IsHermitian on ℝ), the sign lemmas and the q bound use an order.
