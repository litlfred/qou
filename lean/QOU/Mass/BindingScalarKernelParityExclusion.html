    Tier 0 — the invariant, as pure arithmetic #

    The whole exclusion rests on two facts about ℕ. They are isolated here so that the obstruction is visible without any polynomial algebra.

    source
    theorem QOU.Mass.BindingScalarKernelParityExclusion.riccati_top_degrees_ne (p u : ℕ) :
    1 + 2 * p ≠ 2 * u

    The parity. The PP term of the cleared q-Riccati has degree 1 + 2p and the QQ term has degree 2u; the first is odd-shaped and the second even, so they can never coincide. Equivalently 2u − 2p = 1 has no solution over ℤ. This single fact is the Newton slope's denominator 2.

    source
    theorem QOU.Mass.BindingScalarKernelParityExclusion.riccati_degree_unique_max (p u : ℕ) :
    p + u < 1 + 2 * p ∧ 2 * u < 1 + 2 * p ∨ p + u < 2 * u ∧ 1 + 2 * p < 2 * u

    Unique strict maximum. For every pair of degrees, exactly one of the three cleared-Riccati terms attains the top degree: T₁ = 1 + 2p when u ≤ p, and T₃ = 2u when p < u. The middle term T₂ = p + u never wins. A lone top-degree term has nothing to cancel against.

        THE EXCLUSION. For any field K and any q ≠ 0, 1, the cleared q-Riccati residual of L_𝒦 is nonzero for every pair of nonzero polynomials. Hence the q-Riccati has no solution a ∈ K(x), so L_𝒦 has no first-order right factor and the rank-2 module 𝒦 is irreducible.

        The proof is the parity: by riccati_degree_unique_max exactly one of the three terms attains the top degree — the PP term (degree 1 + 2p) when u ≤ p, the QQ term (degree 2u) when p < u — and by sub_sub_ne_zero_of_top_unique a lone top-degree term cannot cancel.

        Tier 4 — how much an UNCONSTRAINED kernel would leave undetermined #

        Logically independent of the exclusion, and it sharpens conj:binding-manybody-eq-jet-filtration's "supplies a derived target for R(q,x) but does not determine it" into an exact count.

        source
        theorem QOU.Mass.BindingScalarKernelParityExclusion.kernel_coeff_existsUnique {K : Type u_1} [Field K] {q : K} (h : ℕ → K) (h0 : h 0 ≠ 0) (r : ℕ → K) (k : ℕ) :
        ∃! rk : K, (q ^ (k + 1) - 1) * h (k + 1) = rk * h 0 + ∑ j ∈ Finset.range k, r (j + 1) * h (k - j)

        Exactly one new kernel coefficient per body order. Matching the coefficient of x^{k+1} in G(qx) = R(q,x)·G(x) expresses (q^{k+1} − 1)·h_{k+1} as r_{k+1}·h_0 plus a tail involving only kernel coefficients of order ≤ k. For ANY body sequence with h_0 ≠ 0 that equation has exactly one solution r_{k+1} — whatever the body terms are.

        So kernel coefficients and body terms are in bijection, one for one: an unconstrained scalar ansatz is a reparametrisation of the unknowns, not a determination of them, and "ρ·A is an output of the recursion" is false as stated — the recursion transfers the unknown, it does not remove it. Every bit of the ansatz's content therefore lives in the FINITENESS of the Laurent window, which is precisely what no_rational_riccati_solution shows is unattainable in the scalar form.
