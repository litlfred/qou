    The intra-nucleon ordering: what it moves, and what it provably cannot #

    computations/probes/the_intra_ordering_moves_absolute_traces_not_the_bond_model_probe.py (beans qou-hu1f, qou-gpb5) measured that the two production assemblies of one nucleon's intra word — the grouped σ₀³σ₁⁻³ that atom_braid_word_3A emitted before 2026-09-05, and the interleaved (σ₀σ₁⁻¹)³ that is β_Bor — give traces differing by 3.785× to 14.325× at A = 2 … 4, while the character ratio tr_M(W_atom)/tr_M(W_free) is identical under both to ~1e-60.

    This file proves the algebra behind that split. It is deliberately a scalar file, like QOU.Mass.ChainTraceFactor which it extends: the Hecke-algebra input — that the chain closure is a connected sum and the Markov trace is multiplicative over it, so that tr_M(W_atom) = F · ∏ᵢ (z + dᵢ) and tr_M(W_free) = F for a common intra factor F — is a classical theorem this file assumes rather than formalises, exactly as ChainTraceFactor assumes it. What is proved is everything that follows from it.

    What is proved #

    • The ratio cannot see the intra ordering (chainRatio_eq_of_intra). Two orderings with intra factors F₁ and F₂ — arbitrary, unrelated, and in the measurement a factor of 14 apart — give the same ratio. No hypothesis links F₁ to F₂; the invariance is cancellation, not coincidence. Hence L and the three bond constants are untouched by the ordering (L_chain_eq_sum_of_intra), which is the formal content of the probe's ~1e-60 agreement.

    • The difference route can, and does (chainDiff_scales, chainDiff_ne). binding_canonical computes tr_M(W_free) − tr_M(W_atom), which equals F (1 − P) and so is exactly proportional to the intra factor: changing the ordering scales it by F₁/F₂ and changes its value whenever F₁ ≠ F₂ and the interface product P ≠ 1.

    • The two confinement words are different objects (grouped_ne_borromean). The grouped word's value is the per-generator product tr₂(σ³) · tr₂(σ⁻³); the interleaved word's is the Borromean closed form (4 + h² − 3h⁴ − h⁶)/(h² + 4) of QOU.BraidsAndKnots.BorromeanMarkovTrace. At q = 1 — the undeformed point, where h = 0 and both are elementary — the first is 1/4 and the second is 1. So they differ as functions of q, and the discrepancy is not an artefact of the deformation. (At q₀ the measured values are 0.180064539535501098 and 0.998562163836787266, an 82 % gap; those sixty digits are the probe's, not this file's.)

    What is NOT proved #

    That the interleaved word is the one the framework wants — that is settled by quark-antiquark-production-reduction ("confined by the Borromean braid B₃") plus the 5e-60 match to the closed form, both outside Lean. That the chain trace factors at all (the connected-sum input above). Any numerical value at q₀. And nothing here says the bond model is right — only that whatever it is, the intra ordering cannot change it. B = α L remains the open interpretation of rem:bond-log-ratio-is-a-free-energy.

    The difference route can, and the scaling is exact #

    theorem QOU.Mass.IntraOrdering.chainDiff_scales {F₁ F₂ P : ℝ} (h₂ : F₂ ≠ 0) :
    F₁ - F₁ * P = F₁ / F₂ * (F₂ - F₂ * P)

    binding_canonical's Δtr is proportional to the intra factor. Its tr_M(W_free) − tr_M(W_atom) equals F (1 − P), so changing the ordering multiplies it by F₁/F₂ — the same 3.785 … 14.325 the probe measures on the traces themselves.

    The two confinement words are different objects #

    tr₂(σᵏ) = w₊ qᵏ + w₋ (−q⁻¹)ᵏ, the level-2 Wenzl expectation of the block eigenvalues. The grouped word σ₀³σ₁⁻³ has each generator's letters contiguous, so its trace is the per-generator product tr₂(σ³) · tr₂(σ⁻³). The interleaved word (σ₀σ₁⁻¹)³ does not, and its trace is the Borromean closed form.
