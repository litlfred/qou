    QOU.Mass.DeuteronBindingDressing #

    Proposition (conditional on conj:borromean-quark) for content block prop:deuteron-binding-dressing-proton-net: The deuteron binding dressing (1 − h/2) is the proton single-wrap normal-form net coefficient net(p).

    Empirically (probe probe_d_qbeta_h_invariant / probe_d_dressing_is_proton_net) the trace-canonical deuteron binding is reproduced to +0.12 % of AME2020 by B_D = E₀ · (1 − h/2) · Δtr with E₀ = m_e / (q(q−1)) and Δtr the free−bound Markov-trace difference. This file shows the dressing factor (1 − h/2) is not a new object: it is exactly net(p) = 1 − h/2, the proton [u,u,d] single-wrap NF net coefficient carried by conj:borromean-quark (the same conjecture the neutron Q-value already assumes — see QOU.ParticleInteractions.neutron_qvalue_symbolic).

    The load-bearing algebra (all exact in a field, derived from the same class inputs — no new hypothesis, no fit):

    • Δ(net) = net(n) − net(p) = −h · net(p) (division-free)
    • |Δ(net)| = h · net(p) (0 < h < 2 regime)
    • Q_β = E₀ · h · net(p) (from the Q-value ansatz)
    • E₀ · net(p) · Δtr = Q_β · Δtr / h (h ≠ 0)

    so the bar-even binding dressing net(p) and the bar-odd Q-value are the same categorical object separated by the single Hecke factor h (h = q − q⁻¹, bar-odd). This identifies the dressing structurally; the magnitude match to AME remains observation-tier and conditional on conj:borromean-quark (NOT wired — the Δ_λ gate stands).
