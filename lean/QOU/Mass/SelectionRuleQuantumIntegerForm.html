    This file completes the categorical [2]_q derivation of the lever-1 binding-kernel trace, joining the two ends already in the corpus:

    • HeckeStokesEigenvalues — the quantum integer [2]_q = q + q⁻¹ is the gap λ₊ − λ₋ between the two Hecke / non-abelian-Stokes eigenvalues λ₊ = q, λ₋ = −q⁻¹;
    • TauQuantumIntegerForm (#2774) — τ = 3(3[2]² + 2)/(4([2] + 2)) is a function of [2]_q.

    The middle of the chain is the selection-rule mean quantum dimensions lw(c) (the anchors a_D^(c+1)). They are proved in the corpus in q-form / h-form (h = q − q⁻¹, bar-odd): lw(1)=1, lw(2)=([2]_q+2)/3 (selection-rule-c2-prediction, the sum-form), and lw(3) = 5/2 + (3/4)(q − q⁻¹)² (selection-rule-anchors-c1-c3). This file adds the two missing links: lw(3) in the bar-even [2]_q = q + q⁻¹ form, and the explicit assembly τ = (lw(1) + lw(3))/lw(2) landing on TauQuantumIntegerForm's closed form — so the whole trace is visibly built from the single quantum integer [2]_q.

    Ground ring ℝ (the selection-rule anchors live there, q₀-evaluable; matches the c1–c3 files). Pure field algebra (field_simp; ring). This does not touch the open keystone (the geometric↔categorical identity a_D^(n) = ∮ λ_SW^(n) = lw(n−1)); it formalises the categorical half, lw → τ, in [2]_q.

    Scope — the depth-2 form is the n ≤ 4 germ, NOT the period recurrence (fork verdict, coordination on #2795; companion Lean lemma QOU.Mass.sl2Invariant_eq_zeroth / prop:sl2-period-conserved-quantity).
