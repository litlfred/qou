    QOU.Mass.C1KernelAsShiftSum — the C1 kernel is not a derivative-only operator #

    conj:qdifference-kernel-prolongation-symbol asks that the binding kernel

    L_K = (q²x − 1)σ² − (q − 1)σ − (q − 1), x = q^m, σ : x ↦ qx,

    be the symbol of the jet-prolongation operator D_q at a ramified pole of the colour curve. The ramified shift calculus (#6474, #6498, #6523) narrowed the admissible D_q from the target properties (residue, (−1)^n residual) and left one class alive at the low body orders: a derivative-only mixed-shift operator — every term annihilates constants — such as Jackson [e+1]_q σ + q-d/dz [e+2]_q σ².

    This module reads the kernel itself in the same calculus and asks whether such an operator can have it as its symbol. It cannot, in either natural dictionary.

    • Dictionary (a): σ = the unit t-shift, x = q^e. Then L_K = shiftOp 2 (q^{e+2} − 1) − (q−1)·shiftOp 1 (1) − (q−1)·id (c1KernelOp). The shift-2 term is derivative-like (q^0 − 1 = 0); the shift-1 term has the constant multiplier −(q−1), which does not vanish at e = −1; and there is a zeroth-order term.
    • Dictionary (b): the ramified double cover, x^{1/2} = u^e, q = u², σ = the t-shift by 2 (c1KernelOpCover). All shifts are even; the zeroth-order term is −(u² − 1).

    The invariant is one line: a derivative-only sum kills the constant seed (sumOp_derivativeOnly_const), and the kernel does not — its zeroth-order term evaluates the constant seed to −(q − 1) ≠ 0 in every dictionary (c1KernelOp_const_ne_zero, c1KernelOpCover_const_ne_zero). Hence derivativeOnly_ne_c1KernelOp / derivativeOnly_ne_c1KernelOpCover: no derivative-only shift-sum is the kernel. If the conjecture holds, D_q has a non-derivative component — the #6474 clause reached by a third route, from the kernel's own shape rather than from the residue or the residual.

    The kernel is, on the other hand, exactly in the #6474 admissible class under (a): odd shift with a non-vanishing multiplier at −1 (c1Kernel_shiftOne_not_annihilatesConstants), and its residue on a power-series seed is dead at level 1 and alive at level 2, reading the branch value and the leading square-root coefficient: L_K² f (−2) = (q − 1)² (f 0 − f 1) (c1KernelOp_residue_two). Under (b) the residue is alive already at level 1, −(u² − 1) f 0, and the operator commutes with the covering monodromy (c1KernelOpCover_comm_mono), so it carries no odd component at all.

    Nothing here identifies D_q with L_K. It says what D_q would have to be if the identification held.
