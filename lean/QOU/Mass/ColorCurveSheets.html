    Vieta reconstruction. If X₁,X₂,X₃ are the three sheets over z — i.e. their elementary symmetric functions are the (q-integer) coefficients of C_z — then they reconstruct the curve: (X−X₁)(X−X₂)(X−X₃) = C_z(X). The sheets are governed collectively by the q-integer symmetric functions; branch selection breaks this S₃ symmetry.

    The defect is the product of gaps to the shifted sheets. On Σ (C_z(X)=0), if Y₁,Y₂,Y₃ are the sheets over the shifted base qz (C_{qz}(Y)=∏(Y−Yᵢ) at Y=X), then the q-shift defect is Δ_q^z C(X,z) = (X−Y₁)(X−Y₂)(X−Y₃). Thus the branch is coherent (defect 0) iff X equals one shifted sheet; selecting that sheet is the branch datum D_q must provide.
