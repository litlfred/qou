    The admissible descent of the energy IS the crossing count #

    crossingEnergy_lt_of_cancelling_pair says the energy is a word functional, so it does not descend to the braid element. A word functional has exactly one canonical descent — its extremum over the fibre — and here that extremum is computable, because the writhe is constant on a braid class while the count is not.

    isLeast_energy_iff_isLeast_count_of_writhe_eq is the consequence, over any finite family of presentations sharing a writhe (which every braid class is): the energy-minimal presentation is exactly the count-minimal one. So making the energy admissible turns it into the crossing-count cost it was proposed to replace, up to an additive invariant.

    This is why the Bring embedding is the load-bearing part of the owner's proposal rather than an implementation detail. The corpus's own QOU.MobiusEnergy.ClassicalMobiusEnergy is likewise a functional of an EMBEDDING — its docstring says so outright, "this structure holds no isotopy-class data" — so it inherits the same defect, and what repairs it is either the FHW minimiser or a canonical embedding. The Bring surface is the candidate canonical embedding. Without one, swapping in an embedding energy swaps one presentation-dependent cost for another.

    The energy-minimal presentation is the count-minimal one. Over any finite nonempty family of presentations sharing a writhe — which is what a braid class is, the writhe being invariant — minimising crossingEnergy and minimising the crossing count are the same problem.
