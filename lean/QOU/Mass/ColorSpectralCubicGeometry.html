    QOU.Mass.ColorSpectralCubicGeometry — branch geometry of the color spectral cubic #

    The color (formerly "coral") spectral curve of the Lever-1 binding kernel is the cubic in the color coordinate X, over the spacetime ratio z = √q − 1/√q:

    C_z(X) := X³ + (z+1)X² + (z²+z+1)X + (z³+z²+z+1) (color-spectral-cubic)

    with Seiberg–Witten differential λ_SW = X dz. Its ramification (branch) points in z — where two color sheets collide — are the zeros of the X-discriminant disc_X C_z. They are the locations of the exceptional divisors S_c over which the abelianised Cartan connection (mi-sw-abelianisation-cartan) and the jet-prolongation λ_SW^(n) live, so the branch locus is the geometric substrate of the prolongation residues.

    This file formalises the (a)-baseline geometry that the Lever-1 first probe (lever1_color_sigma_geometry_probe.py, PR #2755) established numerically: the closed form of disc_X C_z and its palindromic z ↦ 1/z symmetry.

    Companion to QOU.Mass.ColorCubicPowerSums (the color-root power sums p_n = −(zⁿ+1)), which records that the spectral data is q-free — forcing the binding prolongation to be a q-difference operator (z2bn second probe). This file adds the orthogonal datum: the branch locus (discriminant + its z ↦ 1/z symmetry), where the exceptional divisors S_c and the prolongation residues sit.

    Scope. A SUPPORTING brick for the #1765 prolongation identity a_D^(n) = ∮ λ_SW^(n) = lw(n−1). It does not construct the prolongation operator itself (the owner-gated, multi-session keystone — the abelianised λ_SW holonomy / the q-difference kernel R(q,x)); it only certifies the curve geometry beneath it. The bare differential's residue route is already pinned negative (Res(X dz)=0 at every finite branch point, PR #2755); the open core is the explicit λ_SW^(n) on S_c, not this geometry.

    Ground ring is a generic [CommRing R] (§7c): the identities are q-rational at the fibre level — pure field algebra, closed by ring / field_simp, with no archimedean construct (the substrate-anchor specialisation R := ℝ at the spacetime ratio z = √q − 1/√q happens at the consumer). Only the palindromic lemma (which inverts z) needs [Field R].

      Palindromic symmetry of the branch locus. D is palindromic, i.e. z⁶ · D(1/z) = D(z): the six ramification points are stable under z ↦ 1/z (coefficients [16,24,35,50,35,24,16] read the same both ways). The reciprocal symmetry of the branch locus is a constraint any prolongation residue sum on the S_c must respect.
