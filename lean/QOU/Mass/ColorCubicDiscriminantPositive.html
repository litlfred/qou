    Colour-cubic discriminant: palindromic + strictly positive on ℝ #

    Companion to QOU.Mass.ColorCubicNoBranch (irreducibility route) and QOU.Mass.ColorCurveQIntegerForm (q-integer coefficient form). Here we record the explicit discriminant of the trefoil colour cubic

    C(X;z) = X³ + (z+1)X² + (z²+z+1)X + (z³+z²+z+1) = [1]_z X³ + [2]_z X² + [3]_z X + [4]_z (the q-integer cubic),

    which a symbolic computation (sympy.discriminant) gives as

    Δ(z) = -(16z⁶ + 24z⁵ + 35z⁴ + 50z³ + 35z² + 24z + 16),

    a sextic ⇒ the colour channel has exactly 6 branch points (= the 6 saddle points of Bring's surface). We prove two structural facts about the sextic P(z) := -Δ(z):

    • discSextic_palindromic — palindromic / reciprocal: over a field, for z ≠ 0, z⁶ · P(z⁻¹) = P(z). This is the q ↦ q⁻¹ bar-symmetry of the colour channel, at the level of the branch locus.
    • discSextic_pos — strictly positive on ℝ: 0 < P(z) for every real z, via an exact SOS certificate. Hence Δ(z) < 0 for all real z (colorCubicDiscriminant_neg): the colour cubic has no real branch point, i.e. all 6 saddle points are strictly off the real spacetime axis. The saddle-point orbit is genuinely complex — so a torus-knot offset relative to it has a nonzero angle by construction.

    The SOS certificate (from an exact PSD Gram of the sextic): P = (1/400)(80z³+60z²+2z+17)² + c₁(504z²+426z−43)² + c₂(4386z+3721)² + D₃ with c₁, c₂, D₃ > 0 — the three squares are the nlinarith hints below.

    §7c: discSextic and the palindromic functional equation are stated over a generic CommRing / Field; positivity is an archimedean (ℝ) statement.

    Bean qou-vhv6 (well-posed torus-knot → q-Bring offset). Derived, not fitted: the discriminant is computed from the corpus colour cubic and the SOS is a machine-verifiable certificate. The identity Δ(z) = disc_X C(X;z) itself is symbolic-exact (sympy), not re-proved in Lean here — discSextic is the recorded result whose properties this file certifies.
