          theorem QOU.Mass.BrunnianBindingChannel.brunnian_channels_strict_mono (m : ℕ) (q : ℝ) (hq : 1 < q) :
          signedChannel m m q = signedChannel (m + 1) (m + 1) q ∧ countChannel m m q < countChannel (m + 1) (m + 1) q

          The two mutual invariants genuinely diverge for a Brunnian pair. The signed channel is zero for every m (any number of sign-cancelling crossings), while the count channel strictly increases with m. So "linking number" and "coordination count" are not two names for one multiplier: on a Brunnian link they take different values, and only the unsigned one is the binding carrier.

          When do the two mutual invariants agree? — the all-positive criterion #

          The channel lemmas above compare the two channels' values. The sharper question is when their multiplicities — the writhe n₊ − n₋ = 2·lk and the crossing count n₊ + n₋ — agree, since it is the multiplicity that plays the role of the binding multiplier. They agree exactly on all-positive braids (n₋ = 0), and are maximally separated on Brunnian pairs (n₊ = n₋, signed multiplicity 0). The deuteron's positive Hopf carrier is the all-positive case, which is why the linking number and the coordination count give the same answer there and diverge from A ≥ 3.

              Sub-question 1 — the crossing count → coordination count map (bean qou-brng (1)) #

              Each pair inside the Borromean carrier L6a4 is a minimal Brunnian bond: two inter-component crossings of opposite sign (n₊ = n₋ = 1, pairwise linking number 0). Its count-channel multiplicity ½(n₊+n₋) = 1 — one unit of coordination per bonded pair. A fully-bonded A-nucleon cluster then carries C(A,2) coordination units and per-nucleon z = A−1. Which pairs actually bond is the coordination-graph question — saturation-limited for heavy A (QOU.Mass.CoordinationSaturation).

              theorem QOU.Mass.BrunnianBindingChannel.cluster_coordination_count (A : ℕ) :

              Coordination count of a fully-bonded cluster.               Sub-question 2 — bar-parity: the assignment is FORCED, not chosen (bean qou-brng (2)) #

              Why does the binding/mutual multiplier enter as a bare integer count while the mass/self multiplier keeps the full q-dimension self-trace lw = (Σ_{λ⊢c+1} dim_q λ)/(c+1)? Because the two live in complementary bar-parity sectors of the bar-involution q ↦ q⁻¹:

              • the mass/self multiplier is bar-even — a quantum-dimension self-trace, and every quantum dimension satisfies dim_q = dim_{q⁻¹} (e.g. [2]_q = q + q⁻¹), so lw is invariant under q ↦ q⁻¹ (the cosh sector);
              • the mutual/binding count channel ∝ (q − q⁻¹) is bar-odd — it changes sign under q ↦ q⁻¹ (the sinh sector).

              A nonzero bar-even function is never bar-odd, so a mass-type multiplier can never be a binding-type one: the assignment is forced by parity. This is the same cosh/sinh split the corpus uses elsewhere (Q_β is the bar-odd sinh partner of the bar-even cosh meson scale m_π; the crossing-energy strong channel is q+q⁻¹, the Coulomb channel q−q⁻¹). What stays open is the Hamiltonian-level claim that rest mass IS the closed self-trace and binding IS the open mutual pairing — the parity distinction is proved here; its dynamical origin is not.
