    Why the crossing model's mutual channel cannot be a bare pair sum #

    Two results landed on main on 2026-08-30 from different sessions, and this file is the consequence of putting them together.

    • QOU.Mass.SelfMutualPartition.binding_eq_neg_mutualEnergy (bean qou-brng, #6145) — with binding defined the standard way, B := Σ(isolated self masses) − (assembled mass), the self energies cancel exactly and B = −(mutual energy), where the mutual energy is the sum of energy c over the inter-component crossings of the assembled diagram.
    • QOU.Mass.PairwiseCumulantVanishing.b3_eq_zero_of_isPairwise (bean qou-bhzt, #6101) — a pairwise-additive binding has identically vanishing connected 3-body cumulant.

    The first is a sum over crossings, and every crossing joins exactly two components, so that sum groups by unordered pair with no further hypothesis: mutualEnergy = Σ_{i<j} w_ij. That looks like the second result's IsPairwise, and if it were, R2 would be refuted on contact — AME2020 measures b₃ = +1.4262225 (QOU.MassTheory.AmeBindingNotPairwise.ame_b3_eq), not zero.

    It is not, and the gap is exactly one hypothesis: whether a pair's weight w_ij is the same in every assembly containing that pair. Call that PairLocal. This file proves that the hypothesis is the whole of the difference, and therefore that the measured b₃ forces the crossing structure between two components to depend on what else is present.

    The results #

    • isPairwise_of_pairLocal — assembly-independent pair weights ⇒ the induced binding map is pairwise-additive.
    • b3_eq_zero_of_pairLocal — hence b₃ = 0 identically.
    • not_pairLocal_of_b3_ne_zero — hence a nonzero measured b₃ refutes pair-locality. This is the load-bearing direction: it is a constraint the framework's carrier has to satisfy, derived from AME rather than assumed.

    The proposed escape, and why it is NOT available — CORRECTED 2026-09-07 #

    This section originally read: "A Brunnian carrier is precisely a maximal violation of PairLocal … the corpus already establishes that the ³He/⁴He carrier L6a4 is Brunnian (QOU.Mass.BrunnianBindingChannel, #6125) … b3_eq_neg_tripleMutual_of_brunnian is the falsifiable payoff … Measuring b₃ therefore pins the Brunnian triple channel to a specific number." The escape does not work, and the cited file is what rules it out.

    Two different properties were sharing the name Brunnian:

    condition true of L6a4?
    topological (QOU.KnotTheory.Brunnian, BrunnianBindingChannel) pairwise linking number = 0, i.e. n₊ = n₋ yes
    the hypothesis below, now NoIsolatedPairEnergy pairwise mutual energy w₂ = 0 no

    BrunnianBindingChannel establishes the first and then proves brunnian_binding_survives: for a crossing pair at q > 1 the count channel is strictly positive, so w₂ ≠ 0. Its whole point is that binding survives the Brunnian vanishing — the opposite of what the citation above claimed it established. Independently, the AME cumulant b₂ = 2.2245662 MeV refutes w₂ = 0 outright, by the same contrapositive not_pairLocal_of_b3_ne_zero uses one hypothesis up. Both refutations are sorry-free in QOU.Mass.BrunnianHypothesisNotSatisfied.

    So the hypothesis is renamed to say what it is (NoIsolatedPairEnergy), and the theorems below are kept and remain true — as implications with a hypothesis this carrier does not satisfy. not_pairLocal_of_noIsolatedPairEnergy is in any case redundant: not_pairLocal_of_b3_ne_zero reaches ¬PairLocal from data with no unsatisfied hypothesis at all.

    And the falsifiable payoff was collected elsewhere, as a negative. QOU.Mass.BrunnianTripleExhaustion computes the count-channel prediction directly and gets b₃/b₂ = M − 3, an integer for any total crossing count M, against a measured ratio strictly inside (0,1): no_carrier_reproduces_b3 — no carrier of any crossing number works, missing by at least 55.98 % of the measured value, and the Borromean rings themselves (M = 3) give b₃/b₂ = 0 exactly. That is the honest state of the three-body channel; reading b₃ off as the triple mutual energy would have hidden it.

    Scope #

    This is a conditional structural result, not a magnitude derivation. It says what the carrier must do; it does not identify the carrier, does not compute w₃, and does not touch the ℏ_q² saturation question (qou-brng R3, measured numerology-suspect). The CrossingModel here records a pair's mutual energy in each of the three cluster sizes on four components — it is a faithful bookkeeping of the crossing sum grouped by pair, not a new physical postulate.

    Base ring is a generic CommRing for everything but the sign corollary, which needs an ordering (AGENTS.md §7c).

    Beans qou-brng, qou-bhzt.

    source
    structure QOU.Mass.CrossingModelPairLocality.CrossingModel (R : Type u_2) [CommRing R] :
    Type u_2

    The inter-component crossing energy of a four-component assembly, grouped by the pair each crossing joins. Every crossing joins exactly two components, so this grouping is a bookkeeping identity rather than an assumption; what is not assumed is that a pair's weight is the same across cluster sizes, which is why there are three fields rather than one.

    • w2 i j — the mutual energy of the pair {i,j} in the isolated two-component assembly;
    • w3 i j k — the mutual energy of the pair {i,j} inside the triple {i,j,k};
    • w4 i j — the mutual energy of the pair {i,j} inside the full four-component assembly.
    • w2 : Fin 4 → Fin 4 → R

      mutual energy of the pair {i,j} measured in the isolated pair

    • w3 : Fin 4 → Fin 4 → Fin 4 → R

      mutual energy of the pair {i,j} measured inside the triple {i,j,k}

    • w4 : Fin 4 → Fin 4 → R

      mutual energy of the pair {i,j} measured inside the four-component assembly

                The Brunnian carrier is exactly the escape #

                source
                theorem QOU.Mass.CrossingModelPairLocality.not_pairLocal_of_noIsolatedPairEnergy {R : Type u_1} [CommRing R] {C : CrossingModel R} (hB : C.NoIsolatedPairEnergy) {i j k : Fin 4} (h3 : C.w3 i j k ≠ 0) :
                ¬C.PairLocal

                A Brunnian carrier violates pair-locality, provided some triple is nontrivial — which is the defining content of Brunnian-ness, that the whole is linked while every pair is not. So the Brunnian property is not incidental: it is what lets binding = −(mutual energy) coexist with a nonzero measured 3-body cumulant.

                The sign, over an ordered ring #

                source
                theorem QOU.Mass.CrossingModelPairLocality.tripleMutual_neg_of_b3_pos {R : Type u_1} [CommRing R] [PartialOrder R] [IsOrderedRing R] {C : CrossingModel R} (hB : C.NoIsolatedPairEnergy) {i j k : Fin 4} (hb : 0 < C.toBindingMap.b3 i j k) :
                C.tripleMutual i j k < 0

                A positive 3-body cumulant forces the Brunnian triple channel to be negative. With the pair terms gone, b₃ = −(triple mutual energy) exactly, so the sign of the measured cumulant fixes the sign of the channel with nothing in between. AME gives b₃ > 0, hence an attractive (negative-energy) three-body mutual channel.
