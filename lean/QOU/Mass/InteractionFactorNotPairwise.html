    The interaction amplitude cannot factorise over pairs #

    QOU.Mass.ArchimedeanTraceBridge derives the self/mutual partition at the Markov-trace level: if the assembly amplitude is (∏ Tᵢ)·I — constituent self-traces times an interaction factor I — then the mass-deficit binding is exactly −log I = bindingFreeEnergy I. That converts the multiplicative categorical trace into the additive energy SelfMutualPartition had assumed.

    It leaves I unstructured, and there is one obvious way to structure it. The §10a Cauchy argument (see PairwiseCumulantVanishing's docstring) says the trace-to-energy map is a logarithm; the natural next move is to give each pair its own interaction factor and take the cluster's factor to be the product,

    I(S) = ∏_{i<j ∈ S} I_ij .

    This file says that move is refuted by the data, and it is worth stating before someone makes it, because the multiplicative form disguises what it is: −log ∏ I_ij = Σ (−log I_ij) is a sum over bonds, so it is pairwise-additive in the sense of PairwiseCumulantVanishing.IsPairwise, and every such assembly has b₃ = 0 identically. AME2020 measures b₃ = +1.4262225 (QOU.MassTheory.AmeBindingNotPairwise.ame_b3_eq).

    So the interaction factor of a cluster is not the product of its pairs' factors. Whatever I is, it carries irreducibly many-body structure — which is the multiplicative face of the same constraint QOU.Mass.CrossingModelPairLocality states additively (a pair's crossing weight is not assembly-independent), and which a Brunnian carrier supplies.

    Why this needed saying separately #

    The additive statement and the multiplicative one look different enough that closing one does not visibly close the other. bindingFreeEnergy is defined through Real.log, and a product inside a logarithm reads as "the natural factorisation" rather than as "a bond sum in disguise" — which is exactly the shape §10a's own conclusion invites. The whole content of pairFactorised_b3_eq_zero is that log moves it from one column to the other.

    Ground ring ℝ (§7c: Real.log is archimedean). Beans qou-w6vh, qou-brng, qou-bhzt.

    structure QOU.Mass.InteractionFactorNotPairwise.PairFactorised :
    Type

    An interaction factor assigned per pair, with every factor positive so the archimedean functor log is defined on it. A cluster's interaction factor is the product over the pairs it contains — the multiplicative analogue of pair-locality.

    • I : Fin 4 → Fin 4 → ℝ

      the interaction factor of the pair {i,j}

    • pos (i j : Fin 4) : 0 < self.I i j

      positivity, discharged for the framework's quantum-integer amplitude class at q > 1 (ArchimedeanTraceBridge, QOU.MathlibExt.qInt_pos)
