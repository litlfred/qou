    QOU.Mass.SumSYTEqInvolution — ∑_{λ ⊢ n} f^λ = I(n) #

    The Robinson–Schensted corollary, proved without RSK.

    QOU.Mathlib.sum_numSYT_recurrence' gives, unconditionally,

    s(n+2) = s(n+1) + (n+1)·s(n),        s(k) := ∑_{|μ| = k} #SYT(μ),

    which is verbatim the recurrence defining QOU.Mass.involutionNumber (involutionNumber_succ_succ). All that is left is the two base cases, and this file supplies them:

    • numStandardYoungTableaux_bot — the empty shape carries exactly one tableau. Both halves are proved rather than assumed: Subsingleton because every entry of a tableau on ⊥ is forced to 0 by SemistandardYoungTableau.zeros, and Nonempty by exhibiting that tableau (botSsyt, whose row/column conditions are vacuous and whose Set.BijOn obligation is ∅ → Set.Icc 1 0).
    • sum_numSYT_size_zero — s(0) = 1, since ⊥ is the only diagram with no cells.
    • sum_numSYT_size_one — s(1) = 1, read off sum_numSYT_succ_split 0: the corner-weighted term vanishes because ⊥ has no removable corner, leaving s(1) = s(0).

    Composing with card_involutions_eq_involutionNumber (already in InvolutionNumbers) gives sum_numSYT_eq_card_involutions: the sum of SYT counts over a rank equals the number of involutions in S_n — the statement RSK is normally invoked for.

    Why this is not circular #

    Nothing here counts involutions by tableaux. involutionNumber is defined by its recurrence and is separately proved to count involutions; the SYT side reaches the same recurrence through the differential-poset structure of Young's lattice (QOU.Mathlib.upSum_eq). The two are then matched as sequences.

    Base ring: ℕ throughout — pure counting, nothing archimedean (§7c).

    References #

    • [stanley1999ec2] Enumerative Combinatorics I, §3.21 (differential posets)
    • [manuscript] QOU/Mathlib/YoungLatticeUpOperator.lean
