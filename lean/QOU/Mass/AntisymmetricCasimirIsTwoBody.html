            theorem QOU.Mass.AntisymmetricCasimirIsTwoBody.pairSum_eq {R : Type u_1} [CommRing R] (n N : R) :
            pairSumX4N n N = -((N + 1) * pairCountX2 n)

            The two-body sum is exactly proportional to the pair count, for every N. 2N·S(n) = −(N+1)·C(n,2), stated here as 4N·S = −(N+1)·2C(n,2).

            This is the load-bearing statement: such an interaction contributes a constant per pair, hence a constant coordination weight, hence b₃ = 0. No antisymmetric-column Casimir of any SU(N) can carry three-body content.
