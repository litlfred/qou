    The twist exponent is never real — e^{2πiμ} = −q² forces Im μ = −ln q / π #

    Bean qou-d0qq, framework-side connection plan §C step 4 (docs/audits/2026-09-07-route-c-step3-and-kz-level-plans.md).

    Where this sits #

    UntwistedMonodromyPrune showed that no finite-order or quasi-unipotent (untwisted Gauss–Manin) monodromy has an eigenvalue of modulus > 1, so the framework's geometric connection cannot by itself produce the Hecke eigenvalue ratio −q² with real q > 1. The surviving candidate is a twisted period — a local system with exponent μ, whose pseudo-reflection monodromy has eigenvalues {1, e^{2πiμ}} (Deligne–Mostow (2.11)). Matching the Hecke pair {q, −q⁻¹} up to the overall rescaling that KZLevelFromHeckeNormalisation already handles means matching the ratio: e^{2πiμ} = −q².

    What is proved #

    • norm_exp_two_pi_I_mul — ‖e^{2πiμ}‖ = e^{−2π Im μ}.
    • im_eq_of_exp_eq_neg_sq — if e^{2πiμ} = −q² with real q > 0, then Im μ = −ln q / π. The imaginary part of the exponent is the level: with the block's ħ = (2/iπ) ln q, Im μ = −(π/2)·(ħ/i)·(1/π), i.e. μ and ħ carry the same ln q, so the "level" of KZLevelFromHeckeNormalisation and the twist exponent here are two names for one number.
    • im_ne_zero_of_exp_eq_neg_sq — for q > 1 that imaginary part is non-zero: no twisted period with a real exponent — no unitary local system, no rational twist, no root-of-unity twist — gives the Hecke ratio. Scope-(b): the invariant is the modulus of the monodromy eigenvalue. not_exp_eq_neg_sq_of_im_eq_zero is the same statement in refutation form.
    • rescaled_pair — the pseudo-reflection pair {1, e^{2πiμ}}, rescaled by any c with product −1, becomes {±q, ∓q⁻¹}: c² = q⁻² and (c·e^{2πiμ})² = q², through KZLevelFromHeckeNormalisation.eigen_sq_of_product_ratio.

    What is NOT established #

    Nothing here says the substrate connection is a twisted period, or derives μ. It says what μ would have to be if it were — a non-real exponent carrying ln q₀ — and that a real-exponent twist is excluded. Under the 2026-08-02 forever-gate q₀ is an input, so μ(q₀) is a label, not a derivation; the twist itself remains the one thing the framework does not supply (§C step 2, colour_cubic_abelian_twist_holonomy_probe: the abelianised Cartan connection contributes zero twist). Real-valued only where the modulus and the logarithm live; μ and the eigenvalues are complex.
