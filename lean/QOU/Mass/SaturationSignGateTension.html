    def QOU.Mass.SaturationSignGateTension.PassesSignGate {R : Type u_1} [CommRing R] [LinearOrder R] (d₃ d₄ : R) :
    Prop

    The sign gate, as the measurement states it: a standalone term reproduces the observed b₃ > 0, b₄ > 0 only if its third and fourth differences share a sign, i.e. their product is positive.

      theorem QOU.Mass.SaturationSignGateTension.two_terms_necessary {R : Type u_1} [CommRing R] [LinearOrder R] [IsStrictOrderedRing R] {d₃ d₄ : R} (hmeas : PassesSignGate d₃ d₄) (halt : d₃ > 0 ∧ d₄ ≤ 0) :
      False

      Two terms are necessary. A single term whose high differences alternate — which is what saturation forces — cannot reproduce the measured cumulants. So b₃ > 0 together with b₄ > 0 and a saturating binding requires at least two contributions of opposite curvature.

      This is the cumulant-side form of the coordination weight's non-monotonicity (CoordinationWeightDifferences); the two arguments agree.
