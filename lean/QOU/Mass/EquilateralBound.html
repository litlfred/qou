    def QOU.Mass.EquilateralBound.EquilateralGram {V : Type u_1} [NormedAddCommGroup V] [InnerProductSpace ℝ V] {ι : Type u_2} [DecidableEq ι] (v : ι → V) (d : ℝ) :
    Prop

    The equilateral Gram condition. Vectors from a common apex to points that are pairwise at distance d, and each at distance d from the apex, satisfy ⟪vᵢ, vⱼ⟫ = d²/2 + (d²/2)·δᵢⱼ — the diagonal is d², the off-diagonal d²/2. Writing both cases as one formula is what makes the identity below a two-line computation.

      inner ℝ (∑ i ∈ s, g i • v i) (∑ i ∈ s, g i • v i) = d ^ 2 / 2 * ((∑ i ∈ s, g i) ^ 2 + ∑ i ∈ s, g i ^ 2)

      The key identity. For an equilateral Gram configuration, ‖Σ cᵢ vᵢ‖² = (d²/2)·[(Σ cᵢ)² + Σ cᵢ²]. Both bracketed terms are sums of squares, so the quadratic form is positive definite whenever d ≠ 0 — this is the whole content of the bound, obtained without computing an eigenvalue.

      Equilateral vectors are linearly independent. Immediate from the identity: a vanishing combination forces (Σcᵢ)² + Σcᵢ² = 0, and both terms are non-negative, so every cᵢ = 0.

      theorem QOU.Mass.EquilateralBound.equidistant_card_le_three_in_R3 {ι : Type u_2} [Fintype ι] [DecidableEq ι] {p : ι → EuclideanSpace ℝ (Fin 3)} {p₀ : EuclideanSpace ℝ (Fin 3)} {d : ℝ} (hd : d ≠ 0) (hp₀ : ∀ (i : ι), ‖p i - p₀‖ = d) (hpp : ∀ (i j : ι), i ≠ j → ‖p i - p j‖ = d) :
      Fintype.card ι ≤ 3

      The arc's corollary: at most FOUR mutually equidistant centres in ℝ³. Formally, at most three points can sit at a common positive distance d from each other AND from a fourth point p₀ — a fifth mutually-adjacent centre does not fit in three dimensions.

      Consequence for the α-cluster model: the complete α-graph with N_bonds = C(n_α, 2) presupposes that every pair of α-centres is a nearest-neighbour bond at a common distance. That configuration is geometrically unrealizable for n_α ≥ 5, so the complete-graph bond count is not merely inaccurate there — it describes no configuration at all, and the coordination count must fall back to a close-packing value.
