    The data-side refutation: a nonzero pair binding refutes Brunnian.

    AME2020 measures b₂ = 2.2245662 MeV (computations/probes/pair-locality-shortfall.witness.json), and the pair cumulant of the induced binding map is −w₂. So the measured two-body channel refutes CrossingModel.NoIsolatedPairEnergy on the physical carrier — the same move not_pairLocal_of_b3_ne_zero makes for the three-body channel, one hypothesis down.

    The structure-side refutation: a crossing pair at q > 1 refutes Brunnian.

    If a pair's isolated mutual energy is the count channel of a topologically Brunnian pair — which is exactly what brunnian_energy_is_count_channel says it is, the signed channel being empty — then for any actual crossing (0 < m) at a substrate modulus q > 1 that energy is strictly positive, so w₂ ≠ 0.

    L6a4 is a crossing pair: its six crossings distribute two to each component pair, with n₊ = n₋ = 1, so m = 1 > 0. Hence CrossingModel.NoIsolatedPairEnergy fails for it on structural grounds alone, with no appeal to data.
