    def QOU.Mass.SLq2Label.SeifertRelation (c n g mu : ℤ) :
    Prop

    The Euler-characteristic relation for a closed braid on n strands with c crossings whose closure bounds a genus-g Seifert surface with μ boundary components: χ = n - c and χ = 2 - 2g - μ. Carried as a hypothesis — it is Seifert's algorithm, not something proved here.
