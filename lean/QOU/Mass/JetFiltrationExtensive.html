    QOU.Mass.JetFiltrationExtensive — the extensive property of the jet-filtration binding #

    Formalises the provable core of the body-order (jet-filtration) binding (conj:binding-manybody-eq-jet-filtration), the compute consumer implemented in computations/binding_manybody_jet_filtration.py:

    B(K) = Σ_{n=2}^{A} m_e · a_D^(n) · w_n(K),

    where a_D^(n) = ∮_{S_c} λ_SW^(n) is the non-abelian-Stokes SW prolongation period (QOU.Mass.NaStokesKeystone), equal to the super-exponential mean quantum dimension lw(n−1) (#1765), and w_n(K) is the (open) per-atom residue weight.

    What is proved here (sorry-free, generic): the binding is extensive — a finite sum of non-negative body-order contributions, hence monotone non-decreasing in the body-order cutoff A, and strictly increasing when a new body-order has positive contribution. This is precisely the property the intensive rotation-phase θ_λ = arccos(χ^λ/d_λ) route structurally lacked (probe_full_theta_binding.py: the bounded phase cannot grow with A).

    What is NOT proved here (honest scope): the super-exponential magnitude of a_D^(n) (the √-branch falling-factorial growth), the na-Stokes identity a_D^(n) = lw(n−1) (the owner-gated keystone conj #1765), and the residue weight w_n(K) (the open q-difference "ρ·A" kernel) are conjectural inputs, carried here as abstract non-negativity/positivity hypotheses on aD, w — not derived.
