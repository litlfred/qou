    Untwisted geometric monodromy cannot be the substrate monodromy #

    Bean qou-d0qq, step 1 of the framework-side plan (docs/audits/2026-09-07-route-c-step3-and-kz-level-plans.md follow-up, agent fw-conn).

    The Gauss–Manin local system of an algebraic family has quasi-unipotent local monodromy: every eigenvalue λ of a lasso monodromy satisfies (λ^k − 1)^m = 0 for some k, m ≥ 1; for the colour cubic's root map the fibre is three points and the monodromy is a permutation, so λ^k = 1 outright. The framework's Step-2 monodromy generators have the Hecke eigenvalues q₀ and −q₀⁻¹ with the real substrate parameter q₀ > 1. These are incompatible over any linearly ordered field, so no untwisted geometric monodromy of a framework family can be the substrate connection: the deformation 1 ↦ q₀ must enter as a twist (a rank-one local system with non-unitary exponent), which is exactly what the Knizhnik–Zamolodchikov side supplies through the level (KZLevelFromHeckeNormalisation).

    Scope-(b): the invariant is finiteness/quasi-unipotence of geometric monodromy, and the statement below is the only arithmetic it needs. It says nothing about twisted Gauss–Manin systems, which are the surviving candidate (C3′ of the plan).

    Base ring: a linearly ordered field; the reals are one instance. No archimedean analysis.

    A finite-order eigenvalue is never a real number greater than one.

    Quasi-unipotence (λ^k − 1)^m = 0 (with m ≥ 1) already forces λ^k = 1 in a field, so a quasi-unipotent eigenvalue is never > 1 either.

    The negative Hecke eigenvalue −q⁻¹ is not of finite order either (for q > 1): its k-th power has absolute value q^{−k} < 1.
