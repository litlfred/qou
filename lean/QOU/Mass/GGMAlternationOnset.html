    QOU.Mass.GGMAlternationOnset — where the (−1)^m alternation of the GGM carrier can start #

    The Stokes carrier 𝒢 of conj:c1-ggm-bar-parity-faces is the three-term recurrence

    y (m+2) = (2 − q^(m+1)) · y (m+1) − y m.

    Its sign pattern is the Door-v residual: the growing solution alternates as (−1)^m asymptotically. The block's scope note says that while 2 − q^(m+1) > 0 "no sign change occurs". That is not what the recurrence does — with a coefficient in (0, 2) it is oscillatory, and every solution measured changes sign inside that window (computations/probes/door_v_residual_scope_probe.py, six initial data, proton q₀). What IS true, initial-data-independent, and what the block's argument actually needs:

    • while the coefficient is positive, a sign flip is always followed by a same-sign pair (no_double_flip_of_pos) — strict (−1)^m alternation at two consecutive pairs is impossible for every solution;
    • once the coefficient is negative, a same-sign pair is always followed by a flip (flip_after_same_of_neg), and a flip persists as soon as |y m| < |c| · |y (m+1)| (flip_persists_of_lt).

    Consequence for the shift calculus: the full-alternation hypothesis of QOU.Mathlib.RamifiedShiftCalculusSum.twoOp_residual_kills_residue (#6498) is unsatisfiable by the GGM carrier at any q < 2 (ggm_full_alternation_impossible_of_lt_two) — it fails already at m = 0. The residual the corpus measures is the from-level-n₀ form, handled by twoOp_residual_from_level_kills_residue_beyond, and the coefficient threshold q^(m+1) < 2 (at the proton q₀: m ≤ 5) is a lower bound on n₀.

    Ground ring: the sign statements need an ordered field, so per §7c this module sits on the archimedean side of the wall — [Field K] [LinearOrder K] [IsStrictOrderedRing K].
