    Lagrange resolvent — cyclic eigenvalue (offset angle from the C₅ stabilizer) #

    The rigorous tier-1 of the torus-knot → q-Bring offset question (bean qou-vhv6): the angle of the offset is fixed by the embedding's cyclic stabilizer root of unity, independently of any (still-open) magnitude closure.

    On Bring's surface the base coordinates x₀,…,x₄ are diagonalized by the C₅ ⊂ S₅ cyclic action via the Lagrange-resolvent DFT w_k = (1/√5) Σ_j ζ₅^{kj} x_j. Writing w := ζ₅^k (a 5th root of unity, since ζ₅⁵ = 1), the cyclic shift x_j ↦ x_{j+1} acts on the (unnormalized) k-th resolvent by multiplication by w:

    w · (x₁ + w x₂ + w² x₃ + w³ x₄ + w⁴ x₀) = x₀ + w x₁ + w² x₂ + w³ x₃ + w⁴ x₄.

    I.e. the k-th resolvent is an eigenvector of the cyclic shift with eigenvalue ζ₅^{-k} (equivalently ζ₅^k · (shift·w_k) = w_k). The offset's argument therefore advances by arg(ζ₅^{-k}) = -2πk/5 under the C₅ action — the angle is a stabilizer root of unity, exactly as claimed. (The trefoil colour channel uses the S₃ cube-root sub-DFT, w = ζ₃; the w⁵ = 1 below specializes to w³ = 1 via the same identity with the length-3 resolvent.)

    §7c: stated over a generic CommRing R with w a formal 5th root of unity — no archimedean input. Derived, not fitted: a one-line linear_combination identity over w⁵ = 1.

    Bean qou-vhv6; companion to QOU.Mass.ColorCubicDiscriminantPositive (which establishes that the 6 saddle points are strictly complex, so the offset angle is nonzero and this eigenvalue phase is the object that carries it).

    theorem QOU.Mass.lagrangeResolvent_cyclic_eigenvalue {R : Type u_1} [CommRing R] (w x0 x1 x2 x3 x4 : R) (hw : w ^ 5 = 1) :
    w * (x1 + w * x2 + w ^ 2 * x3 + w ^ 3 * x4 + w ^ 4 * x0) = x0 + w * x1 + w ^ 2 * x2 + w ^ 3 * x3 + w ^ 4 * x4

    Cyclic eigenvalue of the Lagrange resolvent. For w a formal 5th root of unity (w⁵ = 1), the cyclic shift x_j ↦ x_{j+1} multiplies the k-th resolvent w_k = x₀ + w x₁ + w² x₂ + w³ x₃ + w⁴ x₄ (with w = ζ₅^k) by w: w · (shift · w_k) = w_k. Hence the resolvent is an eigenvector of the shift with eigenvalue w⁻¹ = ζ₅^{-k} — the offset angle is the stabilizer root of unity.
