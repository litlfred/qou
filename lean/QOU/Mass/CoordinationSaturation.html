    QOU.Mass.CoordinationSaturation — nuclear saturation forces bounded coordination #

    Formalizes the qualitative saturation argument already stated in the corpus (folio/quantum-observable-universe/mass-theory/bk-coordination-multiplicity.md, § "for n_α ≥ 5 the complete graph over-counts … saturates to a geometric close-packing count"; q-clt-jsj-mass-scaling.md, B/𝔪 "plateaus under nuclear saturation"). This file is the machine-checked structural half; it claims no new physics beyond hardening that argument.

    Setup #

    Write the extensive binding as B(A) = ε · M(A), with M(A) the total nucleon–nucleon bond count and ε > 0 a per-bond energy. The observed fact is saturation: the binding per nucleon B(A)/A approaches a finite plateau (~8 MeV/nucleon empirically), it does not grow with A.

    Result — the structural dichotomy #

    • complete-graph coordination M = C(A,2) (every nucleon bonded to all A−1 others — the z = A−1 law of CoordinationBinding.lean) gives B(A)/A = ε·(A−1)/2, unbounded: no plateau, the cluster over-binds without limit (complete_graph_no_saturation). Saturation therefore refutes the complete graph for heavy A.
    • bounded-degree coordination (each nucleon bonded to a fixed k neighbours; handshake ⇒ k·A/2 bonds) gives B(A)/A = ε·k/2, constant in A (bounded_degree_saturates): saturation.

    Conclusion and scope #

    Nuclear saturation forces a bounded coordination number (the close-packing / fcc picture, arc item 3a) and excludes the complete-graph z = A−1 (arc item 1, refuted at ⁴He). That is the structural content proved here.

    The close-packing input is IMPORTED Euclidean geometry, now held and cited (bean qou-ywbd); a citation makes it citable, not derived, and the four objections of docs/audits/2026-09-05-max-contact-sub-affine-negative.md §5 stand:

    -- Ref: [bezdek-reid2013] Thm 1(i) — touching pairs in any packing of n unit balls in E³ number < 6n − 0.926·n^{2/3}: bulk 12·n/2 from the kissing number k = 12, minus a sub-affine surface deficit. This is the bounded-degree k of perNucleonBounded. -- Ref: [hales-etal2010] — the Kepler theorem statement (fcc density is maximal); the paper proves no kissing- or contact-number bound, so it does not source k = 12.

    The REMAINING open input — that the binding multiplier is the mutual (coordination) linking count while the mass multiplier is the self-linking weight lw — is the linking-vs-self-energy amplitude assignment (bean qou-brng); it is not proved here and must not be read as proved.

    Ground ring ℝ (§7c: archimedean — the statement is unboundedness of B/A). No fit: ε, k are free positive parameters, nothing is tuned to data.

    Convergent frontier (2026-08-29/30 sibling landings) #

    This lemma uses a single uniform per-bond energy ε — which is exactly the order-independent regime L_pp = L_pn = L_nn that QOU.MassTheory.ChainBondOrderDependence.orderIndependent_iff proves is the only arrangement-invariant one (a nucleus has no arrangement, so bond-type- dependent scalars are a non-physical DOF, scope-(b) excluded at q ≠ 1, bean qou-m07y). So the saturation dichotomy here holds precisely in the physical case.

    Together these exclude both simple coordination ansätze — the complete graph (here, by saturation) and the linear chain (there, by arrangement-dependence) — and sit alongside QOU.MassTheory.BodyOrderRecurrenceDepth (body-order depth ≥ 3 forced) and QOU.MassTheory.MayerThreeBodySign (the 3-body cumulant sign is threshold-forced, wrong against AME).
