        The product formula. Under a one-dimensional block, the character of a word is the product over its letters of c·q + d. This is the whole reason the dominant summand's log-weight cannot carry a connected many-body term at A ≤ 4: the word enters only through a product of per-letter scalars.

        Factorisation over a concatenation. The atom word is the free word with interface letters inserted; under a one-dimensional block the character factorises over any split of the letter list, so the interface letters contribute a separate factor — one per bond, which is exactly "bond additive" once a logarithm is taken on the archimedean side.
