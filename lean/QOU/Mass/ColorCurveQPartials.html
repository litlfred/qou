    The branch-selection defect. On the curve C_z(X) = 0, the z-partial collapses to Δ_q^{z} C(X,z) = C_{qz}(X) — the (generically nonzero) measure of how far X fails to remain a root under z ↦ qz. Its vanishing would require X to be simultaneously a root over z and qz; the correction to a genuine root X_q of C_{qz} is the branch datum the q-difference prolongation must choose. This is the precise remaining W1 obstruction.
