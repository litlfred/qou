    QOU.Mass.FormalityBridgeF1 — the formality bridge: what forces b₃ = 0, and what breaks it #

    The research doc docs/audits/2026-09-05-binding-gap-groupoids-derived-categories-full-tower.md §2.1 states, as the first formalize-first item, the conjecture

    (F1) a semisimple algebra with a Markov trace, evaluated on a sequential juxtaposition of pairwise interface words, gives a pair-local log tr, hence b₃ = 0.

    Corpus-grep, run before writing this file, found F1 already proved in three pieces — the doc's §7 item 1 was a miss on my part, and this file is the composition, not a new theorem:

    piece where what it proves
    monotone word ⇒ product of letter traces QOU.MathlibExt.MonotoneWordFactorizes.tr_stabWord_eq_prod_tr tr(s n ⋯ s (n+k−1)) = ∏ tr(s (n+i)) for stabilising letters
    bond product + any multiplicative-to-additive map ⇒ b₃ = 0 QOU.Mass.MarkovBondRuleForcesPairwise.b3_eq_zero_of_bondRule the b₃ conclusion
    interleaving breaks the product QOU.MathlibExt.InterleavedCrossTerm.interleaved_sub_sequential exact cross term z (z² − h z − 1) at n = 4

    What is added here is the one sentence that joins them, stated so that both hypotheses of F1 are visible in the type:

    • f_tr_stabWord_eq_sum — the sequential half. For any map f with f (x·y) = f x + f y (no continuity; §10a's Cauchy condition and nothing more), the f-image of a monotone word's trace is the sum of the letters' f-images. So log tr of a sequential word is a bond sum by construction, and b3_eq_zero_of_bondRule finishes. Neither semisimplicity nor any property of the trace beyond the Markov axioms enters — which is the point: the zero is forced by the ordering, and the semisimplicity in F1's statement is doing no work at this step. (Where semisimplicity does bite is one level up: QOU.Hecke.H2KaehlerDegrees, the other prune.)
    • interleaved_ne_sequential — the interleaved half. Whenever the cross term is non-zero (at convention D it is −(q⁴+q²+1)/(q²+1)², never zero for real q > 0, InterleavedCrossTerm.crossTerm_neg_convD), the interleaved word's trace is not the sequential one, so its log is not the bond sum of the same letters. That is the exact sense in which the corpus's one positive many-body signal (qou-kru4) is the failure of F1's ordering hypothesis and not a counterexample to F1.

    What this settles for the derived route #

    F1 has two hypotheses — a trace that is Markov, and a word that is monotone — and the corpus now shows in Lean that the second is the one the production chain satisfies and the interleaved class violates. So a many-body term at degree zero must come from word topology; a many-body term from the algebra must come from a place the Markov trace on the semisimple fibre cannot see, which is H2KaehlerDegrees' subject. Nothing here moves a number.

    F1, interleaved half. With a non-zero cross term, the interleaved word g₀ g₁ g₀ g₂ g₁ does not have the trace of the sequential word g₀² g₁² g₂ with the same letter content — so its log is not that bond sum.
