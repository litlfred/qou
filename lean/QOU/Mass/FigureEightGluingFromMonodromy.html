    The figure-eight gluing data from its braid monodromy — the algebraic spine #

    Companion to computations/probes/nz_gluing_from_braid_monodromy_probe.py (bean qou-n2cl). The probe derives the Neumann–Zagier data of S³ ∖ 4₁ from the canonical 3-braid (σ₁σ₂⁻¹)² with no SnapPy input: Stallings monodromy → LR word → Floyd–Hatcher layering → edge and cusp rows. The combinatorial layering is checked by the probe against five independent facts; this file records the three algebraic identities in that chain that are theorems rather than computations:

    • stallings_monodromy_eq — the product of the two Dehn-twist matrices on H₁ of the fibre, one per crossing column of (σ₁σ₂⁻¹)², is [[2,1],[1,1]] = R·L; stallings_trace_three and stallings_charpoly give trace 3 and det(t − φ) = t² − 3t + 1, the Alexander polynomial of 4₁ (the trefoil control σ₁σ₂σ₁σ₂ ↦ T₁T₂, trace 1, is trefoil_control_trace_one).
    • figure_eight_gluing_at_sixth_root — the derived edge equation z₀ z₁ (1 − z₀)(1 − z₁) = 1 (Thurston's) is satisfied at z₀ = z₁ = ω for ANY ω with ω² − ω + 1 = 0, i.e. at a primitive sixth root of unity; the geometric solution e^{iπ/3} is one of these. Stated over a generic commutative ring, §7c.
    • rrl_gluing_at_sqrt_minus_seven — the R²L bundle's first derived edge equation holds at the exact shapes (5 + r)/4, (3 + r)/4, (3 + r)/4 for ANY r with r² = −7 in a field of characteristic zero; the probe identified the numeric root there and re-verified all three rows in sympy; this is the same verification as a theorem for the row that carries the content (the other two are its rect-form partners).

    Nothing here asserts the triangulation is geometric — that is the probe's Im z > 0 check and its volume; these are the exact identities the geometry rests on.

    -- Ref: [manuscript] computations/probes/nz_gluing_from_braid_monodromy_probe.py -- Ref: library/arxiv-0808.2716v4 (Futer–Kalfagianni–Purcell) §2.2 for the R, L matrices. -- Ref: [martelli2016] §14.1 (Prop 14.1.8 + Fig 14.7: the two-tetrahedron, valence-6 triangulation -- with all shapes e^{iπ/3} is the figure-eight complement — the literature form of the -- sixth-root check), §15.2 (Prop 15.2.9 / 15.2.11: the incidence rows generate a Lagrangian -- subspace — the NZ symplectic check; Cor 15.2.8: rk A = 2n − c). Not in that book: the -- Guéritaud two-bridge layering and the Floyd–Hatcher bundle layering (keep FKP for those).

        theorem QOU.Mass.FigureEightGluingFromMonodromy.rrl_gluing_at_sqrt_minus_seven {K : Type u_1} [Field K] [CharZero K] {r : K} (hr : r ^ 2 = -7) :
        ((5 + r) / 4) ^ 2 * ((3 + r) / 4) * ((3 + r) / 4) * (1 - (3 + r) / 4) * (1 - (3 + r) / 4) = 1

        The three derived edge rows of the R²L bundle, in rect form Π z^a (1−z)^b: a = (2,1,1), b = (0,1,1); a = (−2,0,0), b = (2,−2,−2); a = (0,−1,−1), b = (−2,1,1). At the exact shapes z₀ = (5+r)/4, z₁ = z₂ = (3+r)/4 with r² = −7 the first row equals 1; the other two are its inverse-type partners and follow from the same identity. Only the first is stated; the probe verifies all three symbolically.
