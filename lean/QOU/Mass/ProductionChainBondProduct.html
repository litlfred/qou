    The bond product for the PRODUCTION chain layout #

    Bean qou-qqm4. That bean measures

    tr_M(W_atom) / tr_M(W_free) = ∏_bonds (z + d_b)

    on the production atom word to orders ≤ 5 and A ≤ 5, and records under §"What is NOT established" that the identity is NOT PROVED. This file proves it for the production layout, from two results that landed on 2026-09-13 and were never connected:

    • Hecke.InteriorMarkovOfBraidedTower.tr_bondWord_of_braided — the bond product tr (bondWord) = (∏ (z + d_k)) · tr (freeWord) on every braided tower, given the chain hypotheses ChainOK; and
    • Mass.StrandLayoutForcesChain — nucleon k occupies strands 3k, 3k+1, 3k+2 of a 3A-strand braid, so the inter-nucleon bridges are exactly the generators σ_{3k+2}, k = 0 … A−2, and they join consecutive nucleons only.

    The second supplies precisely the indices the first quantifies over, and the level arithmetic then discharges ChainOK outright.

    The layout, and why ChainOK is immediate #

    Block k is a word in the generators that stay inside nucleon k's strands, i.e. in {σ_{3k}, σ_{3k+1}}; bond k is σ_{3k+2}. ChainOK asks three things of consecutive entries, and each is arithmetic on 3k+2:

    requirement here why
    bonds strictly increase 3k+2 < 3(k+1)+2 omega
    block k+1 lies above bond k blk (k+1) ∈ upper (3k+2) its generators are 3k+3, 3k+4 > 3k+2
    block k+1 lies below bond k+1 blk (k+1) ∈ H (3k+5) its generators are 3k+3, 3k+4 < 3k+5

    So the production chain is a ChainOK chain because of where the strands put the bridges, which is StrandLayoutForcesChain's content, not an assumption about the word.

    What is proved #

    tr_productionChain — the bond product for the production layout on any MarkovTower satisfying InteriorMarkov.

    tr_productionChain_braided — the same with no hypothesis beyond BraidedTower, since interiorMarkov_of_braided discharges InteriorMarkov there. This is the derived form: three defining relations of H_n(q) in, the bond product out.

    prod_chainFrom rewrites the list product into ∏ k ∈ range n, (z + d k), the shape qou-qqm4 states the measurement in.

    🛑 What is NOT proved — read before quoting this as closing qou-qqm4 #

    1. The bond letters are hypotheses, not derived. The chain carries an arbitrary scalar d k per bond. That the production letters are pp ↦ 0, nn ↦ −h, pn ↦ d_averaged_weighted is qou-qqm4's measured input (letter_d_series), and nothing here derives it. What is derived is that whatever those scalars are, the trace factorises as the product of z + d.
    2. The blocks are hypotheses too. hlow / hup say each nucleon word sits in its own strand window. That is what StrandLayoutForcesChain establishes about atom_braid_word_3A, but the bridge from the Python producer to a Lean term is not made here — there is no Lean model of atom_braid_word_3A.
    3. No binding coefficient. Nothing here computes c₂ or any c_k.
    4. A = 1 is excluded and is not a gap: a one-nucleon atom has no bond, the product is empty, and the statement degenerates.

    So this closes the structural half of qou-qqm4 item 3 — the factorisation is a theorem rather than a measurement — and leaves the identification of the letters where it was.

    The production chain. chainFrom blk d start n lists n bonds starting at nucleon start: bond j is the generator σ_{3(start+j)+2} carrying the scalar d (start+j), followed by nucleon block blk (start+j+1).

    The indices are StrandLayoutForcesChain's: the bridge between nucleons k and k+1 is σ_{3k+2}, and no other single generator joins two nucleons.
