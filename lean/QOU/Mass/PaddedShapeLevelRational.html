    QOU.Mass.PaddedShapeLevelRational — level-rationality of padded-shape q-dimensions #

    Lean face of the §8/§10 decisive probe of docs/audits/2026-07-03-parabolic-cylinder-channel-interpolation.md (computations/deligne_stable_range_binding_period_probe.py, 99/99 exact): for a fixed shape μ, the padded family n ↦ dim_q(μ[n]), μ[n] = (n − |μ|, μ), is a fixed rational function of (q, qⁿ) — the per-shape restriction property of the Deligne/TL interpolated level.

    The identity factors into two inputs of different kinds, and this file formalizes the division of labor explicitly:

    • Algebraic content (proved here, sorry-free). The q-factorial telescope [n]_q! = [n−K]_q! · ∏_{i<K} [n−i]_q (qFactorial_telescope, induction on K) and its division form (qFactorial_div_telescope, at 1 < q where [·]_q! ≠ 0).
    • Combinatorial input — now PROVED in general (§HookSplit below). The hook multiset of the padded shape splits as hooks(μ[n]) = {1, …, n−|μ|−μ₁} ⊎ {n − c_j}_{j ≤ μ₁} ⊎ hooks(μ) with c_j = |μ| + j − 1 − leg_j(μ) — equivalently hookProd q (μ[n]) = [n−K]_q! · D with K = |μ| + μ₁ and D the fixed shifted-bracket × H_q(μ) factor. Originally probe-verified (99/99) and carried as the named hypothesis hsplit of qdim_level_rational_of_hook_split; the general combinatorial proof is hookProd_cons_split (prepending a first row leaves lower-row hooks unchanged — definitionally, hookLength_cons_succ := rfl — and the new row-0 hooks split at the column bound B into shifted brackets and a tail q-factorial). The unconditional closed form is qDim_cons_level_rational. To dodge ℕ-subtraction rewriting, the first row is parametrized as B + t (instantiate t := r − B); the shape hypothesis is only ∀ p ∈ μ, p ≤ B — no sortedness of μ is needed.

    Together: qDim q (μ[n]) = ∏_{i<K} [n−i]_q / D — numerator a fixed window of K = |μ|+μ₁ brackets sliding with the level, denominator level-independent. Each [n−i]_q is Laurent in qⁿ, so the family is rational in (q, qⁿ) of degree bounded by K — "polynomial in the level", the q-dimension face of Deligne interpolation \cite{deligne2007-st}.

    Ground ring: ℝ, matching the canonical QPartitionCombinatorics infrastructure this file consumes (its nonvanishing lemmas live at 1 < q); the telescope itself is ordering-free and would state over any field — kept here to avoid duplicating the q-bracket stack (§7c note).

      HookSplit — the general combinatorial hook decomposition, proved #

      Discharges the hsplit hypothesis of qdim_level_rational_of_hook_split for every padded shape (B + t) :: μ with all parts of μ bounded by B. Structure:

      • prepending a row leaves lower-row hooks untouched (hookLength_cons_succ, a rfl — legs count rows below, and get?/drop on a cons reduce structurally);
      • row-0 hooks are (first − j − 1) + leg_j(μ) + 1 (hookLength_cons_zero), and the legs vanish beyond the column bound B (legCount_eq_zero), so columns ≥ B contribute the tail q-factorial [t]_q! (prod_desc_eq_qFactorial);
      • fold-to-product plumbing (foldl_mul_eq_prod, hookProd_eq_prod) converts the hookProd fold into Finset.prod form where the range splits (Finset.prod_range_succ', Finset.prod_range_add) apply.
