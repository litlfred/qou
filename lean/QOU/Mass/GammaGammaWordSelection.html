                    def QOU.Mass.GammaGammaWordSelection.altChar {R : Type u_1} [Field R] (q : R) (w : Word) :
                    R

                    The one-dimensional alternating representation of the Hecke algebra, σᵢ ↦ −q⁻¹ (and so σᵢ⁻¹ ↦ −q), evaluated on a word. Under convention D the trace-form radical of H₃(q) is spanned by v_k = (−1/q)^{len b_k} — the alternating character of the normal-form basis — so this is the functional whose vanishing decides whether a word's normal form lies in the range of the Gram.
