    Free moments as non-crossing-partition sums #

                Speicher's first-block recursion (n ≤ 4) #

                m_n = ∑_{s=1}^{n} κ_s ∑_{i₁+…+i_s = n−s} m_{i₁}⋯m_{i_s}, where s is the size of the block of π ∈ NC(n) containing 1 and the inner sum fills the s gaps between its legs with non-crossing partitions (hence products of lower free moments). These are the defining recurrences of the free moment sequence and are the base cases of the general-n recursion (next brick). Each is a ring identity.

                The freeness discriminator #

                The single crossing partition {{1,3},{2,4}} is the whole difference between the classical and free order-4 moments.

                source
                theorem QOU.Mass.classical_sub_free_moment4 {R : Type u_1} [CommRing R] (κ : ℕ → R) :
                classicalMoment4 κ - freeMoment4 κ = κ 2 ^ 2

                Free/classical order-4 gap = the crossing partition. classicalMoment4 − freeMoment4 = κ₂², the contribution of the one crossing pair-partition {{1,3},{2,4}} that freeness removes.

                Bridge to the Catalan count via the all-ones evaluation #

                Setting every cumulant to 1 makes each non-crossing partition contribute 1, so freeMomentₙ evaluated at κ ≡ 1 equals |NC(n)| = C_n. We express C_n as Mathlib's Nat.centralBinom n / (n + 1) (which equals Nat.catalan n by Nat.catalan_eq_centralBinom_div), grounding the NC-partition reading of the moment polynomials in Mathlib without redefining the Catalan numbers.
