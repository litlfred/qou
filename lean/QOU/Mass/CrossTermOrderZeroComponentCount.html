    Component count decides the order-zero part of a Markov-trace cross term #

    qou-5vhf step 2 (bean qou-0fxk). Two measured facts on main:

    • at q = 1 the Markov trace on ℚSₙ is the component-count trace tr₁(w) = 2^{c(w)−n} (T1 probe, route-C probe; verified on all of S₃, S₄ in the spin-½ representation), so the order-zero part of any cross term tr(w) − tr(w') is 2^{c(w)−n} − 2^{c(w')−n};
    • the corpus's irreducible 3-body operator, the Brunnian band word w₃ = [A₀₁, A₁₂] against the 3-unlink, has deficit 1 − τ_Borr(h) = h⁴(3+h²)/(h²+4) (one_sub_tauBorromean), i.e. q-adic order four — its closure has the unlink's three components, so its order-zero part vanishes.

    This file carries the two elementary statements that join them:

    1. componentTrace_eq_iff — the order-zero cross term vanishes iff the component counts agree (2^k is injective). Component-changing word pairs (the n = 4 trefoil-vs-chain, components 1 vs 3) have an O(1) classical cross term; component-preserving pairs have a cross term of positive q-adic order — a pure q-deformation effect.
    2. one_sub_tauBorromean_eq_h4_mul / one_sub_tauBorromean_le — the Brunnian deficit is h⁴ times a bounded factor (≤ 1 for h² ≤ 1, and < 1 always, since (3+h²)/(h²+4) < 1), so at the substrate pin, where h = q₀ − q₀⁻¹ ≈ 0.209, it is bounded by h⁴ ≈ 1.9 × 10⁻³. The framework's classical cross terms are O(1); the missing 3-body magnitude the Δ_λ audit names (quoted there as μ₃ ≈ 0.47 MeV against E₀ ≈ 4.19 MeV, i.e. ≈ 0.11 in trace units) sits between the two classes. Nothing here evaluates a target; the inequalities are exact bounds.

    Base ring §7c: the component lemma is over ℚ (exact, no ordering); the deficit bounds are over ℝ, inherited from tauBorromean : ℝ → ℝ, because they are ordering facts.
