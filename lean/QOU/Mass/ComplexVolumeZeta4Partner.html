      ζ₄-partner relation. The ζ₄ = i rotation z ↦ z + iπ/2 sends the complex-volume model to V₀(z + iπ/2) = −cosh z + i·sinh z — it negates the bar-even (binding / Re) channel and keeps the bar-odd (Q_β / Im) channel.       The full ζ₄ orbit and its isotypic decomposition (bean 5nx8, 2026-07-02) #

      One layer beyond the partner relation: the ζ₄ action on the model closes into an order-4 orbit (ζ₄² = −1, ζ₄³ = channel-conjugation), the two non-faithful isotypes vanish (orbit sum = trivial isotype = 0; alternating sum = sign isotype = 0), and the model decomposes exactly into the two faithful eigencomponents (1 ± i)/2 · e^{±z} (eigenvalues ±i under the rotation). The binding (cosh, bar-even) and Q_β (i·sinh, bar-odd) channels are the symmetric/antisymmetric recombinations of the faithful pair. Constraint on the open single-𝔙 assembly (the 5nx8 gap map's OPEN piece): any candidate assembly must be ζ₄-faithful — a nonzero orbit sum or a nonzero sign-isotype component falsifies it. Structural advance, not a closure.

      The ζ₄-faithfulness constraint as a §3b conjecture class (bean 5nx8) #

      The 5nx8 deliverable: turn the "any candidate single-𝔙 assembly must be ζ₄-faithful" constraint (identified above from vModel_orbit_sum_zero + vModel_sign_isotype_zero) into a formal, falsifiable object — a predicate on candidate assemblies plus the §3b conjecture class carrying the open single-𝔙 assembly as a ζ₄-faithful fibre object.

      • IsZeta4Faithful A — the predicate: the ζ₄-orbit sum (trivial isotype) and alternating orbit sum (sign isotype) of A both vanish, i.e. A is supported entirely on the two faithful ±i characters.       • vModel_isZeta4Faithful — the proved cosh + i·sinh model satisfies it (non-vacuity: the constraint is realisable, so the conjecture asks something possible; what is open is whether the physical assembly is such an object).
