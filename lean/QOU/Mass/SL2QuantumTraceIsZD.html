    Convention D is the U_q(𝔰𝔩₂) fundamental quantum trace — and 𝔰𝔩₃ is not it #

    Bean qou-d0qq, prune P2 of docs/audits/2026-09-07-route-c-step3-and-kz-level-plans.md §B.

    On the fundamental representation V of U_q(𝔰𝔩_N), the braiding Ř on V ⊗ V has eigenvalue q on Sym² V and −q⁻¹ on Λ² V (the paper's §7a normalisation (σ − q)(σ + q⁻¹) = 0), so its quantum trace, normalised by the quantum dimension [N]_q² of V ⊗ V, is the Markov parameter

    z_N = (q · dim_q Sym² V − q⁻¹ · dim_q Λ² V) / [N]_q².

    With dim_q Sym² V = [N]_q [N+1]_q / [2]_q and dim_q Λ² V = [N]_q [N−1]_q / [2]_q this collapses to z_N = q^N / [N]_q; the file states the two cases the plan needs explicitly, from the quantum dimensions, so nothing about the general formula is assumed:

    • zSL2_eq_zD — N = 2: (q[3]_q − q⁻¹) / [2]_q² = q³/(q²+1), i.e. exactly the framework's convention-D parameter z_D (QOU.Mass.RouteCPivotalVsCount.zD). This is the algebraic form of the 5 × 10⁻³¹ agreement the planning agent measured.
    • zSL3_eq — N = 3: (q[3]_q[4]_q/[2]_q − q⁻¹[3]_q) / [3]_q² = q⁵/(q⁴+q²+1).
    • zSL3_ne_zD — the two are never equal: z_3 = z_2 would force q²(q²+1) = q⁴+q²+1, i.e. 0 = 1. No ordering, no ℝ: the plan expected an archimedean inequality here and it is not needed.

    Consequence for the KZ front: the target algebra that reproduces the framework's trace convention is 𝔰𝔩₂, and the identification question is q₀-free.
