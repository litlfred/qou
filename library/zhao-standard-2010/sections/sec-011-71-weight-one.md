Weight one
From §3 we know that all relations in weight one follow from (11) and (12), and
no RDS exists. The relations in weight one are crucial for higher level cases
because they provide the weight one relations considered in §3. Moreover, easy
computation by (11) and (12) shows that there is a hidden integral structure,
