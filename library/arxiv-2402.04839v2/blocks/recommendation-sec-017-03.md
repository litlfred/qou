Inserting this into the first terms of \Cref{eq:DvolW,eq:DvolP}, we can see that---for $p$ sufficiently large---the minimiser must be obtained by filling slope $(p, -1)$ on $W$ (setting $p\geq 0$ without loss): For $(p,\pm 1)$, the function $\Delta \vol_P (p,q)$ converges to $\frac{\pi^2}{2p^2}$ plus lower order terms, while $\Delta \vol_W (p,q)$ converges to $\frac{2\pi^2}{p^2}$ plus lower order terms; and we have $\Delta \vol_W (p,-1) = \frac{2\pi^2}{p^2 -4p + 1}$ while $\Delta \vol_W (p,1) = \frac{2\pi^2}{p^2 +4p + 1}$.
