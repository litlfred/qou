\begin{prop}\label{prop:mathieu} If $k\equiv n\pmod{2}$ then \begin{align} c(n,k,q)&=\sum_{m\in M(n,k)}q^{c(m)+\cro(m)} \nonumber\\ &=(1-q)^{-\frac{n-k}{2}}\sum_{j\geq 0}{n\choose \frac{n-k-2j}{2}} (-1)^j q^{j\choose 2} \frac{[k+2j]}{[k+j]}{k+j\brack j}\label{eq:lucasformula}\\ &=(1-q)^{-\frac{n-k}{2}}\sum_{j\geq 0} \left({n\choose \frac{n-k-2j}{2}}-{n\choose \frac{n-k-2j-2}{2}}\right) (-1)^j q^{{j+1\choose 2}}{k+j\brack k}.\label{eq:cn} \end{align} \end{prop} In the next section we shall give another proof by using the orthogonality of the continuous $q$-Hermite polynomials.
