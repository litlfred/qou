A CURIOUS q-ANALOGUE OF HERMITE POLYNOMIALS
JOHANN CIGLER AND JIANG ZENG
Abstract. Two well-known q-Hermite polynomials are the continuous and discrete q-Hermite
polynomials.
In this paper we consider a new family of q-Hermite polynomials and prove
several curious properties about these polynomials. One striking property is the connection
with q-Fibonacci and q-Lucas polynomials. The latter relation yields a generalization of the
Touchard-Riordan formula.
