It is accurate to state that one of the most important develop ments in
probability theory in the last century was Stein’s method. S tein’s method
is a highly original technique and has been useful in proving normal and
Poisson approximation theorems in probability problems wi th limited infor-
mation such as the knowledge of only a few moments of the rando m variable.
Stein’s method can be diﬃcult to work with and often the bound s arising
are not sharp, even in simple problems. But it sometimes is th e only option
available and has been a smashing success in Poisson approxi mation in com-
putational biology. Good surveys of Stein’s method (two of t hem books) are
[ArGG],[BHJ],[Stn1],[Stn2].
