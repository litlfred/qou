Depth-dependent bound on the PT negativity and the CCNR negativity
Here, we prove the depth-dependent bound:
E(ρ) ≤d|∂A| ln 2 .
(109)
To this end, we recall that the Schmidt number s(ρ) is an entanglement monotone for mixed
states [105]. Since S0(|ψ⟩) ≤d|∂A| ln 2 in noiseless quantum circuits [51], it follows that we have
the bound
s(ρ) ≤2d|∂A|,
(110)
in the case of noisy circuits. Now, combining Eq. (110) and Eq. (106), we obtain Eq. (109). Finally,
we note that by Eq. (108), we can also show
C(ρ) ≤d|∂A| ln 2 .
(111)
