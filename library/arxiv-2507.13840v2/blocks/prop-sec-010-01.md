Any ensemble of PRDMs with entropy S2 = O(log n) must have PT negativity E(ρ) = ω(log n) with high probability. This is proven in Appendix I. The key idea is to assume by contradiction that there exists a PRDM with E(ρ) = O(log n). This would im- ply that p4 = 1/poly(n), which can be efficiently distinguished from Haar random states via the efficient measurement of the PT moment p4 in Appendix A. A related notion is pseudoentanglement [2, 10], which are (efficiently preparable) ensemble of states with low entanglement g(n) that are in- distinguishable from an ensemble with high en- tanglement f(n).
