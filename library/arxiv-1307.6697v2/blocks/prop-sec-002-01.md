The cross-ratio coordinates (or z-coordinates). 3.3. Hyperbolic ideal tetrahedra. An ideal hyperbolic tetrahedron is given by 4 points on the boundary of H3 R, i.e. P1(C). Up to the action of PSL(2, C), these points are in homogeneous coordinates [0, 1], [1, 0], [1, 1] and [1, z] – the complex number z being the cross-ratio of these four points.
