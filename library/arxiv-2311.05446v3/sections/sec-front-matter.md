NEW BRUNN–MINKOWSKI AND FUNCTIONAL
INEQUALITIES VIA CONVEXITY OF ENTROPY
GAUTAM AISHWARYA AND LIRAN ROTEM
Abstract.We study the connection between the concavity properties
of a measureνand the convexity properties of the associated relative
entropyD(·∥ν) along optimal transport. As a corollary we prove a new
dimensional Brunn–Minkowski inequality for centered star-shaped bod-
ies, when the measureνis log-concave with a p-homogeneous potential
(such as the Gaussian measure). Our method allows us to go beyond the
usual convexity assumption on the sets that is fundamentally essential
for the standard differential-geometric technique in this area.
We then take a finer look at the convexity properties of the Gauss-
ian relative entropy, which yields new functional inequalities. First we
obtain curvature and dimensional reinforcements to Otto–Villani’s HWI
inequality in Gauss space, when restricted to even strongly log-concave
measures. As corollaries, we obtain improved versions of Gross’ Loga-
rithmic Sobolev inequality and Talagrand’s transportation cost inequal-
ity in this setting.
