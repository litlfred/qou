Theorem 6.5. Let R be a subring of ¯Q, let Z⊂ Z R a⇔R-connected subset,
and let Z ′ ⊂ Z. Suppose that for some ζ∈ Z there are inﬁnitely many
elements ξ∈ Z ′ with ξ⇔R ζ. Then the homomorphism τR
Z,Z ′ : R[q]Z →
PZ ′(R) is injective.
Proof. The proof is similar to that of Theorem 6.1 with the cyclotomi c
polynomials replaced with the polynomials q− ζ with ζ a roots of unity.
The details are left to the reader. □
