Deﬁnition 6.3 ([14], [15]).
(a) An L-module M is called restricted if for any a ∈C, v ∈M there is an integer
T such that for any n ⩾T one has a(n)v = 0.
(b) An L-module M is called a highest weight module if it is generated over L by
a single element m ∈M such that L+m = 0, where L+ is the subspace of L
generated by {a(n) | a ∈B, n ⩾0}. In this case, m is called the highest weight
vector.
