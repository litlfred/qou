Abstract
We analyze the eﬀective low–energy ﬁeld theory of Goldstone bosons and baryons
chirally coupled to massive spin–1 ﬁelds. We use the electromagnetic baryon form
factors to demonstrate the formal equivalence between the vector and the tensor ﬁeld
formulation for the spin–1 ﬁelds. We also discuss the origin of the so–called Weinberg
term in pion–nucleon scattering and the role of ρ–meson exchange. Chirally coupled
vector mesons do not give rise to this two–pion nucleon seagull interaction but
rather to higher order corrections. Some problems of the formal equivalence arising
in higher orders and related to loops are touched upon.
