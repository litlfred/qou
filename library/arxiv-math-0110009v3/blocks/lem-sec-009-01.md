Suppose S is a subset of Rn such that 0 ∈S, there are n linearly independent vectors in S, and for all x, y ∈S, the distance |x −y| is the square root of an even integer. Then the subgroup of Rn generated by S is an even integral lattice. Recall that an integral lattice is a lattice in which the inner product of each pair of vectors is an integer; it is even if every vector has even norm.
