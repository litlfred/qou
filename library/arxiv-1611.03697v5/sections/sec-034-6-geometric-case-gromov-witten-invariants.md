\label{gw}

In this section we consider a class of BPS structures related to closed topological string theory  on a compact Calabi-Yau threefold. In mathematical terms they arise from stability conditions on the category of coherent sheaves supported in dimension $\leq 1$. These BPS structures are uncoupled but not finite. We will show that  formally applying the expression \eqref{em}  in this case reproduces the genus 0 degenerate contributions to the Gromov-Witten generating function.
In \cite{tocome} we give a more careful analysis for the special case of the resolved conifold.
