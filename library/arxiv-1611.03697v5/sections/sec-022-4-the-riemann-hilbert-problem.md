In this section we discuss the Riemann-Hilbert problem deﬁned by a convergent BPS structure.
It is closely related to the Riemann-Hilbert problem considered by Gaiotto, Moore and Neitzke
[16], and has also been studied by Stoppa and his collaborators [1, 14].
