Let us also choose a projective structure on the Riemann surface $S$.\footnote{ If the quadratic differential $q$ has a double pole at a point $p\in S$ one should assume that this projective structure (represented in \cite{IN} by the term $Q_2(z)$) also has a pole of a particular form \cite[Assumption 2.5]{IN}.} We can then invariantly consider the holomorphic Schr{\"o}dinger equation \[\hbar^2 \frac{d^2}{dz^2} y(z,\hbar) = q(z)\cdot y(z,\hbar),\] where $z$ is a co-ordinate in the chosen projective structure.
