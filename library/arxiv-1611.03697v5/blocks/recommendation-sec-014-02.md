Composing with the (twisted) characters of $\bT_\pm$, we can alternatively encode the solution in either of the two systems of maps \[\Phi_\gamma =x_\gamma\circ \Phi\colon \bC^*\to \bC^*, \qquad \Psi_\gamma=y_\gamma\circ \Psi\colon \bC^*\to \bC^*.\] Condition (c) is then the statement that for each $\gamma\in \Gamma$ there should exist $k>0$ such that \[|t|^{-k}<|\Phi_\gamma(t)|<|t|^k, \qquad |t|\gg 0.\]
