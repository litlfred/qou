Work of Iwaki and Nakanishi \cite{IN} shows that the Riemann-Hilbert problem corresponding to a pair $(S,q)$ is closely related to exact WKB analysis of the corresponding Schr{\"o}dinger equation \[\hbar^2 \,\frac{d^2}{dz^2} y(z;\hbar)= q(z) y(z,\hbar),\] where $z$ is some local co-ordinate in a fixed projective structure on $S$, and $\hbar$ should be identified with the variable $t$ in Problem \ref{an}.
