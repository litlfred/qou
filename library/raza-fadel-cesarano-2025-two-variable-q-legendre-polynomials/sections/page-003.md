generating new families of q-special polynomials and demonstrating their quasi-monomiali-
ty. This treatment provides a framework for understanding q-special polynomials as solutions
to generalized forms of q-partial differential equations and q-integro-differential equations. It
can also derive additional classes of q-generating functions and generalizations of q-special
functions. Recently, q-special polynomials such as q-Laguerre polynomials of two variables [3]
and q-Hermite-based Appell polynomials of two variables [11] were effectively achieved by
