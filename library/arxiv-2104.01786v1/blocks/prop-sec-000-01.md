The suggestion that octonions may provide a useful language for describing elementary particles is almost as old as the Standard Model (SM) itself, see [@Gunaydin:1973rs] and references therein.[^1] Thus, it was observed early on that the 8-dimensional space of octonions, after a complex structure is chosen, splits as ${\mathbb O}={\mathbb C}\oplus{\mathbb C}^3$, with the first factor naturally corresponding to leptons and the second one describing quarks.
