Cliﬀord algebra Cliﬀ7
Octonions give rise to a very convenient model for the Cliﬀord algebra Cliﬀ7. Indeed, we can
identify
Cliﬀ7 = Im(O).
(10)
To realise this identiﬁcation, we consider the endomorphisms of O given by the operators
Ei := Lei
(11)
of left multiplication by an imaginary octonion. These operators anti-commute and square to
minus the identity
EiEj + EjEi = −2δij.
(12)
Thus, the generate the Cliﬀord algebra Cliﬀ7.
The Cliﬀord generators can be described very explicitly as 8 × 8 anti-symmetric matrices
acting on 8-dimensional columns that represent elements of O. Using the octonion product
encoded in (9) we get
E1
= −E12 + E38 −E47 + E56
(13)
E2
= −E13 −E28 + E46 + E57
E3
= −E14 + E27 −E36 + E58
E4
= −E15 −E26 −E37 −E48
E5
= −E16 + E25 + E34 −E78
E6
= −E17 −E24 + E35 + E68
E7
= −E18 + E23 + E45 −E67.
These are all 8×8 real (anti-symmetric) matrices, and the octonions in the column are ordered
as I, e1, . . . , e7. They also have the property EiI = ei, where both I, ei are 8-component columns.
