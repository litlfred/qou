6.2 Identiﬁcation with particles
The complex structure J splits O into its (1 , 0), (0, 1) subspaces, each of them being a copy
of C4. Moreover, there is a preferred copy of C, arising as the one spanned by I,u ∈ O.
This is where leptons will live. Similarly, the complex structure ˜J splits ˜O into its (1, 0), (0, 1)
subspaces. We know that each of these describes Lorentz 2-com ponent spinors of the same
chirality. As in all GUT discussions, we can concentrate on only one of these spaces, thus
describing the particle content using Lorentz spinors of the same c hirality.
Putting all of this together, we see that O × ˜O splits as
O × ˜O =L ⊕Q ⊕ ¯L ⊕ ¯Q. (84)
The ﬁrst two terms here are in the (1 , 0) subspace of J, the second two are in the (0 , 1)
eigenspace. Each of these spaces is also a fundamental represen tation of SU(2) ⊂ Spin(4, 3),
and a Lorentz spinor. Concentrating on the (1 , 0) eigenspace of ˜J selects 2-component Lorentz
spinors of the same chirality. Thus, the projection of every term in (84) onto the (1, 0) eigenspace
of ˜J gives a fundamental representation of SU(2) that is also a 2-comp onent Lorentz spinor.
