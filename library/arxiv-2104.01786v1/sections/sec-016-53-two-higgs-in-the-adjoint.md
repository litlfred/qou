5.4 Two Higgs ﬁelds in the fundamental

5.5 A model with only fundamental Higgs ﬁelds
There is also a version of the model with Higgs in the fundamental rep resentations that does not
introduce the bi-fundamental Higgs Φ, see e.g. [26]. Indeed, the t wo VEV’s (81) are suﬃcient
to break SU(2) L × SU(2)R × U(1)B−L down to the electromagnetic U(1) EM . The drawback of
this model is that it does not allow for Yukawa-type mass terms. How ever, such terms can be
produced using higher dimensional operators with two copies of the Higgs, see [26].
