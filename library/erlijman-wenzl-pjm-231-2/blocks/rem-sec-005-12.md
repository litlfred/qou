If the stabilizer StabGs 1⃗λ is trivial, which usually is the case for most labels, the bimodule M⃗λ is irreducible, and its decomposition into N–M-bimodules is again determined by the fusion coefﬁcients Lν⃗ λ. Unfortunately, our theorem does not say anything about what End(M⃗λ) looks like if |StabGs 1⃗λ| ≥4. For example, if the stabilizer has four elements, End(M⃗λ) could be isomorphic to C4 or to the 2 × 2 matrices. Neither does it say how the submodules of M⃗λ decompose into irreducible N–M modules in these cases.
