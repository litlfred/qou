For the geometric branch, the solution to the first equation in Table [\[tab:Seqns\]](#tab:Seqns){reference-type="ref" reference="tab:Seqns"} is chosen to be $$S_0^{({\rm geom})} (u) = \frac{i}{2}{\mbox{Vol\,}}({\bf 4_1};0) + \int_0^u du\, v_{{\rm geom}} (u) + i\pi u\,,
\label{GS0}$$ with $v_{{\rm geom}} (u)$ as in [\[v41\]](#v41){reference-type="eqref" reference="v41"}. The integration constant $i({\mbox{Vol\,}}(0)+i\rm{CS}(0))$ is not important for determining the remaining coefficients (since only derivatives of $S_0$ appear in the equations), but we have fixed it by requiring that $S_0^{({\rm geom})}(0) = \frac{i}{2}({\mbox{Vol\,}}({\bf 4_1};0)+i {\rm CS} ({\bf 4_1};0))
= \frac{i}{2}{\mbox{Vol\,}}({\bf 4_1};0) = (1.01494\ldots)i$, as expected for the classical action of Chern-Simons theory.

$\overline{\hspace{2in}}$\
According to [\[sonetorsion\]](#sonetorsion){reference-type="eqref" reference="sonetorsion"}, the coefficient $S_1^{({\rm geom})} (u)$ in the perturbative Chern-Simons partition function should be related to the Reidemeister-Ray-Singer torsion of $M$ twisted by ${\cal A}^{({\rm geom})}$, which has been independently computed. Our function matches

[^2]

that appearing in *e.g.* [@gukov-2006], up to a shift by $-\log\pi$. The constants of integration for the remaining coefficients have been fixed by comparison to the asymptotics of the colored Jones polynomial, using [\[Ss\]](#Ss){reference-type="eqref" reference="Ss"} and the results of Section [\[sec:arith_example\]](#sec:arith_example){reference-type="ref" reference="sec:arith_example"}.

[^1]: It is computationally advantageous to express everything in terms of $m=e^u$ and $m \frac{d}{dm}=\frac{d}{du}$, *etc.*, when implementing this on a computer.

[^2]: To compare with [@gukov-2006], note that $k_{{\rm there}} = k_{{\rm here}} = i \pi/\hbar$, and $u_{{\rm there}} = 2u_{{\rm here}}$. The shift by $-\log(\pi)$ is directly related to a jump in the asymptotics of the colored Jones polynomial at $u=0$.
