According to the Generalized Volume Conjecture [@gukov-2003], the invariant ${\cal J}(K;N)$ should have the asymptotics[^1] $$\label{JKn} {\cal J}(K;N) \;\sim\; \frac{ Z^{\text{(geom)}}(M;\,i\pi/N)} {Z({\bf S}^1 \times {\bf D}^2;\,i\pi/N)}\;\sim\;N^{3/2}\, \exp\left( \sum_{n=0}^\infty s_n \biggl(\frac{2\pi i}N\biggr)^{n-1} \right)$$ as $N\to\infty$, where $$\begin{aligned} S_n^{(\rm geom)}(0) & = s_n \cdot 2^{n-1} \qquad(n \ne 1) \label{Ss} \\ S_1^{(\rm geom)}(0) & = s_1 + \frac{1}{2} \log 2 \qquad(n=1) \nonumber \end{aligned}$$ are the perturbative $SL(2,{\mathbb{C}})$ invariants of $M = {\bf S}^3 \smallsetminus K$.
