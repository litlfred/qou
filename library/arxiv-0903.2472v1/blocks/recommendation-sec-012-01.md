As explained in Section [\[sec:ideal\]](#sec:ideal){reference-type="ref" reference="sec:ideal"}, the shape parameters $z$ and $w$ must obey consistency relations, which in the case of the figure-eight knot reduce to a single algebraic relation (see *e.g.* Chapter 4 of [@thurston-1980] and Section 15 of [@neumann-2004]): $$(z-1)(w-1)=z^2w^2 \,. \label{figeightgluing}$$ The shape parameters $z$ and $w$ are related to the $SL(2,{\mathbb{C}})$ holonomy eigenvalue, $l$, along the longitude[^1] of the knot in the following way $$\frac{z^2}{z-1} = -l\,, \qquad \frac{w^2}{w-1} = - l^{-1}\,, \label{lholviashape}$$ which automatically solves the consistency condition [\[figeightgluing\]](#figeightgluing){reference-type="eqref" reference="figeightgluing"}.
