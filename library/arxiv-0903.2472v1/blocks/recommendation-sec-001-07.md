The moduli space of flat $G_{{\mathbb{C}}}$ connections on a 3-manifold $M$ with a single toral boundary, $\partial M = T^2$, defines a complex Lagrangian submanifold $${\cal L}\subset ( {\Bbb{T}}_{{\mathbb{C}}} \times {\Bbb{T}}_{{\mathbb{C}}} ) / {\cal W}\;.$$ (More precisely, this Lagrangian submanifold comprises the top-dimensional (stable) components of the moduli space of flat $G_{{\mathbb{C}}}$ connections on $\Sigma$.) In particular, a generic irreducible component of ${\cal L}$ is defined by $r$ polynomial equations $$A_i (l,m) \;=\; 0\,, \qquad i = 1, \ldots, r\,, \label{aizero}$$ which must be invariant under the action of the Weyl group ${\cal W}$ (which simultaneously acts on the eigenvalues $l_1, \ldots, l_r$ and $m_1, \ldots, m_r$).
