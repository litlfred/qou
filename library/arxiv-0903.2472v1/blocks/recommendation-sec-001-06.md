Since we are interested only in the "holomorphic" sector of the theory, we shall look only at the kinetic term for the field ${\cal A}$ (and not $\bar {\cal A}$); it leads to a holomorphic symplectic 2-form on ${{\cal M}}_{{\rm flat}}(G_{{\mathbb{C}}}, \Sigma)$: $$\omega \;=\; {i \over 4 \hbar} \int_{\Sigma} {\rm Tr}~ \delta {\cal A}\wedge \delta {\cal A}\,. \label{msympl}$$ We note that this symplectic structure does not depend on the complex structure of $\Sigma$, in accord with the topological nature of the theory.
