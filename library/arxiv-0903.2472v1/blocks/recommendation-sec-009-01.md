Using the leading term in [\[qdl-hbarexpansion\]](#qdl-hbarexpansion){reference-type="eqref" reference="qdl-hbarexpansion"}, we can approximate it to leading order as $$H (M;\hbar, u) \; \underset{\hbar \to 0}{\sim} \; \int\,e^{\frac{1}{\hbar} V(p_1, \ldots, p_{N-1}, u)}\; dp_1 \ldots dp_{N-1}\,, \label{Hik_V_limit}$$ with the "potential" $$V({\bf p},u) \;=\; \frac{1}{2} \sum_{i=1}^N \epsilon_i \text{Li}_2(-\exp(g_i({\bf p},2u) + i\pi \epsilon_i))+\frac{1}{2} f({\bf p},2u,\hbar=0)\,. \label{Hik_gen_V}$$ As explained below [\[qdl-hbarexpansion\]](#qdl-hbarexpansion){reference-type="eqref" reference="qdl-hbarexpansion"}, the branches of $\text{Li}_2$ must be chosen appropriately to coincide with the half-lines of poles and zeroes of the quantum dilogarithms in [\[gen_Hik_integral\]](#gen_Hik_integral){reference-type="eqref" reference="gen_Hik_integral"}.
