Integrable Uℏg-modules are well–known to be faithful, i.e., the only element of
Uℏg acting trivially on every integrable module is zero [Lus93, Prop. 3.5.4]. To
the best of our knowledge, the analogous result for the more restrictive class of
integrable modules in category O does not appear in the literature. We present
here a proof due to P. Etingof, which establishes faithfulness for a larger algebra
containing Uℏg.

3.4. Proof of Theorem 3.1. Assume that x ∈Dℏacts trivially on every category
O integrable Uℏg–module.
We shall prove that x acts trivially on any Verma
module, so that x = 0 by Proposition 3.2.
Clearly, x acts trivially on M(λ) if and only if, for any v ∈M(λ) and f ∈M(λ)∗,
the matrix coeﬃcient (xv, f)M(λ) vanishes.
By Proposition 3.3, it is enough to
check that this holds for λ in a Zariski open subset of h∗. To this end, note that,
if v ∈M(λ)β, then xv = x(β)v, where x(β) ∈Ug is the truncation of x at β.
Therefore, it is possible to choose λ ∈P+ large enough such that
(xv, f)M(λ) = (xv, f)L(λ) = 0
i.e., (xv, f)M(λ) is equal to the matrix coeﬃcient of x on the unique irreducible
quotient L(λ) of M(λ).
By assumption on x, the latter is zero, since L(λ) is
integrable for λ ∈P+. The result follows.
