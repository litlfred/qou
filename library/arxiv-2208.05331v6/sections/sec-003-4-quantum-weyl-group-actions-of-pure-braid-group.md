4.7. Pure braid group actions for Uqg. Let K be a ﬁeld of characteristic zero,
q ∈K× an element of inﬁnite order, e.g., q ∈C× not a root of unity or q ∈Q(q),
and Uqg the corresponding quantum group over K.
The deﬁnition of (integrable) category O∞Uqg–modules is similar to the formal
case (see e.g., [Lus93, Ch. 3]). The analogues of Theorem 4.5 and Section 4.6 hold
for Uqg and deﬁnes actions of PW on category O∞modules.
In this case, the quantum Casimirs qKi do not lie in Uqg, but in the Drinfeld
algebra Dq of Uqg, and the morphism K takes values in Dq. Note that the latter
acts on any category O∞module V since, for any v ∈V, (Uqn+)βv = 0 for all but
ﬁnitely many β ∈Q+.
