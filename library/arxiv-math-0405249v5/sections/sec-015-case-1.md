::: prop
In case 1, $\mu = 1$, $\lambda\notin \{ q^{-(N+2)} \}_{N \geq 0}$, and $\mu \neq 1$, $\lambda=1$, $HC_0^\sigma(\mathcal{A})$ is infinite-dimensional, $HC_{2n+1}^\sigma(\mathcal{A}) = k[ \omega_1]$, and $HC_{2n+2}^\sigma(\mathcal{A}) = k[1]$, where $[\omega_1]$ is the distinguished generator of $HH_1^\sigma(\mathcal{A})$.
:::

::: pf
By definition, $HC_0^\sigma(\mathcal{A}) = HH_0^\sigma(\mathcal{A})$, generated by $[1]$, together with $[x^{r+1}]$ ($r \geq 0$), for those $x \in \{a,b,c,d\}$ with $\sigma(x) = x$, while $HH_1^\sigma(\mathcal{A})$ is generated by $[(x^r, x)]$, $(r \geq 0$) for the same set of $x$, together with the distinguished generator $[\omega_1 ] = (\mu^{-1} -1) [(d,a)] + (q - q^{-1}) [(b,c)]$. We have $$\begin{aligned}
&&B_0 [1] = [(1,1)]= [ b (1,1,1)]=0,\nonumber\\
&&B_0 [x^{r+1}] = [(1,x^{r+1})] = (r+1) [(x^r ,x)]\nonumber
\end{aligned}$$ by Lemma [\[lemma:B_0_x\^s_y\^t\]](#lemma:B_0_x^s_y^t){reference-type="ref" reference="lemma:B_0_x^s_y^t"}. Hence $\mathrm{ker}(B_0 ) = k[1]$, and $HH_1^\sigma(\mathcal{A}) = \mathrm{im}(B_0 ) \oplus k [\omega_1]$. Further $HH_n^\sigma(\mathcal{A}) = 0$ for $n \geq 2$ in each case. Hence the spectral sequence stabilizes at the second page:

$$\begin{CD}
    @ VVV @ VVV @ VVV @ . @ . @ . @ .\\
    {0} @ <<< {k [\omega_1]} @ <<< {k[1]} @ . @ . @ . @ . @ .\\
    @ VVV @ VVV @ . @ . @ . @ . @ .\\
{k[\omega_1]} @ <<< {k[1]} @ . @ . @ . @ . @ . \\
    @ VVV @ . @ .  @ . @ . @ . @ .\\
    {HH_0^\sigma(\mathcal{A})} @ . @ . @ . @ . @ . @ .\\
        \end{CD}$$\
with all further maps being zero. The result follows.
:::
