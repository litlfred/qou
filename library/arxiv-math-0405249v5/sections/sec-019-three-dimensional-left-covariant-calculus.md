[]{#section:3dcalculus label="section:3dcalculus"}

The automorphism of $\mathcal{A}(SL_q (2))$ corresponding to Woronowicz's three-dimensional left-covariant calculus over quantum $SU(2)$ is $$\label{3dcalc_aut}
\sigma(a) = q^{-2} a, \quad
\sigma(b) = q^4 b, \quad
\sigma(c) = q^{-4} c,\quad
\sigma(d) = q^2 d$$ The twisted cyclic 3-cocycle over $\mathcal{A}(SL_q (2))$ arising from this calculus was written down in [@sw1] section 3 (denoted by $\tau_{\omega,h}$) and [@kmt] section 5 (corresponding to the linear functional $\int$).

::: thm
[]{#3dcalc_cocycle_is_trivial label="3dcalc_cocycle_is_trivial"} For $\mathcal{A}= \mathcal{A}(SL_q (2))$, we have $HC^3_\sigma(\mathcal{A}) =0$ for the automorphism ([\[3dcalc_aut\]](#3dcalc_aut){reference-type="ref" reference="3dcalc_aut"}), hence the twisted cyclic 3-cocycle corresponding to $\tau_{\omega,h}$ and $\int$ is a trivial element of twisted cyclic cohomology.
:::

Specializing Proposition [\[cyclic_case_3\]](#cyclic_case_3){reference-type="ref" reference="cyclic_case_3"} to the automorphism ([\[3dcalc_aut\]](#3dcalc_aut){reference-type="ref" reference="3dcalc_aut"}), we obtain:

::: prop
[]{#tch_3dcalc label="tch_3dcalc"} For $\sigma= \sigma_{\lambda,\mu}$, with $\lambda= q^{-2}$, $\mu= q^4$, we have

1.   $HC_0^\sigma(\mathcal{A}) = HH_0^\sigma(\mathcal{A}) \cong k^2$, with generators $[ d^4 c^2]$, $[ a^4 b^2]$,

2.   $HC_1^\sigma(\mathcal{A})  \cong k^2$ generated by $[( a^4 b,b)]$, $[(d^4 c ,c)]$,\
    equivalently $[( d^3 c^2 ,d)]$, $[(a^3 b^2 , a)]$.

3.   $HC_n^\sigma(\mathcal{A}) = 0, \quad n \geq 2$
:::

By duality between twisted cyclic homology and cohomology we have

::: cor
$HC^0_\sigma(\mathcal{A}) \cong k^2 \cong HC^1_\sigma(\mathcal{A})$, $HC^n_\sigma(\mathcal{A}) =0$ for $n \geq 2$.
:::

So the twisted cyclic 3-cocycles coming from Woronowicz's three dimensional calculus that appear in [@sw1] section 3 (denoted by $\tau_{\omega,h}$) and [@kmt] section 5 (corresponding to the linear functional $\int$) are necessarily trivial elements of twisted cyclic cohomology, thus proving Theorem [\[3dcalc_cocycle_is_trivial\]](#3dcalc_cocycle_is_trivial){reference-type="ref" reference="3dcalc_cocycle_is_trivial"}.
