[]{#section_diff_calc_suq2 label="section_diff_calc_suq2"}

In this section we identify the classes in twisted cyclic cohomology of $\mathcal{A}(SL_q(2))$ of the twisted cyclic cocycles arising from the three and four dimensional covariant differential calculi originally discovered for quantum $SU(2)$ by Woronowicz.
