Despite the above, if the characteristic of the ground ﬁeld is big eno ugh in compar-

Corollary. For every prime number p /greaterorequalslant5, there are inﬁnitely many pairwise non-

Proof. Let X be a projective, non-singular, absolutely irreducible algebraic curv e of
