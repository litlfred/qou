The Weierstrass points of Fermat’s quartic were obtained by Hasse in [13]. Besides, the Weierstrass points of C3 can be easily computed, since they are the ﬁxed points of the involutions conjugate to [x : y : z] 7→[−x : y : z]. Explicitly, the Weierstrass points of C3 are [0 : 1 : ζ], [1 : ζ : 0], [ζ : 0 : 1] where ζ = ± q −3± √ . As simple Weierstrass points are precisely ordinary ﬂexes, the Weierstrass points of Ct for t̸ = 0, −1, ±2, 3, t0 are the points of Ct at which the Hessian determinant vanishes.
