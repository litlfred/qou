Let R be a commutative ring with unity, A an R-algebra which is free as an R-module, (B, <) an admissible system on A, and let I be a two sided ideal of A which is generated by a unital Gr¨obner basis: G = {gγ | γ ∈Γ}. Then in the division algorithm, we may choose hi ∈I so that hi = gγi for some γi ∈Γ.
