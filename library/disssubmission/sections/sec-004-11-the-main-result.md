Let X be a complex abelian variety. For charactersχ : π1(X,0)−→C∗
of the fundamental group we denote byLχ the corresponding local system
of complex vector spaces onX of rank one, and for a bounded constructible
sheaf complexK∈ Dbc (X,C) we consider the twistKχ = K⊗C Lχ. With
these notations, our vanishing theorem can be formulated as
THEOREM 1.1. Let P∈ Perv(X,C) be a perverse sheaf. Then for most
charactersχ we have
Hi(X,Pχ) =0 for i ⁄= 0.
Here we use the following terminology: For abelian subvarietiesA⊆ X
let K(A) be the group of charactersχ : π1(X,0)−→C∗ whose restriction
to the subgroupπ1(A,0)⊆ π1(X,0) is trivial. We then say that a statement
holds for most charactersχ if it holds for allχ in the complement of a thin
set of characters, where by a thin set of characters we mean a ﬁnite union
of translatesχi·K(Ai) for certain non-zero abelian subvarietiesAi⊆ X and
suitable charactersχi of π1(X,0). The same terminology will be used in
theorems 1.4 and 1.5 below for line bundlesL∈ Pic0(X).
