1.4. Negligible perverse sheaves
A crucial ingredient for the proof of our vanishing theorem to be given
in section 2.7 will be a classiﬁcation of all perverse sheaves onX of Euler
characteristic zero. To obtain this classiﬁcation we use an index formula for
D-modules, extending the arguments of [37, cor. 1.4] which have shown
that on a complex abelian varietyX every perverse sheafP∈ Perv(X,C)
has Euler characteristicχ(P)≥ 0. As we mentioned earlier, this is the only
part of our proof of theorem 1.1 which so far only works overC.
PROPOSITION 1.6. Let P∈ Perv(X,C) be a simple perverse sheaf.
(a) One hasχ(P) = 0 iff there exists an abelian subvariety A↪→ X of
positive dimension with quotient morphism q: X↠B = X/A such
that P∼= Lϕ⊗q∗(Q)[dim(A)]
for some Q∈ Perv(B,C) and some characterϕ∈ Π(X).
(b) One hasχ(P) =1 iff P is a skyscraper sheaf on X of rank one.
