If X is a complex ppav of dimension g equipped with a smooth symmetric theta divisor Θ ⊂X, then G(δΘ) = ( SOg!(C) for g odd, Spg!(C) for g even, and δΘ corresponds to the standard representation of this group. Intuitively, this conjecture says that in marked contrast with the singular situation for Jacobian varieties, for a smooth theta divisor Θ the Tannaka group G(δΘ) should be as large as possible — see lemma 4.6 below.
