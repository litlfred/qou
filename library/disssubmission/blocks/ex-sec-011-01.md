The axioms (D1) – (D5) hold if D ⊆Db c (X,Λ) is the full subcategory of all direct sums of degree shifts of semisimple perverse sheaves which in case char(k) > 0 are deﬁned over some ﬁnite ﬁeld. For k = C this follows from [35] together with [14], [43], or alternatively from [87] and [78]. On the other hand, for char(k) > 0 one can invoke the mixedness results of [69] and [10]. Note that in the above example we could also replace the category D by the full subcategory of objects of geometric origin in the sense of [10, sect. 6.2.4].
