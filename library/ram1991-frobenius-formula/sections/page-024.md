Work of Vershik and Kerov

The Frobenius formula appears explicitly on p. 36 of a recent preprint of Kerov
[K] with a reference to [VK1] for a proof. We have not been able to find any
reference to or proof of the Frobenius formula in [VK1]. There is, however,
another paper of Vershik-Kerov [VK2] which gives the relationship of the function
q, to the characters of the infinite dimensional Hecke algebra H_,(q). In [VK2] they
give the generating function of the g, and remark that this is a Hall-Littlewood
polynomial. They also remark that the characters of H,,(q) are related to the
solutions of the quantum Yang-Baxter equation (our §2) and give the action of the
R-matrix on tensor space (our (3.2)). Lastly, they give necessary and sufficient
conditions that a character of H.,(q) is semi-Markov, ie. whether the character is
an Ocneanu trace. Despite the fact that there are no proofs given, these remarks
give an outline of the mechanisms involved in obtaining the Frobenius formula.
