Let $c$ be an enhanced Ptolemy assignment on $K$ where all the Ptolemy coordinates of the top and and bottom edges of $K$ are nonzero. The identification relations are given by $c_{12,k}=t_kh_kc_{13,k-1}$, $c_{02,k}=b_kh_kc_{03,k-1}$ and $c_{01,k}=c_{01,k-1}t_kb_k$, where the $t_k$, $b_k$ and $h_k$ are monomials in the $m_s$ and $l_s$ (see Figure [\[fig:FatEdgeNbh\]](#fig:FatEdgeNbh){reference-type="ref" reference="fig:FatEdgeNbh"}). An elementary modification (we leave the details to the reader) of the proof of Lemma [\[lemma:edgeRelationEquivalence\]](#lemma:edgeRelationEquivalence){reference-type="ref" reference="lemma:edgeRelationEquivalence"} shows that we have $$\label{eq:EdgeEquationsEnhanced}
\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}\Big(\frac{c_{23,k}}{c_{12,k}c_{13,k}}\prod_{j=1}^k t_j^2\Big)=0\iff\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}\Big(\frac{c_{23,k}}{c_{02,k}c_{03,k}}\prod_{j=1}^k b_k^2\Big)=0,$$ and that both are satisfied if the $c_{01,k}$ are nonzero. We refer to these as *edge relations*. The geometric interpretation of the edge relations is given in Figure [1](#fig:FatEdgeRelationTop){reference-type="ref" reference="fig:FatEdgeRelationTop"}.
