[]{#sec:PtolemyBrief label="sec:PtolemyBrief"} The Ptolemy variety $P(\mathcal T)$ is given by a *Ptolemy coordinate* for each edge and a *Ptolemy relation* for each simplex, and consists of the set of solutions where each coordinate is nonzero (see Section [\[sec:PtDecCocReview\]](#sec:PtDecCocReview){reference-type="ref" reference="sec:PtDecCocReview"} for a more detailed review). Just like the gluing equation variety it parametrizes decorated representations, and depends on the triangulation. More precisely, $P(\mathcal T)$ parametrizes generically $P$-decorated boundary-unipotent $\mathop{\mathrm{SL}}(2,\mathbb C)$-representations. There are three natural modifications of $P(\mathcal T)$ (see Section [\[sec:OtherVariants\]](#sec:OtherVariants){reference-type="ref" reference="sec:OtherVariants"}):

-   A Ptolemy variety $P^\sigma(\mathcal T)$, with $\sigma\in H^2(M,\partial M;\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z)$, for boundary-unipotent $\mathop{\mathrm{PSL}}(2,\mathbb C)$-representations with obstruction class to lifting to a boundary-unipotent $\mathop{\mathrm{SL}}(2,\mathbb C)$-representation given by $\sigma$ [@GaroufalidisThurstonZickert; @PtolemyField].

-   An "enhanced" Ptolemy variety $\mathcal EP(\mathcal T)$ for $\mathop{\mathrm{SL}}(2,\mathbb C)$-representations that are not necessarily boundary-unipotent [@ZickertEnhancedPtolemy].

-   A "reduced" Ptolemy variety $P(\mathcal T)_{\mathop{\mathrm{red}}}$ for $B$-decorations instead of $P$-decorations. It is the quotient of $P(\mathcal T)$ by a $(\mathbb C^*)^c$-action, where $c$ is the number of boundary components of $M$ [@GaroufalidisThurstonZickert; @PtolemyField].

In this paper we construct another modification:

-   A triangulation independent refinement $\overline P(\mathcal T)$.

The modifications can all be applied independently, e.g., one also has $P(\mathcal T)_{\mathop{\mathrm{red}}}^\sigma$ and $\mathcal E\overline P(\mathcal T)$, but we shall not consider enhanced Ptolemy varieties with obstruction classes here (see Remark [\[rm:EnhancedPSLExample\]](#rm:EnhancedPSLExample){reference-type="ref" reference="rm:EnhancedPSLExample"} for a brief discussion).

::: remark
There is an explicit regular map from each (triangulation dependent) Ptolemy variety to the gluing equation variety, but we shall not need this here (see e.g. [@GaroufalidisThurstonZickert; @GaroufalidisGoernerZickert; @ZickertEnhancedPtolemy]).
:::
