Deﬁnition 3.5.Let ρ: π1(M)→ SL(2, C) be boundary-Borel. A P-decoration of ρ is a ρ-
equivariant assignment ofP-cosets to each triangular face of~T .
In order to state the analogue of the fundamental correspondence (2.4) we need to consider
another decomposition ofM called thefattened decomposition. This decomposition is obtained
from the decomposition by truncated simplices by replacing each hexagonal face by a hexagonal
prism. Its edges consist of the usual long and short edges together withface pairing edgesjoining
two corresponding vertices on each side of a prism (see Figure 4). We refer to [13, Sec. 4.3.1] for
details.
Deﬁnition 3.6.A fattened natural cocycleis a cocycle on the fattened decomposition restricting
to a natural cocycle on each truncated simplex and labeling face pairing edges by diagonal
elements.

Remark 3.7.The projection ofEP (T ) onto thems,ls coordinates is the variety of eigenvalues
of µs and λs under SL(2, C)-representations. In particular, if M has a single torus boundary
component, the one-dimensional components give rise to factors of theA-polynomial.
