Deﬁnition 4.1.A G-cocycle on M is a labeling of the oriented edges ofM satisfying (i) and
(iv) of Deﬁnition 2.16. AG-cocycle satisfying (ii) as well is called a(G,P )-cocycle.
AG-cocycleτ determines (up to conjugation) a representationπ1(M)→G by taking products
along edge paths. Note that ifτ is a (G,P )-cocycle this representation is boundary-unipotent
and is canonically decorated. Hence, a(G,P )-cocycle determines a decoration.
Deﬁnition 4.2.If D is a decoration andτ a (G,P )-cocycle, we say thatτ is compatible with
D if the decoration determined byτ equals D.

Deﬁnition 4.3.A zero-cochain is a mapη : V (T )→G. The coboundary of a zero-cochainη is
