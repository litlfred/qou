[]{#sec:Enhanced label="sec:Enhanced"} Assume that each boundary component $\partial_s M$ is a torus, and that we have fixed a meridian $\mu_s$ and a longitude $\lambda_s$ in $H_1(\partial_s M)$. The definition of $\mathcal EP(\mathcal T)$ involves a choice of *fundamental rectangle* $R_s$ for each boundary component $\partial_s M$, such that the triangulation induced on the torus obtained by identifying the sides of $R_s$ agrees with the triangulation of $\partial_s M$ induced by $\mathcal T$. It is given by the usual variables $c_{ij,k}$ as well as additional variables $m_s,l_s$ indexed by the boundary components.

::: definition
[]{#def:EnhancedPtolemy label="def:EnhancedPtolemy"} The *enhanced Ptolemy variety* $\mathcal EP(\mathcal T)$ is the Zarisky open subset, where all $c_{ij,k}$ and all $m_s,l_s$ are nonzero, of the zero set of the ideal in $\mathbb Q[{\{c_{ij,k}\}}\cup\{m_s,l_s\}]$ generated by the Ptolemy relations, the edge orientation relations together with *modified identification relations* of the form $c_{ij,k}=pc_{i'j',k'}$, where $p$ is a monomial in the $m_s$ and $l_s$.
:::

We refer to [@ZickertEnhancedPtolemy] for the precise definition of the modified identification relations. They are illustrated in Figure [\[fig:FundRectIdentifications\]](#fig:FundRectIdentifications){reference-type="ref" reference="fig:FundRectIdentifications"} in the case where there is a single boundary torus, and where the sides $\mu'$ and $\lambda'$ of the fundamental rectangle agree with $\mu$ and $\lambda$ (if they don't agree perform the appropriate coordinate change). Here $i_j$ denotes the triangle near vertex $i$ of simplex $j$.

A representation which is not boundary-unipotent does not have a $P$-decoration as defined in Section [\[sec:Decorations\]](#sec:Decorations){reference-type="ref" reference="sec:Decorations"}. We shall therefore need a slightly modified definition. The definition below appears to depend on the triangulation, but one easily checks that it only depends on a choice of base point in each boundary component.

::: definition
Let $\rho\colon\pi_1(M)\to\mathop{\mathrm{SL}}(2,\mathbb C)$ be boundary-Borel. A *$P$-decoration* of $\rho$ is a $\rho$-equivariant assignment of $P$-cosets to each triangular face of $\widetilde{\overline\mathcal T}$.
:::

In order to state the analogue of the fundamental correspondence [\[eq:FundamentalCorrespondence\]](#eq:FundamentalCorrespondence){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:FundamentalCorrespondence"} we need to consider another decomposition of $M$ called the *fattened decomposition*. This decomposition is obtained from the decomposition by truncated simplices by replacing each hexagonal face by a hexagonal prism. Its edges consist of the usual long and short edges together with *face pairing edges* joining two corresponding vertices on each side of a prism (see Figure [1](#fig:EPCocycle){reference-type="ref" reference="fig:EPCocycle"}). We refer to [@ZickertEnhancedPtolemy Sec. 4.3.1] for details.

::: definition
A *fattened natural cocycle* is a cocycle on the fattened decomposition restricting to a natural cocycle on each truncated simplex and labeling face pairing edges by diagonal elements.
:::

The analogue of the fundamental correspondence is $$\label{eq:FundamentalCorrespondenceGeneral}
\left\{\text{Generic $P$-decorations}\right\}\overset{\text{1:1}}{\longleftrightarrow}\mathcal EP(\mathcal T)\overset{\text{1:1}}{\longleftrightarrow}\left\{\text{Fattened natural cocycles on $\overline\mathcal T$}\right\}.$$ The maps are illustrated in Figure [1](#fig:EPCocycle){reference-type="ref" reference="fig:EPCocycle"} and described in detail in [@ZickertEnhancedPtolemy Sec. 4.3].

::: remark
The projection of $\mathcal EP(\mathcal T)$ onto the $m_s,l_s$ coordinates is the variety of eigenvalues of $\mu_s$ and $\lambda_s$ under $\mathop{\mathrm{SL}}(2,\mathbb C)$-representations. In particular, if $M$ has a single torus boundary component, the one-dimensional components give rise to factors of the $A$-polynomial.
:::
