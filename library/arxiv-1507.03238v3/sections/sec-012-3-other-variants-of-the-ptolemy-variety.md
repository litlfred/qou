## Ptolemy coordinates for $\mathop{\mathrm{PSL}}(2,\mathbb C)$-representations {#sec:PSLPtolemy}

A manifold may have boundary-unipotent representations in $\mathop{\mathrm{PSL}}(2,\mathbb C)$ that do not lift to boundary-unipotent representations in $\mathop{\mathrm{SL}}(2,\mathbb C)$. For example, a geometric representation of a complete one-cusped hyperbolic manifold has no boundary-unipotent lifts (any lift of a longitude has trace $-2$, not $2$ [@Calegari]). The obstruction to existence of a boundary-unipotent lift is a class $\sigma\in H^2(\widehat M;\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z)$. For each such class there is a Ptolemy variety $P^\sigma(\mathcal T)$.

Let $\sigma\in C^2(\widehat M;\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z)$ be a cellular cocycle representing a class in $H^2(\widehat M;\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z)$. Although $\sigma$ may not be a coboundary, its restriction $\sigma_k$ to each simplex $\Delta_k$ is a coboundary. Fix $\eta_k\in C^1(\Delta_k;\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z)$ such that $\delta(\eta_k)=\sigma_k$. We identify $\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z$ with $\{\pm 1\}$.

::: definition
The Ptolemy variety $P^\sigma(\mathcal T)$ is the variety generated by the Ptolemy relations, the edge orientation relations, and the *modified edge identification relations* $$\label{eq:EdgeIdentificationsSigma}
c_{ij,k}=(\eta_{ij,k}\eta_{i'j',k'})c_{i'j',k'},\qquad \text{ if } \varepsilon_{ij,k}=\varepsilon_{i'j',k'}.$$ Here $\eta_{ij,k}$ denotes the value of $\eta_k$ on the $ij$-edge of $\Delta_k$.
:::

Up to canonical isomorphism this is independent of the choices of $\eta_k$, and only depends on the class of $\sigma$ in $H^2(\widehat M;\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z)$.

::: remark
In [@PtolemyField], the Ptolemy relations are modified, but not the identification relations. One easily checks that the two definitions agreee.
:::

The analogue of the fundamental correspondence [\[eq:FundamentalCorrespondence\]](#eq:FundamentalCorrespondence){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:FundamentalCorrespondence"} is $$\label{eq:FundamentalCorrespondencePSL}
\xy*[c]\xybox{\xymatrix{
\left\{\txt{Generically decorated\\
$(\mathop{\mathrm{PSL}}(2,\mathbb C),P)$-representations\\with obstruction class $\sigma$}\right\}\ar@/_1pc/@{<->}[rr]_-{1:1}&P^\sigma(\mathcal T)\ar[l]_-{1:z}\ar[r]^-{z:1}&\left\{\txt{Natural $\mathop{\mathrm{PSL}}(2,\mathbb C)$-cocycles on $\overline\mathcal T$\\with obstruction class $\sigma$}\right\},}}\endxy$$ where $z$ is the order of $Z^1(\widehat M;\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z)$, the group of $\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z$-valued $1$-cocycles. The maps are defined as in [\[eq:FundamentalCorrespondence\]](#eq:FundamentalCorrespondence){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:FundamentalCorrespondence"}, i.e., given by Figure [\[fig:FundamentalCorrespondence\]](#fig:FundamentalCorrespondence){reference-type="ref" reference="fig:FundamentalCorrespondence"}; see [@GaroufalidisThurstonZickert (9.31)] and [@PtolemyField (1-4)].

::: remark
A coboundary in $Z^1(\widehat M;\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z)$ acts trivially on the reduced Ptolemy variety, so the map from $P^\sigma(\mathcal T)_{\mathop{\mathrm{red}}}$ to the set of $B$-decorations is $k:1$, where $k=\big\vert H^1(\widehat M;\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z)\big\vert$.
:::
