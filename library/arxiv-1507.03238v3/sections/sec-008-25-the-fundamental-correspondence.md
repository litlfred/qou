3.1. Ptolemy coordinates forPSL(2, C)-representations. A manifold may have boundary-
unipotent representations inPSL(2, C) that do not lift to boundary-unipotent representations in
SL(2, C). For example, a geometric representation of a complete one-cusped hyperbolic manifold
has no boundary-unipotent lifts (any lift of a longitude has trace−2, not2 [2]). The obstruction
to existence of a boundary-unipotent lift is a classσ∈H 2(ˆM; Z/2Z). For each such class there
is a Ptolemy varietyPσ(T ).

σ may not be a coboundary, its restrictionσk to each simplex ∆k is a coboundary. Fixηk∈
C1(∆k; Z/2Z) such thatδ(ηk) =σk. We identifyZ/2Z with{±1}.
