[]{#sec:PtDecCocReview label="sec:PtDecCocReview"} This section summarizes results of [@GaroufalidisThurstonZickert] and [@GaroufalidisGoernerZickert]; see also [@PtolemyField] for a review of the case when $n=2$ with many worked out examples.

Let $M$ be a compact, orientable $3$-manifold with non-empty boundary and let $\widetilde M$ be the universal cover of $M$. Let $\widehat M$ and $\scalerel*{\widehat{\widetilde M}}{\widetilde{M^2}}$ denote the spaces obtained from $M$ and $\widetilde M$, respectively, by collapsing each boundary component to a point. Given a topologically ideal triangulation $\mathcal T$ of $M$ we refer to the cells as *vertices*, *edges*, *faces* and *simplices* of $\mathcal T$.
