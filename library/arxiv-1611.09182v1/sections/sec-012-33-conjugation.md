3.3 Conjugation
In this text, the complex conjugate of an element a will be denoted a∗. The conjugate ∗
maps the complex i↦→−i, in the usual way.
The quaternion conjugate ofa will be denoted~a, and∼ maps the quaternionicϵx↦→−ϵx,

That which we call the hermitian conjugate of a will be denoted a†, and † is the
result of performing both the complex and quaternion conjugates simultaneously: i↦→−i,
ϵx↦→−ϵx, ϵy↦→−ϵy, and ϵz↦→−ϵz.
