The reader is encouraged to conﬁrm that indeed Ψ D transforms as a Dirac spinor, Ψ L
transforms as a left-handed Weyl spinor, and ΨR transforms as a right-handed Weyl spinor
under SL(2, C). In other words, the complex coeﬃcients within Ψ D transform exactly as
would the complex components of a four-dimensional column vector, representing a Dirac
spinor, from standard quantum ﬁeld theory. It is for this reason that the names Ψ D, Ψ L,
and ΨR were given to these generalized ideals early on.
