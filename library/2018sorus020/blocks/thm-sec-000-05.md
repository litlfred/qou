Let X be a geometric component of the character variety X(M) of a hyperbolic manifold M. Then tor(M, ρ) is a regular function on X, that vanishes at a character χ if and only if the vector space H1(M, ¯ρ) is non trivial, where ¯ρ is a representation ¯ρ : π1(M) →SL2(C) whose character is χ. The fact that the torsion has no poles on X was expected. Yet, to our knowledge, there has been no such formal statement to date, and it is interesting in and by itself. The characterization in terms of jump of dimension of the vector space H1(M, ¯ρ) is also notalbe, because it relates the torsion with the deformation theory of semi- simple representations
