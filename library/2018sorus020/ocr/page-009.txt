The torsion of twisted cohomological complexes by a representation p : ™(M) >
SL2(C) for M a hyperbolic 3-manifold has been studied for a long time. Let us
mention the seminal work of Joan Porti in his PhD dissertation (see the
survey too) for the adjoint torsion, and of Teruaki Kitano
for the standard torsion and the relation with the twisted Alexander polynomial.
Many other authors have substantially contributed to the topic in the past ten
years, including Jérome Dubois, Nathan Dunfield, Stefan Friedl, Takahiro Kitayama,
Yoshikazu Yamaguchi.
