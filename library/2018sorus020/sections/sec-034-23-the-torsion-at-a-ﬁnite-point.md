The torsion at a ﬁnite point
Recall that X is a one-dimensional component of the character variety X(M) of
a 3-manifold M with boundary a torus, and the same homology as a circle. The
curve Y is the smooth projective model of a double covering of X, namely the
augmented character variety. Throughout this section v ∈Y is a ﬁnite point, and
ρ : Γ →SL2(Ov) is a convergent tautological representation (see Proposition 1.3.11
and what follows). We prove the following :
53
Theorem (Theorem 2.0.1). The diﬀerential form tor(M, Ad ◦ρ) vanishes at v with
order the length of the torsion part in the Ov-module H1(M, Ad ◦ρ)v. In particular
tor(M, Ad ◦ρ) has no poles nor zeros if v projects on a smooth point of X(M).
