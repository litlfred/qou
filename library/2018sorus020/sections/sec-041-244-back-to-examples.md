1. The trefoil knot.
The incompressible surface Σ is an annulus, hence ρΣ : Z →SL2(k(t)) is
abelian, and the theorem cannot apply. Nevertheless the torsion has a pole of
order one at ideal points corresponding to Σ, hence the equality of theorem
remains true.
2. The ﬁgure-eight knot.
There are two incompressible surfaces Σ1 and Σ2 that are two-holed tori,
and the torsion vanishes at order 1 at each ideal point. Again the equality
1 = −χ(Σi) −1 holds for m = n = 1
3. The knot 5.2.
There are two incompressible surfaces Σ1 and Σ2 (see Figure 1 in the intro-
duction), and we have χ(Σ1) = −4, χ(Σ2) = −2. At the ideal points, the
torsion vanishes at order 1 (corresponds to Σ1) and 3 (corresponds to Σ2),
thus the equality of Theorem 2.0.2 holds.
4. The knot 6.1.
Again, there are two incompressible surface, the ﬁrst of Euler characteristic
−2 (a two-holed torus), and the second of Euler characteristic −6 (a two-holed
genus 3 surface). At the corresponding ideal points, the vanishing order of
tor(M, Ad ◦ρ) is 1, respectively 5, that corresponds again with the equality
case of the theorem.
