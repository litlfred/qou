Alexander module and character varieties
In this section we prove a theorem due independently to Burde and DeRham ([Bur67,
deR67]), that relies the so-called Alexander module of a 3-manifold M, and in parti-
cular roots of its Alexander polynomial, with some particular point in the character
varieties X(M).
