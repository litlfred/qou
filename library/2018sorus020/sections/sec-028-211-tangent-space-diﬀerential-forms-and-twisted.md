Tangent space, diﬀerential forms and twisted cohomo-
logy
The results of this section can be found in [Mar15]. It’s a well-known result [LM85,
Wei64] that the tangent space of the character variety at an irreducible character is
isomorphic to the ﬁrst group of twisted cohomology with the adjoint action. Saito’s
Theorem allows us to prove it in the skein context. This results can serve as a
motivation to study the adjoint-twisted complex, which appears to be related with
the geometry of the character variety.
Deﬁnition 2.1.1. Let ρ : Γ →SL2(k) be a representation. The action by conjuga-
tion of the group SL2(k) on its Lie algebra induces an action of Γ on sl2(k), called
the adjoint action. We deﬁne the complex C∗(M, Ad ◦ρ) of twisted cohomology for
this action with coeﬃcients in sl2(k).

Theorem 2.1.2. Let χ be an irreducible character, and ρ : π1(M) →SL2(k) a re-
presentation which lifts χ. Then we have an isomorphism TχX(M) ≃H1(M, Ad ◦ρ),
where the cohomology coeﬃcient is sl2(k) with the adjoint action.
Sketch of proof. Recall that the Zariski tangent space at a point χ is deﬁned as
the set of morphism φε : B[Γ] →k[ε]/(ε2) such that φ0 = χ. Given a cocyle ϕ ∈
H1(M, Ad ◦ρ), one produces a tangent vector at the point χρ by taking the character
associated to ρε : Γ →SL2(k[ε]/(ε2)), where ρε = (Id +εϕ)ρ. Then the Saito theorem
ensure this map is invertible.
Deﬁnition 2.1.3. Given a ring A, and a A-algebra B, we deﬁne the B-module
of A-derivations ΩB/A to be the free B-module generated by the db’s, divided by
the relations {∀a ∈A, da = 0, ∀b1, b2 ∈B, d(b1 + b2) = db1 + db2 and d(b1b2) =
b1db2 + b2db1}. If X is an irreducible algebraic variety with function ﬁeld k(X), we
denote by Ωk(X)/k the k(X)-vector space of rational diﬀerential forms over X. It is
a classical fact (see [Liu06] for instance) that its dimension as a k(X)-vector space
is the dimension of X as a variety over k.
