In this section we give various deﬁnitions used for the Redemeister torsion. Refe-
rences are [Mil66], [GKZ94, Appendix A], [Por97, Chapitre 0]. We stress out the fact
that we use a convention (namely, how we take the alternating sum in the deﬁnition
of the determinant of a complex) that corresponds to [GKZ94], but not to [Mil66].
