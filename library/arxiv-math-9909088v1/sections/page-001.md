THE GAUSS MAP AND A NONCOMPACT
RIEMANN-ROCH FORMULA FOR CONSTRUCTIBLE
SHEAVES ON SEMIABELIAN VARIETIES
J. Franecki, M. Kapranov
Abstract: For an irreducible subvariety Z in an algebraic group G we deﬁne
an integer gdeg(Z) ≥0 as the degree, in a certain sense, of the Gauss map of Z. It
can be regarded as a substitution for the intersection index of the conormal bundle
to Z with the zero section of T ∗G, even though G may not be compact. For G a
semiabelian variety (in particular, an algebraic torus (C∗)n), we prove a Riemann-
Roch type formula for constructible sheaves on G which involves our substitutions
for the intersection indicies. As a corollary, we get that a perverse sheaf on such a
G has nonnegative Euler characteristic, generalizing a theorem of Loeser-Sabbah.

Both the deﬁnition of CC(F) and the formula (1.1) (for compact X) extend to
the case when F is a bounded constructible complex (i.e., a complex of sheaves with
constructible cohomology).
When X is not compact, χ(X, F) still makes sense, but (1.1) is not applicable.
We face, therefore, an interesting noncompact Riemann-Roch problem of ﬁnding
χ(X, F) in terms of invariants intrinsic to X (in particular, not involving the choice
of compactiﬁcation).
