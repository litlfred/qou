Theorem 9.2.7. Let N ⊂ M be a ﬁnite depth subfactor with ﬁnite index, k be

groupoid such that (N ⊂ Mk) ∼= (N ⊂ N>⊳B). Then Bimod N −N (N ⊂ M ) and
Rep(B∗) are equivalent as tensor categories.
Proof. First, we observe that
BimodN −N (N ⊂M ) = Bimod N −N (N ⊂Ml)

are also objects of Bimod N −N (N ⊂ M ). Conversely, since irreducible N − N
subbimodules of NL2(Mi)N are contained in the decomposition of NL2(Mi+1)N for
