terms of C∗-quantum groupoids. Recall that a II 1 factor is a ∗-algebra of operators
on a Hilbert space that coincides with its second centralizer, or, equ ivalently, is
weakly closed (i.e., a von Neumann algebra) with the trivial center tha t admits a
ﬁnite trace. An example of such a factor is the group von Neumann a lgebra of a
