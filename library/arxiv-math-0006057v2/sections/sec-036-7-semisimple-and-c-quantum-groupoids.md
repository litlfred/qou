7.1. Deﬁnitions.
Deﬁnition 7.1.1. A quantum groupoid is said to be semisimple (resp., ∗- or C∗-
quantum groupoid ) if its algebra H is semisimple (resp., a ∗-algebra over a ﬁeld
k with involution, or ﬁnite-dimensional C∗-algebra over the ﬁeld C and ∆ is a
∗-homomorphism).
Groupoid algebras and their duals give examples of commutative and c ocom-
mutative semisimple quantum groupoids (Corollary 3.3.2), which are C∗-quantum
groupoids if the ground ﬁeld is C (in which case g∗ =g−1 for all g ∈G).
We will describe the class of quantum groupoids possessing Haar integrals, i.e.,
normalized two-sided integrals (note that if such an integral exists then it is unique
and is an S-invariant idempotent).
