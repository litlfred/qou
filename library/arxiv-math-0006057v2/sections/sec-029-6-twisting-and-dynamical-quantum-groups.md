In this section we present a generalization of the Drinfeld twisting co nstruction
to quantum groupoids developed in [NV1], [Xu], and [EN]. We show that dynami-
cal twists of Hopf algebras give rise to quantum groupoids. An importan t concrete
example is given by dynamical quantum groups at roots of 1 [EN], which are dy-
namical deformations of the Drinfeld-Jimbo-Lusztig quantum grou ps Uq(g). The
resulting quantum groupoids turn out to be selfdual, which is a funda mentally new
property, not satisﬁed by Uq(g).
