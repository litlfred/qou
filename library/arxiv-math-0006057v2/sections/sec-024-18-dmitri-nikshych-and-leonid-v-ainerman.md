Proposition 5.2.9. Given a quasitriangular quantum groupoid (H, R), consider a
linear map F : ˆH →H given by
F :φ ↦→(φ ⊗ id)(R21R), φ ∈ ˆH.(33)
Then the range of F belongs to CH (Hs), the centralizer of Hs.
Proof. Takey ∈Hs. Then we have :
φ(R(2)R′(1))R(1)R′(2)y =φ(R(2)yR′(1))R(1)R′(2) =φ(R(2)R′(1))yR(1)R′(2),

Deﬁnition 5.2.10 (cf. [Ma], 2.1.12) . A quasitriangular quantum groupoid is fac-
torizable if the above map F : ˆH →CH (Hs) is surjective.
The factorizability of H means that R is as non-trivial as possible, in contrast
to triangular quantum groupoids, for which R21 = ¯R and the range of F is equal
to Ht.
