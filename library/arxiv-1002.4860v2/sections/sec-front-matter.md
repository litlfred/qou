A STATE-SUM FORMULA FOR THE ALEXANDER POLYNOMIAL
SAMSON BLACK
Abstract. We develop a diagrammatic formalism for calculating the Alexander
polynomial of the closure of a braid as a state-sum. Our main tools are the Markov
trace formulas for the HOMFLY-PT polynomial and Young’s semi-normal repre-
sentations of the Iwahori-Hecke algebras of type A.
