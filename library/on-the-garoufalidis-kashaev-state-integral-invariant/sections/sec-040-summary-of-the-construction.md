Summary of the Construction
To summarize, the construction consists of:
• An ideal triangulation X of M;
• A balanced angle structure θ: X3,1 →C;
• State variables x ∈AX2, dynamical variables z ∈AX3;
35
• The kinematical kernel KX(y, z);
• The dynamical weight DX,θ(z);
• The final integral yielding FR,X,θ(y).
The analytic convergence, Pachner invariance, and angle gauge invariance of this
integral will be elucidated systematically in the chapters to follow.
