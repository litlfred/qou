The class of LCA groups encompasses:
7
• Euclidean spaces Rn.
• The circle group T (written multiplicatively), central to Fourier series.
• Discrete groups such as Z, finite cyclic groups Z/nZ, and their countable direct
sums.
• Compact profinite groups, as inverse limits of finite abelian groups.
• Non-Archimedean fields such as Qp.
• Direct sums and products of LCA groups, which are also LCA groups. For
example, the group R× Z is an LCA group as both R and Z are LCA.
