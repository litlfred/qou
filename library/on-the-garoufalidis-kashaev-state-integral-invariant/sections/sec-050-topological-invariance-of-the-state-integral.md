STATE-INTEGRAL
We now show, the state-integral, constructed using the angle-dependent quantum
dilogarithm Ψa,c over a self-dual Gaussian group, defines a topological invariant of
3-manifolds with torus boundary. This invariance rests on three core principles:
(P1) Invariance under ordered Pachner 2 ↔3 moves — ensured by the angled
pentagon identity of Ψa,c;
(P2) Analytic convergence and tempered behavior — guaranteed by positivity
conditions on angle parameters;[7, 1]
(P3) Gauge invariance under internal angle shifts — due to symmetries of the
angle-dependent dilogarithm.
[7] Together, these properties confirm that the state-integral is determined entirely
by the topology of the 3-manifold and its boundary holonomy data, independent of
auxiliary combinatorial choices.
