Triangle Angle Condition
When three angles a, b, c ∈C are assigned to a triangle, they are required to
satisfy:
a + b + c = (1, −1),
ensuring a canonical normalization. In terms of a and c, the third angle b is given by:
b = (1, −1) −a −c = (1 −˙a −˙c, −(¨a¨c)−1).
