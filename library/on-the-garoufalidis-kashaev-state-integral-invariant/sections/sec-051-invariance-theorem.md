Theorem 6 (P1: Pachner Move Invariance). LetX∼X′ via Pachner moves. Then
for any compatible balanced angle structure θ, the state-integrals coincide:
FF,X,θ =FF,X′,θ′.
Sketch. Each local move is mediated by the angled pentagon identity of Ψ a,c. The
identity ensures that integrating over three tetrahedra is analytically equal to the
product over two, provided angle parameters satisfy the required balancing conditions.
Since each such move preserves the face pairing relations and global structure, the
overall integral remains invariant.
