The main results of this work include:
3
1. A construction of the state-integral FF,X,θ over an Archimedean local field F ,
depending on an ideal triangulation X and a balanced angle structure θ.
2. An exposition that, under positivity assumptions on the angle data and a homo-
logical constraint on the complex, the integrand defines a tempered distribution
and the state-integral converges
3. A demonstration that the deformed quantum dilogarithm Ψ a,c satisfies a pen-
tagon identity, supporting invariance of the partition function under Pachner
2↔ 3 moves.
4. A definiton of gauge transformations of internal angle data leave the state-
integral unchanged, owing to functional symmetries of Ψ a,c.
