Target Statement for the Archimedean State-Integral
Let M be a compact, oriented 3-manifold equipped with an ordered ideal tri-
angulation X.
The analytic target of the Archimedean state-integral depends on
the topology and boundary structure of M, and reflects the deformation parameters
introduced via the angle-dependent quantum dilogarithm Ψa,c.
