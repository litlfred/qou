This thesis considers a class of analytic topological constructions known as face-
type state-integrals, focusing on their formulation over Archimedean local fields. These
integrals originate in a framework developed by Andersen, Garoufalidis, and Kashaev,
which draws together tools from quantum topology, Teichm¨ uller theory, and aspects
of quantum group representation theory.
Face-type state-integrals aim to assign quantum amplitudes to triangulated 3-
manifolds by combining combinatorial input (such as ideal triangulations and an-
gle structures) with algebraically structured weight functions. Although the ana-
lytic formalism involves elements of harmonic analysis—particularly through Fourier-
theoretic techniques on self-dual locally compact abelian groups—this thesis mainly
concentrates on the algebraic and topological structure of the construction. The
analytic side plays a supporting role, primarily enabling the expression of gluing
relations and functional identities in a distributional setting.
This thesis surveys the construction of the face-type state integral for Archimedean
fields emphasizing convergence, algebraic structure, and topological invariance. The
outcome is a tempered distribution associated to a 3-manifold with torus boundary,
depending on triangulation and angle data in a controlled and gauge-invariant way.
