Angled Quantum Dilogarithm
To define the analytic weight functions that drive the state-integral invariant, we
now introduce an angle-deformed quantum dilogarithm Ψa,c derived from the primitive
function φ. This deformation encodes geometric data and satisfies a pentagon identity
modified by angle parameters, crucial for proving the topological invariance of the
state-integral under Pachner moves.
