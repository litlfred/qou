Conditions Ensuring Convergence
From Lemma 2, the function Ψa,c is a tempered distribution on A under:
0 < ˙a, ˙b, ˙c < 1, ˙a + ˙b + ˙c = 1.
This ensures:
• Local integrability of the integrand;
• Polynomial boundedness at infinity;
• Distributional action on test functions ϕ ∈S(A(∂X)2).
Theorem 7 (P2: Analytic Control). Let θ be a balanced angle structure satisfying
the positivity constraints above.
Then FF,X,θ ∈S′(A(∂X)2), the space of tempered
distributions on the boundary face state space.
