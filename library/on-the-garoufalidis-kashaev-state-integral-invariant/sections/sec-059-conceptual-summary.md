topological invariant by combining:
• Triangulation combinatorics, captured via Pachner moves;
• Angle structures, encoding geometric deformation parameters;
• Quantum harmonic analysis over Gaussian groups;
• Functional identities of the quantum dilogarithm Ψa,c;
• Tempered distribution theory, ensuring analytic well-definedness.
