Character Groups and Duality
Pontryagin duality equips every LCA group with a harmonic dual.
Definition 2. A homomorphism of LCA groups is a continuous group homomorphism
between locally compact abelian groups.
8
An isomorphism of LCA groups is a group isomorphism
ϕ: L →H
that is also a homeomorphism. In other words, it is a bijective continuous map whose
inverse is also continuous.
Definition 3. Let L be a locally compact abelian (LCA) group, equipped with its
topology as a topological group. A Pontryagin character on L is a continuous group
homomorphism χ: L →T, where T = {z ∈C | |z| = 1} is the circle group with its
standard topology.
The set of all such characters forms the Pontryagin dual group bL := Homcont(L, T),
which is itself a topological group when endowed with the compact-open topology—that
is, the topology of uniform convergence on compact subsets of L.
This makes bL into an LCA group as well.
