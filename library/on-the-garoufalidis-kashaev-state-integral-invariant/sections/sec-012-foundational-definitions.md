Definition 1. A locally compact abelian group (LCA group) is a topological group L
satisfying:
1. Abelian structure: xy =yx for all x,y∈L.
2. Local compactness: Every pointx∈L admits a neighborhood whose closure
is compact.
3. Hausdorff separation: Distinct points are separable via disjoint open sets.
The combination of commutativity and local compactness ensures the existence
of Haar measure and well-posed harmonic analysis.
