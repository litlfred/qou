The 2↔ 3 Pachner move alters an ideal triangulation by replacing two tetrahedra
sharing a face with three tetrahedra sharing an edge. At the analytic level, this local
modification is governed by the angled pentagon identity satisfied by the function
Ψa,c, as developed in Theorem 5.
