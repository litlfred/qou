Hierarchy of the Pentagon Identities
All pentagon identities that appear in this thesis are avatars of one and the same 
distributional equality on a Gaussian group. They differ only by the choice of
1. the underlying Gaussian group G, and
2. an angle deformation implemented by rescalings and multiplicative characters
(the “angle parameters”).

equivalence implies equal invariants, even after angle deformation.
This completes the construction of the angle-dependent dilogarithmic weights used
in the face-type state-integral. These functions encode local combinatorial data (from
triangulations) and analytic structure (from harmonic analysis) in a unified framework
suitable for topological quantum field theory.
