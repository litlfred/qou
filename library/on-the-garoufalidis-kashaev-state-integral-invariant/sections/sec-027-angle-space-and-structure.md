Angle Space and Structure
Let us define the angle space as the product group:
C := R × B, where B := F ×
is the multiplicative group of a local field F with the group law being addition for
the first component and multiplication for the second; so the the product is a LCA
group.
An element a ∈C is called an angle and is written as
a = (˙a, ¨a),
where ˙a ∈R is the additive component and ¨a ∈B is the multiplicative component.
This structure encodes both real and field-valued geometric data.
We define an involutive automorphism (conjugation) on C by:
19
¯a := (˙a, ¨a−1),
which fixes the additive part and inverts the multiplicative component. This conju-
gation satisfies ¯¯a = a and extends to a group automorphism of C.
