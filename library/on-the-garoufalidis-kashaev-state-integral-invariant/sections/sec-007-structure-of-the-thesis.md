Structure of the Thesis
The thesis is organized as follows:
• Chapter 2 introduces locally compact abelian groups and Pontryagin duality,
along with the Gaussian group structures used in the formulation of the integral.
• Chapter 3 presents the quantum dilogarithm, its deformation by angle data,
and its key functional identities, including inversion and the pentagon relation.
• Chapter 4 defines the face-type state-integral, detailing the roles of kinematical
constraints and dynamical weights.
• Chapter 5 describes the output space of the integral, including the structure of
its dependence on boundary holonomy variables.

• Chapter 6 describes invariance under Pachner moves and angle gauge transfor-
mations.
