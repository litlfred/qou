Connections to Representation Theory
Although harmonic analysis appears only peripherally in this work, the representa-
tion-theoretic context provides important background motivation. At a classical level,
the moduli space of flat G-connections on a 3-manifold (character variety) and the
boundary data in the state-integral may be interpreted as holonomy-type parameters
in this setting.
In the quantum case, the function Ψa,c has close connections to the representation
theory of quantum groups, including Uq(sl2), where it arises in the construction of
intertwiners and associators.
The pentagon identity reflects categorical coherence
relations in braided tensor categories, echoing the structure of TQFTs defined via
modular functors [7].
