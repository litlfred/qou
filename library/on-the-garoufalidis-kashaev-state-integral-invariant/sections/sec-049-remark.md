Remark
The analytic nature of the state-integral reflects a synthesis of:
• Combinatorial topology via ideal triangulations and face gluings;
• Harmonic analysis on self-dual Gaussian groups;
• Functional identities of the angled quantum dilogarithm Ψa,c, including the
pentagon relation;
• Quantum holonomy representation theory via angle structures and boundary
data.
This aligns with frameworks developed in Garoufalidis–Kashaev and Andersen–
Kashaev, and elucidates the role of quantum deformation parameters in determining
the analytic and distributional nature of the state-integral.
