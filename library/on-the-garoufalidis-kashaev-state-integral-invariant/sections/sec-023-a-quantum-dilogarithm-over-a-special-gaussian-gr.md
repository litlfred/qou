A quantum dilogarithm over a Special Gaussian Group
Definition 7. [7] The quantum dilogarithm over A = ˆB × B for B = F × is the
function
φ : ˆB × (B \ {−1}) →T, φ(α, x) := α(1 + x)
We refer to φ as a tempered distribution on A, undefined at x = −1, but this is
inconsequential since {−1} is a measure-zero subset of B.
