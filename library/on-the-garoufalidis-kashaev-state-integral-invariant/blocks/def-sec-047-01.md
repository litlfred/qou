Let X be an ordered ideal triangulation of a compact 3-manifold M, and let G be an abelian group. A k-cochain on X with coefficients in G is a function defined on the set of combinatorial k-simplices of X, respecting orientation. We denote: Ck(X; G) := {ϕ: Xor k →G}, where Xor k is the set of oriented k-simplices in the triangulation X, with orientation induced by the global vertex ordering. These cochains form an abelian group under pointwise addition.
