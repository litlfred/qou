Let L be a locally compact abelian (LCA) group, equipped with its topology as a topological group. A Pontryagin character on L is a continuous group homomorphism χ: L →T, where T = {z ∈C | |z| = 1} is the circle group with its standard topology. The set of all such characters forms the Pontryagin dual group bL := Homcont(L, T), which is itself a topological group when endowed with the compact-open topology—that is, the topology of uniform convergence on compact subsets of L. This makes bL into an LCA group as well.
