Let A be a finite dimensional spherical Hopf algebra. Then the category of projective left A-modules is a spherical category. This constructs a spherical subcategory with J finite but we know of no applica- tions. If A is semisimple this result is superfluous and in the example of the quantised enveloping algebra of SL(2) given in [9] every indecomposable projective has quantum dimension 0 so that J is empty. The category of projective left A-modules is a subcategory of the spheri- cal category of left A-modules.
