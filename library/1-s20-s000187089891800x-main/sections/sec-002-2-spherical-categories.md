In this section we introduce spherical categories. A pivotal category is a
category with duals and with some additional structure and a spherical
category is a pivotal category in which the additional structure satisfies an
extra condition.
Strict pivotal categories are discussed in [3], where it is shown that the
category of oriented planar graphs up to isotopy with labelled edges and
with a distinguished edge at each vertex is a strict pivotal category.
