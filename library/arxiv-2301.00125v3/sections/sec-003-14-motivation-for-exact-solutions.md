In Appendix B we give a rapid review of some points of that developmen t. Our purpose in the appendix
is to demonstrate the role of calculating higher moments in the physic ists’ “replica symmetry breaking”
approach. In the present paper, we are interested in the higher m oments of Zn,k, starting with the second
moment. We call this the generalized Ulam problem. Technically, the ge neralized Ulam problem should be
the problem of stating as much as possible about the distribution of Zn,k for the doubly-indexed sequence
of numbers for n and k. But we are most hopeful to be able to calculate the positive integer moments, in
some asymptotic sense. See Section 5 for a discussion of the relatio n between the 2nd moment and higher
moments.
