Neutrinos are particles which show up in certain interactions, like the decay of
a neutron into a proton, an electron, and an antineutrino

Indeed, neutrinos ν have antiparticlesν, just like quarks and all other particles. The
electron’s antiparticle, denotede+, was the ﬁrst discovered, so it wound up subject
to an inconsistent naming convention: the ‘antielectron’ is called a positron.
Neutrinos carry no charge and no color. They interact very weakly with other
particles, so weakly that they were not observed until the 1950s, over 20 years
after they were hypothesized by Pauli. Collectively, neutrinos and e lectrons, the
fundamental fermions that do not feel the strong force, are ca lled leptons.
In fact, the neutrino only interacts via the weak force . Like the electromag-
netic force and the strong force, the weak force is a fundamenta l force, hypothesized
to explain the decay of the neutron, and eventually required to exp lain other phe-
nomena.
