Let us look for a group that extends SU(5) and has an irrep whose d imension is
some power of 2. The dimension is a big clue. What representations ha ve dimensions
that are powers of 2? Spinors.
What are spinors? They are certain representations of Spin( n), the double cover
of the rotation group in n dimensions, which do not factor through the quotient
SO(n). Their dimensions are always a power of two. We build them by exhibitin g
Spin(n) as a subgroup of a Cliﬀord algebra. Recall that the Cliﬀord algebra Cliﬀn
is the associative algebra freely generated by Rn with relations
vw +wv = −2⟨v,w ⟩.
If we take products of pairs of unit vectors in Rn inside this algebra, these generate
the group Spin( n): multiplication in this group coincides with multiplication in
the Cliﬀord algebra. Using this fact, we can get representations of Spin(2n) from
modules of Cliﬀ n.

All this works for any n, but we are especially interested in the case n = 5.

representation of Spin(2n) extend the representation of SU( n) on Λ Cn?
