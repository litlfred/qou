For the proof of $(ii)$ we must show the following $$\int_\mathcal{T} m_{B_1,B_2,B_3}\,\mu_1\wedge \mu_2\wedge \mu_3=\int_{g(\mathcal{T})} m_{g_\ast B_1,g_\ast B_2, g_\ast B_3}\,\mu_1\wedge \mu_2\wedge \mu_3\ .$$ Theorem [\[th:multi-ergodic\]](#th:multi-ergodic){reference-type="ref" reference="th:multi-ergodic"} tells us that $m_{B_1,B_2,B_3}$ and $m_{g_\ast B_1,g_\ast B_2, g_\ast B_3}$ admit $L^1$-averages $\bar{m}_{B_1,B_2,B_3}$ and $\bar{m}_{g_\ast B_1,g_\ast B_2, g_\ast B_3}$, under actions of $B_i$ and $g_\ast B_i$ respectively.
