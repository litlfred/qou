Let D be a G-BSFC, then there exist a strict G-BSFC D′ with a strict G-action and an equivalence F : D −→D′ of G-crossed braided categories. Thus there is no loss of generality to restrict the study on strict G-BSFCs with strict G-actions, which is the assumption for the rest of the paper. Namely, one can assume that the αX,Y,Z, lX, rX, γg;X,Y , σg, δg,h;X, ϵX are identities.
