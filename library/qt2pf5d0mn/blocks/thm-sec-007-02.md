The partition function ZD(M; T ) is an invariant of closed oriented 4-manifolds. To prove the Main Theorem, we need to show that ZD(M; T ) is independent on: 1. the choice of representatives for each triangle color, 2. the ordering of vertices of a triangulation, 3. the choice of a triangulation. These will be proved in Section 4.1, Section 4.2, and Section 4.4, respectively. Below we derive two other formulas for the partition function, which will be used in the invariance proof.
