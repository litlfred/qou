Abstract

We consider the correlation between the binding energies of the triton and the a-particle which
is empirically observed in calculations employing different phenomenological nucleon-nucleon in-
teractions. Using an effective quantum mechanics approach for short-range interactions with large
scattering length |a| >> , where £ is the natural low-energy length scale, we construct the effective
interaction potential at leading order in ¢/|a|. In order to renormalize the four-nucleon system, it
is sufficient to include a SU(4)-symmetric one-parameter three-nucleon interaction in addition to
the S-wave nucleon-nucleon interactions. The absence of a four-nucleon force at this order explains
the empirically observed correlation between the binding energies of the triton and the a-particle.
We calculate this correlation and obtain a prediction for the a-particle binding energy. Corrections
to our results are suppressed by ¢/|a].
