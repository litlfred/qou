[]{#formalism label="formalism"}

In this section, we introduce the virial expansion for a system of neutrons, protons, and alpha particles. We first discuss our choice of $n$, $p$ and $\alpha$ particle degrees of freedom. Then we present the virial equation of state expanded to second order in the fugacities and calculate the entropy and the energy. Finally, we relate the second virial coefficients to the relevant scattering phase shifts.
