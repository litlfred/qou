Isospin observables in asymmetric heavy-ion collisions,
such as the N/Z ration of emitted fragments, are often
analyzed with semiclassical simulations, where nucleons
move in isospin-dependent mean ﬁelds and undergo two-
nucleon collisions. The isospin-dependent mean-ﬁeld im-
plies a certain symmetry energy. The density depen-
dence of this symmetry energy can be adjusted to im-
prove agreement with the data, e.g., see [45]. This phe-
nomenological symmetry energy at low densities is in gen-
eral much smaller than our virial SE. This is because the
symmetry energy in the virial expansion includes the con-
tributions of clusters with high central densities even at
low baryon density. The relation between SE and the
symmetry energy of mean-ﬁeld models for single nuclei
should therefore be investigated in future work.
