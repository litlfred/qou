The generating function ZG(M; z) has an analytic continuation to C \ eΛ where eΛ is a ﬁnite set containing zero and the exponentials of the negatives of the complex classical Chern-Simons values. In other words, the conjecture is that the generating function ZG(M; z) determines the germ at zero of a resurgent function. This conjecture is formally motivated from resurgence of Laplace integrals and the (non-rigorous) path integral formula for the WRT invariant, as explained in [26].
