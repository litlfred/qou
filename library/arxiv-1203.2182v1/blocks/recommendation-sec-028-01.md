In particular, the Yang-Yang function of the quantum integrable system is identified with the following limit of the instanton partition function in the absence of any surface operators [@NS]: $$\lim_{\epsilon_1 \to 0} \, \epsilon_1 \, \log Z_{\text{BPS}}^{\text{closed}} (\epsilon_1, \epsilon_2) \; = \; \frac{1}{\epsilon_2} {\mathcal{F}}^{(0)} + {\mathcal{F}}^{(1)} + {\cal O }(\epsilon_2) \label{YangYang}$$ This expansion should not be confused with a similar-looking expansion in [\[ZopenW\]](#ZopenW){reference-type="eqref" reference="ZopenW"}, which describes the behavior of the *open* BPS partition function or, from the gauge theory viewpoint, represents the contribution of a surface operator.
