In arbitrary framing such an amplitude reads $$Z_{\text{BPS}}^{\text{open}} \; = \; \sum_R f_R(q_1,q_2)^{-f} C_{\bullet\bullet R}(q_1^{-1},q_2^{-1}) s_R(x) \,.$$ Performing the summation we get $$Z_{\text{BPS}}^{\text{open}} \; = \; \sum_{k=0}^{\infty} (-1)^{(f+1)k} q_2^{\frac{f}{2}k(k-1)} \Big(x \frac{q_1}{\sqrt{q_2}}\Big)^k \prod_{i=1}^k \frac{1}{1 - q_1 q_2^{i-1}} \,. \label{Zopen-BPS-C3}$$ For $f=0$ this result reduces, as it should, to the brane amplitude in ${\mathbb C}^3$ in phase III found in [@Iqbal:2007ii] (after identifying $x=-Q$).
