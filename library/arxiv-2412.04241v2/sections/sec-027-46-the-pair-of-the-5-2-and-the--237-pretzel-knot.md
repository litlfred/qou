The perturbative series associated to the three boundary-parabolic representations of the
(−2, 3, 7)-pretzel knot with values in the abelian number field K = Q(2 cos(2π/7)) give an
element of HOK[1/7]. The corresponding element of the Bloch group is 3-torsion. We have
computed 400 terms of the series at m = 1 and have checked their 7-integrality. For instance,
the coefficient of x400 of this series is 21997 · 3596 · 7466, which improves to 21997 · 7466 upon
taking the third power of the series, illustrating part (b) of Corollary 1.11 of Section 1.7.
