In this section we will describe two beautiful consequences of a formal power series satis-
fying a simple system of linear q-difference equations. On one hand these series will have a

subtle integrality property calledadmissibility — introduced by Kontsevich-Soibelman [36] —
and on the other hand they will satisfy algebraicity. The combination of these two properties
will provide the proofs of our main theorems. We will begin by explaining these results for
the case of the Pochhammer symbol. Then we will describe some generalities on admis-
sible series and an elementary proof that our series are admissible. This will lead us to
discuss the generalisation to level m admissible series, which come from the restriction to
congruence sums of the original admissible series. After that we will give a different formula
for the same objects using formal Gaussian integration and an example of how the WKB
(Wentzel–Kramers–Brillouin) method can be used to prove a similar statement. We will end
this section with proofs of Theorem 3 and Theorem 4 of Section 1.7, which require combining
all of the previous results.
