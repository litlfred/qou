Let F (1) and F (2) be admissible series with the same number of variables t, and L(i) n , V (i)(t), δ(i)(t), and U (i) m (t) (i = 1, 2) be as defined before Lemma 2.3. Then (a) V (1) = V (2) if and only if L(1) n (1) = L(2) n (1) for all n ≥1. (b) F (1) = F (2) if and only if V (1) = V (2), δ(1) = δ(2) and U (1) m = U (2) m for all m ≥1. (c) F (1) = F (2) if and only f (1) m = f (2) m for some (and hence for every) m ≥1.
