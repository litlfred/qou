A.3 Combinatorics at the cusp

To describe the combinatorics of the boundary component(s) of S°\K, we will first
focus on the product region between the pleated surfaces S, and S,. In the layered
triangulation of this product region, each layer A; consists of two tetrahedra, Dj and
D‘, as in Figure 16. It is clear from the figure that each tetrahedron has exactly one
vertex at each puncture of S;, ie at each strand of the 4—string braid between S; and
S.. Since the combinatorics of the four strands are identical, let us focus on a single
puncture of the 4—punctured sphere.

The tetrahedron layer A; intersects the neighborhood of a puncture in two boundary
triangles, one from a truncated vertex of D; and one from D/. These boundary triangles
meet at two vertices that come from shared edges of D; and D/. (This completes a
loop, corresponding to the meridian of a component of K.) The apices of the two
triangles point in different directions, as in Figure 3. As with punctured torus bundles,
these layers of boundary triangles are stacked together, forming fans that correspond to
syllables of the word 2.
