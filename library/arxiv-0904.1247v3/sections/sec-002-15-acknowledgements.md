# Preliminaries on $\infty$-categories {#prelim section}

In this section, we summarize relevant technical foundations for homotopical algebra with $\infty$-categories. It is beyond the scope of this paper to provide a comprehensive discussion of these topics. We will instead focus on highlighting the particular concepts, results and their references that play a role in what follows. Our primary source is [@HA]; for a less condensed overview, one could also consult [@BFN] and [@DGcat].

Throughout this paper, we will work over the complex numbers $\mathbb C$ (or any fixed algebraically closed field of characteristic zero). We will study (pre-triangulated) dg categories by means of the underlying $\mathbb C$-linear stable $\infty$-category. See [@DGcat] for an $\infty$-categorical study of dg categories and [@cohn] for the precise identification of the homotopy theory of pre-triangulated dg categories over a field $k$ with $k$-linear stable $\infty$-categories.
