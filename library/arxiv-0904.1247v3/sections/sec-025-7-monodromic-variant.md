[]{#monodromic section label="monodromic section"} In this section, we extend our previous results to Hecke categories of monodromic ${\mathcal D}$-modules. For simplicity, we will only consider unipotent monodromy, though generalizations to arbitrary monodromies should hold. Most of what we state is purely algebraic and could be presented in far greater generality.
