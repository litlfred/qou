Let $X$ be a stack and $\mathcal A_X\in {\mathcal D}(X)$ a commutative algebra object.

We refer to objects of the category of $\mathcal A_X$-modules $${\mathcal D}_{\mathcal A_X}(X) := \mathop{\mathrm{Mod}}_{\mathcal A_X}({\mathcal D}(X))$$ as monodromic ${\mathcal D}$-modules.

A smooth map $\pi:\tilde X \to X$ provides a natural source of such algebras. The Barr-Beck-Lurie theorem applied to the adjunction $$\xymatrix{ \pi^*: {\mathcal D}(X) \ar[r]<+.5ex> & \ar[l]<+.5ex>  {\mathcal D}(\tilde X) : \pi_*}$$ provides an equivalence of the full subcategory of ${\mathcal D}(\tilde X)$ generated by $*$-pullbacks from ${\mathcal D}(X)$ with modules over the monad $T=\pi_*\pi^*$ acting on ${\mathcal D}(X)$.

By the projection formula, the monad $T$ is represented by the algebra object $$\mathcal A_X = \pi_*\pi^*\omega_X  \in {\mathcal D}(X)$$ Thus one can view ${\mathcal D}_{\mathcal A_X}(X)$ as the ${\mathcal D}$-module affinization of $\tilde X$.

::: example
If $\pi:\tilde X\to X$ is a torsor for a torus $H$, then a monodromic ${\mathcal D}$-module on $X$ in the above sense is equivalent to a traditional $H$-monodromic ${\mathcal D}$-module on $\tilde X$ with unipotent monodromy around $H$.
:::

Recall by Proposition [\[BG semirigid\]](#BG semirigid){reference-type="ref" reference="BG semirigid"} that if $X$ has smooth diagonal, then the monoidal category ${\mathcal D}(X)$ is semi-rigid with a canonical pivotal structure. Since the algebra $\mathcal A_X$ is commutative, the category ${\mathcal D}_{\mathcal A_X}(X)$ is naturally symmetric monoidal with unit $\mathcal A_X$. We have the following algebraic observation extending Proposition [\[BG semirigid\]](#BG semirigid){reference-type="ref" reference="BG semirigid"}.

::: prop
For a stack $X$ with smooth diagonal, the monoidal category ${\mathcal D}_{\mathcal A_X}(X)$ is semi-rigid with a canonical pivotal structure.
:::

::: proof
*Proof.* For compact objects $\mathfrak M\in {\mathcal D}(X)$, objects of the form $\mathcal A_X \otimes \mathfrak M\in {\mathcal D}_{\mathcal A_X}(X)$ are compact and generate. Recall that $\mathfrak M^\vee = \mathbb D_X(\mathfrak M)[2\dim X]\in {\mathcal D}(X)$ is the monoidal dual of $\mathfrak M\in {\mathcal D}(X)$. It is simple to check that $\mathcal A_X\otimes \mathfrak M^\vee\in {\mathcal D}_{\mathcal A_X}(X)$ is the monoidal dual of $\mathcal A_X \otimes \mathfrak M\in {\mathcal D}_{\mathcal A_X}(X)$. ◻
:::
