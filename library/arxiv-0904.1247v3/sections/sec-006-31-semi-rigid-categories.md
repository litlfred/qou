Deﬁnition 3.2. A monoidal category A∈Alg(StC) is said to be rigid if
• The multiplication map µ :A⊗A→A is quasi-proper.
• The natural lax Ae-linearity of the right adjoint µr is strict.
• The unit 1A∈A is compact.
