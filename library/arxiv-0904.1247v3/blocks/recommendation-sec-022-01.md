We must construct a morphism of $\infty$-local systems $$\xymatrix{ \tau:\int_{\mathbb S} \mathcal H\ar[r] &\mathbb{V}\hspace{-0.25em}\operatorname{ect}_\mathbb C }$$ Moreover, for a given framed circle $S$ and pair of disjoint open intervals $I_1 \coprod I_2 \subset S$ (hence any framed circle with any pair of disjoint open intervals), the natural composite map $$\xymatrix{ \mathcal H\otimes \mathcal H\simeq \mathcal H^{\pi_0(I_1\coprod I_2)} \ar[r] & \int_{S} \mathcal H\ar[r]^-{\tau_S} &\mathbb{V}\hspace{-0.25em}\operatorname{ect}_\mathbb C }$$ must be the evaluation pairing of a self-duality of $\mathcal H\in St_\mathbb C$.
