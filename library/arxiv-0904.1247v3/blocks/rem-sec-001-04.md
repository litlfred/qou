Perhaps the most interesting aspect of the identiﬁcation of ChG with the monoidal trace of HG is the consequence that every dualizable Hecke module M has a character χM ∈ChG. In other words, character sheaves arise as characters of such Hecke modules. To illustrate this point, consider a spherical subgroup K ⊂G (for example, K a parabolic subgroup, or the ﬁxed points of an involution). Harish Chandra (g, K)-modules with trivial inﬁnitesimal character are equivalent to K-equivariant D-modules on G/B.
