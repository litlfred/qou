The identiﬁcations of the theorem endow ChG with rich algebraic structures. As a monoidal center, ChG is naturally an E2-algebra. As a monoidal trace, ChG comes equipped with an S1-action. In fact, the S1-action is compatible with the loop rotation action on all D-modules on the loop space G/ad G = L(BG).
