Fix α > 0. There exists a (unique) family of functions J(α) λ such that the following three conditions are fulﬁlled: TR The matrix of J(α) λ written in the monomial basis is upper triangular. OR The J(α) λ form an orthogonal basis of the ring of symmetric functions endowed with the scalar product ⟨·, ·⟩α. N The coefﬁcient of p|λ| in the power sum expansion of J(α) λ is equal to 1. These functions are called Jack symmetric functions. When α = 1 they correspond, up to multiplication by a scalar, to Schur functions.
