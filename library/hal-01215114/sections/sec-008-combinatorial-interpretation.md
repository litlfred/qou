The triplet of pair-partitions (S(T ),S 1,S 2) can be seen as a map (bipartite graph drawn on a — possibly
non-orientable — two-dimensional, borderless surface), see [GJ96]. The statistics considered in Theorem
3, Theorem 9, Theorem 10 and Theorem 11 are natural in this context: L(S(T ),S 1) andL(S(T ),S 2)
correspond respectively to white and black vertices, whileL(S1,S 2) corresponds to the faces.
Therefore, our results can be rephrased in a natural way using graphs on surfaces. For more details and
precise statements see the complete version of this paper [F´S11b].
