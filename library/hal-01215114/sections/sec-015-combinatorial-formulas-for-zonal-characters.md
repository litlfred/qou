Combinatorial formulas for zonal characters
In this section we give new formulas for zonal characters (the latter are deﬁned in Section 3.2), which are
analogues of some recent formulas for characters of the symmetric groups and establish some particular
cases of some conjectures of Lassalle. These formulas can be derived relatively easily from our main
result, as done in the complete version of this paper [F´S11b].
