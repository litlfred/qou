Normalization
In this paragraph we prove that the functions Yλ satisfy property N.
Let λ be a partition of n. We ﬁx a bijective ﬁlling T of 2λ. We have to ﬁnd the T-admissible couples
(S1, S2) with µ(L(S1, S2)) = 1n. The last condition is equivalent to S1 = S2 and a couple (S1, S1) is
T-admissible if and only if S1 = S(T). In this case, (−1)(S(T ),S1) = 1. Therefore, [p1n]Yλ = 1 and
Theorem 3 is proved.
