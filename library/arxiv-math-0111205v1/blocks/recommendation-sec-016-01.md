In order to show that $T$ is a braided tensor functor we must prove that the diagram \[ \begin{diagram} T(X\boxtimes Y)\otimes T(Z\boxtimes W) & \rTo^{c_{\2C}} & T(Z\boxtimes W)\otimes T(X\boxtimes Y) \\ \dTo^{F_2} && \dTo_{F_2} \\ T(X\boxtimes Y\otimes Z\boxtimes W) & \rTo_{T(c_{\2K\boxtimes\2L})} & T(Z\boxtimes W\otimes X\boxtimes Y) \end{diagram} \] commutes, where $c_{\2K\boxtimes_\7F\2L}=c_\2K\boxtimes c_\2L$ is the direct product braiding.
