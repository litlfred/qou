[]{#sec:organize label="sec:organize"}

In Section [\[sec:deform-cone\]](#sec:deform-cone){reference-type="ref" reference="sec:deform-cone"}, we review background information on cone deformations.

In Section [\[sec:cusp-est\]](#sec:cusp-est){reference-type="ref" reference="sec:cusp-est"}, we begin the proofs of Theorems [\[thm:main-cusp\]](#thm:main-cusp){reference-type="ref" reference="thm:main-cusp"}, [\[thm:main-norm-length\]](#thm:main-norm-length){reference-type="ref" reference="thm:main-norm-length"}, and [\[thm:main-norm-height\]](#thm:main-norm-height){reference-type="ref" reference="thm:main-norm-height"} using information set up in Section [\[sec:deform-cone\]](#sec:deform-cone){reference-type="ref" reference="sec:deform-cone"}. This leads us to results which contain certain boundary terms.

In Section [\[sec:bdrybound\]](#sec:bdrybound){reference-type="ref" reference="sec:bdrybound"}, we give bounds on these boundary terms, which allow us to conclude the proof of Theorems [\[thm:main-cusp\]](#thm:main-cusp){reference-type="ref" reference="thm:main-cusp"} and [\[thm:main-norm-length\]](#thm:main-norm-length){reference-type="ref" reference="thm:main-norm-length"}.

We complete the proof of [\[thm:main-norm-height\]](#thm:main-norm-height){reference-type="ref" reference="thm:main-norm-height"} in Section [\[sec:merbound\]](#sec:merbound){reference-type="ref" reference="sec:merbound"}.

Finally, in Section [\[sec:knots\]](#sec:knots){reference-type="ref" reference="sec:knots"} we apply the results of the previous sections to knots, and present the proof of Theorem [\[thm:main-knot-cusp\]](#thm:main-knot-cusp){reference-type="ref" reference="thm:main-knot-cusp"}.
