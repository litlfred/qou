Let K be a knot in S3 which admits a prime, twist reduced diagram with a total of n ≥2 twist regions, with each twist region containing at least c ≥116 crossings. In a hyperbolic structure on S3 −K, take a horoball neighborhood U about K. Normalize so that the meridian on ∂U has length 1. Then the height H of the cusp of S3 −K satisﬁes: H ≥n (1 −f(c))2. Here f(c) is a positive function of c which approaches 0 as c increases to inﬁnity. Additionally, H ≤n(√n −1 + f(c))2.
