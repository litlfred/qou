The q-Fourier transform of q-distributions.
M.A.Olshanetsky
ITEP, 117259, Moscow
e-mail olshanet@heron.itep.ru
V.-B.K.Rogov
MIIT, 101475, Moscow

Abstract
We consider functions on the lattice generated by the integer powers of q2 for
0 < q < 1 and construct the q-analog of Fourier transform based on the Jackson
integral in the space of distributions on the lattice.
