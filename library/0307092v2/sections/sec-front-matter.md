Abstract
We deﬁne an extended Bloch group and show it is naturally isomorphic to
H3(PSL(2, C)δ; Z). Using the Rogers dilogarithm function this leads to an ex-
act simplicial formula for the universal Cheeger–Chern–Simons class on this
homology group. It also leads to an independent proof of the analytic relation-
ship between volume and Chern–Simons invariant of hyperbolic 3–manifolds
conjectured in [16] and proved in [24], as well as eﬀective formulae for the
Chern–Simons invariant of a hyperbolic 3–manifold.
