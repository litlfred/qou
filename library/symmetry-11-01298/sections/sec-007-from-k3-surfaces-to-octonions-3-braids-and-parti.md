The results of the previous section illustrated the power of the approach and its relation to particle
physics. In this section, we will discuss the topological reasons and the relation to the models of Furey,
Gresnigt and Bilson–Thompson. In these models, 3-braids, octonions and quaternions play a key role.
Therefore, we have to understand how these structures will naturally appear in the K3 surface.
