KYUNGPOOK Math. J. 48(2008), 255-280
A Formula for the Colored Jones Polynomial of 2-Bridge Knots
Toshie Takata
Department of Mathematics, Faculty of Science, Niigata University, Niigata 950-
2181, Japan
e-mail : takata@math.sc.niigata-u.ac.jp
Abstract. We derive a formula for the colored Jones polynomial of 2-bridge knots. For
a twist knot, a more explicit formula is given and it leads to a relation between the degree
of the colored Jones polynomial and the crossing number.
