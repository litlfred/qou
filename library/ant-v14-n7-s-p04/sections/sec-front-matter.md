Elliptic curves over totally real cubic ﬁelds are modular
Maarten Derickx, Filip Najman and Samir Siksek
We prove that all elliptic curves deﬁned over totally real cubic ﬁelds are modular. This builds on previous
work of Freitas, Le Hung and Siksek, who proved modularity of elliptic curves over real quadratic ﬁelds,
as well as recent breakthroughs due to Thorne and to Kalyanswamy.
