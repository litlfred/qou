Abstract

This work begins with a review of complexification and realification of Hopf algebras.
We emphasize the notion of multiplier Hopf algebras for the description of different classes
of functions (compact supported, bounded, unbounded) on complex quantum groups and
the construction of the associated left and right Haar measure. Using a continuation of 67
symbols of SU, (2) with complex spins, we give a new description of the unitary represen-
tations of SL,(2,C)p and find explicit expressions for the characters of SL,(2,C)r. The
major theorem of this article is the Plancherel theorem for the Quantum Lorentz Group.
