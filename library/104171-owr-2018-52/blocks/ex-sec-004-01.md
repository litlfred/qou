(i) Let k be a Lie algebra and ı : k →A a Lie algebra map into an associative algebra A, i.e., a linear map such that ı([x, y]) = ı(x)ı(y) − ı(y)ı(x), ∀x, y ∈k. We let I = Ak be a left ideal of A generated by the image of ı. In this case, we have N(I)/I = (A/Ak)k, the centralizer of ı(k) in A/Ak. Similarly, one can consider a right ideal, kA, generated by the image of ı and the corresponding algebra (A/kA)k. (ii) Let A1, A2, Z, be a triple of associative rings and ιi : Z →Ai, i = 1, 2, a pair of ring homomorphisms. Let I be a right ideal of Aop 1 ⊗A2 generated by the elements ι1(z) ⊗1 −1 ⊗ι2(z), z ∈Z.
