[HW18, Theorem 6.2] Gλ = SL(Vλ) if L(λ) is not (SD). If L(λ) is (SD) and Vλ|Gλ′ is irreducible, Gλ = SO(Vλ) respectively Gλ = Sp(Vλ) according to whether L(λ) is orthogonal or symplectic selfdual. If L(λ) is (SD) and Vλ|Gλ′ decomposes into at least two irreducibe representations, then Gλ ∼= SL(W) for Vλ|Gλ′ ∼= W ⊕W ∨. We conjecture that the last case in the theorem doesn’t happen. The ambiguity in the determination of Gλ is only due to the fact that we cannot exclude special elements with 2-torsion in π0(Hn|n).
