The block Dmod(g)G Spr corresponding to the unique cuspidal datum (T, E) for a maximal torus T is known as the Springer block. The theorem in particular gives an equivalence Dmod(g)G Spr ≃Dmod(t)W = Dt#W −mod where W is the Weyl group of G (with respect to T ). In the case G = GLn (or PGLn) the Springer block is the entire category. Thus there is an equivalence Dmod(gln)GLn ≃Dmod(An)Sn ≃DAn#Sn −mod
