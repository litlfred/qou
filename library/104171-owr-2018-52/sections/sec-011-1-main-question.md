Let g be a simple complex ﬁnite-dimensional Lie algebra and Lg = g⊗ C[t±1] its
loop algebra. Drinfeld and Jimbo associated to each complex number q∈ C∗ a
Hopf algebraUq(Lg) called a quantum group. Its representation theory is impor-
tant, in particular from the point of view of quantum integrable syst ems. Though
it has been intensively studied from several geometric, algebraic, c ombinatorial
perspectives, some basic questions are still open, such as the dime nsion and char-
acter of simple ﬁnite-dimensional modules.
Inspired by the general framework of monoidal categoriﬁcation o f cluster alge-
bras [HL1], we get new results in this direction.
