Suppose that τ is δ-wobbly. Let F0 = T (λ)y0, F1 = T (λ)y1 be the ﬁbres of T (λ) →Sn at points y0, y1, whose distance in the standard metric is dist(y0, y1) ≥2πδ. Then τ(F0) intersects F1 transversally and at a single point y, which satisﬁes 2πR′(||y||) = dist(y0, y1). In the special case where y1 = A(y0) one has y = y1; and then the tangent space of T (λ) at y can be identiﬁed symplectically with Cn in such a way that the subspaces tangent to τ(F0), T (0), F1 become respectively, Rn, e2πi/3Rn, and eπi/3Rn.
