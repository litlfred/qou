Take three R-graded vector spaces C′, C, C′′, each of them with a diﬀerential of order (0; ∞). Suppose that we have diﬀerential maps b : C′ →C, c : C →C′′ and a homotopy h : C′ →C′′ between c ◦b and the zero map, such that that the following conditions are satisﬁed for some ǫ > 0: (i) C′, C′′ have gap (0; 3ǫ), and C has gap (0; 2ǫ).
