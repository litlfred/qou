An *exact Lefschetz fibration*[^1] over $S$ consists of data $(E,\pi,\Omega,\Theta,J_0,j_0)$ as follows. $E$ is a $(2n+2)$-dimensional manifold whose boundary is the union of two faces $\partial_hE$ and $\partial_vE$, meeting at a codimension two corner. $\pi: E \rightarrow S$ is a proper map with $\partial_vE = \pi^{-1}(\partial S)$ (so this may be empty), and such that both $\pi|\partial_hE: \partial_hE \rightarrow S$ and $\pi|\partial_vE: \partial_vE \rightarrow \partial S$ are smooth fibre bundles. $\pi$ can have at most finitely many critical points, and no two may lie on the same fibre (moreover, because of the previous assumptions, they must lie in the interior of $E$).
