Let (E, π), Q and κ be as in Proposition 2.28. Consider two complex structures jk on S, k = 0, 1, and correspondingly two almost complex structures Jk ∈Jreg,h(E, π, Q, j, {Je}); note that the Je are supposed to be the same for both k. Then the maps CΦ0 rel(E, π, Q, Jk) are homotopic by a chain homotopy which is of order (−κ; ∞). It is a familiar idea that when dealing with maps between R-graded vector spaces, the “lowest order term” is usually the most important, and knowing it is often suﬃcient to resolve a question. A particular instance of this is relevant for our purpose.
