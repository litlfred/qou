Wrapping it up
Technically, the proof of the exact sequence is an application of Lemma 2.32.
The spaces C′, C, C′′ which occur in the lemma will be Floer cochain spaces,
and the maps b, c relative invariants. While the deﬁnition of b is rather straight-
forward, that of c uses some of the geometry from Chapter 1, speciﬁcally the
standard ﬁbrations constructed in Section 1.2. In both cases, the desired prop-
erties follow from nonnegative curvature, that is to say Proposition 2.28 and
related results. The homotopy h is obtained by comparing two diﬀerent con-
structions of a particular exact Lefschetz ﬁbration. Nonnegative curvature again
plays a role in analyzing it, but the vanishing theorem of Section 2.3 is also im-
portant.
