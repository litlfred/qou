Of course, perturbation theory means studying formal power se ries that rarely converge. Perturbation
theory at the trivial ﬂat connection in a knot complement converge s, as it resums to a Laurent polynomial
in eh; namely the nth colored Jones polynomial. The volume conjecture for small comple x angles is precisely
the statement that perturbation theory for abelian ﬂat connect ions (near the trivial one) does converge.
At the moment, there is no physics (or otherwise) formulation of pe rturbation theory of the Chern-Simons
