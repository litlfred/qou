33. From AW (g, q, ℓ) to a compatible unitary coboundary wqh structure on
A(Vgk)
In this section we are interested in the module categories of affine vertex operator algebras Vgk
at positive integer levels k. This is an important class of vertex operator algebras
associated to affine Lie algebras. Every vertex operator algebra V has an associated associative algebra, called the Zhu algebra A(V ) [197] recalled in Sect. 22. We shall briefly
recall a natural identification of the Zhu algebra in the case of affine VOAs.
The aim of this section is to apply the Drinfeld-Kohno theorem 30.4 and then transport
all the untwisted unitary coboundary structure from AW (g, q0, ℓ) to the Zhu algebra A(Vgk)
via a beautiful continuous path argument that has been discovered by Wenzl in [185]. Using
the methods of Sect. 15, we derive in this way a unitary modular tensor category structure
on Rep(Vgk). In the second part of the section, we describe this structure of A(Vgk) in more
detail.
Summarizing, in this section, we prove part (a) and (b) of the following main Theorem
2.8. Part (c) constitutes a statement about our analogue of Kazhdan-Lusztig-Finkelberg
theorem, Theorem 2.11, in the setting of affine vertex operator algebras with HuangLepowsky ribbon braided tensor category structure, and will be reformulated more precisely in the following section, see Theorem 2.11, where we shall give a first idea of proof
connecting with work by Kirillov. In Sect. 34 we shall study the comparison between the
