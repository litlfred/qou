UNITARY COBOUNDARY WEAK QUASI-HOPF ALGEBRAS IN THE
KAZHDAN-LUSZTIG-FINKELBERG THEOREM
SERGIO CIAMPRONE, MARCO VALERIO GIANNONE, AND CLAUDIA PINZARI
Dedicated to the memory of Sergio Doplicher and John E. Roberts.
Abstract. We construct, for every complex simple Lie algebra g and every positive
integer level k, a unitary modular tensor category structure on the module category of
the affine vertex operator algebra Vgk, equivalent to the quantum group fusion category
C(g, q, ℓ), within the Doplicher–Roberts quantum group duality program for the Wess–
Zumino–Witten model. The construction reaches positive integer levels that Finkelberg’s
argument does not cover, namely E6 and E7 at k = 1 and E8 at k = 2, and it treats simplylaced and non-simply-laced types uniformly, whereas the Kazhdan–Lusztig construction
was carried out for the simply-laced case.
We establish that a weak quasi-fibre structure on a functor preserving a coboundary
symmetry in the sense of Drinfeld extends a symmetric functor under permutation symmetry. Using Wenzl’s functor associated with the unitary quantum group fusion category,
we construct such a structure compatible with the associativity morphisms, yielding unitary coboundary weak Hopf C
∗
-algebras for all Lie types and positive integer levels, a
new class axiomatically introduced here. Via a specialized Drinfeld twist for our algebras
and the Wenzl de-quantization curve, this structure is transported onto the Zhu algebra,
which becomes a unitary coboundary weak quasi-Hopf C
∗
-algebra with a 3-coboundary
associator. Rigidity is obtained from the quantum group side, without the Knizhnik–
Zamolodchikov equations or the Verlinde formula.
We further analyze the type A case via classification methods based on Kazhdan–Wenzl
theory and our weak Hopf algebra framework, which gives insight into the determination
of associativity from the braiding in the general case. Our methods settle several further
problems: Galindo’s question on the uniqueness of unitary tensor structures, Kirillov’s
conjecture on the positivity of a Hermitian form on the braided tensor category of modules
of an affine Lie algebra introduced by Beilinson, Feigin and Mazur, the quantum group
structure on the Zhu algebra sought by Frenkel and Zhu, and the Kazhdan–Lusztig–
Finkelberg equivalence for all Lie types and all positive integer levels without passing
through the negative shifted levels, as raised by Huang. We also compare our braided
tensor structure with that of Huang and Lepowsky.
