(New grading notation for modes of module maps) Let a ∈ V be a homogeneous element of conformal weight (or degree) d ∈ Z, i.e. such that L0a = da. For every V -module M then a M n is defined by a M n := a M (n−1), n ∈ Z. (23.1) For a general a ∈ V aM n is defined by linearity. With respect to the new notation (23.1) and to the new gradation for the homogeneous subspaces of a module defined in (22.13), the grading relation (22.11) becomes a M n : M(r) → M(r+d−n)for r ∈ R, n ∈ Z. (23.2) Thus after a first increase by d = deg(a), energy further decreases by −n for n > 0 and increases by −n for n < 0.
