Let C1 and C2 be tensor equivalent C ∗ -tensor categories endowed with an integral weak dimension function (e.g. they are finite semisimple tensor categories). Then C1 and C2 are also unitarily tensor equivalent. These theorems are interesting especially taking into consideration the fact that the abstract theorem 15.8 on which they rely uses only the choice of an integral weak dimension function on the affine Lie algebra fusion category. This category naturally admits such a dimension function given by representations of the simple Lie algebra g and no inner structure is needed in detail, other than the two cited milestone theorems.
