Let (C, ⊗, ι) be a semisimple pre-tensor category with a generating object V and admitting a faithful weak quasi-tensor functor (F, F, G) : C → Vec into the category of finite dimensional vector spaces. Let c(ρ, σ) : ρ ⊗ σ → σ ⊗ ρ be a normalized invertible natural transformation and let V satisfy the duality property with respect to c. Let α and β be two associativity morphisms for (C, ⊗, ι) such that (C, ⊗, ι, α, c) and (C, ⊗, ι, β, c) are braided tensor categories. Let V be defined as in (8.4). Assume that α = β on V.
