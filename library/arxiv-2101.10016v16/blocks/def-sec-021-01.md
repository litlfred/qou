A vertex operator algebra is a Z-graded vector space V V = M n∈Z V(n), with finite dimensional homogeneous spaces V(n) and V(n) = 0 for n sufficiently small, together with a triple (Y, 1, ν), where Y is the state-field correspondence, a linear map Y : V → End(V )[[x, x−1 ]], a → Y (a, x) = X n∈Z a(n)x −n−1 , with x is a formal variable. The element Y (a, x) is called the vertex operator associated with a. The element 1 lies in V(0) and is called the vacuum, and ν ∈ V(2) is called the Virasoro vector.
