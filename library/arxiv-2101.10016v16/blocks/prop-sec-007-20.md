Let (C, ⊗, ι) be a semisimple pre-tensor category with an object V . Let α and β be associativity morphisms making (C, ⊗, ι) into a tensor category such that α = β on V. Then the following properties are equivalent: (a) α = β in addition on triples of the form {(Vλ, V 2, V ),(V, V 2, Vλ), Vλ ∈ Irr(C)}, (b) extremal associativity morphisms satisfy α ′ = β′ on V′ , (c) extremal associativity morphisms satisfy α ′ = β′ on V′′ .
