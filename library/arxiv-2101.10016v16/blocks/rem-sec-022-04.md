By Theorem 2.11, the category C(g, q, ℓ) is tensor equivalent to Rep(Vgk ) for the classical Lie types and G2. The weak dimension functions for the functors FVgkand F(g,q) regarded as defined on the same family of irreducible objects, have the same range in Z≥0. it follows that A(Vgk ) and A(g, q, ℓ) are, up to a twist, isomorphic weak quasi-Hopf algebras, cf. the discussion after Theorem 7.9. (By the main result of [25], A(slN , q, ℓ) admits a natural structure of weak Hopf algebra. We shall extend this result to the other Lie types later on, and it will be useful) Here we have followed the original ideas of [125], [82].
