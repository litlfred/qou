An associator on an arbitrary triple is uniquely determined by its values on the family V of triples (Vλ, V, V ), (V, Vλ, V ), (V, V, Vλ) under some extra assumptions. This claim is stated and proved in Theorem 8.21. The claim implies that any other associator on A(Vgk ) that restricts to a CFT-type pre-associator associated to the same pair (F0, G0), it must coincide with the associator of A(Vgk ) constructed in Theorem 2.8 (a) on every triple of representations. We shall find in Theorem 37.24 that also Huang-Lepowsky associativity morphisms restrict to the same CFT-type pre-associator ΦF0.G0 on V.
