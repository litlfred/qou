In a tensor category with right duality, let c be a generalised coboundary satisfying (27.13) (e.g. a braided symmetry) with balancing structure v, and let w ∈ (1, 1) be a natural isomorphism compatible with duality such that w 2 = v. Then cw is a coboundary, c w 2 = 1 ⊗ 1, v w = 1, and uw is a spherical structure coinciding with that defined by c and v as in Prop. 27.18. The construction of c w is the analogue of Drinfeld construction of unitarized R-matrix in a ribbon Hopf algebra. In Sect. 28 we shall study the relation with Ω-involution.
