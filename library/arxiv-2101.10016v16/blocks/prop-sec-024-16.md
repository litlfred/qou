Let q and q ′ ∈ C be either not non-trivial roots of unity or else square to primitive roots of unity of order ℓ > N + 1 and let us endow both C(slN , q, ℓ) and C(slN , q′, ℓ) with some braided symmetry. If there is an isomorphism of based rings f : Gr(C(slN , q, ℓ)) → Gr(C(slN , q′, ℓ)) identifying the generating representations and compatible with the canonical ribbon structures then there is a ribbon tensor equivalence F : C(slN , q, ℓ) → C(slN , q′, ℓ) inducing f, q and q ′ are related as in Prop. 25.5, and we may arrange q = q ′ .
