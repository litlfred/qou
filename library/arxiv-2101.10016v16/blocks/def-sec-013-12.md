The element h is called the Haar element. The following lemma extends a known idea in the framework of coassociative quantum groups which, to our knowledge, dates back to [190]. Here we consider a modification due to non-triviality of the associator, where the need of the special form that the associator takes for weak Hopf algebras is apparent. We are not aware of validity of an analogous lemma in a general quasi-coassociative framework.
