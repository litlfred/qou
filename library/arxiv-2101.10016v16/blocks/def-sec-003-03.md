Let C be a semisimple pre-tensor category. An object ρ ∈ C is called generating if every simple object of C is a summand of some tensor power of ρ defined by some parenthesisation. A tensor power of ρ is an object defined as the result of iterative applications of the bifunctor ⊗ to pairs with entries ι or ρ. We define the order of a tensor power of ρ in a natural way. Different parenthesisations of the same order of two tensor powers of ρ are equivalent in a tensor category, thus it suffices to verify the generating property on a preferred choice of parenthesisation in this case, and is independent of the choice of the associativity morphism
