We consider the bialgebra Cω(G) of complex valued functions of a finite group G with the usual coproduct and associator given by a T-valued 3-cocycle ω, see Ex. 7.12. A natural unitary structure is given by the C ∗ -structure of Cω(G) and Ω = I. More generally, a general Ω-involution for Cω(G) over the same C ∗ -algebra is given by a normalized 2-cocycle Ω(g, h) with values in R ×, that is a function satisfying Ω(1, g) = Ω(g, 1) = 1 and Ω(g, h)Ω(gh, k) = Ω(h, k)Ω(g, hk) for all g, h, k ∈ G.
