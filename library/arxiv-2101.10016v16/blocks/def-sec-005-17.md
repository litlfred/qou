An algebra A is called discrete if it is isomorphic to a direct sum of full matrix algebras. A discrete weak quasi bialgebra (Hopf algebra) is a discrete algebra endowed with coproduct, counit and associator where the axioms of a weak quasi bialgebra are modified as follows.
