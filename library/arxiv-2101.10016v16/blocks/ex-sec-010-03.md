The Hopf algebras Uq(g) for |q| = 1 considered by Wenzl in [185] are for us important examples of Ω-involutive Hopf algebras with a non-trivial selfadjoint 2- cocycle Ω in the sense of part b) of the previous remark. We shall discuss these examples in Section 32. In this case, Ω is canonically induced by the R-matrix1. Furthermore, in Sections 28, 30, 32 we shall construct new examples of semisimple Ω-involutive or unitary weak Hopf algebras associated to Uq(g) for q a suitable root of unity, corresponding to the associated unitary fusion categories. We next extend the 2-cocycle property of Ω from bialgebras to weak bialgebras.
