Let A be a discrete Hermitian coboundary wqh with compatible ∗-involution and let Ω by a trivial involution with respect to a tensor product representation ρ ⊗ σ. Thus we have on the space of ρ ⊗ σ, Ω = ∆(I) ∗∆(I) = R, R = ∆(I) ∗w ⊗ w∆(w−1 ). (30.1) When the Ω-involution is in addition strongly trivial with respect to ρ⊗σ (recall that this is automatic when A is discrete unitary by Prop. 4.21) then in the representation space of ρ ⊗ σ, Ω = ∆(I) = R, R = w ⊗ w∆(w −1 ). (30.2) In particular, if this holds for any pair of simple representations ρ, σ then A has a cocommutative coproduct (∆ = ∆(op)) by centrality of w.
