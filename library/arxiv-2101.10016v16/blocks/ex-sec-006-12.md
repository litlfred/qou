Let G be a finite group. Consider the category C = VecG of finite dimensional G-graded vector spaces with tensor product defined by convolution and trivial associativity morphisms. The representation ring is ZG. The constant function D = 1 is a dimension function, giving rise to the commutative bialgebra C(G) of complex functions f on G with usual coproduct ∆(f)(g, h) = f(gh). Prop. 7.11 reduces to the known classification of tensor categories with this representation ring.
