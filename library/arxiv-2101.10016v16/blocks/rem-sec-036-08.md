Associativity of intertwining operators is referred the (Non-meromorphic) Operator Product Expansion (OPE) of chiral vertex operators, in the works by Huang and Lepowsky. Note that in physics literature one often meets OPE for primary fields. Since primary fields are of main interest for our purposes, to avoid possible confusion, we shall refer to Huang-Lepowsky associativity as OPE of intertwining operators. See also Def. 37.12 for a reformulation in terms of irreducible modules.
