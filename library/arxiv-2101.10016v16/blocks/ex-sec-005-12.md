It follows from Cor. 6.11 that A = Funω(G) has a strong antipode if and only if ω(g, g−1, g) = 1 for all g. For example, when G = ZN , each complex N-th root of unity w induces the 3-cocycle ωw(a, b, c) = w γ(a,b)c , with γ(a, b) = ⌊ a+b N ⌋ − ⌊ a N ⌋ − ⌊ b N ⌋, where ⌊λ⌋ is the greatest integer not exceeding λ. Furthermore this association gives an isomorphism of the group of N-th roots of unity with H3(ZN , T). If h is the natural generator of ZN , ω(h, h−1, h) = w.
