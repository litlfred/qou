Let B be a algebra, p an idempotent in B, f : A → B a homomorphism and g : A → B an anti-homomorphism with f(I) = g(I) = p, and ρ, σ ∈ pBp such that: g(a(1))ρf(a(2)) = ε(a)ρ, f(a(1))σg(a(2)) = ε(a)σ (6.23) where a ∈ A. Moreover, f(x)σg(y)ρf(z) = p, g(p)ρf(q)σg(r) = p (6.24) In addition, we have an idempotent q ∈ B, ρ, σ ∈ qBp and an anti-homomorphism g : A → B with g(I) = q also satisfying (6.23) - (6.24) (in (6.24) q replaces p). Then there exists a unique partially invertible element F ∈ B with D(F) = p, R(F) = q, such that F ρ = ρ, σF = σ (6.25)
