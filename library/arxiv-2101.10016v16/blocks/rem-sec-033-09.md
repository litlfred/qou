Note that at this point of the paper, the notation V seems confusing, in that it both denotes the fundamental representation of a complex Lie algebra g as in [185] and a general vertex operator algebra as in Sect. 22. When the two notions are used simultaneously, we shall refrain from using V for a vertex operator algebra. We hope that consistent use of the notation Vgkfor the affine vertex operator algebra at level k associated to g, clarifies the possible confusion.
