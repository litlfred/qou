(closely related results in the literature) We recall that a characterization of braided slN,∞-type categories has been made explicit Neshveyev and Yamashita in [140], see also the thesis by Jordans [101], and a classification of the braided symmetries may be found in [148]. In [140] the authors settle down the problem of reconstructing the twisted categories obtained from Kazhdan-Wenzl classification, see Theorem 25.4, as representation categories of quantum groups of their own, for q a positive real number.
