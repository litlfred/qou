If V = Vgk , under Frenkel-Zhu correspondence of affine Lie algebras and affine vertex operator algebras, the two notions of primary field given in Sect. 34 and Subsect. 38.1 coincide. In this correspondence ϕ becomes Yr and the initial term Tϕ becomes Y0. Thus Y0 determines Yr and Y. The space of initial terms Y0 has been characterized in Theorem 2.3 in [170] for sl2 and Theorem 3.2.3 in [61] for a general g, and described in terms of a universal tensor product property of modules of A(Vgk) in Theorem 34.7. Moreover, the maps Y → Yr, Yr → Y0 are linear isomorphisms.
