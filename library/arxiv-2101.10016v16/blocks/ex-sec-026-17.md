Consider the tensor category C = VecG of finite dimensional G-graded vector spaces over a finite abelian group G, with tensor product defined in the standard way, for V = (Vg) and W = (Wh), (V ⊗ W)k = ⊕gh=kVg ⊗ Vh, and natural associator, see [50]. Then every group element g defines a 1-dimensional space δg of grade g and these are all the irreducible objects up to equivalence. We have that δ −1 g is both a right and left dual of δg and duality equations are solved by the identity maps.
