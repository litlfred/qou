Let g be a complex simple Lie algebra, q a complex root of unity such that q 2 has order ℓ in the sense of Def. 19.1. Then a) (G˜ q, ⊠, α) is a semisimple tensor category, b) the canonical linear equivalence E : G˜ q → Gq admits a unique structure of tensor equivalence (E, E) : (G˜ q, ⊠, α) → (Gq, ⊗, 1) such that Eλ,µ : E(Vλ)⊗E(Vµ) → E(Vλ ⊠ Vµ), λ, µ ∈ Λ +(q) acts as Fλ,µ and we have that E −1 λ,µ acts as Gλ,µ, c) the pair (F, G) is a weak tensor structure for the forgetful functor W : G˜ q → Vec, therefore AW (g, q, ℓ) = Nat0(W) is a ribbon weak Hopf algebra, d) a different choice of pn, pλ changes AW (g, q, ℓ) by a trivial twist.
