Note that we do not have a statement about unitarization of a weak tensor ∗-functor. Specifically, a unitary weak tensor ∗-functor to the category of Hilbert spaces is automatically tensor for large classes of semisimple unitary tensor tensor categories and the category necessarily has an integer-valued dimension function, we refer to Corollary 16.8 for details.
