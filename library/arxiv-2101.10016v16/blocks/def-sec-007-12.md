Let (C, ⊗, ι, c) be a pre-tensor category with invertible natural transformation c(ρ, σ) : ρ⊗σ → σ⊗ρ. Let V ∈ C be an object satisfying the duality property with respect to c. Let α be associativity morphisms in C making (C, ⊗, ι, α, c) into a braided tensor category. We shall say that V satisfies the braid group duality property. Two tensor powers of V of the same order of the kind described in Examples 8.10 will be referred to as in the same orbit under the action of the braid group.
