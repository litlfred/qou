The forgetful functor F : Rep(A) → Vec of a weak quasi-bialgebra A is weak quasi-tensor with Fρ,σ = ∆(I) and Gρ,σ the inclusion map. We give a categorical interpretation of the notion of twist of a weak quasi-Hopf algebra, extending properties known for quasi-Hopf algebras. Let A be a unital discrete algebra endowed with two weak quasi-bialgebra structures (A, ε, ∆, Φ), (A, ε, ∆′, Φ ′ ). We may correspondingly form two tensor categories Rep(A), Rep′(A) and the functor E : Rep(A) → Rep′(A) acting identically on objects and morphisms.
