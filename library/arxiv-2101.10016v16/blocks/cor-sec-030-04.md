Let q0 be a minimal root of unity of large enough order. Then for all q varying on the arc Tq0,1 of the unit circle clockwise connecting q0 to 1, we have that Λ+(q0) ⊂ Λ+(q) and the latter equals the whole Weyl chamber Λ + for q = 1; for λ ∈ Λ+(q0), and q varying continuously in Tq0,1, the Kashiwara-Lusztig basis of Vλ,A′ specializes to a basis of Vλ(q); the invariant Hermitian form of Vλ(q) is positive definite and is uniquely determined by (vλ(q), vλ(q)) = 1 and is invariant with respect to the compact real form of U(g) for q = 1.
