(V-pre-associator of CFT-type) Let (C, ⊗, ι) be a semisimple linear pretensor category (as in Def. 4.1), (F, F, G) : C → Vec a faithful weak quasi-tensor functor. Consider the discrete algebra A = Nat(F) with coproduct ∆ defined by (F, G) as in (7.1) and corresponding pre-associator of CFT-type ΦF,G as in Def. 8.4. Given a collection V in Ob(C) ×3 we say that the pre-associator ΦF,G is a V-pre-associator of CFT-type if there is a Φ ∈ A ⊗ A ⊗ A such that (a) Φ is an associator for ∆ (thus (A, ∆, Φ) is a discrete weak quasi-bialgebra) (b) Φ restricts to ΦF,G on V.
