Let G be a finite subgroup of the unitary automorphism group of V and let V G be the corresponding orbifold unitary sub VOA. It is conjectured that always V G satisfies (a), (b), (c) and that Rep(VG) is braided tensor equivalent to Rep(Dω(G)) ≃ Z(Vecω G), for some 3-cocycle ω ∈ Z (G, T). Here, Dω(G) the twisted quantum double quasi-Hopf algebra introduced in [31] and Z(Vecω G) is the center of Vecω G, [50].
