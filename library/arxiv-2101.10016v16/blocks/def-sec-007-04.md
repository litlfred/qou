(pre-associator of CFT-type) Let (C, ⊗, ι) be a semisimple linear pretensor category (as in Def. 4.1) and (F, F, G) : C → Vec a faithful weak quasi-tensor functor. Consider the discrete algebra A = Nat(F) with coproduct ∆ defined by (F, G) as in (7.1). We set (ΦF,G)ρ,σ,τ = (G1,2F1,2)ρ,σ,τ ◦ (G2,1F2,1)ρ,σ,τ (8.2) where (F1,2)ρ,σ,τ = Fρ,σ⊗τ ◦ 1F(ρ) ⊗ Fσ,τ , (G1,2)ρ,σ,τ = 1F(ρ) ⊗ Gσ,τ ◦ Gρ,σ⊗τ , (F2,1)ρ,σ,τ = Fρ⊗σ,τ ◦ Fρ,σ ⊗ 1F(τ), (G2,1)ρ,σ,τ = Gρ,σ ⊗ 1F(τ) ◦ Gρ⊗σ,τ . Then ΦF,G is a pre-associator for A as in Def. 6.3. The pre-associator ΦF,G is called of CFT-type.
