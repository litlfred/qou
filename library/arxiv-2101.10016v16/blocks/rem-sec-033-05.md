By Theorem 2.3 in [170] and Theorem 3.2.3 in [61], a quotient relation is well known that describes morphisms of HomG(Vh⊗Hi[0], Hj[0]) that arise as initial terms of primary fields. This quotient relation is a property of the irreducible representation Hfin i . It is derived from a presentation of the ideal of a Verma module at level the positive integer k with quotient the irreducible Hfin i (or Hfin j ). For all g, the linear space IG(Vh ⊗ Hi[0], Hj[0]) has dimension given by the dimension of the corresponding primary fields by Prop. 34.4.
