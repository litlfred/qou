Let (C, ⊗, ι) be a semisimple linear pre-tensor category (Def. 4.1) and let c(ρ, σ) : ρ ⊗ σ → σ ⊗ ρ be an invertible natural transformation such that c(ι, σ) and c(ρ, ι) are identity morphisms for all objects ρ and σ. We shall say that an object V of C satisfies the duality property with respect to c if for any positive integer r ≥ 1 the morphism space (V r , V r) between any two tensor powers of V of the kind of Example 8.10 is linearly generated by finite compositions of the component morphisms of c, their tensor products with identity morphisms and tensor products of morphisms in (V, V ) with identity morphisms.
