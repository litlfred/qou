Let (C, ⊗, ι) be a semisimple pre-tensor category, (F, F, G) : C → Vec a faithful weak quasi-tensor structure. Usually in Tannakian reconstruction, one starts from associativity morphisms in C to endow Rep(A) with associativity morphisms via (F, F, G), where A = Nat0(F) has associated coproduct ∆F,G. But the construction also works in the other direction. If (A, ∆F,G) has an associator Φ such that (A, ∆F,G, Φ) is a weak quasibialgebra then Tannakian quasi equivalence (E, E) : C → Rep(A) pulls back Φ uniquely to associativity morphisms α in C in such a way that (C, ⊗, ι, α) is a tensor category, and (E, E) becomes a tensor equivalence.
