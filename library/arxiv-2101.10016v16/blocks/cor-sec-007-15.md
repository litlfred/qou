Let (C, ⊗, ι) be a semisimple linear pre-tensor category (Def. 4.1) with a generating object V . Let c(ρ, σ) : ρ ⊗ σ → σ ⊗ ρ, ρ, σ ∈ C, be a normalized invertible natural transformation and let V satisfy the duality property with respect to c. Let (F, F, G) : C → Vec a faithful weak quasi-tensor functor. Then an associator Φ of A = Nat0(F) making c into a braided symmetry and extending the pre-associator ΦF,G on V is unique.
