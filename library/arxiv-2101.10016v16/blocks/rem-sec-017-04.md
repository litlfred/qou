In the setting of rigid tensor C ∗ -categories with infinitely many simple objects, Van Daele and Wang constructed compact quantum groups Ao(F) associated to an invertible matrix F with rk(F) ≥ 2 satisfying suitable properties [175], which reduce to Woronowicz compact quantum groups SUq(2) for rk(F) = 2. For a given q > 1, Rep(Ao(F)) turns out to be tensor equivalent to Rep(SUq(2)) with q suitably determined by F [7, 8]. It follows that Rep(SUq(2)) admits the (non-weak) dimension function taking the generating representation to the rank of F.
