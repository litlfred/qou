The examples that we have studied in the paper show that there may be more than a natural choice of integral weak dimension functions associated to a fusion category. For example, for the pointed fusion categories arising from quantum groups at roots of unity (or vertex operator algebras) at the minimal root (level), FPdim(g) = 1 on every irreducible object g, so FPdim is already an integral dimension function. Another natural choice is associated to Wenzl functor or to Zhu’s functor. Consider for each level k, Gr(C(g, q, ℓ))) for q = e iπ/ℓ , ℓ = d(k + hˇ) and regard it as a quotient of the classical representation ring R(g) associated to g.
