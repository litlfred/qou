THE COLORED JONES POLYNOMIAL AND THE A-POLYNOMIAL
FOR TWIST KNOTS
TOSHIE TAKATA
Abstract. @We show that for a twist knot, the A-polynomial can be obtained from recur-
rences for the summand in Masbaum’s formula of the colored Jones polynomial. Our result
supports the AJ conjecture due to S.Garoufalidis.
