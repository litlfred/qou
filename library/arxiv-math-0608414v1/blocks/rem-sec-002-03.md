The Laplace transform L is a continuous operator from L1 b(ΦD) to the space of analytic functions in the half plane ℜ(x) > b with the uniform norm. Let K ∈C be a bounded domain, diam (K) = D < ∞. On the space of continuous functions on K we take the uniform norm with exponential weight: ∥f∥u := D sup p∈K {|f(p)|e−b|p|} (2.5) (which is equivalent to the usual uniform norm). Let O ⊂D, O ∋0 be a star-shaped, open set, diam(O) = D containing a ray segment Φ. Let A be the space of analytic functions f in O such that f(0) = 0, endowed with the norm (2.5).
