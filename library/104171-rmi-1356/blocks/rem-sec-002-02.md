Special cases of Definition 3.1 are: (1) The case g D 0 is the classical braid group Bn D B0;n. (2) For g D 1, the handlebody braid group is the braid group of extended affine type A, which is also the braid group of Coxeter type C D B, see [7] or [1]. (3) A perhaps surprising fact is that the handlebody braid group for g D 2 is isomorphic to the braid group of affine Coxeter type C, see [1]. (4) To the best of our knowledge, the case g > 2 was first studied in [64], and then further in [28].
