Let Γ ⊂R3 be an oriented differentiable graph that is transversal to the standard contact structure ξ of R3. Let e ⊂Γ be an edge having a local parameterization function f(t): [0, 1] →R3 where f(t) = (ρ(t), θ(t), z(t)). If dθ dt is not positive for all t then there exists a transversal isotopy of Γ which is the identity everywhere except on a small neighborhood of e such that for a resulting parameterization bf(t) = (bρ(t), bθ(t),bz(t)) we have dbθ dt being everywhere positive.
