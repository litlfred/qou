Stabilization in the braid groups II:
Transversal simplicity of knots
JOAN S BIRMAN
WILLIAM W MENASCO
The main result of this paper is a negative answer to the question: are all transversal
knot types transversally simple? An explicit inﬁnite family of examples is given of
closed 3–braids that deﬁne transversal knot types that are not transversally simple.
The method of proof is topological and indirect.
