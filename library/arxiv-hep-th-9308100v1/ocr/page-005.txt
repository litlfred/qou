When dealing with quantum fields, the space of states for a Rindler observer consists of
the functionals of fields defined on the positive R axis. This restriction requires the usual
Minkowski space vacuum to be described in Rindler space as a density matrix for a mixed

state. The appropriate density matrix was obtained by Unruh [6] and is given by
p = exp(—27Hp). (2.4)
