2. Volume conjecture and its generalizations
In [23] Kashaev introduced link invariants parametrized by an integer N greater
than one, by using the quantum dilogarithm. Moreover he obse rved in [24] that
for the hyperbolic knots 4 1, 5 2, and 6 1 his invariants grow exponentially and
the growth rates give the hyperbolic volumes of the knot comp lements. He also
conjectured this would also hold for any hyperbolic knot in S3.
