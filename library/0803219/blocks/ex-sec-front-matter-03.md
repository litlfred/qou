A knot that can be put on the standard torus is called a torus knot. Such knots are parametrized by two coprime integers, and up to mirror image we may assume that they are both positive. We also assume that they are bigger than 1. For the torus knot T(a, b) of type (a, b) with positive coprime integers a and b with a > 1 and b > 1, the fundamental group π1(T(a, b)) also has a presentation with two generators and one relation: ⟨g, h | ga = hb⟩.
