We shall need the non-degenerate $E$-bilinear pairing $$\begin{aligned} E[G] \times \bigoplus_{\t: k \to \C} E & \to E \\ (\smallsum_\s a_\s \s, (b_\t)_\t ) & \mapsto \smallsum_\s a_\s b_{\phi_\infty\circ\s^{-1}} \,. \end{aligned}$$ It factors through the left $E[G]$-action on $\oplus_\t E$ (given by $\s((b_\t)_\t) = (b_{\t\circ\s^{-1}})_\t$), hence is trivial on $E[G](1-\pi)\times \pi(\oplus_\t E)$ and $E[G]\pi\times (1-\pi)(\oplus_\t E)$.
