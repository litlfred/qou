In [28], Greenberg proves that the Main Conjecture of Iwasawa theory implies the p-adic Artin conjecture, that is, that the p-adic L-function of an Artin character χ is analytic on the open ball B if it does not contain the trivial character, and has at most a simple pole at s = 1 otherwise. It therefore follows from the proof of the Main Conjecture by Mazur and Wiles [38] for p odd that Lp(n, χ, k) for p̸ = 2 is deﬁned for all integers n̸ = 1.
