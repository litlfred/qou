[]{#sec:gener label="sec:gener"} In this section, we consider an $m$-split distance distribution defined on a partition of $\{1, \dots, n\}$ with arbitrary $m$ subsets, say $T_{1}, \dots, T_{m}$, each of size $\lvert T_{p}\rvert = n_{p}$ for $p \in \{1, \dots, m\}$. Our method can be generalized in this case to introduce more semidefinite constraints. Proofs to these generalizations are similar to the $2$-split case and will be omitted. Finally, we discuss the underlying association scheme structure.\
