arXiv:2203.06568v4  [cs.IT]  10 Jun 2023
Semideﬁnite programming bounds for binary codes
from a split Terwilliger algebra
Pin-Chieh Tseng, Ching-Yi Lai, and Wei-Hsuan Yu
Abstract
We study the upper bounds for A(n, d), the maximum size of codewords with length n and Hamming distance at least d.
Schrijver studied the Terwilliger algebra of the Hamming scheme and proposed a semideﬁnite program to bound A(n, d). We
derive more sophisticated matrix inequalities based on a split Terwilliger algebra to improve Schrijver’s semideﬁnite programming
bounds on A(n, d). In particular, we improve the semideﬁnite programming bounds on A(18, 4) to 6551.
