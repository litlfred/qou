The skein of a planar surface

boundary points for some integer n ≥0. The Homﬂy skein S(F) is deﬁned as
the module of linear combinations of oriented tangles in F quotiented by regular

The scalars can be chosen as Z[v±1, s±1] with powers of s−s−1 in the denomi-
nators. If necessary, we can choose the scalars to be the ﬁeld of rational functions
in v and s. This will occur in section 3 when we divide a quasi-idempotent by a
scalar to turn it into an idempotent.
For the skein of any planar surface F there is a map ρ : S(F) →S(F) which
is induced by switching all crossings and replacing s by s−1 and v by v−1. The
skein relations are preserved by this map. We remark that ρ is not linear.
