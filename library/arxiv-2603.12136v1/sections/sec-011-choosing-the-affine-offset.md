Althoughδ C is a natural choice, it does have a few problematic aspects. First of
all, the productAδC that appears in the right-hand sideb−Aδ C of the linear pro-
gram after applying the transformationx=x′ +δcan be problematic. IfAcontains
large nonzero-entries and/orδC is large due to variables with large domains, then the
productAδ C may result in large numerical values in the right-handb−AδC in the
linear program obtained from the reflection reduction. This is undesirable, as it can
lead to numerical problems that slow down the linear programming solver. Thus, we
generally prefer offsetsδthat are sparse and/or have small norms.
