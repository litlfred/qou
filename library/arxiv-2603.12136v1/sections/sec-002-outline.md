In Section 2 we introduce DRCR in further detail and define relevant notation.
Section 3 extends DRCR to also handle reflection symmetries that arise from signed
permutation matrices. In Section 4, we explore how DRCR can be extended and
specialized for mixed-integer linear programs. In Section 5, we formulate a DRCR
algorithm that detects reflection symmetries in MILP problems. Section 6 presents
computational experiments on MIPLIB 2017.
