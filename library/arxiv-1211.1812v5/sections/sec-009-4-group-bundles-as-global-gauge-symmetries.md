[]{#sec.gauge label="sec.gauge"}

In the previous section we proved that the superselection structure $Z^{1,\bullet}(\EuFrak{R})$ can be described by a presheaf and, as a consequence of [@Vas12], as the dual of a group (net) bundle $\EuFrak{G}$ when an embedding is given. To illustrate the way in which $\EuFrak{G}$ should behave like a gauge group, we give a class of examples in which an action of the type ([\[eq.GA00\]](#eq.GA00){reference-type="ref" reference="eq.GA00"}) is implemented and representations of the type defined in §[\[sec.back.nets\]](#sec.back.nets){reference-type="ref" reference="sec.back.nets"} appear.

The procedure that we use is based on geometric arguments and can be applied in a very general setting (the input is a field algebra carrying an action by a gauge group), thus it yields a new approach to construct superselection sectors with nontrivial holonomy, different from the one used in [@BFM09] in the case of massive boson fields on the Einstein spacetime ($S^1 \times {\mathbb R}$).
