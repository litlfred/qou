W e investigate the Reshetikhin–Turaev invariants associa ted to SU(2) for the 3–
manifolds M obtained by doing any rational surgery along the ﬁgure 8 knot . In partic-
ular, we express these invariants in terms of certain comple x double contour integrals.
These integral formulae allow us to propose a formula for the leading asymptotics of
the invariants in the limit of large quantum level. W e analyz e this expression using
the saddle point method. W e construct a certain surjection f rom the set of stationary
points for the relevant phase functions onto the space of con jugacy classes of nonabelian
SL(2,C)–representations of the fundamental group of M and prove that the values of
these phase functions at the relevant stationary points equ als the classical Chern–Simons
invariants of the corresponding ﬂat SU(2)–connections. Ou r ﬁndings are in agreement
with the asymptotic expansion conjecture. Moreover, we cal culate the leading asymp-
totics of the colored Jones polynomial of the ﬁgure 8 knot fol lowing Kashaev [14]. This
leads to a slightly ﬁner asymptotic description of the invar iant than predicted by the
volume conjecture [24].
