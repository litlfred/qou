K(r) and τr(Mp/q)
In this section we investigate the large r asymptotics of τr(Mp/q) or more precisely
the leading term of this asymptotics, using the saddle point method. We begin
by calculating the large r asymptotics of J ′
K(r) using the expression (3.21). This
calculation will demonstrate the use of the saddle point method and w ill serve as a
warm up for the more diﬃcult considerations of the asymptotics of τr(Mp/q) in the
ﬁnal part of this section.
