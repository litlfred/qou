(If $(x_{0},y_{0})$ is a non-degenerate critical point on the boundary of $\Sigma$, then one or both or the integrals $\int_{-\delta}^{\delta}$ should be replaced by $\int_{0}^{\delta}$ (or $\int_{-\delta}^{0}$) and the contribution coming from that point in \refthm{thm:asymptotics} should be multiplied by $\frac{1}{2}$ or $\frac{1}{4}$.) By a Taylor expansion we find that $$ \Psi(x_{0} + \alpha s,y_{0} + \beta t) = \Psi(x_{0},y_{0}) + \frac{1}{2}\la A\left(\begin{array}{c} s\\t\end{array} \right), \left(\begin{array}{c} s\\t\end{array}\right) \ra + h(s,t), $$ where $A = \diag(\alpha,\beta) H \diag(\alpha,\beta)$ and $$ \la \left(\begin{array}{c} x_{1}\\y_{1}\end{array}\right), \left(\begin{array}{c} x_{2}\\y_{2}\end{array}\right)\ra = x_{1}x_{2} + y_{1}y_{2} $$ for $(x_{1},y_{1}),(x_{2},y_{2}) \in \C^{2}$, and where $h(s,t)$ is a remainder term being a sum of terms of the form $c_{m,n}s^{m}t^{n}$, $m,n \in \{0,1,2,\ldots,...\}$, $m+n \geq 3$, $c_{m,n} \in \C$.
