We shall need the following variant of the Taylor expansion of $\til{\beta}(z)$ to second order around $z_0$: \begin{equation*} \label{eqn:152} \til{\beta}(z) = \til{\beta}(z_0) + \int_{\pi} (\d \til{\beta})(z_0) + \opn{R}^2(\til{\beta}, \pi) . \end{equation*} Here $\d \til{\beta} \in \Omega^1(Z) \otimes \h$, and $(\d \til{\beta})(z_0) \in \Omega^1_{\mrm{const}}(Z) \otimes \h$ is the associated constant form (see Definition \ref{dfn:26}).
