Given a homomorphism of monoids $\phi : \opn{Wrd}(S) \to M$, and a word $w(\bsym{s}) \in \opn{Wrd}(\bsym{s})$, we shall use the following ``substitution notation'': \[ w(\bsym{\gamma}) = w(\gamma_1, \ldots, \gamma_n) := \phi(w(\bsym{s})) \in M , \] where $\bsym{\gamma}$ is the sequence \[ \bsym{\gamma} = (\gamma_1, \ldots, \gamma_n) := \bigl( \phi(s_1), \ldots, \phi(s_n) \bigr) \] in $M$.
