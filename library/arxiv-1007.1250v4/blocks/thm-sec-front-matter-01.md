Our main result is the 3-dimensional nonabelian Stokes Theorem. This is a totally new result. The methods we use are: the CBH Theorem for the nonabelian exponential map; piecewise smooth geometry of polyhedra; and some basic algebraic topol- ogy. The motivation for this work comes from twisted deformation quantization and descent for nonabelian gerbes. Similar questions arise in nonabelian gauge theory. Contents 0. Introduction 1.
