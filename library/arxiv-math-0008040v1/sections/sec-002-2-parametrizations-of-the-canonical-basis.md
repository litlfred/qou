For positive integers p < qwe denote by [ p,q ] the set {p,p + 1,... ,q }, and for a rational number
x we denote by ⌈x⌉ the smallest element of {y ∈ Z : x ≤y}.
Let g be the simple Lie algebra over C of type An andU be the quantized enveloping algebra of g.
Then U is a Q(v)-algebra generated by the elements Ei, Fi, Kµ , i ∈ {1, 2,... ,n }, µ ∈Q, the root
lattice of g. Let U + be the subalgebra generated by the Ei and U − the subalgebra generated by
the Fi.
Let W be the Weyl group of g with Coxeter generators s1,s 2,... ,s n. It has a unique element w0
of maximal length k. We shall identify a reduced expression si1si2 · · ·sik for w0 with the k-tuple
i = (i1,i 2,... ,i k). We shall denote by χ n the set of all reduced expressions i = (i1,i 2,... ,i k)
for w0. Given i, i′ ∈ χ n, we say that i ≃ i′ if there is a sequence of commutations (of the form
sisj = sjsi with |i −j| > 1) which, when applied to i, give i′. This is an equivalence relation on
χ n, and the equivalence classes are called commutation classe s.
For each i ∈ χ n there are two parametrizations of the canonical basis B for U −. The ﬁrst arises
from Lusztig’s approach to the canonical basis [11, §14.4.6], and the second arises from Kashiwara’s
approach [7].
