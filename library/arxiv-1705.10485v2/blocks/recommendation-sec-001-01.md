If $D=\I \mathbb{R}$, we shall just speak of mod-$\phi$ convergence; it is then convenient to use the notation $$\begin{aligned} \theta_n(\xi)&=\mathbb{E}[\E^{\I \xi X_n}]\,\E^{-t_n\eta(\I \xi)};\\ \theta(\xi)&= \psi(\I\xi), \end{aligned}$$ so that mod-$\phi$ convergence corresponds to $\lim_{n \to \infty}\theta_n(\xi)= \theta(\xi)$ (uniformly for $\xi$ in compact subsets of $\mathbb{R}$).
