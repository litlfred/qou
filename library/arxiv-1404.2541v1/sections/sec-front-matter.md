arXiv:1404.2541v1  [math.CA]  9 Apr 2014
The Stokes phenomenon for the Ramanujan’s
q-diﬀerence equation and its higher order
extension
Takeshi MORITA∗
Abstract
We show connection formulae of local solutions of the Ramanu-
jan equation between the origin and the inﬁnity. These solutions are
given by the Ramanujan function, the q-Airy function and the diver-
gent basic hypergeometric series 2ϕ0(0, 0; −; q, x). We use two diﬀerent
q-Borel-Laplace resummation methods to obtain our connection for-
mulae. We also introduce the q-Borel-Laplace transformation of level
r−1, which are higher order extension of these transformations. These
methods are useful to obtain an asymptotic formula of a divergent se-
ries rϕ0(0, 0, . . . , 0; −; q, x).
