For any link L, V L ( - 1 ) = A L ( - 1 ) . That the trace invariant may always be calculated by using Theorem 12 follows from the proof of the same thing for the Alexander polynomial. We urge the reader to try this method on, say, the trefoil knot. The special nature of the algebras An when t is a relevant root of unity can be exploited to give information about VL at these values.
