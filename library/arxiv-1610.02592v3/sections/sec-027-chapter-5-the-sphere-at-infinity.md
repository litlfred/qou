We have discovered that every complete hyperbolic manifold is a quotient
M = Hn/Γ for some discrete group Γ < Isom(Hn) acting freely, and we now
raise the following question: how does Γ act on the boundary ∂Hn at inﬁnity?
Does the action of Γ on ∂Hn furnish some information on the geometry of M?
