In every dimension n ⩾2 there exists a unique simply connected complete
Riemannian manifold with constant sectional curvature 1, 0, or −1. These are
the sphere Sn, the Euclidean space Rn, and the hyperbolic space Hn.
These manifolds are the three most important spaces in Riemannian geom-
etry. We introduce in this chapter the least familiar and the most interesting
of the three: hyperbolic space.
