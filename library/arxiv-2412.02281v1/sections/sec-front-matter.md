Explicit evaluation of the q-Stokes matrices for certain conﬂuent
hypergeometric q-diﬀerence equations
Jinghong Lin, Yiming Ma and Xiaomeng Xu
Abstract
We prove a connection formula for the basic hypergeomtric function nϕn−1 (a1, ..., an−1, 0; b1, ..., bn−1; q, z)
by using the q-Borel resummation. As an application, we compute q-Stokes matrices of a special conﬂuent
hypergeometric q-diﬀerence system with an irregular singularity. We show that by letting q →1, the q-Stokes
matrices recover the known expressions of the Stokes matrices of the corresponding conﬂuent hypergeometric
diﬀerential system.
