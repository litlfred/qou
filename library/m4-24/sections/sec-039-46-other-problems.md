Problem 4.18 Deﬁne a skein module of 3-manifolds, and calculate it.
Remark The quantum Hilbert space (or the space of conformal blocks) of
(S2, 4 points) is known to be ﬁnite dimensional. This is a reason why a quan-
tum invariant of links satisﬁes a skein relation; it is a linear relation of tan-
gles bounded by (S2, 4 points) whose invariants are linearly dependent in the
quantum Hilbert space. The quantum Hilbert space of a closed surface, say, a
torus, is also known to be ﬁnite dimensional. Hence, a quantum invariant of 3-
manifolds satisﬁes a “skein relation”; it should be a linear relation of 3-manifolds
bounded by a surface. A skein module of 3-manifolds might be deﬁned to be
a module spanned by closed oriented 3-manifolds subject to a suitably cho-
sen “skein relation” among 3-manifolds. It is a problem to deﬁne such a skein
module which can be calculated.
