Remark (X.-S. Lin) It would be a basic problem to look into the zero distri-
bution of the family of polynomials with bounded degree such that coeﬃcients
are all integers and coeﬃcients sum up to 1, and compare it with the zero
distribution of the Jones polynomial on the collection of (alternating) knots
with bounded crossing number. The paper [317] discusses the zero distribution

particularly interesting to compare the plotting shown in this paper with the
plottings in Figures 2 and 3 for the zeros of the Jones polynomials.

VK(−1) is just ∆K(−1), which is the order
of the torsion in the homology of the double cover of S3 branched over K.
log VK(−1) is one of the ﬁrst terms of the volume conjecture (Conjecture 1.19).
Figure 4 suggests that for alternating knots with a ﬁxed number of crossings,
log VK(−1) is almost a linear function of the volume.
Figure 5 suggests that there should be an inequality
log VK(−1)

for some constants a and b. For 2-bridge knots, Agol’s work on the volumes of
2-bridge knots [1] can be used to prove such an inequality with a = b = 2/v3
(here, v3 is the volume of a regular ideal tetrahedron).
