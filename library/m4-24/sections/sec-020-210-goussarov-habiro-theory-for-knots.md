Related to Vassiliev invariants of knots, equivalence relations among knots have
been studied by Goussarov [152, 153] and Habiro [165], which is called the
Goussarov-Habiro theory for knots. These equivalence relations are helpful for
us to study structures of the set of knots.

relation generated by either of the following relations,
