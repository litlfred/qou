3.6 The Chern-Simons series of configuration space integrals

Question 3.12 (C. Lescop) Is the Kontsevich integral of a (zero-framed)
knot equal to the Chern-Simons series of configuration space integrals of the
same knot (with Gauss integral 0)?

versal Vassiliev knot invariant that admits a natural and beautiful symmetric
definition that will be given below before describing the present situation of
