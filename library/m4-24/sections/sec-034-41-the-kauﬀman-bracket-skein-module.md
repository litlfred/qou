skein module S2,∞(M) of M is deﬁned to be the R module spanned by unori-
ented framed links in M (including the empty link) subject to the relations

is a generalization of the Jones polynomial (in its Kauﬀman bracket interpreta-

type skein modules. It can be interpreted as a quantization of the co-ordinate
ring of the character variety of SL(2, C) representations of the fundamental
group of the manifold M , [71, 343, 74, 344].

Problem 4.5 Let F be a surface and I an interval. Describe the algebra
S2,∞(F × I).
Remark
S2,∞(F × I) is an algebra (usually noncommutative). It is ﬁnitely
generated algebra for a compact F [72], and has no zero divisors [344]. The
center of the algebra is generated by boundary components of F [75, 344].
