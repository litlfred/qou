9 The state-sum invariants of 3-manifolds derived
from 6j-symbols

Turaev and Viro [392] introduced a formulation of a state-sum invariant of 3-
manifolds as a state-sum on triangulations of 3-manifolds derived from certain
6j-symbols. After that, Ocneanu gave a general formulation of this state-sum
for general 6j-symbols and constructed 3-manifold invariants from subfactors
based on this formulation. This general formulation was also given by Barrett
and Westbury [41].
