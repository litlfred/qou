The LMO invariant ZLMO(M) ∈A(∅) of closed oriented 3-manifolds was in-
troduced in [249].
The LMO invariant of rational homology 3-spheres was
reformulated by Aarhus integral [36]. The LMO invariant is a universal per-
turbative invariant of rational homology 3-spheres (see [320, 36, 321]), and a
universal ﬁnite type invariant of integral homology 3-spheres [244].
