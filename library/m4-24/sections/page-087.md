Remark Let X be a connected finite quandle, and let hx(K) denote the
number of representations of the knot quandle of a knot K to X. Then, hx
is multiplicative with respect to connected sum of knots. It is known (see, for
example, [321]) that any Q-valued Vassiliev invariant is equal to a polynomial
in some primitive Vassiliev invariants, where primitive Vassiliev invariants are
additive with respect to connected sum of knots. Hence, hx is not a Vassiliev
invariant, unless it is constant. (See also [4] for another proof.)
