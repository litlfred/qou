Problem 1.1 ([188, Problem 1]) Find a non-trivial knot K with VK(t) = 1.
Remark It is shown by computer experiments that there are no non-trivial
knots with VK(t) = 1 up to 17 crossings of their diagrams [102], and up to 18
crossings [407]. See [52] (and [53]) for an approach to ﬁnd such knots by using
representations of braid groups.
Remark Two knots with the same Jones polynomial can be obtained by mu-
tation. A mutation is a relation of two knots, which are identical except for a
ball, where they diﬀer by π rotation of a 2-strand tangle in one of the following
ways (see [12] for mutations).
For example, the Conway knot and the Kinoshita-Terasaka knot are related by
a mutation.
