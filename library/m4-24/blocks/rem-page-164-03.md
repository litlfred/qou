It is shown by W. Thurston that the hyperbolic vol- ume of a hyperbolic 3-manifold is quasi-linear in the minimal number of ideal tetrahedra in a “spun triangulation” (i.e. the minimal number of ideal tetra- hedra in some link complement in the 3-manifold). It is shown by J. Brock [68] that the volume of a mapping torus is quasi-linear in the pants translation distance (for fixed genus).
