It is shown by Goussarov (see [154]) that there exists a Gauss diagram formula for any Vassiliev invariant of classical knots. His proof is an algorithmical proof, assuming the existence of such a Vassiliev invariant, and does not give a new proof of Kontsevich theorem “any weight system can be integrated to an invariant of knots”. It would be nice to have a new direct combinatorial proof, which would imply Kontsevich theorem. Then, it would work for virtual knots.
