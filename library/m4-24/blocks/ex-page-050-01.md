Let I be the ideal generated by linking numbers. In this case, Z is the set of algebraically split links. Is it true that every finite type invariant that vanishes on algebraically split links is a sum of multiples of linking numbers? I believe it is true, and I believe it follows from the results of Appleboim [13], but I’m afraid Appleboim’s paper is incomplete and while I believe it I cannot vouch for its validity.
