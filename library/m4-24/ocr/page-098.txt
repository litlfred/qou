Problem 6.7 (S.J. Bigelow) Is there a faithful representation of B, into a
group of matrices over Q?

Remark There is a faithful representation of Bs into GL(2,Z). The problem
is open for all n > 4.

There is a faithful representation of B, into a group of matrices over Z[g*?, t*?].
Krammer’s proof of this fact [229] works when t is assigned any value between
0 and 1. However it is not known whether there is an algebraic value of q for

which the representation remains faithful.
