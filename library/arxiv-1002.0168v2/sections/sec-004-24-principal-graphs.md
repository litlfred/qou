Given a unitary irreducible shaded planar algebra P the principal graphs are a pair
of bipartite graphs which together encode the fusion rules for tensoring with the
single strand.
The principal graph has even vertices corresponding to isomorphism classes of simple
projections in Veven,+ and odd vertices corresponding to the isomorphism classes of
simple projections in Vodd,+. An even vertex V and an odd vertex W are connected
by dim Hom (W⊗X,V ) edges, where X is the single strand. The dual principal
graph has even vertices corresponding to isomorphism classes of simple projections
in Veven,−, odd vertices corresponding Vodd,−, and edges given by the fusion with
the single strand of the opposite shading.
A subfactor is called ﬁnite depth if the principal graph is ﬁnite.
