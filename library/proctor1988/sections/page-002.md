0. Classical Vandermonde identities

The denominator of the Weyl character formula for finite dimensional represen-
tations of simple Lie groups can be written as either an alternating sum over
the associated Weyl group W or as a product over the positive roots 4* of
the Lie group. Equating the two expressions yields the denominator identity
associated to that Lie group. But one need not refer to Lie groups in order
to write down the identities: Only knowledge of the root system is needed.
And in fact these identities have important occurrences outside of representation
theory. The identity associated to GL, is the familiar elementary Vandermonde
determinant identity. The denominator identities for affine root systems were
originally formulated independently of Lie theory; some of them specialize to
famous eta and theta function identities [Mcd1].
