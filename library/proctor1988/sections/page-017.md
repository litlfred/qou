6. Other occurrences of Sp2,+1

A symplectic manifold is a real differentialble manifold together with a smooth
non-degenerate closed 2-form. So the manifold must be even dimensional; let
the dimension be 2n. At a point the group of linear transformations of the
tangent space which fix the 2-form is Sp,,(IR). “Contact” manifolds are odd
dimensional real manifolds which arise in classical mechanics as time dependent
companions to symplectic manifolds: 2n+1=n space dimensions +n velocity
dimensions +1 time dimension. One definition of contact manifold goes as above,
except now the 2-form is required to be of maximal possible rank [AbMa].
Hence the group of tangent symmetries at a point is now Sp.,4,(R). (Often
“contact manifold” is defined to be what [AbMa] calls “exact contact mani-
fold”; then the point symmetry group is Sp, (IR).)
