The combinatorial identity Corollary 4.1 was the original motivation for
this work. Product identities of this form which enumerate various sets of plane
partitions have been studied by combinatorialists on and off over the last 80
years, so it is somewhat surprising that this identity had not been found before.

with the Weyl dimension product formula. The desire to extend this proof to
the case of even p led to the definition of Sp,,_, and to Theorem 2.1.
