1. Introduction

In this paper we modify the usual definition of symplectic group as follows:

Definition. A symplectic group Sp(V, B) on a vector space V is a subgroup
of GL(V) which preserves a non-degenerate skew symmetric bilinear form B
of maximal possible rank.
