In this paper we will define symplectic groups on odd dimensional vector spaces.
These Lie groups are not simple or reductive. We will obtain a character formula
for certain tensor representations of these groups. This formula is very similar
to the Weyl character formula for representations of simple Lie groups. The
form of this formula and of related objects and identities will show that the
odd dimensional symplectic groups defined here fit very nicely into a framework
established by the usual even symplectic and odd and even orthogonal groups,
thereby filling “gaps” which would otherwise exist. One of the most important
examples of this occurs at the level of “denominator identities”: The denomina-
tor of our character formula can be written as a product over the positive
Toots of type BC,. But denominator identities have an existence and importance
in mathematics independent of Lie groups. In order to emphasize the indepen-
dence of this situation from Lie theory, we will describe it first: Some readers
may prefer to skip ahead to the introduction (Section 1) at this point.
