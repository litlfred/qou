CHARACTER DEFLATIONS AND A GENERALIZATION
OF THE MURNAGHAN–NAKAYAMA RULE
ANTON EVSEEV, ROWENA PAGET AND MARK WILDON
Abstract. Given natural numbers m and n, we deﬁne a deﬂation map
from the characters of the symmetric group Smn to the characters of Sn.
This map is obtained by ﬁrst restricting a character of Smn to the wreath
product Sm ≀Sn, and then taking the sum of the irreducible constituents
of the restricted character on which the base group Sm × · · · × Sm acts
trivially. We prove a combinatorial formula which gives the values of
the images of the irreducible characters of Smn under this map. We
also prove an analogous result for more general deﬂation maps in which
the base group is not required to act trivially. These results generalize
the Murnaghan–Nakayama rule and special cases of the Littlewood–
Richardson rule. As a corollary we obtain a new combinatorial formula
for the character multiplicities that are the subject of the long-standing
Foulkes’ Conjecture. Using this formula we verify Foulkes’ Conjecture
in some new cases.
