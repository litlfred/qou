M¨obius Energy of Thick Knots

Abstract
The M¨obius energy of a knot is an energy functional for smooth curves based on
an idea of self-repelling. If a knot has a thick tubular neighborhood, we would
intuitively expect the energy to be low. In this paper, we give explicit bounds for
energy in terms of the ropelength of the knot, i.e. the ratio of the length of a thickest
tube to its radius.
Key words: M¨obius energy, knot energy, ropelength, thickness, physical knots
