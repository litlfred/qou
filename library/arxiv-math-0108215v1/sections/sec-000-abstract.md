The M¨ obius energy of a knot is an energy functional for smoot h curves based on
an idea of self-repelling. If a knot has a thick tubular neigh borhood, we would
intuitively expect the energy to be low. In this paper, we giv e explicit bounds for
energy in terms of the ropelength of the knot, i.e. the ratio o f the length of a thickest
tube to its radius.
Key words: M¨ obius energy, knot energy, ropelength, thickness, physi cal knots
