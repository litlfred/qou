Because K is a smooth closed curve with maximum curvature ≤ 1 (by Lemma
2 and the fact that we have normalized K to have thickness radius = 1),
the total curvature of K is at most L. But by Fenchel’s theorem, the total
curvature of a closed curve is at least 2 π. Thus L ≥ 2π > 8/π, so the linear
part of (7) is negative, and we have
EO4(K) ≤ 1
