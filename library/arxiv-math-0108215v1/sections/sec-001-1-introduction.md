the amount of “rope” needed to make the knot. This is the energy in troduced
in [1] and studied extensively in [2,3]. We follow an overall approach sugg ested
by G. Buck for showing that any inverse-square knot energy shou ld be bounded
by the 4 /3 power of the ropelength.
