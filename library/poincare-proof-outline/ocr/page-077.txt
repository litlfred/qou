7  H-betweenness in the Poincaré model

7.1 H-betwenness expressed by a cross-ratio

The point v is h-between u and w if the cross-ratio between the pairs u and w and v and inverse of v is real
and negative.

definition poincare-between :: complex-homo = complez-homo = complex-homo = bool where
poincare-between uv w +<—
u=vVv=wVv
(let cr = cross-ratio uv w (inversion v)
in is-real (to-complex cr) A Re (to-complex cr) < 0)

7.1.1 H-betwenness is preserved by h-isometries

Since they preserve cross-ratio and inversion, h-isometries (unit disc preserving Mobius transformations and
conjugation) preserve h-betweeness.
