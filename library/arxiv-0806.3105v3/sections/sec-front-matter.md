arXiv:0806.3105v3  [hep-th]  4 Nov 2008
KNOTS AND PREONS
Robert J. Finkelstein
Department of Physics and Astronomy
University of California, Los Angeles, CA 90095-1547
Abstract. It is shown that the four quantum trefoil solitons that are described by the
irreducible representations D3/2
mm′ of the quantum algebra SLq(2) (and that may be identiﬁed
with the four families of elementary fermions (e, µ, τ; νeνµντ; d, s, b; u, c, t)) may be built out
of three preons, chosen from two charged preons with charges (1/3,-1/3) and two neutral
preons. These preons are fermions and are described by the D1/2
mm′, representation of SLq(2).
There are also four bosonic preons described by the D1
mm′ and D0
00 representations of SLq(2).
The knotted standard theory may be replicated at the preon level and the conjectured
particles are in principle indirectly observable.
