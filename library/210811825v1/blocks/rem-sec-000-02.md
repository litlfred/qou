For compact Riemann surfaces of constant curvature, the uniformization theorem (introduced in Section 2.2.1) enables us to normalize the Gaussian curvature to K = ±1 or K = 0, depending respectively on whether the surface is positively or negatively curved, or ﬂat. On surfaces of constant negative curvature, the Gauss-Bonnet theorem then implies Area(S) = 4π(g −1), where g is the genus of the surface.
