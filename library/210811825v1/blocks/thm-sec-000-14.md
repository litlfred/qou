The number of automor- phisms (not including reﬂections) of a compact Riemann surface of genus g > 1 cannot exceed 84(g −1). We see that for genus 3, this bound is 168. The symmetry group of the Klein quartic is therefore maximal. Any surface that achieves this bound is referred to as a Hurwitz surface. A Hurwitz surface does not occur for every single genus g > 1; the next Hur- witz surface is the Macbeath surface of genus 7, which has 504 orientation preserving isometries [55].
