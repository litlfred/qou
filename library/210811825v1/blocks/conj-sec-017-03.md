For n ≥2, among all compact Riemann surfaces of genus n with con- stant negative curvature, the surface with the largest automorphism group globally maxi- mizes the ﬁrst positive eigenvalue in the spectrum of the Laplace-Beltrami operator. However, this assumes that there is a unique surface that maximizes the automorphism group. If we restrict our attention to Hurwitz surfaces, we can conjecture that the Macbeath surface in genus 7 has this maximization property.
