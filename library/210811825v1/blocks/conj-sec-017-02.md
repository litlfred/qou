Among all compact Riemann surfaces of genus 3 with constant nega- tive curvature, the Klein quartic globally maximizes the ﬁrst positive eigenvalue in the spectrum of the Laplace-Beltrami operator. In Appendix D we have carried out numerical experiments to support this conjecture. We calculated the spectrum of the surface denoted M3 in [72], which maximizes the systole length for genus 3 surfaces, and found it to have a smaller value of λ1 than the Klein quartic.
