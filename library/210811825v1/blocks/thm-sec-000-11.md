The dimension of the ﬁrst eigenspace of ∆(B) is 3. This result was stated by Jenni in [42] after being proved in his thesis [41]. Upon careful inspection, a gap was found in this proof, relating to the analysis of 3 and 4 dimensional irreducible representations. In this work, we give a proof, utilizing the entire automorphism group as opposed to a smaller subgroup. We give the associated irreducible representation explicitly.
