Let K be an integral domain and suppose that (M, ⊲, ⊳) is a E-bimodule with values in K-modules. If Γ is a K-module then we deﬁne an E-bimodule HE by HE(X, Y ) = HomK(M(Y, X), Γ) with operations ⊲and ⊳deﬁned as follows. Let f : Y →Z and f ′ : Z →X be morphisms of E and let φ be an element of HE(X, Y ) then (f ⊲φ)(m) = φ(m ⊳f) and (φ ⊳f ′)(m′) = φ(f ′ ⊲m′) where m ∈M(Z, X) and m′ ∈M(Y, Z).
