The crossing number of two curves is given by the Gauss integral (1) where, however, one takes the absolute value of the numerator. Geometrically, such an integral gives the number of over-crossings in B. KHESIN a plane projection of two curves and averaged over all directions of the projection. This number is not a homotopy invariant, but one can “force” it to be invariant by minimizing over all homotopies of two given space curves.
