An example of such a triple of ﬁelds is given by a divergence-free vector ﬁeld conﬁned to three solid tori which form a Borromean link and have no net twist inside each of the tori. One can think of such a ﬁeld as a triple, where each of the three ﬁelds is supported in its own component. In this case, one can choose the potential 1-forms αj satisfying d(αi ∧αj) = 0.
