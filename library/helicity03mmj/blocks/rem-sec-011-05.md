To give an ergodic interpretation of the higher Hopf invariant (4) one should consider the linking of diﬀerent leaves of the same foliation deﬁned by the form β. In the latter case one can guarantee that there exists a harmonic form ¯ν on M, such that the harmonic form on M × M is its square: ν = ¯ν ⊗¯ν. For arbitrary transverse holonomy-invariant measures the harmonic measure ν is not smooth in general, cf. [17]. Note that this “Brownian interpretation” does not involve short path systems.
