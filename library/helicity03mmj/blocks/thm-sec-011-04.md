Consider a pair of foliations F1 and F2 of linking dimensions k and l in a compact manifold M. Let ν be a harmonic measure on M×M for the foliation F = F1×F2 for any Riemannian metric on M. Then the space average over M×M for the Brownian ergodic average ˜f of the function f(x, y) := iF(x,y)LF(x, y) with respect to the measure ν does not depend on the Riemannian metric on M and is equal to the cross-helicity H(F1, F2) of the foliations.
