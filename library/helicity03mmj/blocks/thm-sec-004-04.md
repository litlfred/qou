Suppose a vector ﬁeld ξ in R3 has an invariant torus T forming a nontrivial knot K. Then Z T (ξ, ξ) dx3 ≥  π · Vol(T) 1/3 · |Flux ξ|2 · (2 · genus(K) −1), where Flux ξ is the ﬂux of ξ through a crossection of T, Vol(T) is the volume of the solid torus, and genus(K) is the genus of the knot K. Recall, that for any knot its genus is the minimal number of handles of a spanning (oriented) surface for this knot. For an unknot the genus is 0, since one can take a disk as a spanning surface. For a nontrivial knot one has genus(K) ≥1 and, therefore, the above energy is bounded away from zero: R (ξ, ξ) dx3 > 0.
