A similar operator e LF : Ω∗(M) →Ω∗(M) corresponding to a linking form LF ∈Ω∗(M × M) on a manifold M sends β(y) 7→ Z My LF(x, y) ∧β(y). GEOMETRY OF HIGHER HELICITIES The key property of this operator is that it sends an exact form to its potential: e LF(β) = d−1β. (A linking form is not unique, and so is not a potential.) This property is related to the fact that the diﬀerential of the linking form gives the δ-form δ(x, y) supported on the diagonal ∆⊂M × M: LF(x, y) = d−1 y δ(x, y), see details in [2].
