MOSCOW MATHEMATICAL JOURNAL
Volume 3, Number 3, July–September 2003, Pages 989–1011
GEOMETRY OF HIGHER HELICITIES
BORIS A. KHESIN
To Vladimir Igorevich Arnold on the occasion of his 65th birthday
Abstract. We revisit an interpretation of higher-dimensional helicities
and Hopf–Novikov invariants from the point of view of the Brownian
ergodic theorem. We also survey various results related to Arnold’s the-
orem on the asymptotic Hopf invariant on three-dimensional manifolds
and recent work on linking of a vector ﬁeld with a foliation, the asymp-
totic crossing number, short path systems, and relations with the Calabi
invariant.
2000 Math. Subj. Class. 37A15, 55Q25, 76W05.
Key words and phrases. Asymptotic Hopf invariant, linking number, link-
ing form, measured foliation, ergodic theorems.
