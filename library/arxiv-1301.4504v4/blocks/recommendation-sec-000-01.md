We will review the AMPS argument in section [\[firewallsect\]](#firewallsect){reference-type="ref" reference="firewallsect"} below, but a key point in motivating their setup is a claim that an infalling observer is able to extract information from the Hawking radiation of a black hole prior to falling in.[^2] The model black hole of Hayden and Preskill [@HaydenPreskill], also partially reviewed below, suggests that this should be possible provided that the observer is able to perform a sophisticated nonlocal measurement on the Hawking radiation that has come out so far.
