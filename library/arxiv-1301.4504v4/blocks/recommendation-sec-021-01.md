Having fixed $S_0$ we can also derive an interesting bound on the AdS radius $R$ in Planck units: we know that we must have $T>L^{-1}$ to avoid starting the computation below the phase transition discussed in the previous section, so we must have $$(TL)^3=\left(\frac{r_0L}{R}\right)^3=S_0\left(\frac{R}{\ell_p}\right)^{-8}>1.$$ Thus the AdS radius in Planck units is bounded by a polynomial in $S_0$ and we can neglect it in most equations.
