[]{#decsect label="decsect"} The theory of quantum error correcting codes has interesting implications for the AMPS experiment, which we discuss in this section.[^1] We will review the main points of this theory assuming no prior experience with the subject. This will be something of a sidetrip from our main exposition, so casual readers may want to skip over this section in a first reading. For the impatient the conclusions of relevance for AMPS are summarized at the end of the section.

[^1]: A recent paper [@Verlinde:2012cy] also discussed the AMPS argument in the language of error correction; their use of error correction was quite different from that we discuss here.
