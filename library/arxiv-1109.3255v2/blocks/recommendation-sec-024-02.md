We take $\ell(1)$ to be a certain path in the base: the number of times that $\ell(1)$ must wind between the singularities and the vertical boundaries/corners is determined by $B$: it is essential that the number of turns $\ell(1)$ makes between two consecutive singularities (or between a boundary and the neighboring singularity) is the affine width of the corresponding portion of $B$.
