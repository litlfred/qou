The relation defining the horizontal distribution is $\omega(X,Y) = 0$, or, $$\begin{split} 0 &= \left\{K_{\eta\eta}\frac{dw}{w} \wedge \frac{d\bar{w}}{\bar{w}} + K_{\eta\xi}\left(\frac{dw}{w} \wedge \frac{d\bar{z}}{\bar{z}} + \frac{dz}{z} \wedge \frac{d\bar{w}}{\bar{w}}\right) + K_{\xi\xi} \frac{dz}{z} \wedge \frac{d\bar{z}}{\bar{z}}\right\}(X,Y)\\ &= K_{\eta\xi}\left(\frac{dw(X)}{w}\frac{d\bar{z}(Y)}{\bar{z}} - \frac{d\bar{w}(X)}{\bar{w}}\frac{dz(Y)}{z}\right) + K_{\xi\xi}\left(\frac{dz(X)}{z}\frac{d\bar{z}(Y)}{\bar{z}}- \frac{dz(Y)}{z}\frac{d\bar{z}(X)}{\bar{z}}\right)\\ &= \left(K_{\eta\xi}\frac{dw(X)}{w} + K_{\xi\xi} \frac{dz(X)}{z}\right)\frac{d\bar{z}(Y)}{\bar{z}} - \text{ complex conjugate} \end{split}$$ Since $dz(Y)$ can have any phase, this shows that the quantity in parentheses on the last line must vanish: $$\label{eq:symplectic-connection} d\log z (X) = -\frac{K_{\eta\xi}}{K_{\xi\xi}}d\log w (X)$$ Tropically, this formula has the following interpretation: In the $(\eta,\xi)$ coordinates, the vertical tangent space is spanned by the vector $(0,1)$.
