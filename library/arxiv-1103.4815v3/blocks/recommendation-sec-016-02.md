Now we look at the bijection that the smooth functor $\tau'$ induces on Hom-sets: \begin{equation*} \tau': \hom{(p',h_1^{-1})}{(p',h_2^{-1})} \to \hom{(p',R(p',h_1^{-1}))}{(p',R(p',h_2^{-1}))}\text{.} \end{equation*} On the right hand side, we have the element $(\id_{p'},\tilde\rho_2^{-1} \circ \rho \circ \tilde\rho_1)$, and the corresponding unique element on the left hand side must be of the form $(\id_{p'},\gamma)$, for some $\gamma \in \Gamma_1$ with $R(\id_{p'},\gamma) = \tilde\rho_2^{-1} \circ \rho \circ \tilde\rho_1$.
