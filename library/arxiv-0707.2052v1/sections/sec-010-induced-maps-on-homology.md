In this section we deﬁne HH•(X), the Hochschild homology of a space X,
and show that given a kernel Φ : X→ Y we get pull-back and push-forward
maps, Φ∗ : HH•(Y)→ HH•(X) and Φ∗ : HH•(X)→ HH•(Y), such that if Φ
is right adjoint to Ψ then Ψ∗ = Φ∗.
