[]{#sec:cardy label="sec:cardy"}

We conclude with a discussion of open-closed topological field theories in the B-model and we prove that a condition holds for Hochschild homology which is equivalent to the Cardy Condition in the Calabi-Yau case. Appropriate references for open-closed 2d topological field theories include Moore-Segal [@MooSeg], Costello [@Cos] and Lauda-Pfeiffer [@LaudaPfeifer].
