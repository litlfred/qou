To have a non-commutative example at hand, consider the case when $G$ is a finite group acting trivially on a point. The orbifold $BG$ is defined to be the global quotient $[{\,\cdot\,}/G]$ and then the category of coherent sheaves on the orbifold $BG$ is precisely the category of finite dimensional representations of $G$. One can naturally identify $\mathop{\mathrm{HH}}_0(BG)$ with the space of conjugation invariant functions on $G$, and the Chern character of a representation $\rho$ is precisely the representation-theoretic character of $\rho$. See [@WilGr] for details.
