[]{#sec:chernchar label="sec:chernchar"}

In this section we define the Chern character map $\mathop{\mathrm{ch}}\colon \mathop{\mathrm{K}}_0(X)
\rightarrow\mathop{\mathrm{HH}}_0(X)$. We discuss the relationship between our construction and the one of Markarian [@Mar Definition 2]. Then we show that the Chern character maps the Euler pairing to the Mukai pairing: we call this the Semi-Hirzebruch-Riemann-Roch Theorem.
