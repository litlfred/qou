First recall that for a space $X$, one way to define its Hochschild *cohomology* is as $$\mathop{\mathrm{HH}}^\bullet(X):=\mathop{\mathrm{Ext}}_{X\times X}^\bullet({\mathscr O}_\Delta,{\mathscr O}_\Delta).$$ However, the ext-group is just the hom-set $\mathop{\mathrm{Hom}}^\bullet_{\mathbf{D}(X\times
  X)}({\mathscr O}_\Delta,{\mathscr O}_\Delta)$ which by the definition of ${\mathscr{V}\textsf{ar}}$ is just $\mathop{\mathrm{{2\mbox{-}Hom}}}^\bullet_{\mathscr{V}\textsf{ar}}(\mathsf {Id}_X, \mathsf {Id}_X)$. In terms of diagrams, we can thus denote an element $\varphi\in \mathop{\mathrm{HH}}^\bullet(X)$ as $$\raisebox{-.45\height}{}.$$ Note that the grading is not indicated in the picture, but this should not give rise to confusion.
