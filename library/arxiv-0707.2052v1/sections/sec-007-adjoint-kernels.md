[]{#sec:adjoints label="sec:adjoints"}

The reader is undoubtably familiar with the notion of adjoint functors. It is easy and natural to generalize this from the context of the 2-category ${\mathscr{C}\textsf{at}}$ of categories, functors and natural transformations to the context of an arbitrary 2-category. In this section it is shown that every kernel, considered as a 1-morphism in the 2-category ${\mathscr{V}\textsf{ar}}$, has both a left and right adjoint: this is a consequence of Serre duality, and is closely related to the familiar fact that every integral transform functor has both a left and right adjoint functor.

Using these notions of left and right adjoints we define *left partial trace maps*, and similarly *right partial trace maps*. These can be viewed as partial versions of the Serre trace map. This construction is very much the heart of the paper.
