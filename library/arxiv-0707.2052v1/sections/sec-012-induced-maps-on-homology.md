[]{#sec:defmaps label="sec:defmaps"} In this section we define $\mathop{\mathrm{HH}}_\bullet(X)$, the Hochschild homology of a space $X$, and show that given a kernel $\Phi\colon X\to Y$ we get pull-back and push-forward maps, $\Phi^*\colon \mathop{\mathrm{HH}}_\bullet(Y)\to
\mathop{\mathrm{HH}}_\bullet(X)$ and $\Phi_*\colon \mathop{\mathrm{HH}}_\bullet(X)\to \mathop{\mathrm{HH}}_\bullet(Y)$, such that if $\Phi$ is right adjoint to $\Psi$ then $\Psi^*=\Phi_*$.
