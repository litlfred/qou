We shall also use its multiplicative translates $\theta_{q,c}(z) = \theta_{q}(z/c)$ (for $c \in {\mathbf C}^{*}$); the function $\theta_{q,c}$ is holomorphic in ${\mathbf C}^{*}$ with simple zeroes, all located on the discrete $q$-spiral $[-c;q]$ and satisfies the functional equation: $\sigma_q\theta_{q,c} = \frac{z}{c} \theta_{q,c}$.\ As is customary for congruence classes, we shall write $\overline{a} = a \pmod{q^{{\mathbf Z}}}$ for the image of $a \in {\mathbf C}^{*}$ in the elliptic curve ${\mathbf{E}_{q}}$.
