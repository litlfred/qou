We compare their Laurent series coefficientwise and get: $$\forall n \in {\mathbf Z}\;,\; (1 - \lambda q^{n}) g_{n} = v_{n}.$$ If $\lambda\not\in [1;q]$ (prohibited direction of summation), there is a unique solution $g \in {\mathcal{O}({\mathbf C}^{*},0)}$ (it does converge where it should), thus a unique solution $f \in {\mathcal{M}({\mathbf C}^{*},0)}$ such that $\theta_{q,\lambda} f$ has no poles in ${\mathbf C}^{*}$.
