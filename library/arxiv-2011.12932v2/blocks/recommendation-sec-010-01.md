Then, the idea behind bichrome graphs is essentially the following: on the one hand, the red part of the graph should be interpreted as prescribing surgery instructions, and it has to be processed through Lyubashenko's algorithm, that is, invoking the universal property \eqref{E:univ_prop_coend} of the coend $\calL$, in order to extract a morphism of $\calC$ (red minima of diagrams translate to the integral $\Lambda$ under this correspondence); on the other hand, the blue part supports the standard graphical calculus implemented by the Reshetikhin--Turaev functor.
