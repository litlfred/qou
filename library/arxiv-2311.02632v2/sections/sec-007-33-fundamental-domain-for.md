Theorem 3.8. The polygonF is the Dirichlet domain centered at 0.
Proof. Referring to (3.9), one can clearly see that D(0) is a subset ofF. It is sufficient
to verify that every interior point of F lies within D(0). Then, considering that both
sets are closed, it implies that the two sets are the same.
Letz∈F\ D(0) be an interior point ofF. Given that D(0) is a fundamental domain
for Γ, there exists a transformation γ∈ Γ such that γz∈ D(0)⊆F . Consequently,
both z and γz reside inF, which implies that they are boundary points of F. From
this, we conclude that D(0) andF are indeed identical. □
