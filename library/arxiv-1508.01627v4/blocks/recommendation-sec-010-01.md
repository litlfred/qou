If we write $d := d_\ell(q)$ for the order of $q$ modulo $\ell$ as in Section [\[subsec:regss\]](#subsec:regss){reference-type="ref" reference="subsec:regss"} and then $n = ad+r$, $0\leq r < d$, for odd $d$ respectively $n=ae+r$, $0\leq r < e$, for even $d=2e$, then our assumption on $\ell$ implies $a\geq 2$ because the cyclotomic polynomial $\Phi_d(q)$ must divide the order of ${\mathbf{G}}(q)$ at least twice.
