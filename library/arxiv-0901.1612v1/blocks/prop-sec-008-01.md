The fundamental solution of the scalar Laplacian on the 3-torus T 3 is given by the formula ϕ(x, y, z) = −1 8π3 X ei(ℓx+my+nz) ℓ2 + m2 + n2 where the sum is over all integers ℓ, m and n (positive and negative) with the exception of ℓ= m = n = 0. Even though we have expressed ϕ in terms of complex exponentials, the value of ϕ is real for real values of x, y and z because of the symmetry of the coeﬃcients, and can therefore also be expressed as a Fourier cosine series.
