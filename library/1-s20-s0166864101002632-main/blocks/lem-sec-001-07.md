Suppose K is a smooth knot with R(K) = 1. Fix x ∈K and let Y be the subset of K consisting of all points y with arc(x,y) ⩾π. Let ν1,ν2,...,νm > 0 be numbers such that ν1 + ν2 + ··· + νm = L −2π (i.e., µ(Y)). Then there exists a sequence of radii 2 ⩽ρ1 ⩽ρ2 ⩽··· ⩽ρm E.J. Rawdon, J. Simon / Topology and its Applications 125 (2002) 97–109 and a partition of Y into measurable sets W1,W2,...,Wm such that (a) for each j, µ(Wj) = νj, and (b) W1 ⊆Y ∩S[2,ρ1] and for each j > 1, Wj ⊆Y ∩S[ρj−1,ρj].
