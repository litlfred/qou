To numerically compute the integer coeﬃcients of the above ˜q-series, we need a high
precision numerical computation of the Laplace integrals. And here lies the issue. In practice,
only a few hundred coeﬃcients of the series ϕ(τ) can be obtained. For instance, for the 41
knot, the stationary phase of the state integral allows one to compute 300 coeﬃcients of
ϕσ1,2(τ), and for the 52 knot about 200 coeﬃcients of ϕσ1,2,3(τ) can be obtained. Alternatively,
