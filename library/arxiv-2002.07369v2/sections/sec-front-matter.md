GR¨OBNER-SHIRSHOV BASES FOR THE COXETER GROUPS
OF THE TYPES G2, F4, E6, E7 AND E8
XIAOWEI PANG∗AND JUN WANG∗∗
Abstract. It is known that the finite Coxeter groups are classified in terms
of Coxeter-Dynkin diagrams. The authors are mainly interest in the Gr¨obner-
Shirshov bases of finite Coxeter groups. In this paper, the authors obtain the
Gr¨obner-Shirshov basis for Coxeter group of type E8, while giving simpler
Gr¨obner-Shirshov bases for the Coxeter groups of the types E6, E7. The new
presentations of En, n = 6, 7, 8 which are given in this paper has enormous
effects in reducing the amount of calculation.
