Abstract

We study the question of whether submodular functions of random variables satisfying
various notions of negative dependence satisfy Chernoff-like concentration inequal-
ities. We prove such a concentration inequality for the lower tail when the random
variables satisfy negative association or negative regression, partially resolving an
open problem raised in ([1]). Previous work showed such concentration results for
random variables that come from specific dependent-rounding algorithms ([2, 3]). We
discuss some applications of our results to combinatorial optimization and beyond. We
also show applications to the concentration of read-k families [4] under certain forms
of negative dependence; we further show a simplified proof of the entropy-method
approach of [4].
