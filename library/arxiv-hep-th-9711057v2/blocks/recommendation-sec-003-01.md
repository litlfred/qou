Here we proceed as follows: Since the polynomial $p_n^{(N_f)}(x,M)$ has to be invariant under permutations of the masses $M_f$ and must have a finite limit when all quark masses are degenerate, a reasonable guess for the normalization function is \begin{eqnarray} C_n^{(N_f)}(M) \ = \ \det\left[L_{n+f-1}^{(0)} (-N\Sigma^2M_{f^\prime}^2)\right]_{f,f^\prime=1,\ldots,N_f}\:, \label{polnorm} \end{eqnarray} see Sec.~\ref{sec23} below, in particular Eq.~(\ref{chefformel}).
