The microscopic spectrum of the QCD Dirac operator with ﬁnite quark masses
T. Wilke1, T. Guhr1, and T. Wettig2
1Max Planck Institut f¨ur Kernphysik, Postfach 103980, D-69029 Heidelberg, Germany
2Institut f¨ur Theoretische Physik, Technische Universit¨at M¨unchen, D-85747 Garching, Germany
(November 10, 1997)
We compute the microscopic spectrum of the QCD Dirac operator in the presence of dynamical
fermions in the framework of random-matrix theory for the chiral Gaussian unitary ensemble. We
obtain results for the microscopic spectral correlators, the microscopic spectral density, and the
distribution of the smallest eigenvalue for an arbitrary number of ﬂavors, arbitrary quark masses,
and arbitrary topological charge.
