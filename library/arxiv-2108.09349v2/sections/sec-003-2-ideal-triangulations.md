a clasp at each end. The 4-braid lives in a product region, S2×I, and its complement in this region
is also a product,S×I, whereS is a 4-punctured sphere. In [9, Appendix], the product region, S×I,
appears between two nested pillowcases and Futer showed that the region can be triangulated using
a sequence of layers of ideal tetrahedra. In Appendix A, we position the product region vertically.
As a result, the faces of Futer’s layered tetrahedra can be easily seen in the braid complement, and
the bottom of one layer is identiﬁed to the top of the next according to the half-twist between them.
