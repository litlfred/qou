GEOMETRIC TRIANGULATIONS OF A FAMILY OF HYPERBOLIC 3-BRAIDS
BARBARA NIMERSHIEM
Abstract. We construct topological triangulations for complements of (−2, 3, n)-pretzel knots
and links with n ≥7. Following a procedure outlined by Futer and Gu´eritaud, we use a theorem
of Casson and Rivin to prove the constructed triangulations are geometric. Futer, Kalfagianni,
and Purcell have shown (indirectly) that such braids are hyperbolic. The new result here is a
direct proof.
