Deﬁnition 3.4. Any symmetry,ρ, of a triangulation,τ, induces a map on the space of positive angle
structures by assigning the angles of T toρ(T ) for all T∈τ. When such an assignment results in a
positive angle structure for all points in A(τ), we say ρ induces a symmetry of the space of positive
angle structures, which we will also denote by ρ. More speciﬁcally, given a symmetry ρ :τ→τ with
ρ(Ti) = Tj, let θ∈A (τ) and deﬁne the coordinates of ρθ by ρθ3j−2 = θ3i−2, ρθ3j−1 = θ3i−1, and
ρθ3j = θ3i. The angles of ρθ are positive and meet Conditions (1) and (2) of Deﬁnition 3.1, so ρ
induces a symmetry of A(τ) whenever Condition (3) is also met.
Observation 3. A symmetry ρ : A(τ) → A(τ) rearranges the angles of θ triple by triple, so
V(θ) =V(ρθ).
