Consider a 3-component link $L=\{L_1, L_2, L_3\}$ in $S^3$ parametrized by $\{\mathsf{x}(s),\mathsf{y}(t),\mathsf{z}(u)\}$ and the following map $$\begin{gathered} \label{eq:F} F_L:S^1\times S^1\times S^1\stackrel{L}{\longrightarrow} \text{Conf}(S^3,3)\stackrel{H}{\longrightarrow} S^2,\qquad L(s,t,u)=(\mathsf{x}(s), \mathsf{y}(t), \mathsf{z}(u))\ . \end{gathered}$$ In order to explain the map $H:\text{Conf}(S^3,3)\longrightarrow S^2$, one must notice that $\text{Conf}(S^3,3)\subset S^3\times S^3\times S^3$ is diffeomorphic to $S^3\times \text{Conf}(\mbox{\bbb R}^3,2)=S^3\times \mbox{\bbb R}^3\times (\mbox{\bbb R}^3\setminus\{0\})$, and consequently deformation retracts onto $S^3\times S^2$.
