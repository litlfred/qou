For the proof of $(ii)$ we must show the following $$\int_\mathcal{T} m_{X_1,X_2,X_3}\,\mu_1\wedge \mu_2\wedge \mu_3=\int_{g(\mathcal{T})} m_{g_\ast X_1,g_\ast X_2, g_\ast X_3}\,\mu_1\wedge \mu_2\wedge \mu_3\ .$$ Theorem [\[th:multi-ergodic\]](#th:multi-ergodic){reference-type="ref" reference="th:multi-ergodic"}, tells us that $m_{X_1,X_2,X_3}$ and $m_{g_\ast X_1,g_\ast X_2, g_\ast X_3}$ admit $L^1$-averages $\bar{m}_{X_1,X_2,X_3}$ and $\bar{m}_{g_\ast X_1,g_\ast X_2, g_\ast X_3}$, under actions of $X_i$ and $g_\ast X_i$ respectively.
