Abstract
We give a simple construction of Markov traces for Iwahori–Hecke algebras associated with the crystallographic
Coxeter groups of types An, Bn and Dn. In types B and D, it is new and generalises a known construction in type A
employing symmetric polynomials in multiplicative Jucys–Murphy elements.
