A over a noncommutative algebra to order 3
In this section we construct jet bundles or more precisely their sections as ‘jet bimodule’
Jk
A for k ≤3 over a potentially noncommutative algebra A equipped with a diﬀerential graded
algebra (Ω, d). These low order cases allow us to see explicitly how the progressively stronger
geometric data emerge and also provide a template for the general case in Section 4. For J 2
A, we
will need to equip Ω 1 with a torsion free connection ∇while for J 3
A and above, this will need to
be ﬂat and obey further conditions. In Section 5, we will extend this construction further to jet
bimodules over a given bimodule E equipped with a ﬂat bimodule connection ∇E. In physical
terms, we restrict in the present section to ‘scalar ﬁelds’ where E =A and ∇E = d.
