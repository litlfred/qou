\label{section:rp}

Given a link $L\subset \mathbb{R}  P^3$ Huynh and Le \cite[Section~5.3.2]{HL08} use Reidemeister torsion corresponding to abelian representations
to define an invariant $\nabla_L(t)$ which lies in general in $\mathbb{Z}[t^{\pm 1}, (t-t^{-1})^{-1}]$ and has no indeterminacy.
Furthermore they show in \cite[Theorem~5.7]{HL08} the surprising fact that this invariant satisfies in fact a skein relation.
