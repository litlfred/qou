The second shuﬄe relations for symmetric elliptic Hodge correlators hold modulo the depth ﬁltration. The lower-depth terms are independent of the base point 푠. The lower-depth correction terms in depth > 2 are not known. In particular, the corrected correlators do not satisfy the second shuﬄe relations in higher depth. However, calculations in low weight support this conjecture. We may expect the elliptic relations to be deformations of the relations for P1 (see §3.4.2).
