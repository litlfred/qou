Structure
In §3.1.2 we explain our results on the level of Hodge correlator integrals.
In §3.2 we establish some properties of motivic correlators on elliptic curves. The main new result of this
section is the dihedral symmetry relation for depth 2 correlators (Theorem 3.8).
In §3.3 we review the deﬁnitions of the Bianchi complexes, deﬁne the modular complexes for imaginary
quadratic ﬁelds, and construct a map between the two in the Gaussian and Eisenstein cases. In §3.3.3 we
combine the results of the two preceding sections to prove the main results relating Bianchi complexes and
the elliptic Galois Lie algebra.
In §3.4, we show how our results generalize those of [G3, G8] and those in Chapter 2.
