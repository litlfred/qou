Let A be a ﬁnite dimensional spherical Hopf algebra. Then the cate- gory of projective left A-modules is a spherical category. This constructs a spherical subcategory with J ﬁnite but we know of no applications. If A is semisimple this result is superﬂuous and in the example of the quantised enveloping algebra of SL(2) given in [Reshetikhin and Turaev 1991] every indecomposable projective has quantum dimension 0 so that J is empty. The category of projective left A-modules is a subcategory of the spherical cat- egory of left A-modules.
