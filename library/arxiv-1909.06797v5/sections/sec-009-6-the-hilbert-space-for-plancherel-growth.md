As we have reviewed in section 2, the unitary matrix models admit a phase space or droplet
description in two dimensions spanned by ( h,θ ). Following [22] we show that the growth of
Young diagrams can be described in terms of evolution of classical or coherent states in a Hilbert
space. The goal of this section is to construct the Hilbert space by quantising the free Fermi
droplets.
