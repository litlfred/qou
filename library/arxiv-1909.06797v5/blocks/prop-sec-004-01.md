It was observed by \cite{LogShe,VerKer77} that when the growth process follows Plancherel transition probability (\ref{eq:transmeasure}) the asymptotic shape of rescaled Young diagrams converges uniformly to a unique curve given by \begin{equation} \label{eq:kerovfunction} \lim_{k \ra \infty}\hat v_k(u)\equiv\Omega(u)= \begin{cases} \frac{2}{\pi}(u \sin^{-1} \frac{u}{2}+\sqrt{4-u^2}) &\mbox{if} \quad |u| \leq 2\\ |u|& \mbox{if} \quad |u|>2. \end{cases} \end{equation}
