For tuples C with no trivial entries we can use simpler e–triangulations of F to deﬁne exponential I–parameters (see Figure 8). Namely, for each of the triangles tv of the base surface S we remove the interior of a monogon inside tv, and triangulate the resulting quadrilateral by adding an edge ev with endpoints v and the b–output vertex of the opposite edge. We extend b by orienting ev from that vertex to v. Proposition 3.4 applies to the cocycle parameters based on such e–triangulations, and the treatment of exponential I–parameters works as well.
