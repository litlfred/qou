For parabolic elements g 2 PSL.2; R/ we have two conjugacy classes, that can be distinguished by a sign. So, replacing PSL.2; C/ with PSL.2; R/, the constructions of this section still work by associating a sign to each parabolic end of the surface S n V . Note that for PSL.2; R/–valued cocycles the idealization procedure described in Section 3.3 below gives only degenerate tetrahedra (with real cross-ratio moduli).
