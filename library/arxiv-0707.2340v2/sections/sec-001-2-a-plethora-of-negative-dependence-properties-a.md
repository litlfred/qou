\label{s2-plet}

In this section we explain in terms of generating 
polynomials the probabilistic notions and negative 
dependence properties studied in e.g.~\cite{L2,P,W}, and we clarify the 
connections between these as well as 
new classes of probability measures that we introduce 
below. We then formulate the problems and conjectures 
made in \cite{L2,P,W} that we solve in the next sections.
