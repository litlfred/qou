THE COLORED JONES POLYNOMIALS OF THE
FIGURE-EIGHT KNOT AND ITS DEHN SURGERY SPACES
HITOSHI MURAKAMI AND YOSHIYUKI YOKOTA
Abstract. We calculate limits of the colored Jones polynomials of the ﬁgure-
eight knot and conclude that in most cases they determine the volumes and
the Chern–Simons invariants of the three-manifolds obtained by Dehn surgeries
along it.
