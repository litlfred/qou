REMARK. Since a typical word in Schreier’s normal form does not
have the symmetries of Corollary 7.1 we conclude that most links of
braid index 3 are neither invertible nor amphicheiral. On the other
hand, since the standard knot tables were organized by crossing num-
ber, the examples which formed the core body of data for early workers
in knot theory had many symmetries.
