PROPOSITION 3.2. There is a natural diagram for L on the bound-

The overcrossing arcs are on OT. The undercrossing arcs are the arcs

Proof. The link L isin OT except for small 6-intervals about each
F-singularity, e.g. [03, 97] in Figure 3.6, when L penetrates into B,.

which penetrates B» is precisely the subarc we called L2(k) in Figure
3.1(b). Figure 3.7 shows how these arcs look on a typical 2-sphere
OB;. They are the connecting threads which join up the digons in a

the decomposition of F into subsets 1’, 1”, 2 and 3 will appear on

It follows immediately that if we stand inside T and look out onto
