1. Preliminaries

We start with describing a main constituent element of hyperbolic 3-
manifolds with boundaries, called a hyperbolic truncated tetrahedron with one ideal
vertex which is a polyhedron in the hyperbolic 3-space H* bounded by three
right-angled pentagons with one ideal vertex, one right-angled hexagon and
three triangles (see Fig. 1.3).
