In 2009, Borcea and Brändén characterized all linear operators on multivariate polynomials
which preserve the property of being non-vanishing (stable) on products of prescribed open
circular regions. We give a representation theoretic interpretation of their ﬁndings, which
generalizes and simpliﬁes their result and leads to a conceptual uniﬁcation of many related
results in polynomial stability theory. At the heart of this uniﬁcation is a generalized Grace’s
theorem which addresses polynomials whose roots are all contained in some real interval
or ray. This generalization allows us to extend the Borcea–Brändén result to characterize a
certain subclass of the linear operators which preserve such polynomials.
