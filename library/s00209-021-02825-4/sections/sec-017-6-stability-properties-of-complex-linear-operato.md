In [ 1], Borcea and Brändén were concerned with classifying the class of weak /Omega1-stability
preserving operators, where /Omega1is some product of open circular regions. What they found is
that an operator preserves weak /Omega1-stability if a particular associated polynomial (what they
called the symbol) is /Omega1-stable. However, the “only if” direction does not necessarily hold. In
particular, there are some weak /Omega1-stability preserving operators for which the corresponding
symbol is not /Omega1-stable. They then showed that this could only happen under very speciﬁc
circumstances: the operator must have image of dimension at most one.
