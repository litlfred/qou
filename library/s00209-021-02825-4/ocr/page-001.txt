Abstract

In 2009, Borcea and Brandén characterized all linear operators on multivariate polynomials
which preserve the property of being non-vanishing (stable) on products of prescribed open
circular regions. We give a representation theoretic interpretation of their findings, which
generalizes and simplifies their result and leads to a conceptual unification of many related
results in polynomial stability theory. At the heart of this unification is a generalized Grace’s
theorem which addresses polynomials whose roots are all contained in some real interval
or ray. This generalization allows us to extend the Borcea—Brindén result to characterize a
certain subclass of the linear operators which preserve such polynomials.

1 Introduction

In 1914, Polya and Schur [14] characterized the set of diagonal linear operators on polyno-

extending this characterization to other classes of linear operators. This program in essence
came to a close in 2009 with a paper of Borcea and Brindén [1], which gave a complete
characterization of linear operators on polynomials which preserve real-rootedness.
