6 Stability properties of complex linear operators

In [1], Borcea and Brandén were concerned with classifying the class of weak (2-stability
preserving operators, where Q is some product of open circular regions. What they found is
that an operator preserves weak Q-stability if a particular associated polynomial (what they
called the symbol) is Q-stable. However, the “only if” direction does not necessarily hold. In
particular, there are some weak {2-stability preserving operators for which the corresponding
symbol is not Q-stable. They then showed that this could only happen under very specific
circumstances: the operator must have image of dimension at most one.
