Let p ∈VC(λ)be both Hm +-stable and Hm −-stable. Then, p is a (complex) scalar multiple of a real stable polynomial. In particular, if nonzero q,r ∈VR(λ) are such that q ≪r and r ≪q, then r is a (real) scalar multiple of q. The next result provides the degeneracy cases in the Borcea–Brändén characterizations (recall the dimension restrictions of Theorems 1.1 and 1.2). We will use this result to explicate the link between our operator characterization and the Borcea–Brändén characterization (see Lemmas 6.11 and 7.3).
