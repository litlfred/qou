Fix a rotation φ ∈SL2(C) with pole (−d : c). The polar derivative dφ then acts on p ∈V (n) in the following way. First, consider the zeros of p as being placed in the Riemann sphere via stereographic projection. Next, rotate the sphere via φ, which moves (−d : c) to inﬁnity at the top of the sphere. Apply the derivative to the new polynomial given by the new locations of the zeros. Finally, undo the original rotation via φ−1, which moves inﬁnity back to the pole (−d : c). J. Leake
