Let λ = (1, 1, 1), denote E := CP1\D, and consider the polynomial p := x1x2x3 −y1y2y3 = Hmgλ(x1x2x3 −1). First, it is easy to see that Dλ(p ⊗p) = 0. Also, p is D3-stable and E3-stable, but it is not D 3-stable nor E3-stable (zero at (xk, yk) = (1, 1) for k ∈[3]). That is, the fact that Dλ(p ⊗p) = 0 does not contradict (ii) or (iii) of the previous theorem. On the other hand, p is both (D × D × D)-stable and (E × E × E)-stable. This shows that (D × D × D, E × E × E) is not a Grace pair. That is, mixed open and closed stability regions cannot be included in (ii) and (iii) of the previous theorem.
