6 (Schur’s Lemma) Let V , V ′, W be representations of a group G, and suppose V , V ′ are irreducible. Then: (i) Any G-invariant map π : W →V is either surjective or the zero map. (ii) Any G-invariant map ι : V →W is either injective or the zero map. (iii) Any G-invariant map ψ : V →V ′ is either an isomorphism or the zero map. Further, if V , V ′ are vector spaces over an algebraically closed ﬁeld, then ψ is unique up to scalar. Applying Schur’s lemma to each of the rth transvectants (discussed at the end of Sect. 3.2) yields the desired representation decomposition.
