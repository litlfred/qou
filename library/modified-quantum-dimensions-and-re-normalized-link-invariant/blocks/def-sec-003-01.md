A E-bimodule is a map M : Ob(E) × Ob(E) →Sets endowed with two operations:  : HomE(Y, Z) × M(X, Y ) →M(X, Z),  : M(Y, Z) × HomE(X, Y ) →M(X, Z), where X, Y and Z are any objects of E. Given morphisms f and g of E and m ∈M we require that: (1) (f ◦g)  m = f  (g  m); (2) m  (f ◦g) = (m  f)  g; (3) (f  m)  g = f  (m  g); whenever the operations in these equalities make sense.
