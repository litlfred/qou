We call M a monoidal E-bimodule if it is endowed with two bilinear operations: : HomE(X, Y ) × M(X′, Y ′) →M(X ⊗X′, Y ⊗Y ′), : M(X, Y ) × HomE(X′, Y ′) →M(X ⊗X′, Y ⊗Y ′) such that for any morphisms f, g and h in E and any m ∈M: (1) (f ⊗g) m = f (g m); (2) m (f ⊗g) = (m f) g; (3) f (m g) = (f m) g; (4) (f ◦g) (h  m) = (f ⊗h)  (g m); (5) (h  m) (f ◦g) = (h ⊗f)  (m g); (6) (f ◦g) (m  h) = (f m)  (g ⊗h); (7) (m  h) (f ◦g) = (m f)  (f ⊗g); whenever the operations in these equalities make sense.
