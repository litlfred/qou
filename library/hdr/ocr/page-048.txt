Chapter 6

Yokonuma—Hecke Algebras

Yokonuma-Hecke algebras were introduced by Yokonuma for the same reason that Ariki-Koike alge-
bras were introduced by Ariki and Koike: as generalisations of Iwahori—Hecke algebras. More precisely,
the Iwahori—Hecke algebra associated to a finite reductive group G is the centraliser algebra associated
to the permutation representation of G with respect to a Borel subgroup of G. The Yokonuma-—Hecke
algebra is the centraliser algebra associated to the permutation representation of G with respect to a
maximal unipotent subgroup of G. Thus, Yokonuma—Hecke algebras can be also regarded as particular
cases of unipotent Hecke algebras.
