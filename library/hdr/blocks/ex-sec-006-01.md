The irreducible representations of the symmetric group Sn are parametrised by the par- titions of n. For W = S3, there are three irreducible representations. Let E(3), E(2,1) and E(1,1,1) denote respectively the trivial, reﬂection and sign representation of S3. Using again the notation of Example 2.1, we have sE(3) = (q2l + 1)(q4l + q2l + 1), sE(2,1) = q2l + 1 + q−2l, sE(1,1,1) = (q−2l + 1)(q−4l + q−2l + 1). We can deﬁne the functions a : Irr(W) →Z and A : Irr(W) →Z by setting a(E) := −valuationq(sE) and A(E) := −degreeq(sE).
