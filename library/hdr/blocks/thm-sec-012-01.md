Let m = (mC,j)(C∈A/W )(0⩽j⩽eC−1) be a family of integers and let ϕm : vC,j 7→qmC,j be the corresponding cyclotomic specialisation. The Rouquier families of Hϕm(W) are unions of the Rouquier families associated with the essential hyper- planes that m belongs to and they are minimal with respect to this property. Thanks to the above result, it is enough to do calculations in a ﬁnite number of cases in order to obtain the families of characters for all cyclotomic Hecke algebras, whose number is inﬁnite.
