By repeating the proof of [CGG, Proposition 4.6] for W = G(l, 1, n), we can obtain that the images of the standard modules via the KZ-functor agree with the Specht modules deﬁned by the cellular structure on the Ariki–Koike algebras in [DJM], provided that the parameters k are chosen to belong to the so-called “asymptotic region” (see [Ro2, Proposition 6.4] for the explicit description of this region).
