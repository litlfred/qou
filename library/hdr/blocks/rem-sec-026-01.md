Let p be a prime number. In a recent series of papers [Vil] [Vi2] [Vi3], Vignéras introduced and studied a large family of algebras, called pro-p-Iwahori—Hecke algebras. They generalise convolution algebras of compactly supported functions on a p-adic connected reductive group that are bi-invariant under the pro-p-radical of an Iwahori subgroup, which play an important role in the p-modular repre- sentation theory of p-adic reductive groups. In we have shown that the affine Yokonuma~Hecke algebra Y(d,00,n) is a pro-p-Iwahori-Hecke algebra.
