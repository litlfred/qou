This interpretation of a particle as made up of two real coupled strings, which

When the original Schrédinger Equation is used with complex wave function,
this internal string-like characteristic is not showing because we treat the real
and imaginary parts as a single entity. However, when the Equation is separated
into two parts, these two parts can be treated as independent strings interacting
with each other where both tension and interaction fall off abruptly inversely
proportional to time. This may be a result of weakening due to increased dis-
tance between the two strings, together with tension drop inside the strings.
