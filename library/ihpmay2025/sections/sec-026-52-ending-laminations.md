Thurston [75] showed that if the compact core of N has incompressible boundary then the
ending lamination of any simply degenerate end is well-defined. He also showed that the end
lamination is filling (intersects every simple closed curve) and minimal (contains no proper
sublamination). His proof relied on the following crucial estimate, whose proof and statement
were corrected by Bonahon [13].
