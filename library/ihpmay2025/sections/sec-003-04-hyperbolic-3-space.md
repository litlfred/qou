generated by these inversions, translations, and dilations, acts transitively on the orthonormal
frame bundle of H3, we see that this group is the full isometry group of H3. In particular, e can
identify Isom+(H3) with PSL(2, C). Therefore, H3 has constant sectional curvature, and since
it contains a totally geodesic copy of H2, the constant is−1.
