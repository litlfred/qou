One may view the conformal boundary of a convex cocompact hyperbolic as capturing the
asymptotic geometry of the 3-manifolds, so from this viewpoint Bers’ parametrization theo-
rems assert that a convex cococompact hyperbolic 3-manifold is determined by its asymptotic
geometry.
One may associate a geodesic lamination to a simply degenerate end, which we call its ending
lamination, which captures the asymptotic geometry. Thurston conjectured that a hyperbolic 3-
manifold is completely determined by its conformal boundary and the ending laminations of its
simply degenerate ends. The proof of the Ending Lamination Theorem verified his conjecture.
