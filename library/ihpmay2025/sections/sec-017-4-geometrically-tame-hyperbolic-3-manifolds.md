A hyperbolic manifold NΓ is said to have no cusps if Γ contains no parabolic elements.
Equivalently, we can say that every non-trivial element of Γ has non-zero translation length
and gives rise to a closed geodesic in NΓ. We will assume from now on, for simplicity, that NΓ
has no cusps. This will only be a technical issue in the results we will discuss, but in their proofs
one must consider manifolds with cusps (so we are hiding important details in some sense). We
will continue to assume that Γ is finitely generated and torsion-free.
There exist many important hyperbolic 3-manifolds without cusps and we will discuss their
topology and geometry in this section.
