IHP LECTURES ON HYPERBOLIC 3-MANIFOLDS
RICHARD D. CANARY
Abstract. These notes are designed to accompany my mini-course on hyperbolic 3-manifolds
at IHP in May 2025. The goal is to develop enough understanding of hyperbolic 3-manifolds
to understand the background statements and some consequences of the Tameness Theorem,
the Ending Lamination Theorem and the Density Theorem. The notes contain much more
material than we will be able to cover in the actual mini-course.
