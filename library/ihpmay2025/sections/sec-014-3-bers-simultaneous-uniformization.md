If S is a closed oriented surface, we call a convex cocompact uniformization of S × [0, 1] (and
its image) quasifuchsian. We also use the simplifying notation
QF(S) = CC0(S × [0, 1]).
Since S × [0, 1] admits an orientation-reversing homeomorphism homotopic to the identity,
we may assume that hρ is orientation-preserving. Therefore, we get a map
B : QF(S) →T (S) × T ( ¯S)
given by
B(ρ) = (∂cNρ, hρ)

where ¯S is S with the opposite orientation. The main goal of this section is to show that B is
a homeomorphism.
Remark: If M is a compact, irreducible 3-manifold and h : S × [0, 1] →M is a homotopy
equivalence, then h is homotopic to an orientation-preserving homeomorphism (see Section 10
of Hempel [39]). It follows that
CC(S × [0, 1]) = CC0(S × [0, 1]).
