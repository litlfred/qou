(Special case of Fellow Traveller Property) Given K ≥1 and C ≥0 there exists R = R(K, C) so that if J is a closed interval in R and f : J →H3 is a (K, C)-quasi-isometric embedding, [a, b] ⊂J and L is a geodesic in H3 joining f(a) to f(b), then the Hausdorff distance between L and f([a, b]) is at most R. Reminder: Suppose that C and D are closed subsets of a metric space Y . We say that the Hausdorff distance between C and D is at most R if both (1) d(c, D) ≤R for all c ∈C, and (2) d(d, C) ≤R for all d ∈D.
