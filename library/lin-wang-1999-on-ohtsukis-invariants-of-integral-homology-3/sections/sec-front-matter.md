Abstract 
We provide some more explicit formulae to facilitate the computation of Ohtsuki's rational 
invariants ,k,~ of integral homology 3-spheres extracted from Reshetikhin-Turaev SU(2) quantum invari- 
ants. Several interesting consequences will follow from our computation of A2. One of them says that 
A2 is always an integer divisible by 3. It seems interesting to compare this result with the fact shown 
by Murakami that )u is 6 times the Casson invariant. Other consequences include some general criteria 
for distinguishing homology 3-spheres obtained from surgery on knots by using the Jones polynomial.

Introduction 
In [1], Ohtsuki extracted a series of rational topological invariants of oriented homology 3- 
spheres from the SU(2) quantum invariants of Reshetikhin and Turaev [2]. Physically, they 
correspond to the coefficients of the asymptotic expansion of Witten's Chern-Simons path 
integral at the trivial connection as shown by Rozansky [3]. The original derivation of these 
invariants, however, seems too complicated to make any practical computation possible. We 
will present here an attempt to conceptualize as well as to simplify the derivation of Ohtsuki's 
invariants. 
The simplified derivation will lead to surgery formulae for Ohtsuki's invariants, 
which are very practical and straightforward when one comes to actual computation. Various 
interesting consequences will follow from our formulae for Ohtsuki's invariants.
