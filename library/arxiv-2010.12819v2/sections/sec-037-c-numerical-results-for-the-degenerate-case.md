Within this Appendix we provide numerical evidence for the results (108) and (111) given in Section 6. Through-
out this Appendix we take x =π, so that q is not a root of unity. We consider states with degenerate conformal
weights h = hr,s and/or ¯h = hr,s. We shall use the same notation |u⟩,|v⟩,|w⟩... for scaling states and the
corresponding states in the continuum.
