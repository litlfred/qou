We define the *free cumulants* $R_2^\lambda,R_3^\lambda,\dots$ as

$$R_{k}^\lambda = \lim_{s\to\infty} \frac{1}{s^{k}}
\Sigma^{s\lambda}_{k-1},$$

in other words each free cumulant is asymptotically the dominant term of the character on a cycle of appropriate length in the limit when the Young diagram tends

to infinity.

One of the reasons why free cumulants are so useful in asymptotic representation theory is that they are homogeneous with respect to dilations of the Young diagrams, namely $$R^{s\lambda}_k = s^k R^{\lambda}_k.$$
