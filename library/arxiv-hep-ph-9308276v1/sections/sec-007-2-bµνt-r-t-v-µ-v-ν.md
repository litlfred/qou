Making use of the equations of motion, the above list can be reduced to eleven independent
operators. In particular, one could eliminate the 11, 12 and 13 term s by redeﬁning the rest of the
terms [10]. However, since we will not restrict ourselves to calculate on-shell matrix elements, we
keep the complete basis given in eq.(12). Among all these 14 terms, o nly the ﬁrst six are truly
needed as counterterms to absorb the new divergent structure s that appear when calculating to
one loop with the non-renormalizable Lagrangian LNL of eq.(7). However, we retain the complete
set to parametrize all the eﬀects of a heavy Higgs to one loop order including both ﬁnite and
divergent contributions.
