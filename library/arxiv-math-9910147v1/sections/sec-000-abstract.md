We study the tensor product of principal unitary representa tions of the quantum Lorentz
Group, prove a decomposition theorem and compute the associ ated intertwiners. We show
that these intertwiners can be expressed in terms of complex continuations of 6 j symbols
of Uq(su(2)). These intertwiners are expressed in terms of q-Racah polyno mials and Askey-
Wilson polynomials. The orthogonality of these intertwine rs imply some relation mixing
these two families of polynomials. The simplest of these rel ations is the orthogonality of
Askey-Wilson Polynomials.
