Abstract
We study the tensor product of principal unitary representations of the quantum Lorentz
Group, prove a decomposition theorem and compute the associated intertwiners. We show
that these intertwiners can be expressed in terms of complex continuations of 6j symbols
of Uq(su(2)). These intertwiners are expressed in terms of q-Racah polynomials and Askey-
Wilson polynomials. The orthogonality of these intertwiners imply some relation mixing
these two families of polynomials. The simplest of these relations is the orthogonality of
Askey-Wilson Polynomials.
