The volume conjecture and its generalization discussed in the previous section imply
that, for generic values of a, the asymptotic behavior of the colored Jones polynomial is
dominated by a ﬂat SL(2,C) connection. This observation forms a very nice and complete
picture once considered along with the Melvin-Morton-Rozansky conjecture regarding the
asymptotic behavior of the colored Jones polynomial for integer values of k. Using path
integral interpretation, in this section we will try to explain this general picture, which
will allow us to formulate an analog of the Melvin-Morton-Rozansky conjecture for the
contribution of a non-trivial ﬂat connection.
