In this section we complete our proof theorem 7.6 by proving the cubic relation for the braid group
generators. We show that two sides of the relations realize the same matrix factorization that has a geometric
interpretation: the left hand side is realized by the extension of the complex of the minimal resolution of the
ideal I121 and the other side for the ideal I212. First we introduce the ideals and discuss their properties.
