The locally closed locus $\calX^{crit}_{\ell,st}$ defined by the condition that there is vector $u\in V$ such that $$\label{eq: cyclic}
 \CC[\Ad_{g_i}(Y_i),X]u=\CC^n.$$ From above discussion we see that the image $\pi_i(\calX^{crit}_{\ell,st})$ is inside $\mathcal{CV}_B^{st}$ because $u_i=g_i^{-1}(u)$ is cyclic $$\CC[Y_i,\tilde{X}_{i,+}]u_i=\CC^n.$$ We denote intersection of $\calX^{crit}_\ell/U^\ell$ with the stable locus by $\calX^{crit}_{\ell,st}/U^\ell$. Let us denote $j_{st}$ the inclusion of the stable locus.

Let us introduce slightly bigger space $\calX^{crit}_{\ell,fr}$ that is a subvariety of $\calX^{crit}_\ell\times V$ consisting of collections of elements $(X,g_1,Y_1,\dots,g_\ell,Y_\ell,u)$ satisfying conditions ([\[eq: cyclic\]](#eq: cyclic){reference-type="ref" reference="eq: cyclic"}). The space $\calX^{crit}_{\ell,fr}$ has a natural action of the group $\hat{G}\times B^{\ell}$, $\hat{G}:=G\times \CC^*$, where $\CC^*$-factor acts on the space $V$ by rescaling. Let us denote by $\St_\ell$ the quotient $\hat{G}\backslash \calX^{crit}_{\ell,fr}/B^\ell$.
