KNOT HOMOLOGY AND SHEAVES ON THE HILBERT SCHEME OF POINTS ON
THE PLANE
ALEXEI OBLOMKOV AND LEV ROZANSKY
Abstract. For each braid β ∈Brn we construct a 2-periodic complex Sβ of quasi-coherent C∗× C∗-
equivariant sheaves on the non-commutative nested Hilbert scheme Hilbfree
1,n .
We show that the triply
graded vector space of the hypercohomology H(Sβ ⊗∧•(B)) with B being tautological vector bundle, is an
isotopy invariant of the knot obtained by the closure of β. We also show that the support of cohomology of
the complex Sβ is supported on the ordinary nested Hilbert scheme Hilb1,n ⊂Hilbfree
1,n , that allows us to
relate the triply graded knot homology to the sheaves on Hilb1,n
