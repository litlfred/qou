[]{#sec: link inv label="sec: link inv"} In this section we explain how for a word $\beta$ of generators of the braid group $\Brgr_n$ one can construct a two-periodic complex $C_\beta$ of quasi-coherent sheaves on the space $\calX_\ell$ with the property that its homology is supported on a version of the Steinberg-like variety $\St_\ell$ that covers the nested Hilbert Scheme $\Hilb_{1,n}$ of $n$-points on $\CC^2$. We show later that $B^\ell\times G$-invariant part $\cohog{*}{C_\beta}$ is related to the isotopy invariant of the link $L(\beta)$. First let us remind a definition of the nested Hilbert scheme.
