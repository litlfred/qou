Notes and references. It is well-known that for any closed orientable
surface Σ g of genus g, the natural map
Mod(Σ g) → Sp(Σ g)
is surjective [MKS, Thm. N13] (see also [Bu, §7], [FM, Thm. 7.3]). One
can then ask, which subgroups H ⊂ Sp(Σ g) lift to Mod(Σ g)? This is a
homological form of the Nielsen realization problem; cf. [K er]. Question
11.1 is related to certain special cases of this question, wh ereH is an abelian
group of the form ⟨S,T ⟩.
