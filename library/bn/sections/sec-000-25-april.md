Abstract
This paper gives an account of the unitary representations of the
braid group that arise via the Hodge theory of cyclic branched cover-
ings of P1, highlighting their connections with ergodic theory, complex
reﬂection groups, moduli spaces of 1-forms and open problems in sur-
face topology.
