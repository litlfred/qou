The image of B4 has inﬁnite index in Sp(X)T 18. Notes and references. It is well-known that for any closed orientable surface Σg of genus g, the natural map Mod(Σg) →Sp(Σg) is surjective [MKS, Thm. N13] (see also [Bu, §7], [FM, Thm. 7.3]). One can then ask, which subgroups H ⊂Sp(Σg) lift to Mod(Σg)? This is a homological form of the Nielsen realization problem; cf. [Ker]. Question 11.1 is related to certain special cases of this question, where H is an abelian group of the form ⟨S, T⟩. A version of question 11.1 for ﬁnite abelian coverings of closed surfaces is addressed in [Lo1].
