Given (r, s) with r > 0, we let Hr,s ∼= U(r, s)/ U(r−1, s) × U(1) denote the space of positive lines in the Hermitian vector space Cr,s. Thus Hr,0 ∼= CPr−1 and H1,s ∼= CHs. In general, Hr,s carries an invariant Her- mitian metric of signature (r −1, s).
