Since \[ t_k \bar t_j t_j \bar t_i(x,f) = (x,fg_{ij}(x)g_{jk}(x)) \] while \[ t_k \bar t_i(x,f) = (x, f g_{ik}(x)) \] we have \[ t_k \tau_j \bar t_i (x,f) \maps (x, fg_{ij}(x)g_{jk}(x)) \to (x,fg_{ik}(x)).\] Since this morphism must be the identity on the first factor, we have \[ t_k \tau_j \bar t_i (x,f) = (1_x, f h_{ijk}(x)) \] where $h_{ijk}(x) \maps g_{ij}(x) g_{jk}(x) \to g_{ik}(x)$ depends smoothly on $x$.
