Abstract. We prove that the N-colored Jones polynomial for the torus
knot Ts, t satisﬁes the second order diﬀerence equation, which reduces to
the ﬁrst order di ﬀerence equation for a case of T2, 2m+1. We show that
the A-polynomial of the torus knot can be derived from the di ﬀerence
equation. Also constructed is a q-hypergeometric type expression of the
colored Jones polynomial for T2, 2m+1.
