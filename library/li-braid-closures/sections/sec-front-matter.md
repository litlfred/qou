Knot polynomials on braid closures
Eddy Li
January 29, 2025
Abstract
We explore properties of the Jones and Alexander polynomials of braid closures
of braids of index 3. We analyze how the evaluations of these polynomials at t = −1
force implications regarding the topological structure of the braid closure, such as its
number of circles or the existence of splittings. As a consequence, we construct an
infinite number of pairs of distinct non-split links that have the same HOMFLYPT
polynomial.
