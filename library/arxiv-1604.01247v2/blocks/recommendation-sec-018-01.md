In the following we shall be concerned with the category of real unital Jordan algebras so, if $J$ is such a unital Jordan algebra, a $J$-bimodule is a unital $J$-module which will be simply refered to as a $J$-module when no confusion arises.\ Let $J$ be a unital Jordan algebra and let us define *its center* $Z(J)$ by $$Z(J)=\{z\in J \vert [x,y,z]=[x,z,y]=0, \forall x,y\in J\} \label{center}$$ where $[x,y,z]=(xy)z-x(yz)$ denotes the associator of $x,y,z\in J$.
