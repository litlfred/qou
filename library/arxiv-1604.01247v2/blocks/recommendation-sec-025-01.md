We shall refer to $\nabla$ as the *covariant differential* while $\nabla_X$ for $X\in {\mbox{Der}}(J)$ will be refered to as the covariant *derivative along* $X$.\ From the axioms ([\[covdiff\]](#covdiff){reference-type="ref" reference="covdiff"}), it follows that one has $$\nabla^2(\omega\Phi)=\omega\nabla^2(\Phi) \label{covcurv}$$ for any $\omega\in \Omega_{\mbox{\tiny Der}}(J)$ and $\Phi\in\Omega_{\mbox{\tiny Der}}(M)$ furthermore $$\nabla^2(m)(X,Y)=R_{X,Y}(m)$$ for $m\in M$.
