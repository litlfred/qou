11. Polynomial type representations of U(∞)
Polynomial representations of U(n) are obtained via q-Schur algebra representations and
are all highest weight modules. In this section, we will see that the U(∞)-modules arising
from weight modules over inﬁnite q-Schur algebras are in general not highest weight modules.
Thus, we obtain a “nonstandard” representation theory for U(∞).
Let r be a positive integer, and let Cr be the full subcategory of C consisting of U(∞, r)-
modules which are weight modules when regarded as U(∞)-modules via ζr : U(∞) →
U(∞, r). We also deﬁne Cpol to be the full subcategory of C consisting of weight U(∞)-
modules M such that wt(M) ⊆N∞. (Recall that the elements of N∞have ﬁnite support.)
We call the objects in Cpol polynomial representations.
