The category K(∞, r)-mod, and hence the category U(∞, r)-mod, is isomorphic to the category S(∞, r)-mod. Thus, S(∞, r)-mod can be regarded as a subcategory of C. We want to classify the irreducible modules in S(∞, r)-mod. Let Ω(∞, r) := S(∞, r)k̟ . Since S(∞, r)kλ = K(n, r)kλ for all λ ∈Λ(∞, r) (see 9.3), it follows that Ω(∞, r) is a weight S(∞, r)-module. Moreover, it is an (S(∞, r),H)-bimodule.
