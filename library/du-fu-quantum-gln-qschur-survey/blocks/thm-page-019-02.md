There is a bijection λ 7→L(λ) between X(∞) and the set of isomorphism classes of irreducible U(∞)-modules in the category Chi (resp., in the category O). The classiﬁcation of irreducible integrable modules seems very hard. However, it is possible to classify all irreducible integrable modules in Chi, i. e., all irreducible modules in Chi ∩Cint. We shall see in the next section that there exist irreducible integrable modules which are not in Chi.
