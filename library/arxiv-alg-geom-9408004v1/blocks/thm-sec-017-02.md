Let D = D(X /M) be the relative Deligne group of a com- plete family of CY threefolds, let eD = D( f X / f M) be the relative Deligne group of the gauged family, and let J , f J be the corresponding relative Jaco- bians. Then there is a natural contact structure κ on D with symplectiﬁcation σ = dτ on eD with the following properties: (a) σ, τ and κ restrict to the previously constructed structures on f J and J . (b) The ﬁbration eD →f M is Lagrangian. (c) All normal functions ν(A( f X / f M)) are integral manifolds for τ (hence isotropic for σ), and normal functions ν(A(X /M)) are integral mani- folds for κ.
