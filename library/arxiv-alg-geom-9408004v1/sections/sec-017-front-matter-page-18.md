The intermediate Jacobian is the connected component of the origin in a
larger group D(X), the Deligne cohomology group of X, cf. [EZ]. This ﬁts
in the exact sequence
0 →J(X) →D(X)
ϕ→H2,2(X, Z) →0,
where H2,2(X, Z) := H2,2(X, C) ∩H4(X, Z). For a Calabi-Yau, this is the
same as H4(X, Z). (The relation of D to J is analogous to that of Pic to
Pic0.) The Abel-Jacobi map A0(X) →J(X) extends to an Abel-Jacobi
(aka cycle-class) map,
ν : A(X) →D(X),
such that ϕ ◦ν : A(X) →H2,2(X, Z) sends a cycle to its fundamental class.
