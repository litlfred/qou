Physicists believe [Gep] that either the A-model or B-model construc-
tions set up bijections between CFTs and CY families. This, together with
the existence of an internal symmetry of CFT which interchanges A and B
variables, led [Di,LVW] to the mirror conjecture, which says that each CY
family X →M, perhaps with some extra data, determines another family
X ′ →M′ such that the A-model CFT of the former is isomorphic to the
B-model CFT of the latter, and vice versa.
It is not clear (to us) what the precise mathematical formulation of this
conjecture should be. What is clear, though, is that the conjecture involves
the existence of an isomorphism, called the mirror map.
