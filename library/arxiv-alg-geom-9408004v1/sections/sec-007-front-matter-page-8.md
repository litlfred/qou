(4) Another rather straightforward extension involves replacing the sym-

2-vector ψ ∈Γ(Λ2TX ) satisfying a closedness condition. Now we start with
an inclusion i : V∗֒ →TU (or α : V ∗⊗OU֒ →TU) and we ask whether
a given family π : X →U of abelian varieties (or complex tori) admits a
Poisson structure ψ such that π∗ψ = 0 on U and which induces the given i.
As before, the answer amounts to a cubic condition,
dp ◦i ∈Γ(Sym3V).
We refer to [DM] for the details.

that this last tensor is in Sym3T ∗U, follows from the previous symmetry
condition, but is in general too weak to imply it.)
