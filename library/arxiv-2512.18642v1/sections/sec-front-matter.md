A Hidden Quantum Markov model framework for
Entanglement and Topological Order in the AKLT Chain

Abstract
This paper introduces a hidden quantum Markov models (HQMMs) framework to the Af-
fleck–Kennedy–Lieb–Tasaki (AKLT) state—a cornerstone example of a symmetry-protected
topological (SPT) phase. The model’s observation system is the physical spin-1 chain, which
emerges from a hidden spin-½ layer through well-defined quantum emission operation. We
show that the underlying Markov dynamics caputure maximal entanglement through the use
of significant channels relevant to the AKLT state. We also show that SPT order induces
a covariance on the observation decoding channels. This establishes an additional bridge
between the quantum Machine learning and many-body physics, with promising implication
in topological order and quantum information.
Keywords AKLT, Hidden Markov Model, SPT Order, Quantum Theory, Entanglement
