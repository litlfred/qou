To estimate the integral $R_n$, we shall adapt the proof of Proposition \ref{prop:berryesseen} to the special case of a sequence $(Z_n)_{n \in \N}$ that converges in the mod-Gaussian sense, with parameters $t_n$, limit function $\exp(Kz^3)$, and with the approximation \begin{equation} \log \varphi_{Z_n}(z) = \frac{t_n\,z^2}{2} + K\,z^3 + O\!\left(\frac{z}{(t_n)^{1/4}}\right) \label{EqLogPhi} \end{equation} that is valid for every $|z| \leq \Delta (t_n)^{1/4}$ with $\Delta>0$.
