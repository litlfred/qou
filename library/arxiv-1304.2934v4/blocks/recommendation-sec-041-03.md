\begin{align} \esper [X_1 \cdots X_r] &= \sum_{\pi \in \qym(r)} \prod_{C \in \pi} \kappa(X_i\,;\, i \in C); \label{eq:cumulant2moment} \\ \kappa (X_1,\dots,X_r) &= \sum_{\pi \in \qym(r)} \mu(\pi) \prod_{C \in \pi} \esper\!\left[ \prod_{i \in C} X_i \right]. \label{eq:moment2cumulant} \end{align} In these equations, $C \in \pi$ shall be understood as ``$C$ is a part of the set partition $\pi$''.
