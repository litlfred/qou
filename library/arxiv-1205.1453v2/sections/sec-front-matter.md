arXiv:1205.1453v2  [math.CA]  9 May 2012
An asymptotic formula of the divergent
bilateral basic hypergeometric series
Takeshi MORITA∗
Abstract
We show an asymptotic formula of the divergent bilateral basic
hypergeometric series 1ψ0(a; −; q, ·) with using the q-Borel-Laplace
method. We also give the limit q →1 −0 of our asymptotic formula.
