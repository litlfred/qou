In the equation [\[bqua\]](#bqua){reference-type="eqref" reference="bqua"}, we put $z=bx$ and take the limit $b\to\infty$, we obtain the equation $$\left\{\left(\frac{1}{q}-ax\right)\sigma_q+x\right\} \tilde{u}(x)=0.\label{bqua2}$$ The formal solution of [\[bqua2\]](#bqua2){reference-type="eqref" reference="bqua2"} is $$\label{b2so}
\tilde{u}(x)={}_1\psi_0(a;-;q,x),$$ which is divergent around the origin. We consider "the basic hypergeometric type" solution around the infinity. We assume that the solution around the infinity is given by $$\tilde{u}_\infty (x):=\frac{\theta (ax)}{\theta (x)}v_\infty (x)=\frac{\theta (ax)}{\theta (x)}\sum_{n\ge 0}v_nx^{-n}, \qquad v_0=1.$$ By the $q$-difference equation of the theta function, we obtain the equation $$\left\{\left(\frac{1}{aq}-x\right)\sigma_q+x\right\}v_\infty (x)=0.$$ We remark that the function $\theta (ax)/\theta (x)$ satisfies the following $q$-difference equation $$u(qx)=q^{-\alpha }u(x)$$ which is also satisfied by the function $u(x)=x^{-\alpha}$ with $\log_qa=\alpha$. We can check out that the series $v_\infty (x)$ is $$v_\infty (x)=e_q\left(\frac{1}{ax}\right)=\frac{1}{\left(1/ax;q\right)_\infty},\qquad \left|\frac{1}{ax}\right|<1.$$ Therefore, one of the solution of [\[bqua2\]](#bqua2){reference-type="eqref" reference="bqua2"} around the infinity is $$\label{b2si}
\tilde{u}_\infty(x)=\frac{\theta (ax)}{\theta (x)}e_q\left(\frac{1}{ax}\right).$$ In the following section, we study the relation between these solutions [\[b2so\]](#b2so){reference-type="eqref" reference="b2so"} and [\[b2si\]](#b2si){reference-type="eqref" reference="b2si"} with using the $q$-Borel-Laplace method of the first kind.

::: rem
We remark that "the bilateral basic hypergeometric type solution" of [\[bqua2\]](#bqua2){reference-type="eqref" reference="bqua2"} around the infinity is given by $$w_\infty (x)={}_1\psi_1\left(0;\frac{q}{a};q,\frac{1}{ax}\right)
=\sum_{n\in\mathbb{Z}}\frac{1}{(q/a;q)_n}\left(\frac{1}{ax}\right)^n.$$ But this solution is not suitable for our argument. We choose and deal with the solution [\[b2si\]](#b2si){reference-type="eqref" reference="b2si"}.
:::
