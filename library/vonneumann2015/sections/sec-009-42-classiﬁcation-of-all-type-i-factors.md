Classiﬁcation of all type I factors
We will classify all type I factors quite easily. We begin with the model,
which we have already seen.

Remark 4.2.2. The importance of being spatial.

our isomorphism using a unitary between the underlying Hilbert spaces. In
general given von Neumann algebras M and N generated by S and T respec-
