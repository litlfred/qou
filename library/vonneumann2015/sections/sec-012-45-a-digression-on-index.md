A digression on index.
If N ⊆M are type I factors we have seen that there is an integer k (possibly
∞) such that M is the algebra of k × k matrices over N. If k < ∞, M is
thus a free left N-module of rank k2. It seems reasonable to call the number
k2 the index of N in M and write it [M : N]. This is because, if H < G
are groups and CH ⊆CG their group algebras, the coset decomposition of
G shows that CG is a free left CH-module of rank [G : H].
