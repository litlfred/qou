Polar decomposition
Exercise 2.3.1. Show that every positive operator a has a unique positive
square root a1/2.
Given an arbitrary a ∈B(H) we deﬁne |a| = (a∗a)1/2.
Exercise 2.3.2. Show that there is a partial isometry u such that a = u|a|,
and that u is unique subject to the condition that its initial domain is ker(a)⊥.
The ﬁnal domain of this u is Im(a) = ker(a∗)⊥.
