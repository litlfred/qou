Exercises on two projections.
Let p and q be projections onto closed subspaces H and K of the Hilbert
space U respectively. Let M = {p, q}′′.
Exercise 7.3.1. Show that U = (H∩K)⊕(H⊥∩K⊥)⊕(H∩K⊥)⊕(H⊥∩K)⊕W
for some W and this decomposition is invariant under p and q.
Exercise 7.3.2. Show that, on W, p and q are in “general position”, i.e.
p ∧q = 0, p ∨q = 1, (1 −p) ∧q = 0 and (1 −p) ∨q = 1.
