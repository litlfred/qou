Tensor product of von Neumann algebras.
If M is a von Neumann algebra on H and N is a von Neumann algebra on
K we deﬁne M ⊗N to be the von Neumann algebra on H ⊗K generated by
{x ⊗y : x ∈M, y ∈N}.
Exercise 4.3.1. Show that M ⊗N contains the algebraic tensor product
M ⊗alg N as a strongly dense *-subalgebra.
