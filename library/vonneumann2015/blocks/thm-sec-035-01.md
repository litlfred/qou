Let A : H →K be a closed, densely deﬁned operator. Then: (i) A∗A and AA∗are positive self-adjoint operators (hence (A∗A)1/2 and (AA∗)1/2 exist). (ii) There exists a partial isometry with initial space Range(A∗A)1/2 and ﬁnal space Range(A) and A = v(A∗A)1/2. (iii) If A = uB for some positive B and partial isometry v with initial space Range(B) then u = v and B = (A∗A)1/2. (iv) In addition A = (AA∗)1/2v.
