Let (A, A1, A2, φ) and (B, B1, B2, ψ) be algebras and states as in deﬁnition 18.5.18, with A1 and A2 free with respect to φ and B1 and B2 free with respect to ψ. Suppose θi are unital *-isomorphisms from Ai to Bi for i = 1, 2, taking φ to ψ. Then there is a unique *-isomorphism from the algebra generated by A1 and A2 onto the algebra generated by B1 and B2 extending θ1 and θ2.
