If S ⊆B(H), we call (S ∪S∗)′′ the von Neumann algebra generated by S. It is, by theorem 3.2.2 the weak or strong closure of the *-algebra generated by 1 and S. Most constructions of von Neumann algebras begin by considering some family of operators with desirable properties and then taking the von Neumann algebra they generate. But is is quite hard, in general, to get much control over the operators added when taking the weak closure, and all the desirable properties of the generating algebra may be lost.
