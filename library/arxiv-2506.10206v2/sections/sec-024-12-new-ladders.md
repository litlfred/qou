We derive a three-term ladder identity, given in [\[previously8p8\]](#previously8p8){reference-type="eqref" reference="previously8p8"} below, with arguments in $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-7})$, and this ladder relation appears to be new. We also introduce a derivation of the Lewin-Loxtin ladders using an elementary analytic approach, which, to the best of our knowledge, is the first derivation of its kind. But finding new ladder relations is difficult, as computer searches and other methods have exhausted the known options.
