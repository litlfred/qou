We derive a three-term ladder identity, given in (39) below, with arguments inQ(√−7), and
this ladder relation appears to be new. We also introduce a derivation of the Lewin-Loxtin
33
ladders using an elementary analytic approach, which, to the best of our knowledge, is the
first derivation of its kind. But finding new ladder relations is difficult, as computer searches
and other methods have exhausted the known options.
