Through the use of the Landen identity in [\[Landenformula\]](#Landenformula){reference-type="eqref" reference="Landenformula"}, and by solving for $u$ and $z$, this leads to: $$\begin{aligned}
\frac{-2u^2 + 3u + \sqrt{-u (3u - 4)}}{4u - 2u^2} = z,
\frac{-2u^2 + 3u - \sqrt{-u (3u - 4)}}{4u - 2u^2} = \frac{z}{z - 1}
\end{aligned}$$ The solutions to $u^3 - 4 u^2 + 3 u + 1 = 0$ correspond to the same root structure as trigonometric values of multiples of $\frac{\pi}{7}$. The valid solution $u=2\cos (\frac{3\pi}{7})+1$ proves one of the Watson two-term identities through some additional manipulations.
