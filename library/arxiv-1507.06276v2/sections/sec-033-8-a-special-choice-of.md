In the following we want to determine the coproduct of the element K ∈ U from
Corollary 7.7. We aim to show that K is a ττ 0-universal K-matrix for Bc,s, that is
that the coproduct ∆(K) is given by (4.16). This, however, will only hold true for
a suitable choice of ξ.
