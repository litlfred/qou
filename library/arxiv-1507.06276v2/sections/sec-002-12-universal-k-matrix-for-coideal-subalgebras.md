Recall that a subalgebra B of Uq(g) is called a right coideal subalgebra if
∆(B) ⊂B ⊗Uq(g).
In the present paper we introduce the notion of a universal K-mat rix for a right
coideal subalgebra B of Uq(g). A universal K-matrix for B is an element K in a
suitable completion U of Uq(g) with the following properties:
(1) In U the universal K-matrix K commutes with all b ∈B.
(2) The relation
