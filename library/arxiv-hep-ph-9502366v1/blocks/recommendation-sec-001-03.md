Since there are eight broken axial generators of the chiral group, Qa A = Qa R −Qa L, there should be eight pseudoscalar Goldstone states |Ga⟩, which we can identify with the eight lightest hadronic states (π+, π−, π0, η, K+, K−, K0 and ¯K0); their small masses being generated by the quark-mass matrix, which explicitly breaks the global symmetry of the QCD Lagrangian.
