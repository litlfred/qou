3 A new proof of Markov’s Theorem

In our proof the closed braid X will be assumed to be the boundary of a
Markov surface F. The central object of study is the pair (X,F) rather than
simply the closed braid X. The reader is referred to §2 for the description
of the stabilizing and destabilizing moves, as they are reflected in changes in
F. They play a central role in our proof.

The new proof of the MT was suggested to us by ideas which were
sketched by D. Bennequin in [2], however his proof made use of the char-
acteristic foliation of a surface bounded by a transverse knot, rather than
the braid foliations which were described in this paper. We read his proof
of the MT many times before we wrote this paper but we only arrived at
our present understanding of it comparatively recently, because of its very
sketchy nature. (We may, however, have misunderstood what he intended
to say.)
