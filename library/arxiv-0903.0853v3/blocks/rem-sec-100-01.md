3.1. — A similar description can be given for free modules of inﬁnite rank. Such a K-module is isomorphic to K(I), and one should replace GLn(K) by GLI(K) and Matp,n(K) by MatJ×I(K). Inﬁnite “matrices” are to be understood as follows: elements of EndK(K(I)) are families (ai,j) of elements of K indexed by I × I and such that each column (j ﬁxed) has ﬁnite support. The set MatI(K) := EndK(K(I)) is a unitary ring and GLI(K) := AutK(K(I)) is its group of units. There is a similar condition for MatJ×I(K).
