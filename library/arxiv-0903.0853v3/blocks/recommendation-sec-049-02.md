We shall now use lemma [\[lemm:reste\]](#lemm:reste){reference-type="ref" reference="lemm:reste"} to prove that (ii) $\Rightarrow$ (i); by linearity, it suffices to check that a series of the form: $$\label{eqn:Salpha} S(z) := \sum_{n\ge 0}\dfrac{\alpha_n}{(z-\lambda q^{-n})^{j+1}}$$ defines a function in space $A_q^\Lambda$, where $\lambda = \lambda_i$ for some $i\in\{1,2,\ldots,m\}$, $0 \leq j < \nu_i$ and where $(\alpha_n)$ denotes a $q$-Gevrey sequence of order $(-\nu)$: there are $C$, $A > 0$ such that: $$\label{eqn:alphan} \forall n \in {\mathbf N}~,~ \left |\alpha_n \right |\leq C A^n \left |q\right |^{-\nu n(n-1)/2}.$$
