::: defi []{#defi:Lambda1Lambda label="defi:Lambda1Lambda"} When $f$ and $\hat f$ satisfy condition [\[eqn:masymptotique\]](#eqn:masymptotique){reference-type="eqref" reference="eqn:masymptotique"}, we shall say that *$f$ admits $\hat f$ as an asymptotic expansion at $0$ along the divisors $(\Lambda_1,\Lambda)$.* If moreover $\hat f$ is the null series, we shall say that *$f$ is flat at $0$ along divisors $(\Lambda_1,\Lambda)$.* :::
