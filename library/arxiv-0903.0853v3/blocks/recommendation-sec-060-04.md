Thus, for $f \in {\mathcal{M}}({\mathbf C}^{*},0)$, and for a finite family $(a_{i})$ of points of ${\mathbf C}^{*}$ and $(n_{i})$ of integers, we shall translate the condition: "The germ $f$ at $0$ has all its poles on $\bigcup [a_{i};q]$, the poles on $[a_{i};q]$ having at most multiplicity $n_{i}$" by an inequality of divisors on ${\mathbf{E}_{q}}$: []{#not86 label="not86"} $${\text{div}}_{{\mathbf{E}_{q}}}(f) \geq - \sum n_{i} [\overline{a_{i}}].$$ We introduce a finite subset of ${\mathbf{E}_{q}}$ defined by resonancy conditions: $$\Sigma_{A_{0}} := \bigcup_{1 \leq i < j \leq k} S_{i,j}, \text{~where~} S_{i,j} := \{p(-a) \in {\mathbf{E}_{q}}~ | ~ q^{{\mathbf Z}} a^{\mu_{i}} \text{Sp}(A_{i}) \cap q^{{\mathbf Z}} a^{\mu_{j}} \text{Sp}(A_{j}) \neq \emptyset\}.$$ Here, $\text{Sp}$ denotes the spectrum of a matrix.
