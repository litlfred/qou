Following [@CZCBHG][^1], one can prove the following formula for the Stokes operators: $$S_{\lambda} \hat{f} - S_{\mu} \hat{f} = (q^{-1};q^{-1})_{\infty}^{2} \dfrac{{\theta_{q}}(-\lambda/\mu) {\theta_{q}}(z/\lambda\mu)} {{\theta_{q}}(-1/\lambda) {\theta_{q}}(-1/\mu) {\theta_{q}}(\lambda/z) {\theta_{q}}(z/\mu)} \sum_{n \in {\mathbf Z}} \dfrac{(-1)^{n} q^{-n^{2}}}{(q^{-1};q^{-1})_{n}} z^{n}.$$ We shall not attempt to prove this, but a similar formula is checked in the next case.\ Taking $a := 0, b := q^{-1}$ yields: $$\hat{f} = \sum_{n \geq 0} q^{n(n+1)/2} z^{n} = \text{\tencyr\cyracc Ch}(q z),$$ which is solution of the $q$-Euler equation $(q z \sigma_q- 1) f = -1$.
