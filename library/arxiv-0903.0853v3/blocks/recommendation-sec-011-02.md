One can then form the Ore ring of difference operators: $$\label{not9} {\mathcal{D}}_{K,\sigma} := K \langle T,T^{-1} \rangle$$ characterized by the twisted commutation relation: $$\forall k \in {\mathbf Z}~,~ x \in K ~,~ T^{k} x = \sigma^{k}(x) T^{k}.$$ We shall rather write somewhat improperly ${\mathcal{D}}_{K,\sigma} := K \langle \sigma,\sigma^{-1} \rangle$ and, for short, ${\mathcal{D}}:= {\mathcal{D}}_{K,\sigma}$ in this section.
