We shall mostly have use for two slightly different forms of them.\ In chapter [\[chapter:asymptotictheory\]](#chapter:asymptotictheory){reference-type="ref" reference="chapter:asymptotictheory"}, we shall use: []{#not6 label="not6"} $$\label{eqn:tripleproduit} \theta(z;q) := \sum_{n\in{\mathbf Z}} q^{-n(n-1)/2} z^n = \prod_{n\in{\mathbf N}}(1 - q^{-n-1})(1 + q^{-n} z)(1 + q^{-n-1}/z).$$ The second equality is Jacobi's celebrated triple product formula.
