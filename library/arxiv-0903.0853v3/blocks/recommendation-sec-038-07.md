Every open subset of ${\mathbf{E}_{q}}$ is the horizon of some $\Sigma$-invariant open subset of ${\mathbf C}^*$ (actually, of a great many of them !) and, from now on, we shall often write $U_{\infty}$ an arbitrary open subset of ${\mathbf{E}_{q}}$ and $U$ some adequate $\Sigma$-invariant open subset of ${\mathbf C}^*$ such that $U_{\infty} := p(U)$.\ We introduce three sheaves on ${\mathbf{E}_{q}}$, also denoted ${\mathscr{A}}$, ${\mathscr{A}}_{0}$ and ${\mathscr{B}}$; no confusion should be caused by this overloading of notations.
