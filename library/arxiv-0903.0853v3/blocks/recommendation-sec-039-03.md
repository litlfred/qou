(We keep the letters $U$ and $K$ to denote respectively a $\Sigma$-invariant open subset of ${\mathbf C}$ and an invariant strict subset of $U$.) We shall use the following standard conventions; for $k := (k_1,k_2) \in {\mathbf N}^2$, we set: $$f^{(k)} := \dfrac{\partial^{k_1}}{\partial x^{k_1}} \dfrac{\partial^{k_2}}{\partial y^{k_2}} \,f \cdot$$ We also write $k ! := k_1 ! k_2 !$, $(x,y)^k := x^{k_1} y^{k_2}$, $\left |k \right |:= k_1 + k_2$, etc.
