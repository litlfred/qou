Before describing the contents of the paper in the following paragraph, we shall first say briefly what it could contain but does not.\ The kernel of the present work is the detailed proofs of some results announced in [@RSZ1], [@RSZ2] and in various seminars and conferences between 2004 and 2006, but there are also some new results in the same spirit and some examples.\ In this paper, we limit ourselves to the case $\left |q\right |\neq 1$.
