Its coefficients satisfy the equations: $$b z \sigma_qf - a f = u \text{~and~} c z \sigma_qh - b h = w.$$ (The coefficient $x$ is irrelevant here.) We shall see in section [\[section:qEuler\]](#section:qEuler){reference-type="ref" reference="section:qEuler"} how to compute the corresponding cocycles $(f_{c,d})$ and $(h_{c,d})$, but what is clear here is that they are respectively linear functions of $u$ and of $w$.
