Last, two fuchsian modules (Kn, ΦA), (Kn′, ΦA′) with A ∈GLn(C),
A′ ∈GLn′(C) having all their eigenvalues in the fundamental annulus are
isomorphic if and only if the matrices A, A′ are similar (so that n = n′).
It follows that any pure isoclinic module of slope µ is equivalent to some
module (Kn, ΦzµA) with A ∈GLn(C), the matrix A having all its eigen-
values in the fundamental annulus; and that two such modules (Kn, ΦzµA),
(Kn′, ΦzµA′) are isomorphic if and only if the matrices A, A′ are similar.
