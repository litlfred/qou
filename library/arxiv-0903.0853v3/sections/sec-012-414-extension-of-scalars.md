(K, σ) consists in an extension K′ of K and an automorphism σ′ of K′ which
restricts to σ on K. Any diﬀerence module M = (E, Φ) over (K, σ) then gives
rise to a diﬀerence module M′ = (E′, Φ′) over (K′, σ′), where E′ := K′ ⊗K E
and Φ′ := σ′ ⊗Φ is deﬁned the same way as above. We then write ΓK′(M)
the K′σ′-vector space Γ(M′).
