An important particular object is the *unity* []{#not15 label="not15"} $\underline{1}$, which may be described either as $(K,\sigma)$ or as ${\mathcal{D}}/{\mathcal{D}}P$ with $P = \sigma - 1$. For any difference module $M = (E,\Phi)$ the $K^{\sigma}$-vector space $\text{Hom}(\underline{1},M)$ can be identified with the kernel of the $K^{\sigma}$-linear map $\Phi - Id: E \rightarrow E$; in case $M =(K^{n},\Phi_{A})$, this boils down to the space $\{X \in K^{n} ~ | ~\sigma X = A X\}$ of solutions of a "$\sigma$-difference system"; whence our definition of $\Phi_{A}$. The *functor of solutions* $M \leadsto \Gamma(M) := \text{Hom}(\underline{1},M)$, from the category $DiffMod(K,\sigma)$ to the category of finite dimensional (see remark [\[rema:fibrefunctors\]](#rema:fibrefunctors){reference-type="ref" reference="rema:fibrefunctors"}) vector spaces over $K^{\sigma}$ is left exact and $K^{\sigma}$-linear. We shall have use for its right derived functors $\Gamma^{i}(M) = \text{Ext}^{i}(\underline{1},M)$ (*see* [@BAH]).
