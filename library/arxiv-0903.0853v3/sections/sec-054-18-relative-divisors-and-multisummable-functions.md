[]{#section:relativedivisorsandmultisummable label="section:relativedivisorsandmultisummable"}

Theorems [\[theo:BorelRitt\]](#theo:BorelRitt){reference-type="ref" reference="theo:BorelRitt"} and [\[theo:Lambdaplate\]](#theo:Lambdaplate){reference-type="ref" reference="theo:Lambdaplate"} show that, for each divisor $\Lambda$ with degree $\nu \in {\mathbf N}^*$, the datum of a $q$-Gevrey series of order $1/\nu$ is equivalent to the datum of an element in space ${\mathbb A}_q^\Lambda$ together with a function of the form $\dfrac{h}{\theta_\Lambda}$ for some $h \in {{\mathbf C}(\{z\})}$. When studying $q$-difference equations, we shall observe that the function $h$ in the correction term $\dfrac{h}{\theta_\Lambda}$ will have to be determined in some space of asymptotic functions similar to ${\mathbb A}_q^\Lambda$, and that is why we are now going to define a notion of asymptoticity involving more than one divisor level.
