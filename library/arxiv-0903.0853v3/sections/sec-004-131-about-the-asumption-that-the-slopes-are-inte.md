[]{#subsubsection:integralslopes label="subsubsection:integralslopes"}

The "abstract" part of our paper does not require any assumption on the slopes : that means general structure theorems, *e.g.* theorem [\[theo:schemadesmodules\]](#theo:schemadesmodules){reference-type="ref" reference="theo:schemadesmodules"} and proposition [\[prop:schemadesmodulesqGevrey\]](#prop:schemadesmodulesqGevrey){reference-type="ref" reference="prop:schemadesmodulesqGevrey"}. The same is true of the decomposition of the local Galois group of an irregular equation as a semi-direct product of the formal group by a unipotent group in [@RS1; @RS2].\
However, all our *explicit* constructions (Birkhoff-Guenther normal form, privileged cocycles, discrete summation) rest on the knowledge of a normal form for pure modules, that we have found only in the case of integral slopes.\
In [@vdPR] van der Put and Reversat classified pure modules with non integral slopes. The extension of our results to the case of non integral slopes, using [@vdPR], does not seem to have been done - and would be very useful[^1].

[^1]: Long after the present work had been submitted for publication, this task was undertaken by Virginie Bugeaud, see [@VB].
