Let $M = (E,\Phi)$ and $N = (F,\Psi)$. The map $T_{\Phi,\Psi}: f \mapsto \Psi \circ f \circ \Phi^{-1}$ is a semi-linear automorphism of the $K$-vector space $\mathcal{L}_{K}(E,F)$, whence a difference []{#not16 label="not16"} module $\underline{\text{Hom}}(M,N) := \bigl(\mathcal{L}_{K}(E,F),T_{\Phi,\Psi}\bigr)$. Then one has $\underline{\text{Hom}}(\underline{1},M) = M$ and $\Gamma\bigl(\underline{\text{Hom}}(M,N)\bigr) = \text{Hom}(M,N)$. The *dual* of $M$ is $M^{\vee} := \underline{\text{Hom}}(M,\underline{1})$, so that $\text{Hom}(M,\underline{1}) = \Gamma(M^{\vee})$. For instance, the dual of $M =(K^{n},\Phi_{A})$ is $M^{\vee} =(K^{n},\Phi_{A^{\vee}})$, where $A^{\vee} := {}^{t} A^{-1}$.
