We should mention that $\fu$ and~$\fv$ can be defined invariantly, without using any triangulation, as follows: the deformed hyperbolic structure on~$M$ corresponds to a homomorphism $\rho:\G\to\PSL_2(\C)$ near to the inclusion map, and then $\fu$ and~$\fv$ are simply the logarithms of the ratios of the eigenvalues of the images under~$\rho$ of the meridians and longitudes, respectively.
