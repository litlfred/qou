On the other hand, as described at the end of the last section, the hyperbolic volume should actually be seen as the imaginary part of a complexified volume taking values in~$\C/4\pi^2\Z$, so we would like to replace the function~$D(z)$ by some complex-valued version of the dilogarithm which, even though it may be many-valued at individual arguments~$z$, becomes one-valued modulo~$4\pi^2$ if we take a linear combination of its values with arguments belonging to the Bloch group.
