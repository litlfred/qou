We study the determinants of Toeplitz matrices as the size of the matrices tends
to inﬁnity, in the particular case where the symbol has two jump discontinuities and
tends to zero on an arc of the unit circle at a suﬃciently fast rate. We generalize an
asymptotic expansion by Widom [22], which was known for symbols supported on
an arc. We highlight applications of our results in the Circular Unitary Ensemble
and in the study of Fredholm determinants associated to the sine kernel.
