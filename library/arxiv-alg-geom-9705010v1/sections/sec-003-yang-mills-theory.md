This Lagrangian is considered as a functional on an appropriate space

inﬁnity for the integral to converge). The equation dF = 0 is again the Bianchi
identity, while d ∗F = 0 is the equation of the critical locus of L. Either from the
Lagrangian description or directly from the equations, it is clear that the symmetry
group includes global isometries of X which lift to V (“the Poincare group”) as well
as local diﬀeomorphisms of the bundle (“the gauge group”) and, in dimension 4,
the duality F 7→∗F. In the original model of Yang and Mills, the group G was
SU(2), while in QCD it is SU(3). For any “realistic” model of all of particle physics,
G would need to contain at least U(1) × SU(2) × SU(3).
