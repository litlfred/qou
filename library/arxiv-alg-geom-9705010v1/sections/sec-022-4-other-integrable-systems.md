Having studied in some detail the system which solves one particular family of
SYM theories, we proceed to consider what is known for some of the other families.
Our discussion will include,
• The pure N = 2 SYM for arbitrary gauge group G, and its relation to the
Toda systems.
• The situation when fundamental matter is added to the pure theory.
