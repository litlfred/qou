Adding matter
In a quantum ﬁeld theory, a matter particle is represented by a fermion ﬁeld
ψ which, in the case of a gauge theory, needs to be in some repres entation of G.
It is added (or: coupled) to the theory by adding to the YM Lagr angian a term
corresponding to the Lagrangian of the free particle (this i ncludes a parameter, the
particle mass m), and another term corresponding to its interaction with th e YM
ﬁeld. In SYM the ﬁeld needs to be in a representation ρ of G, tensored with an
N = 2 multiplet, to preserve the SUSY. (The relevant multiplet , which is built from
