V.
433 AT COLLIDERS
We have discussed above that the gauge theory based on
SU(4)C ⊗SU(3)L ⊗SU(3)R predicts the existence of lepto-
phobic gauge bosons and of stable colored or electric charged
fermionic ﬁelds. See Ref. [27] for the current experimental
bounds on the leptophobic gauge boson assuming that it de-
cays only into SM quarks, Ref. [28] for the searches of vector-
like fermions and Ref. [29] for the signatures of stable charged
particles.
