Abstract. W e extend partition-theoretic work of Andrews, Bressoud, a nd Burge to overpartitions, deﬁning
the notions of successive ranks, generalized Durfee square s, and generalized lattice paths, and then relating
these to overpartitions deﬁned by multiplicity conditions on the parts. This leads to many new partition
and overpartition identities, and provides a uniﬁcation of a number of well-known identities of the Rogers-
Ramanujan type. Among these are Gordon’s generalization of the Rogers-Ramanujan identities, Andrews’
generalization of the G¨ ollnitz-Gordon identities, and Lo vejoy’s “Gordon’s theorems for overpartitions.”
