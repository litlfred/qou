SUMMABILITY FOR STATE INTEGRALS OF HYPERBOLIC KNOTS
VERONICA FANTINI AND CAMPBELL WHEELER
To Maxim Kontsevich, on the occasion of his 60th birthday
Abstract. We prove conjectures of Garoufalidis-Gu-Mari˜no [9] that perturbative series
associated with the hyperbolic knots 41 and 52 are resurgent and Borel summable. In the
process, we give an algorithm that can be used to explicitly compute the Borel–Laplace
resummation as a combination of state integrals of Andersen–Kashaev [1]. This gives a
complete description of the resurgent structure in these examples and allows for explicit
computations of Stokes constants.
