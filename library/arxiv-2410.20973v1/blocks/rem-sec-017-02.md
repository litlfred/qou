4. The previous remark about the function Lθ also applies to the quantum dilogarithm. An easy way to see that Dθ is the correct branching of the multivalued function Li2(e(z))/(2πi)2 is that the zeros and poles of Φ(zτ; τ) accumulate to the lines C\Cθ in the limit as τ tends to inﬁnity. Here the most important point is that this domain remains connected for all θ ∈(0, 2π), which was not true for the lone Pochhammer symbol.
