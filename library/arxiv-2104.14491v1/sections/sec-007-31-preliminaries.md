By definition, a positive polynomial is a Laurent polynomial in $q$ and $t$ with non-negative coefficients. Moreover, in our cases these coefficients are integers by construction. Throughout the text $$\begin{aligned}
\{x\}=x-x^{-1}.\label{figx}
\end{aligned}$$ The jumps in the Khovanov polynomials at critical points of the evolution flow are captured by the sign and step functions, $$\begin{aligned}
\mathtt{sgn}_x=\left\{\begin{array}{cl}-1,&x<0\\0,&x=0\\1,&x>0\end{array}\right.,\ \ \
\Theta_x=\left\{\begin{array}{cl}0,&x\le0\\1,&x> 0\end{array}\right..
\end{aligned}$$
