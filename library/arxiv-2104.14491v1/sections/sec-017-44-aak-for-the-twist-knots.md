AAK for the twist knots
The simplest twist knots (with 1,2,3 half-twist) are the prime knots 31, 52, 72 (positive half-twists), and
41, 61, 81 (negative half-twists). The AAK for the up to 2 half-twists are explicitly given in (26). Below
we present the explicit evolution formulae for the entire twist family Twn (an even integer n is the crossing
number, not to mix the half-twist number l = ±n−2
2 ). Both decompositions (16) and (15) give the same
AAK. The obtained formulae describe a “smooth” exponential dependence on n in each of the domains
n < 0 and n > 0, aside the critical jumps at the unknot n = 0.
