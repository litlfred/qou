Now one can combine companion-evolution formulae ([\[tor-evo\]](#tor-evo){reference-type="ref" reference="tor-evo"},[\[tw-evo\]](#tw-evo){reference-type="ref" reference="tw-evo"}) with pattern-evolution formulae ([\[ppcd-tw-sat\]](#ppcd-tw-sat){reference-type="ref" reference="ppcd-tw-sat"},[\[ppcd-tor-sat\]](#ppcd-tor-sat){reference-type="ref" reference="ppcd-tor-sat"},[\[ppcd-u-tor-sat\]](#ppcd-u-tor-sat){reference-type="ref" reference="ppcd-u-tor-sat"}) to obtain double-evolution diagrams as in [@DBPev; @APev]. A subtle point is that the invariant pattern-evolution variable $k$ is related to the parameters of the knot diagram via a companion-dependent expression, as we discussed in sec.[\[sec:sat\]](#sec:sat){reference-type="ref" reference="sec:sat"}. If the companion's crossing number $n$ and the pattern's half-twist number $w$ are chosen as variables, the critical values of the pattern lie on a line determined by the relevant $\mathtt{Sh}_{\mathcal{K}}$ from ([\[ShKn\]](#ShKn){reference-type="ref" reference="ShKn"}) for the companion family we consider. In addition, there are critical values of the companion evolution (when the companion is the unknot). Altogether the critical lines divide the parametric plane into four evolution domains, as shown in fig.[\[fig:dbsat\]](#fig:dbsat){reference-type="ref" reference="fig:dbsat"}.
