The unreduced (label $u$) AAK polynomials for the two-strand torus knots have the following form $$\begin{aligned}
\mathfrak{K}^{\mathtt{Tor}_n}&=&t\left(q^2t\right)^{\mathtt{Sh}_{\mathtt{Tor}_n}\!\!-4n}\hspace{-5mm}\sum_{\lambda=1,q^2t,q^6t^4}\hspace{-5mm}C^{\mathtt{Tor}_{\mathtt{sgn}_n}}_{\lambda}\lambda^{n-\mathtt{sgn}_n},\\
{_u}\mathfrak{K}^{\mathtt{Tor}_n}&=&\left(q^2t\right)^{\mathtt{Sh}_{\mathtt{Tor}_n}}\Big(q^{n-\mathtt{Sh}_{\mathtt{Tor}_n}}\left(q^{-1}+q\right)+q^{-2}t^{-\Theta_n}\left(1+q^4t\right)
\left(q^2t\right)^{-4n}\hspace{-5mm}\sum_{\lambda=1,q^2t,q^6t^4}\hspace{-5mm}{_u}C^{\mathtt{Tor}_{\mathtt{sgn}_n}}_{\lambda}\lambda^{n-\mathtt{sgn}_n}\Big).



\label{tor-evo}
\end{aligned}$$ Here $$\begin{aligned}
\sum_{\lambda=1,q^2t,q^6t^4}\hspace{-5mm}C^{\mathtt{Tor}_{\mathtt{sgn}_n}}_{\lambda}=0,\hspace{1cm}
\sum_{\lambda=1,q^2t,q^6t^4}\hspace{-5mm}{_u}C^{\mathtt{Tor}_{\mathtt{sgn}_n}}_{\lambda}=-(q^2t)^{-4\mathtt{sgn}_n}.
\end{aligned}$$ The coefficients for the reduced polynomials explicitly equal $$\begin{aligned}
\begin{array}{|c|c|c|c|}
    \hline\rule{0pt}{4mm}
    \lambda&1&q^2t&q^6t^4\\
    \hline\rule{0pt}{7mm}
    \grave{C}_{\lambda}^{\mathtt{Tor}}&\cfrac{q^2t}{(1-q^2t)(1-q^6t^4)}&-(q^6t^4)^{-\Theta_n}\cfrac{1+q^{12}t^8}{(1-q^2t)(1-q^8t^6)}&\cfrac{1+q^{14}t^9}{q^2t(1-q^6t^4)(1-q^8t^6)}\\[3mm]
    \hline\rule{0pt}{7mm}
    \grave{\grave{C}}^{\lambda}_{\mathtt{Tor}}&0&-(q^6t^4)^{-\Theta_n}\cfrac{q^2t^2(1+q^6t^3)}{(1-q^4t^2)(1+q^4t^3)}&-\cfrac{q^2t^2}{1-q^8t^6}\\[3mm]
    \hline
\end{array}
\end{aligned}$$ The unreduced coefficients are expressed via reduced ones as (for $n<0$) $$\begin{aligned}
\begin{array}{|c|c|c|c|}
    \hline\rule{0pt}{4mm}
    \lambda&1&q^2t&q^6t^4\\
    \hline\rule{0pt}{7mm}
     C_{\lambda}^{\mathtt{Tor}_-}-{_u}C_{\lambda}^{\mathtt{Tor}_-}&\cfrac{q^4t^3}{1-q^6t^4}&\cfrac{1+q^2t^2}{1+q^4t^3}&
     q^{-6}t^{-4}\left(q^{-2}\!+\!1\right)-\cfrac{1+q^6t^4}{q^6t^4(1+q^4t^3)}-\cfrac{1}{q^2t(1-q^6t^4)}\\[3mm]
     \hline
\end{array}
\end{aligned}$$
