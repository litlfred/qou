We call the invariant $\mathfrak{K}^{\mathcal{K}}$ an **asymptotic adjoint Khovanov**/**AAK** polynomial. It is *almost* the adjoint/symmetric Khovanov polynomial. Namely, the polynomial $$\begin{aligned}
\widehat{\mathtt{Kh}}^{\mathcal{K}}_k(q,t)=
(-t)^{-1}\left(q^2t\right)^{-\mathtt{Sh}_{\mathcal{K}}}\Big((-t)^{\Theta_k}-\left(1-q^4t^2\right){\mathfrak{K}}^{\mathcal{K}}(q,t)\Big)\label{FAK}
\end{aligned}$$ stands for the symmetric polynomial in ([\[ppcd-tw-sat\]](#ppcd-tw-sat){reference-type="ref" reference="ppcd-tw-sat"},[\[ppcd-tor-sat\]](#ppcd-tor-sat){reference-type="ref" reference="ppcd-tor-sat"}) when one *naively* compares the decompositions of the satellite polynomials over the coloured ones in the Khovanov and in the HOMFLY cases. In the particular case $t=-1$ one gets the first coloured Jones, $$\begin{aligned}
\widehat{\mathtt{Kh}}^{\mathcal{K}}_k(q,t=-1)=J_2(q)\label{J-Kh-col}
\end{aligned}$$ The $\widehat{\mathtt{Kh}}^{\mathcal{K}}_k$ is *not* a positive polynomial, but $\widehat{\mathtt{Kh}}^{\mathcal{K}}_k/(1-q^4t^2)$ is expanded into a positive Laurent series in $t^{\mathtt{sgn}_k}$. The unreduced counterpart equals $$\begin{aligned}
{_u}\widehat{\mathtt{Kh}}^{\mathcal{K}}_k(q,t)=\left(q^2t\right)^{-\mathtt{Sh}_{\mathcal{K}}}\Big(q^{-2}(-t)^{\Theta_k}+{{_u}\mathfrak{K}}^{\mathcal{K}}(q,t)\Big)
\label{TBAKu}
\end{aligned}$$ and satisfies $$\begin{aligned}
{_u}\widehat{\mathtt{Kh}}^{\mathcal{K}}_k(q,t=-1)=\textstyle{\frac{1+q^2+q^4}{q^2}}J_2(q).\label{Ju-Khu-col}
\end{aligned}$$ The non-positivity of ([\[FAK\]](#FAK){reference-type="ref" reference="FAK"}) can imply that the satellite polynomial, which is the Poincare polynomial of the corresponding Khovanov complex, does not contain *all* "trivial-representation" generators (and hence becomes sign-indefinite when we naively subtract them all).
