The unreduced Khovanov polynomials are expectedly more complicated compared to the reduced ones. In particular, one must extract an "extra" exceptional pair in ([\[ppcd-u-tor-sat\]](#ppcd-u-tor-sat){reference-type="ref" reference="ppcd-u-tor-sat"}), and our unreduced formulae are invalid for the unknot. The simple quantum group relations are restored for $t=-1$, $$\begin{aligned}
\frac{\mathtt{Kh}_u^{\mathcal{K}}(q,t=-1)}{\mathtt{Kh}(q,t=-1)}=\frac{\widehat{\mathtt{Kh}}_u^{\mathcal{K}}(q,t=-1)}{\widehat{\mathtt{Kh}}^{\mathcal{K}}(q,t=-1)}=\frac{q^4+q^2+1}{q^2}.
\end{aligned}$$
