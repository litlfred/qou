Precaution
The program from [35] seems to work incorrectly for some knot diagrams. The simplest example is the
diagram X[1, 2, 2, 1] that represents the twisted unknot. This can be usually coped with by inserting a long
enough trivial two-strand braid of the form (1, −1, 1, −1, . . .) (converted to a Gauss diagram).
