We have analyzed the structure of the functional Z which generates the Green 
functions of the quark currents and of the operator tr (G,,,(~""). The Ward identities 
associated with chiral U(Nr)xU(NI) specify the manner in which this functional 
transforms under local chiral transformations of the external fields, if the quark 
loops did not generate anomalies, Z would be invariant under these transformations. 
The anomalies imply that an infinitesimal chiral transformation produces a specific, 
explicitly known change in Z, given by a polynomial of fourth order in the external 
vector and axial vector fields. (The anomalous contribution from the gluon operator 
tr (G,~G "~) is accounted for by a change in the vacuum angle which is also treated 
as an external field.) 
We construct the general solution of the Ward identities by power series expansion 
in the external momenta and in the masses of the three light quarks u, d and s. The 
leading contribution in this expansion is governed by classical field theory: the 
leading low-energy representation of the generating functional [O(p2, ~ff)] is given 
by the classical (unquantized) action of the nonlinear g-model which is characterized 
by two constants Fo and Bo. The main result of the paper is an explicit representation 
of the generating functional at first nonleading order in the low-energy expansion
