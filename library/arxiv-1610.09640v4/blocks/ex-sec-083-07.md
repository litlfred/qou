Let A be a DG ring such that H(A) is nonzero, and consider the free DG module P := A ∈D(A). The functor F := RHomA(P, −) : D(A) →D(K) is isomorphic to the forgetful functor, so it has cohomological displacement [0, 0] and cohomological dimension 0. Thus the DG module P has projective concentration [0, 0] and projective dimension 0. Note however that the cohomology H(P) could be unbounded!
