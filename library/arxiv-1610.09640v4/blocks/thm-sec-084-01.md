Let A be a left noetherian ring, let N be an abelian category, let ⋆ be a boundedness indicator, let F, G : D⋆ f (A) →D(N) be triangulated functors, and let ζ : F →G be a morphism of triangulated functors. Assume that the morphism ζA : F(A) →G(A) in D(N) is an isomorphism. (1) If ⋆= −, and if F and G have bounded above cohomological displacements, then ζM : F(M) →G(M) is an isomorphism for every M ∈D− f (A). Derived Categories | Amnon Yekutieli |
