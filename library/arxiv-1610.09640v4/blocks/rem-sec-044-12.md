The story of the concept “saturated full triangulated subcate- gory” is convoluted. In the text [155] from 1977, J.-L. Verdier introduced épaisse full triangulated subcategories (a rather messy deﬁnition). However, J. Rickard proved (see [125, Proposition 1.3], published in 1989) that épaisse is equivalent to saturated, as deﬁned above. Rickard did not give a name for this new and useful property. Verdier died in 1989. A few years later, in 1996, G. Maltsiniotis published Verdier’s thesis [156].
