Assume A is a commutative DG ring (Deﬁnitions 3.1.22 and 3.3.4), with reduction ¯A = H0(A). Here we do not need to assume ﬂatness over the base ring K. A DG A-module can be viewed as a central DG A-bimodule, so we have a derived tensor product (−⊗L A −) : D(A) × D(A) →D(A). This operation is symmetric, i.e. S ⊗L A T ∼= T ⊗L A S. A DG A-module T is called a tilting DG A-module if there is some S ∈D(A) such that S ⊗L A T ∼= A. The commutative derived Picard group of A is the group DPicA(A), whose elements are the isomorphism classes in D(A) of the tilting DG Derived Categories | Amnon Yekutieli |
