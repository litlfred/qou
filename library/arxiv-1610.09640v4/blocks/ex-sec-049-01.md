Suppose A has a single object, say x. Here is what the deﬁnition above says in this case. We have a ring A := A(x, x) and a multiplicatively closed set S := S(x, x) ⊆A. The localization of A with respect to S is a ring AS := AS(x, x), with a ring homomorphism Q : A →AS, such that Q(S) ⊆(AS)×, the group of invertible elements of AS. Condition (Loc3) says that any ring homomorphism F : A →B such that F(S) ⊆B× factors uniquely through AS.
