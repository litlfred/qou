Let M be a graded K-module. Then the graded module EndK(M) := HomK(M, M) = M i∈Z HomK(M, M)i, with the operation of composition, is a graded K-ring. The prototypical manifestation of the Koszul sign rule is the next deﬁnition. Deﬁnition 3.1.15. Let A = L i∈Z Ai be a graded K-ring. We say that A is a weakly commutative graded ring if b·a = (−1)i·j ·a·b for all a ∈Ai and b ∈Aj.
