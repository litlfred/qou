Let A be any DG ring. We will prove later that K(A) has enough K-projectives. Therefore, given a triangulated functor F : K(A) →E into a triangulated category E, the left derived functor (LF, ηL) : D(A) →E exists. Suppose we choose a system of K-projective resolutions (P, ρ) in K(A). Then we get a presentation of (LF, ηR) as follows: LF(M) := F(P(M)) and ηL M := F(ρM).
