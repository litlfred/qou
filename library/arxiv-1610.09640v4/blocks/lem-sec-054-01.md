Suppose φ, ψ : M →N are morphisms in Cstr(A, M) such that ψ −φ is a coboundary in HomA,M(M, N). Then H(φ) = H(ψ), as morphisms H(M) →H(N) in Gstr(M). Exercise 7.2.2. Prove the lemma. It follows that there is an induced functor (7.2.3) H : K(A, M) →Gstr(M). We shall be interested in its degree 0 component H0. Of course all other components can be recovered from H0 by the translation functor: Hi = H0 ◦Ti.
