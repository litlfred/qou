Unlike the commutative theory, the Hilbert Basis Theorem does not hold in the noncommutative setting. Thus, a ﬁnitely generated K-ring A need not be noetherian; see Example 15.2.19. Likewise, if A and B are noetherian ﬁnitely generated K-rings, the tensor product A ⊗B need not be noetherian; there is a counterexample in [128]. See [7] for a deep discussion of permanence properties of NC noetherian rings.
