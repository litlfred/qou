Let A be a noetherian connected graded ring, and let d be a natural number. The following ﬁve conditions are equivalent. (i) The graded ring A is regular, of graded global dimension d. (ii) The graded projective dimension of the graded A-module K is d. (iii) The graded K-modules TorA i (K, K) vanish for all i > d, but not for i = d. (iv) The graded K-modules Exti A(K, K) vanish for all i > d, but not for i = d. (v) Let P →A be the minimal graded-free resolution of the graded Aen-module A.
