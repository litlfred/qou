Let A be a DG ring, let E ⊆D(A) be a full triangulated subcategory which is closed under inﬁnite direct sums, and let L be a compact generator of E. Choose an isomorphism L ∼= P in D(A), where the DG A-module P is either K-projective or K-injective, and deﬁne the DG ring B := EndA(P)op. Consider the triangulated functors F := RHomA(P, −) : D(A) →D(B) and G := P ⊗L B (−) : D(B) →D(A). Then the functor F|E : E →D(B) is an equivalence, with quasi-inverse G.
