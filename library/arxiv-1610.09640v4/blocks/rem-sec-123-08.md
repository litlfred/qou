Here is a quick discussion of the evolution of the Calabi-Yau concept. In algebraic geometry, an n-dimensional smooth proper scheme X over a ﬁeld K has the canonical sheaf ωX := Ωn X/K; this is a rank 1 locally free OX-module. In the paper [24] from 1990, A.I. Bondal and M.M. Kapranov introduced the Serre functor (18.6.21) S := ωX[n] ⊗OX (−). This is an auto-equivalence of the derived category Db c(X) = Db c(Mod OX) which admits a bifunctorial isomorphism (18.6.22) HomDb c (X)(M, N) ∼= HomDb c (X)(N, S(M))∗. Here (−)∗:= HomK(−, K).
