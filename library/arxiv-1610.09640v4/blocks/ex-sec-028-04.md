Let M be a DG K-module. Consider the DG K-module EndK(M) := HomK(M, M) from Deﬁnition 3.2.3(2). Composition of endomor- phisms is an associative multiplication on EndK(M) that respects the grading, and the graded Leibniz rule holds. We see that EndK(M) is a DG K-ring. It is usually neither weakly commutative nor nonpositive.
