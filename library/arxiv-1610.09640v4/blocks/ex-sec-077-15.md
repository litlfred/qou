A special yet very important case of Theorem 11.4.40 is this: A is a left noetherian ring, and M is a complex of A-modules with bounded above cohomology, such that each Hi(M) is a ﬁnitely generated A-module. Then M has a resolution P →M, where P is a complex of ﬁnitely generated free A-modules, and sup(P) = sup(H(M)). Compare this to Example 11.3.22.
