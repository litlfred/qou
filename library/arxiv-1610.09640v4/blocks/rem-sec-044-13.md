As mentioned above, in Remark 5.3.9, the cone of a morphism α : L →M in a triangulated category D is usually not functorial. However, if D is endowed with a t-structure, then some cones can be made functorial. Here is a quick explanation. The concept of t-structure was introduced by A.A. Beilinson, J. Bernstein and P. Deligne in the book [16]. A t-structure on a triangulated category D consists of a pair (D≤0, D≥0) of full subcategories of D, that satisfy a few axioms (see e.g. [75, Chapter X] or [187, Deﬁnition 4.1]).
