The structure of triangulated categories is poorly understood, and there is no classiﬁcation of triangulated functors between them. This is true even for the derived category D(A) of a ring A. For instance, a famous open question of J. Rickard is whether every triangulated auto-equivalence F of D(A) is isomorphic to P ⊗L A (−) for some tilting complex P. More on this issue in Section 14. In this book the role of the triangulated structure on derived categories is sec- ondary. It is mostly used for induction on the amplitude of the cohomology of complexes; cf. Section 12.
