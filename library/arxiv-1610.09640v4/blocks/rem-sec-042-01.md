Some texts give a more relaxed deﬁnition: the translation functor T is only required to be an additive auto-equivalence of K. The resulting theory is more complicated (it is 2-categorical, but most texts try to suppress this fact). Later in the book (see Deﬁnition 12.1.7) we will write M[k] := Tk(M), the k-th translation of an object M. Deﬁnition 5.1.3. Suppose (K, TK) and (L, TL) are T-additive categories. A T- additive functor between them is a pair (F, τ), consisting of an additive functor F : K →L, together with an isomorphism τ : F ◦TK ≃ −→TL ◦F of functors K →L, called a translation isomorphism.
