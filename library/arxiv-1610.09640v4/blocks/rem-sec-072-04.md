In view of Proposition 10.3.4, the reader might wonder why we bother with K-ﬂat DG modules. The reason is that there are situations in which there are enough K-ﬂat objects but not enough K-projective objects. One such situation is that of the category C(A) = C(Mod A), where (X, A) is a ringed space and Mod A is the category of sheaves of A-modules; see Example 11.3.16.
