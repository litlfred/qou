Suppose M ∈M(A, gr) is an m-torsion module. Consider its minimal graded-injective resolution 0 →M →I0 →I1 →· · · . By Lemma 16.3.23 and induction on q, we see that all the graded modules Iq are m-torsion; in fact Iq ∼= A∗⊗W q for some W q ∈M(K, gr). Thus RΓm(M) ∼= Γm(I) = I ∼= M, so RqΓm(M) = 0 for all q > 0, and M is a graded-m-ﬂasque A-module.
