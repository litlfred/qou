Let A be a local ring, with maximal ideal m and residue ﬁeld k(m). Recall that A is called Gorenstein if the free module A has ﬁnite injective dimension. The ring A is called called Cohen-Macaulay if its depth is equal to its dimension, where the depth of A is the minimal integer i such that Exti A(k(m), A)̸ = 0.
