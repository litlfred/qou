Let A be a noetherian ring which is homologically smooth over K, let n be a natural number, and let ν be an automorphism of A. The following two conditions are equivalent. (i) A is a twisted CY ring of dimension n with Nakayama automorphism ν. (ii) The complex R := A(ν−1)[n] is a rigid dualizing complex over A. Thus the dualizing automorphism is the inverse of the Nakayama automorphism. Derived Categories | Amnon Yekutieli |
