Let A be a noetherian ring, let R be a NC dualizing complex over A, let B be a second ring, and let DA and DAop be the duality functors asso- ciated to R. Let ⋆be a boundedness indicator, and let M ∈D⋆ (f,..)(A ⊗Bop). Then the following hold: (1) The complex DA(M) belongs to D−⋆ (..,f)(B ⊗Aop), where −⋆is the reversed boundedness indicator. (2) The derived Hom-evaluation morphism evR,R M : M →DAop(DA(M)) in D(A ⊗Bop) is an isomorphism.
