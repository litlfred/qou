In general the isomorphism χ in the corollary above is not unique, and thus the cone of α is not functorial in the morphism α. However, in some special cases χ is unique – see Remark 5.3.26 below. Note also that the standard cones and triangles in the DG category C(A, M) are functorial, by Proposition 4.2.6. This implies that in the triangulated categories K(A, M) and D(A, M) we can sometimes arrange to have functorial cones.
