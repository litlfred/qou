In many cases inverse and direct limits do not exist in C because “its objects are too small”. This happens in the category Setﬁn of ﬁnite sets, and also in the category Abﬁn of ﬁnite abelian groups (see Example 1.8.3 above). There is a very eﬀective method to enlarge C just enough so that the bigger category will have the desired limits. This is done by means of the categories Ind(C) and Pro(C) of ind-objects and pro-objects of C, respectively. See [KaSc1, Sec 1.11] and [KaSc2, Sec 6.1] for detailed discussions.
