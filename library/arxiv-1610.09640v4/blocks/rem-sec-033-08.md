Here is a generalization of Deﬁnition 3.8.3. Instead of a DG central K-ring A we can take a small K-linear DG category A. We then deﬁne the K-linear DG category C(A, M) := DGFun(A, C(M)) as in Exercise 3.5.8. This is indeed a generalization of Deﬁnition 3.8.3: when A has a single object x, and we write A := EndA(x), then the functor M 7→M(x) is an isomorphism of DG categories C(A, M) ≃ −→C(A, M). In the special case of M = M(K) = Mod K, the DG category C(A, M) is what Keller [79] calls the DG category of DG A-modules.
