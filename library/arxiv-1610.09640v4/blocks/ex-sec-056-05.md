In it there is the thick abelian subcategory QCoh OX of quasi-coherent sheaves, and in that there is the thick abelian subcategory Coh OX of coherent sheaves. Passing to complexes, let us write C(X) := C(Mod OX), the DG category of unbounded complexes.
