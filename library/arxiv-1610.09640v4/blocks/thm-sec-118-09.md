Let A be a noetherian ring, and let R be a NC dualizing complex over A. (1) Given a tilting complex T over A, the complex R′ := T ⊗L A R is also a NC dualizing complex over A. (2) Given a second NC dualizing complex R′ over A, the complex T := RHomA(R, R′) is a tilting complex over A, and R′ ∼= T ⊗L A R in D(Aen). (3) If T is a tilting complex over A, and if T ⊗L A R ∼= R in D(Aen), then T ∼= A in D(Aen). Derived Categories | Amnon Yekutieli |
