If A is a ring, then a free DG A-module P is a complex of free A-modules with zero diﬀerential. A semi-free DG A-module P is also a complex of free A-modules, but there is a diﬀerential on it, and there is a subtle condition on P imposed by the existence of a semi-free ﬁltration.
