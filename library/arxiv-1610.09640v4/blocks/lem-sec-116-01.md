Let R be a graded NC dualizing complex over A, with duality functors DA, DAop : D(Aen, gr) →D(Aen, gr). The following four conditions are equivalent. (i) There is an isomorphism DA(K) ∼= K in D(Aen, gr). (i′) There is an isomorphism DAop(K) ∼= K in D(Aen, gr). (ii) There is an isomorphism RΓm(R) ∼= A∗⊗AL in D(Aen, gr), for some graded invertible A-bimodule L generated in algebraic degree 0.
