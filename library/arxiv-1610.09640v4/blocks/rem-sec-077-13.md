Let A be a DG ring whose cohomology H(A) is a nonpositive graded ring. Deﬁne A′ := smt≤0(A), the smart truncation of A below 0. It is easy to see that A′ is a DG subring of A, and the inclusion A′ →A is a DG ring quasi- isomorphism. By Theorem 12.7.2 there is an equivalence of triangulated categories D(A) →D(A′). This says that for many purposes we can assume that A itself is a nonpositive DG ring.
