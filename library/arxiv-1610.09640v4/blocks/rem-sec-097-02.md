There is a delicate issue here. Even though the category C(A⊗ Bop) has enough K-ﬂat objects, they can not be used to calculate (−⊗L B −). The reason is this: given N ∈C(B), the DG (A ⊗Bop)-modules that are acyclic for the DG functor (−) ⊗B N are those that are K-ﬂat over Bop. In general, a K-ﬂat DG (A ⊗Bop)-module M is not K-ﬂat as a Bop-module. A suﬃcient condition for a K-ﬂat DG (A ⊗Bop)-module M to be K-ﬂat over Bop is that A is K-ﬂat as a DG K-module.
