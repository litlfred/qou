Tilting theory and derived Morita theory have their origins in the representation theory of ﬁnite dimensional algebras (in our terminology these are “ﬁnite central K-rings”, where K is a base ﬁeld). Among the ﬁrst examples of tilting are the reﬂection functors in the paper [19] by I.N. Bernstein, I.M. Gelfand and V.A. Ponomarev. Later these functors were understood to be of the form HomA(T, −) for a tilting A-module T. These ideas were generalized by M. Auslander, M. Platzeck and I. Reiten [11], and later by S.
