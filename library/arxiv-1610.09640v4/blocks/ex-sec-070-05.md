Let ⋆be some boundedness condition (namely b, + or −). We know that K⋆(A, M) is a full triangulated subcategory of K(A, M). Hence K⋆(A, M)inj is a triangulated subcategory too. Deﬁnition 10.1.11. Let K be a full triangulated subcategory of K(A, M). We say that K has enough K-injectives if every DG module M ∈K admits a K-injective resolution inside K. I.e. there is a quasi-isomorphism ρ : M →I where I ∈Kinj. Here is the crucial fact regarding K-injectives.
