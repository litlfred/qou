Let A be a DG ring and let L be a DG A-module. The following four conditions are equivalent. (i) L is algebraically perfect. (ii) L is a direct summand in D(A) of a ﬁnite semi-free DG A-module. (iii) For every DG ring B, every DG module M ∈D(A ⊗Bop) and every DG module N ∈D(B), the tensor-evaluation morphism evR,L L,M,N : RHomA(L, M) ⊗L B N →RHomA(L, M ⊗L B N) in D(K) is an isomorphism. (iv) L is a compact object of D(A). Derived Categories | Amnon Yekutieli |
