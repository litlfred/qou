Let E be an exact category in the sense of D. Quillen [122]. Assume that E is a Frobenius category, i.e. E has enough injectives and enough projectives, and an object is injective if and only if it is projective. The stable category of E is the quotient category E := E / E0, where E0 ⊆E is the two-sided ideal of morphisms factoring through a projective object. The set of objects of E is the same as that of E. D. Happel [58] has shown that E is a triangulated category.
