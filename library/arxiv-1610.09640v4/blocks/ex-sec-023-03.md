Let f : A →B be a ring homomorphism. There are two additive functors associated to f : the restriction functor Restf : Mod B →Mod A and the induction functor Indf : Mod A →Mod B. Given a B-module N, the A-module Restf(N) has the same underlying K-module, and A acts on it through f. For an A-module M, the induced B-module is Indf(M) := B ⊗A M.
