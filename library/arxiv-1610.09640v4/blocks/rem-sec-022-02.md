Roughly speaking, they show that the abelian category Ind(Mop) of ind- objects of M has an injective cogenerator. This implies that the abelian category Pro(M) of pro-objects, that’s equivalent to Ind(Mop)op, has a projective generator, say P. Deﬁning the ring A := EndPro(M)(P)op, there is an equivalence of abelian categories Pro(M) ≈Mod A. On the other hand, the Yoneda functor is a fully faithful embedding M →Pro(M).
