Let A, B, C and D be graded rings. For complexes L ∈ D(A ⊗Cop, gr), M ∈D(A ⊗Bop, gr) and N ∈D(B ⊗Dop, gr) there is a morphism evR,L L,M,N : RHomA(L, M) ⊗L B N →RHomA(L, M ⊗L B N) in D(C ⊗Dop, gr), called derived graded tensor-evaluation. The morphism evR,L L,M,N is functorial in the objects L, M, N. Moreover, if all three conditions below hold, then evR,L L,M,N is an isomorphism. Derived Categories | Amnon Yekutieli |
