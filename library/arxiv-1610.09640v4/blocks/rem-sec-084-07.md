In Theorems 12.5.2, 12.5.7 and 12.5.8 the source category is D⋆ f (A), for a left noetherian ring A. There are variants of these theorems in which the source category is D⋆ f (A), for a left pseudo-noetherian DG ring A; and also with source category D⋆ M0(M) for suitable abelian categories M0 ⊆M. We leave it to the reader to investigate these variations. Derived Categories | Amnon Yekutieli |
