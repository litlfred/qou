Let u : A →B be a quasi-isomorphism of DG K-rings. Then: (1) The restriction functor Restu : D(B) →D(A) is an equivalence of K-linear triangulated categories, with quasi-inverse LIndu. (2) For every M ∈D(Bop) and N ∈D(B) there is an isomorphism M ⊗L AN ≃ −→ M ⊗L B N in D(K). This isomorphism is functorial in M and N. (3) For every M, N ∈D(B) there is an isomorphism RHomA(M, N) ≃ −→ RHomB(M, N) in D(K). This isomorphism is functorial in M and N.
