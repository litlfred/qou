Let K be a triangulated category, and let e : M →N be a morphism in K. The following two conditions are equivalent: (i) There is a morphism e′ : M ′ →N in K such that (e, e′) : M ⊕M ′ →N is an isomorphism. In other words, M is a direct summand of N, with embedding e : M →N. (ii) There is a morphism p : N →M in K such that p ◦e = idM. In other words, M is a retract of N, with embedding e : M →N.
