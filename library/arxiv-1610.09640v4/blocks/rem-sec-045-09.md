In 1961, A. Dold and D. Puppe [44] tried to formalize the prop- erties of the stable homotopy category of topological spaces. What they got is a theory almost identical to the theory of triangulated categories studied here (Deﬁ- nition 5.1.10), with the exception of the octahedral axiom (TR4). In more modern terms we can consider the notion of a stable model category, that we now deﬁne. Let C be a pointed Quillen model category, meaning that it has a zero object.
