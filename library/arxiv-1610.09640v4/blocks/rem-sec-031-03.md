A possible ambiguity could arise in the meaning of HomM(M, N) if M, N ∈M. Does it mean the K-module of morphisms in the category M ? Or, if we view M and N as complexes by the canonical embedding M ⊆C(M), does HomM(M, N) mean the complex of morphisms in the DG category C(M) ? It turns out that there is no actual diﬃculty: since the complex of K-modules HomM(M, N) is concentrated in degree 0, we may view it as a single K-module, and this is precisely the K-module of morphisms in the category M.
