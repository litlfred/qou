Let A be a left noetherian ring, let N be an abelian category, let N0 ⊆N be a thick abelian subcategory, let ⋆be a boundedness condition, and let F : D⋆ f (A)op →D(N) be a triangulated functor. Assume that F(A) belongs to DN0(N). (1) If ⋆= −, and if F has bounded below cohomological displacement, then F(M) belongs to DN0(N) for every M ∈D− f (A). (2) If ⋆= ⟨empty⟩, and if F has bounded cohomological displacement, then F(M) belongs to DN0(N) for every M ∈Df(A).
