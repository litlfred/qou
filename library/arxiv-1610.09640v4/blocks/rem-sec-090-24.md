A rank 1 projective A-module L is also called an invertible A-module. This is because L is invertible for the tensor product. Recall that the group of isomorphism classes of invertible A-modules is the commutative Picard group PicA(A). The commutative derived Picard group DPicA(A) is the abelian group PicA(A)× Zm that classiﬁes dualizing complexes over A, as in Corollary 13.1.53.
