In this remark we consider Deligne-Mumford stacks, of ﬁnite type over the regular noetherian base ring K. Due to the étale functoriality of the rigid residue complexes, as explained in Remark 13.5.17, every such stack X admits a rigid residue complex (KX, ρX). Here the rigid structure ρX = {ρ(U,g)} is indexed by the étale maps g : U →X from strictly EFT aﬃne K-schemes U. To a map f : Y →X between DM stacks we associate the ind-rigid trace trf : f∗(KY) →KX, which is a homomorphism of graded quasi-coherent sheaves on X.
