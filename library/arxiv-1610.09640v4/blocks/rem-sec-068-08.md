Deriving triangulated bifunctors that are contravariant in one or two arguments involves passage to opposite categories; see Deﬁnition 9.2.9. Propo- sition 7.6.3 tells us that when Kop i ⊆K(Ai, Mi)op are full triangulated subcategories, the sets Sop i of quasi-isomorphisms in them are of cohomological origin. So we are still within the conditions of Setup 9.3.1, and Theorems 9.3.11 and 9.3.16 can be applied. We leave it to the reader to work out the details in the various cases.
