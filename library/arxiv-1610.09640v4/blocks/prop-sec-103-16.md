Let A be a connected graded ring, and let M ∈M(A, gr) be a bounded below graded module. Deﬁne V := K ⊗A M ∈M(K, gr). (1) There is a surjection π : A ⊗V ↠M in M(A, gr), such that the diagram A ⊗V π / β  M α  V id / V in which the morphisms α and β are the canonical projections, is commu- tative. (2) If V = 0 then M = 0. (3) If M is a graded-projective A-module, then the surjection π above is bijec- tive. Hence M is a graded-free A-module.
