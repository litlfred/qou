Let K be a triangulated category, let S be a denominator set of cohomological origin in K, and let K′ ⊆K be a full triangulated subcategory. Deﬁne S′ := K′ ∩S. Assume either of these conditions holds: (r) Let M ∈Ob(K). If there exists a morphism s : M →L in S with L ∈ Ob(K′), there exists a morphism t : K →M in S with K ∈Ob(K′). (l) Let M ∈Ob(K). If there exists a morphism s : L →M in S with L ∈ Ob(K′), there exists a morphism t : M →K in S with K ∈Ob(K′). Then the functor FS′ : K′ S′ →KS is fully faithful.
