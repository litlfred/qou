Then F has a left derived functor (LF, ηL) : D →E. Furthermore, for every P ∈Kprj the morphism ηL P : LF(P) →F(P) in E is an isomorphism. Deﬁnition 10.2.16. Let K be a full triangulated subcategory of K(A, M), and assume K has enough K-projectives. A system of K-projective resolutions in K is a pair (P, ρ), where P : Ob(K) →Ob(Kprj) is a function, and ρ = {ρM}M∈Ob(K) is a collection of quasi-isomorphisms ρM : P(M) →M in K. Moreover, if M ∈ Ob(Kprj), then P(M) = M and ρM = idM.
