Assume A is a ring, and P ∈M(A) = Mod A is a progenerator, i.e. P is a ﬁnitely generated projective A-module, such that every nonzero A-module N admits a nonzero homomorphism P →N. Let B := EndA(P)op. Because P is a compact generator of D(A), the theorem says that F := HomA(P, −) : D(A) →D(B) is an equivalence of triangulated categories. Classical Morita Theory (see e.g. [130, Section 4.1]) says that F restricts to an equivalence of abelian categories F = HomA(P, −) : M(A) →M(B).
