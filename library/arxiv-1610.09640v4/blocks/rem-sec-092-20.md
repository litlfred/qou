Here is more on the CM (Cohen-Macaulay) property and dual- ity. Let A be a noetherian ring with connected spectrum. Assume A has a dualizing complex R, and corresponding dimension function dimR. Consider a complex M ∈Db f (A). In [62] Grothendieck deﬁnes M to be a CM complex with respect to R if for every prime ideal p ⊆A and every i̸ = −dimR(p) the local cohomology satisﬁes Hi p(Mp) = 0.
