NC semi-free DG rings have important lifting properties within DGRng/c K. V. Hinich, in [66], calls them standard coﬁbrant objects of the Quillen model structure on DGRng/c K. One can also consider the full subcategory DGRng≤0/c K of nonpositive NC DG rings. NC nonpositive semi-free DG rings are studied in detail in [173]. This is done in a slightly more general context: the base ring K is allowed to be a commutative DG ring (see next remark), and K →A is a central homomorphism of DG rings (i.e. the image of K is in Cent(A)). The lifting properties are the same. See Remark 6.2.38 for the related A∞theory.
