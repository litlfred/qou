Let (F, τ) : (K, TK) →(L, TL) be a triangulated functor between triangulated categories. Assume F is an equivalence (of abstract categories), with quasi-inverse G : L →K, and with adjunction isomorphisms α : G ◦F ≃ =⇒IdK and β : F ◦G ≃ =⇒IdL. Then there is an isomorphism of functors ν : G ◦TL ≃ =⇒TK ◦G such that (G, ν) : (L, TL) →(K, TK) is a triangulated functor, and α and β are isomorphisms of triangulated functors.
