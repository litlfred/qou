Let A be a DG K-ring, and let K1 ⊆K(Aop) and K2 ⊆K(A) be full triangulated subcategories. Assume that either K1 or K2 has enough K-ﬂat objects. Let Di denote the localization of Ki with respect to the quasi-isomorphisms in it. Then the triangulated bifunctor (−⊗A −) : K1 × K2 →D(K) has a left derived bifunctor (−⊗L A −) : D1 × D2 →D(K). Moreover, if either P1 ∈K1 or P2 ∈K2 is K-ﬂat, then the morphism ηL P1,P2 : P1 ⊗L A P2 →P1 ⊗A P2 in D(K) is an isomorphism. Derived Categories | Amnon Yekutieli |
