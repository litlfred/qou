Like in Remark 14.2.11, even though the category C(A ⊗Bop) has enough K-projective DG modules, they can not be used to calculate RHomA(−, N). This is because a K-projective DG (A ⊗Bop)-module P is not, in general, K-projective over A. A suﬃcient condition for a K-projective DG (A ⊗Bop)-module P to be K- projective over A is that B is K-projective over K. This is a bit stronger than the condition that B is K-ﬂat over K.
