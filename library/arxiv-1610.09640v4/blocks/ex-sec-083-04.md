Suppose A and B are rings and F : M(A) →M(B) is a left exact additive functor. We get a triangulated functor RF : D(A) →D(B), and Hi(RF(M)) = RiF(M) for all M ∈M(A). Taking C := M(A), with its canonical embedding into D(A), the cohomological dimension of RF relative to M(A) equals the usual right cohomological dimension of the functor F.
