Let A be a ring. Unlike projectives, the structure of injective objects in Mod A is very complicated, and not much is known (except that they exist). However if A is a commutative noetherian ring then we know this: every injective module I is a direct sum of indecomposable injective modules; and the indecomposables are parameterized by Spec(A), the set of prime ideals of A. These facts are due to Matlis; see Subsection 13.2 in the book. Deﬁnition 2.7.7. We say M has enough injectives if every M ∈M admits a monomorphism M →I to an injective object I.
