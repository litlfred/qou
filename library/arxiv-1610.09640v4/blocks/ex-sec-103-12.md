Let K[t] be the commutative polynomial ring in a variable t of algebraic degree 1. Next let A := K[t]⟨x, y⟩/(y·x −x·y −t2), where x and y are noncommuting variables of algebraic degree 1 (that commute with t). This is the homogeneous ﬁrst Weyl algebra. It is a connected graded K- ring, noetherian, but not commutative. Indeed, if char(K) = 0, then the center of A is K[t].
