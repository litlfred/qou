Here are a few historical notes on Theorems 14.5.7 and 14.5.17. The concept of derived Picard group was discovered independently, around 1997, by R. Rouquier and A. Zimmermann [132] (who had used the notation TrPic(A) for this group) and Yekutieli [165]. The motivations of the two teams of authors were very diﬀerent: Rouquier and Zimmermann were interested in invariants of ﬁ- nite dimensional algebras, whereas Yekutieli was trying to classify noncommutative dualizing complexes (See Section 18).
