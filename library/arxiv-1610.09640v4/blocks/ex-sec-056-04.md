Consider Mod Z = Ab. As above we have the thick abelian subcategory Abf = Modf Z of ﬁnitely generated abelian groups. There is also the thick abelian subcategory Abtors of torsion abelian groups (every element has a ﬁnite order). The intersection of Abtors and Abf is the category Abﬁn of ﬁnite abelian groups. This is also thick.
