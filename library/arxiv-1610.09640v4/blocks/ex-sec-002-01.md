Consider the aﬃne algebraic variety X ⊆A3 R which is the union of a plane and a line that meet at a point, with coordinate ring A = R[t1, t2, t3]/(t3·t1, t3·t2). See ﬁgure 1. A dualizing complex R over A must live in two adjacent degrees; namely there is some i such that both Hi(R) and Hi+1(R) are nonzero. This calculation is worked out in full in Example 13.3.12.
