The squaring operation SqB/A is functorial also in the ring B, in two directions. For this remark we assume that the ring A is noetherian, and the rings B and C are ﬂat essentially ﬁnite type A-rings. (See Remark 13.4.24 regarding the ﬂatness.) Then there is a backward morphism Squ/A(θ) : SqC/A(N) →SqB/A(M) in D(B) over u. The morphism Squ/A(θ) is functorial in u and θ in an obvious sense.
