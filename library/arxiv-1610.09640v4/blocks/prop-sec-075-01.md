(1) Let {Mk}k∈N be a direct system in Cstr(A, M). Assume that for every i the direct limit limk→M i k exists in M. Then the direct limit M = limk→Mk exists in Cstr(A, M), and in degree i it is M i = limk→M i k. (2) Let {Mk}k∈N be an inverse system in Cstr(A, M). Assume that for every i the inverse limit lim←k M i k exists in M. Then the inverse limit M = lim←k Mk exists in Cstr(A, M), and in degree i it is M i = lim←k M i k.
