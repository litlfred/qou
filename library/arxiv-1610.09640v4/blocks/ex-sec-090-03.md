Let A be a regular ring. Taking R := A we see that R satisﬁes condition (ii) of Deﬁnition 13.1.9. The other two conditions hold regardless of the regularity of A. Thus the complex R = A is a dualizing complex over the ring A. In Subsection 0.2 of the Introduction we used this fact for the ring A = Z. Deﬁnition 13.1.12. Given a dualizing complex R ∈D(A), the duality functor associated to it is the triangulated functor D : D(A)op →D(A), D := RHomA(−, R).
