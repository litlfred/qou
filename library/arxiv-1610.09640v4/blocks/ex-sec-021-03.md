Let M1 be the category of ﬁnitely generated abelian groups, and let M0 be the category of ﬁnite abelian groups. Then M1 is a full abelian subcategory of Ab, and M0 is a full abelian subcategory of M1. Exercise 2.3.15. Let N be the full subcategory of Ab whose objects are the ﬁnitely generated free abelian groups. It is an additive subcategory of Ab (since it is closed under direct sums). (1) Show that N is closed under kernels in Ab. (2) Show that N is not closed under cokernels in Ab, so it is not a full abelian subcategory of Ab. (3) Show that N has cokernels (not the same as those of Ab). Still, it fails to be an abelian category. Exercise
