Let A be a noetherian ring, and let A →B be a ﬁnite ﬂat ring homomorphism. So B is a ﬁnitely generated projective A-module. Deﬁne ∆B/A := HomA(B, A) ∈M(B). It can be shown (see [175]) that the complex ∆B/A has a canonical rigidifying isomorphism relative to A. I.e. there is a rigidifying isomorphism ρ : ∆B/A ≃ −→SqB/A(∆B/A) in D(B) which is invariant under A-ring automorphisms of B.
