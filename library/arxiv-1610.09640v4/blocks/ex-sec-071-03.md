Let ⋆be some boundedness condition (namely b, + or −). Since K⋆(A, M) is a full triangulated subcategory of K(A, M), we see that K⋆(A, M)prj is a full triangulated subcategory too. Deﬁnition 10.2.7. Let K be a full triangulated subcategory of K(A, M). We say that K has enough K-projectives if every DG module M ∈K admits a K-projective resolution inside K. I.e. there is a quasi-isomorphism ρ : P →M where P ∈Kprj.
