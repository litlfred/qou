Let A be any DG ring. We will prove later that K(A) has enough K-injectives. Therefore, given a triangulated functor F : K(A) →E into a triangulated category E, the right derived functor (RF, ηR) : D(A) →E exists. Suppose we choose a system of K-injective resolutions (I, ρ) in K(A). Then we get a presentation of (RF, ηR) as follows: RF(M) := F(I(M)) and ηR M := F(ρM).
