Let A be a noetherian commutative ring. (1) Let p ⊆A be a prime ideal, and let J(p) be the injective hull of the Ap- module k(p). Then, as an A-module, J(p) is injective, indecomposable and p-torsion. (2) Suppose I is an indecomposable injective A-module. Then I ∼= J(p) for a unique prime ideal p ⊆A. (3) Every injective A module I is a direct sum of indecomposable injective A- modules.
