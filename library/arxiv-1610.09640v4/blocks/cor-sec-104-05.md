Let A, B and C be graded rings. (1) The triangulated right derived bifunctor RHomA(−, −) : D(A ⊗Bop, gr)op × D(A ⊗Cop, gr) →D(B ⊗Cop, gr) exists. (2) Let M ∈D(A ⊗Bop, gr) and N ∈D(A ⊗Cop, gr). Assume that either M is K-graded-projective over A, or N is K-graded-injective over A. Then the morphism ηR M,N : HomA(M, N) →RHomA(M, N) in D(B ⊗Cop, gr) is an isomorphism. (3) Let B′ →B and C′ →C be graded K-ring homomorphisms. Then the diagram D(A ⊗Bop, gr)op × D(A ⊗Cop, gr) RHomA(−,−) / Rest × Rest  D(B ⊗Cop, gr) Rest  D(A ⊗B′ op, gr)op × D(A ⊗C′ op, gr) RHomA(−,−) / D(B′ ⊗C′ op, gr) is commutative up to isomorphism.
