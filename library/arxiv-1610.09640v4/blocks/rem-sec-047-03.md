Let K be a triangulated category. There is a way to introduce a triangulated structure on the opposite category Kop. The translation functor is Top := Op ◦T−1 ◦Op−1. The distinguished triangles are deﬁned to be N Op(β) −−−−→M Op(α) −−−−→L Op(−T−1(γ)) −−−−−−−−−→Top(N), where L α−→M β−→N γ−→T(L) goes over all distinguished triangles in K. See [76, Remark 10.1.10(ii)]. This approach has an advantage: it allows to talk about contravariant triangulated functors K →L without any restriction on the category K.
