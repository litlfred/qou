A functor F : C→D is called an isomorphism of categoriesif it is bijective
on sets of objects and on sets of morphisms. It is clear that an isomorphism of
categories is an equivalence. IfF is an isomorphism of categories, then it has an
inverse isomorphismF−1 : D→C, which is unique.
