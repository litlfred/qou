This section is devoted to the general theory of Ore localization of categories.
We are given a category A and a multiplicatively closed set of morphisms S ⊆A.
The localized category AS, gotten by formally inverting the morphisms in S, always
exists. The goal is to have a presentation of the morphisms of AS as left or right
fractions.
In Section 7 we shall apply the results of this section to triangulated categories.
