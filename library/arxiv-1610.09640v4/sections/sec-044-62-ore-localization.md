We are given a categoryA and a multiplicatively closed set of morphismsS⊆A.
The localized categoryAS, gotten by formally inverting the morphisms inS, always
exists. The goal is to have a presentation of the morphisms ofAS as left or right
fractions.
In Section 7 we shall apply the results of this section to triangulated categories.
