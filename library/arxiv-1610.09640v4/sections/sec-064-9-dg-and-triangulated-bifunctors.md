In this section we extend the theory of triangulated derived functors, that was
studied in Section 8, to triangulated bifunctors.
As stated in Convention 1.2.4,
all linear structures and operations (such as categories and functors) are K-linear,
where K is a ﬁxed commutative base ring. The symbol ⊗means ⊗K.
