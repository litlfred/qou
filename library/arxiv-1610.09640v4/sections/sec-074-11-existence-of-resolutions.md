In this section we continue in the more concrete setting: K is a nonzero commu-
tative base ring, A is a central DG K-ring, and M is a K-linear abelian category; see
Convention 1.2.4. The DG category C⋆(A, M) was introduced in Section 3.8. We
will prove existence of K-projective and K-injective resolutions in C⋆(A, M), under
suitable conditions.
