Remark 12.9.4. Let L be a DG A-module. Following [21], let us say that L
is pseudo-coherent if L is isomorphic in D(A) to a pseudo-ﬁnite semi-free DGA-
module P. Clearly pseudo-coherent implies derived pseudo-ﬁnite. According to
[144, Lemma tag=064X] the converse is true whenA is a ring; but we do not know
if the converse is true in general.
Anyhow, the attribute “derived pseudo-ﬁnite” is very useful, and it can replace
“pseudo-coherent” for most applications. For instance, see condition (i) in Theorem
12.9.10 below.

in general.
