We start by fixing a finite field ${\mathbb F}_q$ and a directed graph $D$, which might look like this: $$\xymatrix@=10pt{ &&&&& \bullet \\ \bullet \ar@(ul,dl)[] \ar@/^1pc/[rr] && \bullet \ar@/^1pc/[ll] \ar@/^1pc/[rr] \ar@/_1pc/[rr] \ar[rr] && \bullet \ar[ur] \ar[dr] \\ &&&&& \bullet }$$ We shall call the category $Q$ freely generated by $D$ a **quiver**.
