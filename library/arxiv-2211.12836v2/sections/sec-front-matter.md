QUANTUM COHOMOLOGY OF THE GRASSMANNIAN AND UNITARY
DYSON BROWNIAN MOTION
J´ER´EMIE GUILHOT, C´EDRIC LECOUVEY, AND PIERRE TARRAGO
Abstract. We study a class of commuting Markov kernels whose simplest element describes the
movement of k particles on a discrete circle of size n conditioned to not intersect each other. Such
Markov kernels are related to the quantum cohomology ring of the Grassmannian, which is an
algebraic object counting analytic maps from P1(C) to the Grassmannian space of k-dimensional
vector subspaces of Cn with prescribed constraints at some points of P1(C). We obtain a Berry-
Esseen theorem and a local limit theorem for an arbitrary product of approximately n2 Markov
kernels belonging to the above class, when k is ﬁxed. As a byproduct of those results, we derive
asymptotic formulas for the quantum cohomology ring of the Grassmannian in terms of the heat
kernel on SU(k).
