For universality, take n = 4 and regard each Tk as operating on V ⊗V where V is a single qubit space. Then
the braiding operators Tk each satisfy the Yang-Baxter equation and are entangling operators, and so we have

shows that Majorana fermions can be detected and controlled, then it is possible that quantum computers based
on these topological unitary representations will be constructed.
