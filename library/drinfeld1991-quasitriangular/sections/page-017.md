In scattering theory a plane wave denotes a solution of the free Schrodinger
equation. More precisely, a plane wave is the kernel of the unitary integral
transformation that diagonalizes the kinetic energy operator (in the case of
Euclidean space R” this turns out to be the same as the Fourier transform
on L,(R")). The present article is devoted to study of the plane wave for a
system of three particles with fixed total orbital momentum equal to zero.
