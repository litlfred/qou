The experimental magnetic form factors (shortened to f.f.'s, throughout the reminder of this section) of ${}^3\text{H}$ and ${}^3\text{He}$ [@Amroun:1994qj; @Sick2001] are well reproduced by the VMC calculation as can be seen in Figs. [\[fig:MFF_IIb_co\]](#fig:MFF_IIb_co){reference-type="ref" reference="fig:MFF_IIb_co"}a and [\[fig:MFF_IIb_co\]](#fig:MFF_IIb_co){reference-type="ref" reference="fig:MFF_IIb_co"}b. The contribution of the N3LO(NM) and N3LO(OPE) fitted terms are able to fill in the diffraction minimum appearing at $q\simeq3$ fm$^{-1}$ in the magnetic f.f.'s of ${}^3\text{He}$. The ${}^3\text{H}$ diffraction is not visible in the figure but a similar effect is present. We refer to Ref. [@Gnech:2022vwr] for a more detailed discussion. Similar results are obtained for all the other NV2+3 interactions. Note that the VMC calculations are in perfect agreement with the Hyperspherical Harmonics results of Ref. [@Gnech:2022vwr].
