A 0–2 move is also called a lune move in the dual language of standard spines [Mat87, Mat07, Pie88, BP97]. In [Mat87, Lem.2.1.11] and [Pie88] (see also [Pet95, Prop.I.1.13]) it was shown that a 0–2 move follows from a combination of 2–3 moves as long as the initial triangulation has at least 2 ideal tetrahedra.
