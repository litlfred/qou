Suppose that M is a compact oriented irreducible 3-manifold with incom- pressible tori boundary components and no immersed incompressible tori or Klein bottles, except those which are homotopic into the boundary tori. Then M admits an ideal trian- gulation T having an index structure. Moreover if M has no essential annuli (i.e M is an-annular) then for any ﬁnite sheeted covering space f M, the lifted triangulation also admits an index structure.
