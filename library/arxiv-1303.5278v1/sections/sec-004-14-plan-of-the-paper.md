[]{#sub.plan label="sub.plan"}

In Section [\[sec.definitions\]](#sec.definitions){reference-type="ref" reference="sec.definitions"} we review the basic definitions of ideal triangulations, efficiency, angle structures and index structures.

In Section [\[sec: index structures and 1-efficiency\]](#sec: index structures and 1-efficiency){reference-type="ref" reference="sec: index structures and 1-efficiency"} we prove Theorem [\[index_structure_iff_1-efficient\]](#index_structure_iff_1-efficient){reference-type="ref" reference="index_structure_iff_1-efficient"}. So for an ideal triangulation, existence of an index structure is equivalent to being 1-efficient.

In Section [\[sec.review\]](#sec.review){reference-type="ref" reference="sec.review"} we review the basic properties of the tetrahedron index from [@Ga:index], and give a detailed discussion of the 3D index for an ideal triangulation of a cusped 3-manifold. In Section [\[sec.02move\]](#sec.02move){reference-type="ref" reference="sec.02move"} we study the behaviour of the 3D index under the 0--2 and 2--0 move.

In Section [\[sec.XEPM\]](#sec.XEPM){reference-type="ref" reference="sec.XEPM"} we discuss the Epstein-Penner ideal cell decomposition and its subdivision into regular triangulations. At the end of Section [\[sub.moving.paths\]](#sub.moving.paths){reference-type="ref" reference="sub.moving.paths"} we prove Theorems [\[thm.2\]](#thm.2){reference-type="ref" reference="thm.2"} and [\[thm.3\]](#thm.3){reference-type="ref" reference="thm.3"}.

In Section [\[sec.computation\]](#sec.computation){reference-type="ref" reference="sec.computation"} we compute the first terms of the 3D index for some example manifolds.

Finally in the appendix, we give a detailed and self-contained proof of the invariance of the 3D index of 1-efficient triangulations under 2--3 moves, following [@Ga:index] and [@DGG2].
