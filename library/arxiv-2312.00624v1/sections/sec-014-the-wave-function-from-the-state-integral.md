[]{#sec: stateintegral label="sec: stateintegral"} In this section, we consider the Andersen--Kashaev invariant [@ak; @ak2; @dimofte-rev] $\chi_{{\cal K}}(u;\hbar)$ in its original form as a finite-dimensional state integral, whose integrand is a product of Faddeev's quantum dilogarithm functions [@faddeev]. We evaluate it directly at rational values of $\hbar$ by applying the techniques of [@garou-kas], thus providing a third method for the computation of the exact wave function. For simplicity, we focus on the case of the figure-eight knot and show that this third approach agrees with the two previous methods presented in Section [\[sec: main\]](#sec: main){reference-type="ref" reference="sec: main"}. A similar calculation in the case ${\mathsf{b}}^2=1$ was done in unpublished work by Szabolcs Zakany.
