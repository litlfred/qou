In Section 3.3, we showed that the exact wave function χ(u; ℏ) in Eq. (3.13) at rational values of
ℏ is determined by the functions Υ (α)(X), defined in Eq. (3.45), and their derivatives with respect
to q, up to the constants Cα and the exponential prefactors containing s(α)
0,1 (x). We will now prove
that the functions Υ(α)(X) can be obtained by solving directly the quantum A-polynomial in closed
form. We will focus on q-difference equations of order two and three. These are the relevant cases
for the examples studied in Section 4. The matrix formalism presented below was used in [12] for a
second-order q-difference equation in the context of quantum mirror curves.
