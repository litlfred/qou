In this section, we study the non-perturbative wave function χK(u; ℏ) introduced in Section 2 by
exploiting ideas from topological string theory. In particular, we propose various new conjectures
on the structure of this wave function, which are motivated by similar results in the open version
of the TS/ST correspondence [10–12, 18] and by our detailed analysis of the examples presented
in Section 4. In the following, we will not explicitly indicate the knot K to avoid the cluttering of
notation.
