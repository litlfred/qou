[]{#sec: main label="sec: main"}

In this section, we study the non-perturbative wave function $\chi_{{\cal K}}(u;\hbar)$ introduced in Section [\[sec: theory\]](#sec: theory){reference-type="ref" reference="sec: theory"} by exploiting ideas from topological string theory. In particular, we propose various new conjectures on the structure of this wave function, which are motivated by similar results in the open version of the TS/ST correspondence [@kpamir; @mz-wv; @mz-wv2; @szabolcs] and by our detailed analysis of the examples presented in Section [\[sec: examples\]](#sec: examples){reference-type="ref" reference="sec: examples"}. In the following, we will not explicitly indicate the knot ${\cal K}$ to avoid the cluttering of notation.
