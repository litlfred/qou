In this section, we consider the Andersen–Kashaev invariant [1, 27, 33]χK(u; ℏ) in its original form as
a finite-dimensional state integral, whose integrand is a product of Faddeev’s quantum dilogarithm
functions [14]. We evaluate it directly at rational values of ℏ by applying the techniques of [24], thus
providing a third method for the computation of the exact wave function. For simplicity, we focus
on the case of the figure-eight knot and show that this third approach agrees with the two previous
methods presented in Section 3. A similar calculation in the case b2 = 1 was done in unpublished
work by Szabolcs Zakany.
