In this section, we review the fundamental aspects of complex CS theory on a closed three-manifold
and the construction of the classical and quantumA-polynomials. The physical understanding of the
connection to A-polynomials was developed in [5], and an excellent summary can be found in [27].
The AJ conjecture, which is at the basis of many of our computations, was proposed in [4]. We
pay special attention to the case of CS theory with SL(2 , C) gauge group on the complement of a
hyperbolic knot in the three-sphere and introduce the two benchmark examples that we will consider
later in this work, that is, the figure-eight and the three-twist knots.
