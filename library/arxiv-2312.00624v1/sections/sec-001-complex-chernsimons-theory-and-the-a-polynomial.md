[]{#sec: theory label="sec: theory"} In this section, we review the fundamental aspects of complex CS theory on a closed three-manifold and the construction of the classical and quantum $A$-polynomials. The physical understanding of the connection to A-polynomials was developed in [@gukov], and an excellent summary can be found in [@dimofte-rev]. The AJ conjecture, which is at the basis of many of our computations, was proposed in [@stavros-aj]. We pay special attention to the case of CS theory with $\text{SL}(2,{\mathbb C})$ gauge group on the complement of a hyperbolic knot in the three-sphere and introduce the two benchmark examples that we will consider later in this work, that is, the figure-eight and the three-twist knots.
