[]{#levi label="levi"} Let $T\subset L\subset G$ be a Levi subgroup. We denote by $P_L$ (resp. $P^-_L$) the parabolic subgroup generated by $L$ and the positive (resp. negative) Borel subgroup $B$ (resp. $B_-$). We denote by $W_L\subset W$ the Weyl group of $L$. We denote by $\pi_L$ the projection from ${\frak t}/W_L$ to ${\frak t}/W$. We denote by $X^+_L$ the set of highest weights of irreducible $\check{L}{}$-modules, where $\check{L}{}\subset{\check{G}}$ stands for the Langlands dual Levi subgroup. We have a natural projection from $X^+_L$ to the lattice $X^*(Z(\check{L}{}))$ of characters of the center $Z(\check{L}{})$ of $\check{L}{}$. The set of $P^-_L({\mathbf F})$-orbits in $\Gr_G$ is numbered by $X^*(Z(\check{L}{}))$. For $\lambda\in X^*(Z(\check{L}{}))$ we will denote the corresponding orbit by $_L\fT_\lambda$, and its closure by $_L\ofT_\lambda$. The locally closed embedding of $\fT_\lambda$ into $\Gr_G$ is denoted by $\imath_\lambda$. For an $\check{L}{}$-module $V$ we denote by $S_L(V)$ the corresponding $L({\mathbf O})\rtimes{{\mathbb G}_m}$-equivariant perverse sheaf on $\Gr_L$.

::: lem
[]{#Chicago label="Chicago"} For $V\in Rep({\check{G}})$, the ${\mathcal O}({\frak t}/W_L\times{\frak t}/W\times{\mathbb A}^1)$-module $(\pi_L,\Id,\Id)^*H^\bullet_{{\bf G_O}\rtimes{{\mathbb G}_m}}(\Gr_G,S(V))$ carries a canonical filtration $F^\bullet_L$ such that the associated graded is equipped with a canonical isomorphism $$\ ^{\operatorname{top}}\Xi_L:\ gr(\pi_L,\Id,\Id)^*H^\bullet_{{\bf G_O}\rtimes{{\mathbb G}_m}}(\Gr_G,S(V))
{\stackrel{\sim}{\longrightarrow}}(\Id,\pi_L,\Id)_*
H^\bullet_{L({\mathbf O})\rtimes{{\mathbb G}_m}}(\Gr_L,S_L(V|_{\check{L}{}})).$$
:::

*Proof* : We have $(\pi_L,\Id,\Id)^*H^\bullet_{{\bf G_O}\rtimes{{\mathbb G}_m}}(\Gr_G,S(V))=
H^\bullet_{L({\mathbf O})\rtimes{{\mathbb G}_m}}(\Gr_G,S(V))=H^\bullet_{L\times{{\mathbb G}_m}}
(\Gr_G,S(V))$. The canonical filtration in question is filtration by the images of $r_\lambda:\ H^\bullet_{_L\ofT_\lambda,L\times{{\mathbb G}_m}}(\Gr_G,S(V))\to
H^\bullet_{L\times{{\mathbb G}_m}}(\Gr_G,S(V))$ (cohomology with supports; here $\lambda\in X^*(Z(\check{L}{}))$). The associated graded of this filtration is $\bigoplus_{\lambda\in X^*(Z(\check{L}{}))}H^\bullet_{_L\fT_\lambda,L\times{{\mathbb G}_m}}
(\Gr_G,S(V))$. Let $\fp^\lambda:\ _L\fT_\lambda\to\Gr_L$ denote the natural $L({\mathbf O})\rtimes{{\mathbb G}_m}$-equivariant projection. Then we have $H^\bullet_{_L\fT_\lambda,L\times{{\mathbb G}_m}}(\Gr_G,S(V))=
H^\bullet_{L\times{{\mathbb G}_m}}(\Gr_L,\fp^\lambda_*\imath_\lambda^!S(V))$. However, according to [@BD], we have a canonical isomorphism $\bigoplus_{\lambda\in X^*(Z(\check{L}{}))}\fp^\lambda_*\imath_\lambda^!S(V)=
S_L(V|_{\check{L}{}})$. The lemma is proved. $\Box$
