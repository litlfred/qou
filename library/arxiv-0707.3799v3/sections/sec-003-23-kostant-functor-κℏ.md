[]{#Kostant label="Kostant"} We now proceed to define a functor ${\kappa_\hbar}:\HC_\hbar\to QCoh^{{\mathbb G}_m}((\check{\mathfrak t}{}^*/W)^2\times {\mathbb A}^1)$.

Let $\psi:U_\hbar (\check{\mathfrak n}{}_-)\to\mathsf k[\hbar]$ be a homomorphism such that $\psi(f_\alpha)=1$ for any simple root $\alpha$, and a root generator $f_\alpha\in\check{\mathfrak n}{}_-\subset U_\hbar(\check{\mathfrak n}{}_-)$.

Define $U_\hbar^2(\check{\mathfrak n}{}_-)\subset U_\hbar ^2$ by $U_\hbar^2(\check{\mathfrak n}{}_-)=U_\hbar(\check{\mathfrak n}{}_-)
\otimes U(\check{\mathfrak n}{}_-)$. We extend $\psi$ to a character $\psi_{(2)}: U_\hbar^2(\check{\mathfrak n}{}_-)
=U_\hbar(\check{\mathfrak n}{}_-) \otimes U(\check{\mathfrak n}{}_-)
\to\mathsf k[\hbar]$ trivial on the second multiple. Note that its restriction to the first copy of $U_\hbar$ is $\psi$, and its restriction to the second copy is $(-\psi)$.

We set ${\kappa_\hbar}(M)=(M\overset{\rm L}{\otimes}_{U_\hbar(\check{\mathfrak n}{}_-)_2}(-\psi))^{\check{N}{}_-}$ where the action of the second copy of $U_\hbar$ is used (though using the first one we get a canonically isomorphic functor). Clearly, ${\kappa_\hbar}(M)$ is equipped with the action of the Harish-Chandra center $Z(U_\hbar)\otimes_{\mathsf k[\hbar]}Z(U_\hbar)=
\CO((\check{\mathfrak t}{}^*/W)\times(\check{\mathfrak t}{}^*/W)\times{\mathbb A}^1)$, and with the grading (coming from the action of the Cartan element $h$ of the principal $\mathfrak{sl}_2$), so we may view ${\kappa_\hbar}(M)$ as a ${{\mathbb G}_m}$-equivariant quasicoherent sheaf on $(\check{\mathfrak t}{}^*/W)^2\times{\mathbb A}^1$.

If $X$ is a scheme, and $Z\subset X$ is a closed subscheme let $N_XZ$ be the *deformation to the normal cone* to $Z$, see [@Fu]. It is equipped with a morphism $N_XZ\to X\times{\mathbb A}^1$ (with coordinate $\hbar$ on ${\mathbb A}^1$), and is defined as the relative spectrum of the sheaf of subalgebras in $\CO_X[\hbar^{\pm1}]$ generated by the elements $f\hbar^{-1},\ 
f\in\CO_X:\ f|_Z=0$. In other words, $N_XZ$ is the affine blowup $\operatorname{Bl}_{Z\times\{0\}}^{\operatorname{aff}}(X\times\BA^1)$ of $X\times\BA^1$ at $Z\times\{0\}$: the complement of the strict transform of $X\times\{0\}$ in the blowup $\operatorname{Bl}_{Z\times\{0\}}(X\times\BA^1)$.

If $M$ is a free $\mathsf k[\hbar]$-module, the action of $\CO((\check{\mathfrak t}{}^*/W)^2\times{\mathbb A}^1)$ on ${\kappa_\hbar}(M)$ extends uniquely to the action of $\CO(N_{(\check{\mathfrak t}{}^*/W)^2}\Delta)$ where $\Delta\subset(\check{\mathfrak t}{}^*/W)^2$ is the diagonal. So we can and will view ${\kappa_\hbar}(M)$ as a ${{\mathbb G}_m}$-equivariant coherent sheaf on $N_{(\check{\mathfrak t}{}^*/W)^2}\Delta$.

For $V\in Rep({\check{G}})$ we define the $\hbar$-Harish-Chandra bimodule $Fr(V)$ by $Fr(V)=U_\hbar\otimes V$ with its natural ${\check{G}}$-module structure $g(y\otimes v)=Ad(g)(y)\otimes g(v)$, and with the $U_\hbar \otimes U_\hbar$ action specified by $x\otimes u(y\otimes
v)= xyu\otimes v +\hbar\cdot xy \otimes u(v)$ for $x,u\in \check{\mathfrak g}{}
\subset U_\hbar$. In other words, $Fr(V)$ is obtained by applying the induction (left adjoint to the restriction functor) $Rep({\check{G}})\to \HC_\hbar$ to $V$. We set $\phi(V):=\kappa_\hbar(Fr(V))$.

Clearly $Fr(V)$ is a projective object of $\HC_\hbar$ for any $V\in Rep({\check{G}})$; we call an object of the form $Fr(V)$ a free $\hbar$-Harish-Chandra bimodule. We define the full subcategory $\HC_\hbar^{fr}\subset \HC_\hbar$ to consist of all free objects.
