Let $\CalD_\hbar({\check{G}})$ stand for the sheaf of $\hbar$-differential operators on ${\check{G}}$: its global sections is the smash product of $U_\hbar$ and $\CO({\check{G}})$. The action of $\check{\mathfrak n}{}_-$ by the left-invariant (resp. right-invariant) vector fields on ${\check{G}}$ gives rise to the homomorphism $\sfl$ (resp. $\sr$): $U_\hbar(\check{\mathfrak n}{}_-)\to\CalD_\hbar({\check{G}})$. Let $I_\psi\subset\CalD_\hbar({\check{G}})$ be the left ideal generated by the $\hbar$-differential operators of the sort $\sfl(u_1)-\psi(u_1)+\sr(u_2)+\psi(u_2);\ u_1,u_2\in U_\hbar(\check{\mathfrak n}{}_-)$. We consider the quantum hamiltonian reduction $$(\CalD_\hbar({\check{G}})/I_\psi)^{\check{N}{}_-\times\check{N}{}_-}$$ where the first (resp. second) copy of $\check{N}{}_-$ acts on ${\check{G}}$ (and hence on $\CalD_\hbar({\check{G}})$) by the left (resp. right) translations: $(n_1,n_2)\circ g:=n_1gn_2^{-1}$. It is an algebra containing a commutative subalgebra $Z(U_\hbar)$ (via the embedding $Z(U_\hbar)\hookrightarrow\CalD_\hbar({\check{G}})$ as both left- and right-invariant $\hbar$-differential operators). Note that the action of $\check{N}{}_-\times\check{N}{}_-$ on the "big Bruhat cell" $C_{w_0}:=\check{N}{}_-\cdot\check{T}{}\cdot w_0\cdot\check{N}{}_-\subset{\check{G}}$ is free, and hence the quantum hamiltonian reduction of $\CalD_\hbar(C_{w_0})$ is isomorphic to $\CalD_\hbar(\check{T}{})$. This is the classical Kazhdan-Kostant construction of the quantum Toda lattice, see [@K2]. Thus, the quantum Toda lattice is a certain localization of $(\CalD_\hbar({\check{G}})/I_\psi)^{\check{N}{}_-\times\check{N}{}_-}$. In what follows we will call $(\CalD_\hbar({\check{G}})/I_\psi)^{\check{N}{}_-\times\check{N}{}_-}$ "the quantized Toda lattice", somewhat abusing the language. The following result was conjectured in [@BFM].

::: thm
[]{#TodaTh label="TodaTh"} The convolution algebra of equivariant homology $H^{{\bf G_O}\rtimes {{\mathbb G}_m}}_\bullet(\Gr)$ is naturally isomorphic to the quantized Toda lattice $(\CalD_\hbar({\check{G}})/I_\psi)^{\check{N}{}_-\times\check{N}{}_-}$. The embedding $Z(U_\hbar)\simeq H_{{\bf G_O}\rtimes{{\mathbb G}_m}}(pt)\hookrightarrow 
H^{{\bf G_O}\rtimes{{\mathbb G}_m}}_\bullet(\Gr)$ corresponds to the embedding $Z(U_\hbar)\hookrightarrow(\CalD_\hbar({\check{G}})/I_\psi)^{\check{N}{}_-\times\check{N}{}_-}$.
:::
