(AJ conjecture) For every knot K in S3, ε(αK) is equal to the A- polynomial AK(M, L), up to multiplication by a polynomial depending on M only. The A-polynomial of a knot was introduced by Cooper et al. [CCGLS]; it describes the SL2(C)-character variety of the knot complement as viewed from the boundary torus. The A-polynomial always contains the factor L −1 coming from the abelian component of the character variety, so we can write AK = (L −1)A′ K where A′ K ∈Z[M, L].
