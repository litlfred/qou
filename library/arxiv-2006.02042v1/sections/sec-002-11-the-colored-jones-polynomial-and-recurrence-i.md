Let ε be the map reducing t = −1. Motivated by the theory of quantum A-ideals
of Frohman-Gelca-LoFaro [FGL], Garoufalidis [Ga] formulated the AJ conjecture which
relates the A-polynomial and recurrence polynomial of a knot in the 3-pshere.
Conjecture 1. (AJ conjecture) For every knot K in S3, ε(αK) is equal to the A-
polynomial AK(M, L), up to multiplication by a polynomial depending on M only.
The A-polynomial of a knot was introduced by Cooper et al. [CCGLS]; it describes
the SL2(C)-character variety of the knot complement as viewed from the boundary torus.
