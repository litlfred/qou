Let ε be the map reducing t = −1. Motivated by the theory of quantum A-ideals
of Frohman-Gelca-LoFaro [FGL], Garoufalidis [Ga] formulated the A J conjecture which
relates the A-polynomial and recurrence polynomial of a knot in the 3-pshere.
Conjecture 1. (AJ conjecture) For every knot K in S3, ε(αK) is equal to the A-
polynomial AK(M, L), up to multiplication by a polynomial depending on M only.
The A-polynomial of a knot was introduced by Cooper et al. [CCGLS]; it describes
the SL2(C)-character variety of the knot complement as viewed from the bo undary torus.
The A-polynomial always contains the factor L − 1 coming from the abelian component
of the character variety, so we can write AK = (L − 1)A′
K where A′
K ∈ Z[M, L].
The AJ conjecture has been conﬁrmed for the trefoil knot and ﬁg ure eight knot [Ga],
