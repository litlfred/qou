and a positive integer n, let JK(n) ∈ Z[t±1] denote the n-colored Jones polynomial of
K with zero-framing. The polynomial JK(n) is the quantum link invariant, as deﬁned
by Reshetikhin and Turaev [RT], associated to the Lie algebra sl2(C), with the color n
standing for the irreducible sl2(C)-module Vn of dimension n. Here we use the functorial
normalization, i.e. the one for which Junknot(n) = [ n] := ( t2n − t−2n)(t2 − t−2). It is known
that JK(1) = 1 and JK(2) is the ordinary Jones polynomial [Jo]. The color n can take
non-positive integer values by setting JK(−n) = −JK(n) and JK(0) = 0.
Consider a discrete function f : Z → R := C[t±1] and deﬁne the linear operators L, M
acting on such functions by ( Lf )(n) := f (n + 1) and ( M f)(n) := t2nf (n). It is easy to see
that LM = t2M L, and that L±1, M ±1 generate the quantum torus T , a non-commutative
ring with presentation T := R⟨L±1, M ±1⟩/(LM − t2M L).
Let AK := {P ∈ T | P JK = 0} which is a left-ideal of T , called the recurrence ideal of
K. It was proved in [GL] that for every knot K, the recurrence ideal AK is non-zero. An
element in AK is called a recurrence relation for the colored Jones polynomial of K.
