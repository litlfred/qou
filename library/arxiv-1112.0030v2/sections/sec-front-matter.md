Homological algebra of knots and BPS states
Sergei Gukov and Marko Stoˇsi´c
Abstract. It is known that knot homologies admit a physical description
as spaces of open BPS states.
We study operators and algebras acting on
these spaces.
This leads to a very rich story, which involves wall crossing
phenomena, algebras of closed BPS states acting on spaces of open BPS states,
and deformations of Landau-Ginzburg models.
One important application to knot homologies is the existence of “colored
diﬀerentials” that relate homological invariants of knots colored by diﬀerent
representations.
Based on this structure, we formulate a list of properties
of the colored HOMFLY homology that categoriﬁes the colored HOMFLY
polynomial.
By calculating the colored HOMFLY homology for symmetric
and anti-symmetric representations, we ﬁnd a remarkable “mirror symmetry”
between these triply-graded theories.
