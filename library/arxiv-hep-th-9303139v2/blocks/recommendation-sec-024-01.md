In order not to write down integrals of $J$, we introduce scalar field:\footnote{ One can consider $\phi $ as introduced for simplicity of notation, but it should be kept in mind that the scalar-field representation is in fact more fundamental for {\it generic} $\tau$-functions, not related to the level $k=1$ Kac-Moody algebras (this phenomenon is well known in conformal filed theory, see \cite{GMMOS} for more details). } \be \phi(z) = \sum_{\stackrel{k\neq 0}{k\in Z-0}}\frac{J_{-k}}{k}z^k + \phi_0 + J_0\log z, \ee such that $\partial\phi(z) = J(z)$.
