In order to find a solution of (\ref{ralap}), say, for $j=0$, one should just take an angular average of a plane wave: \be \phi_k(r) = \int e^{ik\vec r\vec\nu}d^{D-1}\vec\nu; \ \ \ \mid\vec\nu\mid = 1. \ee This integral representation expresses the solutions of (\ref{ralap}) through Bessel functions, and this is in fact the proper way to derive the well-known formula: \be \phi_k(r) = 2^{\frac{D}{2}-1}\Gamma\left(\frac{D}{2}\right) (kr)^{1-\frac{D}{2}} J_{\frac{D}{2}-1}(kr). \ee If one expands the exponent in the integral in a series, the standard expansion for the Bessel function arises.
