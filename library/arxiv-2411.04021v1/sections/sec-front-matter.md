On the Gram determinants of the Specht modules
Linda Hoyer
∗
Lehrstuhl für Algebra und Zahlentheorie, RWTH Aachen University,
Germany
Abstract
For every partition λ of a positive integer n, let Sλ be the corresponding
Specht module of the symmetric group Sn, and let det(λ) ∈Z denote the
Gram determinant of the canonical bilinear form with respect to the standard
basis of Sλ. Writing det(λ) = m · 2a(2)
λ
for integers a(2)
λ
and m with m odd, we
show that if the dimension of Sλ is even, then a(2)
λ
is also even. This confirms
a conjecture posed by Richard Parker in the special case of the symmetric
groups.
Keywords: Symmetric group, Specht module, Young diagram, beta-numbers,
hook removal, Gram determinant. MSC: 05E10.
