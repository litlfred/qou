For the limits of universal behavior the same restrictions as in the th ree-body system
apply. With momenta of order 1 /l (or energies of order 1 /(ml2)) one is able to probe details
of the short-distance mechanism leading to the large scattering len gth and the eﬀective
theory does no longer apply. As a consequence, only states with Kl ≪ 1 and systems with
l/|a| ≪ 1 will show the universal behavior in Fig. 2. Which states are within the universal
window then depends on the actual values of a andl in the system under consideration. For
4He atoms there is some evidence that both tetramer states are wit hin this range [12].
