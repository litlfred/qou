A Lagrangian which describes nucleon-to-resonance radiative transition is given in the form of a contact term: $$\begin{aligned} & \displaystyle{\mathcal{L}^{\Delta N\gamma}=i\Theta\frac{e}{\Lambda}\bar{N}\gamma^{\mu}\gamma_{5}Q\Delta^{\nu}F_{\mu\nu},}\nonumber \\ \label{eq:9-1}\\ & F_{\mu\nu}=\partial_{\mu}\mathcal{A}_{\nu}-\partial_{\nu}\mathcal{A}_{\mu}.\nonumber \end{aligned}$$ Here, $\Lambda\sim1\, GeV$ is the scale of chiral symmetry breaking and $\Theta$ is the coupling strength, determined from the branching ratio of the radiative decay, $\Delta\rightarrow N\gamma$.
