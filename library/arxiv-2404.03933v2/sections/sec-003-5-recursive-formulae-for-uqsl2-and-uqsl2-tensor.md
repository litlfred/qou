Denote by T (lk1 +k0) the indecomposable tilting module over Uq(sl2) of type 1 with the
highest weight lk1 +k0, with 0≤k0≤l− 1, k1≥ 0. Note that modules T (k) =L(k) =W (k)
are irreducible when 0≤k≤l− 1 or k =lk1− 1.
Then we have:
T (k0)⊗T (1) = T (k0 + 1)⊕T (k0− 1), 0≤k0≤l− 2,(4)
T (lk1 +k0)⊗T (1) = T (lk1 +k0 + 1)⊕T (lk1 +k0− 1), 1≤k0≤l− 3, k1≥ 1,
T (lk1 +l− 2)⊗T (1) = T (l(k1 + 1)− 3)⊕T ((k1 + 1)l− 1)⊕T ((k1− 1)l− 1), k 1≥ 2,
T (2l− 2)⊗T (1) = T (2l− 1)⊕T (2l− 3),
T (lk1− 1)⊗T (1) = T (lk1), k 1≥ 1,
T (lk1)⊗T (1) = T (lk1 + 1)⊕ 2T (lk1− 1), k 1≥ 1.
We assume everywhere that T (m) = 0 if m< 0.
Similarly, we can derive recursive tensor product decomposition over the small quantum
group uq. Over uq(sl2) we have the following indecomposable modules that can appear in the
powers of T (1) =L(1) of type 1:
W (k0) =L(k0) =T (k0), for 0≤k0≤l− 2,
T+(l− 1) =T (l− 1),
T+(l +k0) =T (l +k0), for 0≤k0≤l− 2,
T−(l− 1) =T (l− 1)⊗ 1−,
T−(l +k0) =T (l +k0)⊗ 1−, for 0≤k0≤l− 2.
