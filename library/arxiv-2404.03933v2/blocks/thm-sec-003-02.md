Let j ∈Z≥0, and 0 ≤k0 ≤l −2. The multiplicities m(l) l−1,±j(N) and m(l) l+k0,±j(N) are given by the formulae m(l) l−1,±j(N) = X s≥⌊j 2 ⌋ M(l) l−1+j(mod2)l+2sl(N); m(l) l+k0,±j(N) = X s≥⌊j 2 ⌋ M(l) l+k0+j(mod2)l+2sl(N). The upper limit for s can be calculated as ⌊N−(l−1+j(mod2)l) 2l ⌋or ⌊N−(l+k0+j(mod2)l) 2l ⌋, but in fact this is unnecessary because for higher s the coefficients M(l) • (N) under the sum are zero.
