Let G be a ﬁnite group of order divisible by p, where p ∈{2, 3}. Let P ∈Sylp(G) and let B0 be the principal p-block of G. Then |Irrp′(B0)σ1| = p if, and only if, P is cyclic, where Irrp′(B0)σ1 is the set of irreducible characters in B0 with degree relatively prime to p that are ﬁxed under the action of σ1. With the deﬁnition above, σ1 is an element of the subgroup H ≤G consisting of all σ ∈G for which there exists some integer f such that σ(ξ) = ξ p f whenever ξ is a root of unity of order not divisible by p.
