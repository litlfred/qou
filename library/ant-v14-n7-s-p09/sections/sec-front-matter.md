Galois action on the principal block
and cyclic Sylow subgroups
Noelia Rizo, A. A. Schaeﬀer Fry and Carolina Vallejo
We characterize ﬁnite groups G having a cyclic Sylow p-subgroup in terms of the action of a speciﬁc
Galois automorphism on the principal p-block of G, for p = 2, 3. We show that the analog statement for
blocks with arbitrary defect group would follow from the blockwise McKay–Navarro conjecture.
