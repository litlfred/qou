guaranteed, for the following reason. The condition on the r esidues of ω is consistent
with S-duality and triality, as we saw in equation (17.37). Furthe rmore, in using that
condition to ﬁnd F , the “boundary” condition, given by the cubic function ( x −e1u)(x −
e2u)(x −e3u), is also S-dual and triality-invariant. Moreover, the condition on ω led to
a unique determination of F , up to the possibility of redeﬁning x and adding a constant
to u. Therefore, up to such transformations, the solution must h ave all the symmetries of
