Octahedral coordinates from the Wirtinger presentation
Calvin McPhail-Snyder
Duke University
calvin@sl2.site
Abstract
Let ρ be a representation of a knot group (or more generally, the funda-
mental group of a tangle complement) into SL2(C) expressed in terms of
the Wirtinger generators of a diagram D. This diagram also determines
an ideal triangulation of the complement called the octahedral decompo-
sition. ρ induces a hyperbolic structure on the complement of D, and in
this note we give a direct algebraic formula for the geometric parameters
of the octahedral decomposition induced by this structure. Our formula
gives a new, explicit criterion for whether ρ occurs as a critical point of
the diagram’s Neumann-Zagier–Yokota potential function.
