Some easy examples of dualizing complexes over noncommutative ring s are given
in Example 2.3.
The next proposition oﬀers an explanation of the name “dualizing com plex.”
The duality functors associated to R are the contravariant functors
D := R HomA(−,R ) : D(ModA) → D(ModB◦)
D◦ := R HomB◦ (−,R ) : D(ModB◦) → D(ModA).
Proposition 1.3. LetR ∈ D(Mod(A ⊗B◦)) be a dualizing complex.
1. For any M ∈ Df (ModA) one has DM ∈ Df (ModB◦) and M ∼=D◦DM .
