Let R ∈ D(Mod(A ⊗B◦)) be a dualizing complex, and let I be the minimal injective resolution of R in Db(ModA). Let Zi := Ker(Ii →Ii+1) and let M be a ﬁnitely generated left A-module. Then there exist f1,... ,f n ∈ Hom(M,Zi) such that for every N ⊂⋂ j Ker(fj) the natural map Exti A(M,R ) → Exti A(N,R ) is zero; or equivalently, the natural map Exti A(M/N,R ) → Exti A(M,R ) is surjective.
