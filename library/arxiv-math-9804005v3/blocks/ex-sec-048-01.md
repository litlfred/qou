Let us consider a simple case of Theorem 7.1. Let A =B[t] where t is a central variable of degree 1. Let RB be the balanced dualizing complex of B. Then the balanced dualizing complex of A is nothing but RA =RB ⊗k[t] Ω 1 k[t]/k[1] ∼=RB[t](−1)[1] where Ω 1 k[t]/k = k[t] · dt ∼= k[t](−1) as graded k[t]-modules. This follows from [VdB] Theorem 7.1, which states that if B,C are noetherian connected graded k- algebras with balanced dualizing complexes RB andRC respectively, and ifB ⊗C is noetherian, then RB ⊗RC is a balanced dualizing complex over B ⊗C. By Theorem 5.1,A is graded Auslander and Kdim-(resp. GKdim-)Macaulay if and only if B is.
