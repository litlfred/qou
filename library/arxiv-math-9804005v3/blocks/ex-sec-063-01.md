Let A be the quantum plane kq[x,y ] := k⟨x,y ⟩/(yx −qxy) for q ∈ k× with q2 ⁄= 1. The automorphism σ in Proposition 6.18(4) is σ(x) = qx, σ(y) = q−1y (cf. [Ye1] Examples 6.21 and 7.14). Consider the ideal I =A·f ·A where f :=x−y, which is not normal. An easy computation shows that B ∼=k[ǫ] withǫ2 = 0, and ǫ ≡x ≡y (modI). Now consider the graded Ae-module N := Ext 2 A(B,A ). One has Nσ(−2) ∼= B∗ as Ae-modules, so N is killed by qx −q−1y = σ(f ) ∈ A◦, and hence N can not be a B◦-module.
