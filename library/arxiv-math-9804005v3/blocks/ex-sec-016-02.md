Let A be a noetherian commutative regular ring of inﬁnite Krull dimension – see Nagata’s example [Na] Appendix, p. 203 Example 1. The complex R =A is a pointwise dualizing complex (see [RD] Section V.8), and R Hom A(−,A ) : Df (ModA) → Df (ModA) is a duality. However by Theorem 1.13, there is no dualizing complex in Db(Mod(A ⊗B◦)) for any right noetherian k-algebra B.
