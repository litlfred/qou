Both the parallel with the generating function $\mathrm{e}^{| \mathcal{G} |z}$ and the notion that $F$ is the type of "$\mathcal{G}$-coloured finite sets" suggests that it makes sense $F$ should be seen as $E^{\mathcal{G} \times Z}$ - that is, composition of the type $E^{\Box}$ ("finite sets of") with $\mathcal{G} \times Z$ (the product type whose objects are one-element sets together with objects of $\mathcal{G}$ - i.e. $\mathcal{G}$-coloured one-element sets).
