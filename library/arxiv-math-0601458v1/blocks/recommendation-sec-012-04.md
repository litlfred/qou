A morphism between two such objects includes morphisms of the form ([\[eq:typemorphism\]](#eq:typemorphism){reference-type="ref" reference="eq:typemorphism"}) between the objects of $\Psi$ and $\Phi$; this gives bijections between the underlying sets which must be compatible with those which are part of the $\left\langle{\Psi,\Phi}\right\rangle$ objects (of the form ([\[eq:pullbackobject\]](#eq:pullbackobject){reference-type="ref" reference="eq:pullbackobject"})) themselves.
