[]{#harmoscsec label="harmoscsec"}

We have defined structure types as functors of a certain kind (faithful functors $\text{$F : \boldsymbol{\operatorname{{FinSet_0}}}\rightarrow
\boldsymbol{\operatorname{{Set}}}$}$), and made the analogy between these and formal power series. Just as a single formal power series is really only of interest in the setting of the space of all formal power series, so too a given structure type acquires more meaning when we think of it in the setting of all such functors. In particular, one thing we are interested in for the purposes of categorifying quantum mechanics are the categorified versions of algebras of operators on this space.

To see why this is so, we will first describe the Weyl algebra - an algebra of operators on the Hilbert space of states of the quantum harmonic oscillator - then see how to use structure types to categorify it. Readers who are familiar with quantum mechanics may wish to skip to section [\[sec:strtypeweyl\]](#sec:strtypeweyl){reference-type="ref" reference="sec:strtypeweyl"}
