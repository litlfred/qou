Also, we can use any other diagram representing K for this construction, as
long as the twist number (the sum of the signs of crossings) remains the same.
It follows that we always obtain a periodic knot if we apply a ‘periodic satellite
construction ’, that is we choose a diagram representing K with the twist number
divisible by p, then we apply the ‘k parallel strings construction’ taking k not
divisible by p and finally we insert the C’ tangle in a suitable manner. This
enables us to iterate the use of our criterion.
