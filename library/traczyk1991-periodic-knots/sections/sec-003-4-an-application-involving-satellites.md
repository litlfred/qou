4 An application involving satellites
As announced in the Introduction the criterion for periodicity described in this
paper excludes the possibility of period 5 for all but 16 10-crossing knots. Let
us consider in detail one of them, the 10146. The P polynomial of 10146 is
trivial, so the criterion does not exclude the possibility of the considered knot
being 5-periodic. However, the range of our criterion may be enhanced in the
following manner.

It follows that we always obtain a periodic knot if we apply a 'periodic satellite
construction ', that is we choose a diagram representing K with the twist number
divisible by p, then we apply the k parallel strings construction' taking k not
divisible by p and finally we insert the C' tangle in a suitable manner. This
enables us to iterate the use of our criterion.
