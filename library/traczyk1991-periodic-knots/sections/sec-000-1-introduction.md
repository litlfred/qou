1 Introduction
The purpose of this paper is to demonstrate an unexpectedly strong regularity
of the Po polynomial of periodic knots. Here Po is the part of the skein polynomial
P written in variables l and m as in [L-MJ, gathering the terms of degree
O in the second variable (m). We consider knots of cyclic period p, where p
is a prime number (p+2). A knot K is said to have cyclic period p if it may
be isotoped into a form invariant under the standard rotation of R3 by a 2π/p
angle. Throughout this paper we consider the Po polynomial with coefficients
in Zp. It is well known that only terms of even degrees appear in P (for a
link of odd number of components -- a knot in particular). Our main theorem
is the following.
Theorem 1.1 If K is a p-periodic knot (p + 2) and the linking number of the periodic
version of K with the axis of rotation is equal to k, then in the polynomial Po(K)
-Ea2; j2i the sum a2i + a2i+ 2 of two consecutive coefficients is equal to O, except
possibly when 2i+ 1 = ±k(mod p).
Let us observe that Theorem 1.1 implies that the degree of the lowest term
appearing in Po(K) must be congruent modulo p to ±k+1 and the degree
linking number of K around the axis of rotation is uniquely determined by
the Po polynomial. It follows that if we want to use the theorem as a criterion
for periodicity, we do not have to consider several cases of possible (mod p)-
values of the linking number: the unique (up to sign) possible value is immediate-
ly obvious. The following example illustrates the theorem.

As an example application of the criterion presented in this paper let us
mention that it excludes the possibility of the knots 1018, 1024, 1055, 10132
and 1013z having period 5. These were mistakenly classified as 5-periodic in
[B-ZJ. This correction to the tables was also obtained by Kodama and Sakuma
[K-S]. To further illustrate the power of the criterion let us describe how it
works in case of period 5 for 10-crossing knots. There are 165 10-crossing prime
knots listed in [Rj. Of these, just two are in fact 5-periodic: 10123 and 10124.
When applied to the remaining 163 the criterion immediately excludes 149.
