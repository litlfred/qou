1 Introduction

The purpose of this paper is to demonstrate an unexpectedly strong regularity
of the Ry polynomial of periodic knots. Here P) is the part of the skein polynomial
P written in variables | and m as in [L-M], gathering the terms of degree
0 in the second variable (m). We consider knots of cyclic period p, where p
is a prime number (p+2). A knot K is said to have cyclic period p if it may
be isotoped into a form invariant under the standard rotation of R* by a 2x/p
angle. Throughout this paper we consider the PR, polynomial with coefficients
in Z,. It is well known that only terms of even degrees appear in P (for a
link of odd number of components — a knot in particular). Our main theorem
