Since f is harmonic and since ∇2 log f = ∇2f f −∇f ⊗2 f 2 , we see that in order for f to be log-concave, the Hessian of f can have at most one positive eigenvalue, due to which the family of log-concave functions, or in other words functions satisfying condition (4) with β = 0, is rather restricted.
