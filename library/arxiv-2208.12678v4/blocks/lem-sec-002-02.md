The (1 + ε) bi-Lipschitz property in the prior lemma is a weaker notion of an isometric embedding. In proving the existence of minimizers for the M¨obius-Plateau energy, we will need to ensure that arc length is preserved in the limit of a minimizing sequence. Were we afforded the luxury of our curves being isometric embeddings of fixed length, length preservation in the limit would trivially follow.
