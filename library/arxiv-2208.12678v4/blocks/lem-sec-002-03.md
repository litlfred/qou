Let γi : R/LiZ →R3 be a sequence of rectifiable simple closed curves of uniformly bounded energy, EM. Assume that the curves are all parametrized by arc-length and that L = limi→∞Li > 0 exists. If γi(0) is a bounded sequence of points, then there is a subsequence γik of γi which converges uniformly to a rectifiable simple curve γ : R/LZ →R3. Moreover, EM is sequentially lower semicontinuous under this convergence, i.e., EM[γ] ≤lim inf ik→∞EM[γik]
