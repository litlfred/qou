Let M 3 be a triangulated three-dimensional manifold. In this paper
we deﬁne a combinatorial analogue of scalar curvature forM 3, and also a
combinatorial analogue of conformal deformation of the metric. We further
deﬁne a functional S on the combinatorial conformal deformation space,
show that S is concave, and show that critical points ofS correspond pre-
cisely to metrics of constant combinatorial scalar curvature onM
