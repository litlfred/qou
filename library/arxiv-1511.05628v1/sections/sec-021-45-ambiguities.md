[]{#sub.ambiguities label="sub.ambiguities"}

The state integral [\[SSk\]](#SSk){reference-type="eqref" reference="SSk"} has an intrinsic multiplicative ambiguity [@D-levelk Eqn. 5.8]. Namely, it is only defined modulo multiplication by factors (at worst) of the form

$$\big(e^{\tfrac{\pi^2}{6k\hbar}}\big)^{a_1}  
\big(\zeta^{\frac{1}{24}}\big)^{a_2} 
\big(e^{\tfrac{\hbar}{24k}}\big)^{a_3}\,,\qquad a_1,a_2,a_3\in \mathbb{Z}\,.$$

The second and third factors affect $\tau_{k}$ and $S_{2,k}$, respectively, in the asymptotic expansion. Higher-order terms in the expansion are unaffected. These ambiguities in the state integral are consistent with those discovered experimentally for $\phi_{\gamma,\zeta}$, as discussed in Section [\[sub.inv\]](#sub.inv){reference-type="ref" reference="sub.inv"}.
