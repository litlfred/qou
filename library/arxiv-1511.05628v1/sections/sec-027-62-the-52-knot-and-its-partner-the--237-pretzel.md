The $5_2$ knot is a hyperbolic knot with volume $2.8281\dots$ with $3$ ideal tetrahedra and trace field $F_{5_2}=\mathbb Q(\alpha)$ where $\alpha=0.8774 \dots - 0.7448 \dots i$ is a root of $$x^3 -x^2 + 1 = 0$$ $F_{5_2}$ is of type $[1,1]$ with discriminant $-23$.

The (mirror image of) the $(-2,3,7)$ pretzel knot is a hyperbolic knot same volume and trace field as the $5_2$ knot. In fact, the complements of the two knots can be obtained from the same triple of ideal tetrahedra with two different face pairing rules. So, we will use $\alpha$ and $F$ as in Section [\[sub.52\]](#sub.52){reference-type="ref" reference="sub.52"}. The 1-loop invariant at $k=1$ and its norm is given by
