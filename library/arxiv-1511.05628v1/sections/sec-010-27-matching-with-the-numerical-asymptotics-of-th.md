[]{#sub.matching label="sub.matching"}

Numerical asymptotics of the Kashaev invariant were obtained by Zagier and the second author in [@GZ1] for several knots, summarized in Table [1](#tab.num.kashaev){reference-type="ref" reference="tab.num.kashaev"}. Our $n$-loop invariants at level $k$, presented in Section [\[sec.compute\]](#sec.compute){reference-type="ref" reference="sec.compute"}, agree with the numerical computations of [@GZ1]. This is a strong consistency test for all computational methods.
