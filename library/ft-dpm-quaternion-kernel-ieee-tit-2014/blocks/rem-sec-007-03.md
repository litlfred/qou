Since the QRKHS is built upon a left-module, and not a ﬁeld as the standard RKHS, the derivation of Theorems 1 and 2 conﬁrms that commutativity is not a requirement for constructing feature spaces over division rings and also paves the way for the study of relationships between QRKHS built over left- and right-modules. We would also like to emphasise that the aim of the proofs provided is not claim a radical difference between Theorems 1 and 2, and their real versions, but to show that although the corresponding proofs follow the same criteria, the quaternion case requires more attention due to the lack of commutativity.
