Composition-Diamond lemma for modules was ﬁrst formulated and pro ved by S.-J. Kang
and K.-H. Lee in [15] and [19]. According to their approach, a Gr¨ obne r-Shirshov basis
of a cyclic module M over an algebra A is a pair ( S, T ), where S is the set of the
deﬁning relations of A, A = k⟨X|S⟩ and T is the deﬁning relations for the A-module
AM = modA⟨e|T ⟩. Then Kang-Lee’s Lemma says that ( S, T ) is a Gr¨ obner-Shirshov pair
for A-module AM = modA⟨e|T ⟩ if S is a Gr¨ obner-Shirshov basis of A and T is closed
under the right-justiﬁed composition with respect to S, and for f ∈ S, g ∈ T , such that
(f, g )w is deﬁned and ( f, g )w ≡ 0 mod(S, T ; w).
They gave applications of this lemma for irreducible modules over sln(k) [16], Specht
modules over Hecke algebras and Ariki-Koike algebras in [17] and [18]. Some years later,
