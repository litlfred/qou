3.5.1. Generalized Chern character. Classically, the Chern character is a homomorphism of rings from
the topological K-theory of a manifold into the de Rham cohomology of the manifold. Giv en a vector
bundle on a manifold M , the Chern character map produces an element in the de Rham coho mology
of the manifold. In the algebraic framework, a commutative associa tive algebra A plays the role of the
manifoldM , and a vector bundle in topological K-theory is replaced by a ﬁnitely-generated projective
module over A in algebraic K-theory. Passing to the non-commutative setting where A is no longer
assumed commutative, the Chern character map can still be deﬁne d, giving a homomorphism from
K0(A) into the cyclic homology of A (see chapter 8 of [41]). The zeroth cyclic homology HC 0(A) is
isomorphic to the zeroth Hochschild homology HH 0(A) [41, Section 2.1.12], so we can view the Chern
character as a map from K0(A) → HH0(A). In this section we study a further generalization of the
Chern character map.
