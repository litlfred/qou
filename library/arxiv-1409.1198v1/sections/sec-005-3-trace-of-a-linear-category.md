In this section we deﬁne trace or zeroth Hochschild homology group s for linear and additive cate-
gories, and discuss their properties.
A category C is called linear if its hom-spaces are equipped with structures of abelian groups an d
the composition maps are bilinear. Such a structure is sometimes calle d an Ab-category, or a category
enriched in the monoidal category Ab of abelian groups. A linear functor between two linear categories
C and D is a functor F : C → D such that for x,y ∈ Ob(C) the map F : C(x,y ) → D (F (x),F (y)) is an
abelian group homomorphism.
