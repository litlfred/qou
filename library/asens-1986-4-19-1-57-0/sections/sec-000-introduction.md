for computing the index and the entropy for pairs of hyperfinite factors N c R that can

an open problem whether any pair N c= R of finite index can be realized in this way. But
anyway it seems reasonable to believe that, if ^ denotes the set of all possible values of
the index of subf actors with trivial relative commutant, then, as in the case <4, for any

such that the corresponding pair of factors is the inductive limit of pairs of finite

index k (e. g. k < 3 + 2 /2) it would be enough to construct sequences of finite dimensional
algebras such that the limit of the entropies equals the limit of the logarithm of the

By our results this would avoid the computation of the relative commutant
(which is usually very difficult).
