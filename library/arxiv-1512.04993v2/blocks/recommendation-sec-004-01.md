The Aharonov-Anandan-Bohm bound involves the standard deviation of the energy, \begin{equation} \textrm{orthogonality time} \ \geq \frac{\pi \hbar }{2 \Delta E} . \end{equation} Since time is not an operator in quantum mechanics, the energy-time uncertainty relation does not say that the time must be uncertain \cite{Anandan:1990fq,Aharonov:1961mka}, instead it says that time must be spent to evolve to an orthogonal state.
