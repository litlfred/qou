8.2 Open questions
A number of open questions remain.
8.2.1 The deﬁnition of complexity
In this paper we have deﬁned complexity as the number of primitive gates in the mini-
mal quantum circuit that builds our state. This deﬁnition is not ideal. For example, in
our deﬁnition the value of the complexity depends on the choice of the set of primitive
gates.
One open question is where there is a better deﬁnition. In particular, is there
a deﬁnition that is suitable for continuous Hamiltonian systems and that can give
meaning to the prefactor in the bound Eq. 2.14?
Further, assuming such a deﬁnition can be found, is the universality of our re-
sults indicating a universal dynamics of black holes, independent of mass, dimension,
rotation, etc?
For an initial attempt to address these questions, see Appendix A.1.
