RESURGENCE, HABIRO ELEMENTS AND STRANGE IDENTITIES
SAMUEL CREW, VERONICA FANTINI, ANKUSH GOSWAMI, ROBERT OSBURN,
AND CAMPBELL WHEELER
Abstract. We prove resurgence properties for the Borel transform of a formal power series
associated to elements in the Habiro ring that come from radial limits of partial theta series via
strange identities. As an application, we prove a conjecture in quantum topology due to Costin
and Garoufalidis for two families of torus knots.
