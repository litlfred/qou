[]{#sec:remarks label="sec:remarks"} In order to apply Theorems [\[main\]](#main){reference-type="ref" reference="main"} and [\[main2\]](#main2){reference-type="ref" reference="main2"} and thus verify Conjecture [\[cgconj\]](#cgconj){reference-type="ref" reference="cgconj"} for all torus knots $T(s,t)$, one needs to prove the relevant strange identity in [\[Hstrange\]](#Hstrange){reference-type="eqref" reference="Hstrange"}. The first obstruction in this task is finding an explicit "non-cyclotomic\" expansion for $J_N(T(s,t); q)$ from which an element in $\mathcal{H}$ such as the one in [\[xm\]](#xm){reference-type="eqref" reference="xm"} can be extracted. The Rosso-Jones formula for $J_N(T(s,t); q)$ does not appear to be sufficient [@morton page 132]. Instead, one should consider the walks along braids method in [@armond; @bs]. The second obstruction is in determining the underlying $q$-series identity which implies the strange identity. Thus, it would be of substantial interest to further develop the techniques in [@lovejoy]. Finally, what can one say about Conjecture [\[cgconj\]](#cgconj){reference-type="ref" reference="cgconj"} for satellite or hyperbolic knots?
