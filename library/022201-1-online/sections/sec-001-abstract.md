We establish a polynomial-time approximation algorithm for partition functions of quantum spin models at high temperature. Our algorithm
is based on the quantum cluster expansion of Netoˇcný and Redig and the cluster expansion approach to designing algorithms due to Helmuth,
Perkins, and Regts. Similar results have previously been obtained by related methods, and our main contribution is a simple and slightly
sharper analysis for the case of pairwise interactions on bounded-degree graphs.
