We have discussed how classical algorithms based on cluster expansion methods apply to quantum spin systems at high temperature.
Our focus has been on conveying the simplicity of the method, which has appeared previously in other forms.1,2 We note that it may be
possible to use the Markov chain polymer approach of Ref. 25 to obtain an algorithm with an improved runtime.
For discrete classical spin systems, expansion methods have also been used at low temperatures, i.e., when β ≫1.4,26–29 It would be inter-
esting to adapt these methods to quantum systems, e.g., by developing algorithms based on Pirogov–Sinai methods for quantum perturbations
of classical systems.30 We remark, however, that it seems difficult to use this approach for low-temperature quantum systems with an infinite
degeneracy of ground states, for example, when the set of ground states possesses a continuous symmetry.
