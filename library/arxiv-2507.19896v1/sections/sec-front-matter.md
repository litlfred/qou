Generalized Markov traces and Jucys-Murphy elements
Kostiantyn Tolmachov, Heorhii Zhylinskyi
Abstract
We give a simple construction of Markov traces for Iwahori-Hecke algebras associated
with infinite series of crystallographic Coxeter groups. In types B and D it is new,
and generalizes a known construction in type A employing symmetric polynomials in
multiplicative Jucys-Murphy elements.
