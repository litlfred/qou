In Section 3 we first define the Markov traces and give, as a warm-up, a construction
of a Markov trace defined by a uniform formula in all types A, B, D. See Theorem 3.1.4.
In Section 3.2 we recall the results of Geck and Lambropoulou and give the formulas
for the Markov traces in types B and D depending on a free parameter (Theorem 3.2.2
and Corollary 3.2.5, respectively). In Section 3.3 we specialize our results to obtain the
geometric Markov traces.
