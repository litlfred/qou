The functionals $\phi_{n-1}$ and $\phi_n$, $n \geq 2$ must satisfy \myitem[.]{(U1)}\label{item:5} \( \phi_n(\iota(h)) = \rho \phi_{n-1}(h). \) This property is a generalization of a rule for removing an unlinked circle in the theory of link invariants. \myitem[.]{(M2)}\label{item:6} \( \phi_n(\iota(h)t_n) = \mu \phi_{n-1}(h). \) This property is a generalization of the second Markov move from the theory of link invariants. \end{enumerate}
