[]{#s:bulkq label="s:bulkq"} In the previous section, we derived the $q$-conformal two-point function by constraining its form [\[res\]](#res){reference-type="eqref" reference="res"} through the requirement of invariance under the global part of the $q$-conformal group. Furthermore, we interpreted its Fourier transform as encoding information about the quasi-normal modes [\[qnm\]](#qnm){reference-type="eqref" reference="qnm"} of a dual bulk system. This observation hints at the presence of a black hole in the bulk, whose properties we now seek to identify.
