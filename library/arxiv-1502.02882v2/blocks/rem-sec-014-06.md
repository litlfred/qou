As in Remark 4.3, the pivotal indicator p(Xi) of a self-dual simple object is closely related, but not equal to, the 2nd Frobenius-Schur in- dicator ν2(Xi) of a self-dual simple object, as deﬁned in [23]. The former is deﬁned using only the underlying fusion category, while the latter depends on a pivotal structure γ, whence we can write it as νγ 2 (Xi). The precise relationship is νγ 2 (Xi) = γip(Xi) where γi = ±1 is the sign associated to the self-dual object Xi in the pivotal structure γ (see Section 5.1).
