Firstly, every ring homomorphism \[ [\Rep(G)] \rightarrow \mathbb{C} \] must take the form $V \mapsto \Tr_V(g)$ for some fixed $g \in G$ --- because a character like this certainly {\em is} a ring homomorphism, and there are as many such distinct characters as there are conjugacy classes in the group, which must exhaust all the ring homomorphisms since $[\Rep(G)]_\mathbb{C}$ is isomorphic to the space of functions on the conjugacy classes.
