Deﬁnition 6.0.1. Deﬁne the ring of multiple modular values with respect to the
group Γ = SL 2(Z) to be the subring of C generated by the coeﬃcients of CS.
The series CS is a kind of analogue of Drinfeld’s associator Z. Its terms of
degree 1 in the Ai are precisely the Eichler-Shimura integrals (6 .1) and (6 .2).
