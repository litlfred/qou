described in §5 can be directly related to the appearance of special values of L-

In conclusion, a rather optimistic hope is that a theory of motivic multiple
modular values for congruence subgroups of SL2(Z) might provide a more natural
construction of the periods of mixed Tate motives over cyclotomic ﬁelds (and much
more) than the motivic fundamental groupoid of the projective line minus Nth
roots of unity, which suﬀers from the depth defect in the case N=1 (§5), and from
absent periods in non-exceptional cases such as N=5 (§4).
