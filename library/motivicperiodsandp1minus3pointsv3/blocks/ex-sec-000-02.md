Let T be any category of mixed Tate motives over a number ﬁeld. It contains the Lefschetz motive L = Q(−1), which is the motive H1(P1\{0, ∞}). Its de Rham cohomology is the Q-vector space spanned by the class [ dz z ] and its Betti homology is spanned by a small positive loop γ0 around 0. The Lefschetz motivic period is Lm = [L, [ dz z ], [γ0]] ∈ Pm T . Its period is per(Lm) = 2πi. It transforms, under the rational points of the de Rham Galois group of T , by Lm 7→λLm, for any λ ∈Q×.
