The Q-vector space spanned by multiple zeta values of weight N has dimension at most dN, where the integers dN are deﬁned in (1.6). So far, this does not use the action of the motivic Galois group, only a bound on the size of the motivic periods of MT (Z). The role of P1\{0, 1, ∞} is that the automorphism group of its fundamental groupoid yields a formula for the coaction (1.4) on the motivic multiple zeta values (§2.5). The main theorem uses this coaction in an essential way, and is inspired by a conjecture of M. Hoﬀman [25].
