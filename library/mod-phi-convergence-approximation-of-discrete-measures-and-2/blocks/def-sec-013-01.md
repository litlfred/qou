— Let (νn)n∈N be a general scheme of approximation of a sequence of random variables (Xn)n∈N that converges mod-φ with parameters λn. The derived scheme of approximation with constant residue is the new scheme (σn)n∈N, deﬁned by the Fourier transforms c σn(ξ) = eλnφ(ξ) χ(ξ), where χ(ξ) = limn→∞χn(ξ) is the limit of the residues of the law νn (with the notations of Deﬁnition 1.10).
