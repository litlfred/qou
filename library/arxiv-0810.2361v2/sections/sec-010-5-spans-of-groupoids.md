[]{#sec:spans label="sec:spans"}

We have already seen how essentially finite groupoids give rise to 2-vector spaces. In this section, we will show the weak functoriality of these assignments. In particular, we first must describe how our 2-functor $\Lambda$ will produce 2-linear maps from spans of groupoids.
