The idea is that given a span of groupoids, as in Figure [2](#fig:grpd-span){reference-type="ref" reference="fig:grpd-span"} (which suppresses the homomorphisms labelling the strands in the span, but should be compared with Figure [1](#fig:set-span){reference-type="ref" reference="fig:set-span"}), there will be a "transfer" 2-linear map from the KV 2-vector space associated to the source of the span, to that associated to the target.
