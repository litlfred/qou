In general, the coherence conditions for the "unit" isomorphism $$U_A : 1_{\Lambda(A)} \stackrel{\sim}{\mathop{\rightarrow}} \Lambda(1_A)$$ which accomplishes weak preservation of identities, say that it must make the following commute for any span $X: A_1 \mathop{\rightarrow}A_2$: $$\label{eq:wk2fncunit} \xymatrix{ \Lambda(X) & & \\ & & \\ \Lambda(X) \circ \Lambda(1_{A_1}) \ar[uu]_{1 \otimes U_{A_1}} & & \Lambda(X \circ 1_{A_1})\ar[uull]_{\Lambda(r_X)} \ar[ll]_{\beta_{X,1_{A_1}}} }$$ where $r_{A_1}$ is the right unitor for $A_1$.
