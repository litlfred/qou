ON THE COMPUTATION OF THE DIFFERENCE GALOIS GROUPS OF ORDER
THREE EQUATIONS.
THOMAS DREYFUS AND MARINA POULET
Abstract. In this paper we consider the problem of computing the diﬀerence Galois
groups of order three equations for a large class of diﬀerence operators including the shift
operator (Case S), the q-diﬀerence operator (Case Q), the Mahler operator (Case M) and
the elliptic case (Case E). We show that the general problem can be reduced to several
ancillary problems. We prove criteria to detect the irreducible and imprimitive Galois
groups. Finally, we give a suﬃcient condition of diﬀerential transcendence of solutions
of order three diﬀerence equations. We also compute the diﬀerence Galois group of an
equation suggested by Wadim Zudilin.
