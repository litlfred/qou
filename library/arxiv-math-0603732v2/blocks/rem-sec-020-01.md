Ideally the twisted Hochschild dimension should be deﬁned in terms of twisted Hochschild homology HHσ n(A). Unfortunately our computation in later sections uses knowledge of Hn(A, σA); and we don’t know in general when σ will be diagonalisable. Further, it’s not immediately obvious whether Hn(A, σA)̸ = 0 is equivalent to HHσ n(A)̸ = 0 when σ is not diagonalisable. The above deﬁnition is therefore chosen so as to ﬁnesse this uncertainty.
