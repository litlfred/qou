The starting point for the work described in this paper was the observa-
tion by Hadﬁeld and Kr¨ahmer [HK1] that, when calculating the Hochschild co-
homology of the quantised function algebra Oq(SL(2)), twisting the coeﬃcient
bimodule Oq(SL(2)) by a suitable algebra automorphism ν, and so calculating
H∗(Oq(SL(2)), νOq(SL(2))1), avoids the “dimension drop” which occurs if one
works with untwisted coeﬃcients.
Our initial aim was to understand why this
happens, and to extend their result to other quantised function algebras and then
to other classes of Hopf algebra A.
