Some homological and Hopf algebra background is given in §§1 and 2 respec-
tively. Twisted Hochschild homology is recalled and studied in §3, and Theorem
0.4(a) is proved. Rigid dualising complexes are deﬁned in §4, and Theorems 0.2,
0.3 and 0.6 are proved. In §5 we study twisted Hochschild cohomology, and prove
Theorem 0.4(b) and Corollary 0.4.
