[]{#xxsec0.3 label="xxsec0.3"} To describe the Nakayama automorphism of $A$ we need to recall from [@LWZ] the definition of the *left integral* of $A$. When $A$ is AS-Gorenstein of dimension $d$, $\mathrm{Ext}^i_A({_{A}k},{_{A}A})$ is 0 except when $i=d$, and $\mathrm{Ext}^d_A({_{A}k},{_{A}A})$, the left integral $\textstyle \int^l_A$ of $A$, has $k$-dimension 1. Thus $\mathrm{Ext}^d_A({_{A}k},{_{A}A}) \cong \, {^1k^{{\xi}}}$, where the right $A-$action on the trivial module $k$ is twisted by a certain left winding automorphism ${\xi}$ of $A$. (See $\S\S$<!-- -->1.3 and 2.5.) Let $S$ be the antipode of $A$. We prove, also in Proposition 4.5:

::: theorem
Let $A$ be as in Theorem [\[xxsec0.2\]](#xxsec0.2){reference-type="ref" reference="xxsec0.2"}. Then the Nakayama automorphism $\nu$ of $A$ is $S^2 {\xi}$.
:::
