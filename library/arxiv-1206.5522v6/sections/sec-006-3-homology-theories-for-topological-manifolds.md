Factorization homology evaluates on a general manifold as the average over 'factorizations' of the manifold into disks of the values of an $n$-disk algebra on such disks. We make this precise by defining factorization homology as the left Kan extension of an $n$-disk algebra along the inclusion $\mathop{\mathrm{{\mathcal D}\sf isk}}_n \hookrightarrow \mathop{\mathrm{{\mathcal M}\mathsf{fld}}}_n$.\
For this section, we fix a symmetric monoidal $\infty$-category $\mathcal V$.
