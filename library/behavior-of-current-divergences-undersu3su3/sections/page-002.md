Many authors have taken steps in the same direction,”
and in particular we should mention the work of
Glashow and Weinberg‘ as being the most closely
related to ours. Still, we find that our approach leads
to results and to a formulation of the situation that, to
the best of our knowledge, have not yet been reported
in the literature.
