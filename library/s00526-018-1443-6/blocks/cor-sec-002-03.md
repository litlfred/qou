Let γ : R/Z →Rn be a closed, simple, and by arc-length parametrized curve of ﬁnite Möbius energy. If γ is a critical point of the Möbius energy E, then γ is analytic. There exist many results on the analyticity of solutions of classical analytic nonlinear elliptic partial differential equations. In 1904, Bernstein showed the analyticity of a solution of class C3 to a nonlinear analytic elliptic equation of two independent variables in [3] by estimating higher order derivatives of solutions.
