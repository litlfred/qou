Let C be an autonomous category which is centralizable, that is, such that the trivial Hopf monad 1C is centralizable. In that case, the centralizer Z = Z1C and the double D1C of 1C coincide. Then, by Theorem 4.2, Z is a quasitriangular Hopf monad on C such that Z(C) ∼= Z- C as braided category. In particular Z(C) is seen as the category of modules over a quasitriangular Hopf monad. In Section 5.1, we explicitly describe Z in terms of C when C is a fusion category.
