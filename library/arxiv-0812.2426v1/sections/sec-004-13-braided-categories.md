A *braided* category is a monoidal category endowed with a *braiding*, that is, a natural isomorphism $\tau_{X,Y} \colon X \otimes Y \to Y \otimes X$ satisfying: $\tau_{X,Y\otimes Z}=(\mathrm{id}_Y \otimes \tau_{X,Z})(\tau_{X,Y} \otimes \mathrm{id}_Z)$ and $\tau_{X
\otimes Y,Z}=(\tau_{X,Z} \otimes \mathrm{id}_Y)(\mathrm{id}_X \otimes \tau_{Y,Z})$.
