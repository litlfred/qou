[]{#sect-coend-cat label="sect-coend-cat"} Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an autonomous category. If it exists, the coend $C=\int^{X \in \mathcal{C}} \leftidx{^\vee}{\!X}{} \otimes X$ of the functor $F\colon
\mathcal{C}^\mathrm{op}\times \mathcal{C}\to \mathcal{C}$ defined by $F(X,Y)=\leftidx{^\vee}{\!X}{}\otimes Y$ is called the *coend of $\mathcal{C}$*. The object $C$ is then a coalgebra in $\mathcal{C}$ which coacts universally on the objects of $\mathcal{C}$ via the the (right) coaction: $$\delta_X=(\mathrm{id}_X \otimes i_X)(\mathrm{coev}_X \otimes \mathrm{id}_X) \colon X \to X \otimes C,$$ where $i_Y \colon\leftidx{^\vee}{\!Y}{} \otimes Y \to C$ is the universal dinatural transformation.

Furthermore, when $\mathcal{C}$ is braided, $C$ is a Hopf algebra in $\mathcal{C}$ (see [@Maj1; @Lyu1]).
