Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a sovereign category which admits a coend. The *left* and *right dimensions of $\mathcal{C}$* are defined respectively as the left and right dimensions of its coend. These dimensions are actually independent of the choice of sovereign structure on $\mathcal{C}$. If they coincide (for instance when $\mathcal{C}$ is a ribbon category or $\mathcal{C}$ is a fusion category), they are called the *dimension of $\mathcal{C}$* and denoted $\dim \mathcal{C}$.
