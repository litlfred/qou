A *sovereign category* is a left autonomous category endowed with a strong monoidal natural transformation $\phi_X\colon X
\to \leftidx{^{\vee\vee}}{\!X}{}$. Such a transformation is then an isomorphism. A sovereign category is actually autonomous. Furthermore, in a sovereign category $\mathcal{C}$, one can define the *left and right traces* of an endomorphism $f\colon X \to X$ as: $$\begin{aligned}
&\mathrm{tr}_l(f)=\mathrm{ev}_X(\mathrm{id}_{\leftidx{^\vee}{\!X}{}} \otimes f\phi_X^{-1}) \mathrm{coev}_{\leftidx{^\vee}{\!X}{}} \in \mathrm{End}_\mathcal{C}(\mathbb{1}), \\
&\mathrm{tr}_r(f)=\widetilde{\mathrm{ev}}_X( f\phi_{{X}^{\vee\vee}} \otimes \mathrm{id}_{{X}^\vee}) \widetilde{\mathrm{coev}}_{{X}^\vee} \in \mathrm{End}_\mathcal{C}(\mathbb{1}),
\end{aligned}$$ and the *left and right dimensions* of an object $X$ as $\dim_l(X)=\mathrm{tr}_l(\mathrm{id}_X)$ and $\dim_r(X)=\mathrm{tr}_r(\mathrm{id}_X)$. We have $\dim_r(X)=\dim_l(\leftidx{^\vee}{\!X}{})$.
