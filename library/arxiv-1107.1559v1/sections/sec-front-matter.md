Abstract
Starting with the quaternionic formulation of isospin SU(2) group, we have derived the
relations for diﬀerent components of isospin with quark states. Extending this formalism to
the case of SU(3) group we have considered the theory of octonion variables. Accordingly, the
octonion splitting of SU(3) group have been reconsidered and various commutation relations for
SU(3) group and its shift operators are also derived and veriﬁed for diﬀerent iso-spin multiplets
i.e. I, U and V - spins.
Keywords: SU(3), Quaternions, Octonions and Gell Mann matrices.
