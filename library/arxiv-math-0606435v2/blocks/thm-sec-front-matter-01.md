If L is a strongly quasi-positive link, and has braid index 3, then L is has a strongly quasi-positive 3-braid representation. This result is an application of the work in [St2] on Xu’s normal form [Xu]. It legitimizes the term “strongly quasi- positive 3-braid link”, which otherwise would be linguistically sloppy, for it may mean a 3-braid link that is strongly quasi-positive, or the link (which is the closure) of a strongly quasi-positive 3-braid.
