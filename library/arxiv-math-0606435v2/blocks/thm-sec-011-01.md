Let L be a strongly quasi-positive 3-braid link. Then the following are equivalent: 1) L’s minimal genus surface is a Hopf plumbing, 2) L’s minimal genus surface is a ﬁber surface, 3) maxdeg∆(L) = 1 −χ(L) and maxcf∆(L) = ±1, 4) L’s Xu normal form is not R, with syllable length of R divisible by 3. In other words, the Xu normal form is not an extension (in the sense of deﬁnition 2.1) of [(123)k] for k > 0. 5) Some minimal band form of L contains σk 1σl 2σm 1 or σk 2σl 1σm 2 as subword for k,l,m > 0, or is σk 1σl 2.
