Ishikawa asks in [I] whether strongly quasi-positive knots satisfy the equality TB = 2gs −1, where TB is the maximal Thurston-Bennequin invariant. The proof of the lemma shows that there are inﬁnitely many strongly quasi-positive 3-braid knots with TB < 2gs −1: take any of the knots with l > 2.
