We recall brieﬂy the deﬁnition of Gauß sum invariants. They were introduced ﬁrst in [Fi] for braids, and later [Fi2, PV]
for knots. It is known that all they give formulas for Vassiliev invariants.
Deﬁnition 2.2 ([Fi2]) A Gauß diagram of a knot diagram is an oriented circle with arrows connecting points on
it mapped to a crossing and oriented from the preimage of the u ndercrossing (underpass) to the preimage of the
overcrossing (overpass).
We will call a pair of crossings whose arrows intersect in the Gauß diagram a linked pair.
