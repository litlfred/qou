The Murnaghan--Nakayama rule expresses $\chi^{\bm{\la}}_{\bm{\mu}}$ in terms of irreducible characters where one part is removed from the lower multipartition $\bm{\mu}$. In contrast, Corollary \ref{c:dualMN2} computes $\chi^{\bm{\la}}_{\bm{\mu}}$ using irreducible characters in which at least one part is removed from the upper multipartition $\bm{\la}$. For this reason, we refer to equation \eqref{e:dualMN2} as the dual Murnaghan--Nakayama rule for $\chi^{\bm{\la}}_{\bm{\mu}}$.
