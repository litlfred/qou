Remark 4.5. Pfeiffer’s recursion in [Pfe97, Sec. 12] computes irreducible character values on the minimal-length representatives of conjugacy classes in the type B Weyl group,
and its coefficients in the hook-case may depend on additional content-type data of the
embedding. In contrast, our specialization is obtained from the Frobenius–type formula for
the Ariki–Koike algebra and yields a uniform recurrence on our standard elements, with
coefficients determined solely by the generalized (double) ribbon statistics cc( · ) and ht( · )
(together with the parameter choice ϵ).
