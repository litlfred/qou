If p and q are coprime positive integers then the (p, q) cable of K is smoothly concordant to the (p, q) cable of J in a smooth Z[ 1 p]-homology S3 × [0, 1] if and only if K is smoothly concordant to J in a smooth homology Z[ 1 p]-homology S3 × [0, 1]. The case p = 2 of Corollary 3 was proved previously by the third author and indeed was one of the inspirations for the current paper. An important ingredient in our proof is a well-known relationship between concordance of knots and ho- mology cobordism of certain 3-manifolds associated to the knots via surgery.
