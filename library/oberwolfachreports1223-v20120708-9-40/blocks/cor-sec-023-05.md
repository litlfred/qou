Suppose G ∼= M ⋊Z. If the coordinate ring of G is a principal ideal domain, then every ﬁnitely generated para-G group is isomorphic to G. The Lamplighter group is among numerous examples covered by this last corol- lary. We prove a classifying theorem for para-equivalence of ﬁnitely generated, resid- ually nilpotent, metabelian groups.
