Deﬁne the Ideal Class Monoid of G as follows: Cℓ(G) = {S-fractional submodules of S−1[G, G]} Aut(G) . Isomorphism classes of groups para-equivalent to G lie in one-to-one correspon- dence to elements of Cℓ(G). Here Aut(G) acts on the module S−1[G, G] by conjugation, and thus on the set of S-fractional sub-modules. We apply this result in the talk to compute para-equivalence classes of torus bundles over S1.
