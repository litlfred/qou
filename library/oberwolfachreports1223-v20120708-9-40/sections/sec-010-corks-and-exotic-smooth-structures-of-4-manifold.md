Cagri Karakurt
It is known that any two simply connected homotopy equivalent closed smooth
4-manifolds diﬀer by a surgery along a contractible codimension 0 sub-manifold
so-called cork. Understanding gauge theoretical properties of corks play a crucial
role in smooth classiﬁcation of 4-manifolds. In this talk I will present a joint work
with S. Akbulut on calculation of relative Ozsv´ath-Szab´o invariants of an inﬁnite
family corks.
