Nikolai Saveliev
I will talk about an invariant introduced in our joint work with Tom Mrowka
and Daniel Ruberman with an eye toward the study of homology cobordisms in
dimension four. The deﬁnition of the invariant uses Seiberg–Witten gauge theory
on homology S1 × S3 and some index theory on manifolds with periodic ends. I
will deﬁne the invariant and describe its properties and calculations available up
to date.
