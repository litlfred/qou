Graph-links and Bouchet graphs
DENIS ILYUTKO

It is well known that classical and virtual knots can be represented by Gauss
diagrams, and the whole information about the knot and its invariants can be
read out of any Gauss diagram encoding it. Whenever a Gauss diagram does
not describe any embedded 4-valent graph in R? (just because the corresponding
Gauss code is not planar), one gets a virtual knot, where generic immersion points
of intersections of edges are encircled.
