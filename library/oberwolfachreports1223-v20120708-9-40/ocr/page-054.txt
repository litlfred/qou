Combinatorial Spatial Graph Floer Homology
SHELLY L. HARVEY

Knot Floer homology, introduced by Ozsvéth and Szabé [6], and independently
by Rasmussen [8], is an invariant of knots in $* that categorifies the Alexander
polynomial. Knot Floer homology is widely studied because of its many applica-
tions in low-dimensional topology. For example, it detects the unknot [5], detects
whether a knot is fibered [1, 4], and detect the genus of a knot [5]. The theory
was generalized to links in [7]. We extend their theory for balanced spatial graphs
in S°.
