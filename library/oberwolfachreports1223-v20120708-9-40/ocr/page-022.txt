We show that, in contrast to the general case, the directed m-string satellites
of any knots K and K’ made up of symmetric tangles as above share the same
Homfly polynomial for all m < 5. In addition the satellites of K and K’ based on
the (m,n) torus knot pattern, where m and n are coprime, have the same Homfly
polynomial for all values of m.

However m-string satellites of K and K’ other than the true (m,n) cables can
have different Homfly polynomials when m > 5.

It can be shown explicitly that the 6-parallels of the two pretzel knots in figure
3 have different Homfly polynomials.
