Deﬁnition 2.8. Let W be a Coxeter group. An element w is said to be fully commutative if
any reduced word for w can be obtained from any other by interchange of adjacent commuting
generators.
Stembridge [16] classiﬁed all of the Coxeter groups that have ﬁnitely many fully commutative
elements. His results completed the work of Fan [5], who had done this for the simply-laced
types. In the same paper [5], Fan showed that the fully commutative elements parameterized
natural bases for corresponding quotients of Hecke algebras. In type An, these give rise to
the Temperley–Lieb algebras (see [8]). Fan and Stembridge also enumerated the set of fully
commutative elements. In particular, they showed the following.
