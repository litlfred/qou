This gives a Markov chain $L_{\tau}$ by defining the probability of transitioning from $\lambda$ to $\rho$ as \[ L_{\tau}(\rho,\lambda) := \frac{dim(\rho)}{dim(\lambda) dim(\tau)} \frac{1}{|G|} \sum_{g} \chi^{\lambda}(g) \chi^{\tau}(g) \overline{\chi^{\rho}(g)}. \] It is worth remarking that since Stein's method is in the spirit of Taylor approximation, $\tau$ should typically be chosen to be as close as possible to the trivial representation.
