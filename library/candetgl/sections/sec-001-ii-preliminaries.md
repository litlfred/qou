B. Applications: A quick guide to the literature
For a glance at the prehistory of the writhing number, see Gauss’s half-page note 7 ~1833! on
an integral formula for the linking number of two disjoint closed curves in 3-space. Rewrite his
expression in modern notation and let the two curves coincide and you will have the formula for
the writhing number.
