The Biot–Savart operator is a bounded operator, and hence extends to a bounded operator on the L2 completion of its domain, where it is both compact and self-adjoint. The eigenﬁelds of this operator which correspond to its extreme eigenvalues turn out to be the vector ﬁelds in V with minimum energy for given helicity. If we start with a vector ﬁeld V which is divergence-free and tangent to the boundary of its domain V, that is, a ﬂuid knot, then its magnetic ﬁeld BS(V), though divergence-free, will in general not be tangent to the boundary of V.
