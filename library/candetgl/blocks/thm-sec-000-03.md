The image of the Biot–Savart operator is a proper subspace of the image of curl, whose orthogonal projection into the subspace of ‘‘ﬂuxless knots’’ is one-to-one. Vector ﬁelds on the domain V which are divergence-free and tangent to its boundary are called ﬂuid knots; we explain this terminology in Sec. IV. Fluxless knots are ﬂuid knots with zero ﬂux through every cross-sectional surface (S,]S),(V,]V). The above theorems lead to several interesting examples of ‘‘impossible’’ magnetic ﬁelds.
