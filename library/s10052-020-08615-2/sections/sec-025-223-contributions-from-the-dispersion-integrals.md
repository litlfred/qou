22.4 Result for mQED and mQCD
Collecting the various contributions, the part of the proton–
neutron mass difference that is due to the e.m. interaction
becomes
mQED = 0.58 ± 0.16 MeV.
(106)
The observed mass difference then yields
mQCD = −1.87 ∓0.16 MeV.
(107)
The result for mQCD provides a more precise estimate for the
leading Wilson coefﬁcient:
C = 5.7(1.1) × 10−4 GeV2.
(108)
