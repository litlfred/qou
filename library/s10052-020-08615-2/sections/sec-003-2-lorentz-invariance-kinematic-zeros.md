The problem with this choice is that, in contrast to T1, T2,
the amplitudes ˆT1, ˆT2 contain kinematic zeros. This makes
it difﬁcult to determine their asymptotic behaviour. That is
important because analytic functions are fully determined by
their singularities only if the asymptotic behaviour is known.
In dispersion theory, theoretical constraints are needed to
determine the asymptotic properties of the amplitudes.
