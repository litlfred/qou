The elastic contribution to the e.m. part of the mass difference
is determined by the form factors. In early work, the experi-
mental information about these was adequately described by
the dipole formulae (see e.g. appendix A of [11]). They yield
0.63 MeV for the proton and −0.13 MeV for the neutron,
so that the elastic contribution to the self-energy difference
amounts to mel = 0.76 MeV [4]. In the meantime, the pre-
cision to which the form factors are known has increased
signiﬁcantly [77–80]. Using this information, we obtain
mel = 0.75 ± 0.02 MeV.
