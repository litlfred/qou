The Cottingham formula expresses the leading
contribution of the electromagnetic interaction to the proton-
neutron mass difference as an integral over the forward
Compton amplitude. Since quarks and gluons reggeize, the
dispersive representation of this amplitude requires a sub-
traction. We assume that the asymptotic behaviour is dom-
inated by Reggeon exchange. This leads to a sum rule that
expresses the subtraction function in terms of measurable
quantities. The evaluation of this sum rule leads to m p−n
QED =
0.58 ± 0.16 MeV.
