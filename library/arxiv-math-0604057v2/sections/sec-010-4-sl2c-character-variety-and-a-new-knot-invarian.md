In this section, let $K$ be a hyperbolic knot in $S^3$ and $M_K$ its complement. Then $M_K$ is a hyperbolic $3$-manifold of finite volume with boundary $\partial M_k$ a torus. Since $M_K$ is hyperbolic, there is a discrete faithful representation $\rho_0\in R(M_K)$ corresponding to its hyperbolic structure. We denote by $R_0$ an irreducible component of $R(M_K)$ containing $\rho_0$. Let $X_0=t(R_0)$. By [@CS], $X_0\subset X(M_K)$ is an irreducible affine variety of dimension $1$.
