4. SL2(C) Character V ariety and a New Knot Invariant
In this section, let K be a hyperbolic knot in S3 and MK its complement. Then MK is a
hyperbolic 3-manifold of ﬁnite volume with boundary ∂Mk a torus. Since MK is hyperbolic, there
is a discrete faithful representation ρ0 ∈ R(MK ) corresponding to its hyperbolic structure. We
denote by R0 an irreducible component of R(MK ) containing ρ0. Let X0 = t(R0). By [CS],
X0 ⊂ X(MK ) is an irreducible aﬃne variety of dimension 1.
