3. A-polynomial, regulator and K2 of a curve
In this section, we brieﬂy recall the generalized volume conjecture. Using the regulator map of a
curve, we show that the form r(l, m) = ξ(l, m) + iη(l, m) over the 1-dimensional character variety
Yh has exact imaginary part and the rationality of the real part.
This provides an aﬃrmative
answer to Gukov’s quantization over Yh. Moreover, dr(l, m) = dl
l ∧dm
m = 2πiC1(L) = 0 justiﬁes
that r(l, m) is the Chern-Simons class from the ﬁrst Chern class C1.
