Let K be a knot in S3 and MK its complement. That is, MK = S3−NK where NK is the open
tubular neighborhood of K in S3. MK is a compact 3-manifold with boundary ∂MK = T 2 a torus.
Denote by R(MK) = Hom(π1(MK), SL2(C)) and R(∂MK) = Hom(π1(∂MK), SL2(C)). By [CS],
they are aﬃne algebraic sets over the complex numbers C and so are the corresponding character
varieties X(MK) and X(∂MK). We also have the canonical surjective morphisms t : R(MK) −→
X(MK) and t : R(∂MK) −→X(∂MK) which map a representation to its character. The natural
homomorphism i : π1(∂MK) −→π1(MK) induces the restriction maps r : X(MK) −→X(∂MK)
and r : R(MK) −→R(∂MK).
