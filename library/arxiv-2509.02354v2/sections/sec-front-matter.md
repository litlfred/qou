THE HOLONOMY BRAIDING FOR Uξ(sl2) IN TERMS OF GEOMETRIC
QUANTUM DILOGARITHMS
CALVIN MCPHAIL-SNYDER AND NICOLAI RESHETIKHIN
Abstract. We derive an explicit formula for the holonomy R-matrix of quantum sl2 at a
root of unity. We show it factorizes into a product of four quantum dilogarithms and satisfies
a holonomy Yang-Baxter equation. This factorization extends previously known results and
we collect many existing results needed for our computation.
