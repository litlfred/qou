Set $\xi=\exp(\frac{\pi i}{N})$ for $N \ge 2$ an integer. Denote by $\mathcal{U}_\xi$ the specialization of $\mathcal{U}_q(\sla)$ to $q=\xi$.

::: proposition
[]{#thm:qgrp-center label="thm:qgrp-center"}

::: thmenum
The center $\mathcal{Z}$ of $\mathcal{U}_\xi$ is generated by $$\mathcal{Z}_0 = \mathbb{C}[K^{\pm N}, E^N, F^N]$$ and the Casimir element $\Omega$ [\[eq:Casimir\]](#eq:Casimir){reference-type="ref" reference="eq:Casimir"}, subject to the relation $$P_N(\Omega) = E^N F^N - (K^N + K^{-N})$$ where $P_N$ is the $N$th renormalized Chebyshev polynomial defined by the identity $$P_N(t + t^{-1}) = t^N+ t^{-N}.$$

The central subalgebra $\mathcal{Z}_0$ is a Hopf subalgebra of $\mathcal{U}_\xi$.
:::
:::

As for any commutative Hopf algebra, the set of characters (algebra homomorphisms) $\chi : \mathcal{Z}_0 \to \mathbb{C}$ is a group with multiplication $$(\chi_1 \cdot \chi_2)(x) \mathrel{:=}(\chi_1 \otimes \chi_2)(\Delta(x)).$$ In this case the Hopf subalgebra $\mathcal{Z}_0$ is isomorphic to the algebra of functions on the algebraic group $$\slg^*
  =
  \left\{ \def\given{\ \middle| \ }  
    \left(
      \begin{bmatrix}
        \kappa & 0 \\
        \phi & 1
      \end{bmatrix}
      ,
      \begin{bmatrix}
        1 & \epsilon \\
        0 & \kappa
      \end{bmatrix}
    \right) 
    \given
    \kappa \ne 0
   \right\}  
  \subseteq
  \operatorname{GL}_2(\mathbb{C})
  \times
  \operatorname{GL}_2(\mathbb{C}).$$ The algebra $\mathcal{U}_\xi$ is finite-dimensional over $\mathcal{Z}_0$, so we can think of $\mathcal{U}_\xi$ as a sheaf of algebras over $\operatorname{Spec} \mathcal{Z}_0 = \slg^*$. The map $$\psi : \slg^* \to \slg, \psi(x^+, x^-) = x^+ (x^-)^{-1}$$ is a birational equivalence, but not a group homomorphism.
