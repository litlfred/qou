Challenges and extensions
In this ﬁnal chapter we look to the future of ACSV, discussing the most im-
portant challenges and extensions of current results. Work attacking several of
these problems is ongoing. The breadth of behavior exhibited by multivariate
generating functions is vast, and new applications arise constantly that require
additional techniques.
