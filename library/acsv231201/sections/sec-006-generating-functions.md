This chapter provides a crash course on generating functions and their uses
in enumeration. For a more lengthy introduction, we recommend [Wil06] –
other standard references include [FS09, Chapters I–III], [vLW01, Chapter 14],
[Sta97, Section 1.1], and the comprehensive treatment in [GJ04].
Throughout this book, we use the notation [n] :={1,..., n} and writeδ j for
the vector of length d with a 1 in its jth coordinate and a 0 elsewhere.
