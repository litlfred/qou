Let V be the union of two complex hypersurfaces in dimen- sion three. Any point w on the stratum S deﬁned intersection of these two hypersurfaces is a (non-smooth) transverse multiple point. The stratum S has codimension k = 2 and the homology of the normal link is generated by a 2-torus βw. ◁ There is a theory of multiple residues for transverse multiple points, not too much more diﬃcult than the residue forms already introduced, and asymptotics for an integral over a quasi-local cycle may be computed rather neatly using this approach.
