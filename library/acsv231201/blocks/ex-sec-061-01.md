Let F(x, y, z) = 1/Q(x, y, z)β where Q(x, y, z) = (1 −x)(1 −y) + (1 −x)(1 −z) + (1 −y)(1 −z). Friedrichs and Lewy [FL28] were interested in nonnegativity of the power series coeﬃcients of F when β = 1 while they studied a discretized time- dependent wave equation in two spatial dimensions; such nonnegativity was proven for β > 1/2 in classical work of Szeg˝o [Sze33]. A vast generalization of this result, applying to the reciprocal of the Tutte polynomial of a graph with all variables e replaced by 1 −e, was later studied by Scott and Sokal [SS14].
