If M is an oriented manifold then for any p-simplex σ : ∆p →M, integra- tion of a p-form ω over σ is deﬁned by Z σ ω := Z ∆p σ∗(ω), where σ∗is the pullback of ω by σ from a form on M to a form on ∆p ⊂Rp. It is important to note that we have deﬁned integration not over a set (the range of σ) but over a parametrization (σ itself)
