Because we work with complex vector spaces, H∗(C) is sometimes called homology with coeﬃcients in C to distinguish it from the analogous construction with integer coeﬃcients. While the theory with integer coeﬃ- cients is richer, taking coeﬃcients in a ﬁeld better suits the purposes of com- puting integrals. With integer coeﬃcients, the spaces of chains, cycles, and boundaries are Z-modules, and their quotients are abelian groups, hence “ho- mology group” rather than “homology vector space.” To discuss the homology of a manifold, we must deﬁne an appropriate chain complex.
