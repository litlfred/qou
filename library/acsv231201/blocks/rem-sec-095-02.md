The proof of this result in [GM88] is somewhat diﬃcult, mostly due to the necessity of establishing the invariance properties of the complex link. The result, however, is very useful. For example, suppose that X is the complement of the zero set of a polynomial in n variables. Then d = n −1 and the homotopy dimension of X is at most n. Note that X may have strata of any complex dimension j ≤d, and that the complement of a j-dimensional complex space in Cn has homotopy dimension 2n −2 j.
