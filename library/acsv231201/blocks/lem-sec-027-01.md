Let W = {z ∈Cd : Re{S (z)} > 0} and suppose that α ∈GLd(C) maps Rd into W. If M = αTα is the matrix representing the quadratic form S ◦α and π is the projection map from Cd onto Rd then π ◦α is orientation preserving on Rd if and only if det α is the product of the principal square roots of the eigenvalues of M (rather than the negative of this). Proof First suppose α ∈GLd(R). Then M has positive eigenvalues, and the product of their principal square roots is positive.
