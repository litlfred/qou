6. Every complex manifold is orientable when considered as a real manifold. A smooth map between two real manifolds M and M′ of dimensions m and n is a function f : M →M′ such that for any p ∈M and any chart maps φ on a chart of M containing p and ψ on a chart of M′ containing f(p), the function ψ ◦f ◦φ−1 from a subset of Rm to Rn is smooth. Holomorphic maps between complex manifolds are deﬁned analogously, requiring ψ ◦f ◦φ−1 to be holomorphic instead of smooth.
