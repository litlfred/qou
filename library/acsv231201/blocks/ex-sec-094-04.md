20. Suppose that V∗is a smooth algebraic hypersurface near one of its points p. (1) If X = V then N(X) is the single point p. Formally, the homotopy type is that of ({p}, ∅). (2) If X = M then N(X) has the homotopy type of (D\0, q), where D is a small disk and q is a point on the boundary of D. This is the reduced homotopy type of a circle, cyclic in dimension 1 and null in every other dimension.◁ The following theorem is stated for the case X = V∗in [GM88, Theo- rem SMT B on page 8] and for the case X = M in [GM88, unnamed theorem on page 12]; the equivalent characterizations of the homotopy type are stated in [GM88, pages 7, 66–67, 120–122]
