9. Let X be the unit ball in Rn and A be the unit sphere S n−1. To compute the homology of the pair (X, A) we consider the unit n-simplex ∆n and its boundary: topologically (∆n, ∂∆n) and (Bn, S n−1) are homeomorphic, so their relative homologies are the same. The simplicial chain complex for ∆n contains all faces of ∆n, hence it has simplices in dimensions 0 through n. The complex of the pair is non-trivial only in dimension n because every face of dimension less than n is supported on ∂∆n.
