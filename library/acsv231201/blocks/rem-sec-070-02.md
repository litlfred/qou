More generally, we may allow µ to be any measure on Zd−1 whose moment generating function is ﬁnite everywhere. This takes us out of the the- ory of amoebas of polynomials. Because we have not developed the theory of analytic amoebas, we do not include a statement or proof of this result. The greatest generality for weak laws via this type of ar- gument is achieved by weakening the hypothesis to ﬁniteness of the moment generating function in a neighborhood of the origin.
