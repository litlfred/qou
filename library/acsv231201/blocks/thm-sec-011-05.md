Then V is contained in the sum of subspaces Vf +Vg spanned by the derivatives of f and g. Hence, V is a ﬁnite-dimensional space with dimension at most dim(Vf ) + dim(Vg) and f(z) + g(z) is D-ﬁnite. Similarly, the products f (r)(z)g(s)(z) of the partial derivatives of f and g span a ﬁnite dimensional space W of dimension at most dim(Vf ) · dim(Vg).
