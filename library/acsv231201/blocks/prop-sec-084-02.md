Let M be a diﬀerentiable manifold, let C be the chain com- plex whose chains are linear combinations of singular simplices on M, and let C′ be the chain complex whose chains are linear combinations of smooth Algebraic topology simplices on M. Then for any n ∈N the homology groups Hn(C) and Hn(C′) are isomorphic. Proof See [Eil47]. □ We write C(M) for the chain complex deﬁned by the singular n-chains in M. The homology group Hn(C(M)) is written Hn(M) and called the nth singular homology group of M.
