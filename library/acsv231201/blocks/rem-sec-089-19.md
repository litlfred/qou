Recall that the factors {Qi : 1 ≤i ≤k} are deﬁned only up to transformations multiplying each Qi by a complex number λi, satisfying Qk i=1 λm1 i = 1. This multiplies det ΓΨ by Qk i=1 λi and divides (rΓ−1 Ψ )m−1 by Qk i−1 λmi−1 i , thus leaving the ratio (rΓ−1 Ψ )m−1/ det ΓΨ which appears in (C.2.15) invariant. Later, when we need to compute orientations, it will be convenient to normalize each Qi to have constant term 1, simultaneously normalizing P to have constant term a0.
