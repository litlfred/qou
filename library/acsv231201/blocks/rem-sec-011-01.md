The more natural deﬁnition of a D-ﬁnite series is one whose deriva- tives span a ﬁnite dimensional space over the polynomial ring C[z]. Vector spaces over ﬁelds are simpler than modules over rings, so we phrase this in terms of vector space dimension over the rational functions instead. This re- quires that the rational functions act on C[[z]], which requires us to extend C[[z]] to C((z)).
