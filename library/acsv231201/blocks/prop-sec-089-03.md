Let ω be a holomorphic d-form with smooth simple poles on V, represented as a quotient ω = P(z)/Q(z) dz of analytic functions on M ∩D for some domain D ⊂Cd on which the gradient of Q does not vanish. If W = V ∩D and ι : W ,→D is the inclusion of W into Cd then there is a (d −1)-form θ on D solving dQ ∧θ = P dz and any such solution restricts to a unique (d −1)-form Res(ω) = ι∗θ on W called the residue of ω on W.
