The set critical(r) of all (stratiﬁed) critical points of any rational function F ∈Q(z) in a direction ˆr is deﬁned by a ﬁnite collection of polynomial systems over Q[r][z], each involving polynomial equalities and in- equalities. The polynomials in these (in)equalities can be computed explicitly. The complexity of computing critical points, discussed in Melczer and Salvy [MS21], is typically lower than the complexity of verifying which (if any) are minimal points.
