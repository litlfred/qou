Gradient flows form an important component of our analytic toolbox. In-
deed, rather than computing a flow for each example, standard results in Morse
theory usually guarantee the existence of flows that push domains of integra-
tion down to points where saddle point approximations can be computed. Un-
fortunately, these results require the height map to be proper (meaning that the
set of points with height in a closed interval is compact). Because this proper-
ness condition is usually not satisfied in our setting, it is possible for the desired
flows not to exist.
