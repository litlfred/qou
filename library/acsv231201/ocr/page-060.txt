Remark. The more natural definition of a D-finite series is one whose deriva-
tives span a finite dimensional space over the polynomial ring C[z]. Vector
spaces over fields are simpler than modules over rings, so we phrase this in
terms of vector space dimension over the rational functions instead. This re-
quires that the rational functions act on C[[z]], which requires us to extend
C[[z]] to C((z)).
