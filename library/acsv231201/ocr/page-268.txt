We give a proof of Theorem 9.5 in Section 9.1 using the surgery method for
ACSV, which works in the presence of smooth finitely minimal contributing
points. Although the requirement of finite minimality makes proofs simpler, it
is computationally difficult to check, and rules out cases that can be handled by
our other results. In Section 9.2 we use more advanced techniques (including
the theory of hyperbolic polynomials developed in Chapter 11) to prove an
extension of Theorem 9.5 that ignores non-critical points and only requires
that the torus T(w) contains a finite number of critical points. This gives a
large computational advantage, because generically there are a finite number
of critical points described by a zero-dimensional algebraic set.
