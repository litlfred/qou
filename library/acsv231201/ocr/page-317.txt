Having surveyed the different types of multiple points we will analyze, we
now present our main asymptotic results. Effective methods to compute the

(at least some of) the coefficients x, in (10.1), which is done in Section 10.3.

coefficients x, (which are all 0 or +1), while in general we give results only in
