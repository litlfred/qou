It is interesting to note that even though the generating function in Exam-
ple 2.29 is rational, the main diagonal AF is transcendental (i.e., not algebraic).
Perhaps the easiest way to see this is to compute asymptotics of the diagonal
coefficients using the methods of Chapter 9 and verify that they do not have
asymptotic growth compatible with being an algebraic series [Mel21, Corol-
lary 2.1].

Although the classes of rational and algebraic series are not closed under
taking diagonals, the class of D-finite series is.

Theorem 2.30 (Lipshitz [Lip88]). Any elementary diagonal of a D-finite series
in d variables is a D-finite series in d — \ variables. In particular, the main
