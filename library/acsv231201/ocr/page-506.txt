C.2.2 Residue forms on smooth higher order poles

Let w and w’ be holomorphic d-forms on M with simple poles on the smooth
variety V = Vo. If w and w’ are cohomologous in H4(M) then [Res(w)] =
[Res(w’)] in H41(V,), and the study of residue classes of forms with smooth
higher order poles can be reduced to those with smooth simple poles using this

property.
