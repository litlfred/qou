[]{#subsec:MainResult label="subsec:MainResult"}

We can now state the general Habiro descent result. We let $\qIW_m\Omega_{-/A}^*$ denote the $m$-truncated $q$-de Rham Witt complex from [@qWitt Definition ] and $\qIW_m\deRham_{-/A}\colon \cat{AniAlg}_A\rightarrow \Dd(A[q])$ its non-abelian derived functor.

::: thm
[]{#thm:HabiroDescent label="thm:HabiroDescent"} Let $A$ be a perfectly covered $\Lambda$-ring and $\cat{AniAlg}_A^{\qHodge}$ be the $\infty$-category of animated $A$-algebras equipped with a $q$-Hodge filtration on their $q$-de Rham complex.

::: alphanumerate
Let $\widehat{\Dd}_\Hh(A[q])\subseteq \Dd(A[q])$ denote the full sub-$\infty$-category of Habiro-complete objects . Then the $q$-Hodge complex functor admits a symmetric monoidal factorisation[]{#enum:HabiroDescent label="enum:HabiroDescent"} $$\begin{tikzcd}[column sep=huge]
                    & \widehat{\Dd}_\Hh\bigl(A[q]\bigr)\dar["(-)_{(q-1)}^\complete"]\\
                    \cat{AniAlg}_A^{\qHodge}\rar["\qHodge_{-/A}"']\urar[dashed,"\qHhodge_{-/A}"] & \widehat{\Dd}_{(q-1)}\bigl(A[q]\bigr)
                \end{tikzcd}$$

For all $m\in\IN$, the quotient $\qHhodge_{-/A}/(q^m-1)$ admits an exhaustive ascending filtration $\fil_\star ^{\qIW_m\Omega}(\qHhodge_{-/A}/(q^m-1))$ with associated graded[]{#enum:qdRWComparison label="enum:qdRWComparison"} $$\gr_*^{\qIW_m\Omega}\bigl(\qHhodge_{-/A}/(q^m-1)\bigr)\simeq \Sigma^{-*}\qIW_m\deRham_{-/A}^*\,.$$ Furthermore, $\fil_\star ^{\qIW_m\Omega}(\qHhodge_{-/A}/(q^m-1))$ can be equipped with a canonical lax symmetric monoidal structure compatible with the one on $\qHhodge_{-/A}/(q^m-1)$, and the equivalence above is an equivalence of lax symmetric monoidal functors.
:::
:::

::: exm
[]{#exm:HabiroDescentCoordinateCase label="exm:HabiroDescentCoordinateCase"} If $S$ is a smooth over $A$ and $\square\colon A[x_1,\dotsc,x_n]\rightarrow S$ is an étale framing, then we can define a filtration on the coordinate-dependent $q$-de Rham complex $\qOmega_{S/A, \square}^*$ via $$\fil_{\qHodge, \square}^n\qOmega_{S/A, \square}^*\coloneqq (q-1)^{\max\{n-*,0\}}\qOmega_{S/A, \square}^*\,.$$ (compare the construction in [\[par:qHodgeFiltrationInCoordinates\]](#par:qHodgeFiltrationInCoordinates){reference-type="ref" reference="par:qHodgeFiltrationInCoordinates"}). As explained in [\[rem:CompleteFiltration\]](#rem:CompleteFiltration){reference-type="ref" reference="rem:CompleteFiltration"}, we can take the pullback along $\qdeRham_{S/A}\rightarrow \qOmega_{S/A, \square}^*$ to get a filtration $\fil_{\qHodge,\square}^\star\qdeRham_{S/A}$ on the derived $q$-de Rham complex. It's straightforward to equip it with the additional structure from [\[def:qHodgeFiltration\]](#def:qHodgeFiltration){reference-type="ref" reference="def:qHodgeFiltration"}[\[enum:qHodgeFiltrationOnqdR\]](#enum:qHodgeFiltrationOnqdR){reference-type="ref" reference="enum:qHodgeFiltrationOnqdR"}--[\[enum:qHodgeRationalpComplete\]](#enum:qHodgeRationalpComplete){reference-type="ref" reference="enum:qHodgeRationalpComplete"}: Just construct everything on the level of complexes and then take the pullback.

Therefore, the pair $(S, \fil_{\qHodge, \square}^\star \qdeRham_{S/A})$ determines an $\IE_0$-algebra in $\cat{AniAlg}_A^{\qHodge}$. We'll explain in [@qdeRhamku Remark ] that it can be refined to an $\IE_\infty$-algebra. The derived $q$-Hodge complex associated to $(S, \fil_{\qHodge, \square}^\star \qdeRham_{S/A})$ is the coordinate-dependent $q$-Hodge complex $\qHodge_{S/A, \square}^*$. Indeed, as we've seen in [\[rem:CompleteFiltration\]](#rem:CompleteFiltration){reference-type="ref" reference="rem:CompleteFiltration"}, in the definition of $\qHodge_{-/A}$ it doesn't matter whether we work with the $q$-Hodge filtration on $\qdeRham_{-/A}$ or its completion. Since $\fil_{\qHodge, \square}^\star \qOmega_{S/A, \square}^*$ is already complete, it's automatically the completion of its pullback $\fil_{\qHodge, \square}^\star \qdeRham_{S/A}$. We conclude that the corresponding derived $q$-Hodge complex is $$\colimit\Bigl( \fil_{\qHodge, \square}^0\qOmega_{S/A, \square}^*\xrightarrow{(q-1)} \fil_{\qHodge, \square}^1\qOmega_{S/A, \square}^*\xrightarrow{(q-1)}\dotsb\Bigr)\cong \qHodge_{S/A, \square}^*\,,$$ as claimed.

In this case, [\[thm:HabiroDescent\]](#thm:HabiroDescent){reference-type="ref" reference="thm:HabiroDescent"}[\[enum:HabiroDescent\]](#enum:HabiroDescent){reference-type="ref" reference="enum:HabiroDescent"} shows that $\qHodge_{S/A, \square}^*$ descends to an $\IE_\infty$-algebra $\qHhodge_{S/A, \square}$ in $\widehat{\Dd}_\Hh(A[q])$. As we'll see in [\[cor:qDRWSmoothAnimation\]](#cor:qDRWSmoothAnimation){reference-type="ref" reference="cor:qDRWSmoothAnimation"} below, $\Sigma^{-n}\qIW_m\deRham_{S/A}^n\simeq \qIW_m\Omega_{S/A}^n$ holds for all $n$. Thus, [\[thm:HabiroDescent\]](#thm:HabiroDescent){reference-type="ref" reference="thm:HabiroDescent"}[\[enum:qdRWComparison\]](#enum:qdRWComparison){reference-type="ref" reference="enum:qdRWComparison"} shows $$\H^*\bigl(\qHhodge_{S/A, \square}/(q^m-1)\bigr)\cong \qIW_m\Omega_{S/A}^*$$ as graded $A[q]/(q^m-1)$-modules. With a little more effort (see [\[cor:CDGA\]](#cor:CDGA){reference-type="ref" reference="cor:CDGA"} below), we can even get an equivalence as differential-graded $A[q]/(q^m-1)$-algebras, so we obtain an improved version of [@qWitt Theorem ].

[^1]

In fact, $\qHhodge_{S/A, \square}$ can be described as an explicit complex; this was first presented in [@HabiroCohomologyLecture Lecture [4](https://archive.mpim-bonn.mpg.de/id/eprint/5155/10/habiro_cohomology04.mp4)]. To this end, equip $A[x_1,\dotsc,x_n]$ with the *toric $\Lambda$-$A$-algebra structure* in which the Adams operations are given by $\psi^m(x_i)=x_i^m$ and consider the relative Habiro ring $\Hh_{S/A[x_1,\dotsc,x_n]}$. For $i=1,\dotsc,n$ let $\gamma_i$ be the $A[q]$-algebra endomorphism of $A[x_1,\dotsc,x_n,q]$ given by $\gamma_i(x_i)=qx_i$ and $\gamma_i(x_j)=x_j$ for $j\neq i$. We wish to extend $\gamma_i$ to an automorphism of $\Hh_{S/A[x_1,\dotsc,x_n]}$. To do so, we'll extend $\gamma_i$ to each of the factors of the equaliser in [\[lem:ComparisonWithGSWZ\]](#lem:ComparisonWithGSWZ){reference-type="ref" reference="lem:ComparisonWithGSWZ"}. Fix $m\in\IN$ and put $S^{(m)}\coloneqq (S\otimes_{A[x_1,\dotsc,x_n],\psi^m}A[x_1,\dotsc,x_n])[\zeta_m]$. Consider the diagram $$\begin{tikzcd}
                A[x_1,\dotsc,x_n,\zeta_m]\llbracket q-\zeta_m\rrbracket\dar["\square"']\rar["\gamma_i"] & S^{(m)}\llbracket q-\zeta_m\rrbracket\dar\\
                S^{(m)}\llbracket q-\zeta_m\rrbracket\rar["\ov\gamma_i^{(m)}"]\urar["\gamma_i^{(m)}"{pos=0.33},dashed] & S^{(m)}
            \end{tikzcd}$$ where $\ov\gamma_i^{(m)}$ is given by the identity on the tensor factor $S$, $\ov\gamma_i^{(m)}(x_i)=\zeta_m x_i$, and $\ov\gamma_i^{(m)}(x_j)=x_j$ for $j\neq i$. By the infinitesimal lifting property of formally étale morphisms, there exists a unique dashed arrow $\gamma_i^{(m)}$ making the diagram commutative. Then $\bigl(\gamma_i^{(m)}\bigr)_{m\in\IN}$ defines the desired automorphism $\gamma_i$ of $\Hh_{S/A[x_1,\dotsc,x_n]}$ via [\[lem:ComparisonWithGSWZ\]](#lem:ComparisonWithGSWZ){reference-type="ref" reference="lem:ComparisonWithGSWZ"}. It's also straightforward to check that $\gamma_i\equiv \id\mod x_i$.

Letting $\q\widetilde{\partial}_i\coloneqq (\gamma_i-\id)/x_i$ and $\q\widetilde{\nabla}\coloneqq \sum_i\q\widetilde{\partial}_i\d x_i$, the Koszul complex of the commuting endomorphisms $\q\widetilde{\partial}_i$, $$\biggl(\Hh_{S/A[x_1,\dotsc,x_n]}\overset{\q\widetilde{\nabla}}{\longrightarrow} \bigoplus_{i}\Hh_{S/A[x_1,\dotsc,x_n]}\d x_i\overset{\q\widetilde{\nabla}}{\longrightarrow}\dotsb\overset{\q\widetilde{\nabla}}{\longrightarrow}\Hh_{S/A[x_1,\dotsc,x_n]}\d x_1\dotsm\d x_n\biggr)\,,$$ is an explicit complex representing $\qHhodge_{S/A, \square}$. This can be shown by unravelling the proof of [\[thm:HabiroDescent\]](#thm:HabiroDescent){reference-type="ref" reference="thm:HabiroDescent"} (which is less horrible than it sounds).
:::

[\[exm:HabiroDescentCoordinateCase\]](#exm:HabiroDescentCoordinateCase){reference-type="ref" reference="exm:HabiroDescentCoordinateCase"} covers in particular the case of étale $A$-algebras. In this special case, we recover a familiar construction.

::: cor
[]{#cor:ComparisonWithGSWZ2 label="cor:ComparisonWithGSWZ2"} If $R$ is étale over $A$, then $\qHhodge_{R/A}$ is the relative Habiro ring $\Hh_{R/A}$ constructed in [\[con:RelativeHabiroRing\]](#con:RelativeHabiroRing){reference-type="ref" reference="con:RelativeHabiroRing"}.
:::

::: proof
*Proof.* This is clear from the explicit presentation in [\[exm:HabiroDescentCoordinateCase\]](#exm:HabiroDescentCoordinateCase){reference-type="ref" reference="exm:HabiroDescentCoordinateCase"}, but it can also be shown without having to unravel the proof of [\[thm:HabiroDescent\]](#thm:HabiroDescent){reference-type="ref" reference="thm:HabiroDescent"}.

We'll see in [\[cor:qDRWSmoothAnimation\]](#cor:qDRWSmoothAnimation){reference-type="ref" reference="cor:qDRWSmoothAnimation"} that $\qIW_m\deRham_{R/A}^n\simeq \Sigma^{-n}\qIW_m\Omega_{R/A}^n$ holds for all $n\geqslant 0$ whenever $R$ is smooth over $A$. If $R$ is étale, then combining this observation with [\[thm:HabiroDescent\]](#thm:HabiroDescent){reference-type="ref" reference="thm:HabiroDescent"}[\[enum:HabiroDescent\]](#enum:HabiroDescent){reference-type="ref" reference="enum:HabiroDescent"} and [@qWitt Proposition ] shows $$\qHhodge_{R/A}/(q^m-1)\simeq \qIW_m(R/A)\simeq \Hh_{R/A}/(q^m-1)\,.$$ By uniqueness of deformations of étale extensions, these automatically lift to a unique equivalence of $\IE_\infty$-$\Hh$-algebras $(\qHhodge_{R/A})_{(q^m-1)}^\complete\simeq \Hh_{R/A,  m}$; furthermore, uniqueness also ensures that these equivalences are compatible for varying $m$. It follows that $\qHhodge_{R/A}\simeq \Hh_{R/A}$, as desired. ◻
:::

The proof of [\[thm:HabiroDescent\]](#thm:HabiroDescent){reference-type="ref" reference="thm:HabiroDescent"} has many ingredients and will occupy . Before we get lost in the technicalities, let us already outline the main argument and point out where the missing pieces will be provided.

::: proof
*Proof outline of [\[thm:HabiroDescent\]](#thm:HabiroDescent){reference-type="ref" reference="thm:HabiroDescent"}.* The argument will proceed similarly to the proof of [\[lem:qHodgeSymmetricMonoidal\]](#lem:qHodgeSymmetricMonoidal){reference-type="ref" reference="lem:qHodgeSymmetricMonoidal"} above; in particular, for $m=1$, the arguments below will recover the proof of [\[lem:qHodgeSymmetricMonoidal\]](#lem:qHodgeSymmetricMonoidal){reference-type="ref" reference="lem:qHodgeSymmetricMonoidal"}. In [\[subsec:TwistedqDeRham\]](#subsec:TwistedqDeRham){reference-type="ref" reference="subsec:TwistedqDeRham"} we'll introduce *twisted $q$-de Rham complexes* for all $m\in\IN$. These are $(q^m-1)$-complete $\IE_\infty$-$A[q]$-algebras $\qdeRham_{R/A}^{(m)}$ satisfying $$\qdeRham_{R/A}^{(m)}/(q^m-1)\simeq \qIW_m\deRham_{R/A}$$ (see [\[prop:TwistedqDeRhamDeformsqDRW\]](#prop:TwistedqDeRhamDeformsqDRW){reference-type="ref" reference="prop:TwistedqDeRhamDeformsqDRW"}

[^2]

). By animating the stupid filtration $\qIW_m\Omega_{-/A}^{\geqslant n,*}$, we obtain a filtration $\fil_{\Hhodge_m}^\star \qIW_m\deRham_{-/A}$ on $\qIW_m\deRham_{-/A}$. For $m=1$, this is the Hodge filtration on $\deRham_{-/A}$; for higher $m$, it should be thought of as a $q$-Witt vector analogue of the Hodge filtration. By construction, $$\gr_{\Hhodge_m}^n\qIW_m\deRham_{-/A}\simeq \qIW_m\deRham_{-/A}^n\,.$$ In [\[subsec:TwistedqHodgeFiltration\]](#subsec:TwistedqHodgeFiltration){reference-type="ref" reference="subsec:TwistedqHodgeFiltration"}, specifically in [\[prop:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationqDeforms\]](#prop:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationqDeforms){reference-type="ref" reference="prop:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationqDeforms"}, we'll show that for any given $q$-Hodge filtration $\fil_{\qHodge}^\star\qdeRham_{R/A}$, we can construct a filtration $\fil_{\qHhodge_m}^\star \qdeRham_{R/A}^{(m)}$ satisfying $$\fil_{\qHhodge_m}^\star \qdeRham_{R/A}^{(m)}/(q^m-1)\simeq  \fil_{\Hhodge_m}^\star \qIW_m\deRham_{R/A}\,.$$ We'll also verify that $\fil_{\qHhodge_m}^\star \qdeRham_{R/A}^{(m)}$ is lax symmetric monoidal in $(R, \fil_{\qHodge}^\star \qdeRham_{R/A})$ and that the equivalence above can be upgraded to an equivalence of lax symmetric monoidal functors $\cat{AniAlg}_A^{\qHodge}\rightarrow \Mod_{(q^m-1)^\star A[q]}( \Fil\Dd(A))$.

With this construction, we'll build the desired Habiro descent of $\qHodge_{R/A}$ in [\[subsec:qHodgeHabiroDescent\]](#subsec:qHodgeHabiroDescent){reference-type="ref" reference="subsec:qHodgeHabiroDescent"} by mimicking the definition of the $q$-Hodge complex in [\[par:WellBehavedqHodgeComplex\]](#par:WellBehavedqHodgeComplex){reference-type="ref" reference="par:WellBehavedqHodgeComplex"}. For all $m\in\IN$, we define $$\qHhodge_{R/A,  m}\coloneqq \colimit\Bigl( \fil_{\qHhodge_m}^{0}\qdeRham_{R/A}^{(m)}\xrightarrow{(q^m-1)} \fil_{\qHhodge_m}^{1}\qdeRham_{R/A}^{(m)}\xrightarrow{(q^m-1)}\dotso\Bigr)_{(q^m-1)}^\complete\,.$$ In [\[prop:HabiroDescent\]](#prop:HabiroDescent){reference-type="ref" reference="prop:HabiroDescent"}, we'll show $(\qHhodge_{R/A,  m})_{(q^d-1)}^\complete\simeq \qHhodge_{R/A,  d}$ whenever $d\mid m$. It follows that $\qHhodge_{R/A}\coloneqq \limit_{m\in\IN}\qHhodge_{R/A,  m}$ determines a Habiro descent of $\qHodge_{R/A}$, thus proving [\[thm:HabiroDescent\]](#thm:HabiroDescent){reference-type="ref" reference="thm:HabiroDescent"}[\[enum:HabiroDescent\]](#enum:HabiroDescent){reference-type="ref" reference="enum:HabiroDescent"}, except for the symmetric monoidality statement. As in the proof of [\[lem:qHodgeSymmetricMonoidal\]](#lem:qHodgeSymmetricMonoidal){reference-type="ref" reference="lem:qHodgeSymmetricMonoidal"}, it's formal to construct a lax symmetric monoidal structure on $\qHhodge_{-/A}$ which reduces to the one on $\qHodge_{-/A}$ after $(q-1)$-completion; see [\[con:HabiroDescent\]](#con:HabiroDescent){reference-type="ref" reference="con:HabiroDescent"} for the details. Strict symmetric monoidality will then be checked in [\[lem:HabiroDescentSymmetricMonoidal\]](#lem:HabiroDescentSymmetricMonoidal){reference-type="ref" reference="lem:HabiroDescentSymmetricMonoidal"}, finishing the proof of [\[thm:HabiroDescent\]](#thm:HabiroDescent){reference-type="ref" reference="thm:HabiroDescent"}[\[enum:HabiroDescent\]](#enum:HabiroDescent){reference-type="ref" reference="enum:HabiroDescent"}.

To show [\[thm:HabiroDescent\]](#thm:HabiroDescent){reference-type="ref" reference="thm:HabiroDescent"}[\[enum:qdRWComparison\]](#enum:qdRWComparison){reference-type="ref" reference="enum:qdRWComparison"}, we will mimic the arguments for the conjugate filtration (and in fact, for $m=1$, the desired filtration on $\qHhodge_{R/A}/(q-1)\simeq \qHodge_{R/A}/(q-1)$ *is* the conjugate filtration). By the same argument as in [\[par:ConjugateFiltration\]](#par:ConjugateFiltration){reference-type="ref" reference="par:ConjugateFiltration"}, we obtain $$\qHhodge_{R/A,  m}/(q^m-1)\simeq \colimit\Bigl(\gr_{\qHhodge_m}^{0}\qdeRham_{R/A}^{(m)}\xrightarrow{(q^m-1)}\gr_{\qHhodge_m}^{1}\qdeRham_{R/A}^{(m)}\xrightarrow{(q^m-1)}\dotso\Bigr)\,.$$ The colimit defines an exhaustive ascending filtration on $\qHhodge_{R/A,  m}/(q^m-1)$, which we take to be our definition of $\fil_\star ^{\qIW_m\Omega}(\qHhodge_{R/A,  m}/(q^m-1))$. The associated graded of this filtration can be determined by via [\[lem:FiltrationAbstract\]](#lem:FiltrationAbstract){reference-type="ref" reference="lem:FiltrationAbstract"} (for this we identify the filtered ring $(q^m-1)^\star A[q]$ with the graded ring $A[q,\beta,t]/(\beta t-(q^m-1))$, where $\abs{q}=0$, $\abs{\beta}=1$, and $\abs{t}=-1$): We obtain $$\gr_*^{\qIW_m\Omega}\bigl(\qHhodge_{R/A,  m}/(q^m-1)\bigr)\simeq \Sigma^{-*}\qIW_m\deRham_{R/A}^*\simeq \gr_{\Hhodge_m}^*\qIW_m\deRham_{R/A}\,.$$ As in the proof of [\[lem:qHodgeSymmetricMonoidal\]](#lem:qHodgeSymmetricMonoidal){reference-type="ref" reference="lem:qHodgeSymmetricMonoidal"}, the lax symmetric monoidality statements are formal, and so the proof of [\[thm:HabiroDescent\]](#thm:HabiroDescent){reference-type="ref" reference="thm:HabiroDescent"}[\[enum:qdRWComparison\]](#enum:qdRWComparison){reference-type="ref" reference="enum:qdRWComparison"} is finished. ◻
:::
