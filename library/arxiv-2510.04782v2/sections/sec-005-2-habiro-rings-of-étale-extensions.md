[]{#sec:HabiroRingsEtale label="sec:HabiroRingsEtale"}

Fix a perfectly covered $\Lambda$-ring $A$. The goal of this section is to construct a *relative Habiro ring* $\Hh_{R/A}$ for any étale algebra $R$ over $A$, and to relate this construction to the theory of $q$-Witt vectors. In the case where $A=\IZ$, our construction $\Hh_{R/\IZ}$ recovers the ring $\Hh_R$ from [@HabiroRingOfNumberField Definition ].

As we'll see in [\[sec:HabiroDescent\]](#sec:HabiroDescent){reference-type="ref" reference="sec:HabiroDescent"}, the construction of $\Hh_{R/A}$ is a special case of a much more general construction. However, the general case is vastly more technical, and we hope that discussing the étale case first will make the general case easier.
