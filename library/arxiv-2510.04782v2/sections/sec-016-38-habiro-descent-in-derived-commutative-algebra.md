doc_brief: "$q$-Hodge complexes over the Habiro ring — — Peter Scholze has raised the question whether some variant of the q-de Rham complex is already defined over the Habiro ring H := limm∈N Z[q]∧ (qm−1)."
---
Raksit [@RaksitFilteredCircle] has introduced $\infty$-categories of *derived commutative algebras*, along with filtered, graded, and differential-graded variants. The filtrations $\fil_{\Hhodge_m}^\star \qIW_m\deRham_{R/A}$ admit canonical filtered derived commutative $A[q]/(q^m-1)$-algebra structures (if $R$ is a polynomial $A$-algebra, these structures can be constructed on the level of complexes, then one can pass to animations) and the $\IE_\infty$-structure on the derived $q$-de Rham complex $\qdeRham_{R/A}$ can be canonically enhanced to a derived commutative $A[q]$-algebra structure (see [\[par:DerivedCommutativeLift\]](#par:DerivedCommutativeLift){reference-type="ref" reference="par:DerivedCommutativeLift"}).

In this subsection, we sketch how [\[thm:HabiroDescent\]](#thm:HabiroDescent){reference-type="ref" reference="thm:HabiroDescent"} can be made compatible with these derived commutative structures. As a warm-up, let us instead consider $\IE_n$-monoidal structures for some $0\leqslant n\leqslant \infty$.

::: numpar
[]{#par:EnMonoidalUpgrade label="par:EnMonoidalUpgrade"} Let $(R, \fil_{\qHodge}^\star \qdeRham_{R/A})\in \cat{AniAlg}_A^{\qHodge}$. Suppose that the $q$-Hodge filtration $\fil_{\qHodge}^\star \qdeRham_{R/A}$ can be equipped with the structure of an $\IE_n$-algebra in filtered $(q-1)^\star A[q]$-modules, compatible with the $\IE_\infty$-$A[q]$-algebra structure on $\qdeRham_{R/A}$. Suppose furthermore that the data from [\[def:qHodgeFiltration\]](#def:qHodgeFiltration){reference-type="ref" reference="def:qHodgeFiltration"}[\[enum:qHodgeFiltrationOnqdR\]](#enum:qHodgeFiltrationOnqdR){reference-type="ref" reference="enum:qHodgeFiltrationOnqdR"}--[\[enum:qHodgeRationalpComplete\]](#enum:qHodgeRationalpComplete){reference-type="ref" reference="enum:qHodgeRationalpComplete"} can be made compatible with this $\IE_n$-structure. Then $(R, \fil_{\qHodge}^\star \qdeRham_{R/A})$ becomes an $\IE_n$-algebra in $\cat{AniAlg}_A^{\qHodge}$.

By the symmetric monoidality statement in [\[thm:HabiroDescent\]](#thm:HabiroDescent){reference-type="ref" reference="thm:HabiroDescent"}[\[enum:HabiroDescent\]](#enum:HabiroDescent){reference-type="ref" reference="enum:HabiroDescent"}, we can conclude that the Habiro--Hodge complex $\qHhodge_{R/A}$ becomes an $\IE_n$-algebra in $\widehat{\Dd}_\Hh(A[q])$. Similarly, the lax symmetric monoidality statements in [\[thm:HabiroDescent\]](#thm:HabiroDescent){reference-type="ref" reference="thm:HabiroDescent"}[\[enum:qdRWComparison\]](#enum:qdRWComparison){reference-type="ref" reference="enum:qdRWComparison"} show that $\fil_\star ^{\qIW_m\Omega}(\qHhodge_{R/A}/(q^m-1))$ becomes a filtered $\IE_n$-algebra and the identification of its associated graded $$\gr_*^{\qIW_m\Omega}\bigl(\qHhodge_{R/A}/(q^m-1)\bigr)\simeq \Sigma^{-*}\qIW_m\deRham_{R/A}^*\simeq \gr_{\Hhodge_m}^*\qIW_m\deRham_{R/A}$$ becomes a graded $\IE_n$-monoidal equivalence.
:::

::: numpar
[]{#par:qHhodgeDAlg label="par:qHhodgeDAlg"} Similar to [\[par:EnMonoidalUpgrade\]](#par:EnMonoidalUpgrade){reference-type="ref" reference="par:EnMonoidalUpgrade"}, suppose that $\fil_{\qHodge}^\star \qdeRham_{R/A}$ can be equipped with the structure of a *filtered derived commutative algebra* over $(q-1)^\star A[q]$, that is, an element in the slice $\infty$-category $( \Fil\DAlg_{A[q]})_{(q-1)^\star A[q]/}$, where $\Fil\DAlg_{A[q]}$ is Raksit's $\infty$-category of filtered derived commutative $A[q]$-algebras [@RaksitFilteredCircle Definition ]. Suppose furthermore that this derived commutative structure is compatible with the derived commutative $A[q]$-algebra structure on $\qdeRham_{R/A}$ (see [\[par:DerivedCommutativeLift\]](#par:DerivedCommutativeLift){reference-type="ref" reference="par:DerivedCommutativeLift"}) and that the data from [\[def:qHodgeFiltration\]](#def:qHodgeFiltration){reference-type="ref" reference="def:qHodgeFiltration"}[\[enum:qHodgeFiltrationOnqdR\]](#enum:qHodgeFiltrationOnqdR){reference-type="ref" reference="enum:qHodgeFiltrationOnqdR"}--[\[enum:qHodgeRationalpComplete\]](#enum:qHodgeRationalpComplete){reference-type="ref" reference="enum:qHodgeRationalpComplete"} can be made compatible with the filtered derived commutative algebra structures everywhere.

For example, this can be done in the special cases from [\[exm:HabiroDescentCoordinateCase\]](#exm:HabiroDescentCoordinateCase){reference-type="ref" reference="exm:HabiroDescentCoordinateCase"} above and [\[con:GlobalqHodge\]](#con:GlobalqHodge){reference-type="ref" reference="con:GlobalqHodge"} below. In the former case, we'll verify this in [@qdeRhamku Remark ], in the latter case see [\[rem:qHodgeQuasiregularDAlg\]](#rem:qHodgeQuasiregularDAlg){reference-type="ref" reference="rem:qHodgeQuasiregularDAlg"}.
:::

::: lem
In the situation of [\[par:qHhodgeDAlg\]](#par:qHhodgeDAlg){reference-type="ref" reference="par:qHhodgeDAlg"}, $\qHhodge_{R/A}$ admits a canonical derived commutative $A[q]$-algebra structure. Furthermore, for all $m\in\IN$, $\fil_\star^{\qIW_m\Omega}(\qHhodge_{R/A}/(q^m-1))$ admits a filtered derived commutative $A[q]/(q^m-1)$-algebra structure, compatible with the derived commutative $A[q]/(q^m-1)$-algebra structure on $\qHhodge_{R/A}/(q^m-1)$, and the equivalence $$\gr_*^{\qIW_m\Omega}\bigl(\qHhodge_{R/A}/(q^m-1)\bigr)\simeq \Sigma^{-*}\qIW_m\deRham_{R/A}^*\simeq \gr_{\Hhodge_m}^*\qIW_m\deRham_{R/A}$$ from [\[thm:HabiroDescent\]](#thm:HabiroDescent){reference-type="ref" reference="thm:HabiroDescent"}[\[enum:qdRWComparison\]](#enum:qdRWComparison){reference-type="ref" reference="enum:qdRWComparison"} is an equivalence of graded derived commutative $A[q]/(q^m-1)$-algebras.
:::

::: proof
*Proof sketch.* First note that our results about the Nygaard filtration, specifically [\[prop:NygaardComparison\]](#prop:NygaardComparison){reference-type="ref" reference="prop:NygaardComparison"} and [\[lem:NygaardRationalisation\]](#lem:NygaardRationalisation){reference-type="ref" reference="lem:NygaardRationalisation"}, also hold true as equivalences of filtered derived commutative algebras, since the proofs work in this setting as well. By tracing through [\[con:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationpAdic\]](#con:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationpAdic){reference-type="ref" reference="con:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationpAdic"}--[\[con:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationFunctorial\]](#con:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationFunctorial){reference-type="ref" reference="con:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationFunctorial"}, we now see that each $\fil_{\qHhodge_m}^\star \qdeRham_{R/A}^{(m)}$ acquires a filtered derived commutative algebra structure over $(q^m-1)^\star A[q]$, and that the transition maps in [\[con:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationFunctorial\]](#con:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationFunctorial){reference-type="ref" reference="con:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationFunctorial"} are compatible with these structures.

The construction from [\[con:HabiroDescent0\]](#con:HabiroDescent0){reference-type="ref" reference="con:HabiroDescent0"} produces a canonical derived commutative algebra structure on $\Hh_{R/A,  m}$, because we can view the construction as a filtered localisation followed by restriction to filtered degree $0$; compare [\[par:ConjugateFiltration\]](#par:ConjugateFiltration){reference-type="ref" reference="par:ConjugateFiltration"}. It is then clear from [\[con:HabiroDescent\]](#con:HabiroDescent){reference-type="ref" reference="con:HabiroDescent"} that $\qHhodge_{R/A}$ acquires a derived commutative $A[q]$-algebra structure. Moreover, since the filtration $\fil_\star^{\qIW_m\Omega}(\qHhodge_{R/A}/(q^m-1))$ and the identification of its associated graded were constructed in a completely way (see the proofs of [\[lem:ConjugateFiltration,lem:FiltrationAbstract\]](#lem:ConjugateFiltration,lem:FiltrationAbstract){reference-type="ref" reference="lem:ConjugateFiltration,lem:FiltrationAbstract"}), they will also work on the level of derived commutative algebras. The only input this needs is that [\[prop:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationqDeforms\]](#prop:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationqDeforms){reference-type="ref" reference="prop:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationqDeforms"} holds as an equivalence of filtered derived commutative $A[q]/(q^m-1)$-algebras, which is again apparent from the constructions. ◻
:::

But there's one more piece of structure.

::: numpar
Since $\qIW_m\Omega_{-/A}^*$ is a functor with values in commutative differential-graded $A[q]/(q^m-1)$-algebras, we see that its animation $\Sigma^{-*}\qIW_m\deRham_{-/A}^*\simeq\gr_{\Hhodge_m}^*\qIW_m\deRham_{-/A}$ upgrades to a functor with values in Raksit's $\infty$-category $\cat{DG}_-\DAlg_{A[q]/(q^m-1)}$ of derived differential-graded $A[q]/(q^m-1)$-algebras [@RaksitFilteredCircle Definition ].

By transfer of structure, the associated graded $\gr_*^{\qIW_m\Omega}(\qHhodge_{R/A}/(q^m-1))$ becomes an element in $\cat{DG}_-\DAlg_{A[q]/(q^m-1)}$ as well. Via the following corollary, we can figure out what the differentials are, at least in the case where $R$ is smooth over $A$.
:::

::: cor
[]{#cor:CDGA label="cor:CDGA"} Let $(S, \fil_{\qHodge}^\star\qdeRham_{S/A})\in \cat{AniAlg}_A^{\qHodge}$ be an object such that $S$ is smooth over $A$. Then:

::: alphanumerate
$\fil_\star^{\qIW_m\Omega}(\qHhodge_{S/A}/(q^m-1))$ is the Whitehead filtration $\tau_{\geqslant \star}(\qHhodge_{S/A}/(q^m-1))$.[]{#enum:qWOmegaFiltrationPostnikov label="enum:qWOmegaFiltrationPostnikov"}

The equivalence from [\[thm:HabiroDescent\]](#thm:HabiroDescent){reference-type="ref" reference="thm:HabiroDescent"}[\[enum:qdRWComparison\]](#enum:qdRWComparison){reference-type="ref" reference="enum:qdRWComparison"} becomes an isomorphism of graded $A[q]/(q^m-1)$-modules []{#enum:CohomologyOfHabiro label="enum:CohomologyOfHabiro"} $$\H^*\bigl(\qHhodge_{S/A}/(q^m-1)\bigr)\cong \qIW_m\Omega_{S/A}^*$$ .

[]{#enum:BocksteinDifferential label="enum:BocksteinDifferential"} Under the isomorphism from [\[enum:CohomologyOfHabiro\]](#enum:CohomologyOfHabiro){reference-type="ref" reference="enum:CohomologyOfHabiro"}, the canonical differential on $\qIW_m\Omega_{S/A}^*$ corresponds to the Bockstein differential on $\H^*(\qHhodge_{S/A}/(q^m-1))$.
:::
:::

::: proof
*Proof.* We've seen in [\[cor:qDRWSmoothAnimation\]](#cor:qDRWSmoothAnimation){reference-type="ref" reference="cor:qDRWSmoothAnimation"} that $\qIW_m\Omega_{S/A}^n\simeq \qIW_m\deRham_{S/A}^n$ for all $n$. It follows that each graded piece $\gr_n^{\qIW_m\Omega}(\qHhodge_{S/A}/(q^m-1))$ is concentrated in cohomological degree $n$. Since $\fil_\star^{\qIW_m\Omega}(\qHhodge_{S/A}/(q^m-1))$ is bounded below and thus complete, it has to be the Whitehead filtration. This shows [\[enum:qWOmegaFiltrationPostnikov\]](#enum:qWOmegaFiltrationPostnikov){reference-type="ref" reference="enum:qWOmegaFiltrationPostnikov"} as well as the graded $A[q]/(q^m-1)$-module isomorphism from [\[enum:CohomologyOfHabiro\]](#enum:CohomologyOfHabiro){reference-type="ref" reference="enum:CohomologyOfHabiro"}. The isomorphism as graded $A[q]/(q^m-1)$-algebras follows from [\[par:EnMonoidalUpgrade\]](#par:EnMonoidalUpgrade){reference-type="ref" reference="par:EnMonoidalUpgrade"}.

It remains to show [\[enum:BocksteinDifferential\]](#enum:BocksteinDifferential){reference-type="ref" reference="enum:BocksteinDifferential"}. Similar to the proof of [\[lem:ConjugateFiltration\]](#lem:ConjugateFiltration){reference-type="ref" reference="lem:ConjugateFiltration"}, let us identify the filtered ring $(q^m-1)^\star A[q]$ with the graded ring $A[q,\beta,t_m]/(\beta t_m-(q^m-1))$, where $\abs{q}=0$, $\abs{\beta}=1$, and $\abs{t_m}=-1$.

[^1]

The filtered structure comes from the $A[t_m]$-module structure. In particular, modding out $t_m$ is the same as passing to the associated graded. Let us also regard the filtrations $\fil_{\qHhodge_m}^\star\qdeRham_{S/A}^{(m)}$ and $\fil_{\Hhodge_m}^\star\qIW_m\deRham_{S/A}$ as graded $A[q,\beta,t_m]/(\beta t_m-(q^m-1))$-modules $\fil_{\qHhodge}^*$ and $\fil_{\Hhodge}^*$. Finally, let us denote by $\beta^{-\star} A[\beta]$ the ascendingly filtered graded ring $$\beta^{-\star}A[\beta]\coloneqq \left(\dotsb\overset{\beta}{\longrightarrow}A[\beta](1)\overset{\beta}{\longrightarrow}A[\beta](0)\overset{\beta}{\longrightarrow}A[\beta](-1)\overset{\beta}{\longrightarrow}\dotsb\right)\,,$$ As explained in the proof of [\[lem:FiltrationAbstract\]](#lem:FiltrationAbstract){reference-type="ref" reference="lem:FiltrationAbstract"}, the filtration $\fil_\star^{\qIW_m\Omega}(\qHhodge_{S/A}/(q^m-1))$ can be written as follows: $$\fil_\star^{\qIW_m\Omega}\bigl(\qHhodge_{S/A}/(q^m-1)\bigr)\simeq \bigl( \fil_{\qHhodge}^*/t_m\lotimes_{A[\beta]}\beta^{-\star}A[\beta]\bigr)_0$$ (note that $*$ on the right-hand side refers to the graded degree whereas $\star$ corresponds to the filtration degree). Now consider the Bockstein cofibre sequence for $\fil_{\qHhodge}^*/t_m$. It fits into a commutative diagram of graded $A[q,\beta,t_m]/(\beta t_m-(q^m-1))$-modules $$\begin{tikzcd}
                \fil_{\qHhodge}^*(-1)/t_m\rar["t_m"] &  \fil_{\qHhodge}^*/t_m^2\rar &  \fil_{\qHhodge}^*/t_m\\
                \fil_{\qHhodge}^*/t_m\uar["\beta"]\urar["(q^m-1)"'] & &
            \end{tikzcd}$$ If we apply $(-\lotimes_{A[\beta]}A[\beta^{\pm 1}])_0$ to this diagram, the left vertical arrow becomes an equivalence and so the cofibre sequence from the top row will become equivalent to the Bockstein cofibre sequence $$\qHhodge_{R/A}/(q^m-1)\xrightarrow{(q^m-1)}\qHhodge_{R/A}/(q^m-1)^2\longrightarrow \qHhodge_{R/A}/(q^m-1)\,.$$ If we apply $(-\lotimes_{A[\beta]}\beta^{-\star}A[\beta])_0$ to the top row, we get a filtration on this cofibre sequence. By [\[enum:qWOmegaFiltrationPostnikov\]](#enum:qWOmegaFiltrationPostnikov){reference-type="ref" reference="enum:qWOmegaFiltrationPostnikov"}, this filtration will be of the form $$\tau^{\leqslant \star+1}\bigl(\qHhodge_{S/A}/(q^m-1)\bigr)\longrightarrow\bigl( \fil_{\qHhodge}/t_m^2\lotimes_{A[\beta]}\beta^{-\star}A[\beta]\bigr)_0\longrightarrow\tau^{\leqslant \star }\bigl(\qHhodge_{S/A}/(q^m-1)\bigr)$$ where $\bigl( \fil_{\qHhodge}/t_m^2\lotimes_{A[\beta]}\beta^{-\star}A[\beta]\bigr)_0$ is an ascending filtration on $\qHhodge_{S/A}/(q^m-1)^2$ that lies between $\tau^{\leqslant \star}$ and $\tau^{\leqslant \star +1}$. After passing to associated gradeds, the connecting morphism will then necessarily be the usual Bockstein differential $$\H^*\bigl(\qHhodge_{S/A}/(q^m-1)\bigr)\longrightarrow \H^{*+1}\bigl(\qHhodge_{S/A}/(q^m-1)\bigr)\,.$$ On the other hand, the associated graded of $\beta^{-\star}A[\beta]$ is given by $\bigoplus_{i\in\IZ}A(-i)$. If we apply $(-\lotimes_{A[\beta]}\bigoplus_{i\in\IZ}A(-i))_0$ to the top row of the diagram, we get the Bockstein cofibre sequence $$\fil_{\Hhodge}^*(-1)/t_m\overset{t_m}{\longrightarrow} \fil_{\Hhodge}^*/t_m^2\longrightarrow \fil_{\Hhodge}^*/t_m\,,$$ because $\fil_{\Hhodge}^*\simeq  \fil_{\qHhodge}^*/\beta$ by [\[prop:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationqDeforms\]](#prop:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationqDeforms){reference-type="ref" reference="prop:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationqDeforms"}. Since $\fil_{\Hhodge}^\star\qIW_m\deRham_{R/A}$ is the stupid filtration on the complex $\qIW_m\Omega_{R/A}^*$, the differential of $\qIW_m\Omega_{R/A}^*$ agrees with the connecting morphism for the Bockstein cofibre sequence of $\fil_{\Hhodge}^*/t_m$. This finishes the proof of [\[enum:BocksteinDifferential\]](#enum:BocksteinDifferential){reference-type="ref" reference="enum:BocksteinDifferential"}. ◻
:::

[^1]: In [@qdeRhamku], we'll recognise $\IZ[q,\beta,t_m]/(\beta t_m-(q^m-1))\cong \pi_*(\ku^{C_m})$.
