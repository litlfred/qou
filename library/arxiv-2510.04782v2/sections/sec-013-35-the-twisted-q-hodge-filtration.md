[]{#subsec:TwistedqHodgeFiltration label="subsec:TwistedqHodgeFiltration"}

For smooth $A$-algebras $S$, let $\fil_{\Hhodge_m}^\star \qIW_m\Omega_{S/A}^*$ denote the stupid filtration given by $\qIW_m\Omega_{S/A}^{\smash{\geqslant} n,*}$ in degree $n$. In general, let $$\fil_{\Hhodge_m}^\star \qIW_m\deRham_{-/A}\colon \cat{AniAlg}_A\longrightarrow \CAlg\Bigl( \Fil\Dd\bigl(A[q]/(q^{m}-1)\bigr)\Bigr)$$ be the animation of this functor. In this subsection, we'll show that once $\qdeRham_{R/A}$ is equipped with a $q$-Hodge filtration, the filtration $\fil_{\Hhodge_m}^\star \qIW_m\deRham_{R/A}$ admits a canonical $q^m$-deformation $$\fil_{\qHhodge_m}^\star \qdeRham_{R/A}^{(m)}\,.$$ This will eventually allow us to prove [\[thm:HabiroDescent\]](#thm:HabiroDescent){reference-type="ref" reference="thm:HabiroDescent"} in [0.1](#subsec:qHodgeHabiroDescent){reference-type="ref" reference="subsec:qHodgeHabiroDescent"} below. Let us first explain how $\fil_{\Hhodge_m}^\star \qIW_m\deRham_{R/A}$ is related to the Nygaard filtration from [\[subsec:Nygaard\]](#subsec:Nygaard){reference-type="ref" reference="subsec:Nygaard"}.

::: lem
[]{#lem:HodgevsNygaard label="lem:HodgevsNygaard"} For all smooth $A$-algebras $S$, all primes $p$ and all $\alpha\geqslant 1$ the diagram $$\begin{tikzcd}
                \fil_{\Hhodge_{p^\alpha}}^n\qIW_{p^\alpha}\Omega_{S/A}^*\rar\dar\drar[pullback] &  \fil_\Nn^n\qIW_{p^\alpha}\Omega_{S/A}^*\dar["p^{-n}\overtilde{F}_p"]\\
                \fil_{\Hhodge_{p^{\alpha-1}}}^n\qIW_{p^{\alpha-1}}\Omega_{S/A}^*\rar & \qIW_{p^{\alpha-1}}\Omega_{S/A}^*
            \end{tikzcd}$$ becomes a pullback in $\Dd(A[q])$ for all $n\geqslant 0$.
:::

::: proof
*Proof.* It's enough to check that the induced map on horizontal cofibres is an equivalence. Since $\fil_{\Hhodge_{p^\alpha}}^n\qIW_{p^\alpha}\Omega_{S/A}^*\rightarrow \fil_\Nn^n\qIW_{p^\alpha}\Omega_{S/A}^*$ is injective, the cofibre agrees with the cokernel, which is given by $$\left(p^{n-1}V_p\bigl(\qIW_{p^{\alpha-1}}\Omega_{S/A}^0\bigr) \rightarrow\dotsb\rightarrow p^0V_p\bigl(\qIW_{p^{\alpha-1}}\Omega_{S/A}^{n-1}\bigr)\rightarrow 0\rightarrow0\rightarrow\dotsb\right)\,.$$ Under $p^{-n}\overtilde{F}_p$, this complex is mapped isomorphically onto $$\left(\qIW_{p^{\alpha-1}}\Omega_{S/A}^0 \rightarrow\dotsb\rightarrow \qIW_{p^{\alpha-1}}\Omega_{S/A}^{n-1}\rightarrow 0\rightarrow0\rightarrow\dotsb\right)\,,$$ which is the cokernel (and the cofibre) of $\fil_{\Hhodge_{p^{\alpha-1}}}^n\qIW_{p^{\alpha-1}}\Omega_{S/A}^*\rightarrow \qIW_{p^{\alpha-1}}\Omega_{S/A}^*$ ◻
:::

::: cor
[]{#cor:qDRWSmoothAnimation label="cor:qDRWSmoothAnimation"} If $S$ is smooth over $A$, then $\qIW_m\deRham_{S/A}^n\simeq \Sigma^{-n}\qIW_m\Omega_{S/A}^n$ for all $m\in\IN$ and all degrees $n\geqslant 0$.
:::

::: proof
*Proof.* It's enough to show this rationally and after $p$-completion for all primes $p$. Rationally, [@qWitt Corollary ] shows $$\qIW_m\Omega_{S/A}^n\otimes_\IZ\IQ\cong \prod_{d\mid m}\left(\Omega_{S/A}^n\otimes_{A,\psi^d}(A\otimes\IQ)[\zeta_d]\right)$$ and it's well-known that the values of $\Omega_{-/A}^n$ on smooth $A$-algebras don't change under animation. After $p$-completion, [@qWitt Lemma ] allows us to restrict to the case where $m=p^\alpha$ is a prime power. Since $\qIW_{p^{\alpha}}\deRham_{S/A}^n\simeq \gr_{\Hhodge_{p^\alpha}}^n\qIW_{p^{\alpha}}\deRham_{S/A}$, it will be enough to show that the filtration $\fil_{\Hhodge_{p^\alpha}}^\star \qIW_{p^\alpha}\Omega_{S/A}^*$ is unchanged under animation. This follows via induction on $\alpha$ from [\[lem:HodgevsNygaard\]](#lem:HodgevsNygaard){reference-type="ref" reference="lem:HodgevsNygaard"} and [\[cor:NygaardQuasisyntomicDescent\]](#cor:NygaardQuasisyntomicDescent){reference-type="ref" reference="cor:NygaardQuasisyntomicDescent"}[\[enum:NygaardSmooth\]](#enum:NygaardSmooth){reference-type="ref" reference="enum:NygaardSmooth"}. ◻
:::

We now set out to construct the desired $q^m$-deformation of $\fil_{\Hhodge_m}^\star \qIW_m\deRham_{R/A}$.

::: numpar
[]{#con:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationpAdic label="con:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationpAdic"} Let's first construct the filtration for prime powers $m=p^\alpha$ and after $p$-completion. We'll use a recursive definition. For $\alpha=0$, $\qdeRham_{R/A}^{(p^0)}\simeq \qdeRham_{R/A}$ is just the $q$-de Rham complex and we choose $$\fil_{\qHhodge_{p^0}}^\star \bigl(\qdeRham_{R/A}^{(p^0)}\bigr)_p^\complete\coloneqq   \fil_{\qHodge}^\star \left(\qdeRham_{R/A}\right)_p^\complete$$ to be the given $q$-Hodge filtration. For $\alpha\geqslant 1$, we consider the "rescaling" of the filtration $\fil_{\qHhodge_{p^{\alpha-1}}}^\star \bigl(\qdeRham_{R/A}^{(p^{\alpha-1})}\bigr)_p^\complete$ by $\Phi_{p^\alpha}$, that is, $$\Phi_{p^\alpha}(q)^\star  \fil_{\qHhodge_{p^{\alpha-1}}}^\star \coloneqq \left( \fil_{\qHhodge_{p^{\alpha-1}}}^0\xleftarrow{\Phi_{p^\alpha}(q)} \fil_{\qHhodge_{p^{\alpha-1}}}^1\xleftarrow{\Phi_{p^\alpha}(q)}\dotsb\right)\,.$$ We also equip $\bigl(\qdeRham_{R/A}^{(p^{\alpha-1})}\bigr)_p^\complete$ with its $\Phi_{p^\alpha}(q)$-adic filtration.

Then we define $\fil_{\Hhodge_{p^\alpha}}^\star \bigl(\qdeRham_{R/A}^{(p^\alpha)}\bigr)_p^\complete$ as the following pullback of filtered objects: $$\begin{tikzcd}
                \fil_{\qHhodge_{p^\alpha}}^\star \bigl(\qdeRham_{R/A}^{(p^\alpha)}\bigr)_p^\complete\rar\dar\drar[pullback] &  \fil_\Nn^\star \bigl(\qdeRham_{R/A}^{(p^\alpha)}\bigr)_p^\complete\dar["\phi_{p/A[q]}"]\\
                \Phi_{p^\alpha}(q)^\star  \fil_{\qHhodge_{p^{\alpha-1}}}^\star \bigl(\qdeRham_{R/A}^{(p^{\alpha-1})}\bigr)_p^\complete\rar & \Phi_{p^\alpha}(q)^\star \bigl(\qdeRham_{R/A}^{(p^{\alpha-1})}\bigr)_p^\complete
            \end{tikzcd}$$ Using this pullback diagram, we can also inductively equip $\fil_{\qHhodge_{p^\alpha}}^\star \bigl(\qdeRham_{R/A}^{(p^\alpha)}\bigr)_p^\complete$ with the structure of a filtered module over the filtered ring $(q^{p^\alpha}-1)^\star A[q]$.
:::

::: rem
[]{#rem:PullbackModqpa-1 label="rem:PullbackModqpa-1"} If we reduce the pullback diagram above modulo $(q^{p^\alpha}-1)$ (where we invoke Convention [\[conv:QuotientConvention\]](#conv:QuotientConvention){reference-type="ref" reference="conv:QuotientConvention"} as usual), we obtain the pullback diagram from [\[lem:HodgevsNygaard\]](#lem:HodgevsNygaard){reference-type="ref" reference="lem:HodgevsNygaard"}. Indeed, this follows via induction on $\alpha$, using [\[prop:NygaardComparison\]](#prop:NygaardComparison){reference-type="ref" reference="prop:NygaardComparison"}. It follows that $$\fil_{\qHhodge_{p^\alpha}}^\star \bigl(\qdeRham_{R/A}^{(p^\alpha)}\bigr)_p^\complete/(q^{p^\alpha}-1)\simeq  \fil_{\Hhodge_{p^\alpha}}(\qIW_{p^\alpha}\deRham_{R/A})_p^\complete\,.$$
:::

::: numpar
[]{#par:TwistedqHodgeLaxSymmetricMonoidalI label="par:TwistedqHodgeLaxSymmetricMonoidalI"} The functor $$\fil_{\Hhodge_{p^\alpha}}^\star \bigl(\qdeRham_{-/A}^{(p^\alpha)}\bigr)_p^\complete\colon \cat{AniAlg}_A^{\qHodge}\longrightarrow  \Mod_{(q^{p^\alpha}-1)^\star A[q]}\Bigl(\Fil \Dd\bigl(A[q]\bigr)\Bigr)_{(p,q-1)}^\complete$$ comes equipped with a canonical lax symmetric monoidal structure. This follows from the recursive construction. For $\alpha=0$, [\[lem:qHodgeSymmetricMonoidal\]](#lem:qHodgeSymmetricMonoidal){reference-type="ref" reference="lem:qHodgeSymmetricMonoidal"} even provides a symmetric monoidal structure. For $\alpha\geqslant 1$, we must equip the legs of the pullback in [\[con:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationpAdic\]](#con:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationpAdic){reference-type="ref" reference="con:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationpAdic"} with the structure of symmetric monoidal transformations. This is not hard. First, the Frobenius $$\phi_{/A[q]}\colon  \fil_\Nn^\star \bigl(\qdeRham_{R/A}^{(p^\alpha)}\bigr)_p^\complete\longrightarrow \Phi_{p^\alpha}(q)^\star \bigl(\qdeRham_{R/A}^{(p^{\alpha-1})}\bigr)_p^\complete$$ becomes a symmetric monoidal transformation by quasi-syntomic descent from the case where $R$ is a $p$-complete quasi-syntomic $A$-algebra with a surjection $\widehat{A}_p\langle x_i^{1/p^\infty}\ |\ i\in I\rangle \twoheadrightarrow R$. In this case, we're dealing with filtrations of rings by ideals, so symmetric monoidality is automatic.

Second, the functor that "rescales" a filtration by $\Phi_{p^\alpha}(q)$ as in [\[con:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationpAdic\]](#con:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationpAdic){reference-type="ref" reference="con:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationpAdic"} is lax symmetric monoidal. Indeed, if we regard our filtered objects as graded modules over $\IZ[q,t]$, with the filtration parameter $t$ in graded degree $-1$, then rescaling corresponds to restriction along the $\IZ[q]$-linear map $\IZ[q,t]\rightarrow \IZ[q,t]$ that sends $t\mapsto \Phi_{p^\alpha}(q)t$. This is lax symmetric monoidal.
:::

::: numpar
[]{#par:TwistedqHodgeLaxSymmetricMonoidalII label="par:TwistedqHodgeLaxSymmetricMonoidalII"} It follows from the construction in [\[con:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationpAdic\]](#con:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationpAdic){reference-type="ref" reference="con:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationpAdic"} that we have a canonical map $$\fil_{\Hhodge_{p^\alpha}}^\star \bigl(\qdeRham_{R/A}^{(p^\alpha)}\bigr)_p^\complete\longrightarrow  \fil_{\Hhodge_{p^{\alpha-1}}}^\star \bigl(\qdeRham_{R/A}^{(p^{\alpha-1})}\bigr)_p^\complete$$ compatible with the relative Frobenius $\phi_{p/A[q]}\colon \bigl(\qdeRham_{R/A}^{(p^\alpha)}\bigr)_p^\complete\rightarrow\bigl(\qdeRham_{R/A}^{(p^{\alpha-1})}\bigr)_p^\complete$, because the "rescaling" by $\Phi_{p^\alpha}(q)$ of any filtration in non-negative degrees has a canonical map back to the original filtration. Moreover, by the discussion in [\[par:TwistedqHodgeLaxSymmetricMonoidalI\]](#par:TwistedqHodgeLaxSymmetricMonoidalI){reference-type="ref" reference="par:TwistedqHodgeLaxSymmetricMonoidalI"}, the map above can be canonically equipped with the structure of a symmetric monoidal transformation.
:::

::: lem
[]{#lem:TwistedqHodgepAdicCompatibilityI label="lem:TwistedqHodgepAdicCompatibilityI"} For all primes $p$ and all $\alpha\geqslant 0$, there exists a canonical equivalence of filtered $(q^{p^\alpha}-1)^\star A[q]$-modules $$\fil_{\qHhodge_{p^\alpha}}^\star \bigl(\qdeRham_{R/A}^{(p^\alpha)}\bigr)_p^\complete\bigl[\localise{p}\bigr]_{\Phi_{p^\alpha}(q)}^\complete\overset{\simeq}{\longrightarrow} \fil_{(\Hodge,\Phi_{p^\alpha}(q))}^\star \bigl(\deRham_{R/A}\lotimes_{A,\psi^{p^\alpha}}A\bigr)_p^\complete\bigl[\localise{p},q\bigr]_{\Phi_{p^\alpha}}^\complete\,,$$ where $\fil_{(\Hodge,\Phi_{p^\alpha}(q))}^\star$ denotes the combined Hodge and $\Phi_{p^\alpha}(q)$-adic filtration. This equivalence is compatible with $(\qdeRham_{R/A})_p^\complete[1/p]_{(q-1)}^\complete\simeq (\deRham_{R/A})_p^\complete[1/p]\qpower$.
:::

::: proof
*Proof.* For $\alpha=0$, this is the condition from [\[def:qHodgeFiltration\]](#def:qHodgeFiltration){reference-type="ref" reference="def:qHodgeFiltration"}[\[enum:qHodgeRationalpComplete\]](#enum:qHodgeRationalpComplete){reference-type="ref" reference="enum:qHodgeRationalpComplete"}. So let $\alpha\geqslant 1$. After applying $(-)[1/p]_{\Phi_{p^\alpha}(q)}^\complete$, the polynomial $(q^{p^{\alpha-1}}-1)$ becomes invertible, and so the filtered $(q^{p^{\alpha-1}}-1)^\star A[q]$-module $$\fil_{\qHhodge_{p^{\alpha-1}}}^\star \bigl(\qdeRham_{R/A}^{(p^{\alpha-1})}\bigr)_p^\complete\bigl[\localise{p}\bigr]_{\Phi_{p^\alpha}(q)}^\complete$$ must be the constant filtration on $\bigl(\qdeRham_{R/A}^{(p^{\alpha-1})}\bigr)_p^\complete[1/p]_{\Phi_{p^\alpha}(q)}^\complete$. Consequently, after applying $(-)[1/p]_{\Phi_{p^\alpha}(q)}^\complete$ the bottom horizontal arrow in the pullback diagram from [\[con:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationpAdic\]](#con:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationpAdic){reference-type="ref" reference="con:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationpAdic"} becomes an equivalence and thus the top horizontal arrow becomes an equivalence too. The desired assertion then follows via base change from [\[lem:NygaardRationalisation\]](#lem:NygaardRationalisation){reference-type="ref" reference="lem:NygaardRationalisation"}. ◻
:::

::: lem
[]{#lem:TwistedqHodgepAdicCompatibilityII label="lem:TwistedqHodgepAdicCompatibilityII"} For all primes $p$, all $\alpha\geqslant 1$, and all $0\leqslant i\leqslant \alpha-1$, the canonical map from [\[par:TwistedqHodgeLaxSymmetricMonoidalII\]](#par:TwistedqHodgeLaxSymmetricMonoidalII){reference-type="ref" reference="par:TwistedqHodgeLaxSymmetricMonoidalII"} induces an equivalence of filtered $(q^{p^\alpha}-1)^\star A[q]$-modules $$\fil_{\qHhodge_{p^\alpha}}^\star \bigl(\qdeRham_{R/A}^{(p^\alpha)}\bigr)_p^\complete\bigl[\localise{p}\bigr]_{\Phi_{p^{\smash{i}}}(q)}^\complete\overset{\simeq}{\longrightarrow} \fil_{\qHhodge_{p^{\alpha-1}}}^\star \bigl(\qdeRham_{R/A}^{(p^{\alpha-1})}\bigr)_p^\complete\bigl[\localise{p}\bigr]_{\Phi_{p^{\smash{i}}}(q)}^\complete\,.$$
:::

::: proof
*Proof.* After $(-)[1/p]_{\Phi_{p^{\smash{i}}}(q)}^\complete$, the polynomial $\Phi_{p^\alpha}(q)$ becomes invertible. Consequently, the "rescaling" of filtrations in [\[con:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationpAdic\]](#con:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationpAdic){reference-type="ref" reference="con:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationpAdic"} has no effect anymore. Moreover, it follows that the filtered $\Phi_{p^\alpha}(q)^\star A[q]$-module $$\fil_\Nn^\star \bigl(\qdeRham_{R/A}^{(p^\alpha)}\bigr)_p^\complete\bigl[\localise{p}\bigr]_{\Phi_{p^{\smash{i}}}(q)}^\complete$$ must be the constant filtration on $\bigl(\qdeRham_{R/A}^{(p^\alpha)}\bigr)_p^\complete\bigl[\localise{p}\bigr]_{\Phi_{p^{\smash{i}}}(q)}^\complete$. Thus, after applying $(-)[1/p]_{\Phi_{p^{\smash{i}}}(q)}^\complete$, the pullback from [\[con:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationpAdic\]](#con:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationpAdic){reference-type="ref" reference="con:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationpAdic"} collapses to the desired equivalence. ◻
:::

Let us finally construct the filtration $\fil_{\qHhodge_m}^\star \qdeRham_{R/A}^{(m)}$ in general.

::: numpar
[]{#con:TwistedqHodgeFiltration label="con:TwistedqHodgeFiltration"} Choose $N\neq 0$ divisible by $m$ (we'll argue below that the choice of $N$ doesn't matter). For every divisor $d\mid m$ and every prime $p\mid N$, write $m=p^{v_p(m)}m_p$ and $d=p^{v_p(d)}d_p$, where $m_p$ and $d_p$ are coprime to $p$. Using the animated version of [\[lem:TwistedqDeRhamFractureSquare\]](#lem:TwistedqDeRhamFractureSquare){reference-type="ref" reference="lem:TwistedqDeRhamFractureSquare"}, we obtain a pullback diagram $$\begin{tikzcd}
                \qdeRham_{R/A}^{(m)}\rar\dar\drar[pullback] & \prod_{p\mid N}\prod_{d_p\mid m_p}\left(\qdeRham_{R/A}\lotimes_{A[q],\psi^{p^{\smash{v_p(m)}}d_p}}A[q]\right)_{(p,\Phi_{d_p}(q))}^\complete\dar["\left(\phi_{p/A[q]}^{v_p(m/d)}\right)_{p\mid N,\,d\mid m}"]\\
                \prod_{d\mid m}\left(\qdeRham_{R/A}\lotimes_{A[q],\psi^d}A\bigl[\localise{N},q\bigr]\right)_{\Phi_d(q)}^\complete\rar & \prod_{p\mid N}\prod_{d \mid m}\Bigl(\qdeRham_{R/A}\lotimes_{A[q],\psi^{d}}A[q]\Bigr)_p^\complete\bigl[\localise{p}\bigr]_{\Phi_d(q)}^\complete
            \end{tikzcd}$$ To construct $\fil_{\qHhodge_m}^\star \qdeRham_{R/A}^{(m)}$, we'll equip each factor of the pullback above with a filtration and then check that these filtrations are compatible.

::: alphanumerate
On the factor $(\qdeRham_{R/A}\lotimes_{A[q],\psi^d}A[1/N,q])_{\Phi_d(q)}^\complete$ for any $d\mid m$, we put the base-changed $q$-Hodge filtration[]{#enum:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationRational label="enum:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationRational"} $$\left(\fil_{\qHodge}^\star \qdeRham_{R/A}\lotimes_{A[q],\psi^d}A\bigl[\localise{N},q\bigr]\right)_{\Phi_d(q)}^\complete\,.$$

On the factor $(\qdeRham_{R/A}\lotimes_{A[q],\psi^{d}}A[q])_p^\complete[1/p,q]_{\Phi_d(q)}^\complete$ for any prime $p\mid N$ and any $d\mid m$, we put again the base-changed $q$-Hodge filtration.[]{#enum:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationpAdicRational label="enum:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationpAdicRational"}

On the factor $(\qdeRham_{R/A}\lotimes_{A[q],\psi^{p^{\smash{v_p(m)}}d_p}}A[q])_{(p,\Phi_{d_p}(q))}^\complete$ for any prime $p\mid N$ and any $d_p\mid m_p$, we put the base-changed filtration[]{#enum:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationpAdic label="enum:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationpAdic"} $$\left( \fil_{\qHhodge_{p^{\smash{v_p(m)}}}}^\star \bigl(\qdeRham_{R/A}^{(p^{v_p(m)})}\bigr)_p^\complete\lotimes_{A[q],\psi^{d_p}}A[q]\right)_{(p,\Phi_{d_p}(q))}^\complete\,.$$
:::

Moreover, each of these filtrations is canonically a module over the filtered ring $(q^m-1)^\star A[q]$. It's clear that [\[enum:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationRational\]](#enum:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationRational){reference-type="ref" reference="enum:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationRational"} and [\[enum:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationpAdicRational\]](#enum:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationpAdicRational){reference-type="ref" reference="enum:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationpAdicRational"} are compatible as filtered $(q^m-1)^\star A[q]$-modules. To check that [\[enum:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationpAdic\]](#enum:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationpAdic){reference-type="ref" reference="enum:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationpAdic"} and [\[enum:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationpAdicRational\]](#enum:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationpAdicRational){reference-type="ref" reference="enum:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationpAdicRational"} are compatible, we may reduce via base change to the case where $m=p^\alpha$ is a power of $p$. From [\[lem:TwistedqHodgepAdicCompatibilityI,lem:TwistedqHodgepAdicCompatibilityII\]](#lem:TwistedqHodgepAdicCompatibilityI,lem:TwistedqHodgepAdicCompatibilityII){reference-type="ref" reference="lem:TwistedqHodgepAdicCompatibilityI,lem:TwistedqHodgepAdicCompatibilityII"} and our assumptions on $\fil_{\qHodge}^\star$ we deduce that both filtrations can be identified with the combined Hodge and $\Phi_{p^\alpha}(q)$-adic filtration $$\fil_{(\Hodge,\Phi_{p^\alpha}(q))}^\star \bigl(\deRham_{R/A}\lotimes_{A,\psi^{p^\alpha}}A\bigr)_p^\complete\bigl[\localise{p},q\bigr]_{\Phi_{p^\alpha}(q)}^\complete\,,$$ which yields the desired compatibility.

Let us now argue that the choice of $N$ is irrelevant. Suppose $N\mid N'$. Then the pullback diagrams for $N'$ is obtained from the pullback square for $N$ by replacing the bottom left corner $\prod_{d\mid m}(\qdeRham_{R/A}\lotimes_{A[q],\psi^{d}}A[1/N,q])_{\Phi_d(q)}^\complete$ by the pullback square $$\begin{tikzcd}
                \prod_{d\mid m}\left(\qdeRham_{R/A}\lotimes_{A[q],\psi^d}A\bigl[\localise{N},q\bigr]\right)_{\Phi_d(q)}^\complete\rar\dar\drar[pullback] & \prod_{\ell\vphantom{\mid}}\prod_{d\mid m}\left(\qdeRham_{R/A}\lotimes_{A[q],\psi^d}A\bigl[\localise{N},q\bigr]\right)_{(\ell,\Phi_d(q))}^\complete\dar\\
                \prod_{d\mid m}\left(\qdeRham_{R/A}\lotimes_{A[q],\psi^d}A\bigl[\localise{N'},q\bigr]\right)_{\Phi_d(q)}^\complete \rar &\prod_{\ell\vphantom{\mid}}\prod_{d\mid m}\left(\qdeRham_{R/A}\lotimes_{A[q],\psi^d}A\bigl[\localise{N},q\bigr]\right)_\ell^\complete\bigl[\localise{\ell}\bigr]_{\Phi_d(q)}^\complete
            \end{tikzcd}$$ where the product is taken over all primes $\ell$ such that $\ell\mid N'$ but $\ell\nmid N$. Note that for any such prime we also have $\ell\nmid m$, so each $v_\ell(m/d)=0$ and so each iterated Frobenius $\phi_{\ell/A[q]}^{v_\ell(m/d)}$ is the identity. Moreover, we see that the filtrations we put on the different factors is always $\fil_{\qHodge}^\star$, base changed along $\psi^d$. It follows that the filtrations constructed using $N$ and using $N'$ must indeed agree, as claimed. To get a canonical construction, we can let $N$ vary through a totally ordered initial sub-poset of $\IN$ (like $\{n!\}_{n\geqslant m}$) and then take the limit. This finishes the construction of $\fil_{\qHhodge_m}^\star \qdeRham_{R/A}^{(m)}$.

The construction is clearly functorial. Using [\[par:TwistedqHodgeLaxSymmetricMonoidalI\]](#par:TwistedqHodgeLaxSymmetricMonoidalI){reference-type="ref" reference="par:TwistedqHodgeLaxSymmetricMonoidalI"} and [\[par:TwistedqHodgeLaxSymmetricMonoidalII\]](#par:TwistedqHodgeLaxSymmetricMonoidalII){reference-type="ref" reference="par:TwistedqHodgeLaxSymmetricMonoidalII"} we also see that the functor $$\fil_{\qHhodge_m}^\star \qdeRham_{-/A}^{(m)}\colon \cat{AniAlg}_A^{\qHodge}\longrightarrow \Mod_{(q^m-1)^\star A[q]}\Bigl( \Fil\Dd\bigl(A[q]\bigr)\Bigr)_{(q^m-1)}^\complete$$ comes equipped with a canonical lax symmetric monoidal structure.
:::

::: prop
[]{#prop:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationqDeforms label="prop:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationqDeforms"} For all $m\in\IN$, the equivalence $\qdeRham_{R/A}^{(m)}/(q^m-1)\simeq \qIW_m\deRham_{R/A}$ from the animated version of [\[prop:TwistedqDeRhamDeformsqDRW\]](#prop:TwistedqDeRhamDeformsqDRW){reference-type="ref" reference="prop:TwistedqDeRhamDeformsqDRW"} upgrades canonically to an equivalence of filtered $A[q]/(q^m-1)$-modules $$\fil_{\qHhodge_m}^\star \qdeRham_{R/A}^{(m)}/(q^m-1)\overset{\simeq}{\longrightarrow} \fil_{\Hhodge_m}^\star \qIW_m\deRham_{R/A}$$ .
:::

::: proof
*Proof sketch.* We analyse the effect of $(-)/(q^m-1)$ on each of the factors in [\[con:TwistedqHodgeFiltration\]](#con:TwistedqHodgeFiltration){reference-type="ref" reference="con:TwistedqHodgeFiltration"}. For the factors in [\[con:TwistedqHodgeFiltration\]](#con:TwistedqHodgeFiltration){reference-type="ref" reference="con:TwistedqHodgeFiltration"}[\[enum:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationpAdic\]](#enum:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationpAdic){reference-type="ref" reference="enum:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationpAdic"}, note that $(q^m-1)$ and $\Phi_{d_p}(q^{p^{v_p(m)}})$ will only differ by a unit upon $(p,\Phi_{d_p}(q))$-adic completion. Then the argument in [\[rem:PullbackModqpa-1\]](#rem:PullbackModqpa-1){reference-type="ref" reference="rem:PullbackModqpa-1"} plus base change shows that after modding out $(q^m-1)$ we get $$\left( \fil_{\Hhodge_{p^{\smash{v_p(m)}}}}^\star \qIW_{p^{\smash{v_p(m)}}}\deRham_{R/A}\lotimes_{A[q],\psi^{d_p}}A[q]\right)_p^\complete/\Phi_{d_p}\bigl(q^{p^{v_p(m)}}\bigr)$$ It follows from [@qWitt Lemma ] that $\fil_{\Hhodge_m}^\star (\qIW_m\deRham_{R/A})_p^\complete$ is indeed a product of factors of this form.

For the factors in [\[con:TwistedqHodgeFiltration\]](#con:TwistedqHodgeFiltration){reference-type="ref" reference="con:TwistedqHodgeFiltration"}[\[enum:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationRational\]](#enum:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationRational){reference-type="ref" reference="enum:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationRational"}, note that $(q^m-1)$ and $\Phi_d(q)$ will only differ by a unit after $(-)[1/N]_{\Phi_d(q)}^\complete$. By construction, the $q$-Hodge filtration becomes the Hodge filtration modulo $(q-1)$. Thus, after base change along $\psi^d\colon A[q]\rightarrow A[q]$, we get $$\left(\fil_{\qHodge}^\star \qdeRham_{R/A}\lotimes_{A[q],\psi^d}A\bigl[\localise{N},q\bigr]\right)_{\Phi_d(q)}^\complete/\Phi_d(q)\simeq \fil_{\Hodge}^\star \deRham_{R/A}\lotimes_{A,\psi^d}A\bigl[\localise{N},\zeta_d\bigr]\,.$$ It follows from [@qWitt Corollary ] that $\fil_{\Hhodge_m}^\star \qIW_m\deRham_{R/A}[1/N]$ is indeed a product of factors of this form. The same argument applies for the factors in [\[con:TwistedqHodgeFiltration\]](#con:TwistedqHodgeFiltration){reference-type="ref" reference="con:TwistedqHodgeFiltration"}[\[enum:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationpAdicRational\]](#enum:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationpAdicRational){reference-type="ref" reference="enum:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationpAdicRational"}. ◻
:::

::: rem
It follows from the proof that the equivalence in [\[prop:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationqDeforms\]](#prop:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationqDeforms){reference-type="ref" reference="prop:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationqDeforms"} is, in fact, an equivalence of lax symmetric monoidal functors $\cat{AniAlg}_A^{\qHodge}\rightarrow  \Fil\Dd(A[q]/(q^m-1))$. Thus, if $(R, \fil_{\qHodge}^\star \qdeRham_{R/A})$ admits the structure of an $\IE_n$-algebra in $\cat{AniAlg}_A^{\qHodge}$ for any $0\leqslant n\leqslant\infty$, then the equivalence in [\[prop:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationqDeforms\]](#prop:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationqDeforms){reference-type="ref" reference="prop:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationqDeforms"} will be one of filtered $\IE_n$-$A[q]/(q^m-1)$-algebras.
:::

::: numpar
[]{#con:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationFunctorial label="con:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationFunctorial"} Whenever $n\mid m$, there's a canonical map of filtered objects $$\fil_{\qHhodge_m}^\star \qdeRham_{R/A}^{(m)}\longrightarrow  \fil_{\qHhodge_n}^\star \qdeRham_{R/A}^{(n)}\,.$$ To construct this, we look at the factors of the pullback from [\[con:TwistedqHodgeFiltration\]](#con:TwistedqHodgeFiltration){reference-type="ref" reference="con:TwistedqHodgeFiltration"} (we're allowed to use the same $N$ for both $m$ and $n$). For the factors from [\[con:TwistedqHodgeFiltration\]](#con:TwistedqHodgeFiltration){reference-type="ref" reference="con:TwistedqHodgeFiltration"}[\[enum:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationRational\]](#enum:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationRational){reference-type="ref" reference="enum:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationRational"} and [\[enum:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationpAdicRational\]](#enum:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationpAdicRational){reference-type="ref" reference="enum:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationpAdicRational"}, we simply project to those where $d\mid n$. For the factors from [\[con:TwistedqHodgeFiltration\]](#con:TwistedqHodgeFiltration){reference-type="ref" reference="con:TwistedqHodgeFiltration"}[\[enum:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationpAdic\]](#enum:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationpAdic){reference-type="ref" reference="enum:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationpAdic"}, we first project to those where $d_p\mid n_p$ and then we use the maps from [\[par:TwistedqHodgeLaxSymmetricMonoidalII\]](#par:TwistedqHodgeLaxSymmetricMonoidalII){reference-type="ref" reference="par:TwistedqHodgeLaxSymmetricMonoidalII"}, base changed along $\psi^{d_p}\colon A[q]\rightarrow A[q]$.

It's clear from the construction that these maps $\fil_{\qHhodge_m}^\star \qdeRham_{R/A}^{(m)}\rightarrow  \fil_{\qHhodge_n}^\star \qdeRham_{R/A}^{(n)}$ assemble canonically into a symmetric monoidal transformation of lax symmetric monoidal functors. With some more effort, one can also make these transformations functorial in $n,m\in\IN$, where $\IN$ denotes the category of natural numbers partially ordered by divisibility. For our purposes, the existence of the individual maps is enough, as any $\limit_{m\in\IN}$ can be replaced by the limit over the sequential subdiagram given by $\{n!\}_{n\geqslant 1}$. We will therefore not spell out the construction of this additional functoriality.
:::

## Habiro descent for $q$-Hodge complexes {#subsec:qHodgeHabiroDescent}

In this subsection, we'll finish the proof of [\[thm:HabiroDescent\]](#thm:HabiroDescent){reference-type="ref" reference="thm:HabiroDescent"}, following the outline that we have explained at the end of [\[subsec:MainResult\]](#subsec:MainResult){reference-type="ref" reference="subsec:MainResult"}.

::: numpar
[]{#con:HabiroDescent0 label="con:HabiroDescent0"} For all $m\in\IN$, we consider the colimit $$\qHhodge_{R/A,m}\coloneqq \colimit\Bigl( \fil_{\qHhodge_m}^{0}\qdeRham_{R/A}^{(m)}\xrightarrow{(q^m-1)} \fil_{\qHhodge_m}^{1}\qdeRham_{R/A}^{(m)}\xrightarrow{(q^m-1)}\dotso\Bigr)_{(q^m-1)}^\complete\,.$$ In the following, we'll informally write $$\qHhodge_{R/A,  m}\simeq \qdeRham_{R/A}^{(m)}\left[\frac{ \fil_{\qHhodge_m}^i}{(q^m-1)^i}\ \middle|\ i\geqslant 1\right]_{(q^m-1)}^\complete$$ and we'll say that *$\qHhodge_{R/A,  m}$ is given by adjoining $(q^m-1)^{-\star} \fil_{\qHhodge_m}^\star$ to $\qdeRham_{R/A}^{(m)}$*. We'll also use similar notation and terminology for related filtrations such as the Nygaard filtration or the combined Hodge and $\Phi_d(q)$-adic filtration for some $d\mid m$.
:::

::: prop
[]{#prop:HabiroDescent label="prop:HabiroDescent"} Let $m\in\IN$. For all divisors $n\mid m$, the map from [\[con:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationFunctorial\]](#con:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationFunctorial){reference-type="ref" reference="con:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationFunctorial"} induces an equivalence $$\bigl(\qHhodge_{R/A,  m}\bigr)_{(q^n-1)}^\complete\overset{\simeq}{\longrightarrow}\qHhodge_{R/A,  n}\,.$$ In particular, $\qHhodge_{R/A,  m}$ is a descent of $\qHodge_{R/A}$ along $\IZ[q]_{(q^m-1)}^\complete\rightarrow \IZ\qpower$.
:::

::: proof
*Proof sketch.* Again, we look at the different factors from [\[con:TwistedqHodgeFiltration\]](#con:TwistedqHodgeFiltration){reference-type="ref" reference="con:TwistedqHodgeFiltration"}. Let's start with those from [\[con:TwistedqHodgeFiltration\]](#con:TwistedqHodgeFiltration){reference-type="ref" reference="con:TwistedqHodgeFiltration"}[\[enum:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationRational\]](#enum:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationRational){reference-type="ref" reference="enum:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationRational"} for some $d\mid m$. If $d\nmid n$, then $\Phi_d(q)$ and $(q^n-1)$ are coprime in $\IQ[q]$ and so the factor will die after $(q^n-1)$-completion. Therefore in $(\qHhodge_{R/A,  m})_{(q^n-1)}^\complete$ only those factors where $d\mid n$ will survive. These are precisely the factors that are also used in the construction of of $\qHhodge_{R/A,  n}$. Moreover, if $d\mid n$ then both $(q^m-1)$ and $(q^n-1)$ are unit multiples of $\Phi_d(q)$ in $\IQ[q]_{\Phi_d(q)}^\complete$, so it doesn't matter whether we adjoin $(q^m-1)^{-\star} \fil_{(\Hodge,\Phi_d(q))}^\star$ or $(q^n-1)^{-\star} \fil_{(\Hodge,\Phi_d(q))}^\star$. It follows that on the factors from [\[con:TwistedqHodgeFiltration\]](#con:TwistedqHodgeFiltration){reference-type="ref" reference="con:TwistedqHodgeFiltration"}[\[enum:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationRational\]](#enum:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationRational){reference-type="ref" reference="enum:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationRational"} we get indeed an equivalence. The same argument applies to the factors from [\[con:TwistedqHodgeFiltration\]](#con:TwistedqHodgeFiltration){reference-type="ref" reference="con:TwistedqHodgeFiltration"}[\[enum:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationpAdicRational\]](#enum:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationpAdicRational){reference-type="ref" reference="enum:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationpAdicRational"}.

It remains to show that we also get an equivalence on the factors from [\[con:TwistedqHodgeFiltration\]](#con:TwistedqHodgeFiltration){reference-type="ref" reference="con:TwistedqHodgeFiltration"}[\[enum:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationpAdic\]](#enum:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationpAdic){reference-type="ref" reference="enum:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationpAdic"}. So let's consider such a factor for some prime $p$ and some $d_p\mid m_p$. Using induction, we may assume that $m$ and $n$ differ only by a single prime factor. If that prime is different from $p$, then $(q^m-1)$ and $(q^n-1)$ will differ by a unit after $(p,\Phi_{d_p}(q))$-completion and we can argue as above. So assume $n=m/p$. Via base change along $\psi^{d_p}\colon A[q]\rightarrow A[q]$, we may reduce to the case where $m=p^\alpha$ is a prime power and $n=p^{\alpha-1}$. From [\[con:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationpAdic\]](#con:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationpAdic){reference-type="ref" reference="con:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationpAdic"} we obtain a pullback diagram $$\begin{tikzcd}[column sep=small]
                \bigl(\qdeRham_{R/A}^{(p^\alpha)}\bigr)\left[\frac{ \fil_{\qHhodge_{p^\alpha}}^i}{(q^{p^\alpha}-1)^i}\ \middle|\ i\geqslant 0\right]_{(p,q^{p^{\alpha-1}}-1)}^\complete\rar\dar\drar[pullback] & \bigl(\qdeRham_{R/A}^{(p^\alpha)}\bigr)\left[\frac{ \fil_\Nn^i}{(q^{p^\alpha}-1)^i}\ \middle|\ i\geqslant 0\right]_{(p,q^{p^{\alpha-1}}-1)}^\complete\dar\\
                \bigl(\qdeRham_{R/A}^{(p^{\alpha-1})}\bigr)\left[\frac{ \fil_{\qHhodge_{p^{\alpha-1}}}^i}{(q^{p^{\alpha-1}}-1)^i}\ \middle|\ i\geqslant 0\right]_{(p,q^{p^{\alpha-1}}-1)}^\complete\rar & \bigl(\qdeRham_{R/A}^{(p^{\alpha-1})}\bigr)\left[\frac{1}{(q^{p^{\alpha-1}}-1)}\right]_{(p,q^{p^{\alpha-1}}-1)}^\complete
            \end{tikzcd}$$ To finish the proof, we must show that the left vertical arrow is an equivalence. Since the diagram is a pullback, it will be enough to show that the right vertical arrow is an equivalence.

[^1]

This is now purely an assertion about the Nygaard filtration. Via base change, we may reduce to the case $\alpha=1$. This case will be shown in [\[lem:NygaardDenominators\]](#lem:NygaardDenominators){reference-type="ref" reference="lem:NygaardDenominators"} below. ◻
:::

::: lem
[]{#lem:NygaardDenominators label="lem:NygaardDenominators"} The relative Frobenius $\phi_{p/A[q]}\colon \bigl(\qdeRham_{R/A}^{(p)}\bigr)_p^\complete\rightarrow (\qdeRham_{R/A})_p^\complete$ induces functorial equivalences $$\begin{aligned}
            \bigl(\qdeRham_{R/A}^{(p)}\bigr)_p^\complete\left[\frac{ \fil_\Nn^i}{\Phi_p(q)^i}\ \middle|\ i\geqslant 0\right]_{(p,q-1)}^\complete&\overset{\simeq}{\longrightarrow} \left(\qdeRham_{R/A}\right)_p^\complete\,,\\ \bigl(\qdeRham_{R/A}^{(p)}\bigr)_p^\complete\left[\frac{ \fil_\Nn^i}{(q^p-1)^i}\ \middle|\ i\geqslant 0\right]_{(p,q-1)}^\complete&\overset{\simeq}{\longrightarrow} \left(\qdeRham_{R/A}\right)_p^\complete\left[\frac{1}{(q-1)}\right]_{(p,q-1)}^\complete\simeq 0\,.
        
\end{aligned}$$
:::

::: proof
*Proof.* We start with the first equivalence. Since both sides are $\Phi_p(q)$-complete, it will be enough to show the equivalence modulo $\Phi_p(q)$. The same argument as in [\[par:ConjugateFiltration\]](#par:ConjugateFiltration){reference-type="ref" reference="par:ConjugateFiltration"} shows $$\bigl(\qdeRham_{R/A}^{(p)}\bigr)_p^\complete\left[\frac{ \fil_\Nn^i}{\Phi_p(q)^i}\ \middle|\ i\geqslant 0\right]/\Phi_p(q)\simeq \colimit\Bigl(\gr_\Nn^0\xrightarrow{\Phi_p(q)}\gr_\Nn^1\xrightarrow{\Phi_p(q)}\dotsb\Bigr)\,.$$ The divided Frobenius $\Phi_p(q)^{-i}\phi_{p/A[q]}$ maps $\gr_\Nn^i$ isomorphically onto $\fil_i^\mathrm{conj}(\qdeRham_{R/A}/\Phi_p(q))$ (by [@Prismatic Theorem ] plus quasi-syntomic descent and animation to cover all animated $A$-algebras $R$). Since the conjugate filtration is exhaustive, this shows the first of the two claimed equivalences.

For the second equivalence, note that the inclusion of the diagonal into any $\IZ_{\geqslant 0}\times \IZ_{\geqslant 0}$-shaped diagram is coinitial. Therefore, we can write $$\bigl(\qdeRham_{R/A}^{(p)}\bigr)_p^\complete\left[\frac{ \fil_\Nn^i}{(q^p-1)^i}\ \middle|\ i\geqslant 0\right]\simeq \colimit\left(\begin{tikzcd}
                \fil_\Nn^0\dar["(q-1)"]\rar["\Phi_p(q)"] &  \fil_\Nn^1\dar["(q-1)"]\rar["\Phi_p(q)"] & \dotsb \\
                \fil_\Nn^0\dar["(q-1)"]\rar["\Phi_p(q)"] &  \fil_\Nn^1\dar["(q-1)"]\rar["\Phi_p(q)"] & \dotsb \\
                \vdots & \vdots & \ddots
            \end{tikzcd}\right)$$ By the first equivalence, the $(p,q-1)$-completed colimit of every row in this diagram is $(\qdeRham_{R/A})_p^\complete$. If we then take the colimit in the vertical direction, the second equivalence follows and we're done ◻
:::

::: numpar
[]{#con:HabiroDescent label="con:HabiroDescent"} Let $(R, \fil_{\qHodge}^\star \qdeRham_{R/A})$ be an object in $\cat{AniAlg}_A^{\qHodge}$. We define the *Habiro--Hodge complex of $R$ over $A$* to be $$\qHhodge_{R/A}\coloneqq \limit_{m\in\IN}\qHhodge_{R/A,  m}\,.$$ The same argument as in the proof of [\[lem:qHodgeSymmetricMonoidal\]](#lem:qHodgeSymmetricMonoidal){reference-type="ref" reference="lem:qHodgeSymmetricMonoidal"} allows us to equip $\qHhodge_{-/A,  m}$ with a lax symmetric monoidal structure for all $m\in\IN$; thanks to [\[con:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationFunctorial\]](#con:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationFunctorial){reference-type="ref" reference="con:TwistedqHodgeFiltrationFunctorial"}, the equivalences from [\[prop:HabiroDescent\]](#prop:HabiroDescent){reference-type="ref" reference="prop:HabiroDescent"} will be compatible with this lax symmetric monoidal structure. It follows that there's a diagram of lax symmetric monoidal functors $$\begin{tikzcd}[column sep=huge]
                & \widehat{\Dd}_\Hh\bigl(A[q]\bigr)\dar["(-)_{(q-1)}^\complete"]\\
                \cat{AniAlg}_A^{\qHodge}\rar["\qHodge_{-/A}"']\urar[dashed,"\qHhodge_{-/A}"] & \widehat{\Dd}_{(q-1)}\bigl(A[q]\bigr)
            \end{tikzcd}$$
:::

::: lem
[]{#lem:HabiroDescentSymmetricMonoidal label="lem:HabiroDescentSymmetricMonoidal"} The lax symmetric monoidal functor $\qHhodge_{-/A}\colon \cat{AniAlg}_A^{\qHodge}\rightarrow \widehat{\Dd}_\Hh(A[q])$ is, in fact, symmetric monoidal.
:::

::: proof
*Proof.* It will be enough to show that for all $m\in\IN$ the functor $$\qHhodge_{-/A}/(q^m-1)\left[\bigl\{(q^d-1)^{-1}\}_{d\mid m,\, d\neq m}\right]$$ is symmetric monoidal. The same argument as in the proof of [\[lem:qHodgeSymmetricMonoidal\]](#lem:qHodgeSymmetricMonoidal){reference-type="ref" reference="lem:qHodgeSymmetricMonoidal"} allows us to equip the filtration $\fil_\star ^{\qIW_m\Omega}(\qHhodge_{-/A}/(q^m-1))$ from [\[thm:HabiroDescent\]](#thm:HabiroDescent){reference-type="ref" reference="thm:HabiroDescent"}[\[enum:qdRWComparison\]](#enum:qdRWComparison){reference-type="ref" reference="enum:qdRWComparison"} with a lax symmetric monoidal structure. Symmetric monoidality can then be checked on the associated graded $$\gr_*^{\qIW_m\Omega}\bigl(\qHhodge_{-/A}/(q^m-1)\bigr)\simeq \Sigma^{-*}\qIW_m\deRham_{-/A}^*\simeq \gr_{\Hhodge_m}^*\qIW_m\deRham_{-/A}\,.$$ Thus, it would be enough to show that $\fil_{\Hhodge_m}^\star \qIW_m\deRham_{-/A}$ is symmetric monoidal. This is not true on the nose. However, once we invert $(q^d-1)$ for all divisors $d\mid m$, $d\neq m$, we claim that the first ghost map $$\gh_1\colon  \fil_{\Hhodge_m}^\star \qIW_m\deRham_{-/A}\overset{\simeq}{\longrightarrow} \fil_{\Hodge}^\star \deRham_{-/A}\lotimes_{A,\psi^m}A[\zeta_m]$$ becomes an equivalence. If we can show this, we're done, since the Hodge filtration $\fil_{\Hodge}^\star \deRham_{-/A}$ is symmetric monoidal.

To prove this claim, observe that for any ordinary $R$-algebra $A$ and any $d\mid m$, $d\neq m$, the $q$-de Rham--Witt complex $\qIW_d\Omega_{R/A}^*$ is $(q^d-1)$-torsion and so it dies after inverting $(q^d-1)$. With this observation, a simple comparison of universal properties (compare the argument in [@qWitt Lemma ]) shows that $$\gh_1\colon \qIW_m\Omega_{R/A}^*\left[\bigl\{(q^d-1)^{-1}\}_{d\mid m,\, d\neq m}\right]\overset{\cong}{\longrightarrow} \Omega_{R/A}^*\otimes_{A,\psi^m}A\left[\zeta_m,\bigl\{(\zeta_m^d-1)^{-1}\}_{d\mid m,\, d\neq m}\right]$$ is an isomorphism of complexes. In particular, it induces an isomorphism on stupid filtrations. By passing to animations, the above claim about $\gh_1$ follows and so we're done. ◻
:::

At this point, we've assembled all the ingredients to carry out the proof of [\[thm:HabiroDescent\]](#thm:HabiroDescent){reference-type="ref" reference="thm:HabiroDescent"} as outlined at the end of [\[subsec:MainResult\]](#subsec:MainResult){reference-type="ref" reference="subsec:MainResult"}, and so the proof is finally finished.
