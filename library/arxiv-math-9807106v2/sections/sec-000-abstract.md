of ﬁnite dimensional representations of sl(3). It is generated by characters with values in
the group ring Z[ ˜W ] of the extended aﬃne Weyl group of ˆsl(3)k atk ⁄∈Q. The ‘quantised’
version at rational level k + 3 = 3 /p realises the fusion rules of a WZW conformal ﬁeld
theory based on admissible representations of ˆsl(3)k.
