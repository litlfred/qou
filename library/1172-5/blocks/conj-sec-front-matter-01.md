Let $J_{N}(K)$ be the $N$-colored Jones polynomial of a knot $K$ evaluated at $\exp(\frac{2\pi\sqrt{-1}}{N})$ . Then $\lim_{Narrow\infty}\frac{\log|J_{N}(I\mathrm{f})|}{N}=\frac{v_{3}}{2\pi}||K||$, where $||K||$ is the Gromov norm (or the simplicial volume) of the complement of $K$ and $v_{3}$ is the hyperbolic volume of the regular ideal tetrahedron.
