Now I propose a very ambiguous conjecture.
Conjecture 5.1 (Volume conjecture for closed three-manifolds). For any closed
three-manifold $M$
$N arrow\infty 0-\lim\frac{2\pi\sqrt{-1}\log\tau N(M)}{N}=\mathrm{C}\mathrm{S}(M)+\sqrt{-1}\mathrm{V}_{0}1(M)$ ,
where
$\mathrm{V}\mathrm{o}\mathrm{l}(M):=v_{3}||M||$ with
$||M||$ the simplicial volume of $M$ .
A weaker but more precise conjecture is
Conjecture 5.2. For an integer
$p$ put
$V_{p}(_{Z,w}).--- \mathrm{L}\mathrm{i}_{2}(Zw)+\mathrm{L}\mathrm{i}_{2}(\frac{z}{w})+\frac{p}{4}(\log z)2-\log_{Z}\log w$ .
Then there exists
$(\zeta_{p},\omega_{p})$ such that
(1)
$\frac{\partial V_{p}}{\partial z}(\zeta_{p}, \omega_{p})=\frac{\partial V_{p}}{\partial w}(\zeta_{p}, \omega_{p})=0$ , and
(2)
$V_{\mathrm{p}}(\zeta_{\mathrm{P}}, \omega_{\mathrm{p}})=\mathrm{C}\mathrm{S}(M_{P})+\sqrt{-1}\mathrm{V}_{0}1(M_{p})$ .
Here
$M_{p}$ is the closed three-manifold obtained from the three-sphere by p-surgery
along the ﬁgure-eight knot.
Remark 5.3. Conjecture 5.2 is numerically true (up to 8 digits or so) for $p=$
$0,$ $\pm 1,$
$\pm 2,$ $\pm 3,$
$\pm 5,$ $\pm 6,$
$\ldots,$ $\pm 100$ .
Remark 5.4. Note that
$V_{p}(1, w)=- \mathrm{L}\mathrm{i}_{2}(w)+\mathrm{L}\mathrm{i}_{2}(\frac{1}{w})$ and this appears in calcu-
lations about the ﬁgure-eight knot [2, 3.15]. More precisely
$\omega:=\exp(-\frac{\pi\sqrt{-1}}{3})$
satisﬁes
(1)
$\frac{\partial V_{p}}{\partial w}(1,\omega)=0$ , and
(2)
$V_{p}(1,\omega)=\mathrm{C}\mathrm{S}(4_{1})+\sqrt{-1}\mathrm{V}\mathrm{o}\mathrm{l}(4_{1})$.
Finally I raise some natural problems.
Problem 5.5. Can one generalize Conjecture 5.2 to rational surgery? Compare
with [15].
Problem 5.6.
$\mathrm{F}\mathrm{o}1$ any knot
$K$ (or more generally link) and any rational number
$p$ , does there exist a function
$V_{p}$ as above?
Remark 5.7. Y. Yokota told me that Conjecture 5.2 and Problem 5.5 can be solved
by considering deformations of tetrahedron decomposition described in [13].
