Year of discovery
Similarly to the previous version of NUBASE [3, 4], Ta-
ble I includes information about the year of discovery for each
nuclide in its ground or isomeric state. For the former, rec-
ommendations by Thoennessen [21] were adopted. Similar
criteria were used when assigning the year of discovery for
isomeric states.
