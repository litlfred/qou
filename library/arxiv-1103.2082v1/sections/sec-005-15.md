Deligne’s construction naturally generalizes to a wide variety of settings within representation
theory. We expect that modiﬁed traces should exist for many of these other categories and it
would be interesting to investigate this question. Deligne’s category Re p(St) has a relatively
elementary structure (for example, it has a single nontrivial tensor ideal) and we expect that
studying modiﬁed traces in these other settings will be signiﬁcantly more involved.
