We now consider the general case when t is a nonnegative integer. Let N be the tensor
ideal of negligible morphisms and letI =I(N ). Recall that by deﬁnition we call the objects
ofI negligible. In this section we show there exists a nonzero trace on I in Rep(St) when t
is a nonnegative integer.
