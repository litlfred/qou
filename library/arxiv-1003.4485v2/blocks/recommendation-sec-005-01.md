So, the fourth and final piece of data in our crossed module is: \begin{itemize} \item An action $\alpha$ of $G$ on $H$ given by: \[ \xymatrix{ \bullet &&\bullet \ar@/_2.5ex/[ll]_{g}="g1"\ar@/^2.5ex/[ll]^{g}="g2" \ar@{=>}^{1_g} "g1"+<0ex,-2.5ex>;"g2"+<0ex,2.5ex> &&\bullet \ar@/_2.5ex/[ll]_{1_\bullet}="g1"\ar@/^2.5ex/[ll]^{t(h)}="g2" \ar@{=>}^{h} "g1"+<0ex,-2.5ex>;"g2"+<0ex,2.5ex> &&\bullet \ar@/_2.5ex/[ll]_{g^{-1}}="g1"\ar@/^2.5ex/[ll]^{g^{-1}}="g2" \ar@{=>}^{1_g^{-1}} "g1"+<0ex,-2.5ex>;"g2"+<0ex,2.5ex> } \quad = \quad \xymatrix{ \bullet &&\bullet \ar@/_2.5ex/[ll]_{1}="g1"\ar@/^2.5ex/[ll]^{t(\alpha(g)(h))}="g2" \ar@{=>}^{\alpha(g)(h)} "g1"+<-1ex,-2.5ex>;"g2"+<-1ex,2.5ex> } \] \end{itemize} A crossed module $(G,H,t,\alpha)$ must also satisfy two more equations which follow from the definition of a 2-group.
