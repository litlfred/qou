Let X be a quasi-compact ﬁbered category over R and D ⊆Vect(X) be a full subcategory that generates QCoh(X). Then Ω∗: QCoh XA →LR(D, A) is fully faithful with essential image LexR(D, A) and it has an exact left adjoint F∗,D : LR(D, A) →QCoh XA. In particular Ω∗and (F∗,D)| LexR(D,A) are quasi-inverses of each other. Assume that D is a monoidal subcategory of Vect(X). Then the functor Ω∗and F∗,D extends to adjoint functors Ω∗: QAlg XA →MLR(D, A) and A∗,C : MLR(D, A) →QAlg XA. Moreover Ω∗: QAlg(XA) →MLexR(D, A) is an equivalence and (A∗,C)| MLexR(D,A) is a quasi-inverse.
