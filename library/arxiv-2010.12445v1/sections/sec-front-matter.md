STACKS OF FIBER FUNCTORS AND TANNAKA’S RECONSTRUCTION
FABIO TONINI
Abstract. Given a quasi-compact category ﬁbered in groupoids X and a monoidal subcat-
egory C of its category of locally free sheaves Vect(X), we are going to introduce the stack of
ﬁber functors FibX,C with source C, which comes equipped with a map PC : X →FibX,C and
a functor G : C →Vect(FibX,C).
If C generates QCoh(X) and X is an fpqc stack with quasi-aﬃne diagonal, we show that
PC : X →FibX,C is an equivalence, as it happens by Tannaka’s reconstruction when X is
an aﬃne gerbe over a ﬁeld. In general, under mild assumption on C, e.g. C = Vect(X), we
show that FibX,C is a quasi-compact fpqc stack with aﬃne diagonal and that the image G(C)
generates QCoh(FibX,C).
