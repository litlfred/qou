Mathematical papers intended for publication in the Pacific Journal of Mathematics should be in typed
form or offset-reproduced (not dittoed), double spaced with large margins. Please do not use built up fractions
in the text of the manuscript. However, you may use them in the displayed equations. Underline Greek letters
in red, German in green, and script in blue. The first paragraph must be capable of being used separately as
a synopsis of the entire paper. In particular it should contain no bibliographic references. Please propose a
heading for the odd numbered pages of less than 35 characters. Manuscripts, in triplicate, may be sent to any
one of the editors. Please classify according to the 1991 Mathematics Subject Classification scheme which
can be found in the December index volumes of Mathematical Reviews. Supply name and address of author
to whom proofs should be sent. All other communications should be addressed to the managing editor, or
Julie Speckart, University of California, Los Angeles, California 90024-1555.
