HAROLD WIDOM’S WORK IN RANDOM MATRIX THEORY
IVAN Z. CORWIN, PERCY A. DEIFT, AND ALEXANDER R. ITS
Abstract. This is a survey of Harold Widom’s work in random matrices. We start with
his pioneering papers on the sine-kernel determinant, continue with his and Craig Tracy’s
groundbreaking results concerning the distribution functions of random matrix theory, touch
on the remarkable universality of the Tracy-Widom distributions in mathematics and physics,
and close with Tracy and Widom’s remarkable work on the asymmetric simple exclusion pro-
cess.
