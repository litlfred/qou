If $G$ is an affine group scheme over a field $k$, classical Tannaka's reconstruction problem consists in reconstructing the group $G$ from $\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Rep}}}^{G}k$, its category of finite representations: if $F\colon\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Rep}}}^{G}k\arr\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Vect}}}k$ is the forgetful functor then $G$ is canonically isomorphic to the sheaf of automorphisms of $F$ (opportunely defined, see [@Deligne1982 Proposition 2.8]). More generally one can recover the stack $\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{B}}}G$ of $G$-torsors by looking at fiber functors. Given a ring $A$ denote by $\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Loc}}}A$ the category of locally free sheaves of finite rank over $A$, that is finitely generated projective $A$-modules. If $\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{SMon}}}_{k}^{G}$ is the stack over $k$ whose fiber over a $k$-algebra $A$ is the category of $k$-linear, strong monoidal and exact functors $\Gamma\colon\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Rep}}}^{G}k\arr\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Loc}}}A$ then the functor $$\begin{aligned}
\label{tannaka functor}
\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{B}}}G\arr\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{SMon}}}_{k}^{G}\comma(\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Spec}}}A\arrdi s\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{B}}}G)\longmapsto(s_{|\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Rep}}}^{G}k}^{*}\colon\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Rep}}}^{G}k\arr\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Loc}}}A) \tag{$\star$}
\end{aligned}$$is an equivalence of categories (see [@Deligne1982 Theorem 3.2]). Here $\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Rep}}}^{G}k$ is thought of as the category of locally free sheaves of finite rank on $\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{B}}}G$.

The above functor can be defined in a way more general context. Let us introduce some notations and definitions. We fix a base commutative ring $R$ and a category fibered in groupoids $\stX$ over $R$. We say that $\stX$ is *pseudo-algebraic* (resp. *quasi-compact*) if there exists a scheme (resp. affine scheme) $X$ and a map $X\arr\stX$ representable by fpqc covering of algebraic spaces. We denote by $\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{QCoh}}}\stX$ and $\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Loc}}}\stX$ the categories of quasi-coherent sheaves and locally free sheaves of finite rank respectively (see Section [\[sec: Preliminaries on sheaves\]](#sec: Preliminaries on sheaves){reference-type="ref" reference="sec: Preliminaries on sheaves"}). Given a full subcategory $\shC$ of $\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{QCoh}}}\stX$ we say that $\shC$ generates $\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{QCoh}}}\stX$ if all quasi-coherent sheaves on $\stX$ are quotient of a (possibly infinite) direct sum of sheaves in $\shC$. We say that $\stX$ satisfies (or has) the *resolution property* if $\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Loc}}}\stX$ generates $\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{QCoh}}}\stX$.

Given a monoidal and additive full subcategory $\shC$ of $\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Loc}}}\stX$ and a category fibered in groupoids $\stY$ over $R$ define $$$\text{Fib}_{\stX,\shC}(\stY)$ as the category of $R$-linear and strong monoidal functors $\Gamma\colon\shC\arr\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Loc}}}\stY$ which are exact on right exact sequences in $\shC$ (in the ambient abelian category $\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{QCoh}}}\stX$). Denote also by $\text{Fib}_{\stX,\shC}$ the stack over $R$ whose fiber over an $R$-algebra $A$ is $\text{Fib}_{\stX,\shC}(\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Spec}}}A)$ and by $\shP_{\shC}$ the functor $$\shP_{\shC}\colon\stX\arr\text{Fib}_{\stX,\shC}\comma(\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Spec}}}A\arrdi s\stX)\longmapsto(s_{|\shC}^{*}\colon\shC\arr\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Loc}}}A)$$ The functor ([\[tannaka functor\]](#tannaka functor){reference-type="ref" reference="tannaka functor"}) is obtained by taking $R=k$, $\stX=\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{B}}}G$ and $\shC=\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Loc}}}\stX$. We prove the following non-neutral form of Tannaka's reconstruction.

::: thm*
Let $\stX$ be a quasi-compact stack over $R$ for the fpqc topology with quasi-affine diagonal and $\shC\subseteq\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Loc}}}\stX$ be a full, additive and monoidal subcategory with duals generating $\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{QCoh}}}\stX$.

If $\Gamma\colon\shC\arr\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Mod}}}A,$ where $A$ is an $R$-algebra, is an $R$-linear, contravariant and strong monoidal functor such that $\Gamma$, as well as $\Gamma\otimes_{A}k$ for all geometric points $\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Spec}}}k\arr\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Spec}}}A$, is left exact on right exact sequences in $\shC$ then there exists $\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Spec}}}A\arrdi s\stX$ such that $\Gamma\simeq(s^{*})^{\vee}$.

The functor $\shP_{\shC}\colon\stX\arr\text{Fib}_{\stX,\shC}$ is an equivalence of stacks and, if $\stY$ is a category fibered in groupoids, the functor $$\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Hom}}}(\stY,\stX)\arr\text{Fib}_{\stX,\shC}(\stY)\comma(\stY\arrdi f\stX)\longmapsto f_{|\shC}^{*}\colon\shC\arr\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Loc}}}(\stY)$$ is an equivalence of categories.
:::

Notice that the two conclusions in the last statement are equivalent. In the case $\shC=\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Loc}}}\stX$, the functor $\shP_{\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Loc}}}(\stX)}$ has already been proved to be an equivalence in the neutral case, that is $\stX=\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{B}}}_{R}G$, where $G$ is a flat and affine group scheme over $R$ (see [@Broshi2013 Theorem 1.2], where $R$ is a Dedekind domain, and [@Schappi2011 Theorem 1.3.2] for general rings $R$), for particular quotient stacks over a field (see [@Savin2006] and [\[rem: cases for resolution property\]](#rem: cases for resolution property){reference-type="ref" reference="rem: cases for resolution property"}) and for quasi-compact and quasi-separated schemes (see [@Brandenburg2012 Proposition 1.8]). We also show an almost converse of Theorem above:

::: thm*
\[[\[thm:when FibX,C has the resolution property\]](#thm:when FibX,C has the resolution property){reference-type="ref" reference="thm:when FibX,C has the resolution property"}\] Let $\stX$ be a quasi-compact category fibered in groupoids over $R$ admitting a surjective (on equivalence classes of geometric points) map $X\arr\stX$ from a scheme whose connected components are open (e.g. a connected or Noetherian algebraic stack) and let $\shC\subseteq\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Loc}}}\stX$ be a full monoidal subcategory with duals such that $\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Sym}}}^{n}\E\in\shC$ for $n\in\N$ and $\E\in\shC$ if $\E$ has local rank not invertible in $R$ (e.g. $\shC=\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Loc}}}(\stX)$ or $\shC$ consists of invertible sheaves). Then $\text{Fib}_{\stX,\shC}$ is a quasi-compact stack in groupoids for the fpqc topology over $R$ with affine diagonal and with a collection of tautological locally free sheaves $\{\shG_{\E}\}_{\E\in\shC}$ generating $\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{QCoh}}}(\text{Fib}_{\stX,\shC})$ and such that $\shP_{\shC}^{*}\shG_{\E}\simeq\E$.
:::

For instance it follows that $\stX\arr\text{Fib}_{\stX,\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Loc}}}(\stX)}$ is universal among the maps $w\colon\stX\arr\stY$ where $\stY$ is a quasi-compact stack over $R$ with quasi-affine diagonal and the resolution property.

There are also variants of theory above where $\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Loc}}}(-)$ is replaced by $\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Coh}}}(-)$ or $\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{QCoh}}}(-)$ or the derived category $D(-)$ (see [@Lurie2004; @Schappi2012; @Brandenburg2012; @Brandenburg2014; @Bhatt2014]). Although not explicitly stated elsewhere, for stacks with the resolution property and with affine diagonal (which is automatic in the algebraic case, see [@Totaro2002]) those results and the fact that $\shP_{\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Loc}}}(\stX)}$ is an equivalence can be proved to be equivalent: one can pass from quasi-coherent sheaves to locally free sheaves via dualizable objects and, for the converse, extend functors from $\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Loc}}}(-)$ to $\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{QCoh}}}(-)$ following the proof of [@Bhatt2014 Corollary 3.2]. We complete this picture by showing that in general the resolution property implies the affineness of the diagonal (see [\[cor:quasi-affine and resolution implies affine\]](#cor:quasi-affine and resolution implies affine){reference-type="ref" reference="cor:quasi-affine and resolution implies affine"}). One of the ingredients in the proof is the classification of quasi-compact stacks whose quasi-coherent sheaves are generated by global sections, called pseudo-affine. In the algebraic case those coincide with quasi-affine schemes (see [@Gross2013 Proposition 3.1]), while, in general, we prove they are (arbitrary) intersection of quasi-compact open subschemes (thought of as sheaves) of affine schemes (see [\[prop:arbitrary intersection of quasi-compact open is pseudo-affine\]](#prop:arbitrary intersection of quasi-compact open is pseudo-affine){reference-type="ref" reference="prop:arbitrary intersection of quasi-compact open is pseudo-affine"}).

The proof of Tannaka's reconstruction we present does not reduce to the case of quasi-coherent sheaves as explained above but it follows a different path. It is obtained by developing a theory of sheafification functors which I think is interesting on its own and it is the heart of the paper. In what follows $A$ will denote an $R$-algebra, $\stX$ a category fibered in groupoids over $R$ and $\shC$ a full subcategory of $\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{QCoh}}}\stX$. The idea is simple: if $s\colon\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Spec}}}A\arr\stX$ is a map (say quasi-affine so that $s_{*}$ preserves quasi-coherency) then we have natural isomorphisms of $A$-modules $$s^{*}\E\simeq\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Hom}}}_{A}(s^{*}\duale{\E},A)\simeq\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Hom}}}_{\stX}(\duale{\E},s_{*}\odi{\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Spec}}}A})$$ By passing from covariant functors to *contravariant* ones we can always define $$\Omega^{\shF}\colon\shC\arr\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Mod}}}A\comma\Omega_{\E}^{\shF}=\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Hom}}}_{\stX}(\E,\shF)\text{ for }\shF\in\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{QCoh}}}(\stX\times A)$$ which are $R$-linear contravariant functors. If $\L_{R}(\shC,A)$ is the category of contravariant $R$-linear functors $\shC\arr\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Mod}}}A$ we obtain a functor $$\Omega^{*}\colon\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{QCoh}}}(\stX\times A)\arr\L_{R}(\shC,A)$$ Quite surprisingly (since we started from group schemes as motivation) we recover also this well known situation (see [\[example: quasi-projective case\]](#example: quasi-projective case){reference-type="ref" reference="example: quasi-projective case"} for details). If $X$ is a quasi-projective and quasi-compact scheme over $R$ with very ample invertible sheaf $\odi X(1)$ consider $\shC_{X}=\{\odi X(n)\}_{n\in\Z}$, set $S_{X}$ for the homogeneous coordinate ring of $(X,\odi X(1))$ and use $\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{GMod}}}(-)$ to denote the category of graded modules. We have that $\L_{R}(\shC_{X},A)$ is equivalent to $\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{GMod}}}(S_{X}\otimes_{R}A)$ and $\Omega^{*}$ corresponds to $$\Gamma_{*}\colon\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{QCoh}}}(X\times A)\arr\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{GMod}}}(S_{X}\otimes_{R}A)\comma\Gamma_{*}(\shF)=\bigoplus_{n\in\Z}\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{H}}}^{0}(X,\shF(n))$$ It is a classical result, at least over a field, that the above functor is fully faithful. Thus in general we can ask under what conditions the functor $\Omega^{*}\colon\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{QCoh}}}(\stX\times A)\arr\L_{R}(\shC,A)$ is fully faithful as well. Another property that the functor $\Gamma_{*}$ has is the existence of a left adjoint, namely the homogeneous sheafification functor $\fasc -\colon\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{GMod}}}(S_{X}\otimes_{R}A)\arr\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{QCoh}}}(X\times A)$. It turns out that also $\Omega^{*}\colon\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{QCoh}}}(\stX\times A)\arr\L_{R}(\shC,A)$ has a left adjoint $\shF_{*,\shC}\colon\L_{R}(\shC,A)\arr\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{QCoh}}}(\stX\times A)$ when $\shC$ is essentially small: by analogy we call this functor a *sheafification* functor.

In the context of Tannaka's reconstruction we also have that sheaves of algebras correspond to (lax) monoidal functors $\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Loc}}}^{G}R\arr\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Mod}}}A$. This is true in general. If $\shC$ is a monoidal subcategory of $\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{QCoh}}}\stX$, $\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{ML}}}_{R}(\shC,A)$ denotes the category of $R$-linear, contravariant and monoidal functors $\shC\arr\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Mod}}}A$ and $\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{QAlg}}}(\stX\times A)$ the category of quasi-coherent sheaves of algebras on $\stX$ then $\Omega^{*}$ extends to a functor $\Omega^{*}\colon\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{QAlg}}}(\stX\times A)\arr\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{ML}}}_{R}(\shC,A)$ and, if $\shC$ is essentially small, $\shF_{*,\shC}$ extends to $\alA_{*,\shC}\colon\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{ML}}}_{R}(\shC,A)\arr\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{QAlg}}}(\stX\times A)$, which is still a left adjoint of $\Omega^{*}$.

Coming back to the fully faithfulness of $\Omega^{*}$ we prove the following.

::: thm*
Let $\stX$ be a pseudo-algebraic category fibered in groupoids over $R$ and $\shC\subseteq\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{QCoh}}}\stX$ be a full subcategory generating $\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{QCoh}}}\stX$. Then the functor $\Omega^{*}\colon\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{QCoh}}}(\stX\times A)\arr\L_{R}(\shC,A)$ is fully faithful and, if $\shC$ is essentially small, then $\shF_{*,\shC}\colon\L_{R}(\shC,A)\arr\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{QCoh}}}(\stX\times A)$ is exact and the natural map $\shG\arr\shF_{\Omega^{\shG},\shC}$ is an isomorphism.
:::

If $X$ is quasi-projective then it is a classical fact that $\shC_{X}=\{\odi X(n)\}_{n\in\Z}$ generates $\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{QCoh}}}X$ and thus we recover the classical properties of $\Gamma_{*}\colon\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{QCoh}}}(X\times A)\arr\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{GMod}}}(S_{X}\otimes_{R}A)$. Theorem above when $\shC$ consists of a single object is a rephrasing of classical Gabriel-Popescu's theorem for the category $\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{QCoh}}}\stX$ (see [\[thm: Gabriel Popescu\'s theorem\]](#thm: Gabriel Popescu's theorem){reference-type="ref" reference="thm: Gabriel Popescu's theorem"}). When $\shC$ is monoidal and generates $\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{QCoh}}}\stX$ we also have that $\Omega^{*}\colon\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{QAlg}}}(\stX\times A)\arr\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{ML}}}_{R}(\shC,A)$ is fully faithful (see [\[thm:fundamental for sheaf of algebras and monoidal functors\]](#thm:fundamental for sheaf of algebras and monoidal functors){reference-type="ref" reference="thm:fundamental for sheaf of algebras and monoidal functors"}).

The second problem we address is to describe the essential image of $\Omega^{*}$. The main idea behind this description is that $\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Hom}}}_{\stX}(-,\shF)$ for $\shF\in\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{QCoh}}}\stX$ is a left exact functor. Since the domain $\shC$ of the functors $\Omega^{\shF}$ is not abelian we need an ad hoc definition of exactness. A *test sequence* in $\shC$ is an exact sequence $$\shT_{*}\colon\bigoplus_{k\in K}\E_{k}\arrdi{\alpha}\bigoplus_{i\in I}\E_{i}\arr\E\arr0\text{ where }\E,\E_{i},\E_{k}\in\shC\text{ and }I,J\text{ are sets}$$ such that $\alpha(\E_{k})$ is contained in a finite sum for all $k\in K$. A test sequence is called finite if $K$ and $I$ are finite sets. Given $\Gamma\in\L_{R}(\shC,A)$ we will say that $\Gamma$ is exact on a test sequence $\shT_{*}$ in $\shC$ if the complex of $A$-modules (see [\[def: left exact functors\]](#def: left exact functors){reference-type="ref" reference="def: left exact functors"}) $$0\arr\Gamma_{\E}\arr\prod_{i\in I}\Gamma_{\E_{i}}\arr\prod_{k\in K}\Gamma_{\E_{k}}$$ is exact. We denote by $\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Lex}}}_{R}(\shC,A)$ the full subcategory of $\L_{R}(\shC,A)$ of functors which are exact an all test sequences. We have the following:

::: thm*
Let $\stX$ be a pseudo-algebraic category fibered in groupoids over $R$ and $\shC\subseteq\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{QCoh}}}\stX$ be a full subcategory generating $\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{QCoh}}}\stX$. Then $\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Lex}}}_{R}(\shC,A)$ is the essential image of the (fully faithful) functor $\Omega^{*}\colon\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{QCoh}}}(\stX\times A)\arr\L_{R}(\shC,A)$. If $\stX$ is quasi-compact and all sheaves in $\shC$ are finitely presented then $\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Lex}}}_{R}(\shC,A)$ is the subcategory of $\L_{R}(\shC,A)$ of functors which are exact on finite test sequences.
:::

In particular, when $\shC$ is essentially small, $\Omega^{*}\colon\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{QCoh}}}(\stX\times A)\arr\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Lex}}}_{R}(\shC,A)$ and $\shF_{*,\shC}\colon\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Lex}}}_{R}(\shC,A)\arr\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{QCoh}}}(\stX\times A)$ are quasi-inverses of each other. The two theorems above apply in the following situations (see [\[thm:equivalence for QCoh X\]](#thm:equivalence for QCoh X){reference-type="ref" reference="thm:equivalence for QCoh X"}, [\[thm:equivalence for noetherian algebraic stacks\]](#thm:equivalence for noetherian algebraic stacks){reference-type="ref" reference="thm:equivalence for noetherian algebraic stacks"} and [\[thm:equivalence for Loc X\]](#thm:equivalence for Loc X){reference-type="ref" reference="thm:equivalence for Loc X"}): if $\shC=\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{QCoh}}}\stX$ then $\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Lex}}}_{R}(\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{QCoh}}}\stX,A)$ is the category of contraviant, $R$-linear and left exact functors $\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{QCoh}}}\stX\arr\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Mod}}}A$ which transform direct sums into products, if $\shC=\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Coh}}}(\stX)$ (resp. $\shC=\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Loc}}}(\stX)$) and $\stX$ is a notherian algebraic stack (resp. $\stX$ is quasi-compact and has the resolution property) then $\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Lex}}}_{R}(\shC,A)$ is the category of contraviant, $R$-linear and left exact functors $\shC\arr\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Mod}}}A$.

When $\shC$ is essentially small there is another cohomological characterization of the functors in $\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Lex}}}_{R}(\shC,A)$. A collection of maps $\shU=\{\E_{i}\arr\E\}$ in $\shC$ is called jointly surjective if the map $\bigoplus_{i\in I}\E_{i}\arr\E$ is surjective. Given such a collection $\shU$ we set $\Delta_{\shU}=\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Im}}}(\bigoplus_{i\in I}\Omega^{\E_{i}}\arr\Omega^{\E})\in\L_{R}(\shC,R)$. Denote by $\shC^{\oplus}$ the subcategory of $\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{QCoh}}}\stX$ consisting of all possible finite direct sums of sheaves in $\shC$. We have:

::: thm*
Let $\stX$ be a pseudo-algebraic category fibered in groupoids over $R$ and $\shC\subseteq\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{QCoh}}}\stX$ be a full and essentially small subcategory generating $\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{QCoh}}}\stX$. Then $\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Lex}}}_{R}(\shC,A)$ is the full subcategory of $\L_{R}(\shC,A)$ of functors $\Gamma$ satisfying $$\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Hom}}}_{\L_{R}(\shC,R)}(\Omega^{\E}/\Delta_{\shU},\Gamma)=\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Ext}}}_{\L_{R}(\shC,R)}^{1}(\Omega^{\E}/\Delta_{\shU},\Gamma)=0$$ for all jointly surjective collections of maps $\shU=\{\E_{i}\arr\E\}_{i\in I}$ in $\shC$. If $\stX$ is quasi-compact and the sheaves in $\shC$ are finitely presented we can consider only finite collections $\shU$.

We have $\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Lex}}}_{R}(\shC^{\oplus},A)\simeq\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Lex}}}_{R}(\shC,A)$ via the restriction $\shC\arr\shC^{\oplus}$ and, if $\shC$ is additive and $\shJ$ is the smallest Grothendieck topology on $\shC$ containing the sieves $\Delta_{\shU}$ for all jointly surjective collections $\shU=\{\E_{i}\arr\E\}_{i\in I}$ in $\shC$, then $\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Lex}}}_{R}(\shC,A)$ coincides with the category of sheaves of $A$-modules $\shC^{\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{op}}}}\arr\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Mod}}}A$ on the site $(\shC,\shJ)$ which are $R$-linear.
:::

Besides Tannaka's reconstruction problem, theory above has two other applications. The first, which is also the original motivation, is the theory of Galois cover. More precisely in my Ph.D. thesis [@Tonini2013] I have worked out theory above in the case $\stX=\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{B}}}G$ and $\shC=\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Loc}}}\stX$, where $G$ is a finite, flat and finitely presented group scheme over $R$. Notice that $\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{B}}}G$ satisfies the resolution property in this case (see [\[rem: resolution property for BG cases\]](#rem: resolution property for BG cases){reference-type="ref" reference="rem: resolution property for BG cases"}). The proof presented in [@Tonini2013] makes use of representation theory and can not be generalized to arbitrary categories fibered in groupoids. The goal was to look at Galois covers with group $G$ as particular monoidal functors, as $G$-torsors can be thought of as particular strong monoidal functors, and the motivation was the study of non-abelian Galois covers, where a direct approach as in the abelian case (see [@Tonini2011]) fails due to the complexity of the representation theory.

A second application is to the theory of Cox rings and homogeneous sheafifications. The idea is to consider $\shC_{H}=\{\shL\}_{\shL\in H}\subseteq\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Loc}}}\stX$ where $H$ is a subgroup of $\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{Pic}}}\stX$. As in the projective case we have a homogeneous coordinate ring $$S_{H}=\bigoplus_{\shL\in H}\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{H}}}^{0}(\stX,\shL)$$ (opportunely defined), $\L_{R}(\shC_{H},A)$ is equivalent to $\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{GMod}}}(S_{H}\otimes_{R}A)$, the category of $H$-graded $(S_{H}\otimes_{R}A)$-modules, $\Omega^{*}$ corresponds to $$\Gamma_{*}\colon\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{QCoh}}}(\stX\times A)\arr\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{GMod}}}(S_{H}\otimes_{R}A)\comma\Gamma_{*}(\shF)=\bigoplus_{\shL\in H}\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{H}}}^{0}(\stX,\shF\otimes\shL)$$ and its adjoint $\shF_{*,\shC_{H}}$ behaves like a homogeneous sheafification. Moreover in more concrete geometric situations, e.g. when $\stX$ is a normal variety, there are analogous constructions for reflexive sheaves of rank $1$. We expect that this theory covers all known cases where $\Gamma_{*}$ is proved to be fully faithful (see for instance [@Cox2011 Appendix of Chapter 6] and [@Kajiwara1998 Section 2]).

Applications above are not described in the present paper, but, hopefully, they will be subjects of future ones.

The outline of the paper is the following. The first section introduces the notion and the basic properties of quasi-coherent sheaves on fibered categories, while the second one is a general study of sheafification functors. In the third section we study the fully faithfulness and the essential image of the functor $\Omega^{*}\colon\mathop{\mathrm{\textup{QCoh}}}(\stX\times A)\arr\L_{R}(\shC,A)$. In the fourth section we rewrite the results obtained in the case of the stack of $G$-torsor in terms of the representation theory of $G$ and finally, in the last section, we prove the non-neutral Tannaka's reconstruction.
