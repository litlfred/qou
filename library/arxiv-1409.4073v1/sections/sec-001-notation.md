In this paper we work over a base commutative, associative ri ng R with unity. If not stated
otherwise a ﬁber category will be a category ﬁbered in groupo ids over Aﬀ /R, the category of
aﬃne schemes over Spec R, or, equivalently, the opposite of the category of R-algebras. A scheme
or an algebraic space X over SpecR will be thought of as the ﬁbered category of maps from an
aﬃne scheme to X, denoted by Aﬀ /X. A map or morphism of ﬁbered categories is a functor
over Aﬀ /R. Recall that by the 2-Yoneda lemma objects of a ﬁbered category X can be thought
of as maps T − → X from an aﬃne scheme. An fpqc stack will be a stack for the fpqc t opology.
A map f : X ′ − → X of ﬁbered categories is called representable if for all maps T − → X from
an aﬃne scheme (or an algebraic space) the ﬁber product T ×X X ′ is (equivalent to) an algebraic
space.
Given a ﬂat and aﬃne group scheme G overT we denote by B RG the stack of G-torsors for
the fpqc topology, which is an fpqc stack with aﬃne diagonal. When G − →SpecR is ﬁnitely
presented (resp. smooth) then B RG coincides with the stack of G-torsors for the fppf (resp.
étale) topology.
