We have A(t)B(1/t)T = B(t)A(1/t)T. (17) In other words, A(t)B(1/t)T is symmetric under tranposition followed by t →1/t. In particular, A(ω)B(ω)∗is Hermitian for ω ∈C with |ω| = 1 and so is B(ω)−1A(ω) if B(ω) is non-singular. Here X∗is the complex conjugate of the transpose of a matrix X.
