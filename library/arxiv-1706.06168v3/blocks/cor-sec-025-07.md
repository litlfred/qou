A.2 Decomposition of V (λ) ⊗V (µ) Fix λ, µ ∈Nm 0 , and let V (λ) and V (µ) denote the irreducible representations of (SL2(C))m given by the outer tensor products: V (λ) ∼= V (λ1) ⊠· · · ⊠V (λm) V (µ) ∼= V (µ1) ⊠· · · ⊠V (µm) We next generalize the above results to the inner tensor product of these two representations, V (λ) ⊗V (µ). In particular, we determine the decomposition of this tensor product and deﬁne a multivariate apolarity form. Note that these statements strictly generalize the previous analogous statements.
