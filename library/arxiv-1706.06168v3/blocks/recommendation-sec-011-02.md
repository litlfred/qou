This complex linear combination is given as follows, where $s_q$ and $s_p$ denote the respective sums of the roots of $q$ and $p$: $$\partial_x^{n-1} \big(q(x,y) + ip(x,y)\big) = (n! \cdot x - (n-1)! \cdot s_q y) + i(n! \cdot x - (n-1)! \cdot s_p y) = n!(1+i)\left(x - \frac{s_q + i s_p}{n(1 + i)} y\right)$$ Since this polynomial is $\mathcal{H}_+$-stable, it must be that $\frac{s_q + i s_p}{n(1 + i)} \in \overline{\mathcal{H}_-}$.
