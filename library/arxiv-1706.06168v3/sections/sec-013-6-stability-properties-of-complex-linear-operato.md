operators, where Ω is some product of open circular regions. What they found is that an operator preserves
weak Ω-stability if a particular associated polynomial (what they calle d the symbol) is Ω-stable. However,
the “only if” direction does not necessarily hold. In particular, ther e are some weak Ω-stability preserving
operators for which the corresponding symbol is not Ω-stable. Th ey then showed that this could only happen
under very speciﬁc circumstances: the operator must have image of dimension at most one.
