In this context, we will mean by the Hecke algebra Hn(q, Q) of type Bn the free complex
algebra with 1 and generators t, g1, g2, . . . , gn−1 and parameters q, Q ∈ C with deﬁning
relations
(H1) gigi+1gi = gi+1gigi+1 for i = 1, 2, . . . , n − 2;
(H2) gigj = gjgi, whenever | i − j |≥ 2;
(H3) g2
i = (q − 1)gi + q for i = 1, 2, . . . , n − 1;
(H4) t2 = (Q − 1)t + Q;
(H5) tg1tg1 = g1tg1t;
