We compute the weights, i.e. the values at the minimal idempo tents, for the Markov
trace on the Hecke algebra of type Bn and type Dn. In order to prove the weight
formula, we deﬁne representations of the Hecke algebra of ty pe B onto a reduced
Hecke Algebra of type A. To compute the weights for type D we use the inclusion of
the Hecke algebra of type D into the Hecke algebra of type B.
