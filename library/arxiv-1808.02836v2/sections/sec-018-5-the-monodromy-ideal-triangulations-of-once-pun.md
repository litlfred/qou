[]{#sec:monodromy ideal triangulations label="sec:monodromy ideal triangulations"}

In this section we apply Theorem [\[thm:sumofnorms\]](#thm:sumofnorms){reference-type="ref" reference="thm:sumofnorms"} to a nice class of examples known as the (canonical) monodromy ideal triangulations of once-punctured torus bundles over the circle. In particular, we show that these are minimal. In the last subsection we describe an infinite family of triangulations of semi-bundles that we conjecture to be minimal.
