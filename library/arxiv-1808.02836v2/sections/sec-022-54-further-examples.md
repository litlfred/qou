Further examples
The orientable cusped hyperbolic census up to nine tetrahedra, described and veriﬁed in [5] and partially
shipped with Regina [6], contains 162182 minimal triangulations of 61911 cusped hyperbolic 3-manifolds.
There are exactly 26 triangulations for which the lower bound in Theorem 1 is attained. Of these, 22 are
triangulations of once-punctured torus bundles, but the remaining four are not. Their isomorphism signatures
are
32
• gLLMQbeefffehhqxhqq of manifold s781,
• iLLLQPcbefgffhhhxxhaqxxqh of manifold t05624,
• iLLLQPcbefgffhhhhhqaxhhxq of manifold t06056,
• iLLwQPcbeefgehhhhhqhhqhqx of manifold t12546.
These examples, as well as inﬁnite families of minimal triangulations they are contained in, will be described
in detail in a separate note.
