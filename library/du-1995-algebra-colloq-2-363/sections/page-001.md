A NOTE ON QUANTIZED WEYL
RECIPROCITY AT ROOTS OF UNITY
Jie Du
School of Mathematics
University of New South Wales
Sydney, 2052, Australia
In this note we show that the epimorphism over Q(v) from the quantized enveloping
algebra U to a q-Schur algebra can be taken up to any ﬁeld via the quantized Kostant
Z[v, v−1]-form of U.
0. Introduction
Let n, r be integers ⩾1 and let V be a vector space of dimension n over C.
Classically, we have natural commuting actions of the general linear group GL(V)
and the symmetric group Sr on the tensor space V⊗r, and both actions satisfy
the so-called Weyl reciprocity [W] (or the double centralizer property), which has
been used by Schur and Weyl to link representations of Sr with representations
of GL(V). This reciprocity is also true over any ﬁeld by taking them up over Z
in [CL]. That is, if AZ is the Kostant Z-form of the universal enveloping algebra
A = A(gln(Q)) and V is Z-free and of rank n, then the natural homomorphisms
(0.a)
AZ →EndZ[Sr](V ⊗r),
Z[Sr] →EndAZ(V ⊗r)
are surjective (see [CL, Theorem 3.1] where n ≥r for the second surjection and [CP,
4.1] or [D,§2 Corollary] in general). The algebra EndZ[Sr](V ⊗r) and its extensions
to ﬁelds are usually called Schur algebras.
