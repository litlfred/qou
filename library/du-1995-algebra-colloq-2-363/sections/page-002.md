is also true when the parameter q is a root of 1. In this note, we will prove that
the quantum version over any ﬁeld F of the ﬁrst epimorphism in (0.a) is true for
any q̸ = 0 in F (Theorem 3.6). To this end, it suﬃces to take them up over A as is
in (0.a) and prove the homomorphism (1.2) from U to a q-Schur algebra over Q(v)
sends the quantized Kostant A-form U of U onto the q-Schur algebra over A.
We shall use the explicit construction ([BLM]) of a surjective map αr from U to
the q-Schur algebra Sn,r over the ﬁeld Q(v). This will be described in section 1. In
section 2, the quantized Kostant A-form U of U is deﬁned, based on the integral
quantum group ′U of type A deﬁned in [L1]. We shall see that U contains ′U and
is free over ′U. The main result of this note will be proved in section 3.
We remark that the quantum integral version of the second epimorphism in (0.a)
follows from our result and the result in [DJ,6.6] for n ≥r. It is very likely that
the result holds in general.
