If a local representation ρ is isomorphic to its pull-back φ∗ρ under φ (an instance is canonically associated to the hyperbolic holonomy of M [7, 8]), then the trace of a suitably normalized QT intertwiner which relates ρλ to φ∗ρλ′, λ′ := φ(λ), should be an invariant of M (possibly depending on φ) and the isomorphism class of ρ. • The QT intertwiners can be constructed by composing elementary intertwiners, associated to the diagonal exchanges in a sequence relating two ideal triangulations of S, and these were identiﬁed with the Kashaev 6j-symbols in [2]. • The QHI of M can be deﬁned by QH state sums over “layered” triangulations of M, that is, roughly, 3-dimensional ideal triangulations that realize sequences of diagonal exchanges relating the surface ideal triangulations λ and φ(λ), in such a way that every diagonal exchange is associated to a tetrahedron of the triangulation.
