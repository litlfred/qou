The equivalence $U$ between $\star$ and $\sharp=U(\star)$ must therefore be a convolution operator by a (formal) distribution $u\in\cd'(\SS)$ on $\SS$ i.e. of the form: \begin{equation}\label{conv} (U f)(x) = \int_{\SS\,} u(y^{-1}x)\,f(y) \,{\rm d}^Ly\;, \end{equation} where ${\rm d}^Ly$ denotes a left-invariant Haar measure on $\SS$ (remark that it coincides with the Liouville area form on $\SS=\DD=\MM$).
