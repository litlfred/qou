Recall that according to general ideas of extended field theory (see \ocite{lurie}), an extended 3d TQFT should assign to a closed 1-manifold a 2-vector space, or an abelian category, and to a 2-cobordism between two 1-manifolds, a functor between corresponding categories (which in the special case of cobordism between two empty 1-manifolds gives a functor $\Vect\to \Vect$, i.e. a vector space).
