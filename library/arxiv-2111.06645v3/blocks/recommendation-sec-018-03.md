Finally, we should mention that equation [\[Orthogonality1\]](#Orthogonality1){reference-type="eqref" reference="Orthogonality1"} also gives us a formula for the inverse of the full matrix $\mathbf{J}(x)$, because of the block triangular form of the latter as given in [\[Jblock\]](#Jblock){reference-type="eqref" reference="Jblock"}, namely $$\mathbf{J}(x)^{-1}= \begin{pmatrix} 1 & -Q(x)B^{\text{red}}\mathbf{J}^{\text{red}}(-x)^t \\ 0 & B^{\text{red}}\mathbf{J}^{\text{red}}(-x)^t \end{pmatrix} ,$$ in which the elements of the top row are bilinear in the entries of $\mathbf{J}(x)$ and $\mathbf{J}(-x)$ rather than merely linear as is the case for the other rows.
