To find how $S(\theta_1,\theta_2;\zeta)$ transform under the Galois group, we compute $$\begin{aligned} \frac{a_{r,s}(\zeta \theta_1,\theta_2;\zeta)}{a_{r+1,s}(\theta_1,\theta_2;\zeta)} &= \theta_2 (1-\zeta \theta_1)^{-2} = a_{1,0}(\theta_1,\theta_2;\zeta)^{-1},\\ \frac{a_{r,s}(\theta_1,\zeta \theta_2;\zeta)}{a_{r,s+1}(\theta_1,\theta_2;\zeta)} &= \zeta \theta_1\big(1-\zeta^{-1}\theta_2^{-1}\big) = a_{0,1}(\theta_1,\theta_2;\zeta)^{-1} \end{aligned}$$ (where the left-hand side of the above equations is independent of $r$ and $s$ hence it must equal to the right-hand side).
