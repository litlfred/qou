In this section, we will show how one can go beyond the original QMC as described in Section [\[sec.QMCK\]](#sec.QMCK){reference-type="ref" reference="sec.QMCK"} or its generalization as described in Section [\[sub.GQMC\]](#sub.GQMC){reference-type="ref" reference="sub.GQMC"}. We will present this via a series of successive refinements, each one found experimentally and building on its predecessors. This will culminate in the complete, though of course still conjectural, definition (in Sections [\[sub.4.1\]](#sub.4.1){reference-type="ref" reference="sub.4.1"}--[\[sub.4.4\]](#sub.4.4){reference-type="ref" reference="sub.4.4"}) of the matrices $\mathbf{J}$ and $\mathbf\Phi$ discussed in the introduction and of the final refinement (in Section [\[sub.4.5\]](#sub.4.5){reference-type="ref" reference="sub.4.5"}) of the original quantum modularity conjecture.
