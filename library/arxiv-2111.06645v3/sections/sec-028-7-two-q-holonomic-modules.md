[]{#sec.NewHolonomy label="sec.NewHolonomy"}

In Part I we were led by the refined quantum modularity conjecture to find an entire matrix $\mathbf{J}^{(K)}$ of Habiro-like functions generalizing the Kashaev invariant, the first column being the vector of formal power series found in [@DG; @DG2]. In this section, we will study the structure of the other columns of this matrix and will see that they have a natural "$q$-holonomic structure" in terms of an infinite collection of functions that satisfy a recursion of finite length and hence all lie in a finite-dimensional module. In Section [\[sub.qholo\]](#sub.qholo){reference-type="ref" reference="sub.qholo"}, we explain this in detail for the case of the $4_1$ knot, where explicit formulas for the entries of $\mathbf{J}^{(K)}$ were already given in Part I. In the next subsection, we give the corresponding formulas for the $5_2$ knot, where the matrix in question has size $4\times4$ instead of $3\times3$. These are considerably more complicated than for the $4_1$ knot and have the interesting new feature that Dedekind sums appear. In Section [\[sub.statesum\]](#sub.statesum){reference-type="ref" reference="sub.statesum"}, we explain how these formulas could be guessed. This ansatz involves studying the $q$-hypergeometric series defining the original Kashaev invariant via stationary points and formal Gaussian summation, analogous to what was done in Section [\[sub.PhiPerturbative\]](#sub.PhiPerturbative){reference-type="ref" reference="sub.PhiPerturbative"} for Nahm sums and what will be done in Section [\[sec.QMC41\]](#sec.QMC41){reference-type="ref" reference="sec.QMC41"} for the Kashaev invariant of the $4_1$ knot. In the final subsection, we discuss the point already alluded to at the end of Section [\[sec.perturbation\]](#sec.perturbation){reference-type="ref" reference="sec.perturbation"} that the power series in $h$ studied there have a $q$-holonomic structure with the *same* coefficients as the one associated to the matrix $\mathbf{J}$. This is for the momenta purely experimental and is one of the many mysteries associated with the subject. In Section [\[sub.qholonomicPhi\]](#sub.qholonomicPhi){reference-type="ref" reference="sub.qholonomicPhi"}, we also briefly mention two further conjectural objects associated to knots (or more generally to half-symplectic matrices) that we believe share the same $q$-holonomic structure.
