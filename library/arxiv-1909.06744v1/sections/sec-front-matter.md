Springer correspondence for symmetric spaces
Toshiaki Shoji
Abstract. This is a survey on the Springer correspondence for symmetric spaces.
We discuss various generalization of the theory of the Springer correspondence for
reductive groups to symmetric spaces and exotic symmetric spaces associated to
classical groups.
