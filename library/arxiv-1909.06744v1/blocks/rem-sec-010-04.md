By comparing Proposition 12.2 and Propositions 12.3, 12.4, we ﬁnd very interesting phenomena. In the group case, ZGθ(x) involves the subgroups of type Sp and SO. Hence in the study of unipotent classes in H, the case of Sp and of SO cannot be separated. While in the case of symmetric spaces, the structure of ZGθ(x) is completely separated, namely in the case of H = SpN, ZGθ(x) involves only subgroups of type Sp, and similarly for H = SON. All the properties of ZGθ(x) in the case of symmetric spaces are inherited from similar properties in the group case, but they produce two extreme phenomena to the opposite directions.
