The composition Lθ κBκ is called the Borel-Laplace sum with shift κ in direc- tion θ, which we will denote by Sθ κ. If the Borel-Laplace sum converges for a formal power series φ, we say that φ is 1-summable in direction θ. The Borel and Laplace transforms are formally inverses on monomials zn with ℜ(n+κ) > 0, and if φ is a series which converges in a neighborhood of 0, then φ = Lθ κBκ(φ) for all θ and for all κ such that ℜ(κ) > 0. Despite the fact that in general φ̸ = Lθ κBκ(φ), the Borel–Laplace sum of a Gevrey-1 divergent series is of interest to us since φ appears as the asymptotic expansion of Sθ(φ).
