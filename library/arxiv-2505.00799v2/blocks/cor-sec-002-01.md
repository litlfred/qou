The asymptotic series of Eichler integrals of half-integral weight cusp forms, periodic functions of Maass waveforms with s = 1/2, and the periodic function of the real- analytic Eisenstein series with s = 1/2 are modular resurgent series. Moreover, the median resummation of each of these series recovers the original function, which is a holomorphic quantum modular form. The paper is organized as follows. In Section 2, we develop the necessary background on resurgence, as well as holomorphic modular and Maass forms.
