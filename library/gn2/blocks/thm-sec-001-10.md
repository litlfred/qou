Consider the character ring χQ(π1(EKm)) and the KBSA KQ −1(EKm) whose coeﬃcient ﬁelds are Q. Then KQ −1(EKm) has trivial nilradical. Therefore the following holds: χQ(π1(EKm)) = Q[x, y]/⟨Rm(−1)⟩.
