Section 3 is devoted to the study of integrals in weak Hopf alg ebras. Using the notion of
weak Hopf modules which is a generalization of the Hopf modules [1, 19] we show t hat non-zero
integrals exist. A weak Hopf version of Maschke’s Theorem ch aracterizes semisimple WHAs
as those possessing normalized integrals. An other importa nt class of WHAs are those which
are Frobenius algebras. They are characterized by possessi ng non-degenerate left integrals.
This class is a selfdual class by the Duality Theorem of non-d egenerate integrals. We conclude
with giving necessary and suﬃcient criteria for the existen ce of Haar integrals, i.e. normalized
non-degenerate 2-sided integrals in a WHA.
