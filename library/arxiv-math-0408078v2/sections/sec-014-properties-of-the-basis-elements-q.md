When an element X ∈C is used to decorate a framed knot K, the Homﬂy
polynomial, P(K; X) ∈Λ, of the decorated knot gives a 2-variable invariant
of K determined by X. Each basis element Qλ,µ gives such an invariant of
K, and these determine the invariants for all choices of X. When µ = φ
these invariants, for each ﬁxed λ, give a family of 1-variable invariants after
setting v = s−N which coincide, up to a power of s, with the quantum group
invariants of K determined by the irreducible sl(N)q-module corresponding
to the partition λ.
In general, the 2-variable invariant from Qλ,µ, when evaluated at v = s−N,
gives the sl(N)q quantum invariant for an irreducible module whose partition
depends on λ, µ and N. The simplest example where both λ and µ occur
non-trivially is Q✷,✷, where ✷represents the unique partition of 1. The 2-
variable invariant in this case, after setting v = s−N, evaluates the quantum
invariant when the knot is coloured by the adjoint representation of sl(N)q
for each N. Since Q✷,✷= h1h∗
1 −1 this invariant is very nearly the Homﬂy
polynomial of the reverse parallel of K with one strand in each direction.

In the skein C the replacement of s by −s−1, while ﬁxing v and leaving
diagrams unaltered, has the eﬀect of interchanging rows and columns in the
Young diagram of each partition λ to give its conjugate partition λ∨. Then
Qλ,µ becomes Qλ∨,µ∨, and the invariant P(K, Qλ∨,µ∨) is given by changing s
to −s−1 in P(K : Qλ,µ). Where both partitions λ and µ are self-conjugate
the invariant is symmetric in s and −s−1, and can then be written in terms
of v and z = s −s−1, as is the case for the ordinary Homﬂy polynomial of
K, which comes from the decoration of K by the single string Q✷,φ.

3.3

For a framed knot K the invariants P(K : Qλ,µ) can be expressed in terms of
the eigenvalues of a map K : C →C, described as follows. Draw a diagram

any element X ∈C to give the element K(X) ∈C. Thus P(K : X) is the
Homﬂy polynomial < K(X) >.
