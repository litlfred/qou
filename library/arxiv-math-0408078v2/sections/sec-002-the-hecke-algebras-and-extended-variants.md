The Hecke algebras and extended variants
Write Hn for the skein S(F) of n-tangles, where F is a rectangle with n inputs
at the bottom and n outputs at the top. Composing n-tangles by placing
one above another induces a product which makes Hn into an algebra. It has
a linear basis of n! elements, and is isomorphic to the Hecke algebra Hn(z),
