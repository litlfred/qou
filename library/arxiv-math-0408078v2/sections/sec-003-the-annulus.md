The annulus
The Homﬂy skein of the annulus, C, as discussed in [13] and originally in [22],
is the framed Homﬂy skein S(S1 × I). An element X ∈C will be indicated

This is a Λ-linear map, whose image we call Cn,p.
Every diagram in the
annulus represents an element in some Cn,p.
Turaev [22] showed that C is freely generated as an algebra by the set
{Am : m ∈Z} where Am, m̸ = 0, is represented by the closure of the braid
σ|m|−1 · · · σ2σ1,
Am =
|m|-1 .
The orientation of the curve around the annulus is counter-clockwise for
positive m and clockwise for negative m. The element A0 is the identity
element and is represented by the empty diagram.
The algebra C is the product of two subalgebras C+ and C−generated by
{Am : m ∈Z, m ≥0} and {Am : m ∈Z, m ≤0} respectively.
The algebra C+ is spanned by the subspaces Cn,0 which arise from the
closure of elements in Hn, and has been studied quite extensively [16, 10, 14,
6]. In particular, there is a good basis of C+ consisting of closures of certain
idempotents of Hn.
The basis elements {Qλ,µ} for the whole of C, which we construct here, are
identiﬁed in section 3 as eigenvectors of the meridian maps deﬁned in section
1.7, following the Schur function methods of Lukac [10] for C+. This fact sug-
gests that it should be useful and relatively easy to express other elements of
C in this basis. Lukac also shows that the Gyoja-Aiston idempotents for Hn,
