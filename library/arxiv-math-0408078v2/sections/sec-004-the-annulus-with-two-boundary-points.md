As an algebra, A is commutative. A straightforward skein theoretic proof

obvious.
Remark.
A skein which is linearly isomorphic to A is used by Kawagoe
[6] and other authors. Their version is based on the annulus with input and
output points on the same component. More recently A has come into use,
as its algebraic properties allow for some satisfyingly clean proofs. See also
[14], and work by Lukac [10].
