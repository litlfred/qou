The meridian maps of the skein C
We introduce here the linear endomorphisms ϕ, ¯ϕ of C which are of central
importance in this work.

loop, oriented in either direction, to give diagrams ϕ(X) and ¯ϕ(X) in the
annulus as indicated. This induces linear endomorphisms ϕ, ¯ϕ of the skein
C, called the meridian maps.
