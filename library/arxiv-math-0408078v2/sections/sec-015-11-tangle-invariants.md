When µ = φ it can be shown [15], using properties of the Gyoja-Aiston
idempotents, that the eigenvalues Kλ,φ, although conceivably rational func-
tions in v and s, are in fact integral elements of Λ, lying in the subring
Λ0 = Z[s±1, v±1]. This is also the case for K✷,✷, and we believe that it is true
in general.
By way of example we give the invariant K✷,✷for the trefoil and for the
ﬁgure-eight knot, in terms of v and z = s −s−1.
For the trefoil we get v2−4v4+4v6+z2(1+2v2−7v4+4v6)+z4(v2−2v4+v6)
for some choice of framing - there is a factor of v2 to be used for each change
of framing.
For the ﬁgure-eight with zero framing the result is symmetric in v±1, and
is 3−2z2 −6z4 −2z6 +(v2+v−2)(−2−z2 +2z4+z6)+(v4+v−4)(1+2z2+z4).
