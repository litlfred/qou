KNIZHNIK-ZAMOLODCHIKOV EQUATIONS IN DELIGNE
CATEGORIES
P. Etingof ⋆, I. Motorin*, A. Varchenko♢, and I. Zhu†
Abstract. We consider the Knizhnik-Zamolodchikov equations in Deligne Categories
in the context of (gl𝑚, gl𝑛) and (so𝑚, so2𝑛) dualities. We derive integral formulas for the
solutions in the first case and compute monodromy in both cases.
