A VERSION OF THE VOLUME CONJECTURE
HITOSHI MURAKAMI
Abstract. We propose a version of the volume conjecture that would relate a
certain limit of the colored Jones polynomials of a knot to the volume function
deﬁned by a representation of the fundamental group of the knot complement
to the special linear group of degree two over complex numbers.
We also
conﬁrm the conjecture for the ﬁgure-eight knot and torus knots. This version
is diﬀerent from S. Gukov’s because of a choice of polarization.
