Let $G$ be the Coxeter group of type $H_4$,

and take the complex field $\mathbb{C}$ as our base field. Then the group algebra $\mathbb{C}[G]$ is generated by four elements $s_i\ (i=1,2,3,4)$ with the defining relations: $$\begin{aligned}
{\rm (quadratic\;\; relations)} & s_1^2=s_2^2=s_3^2=s_4^2 =1, \label{quadratic relations}\\

& (s_1s_2)^5=(s_2s_3)^3=(s_3s_4)^3 =1=(s_1s_3)^2=(s_1s_4)^2=(s_2s_4)^2.
\end{aligned}$$ We define our monomial order to be the degree-lexicographic order with $s_4>s_3>s_2>s_1$. Combining the above relations, we get six braid relations: $$\begin{aligned}
{\rm (braid\;\; relations)}&s_2s_1s_2s_1s_2=s_1s_2s_1s_2s_1,\  s_3s_2s_3=s_2s_3s_2,\  s_4s_3s_4=s_3s_4s_3  \label{braid relations}\\
&s_3s_1=s_1s_3,\ \ s_4s_1=s_1s_4,\ \ s_4s_2=s_2s_4. \nonumber
\end{aligned}$$ We denote by $R$ the set of relations ([\[quadratic relations\]](#quadratic relations){reference-type="ref" reference="quadratic relations"}) and ([\[braid relations\]](#braid relations){reference-type="ref" reference="braid relations"}), that is, $$\begin{aligned}
R&=&\{\ s_1^2-1,\ \ s_2^2-1,\ \ s_3^2-1,\ \ s_4^2-1,\ \ \nonumber \\
&&\ \ s_2s_1s_2s_1s_2-s_1s_2s_1s_2s_1,\ s_3s_2s_3-s_2s_3s_2,\ s_4s_3s_4-s_3s_4s_3\  \nonumber  \\
&&\ \ s_3s_1-s_1s_3,\ \ s_4s_1-s_1s_4,\ \ s_4s_2-s_2s_4,\ \ \}.\nonumber \label{defining_relations}
\end{aligned}$$ Note that the group algebra $\mathbb{C}[G]$ is isomorphic to $\mathbb{C}\langle s_1,s_2,s_3,s_4\rangle /I$, where $I$ is the two-sided ideal generated by $R$ in the free associative algebra $\mathbb{C}\langle s_1,s_2,s_3,s_4\rangle$.

A reducible monomial is called minimal if its every proper submonomial is not reducible. Our strategy to find relations is considering all minimal reducible monomials until we obtain the smallest set of standard monomials. We check the relations in order of the degree of a monomial, one by one. While the leading monomial of the above relation (b) is not minimal, we are writing explicitly for subsequent computations.
