In section [\[sec:preliminaries\]](#sec:preliminaries){reference-type="ref" reference="sec:preliminaries"} we recall the basics of the various geometries that we use, mostly to fix notation. The rest of the paper consists of three main parts, namely sections [\[sec:outlook\]](#sec:outlook){reference-type="ref" reference="sec:outlook"}-[\[sec:girth\]](#sec:girth){reference-type="ref" reference="sec:girth"}, [\[sec:every_scale\]](#sec:every_scale){reference-type="ref" reference="sec:every_scale"}-[\[sec:inequalities\]](#sec:inequalities){reference-type="ref" reference="sec:inequalities"} and [\[sec:projective_total_volume\]](#sec:projective_total_volume){reference-type="ref" reference="sec:projective_total_volume"}, that are largely independent of each other. In Section [\[sec:outlook\]](#sec:outlook){reference-type="ref" reference="sec:outlook"} we dwell on the projective nature of the Funk metric, and its transformation under projective maps. Definition [\[def:conomadic\]](#def:conomadic){reference-type="ref" reference="def:conomadic"} is key. We establish the duality property of the Funk volume element, proving part i) of Theorems [\[main:projective_invariants\]](#main:projective_invariants){reference-type="ref" reference="main:projective_invariants"} and [\[main:duality\]](#main:duality){reference-type="ref" reference="main:duality"}, and Corollary [\[cor:hilbert_volume\]](#cor:hilbert_volume){reference-type="ref" reference="cor:hilbert_volume"}. In section [\[sec:billiards\]](#sec:billiards){reference-type="ref" reference="sec:billiards"} we discuss general Funk billiards and prove their projective invariance and duality properties, namely part ii) of Theorems [\[main:projective_invariants\]](#main:projective_invariants){reference-type="ref" reference="main:projective_invariants"} and [\[main:duality\]](#main:duality){reference-type="ref" reference="main:duality"}. Then in section [\[sec:conics\]](#sec:conics){reference-type="ref" reference="sec:conics"} we focus on Funk billiards inside an ellipse, which is just the standard hyperbolic billiard, and prove Theorem [\[main:conics\]](#main:conics){reference-type="ref" reference="main:conics"}. In section [\[sec:girth\]](#sec:girth){reference-type="ref" reference="sec:girth"}, we establish the Funk analogue of Schäffer's dual girth conjecture, completing the proof of Theorems [\[main:projective_invariants\]](#main:projective_invariants){reference-type="ref" reference="main:projective_invariants"} and [\[main:duality\]](#main:duality){reference-type="ref" reference="main:duality"}. In section [\[sec:every_scale\]](#sec:every_scale){reference-type="ref" reference="sec:every_scale"} we begin the study of the volume of balls in Funk geometry, establishing Proposition [\[prop:centro_affine_main\]](#prop:centro_affine_main){reference-type="ref" reference="prop:centro_affine_main"}. Then in section [\[sec:inequalities\]](#sec:inequalities){reference-type="ref" reference="sec:inequalities"} we prove the various inequalities, namely Theorems [\[main:bodies_inequality\]](#main:bodies_inequality){reference-type="ref" reference="main:bodies_inequality"},[\[main:inequality_functional\]](#main:inequality_functional){reference-type="ref" reference="main:inequality_functional"} and Corollary [\[cor:entropy\]](#cor:entropy){reference-type="ref" reference="cor:entropy"}. Section [\[sec:projective_total_volume\]](#sec:projective_total_volume){reference-type="ref" reference="sec:projective_total_volume"} is dedicated to the proof of Theorem [\[main:beta\]](#main:beta){reference-type="ref" reference="main:beta"}.
