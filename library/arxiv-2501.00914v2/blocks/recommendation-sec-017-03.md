Thus Gabai \cite[Theorem~1.1]{gabai-solid-tori} showed that either \begin{itemize} \item $Z \cong S^1\times D^2$, and $P$ is either a $0$-bridge braid or a $1$-bridge braid; or \item $Z \cong (S^1\times D^2) \# Y$, where $Y$ is closed with $|H_1(Y)|$ finite and nontrivial. \end{itemize} Scharlemann \cite{scharlemann-reducible} showed that in the latter case $P$ must be a cable of a $0$-bridge braid, and $Y$ is then a lens space.
