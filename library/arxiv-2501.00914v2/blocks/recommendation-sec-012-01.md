But this is an immediate consequence of the adjunction inequality for the cobordism $X^\circ_n$, which contains the surface $\hat\Sigma_n$ of positive self-intersection: if $F_{X^\circ_n,\spint} \neq 0$ then $\spint$ must satisfy \[ \left| \langle c_1(\spint), [\hat\Sigma_n] \rangle \right| + \underbrace{[\hat\Sigma_n] \cdot [\hat\Sigma_n]}_{=n} \leq \underbrace{2g(\hat\Sigma_n)-2}_{=2g-2} \] and this is impossible. \end{proof}
