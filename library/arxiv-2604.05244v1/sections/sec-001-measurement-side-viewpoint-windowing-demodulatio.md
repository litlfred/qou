[]{#sec:measurement label="sec:measurement"}

We begin on the instrument side. The key object is the detection kernel $K_{\rm phys}$ [@Clerk2010], which encodes everything the acquisition chain does to the raw signal before reporting a number. We first define it in the simplest case, then generalize and discuss its role.
