We now turn to the device side. The periodically driven scatterer is described by a Floquet scattering matrix $S_{nm}(E)$ [@MoskBuett2002; @LiReichl1999] that connects incoming and outgoing sideband operators. From $S$ and the incoming occupation $N$, we construct the outgoing one-body correlator $C$ and derive the projection identity that bridges the measurement and device sides.
