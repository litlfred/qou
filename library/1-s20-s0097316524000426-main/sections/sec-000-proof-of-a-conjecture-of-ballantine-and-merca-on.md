In 2012, Andrews and Merca proved a truncated theorem on 
Euler’s pentagonal number theorem. Their work has opened 
up a new study of truncated series. Recently, Ballantine and 
Merca posed a conjecture on inﬁnite families of inequalities 
involving b6(n), which counts the number of 6-regular partitions 
of n. In this paper, we conﬁrm Ballantine and Merca’s 
conjecture on linear equalities of b6(n)b a s e d on a formula 
of the number of partitions of n into parts not exceeding 3
due to Cayley.
