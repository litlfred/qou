Consider an ideal hyperbolic 2>-simplex, that is a simplex A with all four vertices on the sphere of points at infinity. If a, fi, y are the dihedral angles along three edges meeting at a common vertex, then a + fi + y = IT, and volume(A) = Ji(a) + Ji({i) + Ji(y).
