Let us summarise our findings for the41 knot.
•We complete the2×2matrixJ red(x,q)of(x,q)-series to the3×3matrixJ(x,q)by

•A distinguished entry ofJ(x,q)is the Gukov–Manolescu series.
•The matrixJ(x,q)determines explicitly (but conjecturally) the Stokes constants and
hence the resurgence structure of the three perturbative formal power series.
•The matrixJ(x,q)conjecturally computes an extension of the 3D-index in a sector

•We complete the2×2matrix of descendant Andersen–Kashaev state-integrals to a

As a second example, we present our results for the52 knot. In this case, we complete the
3×3matrixJ red(q)to a4×4one, and use it to describe explicitly the Stokes constants of
the4asymptotic series in half of the complex plane, thus completing the resurgence question
of those asymptotic series. However, the5 2 knot reveals a new puzzle: the4×4matrix
is a block of a6×6matrix whose rows are a fundamental solution to a sixth order linear
q-difference equation, namely the one satisfied by the descendant colored Jones polynomial
