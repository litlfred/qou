Finally, in order to compute the global Stokes matrix $\mathfrak{S}_{II\rightarrow III}(\tilde{q})$, we need to take into account that the odd powers of $\tau^{1/2}$ on both sides of [\[eq:GMsPhi\]](#eq:GMsPhi){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:GMsPhi"} give rise to additional $-1$ factors when one crosses the branch cut at the negative real axis, and [\[eq:SMM\]](#eq:SMM){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:SMM"} should be modified by $$\mathfrak{S}_{II\rightarrow III}(\tilde{q}) = \mathrm{diag}(1,-1,-1) M_{III}(\tilde{q})^{-1}\cdot M_{II}(\tilde{q}),$$ and we find $$\mathfrak{S}_{II\rightarrow III} = \begin{pmatrix} 1&1&0\\ 0&1&0\\ 0&-3&1 \end{pmatrix}.$$ Similarly this can be identified as $\mathfrak{S}_{\pi}$ associated to the ray $\rho_{\pi}$ and it can be factorised as $$\mathfrak{S}_{\pi} = \mathfrak{S}_{\iota_{0,1}}\mathfrak{S}_{\iota_{2,1}},\quad \mathfrak{S}_{\iota_{0,1}} = \begin{pmatrix} 1&1&0\\ 0&1&0\\ 0&0&1 \end{pmatrix},\quad \mathfrak{S}_{\iota_{2,1}} = \begin{pmatrix} 1&0&0\\ 0&1&0\\ 0&-3&1 \end{pmatrix}.$$ Note that the local Stokes matrices $\mathfrak{S}_{\iota_{0,1}}$ and $\mathfrak{S}_{\iota_{2,1}}$ also commute.
