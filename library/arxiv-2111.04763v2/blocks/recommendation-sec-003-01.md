We should point out that although a proof of resurgence of the asymptotic series $\Phi^{(\sigma)}(h)$ is *still* missing, the current paper (as well as the prior ones [@GGM:resurgent; @GGM:peacock]) provide a complete description of their resurgent structure (namely the location of the singularities and a calculation of the Stokes constants) with precise statements, complemented by extensive numerical computations (including a numerical computation of the Stokes constants).
