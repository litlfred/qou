If L/K ab is a ﬁnite extension of degree D = [L : K ab], then h(α) ≥CK,D > 0 for all nonzero, nonroot of unity α ∈L, where CK,D is a constant depending only on K and D. In particular, B0(L) ≥CK,D > 0. This result generalizes a result of Amoroso and Dvornicich [2000], which states that h(α) ≥(log 5)/12 for all nonzero, nonroot of unity α ∈Qab. In particular, B0(Qab) ≥(log 5)/12. For our purposes, another useful result from the paper [Amoroso and Dvornicich 2000] is the following.
