Let f be a monic squarefree polynomial in Fq[x] and χ f be the quadratic character with conductor f . Let C be the hyperelliptic curve deﬁned by y2 = f (x) and let P ∈Z[x] be the characteristic polynomial of geometric Frobenius acting on the Jacobian of C. Then for any s̸ = 0, L(s, χ f ) = 0 if and only if P(qs) = 0. This is immediate from the description of P as the numerator of the zeta function of C, and the connection of the latter to L(s, χ f ) (see, for instance, [Rudnick 2010, Section 2]).
