Let S = PSLϵ n(q) be simple with q a power of 3 and n ≥2 and let X ≤Aut(S) such that X/S is a Sylow 3-subgroup of Aut(S)/S. Then there exist nontrivial χ1, χ2, χ3 ∈Irr3′(B0(S))σ1 that are pairwise not Aut(S)-conjugate and such that χ1 is X-invariant. Further, if n ≥3, then χ1 may be chosen to be Aut(S)-invariant, and if n ≥5, then χ1, χ2, χ3 may all be chosen to be Aut(S)-invariant.
