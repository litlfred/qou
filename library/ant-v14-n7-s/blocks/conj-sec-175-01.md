Let K be a number ﬁeld, let φ(x) ∈K[x] be a polynomial of degree d ≥2, let α ∈K, and assume that α is not an exceptional point for φ. Then K∞(φ, α)/K is an abelian extension if and only if the pair (φ, α) is K ab-conjugate to the pair (ψ, β) occurring in one of the following two families of examples: (i) ψ(x) = xd and β = ζ, a root of unity in K. (ii) ψ(x) = Td(x) is the d-th Chebyshev polynomial and β = ζ + ζ −1, where ζ is a root of unity in K.
