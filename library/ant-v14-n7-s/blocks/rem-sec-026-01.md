The theory of automorphic representations of G∗is more complicated than that of G: not all cuspidal representations are globally generic, and the naive formulation of strong multiplicity one is false, due to the presence of nontrivial global L-packets. In practice, this means that although cusp forms for G∗ do have Fourier–Whittaker expansions, one cannot necessarily recover all of their Fourier–Whittaker coef- ﬁcients from the action of the Hecke algebra of G∗(A f Q).
