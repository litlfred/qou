The Asai (or twisted tensor) L-function of a Bianchi modular form 9 is the L-function attached to the
tensor induction to Q of its associated Galois representation. When 9 is ordinary at p we construct a
p-adic analogue of this L-function: that is, a p-adic measure on Z×
p that interpolates the critical values
of the Asai L-function twisted by Dirichlet characters of p-power conductor. The construction uses
techniques analogous to those used by Lei, Zerbes and the ﬁrst author in order to construct an Euler
system attached to the Asai representation of a quadratic Hilbert modular form.
