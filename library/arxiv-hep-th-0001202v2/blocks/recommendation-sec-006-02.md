In the full Hopf algebra, we must show the existence of $P_{n,2}$ asymmetric pairings enumerated by $$P_2(x):=\sum_n P_{n,2} x^n =\dfrac12[H_1(x)]^2-\dfrac12H_1(x^2) =x^3+x^4+3x^5+5x^6+13x^7+28x^8+\ldots \label{P2}$$ Part of what is required is clearly provided by $$W(l_1,X)=l_1 X-2B_+(X) \label{WX1}$$ since ([\[BP\]](#BP){reference-type="ref" reference="BP"}) shows that $$U_1\circ W(l_1,X)=l_1\otimes X+X\otimes l_1 -2(P\circ B_+)(X\otimes e+e\otimes X)=l_1\otimes X-X\otimes l_1 \label{U1X}$$ has the desired antisymmetry.
