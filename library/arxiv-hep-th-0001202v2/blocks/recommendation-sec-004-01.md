Submitting $1,1,1,2,3,8,16,41$ to Neil Sloane's superseeker [@EIS], we learnt that it is generated by the first 8 terms of $$H_1(x)=\frac{R(x)-x}{R(x)}=1-\prod_{n>0}(1-x^n)^{r_n}. \label{H}$$ At the time, Sloane had no idea that we were studying the bigrading of rooted trees and told us "it is pretty unlikely this is your sequence, but I thought I should pass this along just in case".
