For a Weyl group, one can prove Soergel’s Categoriﬁcation Th eorem easily by
transferring known facts about H to SBim using hypercohomology . Soergel’s proof

Soergel’s theory (or the Bott-Samelson description of H) states that the objects of
SBim are generated by the objects Bs. Moreover, there are isomorphisms between
objects in SBim which lift the quadratic and braid relations of the Hecke algebra.
(One should not categorify the Iwahori presentation given a bove, but a presentation
using the Kazhdan-Lusztig generators which we will describ e below.) Heuristically
speaking, this is the categorical analogue of Iwahori’s alg ebraization of H, on the
level of objects. However, in H or SBim there is a whole new layer of structure, with
no analogue in the Hecke algebra: the composition of morphis ms.
