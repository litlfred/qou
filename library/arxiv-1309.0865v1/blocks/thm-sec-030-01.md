It is worse than that the rotation of one bi- valent vertex is not the other. The bivalent vertex itself is not invariant under 360 degree twisting, being off by a sign. Any morphism with an even number of biva- lent vertices will be cyclic (i.e. invariant under 360 degree twists), but this still does not imply that twisting by 180 degrees is equal to rotation by 180 degrees, because bivalent vertices do not behave this way consistently.
