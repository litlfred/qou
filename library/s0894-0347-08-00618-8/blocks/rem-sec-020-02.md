We note that to disprove just the strongest version of Pemantle’s con- jecture on ULC rank sequences (Conjecture 2.3) we can simply invoke Example 2.2 in §2.1, which combined with Theorem 3.7 shows that Conjecture 2.3 actually fails even for symmetric measures. To give yet another counterexample to Conjecture 2.3 we can also employ a construction used in [55]. Indeed, the measure in P4 consid- ered in [55, Theorem 3.5] is seen to be NA but not ULC. Note though that both examples mentioned above fail to be NLC (hence also CNA+, CNA, h-NLC+, and h-NLC), so they do not provide counterexamples to the weaker conjectures (Conjectures 2.4 and 2.5).
