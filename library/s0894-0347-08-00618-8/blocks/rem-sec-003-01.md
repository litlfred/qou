As we explain in §3.1 and §4, a strongly Rayleigh measure is necessarily Rayleigh. The terminology adopted is motivated by the fact that the equivalent condition for real stability for multi-aﬃne polynomials in Theorem 4.1 is a natural strengthening of the deﬁnition of a Rayleigh polynomial/measure (Deﬁnition 2.5). The term is taken from [20], where a matroid was deﬁned to be strongly Rayleigh if (5) holds for its bases-generating polynomial.
