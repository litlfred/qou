The analog of Theorem 5.2 for asymmetric exclusion processes is false. A simple example is obtained by taking S = {1, 2} in which only transitions from state 1 to state 2 are allowed. If the initial distribution gives probability 1 4 to each of the conﬁgurations in {0, 1}S, then the limiting distribution as t →∞is given by µ({11}) = µ({00}) = 1 4, µ({10}) = 0, and µ({01}) = 1 2.
