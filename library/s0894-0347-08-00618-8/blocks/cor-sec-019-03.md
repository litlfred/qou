If µ is an almost symmetric measure (i.e., its generating polyno- mial is symmetric in all but possibly one variable; cf. Deﬁnition 2.1), then (1) µ is Rayleigh/h-NLC+ ⇐⇒µ is CNA+; (2) µ is h-NLC ⇐⇒µ is CNA. This shows that Pemantle’s Conjecture 2.6 is true for almost symmetric measures (recall that by the results in §4.2, Conjecture 2.6 also holds for strongly Rayleigh measures and PHR measures).
