For the case of interest in this paper, namely (2, 2)-intersections in P2g+1 with g ≥2, the ampleness hypothesis (4.11) holds by Lemma 4.4. When g = 1, this conditions fails; the class c1(P3) −2PD[E] lies on the boundary of the K¨ahler cone of the blow-up of P3 along a complete intersection elliptic curve. In that case, there is no monotone correspondence, which ﬁts with the fact that the elliptic curve has no balanced Fukaya category and F(Σ1) is naturally deﬁned over ΛC.
