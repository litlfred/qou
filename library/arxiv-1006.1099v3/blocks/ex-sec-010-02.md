We noted in the Introduction that M(Σ2) ∼= Q0∩Q1 ⊂P5. The map µ : H1(Σ; Z) → H3(M(Σ); Z) is an isomorphism, equivariant for the action of the symplectic group Sp4(Z). The quantum cohomology of any closed symplectic manifold splits into generalised eigenspaces for the action of quantum multiplication by the ﬁrst Chern class, cf. Section 3.1 for a fuller discussion. For M(Σ2) one can compute this explicitly, starting from a presentation of quantum cohomology derived by Donaldson.
