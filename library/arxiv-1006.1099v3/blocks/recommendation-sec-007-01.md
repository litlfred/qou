If one believes that Fukaya categories should behave well under birational transformations -- a view espoused by Katzarkov and his collaborators, see for instance [@KKOY], and encouraged by the particular cases treated in this paper -- then one expects $\EuScript{F}(W)$ to be built out of $\EuScript{F}({\mathbb P}^1)$, which has two semisimple summands, and of $\EuScript{F}(\EuScript{M}(\Sigma_2))$, whilst $\EuScript{F}(W')$ should be assembled from $\EuScript{F}({\mathbb P}^3)$ (four semisimple summands) and $\EuScript{F}(\Sigma_2)$.
