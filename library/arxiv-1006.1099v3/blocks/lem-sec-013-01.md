Let Σg be a closed surface and σ ⊂Σg a separating simple closed curve. There is another separating simple closed curve σ′ ⊂Σg for which the products τk στ −k σ′ are pseudo-Anosov mapping classes for all k ≥1. In fact, one chooses σ′ so that σ ∪σ′ ﬁll Σg in the sense that their complement is a union of disks. Then any word in positive twists along σ and negative twists along σ′ (with both twists appearing) yields a pseudo-Anosov diﬀeomorphism; a proof is given in [31, Expos´e 13, Th´eor`eme III.3].
