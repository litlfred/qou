Every twisted complex in T w F(S0) is quasi-isomorphic to a pair of graded vector spaces, one supported at each point of S0. The endomorphism algebra of any idempotent is therefore a sum of matrix algebras, hence has rank two if and only if it is a direct sum of two one-dimensional matrix algebras. It follows that, for an even-dimensional quadric, DπF(Q; 0) contains a unique spherical object up to quasi-isomorphism.
