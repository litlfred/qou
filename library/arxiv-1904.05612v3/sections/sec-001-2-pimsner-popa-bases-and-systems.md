Recall, from [17], that given a unital inclusion of von Neumann algebras N ⊂M with a
faithful normal conditional expectation E from M onto N, a family {mj}j in M is called
a right orthogonal system for M over N with respect to E if E(m∗
i mj) = δijfj for some
projections {fj}j in N. In this article, we will be dealing only with ﬁnite right orthogonal
systems.
