ON ORTHOGONAL SYSTEMS, TWO-SIDED BASES AND REGULAR
SUBFACTORS
KESHAB CHANDRA BAKSHI AND VED PRAKASH GUPTA
Abstract. We prove that a regular subfactor of type II1 with ﬁnite Jones index always
admits a two-sided Pimsner-Popa basis. This is preceded by a pragmatic revisit of Popa’s
notion of orthogonal systems.
