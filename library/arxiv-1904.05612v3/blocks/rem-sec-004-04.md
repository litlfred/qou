Corollary 2.14 could be related somewhat to a recent question asked by Popa in [18] about the maximum number of unitaries possible in an orthonormal basis (in the sense of [14]) of a given subfactor. It, at least, tells us that every ﬁnite index subfactor N ⊂M of type II1 always admits a Pimsner-Popa basis (not necessarily orthonormal) containing at least |G| many unitaries. In view of Corollary 2.14, calculating cardinality of G becomes quite relevant. However, in practice, we are yet to ﬁnd a suitable way to calculate the cardinality of G.
