et al. , 2006 ) at BNL in a 1.45 T superconducting storage

discussed in past CODA TA adjustment reports. The relativ-

dependence on the static electric field used for transverse
confinement is tuned out. The spread in their velocities leads
to a negligible contribution to the uncertainty budget. The
