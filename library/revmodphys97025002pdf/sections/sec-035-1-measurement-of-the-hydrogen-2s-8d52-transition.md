Brandt et al. (2022) have measured the frequency of the
2S −8D5=2 transition in hydrogen with a relative uncertainty
of 2.6 × 10−12. The same transition had been measured earlier
by de Beauvoir et al. (1997) at LKB/SYRTE and that
measurement
and
the
recent
measurement
differ
by
13.3ð6.7Þ kHz. The more recent result has an uncertainty
that is more than 3 times smaller than the earlier result.
