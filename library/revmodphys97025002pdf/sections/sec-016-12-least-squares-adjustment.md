The 2022 CODATA set of recommended values of the
constants are based on a least-squares adjustment of 133 input
data and 79 adjusted constants, thus its degrees of freedom is
ν ¼ N–M ¼ 54. The χ2 of the initial adjustment with no
expansion factors applied to the uncertainties of any data is
109.6; for 54 degrees of freedom, this value of χ2 has only a
0.001% probability of occurring by chance. Moreover, eight
input data account for about 70% of it. To reduce χ2 to an
acceptable level, an expansion factor of 1.7 is applied to the
uncertainties of all the data in Tables XI, XII, and XIVand 2.5
to those of data items D1–D6 in Table XXV. Thus, for the final
adjustment on which the 2022 recommended values are based,
χ2 is 44.2, which for ν ¼ 54 has a probability of occurring by
chance of 83%. See Appendixes E and F of Mohr and Taylor
(2000) for details of the adjustment (Sec. XV).
