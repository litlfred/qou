Table XXXVIII indicates that the new data that became
available for the 2022 adjustment have led to significant
reductions in the uncertainties of the 2022 recommended
values of many constants. Nevertheless, it also shows that the
uncertainties of a number of constants have increased slightly,
mainly because of the increase in the uncertainty of α.
