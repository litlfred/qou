The ionization energies of 1H, 3H, 3He, 4He, 12C, and 28Si are
from the 2022 National Institute of Standards and Technology
(NIST) Atomic Spectra Database (ASD). The required ioniza-
tion energy for
3Heþ and the binding energies for 12C4þ, 12C5þ,
12C6þ, and 28Si13þ are derived from these as appropriate and are
input data for the 2022 adjustment (Sec. II.A; Table IV; and
Table XXV, D8, D12, D22–D24). [The binding energies of the
molecular ions Hþ
2 and HDþ in Eqs. (12) and (13) of Sec. II, and
also D25 and D26 in Table XXV, are input data too but were
available for the 2018 adjustment.]
