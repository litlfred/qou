The experimental values of the spin precession to cyclo-
tron frequency ratios ωsð12C5þÞ=ωcð12C5þÞ and ωsð28Si13þÞ=
ωcð28Si13þÞ, which determine ArðeÞ, are input data in the 2018
adjustment and the same values are input data in the 2022
adjustment. However, their observational equations, both of
which contain ArðeÞ as an adjusted constant, also contain as an
adjusted constant the g-factors geð12C5þÞ and geð28Si13þÞ,
respectively. These are calculated from theory and improve-
ments in the theory have reduced their uncertainties, thereby
leading to a recommended value of ArðeÞ with a reduced
uncertainty (Secs. VIII.A and VIII.B and Tables XXI, XXII,
XXV, and XXXI, D7, D9, D10, D13).
