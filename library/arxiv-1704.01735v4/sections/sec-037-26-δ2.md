5. An explicit construction of the modular curve X(13)
In this section, we will study the following classical problem in the theo ry
of modular curves, which goes back to F. Klein (see [24] and [25]).
Problem 5.1. Let p ≥ 7 be a prime number. Give an explicit con-
struction of the modular curve X(p) of level p from the invariant theory for
PSL(2, p) using projective algebraic geometry.
