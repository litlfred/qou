Using the identification of right $\mathbb{C}_q(\mathcal{K})$-comodules and left $\mathcal{U}_q$-modules, the subspace of $\mathbb{C}_q(\mathcal{M})$-invariant elements in $V$ coincides with the subspace of $\mathcal{U}_q(\mathfrak{m}^{\mathbb{C}})$-invariant elements in $V$, $$V^{\mathbb{C}_q(\mathcal{M})}=\{v\in V \,\, | \,\, Xv=\epsilon(X)v\quad \forall X\in \mathcal{U}_q(\mathfrak{m}^{\mathbb{C}}) \}.$$ In translating the general [CQG ]{.sans-serif}algebra theory from right $\mathbb{C}_q(\mathcal{K})$-modules to left $\mathcal{U}_q$-representations, we shall refer to $\mathbb{C}_q(\mathcal{K})$-spherical representations as $\mathcal{U}_q(\mathfrak{m}^{\mathbb{C}})$-spherical representations.
