[]{#evaluation label="evaluation"}

In this section we derive evaluation formulas for multivariable little and big $q$-Jacobi polynomials by degenerating evaluation formulas for the Macdonald-Koornwinder polynomials [@K]. Evaluation formulas for Macdonald-Koornwinder polynomials have been obtained in [@vD], [@Sa] and [@S2]. The evaluation formulas for the multivariable little $q$-Jacobi polynomials are used in the next section to obtain explicit expressions for the generalized dimensions $D_q$ (see [\[Dgeneralized\]](#Dgeneralized){reference-type="eqref" reference="Dgeneralized"}) and their degenerations.
