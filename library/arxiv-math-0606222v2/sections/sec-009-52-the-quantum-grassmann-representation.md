In this subsection we discuss the standard quantum analog of the complex Grassmannian $\mathcal{G}(n,d;\mathbb{C})\simeq \mathcal{K}/\mathcal{M}$ and its associated quantum Grassmann representation $\mathbb{C}_q(\mathcal{K}/\mathcal{M})$. The one-parameter family of quantum complex Grassmannians from [@NDS] is discussed separately in Section [\[quantum\]](#quantum){reference-type="ref" reference="quantum"}.

We define a Gelfand pair $(\mathbb{C}_q(\mathcal{K}), \mathbb{C}_q(\mathcal{M}))$ of [CQG ]{.sans-serif}algebras as follows. Write $\mathfrak{m}^{\mathbb{C}}\simeq\mathfrak{gl}_{d-n}(\mathbb{C})\times\mathfrak{gl}_n(\mathbb{C})$ for the complexified Lie algebra of $\mathcal{M}$. We view $\mathfrak{m}^{\mathbb{C}}$ as Lie subalgebra of $\mathfrak{gl}_d(\mathbb{C})$ in the standard way and write $$\mathcal{U}_q(\mathfrak{m}^{\mathbb{C}})\simeq\mathcal{U}_q(\mathfrak{gl}_{d-n}(\mathbb{C}))\otimes\mathcal{U}_q(\mathfrak{gl}_n(\mathbb{C}))$$ for the Hopf $*$-subalgebra of $\mathcal{U}_q=\mathcal{U}_q(\mathfrak{gl}_d(\mathbb{C}))$ generated by $K_i^{\pm 1}$ ($i=1,\ldots,d$) and $x_j,y_j$ ($j\in\{1,\ldots,d-1\}\setminus \{d-n\}$). Let $\mathbb{C}_q(\mathcal{M})$ be the span of the matrix coefficients of the finite dimensional irreducible $\mathcal{U}_q(\mathfrak{m}^{\mathbb{C}})$-representations of type one. Then $(\mathbb{C}_q(\mathcal{M}),p)$ is a quantum subgroup of $\mathbb{C}_q(\mathcal{K})$, with $p$ the surjective Hopf $*$-algebra morphism $p:\mathbb{C}_q(\mathcal{K})\rightarrow \mathbb{C}_q(\mathcal{M})$ defined by $p(f)=f|_{\mathcal{U}_q(\mathfrak{m}^{\mathbb{C}})}$.

Suppose $V$ is a finite dimensional right $\mathbb{C}_q(\mathcal{K})$-comodule. Using the identification of right $\mathbb{C}_q(\mathcal{K})$-comodules and left $\mathcal{U}_q$-modules, the subspace of $\mathbb{C}_q(\mathcal{M})$-invariant elements in $V$ coincides with the subspace of $\mathcal{U}_q(\mathfrak{m}^{\mathbb{C}})$-invariant elements in $V$, $$V^{\mathbb{C}_q(\mathcal{M})}=\{v\in V \,\, | \,\, Xv=\epsilon(X)v\quad \forall X\in \mathcal{U}_q(\mathfrak{m}^{\mathbb{C}}) \}.$$ In translating the general [CQG ]{.sans-serif}algebra theory from right $\mathbb{C}_q(\mathcal{K})$-modules to left $\mathcal{U}_q$-representations, we shall refer to $\mathbb{C}_q(\mathcal{K})$-spherical representations as $\mathcal{U}_q(\mathfrak{m}^{\mathbb{C}})$-spherical representations. The branching rules for finite dimensional $\mathcal{U}_q$-representations of type one, when viewed as representations of $\mathcal{U}_q(\mathfrak{m}^{\mathbb{C}})\subset
\mathcal{U}_q$, are the same as for the classical Gelfand pair $(\mathcal{K},\mathcal{M})$. Consequently $(\mathbb{C}_q(\mathcal{K}), \mathbb{C}_q(\mathcal{M}))$ is a quantum Gelfand pair, with associated unitary spherical dual $\Sigma_{\mathbb{C}_q(\mathcal{M})}\simeq (\mathcal{K}/\mathcal{M})\hat{~}
\simeq \Lambda_n$ (compare with the classical complex Grassmannian of Subsection refcG, e.g. [\[natural\]](#natural){reference-type="eqref" reference="natural"}). In particular, the $\mathcal{U}_q$-representations $$V_\lambda^q:=L_{\lambda^\natural}^q,\quad (\lambda\in\Lambda_n)$$ form a complete set of representatives of the finite dimensional, irreducible $\mathcal{U}_q(\mathfrak{m}^{\mathbb{C}})$-spherical $\mathcal{U}_q$-representations. The right $\mathbb{C}_q(\mathcal{K})$-comodule $\mathbb{C}_q(\mathcal{K})_{\mathbb{C}_q(\mathcal{M})}$ now identifies with the *quantum Grassmann representation* $$\mathbb{C}_q(\mathcal{K}/\mathcal{M}):=\{f\in \mathbb{C}_q(\mathcal{K}) \,\,\, | \,\,\, f(X
 Y)=f(X)\qquad \forall\, X\in\mathcal{U}_q, \forall\,
Y\in\mathcal{U}_q(\mathfrak{m}^{\mathbb{C}}) \},$$ viewed as left $\mathcal{U}_q$-module by the regular $\mathcal{U}_q$-action $$\bigl( Xf\bigr)(X^\prime)=f(S(X) X^\prime),\qquad
f\in \mathbb{C}_q(\mathcal{K}/\mathcal{M}),\,\,X,X^\prime\in\mathcal{U}_q.$$ It has the multiplicity free decomposition $$\mathbb{C}_q(\mathcal{K}/\mathcal{M})\simeq\bigoplus_{\lambda\in\Lambda_n}V_\lambda^q$$ in irreducible $\mathcal{U}_q$-representations.

For $\lambda\in\Lambda_n$ we fix a nonzero $\mathcal{U}_q(\mathfrak{m}^{\mathbb{C}})$-invariant vector $v_\lambda^q\in V_\lambda^q$, and we choose a scalar product $\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle_\lambda$ on $V_\lambda^q$ which turns $V_\lambda^q$ into a $*$-unitary $\mathcal{U}_q$-representation. The quantum zonal spherical functions $\varphi_\lambda^q\in
\mathbb{C}_q(\mathcal{K}/\mathcal{M})$ ($\lambda\in\Lambda_n$) are given by $$\varphi_\lambda^q(X)=\langle Xv_\lambda^q,
K^{-\delta}v_\lambda^q\rangle_\lambda,\qquad X\in\mathcal{U}_q,$$ where $K^{\pm\delta}$ is the linear endomorphism of $V_\lambda^q$ satisfying $$K^{\pm\delta}|_{V_\lambda^q[\nu]}=q^{\pm\langle
\delta,\nu\rangle}\hbox{Id}_{V_\lambda^q[\nu]},\qquad \forall
\nu\in\mathbb{Z}^d$$ (which is the square root of the positive definite linear operator on $V_\lambda^q$ defined by the action of $K^{\pm 2\delta}$ on $V_\lambda^q$). We define for $\psi\in\hbox{End}_{\mathbb{C}}(V_\lambda^q)$, $$\varphi_\lambda^q(\psi):=\langle \psi(v_\lambda^q),
K^{-\delta}v_\lambda^q\rangle_\lambda,$$ in particular $\varphi_\lambda^q(K^{\delta})=\|v_\lambda^q\|_\lambda^2$. The most convenient form of the quantum dimension formula (Proposition [\[propqdim\]](#propqdim){reference-type="ref" reference="propqdim"}) then reads $$\label{dimforquantum}
\hbox{Dim}_q\bigl(V_\lambda^q\bigr)=\frac{\bigl(\varphi_\lambda^q(K^{\delta})\bigr)^2}{
\| \varphi_\lambda^q\|_h^2}, \qquad \lambda\in\Lambda_n.$$
