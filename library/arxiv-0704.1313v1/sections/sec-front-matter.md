Mutant knots and intersection graphs
S. V. Chmutov ∗, S. K. Lando †
April 10, 2007
Abstract
We prove that if a ﬁnite order knot invariant does not disting uish mutant knots,
then the corresponding weight system depends on the interse ction graph of a
chord diagram rather than on the diagram itself. The convers e statement is
easy and well known. We discuss relationship between our res ults and certain
Lie algebra weight systems.
