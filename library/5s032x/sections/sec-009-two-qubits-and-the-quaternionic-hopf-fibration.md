Thus, for non-entangled qubits, the fibration simplifies to S2 ⊗S2, the product of the individual qubit spaces, and
the extra dimensions associated with the additional quaternionic imaginary units vanish. Hence, only entangled qubit
spaces lead to the above description proper. The fiber in the construction then contains the degrees of freedom of one
qubit, while the base space contains the other plus the entanglement degrees of freedom.
This also means the construction can be iterated: the S3 fiber, as the state space of the primary qubit, can itself
again be written as an S1 fibration over a S2 base.
