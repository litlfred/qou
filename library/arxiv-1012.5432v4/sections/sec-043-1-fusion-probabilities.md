Finally, the probability results obtained for Fibonacci anyons in [@PreskillCh9] can be extended to anyons based on the quantum groups of finite groups as well. Adopting Preskill's diagram convention of weighting by a compensating factor of $\sqrt{d_A}$ for each anyon-antianyon kink in a particle A's worldline, the dimension becomes equal to that of a topologically equivalent worldline. (Ordinarily every anyon-antianyon worldline zigzag would generate a factor of $\dfrac{1}{d_A}$ where $d_A$ denotes the quantum dimension of particle A.)

Consider the topological quantum computation schemes in which particle-antiparticle pairs are created from the vacuum. (This is standard in Kitaev's, Preskill's and Mochon's schemes.) Using the above diagram convention, Figures \[9\] and \[10\] show that the following formula for the probability that a particular fusion product occurs holds:

$$\mathbb{P}(A B \rightarrow C) = \dfrac{N_{AB}^C d_C}{d_A d_B}$$
