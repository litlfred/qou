The spectrum in $\mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z}$ theories consists of pure charges, pure fluxes, and dyons produced by fusing these charges and fluxes. Let $$| a \rangle | n \rangle = |a, n \rangle$$ denote a flux $a$ ($\in \mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z}$), charge $n$ ($\in \mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z}$) particle.
