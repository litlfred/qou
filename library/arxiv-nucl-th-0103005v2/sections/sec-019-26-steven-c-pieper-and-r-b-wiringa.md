Two (2 +;0+1) states occur very close together in the spectrum of 8Be at 16.6
and 16.9 MeV excitation; these isospin-mixed states come fr om blending the (2 +;1)
isobaric analog of the 8Li ground state with the second (2 +;0) excited state. There
are also fairly close (1 +;0,1) and (3 +;0,1) pairs at slightly higher energies in the 8Be
spectrum. The isospin-mixing matrix elements that connect these pairs of states,
