After other parameters in the trial function have been optim ized, a series of
calculations are made in which the βLS[n] of Eq.(32) may be diﬀerent in the left-
and right-hand-side wave functions to obtain the diagonal a nd oﬀ-diagonal matrix
elements of the Hamiltonian and the corresponding normaliz ations and overlaps.
The resulting N ×N matrices are diagonalized to ﬁnd the βLS[n] eigenvectors, using
generalized eigenvalue routines because the correlated Ψ V are not orthogonal. This
allows us to project out not only the ground states, but excit ed states of the same
(J π;T ) quantum numbers. For example, the ground states of 6Li, 7Li, and 8Li are
obtained from 3 ×3, 5 ×5, and 7 ×7 diagonalizations, respectively.
