While the CD and CSB terms are small, there is a clear need for t heir presence.
The Nijmegen group studied many NN potentials from the 1980s and before, and
found that potentials ﬁt to np data in T = 1 states did not ﬁt pp data well even
after allowing for standard Coulomb eﬀects, and vice versa (2 4). All ﬁve modern
NN potentials, Reid93, Nijm I and II (25), CD Bonn (26), and A V18 , which ﬁt pp

term is required to ﬁt the diﬀerence between the pp andnn scattering lengths, and
is consistent with the mass diﬀerence between 3H and 3He, as shown below. The
identiﬁcation of the proper CI, CD, and CSB NN force components is important in
setting the proper baseline for the introduction of NNN forces, which are required
to ﬁt the 3H and 3He binding energies as a primary constraint.
