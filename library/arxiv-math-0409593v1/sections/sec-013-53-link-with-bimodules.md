For s ∈S, then Fs is obtained as in §2.2.1 for the canonical morphism πs : B →Ps, where Ps is
the variety of parabolic subgroups of type s. So, Fs is invertible, with inverse Fs−1 = R(ps)∗(qs)!.
It follows that Fw is invertible for w ∈W, with inverse Fw−1 = R(pw)∗(qw)!, hence we get a
morphism from BW to the group of isomorphism classes of invertible functors on Db(B).
