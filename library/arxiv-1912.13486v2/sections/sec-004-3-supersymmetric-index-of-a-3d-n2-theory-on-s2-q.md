In this section, we would like to extend the discussion above to the case of plumbed 3-manifolds. After having an effective description of a 3d $\mathcal{N}=2$ theory $T[M_3]$ for a plumbing graph from the homological blocks, we obtain the index of $T[M_3]$ and discuss the invariance of it under the 3d Kirby moves. We also give some remarks on the topologically twisted index of $T[M_3]$ on $S^2 \times_q S^1$.
