Index for a Model of 3d-3d Correspondence for
Plumbed 3-Manifolds
Hee-Joong Chung
Yau Mathematical Sciences Center, Tsinghua University, Beijing 100084, China
Abstract: We consider the S2 ×q S1 supersymmetric index of a 3d N = 2 theory T[M3]
when M3 is a plumbed 3-manifold. We engineer an eﬀective description of T[M3] from
the expression of the homological block for plumbed 3-manifolds as a D2 ×q S1 partition
function of a 3d N = 2 theory T[M3] with a boundary condition.
We check that the
supersymmetric index for such a T[M3] is invariant under the 3d Kirby moves.
