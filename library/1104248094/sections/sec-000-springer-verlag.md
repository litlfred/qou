Hochschild and Cyclic Homology of Quantum Groups
Ping Feng1'* and Boris Tsygan2'**
1 Department of Physics, Harvard University, Cambridge, MA 02138, USA
2 Department of Mathematics, Harvard University, Cambridge, MA 02138, USA
Received October 23, 1990; in revised form February 20, 1991
Abstract. For an arbitrary complex linear semisimple Lie group G, we consider
Hopf algebras of the deformations of the formal and algebraic functions on G.
The Hochschild and cyclic homology of these Hopf algebras are computed when
the value of the deformation parameter is generic.
