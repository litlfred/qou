Section 4 is devoted to proving that the maps Ψ : B(∞) −→B(∞) and Ψλ : B(λ) −→B(λ)
are crystal isomorphisms. To accomplish this, we give a suﬃcient condition in Lemma 4.1 for
the isomorphism Ind(∆(a,b) ⊠∆(c,d)) ≃Ind(∆(c,d) ⊠∆(a,b)) to exist for ﬁnite classical types.
Using this condition together with Lemma 4.3 of [15] and the surjective homomorphism
Ind(∆(a,b) ⊠∆(b,c)) ։ ∆(a,c), we prove in Lemma 4.3 that Nn(v) = hd Ind∆(a(v); n).
It
follows from that result and the choice of the sequence corresponding to k that Nk(v) =
hd Ind∆(a(v); k) for all k = 1, . . . , n.
Combining this with the crystal isomorphism Φ in
(0.1), we establish the crystal isomorphisms Ψ : B(∞) −→B(∞) and Ψλ : B(λ) −→B(λ).
This then gives an explicit realization compatible with the Kashiwara operators of all the
irreducible graded modules over R and Rλ for ﬁnite classical types.
