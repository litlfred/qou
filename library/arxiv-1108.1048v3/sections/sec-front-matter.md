CONSTRUCTION OF IRREDUCIBLE REPRESENT A TIONS
OVER KHOV ANOV-LAUDA-ROUQUIER ALGEBRAS
OF FINITE CLASSICAL TYPE
GEORGIA BENKART, SEOK-JIN KANG 1,2, SE-JIN OH 3,4, AND EUIYONG PARK
In Memory of Professor Hyo Chul Myung
Abstract. We give an explicit construction of irreducible modules ove r Khovanov-Lauda-
Rouquier algebras R and their cyclotomic quotients Rλ for ﬁnite classical types using a
crystal basis theoretic approach. More precisely, for each element v of the crystal B(∞ )
(resp. B(λ )), we ﬁrst construct certain modules ∆( a; k) labeled by the adapted string a of v.
We then prove that the head of the induced module Ind
(
∆( a; 1)⊠· · ·⊠∆( a; n)
)
is irreducible
and that every irreducible R-module (resp. Rλ-module) can be realized as the irreducible
head of one of the induced modules Ind(∆( a; 1) ⊠ · · ·⊠ ∆( a; n)). Moreover, we show that
our construction is compatible with the crystal structure o n B(∞ ) (resp. B(λ )).
