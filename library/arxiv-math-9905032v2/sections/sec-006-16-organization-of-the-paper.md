Section [\[s2\]](#s2){reference-type="ref" reference="s2"} contains the proof of Theorems [\[t1\]](#t1){reference-type="ref" reference="t1"} and [\[t1b\]](#t1b){reference-type="ref" reference="t1b"} and also various formulas for the kernels $\mathsf{K}$ and $\mathsf{J}$. We also discuss a difference operator which commutes with $\mathsf{J}$ and its possible applications.

Section [\[s3\]](#s3){reference-type="ref" reference="s3"} deals with the behavior of the Plancherel measure in the bulk of the spectrum; there we prove Theorem [\[t2\]](#t2){reference-type="ref" reference="t2"}. Theorem [\[t3\]](#t3){reference-type="ref" reference="t3"} and a similar result (Theorem [\[t4\]](#t4){reference-type="ref" reference="t4"}) for the poissonized measure $M^\theta$ are established in Section [\[s4\]](#s4){reference-type="ref" reference="s4"}.

At the end of the paper there is an Appendix, where we collected some necessary results about Fredholm determinants, point processes, and convergence of trace class operators.
