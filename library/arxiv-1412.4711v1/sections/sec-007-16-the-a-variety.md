Consider the closure of the image of DecX(π1(M), SL(3, C)) in DecX(Z2, G).
Let DM be the union of the component of maximal dimension of this closure.
Consider now a natural embedding C∗4 ⊂CP 4 given by (L, L∗, M, M∗, 1)
and ¯DM the closure of the image of DM. The ideal boundary of DM is
¯DM \ DM. Essentially the following deﬁnition was given independently in
[23].
Deﬁnition 1.7. We deﬁne the A-variety of M (for n = 3 and with a choice
of basis of the boundary torus homology) to be the closure of DM in C4.
We deﬁne the A-ideal to be its deﬁning ideal.
