complement (which we refer as the standard triangulation). In this section
we prove the following theorem.

As said in the introduction, we strongly believe that the character variety
of the ﬁgure eight knot complement does not contain any other irreducible
component with irreducible representations. A proof should appear else-
where.
In order to prove the theorem, we will compute 3 aﬃne varietiesD1,D 2,D 3,
3 birational maps π1,π 2,π 3 deﬁned everywhere on the Di’s and 3 polynomi-
als H1,H 2,H 3 in Q[X1,X 2,X 3] such that :
• (a) Defor(M,T ) =∪i=1...3Di.
• (b) V (Hi) =πi(Di),i = 1... 3.
• (c) πi(Di)⊂ V (Hi) is smooth and π1 realizes a homemorphism on
its image.
• (d) V (Hi) is irreducible in C3 or equivalently Hi is irreducible in
C[X1,X 2,X 3].
• (e) Di⁄=Dj for i⁄=j and i,j = 1... 3.
• (f)∩i=1...3Di⁄=∅.
Under assumptions (a) to (f), πi(Di) is a smooth Zariski-open subset in
the aﬃne irreducible algebraic variety V (Hi), it is thus connected – see [18,
4.16]. As πi is is a homeomorphism on its image, Di is also smooth and
connected. As ∩i=1...3Di⁄=∅, then Defor(M,T ) is also connected. So the
theorem follows from those properties.
