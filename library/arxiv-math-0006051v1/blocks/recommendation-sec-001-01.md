We have $$p^{-1} \log(\alpha(1+pw)) = p^{-1} \log(1+pw) \equiv w \pmod{p}\;.$$ If we assume that $a_k\in Z[1/(n+1)!]$ we now find from the last computation and from proposition [\[maincong\]](#maincong){reference-type="ref" reference="maincong"}, $$\begin{aligned} p^{1-n} &DF_n(\alpha(1+pw))\\ &\equiv (1-\alpha) \sum_{k=0}^{n-1} w^k(a_k+ (k+1) a_{k+1})\sum_{m=0}^{n-k-1} \frac{1}{m!} \widetilde{\operatorname{Li}}_{n-k-1-m}(\alpha) w^m \\ &\equiv (1-\alpha) \sum_{l=0}^{n-1} w^l \sum_{k=0}^l (a_k + (k+1) a_{k+1}) \frac{1}{(l-k)!} \widetilde{\operatorname{Li}}_{n-l-1}(\alpha)\;. \end{aligned}$$ It follows that to make the reduction of $p^{1-n} DF_n$ independent of $w$ for all $p>n+1$ it is necessary and sufficient that for $l=1,\ldots,n-1$ we have $$\label{conds} \sum_{k=0}^l (a_k + (k+1) a_{k+1}) \frac{1}{(l-k)!} =0\;.$$ If this is satisfied then the reduction of $p^{1-n} DF_n(\alpha(1+pw))$ is $(a_0+a_1) (1-\alpha) \widetilde{\operatorname{Li}}_{n-1}(\alpha)\equiv -(a_0+a_1) \operatorname{li}_{n-1}(\sigma(\alpha))$ so we should also require $a_0+a_1=-1$.
