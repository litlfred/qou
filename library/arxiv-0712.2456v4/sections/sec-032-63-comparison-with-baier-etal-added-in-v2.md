In summary our results for τΠ and λ1 are in agreement with those of [37]. Putting
together the results of our paper with the value of κ determined in [37] we have a pre-
diction for the full two ﬂuid dynamical stress tensor of a con formal ﬂuid dual to gravity,
propagating on an arbitrary curved background.
