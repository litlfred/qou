In summary, it is always possible to ﬁnd a physically unique s olution for the metric g(n),
which, in turn, yields the form of the nth order ﬂuid dynamical stress tensor (using the
usual AdS/CFT dictionary). This process, being iterative, can be used to recover the ﬂuid
dynamics stress tensor to any desired order in the derivativ e expansion.
In § 3.3 and § 3.4 we will provide a few more details of our perturbative pro cedure,
in the context of implementing this procedure to ﬁrst and sec ond order in the derivative
expansion.
