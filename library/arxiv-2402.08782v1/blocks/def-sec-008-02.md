A Farey circuit is a collection of points v0, v1, · · · vn each with a Hecke-Farey coordinate such that vi is adjacent to vi+1 and v0 = vn. A Farey circuit on M4(5) is given by H2, E1, F2, B1, J2, K1, C2, L1, E2, B1, F2, D1, H2 (12 vertices).
