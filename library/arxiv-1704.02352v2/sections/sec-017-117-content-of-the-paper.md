In [\[sec:approx-fact-characters\]](#sec:approx-fact-characters){reference-type="ref" reference="sec:approx-fact-characters"} we introduce the main algebraic tool for our considerations, namely [\[def:approx-factorization-charactersB\]](#def:approx-factorization-charactersB){reference-type="ref" reference="def:approx-factorization-charactersB"} which gives several convenient characterizations of the approximate factorization property. In [\[sec:proof-of-key-tool-short\]](#sec:proof-of-key-tool-short){reference-type="ref" reference="sec:proof-of-key-tool-short"} we prove this result. [\[sec:moments-determine-measure\]](#sec:moments-determine-measure){reference-type="ref" reference="sec:moments-determine-measure"} is devoted to some technical results, mostly related to probability measures which are uniquely determined by their moments. In [\[sec:proof-of-LLN\]](#sec:proof-of-LLN){reference-type="ref" reference="sec:proof-of-LLN"} we give the proof of Law of Large Numbers ([\[theo:lln\]](#theo:lln){reference-type="ref" reference="theo:lln"}). Finally, in [\[sec:proof-of-CLTCLT\]](#sec:proof-of-CLTCLT){reference-type="ref" reference="sec:proof-of-CLTCLT"} we give the proof of Central Limit Theorem ([\[theo:clt\]](#theo:clt){reference-type="ref" reference="theo:clt"}).
