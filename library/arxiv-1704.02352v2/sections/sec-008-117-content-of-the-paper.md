In [1](#sec:approx-fact-characters){reference-type="ref" reference="sec:approx-fact-characters"} we introduce the main algebraic tool for our considerations, namely [\[def:approx-factorization-charactersB\]](#def:approx-factorization-charactersB){reference-type="ref" reference="def:approx-factorization-charactersB"} which gives several convenient characterizations of the approximate factorization property. In [\[sec:proof-of-key-tool-short\]](#sec:proof-of-key-tool-short){reference-type="ref" reference="sec:proof-of-key-tool-short"} we prove this result. [\[sec:moments-determine-measure\]](#sec:moments-determine-measure){reference-type="ref" reference="sec:moments-determine-measure"} is devoted to some technical results, mostly related to probability measures which are uniquely determined by their moments. Finally, in [\[sec:proof-of-CLTCLT\]](#sec:proof-of-CLTCLT){reference-type="ref" reference="sec:proof-of-CLTCLT"} we give the proof of Central Limit Theorem ([\[theo:clt\]](#theo:clt){reference-type="ref" reference="theo:clt"}).

# Approximate factorization of characters {#sec:approx-fact-characters}

The purpose of this section is to give a number of conditions which are equivalent to the approximate factorization property ([\[def:approx-factorization-charactersA\]](#def:approx-factorization-charactersA){reference-type="ref" reference="def:approx-factorization-charactersA"}). These conditions often turn out to be more convenient in applications, such as the ones from [@DolegaSniady-examples-AFP].
