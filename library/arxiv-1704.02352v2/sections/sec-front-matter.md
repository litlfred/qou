GAUSSIAN FLUCTUATIONS
OF JACK-DEFORMED RANDOM YOUNG DIAGRAMS
MACIEJ DOŁ ˛EGA AND PIOTR ´SNIADY
ABSTRACT. We introduce a large class of random Young diagrams which
can be regarded as a natural one-parameter deformation of some classi-
cal Young diagram ensembles; a deformation which is related to Jack
polynomials and Jack characters. We show that each such a random
Young diagram converges asymptotically to some limit shape and that
the ﬂuctuations around the limit are asymptotically Gaussian.
