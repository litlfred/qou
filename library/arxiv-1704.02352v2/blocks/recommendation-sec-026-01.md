::: lemma []{#lem:Sniady2006-recycled-zombie label="lem:Sniady2006-recycled-zombie"} Assume that $X\subseteq \mathscr{P}$ is a set with the property that each $z\in\mathscr{P}$ can be expressed as a polynomial in the elements of $X$: $$\label{eq:takie-jednomiany-lubie} z= \sum_{1\leq i \leq n} z_{i,1} \cdots z_{i,l_i}$$ for some $n\geq 0$ and $z_{i,j}\in X$ in such a way that such that for each value of $1\leq i\leq n$ $$\label{eq:lost-assumption} \deg z \geq \deg z_{i,1}+ \cdots +\deg z_{i,l_i};$$ in other words the degree of each monomial should be bounded from above by the degree of $z$.
