We shall explore this link on a particular example of random matrices called *$\beta$-ensembles* or *$\beta$-log gases* [@Forrester], i.e. the probability distributions on $\mathbb{R}^n$ with the density of the form $$p(x_1,\dots,x_n) = \frac{1}{Z} e^{V(x_1)+\cdots+V(x_n)} \prod_{i<j} |x_i - x_j|^{\beta},$$ where $V$ is some real-valued function and $Z$ is the normalization constant.
