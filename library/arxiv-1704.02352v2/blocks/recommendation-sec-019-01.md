For any tuple $x_1,\dots,x_\ell \in\mathcal{A}$ we define their *conditional cumulant* as $$\begin{gathered} \kappa_{\mathcal{A}}^{\mathcal{B}}(x_1,\dots,x_\ell) = [t_1 \cdots t_\ell] \log \mathbb{E}e^{t_1 x_1+\dots+t_\ell x_\ell} = \\ \left. \frac{\partial^\ell}{\partial t_1 \cdots \partial t_\ell} \log \mathbb{E}e^{t_1 x_1+\dots+t_\ell x_\ell} \right|_{t_1=\cdots=t_\ell=0} \in \mathcal{B}, \end{gathered}$$ where the operations on the right-hand side should be understood in the sense of formal power series in variables $t_1,\dots,t_\ell$.
