A Young diagram λ ∈Y can be seen as a subset of Z via the map λ 7→{λi −i, i = 1, 2, ...}
or some shifted version of it. More precisely, when we consider partitions λ ∈Y with ﬁxed

while when the lengths may vary, it is convenient to consider the inﬁnite sets

the latter providing a more symmetric picture. In any case, the pushforward of a probability
measure on Y by one of these maps is thus a point process on a discrete subset of R. In
the cases we deal with, namely the poissonized Plancherel measure, the z-measures and
its limiting process with the Gamma kernel, these point processes are determinantal point
processes, see deﬁnition 2.3 below.
