The paper is organized as follows. In section [\[sec2\]](#sec2){reference-type="ref" reference="sec2"}, we collect definitions and basic properties of Christoffel deformations, orthogonal polnomial ensembles and determinantal point processes, and formally state that such deformations contain the description of their Palm measure.

In section [\[sec3\]](#sec3){reference-type="ref" reference="sec3"}, we introduce the Charlier ensemble and the Plancherel measure. We describe the Christoffel deformation of the Charlier ensemble and pass to the limit, in the same regime as in [@johanssonplancherel]. This leads to a deformation of the poissonized Plancherel measure, i.e. the process with the discrete Bessel kernel, see Theorem [\[thm1\]](#thm1){reference-type="ref" reference="thm1"}. We finally give the correlation kernel of the Palm measures of the discrete bessel point process.

In section [\[sec4\]](#sec4){reference-type="ref" reference="sec4"}, we introduce the $z$-measures and define their Christoffel deformations. We describe the Christoffel deformations in the case where the parameters belong to the degenerate series (which is nothing but the Meixner ensemble) and prove that a similar determinantal formula also holds for the other series, see Theorem [\[thmzmeas\]](#thmzmeas){reference-type="ref" reference="thmzmeas"}. We further give a description of the Palm measures of the $z$-measures in Theorem [\[thmpalmzmeas\]](#thmpalmzmeas){reference-type="ref" reference="thmpalmzmeas"}. We then pass to the limit towards the deformed Gamma kernel in the case of the one point deformation, see Theorem [\[thmgamma\]](#thmgamma){reference-type="ref" reference="thmgamma"}.
