15. The vector triple product in R*.

Let Xo = (1,0,0,0), X; = (0, 1,0,0), X2 = (0,0,1,0) and X; = (0, 0, 0, 1)
be the standard orthonormal basis for R*. Then, for x,v,w € R*, we define the
vector triple product

Xo Xi XQ XB
Vo Vi V2 V3
Wo Wi W2 W3
Xo Xi X. X3

[x, v, w] = det € R'‘.

Note that the first three rows of the above "determinant" consist of real numbers,
while the last row consists of vectors in R*. The value [x, v, w] of this
determinant is a vector, orthogonal to x , v and w, whose length is equal to the
volume of the 3-dimensional parallelepiped spanned by x, v and w. Note that
[x, V, W] is an alternating, multilinear function of its three arguments.
