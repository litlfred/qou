Let  X0  =  (1, 0, 0, 0) ,  X1  =  (0, 1, 0, 0) ,  X2  =  (0, 0, 1, 0)  and  X3  =  (0, 0, 0, 1) 
be the standard orthonormal basis for  R1,3 .  Then for  x , v , w  ∊  R1,3 ,  we define 
the vector triple product 
 
                                                               x0   x1   x2   x3 
                                                               v0   v1   v2   v3                      
                                  [x, v, w]  =  det                               ∊  R1.3 . 
                                                               w0  w1  w2  w3         
                                                               X0 -X1 -X2 -X3  
 
Note that  [x, v, w]  is an alternating multilinear function of its three arguments. 
 
For  y  ∊  R1,3 ,  we have 
 
                                                                          x0   x1   x2   x3 
                                                                          v0   v1   v2   v3                      
                                  < [x, v, w] , y >  =  det                              . 
                                                                          w0  w1  w2  w3          
                                                                          y0   y1   y2   y3 
 
Thus  [x, v, w]  is a vector in  R1,3  whose inner product with each of  x , v  and  w 
is zero.   
 
If  A  ∊  SO(1,3) ,  we have 
 
[Ax, Av, Aw]  =  A[x, v, w] .
