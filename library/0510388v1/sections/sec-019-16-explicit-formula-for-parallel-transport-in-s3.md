Let  x  and  y  be any pair of distinct, non-antipodal points in  S3  ⊂  R4 , 
equivalently, any pair of linearly independent unit vectors in  R4 .  Then there 
is a unique element  M ∊ SO(4)  that maps  x  to  y  and leaves fixed the 
two-dimensional subspace of  R4  consisting of all vectors orthogonal to both 
x  and  y .  For  v ∊ R4 ,  this mapping is given by 
 
 
                           < x + y , v >                1 + 2 < x, y >                  1 
M(v)  =  v   —    —————————   x   +   〈——————————  x  —  —————————  y ,  v 〉 y  . 
                          1  +  < x, y >                1  +  < x, y >             1  +  < x, y > 
 
 
Direct calculation shows that  M  ∊  SO(4) ,  and is therefore the desired isometry. 
 
If  v  ∊  TxS3 ,  then  < v, x >  =  0  and the formula for  M(v)  simplifies to 
 
 
                                                             < y, v > 
                              M(v)  =  v   —   —————————— (x  +  y) . 
                                                        1  +  < x, y > 
 
 
In this case,  M(v)  is the result of parallel transport of  v  from  x  to  y ,  so we 
will write 
 
 
                                                                      < y, v > 
                          Pyxv  =  M(v)  =  v   —   —————————— (x  +  y) . 
                                                                  1  +  < x, y >
