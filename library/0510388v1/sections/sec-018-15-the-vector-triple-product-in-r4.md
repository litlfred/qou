Let  X0  =  (1, 0, 0, 0) ,  X1  =  (0, 1, 0, 0) ,  X2  =  (0, 0, 1, 0)  and  X3  =  (0, 0, 0, 1) 
be the standard orthonormal basis for  R4 .  Then, for  x, v, w  ∊  R4 ,  we define the 
vector triple product  
 
                                                               x0   x1   x2   x3 
                                                               v0   v1   v2   v3                      
                                  [x, v, w]  =  det                               ∊  R4 . 
                                                               w0  w1  w2  w3         
                                                               X0  X1  X2  X3  
 
Note that the first three rows of the above "determinant" consist of real numbers, 
while the last row consists of vectors in  R4 .  The value  [x, v, w]  of this 
determinant is a vector, orthogonal to  x ,  v  and  w ,  whose length is equal to the 
volume of the 3-dimensional parallelepiped spanned by  x ,  v  and  w .  Note that 
[x, v, w]  is an alternating, multilinear function of its three arguments. 
 
For  y  ∊  R4 ,  we have 
 
                                                                          x0   x1   x2   x3 
                                                                          v0   v1   v2   v3                      
                                  < [x, v, w] , y >  =  det                              . 
                                                                          w0  w1  w2  w3          
                                                                          y0   y1   y2   y3 
 
If  A  ∊  SO(4) ,  then 
 
[Ax, Av, Aw]  =  A[x, v, w] .
