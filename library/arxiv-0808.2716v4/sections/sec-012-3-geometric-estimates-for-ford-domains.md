This section contains a number of geometric estimates on the Ford domains of Farey manifolds. We begin with a few estimates (Lemmas [\[lemma:triangle-ineq\]](#lemma:triangle-ineq){reference-type="ref" reference="lemma:triangle-ineq"}--[\[lemma:length-non-canonical\]](#lemma:length-non-canonical){reference-type="ref" reference="lemma:length-non-canonical"}) that apply to all triangulated cusped hyperbolic manifolds, and are generally known to hyperbolic geometers. We then restrict our attention to Farey manifolds, and establish several estimates about their Ford domains. The main result of this section is Proposition [\[prop:horosize\]](#prop:horosize){reference-type="ref" reference="prop:horosize"}: every zigzag contains an edge whose length outside a maximal cusp is universally bounded.
