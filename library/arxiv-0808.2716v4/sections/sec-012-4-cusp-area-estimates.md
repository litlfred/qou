In this section, we apply the results of Section 3 to prove qua ntitative estimates on the
cusp area of Farey manifolds. For most of the section, we shal l focus on punctured torus
bundles. At the end of the section, we will generalize these r esults to 4–punctured sphere
bundles and 2–bridge links.
