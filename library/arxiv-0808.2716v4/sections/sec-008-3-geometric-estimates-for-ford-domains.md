This section contains a number of geometric estimates on the Ford domains of Farey
manifolds. We begin with a few estimates (Lemmas 3.1–3.3) th at apply to all triangulated
cusped hyperbolic manifolds, and are generally known to hyp erbolic geometers. We then
restrict our attention to Farey manifolds, and establish se veral estimates about their Ford
domains. The main result of this section is Proposition 3.6: every zigzag contains an edge
whose length outside a maximal cusp is universally bounded.
