[]{#sec:cusp-area label="sec:cusp-area"}

In this section, we apply the results of Section [\[sec:horosphere\]](#sec:horosphere){reference-type="ref" reference="sec:horosphere"} to prove quantitative estimates on the cusp area of Farey manifolds. For most of the section, we shall focus on punctured torus bundles. At the end of the section, we will generalize these results to $4$--punctured sphere bundles and 2--bridge links.
