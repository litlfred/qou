[]{#sec:farey-ford label="sec:farey-ford"}

In this section, we review the canonical triangulations of Farey manifolds. We begin by recalling the definition of the Ford domain and the canonical polyhedral decomposition that is its dual. We then describe the combinatorics of the canonical polyhedral decomposition for each of the three families of Farey manifolds; for the manifolds in question, it is always a triangulation. Along the way, we introduce a number of terms and notions that will be needed in the ensuing arguments.
