Let N be a 4–punctured sphere bundle with monodromy Ω= Rp1Lq1 · · · RpsLqs, and with the property that the monodromy ﬁxes one preferred boundary circle of the 4–holed sphere. Let D be the maximal horoball neighborhood of the cusp corresponding to this preferred puncture, and let γ be any simple closed curve on ∂D that is transverse to the ﬁbers. Then area(∂D) ≥16 √ 147 s and ℓ(γ) ≥8 √ 147 s.
