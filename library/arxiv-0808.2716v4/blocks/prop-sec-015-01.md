[\[prop:horosize\]](#prop:horosize){reference-type="ref" reference="prop:horosize"} should be compared to previous work of Jørgensen [@jorgensen Lemma 4.3], which was carefully written down by Akiyoshi, Sakuma, Wada, and Yamashita [@aswy-book Lemma 8.1.1]. After adjusting for slightly different choices of normalization, Jørgensen's Lemma 4.3 can be summarized as saying that, whenever a zizag of a quasifuchsian punctured torus group is dual to six faces of the Ford domain, one of those faces has radius at least $$\mu/(4+2\sqrt{5}).$$
