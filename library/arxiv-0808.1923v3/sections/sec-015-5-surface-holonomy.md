From the viewpoint of a transport 2-functor, parallel transport and holonomy are basically evaluation
on paths or bigons. Let tra : P2(M ) /d47/d47 T be a transport 2-functor with BG-structure on M . Its
ﬁbres over points x,y ∈ M are objects tra( x) and tra( y) in T , and we say that its parallel transport
along a path γ :x /d47/d47 y is given by the 1-morphism
tra(γ) : tra(x) /d47/d47 tra(y)
in T , and its parallel transport along a bigon Σ : γ /d43/d51 γ′ is given by the 2-morphism
tra(Σ) : tra( γ) /d43/d51 tra(γ′)
inT . The rules how parallel transport behaves under the composition o f paths and bigons are precisely
the axioms of the 2-functor tra; see [SW, Deﬁnition A.5]. We give som e examples. If γ1 :x /d47/d47 y and
γ2 :y /d47/d47 z are composable paths, the separate parallel transports along th e two paths are related to
the one along their composition by the compositor
cγ1,γ2 : tra(γ2) ◦ tra(γ1) /d43/d51 tra(γ2 ◦γ1)
of the 2-functor tra. If id x is the constant path at x, the parallel transport along id x is related to the
identity at the ﬁbre tra( x) by the unitor
ux : tra(idx) /d43/d51 idtra(x).
The parallel transports along vertically composable bigons Σ : γ1 /d43/d51 γ2 and Σ ′ :γ2 /d43/d51 γ3 obey
tra(Σ ′ • Σ) = tra(Σ ′) • tra(Σ).
In the following we focus on certain bigons that parameterize surfa ces; the parallel transport along
these bigons will be called the holonomy of the transport 2-functor tra.
