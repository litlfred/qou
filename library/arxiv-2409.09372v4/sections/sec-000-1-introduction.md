Let us remark that the degenerate cyclotomic Hecke algebras are degenerate versions of the Ariki–
Koike algebras or the cyclotomic Hecke algebra of typeG(m,1,n), which originates from the works of
Drinfel’d [7] and Cherednik [6] and was mentioned explicitly in Grojnowski’s preprint [11], Kleshchev’s
book [16] would be a good reference. These two classes of algebras are closely related, as evidenced
by Brundan and Kleshchev’s seminal work [4], which shows that both algebras are isomorphic to a
cyclotomic Khovanov–Lauda–Rouquier (KLR) algebra where the weight is determined by the “cyclotomic
parameters”. Statements that are regarded as theorems in the setting of the cyclotomic Hecke algebras
are often adopted as statements in the setting of the degenerate cyclotomic Hecke algebras, and vice
versa (see e.g. [1,§6], [4,5,23,24] etc.).
In [13], Jones constructed the Jones polynomial for knots inS 3 by using Ocneanu’s Markov trace on
the Iwahori–Hecke algebras of typeAand posed the question about similar constructions on other Hecke
algebras as well as in other 3-manifolds. In [17] Lambropoulou constructed the first Markov trace on the
Iwahori–Hecke algebras of typeBand related it to the knot theory of the solid torus. Then Geck and
Lambropoulou gave a full classification of all Markov traces on these algebras in [8, 10]. Furthermore
Lambropoulou [18] constructed the Markov traces on the cyclotomic Hecke algebras, Juyumaya [15]
proved the existence of Markov trace on the Yokonuma–Hecke algebras and so on. Moreover, as we
remarked in [24, Remarks 5.7(ii)], it is interesting to investigate the “Markov traces” on (cyclotomic)
KLR algebras, which will be helpful to understand the (Markov) traces on (degenerate) cyclotomic Hecke
algebras.
Inspired by the aforementioned works, it is natural and interesting to study Markov traces onH n(u).
The aim of the paper is to define and construct the (non-)normalized Markov traces onH n(u) along
the line of Lambropoulou’s argument in [18], and investigate their specializations. A point should be
noted that the most natural way to define and study the Markov traces onH n(u) is to combine the
Brundan–Kleshchev isomorphism and Lambropoulou’s work [18]. Unfortunately the isomorphism is
too complicated to attack the problem. Roughly speaking, Brundan and Kleshchev constructed the
