MARKOV TRACES ON DEGENERATE CYCLOTOMIC HECKE ALGEBRAS
DEKE ZHAO
Dedicated to Professor Yingbo Zhang on the occasion of her 80th birthday
Abstract. Let Hn(u) be the degenerate cyclotomic Hecke algebra with parameter u = {u1, . . . , um}.
The paper aims to define and construct the (non-)normalized Markov traces on the sequence {Hn(u)}∞
n=1.
This allows us to provide the Brou´e–Malle–Michel symmetrizing trace on Hn(u) and show that the
Brundan–Kleshchev trace is a specialization of the non-normalized Markov traces on Hn(u).
