• The number of vertices, edges, faces, and simplices, of a triangulation T are denoted
byv, e, f, and s, respectively.
• The standard basic vectors in Zk are denoted by e1,...,e k.
• The (oriented) edge of simplex k from vertex i to j is denoted by εij,k.
• The matrix groups{( 1 x
0 1 )} and{(a x
0 a−1 )} are denoted byP andB, respectively. The
higher rank analogue of P is denoted by N.
• A representation is boundary-parabolic if it takes each peripheral subroup to a conju-
gate of P . Such is also called a ( G,P )-representation (G = SL(2, C) or PSL(2, C)).
In the higher rank case, such a representation is called boundary-unipotent.
• A triangulation is ordered if εij,k∼εi′j′,k′ implies that i<j ⇐⇒ i′ <j′.
