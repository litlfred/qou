Deﬁnition 6.1. A spherical Hopf algebra over a ﬁeld k consists of a ﬁnite dimen-
sional vector space A together with the following data:
1. a multiplication µ,
2. a unit η :F→A,
3. a comultiplication ∆: A→A⊗A,
4. a counit ϵ: A→F,
5. an antipodal map γ : A→A,
6. an element w∈A.
The data (A,µ,η, ∆,ϵ,γ ) is required to deﬁne a Hopf algebra. The conditions
on the element w are the following:
1. γ2(a)= waw−1 for all a∈A.
2. ∆(w)= w⊗w.
3. tr(θw)=t r (θw−1) for all left A-modules V and all θ∈ EndA(V ).
It follows from the condition ∆( w)= w⊗w that γ(w)= w−1 and that ϵ(w)=1 .
Such elements are called group-like.
