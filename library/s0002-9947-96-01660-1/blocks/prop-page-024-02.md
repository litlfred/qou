Let A be a spherical Hopf algebra over an algebraically closed ﬁeld. Then the non-degenerate quotient of the spherical category of ﬁnitely generated left A-modules is semisimple. A corollary to the proof of this proposition is that the nondegenerate quotient of any spherical subcategory of the category of left A-modules which is closed under taking direct summands is a semisimple spherical category. Thus, in order to con- struct a manifold invariant, it is suﬃcient to construct such spherical subcategories which are ﬁnite and have non-zero dimension.
