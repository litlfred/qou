Not all spherical Hopf algebras are modular Hopf algebras in the sense of [17]. For example, the group algebra of a ﬁnite group over a ﬁeld of characteristic 0, or more generally, any cocommutative, involutory, semisimple Hopf algebra, is spherical and not modular. Also, not all spherical Hopf algebras are ribbon Hopf algebras. The dual of the group algebra of a non-commutative ﬁnite group can be made a spherical Hopf algebra. However, the representation ring is non-commutative, and so the Hopf algebra cannot be quasi-triangular. The next proposition gives the application of spherical Hopf algebras to state sum models.
