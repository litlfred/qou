Since closed (3-)manifolds are examples of singular 3-manifolds, this determines an invariant of closed manifolds. Throughout this section, the proof refers to closed manifolds, which, as is the general convention in this paper, are taken to be oriented. However every statement is also true for oriented singular manifolds.
