§ 5. Discussion 
In the formulation of lattice gravity, we have treated the vierbein epa and the 
connection Apab as independent dynamical fields. This is called the first· order form as 
only first derivatives appear in the action. 
In the lattice gravity, it is simplest to quantize in the first-order form with independent 
metric and connection. If the quantization is defined in first-order form, the boundary 
terms of Gibbons and Hawking14) are not needed. However, it is not at all clear in the 
lattice formulation whether the same theory is obtained in two cases, in one of which we 
think the connection field as independent quantum variables and, in another case, the 
connection field is regarded as derived from the vierbein field. 
In our formulation, connections A p ab are the gauge fields of the Lorentz group and 
vierbeins epa are coupled as additional matter fields. In this sense, our standpoint is close 
to Utiyama's one.12) 
He showed that if one requires the invariance of Lagrangian under 
the local Lorentz transformation, then it is necessary to introduce the gravitational 
variables, which include both the metric (or vierbein) and the affiine connection. This is 
a generalization of the well-known fact that the invariance of the Lagrangian under the 
local phase transformation leads to the existence of the electromagnetic field. 
As pointed out by Kibble/ 3) Utiyama introduced a priori curvilinear coordinates and 
a set of 16 parameters epa. More unsatisfactory, the epa were treated as given functions 
of x, but in the later stage they were treated as field variables and interpreted as the 
components of a vierbein system in a pseudo-Riemannian space-time. 
Kibble improved this situation as follows. If one consider the local Poincare group, 
the vierbein components epa as well as the local affine connection A pab can be introduced 
as new field variables. This means that only one system of coordinates is required, and 
that a pseudo-Riemannian metric need not be introduced a priori. Therefore, we can 
start with the ordinary formulation of Lorentz invariance including translation invariance 
in terms of rectangular coordinates in fiat space-time. The interpretation of the theory in 
terms of a psudo-Riemannian space may be made later if desired.

We remark that the existence of the transfer matrix T with the property 0 ~ T ~ 1 and 
hence the positive Hamiltonian H~O can be proved, provided only the time translation 
invariance, which is satisfied if we take periodic boundary conditions in time direction. 
(In the continuum limit, translation invariance in time direction will imply the existence 
of the time-like killing vector and hence the space-time is stationary.) However, for other 
space directions we can take arbitrary boundary conditions (but compatible with 
reflection positivity) so as to give a non-trivial background. For example, Schwarzschild 
solutionIS) has no singularity in Euclidean section and the topology of space-time given by 
this solution is S2 x R2. This solution is positive definite and asymptotically flat. The 
choice of boundary conditions may restrict the possible form of the background space-
time. To see the realizability of above strategy, it is of considerable importance to study 
the behavior of G( n; fl.))) versus the coupling constant on the lattice.
