For $x\in\Omega^n$ and $A\subset\Omega^n$, we define Talagrand's convex distance to be $$d_T(x,A)=\min\left\{ t \; : \;\forall \{\alpha_i\}, \;\exists y\in A \;\mathrm{such}\;\mathrm{that}\; \sum_{i=1}^n \alpha_i \mathbbm{1}\{x_i\not = y_i\}\leq t\left(\sum_{i=1}^n \alpha_i ^2 \right)^{\frac{1}{2}}\right\}$$ The similarity between $d_T(x,A)$ and Hamming's distance $$d_H(x,A)=\inf_{y\in A} \sum_{i=1}^n \mathbbm{1}(x_i\not = y_i)$$ should be noted.
