We shall encounter below similar situations associated to different classification problems (in a little more general setting: $\Gamma$ will not be in general a finitely generated group).\ The first example (our baby example) is the category of local meromorphic regular-singular connections, or equivalently the category ${\mathcal D}^{(0)}_f$ of regular singular $\mathcal D$-modules, where $\mathcal D={{\mathbf C}(\{z\})}[d/dz]$ (${{\mathbf C}(\{z\})}$ is the field of fractions of ${{\mathbf C}\{z\}}$).
