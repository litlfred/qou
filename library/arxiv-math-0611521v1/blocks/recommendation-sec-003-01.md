Moreover, we shall assume the following normalisation due to Birkhoff and Guenther (see [@RSZ], [@JSSTO]): $$\forall i < j \;,\; \text{~all coefficients of $U_{i,j}$ belong to~} \sum_{\mu_{i} \leq d < \mu_{j}} {\mathbf C}z^{d}.$$ Then, putting $A'_{i} = c^{\mu_{i}} A_{i} \in GL_{r_{i}}({\mathbf C})$ and $U'_{i,j} = (z/c)^{-\mu_{i}} \theta_{c}^{\mu_{j} -\mu_{i}} U_{i,j} \in {\text{Mat}}_{r_{i},r_{j}}({\mathcal{O}({\mathbf C}^{*})})$, we have: $$A'_{U'} \underset{def}{=} T_{c,A_{0}}[A_{U}] = \begin{pmatrix} A'_{1} & \ldots & \ldots & \ldots & \ldots \\ \ldots & \ldots & \ldots & U'_{i,j} & \ldots \\ 0 & \ldots & \ldots & \ldots & \ldots \\ \ldots & 0 & \ldots & \ldots & \ldots \\ 0 & \ldots & 0 & \ldots & A'_{k} \end{pmatrix}.$$ Now, if the images in ${\mathbf{E}_{q}}$ of the spectra $Sp(A'_{i})$ are pairwise disjoint, there is a unique $F' \in {\mathfrak{G}}({\mathcal{O}({\mathbf C}^{*})})$ such that $F'[A'_{0}] = A'_{U'}$.
