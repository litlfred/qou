The field of constants of ${\mathcal{M}}({\mathbf C}^{*},0)$ and that of ${\mathcal{M}}({\mathbf C}^{*})$ can be identified with a field of elliptic functions, the field ${\mathcal{M}}({\mathbf{E}_{q}})$ of meromorphic functions over the complex torus ${\mathbf{E}_{q}}= {\mathbf C}^{*}/q^{{\mathbf Z}}$.\ We shall write $\overline{a} = \pi(a) \in {\mathbf{E}_{q}}$ for the image of $a \in {\mathbf C}^{*}$ by the natural projection $\pi: {\mathbf C}^{*} \rightarrow {\mathbf{E}_{q}}$, and $[a;q] = a q^{{\mathbf Z}} = \pi^{-1}(\overline{a}) \subset {\mathbf C}^{*}$ for the preimage of $\overline{a}$ in ${\mathbf C}^{*}$, a discrete $q$-spiral.
