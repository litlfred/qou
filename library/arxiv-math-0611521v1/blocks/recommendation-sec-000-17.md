The geometric meaning of the problem is clear: $St_d$ corresponds to a loop around a whole bunch of points: all the $(q,{\bf d})$ corresponding to all the $q\in \frac{1}{z}{\mathbf C}[\frac{1}{z}]$ admitting $d$ as a line of maximal decay for $e^q$ (we shall say in that case that $q$ is supported by $d$ and note $(q,{\bf d})\in d$), but a "good" fundamental group must allow loops around each *individual* point $(q,{\bf d})$.
