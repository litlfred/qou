relations in Proposition 2.6. The resulting formula will turn out to be a q-analogue
of Graf’s addition formula and, at the same time, of the discontinuous integral
of Weber and Schafheitlin.

type addition formula part can also be done for the q-Bessel functions of Jackson
and Ismail, by similar methods, which go back to Heine, the founding father of
