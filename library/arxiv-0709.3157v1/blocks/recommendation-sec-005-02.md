The following results are quoted $$\frac{f_{V\,1}^{\perp}}{f_{V\,1}}\left(1\,\mbox{GeV}\right)\,=\,0.75\pm0.14\,,\quad \lim_{n\to\infty}\left| \frac{f_{V\,n}^{\perp}}{f_{V\,n}}\right| \sim \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\,.$$ An intriguing result found in Ref.
