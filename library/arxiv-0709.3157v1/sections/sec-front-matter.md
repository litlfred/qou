Chiral Lagrangians with tensor sources
1
V. Mateu
Departament de Física Teòrica, IFIC, Universitat de València-CSIC
Apt. Correus 22085, E-46071 València, Spain.
Abstract. The implementation of tensor sources in Chiral Lagrangians allows the computation of
Green functions and form factors involving tensor currents, that is, quark bilinears of the form
¯qi σ µν q j. Whereas only four new terms show up at O(p4), we ﬁnd around a hundred of them
at O(p6). So it becomes essential to ensure that this set o operators is indeed minimal and non–
redundant (i.e., it is a basis). We discuss two phenomenological applications in the context of vector
meson resonances and the radiative pion decay.
Keywords: Chiral Lagrangians, Nonperturbative Effects, Spontaneous Symmetry Breaking, QCD.
