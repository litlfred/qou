We have included tensor sources in the χPT formalism and explicitly constructed the
O(p4) and O(p6) Lagrangians. The challenging part of this construction is t o ﬁnd a
complete and non–redundant set of operators. For the reduct ion of the basis we have
followed standard procedures. We have demonstrated that th ere is no odd–intrinsic–
parity sector with tensor sources at the O(p6) level.
This formalism has proved to be useful for a better understan ding of vector–meson
spectroscopy and also for disentangling a possible tensor i nteraction beyond the SM in
the radiative pion decay process.
