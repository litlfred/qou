In the case $\k=\C$ , assuming that $f$ is convergent near zero section, the value $MF_x(f)$ at a point $(x,\epsilon)\in X(\C)\times \C^\ast$ should be thought of as a representative of the alternating sum $$\sum_i(-1)^i[H^i(f^{-1}(\varepsilon)\cap B_{0,x})]$$ where $\varepsilon\ne 0$ is a sufficiently small complex number and $B_{0,x}$ is a small open ball around $0$ in the fiber $V_x$.
