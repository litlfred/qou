We prove the universality of correlation functions of chiral unitary and unitary ensembles of ran-
dom matrices in the microscopic limit. The essence of the proof consists in reducing the three-term
recursion relation for the relevant orthogonal polynomials into a Bessel equation governing the
local asymptotics around the origin. The possible physical interpretation as the universality of
soft spectrum of the Dirac operator is brieﬂy discussed.
