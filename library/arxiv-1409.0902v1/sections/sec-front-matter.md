COCENTERS AND REPRESENT A TIONS OF AFFINE HECKE
ALGEBRA
DAN CIUBOTARU AND XUHUA HE
Abstract. In this paper, we study the relation between the cocenter and
the representation theory of aﬃne Hecke algebras. The appro ach is based
on the interaction between the rigid cocenter, an important subspace of the
cocenter, and the dual object in representation theory, the rigid quotient of
the Grothendieck group of ﬁnite dimensional representatio ns.
