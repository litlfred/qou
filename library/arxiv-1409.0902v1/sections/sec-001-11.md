A familiar object in the literature is the elliptic quotient ¯R0(H) obtained by tak-
ing the quotient of R(H)C by the span of all proper parabolically induced modules
[BDK]. The elliptic representation theory of reductive p-adic groups and associated
Hecke algebras has been an area of active research, e.g., [Ar], [BDK], [Be], [Kaz1],
[SS], [Re], [OS2]. The dual object to ¯R0(H) is the elliptic cocenter ¯Hell, the sub-
space of ¯H on which all proper parabolically induced modules vanish. However, as
