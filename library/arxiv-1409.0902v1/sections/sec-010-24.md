3. Clifford Theory for H ⋊⟨δ⟩
In this section, we consider the aﬃne Hecke algebras H together with an auto-
morphism δ and the Cliﬀord theory. Notice that even if one is just interested in
the representation theory of H, automorphisms of its parabolic subalgebras appear
in the study of induction and restriction functors. See also §4.
