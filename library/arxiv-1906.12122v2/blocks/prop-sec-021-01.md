Resumming effective range corrections to all orders in the deuteron sector, \textcite{Bedaque:1997qi} calculated the scattering length to be $6.33~\fm$, in very good agreement with the experimental value {6.35(2)~\fm~\cite{Dilg:1971ab}.} {A perturbative treatment of effective-range corrections according to Eq.~\eqref{eq:ERE-d} gives $(5.09 + 0.98 + 0.21 = 6.28)~\fm$ to \NNLO, with an estimated 3\% uncertainty.
