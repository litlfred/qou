At N$^2$LO we must consider two insertions of $C_2$ and one insertion of $C_4$; the latter is determined entirely in terms of $r_0^2$, the shape parameter emerging at N$^3$LO~\cite{Kaplan:1998tg,Kaplan:1998we,vanKolck:1998bw}. {Generally, enhancements depend on the partial waves involved.} The interactions contributing to such waves are operators in the ``$\cdots$'' of Eq.~\eqref{eq:L-NN-simple} that make $T$ dependent on the scattering angle.
