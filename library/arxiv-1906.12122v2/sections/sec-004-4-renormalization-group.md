the beta function will in general change as one goes to
higher orders. Thus the expansion in Pionless EFT can
be thought of as an expansion around the unitary limit
of inﬁnite scattering length, similar to the expansion in
Chiral EFT around the chiral limit of vanishing quark

other hand, the coupling C0 is independent of µ (Kaplan
et al., 1996). In this scheme the unitary limit cannot be
reached for any ﬁnite value of the coupling.
