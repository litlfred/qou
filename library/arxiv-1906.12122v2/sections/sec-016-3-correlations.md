EFTs in general, and Halo EFT in particular, provide
model-independent correlations between diﬀerent observ-
ables. In Pionless EFT, the most prominent universal
correlations were discussed in Secs. II.C.2 and II.C.6.
Such correlations have also proven useful in the analy-
sis of universal properties of ultracold atoms (Braaten
and Hammer, 2006).

These expressions can be interpreted as correlations be-
tween scattering observables and electromagnetic prop-
erties. Analogously, there are correlations between dif-
ferent electromagnetic observables.
