The nuclear physics landscape has been redesigned as a sequence of eﬀective ﬁeld theories
(EFTs) connected to the Standard Model through symmetries and lattice simulations
of Quantum Chromodynamics (QCD). EFTs in this sequence are expansions around
diﬀerent low-energy limits of QCD, each with its own characteristics, scales, and ranges
of applicability regarding energy and number of nucleons. We review each of the three
main nuclear EFTs—Chiral, Pionless, Halo/Cluster—highlighting their similarities, dif-
ferences, and connections. In doing so, we survey the structural properties and reactions
of nuclei that have been derived from the ab initio solution of the few- and many-body
problem built upon EFT input.
