In [14] Chapter 6, §4, the notion of manifold in noncommutative geometry was discussed in
terms of Poincar´e duality in KO-homology. In [16] this Poincar´e duality was shown to hold
rationally for the ﬁnite noncommutative geometry used there. We now investigate how the
new ﬁnite noncommutative geometry F considered here behaves with respect to this duality.
