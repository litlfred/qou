The 31 real parameters of (2.46) correspond to the Yukawa parameters in the standard model with neutrino mixing and Majorana mass terms. In fact, the parameters in C3 correspond to the masses of the quarks and the quark mixing angles of the CKM matrix, while the additional parameters of C1 give the lepton masses, the angles of the PMNS mixing matrix and the Majorana mass terms.
