Let A ⊂ALR be an involutive unital subalgebra of ALR. Then the following properties hold. 1. If the restriction of π and π′ to A are disjoint, then there is no oﬀdiagonal Dirac operator for A. 2. If there exists an oﬀdiagonal Dirac for A, then there exists a pair e, e′ of minimal projections in the commutants of π(ALR) and π′(ALR) and an operator T such that e′Te = T̸ = 0 and A ⊂A(T).
