The automorphism group of the exceptional algebra $J^8_3$ is the exceptional group $F_4$ while the subgroup which preserves the representation of the octonions occurring in the elements of $J^8_3$ as elements of $\mathbb C\oplus \mathbb C^3$ is isomorphic to $SU(3)\times SU(3)/\mathbb Z_3$ where the first factor $SU(3)$ should be again identified to the color group $SU(3)_c$ (see Section 3 and [@mdv:2016b] and Section 2.12 of [@yok:2009]).\ In [@tod-dre:2018] it has been suggested that the quantum space corresponding to the Jordan subalgebra $J^8_2=JSpin_9$ of $J^8_3$ is relevant for the description of the fundamental fermions of the Standard Model (i.e. of one generation).
