Jason Parsley, in his thesis (2004), proves Maxwell's equation in the case that the current flow J runs only within some bounded subdomain Ω ⊂ S3 . In that situation, the ∂E/∂t term also reflects the time rate of change of the electric field due to the gradual deposit or removal of charge along the boundary of Ω . His proof of this in left-invariant format inspired our own arguments.
