In the following we shall identify a Young diagram $\lambda$ with the set of its boxes which we regard as a subset of $\mathbb{N}^2$ given by a graphical representation of $\lambda$ according to the French notation; namely, for a partition $\lambda=(\lambda_1,\dots,\lambda_k)$ it is the set $$\label{eq:identifyNatural}
\lambda = \bigcup_{1\leq i\leq k}   \{1,2,\dots,\lambda_i\} \times  \{i\}=
\{(p,q)\in\mathbb{N}^2: 1\leq p \leq \lambda_q\}.$$
