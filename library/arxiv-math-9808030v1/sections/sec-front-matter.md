Planscherel Measure on Eq(2)
H. Ahmedov1 and I. H. Duru2,1
1. TUBITAK, Feza G¨ursey Institute, P.O. Box 6, 81220 Cengelkoy, Istanbul,
Turkey 1.

Abstract
Following the construction of the invariant integral and the scalar product
for the quantum Euclidean group Eq(2), we obtained the full matrix elements
of its unitary irreducible representations from SUq(2) by contraction and then
derived the Planscherel measure.
