2.3. Coxeter elements.
Deﬁnition 2.9. For a root system generated by simple roots ∆= ⟨{ α1, α2, . . . , αr }⟩, any element c =
sα1sα2 · · · sαr is a Coxeter element. The ordering of the roots is irrelevant: any such product of all simple
roots is a Coxeter element. All Coxeter elements have the same order, which is the Coxeter number,
denoted h.
