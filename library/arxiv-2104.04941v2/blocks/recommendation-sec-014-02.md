As with $p$, we shall define $\mu_v$ by pullbacks--in this case, the pullback of the coordinates $x'_w$ and $a'_w$ for vertices $w$ of $\mu_v(Q)$. \begin{align*} \mu_v^{\ast} x'_w &= \begin{cases} x_v^{-1} & \text{if $v' = w$} \\ x_w(1 + x_v^{\sgn \epsilon(v,w)})^{\epsilon(v,w)} & \text{if $v' \ne w$} \\ \end{cases} \\ \mu_v^{\ast} a'_w &= \begin{dcases} \frac{1}{a_v} \left( \prod_{\epsilon(v,z) > 0} a_z^{\epsilon(v,z)} + \prod_{\epsilon(v,z) < 0} a_z^{-\epsilon(v,z)} \right) & \text{if $v' = w$} \\ a_w & \text{if $v' \ne w$} \end{dcases} \end{align*}
