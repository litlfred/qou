The contents of this paper are as follows. Section 2 develops the com-
binatorics needed to adapt Bolthausen’s method to the case o f character
ratios, and then proves an upper bound of Cn −1/ 2. Section 3 then recalls
the Jack α measure on partitions (here α > 0 is a parameter) and why it
is interesting. It then brieﬂy indicates the modiﬁcations t o the Plancherel
case needed to prove a central limit theorem with an error ter m of Cα n−1/ 2,
