Beyond the quantum corrections to processes already descri bed by L2, at next-to-leading order we
will encounter another important feature, namely, the eﬀec tive Wess-Zumino-Witten (WZW) action.
The WZW action provides an eﬀective description of the const raints due to the anomalous Ward
identities. In general, anomalies arise if the symmetries o f the Lagrangian at the classical level are not
supported by the quantized theory after renormalization.
