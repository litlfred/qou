The coefficient $k_5$ is free of higher-order LECs and is given in terms of the axial-vector coupling constant $\texttt{g}_A$ and the pion-decay constant $F$: $$k_5 = \frac{3 \texttt{g}_A^2}{1024\pi^3 F^4}\,\left(16\texttt{g}_A^2-3\right).$$ While the values for both $\texttt{g}_A$ and $F$ should be taken in the chiral limit, we evaluate $k_5$ using the physical values $g_A=1.2695(29)$ and $F_\pi=92.42(26)$ MeV.
