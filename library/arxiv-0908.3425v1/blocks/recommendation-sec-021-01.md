The transformation behavior of the special unitary matrix $U$ of Eqs. ([\[3:1:2:upar\]](#3:1:2:upar){reference-type="ref" reference="3:1:2:upar"}) and ([\[3:1:2:phisu3\]](#3:1:2:phisu3){reference-type="ref" reference="3:1:2:phisu3"}) under $G=\mbox{SU(3)}_L\times\mbox{SU(3)}_R$, parity $P$, and charge conjugation $C$ is given by $$U\stackrel{G}{\mapsto}V_R U V_L^{\dagger},\quad U(\vec{x},t)\stackrel{P}{\mapsto}U^\dagger(-\vec{x},t),\quad U\stackrel{C}{\mapsto}U^T.$$ Given an object $A$ transforming as $V_R A V_L^\dagger$, such as $U$ or $\chi$, the covariant derivative of $A$, $D_\mu A$, is defined as $$\label{3:2:3:kaa} D_\mu A\equiv\partial_\mu A -i r_\mu A+iA l_\mu \mapsto D'_\mu A' = V_R(D_\mu A) V_L^\dagger.$$ The defining property is that the covariant derivative should transform as the object it acts on.
