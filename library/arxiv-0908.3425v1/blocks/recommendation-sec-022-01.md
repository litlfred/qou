The estimate $|\bar{l}_3|\leq 16$ implies that the Gell-Mann-Oakes-Renner relation is indeed a decent starting point, because the contribution of the second term of Eq. ([\[4:10:Mpi2\]](#4:10:Mpi2){reference-type="ref" reference="4:10:Mpi2"}) to the pion mass is approximately given by $$-\frac{\bar{l}_3 M_\pi^2}{64\pi^2 F_\pi^2} M_\pi =-0.054 M_\pi\,\,\mbox{for}\,\, \bar{l}_3=16,$$ i.e., more than 94 % of the pion mass must stem from the quark condensate [@Colangelo:2001sp].
