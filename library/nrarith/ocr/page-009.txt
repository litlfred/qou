4. Short geodesics in arithmetic orbifolds

The main results of this section are Theorem 4.6 and its Corollary, which set strong limits
on the possible lengths of short geodesics in arithmetic cusped hyperbolic orbifolds,
thus giving a necessary condition for arithmeticity. We also describe a conjectural
statement of the same type for closed arithmetic orbifolds, dependent on the “Lehmer
Conjecture” of number theory. We first recall some basic relevant facts about arithmeticity
of Kleinian groups, including the algebraic characterization of arithmeticity given in [MR]
and [R1, Chap. 2].
