Let M = H3/Γ be a manifold (that is, Γ has no torsion). If Cok(H1(∂M; Z) →H1(M; Z)) is ﬁnite of odd order then k(Γ) = Q(tr Γ). In par- ticular, this holds if M is the complement of a link in a Z/2-homology sphere. Proof of Corollary. Note that if P is the subgroup of Γ generated by parabolic elements, then Γ/P = Cok(H1(∂M; Z) →H1(M; Z)), so Γ/Γ(2)P is the largest quotient of Cok(H1(∂M; Z) →H1(M; Z)) of exponent 2. Thus the condition of the corollary is equivalent to: Γ = Γ(2)P . But certainly P ⊆Γk(Γ), so Γ = Γk(Γ), so k(Γ) = Q(tr Γ).
