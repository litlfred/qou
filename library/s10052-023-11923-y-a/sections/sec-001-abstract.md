An algebraic representation of three genera-
tions of fermions with SU(3)C color symmetry based on
the Cayley–Dickson algebra of sedenions S is constructed.
Recent constructions based on division algebras convinc-
ingly describe a single generation of leptons and quarks with
Standard Model gauge symmetries. Nonetheless, an alge-
braic origin for the existence of exactly three generations
has proven difﬁcult to substantiate. We motivate S as a nat-
ural algebraic candidate to describe three generations with
SU(3)C gauge symmetry. We initially represent one genera-
tion of leptons and quarks in terms of two minimal left ideals
of Cℓ(6), generated from a subset of all left actions of the
complex sedenions on themselves. Subsequently we employ
the S3 automorphism of order three, which is an automor-
phism of S but not of O, to generate two additional genera-
tions. Given the relative obscurity of sedenions, efforts have
been made to present the material in a self-contained manner.
