On the other hand, the order two automorphism ϵ does
not generate transformations between the three generations.
Applying ϵ to the ladder operators Ai and A†
i generates an
complementary set of ladder operators satisfying the same
anticommutation relations as Ai and A†
i (since ϵ is an auto-
morphism of Cℓ(8)), and hence can likewise be used to con-
struct minimal ideals. The automorphism ϵ can then be used
to incorporate an additional degree of freedom (perhaps chi-
rality, handedness, or spin). It is not immediately clear at
present however what the most natural physical interpreta-
tion for ϵ is.
