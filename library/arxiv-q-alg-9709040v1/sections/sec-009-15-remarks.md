In general, one should consider bidiﬀerential operators Bi with complex coeﬃcients, as we expect to
associate by quantization self-adjoint operators in a Hilbert space to real-valued classical observables. In
this paper we deal with purely formal algebraic properties of the deformation quantization and work mainly
over the ﬁeld R of real numbers.
Also, it is not clear whether the “deformation quantization” is natural for quantum mechanics. This
question we will discuss in the next paper. A topological open string theory seems to be more relevant.
