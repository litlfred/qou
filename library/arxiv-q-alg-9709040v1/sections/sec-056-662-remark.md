The vanishing of the integral in the lemma from 6.6 has higher -dimensional analogue which is crucial
in the perturbative Chern-Simons theory in the dimension 3, and its generalizations to dimensions ≥ 4 (see
[Ko1]). However, the vanishing of integrals in dimensions ≥ 3 follows from a much simpler fact which is the
existence of a geometric involution making the integral equ al to minus itself. In the present paper we will
use many times such kind of arguments involving involutions .
