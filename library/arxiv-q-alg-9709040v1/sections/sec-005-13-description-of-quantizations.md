Theorem. The set of gauge equivalence classes of star products on a smooth manifold X can be naturally
identiﬁed with the set of equivalence classes of Poisson structures depending formally on ¯h:
α = α(¯h) = α1¯h + α2¯h2 + . . . ∈Γ(X, ∧2TX)[[¯h]], [α, α] = 0 ∈Γ(X, ∧3TX)[[¯h]]
modulo the action of the group of formal paths in the diﬀeomorphism group of X, starting at the identity
diﬀeomorphism.
Any given Poisson structure α(0) gives a path α(¯h) := α(0) · ¯h and by the Theorem from above, a
canonical gauge equivalence class of star products.
