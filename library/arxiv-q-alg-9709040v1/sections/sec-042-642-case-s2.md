Points pi for i ∈S1 ⊂{1, . . . , n} and points qj for j ∈S2 ⊂{1, . . . , m} move close to each other and to
the horizontal line R. The condition is that 2n2+m2−2 ≥0 and n2+m2 ≤n+m−1 where n2 := #S1, m2 :=
#S2. The corresponding stratum is isomorphic to Cn1,m1 × Cn2,m2 where n1 := n −n2, m1 = m −m2 + 1.
The integral of this stratum decomposes into the product of two integrals. It vanishes if the number of edges
of Γ connecting vertices from S1 ⊔S2 is not equal to 2n2 + m2 −2.
