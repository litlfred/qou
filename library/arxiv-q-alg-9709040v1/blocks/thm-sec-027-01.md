This is a version of Kostant-Hochschild-Rosenberg theorem which says that for a smooth aﬃne algebraic variety Y over ﬁeld k of characteristic zero, the Hochschild cohomology of algebra O(Y ) coincides with the space ⊕k≥0Γ(X, ∧kTY )[−k] of algebraic polyvector ﬁelds on Y .
