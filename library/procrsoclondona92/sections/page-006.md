3. The helicity of a single knotted flux tube

If a flux tube is knotted (as for example in figure 3) then its axis C is necessarily
a twisted closed curve in R*, and we cannot avoid consideration of the twist of B
within the tube itself (which, as will become clear, may change as the flux tube is
continuously deformed). It is useful first to define a standard procedure for the
construction of a flux tube of prescribed helicity around any given knot K. Here we
adopt the procedure of Moffatt (1990).
