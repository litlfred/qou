Here we shall study the process $\pi^0 (\eta) \to \gamma \gamma^*$ (and the related one $e^+ e^- \to \omega, \rho^0 \to \pi^0 \gamma$) in the limit of complete vector dominance, *i.e.* assuming that the ${\cal O} (p^6)$ couplings are saturated by the contribution coming from integrating out the spin-1 particles as is the case for the ${\cal O} (p^4)$ chiral Lagrangian couplings [@egpr].
