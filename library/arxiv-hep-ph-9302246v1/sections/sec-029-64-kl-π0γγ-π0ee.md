The decay $K_L \to \pi^0 e^+ e^-$ has received a great deal of attention as a possible candidate for observing CP-violation effects (for a review see Ref. [@pr]). The $K_L$ state consists mostly of a CP-odd state $K_2$ with a small mixing of the CP-even state $K_1$,

$$\label{kl} \matrix{
K_L \simeq K_2\,+\, \epsilon\,K_1
\hfill\cr\cr}$$

where $|\epsilon| \simeq 2.26 \times 10^{-3}$ is the standard CP-violation parameter in $K \to \pi \pi$ decays. This decay is interesting because "direct" CP-violation in the $K_2$-decay amplitude and "indirect" ($\epsilon$ effect) CP-violation branching ratios are estimated to be of the same order [@epr3]-[@fr]. (Of the order of $10^{-11}$ [@ddg; @fr].)

In addition to these CP-violating decay modes there is a two-photon exchange contribution to the transition $K_L \to \pi^0 e^+ e^-$ which is CP-conserving. In order to interpret future experimental measurements of this decay it is crucial to elucidate if this decay mode may compete or not with the CP-violating one-photon exchange contributions. The two-photon exchange decay mode was studied in Ref. [@epr3] at ${\cal O} (p^4)$ in the context of chiral perturbation theory and was shown to be suppressed at this order [@epr3; @dono]. At ${\cal O} (p^6)$ there is a vector meson exchange diagram (shown in Figure [\[fig8\]](#fig8){reference-type="ref" reference="fig8"}) for the CP-conserving two-photon exchange contribution which a priori could be potentially large [@seh] and may compete with the one-photon CP-violating decay modes. We can make a prediction for the absortive ${\cal O} (p^6)$ vector meson dominance (VMD) contribution to the process $K_L \to
\pi^0 \gamma^* \gamma^* \to \pi^0 e^+ e^-$ in the model we are considering. To this VMD prediction one must add further direct weak ${\cal O} (p^6)$ contributions, as was pointed out in Ref. [@epr5]. In this Reference, the whole ${\cal O} (p^6)$ contribution to $K_L \to \pi^0 \gamma^*
\gamma^* \to \pi^0 e^+ e^-$ was estimated by using the so-called "weak deformation" model. Disregarding the ${\cal O} (p^4)$ helicity suppressed contributions, these authors find

$$\label{brk} \matrix{
{\rm BR} \, \left(K_2 \to \pi^0 e^+ e^-\right)
{\displaystyle|}_{\rm Abs} \simeq 4.4 \, a_V^2 \, 10^{-12}
\hfill\cr\cr}$$

with

$$\left|a_V \right| \equiv \frac{\displaystyle 512\, \pi^2\, h_V^2\, m_{K^0}^2}
{\displaystyle 9 \, M_V^2}$$

where $h_V$ is the vector coupling introduced in the Lagrangian in Eq. ([\[47\]](#47){reference-type="ref" reference="47"}) and the intermediate resonance $V$ denotes the $\rho^0$ and $\omega$ vector particles.

With our determination of this coupling and using the set of input parameters in Eq. ([\[param\]](#param){reference-type="ref" reference="param"}), we obtain $h_V\,=$ 0.030 which leads to

$$\label{hv1} \matrix{
\left|a_V\right|\,=\, 0.21 \, .\hfill\cr\cr}$$

If we use the higher value for $g_A\,=\,0.76$, which as we have seen is favoured by the overall data on spin-1 particle decays, then we obtain $h_V\,=$ 0.033 which leads to

$$\label{hv2} \matrix{
 \left|a_V\right|\,=\, 0.25 \, .\hfill\cr\cr}$$

These values for $\left|a_V\right|$ are in good agreement with the different phenomenological estimates [@fr; @epr5] made for the branching ratio in Eq. ([\[brk\]](#brk){reference-type="ref" reference="brk"}).

Recently, a measurement of the branching ratio for the CP-conserving decay $K_L \to \pi^0 \gamma \gamma$ has been reported by the NA31 experiment at CERN [@na31]. This measurement gives the following bounds at 90 $\%$ C.L.,

$$\matrix{ - \,0.32 \,<\,a_V\,< 0.19 \, .
\hfill\cr\cr }$$
