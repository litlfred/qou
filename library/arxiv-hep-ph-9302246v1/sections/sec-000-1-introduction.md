[]{#1 label="1"}

At low-energies the strong, electromagnetic and weak interactions of the lightest pseudoscalar mesons can be described by an effective chiral Lagrangian [@sw; @gl]. This Lagrangian depends on a number of coupling constants which are not fixed by symmetry requirements alone but are, in principle, determined by the dynamics of the underlying QCD theory. Recently, there have been attempts to derive the low-energy effective chiral action of QCD [@bbr; @ert] with and without the inclusion of the low-lying resonances. The lightest vector, axial-vector and scalar resonances play an important rôle in the determination of the low-energy interactions between pseudoscalar mesons in the context of chiral Lagrangians at ${\cal O} (p^4)$ as shown in Ref. [@egpr].

In this work we shall study the chiral Lagrangians for spin-1 particles to ${\cal O} (p^3)$ in the chiral expansion, in the context of the extended Nambu--Jona-Lasinio (ENJL) cut-off model described in Ref. [@bbr]. In particular, we shall calculate the couplings of spin-1 particles to Goldstone bosons and external sources. As an application, we shall then predict the decay rates for the transitions $V \to \pi \gamma$, $V \to \pi \pi \pi$, $V \to \pi \pi
\gamma$, $V \to V' \gamma$, $V \to V' \pi$, $A \to
\pi \pi \pi$, $A \to \pi \gamma$, $A \to V \pi$ and $A \to V \gamma$. Here $V$ and $A$ denote vector and axial-vector particles. We shall also discuss the vector meson dominance predictions for the $\pi^0 \to \gamma \gamma^*$ and $K_L \to \pi^0 \gamma^* \gamma^* \to \pi^0
e^+ e^-$ transitions. The paper is organized as follows. In Section [\[2b\]](#2b){reference-type="ref" reference="2b"} we give a brief summary of what is known about low-energy chiral Lagrangians from general symmetry requirements alone. In Section [\[2\]](#2){reference-type="ref" reference="2"} we summarize some of the ENJL results found in Ref. [@bbr]. In Section [\[3\]](#3){reference-type="ref" reference="3"} we shall then study the anomalous sector of the theory and in Section [\[4\]](#4){reference-type="ref" reference="4"} the non-anomalous sector. Finally, in Section [\[5\]](#5){reference-type="ref" reference="5"} we discuss the phenomenological applications to the decays involving spin-1 particles. The conclusions are given in Section [\[6\]](#6){reference-type="ref" reference="6"}.
