\textit{A Kazhdan-Lusztig basis is unique.}
 \\ A tidy proof of this lemma can be found in \ref{ref2} 

 
\subsubsection{Lemma:} \textit{For every $s \in S,$ the Kazhdan-Lusztig basis element $b_s$ has the form $b_s = \delta_s + v$}
 \\ \textit{Proof:} $\delta_s + v$ satisfies the degree bounded form by definition, and we have 
 $$\overline{\delta_s + v} = \delta^{-1}_s + v^{-1} = \delta_s + (v - v^{-1}) + v^{-1} = \delta_s + v,$$
 Uniqueness of the Kazhdan-Lusztig basis gives us the desired result. \Laughey
