Recall the Kazhdan-Lusztig basis $\{b_w \st w \in W\}$ of the Hecke algebra $\mathcal{H},$ given a Coxeter system $(W,S).$ We want to know what this has to do with a Weyl group $W.$ $\mathcal{H}$ also admits a standard basis $\{\delta_w \st w \in W\},$ and the translation matrix from the KL basis to the standard one is upper triangular with respect to Bruhat order. We have
$$b_x = \sum_{y \le x}h_{y,x}(v)\delta_y,$$
