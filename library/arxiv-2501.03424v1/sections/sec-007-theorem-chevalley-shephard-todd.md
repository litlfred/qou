Theorem (Chevalley-Shephard-Todd)
Let I ⊂S be a subset such that WI = ⟨I⟩⊂W is ﬁnite. Then the ring of WI-invariants
RWI = {x ∈R | w · x = x, ∀w ∈W}
is a polynomial ring, and R is a Z-graded free module over RWI of ﬁnite rank.
