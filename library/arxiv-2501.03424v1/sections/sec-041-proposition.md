Proposition
If W is ﬁnite, and h is reﬂection faithful, the assignment
k ⊗R Hom•
R-gmod-R(B, B′) −→Hom•
gmod-R(k ⊗R B, k ⊗R B′)
is an isomorphism for any Soergel bimodules B, B′.
Before we move into the ﬁnal part of the story, recall that Soergel bimodules give the categori-
ﬁcation of the Hecke algebra H, and that the split Grothendieck group is a Z[v, v−1]-algebra with
the isomorphism
[SBim(h, W)]⊕≃H.
When we descend to Soergel modules, we notice that the category SBim(h, W) is not monoidal.
But we still take SBim(h, W) to be a right module category of SBim(h, W), furnished by
(−) ⊗R (−) : SBim(h, W) × SBim(h, W) −→SBim(h, W).
Thus we take the split Grothendieck group [SBim(h, W)]⊕to be a right [SBim(h, W)]⊕-module.
