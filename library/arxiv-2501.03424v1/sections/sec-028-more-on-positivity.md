Recall the conjecture from earlier of Kazhdan-Lusztig, stating that Kazhdan-Lusztig polynomials have non-negative coefficients. The original proof (done by Kazhdan and Lusztig) required the geometric notion of flag varieties. We'll briefly introduce this here.
