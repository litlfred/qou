2.1.1 Theorem (Chevalley-Shephard-T odd)
Let I⊂ S be a subset such that WI =⟨I⟩⊂ W is ﬁnite. Then the ring of WI -invariants
RWI ={x∈ R| w· x = x,∀w∈ W}
is a polynomial ring, and R is a Z-graded free module over RWI of ﬁnite rank.
2.1.2 Example
Let W be the Weyl group of g = sl2, i.e. W = S2, such that S ={s}, i.e. WI is generated by
a single reﬂection. Letting R = R[α], the W -action is given by s(α) = −α. Thus Rs = R[α2]. It
follows that R has the decomposition
R = Rs⊕ (Rs· α).
More generally, in a Coxeter system any polynomial P ∈ R can be written P = a + bαs, where
a, b∈ Rs. Since deg( αs) = 2 , we have R∼= Rs⊕ Rs(−2) as graded bimodules.
