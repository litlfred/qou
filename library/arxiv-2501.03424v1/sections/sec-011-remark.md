2.3
Grothendieck groups, Soergel’s categorization
Probably the most important technical attribute of Soergel bimodules is their role as the ”cate-
goriﬁcation” of the Hecke algebra, and their connection to the Kazhdan-Lusztig basis of said Hecke
algebra. In pursuit of properly expositing this, we will interface now with one of the many beautiful
constructions in math, the Grothendieck group.
The split Grothendieck group [SBim]⊕of SBim is an abelian group generated by classes [B] of
Soergel bimodules in SBim. We equip the following simple yet universally powerful condition that
[B] = [B′] + [B′′]
where B ≃B′ ⊕B′′. Since SBim is a monoidal category, we can give [SBim]⊕the structure of a
ring by setting [B][B′] = [BB′]. Moreover, by setting v[B] = [B(1)], we give [SBim]⊕the structure
of a Z[v±1]-algebra.
