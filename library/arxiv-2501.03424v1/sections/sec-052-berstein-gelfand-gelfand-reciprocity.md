For a complex semisimple Lie algebra $\g,$ let $\mathcal{C}$ denote the subcategory of $U(\g)$-Mod consisting of modules $V$ admitting finite dimensional weight spaces. $$\mathbf{D}(V) = \bigoplus_{\lambda \in \h^*}V[\lambda]^*.$$
