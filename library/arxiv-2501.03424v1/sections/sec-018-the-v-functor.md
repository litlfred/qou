Soergel’s proof makes use of two important functors. One of them , denoted V, connects the
subcategoryO0 to Soergel modules, which we have just deﬁned in the previous sect ion. In pursuit
of a more mathematical deﬁnition, we consider the fact that a W -invariant polynomial f∈ RWI
passes freely through every tensor product (⊗Rs ) in a Bott-Samelson bimodule BS (w). This implies
two things. One is that f∈ RWI will act the same on the right and left of any Soergel bimodule.
Secondly, if f is of strictly positive degree, it will act as zero on any Soergel module. A deﬁnition
here clariﬁes this acknowledgement:
4.1.1 Deﬁnition
If W is ﬁnite, let RWI
+ be the graded subspace of RWI containing everything of strictly positive
degree. Let IW be the homogenous ideal generated by RWI
+ . The coinvariant algebra of W is the
graded algebra
C = R/IW .
Following from above, IW acts as zero on any Soergel module, hence Soergel modules in gene ral
can be viewed as graded right C-modules.
