Whenever we have, for $n,m\geq 1$, tuples $(a_1,\dots,a_n)$ and $(b_m,\dots, b_1)$ from $\cA$ such that both are centered and cyclically alternating then we have \begin{enumerate} \item If $n\not=m$, then $$\ff_2(a_1\cdots a_n, b_m\cdots b_1)=0.$$ \item If $n=m=1$ and $a\in\cA_i$, $b\in\cA_j$, with $i\not= j$, then $$\ff_2(a,b)=0.$$ \item If $n=m\geq2$, then $$\ff_2(a_1\cdots a_n, b_n\cdots b_1)= \sum_{k=0}^{n-1} \ff_1(a_1b_{1+k})\cdot\ff_1(a_2b_{2+k})\cdots \ff_1(a_nb_{n+k}).$$ \end{enumerate} For a visualization of this formula, one should think of two concentric circles with the $a$'s on one of them and the $b$'s on the other.
