Thus the order of (\ref{generalterm}) is \begin{eqnarray*}\lefteqn{ -(m+n) + \#(\pi_1) + \#(\pi_2) + c }\\ &=& - \big( m + 1 - \#(\pi_1) - \#(\gamma_m\pi_1^{-1})\big) - \big(n + 1 - \#(\pi_2) - \#(\gamma_n\pi_2^{-1}) \big) \\ &&\quad \mbox{} + c + 2 - (r + s) \end{eqnarray*} Hence (\ref{generalterm}) will vanish unless three conditions are satisfied: we must have that $\pi_1$ and $\pi_2$ are non-crossing and $c = r + s -2$, i.e. $\tau$ has one pair and the rest of its blocks are singletons.
