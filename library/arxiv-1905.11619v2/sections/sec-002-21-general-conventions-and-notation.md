Throughout the paper $M$ is a finite von Neumann algebra with a trace $\tau$. Let $\Omega_\tau \in L_2(M) := L_2(M, \tau)$ be the cyclic vector given by $1 \in M$. We denote by $\mathcal{S}_p$ the Schatten-von Neumann class, which is the non-commutative $L_p$-space associated with $B(H)$ and its trace.
