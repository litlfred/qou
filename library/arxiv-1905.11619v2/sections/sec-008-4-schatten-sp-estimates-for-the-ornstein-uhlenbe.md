[]{#Sect=Orhnstein-Uhlenbeck label="Sect=Orhnstein-Uhlenbeck"} We show that $q$-Gaussians $\Gamma_q(H_{\mathbb{R}})$ with the Ornstein-Uhlenbeck semi-group are gradient-$\mathcal{S}_p$ for a range of $q$ depending on $p$ and the dimension of the Hilbert space $H_{\mathbb{R}}$.
