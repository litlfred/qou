If we treat $R_{n,k}^{\ast}$ as a map from $H_q^{\otimes n+k}$ to $H_q^{\otimes n}\otimes H_q^{\otimes k}$, then each summand in [\[Eqn=Tripleproduct\]](#Eqn=Tripleproduct){reference-type="eqref" reference="Eqn=Tripleproduct"} should be thought of as an element of the space $H_{q}^{\otimes n-r-s} \otimes H_q^{\otimes m-s-j} \otimes H_q^{\otimes k-j-r}$ and this norm is bounded by a constant depending on $q$ and $\dim(H)$ (coming from the inner product pairing).
