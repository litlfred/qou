Lines from 28 neutron-capture elements were observed in CS 31082-001 , mainly concentrated in the blue and UV parts of the spectrum. The full linelist for heavy elements is provided in an Appendix to this paper, which also lists the references for our adopted oscillator strengths. When available from the paper of Sneden et al. [@sneden96], the same oscillator strengths were adopted in order to make the comparison to the star CS 22892-052   easier. However, more lines were measured here, hence we had to supplement this compilation with additional information. In particular, we note that new results on the lifetimes and branching factors of both uranium (Nilsson et al. [@nilsson01a]) and thorium (Nilsson et al. [@nilsson01b]) transitions are now available; we make use of them here (Table [1](#T-Th){reference-type="ref" reference="T-Th"}). The Appendix also lists equivalent widths of the lines in CS 31082-001 and the individual derived abundances. In cases when blending was severe, or hyperfine structure was important, abundances were determined by comparing the observations directly to synthetic spectra (in these cases, no equivalent widths are listed in the Appendix). Hyperfine structure was included for the Ba and Eu lines. The mean abundances obtained for each element are listed in Table [2](#T-heavy){reference-type="ref" reference="T-heavy"}. Figs. [\[F-Nb-Ru-Rh\]](#F-Nb-Ru-Rh){reference-type="ref" reference="F-Nb-Ru-Rh"} to [\[F-U\]](#F-U){reference-type="ref" reference="F-U"} are representative examples of the quality of the observed spectrum, and the fits to synthetic spectra.
