[]{#key label="key"}

In this section, we prove that the Lawrence-Krammer representation is faithful. The main ingredient in the proof is the following lemma.

::: lem
Let $N$ be a noodle and let $F$ be a fork. Then $\langle N,F \rangle = 0$ if and only if $T(F)$ is isotopic relative to $\partial D \cup P$ to an arc which is disjoint from $N$.
:::
