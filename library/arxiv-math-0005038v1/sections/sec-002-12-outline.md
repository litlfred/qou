In Section [\[fn\]](#fn){reference-type="ref" reference="fn"} we will define forks and noodles. These will be one-dimensional objects in the disc designed to represent elements of the second homology and cohomology of $\tilde{C}$. We define a pairing between forks and noodles, which will be preserved by any element of the kernel of the Lawrence-Krammer representation.

In Section [\[key\]](#key){reference-type="ref" reference="key"} we prove that pairing between forks and noodles detects geometric intersection between the corresponding edges in the disc. We use this to show that a braid in the kernel of the Lawrence-Krammer representation must be trivial.

In Section [\[lkmatrices\]](#lkmatrices){reference-type="ref" reference="lkmatrices"} we compute the Lawrence-Krammer representation explicitly in terms of generators and basis elements.
