Lemma 3.6. Let α and β be simple closed curves in D \ P which intersect trans-
versely at ﬁnitely many points. The following are equivalen t.
• α is isotopic relative to ∂D ∪ P to a simple closed curve which intersects β at

• α and β cobound a “digon”, that is, an embedded disc in D\P whose boundary
consists of one subarc of α and one subarc of β.
A proof can be found in [PR99, Proposition 3.7], or [FLP91, Proposition 3.10].
Suppose σ ∈ H(D, P ) is a homomorphism representing an element of the kernel
of the Lawrence-Krammer representation. We will show that σ is isotopic relative
to ∂D ∪ P to the identity map.
Take D to be the unit disc centered at the origin in the complex plane, and
take p1, . . . , pn to be real numbers satisfying −1 < p 1 < · · · < p n < 1. For
i = 1, . . . , n− 1, let Ei be the horizontal edge from pi to pi+1. For i = 1, . . . , n, let
Ni be the noodle which winds around pi and no other punctures, intersecting the
real axis twice. See Figure 3.
