BRAID GROUPS ARE LINEAR
STEPHEN J. BIGELOW
Abstract. The braid groups Bn can be deﬁned as the mapping class group
of the n-punctured disc. The Lawrence-Krammer representation of the braid
group is the induced action on a certain twisted second homology of the space C
of unordered pairs of points in the n-punctured disc. Recently, Daan Krammer
showed that this is a faithful representation in the case n = 4. In this paper,
we show that it is faithful for all n.
