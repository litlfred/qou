For any integer n ≥1, there exist inﬁnitely many hyperbolic tunnel number n manifolds with at most one exceptional Dehn ﬁlling. The Berge knots form a class of tunnel number one knots in S3 that admit a lens space Dehn ﬁlling. Thus these knots have at least two exceptional Dehn ﬁllings: the lens space and S3. Baker showed that inﬁnitely many come from Dehn ﬁlling a minimally twisted chain link. It follows from his work that the cusp shapes of these knots limit to a single Euclidean similarity structure; see Proposition 6.1.
