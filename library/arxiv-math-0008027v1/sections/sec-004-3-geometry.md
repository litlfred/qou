In this section I will describe how to decompose a knot complement S3 \ K into
ideal topological tetrahedra and how these deﬁne hyperbolic stru cture when K is
the ﬁgure-eight knot. Here ‘ideal’ means that each vertex is at the cusp (the inﬁnite
point corresponding to K). (That is, all vertices of the tetrahedra meet at a point,
and the complement of the regular neighborhood of the point is S3 \ K.)
Fix a knot diagram throughout.
