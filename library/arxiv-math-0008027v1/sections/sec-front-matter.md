KASHAEV’S INVARIANT AND THE VOLUME OF A
HYPERBOLIC KNOT AFTER Y. YOKOTA
HITOSHI MURAKAMI
Abstract. I follow Y. Yokota to explain how to obtain a tetrahedron de-
composition of the complement of a hyperbolic knot and compare it with the
asymptotic behavior of Kashaev’s link invariant using the ﬁgure-eight knot as
an example.
