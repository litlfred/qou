LANDEN TRANSFORMATIONS AND THE INTEGRATION OF
RATIONAL FUNCTIONS
GEORGE BOROS AND VICTOR H. MOLL
Abstract. We present a rational version of the classical Landen transfor-
mation for elliptic integrals. This is employed to obtain explicit closed-form
expressions for a large class of integrals of even rational functions and to de-
velop an algorithm for numerical integration of these functions.
