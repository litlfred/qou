Note that a (G, H)-cocycle σ on ∆determines a G/H-decoration D on ∆. We say that D is compatible with σ. To see this note that σ is the coboundary of a 0-cochain τ on ∆, which is unique up to left multiplication by an element in G. The value of τ at the vertices near a vertex of ∆are all in the same H-coset. Hence, we have a decoration on ∆. Similarly, a (G, B, H)-cocycle on ∆determines a G/B-decoration on ∆. It follows that the maps Lαβ and Lαβγ are bijective with explicit inverses.
