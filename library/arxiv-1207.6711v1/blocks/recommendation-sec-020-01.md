For general concrete triangulations the Ptolemy coordinates on faces of different simplices must be identified by signs given by the face pairing permutations. \begin{definition}\label{def:PtolemyAssignmentGeneral} A Ptolemy assignment on $(M,\T)$ is a Ptolemy assignment for each simplex of $\T$ such that Ptolemy coordinates on identified faces are identified via the pullback of the permutation matrix.
