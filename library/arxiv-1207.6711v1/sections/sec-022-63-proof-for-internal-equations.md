Lemma 6.10. A Ptolemy assignment on ∆3
n gives rise to a shape assignment satisfying the
internal gluing equations.
Proof. Let t be of the form t = α + 1111 with α∈ ∆3
n−4(Z). The gluing equation for t
involves
(6.9) z0011
α+1100z0101
α+1010z0110
α+1001z1001
α+0110z1010
α+0101z1100
α+0011.
When expanding this using (6.1), the two signs cancel and the numerator and denominator
both consist of all Ptolemy coordinates cα+β where β is a permutation of (0, 1, 1, 2). Hence,
the product is 1. □
Corollary 6.11. Theorem 6.3 holds for the internal gluing equations. □
