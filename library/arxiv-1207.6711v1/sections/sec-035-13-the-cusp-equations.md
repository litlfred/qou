Lemma 13.2. The representation ρ determined (up to conjugation) by σ is boundary-
unipotent if and only if the cusp equation at each level is satisﬁed for each edge path repre-
senting a generator of the fundamental group of a boundary component of ∂M .
Proof. By deﬁnition, ρ is boundary-unipotent if and only if for each closed edge path λ in
∂M , the product of the labelings of edges traversed by λ is inN. This proves the result. □
