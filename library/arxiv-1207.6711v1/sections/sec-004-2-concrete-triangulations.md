Remark 1.5. In Garoufalidis-Thurston-Zickert [11] we deﬁned the volume (in fact, complex
volume) of a boundary-unipotent SL(n, C)-representation and gave an explicit formula using
the Ptolemy coordinates. Similary, one can deﬁne the volume of a decorated PGL( n, C)-
representation by adding the volumes of each of the shapes. The volume is an invariant of
a decorated PGL(n, C)-representation (in the sense of Remark 8.5), but we do not know if
the volume is independent of the decoration. This is non-trivial even for n = 2, where it was
ﬁrst proved by Francaviglia [9]. We shall not deal with this here.
