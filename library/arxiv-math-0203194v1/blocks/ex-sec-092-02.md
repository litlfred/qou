Already in case n = 2, the structure of ∆= ∆2 is fairly complicated. For a ﬁxed vertex Λ, the set of all vertices adjacent of Λ is divided in two; one for those corresponding to q2 + q + 1 k-rational points in P2 k and the other for those corresponding to q2 + q + 1 k-rational lines in P2 k. So it is convenient to consider the surface B which is obtained by blowing-up of P2 k at every k-rational points. Two vertices adjacent to Λ forms with Λ a 2-simplex if and only if the corresponding lines in B intersect.
