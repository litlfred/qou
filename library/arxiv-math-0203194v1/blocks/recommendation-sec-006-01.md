\begin{para}\label{para:p-adic-manifold} {\itshape $p$-adic manifolds.} We shall be mainly concerned with paracompact (strictly) $K$-analytic spaces $S$ which satisfy the following assumption: \begin{quote} any $s\in S$ has a neighborhood $U(s)$ which is isomorphic to an affinoid subdomain of some space $V_s$ which admits locally an \'etale morphism to the affine space $\mathbb{A}^{\dim S}$. \end{quote} For convenience, we shall call such a space $S$ a {\it $K$-manifold}\index{Kmanifold@$K$-manifold}, and simply a {\it $p$-adic manifold}\index{padicmanifold@$p$-adic manifold} if $K=\C_p$.
