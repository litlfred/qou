Although we shall not use this fact, let us mention that the ``Dieudonn\'e functor'' ${\D}$ from $p$-divisible groups on $S_0$ to $F$-crystals is fully faithful under further mild assumptions on $S_0$ (and even an equivalence with values in the category of ``Dieudonn\'e crystals'', \ie finite locally free $F$-crystals for which $F$ admits an inverse up to $p$ --- the Verschiebung); without any further assumption on $S_0$, it induces an equivalence of categories between ind-etale $p$-divisible groups on $S_0$ and unit-root $F$-crystals (\ie finite locally free $F$-crystals for which $F$ is an isomorphism), \cf \cite{tddc1}, \cite{jong95:_cryst_dieud}. \end{para}
