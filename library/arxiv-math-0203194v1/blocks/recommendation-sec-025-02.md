The canonical $\ovl{\Q}$-subspace of $\; \mathrm{H}^1_{\mathrm{crys}}((A_0)_{\ovl{\F}_p}/\ovl{\Q}_p)\;$ defined in \ref{betti-la} will be denoted by \begin{equation*} \mathrm{H}^1_{\mathrm{B}}(A_{\C_p}, \ovl{\Q}) \subset \mathrm{H}^1_{\mathrm{crys}}\bigl((A_0)_{\ovl{\F}_p}/\ovl{\Q}_p\bigr) \end{equation*} because, as we shall see, it shares many properties with the Betti space $\mathrm{H}^1_{\mathrm{B}}(A_\C, \ovl{\Q})= \mathrm{H}^1_{\mathrm{B}}(A_\C,\Z)\otimes \ovl{\Q}$ of a complex abelian variety $A_\C$).
