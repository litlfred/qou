If instead $p$ is critical (but still {\it does not divide $N$}), as we shall assume henceforth, \v{C}erednik's theorem shows that ${\mathcal{X}^+(N)}_{\C_p}$ is a {\it Mumford curve} uniformized by the Drinfeld space $\Omega_{\C_p}=\P^1_{\C_p}\setminus \P^1(\Q_p)$ (at least if $N$ is large enough); in fact, $\Sh$ becomes a Mumford curve over some finite unramified extension of $\Q_p$ (note that $\Q(\zeta_N)\subset {\Q_p^{\mathrm{ur}}}$).
