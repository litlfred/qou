3. Local and global monodromy of p-adic diﬀerential equations.
Abstract: We brieﬂy review and compare the theory of singularities of ordi-
nary linear diﬀerential equations in the formal, complex an d p-adic contexts. We
then outline the Christol-Mebkhout theory of p-adic slopes of diﬀerential modules
over annuli. There is a close relation with Galois represent ations of local ﬁelds of
characteristic p, expressed by Crew’s p-adic local monodromy conjecture, which
has been recently proved [ And02b]. Complications occuring in the p-adic context
reﬂect the fact that the ´ etale fundamental groups of annuli are non-abelian.
We then deﬁne and study the non-archimedean Riemann-Hilber t functor, which
attaches a vector bundle with integrable connection to any c ontinuous representa-
tion with discrete coimage of the ´ etale fundamental group o f a non-archimedean
manifold. Connections in the image are characterized by the fact that the ´ etale
sheaf of germs of solutions is locally constant.
