In a connected rigid analytic variety S, one has the following avatar of
analytic continuation [ Be96, 0.1.13]: assume that there is no admissible
covering formed by two disjoint non-empty open subsets, and let f be an
analytic function on S. If there is a connected open subset U over which f
vanishes, then f = 0.
