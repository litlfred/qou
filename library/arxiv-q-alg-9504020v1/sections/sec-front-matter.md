Abstract
The link invariant, arising from the cyclic quantum dilogarithm via the
particular R-matrix construction, is proved to coincide with the invariant
of triangulated links in S3 introduced in [14]. The obtained invariant, like
Alexander-Conway polynomial, vanishes on disjoint union of links. The
R-matrix can be considered as the cyclic analog of the universal R-matrix
associated with Uq(sl(2)) algebra.
