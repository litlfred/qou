According to [@K], cyclic quantum dilogarithm leads to invariants of triangulated links in triangulated three-manifolds. It was conjectured that these invariants are independent of triangulations used. The $R$-matrix derivation, presented in this paper (Sect.[\[sec2\]](#sec2){reference-type="ref" reference="sec2"}), proves the conjecture for the case of three-sphere. The essential part of the proof is the realization of the $R$-matrix ([\[R-matrix\]](#R-matrix){reference-type="ref" reference="R-matrix"}) as a three-dimensional partition function ([\[partfun\]](#partfun){reference-type="ref" reference="partfun"}), ([\[octahed\]](#octahed){reference-type="ref" reference="octahed"}) for the triangulated three-ball with properly chosen tangle in it.

The $R$-matrix ([\[R-matrix\]](#R-matrix){reference-type="ref" reference="R-matrix"}) can be considered as a cyclic counterpart of the universal $R$-matrix for the algebra $U_q(sl(2))$. The factorized structure of the matrix elements ([\[R\]](#R){reference-type="ref" reference="R"}), ([\[4W\]](#4W){reference-type="ref" reference="4W"}) probably reflects the fact that this $R$-matrix can be obtained in some limit from the chiral Potts model $R$-matrix [@BPA].
