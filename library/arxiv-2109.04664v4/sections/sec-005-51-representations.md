Let $X$ be the complement of the open tubular neighborhood of $E\subset S^3$. The fundamental group $\pi_1(X)$ is presented as $$\langle
    x,y
    \mid
    wx=yw
  \rangle,$$ with $w:=xy^{-1}x^{-1}y$. Let $\rho$ be a non-Abelian representation of $\pi_1(X)$ to $\rm{SL}(2;\mathbb{C})$ given by $$\label{eq:presentation_F_2}
  \rho(x)
  =
  \begin{pmatrix}e^{\xi/2}&1\\0&e^{-\xi/2}\end{pmatrix},
  \quad
  \rho(y)
  =
  \begin{pmatrix}e^{\xi/2}&0\\-d&e^{-\xi/2}\end{pmatrix},$$ where $d$ annihilates the Riley polynomial $$\label{eq:Riley}
  d^2-(2\cosh(\xi)-3)d-2\cosh(\xi)+3.$$ The preferred longitude $\lambda$ is presented by $y^{-1}xyx^{-2}yxy^{-1}$ and it is sent to $$\rho(\lambda)
  =
  \begin{pmatrix}
    e^{\eta/2}&\ast \\
    0          &e^{-\eta/2}
  \end{pmatrix},$$ where $$\begin{gathered}
  \eta
  :=
  \log
  \left(
    \frac{1}{2}
    \left(\vphantom{\frac{1}{2}}
      e^{2\xi}-e^{\xi}-e^{-\xi}+e^{-2\xi}-2
    \right.
  \right.
  \\
  \left.
    \left.
      +(e^{-\xi}-e^{\xi})\sqrt{(e^{\xi}+e^{-\xi}-3)(e^{\xi}+e^{-\xi}+1)}
    \right)
  \right).
\end{gathered}$$
