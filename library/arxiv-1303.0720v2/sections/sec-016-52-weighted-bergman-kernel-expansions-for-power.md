It is – as mentioned previously – generally speaking a diﬃcult task to obtain the weighted
Bergman kernel explicitly.
Instead, we typically need to resort to approximate formulae.
Provided that the potential Q is suﬃciently smooth with ∆Q > 0, the weighted Bergman
kernel has an asymptotic expansion (up to an error term of order O(m−k))
