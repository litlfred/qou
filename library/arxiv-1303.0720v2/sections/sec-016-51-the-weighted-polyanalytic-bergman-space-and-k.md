[]{#subsec-wpbspw label="subsec-wpbspw"} As before, $\Omega$ is a domain in ${\mathbb C}$, and we consider weights of the form $\omega=\mathrm{e}^{-2mQ}$, where $Q$ is a real-valued potential (function) on $\Omega$ and $m$ a scaling parameter, which is real and positive, and we are interested in the asymptotics as $m\to+\infty$. By abuse of notation, we may at times refer to $Q$ as a weight. We may interpret this family as formed by the powers of an initial weight $\mathrm{e}^{-Q}$. We will think of the potential $Q$ as fixed, and to simplify the notation, we write $\mathrm{A}^2_m$ in place of $\mathrm{A}^2(\Omega,\mathrm{e}^{-2mQ})$. Likewise, the reproducing kernel of $\mathrm{A}^2_m$ will be written $K_m$. More generally, we are also interested in the weighted Bergman spaces of polyanalytic functions $\mathrm{PA}^2_{q}(\Omega,\mathrm{e}^{-2mQ})$, which we simplify notationally to $\mathrm{PA}^2_{q,m}$. The associated polyanalytic Bergman kernel will be written $K_{q,m}$. Hopefully this notation will not lead to any confusion. The related kernel $$\tilde K_{q,m}(z,w)=K_{q,m}(z,w)\mathrm{e}^{-m[Q(z)+Q(w)]},
\label{eq-corrkern1}$$ known as the *correlation kernel*, then acts boundedly on $L^2(\Omega)$. We may think of both $m$ and $q$ as quantization parameters, because we would generally expect the integral operator associated with [\[eq-corrkern1\]](#eq-corrkern1){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq-corrkern1"} to tend to the identity as $m$ or $q$ tends to infinity. When $q$ tends to infinity, this is related to the question of approximation by polynomials in $z$ and $\bar z$ in the weighted space $L^2(\Omega,\mathrm{e}^{-2mQ})$. When instead $m$ tends to infinity, "geometric" properties of $\Omega$ and $Q$ become important; e.g., we would need to ask that $\varDelta Q\ge0$.
