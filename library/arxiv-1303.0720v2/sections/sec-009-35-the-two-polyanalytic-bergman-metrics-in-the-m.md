To make this presentation as simple as possible, we now focus our attention on the biholomorphic (bianalytic) case $q=2$. Then $$\mathbf{E}_{\otimes2}[K_2](z,z';z,z')=
4\frac{1-|z'|^2}{(1-|z|^2)^3}+6\frac{|z-z'|^2}{(1-|z|^2)^4},$$ and if we substitute $z'=z+\epsilon$, the result is $$\begin{gathered}
\mathbf{E}_{\otimes2}[K_2](z,z+\epsilon;z,z+\epsilon)=
4\frac{1-|z+\epsilon|^2}{(1-|z|^2)^3}+6\frac{|\epsilon|^2}{(1-|z|^2)^4}
\\
=4\frac{1}{(1-|z|^2)^2}
-4\frac{\bar\epsilon z+\epsilon\bar z}{(1-|z|^2)^3}
+|\epsilon|^2\frac{2+4|z|^2}{(1-|z|^2)^4},
\end{gathered}$$ so that Bergman's first metric [\[eq-bm101\]](#eq-bm101){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq-bm101"} becomes $$\mathrm{d}s_{2,\omega,\epsilon}^{\text{\ding{174}}}(z)^2
=\bigg\{4\frac{1}{(1-|z|^2)^2}
-8\frac{\mathop{\mathrm{Re}}[\bar\epsilon z]}{(1-|z|^2)^3}
+|\epsilon|^2\frac{2+4|z|^2}{(1-|z|^2)^4}\bigg\}|\mathrm{d}z|^2, 
\label{eq-temp1}$$ indexed by $\epsilon$. The corresponding $2\times2$ matrix $$2
\begin{pmatrix}
2(1-|z|^2)^{-2} & -2z(1-|z|^2)^{-3}
\\
-2\bar z(1-|z|^2)^{-3} & (1+2|z|^2)(1-|z|^2)^{-4}
\end{pmatrix}$$ is then positive definite (this fact generalizes to arbitrary $q$ as we mentioned previously).

A more involved calculation shows that Bergman's second polyanalytic metric [\[eq-bpam2\]](#eq-bpam2){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq-bpam2"} obtains the form $$\mathrm{d}s_{2,\omega,\epsilon}^{\text{\ding{175}}}(z)^2
=\frac{4}{(1-|z|^2)^2}\vert\mathrm{d}z\vert^2\quad (\mathrm{mod}\,
\epsilon,\bar\epsilon),$$ where the modulo is taken with respect to the ideal generated by $\epsilon$ and $\bar\epsilon)$. In this case, this is the same as putting $\epsilon=0$ at the end.

This is actually the same as [\[eq-temp1\]](#eq-temp1){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq-temp1"} to this lower degree of precision.
