[\[binegliprop\]](#binegliprop){reference-type="ref" reference="binegliprop"} is stated for bi-negligible amplitudes (i.e., where $q=2$). However, it is rather clear how to formulate the corresponding result for general $q$. In (i), we get $q$ operators ${\mathbf S},\ldots,{\mathbf S}^{(q-1)}$ instead of ${\mathbf S},{\mathbf S}'$, and in (ii), we get a product of $q$ copies of the operator $\boldsymbol{\nabla}$ interlaced with $q-1$ copies of the multiplication operator ${\mathbf M}_{\bar\partial_w\theta}$.
