In view of the negligible amplitude calculation [\[nablanegli\]](#nablanegli){reference-type="eqref" reference="nablanegli"}, the approximate reproducing property (with a worse precision) will be kept if we replace $M_m$ by the kernel $$K^{\langle k\rangle}_{m}(z,w):=\Lfun^{\langle k\rangle}(z,w)\,\mathrm{e}^{2mQ(z,w)},$$ where $$\Lfun^{\langle k\rangle}:=m\Lfun_0+\Lfun_1+\cdots+m^{-k+1} \Lfun_k \label{eq-Lfunexp1}$$ is a finite asymptotic expansion (where each term $\Lfun_j$ is independent of $m$), with $$\Lfun^{\langle k\rangle}= \frac{2m}{\pi}\bar\partial_w \theta + \bar\partial_w\theta \, [\boldsymbol{\nabla}X]^{\langle k\rangle}. \label{negliampeq}$$ Here, $X\sim X_0+m^{-1}X_1+m^{-2}X_2+\ldots$ is an asymptotic expansion in $m$ (where every $X_j$ is independent of $m$), and each $X_j(z,w)$ should be holomorphic in $z$ and $C^\infty$-smooth in $w$.
