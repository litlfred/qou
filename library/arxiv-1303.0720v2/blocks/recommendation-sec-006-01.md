The curvature form for the original metric [\[eq-metric1\]](#eq-metric1){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq-metric1"} is (up to a positive constant factor) given by $$\varkappa:=-\varDelta\log\omega(z)\mathrm{d}A(z), \label{eq-curv1}$$ The curvature form for Bergman's first metric [\[eq-Bmetric1\]](#eq-Bmetric1){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq-Bmetric1"} is similarly $$\varkappa^{\text{\ding{172}}}:=-\varDelta\log[K(z,z)\omega(z)]\mathrm{d}A(z) =-[\varDelta\log K(z,z)+\varDelta\log\omega(z)]\mathrm{d}A(z), \label{eq-curv2}$$ and we are led to propose the difference $$\varkappa-\varkappa^{\text{\ding{172}}}=(\varDelta\log K(z,z))\mathrm{d}A(z), \label{eq-curv3}$$ as the two-dimensional volume form of a metric, *Bergman's second metric*: $$\mathrm{d}s_\omega^{\text{\ding{173}}}(z)^2:=\varDelta\log K(z,z)|\mathrm{d}z|^2. \label{eq-Bmetric2}$$ We should remark that unless $K(z,z)\equiv0$, the function $z\mapsto\log K(z,z)$ is subharmonic in $\Omega$.
