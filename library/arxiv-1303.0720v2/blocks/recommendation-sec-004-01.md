Alongside with the kernel $K_q$, we should also be interested in its *lift* $$\mathbf{E}_{\otimes2}[K_q](z,z';w,w'):= \sum_{j=1}^{+\infty}\mathbf{E}[\phi_j](z,z')\overline{\mathbf{E}[\phi_j](w,w')}, \qquad (z,z',w,w')\in\Omega\times{\mathbb C}\times\Omega\times{\mathbb C}. \label{eq-bk3}$$ The lifted kernel $\mathbf{E}_{\otimes2}[K_q]$ is also independent of the choice of basis, just like the kernel $K_q$ itself.
