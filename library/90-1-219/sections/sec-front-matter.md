It is shown that the Dirac operator becomes 2 x 2 matrix acting on the space of chiral fermions 
in the previously·proposed quaternionic formulation of the Dirac theory. The interaction part of 
thus· obtained Dirac operator formally corresponds to the 2 x 2 matrix of the generalizedone·form of 
Coquereaux et al. in the non-commutative geometric approach to the electro-weak unification. It is 
pointed out that the mathematical formulation of Coquereaux et al. allows to introduce an arbitrary 
parameter which can be reduced to unity unless it vanishes and whose presence spoils a unique 
construction of Yang-Mills-Higgs theory in their approach. For instance, one may choose the 
parameter to vanish, resulting in only pure Yang-Mills theory. It is also pointed out that the original 
model construction of Coquereaux et al. corresponds to employ a quaternion algebra which is 
isomorphic to but totally independent of that used in the Dirac theory. According to this model 
construction the Higgs boson mass is predicted to be the same as that of the gauge boson in a toy 
model with U(l) X U(l) down to U(l). Bosonic sector of the Weinberg-Salam theory is also 
constructed along the same line using quaternions with components being 2 x 2 matrices. The 
construction is unique without recourse to SU(2l1) and predicts mH=,fimw and sin28w=1/4 at the 
classical level. 
§ 1. Introduction 
Recently several peoplel)-6) have opened up a new territory to reformulate the 
standard model based on the ideas of the non-commutative geometry.l) To state as 
simple as possible the reformulation consists of regarding the gauge and Higgs fields 
as a single, generalized Yang-Mills fit;ld without postulating higher dimensional 
space-time as in the Kaluza-Klein theory but by staying in the ordinary 4-dimensional 
space-time with some sort of discrete structure. This approach is expectedl)-6) to 
bring forth new constraints on the otherwise-arbitrary parameters in the standard 
model without imagining hypothetical space-time structure and/or introducing 
numerous new elementary particles. 
As a concrete realization of the ideas in the electro-weak sector7) of the standard 
model, which does not presuppose any detailed knowledge of the non-commutative 
geometryl) and on which we focus in this paper, Coquereaux et al.3) introduced 
generalized one-form constituting 2 X 2 matrix and invented new algebra by defining 
the product 8 and the matrix derivative dm. The most intriguing point of Ref. 3) is 
that the unification of gauge and Higgs fields based on the new associative, non-
commutative algebra automatically leads to the Yang-Mills-Higgs theory and makes 
a clear prediction on the gauge and Higgs boson masses: the ratio is 12 in a toy model 
with U(I) x U(I) down to U(I). *) Extension to more realistic models using the 
algebra 5U(211) necessitates 3 X 33),8) or 4 X 43) matrix formalisms, but the original 2 X 2

In the course of studying the new algebra we find that their mathematical formu-
lation is closely related to quaternions in two different aspects. One is the chiral 
fermions, and the other the nontrivial prediction of the mass and coupling constant 
relations. Almost a decade ago we presented9) the quaternionic formulation of the 
Dirac theory where the presence of the mass term necessitates the decomposition of 
the spinor-quaternion into the left ideals corresponding to the chiral fermions. The 
spinor-quaternion is a single-component Dirac spinor defined over the biquaternions 
Q. It is naturally derived10) from the 2-component formalism of the Dirac spinor 
defined over real quaternions H. In the next section we succinctly review the 
formalism of Refs. 9) and 10) to show that the Dirac operator defined over Q becomes 
2 x 2 matrix acting on the space of the chiral fermions, whose interaction part for-
mally corresponds to the generalized one-form in Ref. 3). Relation to the conven-
tional 4-component notation will scarcely be mentioned because it was fully discussed 
in Refs. 9) and 10).
