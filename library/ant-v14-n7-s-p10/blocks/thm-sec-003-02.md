If α ∈Q(µ2∞) is nonzero and not a root of unity, then h(α) ≥(log 2)/4. In particular, B0(Q(µ2∞)) ≥(log 2)/4. Basically all of the ideas needed to prove this result are present in Proposition 2 of [Amoroso and Dvornicich 2000], which treats the cyclotomic ﬁelds Q(ζk) for 4 | k. The statement of the height bound in that result excludes certain elements of Q(ζk), but we can easily recover the bound for these excluded elements in the special case that k = 2m. As it may be of some interest, we include the complete proof in this case.
