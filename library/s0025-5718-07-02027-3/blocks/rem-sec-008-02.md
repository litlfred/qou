(1) The estimates in Theorem 8.2 are based on computing the value at z from scratch. As mentioned in Remark 8.1, if we want to compute Lin(z) for several z in the same residue disc Uζ, then it is more eﬃcient to compute the approximation of the truncation of Fn,ζ(t) and remember it, since then only (g) will have to be performed again. (2) The time estimates in Theorem 8.2 are worst case estimates. If z is closer to the root of unity ζ the complexities of (f) and (g) are reduced, since one needs fewer terms in the power series expansion around ζ to achieve the required precision.
