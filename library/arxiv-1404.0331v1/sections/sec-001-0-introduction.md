0.1. The colored Jones polynomial. For a knot K in S3 and a positive integer n,
let JK(n) ∈Z[t±1] denote the n-colored Jones polynomial of K with zero-framing. The
polynomial JK(n) is the quantum link invariant, as deﬁned by Reshetikhin and Turaev
[RT], associated to the Lie algebra sl2(C), with the color n standing for the irreducible
sl2(C)-module Vn of dimension n. Here we use the functorial normalization, i.e. the one
for which the colored Jones polynomial of the unknot U is
JU(n) = [n] := t2n −t−2n
t2 −t−2 .
It is known that JK(1) = 1 and JK(2) is the usual Jones polynomial [Jo]. The colored
Jones polynomials of higher colors are more or less the usual Jones polynomials of parallels
of the knot.
The color n can be assumed to take negative integer values by setting
JK(−n) := −JK(n). In particular, one has JK(0) = 0.
