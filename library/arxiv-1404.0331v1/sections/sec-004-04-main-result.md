The strong AJ conjecture was conﬁrmed for the trefoil knot (by Sikora [Si]) and all
torus knots (by Tran [Tr]). In this paper we consider the strong A J conjecture for cabled
knots over torus knots. Recall that the set of non-trivial torus knots T (p, q) in S3 can
be indexed by pairs of relatively prime integers ( p, q) satisfying |p| > q ≥ 2. Also recall
that an ( r, s)-cabled knot over a knot K in S3 is the knot which can be embedded in the
boundary torus of a tubular neighborhood of K in S3 as a curve of slope r/s with respect
to the meridian/longitude coordinates of K satisfying ( r, s) = 1 and s ≥ 2.
Our main result is the following.
Theorem 1. The strong AJ conjecture holds true for each (r, s)-cabled knot over each
(p, q)-torus knot if r is not a number between 0 and pqs.
