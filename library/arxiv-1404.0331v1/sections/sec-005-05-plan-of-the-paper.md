In Section \ref{cjp} we prove some properties of the colored Jones polynomial of cabled knots. In Section \ref{general} we prove a result about the relationship between the AJ conjecture and the strong AJ conjecture, and consequently prove one part of Theorem \ref{th2}. The proof of the other part of Theorem \ref{th2} is given in Sections \ref{q>2}--\ref{s=2}.
