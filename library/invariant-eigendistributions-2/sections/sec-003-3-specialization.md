In the above construction, choose V = {ν}, a single point. Then
B∗(ν) = {(b, ν)|ν ∈b⊥},
which may be identiﬁed with
Bν = {b ∈B|ν ∈b⊥}.
This is the ﬁxed point set of (the one parameter group generated by) ν, when g∗is identiﬁed with g. We
thus have a representation of W in H∗(Bν).
The component group A(ν) of the stabilizer of ν in G acts on H∗(Bν). This action commutes with
the action of W, because the elements of A(ν) have representatives in U (the compact form of G) and the
operators aλ(w) = pwλ−1 ◦pλ commute with the action of U on B∗, the pλ being U-equivariant.
The Weyl group W acts on the ﬂag-manifold B ≈U/T by
a(w) · u · b1 = u · w−1b1(u ∈U).
