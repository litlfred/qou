Since in usual quantum gauge theories on commutative space-time, the weights of Feynman graphs can be calculated as values of so called multiple polylogarithms (see [@BK], [@Kre]) and this fact plays a considerable role for the structural properties of quantum field theory (see [@CK], [@Kon], [@KoSo]), a first step toward perturbative quantum field theory on $S^{4,\theta }$ should be to look for $q$ -deformations of multiple polylogarithms.
