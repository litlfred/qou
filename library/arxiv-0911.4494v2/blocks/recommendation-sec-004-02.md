Finally, we must establish our claim that \begin{equation*} \Tr_{B_{n+1}}(T_{n}\iota(a)) = -t \cdot \Tr_{B_n}(a) \end{equation*} This follows from Corollary~\ref{final}, which shows that our trace coincides with that considered by Gomi and from \cite[\S 4.4 \& 4.5]{Gomi} where Gomi shows that his trace corresponds to that constructed by Geck and Lambropoulou, which has this property by \cite[Proposition 4.5]{GL}. \end{proof}
