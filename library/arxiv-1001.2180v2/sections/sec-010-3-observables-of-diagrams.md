[]{#sect:observables label="sect:observables"}

In this section, we introduce the algebra of observables of diagrams $\mathscr{O}$. This subalgebra of the algebra of functions on the set of all Young diagrams $\mathscr{Y}=\bigsqcup_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \mathscr{Y}_n$ is isomorphic to the algebra of (complex) symmetric functions, and it is known to be a powerful tool in the setting of asymptotic representation theory of symmetric groups, see [@IO02]. In this paper, the authors introduced four algebraic bases of $\mathscr{O}$: the power sums $(p_k)_{k\geq 1}$ in the Frobenius coordinates, the power sums $(\widetilde{p}_k)_{k \geq 2}$ in the interlacing coordinates, the normalized characters $(\varSigma_k)_{k \geq 1}$ and the free cumulants $(R_k)_{k \geq 2}$. In the following, we shall use the power sums $p_k$, the normalized characters $\varSigma_k$ and their quantizations $\varSigma_{k,q}$; this section is devoted to their presentation.
