2. The q-Plancherel measure
In this section, we deﬁne and present basic properties of the q-Plancherel measure. The characterization
given in paragraph 2.2 is the one which will be used in the next sections. The algebraic interpretation of
paragraph 2.3 gives some motivation to consider at this measure.
Let us ﬁx some notations related to the representation theory of Sn. If λ is a partition in Yn, it
corresponds to an irreducible Sn-module Sλ — the Specht module of type λ — whose dimension will be
denoted dim λ. We shall also denote ςλ the irreducible character of Sλ, and χλ the normalized character,
so that ςλ = (dim λ) χλ.
