Recall the definition of the curvature 2-form $\W$ of a connection 1-form $\w$ as the covariant exterior derivative of the 1-form $\w$.

::: Definition
Let ${\Gc=(\d\colon E \to G,\tr)}$ be a Lie crossed module, and let $P \to  M$ be a smooth principal $G$-bundle. The 2-curvature 3-form of a $\Gc$-categorical connection $(\w,m)$ on $P$ is defined as $$\M=d m\circ(H \times H \times H).$$ In other words: $$\M(A,B,C)=dm(A^H,B^H,C^H),$$ where $A,B,C \in \X(P)$ are smooth vector fields on $P$.
:::
