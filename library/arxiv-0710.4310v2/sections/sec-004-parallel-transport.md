[]{#Hol label="Hol"}

Let $P$ be a principal $G$-bundle over the manifold $M$. Let $\w \in \A^1(P,\lg)$ be a connection on $P$. Recall that $\w$ determines a parallel transport along smooth curves. Specifically, given $x \in M$ and a smooth curve $\gamma\colon [0,1] \to M$, with $\gamma(0)=x$, then there exists a smooth map: $$(t,u) \in [0,1] \times P_x \mapsto {\mathcal{H}}_\w(\ga,t,u) \in  P,$$ uniquely defined by the conditions:

1.  $\frac{d}{d t} {\mathcal{H}}_\w(\ga,t,u)=\left(\t{\frac{d}{dt} \ga(t)}\right)_{{\mathcal{H}}_\w(\ga,t,u) };\forall t \in [0,1], \forall u \in P_x,$

2.  ${\mathcal{H}}_\w(\ga,0,u)=u; \forall u \in P_x.$

In particular this implies that ${\mathcal{H}}_\w(\ga,t)$, given by $u\mapsto \mathcal{H}_\w(\ga,t,u)$, maps $P_x$ bijectively into $P_{\ga(t)}$, for any $t \in [0,1]$. Recall that the parallel transport is $G$-equivariant, in other words: $$\H_\w(\ga,t,ug)=\H_\w(\ga,t,u)g, \forall g \in G, \forall u \in P_x.$$
