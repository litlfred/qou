Recall the deﬁnition of the curvature 2-form Ω of a connection 1-f ormω as the
covariant exterior derivative of the 1-form ω.
Deﬁnition 28 (2-Curvature) Let G = (∂ : E →G,⊲ ) be a Lie crossed mod-
ule, and let P → M be a smooth principal G-bundle. The 2-curvature 3-form
of a G-categorical connection (ω,m ) on P is deﬁned as
M =dm ◦ (H ×H ×H).
In other words:
M(A,B,C ) = dm(AH,BH,CH ),
whereA,B,C ∈ X(P ) are smooth vector ﬁelds on P .
