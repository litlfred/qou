Theorem 10. With notation as in Theorem 9, the faces F1, . . . , Fk form a
curve in the Gaussian image of X with geodesic curvature not exceeding
3k exp(−ρ/N)).
Remark 11. The reader is referred to [8, 10] for a more thorough
discussion of geodesics on spherical cone manifold, but suﬃce it
to say that the contribution of the face Fi to the geodesic curvature
is 0 if the two edges are (hyper)parallel, and equal to the angle of
intersection if they intersect.
