Let G be a reductive algebraic group over k with Lie algebra g. Let Rep(G) denote the category of algebraic representations of G. Then Hi(Rep(G)) = (Λig)G for all i. Indeed, Rep(G) = Rep(O(G)∗). Therefore, Cn(Rep(G)) = (O(Gn)∗)G, where G acts diagonally by conjugation. Since G is reductive, the cohomology of this complex is the G-invariants in the Hochschild cohomology of O(G) with coeﬃcients in the trivial representation (corresponding to 1 ∈G). This cohomology is well known to be Λg, so the answer is (Λg)G, as desired.
