Let G be a ﬁnite group, and C = VecG be the category of G-graded vector spaces over an algebraically closed ﬁeld k (of any characteristic). Then Hi(C) = Hi(O(G)), the Hochschild cohomology of O(G) as a coalgebra, where O(G) is the Hopf algebra of k-valued functions on G. In other words, Hi(C) = Hi(G, k), the group cohomology of G with coeﬃcients in k.
