9.3. F aithfulness of lifting. In this section we will show that the lifting procedure
of nondegenerate fusion (braided, symmetric) categories is faith ful, i.e. if two such
categories over k have equivalent liftings, then they are equivalent. We will also
show that if two tensor functors from a nondegenerate fusion ca tegory over k to
another fusion category have isomorphic liftings, then they are iso morphic.
Let R be a complete local ring, F the fraction ﬁeld of R, I ⊂ R the maximal
ideal, and k =R/I the residue ﬁeld.
Theorem 9.6. (i) Let C1, C2 be fusion categories deﬁned over R, such that the
fusion categories C1/I, C2/I are nondegenerate, and the categories C1 ⊗R F, C2 ⊗R F
are tensor equivalent. Then C1, C2 (and hence C1/I, C2/I) are tensor equivalent.
(ii) The same statement is valid for braided and symmetric fu sion categories.
Proof. (i) Pick a tensor equivalence E : C1⊗RF → C 2⊗RF. Now pick an equivalence
of C1, C2 as additive categories, which is the same as E at the level of isomorphism
classes of objects. By doing this, we may assume without loss of gen erality that
C1 and C2 are really one category ( C, ⊗) over R with two diﬀerent associativity
isomorphisms Φ 1, Φ 2 and evaluation maps ev 1, ev2 (the coevaluation maps of simple
objects of both categories may be identiﬁed with any ﬁxed collection of such maps
by renormalization).
We know that there exists an invertible element J of C2(C) ⊗R F such that
(Φ 1, ev1)J = (Φ 2, ev2). (where J denotes the twisting by J). Our job is to show
that such J can be found in C2(C), without localization to F.
Consider the aﬃne group scheme T (deﬁned over R) of all invertible elements of
C2(C). Let T0 be the subscheme of T consisting of “trivial” twists, i.e. those of the
form ∆( x)(x−1 ⊗x−1), where x is an invertible element of C1(C) (i.e. the center of
C). It is clear that T0 is central in T .
Remark. Since the algebra C2(C) is a direct sum of matrix algebras (split over
R),T is a direct product of general linear groups.

Corollary 9.10. If two semisimple cosemisimple Hopf algebras over k have iso-

Proof. We can regard these two Hopf algebras as pairs ( C1,F 1) and (C2,F 2). By the-
