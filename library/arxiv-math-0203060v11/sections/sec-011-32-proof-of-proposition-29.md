The first statement is straightforward. To prove the second statement, let $N_i$ be the matrix whose entries are given by $(N_i)_{kj}={\rm dim}{\rm Hom}(X_j,X_i\otimes X_k)$. Then, like in the proof of Theorem [\[posi\]](#posi){reference-type="ref" reference="posi"}, $N_i\mathbf d=d_i\mathbf d$. Let us multiply this equation on the left by $\mathbf d^*$. (the hermitian adjoint). We have $\mathbf d^*N_i=(N_i^t\mathbf d)^*=
(N_{i^*}\mathbf d)^*=\overline{d_{i^*}}\mathbf d^*$. Thus the multiplication will yield $$\overline{d_{i^*}}|\mathbf d|^2=d_i|\mathbf d|^2.$$ Since $|\mathbf d|^2>0$, this yields the second statement. The proposition is proved.
