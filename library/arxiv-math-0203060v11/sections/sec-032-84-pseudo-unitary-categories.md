Corollary 8.36. The class of fusion categories with integer Frobenius-Perr on di-
mensions is closed under the operation of taking a component of a quotient category.
Proof. Let C be a fusion category with integer Frobenius-Perron dimensions, D an
indecomposable multi-fusion category, and and F : C → D be a surjective tensor
functor (so D is a quotient of C). Let M be an indecomposable module category
over D. Then F ∗ : D∗ → C ∗ is an injective tensor functor between the corresponding
dual fusion categories (C∗ is fusion since M is indecomposable over C, by surjectivity
of F ). By Theorem 8.35, C∗ has integer Frobenius-Perron dimensions. Hence, so
does D∗ as its full subcategory. But any component category Dii of D is dual to
D∗ with respect to the i-th part of M as a D∗-module. Thus, Dii has integer
Frobenius-Perron dimensions by Theorem 8.35. □
