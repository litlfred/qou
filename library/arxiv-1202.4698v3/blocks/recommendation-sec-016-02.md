Considering the representation $\pi_s$, for example, we will find that $\pi_s(\CL_t)$ can be represented in the form \begin{equation}\label{Diffop} \pi_s(\CL_t)\psi_s(l_s)\,=\,\big[ D_+(l_s)e^{+\sk_s}+D_0(l_s)+D_-(l_s)e^{-\sk_s}\big]\psi_s(l_s)\,. \end{equation} This formula should of course reproduce \rf{FN-FK} or \rf{FN-FK'} in the classical limit, but due to ordering issues and other possible quantum corrections it is a priori far from obvious how to define the coefficients $D_\ep(l_s)$, $\ep=-,0,+$.
