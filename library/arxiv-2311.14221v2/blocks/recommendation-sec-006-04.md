If $f:X\to Y$ is an isomorphism in $\ca$ then \begin{align}\label{duality-iso} \begin{split} (\id_{ {}^*X}\ot f )\coev_X=& ({}^*f\ot \id_Y )\coev_Y,\\ \ev_Y (f\ot \id_Y)=&\ev_X (\id_X\ot {}^*f). \end{split} \end{align} It is well known that for any pair of objects $X, Y\in \ca$ there are canonical natural isomorphisms \begin{equation}\label{duals-product-r} \phi^r_{X,Y}: Y^* \ot X^* \to(X\ot Y)^*, \end{equation} \begin{equation}\label{duals-product-l} \phi^l_{X,Y}: {}^*Y \ot {}^*X \to {}^*(X\ot Y). \end{equation} These isomorphisms allow us to compute coevaluation and evaluation maps of duals; more precisely we shall need the following identity \begin{equation}\label{coev-of-dual} \ev_{{}^*V}= {}^*(\coev_X) \phi^l_{{}^*X,X}. \end{equation}
