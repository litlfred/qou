For any $B\in \Bc$, $V\in \vc$ there are natural isomorphisms \begin{equation}\label{ts} t_{B,V}: {}^*(B \triangleleft V)\to {}^*B \triangleleft {}^*V\end{equation} such that the diagram \begin{equation}\label{diagram-ts} \xymatrix{ {}^*F(B \triangleleft V) \ar[d]^{{}^*(c^{-1}_{B,V})}\ar[rr]^{\qquad\; \qquad \; \qquad F(t_{B,V})\qquad \; \qquad\; \qquad}&& F({}^*B \triangleleft {}^*V) \ar[d]^{c_{{}^*B,{}^*V}} \\ {}^*(F(B)\ot V)\ar[rr]_{\qquad \qquad \; \qquad\sigma_{ {}^*V, {}^*F(B)} (\phi^l_{F(B),V})^{-1} \qquad \; \qquad\; \qquad}&& F({}^*B) \ot {}^*V,} \end{equation} is commutative. \end{lema} \pf We shall freely use the fact that $F({}^*B)= {}^*F(B)$, for any $B\in \Bc$.
