1. (i) We write CrtSp for the category whose objects are the Cartesian spaces Rn, n ∈N, and whose morphisms are the smooth func- tions between these. Hence this is the full subcategory of smooth manifolds CrtSp SmthMfd on the Cartesian spaces. We will denote generic Cartesian spaces by U, V ∈ CrtSp. (ii) We regard this category as a site by equipping it with the coverage (Grothendieck pre-topology) of differentiably good open covers.
