The main Gaussian measure ([\[Gm\]](#Gm){reference-type="ref" reference="Gm"}) itself enjoys a curious property: it can be expanded into a simple sum over the Schur functions containing only self-conjugated Young diagrams: $$\begin{aligned}
\label{Gmex}
d\mu \sim
\exp\left\{-\sum_{k=1}^\infty \left( \frac{p_{2k-1}^2-2p_{2k-1}}{2(2k-1)} + \frac{p_{2k}^2}{4k}\right)\right\}
=\sum_{R=R^\vee}(-1)^{|R|(|R|+1)+h_R(h_R+1)\over 2}S_R
= \nonumber\\
= \sum_{h_R=1}^\infty \sum_{k_1\ge k_2 \ge k_{h_R}\geq 0}^\infty  (-1)^{\sum_{i=1}^{h_R} k_i}\cdot
S_{(k_1,\ldots,k_{h_R}|k_1,\ldots,k_{h_R})}
\end{aligned}$$ where $h_R$ is the number of hooks that form the Young diagram $R$, and $(\vec \alpha|\vec\beta)$ is the Fröbenius notation of the Young diagram [@Mac].
