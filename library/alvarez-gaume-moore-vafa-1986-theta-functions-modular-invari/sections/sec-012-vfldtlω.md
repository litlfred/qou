In the previous sections we have been able to compute the spin-structure
dependence of the determinant of the chiral Dirac operator on Riemann surfaces.

In this section, we will apply those results to discuss some aspects of the higher loop
heterotic string amplitudes. In particular, as a first application, we will verify some
results of Witten on global anomalies [30]. It has recently been pointed out that
two-dimensional anomaly cancellation does not completely fix the heterotic string
amplitudes [31, 32]. As a second application, we will discuss this ambiguity from
the point of view of modular orbits of theta characteristics.
We begin by presenting an expression for the higher loop vacuum to vacuum
amplitudes in the heterotic string. At #-loops this amplitude can be derived from a
functional integral over two-dimensional supergeometries of a ^-handled
Riemann surface Σ, and over quantum matter fields living on this surface [2]. For
the heterotic string a supergeometry on a Riemann surface is specified by a
Riemannian metric (a background graviton field configuration) together with a
section of L~

may be described as follows. There are 10 left- and right-moving bosonic

10-dimensional spacetime. Spin waves on the world surface are described by the
