If un : D2 →(W n, Jn, Ln) is a sequence of Jn holomorphic disc to the n−stretched manifolds, with boundary on Ln, and with bounded energy, i.e. E(u) < E < ∞, then there is a subsequence of un that converges to a stable broken disc. This theorem is mentioned in Section 11.3 in [BEH+03] for the case of non-degenerate Reeb chords. Our case is a Morse-Bott version for Reeb chords. The proof in this case is the same as the usual SFT compactness theorem apart from some analytic changes in the lemmas in section 5 in [BEH+03].
