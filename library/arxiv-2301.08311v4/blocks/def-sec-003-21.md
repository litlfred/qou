We call a broken strip u high-index if vir(u) > 0. In the unbroken compact case, the moduli space Maslov 2 discs with a boundary constraint of passing through a fixed point is of expected dimension 0 and contributes to the disc potential. We define a notion of point constrained broken disc so that we can later compare counts of Maslov two discs passing through a point of the unbroken Lagrangian L with the broken Lagrangian L. Given a point p ∈Lin that is not in the cylindrical part of the Lagrangian Lin, we define the notion of broken disc with point constraint, the choice of p is not relevant since L is assumed to be connected.
