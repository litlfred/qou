In Pn, if we take L to be the Clifford torus, then there is a nice divisor D adapted to the standard J0. We can take two fibers of the map πm in some standard affine chart of Pn and perturb them to obtain a smooth divisor in Pn that makes L exact in the complement. From now on, we assume the existence of a nice divisor. If u is a non-constant holomorphic map in the outside piece with boundary on Lout, then it must intersect D as otherwise exactness of Lsing would imply symplectic area of u is 0.
