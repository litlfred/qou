Let M, L, K be manifolds as described in C[1-9]. Assume that there is a nice divisor D in M. Let Lµ be a local higher mutation of L. Let ρL, ρK be choices of local systems on L, K such that ρL is given as in equation (1.1.1). Let ρLµ be the mutated local system as in equation (1.1.2). Then, we have an isomorphism between the disc potentials of the Lagrangians, WL(ρL) ∼= WLµ(ρLµ) Moreover, if WL(ρL) = WK(ρK), then we have isomorphism of the vector spaces HF((Lµ, ρLµ), (K, ρK)) ∼= HF((L, ρL), (K, ρK))
