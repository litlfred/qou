There exists a complex manifold eCn obtained by iterated blow up of Cn along complex subspaces and a smooth map eϕ : eCn →Pn−1 such that the following diagram commutes, eCn Pn−1 Cn eϕ eπ ϕ where eπ is a composition of blowdown maps. Now, ϕ can be extended to a map eϕ : eC →Pn−1 by doing an inductive iterated blowup. Now the vertical subbundle of u∗TCn over z is defined by ker eϕ ◦eu(z) where eu is the lift of the disc to the blowup. Because the disc u maps to trivial cylinders (or strips) over Reeb orbits (or chords) near the punctures, we can check that there is an extension of eϕ ◦eu to the whole disc.
