Let (α, β) ∈T be the Thoma parameters of a Yang-Baxter character χR. Then P i αi + P i βi = 1, and only ﬁnitely many αi or βi are non-zero. We can now give a ﬁrst example of an extremal non-Yang-Baxter character, namely the Plancherel trace χ(σ) = δσ,e. By (2.6), the Plancherel trace has Thoma parameters α = β = 0 and therefore violates the condition P i αi + P i βi = 1. Its GNS repre- sentation is the left regular representation, which is too large to be of Yang-Baxter form.
