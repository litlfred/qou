[23] Let T denote the collection of all sequences {αi}i∈N, {βi}i∈N of real numbers such that i) αi ≥0 and βi ≥0, ii) αi ≥αi+1 and βi ≥βi+1, iii) P i αi + P j βj ≤1. The pairs of sequences (α, β) ∈T are in bijection with extremal characters of S∞. On an n-cycle, the character χ corresponding to (α, β) ∈T takes the value χ(cn) = X i αn i + (−1)n+1 X i βn i , n ≥2. (2.6) We will call the parameters (α, β) ∈T the Thoma parameters of a character. As a consequence of these results, any R ∈R0 deﬁnes a point (α, β) ∈T.
