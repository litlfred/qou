We ﬁrst show that Meyer Theorem 3.2 follows from Novikov-Wall Theorem.
If one splits the pair of pants in two halves as in Figure 10, the manifold W
appears as the union of two 4-manifolds glued along part of their boundaries,
as discussed above. Each one of the two pieces has a signature equal to 0 since
they are both homeomorphic to the product of a 3-manifold by an interval.
One is therefore led to evaluate the defect δ in this situation. In this case, the
surface Σ consists of two copies of F and the three corresponding Lagrangian
subspaces of H1(F; R) ⊕H1(F; R) are the graphs of the identity, and of the
actions of the two given diﬀeomorphisms in homology. The deﬁnition of Maslov
ternary index δ reduces precisely to the deﬁnition of the Meyer cocycle that
we gave in the introduction and we get indeed Meyer Theorem from Novikov-
Wall Theorem.
