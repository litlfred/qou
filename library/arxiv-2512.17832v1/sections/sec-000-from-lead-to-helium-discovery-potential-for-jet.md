Jet Quenching in the Smallest Collision Systems
Coleridge Faraday,1, ∗Ben Bert,1, † Jack Brand,1, ‡ Werner Vogelsang,2, § and W. A. Horowitz1, 3, ¶
1Department of Physics, University of Cape Town, Private Bag X3, Rondebosch 7701, South Africa
2Institute for Theoretical Physics, University of T¨ubingen, Auf der Morgenstelle 14, D-72076 T¨ubingen, Germany
3Department of Physics, New Mexico State University, Las Cruces, New Mexico, 88003, USA
(Dated: December 22, 2025)
We present perturbative quantum chromodynamics (pQCD) predictions for the modification to
the yield of high-momentum particles in very light ion collisions—10B+10B, 6Li+6Li, 4He+4He, and
3He + 3He—both with and without medium-induced energy loss. We show that there is non-trivial
suppression expected from our partonic energy loss model in symmetric systems from 208Pb+ 208Pb
to 3He + 3He and in asymmetric systems A + B, and that the energy loss scales approximately with
(
√
AB)1/3. Further, we find that deep inelastic scattering measurements in 3He and 6Li tightly
constrain the nPDF baseline, making these isotopes a particularly clean environment for observing
final-state partonic energy loss induced by the formation of a quark-gluon plasma in these very small
systems.
