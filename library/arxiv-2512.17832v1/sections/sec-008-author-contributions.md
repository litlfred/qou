WAH and CF jointly
conceptualized the study.
CF performed the energy-
loss and hydrodynamic calculations and wrote all drafts.
BB and JB performed exploratory PYTHIA simulations
and cross-checked the nPDF uncertainty implementa-
tion. WV performed the NLO pQCD calculations with
nPDFs and wrote most of the section on nPDF results.
WAH supervised the project.
All authors edited the
manuscript and contributed to the interpretation and sci-
entific conclusions.
