AN EXPERIMENTAL INVESTIGATION OF NEUMANN’S
CONJECTURE
STEPHEN GILLES AND PETER HUSTON
Abstract. We use a large census of hyperbolic 3-manifolds to experimentally
investigate a conjecture of Neumann regarding the Bloch Group. We present
an augmented census including, for feasible invariant trace ﬁelds, explicit man-
ifolds (associated to that ﬁeld) that appear to generate the Bloch group of that
ﬁeld. We also make use of Ptolemy coordinates to compute “exotic volumes”
