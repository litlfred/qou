Estimate of solutions of systems of equations
In Sections 3.2, 4.2 and 5.2, we estimate solutions of some systems of equations by using
numerical solutions obtained by Newton’s method. As mentioned in Remarks 3.1, 3.2 and
55
4.1, this argument is not partially rigorous, since we do not estimate the error terms of
such solutions. In order to complete their proofs, we show how to estimate such solutions
rigorously in this appendix. We explain 1-, 2-, 3-variable cases in Sections A.1, A.2, A.3
respectively.
