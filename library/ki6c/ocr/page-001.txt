On the asymptotic expansions of
the Kashaev invariant of the knots with 6 crossings

Tomotada Ohtsuki and Yoshiyuki Yokota

Abstract

We give presentations of the asymptotic expansions of the Kashaev invariant of the knots with
6 crossings. In particular, we show the volume conjecture for these knots, which states that the
leading terms of the expansions present the hyperbolic volume and the Chern-Simons invariant of
the complements of the knots. As higher coefficients of the expansions, we obtain a new series of
invariants of these knots.

A non-trivial part of the proof is to apply the saddle point method to calculate the asymptotic
expansion of an integral which presents the Kashaev invariant. A key step of this part is to give a
concrete homotopy of the (real 3-dimensional) domain of the integral in C* in such a way that the
boundary of the domain always stays in a certain domain in C* given by the potential function of
the hyperbolic structure.
