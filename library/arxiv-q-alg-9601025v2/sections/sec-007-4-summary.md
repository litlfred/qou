We have demonstrated on three examples of hyperbolic knots, tha t
the link invariant, deﬁned in [15, 16] via cyclic quantum dilogarithm,
in the asymptotic limit N → ∞ reproduces the hyperbolic volume of
a knot, see formula (1.1). This result implies a possible relation of
the corresponding combinatorial TQFT to quantum 2+1-dimensiona l
gravity.
