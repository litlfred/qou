We have demonstrated on three examples of hyperbolic knots, that the link invariant, defined in [@K1; @K2] via cyclic quantum dilogarithm, in the asymptotic limit $N\to\infty$ reproduces the hyperbolic volume of a knot, see formula ([\[main\]](#main){reference-type="ref" reference="main"}). This result implies a possible relation of the corresponding combinatorial TQFT to quantum 2+1-dimensional gravity.
