The Quantum G2 Link Invariant
Greg Kuperberg ∗
University of Chicago
October 7, 1991
Abstract
We derive an inductive, combinatorial deﬁnition of a polynomial-valued regular isotopy invariant of
links and tangled graphs. We show that the invariant equals the Reshetikhin-Turaev invariant corre-
sponding to the exceptional simple Lie algebra G2. It is therefore related to G2 in the same way that the
HOMFLY polynomial is related to An and the Kauﬀman polynomial is related to Bn, Cn, and Dn. We
give parallel constructions for the other rank 2 Lie algebras and present some combinatorial conjectures
motivated by the new inductive deﬁnitions.
This paper is divided into two parts. In the ﬁrst part we derive from ﬁrst principles some variants of
the link invariant known as the Jones polynomial. In the second part we show that these invariants are the
same known invariants constructed using rank 2 Lie algebras, and we discuss some of their properties.
