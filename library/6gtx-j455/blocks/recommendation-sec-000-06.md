Looking at trajectories that would eventually reach the tachyon-free region (where we would expect GR to emerge), we see that all of them (except the one trajectory that follows the red dotted separatrix) must first go in the region where both λ and ξ are positive: λ always monotonically grows, while ξ first grows before decreasing and eventually crossing the tachyon divide at a scale that we call μ0.
