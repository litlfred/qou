LINEAR PROGRAMMING BOUNDS FOR HYPERBOLIC SURFACES
MAXIME FORTIER BOURQUE AND BRAM PETRI
Abstract.We adapt linear programming methods from sphere packings to closed hy-
perbolic surfaces and obtain new upper bounds on their systole, their kissing number, the
first positive eigenvalue of their Laplacian, the multiplicity of their first eigenvalue, and
their number of small eigenvalues. Apart from a few exceptions, the resulting bounds are
the current best known both in low genus and as the genus tends to infinity. Our methods
also provide lower bounds on the systole (achieved in genus 2 to 7, 14, and 17) that are
sufficient for surfaces to have a spectral gap larger than 1/4.
