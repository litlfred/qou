THE SUM-PRODUCT CONJECTURE IS FALSE FOR REAL
NUMBERS
THOMAS F. BLOOM, WILL SAWIN, CARL SCHILDKRAUT, AND DMITRII ZHELEZOV
Abstract. We disprove the sum-product conjecture for real numbers by con-
structing arbitrarily large A ⊂R (whose elements are algebraic integers in a
number field of degree ≍log|A|) such that
max(|A + A| , |AA|) ≤|A|2−c
where c > 0 is an absolute constant.
