There exists an integer k ≥2 and an absolute constant C > 1 such that, for infinitely many d, there exists a number field K of degree d such that there are at least Cd non-degenerate solutions to (6.1) with xi ∈O× K. This is, in hindsight, a simple consequence of the fact that units of bounded height still have small height after O(1) many sums, and so the size of their k-fold sumset is small. To highlight the simplicity of the example we will present the construction from first principles, at the cost of some slight repetition of earlier arguments.
