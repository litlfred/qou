Algebraic & Geometric Topology 12 (2012) 791–804
The twisted Alexander polynomial for ﬁnite abelian covers
over three manifolds with boundary
JÉRÔME DUBOIS
YOSHIKAZU YAMAGUCHI
We provide the twisted Alexander polynomials of ﬁnite abelian covers over three-
dimensional manifolds whose boundary is a ﬁnite union of tori. This is a generaliza-
tion of a well-known formula for the usual Alexander polynomial of knots in ﬁnite
cyclic branched covers over the three-dimensional sphere.
