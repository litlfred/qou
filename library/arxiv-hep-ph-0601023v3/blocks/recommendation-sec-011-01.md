One can now integrate on the Higgs triplet field and obtain an effective interaction at low energy which contains baryon and lepton number violating dimension five operators with chirality LLLL and RRRR such that \begin{eqnarray} \it W(LLLL)&=& \frac{1}{M} \epsilon_{abc}(Pf_1^uV)_{ij}(f_2^d)_{kl} ( \tilde u_{Lbi}\tilde d_{Lcj}(\bar e^c_{Lk}(Vu_L)_{al}- \nu^c_kd_{Lal})+...)\nonumber\\ &+& H.c. \nonumber\\ W(RRRR)&=& -\frac{1}{M} \epsilon_{abc}(V^{\dagger} f^u)_{ij}(PVf^d)_{kl} (\bar e^c_{Ri}u_{Raj}\tilde u_{Rck}\tilde d_{Rbl}+...) \nonumber\\ &+& H.c. \label{LLLLRRRRR} \end{eqnarray} where V is the CKM matrix and $f_i$, $P_i$ are generational phases \begin{equation} P_i=(e^{i\gamma_i}), ~\sum_i \gamma_i=0; ~i=1,2,3 \label{genphases} \end{equation} Both LLLL and RRRR interactions must be taken into account in a full analysis and their relative strength depends on the part of the parameter space where their effects are computed.
