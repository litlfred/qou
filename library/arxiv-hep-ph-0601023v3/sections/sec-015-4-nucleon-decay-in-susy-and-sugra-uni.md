Supersymmetry in four space-time dimensions [48, 49] arise s algebraically
from the ”graded algebra” involving the spinor charge Qα along with the
generators of the Lorentz algebra Pµ and Mµν. Among the remarkable fea-

local relativistic ﬁeld theory is the supersymmetric algeb ra [50]. The above
implies that supersymmetry appears as the only unique grade d extension of

metric models enjoy the advantage of a no-renormalization t heorem [51, 52]
making the theory technically natural. However, one appare nt disadvantage
