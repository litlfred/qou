Setting gs ∼ 1, αG = 0.04, y = 0.2, and MG = MX gives Cst ≃ 0.5 − 1.2.
Since the current estimate of the GUT prediction is τGUT = 1.6 × 1036yr
for values of αG = 0.04 and MX = 2 × 1016 GeV, one ﬁnds that the string
prediction in this case is (0 .8 − 1.9) × 1036 years. The more recent analysis of
Ref. [375] gives the range (0.5−2.1)×1036 yr. The current experimental limit
on this decay mode is τexp(p →e+π0)> 1.6 × 1033 yr (Table 1. See, however,
Ref. [376] which givesτexp(p →e+π0)> 4.4×1033 yr). The next generation of
proton decay experiment using underground water Cherenkov detectors may
improve the experimental lower limit for this mode by a facto r of 10 close
to 10 35 yr [31] which, however, falls short of the theory prediction above.
However, one must keep in mind the model dependence of the the oretical
prediction arising from the assumed values of LQ,gs, assumption on fermion
mixings etc. Thus, for example, if LQ lies in the range 1-10 [377], then Cst
will lie in the range (0.4-19) which is a signiﬁcant shift fro m the previous
estimate.
