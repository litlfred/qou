in the plane transverse to the direction of the loop in 3d [33]. It is known, however that

choice of the potential [41, 42, 45, 46, 47]. In particular, it was proposed to consider the
potential [48, 49], which explicitly breaks theO(3) symmetry of the planar Skyrme model to

In the sector of degreeQ = 3 we also found another solution3(~A1 ≬ ~A1) ~A1 (Table 1). Since
the energies of both solutions are very close, we are not able to identify which conﬁguration
represents a global minimum, our algorithm suggests that the conﬁguration with hexagonal
symmetry of the location curve of the preimage of the point⃗φ = (0, 0,−0.9) has slightly lower
energy.
Considering the structure of theQ = 3 Hopﬁon solutions in the (1) with the potential (15),
we found that the energy density isosurfaces again represents two buckled tubular loops with
interlinking number three, see Table 2. Thus, as before, the location curvesC1 = ⃗φ−1(1, 0, 0)
andC−1 = ⃗φ−1(−1, 0, 0) deﬁne the spacial distribution of the energy density. Similar to the
corresponding Q = 1, 2 solutions we discussed above, the energy is not equally distributed
among these tubes. This observation holds for all conﬁgurations in the sectors of higher degrees.
At degree four in the model (1) with potentialV = m2φ1
2 we found solution of three
diﬀerent types, see Table 1. The minimal energy conﬁguration is of the type4(L1,1 ≬L1,1) ~A2,2.
The energy density isosurfaces of this very symmetric Hopﬁon represent four tubular loops
interlinked with each other, see the corresponding plot in the Table 1.
The conﬁguration of the form4(~A1,1 ≬ ~A1,1) ~A4,1 has less symmetry, it is a local minimum of
the energy functional. In this sector we also found the dihedrally symmetric Hopﬁon4(A1,1 ≬
A1,1)A4,1, its energy is even higher. Similar to the corresponding solutions in the sectors of lower
degrees, for large values of the energy, the energy density isosurface forms a bead necklace with
8 beads and the bounding planar curveC = ⃗φ−1(0, 0,−1) possessing dihedral symmetryD4.
Q = 4 Hopﬁons in the model (1) with mixed potential (15) are of similar forms, see Table
2. Surprisingly, we found that lowest energy conﬁguration in this sector is4(~A1 ≬L1,1) ~A4,1.
More symmetric solution4(A1 ≬A1)A4,1 then has almost the same energy as the conﬁguration
4(L1,1 ≬L1,1)A2,2, so it is diﬃcult to identify which state is the ﬁrst local minimum. Remind
