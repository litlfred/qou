To ﬁnd the stationary points of the energy functional (4) with the potential (6) we make
use of the numerical minimization technique described in [39, 40]. The ﬁelds are discretized on
the grid with1503, or 2003 point with spatial grid spacings∆x = 0.1, 0.06 or ∆x = 0.05. The
initial conﬁguration were produced via the rational map approximation as described above. As
a consistency check, we verify that our algorithm correctly reproduces the known results for
the Hopﬁon conﬁguration of the usual rescaled massless Faddeev-Skyrme model atm = 0, it
agrees with previously known value within0.5% accuracy. For each solution we evaluated the
value of the Hopf invariant and checked that the corresponding virial relationE2 + 3V =E4
between the potential, quadratic, and quartic in derivatives terms in (4), holds. The estimated
errors are of order of10−2 or smaller.
