It is known that, according to the scaling arguments of the Derrick’s theorem, stable soliton
solitons may exist in the truncatedc2 = 0 submodel of (1), which contains only Skyrme term
and a potential [25, 50]. This submodel eﬀectively corresponds to the inﬁnite mass limitm→
∞. Interestingly, these Hopﬁons usually are solitons with compact support, the conﬁguration
possess no exponentially decaying tail, instead the ﬁeld components attain the vacuum values
outside some ﬁnite domain in space.
In our simulations we considered thec2 = 0 submodel of (1) with the symmetry breaking
potential (6). We ﬁx the scale by settingm = 4. Since there are usually severe diﬃculties
with the numerical simulations of the compactons, we make use of the smallc2 parameter as a
regulator in our numerical scheme.
In Fig. 8 we present the solutions of this submodel with Heisenberg type symmetry breaking
potential. As in the case of the usual pion-mass type potential, these solurions are compactons.
Note that the interior domain, where the ﬁelds are located, is not spherically symmetric.

We found that the solutions of thec2 = 0 submodel are similar to the corresponding minimal
energy conﬁgurations in the full Faddeev-Skyrme with potential (6). On the other hand, the
planar Skyrmions in the cross sections of such compact Hopﬁons have more prominent dihedral
symmetry. The spacial distribution of the energy density possess much "sharper" structure, as
expected.
