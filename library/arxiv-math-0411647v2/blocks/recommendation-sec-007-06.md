We consider a filtration on the commutative algebra generated by $\Sigma_{k_1,\dots,k_m}\in{\mathbb{C}}(\widetilde{{{S}}_{\infty}})$ by setting $$\label{eq:filtration} \deg \Sigma_{k_1,\dots,k_m}=(k_1+1)+(k_2+1)+\cdots+(k_m+1).$$ It is easy to check that the elements from the family $\Sigma_{k_1,\dots,k_m}\in{\mathbb{C}}(\widetilde{{{S}}_{\infty}})$ indexed by integer $m\geq 0$ and $k_1\geq \cdots \geq k_m\geq 1$ are linearly independent and therefore this definition makes sense but it is not clear that this formula indeed defines a filtration; we shall prove it in Corollary [\[cor:filtration\]](#cor:filtration){reference-type="ref" reference="cor:filtration"}.
