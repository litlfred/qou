In Section [\[subsec:definicjasigma\]](#subsec:definicjasigma){reference-type="ref" reference="subsec:definicjasigma"} we shall define such a family $\Sigma_{k_1,\dots,k_m}\in{\mathbb{C}}({{S}}_{q})$ which has a particularly simple structure: $\Sigma_{k_1,\dots,k_m}$ is (up to a normalizing factor) an indicator of the conjugacy class of permutations with a prescribed cycle decomposition $k_1,\dots,k_m$.
