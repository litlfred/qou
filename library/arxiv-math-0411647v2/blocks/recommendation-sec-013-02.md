Inspired by [\[eq:momentmappa\]](#eq:momentmappa){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:momentmappa"} we shall denote by $M^{\mathop{\mathrm{JM}}}_\rho$ the partition--indexed moments of the Jucys--Murphy element, defined by $$\label{eq:momentmap} M^{\mathop{\mathrm{JM}}}_{\rho}=\prod_{1\leq s\leq r} M^{\mathop{\mathrm{JM}}}_{|\rho_s|}=\prod_{1\leq s\leq r} {\mathbb{E}}(J^{|\rho_s|})\in{\mathbb{C}}(\widetilde{{{S}}_{\infty}}),$$ where $\rho=\{\rho_1,\dots,\rho_r\}$ is a non--crossing partition.
