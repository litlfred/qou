The conditional expectation ${\mathbb{E}}_{{\mathbb{C}}({{S}}_{A})} :{\mathbb{C}}({{S}}_{A\cup\{\ast\}})\rightarrow {\mathbb{C}}({{S}}_{A})$ will be given by the orthogonal projection, i.e. $${\mathbb{E}}_{{\mathbb{C}}({{S}}_{A})}(\sigma)=\begin{cases} \sigma & \text{if } \sigma\in{{S}}_{A} \\ 0 & \text{if } \sigma\notin{{S}}_{A} \end{cases}= \begin{cases} \sigma & \text{if } \sigma(\ast)=\ast, \\ 0 & \text{if } \sigma(\ast)\neq \ast.\end{cases}$$ In the rest of this article, when it does not lead to confusions, instead of ${\mathbb{E}}_{{\mathbb{C}}({{S}}_{A})}$ we shall simply write ${\mathbb{E}}$.
