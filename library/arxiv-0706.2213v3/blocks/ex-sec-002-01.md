For a knot K in S 3, we let EK (resp. Π(K)) denote the exterior (resp. the group) of K, i.e. EK = S 3 \ N(K), where N(K) is an open tubular neighborhood of K (resp. Π(K) = π1(EK)). Convention. Suppose that S 3 is oriented. The exterior of a knot is thus oriented and we know that it is bounded by a 2-dimensional torus T 2.
