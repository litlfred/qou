NON–ABELIAN REIDEMEISTER TORSION FOR TWIST KNOTS
J´ER ˆOME DUBOIS, VU HUYNH, AND YOSHIKAZU YAMAGUCHI
Abstract. This paper gives an explicit formula for the SL 2(C)−non–abelian Reidemeister
torsion as deﬁned in [Dub06] in the case of twist knots. For hyperbolic twist knots, we
also prove that the non–abelian Reidemeister torsion at the holonomy representation can
be expressed as a rational function evaluated at the cusp shape of the knot.
