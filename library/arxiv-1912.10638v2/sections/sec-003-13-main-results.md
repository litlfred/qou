The main goal of this paper is to study the asymptotics of the colored Jones polynomials of the Whitehead chains defined by R. van der Veen in [@V08].

For any $a\in {\mathbb Z}$, $b\in {\mathbb N}$, $c,d\in{\mathbb N}\cup\{0\}$ with $c+d\geq 1$, the Whitehead chain $W_{a,b,c,d}$ is obtained by stacking $a$ full twists, $b$ belts, $c$ clasps and $d$ mirror clasps and then taking the closure (Figure [1](#fullpicture){reference-type="ref" reference="fullpicture"}). All the Whitehead chains $W_{a,1,c,d}$ are hyperbolic with $$\operatorname{Vol}({\mathbb S}^3\backslash W_{a,1,c,d}) = (c+d) v_8 ,$$ where $v_8$ is the volume of an ideal regular octahedron.

For $b>1$, the Whitehead chains $W_{a,b,c,d}$ is not hyperbolic but it contains a hypebolic piece in the JSJ decomposition with $$v_3 ||{\mathbb S}^3\backslash W_{a,b,c,d}|| = (c+d) v_8$$
