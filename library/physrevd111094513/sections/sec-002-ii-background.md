Although the subtraction function is not experimentally
accessible, it can be estimated in both the low Q2 and high
Q2 regions.

puted in a variety of formalisms, including effective field
theory calculations [2–6,17–19,23,36–39]. These calcula-
tions have sizeable errors and are not always consistent
with one another [22].
