Furthermore, the result indicates a smooth transition
between the low and high Q2 regimes. The results
presented here establish a pathway for improved lattice
calculations of the subtraction function to deliver improved
estimates of the proton-neutron mass difference and the
proton charge radius.
The work presented here introduces a number of avenues
of future research, particularly by incorporating a consid-
eration of finite volume effects, and by repeating this work
for domain wall fermions (see Ref. [24]). Given the lack of
a Wilson term, one would anticipate Oða2Þ discretization
artifacts for domain wall fermions, as opposed to the OðaÞ
artifacts present here.
The precision and applicability of our calculation could be
further improved by repeating these calculations for pion
masses closer to the physical value, and with additional
configurations of different lattice spacings. Furthermore, the
precision of the approach presented here could be evaluated
by extracting hxi and gS from fits to the subtraction function,
and comparing to the values in Table I.
