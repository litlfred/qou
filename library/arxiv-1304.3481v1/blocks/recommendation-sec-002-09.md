Going back to original $q$-degrees, i.e. the ordinary version of the colored homology $\CH^{\lambda}(K)$, the above relations reduce to two-variable (in variables $a$ and $t$) exponential growths: \be {\CP}^{S^{r}}(K)(a,q=1,t_{r}=t,t_c=1)=\left(\CP^{\tableau1}(K)(a,q=1,t)\right)^{r}, \ee and \be {\CP}^{\Lambda^{r}}(K)(a,q=1,t_{r}=1,t_c=t)=\left(\CP^{\tableau1}(K)(a,q=1,t)\right)^{r}. \ee Although such nice (and surprising) exponential growth properties should exist for large classes of knots, it is not expected that it should hold in general, i.e. for every knot.
