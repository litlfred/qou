\begin{definition} The {\em unreduced} moduli space $\overline{\CM}_{p,q}(r)$ is defined in the affine space with the coordinates $u_1, \ldots, u_{rp}; v_1,\ldots,v_{rq}$ by the equation \begin{equation} \label{equv} (1+u_1z+u_2z^2+\ldots+u_{rp}z^{rp})^{q}=(1+v_1z+v_2z^2+\ldots+v_{rq}z^{rq})^{p}. \end{equation} which should hold at every coefficient of its expansion in powers of $z$. \end{definition}
