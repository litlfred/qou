The $\tableau{2 1}$-colored HOMFLY polynomial of the trefoil is given by \begin{align} P^{\tableau{2 1}} (3_1)(a,q) \; = \; & -a^{12} + a^{10} (q^{-6} + q^{-4} + q^{-2} + q^2 + q^4 + q^6) \notag \\ & + a^{8} (-q^{-10} - 2 q^{-6} - 3 q^{-2} - 3 q^2 - 2 q^6 - q^{10}) \notag \\ & + a^{6} (q^{-10} + 2 q^{-6} - q^{-4} + 2 q^{-2} + 2 q^2 - q^4 + 2 q^6 + q^{10}). \notag \end{align} This polynomial has 31 terms, and therefore any homology theory categorifying it, should have at least 31 generators.
