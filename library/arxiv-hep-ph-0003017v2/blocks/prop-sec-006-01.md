Our starting point is that the topological susceptibility of the quenched gauge fields is nonvanishing, i.e. $$\frac { \langle \nu^2 \rangle_q} V \ne 0\:.$$ Invoking the central limit theorem, we assume that $P(\nu)$ is Gaussian, $$P(\nu) = \frac 1{\sqrt{2 \pi \langle \nu^2 \rangle_q}}\: e^{-\frac{\nu^2}{2 \langle \nu^2 \rangle_q }}\:,$$ which has been verified by quenched lattice QCD simulations.
