For $M_3=L(p,1)$, or more generally $M_3=O(-p)\rightarrow \Sigma_g$, we have $\Lambda_{M_3}= {\mathbb Z}_p/{\mathbb Z}_2$ and the basic Wilson loops in $T[M_3]$ are given by $W_{\lambda}$ with $\lambda=0,\ldots, p$.[^1] These correspond to the "$\lambda$-cablings" of the unknot [\[Gamma-Lp1\]](#Gamma-Lp1){reference-type="eqref" reference="Gamma-Lp1"} wrapping the Hopf fiber of $L(p,1)$.

More generally, for plumbed $M_3$ (see section [\[sec:plumbed\]](#sec:plumbed){reference-type="ref" reference="sec:plumbed"}), if $(K^{\lambda}, {\mathcal R}_{\lambda})$ is a $\lambda$-cabling of the unknot wrapping the "Hopf fiber" associated with the node $v$ of the plumbing graph, we have $\Gamma_{K,\lambda}{=}W_{\lambda_v}$, where $\lambda_v$ is the representation of ${\mathcal G}$ obtained from the representation $\lambda$ of $G$ via the "evaluation homomorphism" $e_v: {\mathcal G}\rightarrow G$ for the node $v$.

[^1]: Notice that we have added odd representations according to description in footnote [\[ft:Global\]](#ft:Global){reference-type="ref" reference="ft:Global"}.
