For electroweak, David Hestenes\cite{1982FoPh...12..153H}, Antony Lewis (in unpublished notes) and Chris Doran and myself\cite{d2003geometric}, have pointed out that given an STA Dirac spinor $\psi$ (which for electroweak will generally be some combination of massless left-handed electron and neutrino wavefunctions), then starting from a Lagrangian, we find that the symmetry we should look for is to find all multivectors $N$ such that when we carry out the transformation $\psi \mapsto \psi e^N$, then $\psi \go \psirev$, the {\em Dirac current}, is invariant.
