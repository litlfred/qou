We shall make use of the canonical isomorphisms \begin{equation}\label{canonicalisomorphisms} H_3(\SL(n,\C))\cong H_3(\SL(3,\C))\cong H_3(\SL(2,\C))\oplus K_3^M(\C). \end{equation} The first isomorphism is induced by stabilization (see Suslin~\cite{Suslin1046}) and the second isomorphism is the $\pm$-eigenspace decomposition with respect to the transpose-inverse involution on $\SL(3,\C)$ (see Sah~\cite{Sah}).
