FROM HOPF ALGEBRAS TO TENSOR CATEGORIES
N. ANDRUSKIEWITSCH, I. ANGIONO, A. GARCÍA IGLESIAS,
B. TORRECILLAS, C. VAY
Abstract. This is a survey on spherical Hopf algebras. We give criteria
to decide when a Hopf algebra is spherical and collect examples. We
discuss tilting modules as a mean to obtain a fusion subcategory of the
non-degenerate quotient of the category of representations of a suitable
Hopf algebra.
