The Nichols algebra of V exists and is unique up to isomorphism. We sketch
a way to construct B(V ) and prove its unicity, see [AS2]. Note that the tensor
algebra T (V ) admits a unique structure of graded braided Hopf algebra in
H
HYD such that V ⊆ P (V ). Consider the class S of all the homogeneous
two-sided Hopf ideals I ⊆ T (V ) such that I is generated by homogeneous
elements of degree ≥ 2 and is a Yetter-Drinfeld submodule of T (V ). Then
B(V ) is the quotient of T (V ) by the maximal element I(V ) of S. Thus, the
canonical projection π :T (V ) → B (V ) is a Hopf algebra surjection in H
HYD .
