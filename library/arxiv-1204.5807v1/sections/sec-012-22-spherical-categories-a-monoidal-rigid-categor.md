X ∗∗ is monoidally isomorphic to X [FY1]; this implies that the left and right
dualities coincide. For instance, if H is a Hopf algebra and ω ∈ G(H) is a
pivot, then RepH is pivotal [BaW1, Proposition 3.6]. In a pivotal category
C, there are left and right traces trL, trR : End(X) → End(1), for any X ∈ C .
If C = RepH and V ∈ RepH, then these traces are deﬁned by
trL(ϑ) = tr V (ϑω), trR(ϑ) = tr V (ϑω−1), ϑ ∈ EndH (V ).(2.3)
A spherical category is a pivotal one where the left and right traces coincide.
Thus, RepH is a spherical category, whenever H is a spherical Hopf algebra.
Remark 2.3. If D is a rigid monoidal (full) subcategory of a spherical
category C, then D is also spherical.
