Let $\Bbbk$ be an algebraically closed field of characteristic 0 and $\Bbbk^{\times}$ its multiplicative group of units. All vector spaces, algebras, unadorned Hom and ${\otimes}$ are over $\Bbbk$. By convention, ${\mathbb N}= \{1, 2, \dots\}$ and ${\mathbb N}_0 = {\mathbb N}\cup \{0\}$.

Let $G$ be a finite group. We denote by $Z(G)$ the center of $G$ and by $\operatorname{Irr}G$ the set of isomorphism classes of irreducible representations of $G$. If $g\in G$, we denote by $C_{G}(g)$ the centralizer of $g$ in $G$. The conjugacy class of $g$ is denoted by ${\mathcal O}_g$ or by ${\mathcal O}^G_g$, when emphasis on the group is needed.

Let $A$ be an algebra. We denote by $Z(A)$ the center of $A$. The category of $A$-modules is denoted ${{A}{\text -}\operatorname{Mod}}$; the full subcategory of finite-dimensional objects is denoted ${{A} {\text -}\operatorname{mod}}$. The set of isomorphism classes of irreducible[^1] objects in an abelian category ${\mathcal C}$ is denoted $\operatorname{Irr}{\mathcal C}$; we use the abbreviation $\operatorname{Irr}A$ instead of $\operatorname{Irr}{{A} {\text -}\operatorname{mod}}$.
