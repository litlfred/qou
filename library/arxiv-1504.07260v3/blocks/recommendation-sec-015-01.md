Any two elements $(P_1,Q_1), (P_2,Q_2)\in \Lambda_{em}$ must satisfy the Dirac quantization condition $$\label{DiracQuant} P_1 L Q_2 -P_2 L Q_1 \in \mathbb{Z}\ .$$This implies $$\label{emDirac} \Lambda_e\oplus \Lambda_m\subseteq \Lambda_{em}\subseteq \Lambda_e^*\oplus \Lambda_m^*$$and $$\label{eminclude} \Lambda_e\subseteq \Lambda_m^*\qquad \Lambda_m\subseteq \Lambda_e^*\ ,$$for the lattices of purely magnetic and purely electric charges (the two inclusion are actually equivalent).
