The Atkin-Lehner S-duality $W_e$ acts on the electric-magnetic charges by $$\begin{pmatrix} Q \\ P \end{pmatrix}\to \begin{pmatrix} Q' \\ P' \end{pmatrix}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{e}}\begin{pmatrix} de & -b\\ -Nc & ae \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} Q \\ P \end{pmatrix}\ .$$As a consequence, the electric and magnetic lattices $\Lambda_e,\Lambda_m$ and $\Lambda_e',\Lambda_m'$ of two CHL models related by $W_e$ must be related as follows $$\Lambda_e'\cong {\rm span}_\mathbb{Z}\Bigl(\sqrt{e}\Lambda_e\cup \frac{1}{\sqrt{e}}\Lambda_m\Bigr)\subset \Lambda_m\otimes \mathbb{R}$$and $$\Lambda_m'\cong {\rm span}_\mathbb{Z}\Bigl(\frac{N}{\sqrt{e}}\Lambda_e\cup \sqrt{e}\Lambda_m\Bigr)\subset \Lambda_m\otimes \mathbb{R}\ .$$ We have verified these relations in several examples.
