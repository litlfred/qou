The ﬁnal step in identifying the T-dual theory with a CHL model is to determine the
Frame shape of the quantum symmetryQ of the orbifold. Once again, it is suﬃcient to
consider the case whereg has prime order. The previous construction shows that, among
the eigenvalues ofQ, 1 appears with multiplicitym(1) +m(N) and each primitive root of
unity appears with multiplicitym(N). Thus, the Frame shape ofQ is 1m(1)Nm(N), which
is the same asg. We conclude that heterotic T-duality maps each CHL model to itself,
possibly at a diﬀerent point in the moduli space.
