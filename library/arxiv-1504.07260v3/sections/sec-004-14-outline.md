In section [\[sec:CHL\]](#sec:CHL){reference-type="ref" reference="sec:CHL"} we introduce the CHL models that we shall analyse in the paper and we discuss them from the point of view of heterotic-type II duality. We also prove consistency of a non-geometric CHL models and present a partial classification. In section [\[s:Sdual\]](#s:Sdual){reference-type="ref" reference="s:Sdual"} we then prove that these CHL models are invariant under Fricke S-duality and analyse the implications for heterotic-type II duality. We also show that the Fricke S-duality in fact generalise to the full Atkin-Lehner involutions. In section [\[s:emlattices\]](#s:emlattices){reference-type="ref" reference="s:emlattices"} we perform a detailed analysis of the electric-magnetic charge lattices for certain CHL models and verify that they satisfy the N-modularity conditions that follow from Fricke S-duality. In section [\[sec:BPScount\]](#sec:BPScount){reference-type="ref" reference="sec:BPScount"} we calculate the 1/2 BPS-indices (i.e. the fourth helicity supertraces) for both the type IIA and the heterotic CHL models. In the type IIA picture we integrate this index against the fundamental domain of the modular group to obtain the one-loop topological string amplitude, from which we can extract the 1/2 BPS-saturated $R^2$-coupling and verify that this is invariant under Fricke S-duality, precisely when the CHL model is self-dual. In section [\[sec:conclusions\]](#sec:conclusions){reference-type="ref" reference="sec:conclusions"} we present some conclusions and suggestions for future work. Some technical details are relegated to the appendix. In appendix [\[s:nongeom\]](#s:nongeom){reference-type="ref" reference="s:nongeom"} we generalise our analysis to non-geometric CHL models where the level-matching condition does not hold. We verify that all of our results concerning Fricke S-duality and N-modularity also hold in this more general setting. In appendix [\[a:hetTdual\]](#a:hetTdual){reference-type="ref" reference="a:hetTdual"} we discuss Fricke T-duality in heterotic CHL models, and, finally, in appendix [\[s:lattices\]](#s:lattices){reference-type="ref" reference="s:lattices"} we provide tables listing the symmetries and various charge lattices in the CHL models we study.
