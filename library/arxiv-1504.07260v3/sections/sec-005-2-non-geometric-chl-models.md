[]{#sec:CHL label="sec:CHL"}

In this section we introduce the CHL models we will study in this paper. To this end we begin by recalling some standard facts about heterotic-type II dualities, after which we present the (non-geometric) CHL models and demonstrate their consistency. We also provide a partial classification of all such models.
