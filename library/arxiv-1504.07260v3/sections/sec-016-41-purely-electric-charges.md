The lattice of electric charges of the CHL model can be most conveniently described in the heterotic frame. Let $u,u^*$ denote two null vectors generating $\Gamma_{S^1}$, so that $$u\cdot u=u^*\cdot u^*=0\qquad u\cdot u^*=1\qquad \Gamma_{S^1}=\langle u, u^*\rangle\ .$$We can consider $u$ as representing a unit of winding along $S^1$ and $u^*$ as a unit of momentum. Let $g$ be a symmetry of order $N$ of the K3 model in the dual type IIA picture, so that $g$ acts by automorphisms of $\Gamma_{K3}\cong \Gamma^{4,20}$ and fixes $\Gamma_{S^1}$ and $\Gamma_{\tilde S^1}$. The symmetry $g$ fixes a sublattice $\Gamma_{K3}^g\subseteq \Gamma_{K3}$ of signature $(4,d)$, $0\le d\le 20$. In this section, we assume that both in the heterotic and in the type IIA picture, a $g$-twisted ground state satisfies the level matching condition, i.e. $\lambda=1$. This means that the shift $\delta$ can be taken to have the same order $N$ as the symmetry $g$, for example $$\delta:=u/N \ ,$$representing a shift along $S^1$ of $1/ N$-th of a period. The case $\lambda>1$ is considered in appendix [\[s:pureelectgen\]](#s:pureelectgen){reference-type="ref" reference="s:pureelectgen"}.

We can write a basis of perturbative states in the parent (unorbifolded) heterotic string picture as a tensor product $$|\mathcal{N};(\tilde p,p,v)\rangle=\mathcal{N}|(\tilde p,p,v)\rangle \ .$$where $|(\tilde p,p,v)\rangle$ is a lowest level state with Narain winding-momentum $$(\tilde p,p,v)\in \Gamma_{\tilde S^1}\oplus\Gamma_{S^1}\oplus\Gamma_{K3}\ ,$$and $\mathcal{N}$ is a combination of left- and right-moving oscillators.

The orbifold projection excludes $20-d$ right-moving bosonic oscillators, so that the gauge group of the low energy effective field theory is reduced from $U(1)^{28}$ to $U(1)^{8+d}$ and the state $|\mathcal{N};(\tilde p,p,v)\rangle$ has electric charge $$\tilde p+p+\Pi_g(v)\in \mathbb{R}^{4,4+d}\ ,$$with respect to this reduced gauge group. Here, $$\Pi_g(v):=\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^N g^i(v)\in \Gamma_{K3}^g\otimes \mathbb{R}\ ,\qquad v\in \Gamma_{K3}\ ,$$ is the projection of $v\in \Gamma_{K3}$ onto the $g$-invariant subspace $\Gamma_{K3}^g\otimes \mathbb{R}$. Since $\Gamma_{K3}$ is self-dual, it follows that $$\Pi_g(\Gamma_{K3})=(\Gamma_{K3}^g)^*\ .$$For each electric charge vector $\tilde p+p+\Pi_g(v)$, one can find a configuration of oscillators such that the corresponding state is $\hat g$-invariant.

We conclude that the lattice of electric charges for the untwisted sector is $$\label{untwproj} \Lambda_e^{U}=(\Gamma_{K3}^g)^*\oplus \Gamma_{S^1} \oplus \Gamma_{\tilde S^1}\ .$$In the $\hat g$-twisted sector, *before* the projection over the $\hat g$-invariant states, the possible $U(1)^{8+d}$ charges form a translate of the lattice [\[untwproj\]](#untwproj){reference-type="eqref" reference="untwproj"} by $\delta=u/ N$. After projecting onto the $\hat g$ invariant subspace, we expect only a subset of these charges to survive, and this subset should form a translate of the lattice [\[untwproj\]](#untwproj){reference-type="eqref" reference="untwproj"}, in order to have a consistent OPE between untwisted and twisted states.

More generally, since the spectrum of the orbifold is generated by the OPE of $\hat g$-invariant $\hat g$-twisted fields, we obtain that the lattice of electric charges is

$$\label{electLattsimpl} \Lambda_e=(\Gamma_{K3}^g)^*\oplus \langle \frac{u}{N}, u^*\rangle \oplus \Gamma_{\tilde S^1}\ ,\qquad \text{for }\lambda=1\ .$$ We stress that this result is only valid when $\lambda=1$, i.e. when the level matching condition is satisfied.
