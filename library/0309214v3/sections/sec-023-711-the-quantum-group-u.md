The quantum group U
The quantum group U = Uq(g) associated to g is a Hopf algebra deﬁned over
Q(v), where v is the usual quantum parameter (see [17, 22]). Here our v is
the same as v of Lusztig [22] and is equal to q of Jantzen [17], while our q is
v2. The standard generators of U are Eα, Fα, Kα for α ∈{α1, . . . , αℓ}. For a
full set of relations, as well as a good introduction to quantum groups, see [17].
Note that all the Kα’s commute with each other.
