In this appendix we will prove Proposition 7.3 for the rank 2 Lie algebras A2
and B2. We will achieve this by a brute-force calculation.
First, let us discuss some simpliﬁcation, due to symmetry. The transition ma-
trix of U leaves invariant each of U+, U−, U0. On U0 the transition matrix is
identity. Hence it’s enough to consider the restriction of the transition matrix
in U−and U+. Furthermore, the Cartan symmetry (the operator τ of [17])
reduces the case of U+ to that of U−.
