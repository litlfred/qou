For each fundamental root α ∈ {α1, . . . , αℓ} there is an algebra automorphism
Tα : U → U , as described in [17, Chapter 8]. These automorphisms satis fy the
following relations, known as the braid relations, or Coxeter moves .
If ( α, β) = 0, then TαTβ = TβTα .
If ( α, β) = −1, then TαTβTα = TβTαTβ .
If ( α, β) = −2, then TαTβTαTβ = TβTαTβTα .
If ( α, β) = −3, then TαTβTαTβTαTβ = TβTαTβTαTβTα .
Note that the Weyl group is generated by sα with exactly the above relations,
replacing Tα by sα , and the extra relations s2
