Theorem 1 follows from the fact that the colored Jones function can be built
from elementary blocks that are q–holonomic, and the operations that patch
the blocks together to give the colored Jones function preserve q–holonomicity.
IN Bernstein deﬁned the notion of holonomic functions f : Rr −→C, [2, 3].
For an excellent and complete account, see Bjork [4]. Zeilberger’s brilliant idea
was to link the abstract notion of holonomicity to the concrete problem of algo-
rithmically proving combinatorial identities among hypergeometric functions,
see [35, 33] and also [28]. This opened an entirely new view on combinatorial
identities.
Sabbah extended Bernstein’s approach to holonomic functions and deﬁned the
notion of a q–holonomic function, see [31] and also [6].
