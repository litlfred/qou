Our luck with the colored Jones function is that we can identify it with a
multisum of a q–proper hypergeometric function. Are we really lucky, or is
there some deeper explanation? We believe that there is a underlying geomet-
ric reason for coincidence, which in a sense explains the underlying geometry
of topological quantum ﬁeld theory. We will postpone to a later publication
applications of this principle to Hyperbolic Geometry; [11].
