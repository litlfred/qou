A precise determination of the electromagnetic self-energy contribution to Mp −Mn remains
an outstanding theoretical challenge. We have updated the Cottingham determination of δMγ using
modern theoretical knowledge and experimental determinations of the nucleon structure functions,
Eq. (3.4). In the process, a technical oversight in the old determination was uncovered invalidating
the result. The limiting factor in a precise determination comes from modeling the behavior of the
unknown subtraction function between the low and high Q2 limits where it is known. Presently, the
large uncertainty can be traced directly to the poorly determined isovector magnetic polarizability.
Once this quantity is precisely known, an improved understanding of T p−n
1
(0,Q2) will be required
to make progress.
