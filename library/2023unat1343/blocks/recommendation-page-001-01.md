Raschdorf, a former staff member of UNAMI, contested three administration decisions: (i) the administrative decision not to renew her fixed-term appointment beyond 31 May 2019; (ii) the 1 September 2020 decision of the Division of Healthcare Management and Occupational Safety and Health (DHMOSH) not to recommend her for a disability pension to the United Nations Staff Pension Committee (UNSPC); and (iii) the 5 November 2020 decision of the Advisory Board on Compensation Claims (ABCC) to reject her claim for compensation on the ground that her claim was time-barred.
