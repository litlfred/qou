(Analyticity) For every N ≥1, the amplitudes of QHFT bordisms supported by X = (Y, LF, α±) vary analytically with the boundary structure in Def(X), up to sign and multiplication by Nth roots of unity. This follows immediately from the fact that the matrix dilogarithms are analytic, together with the fact that any path in Def(X) lifts to a path of log-branches via the relations induced by Deﬁnition 5.2 (4).
