For parabolic elements g ∈PSL(2, R) we have two conjugacy classes, that can be distinguished by a sign. So, replacing PSL(2, C) with PSL(2, R), the constructions of this section still work by associating a sign to each parabolic end of the surface S \ V . Note that for PSL(2, R)-valued cocycles the idealization procedure described in Section 3.3 below gives only degenerate triangles and tetrahedra (with real shapes or cross-ratio moduli).
