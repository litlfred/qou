Exponential shear-bend parameters do not depend on branchings but the orientation of F (taking the opposite branching edge orientation simultaneously exchanges the left and right tri- angles). However, branchings govern all choices in QHFT tensors. Also, they allow us to interpret exponential shear-bend parameters as exponential I-parameters, thus coming from 1-cocycles repre- senting arbitrary PSL(2, C)-characters on the triangulated boundary of arbitrary compact orientable 3-manifolds. When C has no trivial entries, the maps W ± deﬁne decorated shear-bend parameter spaces similar to those occurring in [22, 26].
