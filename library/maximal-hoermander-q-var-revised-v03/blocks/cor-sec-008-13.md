Let Ω⊆Rd be open non-empty equipped with Lebesgue measure. Let A be the unbounded selfadjoint operator on L2(Ω) associated to the form a : H1 0(Ω) × H1 0(Ω) →C a(u, v) = Z Ω d X j,k=1 akj∂ku∂jvdx, where akj = ajk : Ω→R are bounded measurable such that the principal part is elliptic.
