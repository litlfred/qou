Let P : Rd →R+ be a measurable function taking positive values and P(D) the associated positive self-adjoint Fourier multiplier. Assume that e−tP (D) is lattice positive (more generally, |e−tP (D)f| ⩽St|f| for some positive contraction semigroup St : L2(Rd) →L2(Rd)). Let f ∈D(P(D)δ) with δ > 2 (e.g. if |P(ξ)| ∼= |ξ|a, then f ∈Hs(Rd) with s > 2a suﬃces). Then eitP (D)f(x) →f(x) a.e. x ∈Rd as t →0+.
