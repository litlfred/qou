In NLEFT, the degrees of freedom are nucleons and pions rather than quarks and gluons as in lattice
QCD. Hence, we have to discretize the previous Hamiltonians in all spatial dimensions. If one uses
Monte Carlo methods and the inﬁnite time extrapolation, then one also has to discretize it in its temporal
dimension. In the following we explain how to put the system in a discrete box of ﬁnite volume and how
we could use ﬁnite volume eﬀects to extract physical information in the inﬁnite volume. Moreover, we
explain the Monte Carlo methods and discuss limits of this method like the sign problem.
