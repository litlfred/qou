In the following we do a benchmark calculation for L = 4 and a = 1.97 fm which we can compare
with an exact Lanczos calculation. Then we do the calculation for L = 6 and a = 1.97 fm, L = 7 and
a = 1.64 fm and L = 9 and a = 1.32 fm. The physical box length is between 11 fm and 12 fm, and it
is large enough that ﬁnite volume errors will be within truncation errors due to chiral expansion and
uncertainties in the respective low-energy coupling constants (LECs).
