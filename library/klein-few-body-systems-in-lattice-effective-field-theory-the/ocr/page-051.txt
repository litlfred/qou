This is also a good check for other regularizations procedures. We will use this condition to find the best

Given that the typical extension of a nucleon is given by a scale of r ~ 0.85 fm, this means that the EFT
can be used for all momenta that do not lead to a resolution of the internal nucleon structure, at least in
the pionless theory. A direct comparison with the results of a continuum calculation is difficult because
