Beside the contact interaction, we have also long-range interactions at leading order. Before the modern
formulation of an effective field theory, one already assumed the one-pion exchange as a central part of
the theory going back to Yukawa [76]. Obviously, a cutoff is necessary due to the singular structure at the
origin which was difficult to explain before the full understanding of effective field theories. An early
work on the one-pion exchange, the cutoff dependence and deuteron observables can be found in [77].
