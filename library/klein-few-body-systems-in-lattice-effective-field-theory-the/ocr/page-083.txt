The N2LO three-boson interaction proportional to C3, is particularly interesting. We have used it to
cancel lattice errors at O(a’), and it does indeed remove residual errors in the structure and binding of
bound states with three or more bosons. We note that for zero-range bosons in three spatial dimensions,
the three-boson interaction is required at LO to properly renormalize the theory [67, 69], see also [157].
