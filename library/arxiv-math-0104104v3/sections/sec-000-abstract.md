on quaternionic ﬂag manifolds, which geometrically determ ine the Bruhat
cell decomposition. These structures naturally descend fr om the sym-
plectic group Sp(n), and are related to the dressing action given by the
Iwasawa decomposition of the general linear group over the q uaternions,
GLn(H).
