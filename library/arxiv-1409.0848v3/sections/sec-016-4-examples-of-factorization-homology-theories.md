\label{section:examples}

In this section we give examples of factorization homology over stratified spaces. To illustrate the relevance to low-dimensional topology, we show that the free $\disk_{3,1}^{\fr}$-algebra can distinguish the homotopy type of link complements, and in particular defines a non-trivial link invariant.
