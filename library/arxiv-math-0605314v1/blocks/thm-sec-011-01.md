We call $J_K(P''_n)$ the *$n$th reduced Jones polynomial* of $K$. It is clear that $J_K(P''_0)=1$. For $n=1$, we have $$J_K(P''_1) = (J_K(\V_1)-[2])/\{2\}\{3\}.$$ The quantity $-q^{-2}J_K(P''_1)$ is known as the *reduced Jones polynomial* of $K$, and appears in the original paper of Jones [@Jones2 Proposition 12.5].
