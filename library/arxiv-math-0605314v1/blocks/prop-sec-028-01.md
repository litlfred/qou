[\[r56\]](#r56){reference-type="ref" reference="r56"} shows that Conjecture 6.1 in our previous paper [@H:cyclotomic] is false. It follows that we can not prove, using only properties of $\Zqh$, that the $\tau _\zeta (M)$ for any infinitely many $\zeta \in \calZ$ would determine $J_M$ and hence $\tau (M)$. It should be remarked that it is still open whether the WRT invariants at any infinitely many roots of unity determine $J_M$ or not.
