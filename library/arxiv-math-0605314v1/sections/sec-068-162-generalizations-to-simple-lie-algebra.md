[]{#sec:general label="sec:general"}

In a joint work with Le [@H-Le:in-preparation], for each simple Lie algebra $\g$, we will generalize the invariant $J_M\in \Zqh$ of integral homology sphere $M$ to an invariant $J^\g_M\in \Zqh$. This invariant can be characterized by the property that for each root of unity $\zeta$ such that the quantum $\g$ invariant $\tau ^\g_M$ is well-defined, we have $\mods _\zeta (J^\g_M)=\tau ^\g_\zeta$. This specialization property holds also for the projective quantum $\g$ invariant. See [@Ohtsuki5 Conjecture 7.29] for a precise statement.
