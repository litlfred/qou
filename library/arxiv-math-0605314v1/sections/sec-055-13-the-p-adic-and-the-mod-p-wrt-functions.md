[]{#sec13 label="sec13"}

In this section, we first observe that the invariant $J_M\in \Zqh$ can be formally evaluated at any element in a commutative, unital ring, and in particular at any complex number. Then we introduce the $p$-adic analytic versions and mod $p$ versions of the WRT functions.

Most constructions in this section can be applied to any invariants of links and $3$-manifolds which take values in $\Zqh$.
