[]{#sec:filtration-completion label="sec:filtration-completion"}

Here we introduce filtrations for the algebras $\Uq$ and $\Uqv$, and also for the tensor powers $\Uqvn$ of $\Uqv$. These filtrations produce the associated completions. The definitions below are equivalent to those in [@H:uqsl2].

First, we consider the filtration and the completion for $\Uq$. For $p\ge 0$, set $$\modF _p(\Uq)=\Uq e^p\Uq,$$ the two-sided ideal in $\Uq$ generated by $e^p$. Let $\tUq$ denote the "completion in $U_h$" of $\Uq$ with respect to the decreasing filtration $\{\modF _p(\Uq)\}_{p\ge 0}$, i.e., $\tUq$ is the image of the homomorphism $$\label{eq:6}
  \varprojlim_{p\ge 0} \Uq/\modF _p(\Uq) \rightarrow  U_h$$ induced by $\Uq\subset U_h$. (Conjecturally, [\[eq:6\]](#eq:6){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:6"} is injective, see [@H:uqsl2 Conjecture 7.2].) Clearly, $\tUq$ is a $\Zqq$-subalgebra of $U_h$.

Second, we consider $\Uqv$. For $p\ge 0$, set $$\modF _p(\Uqv)=\modF _p(\Uq)\cap \Uqv=\Uqv e^p\Uqv.$$ We have a $(\modZ /2\modZ )$-grading $\modF _p(\Uq)=\modF _p(\Uqv)\oplus K\modF _p(\Uqv)$. Let $\tUqv$ denote the "completion in $U_h$" of $\Uqv$ with respect to $\{\modF _p(\Uqv)\}_{p\ge 0}$, i.e., the image of $$\varprojlim_{p\ge 0}\Uqv/\modF _p(\Uqv)\rightarrow U_h.$$ Note that $\tUqv$ is a $\Zqq$-subalgebra of $\tUq$, and $\tUq$ has a $(\modZ /2\modZ )$-graded $\Zqq$-algebra structure $$\tUq=\tUqv\oplus K\tUqv$$ induced by [\[eq:56\]](#eq:56){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:56"}.

Note that the elements of $\tUq$ (resp. $\tUqv$) are the elements of $U_h$ that can be expressed as infinite sums $  \sum_{i=0}^\infty \sum_{j=1}^{N_i} x_{i,j}e^iy_{i,j},$ where $N_0,N_1,\ldots\ge 0$, and $x_{i,j},y_{i,j}\in \Uq$ (resp. $x_{i,j},y_{i,j}\in \Uqv$) for $i\ge 0$, $1\le j\le N_i$.

Now, we define the filtration for $\Uqvn$, $n\ge 1$, by $$\modF _p(\Uqvn)
  =\sum_{i=1}^n\Uqvx{(i-1)}\otimes \modF _p(\Uqv)\otimes \Uqvx{(n-i)}\subset \Uqvn.$$ Thus, an element of $\Uqvn$ is in the $p$th filtration if and only if it is expressed as a sum of terms each having at least one tensor factor in the $p$th filtration. Define the "completed tensor product" $\tUqvn= \tUqv\tO\cdots\tO\tUqvn$ to be the "completion in $\Uhn$" of $\Uqvn$ with respect to this filtration, i.e., the image of the homomorphism $$\varprojlim_{p\ge 0}\Uqvn/\modF _p(\Uqvn)\rightarrow \Uhn.$$ For $n=0$, it is natural to set $$\modF _p(\Uqvx{0})=\modF _p(\Zqq)=
  \begin{cases}
    \Zqq&\text{if $p=0$,}\\
    0&\text{otherwise.}
  \end{cases}$$ Thus we have $$(\tUqv)^{\tO 0} = \Zqq.$$

In what follows, we will also need the filtrations and completions of other iterated tensor products of $\Uq$ and $\Uqv$, whose definitions should be obvious from the above definitions. For example, $\Uq\otimes \Uqv$ has a filtration defined by $$\modF _p(\Uq\otimes \Uqv)=\modF _p(\Uq)\otimes \Uqv+\Uq\otimes \modF _p(\Uqv).$$ Moreover, $\tUq\tO\tUqv$ is defined to be the image of $\varprojlim_{p\ge 0}\modF _p(\Uq\otimes \Uqv)\rightarrow U_h^{\ho2}$.
