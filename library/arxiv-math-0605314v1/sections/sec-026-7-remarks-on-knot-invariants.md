In this section we discuss some consequences of the results in Section [\[sec6\]](#sec6){reference-type="ref" reference="sec6"}. The reader may skip this section in the first reading, since it is not necessary for the proof of the existence of the invariants $J_M$ for integral homology spheres.

Throughout this section, let $T$ be a bottom knot and let $K=\cl(T)$ be the closure of $T$. By abuse of notation, we set $J_K=J_T\in Z(\tUqv)$.
