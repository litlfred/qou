[]{#sec:an-alternative-proof label="sec:an-alternative-proof"}

Here we give an alternative proof of Theorem [\[thm:41\]](#thm:41){reference-type="ref" reference="thm:41"} which does not use Theorem [\[r32\]](#r32){reference-type="ref" reference="r32"}, but uses the existence of the invariant $\tau _\zeta (M)$.

Let $M$ and $L$ be as in Section [\[sec:defin-unif-sl2\]](#sec:defin-unif-sl2){reference-type="ref" reference="sec:defin-unif-sl2"}. Let $(J_M)_L$ denote the right-hand side of [\[eq:42\]](#eq:42){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:42"}. Let $L'$ be another link such that $(S^3)_{L'}=M$. We have to show that $(J_M)_L=(J_M)_{L'}$. By Theorem [\[thm:65\]](#thm:65){reference-type="ref" reference="thm:65"}, we have $\mods _\zeta ((J_M)_L)=\tau _\zeta (M)=\mods _\zeta ((J_M)_{L'})$ for each $\zeta \in \calZ$. (Note that we do not need the invariance of $J_M$ in the proof of Theorem [\[thm:65\]](#thm:65){reference-type="ref" reference="thm:65"}.) Hence, Proposition [\[r85\]](#r85){reference-type="ref" reference="r85"} implies $(J_M)_L=(J_M)_{L'}$. This completes the proof.

Similar idea has been used in [@Beliakova-Blanchet-Le; @Le4]. See Section [\[sec:rati-homol-spher\]](#sec:rati-homol-spher){reference-type="ref" reference="sec:rati-homol-spher"}.
