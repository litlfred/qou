We use the following version of Fenn-Rourke’s theorem, whic h was conjectured
by Hoste [28].
Theorem 10.1 ([21]). Two admissible framed linksL andL′ inS3 yield orientation-
preserving homeomorphic results of surgery if and only if L and L′ are related by
a sequence of isotopies and Hoste moves . Here a Hoste move is deﬁned to be a
Fenn-Rourke move between two admissible framed links.
