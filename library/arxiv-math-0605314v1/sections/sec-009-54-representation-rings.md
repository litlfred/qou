[]{#sec:representation-ring- label="sec:representation-ring-"}

For a commutative ring $A$ with unit, let $\modR _A$ denote the $A$-algebra $$\modR _A = \Span_A\{\V_n\zzzvert n\ge 0\}.$$ with the multiplication induced by tensor product. Since each finite-dimensional representation of $U_h$ is a direct sum of copies of $\V_n$, $n\ge 0$, we may regard $\modR _A$ as the *representation ring* of $U_h$ over $A$.

By the well-known isomorphism of left $U_h$-modules $$\V_m\otimes \V_n \cong
  \V_{|m-n|}\oplus\V_{|m-n|+2}\oplus\cdots\oplus\V_{m+n},$$ we have the identity in $\modR _A$ $$\V_m\V_n
  = \V_{|m-n|}+\V_{|m-n|+2}+\cdots+\V_{m+n}.$$ As an $A$-algebra, $\modR _A$ is freely generated by $\V_1$, i.e., $\modR _A\cong A[\V_1]$. Thus we identify $\modR _A$ with $A[\V_1]$.

For $y=\sum_n a_n \V_n$ ($a_n\in \modQ [[h]]$) and $x\in U_h$, we set $$\trq^{y}(x) = \sum_{n}a_n\trq^{\V_n}(x).$$ Thus we have bilinear maps $$\begin{gathered}
  \trq^{-}(-)\zzzcolon \modR _{\Zvv}\times U_h\rightarrow \modQ [[h]],\\
  \trq^{-}(-)\zzzcolon \modR _{\modQ (v)}\times U_h\rightarrow \modQ ((h)),
\end{gathered}$$ where $\modQ ((h))$ denote the quotient field of $\modQ [[h]]$.

Similarly, for a link $L$ we have multilinear maps $$\begin{gathered}
  J_L\zzzcolon \modR _{\Zvv}\times \cdots\times \modR _{\Zvv}\rightarrow  \modZ [v^{1/2},v^{-1/2}],\\
  J_L\zzzcolon \modR _{\modQ (v)}\times \cdots\times \modR _{\modQ (v)}\rightarrow  \modQ (v^{1/2}).
\end{gathered}$$ If the framing of every component of $L$ is even, then $J_L$ above take values in $\Zvv$, $\modQ (v)$, respectively.
