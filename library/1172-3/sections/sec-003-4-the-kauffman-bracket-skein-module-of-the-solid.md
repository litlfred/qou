We use the Kauﬀman bracket skein module $B=B(V)$ of the solid
torus $V=S^{1}\cross D^{2}$. For details, see [BHMV].
We use the familiar commutative $\mathrm{Z}[A, A^{-}1]$-algebra structure of $B$.
$B$is a polynomial algebra $\mathrm{Z}[A, A^{-1}][z]$, where $z$is represented by the
framed knot $K$in $V$which winds just once along the core of $V$.
Let $B^{\mathrm{e}\mathrm{v}\mathrm{e}\mathrm{n}}$denote the $\mathrm{Z}[A, A^{-}1]$-subalgebra of $B$generated by $z^{2}$,
and set $B^{\mathrm{o}\mathrm{d}\mathrm{d}}=zB^{\mathrm{e}\mathrm{v}\mathrm{e}\mathrm{n}}$. Then the direct sum decomposition $B=$
$B^{\mathrm{e}\mathrm{v}\mathrm{e}\mathrm{n}}\oplus B^{\mathrm{o}\mathrm{d}\mathrm{d}}$gives a $\mathrm{Z}/2$-graded algebra structure on $B$.
In [BHMV] the natural basis $\{e_{i}|i=0,1,2, \ldots\}$of $B$is deﬁned in-
ductively by $e_{0}--1,$$e_{1}=z$and $ze_{j}--e_{j-}1+e_{j+1}$for $j\geq 1$. We have
$e_{j}\in B^{\mathrm{e}\mathrm{v}\mathrm{e}\mathrm{n}}$if $j$is even and, $e_{j}\in B^{\mathrm{o}\mathrm{d}\mathrm{d}}$otherwise. Each $e_{i}$corresponds
to the $(i+1)$ -dimensional irreducible representation of the quantum
group $U_{q}(sl_{2})$.
