2 The Helon Model
The helon model depicts elementary particles as a braid diag ram of three twisted ribbons.
The spinor degrees of freedom such as chirality and particle /anti-particle are encoded by
the braids, while the charges are encoded by the twists of the ribbons. CPT invariance
of particle states in this model will involve both, the braid data, and the twists in helon
model diagrams. The original paper presented four diﬀerent braid diagrams with two
braid operators [18], while later work appeared to present o nly two [19].
