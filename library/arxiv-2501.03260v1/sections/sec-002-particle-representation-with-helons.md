The gauge bosons are also assigned braided ribbon structure , but the gauge bosons
are only given twists and are not associated with any braid op erators. The electric

proposes that the electrically-neutral Z-boson combines a twist/anti-twist that does not
annihilate each other, yet is found on each of the three ribbo ns without any braiding.
