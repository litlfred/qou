Temperley-Lieb algebra. If δ1 and δ2 are two non-zero scalars, one deﬁnes TL(n, δ1, δ2) as being the subspace of Pn(∅) spanned by the tangles with no closed loops. Deﬁning multiplication on TL(n, δ1, δ2) by multiplication as in Pn(∅) except that one multiplies by a factor δ1 for each loop and δ2 for each loop , then discarding the loop. Clearly the map from Pn(∅) to TL(n, δ1, δ2) given by multiplying by δ1’s or δ2’s then discarding loops, gives a Φ exhibiting TL(n, δ1, δ2) as a planar algebra. For general values of δ1 and δ2, TL(n) is not non-degenerate. An extreme case is δ1 = δ2 = 1 where Z(c(T1 −T2)) = 0 for all relevant tangles c, T1, T2.
