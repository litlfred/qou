For $d\geq 14$, let ${\mbox{\boldmath${i}$}}, {\mbox{\boldmath${i}$}}'\in[n, d]$ be the functions which give the perfect $({\mbox{\boldmath${i}$}}, {\mbox{\boldmath${i}$}}')$-coloring of $\pi$ below. $$\includegraphics{diss44.pdf}$$ For an $r$-cycle $\sigma\in S_d$ to satisfy $\sigma(i_j)=i'_j$ for $j=1,\ldots, n$, $\sigma$ must fix the $3=b$ numbers $3, 9, 10$, and map $$\label{chains} 1\to 5\to 13,\qquad 2\to 14,\qquad 4\to 11,\qquad 7\to 8\to 6\to 12.$$ Clearly, such an $r$-cycle exists if and only if $r\geq11=a-b$ and $d\geq r+3=r+b$.
