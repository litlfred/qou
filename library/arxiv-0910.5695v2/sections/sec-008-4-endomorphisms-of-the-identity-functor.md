In this section we study endomorphisms of the identity functor on Re p0(St;F )
constructed using certain central elements in group algebras of symmetric groups.
These endomorphisms of the identity functor will play a key role in describing the
blocks in Re p(St;F ). This role is analogous to the role the Casimir element plays
in Lie theory.
