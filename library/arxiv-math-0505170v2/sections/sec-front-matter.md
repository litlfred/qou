AN AVERAGING PROCESS FOR UNIPOTENT GROUP
ACTIONS
AMNON YEKUTIELI
Abstract. We present an averaging process for sections of a torsor under a
unipotent group. This process allows one to integrate local sections of such a
torsor into a global simplicial section. The results of this paper have applica-
tions to deformation quantization of algebraic varieties.
