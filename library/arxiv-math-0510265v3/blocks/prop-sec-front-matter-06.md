Only the middle isomorphism is non-trivial. Let Bi,i+1 = R ⊗Ri,i+1 R{−3}, where Ri,i+1 is the ring of invariants under the action of S3 on R permuting xi, xi+1, xi+2. Soergel shows Bi ⊗R Bi+1 ⊗R Bi ∼= Bi,i+1 ⊕Bi, Bi+1 ⊗R Bi ⊗R Bi+1 ∼= Bi,i+1 ⊕Bi+1, which implies the middle isomorphism.
