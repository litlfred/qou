If braid words σ and eσ represent the same element of the braid group then complexes F(σ) and F(eσ) are isomorphic in B(R). See Rouquier [R, Section 3] for a proof of this proposition and of more general results. In particular, the tensor product F(σi) ⊗R F(σ−1 i ) is chain homotopy equivalent to the complex 0 −→R −→0 of R-bimodules. The tensor product over R is a bifunctor B(R) × B(R) −→B(R), and each object N of B(R) gives rise to an endofunctor of the category B(R) which takes a complex M to the tensor product M ⊗R N.
