Coeﬃcients of polynomials Py,w are nonnegative integers. The multiplication in the basis {C′ w} has coeﬃcients in N[q, q−1]. Kazhdan-Lusztig’s proof of this result [KL2] is essentially a categoriﬁca- tion: coeﬃcients of Py,w are realized as dimensions of cohomology groups associated to simple perverse sheaves on the ﬂag variety G/B, where G = SL(m, C) and B a Borel subgroup of G. Multiplication in the basis {C′ w} corresponds to the convolution of correspondences on the product variety G/B×G/B.
