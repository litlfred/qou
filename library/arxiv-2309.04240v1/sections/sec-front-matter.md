BURAU REPRESENTATION OF BRAID GROUPS AND
q-RATIONALS
SOPHIE MORIER-GENOUD, VALENTIN OVSIENKO, AND ALEXANDER VESELOV
Abstract. We establish a link between the new theory of q-deformed ratio-
nal numbers and the classical Burau representation of the braid group B3. We
apply this link to the open problem of classiﬁcation of faithful complex spe-
cializations of this representation. As a result we provide an answer to this
problem in terms of the singular set of the q-rationals and prove the faithful-
ness of the Burau representation specialized at complex t ∈C∗outside the
annulus 3 −2
