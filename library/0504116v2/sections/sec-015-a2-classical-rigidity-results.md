Let N be a smooth 3–manifold without boundary and supposeρ0 : π1(N)→ PSL(2, C)
is the holonomy representation for a complete ﬁnite-volume hyperbolic structure on N .
The following result is due to Weil [16], and can be considered as a local version of
Mostow’s rigidity Theorem for compact hyperbolic 3–manifolds.
Theorem A.2 Suppose N is compact. Then H1(π1(N); sl(2, C);ρ0) = 0.
Suppose now that N compactiﬁes to a manifold ¯N with non-empty boundary ∂ ¯N =
T1⊔... ⊔ Tk . In this case ρ0 admits non-trivial deformations, so we cannot expect
H1(π1(N); sl(2, C);ρ0) to be trivial. If K is a subgroup of π1(N), the natural injection
iK : K→π1(N) induces a map on cohomology
i∗
K : H1(π1(N); sl(2, C);ρ0)→ H1(K; sl(2, C);ρ0◦ iK).
