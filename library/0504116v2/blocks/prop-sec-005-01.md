Now for i = 1, 2 we have tan αi = 2 cot(αi/2)/(cot2(αi/2) − 1) = 2 exp Lθ(ei6)/(exp(2Lθ(ei6)) −1). Combining these equations we ﬁnally get exp σθ(F126) = sinh Lθ(e16)/ sinh Lθ(e26).
