Let ∆be a partially truncated tetrahedron and let ∆(1) be the set of edges of ∆. The geometric realizations of ∆are parameterized up to isometry by the dihedral angle assignments θ : ∆(1) →(0, π) such that for each vertex v of ∆, if e1, e2, e3 are the edges that emanate from v, then θ(e1) + θ(e2) + θ(e3) is equal to π for ideal v and less than π for non-ideal v.
