1.2. Triangulations

Let N be a compact orientable manifold and let N be obtained from N by removing
the toric components of ON. We define a partially truncated triangulation of N to
be a realization of N as a gluing of some A*’s along a pairing of the lateral hexagons
induced by a simplicial pairing of the faces of the A’s. When N is endowed with a
hyperbolic structure, a partially truncated triangulation of N is called geometric if, for
each tetrahedron A of the triangulation, the pull-back to A* of the Riemannian metric
of N defines a geometric realization of A. Equivalently, the hyperbolic structure of N
should be obtained by gluing geometric realizations of the A’s along isometries of their
lateral hexagons.
