FROM STATE INTEGRALS TO q-SERIES
STAVROS GAROUFALIDIS AND RINAT KASHAEV
Abstract. It is well-known to the experts that multi-dimensional state integrals of prod-
ucts of Faddeev’s quantum dilogarithm which arise in Quantum Topology can be written
as ﬁnite sums of products of basic hypergeometric series in q = e2πiτ and ˜q = e−2πi/τ. We
illustrate this fact by giving a detailed proof for a family of one-dimensional integrals which
includes state-integral invariants of 41 and 52 knots.
