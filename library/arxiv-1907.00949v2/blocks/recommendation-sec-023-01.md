We seek the solution to $$\label{eq:dis} \begin{tabular}{rl} maximize & $ \mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(Y^{\scriptscriptstyle\mathsf{T}}MY)$ \\[1ex] subject to & $\llbracket Y \rrbracket\in \mathop{\mathrm{Flag}}(n_1,\dots,n_d; n)$. \end{tabular}$$ Here $Y \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times n_d}$, $Y^{\scriptscriptstyle\mathsf{T}}Y = 1$, and the objective function is well-defined as a function on the flag manifold: If we have $Y$ and $Y'$ with $\llbracket Y \rrbracket= \llbracket Y'\rrbracket$, then they must be related as in [\[eq:rep\]](#eq:rep){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:rep"} and thus $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(Y^{\scriptscriptstyle\mathsf{T}}MY) = \mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(Y^{\prime{\scriptscriptstyle\mathsf{T}}} MY')$.
