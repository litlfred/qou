Theorem 2.5. Let G be a compact Lie group with a bi-invariant metric g and H be a subgroup
such that M =G/H is a smooth manifold (e.g., H is closed subgroup). Then M =G/H together

geodesics on a Riemannian manifold. But in the case of a geodesic orbit space, since its geodesics
are simply orbits of one-parameter subgroups of G, the task reduces to determining the latter. The
next result [18, Theorem 1.3.5] will be helpful towards this end.
