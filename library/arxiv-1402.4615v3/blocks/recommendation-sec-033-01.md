By Lemma [\[lem:SC\]](#lem:SC){reference-type="ref" reference="lem:SC"} [\[eq:SC-1\]](#eq:SC-1){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:SC-1"}, $g_{\mu_1,\mu_2,(1^l)}$, and hence $H^{(i,j)}_{\mu_1,\mu_2,(1^l)}$, vanishes unless $$|\mu_1|+\ell(\mu_1) + |\mu_2|+\ell(\mu_2) \ge 2l \ge 2(i+j).$$ Comparing it with the conditions under the first summation symbol of [\[eq:to_bound\]](#eq:to_bound){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:to_bound"}, we have, in fact, equalities instead of inequalities above, *i. e.* $$l = i+j = |\mu_1|+\ell(\mu_1) = |\mu_2|+\ell(\mu_2).$$ Moreover, in this case, by Lemma [\[lem:SC\]](#lem:SC){reference-type="ref" reference="lem:SC"} [\[eq:SC-1\]](#eq:SC-1){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:SC-1"}, we have that $g_{\mu_1, \mu_2; (1^{i+j})} = 0$ unless $\mu_1 \cup \mu_2 = 1^{i+j}$, which means that $\mu_1 = \mu_2 = (1^{(i+j)/2})$ (in particular, $i+j$ must be even).
