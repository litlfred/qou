As in the previous sections, most of our objects are indexed by integer partitions. Therefore it will be useful to use some short notations for small modifications (adding or removing a box or a part) of partitions. We denote by $\mu \cup (r)$ ($\mu \setminus (r)$, respectively) the partition obtained from $\mu$ by adding (deleting, respectively) one part equal to $r$. We denote by $\mu_{\downarrow r}=\mu \setminus (r) \cup (r-1)$ the partition obtained from $\mu$ by removing one box in a row of size $r$. The reader might wonder what do $\mu \setminus (r)$ and $\mu_{\downarrow r}$ mean if $\mu$ does not have a part equal to $r$: we will not use these notations in this context. Finally, if $i$ is an inner corner of $\lambda$, we denote by $\lambda^{(i)}$ the diagram obtained from $\lambda$ by adding a box at place $i$.
