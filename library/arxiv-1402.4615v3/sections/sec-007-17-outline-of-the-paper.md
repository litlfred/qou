The paper is organized as follows. Section [\[SectDefKerov\]](#SectDefKerov){reference-type="ref" reference="SectDefKerov"} gives all definitions and background on Jack characters, free cumulants and Kerov polynomials. In Section [\[SectPolynomial\]](#SectPolynomial){reference-type="ref" reference="SectPolynomial"} we prove the polynomiality of the coefficients of Kerov's polynomials, with bounds on the degrees. Then our second main result (that is the polynomiality of structure constants, with precise bound on the degree) is proved in Section [\[SectStructureConstants\]](#SectStructureConstants){reference-type="ref" reference="SectStructureConstants"}. Section [\[SectSpecialValues\]](#SectSpecialValues){reference-type="ref" reference="SectSpecialValues"} presents technical statements on structure constants, that will be used in the analysis of large Young diagrams. The last three sections deal with convergence results for large Young diagrams: Section [\[sect:FirstOrder\]](#sect:FirstOrder){reference-type="ref" reference="sect:FirstOrder"} presents the first order asymptotics, Section [\[sect:CLT\]](#sect:CLT){reference-type="ref" reference="sect:CLT"} gives the central limit theorem for Jack characters and Section [\[sect:CLT2\]](#sect:CLT2){reference-type="ref" reference="sect:CLT2"} establishes the Gaussian fluctuations of large random Young diagrams around the limit shape.

Appendices are devoted to partial answers or some solutions to questions from the literature.
