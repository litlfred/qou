B.2. Let α ∈Z ∪{±∞} \ {0}. For any n ∈N∗, let Zp,α,n be the Q-vector space generated by the numbers ζp,α(w) with w a word of weight n: (1) For α ∈N∗, Zp,α,n = σ −α(Zp,1,n), and Zp,−α,n = σ α(Zp,−1,n).
