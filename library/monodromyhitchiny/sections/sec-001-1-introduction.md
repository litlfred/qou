It is enough to show the above theorem in the context of Conforma l Field Theory as deﬁned

Quantum Field Theory are predicted to be equivalent by work in progr ess of Andersen and
Ueno ([AU1], [AU2] and [AU3]), the above theorem should follow from th e work of Funar and
