An integral expression has been presented which generalizes the third-order invariant known from the Massey triple product, to an invariant not limited to mutually unlinked flux tubes, if the involved fields allow for potentials that obey $\nabla\cdot\left(\bA^i\times\bA^j\right)=0$ for $i\ne j$. An example shows that the new invariant $H^{(3)}$ is a true generalization. In our derivation helicity and $H^{(3)}$ emerge from the same general identity, which involves the Chern-Simons three-form in the context of an $SU(2)$ gauge symmetry. Whether this identity leads to further results for other gauge groups has not yet been investigated, but it is clear that only expressions quadratic and cubic in magnetic flux can be obtained. The constructed invariant is to be seen as a "cross-linkage" of three fields. It still remains to clarify whether or how a total third-order invariant can be constructed and whether this is possible with the help of a cross third-order linkage such as in the case of helicity. There might e.g. exist a subdivision of a single field into three components such that the total third-order linkage is determined by the cross-linkage alone. Unfortunately, the antisymmetry of $H^{(3)}(\bB^1,\bB^2,\bB^3)$ seems to be one of the key problems for a further generalization analogous to helicity.
