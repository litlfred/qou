Any rectiﬁable loop with energy less than 6π +4 ≈22.84954 is unknot- ted. Computer experiments of [A] as reported in [O3] and independently by the ﬁrst author yield an essential knot (a trefoil) with energy ≈74. It may be estimated [S,T,W] that the number K(n) of distinct knots of at most n crossings satisﬁes 2n ≤K(n) ≤2 · 24n. Hence the number of knot types with representatives below a given energy threshold can also be bounded by an exponential.
