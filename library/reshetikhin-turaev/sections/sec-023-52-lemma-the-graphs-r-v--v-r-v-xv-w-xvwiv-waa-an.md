This lemma follows from the obvious fact that each graph (considered up to 
isotopy) may be drawn so that it lies in a standard position with respect to some 
square lattice in the plane of the picture. The words "standard position" mean that 
in each square of the lattice the picture looks as in Fig. 2. 
Following the lines of group theory we may consider relations between 
generators. The following lemma provides us with a "generating set" of relations 
which meansn that all possible relations between our generators follow from the 
given ones. We shall present the relations in a pictorial form.
