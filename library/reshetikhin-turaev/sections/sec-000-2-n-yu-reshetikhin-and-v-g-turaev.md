L.O.M.I., Fontanka 27, SU-191011 Leningrad, USSR 
Abstract. The generalization of Jones polynomial of links to the case of graphs 
in R 3 is presented. It is constructed as the functor from the category of graphs to 
the category of representations of the quantum group.
