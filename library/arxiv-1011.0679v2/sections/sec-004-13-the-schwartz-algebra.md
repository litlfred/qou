Remark 1.3. Suppose that L is a reductive p-adic group and that K ⊂ L is a
compact open subgroup. Clearly (21) is similar to the Plancherel isomorphism for
the Harish-Chandra–Schwartz algebra S(L) [Wal]. One can easily deduce from [Wal]
that the subalgebra S(L,K ) ⊂ S (L) of K-biinvariant functions has exactly the same
shape as (22), see [Sol1, Theorem 10] .
