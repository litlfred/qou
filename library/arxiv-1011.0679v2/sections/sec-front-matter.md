A FORMULA OF ARTHUR AND AFFINE HECKE ALGEBRAS
ERIC OPDAM AND MAARTEN SOLLEVELD
Abstract. Let π, π′ be tempered representations of an aﬃne Hecke algebra with
positive parameters. We study their Euler–Poincar´e pairing EP(π, π′), the alter-
nating sum of the dimensions of the Ext-groups. We show that EP(π, π′) can be
expressed in a simple formula involving an analytic R-group, analogous to a for-
mula of Arthur in the setting of reductive p-adic groups. Our proof applies equally
well to aﬃne Hecke algebras and to reductive groups over nonarchimedean local
ﬁelds of arbitrary characteristic.
