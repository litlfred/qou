Introduction
Let F be a non-archimedean local ﬁeld, and let L be the group of rational points
of a connected reductive algebraic group L deﬁned over F. Aﬃne Hecke algebras
are powerful tools to describe Bernstein blocks [BeDe] of th e category of smooth
representations of L explicitly, in a way compatible with harmonic analysis. For
example, if a Bernstein block B admits a type τ then the theory of types [BuKu]
establishes a Morita equivalence between B and the module category of the Hecke
algebra Hτ of the type τ , such that the Plancherel measure of L restricted to B
corresponds to the spectral measure of Hτ viewed as a Hilbert algebra [BHK]. In
various cases the Hecke algebras of types have been describe d explicitly as aﬃne
Hecke algebras [Mor, Lus2]. Other categorical equivalence s describing Bernstein
blocks in terms of aﬃne Hecke algebras (based on Bernstein’s second adjointness
Theorem) have been given in many cases [Hei]. In this way aﬃne Hecke algebras
can be used for the explicit computation of aspects of the har monic analysis on L.

Analytic R-groups of aﬃne Hecke algebras describe the decomposition o f tem-
pered parabolically induced modules of the Hecke algebra [D eOp2], in analogy with
the theory of analytic R groups for reductive groups [HC, KnSt, Sil]. The analytic
R-groups of aﬃne Hecke algebras are deﬁned in terms of the Plan cherel density,
which is known known explicitly [Opd1, DeOp1, OpSo2]. There fore the analytic R-
groups of aﬃne Hecke algebras are amenable to explicit deter mination [Slo]. It is an
interesting question how the analytic R-groups for parabolic induction of tempered
representations of aﬃne Hecke algebras are related to the an alyticR-groups for para-
bolic induction of tempered representations of reductive g roups (some results in this
direction have been obtained by Roche [Roc]). In the present paper we will focus on
the analytic R-groups of aﬃne Hecke algebras, in line with our point of view that
aﬃne Hecke algebras are tools for explicit descriptions of t he harmonic analysis on L.
