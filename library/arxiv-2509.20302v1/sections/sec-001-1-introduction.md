Tracy--Widom distributions [@Tracy:1993xj] appear in many areas of physics and mathematics, providing surprising connections between seemingly unrelated systems. In particular, distributions related to Bessel kernels describe eigenvalues of random matrices in the Laguerre ensemble in an appropriate scaling limit [@Forrester:1993vtx]. Their generalizations have recently emerged in supersymmetric gauge theories - especially in the context of the AdS/CFT correspondence [@Aharony:1999ti] - yielding exact expressions valid at all values of the 't Hooft coupling for a variety of observables, including Wilson loop expectation values [@Pestun_2017], four-point functions of infinitely heavy BPS operators [@Coronado:2018ypq; @Coronado:2018cxj; @Kostov:2019stn; @Bargheer:2019kxb; @Kostov:2019auq; @Belitsky:2019fan; @Bargheer:2019exp; @Belitsky:2020qrm; @Belitsky:2020qir], flux-tube correlators [@Beisert:2006ez; @Belitsky:2019fan; @Basso:2020xts] and many quantities obtained via localization [@Beccaria:2020hgy; @Beccaria:2021vuc; @Billo2022; @Beccaria:2023kbl; @Korchemsky2025]. Remarkably, all these observables can be expressed as determinants of semi-infinite matrices [@Beccaria:2022ypy] of the form ([\[eq:detdef\]](#eq:detdef){reference-type="ref" reference="eq:detdef"}) .

The mathematical literature contains a long history of studying strong-coupling expansions of such determinants (see, e.g., [@Szego1915; @Szego1952; @Kac1954; @Akhiezer1966; @FisherHartwig1969; @Bottcher1995OnsagerFisherHartwig; @Basor2012BriefHistorySzego]). More recently, integro-differential equation methods [@Its1990] have enabled systematic expansions to arbitrary order [@Belitsky:2020qrm; @Belitsky:2020qir]. These series are asymptotic and require non-perturbative completions [@Beccaria:2022ypy]. Using the analytic structure of the resolvent, non-perturbative corrections were computed systematically [@Bajnok2024; @Bajnok20251; @Bajnok20252], and resurgence relations [@Marino:2012zq; @Dorigoni:2014hea; @Aniceto:2018bis] between the perturbative series and the first few non-perturbative terms have been verified [@Bajnok2024; @Bajnok20251; @Bajnok20252].

In this Letter, we go beyond previous studies and analyze the determinants themselves, uncovering a remarkably simple structure of the full trans-series. All non-perturbative corrections share a universal form, with only the moments undergoing a straightforward and intuitive transformation. Our results reveal a deeper organizing principle for the non-perturbative dynamics of gauge theory observables and highlight the role of resurgence in understanding generalized Tracy--Widom distributions.
