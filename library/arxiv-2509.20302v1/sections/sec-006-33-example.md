In the case of {x1,x2,x3|y1,...} the non-perturbative
corrections can be represented by a 3 dimensional cube.
The vertex {} represents the perturbative part, D{},
while each other vertex represents a non-perturbative
correction ,D{1},....
The oriented edges show non-
trivial resurgence relations between them.
The alien
derivatives move in the corresponding directions and
add their index to the set.
In the general case of the symbol {x1,...,xl|y1,...}
the non-perturbative corrections and resurgence rela-
tions can be visualized on an l-dimensional cube.
