3-manifold technology
In this appendix, we explain a few concepts in the theory of hyperbolic 3-manifolds
that are relevant to our computations in 3d gravity. We refer to [11] for many other
relevant tools and concepts of hyperbolic 3-manifolds, as well as appendix A of [131].
