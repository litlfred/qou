studyhandlebody-knots. A genus-ghandlebody-knot is the 3-manifold obtained by
removing a genus-ghandlebody from S 3. These manifolds can be represented using
knotted trivalent graphs; they are generalizations of regular knots, now including
trivalent vertices. A handlebody-knot is said to be trivial if the manifold, that is
the complement of the knot in S 3, is itself a handlebody. The simplest example of a
