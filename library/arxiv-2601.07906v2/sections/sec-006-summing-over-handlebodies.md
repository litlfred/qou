\label{sec:3}




In this section, we will start to build up the ensemble from the boundary. As we proceed, we will start to recover the gravitational sum over 3-manifolds. Here we will only be concerned with the constraints of crossing symmetry and modular invariance, leaving the additional assumption of typicality for section \ref{sec:minimal completion}. The simplest class of topologies that, when summed over, solve crossing and modular invariance are the handlebody topologies, which we study in this section.
