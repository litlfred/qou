The fundamental group of the gravitational machine
In this appendix, we further discuss the group theoretic description of the fundamen-
tal groups produced by the machine. In particular, we prove the theorems used in
section 4.3 to show that all fundamental groups of the 3-manifolds produced by the
machine do not have Z × Z subgroups. This requires us to introduce some elements
of Bass-Serre theory, which turns out to be a very useful tool. Here, we give a brief
introduction to the subject, with [95] being the standard reference. We begin in B.1
with examples that motivate and explain the main ideas without giving any formal
definitions. Then, we move to the definitions and main constructions in B.2 and B.3.
These will allow us to prove the relevant theorems in B.4.
