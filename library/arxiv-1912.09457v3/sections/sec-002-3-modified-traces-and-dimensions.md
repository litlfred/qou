[]{#sec:traces label="sec:traces"}

We recall the definition of a modified trace function. The existence of such trace functions on thick ideals is in general difficult and often only known on the thick ideal of projective objects. We show that these exist for our rigid spherical category provided it admits a lift to a local ring whose maximal ideal is principal.

## The concept of a modified trace

Let $\mathcal C_R$ be rigid spherical monoidal over a local ring $R$. As in Section [\[sec:prel\]](#sec:prel){reference-type="ref" reference="sec:prel"} we assume that $\mathcal C_R$ is braided in order to identify left and right duals. We follow [@Geer-Kujawa-Patureau-Mirand] [@Geer-Kujawa-Patureau-Mirand-ambidextrous] [@Geer-Patureau-Mirand-Virelizer] [@Geer-Patureau-Mirand-projective] in the definition of a trace function. Recall that for any objects $X,Y \in \mathcal C$ and any endomorphism $f \in End_\mathcal{C}(X \otimes Y)$ we have the left trace $t_L(f) \in End_{\mathcal C}(X)$ and the right trace $t_R(f) \in End_{\mathcal C}(Y)$ defined as follows $$\begin{aligned}
 tr_L(f) & = (d_X \otimes id_Y) \circ (id_{X^*} \otimes f) \circ (\tilde{i}_X \otimes id_Y) \in End_{\mathcal{C}} (X) \\ tr_R(f) & = (id_X \otimes \tilde{d}_Y) \circ (f \otimes id_{Y^*}) \circ (id_X \otimes i_{Y}) \in End_{\mathcal{C}}(Y).
\end{aligned}$$

::: definition
[]{#D:trace label="D:trace"} If $I$ is a thick ideal in $\mathcal{C}$ then a *trace on $I$* is a family of linear functions $$\{\operatorname{\mathsf{t}}_V:{\rm End}_\mathcal{C}(V)\rightarrow R\}$$ where $V$ runs over all objects of $I$ and such that the following two conditions hold.

1.  If $X\in I$ and $Y\in \mathcal{C}$ then for any $f\in End_\mathcal{C}(X\otimes Y)$ we have $$\operatorname{\mathsf{t}}_{X\otimes Y}\left(f \right)=\operatorname{\mathsf{t}}_X \left( \mathop{\mathrm{t}}_R(f)\right).$$

2.  If $X,Y\in I$ then for any morphisms $f:X\rightarrow Y$ and $g:Y\rightarrow X$ in $\mathcal{C}$ we have $$\operatorname{\mathsf{t}}_X(g\circ f)=\operatorname{\mathsf{t}}_Y(f \circ g).$$
:::

Given such a trace on a thick ideal $I$, $\{t_{V} \}_{V \in I}$, define the *modified dimension* function on objects of $I$ as the modified trace of the identity morphism: $$d\left(V \right) =t_{V}(id_{V}).$$

## Existence of modified traces

As was shown in [@Geer-Kujawa-Patureau-Mirand-ambidextrous], modified trace functions exist on the thick ideal of projective objects in a number of examples. Beyond that, little seems to be known for general thick ideals.

Let $R$ be a local domain (which is not a field) whose maximal ideal $(p)$ is generated by the element $p$. Let $I$ be a thick ideal all of whose objects are $k$-negligible (with respect to $(p)$), such as e.g. the ideal $N_k$ of all $k$-negligible objects. Then we define the modified trace $Tr^{(k)}_X$ and modified dimension $\ensuremath{\operatorname{dim}}^{(k)}(X)$ for an object $X$ in $I$ by $$Tr^{(k)}_X(a)\ =\ \frac{1}{p^k}\ Tr_X(a),\hskip 3em \ensuremath{\operatorname{dim}}^{(k)}(X)\ =\ \frac{1}{p^k}\ \ensuremath{\operatorname{dim}}(X),$$ where $a\in{\rm End}(X)$. Note that this is well-defined since $Tr_X(a) \in (p)^k \ \forall a \in End(X)$. It is clear that $Tr^{(k)}_X(id_X)\ = \ensuremath{\operatorname{dim}}^{(k)}(X)$.

We list some elementary properties of these modified traces.

::: lemma
[]{#modifiedlem label="modifiedlem"} Let $X,Y$ be objects in $I$, and let $Z$ be an object in $\mathcal C$. Then we have

\(a\) $Tr^{(k)}_Y(ab)=Tr^{(k)}_X(ba)$ for all morphisms $a:X\to Y$ and $b:Y\to X$,

\(b\) $Tr^{(k)}_{X\otimes Z}(a\otimes c)=Tr^{(k)}_X(a)Tr_Z(c)$ and $\ensuremath{\operatorname{dim}}^{(k)}(X\otimes Y)=\ensuremath{\operatorname{dim}}^{(k)}(X)\ensuremath{\operatorname{dim}}(Y)$ for $a\in{\rm End}(X)$, $c\in {\rm End}(Z)$.

\(c\) $Tr^{(k)}_{X\otimes Y}(f) = Tr^{(k)}_X(t_R(f))$ for all $f \in End(X\otimes Y)$.
:::

::: proof
*Proof.* These properties hold for the ordinary trace. By our assumptions the renormalizations $Tr^{(k)}$ are also well-defined for the elements to which they are applied in our statements. As the renormalization factor is the same on both sides of the equation, the statements are also true for $Tr^{(k)}$. ◻
:::

Taking the images of these modified traces defines modified trace functions $Tr^{(k)}_X$ on $N_k \subset \mathcal C$. These modified traces are nontrivial on $N_k \setminus N_{k+1} \subset \mathcal C$ since there exists $a \in End_{\mathcal C_R}(X)$ satisfying $Tr_X(a) \in (p^k) \setminus (p^{k+1})$ for $X \in N_k \setminus N_{k+1} \subset \mathcal C_R$.

::: corollary
Suppose that $R$ is not a field and that $\mathfrak{m} = (p)$ is principal. Every thick ideal in a mod $\mathfrak{m}$ evaluation carries a nontrivial modified trace functions.
:::

::: corollary
Every thick ideal in the following categories admits a nontrivial modified trace.

1.  The Deligne categories $Rep(S_t)$, $Rep(GL_t)$, $Rep(O_t)$, $t \in {\mathbb C}$.

2.  Let $Tilt(U_q(\mathfrak{g}), {\mathbb Q}(q))$ denote the category of tilting modules in the category of finite dimensional modules of Lusztig's quantum group where $\mathfrak{g}$ is a semisimple Lie algebra and $q$ a primitive $\ell$-th root of unity where $\ell > h$ and $\ell$ is not divisible by 3 if $\mathfrak{g}$ contains $\mathfrak{g}_2$.

3.  Let $Tilt(G,k)$ denote the category of tilting modules in the category of finite dimensional representations of $G$, where $G$ is a semisimple and simply connected algebraic group over a perfect field $\Bbbk$ of characteristic $p >0$.
:::

::: proof
*Proof.* Each category is obtained as a mod $\mathfrak{m}$ reduction of a monoidal category over a discrete valuation ring (see theorem [\[thm:tilting-eval\]](#thm:tilting-eval){reference-type="ref" reference="thm:tilting-eval"} and [@Heidersdorf-Wenzl-Deligne]). ◻
:::

::: remark
The results of this section generalize to the situation of section [\[sec:general\]](#sec:general){reference-type="ref" reference="sec:general"} where we have a full and surjective monoidal functor $\mathcal{C}_R \to \mathcal{C}$ for $R$ a local ring with principal maximal ideal.
:::
