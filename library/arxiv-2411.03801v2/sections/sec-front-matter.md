1-LOOP EQUALS TORSION FOR TWO-BRIDGE KNOTS
STAVROS GAROUFALIDIS AND SEOKBEOM YOON
Abstract. Motivated by the conjectured asymptotics of the Kashaev invariant, Dimofte
and the first author introduced a power series associated to a suitable ideal triangulation
of a cusped hyperbolic 3-manifold, proved that its constant (1-loop) term is a topological
invariant and conjectured that it equals to the adjoint Reidemeister torsion. We prove this
conjecture for hyperbolic 2-bridge knots by combining the work of Ohtsuki–Takata with an
explicit computation.
