Let C be an oriented surface with a speciﬁed triangulation (for example, a boundary torus of M with the tessellation by boundary triangles). A segment in C is an embedded arc in one triangle, which is disjoint from the vertices of C, and whose endpoints lie in distinct edges of C. A normal closed curve σ ⊂C is an immersed closed curve that is transverse to the edges of C, such that the intersection between σ and a triangle is a union of segments.
