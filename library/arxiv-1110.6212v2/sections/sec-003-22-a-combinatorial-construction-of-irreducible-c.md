To conclude the proof we show that the module MJ (t) is irreducible and calibrated. By
the construction the generalised weight spaces of MJ (t) are MJ (t)wt, with w ∈ FJ (t), and each
generalised weight space has dimension 1. So MJ (t) is calibrated. Furthermore, it follows that if
M is a proper submodule of MJ (t) then there is w, w′ ∈ FJ (t) with wt ⁄= w′t such that Mwt ⁄= 0
and Mw′t = 0, contradicting Proposition 2.5. Thus MJ (t) is irreducible.
