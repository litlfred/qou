Now, in order for the gauge theory $T[M]$ to flow (in a straightforward way) to a superconformal theory in the infrared, two conditions are necessary: 1) some $U(1)_R$ R-symmetry must be preserved; and 2) there must exist a choice of $U(1)_R$ symmetry such that the charges of all chiral operators are non-negative, because in a superconformal theory R-charges are proportional to operator dimensions.
