If two 1-cusped hyperbolic 3-manifolds M and M ′ have 1-e cient ideal tri- angulations T and T′ with equal angle structure matrices, then IT(0)(q) = IT′(0)(q). For example, M and −M have such triangulations, where −M denotes the orientation reversed cusped hyperbolic manifold. Also, Mφ and M−φ have such triangulations for a pseudo- Anosov homeomorphism φ of a once punctured torus, where Mφ denotes the mapping torus of φ.
