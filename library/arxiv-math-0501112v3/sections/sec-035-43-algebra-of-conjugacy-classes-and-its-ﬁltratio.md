In fact, to deﬁne correctly the notion of the disjoint produc t • from Sec-

tations and not the group algebra C(Sq ). The reason for this is that we must
distinguish two kinds of ﬁx-points since, for example, Σ 1,1 and Σ 1 repre-
sent multiples of each other in C(Sq ), but the disjoint product treats them
differently.
One can show [IK99, ´Sni04] that the family of normalized conjugacy
classes (Σ k1 ,...,k n ) and the family of free cumulants (Ri) generate the same
ﬁltered algebra, called algebra of conjugacy classes, when for the degrees
of the generators we take
deg Σ k1 ,...,k n = (k1 + 1) + · · · + (kn + 1),
deg Ri = i.
The above statement holds true both when as the product we take the usual
product of partial permutations (in this case we denote the resulting algebra
by A) and when as the product we take the disjoint product (in this case we
denote the resulting algebra by A•).
