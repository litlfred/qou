In Section [\[sec:preliminaries\]](#sec:preliminaries){reference-type="ref" reference="sec:preliminaries"} we present briefly all necessary notions needed to state the main result and its applications. In Section [\[sec:representations\]](#sec:representations){reference-type="ref" reference="sec:representations"} we present the main result: Theorem and Definition [\[theo:main\]](#theo:main){reference-type="ref" reference="theo:main"} where four equivalent conditions are given which ensure Gaussian fluctuations of the characters and of the shape of the Young diagrams. We also show that the considered class of representations has many interesting examples and that it is closed under some natural operations such as induction, restriction, outer product and tensor product of representation. Section [\[sec:Proof-main-result\]](#sec:Proof-main-result){reference-type="ref" reference="sec:Proof-main-result"} contains the proof of the main result.
