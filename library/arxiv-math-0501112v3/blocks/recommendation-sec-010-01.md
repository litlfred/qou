We can treat any commuting family of elements of the group algebra ${\mathbb{C}}(S_q)$ as a family of random variables equipped with the mean value given by the normalized character: $$\label{eq:wartoscoczekiwana} {\mathbb{E}}X:= \chi^{\rho_q}(X)= \frac{\mathop{\mathrm{Tr}} \rho_q(X)}{\mathop{\mathrm{Tr}}\rho_q(1)}.$$ It should be stressed that in the general case we treat elements of ${\mathbb{C}}(S_q)$ as random variables only on a purely formal level; in particular we do not treat them as functions on some Kolmogorov probability space.
