Biane [@Biane2001approximate] proved (under some mild technical assumptions) that Young diagrams contributing to some finite-dimensional reducible representation of the symmetric group $S_q$ will concentrate around some limit shape if and only if the normalized character of the representation $$\chi(\pi):=\frac{\mathop{\mathrm{Tr}}\rho(\pi)}{\mathop{\mathrm{Tr}}\rho(e)}$$ approximately factorizes, i.e. informally speaking $$\label{eq:approximate} \chi( \sigma_1 \cdots \sigma_n) \approx \chi(\sigma_1) \cdots \chi(\sigma_n)$$ for all permutations $\sigma_1,\dots,\sigma_n$ with disjoint supports (to be more precise: we consider a sequence of representations $(\rho_q)$ where $\rho_q$ is a representation of $S_q$ and the approximate equality [\[eq:approximate\]](#eq:approximate){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:approximate"} should hold in the limit $q\to\infty$).
