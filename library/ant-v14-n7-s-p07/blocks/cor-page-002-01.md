Let A be a constant abelian variety deﬁned over Fq(x). For each f ∈Fqm[x], denote by A f the quadratic twist of A ⊗Fqm(x) by f . Let Rn,m be the set { f ∈ Fqm[x], squarefree, of deg n : A f has positive rank}. Then, lim m→∞lim sup n→∞ |Rn,m| qm(n+1) = 0. Motivated by [Bui and Florea 2018, Corollary 2.2] and the analogous results over Z of Conrey, Ghosh and Gonek [Conrey et al. 1998], we bound the multiplicity of the zeros of ZC, and obtain further information on nonvanishing at s = 1 2.
