Let C be a hyperelliptic curve of genus at least 2 over Fq and S be the set of Weierstrass points of C. The Frobenius acts on S by permuting the 2g + 2 Weierstrass points via some permutation π. Suppose that either • g is even and π is a (2g+2)-cycle; or • π is the product of two disjoint cycles of odd length. Then: (1) The point s = 1 2 is not a zero of ZC. (2) All zeros of ZC are of multiplicity at most 2. Moreover, if π is the product of two disjoint cycles of coprime lengths, all zeros of ZC are simple.
