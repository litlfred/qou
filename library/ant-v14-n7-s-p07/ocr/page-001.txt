Nonvanishing of hyperelliptic zeta functions
over finite fields

Jordan S. Ellenberg, Wanlin Li and Mark Shusterman

Fixing f € R and a finite field F, of odd characteristic, we give an explicit upper bound on the proportion
of genus g hyperelliptic curves over F, whose zeta function vanishes at 5 + it. Our upper bound is
independent of g and tends to 0 as g grows.
