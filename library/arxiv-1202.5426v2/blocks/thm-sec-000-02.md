In [BR12], improving a previous result [Rt12], the smoothness of stationary points of ﬁnite energy was already shown for the case of E(α) := E(α,1), α ∈(2, 3), instead of the M¨obius energy. It is worth noting, that those energies lead to a subcritical Euler-Langrage equation, and that in some sense the regularity theory can be based on Sobolev embeddings for fractional Sobolev and Besov spaces.
