Hard analysis meets critical knots
Stationary points of the M ¨obius energy are smooth
S. Blatt∗ Φ. Reiter∗∗ A. Schikorra∗∗∗
June 30, 2018
We prove that if a curveγ∈ H3/ 2(R/ Z, Rn) parametrized by arc length is a sta-
tionary point of the M¨ obius energy introduced by Jun O’Harain [O’H91], thenγ
is smooth. Our methods only rely on purely analytical argume nts, entirely with-
out using M¨ obius invariance. Furthermore, they are not fundamentally restricted
to one-dimensional domains, but are generalizable to arbitrary dimensions.
