Hard analysis meets critical knots
Stationary points of the M¨obius energy are smooth
S. Blatt∗
Φ. Reiter∗∗
A. Schikorra∗∗∗
June 30, 2018
We prove that if a curve γ ∈H3/2(R/Z, Rn) parametrized by arc length is a sta-
tionary point of the M¨obius energy introduced by Jun O’Hara in [O’H91], then γ
is smooth. Our methods only rely on purely analytical arguments, entirely with-
out using M¨obius invariance. Furthermore, they are not fundamentally restricted
to one-dimensional domains, but are generalizable to arbitrary dimensions.
