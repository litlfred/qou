Abstract
We review how nuclear forces emerge from low-energy QCD via chiral eﬀective ﬁeld theory. The presentation
is accessible to the non-specialist. At the same time, we also provide considerable detailed information
(mostly in appendices) for the beneﬁt of researchers who wish to start working in this ﬁeld.
Keywords:
Low-energy QCD, eﬀective ﬁeld theory, chiral perturbation theory, nuclear forces, nucleon-nucleon
scattering
