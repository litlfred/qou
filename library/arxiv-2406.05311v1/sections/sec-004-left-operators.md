Proposition 5.24. If v is a composition of classical operators that is a forest, then it is
equivalent to a composition RC of a classical row and a classical column.

ζ is a product of disjoint cycles, with each cycle corresponding to a tree in the graph of ζ.

L(ζ) = # supp(ζ) − s(ζ), and we conclude that ζ is a minimal cycle.
