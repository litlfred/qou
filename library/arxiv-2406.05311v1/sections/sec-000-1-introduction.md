The is a method for computing values of the irreducible characters of symmetric groups [@Mu37; @Na41a; @Na41b]. Under the identification of the character ring with the ring of symmetric functions, it translates into a rule for the multiplication of a Schur symmetric function by a Newton power sum. As explained in [@MorrisonSottile Sect. 2], in the cohomology ring of a Grassmannian, the Murnaghan--Nakayama formula is a formula for the intersection product of a Schubert variety with a component of the Chern character of the tautological bundle (a tautological class), as these tautological classes are essentially Newton power sums. Consequently, any formula describing multiplication by a tautological class will also be called a Murnaghan--Nakayama rule.

Murnaghan--Nakayama rules have been described for many different algebras that carry a notion of a Newton power sum.

Fomin and Green gave a version for noncommutative symmetric functions, which led to formulas for characters of representations associated to stable Schubert and Grothendieck polynomials [@FoGr98]. Assaf and McNamara gave a skew version [@A-McN], which Konvalinka generalized to a skew rule for multiplication by a 'quantum' (perturbed by a parameter $q$) power sum function [@Ko12]. Bandlow, *et al.* gave a version in the cohomology of an affine Grassmannian [@BSZ11], and Lee gave a version in the cohomology of the affine flag manifold [@Lee].

Tewari gave a version for noncommutative Schur functions [@Tewari], and Berg *et al.* for the immaculate noncommutative symmetric functions [@BBSSZ]. Wildon gave a plethystic version [@Wildon]. Ross gave a version for loop Schur functions [@Ro14], providing a fundamental step in the orbifold Gromov--Witten/Donaldson--Thomas correspondence [@RoZo13]. Morrison and Sottile gave Murnaghan--Nakayama rules in the cohomology ring of a flag manifold and in the quantum cohomology of the Grassmannian [@MorrisonSottile]. Khanh, *et al.* gave a version in the Grothendieck ring of the Grassmannian [@KHST]. Khanh also gave a version for $K$-$k$-Schur and $k$-Schur functions [@Khanh], generalizing [@BSZ11]. Fan, *et al.* gave a Murnaghan--Nakayama formula for Chern--Schwartz--MacPherson classes of Schubert cells in the flag variety [@FGX].

We first derive a formula for the multiplication of a Schubert polynomial by a hook Schur polynomial and use this to give a new proof of the Murnaghan--Nakayama formula in the cohomology of a flag variety that was originally derived in [@MorrisonSottile]. This hook multiplication formula is related to the formula of Mészáros et al. [@MPP Thm. 2], but it does not use the noncommutative Fomin--Kirillov algebra [@FominKirillov].

Our main result (Corollary [\[C:quantumMN\]](#C:quantumMN){reference-type="ref" reference="C:quantumMN"}) is a Murnaghan--Nakayama rule in the small quantum cohomology ring of the flag manifold, proving a formula conjectured by Morrison at FPSAC 2014 [@Morrison]. This follows from a formula (Theorem [\[thm:q_hook_product\]](#thm:q_hook_product){reference-type="ref" reference="thm:q_hook_product"}) for multiplication by a quantum Schur polynomial indexed by a hook partition. Mészáros et al. [@MPP Thm. 15] also gave a formula for multiplication by a hook quantum Schur polynomial, which would imply our formula. Unfortunately, their proof is not valid, as we explain in Section [\[SS:R_MPP\]](#SS:R_MPP){reference-type="ref" reference="SS:R_MPP"}---this necessitates the arguments we give. Our arguments involve a detailed analysis of chains and intervals in the , a partial order relevant to multiplication in the quantum cohomology of a flag manifold. We also use the "quantum-equals-classical" result of Leung and Li [@LeungLi2012] as well as Postnikov's cyclic symmetry [@Postnikov_symmetry] to show that structure constants for quantum products by hook quantum Schur polynomials are equal to structure constants for the classical product from [@So96], and thereby deduce our results.

In Section [\[sec:classic-mn-rule\]](#sec:classic-mn-rule){reference-type="ref" reference="sec:classic-mn-rule"} we develop some background on the flag variety and Bruhat order, giving a new proof of the Murnaghan--Nakayama formula in the cohomology of the flag variety. Section [\[S:qCoh\]](#S:qCoh){reference-type="ref" reference="S:qCoh"} develops background on the quantum cohomology ring of the flag variety, and gives a proof of the Murnaghan--Nakayama formula in this setting, which relies on results established in two subsequent sections. We extract some results of Leung and Li [@LeungLi2012] in Section [\[quantum-equals-classical\]](#quantum-equals-classical){reference-type="ref" reference="quantum-equals-classical"}, including a version of their "quantum-equals-classical" result (Lemma [\[L:Quantum_is_Classical\]](#L:Quantum_is_Classical){reference-type="ref" reference="L:Quantum_is_Classical"}). The final Section [\[left-operators\]](#left-operators){reference-type="ref" reference="left-operators"} develops a detailed combinatorics of chains in the quantum Bruhat order to prove the second result used in our proof of the quantum Murnaghan--Nakayama rule (Theorem [\[Th:nonZero\]](#Th:nonZero){reference-type="ref" reference="Th:nonZero"}). It is the technical heart of this paper.

A theme in the paper [@BS-Bruhat] is the relation between intervals in the $k$-Bruhat order $\leq_k$ (a suborder of the Bruhat order on $S_n$) and Littlewood--Richardson coefficients $c^w_{u,v}$ when $v$ is a Grassmannian permutation. For example, the isomorphism type of an interval $[u,w]_k$ in the $k$-Bruhat order only depends upon $wu^{-1}$, and $c^w_{u,v}$ also only depends upon $wu^{-1}$ when $v$ is Grassmannian. Only substantially weaker versions of these and related results hold for quantum cohomology. While an interval $[u,q^\alpha w]_k^q$ in the quantum $k$-Bruhat order (see Section [\[quantum-k-bruhat-order\]](#quantum-k-bruhat-order){reference-type="ref" reference="quantum-k-bruhat-order"}) is not determined by $wu^{-1}$, its isomorphism type is preserved by some operations including a cyclic shift (Corollary [\[C:equivalences\]](#C:equivalences){reference-type="ref" reference="C:equivalences"}) that is related to Postnikov's cyclic shift and is distinct from the cyclic shift in [@BS-Bruhat] (which does not preserve isomorphism type). When $v$ is Grassmannian, we show that the numerical part $N^{w,\alpha}_{u,v}$ of a quantum Littlewood--Richardson coefficient (see Section [\[quantum-equals-classical\]](#quantum-equals-classical){reference-type="ref" reference="quantum-equals-classical"}) only depends upon $wu^{-1}$ (but $q^\alpha$ may change); this is Theorem [\[Th:quantumIndependence\]](#Th:quantumIndependence){reference-type="ref" reference="Th:quantumIndependence"}.
