A composition v deﬁnes a (multi)graph whose vertices are its support. It has one edge
between a and b for each operator vab appearing in v. We say that v is connected if its graph
is connected, that v is a path if its graph is a path with a1 or b1 as an endpoint and #({ai, bi}∩
{ai+1, bi+1}) = 1 for all i. It is a tree if its graph is tree. A composition of two connected
compositions is crossing if their supports are disjoint and crossing. Note that noncrossing
products of trees are minimal. Indeed, each tree is minimal, so a noncrossing product of
trees is a noncrossing product of minimal compositions of operators, and hence is minimal.
