In this section, we will now combine the ingredients of Sections 2 and 3 to provide the map
from a pair (M, Π), whereM is a 3-manifold and Π a polarization of its boundary phase space
P∂M , to a 3d SCFT TM,Π, with speciﬁed couplings to background gauge ﬁelds and chiral
multiplets. We will do so in two steps. First, we attach a 3d theory to any triangulation
{∆i}N
i=1 of the three-manifold M, and then we show that diﬀerent triangulations of the same
three-manifold give mirror descriptions of the 3d SCFT.
