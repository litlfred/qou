As we discussed in section [\[sec:S3b\]](#sec:S3b){reference-type="ref" reference="sec:S3b"}, a supersymmetric Chern-Simons interaction at level $k$ for the background gauge field contributes to the partition function a factor [\[z3dCSk\]](#z3dCSk){reference-type="eqref" reference="z3dCSk"}: $$\mathcal{Z}_{CS_k} = e^{- i \pi k \tilde m^2} \,.$$ Much like the partition function of the theory $T_{\Delta}$, it obeys the following $q$-difference equation: $$\left( \hat z'' - q^{\frac k2} \, \hat z^k \right) \mathcal{Z}_{CS_k} = \left( e^{ib \partial_{\tilde m}} - e^{i\pi b^2 k+2\pi bk\tilde m} \right) \mathcal{Z}_{CS_k} = 0 \,.$$ According to [\[whhlines\]](#whhlines){reference-type="eqref" reference="whhlines"}, this identity should be interpreted as a statement that at a 3d boundary with Chern-Simons term at level $k$ a 't Hooft operator with one unit of a magnetic flux is equivalent to a Wilson operator of electric charge $k$, $$H - e^{i\pi b^2 k} \, W^{k} \; \simeq \; 0 \,.$$ This is indeed correct, as one can easily verify by doing a direct path integral manipulation.
