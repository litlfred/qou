Two remarks are in order. They relate to Propositions 5.6.1 and 5.6.2. (1) In both propositions the basic assumption is that the region R is embedded. Of course this will be the case if no clasp arcs intersect R. But it will also be the case if (a) clasp arcs intersect R but no clasp arc pair intersects R, or (b) there are no long clasp arcs which intersect R.
