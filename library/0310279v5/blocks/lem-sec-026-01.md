The reader may also wonder why our complexity function ignores all the singularities that are in normal neighborhoods but not on the clasp arcs. The reason is again simple: when we constructed normal neighborhoods we created lots of inessential b–arcs. If we omit them, we will be forced to give up normal neighborhoods, and we don’t want to do that now. Finally, the reader may wonder why we are not including braid index in our complexity function. That is a more subtle matter. At this stage in the work it sufﬁces to say that we will introduce it later (see the augmented complexity function of Section 6.)
