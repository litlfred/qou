An annulus component of CA is standard if every component E ⊂Gϵ,δ on the annulus satisﬁes the following conditions: (1) E is homeomorphic to either S1 or [0, 1], ie either a circle or a line. (2) If E is a circle then it is a core circle of the annulus component. (3) If E is a line with ∂E = p ∪p′ then p ∈Xϵ and p′ is near X−ϵ.
