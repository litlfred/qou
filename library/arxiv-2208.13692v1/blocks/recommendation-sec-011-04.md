Our results agree with those of [@Weber2005], wherein the author describes an order-4 rotation (which he calls $\phi$, but we shall call $\tilde{U}$), and calculates the quotient by its action $T=\mathcal{B}/\left\langle \tilde{U} \right\rangle$ to be $$T:\ y^2=4 x^3-75 x -1475,\quad j_T=-\frac{25}{2} = j(5 \tau_0) .$$ Note that our strategy of semi-invariants can be used directly to calculate the quotient by the 4-cycle $(1324)$, something we were unable to do in the HC-model because of the non-linearity of the automorphism $U$.
