Both [RR92] and [Web05] consider the quotient ofB by the action of subgroups of S5. In
this section we shall study the various quotients of Bring’s curve of nonzero genus and the
relations between them, both clarifying and extending previous work. In §4.1 we will use the
Riemann-Hurwitz theorem to describe possible quotients. In §4.2 we shall note the various
relationships we expect between the quotients just on group theoretic grounds, while in
§4.3-§4.5 we turn to their explicit construction. In so doing we discover a number of curious
isomorphisms beyond those expected. In §4.6 we summarise our calculations and relate them
to known isogeny results. Throughout we will use the following: for any subgroup H of the
automorphisms Aut(C) of a curve C, H≤ Aut(C), then the normaliser NAut(C)(H) acts on
the H-orbits and NAut(C)(H)/H≤ Aut(C/H); if g∈ NAut(C)(H) we will denoted by g the
H-coset of NAut(C)(H) containing this. The quotient curves of this section are summarised
in Figures 3 and 5.
