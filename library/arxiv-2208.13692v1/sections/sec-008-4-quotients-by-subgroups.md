[]{#sec: quotients by subgroups label="sec: quotients by subgroups"}

Both [@Riera1992] and [@Weber2005] consider the quotient of $\mathcal{B}$ by the action of subgroups of $S_5$. In this section we shall study the various quotients of Bring's curve of nonzero genus and the relations between them, both clarifying and extending previous work. In §[\[subsec: genera of quotients\]](#subsec: genera of quotients){reference-type="ref" reference="subsec: genera of quotients"} we will use the Riemann-Hurwitz theorem to describe possible quotients. In §[\[subsec: relations between quotients\]](#subsec: relations between quotients){reference-type="ref" reference="subsec: relations between quotients"} we shall note the various relationships we expect between the quotients just on group theoretic grounds, while in §[\[subsec: quotients by 4 and 2-2 cycle\]](#subsec: quotients by 4 and 2-2 cycle){reference-type="ref" reference="subsec: quotients by 4 and 2-2 cycle"}-§[\[subsec: quotients by transposition\]](#subsec: quotients by transposition){reference-type="ref" reference="subsec: quotients by transposition"} we turn to their explicit construction. In so doing we discover a number of curious isomorphisms beyond those expected. In §[\[subsec: summarising quotients\]](#subsec: summarising quotients){reference-type="ref" reference="subsec: summarising quotients"} we summarise our calculations and relate them to known isogeny results. Throughout we will use the following: for any subgroup $H$ of the automorphisms $\mathop{\mathrm{Aut}}(\mathcal{C})$ of a curve $\mathcal{C}$, $H\le \mathop{\mathrm{Aut}}(\mathcal{C})$, then the normaliser $N_{\mathop{\mathrm{Aut}}(\mathcal{C})}(H)$ acts on the $H$-orbits and $N_{\mathop{\mathrm{Aut}}(\mathcal{C})}(H)/H \le \mathop{\mathrm{Aut}}(\mathcal{C}/H)$; if $g\in N_{\mathop{\mathrm{Aut}}(\mathcal{C})}(H)$ we will denoted by $\overline{g}$ the $H$-coset of $N_{\mathop{\mathrm{Aut}}(\mathcal{C})}(H)$ containing this. The quotient curves of this section are summarised in Figures [\[eq: structure of quotient curves\]](#eq: structure of quotient curves){reference-type="ref" reference="eq: structure of quotient curves"} and [\[eq: tracking fixed points\]](#eq: tracking fixed points){reference-type="ref" reference="eq: tracking fixed points"}.
