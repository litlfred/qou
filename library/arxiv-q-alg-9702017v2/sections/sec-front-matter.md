Abstract
We use a skein-theoretic version of the Hecke algebras of type A to
present three-dimensional diagrammatic views of Gyoja’s idempotent el-
ements, based closely on the corresponding Young diagram λ.
In this
context we give straightforward calculations for the eigenvalues fλ and mλ
of two natural central elements in the Hecke algebras, namely the full curl
and the sum of the Murphy operators. We discuss their calculation also
in terms of the framing factor associated to the appropriate irreducible
representation of the quantum group, SU(N)q.
