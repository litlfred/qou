The situation here is similar to the case of first order fusion except that the coefficients associated
with the constituents of the input columns are the available antiquirks—that is, those left over from the
first order fusion process, because they did not form junctions. With that in mind, we have reproduced
in Table B1, below, the list of first order fusion words with the junctions that can be formed and added
a column with the corresponding available antiquirks. The genesis of the added column will also be
formalized but first we discuss it informally. For example, in the case of BB*, if an x junction is
formed, the B* FMS has both a d* and a u* antiquirk left over and since four x junctions can form, we
have the term 4(d* + u*). On the other hand, if a y junction forms, two d* antiquirks are left over so
we have the term 2d*. Summing we have the term 4(d* + u*) + 2d* as shown in the figure.
