The existence of a pentaquark was originally sugg ested by researchers at the Petersburg Institute in 
Russia in 1997. There was supporting evidence in da ta taken in 2002 at Osak a University in Japan 
using carbon targets bombarded by X-rays and in a review in Germany of accelerator data taken in the 
years 1997/98. However subsequent disappointing expe rimentation has since led to a consensus of 
doubt as to the actual existence of a pentaquirk; perhaps the subject ought to be revisited.
