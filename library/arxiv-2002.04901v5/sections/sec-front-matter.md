WEAK PROREGULARITY, DERIVED COMPLETION, ADIC FLATNESS,
AND PRISMS
AMNON YEKUTIELI
Abstract. We begin by recalling the role that weak proregularity of an ideal in a commu-
tative ring has in derived completion and adic ﬂatness. We also introduce the new concepts
of idealistic and sequential derived completion, and prove a few results about them, includ-
ing the fact that these two concepts agree iﬀthe ideal is weakly proregular. Next we study
the local nature of weak proregularity, and its behavior w.r.t. ring quotients. These results
allow us to prove our main theorem, which states that weak proregularity occurs in the
context of bounded prisms. Prisms belong to the new groundbreaking theory of perfectoid
rings, developed by Scholze and his collaborators. Since perfectoid ring theory makes
heavy use of derived completion and adic ﬂatness, we anticipate that our results shall help
simplify and improve some of the more technical aspects of this theory.
