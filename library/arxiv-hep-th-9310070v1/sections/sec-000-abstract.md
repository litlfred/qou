A quantum generalization of Rogers’ ﬁve term, or “pentagon” dilogarithm identity
is suggested. It is shown that the classical limit gives usua l Rogers’ identity. The case
where the quantum identity is realized in ﬁnite dimensional space is also considered and
the quantum dilogarithm is constructed as a function on Ferm at curve, while the identity
itself is equivalent to the restricted star-triangle relat ion introduced by Bazhanov and
Baxter.
