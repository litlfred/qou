Abstract
A quantum generalization of Rogers’ ﬁve term, or “pentagon” dilogarithm identity
is suggested. It is shown that the classical limit gives usual Rogers’ identity. The case
where the quantum identity is realized in ﬁnite dimensional space is also considered and
the quantum dilogarithm is constructed as a function on Fermat curve, while the identity
itself is equivalent to the restricted star-triangle relation introduced by Bazhanov and
Baxter.
