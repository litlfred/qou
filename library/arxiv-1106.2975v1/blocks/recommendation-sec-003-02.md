The operator ${\mathbf T}_{m,r}$ now becomes an isometric isomorphism $${\mathbf T}_{m,r}:A^2_{m,1}({\mathbb C})\to\delta A^2_{m,n,r+1}({\mathbb C}),\quad {\mathbf T}_{m,r}:\mathrm{Pol}_{m,n,1}\to{\delta\mathrm{Pol}}_{m,n,r+1}.$$ We obtain the orthogonal decompositions $$\begin{aligned} \mathrm{Pol}_{m,n,q} &= \bigoplus_{r=0}^{q-1} {\delta\mathrm{Pol}}_{m,n,r+1}, & A^2_{m,q}({\mathbb C})=\bigoplus_{r=0}^{q-1} \delta A^2_{m,r+1}({\mathbb C}). \end{aligned}$$ As a consequence, if $K_{\delta:m,n,r+1}$ and $K_{\delta:m,r+1}$ denote the reproducing kernels for the spaces ${\delta\mathrm{Pol}}_{m,n,r+1}$ and $\delta A^2_{m,r+1}({\mathbb C})$, respectively, we must have that $$\begin{aligned} \label{kernelsubspacedecomposition} K_{m,n,q} &= \sum_{r=0}^{q-1} K_{\delta:{m,n,r+1}}, & K_{m,q} &=\sum_{r=0}^{q-1} K_{\delta:{m,r}}. \end{aligned}$$
