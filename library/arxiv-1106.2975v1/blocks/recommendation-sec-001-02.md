As we shall see, Proposition [\[kernelestimate\]](#kernelestimate){reference-type="ref" reference="kernelestimate"} implies that the Berezin density $$\berd^{\langle z\rangle}_{m,n,q}(w)= \frac{|K_{m,n,q}(z,w)|^2 }{K_{m,n,q}(z,z)}\mathrm e^{-m|w|^2}$$ behaves locally near $z$ like the Berezin density $$\begin{gathered} \label{eq-interm3} \berd^{\langle z\rangle}_{m,q}(w)= \frac{| K_{m,q}(z,w)|^2 }{K_{m,q}(z,z)}\mathrm e^{-m|w|^2}= \frac{m^2L^1_{q-1}(m|z-w|^2)^2\mathrm e^{2m\operatorname{Re}z\bar w}} {mL^1_{q-1}(0)\,\mathrm e^{m|z|^2}}\,\mathrm e^{-m|w|^2} \\ =\frac{m}{q}L^1_{q-1}(m|z-w|^2)^2\,\mathrm e^{-m|z-w|^2}. \end{gathered}$$ To make this precise, we recall the definition of the blow-up Berezin density: $$\hat{\berd}^{\langle z\rangle}_{m,n,q}(\xi) = m^{-1}\berd^{\langle z\rangle}_{m,n,q}(z+m^{-1/2}\xi).$$
