The identity (for $p=1,2,3,\ldots$) $$\label{recurrencerelation} L^{\alpha+p}_r(x)= \sum_{s=0}^r \binom{r-s+p-1}{p-1}\, L_{s}^{\alpha}(x)$$ plus the standard orthogonality properties of the Laguerre polynomials gives that $$\int_0^{+\infty}t^{i_1}L_{r_1}^{i_1}(t)L_{r_2}^{i_1+l}(t)\mathrm e^{-t}{\mathrm d}t= \frac{(r_1+i_1)!(r_2-r_1+l-1)!}{r_1!(r_2-r_1)!(l-1)!},$$ where the right-hand side should be interpreted as $0$ for $r_2<r_1$.
