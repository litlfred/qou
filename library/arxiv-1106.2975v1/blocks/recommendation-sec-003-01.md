We clearly have that $$\mathrm{span}\big\{{\mathbf T}_{m,r}[e_{j,m}]:\,\, 0\le j\le n-1,\,\,0\le r\le q-1 \big\}=\mathrm{Pol}_{m,n,q},$$ and in view of the orthogonality properties in Proposition [\[on-kanta\]](#on-kanta){reference-type="ref" reference="on-kanta"}, we also must have $$\mathrm{span}\{{\mathbf T}_{m,r}[e_{j,m}]:\,\, 0\le j\le n-1\} =\mathrm{Pol}_{m,n,r+1}\ominus \mathrm{Pol}_{m,n,r}, \label{eq-resspace1}$$ where the "$\ominus$" is with respect to the inner product in $L^2({\mathbb C},\mathrm e^{-m|z|^2})$.
