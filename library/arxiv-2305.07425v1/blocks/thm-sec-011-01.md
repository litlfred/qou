Let M be a non-geometric 3-manifold with empty or toroidal boundary. If the torus decomposition of M contains any of the following, then π1(M) is H–inaccessible: (1) a hyperbolic piece which contains a boundary torus of M; (2) two hyperbolic pieces glued along a torus; (3) an isolated non-elementary Seifert ﬁbered piece; or (4) a non-elementary maximal graph manifold component.
