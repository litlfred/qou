In this section, we prove Theorem 1.4: every Croke–Kleiner a dmissible group has a ﬁnite
index subgroup that is H–inaccessible.
Fix a Croke–Kleiner admissible group G = (Γ, {Gµ}, {Gα}, {τα}). We partition the vertex
set T 0 of the Bass-Serre tree into two disjoint collections of vert ices V1 and V2 such that if v
and v′ are in Vi then dT (v,v ′) is even. Since any automorphism of T either preserves V1 and
V2 setwise or interchanges them, we have:
Lemma 3.1 ([NY20, Lemma 4.6]) . Let G = (Γ , {Gµ}, {Gα}, {τα}) be a Croke-Kleiner ad-
missible group. There exists a subgroup G′ ≤G =π1(G) of index at most 2 in G so that G′
preserves V1 and V2 and G′ is also a Croke-Kleiner admissible group.
Let G′ be the ﬁnite index subgroup of G given by Lemma 3.1. In light of Lemma 2.3, to
show thatG′ is H-inaccessible, it suﬃces to construct commuting ai ∈G′ and actionsG′ ↷ Xi
fori = 1, 2 such that ai is elliptic with respect to the action G′ ↷ X3−i and loxodromic with
respect to the action G′ ↷ Xi. Our spaces Xi will be quasi-trees of metric spaces.
