Let $[M]$ be a bordism, and $f_0, f_1:M\to \mathbb{R}$ admissible excellent Morse functions inducing two generating compositions. We claim that there is a path from $f_0$ to $f_1$ which exhibits finitely many prime-commutativity relations, co/legs and co/waist relations, or fails to be excellent Morse at finitely many times due to commutative Frobenius relations. Thus the two compositions of $[M]$ are related by a sequence of prime relations and commutative Frobenius relations.
