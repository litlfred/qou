Let A be a commutative Frobenius algebra over k. Then A is an L-algebra (equiv. P-algebra) if there are elements 1p ∈A (equiv. endomorphisms ep : A →A that acts by multiplication by 1p) for every diffeomorphism class of connected oriented irreducible 3-manifold p. The remarkable feature of L and P algebras is their simplicity. We are supposed to think of such an algebra as Z(S2) for some 3d TFT Z : Cob(3) →Vectk. Even though the bordisms of S2 are very complicated, geometry forces G1 and G2 presentations to be extremely simple, which in turn forces the prime structures of L and P algebras to be also extremely simple.
