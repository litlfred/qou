There are six ways to cut out three disjoint three-balls away from the gluing boundary such that a factor $p\setminus \operatorname{int}(D^3 \sqcup D^3)$ is present: Topologically, one ball must be cut from $p\setminus \operatorname{int}(D^3)$ to recover the prime endomorphism $p \setminus \operatorname{int}(D^3 \sqcup D^3)$, the other two must be cut from the trivially glued on three-ball.
