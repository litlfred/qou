1.8. (Generators and Relations) Let G be a directed graph and R a binary relation on F(G), the free category of G. Then we say F(G)/R has generators G and relations R. An isomorphism F(G)/R →C and ⟨G | R⟩form a presentation of C. We say the morphisms of C are generated by the edges of G and the objects by the vertices, and that the binary relation R is sufficient.
