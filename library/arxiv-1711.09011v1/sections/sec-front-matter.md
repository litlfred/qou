Quantum groups and braid groups as fundamental
symmetries
Niels G. Gresnigt∗†
Xi’an Jiaotong-Liverpool University
E-mail: niels.gresnigt@xjtlu.edu.cn
The role of quantum groups and braid groups in the description of Standard Model particles is
discussed. Some recent results on the use of the quantum groupSUq(3) as a ﬂavour symmetry are
reviewed and a connection between two descriptions of Standard Model symmetries, one based
on the normed division algebras and the other describing elementary matter as braided objects, is
presented.
EPS-HEP 2017, European Physical Society conference on High Energy Physics
5-12 July 2017
Venice, Italy
