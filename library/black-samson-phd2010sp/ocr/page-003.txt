An Abstract of the Dissertation of

Samson Black for the degree of Doctor of Philosophy

in the Department of Mathematics to be taken June 2010

Title: REPRESENTATIONS OF HECKE ALGEBRAS AND THE ALEXANDER
POLYNOMIAL

Approved:

Dr. Arkady Vaintrob, Co-Chair

Approved:

Dr. Jonathan Brundan, Co-Chair

We study a certain quotient of the Iwahori-Hecke algebra of the symmetric group Su, called
the super Temperley-Lieb algebra STLy. The Alexander polynomial of a braid can be computed
via a certain specialization of the Markov trace which descends to STLqg. Combining this point
of view with Ocneanu’s formula for the Markov trace and Young’s seminormal form, we deduce a
new state-sum formula for the Alexander polynomial. We also give a direct combinatorial proof of

this result.
