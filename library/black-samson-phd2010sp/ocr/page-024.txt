I.4.1. Temperley-Lieb Arc Diagrams

Definition II.4.1. A (d,d')-are diagram D is a topological embedding of the disjoint union of
(d+d’)/2 intervals in the strip IR x (0, 1] such that the boundary of the embedded 1-manifold consists
of the points {(1,0),...,(d’,0)} U{ (1, 1),...,(d,1)}. A (d,d)-diagram is called a Temperley-Lieb
diagram on d strands. Let [D] denote the isotopy class of an arc diagram D, keeping its endpoints

fixed.
