It should be noted that the contributions of topologically nontrivial maps from the source circle to the target circle are, strictly speaking, beyond our usual setting of perturbation theory, since they are exponentially small in h. To be specific, the contribution from maps of degree N mostly comes from those maps which are close to the minimal action map qn (t) = 2xtNr/L, so it is of the order er Nr/Lh The maps gy(t) are the simplest examples of “instantons” — noncon- stant solutions of the classical equations of motion, which have finite action (and are nontrivial in the topological sense).
