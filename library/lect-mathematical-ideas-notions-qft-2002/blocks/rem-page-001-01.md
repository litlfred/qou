The name “least action principle” comes from the fact that in some cases (for example when U" < 0) the action is not only extremized but also minimized at the solution q(t). In general, however, it is not the case, and the trajectory of the particle may not be a minimum, but only a saddle point of the action. Therefore, the law of motion is better formulated as the “extremal (or stationary) action principle”; this is the way we will think of it in the future.
