We shall now proceed as follows. (i) We will explicitly give the correspondence between a (generic) cubic in P3 and the vanishing condition for the system of cubics. (ii) We construct the cubic which corresponds to the vanishing condition for the cubics through the six points Vk on the HC-model. (iii) We verify that the constructed map is birational from P2 to the cubic. (iv) Motivated by the geometry of Bring’s curve, we ﬁnd a collineation which maps the found cubic to the standard Clebsch surface (deﬁned below) in P4. (v) We verify that restricting to the HC-model in P2 corresponds to restricting to Bring’s curve in the Clebsch surface. (vi) We give examples of this new birational map from the (normalisation) of the HC-model to Bring’s on some particular points.
