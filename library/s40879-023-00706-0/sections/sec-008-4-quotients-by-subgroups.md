the relations between them, both clarifying and extending previous work. In Sect. 4.1

we shall note the various relationships we expect between the quotients just on group
theoretic grounds, while in Sects. 4.3–4.5 we turn to their explicit construction. In
so doing we discover a number of curious isomorphisms beyond those expected. In
Sect. 4.6 we summarise our calculations and relate them to known isogeny results.
