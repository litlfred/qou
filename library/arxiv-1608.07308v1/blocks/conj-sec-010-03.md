The spectral sequence from HOMFLY-PT homology (given by (1.28)) to the glN homology of P(1,...,1) degenerates after the ﬁrst nontrivial differential dN, which is given by the equation: (1.30) dN n X k=1 zk−1ξk ! = n X k=1 zk−1uk !N mod zn, dN(ui) = 0. This conjecture has been extensively veriﬁed against computer-generated data for N = 2 and 3 (see [29, 31]). We prove that Conjecture 1.15 immediately follows from Conjecture 1.13.
