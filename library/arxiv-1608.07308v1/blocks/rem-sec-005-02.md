While the present paper was being written, Oblomkov and Rozansky ([45]) inde- pendently gave an alternative construction of objects very similar to B(σ) and F(σ), although in a very different presentation. Speciﬁcally, their construction associates to any braid an object in the category of matrix factorizations, which descends to an object on the commuting vari- ety. The authors then show that the corresponding object is actually supported on the Hilbert scheme. We strongly suspect that their objects coincide with ours, and hope that the connection will be elucidated in the near future.
