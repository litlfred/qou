::: remark When working in the upgraded category [\[eqn:upgrade\]](#eqn:upgrade){reference-type="eqref" reference="eqn:upgrade"} rather than $\mathrm{SBim}_n$, one must be careful with Markov invariance, i.e. the statement ([@Kh]) that for $M\in \mathrm{SBim}_{n+1}$ one has: $$\mathop{\mathrm{Tr}}(M \otimes \sigma_n^{\pm 1})\simeq M \ \in \mathrm{SBim}_n$$ In the upgraded category, this equation becomes: $$\label{eqn: R1 upgraded} \mathop{\mathrm{Tr}}(M \otimes \sigma_n^{\pm 1})\simeq \left[M\otimes \mathbb{C}[x_{n+1}] \xrightarrow{x_n\otimes 1- 1 \otimes x_{n+1}} M\otimes \mathbb{C}[x_{n+1}] \right] \ \in \mathrm{SBim}_n[x_{n+1}]$$ The proof is straightforward and we leave it to the reader.
