Therefore, the matrices $X,Y: V \rightarrow V$ give rise to endomorphisms of the tautological bundle on the whole of $\mathrm{FHilb}_n$, which we will denote by the same letters: $$q\mathcal{T}_n \stackrel{X}\longrightarrow \mathcal{T}_n, \qquad t\mathcal{T}_n \stackrel{Y}\longrightarrow \mathcal{T}_n$$ In the formulas above, one must twist the tautological bundle by the torus characters $q,t$ in order for the endomorphisms $X,Y$ to be $\mathbb{C}^* \times \mathbb{C}^*$ equivariant.
