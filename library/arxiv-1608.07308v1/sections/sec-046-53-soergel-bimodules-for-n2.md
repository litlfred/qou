[]{#sub:soergel n=2 label="sub:soergel n=2"}

The category of Soergel bimodules is generated by two objects: $R = \mathbb{C}[x_1,x_2]$ and $B = R \otimes_{R^{(12)}} R$. With our grading conventions, we have: $$\label{eqn:square}
B^2 = B \otimes_R B \cong q^{\frac 12}B\oplus q^{- \frac 12}B$$ In the reduced category, we can set $x_1+x_2=0$ and $x_1-x_2 = x$, and write $\overline{R}=\mathbb{C}[x]$ and: $$\overline{B}=\overline{R}\otimes_{\overline{R}^s}\overline{R}=\mathbb{C}[x]\otimes_{\mathbb{C}[x^2]}\mathbb{C}[x].$$ This object also satisfies property [\[eqn:square\]](#eqn:square){reference-type="eqref" reference="eqn:square"}, and moreover: $$\mathop{\mathrm{Hom}}(\mathbf{1},\overline{B})\simeq \mathop{\mathrm{Ext}}^1(\mathbf{1},\overline{B})=\overline{R}$$ are rank 1 modules over $\overline{R}$. In terms of grading, note that $\mathop{\mathrm{Ext}}^1$ differs from $\mathop{\mathrm{Hom}}$ by a shift by the equivariant weight $a^{-1}q^{-1}$, which is an incarnation of the wedge product in [\[eqn:iota wedge\]](#eqn:iota wedge){reference-type="eqref" reference="eqn:iota wedge"}. Thus: $$\label{eqn:basic}
\mathop{\mathrm{{RHom}}}^{\bullet}(\mathbf{1},\overline{B})=\wedge^{\bullet} \left( \frac {\xi}{qa} \right)\otimes \overline{R}$$ for a formal variable $\xi$. The object in the Soergel category which corresponds to a single positive crossing $\sigma$ is the following complex: $$\sigma = \left[\overline{B}\xrightarrow{1\otimes 1 \mapsto 1} \frac {s\overline{R}}{q^{\frac 12}} \right]$$ The powers of $s$ mark homological degree, and so they are always consecutive integers in a complex. We mainly use them to pinpoint the 0--th term of a complex, and to compare with formulas from geometry. Similarly, the object in the Soergel category which corresponds to a single negative crossing $\sigma^{-1}$ is: $$\sigma^{-1} = \left[\frac {q^{\frac 12}\overline{R}}s \xrightarrow{1 \mapsto x \otimes 1 + 1 \otimes x} \overline{B}\right]$$ Let us write $\overline{\mathbf{FT}}= \overline{\mathbf{FT}}_2$ for the image of the full twist in the reduced Soergel category, and note that $\overline{\mathbf{FT}}= \sigma^2$. Therefore, formula [\[eqn:square\]](#eqn:square){reference-type="eqref" reference="eqn:square"} allows us to write: $$\overline{\mathbf{FT}}= \left[q^{\frac 12} \overline{B}\to \frac {s \overline{B}}{q^{\frac 12}} \to \frac {s^2 \overline{R}}q \right]$$ Recall that the connection between the parameters $s$ and $t$ is given by $s^2 = qt$. The two eigenmaps that span the space $\mathop{\mathrm{Hom}}(\mathbf{1}, \overline{\mathbf{FT}})$ are described in the following diagram: $$\label{eqn:eigenmaps2}
\includegraphics{5_12.png}$$ where $z = (1\mapsto x \otimes 1 + 1 \otimes x)$ and $w = \text{Id}$. As we will see in more examples in the next Subsection, it is no coincidence that the only maps from $\overline{R}$ into non-negative powers of the full twist have integer $q,t$--weights: this is called the "parity miracle\" by [@EH].

::: proposition
[]{#prop:koszul=0 n=2 label="prop:koszul=0 n=2"} We have the following relation in the category $\mathrm{SBim}_2$: $$0 \stackrel{\emph{h.e.}}\cong \left[ ... \xrightarrow{\alpha_1} q \overline{\mathbf{FT}}^{-2} \oplus q \overline{\mathbf{FT}}^{-2} \xrightarrow{\alpha_2}  \overline{\mathbf{FT}}^{-2} \oplus q \overline{\mathbf{FT}}^{-1} \xrightarrow{\alpha_1} \overline{\mathbf{FT}}^{-1} \oplus \overline{\mathbf{FT}}^{-1} \xrightarrow{(z,w)} \mathbf{1} \right]$$ where the maps $\alpha_1$ and $\alpha_2$ are given by: $$\label{eqn:alphas}
\alpha_1 = \left( \begin{array}{cc}
w & 0 \\
-z & x \end{array} \right) \qquad \text{and} \qquad  \alpha_2 = \left( \begin{array}{cc}
x & 0 \\
z & w \end{array} \right)$$
:::

::: proof
*Proof.* Remark that this complex is filtered by complexes: $$\begin{gathered}
\label{eqn:small koszul}
\left[\overline{\mathbf{FT}}^{-k-2}  \xrightarrow{(w,-z)} \overline{\mathbf{FT}}^{-k-1} \oplus \overline{\mathbf{FT}}^{-k-1} \xrightarrow{(z,w)} \overline{\mathbf{FT}}^{-k} \right] = \\
= \overline{\mathbf{FT}}^{-k-2}\otimes \mathop{\mathrm{Cone}}\left[\mathbf{1}\xrightarrow{w}\overline{\mathbf{FT}}\right]\otimes \mathop{\mathrm{Cone}}\left[\mathbf{1}\xrightarrow{z}\overline{\mathbf{FT}}\right],
\end{gathered}$$ so it is sufficient to prove that $\mathop{\mathrm{Cone}}(z)\otimes \mathop{\mathrm{Cone}}(w)\simeq \mathop{\mathrm{Cone}}(w)\otimes \mathop{\mathrm{Cone}}(z) \simeq 0$ (indeed, this would imply that the complexes in the left hand side of [\[eqn:small koszul\]](#eqn:small koszul){reference-type="eqref" reference="eqn:small koszul"} are contractible). Since: $$\mathop{\mathrm{Cone}}\left[\mathbf{1}\xrightarrow{w}\overline{\mathbf{FT}}\right]=[\overline{B}\xrightarrow{} \overline{B}],$$ and: $$\overline{B}\otimes \mathop{\mathrm{Cone}}(z)\simeq \overline{B}\otimes [\overline{R}\to \overline{B}\to \overline{B}\to \overline{R}]\simeq [\overline{B}\to \overline{B}\oplus \overline{B}\to \overline{B}\oplus \overline{B}\to \overline{B}]\simeq 0,$$ we conclude that $\mathop{\mathrm{Cone}}(w) \otimes \mathop{\mathrm{Cone}}(z) \simeq 0$. The case of $\mathop{\mathrm{Cone}}(z) \otimes \mathop{\mathrm{Cone}}(w)$ is analogous. ◻
:::

## $\mathrm{Proj}$ construction {#sub:proj construction}

The purpose of this Subsection is to construct the functors: $$\label{eqn:functors n=2}
D^b\left(\mathrm{Coh}_{\mathbb{C}^{*} \times \mathbb{C}^*} \left( \mathrm{FHilb}_2\right) \right) \xtofrom[\iota_*]{\iota^*} K^b(\mathrm{SBim}_2)$$ and prove Conjecture [\[conj:1\]](#conj:1){reference-type="ref" reference="conj:1"} for $n=2$. To keep our notation simple, we will perform the computation for the reduced versions of the above categories. As was shown in Section [\[sec:geometry\]](#sec:geometry){reference-type="ref" reference="sec:geometry"}, in order to construct $\iota_*$ one needs to prove the following isomorphism of graded algebras: $$\label{eqn:volo}
\bigoplus_{k=0}^\infty \mathop{\mathrm{Hom}}\left(\mathbf{1},\overline{\mathbf{FT}}^{k}\right) \cong \bigoplus_{k=0}^\infty \mathop{\mathrm{Hom}}\left( \overline{\mathrm{FHilb}}_2,\mathcal{O}(k) \right)$$ To compute the left hand side of [\[eqn:volo\]](#eqn:volo){reference-type="eqref" reference="eqn:volo"}, recall from [@Kh] that we have the following identity in $\mathrm{SBim}_2$ for all $k>0$: $$\overline{\mathbf{FT}}^{k}\simeq \left[\underbrace{q^{k-\frac 12}\overline{B}\rightarrow q^{k-\frac 32} s \overline{B}\rightarrow \cdots \rightarrow \frac {s^{2k-3} \overline{B}}{q^{k-\frac 52}} \rightarrow \frac {s^{2k-2} \overline{B}}{q^{k-\frac 32}} \rightarrow \frac {s^{2k-1} \overline{B}}{q^{k-\frac 12}}}_{2k} \rightarrow \frac {s^{2k} \overline{R}}{q^k} \right]$$ where the maps alternate between $\frac {x \otimes 1 - 1 \otimes x}2$ and $\frac {x \otimes 1 + 1 \otimes x}2$. Since $s=-\sqrt{qt}$, we have: $$\mathop{\mathrm{Hom}}(\mathbf{1},\overline{\mathbf{FT}}^{k}) \simeq \left[\underbrace{q^k \overline{R}\stackrel{0}\rightarrow q^{k-1} t^{\frac 12} \overline{R}\xrightarrow{x} \cdots \stackrel{0}\rightarrow q  t^{k-\frac 32} \overline{R}\xrightarrow{x} q t^{k-1} \overline{R}\stackrel{0}\rightarrow t^{k-\frac 12} \overline{R}}_{2k} \xrightarrow{x} t^k \overline{R}\right]$$ $$\label{eqn:hom}
\cong z^k \mathbb{C}[x] \bigoplus_{i=1}^{k}w^{i} z^{k-i} \frac {\mathbb{C}[x]}x  = \overline{A}^k$$ One can think of $z$, $w$ as formal variables of degrees $q$, $t$, but they actually correspond to the maps of [\[eqn:eigenmaps2\]](#eqn:eigenmaps2){reference-type="eqref" reference="eqn:eigenmaps2"} under the required isomorphism [\[eqn:volo\]](#eqn:volo){reference-type="eqref" reference="eqn:volo"}. This establishes [\[eqn:volo\]](#eqn:volo){reference-type="eqref" reference="eqn:volo"} as an isomorphism of $\mathbb{C}[x]$--modules. We claim that this isomorphism also preserves the algebra structures, and therefore the functor $\iota_*$ is well-defined. By construction: $$\iota_*(\overline{\mathbf{FT}}^k) = \mathcal{O}(k)$$ for all $k\geq 0$. As for the functor $\iota^*$ of [\[eqn:functors n=2\]](#eqn:functors n=2){reference-type="eqref" reference="eqn:functors n=2"}, we require: $$\iota^*(\mathcal{O}(k)) := \overline{\mathbf{FT}}^k$$ and: $$\iota^* \left(q\mathcal{O}\stackrel{z}\longrightarrow \mathcal{O}(1) \right) \ \text{ and } \ \iota^* \left(q\mathcal{O}\stackrel{w}\longrightarrow \mathcal{O}(1) \right) \  = \text{ the maps \eqref{eqn:eigenmaps2}}$$ However, note that this assignment simply defines a functor: $$\mathrm{Coh}\left( \mathrm{Spec}\ \overline{A}/ \mathbb{C}^* \right) \xrightarrow{\iota^*} \mathrm{SBim}_2$$ since $\overline{A}$ is the homogeneous coordinate ring of $\overline{\mathrm{FHilb}}_2$. We wish to show that this functor factors through $D^b(\mathrm{Coh}(\mathrm{Proj}\ \overline{A}))$. To do so, we must prove that the object: $$\label{eqn:quiero}
0 \stackrel{\mathrm{q.i.s.}}\cong \overline{A}_0 = \frac {\overline{A}}{(z,w)} \text{ on } \overline{\mathrm{FHilb}}_2 \qquad \xrightarrow{\text{goes to}} \qquad \iota^*(\overline{A}_0) \stackrel{\text{h.e.}}\cong 0 \text{ in } \mathrm{SBim}_2$$ To compute the image of $\overline{A}_0$ under $\iota^*$, we need to resolve this object in terms of free $\overline{A}$ modules. The standard choice is the Koszul resolution, which is infinite because $\overline{\mathrm{FHilb}}_2$ is singular: $$0 \stackrel{\mathrm{q.i.s.}}\cong \left[ ... \xrightarrow{\alpha_1} q\overline{A}(-2) \oplus q\overline{A}(-2) \xrightarrow{\alpha_2}  \overline{A}(-2) \oplus q\overline{A}(-1) \xrightarrow{\alpha_1}\overline{A}(-1) \oplus \overline{A}(-1) \xrightarrow{(z,w)} \overline{A}\right]$$ where the maps alternate between those of [\[eqn:alphas\]](#eqn:alphas){reference-type="eqref" reference="eqn:alphas"}. Then [\[eqn:quiero\]](#eqn:quiero){reference-type="eqref" reference="eqn:quiero"} follows from Proposition [\[prop:koszul=0 n=2\]](#prop:koszul=0 n=2){reference-type="ref" reference="prop:koszul=0 n=2"}.

::: remark
[]{#rem:a grading n=2 label="rem:a grading n=2"}

By analogy with [\[eqn:hom\]](#eqn:hom){reference-type="eqref" reference="eqn:hom"}, we have: $$\mathop{\mathrm{Ext}}^1(\mathbf{1},\overline{\mathbf{FT}}^{k}) \cong \left[\underbrace{q^{k-1} \overline{R}\stackrel{0}\rightarrow q^{k-1} t^{\frac 12} \overline{R}\xrightarrow{x} \cdots \stackrel{0}\rightarrow q t^{k-\frac 32} \overline{R}\xrightarrow{x}  t^{k-1} \overline{R}\stackrel{0}\rightarrow t^{k-\frac 12} \overline{R}}_{2k} \stackrel{1}\rightarrow t^k \overline{R}\right] \cong$$ $$\cong \ z^{k-1} \mathbb{C}[x] \bigoplus_{i=1}^{k-1} w^{i} z^{k-1-i} \frac {\mathbb{C}[x]}x = \overline{A}^{k-1}$$ and therefore: $$\mathop{\mathrm{{RHom}}}\left(\mathbf{1},\overline{\mathbf{FT}}^k \right) \cong \mathop{\mathrm{Hom}}\left(\mathbf{1},\overline{\mathbf{FT}}^{k-1} \right)$$ This is precisely [\[eqn:rhom vs hom\]](#eqn:rhom vs hom){reference-type="eqref" reference="eqn:rhom vs hom"} for $M = \overline{\mathbf{FT}}^k$ and $\overline{T}_2 = \iota^*(\overline{\mathcal{T}}_2) = \iota^*(\mathcal{O}(1)) = \overline{\mathbf{FT}}$.
:::
