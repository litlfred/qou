[]{#sub:linear label="sub:linear"}

We start by recalling certain classical constructions in algebraic geometry which will guide all subsequent generalizations. Let $X$ be a projective algebraic variety and let $\mathcal{L}$ be a line bundle (i.e. a rank one locally free sheaf) over $X$. One says that $\mathcal{L}$ is generated by global sections if the map of sheaves: $$\mathcal{O}_X \otimes \Gamma(X, \mathcal{L}) \rightarrow \mathcal{L}$$ is surjective. If we choose a basis $s_0,...,s_n$ of the vector space $\Gamma(X,\mathcal{L})$, this comes down to requiring that any local section of $\mathcal{L}$ is a linear combination of the sections $s_0,...,s_n$. Moreover, the above datum gives rise to a map: $$\label{eqn:embedding}
X \stackrel{\iota}\rightarrow \mathbb{P}^n, \qquad x \mapsto [s_0(x):...:s_n(x)]$$ Global generation implies the fact that the sections $s_0,...,s_n$ cannot all vanish simultaneously. Moreover, while $s_i$ are sections of the line bundle $\mathcal{L}$, their ratios are well-defined functions on $X$. To this end, we may define the open subset: $$X_i =\{s_i(x)\neq 0\} \subset X$$ where the ratios $s_j/s_i$ are well--defined. Hence the map [\[eqn:embedding\]](#eqn:embedding){reference-type="eqref" reference="eqn:embedding"} restricts to a map: $$X_i \rightarrow U_i = \{z_i \neq 0\} \subset \mathbb{P}^n$$ If we let $\mathcal{O}(1)$ denote the tautological line bundle on $\mathbb{P}^n$, then we have: $$\iota^*(\mathcal{O}(k)) = \mathcal{L}^{\otimes k}, \quad \forall \ k\in \mathbb{Z}$$ The functor $\iota^*$ is monoidal, and is the left adjoint of the direct image functor: $$\label{eqn:embedding2}
\mathrm{Coh}(X) \xtofrom[\iota^*]{\iota_*} \mathrm{Coh}(\mathbb{P}^n)$$ In the remainder of this section, we present a generalization of this construction, where the role of the map $\iota:X \rightarrow \mathbb{P}^n$ is replaced by an abstract categorical setup inspired by [\[eqn:embedding2\]](#eqn:embedding2){reference-type="eqref" reference="eqn:embedding2"}.

::: remark
[]{#rem:prod cones label="rem:prod cones"}

By deriving the functors in question, we may write [\[eqn:embedding2\]](#eqn:embedding2){reference-type="eqref" reference="eqn:embedding2"} at the level of derived categories. Then the sections can be thought of as complexes: $$\left[ \mathcal{O}_X \stackrel{s_i}\rightarrow \mathcal{L}\right] \in D^b(\mathrm{Coh}(X))$$ which are supported on $\{ X\setminus X_i \} = \{s_i=0\}$. The product of these complexes: $$\label{eqn:tensor}
\bigotimes_{i=0}^{n} \left[ \mathcal{O}_X \stackrel{s_i}\rightarrow \mathcal{L}\right]$$ is therefore supported on the set where all $s_i$ vanish simultaneously, which by assumption is the empty set. Therefore, [\[eqn:tensor\]](#eqn:tensor){reference-type="eqref" reference="eqn:tensor"} is quasi-isomorphic to 0, and hence it vanishes in $D^b(\mathrm{Coh}(X))$. Put differently, the vanishing of [\[eqn:tensor\]](#eqn:tensor){reference-type="eqref" reference="eqn:tensor"} is forced upon us by the vanishing of the Koszul complex: $$\bigotimes_{i=0}^{n} \left[ \mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^n} \stackrel{z_i}\rightarrow \mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^n}(1) \right] \stackrel{\mathrm{q.i.s.}}\cong 0 \ \in \ D^b(\mathrm{Coh}(\mathbb{P}^n))$$ and the fact that the derived version of the functor $\iota^*$ in [\[eqn:embedding2\]](#eqn:embedding2){reference-type="eqref" reference="eqn:embedding2"} is monoidal.
:::

::: remark
Projective space can be defined more scheme-theoretically as: $$\mathbb{P}^n = \mathrm{Proj}\left(\bigoplus_{k=0}^{\infty} S^k\mathbb{C}^{n+1} \right)$$ Then the map [\[eqn:embedding\]](#eqn:embedding){reference-type="eqref" reference="eqn:embedding"} is given by the map $\mathbb{C}^{n+1} \to \Gamma(X,\mathcal{L})$ induced by the choice of the sections $s_0,...,s_n$, and in fact global generation translates into: $$X = \mathrm{Proj}\left(\bigoplus_{k=0}^{\infty} \Gamma(X,\mathcal{L}^{\otimes k}) \right).$$
:::
