[]{#sub:soergel label="sub:soergel"}

The category of Soergel bimodules, which we will denote $\mathrm{SBim}_n$, is a categorification of the Hecke algebra. We will consider $R = \mathbb{C}[x_1,...,x_n]$ and study graded $R-$bimodules, where $\deg x_i = 1$. We will write $q M$ for the graded module $M$ with the grading shifted by 1. Among the most important such $R-$bimodules are the elementary **Bott-Samelson** bimodules: $$\label{eqn:bs}
B_i = q^{-\frac 12} R \otimes_{R^{i,i+1}} R$$ for any simple transposition $s_i = (i,i+1)$, where we write $R^{i,i+1}$ for those polynomials which are invariant under $s_i$. In other words, $R^{i,i+1}$ consists of polynomials which are symmetric in $x_i$ and $x_{i+1}$, and therefore $R$ has rank 2 over $R^{i,i+1}$. Therefore, $B_i$ has rank 2 as an $R-$module.

::: definition
[]{#def:soergel label="def:soergel"} The category $\mathrm{SBim}_n$ is the Karoubian envelope of the smallest full subcategory of $R\text{--mod--}R$ that contains the Bott-Samuelson modules $B_i$ and is closed under $\otimes_R$ and grading shifts. Objects of $\mathrm{SBim}_n$ will be called **Soergel bimodules**.
:::

The category $\mathrm{SBim}_n$ is monoidal with respect to the operation of tensoring bimodules over $R$. Clearly, the unit object is $\mathbf{1} := R$, viewed as a bimodule over itself. Note that $\mathrm{SBim}_n$ is neither abelian, nor symmetric. Let: $$B_{i,i+1} = q^{-1} R \otimes_{R^{i,i+1,i+2}} R$$ where $R^{i,i+1,i+2}$ denotes the set of polynomials which are symmetric in $x_i,x_{i+1},x_{i+2}$. Then one can check the following identities [@Kh; @Soergel]: $$\label{eqn:BS1}
B_i^2\simeq q^{\frac 12} B_i\oplus q^{-\frac 12} B_i, \qquad B_iB_j\simeq B_jB_j\ \text{for}\ |i-j|>1,$$ $$\label{eqn:BS2}
B_iB_{i+1}B_{i}\simeq B_i\oplus B_{i,i+1}\ \Rightarrow\ B_iB_{i+1}B_{i}\oplus B_{i+1}\simeq B_{i+1}B_{i}B_{i+1}\oplus B_{i}.$$ It was shown in [@Soergel] that the split graded Grothendieck group of $\mathrm{SBim}_n$ is generated by the classes of $B_i$ and is isomorphic to $H_n$. Indeed, one can identify $[B_i]=\sigma_i+q^{-\frac 12}$ and show that [\[eqn:BS1\]](#eqn:BS1){reference-type="eqref" reference="eqn:BS1"}--[\[eqn:BS2\]](#eqn:BS2){reference-type="eqref" reference="eqn:BS2"} imply [\[eqn:hecke1\]](#eqn:hecke1){reference-type="eqref" reference="eqn:hecke1"}--[\[eqn:hecke3\]](#eqn:hecke3){reference-type="eqref" reference="eqn:hecke3"}.
