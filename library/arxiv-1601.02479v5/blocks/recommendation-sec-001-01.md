The main conclusion of this paper is that the introduction of the cohomological Hall algebra structure associated to a quiver, a potential and a Serre subcategory $\mathcal{S}$ should be seen as a first step towards forging a connection between the theory of refined Donaldson--Thomas invariants and the theory of quantum enveloping algebras, and that the proof of the integrality conjecture is the motivic shadow of a Poincaré--Birkhoff--Witt theorem for the associated quantum enveloping algebra.
