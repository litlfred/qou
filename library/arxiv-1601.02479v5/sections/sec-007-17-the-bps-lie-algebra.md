[]{#BPSLA label="BPSLA"} One of the main contributions of the current paper, and a direct corollary of the above structural theorems, is the definition of the BPS Lie algebra. This is a natural Lie algebra, for any choice of $Q,W,\upmu,\mathcal{S},\uppsi$ and $\upmu$-generic stability condition $\upzeta$, the characteristic function of which recovers the (refined) BPS invariants for this data. Part of Theorem [\[qea\]](#qea){reference-type="ref" reference="qea"} states that $\mathop{\mathrm{Gr}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathfrak{P}}}}(\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(^{\uppsi}\!\!\mathcal{A}_{W,\upmu}^{\upzeta,\mathcal{S}}))$ is supercommutative. On the other hand, the perverse filtration on $\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(^{\uppsi}\!\!\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{A}}}_{W,\upmu}^{\upzeta,\mathcal{S}})$ begins in degree one, and in degree one is given precisely by a half Tate-twist of the cohomological BPS invariants. It follows that there is an inclusion of Lie algebras $$\label{BPSdomain}
\mathbb{L}^{1/2}\otimes\mathop{\mathrm{BPS}}_{W,\upmu}^{\upzeta,\mathcal{S}}\hookrightarrow \mathop{\mathrm{H}}(^{\uppsi}\!\!\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{A}}}_{W,\upmu}^{\upzeta,\mathcal{S}}),$$ i.e. that the first perverse piece of the target is closed under the commutator Lie bracket (since this bracket becomes zero in the perverse associated graded algebra). We call the domain of ([\[BPSdomain\]](#BPSdomain){reference-type="ref" reference="BPSdomain"}) the *BPS Lie algebra* associated to the data $(Q,W,\upzeta,\upmu,\mathcal{S},\uppsi)$. In a forthcoming paper the first author shows that for a specific choice $\tilde{Q},\tilde{W}$ of quiver with potential depending on a given quiver $Q$, $$\mathop{\mathrm{H}}^0(\mathbb{L}^{1/2}\otimes \mathcal{BPS}_{\tilde{W}})\cong\mathfrak{g}_{Q_{\mathrm{re}}}^+,$$ where $\mathfrak{g}_{Q_{\mathrm{re}}}^+$ is the positive half of the Kac--Moody Lie algebra for $Q_{\mathrm{re}}$, the quiver obtained from $Q$ by deleting all vertices for which there are loops, along with all arrows to or from these vertices. In particular, the BPS Lie bracket can be highly nontrivial.
