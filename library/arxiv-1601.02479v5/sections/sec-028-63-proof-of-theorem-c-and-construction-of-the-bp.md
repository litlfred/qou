[]{#ThmCproof label="ThmCproof"}

We start with a couple of technical lemmas for the proofs in this section.

::: lemma
[]{#Tech1 label="Tech1"} Let $\mathcal{F},\mathcal{G}\in\mathop{\mathrm{MMHM}}(X)$. Then $f\colon \mathcal{F}\rightarrow \mathcal{G}$ is an isomorphism if and only if $f_x$ is an isomorphism for every closed point $x\in X$.
:::

::: proof
*Proof.* One implication is trivial, so we assume that $f_x$ is an isomorphism for every $x$ and prove that $f$ is an isomorphism. Since the functor $$\mathop{\mathrm{forg}}^{\mathop{\mathrm{mon}}}_{X}\colon \mathop{\mathrm{MMHM}}(X)\rightarrow \mathop{\mathrm{Perv}}(X)$$ is faithful, it suffices to prove the lemma with the category $\mathop{\mathrm{MMHM}}(X)$ replaced by $\mathop{\mathrm{Perv}}(X)$. Write $C=\textrm{cone}(f)$. Assume for a contradiction that $C\neq 0$. Let $i\in\mathbb{Z}$ be the lowest number such that the constructible cohomology sheaf $\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}_{\textrm{con}}^i(C)$ is nonzero, and set $$\mathcal{R}\colonequals \mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}_{\textrm{con}}^i(C)[-i].$$ Denote by $\iota_x\colon x\hookrightarrow X$ the inclusion. The functor $\iota^*_x$ is exact for the natural t-structure on the derived category of constructible sheaves, and there is an isomorphism $\iota_x^*C\cong \textrm{cone}(f_x)$, so the first term in the exact sequence $$\rightarrow \mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}^{i-1}_{\textrm{con}}(\iota_x^*\uptau_{>i}C)\rightarrow \mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}^i_{\textrm{con}}(\iota_x^*\mathcal{R})\rightarrow \mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}^i_{\textrm{con}}(\iota^*_xC)\rightarrow.$$ is zero, as well as the last, by supposition. Then $\mathcal{R}[i]$ is a nonzero constructible sheaf satisfying $\iota^*_x\mathcal{R}[i]=0$ for all $x$, a contradiction. ◻
:::

::: lemma
[]{#Tech2 label="Tech2"} Let $\mathcal{F},\mathcal{G}\in\mathop{\mathrm{MMHM}}(X)$, with $\mathop{\mathrm{forg}}^{\mathop{\mathrm{mon}}}_{X}(\mathcal{F})$ and $\mathop{\mathrm{forg}}^{\mathop{\mathrm{mon}}}_{X}(\mathcal{G})$ semisimple perverse sheaves, and let $f\colon \mathcal{F}\rightarrow\mathcal{G}$ be a morphism between them. Then $f=0$ if and only if $f_x=0$ for every closed point $\iota_x\colon x\hookrightarrow X$.
:::

::: proof
*Proof.* Again, one direction is trivial, and we may assume that we are working in $\mathop{\mathrm{Perv}}(X)$ throughout, by faithfulness of $\mathop{\mathrm{forg}}^{\mathop{\mathrm{mon}}}_{X}$. So assume that $f_x=0$ for all $x$. Let $\{S_t\}_{t\in T}$ be the set of all simple perverse sheaves appearing in a direct sum decomposition of $\mathcal{F}$ and $\mathcal{G}$. Then there is a commutative diagram $$\xymatrix{
\bigoplus_{t\in T} S_t\otimes_{\mathbb{C}}\mathop{\mathrm{Hom}}(S_t,\mathcal{F})\ar[r]^-{\cong}\ar[d]_{\bigoplus_t \mathop{\mathrm{id}}_t\otimes f_t}&\mathcal{F}\ar[d]_f\\
\bigoplus_{t\in T} S_t\otimes_{\mathbb{C}}\mathop{\mathrm{Hom}}(S_t,\mathcal{G})\ar[r]^-{\cong}& \mathcal{G}
}$$ where $\mathop{\mathrm{id}}_t\colon S_t\rightarrow S_t$ is the identity map, and $f_t\colon \mathop{\mathrm{Hom}}(S_t,\mathcal{F})\rightarrow \mathop{\mathrm{Hom}}(S_t,\mathcal{G})$ is obtained from composition with $f$. Writing $$S_t=\tilde{\mathcal{IC}}_{Y}(\mathcal{L})[\dim(Y)]$$ for $Y\subset X$ a closed irreducible subvariety, and $\mathcal{L}$ a local system defined on $Y'\subset Y$ a regular open subvariety, and picking $x\in Y'$, we deduce that $\iota_x^*\mathop{\mathrm{id}}_t\neq 0$, and so $f_t=0$ for all $t$. ◻
:::

With these lemmas in hand, we move back to consideration of cohomological Hall algebras. Throughout this section we assume that $\upzeta$ is $\upmu$-generic. By Proposition [\[inccor\]](#inccor){reference-type="ref" reference="inccor"} there is a canonical embedding $$\iota=\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}(\dim_*\tilde{\omega}^{\upzeta,!}_{\upmu}\Upsilon_{W,\upmu}^{\upzeta})$$ of $\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\mathop{\mathrm{B\mathbb{C}^*}})_{\mathop{\mathrm{vir}}}\otimes \mathop{\mathrm{BPS}}_{W,\upmu}^{\upzeta,\mathcal{S}}$ inside $\mathop{\mathrm{Gr}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathfrak{P}}}}(\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{A}}}^{\upzeta,\mathcal{S}}_{W,\upmu}))$.

::: proposition
[]{#primitivity label="primitivity"} The subspace $\iota(\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\mathop{\mathrm{B\mathbb{C}^*}})_{\mathop{\mathrm{vir}}}\otimes \mathop{\mathrm{BPS}}_{W,\upmu}^{\upzeta,\mathcal{S}})$ is a primitive subspace in $\mathop{\mathrm{Gr}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathfrak{P}}}}(\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{A}}}_{W,\upmu}^{\upzeta,\mathcal{S}}))$, i.e. the following composition of maps is zero: $$\begin{aligned}
&\bigoplus_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}\in \Uplambda_{\upmu}^{\upzeta}\setminus\{0\}}\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\mathop{\mathrm{B\mathbb{C}^*}})_{\mathop{\mathrm{vir}}}\otimes\mathop{\mathrm{BPS}}_{W,\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}^{\upzeta,\mathcal{S}}\xrightarrow{\iota}\mathop{\mathrm{Gr}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathfrak{P}}}}(\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{A}}}^{\upzeta,\mathcal{S}}_{W,\upmu}))\xrightarrow{\mathop{\mathrm{Gr}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathfrak{P}}}}(\Delta^{\upzeta,\mathcal{S}}_{W,\upmu})}\\&\bigoplus_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''\in\Uplambda_{\upmu}^{\upzeta}}\mathop{\mathrm{Gr}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathfrak{P}}}}\left((\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{A}}}^{\upzeta,\mathcal{S}}_{W,\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'})\boxtimes^{\mathop{\mathrm{tw}}}_+\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{A}}}^{\upzeta,\mathcal{S}}_{W,\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}))[\mathfrak{L}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}^{-1}]\right)
\twoheadrightarrow \bigoplus_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''\in\Uplambda_{\upmu}^{\upzeta}\setminus\{0\}}\mathop{\mathrm{Gr}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathfrak{P}}}}\left((\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{A}}}^{\upzeta,\mathcal{S}}_{W,\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'}\boxtimes^{\mathop{\mathrm{tw}}}_+\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{A}}}^{\upzeta,\mathcal{S}}_{W,\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''})[\mathfrak{L}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}^{-1}]\right).
\end{aligned}$$
:::

::: proof
*Proof.* We need to show that for decompositions $\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}=\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'+\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''$, with $\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'\neq 0\neq \mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''$, the image of $\iota(\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\mathop{\mathrm{B\mathbb{C}^*}})_{\mathop{\mathrm{vir}}}\otimes\mathop{\mathrm{BPS}}_{W,\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}^{\upzeta,\mathcal{S}})$ under the map $$\begin{aligned}
\label{int4}
&\mathop{\mathrm{Gr}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathfrak{P}}}}(\Delta^{\upzeta,\mathcal{S}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''})\colon \mathop{\mathrm{H}}\left(\mathcal{M}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}},\mathcal{S}},\tilde{\omega}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}^!\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(p^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}},*}\upphi^{\mathop{\mathrm{mon}}}_{\mathfrak{Tr}(W)^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}}\mathcal{IC}_{\mathfrak{M}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}}\right)\right)\rightarrow \\ \nonumber&\left(\mathop{\mathrm{H}}\left(\mathcal{M}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}},\mathcal{S}},\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(p^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',*}\upphi^{\mathop{\mathrm{mon}}}_{\mathfrak{Tr}(W)^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'}}\mathcal{IC}_{\mathfrak{M}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}}\right)\right)\boxtimes_{+}^{\mathop{\mathrm{tw}}} \mathop{\mathrm{H}}\left(\mathcal{M}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}},\mathcal{S}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''},\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(p^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'',*}\upphi^{\mathop{\mathrm{mon}}}_{\mathfrak{Tr}(W)^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}}\mathcal{IC}_{\mathfrak{M}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}}\right)\right)\right)[\mathfrak{L}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}^{-1}]
\end{aligned}$$ is zero. We assume that $\mathcal{M}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-st}}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}\neq \emptyset$, or the statement is trivial, for then by definition $\mathop{\mathrm{BPS}}_{W,\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}^{\upzeta,\mathcal{S}}=0$. Denote by $R\Upgamma$ the derived global sections functor on $\mathcal{M}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}},\mathcal{S}}$. In terms of the commutative diagram $$\label{int5}
\xymatrix{
R\Upgamma\left(\tilde{\omega}^{\upzeta,!}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(p^{\upzeta}_{\upmu,*}\upphi^{\mathop{\mathrm{mon}}}_{\mathfrak{Tr}(W)_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}^{\upzeta}}\mathcal{IC}_{\mathfrak{M}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}}(\mathbb{Q})\right)\right)\ar[r]^-{\mathop{\mathrm{Gr}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathfrak{P}}}}(\upgamma)} &R\Upgamma\left(\tilde{\omega}^{\upzeta,!}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(p^{\upzeta}_{\upmu,*}s_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'',*}\upphi^{\mathop{\mathrm{mon}}}_{\mathfrak{Tr}(W)^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}}\mathbb{L}^{(\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}},\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}})/2}_{\mathfrak{M}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}}\right)\right)\\
R\Upgamma\left(\tilde{\omega}^{\upzeta,!}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}\upphi^{\mathop{\mathrm{mon}}}_{\mathcal{T}r(W)_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}^{\upzeta}}\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(p^{\upzeta}_{\upmu,*}\mathcal{IC}_{\mathfrak{M}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}}(\mathbb{Q})\right)\right)\ar[r]^-{\upgamma'} \ar[u]^{\mathbb{L}^{(\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}},\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}})/2}\otimes R\Upgamma\left( \tilde{\omega}^{\upzeta,!}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}\upnu_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}\right)}& R\Upgamma\left(\tilde{\omega}^{\upzeta,!}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}\upphi^{\mathop{\mathrm{mon}}}_{\mathcal{T}r(W)_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}^{\upzeta}}\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(p^{\upzeta}_{\upmu,*}s_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'',*}\mathbb{L}^{(\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}},\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}})/2}_{\mathfrak{M}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}}\right)\right)\ar[u]^{\mathbb{L}^{(\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}},\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}})/2}\otimes R\Upgamma\left(\tilde{\omega}^{\upzeta,!}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}\upnu_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}\right)}\\
R\Upgamma\left( \tilde{\omega}^{\upzeta,!}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}\upphi^{\mathop{\mathrm{mon}}}_{\mathcal{T}r(W)^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}}\mathcal{IC}_{\mathcal{M}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}}(\mathbb{Q})\otimes\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\mathop{\mathrm{B\mathbb{C}^*}})_{\mathop{\mathrm{vir}}}\right)\ar[u]^{\iota}
}$$ with $\upgamma$ as in ([\[gammadef\]](#gammadef){reference-type="ref" reference="gammadef"}), it is sufficient to show that $$\mathop{\mathrm{Gr}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathfrak{P}}}}(\upgamma)\circ(\mathbb{L}^{(\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}},\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}})/2}\otimes R\Upgamma(\tilde{\omega}^{\upzeta,!}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}\upnu_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}))\circ\iota=0.$$ The maps $\upnu_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}$ and $\upnu_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}$ are as in Proposition [\[cvs\]](#cvs){reference-type="ref" reference="cvs"}. On the other hand, the map $\upgamma'\iota$ is obtained by applying $R\Upgamma\left(\tilde{\omega}^{\upzeta,!}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}\upphi^{\mathop{\mathrm{mon}}}_{\mathcal{T}r(W)_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}^{\upzeta}}\right)$ to a map $$\begin{aligned}
\label{nearmiss}
\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\mathop{\mathrm{B\mathbb{C}^*}})_{\mathop{\mathrm{vir}}}\otimes\mathcal{IC}_{\mathcal{M}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}}(\mathbb{Q})\rightarrow \mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(p^{\upzeta}_{\upmu,*}s_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'',*}\mathbb{L}^{(\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}},\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}})}_{\mathfrak{M}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}}\right)
\end{aligned}$$ which preserves perverse degree, and so is necessarily zero, since for every cohomological degree, the domain and the target of ([\[nearmiss\]](#nearmiss){reference-type="ref" reference="nearmiss"}) are pure complexes of mixed Hodge modules with distinct strict support. ◻
:::

Let $\mathcal{P}^{\upzeta,\mathcal{S}}_{W,\upmu}\subset \mathop{\mathrm{Gr}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathfrak{P}}}}(\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{A}}}^{\upzeta,\mathcal{S}}_{W,\upmu}))$ be the subalgebra generated by $$\bigoplus_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}\in\Uplambda_{\upmu}^{\upzeta}}\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\mathop{\mathrm{B\mathbb{C}^*}})_{\mathop{\mathrm{vir}}}\otimes\mathop{\mathrm{BPS}}_{W,\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}^{\upzeta,\mathcal{S}}.$$ Since $\mathcal{P}^{\upzeta,\mathcal{S}}_{W,\upmu}$ is generated by primitive elements of the localised Hopf algebra, it follows from Lemma [\[PAB\]](#PAB){reference-type="ref" reference="PAB"} that the localised bialgebra structure on $\mathcal{P}^{\upzeta,\mathcal{S}}_{W,\upmu}$ lifts to an honest cocommutative bialgebra structure. Here, cocommutativity is with respect to the symmetrizing morphism ${}^{\uptau}\mathbf{sw}$ defined in Section [\[lc_signs\]](#lc_signs){reference-type="ref" reference="lc_signs"}. We denote by $\overline{\Delta}_{W,\upmu}^{\upzeta,\mathcal{S}}\colon \mathcal{P}^{\upzeta,\mathcal{S}}_{W,\upmu}\rightarrow \mathcal{P}^{\upzeta,\mathcal{S}}_{W,\upmu}\boxtimes_+ \mathcal{P}^{\upzeta,\mathcal{S}}_{W,\upmu}$ the induced coproduct.

::: corollary
[]{#trueHopf label="trueHopf"} Assume that $\upzeta$ is $\upmu$-generic. The triple $$\left(\mathcal{P}_{W,\upmu}^{\upzeta,\mathcal{S}},\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(\dim_*\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}(\mathop{\mathrm{\tilde{\ast}}}_{W,\upmu}^{\upzeta,\mathcal{S}})\right), \overline{\Delta}^{\upzeta,\mathcal{S}}_{W,\upmu}\right).$$ extends to a Hopf algebra.
:::

::: proof
*Proof.* All that is missing is a compatible antipode, but existence and uniqueness of an antipode is a formal consequence of connectedness of the algebra. ◻
:::

We define $\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}(\Upgamma_{W,\upmu}^{\upzeta})$ to be the composition of maps in the following diagram $$\xymatrix{
{}^{\uptau}\!\mathop{\mathrm{Sym}}_{\boxtimes_{\oplus}}(\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\mathop{\mathrm{B\mathbb{C}^*}})_{\mathop{\mathrm{vir}}}\otimes\mathcal{BPS}_{W,\upmu}^{\upzeta})\ar@{^(->}[r]& \mathop{\mathrm{Free}}_{\boxtimes_{\oplus}}(\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\mathop{\mathrm{B\mathbb{C}^*}})_{\mathop{\mathrm{vir}}}\otimes\mathcal{BPS}_{W,\upmu}^{\upzeta})\ar[dl]^-{\;\;\;\mathop{\mathrm{Free}}_{\boxtimes_{\oplus}}(\Upsilon_{W,\upmu}^{\upzeta})}\\
 \mathop{\mathrm{Free}}_{\boxtimes_{\oplus}}\left(\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}(\mathcal{A}_{W,\upmu}^{\upzeta})\right)\ar[r]&\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(\mathcal{A}_{W,\upmu}^{\upzeta}\right),
}$$ where the upper horizontal arrow comes from the natural inclusion $${}^{\uptau}\!\mathop{\mathrm{Sym}}^n_{\boxtimes_{\oplus}}(\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\mathop{\mathrm{B\mathbb{C}^*}})_{\mathop{\mathrm{vir}}}\otimes \mathcal{BPS}_{W,\upmu}^{\upzeta})\subset \underbrace{\left(\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\mathop{\mathrm{B\mathbb{C}^*}})_{\mathop{\mathrm{vir}}}\otimes\mathcal{BPS}_{W,\upmu}^{\upzeta}\right)\boxtimes_{\oplus}\ldots\boxtimes_{\oplus}\left(\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\mathop{\mathrm{B\mathbb{C}^*}})_{\mathop{\mathrm{vir}}}\otimes\mathcal{BPS}_{W,\upmu}^{\upzeta}\right)}_{n \;\textrm{times}},$$ the lower horizontal arrow is given by iterated relative CoHA multiplication, and $\Upsilon_{W,\upmu}^{\upzeta}$ is as in ([\[caninc\]](#caninc){reference-type="ref" reference="caninc"}). The relative version of Theorem [\[qea\]](#qea){reference-type="ref" reference="qea"} amounts to the following theorem.

::: theorem
[]{#zPBW label="zPBW"} The map $\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}(\Upgamma_{W,\upmu}^{\upzeta})$ is an isomorphism.
:::

::: proof
*Proof.* It is enough to show that $\Xi=\mathbb{D}^{\mathop{\mathrm{mon}}}_{\mathcal{M}_{\upmu}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}}\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}(\Upgamma^{\upzeta}_{W,\upmu})$ is an isomorphism, and for this it is enough to show that $\Xi|_{x}$ is an isomorphism for each $x$ a point in $\mathcal{M}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}$, with $\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}\in\Uplambda^{\upzeta}_{\upmu}$, by Lemma [\[Tech1\]](#Tech1){reference-type="ref" reference="Tech1"}. Let $x$ represent the polystable $\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}$-dimensional $\mathbb{C}Q$-representation $$\bigoplus_i V_i\otimes_{\mathbb{C}} \uprho_i$$ where the $\uprho_i$ are pairwise distinct $\upzeta$-stable $\mathbb{C}Q$-representations of slope $\upmu$, and $V_i$ are finite-dimensional complex vector spaces. Let $\mathfrak{M}^{x}\subset\mathfrak{M}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}_{\upmu}$ be the reduced substack, of which the closed points represent $\mathbb{C}Q$-representations that are objects of the Serre subcategory for which the objects are all of the iterated extensions of the representations $\uprho_i$. Let $\mathcal{M}^{x}\subset \mathcal{M}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}_{\upmu}$ be the discrete subscheme for which the points represent finite direct sums of the $\uprho_i$. Then $$\mathfrak{M}^{x}\cap \mathfrak{M}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}\supset (p^{\upzeta}_{\upmu})^{-1}(x)$$ and $\mathcal{M}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}^x\subset \mathcal{M}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}$ is a discrete set of points containing $x$. So $\Xi\lvert_x$ is an isomorphism if and only if $\Xi\lvert_{{\mathcal{M}}^x_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}}$ is. By Theorem [\[ThmA\]](#ThmA){reference-type="ref" reference="ThmA"} and Proposition [\[underlying_same\]](#underlying_same){reference-type="ref" reference="underlying_same"}, there is an isomorphism of $\mathbb{N}^{Q_0}\oplus\mathbb{Z}$-graded vector spaces (the extra $\mathbb{Z}$-factor is the cohomological grading) $$\label{fout}
{}^{\uptau}\!\mathop{\mathrm{Sym}}_{\boxtimes_+}\left(\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\mathop{\mathrm{B\mathbb{C}^*}})_{\mathop{\mathrm{vir}}}\otimes \mathop{\mathrm{BPS}}^{\upzeta,x}_{W,\upmu}\right)\cong \mathop{\mathrm{H}}_c(\mathfrak{M}^x,\mathfrak{IC}_{W,\upmu}^{\upzeta})^{\vee}.$$ On the other hand, by Proposition [\[primitivity\]](#primitivity){reference-type="ref" reference="primitivity"} the subspaces $$\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\mathop{\mathrm{B\mathbb{C}^*}})_{\mathop{\mathrm{vir}}}\otimes \mathop{\mathrm{BPS}}^{\upzeta,x}_{W,\upmu}\subset \mathop{\mathrm{Gr}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathfrak{P}}}}\left(\mathop{\mathrm{H}}_c(\mathfrak{M}^x,\mathfrak{IC}_{W,\upmu}^{\upzeta})^{\vee}\right)$$ are primitive for the induced coproduct, so they form a Lie algebra under the commutator Lie bracket. Letting $\mathfrak{g}$ be the space of primitives for $\mathop{\mathrm{Gr}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathfrak{P}}}}\left(\mathop{\mathrm{H}}_c(\mathfrak{M}^x,\mathfrak{IC}_{W,\upmu}^{\upzeta})^{\vee}\right)$, and letting $U(\mathfrak{g})$ be the universal enveloping algebra of $\mathfrak{g}$,then by the Milnor--Moore theorem, there is an isomorphism $$U(\mathfrak{g})\cong \mathop{\mathrm{Gr}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathfrak{P}}}}\left(\mathop{\mathrm{H}}_c(\mathfrak{M}^x,\mathfrak{IC}_{W,\upmu}^{\upzeta})^{\vee}\right).$$ Setting $\upsilon=\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}(\dim_*\tilde{\omega}_{\upmu}^!\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}(\Upgamma_{W,\upmu}))$ there is a commutative diagram $$\xymatrix{
{}^{\uptau}\!\mathop{\mathrm{Sym}}_{\boxtimes_+}\left(\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\mathop{\mathrm{B\mathbb{C}^*}})_{\mathop{\mathrm{vir}}}\otimes \mathop{\mathrm{BPS}}^{\upzeta,x}_{W,\upmu}\right)\ar@{^{(}->}[d]\ar[r]^-{\cong}\ar[dr]^-{\upsilon} &U\left(\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\mathop{\mathrm{B\mathbb{C}^*}})_{\mathop{\mathrm{vir}}}\otimes \mathop{\mathrm{BPS}}^{\upzeta,x}_{W,\upmu}\right)\ar[d]
\\{}^{\uptau}\!\mathop{\mathrm{Sym}}_{\boxtimes_+}\left(\mathfrak{g}\right)\ar[r]^-{\cong}&\mathop{\mathrm{Gr}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathfrak{P}}}}\left(\mathop{\mathrm{H}}_c(\mathfrak{M}^x,\mathfrak{IC}_{W,\upmu}^{\upzeta})^{\vee}\right)
}$$ where the top and bottom horizontal arrows are the PBW isomorphism and the Milnor--Moore isomorphism, respectively. It follows that $\upsilon$ is injective, and hence an isomorphism, since the domain and the codomain have the same graded dimensions, by ([\[fout\]](#fout){reference-type="ref" reference="fout"}). The dual of the restriction of this map to the degree $\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}$ piece is $\Xi|_{{\mathcal{M}}^x_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}}$, which is thus an isomorphism, as required. ◻
:::

We construct the map $$\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\Upgamma_{W,\upmu}^{\upzeta,\mathcal{S}})\colon {}^{\uptau}\!\mathop{\mathrm{Sym}}_{\boxtimes_+}\left(\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\mathop{\mathrm{B\mathbb{C}^*}})_{\mathop{\mathrm{vir}}}\otimes\mathop{\mathrm{BPS}}^{\upzeta,\mathcal{S}}_{W,\upmu}\right)\rightarrow\mathop{\mathrm{H}}\left(\mathcal{A}^{\upzeta,\mathcal{S}}_{W,\upmu}\right)$$ in the same way as $\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}(\Upgamma_{W,\upmu}^{\upzeta})$, using Corollary [\[Inccor\]](#Inccor){reference-type="ref" reference="Inccor"} instead of Corollary [\[inccor\]](#inccor){reference-type="ref" reference="inccor"}. Then this map preserves the perverse filtration, and $$\mathop{\mathrm{Gr}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathfrak{P}}}}\left(\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\Upgamma_{W,\upmu}^{\upzeta,\mathcal{S}})\right)=\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}(\dim_*\tilde{\omega}^{\upzeta,!}_{\upmu}\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}(\Upgamma_{W,\upmu}^{\upzeta}))$$ is an isomorphism, so that $\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\Upgamma_{W,\upmu}^{\upzeta,\mathcal{S}})$ is at least an injection. The domain and target of this map are graded vector spaces of the same dimension (finite in every graded degree) by Theorem [\[ThmA\]](#ThmA){reference-type="ref" reference="ThmA"}. It follows that $\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\Upgamma_{W,\upmu}^{\upzeta,\mathcal{S}})$ is an isomorphism. This completes all but the free commutativity statements of Theorem [\[qea\]](#qea){reference-type="ref" reference="qea"}, which we now deduce as a corollary.

::: corollary
[]{#twSC label="twSC"} The (twisted) relative cohomological Hall algebra $\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}(\mathcal{A}^{\upzeta,\mathcal{S}}_{W,\upmu})$ is a free commutative algebra (for the symmetrizing morphism ${}^{\uptau}\!\textbf{sw}$), as is the algebra $\mathop{\mathrm{Gr}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathfrak{P}}}}\left(\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\mathcal{A}^{\upzeta,\mathcal{S}}_{W,\upmu})\right)$, and so the twisted algebra $\mathop{\mathrm{Gr}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathfrak{P}}}}\left(\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(^{\uppsi}\!\mathcal{A}^{\upzeta,\mathcal{S}}_{W,\upmu})\right)$ is a free supercommutative algebra (for the usual symmetrizing morphism $\textbf{sw}$).
:::

::: proof
*Proof.* The result regarding the relative cohomological Hall algebra follows from Theorem [\[zPBW\]](#zPBW){reference-type="ref" reference="zPBW"} and the claim that $\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}(\mathcal{A}^{\upzeta}_{W,\upmu})$ is commutative. This claim in turn can be checked at fibres as in the proof of Theorem [\[zPBW\]](#zPBW){reference-type="ref" reference="zPBW"}, this time using Lemma [\[Tech2\]](#Tech2){reference-type="ref" reference="Tech2"}, and so it is sufficient to prove commutativity for $\mathop{\mathrm{Gr}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathfrak{P}}}}\left(\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\mathcal{A}^{\upzeta,\mathcal{S}}_{W,\upmu})\right)$ (as in the same proof). Let $\mathcal{P}\subset \mathcal{K}=\mathop{\mathrm{Gr}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathfrak{P}}}}\left(\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\mathcal{A}^{\upzeta,\mathcal{S}}_{W,\upmu})\right)$ be the subspace of primitives. Then the algebra generated by $\mathcal{P}$ is a cocommutative sub bialgebra of $\mathcal{K}$, and so there is a chain of inclusions $${}^{\uptau}\!\mathop{\mathrm{Sym}}\left(\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\mathop{\mathrm{B\mathbb{C}^*}})_{\mathop{\mathrm{vir}}}\otimes \mathop{\mathrm{BPS}}^{\upzeta,\mathcal{S}}_{W,\upmu}\right)\subset {}^{\uptau}\!\mathop{\mathrm{Sym}}(\mathcal{P})\subset \mathcal{K}$$ where the first inclusion is given by Proposition [\[primitivity\]](#primitivity){reference-type="ref" reference="primitivity"} and the second by the Milnor--Moore theorem. Since by Theorem [\[ThmA\]](#ThmA){reference-type="ref" reference="ThmA"} the first and last graded vector spaces have the same graded dimensions, all of the inclusions are equalities, and in particular $\mathcal{P}=\mathop{\mathrm{BPS}}^{\upzeta,\mathcal{S}}_{W,\upmu}\otimes\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\mathop{\mathrm{B\mathbb{C}^*}})_{\mathop{\mathrm{vir}}}$, and $\mathcal{K}$ is the universal enveloping algebra of $\mathcal{P}$, considered as a Lie sub-algebra of $\mathcal{K}$. On the other hand, the Lie bracket on $\mathcal{P}$ is zero, since it respects the perverse grading, and $\mathcal{P}$ is concentrated entirely in odd perverse degree. The statement regarding the $\uppsi$-twisted algebra then follows. ◻
:::

Finally, we are in a position to define the BPS Lie algebra:

::: corollary
Let $\uppsi$ be as in ([\[pcocdef\]](#pcocdef){reference-type="ref" reference="pcocdef"}). The subspace $$\mathbb{L}^{1/2}\otimes \mathop{\mathrm{BPS}}^{\upzeta,\mathcal{S}}_{W,\upmu}=\mathop{\mathrm{\mathfrak{P}}}_{\leq 1}\left(\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\mathcal{A}^{\upzeta,\mathcal{S}}_{W,\upmu})\right)\subset \mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\mathcal{A}^{\upzeta,\mathcal{S}}_{W,\upmu})$$ is closed under the commutator Lie bracket in $\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(^{\uppsi}\!\mathcal{A}^{\upzeta,\mathcal{S}}_{W,\upmu})$, and so carries the structure of a Lie algebra, called the **BPS Lie algebra**.
:::

The proof is an immediate consequence of Corollary [\[twSC\]](#twSC){reference-type="ref" reference="twSC"}, as explained in Section [\[BPSLA\]](#BPSLA){reference-type="ref" reference="BPSLA"}.

## Proof of Theorem [\[strongPBW\]](#strongPBW){reference-type="ref" reference="strongPBW"} {#CWCF}

We finish by proving Theorem [\[strongPBW\]](#strongPBW){reference-type="ref" reference="strongPBW"}. Fix a generic stability condition $\upzeta$. By Corollary [\[inccor\]](#inccor){reference-type="ref" reference="inccor"} there are canonical split inclusions $$\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\mathop{\mathrm{B\mathbb{C}^*}})_{\mathop{\mathrm{vir}}}\otimes \mathcal{BPS}^{\upzeta}_{W,\upmu}\rightarrow \mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(p_{\upmu,*}^{\upzeta}\mathfrak{IC}_{W,\upmu}^{\upzeta}\right)$$ giving rise to canonical split inclusions $\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\mathop{\mathrm{B\mathbb{C}^*}})_{\mathop{\mathrm{vir}}}\otimes q^{\upzeta}_{\upmu,*}\mathcal{BPS}^{\upzeta}_{W,\upmu}\rightarrow q_{\upmu,*}^{\upzeta}\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(p^{\upzeta}_{\upmu,*}\mathfrak{IC}_{W,\upmu}^{\upzeta}\right)$ and thus split inclusions $$\iota'_{\upmu}\colon \mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\mathop{\mathrm{B\mathbb{C}^*}})_{\mathop{\mathrm{vir}}}\otimes q^{\upzeta}_{\upmu,*}\mathcal{BPS}_{W,\upmu}^{\upzeta}\hookrightarrow \mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(q^{\upzeta}_{\upmu,*}p^{\upzeta}_{\upmu,*}\mathfrak{IC}^{\upzeta}_{W,\upmu}\right)$$ by the decomposition theorem and properness of $q^{\upzeta}_{\upmu}$. Via the proof of Theorem [\[gcDT\]](#gcDT){reference-type="ref" reference="gcDT"} we can pick a right inverse $\upeta_{\upmu,*}$ to the restriction map in cohomology $$\upeta_{\upmu}^*\colon\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(p_{\upmu,*}\mathfrak{IC}_{W,\upmu}\right)\rightarrow\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(q^{\upzeta}_{\upmu,*}p^{\upzeta}_{\upmu,*}\mathfrak{IC}^{\upzeta}_{W,\upmu}\right)$$ for each $\upmu$. We set $$\iota_{\upmu}=\upeta_{\upmu,*}\iota'_{\upmu}\colon \mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\mathop{\mathrm{B\mathbb{C}^*}})_{\mathop{\mathrm{vir}}}\otimes q^{\upzeta}_{\upmu,*}\mathcal{BPS}_{W,\upmu}^{\upzeta} \rightarrow \mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(p_{\upmu,*}\mathfrak{IC}_{W,\upmu}\right).$$

::: theorem
[]{#sPBW label="sPBW"} Let $\upzeta$ be a generic stability condition, and consider the morphism $$\label{finalPBW}
\boxtimes_{\oplus,\infty \xrightarrow{\upmu}-\infty}^{\mathop{\mathrm{tw}}} \left({}^{(\uptau)}\!\mathop{\mathrm{Sym}}_{\boxtimes_{\oplus}}\left(\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\mathop{\mathrm{B\mathbb{C}^*}})_{\mathop{\mathrm{vir}}}\otimes q_{\upmu,*}^{\upzeta}\mathcal{BPS}_{W,\upmu}^{\upzeta}\right)\right)\xrightarrow{^{(\neg\uppsi)}\! \mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}(\mathop{\mathrm{\tilde{\ast}}}_W)} \mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(p_*\mathfrak{IC}_W\right)$$ induced by the inclusions $\iota_{\upmu}$ and the Hall algebra structure on $\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}({}^{(\uppsi)}\!\!\mathcal{A}_W)$, where the bracketed superscripts indicate that if we are not taking the symmetrizing morphism ${}^{\uptau}\mathbf{tw}$ to define the functor ${}^{\uptau}\!\mathop{\mathrm{Sym}}_{\boxtimes_{\oplus}}$, then we take the $\uppsi$-twisted multiplication, for $\uppsi$ a bilinear form as in ([\[pcocdef\]](#pcocdef){reference-type="ref" reference="pcocdef"}). Then ([\[finalPBW\]](#finalPBW){reference-type="ref" reference="finalPBW"}) is an isomorphism.
:::

::: proof
*Proof.* We consider dual compactly supported cohomology, to match Theorem [\[gcDT\]](#gcDT){reference-type="ref" reference="gcDT"}. In addition, we will assume that $W=0$ to ease the notation a little --- applying the vanishing cycles functor to all of our arguments yields the general case by Lemma [\[cohacom\]](#cohacom){reference-type="ref" reference="cohacom"}.

Let $\overline{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}\in\mathop{\mathrm{HN}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}$ be a Harder--Narasimhan type. Extending the notation $\mathfrak{M}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}$, we let $\mathfrak{M}_{\overline{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}}$ be the stack of all $\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}$-dimensional modules equipped with a filtration by submodules with the dimensions of successive subquotients given by $\overline{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}$. We define $$s_{\overline{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}}\colon\mathfrak{M}_{\overline{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}}\rightarrow \mathfrak{M}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}$$ to be the map of stacks given by forgetting the filtration. In common with the map $s_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}$ of diagram ([\[smap\]](#smap){reference-type="ref" reference="smap"}), which corresponds to the special case $\overline{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}=(\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'')$, the map $s_{\overline{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}}$ factors as a proper surjection and a closed inclusion, and is therefore proper.

The Harder--Narasimhan type of the underlying $\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}$-dimensional module of a filtered module parametrised by a point of $\mathfrak{M}_{\overline{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}}$ need not be $\overline{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}$. On the other hand, by the proof of [@Reineke_HN Prop.3.7], the proper map $s_{\overline{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}}$ factors through the closed inclusion of stacks $$i_{\leq \overline{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}}\colon\mathfrak{M}^{\upzeta}_{\leq\overline{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}}\hookrightarrow \mathfrak{M}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}.$$

By abuse of notation, for each dimension vector $\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}$ we continue to denote by $\upeta_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}},*}$ the right inverse to the map $$\mathbb{D}^{\mathop{\mathrm{mon}}}_{\mathcal{M}}\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(p_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}},!}\mathcal{IC}_{\mathfrak{M}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}}(\mathbb{Q})\right)\rightarrow \mathbb{D}^{\mathop{\mathrm{mon}}}_{\mathcal{M}}\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(q_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}},!}^{\upzeta}p_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}},!}^{\upzeta}\mathcal{IC}_{\mathfrak{M}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}}(\mathbb{Q})\right)$$ defined by the map already denoted $\upeta_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}},*}$. Given a stack $\mathfrak{N}\xrightarrow{r} \mathcal{M}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}$ over $\mathcal{M}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}$, we denote by $\mathcal{P}(\mathfrak{N})=\mathbb{D}^{\mathop{\mathrm{mon}}}_{\mathcal{M}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}}\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}(r_!\mathbb{Q}_{\mathfrak{N}})$ the induced complex of mixed Hodge modules on $\mathcal{M}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}$. Consider the commutative diagram $$\xymatrix{
&\mathfrak{M}_{\leq \overline{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}}^{\upzeta}\ar[dr]^{i_{\leq\overline{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}}}\\
\mathfrak{M}_{\overline{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}}^{\upzeta}\ar[d]_{r_{\overline{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}}^{\upzeta}}\ar[ur]^{\upbeta}\ar[r]^{\upgamma} & \mathfrak{M}_{\overline{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}} \ar[u]^{\upalpha}\ar[r]_{s_{\overline{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}}} \ar[d]_{r_{\overline{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}}}& \mathfrak{M}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}\\
\mathfrak{M}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^1}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}\times\ldots\times\mathfrak{M}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^s}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}\ar[r]^{\updelta} & \mathfrak{M}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^1}\times\ldots\times\mathfrak{M}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^s}
}$$ where the map $\upalpha$ exists due to the comments above.

We consider the diagram of complexes of mixed Hodge modules $$\xymatrix{
&\mathcal{P}(\mathfrak{M}_{\leq \overline{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}}^{\upzeta})\ar[dl]^{\upbeta^*}\ar[dr]^{i_{\leq\overline{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}},*}}\\
\mathcal{P}(\mathfrak{M}_{\overline{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}}^{\upzeta})\ar@/^/@{.>}[ur]^{\upbeta_*} \ar[d]_{r^{\upzeta}_{\overline{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}},*}}^{\cong} \ar@/_/@{.>}[r]_{\upgamma_*}&\ar[u]^{\upalpha_*} \ar[l]_{\upgamma^*}\mathcal{P}(\mathfrak{M}_{\overline{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}})\ar[r]_{s_{\overline{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}},*}}\ar[d]_{r_{\overline{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}},*}}^{\cong} & \mathcal{P}(\mathfrak{M}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}})\\
\mathbb{L}^{(\overline{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}},\overline{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}})}\otimes\mathcal{P}(\mathfrak{M}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^1}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}\times\ldots\times\mathfrak{M}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^s}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}) \ar@/_/@{.>}[r]_-{\updelta_*}& \mathbb{L}^{(\overline{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}},\overline{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}})}\otimes\mathcal{P}(\mathfrak{M}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^1}\times\ldots\times\mathfrak{M}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^s})\ar[l]_-{\updelta^*}
}$$ with $(\overline{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}},\overline{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}})$ defined as in ([\[dddotdef\]](#dddotdef){reference-type="ref" reference="dddotdef"}). The map $\updelta_*\colonequals \mathbb{L}^{(\overline{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}},\overline{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}})}\otimes\left(\oplus_*(\upeta_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^1,*}\boxtimes\ldots\boxtimes\upeta_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^s,*})\right)$ is a right inverse to the natural restriction map $\updelta^*$. Then we define $$\upgamma_*\colonequals r_{\overline{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}},*}^{-1}\updelta_*r^{\upzeta}_{\overline{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}},*},$$ a right inverse to $\upgamma^*$. Then set $\upbeta_*\colonequals \upalpha_*\upgamma_*$. It can easily be verified that $\upgamma^*=\upbeta^*\upalpha_*$, and so $\upbeta_*$ is a right inverse to $\upbeta^*$.

The map $\upeta_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^1,*}(\mathcal{H}(\mathcal{A}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^1}^{\upzeta}))\boxtimes_{\oplus}^{\mathop{\mathrm{tw}}}\ldots \boxtimes_{\oplus}^{\mathop{\mathrm{tw}}}\upeta_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^s,*}(\mathcal{H}(\mathcal{A}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^1}^{\upzeta}))\rightarrow \mathcal{H}(\mathcal{A}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}})$ has image in the piece $\mathbb{L}^{(\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}},\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}})/2}\otimes \mathcal{P}(\mathfrak{M}^{\upzeta}_{\leq\overline{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}})$ of the filtration of $\mathcal{H}(p_*\mathfrak{IC}_0)$ by Harder--Narasimhan types. By the commutativity of the above diagram, the resulting map $$\upgamma^*\left(\upeta_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^1,*}(\mathcal{H}(\mathcal{A}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^1}^{\upzeta}))\boxtimes_{\oplus}^{\mathop{\mathrm{tw}}}\ldots \boxtimes_{\oplus}^{\mathop{\mathrm{tw}}}\upeta_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^s,*}(\mathcal{H}(\mathcal{A}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^1}^{\upzeta}))\right)\rightarrow  \mathbb{L}^{(\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}},\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}})/2}\otimes\upbeta^*\mathcal{P}(\mathfrak{M}^{\upzeta}_{\leq\overline{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}})$$ is an isomorphism. Filtering the domain and the target of the map $$\Psi\colon \boxtimes_{\oplus,\infty \xrightarrow{\upmu}-\infty}^{\mathop{\mathrm{tw}}}\left(\upeta_{\upmu,*}(\mathcal{H}(\mathcal{A}_{\upmu}^{\upzeta}))\right)\rightarrow \mathcal{H}(\mathcal{A})$$ by the filtration indexed by the Harder--Narasimhan filtration, it follows that the associated graded morphism is an isomorphism between pure complexes of mixed Hodge modules, and hence $\Psi$ is an injection, and so an isomorphism, since by Theorem [\[CWCT\]](#CWCT){reference-type="ref" reference="CWCT"} the domain and image are isomorphic.

Since $\upeta_{\upmu}^*$ is a morphism of monoids, the diagram $$\xymatrix{
\upeta_{\upmu}^* {}^{(\uptau)}\!\mathop{\mathrm{Sym}}_{\boxtimes_{\oplus}}\left(\upeta_{\upmu,*}\mathcal{BPS}_{\upmu}^{\upzeta}\otimes\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\mathop{\mathrm{B\mathbb{C}^*}})_{\mathop{\mathrm{vir}}}\right)\ar[d]^{\cong}\ar[r]& \upeta_{\upmu}^*\mathcal{H}({}^{(\neg\uppsi)}\!\!\mathcal{A}_{\upmu})\ar[d]^{\cong}\\
{}^{(\uptau)}\!\mathop{\mathrm{Sym}}_{\boxtimes_{\oplus}}\left(\mathcal{BPS}_{\upmu}^{\upzeta}\otimes\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\mathop{\mathrm{B\mathbb{C}^*}})_{\mathop{\mathrm{vir}}}\right)\ar[r]& \mathcal{H}({}^{(\neg\uppsi)}\!\!\mathcal{A}_{\upmu}^{\upzeta})
}$$ commutes, with the horizontal maps defined via the relative multiplications on $\mathcal{H}({}^{(\uppsi)}\!\!\mathcal{A}_{\upmu})$ and $\mathcal{H}({}^{(\uppsi)}\!\!\mathcal{A}_{\upmu}^{\upzeta})$ respectively. We deduce as above that the theorem is true after passing to the associated graded with respect to the Harder--Narasimhan filtration, and so is injective, and hence an isomorphism since the domain and target are isomorphic pure monodromic mixed Hodge modules by Theorems [\[ThmA\]](#ThmA){reference-type="ref" reference="ThmA"} and [\[CWCT\]](#CWCT){reference-type="ref" reference="CWCT"}. ◻

::: corollary
For a generic stability condition $\upzeta$ there exist embeddings $\mathop{\mathrm{BPS}}^{\upzeta,\mathcal{S}}_{W,\upmu}\otimes\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\mathop{\mathrm{B\mathbb{C}^*}})_{\mathop{\mathrm{vir}}}\subset \mathop{\mathrm{H}}({}^{(\uppsi)}\!\!\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{A}}}_{W})$ such that the induced morphism $$\boxtimes_{\oplus,\infty \xrightarrow{\upmu}-\infty}^{\mathop{\mathrm{tw}}}\left({}^{(\uptau)}\!\mathop{\mathrm{Sym}}_{\boxtimes_+}\left(\mathop{\mathrm{BPS}}^{\upzeta,\mathcal{S}}_{W,\upmu}\otimes\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\mathop{\mathrm{B\mathbb{C}^*}})_{\mathop{\mathrm{vir}}}\right)\right)\xrightarrow{{}^{(\neg\uppsi)}\!\!\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\mathop{\mathrm{\tilde{\ast}}}_W)}\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{A}}}^{\mathcal{S}}_W)$$ is an isomorphism.

The meaning of the bracketed superscripts is as in Theorem [\[sPBW\]](#sPBW){reference-type="ref" reference="sPBW"}.

::: proof
*Proof.* By Theorem [\[sPBW\]](#sPBW){reference-type="ref" reference="sPBW"} and Proposition [\[PCoha\]](#PCoha){reference-type="ref" reference="PCoha"}, the corollary is true after replacing $\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{A}}}^{\mathcal{S}}_W)$ with the perverse associated graded $\mathop{\mathrm{Gr}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathfrak{P}}}}(\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{A}}}^{\mathcal{S}}_{W}))=\mathop{\mathrm{H}}_c(\mathcal{M}^{\mathcal{S}},\dim_!\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}(p_*\mathfrak{IC}_W))$. Then for any lift of the embedding $\mathop{\mathrm{BPS}}^{\upzeta,\mathcal{S}}_{W,\upmu}\otimes\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\mathop{\mathrm{B\mathbb{C}^*}})_{\mathop{\mathrm{vir}}}\subset \mathop{\mathrm{Gr}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathfrak{P}}}}(\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{A}}}^{\mathcal{S}}_{W}))$ to an embedding $\mathop{\mathrm{BPS}}^{\upzeta,\mathcal{S}}_{W,\upmu}\otimes\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\mathop{\mathrm{B\mathbb{C}^*}})_{\mathop{\mathrm{vir}}}\subset \mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{A}}}^{\mathcal{S}}_{W})$, the result follows. ◻
