Assume that $\upzeta$ is a $\upmu$-generic Bridgeland stability condition. As in [@DaMe4] and the introduction, for $\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}\in\Uplambda_{\upmu}^{\upzeta}\setminus \{0\}$ we define the following elements of $\mathop{\mathrm{MMHM}}(\mathcal{M}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}})$ and $\mathcal{D}^{b}(\mathop{\mathrm{MMHM}}(\mathop{\mathrm{pt}}))$, respectively $$\begin{aligned}
\mathcal{BPS}_{W,\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}^{\upzeta}\colonequals&\begin{cases} \upphi^{\mathop{\mathrm{mon}}}_{\mathcal{T}r(W)^\upzeta_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}}\mathcal{IC}_{\mathcal{M}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}}(\mathbb{Q})&\textrm{if }\mathcal{M}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-st}}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}\neq\emptyset ,\\0&\textrm{otherwise}\end{cases}\\
\mathop{\mathrm{BPS}}^{\upzeta,\mathcal{S}}_{W,\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}\colonequals &\mathop{\mathrm{H}}_c\left(\mathcal{M}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}},\mathcal{S}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}},\mathcal{BPS}_{W,\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}^{\upzeta}\right)^{\vee}.
\end{aligned}$$

::: remark
Note that from our shift convention on $\mathcal{IC}_{\mathcal{M}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}}(\mathbb{Q})$, along with exactness of $\upphi^{\mathop{\mathrm{mon}}}_{\mathcal{T}r(W)^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}}$ it follows that $\mathcal{BPS}_{W,\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}^{\upzeta}$ is indeed a genuine (monodromic) mixed Hodge module, instead of merely being an element of $\mathcal{D}^{b}(\mathop{\mathrm{MMHM}}(\mathcal{M}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}))$. One can find examples in which it is not pure. For example consider the three loop quiver $Q$ and the potential $W=x^p+y^q+z^r+axyz$, with $a\neq 0$ and $p^{-1}+q^{-1}+r^{-1}<1$. Then $\mathcal{M}_1=\mathbb{A}^3$, and on $\mathcal{M}_1$ there is an identity $\mathrm{Tr}(W)=W$, and the potential has an isolated singularity at the origin. The cohomology $\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}(\upphi^{\mathop{\mathrm{mon}}}_{W}\mathbb{Q}_{\mathbb{A}^3})$ is not pure (see [@DMSS15 Rem.3.5] [@Kul98 Ex.7.3.5] [@Sch85 Ex.9.1]), and so $$\mathcal{BPS}_{W,1}\colonequals \mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}(\upphi^{\mathop{\mathrm{mon}}}_{\mathcal{T}r(W)_{1}}\mathbb{L}^{-3/2}_{\mathcal{M}_1})$$ is not pure either.
:::

::: remark
[]{#sdrem label="sdrem"} By Proposition [\[basicfacts\]](#basicfacts){reference-type="ref" reference="basicfacts"} there are isomorphisms $$\begin{aligned}
\mathbb{D}^{\mathop{\mathrm{mon}}}_{\mathcal{M}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}}\upphi^{\mathop{\mathrm{mon}}}_{\mathcal{T}r(W)^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}}\mathcal{IC}_{\mathcal{M}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}}(\mathbb{Q})\cong& \upphi^{\mathop{\mathrm{mon}}}_{\mathcal{T}r(W)^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}}\mathbb{D}_{\mathcal{M}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}}\mathcal{IC}_{\mathcal{M}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}}(\mathbb{Q})\\
\cong&\upphi^{\mathop{\mathrm{mon}}}_{\mathcal{T}r(W)^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}}\mathcal{IC}_{\mathcal{M}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}}(\mathbb{Q})
\end{aligned}$$ and so $$\label{sdual}
\mathbb{D}^{\mathop{\mathrm{mon}}}_{\mathcal{M}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}}\mathcal{BPS}_{W,\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}^{\upzeta}\cong\mathcal{BPS}_{W,\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}^{\upzeta}.$$
:::

::: remark
Recall that in the special case $\mathfrak{M}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}},\mathcal{S}}=\mathfrak{M}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}$, we omit the superscript $\mathcal{S}$ from all notation. By Remark [\[sdrem\]](#sdrem){reference-type="ref" reference="sdrem"}, the definition of $\mathop{\mathrm{BPS}}^{\upzeta}_{W,\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}$ can be simplified via the isomorphism $$\mathop{\mathrm{BPS}}^{\upzeta}_{W,\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}\colonequals \mathop{\mathrm{H}}_c\left(\mathcal{M}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}},\mathcal{BPS}_{W,\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}^{\upzeta}\right)^{\vee}\cong \mathop{\mathrm{H}}\left(\mathcal{M}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}},\mathcal{BPS}_{W,\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}^{\upzeta}\right).$$
:::

::: definition
[]{#abbrev label="abbrev"} We make the abbreviation of symbols $$\mathfrak{IC}_{W,\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}^{\upzeta}\colonequals \upphi^{\mathop{\mathrm{mon}}}_{\mathfrak{Tr}(W)^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}}\mathcal{IC}_{\mathfrak{M}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}}(\mathbb{Q})$$ and $$\mathfrak{IC}_{W,\upmu}^{\upzeta}\colonequals \bigoplus_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}\in\Uplambda_{\upmu}^{\upzeta}}\upphi^{\mathop{\mathrm{mon}}}_{\mathfrak{Tr}(W)^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}}\mathcal{IC}_{\mathfrak{M}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}}(\mathbb{Q}).$$
:::

::: theorem
[]{#weakPBW label="weakPBW"} Assume that $\upzeta$ is a $\upmu$-generic stability condition on the quiver $Q$. There is an isomorphism in $\mathcal{D}^{\geq, \mathrm{lf}}(\mathop{\mathrm{MMHM}}(\mathcal{M}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}_{\upmu}))$ $$\label{weakeq}
\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}(p^{\upzeta}_{\upmu,*}\mathfrak{IC}_{W,\upmu}^{\upzeta})\cong\mathop{\mathrm{Sym}}_{\boxtimes_{\oplus}}\left(\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\mathop{\mathrm{B\mathbb{C}^*}})_{\mathop{\mathrm{vir}}}\otimes\mathcal{BPS}_{W,\upmu}^{\upzeta}\right)$$ and an isomorphism in $\mathcal{D}^{\geq, \mathrm{lf}}(\mathop{\mathrm{MMHM}}(\Uplambda_{\upmu}^{\upzeta}))$ $$\label{vweakeq}
\bigoplus_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}\in\Uplambda_{\upmu}^{\upzeta}}\mathop{\mathrm{H}}_c\left(\mathfrak{M}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}},\mathcal{S}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}},\mathfrak{IC}_{W,\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}^{\upzeta}\right)^{\vee}\cong\mathop{\mathrm{Sym}}_{\boxtimes_{+}}\left(\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\mathop{\mathrm{B\mathbb{C}^*}})_{\mathop{\mathrm{vir}}}\otimes\mathop{\mathrm{BPS}}^{\upzeta,\mathcal{S}}_{W,\upmu}\right).$$
:::

::: proof
*Proof.* We start by proving that there is an isomorphism as in ([\[weakeq\]](#weakeq){reference-type="ref" reference="weakeq"}). By definition, the left hand side of ([\[weakeq\]](#weakeq){reference-type="ref" reference="weakeq"}) is isomorphic to its total cohomology. On the other hand, $\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\mathop{\mathrm{B\mathbb{C}^*}})_{\mathop{\mathrm{vir}}}$ is also isomorphic to its total cohomology, and $\mathcal{BPS}_{W,\upmu}^{\upzeta}$ is an object of $\mathop{\mathrm{MMHM}}(\mathcal{M}_{\upmu}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}})\subset \mathcal{D}(\mathop{\mathrm{MMHM}}(\mathcal{M}_{\upmu}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}))$, and is trivially isomorphic to its total cohomology. It follows from the exactness of $\boxtimes_{\oplus}$ (Proposition [\[biExp\]](#biExp){reference-type="ref" reference="biExp"}) that the right hand side of ([\[weakeq\]](#weakeq){reference-type="ref" reference="weakeq"}) is also isomorphic to its total cohomology, and so it is sufficient to construct the isomorphism ([\[weakeq\]](#weakeq){reference-type="ref" reference="weakeq"}) at each cohomological degree. We first show the result, under the assumption that $W=0$. That is, we show that $$\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}(p^{\upzeta}_{\upmu,*}\mathcal{IC}_{\mathfrak{M}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}_{\upmu}}(\mathbb{Q}))\cong\mathop{\mathrm{Sym}}_{\boxtimes_{\oplus}}(\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\mathop{\mathrm{B\mathbb{C}^*}})_{\mathop{\mathrm{vir}}}\otimes\mathcal{BPS}_{W=0,\upmu}^{\upzeta}).$$ For this special case, the idea of the proof is to use the purity of all relevant complexes of monodromic mixed Hodge modules to upgrade the results of [@Meinhardt14], from equalities in a Grothendieck ring, to isomorphisms. By Lemma [\[appLem\]](#appLem){reference-type="ref" reference="appLem"}, for fixed $n\in\mathbb{Z}$ and $\mathbf{f}\gg 0$ the map $$\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}^n\left(\uppi\hspace{0.05em}_{\mathbf{f},\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}},*}^{\upzeta}\mathbb{L}^{(\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}},\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}})/2}_{\mathcal{M}_{\mathbf{f},\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}^{\upzeta}}\right)\rightarrow\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}^n\left(p^{\upzeta}_{\upmu,*}\mathfrak{IC}_{0,\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}^{\upzeta}\right)$$ is an isomorphism. It follows that $\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}(p^{\upzeta}_{\upmu,*}\mathfrak{IC}_{0,\upmu}^{\upzeta})$ is pure, since $\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}^n(\uppi\hspace{0.05em}_{\mathbf{f},\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}},*}^{\upzeta}\mathbb{L}^{(\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}},\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}})/2}_{\mathcal{M}_{\mathbf{f},\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}^{\upzeta}})$ is a pure mixed Hodge module of weight $n$, as purity is preserved by direct image along proper maps. It follows that $\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}(p^{\upzeta}_{\upmu,*}\mathfrak{IC}^{\upzeta}_{0,\upmu})$ is locally finite in the sense of Definition [\[lfDef\]](#lfDef){reference-type="ref" reference="lfDef"}, as is $\mathop{\mathrm{Sym}}_{\boxtimes_{\oplus}}(\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\mathop{\mathrm{B\mathbb{C}^*}})_{\mathop{\mathrm{vir}}}\otimes\mathcal{BPS}_{0,\upmu}^{\upzeta})$, by Lemma [\[lfRem\]](#lfRem){reference-type="ref" reference="lfRem"}, since $$\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\mathop{\mathrm{B\mathbb{C}^*}})_{\mathop{\mathrm{vir}}}\otimes\mathcal{BPS}_{0,\upmu}^{\upzeta}\in\mathcal{D}^{\geq, \mathrm{lf}}(\mathop{\mathrm{MMHM}}(\mathcal{M}_{\upmu}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}\setminus\mathcal{M}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}_0)).$$ The element $\mathop{\mathrm{Sym}}_{\boxtimes_{\oplus}}(\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\mathop{\mathrm{B\mathbb{C}^*}})_{\mathop{\mathrm{vir}}}\otimes \mathcal{BPS}_{0,\upmu}^{\upzeta})$ is also a pure locally finite complex of monodromic mixed Hodge modules, by Proposition [\[symmPure\]](#symmPure){reference-type="ref" reference="symmPure"} and Lemma [\[lfRem\]](#lfRem){reference-type="ref" reference="lfRem"}, and so both $\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}(p^{\upzeta}_{\upmu,*}\mathcal{IC}_{\mathfrak{M}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}_{\upmu}}(\mathbb{Q}))$ and $\mathop{\mathrm{Sym}}_{\boxtimes_{\oplus}}(\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\mathop{\mathrm{B\mathbb{C}^*}})_{\mathop{\mathrm{vir}}}\otimes\mathcal{BPS}_{0,\upmu}^{\upzeta})$ are direct sums of simple pure mixed Hodge modules, and are isomorphic if and only if they have the same class in $\mathop{\mathrm{K}}_0(\mathcal{D}^{\geq, \mathrm{lf}}(\mathop{\mathrm{MMHM}}(\mathcal{M}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}_{\upmu})))$.

Inside $\mathop{\mathrm{K}}_0(\mathop{\mathrm{MMHM}}(\mathcal{M}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}))$ consider the subgroup $\mathcal{I}_i$ generated by classes of monodromic mixed Hodge modules of weight greater than $i$. Then we identify each $\mathop{\mathrm{K}}_0(\mathcal{D}^{\geq, \mathrm{lf}}(\mathop{\mathrm{MMHM}}(\mathcal{M}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}})))$ with the completion of $\mathop{\mathrm{K}}_0(\mathop{\mathrm{MMHM}}(\mathcal{M}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}))$ with respect to $\mathcal{I}_i$, allowing us to make sense of the limits below. For an object $\mathcal{F}\in \mathcal{D}^{\geq, \mathrm{lf}}(\mathop{\mathrm{MMHM}}(\mathcal{M}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}_{\upmu}))$ we let $[\mathcal{F}]_{\mathop{\mathrm{K}}_0}$ denote the corresponding class in the Grothendieck group of $\mathcal{D}^{\geq, \mathrm{lf}}(\mathop{\mathrm{MMHM}}(\mathcal{M}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}_{\upmu}))$. From the fact that the map $\Phi^n_{0,\mathbf{f},\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}$ of equation ([\[PhiBar\]](#PhiBar){reference-type="ref" reference="PhiBar"}) is an isomorphism for fixed $n$ and $\mathbf{f}\gg 0$ we deduce that $$\begin{aligned}
\left[\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(p^{\upzeta}_{\upmu,*}\mathcal{IC}_{\mathfrak{M}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}_{\upmu}}(\mathbb{Q})\right)\right]_{\mathop{\mathrm{K}}_0}=&\lim_{\mathbf{f}\mapsto \infty}[\bigoplus_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}\in\Uplambda_{\upmu}^{\upzeta}}\mathbb{L}^{\mathbf{f}\cdot \mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}/2}\otimes\uppi\hspace{0.05em}_{\mathbf{f},\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}},*}^{\upzeta}\mathcal{IC}_{\mathcal{M}_{\mathbf{f},\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}}(\mathbb{Q})]_{\mathop{\mathrm{K}}_0}\\=&\lim_{\mathbf{f}\mapsto\infty}\left[\mathop{\mathrm{Sym}}_{\boxtimes_{\oplus}}\left(\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\mathbb{P}^{\mathbf{f}\cdot \mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}-1})_{\mathop{\mathrm{vir}}}\otimes\mathbb{L}^{\mathbf{f}\cdot \mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}/2}\otimes \mathcal{BPS}_{0,\upmu}^{\upzeta}\right)\right]_{\mathop{\mathrm{K}}_0}\\=&\left[\mathop{\mathrm{Sym}}_{\boxtimes_{\oplus}}\left(\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\mathop{\mathrm{B\mathbb{C}^*}})_{\mathop{\mathrm{vir}}}\otimes \mathcal{BPS}^{\upzeta}_{0,\upmu}\right)\right]_{\mathop{\mathrm{K}}_0}
\end{aligned}$$ as required --- for the second equality we have used the main result of [@Meinhardt14]. For the case $W\neq 0$, by Proposition [\[TSeq\]](#TSeq){reference-type="ref" reference="TSeq"} we deduce the existence of isomorphisms $$\begin{aligned}
\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(p^{\upzeta}_{\upmu,*}\upphi^{\mathop{\mathrm{mon}}}_{\mathfrak{Tr}(W)^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}}\mathcal{IC}_{\mathfrak{M}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}_{\upmu}}(\mathbb{Q})\right)&\cong^{\upnu_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}}\upphi^{\mathop{\mathrm{mon}}}_{\mathcal{T}r(W)^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}}\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(p^{\upzeta}_{\upmu,*}\mathcal{IC}_{\mathfrak{M}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}_{\upmu}}(\mathbb{Q})\right)\\
&\cong \upphi^{\mathop{\mathrm{mon}}}_{\mathcal{T}r(W)^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}}\mathop{\mathrm{Sym}}_{\boxtimes_{\oplus}}\left(\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\mathop{\mathrm{B\mathbb{C}^*}})_{\mathop{\mathrm{vir}}}\otimes\mathcal{BPS}^{\upzeta}_{0,\upmu}\right)\\
&\cong \mathop{\mathrm{Sym}}_{\boxtimes_{\oplus}}\left(\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\mathop{\mathrm{B\mathbb{C}^*}})_{\mathop{\mathrm{vir}}}\otimes \upphi^{\mathop{\mathrm{mon}}}_{\mathcal{T}r(W)^{\upzeta}_{\upmu}}\mathcal{BPS}^{\upzeta}_{0,\upmu}\right)\\
&= \mathop{\mathrm{Sym}}_{\boxtimes_{\oplus}}\left(\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\mathop{\mathrm{B\mathbb{C}^*}})_{\mathop{\mathrm{vir}}}\otimes \mathcal{BPS}^{\upzeta}_{W,\upmu}\right)
\end{aligned}$$ where for the third isomorphism we have used that $\upphi^{\mathop{\mathrm{mon}}}_{\mathcal{T}r(W)_{\upmu}^{\upzeta}}$ is a symmetric monoidal functor (Proposition [\[TSeq\]](#TSeq){reference-type="ref" reference="TSeq"}). For the absolute case, we compose the following chain of isomorphisms, the first of which is Proposition [\[bcprop\]](#bcprop){reference-type="ref" reference="bcprop"}. $$\begin{aligned}
\bigoplus_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}\in\Uplambda_{\upmu}^{\upzeta}}\mathop{\mathrm{H}}_c\left(\mathfrak{M}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}},\mathcal{S}}_{\upmu},\mathfrak{IC}_{W,\upmu}^{\upzeta}\right)^{\vee}\cong&\bigoplus_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}\in\Uplambda_{\upmu}^{\upzeta}}\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(\dim_!\tilde{\omega}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}^{\upzeta,*}\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(p^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}},!}\mathfrak{IC}_{W,\upmu}^{\upzeta}\right)\right)^{\vee}\\
\cong&\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(\dim_*\tilde{\omega}_{\upmu}^{\upzeta,!}\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(p^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}},*}\mathfrak{IC}_{W,\upmu}^{\upzeta}\right)\right)\\
\cong&\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(\dim_*\tilde{\omega}_{\upmu}^{\upzeta,!}\mathop{\mathrm{Sym}}_{\boxtimes_{\oplus}}\left(\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\mathop{\mathrm{B\mathbb{C}^*}})_{\mathop{\mathrm{vir}}}\otimes \mathcal{BPS}_{W,\upmu}^{\upzeta}\right)\right)\\
\cong&\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(\mathop{\mathrm{Sym}}_{\boxtimes_+}\left(\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\mathop{\mathrm{B\mathbb{C}^*}})_{\mathop{\mathrm{vir}}}\otimes \dim_*\tilde{\omega}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}^{\upzeta,!}\mathcal{BPS}_{W,\upmu}^{\upzeta}\right)\right)\\
\cong&\mathop{\mathrm{Sym}}_{\boxtimes_+}\left(\bigoplus_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}\in\Uplambda_{\upmu}^{\upzeta}}\left(\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\mathop{\mathrm{B\mathbb{C}^*}})_{\mathop{\mathrm{vir}}}\otimes\mathop{\mathrm{H}}_c(\mathcal{M}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}\mathcal{S}},\mathcal{BPS}_{W,\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}^{\upzeta})^{\vee}\right)\right)
\end{aligned}$$ to construct the isomorphism ([\[vweakeq\]](#vweakeq){reference-type="ref" reference="vweakeq"}). ◻
:::

::: corollary
[]{#inccor label="inccor"} There is a canonical split inclusion $$\label{caninc}
\Upsilon_{W,\upmu}^{\upzeta}\colon\mathop{\mathrm{H}}(\mathop{\mathrm{B\mathbb{C}^*}})_{\mathop{\mathrm{vir}}}\otimes\mathcal{BPS}^{\upzeta}_{W,\upmu}\rightarrow \mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(p_{\upmu,*}^{\upzeta}\mathfrak{IC}_{W,\upmu}^{\upzeta}\right)$$
:::

::: proof
*Proof.* First we prove the corollary in the case $W=0$. In this case, the left hand side of ([\[caninc\]](#caninc){reference-type="ref" reference="caninc"}) is precisely the summand of the right hand side with strict supports equal to $\mathcal{M}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}$ for $\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}\in\Uplambda^{\upzeta}_{\upmu}$ by Theorem [\[weakPBW\]](#weakPBW){reference-type="ref" reference="weakPBW"}, and the result follows from the decomposition theorem. For the general case, we apply the functor $\upphi^{\mathop{\mathrm{mon}}}_{\mathcal{T}r(W)_{\upmu}^{\upzeta}}$ to the inclusion, and its left inverse, from the case $W=0$. ◻
:::
