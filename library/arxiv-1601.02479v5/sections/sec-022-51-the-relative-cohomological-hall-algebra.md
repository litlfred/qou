[]{#relc label="relc"} In this section we define the relative cohomological Hall algebra $\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}(\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{A}}}_{W,\upmu}^{\upzeta})$. For now we will not assume that the stability condition $\upzeta\in\mathbb{H}_+^{Q_0}$ is generic. The underlying cohomologically graded monodromic mixed Hodge module of $\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}(\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{A}}}_{W,\upmu}^{\upzeta})$ is $\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(p^{\upzeta}_{\upmu,*}\mathfrak{IC}_{W,\upmu}^{\upzeta}\right)$ from Definition [\[abbrev\]](#abbrev){reference-type="ref" reference="abbrev"} and Theorem [\[weakPBW\]](#weakPBW){reference-type="ref" reference="weakPBW"}. We will define morphisms $$\label{multint}
\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(\mathop{\mathrm{\tilde{\ast}}}_{W,\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}^{\upzeta}\right)\colon\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(p^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',*}\mathfrak{IC}_{W,\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'}^{\upzeta}\right)\boxtimes^{\mathop{\mathrm{tw}}}_{\oplus} \mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(p^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'',*}\mathfrak{IC}_{W,\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}^{\upzeta}\right)\rightarrow\\   \mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(p^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}},*} \mathfrak{IC}_{W,\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}^{\upzeta}\right)$$ for all $\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}=\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'+\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''$ with $\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''\in\Uplambda_{\upmu}^{\upzeta}$ satisfying the natural associativity condition for a monoid in the category $\mathcal{D}^{\geq, \mathrm{lf}}(\mathop{\mathrm{MMHM}}(\mathcal{M}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}_{\upmu}))$ with the twisted monoidal product $\boxtimes^{\mathop{\mathrm{tw}}}_{\oplus}$. The result is a *relative* version of the cohomological Hall algebra of Kontsevich and Soibelman [@KS2] in the sense explained in Section [\[abvsrel\]](#abvsrel){reference-type="ref" reference="abvsrel"}. We define ([\[multint\]](#multint){reference-type="ref" reference="multint"}) as the composition of two morphisms, the first of which is defined in terms of the commutative diagram $$\xymatrix{
&X^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}/\left(G_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'}\times G_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}\right)\ar[dl]_{r_1}\ar[dr]^{r_2}\\
\left(X_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}\times X_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}\right)/\left(G_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'}\times G_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}\right)\ar[ddr]^{p_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'}^{\upzeta}\times p_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}^{\upzeta}}\ar[d]^{\cong} &&X_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}/G_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}\ar[d]^{\cong}\ar[ddl]_{p_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}^{\upzeta}}\\
\mathfrak{M}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}\times\mathfrak{M}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}\ar[dr]&&\mathfrak{M}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}\ar[dl]\\
&\mathcal{M}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'}\times\mathcal{M}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}\ar[r]^-{\oplus}&\mathcal{M}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}.
}$$ We treat the vertical isomorphisms as identities in what follows. We consider the following composition of isomorphisms $$\begin{aligned}
&\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(p^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',*}\upphi^{\mathop{\mathrm{mon}}}_{\mathfrak{Tr}(W)^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'}}\mathbb{Q}_{\mathfrak{M}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}}\right)\boxtimes \mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(p^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'',*}\upphi^{\mathop{\mathrm{mon}}}_{\mathfrak{Tr}(W)^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}}\mathbb{Q}_{\mathfrak{M}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}}\right)\cong^{\mathtt{TS}}\\
&\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left((p^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'}\times p^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''})_*\upphi^{\mathop{\mathrm{mon}}}_{\mathfrak{Tr}(W)^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'}\boxplus\mathfrak{Tr}(W)^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}}\mathbb{Q}_{\mathfrak{M}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'}\times\mathfrak{M}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}}\right)\cong\\
& \mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(((p^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'}\times p^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''})\circ r_{1})_*\upphi^{\mathop{\mathrm{mon}}}_{\mathfrak{Tr}(W)^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'}\boxplus\mathfrak{Tr}(W)^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}\circ r_1}\mathbb{Q}_{X^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}/\left(G_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'}\times G_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}\right)}\right)\cong\\
&\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(p^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'',*}\upphi^{\mathop{\mathrm{mon}}}_{\mathfrak{Tr}(W)^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}}\mathbb{Q}_{\mathfrak{M}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}}\right)
\end{aligned}$$ to obtain the isomorphism $$\begin{aligned}
\upalpha^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}\colon&\oplus_*\left(\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(p^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',*}\upphi^{\mathop{\mathrm{mon}}}_{\mathfrak{Tr}(W)^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'}}\mathbb{Q}_{\mathfrak{M}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}}\right)\boxtimes \mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(p^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'',*}\upphi^{\mathop{\mathrm{mon}}}_{\mathfrak{Tr}(W)^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}}\mathbb{Q}_{\mathfrak{M}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}}\right)\right)\xrightarrow{\cong}\\& \oplus_*\left(\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(p^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'',*}\upphi^{\mathop{\mathrm{mon}}}_{\mathfrak{Tr}(W)^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}}\mathbb{Q}_{\mathfrak{M}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}}\right)\right).
\end{aligned}$$ Next consider the commutative diagram of stacks $$\label{smap}
\xymatrix{
\mathfrak{M}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}\ar[d]^{\cong}\ar[rr]^-{s^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}}&&\mathfrak{M}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}\ar[d]^{\cong}\\
X^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}/G_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}\ar[d]^{p^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}}\ar[r]^{\iota^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}}&X^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}/G_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}\ar[r]^{c^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}}&X^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}/G_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}\ar[d]^{p^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}}\\
\mathcal{M}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'}\times\mathcal{M}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}\ar[rr]^{\oplus}&&\mathcal{M}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}.
}$$ Note that $\iota_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}^\upzeta$ is a closed inclusion, while $c_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}^\upzeta$ is proper and representable, and so $s_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}^\upzeta$ is a proper and representable morphism of stacks.

We define $V_N=\prod_{i\in Q_0}\mathop{\mathrm{Hom}}(\mathbb{C}^N,\mathbb{C}^{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}_i})$, and $U_N=\prod_{i\in Q_0}\mathop{\mathrm{Hom}}^{\mathrm{surj}}(\mathbb{C}^N,\mathbb{C}^{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}_i})$. For $N\geq \max(\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}_i)$, the group $G_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}$ acts freely on $U_N$, as does $G_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}\subset G_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}$. We define $$\begin{aligned}
\label{XNdef}&X^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}},N}=X_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}\times_{G_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}}U_N\\
\label{XXNdef}&X^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'',N}=X_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}\times_{G_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}}U_{N},
\end{aligned}$$ and let $\mathrm{Tr}(W)^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}},N}$ and $\mathrm{Tr}(W)^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'',N}$ denote the functions induced by $\mathrm{Tr}(W)$ on $X^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}},N}$ and $X^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'',N}$ respectively.

Passing to the limit of the composition of morphisms $$\begin{aligned}
\label{preLim}
\upbeta^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'',N}\colon &\oplus_*\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(p^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'',N,*}\upphi^{\mathop{\mathrm{mon}}}_{\mathrm{Tr}(W)^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'',N}}\mathbb{Q}_{X_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'',N}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}}\right)\xrightarrow{\cong }
\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left((p^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}},N}\circ s^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'',N})_*\upphi^{\mathop{\mathrm{mon}}}_{\mathrm{Tr}(W)^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'',N}}\mathbb{Q}_{X_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'',N}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}}\right)\xrightarrow{\cong}\\ \nonumber
&\rightarrow \mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(p^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}},N,*}\upphi^{\mathop{\mathrm{mon}}}_{\mathrm{Tr}(W)^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}},N}}s^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'',N,*}\mathbb{Q}_{X^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'',N}}\right)\rightarrow \mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(p^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}},N,*}\upphi^{\mathop{\mathrm{mon}}}_{\mathrm{Tr}(W)^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}},N}}\mathbb{L}^{(\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'')}_{X_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}},N}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}}\right)
\end{aligned}$$ we obtain $$\begin{aligned}
\label{betadef}
\upbeta^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}\colon &\oplus_*\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(p^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'',*}\upphi^{\mathop{\mathrm{mon}}}_{\mathfrak{Tr}(W)^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}}\mathbb{Q}_{\mathfrak{M}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}}\right)\rightarrow \mathbb{L}^{(\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'')}\otimes \mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(p^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}},*}\upphi^{\mathop{\mathrm{mon}}}_{\mathfrak{Tr}(W)^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}}\mathbb{Q}_{\mathfrak{M}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}}\right).
\end{aligned}$$ Here we have used that $\oplus_*\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(p^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'',N,*}\upphi^{\mathop{\mathrm{mon}}}_{\mathrm{Tr}(W)^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'',N}}\mathbb{Q}_{X_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'',N}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}}(\mathbb{Q})\right)$ is naturally isomorphic to $\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(\oplus_*p^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'',N,*}\upphi^{\mathop{\mathrm{mon}}}_{\mathrm{Tr}(W)^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'',N}}\mathbb{Q}_{X_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'',N}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}}(\mathbb{Q})\right)$ since $\oplus_*$ is exact, as $\oplus$ is finite. We define the morphism $\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(\mathop{\mathrm{\tilde{\ast}}}_{W,\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}^{\upzeta}\right)$ to be the composition $$\begin{aligned}
&\mathbb{L}^{\langle\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'\rangle/2}\otimes\mathbb{L}^{\upchi(\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}')/2}\otimes\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(p^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',*}\upphi^{\mathop{\mathrm{mon}}}_{\mathfrak{Tr}(W)^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'}}\mathbb{Q}_{\mathfrak{M}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}}\right)\boxtimes \left(\mathbb{L}^{\upchi(\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'')/2}\otimes\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(p^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'',*}\upphi^{\mathop{\mathrm{mon}}}_{\mathfrak{Tr}(W)^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}}\mathbb{Q}_{\mathfrak{M}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}}\right)\right)\xrightarrow{\cong}\\
&\mathbb{L}^{\langle\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'\rangle/2}\otimes\mathbb{L}^{\upchi(\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}')/2}\otimes\mathbb{L}^{\upchi(\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'')/2}\otimes\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(p^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',*}\upphi^{\mathop{\mathrm{mon}}}_{\mathfrak{Tr}(W)^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'}}\mathbb{Q}_{\mathfrak{M}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}}\right)\boxtimes \mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(p^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'',*}\upphi^{\mathop{\mathrm{mon}}}_{\mathfrak{Tr}(W)^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}}\mathbb{Q}_{\mathfrak{M}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}}\right)\xrightarrow{=}\\
&\mathbb{L}^{\langle\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'\rangle/2+\upchi(\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}')/2+\upchi(\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'')/2}\otimes\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(p^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',*}\upphi^{\mathop{\mathrm{mon}}}_{\mathfrak{Tr}(W)^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'}}\mathbb{Q}_{\mathfrak{M}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}}\right)\boxtimes \mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(p^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'',*}\upphi^{\mathop{\mathrm{mon}}}_{\mathfrak{Tr}(W)^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}}\mathbb{Q}_{\mathfrak{M}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}}\right)\xrightarrow{\mathop{\mathrm{id}}\otimes (\upbeta_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}\circ \upalpha_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}})}\\
&\mathbb{L}^{\langle\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'\rangle/2+\upchi(\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}')/2+\upchi(\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'')/2}\otimes\mathbb{L}^{\upchi(\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'')}\otimes\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(p^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'+\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'',*}\upphi^{\mathop{\mathrm{mon}}}_{\mathfrak{Tr}(W)^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'+\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}}\mathbb{Q}_{\mathfrak{M}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'+\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}}\right)\xrightarrow{=}\\
&\mathbb{L}^{\upchi(\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'+\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'+\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'')/2}\otimes \mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(p^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'+\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'',*}\upphi^{\mathop{\mathrm{mon}}}_{\mathfrak{Tr}(W)^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'+\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}}\mathbb{Q}_{\mathfrak{M}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}'+\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}}\right).
\end{aligned}$$ We define $$\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(\mathop{\mathrm{\tilde{\ast}}}^{\upzeta}_{W,\upmu}\right)=\bigoplus_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''\in\Uplambda_{\upmu}^{\upzeta}}\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(\mathop{\mathrm{\tilde{\ast}}}_{W,\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}',\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}''}^{\upzeta}\right).$$ Let $\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^{(h)}$ for $h=1,2,3$ be a triple of dimension vectors, and set $$\mathbb{J}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{e}}}}\colonequals \mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(p^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{e}}},*}\upphi^{\mathop{\mathrm{mon}}}_{\mathfrak{Tr}(W)^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{e}}}}}\mathbb{Q}_{\mathfrak{M}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{e}}}}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}}\right).$$ By the standard argument (see [@KS2]) the diagram $$\begin{aligned}
\label{Icom}
\xymatrix{
\mathbb{J}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^{(1)}}\boxtimes_{\oplus}\mathbb{J}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^{(2)}}\boxtimes_{\oplus}\mathbb{J}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^{(3)}}\ar[d]\ar[dr]^{\kappa}\ar[r]&\mathbb{L}^{\upchi(\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^{(2)},\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^{(3)})}\otimes\mathbb{J}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^{(1)}}\boxtimes \mathbb{J}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^{(2)}+\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^{(3)}}\ar[d]
\\
\mathbb{L}^{\upchi(\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^{(1)},\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^{(2)})}\otimes \mathbb{J}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^{(1)}+\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^{(2)}}\boxtimes_{\oplus}\mathbb{J}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^{(3)}}\ar[r]&\mathbb{L}^{\upchi(\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^{(1)},\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^{(2)})+\upchi(\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^{(1)},\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^{(3)})+\upchi(\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^{(2)},\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^{(3)})}\otimes \mathbb{J}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^{(1)}+\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^{(2)}+\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^{(3)}}
}
\end{aligned}$$ commutes. It follows that we can describe both the maps $$\begin{aligned}
\label{mapone}
&\left(\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(p^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^{(1)},*}\mathfrak{IC}_{W,\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^{(1)}}^{\upzeta}\right)\boxtimes_{\oplus}^{\mathop{\mathrm{tw}}}\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(p^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^{(2)},*}\mathfrak{IC}_{W,\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^{(2)}}^{\upzeta}\right)\right)\boxtimes_{\oplus}^{\mathop{\mathrm{tw}}}\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(p^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^{(3)},*}\mathfrak{IC}_{W,\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^{(3)}}^{\upzeta}\right)\rightarrow \\&\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(p^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^{(1)}+\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^{(2)}+\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^{(3)},*}\mathfrak{IC}_{W,\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^{(1)}+\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^{(2)}+\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^{(3)}}^{\upzeta}\right)\nonumber
\end{aligned}$$ and $$\begin{aligned}
\label{maptwo}
&\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(p^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^{(1)},*}\mathfrak{IC}_{W,\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^{(1)}}^{\upzeta}\right)\boxtimes_{\oplus}^{\mathop{\mathrm{tw}}}\left(\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(p^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^{(2)},*}\mathfrak{IC}_{W,\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^{(2)}}^{\upzeta}\right)\boxtimes_{\oplus}^{\mathop{\mathrm{tw}}}\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(p^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^{(3)},*}\mathfrak{IC}_{W,\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^{(3)}}^{\upzeta}\right)\right)\rightarrow \\&\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(p^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^{(1)}+\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^{(2)}+\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^{(3)},*}\mathfrak{IC}_{W,\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^{(1)}+\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^{(2)}+\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^{(3)}}^{\upzeta}\right)
\end{aligned}$$ via the recipe: move all of the half Tate twists to the front, and then apply the map $\kappa$ to the final three tensor factors. On the other hand, the natural map between the domains of ([\[mapone\]](#mapone){reference-type="ref" reference="mapone"}) and ([\[maptwo\]](#maptwo){reference-type="ref" reference="maptwo"}) involve commuting the $\mathbb{L}^{\langle \mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^{(3)},\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^{(2)}/2\rangle}$ twist in the second monoidal product past the $\mathbb{L}^{\upchi(\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^{(1)},\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^{(1)})/2}$ twist in the definition of $\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(p^{\upzeta}_{\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^{(1)},*}\mathfrak{IC}_{W,\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^{(1)}}^{\upzeta}\right)$, introducing the sign $(-1)^{\langle \mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^{(3)},\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^{(2)}\rangle\upchi(\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^{(1)},\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbf{d}}}^{(1)})}$, as in the definition ([\[AssocSign\]](#AssocSign){reference-type="ref" reference="AssocSign"}) of the associator natural isomorphism for the monoidal structure $\boxtimes_{\oplus}^{\mathop{\mathrm{tw}}}$. We deduce

::: proposition
The pair $$\left(\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(p^{\upzeta}_{\upmu,*}\mathfrak{IC}_{W,\upmu}^{\upzeta}\right),\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(\mathop{\mathrm{\tilde{\ast}}}^{\upzeta}_{W,\upmu}\right)\right)$$ is a monoid in $\mathcal{D}^{\geq, \mathrm{lf}}(\mathop{\mathrm{MMHM}}(\mathcal{M}^{\upzeta}_{\upmu}))$.
:::

::: definition
We denote by $\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}(\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{A}}}_{W,\upmu}^{\upzeta})$ the monoid $\left(\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(p_{\upmu,*}^{\upzeta}\mathfrak{IC}_{W,\upmu}^{\upzeta}\right),\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}(\mathop{\mathrm{\tilde{\ast}}}^{\upzeta}_{W,\upmu}),\mathcal{IC}_{\mathcal{M}_0^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}}}(\mathbb{Q})\right)$ in $\left(\mathcal{D}^{\geq, \mathrm{lf}}(\mathop{\mathrm{MMHM}}(\mathcal{M}_{\upmu}^{\upzeta\mathop{\mathrm{-ss}}})),\boxtimes^{\mathop{\mathrm{tw}}}_{\oplus}\right)$.
:::

Recall from Proposition [\[cvs\]](#cvs){reference-type="ref" reference="cvs"} the isomorphisms $\upnu_{\upmu}\colon\upphi^{\mathop{\mathrm{mon}}}_{\mathcal{T}r(W)^{\upzeta}_{\upmu}}\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(p^{\upzeta}_{\upmu,*}\mathfrak{IC}_{0,\upmu}^{\upzeta}\right)\cong \mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(p^{\upzeta}_{\upmu,*}\mathfrak{IC}_{W,\upmu}^{\upzeta}\right)$. We will use the following technical lemma in the proof of Theorem [\[strongPBW\]](#strongPBW){reference-type="ref" reference="strongPBW"}.

::: lemma
[]{#cohacom label="cohacom"} The following diagram commutes: $$\xymatrix{
\upphi^{\mathop{\mathrm{mon}}}_{\mathcal{T}r(W)_{\upmu}^{\upzeta}}\left(\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(p_{\upmu,*}^{\upzeta}\mathfrak{IC}_{0,\upmu}^{\upzeta}\right)\boxtimes_{\oplus}^{\mathop{\mathrm{tw}}} \mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(p_{\upmu,*}^{\upzeta}\mathfrak{IC}_{0,\upmu}^{\upzeta}\right)\right)\ar[rr]^-{\upphi^{\mathop{\mathrm{mon}}}_{\mathcal{T}r(W)^{\upzeta}_{\upmu}}\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}(\mathop{\mathrm{\tilde{\ast}}}^{\upzeta}_{0,\upmu})}\ar[d]^{(\upnu_{\upmu}\boxtimes^{\mathop{\mathrm{tw}}}_{\oplus} \upnu_{\upmu})\circ \mathtt{TS}^{-1}} &&\upphi^{\mathop{\mathrm{mon}}}_{\mathcal{T}r(W)^{\upzeta}_{\upmu}}\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}\left(p^{\upzeta}_{\upmu,*}\mathfrak{IC}_{0,\upmu}^{\upzeta}\right)\ar[d]^{\upnu_{\upmu}}\\

::: proof
*Proof.* Here $\mathtt{TS}$ is the Thom--Sebastiani isomorphism. We break the two horizontal arrows into their constituent parts, given by the constituent morphisms of the composition $\mathop{\mathrm{\mathcal{H}}}(\mathop{\mathrm{\tilde{\ast}}}^{\upzeta}_{W,\upmu})$. Then the problem reduces to several trivial commutativity statements regarding smaller squares. ◻
