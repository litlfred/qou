CLOSED HYPERBOLIC 3-MANIFOLDS OBTAINED BY DOING
INTEGRAL SURGERY ALONG THE TWIST KNOT
QINGTAO CHEN AND SHENGMAO ZHU
Abstract. This is the third article in a series devoted to the study of the asymptotic
expansions of various quantum invariants related to the twist knots. In this paper, by us-
ing the saddle point method developed by Ohtsuki and Yokota, we obtain an asymptotic
expansion formula for the Reshetikhin-Turaev invariants of closed hyperbolic 3-manifolds
