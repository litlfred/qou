Zeilberger’s original n x k-Gog trapezoids are just (0,n, k)-Gog trapezoids as defined by
Krattenthaler. According to [28,21,22], we define minima and maxima of Gog trapezoids
as follows:

« A minimum is an entry equal to 1. (The minima are located at the bottom of the
leftmost NE-diagonal.)

« A mazimum is an entry in the k-th NE-diagonal that is equal to the upper bound
for the entries in its SE-diagonal.
