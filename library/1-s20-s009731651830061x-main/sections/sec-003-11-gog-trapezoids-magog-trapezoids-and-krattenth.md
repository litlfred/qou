Zeilberger’s original n × k-Gog trapezoids are just (0, n, k)-Gog trapezoids as deﬁned by 
Krattenthaler. According to [28,21,22], we deﬁne minima and maxima of Gog trapezoids 
as follows:
• A minimum is an entry equal to 1. (The minima are located at the bottom of the 
leftmost NE-diagonal.)
• A maximum is an entry in the k-th NE-diagonal that is equal to the upper bound 
for the entries in its SE-diagonal.
In the example, we have 3 minima (indicated in red2) and 2 maxima (indicated in blue).
