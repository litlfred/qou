In this section we derive constant term formulas for the number of (m, n, k)-Magog 
trapezoids with p maxima and q minima. Together with Theorem 9, where a constant 
term formula for the number of (m, n, k)-Gog trapezoids with p minima and q maxima is 
provided, this could serve as a framework to give a computational proof of Conjecture 1.
In special cases such constant term formulas have been derived before: Zeilberger gave 
a constant term expression for the unrestricted enumeration of (0, n, k)-Magog trapezoids
