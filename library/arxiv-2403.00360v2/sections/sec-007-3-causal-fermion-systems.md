[]{#seccfsintro label="seccfsintro"} We now give a self-contained introduction to the basic structures of a causal fermion system. We keep the explanations short, noting that detailed and elementary introductions to the physical and mathematical concepts can be found in the review papers [@dice2014; @review] and the comparison paper [@mmt-cfs Section 2.1]. We also refer the interested reader to the textbooks [@cfs; @intro] and the website [@cfsweblink].
