[]{#secqg label="secqg"} The kernel of the fermionic projector [\[Pxyabs\]](#Pxyabs){reference-type="eqref" reference="Pxyabs"} encodes geometric structures giving rise to a mathematically concise setting of a Lorentzian quantum geometry [@lqg]. One important structure is the *spin connection* $D_{x,y}$, being a unitary mapping (with respect to the spin inner product [\[ssp\]](#ssp){reference-type="eqref" reference="ssp"}) between the spin spaces, $$\label{Dxy}
D_{x,y} \::\: S_y \rightarrow S_x \qquad \text{unitary}.$$ A first idea for construction $D_{x,y}$ is to take a polar decomposition of $P(x,y)$. This idea needs to be refined in order to also obtain a metric connection and to ensure that the different connections are compatible. Here for brevity we omit the details and refer to [@lqg] or the review [@nrstg]. In general terms, it turns out that there is a canonical spin connection [\[Dxy\]](#Dxy){reference-type="eqref" reference="Dxy"}, provided that the operators $x$ and $y$ satisfy certain conditions, which are subsumed in the notion that the spacetime points be *spin-connectable*. *Curvature* $\mathfrak{R}$ can be defined as the holonomy of the spin connection. Thus, in the simplest case, for three points $x,y,z \in M$ which are mutually spin-connectable, one sets $$\mathfrak{R}(x,y,z) = D_{x,y}\: D_{y,z}\:D_{z,x} \::\: S_x \rightarrow S_x \:.$$ In [@lqg] it is also shown that these structures give back the setting of Lorentzian spin geometry as used in GR.

As outlined in Section [\[secdynamics\]](#secdynamics){reference-type="ref" reference="secdynamics"}, the causal action principle describes the dynamics of our quantum geometry. In this sense, causal fermion systems are a specific proposal for a theory of quantum gravity. However, the connection to other approaches to quantum gravity (like canonical quantum gravity [@kiefernew] or loop quantum gravity [@thiemann]) has not yet been worked out.
