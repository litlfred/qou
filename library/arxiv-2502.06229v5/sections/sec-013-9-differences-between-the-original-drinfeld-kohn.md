Differently from the original Drinfeld-Kohno theorem, we do not study the problem
of equivalence associativity morphisms in our version version of Drinfeld-Kohno theorem.
We do this in Sect. 8 in [10] with the mentioned different ideas that appeal to duality

More specifically, we first construct semisimple weak Hopf algebras AW(g, q) associated
to fusion categories of quantum groups at roots of unity for all Lie types. We show that
AW(g, q) satisfy a triviality property of the associator, which makes the definining weak
quasi-tensor functor (via a suitable Tannakian duality construction) into a weak tensor
functor.
These AW(g, q) play the role of the quantum group over a formal variable in Drinfeld-
Kohno theorem.
This construction is based on the analysis in [99] and the triviality
property of the associator is based on properties observed by Gelfand and Kazhdan for
morphisms between non negligible and negligible objects [36], following the same ideas as
in [9] in the type A case.
Then we use a quantization continuous curve that is described in [99] to connect ir-
reducible representations of AW(g, q) with irreducible representations of the Zhu algebra
A(Vgk) associated to a corresponding affine Lie algebra, or vertex operator algebra at a
positive integer level.
We apply the natural isomorphism given by extremes of Wenzl continuos curve defining
quantization of a suitable finite class of representations of the classical Lie algebra (the
Weyl alcove defined by the roots of unity) to representations of Uq(g). Then we apply our
Drinfeld-Kohno theorem to endow the Zhu algebra with the structure of a weak quasi-Hopf
algebra with coboundary matrix.
These two passages, in analogy with the original Drinfeld-Kohno theorem, allow to
construct the structure of a weak quasi-Hopf algebra in our case on the Zhu algebra A(Vgk).
At this point we can use Zhu’s linear equivalence and transport the ribbon braided tensor
structure to the module category of the associated affine vertex operator algebra.
As
already explained we verify that braided symmetry and associativity morphisms correspond
to the correct braided symmetry and associativity morphisms in the setting of vertex
operator algebras on special pairs or triples of representations of the weak quasi-Hopf
algebra.
The unitary structure associated to a coboundary of a semisimple weak quasi-Hopf al-
gebra allows directly unitary representations of the braid group, and, similarly to the case
of Uq(g), anticommutes with the comultiplication. This property resembles the antimulti-
plicativity property of the ∗-involution in a noncommutative topological space. The unitary
