In Doplicher-Roberts theory, a unique gauge compact subgroup of SU(d) is in particular
constructed in the case of a rigid symmetric tensor C∗-category with simple unit and a
single generating object, to describe the category when the generating property of the per-
mutation group is not satisfied. Their theory has applications to the category of localized
endomorphisms arising from the local observable algebras in high dimensional AQFT, and
leads to the construction of the field algebra [20], [21].
For the class of the compact quantum groups of Woronowicz there is a construction of
their quantum homogeneous spaces and Hilbert bimodule representations developed for
non-symmetric rigid tensor categories with simple unit [85], [89]. The construction of the
homogeneous space and bimodule representations of the compact quantum group extends
to compact quantum groups the idea of closed subgroup construction in Doplicher-Roberts
theory. In that quantum case the homogeneous space is an ergodic action of a compact
quantum group of a unital C∗-algebra, and may not arise from a quantum subgroup.
In the setting of compact quantum groups, unitary representations of the braid group
are not known to arise in a natural way.

categories at roots of unity in [99], and this paper was our first motivation to study these
quantum groups and construct weak Hopf C∗-algebras in the sense of [10], with precedents
the examples by Mack and Schomerus for sl2, described as weak quasi-Hopf algebras in

its extension to slN studied in [9] where the study of the unitary structure was included
following the results of [99].
Our weak Hopf C∗-algebras are suitable extensions of the compact quantum groups, and
in our experience the failure of strict co-associativity has played a mild role, and has allowed
the development of a theory extending that of Hopf algebras, or of compact quantum
groups if one allows twisted unitary structure, called Ω-involutions in [10] for which classes
of examples arising from ribbon tensor C∗-categories allow unitary representations of the
braid group in a natural way, called unitary coboundary weak Hopf C∗-algebras in the
same paper.
It is then desirable to search for further generalizations of quantum homogeneous spaces
and bimodule representations to these weak Hopf algebras to describe ribbon braided tensor
C∗-categories with unitary representations of the braid group, such as the representation
categories of models of CFT: conformal nets where the unitary structure is given from the
start, or related vertex operator algebras where is constructed. In the affine case that we
have studied in our papers, the unitary, rigid, and modular structure in the latter can be
transported from that of the quantum group fusion category via the equivalence. This
may be useful for those models for which, in analogy to the theory of Doplicher-Roberts
for rigid symmetric tensor categories, the generating property of the braid group is not
satisfied for the algebras of intertwining morphisms of the category. Or also for models for
which weak Hopf algebras can not arise from failure of submultiplicativity of the integer
valued dimension function of representations of the Zhu algebra. Natural classes of models
to study in this direction are referred to as coset models.
Such an extension may prove useful to construct a field algebra from a braided tensor C∗-
category, for which conformal nets provides examples, from the algebra of local observables,
taking into consideration the quantum group fusion categories at roots of unity and the
associated weak Hopf C∗-algebras constructed in [10], as developments of early works in
that direction of the 90s.
Other interesting future developments may regard applications in noncommutative ge-
ometry in the sense of Connes [12] of our weak Hopf algebras AW(g, q) associated to the
quantum group fusion category C(g, q) or their twisted weak quasi-Hopf algebra structures
on the Zhu algebra of an affine vertex operator algebra at a positive integer level Vgk to the
study of Dirac operators, by expanding an analog in conformal field theory of the ground-
breaking work by Neshveyev and Tuset on Dirac operators for compact quantum groups
in the setting of Kazhdan-Lusztig theorem for real values of the quantization parameter q
[79].
