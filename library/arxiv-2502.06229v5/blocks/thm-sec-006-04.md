The semisimple ∗-algebra AW(g, q) := Nat(W) of natural transformations of W admits a natural structure of unitary compatible coboundary weak Hopf algebra derived from C(g, q) and W. Thanks to Theorem 29.4 in [10]–our version of Drinfeld-Kohno theorem–the Zhu algebras A(Vgk) become unitary coboundary weak quasi-Hopf algebras with a Drinfeld twist from AW(g, q), by our version of Drinfeld-Kohno theorem. This is summarized in the following section, and detailed in Sect. 33 of [10].
