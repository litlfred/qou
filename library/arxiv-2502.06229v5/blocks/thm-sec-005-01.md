Let g be a complex simple Lie algebra and k a positive integer, the level. Let q = e iπ d(k+h∨), with h∨the dual Coxeter number and d the ratio of the square lengths of the long to the short root. Then the unitary coboundary weak Hopf algebra AW(g, q) induces a rigid braided tensor category structure on the module category of the affine vertex operator algebra Vgk. Furthermore we construct, via the Drinfeld twist method, a braided tensor equivalence between this category and the modular quantum group fusion category C(g, q) for all Lie types.
