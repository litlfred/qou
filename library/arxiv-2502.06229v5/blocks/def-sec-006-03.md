A (compatible) unitary coboundary weak quasi-Hopf algebra A is defined by the following data: a) A unital weak quasi-Hopf algebra A endowed with a C∗-algebra involution with an antipode (S, α, β) b) a ribbon structure (R, v) for A associated to (S, α, β) such that the ribbon element v ∈A is unitary, c) a unitary central square root w ∈A of v such that ε(w) = 1, S(w) = w, d) ˜A = Aop as quasitriangular weak quasi-bialgebras, e) R = Rw−1 ⊗w−1∆(w) is positive. The element R is the R-matrix; v ∈A is a central element defining the ribbon structure; the operator R is Drinfeld coboundary matrix and gives the Ω-involution Ω= R in the sense of Sect. 1
