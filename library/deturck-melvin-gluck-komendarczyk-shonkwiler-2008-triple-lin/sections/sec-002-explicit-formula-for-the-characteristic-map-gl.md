The derivation of this formula is based on the model of the Grassmann

product space V2 R4 ∼= R6, as presented in Gluck and Warner [1983].
Note that the map gL is “symmetric” in the sense that it transforms
under any permutation of the components of L by precomposing with the
corresponding permutation automorphism of T 3, and then multiplying by
the sign of the permutation.
