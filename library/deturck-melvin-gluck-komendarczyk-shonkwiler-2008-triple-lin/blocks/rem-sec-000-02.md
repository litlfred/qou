Milnor’s µ-invariant, typically denoted µ123, is descriptive of a single three-component link. In contrast, Pontryagin’s ν-invariant is the cohomology class of a difference cocycle comparing two maps from T 3 to S2 that are homotopic on the 2-skeleton of T 3. In particular, it assigns to any pair g, g′ of such maps whose degrees on the coordinate 2-tori are p, q and r, an integer ν(g, g′) that is well-defined modulo 2 gcd(p, q, r). With this understanding, the last statement in Theorem A asserts that 2(µ(L) −µ(L′)) ≡ν(gL, gL′) mod 2 gcd(p, q, r), for any two links L and L′ with the same pairwise linking numbers p, q and r.
