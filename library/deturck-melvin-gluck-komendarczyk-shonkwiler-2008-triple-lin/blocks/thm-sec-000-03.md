If the pairwise linking numbers p, q and r of the three components of L are all zero, then Milnor’s µ-invariant of L is given by the formula µ(L) = 1 Z T 3×T 3 VL(σ) × VL(τ)  ∇σφ (σ −τ) dσ dτ. Here ∇σ indicates the gradient with respect to σ, the difference σ −τ is taken in the abelian group structure of the torus, and dσ and dτ are volume elements. The integrand is invariant under the action of the group SO(4) of orientation-preserving rigid motions of S3 on the link L, attesting to the naturality of the formula. We will see in the next section that the integral above expresses the “helicity” of the vector field VL on T 3.
