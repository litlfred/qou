The bracket polynomial provides a connection between knot theory and physics, in that the state summation expression for it exhibits it as a generalized partition function deﬁned on the knot diagram. Partition functions are ubiquitous in statistical mechanics, where they express the summation over all states of the physical system of probability weighting functions for the individual states. Such physical partition functions contain large amounts of information about the corresponding physical system.
