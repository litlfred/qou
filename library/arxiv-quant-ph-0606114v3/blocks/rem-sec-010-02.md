There are many gates other than CNOT that can be used as universal gates in the presence of local unitary transformations. Some of these are themselves topological (unitary solutions to the Yang-Baxter equation, see [52, 81]) and themselves generate representations of the Artin Braid Group. Replacing CNOT by a solution to the Yang-Baxter equation does not place the local unitary transformations as part of the corresponding representation of the braid group.
