Evaluations
In this section we discuss the structure of the evaluations for ∆n and the
theta and tetrahedral networks. We refer to [36] for the details behind these
formulas. Recall that ∆n is the bracket evaluation of the closure of the n-
strand projector, as illustrated in Figure 32. For the bracket variable A, one

2r −4. All the summations in the formulas for recoupling are restricted to
admissible triples of this form.
