We now discuss the Jones polynomial. We shall construct the Jones polynomial
by using the bracket state summation model [34]. The bracket polynomial,
invariant under Reidmeister moves II and III, can be normalized to give an
invariant of all three Reidemeister moves. This normalized invariant, with a
change of variable, is the Jones polynomial [32, 33]. The Jones polynomial was
originally discovered by a diﬀerent method than the one given here.

Thus Property 1 is a consequence of the other two properties.
