III.
CONSERVED DEGREES OF FREEDOM
Note that the decomposition (2) of the state space H
sums over topologically distinct embeddings of the sur-
faces in Σ. For example, the two graphs of Fig.3 may
correspond to the same state in HΓ but correspond to
distinct states in H. They are distinct physical states be-
cause they are braided differently.
The basic point of this paper is a simple one and
can be summarized as follows. The braiding content of
states in H remains invariant under the action of A evol.

will be conserved under this evolution. As the quantum
geometry evolves the conserved structure represented
