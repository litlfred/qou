Moments of quadratic twists of
elliptic curve L-functions over function ﬁelds
Hung M. Bui, Alexandra Florea, Jonathan P. Keating and Edva Roditty-Gershon
We calculate the ﬁrst and second moments of L-functions in the family of quadratic twists of a ﬁxed
elliptic curve E over Fq[x], asymptotically in the limit as the degree of the twists tends to inﬁnity. We
also compute moments involving derivatives of L-functions over quadratic twists, enabling us to deduce
lower bounds on the correlations between the analytic ranks of the twists of two distinct curves.
