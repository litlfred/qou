Explicitly, if $\sigma_i = \mathfrak{f}_{E}^{n_i} s_E(\gamma_i)$, then we must show that $$(\Phi^{n_1} \circ f^{-1}_{\gamma_1}) \circ (\Phi^{n_2} \circ f^{-1}_{\gamma_2}) = \Phi^{n_1+n_2 + c_E(\gamma_1,\gamma_2)}f^{-1}_{\gamma_1\gamma_2}.$$ By construction, we have $$f^{-1}_{\gamma_1\gamma_2} = f^{-1}_{\gamma_1} \circ f^{-1}_{\gamma_2} \circ \Phi^{-c_E(\gamma_1,\gamma_2)},$$ which implies the desired equality.
