Consider a tower of finite Galois extensions $K/E/F$. Fix an anti-uniformizer $\varpi_E$ of $E$. Let $K' \subset K$ be the maximal unramified extension of $E$. Then $K/K'$ is totally ramified and we have our fixed anti-uniformizer $\varpi_K$ such that $N_{K/K'}(\varpi_K)=\varpi_E$. We define a transition functor $p_{K/E}: R\mathcal{I}_{E/F, s} \to R\mathcal{I}_{K/F, s}$ defined according to our choices of uniformizers. We pick a lift of the Frobenius of $F$ in the absolute Weil group $W_{\overline{F}/F}$, whose restriction we use in our definitions of the actions of $W_{L_{K}/F}$ and $W_{L_{E}/F}$ on $R_{K/F}$ and $R_{E/F}$, respectively. To begin, we consider the $\prod\limits_{\tau \in \Gamma_{K/F}} \mathbb{Z}$-graded ring $R'_{E/F, K}$ whose $(c_{\tau})_{\tau}$-graded piece is $0$ if the grading is not fixed under the $\Gamma_{K/E}$-action and is the $(c_{\overline{\tau}})_{\sigma}$-graded piece of $R_{E/F}$ otherwise. We then define $R_{E/F, K} := R'_{E/F, K} \otimes_{L_E} L_K$. We give $R_{E/F, K}$ the diagonal $W_{L_K/F}$-action whereby $W_{L_K/F}$ acts on the first factor by projection to $W_{L_E/F}$ and on the second factor by the natural Galois action. Note that we have an inclusion of graded rings $$R_{E/F, K} \hookrightarrow R_{K/F}.$$ Moreover, we claim this inclusion respects the $W_{L_K/F}$-actions. We need only check that the actions of $\mathfrak{f}_{F,K}$ (our chosen lift of Frobenius) agree on $1_{(c_{\tau})_{\tau}}$ since the actions of $\Gamma_{L_K/L}$ both act by the natural Galois action and shift the gradings in the same way. We have $$\mathfrak{f}_{F,K}(1_{(c_{\tau})_{\tau}}) = \prod\limits_{\tau' \in \Omega_{K}} \tau'(\varpi_K)^{c_{\mathfrak{f}_{F,K}^{-1}\tau'}}_{(c_{\mathfrak{f}_{F,K}^{-1}\tau})_{\tau}} = \prod\limits_{\sigma' \in \Gamma_{K'/(K \cap L)}} \prod\limits_{\tau'' \in \Gamma_{K/K'}} \sigma'\tau''(\varpi_K)^{c_{\mathfrak{f}_{K,F}^{-1}\tau'}}_{(c_{\mathfrak{f}_{K,F}^{-1}\tau})_{\tau}}$$ $$= \prod\limits_{\sigma' \in \Gamma_{K'/(K \cap L)}} \sigma'(\varpi_E)^{c_{\mathfrak{f}_{F,E}^{-1}\sigma'}}_{(c_{\mathfrak{f}_{F,E}^{-1}\sigma})_{\sigma}} = \prod\limits_{\sigma' \in \Gamma_{E/(E \cap L)}} \sigma'(\varpi_E)^{c_{\mathfrak{f}_{F,E}^{-1}\sigma'}}_{(c_{\mathfrak{f}_{F,E}^{-1}\sigma})_{\sigma}} = [\mathfrak{f}_{F,E}1_{(c_{\sigma})_{\sigma}}] \otimes 1 = \mathfrak{f}_{F,K}(1_{(c_{\sigma})_{\sigma}} \otimes 1),$$

To obtain the fourth equality, we have used the fact that the restriction-to-$E$ map induces a surjection $\Gamma_{K'/(K \cap L)} \twoheadrightarrow \Gamma_{E/(E \cap L)}$, with kernel equal to $\Gamma_{K'/[E \cdot (K \cap L)]}$, where now $E \cdot (K \cap L) = K'$, since the maximal unramified subextension of $K/E$ is the compositum of $E$ and the maximal unramified subextension of $K/F$, by elementary algebraic number theory. The last equality follows from the fact that, by construction, the image of $\mathfrak{f}_{F,K}$ in $W_{L_{E}/F}$ is $\mathfrak{f}_{F,E}$.

We are now ready to define the functor $p_{K/E}$. For an object $M$ of $R\mathcal{I}_{E/F, s}$, we define $M_K$ to be the $\prod\limits_{\tau \in \Gamma_{K/F}} \frac{1}{s} \mathbb{Z}$-graded $R_{E/F, K}$-module whose $(c_{\tau})_{\tau}$-graded piece is $0$ if the grading is not fixed under the $\Gamma_{K/E}$-action and is the $(c_{\overline{\tau}})_{\overline{\tau}}$ graded piece of $M \otimes_{L_{E}} L_K$ otherwise. We then define $p_{K/E}(M) := M_K \otimes_{R_{E/F, K}} R_{K/F}$ (with the induced grading) and define the $W_{L_K/F}$-action diagonally, noting this is well-defined by the $W_{L_K/F}$-equivariance of $R_{E/F, K} \hookrightarrow R_{K/F}$.

Note also that there is a similar transition functor $p'_{K/E} \colon \mathcal{I}_{K/F,s} \to \mathcal{I}_{E/F,s}$, defined simply by sending the $W_{L_{E}/F}$-module $M$ to the $W_{L_{K}/F}$-module $M \otimes_{L_{E}} L_{K}$ with diagonal $W_{L_{K}/F}$-action induced on the first factor by projection to $W_{L_{E}/F}$ and grading given the same way as with the functor $p_{K/E}$. Note that, trivially, the transition functors $p'_{K/E}$ and $p_{K/E}$ are compatible with the quotient maps $\mathcal{I}_{?/F,s} \to R\mathcal{I}_{?/F,s}$, where $?=E$ or $K$. By abuse of notation, we will also use $p'_{K/E}$ to denote the functor from $P_{K/F,s}$ to $P_{E/F,s}$ given in the same way as $p'_{K/E}$, except now with diagonal $\Gamma_{L_{K}/K}$-action on $M \otimes L_{K}$ induced on the first factor via projecting into $\Gamma_{L_{E}/E}$.

As usual, to describe what $p_{K/E}$ does on bands, we will first describe what $p'_{K/E}$ does, which in turn we will do via our identifications of $P_{E/F,s}$ and $P_{K/F,s}$ with the categories $\mathcal{E}_{(c_{\sigma})_{\sigma}}$ and $\mathcal{E}_{(d_{\sigma})_{\sigma}}$, respectively. Here, as in §[\[sec:prod\]](#sec:prod){reference-type="ref" reference="sec:prod"}, the tuples $(c_{\sigma})_{\sigma}$ and $(d_{\sigma})_{\sigma}$ are the representatives of the anti-canonical class in $H^{2}(\Gamma_{E_{s}/E}, E_{s}^{\times})$ and $H^{2}(\Gamma_{K_{s}/K}, K_{s}^{\times})$, respectively corresponding to $\varpi_{E}$ and $\varpi_{K}$. Conjugating $p_{K/E}'$ by these equivalences gives a functor from $\text{Rep}_{E_{s}}(\mathcal{E}_{(c_{\sigma})_{\sigma}})$ to $\text{Rep}_{K_{s}}(\mathcal{E}_{(d_{\sigma})_{\sigma}})$, which we will explicitly compute.

Recall that, given an $L_{E}$-representation $(V, \rho)$ of the group $\mathcal{E}_{(c_{\sigma})_{\sigma}}$, determined by the homomorphism $h_{\rho} \colon \prod_{\Gamma_{E/F}} \mathbb{G}_{m}(L_{E}) \to \mathrm{GL}(V)$ and the $\mathfrak{f}_{E}$-semilinear automorphism induced by $((1),\mathfrak{f}_{E})$, we obtain an object $(V', \Phi, \mathcal{D})$ of $P_{E/F,s}$ as in §[\[sec:prod\]](#sec:prod){reference-type="ref" reference="sec:prod"}, with $V' = V \otimes_{E_{s}} L_{E}$, $\Phi$ given by the action $((1),\mathfrak{f}_{E})$, and $V'_{(h_{\sigma}/s)} = V_{(h_{\sigma})} \otimes_{E_{s}} L_{E}$. Then, by definition, $p'_{K/E}$ sends this to the object $(V' \otimes_{L_{E}} L_{K}, \Phi', \mathcal{D}')$, where $\Phi'$ acts diagonally on $V' \otimes_{L_{E}} L_{K}$ by $\Phi^{f(K/E)} \otimes \mathfrak{f}_{K}$ and $V''_{(h_{\tau}/s)} = 0$ if $(h_{\tau}/s)$ is not $\Gamma_{K/E}$-fixed, and equals $V'_{(h_{\bar{\tau}}/s)} \otimes_{L_{E}} L_{K}$ otherwise. To compute the image of this object under the equivalence, we note that the subspace $$V''_{(h_{\tau}/s)} = V'_{(h_{\bar{\tau}}/s)} \otimes_{L_{E}} L_{K} = V_{(h_{\bar{\tau}})} \otimes_{E_{s}} L_{K} = (V_{(h_{\bar{\tau}})} \otimes_{E_{s}} K_{s}) \otimes_{K_{s}} L_{K}$$ has an $L_{K}$-basis given by $\{e_{i} \otimes 1 \otimes 1\}$, where $e_{i}$ is an $E_{s}$-basis of $V_{(h_{\bar{\tau}})}$ on which $\Phi^{s}$ acts by $\prod_{\sigma \in \Gamma_{E/F}} \sigma(\varpi_{E})^{h_{\sigma}}$. Thus, $$(\Phi')^{s}(e_{i} \otimes 1 \otimes 1) =[\prod_{\sigma \in \Gamma_{E/F}} \sigma(\varpi_{E})^{f(K/E)h_{\sigma}}] (e_{i} \otimes 1 \otimes 1),$$ which, by construction, equals $$[\prod_{\sigma \in \Gamma_{E/F}} \sigma(\prod_{\gamma \in \Gamma_{K/K'}} \gamma(\varpi_{K}))^{f(K/E)h_{\sigma}}](e_{i} \otimes 1 \otimes 1).$$ Finally, the above expression may be rewritten as $$[\prod_{\tau \in \Gamma_{K/F}} \tau(\varpi_{K})^{h_{\bar{\tau}}}] (e_{i} \otimes 1 \otimes 1),$$ showing that the corresponding representation of $\mathcal{E}_{(d_{\sigma})}$, denoted by $(W, \rho')$, has $W_{(h_{\tau})} = 0$ if $(h_{\tau})$ is not $\Gamma_{K/E}$-stable, and is $V_{(h_{\bar{\tau}})} \otimes_{E_{s}} K_{s}$ otherwise. Thus, if the automorphism of the fiber functor $\omega_{K}$ acts on $W_{(h'_{\tau})}$ via scaling by $\prod_{\tau \in \Gamma_{K/F}} a_{\tau}^{h'_{\tau}}$, (where $(a_{\tau}) \in \prod_{\Gamma_{K/F}} \mathbb{G}_{m}(K_{s})$), then the above calculation shows that we get an induced automorphism of $(\omega_{E})_{K}$, which acts on $V_{(h_{\sigma})} \otimes_{E_{s}} K_{s}$ by $\prod_{\sigma \in \Gamma_{E/F}} (\prod_{\tau \in \Gamma_{K/F}, \tau \mapsto \sigma} a_{\tau})^{h_{\sigma}}$, which shows that the induced map, on $K_{s}$-points, is given by the "norm\" map $\prod_{\Gamma_{K/F}} \mathbb{G}_{m}(K_{s}) \to \prod_{\Gamma_{E/F}} \mathbb{G}_{m}(K_{s})$.

By construction, the $F$-group morphism on bands from $\text{Res}_{K/F}(\mathbb{G}_{m})$ to $\text{Res}_{E/F}(\mathbb{G}_{m})$ induced by $p'_{K/E}$ is given on $K_{s}$-points via the map described explicitly at the bottom of the previous paragraph; the same is true of the canonical "norm map\" $\text{Res}_{K/F}(\mathbb{G}_{m}) \to \text{Res}_{E/F}(\mathbb{G}_{m})$. Since both schemes are tori and $K_{s}$ is infinite, by unirationality this implies that the two maps coincide. The following diagram of transition maps commutes $$\begin{tikzcd}
\mathcal{I}_{E/F,s} \arrow{d} \arrow{r} & \mathcal{I}_{K/F,s} \arrow{d} \\
R\mathcal{I}_{E/F,s} \arrow{r} & R \mathcal{I}_{K/F,s},
\end{tikzcd}$$ where the vertical arrows are the quotient maps discussed above, which means that the morphism of $F$-group schemes $\text{Res}_{K/F}(\mu_{s}) \to \text{Res}_{E/F}(\mu_{s})$ associated to $p_{K/E}$ is induced by the inclusion $\text{Res}_{K/F}(\mu_{s}) \hookrightarrow \text{Res}_{K/F}(\mathbb{G}_{m})$ followed by the map induced by $p'_{K/E}$, which implies that it is also given by the norm map. Finally, for $s \mid s'$, we define the transition map $p_{K/E,s',s} \colon \mathcal{I}_{E/F,s} \to \mathcal{I}_{K/F,s'}$ to be the composition $$\mathcal{I}_{E/F,s} \xrightarrow{p_{K/E}} \mathcal{I}_{K/F,s} \xrightarrow{\iota_{s,s'}} \mathcal{I}_{K/F,s'},$$ which on bands gives the map $\text{Res}_{K/F}(\mu_{s'}) \to \text{Res}_{E/F}(\mu_{s})$ of $F$-group schemes given by $N_{K/F}$ composed with the $s'/s$-power map.

## The category $\mathrm{RigIsoc}_{F}$ {#sec:RigIsocdef}

We are now ready to define the Tannakian category over $F$ which parametrizes rigid inner forms. We define the category $\mathrm{RigIsoc}_{F}$ to be the direct limit of the categories $R\mathcal{I}_{E/F,s}$ over the inductive system of tuples $(E, s)$, where $E/F$ is a finite Galois extension and $s \in \mathbb{N}$, which is ordered by the relation that $(K,s') > (E,s)$ if and only if $K$ contains $E$ and $s \mid s'$, with respect to the transition maps $p_{K/E,s,s'}$ defined in the previous subsection. It will frequently be convenient to fix a cofinal system of objects in the indexing category indexed by $\mathbb{N}$, denoted by $(E_{k}, n_{k})$. Note that $\varinjlim_{k} R\mathcal{I}_{k} = \mathrm{RigIsoc}_{F}$, where $R\mathcal{I}_{k} := R\mathcal{I}_{E_{k}/F,n_{k}}$.

By construction, $\mathrm{RigIsoc}_{F}$ is a Tannakian category over $F$, with fiber functor over $\overline{L}$, and the corresponding gerbe is banded by the $F$-group scheme $u = \varprojlim_{k} \text{Res}_{E_{k}/F} (\mu_{n_{k}})$. Note that each category $R\mathcal{I}_{E/F,s}$ has a fiber functor over the finite extension $E_{s}/F$, given explicitly by sending $(V, \mathcal{D}, \Phi)$ to the (finite-dimensional) $E_{s}$-vector space $$\label{keyfiberfunctor}
\bigoplus_{0 \leq (a_{\sigma}/s) < 1} ([\bigoplus_{(b_{\sigma}) \in \prod_{\Gamma_{E/F}} \mathbb{Z}} V^{\mathfrak{f}_{E}^{s} \cdot (\prod \sigma(\varpi_{E})^{-[a_{\sigma}+sb_{\sigma}]})}] \otimes_{R_{E/F}} R_{E/F}/I),$$ where $V^{\mathfrak{f}_{E}^{s} \cdot (\prod \sigma(\varpi_{E})^{-[a_{\sigma}+sb_{\sigma}]})}$ denotes the subgroup of $V$ of all elements on which $\mathfrak{f}_{E}^{s}$ acts by the scalar $\prod_{\sigma \in \Gamma_{E/F}}\sigma(\varpi_{E})^{-[a_{\sigma}+sb_{\sigma}]}$. Note that the above expression is well-defined, since the internal direct sum is $R_{E/F}$-stable. We may extend this to a fiber functor over $\overline{F}$ by simply post-composing the above fiber functor over $E_{s}$ with $- \otimes_{E_{s}} \overline{F}$. By considering the automorphisms of this fiber functor over $\overline{F}$, we get a canonical cohomology class in the group $H^{2}(\Gamma_{\overline{F}/F}, \text{Res}_{E/F}(\mu_{s})(\overline{F}))$, which we now compute. Since $R\mathcal{I}_{E/F,s}$ is a quotient of $\mathcal{I}_{E/F,s}$, the above cohomology class maps to the class corresponding to the group of automorphisms of the fiber functor $\mathcal{I}_{E/F,s} \to \text{Vect}_{\overline{F}}$ given explicitly by tensoring the $E_{s}$-fiber functor $$(V, \mathcal{D}, \tilde{\Phi}) \mapsto \bigoplus_{(a_{\sigma}/s) \in \prod_{\Gamma_{E/F}} \frac{1}{s}\mathbb{Z}} V^{\mathfrak{f}_{E}^{s} \cdot(\prod \sigma(\varpi_{E})^{-a_{\sigma}})}$$ with $\overline{F}$ over $E_{s}$, under the canonical map $H^{2}(\Gamma_{\overline{F}/F}, \text{Res}_{E/F}(\mu_{s})(\overline{F})) \to H^{2}(\Gamma_{\overline{F}/F}, \text{Res}_{E/F}(\mathbb{G}_{m})(\overline{F}))$, noting that the above fiber functors are compatible with the quotient functor from $\mathcal{I}_{E/F,s}$ to $R\mathcal{I}_{E/F,s}$, and using [@MilneQuot §2.10].

Because of the above observation, it will be useful to first describe the corresponding cohomology class in $H^{2}(\Gamma_{\overline{F}/F}, \text{Res}_{E/F}(\mathbb{G}_{m})(\overline{F}))$ corresponding to $\mathcal{I}_{E/F,s}$. Since the Tannakian category $\mathcal{I}_{E/F,s}$ over $F$ has base-change to $E$ which is isomorphic as Tannakian categories over $E$ to $P_{E/F,s}$, it follows that the image of this class in $H^{2}(E, \text{Res}_{E/F}(\mathbb{G}_{m})(\overline{F}))$ is the $\Gamma_{E/F}$-fold product of the classes $-1/s \in \mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}$ in $\prod_{\Gamma_{E/F}} H^{2}(E, \mathbb{G}_{m}(\overline{F}))$. In particular, the image of this class under the Shapiro isomorphism $$H^{2}(\Gamma_{\overline{F}/F}, \text{Res}_{E/F}(\mathbb{G}_{m})(\overline{F})) \xrightarrow{\sim} H^{2}(\Gamma_{\overline{F}/E}, \mathbb{G}_{m}(\overline{F}))$$ is the class mapping to $-1/s \in \mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}$ under the local invariant map.

Recall that $H^{2}(\Gamma_{\overline{F}/F}, u(\overline{F})) = \varprojlim_{k} H^{2}(\Gamma_{\overline{F}/F}, u_{k}(\overline{F}))$, where $u_{k} := \text{Res}_{E_{k}/F}(\mu_{n_{k}})$. Using Hilbert 90 and Shapiro's Lemma, we see that the latter groups fit into the exact sequence $$0 \to H^{2}(\Gamma_{\overline{F}/F}, u_{k}(\overline{F})) \to H^{2}(\Gamma_{\overline{F}/F}, \text{Res}_{E_{k}/F}(\mathbb{G}_{m})(\overline{F})) \xrightarrow{x \mapsto n_{k}x} H^{2}(\Gamma_{\overline{F}/F}, \text{Res}_{E_{k}/F}(\mathbb{G}_{m})(\overline{F})),$$ and so we get, via Shapiro's Lemma and the local invariant map, a canonical identification of the cohomology group $H^{2}(\Gamma_{\overline{F}/F}, u_{k}(\overline{F}))$ with $-\frac{1}{n_{k}}\mathbb{Z}/\mathbb{Z}$. Moreover, it follows from our above discussion that the gerbe corresponding to the Tannakian category $R\mathcal{I}_{k}$ has cohomology class equal to the element $-\frac{1}{n_{k}}$. For $l > k$, the projection map $H^{2}(\Gamma_{\overline{F}/F}, u_{l}(\overline{F})) \to H^{2}(\Gamma_{\overline{F}/F}, u_{k}(\overline{F}))$ corresponds (via the $\overline{F}$-fiber functor given by direct limit of the $\overline{F}$-fiber functors for each $R\mathcal{I}_{k}$, which are evidently compatible) to the multiplication-by-$n_{l}/n_{k}$ map from $\frac{1}{n_{l}}\mathbb{Z}/\mathbb{Z}$ to $\frac{1}{n_{k}}\mathbb{Z}/\mathbb{Z}$. We thus conclude that the category $\mathrm{RigIsoc}_{F}$ corresponds to the class $-1 \in \widehat{\mathbb{Z}} = \varprojlim_{k} \frac{1}{n_{k}}\mathbb{Z}/\mathbb{Z}= H^{2}(\Gamma_{\overline{F}/F}, u(\overline{F}))$, which is exactly what we want. All in all, we have proved:

::: thm
The Galois gerbe given associated to the gerbe of fiber functors of $\mathrm{RigIsoc}_{F}$ represents the class $-1 \in \widehat{\mathbb{Z}} \cong H^{2}(\Gamma_{\overline{F}/F},u(\overline{F}))$. In particular, we may identify $\mathrm{RigIsoc}_{F}$ with $\mathop{\mathrm{Rep}}(\mathcal{E}_{\mathop{\mathrm{Kal}}})$.
:::

[]{#rem: Kalbbandvariant label="rem: Kalbbandvariant"} As noted in the introduction, we use an alternative presentation of the group scheme $u$ to the presentation $u'$ (a priori a different group) used in [@Kalannals]. Nevertheless, the map $u \to u'$ is an isomorphism. It suffices to show this at the level of character modules, where the map is the inclusion $$\varinjlim_{E/F} (\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z})[\Gamma_{E/F}]_{0} \to \varinjlim_{E/F} (\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z})[\Gamma_{E/F}],$$ where the direct limit is over all finite Galois extensions. Surjectivity comes from the fact that for a fixed $x \in \mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}[\Gamma_{E/F}]$ which is $m$-torsion, if we take $K/E/F$ finite Galois with $m \mid [K \colon E]$ then the image of $x$ in $\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}[\Gamma_{K/F}]$ lies in $\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}[\Gamma_{K/F}]_{0}$. We warn that this result is false when $F = \mathbb{R}$.

# The structure of $\mathrm{RigIsoc}_{F}$

In this section we study the structure of $\mathrm{RigIsoc}_{F}$ as an abelian tensor category and construct examples of its objects.
