A TANNAKIAN DESCRIPTION OF THE LOCAL KALETHA GERBE
ALEXANDER BERTOLONI MELI AND PETER DILLERY
ABSTRACT. We construct, for a p-adic field F, an explicit semisimple Tannakian category RigIsocF whose
category of fiber functors recovers Kaletha’s Galois gerbe EKal. We then classify and write down the simple
objects in RigIsocF , all of which come from elliptic twisted Levi subgroups of GLn.
