We fix the following notation

-   We fix $F$ a $p$-adic local field.

-   Let $L$ be the completion of the maximal unramified extension of $F$ and fix an algebraic closure $\overline{L}$.

-   Let $\overline{F}$ be the algebraic closure of $F$ in $\overline{L}$.

-   We will consider finite Galois extensions $E/F$ and denote by $L_E \subset \overline{L}$ the completion of the maximal unramified extension of $E$.

-   Denote by $W_{L_E/E}$ the subgroup of $\Gamma_{L_E/E}$ generated by integral powers of a fixed choice of Frobenius morphism which we denote by $\mathfrak{f}_E$.

-   For each $E$, fix $\varpi_E$ an anti-uniformizer of $E$ such that if $K/E/F$ and $K'/E$ is the maximal unramified subextension, we have $N_{K/K'}(\varpi_{K}) = \varpi_{E}$ (this exists by [@Fargues Lemme 5.5]).

-   Let $E_{s}$ denote the unramified extension of $E$ of degree $s \in \mathbb{N}$.
