By Lemma [\[lem:simple\]](#lem:simple){reference-type="ref" reference="lem:simple"} it suffices to understand the $X \in \mathop{\mathrm{obj}}(\mathrm{RigIsoc}_{F})$ corresponding to basic classes in the elliptic twisted Levi subgroups $M \xrightarrow{\sim} \mathrm{Res}_{E/F}(\mathrm{GL}_{s})$ of $\mathrm{GL}_n$ which are not isomorphic to an object of the form $Y^{\oplus c}$ for a basic object $Y$ corresponding to $\mathrm{Res}_{E/F}(\mathrm{GL}_{s'})$ for $cs' = s$ with $c>1$. Indeed, such an $X$ would have $z_{X}$ valued in the non-elliptic twisted Levi subgroup $\mathrm{Res}_{E/F}(\mathrm{GL}_{s'})^{c}$.

By [@Kalannals Corollary 5.4], for a twisted Levi subgroup $M \subset \mathrm{GL}_n$, we have a bijection $$\label{TNM}
    H^1_{\mathop{\mathrm{bas}}}(\mathcal{E}_{\mathop{\mathrm{Kal}}}, M) \cong X^{\ast}(\pi_0(Z(\widehat{\bar{M}})^+))=X^{\ast}(Z(\widehat{\bar{M}})^+)_{\mathop{\mathrm{tor}}} ,$$ where $Z(\widehat{\bar{M}})^+$ is the pre-image of $Z(\widehat{M})^{\Gamma_{\overline{F}/F}}$ in $\widehat{\bar{M}} \cong \widehat{M}_{\mathop{\mathrm{sc}}} \times \varprojlim\limits_{z \mapsto z^n} Z(\widehat{M})^{\circ}$. If $M$ is elliptic then $$M \cong \mathrm{Res}_{E/F} \mathrm{GL}_{s},$$ for some extension $E/F$ of degree $m$ such that $n=ms$. Then $Z(\widehat{M})^{\Gamma_{\overline{F}/F}} \cong \mathbb{C}^{\times}$ and $Z(\widehat{\bar{M}})^+$ is isomorphic to the subgroup of $(\mu_s)^m \times (\varprojlim\limits_{z \mapsto z^n} \mathbb{C}^{\times})^m$ given by $((a_1,\dots,a_m), (b_1, \dots , b_m))$ such that $a_1\overline{b_1}= \dots =a_m\overline{b_m}$ where $\overline{b_i}$ is the projection of $b_i$ to the final $\mathbb{C}^{\times}$. Then we have the Cartesian diagram $$\begin{tikzcd}
Z(\widehat{\bar{M}})^+ \arrow[r] \arrow[d] & (\mu_s)^m \times \varprojlim\limits_{z \mapsto z^n} Z(\widehat{M}) \arrow[d]  \\
Z(\widehat{M})^{\Gamma_{\overline{F}/{F}}} \arrow[r] & Z(\widehat{M})
    \end{tikzcd}$$ Passing to characters, we have the identifications $$X^{\ast}(Z(\widehat{M})^{\Gamma_{\overline{F}/F}}) = X^{\ast}(Z(\widehat{M}))_{\Gamma_{\overline{F}/F}} = X^{\ast}(Z(\widehat{M}))/ X^{\ast}(Z(\widehat{M}))_0,$$ where $X^{\ast}(Z(\widehat{M}))_0$ is generated by $\gamma(x) - x$ for $\gamma \in \Gamma_{\overline{F}/F}$ and $x \in X^{\ast}(Z(\widehat{M}))$ or equivalently the augmentation ideal under identification of $X^{\ast}(Z(\widehat{M}))$ with $\mathbb{Z}[\Gamma_{E/F}]$ (or $\mathbb{Z}[\Gamma_{\overline{F}/E} \backslash \Gamma_{\overline{F}/F}] = \mathop{\mathrm{Hom}}_{F}(E,\overline{F})$ if $E/F$ is not Galois). Further, note that $X^{\ast}(\varprojlim\limits_{z \mapsto z^n} Z(\widehat{M})) = X^{\ast}(Z(\widehat{M}))_{\mathbb{Q}}$.

Thus we finally have that $$X^{\ast}(Z(\widehat{\bar{M}})^+) \cong [(\mathbb{Z}/s\mathbb{Z})^m \times X^{\ast}(Z(\widehat{M}))_{\mathbb{Q}}] / X^{\ast}(Z(\widehat{M}))_0,$$ and hence $$X^{\ast}(Z(\widehat{\bar{M}})^+)_{\mathop{\mathrm{tor}}} \cong [(\mathbb{Z}/s\mathbb{Z})^m \times X^{\ast}(Z(\widehat{M}))_{\mathbb{Q}, 0}] / X^{\ast}(Z(\widehat{M}))_0.$$

The inflation-restriction exact sequence [@Kalannals (3.5)] $$1 \rightarrow H^1(F, M) \rightarrow H^1_{\mathop{\mathrm{bas}}}(\mathcal{E}_{\mathop{\mathrm{Kal}}}, M) \rightarrow \mathop{\mathrm{Hom}}(u,Z(M))^{\Gamma_{\overline{F}/F}},$$ and vanishing of the first term implies that $H^1_{\mathop{\mathrm{bas}}}(\mathcal{E}_{\mathop{\mathrm{Kal}}}, M) \cong X^{\ast}(Z(\widehat{\bar{M}})^{\Gamma_{\overline{F}/F}})$ embeds into $\mathop{\mathrm{Hom}}(u,Z(M))^{\Gamma_{\overline{F}/F}}$, which admits the follow chain of identifications $$\cong \mathop{\mathrm{Hom}}(X^{\ast}(Z(M)), X^{\ast}(u))^{\Gamma_{\overline{F}/F}} = \mathop{\mathrm{Hom}}(X^{\ast}(Z(M)) , \mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}) \cong X^{\ast}(Z(\widehat{M}))_{\mathbb{Q}} / X^{\ast}(Z(\widehat{M})) \cong (\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z})^m.$$ We claim that the map $$/ X^{\ast}(Z(\widehat{M}))_0 \rightarrow X^{\ast}(Z(\widehat{M}))_{\mathbb{Q}} / X^{\ast}(Z(\widehat{M}))$$ is given by $(a_i, b_i) \mapsto \frac{b_i}{s} - \frac{a_i}{s}$---it is easy to check the image is $[(\mathbb{Q}^m)_0 + (\mathbb{Z}/s\mathbb{Z})^m]/\mathbb{Z}^m \subset (\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z})^m$.

::: proof
*Proof of claim.* To verify that the map has the above claimed form, we check at a finite $k$-level and compose with the intermediate map from the proof of [@DS24 Proposition 4.21] $$\label{intermedeq}
    H^1(\mathcal{E}_{\mathop{\mathrm{Kal}}}, Z_{M,k} \to M) \to \mathop{\mathrm{Hom}}(X^{*}(Z_{M,k}), \mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}) \to \mathop{\mathrm{Hom}}(X^{*}(Z(M)), \mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}),$$ where $Z_{M,k}$ is the subgroup of $Z(M)$ consisting of all those elements whose $k$th power lies in $Z(M_{\text{der}})$. We claim the middle group is canonically identified with the character group of the kernel of the map $\widehat{M/Z_{M,k}} \to \widehat{M}$, denoted by $K$, which is isomorphic to $$\frac{(\mathbb{Z}/s\mathbb{Z})^{m} \times \mathbb{Z}^{m}}{k\mathbb{Z}^{m}},$$ where the embedding of $k\mathbb{Z}^{m}$ is given by $(a_{1}, \dots, a_{m}) \mapsto (\overline{a_{1}/k}, \dots, \overline{a_{m}/k}) \times (a_{1}, \dots, a_{m})$. Granting this, the first map in [\[intermedeq\]](#intermedeq){reference-type="eqref" reference="intermedeq"} is the one induced by the inclusion $$(\mathbb{Z}/s\mathbb{Z})^{m} \times (\mathbb{Z}^{m})_{0} \hookrightarrow (\mathbb{Z}/s\mathbb{Z})^{m} \times \mathbb{Z}^{m}.$$ Finally, we compute the right-hand map of [\[intermedeq\]](#intermedeq){reference-type="eqref" reference="intermedeq"} and prove the claim by writing out the explicit identification $$X^{*}(K) \xrightarrow{\sim} \mathop{\mathrm{Hom}}(X^{*}(Z_{M,k}), \mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}).$$ First, set $T$ to be a maximal torus of $M$, and $Z(M_{1}) := Z(M)/Z(M_{\text{der}})$. Then we have the exact sequence $$0 \rightarrow Z_{M,k} \rightarrow T \rightarrow T/Z_{M,k} \rightarrow 0,$$ and isomorphism $$T/Z_{M,k} \cong T_{\mathop{\mathrm{ad}}} \times Z(M_1)/Z(M_1)[k],$$ as in [@Kaletha18 p. 71]. Thus, we have the exact sequence $$\label{annoyingSESbis}
   0 \to X^{*}(T_{\mathop{\mathrm{ad}}}) \oplus X^{*}(\frac{Z(M_{1})}{Z(M_{1})[k]}) \to X^{*}(T) \to  X^{*}(Z_{M,k})  \to 0.$$

Now pick an embedding $\widehat{T} \hookrightarrow \widehat{M}$ and we denote the image also by $\widehat{T}$. We now apply the functor $\mathop{\mathrm{Hom}}_{\mathbb{Z}}(-,\mathbb{Z})$ to [\[annoyingSESbis\]](#annoyingSESbis){reference-type="eqref" reference="annoyingSESbis"}, omitting the first term, and via the injective resolution $\mathbb{Z}\to \mathbb{Q}\to \mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}$, identifying $\text{Ext}^{1}_{\mathbb{Z}}(X^{*}(Z_{M,k}), \mathbb{Z})$ with $\mathop{\mathrm{Hom}}(X^{*}(Z_{M,k}),\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z})$, yields the exact sequence $$\label{annoyingSES}
X^{*}(\widehat{T}) \to X^{*}(\widehat{T}_{\mathop{\mathrm{der}}}) \oplus X^{*}(Z(\widehat{M})) \to \mathop{\mathrm{Hom}}(X^{*}(Z_{M,k}), \mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}) \to 0,$$ where the first map is restriction to $\widehat{T}_{\mathop{\mathrm{der}}}$ plus the restriction along $Z(\widehat{M}) \xrightarrow{[k]} Z(\widehat{M}) \rightarrow \widehat{T}$ (using that $\widehat{Z(M_1)} = Z(\widehat{M})^{\circ} = Z(\widehat{M})$). It suffices to compute the explicit description of the second map in [\[annoyingSES\]](#annoyingSES){reference-type="eqref" reference="annoyingSES"}, which maps $X^{*}(K)$ to $\mathop{\mathrm{Hom}}(X^{*}(Z_{M,k}), \mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z})$ and is a connecting homomorphism.

We choose the standard identification of $X^{*}(\widehat{T}) = X^{*}((\mathbb{C}^{\times,s})^{m})$ with $(\mathbb{Z}^{s})^{m}$, so that $X^{*}(\widehat{T}_{\mathop{\mathrm{der}}}) = (\mathbb{Z}^{s}/\Delta(\mathbb{Z}))^{m}$ and $X^{\ast}(Z(\widehat{M})) = (\mathbb{Z}^{s}/(\mathbb{Z}^{s})_{0})^{m}$. We identify the former with $(\mathbb{Z}^{s-1})^{m}$ and the latter with $\mathbb{Z}^{m}$ in the using the maps $((\overline{c_{i,j})_{1 \leq i \leq s}}) \mapsto ((c_{i,j} - c_{s,j})_{1 \leq i \leq s-1})$ and $(\overline{y_{i,j}}) \mapsto (y_{1,j} + \dots + y_{s,j})$, respectively (corresponding to bases $\{e_{1,j}, \dots ,e_{s-1,j}\}_{1 \leq j \leq m}, \{e_{1,j}\}_{1 \leq j \leq m}$). Under this choice of coordinates, the first map explicitly sends $((c_{i,j})_{1 \leq i \leq s})_{1 \leq j \leq m}$ to $(c_{1,j} -c_{s,j}, \dots c_{s-1,j}-c_{s,j},c_{1,j}+ \dots + c_{s,j})_{1 \leq j \leq m}$. Thus, given $(x_{1,j}, \dots x_{s-1,j}, y_{j})_{j} \in X^{*}(\widehat{T}_{\mathop{\mathrm{der}}}) \oplus X^{*}(Z(\widehat{M})),$ we lift it to $X^{*}(\widehat{T})_{\mathbb{Q}}$, yielding $((x_{i,j}+y_{j}/ks - (x_{1,j} + \dots x_{s-1,j})/s, y_{j}/ks - (x_{1,j} + \dots x_{s-1,j})/s)_{1 \leq i \leq s-1})_{j}$ and then take its image in $X^{*}(\widehat{T})_{\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}}$, yielding $$(y_{j}/ks - (x_{1,j} + \dots x_{s-1,j})/s)_{j} \in (\mathbb{Z}/ks\mathbb{Z})^{m} = \mathop{\mathrm{Hom}}((\mathbb{Z}/ks\mathbb{Z})^{m}, \mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}) = \mathop{\mathrm{Hom}}(X^{*}(Z_{M,k}), \mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}).$$

It follows that when we factor the image of $(x_{1,j}, \dots x_{s-1,j}, y_{j})_{j}$ as in the previous paragraph via the second map of [\[annoyingSES\]](#annoyingSES){reference-type="eqref" reference="annoyingSES"} through restriction to $X^{*}(Z(\widehat{M}_{\text{der}})) \oplus X^{*}(Z(\widehat{M}))$, we obtain $$(x_{1,j} + \dots + x_{s-1,j}, y_{j})_{j} \in X^{*}(Z(\widehat{M}_{\text{der}})) \oplus X^{*}(Z(\widehat{M})) \mapsto (y_{j}/ks - (x_{1,j} + \dots x_{s-1,j})/s)_{j},$$ which is exactly the claimed map. ◻
:::

It remains to construct elements $X$ of $\mathrm{RigIsoc}_{F}$ with cocycle $z_X$ factoring through $M \xrightarrow{\sim} \mathrm{Res}_{E/F} \mathrm{GL}_s$ and basic for that group with gradings matching the above slopes. Note that since there is an injection into $\mathop{\mathrm{Hom}}(u,Z(M))^{\Gamma_{\overline{F}/F}}$, we can identify the class of a basic object from just its grading.

::: ex
[]{#objex label="objex"} We consider the case where $E/F$ is unramified of degree $m$. Suppose the image of the slope map is the class of $(a_{\sigma}/r)_{\sigma}$ for some $r$ divisible by $s$ and $a_{\sigma} \in \mathbb{Z}$ and $\sigma \in \Gamma_{E/F}$ such that $\sum\limits_{\sigma \in \Gamma_{E/F}} a_{\sigma}/r = b/s$ for some $b \in \mathbb{Z}$.

We first analyze the case where $b=0$. Up to modifying $(a_{\sigma}/r)_{\sigma}$ by an element of $\mathbb{Z}^m$, we may assume that the $a_{\sigma}/r$ satisfy $a_{\sigma} \neq a_{\sigma'}$ for $\sigma \neq \sigma'$. Now we define the $L_E$ vector space $V$ such that its $\gamma \cdot (a_{\sigma})_{\sigma}$-graded piece is $s$-dimensional, for each $\gamma \in \Gamma_{E/F}$. Let $\alpha = \sum\limits_{\sigma} a_{\sigma}/r \in \mathbb{Z}$ and define $\tilde{\Phi}$ such that $\mathfrak{f}$ acts semilinearly, permutes the grading by $\mathfrak{f}$, and scales a basis of the $(a_{\sigma})_{\sigma}$-graded piece by $\varpi^{\alpha}$. Then $\mathfrak{f}_E$ scales a basis of $V$ by $\varpi^{\alpha}$ and hence $\mathfrak{f}^r_E$ scales by $r\alpha = \sum\limits_{\sigma} a_{\sigma}$. To get an object of $R\mathcal{I}_{E/F, r}$, we then tensor by $R_{E/F}$.

We now move to the case where $b \neq 0$. We let $K/E$ be the splitting field of a degree $r$ totally ramified extension and we will construct an object in $R\mathcal{I}_{K/F, r}$. We lift the grading $(a_{\sigma}/r)_{\sigma}$ to $K$. Namely we have a tuple $\mu \in (\frac{1}{r} \mathbb{Z}/ \mathbb{Z})^{\Gamma_{K/F}}$ with some multiple $rk$-many copies of each $a_{\sigma}/r$. Then $\Gamma_{K/E}$ permutes the tuple trivially. Observe that now the sum $\alpha$ over all entries of $\mu$ is an integer. We again define $V$ to be an $L_K$ vector space with $\gamma \cdot \mu$-graded piece $s$-dimensional. Define $\tilde{\Phi}$ such that $\mathfrak{f}$ permutes the graded pieces and scales a basis of $\mu$ by $\prod\limits_{\sigma \in \Gamma_{K/F}} \sigma(\varpi_K)^{a_{\sigma}k}$. Then $\mathfrak{f}_E$ scales a basis of $V$ by an appropriate power of $\mathfrak{f}$ applied to $\prod\limits_{\sigma \in \Gamma_{K/F}} \sigma(\varpi_K)^{a_{\sigma}k}$ and $\mathfrak{f}^r_E$ scales by $\prod\limits_{\sigma \in \Gamma_{K/F}} \sigma(\varpi_K)^{a_{\sigma}rk}$. Finally, we tensor with $R_{K/F}$. The case for a general Galois $E/F$ is similar.
:::

::: lem
Given $s \in \mathbb{N}$, $E/F$ Galois, and $(a_{i}) \in (\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z})^{\Gamma_{E/F}}$ with $\sum a_{i} \in \frac{1}{s}\mathbb{Z}/\mathbb{Z}$, the object $X$ constructed in Example [\[objex\]](#objex){reference-type="ref" reference="objex"} is simple if and only if $\sum a_{i}$ is coprime to $s$.
:::

::: proof
*Proof.* If $c$ is the greatest common divisor of $\sum a_{i}$ and $s$ then $\sum a_{i} \in \frac{1}{s/c}\mathbb{Z}/\mathbb{Z}$, and so Example [\[objex\]](#objex){reference-type="ref" reference="objex"} also yields an object $Y$ with $z_{Y}$ factoring through $\mathrm{Res}_{E/F}(\mathrm{GL}_{s/c})$, and each $\gamma \cdot (a_{\sigma})$-graded summand of $X$ is the $c$-fold sum of the $\gamma \cdot (a_{\sigma})$-graded summand of $Y$, showing that $X$ cannot be simple if $c>1$. Conversely, if $\sum a_{\sigma}$ is coprime to $s$ then each $\gamma \cdot (a_{\sigma})$-graded summand of $X$ is simple as an $E$-isocrystal, and is a fortiori simple in $\mathrm{RigIsoc}_{F}$. ◻
:::

Continuing to take $G = \mathrm{GL}_{n}$, for fixed $[z_{X}] \in H^{1}_{\text{alg}}(\mathcal{E}_{\mathop{\mathrm{Kal}}}, G)$ we proved that one can find a representative $z_{X}$ such that $\nu_{X}$ is defined over $F$ and $Z_{G}(\nu_{X}) = M \xrightarrow{\sim} \mathrm{Res}_{E/F}(\mathrm{GL}_{s})$ with $E/F$ finite Galois and $X$ simple. Let us see how unique such an $M$ arising from $[z_{X}]$ is. The above work shows that the representative $z_{X}$ with $F$-rational $\nu_{X}$ is unique up to translating by a $g$-coboundary with $g \in G(\overline{F})$ such that $dg \in Z^{1}(F, M_{\mathop{\mathrm{ad}}})$, which replaces $M$ by $\mathop{\mathrm{Int}}(g)(M)$ (we call any $M'$ which is conjugate to $M$ by such an element $g$ a stable conjugate of $M$). Since we insist that $M \cong \mathrm{Res}_{E/F}(\mathrm{GL}_{s})$---a quasi-split group---and any other such $M' \cong \mathrm{Res}_{E'/F}(\mathrm{GL}_{s'})$ is an inner form of $M$ via the twisting isomorphism $\mathop{\mathrm{Int}}(g)$---two such $M$ and $M'$ are conjugate by such a $g$ if and only if $s = s'$ and $E = E'$ (see Remark [\[Galrem\]](#Galrem){reference-type="ref" reference="Galrem"} for more details). In particular, $M$ is completely determined, and thus $z_{X}$ is as well up to translating by $g$-coboundaries for $g \in N_{G}(M)(\overline{F})$ mapping into $[N_{G}(M)/M](F)$.

::: rem
[]{#Galrem label="Galrem"} If $E/F$ and $E'/F$ are finite and separable extensions then the twisted Levi subgroups $\mathrm{Res}_{E/F}(\mathrm{GL}_{s}) \xrightarrow{\sim} M$ and $\mathrm{Res}_{E'/F}(\mathrm{GL}_{s'}) \xrightarrow{\sim} M'$ of $\mathrm{GL}_{n}$ are stably conjugate if and only if $s = s'$, $E$, $E'$ are isomorphic as extensions of $F$, which implies they have the same Galois closure $\widetilde{E}$. The proof of [@FintzenSchwein Lemma 4.3.4] shows that $M$ and $M'$ are constructed by twisting a Levi subgroup $M_{0}$ of $G$ by two cocycles $z_{M}$ and $z_{M'}$ representing a fixed class in $H^{1}(F,W_{0})$, where $W_{0} := N_{G}(M_{0})/M_{0}$. Since $M \xrightarrow{\sim} \mathrm{Res}_{E/F}(\mathrm{GL}_{s})$, the cocycle $z_{M}$ is the homomorphism $\Gamma_{\overline{F}/F} \to S_{[E \colon F]}$ induced by the action of $\Gamma_{\overline{F}/F}$ on $\mathop{\mathrm{Hom}}_{F}(E,\overline{F})$---in particular, it has kernel $\Gamma_{\overline{F}/\widetilde{E}}$, similarly for $z_{M'}$. Since $z_{M}$ is cohomologous to $z_{M'}$, these two homomorphisms are conjugate, meaning that the $\Gamma_{\overline{F}}$-sets $\mathop{\mathrm{Hom}}(E, \overline{F})$ and $\mathop{\mathrm{Hom}}(E', \overline{F})$ are isomorphic. It follows from Grothendieck's Galois theory that $E$ and $E'$ are isomorphic as field extensions as desired.
:::

For $E/F$ Galois, after making the identification $$(F) \xrightarrow{\sim} \Gamma_{E/F},$$ the observations in the paragraph preceding Remark [\[Galrem\]](#Galrem){reference-type="ref" reference="Galrem"} are reflected by the fact that, for the tuple $(a_{i}) \in (\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z})^{\Gamma_{E/F}}$ with $\sum a_{i} \in \frac{1}{s}\mathbb{Z}/\mathbb{Z}$ corresponding to $X$, we obtain an isomorphic object if $(a_{i})$ is replaced by any of its $\Gamma_{E/F}$-translates. In fact, another such tuple $(b_{i})$ gives an object isomorphic to $X$ in $\mathrm{RigIsoc}_{F}$ if and only if they are in the same $\Gamma_{E/F}$-orbit. We conclude that:

::: thm
1.  For $E/F$ Galois, the isomorphism classes of simple objects $X$ in $\mathrm{RigIsoc}_{F}$ with $Z_{G}(\nu_{X}) \xrightarrow{\sim} \mathrm{Res}_{E/F}(\mathrm{GL}_{s})$ are in bijection with $\Gamma_{E/F}$-orbits of tuples $(a_{i}) \in (\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z})^{\Gamma_{E/F}}$ with $\sum a_{i} \in \frac{1}{s}\mathbb{Z}/\mathbb{Z}$, $(\sum a_{i},s) =1$ which are not fixed by any subgroup $H \subseteq \Gamma_{E/F}$ with $H \neq \{1\}$.

2.  More generally, for $E/F$ separable with Galois closure $\widetilde{E}$, the isomorphism classes of simple objects $X$ in $\mathrm{RigIsoc}_{F}$ with $Z_{G}(\nu_{X}) \xrightarrow{\sim} \mathrm{Res}_{E'/F}(\mathrm{GL}_{s})$ for $E'$ the image of an $F$-linear embedding of $E$ in $\overline{F}$ are in bijection with $\Gamma_{\widetilde{E}/F}$-orbits of pairs $(E', (a_{i}))$ consisting of such a field $E'$ and a tuple $(a_{i}) \in (\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z})^{\mathop{\mathrm{Hom}}_{F}(E',\widetilde{E})}$ with $\sum a_{i} \in \frac{ 1}{s }\mathbb{Z}/\mathbb{Z}$, $(\sum a_{i},s) = 1$ which are not induced by an element of $(\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z})^{\mathop{\mathrm{Hom}}_{F}(K,\widetilde{E})}$ for $F/K/E'$ a proper subextension.

In particular, (2) gives a complete classification of the simple objects of $\mathrm{RigIsoc}_{F}$.
:::

::: proof
*Proof.* We give the proof of (1)---the proof of (2) is similar after applying Remark [\[Galrem\]](#Galrem){reference-type="ref" reference="Galrem"}.

The description of all simple objects whose isomorphism classes are in the image of $H^{1}(\mathcal{E}_{\mathop{\mathrm{Kal}}}, M)$ and have Newton centralizer exactly $M$ as the above-described tuples in $(\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z})^{\Gamma_{E/F}}$ is obtained by combining the explicit computation of the map [\[TNM\]](#TNM){reference-type="eqref" reference="TNM"} given above with the observation that if $H \subseteq \Gamma_{E/F}$ fixes $(a_{i})$ then the corresponding simple object $X$ has $\mathrm{Res}_{E^{H}/F}(\mathrm{GL}_{s \cdot |H|}) \subseteq Z_{G}(\nu_{X})$. The fact that two such elements have the same image in $H^{1}(\mathcal{E}_{\mathop{\mathrm{Kal}}}, G)$ if and only if they are in the same $\Gamma_{E/F}$-orbit follows from the proof of [@DS24 Proposition 3.9]. ◻
:::
