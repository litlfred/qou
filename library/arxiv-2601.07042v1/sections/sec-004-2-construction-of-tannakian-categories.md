All Tannakian categories constructed in this section are built from the category of isocrystals, whose objects are pairs $(V,\Phi)$, where $V$ is a finite-dimensional $L$-vector space and $\Phi$ is a $\mathfrak{f}_{F}$-semilinear automorphism and whose morphisms are $\Phi$-equivariant linear maps. For a fixed $s \in \mathbb{N}$, one can also consider the full subcategory of all $(V,\Phi)$ which have a basis on which $\mathfrak{f}_{F}^{s}$ acts on each basis element by a (possibly different) power of $\varpi_{F}$.
