EVALUATION OF STATE INTEGRALS AT RATIONAL POINTS
STAVROS GAROUFALIDIS AND RINAT KASHAEV
Abstract. Multi-dimensional state-integrals of products of Faddeev’s quantum diloga-
rithms arise frequently in Quantum Topology, quantum Teichm¨uller theory and complex
Chern–Simons theory. Using the quasi-periodicity property of the quantum dilogarithm, we
evaluate 1-dimensional state-integrals at rational points and express the answer in terms of
the Rogers dilogarithm, the cyclic (quantum) dilogarithm and ﬁnite state-sums at roots of
unity. We illustrate our results with the evaluation of the state-integrals of the 41, 52 and
(−2, 3, 7) pretzel knots at rational points.
