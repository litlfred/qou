::: remark []{#rem.algebraic label="rem.algebraic"} It [@GZ1] (see also [@DG2]) it was observed that although $(G^+(\theta_+))^N$ and $(\slashed{\operatorname{D}}_N(\theta_+,\zeta_N))^N$ lie in the field $F_{G,N}=\mathbbm Q(\theta_+,\zeta_N)$, their ratio lies in the smaller field $\mathbbm Q(z,\zeta_N)$ (where $z$ satisfies $\operatorname{g}(z)=1$) which is an extension of $\mathbbm Q(z)$ by $\zeta_N$.
