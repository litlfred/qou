## The rank of $V$: part (a) of Theorem [\[thm.1\]](#thm.1){reference-type="ref" reference="thm.1"} {#sub.rankV}

An application of the `HolonomicFunctions.m` computer algebra package [@Kou] implies that the linear $q$-difference operators corresponding to the recursions of Equations [\[eq.rec1\]](#eq.rec1){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq.rec1"} and [\[eq.rec2\]](#eq.rec2){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq.rec2"} is a Gröbner basis and the corresponding module has rank $2$. Said differently, $f(m,e)$ is a unique $\mathbb Z[q^{\pm 1/2}]$ linear combination of $A$ and $B$ where $A=f(0,0)$ and $B=f(0,1)$.
