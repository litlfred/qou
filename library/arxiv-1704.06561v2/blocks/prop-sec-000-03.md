Till now the inclusion of QED eﬀects in lattice QCD simulations has been carried out follow- ing mainly two methods: in the ﬁrst one QED is added directly to the action and QED+QCD simulations are performed at few values of the electric charge (see, e.g., Ref. [9, 14]), while the second one, the RM123 approach of Ref. [8], consists in an expansion of the lattice path-integral in powers of the two small parameters ( bmd −bmu) and αem, namely αem ≈( bmd −bmu)/ΛQCD ≈1%.
