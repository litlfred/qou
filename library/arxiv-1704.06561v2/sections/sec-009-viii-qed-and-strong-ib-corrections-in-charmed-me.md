In this section using the RM123 approach we address the evaluation of the leading-order e.m. and
strong IB corrections to the D-meson mass splitting (MD+ −MD0), and the determination of the
leading-order e.m. corrections to the D-meson mass combination (MD+ + MD0) and to the Ds-
meson mass MD+
s . In the case of D-meson mass splitting we make use of the determination (66) of
the u- and d-quark mass diﬀerence done in the kaon sector (see Section VI) to evaluate the strong
IB correction and therefore to predict for the ﬁrst time the physical mass splitting (MD+ −MD0)
on the lattice.
