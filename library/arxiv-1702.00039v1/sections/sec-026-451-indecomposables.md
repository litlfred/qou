Why did we us the letter I to denote the set {R,B s,B r,B sr,B rs,B srs}? Be-
cause they are indecomposable objects. Let us see why.
We will use the following notation. A min in the subindex of a graded object
means the minimal i for which its degree i part is non-zero. For example
(Bs)min = (Bs)−1
As we have seen, every element M∈I is generated as an R-bimodule by the
element 1⊗∈ Mmin. Let us suppose that M = N⊕P . Then we have that
Mmin =Nmin⊕Pmin asR-vector spaces, but the dimension of Mmin overR is
one, so Mmin is either Nmin or Pmin and thus M is either N or P . Thus M is
indecomposable.
Caution! It is a particularity of the group S3 that all the indecomposable Soergel
bimodules are generated by 1⊗. For example in S4 the indecomposable Bs2s1s3s2 is
not generated by 1⊗.
