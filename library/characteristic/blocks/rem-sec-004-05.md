Conjecture 1 may be translated as an equality of two polynomials with two commuting variables and integer coeﬃcients; see Conjecture 2 below. Since these polynomials are computable by elimination, it follows that Conjecture 1 is in principle a decidable question. This is in contrast to the Hyperbolic Volume Conjecture (due to Kashaev-Murakami-Murakami; see [Ka, MM]) which involves the existence and identiﬁcation of a limit of complex numbers.
