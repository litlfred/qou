Pure braids
There is a natural group homomorphism π : Bn →Σn which sends a braid to the corresponding
permutation of the marked points. Alternatively, the homomorphism is characterized by the
property that:
σi 7→(i, i + 1).
Deﬁnition 2.12. The pure braid group on n strands, denoted by Pn, is the kernel of the homo-
morphism π : Bn →Σn.
The group Pn consists of those braids such that each strand starts and ends at the same
point. These are closed paths in the conﬁguration space ˆCn(C), and in this way one obtains an
identiﬁcation:
Pn ∼= π1( ˆCn(C), p).
Remark 2.13. Let G be an abelian group. An Eilenberg-Maclane space K(G, n) is a topological
space such that:
• πn(K(G, n)) ∼= G.
• πm(K(G, n)) ∼= {0} for m̸ = n.
Given G and n, the spaces K(G, n) exist and are well deﬁned up to homotopy equivalence. The
conﬁguration spaces of points in the plane are important examples of Eilenberg-Maclane spaces:
Cn(C) ∼= K(Bn, 1).
ˆCn(C) ∼= K(Pn, 1).
