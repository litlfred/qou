We have already encountered an important example of a bialgebra, the universal enveloping algebra U(g) of a Lie algebra g. Recall that the coproduct is the unique algebra homomorphism ∆: U(g) →U(g) ⊗U(g), with the property that ∆(x) = 1 ⊗x + x ⊗1, for any x ∈g. The bialgebra U(g) is cocommutative but in general it is not commutative. Given modules V, W of a bialgebra A, the vector space V ⊗W, is also a module of A with action given by: a(v ⊗w) := ∆(a)(v ⊗w).
