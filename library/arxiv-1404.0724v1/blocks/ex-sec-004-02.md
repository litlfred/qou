The notation Mod(S) for the mapping class group is motivated by the example of the torus T, where: Mod(T) ∼= SL2(Z). The isomorphism is deﬁned by the action of Mod(T) on homology. Deﬁnition 2.8. Let S be a surface and Q ⊂S a ﬁnite set of marked points. We denote by Homeo(S, Q) the group of homeomorphisms of S that ﬁx Q as a set and ﬁx the boundary pointwise. The mapping class group of S, seen as a surface with marked points is Mod(S, Q) := π0(Homeo+(S, ∂S) ∩Homeo(S, Q)).
