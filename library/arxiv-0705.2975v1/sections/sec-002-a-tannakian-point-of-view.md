In this section we shall give another proof of Theorem 2.9 for q-diﬀerence equations in the
analytic situation. Let M(C∗) be the ﬁeld of functions f (x) meromorphic on C∗ = C\{0}
with the automorphism σ(f (x)) = f (qx) where q ∈ C∗ is a ﬁxed complex number with
|q| ⁄= 1. As noted before, the constants CM(C∗) in this situation correspond to meromorphic
functions M(E) on the elliptic curve E = C∗/qZ. We shall show how the theory of tan-
nakian categories also yields a proof of Theorem 2.9 when k = C(x) and K =k(CM(C∗)).

modules ([23], Ch. 1.4) and tannakian categories ([7],[6]; see [24], Appen dix B or [3] for an

Aut⊗(ωM ) ⊗CCK ≃Aut⊗(ωMK ) ⊗CK CK.
The proof is divided in two parts. In the ﬁrst part, we will construct a ﬁber functor ˜ωM

associated to ωM . In the second part, we will compare the Galois group associated to ωMK
and the Galois group associated to ˜ωM , and ﬁnally relate these groups to the Galois groups
considered in Theorem 2.9.b).
