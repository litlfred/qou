On the Deﬁnitions of Diﬀerence Galois Groups
Zo´e Chatzidakis∗, Charlotte Hardouin, Michael F. Singer†
April 24, 2006
Abstract
We compare several deﬁnitions of the Galois group of a linear diﬀerence equation
that have arisen in algebra, analysis and model theory and show, that these groups
are isomorphic over suitable ﬁelds. In addition, we study properties of Picard-Vessiot
extensions over ﬁelds with not necessarily algebraically closed subﬁelds of constants.
