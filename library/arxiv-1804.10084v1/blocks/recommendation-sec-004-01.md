We consider the following quantity: $$\begin{aligned} \mathsf{Var}\big[\sum_{i \in L} X_i \mid X_K=a_K\big] = \sum_{i \in L} \left( \mathsf{Var}[X_i \mid X_K=a_K] + \sum_{j \in L \setminus \{i\}} \mathsf{Cov}[X_i, X_j \mid X_K=a_K] \right). \end{aligned}$$ Variance is always nonnegative, so at least one term of the summation over $i \in L$ must be non-negative: $$\label{eq:1} \mathsf{Var}[X_i \mid X_K=a_K] + \sum_{j \in L \setminus \{i\}} \mathsf{Cov}[X_i, X_j \mid X_K=a_K] \geq 0.$$ Let us denote $\pi_i = \Pr[X_i=1 \mid X_K=a_K]$.
