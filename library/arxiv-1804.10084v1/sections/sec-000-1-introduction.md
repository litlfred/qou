We study the question whether functions of negatively dependent random variables satisfy concen-
tration bounds, similar to functions of independent random variable s. Many tools are known for
functions of independent random variables — e.g., martingales, Talag rand’s inequality, the Kim-Vu
inequality, and the entropy method for self-bounding functions. I t is also known that Chernoﬀ-
Hoeﬀding bounds for (linear functions of) independent random var iables generalize to negatively
dependent variables, assuming a relatively weak property known as negative cylinder dependence.
A natural question therefore arises, whether more sophisticate d concentration bounds also gener-
alize to some form of negative dependence. In this paper, we discus s the question of concentration
bounds for Lipschitz functions 1 on {0, 1}n, under certain forms of negative dependence.
