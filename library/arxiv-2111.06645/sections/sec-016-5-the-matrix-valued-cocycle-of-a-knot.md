The matrix-valued cocycle of a knot
In this section, we extend the observations of the previous sections to matrix-valued analytic
functions which naturally give rise to a cocycle on on the set of matrix-valued piece-wise analytic
functions on P1(R). What’s more, we conjecture (and in the case of the 41, prove) that this
cocycle, restricted to the rational numbers, exactly agrees with the cocycle of our previous
work [32], which naturally binds the two works together and naturally leads to the concept of
a matrix-valued holomorphic quantum modular form.
