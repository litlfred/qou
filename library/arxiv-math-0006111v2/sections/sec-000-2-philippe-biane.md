Abstract. We prove a factorization-concentration result for characters of s ymmet-
ric groups. This is then applied to the asymptotic behaviour of the decomposition of
the tensor representations. There are connections with the Past ur-Marcenko distri-
bution of random matrix theory, and freely inﬁnitely divisible d istributions.
