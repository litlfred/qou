A crossed groupoid is better known as a strict 2-groupoid, or a crossed module over a groupoid, or a 2-truncated crossed complex; see [Bw]. When Ob(G) is a singleton then G is just a crossed module (or a crossed group). More on this in Remark 3.3. Deﬁnition 1.4. Suppose H = (H1, H2, AdH1↷H2, D) is another crossed groupoid. A morphism of crossed groupoids Φ : G →H is a pair of morphisms of groupoids Φi : Gi →Hi, i = 1, 2, that are equal on objects, and respect the twistings and the feedbacks.
