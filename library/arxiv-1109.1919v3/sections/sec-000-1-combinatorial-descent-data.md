AMNON YEKUTIELI
Abstract. We consider descent data in cosimplicial crossed groupoids. This
is a combinatorial abstraction of the descent data for gerbes in algebraic geom-
etry. The main result is this: a weak equivalence between cosimplicial crossed
groupoids induces a bijection on gauge equivalence classes of descent data.
