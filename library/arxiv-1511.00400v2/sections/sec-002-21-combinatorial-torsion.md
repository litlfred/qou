[]{#sec:comb label="sec:comb"}

For a detailed definition of combinatorial torsion see [@Turaev86; @Milnor66; @Massuyeau; @Nicolaescu]. This section follows mainly [@Memoirs], in particular its convention with the power $(-1)^{i+1}$ instead of $(-1)^{i}$ for the alternated product. (See Remark [\[rem:convention\]](#rem:convention){reference-type="ref" reference="rem:convention"} on this convention.) The definition is given for chain and for cochain complexes, in a way that both homology and cohomology give the same definition of the torsion of a manifold.
