This subsection recalls the basic properties of combinatorial torsion, the main one being Mayer-Vietoris, useful for cut and paste. Bundles over $S^1$ are also considered, in particular instead of just the torsion it is more convenient to consider the twisted Alexander polynomial, which is a torsion by [@Kitano96]. The subsection finishes with examples.
