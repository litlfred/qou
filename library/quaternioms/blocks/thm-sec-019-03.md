For right O-modules HA, HB, HC, there is no parenthesization-independent canonical isomorphism (HA ⊗HB) ⊗HC ∼= HA ⊗ (HB ⊗HC) unless one restricts to an associative subalgebra. Proof idea: Appendix B.
