Let {1, e1, . . . , e7} be the standard octonion basis with e2
k = −1 and eiej = ±ek ac-
cording to the oriented triples encoded by the Fano plane (See Figure 1). Any three units
on a directed line multiply associatively and obey eiej = ek, ejek = ei, ekei = ej (and
the negatives for reversed order). This diagrammatic calculus is indispensable for track-
ing when associators vanish and for designing sequences that remain within (or near)
associative triples.
