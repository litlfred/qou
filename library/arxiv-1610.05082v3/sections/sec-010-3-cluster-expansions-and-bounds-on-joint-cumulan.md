[]{#sec:cluster label="sec:cluster"}

The cluster expansion is a powerful tool in statistical mechanics, which consists in studying a system in terms of macroscopic geometrical objects instead of considering its original microscopic components. It was introduced in a work of Mayer and Montroll [@Mayer] studying molecular distribution and has since been used in several other topics; for the Ising model, see for example [@Farrell] or Chapter $5$ of [@Velenik]. In this section, we will use the cluster expansion in three different regimes of the Ising model to prove the bounds on joint cumulants of Theorem [\[thm:bound_joint_cumulants\]](#thm:bound_joint_cumulants){reference-type="ref" reference="thm:bound_joint_cumulants"}. This will later be useful to apply the theory of weighted dependency graphs. Theorem [\[thm:bound_joint_cumulants\]](#thm:bound_joint_cumulants){reference-type="ref" reference="thm:bound_joint_cumulants"} is proved in [\[SectBoundHT,SectBoundLT,SectBoundMF\]](#SectBoundHT,SectBoundLT,SectBoundMF){reference-type="ref" reference="SectBoundHT,SectBoundLT,SectBoundMF"}, depending on the considered regime.

::: remark
As already mentioned, cluster expansion is a key step to obtain bounds for cumulants in each of the three regimes. Therefore we will use each time classical notation for cluster expansion, such as $\Xi$, $\mathop{\mathrm{wt}}$, ... Note however that these quantities may have different meanings in different regimes. Since they are only used for the proof of [\[thm:bound_joint_cumulants\]](#thm:bound_joint_cumulants){reference-type="ref" reference="thm:bound_joint_cumulants"} and since the proofs in the different regimes are independent from each other, this should not create any difficulty.
:::
