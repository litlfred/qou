Therefore using notation of [@Velenik], $$\Xi^A_{\Lambda;\beta,0} = 1 + \sum_{r \ge 1} \frac{1}{r!} \sum_{(E_1,B_1), \dots, (E_r,B_r) \atop \text{connected}} \ \prod_{i=1}^r \mathop{\mathrm{wt}}(E_i,B_i) \, \prod_{1 \le i<j \le r} \delta\big[(E_i,B_i),(E_j,B_j) \big],$$ where $\delta\big[(E_i,B_i),(E_j,B_j) \big]=1$ if the graphs corresponding to $(E_i,B_i)$ and $(E_j,B_j)$ do not share a vertex, and $0$ otherwise (this factor encodes the fact that connected components should not intersect).
