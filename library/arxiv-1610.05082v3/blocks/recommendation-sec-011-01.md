\end{aligned}$$ This gives the following expression for $Z^A_{\Lambda; \beta,0}$: $$Z^A_{\Lambda; \beta,0}= (\cosh\, \beta)^{|\mathcal{E}_\Lambda|} \left(\prod_{j=1}^r \cosh(t_j) \right) \sum_{\omega \in \Omega_{\Lambda}} \left[ \sum_{E \subseteq \mathcal{E}_\Lambda} \prod_{\{i,j\} \in E} (\sigma_i \sigma_j \tanh(\beta)) \right]\\ \cdot\left[ \sum_{B \subseteq A} \prod_{x_j \in B} (\sigma_{x_j} \tanh(t_j)) \right].$$ Changing the order of summation we should evaluate, for $E \subseteq \mathcal{E}_\Lambda$ and $B \subseteq A$, the quantity $$\sum_{\omega \in \Omega_{\Lambda}} \prod_{\{i,j\} \in E} (\sigma_i \sigma_j) \prod_{x_j \in B} (\sigma_{x_j}).$$ By an easy symmetry argument, this sum is zero unless all $\sigma_i$'s appear an even number of times, which corresponds to the condition $(E,B) \in \mathcal{E}^{\text{even}}_{\Lambda,A}$.
