Let L = Liek(X|S) be an arbitrary Lie algebra with generators X and deﬁning relations
S and U(L) = k⟨X|S(−)⟩ the universal enveloping associative algebra of L. Then
(i) S is a Lie GS basis ⇔ S is an associative GS basis.
(ii) In this case, a linear basis of U(L) is
u1u2 · · · ut, u 1 ≼ u2 ≼ · · · ≼ ut (lex-order), u i ∈ Irr (S) ∩ ALSW (X).
One uses Shirshov factorization theorem:
u ∈ X ∗, ∃! u = u1 · · · ut, u 1 ≼ · · · ≼ ut, u i ∈ ALSW (X).
