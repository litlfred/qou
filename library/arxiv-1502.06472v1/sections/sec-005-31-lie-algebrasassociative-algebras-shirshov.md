Let $L=Lie_k(X|S)$ be an arbitrary Lie algebra with generators $X$ and defining relations $S$ and $U(L)=k\langle X|S^{(-)}\rangle$ the universal enveloping associative algebra of $L$. Then

1.  $S$ is a Lie GS basis $\Leftrightarrow$ $S$ is an associative GS basis.

2.  In this case, a linear basis of $U(L)$ is $$\begin{aligned}
    u_1u_2\cdots u_t,\ \ u_1\preceq u_2\preceq\cdots\preceq u_t\ \mbox{
    (lex-order)},\ \  u_i\in Irr(S)\cap ALSW(X).
    \end{aligned}$$

One uses Shirshov factorization theorem: $$u\in X^*,\ \exists! \ u=u_1\cdots u_t,\ \ u_1\preceq \cdots\preceq
u_t,\  u_i\in  ALSW(X).$$
