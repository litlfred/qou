Pre-Lie algebras are deﬁned by an identity ( x, y, z ) = ( x, z, y ), where ( x, y, z ) = ( xy)z −
x(yz).
Let X = {xi|i ∈ I} be a linear basis of the Lie algebra L and [ xi, x j] = ∑ α t
ijxt =:
{xi, x j} the multiplication table of the linear basis X. Then L has a presentation
L = Lie(xi, i ∈ I|[xi, x j] = {xi, x j}, i, j ∈ I) = Lie(X|S)
and the universal enveloping pre-Lie algebra of L
Upre-Lie(L) = pre-Lie( X|S(−)).
Then S(−) is a GS basis of Upre-Lie(L), L ⊂ Upre-Lie(L) and Irr (S) is a linear basis of
Upre-Lie(L) by Composition-Diamond lemma for pre-Lie algebras (Bokut-Chen- Li [18]).
