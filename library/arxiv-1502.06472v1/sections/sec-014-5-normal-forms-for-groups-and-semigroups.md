--One-relator groups

In (Chen-Zhong [@{[CZhong11]}]), some one-relator groups were studying by means of groups with the standard normal forms (the standard GS bases) in the sense (Bokut [@{[Bok66]}; @{[Bok67]}]). It is known that any one-relator group can be effectively embedded into 2-generator one-relator group $G=gp\langle x,y| x^{i_1}y^{j_1}\dots
x^{i_k}y^{j_k}\rangle,\ k\geq 1$ is the depth. It is proved that a group $G$ of depth $\leq 3$ is computably embeddable into a Magnus-Moldavanskii tower of HNN-extensions with the standard normal form of elements. There are quite a lot of examples that support an old conjecture that the result is valid for any depth.
