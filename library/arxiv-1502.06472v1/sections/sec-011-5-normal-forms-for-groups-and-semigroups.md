G = gp⟨x, y |xi1yj1 . . . x ik yjk⟩, k ≥ 1 is the depth. It is proved that a group G of depth ≤ 3
is computably embeddable into a Magnus-Moldavanskii tower of HNN- extensions with the
standard normal form of elements. There are quite a lot of example s that support an old
conjecture that the result is valid for any depth.
