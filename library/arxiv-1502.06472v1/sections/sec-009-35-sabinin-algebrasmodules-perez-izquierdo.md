Let $(V,\langle;\rangle)$ be a Sabinin algebra, $$\begin{aligned}
\widetilde{S}(V)&=&T(V)/span\langle xaby-xbay +\sum x_{(1)}\langle
x_{(2)};a,b\rangle y|x,y\in T(V),a,b\in V\rangle\\
&\cong&mod\langle X|I\rangle_{k\langle X\rangle}\ \ \mbox{as }\
k\langle X\rangle\mbox{-modules}
\end{aligned}$$ the universal enveloping module for $V$, where $I=\{xab-xba+\sum
x_{(1)}\langle x_{(2)};a,b\rangle|x\in X^*,a>b, \ a,b\in X\}$.

Then $I$ is a GS basis (Chen-Chen-Zhong [@CCZ]) and then $V\subset \widetilde{S}(V)$.
