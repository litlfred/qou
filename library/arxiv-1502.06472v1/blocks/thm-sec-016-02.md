Every countable diﬀerential algebra is embeddable into a simple 2-generated diﬀerential algebra. G. Bergman (Private communication, 2013 [2]) gave an idea how to avoid the restriction on cardinality of the ground ﬁeld. Now Qiuhui Mo proved that the Bergman’s idea works.
