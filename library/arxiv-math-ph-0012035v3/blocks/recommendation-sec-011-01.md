For our further convenience let us formulate an auxiliary "Schrödinger problem" governing the parallel-transport operator [\[par.trans.\]](#par.trans.){reference-type="ref" reference="par.trans."} for the Abelian gauge potential $A$, $$i\frac {d z}{dt }=-\dot{x}^i A_i z, \label{ab.Schr.eq.}$$ which expresses the fact that the "wave function" $z$ should be covariantly constant along the line $L$, i.e. $$D_t z \stackrel{\rm def}{=} \left( \frac {d}{dt } -i\dot{x}^i A_i \right) z =0,$$ where $D_t$ is the absolute covariant derivative.
