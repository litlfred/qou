Historical remarks
The birth of the ideas related to the (non-)Abelian Stokes theorem dates back
to the ninetenth century and is connected with the emergence of the Abelian
Stokes theorem. The Abelian Stokes theorem can be treated as a prototype of
the non-Abelian Stokes theorem or a version of thereof when we conﬁne ourselves
to an Abelian group.
