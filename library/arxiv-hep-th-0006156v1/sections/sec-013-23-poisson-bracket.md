Two rows of $\chi$'s make perfect Cauchy data $$\chi_{2n}=\chi_{2n,-1}\qquad\qquad
\chi_{2n+1}=\chi_{2n+1,0}\;,$$ just in case one may directly check that the lattice canonical bracket of [@fadVol97] $$\{\chi_{2n\pm1},\chi_{2n}\}=\chi_{2n\pm1}
\chi_{2n}\qquad\{\chi_j,\chi_i\}=0 \mbox{ if }|j-i|\not\equiv1\pmod{N}$$ both a) reproduces itself as the $\chi$'s evolve according to the lattice Liouville equation ([\[lle\]](#lle){reference-type="ref" reference="lle"}) and b) translates, by means of formula ([\[lse\]](#lse){reference-type="ref" reference="lse"}) at $k=0$, into the lattice Magri bracket ([\[mb\]](#mb){reference-type="ref" reference="mb"}$'$).
