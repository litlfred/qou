STRONGLY COUPLED QUANTUM DISCRETE LIOUVILLE
THEORY. I: ALGEBRAIC APPROACH AND DUALITY
L.D. FADDEEV, R.M. KASHAEV, AND A.YU. VOLKOV
Abstract. The quantum discrete Liouville model in the strongly cou-
pled regime, 1 < c < 25, is formulated as a well deﬁned quantum me-
chanical problem with unitary evolution operator. The theory is self-
dual: there are two exponential ﬁelds related by Hermitean conjugation,
satisfying two discrete quantum Liouville equations, and living in mu-
tually commuting subalgebras of the quantum algebra of observables.
