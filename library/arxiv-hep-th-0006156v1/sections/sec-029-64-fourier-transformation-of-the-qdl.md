Certain specializations of $\Psi(u,v,w)$ lead to the following Fourier transformation formulas for the QDL: $$\begin{gathered}
\label{fourier1}
  \lefteqn{\phi_+(w)\equiv \int_{\mathbb R}e_b(x)e^{2\pi i
      wx}\,dx
    =\Psi(0,v,w)\vert_{v\to-\infty}}\\
  =e^{2\pi iwc_b-i\pi(1-4c_b^2)/12}/
  e_b(-w-c_b)=e^{-i\pi w^2+i\pi(1-4c_b^2)/12}
  e_b(w+c_b),
\end{gathered}$$ and $$\begin{gathered}
\label{fourier2}
  \lefteqn{\phi_-(w)\equiv \int_{\mathbb R}(e_b(x))^{-1}e^{2\pi
      iwx}\,dx
    =\Psi(u,0,w)\vert_{u\to-\infty}}\\
  =e^{-2\pi iwc_b+i\pi(1-4c_b^2)/12} e_b(w+c_b)=
  e^{i\pi w^2-i\pi(1-4c_b^2)/12}/e_b(-w-c_b),
\end{gathered}$$ The corresponding inverse transformations read: $$\label{finv}
(e_b(x))^{\pm1}=\int_{\mathbb R}\phi_\pm(y)e^{-2\pi ixy}dy,$$ where the pole at $y=0$ is surrounded from below.
