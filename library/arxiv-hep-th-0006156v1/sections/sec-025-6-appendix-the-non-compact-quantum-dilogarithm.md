Let complex $b$ have a nonzero real part $\Re b\ne0$. The non-compact QDL, $e_b(z)$, $z\in\mathbb C$, $|\Im z|<|\Im c_b|$, $$c_b\equiv i(b+b^{-1})/2,$$ is defined by the formula $$\label{ncqdl}
e_b(z)\equiv\exp\left(\frac{1}{4}
\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}
\frac{e^{-2iz x}\, dx}{\sinh(xb)
\sinh(x/b) x}\right),$$ where the singularity at $x=0$ is put below the contour of integration. This definition implies that $e_b(z)$ is unchanged under substitutions $b\to b^{-1}$, $b\to-b$. Using this symmetry, we choose $b$ to lay in the first quadrant of the complex plane, namely $$\Re b>0,\quad \Im b\ge 0,$$ which implies that $$\Im\tau>0,\quad\tau\equiv b^2.$$ This function has the following properties.
