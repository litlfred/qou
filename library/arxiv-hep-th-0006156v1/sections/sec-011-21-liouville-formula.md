Appearance aside, we have already mentioned that the Liouville formula manifests invariance of $\phi$ under simultaneous point-wise Möbius transform of chiral halves $\alpha$ and $\beta$. The so called cross-ratio employed in our difference scheme has just the same property.

    -\beta_{\frac12(j+k-1)}\right)} {\left(\alpha_{\frac12(j-k+1)}
    -\beta_{\frac12(j+k+1)}\right) \left(\alpha_{\frac12(j-k-1)}
