We can only afford a Heisenberg evolution of observables, so let us again consider $\chi_j$ as initial data ($\chi_{2n,-1}=\chi_{2n}$, $\chi_{2n+1,0}=\chi_{2n+1}$) and determine elements $\chi_{j,k+1}$, $j+k\equiv0\pmod2$ step by step using a slightly "quantized" lattice Liouville equation $$\label{qeq}
   \chi_{j,k+1}\chi_{j,k-1}=\frac{q^2\chi_{j-1,k}
   \chi_{j+1,k}}{(1+q\chi_{j-1,k})
   (1+q\chi_{j+1,k})}    .$$ We deliberately present the r.h.s. as a ratio to stress that all the factors there commute with each other, on the contrary, those in the l.h.s commute neither with each other nor with the r.h.s..
