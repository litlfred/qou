A polygon diagram for Bimodθ(C, D) is a cyclic equivalence class of bordered diagrams, represented by a polygon with a diagram for Bimodθ(C, D) in the interior such that all diagram lines are oriented and end in internal vertices or at the sides of the polygon. 2. The evaluation ev(D) of a polygon diagram D is the cyclic evaluation of the associated equivalence class of bordered diagrams. It is clear from Lemma 2.12 that the labeling and the evaluation of a polygon diagram is invariant under rotations of the polygon.
