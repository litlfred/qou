\ref{ex:tangleex} constructs a specific example of a triangulated 3-ball with a ribbon link as a defect. An analogous factorisation of the state sum is obtained for any ribbon link labeled by $\mathcal Z(\mac)$ in any triangulated 3-ball labeled by $\mac$. This follows from the triangulation independence of the state sum and the fact that it is invariant under $\Delta$-moves, which encode ambient isotopies of ribbon links.
