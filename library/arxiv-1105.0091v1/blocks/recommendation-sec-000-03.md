A transcendance basis of $\mathbf{Sym}$ consists in the **power sums** $$p_{k}(X)=\sum_{i=1}^{\infty} \,(x_{i})^{k}.$$ If $\mu=(\mu_{1},\ldots,\mu_{r})$ is a partition, we shall denote $p_{\mu}=p_{\mu_{1}}\cdots p_{\mu_{r}}$; a linear basis of $\mathbf{Sym}$ is therefore $(p_{\mu})_{\mu}$, where $\mu$ runs over $\mathfrak{Y}=\bigsqcup_{n \in \mathbb{N}}\mathfrak{Y}_{n}$ ([@Mac95 §1.2]).
