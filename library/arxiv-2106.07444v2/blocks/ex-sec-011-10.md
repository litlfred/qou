12.3. Suppose that we are in type An−1, meaning W ≃Sn, and that ν = m n with m coprime to n. Here, the principal ζ-block is a line. If V is the standard representation of Sn, then the principal block consists of the exterior powers Λi(V), ordered along the line from i = 0 to i = n −1. We refer to the associated block ideal as the Brauer tree algebra of type An−1. All other blocks are singletons. MINH-TÂM QUANG TRINH
