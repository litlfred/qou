[\[thm:decat\]](#thm:decat){reference-type="ref" reference="thm:decat"} implies that $(\psi, \langle{\tilde{\beta}}\rangle_{\bm{q}})_W \in \bb{Z}[\bm{q}^{\pm \frac{1}{2}}]$. So it remains to show that $(\psi, \langle{\tilde{\beta}}\rangle_{\bm{q}})_W \in \bb{Q}[\bm{q}_0]$. Since $\{-, -\}$ takes values in $\bb{Q}_W = \bb{Q}$, it is enough to get $\phi_{\bm{q}}(\tilde{\sigma}_w) \in \bb{Q}[\bm{q}_0]$ for all $\phi$. For crystallographic irreducible $W$, this is a theorem of Benson--Curtis [@bc] (joint with Springer in the case of $E_7$ and $E_8$).
