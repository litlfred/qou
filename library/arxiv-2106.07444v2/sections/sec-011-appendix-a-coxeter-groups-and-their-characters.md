A.1. Our main reference for this appendix is the book [ GP].
A Coxeter group is a group W that admits a ﬁnite presentation of the form
W = ⟨s ∈S : (st)ms,t = 1⟩,
where ms,s = 1 and ms,t ≥ 1 for all s,t ∈S. We say that W is irreducible iﬀ it
is both nontrivial and not a product of smaller Coxeter group s. The pair ( W,S ) is
called a Coxeter system , and S is called its set of simple reﬂections . We say
that |S| is the rank of W .
If w ∈ W , then an expression for w is a sequence ( s1,...,s ℓ) of elements of
S such that w = s1 · · ·sℓ. The reduced expressions for w are those of minimal
length. The Bruhat order onW is the partial order where w′ ≤w iﬀ some reduced
expression for w′ is a subsequence of a reduced expression for w, not necessarily a
contiguous one. The common length of the reduced expression s for w is called the
Bruhat length of w and denoted |w|.
IfW is ﬁnite, then there is a unique element w0 ∈W of maximal Bruhat length,
known as the longest element of (W,S ) [ GP, §1.5].
