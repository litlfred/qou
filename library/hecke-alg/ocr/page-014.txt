CHAPTER 2

Cellular algebras

Cellular algebras were introduced by Graham and Lehrer in [25,26]. Although
it is possible to give a basis-free approach to the theory of cellular algebras [50],
our perspective is that a cellular algebra is an algebra which has a basis which is
particularly well-adapted to its representation theory.

This Chapter describes the simple modules and blocks of a cellular algebra;
in Chapters 3 and 4 this theory is applied to the Iwahori-Hecke algebras of the
symmetric groups and the g-Schur algebras.
