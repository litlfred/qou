Notes on Chapter 4

1. The g-Schur algebra was introduced by Dipper and James in [15] where they
showed that it completely determined the representation theory of the finite
general linear groups in non-defining characteristic. This mirrors the classical
theory, for which the reader is referred to Green’s excellent book [27].

2. Beilinson, Lusztig and MacPherson [3] have defined the g-Schur algebra using
the geometry of the general linear groups (that these definitions coincide shown
by Du [19]) and showed that there is a natural surjection from the quantum
general linear group U,(gla) onto .Y(d,n). Latter Parshall and Wang [58] (sce
also Dipper and Donkin [12]) showed how to construct U,(glq) from the g-Schur
algebras. For details consult Brundan [5] and Donkin [18].

3. A semistandard basis theorem for the Weyl modules of the g-Schur algebra was
first proved by Dipper and James [16]; the treatment here is considerably easier
and is based on [17].
