descriptionEnsure every research investigation and document-authoring pass systematically scopes its topic areas, shortlists needed literature and empirical datasets, retrieves them from scholarly and reference databases (Crossref/DOI, arXiv, INSPIRE-HEP, PubMed, Semantic Scholar/OpenAlex/Google Scholar, CODATA/PDG/AME2020), ingests them into QOU's Knowledge Graph (folio/references/, library/, test/attestations/bib-verification/), assesses exact relevancy and claim scope, and records provenance in block authorNotes and cites[] links. NO EPHEMERAL LITERATURE NOTES (Owner directive 2026-11-28). Papers and datasets consulted during research are ingested into the Knowledge Graph and assessed for relevancy rather than left in ephemeral chat transcripts. MATH VS COMPARISON BOUNDARY. Triage separates deductive mathematical theorems (claim_domain: "math", proved in-paper) from empirical reference measurements (claim_domain: "science", entering only at QOU's comparison boundary). PROVENANCE IN AUTHORING. Block authorNotes record exact source theorem/equation/page provenance and convention mappings during authoring, while cites[] and prose citations link relevant sources in the published paper.
