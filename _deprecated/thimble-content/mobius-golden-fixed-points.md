*Lean sibling: archimedean specialisation over $\mathbb{R}$ (AGENTS.md §7c). Underlying combinatorial content stays generic to any commutative ring with the same $q$-data.*

**Proposition.**
The Möbius transformation $f(q) = (4-q)/(4q+3)$ arising from the
leading coefficients of the color-tube cubic has fixed points
$q_+ = 1/\varphi$ and $q_- = -\varphi$, where $\varphi = (1+\sqrt{5})/2$
is the golden ratio. Its **bar conjugate**

$$
  g(q)  =  -f(-q)  =  \frac{q + 4}{4q - 3}
$$

— obtained by the involution $q \mapsto -q$ on both the source and
target — has fixed points $\{\varphi, -1/\varphi\}$. Together, the
two Möbius maps fix the **golden quartet**

$$
  \mathrm{Fix}(f) \cup \mathrm{Fix}(g)
  =  \bigl\{ 1/\varphi, \, -\varphi, \, \varphi, \, -1/\varphi \bigr\}.
$$

*Proof for $f$.*
We set $f(q) = q$ to obtain $(4-q) = q(4q+3)$, hence $4q^2 + 4q - 4 = 0$,
or equivalently

$$
q^2 + q - 1 = 0.
$$

The quadratic formula yields

$$
q = \frac{-1 \pm \sqrt{5}}{2}.
$$

Since $\varphi = (1+\sqrt{5})/2$, the roots are $q_+ = (-1+\sqrt{5})/2 = 1/\varphi$
and $q_- = (-1-\sqrt{5})/2 = -\varphi$. The polynomial $q^2 + q - 1$ is the
minimal polynomial of $1/\varphi$ over $\mathbb{Q}$.

The Möbius matrix $M = \bigl(\begin{smallmatrix} -1 & 4 \\ 4 & 3 \end{smallmatrix}\bigr)$
has determinant $\det M = -3 - 16 = -19$ (prime, orientation-reversing),
trace $\operatorname{tr} M = 2$, characteristic polynomial $\lambda^2 - 2\lambda - 19$,
and eigenvalues $1 \pm 2\sqrt{5} = 4\varphi - 1$ and $3 - 4\varphi$.

*Proof for $g$.*
Setting $g(q) = q$ yields $q + 4 = q(4q - 3)$, i.e. $4q^2 - 4q - 4 = 0$,
or

$$
  q^2 - q - 1  =  0,
$$

the **bar-involute** of $q^2 + q - 1$ under $q \mapsto -q$ (or
equivalently $q \mapsto 1/q$ after clearing denominators). Its
roots are $\varphi$ and $-1/\varphi$. The Möbius matrix
$M' = \bigl(\begin{smallmatrix} 1 & 4 \\ 4 & -3 \end{smallmatrix}\bigr)$
has $\det M' = -3 - 16 = -19$, $\operatorname{tr} M' = -2$, and the same
characteristic discriminant as $M$. $\square$

**The four fixed points.**

| Map | Equation | Fixed points | Role |
|-----|---------|--------------|------|
| $f(q) = (4-q)/(4q+3)$ | $q^2 + q - 1 = 0$ | $1/\varphi$, $-\varphi$ | Color tube branch |
| $g(q) = (q+4)/(4q-3)$ | $q^2 - q - 1 = 0$ | $\varphi$, $-1/\varphi$ | Bar-dual branch |

The **positive-axis pair** $\{1/\varphi, \varphi\}$ are exactly the
two square-roots of the golden nodes
([skew-dual golden node](#prop:golden-skew-dual-node))
$1/\varphi^2$ and $\varphi^2$ that bracket the physical band of
substrate parameters $1 < q_0 < \varphi^2$. The
**negative-axis pair** $\{-\varphi, -1/\varphi\}$ are their
sign-conjugates, satisfying the same minimal polynomials with
$q \mapsto -q$.

**Symmetry group.**
The four fixed points form a single orbit under the Klein four-group
$\langle\, q \mapsto -q,\ q \mapsto 1/q \,\rangle$ acting on the
projective $q$-line. This is precisely the
$\mathbb{Z}/2 \times \mathbb{Z}/2$ generated by:

- $\iota_{\mathrm{sign}} \colon q \mapsto -q$ (charge conjugation
 on the substrate parameter, exchanging $f$ and $g$),
- $\iota_{\mathrm{bar}} \colon q \mapsto 1/q$ (Hecke bar involution,
 exchanging the two roots of each map).

**The matrix entries lift accordingly to $\mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb{Z}[i])$.**

The Möbius pair $(M, M')$ extends to a $\mathbb{Z}/4$-equivariant
quartet of matrices over the Gaussian integers
$\mathbb{Z}[i]$, indexed by the $\sigma$-rotations
$q \mapsto i^k q$ for $k = 0, 1, 2, 3$:

| $k$ | Matrix $M^{\sigma^k}$ | Entries in | Fixed pair on $\mathbb{C}^\times$ |
|-----|----------------------|------------|-----------------------------------|
| $0$ | $\begin{pmatrix} -1 & 4 \\ 4 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$ | $\mathbb{Z}$ | $\{1/\varphi,\,-\varphi\}$ |
| $1$ | $\begin{pmatrix} -1 & -4i \\ 4i & 3 \end{pmatrix}$ | $\mathbb{Z}[i]$ | $\{i/\varphi,\,-i\varphi\}$ |
| $2$ | $\begin{pmatrix} -1 & -4 \\ -4 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$ | $\mathbb{Z}$ | $\{-1/\varphi,\,\varphi\}$ (= $-1$ times $k=0$ pair) |
| $3$ | $\begin{pmatrix} -1 & 4i \\ -4i & 3 \end{pmatrix}$ | $\mathbb{Z}[i]$ | $\{-i/\varphi,\,i\varphi\}$ |

The conjugation is by $S = \mathrm{diag}(i, 1) \in \mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb{Z}[i])$:
$M^{\sigma^k} := S^{-k}\,M\,S^k$. Direct computation:

$$
  M^\sigma
   =  \begin{pmatrix} -i & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}
       \begin{pmatrix} -1 & 4 \\ 4 & 3 \end{pmatrix}
       \begin{pmatrix} i & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}
   =  \begin{pmatrix} -1 & -4i \\ 4i & 3 \end{pmatrix}.
$$

The bar conjugate $M'$ admits the same construction:

| $k$ | Matrix $M'^{\sigma^k}$ | Fixed pair |
|-----|------------------------|------------|
| $0$ | $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 4 \\ 4 & -3 \end{pmatrix}$ | $\{\varphi,\,-1/\varphi\}$ |
| $1$ | $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & -4i \\ 4i & -3 \end{pmatrix}$ | $\{i\varphi,\,-i/\varphi\}$ |
| $2$ | $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & -4 \\ -4 & -3 \end{pmatrix}$ | $\{-\varphi,\,1/\varphi\}$ |
| $3$ | $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 4i \\ -4i & -3 \end{pmatrix}$ | $\{-i\varphi,\,i/\varphi\}$ |

The full eight matrices $\{M^{\sigma^k}, M'^{\sigma^k}\}_{k=0}^3$
collectively fix the **golden octet** $G_8 \subset \mathbb{C}^\times$
([moduli symmetry $D_4$](#prop:moduli-symmetry-d4)). Since
the off-diagonal entries take values in $\{4, -4, 4i, -4i\}$ and
the diagonals in $\{\pm 1, \pm 3\}$, every matrix lies in
$\mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb{Z}[i])$ — and $\mathbb{Z}[i]$ is the
**minimal** ring of definition that contains all eight.

**Gaussian-integer factorisation of the entries.**

In $\mathbb{Z}[i]$, the prime $(1+i)$ above the rational prime $2$
satisfies $(1+i)^2 = 2i$ and $(1+i)^4 = (2i)^2 = -4$. Hence the
off-diagonal entries factor as

$$
  4  =  -(1 + i)^4,
  \qquad
  4i  =  -i\,(1 + i)^4.
$$

Sign-flipping yields the full Gaussian prime factorisation

$$
  \pm 4  =  \mp\,(1+i)^4,
  \qquad
  \pm 4i  =  \mp i\,(1+i)^4,
$$

with $(1+i)$ the Gaussian prime above the rational prime $2$. The
diagonal entries $\pm 1, \pm 3$ are units and a Gaussian inert
prime ($3 \equiv 3 \pmod 4$, so $3$ remains prime in $\mathbb{Z}[i]$).

**Significance.**
The natural ring of definition for the Möbius family is
$\mathbb{Z}[i]$, **not** $\mathbb{Z}$. The original $M$ over
$\mathbb{Z}$ is the real-axis section of a richer
$D_4$-equivariant family on $\mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb{Z}[i])$, parametrised
by the eight matrices fixing the golden octet pointwise.

The matrix entries $\{-1, 4; 4, 3\}$ of $f$ derive from the binomial
structure of the color-tube cubic
([color-tube cubic](#rem:color-tube-cubic)):
$4 = \binom{4}{1}$ gives the leading $z$-coefficient of the $c^2$ term,
$3$ is the degree of the cubic, and the sign comes from the numerator
$4 - q = -(q - 4)$. The sign-conjugate map $g$ replaces this with
$q + 4 = +(q - (-4))$ on the bar-dual branch.
