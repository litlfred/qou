Let $G$ be a [formal group](#def:non-commutative-formal-group) over a field
$k$ of characteristic $p > 0$ with an ordered coordinate system
$x_1 < \dots < x_n$. Let $X = \{\delta_{x_j}^{p^r} : 1 \le j \le n,\ r \ge 0\}$
be the divided-power generators, graded by $\deg \delta_{x_j}^{p^r} = p^r$
and ordered by $\delta_{x_j}^{p^s} > \delta_{x_i}^{p^r}$ when $j > i$, or
$j = i$ and $s > r$. In the tensor algebra $TX$ put
$$
  f_\eta = \eta^p - F(\eta), \qquad
  g_{\eta, \zeta} = \eta\zeta - \zeta\eta - \tilde\pi(\eta, \zeta)
  \qquad (\eta > \zeta \in X),
$$
where $F(\eta)$ and $\tilde\pi(\eta, \zeta)$ are the expressions of $\eta^p$
and of the commutator $\eta\zeta - \zeta\eta$ in the multiplicative basis of
$\mathrm{Dist}(G)$. A **Gröbner basis** of an ideal for a monomial
ordering is a generating set whose leading monomials generate the ideal
of leading monomials; equivalently, the monomials avoiding them form a
basis of the quotient.

Reference: \cite{leitner2006}, Remark 2.3 and Proposition 2.4;
\cite{leitnerpawloski2004} for the unital setting.
