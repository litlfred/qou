Let $(A_i)_{i \in I}$ be commutative algebras over a field $k$. Their
**splay** is the diagram of inclusions $A_i \hookrightarrow T$ into the
coproduct $T = \coprod_i A_i$ in the category of algebras (the free
product); $T$ is the **splay algebra** and the $A_i$ are its coordinate
algebras.

A **Poisson structure** on an algebra $A$ is a skew-symmetric
bi-derivation $\pi$ satisfying the Jacobi identity. A **Poisson structure
on the splay** (the paper's name; called here a skewed-Poisson structure)
is a Poisson structure $\pi$ on $T$ with **internal symmetry**:
$$
  \pi(A_i, A_i) = 0 \qquad \text{for every } i \in I.
$$
If $T = \bigcup_n T^{(n)}$ is filtered, $\pi$ is **strongly filtered** when
$\pi(T^{(m)}, T^{(n)}) \subseteq T^{(m+n-1)}$.

The canonical example is the commutator $\pi_c(a, b) = ab - ba$
(\cite{leitner2006}, Example 1.2). In the application the $A_i$ are the
distribution algebras $\mathrm{Dist}(H_i)$ of commutative formal groups.

Reference: \cite{leitner2006}, Definition 1.1.
