A **Witt vector lift** is the structure produced by applying the Witt
vector functor $W$ to a
[commutative formal group](#def:commutative-formal-group) $H / \mathbb{F}_p$
equipped with a
[skewed-Poisson structure](#def:skewed-poisson-structure).  The result
is a formal group $\hat{G} / \mathbb{Z}_p$ whose generic fibre is a
[non-commutative formal group](#def:non-commutative-formal-group) over
$\mathbb{Q}_p$, and which reduces to $H$ modulo $p$.

Reference: \cite{fred2005}, Section 5; \cite{hazewinkel1978}, Chapter V.
