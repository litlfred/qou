An $n$-dimensional **formal group law** over a commutative ring $R$ is
an $n$-tuple of power series $F(X, Y) \in R[[X_1, \dots, X_n, Y_1, \dots, Y_n]]^n$
with
$$
  F(X, 0) = X, \qquad F(0, Y) = Y, \qquad F(F(X, Y), Z) = F(X, F(Y, Z)).
$$
It is **commutative** if in addition $F(X, Y) = F(Y, X)$.

A formal group in the sense of \cite{leitner2006}, §2, is a group object in
smooth formal varieties over $R$; a choice of coordinates presents it as a
formal group law. The paper works over a field $k$ of characteristic $p > 0$.

Reference: \cite{hazewinkel1978}, Chapter I; \cite{leitner2006}, §2.
