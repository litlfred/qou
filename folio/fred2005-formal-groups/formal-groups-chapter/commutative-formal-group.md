A **commutative formal group** over a field $k$ of characteristic $p$
is a formal group law $F \colon \mathcal{O}(H) \times \mathcal{O}(H) \to \mathcal{O}(H)$
satisfying $F(X, Y) = F(Y, X)$ and $F(X, 0) = X$.

This is the base object $H / \mathbb{F}_p$ in the dissertation's
integration theorem.  It carries a coordinate ring $\mathcal{O}(H)$
and a commutative formal group law.

Reference: \cite{hazewinkel1978}, Chapter I.
