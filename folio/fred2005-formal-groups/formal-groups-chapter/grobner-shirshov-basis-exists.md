**Proposition** (\cite{leitner2006}, Proposition 2.4). With the notation of
the [Gröbner basis](#def:grobner-shirshov-basis) definition, impose the
graded lexicographic ordering on $TX$ and let
$I = (f_\eta \mid \eta \in X) + (g_{\eta,\zeta} \mid \eta > \zeta \in X)$. Then
$\{f_\eta, g_{\eta,\zeta}\}$ is a Gröbner basis of $I$, with leading monomials
$\eta^p$ and $\eta\zeta$, and
$$
  \mathrm{Dist}(G) \;\cong\; TX / I
$$
as algebras.

**Theorem** (PBW for splays; \cite{leitner2006}, Theorem 1.9). Let $T$ be a
splay over $k$ whose coordinate algebras $A_i$ have presentations as in
Lemma 1.5 of the paper, let $\pi$ be a strongly filtered
[Poisson structure](#def:skewed-poisson-structure) on $T$, and let
$U = T / (fg - gf - \pi(f, g))$. Fixing a total order on $I$, $U$ is
isomorphic as a $k$-module to $\bigotimes_i A_i$.

For a Lie algebra $\mathfrak{g} = \bigoplus_i \mathfrak{h}_i$ with abelian
$\mathfrak{h}_i$, Theorem 1.9 recovers the usual PBW theorem
(\cite{leitner2006}, Remark 1.10).

Reference: \cite{leitner2006}, §§1–2.
