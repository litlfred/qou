Comparing [the computational boundary](#conj:p-vs-np-boundary) $\mathcal{H}_c = V(\mathfrak{h}_c)$ with the physical jet horizon $\mathcal{H} = V(\mathfrak{h})$ provides an interpretive dictionary between degree-graded jet data and witness verification (see [Table 1](#tbl:p-vs-np-event-horizon-table1-data) and [Table 2](#tbl:p-vs-np-event-horizon-table2-data)).

At a jet horizon where $A_0 = 0$, degree-$0$ fibre evaluations vanish while degree-$\ge 1$ jet data (winding and curvature) remain non-zero. In the skein-tree analogy, extracting the global degree-$0$ sum $P_L(q_0)$ corresponds to degree-$0$ evaluation, checking a single resolution path in $O(c)$ steps corresponds to degree-$1$ transport, and pairwise path correlations correspond to degree-$2$ data. Harlow and Hayden \cite{arxiv-1301.4504v4} independently relate exponential decoding complexity of Hawking radiation to computational hardness assumptions. In the QOU setting, this correspondence is an interpretive analogy between two quotient constructions, not a derivation of $\mathrm{P} \neq \mathrm{NP}$ from horizon geometry.
