*Conditional on an unproved faithfulness postulate linking the resolution-tree residue $\mathrm{res}_{\mathcal{R}}(\chi(A))$ to the classical decision boundary, non-vanishing $\mathrm{res}_{\mathcal{R}}(\chi(A)) \neq 0$ at a non-torsion real parameter $q_0 > 1$ is proposed as a QOU-internal counterpart of $\mathrm{P} \neq \mathrm{NP}$ \cite{cook1971} ([boundary conjecture](#conj:p-vs-np-boundary)).*

**Cited complexity theorems versus speculative gaps.** For the Jones polynomial $V_L(t)$, exact evaluation is $\#\mathrm{P}$-hard outside six roots of unity of orders $\{1, 2, 3, 4\}$ \cite{jaeger1990}, while additive approximation at primitive roots of unity of orders outside the tractable set is $\mathrm{BQP}$-complete \cite{freedman2003,aharonov2009}. Three structural gaps separate those theorems from the conjecture above:

1. **Mismatch with $n \mid 6$ Kummer divisibility.** Under $t = q$ the tractable orders of \cite{jaeger1990} are $\{1, 2, 3, 4\}$, and under $t = q^{\pm 2}$ the orders of $q$ are $\{1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8\}$; neither set equals $\{n : n \mid 6\}$, so Kummer divisibility of $\chi_{\mathrm{eu}}(\Sigma_4) = -6$ does not match the Jaeger--Vertigan--Welsh tractable locus.
2. **Zariski genericity of polynomial residues.** A nonzero polynomial or integer constant ($-6 \neq 0$) is non-vanishing on a cofinite parameter set for purely algebraic reasons having nothing to do with computational complexity. Consequently, the entire complexity-theoretic burden sits in the unproved faithfulness implication $\mathrm{res}_{\mathcal{R}}(\chi(A)) \neq 0 \Rightarrow \mathrm{P} \neq \mathrm{NP}$, which enters only as an uninstantiated hypothesis.
3. **Counting-to-decision gap.** Even given semisimplicity of $H_n(q_0)$ for real $q_0 > 1$ and the cited implication $\mathrm{P} \neq \mathrm{NP} \Rightarrow \mathrm{FP} \neq \#\mathrm{P}$, deducing $\mathrm{P} \neq \mathrm{NP}$ from $\#\mathrm{P}$-hardness of exact trace evaluation requires an unproved counting-to-decision transfer. What *is* proved unconditionally is the restricted bandwidth statement: any procedure that must inspect all $3^{3n}$ leaves of the skein tree at bounded information rate per step runs in super-polynomial time, which constrains naive tree expansion rather than general Turing machines.
