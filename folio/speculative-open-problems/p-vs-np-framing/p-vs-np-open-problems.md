Nine open questions separate the proved Hecke-algebra and trace-cost propositions of the main monograph from [the speculative P-versus-NP conjectures](#conj:qou-p-neq-np); only the first is $\mathrm{P}$ versus $\mathrm{NP}$ itself, while the remaining eight are concrete mathematical or definitional questions inside the QOU model:

1. **The counting-to-decision transfer.** Exact evaluation of the Jones polynomial is $\#\mathrm{P}$-hard \cite{jaeger1990}, and $\mathrm{NP} \subseteq \mathrm{P}^{\#\mathrm{P}}$ gives $\mathrm{P} \neq \mathrm{NP} \Rightarrow \mathrm{FP} \neq \#\mathrm{P}$. The converse transfer required by every skein-trace formulation is $\mathrm{P} \neq \mathrm{NP}$ itself and faces the relativization \cite{bakergillsolovay1975}, natural-proofs \cite{razborovrudich1997}, and algebrization \cite{aaronsonwigderson2009} barriers.
2. **Fixing the observation return model.** Choosing whether an observation of a $c$-crossing braid returns an exact degree-$0$ Markov trace at linear cost $O(c)$ (which would place $\#\mathrm{P}$ inside QOU polynomial time and falsify QOU-internal $\mathrm{P} \neq \mathrm{NP}$) or returns a Born-rule sample (reducing the model to quantum polynomial time) is an unmade definitional choice, not a theorem.
3. **Polynomial simulation overhead.** Establishing that QOU polynomial time contains $\mathrm{P}$ requires proving polynomial-time overhead for simulating a deterministic Turing machine via the $q$-Game of Life or classical Reeb flow under the chosen cost model of item 2.
4. **Outer derivations at roots of unity.** At split semisimple parameters $HH^i(H_n(K, q), M) = 0$ for $i \ge 1$; at roots of unity $\zeta$, computing $\dim HH^1(H_n(\zeta))$ for small $n$ locates where non-inner derivations exist, though as a fixed-rank invariant it cannot encode asymptotic complexity.
5. **Separability and Skolem--Noether for $H_n(q)$ at all ranks.** Deriving split semisimplicity at all ranks from the computed Schur elements via the Geck--Pfeiffer criterion \cite{geckpfeiffer2000}, together with vanishing of higher Hochschild cohomology for split separable algebras.
6. **Trace-preserving outer automorphisms.** Classifying automorphisms of $H_n(K, q)$ that preserve the Markov trace across equal-dimension, equal-weight Wedderburn blocks (extending the result that the Goldman--Iwahori involution preserves the trace only when $q^4 = -1$).
7. **Quantum cellular automata index.** Defining and computing the Gross--Nesme--Vogts--Werner index of the strand shift in the $q$-Game of Life at unitary-fusion roots of unity, and formulating an analogue at non-torsion $q$.
8. **Locality-restricted gauge-versus-flux separation.** Formulating a local curvature-preserving gate class so that global flux-threading intents are separated from local cellular automata by locality rather than single-edge holonomy shift.
9. **Restricted-model lower bounds.** Proving super-polynomial lower bounds against explicitly restricted access models (such as naive skein-tree expansion or read-once bounded-bandwidth protocols), without claiming transfer to general Turing machines.
