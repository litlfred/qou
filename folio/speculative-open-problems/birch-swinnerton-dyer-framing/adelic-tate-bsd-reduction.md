In [the surreal BSD heuristic](#rem:bsd-surreal-bound), matching the leading Taylor prefactor $c$ in $L(\mathcal{E}_q, s) \asymp c \cdot (s - 1)^r$ against a product of periods, regulator, Tamagawa numbers, and $|\mathrm{Sha}|$ \cite{tate1974} is proposed by analogy with Tate's restricted-product factorisation of adelic zeta integrals \cite{tate1967}. While finite-support additivity of per-place valuations in an ordered abelian group holds algebraically, no local $\varepsilon_p$-adic integral in the QOU corpus computes $|\mathrm{Sha}(E/\mathbb{Q})|$ or the Néron--Tate regulator of $E(\mathbb{Q})$, so this identification remains an unproved programme sketch.
