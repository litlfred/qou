*Let $(\mathbf{C}, \varTheta, G, \mathcal{S})$ be a quantum observable universe with aether parameter $q$ and Descartes elliptic curve $\mathcal{E}_q$. Let $\mathcal{E}_q(\mathbf{1}) = \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathbf{C}}(\mathbf{1}, E_q)$ be the invariant Hom-group of categorical rank $r_q = \mathrm{rank}\,\mathcal{E}_q(\mathbf{1})$, let $L(\mathcal{E}_q, s)$ be the formal Euler product over places of Bring's coordinate ring, and let $\mathrm{Sha}_q(\mathcal{E}_q)$ be the monoidal localisation kernel. By analogy with classical BSD \cite{tate1974}, the speculative $q$-BSD conjecture posits:*

1. **Weak $q$-BSD.** *The analytic order of vanishing of $L(\mathcal{E}_q, s)$ at $s = 1$ equals the categorical rank:*

$$
\mathrm{ord}_{s=1} L(\mathcal{E}_q, s) = r_q.
$$

2. **Full $q$-BSD.** *The leading coefficient satisfies*

$$
\frac{L^{(r_q)}(\mathcal{E}_q, 1)}{[r_q]_q!} = \frac{\Omega_q \cdot \mathrm{Reg}_q(\mathcal{E}_q) \cdot |\mathrm{Sha}_q(\mathcal{E}_q)| \cdot \displaystyle\prod_{\mathfrak{m}} c_{\mathfrak{m}}(q)}{|\mathcal{E}_q(\mathbf{1})_{\mathrm{tors}}|^2_q},
$$

*collapsing at $r_q = 0$ (with trivial torsion and empty regulator $\mathrm{Reg}_q = 1$) to $L(\mathcal{E}_q, 1) = \Omega_q \cdot |\mathrm{Sha}_q(\mathcal{E}_q)| \cdot \prod_{\mathfrak{m}} c_{\mathfrak{m}}(q)$.*

**Status.** Both clauses are unproved hypotheses carried in an uninstantiated conjecture class. Neither clause is connected by a proved functor to the arithmetic Mordell--Weil group $E(\mathbb{Q})$ or the classical Tate--Shafarevich group $\mathrm{Sha}(E/\mathbb{Q})$.
