Throughout this paper, $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ denotes a quantum observable universe over a commutative ring $R$ with aether parameter $q \in R^\times$, Iwahori--Hecke quotient $H_n(q)$, Jones--Markov trace $\mathrm{tr}_M$, and categorical Reeb derivation $\xi_q$, as defined in \cite{leitner2026}. In surreal-order comparisons, $\varepsilon$ denotes a positive infinitesimal in the ordered field $\mathbb{R}(\varepsilon)$ inside Conway's surreal numbers $\mathbf{No}$ \cite{conway1976}, with $q = 1 + \varepsilon$, Reeb period scaling $\Omega_q \asymp \varepsilon^{-1}$, crossing-energy gap scaling $\Delta_q \asymp \varepsilon^3$, and $\omega$-adic leading-exponent valuation $\ordw$.

In finite-field curve sections, the prime-power cardinality $|\mathbb{F}_p|$ or $|\mathbb{F}_{p^r}|$ is distinguished explicitly from the deformation parameter $q$: a finite-field cardinality is a fixed integer prime power admitting no continuous limit $q \to 1$.
