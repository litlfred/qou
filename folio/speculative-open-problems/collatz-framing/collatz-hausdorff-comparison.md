**Status: heuristic comparison.** The expression $\Phi(P) = \log_q |P(q)|$ is not defined on general Laurent polynomials $P \in \mathbb{Z}[q^{\pm 1}]$, and $P = 1$ lies outside the domain of [the partial map $\mathcal{C}_q$](#conj:q-collatz), whose positive attractor is $\{q^{-1}, 1 + q^{-1}\}$ ([chapter introduction](#sec:collatz-intro), [withdrawn contact criterion](#conj:collatz-contact-boundary)).

Evaluated purely as a formal real-$q$ heuristic at $q > 1$, the smooth Hausdorff recurrence $F(q) = (q^2 - q + 1)/q$ (which contracts superlinearly to $q = 1$ for $q > 3/4$ with $F'(1) = 0$) contrasts with the discrete step-multiplier ratios $R(v) = [3]_q / [2]_q^v$ ($R(1) > 1$ versus $R(2) < 1$), summarised in [Table 1](#tbl:collatz-hausdorff-comparison-table-data). Nothing in this comparison constrains which integer or Laurent orbits converge.
