Earlier exposition described three parallel faces of [the $q$-Collatz conjecture](#conj:q-collatz):

1. **Laurent-module face (defined).** The partial map $\mathcal{C}_q$ on $\mathbb{Z}[q^{\pm 1}]$ divides by $[2]_q$ when $P(-1) = 0$ and maps $P \mapsto ([3]_q P + 1)/[2]_q$ when $P(-1) = -1$.
2. **Residue face at $q = -1$ (defined, threshold corrected).** Vanishing order at $q = -1$ counts post-odd-step divisions by $[2]_q$; as recorded in [the withdrawn contact-threshold conjecture](#conj:collatz-contact-boundary), the fundamental cycle has one such division per odd step rather than two.
3. **Hecke--Markov braid face (unconstructed).** Interpreting $\mathcal{C}_q$ as an operator on $H_n(q)$ whose iterated Markov trace $\mathrm{tr}_M(\mathcal{C}_q^k(h))$ converges toward the unknot requires an explicit lift of the partial map $\mathcal{C}_q$ from $\mathbb{Z}[q^{\pm 1}]$ to braid or Hecke elements $h \in H_n(q)$; no such lift is defined yet.
