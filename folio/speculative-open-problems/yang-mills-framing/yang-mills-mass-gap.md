*Let $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ be a quantum observable universe over a commutative ring $R$ with real specialization $q > 1$. Let $\nabla$ be a categorical connection on the Frobenius--Hopf object $A$ with curvature $F_\nabla = \nabla^2$, let $\mathfrak{S}_q[\nabla]$ be the $q$-weighted curvature--Levi functional, and let $\Omega_0 \colon R \to \mathrm{End}_{\mathbf{C}}(A)$ be the $G_q$-invariant vacuum functional on the direct-limit algebra $\mathcal{A}_\infty = \varinjlim \mathrm{End}_{\mathbf{C}}(A_n)$. The speculative categorical analogue of the Yang--Mills problem posits:*

1. **Existence.** *There exists a connection $\nabla$ critical for $\mathfrak{S}_q[\nabla]$ whose vacuum functional $\Omega_0$ extends to a positive-definite sesquilinear form on a Hilbert completion $\mathcal{H}_q$.*
2. **Spectral gap.** *Every non-vacuum excitation $\varrho \colon \mathbf{1} \to A$ in the spectrum of the categorical transfer matrix $\mathcal{T}_q \colon \mathrm{End}_{\mathbf{C}}(A_n) \to \mathrm{End}_{\mathbf{C}}(A_n)$ satisfies*

$$
  \mathfrak{m}(\varrho) \geq \Delta_q > 0,
$$

*where $\Delta_q = \tfrac{1}{2}(q - q^{-1})(q - 1)^2$ is the crossing-energy gap functional, and the two-point correlator decays at rate at least $\Delta_q$.*

**Proposed dictionary.** The correspondence between the classical Wightman formulation on $\mathbb{R}^4$ \cite{jaffe2006} and the categorical QOU objects is listed in [Table 1](#tbl:yang-mills-mass-gap-table-data).

**What is proved versus postulated.** The inequality

$$
  \Delta_q = \tfrac{1}{2}(q - q^{-1})(q - 1)^2 > 0 \qquad (q > 1)
$$

is an elementary rational fact: $\Delta_q$ vanishes on $\mu_2 = \{1, -1\}$ and is strictly positive on $(1, \infty)$. What is *not* proved is that a transfer matrix attached to four-dimensional $\mathrm{SU}(N)$ gauge fields has first excited eigenvalue bounded below by $\Delta_q$, or that the $q \to 1$ continuum limit produces a Wightman quantum field theory on $\mathbb{R}^4$ with a surviving physical gap. In the formal carrier, the bound $\Delta_q \le \lambda_1$ is an assumed structure field rather than a theorem, and exponential decay of two-point correlators is not derived from spectral theory.
