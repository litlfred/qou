The classical Yang--Mills Millennium Problem \cite{jaffe2006} asks for the mathematical construction of a four-dimensional quantum Yang--Mills theory on $\mathbb{R}^4$ for every compact simple gauge group $G$, satisfying the Wightman or Osterwalder--Schrader axioms and possessing a strictly positive mass gap $\Delta > 0$ in the spectrum of the Hamiltonian.

Nothing in the Quantum Observable Universe monograph \cite{leitner2026} or in this chapter proves or reduces that problem. Separating what is proved in the main monograph from what is postulated here clarifies the boundary:

1. **Proved rational and differential-geometric ingredients.** For real $q > 1$, the crossing-energy gap functional $\Delta_q = \tfrac{1}{2}(q - q^{-1})(q - 1)^2$ is strictly positive, vanishes on the $2$-torsion subgroup $\mu_2 = \{1, -1\}$, and increases monotonically on $(1, \infty)$. On an archimedean CR realisation, the $q$-weighted curvature--Levi functional weights the horizontal self-dual curvature by the local Levi-form coupling, and Bogomolny self-duality holds on categorical instanton sectors.
2. **Where the gap is postulated rather than derived.** The categorical mass-gap carrier bundles a transfer-matrix spectrum $(\lambda_n)_{n \ge 0}$ together with the inequality $\Delta_q \le \lambda_1$ as one of its own structure fields. Positivity of $\lambda_1$ follows from $\Delta_q > 0$ only once such a structure is supplied; no theorem derives $\Delta_q \le \lambda_1$ from a four-dimensional continuum gauge action. Calling the mass gap true by categorical construction is therefore a relabelling of an elementary rational inequality, not a solution of the Yang--Mills problem.
3. **Where continuum reconstruction remains open.** Passing from a $q$-deformed Frobenius--Hopf algebra at $q > 1$ to a renormalised Wightman field theory on $\mathbb{R}^4$ as $q \to 1$ requires both Osterwalder--Schrader reflection positivity of the antipode-twisted pairing and a non-trivial continuum limit in which the physical gap does not collapse to zero. Both steps remain open conjectures.
