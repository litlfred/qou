If $\mathfrak{g}$ is a Lie algebra with Lie bracket $[\,,\,]_{\mathfrak{g}}$ over a
field $k$, one forms the universal enveloping algebra $U\mathfrak{g}$ as the quotient
of the tensor algebra $T\mathfrak{g}$ by the two-sided ideal:

$$J = (xy - yx - [x,y]_{\mathfrak{g}} \mid x, y \in \mathfrak{g} \hookrightarrow T\mathfrak{g})$$

Choosing a total ordering on the index set $I$ for a $k$-basis
$\mathfrak{g} = k\langle x_i \mid i \in I \rangle$, a Gröbner--Shirshov basis argument \cite{bergman1978} yields the
Poincaré–Birkhoff–Witt theorem: $U\mathfrak{g}$ has a basis of non-decreasing words.

If we replace $k$ by an arbitrary commutative ring $R$, one cannot make use of
classical Gröbner--Shirshov basis techniques — one must prove this by ad-hoc methods.
We view this inability as an unsatisfactory state of affairs that the notion of
a unital Gröbner--Shirshov basis resolves.
