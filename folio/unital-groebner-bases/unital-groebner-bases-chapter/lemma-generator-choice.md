Let $R$ be a commutative ring with unity, $A$ an $R$-algebra which is free as an
$R$-module, $(B, <)$ an admissible system on $A$, and let $I$ be a two-sided
ideal of $A$ generated by a unital Gröbner--Shirshov basis $G = \{g_\gamma \mid \gamma \in \Gamma\}$.
Then in the division algorithm, we may choose $h_i \in I$ so that
$h_i = g_{\gamma_i}$ for some $\gamma_i \in \Gamma$.

*Proof.* Set $h := h_i$, $f := f_i$. Since $G$ is a Gröbner--Shirshov basis, we may write:

$$h = \sum_{k \in K} \lambda_k u_k \cdot g_{\gamma_k} \cdot v_k$$

with $\lambda_k \in R^*$, $u_k, v_k \in B$ and $\gamma_k \in \Gamma$. Denote
$\alpha := \LM(h)$. Choose a representation so that $\alpha$ is minimal and let
$T := \{k \in K \mid \LM(u_k \cdot g_{\gamma_k} \cdot v_k) = \alpha\}$ with $|T|$
minimal. If $|T| = 1$ we are done. Otherwise, for $k_1 \neq k_2 \in T$, the
unitality condition gives $c_{k_i} := \LC(u_{k_i} \cdot g_{\gamma_{k_i}} \cdot v_{k_i}) \in R^\times$,
allowing us to form the S-polynomial and reduce $|T|$ by one — contradicting
minimality. Thus $|T| = 1$. $\square$
