Let $R$ be a commutative ring with unity, $A$ an $R$-algebra which
is free as an $R$-module and without quasi-zeros, $(B, <)$ an
[admissible system](#def:admissible-system) on $A$, and $I \subseteq A$
a subset.

Define the $R$-submodule $O(I)$ of $A$ — the **remainder module** — as the $R$-submodule spanned
by the set

$$
  o(I) := \bigl\{\lambda\beta \;\big|\;
    \lambda \in R^*,\; \beta \in B,\;
    \forall\, h \in I\colon \mathrm{LT}(h) \neq \lambda\beta\bigr\}.
$$

Define also the $R$-module $\tilde{O}(I)$ as the $R$-submodule spanned
by

$$
  \tilde{o}(I) := B \setminus \{\mathrm{LM}(h) \mid h \in I\}.
$$

$\tilde{O}(I)$ is a free $R$-module.
