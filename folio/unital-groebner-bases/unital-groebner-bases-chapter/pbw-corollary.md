Let $\mathfrak{g}$ be a Lie algebra over $R$, a commutative ring with
unity, which is free as an $R$-module, with Lie bracket $[\,{,}\,]_{\mathfrak{g}}$. Then the universal
enveloping algebra

$$
  U\mathfrak{g} = T\mathfrak{g} / J, \qquad
  J = \bigl(xy - yx - [x,y]_{\mathfrak{g}}
    \;\big|\; x, y \in \mathfrak{g} \hookrightarrow T\mathfrak{g}\bigr)
$$

is isomorphic as an $R$-module to $S\mathfrak{g}$, the symmetric
algebra on $\mathfrak{g}$.

In particular, if $R$ is a $\mathbb{Q}$-algebra, we obtain a Gröbner--Shirshov
basis proof of the equivalence of the category of (finite-dimensional)
Lie algebras over $R$ and (finite-dimensional) smooth formal groups
over $R$.
