Let $B$ be a [monomial basis](#def:monomial-basis) well-ordered by $<$.
For $f \in A$, write $f$ uniquely as

$$
  f = c_1 b_1 + c_2 b_2 + \cdots + c_n b_n,
  \qquad c_i \in R^* := R \setminus \{0\},\;
  b_i \in B,\;
  b_1 > b_2 > \cdots > b_n.
$$

Then define:
- the **leading monomial** $\mathrm{LM}(f) := b_1$,
- the **leading coefficient** $\mathrm{LC}(f) := c_1$,
- the **leading term** $\mathrm{LT}(f) := \mathrm{LC}(f)\,\mathrm{LM}(f)$.
