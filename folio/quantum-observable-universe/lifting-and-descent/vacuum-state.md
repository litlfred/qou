Let $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ be a [quantum observable universe](#def:quantum-observable-universe). Let $q$ denote the aether parameter. Let $\mathcal{A}_\infty$ be its [direct limit algebra](#def:direct-limit-algebra). Let $G_q = \mathrm{Aut}^{\otimes}(\tau)$ be the [quantum gauge group](#def:quantum-gauge-group), acting on $\mathcal{A}_\infty$ by conjugation. There exists a unique $R$-linear functional $\varphi \colon \mathcal{A}_\infty \to R$ that is $G_q$-invariant:
$$
  \varphi(g \cdot a) = \varphi(a)
  \quad\text{for all } g \in G_q,
  a \in \mathcal{A}_\infty.
$$
In the archimedean realisation, $G_q = \mathrm{SU}_q(2)$ and $\varphi$ is the unique $\mathrm{SU}(2)$-invariant state on the $C^*$-algebraic direct limit.
