Each step down the brane tower removes a space-like dimension through the $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ rotation and its associated fixed-point set.

1. **Ascent (generation).** Moving from $M_k$ ($\dim = 0$) upward lifts the connection $A$ to higher categorical structures via the jet prolongation ([jet prolongation](#prop:jet-prolongation)).
2. **:refterm[Descent]{#descent} (projection).** Moving downward, the :refterm[descent]{#descent} homomorphism $\mathfrak{d}_n$ ([:refterm[descent]{#descent}](#lem:descent)) acts as an algebraic filter ([algebraic filter](#rem:algebraic-filter)) on the level-$(n{+}1)$ algebra. The :refterm[descent]{#descent} involution $\hat{\tau}_n$ annihilates the transient complement $\mathcal{A}_{n+1}^{\mathrm{trans}}$ ([:refterm[descent]{#descent} eigenspaces](#prop:descent-eigenspaces))—the genuinely new modes at level $n{+}1$ lacking a pre-image at level $n$. At dimension $3$ the connection is unconstrained and its holonomy genuinely non-abelian ([:refterm[descent]{#descent} reduction at dimension 3](#rem:descent-reduction-three)); quotienting by the Reeb flow gives the surface stratum, where the mass becomes central ([:refterm[descent]{#descent} reduction at dimension 2](#rem:descent-reduction-two)). At dimension $1$, only the longitudinal $\mathrm{U}(1)$ backbone remains ([:refterm[descent]{#descent} reduction](#rem:descent-reduction)). At dimension $0$, even this collapses to a single fibre datum ([:refterm[descent]{#descent} reduction to zero](#rem:descent-reduction-zero)).
3. **Round-trip involution.** The composition of ascent and :refterm[descent]{#descent} is the :refterm[descent]{#descent} involution $\hat{\tau}_n$ ([:refterm[descent]{#descent} involution](#def:descent-involution)), acting on $\Gamma(J^1 \mathcal{S}_{n+1})$. Its image $\mathcal{A}_{n+1}^{\mathrm{desc}}$ forms the stable core of the brane algebra at level $n{+}1$. The defining boundary $\partial \mathcal{S}_n$ ([brane boundary](#def:brane-boundary)) marks the geometric locus where the fibrewise :refterm[descent]{#descent} map has a non-trivial kernel.
