The direct-limit algebra provides the arena for a categorical notion of mass. Mass is defined as a Frobenius trace, shown to be well-defined and unique, and connected to the obstruction class through Euler-characteristic stability.

This section establishes that mass arises as a non-commutativity invariant of the direct-limit algebra and proves that the categorical Euler characteristic $\chi(A_n)$ is constant along the tower. This supplies the topological invariant for the mass identity. Constructing the mass as a categorical trace verifies that this trace passes through the brane tower as the unique compatible Frobenius pairing. The section also formulates the conjecture of homological shell condensation: the BB-stratified $E_\infty^{p,>0}(n) = 0$ at the magic values of $n$.

The next section, *Betti Numbers and Harmonic Basis*, uses the Euler-characteristic stability and categorical mass to refine the theory with Hodge-theoretic data.
