The direct-limit algebra provides the arena for a categorical notion of mass. We define mass as a Frobenius trace. We show it is well-defined and unique. We connect it to the obstruction class through Euler-characteristic stability.

We establish that mass arises as a non-commutativity invariant of the direct-limit algebra. We prove that the categorical Euler characteristic $\chi(A_n)$ is constant along the tower. This supplies the topological invariant for the mass identity. We construct the mass as a categorical trace. We verify this trace passes through the brane tower and is the unique compatible Frobenius pairing. We conjecture the specific form of homological shell condensation: the BB-stratified $E_\infty^{p,>0}(n) = 0$ at the magic values of $n$.

The next section, *Betti Numbers and Harmonic Basis*, uses the Euler-characteristic stability and categorical mass to refine the theory with Hodge-theoretic data.
