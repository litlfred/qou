Let $\mathbf{C}$ be an $R$-linear rigid :refterm[monoidal category]{#monoidal-category}. Let
$$
  1  \longrightarrow  \mathcal{B}
   \longrightarrow  \mathfrak{G}'
   \longrightarrow  \mathfrak{G}
   \longrightarrow  1
$$
be a central extension of gerbes on a site $\mathcal{S}$, banded by an abelian sheaf $\mathcal{B}$. The category $\mathbf{C}$ satisfies the **Quantum Lifting Condition** with respect to $(\mathfrak{G}', \mathfrak{G}, \mathcal{B})$ if, for every object $x \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathfrak{G})$, the obstruction to lifting $x$ to an object $x' \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathfrak{G}')$ is classified by a single cohomology class
$$
  \kappa(x)  \in  H^2(\mathcal{S}, \mathcal{B}),
$$
and $x$ admits a lift if and only if $\kappa(x) = 0$.

This is the categorical form of the gerbe central-extension obstruction of Yekutieli \cite{yekutieli2010}; the name *Quantum Lifting Condition* emphasises its role in specialising the categorical aether.
