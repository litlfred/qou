Let $F_A$ be the curvature of the aether parameter ([aether parameter](#def:substrate-parameter)) and let $\alpha$ be a harmonic $1$-form in the $q$-harmonic basis on branes ([q-harmonic basis](#rem:q-harmonic-basis-brane)).

The commutator $[F_A, \alpha]$ classifies $\alpha$ into exactly one of two types:

1. **Fermionic (matter).** $[F_A, \alpha] \neq 0$.
2. **Bosonic (radiation).** $[F_A, \alpha] = 0$.
