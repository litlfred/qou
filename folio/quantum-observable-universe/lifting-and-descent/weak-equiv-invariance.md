Let $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ be a quantum observable universe with recursive brane tower $\{A_n\}_{n \geq 0}$, cosimplicial crossed groupoid structure ([cosimplicial crossed groupoid](#prop:cosimplicial-crossed-groupoid)), and aether parameter $q$. Let $\tau \colon \mathcal{A}_\infty \to \mathbb{C}$ be the vacuum state ([vacuum state](#prop:vacuum-state)). A *weak equivalence* between brane levels $n$ and $m$ is a zigzag of coface and codegeneracy maps:

$$
  \mathfrak{G}_n
  \xleftarrow{\sigma}
  \mathfrak{G}_{k_1}
  \xrightarrow{\delta}
  \cdots
  \xrightarrow{\delta}
  \mathfrak{G}_m.
$$

For every observable $a \in \mathcal{A}_\infty$, the expectation value is invariant under weak equivalence:

$$
  \tau\!\bigl(\iota_n(a|_n)\bigr)
  = 
  \tau\!\bigl(\iota_m(a|_m)\bigr),
$$

where $\iota_n \colon \Gamma(J^1 \mathcal{S}_n, \mathrm{SU}_{q_n}(2)) \hookrightarrow \mathcal{A}_\infty$ is the canonical embedding and $a|_n$ restricts $a$ to level $n$. In particular, physical states (the GNS vectors of $\tau$) remain invariant under fluctuations of the aether parameter $q(p)$ across brane levels.
