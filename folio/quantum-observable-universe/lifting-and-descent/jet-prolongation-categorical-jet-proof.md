**Proof.** Let $s\colon A \to A \otimes A$ be $s(a) = a \otimes 1$. Then $m \circ s = \mathrm{Id}_A$, so $T := s \circ m$ is an idempotent endomorphism of $A \otimes A$. Since $s$ is injective, $\ker T = \ker m = \Omega^1(A)$, and every $x \in A \otimes A$ splits as $x = T(x) + (x - T(x))$ with $T(x) \in \operatorname{im} s$ and $x - T(x) \in \ker m$. The two pieces meet only in $0$: if $s(y) \in \ker m$ then $y = m(s(y)) = 0$. So $A \otimes A = \operatorname{im} s \oplus \Omega^1(A)$. Tensoring on the left with $A$ gives the summand $J^1(A) = A \otimes \Omega^1(A)$ of $A \otimes A \otimes A$.

No Frobenius or Hopf structure on $A$ is used. A Frobenius–Hopf structure on $A$ does not pass to $\Omega^1(A)$ by restriction; see the [remark](#rem:jet-comul-does-not-restrict). $\square$
