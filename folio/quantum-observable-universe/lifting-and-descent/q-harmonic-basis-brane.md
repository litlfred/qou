For each level $n$, the $q$-harmonic $1$-forms $\alpha_{n,i}$ belong to the space $\mathcal{H}^1(\mathcal{S}_n, \mathrm{ad} \mathcal{S}_n)$ of harmonic $1$-forms on $\mathcal{S}_n$ with values in the adjoint bundle $\mathrm{ad} \mathcal{S}_n$. They are characterised by the self-duality condition relative to the instanton connection $A_n$ ([recursive brane tower](#def:recursive-brane-tower)):

$$
  \alpha_{n,i} \in \mathcal{H}^1
  \iff
  d_{A_n}\alpha = 0
  \quad\text{and}\quad
  d_{A_n}^*\alpha = 0.
$$

The Hodge-star operator is induced by the Hermitian metric that the Levi form $L$ ([aether parameter](#def:substrate-parameter)) defines on the horizontal distribution. The index $i$ runs over $1 \leq i \leq b_1(\mathcal{S}_n)$ ([Betti numbers on branes](#rem:betti-numbers-brane)).

At the base level $n = 0$ the index range above gives $b_1(\mathcal{S}_0) = 3$, hence three primary basis vectors $\{\phi_1, \phi_2, \phi_3\}$. At level $n = 1$ the basis lifts: it includes the original modes together with their $J^1$ prolongations ([jet prolongation](#prop:jet-prolongation)), which act as the first-order resolutions of the base flows.
