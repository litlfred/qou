The preceding sections established $q$-torsion as a geometric invariant of the brane tower and showed that the descent-eigenspace mass decomposition encodes particle identity as a knot type. Positioning this within the categorical framework identifies a *particle at a point* as a knot localized at a categorical point of the projective line $\mathbb{P}(V)$. A *bundle* (the physical state at the initial moment) is a finite collection of such localized knots.

This formalizes the operational bundle scenario: an initial state comprises a finite set of pairs $(p_i, K_i)$ where $p_i \in \mathbb{P}(V)$ and $K_i$ is a knot type. The $q$-torsion drives the evolution of these positions across $\mathbb{P}(V) \cong \mathbb{CP}^1$, dictating the mechanical trajectories.
