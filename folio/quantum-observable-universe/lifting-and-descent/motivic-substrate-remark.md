The QOU framework's algebraic substrate appears through two faces: the [Frobenius–Hopf substrate](#def:frobenius-hopf-substrate) and the [adelic substrate](#def:adelic-substrate). Both realize a single underlying object: the [motivic substrate](#def:motivic-substrate), a Grothendieck-ring datum equipped with a tower of realization functors.

The non-archimedean realizations $\rho_p$ at a finite prime $p$ produce local cyclotomic data compatible with the Habiro-ring framework ([Habiro completion](#prop:full-pole-structure-mass-trace)).

The motivic substrate is taken axiomatically: the ring $K_0(\mathrm{Var}_k)$, the Lefschetz class $\mathbb{L}$, the localization $\widehat{\mathcal{M}}$, and the realization functors are posited as given data, and every downstream construction is stated conditionally on this substrate.
