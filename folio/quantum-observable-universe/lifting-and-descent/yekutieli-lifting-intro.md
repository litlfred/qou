The brane tower is constructed level by level. The existence of each level depends on the vanishing of a cohomological obstruction. Measuring this obstruction identifies it as a quantum characteristic class and upgrades a quantum universe to a quantum observable universe via Yekutieli's framework for central extensions of gerbes.

The section introduces the categorical lifting condition, which abstracts the gerbe-theoretic content: an $R$-linear rigid monoidal category $\mathbf{C}$ satisfies the condition with respect to a central extension $1 \to \mathcal{B} \to \mathfrak{G}' \to \mathfrak{G} \to 1$ when each object $x \in \mathfrak{G}$ carries a unique cohomology class $\kappa(x) \in H^2(\mathcal{S}, \mathcal{B})$ obstructing its lift. In the brane-tower setting, this obstruction is a quantum characteristic class that exists and is unique. The gerbe sequence arising from jet prolongation satisfies the quantum lifting condition: the level-$n$ to level-$(n+1)$ lift exists if and only if $\kappa_n = 0$.

Consequently, a quantum universe equipped with observability and a categorical lift is a quantum observable universe. The section interprets the obstruction class physically, connects it to the Kummer filtration, introduces the motivic Galois character $\chi^{\mathrm{mot}}_A$ to record the motivic refinement, and conjectures that $\chi^{\mathrm{mot}}_A$ is non-trivial at almost every finite place when $\chi(A) \neq 0$.
