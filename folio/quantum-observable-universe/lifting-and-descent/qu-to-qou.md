Let $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ be a [quantum universe](#def:quantum-universe) over a Noetherian ring $R$ with aether parameter $q$, satisfying the observability condition
$$
  \mathrm{Hom}(\mathbf{1}, \mathrm{Ad}(A)) = 0
$$
([quantum observable universe](#def:quantum-observable-universe)). Assume the brane tower $\{A_n\}_{n \geq 0}$ constructed via [jet prolongation](#prop:jet-prolongation) satisfies the [Quantum lifting condition](#def:yekutieli-lifting-condition). Then $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ is a quantum observable universe with the following structural properties:

1. *:refterm[Descent]{#descent} terminates.* The geometric :refterm[descent]{#descent} tower has finite length, bounded by $\mathrm{Krulldim} Z(\mathrm{End}_{\mathbf{C}}(A))$. Observability ensures each step is a strict reduction because the $G_q$-action is faithful.
2. *First obstruction equals the Euler class.* Let $\xi$ be the Reeb $\mathrm{U}(1)$-bundle over the base surface $\mathcal{S}$. The obstruction to lifting $\xi$ along the central extension $1 \to \underline{\mathbb{Z}} \to \underline{\mathbb{R}} \to \underline{\mathrm{U}(1)} \to 1$ of sheaves of smooth functions is $\kappa_0 = c_1(\xi) = e(\xi) \in H^2(\mathcal{S}, \mathbb{Z})$. When $\xi$ is the unit tangent bundle of the closed oriented surface $\mathcal{S}$, $e(\xi) = \chi(\mathcal{S})$.
3. *Kummer persistence.* For each $n \geq 1$, $\xi$ admits an $n$-th root, a lift along $1 \to \mu_n \to \mathrm{U}(1) \xrightarrow{(\cdot)^n} \mathrm{U}(1) \to 1$, if and only if $n \mid \chi(\mathcal{S})$. The obstruction is $\chi(\mathcal{S}) \bmod n \in H^2(\mathcal{S}, \mu_n) \cong \mathbb{Z}/n$. If $\chi(\mathcal{S}) \neq 0$, it is non-zero for all but finitely many $n$.
4. *Branch point formula.* The geometric :refterm[descent]{#descent} from $\mathbb{P}(V)$ ([projective line object](#def:projective-line-object)) to points has $B$ branch points with total ramification $R_{\mathrm{tot}} = 2d + \lvert\chi(A)\rvert$ via Riemann--Hurwitz, where $d$ is the degree of the covering.
5. *Terminal fiber data.* At each branch point $p$, the QOU reduces to fiber data $(A, \tau, q(p))$ with the involution $\sigma \colon \mathbb{P}(V) \hookrightarrow \mathrm{Ad}(A)$ ([fibre adjoint involution](#prop:fiber-adjoint-involution)).
6. *Lifting criterion.* Suppose a level transition is presented as a central extension $1 \to \mathcal{B} \to \mathcal{E} \to \mathcal{G} \to 1$ of presheaves of groups, and suppose the level-$n$ torsor lifts locally on the overlaps of a cover. Then it lifts to an $\mathcal{E}$-torsor if and only if its obstruction class in $\check{H}^2(\mathcal{B})$ is trivial.
