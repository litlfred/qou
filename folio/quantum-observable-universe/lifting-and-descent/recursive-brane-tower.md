Let $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ be a quantum observable universe over a ring $R$. Set $A_0 = A$ and inductively $A_{n+1} = J^1(A_n)$, the jet prolongation of the Frobenius–Hopf object $A_n$.

The **recursive brane tower** is the sequence
$$
  A_0 \hookrightarrow A_1 \hookrightarrow A_2 \hookrightarrow \cdots
$$
in $\mathbf{C}$, where each embedding is the canonical inclusion $A_n \hookrightarrow J^1(A_n) = A_{n+1}$ through the unit section.

Each $A_n$ is the **brane** of $A_{n+1}$.
