When $\dim M_k = 3$, no reduction has yet occurred: this is the top of the :refterm[descent]{#descent} ladder, where the geometric data are carried in full.

1. **Connection.** The $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ connection $A_k$ is unconstrained, and its curvature $F_A$ is a $2$-form on a $3$-manifold — the first stratum at which a $3$-form, and hence the inert $3$-curvature of the Yekutieli tower, has room to be non-zero. The holonomy of $A_k$ around loops is non-abelian here, but that is not what distinguishes the stratum: it is non-abelian at dimension $2$ as well. The distinction is carried by the *surface* holonomy and appears in the physical content below.
2. **Bundle.** Principal $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ bundles over a $3$-manifold are trivial, the first obstruction lying in $H^4(M_k; \pi_3 \mathrm{SU}(2))$, which vanishes on a $3$-complex. As at the stratum below, the content is in the connection rather than in the topology of $\mathcal{S}$ — the difference between the two rungs is what the connection is allowed to do, not how the bundle is twisted.
3. **Reeb direction.** The $q$-Reeb vector field ([$q$-Reeb vector field](#ex:q-reeb-vector-field)) is one direction of three, and the horizontal distribution it leaves is a genuine $2$-plane field. Quotienting by the Reeb flow is the first :refterm[descent]{#descent} step, and it is what produces the surface stratum ([:refterm[descent]{#descent} reduction to two dimensions](#rem:descent-reduction-two)).
