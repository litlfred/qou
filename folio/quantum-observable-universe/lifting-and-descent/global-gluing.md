Let $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ be a [quantum observable universe](#def:quantum-observable-universe) with aether parameter $q$. The local deformations of the Frobenius–Hopf object $A$ induce a twisted associative deformation admitting a unique global gluing via Yekutieli's twisted quantization map \cite{yekutieli2008, yekutieli2010}: the direct limit algebra $\mathcal{A}_\infty$ ([direct limit algebra](#def:direct-limit-algebra)) is a well-defined global section of a sheaf of twisted deformation algebras on the underlying site $\mathcal{S}$.
