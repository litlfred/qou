The brane tower carries a coherent QOU structure at every level. The categorical lifting machinery ensures these levels assemble coherently. One now passes to the colimit and studies the emergent algebra.

One defines the direct limit algebra $\mathcal{A}_\infty = \varinjlim_n \mathrm{End}_{\mathbf{C}}(A_n)$. In the smooth case, this recovers $\varinjlim \Gamma(J^1\mathcal{S}_n, \mathrm{SU}_{q_n}(2))$. One interprets this colimit through three lenses: Lusztig's integral form, the profinite jet shell, and Yekutieli's twisted-deformation stack. One then identifies the unique $G_q$-invariant vacuum state functional on $\mathcal{A}_\infty$. Twisted quantization glues the local algebras globally across the tower via the obstruction class. The admissible Gröbner-style ordering lifts to the colimit, providing the well-founded order essential for downstream sections.
