One explicitly realises the algebra $\mathcal{A}_\infty$ ([direct limit algebra](#def:direct-limit-algebra)) as the Lusztig integral form of the quantised enveloping algebra $\mathcal{U}_q(\mathfrak{su}(2))$ \cite{lusztig1993}. At each level $n$, one obtains the fibre algebra $\mathrm{SU}_{q_n}(2)$ by specialising the $\mathbb{Z}[q,q^{-1}]$-form $\mathcal{U}^{\mathbb{Z}}_q(\mathfrak{su}(2))$ ([substrate parameter](#def:substrate-parameter)) along $q \mapsto q_n \in k^{\times}$. The direct limit inherits the divided power basis $(E^{(m)}, F^{(m)}, K^{\pm 1})$ from the integral form. The $q$-deformed scaling function $\exp_q(r) - 1$ (equation [eq:q-exp](#eq:q-exp)) regulates the transition between the divided power bases of the space-like and time-like resolvents.

The specialisation is flat because the divided-power basis of the integral form is free over $\mathbb{Z}[q,q^{-1}]$ \cite{lusztig1993}, so it remains a basis after $q \mapsto q_n$.
