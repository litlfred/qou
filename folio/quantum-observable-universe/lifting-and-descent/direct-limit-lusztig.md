The algebra $\mathcal{A}_\infty$ ([direct limit algebra](#def:direct-limit-algebra)) admits an explicit realisation as the Lusztig integral form of the quantised enveloping algebra $\mathcal{U}_q(\mathfrak{su}(2))$ \cite{lusztig1993}. At each level $n$, the fibre algebra $\mathrm{SU}_{q_n}(2)$ arises by specialising the $\mathbb{Z}[q,q^{-1}]$-form $\mathcal{U}^{\mathbb{Z}}_q(\mathfrak{su}(2))$ ([substrate parameter](#def:substrate-parameter)) along $q \mapsto q_n \in k^{\times}$. The direct limit inherits the divided power basis $(E^{(m)}, F^{(m)}, K^{\pm 1})$ from the integral form. This ensures that the quaternionic jet embedding ([quaternionic spectral resolvents](#def:quaternionic-spectral-resolvents)) remains robust under the arithmetic :refterm[descent]{#descent} to $M_0$. The $q$-deformed scaling function $\exp_q(r) - 1$ (equation [eq:q-exp](#eq:q-exp)) regulates the transition between the divided power bases of the space-like and time-like resolvents.

The Gröbner--Shirshov normal-form theorem \cite{leitnerpawloski2004}, applied to $\mathrm{Dist}(G)$ \cite{fred2005}, guarantees the flatness of this specialisation: the divided-power basis is free over $\mathbb{Z}[q,q^{-1}]$ and hence remains a basis after $q \mapsto q_n$. In the $p$-adic setting, the ([Witt vector lift](#def:witt-vector-lift)) converts this algebraic flatness into a geometric statement. The formal group $\widehat{G}$ over $\mathbb{Z}_p$ reduces modulo $p$ to the commutative formal group $H$ over $\mathbb{F}_p$. The divided-power basis of $\mathrm{Dist}(\widehat{G})$ restricts to the PBW basis of $\mathrm{Dist}(H)$.
