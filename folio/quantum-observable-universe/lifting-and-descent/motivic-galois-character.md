Let $A$ be the Frobenius–Hopf substrate of a quantum observable universe, and let $[A] \in \widehat{\mathcal{M}}$ be its motivic class ([motivic substrate](#def:motivic-substrate)). The **motivic Galois character of $A$** is the homomorphism
$$
  \chi^{\mathrm{mot}}_A
  \;\colon\;
  \mathrm{Gal}\bigl(\mathbb{Q}(\mu_\infty)/\mathbb{Q}\bigr)
  \;\longrightarrow\;
  \mathrm{Aut}_{\widehat{\mathcal{M}}}\bigl([A]\bigr)
$$
giving the action of the cyclotomic Galois group on the realization tower $\{\rho_p\}_{p \in \mathrm{Pl}(\mathbb{Q})}$ ([adelic substrate](#def:adelic-substrate)) of $[A]$. At each finite place $p$, its **local component**
$$
  \chi^{\mathrm{mot}}_{A,\,p}
  \;\colon\;
  \mathrm{Gal}\bigl(\bar{\mathbb{Q}}_p/\mathbb{Q}_p\bigr)
  \;\longrightarrow\;
  \mathrm{Aut}\bigl(\rho_p([A])\bigr)
$$
is the Frobenius character on the cyclotomic projection $\rho_p \colon \widehat{\mathcal{M}} \twoheadrightarrow \mathbb{Z}[q]/\Phi_{p^k}(q)$.
