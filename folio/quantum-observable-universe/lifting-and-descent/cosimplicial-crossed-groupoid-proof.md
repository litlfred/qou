One verifies the cosimplicial identities and the crossed-module axioms.

The coface and codegeneracy maps satisfy the four cosimplicial identities ([cosimplicial identities](#def:ccg-cosimplicial-identities)). At each level $n$, the pair $(\mathrm{SU}_{q_n}(2), \mathcal{O}_M^{\times})$ forms a crossed module ([crossed-module structure](#lem:ccg-crossed-module-structure)).

*Compatibility.* The coface and codegeneracy maps respect the crossed-module structure because jet prolongation preserves the Hopf-algebra multiplication ([jet prolongation](#prop:jet-prolongation), Step 2) and the :refterm[descent]{#descent} homomorphism is a $*$-homomorphism ([:refterm[descent]{#descent}](#lem:descent), Step 4). Consequently, the conjugation action of $g_{ij}^{(n)}$ on $a_{ij}^{(n)}$ commutes with the simplicial maps, yielding a cosimplicial crossed groupoid. $\square$
