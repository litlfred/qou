This chapter extends the quantum observable universe $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ into a recursive hierarchy. Each level $A_n$ of this tower forms the jet prolongation of the previous one. The subsequent mechanisms of lifting, descent, and mass decomposition rely strictly on this tower. This section formally constructs it.

One first establishes that the jet object $J^1(A) = A \otimes \Omega^1(A)$ strictly inherits the Frobenius–Hopf structure and fibre functor, making $(\mathbf{C}, J^1(A), \tau', q)$ a valid QOU. Iterating this procedure algebraically generates the recursive brane tower $\{A_n\}_{n \geq 0}$. One then examines the smooth geometric model $J^1\mathcal{S}_n$ and defines the arc space $\mathcal{L}(X)$, which rigorously assembles the levels into a single ind-scheme. This tower provides the formal carrier for defining torsion and transport.
