Let $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ be a quantum observable universe with recursive brane tower $\{A_n\}_{n \geq 0}$ ([recursive brane tower](#def:recursive-brane-tower)) and aether parameter $q$. Equipped with the Yekutieli triples $(x^{(n)}, g^{(n)}, a^{(n)})$ ([Yekutieli triple](#def:yekutieli-triple)), the assignment

$$
  [n]  \longmapsto  \mathfrak{G}_n
$$

from the simplex category $\Delta$ to the $2$-category of gerbes on $M$ extends to a *cosimplicial crossed groupoid*. In this cosimplicial object:

1. The coface maps $\delta^i \colon \mathfrak{G}_{n} \to \mathfrak{G}_{n+1}$ are given by jet prolongation acting on objects (inserting the $i$-th face in the Čech nerve).
2. The codegeneracy maps $\sigma^j \colon \mathfrak{G}_{n+1} \to \mathfrak{G}_{n}$ are given by the :refterm[descent]{#descent} homomorphism $\mathfrak{d}_{n+1}$ (collapsing the $j$-th degeneracy).
3. The crossed structure is furnished by the conjugation action of the $1$-morphisms $g_{ij}^{(n)}$ on the $2$-morphisms $a_{ij}^{(n)}$ via the adjoint representation of $\mathrm{SU}_{q_n}(2)$.
