*Under the central extension $\mathfrak{G}_{n+1} \to \mathfrak{G}_n$ with local lift data $a_{ij}^{(n)} \in \mathcal{O}_M^{\times}$, assume the cocycle deviation at level $n+1$ is given by the recursion*

$$
  g_{ijk}^{(n+1)}
  =
  a_{ij}^{(n)} \cdot a_{jk}^{(n)} \cdot (a_{ik}^{(n)})^{-1}
  \cdot g_{ijk}^{(n)}.
$$

*Then the recursion holds at every level $n$ and for every triple $(i, j, k)$.*

*This lemma records the recursion as a hypothesis of the descent context; it does not derive it. Whether $g^{(n)}$ satisfies a twisted Čech $2$-cocycle equation, and whether it defines a class in $H^2(M, \mathcal{O}_M^{\times})$, is not part of this statement.*
