In the cosimplicial crossed groupoid of [cosimplicial crossed groupoid](#prop:cosimplicial-crossed-groupoid), the coface map $\delta^0 \colon \mathfrak{G}_n \to \mathfrak{G}_{n+1}$ applied to a local section $x_i^{(n)}$ produces the jet-prolonged section $x_i^{(n+1)} = J^1(x_i^{(n)})$. This is precisely the content of [jet prolongation](#prop:jet-prolongation): the jet prolongation carries information from level $\mathfrak{G}_n$ up to $\mathfrak{G}_{n+1}$, packaged as a coface map. The :refterm[descent]{#descent} is the map in the opposite direction: the left-inverse property $\sigma^0 \circ \delta^0 = \mathrm{id}$ shows that the codegeneracy $\sigma^0 = \mathfrak{d}_{n+1} \colon \mathfrak{G}_{n+1} \to \mathfrak{G}_n$ descends the prolonged section back to level $n$, inverting this map, underpinning the :refterm[descent]{#descent} involution ([:refterm[descent]{#descent} involution](#def:descent-involution)).
