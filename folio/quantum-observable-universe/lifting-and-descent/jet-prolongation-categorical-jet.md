*Let $S$ be a commutative algebra over a commutative ring $R$, let $I = \ker(m\colon S\otimes S \to S)$, and let $J^1(S) := (S \otimes S)/I^2$ be the [jet object](#def:categorical-jet). The section $\ell(a) = [a \otimes 1]$ and the left inverse $r\colon J^1(S) \to S$ induced by $m$ are $R$-algebra maps with $r \circ \ell = \mathrm{id}_S$, and $\ker r = I/I^2 = \Omega^1_{S/R}$. Hence $J^1(S) = \ell(S) \oplus \Omega^1_{S/R}$, and $S$ is a direct summand of $J^1(S)$.*
