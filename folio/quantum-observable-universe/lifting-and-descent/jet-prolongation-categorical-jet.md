*Let $A$ be an algebra over a commutative ring $R$, and let $\Omega^1(A) = \ker(m\colon A\otimes A \to A)$ be the bimodule of Kähler differentials. Then $\Omega^1(A)$ is a direct summand of the $R$-module $A \otimes A$, with complement the image of $a \mapsto a \otimes 1$. Hence the jet object $J^1(A) := A \otimes \Omega^1(A)$ is a direct summand of $A \otimes A \otimes A$.*
