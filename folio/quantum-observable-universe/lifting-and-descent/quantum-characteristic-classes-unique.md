Fix a [quantum observable universe](#def:quantum-observable-universe) and a recursive brane tower $\{A_n\}_{n \ge 0}$. Each [quantum characteristic class](#def:quantum-characteristic-classes) $\kappa_n \in H^2(\mathcal{S}, \mathcal{B})$ is uniquely determined as a cohomology class. Two Čech cocycles obtained from different local lifts of the same object of $\mathfrak{G}_n$ are cohomologous. They represent the same class $\kappa_n$.
