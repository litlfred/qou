Let $(\mathbf{C}, \varTheta, G, \mathcal{S})$ be an archimedean quantum observable universe ([$\mathbb{C}$-quantum observable universe](#ex:c-quantum-observable-universe)) equipped with an instanton connection $A$ on the principal bundle $P$ and a metric $g$ on $\mathcal{S}$. Denote the total space of $\mathcal{S}$ by $\widetilde{M} = \mathcal{S}$. Let $P'$ be the frame bundle and $\mathcal{S}'$ the bundle of orthonormal frames of the first jet bundle $J^1 \mathcal{S}$. The data $(A, g)$ uniquely determines a connection $A'$ on $P'$ such that $(\mathbf{C}, \tilde\varTheta, G, \mathcal{S}')$ structurally forms an archimedean quantum observable universe.

Dimension preservation is one of the **defining conditions** of a jet prolongation rather than a consequence of the others: the prolonged and base spaces are independent data, so nothing forces their CR dimensions to agree except the condition itself. The statement therefore fixes what a prolongation *is*, rather than deriving dimension equality as a corollary of the construction.
