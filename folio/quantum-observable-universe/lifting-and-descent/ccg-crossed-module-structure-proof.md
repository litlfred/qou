**Proof.**
At each level $n$, the pair $(\mathrm{SU}_{q_n}(2), \mathcal{O}_M^{\times})$ forms a crossed module \cite[Def.~1.2, Rem.~1.3]{yekutieli2014-descent}. The boundary map $\partial \colon \mathcal{O}_M^{\times} \to \mathrm{SU}_{q_n}(2)$ sends a unit $a \in \mathcal{O}_M^{\times}$ to the central element $a \cdot \mathbf{1} \in \mathrm{SU}_{q_n}(2)$ via the central extension. The action of $g \in \mathrm{SU}_{q_n}(2)$ on $a \in \mathcal{O}_M^{\times}$ is conjugation: $g \cdot a = g a g^{-1}$. This action is well-defined because $\mathcal{O}_M^{\times}$ is central in the gerbe band, rendering the Peiffer identity $\partial(a) \cdot b = a b a^{-1}$ automatic for the central extension. $\square$
