The map $\ell_n$ embeds $A_n$ as a direct summand retracted by $\mathfrak{d}_{n+1}$: we have $\mathfrak{d}_{n+1} \circ \ell_n = \mathrm{id}$ on $\mathrm{End}_{\mathbf{C}}(A_n)$. Therefore the [descent involution](#def:descent-involution) satisfies
$$
  \hat{\tau}_n^2 = \ell_n \circ \mathfrak{d}_{n+1} \circ \ell_n \circ \mathfrak{d}_{n+1} = \ell_n \circ (\mathfrak{d}_{n+1} \circ \ell_n) \circ \mathfrak{d}_{n+1} = \ell_n \circ \mathrm{id} \circ \mathfrak{d}_{n+1} = \hat{\tau}_n,
$$
so $\hat{\tau}_n$ is an idempotent. Its image, the descended subalgebra $\mathcal{A}_{n+1}^{\mathrm{desc}}$, contains the level-$(n+1)$ endomorphisms that factor through the level-$n$ algebra via ascent.
