Finite-time Navier-Stokes (NS) blowup corresponds to a finite obstruction level $n^*$ in the brane tower. At this level, a velocity field ceases to lift. A counter-example is encoded by the cohomological address $(v_0, T^*, \Sigma, n^*, \kappa_{n^*})$. The local cohomology at the unit defect $z=1$ controls the singular set $\Sigma$.
