At dimension $3$ the physical content is richest, and two of its features exist at no lower stratum.

1. **Primordial vortex.** Vortex filaments are curves in a $3$-manifold, hence of codimension $2$. This is the unique stratum at which they may knot and link. Every knot-theoretic statement in the framework — the braid closure, the Markov trace, the hyperbolic volume — is a statement about this stratum, and has no counterpart below it.
2. **Mass carries the knot.** The surface $\Sigma$ of the [mass identity](#def:mass-identity) is here a $2$-submanifold with boundary, and it is a genuine choice rather than being forced to be the base. Its boundary is a curve in $M_k$, which may be knotted. The $2$-holonomy reduces under the feedback to the holonomy of that boundary, up to a central element, so at this stratum the mass retains non-abelian content and that content is the boundary knot. Descending one step closes the surface and leaves only the central part.
3. **Lepton generations.** The base admits vertical harmonic forms up to degree $3$ ([Betti numbers on branes](#rem:betti-numbers-brane)), the largest range in the ladder. Because the Euler characteristic is stable under :refterm[descent]{#descent} and lifting ([Euler characteristic stability](#prop:euler-char-stability)), the value $\chi = -6$ fixed here is what every lower stratum inherits; the strata differ in the vertical degree available to realise it rather than in the count itself.
