The Kummer filtration of $H^2(\mathcal{S}, \mathbb{G}_m)$ maps to the Hochschild cohomology of the Frobenius–Hopf algebra $A$ via the Hochschild–Kostant–Rosenberg comparison:
$$
  HH^2(\mathcal{U}_q, \mathrm{Ad})
  \xrightarrow{ \mathrm{HKR}}
  H^2(\mathcal{S}, \mathbb{G}_m).
$$
The deformation-theoretic obstruction (Hochschild) and the gerbe-theoretic obstruction (Yekutieli) present two views of the same class. The Kummer filtration pulls back to a filtration on $HH^2$ measuring the $n$-divisibility of deformation obstructions \cite{yekutieli2010}.

**Motivic-Galois interpretation.** The Kummer filtration on $H^2(\mathcal{S}, \mathbb{G}_m)$ restricts the **motivic-Galois filtration** to the cyclotomic subgroup $\mathrm{Gal}(\mathbb{Q}(\mu_\infty)/\mathbb{Q})$. Under the [motivic substrate](#def:motivic-substrate) realization tower $\{\rho_v\}$, the Hochschild class $\kappa \in HH^2(\mathcal{U}_q, \mathrm{Ad})$ projects at each finite place $p$ to a motivic Chern class $\rho_p(\kappa) \in H^1_{\mathrm{Gal}}(\mathrm{Gal}(\bar{\mathbb{Q}}_p/\mathbb{Q}_p), \mu_n(1))$ through the cyclotomic ideal $\mathbb{Z}[q]/\Phi_n(q)$. Perpetual non-vanishing of $\kappa_n$ at every finite place $p \nmid \chi(A)$ ([Kummer tower](#rem:kummer-tower)) means the motivic Galois character of the Frobenius–Hopf substrate is non-trivial in $\mathrm{Hom}(\chi_n, 1)$ for almost every $n$. The HKR comparison bridges the gerbe (geometric) and Hochschild (deformation) realizations of this single motivic class.
