The motivic-Galois layer on the obstruction $\kappa_n \in H^2(\mathcal{S}, \mathbb{G}_m)$ acts via a single character of the cyclotomic Galois group: the [motivic Galois character](#def:motivic-galois-character) of the Frobenius–Hopf substrate. The obstruction $\kappa_n$ ([quantum characteristic classes](#def:quantum-characteristic-classes)) is the image of $\chi^{\mathrm{mot}}_A$ under the [HKR comparison](#rem:hochschild-kummer-connection) to $H^2(\mathcal{S}, \mathbb{G}_m)$.

**Choice of level.**
Working directly with $\chi^{\mathrm{mot}}_A$ replaces per-level Čech-cocycle non-vanishing arguments with a single non-vanishing theorem about the character ([motivic perpetual obstruction](#thm:motivic-perpetual-obstruction), proved via Route A: prime-ideal collapse, $C = a_0(-1) + 2 \cdot a_1(-1)$). The Čech-cocycle and Hochschild presentations of $\kappa_n$ become realizations of $\chi^{\mathrm{mot}}_A$ at the archimedean and Hochschild-deformation places. The [Hochschild–Kummer](#rem:hochschild-kummer-connection) duality corresponds to the duality between these two realizations.

Conditional on the [motivic substrate](#def:motivic-substrate), the character $\chi^{\mathrm{mot}}_A$ is defined and is compatible with each local realization $\rho_p$.
