At each point $p \in M$, the fibre algebra $\mathcal{O}_{q(p)}(\mathrm{SU}(2))$ is a pronilpotent deformation of $\mathcal{O}(\mathrm{SU}(2))$ in the formal parameter $\hbar_q(p) = 1 - q(p)^{-1}$ ([quantum planck constant](#def:quantum-planck-constant)). According to \cite{yekutieli2008}, the coordinate bundle $\mathrm{Coor}_M$ of $M$ (the bundle of formal coordinate systems) carries a twisted deformation datum: a connection and a Maurer–Cartan element in a pronilpotent DG Lie algebra \cite{yekutieli2009}. The obstruction classes $\kappa_n$ ([quantum characteristic classes](#def:quantum-characteristic-classes)) control whether these local twisted deformations glue across coordinate patches.

The vanishing condition $\mathrm{Hom}(\mathbf{1}, \mathrm{Ad}(A)) = 0$ ([quantum observable universe](#def:quantum-observable-universe)) ensures the gerbe $\mathfrak{G}_n$ of local lifts has trivial band at the level of isomorphisms. Consequently, by Theorem 0.10.1 of \cite{yekutieli2015-twisted-dq}, the twisted deformations at each fibre patch together into a global sheaf of algebras on $M$. The direct limit $\mathcal{A}_\infty = \varinjlim\Gamma(J^1 \mathcal{S}_n, \mathrm{SU}_{q_n}(2))$ comprises the global sections of this sheaf, and the vacuum state $\tau$ ([vacuum state](#prop:vacuum-state)) extends uniquely via the $\mathrm{SU}(2)$-averaging argument of that proposition.
