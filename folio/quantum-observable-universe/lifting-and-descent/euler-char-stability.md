Let $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ be a [quantum observable universe](#def:quantum-observable-universe) with archimedean realisation over the base curve $M_0$, and let $\{A_n\}_{n \geq 0}$ be its [recursive brane tower](#def:recursive-brane-tower). Two quantities are stable under jet prolongation, and they are different quantities.

1. *The categorical trace.* $\mathrm{tr}(\mathrm{id}_{A_{n+1}}) = \mathrm{tr}(\mathrm{id}_{A_n})$ in $\mathrm{End}(\mathbf{1}) \cong R$: prolongation preserves the rigidity data of $A_n$, hence its quantum dimension.
2. *The Euler characteristic of the base.* $\chi_{\mathrm{eu}}(M_0)$ is unchanged, because prolongation alters the bundle data over $M_0$ and never $M_0$ itself.

The Euler characteristic of the total space $\mathcal{S}_n$ is **not** stable: for the projectivised frame bundle $S^2 \hookrightarrow \mathcal{S}_{n+1} \to \mathcal{S}_n$, multiplicativity gives $\chi_{\mathrm{eu}}(\mathcal{S}_{n+1}) = 2\,\chi_{\mathrm{eu}}(\mathcal{S}_n)$.
