Let $\ell_n \colon A_n \hookrightarrow A_{n+1}$ be the ascent embedding ([jet prolongation](#prop:jet-prolongation)) and let $\mathfrak{d}_{n+1} \colon \mathrm{End}_{\mathbf{C}}(A_{n+1}) \to \mathrm{End}_{\mathbf{C}}(A_n)$ be the :defterm[descent]{#descent} homomorphism ([descent](#lem:descent)). The **descent involution** at level $n$ is the endomorphism
$$
  \hat{\tau}_n = \ell_n \circ \mathfrak{d}_{n+1} \colon \mathrm{End}_{\mathbf{C}}(A_{n+1}) \longrightarrow \mathrm{End}_{\mathbf{C}}(A_{n+1}).
$$
Its image is the **descended subalgebra** $\mathcal{A}_{n+1}^{\mathrm{desc}} \subseteq \mathrm{End}_{\mathbf{C}}(A_{n+1})$.
