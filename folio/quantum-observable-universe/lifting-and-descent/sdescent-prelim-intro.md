This section describes how structure restricts from $A_{n+1}$ to $A_n$ in the brane tower. The restriction map $r_n \colon A_n \to A_{n-1}$ induces a surjective algebra homomorphism $\mathfrak{d}_n$ on the endomorphism algebras. This map intertwines the $G_q$-actions. Each descending level inherits the complete quantum observable universe (QOU) structure. Composing ascent and descent forms the idempotent descent involution $\hat{\tau}_n = \ell_n \circ \mathfrak{d}_{n+1}$. Its image isolates the descended subalgebra within $\mathrm{End}_{\mathbf{C}}(A_{n+1})$. Both mechanisms are evaluated in the $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ archimedean model. The lifting obstruction calculus requires both the descent involution and the surjectivity of $\mathfrak{d}_n$.
