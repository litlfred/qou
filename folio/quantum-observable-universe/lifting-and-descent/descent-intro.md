The :refterm[descent]{#descent} machinery connects the infinite brane tower to finite-dimensional geometry. Given a quantum observable universe $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ with brane tower $\{A_n\}_{n \geq 0}$ ([recursive brane tower](#def:recursive-brane-tower)), the :refterm[descent]{#descent} lemma ([:refterm[descent]{#descent} lemma](#lem:descent)) shows that each level $A_n$ inherits the full QOU structure from $A$. The :refterm[descent]{#descent} involution ([:refterm[descent]{#descent} involution](#def:descent-involution)) then splits each $A_n$ into eigenspaces whose direct limit recovers the physical algebra. This section develops the :refterm[descent]{#descent} involution, its eigenspace decomposition, and the resulting mass-decomposition formula.

This arc proceeds in three steps. The first step verifies the existence and uniqueness of the descent involution at each tower level; the fixed point of the brane recursion forces this involutive splitting. The second step exhibits the eigenspace decomposition $A_n = A_n^+ \oplus A_n^-$ and identifies the $+$-eigenspace as the physical part of the algebra—the part that survives the classical limit and feeds the mass observables. Finally, these per-level decompositions assemble into the mass-decomposition formula. The next section's cosimplicial machinery promotes this formula into a genuine higher-categorical structure.
