Let $\xi$ denote the Reeb derivation of $A$ ([categorical Reeb derivation](#def:categorical-reeb)) and let $[\cdot,\cdot]_q$ denote the $q$-Lie bracket ([categorical $q$-Lie bracket](#def:categorical-q-lie-bracket)). The *$q$-deformed Reeb derivation* $\xi_q$ on $A$ is

$$
  \xi_q(a) = q^{-1}\xi(a) + (1 - q^{-1})[\xi, X_a]_q,
$$

where $X_a$ is the horizontal derivation associated to $a$, satisfying $\pi_H(da) = \iota_{X_a} \varTheta_A$. The **$q$-Reeb flow** is the unique one-parameter family of automorphisms $\phi_t^q \in \mathrm{Aut}(A)$ generated by $\xi_q$.

```tex
\begin{equation}\label{eq:q-reeb-flow-diagram}
\begin{tikzcd}[row sep=2.5em, column sep=3em]
  A \ar[r, "\phi_t^q"] \ar[d, "\xi_q"']
  & A \ar[d, "\xi_q"] \\
  A \ar[r, "\phi_t^q"']
  & A
\end{tikzcd}
\qquad (\phi_0^q = \mathrm{id})
\end{equation}
```
