**The local cohomology connection.**
The [local cohomology group](#def:local-cohomology-group) $H^1_{\mathfrak{m}_1}(A)$ at the unit defect $z = 1$ measures the obstruction to extending sections across the singularity. At $q = 1$, the defect locus $\{z = 1\}$ is the entire base manifold. The substrate parameter is identically $1$, so:
$$
  H^1_{\mathfrak{m}_1}(A)\big|_{q=1}
  = 
  R^1\Gamma_{\mathfrak{m}_1}(A)
   \neq  0.
$$
This nonvanishing isolates the algebraic origin of the blowup. The $\mathfrak{m}_1$-torsion functor has a nontrivial derived functor exactly at $q = 1$, corresponding to regularity failure.

For $q > 1$, the defect locus $\{z = q^{-1}\} \neq \{z = 1\}$ separates from the unit, giving $H^1_{\mathfrak{m}_1}(A) = 0$. The obstruction vanishes. Regularity holds.
