The mathematics here traces the iterated jet prolongation $A_{n+1} = J^1(A_n)$. The term *brane* refers to each localized layer $A_n$, representing a geometric boundary enclosing level-$n$ internal degrees of freedom.

The lifting condition \cite{yekutieli2010} that governs extensions from $n$ to $n+1$ is termed the *quantum lifting condition*, while the qualifier *Yekutieli* is reserved for the nonabelian multiplicative integration \cite{yekutieli2015} over the tower. These results control structural assembly rather than the Kähler-differential jet object itself; accordingly, the sequence is termed simply the *(recursive) brane tower*.
