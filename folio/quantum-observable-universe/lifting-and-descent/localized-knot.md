Let $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ be a quantum observable universe carrying the [projective line object](#def:projective-line-object) $\mathbb{P}(V) = G/T$. A **localized knot** is a pair
$$
  (p, K) \in \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathbf{C}}(\mathbf{1}, A^{\mathrm{co}T}) \times \mathrm{Knot}
$$
where:
1. $p \colon \mathbf{1} \to A^{\mathrm{co}T}$ is a [categorical point](#def:categorical-point) of the projective line;
2. $K$ is a knot type, an equivalence class of smooth embeddings $S^1 \hookrightarrow S^3$ under ambient isotopy.
