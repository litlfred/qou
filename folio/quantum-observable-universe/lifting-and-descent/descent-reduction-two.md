When $\dim M_k = 2$, the geometric data of the archimedean quantum observable universe are those of a closed surface.

1. **Connection.** The $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ connection $A_k$ retains its full non-abelian character — its curvature $F_A$ is a Lie-algebra-valued $2$-form, and the holonomy around loops in the surface is in general non-abelian, exactly as at the stratum above. What changes is degree: $F_A$ is now of *top* degree, so no $3$-form survives on a $2$-manifold and the inert $3$-curvature of the Yekutieli tower has no support here.
2. **Bundle.** Principal $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ bundles over a closed orientable surface are classified by $\pi_1(\mathrm{SU}(2)) = 0$ and are therefore trivial. The universe's content at this stratum lies entirely in the connection, not in the topology of $\mathcal{S}$.
3. **Reeb direction.** The Reeb direction is not merely constrained here — it is what the :refterm[descent]{#descent} step removed. The passage from $\dim M_k = 3$ is the Reeb quotient, so the $q$-Reeb vector field ([$q$-Reeb vector field](#ex:q-reeb-vector-field)) has been divided out and the horizontal distribution is the whole tangent space.

The :refterm[descent]{#descent} continues to a $1$-dimensional base ([:refterm[descent]{#descent} reduction](#rem:descent-reduction)), where the remaining two directions collapse to the longitudinal $\mathrm{U}(1)$ backbone.
