**What the theorem says.** The Euler characteristic $\chi(\mathcal{S}) = -6$ of Bring's surface fixes the topology of the Reeb bundle $\xi$: its Euler class is $-6$, so $\xi$ has no nowhere-vanishing section, and $\xi$ has an $n$-th root exactly for $n \in \{1, 2, 3, 6\}$. These are statements about the global class on the surface $\mathcal{S}$. On any one-dimensional locus $\Sigma$ every such class restricts to zero, so the theorem gives no obstruction on a vortex-collision curve and no statement about blow-up of a flow.
