Fix an integer $N \geq 3$ and set $q = \zeta_N = e^{2\pi i / N}$, a primitive $N$-th root of unity. Let $\mathbf{C}_{\mathrm{any}}(N)$ be the **truncated category**, the quotient of $\mathrm{Rep}(U_q(\mathfrak{su}_2))$ by the ideal of negligible morphisms \cite{moore-seiberg1989}.

The **observable modular tensor category** at level $N$ is the ribbon fusion category $\mathbf{C}_{\mathrm{any}}(N)$, equipped with the braiding $c$ and ribbon twist $\varTheta$ inherited from the quasi-triangular structure of $U_q(\mathfrak{su}_2)$ at $q = \zeta_N$.
