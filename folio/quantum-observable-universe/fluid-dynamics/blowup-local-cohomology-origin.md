**The local cohomology connection.**
The [local cohomology group](#def:local-cohomology-group) $H^1_{\mathfrak{m}_1}(A)$ at the unit defect $z = 1$ measures the algebraic obstruction to extending sections across the degenerate parameter fibre. At $q = 1$, the defect locus $\{z = 1\}$ coincides with the unit parameter point:

$$
  H^1_{\mathfrak{m}_1}(A)\big|_{q=1}
  = 
  R^1\Gamma_{\mathfrak{m}_1}(A)
   \neq  0.
$$

This nonvanishing records the algebraic degeneration of the $q$-deformed family at $q = 1$, where the $\mathfrak{m}_1$-torsion functor acquires a nontrivial derived functor, rather than establishing finite-time PDE blowup.

For $q > 1$, the shifted locus $\{z = q^{-1}\} \neq \{z = 1\}$ separates from the unit and $H^1_{\mathfrak{m}_1}(A) = 0$, matching the non-degenerate regime of the $q$-deformed model.
