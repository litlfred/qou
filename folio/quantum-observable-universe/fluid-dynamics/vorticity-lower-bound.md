**Conjecture (vorticity lower bound, conditional on [q-Aubin--Lions](#conj:q-aubin-lions)).** Let $v^*$ be the weak limit as $q \to 1$ of the q-NS solution for anti-parallel vortex pair initial data. Let $\omega^* = \nabla \times v^*$ and let $d(t)$ be the separation between the two filament cores. There exist constants $c_1, c_2 > 0$ such that for all $t < T^*$:

$$
  c_1\sqrt{T^* - t} \leq d(t) \leq c_2\sqrt{T^* - t},
  \qquad
  \lVert \omega^*(t) \rVert_{L^\infty} \geq \frac{c_1}{T^* - t},
  \qquad
  \int_0^{T^*} \lVert \omega^*(t) \rVert_{L^\infty}\,dt = +\infty.
$$

These give, respectively, (a) separation decay, (b) a vorticity lower bound, and (c) BKM divergence. In particular, the limit solution $v^*$ loses regularity at $T^*$.
