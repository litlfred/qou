**Braiding non-symmetry.** Evaluating $N \geq 3$ mapping any definitively non-trivial simple object $\mathbf{V} \in \mathbf{C}_{\mathrm{any}}(N)$, the math guarantees

$$
  c_{\mathbf{V}, \mathbf{V}} \circ c_{\mathbf{V}, \mathbf{V}}
    \neq  \mathrm{id}_{\mathbf{V} \otimes \mathbf{V}}.
$$

The mathematical braiding strictly refuses to square cleanly back into the identity across valid simples. This functions as the absolute hallmark denoting a genuinely modular (strictly non-symmetric) tensor category. It acts as the rigid absolute prerequisite unlocking valid anyonic statistics.

**Relation to the commutative limit.** The commutative limit $q \to 1$ corresponds to scaling $N \to \infty$. At this limit, the categorical truncation opens up, negligible morphisms vanish, and the braiding degenerates into a symmetric structure. Consequently, the two specialisations separate:

| Specialisation | $q$ | Braiding | Anyons |
|---|---|---|---|
| Commutative limit | $q = 1$ | strictly symmetric | no |
| Root-of-unity $\mathrm{MTC}$ | $q = \zeta_N$ ($N \geq 3$) | strictly non-symmetric | yes |

The geometric shadow projected onto the genus-$2$ curve $\mathcal{X}_5$ ([the hyperelliptic quintic](#def:hyperelliptic-quintic)) isolates either regime through its fifth-roots-of-unity branch set. Within the commutative limit, the values $\zeta_5^{k}$ mark classical phase defects across a single $\mathrm{U}(1)$ tube; at $q = \zeta_5$, the same discrete data labels the anyonic fusion channels of $\mathbf{C}_{\mathrm{any}}(5)$.
