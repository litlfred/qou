**Acoustic metric and kinematic scalar horizon analogue.**
Longitudinal phase perturbations $\delta\psi$ around an irrotational background flow satisfy the scalar d'Alembertian equation $\Box_{g_{\mathrm{ac}}}\delta\psi = 0$ with respect to the Gordon acoustic metric

$$
  g_{\mathrm{ac}}
  \;=\;
  \frac{\rho}{c_s}
  \begin{pmatrix}
    -(c_s^2 - v^2) & -v^T \\
    -v & \mathbf{1}
  \end{pmatrix},
  \qquad
  c_s^2 = \frac{\partial P}{\partial\rho}\bigg|_{Q_q}.
$$

Wherever $|v| = c_s$, $g_{\mathrm{ac}}$ forms a **kinematic scalar acoustic horizon** that traps longitudinal sound cones in direct Painlevé–Gullstrand analogy with stationary black-hole horizons: center-commuting scalar excitations ($[F_A, \alpha] = 0$) follow null acoustic bicharacteristics, whereas non-commuting defect modes ($[F_A, \alpha] \neq 0$) couple to quantized vortex filaments of $\psi$. Because the irrotational Madelung ansatz propagates only a single longitudinal scalar polarization on a fixed background foliation, $g_{\mathrm{ac}}$ captures scalar causal-cone kinematics alone; the two transverse-traceless spin-2 metric polarizations and full diffeomorphism-invariant gravitational dynamics belong to [GR substrate geometry](#rem:gr-substrate-geometry).
