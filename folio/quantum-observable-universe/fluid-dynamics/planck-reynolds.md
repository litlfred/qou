The [aether parameter](#def:substrate-parameter) $q$ determines the three Reynolds-number variables in the brane fluid: characteristic velocity $c_q = 1/(q-1)$, characteristic length $\ell_q = (q-1)/q$, and dynamic viscosity $\nu_q = \hbar_q/2 = (q-1)/(2q)$. The corresponding **Planck--Reynolds number** is:

$$
  \mathrm{Re}_q
  = \frac{c_q \cdot \ell_q}{\nu_q}
  = \frac{2}{q-1}.
$$

| Regime | $q$ | $\mathrm{Re}_q$ | Character |
|---|---|---|---|
| Classical | $\to 1^+$ | $\to \infty$ | Fully turbulent |
| Physical | $\approx 1.110$ | $\approx 18.2$ | Transitional |
| Deep quantum | $\gg 1$ | $\to 0$ | Laminar |

The aether parameter functions as an inverse turbulence dial. At $q = 1$, the Reynolds number diverges and unleashes the full Kolmogorov cascade, but the contact structure collapses and particle states vanish. At the physical anchor $q \approx 1.110$, the fluid operates in the transitional zone: turbulence supports the structured [discrete vortex spectrum](#prop:discrete-vortex-spectrum) without obliterating it into a continuum.

The laminar--turbulent boundary emerges at $\mathrm{Re}_q \sim 1$, corresponding to $q \sim 3$. Active Kelvin modes require $[k]_q \leq \mathrm{Re}_q^{1/2}$. At the physical anchor $q \approx 1.110$, this constraint dictates a cutoff $k_{\max} \approx 4$, matching exactly the four observed [intensity levels](#rem:vortex-intensity-modes) ($j = \frac{1}{2}, 1, \frac{3}{2}, 2$).
