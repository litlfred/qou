The [conservation of categorical helicity](#prop:helicity-conservation) physically manifests topological charge conservation within the fluid. Under smooth *inviscid* (Euler) evolution, vortex lines are transported by the flow, so the evolution cannot alter the total helicity of a vortex configuration. It merely redistributes helicity among writhe, twist, and linking, as demonstrated by [trefoil chirality flips](#rem:trefoil-vortex-chirality-flip). The statement does not extend to viscous flow. For Navier--Stokes with viscosity $\nu > 0$ the helicity $H = \int v \cdot \omega\,dV$ obeys $dH/dt = -2\nu \int \omega \cdot \mathrm{curl}\,\omega\,dV$, which has no sign and does not vanish in general, and vortex lines of any knot or link type can reconnect while the solution stays smooth \cite{enciso2017}.

The exact quantisation of $\Gamma_i^{(q)} = [j_i]_q[j_i+1]_q$ mandates that each term in the helicity sum remains individually quantised. Consequently, helicity is restricted to a discrete lattice structured by the $\mathrm{SU}_q(2)$ representation labels $\{j_i\}$.
