At $q = \zeta_N$ the representation category $\mathrm{Rep}(U_q(\mathfrak{su}_2))$ is no longer semisimple; quotienting out the ideal of negligible morphisms yields the truncated category $\mathbf{C}_{\mathrm{any}}(N)$, with finitely many simple objects $\mathbf{V}_0, \mathbf{V}_1, \ldots, \mathbf{V}_{N-2}$ \cite{moore-seiberg1989}. The [observable modular tensor category](#def:observable-mtc) is *modular*: the $S$-matrix $S_{ij} = \mathrm{tr}(c_{\mathbf{V}_i, \mathbf{V}_j} \circ c_{\mathbf{V}_j, \mathbf{V}_i})$ is non-degenerate.
