The three-dimensional state-sum and lattice constructions built from a fusion category apply only in the second regime of [the root-of-unity truncation](#prop:root-of-unity-truncation), not at the aether parameter itself.

The Turaev--Viro invariant $|M|_{\mathbf{C}}$ and its identification $|M|_{\mathbf{C}} = \mathrm{RT}_{Z(\mathbf{C})}(M)$ with the Reshetikhin--Turaev invariant of the Drinfeld centre are stated for a spherical fusion category $\mathbf{C}$ with $\dim \mathbf{C} \neq 0$, and the state sum runs over a finite representative set of simple objects \cite{turaev-virelizier2010}; the extended theory \cite{balsam2010} and the modularity of the centre \cite{bruguieres-virelizier2008} carry the same hypothesis. The Levin--Wen string-net Hamiltonians are built from a finite label set whose total quantum dimension $\sum_i d_i^2$ is finite \cite{levin2005}.

For generic $q > 1$ the category of $\mathrm{SU}_q(2)$ representations has infinitely many simple objects $V_j$, each with quantum dimension $[2j+1]_q > 0$, so it is not fusion. A real $q > 1$ is never a root of unity: $q^n \neq 1$ in $\mathbb{C}$ for every $n \geq 1$. The finite quotient on which these constructions are defined therefore does not exist at the [aether parameter](#def:aether-parameter) $q_0$, and the constructions do not apply there as stated.

What does carry over to generic $q$ is the pre-fusion layer: the $6j$-symbols and their pentagon (Biedenharn--Elliott) identities. A state sum written with them has infinitely many labels, and its convergence is not established. The constructions themselves apply to the truncated categories at $q = e^{i\pi/\kappa}$; how their $\kappa \to \infty$ behaviour relates to $q_0$ is a separate analytic question that this remark does not address.

The invariance of these state sums under a flip of a surface triangulation is an associativity (F-move) identity. It holds for spherical fusion categories that admit no braiding, such as the category of vector spaces graded by a nonabelian finite group, so it is not the braid relation $\sigma_0\sigma_1\sigma_0 = \sigma_1\sigma_0\sigma_1$.
