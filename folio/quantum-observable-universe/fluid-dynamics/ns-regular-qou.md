Let $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ be a [quantum universe](#def:quantum-universe) over a closed orientable spectral surface $\mathcal{S}$ with [brane tower](#def:recursive-brane-tower) $\{A_n\}_{n \geq 0}$ and [quantum characteristic classes](#def:quantum-characteristic-classes) $\kappa_n \in H^2(\mathcal{S}, \mathcal{B})$.

The **NS obstruction** of $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ is the global surface Euler class of the Reeb $\mathrm{U}(1)$-bundle $\xi$ on $\mathcal{S}$:

$$
  \mathrm{NS}(A)
  =
  \kappa_0
  =
  e(\xi)
   \in
  H^2(\mathcal{S}, \mathbb{Z}) \cong \mathbb{Z}.
$$

The universe $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ is:

- **NS-regular** if $\mathrm{NS}(A) = 0$;
- **NS-singular** if $\mathrm{NS}(A) \neq 0$.
