The preceding section established the $q$-deformed Navier–Stokes system on $\Omega^1(A)$ alongside its global regularity for any aether parameter $q > 1$. This section builds the obstruction tower governing how that regularity breaks down as $q \to 1$. It also isolates the modular tensor category structure on the limiting observables that survives this degeneration.

The argument proceeds in two tracks. The first six blocks assemble the obstruction tower over the collision locus of two anti-parallel vortex filaments. The boundary observability $\mathrm{Obs}_\partial(A)$ serves as the kernel of the restricted obstruction classes, computed directly via Kummer specialisation and matched against the torus-NS-regular case using root-of-unity truncation. The second track extracts the observable modular tensor category and its inherent braid-group action. This braided action constitutes the categorical residue of the fluid flow, serving as the foundational input for the research programme in the subsequent section.

The two-vortex amalgamation ([approach to exceptional divisor](#sec:approach-to-exceptional-divisor)) extends to three. During a triple-vortex collision, the merged fundamental group $\pi_1(U \setminus K_3)$ hosts a three-strand braid representative. The categorical instanton operates on $\mathbf{V}^{\otimes 3}$ via the $B_3$-representation $\rho_3$ of the [non-symmetric braid-group representation](#prop:bn-mtc-action). Semidefinite and linear programming minimisation identifies the Brunnian (pairwise-unlinked, triple-linked) Borromean braid $\mathcal{B}_3 = (\sigma_0\sigma_1^{-1})^3$ of the [Borromean quark conjecture](#conj:borromean-quark) as the dominant physical representative. Confinement directly matches the non-vanishing triple Milnor invariant $\bar{\mu}_{123}$.
