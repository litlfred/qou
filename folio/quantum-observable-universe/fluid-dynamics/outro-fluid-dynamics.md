This chapter has developed the $q$-deformed Navier--Stokes model on the brane, separating its proved algebraic structure and conditional $q > 1$ regularity framework from the open analytical questions surrounding its $q \to 1^+$ degeneration.

The next chapter ([*Stochastic Mechanics*](#chap:stochastic-mechanics)) develops the Markov-trace transition dynamics on the same categorical state space, connecting the $q$-shell hierarchy and classical-limit degeneration to Nelson-type stochastic processes and adelic $q \to 1$ limits.
