The $q$-deformed hydrodynamics constructed in previous sections yields a closed evolution equation on $\Omega^1(A)$. Using the spectral theory of the $q$-Laplacian and the quantum Peter--Weyl decomposition of the brane, this section establishes global regularity of that evolution for every $q > 1$.

The central result establishes global regularity of the $q$-deformed Navier--Stokes equation: for every $q > 1$, smooth finite-energy initial data extends uniquely to a smooth solution for all time. Three structural mechanisms drive the proof — exponential ultraviolet damping, the enstrophy bound, and nonlinear uniqueness. Spectral analysis and Sobolev embedding for the $q$-Laplacian on $\Omega^1(A)$ supply compact resolvent and the categorical Hodge embedding chain, while the quantum Peter--Weyl theorem establishes completeness of the $\mathrm{SU}_q(2)$ eigenbasis. Combining these spectral inputs with the $q$-Ladyzhenskaya inequality closes the enstrophy evolution and uniqueness estimates. The subsequent section interprets this $q > 1$ regularity theorem as the deformed side of the categorical degeneration and analyses the obstruction arising at $q = 1$.
