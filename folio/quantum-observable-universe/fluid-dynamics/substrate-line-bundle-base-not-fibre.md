**Key property: the QOU structure resides in the base rather than the fibre.**
The rigid triple $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ paired with the core QOU axioms anchors exclusively to the **base** of the family. The aether parameter $q$ acts merely as a coordinate riding on the **fibre** $\mathbb{G}_m$.

Consequently:

- $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathbf{C}}(\mathbf{1}, \mathrm{Ad}(A))$ is strictly independent of $q$.
- $c_1(\mathrm{Ad}(A))$ and $c_2(\mathrm{Ad}(A))$ are strictly independent of $q$.
- The Euler class $e(\xi)$ tracking the Reeb bundle is strictly independent of $q$.

Whereas $\chi(A)$ is a topological invariant of the base, $\mathrm{tr}(\mathrm{id}_A)$ varies along the fibre.

The entire architecture of the obstruction tower $\{\kappa_n \in H^2(\mathcal{S}, \mathcal{B})\}$ is a fixed property of $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ rather than a fluid artefact of the section $q$.
