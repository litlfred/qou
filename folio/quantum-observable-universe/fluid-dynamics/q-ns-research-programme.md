The conditional [global regularity statement](#thm:q-ns-wellposedness) and the [regularity-breakdown conjecture](#conj:regularity-breakdown) organise the brane $q$-Navier--Stokes model around a one-parameter family of deformed hydrodynamic equations and its $q \to 1^+$ degeneration.

**Scope of the $q$-deformed formulation.**
For aether parameter $q > 1$, exponential growth of the $q$-Laplacian eigenvalues $[k]_q^2$ and positive induced viscosity $\nu_q = (q - 1)/(2q)$ supply regularising mechanisms absent at $q = 1$. Studying $q \to 1^+$ probes how those internal mechanisms degenerate, while separating the unrescaled inviscid Euler limit ($\nu_q \to 0$) from fixed-viscosity classical Navier--Stokes dynamics on $\mathbb{R}^3$.

**Five open problems in the $q$-NS programme.**

**Problem 1. Concrete $q$-NS evolution and unconditional well-posedness for $q > 1$.**
Construct a concrete $q$-NS evolution on $\mathcal{O}_q(\mathrm{SU}_2)$ and discharge the existence, uniqueness, and smoothness hypotheses of [global regularity](#thm:q-ns-wellposedness) from the compact-resolvent and exponential-eigenvalue bounds of the $q$-Laplacian.

**Problem 2. Enstrophy behaviour as $q \to 1^+$.**
Determine whether the algebraic-layer enstrophy supremum $\Omega(q)$ diverges as $q \to 1^+$ (Part A of [regularity breakdown](#conj:regularity-breakdown)) and, if so, determine the critical exponent $\alpha$ in $\Omega(q) \sim (q - 1)^{-\alpha}$ beyond heuristic closures.

**Problem 3. Singular-set and vortex-line geometry.**
Clarify admissible singular-set and vortex-line twist configurations in the $q \to 1^+$ limit, testing branch (B) of the [Levi degeneracy conjecture](#conj:levi-degeneracy) on the twist scalar $\xi\cdot\mathrm{curl}\,\xi$ and separating point singularities of discretely self-similar profiles from filament-reconnection events ([vortex reconnection](#rem:vortex-reconnection), [Berry moduli](#rem:berry-vortex-moduli)).

**Problem 4. Universality across QOU realisations.**
Test whether any $q \to 1^+$ critical exponent depends only on the categorical invariants of the QOU (such as $\chi(A)$ and gauge rank) or varies with initial data and modal coupling structure.

**Problem 5. Compactness and unit-layer bridge at $q \to 1^+$.**
Establish compactness for $q$-NS trajectories as $q \to 1^+$ (toward weak Euler solutions in the unrescaled algebraic layer, or toward weak Navier--Stokes solutions in a $c_q$-rescaled or fixed-viscosity family) and determine whether [acoustic horizon formation](#prop:acoustic-horizon-conditions) or vortex-pair approach produces genuine finite-time singularities.

**Summary.**
Within the QOU model, the unconditionally proved fluid-dynamical results are algebraic — the Hecke two-vortex channel algebra, the $\mathcal{O}_q(\mathrm{SU}_2)$ $q$-Legendre triad bounds, discrete shell tail collapse, exact Beltrami flows, and global surface Euler classes — while unconditional $q > 1$ well-posedness and classical three-dimensional regularity or blowup at $q = 1$ are open questions.
