**Definition.**
*For a valid quantum universe $(A, \varTheta)$, the **NS obstruction class** is defined precisely as*

$$
  \mathrm{NS}(A) \;\in\; H^3_{\mathrm{loc}}(A, \varTheta).
$$

*It derives directly from evaluating the continuous obstruction tower driving the $q$-Navier--Stokes system exactly at $q = 1$. Mechanically, $\mathrm{NS}(A)$ acts as the obstruction checking the lift of the $q$-NS solution from brane level $n$ cleanly into level $n + 1$ as $q \to 1$.*

*The NS obstruction binds to the Euler characteristic $\chi(A)$:*

- *$\mathrm{NS}(A) = 0$ holds if and only if the quantum universe remains [NS-regular](#def:ns-regular-qou). The incompressible Navier--Stokes equation secures global regularity.*
- *$\mathrm{NS}(A) \neq 0$ mandates finite-time blowup of initially smooth solutions.*

*Setting.* Throughout this chapter the classical problem is the **unforced** incompressible Navier--Stokes equation ($f \equiv 0$) with smooth initial data: Fefferman's alternatives (A) on $\mathbb{R}^3$ and (B) on $\mathbb{T}^3$ in the Clay formulation. Statements about regularity or blowup refer to this case. Blowup driven by a smooth external force (alternatives (C) and (D)) is a different problem and is not what $\mathrm{NS}(A)$ addresses.
