Let $(A, \varTheta)$ be a valid quantum universe over a spectral surface $\mathcal{S}$ with Reeb bundle $\xi$. Its **NS obstruction class** is the global Euler class of $\xi$ in the surface realisation ([NS-regular QOU](#def:ns-regular-qou)):

$$
  \mathrm{NS}(A) \;=\; e(\xi) \;\in\; H^2(\mathcal{S}, \mathbb{Z}) \cong \mathbb{Z}.
$$
