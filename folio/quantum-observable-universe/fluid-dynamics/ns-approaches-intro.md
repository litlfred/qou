The classical Navier--Stokes regularity question sits inside a one-parameter family of well-posed $q$-deformed equations. The degeneration at $q = 1$ forms a research programme. This section treats the classical limit as a phase transition and identifies the critical exponent of enstrophy divergence. This structural prediction depends only on the Euler characteristic. It formulates a $q$-deformed Aubin--Lions compactness lemma and adapts the Beale--Kato--Majda blowup criterion. Conditional on these conjectures, the representation-theoretic truncation at roots of unity then locates the remaining gap in the growth rate of the vorticity bound. It tracks the geometry of vortex collision via the Levi degeneracy and explicit Townsend strain dynamics. Finally, it provides a self-similar Leray blowup profile constrained by $\mathrm{SU}_q(2)$ representation theory.
