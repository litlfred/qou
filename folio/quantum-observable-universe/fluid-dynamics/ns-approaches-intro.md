The classical Navier--Stokes regularity question sits inside a one-parameter family of well-posed $q$-deformed equations, and its degeneration at $q = 1$ forms a research programme. Treating the classical limit as a phase transition isolates the critical exponent of enstrophy divergence as a structural prediction depending only on the Euler characteristic. The programme formulates a $q$-deformed Aubin--Lions compactness lemma, adapts the Beale--Kato--Majda blowup criterion, tracks the geometry of vortex collision via Levi degeneracy and explicit Townsend strain dynamics, and constructs a discretely self-similar Leray blowup profile constrained by $\mathrm{SU}_q(2)$ representation theory.
