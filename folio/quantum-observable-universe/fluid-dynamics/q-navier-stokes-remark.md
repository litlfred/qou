The [quantum Euler equation](#rem:madelung-fluid) models inviscid flow in the brane fluid. To account for dissipation — the irreversible degradation of coherent vortex motion into thermal fluctuations — one introduces the [$q$-deformed Navier--Stokes equation](#def:q-navier-stokes).

The kinematic viscosity $\nu_q$ stems exclusively from the [substrate parameter](#def:substrate-parameter); it is an induced parameter, not an independent physical constant.

**Incompressibility.**
The constraint $\delta_q v = 0$ ($v \in \ker(\delta_q|_{\Omega^1})$) stands in for the classical $\nabla \cdot v = 0$. The [observability condition](#def:quantum-observable-universe) $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathbf{C}}(\mathbf{1}, \mathrm{Ad}(A)) = 0$ prevents trivial gauge modes from fulfilling this constraint.
