Let $q > 1$ and let $c_q = 1/(q-1)$ be the canonical substrate scale. The classical limit $q \to 1^+$ of the internal $q$-Navier–Stokes dynamics is taken in one of two **unit layers**.

The **substrate layer** has coordinates $(x, t)$ as carried by the substrate, wavenumbers the $q$-integers $[n]_q$, and viscosity

$$
  \nu_q \;=\; \frac{q-1}{2q}.
$$

The **physical layer** has coordinates $(X, T) = (c_q\,x,\; c_q\,t)$, velocity unrescaled, and viscosity

$$
  \nu_{\mathrm{phys}} \;=\; c_q\,\nu_q.
$$

Every statement about the classical limit of a $q$-fluid quantity carries exactly one of the two layer tags.
