Within the conditional [root-of-unity truncation](#conj:root-of-unity-truncation) ansatz, finite-level mode cutoffs bound the cross-sectional core aspect ratio $\kappa(t)$ by $1 + C(\kappa - 1)$, isolating uniform-in-$\kappa$ control of core flattening as an open analytical question of the $q \to 1$ accumulation. On a three-dimensional flow domain, this cross-sectional aspect ratio is distinct from contact non-degeneracy of the vorticity plane field, which is governed instead by the single Levi scalar $\xi\cdot\mathrm{curl}\,\xi$ ([core Levi form](#prop:core-levi-form)).
