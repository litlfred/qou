**Regularity as lifting.**
Conditional on [$q$-NS well-posedness](#thm:q-ns-wellposedness), a solution $v_q(t)$ of the $q$-deformed model is smooth for $q > 1$. Along transfer-free modes (or whenever nonlinear transfer is dominated by exponential $[k]_q^2$ dissipation), the modal amplitudes decay faster than any polynomial and the jet-lifting obstructions vanish across the brane tower:

$$
  |\hat{v}_k(t)| \leq |\hat{v}_k(0)|e^{-\nu_q[k]_q^2 t}
  \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
  \kappa_n(v_q(t)) = 0 \quad \forall n.
$$

Because $\nu_q [k]_q^2 \sim \varepsilon\, q^{2k}$ grows exponentially in $k$ for fixed $q > 1$, such modes lift cleanly to all jet levels; in the $q \to 1^+$ limit both $\nu_q \to 0$ and $[k]_q^2 \to k^2$ lose this exponential margin.
