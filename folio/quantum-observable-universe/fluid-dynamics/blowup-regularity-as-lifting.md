**Regularity as lifting.**
The [q-NS solution](#thm:q-ns-wellposedness) $v_q(t)$ is smooth for $q > 1$. The exponential damping of high modes ensures $v_q$ has exponentially decaying Fourier coefficients. All obstruction classes vanish:
$$
  |\hat{v}_k(t)| \leq |\hat{v}_k(0)|e^{-\nu_q[k]_q^2 t}
  \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
  \kappa_n(v_q(t)) = 0 \quad \forall n.
$$
The decay rate $\nu_q [k]_q^2 \sim \varepsilon\, q^{2k}$ exceeds any polynomial. The field $v_q$ lifts through the tower to all levels.
