Item 1: by [QU-to-QOU](#thm:qu-to-qou), item 2, $e(\xi) = c_1(\xi) = \chi(\mathcal{S}) = -6$ for the unit tangent bundle. A nowhere-vanishing section would trivialise $\xi$ and force $e(\xi) = 0$.

Item 2: by [QU-to-QOU](#thm:qu-to-qou), item 3, $\xi$ has an $n$-th root if and only if $n \mid \chi(\mathcal{S}) = -6$, with obstruction $-6 \bmod n$; see also the [Kummer specialisation](#prop:ns-kummer-specialisation). The positive divisors of $6$ are $1, 2, 3, 6$.

Item 3: $\Sigma$ has dimension $1$, so $H^2(\Sigma, M) = 0$ for every coefficient group $M$ (Godement II.5.12).

*Scope of the curve restriction.* Item 2 holds for the global class on $\mathcal{S}$ ($\kappa_0 \bmod 2\kappa$ for $\kappa \geq 4$), whereas on $\mathcal{S}$ the jet corrections $\delta_n$ lie in the image of $H^2(\mathcal{S}, \mathcal{O}_{\mathcal{S}}) = 0$ and on any one-dimensional locus $\Sigma \subset \mathcal{S}$ the restriction $e(\xi)|_\Sigma$ vanishes identically by item 3. Moreover, the [core-Levi form](#prop:core-levi-form) yields a single Levi scalar $\xi\cdot\mathrm{curl}\,\xi$, and an explicit Beltrami solution refutes the implication from non-degenerate Levi data to finite-time blow-up (branch (A) of the [Levi degeneracy conjecture](#conj:levi-degeneracy)). Conditional blow-up criteria via vorticity lower bounds and [BKM divergence](#thm:q-bkm) require a non-vanishing cohomological obstruction on $\Sigma$, which does not exist when $\dim \Sigma = 1$. Whether the Reeb field vanishes dynamically along $\Sigma$ is a separate PDE question outside the scope of this theorem. $\square$
