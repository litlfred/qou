*Proof.*
Item 1: by [QU-to-QOU](#thm:qu-to-qou), item 2, $e(\xi) = c_1(\xi) = \chi(\mathcal{S}) = -6$ for the unit tangent bundle. A nowhere-vanishing section would trivialise $\xi$ and force $e(\xi) = 0$.

Item 2: by [QU-to-QOU](#thm:qu-to-qou), item 3, $\xi$ has an $n$-th root if and only if $n \mid \chi(\mathcal{S}) = -6$, with obstruction $-6 \bmod n$; see also the [Kummer specialisation](#prop:ns-kummer-specialisation). The positive divisors of $6$ are $1, 2, 3, 6$.

Item 3: $\Sigma$ has dimension $1$, so $H^2(\Sigma, M) = 0$ for every coefficient group $M$ (Godement II.5.12).

*Boundary with curve-localised blowup arguments.* Kummer survival of $\kappa_0 \bmod 2\kappa$ for $\kappa \geq 4$ holds as a statement about the global class on $\mathcal{S}$ (item 2), whereas restricting $e(\xi)$ or jet corrections $\delta_n$ to a one-dimensional curve $\Sigma$ vanishes identically because $H^2(\mathcal{S}, \mathcal{O}_{\mathcal{S}}) = 0$ and $H^2(\Sigma, M) = 0$ (item 3). Likewise, on the three-dimensional flow domain the [core-Levi form](#prop:core-levi-form) reduces to a single Levi scalar $\xi\cdot\mathrm{curl}\,\xi$, and non-degeneracy of that scalar does not force blow-up (see the Beltrami counterexample in the [Levi degeneracy conjecture](#conj:levi-degeneracy)). Conditional blow-up estimates such as the [vorticity lower bound](#conj:vorticity-lower-bound) and [BKM divergence](#thm:q-bkm) would require a non-vanishing class on $\Sigma$ that dimension-$1$ sheaf cohomology does not supply; whether the Reeb vector field vanishes along $\Sigma$ remains a separate dynamical question. $\qquad\blacksquare$
