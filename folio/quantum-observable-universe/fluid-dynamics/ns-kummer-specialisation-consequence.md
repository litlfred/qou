For a QOU over a closed surface $\mathcal{S}$ with $\chi_{\mathrm{eu}}(\mathcal{S}) = -6$, the [Kummer specialisation](#prop:ns-kummer-specialisation) gives a non-zero global class $\chi_{\mathrm{eu}}(\mathcal{S}) \bmod n \in H^2(\mathcal{S}, \mu_n)$ at every level $n \notin \{1, 2, 3, 6\}$. In the internal surface classification, this places the model in the categorically NS-singular class ($\mathrm{NS}(A) = e(\xi) = -6 \neq 0$).

This global Kummer non-divisibility does not, however, produce a non-zero curve restriction $\kappa_n|_\Sigma$ or establish finite-time blowup for the classical Navier--Stokes equation. Item 3 of [QU-to-QOU](#thm:qu-to-qou) holds in the Kummer cohomology groups $H^2(\mathcal{S}, \mu_n)$ rather than as a complementary-subgroup splitting inside $H^2(\mathcal{S}, \mathbb{G}_m)$, which vanishes on a closed surface ($H^2(\mathcal{S}, \mathbb{G}_m) = 0$). Furthermore, on any one-dimensional collision locus $\Sigma \subset \mathcal{S}$, the restriction $e(\xi)|_\Sigma$ vanishes identically because $H^2(\Sigma, \cdot) = 0$. The non-zero class $\chi_{\mathrm{eu}}(\mathcal{S}) \bmod n$ therefore obstructs $n$-th roots of the Reeb bundle on $\mathcal{S}$ for $n \nmid 6$, without deciding regularity of the three-dimensional PDE.
