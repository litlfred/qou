A valid QOU constrained by $\chi_{\mathrm{eu}}(M_0) = -6$ is **NS-singular**: $\mathrm{Obs}_\partial(A) = 0$. The boundary observability vanishes. The classical Navier--Stokes equation permits finite-time blowup.

This result stems from the [complementary-subgroup argument](#thm:qu-to-qou) (item 3). The core topological obstruction $e(\xi)|_\Sigma \neq 0$ lacks any mathematical channel to suffer cancellation against the operative analytic correction $\delta_n|_\Sigma$. They strictly populate complementary summands inside $H^2(\mathcal{S}, \mathbb{G}_m)$.

*Scope.* This consequence is not established. It rests on item 3 of [QU-to-QOU](#thm:qu-to-qou) in its earlier, jet-tower form, which is retracted. On a closed surface $H^2(\mathcal{S}, \mathbb{G}_m) = 0$, so there are no complementary summands in it. The restriction $e(\xi)|_\Sigma$ to the one-dimensional collision locus is zero, because $H^2(\Sigma, \cdot) = 0$. What survives is the [Kummer specialisation](#prop:ns-kummer-specialisation) itself: the global class $\chi(A) \bmod n \in H^2(\mathcal{S}, \mu_n)$ is non-zero for $n \nmid 6$. That is a statement about $n$-th roots of the Reeb bundle, not a regularity verdict. The classical blowup sentence is not supported here; see the disclaimer in [the Descartes NS theorem](#thm:ns-singularity-descartes).
