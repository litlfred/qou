A valid QOU constrained by $\chi_{\mathrm{eu}}(M_0) = -6$ is **NS-singular**: $\mathrm{Obs}_\partial(A) = 0$. The boundary observability vanishes. The classical Navier--Stokes equation permits finite-time blowup.

This result stems from the [complementary-subgroup argument](#thm:qu-to-qou) (item 3). The core topological obstruction $e(\xi)|_\Sigma \neq 0$ lacks any mathematical channel to suffer cancellation against the operative analytic correction $\delta_n|_\Sigma$. They strictly populate complementary summands inside $H^2(\mathcal{S}, \mathbb{G}_m)$.

*Scope.* This consequence is not established: item 3 of [QU-to-QOU](#thm:qu-to-qou) holds in the Kummer tower rather than as a complementary-subgroup splitting in the jet tower. On a closed surface $H^2(\mathcal{S}, \mathbb{G}_m) = 0$, so there are no complementary summands in it, and the restriction $e(\xi)|_\Sigma$ to the one-dimensional collision locus vanishes identically because $H^2(\Sigma, \cdot) = 0$. What holds unconditionally is the [Kummer specialisation](#prop:ns-kummer-specialisation) itself: the global class $\chi(A) \bmod n \in H^2(\mathcal{S}, \mu_n)$ is non-zero for $n \nmid 6$. That is a statement about $n$-th roots of the Reeb bundle, not a regularity verdict, so the classical blowup claim above is not established here.
