For a quantum observable universe with $\chi(A) = -6$, the [Kummer specialisation](#prop:ns-kummer-specialisation) matches item 3 of [QU-to-QOU](#thm:qu-to-qou): the global Kummer class $\chi(A) \bmod n \in H^2(\mathcal{S}, \mu_n)$ is non-zero for every $n \nmid 6$, obstructing the existence of a global $n$-th root of the Reeb bundle $\xi$.

*Scope.* On a closed complex curve $\mathcal{S}$, $H^2(\mathcal{S}, \mathbb{G}_m) = 0$, and on any one-dimensional collision curve $\Sigma$, $H^2(\Sigma, \cdot) = 0$ forces the restriction $e(\xi)|_\Sigma$ to vanish identically. Consequently, the Kummer class on $\mathcal{S}$ records an algebraic obstruction to extracting $n$-th roots of the Reeb bundle rather than a complementary-summand cancellation barrier on $\Sigma$ or a direct blowup verdict for classical Navier--Stokes; see the scope boundary in [the Descartes NS theorem](#thm:ns-singularity-descartes).
