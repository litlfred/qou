These estimates adapt classical two-dimensional Ladyzhenskaya and enstrophy methods to the $q$-deformed Sobolev spaces on $\Omega^1(A)$. For $q > 1$, the convergent series $C_q = (\sum_{k=1}^\infty [k]_q^{-2})^{1/2} < \infty$ supplies the embedding constant within the quantum-group harmonic basis, whereas $C_q$ diverges in the three-dimensional $q \to 1^+$ degeneration.
