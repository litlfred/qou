It opens with Remark: Vortex intensity modes: from classical fluids to $q$-deformation, which contrasts four classical mechanisms for discrete vortex spectra with the $q$-deformed mechanism to anchor the comparison. It then proves Proposition: Discrete vortex mode spectrum — the eigenvalues of the $q$-Laplacian on a filament are $[k]_q^2 \cdot \hbar_q / L_q^2$ — and interpret the resulting levels in Remark: Vortex intensity levels as fluid-dynamical orbitals. The central structural result is Proposition: Categorical helicity conservation: helicity operates as a topological invariant of the braided monoidal structure. Remark: Helicity as quantised topological charge reads this invariant as a discrete charge. It closes with Remark: Vortex reconnection as divisor surgery, establishing the birational picture of filament recombination. The subsequent section, **Turbulence and acoustics**, uses this to build the $q$-deformed energy cascade on tangles of these filaments.
