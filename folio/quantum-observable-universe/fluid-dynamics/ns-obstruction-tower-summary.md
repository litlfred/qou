Restricting $\kappa_n$ to the collision locus $\Sigma$ tests how the NS obstruction behaves across the levels of the brane tower. The [NS obstruction-tower proposition](#prop:ns-obstruction-tower), conditional on its stated hypotheses, reads a non-zero restriction as NS-singular and a vanishing one as NS-regular. As its proof notes, $\Sigma$ is one-dimensional, so $H^2(\Sigma, \cdot) = 0$ and the criterion as stated does not discriminate.

Furthermore, survival of $\kappa_n|_\Sigma$ cannot be identified with non-degeneracy of the Levi form or with a bounded core aspect ratio: the Levi datum of the vorticity plane field is the single scalar $\xi\cdot\mathrm{curl}\,\xi$, and the core aspect ratio is not a Levi quantity. The [Levi degeneracy conjecture](#conj:levi-degeneracy) concerns $\xi\cdot\mathrm{curl}\,\xi$ on the high-vorticity set, and no result connects it to $\kappa_n|_\Sigma$.
