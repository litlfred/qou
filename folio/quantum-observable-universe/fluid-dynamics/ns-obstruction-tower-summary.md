The NS obstruction is measured across the levels of the brane tower by restricting $\kappa_n$ to the collision locus $\Sigma$. The [proposition](#prop:ns-obstruction-tower), conditional on the conjectures named in its banner, reads a non-zero restriction as NS-singular and a vanishing one as NS-regular. As its proof notes, $\Sigma$ is one-dimensional, so $H^2(\Sigma, \cdot) = 0$ and the criterion as stated does not discriminate.

An earlier version of this summary identified the survival of $\kappa_n|_\Sigma$ with non-degeneracy of the Levi form, that is, with a bounded core aspect ratio. That identification is withdrawn. The Levi datum of the vorticity plane field is the single scalar $\xi\cdot\mathrm{curl}\,\xi$, and the core aspect ratio is not a Levi quantity. The [Levi degeneracy conjecture](#conj:levi-degeneracy) is now a statement about $\xi\cdot\mathrm{curl}\,\xi$ on the high-vorticity set, and no result connects it to $\kappa_n|_\Sigma$.
