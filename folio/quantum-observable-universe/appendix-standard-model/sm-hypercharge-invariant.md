The knot-state groupoid is conjectured to carry a **hypercharge invariant**: functions $I_3, Y$ on states, constant on gauge orbits, satisfying the Gell-Mann–Nishijima relation

$$
  Q \;=\; I_3 + \tfrac{1}{2}\,Y
$$

against the derived charge $Q$ of [the Coulomb-charge definition](#def:coulomb-charge), such that $Y$ does *not* factor through the writhe $w$.

The non-factorization clause is the sharp content, and it is not an arbitrary stipulation: the proton and neutron share $Y = +1$ while their writhes differ, so any writhe function fails on the nucleon doublet, which falsifies the writhe as a hypercharge assignment. By the *factorization criterion* ($Y$ factors through $w$ if and only if $Y$ is constant on writhe fibres), any hypercharge invariant forces a writhe-degenerate pair of states separated by $Y$. The conjecture therefore asserts precisely that the state space carries one more independent $\mathrm{U}(1)$'s worth of gauge-invariant data than the writhe provides.

One pure-braid candidate for the second datum is recorded without endorsement in [the truncation conjecture](#conj:sm-gauge-truncation): the ribbon-framing twist, with the helon model as literature precedent \cite{bilson-thompson2005}. On the bundle side, passing from the single rank-$2$ base spinor $V_2$ to the split rank-$5$ fibre $F = V_2 \oplus W_3^*$ over Bring's degree-$3$ cyclic Kummer cover fixes $\mathrm{U}(1)_Y$ uniquely without free parameters: the block-scalar centralizer $\mathrm{diag}(y_2 I_2, y_3 I_3)$ of $\mathfrak{gl}_2 \oplus \mathfrak{gl}_3$ satisfies tracelessness $2y_2 + 3y_3 = 0$ if and only if $y_3 = -\frac{2}{3}y_2$, reproducing the full chiral generation hypercharge spectrum on $\Lambda^1 F \oplus \Lambda^2(F^*)$ together with its global $\mathbb{Z}_6$ kernel and cancellation of all five gauge and gravitational anomalies \cite{baez-huerta2010}. Lifting that bundle-level grading to canonical per-state invariants on the knot-state groupoid (so that each topological state carries $(I_3, Y)$ directly without tuning a framing label) is the open content of this groupoid conjecture.
