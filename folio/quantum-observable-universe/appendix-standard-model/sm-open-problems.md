The boundary between what is established and what is open is now sharp. Scalar restriction along Bring's degree-$3$ cyclic Kummer $\mathbb{Z}_3$-cover $\pi$ constructs the rank-$3$ pushforward bundle $W_3 = \pi_*\mathcal{L}$ alongside the rank-$2$ base spinor $V_2$. On the split bundle $F = V_2 \oplus W_3^*$, the unimodular gauge subalgebra $\mathfrak{su}(3)_{\mathrm{c}} \oplus \mathfrak{su}(2)_L \oplus \mathfrak{u}(1)_Y$, traceless fractional hypercharge $y_3 = -\frac{2}{3}y_2$, global $\mathbb{Z}_6$ kernel, and five-anomaly cancellation on $\Lambda^1 F \oplus \Lambda^2(F^*)$ are proved unconditionally. Also proved are Jimbo's $N = 3$ braided $R$-matrix with $z_3 = q^3/[3]_q$, ultraviolet mixing $\sin^2\theta_W^{\mathrm{UV}} = 3/8$, one-loop beta multiplicities $(7, \frac{19}{6}, -\frac{41}{10})$, and Spinor–Minkowski $V_2 \otimes \bar{V}_2 \simeq \mathbb{R}^{1,3}$ worldline Schwinger propagators. Three major theoretical frontiers stand unproved. First is the archimedean two-loop $\Delta_2$ photon self-energy (and per-loop normalisation) needed to close $\alpha_{\mathrm{EM}}^{-1}$ at the hundredfold-experimental-precision target. Second is three-generation replication together with the Yukawa, CKM, and PMNS textures and per-state framed braid hypercharge. Third is dynamical groupoid freeze-out and the non-perturbative four-dimensional Yang–Mills continuum limit.
