The following ledger consolidates the epistemic status of every structural ingredient this appendix uses; the per-parameter status of the 26 Standard-Model parameters is tabulated in the parameter sections above and is not repeated here. Items marked *open* name the missing object precisely; none of them is discharged elsewhere in this paper.

| Hypercharge $Y$ | open: writhe map falsified; candidate second datum defined — the strand twist, with horizontal gauge invariance proved and the first-generation spectrum battery run — but that battery corroborates **nothing**: it collapses to the single linear equation $T = 3Q$ per flavour (the $-2I_3$ common to candidate and target cancels, leaving the *defining* equation of $Q_{\mathrm{twist}}$ rather than a test of it), the nucleon check is entailed by charge additivity, and the $\mathbb{Z}_6$ condition never sees the assignment. Each flavour is therefore filtered independently, so the $4$ survivors are $1 \times 2 \times 2 \times 1$ multiset counting against totals $3Q = (2,-1,0,-3)$ — **not** a $2500\!:\!1$ filter — and the "uniquely determined" $u$ and $e$ multisets are simply the two totals admitting a single multiset. Whether the twist multisets are *forced* by the categorical braid words is the entire open content; the braid words are **not** missing — [the knot-particle dictionary](#tbl:knot-particle-dictionary-table4-data) gives the quark braid element in $\mathcal{B}_3$ as a single generator ($\sigma_i$ for $\mathfrak{u}$, $\sigma_i^{-1}$ for $\mathfrak{d}$, $\mathfrak{s}$, $\mathfrak{b}$), so the quark writhe is $\pm 1$; what this *closes off* is the braid element as carrier, since the anti-up and the down share $\sigma_i^{-1}$ yet carry $-2/3$ and $-1/3$. The genuinely missing datum is the **environment** label — [the up quark](#def:up-quark) is "the unknot distinguished by its environment", and nothing formalizes that colour-tube datum. | Three-generation structure (why three copies of each fermion?) | **open**, with an adjacent proved divisibility lemma. The genus bound $g(T_{2,n}) = (n-1)/2 \leq 4$ admits odd windings $n \in \{3,5,7,9\}$, and the continuant divisibility $b_a \mid b_n \iff a \mid n$ singles out $9 = 3\cdot3$ as the unique harmonic ($b_9 = b_3\,(h^6+6h^4+9h^2+1)$), leaving $\{3,5,7\}$. **This does not derive the number three.** Within $\{3,5,7,9\}$ the divisibility criterion selects exactly the composite, and compositeness carries no generation content — any criterion removing one of four candidates leaves three. The framework's other filter gives a different count: $S_5$-equivariance admits only $\{3,5\}$, since $S_5$ has no element of order $7$ or $9$, and it classes the tau together with the $n = 9$ ghost as symmetry-broken ([symmetry-preserving leptons](#rem:symmetry-preserving-leptons), [$S_5$-equivariant torus knots](#prop:s5-equivariant-torus-knots)). Nothing but harmonicity separates the observed tau from the unobserved ghost. Also open (scope-(a)): the physical bridge "normal-form-independent winding $\Rightarrow$ generation" and the $\mathrm{net}\to$ mass map | [neutrino-sector section](#prose:sm-params-neutrino); [generation exclusion](../knots-particles-confinement/lepton-meson-torus-knot-dictionary.md) |

Three of these are the items a derivation of the Standard Model cannot do without. The hypercharge assignment requires a second crossing invariant independent of the writhe; the strand twist datum now supplies a gauge-invariant candidate with the falsifiable consistency $Q_{\mathrm{twist}} = Q$ against the crossing-derived charge, but deriving the per-fermion twist multisets from framework states — rather than enumerating them — remains open. The $\mathrm{SU}(3)$ factor now has its algebra: the octonion stabilizer with proved dimension gate and bracket closure; what it lacks is an action on the framework's own state space, which is the surviving open content of the colour conjecture. And the truncation mechanism — why the observable-scale symmetry should be a finite-dimensional Lie group rather than the full groupoid symmetry — has no derivation; every use of the phrase "freeze-out" in this appendix is a heuristic label for that gap, not an explanation.
