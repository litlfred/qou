A derivation of the Standard Model must ultimately account for its free parameters. In the counting used here, the renormalisable Standard Model carries 19 parameters — 9 charged-fermion masses, 4 quark-mixing (CKM) parameters, 3 gauge couplings, 2 Higgs-sector parameters, and the strong-CP angle — and the neutrino-sector extension adds 7 more (3 masses, 3 mixing angles, 1 Dirac CP phase), for a total of 26. If the neutrinos are Majorana, two further phases appear; they are noted in the neutrino section and not counted in the 26.

The following sections walk the 26 parameters in chunks, one section per chunk, and record each parameter's status using the tiers of [the appendix introduction](#prose:sm-intro). Those tiers distinguish *input* (one of the three calibration observables) and *proxy* (exact by construction under the canonical pin) from *partial* (conditional structure with unclosed remainder), *conjectural* (precise identification without derivation), and *open* (unclosed).

The headline count is stated up front so that the chunk tables cannot be misread as progress claims. Of the 26 parameters, **1 is an input** ($m_e$, the dimensional unit), **1 is a calibration proxy** ($m_\mu$, pinning the aether parameter through the $\beta$-sector bar-skew calibration), **1 is partial** ($\alpha_{\mathrm{EM}}$, conditional on two-loop remainder $\Delta_2$ and per-loop measure), **2 are conjectural** ($\sin^2\theta_W$, $m_H$), and **21 are open**. No parameter beyond the calibration set is currently derived unconditionally to experimental precision.
