We conjecture that the colour algebra acts faithfully and linearly on the framework's state space: writing $\mathfrak{g}$ for the colour algebra — the intended realisation is the stabilizer $\mathfrak{su}(3) = \ker(\mathrm{ev}_e) \subset \mathrm{Der}(\mathbb{O})$ of a unit imaginary octonion, the Günaydin–Gürsey colour subalgebra, carried here with the proved gate $\dim \mathfrak{su}(3) = 8 \iff \mathrm{rank}(\mathrm{ev}_e) = 6$ — there is an injective $\mathbb{Q}$-linear map

$$
  \Phi \colon \mathfrak{g} \longrightarrow \mathrm{End}(V)
$$

into the endomorphisms of the three-strand state space $V$. This is the missing $\mathrm{SU}(3)$ ingredient of [the truncation conjecture](#conj:sm-gauge-truncation) at the infinitesimal level: the colour algebra is constructed here \cite{baez2002} \cite{dubois-violette2016} but no construction makes it *act* on the states. Bracket compatibility is the natural strengthening, deliberately omitted from the class so that the conjecture records only the minimal missing ingredient; bracket *closure* of the concrete stabilizer is itself proved — $\mathfrak{su}(3) = \ker(\mathrm{ev}_e)$ is closed under the commutator, hence a Lie subalgebra of $\mathrm{Der}(\mathbb{O}) = \mathfrak{g}_2$, since $[D_1,D_2]\,e = D_1(D_2 e) - D_2(D_1 e) = 0$ — so the strengthening is stateable whenever the conjectured $\Phi$ materialises.

Two consequences are proved unconditionally. First, the dimension obstruction: a faithful linear action forces $\dim \mathfrak{g} \leq (\dim V)^2$. Second, its instantiation at the colour dimension: an $8$-dimensional colour algebra admits no faithful action on a state space of dimension $\leq 2$, since $2^2 = 4 < 8 \leq 3^2 = 9$ — so **three strands are the minimal faithful colour host**. The framework's three-strand baryon structure is thereby not an aesthetic choice but the smallest state space on which the octonionic colour symmetry could act at all; conversely, any proposed colour action on a smaller space is excluded by the cited rank invariant, not merely unlikely.

A calibration proved after the class was first stated sharpens where the open content lives. The abstract class quantifies over an arbitrary state space, and it is *satisfied* — tautologically — by the octonion base module itself: $\mathfrak{su}(3) = \ker(\mathrm{ev}_e)$ is by construction a submodule of $\mathrm{End}(\mathbb{O})$, so the inclusion realises the faithful action on $\mathbb{O} = \mathbb{C} \oplus \mathbb{C}^3$, the $\mathbf{1} \oplus \mathbf{3}$ colour decomposition of \cite{dubois-violette-todorov2018}. The class therefore holds for $\mathbb{O}$, and that is exactly how far the tautology reaches. What remains conjectural — and is the intended content of the conjecture — is the action on the framework's *own* three-strand Hecke state space: an injective $\Phi$ whose target is the braid-state module, compatible with its Hecke structure, not merely some faithful module somewhere.
