Three consequences follow directly from [the definition](#def:gauge-action-groupoid), and each carries a physical reading.

*Reversal refunds the action.* For every gauge transformation $f$, $\mathrm{act}(f^{-1}) = -\mathrm{act}(f)$: a closed gauge loop costs zero action. This is the statement that gauge transformations are symmetries, not processes — the action functional distinguishes deformations only up to gauge orbit.

*Gauge equivalence is an equivalence relation.* A physical process connecting an initial to a final topological state is allowed exactly when a gauge transformation connects them. Selection rules are therefore reachability statements in $\mathbf{G}$: a decay is forbidden when the initial and final states lie in distinct gauge orbits. Conserved quantities are functions on states constant on gauge orbits. The framework currently exhibits two such invariants without fitted parameters: the Coulomb charge $Q = w/2$ of [the Coulomb-charge definition](#def:coulomb-charge) (with $w$ the writhe), and the writhe-to-isospin assignment $w \mapsto I_3$, which matches sign and ratio across the nucleon doublet scale-free. Both are constant on the **horizontal** (conjugation, fixed strand number) orbits only, and are evaluated at the tower's lowest level. This restriction is not optional: conjugation preserves the writhe, whereas the *vertical* move $\beta \in B_n \mapsto \beta\sigma_n^{\pm 1} \in B_{n+1}$ shifts the writhe by $\pm 1$ while presenting the same link, so $Q$ shifts by $\pm 1/2$ there. Since [the brane-tower gauge structure](#rem:markov-moves-brane-tower) counts the vertical move as part of the gauge freedom, a writhe-derived quantity is a function of the state only once a level is fixed. States are Gröbner–Shirshov normal forms, and the observable is the trace at the minimal level — the braid index, itself a link invariant — so the choice of level is canonical rather than arbitrary. The hypercharge $Y$ is *not* among them; the writhe map is falsified as a hypercharge assignment, and the missing object is a second, independent crossing invariant.

*Residual symmetries form a monoid.* The zero-action automorphisms of a fixed state are closed under composition — the unbroken symmetries of that state. The broken part of the symmetry is graded by the action cost, which is the structural seed of the truncation picture in [the Standard-Model truncation conjecture](#conj:sm-gauge-truncation): morphisms of large action are suppressed at observable scales.

Two cautions bound this interpretation. First, the suppression of high-action morphisms at observable scales — the mechanism by which the full categorical symmetry would reduce to an effective low-energy group — is a heuristic; no derivation of a suppression rate exists in the framework, and the remark on [self-duality as constrained 2-group gauge theory](#rem:self-dual-2group) records the higher-categorical refinement (morphisms between morphisms) that the present groupoid formalism truncates away. Second, transition amplitudes of gauge morphisms do not constitute an independent mass mechanism: the framework's masses derive from the volume–density and muon-anchored Hecke-energy chain, and any amplitude-based reading of mass hierarchies must reduce to that chain rather than compete with it. Neither caution is discharged in this appendix; both appear in the closing ledger.
