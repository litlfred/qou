The Standard Model organises the observable interactions around the gauge group $\mathrm{SU}(3) \times \mathrm{SU}(2) \times \mathrm{U}(1)$ acting on point-like fields. The present framework is built instead on extended objects — knots, braids, and topological states — whose natural symmetries are not group actions on points but *categorical*: the gauge transformations are the reversible deformations, braiding operations, and cobordism moves connecting one topological state to another, and these organise into a groupoid rather than a group. This appendix assembles the framework's route from that categorical gauge symmetry to the Standard Model gauge sector.

The route has four stages. First, the gauge symmetry itself is defined as an *action groupoid*: a groupoid of topological states equipped with an action functional, additive under composition, that records the cost mandated by the categorical Lagrangian ([Section on the categorical gauge symmetry](#sec:sm-gauge-symmetry)). Second, this symmetry is conjectured to realise faithfully inside the Hecke algebra $H_n(q)$, so that the algebraic engine of the derivation chain and the gauge structure are two presentations of the same object ([Hecke realization](#sec:sm-hecke-realization)). Third, the archimedean truncation of the gauge symmetry — its image at observable scales under the fibre functor $\tau$ — is conjectured to contain $\mathrm{SU}(3) \times \mathrm{SU}(2) \times \mathrm{U}(1)$ ([Archimedean truncation](#sec:sm-truncation)). Fourth, the action functional acts as a selection rule, and the conservation laws of the Standard Model appear as invariants constant on gauge orbits.

**Calibration convention.** Wherever this appendix requires a dimensional scale, it uses the ideal *mathematical* calibration triple of the framework: the leptonic mass scale $m_e$ (the dimensional unit), the neutron $\beta$-sector bar-skew channel $P_{\mathrm{skew}}^{\beta}$ (whose numerical proxy $m_\mu/m_e \approx \mathrm{Vol}(4_1)/\hbar_q^2$ pins the substrate modulus $q_0$), and the pion-sector bar-even channel $P_{\mathrm{avg}}^{\pi}$ (proxy $m_\pi$). The proton-anchor precision substitution belongs to the physical-observations layer and is not adopted here; it is referenced only where a precision statement must quote the $\mu$–$p$ substrate tension. All scale-bearing statements in the parameter sections below are to be read against this triple.

The epistemic status of each stage is stated where it appears, and the appendix closes with a consolidated ledger of open problems ([Open problems](#sec:sm-open-problems)). The tiers used are: *proved* (established unconditionally from the axioms and definitions of the framework), *derived* (obtained from framework structure without fitted parameters, machine-verified numerically), *conjectural* (stated precisely as an explicit hypothesis without proof), and *open* (a precisely identified missing object). In particular: the groupoid formalism and its basic lemmas are proved; the fibre gauge group $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ is an established theorem of the framework; the Coulomb charge $Q = w/2$ and the writhe-to-isospin map are derived; the Hecke realization, the Standard-Model truncation, and the electroweak torus-link identifications are conjectural; the hypercharge assignment and the $\mathrm{SU}(3)$ colour factor are open. No stage of this appendix is a derivation of the Standard Model; the appendix records how far the derivation currently reaches and exactly what is missing.
