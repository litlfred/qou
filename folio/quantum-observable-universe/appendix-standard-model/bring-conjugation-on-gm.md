**Proposition.** *Let $c$ be complex conjugation, the orientation reversal of Bring's surface. On $\mathbb{G}_m(\mathbb{C}) = \mathbb{C}^\times$:*

1. *if $|q| = 1$, then $c(q) = q^{-1}$;*
2. *if $q$ is real and $q > 1$, then $c(q) = q \ne q^{-1}$.*

*Proof.* $q^{-1} = \bar q/|q|^2$, and $\bar q = q$ for real $q$; $q = q^{-1}$ with $q > 1$ would give $q^2 = 1$. $\square$

**Consequence.** On the unit circle, including the roots of unity $\mu_n$, conjugation is the inversion $q\mapsto q^{-1}$. There the orientation of Bring's surface and the orientation of $\mathbb{G}_m$ are the same binary choice, which by [`prop:hypercharge-sign-is-bring-orientation`](hypercharge-sign-is-bring-orientation.md) is the hypercharge sign. At a real anchor $q_0 > 1$, conjugation fixes $q_0$ and cannot choose between $q_0$ and $q_0^{-1}$, which matches [`prop:braid-chirality-blind-to-hypercharge-sign`](braid-chirality-blind-to-hypercharge-sign.md). Which locus the chirality is read on is a modelling question and is not settled here.
