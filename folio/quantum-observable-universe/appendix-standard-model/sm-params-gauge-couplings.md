The electromagnetic coupling is the most developed of the three. A conditional fixed-point equation for $\alpha_{\mathrm{EM}}$ exists — [the EM-coupling fixed-point proposition](#prop:em-coupling-fixed-point) is a theorem conditional on a named normalization class — and the fibre-level Habiro structure of the coupling is treated in [the bar-involution proposition](#prop:alpha-em-habiro-bar-involution). As recorded in [the derivation-status remark](#rem:alpha-em-derivation-open-problems), the per-loop measure and two-loop remainder $\Delta_2$ are underived, so $\alpha_{\mathrm{EM}}$ remains *partial* rather than closed. Reporting it as derived would launder the conditional hypothesis into an established premise.

Numerically the assembled form is the framework's closest approach to any Standard-Model parameter, and it is stated here so that the *partial* tier is not mistaken for an absence of content. Writing $g = 4$ for the genus of Bring's surface and taking the crossing numbers of $T(2,9)$ and $T(3,5)$,

$$
  \frac{1}{\alpha_{\mathrm{EM}}}
  \;=\;
  q\,[9]_q\,[10]_q \;+\; \frac{2g}{4\pi(g-1)}
  \;+\; \alpha H\,(1 - h^2\alpha) \;+\; \Delta_2,
  \qquad
  H = \frac{[2]_q}{8\pi},
  \qquad
  h = q - q^{-1}.
$$

The symbol $\alpha$ appears on both sides, so the relation is self-consistent rather than a series in an auxiliary small parameter, and it is solved as a fixed point. The $\alpha$-dependent part is written out rather than abbreviated to $O(\alpha)$ because it is not an unspecified remainder: to the order currently derived it is exactly $\alpha H(1 - h^2\alpha)$, in which $h$ is the Hecke structure constant of [the Hecke-algebra convention](#rem:hecke-algebra-from-braiding) — the denominator of the balanced $[k]_q$ — and not the mass-chain quantity $\hbar_q = 1 - q^{-1}$, from which it differs by a factor of about $2.11$. The genuine remainder is $\Delta_2$, the uncomputed archimedean two-loop photon self-energy term. It is *not* the $-h^2H\alpha^2$ piece already carried in the form above: that piece is weight one, a $q$-rational over $\pi$, whereas the two-loop object is weight three of $\zeta(3)$ type, roughly forty times larger, and lives across the Archimedean boundary rather than in the algebraic fibre. Its implied coefficient against CODATA 2022 is $c_2 \approx {{ qou.computations.alpha-em-schwinger-ladder.data.two_loop.c2 | precision: 3 }}$ ($\approx -9.40 \times 10^{-4}$, with the negative sign reflecting the CODATA-2022 edition shift from $+8.1 \times 10^{-4}$), which is *not* the Ohtsuki $\kappa_1 \approx {{ qou.computations.alpha-em-schwinger-ladder.data.two_loop.kappa1_ohtsuki | precision: 4 }}$; the two differ by nearly three orders of magnitude and identifying them would be an error. None of the qualifications attached to that figure is discharged. The derivation passes through three unproved classes, so the result is conditional throughout; and because the leading term alone misses by more than $4 \times 10^6$ times the assembled cubic residual, the agreement is a property of the complete form rather than of any single ingredient within it. The anchor choice, moreover, *is* the residual. The $79.36$ ppb spread between the two pins on the quadratic form is roughly $80$ times the proton-anchored quadratic figure (and more than $215$ times the magnitude of the cubic figure), and no anchor-blind correction of order $\alpha$ can absorb it, so what appears here is the muon–proton aether-parameter tension of [the three-calibration-discipline proposition](#def:three-calibration-discipline) surfacing in a second observable. What keeps the agreement from being anchor-shopping is that the proton calibration was adopted on precision grounds independently of, and prior to, this calculation; that independence is also what allows $\alpha_{\mathrm{EM}}$ to serve as a second witness for the calibration. Both the two-loop $\Delta_2$ coefficient and the per-loop measure are underived, barring the parameter from any tier above *partial*.

For the electroweak mixing angle, two distinct layers must be separated. On the split bundle $F = V_2 \oplus W_3^*$ over Bring's degree-$3$ cyclic Kummer cover, tracelessness $y_3 = -\frac{2}{3}y_2$ fixes $\mathrm{Tr}(T_3^2) = 2$ and $\mathrm{Tr}(Y^2) = 10/3$ across the chiral generation $\Lambda^1 F \oplus \Lambda^2(F^*)$, yielding the canonical ultraviolet boundary ratio $\sin^2\theta_W^{\mathrm{UV}} = \mathrm{Tr}(T_3^2)/(\mathrm{Tr}(T_3^2) + \mathrm{Tr}(Y^2)) = 3/8$ without off-diagonal leptoquark bosons, together with the one-loop Standard Model Dynkin beta multiplicities $(b_3, b_2, b_1) = (7, \frac{19}{6}, -\frac{41}{10})$ for one Higgs doublet. Running $\sin^2\theta_W$ from $3/8$ down to the $Z$-pole target near $0.2312$, however, requires a categorical derivation of the ultraviolet-to-electroweak threshold ratio $\mu_{\mathrm{UV}}/M_Z$, which is open; meanwhile the direct single-fibre trace-ratio ansatz of [the Weinberg-angle conjecture](#conj:weinberg-angle-categorical) remains conjectural.

Two single-fibre numbers circulate for $\cot\theta_W$ and measure different things, so neither refutes the other. Evaluating [the Weinberg-angle conjecture](#conj:weinberg-angle-categorical) at the pure pattern gives $\cot\theta_W \approx 0.659$ against a CODATA target near $1.823$ — the roughly sixty-percent miss the conjecture block reports. A separate single-fibre construction on the $(3,2,1)$ Wedderburn block reaches $1.8208$, a residual of $0.127\%$. **The smaller figure must not be cited as a framework accuracy result.** The residual factor was selected as the closest of $74$ enumerated candidates, the best of them missing the required $1.02239$ by $1.3 \times 10^{-3}$, neither supported at $10^{-3}$ nor falsified at $10^{-2}$ — an inconclusive proximity sweep rather than a derivation.

A second, structural ground bears on that single-fibre $(3,2,1)$ figure. On the unramified $N = 2$ base fibre $V_2$ alone, $(3,2,1)$ has three rows and $\dim V_\lambda = 0$ whenever $\ell(\lambda) > N = 2$, so its Convention-D Ocneanu weight vanishes identically; non-vanishing three-row weight requires passing to the rank-$3$ Kummer pushforward $W_3 = \pi_*\mathcal{L}$ ($z_3 = q^3/[3]_q$, where $e_{(1,1,1)}$ carries weight $[3]_q^{-3} > 0$), rather than subtracting zero-weight traces inside the $N = 2$ carrier.

The strong coupling $\alpha_s(M_Z)$ is open at the $Z$ pole for the companion scale-matching reason. On the chiral exterior generation $\Lambda^1 F \oplus \Lambda^2(F^*)$, the one-loop colour beta coefficient $b_3(N_{\mathrm{gen}}) = 11 - \frac{4}{3}N_{\mathrm{gen}}$ evaluates to $b_3(3) = 7 > 0$ (ultraviolet asymptotic freedom holds for $N_{\mathrm{gen}} \le 8$), fixing the logarithmic slope of $\alpha_s^{-1}(\mu)$; what is absent is a categorical derivation of the infrared confinement scale $\Lambda_{\mathrm{QCD}}$ (or an ultraviolet boundary value $\alpha_s(\mu_{\mathrm{UV}})$ anchored without empirical input) from which $\alpha_s(M_Z)$ could be predicted unconditionally.
