The electromagnetic coupling is the most developed of the three. A conditional fixed-point equation for $\alpha_{\mathrm{EM}}$ exists — [the EM-coupling fixed-point proposition](#prop:em-coupling-fixed-point) is a theorem conditional on a named normalization class — and the fibre-level Habiro structure of the coupling is treated in [the bar-involution proposition](#prop:alpha-em-habiro-bar-involution). What remains open is recorded in [the derivation-status remark](#rem:alpha-em-derivation-open-problems): the per-loop measure is not derived, so $\alpha_{\mathrm{EM}}$ is *partial*, not closed. Reporting it as derived would launder the conditional hypothesis into an established premise.

Numerically the assembled form is the framework's closest approach to any Standard-Model parameter, and it is stated here so that the *partial* tier is not mistaken for an absence of content. Writing $g = 4$ for the genus of Bring's surface and taking the crossing numbers of $T(2,9)$ and $T(3,5)$,

$$
  \frac{1}{\alpha_{\mathrm{EM}}}
  \;=\;
  q\,[9]_q\,[10]_q \;+\; \frac{2g}{4\pi(g-1)}
  \;+\; \alpha H\,(1 - h^2\alpha) \;+\; \Delta_2,
  \qquad
  H = \frac{[2]_q}{8\pi},
  \qquad
  h = q - q^{-1}.
$$

The symbol $\alpha$ appears on both sides, so the relation is self-consistent rather than a series in an auxiliary small parameter, and it is solved as a fixed point. The $\alpha$-dependent part is written out rather than abbreviated to $O(\alpha)$ because it is not an unspecified remainder: to the order currently derived it is exactly $\alpha H(1 - h^2\alpha)$, in which $h$ is the Hecke structure constant of [the Hecke-algebra convention](#rem:hecke-algebra-from-braiding) — the denominator of the balanced $[k]_q$ — and not the mass-chain quantity $\hbar_q = 1 - q^{-1}$, from which it differs by a factor of about $2.11$. Every integer entering the form is structurally fixed — the genus and two crossing numbers — and no coefficient is fitted.

The genuine remainder is $\Delta_2$, the uncomputed archimedean two-loop term. It is *not* the $-h^2H\alpha^2$ piece already carried in the form above: that piece is weight one, a $q$-rational over $\pi$, whereas the two-loop object is weight three of $\zeta(3)$ type, roughly forty times larger, and lives on the archimedean side of the wall rather than in the fibre. Its implied coefficient against CODATA 2022 is $c_2 \approx {{ qou.computations.alpha-em-schwinger-ladder.data.two_loop.c2 | precision: 3 }}$ ($\approx -9.40 \times 10^{-4}$, with the negative sign reflecting the CODATA-2022 edition shift from the earlier $+8.1 \times 10^{-4}$), which is *not* the Ohtsuki $\kappa_1 \approx {{ qou.computations.alpha-em-schwinger-ladder.data.two_loop.kappa1_ohtsuki | precision: 4 }}$; the two differ by nearly three orders of magnitude and identifying them would be an error. Measured against the hundredfold-experimental-precision criterion of [the per-observable status dashboard](#rem:per-observable-100x-status-dashboard), which asks for $\sigma_\alpha/100 \approx 0.00153$ ppb, the cubic form remains about ${{ qou.computations.alpha-em-schwinger-ladder.data.ladder_proton.form_B_miss_vs_100x | precision: 3 }}$ times short — close by the standards of this framework, and not yet at target.

None of the qualifications attached to that figure is discharged. The derivation passes through three unproved classes, so the result is conditional throughout; and because the leading term alone misses by more than $4 \times 10^6$ times the assembled cubic residual, the agreement is a property of the complete form rather than of any single ingredient within it. The anchor choice, moreover, *is* the residual. The $79.36$ ppb spread between the two pins on the quadratic form is roughly $80$ times the proton-anchored quadratic figure (and more than $215$ times the magnitude of the cubic figure), and no anchor-blind correction of order $\alpha$ can absorb it, so what appears here is the muon–proton substrate tension of [the three-calibration-discipline proposition](#def:three-calibration-discipline) surfacing in a second observable. What keeps the agreement from being anchor-shopping is that the proton pin was adopted on precision grounds independently of, and prior to, this calculation; that independence is also what allows $\alpha_{\mathrm{EM}}$ to serve as a second witness for the pin. The per-loop measure, finally, remains underived, and it is that omission which bars the parameter from any tier above *partial*.

The weak mixing angle has a precise categorical candidate — [the Weinberg-angle conjecture](#conj:weinberg-angle-categorical) — but it is a conjecture, and its inputs overlap the conjectural electroweak isotypic structure of [the truncation conjecture](#conj:sm-gauge-truncation); it inherits, in particular, the open hypercharge problem, since $\theta_W$ mixes exactly the two $\mathrm{U}(1)$'s the framework has not yet separated.

Two numbers circulate for this parameter and they measure different things, so neither refutes the other. Evaluating the conjecture's trace ratio at the pure pattern gives $\cot\theta_W \approx 0.659$ against a CODATA target near $1.823$ — the roughly sixty-percent miss the conjecture block reports. A separate construction on the $(3,2,1)$ Wedderburn block reaches $1.8208$, a residual of $0.127\%$. **The smaller figure must not be cited as a framework accuracy result.** Its own computational witness declines to support it: the residual factor was selected as the closest of $74$ enumerated candidates, the best of them missing the required $1.02239$ by $1.3 \times 10^{-3}$, and the witness records the ansatz as neither supported at $10^{-3}$ nor falsified at $10^{-2}$ — an explicitly inconclusive verdict. The surviving exponent is expressible as a corner hook of $(3,2,1)$, which makes it structurally *available*; availability after a proximity search is not derivation, and the sweep ranged well beyond the hooks. The parameter therefore stays *conjectural*.

A second ground bears on the same figure, and it is the stronger of the two because it does not depend on how the exponent was chosen. $(3,2,1)$ has three rows, and the Schur–Weyl row cap gives $\dim V_\lambda = 0$ whenever $\ell(\lambda) > N$; the fibre is the fundamental of $\mathrm{SU}_q(2)$, so $N = 2$ and that block carries no states. Its Ocneanu weight vanishes identically, which is why the conjecture's own trace ratio — built from $\mathrm{tr}_M$, and so blind to the block by construction — cannot reach $1.8208$ at all. The two numbers are therefore not competing estimates of one quantity: one is computed inside the carrier and one outside it. Every route from such a block to a physical number is either a Markov trace, which the row cap sends to zero, or a spectrum, which has no states to act on; a quantity recovered by subtraction is a difference of Markov traces and so, by linearity, is itself the first kind. Deriving the exponent would therefore not close the gap — the reading would remain outside the carrier whatever the exponent turned out to be.

The tractable lever is elsewhere. The Milnor triple linking number $\bar\mu_{123}$ supplies the writhe-independent second crossing invariant and separates the two $\mathrm{U}(1)$'s; what it does not yet supply is their relative coupling normalisation, which inherits the open flavour $\mathrm{SU}(3)$ sector.

The strong coupling is open. The torus-knot anchors assigned to the strong sector are themselves conjectural identifications, and no construction extracts a coupling constant from them; there is no candidate formula for $\alpha_s$ at any scale, and no analogue of the fixed-point equation exists for the colour sector — unsurprisingly, since the $\mathrm{SU}(3)$ action itself is open.
