The neutrino has a carrier identification: [the neutrino-knot definition](#def:neutrino-knot) assigns it the unknot, with the antineutrino as its mirror datum. The unknot has zero hyperbolic volume, so the volume–density mass chain gives the neutrino zero mass — consistent with near-masslessness at leading order, but by the same token the framework currently contains *no mechanism* for the small nonzero masses that oscillation data require. The three mass eigenvalues (equivalently two splittings and an absolute scale) are open, and the framework does not yet distinguish Dirac from Majorana character; on a Majorana reading two additional phases join the count and are equally open.

All four PMNS parameters are open, for the same structural reason as the CKM sector: mixing presupposes a flavour basis, and no categorical flavour structure exists for the three neutrino generations — indeed the framework has no three-generation structure at all yet, for leptons or quarks. The generation-counting problem (why three copies?) is prior to every parameter in this table and is addressed in this paper only as an open item; it belongs on any list of what a Standard-Model derivation from this framework must eventually produce.
