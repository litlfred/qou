The strong-CP angle is entirely open: nothing in this paper predicts $\theta_{\mathrm{QCD}}$ or solves the strong-CP problem. One speculative direction can be named without endorsement: the framework's framing phase $q^{w \cdot c}$ is a topological angle attached to crossings, which is the same *kind* of object as a $\theta$-term, and its per-crossing coupling is already structurally fixed at $c = 1/2$ by [the Coulomb-charge definition](#def:coulomb-charge). Whether the observed smallness of $\theta_{\mathrm{QCD}}$ could arise from the framing sector is a question, not a claim; the framing phase's derived readings (charge and isospin) are already spoken for, and a full derivation would first require the $\mathrm{SU}(3)$ sector itself, which is open.

A first probe of this direction (audit `2026-08-02-theta-qcd-framing-phase-cp-route`) isolates one algebraic step. Charge conjugation acts on the Hecke fibre as the bar-involution $q \mapsto q^{-1}$, which on the substrate circle is complex conjugation; so a colour sector whose total framing phase is bar-symmetric (CP-even) would have a *real* framing phase, forcing $\theta_{\mathrm{QCD}} \in \{0, \pi\}$. That reality implication is proved over a generic base ring. It is not a solution: the CP-evenness hypothesis is undischarged — a single $T(2,9)$ gluon knot has writhe $9 \neq 0$ and is maximally chiral, so CP-evenness would need a colour-singlet writhe cancellation over the $9 = 3 \times 3$ tube structure, which requires the $\mathrm{SU}(3)$ sector, itself open. Moreover $\{0, \pi\}$ is a discrete prediction, not the observed $|\theta_{\mathrm{QCD}}| < 10^{-10}$ smallness, which remains open.
