**Proposition.** *Place each first-generation quark state in one of four slots on
$5 = C \oplus V$: a single colour strand in $\bar 5$, or a strand pair of type
colour$\wedge e_3$, colour$\wedge e_4$ or colour$\wedge$colour in $\Lambda^2 5$, by the
Georgi–Glashow assignment
$u_L, \bar u_L \mapsto$ colour$\wedge e_3$, $d_L, \bar d_L \mapsto$ colour$\wedge e_4$,
$u_R, \bar u_R \mapsto$ colour$\wedge$colour, $d_R, \bar d_R \mapsto$ colour. Mark the
states $\bar u_L, \bar d_L, u_R, d_R$ as dual, meaning they sit in $5 \oplus \Lambda^2 \bar 5$.*

1. *Slot and dual flag together separate all eight states; the slot alone does not.*
2. *The dual flag is set exactly when the chirality bit and the particle bit differ.*
3. *At $\alpha = \tfrac16$ the slot charges are $\tfrac13$, $\tfrac23$, $-\tfrac13$, $-\tfrac23$,
   independent of the colour strand. Negated on dual states, they give $+\tfrac23$ on $u$,
   $-\tfrac13$ on $d$, and the negatives on $\bar u$, $\bar d$.*

**Proof.** Items 1 and 2 are a case check over the eight states. Item 3 is
[the neutral-lepton proposition](#prop:neutral-lepton-fixes-charges) at $\alpha = \tfrac16$. $\square$

**Scope.** The chirality analysis in `ChiralityIndependentOfCrossing` shows the crossing
sign cannot separate the sector and leaves one further label open. This proposition
takes that label to be the strand slot. The slot assignment is the Georgi–Glashow
embedding and is an input; so are the matter content, one neutral lepton and Fermi
exchange, through [the Fermi pair proposition](#prop:fermi-pair-wedge).
