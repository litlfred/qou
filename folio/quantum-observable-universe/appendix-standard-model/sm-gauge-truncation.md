The archimedean truncation of the categorical gauge symmetry (the image of the gauge action groupoid's symmetries at observable scales under the fibre functor $\tau$) is conjectured to contain the Standard Model gauge group. Specifically, there is a group $G_{\mathrm{arch}}$ receiving the truncation together with an injective group homomorphism

$$
  \mathrm{SU}(3) \times \mathrm{SU}(2) \times \mathrm{U}(1)
  \;\hookrightarrow\;
  G_{\mathrm{arch}}.
$$

The statement is deliberately *containment* rather than isomorphism. The truncation may carry symmetry beyond the Standard Model factors, and the global form of the physical gauge group is $(\mathrm{SU}(3) \times \mathrm{SU}(2) \times \mathrm{U}(1))/\Gamma$ with $\Gamma \subseteq \mathbb{Z}_6$ undetermined by current experiment \cite{tong2017}, so an isomorphism claim would overstate both what the framework supports and what experiment fixes. A completed derivation would, conversely, have to predict $\Gamma$, providing a sharper falsifiable output than the containment itself.

The per-factor evidence is uneven, and the conjecture must be read against a structural constraint the framework has already proved. By [the $\mathrm{SU}(2)$-emergence theorem](#thm:su2-emergence), the gauge group of tensor automorphisms of a *single* quantum observable universe fibre is exactly $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ (or its split real form). Consequently the embedding above cannot land in the gauge group of one fibre. Any realisation of the $\mathrm{SU}(3)$ and $\mathrm{U}(1)$ factors must come from symmetry visible only at the groupoid level (multi-strand structure, framing data, and the interaction of distinct fibres), and placing all three factors inside $\mathrm{Aut}^{\otimes}(\tau)$ of one fibre contradicts a proved theorem.

Current per-factor status. On a single unramified base fibre $V_2$, the $\mathrm{SU}(2)_L$ factor is the proved fibre gauge group, while the framing phase supplies the Coulomb charge $Q = w/2$ of [the Coulomb-charge definition](#def:coulomb-charge) at minimal braid index and matches the third isospin component $I_3$ across the nucleon doublet without a fitted scale. On that single fibre, however, the writhe map fails as a hypercharge assignment (the proton and neutron share $Y = +1$ at distinct writhes), requiring either an independent ribbon-framing twist \cite{bilson-thompson2005} or multi-bundle data. Over Bring's genus-$4$ surface ($\chi_{\mathrm{eu}} = -6$), scalar restriction along the degree-$3$ cyclic Kummer $\mathbb{Z}_3$-cover $\pi$ constructs the rank-$3$ vector bundle $W_3 = \pi_*\mathcal{L}$ ($\dim \Lambda^3 W_3 = 1$), equipped on $W_3 \otimes W_3$ with Jimbo's $N = 3$ ($a = q^3$) braided $R$-matrix and HOMFLY-PT parameter $z_3(q) = q^3/[3]_q$. On the split rank-$5$ fibre $F = V_2 \oplus W_3^*$, the $12$-dimensional block-diagonal unimodular Lie subalgebra $\mathfrak{su}(3)_{\mathrm{c}} \oplus \mathfrak{su}(2)_L \oplus \mathfrak{u}(1)_Y$ (omitting all $12$ off-diagonal leptoquark generators of $\mathfrak{su}(5)$) forces the traceless centralizer hypercharge $y_3 = -\frac{2}{3}y_2$. That split fibre reproduces the five chiral multiplets on $\Lambda^1 F \oplus \Lambda^2(F^*)$ with global kernel $\Gamma = \mathbb{Z}_6$, five-anomaly cancellation, Spinor–Minkowski $V_2 \otimes \bar{V}_2 \simeq \mathbb{R}^{1,3}$ worldline Schwinger propagators, and one-loop beta multiplicities $(7, \frac{19}{6}, -\frac{41}{10})$. What keeps the present truncation statement conjectural is the bridge from the knot-state groupoid $\mathbf{G}$ to that split bundle algebra: identifying $G_{\mathrm{arch}}$ as the observable-scale image of $\mathbf{G}$, rather than as an algebraic structure on $F = V_2 \oplus W_3^*$ alone. The electroweak isotypic decomposition proposed in [the $G$-action-on-aether conjecture](#conj:g-action-on-substrate) and the torus-link identifications of [the electroweak triplet conjecture](#conj:electroweak-higgs-torus-link-triplet) are separate conjectural inputs.

Finally, the truncation *mechanism* (the suppression of high-action morphisms at observable scales that would reduce the full categorical symmetry to an effective finite-dimensional group) is a heuristic with no derivation. The conjecture as formalised therefore asserts only the containment; it does not assert that the containment is induced by any particular freeze-out mechanism.
