We conjecture that the archimedean truncation of the categorical gauge symmetry — the image of the gauge action groupoid's symmetries at observable scales under the fibre functor $\tau$ — contains the Standard Model gauge group: there is a group $G_{\mathrm{arch}}$ receiving the truncation together with an injective group homomorphism

$$
  \mathrm{SU}(3) \times \mathrm{SU}(2) \times \mathrm{U}(1)
  \;\hookrightarrow\;
  G_{\mathrm{arch}}.
$$

The statement is deliberately *containment*, not isomorphism: the truncation may carry symmetry beyond the Standard Model factors, and the global form of the physical gauge group is $(\mathrm{SU}(3) \times \mathrm{SU}(2) \times \mathrm{U}(1))/\Gamma$ with $\Gamma \subseteq \mathbb{Z}_6$ undetermined by current experiment \cite{tong2017}; an isomorphism claim would overstate both what the framework supports and what experiment fixes. A completed derivation would, conversely, have to predict $\Gamma$ — a sharper falsifiable output than the containment itself.

The per-factor evidence is uneven, and the conjecture must be read against a structural constraint the framework has already proved. By [the $\mathrm{SU}(2)$-emergence theorem](#thm:su2-emergence), the gauge group of tensor automorphisms of a *single* quantum observable universe fibre is exactly $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ (or its split real form). Consequently the embedding above cannot land in the gauge group of one fibre: any realisation of the $\mathrm{SU}(3)$ and $\mathrm{U}(1)$ factors must come from symmetry visible only at the groupoid level — multi-strand structure, framing data, and the interaction of distinct fibres — and a proposed realisation that places all three factors inside $\mathrm{Aut}^{\otimes}(\tau)$ of one fibre contradicts a proved theorem of the framework.

Current per-factor status. The $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ factor is the proved fibre gauge group. For the $\mathrm{U}(1)$ factor, the framing phase supplies a derived charge — the Coulomb charge $Q = w/2$ of [the Coulomb-charge definition](#def:coulomb-charge), evaluated at the tower's lowest level, since the writhe is preserved by conjugation but shifted by $\pm 1$ under stabilisation — and the writhe ladder matches the third isospin component $I_3$ across the nucleon doublet without a fitted scale (a comparison made at fixed strand number, so the level caveat does not weaken it); the hypercharge $Y$, however, is not obtained: the writhe map is falsified as a $Y$ assignment (the proton and neutron share $Y = +1$ while their writhes differ), and the precisely identified missing object is a second crossing invariant independent of the writhe. One candidate direction, recorded here without endorsement, is the ribbon-framing twist: on a framed link the twist and the writhe are exactly the two independent components of the self-linking number, and the helon model of Bilson-Thompson reads electric charge off ribbon twist in units of $e/3$ \cite{bilson-thompson2005}; whether a per-strand framing datum exists categorically in this framework, and whether it survives the Gell-Mann–Nishijima and hypercharge-spectrum consistency batteries, is untested. For the $\mathrm{SU}(3)$ factor no categorical derivation exists; what is available here is octonion and colour-complex-structure fragments consistent with a colour sector, but no construction of an $\mathrm{SU}(3)$ action from the three-strand structure. The electroweak isotypic decomposition proposed in [the $G$-action-on-substrate conjecture](#conj:g-action-on-substrate) and the torus-link identifications of [the electroweak triplet conjecture](#conj:electroweak-higgs-torus-link-triplet) are themselves conjectural inputs, not support.

Finally, the truncation *mechanism* — the suppression of high-action morphisms at observable scales that would reduce the full categorical symmetry to an effective finite-dimensional group — is a heuristic with no derivation. The conjecture as stated therefore asserts only the containment; it does not assert that the containment is induced by any particular freeze-out mechanism.
