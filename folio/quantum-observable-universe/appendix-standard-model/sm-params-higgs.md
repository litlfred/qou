The Higgs mass has a conjectural identification: the Higgs is assigned the torus link $T(2,8)$ within [the electroweak triplet conjecture](#conj:electroweak-higgs-torus-link-triplet), alongside $W = T(2,4)$ and $Z = T(2,6)$. Because torus links have zero hyperbolic volume, their mass chain rests on two-strand Plancherel trace polynomials $P_m(h)$ ($h = q - q^{-1}$) rather than hyperbolic volume. Dressing the $W \to Z$ genus step by $([2]_q/2)^2 = 1 + h^2/4$ reconciles the $W$- and $Z$-anchored mass ratios ($m_Z/m_W$ within $175$ parts per million of experiment) and places the $(6/5)$-scaled $P_4(h_0)/P_2(h_0)$ scalar-to-gauge ratio near $125.1$–$125.5\;\mathrm{GeV}$ (corresponding to a positive tree-level Higgs quartic $\lambda_H = m_H^2/(2v^2) \approx 0.129$), whereas omitting the quantum-dimension dressing leaves a $-0.66\%$ ($124.37\;\mathrm{GeV}$) residual under the Borromean $2G h_0^4$ correction. Because neither the $6/5$ prefactor nor the higher-order loop factor is derived from the category, the parameter remains *conjectural*.

The electroweak scale $v$ remains open, with two distinct structural routes in tension. First, on the noncommutative Sweedler weak first jet, a four-hop charged-current contraction carries scaling $(q_0^2 - 1)^8 \approx 8.41 \times 10^{-6}$ against $G_F \mathfrak{m}_p^2 \approx 1.03 \times 10^{-5}$ ($-18.1\%$, or $+10.5\%$ on $v \approx 272.1\;\mathrm{GeV}$ under direct Frobenius-norm identification), whereas interpreting $(q_0^2 - 1)^8$ as a squared weak decay rate scales $G_F \mathfrak{m}_p^2$ as $(q_0^2 - 1)^4$ and underpredicts $v$ by more than an order of magnitude at the proton scale ($14.65\;\mathrm{GeV}$). Second, the conditional Bring–Schwinger fixed-point cubic $1/\alpha_v = q([2]_q/2)[4]_q[6]_q[8]_q + 1/\pi - \alpha_v H(1 - \hbar_q^2\alpha_v)$ ($H = [2]_q/(8\pi)$) matches $v/\mathfrak{m}_p$ to $-29.4$ parts per billion (and to $+81.1$ parts per billion without cubic iteration when the loop coupling is fixed to the parameter-free Sweedler factor $2^{1/4}(q_0^2 - 1)^4 < 1/30$, avoiding the $\alpha_2 > 1/30$ incompatibility), yet rests on undischarged selections for the dressing exponent, the per-carrier zero-mode rule, and the $\mathrm{SU}(2)_L$ loop sign. Because $v$ normalises every Standard-Model mass via the Yukawa mechanism inside [the $G$-action-on-substrate conjecture](#conj:g-action-on-substrate), reconciling this open electroweak scale with the calibrated volume–density and Hecke-energy mass chains remains an essential obligation.
