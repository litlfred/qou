The gauge symmetry of the knot-state groupoid is conjectured to realise faithfully inside the Hecke algebra: for every topological state $X$, there is an injective monoid homomorphism

$$
  \Phi_X \colon \mathrm{End}_{\mathbf{G}}(X) \longrightarrow H_n(q)
$$

from the gauge automorphisms of $X$ into the multiplicative monoid of $H_n(q)$.

The surjective part of this picture is standard and is not the conjectural content: the braid group $B_n$ maps onto $H_n(q)$ by imposing the Hecke relation $(\sigma_i - q)(\sigma_i + q^{-1}) = 0$ on the braid generators, as recorded in [the Hecke-algebra-from-braiding remark](#rem:hecke-algebra-from-braiding). The conjectural content is *faithfulness on gauge automorphisms*: the Hecke relation is a quotient, and a quotient can collapse symmetry. The conjecture asserts that no gauge transformation of a physical state is collapsed — gauge equivalence computed in the Hecke image coincides with gauge equivalence computed in $\mathbf{G}$, so that the Markov-trace evaluation of the derivation chain sees the full gauge structure rather than a proper quotient of it.

Consequences, contingent on the conjecture: gauge invariance of an observable becomes equivalent to topological invariance of the corresponding Hecke class, and the generators of the gauge symmetry acquire explicit Hoefsmit matrix realisations. Neither consequence is available unconditionally.

The linear-representation literature both supports and sharpens the conjecture. The Burau representation — the $(n-1)$-dimensional Hecke summand — is unfaithful for $n \geq 5$, so a realisation cannot factor through Burau alone; but the Lawrence–Krammer representation, constructed by Lawrence as a topologically-defined summand in the Jones-representation orbit, is faithful for all $n$ \cite{bigelow2001}, so faithful linear realisation of braid symmetry is the generic-parameter situation. Two residues make the conjecture nontrivial nonetheless: faithfulness at *generic* parameters does not imply faithfulness of the specialisation at the aether parameter $q_0$, and the gauge automorphism monoids of the state groupoid must first be identified with braid groups for the cited results to apply. Both residues are open.
