The conjecture is that the gauge symmetry of the knot-state groupoid realises faithfully inside the Hecke algebra: for every topological state $X$, there is an injective monoid homomorphism

$$
  \Phi_X \colon \mathrm{End}_{\mathbf{G}}(X) \longrightarrow H_n(q)
$$

from the gauge automorphisms of $X$ into the multiplicative monoid of $H_n(q)$.

The surjective part of this picture is standard and is not the conjectural content: the braid group $B_n$ maps onto $H_n(q)$ by imposing the Hecke relation $(\sigma_i - q)(\sigma_i + q^{-1}) = 0$ on the braid generators, as recorded in [the Hecke-algebra-from-braiding remark](#rem:hecke-algebra-from-braiding). The conjectural content is *faithfulness on gauge automorphisms*: the Hecke relation is a quotient, and a quotient can collapse symmetry. The conjecture asserts that no gauge transformation of a physical state is collapsed — gauge equivalence computed in the Hecke image coincides with gauge equivalence computed in $\mathbf{G}$, so that the Markov-trace evaluation of the derivation chain sees the full gauge structure rather than a proper quotient of it.

Consequences, contingent on the conjecture: gauge invariance of an observable becomes equivalent to topological invariance of the corresponding Hecke class, and the generators of the gauge symmetry acquire the concrete matrix realisations (Hoefsmit) already used by the compute layer. Neither consequence is available unconditionally.

Read through the canonical quotient, with the gauge automorphisms of an $n$-strand state identified with $B_n$, the conjecture is the faithfulness of the Iwahori--Hecke representation $B_n \to H_n(q)^{\times}$. Its status in the literature is sharp. The reduced Burau representation is a summand of $H_n(q)$, so Burau faithfulness gives Hecke faithfulness at generic $q$: this covers $n = 3$ (Magnus--Peluso) and $n = 4$ (Bharathram--Birman--Brendle, a 2026 preprint, arXiv:2607.05283). Burau is unfaithful for $n \geq 5$, and whether the full Hecke representation of $B_n$ is faithful for $n \geq 5$ is an open problem; for those $n$ the conjecture contains it. The Lawrence--Krammer representation, faithful for all $n$ \cite{bigelow2001}, does **not** support the conjecture: it factors through the Birman--Murakami--Wenzl algebra, not through $H_n(q)$. The case $n = 2$ is proved exactly: on $B_2 \cong \mathbb{Z}$ the quotient is injective if and only if $q$ is not a root of unity (`braidTwo_hecke_injective_iff` in `QOU/StandardModel/GaugeHeckeCanonicalFaithfulness.lean`). Two residues remain open. Faithfulness at generic $q$ does not imply faithfulness at a specialised value; for $B_3$ the faithful real specialisations of Burau are classified only partially. And the gauge automorphism monoids of the state groupoid must first be identified with braid groups. The Lean class `GaugeHeckeRealization` asks only for *some* injective homomorphism into $H$, which is weaker than the canonical reading stated here; the canonical reading is `FaithfulVia` in the same module.
