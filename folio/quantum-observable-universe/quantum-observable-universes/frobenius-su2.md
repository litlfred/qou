Let $A = \mathcal{O}(\mathrm{SU}(2))$ equipped with pointwise multiplication $\mu(f \otimes g) = fg$. The Frobenius form is defined as the Haar integral $\int_A = \int_G$. The dual comultiplication is given by $\Delta_\mu(f)(g,h) = \int_G f(xg)\overline{h(x)}dx$. The Frobenius relation precisely encodes the associativity of convolution: integrating $f(xy)g(y)$ over $y$ yields the same result whether convolved first with $f$ or first with $g$.
