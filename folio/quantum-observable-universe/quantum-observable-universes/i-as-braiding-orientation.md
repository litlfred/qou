The imaginary unit $i$ serves as the orientation of the braiding $c_{V,V}$ versus $c_{V,V}^{-1}$.

1. **Wick rotation**: The rotation $t \mapsto it$ converting the Laplace half-plane to the Fourier half-plane flips upon $i \mapsto -i$.
2. **CR complex structure**: The operator $J \colon H \to H$ secures the CR orientation.
3. **Arrow of time**: The positive crossing $c_{V,V}$ defines forward time in the Hecke basis ordering, while $c_{V,V}^{-1}$ reverses it. Negating $i$ equates to flipping the crossing orientation.
