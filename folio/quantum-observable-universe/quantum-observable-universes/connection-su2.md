On $A = \mathcal{O}(\mathrm{SU}(2))$ with its left-invariant calculus $\Omega^1(A) = \mathrm{span}\{\omega_1, \omega_2, \omega_3\}$, a connection is a covariant derivative $\nabla f = df + \mathcal{A} \cdot f$, where $\mathcal{A} \in \Omega^1(A)$ is the connection 1-form. It satisfies the Leibniz rule $\nabla(fg) = (\nabla f)g + f(dg)$. The curvature $F_\nabla = d\mathcal{A} + \mathcal{A} \wedge \mathcal{A} \in \Omega^2(A)$ is strictly $A$-linear.
