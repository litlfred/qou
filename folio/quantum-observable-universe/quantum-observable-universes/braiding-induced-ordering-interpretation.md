**Remark (readings of the ordering).** The proposition and its proof use no braiding hypothesis; the readings below attach physical meaning to the letters of $W_n$ and are not part of the statement.

**Two readings of the arrow of time.** The braiding $c_{V,V}$ and the Reeb vector field $\xi$ of the categorical Reeb derivation, defined later in this chapter, are read as two complementary viewpoints on one arrow of time.

- **Categorical arrow** ($c_{V,V}$): which strand crosses *over*. A letter $\sigma_i \in W_n$ is a positive crossing and applies $c_{V,V}$. The negative crossing $\sigma_i^{-1}$, which applies $\bar{c}_{V,V} := c_{V,V}^{-1}$, is not a letter of $W_n$, and $\prec_c$ orders positive words only. On this reading, forward time is Gröbner--Shirshov reduction under $\prec_c$.
- **Geometric arrow** ($\xi$): which direction the Reeb flow runs.

**Crossing reversal and time reversal.** The crossing-reversal involution $K \mapsto K^{\sigma}$, which mirrors all crossings, corresponds to switching the braiding $c_{V,V} \leftrightarrow \bar{c}_{V,V}$. The switch exchanges which crossing the letter $\sigma_i$ denotes, so it changes the reading of a word and leaves the order unchanged: $\prec_c$ depends only on word length and the chosen order on the letters, and is the same for both braidings. On the geometric side the switch is read as the reversal $\xi \mapsto -\xi$ of the Reeb flow. In the Feynman--Stueckelberg reading, an antiparticle $K^{\sigma}$ is its particle $K$ with time reversed, that is, with the opposite braiding. CPT symmetry is then read as invariance of the QOU axioms under the simultaneous operations $c_{V,V} \leftrightarrow \bar{c}_{V,V}$ (T), $K \mapsto K^{\sigma}$ (C), and $\sigma_i \mapsto \sigma_{n-i}$ (P, strand reflection).
