**The counit.** The counit of a Frobenius--Hopf aether is not required to be multiplicative: together with the Frobenius relation and counitality, $\varepsilon(ab) = \varepsilon(a)\,\varepsilon(b)$ forces $x = \varepsilon(x)\cdot 1$ for every $x$, so $A$ would be spanned by its unit.

**The antipode.** The braiding $\beta$ is what makes "reverses" expressible, which is why $\mathbf{C}$ is taken braided; that is no extra demand, since the aether is invoked at the level of a [quantum universe](#def:quantum-universe), which is braided monoidal.

The antipode is constrained but not determined. Every classical unimodular antipode (such as $S(g) = g^{-1}$ on $k[G]$ or inversion on $\mathcal{O}(G)$ at $q = 1$) satisfies the six conditions on $S$, and on a non-commutative $A$ they exclude the trivial choice $S = \mathrm{id}_A$. Away from the involutive unimodular point --- for instance on $\mathcal{O}_q(\mathrm{SU}(2))$ at $q \neq \pm 1$, where $S^2(\beta) = q^{-2}\beta$, or on a non-unimodular finite Hopf algebra such as the Taft algebra $H_4$, where $S^2(X) = -X$ and $\lambda \circ S \neq \lambda$ --- the last two identities deform to the pivotal square law $S \circ S = \gamma_A$ for an automorphism $\gamma_A \colon A \xrightarrow{\sim} A$ and the modular-shifted law $\lambda \circ S = \lambda \circ \mu_{\mathrm{mod}}$. Even in the involutive case these conditions do not single $S$ out: the only condition that does is the convolution identity $\mu \circ (S \otimes \mathrm{id}) \circ \Delta = \eta \circ \varepsilon$, and that identity forces $A$ to be trivial whenever the scalars form a field, so it cannot be imposed here.

**Self-duality.** Self-duality couples the observer trace $\Phi = \lambda \circ \mu$ to the observed state space intrinsically, and it is what makes the left and right categorical traces on $\mathrm{End}(A)$ available.

**Duals are required of $A$ only, not of $\mathbf{C}$.** The non-degeneracy axiom supplies $\mathrm{coev}$ as part of the data, so $A$ carries its own duality datum and no object of $\mathbf{C}$ other than $A$ need have a dual. Consequently a Frobenius--Hopf object is available already at the level of a [quantum universe](#def:quantum-universe), which is braided monoidal but *not* rigid; rigidity is imposed one level up, on a [quantum observable universe](#def:quantum-observable-universe), where duals for every object carry the antiparticles and let a strand close into a knot.
