**Time order is a definition.** One tick is one application of a crossing, as in the proof of ([braiding-induced ordering](#prop:braiding-induced-ordering)). On words, a tick appends one generator, $w \mapsto w\,\sigma_i$. On states, it multiplies by the image of $\sigma_i$, $g \mapsto g\,s_i$. Chronological precedence is the transitive closure of the tick relation. A *closed chain* through $x$ is a finite chain of ticks from $x$ back to $x$. Taking this definition is the only modelling step. Everything below is a theorem about it.

**Words.** A tick is a strict $\prec_c$-step, because it raises the length by one. So every chain of ticks is a chain in $\prec_c$. A well-founded relation has no closed chains: its transitive closure is again well-founded, hence irreflexive. Therefore no word lies on a closed chain. In particular, when the set of words is nonempty, it is false that every word precedes every word. This holds for every braiding. On words, the hypothesis $c_{V,V}^{2} \neq \mathrm{id}$ is not needed.

**States.** This is where $c_{V,V}^{2} \neq \mathrm{id}$ matters. Suppose some crossing has finite order, $s_i^{k+1} = 1$. Then every state $g$ lies on the closed chain $g \to g s_i \to \cdots \to g s_i^{k+1} = g$. A symmetric braiding is the case $k = 1$: $g \to g s_i \to g$. In the other direction, suppose there is a homomorphism $\chi$ from the states to $\mathbb{Z}$ with $\chi(s_i) = 1$ for every $i$. Then $\chi$ rises by one at each tick, so no closed chain exists. For the braid group $B_{n+1}$ itself, the writhe (total exponent sum) is such a $\chi$. So the braid group has no closed chains, while its symmetric quotient $S_{n+1}$ (where $\sigma_i^{2} = 1$) has a closed chain through every element.

**Gödel's universe.** Gödel's rotating solution \cite{goedel1949} is homogeneous, and a closed timelike curve passes through every event. Equivalently, the chronological future $I^{+}(p)$ is all of spacetime for every $p$. Under the tick definition, the theorems above exclude this for words, and for states on which the writhe is defined. The exclusion also covers every other spacetime with a closed timelike curve, since the statement is that no closed chain exists at all. This is not a statement about general relativity: Gödel's metric remains an exact solution of the Einstein equations.
