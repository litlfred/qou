The Frobenius form $\lambda \colon A \to \mathbf{1}$ of the Frobenius--Hopf aether $A$ induces a quasi-isomorphism between $A$ and its rigid dualizing complex $\omega_A^\bullet$:

$$
A \xrightarrow{ \sim } \omega_A^\bullet \quad\text{in } D(A).
$$

The non-degenerate Frobenius pairing $\langle a, b \rangle_F = \lambda(\mu(a \otimes b))$ defines an $A$-bimodule map $\varphi \colon A \to \omega_A^\bullet$ whose cone is acyclic.

This quasi-isomorphism implies a Serre-type duality $\mathrm{Ext}^i_A(M, A) \cong H^{d-i}(M)^\vee$ for finitely generated $A$-modules $M$. The composition $\varepsilon \circ \mu$ factors through the Frobenius form, providing a categorical trace on $\mathrm{End}(A)$. Because $\omega_A^\bullet$ is rigid, the quasi-isomorphism is unique.
