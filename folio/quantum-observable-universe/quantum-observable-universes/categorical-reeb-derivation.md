Let $A$ be a Frobenius--Hopf substrate in a rigid monoidal category $\mathbf{C}$. The **categorical Reeb derivation** is the morphism $\xi_q \colon A \to A$ uniquely determined by:

1. **Normalization:** The derivation is compatible with the contact form $\varTheta$, satisfying $\xi_q \circ \varTheta = \varTheta$.
2. **Derivation property:** The morphism $\xi_q$ satisfies the Leibniz rule with respect to the multiplication $\mu$ of $A$: $\xi_q \circ \mu = \mu \circ (\xi_q \otimes \mathrm{id} + \mathrm{id} \otimes \xi_q)$.

The period of the Reeb derivation is the smallest positive integer $\Omega_q$ such that the iterated composition satisfies $\xi_q^{\Omega_q} = \mathrm{id}_A$.
