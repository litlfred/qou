The representation category $\mathbf{C} = \mathrm{Rep}(\mathrm{SU}(2))$ carries a canonical dagger structure compatible with the Hopf $\ast$-structure of its coordinate algebra. For any equivariant map $f \colon V_j \to V_k$, its dagger $f^{\dagger}$ is the standard Hilbert-space adjoint, satisfying $\langle f^{\dagger}(w), v \rangle_j = \langle w, f(v) \rangle_k$; this adjoint remains equivariant because the inner product on each irreducible representation $V_j$ is $\mathrm{SU}(2)$-invariant. On the coordinate algebra $A = \mathcal{O}(\mathrm{SU}(2))$, the positive GNS pairing $\langle f, h \rangle_{L^2} = \int_{\mathrm{SU}(2)} f^*(g)h(g)\,d\mu_{\mathrm{Haar}}(g)$ uses the conjugate-linear pointwise $\ast$-involution $f^*(g) = \overline{f(g)}$ (which extends to non-involutive Hopf $\ast$-deformations via $(S \circ *)^2 = \mathrm{id}_A$, whereas $\overline{f(g^{-1})} = (S \circ *)(f)(g)$ is the convolution involution). Unitarity manifests categorically as the positivity of the composite $f^{\dagger} \circ f = \lVert f(1) \rVert^2 > 0$ for any non-zero state $f \colon \mathbb{C} \to V_j$.
