The $q$-scalar curvature $\mathrm{Sc}_q = R + O(\hbar_q)$ encodes both the classical scalar curvature and the anomaly contribution from $d_q^2 \neq 0$. The $O(\hbar_q)$ correction adds a contact-curvature term on a non-flat aether. On the contact-flat Bring background, both contributions vanish, yielding $\Delta_\nabla^q = \mathfrak{D}_\nabla^2$.
