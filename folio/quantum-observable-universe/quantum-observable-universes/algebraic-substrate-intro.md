The algebraic substrate is the Frobenius--Hopf algebra $A$ viewed as a graded ring equipped with the Reeb derivation—the differential operator forced by the categorical volume constraint. This section develops the algebraic programme: substrate structure and Reeb derivation, the unit particle as a categorical invariant, spectral trace and Tannaka--Krein duality, geometric stability via the Hasse--Weil bound, and the classical limit recovering the macroscopic continuum.

The spectral trace formula relates the $q$-zeta function of $A$ to the Chebyshev function of the Reeb flow. A stability criterion derives from the Weil bound on the fibre geometry. The classical limit $q \to 1$ emerges as a functor that fails to be an equivalence, with the obstruction to classicality acting as a non-trivial categorical invariant.
