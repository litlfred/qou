Once $G \cong \mathrm{SU}(2)$ is identified and the simple objects $V_j$ are labeled by spin, the rank-one maximal torus $T \subset G$ diagonalizing every $V_j$ determines the weight decomposition of differential forms. The maximal torus is defined categorically as the largest subgroup acting diagonally on the weight subcategory of the fundamental object. This decomposition splits any form uniquely into a weight-zero (horizontal) component and a non-zero-weight (vertical) component. The horizontal forms, carrying integer spin, correspond geometrically to bosons, while mixed forms carry half-integer spin and correspond to fermions. This splitting establishes the geometric origin of spin-statistics directly from the categorical structure.
