For a fixed Lagrangian endomorphism $\Phi \in \mathrm{End}_{\mathbf{C}}(A)$ and transposition $\mathrm{swap}$, the categorical action $\mathfrak{S}_\Phi = \mathrm{ev}_A \circ \mathrm{swap} \circ (\Phi \otimes \mathrm{id}_{A^\vee}) \circ \mathrm{coev}_A$ does not depend on the choice of right dual of $A$. If $(A, D_1)$ and $(A, D_2)$ are two exact pairings and $\varphi \colon D_1 \xrightarrow{\sim} D_2$ is the canonical isomorphism between them, then computing $\mathfrak{S}_\Phi$ with $D_2$ and the transported transposition $(\varphi \otimes \mathrm{id}_A) \circ \mathrm{swap} \circ (\mathrm{id}_A \otimes \varphi^{-1})$ gives the same morphism $\mathbf{1} \to \mathbf{1}$ as computing it with $D_1$ and $\mathrm{swap}$.
