For a QOU meeting the categorical volume constraint, $G_q \cong \mathrm{SU}_q(2)$. The inner-automorphism $2$-group $\mathrm{INN}(G_q)$ governs the quantum flatness condition $F_A = B$ (the vanishing fake curvature condition of higher gauge theory \cite{baez-schreiber2007}), reproducing the instanton equation $F_A^- = 0$ under simplicity. Quantum flatness operates as a derived consequence of the instanton axiom.
