The inside-unit-circle convention assigns $q := \lambda_+$, where $\lambda_+$ is the eigenvalue of $c_{V,V}$ on the symmetric summand $S^2 V$. For real $q > 1$, $\lambda_+ = q$ lies outside the unit circle and $\lambda_- = -q^{-1}$ lies inside. This convention secures positivity for the symmetric eigenvalue and aligns with the standard orientation of the Kauffman bracket.
