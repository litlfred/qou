This section interprets the categorical operators $d_q$, $\delta_q$, $\Delta_q$, and the fermion--boson decomposition through the dictionary of classical vector calculus. The $q$-Laplacian $\Delta_q \alpha = 0$ identifies equilibrium between the spatial environment and internal quantum spin. The closed form $\delta_q = q^{-1}\delta + (1-q^{-1}) \mathcal{L}_\xi^* \circ \iota_\xi$ enables a three-term expansion of $\Delta_q$ into classical diffusion, Coriolis cross-coupling, and pure spin diffusion.

A particle is simultaneously $q$-closed and $q$-coclosed. Allowing $q$ to acquire a phase destroys the positivity of the Haar functional and is excluded by the observability axiom, but the algebraic sector remains internally consistent.
