The $q$-Hodge star $\star_q$ rescales the inner product by the aether parameter $q$, and the $q$-codifferential $\delta_q$ inherits this deformation. The $q$-Laplacian $\Delta_q$ is formally self-adjoint with respect to the Webster inner product.

Self-adjointness guarantees real eigenvalues and orthogonal eigenspaces. The space of particles, $\ker \Delta_q$, is a well-defined subspace with a canonical complement.
