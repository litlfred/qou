Let $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ be a quantum observable universe equipped with connection $\nabla$, substrate parameter $q$, and Reeb derivation $\xi$. The :defterm[categorical Dirac operator]{#categorical-dirac-operator} is the morphism

$$
  \mathcal{D}_\nabla
  = 
  q^{-1}\nabla +  (1 - q^{-1})\iota_\xi\nabla
   \colon  A \to \Omega^1(A).
$$

When $q = 1$ this reduces to $\mathcal{D}_\nabla = \nabla$, the undeformed covariant derivative. The $q$-deformation interpolates between the full connection and its projection onto the Reeb direction. As $q \to \infty$, $\mathcal{D}_\nabla \to \iota_\xi\nabla$, the purely contact component.
