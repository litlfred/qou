Continuing the [$\mathrm{SU}(2)$ fundamental representation](#ex:quantum-universe-su2) on $V = \mathbb{C}^2$, let $H_n(q)$ act on the $n$-fold tensor power $V^{\otimes n}$ via Michio Jimbo's $R$-matrix representation \cite{jimbo1986} for nonzero $q \in \mathbb{C}$ satisfying $q^k \neq 1$ for every $k \ge 1$, equipped with the Jones--Markov trace $\tau_q$ at parameter $z = q^3/(q^2 + 1)$.

For $n = 1$ and $n = 2$, the representation on $V^{\otimes n}$ is faithful and the trace radical $\mathrm{rad}\,\tau_q = \{x \in H_n(q) : \tau_q(x y) = 0 \text{ for all } y \in H_n(q)\}$ vanishes identically. For every $n \ge 3$, because $\dim V = 2$, no nonzero alternating three-tensor exists in $V^{\otimes n}$; the kernel of the Jimbo action coincides as a two-sided ideal with $\mathrm{rad}\,\tau_q \neq 0$, generated by the three-strand $q$-antisymmetriser. Sending the quotient image of each braid generator $\sigma_i$ to $q - e_i$ establishes an isomorphism of $\mathbb{C}$-algebras

$$
  H_n(q) / \mathrm{rad}\,\tau_q
  \;\cong\;
  TL_n(q + q^{-1})
$$

onto the planar Temperley--Lieb algebra \cite{temperley-lieb1971} of Catalan dimension $C_n = \frac{1}{n+1}\binom{2n}{n}$, whose representation on $V^{\otimes n}$ is faithful. By [Rieffel quotient faithfulness](#rem:rieffel-metric-quotient-faithfulness), any Lipschitz seminorm represented through $V^{\otimes n}$ has non-scalar kernel on $H_n(q)$ for $n \ge 3$, whereas descending to the trace-radical quotient $TL_n(q + q^{-1})$ restores a scalar kernel $\mathbb{C} \cdot 1$ and a finite Kantorovich--Rubinstein metric on the state space \cite{rieffel1999}.
