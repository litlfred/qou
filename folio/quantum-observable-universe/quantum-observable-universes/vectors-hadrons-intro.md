With the weight decomposition of forms established, this section develops the trace and basis machinery for intertwiner spaces of tensor powers of the fundamental representation. It first introduces the adjoint trace on endomorphisms, derived from the Frobenius pairing and the fibre-adjoint involution; this trace inherits cyclicity from the Frobenius relation and non-degeneracy from the positive-definiteness of the dagger. Next, it constructs the Pawlowski--Leitner canonical basis on $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{SU}(2)}(V_j, V_{1/2}^{\otimes n})$. Indexed by standard Young tableaux with at most two rows, this basis emerges from contractions with the volume form along the unital Gröbner--Shirshov route, its dimension governed by the hook-length formula. Finally, the adjoint trace equips the PawLei basis with an orthogonal inner product. This pairing coincides with the Frobenius form restricted to $\mathrm{End}(V_j)$, ensuring that fibre integration commutes with the intertwiner expansion of hadron states.
