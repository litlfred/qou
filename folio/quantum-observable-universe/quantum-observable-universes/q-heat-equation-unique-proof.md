The difference $v = u_1 - u_2$ satisfies $\partial_t v = -\Delta_q v$
with $v(0) = 0$.  Project onto isotypic components: each $\pi_j(v)$
satisfies the ODE $\dot{\pi_j(v)} = -\lambda_j(q) \pi_j(v)$ with
$\pi_j(v)(0) = 0$, whose unique solution is $\pi_j(v) \equiv 0$.
Reassembling, $v(t) = 0$ for all $t$, so $u_1 = u_2$.  Uniqueness
is an eigenvalue-by-eigenvalue fact, categorical and base-ring
independent.
