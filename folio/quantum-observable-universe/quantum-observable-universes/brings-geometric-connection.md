The contact structure $\varTheta$, Reeb vector field $\xi$, and aether parameter $q$ reduce to explicit formulas on a genus-$4$ Riemann surface with $S_5$ symmetry. The abstract equilibrium $\Delta_q \alpha = 0$ reduces to computable eigenvalue problems, and the boson--fermion split becomes a Hodge decomposition of the harmonic space.
