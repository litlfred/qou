This section introduces the fibre-adjoint involution, closing the differential-structure machinery. This involution provides the projector onto coadjoint orbits and governs the aether's radial coordinate. After establishing the fibre-functor atlas and defining the involution, the development extracts the radial coordinate, confirms its well-definedness under the Reeb flow, and specializes the involution to $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ in preparation for constructing the connection.
