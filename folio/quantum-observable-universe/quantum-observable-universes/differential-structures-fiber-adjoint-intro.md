This section introduces the fibre-adjoint involution, closing the differential-structure machinery. This involution provides the projector onto coadjoint orbits and governs the substrate's radial coordinate. It first establishes the fibre-functor atlas and define the involution. It then extracts the radial coordinate, confirm its well-definedness under the Reeb flow, and specialize the involution to $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ in preparation for constructing the connection.
