The axioms of the quantum universe and quantum observable universe are modelled on the algebraic data of topological quantum field theory. The Frobenius--Hopf object $A$ carries the $2$d cobordism data and the Hopf structure for quantum-group symmetry. The precise form of the $2$d-cobordism claim is the canonical isomorphism $\mathbf{Cob}(2) \cong \mathbf{Cob}(3)_{S^2,\mathrm{CF}}$ \cite{li2026-two-sphere} (Thm.~1.0.8): the commutative-Frobenius part of the $S^2$-sector of the oriented $3$d bordism category is the $2$d cobordism category, so $Z(S^2)$ is a commutative Frobenius monoid for every $3$d TFT $Z$, and its Frobenius structure is exactly the $2$d cobordism data. The sector's remaining generators, one unit per closed oriented irreducible prime $3$-manifold (Thm.~1.0.5), are not used by these axioms. The identification is at the level of the Frobenius structure only: Li's monoids are commutative in a symmetric monoidal target, whereas $A$ is a Hopf algebra in a braided category and is not assumed commutative, and nothing here identifies $A$ with $Z(S^2)$ of a $3$d TFT or bears on the Hopf half of the Frobenius--Hopf object.

The condition $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathbf{C}}(\mathbf{1}, \mathrm{Ad}(A)) = 0$ acts as the categorical contact condition. It is the condition that would ensure irreducibility of an associated $3$d TQFT; no such TQFT is constructed here, and at generic $q > 1$ the representation category has infinitely many simple objects, so it is not modular. Within the framework it ensures non-degeneracy of the pairing across cobordisms, and full dualisability. In the archimedean realisation, this enforces $H^0(M, \mathrm{Ad}(P)) = 0$, eliminating infinitesimal automorphisms on the instanton connection.
