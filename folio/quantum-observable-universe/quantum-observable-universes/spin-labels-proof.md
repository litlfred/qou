
Because $G = \mathrm{Aut}^{\otimes}(\tau) \cong \mathrm{SU}(2)$
([$\mathrm{SU}(2)$ emergence](#thm:su2-emergence)), the simple objects of $\mathbf{C}$
are indexed by a spin label $j \in \{0, \tfrac12, 1, \tfrac32, \ldots\}$,
with $V_j$ denoting the simple object of categorical dimension $2j + 1$.

The symplectic form $\epsilon \colon \Lambda^2 V \to \mathbf{1}$
([quantum observable universe](#def:quantum-observable-universe), axiom (iii)) is the
unique (up to scalar) $G$-invariant alternating pairing on $V$.
