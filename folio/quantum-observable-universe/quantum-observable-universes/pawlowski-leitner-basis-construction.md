The construction relies on the PBW theorem over commutative rings: the unital Gröbner--Shirshov basis for $U(\mathfrak{su}(2))$ provides a monomial basis for the enveloping algebra, from which the intertwiner basis is derived by contraction with the volume form (the diagram of [Pawlowski–Leitner basis](#prop:pawlowski-leitner-basis)).
