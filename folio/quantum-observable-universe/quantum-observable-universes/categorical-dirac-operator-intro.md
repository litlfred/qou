This section combines the substrate parameter $q$, the connection $\nabla$, and the Reeb derivation $\xi$ into a single first-order operator whose two limits recover the gauge and matter terms of the categorical action. The categorical Dirac operator interpolates between the full covariant derivative at $q = 1$ and its purely contact component $\iota_\xi \nabla$ as $q \to \infty$. This formula defines a unique morphism in $\mathbf{C}$ once $\nabla$, $q$, and $\xi$ are fixed. It is an algebraic first-order interpolation along the contact splitting rather than an analytic Connes spectral triple of the type constructed on $\mathrm{SU}_q(2)$ by Partha Sarathi Chakraborty and Arupkumar Pal \cite{chakraborty-pal2003} and by Ludwik Dąbrowski, Giovanni Landi, Andrzej Sitarz, Walter van Suijlekom, and Joseph C. Várilly \cite{dabrowski-landi-sitarz-vansuijlekom-varilly2005}.
