This section combines the aether parameter $q$, the connection $\nabla$, and the Reeb derivation $\xi$ into a single first-order operator whose two limits recover the gauge and matter terms of the categorical action. The categorical Dirac operator interpolates between the full covariant derivative at $q = 1$ and its purely contact component $\iota_\xi \nabla$ as $q \to \infty$. This formula defines a unique morphism in $\mathbf{C}$ once $\nabla$, $q$, and $\xi$ are fixed.
