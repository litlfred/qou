**Interpretive remark (physics reading, not a mathematical claim).** The [mass-endomorphism conjecture](#conj:mass-endomorphism) says that the graviton is "the residual of $\Phi_{\rm mass}$ after subtracting the Higgs contribution". [The vacuum-residual proposition](#prop:higgs-vacuum-residual-idempotent) gives one precise reading of that sentence at the level of idempotents. Read $e = t^{-1}\varrho_\Phi\varrho_\Phi^{*}$ as the Higgs contribution on $X = A^{(H)}$, and $r = \mathrm{id} - e$ as the residual. On the two-vacuum object $X \otimes X$, the residual of $e \otimes e$ splits along the symmetry $\beta_{X,X}$. Its purely residual, symmetric summand $P_{+} \circ (r \otimes r)$ is the **graviton candidate (candidate 3)**: the symmetric part of (vacuum residual) $\otimes$ (vacuum residual) on $X \otimes X$.

This is a choice of identification. It is not derived. By the "defined residual $\neq$ derived decomposition" rule, $r := \mathrm{id} - e$ is a definition. The proposition proves only that $r$, $R = \mathrm{id} - e \otimes e$ and $P_{\pm} \circ R$ are complementary idempotents. It does not prove that any of them is the graviton, and it does not prove that $\Phi_{\rm mass}$ splits as "$e$-part plus $r$-part". That would need $\Phi_{\rm mass}$ to commute with $e$, which is open.

**What remains before "spin 2" could be claimed.**

1. *A rotation action on $X$.* The labels $(\tfrac12, \tfrac12)$ in [Table 1](#tbl:g-action-on-substrate-table1-data) are $(j, y)$ for $G = \mathrm{SU}(2)_L \times \mathrm{U}(1)_Y$: weak isospin and hypercharge. They are not $(j_L, j_R)$ for a Lorentz or rotation group. So a spin label on $P_{+}\circ(r \otimes r)$ first needs an $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ that acts on $X$, fixes $\varrho_\Phi$, and is identified with spatial rotation. A candidate is the custodial diagonal of $\mathrm{SU}(2)_L \times \mathrm{SU}(2)_R$ acting on the real 4-dimensional doublet. A text search of the corpus finds no custodial group; none is formalised.
2. *The residual must be spin 1, and the object matters.* The spin-1 reading needs $X$ to be the real four-dimensional doublet, with $\operatorname{im} r$ the three directions orthogonal to the vacuum. Neither identification is made here. On the object the corpus actually names, $X = A^{(H)}$ with $\dim_q A^{(H)} = \mathrm{tr}(\mathrm{id}_{A^{(H)}}) = [2]_q$ by (G-2) and $\mathrm{tr}(\mathrm{id}_{\mathbf{1}}) = 1$, item 8 of the proposition gives $\mathrm{tr}(r) = [2]_q - 1$, which is $1$ at $q = 1$: a residual of dimension one, not three. So the spin-2 reading is not available on $A^{(H)}$ as (G-2) describes it.
3. *Removing the trace.* $\mathrm{Sym}^2(\text{spin }1) = \text{spin }2 \oplus \text{spin }0$. Isolating spin 2 needs a further idempotent on $P_{+}\circ(r\otimes r)$ that subtracts the trace part through the evaluation / coevaluation of $\operatorname{im} r$ (a rigid / pivotal datum). The present Lean development does not use one.
4. *Quantum dimensions.* Item 8 of the proposition gives $\mathrm{tr}(e_H) = \mathrm{tr}(\mathrm{id}_{\mathbf{1}})$ for any cyclic trace, so $\mathrm{tr}(e_H) = 1$ whenever the unit has trace $1$. The text of (G-4) assigns $[2]_q$ to "its trace dimension". That figure is $\dim_q A^{(H)}$ from (G-2), not the trace of the rank-one $e_H$. The mismatch is recorded here and left unchanged, for the owner to decide.

No empirical quantity enters the proposition. The value $t = v^2/m_e^2$ in (G-3) plays no role: only $t \neq 0$ is used.
