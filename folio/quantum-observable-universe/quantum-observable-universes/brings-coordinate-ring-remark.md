Bring's curve $\mathcal{B}$ is a smooth algebraic curve of genus $g = 4$; it serves as the spectral coordinate fibre of the algebraic substrate.

The symmetric group $S_5$ acts on $\mathcal{B}$ by permutation of the homogeneous coordinates. This action descends to an $S_5$-action on $\mathcal{O}_q(\mathcal{B})$ by algebra automorphisms, realizing the five-fold combinatorial symmetry of the substrate. The $2g = 8$ fundamental modes of the Reeb spectrum correspond to the $8$ independent cycles of $\mathcal{B}$, providing the spectral basis for the derivation $\xi_q$.
