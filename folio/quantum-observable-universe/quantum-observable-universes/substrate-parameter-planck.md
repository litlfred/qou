The **aether parameter** $q \in \mathbb{C}^*$ is the deformation parameter of the Frobenius--Hopf aether $A$. It governs the non-commutativity of the algebraic aether. At $q = 1$, the algebra becomes commutative and the quantum structure degenerates to its classical shadow.

The **quantum Planck constant** associated to $q$ is

$$
\hbar_q =  1 - q^{-1} =  \frac{q - 1}{q}.
$$

The pair $(q, \hbar_q)$ parametrizes the deviation of $A$ from commutativity. In the classical limit $q \to 1$, $\hbar_q \to 0$ recovers the commutative coordinate ring and the smooth manifold geometry.

The invertibility condition $q \ne 0$ ensures $A$ is a genuine Hopf algebra with well-defined antipode. Because $\hbar_q \ne 0$ whenever $q \ne 1$, the non-commutative regime always carries a non-trivial quantum scale.
