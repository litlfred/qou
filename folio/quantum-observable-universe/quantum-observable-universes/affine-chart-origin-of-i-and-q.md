Both categorical coordinates of $\mathbb{P}(V)$ originate from the transition between the Reeb chart $U_0$ and the antipodal chart $U_1$, governed by $z \cdot w = \kappa$.

For $\mathbb{P}_q(V)$, the homogeneous coordinates satisfy the quantum-plane relation $yx = q\,xy$. The charts are $z = y x^{-1}$ and $w = y^{-1} x$, yielding $z w = q^{-1}$. The substrate parameter $q$ acts precisely as the chart-transition coefficient. The ordering of each quotient matters: the pair $z = y x^{-1}$, $w = x y^{-1}$ multiplies to $1$ in any ring, so the coefficient $q^{-1}$ comes from the relation $yx = q\,xy$ and not from the affine charts alone.

At $q = 1$, the transition degenerates to $z w = 1$. The chart-swap involution $\iota(z) = z^{-1}$ generates a $\mathbb{Z}_2$ automorphism. The quarter-turn generator $\rho_{\pi/2} = i \mathrm{id}_V$ operates as the square root of $\iota$, identifying $i$ as the categorical coordinate. The Galois action exchanging $\pm i$ encapsulates the braiding orientation choice $c_{V,V} \leftrightarrow c_{V,V}^{-1}$.
