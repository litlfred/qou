Consider the representation category $\mathbf{C} = \mathrm{Rep}(\mathrm{SU}(2))$ with the standard forgetful functor $\tau$. The volume constraint requires $\det(g_V) = 1$. Specifically, the alternating form $\epsilon(v \wedge w) = v_1 w_2 - v_2 w_1$ on $V = \mathbb{C}^2$ is $\mathrm{SU}(2)$-invariant. Any tensor automorphism $g$ must preserve $\epsilon$, forcing $g_V \in \mathrm{SL}(2, \mathbb{C})$. The dagger structure enforces unitarity, $g_V^{-1} = g_V^{\dagger}$, reducing the group from $\mathrm{SL}(2, \mathbb{C})$ to its compact real form $\mathrm{SU}(2)$. Consequently $G = \mathrm{Aut}^{\otimes}(\tau) \cong \mathrm{SU}(2)$, recovering the gauge group from the categorical axioms.
