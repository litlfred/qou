The extra denominator $n$ forces tighter convergence constraints. Over $\mathbb{F}_p$, the series truncates to $\mathrm{Li}_2^{q,(p)}(x) = \sum_{n=1}^{p-1} x^n / (n [n]_q)$. Over $\mathbb{Q}_p$ and $\mathbb{C}_p$, it converges within $v_p(x) > 1/(p-1)$.

**What the literature covers.** The sources treat the undeformed case $q = 1$. There the finite truncation is the finite polylogarithm $\mathrm{li}_n(z) = \sum_{k=1}^{p-1} z^k/k^n$, and Besser proves that for $\mathrm{Li}^{(p)}_n(z) := \mathrm{Li}_n(z) - \mathrm{Li}_n(z^p)/p^n$ one has $\mathrm{Li}^{(p)}_n(X) \subset W$ and $\mathrm{Li}^{(p)}_n$ reduces modulo $p$ to $(1 - z^p)^{-1}\,\mathrm{li}_n(z)$, where $W$ is the ring of Witt vectors of $\bar{\mathbb{F}}_p$ and $X = \{z \in W : |z| = |z-1| = 1\}$ \cite[\S2, Prop.~2.1]{besser2000}; at a root of unity $\alpha \in X$, $p^{-n}\mathrm{Li}_n(\alpha) \equiv -\mathrm{li}_n(\sigma(\alpha))/(1-\alpha) \pmod p$ \cite[Cor.~2.2]{besser2000}. Coleman's $p$-adic polylogarithms on $\mathbb{C}_p$ are given by the defining power series on the residue disc of $0$, and $\mathrm{Li}^{(p)}_n$ is a convergent power series in $1/(1-z)$ outside $\{|z - 1| \le p^{-1/(p-1)}\}$ \cite[\S2, Thm.~2.7]{besser-dejeu2008}; that paper gives an algorithm computing them to a prescribed precision \cite[\S\S3--4]{besser-dejeu2008}. The $q$-deformed truncation and the threshold $v_p(x) > 1/(p-1)$ stated above are not taken from these sources, which do not treat $[n]_q$.

On the `hopf-algebra` tag: algebra closure and the shuffle relation depend on the normalisation. Schlesinger takes $\mathrm{Li}_{n_1,\dots,n_m}(z_1,\dots,z_m;q) = \sum_{0<k_1<\dots<k_m} z_1^{k_1}\cdots z_m^{k_m}/\big((1-q^{k_1})^{n_1}\cdots(1-q^{k_m})^{n_m}\big)$, proves that the rational span of these is an associative $\mathbb{Q}$-algebra \cite[\S2, Lemma~2]{schlesinger2001}, and derives $\mathrm{Li}_1(x;q)\,\mathrm{Li}_1(y;q) = \mathrm{Li}_{1,1}(x,y;q) + \mathrm{Li}_{1,1}(y,x;q) + \mathrm{Li}_2(xy;q)$ \cite[Cor.~3]{schlesinger2001}; the source states that this choice differs from the Kirillov and Koornwinder deformations and is made so that the algebra property holds. Its dilogarithm is $\sum_k z^k/(1-q^k)^2$, while the one interpreted here is $\sum_n x^n/(n[n]_q)$, so those results are not established for this normalisation by that paper.
