The universal $R$-matrix of $U_q(\mathfrak{sl}_2)$ admits the compact $q$-Pochhammer dilogarithm factorisation of Ludwig D. Faddeev and Rinat M. Kashaev \cite{faddeev_kashaev1994} and Ludwig D. Faddeev \cite{faddeev1994}, whose logarithm matches the categorical $q$-dilogarithm $\mathrm{Li}_2^q$ at leading order so that $\mathrm{Li}_2^q$ acts as the categorical braiding potential. Under archimedean modular-double realisation, Ludwig D. Faddeev's noncompact integral quantum dilogarithm $\Phi_b$ \cite{faddeev1995} supplies the tetrahedral building block of the three-dimensional state-integral topological quantum field theory constructed by Jørgen Ellegaard Andersen and Rinat M. Kashaev \cite{andersen-kashaev2014} (expounded for the Garoufalidis–Kashaev state-integral invariant by Amelia Palmer Dusenbury \cite{dusenbury2025}).
