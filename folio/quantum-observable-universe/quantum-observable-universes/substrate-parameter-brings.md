On Bring's surface, $V \otimes V \cong V_1 \oplus V_0$. The $R$-matrix eigenvalues are $q$ and $-q^{-1}$.  By the Hecke relation the unordered pair $\{q, -q^{-1}\}$ is intrinsic; the positive-definite dagger selects $q > 0$.  The categorical parameter $q$ is a real-valued function on $M$: at each point $p \in M$ the braiding spectrum extracts a local $q(p) \in \mathbb{R}_{>0}$.  The observed substrate parameter $q_0$ at the point of observation is an empirical value, not derived from the topology of Bring's surface; the Euler characteristic $\chi_{\mathrm{eu}}(M_0) = -6$ constrains the quantum dimension $[2]_q$ but does \emph{not} fix the pointwise value of $q_0$.
