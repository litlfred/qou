The trivial connection $\nabla_0 = d$ has $F_{\nabla_0} = 0$, which
is by definition $\star_q$-self-dual; hence the zero instanton exists.
Non-trivial instantons exist on $\mathrm{SU}_q(2)$-bundles over the
CR realisation ([example](#ex:c-quantum-observable-universe)) via
categorical :refterm[descent]{#descent} of the classical instanton of A. A. Belavin, A. M. Polyakov, A. S. Schwartz, and Yu. S. Tyupkin (BPST) \cite{belavin-polyakov-schwartz-tyupkin1975}
(self-duality, proved below).
