Let $\hat{X}, \hat{P}$ be the position and momentum observables on the fibre $\mathbb{P}^1(\mathbb{C})$. Let $J \colon H \to H$ be the complex structure on the Levi distribution ($J^2 = -\mathrm{Id}$). Let $q \in \mathrm{End}(\mathbf{1})$ be the aether parameter. The **$q$-deformed commutator** of $\hat{X}$ and $\hat{P}$ at a point $p \in M$ is

$$
  [\hat{X}, \hat{P}]_q := J\,\hbar_q(p),
  \qquad \hbar_q(p) = 1 - q(p)^{-1},
$$

where $\hbar_q$ is the quantum Planck constant.
