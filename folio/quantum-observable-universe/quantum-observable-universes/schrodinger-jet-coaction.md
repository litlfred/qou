For a section $\psi \in \Gamma(\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{lin}})$, the Schrödinger dynamics dictate

$$
  J\hbar_q(p)\iota_{\xi_q} D_A \psi =  \hat{H}_q \psi,
$$

where $\hat{H}_q = -\tfrac{1}{2}\hbar_q\Delta_q + V_q$ is the $q$-deformed Hamiltonian. The local quantum Planck constant is $\hbar_q(p) = 1 - q(p)^{-1}$. Contracting $D_A\psi$ with the Reeb vector field $\xi_q$ produces a section of $\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{lin}}$. As $q(p) \to 1$, uncertainty vanishes and the wave trajectory freezes classically.
