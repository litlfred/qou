Let $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ be a quantum observable universe. The **$q$-deformed exterior derivative** $d_q \colon \Omega^k(A) \to \Omega^{k+1}(A)$ is

$$
  d_q =  q^{-1}d + (1 - q^{-1})\pi_V \circ d,
$$

where $d \colon \Omega^k(A) \to \Omega^{k+1}(A)$ is the differential of the non-commutative de Rham complex. The *$q$-adjoint* $\delta_q \colon \Omega^{k+1}(A) \to \Omega^k(A)$ is the formal adjoint of $d_q$ with respect to the dagger structure.
