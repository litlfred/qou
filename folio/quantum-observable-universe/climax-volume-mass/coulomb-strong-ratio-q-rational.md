*For every real $q > 0$ (the aether-anchor regime, $\mathrm{Im}(q) = 0$ at the archimedean place), the ratio satisfies:*

$$
  \tanh(\ln q) \;=\; \frac{q - q^{-1}}{q + q^{-1}} \;=\; \frac{q^2 - 1}{q^2 + 1}.
$$

*On a positive braid closure the antisymmetric-to-symmetric ratio of [the Coulomb charge](#def:coulomb-charge) equals the q-rational expression $(q_0^2 - 1)/(q_0^2 + 1)$ at the aether anchor. The analytic and the algebraic forms are a single object.*

## Significance

The antisymmetric-to-symmetric ratio equals $\tanh(\ln q)$ **on a positive braid closure** — that is, at $n_- = 0$; for mixed crossing signs it acquires the multiplicity factor $\tfrac{n_+ + n_-}{n_+ - n_-}$ and is not a constant of the framework ([decomposition](#prop:crossing-energy-decomposition), item 4). This proposition confirms that on that locus the analytic and the algebraic forms coincide identically over real $q > 0$. The identity below is independent of that scoping: it is a statement about $\tanh$, and holds for every real $q > 0$ whatever the crossing counts. This removes any ambiguity regarding whether the framework employs a "classical-$\tanh$" or a "$q$-deformed $\tanh_q$" realization at the aether anchor.

The forms match because $\exp(\ln q) = q$ for $q > 0$. Therefore,

$$
  \tanh(\ln q)
  \;=\; \frac{e^{\ln q} - e^{-\ln q}}{e^{\ln q} + e^{-\ln q}}
  \;=\; \frac{q - q^{-1}}{q + q^{-1}}
  \;=\; \frac{q^2 - 1}{q^2 + 1}
  \quad\text{(multiply numerator and denominator by $q$).}
$$
