Physically, the $R$-matrix endows the vortex filament $\varrho$ with a "quantum exclusion zone" of radius $\hbar_q$. The filament cannot pass through itself because doing so would demand infinite categorical energy. This constitutes the knot-theoretic analogue of the Pauli exclusion principle, with the non-cocommutativity of $\Delta_q$ serving as fermionic statistics.
