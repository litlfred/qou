Localizing the global Möbius self-avoidance kernel at the crossings of a braid projection decomposes the crossing energy $E_\times$ ([Definition](#def:crossing-energy)) into a symmetric strong baseline and an orientation-odd electromagnetic shift proportional to the Coulomb charge $Q(\mathfrak{H}) = \tfrac{1}{2}(n_+ - n_-)$ ([Definition](#def:coulomb-charge)). This section develops these crossing-energy identities on the trefoil and in the commutative limit over the categorical coordinate ring $R[i, q^{\pm 1}]$.
