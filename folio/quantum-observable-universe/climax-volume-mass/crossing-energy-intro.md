Localizing the global Möbius self-avoidance kernel at the crossings of a braid projection decomposes the crossing energy $E_\times$ ([Definition](#def:crossing-energy)) into a symmetric strong baseline and an orientation-odd electromagnetic shift proportional to the Coulomb charge $Q(\mathfrak{H}) = \tfrac{1}{2}(n_+ - n_-)$ ([Definition](#def:coulomb-charge)). Because the Hecke generator $T = P \circ R$ has eigenvalues $(q, -q^{-1})$, the ratio of the per-crossing Coulomb shift $q - q^{-1}$ to the strong baseline $q + q^{-1}$ is the rational function $\tanh(\ln q) = (q^2-1)/(q^2+1)$ ([Proposition](#prop:coulomb-strong-ratio-q-rational)), and the skein difference $L_+ - L_- = (q - q^{-1})L_0$ is bar-antisymmetric under $q \mapsto q^{-1}$ ([Proposition](#prop:skein-bar-antisymmetry)). This section develops these crossing-energy identities on the trefoil and in the commutative limit over the categorical coordinate ring $R[i, q^{\pm 1}]$.
