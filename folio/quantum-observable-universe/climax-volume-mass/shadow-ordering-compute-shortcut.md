**Remark (computational shortcut from local constancy).**
Following the [unstable-atom](#def:unstable-atom) "Computational shortcut", restrict expensive computations (per-generator $\hat V_i^{\mathrm{full}}$ extraction) to exact evaluation only at points of the [phase-boundary variety](#prop:phase-boundary-variety) $\mathcal{B}$. In the complement $Q \setminus \mathcal{B}$, all shadow prices, the filtration, and the per-generator data are locally constant (by [local constancy of confinement](#prop:confinement-local-constancy)). Linear interpolation between nearest magic-number anchors is mathematically justified.
