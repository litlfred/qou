Let $\beta \in B_n$ be the canonical braid representative — the Gröbner–Shirshov normal form at the minimal level of the brane tower — of a particle knot $K = \hat{\beta}$ (braid closure), with generators $\sigma_{i_1}^{\epsilon_1} \cdots \sigma_{i_k}^{\epsilon_k}$ where $\epsilon_j = \pm 1$. For $q \neq \pm 1$, the **$q$-momentum** is

$$
  p_q(\beta) =  \sum_{j=1}^{k} [\epsilon_j]_q
  =  \sum_{j=1}^{k} \frac{q^{\epsilon_j} - q^{-\epsilon_j}}{q - q^{-1}},
$$

where $[\epsilon]_q = (q^{\epsilon} - q^{-\epsilon})/(q - q^{-1})$ is the $q$-integer.
