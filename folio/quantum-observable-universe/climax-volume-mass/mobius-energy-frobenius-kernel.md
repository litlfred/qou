*Let $\gamma$ be a knot filament and $K_{\mathrm{FH}}(q; s, t) = \mathrm{tr}\bigl(\Delta(\gamma(s)) \otimes \Delta(\gamma(t))\bigr)$ the Frobenius--Hopf kernel. Assume two reductions:*

1. *(Hodge-star reduction) as $q \to 1$, $K_{\mathrm{FH}}(q; s, t)$ converges to the classical kernel $K_{\mathrm{Kill}}(s, t)$ of the Frobenius pairing $\int_A \circ \mu$;*
2. *(Killing-form reduction) at $q = 1$ that pairing is the standard $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ Killing form, and $K_{\mathrm{Kill}}(s, t) = 1 / \lvert \gamma(s) - \gamma(t) \rvert^2$ for $\gamma(s) \neq \gamma(t)$.*

*Then the Frobenius--Hopf kernel collapses to the repulsive $1/r^2$:*

$$
  K_{\mathrm{FH}}(q; s, t)
  \xrightarrow{q \to 1}
  \frac{1}{\lvert \gamma(s) - \gamma(t) \rvert^2}.
$$

*Neither reduction is proved here; both are hypotheses.*
