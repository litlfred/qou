Each Wedderburn block $\lambda \vdash k$ of the Iwahori–Hecke algebra $H_k(q)$ carries a knot-operator energy $E(\lambda, q) = |w_\lambda(q)/d_\lambda(q) - 1/[k]_q!|$ measuring the distance of its normalized Wedderburn weight from the trivial-representation baseline ([Definition](#def:knot-operator-energy)). Ordering partitions by $E(\lambda, q)$ defines the local shadow-price ordering ([Definition](#def:local-shadow-ordering)), whose vanishing locus where two shadow prices cross forms a real-algebraic phase-boundary variety of codimension one ([Proposition](#prop:phase-boundary-variety)). This section proves existence, uniqueness, continuity, and local constancy of the shadow-price ordering off the phase-boundary variety, and concludes by deriving the zero-parameter per-component linking-weight selection rule $\mathrm{lw}(c)(q) = \frac{1}{c+1}\sum_{\lambda \vdash (c+1)} \dim_q V_\lambda$ at its exact $c = 1$ (knot), $c = 3$ (Borromean link), and $c = 2$ (Hopf link) anchors.
