This section proves existence, uniqueness, continuity, and local constancy of the shadow-price ordering off the phase-boundary variety, and concludes by deriving the zero-parameter per-component linking-weight selection rule $\mathrm{lw}(c)(q) = \frac{1}{c+1}\sum_{\lambda \vdash (c+1)} \dim_q V_\lambda$ at its exact $c = 1$ (knot), $c = 3$ (Borromean link), and $c = 2$ (Hopf link) anchors.
