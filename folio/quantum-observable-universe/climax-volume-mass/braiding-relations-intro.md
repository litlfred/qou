The categorical axioms of a quantum observable universe impose polynomial relations on the Iwahori–Hecke generators beyond the bare braid and quadratic relations, acting as a cohomological spectral filter on candidate closed braids. This section translates the quantum-universe and observability axioms into vertex-algebra relations on $H_n(q)$ ([Proposition](#prop:vertex-algebra-relations)) and their prolongations along the brane-tower jet bundle ([Proposition](#prop:vertex-algebra-jet-relations)), identifies the classical skein relation as the Archimedean shadow of these identities, and exhibits the genus-five torus knot $T_{2,11}$ as the first generator annihilated by both Chebyshev genus overflow ($\deg_u a_{11} = 5 > 4 = g(\Sigma_4)$) and the crystallographic obstruction $\mathbb{Z}_{11} \not\hookrightarrow S_5 = \mathrm{Aut}(\Sigma_4)$.
