Diagonalizing the cyclic sheet permutation $C_5 \subset S_5$ on the five branch sheets of Bring's surface via the discrete Fourier transform produces the Lagrange resolvents $w_0, \ldots, w_4$ ([discrete Fourier transform diagonalises $C_5$ on the Bring's surface](#prop:lagrange-resolvent-transform), [Lagrange resolvents and spectral form](#rem:lagrange-resolvents)). In this diagonal basis, the kinematic observables of a braided state take explicit algebraic forms: $q$-momentum is extracted from the braid writhe on the fundamental resolvent ([$q$-Momentum from the braid writhe](#def:q-momentum)), $q$-angular momentum is given by the $\mathcal{U}_q(\mathfrak{su}(2))$ Casimir eigenvalue along the same eigenline ([$q$-Angular momentum from the $\mathrm{SU}_q(2)$ Casimir](#def:q-angular-momentum)), and $q$-deformed energy combines both with the Reeb flow ([$q$-Deformed Energy](#def:q-energy)), yielding the $q$-deformed Noether conservation laws and their controlled non-commutative correction terms ([Noether's theorem in the $q$-deformed setting: symmetries, conservation, and anomalies](#rem:noether-theorem-callout)).
