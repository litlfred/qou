Diagonalizing the cyclic sheet permutation $C_5 \subset S_5$ on the five branch sheets of Bring's surface via the discrete Fourier transform produces the Lagrange resolvents $w_0, \ldots, w_4$ ([Proposition](#prop:lagrange-resolvent-transform), [Remark](#rem:lagrange-resolvents)). In this diagonal basis, the kinematic observables of a braided state take explicit algebraic forms: $q$-momentum is extracted from the braid writhe on the fundamental resolvent ([Definition](#def:q-momentum)), $q$-angular momentum is given by the $\mathcal{U}_q(\mathfrak{su}(2))$ Casimir eigenvalue along the same eigenline ([Definition](#def:q-angular-momentum)), and $q$-deformed energy combines both with the Reeb flow ([Definition](#def:q-energy)), yielding the $q$-deformed Noether conservation laws and their controlled non-commutative correction terms ([Remark](#rem:noether-theorem-callout)).
