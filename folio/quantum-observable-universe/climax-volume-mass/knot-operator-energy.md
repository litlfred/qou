**Definition.**
*Let $H_n(R, q)$ be the Hecke algebra at substrate parameter $q$. For each Wedderburn block indexed by a partition $\lambda \vdash k$ ($k = 2, \ldots, k_{\max}(K, q)$, where $k_{\max}(K, q)$ is the per-atom truncation determined by the interaction-strand requirement of $K$ — see [confinement level](#def:confinement-level)), the **knot operator energy** of $\lambda$ at $q$ is the absolute value of its LP-dual shadow price ([mass coefficient as shadow price](#prop:mass-coefficient-shadow-price)):*

$$
  E(\lambda, q)
  \;:=\;
  \bigl| u_\lambda(q) \bigr|
  \;=\;
  \left|
    \frac{w_\lambda(q)}{d_\lambda(q)}
    \;-\;
    \frac{1}{[k]_q!}
  \right|,
$$

*where $w_\lambda(q) = d_q(\lambda)^2 / [k]_q!$ is the Wedderburn weight and $d_\lambda(q) = \dim_q V_\lambda$ is the quantum dimension.*
