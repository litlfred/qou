**Remark (crossing weights are Hecke eigenvalues rather than postulates).**

The per-crossing weights $(q, -q^{-1})$ of the [crossing energy](#def:crossing-energy) are not an energetic penalty laid on top of a topological framework. They are the **two eigenvalues of the Hecke generator** $T = P \circ R \colon V \otimes V \to V \otimes V$ acting on the symmetric/antisymmetric decomposition. The rigid monoidal structure forces them:

1. *Rigidity fixes the $R$-matrix determinant.* Dualisability of $V$ and the categorical volume constraint ([volume constraint](#def:quantum-observable-universe)) require the braiding $R = c_{V,V}$ to satisfy $\det(R|_{V \otimes V}) = 1$, with eigenvalues $(q, q^{-1})$ on $S^{2} V \oplus \Lambda^{2} V$.
2. *The Hecke generator carries the transposition sign.* The Hecke generator is $T = P \circ R$, where $P$ is the swap. Since $P|_{\Lambda^{2} V} = -\mathrm{id}$, composition flips the sign on the antisymmetric summand. This gives $T$-eigenvalues $(q, -q^{-1})$ with $\det(T) = -1$, which matches the Hecke minimal polynomial $(T - q)(T + q^{-1}) = 0$ ([eigenvalue extraction](#prop:braiding-eigenvalue-extraction)).
3. *Crossing weights are eigenvalues rather than postulates.* Assigning a positive crossing $L_{+}$ the weight $q$ and a negative crossing $L_{-}$ the weight $-q^{-1}$ represents precisely the action of $T$ on a symmetric-channel input and $T^{-1}$ on an antisymmetric-channel input. The sum $\mathfrak{E}_{\mathrm{cross}}(\mathfrak{H})$ is therefore a trace of $T^{\pm 1}$-eigenvalues along the braid. The categorical structure dictates this assignment.
4. *Gröbner--Shirshov resolution.* Under reduction of $\mathfrak{H}$ to Gröbner--Shirshov normal form ([nuclear normal form](#prop:nuclear-groebner-reduction)), each crossing contributes its $T$-eigenvalue exactly once. The net $\mathfrak{E}_{\mathrm{cross}}$ is the resulting Laurent polynomial in $q$, read off the normal form. Removing any of these weights would violate duality and break the Hecke relation.

This quantity is computed entirely from the $R$-matrix braiding ([R-matrix](#rem:r-matrix-braiding)) of $\mathcal{U}_q(\mathfrak{su}(2))$ acting on the tensor product of constituent representations.
