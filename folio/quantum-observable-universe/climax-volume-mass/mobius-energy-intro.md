Whereas [*The Mass Endomorphism*](#chap:mass-endomorphism) evaluates the algebraic trace $\mathrm{tr}_M(\Phi_{\mathrm{mass}}(\beta))$ on the Iwahori–Hecke side, this chapter develops the geometric and kinematic realization of closed braids on the complement manifold $S^3 \setminus K$ and the Bring surface $\Sigma_4$.

The exposition begins by constructing a categorical self-avoidance energy on a closed strand $\varrho \colon S^1 \to \tau(A)$ from the Frobenius double integral, the Hopf comultiplication, and the $q$-Hodge star. In the classical limit $q \to 1$, the Frobenius pairing degenerates onto the $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ Killing form with a repulsive $1/r^2$ kernel ([classical limit of the Frobenius--Hopf kernel is the repulsive $1/r^2$](#lem:mobius-energy-frobenius-kernel)), recovering the Freedman–He–Wang Möbius energy up to a topological constant ([categorical recovery of Möbius energy](#prop:mobius-energy)). Away from the symmetric point ($q \neq 1$), non-cocommutativity of the Hopf coproduct regularizes the short-distance singularity at the scale $\hbar_q = 1 - q^{-1}$ ([$q$-deformed commutator replaces $1/r^2$ with a hard kernel at $r = \hbar_q$](#lem:nsi-q-deformed-kernel)), obstructing self-intersection of distinct strand segments inside the fibre $\tau(A)$ ([obstruction to self-intersection](#cor:non-self-intersection)).
