Whereas [*The Mass Endomorphism*](#chap:mass-endomorphism) evaluates the algebraic trace $\mathrm{tr}_M(\Phi_{\mathrm{mass}}(\beta))$ on the Iwahori–Hecke side, this chapter develops the geometric and kinematic realization of closed braids on the complement manifold $S^3 \setminus K$ and the Bring surface $\Sigma_4$.

A categorical self-avoidance energy on a closed strand $\varrho \colon S^1 \to \tau(A)$ is constructed first, from the Frobenius double integral, the Hopf comultiplication, and the $q$-Hodge star.
