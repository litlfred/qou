Let $K = \hat{\beta}$ be a knot/link with braid representative $\beta \in B_n$. The **$q$-angular momentum** is the eigenvalue of the $q$-Casimir element $C_q$ (the [$q$-Casimir element](#def:q-casimir)) on the spin-$j$ irreducible of $\mathrm{SU}_q(2)$:

$$
  J_q^2(K) =  [j]_q  [j+1]_q
  =  \frac{q^j - q^{-j}}{q - q^{-1}} \cdot
        \frac{q^{j+1} - q^{-(j+1)}}{q - q^{-1}}
$$

where $j$ is the spin quantum number that the Jones--Wenzl projector $p_n$ acting on the $n$-strand braid determines: $j = n/2$ for the highest-weight component.
