Per-generator volume profile for the ${}^{14}\mathrm{C} \to {}^{14}\mathrm{N}$ decay. For each braid generator $\sigma_i$: $k_i$ is its chiral degree (the number of crossings that change type), $V_i({}^{14}\mathrm{C})$ and $V_i({}^{14}\mathrm{N})$ are the parent and daughter generator volumes, and $\Delta V_i = V_i({}^{14}\mathrm{N}) - V_i({}^{14}\mathrm{C})$ is that generator's contribution to the energy change. The converting neutron's own generator $\sigma_6$ carries the dominant asymmetry ($\mathbf{-1.660}$, nearly half of the total $-3.361$) that drives the ${}^{14}\mathrm{C} \to {}^{14}\mathrm{N}$ difference, while the downstream generators $\sigma_7$–$\sigma_{12}$ are unaffected.
