The $2\times 2$ transfer matrix for the knot pair, with entries in $q$; its diagonal entries are opposite in sign, so the trace vanishes.

| Entry | Formula |
|:-----:|---------|
| $(1,1)$ | $\dfrac{q - q^{-1}}{2}$ |
| $(1,2)$ | $1$ |
| $(2,1)$ | $1$ |
| $(2,2)$ | $-\dfrac{q - q^{-1}}{2}$ |
| $\mathrm{tr}$ | $0$ |
