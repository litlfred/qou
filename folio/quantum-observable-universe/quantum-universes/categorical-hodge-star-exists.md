Let $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ be a [quantum universe](#def:quantum-universe) of dimension $n$ with volume form $\mathrm{vol} \in \Omega^n(A)$. The [categorical Hodge star](#def:categorical-hodge-star) $\star \colon \Omega^k(A) \to \Omega^{n-k}(A)$ exists as a morphism in $\mathbf{C}$ and satisfies $\star^2 = \mathrm{id}$. Uniqueness is the next proposition.
