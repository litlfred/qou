Let $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ be a [quantum universe](#def:quantum-universe) over the commutative ring $R$.

The **:defterm[Grothendieck group]{#grothendieck-group}** $K_0(\mathbf{C})$ is the abelian group generated by the isomorphism classes $[X]$ of objects of $\mathbf{C}$, subject to the relations $[X \oplus Y] = [X] + [Y]$.
