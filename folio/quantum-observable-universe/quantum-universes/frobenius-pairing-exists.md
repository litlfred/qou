The Frobenius form makes the multiplication a non-degenerate pairing, yielding the self-duality $A \cong A^\vee$. It sends a pair $(a, b)$ to the scalar $\int_A(\mu(a, b))$. 

Let $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ be a [quantum universe](#def:quantum-universe) with multiplication $\mu$ and Frobenius form $\int_A$. The non-degenerate [Frobenius pairing](#def:frobenius-pairing) $\langle \cdot, \cdot \rangle = \int_A \circ \mu$ exists as a morphism in $\mathbf{C}$. Its adjoint $A \to A^\vee$ is an isomorphism. Uniqueness up to scalar is the subject of the next section.
