Tannaka--Krein duality recovers the gauge group from the fibre functor $\tau$. It comprises four statements:

1. **Reconstruction.** The group $G$ emerges from $\tau$: $G \cong \mathrm{Aut}^{\otimes}(\tau)$.
2. **Equivalence.** The functor $\tau$ establishes a monoidal equivalence $\mathbf{C} \simeq \mathrm{Rep}(G)$.
3. **Rigidity.** Any two fibre functors are isomorphic, reconstructing the same group up to inner automorphism.
4. **Constraint propagation.** Algebraic conditions on $\tau$ propagate to $G$. The volume constraint cuts the automorphism group from $\mathrm{GL}$ down to $\mathrm{SL}$.

**Origin of the deformation parameter.** The deformation parameter enters categorically: when the braiding $c_{V,V}$ is non-symmetric, the parameter is its eigenvalue on $S^2 V$, and Woronowicz's reconstruction recovers the quantum deformation of $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ at that value. This fixes what the parameter *is*, not what it equals — the braiding is assumed data, so its eigenvalue is determined by the assumption rather than by anything upstream of it. Chapter 2 extracts the parameter from the braiding; its numerical value is the CAL-2 calibration, not a categorical consequence.
