Let $\tau \colon \mathbf{C} \to R\text{-}\mathrm{Mod}$ be the fibre functor. A symmetry is a family of $R$-module automorphisms compatible with the tensor product — a monoidal natural automorphism of $\tau$. The **Tannaka dual**, or emergent gauge group,
$$
  G \;=\; \mathrm{Aut}^{\otimes}(\tau)
$$
collects these families.
