A categorical connection equips the observable algebra $A$ with a covariant-derivative structure. The abstract triple carries the same differential geometry as a bundle with connection. Here $\Omega^1(A)$ plays the role of one-forms, and $\nabla$ sends an observable to its differential.

Every [quantum universe](#def:quantum-universe) $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ admits a [categorical connection](#def:categorical-connection), which is a morphism $\nabla \colon A \to \Omega^1(A)$ satisfying the Leibniz rule; uniqueness up to gauge transformation follows immediately after the existence proof.
