Are the axioms of a [quantum universe](#def:quantum-universe) independent?

**Temporal braiding.** Over a characteristic-zero field, Deligne's theorem \cite{deligne1990} shows that every symmetric :refterm[monoidal category]{#monoidal-category} satisfying moderate growth admits a fibre functor. A quantum universe requires only a braiding, and works over an arbitrary commutative ring $R$, so neither symmetry of the braiding nor existence of a fibre functor follows from the monoidal axioms alone. The well-founded braiding axiom is therefore independent of the fibre functor.

**Frobenius--Hopf aether.** A general algebra or bialgebra object in a braided monoidal category need not admit a non-degenerate Frobenius pairing or an involutive antipode. The self-duality $A \cong A^{\vee}$ induced by the Frobenius form and its [snake identities](#def:snake-identity) is strictly stronger than the algebra and coalgebra axioms alone.

**Symmetric Frobenius trace.** A Frobenius aether need not be symmetric: the left and right categorical traces induced by a non-symmetric Frobenius form differ by the Nakayama automorphism, which is the identity morphism exactly when the Frobenius pairing is symmetric. Axiom 3 is therefore a genuine restriction on the [Frobenius--Hopf aether](#def:frobenius-hopf-substrate) rather than a consequence of axiom 2.

**Universal first-order differential calculus.** For smooth commutative algebras, Kähler differentials represent first-order derivations automatically; for noncommutative Frobenius--Hopf aethers \cite{woronowicz1989}, first-order differential calculi need not reduce to a commutative quotient, so carrying the universal kernel $\Omega^1(A) = \ker(\mu \colon A \otimes A \to A)$ as part of the structure is an independent datum rather than a consequence of the algebra laws.

In full generality, the three axioms and the differential calculus datum are mutually independent.
