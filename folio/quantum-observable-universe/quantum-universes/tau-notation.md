The forgetful functor is denoted $\tau$ to avoid conflict with differential-form notation ($\omega_i$, $\omega_V$). The choice of $\tau$ carries physical and mathematical resonances:

- **Tannakian reconstruction.** The letter $\tau$ recalls the Tannaka--Krein theorem reconstructing the gauge group $G = \mathrm{Aut}^{\otimes}(\tau)$.
- **Trace / integration.** In von Neumann algebras, a faithful normal state is denoted $\tau$. The forgetful functor underlies the Frobenius integration $\int_A$, whose notation is fixed in Chapter 2.
- **Proper time.** In the archimedean realisation, $\tau$ represents the observer's proper time. It parameterises the emergence of $R$-modules from the abstract category.
