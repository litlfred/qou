Commutativity is verified by tracing both paths around the diagram.

**Top-right path** (variational $\to$ coupling). This path turns a jet into a substrate parameter $q$ and its Hamiltonian $\hat{H}_q$. Applying the Euler--Lagrange operator $\mathrm{EL}[\mathfrak{S}_q]$ to a jet $j^1_p \sigma$ yields the variational equation $\mathrm{Ric}_{g_\varTheta} = T^{(q)}$ ([GR substrate geometry](#rem:gr-substrate-geometry)). Solving for $L_\varTheta$ determines the Webster metric. This in turn fixes the substrate field via the coupling $q(p) = 1 + \lvert\langle L_\varTheta, F_A^+\rangle\rvert$. This determines $\hbar_q = 1 - q^{-1}$, the $q$-Reeb field $\xi_q$, and the $q$-Hamiltonian $\hat{H}_q = -\tfrac{1}{2}\hbar_q\Delta_q + V_q$ appearing in the transport equation.

**Left-bottom path** (evaluation $\to$ transport). This path turns the same jet into a transported wavefunction $\psi$. The jet evaluation map $j^1_p \sigma \mapsto \sigma(p)$ produces a section $\psi \in \Gamma(\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{lin}})$ by linearising the fibre $\mathbb{P}^1(\mathbb{C})$ at the chosen base-point. Parallel transport along $A$ then imposes $J\hbar_q\iota_{\xi_q} D_A\psi = \hat{H}_q\psi$ ([Schrödinger equation](#rem:schrodinger-jet-coaction)).

**Commutativity.** Both paths share the same input ($j^1_p \sigma$) and output (a section $\psi$ satisfying the transport equation at substrate parameter $q$). The chain rule for the composite $\mathfrak{S}_q[\nabla, L_\varTheta, \psi]$ factorises the variation into two coupled equations:
1. $\delta \mathfrak{S}_q / \delta L_\varTheta = 0$ determines $L_\varTheta$ (hence $q$ and $\hat{H}_q$) along the top row.
2. $\delta \mathfrak{S}_q / \delta \psi = 0$ yields $D_\nabla\psi = 0$ ([variational equations](#prop:variational-equations), Item 3) along the bottom row.

The coupling $q = 1 + \lvert\langle L_\varTheta, F_A^+\rangle\rvert$ ensures that the parameter $q$ matches along both paths. The non-degeneracy of $\int_A$ ([Frobenius pairing](#def:frobenius-pairing)) guarantees the solution sets agree. Solving for $L_\varTheta$ first and substituting into the transport equation yields the same $\psi$ as evaluating the jet and transporting along $A$.
