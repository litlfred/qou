Specialising to the archimedean setting verifies that the categorical formalism recovers the classical gauge-theoretic object. In the archimedean quantum universe, a categorical instanton specialises to an *instanton connection* on $P$. This is a connection $A$ whose curvature $F_A$, restricted to $H = \ker\varTheta$, is self-dual: $F_A|_H = {*_H}F_A|_H$. The $q$-Hodge star $\star_q$ specialises to the Webster Hodge star $*_H$ on $\Lambda^2 H^*$. Consequently, $\star_q$-self-duality recovers classical $*_H$-self-duality.
