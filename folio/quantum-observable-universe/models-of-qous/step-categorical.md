**Categorical step.** The aether parameter $q$ requires a structure to deform. At $q = 1$, the classical symmetric tensor category appears. For $q \neq 1$, the action resolves into a braided one. The aether is a rigid braided monoidal category $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ equipped with a Frobenius–Hopf object $A$ and a fibre functor $\tau$. The parameter $q$ records the deformation of this categorical action. The Reeb derivation $\delta_q$ promotes $q$ to a connection on the categorical bundle; its curvature is the Yekutieli MI tower. Subsequent chapters develop the axioms, derive the Reeb derivation, and construct descent, jets, and the Gröbner–Shirshov filtration to provide the categorical infrastructure.
