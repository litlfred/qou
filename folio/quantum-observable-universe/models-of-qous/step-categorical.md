**Categorical step.** The substrate parameter $q$ requires a structure to deform. At $q = 1$, the classical symmetric tensor category appears. For $q \neq 1$, the action resolves into a braided one. The substrate is a rigid braided monoidal category $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ equipped with a Frobenius–Hopf object $A$ and a fibre functor $\tau$. The parameter $q$ records the deformation of this categorical action. The Reeb derivation $\delta_q$ promotes $q$ to a connection on the categorical bundle; its curvature is the Yekutieli MI tower. The axioms are developed, and the Reeb derivation derived, in subsequent chapters. Descent, jets, and the Gröbner–Shirshov filtration are developed to provide the categorical infrastructure.
