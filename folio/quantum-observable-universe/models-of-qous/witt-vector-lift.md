Let $H$ be a commutative formal group over $\mathbb{F}_p$ equipped with a skewed-Poisson structure $\pi_<$ (a biderivation $\pi_<\colon\mathcal{O}(H)\otimes\mathcal{O}(H)\to\mathcal{O}(H)$ satisfying a graded anti-symmetry and a $p$-truncated Jacobi condition). The **Witt vector lift** is the formal group $\widehat{G}$ over $W(\mathbb{F}_p) \cong \mathbb{Z}_p$ obtained by integrating $\pi_<$ in the sense of Fresse.
