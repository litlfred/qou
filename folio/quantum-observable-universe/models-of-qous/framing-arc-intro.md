Every quantum observable universe passes through five structural stages that carry the abstract axioms of a braided monoidal category into numerical predictions for physical spectra. Each chapter of this monograph resides at a specific stage of this pipeline:

1. **Categorical stage.** A rigid braided monoidal category $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ equipped with a Frobenius–Hopf object $A$, a fibre functor $\tau$, and an internal Reeb derivation $\xi$ deformed by the aether parameter $q$.
2. **Algebraic stage.** The representation of the $n$-strand braid group $B_n$ on $A^{\otimes n}$, its factorization through the Iwahori–Hecke quotient $H_n(q)$ generated by $(\sigma_i - q)(\sigma_i + q^{-1}) = 0$, and its closure under the Jones–Markov trace $\mathrm{tr}_M$.
3. **Geometric stage.** The realization of closed braids as hyperbolic link complements embedded in the genus-four Bring surface $\Sigma_4$ ($\chi(\Sigma_4) = -6$, $b_1(\Sigma_4) = 8$, $\mathrm{Area}(\Sigma_4) = 12\pi$), where ideal $q$-tetrahedral volume and cohomological defect determine topological mass and three-colour charge.
4. **Archimedean stage.** The ring extension $R \to R[i] \to R[i][q, q^{-1}]$ evaluated at the aether parameter $q_0$, pairing the algebraic trace with hyperbolic period integrals via the non-abelian Stokes mass identity.
5. **Physical stage.** The resulting dimensionless mass ratios, nuclear binding energies, $\beta$-decay $Q$-values, and electromagnetic and weak couplings, tested against experimental observation and extended to molecular braids.

This chapter defines the vocabulary of the five-step pipeline and surveys the concrete models—Archimedean contact manifolds, Calabi–Yau and ALE spaces, $p$-adic quantum groups, and finite-field fusion categories—that satisfy the axioms across distinct arithmetic regimes.
