For $q \neq 0$, clearing denominators gives $\mathfrak{E}_\times(q) = (q^2 - 1)/(2q)$. When $q > 1$, both $q^2 - 1 > 0$ and $2q > 0$, so $\mathfrak{E}_\times(q) > 0$, and $(q - 1)^2 > 0$, giving $\Delta_q > 0$. Direct substitution at $q = 1$ gives $\Delta_1 = 0$, while for $q \in \mathbb{R}^\times$ the numerator $(q - 1)^3(q + 1)$ vanishes if and only if $q \in \{1, -1\} = \mu_2$. Finally, for $1 < q_1 \le q_2$, the map $q \mapsto q - q^{-1}$ and the map $q \mapsto (q - 1)^2$ are both positive and non-decreasing, so their product $\Delta_q$ satisfies $\Delta_{q_1} \le \Delta_{q_2}$. $\square$
