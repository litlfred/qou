The Archimedean Hartree computations of Part III require a positive-definite inner product on fibre states. Because a braided monoidal category over a general ground ring $R$ carries only the bilinear Frobenius pairing $\int_A$, an inner product must be constructed from the contact geometry of the Archimedean base.

This section builds that metric by extracting the complex structure from the Levi form of the CR substrate, pairing it with the contact one-form to form the Webster inner product, and transporting the resulting Hilbert-space pairing back across the fibre functor $\tau$. Readers concerned solely with algebraic identities over $\mathbb{Z}[q, q^{-1}]$ may treat this differential bridge as an external evaluation map that becomes active only at the Archimedean wall.
