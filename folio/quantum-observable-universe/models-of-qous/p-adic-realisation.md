To show that the substrate parameter and the surrounding categorical apparatus are not tied to an archimedean base field, the archimedean tuple is realised over a $p$-adic base. Let $k = \mathbb{Q}_p$ and let $\mathbf{C}$ be the category of $F$-isocrystals over $\mathbb{Q}_p$. This is a $\mathbb{Q}_p$-linear rigid monoidal category. The quantum group $\mathcal{O}_q(\mathrm{SU}(2))$ base-changed to $\mathbb{Q}_p$ provides a Frobenius–Hopf object $A$, where the Frobenius form $\int_A$ arises from the $p$-adic Haar measure. The Frobenius endomorphism $\varphi$ on $\mathbf{C}$ plays the role of the Levi-form contraction. The categorical substrate parameter specialises to $q = p^{-v_p(\det\varphi)} \in \mathrm{End}(\mathbf{1}) \cong \mathbb{Q}_p$. The fibre functor $\tau$ is the forgetful functor to $\mathrm{Vect}_{\mathbb{Q}_p}$. The observability condition $\mathrm{Hom}(\mathbf{1}, \mathrm{Ad}(A)) = 0$ amounts to the irreducibility of the associated $F$-crystal, generalising irreducible connection to the $p$-adic setting.

*Key structures.*

* The classical Frobenius integrability theorem constrains $q$-torsion leaves to be Legendrian. Over $\mathbb{Q}_p$, this is replaced by compatibility of the connection with the Frobenius lift. A $p$-adic instanton connection is integrable if and only if the associated $F$-crystal is unit-root.
* Crystal cohomology (de Rham–Witt complex) plays the role of de Rham cohomology.
* The base-field-independent quantum universe and quantum observable universe provide the categorical framework via Tannakian reconstruction.
