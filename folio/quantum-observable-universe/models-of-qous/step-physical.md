**Physical step.** The archimedean specialisation at the observed substrate parameter $q_0$ on Bring's surface predicts a particle spectrum from three calibration inputs ($m_e$, $m_\mu/m_e$, $\mathfrak{Q}_\beta$). Subsequent chapters develop the particle-generation mechanism (LP/SDP confinement, operator-valued folding/unfolding), derive Pauli exclusion and the fermion/boson split from the rigid-braided structure, catalogue these predictions (leptons, quarks, baryons, nuclei, $\mathfrak{Q}_\beta$, magnetic moments) against observation, and extend the framework to atomic, molecular, and organic chemistry.

The later chapters develop fluid dynamics of the brane, stochastic mechanics of the Markov trace, categorical information theory, quantum measurement as fibre-functor behaviour, and the $q$-geometric Langlands correspondence.
