**Physical step.** The archimedean specialisation at the substrate parameter $q_0$ on Bring's surface computes a particle spectrum from the three calibration inputs: the electron mass $m_e$ (fixing the mass scale), the weak-channel substrate modulus $q_0$ (pinned numerically on $m_{\mathfrak{p}}/m_e$, leaving $m_\mu/m_e$ as a model comparison), and the pion mass $m_\pi$ (fixing the orthogonal meson class). This step develops the particle-generation mechanism (LP/SDP confinement, operator-valued folding/unfolding). It derives Pauli exclusion and the $T(2,n)$ fermion/boson split from the rigid-braided structure. It catalogues these model evaluations (leptons, quarks, baryons, nuclei, $\mathfrak{Q}_\beta$, magnetic moments) and compares them against experimental observations. It extends the framework to atomic, molecular, and organic chemistry.

The later chapters develop fluid dynamics of the brane, stochastic mechanics of the Markov trace, categorical information theory, quantum measurement as fibre-functor behaviour, and the $q$-geometric Langlands correspondence.
