Let $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ be a quantum observable universe. The **spatial realization** of $A$ is the space

$$
  M = \mathrm{Spec}(A) = \{ p \colon \mathbf{1} \to A \mid
  p \text{ is a categorical point} \},
$$

equipped with the following structures in $\mathbf{C}$:

1. for each point $p$, the **augmentation ideal** $\mathfrak{m}_p = \ker(p^* \colon A \to \mathbf{1})$;
2. the **contact form** $\varTheta_A \in \Omega^1(A)$, induced by the Frobenius form $\int_A \colon A \to \mathbf{1}$ via pullback along the universal derivation $d \colon A \to \Omega^1(A)$;
3. the **Reeb derivation** $\xi$, the generator of the maximal torus action.
