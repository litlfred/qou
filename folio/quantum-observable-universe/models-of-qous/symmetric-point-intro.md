The mass-gap conjecture locates the vanishing of the gap on the $2$-torsion locus $\mu_2 = \{1, -1\} \subset \mathbb{G}_m$. This section examines the symmetric point $q = 1$ directly, determining which structures of the mass tower $\mathrm{End}_{\mathbf{C}}(A_n)$ survive the specialisation, which degenerate, and at what order in $h = q - 1$ each lost structure restores when the deformation switches back on. The pattern is uniform. The symmetric point deletes no spectrum, but loses resolution. Distinctions that braiding maintains—chirality of crossings, inter-nucleon channel identity, accidental level coincidences—collapse at $q = 1$ and reopen at definite infinitesimal orders.
