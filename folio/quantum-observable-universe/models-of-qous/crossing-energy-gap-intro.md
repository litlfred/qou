Away from the symmetric point $q = 1$, the elementary crossing weight $\mathfrak{E}_\times(q) = \tfrac{1}{2}(q - q^{-1})$ and the square $(q - 1)^2$ combine into a strictly positive rational functional on $(1, \infty)$ that vanishes on the $2$-torsion locus $\mu_2 = \{1, -1\}$. Paired with the variation of the $q$-weighted curvature--Levi functional with respect to the Levi form on a CR manifold, this records the algebraic positivity and geometric back-reaction properties of the model prior to the symmetric-point degeneration.
