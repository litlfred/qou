**Algebraic step.** Turning the categorical action into a computable knot invariant passes from braids to a trace-carrying quotient. The braid group $B_n$ acts on $A^{\otimes n}$. The quotient by the Hecke relation $(\sigma_i - q)(\sigma_i + q^{-1}) = 0$ is the Iwahori–Hecke algebra $H_n(q)$. This algebra controls the combinatorics of the categorical action. The Markov trace $\mathrm{tr}_M$ packages braid words into a class function. Evaluation on $\beta_K$ yields the Jones polynomial $J_K(q) = \mathrm{tr}_M(\rho(\beta_K))$. Subsequent chapters develop braids, knot groups, the Hecke algebra, and the Skein-relation translation.
