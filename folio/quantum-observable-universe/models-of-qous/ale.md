The links of ALE spaces provide a discrete, ADE-indexed family of archimedean quantum observable universes.

Let $\Gamma \subset \mathrm{SU}(2)$ be a finite subgroup. Let $X$ be the minimal resolution of $\mathbb{C}^2 / \Gamma$. The link $M = S^3 / \Gamma$ is a compact 3-dimensional strictly pseudoconvex CR manifold. Equip $M$ with the induced principal $\mathrm{SU}(2)$-bundle $P \to M$ and the state bundle $\mathcal{S} = P \times_{\mathrm{SU}(2)} \mathbb{P}^1(\mathbb{C})$. This specifies an archimedean quantum observable universe.

The McKay correspondence classifies the subgroups $\Gamma$ by ADE Dynkin diagrams. The exceptional divisors of $X \to \mathbb{C}^2/\Gamma$ are copies of $\mathbb{CP}^1$ intersecting in the corresponding Dynkin pattern. Because $S^3/\Gamma$ is connected, the substrate parameter $q$ is constant. The weight decomposition inherits the $\Gamma$-equivariant splitting of forms on $S^3$. The Reeb decomposition reduces to the Hopf fibration $S^3/\Gamma \to S^2$, with the Reeb vector field $\xi$ generating the $\mathrm{U}(1)$ fibre rotation.

This classification yields one quantum observable universe for each Dynkin type ($A_n$, $D_n$, $E_6$, $E_7$, $E_8$). The connection data derives from the instanton moduli space on $X$.
