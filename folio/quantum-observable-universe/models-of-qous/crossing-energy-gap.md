For real $q \neq 0$, define the minimum crossing weight $\mathfrak{E}_\times(q) = \tfrac{1}{2}(q - q^{-1})$ and the **crossing-energy gap functional**

$$
  \Delta_q := \mathfrak{E}_\times(q)\,(q - 1)^2 = \frac{(q^2 - 1)(q - 1)^2}{2q}.
$$

Then:

1. For every real $q > 1$, $\mathfrak{E}_\times(q) > 0$ and $\Delta_q > 0$.
2. At the symmetric point $q = 1$, $\Delta_1 = 0$, and across $\mathbb{R}^\times$ the factor $q^2 - 1$ vanishes precisely on the $2$-torsion subgroup $\mu_2 = \{1, -1\}$.
3. On $(1, \infty)$, $\Delta_q$ is monotonically increasing: $1 < q_1 \le q_2$ implies $\Delta_{q_1} \le \Delta_{q_2}$.
