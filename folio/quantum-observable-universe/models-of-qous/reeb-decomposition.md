In the archimedean realisation, the categorical weight decomposition specialises to the *Reeb decomposition*. The contact form $\varTheta$ canonically separates the $\mathrm{U}(1)$-fibre direction from the base, so the weight grading becomes a split of forms into vertical and horizontal parts. Let $\xi$ denote the *Reeb vector field*, characterised by $\iota_\xi d\varTheta = 0$ and $\varTheta(\xi) = 1$. Every differential $k$-form $\alpha$ ($k \geq 1$) on $M$ decomposes uniquely as

$$
  \alpha = \alpha_H + \varTheta \wedge \iota_\xi\alpha,
$$

where $\alpha_H := \alpha - \varTheta \wedge \iota_\xi\alpha$ is the *horizontal component* ($\iota_\xi\alpha_H = 0$) and $\alpha_V := \varTheta \wedge \iota_\xi\alpha$ is the *vertical component*. The Reeb vector $\xi$ generates the $\mathrm{U}(1)$-action (the maximal torus). The weight-$0$ subspace is the horizontal distribution $H = \ker\varTheta$.
