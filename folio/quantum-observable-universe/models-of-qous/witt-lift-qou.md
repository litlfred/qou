The [skew-Poisson integration theorem](#prop:skew-poisson-integration) is a statement over a field $k$ of characteristic $p$. Its bearing on the $p$-adic realisation is limited, and this remark records what it does and does not supply.

1. It supplies a construction over $k$: from a strongly multiplicative Poisson structure on a splay it builds a bialgebra $U$ with a PBW basis, which the source identifies with $\mathrm{Dist}(G)$ for a non-commutative formal group $G$ over $k$.
2. It does not supply a [Witt vector lift](#def:witt-vector-lift). No lift of $G$ or of $H$ to $W(k)$ or $\mathbb{Z}_p$ is constructed in the source, so the passage from the $\mathbb{F}_p$ fibre algebra to a $\mathbb{Q}_p$ fibre algebra is not a consequence of it.
3. Strong multiplicativity is a compatibility between $\pi$ and the comultiplication of the free product. It is not the Frobenius relation $(\mathrm{Id}\otimes m)\circ(\Delta\otimes\mathrm{Id}) = \Delta\circ m$ used in jet prolongation, and the theorem does not prove that relation.

No unit-root $F$-crystal criterion for integrability appears in the source.
