The Heisenberg uncertainty relation, $[\hat{X},\hat{P}]_q = i\hbar_q$, provides a single physical $q$-commutator between position and momentum. The enveloping-algebra construction generalizes this germ to reconstruct the full substrate from its infinitesimal $q$-commutation data. 

This lifting problem is solved using the Lusztig integral form of $\mathcal{U}_q(\mathfrak{su}(2))$, its triangular decomposition, and the $q$-deformed Poincaré–Birkhoff–Witt (PBW) basis. The integral form and triangular decomposition factor the generators into component subalgebras. The $q$-deformed PBW basis provides an explicit ordered basis for the enveloping algebra. Together, these algebraic structures lift the infinitesimal $q$-commutation relations back to the substrate.
