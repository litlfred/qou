**Archimedean step.** Specialisation at the observed substrate parameter $q_0$ produces archimedean (real-valued, dimensional) quantities and yields a dimensional mass. The machinery is a chain of extensions of the generic categorical content. Stated over a commutative ring $R$, it specialises through $R \to R[i] \to R[i][q, q^{-1}]$. The parameter $q$ is uniquely extracted as a categorical invariant (§\ref{thm:substrate-parameter-unique}); its value $q_0$ at the physical location and energy scale is observed, not derived from Bring topology.

The mass endomorphism $\mathfrak{m}(K) = \mathrm{Vol}(K) \cdot c_q^{-2}$ is the non-abelian Stokes integration of the Reeb flow against the categorical action. It is evaluated using Wedderburn decomposition, partition channels $y_\lambda \chi^\lambda$, crystal bases, and $\Delta_\lambda$ gating.

Alternatives to the archimedean specialisation include $p$-adic ($R \to \mathbb{Q}_p$) and characteristic-$p$ ($R \to \mathbb{F}_p[T]$) substrate completions. These are non-archimedean and do not produce dimensional masses.
