In the Kashiwara crystal limit, pair creation $\gamma\gamma \to e^+e^-$ is a categorical anyon-collapse transition: two photons $\gamma(\varphi), \gamma(\psi) \in H_3(R, q)$, each of the form $1 + \varphi_0\sigma_0 + \varphi_1\sigma_1 + \varphi_{01}\,\sigma_0\sigma_1^{-1}$, meet at a Type-B folded crystal fixed point whose involutive symmetry projects onto a conjugate particle–antiparticle pair: the electron word $\sigma_0\sigma_1\sigma_0 \in B_3$ (closure: the Hopf link) and its bar conjugate $\sigma_0^{-1}\sigma_1^{-1}\sigma_0^{-1}$.

This section formulates the symmetric-crystal pair-production mechanism and the two-photon Breit–Wheeler vertex, defines the anyonic Hecke substrate, proves modular tensor category (MTC) truncation at roots of unity and the Kazhdan–Lusztig classical limit $q \to 1$, and establishes the observation-induced collapse of anyonic braid superpositions onto definite charge eigenstates.
