In the Kashiwara crystal limit, pair creation $\gamma\gamma \to e^+e^-$ is a categorical anyon-collapse transition: two unknotted photonic excitations ($0_1$ modes on the exceptional divisor) meet at a Type-B folded crystal fixed point whose involutive symmetry projects onto a conjugate particle–antiparticle trefoil pair $(3_1, \overline{3_1})$.

This section formulates the symmetric-crystal pair-production mechanism and the two-photon Breit–Wheeler vertex, defines the anyonic Hecke substrate, proves modular tensor category (MTC) truncation at roots of unity and the Kazhdan–Lusztig classical limit $q \to 1$, and establishes the observation-induced collapse of anyonic braid superpositions onto definite charge eigenstates.
