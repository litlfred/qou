The light-nucleus normal forms of `sec:derived-atoms` exhibit a recurring staircase pattern of typed proton and neutron crossings. This section formalizes that pattern by defining the typed Garside element $\Delta_{Z,N}$ of the nuclear braid and expressing the reaction $\mathcal{Q}_q$-value as a Gram-inverse weighted Frobenius norm over conversion channels.

Under Convention D ($z_D = q^3/(q^2+1)$), the Frobenius Gram matrix $G$ on the full Hecke algebra $H_n(q)$ has a non-trivial radical equal to the kernel of the Temperley–Lieb projection ($y_{(1^3)} = 0$), so the inverse-Gram weighting $G^{-1}$ is evaluated on the non-degenerate quotient $H_n(q)/\mathrm{rad}\,G \cong \mathrm{TL}_n(q)$. Proving that the Gram eigenstructure coincides with the adjoint character on the surviving Wedderburn blocks anchors the $\mathcal{Q}_q$-value in the Jucys–Murphy spectral data and prepares the transfer-matrix factorisation of `sec:transfer-beta-decay`.
