**Probe: Minimum Time Bounds & Numerology**

The following heuristic arguments project crossing numbers directly onto time and make unverified claims regarding material penetration:

- "The $T_{3,5}$ mediator requires $10$ $R$-matrix applications — $10$ Planck steps of braid computation."
- "The ratio $E_{\mathrm{box}} / m_\nu \geq 10 \cdot (q + 1) / q \approx 19$ at the substrate parameter $q$: the box energy barrier is $\sim 19$ times the neutrino mass."
- "This is why neutrinos pass through matter virtually unimpeded: every interaction requires the full box machinery..."

These claims confuse the formal crossing number of the $T_{3,5}$ braid word with a chronological sequence of discrete Planck steps. They also fit a ratio ($\approx 19$) that lacks a canonical derivation in the categorical framework.
