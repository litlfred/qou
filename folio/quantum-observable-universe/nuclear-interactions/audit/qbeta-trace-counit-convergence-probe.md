# Probe: Non-perturbative nature of the convergence

The trace-counit equality is not a small-correction limit. At the substrate, the single Markov-trace term misses the target. The $N=0$ contribution $\lvert\mathrm{tr}_M(\Delta)\rvert\cdot\mathfrak{E}_0 \approx 0.345$ MeV falls short of $\mathfrak{Q}_\beta \approx 0.78$ MeV by $-56\%$. 

The Markov trace and the counit differ at leading order by $\varepsilon(\Delta) - \mathrm{tr}_M(\Delta) = h\,(z-1)$. The substrate loop $z = 1/(\sqrt q + 1/\sqrt q)$ does not render this difference small. The counit form matches to $+0.17\%$ at $N=0$. This is the same residual that separates the derived energy scale $\mathfrak{E}_0 \approx 4.186$ MeV from its calibration cross-check $\approx 4.155$ MeV (see [energy scale](#rem:energy-scale-planck)).

A perturbative expansion of the trace tower about its $N=0$ term fails to establish the conjecture because the corrections exceed the leading term. Establishing the relation therefore requires a closed evaluation of the tower-summed difference.
