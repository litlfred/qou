*In the inductive construction of $\mathcal{N}(Z, N)$, the inter-nucleon binding operator $\Phi_k$ selected at step $k$ for the nuclei recorded in this chapter belongs to one of three regimes:*

*(Regime I — Shell filling.) At the first step of the first nuclear shell, the selected operator is the five-crossing three-strand braid*

$$
  \Phi_k = (\sigma_0\sigma_1)^2\sigma_0
  \qquad (k = 1,\; p \to pp),
$$

*whose closure is the $T(2,4)$ torus link.*

*(Regime II — Magic shell closure.) At and immediately after a magic number, the selected operator is $L_\pm$ (closure in $B_3$: the unknot):*

$$
  \Phi_k \in \{L_+ = \sigma_0\sigma_1,\; L_- = \sigma_1\sigma_0\}
$$

*The $L_\pm$ operators, whose closures are the unknot, achieve demand closure at magic shell boundaries.*

*(Regime III — Open shell.) Beyond a closed magic shell, the selected operator reverts to the bare typed crossing:*

$$
  \Phi_k \in \{C_{pp},\; C_{pn},\; C_{nn}\}
$$

The operator transitions from $(\sigma_0\sigma_1)^2\sigma_0$ to $L_+$ at the first magic number, then to $L_-$ at the shell boundary, and finally to $C_{pn}$ in the open shell. The $\mathcal{B}_3$ Borromean operator is not selected at any recorded nuclear step; it appears only as the quark-level confinement operator.
