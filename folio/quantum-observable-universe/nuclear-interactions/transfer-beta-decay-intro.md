Equipped with the Gram-weighted $\mathcal{Q}_q$-value on the Temperley–Lieb quotient, $\beta$-decay transitions admit a compact operator representation. Because the nuclear skein polynomial factors as a product of $2 \times 2$ transfer matrices along the braid word, converting a neutron strand into a proton strand corresponds to inserting a single transition matrix into the universal transfer-matrix product.

This section proves that the transfer-matrix eigenvalues determine the ground-state energy ordering of isobaric configurations, derives symbolic closed forms for the free-neutron decay $Q$-value and the normal-form counit transition, and proves exponential convergence of the trace-counit expansion toward the pre-archimedean difference $\Delta_{\mathrm{pre}}$. It then establishes the basis-change equivalence between the counit formulation (Form A) and the transfer-matrix log-ratio formulation (Form B) of the $\mathfrak{Q}_\beta$ spectral bridge.
