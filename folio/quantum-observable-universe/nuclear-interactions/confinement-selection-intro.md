Gröbner–Shirshov reduction produces a canonical normal form for every braid word in $H_A(q)$, yet not every normal form represents a physically confined bound state. This section supplies the dynamical selection principle: a composite configuration is physically realized if and only if its weight vector minimizes the categorical action subject to Frobenius positivity over the Markov Gram matrix.

In the scalar relaxation, confinement operator selection is a finite-dimensional linear program over the Hecke normal-form basis. Strong LP duality assigns a non-negative shadow price to each irreducible Wedderburn block, and Frobenius reciprocity expresses every downstream binding observable as a Gram-inverse dual pairing against the active support. Lifting the scalar positivity constraints to matrix positivity yields a semidefinite program (SDP) whose feasibility is equivalent to a Hecke spectral-gap condition, while integer-rank level constraints define a mixed-integer SDP whose steepest-descent thimbles govern multi-operator binding and the $\mathfrak{Q}_\beta$ cosine resummation.
