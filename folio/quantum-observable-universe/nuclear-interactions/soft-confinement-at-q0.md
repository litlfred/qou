**1. Soft confinement at the aether parameter.**
The Wedderburn block labels of $H_n(q)$ are $q$-independent, but the Plancherel weights depend sensitively on $q$. At roots of unity, sharp truncation occurs. At the generic real aether parameter $q_0 \approx 1.11$, no representation is strictly confined, but weights decay rapidly, producing a soft confinement level $\ell_*(q_0) \approx 9.6$. Partitions exceeding $\ell_*$ contribute negligibly.

**2. Implication for atomic braids.**
An atomic braid lives in $H_{3A}(q_0)$. Soft confinement implies no truncation for light atoms, exponential suppression for medium atoms, and effective truncation for heavy atoms. This transition dictates why $\alpha$-cluster unfolding is numerically necessary beyond $A \sim 4$.
