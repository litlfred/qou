**Remark (comparison with measurement).** This remark records the [cos-resummation](#prop:q-beta-cos-resummation) evaluated under the proton calibration and compared with the CODATA value.

**Comparison with measurement under the proton calibration $q_0 \approx 1.1099785827513808$.** At $q_0$ one has $z_D \approx 0.6126880371$, $h \approx 0.2090602988$, $\alpha \approx -0.0404857773$ and $\cos\alpha \approx 0.9991805629$. Two bases are recorded side by side; neither is selected by its residual.

1. **Canonical Convention-D base** ($\varepsilon$ evaluated as the Markov trace at $z_D = q^3/(q^2+1)$), $\mathfrak{Q}_\beta^{z_D} = \bigl|h^2/2 - h\,z_D\bigr|\,E_0$ with $E_0 = m_e/(q(q-1))$: $\mathfrak{Q}_\beta^{z_D} \approx 0.4447004085$ MeV, residual $\approx -431571.8$ ppm against CODATA $0.78233341$ MeV. The cos factor gives $\mathfrak{Q}_\beta^{z_D,\cos} \approx 0.4443360045$ MeV, residual $\approx -432037.5$ ppm. **The refinement moves this residual away from zero.** This is forced, not tuned: the base lies below CODATA, so the required factor exceeds $1$, while $\cos\alpha \le 1$ for every real $\alpha$.
2. **Form A** (retired as the base; historical cross-check only), $\mathfrak{Q}_\beta^A = m_e\,|q^2-2q-1|(q+1)/(2q^3) \approx 0.7836458274$ MeV, residual $\approx +1677.6$ ppm. With the convention-D phase, $\mathfrak{Q}_\beta^{A,\cos} \approx 0.7830036789$ MeV, residual $\approx +856.8$ ppm, a $1.96\times$ tightening.
