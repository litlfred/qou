This remark records the [cos-resummation](#prop:q-beta-cos-resummation) evaluated at the proton-anchored $q_0$ and compared with the CODATA value.

**Comparison with measurement at $q_0 \approx 1.1099785827513808$.** At $q_0$ one has $z = q_0^3/(q_0^2+1) \approx 0.6126880371$, $h \approx 0.2090602988$, $\alpha = (z-1)h/2 \approx -0.0404857773$, and $\cos\alpha \approx 0.9991805629$. Two bases are recorded side by side; neither is selected by its residual.

1. **Markov-trace $\Delta$ base** ($\varepsilon$ evaluated as $\mathrm{tr}_M$ at $z = q^3/(q^2+1)$), $\mathfrak{Q}_\beta^{\Delta} = \bigl|h^2/2 - h\,z\bigr|\,E_0$ with $E_0 = m_e/(q(q-1))$: $\mathfrak{Q}_\beta^{\Delta} \approx 0.4447004085$ MeV, residual $\approx -431571.9$ ppm against CODATA 2022 $0.78233355931$ MeV. Dressing by $\cos\alpha$ gives $\mathfrak{Q}_\beta^{\Delta,\cos} \approx 0.4443360045$ MeV, residual $\approx -432037.7$ ppm. **The cosine factor moves this residual away from zero:** the base lies below CODATA, so closing the gap requires a factor exceeding $1$, whereas $\cos\alpha \le 1$ for every real $\alpha$.
2. **Coordinate-sum Form A** ($z = 1$ comparison), $\mathfrak{Q}_\beta^A = m_e\,|q^2-2q-1|(q+1)/(2q^3) \approx 0.7836458274$ MeV, residual $\approx +1677.4$ ppm. Dressed by $\cos\alpha$ at $z = q^3/(q^2+1)$, $\mathfrak{Q}_\beta^{A,\cos} \approx 0.7830036789$ MeV, residual $\approx +856.6$ ppm ($1.96\times$ smaller).

The scored value applies the $T^3$ weight split of the $z = q^3/(q^2+1)$ trace to the $\gamma\gamma$ base $V^3 = (\sigma_0\sigma_1^{-1})^3$, giving $\mathfrak{Q}_\beta \approx 0.3254$ MeV ($-58.40\%$) ([Symbolic $Q$-value of free neutron decay](neutron-qvalue-symbolic.md)); the $\Delta$ evaluation is retained as an unscored comparison.
