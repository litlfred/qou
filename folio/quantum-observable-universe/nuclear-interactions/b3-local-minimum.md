The Borromean braid $\mathcal{B}_3 = (\sigma_0 \sigma_1^{-1})^3$ is a local, rather than global, minimum of the categorical action.

## Global minimum

The confinement LP with the corrected Gram matrix admits a global minimum at $\mathrm{tr}_M = 0$. The Borromean solution $\mathcal{B}_3$ is a local minimum at a vertex of the LP feasible polytope.

## Corrected Gram matrix

The $6 \times 6$ Gram matrix $G_{ij} = \mathrm{tr}_M(b_i \cdot b_j)$ has a negative eigenvalue $\approx -0.956$. This figure is historical: the bilinear Gram of the [Frobenius Gram matrix](#prop:frobenius-gram-matrix) has $\lambda_{\min} \approx -0.855$ under the excised convention C and $\approx -0.7527$ under the canonical weight $z = q^3/(q^2+1)$, so $-0.956$ matches neither, and its producer has not been identified. The Frobenius form is not positive definite.

## Eigenvalue selection

The confinement LP selects $\mathcal{B}_3$ via the $\min|\lambda|$ eigenvalue criterion. This identifies it as the least unstable confined state.
