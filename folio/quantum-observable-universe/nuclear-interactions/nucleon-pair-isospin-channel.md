**Definition.** Let $T_1 \otimes T_1 = A \oplus T_1 \oplus H$ be the isotypic decomposition of an ordered pair of colour sheets, with $\mathrm{Sym}^2 = A \oplus H$ and $\mathrm{Alt}^2 = T_1$. A **nucleon pair** is one of the four channels

$$
  p \;\in\; \{\, pp,\; nn,\; pn_{\mathrm{sym}},\; pn_{\mathrm{alt}} \,\},
$$

carrying total isospin $T(pp) = T(nn) = T(pn_{\mathrm{sym}}) = 1$ and
$T(pn_{\mathrm{alt}}) = 0$. The **colour system** of a pair is the summand of
$T_1 \otimes T_1$ its interface draws on, one of
$\mathrm{altT_1} = T_1$ or $\mathrm{symAH} = A \oplus H$. The **selection map** is

$$
  \mathrm{select}(p) \;=\;
  \begin{cases}
    \mathrm{altT_1}, & T(p) = 1, \\[2pt]
    \mathrm{symAH},  & T(p) = 0 .
  \end{cases}
$$
