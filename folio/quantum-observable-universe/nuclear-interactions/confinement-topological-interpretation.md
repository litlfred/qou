The inequality $L_+^2 + L_-^2 \neq 2\,L_+ L_-$ records noncommutativity of the length-two generators $L_+ = \sigma_0\sigma_1$ and $L_- = \sigma_1\sigma_0$ inside the two-dimensional standard module of $H_3(q)$, where $L_+ L_- \neq L_- L_+$. Under the symmetric Markov-trace bilinear form $G_{ij} = \mathrm{tr}_M(b_i b_j)$ on the six-dimensional normal-form basis of $H_3(q)$ at loop weight $z = q^3/(q^2+1)$, the difference $v_- = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(e_{L_+} - e_{L_-})$ spans the unique negative-eigenvalue direction ($\lambda_-(q) = -\lambda_+(q) < 0$ for $q > 1$), while the three-row antisymmetric sector $(1^3)$ spans the one-dimensional trace radical ($\lambda_0(q) = 0$).

This bilinear indefiniteness does not by itself force optimal value $0$ or select a unique braid in the linear program of [prop:confinement-lp](#prop:confinement-lp). Because the basis begins with the unit element $\mathbf{1}$, the trace vector $t_j = \mathrm{tr}_M(b_j)$ coincides with row $0$ of $G$, so $t \cdot x = (Gx)_0 \geq 0$ holds throughout the cone $Gx \geq 0$; moreover $t \cdot v_- = (Gv_-)_0 = 0$ and $Gv_-$ carries opposite-sign entries on $L_+$ and $L_-$. Operator selection instead descends to the Temperley--Lieb quotient $H_3(q)/\mathrm{rad}\,\tau_q$ equipped with non-circular channel constraints.
