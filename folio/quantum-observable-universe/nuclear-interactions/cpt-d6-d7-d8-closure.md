**Conjecture (CPT closure).** 
*The three discrete symmetries close consistently: there exist actions $C$, $P$, $T$ on closed-braid representatives of $H_n(q)$ such that*

1. *$C$ is the color-sheet flavor-swap $u \leftrightarrow \bar u$, $d \leftrightarrow \bar d$;*
2. *$P$ is the braid-diagram mirror reflection, or writhe sign flip;*
3. *$T$ is the Markov-trace word reversal, or trace-cyclicity dual.*

*The composite $C \cdot P \cdot T$ preserves the Markov trace $\mathrm{tr}_M \colon H_n(q) \to R$. That is, $\mathrm{tr}_M((C \cdot P \cdot T)\beta) = \mathrm{tr}_M(\beta)$ for every closed-braid representative $\beta$.*

The joint statement is that they compose to the CPT theorem at the Markov-trace level. The three components are not logically independent; they collectively constitute the conjecture. Only the components are conjectural: if each of $C$, $P$ and $T$ preserves $\mathrm{tr}_M$, so does their composite, so the joint statement reduces to the three component conjectures. Each component asserts that the named realisation of its symmetry is trace-preserving, and none is proved.

The conjecture is formulated parametrically over the closed-braid space $B$, the trace $\mathrm{tr}$, and the composite action $\mathrm{cpt} = C \cdot P \cdot T$, so downstream results that depend on CPT closure carry this invariance on $(R, B, \mathrm{tr}, \mathrm{cpt})$ as an explicit hypothesis, where $R$ is the coefficient ring of the trace—canonically $\mathbb{Z}[q, q^{-1}]$, with an archimedean evaluation $R := \mathbb{R}$ arising only on the observation side of the substrate–archimedean wall.
