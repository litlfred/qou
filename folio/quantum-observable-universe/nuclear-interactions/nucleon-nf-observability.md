The Gröbner--Shirshov normal form of each nucleon in $H_3(q)$ has exactly six terms. With typed crossings, the sign pattern differs between neutron and proton. Classify each term by word length $\ell$ and observability.

## Sign patterns

With typed crossings, the sign patterns are:

- **Neutron**: three terms negative ($\gamma, L_+, e^-$).
- **Proton**: two terms negative ($\gamma, e^-$).

The neutron's $L_+$ sign flip represents the key effect of typed crossings: the $d$-$d$ crossing shifts the $\sigma_0\sigma_1$ coefficient below zero.

## Observability criterion

A braid word of length $\ell$ is **observable** when the corresponding knot or link is topologically non-trivial:

- **$\ell = 0$**: The identity $\mathbf{1}$ is topologically trivial.
- **$\ell = 1$**: A single crossing $\sigma_i$ is virtual (topologically removable).
- **$\ell = 2$**: The products $L_+ = \sigma_0\sigma_1$ and $L_- = \sigma_1\sigma_0$ close in $B_3$ to a single component, the unknot. They are topologically trivial and not observable.
- **$\ell = 3$**: The product $\sigma_0\sigma_1\sigma_0$ is the electron word; its closure is the Hopf link. It is observable.

## Positive coefficients: observable vs virtual

Both nucleons are **dominated by virtual content**: the $\sigma_1$ term carries $\sim 94\%$ of the total positive amplitude. It cannot be observed as a free particle. The $\ell = 2$ terms $L_+$ and $L_-$ close to the unknot and are also trivial. The only observable term is the electron word $e^-$, and it is negative in both nucleons. No positive coefficient of either nucleon is observable.

## Location of $\sigma_1$: the color tube fiber

The generator $\sigma_1$ acts on the crossing within the color tube fiber between the red and green color coordinates. The dominance of $\sigma_1$ implies: the nucleon's internal energy resides overwhelmingly in the relative motion of color charges within the tube, not in the coupling between color and spacetime. This provides the algebraic expression of **color confinement**.

## Sign pattern and beta decay

The negative terms in each nucleon represent **leptonic deficits** — topological content that must be balanced by the vacuum state. The neutron carries three deficit terms while the proton carries only two. This imbalance is exactly resolved via beta decay $\mathfrak{n} \to \mathfrak{p} + e^- + \bar{\nu}_e$. The neutron transition emits the physical electron and antineutrino to conserve total topological crossing number.

The nucleon normal form therefore splits into a trivial positive bulk ($\sigma_0$, $\sigma_1$, $L_\pm$) and the leptonic deficits, which include its only observable term.
