**The box braid: crossing-by-crossing.**

The process $\gamma\gamma \to \nu\nu^{\sigma}$ has no tree-level realisation. The minimal topology is a box with four vertices and four internal propagators at brane tower level $A_1$.

**Vertex structure.**
- **EM vertices** ($\gamma \leftrightarrow e^-e^+$): The photon's $\dagger$-involution couples to the antisymmetric crossing energy of the electron word $\sigma_0\sigma_1\sigma_0$ (closure: the Hopf link). This creates virtual pairs.
- **Weak vertices** ($e \to \nu$ via $T_{3,5}$): The skein relation converts the charged electron word to the neutral unknot.

**Corollary: suppression of neutrino decay.**
Within this model, every neutrino decay channel is suppressed:
1. **No beta decay.** The neutrino is an elementary knot with no internal constituents.
2. **Suppressed scattering decay.** Any decay channel passes through the $T_{3,5}$ topology. The topological obstruction barrier scales with the invariant crossing number and suppresses the channel.
3. **No simpler process in the model.** $T_{3,5}$ is the only cross-family mediator in this model.

The model therefore predicts strong suppression of neutrino decay. It does not prove that the neutrino cannot decay.
