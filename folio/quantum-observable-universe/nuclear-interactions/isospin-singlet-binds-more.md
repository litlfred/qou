*Let $z = q^{3}/(q^{2}+1)$ be the Markov parameter of convention D and let*

$$
  \mu_+ \;=\; \frac{z + q^{-1}}{q + q^{-1}},
  \qquad
  \mu_- \;=\; \frac{q - z}{q + q^{-1}}
$$

*be the interface scalars of the isospin triplet and singlet. Then*

$$
  \boxed{\;\; \mu_- \;<\; \mu_+ \qquad \text{for every } q > 0. \;\;}
$$

## The ordering is forced, not fitted

The difference of the two scalars is

$$
  \mu_+ - \mu_- \;=\; \frac{2z - h}{q + q^{-1}},
  \qquad h = q - q^{-1},
$$

and under convention D the numerator is an identity with a manifestly positive value:

$$
  2z - h \;=\; \frac{q^{4}+1}{q\,(q^{2}+1)} .
$$

Every factor on the right is a sum of even powers of $q$ divided by a product of
positive terms, so the sign requires no numerical input and no estimate. The
denominator $q + q^{-1}$ is positive for $q > 0$ by the same reading. The ordering
therefore holds for the whole positive axis, not on a window around the substrate
parameter.
