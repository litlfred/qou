*Let $q$ be the aether parameter, $h = q - q^{-1}$ the Hecke skein
parameter, and $\mathfrak{E}_\times(q) = \tfrac{1}{2}(q - q^{-1}) = h/2$ the
per-crossing energy. Then:*

$$
\boxed{\;
\Delta(\mathrm{net})
\;=\;
\frac{h(h-2)}{2}
\;=\;
\frac{(q^2-1)(q^2 - 2q - 1)}{2q^2}
\;=\;
2\,\mathfrak{E}_\times\!\left(\mathfrak{E}_\times - 1\right)
\;}
$$

*vanishes if and only if $h \in \{0, 2\}$, equivalently if and only if*

$$
\mathfrak{E}_\times \in \{0, 1\},
$$

*that is, if and only if each crossing carries either **zero** or **unit**
energy. The two roots are $q = 1$ (undeformed aether, $\mathfrak{E}_\times =
0$) and $q = 1 + \sqrt{2}$, the **silver mean** (unit crossing energy); the
conjugate root $q = 1 - \sqrt{2}$ is negative and outside the aether range.*

*The crossing-energy form is equivalent to the $h$-form by
$\mathfrak{E}_\times = h/2$. Its roots $\{0, 1\}$ are the fixed points of
$x \mapsto x^2$.*

*At $q = 1 + \sqrt{2}$:*

1. *The Hecke quadratic $\sigma^2 = h\sigma + 1$ becomes $\sigma^2 = 2\sigma + 1$. The generator $\sigma$ has eigenvalues equal to the silver-ratio conjugates themselves.*
2. *Proton and neutron single-wrap Markov traces become equal: $\mathrm{net}(p) = \mathrm{net}(n) = 1 - h/2 = 1 - \mathfrak{E}_\times = 0$.*

*Behaviour of the mass formula at the two roots. The volume term carries
$\hbar_q = 1 - q^{-1}$ in the denominator as $\mathrm{Vol}/\hbar_q^{2}$. At
$\mathfrak{E}_\times = 0$, i.e. $q = 1$, that denominator is zero. At
$\mathfrak{E}_\times = 1$ it equals $(1 - (1+\sqrt2)^{-1})^2 = 0.343\ldots$,
and item 2 above states that the trace does not separate proton from neutron
there.*

*Relation to the volume conjecture. The volume-conjecture limit is taken at
roots of unity $q = e^{2\pi i/N}$, for which $|q| = 1$, whereas
$\mathfrak{E}_\times = 1$ gives $|q| = 1 + \sqrt{2}$. The limit in which the
volume term equals the hyperbolic volume is $q \to \infty$, where
$\hbar_q \to 1$ and $\mathrm{Vol}/\hbar_q^{2} \to \mathrm{Vol}$.*
