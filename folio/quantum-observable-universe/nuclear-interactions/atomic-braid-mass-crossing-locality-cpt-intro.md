On the composite atomic braid $H_{A+Z}(q)$, the crossing-graded mass expansion is constrained by local holonomy and discrete spacetime symmetries. This section derives $q$-holonomy locality from the graded connection on the Reeb bundle, proves that the CPT orbit closure is dense in the unitary representation fibre, and establishes the closure of the discrete symmetries $C, P, T$—deriving time-reversal invariance $T$ from trace cyclicity $\mathrm{tr}_M(ab) = \mathrm{tr}_M(ba)$, reading parity $P$ as the mirror reflection of the braid diagram, and obtaining parity/charge-conjugation from Type-B crystal folding. Proving invariance of the atomic mass functional under the Lusztig bar involution $q \mapsto q^{-1}$ secures real-valuedness of the binding energy and characterizes the soft-confinement regime at $q_0$.
