The word-length functional $E(\xi) = \sum_{w \in B} |c_w(q)|\,\ell(w)$ is an algebraic rewriting norm on normal forms, not the nuclear Skein binding energy or the [categorical Hamiltonian](#def:categorical-hamiltonian). Three distinct quantities act on the Gröbner--Shirshov normal form $\xi_{\mathrm{NF}} = \sum_{w \in B} c_w(q)\,w$ of a nuclear braid in $H_A(q)$:

1. **Normal-form evaluation engine for the traced Hamiltonian.** Physical rest and binding energies are evaluated through the Markov trace $\mathfrak{E}_0 \sum_{w \in B} c_w(q)\,\mathrm{tr}_M(w)$ at the Jones--Markov parameter $z = q^3/(q^2 + 1)$, where $\mathfrak{E}_0 = \mathfrak{m}_e/\bigl(q(q - 1)\bigr)$ is the per-tick hadronic chart of the [categorical Hamiltonian](#def:categorical-hamiltonian). Because $\mathrm{tr}_M(u\,\sigma_i^2\,v) = h\,\mathrm{tr}_M(u\,\sigma_i\,v) + \mathrm{tr}_M(u\,v)$ holds identically in $H_A(q)$, Gröbner--Shirshov reduction leaves $\mathrm{tr}_M(\xi)$ strictly invariant at every step while reducing arbitrary braid words to the finite basis $B$ on which $\mathrm{tr}_M(w)$ is evaluated.
2. **Flat $z \to 1$ coefficient-sum shadow.** Setting every basis weight to unity gives the linear sum $\mathrm{net}(\xi_{\mathrm{NF}}) = \sum_{w \in B} c_w(q)$. This recovers the $z \to 1$ shadow on words whose reduced basis terms close with no residual Hecke square, but drops strand-closure factors of $z$: on $H_3(q)$, cyclic invariance equates $\mathrm{tr}_M(\sigma_1^2\sigma_2) = \mathrm{tr}_M(\sigma_1\sigma_2\sigma_1) = z\,(1 + h\,z)$, whereas $\mathrm{net}(\sigma_1^2\sigma_2) = 1 + h$ and $\mathrm{net}(\sigma_1\sigma_2\sigma_1) = 1$.
3. **Archimedean $\ell^1$ word-length rewriting norm.** Over $\mathbb{R}$, $E(\xi_{\mathrm{NF}}) = \sum_{w \in B} |c_w(q)|\,\ell(w)$ weights each basis element by its Coxeter length $\ell(w)$ and the archimedean absolute value $|c_w(q)|$. Unlike $\mathrm{tr}_M$, $E$ changes along Hecke steps by $|c|\,\bigl(|h|\,(\ell - 1) - 2\bigr)$ on isolated monomials and serves as a measure of normal-form complexity rather than binding energy.
