To verify how the three operator regimes of [Nuclear Binding Regimes and Magic Numbers](#sec:nuclear-binding) operate on concrete braid words, this section computes the explicit Gröbner–Shirshov normal forms and Markov-trace binding energies for five benchmark light nuclei:

- **Hydrogen ($^1\mathrm{H}$):** The single-nucleon base case with zero inter-nucleon crossings.
- **Deuterium ($^2\mathrm{H}$):** The minimal two-nucleon bound braid in the $T = 0$ isospin-singlet channel, whose Markov-trace volume integral yields a zero-parameter binding prediction against AME 2020.
- **Helium-4 ($^4\mathrm{He}$):** The first doubly magic closure ($Z = N = 2$), where the two-row Wedderburn stratum saturates.
- **Lithium-6 ($^6\mathrm{Li}$) and Oxygen-16 ($^{16}\mathrm{O}$):** The post-magic transition at $A = 6$ and the second doubly magic closure at $Z = N = 8$, providing the normal-form data consumed by the typed Garside staircase construction in [Typed Garside Element and Gram-Weighted $\mathcal{Q}_q$-Value](#sec:garside-gram).
