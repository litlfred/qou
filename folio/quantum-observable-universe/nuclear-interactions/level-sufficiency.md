*Let $P = (\mathrm{name}, \ell, [K]_M, n_0)$ be a confined particle and let*

$$
  \uparrow_\ell \colon P \longrightarrow P^{\uparrow}
  \quad\text{with}\quad
  P^{\uparrow} = (\mathrm{name}, \ell - 1, [\iota(K)]_M, n_1)
$$

*be an unfolding morphism in the confinement category, where $\iota: H_{n_0}(R, q) \hookrightarrow H_{n_1}(R, q)$ is the Hecke inclusion and $n_1 \ge n_0$. If the coarse witnessed partition truncation satisfies*

$$
  k_{\max}(P, q) \le n_0
$$

*then the Markov trace is preserved along the unfolding:*

$$
  \mathrm{tr}_M\!\left(T_w^{\downarrow}\right)
  \;=\;
  \mathrm{tr}_M\!\left(T_w^{\uparrow}\right)
  \quad\text{in }\mathbb{C}(q),
$$

*where $T_w^{\downarrow} \in H_{n_0}(q)$ and $T_w^{\uparrow} \in H_{n_1}(q)$ are the Gröbner–Shirshov normal forms of $K$ at coarse and fine levels respectively. Consequently, every trace-class invariant $\mathcal{F}$ of $P$ takes the same value on $P$ and $P^{\uparrow}$:*

$$
  \mathcal{F}(P) \;=\; \mathcal{F}(P^{\uparrow}).
$$

*The coarsest level at which $k_{\max}(P, q, n) \le n$ is computationally sufficient. Unfolding to any deeper level is superfluous for trace-class invariants.*
