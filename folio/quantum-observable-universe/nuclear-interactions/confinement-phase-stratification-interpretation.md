*For simple particles, $\Omega(P)$ is expected to be a single connected interval of $\mathbb{R}_{>1}$ containing $q_0$; neither connectedness nor $q_0 \in \Omega(P)$ is proved. For composite particles with multi-block binding, $\Omega(P)$ can be disconnected. Each component corresponds to a distinct configuration of the active support. One physically realises transitions between components as chemical or nuclear rearrangements. Computationally, they appear as regime switches in the witness files.*
