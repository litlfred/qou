**Remark (concrete example: $\gamma\gamma \to e^- e^+$ in the symmetric crystal).**
*The photon is $\gamma(\varphi_0,\varphi_1,\varphi_{01}) = 1 + \varphi_0\sigma_0 + \varphi_1\sigma_1 + \varphi_{01}\,\sigma_0\sigma_1^{-1} \in H_3(R, q)$. This remark treats only its polarisation-free term: at $\varphi = 0$ the photon is the identity $T_e = 1$, which carries no crossings and is distinct from the unknot $0_1$ representing the neutrino. The two-photon state at $\varphi = \psi = 0$ is $\gamma(0)\gamma(0) = T_e \in H_3(R, q)$ on $n = 3$ strands. It has zero crossings, zero graded Hecke energy, and strict bar-invariance. Applying $T_e \mapsto \overline{T_e}$ swaps over- and under-crossings, of which there are none, returning $T_e$ unchanged. The state sits at the highest-weight node of the trivial component of $\mathrm{Cryst}^{\mathrm{sym}}(\mathfrak{so}_{2n+1})$.*

*This state is placed at the high-energy boundary of a heavy nucleus. This fixed external boundary condition supplies energy to the crystal traversal. The intense local energy density forces the system to step down the crystal graph to absorb energy. For this polarisation-free state, symmetric-crystal pair production admits no unimodal lowering step $\gamma\gamma \xrightarrow{\tilde f_i} v$ landing on a single chiral descendant. The admissible step is the paired operator:*

$$
  \gamma\gamma
  \;\xrightarrow{\;(\tilde f_i,\tilde f_{\bar i})\;}\;
  e^-\,\sqcup\,e^+.
$$

*One operator produces the electron word $e^- = \sigma_0\sigma_1\sigma_0 \in B_3$, whose closure is the Hopf link. The bar-conjugate operator simultaneously produces the positron word $e^+ = \sigma_0^{-1}\sigma_1^{-1}\sigma_0^{-1}$. (For general $\varphi, \psi$ the coefficient of $\sigma_0\sigma_1\sigma_0$ in $\mathrm{NF}(\gamma(\varphi)\gamma(\psi))$ is $\varphi_{01}\psi_0 + \varphi_1\psi_{01}$; this remark uses only $\varphi = \psi = 0$.) The bar-conjugate descendant pair conserves the bar-invariance of the source state. Charge ($-e + e = 0$) and chirality ($-\tfrac12 + \tfrac12 = 0$) are conserved structurally.*

**Falsification handle.**
*For the polarisation-free single-photon state, the symmetric-crystal pair production conjecture forbids the channel $\gamma(0) \to e^- e^+$ without invoking energy–momentum conservation as a separate axiom. At $\varphi = 0$ the bar-invariant state $\gamma(0) = 1$ carries no crossings to fold into a paired operator, so the symmetric crystal has no admissible step. This remark makes no claim about $\gamma(\varphi)$ for $\varphi \neq 0$. The empirical absence of $\gamma \to e^- e^+$ in vacuum is consistent with the conjecture on this term. Observation of the channel from a polarisation-free photon without a recoiling external boundary falsifies it.*
