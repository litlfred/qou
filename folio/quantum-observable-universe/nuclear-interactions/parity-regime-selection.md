The parity of the mass number $A$ fixes the sign of the spectator factor in the [branching Q-value factorization](#prop:branching-qvalue-factorization).

## Statement

**Theorem.**
*Let $a, b$ be elements of a strictly ordered field with $a < 0$ and $b < 0$, and let $Z, N$ be natural numbers with $N \geq 1$ and $A = Z + N$. Then*

$$
  (-1)^{A-1}\, a^Z\, b^{N-1} > 0,
$$

*that is, $\mathrm{sign}(a^Z \cdot b^{N-1}) = (-1)^{A-1}$.*

For the per-nucleon channel sums both hypotheses $a < 0$ and $b < 0$ hold for all $q > 1$ ([spectator normalization](#def:spectator-normalization)). How the sign is used to choose a Q-value formula is recorded in the [parity regime dispatch](#rem:parity-regime-dispatch).
