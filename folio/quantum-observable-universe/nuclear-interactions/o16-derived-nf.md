**Example.**
Oxygen-16 ($Z = 8$, $N = 8$, $A = 16$) is the first major-shell doubly-magic nucleus beyond ${}^{4}\mathrm{He}$, following the intermediate $1p_{3/2}$ spin–orbit subshell closure at ${}^{12}\mathrm{C}$ ($Z = N = 6$). Both $Z = 8$ and $N = 8$ are nuclear magic numbers, corresponding to the full completion of the $N = 1$ ($1p_{3/2} + 1p_{1/2}$) shell above the $1s_{1/2}$ core:

$$
  (1s_{1/2})^2\,(1p_{3/2})^4\,(1p_{1/2})^2
  \;=\; 2 + 4 + 2 \;=\; 8
  \qquad (\text{protons and neutrons independently}).
$$

## Construction

The derived atom $\mathcal{A}(8, 8)$ is constructed in four steps:

1. **48 pair productions** yield 16 quark triples.
2. **16 Borromean confinements** yield 8 proton normal forms and 8 neutron normal forms.
3. **3D $\alpha$-simplex and inductive shell assembly:** on the stacked 3-simplex coordination complex $\mathcal{K}_{16}$ ($4\alpha$ tetrahedral hierarchy), the $A = 16$ core carries $f_1(16) = 3A - 6 = 42$ local two-body bonds, $f_2(16) = 3A - 8 = 40$ triangular faces, and $f_3(16) = A - 3 = 13$ tetrahedral cells (or $9N_\alpha - 6 = 30$ bonds, $4N_\alpha = 16$ triangles, and $N_\alpha = 4$ disjoint $\alpha$-tetrahedra on the hierarchical cluster complex $\mathcal{C}_{16}$), projecting onto 15 sequential inductive Gröbner–Shirshov reduction steps along the spanning chain.
4. **8 electron Hopf links** yield the closed $1s^2 2s^2 2p^4$ electron configuration.

## Expected operator sequence and spin–orbit shell closure

Along the inductive spanning reduction, the 15 inter-nucleon operators follow the three-regime pattern. At $Z = N = 6$ (${}^{12}\mathrm{C}$), the spin-aligned $1p_{3/2}$ subshell closes with single-species degeneracy $2j + 1 = 4$ above the $1s_{1/2}$ ($2$) core ($2 + 4 = 6$). Adding the spin-antialigned $1p_{1/2}$ partner of degeneracy $2j + 1 = 2$ completes the $N = 1$ major harmonic-oscillator shell at $2 + 4 + 2 = 8$, where the terminal Hopf link achieves deficit closure for both protons and neutrons.

## Scaling

The inductive spanning reduction for $A = 16$ requires 15 sequential Hecke multiplications, while the 3D simplicial strong energy functional scales linearly with $f_1(A) = 3A - 6$. At $A = 16$, the unreduced braid word contains $\sim 10^{10}$ terms; inductive Gröbner–Shirshov reduction compresses this to $\leq 128$ normal-form terms within $\leq 480$ Hecke steps.

## Doubly magic prediction

The periodic table deficit conjecture predicts that $\mathcal{A}(8, 8)$ minimizes $|\mathrm{dem}|$ among all $A = 16$ isobars, and at balanced isospin $N = Z = 8$ the quadratic Pauli Fermi-asymmetry promotion penalty vanishes identically. Testing the deficit minimum requires evaluating $\mathcal{A}(Z, 16 - Z)$ for $0 \leq Z \leq 16$ and comparing deficit magnitudes. When both the proton and neutron $1s_{1/2}^2 1p_{3/2}^4 1p_{1/2}^2$ shells achieve deficit closure, the doubly-magic nucleus attains $|\mathrm{dem}| = 0$.
