*Let $P$ be a confined particle, with confinement locus $\Omega(P)$, active support $\mathcal{S}^*(P, q)$, binding multiplicity $\mu(P, q)$ and Frobenius stability index $\sigma_F(P, q)$ as in [prop:confinement-phase-stratification](#prop:confinement-phase-stratification). Let $\mathcal{G} \subset \mathbb{R}_{>1}$ be the generic locus of $q$, and call a connected component $\Omega_\alpha$ of $\Omega(P)$ a **confinement phase**. Then:*

1. ***Stratification.** The complement $\mathcal{G} \setminus \Omega(P)$ is a real-algebraic stratification with strata of codimension $\ge 1$, the **phase boundaries** of $P$. Each stratum is the zero locus of a principal minor of the SDP Gram matrix.*
2. ***Strict complementarity on phases.** On each phase $\Omega_\alpha$ the SDP dual pair is continuous and strictly complementary, so the active support and $\mu$ are constant on $\Omega_\alpha$ by [prop:confinement-phase-stratification](#prop:confinement-phase-stratification).*
3. ***Analyticity.** $\sigma_F(P, q)$ is real-analytic on each $\Omega_\alpha$.*
4. ***Scope.** Level sufficiency, shell-closure stability and the Markov-trace preservation of the confinement category functor hold pointwise in each $\Omega_\alpha$ and fail on $\partial\Omega(P)$.*
5. ***Phase transitions.** If a continuous path $q: [0, 1] \to \mathcal{G}$ crosses a phase boundary at $q_0 \in \partial\Omega(P)$, then at $q_0$ either $P$ is not realisable as a closed-braid bound state (**deconfinement**), or $\mu$ jumps, the active support gains or loses a Wedderburn block, and $\sigma_F$ has a non-smooth point (**regime switch**), or both.*

**Supporting argument (not a proof).** *For (1): the entries of the Gram matrix are Laurent polynomials in $q$, so each principal minor that is not identically zero has finitely many real zeros in $\mathcal{G}$; what is not shown is that loss of strict feasibility happens exactly on these zero sets. For (2): strict complementarity holds for generic SDP data (Alizadeh–Haeberly–Overton), but genericity at the substrate parameters is not established. For (4): the proofs of [prop:level-sufficiency](#prop:level-sufficiency) and [prop:shell-closure-stability](#prop:shell-closure-stability) use a well-defined, locally constant active support. For (5): a boundary crossing is either a loss of strict primal feasibility or a dual variable $u^*_\lambda$ passing through zero.*
