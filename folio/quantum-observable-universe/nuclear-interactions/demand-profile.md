**Definition.**
*Let $\mathcal{A}(Z, N) = \sum_w c_w \cdot w$ be a derived atom in normal form. Its **demand profile** is*

$$
  \mathrm{dem}(\mathcal{A})
  \;=\;
  \{(w, c_w) : c_w < 0\}
  \;\subset\;
  \mathrm{NF}(B_A) \times \mathbb{R}_{<0}.
$$

*The **demand magnitude** is $|\mathrm{dem}| = \sum_{c_w < 0} |c_w|$.*
