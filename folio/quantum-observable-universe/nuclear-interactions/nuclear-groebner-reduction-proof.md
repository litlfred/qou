## Proof

Uniqueness of the normal form relies on the confluence half of the Gröbner--Shirshov (Diamond Lemma) machinery applied to $H_A(q)$.

1. **The completed relation set $G$ is a Gröbner--Shirshov basis.** For $A \leq 3$, the three defining families (the Hecke quadratic $\sigma_i^2 - (q - q^{-1})\sigma_i - 1$, the shortlex-decreasing braid relation $\sigma_{i+1}\sigma_i\sigma_{i+1} - \sigma_i\sigma_{i+1}\sigma_i$, and far commutativity $\sigma_i\sigma_j - \sigma_j\sigma_i$ for $i \ge j + 2$) are already closed under composition. At $A \geq 4$, the overlap between the braid relation for $(\sigma_{i-1}, \sigma_i)$ and far commutativity for $(\sigma_i, \sigma_j)$ ($j \leq i - 2$) is resolved by adjoining the $q$-free Bokut--Shiao braid family $\sigma_i\sigma_{i-1}\cdots\sigma_j\sigma_i - \sigma_{i-1}\sigma_i\sigma_{i-1}\cdots\sigma_j$ ($j \leq i - 1$; [Hecke normal form](#def:hecke-normal-form)). Every inclusion and overlap composition in the completed basis $G$ reduces to zero with respect to the length-lexicographic order, verifying classical admissibility for the [Gröbner--Shirshov basis](#unital-groebner-bases:def:groebner-basis) of the Iwahori--Hecke algebra. Moreover, on the block-ordered nuclear word $\mathcal{N}_{\mathrm{raw}}(Z, N)$, intra-block Hecke reductions alone produce strictly increasing generator words $\sigma_S$ that contain no repeated generator index and are therefore immediately irreducible under all of $G$ ([Hecke-only reduction of the nucleus element](#prop:tm-gs-normal-form)).

2. **Confluence ensures a path-independent remainder.** By the Diamond Lemma \cite{bergman1978}, a confluent rewriting system yields a remainder under the [division algorithm](#unital-groebner-bases:def:division-algorithm) independent of the reduction path. Thus, $r(\mathcal{N}_{\mathrm{raw}}(Z, N), G)$ specifies a unique normal form $\mathcal{N}(Z, N)$ in $H_A(q)$.

3. **Termination.** Length-lexicographic order well-orders positive braid monomials. Each rule in $G$ strictly decreases this order, guaranteeing termination. Combined with confluence (which reduces directly to the general [admissible system](#unital-groebner-bases:def:admissible-system) criterion), this establishes both existence and uniqueness.
$\square$
