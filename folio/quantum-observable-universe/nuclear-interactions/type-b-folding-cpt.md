**Conjecture (Type-B folding closure).**
*The $\mathfrak{sl}_n$ Cartan datum on the canonical atomic-braid Hecke algebra $H_n(q)$ folds into a Type-B Cartan datum $\mathrm{B}_{n/2}$ under the involution $\iota: \alpha_i \leftrightarrow \alpha_{n-i}$. The resulting Kashiwara crystal is a symmetric crystal whose GS normal forms are bar-involution invariant by construction. Stable matter = fixed points of the folded crystal; antimatter = the $\iota$-orbit complement. Under this folding, the composite CPT operator decomposes naturally as a tensor of:*

- *C from color-sheet flavor conjugation*
- *P from Type-B Cartan folding*
- *T from Markov-trace cyclicity*

Type-B folding provides an alternative categorical realisation where the role of "P (braid mirror)" is replaced by "the Cartan-fold Z/2", and the bar-invariance emerges from the Type-B symmetric-crystal structure rather than from explicit $\sigma_{i} \leftrightarrow \sigma_{i}^{-1}$ braid manipulation. The Type-B folding view is more compact but requires more category theory.

**Scope at fixed $q$.** Three involutions send a Hecke letter $\sigma_i$ to a letter: $\sigma_i \mapsto \sigma_i^{-1}$, $\sigma_i \mapsto -\sigma_i^{-1}$ and $\sigma_i \mapsto -\sigma_i$. They form a Klein four-group, the composite of any two being the third. Let $P_+$ and $P_-$ be the idempotents of $\sigma_i$ for its eigenvalues $q$ and $-q^{-1}$, and let $e^- = \sigma_0\sigma_1\sigma_0$ with mirror $e^+ = \sigma_0^{-1}\sigma_1^{-1}\sigma_0^{-1}$, as in the [Breit–Wheeler vertex](#rem:breit-wheeler-vertex). The braid mirror $\sigma_i \mapsto \sigma_i^{-1}$ keeps $P_+$ and $P_-$ and sends $e^-$ to $e^+$, which is the profile of $C$ (of $CT$ if $q \mapsto q^{-1}$ is read as antilinear). The map $\sigma_i \mapsto -\sigma_i^{-1}$ exchanges $P_+$ and $P_-$ and sends $e^-$ to $-e^+$, which is the profile of $CP$. Only $\sigma_i \mapsto -\sigma_i$ has the profile of $P$: it exchanges $P_+$ and $P_-$ and sends $e^-$ to $-e^-$.

Neither $\sigma_i \mapsto \sigma_i^{-1}$ nor $\sigma_i \mapsto -\sigma_i$ is realised by an algebra map of $H_n(q)$ that is linear over the scalars, unless $q^2 = 1$. Both exist only as maps covering $q \mapsto q^{-1}$ on the scalars, and that map has no counterpart at the real aether value $q_0$. Hence no letter-level map has the profile of $P$ at fixed real $q$.
