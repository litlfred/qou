Three different sevens attach to the $5_2$ knot. They arise from unrelated
constructions, they live on different layers, and identifying any two of them is
unsupported. The corpus has already lost a claim to exactly that error, so the
distinctions are recorded explicitly here.

## The three

**The determinant.** $\det(5_2) = |\Delta_{5_2}(-1)| = 7$, where
$\Delta_{5_2}(t) = 2t^{2} - 3t + 2$ has discriminant $-7$ and roots
$(3 \pm i\sqrt{7})/4$. This seven is **abelian**: it belongs to the Alexander
polynomial and the $\mathbb{Z}/7$ double branched cover, and the field it
generates is $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-7})$, the imaginary quadratic subfield of
$\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_7)$.

**The $\tau$-even Stokes sum.** The leading Stokes constants of the two
chirality blocks satisfy $\mathrm{dt}^{+} + \mathrm{dt}^{-} = 4 + 3 = 7$
([the $5_2$ DT spectrum split](#prop:fivetwo-dt-spectrum-split)). This
seven is a **sum of two integers**, not a field invariant, and no arithmetic
meaning is attached to it; it appears in the corpus only as one of four distinct
readings used to show that no single scalar reproduces the leading Stokes data.

**The cyclotomic level.** The level-seven ladder
([the $5_2$ level-seven ladder](#prop:fivetwo-level-seven-ladder)) lives in
$\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_7)^{+}$, the **totally real** cubic subfield generated by
$w_k = 1 + 2\cos(2\pi k/7)$, and its seven enters through the quadratic residues
$\{1,2,4\}$ mod $7$.

## None of them is the trace field

The invariant trace field is the cubic of discriminant $-23$
([the $5_2$ trace field](#prop:fivetwo-trace-field)), which is a fourth
object again. Two comparisons are inviting, and both fail.

$\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-7})$ **cannot** sit inside the trace field: $2 \nmid 3$, so
the tower law forbids it. That is the exclusion the determinant-to-trace-field
reading violates.

$\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_7)^{+}$ **has the same degree** as the trace field — both are
cubic — and their minimal polynomials even open the same way,

$$
x^{3} - 2x^{2} - x + 1
\qquad\text{versus}\qquad
x^{3} - 2x^{2} + 3x - 1 .
$$

They are nevertheless different fields, and not marginally: the first has
discriminant $49$, is totally real and cyclic; the second has discriminant
$-23$, is non-Galois with Galois group $S_3$, and has one real place and a
complex-conjugate pair. Matching degrees and matching leading coefficients are
not evidence of anything.

## The reusable form

Before attaching a field to a knot invariant, ask **which layer** the invariant
belongs to. Abelian data — the determinant, the Alexander polynomial, the
branched cover — carries one field. The geometric
$\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{C})$ representation carries another. A cyclotomic level
appearing in a transported identity carries a third. They coincide for no
general reason, and a shared numeral is not a shared structure.
