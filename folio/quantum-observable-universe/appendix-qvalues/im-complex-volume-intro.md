This appendix collects the $\beta$-decay $Q$-value predictions: the transfer-matrix $Q$-value chain, its aether-parameter evaluations, and the benchmark table. These constitute the imaginary-part companion to the binding-energy appendix. While that appendix computes the binding energy $\mathrm{Re}\,\mathfrak{V} = \mathfrak{E}_{\mathrm{bind}}$, this one computes the bar-odd ($\sinh$) weak channel $\mathfrak{Q}_\beta = \mathrm{Im}\mathfrak{V}$, the imaginary part of the complex volume $\mathfrak{V} = E_0\,\mathrm{tr}_M\big((\Pi_{\text{free}}-\mathbf{1})\circ\mathcal{M}\big)$. 

Every step below records this $\mathrm{Im}$ channel, including the $c_k$ truncation-order ladder. All entries are evaluated at the aether parameter $q_0$. Since $\mathrm{Im}(q_0) = 0$, each prediction is a real measured number.
