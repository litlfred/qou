Across the discrete hierarchy from quark triples to composite bound states ([Derived atom](#def:derived-atom), [Confinement universality](#prop:confinement-universality)), operator selection at stratum $k$ is governed over a linearly ordered commutative ring by a confinement-layer datum $(A_k, c_k, \mathcal{A}_k)$, where $A_k \in \mathbb{Z}^{d \times m}$ is the conserved-charge incidence matrix, $c_k$ is the crossing-action or trace cost vector, and $\mathcal{A}_k \subseteq \mathbb{N}^m$ is the admissible motif cone. Given dual shadow prices $y \in K^d$ \cite{farkas1902,kantorovich1939}, column generation \cite{dantzig-wolfe1960} promotes an admissible composite motif $z \in \mathcal{A}_k$ to the next stratum whenever its reduced cost

$$
  \bar{c}_k(z; y) \;=\; \langle c_k, z\rangle - \langle y, A_k z\rangle
$$

is strictly negative and its Hecke image lies in the involution-twisted Markov $\ast$-positivity cone $\tau_q(X X^\ast) \ge 0$ ($T_w^\ast = T_{w^{-1}}$) under the Markov trace weight $z = q^3/(q^2+1)$.

Because the antisymmetrizer $r_{[1^3]} = \sum_{w \in S_3} (-q^{-1})^{\ell(w)} T_w$ spans the trace radical $\mathrm{rad}\,\tau_q$ on $H_3(q)$ at $z = q^3/(q^2+1)$, positivity is evaluated on the five-dimensional Temperley--Lieb quotient $TL_3(q) = H_3(q)/\mathrm{rad}\,\tau_q$ rather than against an untwisted bilinear Gram matrix on $H_3(q)$. At the intranucleon stabilization step of [Derived atom](#def:derived-atom), all five non-identity three-strand normal-form basis words have vanishing hyperbolic volume and both bare three-quark configurations already lie inside the $\ast$-positivity cone; which minimal stabilizer motif is selected across the candidate stabilization arms remains open.
