*Let $q$ be a substrate parameter and $m_e$ a mass scale. The **expression $\kappa_{\mathrm{closed}}$** is*

$$
  \kappa_{\mathrm{closed}}(q, m_e)
  \;=\; -\,\frac{E_0 \cdot \log(1/h)}{[2]_q \cdot [3]_q},
  \qquad
  E_0 = \frac{m_e}{q(q-1)}, \quad h = q - q^{-1},
$$

*where $[n]_q = (q^n - q^{-n}) / (q - q^{-1})$ is the balanced q-bracket.*
