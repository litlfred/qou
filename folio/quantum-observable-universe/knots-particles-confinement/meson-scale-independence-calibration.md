## Calibration structure

The three calibration inputs are independent physical measurements. Each is associated with a decay process:

| Anchor | Observable | Jet order | Measurement |
|--------|-----------|-----------|-------------|
| **CAL-1** | $m_\mathfrak{e}$ | $h^0$ | electron mass (Penning trap) |
| **CAL-2** | $P_{\mathrm{skew}}^{\beta}$ | $h^2$ | neutron $\beta$-sector bar-skew channel ($\tau_n = \hbar / P_{\mathrm{skew}}^{\beta}$) |
| **CAL-3** | $P_{\mathrm{avg}}^{\pi}$ | $h^3$ | pion-sector bar-even channel ($\tau_\pi = \hbar / P_{\mathrm{avg}}^{\pi}$) |

CAL-2 determines $\mathfrak{Q}_\beta = m_n - m_p - m_\mathfrak{e}$ from the neutron lifetime. This fixes $q_0$ via the aether identity. CAL-3 determines $m_\pi$ from the pion lifetime. This fixes the meson mass scale. All other masses (proton, neutron, muon, kaon, $\ldots$) are derived from these three inputs.

The $h^1$ jet order is absent. The bar involution $q \mapsto q^{-1}$ forces all Markov traces of writhe-zero braids to be even functions of $h$. The $h^3$ order enters through the flavor-split $X = (q-1)^2(1-q^{-4})/3 = h^3/6 + \cdots$, which is a skein difference (not a single-braid Markov trace).

## Geometric core of the obstruction

The Hopf-link complement (meson binding topology) has hyperbolic volume $0$. The Borromean complement (baryon) has positive volume. Because $\mathrm{Vol}(\mathrm{Hopf}) = 0$, the volume-proportional geometric contribution to the meson scale vanishes identically, independently of $(m_\mathfrak{e}, q_0)$. The absolute scale therefore enters only through the independent calibration CAL-3 ($P_{\mathrm{avg}}^{\pi}$).
