*Processes* are not sectors: a [fusion process](#def:fusion-process) is a strand-balanced rearrangement inside the strong sector, while β-decay moves proton number on the subject side and is thereby routed to the weak sector. The bar-even and bar-odd channels are intertwined by the bar involution itself — the rotation between the two channel recurrences of [the binding–$Q_\beta$ complex volume conjecture](#conj:binding-qbeta-complex-volume) — so the taxonomy's parity column is a statement about where each sector's observable lives, not a wall between algebras.

The Lean formalization of the [interaction-sector taxonomy](#def:interaction-sector-taxonomy) records the taxonomy as a total assignment and proves that weak is the unique bar-odd-assigned sector, that strong is the unique bar-even one, and that the electromagnetic sector is exactly the one carrying no pure-parity assignment — which is itself a theorem, not an omission, as follows.

At the *coupling* level the electromagnetic cell is now derived. The quantum integer $[n]_q$ is bar-even — invariant under $q \mapsto q^{-1}$ — so in the bare coupling $C(q) = q\,[9]_q[10]_q$ only the exponential prefactor moves under the bar involution, giving the exact decomposition

$$
  C(q) \pm C(q^{-1}) \;=\; (q \pm q^{-1})\,[9]_q[10]_q .
$$

The bar-even part of the electromagnetic coupling is the cosh-channel (strong-side) multiple and the bar-odd part the sinh-channel (weak-side) multiple of one invariant factor $B = [9]_q[10]_q$: the electromagnetic coupling is the full exponential mix $e^{\lambda} B$ with $\lambda = \ln q$, sitting at the intertwiner point between the two pure channels rather than in either. At the substrate anchor the odd-to-even ratio is $\tanh(\ln q_0) \approx 0.10396$ exactly for the bare coupling, and $\approx 0.10356$ for the Chern–Simons-dressed value, whose tower is not bar-covariant in a single parity ($\hbar_q^2 \mapsto q^2 \hbar_q^2$). The observable level follows: $\alpha = C^{-1}$ carries the conjugate exponential $e^{-\lambda}$ (odd-to-even ratio $-\tanh\ln q_0$), and the Hartree scale $\alpha^2$ the doubled $e^{-2\lambda}$ (ratio $-\tanh 2\ln q_0 \approx -0.2057$). Moreover, under the nondegeneracy that holds at the real anchor ($q^2 \neq \pm 1$, $B \neq 0$), the coupling is provably *neither* bar-even *nor* bar-odd — the electromagnetic cell is not unassigned but **provably admits no pure-parity assignment**, in exact contrast to binding (pure even) and $\mathfrak{Q}_\beta$ (pure odd). All of this is proved over any field in the Lean library.
