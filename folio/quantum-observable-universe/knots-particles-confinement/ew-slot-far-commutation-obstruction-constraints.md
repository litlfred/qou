## Forced constraints

This result is a structural exclusion. It proves an impossibility rather than a numerical near-miss. It establishes where the missing structure must reside.

The far-commutation relation covers only pairs with $|i - j| \ge 2$. The adjacent generators $\sigma_2, \sigma_4$ bridge strand-pairs $2\text{--}3$ and $4\text{--}5$. These do not commute with the slots. The categorical linking operation $\mathrm{Lk}_R^{(k)}$ braids between strand-pairs through precisely these generators. The non-abelian $\mathrm{SU}(2)_L$ structure must therefore be carried by the inter-slot linking rather than by the individual slots.

This narrows the group-assignment postulate. The residual $\mathrm{SU}(2)_L \times \mathrm{U}(1)_Y$ input is a property of the bracket-carrying linking data rather than of the mutually commuting slots.
