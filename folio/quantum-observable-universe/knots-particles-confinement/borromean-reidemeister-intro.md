The Borromean rings $6^3_2$, realized as the closure of the three-strand alternating word $\mathcal{B}_3 = (\sigma_0\sigma_1^{-1})^3 \in B_3$, form a Brunnian link: the three components are bound into a single topological composite, yet removing any one component leaves the remaining two unlinked. This section adopts $\mathcal{B}_3$ as the topological carrier of a three-quark baryon, introduces the flavour-typed crossing operator and its Gröbner–Shirshov normal form in $H_3(q)$, proves invariance under all three Reidemeister moves, and demonstrates that single-component closures carry fermionic half-integer spin parity while two-component Hopf closures carry bosonic integer spin parity.
