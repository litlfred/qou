*Let $P_{\mathrm{bare}}$ be a confined particle at level $\ell$. Let $S = (\{e_k\}, \iota, W_{\mathrm{bind}})$ be an electron shell of multiplicity $Z$ attached to it. The **dressing morphism** at $S$ is the arrow in the confinement category:*

$$
  \mathrm{dress}_S\,:\;P_{\mathrm{bare}}\;\longrightarrow\;P_{\mathrm{dressed}}
$$

*This morphism sends $P_{\mathrm{bare}}$ to the **dressed particle**:*

$$
  P_{\mathrm{dressed}}
  \;=\;
  \bigl(
    \mathrm{name}(P_{\mathrm{bare}}) \oplus \mathrm{name}(S),\;
    \ell,\;
    [\iota(K_{\mathrm{bare}}) \cdot W_{\mathrm{bind}}]_M,\;
    n_0 + Z
  \bigr)
$$

*Here $\mathrm{name}(S)$ is the shell label, $[\ \cdot\ ]_M$ denotes Markov equivalence, and the composite braid lives in $H_{n_0 + Z}(q)$ via the cabling embedding.*
