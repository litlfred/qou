**Remark.**
*This proposition gives a categorical origin for stable dressing sequences in the confinement category.*

1. ***Periodicity.** Dressing $\mathrm{dress}_{S_Z}: P^{(Z-1)} \to P^{(Z)}$ traces a chain in the confinement category. The Frobenius stability index $\sigma_F(P^{(Z)}, q)$ is monotone non-decreasing. Its marginal increment $\Delta\sigma_F(Z) = I_1(Z)$ has local maxima at the categorical shell closures $[2, 10, 28, 60, \ldots]$.*
2. ***Categorical inertness.** A categorical shell closure $P^{(Z)}$ is a local terminal object of the dressing sub-diagram. The single-electron dressing $\mathrm{dress}_{S_1}: P^{(Z)} \to P^{(Z+1)}$ is feasible only if every currently tight dual block of the confinement SDP is simultaneously broken.*
3. ***Marginal energy peaks.** The marginal increment $\Delta\sigma_F(Z)$ peaks at the shell closures and dips at the openings via the dual shadow-price structure.*

**Valence as dressing-arrow availability.**
*An object's valence $v(Z)$ is the number of forward dressing arrows $\mathrm{dress}_{S_1}: P^{(Z)} \to P^{(Z+1)}$ that remain feasible. For closure indices $Z \in \{2, 10, 28, 60, \ldots\}$, $v(Z) = 0$ (terminal object).*

**Physical readings at the comparison boundary.** *The following interpretations lie at the empirical comparison boundary rather than inside the algebraic proof; they depend on terminality (claim 3) and on the open dual-lift step 1.*

**Reading (chemical inertness).**
Categorical terminality of noble-gas $P^{(Z)}$ structurally forces chemical inertness. No covalent dressing morphism $\mathrm{dress}_{S_{\mathrm{bond}}}: P^{(Z)} \to P_{\mathrm{molecule}}$ is feasible at room-temperature $q$ without breaking a closed shell. Noble gas compounds require $q$ far from the generic locus to clear the terminality barrier.

**Reading (ionisation energy peaks).**
The first-ionisation energy $I_1(Z)$ is the Markov-trace gap from $P^{(Z)}$ to $P^{(Z-1)} + e^-$. This matches the marginal stability gain $\Delta\sigma_F(Z)$. By [topological LP confinement](#prop:confinement-topological) and the dual shadow-price structure, $I_1$ peaks at shell closures and dips at shell openings. Identifying these closures with empirical noble-gas peaks is conditional on the open shell-capacity derivation; the bare categorical operator does not place the closures at the noble gases without the Madelung structure.
