## The $W$ vs $Z$ slot assignment is a free label

The sector membership $A_+ / A_-$ of each carrier is forced by its spin. A charged carrier must be a bar-odd $A_-$ doublet. What is not forced is which of the two vector slots — $\sigma_1^4$ or $\sigma_3^6$ — hosts the charged $W^\pm$ versus the neutral $Z$. The crossing$\to$charge map $Q = \tfrac12\,\mathrm{writhe}$ on the all-positive braid $\sigma_1^{2k}$ gives $Q(T(2,2k)) = k > 0$ for every carrier. It cannot single out the neutral slot. The surviving $\mathrm{writhe} \to I_3$ map assigns $2 I_3 = 2k$, nonzero on both vector slots. No proved crossing$\to$charge principle selects the $W$ slot: both $W = \sigma_1^4$ and the mirror $W = \sigma_3^6$ satisfy every proved constraint.

## The signed charge-conjugation test

Charge conjugation is crossing reversal. The operation $C = (\sigma \leftrightarrow \sigma^{-1})$ reverses every crossing. Within the even-twist slot family $\sigma_1^{2k} \subset B_2$ — all carriers presented at the same strand number — a carrier is neutral iff it is a fixed point of $C$, i.e. $C$-invariant or amphichiral, and over $\mathbb{Z}$ this fixed-point condition is exactly the vanishing of the signed writhe. The restriction to a fixed level is required: the writhe is not a Markov invariant of the link, so the unknot presented as $\sigma_1^{\pm 1}$ has nonzero writhe without being charged. The argument below compares slots at $n = 2$ throughout and is unaffected.

Admitting the signed asymmetry does not force which slot is $W$: two distinct signed labelings both satisfy the full $C$-invariance neutrality constraint yet disagree on the charged slot. The signed test constrains the neutral slot to be amphichiral, but not which slot it is.

The $C$-invariance criterion excludes every pure even-twist slot from being the neutral member. Each carrier $\sigma_1^{2k}$ has signed writhe $2k \neq 0$, so none is $C$-invariant. The neutral electroweak boson is necessarily not a single even-twist slot; it must be the mixed $(W^3, B) \mapsto (Z, \gamma)$ Weinberg combination.

## The two natural realizations of the second charge also fail

A hypothetical second baryon or color-trace charge nonzero on exactly one vector slot would determine the neutral slot uniquely. The pairwise linking number $\mathrm{Lk}$ and the Milnor triple linking $\bar\mu(123)$ do not qualify. The even-twist carrier is a two-component link, so $\bar\mu(123) \equiv 0$ on every carrier. $\mathrm{Lk}(T(2,2k)) = k \neq 0$ on every carrier. Neither is nonzero on exactly one vector slot.

## The group-structure data does force the label

Topology cannot force the label, but the $\mathrm{SU}(2)_L \times \mathrm{U}(1)_Y$ group assignment can. The genuine missing input is the alignment of the two vector slots with the $\mathfrak{su}(2)_L$ Cartan–Weyl basis. Given the `EWGroupAssignment`, the label is derived: the neutral slot is the Cartan slot, the charged slot is the complementary root slot. The group datum itself remains the open conjectural input.
