*Proof.*
*We construct the normal form (NF) $T_w \in H_{3(Z+N)}(q)$ explicitly.*

*On the intra-nucleon blocks, each Borromean baryon ([borromean baryon](#def:borromean-baryon)) possesses a canonical normal form in $H_3(R, q)$ on three strands; let $T_p, T_n \in H_3(R, q)$ denote the proton and neutron normal forms. Under the block-diagonal inclusion, the disconnected tensor product $T_p^{\otimes Z} \otimes T_n^{\otimes N} \in H_3(R, q)^{\otimes (Z+N)}$ embeds into the many-body algebra $H_{3(Z+N)}(q)$ on the intra-nucleon strands.*

*To couple the individual baryon blocks, let $w_{\mathrm{inter}} \in S_{3(Z+N)}$ be the inter-nucleon connector permutation that realizes the $pp$, $nn$, and $pn$ crossings between adjacent baryon blocks ([atomic braid](#def:atomic-braid) crossing table). Multiplying the intra-nucleon embedding by the connector yields the element $T_w = T_{w_{q^Z}} \cdot T_{w_{\bar q^N}} \cdot T_{w_{\mathrm{inter}}} \in H_{3(Z+N)}(q)$, whose Markov closure $\hat\beta = \hat T_w$ forms a closed braid in $S^3$.*

*By the Diamond Lemma for the Hecke algebra ([hecke normal form](#def:hecke-normal-form)), the reduced normal form $T_w$ is independent of the choice of reduction sequence, so the atom-knot is uniquely defined.* $\square$
