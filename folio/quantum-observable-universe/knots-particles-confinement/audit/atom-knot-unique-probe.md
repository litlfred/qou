# Physical Isomers and Fermion Permutations

Permuting identical fermions multiplies the wavefunction by a sign; it does not generate distinct physical states. Graph-theoretical distinctions that are not invariant under the permutation of identical fermions map to the same physical state in the observable trace.

Distinct realisations of the proton-quark block $w_{q^Z}$ — such as interchanging two protons — yield distinct categorical braid-isotopy classes prior to fermionic antisymmetrisation rather than distinct physical nuclear isomers, which would contradict quantum statistics.
