# Audit Probe: Physical Isomers and Quantum Permutations

**Target:** the uniqueness proof for the atom knot

Permuting identical fermions induces a phase shift in the wavefunction; it does not generate distinct physical states. Graph-theoretical distinctions that are not invariant under the permutation of identical fermions map to the same physical state in the observable trace.

An earlier formulation claimed that distinct realisations of the proton-quark block $w_{q^Z}$—such as interchanging two protons—yield distinct isotopy classes corresponding to distinct physical isomers. This contradicts quantum statistics. 

This physical interpretation is removed from the uniqueness proof. The text now states only that distinct $w_{q^Z}$ realisations yield distinct categorical isotopy classes.
