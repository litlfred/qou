# Audit Probe: Shell Closure vs Empirical Noble Gases

**Target:** the shell-closure stability proposition

The categorical operator closures yield the sequence $Z \in \{2, 10, 28, 60, \ldots\}$. This follows from the $2n^2$ count of the $2\times 2$ transfer matrix $T_{\gamma e}$. The empirical noble-gas numbers are $\mathcal{Z}_{\mathrm{noble}} = \{2, 10, 18, 36, 54, 86\}$. 

An earlier formulation connected the categorical sequence to the empirical noble gases. This requires the Madelung sub-shell ordering, which has not yet been derived categorically. A high-precision categorical $\sigma_F$ dressing chain confirms that $\sigma_F$ alone does not reproduce the noble-gas peaks. The theoretical marginal energy peaks cannot yet be identified with observed noble-gas peaks and alkali troughs ($Z = 3, 11, 19, \ldots$).

This correspondence is removed from the shell-closure stability proposition and its interpretation block. The Madelung ordering must be rigorously derived before making these physical claims.
