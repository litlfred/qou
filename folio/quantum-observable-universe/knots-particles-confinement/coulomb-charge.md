Let $\mathfrak{H}$ be a knot composite with braid word $\beta$. Let $n_+(\beta)$ and $n_-(\beta)$ denote the number of positive and negative crossings in $\beta$. We define the **Coulomb charge** of $\mathfrak{H}$ as the halved writhe:

$$
  Q(\mathfrak{H}) =  \tfrac{1}{2}\bigl(n_+(\beta) - n_-(\beta)\bigr).
$$
