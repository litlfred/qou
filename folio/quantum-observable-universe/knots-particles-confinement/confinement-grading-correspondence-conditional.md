**Theorem (conditional on the crystal-graph realization).**

*Let $\mathsf{C} = [\mathrm{CrystalGraphRealization}]$ be the upstream conjectural class, providing the Kashiwara crystal $\mathrm{Cryst}(H_n(R, q))$ and the Brundan–Kleshchev isomorphism $H_n(R, q) \cong R_\alpha^{\Lambda}$. Conditional on $\mathsf{C}$, the confinement filtration $J^\bullet(q)$ equals the filtration induced by the $\mathbb{Z}$-grading on the KLR algebra: a closed braid $K$ with normal form $T_w$ has confinement level $\ell(K, q)$ iff its KLR-module lift $\mathfrak{M}_K$ has top non-zero graded piece in degree $\ell(K, q)$,*

$$
  T_w \in J^\ell(q) \setminus J^{\ell+1}(q)
  \;\iff\;
  (\mathfrak{M}_K)_d = 0 \;\;\text{for}\;\; d > \ell.
$$
