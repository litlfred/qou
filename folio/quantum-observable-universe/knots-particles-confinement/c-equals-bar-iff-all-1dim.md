*On the Iwahori--Hecke algebra $H_n(q)$, with $\sigma_i^2 = h\sigma_i + 1$ and $h = q - q^{-1}$, charge conjugation $C\colon \sigma_i \mapsto \sigma_i^{-1}$ and the bar involution $\mathrm{bar}\colon q \mapsto q^{-1}$ agree on every irreducible character: for every $n$, every partition $\lambda \vdash n$ and every braid word $\beta$,*

$$
  \chi^\lambda\bigl(C(\beta)\bigr)(q) = \chi^\lambda(\beta)(q^{-1}).
$$

*In particular $C = \mathrm{bar}$ holds at $H_3(q)$ on the two-dimensional representation $(2,1)$, not only on the one-dimensional representations.*
