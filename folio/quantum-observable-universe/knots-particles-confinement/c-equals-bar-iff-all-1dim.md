*On the Iwahori--Hecke algebra $H_3(q)$, with $\sigma_i^2 = h\sigma_i + 1$ and $h = q - q^{-1}$, let charge conjugation $C\colon \sigma_i \mapsto \sigma_i^{-1}$ act on braid words and let the bar involution $\mathrm{bar}\colon q \mapsto q^{-1}$ act on coefficients. For the two-dimensional irreducible representation $(2,1)$ and every braid word $\beta \in B_3$,*

$$
  \chi^{(2,1)}\bigl(C(\beta)\bigr)(q) = \chi^{(2,1)}(\beta)(q^{-1}).
$$

*The same identity holds for the one-dimensional characters of $H_n(q)$ for every $n$, since these send $\sigma_i$ to $q$ or to $-q^{-1}$. So $C = \mathrm{bar}$ holds on every irreducible character of $H_3(q)$, not only on the one-dimensional ones.*
