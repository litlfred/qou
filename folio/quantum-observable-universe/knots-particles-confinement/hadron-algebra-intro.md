The quark and pair-production relations of the preceding section single out those multi-strand configurations in $H_n(q)$ that bind into colour-neutral composites. This section defines a hadron as a closed braid composite whose Coulomb charge and crossing energy lie in the stability stratum of the relation algebra, proves existence and uniqueness of minimal-energy normal-form representatives up to braid isotopy, and shows that confluent Gröbner–Shirshov reduction factorizes any closed strand uniquely into hadron constituents in the Pawlowski–Leitner basis. Equipping a hadron with a marked localization point on its Bring fibre prepares the knot–particle correspondence of the next section.
