*Suppose $\beta_1$ and $\beta_2$ are two braids in $B_{n(K)}$ that both realize the normal form block decomposition $T_w = T_{w_{q^Z}} \cdot T_{w_{\bar q^N}} \cdot T_{w_{\mathrm{inter}}}$, that is, they have the same image in the Hecke algebra $H_{n(K)}(q)$. Then their Markov closures have the same Markov trace:*

$$
  \mathrm{tr}_M(\hat\beta_1) \;=\; \mathrm{tr}_M(\hat\beta_2).
$$

*Isotopy of the closures is not claimed: braids with equal Hecke images can close to non-isotopic links, since the Markov trace is the HOMFLY-PT polynomial and that does not separate links (mutant pairs).*
