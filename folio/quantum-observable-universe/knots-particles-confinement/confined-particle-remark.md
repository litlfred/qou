The Markov class $[K]_M$ of a [confined particle](#def:confined-particle) is not intrinsically confined at any particular $q$; confinement is the $q$-indexed predicate $\mathrm{confined}(P, q)$.

Unstable species are confined particles for all $q$ in the generic locus even though their decay channels are open.

The witness data $k_{\max}(P, q)$ are computed on the canonical representative $\mathrm{GS}(P)$. Two particles are equal if and only if their names are equal, which occurs if and only if $\mathrm{GS}(P_1) = \mathrm{GS}(P_2)$.
