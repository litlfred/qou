Having classified individual leptons, mesons, Borromean baryons, and the electroweak even-twist torus-link triplet $\{T(2,4), T(2,6), T(2,8)\}$, we turn to composite multi-particle states. The Frobenius positivity condition on the Iwahori–Hecke algebra stratifies confined composites into principal shells: a state at confinement level $\ell$ occupies the $\ell$-th shell precisely when the Frobenius form on its active Wedderburn support is positive definite. This section defines the confinement category, its Markov functor, and the dressing morphisms that assemble multi-electron and multi-nucleon shells.
