**Definition.**
*An atom-knot $K$ is **unstable at $q$** if and only if it fails to satisfy the [stable atom](#def:stable-atom) condition. That is, the coefficient vector $x(K, q)$ of $T_w$ within the confinement filtration $J^{\ell(K, q)}(q)$ either fails to lie in $\mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}^N$ or violates $\mathrm{G}(q)\,x(K, q) \geq 0$ in some coordinate.*
