Normal form in $H_3(q)$ of the $\gamma\gamma$ product, with the photon $\gamma(\varphi) = \mathbf{1} + \varphi_0\sigma_0 + \varphi_1\sigma_1 + \varphi_{01}\sigma_0\sigma_1^{-1}$ ([photon](#def:photon)), evaluated at the proton $q_0$. The basis words are $L_+ = \sigma_0\sigma_1$, $L_- = \sigma_1\sigma_0$ and $e^- = \sigma_0\sigma_1\sigma_0$.

| Basis | $\gamma(\varphi)^2$, illustrative weights | $\gamma_+\gamma_-$, eigenvalue pair | Role |
|:---:|---:|---:|:---|
| $\mathbf{1}$ | $+1.004$ | $+0.998$ | Photon identity (vacuum) |
| $\sigma_0$ | $+0.090$ | $-0.010$ | Horizontal polarization |
| $\sigma_1$ | $+0.099$ | $+0.009$ | Vertical polarization |
| $L_+$ | $+0.043$ | $+0.046$ | Positive Hopf link |
| $L_-$ | $+0.003$ | $-0.044$ | Cross-color Hopf |
| $e^-$ | $+0.002$ | $0$ | Trefoil channel (4-term only) |

The first column uses $\varphi = (0.0487, 0.0487, 0.02)$ for both photons. These weights are not derived. The value $0.0487$ is a fit to $2m_e$ (Breit--Wheeler), and $0.02$ has no source; both were reconstructed by matching the earlier rounded entries of this table. The column is a worked illustration of the $\gamma\gamma$ normal form, not a prediction. The second column uses the eigenvalue pair $\gamma_\pm$ with $\varphi_0 = \varphi_1 = 0$ and $\varphi_{01} = qh$, $-q^{-1}h$, where $h = q - q^{-1}$, for which $\gamma_+\gamma_- = (\sigma_0\sigma_1^{-1})^3$. It is a product of two different photons and has no $e^-$ term.
