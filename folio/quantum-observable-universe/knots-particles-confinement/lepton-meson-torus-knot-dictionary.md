*The lepton generations are indexed by odd crossing counts $n = 3, 5, 7$. The first-generation lepton, the electron, is the word $\sigma_0\sigma_1\sigma_0 \in B_3$, whose closure is the Hopf link; it has $n = 3$ crossings. A quark is an unknot strand of $\mathcal{B}_3$ carrying one crossing letter; its index $n$ is the crossing count of its lepton partner, a generation label. One establishes the following correspondence:*

| Generation | $n$ | Lepton | Quark flavor ($H_3$) | Meson ($q\bar{q}$), sum of partner labels |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1st | 3 | $\mathfrak{e}$ (electron) | $u, d$ | $\pi$ (pion), $3 + 3 = 6$ |
| 2nd | 5 | $\mu$ (muon) | $\mathfrak{c}, s$ (charm, strange) | $K$ (kaon), $3 + 5 = 8$ |
| 3rd | 7 | $\tau$ (tau) | $\mathfrak{t}, \mathfrak{b}$ (top, bottom) | $\eta, \phi$; $\phi$ is $5 + 5 = 10$ |

*The electron row uses the word $\sigma_0\sigma_1\sigma_0$. The muon and tau rows are read in the two-strand tower, where the lepton at generation $k$ is the closure of $\sigma_1^{2k+1}$ in $H_2(q)$ (the torus knot $T(2, 2k+1)$); in that tower the electron would be the trefoil $T(2,3)$, which is kept as the backup reading. No three-strand word has yet been derived for the muon or the tau, so their labels $5$ and $7$ are the crossing counts of $T(2,5)$ and $T(2,7)$. The meson in the same row is the Hopf-bound quark pair whose two constituents' partner labels sum to $n + 3$, that is $6, 8, 10$ down the three rows — the meson column is indexed by that total, not by the row's quark generation.*

The two indices run in the same step of $2$, so the column reads as aligned while being offset: only the first row is a pure same-generation pair. By the constituent content of the proof, $\pi = \bar{u}d$ is $3 + 3$, $K = \bar{u}s$ is $3 + 5$ and therefore mixes the first and second generations, and $\phi = s\bar{s}$ is $5 + 5$ and is a pure *second*-generation pair occupying the third row. There is no pure third-generation entry: the third-generation quarkonia are $\Upsilon = \mathfrak{b}\bar{\mathfrak{b}}$, which would sit at $7 + 7 = 14$ crossings and is not computed here, and $\mathfrak{t}\bar{\mathfrak{t}}$, which is not a bound state.
