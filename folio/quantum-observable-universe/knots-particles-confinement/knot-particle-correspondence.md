*Let $V = V_{1/2}$ be the fundamental representation of $\mathcal{U}_q(\mathfrak{su}(2))$, and let $B_n$ act on $V^{\otimes n}$ via the $R$-matrix braiding, through the Iwahori–Hecke algebra $H_n(R, q)$. By $q$-Schur–Weyl duality, $V^{\otimes n}$ decomposes into irreducible summands labelled by the partitions $\lambda \vdash n$ with at most two rows.*

*The Markov trace is a weighted sum of the irreducible characters,*

$$
  \mathrm{tr}_M \;=\; \sum_{\lambda} y_\lambda\, \chi_\lambda ,
$$

*and the link invariant of the closure $\hat\beta$ is a normalisation of $\mathrm{tr}_M(\beta)$. Every irreducible summand therefore contributes, with weight $y_\lambda\,\chi_\lambda(\beta)$, to the invariant of every $n$-strand closure.*

*This is not a bijection between summands and link types. There are $\lfloor n/2 \rfloor + 1$ summands, while for $n \ge 2$ the closures of $n$-strand braids include infinitely many distinct link types, for example the torus links $T(2, k)$.*
