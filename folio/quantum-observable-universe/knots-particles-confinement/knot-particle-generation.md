**Definition.**
*The particle spectrum is generated from a finite set of torus knot generators $T_{m,n}$ (with $\gcd(m,n) = 1$) embedded in the standard torus $\mathbb{T}^2 \subset S^3$, together with two :refterm[linking operations]{#linking-operations} that act on the exceptional divisor and the color-tube fibration. The :defterm[torus knot generators]{#torus-knot-generators} are the family*

$$
  \mathcal{G} =  \bigl\{ T_{m,n}  \big| 
  \gcd(m,n) = 1, m < n,
  m + n \leq N(\chi) \bigr\},
$$

*where $T_{m,n}$ winds $m$ times around the meridian and $n$ times around the longitude of $\mathbb{T}^2$, and $N(\chi)$ is a cutoff determined by the Euler characteristic of the Frobenius--Hopf object.* For the Descartes universe with $\chi_{\mathrm{eu}}(M_0) = -6$, the bound is $N(-6) = 9$, admitting:
