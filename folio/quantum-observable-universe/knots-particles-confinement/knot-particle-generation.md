*Let $N(\chi)$ be the cutoff determined by the Euler characteristic $\chi$ of the Frobenius--Hopf object. The :defterm[torus knot generators]{#torus-knot-generators} are the family of torus knots in the standard torus $\mathbb{T}^2 \subset S^3$*

$$
  \mathcal{G} =  \bigl\{ T_{m,n}  \big| 
  \gcd(m,n) = 1, m < n,
  m + n \leq N(\chi) \bigr\},
$$

*where $T_{m,n}$ winds $m$ times around the meridian and $n$ times around the longitude of $\mathbb{T}^2$.*
