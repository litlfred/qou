*For simple particles, $\Omega(P)$ forms a single connected interval of $\mathcal{G}$ containing $q_0$. For composite particles with multi-block binding, $\Omega(P)$ can be disconnected. Each component corresponds to a distinct configuration of the active support. One physically realises transitions between components as chemical or nuclear rearrangements. Computationally, they appear as regime switches in the witness files.*
