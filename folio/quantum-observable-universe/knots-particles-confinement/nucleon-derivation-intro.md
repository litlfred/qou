Because every interaction factors into elementary two-strand crossings, the section begins by defining the two fundamental crossing observables on a braid word: the crossing energy $E_\times$ and its orientation-odd component, the Coulomb charge $Q$. Applying linear-programming operator selection to the two-photon pair-production vertex selects the three-strand Borromean braid $\mathcal{B}_3 = (\sigma_0\sigma_1^{-1})^3 \in H_3(q)$ as the confinement optimum. Closing the four-term photon braid with $\mathcal{B}_3$ yields the proton and neutron normal forms independently of the incoming photon polarization, completing the constructive chain $\gamma\gamma \to q\bar{q} \to \mathfrak{p}, \mathfrak{n} \to \mathcal{A}(Z, N)$.
