The block decomposition of an [atom](#def:atom-knot) is unique up to normal form rewriting; no positional or temporal ordering is imposed beyond the shortlex-canonical normal form.

Markov-trace invariance guarantees that the closure $\hat K$ forms a well-defined knot or link in $S^3$. No phase or temperature data enters the definition. The atom represents a purely topological object.

Phase, dynamics, and evolution represent separate structures defined on the space of atom-knots. The same atom $K$ may exhibit different confinement levels at different $q$, but $K$ itself remains independent of $q$.
