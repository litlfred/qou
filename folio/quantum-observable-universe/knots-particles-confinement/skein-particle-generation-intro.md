Under the quadratic Hecke skein relation $\sigma_i - \sigma_i^{-1} = (q - q^{-1})\mathbf{1}$, every factorized hadron resolves into a linear combination of torus knots and links $T(m, n)$ together with Borromean three-strand closures. This section constructs the atomic braid $\mathcal{A}(Z, N) \in H_{A+Z}(q)$, proves that the torus family $T(m, n)$ generates the hadron monoid under skein resolution and linking operations, and tabulates the resulting knot–particle dictionary across the lepton, meson, baryon, and gauge sectors.
