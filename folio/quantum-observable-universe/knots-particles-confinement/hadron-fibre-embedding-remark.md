Abstract hadrons encode topological data algebraically. To represent a physical particle, this data must embed into the geometric fibre of the knot bundle over spacetime; a [fibre-compatible hadron embedding](#def:hadron-fibre-embedding) is that embedding.

The fibre-confinement condition is enforced by a finite Möbius energy bound, preventing the knot complement from developing cusps that reach the fibre boundary. The integrability condition ensures the algebraic structure admits a strict geometric realization.
