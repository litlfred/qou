**Conjecture.** Tannaka--Krein duality establishes a self-duality between two perspectives on the aether:

$$
\mathbf{Rep}_{\mathrm{loc}}(A) \simeq \mathbf{Comod}(\mathcal{H}_\xi),
$$

an equivalence of categories lying over the fibre functor $\tau$.

Here, $\mathbf{Rep}_{\mathrm{loc}}(A)$ is the category of localized $A$-module representations, supported at maximal ideals. The category $\mathbf{Comod}(\mathcal{H}_\xi)$ consists of comodules over the Hopf algebra $\mathcal{H}_\xi$ reconstructed from the Reeb spectrum. The geometric side of localized representations at defects matches the spectral side of global quantum group symmetries of the Reeb flow. The fibre functor $\tau$ underlying this equivalence sends each localized module to its fiber at the unit defect. The reconstruction theorem guarantees that $\mathcal{H}_\xi$ is uniquely determined by the monoidal structure of $\mathbf{Rep}_{\mathrm{loc}}(A)$.

**Compatibility with $\tau$ is part of the statement rather than a decoration.** An equivalence that did not commute with the fibre functors would not be the duality claimed here: many inequivalent Hopf algebras have equivalent comodule categories, and it is precisely $\tau$ that separates them. The conjecture therefore states the equivalence and its compatibility as a single condition --- $\tau_{\mathbf{Comod}} \circ e \cong \tau_{\mathbf{Rep}}$ for the same $e$ --- rather than as two independent assertions, which would permit an equivalence and an unrelated natural isomorphism.

**Unconstructed categorical equivalence.** The two categories above are different categories, and nothing in the framework relates them. Abstractly, the duality condition over arbitrary parameter categories is satisfiable --- a category is dual to itself over its own fibre functor --- but that instance is the identity equivalence and establishes only that the condition is satisfiable in principle. No equivalence is yet constructed for $\mathbf{Rep}_{\mathrm{loc}}(A)$ and $\mathbf{Comod}(\mathcal{H}_\xi)$; supplying one is the conjecture.
