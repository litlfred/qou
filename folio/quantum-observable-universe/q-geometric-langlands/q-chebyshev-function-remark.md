To track how defect weight accumulates by norm across the spectral fiber,
one deforms the classical prime-counting apparatus. The [$q$-Chebyshev function](#def:q-chebyshev-function) is this $q$-deformed prime counting function: it accumulates the weights of fundamental defects ordered by norm. The function $\psi_q(X)$ encodes the distribution of matter across the spectral fiber, weighted by the quantum Planck constant. In the classical limit $q \to 1$, we recover the classical Chebyshev function $\psi(X)$.
