To track how defect weight accumulates by norm across the spectral fiber,
one deforms the classical prime-counting apparatus. The :defterm[$q$-Chebyshev function]{#q-chebyshev-function} is the partial sum of the $q$-von Mangoldt
function over the geometry:

$$
\psi_q(X) = \sum_{\substack{\mathfrak{m} \in \mathrm{MaxSpec}(\mathcal{O}_q(\mathcal{B})) \\ N(\mathfrak{m}) \le X}} \Lambda_q(\mathfrak{m}).
$$

This $q$-deformed prime counting function accumulates the weights of fundamental defects ordered by norm. The function $\psi_q(X)$ encodes the distribution of matter across the spectral fiber, weighted by the quantum Planck constant. In the classical limit $q \to 1$, $\psi_q(X)$ recovers the classical Chebyshev function $\psi(X)$.
