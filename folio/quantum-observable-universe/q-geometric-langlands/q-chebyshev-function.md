The :defterm[$q$-Chebyshev function]{#q-chebyshev-function} is the partial sum of the [$q$-von Mangoldt
function](#def:q-von-mangoldt-function) over the geometry:

$$
\psi_q(X) = \sum_{\substack{\mathfrak{m} \in \mathrm{MaxSpec}(\mathcal{O}_q(\mathcal{B})) \\ N(\mathfrak{m}) \le X}} \Lambda_q(\mathfrak{m}).
$$
