For a [$q$-oper](#def:q-oper) in the rank-2 case $\check{G}_q = \mathrm{SU}_{q^{-1}}(2)$, a $q$-oper on a brane curve $X$ is a rank-2 $q$-flat bundle with an invariant line subbundle $\mathcal{L} \subset \mathcal{V}$ satisfying the transversality condition $\nabla_q(\mathcal{L}) \not\subset \mathcal{L} \otimes \Omega^1_q$. This geometric data is equivalent to a $q$-deformed Sturm--Liouville operator $\partial_q^2 + t(z)$, where $t(z) \in H^0(X, (\Omega^1_q)^{\otimes 2})$, providing the rigid spectral data dual to $q$-Hecke eigensheaves.
