Building on the moduli of $q$-local systems, this section constructs the $q$-Hecke operator: a Hecke modification twisted by the Iwahori–Hecke generators $\sigma_i$. This operator acts on the moduli of $G_q$-bundles. A $q$-Hecke eigensheaf diagonalises this action, with eigenvalues landing in the representation ring of the dual group. The rigid fibre functor $\tau$ extracts these eigenvalues as characters, matching the automorphic side to the spectral side in the $q$-Geometric Langlands correspondence.
