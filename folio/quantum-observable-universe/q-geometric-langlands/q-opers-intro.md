This section turns to the spectral side of the conjecture and the canonical objects that realise it. It introduces the $q$-oper: a $\check{G}_q$-bundle on $X$, equipped with a reduction to the Borel subgroup and a $q$-flat connection in oper position. This object provides the rigid spectral data dual to the automorphic $q$-Hecke eigensheaves. The fibre functor maps every $q$-oper to a $q$-Hecke eigensheaf, with the eigenvalue determined by the oper coordinates. This concretely realises the abstract Tannakian mediation.
