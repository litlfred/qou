To measure the arithmetic weight of fundamental defects in a quantum observable universe, the [$q$-von Mangoldt function](#def:q-von-mangoldt-function) deforms the classical von Mangoldt function. The fraction $\frac{N(\mathfrak{m})^{\hbar_q} - 1}{\hbar_q}$ deforms the classical logarithm. As $q \to 1$, we have $\hbar_q \to 0$. L'Hôpital's rule recovers $\Lambda_q(\mathfrak{m}) \to \log N(\mathfrak{m}) = \Lambda(\mathfrak{m})$, restoring the classical von Mangoldt function.
