For $G_q = \mathrm{SU}_q(2)$ ([quantum $\mathrm{SU}(2)$](#def:quantum-su2)), the [$q$-Langlands dual group](#def:q-langlands-dual) is

$$
  \check{G}_q = \mathrm{SU}_{q^{-1}}(2) \cong \mathrm{SO}_{q^{-1}}(3).
$$

The duality relates $\check{G}_q$ to $G_q$ by root system duality together with the inversion $q \mapsto q^{-1}$. The root system of $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ is self-dual. The inversion $q \mapsto q^{-1}$ interchanges the two eigenvalues of the braiding: $q \leftrightarrow -q^{-1}$. This exchanges the symmetric and antisymmetric channels of the Iwahori--Hecke algebra. It represents the categorical manifestation of exchanging roots and coroots. In the $\mathbb{G}_m$-family picture, the Langlands involution is the inversion automorphism $\iota(q) = q^{-1}$. This preserves the torsion subgroups $\mu_n$ and acts as the identity on $\mu_2$.
