The $q$-oper $\nabla_q$ defines a point in $\mathrm{LocSys}_{\check{G}_q}(X)$. Under the conjectured equivalence $\mathbb{L}_q$ ([$q$-GL conjecture](#conj:q-geometric-langlands)), its pre-image $\mathcal{M}_{\nabla_q} = \mathbb{L}_q^{-1}(\delta_{\nabla_q})$ is a $q$-$\mathcal{D}$-module on $\mathrm{Bun}_{G_q}$. Hecke compatibility (property (1) of the conjecture) immediately ensures that $\mathcal{M}_{\nabla_q}$ is an eigensheaf with eigenvalue $\nabla_q$.

To prove irreducibility, suppose $\mathcal{N} \subset \mathcal{M}_{\nabla_q}$ is a non-zero sub-$q$-$\mathcal{D}$-module. The equivalence $\mathbb{L}_q$ maps $\mathcal{N}$ to a non-zero subsheaf of $\delta_{\nabla_q}$. Because skyscraper sheaves are simple, $\mathbb{L}_q(\mathcal{N}) = \delta_{\nabla_q}$. The equivalence then forces $\mathcal{N} = \mathcal{M}_{\nabla_q}$.

Alternatively, irreducibility follows directly from the $q$-oper transversality condition. A proper sub-$q$-$\mathcal{D}$-module would induce a $\nabla_q$-invariant sub-bundle compatible with the Borel reduction. This contradicts the non-vanishing requirement $\mathrm{pr}_{-\alpha_i}(\nabla_q \bmod \check{\mathfrak{b}}_q) \neq 0$. $\square$
