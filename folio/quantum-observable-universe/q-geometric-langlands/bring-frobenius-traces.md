Let $E$ be the projective elliptic curve $y^2 + xy + y = x^3 - x - 2$ (Cremona $50\mathrm{a}1$), and for each prime $p$ set $a_p = p + 1 - |E(\mathbb{F}_p)|$. For the ten good primes $7 \le p \le 41$, the projective point counts determine

$$
  (p, a_p) \in \{(7, 2),\ (11, -3),\ (13, -4),\ (17, -3),\ (19, 5),\ (23, 6),\ (29, 0),\ (31, 2),\ (37, 2),\ (41, -3)\},
$$

and at every listed prime the strict Hasse inequality $a_p^2 < 4p$ holds. Under the cited Jacobian isogeny $J(\mathcal{B}) \sim E^4$ of Bring's curve, each polynomial $(1 - a_p T + pT^2)^4$ therefore has complex-conjugate reciprocal roots of modulus $p^{1/2}$.
