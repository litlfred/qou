**Frobenius pairing.** The bilinear morphism $\langle -, - \rangle_F = \int_A \circ\, \mu \colon A \otimes A \to \mathbf{1}$ obtained by composing multiplication $\mu \colon A \otimes A \to A$ with the Frobenius form $\int_A \colon A \to \mathbf{1}$ (see [Frobenius pairing](#def:frobenius-pairing)). In a quantum observable universe this pairing induces an isomorphism $A \xrightarrow{\sim} A^{\vee}$, making the aether object self-dual and licensing scalar inner products on Hecke and differential modules without auxiliary metric choices.
