**:defterm[Symmetric monoidal category]{#symmetric-monoidal-category}.** A [braided monoidal category](#rem:glossary-braided-monoidal-category) whose braiding is involutive, $\beta_{Y,X} \circ \beta_{X,Y} = \mathrm{id}_{X \otimes Y}$ for all objects $X, Y$. In a symmetric monoidal category over-crossings and under-crossings coincide, factoring the braid group $B_n$ action through the finite symmetric group $S_n$; in QOU this corresponds to the undeformed classical specialization $q = 1$.
