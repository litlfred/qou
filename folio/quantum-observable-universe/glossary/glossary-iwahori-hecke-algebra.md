**Iwahori--Hecke algebra $H_n(q)$.** Over a commutative ring $R$ with unit $q \in R^{\times}$, the quotient $H_n(R, q) = R[B_n] / \langle (\sigma_i - q)(\sigma_i + q^{-1}) \mid 1 \le i \le n - 1 \rangle$ of the group algebra of $B_n$ by the quadratic skein relation $\sigma_i^2 = (q - q^{-1})\sigma_i + \mathbf{1}$ (see [Iwahori--Hecke algebra from braiding](#rem:hecke-algebra-from-braiding)). It is free of rank $n!$ over $R$ with Coxeter normal-form basis $\{T_w : w \in S_n\}$, carries a Graham--Lehrer cellular structure over $\mathbb{Z}[q, q^{-1}]$ indexed by partitions $\lambda \vdash n$, and splits into orthogonal Wedderburn blocks whenever $[n]_q!$ is invertible in $R$ (in particular for every real $q > 0$).
