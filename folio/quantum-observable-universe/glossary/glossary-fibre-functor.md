**Fibre functor $\tau$.** The faithful $R$-linear monoidal functor $\tau \colon \mathbf{C} \to R\text{-}\mathbf{Mod}$ forming the third component of a [quantum universe](#def:quantum-universe) $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$. Physically, $\tau$ serves as an observation frame extracting concrete $R$-module states and scalar traces from morphisms of $\mathbf{C}$. By Tannaka--Krein reconstruction, the tensor automorphisms $G = \mathrm{Aut}^{\otimes}(\tau)$ form the quantum gauge group, and the collection of all fibre functors forms an affine $G$-gerbe (see [fibre-functor atlas](#prop:fibre-functor-atlas)).
