**Crossing energy $\mathfrak{E}_{\mathrm{cross}}$.** The signed skein weight functional assigning $+q$ to each positive crossing ($L_+$) and $-q^{-1}$ to each negative crossing ($L_-$) in a braid representative $\beta$ of a linked knot composite (see [crossing energy](#def:crossing-energy)). Its per-crossing asymmetry $q - q^{-1} > 0$ at $q > 1$ breaks mirror symmetry between chiral partners and drives electromagnetic Coulomb attraction and repulsion in composite braids.
