**Quantum observable universe (QOU).** A [quantum universe](#def:quantum-universe) $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ over $R$ satisfying five structural axioms (see [quantum observable universe](#def:quantum-observable-universe)): rigidity of $\mathbf{C}$, non-degeneracy of the Frobenius pairing $A \xrightarrow{\sim} A^{\vee}$, observability ($\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathbf{C}}(\mathbf{1}, \mathrm{Ad}(A)) = 0$, ruling out hidden global symmetries), measurability ($c_1(\mathrm{Ad}(A)) = c_2(\mathrm{Ad}(A)) = 0$), and the categorical volume constraint ($\Lambda^2 V \xrightarrow{\sim} \mathbf{1}$ on the fundamental object $V$). Rigidity supplies antiparticle duals and closes braid strands into knots, while observability and the volume constraint force all gauge-invariant observables to factor as rational or Habiro-completed functions of the aether parameter $q$.
