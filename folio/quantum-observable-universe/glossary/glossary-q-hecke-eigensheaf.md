**$q$-Hecke eigensheaf.** A $q$-$\mathcal{D}$-module $\mathcal{M}$ on the moduli stack $\mathrm{Bun}_{G_q}(X)$ equipped with functorial isomorphisms $\mathbf{T}_{x,\lambda}^{(q)}(\mathcal{M}) \simeq \mathcal{M} \otimes V_\lambda^{(q)}(\mathcal{L}_q)_x$ for every closed point $x \in X$ and dominant coweight $\lambda$, indexed by a $\check{G}_q$-valued [$q$-local system](#def:q-local-system) $\mathcal{L}_q$ (see [$q$-Hecke eigensheaf](#def:q-hecke-eigensheaf)). The eigensheaf isomorphisms respect braided tensor products of representations $V_\lambda^{(q)}$ and commute at distinct points $x \neq y$ up to $O(q - 1)$ curvature terms, forming the automorphic side of the $q$-geometric Langlands correspondence.
