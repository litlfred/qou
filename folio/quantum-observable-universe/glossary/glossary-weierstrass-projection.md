**Weierstrass projection $\wp_q(z)$.** Associated to the Descartes elliptic curve $\mathcal{E}_q$ with Weierstrass coefficients $p(q), r(q)$ and period lattice $\Lambda_q \subset \mathbb{C}$ (see [Descartes elliptic curve](#prop:descartes-elliptic-curve)), the :defterm[Weierstrass projection]{#weierstrass-projection} is the doubly periodic meromorphic function $\wp_q \colon \mathbb{C}/\Lambda_q \to \mathbb{P}^1$ satisfying

$$
  \wp_q'(z)^2 = 4\wp_q(z)^3 + 4p(q)\wp_q(z) + 4r(q).
$$

The map $z \mapsto [\wp_q(z) : \wp_q'(z) : 1]$ uniformizes $\mathcal{E}_q$ by its complex torus $\mathbb{C}/\Lambda_q$; throughout this monograph the glyph $\wp$ (or $\wp_q$) is reserved exclusively for this elliptic projection.
