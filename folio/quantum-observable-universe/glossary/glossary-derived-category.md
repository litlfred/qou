**:defterm[Derived category]{#derived-category}.** For an abelian category $\mathbf{A}$, the Verdier localization $D(\mathbf{A})$ of the category of cochain complexes in $\mathbf{A}$ (or of its homotopy category) at the class of quasi-isomorphisms, which carries a triangulated structure generated by [distinguished triangles](#rem:glossary-distinguished-triangle) coming from short exact sequences of complexes. The bounded derived category $D^b(\mathbf{A})$ restricts to complexes with bounded cohomology, and $D^b(X)$ for a scheme or variety $X$ denotes $D^b(\mathbf{Coh}(X))$.
