**:defterm[$R$-linear category]{#r-linear-category}.** A category $\mathbf{C}$ enriched over the category of modules over a commutative ring $R$: every hom-set $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathbf{C}}(X, Y)$ is an $R$-module and composition $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathbf{C}}(Y, Z) \times \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathbf{C}}(X, Y) \to \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathbf{C}}(X, Z)$ is $R$-bilinear. In a monoidal $R$-linear category, the tensor product of morphisms is likewise required to be $R$-bilinear.
