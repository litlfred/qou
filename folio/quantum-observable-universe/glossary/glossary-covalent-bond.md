**Covalent bond.** A bond of order $k_b$ between atoms $i$ and $j$ in a molecular braid, realized by Hopf-linking $k_b$ shared electron trefoils $T_{2,3}$ simultaneously across protons of both nuclei via the attractive crossing operator $L_b = \prod_{e=1}^{k_b} \sigma_{g_e(\mathfrak{p}_j)}^{-1} \cdot \sigma_{g_e(\mathfrak{p}_i)}^{-1}$ (see [shared-trefoil covalent bond](#def:covalent-bond-shared-trefoil)). Each shared electron contributes two negative ($\sigma^{-1}$) electromagnetic crossings, one anchored at each nucleus, lowering the Markov-trace free energy relative to separated neutral atoms.
