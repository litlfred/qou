**Geer--Patureau-Mirand--Turaev (GPMT) modified trace.** A non-degenerate cyclic family of trace functionals $\mathsf{t}_I$ defined on ambidextrous projective tensor ideals $I$ of a pivotal or ribbon category whose categorical quantum dimension $\dim_q(V) = \mathrm{tr}(\mathrm{id}_V)$ vanishes (see [Iwahori--Hecke algebra from braiding](#rem:hecke-algebra-from-braiding)). When a cellular Specht or tilting block has $\dim_q(\lambda) = 0$ (such as the antisymmetric block $\lambda = (1^3)$ at $\mathrm{SU}_q(2)$ rank $N = 2$, or at roots of unity), the modified trace extracts finite, non-zero link and $3$-manifold invariants without dividing by vanishing quantum dimensions.
