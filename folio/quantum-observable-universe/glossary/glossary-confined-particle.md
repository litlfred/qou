**Confined particle and confinement level.** A *confined particle* is a quadruple $P = (\mathrm{name}, \ell, [K]_M, n_0)$ recording a particle label, an integer *confinement level* $\ell \ge 0$ ($0$ for quarks, $1$ for nucleons, $2$ for atoms, $3$ for molecules), a Markov-equivalence class $[K]_M$ represented by its Gröbner--Shirshov normal form in $H_{n_0}(q)$, and a minimal coarse strand count $n_0$ (see [confined particle](#def:confined-particle)). The level $\ell$ indexes the ascending *confinement filtration* $J^\ell(q) \subset H_n(R, q)$ generated by Wedderburn blocks whose operator energy satisfies $E(\lambda, q) \le E_\ell(q)$ (see [confinement filtration](#def:confinement-level)).
