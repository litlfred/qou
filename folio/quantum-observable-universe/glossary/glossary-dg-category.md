**:defterm[Differential graded category]{#dg-category}.** A category enriched over cochain complexes of $R$-modules: each hom-object $\mathrm{Hom}(X, Y)$ is a cochain complex, composition $\mathrm{Hom}(Y, Z) \otimes_R \mathrm{Hom}(X, Y) \to \mathrm{Hom}(X, Z)$ is a chain map, and identity morphisms are degree-zero cocycles. Passing to zeroth cohomology $H^0(\mathrm{Hom}(X, Y))$ recovers the underlying homotopy category, while retaining chain-level hom-complexes provides canonical enhancements of [derived categories](#rem:glossary-derived-category) and functorial Hochschild homology.
