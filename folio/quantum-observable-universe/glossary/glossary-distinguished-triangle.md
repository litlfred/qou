**:defterm[Distinguished triangle]{#distinguished-triangle}.** In an additive category $\mathbf{T}$ equipped with an autoequivalence shift $X \mapsto X[1]$, a diagram $X \to Y \to Z \to X[1]$ belonging to the distinguished class of a triangulated structure: closed under isomorphism and rotation, containing $X \xrightarrow{\mathrm{id}} X \to 0 \to X[1]$, extending every morphism $X \to Y$ by a cone $Z$, and satisfying the octahedral axiom. Distinguished triangles serve as the homotopy-invariant counterparts of short exact sequences and induce long exact sequences under homological functors.
