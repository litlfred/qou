**Aeon and CMB boundary datum.** An *aeon* is a connected component of the observable bundle category $\mathrm{Bun}^{\circ}(\mathbb{P}(V))$, uniting all causal light-cone diagrams reachable by zigzags of flow morphisms without crossing a jet horizon (see [aeon](#def:aeon)). At an initial big-bang boundary state $\mathcal{S}_0$ where degree-$0$ data vanishes ($A_0|_{\mathcal{S}_0} = 0$), the *cosmic microwave background (CMB) boundary datum* $\mathrm{CMB}(\mathcal{S}_0) = (\Gamma(U, A_1/A_0)|_{\mathcal{S}_0},\, \Gamma(U, A_2/A_0)|_{\mathcal{S}_0})$ records the surviving degree-$1$ transport and degree-$2$ curvature jet classes (see [CMB boundary datum](#def:cmb-boundary-datum)).
