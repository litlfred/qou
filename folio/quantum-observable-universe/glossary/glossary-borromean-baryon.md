**Borromean baryon (proton and neutron).** A three-constituent hadron $B = \mathcal{B}_3(K_{0_1}, K_{0_1}, K_{0_1}) \oplus \mathcal{L}_H$ formed by threading three quark strands through the three colour tubes $(r, g, b)$ of Bring's surface via the six-crossing Borromean braid $\mathcal{B}_3 = (\sigma_0\sigma_1^{-1})^3$ (see [Borromean baryon](#def:borromean-baryon)). Because the Borromean link is Brunnian, deleting any strand unlinks the remaining two, enforcing topological colour confinement without free single-quark asymptotic states; the $2 + 1$ chirality partition $\mathcal{B}_3(\mathfrak{u}, \mathfrak{u}, \mathfrak{d})$ defines the *proton* $\mathfrak{p}$ (see [categorical proton](#def:categorical-proton)) and the $1 + 2$ partition $\mathcal{B}_3(\mathfrak{u}, \mathfrak{d}, \mathfrak{d})$ defines the *neutron* $\mathfrak{n}$ (see [categorical neutron](#def:categorical-neutron)).
