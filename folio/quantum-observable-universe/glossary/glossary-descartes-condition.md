**Descartes condition.** The geometric selection condition requiring the base curve $M_0$ of an archimedean realization of a quantum observable universe (with $6 \in R^{\times}$) to have genus $g(M_0) = 4$, equivalently topological Euler characteristic $\chi_{\mathrm{eu}}(M_0) = 2 - 2g(M_0) = -6$ (see [Descartes condition](#def:archimedean-condition)). A quantum observable universe satisfying this condition is called a *Descartes universe*; the genus-four base forces six branch points in geometric descent and matches the minimal hyperbolic $\mathbb{Z}/3$ Kummer cover saturating three-strand Seifert cohomology.
