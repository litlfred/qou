**:defterm[Monoidal functor]{#monoidal-functor}.** A functor $F \colon \mathbf{C} \to \mathbf{D}$ between [monoidal categories](#rem:glossary-monoidal-category) equipped with coherence morphisms $\mu_{X,Y} \colon F(X) \otimes F(Y) \to F(X \otimes Y)$ and $\epsilon \colon \mathbf{1}_{\mathbf{D}} \to F(\mathbf{1}_{\mathbf{C}})$ compatible with the associators and unitors. The functor is *lax monoidal* when $\mu_{X,Y}$ and $\epsilon$ are arbitrary morphisms, and *strong monoidal* when both are isomorphisms (as required for the fibre functor $\tau$).
