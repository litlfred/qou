**:defterm[Braided monoidal category]{#braided-monoidal-category}.** A [monoidal category](#rem:glossary-monoidal-category) $\mathbf{C}$ equipped with a natural braiding isomorphism $\beta_{X,Y} \colon X \otimes Y \xrightarrow{\sim} Y \otimes X$ satisfying the two hexagon coherence identities. Unlike a symmetric braiding, $\beta_{Y,X} \circ \beta_{X,Y}$ need not equal $\mathrm{id}_{X \otimes Y}$, so iterated braidings generate representations of Artin's braid group $B_n$ on $n$-fold tensor powers.
