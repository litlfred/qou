**:defterm[Pivotal category]{#pivotal-category}.** A [rigid monoidal category](#rem:glossary-rigid-category) equipped with a monoidal natural isomorphism $\delta_X \colon X \xrightarrow{\sim} X^{\vee\vee}$ identifying each object with its double dual. The pivotal isomorphism allows both left and right categorical traces $\mathrm{tr}_L(f), \mathrm{tr}_R(f) \in \mathrm{End}_{\mathbf{C}}(\mathbf{1})$ to be defined on any endomorphism $f \colon X \to X$; when $\mathrm{tr}_L(f) = \mathrm{tr}_R(f)$ for every $X$ and $f$, the pivotal category is called *spherical*.
