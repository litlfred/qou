**Categorical Dirac operator $\mathcal{D}_\nabla$.** For a categorical connection $\nabla$, aether parameter $q$, and Reeb derivation $\xi$, the morphism $\mathcal{D}_\nabla = q^{-1}\nabla + (1 - q^{-1})\iota_\xi\nabla \colon A \to \Omega^1(A)$ (see [categorical Dirac operator](#def:categorical-dirac-operator)). It interpolates between the full covariant derivative $\nabla$ at $q = 1$ and the purely vertical contact derivative $\iota_\xi\nabla$ as $q \to \infty$, governing both the bosonic curvature square $\mathcal{D}_\nabla^2$ and fermionic matter coupling.
