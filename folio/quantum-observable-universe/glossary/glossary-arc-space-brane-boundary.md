**Arc space and brane boundary.** For a singular locus or exceptional variety $X$ in $\mathbf{C}$, the *$n$-th jet scheme* $\mathcal{L}_n(X)$ represents $\mathrm{Spec}\,R[t]/(t^{n+1})$-valued infinitesimal arcs, and the *arc space* is the projective limit $\mathcal{L}(X) = \varprojlim_n \mathcal{L}_n(X)$ (see [arc space](#def:arc-space)). Within the recursive brane tower $\{A_n\}_{n \ge 0}$, the *defining boundary* $\partial A_n$ consists of subobjects $S \hookrightarrow A_n$ on which the restriction morphism $r_n \colon A_n \to A_{n-1}$ has a non-trivial kernel (see [defining brane boundary](#def:brane-boundary)).
