**Symmetric group $S_3$.** Taking the quotient of the three-strand braid group $B_3$ (see [three-strand braid group](#def:braid-group)) by the involution relations $\sigma_i^2 = \mathrm{id}$ yields the :defterm[symmetric group $S_3$]{#s3-group}, presented as $S_3 = \langle s_1, s_2 \mid s_1^2 = s_2^2 = (s_1 s_2)^3 = \mathrm{id} \rangle$ of order $6$. In QOU, $S_3$ acts as the colour-triality permutation group permuting the three colour tubes $(r, g, b)$ on Bring's surface $\Sigma_4$, and represents the $q \to 1$ specialization of the three-strand Iwahori--Hecke algebra $H_3(q)$.
