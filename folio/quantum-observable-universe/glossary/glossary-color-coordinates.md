**Color sector and color tube coordinates.** On the exceptional divisor $\mathcal{S}_p$, the *color sector coordinates* $(\mathfrak{r}, \mathfrak{g}, \mathfrak{b})$ embed three $q$-twisted spherical sectors separated by $2\pi/3$ phase shifts in the azimuthal angle $\psi$ (see [colour sector coordinates](#def:color-sector-coords)). On Bring's surface $\Sigma_4$ with Lagrange resolvents $w_0, \dots, w_4$ and spacetime ratio $z = x_0/x_1$, the *color tube coordinates* are the shifted spectral differences $c_r = w_2 - z$, $c_g = w_3 - z$, and $c_b = w_4 - z$, paired with the spacelike shift $\sigma = w_1 - z$ (see [colour tube coordinates](#def:color-tube-coordinates)).
