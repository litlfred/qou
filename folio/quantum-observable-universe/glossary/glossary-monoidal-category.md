**:defterm[Monoidal category]{#monoidal-category}.** A category $\mathbf{C}$ equipped with a tensor bifunctor $\otimes \colon \mathbf{C} \times \mathbf{C} \to \mathbf{C}$, a unit object $\mathbf{1}$, an associator natural isomorphism $\alpha_{X,Y,Z} \colon (X \otimes Y) \otimes Z \xrightarrow{\sim} X \otimes (Y \otimes Z)$, and left and right unitors $\lambda_X \colon \mathbf{1} \otimes X \xrightarrow{\sim} X$ and $\rho_X \colon X \otimes \mathbf{1} \xrightarrow{\sim} X$ satisfying Mac Lane's pentagon and triangle coherence axioms.
