**Computational horizon.** For a closed braid $L = \widehat{\beta}$ encoding $N$ Toffoli gates across $c = 3N$ crossings, a jet horizon $\mathcal{H}_c \subset \mathcal{R}(L)$ in the $3^c$-leaf skein resolution tree where witness verification separates exponentially from full trace evaluation (see [computational horizon](#def:computational-horizon)). Degree-$1$ single-path witnesses verify in $O(\mathrm{poly}(c))$ Planck steps, whereas extracting the degree-$0$ Markov-trace decision observable $P_L(q_0)$ across the finite information bandwidth $\mathfrak{B}(q)$ requires $\Omega(3^{c - c_0})$ steps whenever destructive interference fails to compress the tree.
