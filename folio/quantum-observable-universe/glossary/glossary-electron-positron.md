**Electron and positron.** In the lepton sector (outside the colour tubes of $\Sigma_4$), the *electron* $e^-$ is the positive trefoil torus knot $T_{2,3} = \widehat{\sigma_1^3}$ (see [electron trefoil](#def:electron-knot)), and the *positron* $e^+$ is its mirror trefoil $T_{2,3}^\sigma = \widehat{\sigma_1^{-3}}$ with reversed crossing chirality (see [positron mirror trefoil](#def:positron-knot)). Reversing all three crossings flips electric charge between $Q = -1$ and $Q = +1$ and reverses orientation along the Reeb flow, while the electron mass $m_e$ serves as the dimensional leptonic scale anchor (CAL-1).
