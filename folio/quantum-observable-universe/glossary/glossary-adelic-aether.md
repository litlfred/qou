**Adelic aether $\mathbb{A}$.** The restricted direct product $\mathbb{A} = \mathbb{R} \times \prod_p' \mathbb{Q}_p$ uniting the archimedean completion $\mathbb{R}$ with all non-archimedean $p$-adic completions $\mathbb{Q}_p$ whose components lie in $\mathbb{Z}_p$ for almost all primes $p$ (see [adelic aether](#def:adelic-substrate)). In QOU, each finite prime $p$ packages root-of-unity Kummer-level evaluations of the aether while the archimedean factor carries real period and CODATA evaluations, linked globally through Habiro cyclotomic completions.
