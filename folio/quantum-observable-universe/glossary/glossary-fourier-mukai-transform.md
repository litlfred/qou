**:defterm[Fourier–Mukai transform]{#fourier-mukai-transform}.** Given smooth projective varieties $X, Y$ and a kernel object $K \in D^b(X \times Y)$ in the bounded [derived category](#rem:glossary-derived-category), the exact integral functor $\Phi_K \colon D^b(X) \to D^b(Y)$ defined by $\Phi_K(\mathcal{F}) = Rp_{Y*}(p_X^*\mathcal{F} \otimes^L K)$ along the projections $p_X, p_Y$. By Orlov's representability theorem \cite{orlov1997}, every fully faithful exact functor between bounded derived categories of smooth projective varieties arises from a unique Fourier--Mukai kernel, a paradigm extended to $q$-Hecke integral transforms in the $q$-geometric Langlands programme.
