**Categorical Reeb derivation $\xi$.** The canonical endomorphism $\xi \colon A \to A$ generating the action of the maximal torus $T \subset G$ on the aether object via the adjoint coaction (see [categorical Reeb derivation](#def:categorical-reeb)). Dual to the contact $1$-form $\varTheta_A \in \Omega^1(A)$ normalized by $\varTheta_A(\xi) = \mathrm{id}_{\mathbf{1}}$, contraction with $\xi$ splits differential forms into horizontal forms $\Omega^k_H(A) = \ker(\iota_\xi)$ and vertical forms $\varTheta_A \wedge \Omega^{k-1}_H(A)$, singling out the commutative observation axis and the arrow of time.
