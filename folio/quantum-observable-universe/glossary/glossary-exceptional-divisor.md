**Exceptional divisor $\mathcal{S}_p$.** The projective sphere $\mathcal{S}_p \cong \mathbb{P}^1(\mathbb{C})$ inserted at a singular brane or strand-collision point $p$ by blowing up its tangent-normal frame (see [exceptional divisor](#def:exceptional-divisor)). Parameterized by spherical angles $(\phi, \psi)$, $\mathcal{S}_p$ resolves pointlike interaction vertices into smooth projectivized tangent directions on which the three colour sector embeddings and Reeb fibres reside.
