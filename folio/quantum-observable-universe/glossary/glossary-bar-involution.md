**Bar involution.** The Kazhdan--Lusztig ring anti-involution $\overline{(\cdot)} \colon H_n(q) \to H_n(q)$ defined by $\overline{\sigma_i} = \sigma_i^{-1}$ and $\overline{q} = q^{-1}$ (see [bar-involution](#def:bar-involution)). Topologically it reflects braid diagrams by exchanging over-crossings and under-crossings; algebraically its eigenspaces define the bar-even ($\cosh$-like, binding and mass) projection $P_{\mathrm{avg}}$ and the bar-odd ($\sinh$-like, chiral electroweak and $\mathfrak{Q}_\beta$) projection $P_{\mathrm{skew}}$.
