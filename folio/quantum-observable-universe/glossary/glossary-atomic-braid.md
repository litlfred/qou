**Atomic braid and derived atom.** For a neutral nuclide $(Z, N)$ of mass number $A = Z + N$, the *atomic braid* $\mathcal{A} \in H_{A+Z}(q)$ assembles $Z$ protons, $N$ neutrons, and $Z$ electrons on $A + Z$ strands (distinct from the $3A$-strand electron-free nuclear core $\mathcal{B}_K \in H_{3A}(q)$), ordered by minimizing nuclear crossing energy and greedily pairing each electron before a proton (see [atomic braid](#def:atomic-braid)). The *derived atom* $\mathcal{A}(Z, N)$ constructs the composite nuclide from scratch in six algebraic stages: four-term photon pair production, quark selection, Borromean confinement, linear-programming gluon stabilization, inter-nucleon assembly, and Hopf-linked electronic shell attachment, reduced inductively to normal form (see [derived atom](#def:derived-atom)).
