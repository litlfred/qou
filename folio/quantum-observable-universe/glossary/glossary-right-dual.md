**:defterm[Right dual]{#right-dual}.** For an object $X$ in a monoidal category, an object $Y = X^{\vee}$ equipped with an [exact pairing](#rem:glossary-exact-pairing) $(X, Y)$ consisting of $\mathrm{ev} \colon X \otimes Y \to \mathbf{1}$ and $\mathrm{coev} \colon \mathbf{1} \to Y \otimes X$. In QOU, passage from an object $V$ to its dual $V^{\vee}$ reverses strand orientation and exchanges particle representations with their antiparticle duals.
