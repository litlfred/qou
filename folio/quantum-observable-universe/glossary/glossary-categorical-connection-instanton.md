**Categorical connection and instanton.** A *categorical connection* on the aether object $A$ is a morphism $\nabla \colon A \to A \otimes \Omega^1(A)$ obeying the Leibniz rule $\nabla(\mu(a \otimes b)) = \nabla(a) \cdot b + a \cdot \nabla(b)$ (see [categorical connection](#def:categorical-connection)). In a quantum observable universe equipped with the $q$-Hodge star $\star_q$ on $2$-forms, $\nabla$ is a *categorical instanton* when its curvature $F_\nabla \in \Omega^2(A)$ is $\star_q$-self-dual, $\star_q F_\nabla = F_\nabla$ (see [categorical instanton](#def:categorical-instanton)).
