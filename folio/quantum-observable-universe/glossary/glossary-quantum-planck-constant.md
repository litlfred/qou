**Quantum Planck constant $\hbar_q$.** The dimensionless coupling $\hbar_q = 1 - q^{-1} = (q - 1)/q \in R$ measuring the departure of the aether parameter $q$ from the classical limit $q = 1$ (see [quantum Planck constant](#def:quantum-planck-constant)). It appears directly as the vertical Reeb weight in the categorical Dirac operator and $q$-exterior derivative (see [Reeb coefficient identity](#prop:reeb-coefficient-is-hbar-q)), and its square $\hbar_q^2$ governs the geometric convergence rate of the recursive brane-tower expansion.
