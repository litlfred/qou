**Neutrino and antineutrino.** The *neutrino* $\nu$ is the positively oriented closed unknot $K_{0_1}$ with crossing number $0$, Seifert genus $0$, and trivial Jones polynomial $J_\nu(q) = 1$ (see [neutrino unknot](#def:neutrino-knot)), while the *antineutrino* $\nu^\sigma$ is the oppositely oriented mirror unknot $T_{1,0}^\sigma$ (see [antineutrino mirror unknot](#def:antineutrino-knot)). Because the unknot is amphichiral and lacks crossings, both states are electrically neutral ($Q = 0$) and carry zero hyperbolic volume, acquiring mass solely through $(q - 1)$ vacuum drag and separating in $\beta$-decay only by Reeb strand orientation.
