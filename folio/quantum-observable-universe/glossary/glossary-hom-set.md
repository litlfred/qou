**:defterm[Hom-set]{#hom-set}.** For objects $X$ and $Y$ in a category $\mathbf{C}$, the hom-set $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathbf{C}}(X, Y)$ is the collection of all morphisms from $X$ to $Y$. It is also written $\mathbf{C}(X, Y)$ or $X \to Y$. In a locally small category, each hom-set is a genuine set.

When $\mathbf{C}$ is $R$-linear, each hom-set carries the structure of an $R$-module. Composition is $R$-bilinear.
