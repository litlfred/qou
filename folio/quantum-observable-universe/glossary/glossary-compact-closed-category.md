**:defterm[Compact closed category]{#compact-closed-category}.** A [symmetric monoidal category](#rem:glossary-symmetric-monoidal-category) that is also [rigid](#rem:glossary-rigid-category). Because the involutive braiding canonically identifies $X \otimes Y \cong Y \otimes X$, left and right duals coincide and every compact closed category carries a canonical spherical pivotal structure.
