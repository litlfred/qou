**Fermion--boson decomposition.** The split short exact sequence $0 \to \mathcal{H}_{q,H}^k \to \mathcal{H}_q^k \xrightarrow{\pi_V} \mathcal{H}_{q,V}^k \to 0$ on the harmonic particle space $\mathcal{H}_q^k = \ker \Delta_q \subset \Omega^k(A)$ induced by the Reeb weight projections $\pi_H$ and $\pi_V$ (see [fermion--boson decomposition](#def:fermion-boson-decomposition)). Harmonic states in the purely horizontal kernel $\mathcal{H}_{q,H}^k$ ($\alpha_V = 0$) are *bosons*, whereas states carrying a non-zero vertical Reeb component in $\mathcal{H}_{q,V}^k$ ($\alpha_V \neq 0$) are *fermions*.
