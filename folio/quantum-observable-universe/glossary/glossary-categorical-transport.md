**Categorical transport equation.** For a quantum observable universe $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ with instanton $\nabla$ (see [categorical instanton](#def:categorical-instanton)), $q$-exterior derivative $d_q$ (see [categorical $q$-exterior derivative](#def:categorical-q-ext-deriv)), and first jet object $J^1(A)$ (see [categorical jet bundle](#def:categorical-jet)), the :defterm[categorical transport equation]{#categorical-transport} on a section $\psi \in \tau(J^1(A))$ is

$$
  \hbar_q\,\pi_V(\nabla\psi) = \hat{H}_q(\psi),
$$

where $\hat{H}_q = -\tfrac{1}{2}\hbar_q\Delta_q + V_q$ is the $q$-Hamiltonian endomorphism with curvature potential $V_q$. It identifies vertical covariant evolution along the Reeb fibre with action of $\hat{H}_q$, furnishing the categorical precursor of the Schrödinger and Madelung transport equations.
