**Frobenius--Hopf aether.** The distinguished object $(A, \mu, \eta, \Delta, \varepsilon, S)$ of a quantum universe in a braided monoidal category $\mathbf{C}$, equipped with a Frobenius functional $\int_A \colon A \to \mathbf{1}$ (see [Frobenius--Hopf aether](#def:frobenius-hopf-substrate)). It combines an associative unital algebra structure $(\mu, \eta)$, a coassociative counital coalgebra structure $(\Delta, \varepsilon)$, and an involutive braided anti-automorphism $S \colon A \to A$ with the Frobenius law, furnishing the non-commutative coordinate ring of the categorical vacuum prior to spacetime projection.
