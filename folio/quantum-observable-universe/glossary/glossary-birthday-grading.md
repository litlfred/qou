**Birthday grading.** On a $K$-linear category $\mathbf{C}$ over Conway's surreal field $K = \mathbf{No}$, the ordinal-indexed filtration $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathbf{C}}(X, Y)_{\le \alpha}$ consisting of morphisms whose surreal coefficients all have Conway birthday $b(x) \le \alpha \in \mathrm{Ord}$ (see [birthday grading](#def:birthday-grading)). Because real numbers satisfy $b(x) \le \omega$ while infinitesimals $\varepsilon$ and transfinite rates $\omega, \omega^2$ occupy higher ordinal stages, the birthday filtration refines the archimedean norm and tracks asymptotic orders algebraically across the $q \to 1$ and $q \to \infty$ regimes.
