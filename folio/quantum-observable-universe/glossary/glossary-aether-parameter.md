**Aether parameter $q$.** The invertible scalar $q \in R^{\times} \cong \mathrm{End}_{\mathbf{C}}(\mathbf{1})^{\times}$ governing both the Hecke braiding eigenvalue $(\sigma - q)(\sigma + q^{-1}) = 0$ and the interpolation between horizontal and vertical differentials in the non-commutative de Rham complex (see [aether parameter](#def:substrate-parameter)). The specialization $q = 1$ recovers undeformed symmetric braiding and classical differential geometry, whereas $|q| > 1$ breaks parity symmetry between over- and under-crossings and generates positive crossing energy.
