**Quantum flatness.** For a quantum observable universe $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ with quantum gauge group $G_q = \mathrm{Aut}^{\otimes}(\tau)$ (see [quantum gauge group](#def:quantum-gauge-group)) and inner-automorphism $2$-group $\mathrm{INN}(G_q)$, a connection $\nabla$ on $A$ satisfies :refterm[quantum flatness]{#quantum-flatness} when its curvature $2$-form equals the $2$-connection $B$-field datum, $F_\nabla = B$ (see [quantum flatness](#def:quantum-flatness)). Rather than an independent postulate, $F_\nabla = B$ follows from combining the $\star_q$-self-dual instanton equation $\star_q F_\nabla = F_\nabla$ (see [categorical instanton](#def:categorical-instanton)) with the simplicity constraint $B = F_\nabla^+$, eliminating any independent $2$-connection degree of freedom.
