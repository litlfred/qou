**Algebra without quasi-zeros.** Let $R$ be a commutative unital ring and $A$ an $R$-algebra. An element $a \in A$ is a *quasi-zero* if $bac = 0$ for all $b, c \in A$ not both equal to $1$, and $A$ is :defterm[without quasi-zeros]{#no-quasi-zeros} when no non-zero element has this property \cite{leitnerpawloski2004}. Assuming the ground ring and hom-algebras have no quasi-zeros guarantees well-defined leading-term decompositions in Gröbner--Shirshov reduction and rules out phantom morphisms annihilated by all two-sided compositions.
