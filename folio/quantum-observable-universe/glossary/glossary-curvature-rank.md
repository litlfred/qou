**Curvature-rank $r(T_w)$.** For a Hecke basis element $T_w \in H_n(q)$, the smallest non-negative integer $r$ such that the $r$-fold iterated curvature operator maps $T_w$ into the filtration stratum $J^r(q) \setminus J^{r+1}(q)$ (see [curvature-rank](#def:curvature-rank)). Combinatorially, $r(T_w)$ counts the minimal number of Gröbner--Shirshov quadratic reduction steps needed to bring $T_w$ into canonical normal form.
