**$z$-Habiro ring and $(q,z)$-bicompletion.** The *$z$-Habiro ring* $\widehat{\mathbb{Z}[q^{\pm 1}][z]}_z = \varprojlim_n \mathbb{Z}[q^{\pm 1}][z] / (P_n(q, z))$ completes Laurent polynomials along the shifted Pochhammer ideals $P_n(q, z) = \prod_{k=1}^n (1 - q^{-k}z)$, providing an algebraic home for infinite $q$-Pochhammer brane-tower products (see [$z$-Habiro ring](#def:z-habiro-ring)). Combining these ideals with the cyclotomic $q$-Pochhammer ideals $(q; q)_m$ yields the bigraded *$(q, z)$-bicompletion* $\widehat{\mathbb{Z}[q^{\pm 1}, z]}_{q,z} = \varprojlim_{(m,n)} \mathbb{Z}[q^{\pm 1}, z] / ((q; q)_m P_n(q, z))$, which unifies root-of-unity $q$-expansions with $z$-tower series (see [$(q,z)$-bicompletion](#def:qz-bicompletion)).
