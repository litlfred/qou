**Categorical $q$-exterior derivative $d_q$.** The first-order differential operator $d_q = q^{-1}d + (1 - q^{-1})\pi_V \circ d \colon \Omega^k(A) \to \Omega^{k+1}(A)$ on the non-commutative de Rham complex of the aether object $A$ (see [categorical $q$-exterior derivative](#def:categorical-q-ext-deriv)). It scales the horizontal differential by $q^{-1}$ while preserving full weight along the vertical Reeb projection $\pi_V$, and together with its formal adjoint $\delta_q$ defines the categorical $q$-Laplacian $\Delta_q = d_q\delta_q + \delta_q d_q$.
