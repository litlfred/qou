**Observed spacetime value.** Given colour tube coordinates $(\mathbf{r}_z, \mathbf{g}_z, \mathbf{b}_z)$ at a point of Bring's surface $\Sigma_4$ (see [colour tube fibres](#def:color-tube-fibers) and [smoothness of Bring's surface](#rem:brings-smoothness)), the :defterm[observed spacetime value]{#observed-value} is the affine coordinate $z \in \mathbb{A}^1$ recovered from the Vieta linear relation

$$
  \mathbf{r}_z + \mathbf{g}_z + \mathbf{b}_z + 4z + 1 = 0.
$$

This polynomial projection $(\mathbf{r}_z, \mathbf{g}_z, \mathbf{b}_z) \mapsto z$ extracts the base spacetime coordinate directly from the colour fibre data, including at chiral branch points where sheets coalesce onto the exceptional $\mathbb{P}^1(\mathbb{C})$.
