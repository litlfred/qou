**$q$-Navier--Stokes equation.** The dissipative brane-fluid evolution law $\partial_t v + \mu \circ (v \otimes \nabla_q v) = -\rho^{-1}d_q p - d_q Q_q + \tfrac{1}{2}(1 - q^{-1})\Delta_q v$ coupled to $q$-incompressibility $\delta_q v = 0$ on differential forms of the aether object $A$ (see [$q$-deformed Navier--Stokes equation](#def:q-navier-stokes)). Here $Q_q$ is the Madelung quantum potential and $\nu_q = \tfrac{1}{2}\hbar_q = \tfrac{1}{2}(1 - q^{-1})$ is the intrinsic kinematic viscosity generated by non-commutativity of the aether at $q > 1$.
