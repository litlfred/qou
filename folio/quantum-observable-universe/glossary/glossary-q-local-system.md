**$q$-local system and $q$-oper.** On a brane curve $X$, a *$q$-local system* with structure group $G_q$ is a functor $\mathcal{L}_q \colon \Pi_1(X) \to \mathbf{Rep}(G_q)$ whose monodromy around any contractible loop $\varrho$ obeys $\mathcal{L}_q(\varrho) = \mathrm{id} + (q - 1)\Omega_q(\varrho)$ with $q$-holonomy residue $\Omega_q(\varrho)$ (see [$q$-local system](#def:q-local-system)). On the $q$-Langlands dual side $\check{G}_q$ with Borel subgroup $\check{B}_q$, a *$q$-oper* $(\mathcal{P}_{\check{G}_q}, \nabla_q, \mathcal{P}_{\check{B}_q})$ is a $q$-flat bundle with Borel reduction whose connection $\nabla_q$ has non-vanishing projection along every negative simple-root space $\check{\mathfrak{g}}_{-\alpha_i}$ (see [$q$-oper](#def:q-oper)).
