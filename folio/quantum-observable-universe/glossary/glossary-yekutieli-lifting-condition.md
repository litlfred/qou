**Yekutieli lifting condition.** Also called the *Quantum Lifting Condition* (see [Quantum Lifting Condition](#def:yekutieli-lifting-condition)): for a central extension of gerbes $1 \to \mathcal{B} \to \mathfrak{G}' \to \mathfrak{G} \to 1$ banded by an abelian sheaf $\mathcal{B}$ on a site, the obstruction to lifting any object $x \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathfrak{G})$ to $\mathfrak{G}'$ is governed by a single cohomology class $\kappa(x) \in H^2(\mathcal{S}, \mathcal{B})$ that vanishes if and only if a lift exists. In QOU this cohomological criterion controls recursive jet prolongation across levels of the brane tower.
