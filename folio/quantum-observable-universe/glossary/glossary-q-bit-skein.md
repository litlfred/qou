**$q$-bit--Skein identification.** Under the :defterm[$q$-bit--Skein identification]{#q-bit-skein}, each application of the skein relation $L_+ - L_- = (q - q^{-1})L_0$ resolves a single braid crossing and processes one [$q$-bit](#rem:glossary-q-bit) ($\eta_q$) of information. Consequently, the categorical transfer matrix (see [categorical transfer matrix](#def:categorical-transfer-matrix)) acts as an $(n - 1)$-register of $q$-bits read out by the Markov trace, and the categorical reaction $Q$-value measures the net crossing-number balance $(c_{\mathrm{parent}} - c_{\mathrm{daughter}})\cdot \eta_q$.
