**Color-dressed (switched) photon $\gamma$.** An element $\gamma = \mathbf{1} + \varphi_0\sigma_0 + \varphi_1\sigma_1 + \varphi_{01}\sigma_0\sigma_1^{-1} \in H_3(q)$ superposing the uncrossed trivial Hecke identity $\mathbf{1}$ (distinct from the closed-strand neutrino unknot $K_{0_1}$) with linear polarization crossings $(\sigma_0, \sigma_1)$ and the switched Borromean-step generator $\sigma_0\sigma_1^{-1} = L_+ - h\sigma_0$ (see [trivial knot operator on coloured photons](#rem:trivial-knot-photon-interpretation) and [Breit--Wheeler pair production](#def:breit-wheeler-pair-production)). When the switched weight is active ($\varphi_{01} \neq 0$), the $\sigma_0\sigma_1^{-1}$ channel acts as a polarization rotator and supplies the crossing content needed to nucleate charged trefoil pairs in two-photon collisions.
