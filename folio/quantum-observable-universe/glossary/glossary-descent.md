**Descent.** In a quantum observable universe $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ with recursive brane tower $\{A_n\}_{n \ge 0}$, :refterm[descent]{#descent} is the reverse tower traversal passing from jet level $n$ to level $n - 1$ via the restriction morphism $r_n \colon A_n = J^1(A_{n-1}) \to A_{n-1}$ and the induced descent homomorphism $\mathfrak{d}_{n} \colon \mathrm{End}_{\mathbf{C}}(A_{n}) \to \mathrm{End}_{\mathbf{C}}(A_{n-1})$ (see [QU-to-QOU lifting](#thm:qu-to-qou)). Composing $\mathfrak{d}_{n+1}$ with the ascent embedding $\ell_n \colon A_n \hookrightarrow A_{n+1}$ produces the idempotent *descent involution* $\hat{\tau}_n = \ell_n \circ \mathfrak{d}_{n+1}$ projecting level-$(n+1)$ operators onto the descended subalgebra (see [descent involution](#def:descent-involution)).
