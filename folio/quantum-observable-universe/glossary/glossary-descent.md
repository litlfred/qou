Let $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ be a quantum observable universe and let $\{A_n\}_{n \geq 0}$ be the recursive brane tower. The **:refterm[descent]{#descent}** is the reverse traversal of the tower, passing from level $n$ to level $n{-}1$ via the restriction morphism $r_n \colon A_n = J^1(A_{n-1}) \to A_{n-1}$. In the archimedean realisation, this restricts the connection and bundle to the base manifold $M_{n-1}$. The descent terminates in finitely many steps, bounded by $\mathrm{Krulldim} Z(\mathrm{End}_{\mathbf{C}}(A))$.
