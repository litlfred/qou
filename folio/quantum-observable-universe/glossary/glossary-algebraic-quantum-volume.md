**Algebraic quantum volume.** For a knot or confined-state operator $T_K \in H_n(q)$, its full Convention-D Markov trace $\mathrm{Vol}_{\mathrm{alg}}(T_K) := \mathrm{tr}_M(T_K) \in \mathbb{Z}[q^{\pm 1}][(q^2 + 1)^{-1}]$, expanded across partition channels as a weighted sum of irreducible Hecke characters (see [algebraic quantum volume](#def:algebraic-quantum-volume)). It captures the exact pre-archimedean volume invariant inside the categorical aether prior to numerical evaluation at $q_0$ or hyperbolic dilogarithm continuation.
