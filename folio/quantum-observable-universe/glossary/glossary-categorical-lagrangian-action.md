**Categorical Lagrangian and action.** Given a categorical Dirac operator $\mathcal{D}_\nabla$ and state morphism $\psi \colon \mathbf{1} \to A$ with adjoint $\psi^* = \int_A \circ\, S(\psi)$, the *categorical Lagrangian* is the two-term endomorphism $\mathcal{L}_q(\nabla, \psi) = \mathcal{D}_\nabla^2 + \psi^* \otimes \mathcal{D}_\nabla\psi \in \mathrm{End}_{\mathbf{C}}(A)$ combining the bosonic Yang--Mills sector with fermionic coupling (see [categorical Lagrangian](#def:categorical-lagrangian)). Closing a Lagrangian endomorphism $\Phi \in \mathrm{End}_{\mathbf{C}}(A)$ through the rigid evaluation and coevaluation maps yields the scalar *categorical action* $\mathfrak{S}_q = \mathrm{ev}_A \circ (\mathrm{id}_{A^\vee} \otimes \Phi) \circ \mathrm{coev}_A \colon \mathbf{1} \to \mathbf{1}$ (see [categorical action](#def:categorical-action)).
