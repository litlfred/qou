**Bring's surface $\Sigma_4$.** Over a commutative ring $R$ with $30 \in R^{\times}$, the smooth projective genus-four curve in $\mathbb{P}^4_R$ cut out by the first three homogeneous power sums $\sum_{j=0}^4 x_j = \sum_{j=0}^4 x_j^2 = \sum_{j=0}^4 x_j^3 = 0$ (see [Bring's surface](#def:brings-surface)). It has topological Euler characteristic $\chi_{\mathrm{eu}}(\Sigma_4) = -6$ and automorphism group $\mathrm{Aut}(\Sigma_4) \cong S_5$ of order $120$, the maximal automorphism group among all genus-four curves, serving as the universal algebraic fibre hosting three-sheeted colour tubes and torus-knot particle embeddings.
