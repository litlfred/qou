**:refterm[Cemetery group]{#cemetery-group}.** In the category $\mathcal{K}_n$ of finitely generated graded projective Khovanov--Lauda--Rouquier modules \cite{khovanovlauda2009} decategorifying to $H_n(R, q)$, the localizing Serre subcategory $\mathcal{Z}_n = \ker \tau = \{M \in \mathcal{K}_n : \tau(M) = 0\}$ annihilated by the graded Euler-characteristic fibre functor $\tau$ (see [cemetery subcategory](#def:cemetery-group)). Within Gröbner--Shirshov normal-form reduction, $\mathcal{Z}_n$ acts as a homological pruning filter: any state landing in $\mathcal{Z}_n$ has zero observable trace under every subsequent composition.
