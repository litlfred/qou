**Quantum universe.** A tuple $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ consisting of an $R$-linear braided monoidal category $\mathbf{C}$ with simple unit ($\mathrm{End}_{\mathbf{C}}(\mathbf{1}) \cong R$), a distinguished [Frobenius--Hopf aether](#def:frobenius-hopf-substrate) object $A \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathbf{C})$ carrying a first-order differential calculus $\Omega^1(A) = \ker(\mu \colon A \otimes A \to A)$, and a faithful $R$-linear fibre functor $\tau \colon \mathbf{C} \to R\text{-}\mathbf{Mod}$ (see [quantum universe](#def:quantum-universe)). The datum satisfies temporal braiding (well-foundedness of the braiding-induced ordering $\prec_c$), universal action of $A$ on objects of $\mathbf{C}$, and coincidence of the left and right Frobenius traces on $\mathrm{End}_{\mathbf{C}}(A)$.
