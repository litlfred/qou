**Reeb gauge group $U_1(R)$.** In a quantum observable universe $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ over $R$, the :defterm[Reeb gauge group]{#reeb-gauge-group} is the maximal torus $U_1(R) := T = \mathrm{Aut}^{\otimes}(\tau|_{\langle V \rangle})$ of the quantum gauge group $G$, recovering $U(1)$ over $\mathbb{C}$ (see [contact affine chart](#def:contact-affine-chart)). Its infinitesimal generator is the Reeb derivation $\xi$: the $U_1(R)$-action grades $\Omega^k(A)$ into horizontal (weight-$0$) and vertical modes, makes $\mathbb{P}(V) = G/T$ a principal $U_1(R)$-bundle with connection $1$-form $\varTheta_A$, and singles out the Cartan observation direction along which measurement operators commute.
