**Markov trace (Convention D).** The unique linear trace functional $\mathrm{tr}_M \colon H_n(q) \to R$ normalized by $\mathrm{tr}_M(\mathbf{1}) = 1$, $\mathrm{tr}_M(ab) = \mathrm{tr}_M(ba)$, and the stabilization rule $\mathrm{tr}_M(x\sigma_n) = z\,\mathrm{tr}_M(x)$ with loop parameter $z = q^3/(q^2 + 1) = q^2/[2]_q$ (see [Jones--Markov trace](#thm:jones-markov-trace)). On $H_3(q)$, the Convention-D parameter $z = q^3/(q^2 + 1)$ is uniquely singled out as the non-trivial Markov weight whose Wedderburn expansion $\mathrm{tr}_M = \sum_{\lambda \vdash 3} y_\lambda \chi^\lambda$ annihilates the three-strand antisymmetriser, $y_{(1^3)} = 0$ (see [Convention-D antisymmetriser annihilation](#prop:convention-d-kills-antisymmetriser)).
