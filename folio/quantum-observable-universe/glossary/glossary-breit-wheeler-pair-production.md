**Breit--Wheeler pair production.** The two-photon fusion map $(\gamma_1, \gamma_2) \mapsto \mathrm{NF}(\gamma_1 \cdot \gamma_2)$ in the three-strand Iwahori--Hecke algebra $H_3(q)$, expanding the product of two colour-dressed photons into the six-element Coxeter normal-form basis $\{\mathbf{1}, \sigma_0, \sigma_1, L_+, L_-, T_{w_0}\}$ via $\sigma_i^2 = (q - q^{-1})\sigma_i + \mathbf{1}$ and the braid relation (see [Breit--Wheeler pair production](#def:breit-wheeler-pair-production)). Non-zero four-term switched Borromean-step weights ($\varphi_{01}, \psi_{01} \neq 0$ multiplying $\sigma_0\sigma_1^{-1}$) populate the longest-word trefoil channel $T_{w_0} = \sigma_0\sigma_1\sigma_0$, realizing $\gamma\gamma \to e^- e^+$ (and, on colour tubes, quark--antiquark pair creation) algebraically.
