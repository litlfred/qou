Every proof in this paper is accompanied by a formal, machine-verified counterpart, except where a large prior result is cited from the literature; the extent of that verification, and the conditional form in which open conjectures are carried, are recorded in the [formalisation notes](#app:formalisation-notes). All computations—braid-word reductions, Gröbner–Shirshov normal forms, linear programs, hyperbolic volumes—execute symbolically in the substrate parameter $q$ and attach as witnesses to their supporting proofs. The Rust and Python implementations, alongside the formal proofs, reside at [https://github.com/litlfred/qou](https://github.com/litlfred/qou). Large language models assisted the drafting and the formal verification, necessitated by a physical disability preventing sustained typing. Human adjudication remains incomplete.
