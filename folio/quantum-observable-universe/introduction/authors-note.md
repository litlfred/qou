Every proof in this paper possesses a corresponding Lean 4 formalisation, except where explicitly citing external references for large prior results. **737 of 748 (98.5%) provable claims** (theorems, lemmas, propositions, corollaries) carry **full Lean proofs**; the remainder bear the `sorry` marker against an explicit bibliographic reference. To make the boundary of formal verification transparent, the formalised corpus is stratified into two explicitly distinguished tiers: *(a)* **unconditional machine-checked theorems** proved directly over standard Mathlib rings, fields, matrices, and simplicial complexes with zero custom context assumptions — covering the braided and Frobenius–Hopf categorical foundations, Iwahori–Hecke and Specht-module representation identities, Geer–Patureau-Mirand–Turaev modified-trace weights, genus-four Bring-surface topological and symplectic invariants, Convention-D generating functions, and three-dimensional compact-ball simplicial nuclear complexes; and *(b)* **hypothesis-explicit bridge theorems** that carry explicit structure or typeclass hypotheses where ambient formal libraries do not yet provide hyperbolic three-manifold complement volumes, analytic dilogarithm continuations, or continuum quantum-field-theoretic reductions, so that every analytical premise is exposed in the type signature. In addition, the paper poses **87 primary open conjectures** of its own—famous external open problems it connects to, and conjectures conditional on those primaries, are tallied separately—of which **75 (86.2%) are class-axiomatised in Lean** using the conditional-class convention without global axiom instances, allowing downstream results to compile as verified conditional theorems. All computations—braid-word reductions, Gröbner–Shirshov normal forms, linear programs, hyperbolic volumes—execute symbolically or in arbitrary-precision arithmetic in the substrate parameter $q$ and attach as machine-checked witnesses to their supporting blocks. The Rust and Python implementations, alongside the Lean sources, reside at [https://github.com/litlfred/qou](https://github.com/litlfred/qou). Large language models assisted the drafting and formalisation process, necessitated by a physical disability preventing sustained typing and deployed for Lean proof engineering. Human adjudication remains incomplete.
