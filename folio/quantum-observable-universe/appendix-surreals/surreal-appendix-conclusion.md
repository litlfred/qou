The surreal-number lift of QOU raises several questions:

1. **Surreal measure?** The category $\mathbf{No}$ carries no canonical Lebesgue measure. Loeb-type constructions on hyperfinite subsets of $\mathbf{No}$ yield finitely additive measures that pull back to $\mathbb{R}$ under the standard part. We leave open whether such a construction recovers the Frobenius integral $\int_A$ up to an infinitesimal.
2. **Surreal Hilbert-like object.** The scattering pipeline's Hilbert space $\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{phys}}$ lacks a surreal analogue with a canonical inner product. We should investigate whether a birthday-bounded completion yields a weaker surrogate—sufficient for the mass formula but lacking spectral theory.
3. **Categorical size.** Since $\mathbf{No}$ is a proper class, defining $\mathbf{Mod}_{\mathbf{No}}$ or $\mathbf{Rep}_{\mathbf{No}}(\mathrm{SU}_q(2))$ requires universe-polymorphic foundations or restriction to a set-sized Hahn-series sub-field. We sidestep this by stating axioms over a fixed real-closed field $K$ extending $\mathbb{R}$.
4. **Genuine generalisations.** The strict QOU axioms ($\int \circ \eta = \mathrm{id}$, $\mathrm{Hom}(\mathbf{1}, \mathrm{Ad}(A)) = 0$) might force the $\varepsilon$-relaxed versions to coincide with the strict ones in any rigid :refterm[monoidal category]{#monoidal-category}. If so, the $\varepsilon$-flexibility is illusory. We conjecture the flexibility is genuine when the ground ring is not a field ([infinitesimal Frobenius](#conj:infinitesimal-frobenius)), but we lack a proof or counterexample.
5. **Kashaev as a surreal statement.** The reformulation $\mathrm{st}\bigl(2 \pi \log |J_K(q_\omega, \omega)|/\omega\bigr) = \mathrm{Vol}(S^3 \setminus K)$ ([Kashaev surreal](#conj:kashaev-surreal)) is equivalent to the classical conjecture, modulo the meaning of $q_\omega$. It separates the algebraic (Hecke/Markov) content from the transcendental (dilogarithm, hyperbolic volume) content.

**Accomplishments of this appendix:**

* We provide an explicit ambient for the $q \to 1$ limit and renormalisation blow-ups, avoiding informal $\infty$ notation.
* We exhibit an isomorphism between the Hoefsmit seminormal form (central to the mass formula) and Conway's $\{L \mid R\}$ construction.
* We establish a disciplined framework for $\varepsilon$-relaxed axioms.
* We catalogue which QOU constructions are purely algebraic (hence field-polymorphic) versus genuinely analytic.

This appendix serves as raw material for future work rather than a completed extension of the theory.
