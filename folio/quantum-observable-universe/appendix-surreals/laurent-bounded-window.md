Let $(P_j)_{j \ge 0}$ be a domain-stable sequence in $\mathbb{Z}[q^{\pm 1}]$ under $\mathcal{C}_q$.

1. Suppose there exist integers $N_0, m \ge 0$ such that $q^{N_0} P_j \in \mathbb{Z}[q]$ has degree at most $m$ for every $j \ge 0$. Then $(P_j)$ reaches one of the three periodic orbits $\{0\}$, $\{-q^{-2}\}$, or $\{q^{-1},\, 1 + q^{-1}\}$.
2. Equivalently, any domain-stable sequence that never enters one of those three orbits has unbounded exponent support and projects at $q = 1$ to a non-periodic, unbounded integer Syracuse trajectory.
