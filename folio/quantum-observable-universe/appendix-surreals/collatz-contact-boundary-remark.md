The orbit contact average of the [Collatz contact boundary](#def:collatz-contact-boundary) measures the average $[2]_q$-adic valuation of the results of the odd steps of the Skein-Collatz map. The boundary $\mathcal{H}_{Col}(q)$ is the codimension-$1$ locus where the orbit's contact with $D_{[2]}$ is exactly critical. Elements above the boundary (high contact) converge; elements below (insufficient contact) diverge.
