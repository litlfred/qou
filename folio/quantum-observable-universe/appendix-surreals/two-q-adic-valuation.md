For an element $P \in \mathcal{S}(q) \cong \mathbb{Z}[q^{\pm 1}]$ of the Skein module, the $[2]_q$-**adic valuation** is

$$
v_{[2]}(P) := \max\bigl\{k \geq 0 : [2]_q^k \mid P \text{ in } \mathbb{Z}[q^{\pm 1}]\bigr\}.
$$

It is also called the **contact order** of $P$ with the $[2]_q$-divisor $D_{[2]} = \{q : [2]_q = 0\} = \{-1\}$.
