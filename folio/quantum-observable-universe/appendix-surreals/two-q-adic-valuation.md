For an element $P \in \mathcal{S}(q) \cong \mathbb{Z}[q^{\pm 1}]$ of the Skein module, define the $[2]_q$-**adic valuation**:

$$
v_{[2]}(P) := \max\bigl\{k \geq 0 : [2]_q^k \mid P \text{ in } \mathbb{Z}[q^{\pm 1}]\bigr\}.
$$

This counts how many times $[2]_q = q + 1$ divides $P$ in the Laurent polynomial ring. It is the $q$-analog of the $2$-adic valuation $v_2(n)$ that counts trailing zeros in the binary expansion of an integer.

The contact order of $P$ with the $[2]_q$-divisor $D_{[2]} = \{q : [2]_q = 0\} = \{-1\}$ is $v_{[2]}(P)$, the multiplicity of vanishing of $P(q)$ at $q = -1$.

At $q = 1$, $[2]_1 = 2$, and evaluation recovers the ordinary $2$-adic valuation $v_2(P(1))$ whenever no additional cancellation mod $2$ occurs.
