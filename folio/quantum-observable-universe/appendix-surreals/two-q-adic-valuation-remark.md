The [$[2]_q$-adic valuation](#def:two-q-adic-valuation) counts how many times $[2]_q = q + 1$ divides $P$ in the Laurent polynomial ring. It is the $q$-analog of the $2$-adic valuation $v_2(n)$ that counts trailing zeros in the binary expansion of an integer.

As a contact order, $v_{[2]}(P)$ is the order to which $P(q)$ vanishes at $q = -1$. High contact order indicates the orbit grazes the divisor closely (many halvings can be extracted).

At $q = 1$, $[2]_1 = 2$, and $v_{[2]}(n) = v_2(n)$ recovers ordinary $2$-adic valuation.
