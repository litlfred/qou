Let $P_0, P_1, \dots$ be a domain-stable orbit of the Skein-Collatz map $\mathcal{C}_q$ on $\mathbb{Z}[q^{\pm 1}]$ ([$q$-Collatz conjecture](#conj:q-collatz)).

1. Suppose the support stays in a fixed window: there are $N_0, m \geq 0$ such that $q^{N_0} P_j$ is a polynomial of degree at most $m$ for every $j$. Then the orbit reaches one of the three cycles $\{0\}$, $\{-q^{-2}\}$, $\{q^{-1},\, 1 + q^{-1}\}$.
2. Consequently, if the orbit never reaches one of these cycles, then its support lies in no fixed window, and the Syracuse orbit of $P_0(1)$ is not eventually periodic, so it is unbounded.
