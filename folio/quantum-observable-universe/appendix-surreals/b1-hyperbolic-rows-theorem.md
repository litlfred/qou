**Conjecture (the depth correspondence conditionally reduces to Volume Conjecture at Borromean rings, Whitehead link, and twist knots).**

Beyond the figure-8 row and the seven torus-knot rows, the depth correspondence ([depth–period correspondence](#conj:gb-depth-period-correspondence)) holds for additional Bring-embeddable hyperbolic-knot rows, conditional on Volume Conjecture discharges.

See [Table 1](#tbl:b1-hyperbolic-rows-theorem-table1-data). Twist knot $K_n$ has $n$ half-twists ($K_2 = 4_1$ is the figure-8) and genus $1$ for all $n$, so all twist knots are Bring-embeddable. For the Borromean rings and the Whitehead link, the cell pairing and Volume Conjecture discharge are explicit. Over $F = \mathbb{Q}(i)$ the dominant cell pairs by Markov trace with $\mathrm{Li}_2^q(i)$, giving $\mathrm{Vol}(L_{6a4}) = 8G$ and $\mathrm{Vol}(L5a1) = 4G$ with $G$ Catalan's constant, and van der Veen's coloured-Jones asymptotics discharge the Volume Conjecture at both links.

For any hyperbolic Bring-embeddable knot or link $K$ with shape parameter in some number field $F = \mathbb{Q}(z_K)$, three steps apply.
- **Step 1 (Bloch–Suslin)** is unconditionally established for any $F$.
- **Step 2 (cell pairing computation)** uses explicit Markov-trace and Lobachevsky geometry.
- **Step 3 (VC asymptotic)** is knot-specific, requiring a published proof.

Consequently, the depth correspondence at a depth-2 row conditionally reduces to the VC discharge for that knot. The rate-limiting step is the VC discharge per knot, not the Bloch–Suslin chain.

See [Table 2](#tbl:b1-hyperbolic-rows-theorem-table2-data) for Bring-embeddable hyperbolic knots awaiting VC discharge. Each would become a conditionally established row of the depth correspondence upon VC discharge.

Three additional rows of the depth correspondence are conditionally supported by the literature (Borromean, Whitehead; figure-8). Combined with the seven torus-knot rows, eleven total rows of the depth correspondence are supported by the literature. Every published VC discharge for a Bring-embeddable hyperbolic knot yields a conditional row of the depth correspondence.
