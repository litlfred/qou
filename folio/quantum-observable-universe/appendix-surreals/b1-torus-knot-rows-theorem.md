Every torus-knot row of [GS-depth $\leftrightarrow$ Goncharov period-depth correspondence](#conj:gb-depth-period-correspondence) is theorem-level, requiring no part of Goncharov's or Beilinson's conjectures.

Among the seven Bring-natural torus-knot generators of [Bring-embeddable knots](#rem:bring-embeddable-knots):

$$
  \{T(2, 3),\; T(2, 5),\; T(2, 7),\; T(2, 9),\; T(3, 4),\; T(3, 5),\; T(5, 2)\}
$$

none are hyperbolic (torus-knot complements are Seifert-fibred). Thus, the depth-2 row's volume claim is by definition zero for each: $\mathrm{Vol}(T(p, q)) = 0$ in $\mathcal{P}_2 / \mathcal{P}_1$. This depth-2 trivial row of the depth correspondence is by definition theorem-level.

The non-trivial content resides at depth 3. Each torus knot possesses a non-zero Chern–Simons invariant:

$$
  \mathrm{CS}(T(p, q)) = -\frac{(p^2 - 1)(q^2 - 1)}{24 p q} \cdot \pi^2 \pmod{\pi^2 \mathbb{Z}}
$$

(Freed–Gompf 1991, Kirk–Klassen 1990). These are rational multiples of $\pi^2$, hence degenerate depth-3 classes lying entirely in $\mathcal{P}_2$ when reduced modulo the Tate twist.

For each torus-knot row $K = T(p, q)$ in the depth correspondence:
1. **Depth-2 inclusion**: $\mathrm{Vol}(K) = 0 \in \mathcal{P}_2 / \mathcal{P}_1$ by definition in the period filtration.
2. **Depth-3 inclusion**: $\mathrm{CS}(T(p, q)) \in \pi^2 \cdot \mathbb{Q} \subset \mathcal{P}_2$, degenerate.
3. **Volume Conjecture for torus knots**: Discharged by Garoufalidis–Lê (2005). The asymptotic of $J_{T(p,q)}^{(N)}$ at roots of unity equals exactly the CS-rational expression.

Combining these: the depth correspondence at every torus-knot row is theorem-level and degenerate. No genuine trilog content arises here.

The figure-8 row of the depth correspondence realises a non-trivial depth-2 period $\mathrm{Vol}(4_1) = 2\,\mathrm{Cl}_2(\pi/3)$. Torus-knot rows realise zero (depth 2) or Tate-twisted rational (depth 3 but in $\mathcal{P}_2$). For the correspondence's predictive content, the figure-8 row provides the cleaner test; torus-knot rows confirm the framework on degenerate cases.

See [Table](#tbl:b1-torus-knot-rows-theorem-table-data) for per-knot CS values. Note $T(2, 5) \cong T(5, 2)$. These rational $\pi^2$ multiples confirm that torus-knot CS values sit in $\pi^2 \mathbb{Q} \subset \mathcal{P}_2$, not in $\mathcal{P}_3 \setminus \mathcal{P}_2$.

Two row of the depth correspondence classes are now theorem-level: figure-8 (depth-2 non-trivial) and seven torus-knot generators (depth 2 trivial, depth 3 rational). This discharges 8 specific rows of the depth correspondence. Remaining rows of the Bring-embeddable atlas (chiral hyperbolic knots) are open at depth 3, conditional on the Volume Conjecture asymptotic.
