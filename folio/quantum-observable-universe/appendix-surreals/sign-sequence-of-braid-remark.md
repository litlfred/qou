For each fixed standard tableau $t$, the [sign-sequence surreal](#def:sign-sequence-of-braid) assignment $\mathrm{surr}(-, t) : B_n \to \mathbf{No}$ is a multiplicative monoid map: $\mathrm{surr}(\beta_1 \beta_2, t)$ is computed by first applying $\beta_2$'s sign sequence at $t$ to obtain $t'$, then concatenating with $\beta_1$'s sign sequence at $t'$. Changing the tableau permutes the sign sequence by a Young--Jucys--Murphy permutation. We quotient by this action to obtain a tableau-independent functor $\widetilde{\mathrm{surr}} : B_n \longrightarrow \mathbf{No} / \sim$.
