The monoidal localisation kernel $\mathrm{Sha}_q(\mathcal{E}_q)$ of the [invariant Hom-group and localisation datum](#def:categorical-mordell-weil) consists of global classes $x \in H^1_\otimes(\mathbf{C}, E_q)$ satisfying $\mathrm{loc}_\rho(x) = 0$ at every index $\rho$. Over a finite-field fibre where $\mathcal{E}_q \hookrightarrow \mathcal{B}$ is a genus-$1$ sub-curve, the restricted Frobenius eigenvalue on $H^1(\mathcal{E}_q)$ has modulus $q^{1/2}$ under Weil's hypotheses, so each quadratic factor $1 - \alpha_{\mathcal{E}} t + q t^2$ has no root inside $|t| < q^{-1/2}$.
