Let $K$ be a real-closed field extending $\mathbb{R}$ (typically $K = \mathbf{No}$, Conway's surreal numbers). The **birthday** $b(x) \in \mathrm{Ord}$ of $x \in \mathbf{No}$ is the least ordinal stage at which $x$ is constructed in Conway's recursive $\{L \mid R\}$ presentation.

Let $\mathbf{C}$ be a $K$-linear category. The **birthday grading** on $\mathbf{C}$ is the ordinal-indexed filtration of each hom-space

$$
  \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathbf{C}}(X, Y)_{\leq \alpha} = \bigl\{ f \in \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathbf{C}}(X, Y) : \text{every coefficient of } f \text{ has birthday} \leq \alpha \bigr\}.
$$
