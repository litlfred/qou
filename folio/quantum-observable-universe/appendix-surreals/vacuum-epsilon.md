The Hoefsmit scattering pipeline assigns a geometric energy $\mathrm{Geom}(L)$ to each link $L$ ([Hoefsmit scattering pipeline](#conj:hoefsmit-scattering-pipeline)). Vacuum normalisation requires $\mathrm{Geom}(\bigcirc) = 0$ on the unknot. This is a boundary case in the geometric-energy formula and represents a fine-tuned theory (zero vacuum energy). Under a surreal lift, this strict zero can be relaxed.

**Conjecture.** The $\varepsilon$-relaxed vacuum axiom is:
$$
  \mathrm{Geom}(\bigcirc) = \varepsilon \in \mathbf{No}.
$$
Every surreal QOU satisfies this relaxation with some positive infinitesimal $\varepsilon$, and the standard-part recovers the strict vacuum: $\mathrm{st}(\varepsilon) = 0$.

If the conjecture holds:
1. The special case for $\bigcirc$ is absorbed, giving $\mathrm{Geom}(L) = (\text{generic formula}) + \varepsilon \cdot (\text{number of unknot components})$.
2. The diagonal subtraction in the Möbius energy is exactly the $\varepsilon$-vacuum contribution.
3. Every particle mass $\mathfrak{m}(\mathfrak{H})$ becomes $\mathfrak{m}(\mathfrak{H}) + \varepsilon \cdot c_{\mathfrak{H}}$. The standard-part is zero, leaving archimedean predictions unchanged.
4. When the multiplier of $\varepsilon$ is a surreal-infinite mode count, the product can have a nonzero standard part ($\omega \cdot \varepsilon = 1$).

This provides cleaner axioms without fine-tuning and unifies the Möbius regularisation with the $\varepsilon$-vacuum subtraction.

To prove the conjecture, recompute geometric energy and particle masses with $\mathrm{Geom}(\bigcirc) = \varepsilon$. Apply $\mathrm{St}$ to show archimedean predictions are unchanged. Show the diagonal subtraction in the Möbius energy is exactly the $\varepsilon$-vacuum subtraction. Falsification requires finding a particle mass that changes under standard-part.
