**Remark (K-theory $\leftrightarrow$ depth filtration — known at depths 1–2, conjectural at depths $\geq 3$).**

The graded-Mikusiński depth filtration ([GS-depth $\leftrightarrow$ Goncharov period-depth correspondence](#conj:gb-depth-period-correspondence)) on the substrate convolution algebra $\mathcal{H}_q$ admits a K-theoretic description. The depth axis matches the Beilinson–Borel regulator on the algebraic K-theory of the algebraic-number field of definition. This correspondence is a theorem at depths 1 and 2, and Beilinson's conjecture at depths $\geq 3$. We record it to provide the standard period-theoretic language for the framework.

| Depth $n$ | Status | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **theorem** | $K_1(F) = F^\times$; Dirichlet regulator + Baker–Wüstholz on $\log$-linear independence |
| 2 | **theorem (essentially)** | Bloch–Suslin (Suslin 1986, Goncharov 1995): $D$ realises $K_3^{\mathrm{ind}}$ regulator; injectivity after rationalisation. Small open details about generation. |
| 3 | **mostly conjectural** | $K_5^{\mathrm{ind}}(F) \otimes \mathbb{Q}$ has known Borel-rank; whether Goncharov regulator generates $\mathcal{P}_3 / \mathcal{P}_2$ is Beilinson's conjecture |
| $\geq 4$ | **fully conjectural** | Beilinson's conjectures on motivic cohomology |

This Rosetta stone is an analogy that becomes a theorem at low depth and a conjecture at high depth, not a fully-established equivalence at all depths.

| Depth $n$ | Iterated integral grouplike | Algebraic K-theory | Beilinson regulator |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | scalars, $\exp_q$ | $K_1(F) = F^\times$ | $K_1 \to F^\times \otimes \mathbb{R}$ (Dirichlet) |
| 1 | $\log_q$ | $K_1(F)$ via Bloch logs | Dirichlet regulator |
| 2 | $\mathrm{Li}_2^q$ | $K_3^{\mathrm{ind}}(F) \otimes \mathbb{Q}$ | Bloch–Wigner regulator $\to \mathbb{R} / \pi^2 \mathbb{Q}$ |
| 3 | $\mathrm{Li}_3^q$ | $K_5^{\mathrm{ind}}(F) \otimes \mathbb{Q}$ | Goncharov regulator $\to \mathbb{R} / \pi^4 \mathbb{Q}$ |
| $n$ | $\mathrm{Li}_n^q$ | $K_{2n-1}^{\mathrm{ind}}(F) \otimes \mathbb{Q}$ | Beilinson–Borel regulator $\to \mathbb{R} / \pi^{2(n-1)} \mathbb{Q}$ |

Here $F$ is $\overline{\mathbb{Q}}$ or a specific number field $\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_n)$. $K_*^{\mathrm{ind}}$ denotes the indecomposable part of higher K-theory, modulo the image of products of lower K-groups.

The depth correspondence ([GS-depth $\leftrightarrow$ Goncharov period-depth correspondence](#conj:gb-depth-period-correspondence)) asserts $\tau_{\mathrm{arch}}\bigl( G^{(n)}(\mathcal{H}_q) \bigr) \subseteq \mathcal{P}_n$. Under the Beilinson conjectures, the Goncharov filtration $\mathcal{P}_\bullet$ is generated by the Beilinson regulator images of $K_{2n-1}^{\mathrm{ind}}(F) \otimes \mathbb{Q}$ for $n = 1, 2, \ldots$ over all number fields $F$. The correspondence factors as:

$$
G^{(n)}(\mathcal{H}_q) \xrightarrow{\;\;\text{evaluation}\;\;} K_{2n-1}^{\mathrm{ind}}(\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_K)) \otimes \mathbb{Q} \xrightarrow{\;\;\text{Beilinson}\;\;} \mathcal{P}_n / \mathcal{P}_{n-1}.
$$

For a Bring-embeddable knot $K$ with shape parameter $z_K$, the field of definition is $\mathbb{Q}(z_K)$. The relevant K-group is $K_{2n-1}^{\mathrm{ind}}(\mathbb{Q}(z_K)) \otimes \mathbb{Q}$.

| Period | K-group (rationally) | Realised by |
|---|---|---|
| $\mathrm{Vol}(4_1) = 2\,\mathrm{Cl}_2(\pi/3)$ | $K_3^{\mathrm{ind}}(\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_6)) \otimes \mathbb{Q}$ | hyperbolic 3-mfd $S^3 \setminus 4_1$ |
| Catalan $G = \mathrm{Cl}_2(\pi/2)$ | $K_3^{\mathrm{ind}}(\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_4)) \otimes \mathbb{Q}$ | Bloch regulator at 4th root of unity |
| $\mathrm{Cl}_3(2\pi/5)$, $S_3(2\pi/5)$ | $K_5^{\mathrm{ind}}(\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_5)) \otimes \mathbb{Q}$ | open ([Geometric realisation of $\mathrm{Cl}_3(2\pi/5)$ as a Bring-natural depth-3 invariant](#conj:cl3-zeta5-geometric-realization)) |
| $\zeta(3)$ | $K_5^{\mathrm{ind}}(\mathbb{Q}) \otimes \mathbb{Q}$ | Borel regulator on $\mathbb{Q}$ |

We introduce K-theory to provide the standard period-theoretic language. The two languages (Goncharov-Hopf-algebra vs Beilinson-K-theory) are fully equivalent modulo Beilinson's conjectures. The Hopf-algebra language is cleaner for structural relations between grouplikes. The K-theory language is cleaner for citing external results.

What the correspondence requires, in K-theoretic language:
1. **Inclusion target**: $\tau_{\mathrm{arch}}(G^{(n)}) \subseteq \mathcal{P}_n$. We identify $G^{(n)}$ with a subspace of $K_{2n-1}^{\mathrm{ind}}(\mathbb{Q}(z_K)) \otimes \mathbb{Q}$ so that $\tau_{\mathrm{arch}}$ is the Beilinson regulator. The content is this identification.
2. **Sharpness target**: The image generates $\mathcal{P}_n / \mathcal{P}_{n-1}$. Borel's theorem (1977) proves $K_{2n-1}^{\mathrm{ind}}(F) \otimes \mathbb{Q}$ has known explicit rank for $n \geq 2$. Beilinson's conjecture asserts this image generates $\mathcal{P}_n / \mathcal{P}_{n-1}$.

The K-theoretic language partially reduces the correspondence's discharge. Borel pins down dimensions; Beilinson remains the open piece.
