Let $F$ be a number field, $q \in F$ generic, and let $\mathcal{H}_q$ be the substrate convolution Hopf algebra over $F$ ([Frobenius–Hopf substrate](#def:frobenius-hopf-substrate)). The depth-2 identification is:

$$
\tau_{\mathrm{arch}}\!\bigl( G^{(2)}(\mathcal{H}_q) \big/ \mathcal{P}_1 \bigr)_F \cong K_3^{\mathrm{ind}}(F) \otimes \mathbb{Q},
$$

where the right-hand side carries the Bloch–Suslin regulator $K_3^{\mathrm{ind}}(F) \otimes \mathbb{Q} \to \mathbb{R} / \pi^2 \mathbb{Q}$. This identification is a theorem, with proof chain via the Bloch group $\mathfrak{B}(F)$, Suslin's isomorphism, and the archimedean evaluation of $\mathrm{Li}_2^q$.

Combined with [Borel's theorem partially discharges the depth correspondence's sharpness target — explicit dimensions](#prop:borel-partial-resolution), depth 2 of [GS-depth $\leftrightarrow$ Goncharov period-depth correspondence](#conj:gb-depth-period-correspondence) is a theorem over every Bring-natural number field $F = \mathbb{Q}(z_K)$. Depth 3 is conditional on Goncharov's motivic conjecture but has all non-Goncharov steps provable. Depth $\geq 4$ is conditional on Beilinson and Goncharov.
