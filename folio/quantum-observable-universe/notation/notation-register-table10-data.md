| Concept | Font | Symbols |
|---------|------|---------|
| Borel transform | calligraphic | $\mathcal{B}$ — the formal Borel transform, coefficient-level $a_n \mapsto a_n/n!$ ([Écalle–Borel resummation](#def:ecalle-borel-resummation)). Always applied to a series, $\mathcal{B}\tilde f(\xi)$. Distinct from the braid words $\mathcal{B}_K$ / Borromean $\mathcal{B}_3$, which always carry a knot or strand subscript, and from both other "Borel"s (regulator, subalgebra — see the collision list). |
| Lateral / median Laplace–Borel sums | calligraphic + super | $\mathcal{L}^{\theta}$ — Laplace–Borel sum along the ray $e^{i\theta}\mathbb{R}_{\geq 0}$; laterals $\mathcal{L}^{\theta^\pm}$ on a Stokes ray; median resummation $\mathcal{L}^{\mathrm{med}}$ (real-single-valued average). Superscript = direction, never an exponent. |
| Alien derivative | Greek + sub | $\Delta_\omega$ — Écalle's derivation measuring the singularity of $\mathcal{B}\tilde f$ at $\omega$; bridge equation $\Delta_\omega \tilde f = A_\omega\,\partial\tilde f/\partial z$. **Always with singularity subscript $\omega$** — distinct from the per-partition binding weight $\Delta_\lambda$ (partition subscript) and the trace difference $\Delta\mathrm{tr}_M$ (no subscript, argument follows). |
| Stokes constant | italic + sub | $A_\omega$ — the scalar coefficient in the bridge equation at singularity $\omega$; its collection determines the Stokes automorphism. **Always with subscript $\omega$** — bare $A$ is the mass number (or an instanton action when written $\lvert A \rvert$ with explicit context). |
| Standard-part map | roman | $\mathrm{st}$ — the valuation-quotient projection $\mathbf{No}_{\mathrm{fin}} \to \mathbb{R}$ ([standard-part functor](#def:standard-part-functor)); on values of median-summable transseries it acts as the median resummation $\mathcal{L}^{\mathrm{med}}$ ([median-resummation remark](#rem:st-is-median-resummation)). Always roman, never italic $st$. |
| Leading $\omega$-order | roman + sub | $\mathrm{ord}_\omega$ — the leading exponent of the Conway normal form $x = \sum_i \omega^{y_i} r_i$ ([Conway normal form](#rem:conway-normal-form)); the valuation replacing Big-O/$\Theta$ comparisons ($\preceq$, $\prec$, $\asymp$, $\simeq$). Subscript $\omega$ is part of the symbol, not an index. |
