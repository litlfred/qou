| Markov parameter | plain italic | $z = q^3/(q^2+1) = q^2/[2]_q$ — the Markov-trace loop value $\mathrm{tr}_M(\sigma_i)$ of the stabilisation axiom; **convention D** of the [trace-convention registry](../../../docs/coordination/trace-convention-registry.md) and the framework's production trace, $z(q_0) = 0.61268803714646525160$. Older text writes $z = 1/(\check q + \check q^{-1}) = 1/(q^{1/2}+q^{-1/2})$ — **convention C**, excised by the owner ruling of 2026-08-09. C is not a variant normalisation of the same quantity, and the $\check q$ spelling is what made the substitution look harmless. Under this paper's Hecke normalisation $\sigma_i^2 = (q - q^{-1})\sigma_i + \mathbf{1}$ the Temperley–Lieb generator is $e_i := q - \sigma_i$, which satisfies $e_i^2 = [2]_q\,e_i$; the loop parameter is therefore $\delta = [2]_q = q + q^{-1}$, **not** $q^{1/2} + q^{-1/2}$. Convention D reproduces the Jones–Temperley–Lieb normalisation exactly — $\mathrm{tr}_M(e_i) = q - z = q/(q^2+1) = 1/[2]_q = 1/\delta$, the `loop_reciprocal` of [`TemperleyLiebTower.lean`](../lean/QOU/QBeta/TemperleyLiebTower.lean) — whereas C assigned a loop reciprocal to the **Hecke** generator $\sigma_i$, and at $x = q^{1/2}$ rather than $x = q$ in $1/(x + x^{-1})$: $1/\delta = 0.4972905456$ against $z_C = 0.4993203342$, one square root and $0.408\,\%$ apart. **Never** write $1/(q^{1/2}+q^{-1/2})$ for $1/\delta$ (bean `qou-98g5`). Under D the three-strand weights are $(y_{(3)}, y_{(2,1)}, y_{(1^3)}) = ([4]_q/[2]_q^3,\ 1/[2]_q^2,\ 0) = (0.5054042,\ 0.2472979,\ 0)$; under C they were $(0.4153958,\ 0.2809296,\ 0.0227449)$. The selection is forced, not stipulated — see [Primitive Markov-trace axioms](../braids-and-knots/markov-axioms-primitive.md). |
