**:defterm[Aether]{#aether}.** Let $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ be a quantum observable universe. Its **aether** is the self-dual Frobenius–Hopf algebra object $A$ of $\mathbf{C}$ ([Frobenius–Hopf substrate](#def:frobenius-hopf-aether)). The **:defterm[aether parameter]{#aether-parameter}** is the scalar $q \in \mathrm{End}(\mathbf{1})$ of [substrate parameter](#def:aether-parameter); it is a coordinate on the fibre $\mathbb{G}_m$, not on the base ([base, not fibre](#rem:aether-line-bundle-base-not-fibre)). A quantity is **aether-derived** when it is built from $q$ and the categorical data $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ alone.

The aether is a categorical object with no state of motion, in the sense of [Einstein's ether](#rem:einstein-aether). Three neighbouring notions are distinct from it:

- the **brane** is a base object, not the aether ([brane](#rem:glossary-brane));
- the **algebraic layer** is the class of statements valid over a generic commutative ring $R$, before the algebraic→archimedean wall; it is a layer of statements, not an object;
- a **base ring** (the adeles $\mathbb{A}$, a motivic or Hahn coefficient ring) is a ring of coefficients, not the aether.
