Every proof in this paper possesses a corresponding Lean 4 formalisation, except where explicitly citing external references for large prior results. **705 of 720 (97.9%) provable claims** (theorems, lemmas, propositions, corollaries) carry **full Lean proofs**; the remainder bear the `sorry` marker against an explicit bibliographic reference. The paper poses **97 primary open conjectures** of its own—famous external open problems it connects to, and conjectures conditional on those primaries, are tallied separately—of which **72 (74.2%) are class-axiomatised in Lean** using the conditional-class convention. This allows downstream results to persist as fully verified conditional theorems. The Lean sources reside at [https://github.com/litlfred/qou](https://github.com/litlfred/qou).
