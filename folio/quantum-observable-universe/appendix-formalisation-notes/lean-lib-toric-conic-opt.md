**ToricConicOpt** provides solver-external linear, conic, and integer-programming certification over arbitrary linearly ordered fields in Mathlib4 ([gatermann-parrilo2004](#cite:gatermann-parrilo2004), [conti-traverso1991](#cite:conti-traverso1991), [sturmfels-thomas1997](#cite:sturmfels-thomas1997)):

- **Symmetry-reduced conic duality and strict complementary slackness (*SymmetryReducedDualCertificate*).** Proves weak and exact zero-gap primal--dual optimality over generic ordered fields, group-equivariant Reynolds averaging onto Wedderburn central sectors, and support-uniqueness certification on inactive dual-slack indices without invoking floating-point arithmetic.
- **Explicit algebraic radical shadow prices (*ECatRadicalShadowPriceEvaluation*).** Evaluates closed-form dual multipliers on rank-deficient central cones where one sector lies in the trace radical.
- **Toric binomial Gröbner and Graver bases (*NuclearChamberGS*, *ConfinementLayerTower*).** Formalises confluent binomial rewriting systems over finitely generated exponent monoids $\mathbb{N}^k$, strict cost-drop certificates across normal-form promotions, and multi-layer stoichiometric composition towers.
