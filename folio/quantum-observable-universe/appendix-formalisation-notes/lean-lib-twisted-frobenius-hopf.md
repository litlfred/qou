**TwistedFrobeniusHopf** formalises non-symmetric Frobenius and Hopf algebra structures where classical untwisted trace symmetry or antipode involutivity ($S^2 = \mathrm{id}$) fails ([kock2004](#cite:kock2004), [woronowicz1989](#cite:woronowicz1989)):

- **Nakayama-twisted Frobenius pairing (*TwistedFrobeniusTower*, *FrobeniusHopfData*).** Defines ambidextrous Frobenius algebras equipped with an explicit Nakayama automorphism $\nu \in \mathrm{Aut}(A)$ satisfying $\langle a, b \rangle_F = \langle b, \nu(a) \rangle_F$, instantiated concretely on Sweedler's four-dimensional Hopf algebra $H_4$, Taft's $n^2$-dimensional Hopf algebras $T_n(\zeta)$, and $O_q(\mathrm{SU}(2))$ where $S^2 \neq \mathrm{id}$.
- **Twisted $\ast$-Frobenius positive cones (*StarFrobeniusPositiveData*).** Proves that the generator inversion map $T_w \mapsto T_{w^{-1}}^{-1}$ is not $R$-linear at $q \neq \pm 1$, and constructs the sesquilinear $\ast$-pairing $\langle X, Y \rangle_{\tau,\ast} = \tau(X^\dagger Y)$ descending to a positive-definite inner product on $A / \mathrm{rad}\,\tau$.
- **Bicovariant differential calculus dimension obstructions (*CalculusDimensionObstruction*).** Formalises Woronowicz's left-covariant and bicovariant first-order differential calculi on quantum groups, distinguishing the three-dimensional left-covariant 3D calculus from the four-dimensional $4\mathrm{D}_\pm$ bicovariant calculi on $\mathrm{SU}_q(2)$.
