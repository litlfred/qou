**HeckeTemperleyLieb** packages the algebraic and representation-theoretic formalisation of type-$A_{n-1}$ Iwahori--Hecke algebras $H_n(q)$ ([jones1987](#cite:jones1987), [wenzl1988](#cite:wenzl1988)) and their Temperley--Lieb quotients $TL_n(q)$ as a standalone Mathlib4 extension:

- **Kang--Lee--Lee--Oh Gröbner--Shirshov normal forms (*NotAGSBasis*, *HeckeAlgebra*).** Proves the explicit $n = 4$ counterexample establishing that the naive quadratic Hecke plus braid relations fail Diamond Lemma confluence on $B_4^+$, and formalises the completed Kang--Lee--Lee--Oh rewriting system ([kangleeleeoh2002](#cite:kangleeleeoh2002)) together with single-pass normal forms for monotone Coxeter chains (*SL2ChebyshevTrace*).
- **Markov trace radical and Temperley--Lieb quotient (*TemperleyLiebTower*, *BorromeanMarkovTrace*).** Constructs the Jones--Ocneanu Markov trace $\mathrm{tr}_M$ at loop weight $z = q^3/(q^2+1)$, proves exact vanishing of the three-row Wedderburn weight $y_{(1^3)} = 0$ and Gram determinant $\det G = 0$ for $n \ge 3$, and establishes the radical isomorphism $TL_n(q) \cong H_n(q)/\mathrm{rad}\,\tau_q$ of Catalan dimension $C_n$.
- **Young--Hoefsmit seminormal matrices and Schur elements (*ProtonQ3SchurElement*, *CrossNucleonPairWedderburnZRemoval*).** Formalises standard Young tableaux axial distances, seminormal generator matrices ([hoefsmit1974](#cite:hoefsmit1974), [ram1991](#cite:ram1991)), central characters $\chi^\lambda$, and hook-content Schur weights over $\mathbb{Q}(q)$.
