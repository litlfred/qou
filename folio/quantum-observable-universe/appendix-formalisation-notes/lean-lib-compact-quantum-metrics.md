**CompactQuantumMetrics** formalises Rieffel's theory of compact quantum metric spaces ([rieffel1999](#cite:rieffel1999), [rieffel2004](#cite:rieffel2004)) and spectral-triple metric obstructions ([connes1994](#cite:connes1994)) in purely algebraic ordered-field generality:

- **Lipschitz seminorms and scalar-kernel quotient criteria (*FiniteRieffelQuantumMetric*, *Geometry*).** Defines order-unit spaces equipped with a seminorm $L$ whose kernel equals the scalar multiples $\mathbb{F}\cdot\mathbf{1}$, proving boundedness of the induced Monge--Kantorovich metric $d_L(\varphi, \psi) = \sup\{|\varphi(a) - \psi(a)| : L(a) \le 1\}$ on the state space when the quotient $A / \mathbb{F}\mathbf{1}$ has finite radius.
- **Degenerate-trace spectral-triple obstructions (*GramDegeneracyBlocksConnes*).** Proves that any GNS representation built from a trace functional with non-trivial radical $\mathrm{rad}\,\tau \neq \{0\}$ fails faithfulness on the ambient algebra and forces passage to the radical quotient before constructing a spectral triple or quantum metric.
