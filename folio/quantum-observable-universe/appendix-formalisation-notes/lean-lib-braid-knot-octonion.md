**BraidKnotOctonion** collects the geometric, group-theoretic, and non-associative algebraic constructions underlying braid closures, algebraic curves, and division algebras ([birman1974](#cite:birman1974), [baez2002](#cite:baez2002), [bring1786](#cite:bring1786), [braden-northover-2012](#cite:braden-northover-2012)):

- **Artin braid towers and Markov conjugacy (*BraidGroup*, *MassTraceMarkovClass*).** Formalises inductive Artin braid groups $B_n$, stabilization embeddings $B_n \hookrightarrow B_{n+1}$, Markov equivalence classes of closed braids, writhe homomorphisms, and permutation cycle counts (*ClosureComponentsSignInvariant*).
- **Bring's genus-four curve $\Sigma_4$ (*BringsSurface*, *BringConeIcosahedral*).** Formalises the complete intersection $\sum_{i=1}^5 z_i = \sum_{i=1}^5 z_i^2 = \sum_{i=1}^5 z_i^3 = 0$ in $\mathbb{P}^4$, its $S_5$ automorphism action, Euler characteristic $\chi_{\mathrm{eu}}(\Sigma_4) = 2 - 2g = -6$ ($g = 4$), and the isotypic decomposition of $H^1(\Sigma_4)$ into two copies of the four-dimensional standard representation.
- **Octonionic multiplication and $\mathrm{SU}(3)$ colour stabilizer (*EightfoldWay*).** Constructs the eight-dimensional Cayley--Dickson octonion algebra $\mathbb{O}$ over generic rings, Fano-plane structure constants, derivation algebra $\mathfrak{g}_2$, and the $\mathfrak{su}(3)$ subalgebra fixing an imaginary unit.
