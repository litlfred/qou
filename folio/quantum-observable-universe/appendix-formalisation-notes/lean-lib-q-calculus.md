**QCalculus** packages discrete $q$-analysis ([gasper-rahman-2004](#cite:gasper-rahman-2004), [andrews1976](#cite:andrews1976)) and cyclotomic Habiro ring arithmetic ([habiro2004cyclotomic](#cite:habiro2004cyclotomic)) with zero external dependencies beyond Mathlib4:

- **Thomae--Jackson $q$-derivative and $q$-integral (*ThomaeJacksonCalculus*).** Formalises the Jackson difference operator $D_q f(x) = (f(qx) - f(x))/((q-1)x)$, the discrete Thomae--Jackson definite integral $\int_0^a f(x)\,\mathrm{d}_q x = (1-q)a\sum_{k=0}^\infty q^k f(q^k a)$, the $q$-fundamental theorem of calculus, $q$-integration by parts, and monotone Riemann-sum bounds (*ThomaeJacksonCoulombAndAlphaTunneling*).
- **Symmetric vs Gaussian $q$-integers and Habiro cyclotomic tails (*QIntegerConventions*, *Habiro*).** Separates balanced quantum integers $[n]_q = (q^n - q^{-n})/(q - q^{-1})$ from Gaussian polynomials $\{n\}_q = \sum_{j=0}^{n-1} q^j$ via the proved bridge $q^{n-1}[n]_q = \{n\}_{q^2}$, and formalises rising $q$-Pochhammer products $(q;q)_m$ together with bar-involution parity identities ($q \leftrightarrow q^{-1}$).
