**Convention.** A *color observable* is read with the framed generator
$\sigma_i^{\mathfrak{sl}} = q^{-1/N}\sigma_i$ of
[the $\mathrm{SU}(N)$ framing reproduces the one-gluon color factors](#prop:sl-n-color-framing). Every other observable keeps the
Hecke normalisation $(\sigma_i - q)(\sigma_i + q^{-1}) = 0$. The rescaling is not
applied to the Markov parameter $z$, to convention D, or to the writhe-normalised
Markov trace. A uniform rescaling would multiply an unnormalised trace
$\mathrm{tr}_M(w)$ by $q^{-\mathrm{wr}(w)/N}$, where $\mathrm{wr}(w)$ is the
writhe of the word $w$, and would send $z$ to $q^{-1/N} z$. Words of writhe zero
are unaffected; words of nonzero writhe are not, so the scope matters.

**The two-strand color factors.** In the Hecke normalisation the two exchange
channels of $3 \otimes 3 = \bar 3 \oplus 6$ have log-exponents $\pm 1$. Their
ratio is $1$, against the group-theoretic $|c_{\bar 3}/c_6| = 2$. In the
$\mathrm{SU}(3)$ frame the log-exponents are $-\tfrac43$ and $\tfrac23$, so the
ratio is $2$ with both signs correct.

**Origin of the factor $2$.** The identity
$\log|\lambda_{\mathrm{S}}| + \log|\lambda_{\mathrm{A}}| = 0$ for the Hecke
eigenvalues, i.e. $|\det \sigma_i| = 1$ on $V \otimes V$, is the statement that
the $\mathfrak{u}_1$ summand of $\mathfrak{gl}_N = \mathfrak{sl}_N \oplus
\mathfrak{u}_1$ shifts both exponents by the same $1/N$. It is a normalisation
of the generator, not a structural obstruction to the color sector.

**The nuclear channel is not repaired.** The same frame at $N = 2$ gives the
channel ratio $(N+1)/(N-1) = 3$. The nuclear $T = 0$ / $T = 1$ ratio it would
have to reproduce is $\approx 33.7$. So the color frame does not supply the
missing nuclear mechanism. This is a scope-limited negative: it says that this
frame alone does not reach $33.7$, not that framing cannot enter a nuclear
closure.
