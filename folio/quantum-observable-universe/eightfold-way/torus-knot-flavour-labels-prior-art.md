**Remark (prior art: torus knots as flavour labels).**
With symmetric $q$-integers $[n]_q = (q^n - q^{-n})/(q - q^{-1})$, Gavrilik observes that the differences $[n]_q - [n-1]_q$ are the Alexander polynomials of the $(2n-1,2)$ torus knots \cite[\S 2, p. 2]{gavrilik2001}:
$$
[2]_q - 1 = \Delta_q\{3_1\},\qquad [3]_q - [2]_q = \Delta_q\{5_1\},\qquad [4]_q - [3]_q = \Delta_q\{7_1\}.
$$
In the same section the torus knots $5_1, 7_1, 9_1, 11_1$ are put in correspondence with the vector quarkonia $s\bar s$, $c\bar c$, $b\bar b$, $t\bar t$, as part of $U_q(\mathfrak{su}_n)$ mass sum rules.

The identity is parameter-free and uses the same $q$-integers as the corpus. The assignment of the $T(2,2k+1)$ family is a different matter: the [flavour layer of the dictionary](#rem:dictionary-flavor-layer) assigns it to leptons, and the [trefoil–cinquefoil channel](#prop:bring-trefoil-cinquefoil-channel) uses $T(2,3)$ and $T(2,5)$ for the colour tube, while Gavrilik assigns it to quarkonia. These are three different readings of one knot family. The paper fixes its $q$ by fitting mass sum rules to data, so it supplies the identity but no support for any one assignment.
