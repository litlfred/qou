*The :defterm[octonion algebra]{#octonion-algebra} $\mathbb{O}$ is the $8$-dimensional
unital algebra with basis $e_0, e_1, \dots, e_7$, in which $e_0$ is the unit, each
imaginary basis element squares to $-1$,*

$$
  e_i^2 = -1 \qquad (1 \le i \le 7),
$$

*and the imaginary units multiply by the cyclic rule*

$$
  e_i \, e_{i+1} = e_{i+3},
  \qquad
  e_{i+1} \, e_i = -e_{i+3},
$$

*with indices read modulo $7$ on $\{1, \dots, 7\}$, extended to all of $\mathbb{O}$ by
bilinearity.*
