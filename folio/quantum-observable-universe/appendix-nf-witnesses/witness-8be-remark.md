The [Pauli witness for ${}^8\mathrm{Be}$](#def:witness-8be) captures the antisymmetric channel of the reduced configuration. Its weight dilutes as it spreads across the braid generators. Its Pauli factor is

$$
  F_{\mathrm{Pauli}}^{(8)} \approx 0.368
$$

The polynomial $\mathrm{net}(t)$ has $5{,}040$ terms before reduction ($8! / 8 = 5{,}040$) and collapses to $8$ terms in the optimal basis $\{\mathbf{1}, \sigma_0, \ldots, \sigma_6\}$ after $I_8 + I_9$ stripping.

The lower Pauli factor compared to [${}^4\mathrm{He}$](#def:witness-4he) reflects the dilution of the antisymmetric channel across $7$ generators. ${}^8\mathrm{Be}$ is even-even but not doubly magic. Its instability (decay into two $\alpha$-particles) is visible in the weight distribution: the $\ell = 7$ fully correlated term carries less relative amplitude than in neighbouring stable nuclei.
