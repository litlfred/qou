**Existing data sets.**

- Many-body interference measurements in kagome crystals \cite{guo2025}, which probe coherent lattice-scale quantum effects.
- Chiral phonon spectroscopy in rare-earth halide crystals \cite{luo2023}, documenting orientation-dependent effective magnetic fields from lattice vibrations.
- Non-monotonic magnetic friction measurements in commensurate rotor-aether arrays \cite{gu2026}, exhibiting collective magnetically driven dissipation absent physical contact.
- High-$Q$ quartz bulk acoustic wave (BAW) oscillator noise measurements, which log excess phase noise beyond established thermal limits.
- Precision Casimir-effect experiments utilizing crystalline surfaces (such as silicon and gold single crystals).
- Resonant-mass gravitational wave detector data (from AURIGA and NAUTILUS bar detectors), which logged orientation-dependent noise floors in cryogenic crystalline bars.
