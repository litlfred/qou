A periodic crystal lattice acts as a macroscopic, coherent probe of the local value $q|_U$ established in [local aether $q$](#prop:local-substrate-q). The discrete translational symmetry of the lattice isolates a set of reciprocal vectors that couple directly to the horizontal distribution of the CR manifold $M$. Where the lattice spacing is commensurate with the characteristic scale of a $q$-torsion leaf ([$q$-torsion leaf](#ex:q-torsion-leaf)), a resonance condition triggers. The $q$-harmonic forms ([$q$-harmonic form](#def:q-harmonic-form)) on the brane constructively interfere with the crystal's phonon modes, amplifying vacuum fluctuations at specific frequencies. The resonance frequency $\nu_{\mathrm{res}}$ depends entirely on $q|_U$ and lattice geometry. This supplies an independent experimental route to measure the aether parameter.
