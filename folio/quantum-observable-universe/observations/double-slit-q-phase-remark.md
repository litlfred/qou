The decomposition of $\Phi_q^{(i)}$ into geometric and Reeb parts stems from the [$q$-exterior derivative](#def:categorical-q-ext-deriv) $d_q = q^{-1}d + (1 - q^{-1})\pi_V \circ d$.

The $q$-deformed phase difference evaluates to

$$
  \Delta\Phi_q(y)
  =  q^{-1}\frac{2\pi d\sin\theta}{\lambda}
        +  (1 - q^{-1})\Delta\phi_\xi(y),
$$

where $\theta$ is the scattering angle and $\Delta\phi_\xi(y)$ is the Reeb phase mismatch. At $q = 1$, the Reeb term vanishes.
