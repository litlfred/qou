The proton charge radius differs when measured in muonic versus electronic hydrogen. In QOU, this discrepancy arises from generation-dependent aether couplings. Lepton generations correspond to the vertical degree of the Reeb decomposition ([Descartes leptons](#rem:archimedean-leptons)). Each generation couples to the aether parameter $q$ ([aether parameter](#def:substrate-parameter)) through a distinct vertical harmonic form. This coupling modifies the effective electromagnetic interaction between the proton and the orbiting lepton. The $q$-correction depends on the lepton generation. The measured charge radius differs by an amount of order $\hbar_q$ ([quantum planck constant](#def:quantum-planck-constant)). This difference provides a direct test of the Reeb decomposition.
