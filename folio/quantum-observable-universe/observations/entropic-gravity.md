The aether parameter $q$ ([aether parameter](#def:substrate-parameter)) encodes the coupling between the Levi form and the gauge curvature. In the near-classical regime $q \to 1$, gravity emerges as a geometric back-reaction ([GR aether geometry](#rem:gr-substrate-geometry)). Recent work on the quantum mechanics of entropic forces \cite{carney2025} provides a complementary perspective. The entropic interpretation corresponds to the thermodynamic limit of the $q$-deformed partition function on the brane tower.
