With $q = q_0$ fixed by the lepton mass ratio ([skein mass relation](#rem:skein-mass-relation)), the hadronic masses are determined without free parameters. By ([nucleon Borromean topology](#lem:nucleon-borromean-topology)), the proton and neutron are Borromean configurations $6_2^3$ of three quark vortices bound by Hopf links. Their mass contribution arises entirely from the Skein functional. By ([pair Skein mass formula](#lem:pair-skein-mass-formula)), each color-exchange crossing pair contributes $\mathcal{S}_{ab}(q) = (q\mu_a + q^{-1}\mu_b)/(q^{1/2} + q^{-1/2})$. The nucleon mass is the sum over its three pairs. By ([flavor-dressed mass from substrate split](../mass-theory/flavor-dressed-mass-from-substrate-split.md)), the proton and neutron formulas $m_p = \mathcal{S}_{\mathfrak{u}\mathfrak{u}} + 2\mathcal{S}_{\mathfrak{u}\mathfrak{d}}$ and $m_n = 2\mathcal{S}_{\mathfrak{u}\mathfrak{d}} + \mathcal{S}_{\mathfrak{d}\mathfrak{d}}$ together with the substrate-derived splitting [Substrate-pure derivation of the $u$--$d$ flavor split](#prop:flavor-split-substrate) determine the dressed quark masses $\mu_{\mathfrak{u}} \approx 311.10\,\text{MeV}$ and $\mu_{\mathfrak{d}} \approx 312.39\,\text{MeV}$. These reproduce the CODATA proton and neutron masses to within a $-0.53\%$ residual on the splitting $m_n - m_p$ (dominated by the dropped $O((\Delta m / M_N)^2)$ correction to [Substrate-pure derivation of the $u$--$d$ flavor split](#prop:flavor-split-substrate)). See [Dressed-quark numerology at $q_0$](dressed-quark-numerology-at-q0.md) for the per-quark numerology and the closure tabulation. $\square$
