**Existing data sets.**
- Frequency-encoded photonic Chern insulator measurements of Chenier et al. \cite{chenier2026}.
- Many-body interference data in kagome lattices \cite{guo2025}.
- Chiral phonon experiments in rare-earth halides \cite{luo2023}.

**Planned observations.**
- Extension of photonic Chern insulator experiments to tunable $q$-regimes via lattice-geometry variation.
- Fractional quantum Hall experiments in synthetic photonic systems with controllable topology.

**Potential new experiments.**
- Measurement of Hall conductance in photonic lattices at varying effective $q$ parameters. This seeks the $1 - q^{-1}$ correction predicted by the [quantum Planck constant](#def:quantum-planck-constant).
- Cold-atom realisations of $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ gauge fields in optical lattices. This tests the $q$-deformed braiding statistics of [the braids and knots chapter](#chap:path-integrals-braiding).
