**Existing data sets.**

* LIGO/Virgo/KAGRA gravitational wave catalogues (GWTC-1 through GWTC-3).
* Pulsar Timing Array data sets (NANOGrav 15-year, EPTA, PPTA, IPTA).
* Solar System ephemeris tests of general relativity (Cassini, MESSENGER).

**Planned observations.**

* **LISA** (ESA, launch $\sim 2035$): Space-based gravitational wave detector. Sensitive to low-frequency waveform corrections from the $q$-deformed field equations.
* **Einstein Telescope**: Next-generation ground-based detector. Sensitive to post-merger ringdown deviations.

**Potential new experiments.**

* Precision tests of the equivalence principle at quantum scales. These probe whether the aether parameter $q$ ([aether parameter](#def:substrate-parameter)) introduces composition-dependent deviations.
* Tabletop gravity experiments at sub-millimetre scales. These search for $q$-dependent modifications to the Newtonian potential.
