The classical Bethe–Weizsäcker (BW) empirical mass formula decomposes nuclear binding into volume, surface, Coulomb, asymmetry, and pairing terms. In the categorical framework, this phenomenological liquid-drop model is **targeted for categorical replacement** — the program is to *derive* the decomposition from $q_0$ and $m_e$ rather than fit it. This is a program, **not a completed closure**: the per-isotope binding magnitude ($\Delta_\lambda$) is open, six ansätze have failed, and the BW coefficients remain AME least-squares fits.

Instead of mapping empirical BW coefficients to specific algebraic strata (a numerological exercise), the exact macroscopic mass is governed by the $q$-Central Limit Theorem ($q$-CLT) and the Jaco-Shalen-Johannson (JSJ) volume addition. 

## The Canonical Replacement

1. **Volume and Surface Limits:** The strong-force binding is *proposed* to be governed by the canonical scale $\alpha = \mathfrak{m}_e / (q_0(q_0-1))$ times the sum of the logarithmic Markov traces. The empirical $a_V \approx 15.6$ MeV and $a_S \approx 17.2$ MeV are **conjectured** to be artifact parameters of fitting a classical drop to a $q$-deformed Markov trace; deriving them from the trace (rather than fitting) is open.
2. **Coulomb Repulsion:** The electrostatic penalty is *proposed* to arise from the $q$-deformed wedge product over identical-chirality crossings. The two factors of the classic $Z(Z-1)/A^{1/3}$ scaling stand differently, and neither is yet derived without an empirical $a_C$:
   - The **proton-pair multiplicity $Z(Z-1)$** is available from the substrate's own crossing counts, as the exact identity $16\,Z(Z-1) = (n_+ - n_-)(n_+ - n_- - 4)$ following from $n_+ - n_- = 4Z$. Note that this is *quadratic* in the counts: the linear combinations $a\,n_+ + b\,n_- + c$ are separately proved never to equal $Z(Z-1)$. So the multiplicity is not excluded — but exhibiting it as a consequence of the wedge product, rather than as an identity in the counts, is open.
   - The **$A^{-1/3}$ separation law** is **not** derived and is currently an empirical import. All three of the corpus's combinatorial classes for producing a cube root of $A$ were refuted symbolically, and the measured effective exponent on the mirror series is $p^* = 0.252 \pm 0.020$, placing $1/4$ at $0.1\sigma$ and $1/3$ at $4.1\sigma$. Routing instead through the framework's one extensive geometric object does not help: in $\mathbb{H}^3$ a volume $V \propto A$ gives a radius $R \sim \tfrac12 \ln(2V/\pi)$, i.e. $\ln A$ rather than $A^{1/3}$.
3. **Shell Structure:** Magic numbers and shell closures are *proposed* to emerge from the exact trace evaluations rather than from empirical shell-correction terms $\delta_{\mathrm{shell}}(A)$; the selection principle (peaks *only* at magic numbers) is open (`conj:homological-shell-condensation`).

The **goal** is a framework that requires only the substrate parameter $q_0 \approx 1.1100$ and the reference mass $m_e$, with no nuclear-physics input. The empirical Bethe-Weizsäcker equation is **not yet retired**: the absolute per-isotope binding ($\Delta_\lambda$) closure is open.
