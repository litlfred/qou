At $q = q_0$, the beta-stability formula ([beta-stability](#prop:beta-stability)) fixes the beta-stability line $p^*(A)$, the minimum-energy proton count at each mass number:

$$
  p^*(A) =  \frac{A}{2 + c \cdot A^{2/3}},
  \qquad c = \frac{a_C}{2a_A} = 0.01497.
$$

The predicted neutron number at a given proton count is obtained by inverting that line: $n_{\mathrm{pred}}(p) = A^*(p) - p$, where $A^*$ is the fixed point of $A^* = p\left(2 + c\,{A^*}^{2/3}\right)$. We compare $n_{\mathrm{pred}}$ against observed isotopes across 103 elements:
