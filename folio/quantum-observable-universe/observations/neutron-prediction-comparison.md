At $q = q_0$, the beta-stability formula ([beta-stability](#prop:beta-stability)) fixes the beta-stability line $p^*(A)$, the minimum-energy proton count at each mass number:

$$
  p^*(A) =  \frac{A}{2 + c \cdot A^{2/3}},
  \qquad c = \frac{\lambda_C}{2a_{\mathrm{asym}}} = \frac{(q_0 - q_0^{-1})\,[6]_{q_0}}{6\,[2]_{q_0}[7]_{q_0}} \approx 0.01452,
$$

with $\lambda_C = (q - q^{-1})[6]_q\, m_e$ and $a_{\mathrm{asym}} = 3[2]_q[7]_q\, m_e$ in balanced quantum integers, evaluated at the proton-calibrated modulus $q_0$; the factor $m_e$ cancels. The constant $c$ is fixed by the $q$-integer coefficient assignments, not derived: those assignments are stated without an independent derivation of $[6]_q$ or $3[2]_q[7]_q$.

The predicted neutron number at a given proton count is obtained by inverting that line: $n_{\mathrm{pred}}(p) = A^*(p) - p$, where $A^*$ is the fixed point of $A^* = p\left(2 + c\,{A^*}^{2/3}\right)$, rounded to the nearest integer.
