**Candidate crystal-resonator experiments.**

- **Cryogenic rotating-crystal null and gravitational-gradient test:** A cryogenically cooled, slowly rotated ultra-pure single crystal (silicon, sapphire, or quartz) instrumented with SQUID readout and high-$Q$ phonon transducers ([Aether Telescopes](#rem:substrate-telescope)). In flat vacuum, observer covariance at fixed $q_0 \neq 1$ predicts an exact orientation null consistent with $< 10^{-18}$ Kennedy–Thorndike bounds; sidereal or tidal orientation modulation probes exclusively non-vacuum macroscopic state anisotropy such as local gravitational curvature/torsion gradients $\nabla q(x)$ or a finite-temperature KMS background.
- **Orientation-invariant lattice-symmetry spectroscopy:** Comparative acoustic and polariton resonance ratios $\nu_{\mathrm{res}}^{(a)} / \nu_{\mathrm{res}}^{(b)}$ measured at fixed orientation across single crystals of distinct point-group symmetries (cubic, hexagonal, trigonal), probing scalar lattice mode couplings controlled by $q_0$ without invoking preferred-frame vacuum drift.
- **Archival resonant-bar tidal re-analysis:** Re-analysis of cryogenic resonant-bar datasets (AURIGA, NAUTILUS, EXPLORER) for sidereal tidal couplings to non-vacuum gravitational $\nabla q(x)$ gradients or thermal KMS condensates orthogonal to transverse-traceless quadrupole gravitational-wave templates.
