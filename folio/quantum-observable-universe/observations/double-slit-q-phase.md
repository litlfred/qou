Let $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ be a quantum observable universe with [substrate parameter](#def:substrate-parameter) $q \in k^{\times}$, and let $\gamma_i$ ($i = 1,2$) be two paths through slits of width $a$ separated by distance $d$. The **$q$-deformed path phase** along $\gamma_i$ is

$$
  \Phi_q^{(i)}(y)
  =  \frac{1}{\hbar_q}\!\int_{\gamma_i}\! d_q\psi
  =  q^{-1}\Phi_{\mathrm{geom}}^{(i)}(y)
        +  (1 - q^{-1})\Phi_{\mathrm{Reeb}}^{(i)}(y),
$$

where $\Phi_{\mathrm{geom}}^{(i)} = (2\pi/\lambda)\ell_i(y)$ is the geometric phase, $\Phi_{\mathrm{Reeb}}^{(i)} = \int_{\gamma_i} \iota_\xi\varTheta$ is the Reeb phase ([Reeb derivation](#def:categorical-reeb)), and $\hbar_q = 1 - q^{-1}$ is the [quantum Planck constant](#def:quantum-planck-constant). The **$q$-deformed phase difference** is $\Delta\Phi_q(y) = \Phi_q^{(1)}(y) - \Phi_q^{(2)}(y)$.
