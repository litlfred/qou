**Existing data sets.**

- *Planck* 2018 full-sky temperature and polarisation maps (PR3/PR4).
- *WMAP* nine-year data release.
- ACT and SPT high-$\ell$ power spectra.

**Planned observations.**

- *LiteBIRD*: full-sky $B$-mode polarisation survey sensitive to large-angular-scale torsion signatures.
- *CMB-S4*: ground-based CMB survey with improved sensitivity to polarisation anomalies.
- *Simons Observatory*: intermediate-scale polarimetry bridging current and CMB-S4 sensitivities.

**Potential new experiments.**

- A dedicated search for spiral patterns in existing CMB $B$-mode data, testing for the preferred handedness predicted by the Reeb orientation ([Descartes chirality](#rem:archimedean-chirality)).
- Cross-correlation of CMB anomalies (such as the Cold Spot and hemispherical asymmetry) with the angular structure of $q$-torsion leaves.
