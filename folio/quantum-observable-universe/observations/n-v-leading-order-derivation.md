**Conjecture (Leading-order $N_v$).**
*The electroweak topological invariant $N_v$ admits the leading-order closed form*

$$
  N_v \;=\; |S_5| \cdot q_0^{90}
        \;=\; 120 \cdot q_0^{90}.
$$

*The corresponding electroweak scale prediction is $v = 244.79 \;\mathrm{GeV}$, matching CODATA to $-0.58\%$.*

## Conditional derivation sketch

*Assume (i) the Markov-trace-residual rescaling factor is $|S_5| = 120$, which is the content of [the Bring-action rescaling conjecture](n-v-bring-action.md), and (ii) the depth is $90$ generations, which is the content of [the depth-ninety tower conjecture](n-v-tower-depth-ninety.md). Combining these yields the bridge identity $v/m_e = 40 \cdot q_0^{90}$. Substituting into $N_v = 3v/m_e$ gives $N_v = 120 \cdot q_0^{90}$.*

*The step from (i) and (ii) to the displayed form is arithmetic and holds as stated. **Neither premise is established**: both premises remain open conjectures. The section is therefore a conditional derivation, not a proof, and carries no $\square$.*

## Sub-leading correction

The exact CODATA value implies $N_v = 120 \cdot q_0^{90.0558}$. The structural origin of this correction remains unresolved.

## Comparison to other strategies

The parameterization is optimal among non-circular closed forms.

Two separate claims about the prefactor $120$ should not be run together, because only one of them is a derivation. That it **minimizes the residual** is a statement about fit quality — selection against the measured value, and so not evidence for the framework under the parameter-free discipline. That it **derives from the geometric aether** is the assertion of [the Bring-action rescaling conjecture](n-v-bring-action.md), which remains open. The residual-minimisation does not establish the aether origin, and the aether origin is not what the residual measures.
