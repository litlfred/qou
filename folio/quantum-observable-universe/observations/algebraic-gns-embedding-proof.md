*Proof.* We assemble four standard components.

**Tower of inclusions.** The Hecke algebra $H_n(R, q)$ has presentation by braid generators $\sigma_1, \ldots, \sigma_{n-1}$ modulo the braid relations and the quadratic relation $\sigma_i^2 = (q - q^{-1})\sigma_i + 1$. The inclusion $H_n(q) \hookrightarrow H_{n+1}(q)$ adjoins $\sigma_n$. This inclusion is unital, $R$-linear, and commutes with the $*$-structure $\sigma_i^* = \sigma_i$. (This is the $*$-structure for which positivity below holds. It is not the bar involution $\sigma_i \mapsto \sigma_i^{-1}$, $q \mapsto q^{-1}$, which does not preserve the convention-D parameter $z$.) The sequence $\{H_n(q)\}_{n \geq 1}$ forms a filtered inductive system of unital $*$-algebras over $R$, with colimit $H_\infty(q) = \bigcup_n H_n(q)$ [`jones1985`].

**Markov property.** The Markov trace $\mathrm{tr}_{M,n} \colon H_n(q) \to R$ satisfies the recursion $\mathrm{tr}_{M,n+1}(x \cdot \sigma_n) = z \cdot \mathrm{tr}_{M,n}(x)$ for any $x \in H_n(q)$, where $z = q^3/(q^2+1)$ (convention D, [Jones–Markov trace](#thm:jones-markov-trace)). Equivalently, $\mathrm{tr}_{M,n+1}|_{H_n(q)} = \mathrm{tr}_{M,n}$. This compatibility with the inclusions defines a unique $R\otimes\mathbb{Q}$-linear trace $\mathrm{tr}_M \colon H_\infty(q) \to R\otimes\mathbb{Q}$ extending all $\mathrm{tr}_{M,n}$ by the universal property of the colimit.

**Positivity.** Let $q > 0$ be real. Under convention D the Markov trace vanishes on every Wedderburn block with three or more rows, so it factors through the Temperley–Lieb quotient $\mathrm{TL}_n(\delta)$ with loop value $\delta = [2]_q = q + q^{-1}$. Jones proved that the Markov trace on $\mathrm{TL}_n(\delta)$ is a positive functional, $\mathrm{tr}_{M,n}(x^* x) \geq 0$, whenever $\delta \geq 2$ [`jones1985`]. Here $q + q^{-1} \geq 2$ for every real $q > 0$, with equality only at $q = 1$. So $\mathrm{tr}_M$ is a positive trace on $H_\infty(q)$ for every real $q > 0$, and in particular at $q_0$. There is no threshold in $q$ to be near.

In Wedderburn terms, $\mathrm{tr}_{M,n} = \sum_\lambda y_\lambda \chi^\lambda$, and on each block the trace is $y_\lambda$ times the matrix trace in Wenzl's orthogonal (seminormal) representation, where each $\sigma_i$ acts by a real symmetric matrix [`wenzl1988`]. Positivity is then $y_\lambda \geq 0$ for every $\lambda$. The closed-form convention-D Ocneanu weight is proved strictly positive on every shape with at most two rows, for $q > 0$, $q \neq 1$, and zero on the rest. That this closed form is the Markov weight at general $n$ is the classical Ocneanu–Wenzl formula; it is proved here only at $n = 3$ and checked numerically through $n = 6$, so the positivity statement supports the cited result and does not replace it.

**GNS construction.** Define the left ideal $\mathcal{N} := \{x \in H_\infty(q) : \mathrm{tr}_M(x^* x) = 0\}$. Under the production convention D this ideal is **not** trivial: $\mathrm{tr}_M = \sum_\lambda y_\lambda \chi^\lambda$ with $y_\lambda = 0$ exactly when $\ell(\lambda) \geq 3$, so $\mathcal{N}$ is the sum of those Wedderburn blocks — the Temperley–Lieb radical — and the GNS space is the Temperley–Lieb quotient $H_\infty(q)/\mathrm{rad} \cong \mathrm{TL}_\infty(q)$, not $H_\infty(q)$ itself. (Under the excised convention C every $y_\lambda$ was non-zero and $\mathcal{N}$ collapsed to $0$.) Positivity and the Cauchy–Schwarz inequality $|\mathrm{tr}_M(x^* y)|^2 \leq \mathrm{tr}_M(x^* x)\,\mathrm{tr}_M(y^* y)$ guarantee $\mathcal{N}$ is a well-defined ideal. The quotient $V := H_\infty(q) / \mathcal{N}$ forms an $H_\infty(q)$-module under left multiplication. The bilinear form $\langle [x], [y] \rangle := \mathrm{tr}_M(x^* y)$ is sesquilinear and positive definite on $V$. Its completion $\mathcal{H}$ is a separable Hilbert space. Separability follows because $H_\infty(q)$ admits a countable basis as an $R\otimes\mathbb{Q}$-module.

The left-multiplication action $\pi_{\mathrm{GNS}}(a)[x] := [a x]$ extends to a bounded operator on $\mathcal{H}$. The trace Cauchy–Schwarz inequality yields the bound $\|\pi_{\mathrm{GNS}}(a)\| \leq \|a\|_2$. The representation is faithful: $\pi_{\mathrm{GNS}}(a) = 0$ implies $\pi_{\mathrm{GNS}}(a)[1] = [a] = 0$, requiring $a \in \mathcal{N}$, which means $\mathrm{tr}_M(a^* a) = 0$, and thus $a = 0$ in the trace-quotient. The $*$-property $\pi_{\mathrm{GNS}}(a^*) = \pi_{\mathrm{GNS}}(a)^*$ holds on the dense subspace $V \subset \mathcal{H}$ and extends to $\mathcal{H}$ by continuity. $\square$
