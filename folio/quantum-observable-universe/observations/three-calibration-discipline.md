The **three-calibration discipline** consists of two requirements. First, the framework admits exactly three external **calibration inputs**, the three real degrees of freedom of the first cohomology of Bring's curve, $H^1 = 2W$:

| DOF of $H^1(\text{Bring}) = 2W$ | Canonical observable | What it fixes | Numerical proxy (source) |
| ------------------------------- | -------------- | ------------- | ------ |
| $W$-plane **scale** ($h^0$)     | $m_e$ (leptonic mass scale) | the dimensional unit (scale) | $m_e$, CODATA (electron mass) |
| $W$-plane **modulus** ($h^2$)   | $P_{\mathrm{skew}}^{\beta}$ (neutron $\beta$-sector bar-skew channel) | $q_0$ via $m_\mu/m_e = \mathrm{Vol}(4_1)/\hbar_q^2$ | $\mathfrak{Q}_\beta = P_{\mathrm{skew}}^{\beta}$; modulus pinned most precisely by $m_\mu/m_e$ (CODATA muon mass) |
| **orthogonal class** (meson sector, $h^3$) | $P_{\mathrm{avg}}^{\pi}$ (pion-sector bar-even channel) | the $\mathrm{Vol}(\mathrm{Hopf})=0$ meson scale (independent of $m_e, q_0$) | $m_\pi$, PDG (charged-pion mass) |

Second, for every confined hadronic state $X$, the mass functional defined by the algebraic-side chain ([categorical action](#def:categorical-action), [Frobenius pairing](#def:frobenius-pairing), [mass identity](#def:mass-identity), [mass-endomorphism tower](#prop:mass-endomorphism-tower), [Jones–Markov trace](#thm:jones-markov-trace)) factors as

$$
  \mathfrak{m}(X) \;=\;
  m_e^{\,a(X)}\;m_\pi^{\,c(X)}\;Q_X(q_0),
  \qquad
  Q_X(q) \in \mathbb{Q}(q),
$$

with non-negative integer exponents $a(X), c(X)$ determined by the (co)homological grading of the observable $X$, and $q_0$ fixed by the lepton ratio $m_\mu/m_e$.
