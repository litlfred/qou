We calibrate the substrate parameter $q$ using lepton masses alone. The $q$-deformed Skein relation encodes the deformation of the standard Skein identity. **Knot volume verification (SnapPy)** independently checks hyperbolic volumes. A classification of building-block knots provides Jones polynomials and volumes required for mass formulas. This outputs the calibrated value $q_0 \approx 1.1100$. The subsequent section applies this to the full hadron spectrum.
