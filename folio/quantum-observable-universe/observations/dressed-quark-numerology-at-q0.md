At $q_0 \approx 1.10998$, combining [Aether-pure derivation of the dressed-quark mass splitting](../mass-theory/flavor-dressed-mass-from-substrate-split.md) with the nucleon sum $m_p + m_n = 2M_N$ yields:

$$
  \mu_{\mathfrak{u}} \;\approx\; 307.40\,\text{MeV},
  \qquad
  \mu_{\mathfrak{d}} \;\approx\; 308.67\,\text{MeV},
  \qquad
  \mu_{\mathfrak{d}} - \mu_{\mathfrak{u}} \;\approx\; 1.271\,\text{MeV}.
$$

The splitting comes from $M_N$ and $q_0$ through the convention-D [pair Skein mass formula](#lem:pair-skein-mass-formula). The mean is fixed by taking $M_N$ as an input. The resulting $m_n - m_p \approx 1.2917\,\text{MeV}$ sits at $-0.128\%$ from the CODATA value $1.2933\,\text{MeV}$. This is the comparison already recorded for [the conjectured mass split](#conj:flavor-split-aether-mass), not an independent one.

## The mean dressed mass

Under the pair Skein formula with crossing weights $w_+ = q^3/(q^2+1)$ and $w_- = q^{-1}/(q^2+1)$, the nucleon sum is

$$
  m_p + m_n
  \;=\;
  (5w_+ + w_-)\,\mu_{\mathfrak{u}}
  \;+\;
  (w_+ + 5w_-)\,\mu_{\mathfrak{d}}.
$$

With equal dressings this is $6\kappa\,\bar\mu$, where $\kappa = w_+ + w_- = (q^4+1)/\bigl(q(q^2+1)\bigr) \approx 1.0163$ at $q_0$. So the mean dressed mass is $\bar\mu \approx M_N/(3\kappa) \approx 307.95\,\text{MeV}$, below the naive $M_N/3 \approx 312.97\,\text{MeV}$. The unequal weights $w_+ \neq w_-$ move the mean of the two values above to $308.04\,\text{MeV}$.

## Distance from the aether bound

[Two-sided aether bound on the dressed-quark splitting](../mass-theory/flavor-mass-splitting-bound.md) gives an upper bound of $\approx 3.875\,\text{MeV}$ and a sharper bound of a third of that, $\approx 1.292\,\text{MeV}$. The realised value $1.271\,\text{MeV}$ is $32.8\%$ of the upper bound. The ratio is exactly $\tfrac13\,q(q^2+1)/(q^4+1)$.

## Heavier flavors

The aether flavor-split derivation is specific to the nucleon doublet topology. Extensions to heavier flavors remain unconstrained.
