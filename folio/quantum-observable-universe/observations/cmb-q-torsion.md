In the Descartes universe ([Descartes universe](#thm:descartes)), the negative Euler characteristic $\chi(\mathcal{S}) = -6$ forces a non-trivial distribution of angular modes across the CR manifold. The $q$-torsion leaves ([$q$-torsion leaf](#ex:q-torsion-leaf)) imprint a characteristic pattern on the CMB that deviates from the statistically isotropic Gaussian field predicted by $\Lambda$CDM. Specifically, the $q$-deformed Reeb flow ([$q$-Reeb flow](#ex:q-reeb-flow)) introduces torsion into the temperature and polarisation multipoles, producing preferred spiral directions in the $B$-mode spectrum.
