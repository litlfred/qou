Two structural criteria distinguish lepton generations. These are Reeb-vertical degree of knot localisation ([Reeb lepton decomposition](#rem:archimedean-leptons)), and whether the cyclic symmetry embeds in $S_5$ ([symmetry-preserving lepton classification](#rem:symmetry-preserving-leptons)). The electron and muon satisfy both. The tau satisfies only the first. Anomalous magnetic-moment tensions and lepton flavour universality violations observed at $B$-factories may reflect this structural asymmetry rather than new heavy-particle loops. If so, the tension should track the tau channel specifically and not the electron or muon channels, which satisfy both criteria.
