The small residuals observed in electronic versus muonic spectroscopy probe how the local effective deformation parameter $q_{\mathrm{local}}$ responds to the spatial Compton wavelength of the orbiting lepton. This section analyzes the electronic versus muonic proton charge-radius shift ($r_p$), formulates the entropic-gravity coupling from the substrate free-energy gradient, and proves the local substrate-$q$ theorem unifying both shifts.
