The interaction products of [strangelet double-beta decay](#prop:strangelet-double-beta) become electromagnetically visible only through Skein crossing changes. Each crossing change is a beta decay that lowers the degree from $2$ toward $1$. The minimal path from the degree-$2$ fiber (cinquefoil, $5$ crossings) to the degree-$1$ fiber (trefoil, $3$ crossings) requires two crossing changes: a double beta decay.
