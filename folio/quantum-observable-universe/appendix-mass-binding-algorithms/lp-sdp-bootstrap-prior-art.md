The dual-certification procedure in [Solver-external rationalized dual-slack optimality and uniqueness certification](#alg:rationalized-dual-slack-certificate) draws upon four established mathematical and computational traditions, while assembling them to solve a distinct finite-dimensional algebraic selection problem.

## Conformal, sphere-packing, and interior-point bootstraps

The pairing of primal operator weights against dual exclusion functionals enters modern high-energy physics with **Riccardo Rattazzi**, **Vyacheslav S. Rychkov**, **Erik Tonni**, and **Alessandro Vichi** \cite{rattazzi-rychkov-tonni-vichi2008}, who reformulated crossing symmetry and unitarity in four-dimensional conformal field theory as a linear feasibility problem. **Sheer El-Showk**, **Miguel F. Paulos**, **David Poland**, **Slava Rychkov**, **David Simmons-Duffin**, and **Alessandro Vichi** \cite{elshowk2012} subsequently introduced the extremal-functional method, reading the physical spectrum from the zeros of the optimal dual slack vector $c - A^{\mathsf{T}} u^*$ along the boundary of the allowed region. Because polynomial conformal blocks become severely ill-conditioned at binary double precision, **David Simmons-Duffin** \cite{simmons-duffin2015} developed native arbitrary-precision primal--dual interior-point semidefinite solvers, while **Paul J. Goulart** and **Yuwen Chen** \cite{goulart-chen2024} formulated the homogeneous self-dual interior-point conic architecture underlying Clarabel. In discrete geometry, **Henry Cohn** and **Noam Elkies** \cite{cohn-elkies2003} and **Maryna S. Viazovska** \cite{viazovska2017} demonstrated that a dual auxiliary functional whose slack vanishes only on the shell radii of a single configuration pins an algebraic object uniquely---establishing optimality and uniqueness of the $E_8$ lattice packing by strict complementary slackness.

## Symmetry-reduced block decomposition

Reducing a noncommutative positivity cone to scalar and low-rank inequalities indexed by irreducible representations originates with **Philippe Delsarte** \cite{delsarte1973}, who diagonalized commutative association schemes into linear programs over spherical and Krawtchouk characters. **Alexander Schrijver** \cite{schrijver2005} and **Dion Gijswijt**, **Alexander Schrijver**, and **Hajime Tanaka** \cite{gijswijt-schrijver-tanaka2006} extended character diagonalization to noncommutative centralizer $*$-algebras via the Terwilliger algebra, and **Karin Gatermann** and **Pablo A. Parrilo** \cite{gatermann-parrilo2004} established the general Peter--Weyl and Young-diagram isotypic block reduction for group-invariant semidefinite and sum-of-squares programs. Whenever an observable lies in the center of $H_n(q)$, orthogonality of central idempotents collapses matrix semidefinite positivity $\sum_\lambda y_\lambda \mathrm{tr}(M_\lambda^{\mathsf{T}} M_\lambda) \ge 0$ to the scalar partition inequalities $y_\lambda \ge 0$ on every nonempty Wedderburn block.

## Nuclear reduced-density-matrix and trace-loop positivity

In quantum many-body physics, direct variational minimization over the two-body reduced density matrix subject to $(D, Q, G)$ particle--hole positivity was brought into angular-momentum and isospin-coupled $(J, T)$ nuclear structure calculations by **Brecht Verstichel**, **Helen van Aggelen**, **Dimitri Van Neck**, **Paul W. Ayers**, and **Patrick Bultinck** \cite{verstichel-vanaggelen2009}. In noncommutative operator algebras and gauge theory, **Miguel Navascués**, **Stefano Pironio**, and **Antonio Acín** \cite{navascues-pironio-acin2007} introduced the trace Gram matrix hierarchy $\tau(b_i^* b_j) \succeq 0$ over word monoids, and **Vladimir Kazakov** and **Zechuan Zheng** \cite{kazakov-zheng2022,kazakov-zheng2023} combined Makeenko--Migdal loop equations with trace Gram positivity to bound matrix models and lattice Yang--Mills plaquette expectations.

## Certified rational dual witnesses in proof assistants

The algebraic principle that a single dual multiplier vector certifies a global primal bound goes back to **Gyula Farkas** \cite{farkas1902} and to the resolving multipliers (shadow prices) of **Leonid Vitaliyevich Kantorovich** \cite{kantorovich1939}. Separating untrusted numerical optimization from formal kernel verification was pioneered at scale in the Flyspeck proof of the Kepler conjecture by **Thomas C. Hales** and collaborators \cite{hales2017}, where external linear programs generated dual certificates verified inside HOL Light. In the Coq ecosystem, **Sylvie Boldo**, **Catherine Lelay**, and **Guillaume Melquiond** \cite{boldo-lelay-melquiond2015}, **Pierre Roux** \cite{roux2016}, and **Assia Mahboubi** and **Thomas Sibut-Pinote** \cite{mahboubi-sibut-pinote2021} developed rigorous real-analytic, interval, and Cholesky-positivity certificates for untrusted numerical and semidefinite oracles, while **Martin Dvořák** and **Vladimir Kolmogorov** \cite{dvorak-kolmogorov2024} formalized linear-programming duality and Farkas lemmas over linearly ordered fields in dependent type theory.

## Distinctive synthesis in this paper

Unlike continuum conformal or infinite-volume lattice bootstraps---which bound truncated slices of an infinite-dimensional correlator hierarchy---this paper formulates arbitrary-precision conic programs directly on finite-dimensional Hecke and Temperley--Lieb normal-form strata at the fixed modulus $q_0$. Once an interior-point solver identifies a candidate primal--dual pair, rationalizing the dual multiplier vector $u^*$ reduces primal optimality and strict complementary-slackness support uniqueness to exact identities over an arbitrary linearly ordered ring, placing the numerical solver entirely outside the trusted kernel.
