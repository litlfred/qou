**Algorithm ($\mathfrak{Q}_\beta$ truncation ladder).**
*Evaluate $\beta$-decay $Q$-values via the convergent series
$\mathfrak{Q}_\beta = Q_{\mathrm{base}} \cdot \prod_{k = 2}^{N} (1 + c_k\, u^k)$
with $u = b \cdot \hbar_q^2 \approx 0.00852$, truncating at order
$N$ chosen by the target precision.*

### Input

- *Decay channel (parent isotope $A \to$ daughter $A'$).*
- *Substrate $q_0$, electron mass $m_e$ (CAL-1, CAL-2).*
- *Pion-sector bar-even channel (CAL-3) for $P_{\mathrm{avg}}^{\pi}$.*
- *Target precision $\varepsilon \in \mathbb{R}_{> 0}$ → truncation
  order $N \geq \lceil \log_{10}(1/\varepsilon) / 2.07 \rceil - 1$.*

### Output

- *$\mathfrak{Q}_\beta \in \mathbb{R}$ in MeV.*

### Algorithm

```
1. Compute Q_base from the substrate identity m_e · (m_n / m_e)
   modulo the binding-energy contribution.
2. Compute u = b · ℏ_q² where ℏ_q is the q-quantum constant.
3. For k = 2, …, N:
   c_2 = -1                  (structural: exact u^2 coefficient of the
                              explicit-factor product; not derived).
   c_k for k ≥ 3 are q_0-dependent NEAR-MATCHES; N is a truncation
     order, not an inclusion/exclusion criterion.
   Multiply Q_base by (1 + c_k · u^k).
4. Return \mathfrak{Q}_\beta.
```

### Correctness conditions

*[The categorical precision-ladder proposition](#prop:q-beta-categorical-precision-ladder)
establishes the precision ladder and the convergence rate.*

*The $c_k$ coefficients are **tiered**:*

- *$c_2 = -1$: **structural** — the exact $u^2$ coefficient of the
  explicit-factor product $\prod_{k\ge2}(1 + c_k u^k)$, with no fit, no
  CODATA and no $q_0$. Not **proved**: this identity expands the $N{=}2$
  factor rather than deriving it from first principles. State which series — the normalised
  series' coefficient is $\tilde c_2 = c_2 - 1/8 = -9/8$.*
- *$c_k$ for $k \geq 3$: **near-match** with $q_0$-dependent
  swings (e.g. $c_3 = -2 = J_{\mathrm{Hopf}}(q = 1)$ within
  $\sim 3\%$ at the m_p-pin $q_0$).*
- *The **truncation order** $N$ is never reported as "fitted": it is a tier, not an
  inclusion/exclusion criterion. Individual $c_k$ **values** are a different question,
  and some of them **are** fits.*

  *Each $c_k$ value carries one of three tiers: proved, near-match or fitted. Only
  $c_2 = -1$ is structural; $c_3$ is a falsified near-match; $c_4 = -0.97157$ is a
  CODATA fit, with zero residual by construction; $c_5$ onward are open. The whole
  ladder is in the convention-C frame and has not been re-derived under D:
  the non-vanishing of the three-row weight at $z_C$ makes the C-frame ladder and the mass
  channel's three-row forcing mutually exclusive, so this is not a $z$-swap, and every
  $c_k$ conclusion above is C-conditional.*

### Complexity

*$O(N)$ — linear in truncation order.*
