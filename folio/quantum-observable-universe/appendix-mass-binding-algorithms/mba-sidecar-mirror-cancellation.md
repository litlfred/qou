*Let $(Z, N)$ and $(N, Z)$ be a mirror pair at fixed mass number $A = Z + N$, with crossing statistics of the canonical multi-nucleon braid word*
$$
  \sigma + \sigma^{-1} \;=\; 10A - 2,
  \qquad
  \mathrm{avg} \;=\; 2A + 1,
  \qquad
  w \;=\; \sigma - \sigma^{-1} \;=\; 4Z.
$$
*Then both bar-even statistics are mirror-invariant,*
$(\sigma + \sigma^{-1})(Z, N) = (\sigma + \sigma^{-1})(N, Z)$ *and*
$\mathrm{avg}(Z, N) = \mathrm{avg}(N, Z)$, *whereas the bar-odd writhe satisfies*
$$
  w(Z, N) - w(N, Z) \;=\; 4(Z - N).
$$
*Consequently, for any observable decomposing additively into an isoscalar $A$-dependent baseline $\beta(A)$ plus an isospin-asymmetric term $\zeta(Z, N)$,*
$$
  f(Z, N) \;=\; \beta(A) + \zeta(Z, N),
$$
*the mirror difference cancels the $A$-dependent baseline identically:*
$$
  f(Z, N) - f(N, Z) \;=\; \zeta(Z, N) - \zeta(N, Z).
$$

Both bar-even crossing counts depend solely on $A = Z + N$, while the bar-odd writhe $w = 4Z = 2A + 2(Z - N)$ carries the unique crossing-level isospin asymmetry between $(Z, N)$ and $(N, Z)$. In the 3D simplicial and Pauli shell functional of [Step 9b](#alg:per-isotope-binding), both the simplicial strong energy $B_{\mathrm{simp}}(\mathcal{T}_A)$, the symmetric $(N - Z)^2$ Pauli promotion energy, and the even/odd seniority pairing $\delta_{\mathrm{pair}}(v_p, v_n)$ are invariant under $(Z, N) \leftrightarrow (N, Z)$. Mirror binding differences $B(Z, N) - B(N, Z)$ (such as ${}^{3}\mathrm{H} - {}^{3}\mathrm{He}$, ${}^{7}\mathrm{Li} - {}^{7}\mathrm{Be}$, and ${}^{15}\mathrm{N} - {}^{15}\mathrm{O}$) therefore cancel the entire strong simplicial baseline identically and isolate the discrete proton-pair Coulomb potential difference $E_C^{\mathrm{disc}}(\bar{p}) - E_C^{\mathrm{disc}}(p)$ together with any residual bar-odd chiral isovector contribution.
