Completeness here means that the seven rows below exhaust the single-crossing interaction catalogue acting on the transfer matrix: every admissible process is either the identity, one of the two $\beta$ decays, a photon append or remove, or a pair creation or annihilation. Each row is a distinct process, and the final column links back to the operator that defines it in [tm-interactions](#sec:tm-interactions).

| # | Process | Operator | Catalogue entry |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Identity (no interaction) | $\mathbb{I}$ | trivial pair $T_{1,0} \cdot T_{1,0}$ |
| 2 | $\beta^-$ decay ($\mathfrak{d} \to \mathfrak{u}$) | $\Delta_\beta = \tfrac{1}{2}(q - q^{-1})\mathbb{I}$ | [tm-interactions §1](#sec:tm-interactions) |
| 3 | $\beta^+$ decay ($\mathfrak{u} \to \mathfrak{d}$) | $-\Delta_\beta$ | [tm-interactions §2](#sec:tm-interactions) |
| 4 | Photon absorption (Reidemeister I, append) | $T_{\gamma e}(q) = T_{pp}(q)$ | [tm-interactions §3](#sec:tm-interactions) |
| 5 | Photon emission (Reidemeister I, remove) | $T_{pp}(q)^{-1}$ | [tm-interactions §3](#sec:tm-interactions) |
| 6 | Pair creation ($\gamma\gamma \to e^- e^+$) | $-\Delta_\beta$ on the affected crossing | [tm-interactions §4](#sec:tm-interactions) |
| 7 | Pair annihilation ($e^- e^+ \to \gamma\gamma$) | $\Delta_\beta$ on the affected crossing | [tm-interactions §4](#sec:tm-interactions) |
