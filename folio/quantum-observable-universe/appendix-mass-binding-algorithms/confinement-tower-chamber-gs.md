- **QOU delta (five-layer confinement tower and 11-rule physical cluster chamber):** Functorial column promotion $A_{k+1} = A_k M_{k\to k+1}$ preserves charge conservation across five coupled strata:
  1. $\mathcal{L}_0 \to \mathcal{L}_1$ (*hadronic*): projects three-strand motifs onto $TL_3(q_0)$ ([Confinement-layer operator selection](#rem:confinement-layer-operator-selection), keeping intranucleon gluon stabilization open across the candidate arms);
