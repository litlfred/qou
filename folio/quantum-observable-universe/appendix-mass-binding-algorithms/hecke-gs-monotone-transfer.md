**Prior-art reduction.** Confluence of word reduction in the type-$A_{n-1}$ Iwahori--Hecke algebra $H_n(q)$ ($\sigma_i^2 = h\sigma_i + 1$, $h = q - q^{-1}$) follows **Seok-Jin Kang**, **In-Sok Lee**, **Kyu-Hwan Lee**, and **Hyekyung Oh** \cite[Prop.~4.3]{kangleeleeoh2002}, extending **Leonid A. Bokut** and **L.-S. Shiao** \cite{bokut2001} from $q = 1$ finite Coxeter groups by adjoining the completion family $T_{i+1,j} T_{i+1} \to T_i T_{i+1,j}$ ($i \ge j$, where $T_{a,b} = \sigma_a \sigma_{a-1}\cdots \sigma_b$); without that completion family, the three-rule system comprising the quadratic relation, far commutativity, and adjacent braid relation fails confluence at $n \ge 4$. Irreducible Young--Hoefsmit seminormal matrices and Jones--Markov trace weights $y_\lambda(q)$ under $z = q^3/(q^2+1)$ follow **Peter Norbert Hoefsmit** \cite{hoefsmit1974}, **Vaughan F. R. Jones** \cite{jones1987}, **Hans Wenzl** \cite{wenzl1988}, **Arun Ram** \cite{ram1991,ram1997}, **Meinolf Geck** and **Sofia Lambropoulou** \cite{geck-lambropoulou-2004}, and **Rosa C. Orellana** \cite{orellana1998} ([Hecke normal form](#def:hecke-normal-form), [Axiomatic Markov trace](#prop:markov-trace-axiomatic-closed-form), [Wedderburn trace identity](#prop:wedderburn-trace-identity)).

- **Input:** Multi-nucleon braid element $\beta = \prod_{g=0}^{3A-2} (c_g \sigma_g + d_g) \in H_{3A}(q)$ with strictly non-decreasing generator index $g$ (intra-nucleon crossings $\sigma_{3k}, \sigma_{3k+1}$ and single-pass inter-nucleon interfaces $\sigma_{3k+2}$), together with two-row partitions $\lambda \vdash 3A$ ($\ell(\lambda) \le 2$) surviving on $TL_{3A}(q)$.
- **Output:** Exact irreducible characters $\chi^\lambda(\beta) \in \mathbb{Q}(q)$ and normalized Markov trace $\tau_q(\beta) = \sum_{\ell(\lambda) \le 2} y_\lambda(q)\,\chi^\lambda(\beta)$, evaluated symbolically over $\mathbb{Q}(q)$ or at 80 decimal digits at $q = q_0$.
- **QOU delta (monotone fine-Bratteli $2\times 2$ transfer sweep):** Because each factor $O_g = c_g \sigma_g + d_g$ acts on a single step $s_g \subset s_{g+1}$ of the multiplicity-free Gelfand--Tsetlin standard-tableau chain $\varnothing \subset s_1 \subset \cdots \subset s_{3A} = \lambda$, trace closure projects $O_g$ onto its $1\times 1$ (same-row/same-column) or $2\times 2$ (axial-distance) diagonal edge block. Contracting the resulting transfer operator sequentially along the fine-Bratteli edge graph computes $\chi^\lambda(\beta)$ in a single sweep without materializing $(3A)!$ Coxeter basis words or $d_\lambda \times d_\lambda$ representation matrices.
- **Complexity and precision:** $\mathcal{O}(|E_{\mathrm{Bratteli}}(3A)|)$ exact rational operations in $\mathbb{Q}(q)$, scaling with the Catalan two-row path count $\dim TL_{3A}(q) = C_{3A}$ rather than $(3A)!$; numerical specialization at $q_0$ uses 80-decimal-place arithmetic with truncation bound $\le 10^{-60}$.
