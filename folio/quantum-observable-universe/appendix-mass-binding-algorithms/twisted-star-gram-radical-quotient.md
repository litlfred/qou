**Prior-art reduction.** Semisimple quotienting of Iwahori--Hecke algebras modulo three-row Young idempotents onto the Temperley--Lieb algebra $TL_n(q)$ follows **H. N. V. Temperley** and **Elliott H. Lieb** \cite{temperley-lieb1971}, **Vaughan F. R. Jones** \cite{jones1987}, and **Hans Wenzl** \cite{wenzl1988}, while isotypic block diagonalization of invariant Hermitian forms follows **Karin Gatermann** and **Pablo A. Parrilo** \cite{gatermann-parrilo2004} ([Frobenius pairing](#def:frobenius-pairing), [Gram degeneracy obstruction](#prop:gram-degeneracy-blocks-connes)).

- **Input:** Normal-form elements $a, b \in H_n(q)$ at real parameter $q > 0$ under Markov trace weight $z = q^3/(q^2+1)$, or elements of a finite-dimensional pivotal/Hopf substrate model $A$ equipped with counit $\varepsilon$ and Nakayama automorphism $\nu \in \mathrm{Aut}(A)$.
- **Output:** Involution-twisted $\ast$-Gram inner product $\langle \bar{a}, \bar{b}\rangle_\ast \in \mathbb{Q}(q)$ on the radical-free quotient $TL_n(q) = H_n(q)/\mathrm{rad}\,\tau_q$, positivity certificate $\langle \bar{a}, \bar{a}\rangle_\ast \ge 0$, and twisted Frobenius identity residual on $(A, \varepsilon, \nu)$.
- **QOU delta ($\ast$-involution quotient and Nakayama twist):** On $H_n(q)$ at $z = q^3/(q^2+1)$, every partition with $\ell(\lambda) \ge 3$ has vanishing Markov weight ($y_{(1^3)}(q) = 0$ on $H_3(q)$, spanned by the $q$-antisymmetrizer $r_{[1^3]} = \sum_{w \in S_3} (-q^{-1})^{\ell(w)} T_w$), so $\det G = 0$ for all $n \ge 3$; moreover, the untwisted bilinear form $G_{ij} = \tau_q(T_{w_i} T_{w_j})$ is indefinite of signature $(4_+, 1_-, 1_0)$ on $H_3(q_0)$, whereas twisting by the anti-automorphism $T_w^\ast = T_{w^{-1}}$ yields the positive-semidefinite Hermitian $\ast$-Gram matrix $S_{ij} = \tau_q(T_{w_i} T_{w_j}^\ast)$ of signature $(5_+, 0_-, 1_0)$ descending to a strictly positive inner product on $TL_n(q)$ ($\dim TL_n = C_n$). Because $H_n(q)$ admits no $R$-linear Hopf antipode at $q \neq 1$, Hopf-substrate verification replaces the involutory test $S^2 = \mathrm{id}$ with the Nakayama-twisted Frobenius law $\varepsilon(ab) = \varepsilon(b\,\nu(a))$, capturing non-involutory quantum groups ($O_q(\mathrm{SU}(2))$, Sweedler $H_4$, and Taft algebras $T_m(\zeta)$ where $S^2 \neq \mathrm{id}$).
- **Complexity and precision:** $\mathcal{O}(C_n^2)$ operations over $\mathbb{Q}(q)$ after radical projection $x \mapsto x - (\langle x, r_{[1^3]}\rangle / \|r_{[1^3]}\|^2) r_{[1^3]}$, evaluated at 80 decimal digits.
