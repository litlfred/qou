**Prior-art reduction.** Candidate primal--dual solutions for symmetry-reduced linear and semidefinite programs are obtained outside the formal kernel using invariant block diagonalization (**Karin Gatermann** and **Pablo A. Parrilo** \cite{gatermann-parrilo2004}) and arbitrary-precision or homogeneous self-dual interior-point conic solvers (**David Simmons-Duffin** \cite{simmons-duffin2015}; **Paul J. Goulart** and **Yuwen Chen** \cite{goulart-chen2024}), while ordered-field Farkas certification follows **Gyula Farkas** \cite{farkas1902}, **Leonid Vitaliyevich Kantorovich** \cite{kantorovich1939}, and **Martin Dvořák** and **Vladimir Kolmogorov** \cite{dvorak-kolmogorov2024} ([Operator selection LP](#prop:operator-selection-lp), [Physical cluster chamber](#prop:nuclear-chamber-groebner-basis)).

- **Input:** Constraint matrix $A \in R^{m \times n}$, right-hand side $b \in R^m$, and cost vector $c \in R^n$ over a linearly ordered commutative ring $R$ (such as $\mathbb{Q}$ or a rationalized 80-decimal-place interval enclosure of $\mathbb{Q}(q_0)$), together with candidate primal weights $x^\ast \in R_{\ge 0}^n$ and dual shadow prices $u^\ast = (y_0^\ast, y^\ast) \in R^m$.
- **Output:** Exact dual-slack vector $s(u^\ast) = c - A^\mathsf{T} u^\ast \in R^n$, active support $\mathcal{I}_+(x^\ast) = \{j : x_j^\ast > 0\}$, exact duality gap $\Delta_{\mathrm{gap}} = \langle c, x^\ast\rangle - \langle b, u^\ast\rangle$, and boolean certificates of global optimality and primal uniqueness.
- **QOU delta (solver-external strict complementary-slackness verification):** For central observables in $H_n(q)$, orthogonality of central Young idempotents reduces matrix positivity $\sum_\lambda y_\lambda\,\mathrm{tr}(M_\lambda^\mathsf{T} M_\lambda) \ge 0$ on $TL_n(q)$ to the finite scalar linear inequalities $y_\lambda \ge 0$. Rationalizing the numerical oracle's dual multipliers $u^\ast$ turns optimization into three elementary ring identities verified without trusting the solver:
  1. *Primal and dual feasibility:* verify $A x^\ast = b$, $x^\ast \ge 0$, and $s_j(u^\ast) = c_j - (A^\mathsf{T} u^\ast)_j \ge 0$ for all $j \in \{1,\dots,n\}$, certifying the global lower bound $\langle b, u^\ast\rangle \le \langle c, x\rangle$ for every feasible $x$;
  2. *Zero-gap optimality:* verify $\sum_{j=1}^n x_j^\ast s_j(u^\ast) = 0$, certifying global optimality of $x^\ast$;
  3. *Support and solution uniqueness:* verify strict dual slack $s_j(u^\ast) > 0$ on every off-support column $j \notin \mathcal{I}_+(x^\ast)$, forcing $x_j = 0$ for every optimal $x$ and—when the columns $\{A_{\cdot,j}\}_{j \in \mathcal{I}_+(x^\ast)}$ are linearly independent—certifying $x = x^\ast$ uniquely.
- **Complexity and precision:** $\mathcal{O}(m n)$ exact ring multiplications over $\mathbb{Q}$ (accompanied by 80-decimal-place numerical dual gap $\le 10^{-60}$ in emitted witness records).
