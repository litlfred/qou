**Proof.** 
The Borromean braid $\mathcal{B}_3 = (\sigma_0 \sigma_1^{-1})^3$ is a concrete element of the braid group $B_3$, and thus of the Hecke algebra $H_3(q)$ via the standard representation. Typed crossings for the partition $(\mathfrak{u}, \mathfrak{u}, \mathfrak{d})$ and the gluon stabiliser $L_- = \sigma_1 \sigma_0$ are likewise elements of $H_3(q)$. Their product reduces via Gröbner--Shirshov to a canonical Hecke normal form. LP-dual feasibility verifies the non-negativity constraint $G x[\gamma] \ge 0$ at every intermediate step. Consequently, the categorical proton $\mathfrak{p}$ exists as a well-defined, LP-stable element.
