The Hecke bar involution $\sigma \leftrightarrow \sigma^{-1}$ acts on the aether parameter as $q \leftrightarrow q^{-1}$. In terms of $s = \sqrt{q}$, this is $s \leftrightarrow 1/s$. This involution maps the golden-ratio node $q_{\mathrm{gold}} = 1/\varphi^2$ ([golden-ratio degeneration](#rem:golden-ratio-degeneration)) to the skew-dual golden node:

$$
  q^{*}_{\mathrm{gold}}
  =  \varphi^2
  =  \frac{3 + \sqrt{5}}{2}
   \approx  2.618.
$$

The minimal polynomial of $1/\varphi$ over $\mathbb{Q}$ is $s^2 + s - 1$. Applying $s \mapsto 1/s$ and clearing denominators yields the bar-involute:

$$
  s^2 - s - 1.
$$

This is the minimal polynomial of $\varphi$. The involution $s \leftrightarrow 1/s$ exchanges these quadratics up to sign. Their roots form the bar-paired set $\{1/\varphi, \varphi\}$ on the positive real axis.
