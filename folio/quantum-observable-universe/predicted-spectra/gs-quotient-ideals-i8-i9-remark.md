The raw Gröbner--Shirshov normal form ([shell closure normal form](#prop:shell-closure-normal-form)) contains up to $A!$ terms. The [quotient ideals](#def:gs-quotient-ideals-I8-I9) $I_8$ and $I_9$ reduce this complexity by absorbing structurally-determined reduced words into the per-generator transfer-matrix factorisation. Under $I_8$, the coefficient of $\sigma_i \sigma_{i+1}$ is structurally determined by the off-diagonal entry of the transfer matrix product $M_i$, rather than being independent. Under $I_9$, the absorption implements the per-generator transfer matrix factorization by replacing the expanded Hecke element with the product of $\hat{V}_i$ volumes.
