$$
T_{\gamma e}(q)\, v_{\pm} = \lambda_{\pm}(q)\, v_{\pm}.
$$

*For any two topological partitions corresponding to emission poles $n$ and $m$, the relative ratio of their eigenspaces is invariant under continuous deformations of the substrate $q \to q'$, up to a global conformal scaling factor $\alpha(q, q')$.*
