Let $\iota \colon \mathbb{R}_{>0} \to \mathbb{R}_{>0}$ denote the bar involution $\iota(q) = q^{-1}$. The open interval

$$
  \mathcal{B}  :=  \bigl(1/\varphi^2,\,\varphi^2\bigr) \subset  \mathbb{R}_{>0}
$$

is the maximal connected $\iota$-invariant open subinterval of $\mathbb{R}_{>0}$ excluding the golden nodes $\{1/\varphi^2,\,\varphi^2\}$ ([skew-dual golden node](#prop:golden-skew-dual-node)).
