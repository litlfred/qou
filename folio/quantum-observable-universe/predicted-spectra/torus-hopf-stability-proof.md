*Proof.*
The torus knot generators $T_{m,n} \in \mathcal{G}$ have meridional winding $m \in \{2, 3\}$, forced by the genus-4 bound. For any triple $[K_1, K_2, K_3]_p \oplus \mathcal{L}_H$ drawn from $\mathcal{G}$:

1. The Hopf link $\mathcal{L}_H$ contributes a negative baseline crossing energy via the strong binding term $\tfrac{1}{2}(n_+ + n_-)(q + q^{-1})$.
2. The Coulomb correction $Q(\mathfrak{H}) \cdot (q - q^{-1})$ satisfies $|Q(\mathfrak{H})| \leq 1$ for all generators in $\mathcal{G}$, because the genus constraint bounds the maximum crossing asymmetry between $m = 2$ and $m = 3$ strands.
3. Since $\tanh(\ln q) \approx 0.104$, the Coulomb correction is at most $\sim 10\%$ of the strong baseline, insufficient to overcome binding.

In particular:

- **Proton** $\mathfrak{p} = [T_{2,3}, T_{2,3}, T_{2,3}^{\sigma}]_p \oplus \mathcal{L}_H$: The Coulomb charge is $Q(\mathfrak{p}) = +1$. The positive Coulomb correction reduces binding but does not overcome it, leaving $\mathfrak{E}_{\mathrm{cross}}(\mathfrak{p}) < 0$.
- **Neutron** $\mathfrak{n} = [T_{2,3}, T_{2,3}^{\sigma}, T_{2,3}^{\sigma}]_p \oplus \mathcal{L}_H$: The Coulomb charge is $Q(\mathfrak{n}) = 0$. The strong baseline binding is undiminished.

Both are stable hadrons. $\square$
