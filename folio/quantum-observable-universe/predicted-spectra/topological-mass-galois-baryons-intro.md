When the shape field $\mathbb{Q}(z_K)$ of a hyperbolic link complement is a non-trivial algebraic number field, the Galois group $\mathrm{Gal}(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}/\mathbb{Q})$ permutes the algebraic conjugates of $z_K$, generating a finite Galois orbit of Bloch–Wigner periods.

This section examines the Yekutieli transcendence shadow and the physical consequences of algebraic independence versus dependence among knot volumes, proves that the alternating-channel product formula generates the baryon period from the three-strand representation, and establishes the golden-skew dual node identity over $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{5})$.
