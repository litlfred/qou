The [electron](#def:electron-knot) shares its knot type with the [up quark](#def:up-quark). Environment distinguishes them: the up quark threads a color tube on Bring's surface, while the electron orbits the nucleus via Hopf linking. We draw both the electron and the up quark as a solid trefoil to denote the forward time direction; context separates the lepton from the quark.

Only the knot identification is fixed. The numerical value of $m_e$ is CAL-1, the leptonic mass-scale input used to normalize the observable tower; it is not derived from a standalone trefoil formula.
