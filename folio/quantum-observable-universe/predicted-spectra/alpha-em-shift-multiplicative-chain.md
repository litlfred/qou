**Proposition (multiplicative-chain index identity).**
*The torus-knot shift exponents of [the shift proposition](#prop:alpha-shift-torus-knot) take the values*

$$
  \alpha_{\mathrm{shift}}(T_{2,9}) = 9, \qquad \alpha_{\mathrm{shift}}(T_{3,5}) = 10 .
$$

*Hence the chain form agrees with the bare form of [the EM coupling projection](#prop:em-coupling-projection):*

$$
  q \cdot
  \bigl[\alpha_{\mathrm{shift}}(T_{2,9})\bigr]_q \cdot
  \bigl[\alpha_{\mathrm{shift}}(T_{3,5})\bigr]_q
  \;=\;
  q \cdot [9]_q \cdot [10]_q .
$$

*That this product is $\alpha_{EM}^{-1}$ is the claim of the EM coupling projection, not of this proposition.*
