The **quotient ideals** $I_8$ and $I_9$ act on the Gröbner--Shirshov normal form ([shell closure normal form](#prop:shell-closure-normal-form)) as follows:

- **I$_{8}$ (adjacency correlation):** a reduced word $\sigma_i \sigma_{i+1}$ of length 2 is absorbed into $\sigma_{i+1}$ when the latter is present in the normal form.
- **I$_{9}$ (closed shell chain collapse):** a reduced word $w \cdot \sigma_j$ is absorbed when its prefix $w$ is present in the normal form.
