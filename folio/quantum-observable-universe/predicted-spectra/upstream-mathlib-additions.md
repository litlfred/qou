Three unconditional special-function definitions and boundary identities anchor the archimedean dilogarithm sector (see [Table 1](#tbl:upstream-mathlib-additions-table1-data)): (A) the complex dilogarithm power series $\mathrm{Li}_2(z) = \sum_{k \ge 0} z^{k+1}/(k+1)^2$ on the closed unit disk, satisfying $\mathrm{Li}_2(0) = 0$; (B) the single-valued Bloch–Wigner dilogarithm $D(z) = \mathrm{Im}\,\mathrm{Li}_2(z) + \log\lvert z\rvert\,\arg(1-z)$, satisfying $D(0) = 0$; and (C) the Clausen oddness identity $\mathrm{Cl}_2(-\theta) = -\mathrm{Cl}_2(\theta)$, which immediately yields $\mathrm{Cl}_2(0) = 0$.
