Let $F(z, c) \in R[z, c]$ be the color-tube cubic ([color-tube cubic](#rem:color-tube-cubic)) with roots $c_1(z), c_2(z), c_3(z)$ over the categorical base ring $R$. Let $\mathrm{S}_3$ act on the three color sheets by permutation. The canonical Wedderburn decomposition is

$$
  R[\mathrm{S}_3]
  \;\cong\;
  R \oplus R \oplus R^{2 \times 2}
  \quad
  (\text{trivial} \;\oplus\; \text{sign} \;\oplus\; \text{standard}).
$$

The integrand

$$
  \lambda_{\mathrm{baryon}}(z)
  \;:=\;
  \prod_{k=1}^{3} \frac{dc_k}{dz}
$$

is the projection onto the $\mathrm{Alt}^3$ (sign) isotype of the $\mathrm{S}_3$-module $\bigotimes_{k=1}^3 \,T_{c_k}\mathbf{C}$ generated by the color-sheet velocities. The sum $\sum_k dc_k/dz$ projects onto the trivial isotype. The sum $\sum_{k \neq l} (dc_k/dz)(c_l + z)$ projects onto the standard isotype.

Here the product $\prod_{k=1}^{3} dc_k/dz$ is the wedge $dc_1 \wedge dc_2 \wedge dc_3$ evaluated on the diagonal color-sheet frame (see the proof), antisymmetric under sheet exchange — not a symmetric product. This purely algebraic statement holds over $R$ and does not use the archimedean fibre functor $\tau \colon R \to \mathbb{R}$.

The $\mathrm{Alt}^3$ isotype is a *genuinely non-trivial* sign line, not a channel indistinguishable from the trivial one. The monodromy of the color-tube cubic around its discriminant is the full $\mathrm{S}_3$ — computed exactly over $R = \mathbb{Q}[z]$ and cross-checked at $50$ digits (six simple branch points, each carrying a transposition, together generating $\mathrm{S}_3$) — so its sign character is surjective onto $\{\pm 1\}$. Fixing the baryon to this sign isotype therefore fixes the integrand up to a scalar.
