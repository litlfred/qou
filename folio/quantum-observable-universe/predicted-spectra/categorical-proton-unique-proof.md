**Proof.** 
The argument inherits from the [uniqueness of hadrons](#prop:hadron-unique). The Hecke normal form is unique in $H_3(q)$. Permuting the colour tubes $(r, g, b)$ acts via inner Hecke automorphisms that fix the normal-form class, establishing the $S_3$-symmetry. Specifically, the three position assignments $(\mathfrak{u},\mathfrak{u},\mathfrak{d})$, $(\mathfrak{u},\mathfrak{d},\mathfrak{u})$, and $(\mathfrak{d},\mathfrak{u},\mathfrak{u})$ produce Hecke elements related by $S_3$-conjugation. They therefore reduce to the same normal form. Among feasible stabilisers, minimizing $|\mathrm{tr}_M|$ uniquely selects the LP-optimal gluon choice ($L_- = \sigma_1 \sigma_0$).
