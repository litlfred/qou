The Lagrangian endomorphism $\Phi \in \mathrm{End}_{\mathbf{C}}(A)$ of the categorical action decomposes over the irreducible representations of $H_n(q)$. This decomposition and the Frobenius self-duality $A \cong A^\vee$ determine the specific knot operators that mediate any interaction and their corresponding weights.

## Multi-channel interpretation

For Breit--Wheeler pair production $\gamma\gamma \to e^-e^+$, the full interaction is

$$
  \mathcal{S}(\gamma_1, \gamma_2)
  =
  \underbrace{d_{\mathrm{triv}}(q) \cdot P_{T_{1,0}}}_{\text{elastic: polarisation change}}
  +
  \underbrace{d_{\Box}(q) \cdot P_{T_{2,1}}}_{\text{single crossing}}
  +
  \underbrace{d_{\Box\Box}(q) \cdot P_{T_{2,2}}}_{\text{Hopf link (gluon)}}
  +
  \underbrace{d_{\yng(2,1)}(q) \cdot P_{T_{2,3}}}_{\text{trefoil}}
  +  \cdots
$$

All channels fire simultaneously. Two incoming photons can:

* change polarisation (trivial operator, no pair created),
* exchange a gluon ($T_{2,2}$, Hopf link),
* pass through the trefoil operator $T_{2,3}$; under the electron ruling ([electron](#def:electron-knot)) the $e^-e^+$ pair is read from the $\sigma_0\sigma_1\sigma_0$ coefficient ([Breit--Wheeler pair production](#def:breit-wheeler-pair-production)), not from this channel,
* pass through the cinquefoil operator $T_{2,5}$, suppressed by higher crossing energy; the muon word is not ruled, so this is not yet a $\mu^-\mu^+$ channel.

These events occur in a single interaction. The quantum dimension $d_\lambda(q)$ weights each channel. The branching ratios depend entirely on $q$ and the incoming photon polarisation content $\varphi_0, \varphi_1$.

Experiments detect the dominant channel, which carries the largest weight. The subdominant channels contribute to radiative corrections, vacuum polarisation, and the running of coupling constants. The elastic channel ($T_{1,0}$) is always present: photon--photon scattering without pair creation is the leading process, and pair creation is a threshold-activated subdominant channel.
