| Structural component | Mathematical content |
|---|---|
| Pentagon generator set $\mathcal{G}(F) \subset \mathbb{Z}[\mathcal{E}(F)]$ | Five-term alternating sums $\sum_{i=1}^{5} (-1)^{i-1}[e_i]$ |
| Pentagon subgroup $\mathcal{P}(F)$ | Subgroup generated by $\mathcal{G}(F)$ inside $\mathbb{Z}[\mathcal{E}(F)]$ |
| Bloch group $B(F)$ | Quotient abelian group $\mathbb{Z}[\mathcal{E}(F)] / \mathcal{P}(F)$ |
| Abelian group structure on $B(F)$ | Induced by the free abelian group on $\mathcal{E}(F)$ |
| Canonical projection $\pi \colon \mathbb{Z}[\mathcal{E}(F)] \to B(F)$ | Quotient homomorphism sending $\mathcal{P}(F)$ to $0$ |
| Linearity identities $\pi(0) = 0$, $\pi(a + b) = \pi(a) + \pi(b)$ | Additive homomorphism axioms for $\pi$ |
| Universal property of $B(F)$ | Every homomorphism vanishing on $\mathcal{P}(F)$ factors uniquely through $B(F)$ |
| Real specialization $B(\mathbb{R})$ | Concrete instantiation of the pentagon subgroup and quotient at $F = \mathbb{R}$ |
