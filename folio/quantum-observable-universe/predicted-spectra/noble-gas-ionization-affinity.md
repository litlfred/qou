## Ionization energy

The crossing energy of a closed sub-link represents a local maximum across each period. Removing an electron from a noble gas breaks a crossing in a fully interlocked chain. This costs more energy than removing an electron from an incomplete sub-link. Thus, noble gases sit at ionization-energy peaks.

## Electron affinity

A noble gas exhibits near-zero electron affinity. Attaching a new electron requires initiating a fresh sub-link at level $n_k+1$. This electron weakly Hopf-links to the outermost closed shell. The minimal linking number yields a binding energy negligible compared to thermal fluctuations.
