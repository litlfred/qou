**Breit--Wheeler pair production** $\gamma\gamma \to e^- e^+$ is the two-photon fusion product $(\gamma_1, \gamma_2) \mapsto \mathrm{NF}(\gamma_1 \cdot \gamma_2)$ in the three-strand Iwahori--Hecke algebra $H_3(q)$. For two color-dressed photons

$$
  \gamma_1 \;=\; \mathbf{1} + \varphi_0\sigma_0 + \varphi_1\sigma_1 + \varphi_{01}\sigma_0\sigma_1,
  \qquad
  \gamma_2 \;=\; \mathbf{1} + \psi_0\sigma_0 + \psi_1\sigma_1 + \psi_{01}\sigma_0\sigma_1
$$

with non-negative color weights $\varphi_i, \psi_i \ge 0$, the product $\gamma_1 \cdot \gamma_2$ reduces under the Hecke quadratic relation $\sigma_i^2 = h\sigma_i + \mathbf{1}$ ($h = q - q^{-1}$) and the braid relation $\sigma_0\sigma_1\sigma_0 = \sigma_1\sigma_0\sigma_1$ into the six-element Coxeter normal-form basis $\{\mathbf{1},\, \sigma_0,\, \sigma_1,\, L_+ = \sigma_0\sigma_1,\, L_- = \sigma_1\sigma_0,\, T_{w_0} = \sigma_0\sigma_1\sigma_0\}$ of $H_3(q)$. In the three-term specialization ($\varphi_{01} = \psi_{01} = 0$),

$$
  \mathrm{NF}(\gamma_1 \cdot \gamma_2)
  \;=\;
  (1 + \varphi_0\psi_0 + \varphi_1\psi_1)\mathbf{1}
  + (\varphi_0 + \psi_0 + \varphi_0\psi_0 h)\sigma_0
  + (\varphi_1 + \psi_1 + \varphi_1\psi_1 h)\sigma_1
  + \varphi_0\psi_1 L_+
  + \varphi_1\psi_0 L_-,
$$

while nonzero four-term Hopf-dressing weights ($\varphi_{01}, \psi_{01} \neq 0$) populate the longest-word channel $T_{w_0} = \sigma_0\sigma_1\sigma_0$. The combined pair-threshold weight is symmetric under photon exchange ($\gamma_1 \leftrightarrow \gamma_2$) and non-negative whenever the incoming color weights are non-negative.
