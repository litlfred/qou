## Derivation from the topological mass formula

The topological mass formula ([topological mass](#conj:topological-mass-formula)) gives the mass of a knot $K$ as $\mathfrak{m}(K) = \text{Vol}(K) / \hbar_q^2$. For a multi-component link $\mathcal{N}(Z, N)$, the "volume" is not the hyperbolic volume of a single knot complement, because the multi-nucleon link is not hyperbolic. Instead, the topological mass proof ([topological mass proof](#prf:topological-mass)) shows that each transient trace contribution evaluates to the quantum dilogarithm $\Phi_q(z_i)$ on the exceptional divisor $E_i$ ([quantum dilogarithm](#cor:quantum-dilogarithm)). Summing over all exceptional divisors (one per nucleon crossing) yields the total quantum dilogarithm volume $\Phi_q$.

The identification $\Phi_q = \sum \log |\hat{V}_i|$ connects the cosimplicial structure of Ch3 (the Yekutieli triples and crossed groupoid) to the Iwahori–Hecke algebra computation of Ch5 (the generator volumes).
