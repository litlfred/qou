## Proposition

**Let $q > 1$ and let $\mathcal{N}(p, n)$ be the Gröbner--Shirshov normal form of the nuclear braid in $H_A(q)$, constructed by sequential Hopf linking with alternating $\mathfrak{p}$-$\mathfrak{n}$ filling.**

**(i)** *Normal form. $\mathcal{N}(p, n)$ is in Gröbner--Shirshov normal form for every $A \geq 1$, with at most $A!$ terms (the dimension of $H_A(q)$ over $\mathbb{Z}[q^{\pm 1}]$, equal to $|S_A|$) — a finite representation for any nucleus.*

**(ii)** *Symmetric histogram at shell closures. When $p = n$ (doubly-even nuclei), the word-length histogram is a palindrome,*

$$
  h(\ell) =  h(L - \ell)
  \qquad \text{for all } 0 \leq \ell \leq L,
$$

*where $L$ is the maximum word length in the support (the Poincaré polynomials of $S_4$, $S_6$; see [Table 1](#tbl:shell-closure-normal-form-table1-data)). For $p \neq n$ the histogram is not a palindrome.*

**(iii)** *Dominant term at shell closure. The coefficient of the dominant term jumps discontinuously at shell closures, with ratio to the previous step larger at closures ($\times 1.56, \times 2.10, \times 2.95$) than off-closure ($\times 1.25, \times 1.41, \times 1.55$; see [Table 2](#tbl:shell-closure-normal-form-table2-data)).*

**(iv)** *Shell energy gap. The Hecke reduction energy $\Delta E_k$ exhibits a gap at shell breaks: it drops at the closure ($0.274 \to 0.095$) then jumps at the next step ($0.095 \to 1.409$), the ratio $1.409 / 0.095 = 14.8$ being the shell gap in Hecke energy units (see [Table 3](#tbl:shell-closure-normal-form-table3-data)).*
