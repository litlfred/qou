Let $\mathcal{N}(Z, N)$ be a nuclear Gröbner–Shirshov normal form, and let $\beta_{T_{3,5}} = (\sigma_1\sigma_2)^5$ be the braid word of the weak-force torus knot $T_{3,5}$. A **$\beta^-$ transition** inserts $\beta_{T_{3,5}}$ at the converting $\mathfrak{d}$-quark crossing of $\mathcal{N}(Z, N)$, followed by re-reduction:

$$
  \mathcal{N}(Z, N) \cdot \beta_{T_{3,5}}
  \xrightarrow{ \text{reduce}}
  \mathcal{N}(Z + 1,  N - 1)
  \sqcup
  T_{2,3}
  \sqcup
  K_{0_1}.
$$

A **$\beta^+$ transition** is the mirror operation. It inserts the conjugate $T_{3,5}^{\sigma}$ braid with $q \leftrightarrow q^{-1}$:

$$
  \mathcal{N}(Z, N) \cdot \beta_{T_{3,5}}^{\sigma}
  \xrightarrow{ \text{reduce}}
  \mathcal{N}(Z - 1,  N + 1)
  \sqcup
  T_{2,3}^{\sigma}
  \sqcup
  K_{0_1}.
$$
