## Proposition: magic numbers from torus knot shell sizes

Let $q > 1$, and build the nucleus by inductive Gröbner--Shirshov reduction, each shell-boundary crossing mediated by the inter-shell torus-knot operator $\Phi_{\mathrm{shell}}^{(\ell)}$ (Chebyshev crossing coefficients of $T_{2, n_s}$). A shell closes exactly when the word-length histogram of $\bar{\mathcal{N}}(p, n)$ is palindromic, at nucleon count $A_{\mathrm{close}}$, and the closing shell of size $n_s = A_{\mathrm{close}} - A_{\mathrm{prev}}$ is bound by $T_{2, n_s}$. Each embeddable torus knot $T_{r,s}$ on the Bring surface assigns a shell capacity

$$
  \bigl|\mathrm{shell}(T_{r,s})\bigr| = r \times s
$$

(see [Table 2](#tbl:torus-knot-shell-binding-table2-data)). The construction is **proposed** to reproduce the experimental magic numbers

$$
  \{2,\, 8,\, 20,\, 28,\, 50,\, 82,\, 126\}
$$

as cumulative same-type shell occupancies (see [Table 3](#tbl:torus-knot-shell-binding-table3-data)). Whereas the graded spin–orbit Pauli fiber derives $\{2, 8, 20, 28, 50, 82, 126\}$ directly from three-dimensional oscillator degeneracies plus the stretched $j = \ell + \tfrac{1}{2}$ intruder subshell ([spin–orbit magic closures](#prop:spin-orbit-magic-closures)), reconciling the Bring-surface $r \times s$ torus-knot capacities of Table 2 with the Table-3 shell occupancies inside the purely one-dimensional braid-histogram induction is conjectural ([shell construction rules](#rem:shell-construction-rules), [Bring magic-number conjecture](#conj:magic-numbers-bring)). The Hecke energy gap at a shell boundary scales as $|\lambda_+(T_{2,n_s})/\lambda_-(T_{2,n_s})| = q^{2 n_s}$; for the first shell closure (He-4 $\to$ Li-5),

$$
  \frac{\Delta E_5}{\Delta E_4} \approx \frac{1.409}{0.095} = 14.8 .
$$
