The determinant locus and facial rank drop analyzed for the convention-C Gram pencil in [Spectrahedral collapse and conformal rigidity](#prop:spectrahedral-phase-collapse)---together with the permanent rank-$5$ quotient geometry of the canonical convention-D trace---belong to a general convex-algebraic mechanism studied across algebraic optimization, quantum many-body physics, and high-dimensional conic geometry.

## Algebraic boundaries and theta-body hierarchies of spectrahedra

In convex algebraic geometry, **Cynthia Vinzant** \cite{vinzant2014} characterizes the boundary of a spectrahedron $\{x : G(x) \succeq 0\}$ through the determinantal hypersurface $\det G(x) = 0$ and its stratified singular locus, where the positive-semidefinite pencil drops rank and the feasible region collapses onto a lower-dimensional face. **João Gouveia**, **Pablo A. Parrilo**, and **Rekha R. Thomas** \cite{gouveia-parrilo-thomas2010} introduced theta bodies of polynomial ideals to govern when projected spectrahedral relaxations collapse exactly onto the convex hull of an algebraic variety, while **Monique Laurent** \cite{laurent2009} and **Christine Bachoc** and **Frank Vallentin** \cite{bachoc-vallentin2008} developed symmetry-adapted moment and semidefinite hierarchies on rank-deficient invariant Gram cones. Under convention D, where the $q$-antisymmetriser lies in the Gram radical for every $q$, positivity acts natively on the five-dimensional quotient cone rather than through a scalar determinant root.

## Reduced-density-matrix faces, bootstrap kinks, and conic thresholds

In variational quantum mechanics, **Valentin Zauner**, **David Draxler**, **Laurens Vanderstraeten**, **Jutho Haegeman**, and **Frank Verstraete** \cite{zauner-verstraete2016} showed that symmetry-breaking and critical phase boundaries coincide with singular rank-drop faces of projected reduced-density-matrix spectrahedra. The same facial boundary mechanism drives the extremal-functional kinks of **Sheer El-Showk**, **Miguel F. Paulos**, **David Poland**, **Slava Rychkov**, **David Simmons-Duffin**, and **Alessandro Vichi** \cite{elshowk2012,simmons-duffin2015} in conformal field theory, as well as the confinement--deconfinement Gram rank-drop walls identified in lattice gauge bootstraps by **Vladimir Kazakov** and **Zechuan Zheng** \cite{kazakov-zheng2023}. In conic integral geometry, **David L. Donoho** and **Jared Tanner** \cite{donoho-tanner2009} and **Dennis Amelunxen**, **Martin Lotz**, **Michael B. McCoy**, and **Joel A. Tropp** \cite{amelunxen-lotz-mccoy-tropp2014} proved that sharp feasibility thresholds in linear and semidefinite programs are governed by facial projection counts and statistical dimension---a finite-dimensional geometric transition certified algebraically by exact rational dual multipliers and strict complementary slackness.
