**Remark (Three analytic and algebraic prerequisites).**

Carrying the hyperbolic mass-tower decomposition across the archimedean bridge rests on three foundational structures:

| # | Structure | Core mathematical data | Boundary specialisation |
|---|---|---|---|
| (1) | Bloch group $\mathcal{B}(F)$ | Free abelian pre-Bloch group $\mathcal{P}(F) = \mathbb{Z}[F \setminus \{0,1\}]$ modulo the five-term pentagon subgroup, with the Bloch–Wigner regulator | Degenerate symbols $z \in \{0, 1\}$ excluded |
| (2) | Pentagon identity for $\mathrm{Cl}_2$ | Five-term functional equation in general position and at $\theta = \pi/3$ | $\mathrm{Cl}_2(0) = 0$ |
| (3) | Faddeev–Kashaev quasi-classical limit | Asymptotic convergence $(1-q)\log\Phi_n(x; q) \to \mathrm{Li}_2(x)$ | Exact vanishing at $x = 0$ |

Collecting all three hypotheses into a single composite datum isolates the analytic inputs required downstream while confirming their mutual consistency at the boundary specialisations.

## (1) The Bloch group $\mathcal{B}(F)$

For a field $F$, the Bloch group arises as a subquotient of the formal group ring on punctured projective parameters:

- A **pre-Bloch generator** is an element $[z]$ with $z \in F \setminus \{0, 1\}$.
- The **pre-Bloch group** $\mathcal{P}(F)$ is the free abelian group generated by all pre-Bloch elements $[z]$.
- The **pentagon subgroup** $\mathcal{R}_5(F) \subset \mathcal{P}(F)$ is generated by the classical five-term cross-ratio relations, so that the quotient $\mathcal{P}(F)/\mathcal{R}_5(F)$ forms an abelian group admitting the Bloch–Wigner regulator when $F \subseteq \mathbb{C}$.

## (2) Pentagon identity for $\mathrm{Cl}_2$

The five-term identity for the Clausen function $\mathrm{Cl}_2$ is the imaginary-part projection of the standard dilogarithm pentagon:

$$
  \mathrm{Cl}_2(\alpha) - \mathrm{Cl}_2(\beta) + \mathrm{Cl}_2(\gamma)
   - \mathrm{Cl}_2(\delta) + \mathrm{Cl}_2(\varepsilon) = 0
$$

for angular arguments $(\alpha, \beta, \gamma, \delta, \varepsilon)$ satisfying the pentagon closure relation. Both the general five-term identity and its arithmetic evaluation at $\pi/3$ enter the hyperbolic volume decomposition, while at the origin all five terms vanish identically since $\mathrm{Cl}_2(0) = 0$.

## (3) Faddeev–Kashaev quasi-classical limit

The [dilogarithm archimedean limit](#conj:dilogarithm-archimedean-limit) specifies the convergence

$$
  (1 - q) \cdot \log \Phi_n(x; q) \;\to\; \mathrm{Li}_2(x)
$$

along the scaling regime $q \to 1$, $n \to \infty$, and $n(1-q) \to \infty$ of Faddeev (1994) and Kashaev (1997). At the boundary $x = 0$, both sides vanish identically.

## Analytic obligations for unconditional discharge

Replacing these structural hypotheses by unconditional derivations requires three classical ingredients:

1. **Explicit quotient construction for $\mathcal{B}(F)$:** enumerating the five-term pentagon subgroup inside the free abelian pre-Bloch group $\mathcal{P}(F)$ and verifying its universal property.
2. **Fourier-analytic proof of the $\mathrm{Cl}_2$ pentagon:** establishing the five-term Clausen relation directly by absolutely convergent trigonometric-series rearrangements.
3. **Uniform Tauberian asymptotics on $x \in (0, 1)$:** bounding the Riemann-sum remainder of $(1-q)\log\Phi_n(x; q)$ uniformly as $q \to 1$.
