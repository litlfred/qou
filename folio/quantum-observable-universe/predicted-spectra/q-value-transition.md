Let $\mathcal{N}(Z,N)$ be the normal form of a nucleus with $Z$ protons and $N$ neutrons, and let $\mathfrak{S}_q(\mathcal{N})$ be its [categorical action](#def:categorical-action). For a $\beta^-$ transition $\mathcal{N}(Z,N) \to \mathcal{N}(Z{+}1,N{-}1) + e^- + \bar{\nu}_e$, the :defterm[$\mathcal{Q}_q$-value]{#q-value} is the categorical action difference between the parent and the products:

$$
  \mathcal{Q}_q
  = 
  \mathfrak{S}_q\bigl(\mathcal{N}(Z,N)\bigr) -
  \mathfrak{S}_q\bigl(\mathcal{N}(Z{+}1,N{-}1)\bigr) -
  \mathfrak{S}_q(e) -
  \mathfrak{S}_q(\bar{\nu}_e).
$$

When $q$ is fixed we write $Q$ for $\mathcal{Q}_q$.
