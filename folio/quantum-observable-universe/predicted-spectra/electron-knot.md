The **electron** corresponds to the trefoil knot $T_{2,3}$ in the lepton sector.

$$
  e^- =  T_{2,3} \qquad \text{(trefoil, solid ●)}
$$

It shares this knot type with the up quark. Environment distinguishes them: the up quark threads a color tube on Bring's surface, while the electron orbits the nucleus via Hopf linking.

Both the electron and the up quark are drawn as a solid trefoil to denote the forward time direction; context separates the lepton from the quark.

Only the knot identification is fixed here. The numerical value of $m_e$ is CAL-1, the leptonic mass-scale input used to normalize the observable tower; it is not derived here from a standalone trefoil formula.
