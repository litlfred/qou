The **electron** corresponds to the trefoil knot $T_{2,3}$ in the lepton sector.

$$
  e^- =  T_{2,3} \qquad \text{(trefoil, solid ●)}
$$

It is the lepton partner of the [up quark](#def:up-quark) in the [knot–particle dictionary](#rem:knot-particle-dictionary). The two do not share a knot type: the up quark is an unknot in a color tube on Bring's surface, distinguished by its positive crossing orientation in $\mathcal{B}_3$, while the electron is the trefoil and orbits the nucleus via Hopf linking.

The electron is drawn as a solid trefoil to denote the forward time direction.

Only the knot identification is fixed. The numerical value of $m_e$ is CAL-1, the leptonic mass-scale input used to normalize the observable tower; it is not derived here from a standalone trefoil formula.
