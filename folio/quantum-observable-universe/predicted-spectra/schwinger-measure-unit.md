The **Schwinger proper-time measure with unit $\varsigma$** is the scale-invariant density on the proper-time half-line,

$$
\mu_\varsigma \;=\; \varsigma\,\frac{ds}{s}, \qquad s \in (0, \infty),
$$

where $s$ is the Schwinger proper-time variable of the one-loop determinant and the scalar $\varsigma$ is the **Schwinger measure unit**. The **canonical unit** is $\varsigma = 1$.
