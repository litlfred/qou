## Analytic components

**(A)** Complex dilogarithm series.
Defining $\mathrm{Li}_2(z) = \sum_{k \ge 0} z^{k+1}/(k+1)^2$ provides a concrete power-series representation on the closed unit disk $\lvert z\rvert \le 1$, where the $1/(k+1)^2$ decay guarantees absolute convergence by comparison with the geometric and $p$-series bounds.

**(B)** Bloch–Wigner regulator.
Combining the imaginary part of $\mathrm{Li}_2(z)$ with the logarithmic branch-cut correction $\log\lvert z\rvert\,\arg(1-z)$ defines the single-valued real-analytic function $D \colon \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{R}$. Establishing the standard five-term pentagon identity for $D(z)$ reduces to absolute convergence of $\mathrm{Li}_2(z)$ on the unit circle together with Spence's functional equations for the real and imaginary parts of $\mathrm{Li}_2$.

**(C)** Clausen functional equations.
Termwise oddness $\sin(-k\theta) = -\sin(k\theta)$ in the Fourier series $\mathrm{Cl}_2(\theta) = \sum_{k \ge 1} \sin(k\theta)/k^2$ yields the reflection identity $\mathrm{Cl}_2(-\theta) = -\mathrm{Cl}_2(\theta)$ unconditionally. Analogous series rearrangements yield periodicity $\mathrm{Cl}_2(\theta + 2\pi) = \mathrm{Cl}_2(\theta)$ and half-period reflection.

## Structural role

Coupling the explicit series definitions of $\mathrm{Li}_2(z)$ and $D(z)$ with the unconditional boundary and symmetry identities $\mathrm{Li}_2(0) = 0$, $D(0) = 0$, and $\mathrm{Cl}_2(-\theta) = -\mathrm{Cl}_2(\theta)$ replaces abstract function-theoretic axioms with concrete series-convergence and functional-equation obligations.
