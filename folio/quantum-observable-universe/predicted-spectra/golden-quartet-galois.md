The golden quartet

$$
  Q  :=  \bigl\{\,1/\varphi,\;-\varphi,\;\varphi,\;-1/\varphi\,\bigr\}
$$

is the complete root set of the quartic polynomial

$$
  P(x)  :=  x^4 - 3x^2 + 1
   =  (x^2 - x - 1)(x^2 + x - 1)
$$

over $\mathbb{Q}$, and $\varphi \cdot (1/\varphi) = 1$. Replacing $\sqrt{5}$ by $-\sqrt{5}$ in $\varphi = (1+\sqrt{5})/2$ gives $-1/\varphi$, and in $1/\varphi = (\sqrt{5}-1)/2$ gives $-\varphi$.

The finer structure (the two Galois orbits $Q_+ = \{\varphi, -1/\varphi\}$ and $Q_- = \{1/\varphi, -\varphi\}$ of $\mathrm{Gal}(\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{5})/\mathbb{Q})$, their exchange by the bar involution $\iota \colon x \mapsto x^{-1}$, and the transitive faithful action of $\mathrm{Gal}(\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{5})/\mathbb{Q}) \times \langle\iota\rangle \cong \mathbb{Z}/2 \times \mathbb{Z}/2$ on $Q$) is argued in the proof but is not part of this formal statement.
