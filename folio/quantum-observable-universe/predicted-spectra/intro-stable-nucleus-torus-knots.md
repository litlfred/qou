The categorical bound-state assignment distinguishes mesons from nucleons via braid words. However, it does not determine which configurations survive. Stability requires the colour braid to admit a presentation as a torus knot $T(p, q)$. The Hopf fibration over the torus stabilises such knots under the substrate-parameter flow.

We define the stable-nucleus subspace and establish the torus-Hopf factorisation as the obstruction to decay. We identify the relevant torus knots as the prime-power family $T(p^n, q^n)$. We then confirm the uniqueness of the genus-four torus knot mediating the strong force. A Bring-surface argument pins down this force-carrier knot uniquely. This provides the exact assignment between stable isotopes and $T(p^n, q^n)$.
