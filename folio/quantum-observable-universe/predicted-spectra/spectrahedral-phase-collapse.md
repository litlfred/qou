# Spectrahedral Phase Collapse

**Proposition (auxiliary $z_{\mathrm{aux}} = u/(u^2+1)$ Gram pencil).**
*In the pre-Archimedean $q$-regime, write $u = \sqrt{q}$ and let $G(u)$ be the Gram matrix of the Frobenius bilinear form on the Gröbner–Shirshov normal-form basis of $\mathbf{H}_3(q)$, evaluated at the auxiliary loop weight $z_{\mathrm{aux}} = u/(u^2+1) = 1/(q^{1/2}+q^{-1/2})$. Then on $u > 0$ the form degenerates at exactly two points,*

$$
  u = 1/\psi \approx 0.68233, \qquad \psi^3 - \psi^2 - 1 = 0,
$$

*where $\psi$ is the Supergolden Ratio, and*

$$
  u = 1, \qquad \textit{i.e. } q = 1 .
$$

*The supergolden zero has multiplicity $5$ and the classical zero multiplicity $1$. At each, $G$ acquires a kernel and the formal solution space collapses from the higher-dimensional bulk onto a strictly lower-dimensional face of the Hecke spectrahedron.*

The supergolden threshold is the inverse of the first Super Metallic Mean. The substrate sits above both zeros, at $u_0 = \sqrt{q_0} \approx 1.0536$, inside the region $u > 1$ on which $G$ is non-singular — so neither degeneracy is reached by the physical anchor, and the collapse remains distinct from the aeon instantiation at the boundary $q \to 1^+$.

## Scope: comparison with the canonical Markov parameter $z = q^3/(q^2+1)$

At $z_{\mathrm{aux}} = u/(u^2+1)$, the exact factorisation over $\mathbb{Q}(u)$ is

$$
  \det G(u) = -\,\frac{(u-1)(u+1)\,(u^3+u-1)^5\,(u^3+u^2+1)^5\,S_1(u)\,S_2(u)}{u^{10}\,(u^2+1)^{12}},
$$

with $S_1, S_2$ sextic polynomials having no real roots; $(u^3+u^2+1)^5$ has its sole real root at $u = -\psi < 0$, leaving $u = 1/\psi$ and $u = 1$ as the only positive zeros.

At the canonical Markov parameter $z = q^3/(q^2+1)$, by contrast, the $6 \times 6$ Gram determinant on $H_3(q)$ vanishes **identically for every $q$** — $z = q^3/(q^2+1)$ is a polynomial root of $\det G(q, z)$, with null vector spanned by the three-strand $q$-antisymmetriser. Consequently, the isolated supergolden determinant root $u = 1/\psi$ is a property of the auxiliary $z_{\mathrm{aux}} = u/(u^2+1)$ pencil and does not arise on the canonical Markov–Gram form, where positivity acts instead on the five-dimensional Temperley–Lieb quotient.
