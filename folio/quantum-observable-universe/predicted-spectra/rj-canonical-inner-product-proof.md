## Proof

**(i)** The Markov trace factors through the Grothendieck ring: $\mathrm{tr}_M(a) = \sum_\lambda d_\lambda(q) \cdot \mathrm{tr}_\lambda(a)$, where $\mathrm{tr}_\lambda$ is the character of irrep $\lambda$, up to the factor $[2]_q^{-r}$ that converts $d_\lambda$ into the block weight $w_\lambda$. At $z = q^3/(q^2+1)$ the framing ratio is $z/(z - (q - q^{-1})) = q^4$, which collapses each Ocneanu numerator into a quantum integer and gives $w_\lambda = d_\lambda(q)/[2]_q^{\,r}$ with $d_\lambda$ the $\mathfrak{sl}_2$ hook-content dimension ([Markov parameter reconciliation](#rem:markov-z-reconciliation)). Restricting to the class functions, spanned by $\{\chi_\lambda\}$, gives the stated diagonal form.

**(ii)** The irreps $\{\chi_\lambda : \lambda \vdash r\}$ form an orthogonal basis of the center $Z(H_r(q))$ under the Markov trace pairing. Schur orthogonality $\mathrm{tr}_M(\chi_\lambda \chi_\mu) = d_\lambda \delta_{\lambda\mu}$ follows from the semisimplicity of $H_r(q)$ for generic $q$ \cite{rosso-jones1993}; the classes with more than two rows have $d_\lambda = 0$ and lie in the radical. Any other bilinear form diagonal in $\{\chi_\lambda\}$ and normalized at the identity differs only by rescaling the Markov parameter $z$.

**(iii)** The Rosso–Jones formula \cite{rosso-jones1993} decomposes $J(\tau(r,s))$ over irreps of $H_r(q)$. Each irrep contributes its quantum dimension $d_\lambda(q)$ multiplied by the $s$-th power of its full-twist eigenvalue $\omega_\lambda$. This provides the inner product $\langle \omega^s, [\mathrm{triv}] \rangle_{\mathrm{RJ}}$, where $[\mathrm{triv}]$ denotes the class of the trivial representation in $R(H_r(q))$. $\square$
