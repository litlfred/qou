## Error structure

Let $\varepsilon = \delta q / q \approx 1.2 \times 10^{-8}$ (CODATA). The parametric error is $O(\varepsilon)$, which is negligible. The structural errors are $O(1)$ relative to $\varepsilon$: at $z = q^3/(q^2+1)$ they range from $8$–$98\%$ for $\mathcal{Q}_{\mathrm{bare}}$ and $1$–$100\%$ for the combined formula across the six benchmark decays tabulated in the [interpretation](#rem:spectral-decomposition-qvalue-interpretation). These residuals arise from nucleon ordering in $\mathcal{Q}_{\mathrm{bare}}$, degree-$2$ Rosso--Jones weighting over two knots, degree-tower truncation, and, for filled shells, the vanishing of the $(1^3)$ Pauli branch at $z = q^3/(q^2+1)$. Whether the decomposition is complete is a separate claim; these residuals do not test it.
