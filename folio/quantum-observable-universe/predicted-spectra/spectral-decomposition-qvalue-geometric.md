## Error structure

Let $\varepsilon = \delta q / q \approx 1.2 \times 10^{-8}$ (CODATA). The parametric error is $O(\varepsilon)$, which is negligible. The structural errors are $O(1)$ relative to $\varepsilon$: under convention D they are $8$–$98\%$ for $\mathcal{Q}_{\mathrm{bare}}$ and $1$–$100\%$ for the combined formula, over the six benchmark decays tabulated in the [interpretation](#rem:spectral-decomposition-qvalue-interpretation). Until 2026-10-10 this paragraph quoted $2$–$10\%$, a range measured under the excised convention C. These errors arise from nucleon ordering in $\mathcal{Q}_{\mathrm{bare}}$, degree-$2$ Rosso--Jones weighting over two knots, the degree tower truncation, and, for filled shells, the vanishing of the Pauli branch under convention D. Whether the decomposition is complete is a separate claim; these residuals do not test it.
