**Statement.**
Let $\mathcal{A}(p,n)$ be a stable atom whose electron cloud decomposes as

$$
\mathcal{E} = p \cdot e^- = S_1 \sqcup \cdots \sqcup S_k \sqcup R,
$$

where each $S_i$ is a closed sub-Hopf link and $R$ is the residual sub-link. The atom $\mathcal{A}$ exhibits metallic character if and only if:

1. **Incompleteness**: $|R| < 2n_{k+1}^2$. The outermost sub-link contains unfilled linking sites.
2. **Low crossing energy**: $\mathfrak{E}_{\mathrm{cross}}(R) \ll \mathfrak{E}_{\mathrm{cross}}(S_k)$. The outermost electrons link loosely relative to the inner closed shells.
3. **Site availability**: $R$ contains at least one open linking site. The atom permits Hopf linking to an adjacent residual sub-link without disrupting the closed inner shells $S_i$.
