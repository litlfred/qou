The [quantum characteristic classes](#def:quantum-characteristic-classes) $\kappa_n \in H^2(\mathcal{S}, \mathcal{B})$ obstruct lifting at every level of the [cosimplicial crossed groupoid](#prop:cosimplicial-crossed-groupoid). The classes $\kappa_n$ vanish exactly at the levels $n \mid \chi(A)$ and persist at every other level (item 3 of the [QU-to-QOU theorem](#thm:qu-to-qou)). Since infinitely many levels survive, the brane tower never collapses to finite height ([Yekutieli lifting](#prop:yekutieli-lifting)).

Each hyperbolic volume $\mathrm{Vol}(K)$ is the classical limit ($q \to 1$) of the quantum dilogarithm sum across the brane tower. A polynomial relation $P(\mathrm{Vol}(4_1), \mathrm{Vol}(5_2), \mathrm{Vol}(\mathcal{B}_3)) = 0$ over $\mathbb{Q}[q, q^{-1}]$ would algebraically constrain the Chern--Simons invariants of the complements $S^3 \setminus 4_1$, $S^3 \setminus 5_2$, and $S^3 \setminus \mathcal{B}_3$. By the Chern--Simons/volume correspondence, this constraint would force holonomy contributions at different tower levels to close up after finitely many levels.

The cosimplicial structure theorem guarantees an infinite tower. The obstruction classes $\kappa_n$ never simultaneously vanish. The crossed groupoid does not degenerate. This realizes the [motivic perpetual obstruction theorem](#thm:motivic-perpetual-obstruction). The motivic Galois character of the aether is non-trivial at every finite place $p \nmid \chi(A)$. The class $\kappa_n$ is the HKR image of that character at level $n$ ([Hochschild–Kummer connection](#rem:hochschild-kummer-connection)). Consequently, $\kappa_n$ cannot vanish at the corresponding cyclotomic levels. The algebraic independence of the volumes is the *transcendence shadow* of the non-vanishing motivic Galois character. The tower never terminates. No polynomial envelope binds the resulting volumes.

Rigorous proof requires controlling the arithmetic of the quantum dilogarithm at every tower level. This control demands new results analogous to Nesterenko's theorem on the algebraic independence of $E$-function values. These results must generalize to quantum dilogarithm series and the cosimplicial filtration of $\mathrm{SU}_q(2)$-gerbes.
