This section constructs the Bloch-group quotient, establishes the $\mathrm{Cl}_2$ pentagon and partial Faddeev dilogarithm identities formalized in the Lean blueprint, and summarises the upstream real-analytic lemmas connecting polylogarithmic periods to the mass tower.
