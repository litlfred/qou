The **positron** is the mirror trefoil $T_{2,3}^{\sigma}$ in the lepton sector, the antiparticle dual of the electron:

$$
  e^+ =  T_{2,3}^{\sigma}. \qquad \text{(mirror trefoil)}
$$

Like the electron, environment distinguishes the positron from its quark counterpart (the down quark). The mirror operation $\sigma$ reverses all crossings. This reversed crossing chirality shifts the Coulomb charge from $Q = -1$ to $Q = +1$.

Diagrammatically, the positron is drawn as an outlined trefoil to denote reversed time direction. In the Reeb flow, crossing reversal reverses the time orientation, so the positron is an electron with reversed time orientation, recovering the Feynman–Stueckelberg interpretation.
