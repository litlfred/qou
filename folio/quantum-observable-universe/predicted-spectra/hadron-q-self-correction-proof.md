## Net signed crossing

The $q$-deformed wedge product $\wedge_q$ in the gauge sector of the canonical action ([canonical action](#prop:qou-canonical-action)) assigns weights to crossings:
- $q$ to $\mathfrak{p}$--$\mathfrak{p}$ crossings (positive).
- $q^{-1}$ to $\mathfrak{n}$--$\mathfrak{n}$ crossings (negative).
- $(q + q^{-1})/2$ to $\mathfrak{p}$--$\mathfrak{n}$ crossings (neutral average).

Neutral crossings contribute symmetrically and produce no shift in $q$. The imbalance between positive and negative crossings generates the aether field deformation. The $A^2$ normalization reflects linking density: crossings occupy a volume proportional to $A$, making the crossing density per unit volume $\ell_{\mathrm{net}} / A^2$.
