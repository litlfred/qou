**Remark (why a full shell is inert).**
Each electron is the word $e^- = \sigma_0\sigma_1\sigma_0 \in B_3$ ([electron knot](#def:electron-knot)), the positive half twist on three strands, with writhe $3$. Its closure is the Hopf link. A closed sub-Hopf link at level $n$ is a chain where every electron is fully linked with its neighbours. No crossing can be added or removed without breaking the sub-link.

This yields:
> **Full shell = no free linking site.**

A shell fills by exhausting available linking sites at its level, not by reaching a numerical threshold. Topological rigidity drives inertness. No crossing remains available for a new electron.

**Remark (mathematical novelty).**
This topological characterization replaces heuristic chemistry with a rigid sub-Hopf link invariant. It formally binds empirical ionization energy peaks to crossing-energy maxima.
