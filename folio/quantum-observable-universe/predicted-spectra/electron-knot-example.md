The electron is the simplest nontrivial torus knot embedded on $\Sigma_4$: the trefoil $T_{2,3}$. Its Seifert genus is $(2-1)(3-1)/2 = 1 \leq 4$. With $3$ crossings, the trefoil carries $3$ units of information. Its Jones polynomial is:

$$
  J_{T_{2,3}}(q) = -q^{-4} + q^{-3} + q^{-1}
$$

This example records the trefoil geometry only. The electron mass $m_e$ is CAL-1, the leptonic mass-scale input used to normalize the observable tower; it is not derived here from a standalone trefoil formula.
