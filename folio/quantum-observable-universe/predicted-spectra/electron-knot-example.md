The electron word $\sigma_0\sigma_1\sigma_0$ closes to the positive Hopf link $T_{2,2}$, which embeds on $\Sigma_4$: it already lies on a torus, of genus $1 \leq 4$. Its two components bound an annulus, so its Seifert genus is $0$. A minimal diagram has $2$ crossings; the word itself has $3$, so its closure diagram is not minimal.

This example records the link geometry only. The electron mass $m_e$ is CAL-1, the leptonic mass-scale input used to normalize the observable tower; it is not derived here from the link.
