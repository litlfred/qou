Adjoining $Z$ outer electron strands to the nuclear core in $H_{A+Z}(q)$ extends the skein factorisation from nuclear binding to atomic shell structure. The electron-strand transfer operator decomposes into eigenspaces whose eigenvalues label the principal and azimuthal atomic shells, while the quantum dilogarithm volume governs the inter-shell energy gaps and Reidemeister moves mediate radiative transitions between states of differing writhe.

This section establishes the shell-structure eigenvalue theorem, derives the quantum-dilogarithm shell-volume relation, separates purely $q$-algebraic shell invariants from those requiring the genus-$4$ geometry of Bring's surface, and proves the shell-closure normal-form theorem using the Gröbner–Shirshov quotient ideals $I_8, I_9$ and the torus-knot shell-binding hierarchy $T_{2,3} \to T_{2,5} \to T_{2,7} \to T_{2,9}$, against which non-magic shells are carried by the Hopf link alone.
