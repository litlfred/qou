**Remark (Bloch-Wigner dilogarithm $D$ on the unit circle).**

Restricting the Bloch–Wigner dilogarithm to unit-circle shape parameters $z = e^{i\theta}$ reduces the two-term formula to the Clausen function:

$$
  D(z) \;:=\; \mathrm{Im}(\mathrm{Li}_2(z))\;+\; \log|z| \cdot \arg(1 - z),
\qquad
  D(e^{i\theta}) \;=\; \mathrm{Cl}_2(\theta).
$$

Because $\log|z|$ vanishes on $|z| = 1$, this restriction covers the root-of-unity shape parameters governing the figure-eight and Borromean complements.
