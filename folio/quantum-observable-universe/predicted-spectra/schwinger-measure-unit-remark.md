The shape of the [Schwinger proper-time measure](#def:schwinger-measure-unit) is rigid: a density $f$ satisfying the scale-invariance functional equation $f(\lambda s)\,\lambda = f(s)$ equals $f(1)/s$, so $\mu_\varsigma$ is the unique scale-invariant choice up to the single scalar $\varsigma$. Scale invariance is inherited from the [conformal invariance of the horizon boundary datum](#prop:horizon-conformal-invariance): the loop's ultraviolet end has no preferred proper-time scale.

For the canonical unit $\varsigma = 1$, in the logarithmic coordinate the measure of one e-fold $[s, e\,s]$ is exactly $\varsigma$, and with $k_B = 1$ (entropy in nats, [Boltzmann constant](#def:boltzmann-constant)) the unit is denominated in nats per proper-time e-fold. Downstream consumers reference this for the per-loop measure normalization; under the canonical unit and the per-channel $\tfrac12$ split, the working per-channel coefficient is $\tfrac12 \cdot \frac{1}{4\pi} = \frac{1}{8\pi}$.

**Conjectural backing of canonicity.** Defining $\mu_1$ names a construction; the *canonicity* of the choice $\varsigma = 1$ is not proved. It is conditionally forced by the [computational-time identification](#conj:schwinger-measure-computational-time), through the moment identity $n \cdot N(n) = n!$ for the binding normalizer $N(n)$, and, separately, by the framework premises P1 (substrate scale-freedom) and P2 (proper time as computational time) stated there. Unconditionally it remains a unit convention of the same species the framework canonizes elsewhere for discrete time steps. Empirical support is exact rather than numerical: the unit-measure moment identity holds at orders $1$ through $6$, and a pin-invariance falsifier eliminates every intrinsic per-step rival unit.

**Notation.** The unit is written $\varsigma$ (varsigma). The archimedean Lean formalisation writes $c$ for the same scalar; the notation register reserves plain $c$ for color, with $c_q$ and $c_k$ also taken, so this paper uses $\varsigma$.
