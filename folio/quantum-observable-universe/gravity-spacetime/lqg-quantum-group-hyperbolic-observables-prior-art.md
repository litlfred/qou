**Remark (prior art: quantum-group spin networks and hyperbolic geometry).**
Dupuis and Girelli replace $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ by $U_q(\mathfrak{su}(2))$ in loop quantum gravity and build length, area and angle operators from $U_q(\mathfrak{su}(2))$ tensor operators \cite[\S V, pp. 20--24]{dupuis-girelli2013}. In 3d Euclidean LQG with $\Lambda < 0$, a three-leg intertwiner then encodes the quantum state of a hyperbolic triangle: the angle operators quantise the hyperbolic cosine law, and the cosmological constant gives a minimum angle.

This is an independent instance of the step the [comparison with canonical programmes](#rem:lqg-adm-ashtekar-comparison) relies on, that a $q$-deformation of the gauge structure produces hyperbolic geometric data. It does not reach hyperbolic volume: the paper constructs no volume operator for a hyperbolic polyhedron and leaves it as an open route \cite[concluding section, p. 24]{dupuis-girelli2013}, so it gives no support to any volume-valued QOU quantity.
