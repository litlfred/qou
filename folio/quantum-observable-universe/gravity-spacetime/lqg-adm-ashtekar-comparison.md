## Three structural advantages

The dictionary tightens around three points where QOU avoids known obstructions of the canonical programmes:

1. **No "problem of time".** ADM presupposes a continuous time coordinate. There is no inbuilt time. Macroscopic time emerges as the Reeb flow, driven by the $q$-anomaly $d_q^2 \neq 0$. Transition amplitudes between algebraic states are the Frobenius pairings, not Wheeler–DeWitt evolution operators.
2. **No Barbero–Immirzi fudge factor.** The continuous Ashtekar connection uses $\gamma$ as a free parameter to match black-hole entropy. The corresponding parameter in QOU is the substrate $q_0$, which is categorically extracted (unique extraction procedure) and observed at the energy scale of observation — not a tunable coupling constant.
3. **Native self-duality.** Ashtekar's original formulation made the Hamiltonian constraint polynomial via complex self-duality, but required intractable "reality conditions". QOU recovers exact self-duality $\star_q F_\nabla = F_\nabla$ directly. The adelic product and continuous deformation produce the real Lorentzian archimedean projection naturally.

## Limitations of the claim

It is not claimed that QOU derives LQG from first principles. Canonical loop quantum gravity is a self-contained research programme. Every structural ingredient that the canonical programmes introduce as a separate assumption appears in QOU as a derived output of the categorical substrate plus Bring-curve constraint. The relevant QOU-side outputs depend on the OS reflection-positivity conjecture and the surreal mass-gap bound. Granting both, the comparison yields a structural equivalence at the level of the Hamiltonian-constraint reduction.
