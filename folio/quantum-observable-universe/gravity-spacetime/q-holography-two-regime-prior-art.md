**Remark (prior art: $q$-deformed hyperbolic disk in $q$-holography).**
Belaey, Mertens and Papalini solve the $q$-Ward identities for the two-point function on the quantum disk \cite[\S 2.1, pp. 7--8]{belaey2025}. The solution depends on the range of $q$. For $q = e^{i\pi b}$ on the unit circle it is the analytic continuation of their eq. (2.19) to non-integer weight; for $0 < q < 1$ it is a convergent infinite product. In both regimes the correlator shows $q$-deformed hyperbolic disk isometries.

This is an external case, alongside the [comparison with canonical programmes](#rem:lqg-adm-ashtekar-comparison), of one $q$-symmetry having different solutions on the unit circle and on the real line, which is also how QOU separates root-of-unity and real-$q$ evaluations. It is a structural parallel only. The paper's real range is $0 < q < 1$, not $q_0 > 1$, and its $b$ is not checked against the $b$ of the Faddeev quantum dilogarithm used in the corpus, so it is not evidence for any QOU identification.
