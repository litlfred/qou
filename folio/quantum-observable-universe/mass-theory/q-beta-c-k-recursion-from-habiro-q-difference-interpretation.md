**Remark (quantum-modularity of the bar-involution).**
Conjecturally, the bar-involution $q\mapsto q^{-1}$ serves as the fibre-level avatar of the quantum-modular $S$-transform $q\mapsto\tilde q$. The Kashaev asymptotics near roots of unity are quantum modular. Under this reading, the bar-odd requirement on $a_1$ represents the odd part under the $S$-transform, and the $\sqrt{u}$ prefactor represents the radial asymptotic. This remains a motivated analogy rather than a theorem.
