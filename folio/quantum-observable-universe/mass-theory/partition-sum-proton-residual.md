*Let $\mathfrak{m}^{\mathrm{tower}}(\mathfrak{p}, q_0)$ be the pure-tower proton mass. The proton tower residual is compared with the electron-scaled Frobenius co-Casimir trace at $n = 3$:*

$$
  \boxed{
    \Delta m_{\mathfrak{p}}
    \;:=\;
    m_{\mathfrak{p}}^{\mathrm{CODATA}}
    - \mathfrak{m}^{\mathrm{tower}}(\mathfrak{p}, q_0)
    \;\approx\;
    m_e \cdot \mathrm{tr}_M\bigl(C_{\mathrm{Frob}}^{(n=3)}\bigr)
    \;=\;
    m_e\Bigl(2 + \frac{1}{[2]_q}\Bigr).
  }
$$

The self-dual Frobenius algebra gives the co-Casimir element. Its Markov trace analytically simplifies to the sum over partitions of inverse quantum dimensions. Against the recorded residual $\Delta m_{\mathfrak{p}} = 1.2779$ MeV the trace agrees to $0.14\%$. The classical value $(5/2)\,m_e$ agrees to $0.03\%$, so this comparison does not single out the $q$-deformed form. The recorded residual is also not $m_{\mathfrak{p}}^{\mathrm{CODATA}}$ minus the current pipeline tower mass ($938.7228$ MeV gives $-0.451$ MeV). The relation is therefore a numerical comparison against one recorded number, not a derived equality.
