*For braids $\beta_1, \beta_2 \in B_n$ with $\mathrm{tr}_M(\beta_2) \neq 0$, and for the Jones normalisation factor $C(n, q) = (-q^{1/2} - q^{-1/2})^{n-1}$:*

$$
  \frac{J_{K_1}(q)}{J_{K_2}(q)}
  \;=\;
  \frac{C(n, q) \cdot \mathrm{tr}_M(\beta_1)}{C(n, q) \cdot \mathrm{tr}_M(\beta_2)}
  \;=\;
  \frac{\mathrm{tr}_M(\beta_1)}{\mathrm{tr}_M(\beta_2)}.
$$

*The Gram matrix $G_{ij} = \mathrm{tr}_M(b_i \cdot b_j)$, the LP cost vectors $t_k = \mathrm{tr}_M(b_k)$, and the shadow prices $y^*$ from the operator-selection LP are exact up to a global scalar that cancels in every ratio and optimality condition.*
