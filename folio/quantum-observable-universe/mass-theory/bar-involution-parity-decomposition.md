**Lemma.** *Let $\sigma$ be an $R$-linear map of an $R$-module $V$ with $\sigma^2 = \mathrm{id}$. For $x \in V$ put*
$$
\mathrm{even}(x) = x + \sigma x, \qquad \mathrm{odd}(x) = x - \sigma x.
$$
*Then $\mathrm{even}(x) + \mathrm{odd}(x) = 2x$, $\sigma(\mathrm{even}(x)) = \mathrm{even}(x)$ and $\sigma(\mathrm{odd}(x)) = -\mathrm{odd}(x)$.*

The lemma applies in particular to the bar-involution $q \mapsto q^{-1}$ of [Bar-involution and its $P_{\mathrm{avg}}$ / $P_{\mathrm{skew}}$ projections](#def:bar-involution). It holds for every element of every such module, so it says nothing about which element a given observable is the even or odd part of.
