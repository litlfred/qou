**Remark (value at $q_0$ and identification).** At $q_0$ the [third-generation ratio](#prop:gen3-meson-phi-hecke-seifert) evaluates to $(q_0^7 - q_0^{-7})\cdot(15/7)^2 \approx 7.320$.
