For the Markov trace at $z = q^3/(q^2+1)$, no Wedderburn weight $y_\lambda$ is negative: $y_\lambda = [n-2k+1]_q/[2]_q^n > 0$ on the two-row stratum $\lambda = (n-k,k)$ for $q > 0$, and $y_\lambda = 0$ identically for $\ell(\lambda) \geq 3$.

Comparing $y_\lambda$ with the non-negative operator-selection LP dual variables $u_\lambda$, both families are non-negative across all partitions. What separates them is that $y_\lambda$ is a trace weight and $u_\lambda$ a dual shadow price, and that $y_\lambda$ has a non-trivial radical — the $\ell(\lambda) \geq 3$ blocks, on which the trace form vanishes — whereas $u_\lambda$ is not constrained to vanish there.

Because every surviving weight is strictly positive and the remaining blocks lie in the radical, no sign cancellation occurs across the centre of $H_n(q)$: infeasibility of an LP posed over the full Wedderburn span is a property of the trace radical rather than of alternating signs, and SDP moment-matrix positivity holds term by term on the two-row quotient. The total Markov trace is positive on positive semidefinite elements.
