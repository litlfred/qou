**Remark (Jones-Markov weight sign pattern).**
Under the alternative Convention-C trace, the Jones–Markov coefficients become sign-indefinite for $n \geq 4$ ($y_{(1^4)} = -0.00084253$ at $q_0$). Under the canonical Convention-D trace, by contrast, no weight $y_\lambda$ is negative: $y_\lambda = [n-2k+1]_q/[2]_q^n \geq 0$ on the two-row stratum $\lambda = (n-k,k)$, and $y_\lambda = 0$ identically for $\ell(\lambda) \geq 3$ ([`TemperleyLiebTower.lean`](../lean/QOU/QBeta/TemperleyLiebTower.lean)).

The distinction this remark drew from the non-negative operator-selection LP dual variables $u_\lambda$ therefore collapses on the sign axis. What still separates the two is that $y_\lambda$ is a trace weight and $u_\lambda$ a dual price, and that $y_\lambda$ has a radical — the $\ell(\lambda) \geq 3$ blocks, on which the trace form vanishes — while $u_\lambda$ does not.

The three consequences the remark drew from the sign alternation go with it: there is no "indefinite signature on the centre" resolving through cancellation against the symmetric and standard blocks, since nothing cancels; whether an LP over the full Wedderburn span is infeasible is now a statement about the radical rather than about signs; and the SDP moment-matrix recovery of the positive trace, while still true, no longer needs a cancellation argument. The total Markov trace remains positive, as before.
