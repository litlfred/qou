Let $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ be a QOU, let $\varrho \colon \mathbf{1} \to A$
be a vortex with braid representative $\beta \in B_n$, and let
$\Phi_{\mathrm{mass}}(\beta) \in H_n(R, q)$ be its Hecke mass element
([Hecke mass element](#def:hecke-mass-element)).

**Conjecture.**  The categorical mass
([mass identity](#def:mass-identity)) equals the norm of the Markov
trace of the Hecke mass element:

$$
  \mathfrak{m}(\varrho_\beta)
  \;=\;
  \bigl\lvert
    \mathrm{tr}_M\!\bigl(\Phi_{\mathrm{mass}}(\beta)\bigr)
  \bigr\rvert.
$$

Moreover, the splitting into descended and transient parts used in the
[topological decomposition of mass](#conj:topological-mass) descends to
$H_n(R, q)$:

$$
  \mathrm{tr}_M\!\bigl(\Phi_{\mathrm{mass}}(\beta)\bigr)
  \;=\;
  \mathrm{tr}_M\!\bigl(\Phi^{\mathrm{desc}}(\beta)\bigr)
  \;+\;
  \sum_{i=1}^{N}
  \mathrm{tr}_M\!\bigl(\Phi^{\mathrm{trans}}_i(\beta)\bigr).
$$

**Archimedean specialisation.** The categorical mass-squared, the hyperbolic volume $\mathrm{vol} : \mathbb{R}$ and $\hbar : \mathbb{R}$, and the descended trace are the archimedean ($\mathbb{R}$-valued) readouts of the generic Markov-trace mass data; the $R\to\mathbb{R}$ realisation is acknowledged here.
