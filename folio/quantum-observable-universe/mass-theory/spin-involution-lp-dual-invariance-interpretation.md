**Remark.**
Isospin is the $\mathbb{Z}/2$ grading attached to any Frobenius algebra equipped with a spin involution; nucleons are one instance. When physical observables are extracted as $\tau$-traces of products of central elements and braid recipes, the spin-singlet sector and spin-doublet sector decompose canonically under the LP-dual. The Wigner energy term $\sim |Z - N|/A$ in the BW formula is the $T_3$-projection of this grading. It appears because $|Z - N|$ is the $\mathbb{Z}/2$ charge of the recipe, which the LP-dual reads off mechanically.

The proof does not invoke Lie theory or $SU(2)$ symmetries. Any quantum-algebraic system with a trace-preserving spin involution, a $\sigma$-invariant central cost, and a $\sigma$-stable channel set inherits LP-dual spin-invariance. Particle physics exhibits isospin because nucleons populate a 2-row Wedderburn stratum governed by the same algebra.
