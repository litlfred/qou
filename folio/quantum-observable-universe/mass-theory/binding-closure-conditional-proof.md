By obligation (C) ([bond order equals jet degree lemma](#lem:bond-order-eq-jet-degree)),
the binding is the body-order series,
$B(K)=\sum_{n}\,(\text{$n$-body term})$. By the conditional input (B), each
body term factors as $B_n = m_e\,a_D^{(n)}$. Substituting and pulling the
common scale $m_e$ out of the (finite) sum,

$$
B(K) \;=\; \sum_n B_n \;=\; \sum_n m_e\,a_D^{(n)} \;=\; m_e\sum_n a_D^{(n)} .
$$

Rewriting each period through the residue form
([Seiberg–Witten period residue sum proposition](#prop:sw-period-residue-sum)) gives the second
displayed equality of the statement; the growth law of the $a_D^{(n)}$ is the
solution of the $q$-difference recurrence
([binding generating-function $q$-difference proposition](#prop:binding-genfun-qdifference)). The
[conditional binding closure theorem](#thm:binding-closure-conditional)
carries (C) and (B) as explicit hypotheses and
derives the closed form by finite-sum distributivity and termwise congruence —
established modulo input (B). $\qquad\blacksquare$
