Filtering the Iwahori–Hecke algebra $H_n(q)$ by jet-prefix length and Gröbner–Shirshov cellular depth induces a spectral sequence that converges to the associated graded normal-form ring. The differentials of this spectral sequence measure the failure of naive generator multiplicativity across non-commuting braid strands.

This section constructs the jet-prefix spectral sequence, proves that the Frobenius self-pairing induces an involutive chirality grading on its pages, and defines the Hecke curvature 2-cocycle and curvature rank that stratify the $E_\infty$ page. Applying the Diamond Lemma yields finite-step convergence (collapsing at $E_2$ for low-rank two-row blocks), while the top-channel $\Delta_q$ writhe decoration and the operator-valued crystal $R$-matrix sub-lemma govern the torus-knot force-carrier shifts.
