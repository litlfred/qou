*The model neutron-proton mass splitting derives from the quantum dilogarithm volumes:*

$$
  \Delta m := \alpha \cdot \lvert \Phi(\mathfrak{u}\mathfrak{d}\mathfrak{d}) -
    \Phi(\mathfrak{u}\mathfrak{u}\mathfrak{d}) \rvert
  \approx 1.054 \; \mathrm{MeV}
$$

*where $\Phi$ evaluates the respective quark ordering and $\alpha = m_e \times 8G$ sets the binding scale.*

Replacing one positive $\mathfrak{u}\mathfrak{u}$ crossing with a negative $\mathfrak{d}\mathfrak{d}$ crossing causes the splitting. The $\mathfrak{d}\mathfrak{d}$ crossing carries more confinement energy. This makes the model neutron heavier. The volumes are evaluated with the canonical convention-D Markov parameter $z = q^3/(q^2+1)$ at the proton value of $q_0$, pairing each strand with every later strand. With $\alpha \approx 3.7449$, $\Phi(\mathfrak{u}\mathfrak{u}\mathfrak{d}) \approx -0.6149$ and $\Phi(\mathfrak{u}\mathfrak{d}\mathfrak{d}) \approx -0.8964$, the model value is $3.7449 \times 0.2815 = 1.05418935$ MeV.
