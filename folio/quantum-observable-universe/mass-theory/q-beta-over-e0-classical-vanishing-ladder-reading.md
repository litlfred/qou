**Remark (reading for the $c_k$ ladder).** This remark records what the [classical vanishing $S(1) = 0$](#prop:q-beta-over-e0-classical-vanishing) implies for the $c_k$ ladder, and the values at $q_0$ that go with it.

One reading has $c_k$ coming from the $u_{\text{ladder}}$ Taylor expansion of $S(q) = \mathfrak{Q}_\beta(q)/E_0(q)$. Taken literally, this is degenerate. The Taylor expansion of $S/S(1)$ around $q = 1$ is $0/0$. The proved $c_2 = -1$ cannot be recovered this way.

The term $S = \mathfrak{Q}_\beta / E_0$ is the SDP-constraint scalar (canonical at $q_0$, matching the affine target $n_{\text{target}} = S(q_0) \approx 0.18689$). The $c_k$ ladder normalization is $\mathfrak{Q}_\beta / [Q_A \cdot \cos(\alpha_{\text{eff}})]$, with $Q_A$ and $\alpha$ read from the operator-valued $N{=}3$ witness. This proposition exposes the algebraic obstruction that identifies the missing categorical input ($Q_A(q)$ and $\alpha(q)$).

The vanishing $S(1) = 0$ provides the direct-algebraic witness. An operator-theoretic witness arrives from the SDP strict-complementarity analysis. Both routes converge on the bare substrate scale $Q_A$. That scale is the retired Form A ($+0.168\%$ without CODATA input, kept as a historical cross-check); the ruled convention-D base, the Markov trace at $z_D$, gives $0.4491$ MeV ($-42.59\%$) with the tower-summed $E_0$, or $0.4447$ MeV with the order-zero one. The correction to the base is the $c_k$ ladder, which under convention D is not yet derived. Its normalization is not $S = \mathfrak{Q}_\beta/E_0$.
