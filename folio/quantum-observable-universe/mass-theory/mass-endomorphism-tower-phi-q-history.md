**Choice of the $q$-dilogarithm convention ($\mathrm{Li}_2^q$ versus $\Phi_q$).** In the [mass-endomorphism tower](#prop:mass-endomorphism-tower), the series is resummed through the Jackson $q$-dilogarithm $\mathrm{Li}_2^q(z)$ rather than the compact Faddeev function $\Phi_q(z)$. Direct term-by-term $q$-integration of the geometric series produces $\mathrm{Li}_2^q(z)$, which preserves bar-involution isospin symmetry at the real substrate anchor $q_0$ and avoids the mirror-nucleus asymmetry poles introduced by the modular double ratio in $\Phi_q$.
