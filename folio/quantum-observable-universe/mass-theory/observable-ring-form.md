**Conjecture.** *For every canonical observable $\mathcal{O}$ in the paper
(mass $\mathfrak{m}$, Q-value $\mathfrak{Q}_\beta$, binding $B(Z, N)$,
crossing energy $E_{\mathfrak{H}}$, coupling $\alpha$, mixing angle, shell
parameter) there are an element $\hat{\mathcal{O}}(q_0)$, built from $q_0$
alone, and integers $a, b$ with $a + b \in \{0, 1\}$, such that*

$$
  \mathcal{O} \;=\; \hat{\mathcal{O}}(q_0) \cdot m_e^{\,a} \cdot m_\pi^{\,b},
$$

*where $q_0$ is fixed by CAL-2 and $m_e$, $m_\pi$ are the two dimensional
proxies of [the three calibration inputs](#prop:three-calibration-inputs).*

The conjecture does not yet name the ring that $\hat{\mathcal{O}}(q_0)$
lives in, and two facts constrain it.

1. The ring $\mathbb{Z}[q_0^{\pm 1/2}]$ is too small. The derived hadronic
   scale $\mathfrak{E}_0 = m_e/(q_0(q_0-1))$ needs $1/(q_0-1)$, and the
   convention-D Markov parameter $z = q^3/(q^2+1)$ needs $1/(q^2+1)$.
2. The volume form of the mass, $\mathfrak{m}(K) = m_e\,\mathrm{Vol}(K)\,\rho(K)/\hbar_q^2$,
   carries the hyperbolic volume $\mathrm{Vol}(K)$. Whether every such
   factor is itself a function of $q_0$ is part of what is conjectured.
