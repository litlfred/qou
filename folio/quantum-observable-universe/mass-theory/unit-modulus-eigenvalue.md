*Let $\beta$ be a braid word of even length in $H_3(q)$. On the standard representation $\rho_{(2,1)}$, $\det \rho_{(2,1)}(\beta) = 1$, so the two eigenvalues satisfy $\lambda_1 \lambda_2 = 1$. They lie on the unit circle exactly when $\chi_{(2,1)}(\beta)$ is real with $|\chi_{(2,1)}(\beta)| \le 2$; this is not automatic. For the Borromean braid $\beta_{\mathrm{Bor}} = (\sigma_1\sigma_2^{-1})^3$,*

$$
  \chi_{(2,1)}(\beta_{\mathrm{Bor}})
  \;=\;
  -\frac{(q^4 - q^2 + 1)(q^8 - 2q^6 - 2q^2 + 1)}{q^6},
$$

*which equals $2$ at $q = 1$.*
