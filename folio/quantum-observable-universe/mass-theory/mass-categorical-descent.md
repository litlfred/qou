*The mass identity decomposes under the descent involution into a descended component and a transient component:*

$$
  B(Z,N) = B^{\mathrm{desc}}(Z,N) + B^{\mathrm{trans}}(Z,N).
$$

*The descended component resides in the center of the Hecke algebra. The transient component localizes to the exceptional divisors of the brane blow-up.*

**Descended terms (conjectured identification).**
It is conjectured, not shown, that the descended elements are Casimir operators matching the volume, pairing and Wigner terms of the liquid-drop model, evaluated via quantum factorials and Plancherel weights. No derivation of this match is given here. The Wigner term in particular is an empirical term of the semi-empirical mass formula, so the match cannot be used as a derivation of it.

**Transient terms (conjectured identification).**
It is likewise conjectured, not shown, that the transient elements isolate the surface, Coulomb, exchange-parity and magic-number shell corrections, each attached to a geometric blow-up evaluated by a quantum dilogarithm correction. The descent decomposition structures both the mass endomorphism and the electromagnetic coupling.
