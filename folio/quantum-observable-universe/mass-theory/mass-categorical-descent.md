*There is a mass element $\mathfrak M(Z,N)\in H_n(q)$ and a functional $\Phi$ with $B(Z,N)=\Phi(\mathfrak M(Z,N))$ such that, with $\pi_Z$ the central projection of [Centre–cocentre splitting](#thm:centre-cocentre-descent):*

1. *the volume, pairing and Wigner contributions to $B(Z,N)$ depend only on the central part $\pi_Z\mathfrak M$, that is, on the central-character data $\chi^\lambda(\mathfrak M)/d_\lambda$;*
2. *the surface, Coulomb, exchange-parity and shell contributions vanish when $\mathfrak M$ is replaced by $\pi_Z\mathfrak M$.*

What is proved is the algebra: [Centre–cocentre splitting](#thm:centre-cocentre-descent) gives a unique split of an element into a central part and a commutator part whenever $A=Z(A)\oplus[A,A]$, which holds for split semisimple $H_n(q)$. Three consequences constrain this conjecture.

- The split is of an element of the algebra, not of the number $B(Z,N)$. A sum of $\ln|\cdot|$ terms is not a linear functional of an element, so writing $B=B^{\mathrm{desc}}+B^{\mathrm{trans}}$ has content only once $\mathfrak M$ and $\Phi$ are defined. Neither is defined in the corpus.
- Every trace kills the commutator part (item 3 of the theorem). So if $\Phi$ were a trace, the contributions in item 2 would be zero. A nonzero surface, Coulomb or shell sector needs a non-linear $\Phi$, such as the $\ln|\cdot|$ form of [Three-invariant atomic mass formula](#thm:wedderburn-mass-formula).
- The descent idempotent $\hat\tau_n=\ell_n\circ\mathfrak d_{n+1}$ of [Descent involution](#def:descent-involution) is not the central projection: its image contains $g_1$, which is not central in $H_{n+1}(q)$ for $n\ge2$ (item 7 of the theorem). The earlier wording "the descended component resides in the centre", with "descended" read through $\hat\tau_n$, is false.

**Comparison (empirical; not part of any proved statement).** Items 1 and 2 pair central-character data with the volume, pairing and Wigner terms of the liquid-drop model, and the rest with the surface, Coulomb, exchange-parity and magic-number shell terms. These are comparisons with the semi-empirical mass formula, whose Wigner term is itself a fitted empirical term, so the pairing cannot be used as a derivation of it. The further readings of the commutator part as localised on exceptional divisors of a brane blow-up, evaluated by quantum dilogarithm corrections, and as structuring the electromagnetic coupling, have no algebraic definition here and are not claimed.
