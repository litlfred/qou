*The hadronic mass collapses into a combination of neutrino vacuum drag and pure strong-force binding energy. Absolute nucleon mass in MeV is not itself a framework prediction: $m_e$ fixes the MeV scale by fiat, so the predictive content is the dimensionless ratio, and the proton and neutron entries below are that ratio rendered against the $m_e$ unit. The proton and neutron rows also take the mean nucleon mass $M_N$ as an input: the accompanying proof fixes the dressed quark masses from $\bar\mu \approx M_N/3$, so these two rows reproduce that input rather than predict it. What the substrate parameter $q_0$ predicts is the isospin splitting, and the accompanying proof reaches $m_n - m_p$ at a $-0.53\%$ residual — about $6.9\,\text{keV}$. The deuteron and binding-energy rows are tabulated for orientation; neither is derived here, and the binding row sits $1\,\text{keV}$ from CODATA at the precision shown.*
