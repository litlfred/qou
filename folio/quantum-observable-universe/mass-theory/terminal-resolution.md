*Work over a field of characteristic not $3$, with $q \neq 0$, $q^2 + 1 \neq 0$ and $q^4 + 1 \neq 0$. Under the [pair Skein mass formula](#lem:pair-skein-mass-formula), write $\mathcal{S}_{ab} = (q^3\mu_a + q^{-1}\mu_b)/(q^2 + 1)$ and take the nucleon masses to be*

$$
  m_p = \mathcal{S}_{\mathfrak{u}\mathfrak{u}} + 2\,\mathcal{S}_{\mathfrak{u}\mathfrak{d}},
  \qquad
  m_n = 2\,\mathcal{S}_{\mathfrak{u}\mathfrak{d}} + \mathcal{S}_{\mathfrak{d}\mathfrak{d}},
$$

*where $\mu_{\mathfrak{u}}$ and $\mu_{\mathfrak{d}}$ are the dressed quark masses. Write $\kappa = (q^4 + 1)/\bigl(q(q^2+1)\bigr)$. Then:*

*(i) The splitting is exactly*

$$
  m_n - m_p = \kappa\,(\mu_{\mathfrak{d}} - \mu_{\mathfrak{u}}).
$$

*(ii) The map $(\mu_{\mathfrak{u}}, \mu_{\mathfrak{d}}) \mapsto (m_p, m_n)$ is linear with determinant $3\kappa^2$, and it is a bijection: each pair $(m_p, m_n)$ comes from exactly one pair $(\mu_{\mathfrak{u}}, \mu_{\mathfrak{d}})$.*
