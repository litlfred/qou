*Work over a field of characteristic not $3$ containing $q^{1/2}$, with $q^{1/2} \neq 0$, $q^{1/2} + q^{-1/2} \neq 0$ and $[2]_q = q + q^{-1} \neq 0$. Under the [pair Skein mass formula](#lem:pair-skein-mass-formula), in its convention-C frame, write $\mathcal{S}_{ab} = (q\mu_a + q^{-1}\mu_b)/(q^{1/2} + q^{-1/2})$ and take the nucleon masses to be*

$$
  m_p = \mathcal{S}_{\mathfrak{u}\mathfrak{u}} + 2\,\mathcal{S}_{\mathfrak{u}\mathfrak{d}},
  \qquad
  m_n = 2\,\mathcal{S}_{\mathfrak{u}\mathfrak{d}} + \mathcal{S}_{\mathfrak{d}\mathfrak{d}},
$$

*where $\mu_{\mathfrak{u}}$ and $\mu_{\mathfrak{d}}$ are the dressed quark masses. Then:*

*(i) The splitting is exactly*

$$
  m_n - m_p = \frac{[2]_q}{q^{1/2} + q^{-1/2}}\,(\mu_{\mathfrak{d}} - \mu_{\mathfrak{u}}).
$$

*(ii) The map $(\mu_{\mathfrak{u}}, \mu_{\mathfrak{d}}) \mapsto (m_p, m_n)$ is linear with determinant $3\,[2]_q^2/(q^{1/2}+q^{-1/2})^2$, and it is a bijection: each pair $(m_p, m_n)$ comes from exactly one pair $(\mu_{\mathfrak{u}}, \mu_{\mathfrak{d}})$.*
