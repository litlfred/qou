*The QOU framework admits exactly three external calibration inputs.
Beyond these three, no further empirical, fitting, or numerological
input is permitted — every other mass, Q-value, binding energy,
coupling constant, mixing angle, and dimensionless ratio in the paper
is a prediction.*

Here CAL-1 is $m_e$, CAL-2 is pinned through its proxy $m_\mu/m_e$ (which fixes
$q_0$), and CAL-3 is the meson scale with proxy $m_\pi$. The derived hadronic
energy scale $\mathfrak{E}_0 = m_e/(q_0(q_0-1))$ and the CAL-2 proxy
$\mathfrak{Q}_\beta$ are built from $m_e$ and $q_0$ and are not further inputs.
That every observable factors through these three inputs in a fixed algebraic
form is stated separately, as [a conjecture](#conj:observable-ring-form).

The three calibration observables, the allowed and forbidden
substitutions, the (co)homological alignment, and the comparison with
the Standard Model are collected in
