**Proposition (Markov-trace normalization).**
*Let $R$ be a field of characteristic $0$ and $q \in R^\times$ with
$[k]_q \neq 0$ for $2 \le k \le n$, so that $H_n(R, q)$ is semisimple
and the convention-D Markov parameter $z = q^3/(q^2+1)$ is defined
($[2]_q \neq 0$ is $q^2 \neq -1$). For the Hecke algebra $H_n(R, q)$,
the Markov-trace weights $y_\lambda(q)$ and the ordinary
block dimensions $d_\lambda = f^\lambda$ (the number of standard Young
tableaux of shape $\lambda$) satisfy*

$$
  \sum_{\lambda \vdash n} d_\lambda\, y_\lambda(q) = 1 .
$$

*Equivalently $\mathrm{tr}_M(\mathbf{1}) = \sum_\lambda y_\lambda\,\chi^\lambda(\mathbf{1}) = \sum_\lambda y_\lambda d_\lambda = 1$: the Markov trace of the identity is unit, so the weights $y_\lambda$ partition the total Markov weight among the irreducibles.*

The normalization's role in the descent decomposition of mass, the
scale-versus-weight distinction, and the classical limit are collected in
[the Wedderburn trace-identity context](#rem:wedderburn-trace-identity-context).
