The :defterm[$q$-dimension layer]{#q-dimension-layer} is the codomain $R^{|\mathrm{Par}(n)|}$ of the [$\delta_q$-decoration](#def:delta-q-decoration), with one coordinate per partition $\lambda \vdash n$:

$$
  R^{|\,\mathrm{Par}(n)\,|}
  \;=\;
  \bigoplus_{\lambda \vdash n} R,
  \qquad
  b \longmapsto \bigl(\chi_\lambda(b)\bigr)_{\lambda \vdash n}.
$$
