**Conjecture.** *Let $A = H_n(R, q)$ be a semisimple Hecke algebra. Decompose its centre $Z(A)$ by GB-depth of partitions. The analytic quantum dilogarithm admits a centre-stratified expansion:*

$$
  \mathrm{tr}_M\!\bigl(\Phi_q(z_i) \cdot T_w\bigr) = \sum_{k = 0}^{\infty} \bigl\langle \phi_q^{(k)}(z_i),\, T_w \bigr\rangle,
$$

*where the depth-$k$ coefficient $\phi_q^{(k)}$ is the Frobenius dual of the truncation increment. The partial sum $\Phi_q^{[\leq k]}$ captures exactly the $\bigoplus_{j \leq k} Z^{(j)}(A)$-content.*

**Consequence (truncation), if the conjecture holds.**
*For $T_w$ with NF support depth $k_\star(T_w)$, the trace truncates precisely to:*

$$
  \mathrm{tr}_M\!\bigl(\Phi_q(z_i) \cdot T_w\bigr) = \mathrm{tr}_M\!\bigl(\Phi_q^{[\leq k_\star(T_w)]}(z_i) \cdot T_w\bigr).
$$

If the conjecture holds, the analytic infinite product truncates to a finite product as a Frobenius-paired identity, with truncation depth fixed by the NF support of $T_w$. What is proved is weaker: the depth strata of $Z(A)$ are mutually orthogonal under the Frobenius pairing ([partial argument](#rem:quantum-dilogarithm-stratified-partial-argument), Step 1).
