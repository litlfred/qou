The braid-index condition $m \le p$ is part of the definition. $T_{m,p}$ and $T_{p,m}$ are the same knot, but $(m-1)p \ne (p-1)m$ when $m \ne p$, so without the condition one knot would receive two values.

This is a scalar in $R = \mathbb{Z}[q^{\pm 1}]$. It is a different object from the [$q$-dimension decoration](#def:delta-q-decoration) $\delta_q(b) = (\chi_\lambda(b))_{\lambda \vdash n}$, which is a tuple of characters, and it is not the sum of that tuple. The two agree for $m = 2$: for odd $p$ the characters of $\beta_{2,p} = \sigma_1^{p}$ on $V_{(2)}$ and $V_{(1,1)}$ are $q^{p}$ and $(-q^{-1})^{p} = -q^{-p}$, whose sum is $q^{p} - q^{-p}$. They disagree for $m = 3$. For $T_{3,5}$ the characters of $(\sigma_1\sigma_2)^{5}$ on $V_{(3)}$, $V_{(2,1)}$, $V_{(1,1,1)}$ are $q^{10}$, $-1$, $q^{-10}$, whose sum is $q^{10} - 1 + q^{-10}$, while $\delta^{\mathrm{top}}_q(T_{3,5}) = q^{10} - q^{-10}$.
