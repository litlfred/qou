**Remark (neutron-proton discrepancy resolution).**
*The uncalibrated pure-tower formula undershoots the neutron mass due to a $\Phi_q$ pole-distance asymmetry. The free-neutron beta decay prediction must instead use the trace difference directly.*

The raw tower prediction relies on per-generator amplitudes that sit at different distances from the $q_0$ pole. This introduces an artificial scale difference between mirror nucleons. Bypassing the per-nucleon evaluation entirely, the uncalibrated beta-decay $Q$-value emerges directly from the difference of the Markov normal forms:

$$
  \mathfrak{Q}_\beta = \bigl|\mathrm{tr}_{z_D}(\Delta)\bigr| \cdot E_0 \approx 0.4491\;\mathrm{MeV},
$$

with the Markov trace at $z_D = q_0^3/(q_0^2+1)$ (convention D, owner ruling 2026-09-08). Here $E_0 = m_e/\bigl(q_0(q_0-1)(1-\hbar_q^2)\bigr)$ is the tower-summed energy scale, with $\hbar_q = 1 - q_0^{-1}$; the order-zero form gives $0.4447$ MeV. This misses CODATA by $-42.59\%$ ($-333.2$ keV); the convention-D $c_k$ ladder that would correct it is not yet derived. It relies on the topological trace alone, avoiding the single-generator Kashaev approximations. The retired Form A, which weights every basis element by $1$ (the trace at $z = 1$, not admissible under D), gave $0.7836$ MeV, within $1.3$ keV of CODATA; it is kept only as a historical cross-check.
