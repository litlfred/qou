*Any candidate Habiro element $F(q)$ must satisfy the direction-sanity invariant: $|F(q_0)|$ must be in the same order of magnitude as the target ratio $\mathfrak{Q}_\beta^{\text{CODATA}} / [Q_A(q_0) \cdot \cos(\alpha_{\text{eff}}(q_0))]$. This implements the formalize-first magnitude check. This is an order-of-magnitude screen, not a structural exclusion: no proved invariant forbids a candidate outside the window, so a screened-out candidate is not refuted. The target is $\approx 1$ on the retired $z = 1$ base and $\approx 1.76$ on the ruled convention-D base $z_D$.
