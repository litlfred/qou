*In the Iwahori-Hecke algebra $H_n(q)$, with $\sigma_i^2 = (q - q^{-1})\sigma_i + 1$, the multiplicative Jucys-Murphy elements*

$$
  J_1 \;=\; 1, \qquad J_{k+1} \;=\; \sigma_k\, J_k\, \sigma_k,
  \qquad k = 1, \ldots, n-1,
$$

*commute with each other. The elementary symmetric polynomials*

$$
  P_\ell \;:=\; e_\ell(J_1, J_2, \ldots, J_n)
  \;\in\; Z\bigl(H_n(q)\bigr)
$$

*lie in the centre and act on the Wedderburn block $V_\lambda$ (for generic $q$) as the scalar*

$$
  c_\ell(\lambda) \;=\; e_\ell\!\left(\{q^{2c(\square)} :
    \square \in \lambda\}\right)
$$

*where $c(\square) = j - i$ is the content of cell $\square = (i,j)$.*
