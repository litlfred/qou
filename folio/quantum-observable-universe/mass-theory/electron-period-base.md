The prolonged Seiberg--Witten periods satisfy the one-parameter recurrence $a_D^{(n+2)} = \tau\,a_D^{(n+1)} - a_D^{(n)}$ ([Symplectic prolongation (det B = 1) gives a one-parameter recurrence](#prop:sl2-prolongation-recurrence)). The $n=0$ term is the unprolonged period, the **electron period** $a_D^{(e)}$ ([The Seiberg–Witten differential is the jet decomposition of the Archimedean realisation](#prop:jet-sw-tau-decomposition)). Identifying $a_D^{(e)}$ with the leptonic scale $m_e$ (CAL-1) establishes the first calibration as the base of the tower. Every period is generated from the triple $(m_e,\,a_D^{(1)},\,\tau)$, and $m_e$ propagates into every term through the recurrence rather than entering as an external multiplicative prefactor. Concretely, the $n=2$ period is $a_D^{(2)} = \tau\,a_D^{(1)} - m_e$.

The formal backing is `QOU.Mass.second_period_contains_me`, which *derives* that identity from the recurrence together with the base condition $a_0 = m_e$. The base condition itself is a defining field of the tower, not a result: it is the CAL-1 identification, and the accessor `electron_period_is_base` returns it verbatim.
