The prolonged Seiberg--Witten periods satisfy the one-parameter recurrence $a_D^{(n+2)} = \tau\,a_D^{(n+1)} - a_D^{(n)}$ ([$\mathfrak{sl}_2$ prolongation recurrence proposition](#prop:sl2-prolongation-recurrence)). The $n=0$ term is the unprolonged period, the **electron period** $a_D^{(e)}$ ([jet Seiberg–Witten tau decomposition proposition](#prop:jet-sw-tau-decomposition)). Identifying $a_D^{(e)}$ with the leptonic scale $m_e$ (CAL-1) establishes the first calibration as the base of the tower. Every period is generated from the triple $(m_e,\,a_D^{(1)},\,\tau)$, and $m_e$ propagates into every term through the recurrence rather than entering as an external multiplicative prefactor. Concretely, the $n=2$ period is $a_D^{(2)} = \tau\,a_D^{(1)} - m_e$.

This identity follows directly from the recurrence together with the base condition $a_0 = m_e$. The base condition itself is a defining datum of the tower rather than a derived result: it is the CAL-1 identification.
