*Let $A_{\mathrm{magic}} \in \{2, 8, 20, 28, 50, 82, 126\}$ be a magic nucleon count, $n = 3 \cdot A_{\mathrm{magic}}$, and let $\lambda_{\mathrm{shell}} \vdash n$ be the staircase partition of $n$. For an atom $K$ with Hecke word $\mathcal{B}_K \in H_n(q)$, the shell-operator magnitude $y_{\mathrm{target}}(A_{\mathrm{magic}})$ is conjectured to be given in closed form by:*

$$
  y_{\mathrm{target}}(A_{\mathrm{magic}})
  \;=\;
  \frac{C_{\lambda_{\mathrm{shell}}}(q_0)\,\cdot\,y_{\lambda_{\mathrm{shell}}}(q_0)\,\cdot\,|\chi^{\lambda_{\mathrm{shell}}}(\mathcal{B}_K)|}
       {|\mathrm{tr}_M(\mathcal{B}_K)|}
$$

*where $C_{\lambda_{\mathrm{shell}}}(q_0)$ is the closed-form Wedderburn coefficient of $\sigma_{\mathrm{shell}}$, $y_{\lambda_{\mathrm{shell}}}(q_0)$ is the Wenzl-bridge central coefficient, and $\chi^{\lambda_{\mathrm{shell}}}(\mathcal{B}_K)$ is the Hoefsmit character.*

This formulation isolates $\sigma_{\mathrm{shell}}$ as a separately constructed element of $H_n(q)$ supported on the staircase partition. The shell magnitude emerges as a purely categorical observable, entirely independent of $\mathfrak{Q}_\beta$ and the AME chain manifold. At $A_{\mathrm{magic}} = 2$ (the deuteron) the formula evaluates to exactly zero in convention D ($z = q^3/(q^2+1)$): the staircase $(3,2,1)$ has three rows, and the Ocneanu weight of every partition with a third row vanishes at that $z$ for general $n$. So $y_{(3,2,1)}(q_0) = 0$ and the order-of-magnitude benchmark $B(D)/(2 m_p) \approx 0.0012$ is not reproduced. Extending to $A_{\mathrm{magic}} \ge 8$ requires identifying the correct self-conjugate partition for each higher shell.
