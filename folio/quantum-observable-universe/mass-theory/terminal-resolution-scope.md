Part (ii) of [nucleon masses under the pair Skein formula](#prop:terminal-resolution) means that reproducing the measured proton and neutron masses with this formula is a change of variables, not a prediction: the dressed quark masses can always be chosen to fit. The formula carries predictive content only if the dressed masses are fixed independently. The only such input on record is the ratio in [the aether flavor split conjecture](#conj:flavor-split-aether-mass). Its derivation sketch calls the per-quark split a phenomenological ansatz chosen by its agreement with experiment, so it does not yet supply an independent input. Absolute masses in MeV are not predictions in any case: the electron mass fixes the MeV scale by definition.

Both parts hold for the pair Skein formula derived at the canonical Markov parameter $z = q^3/(q^2+1)$. The bijection depends only on the sum $\kappa$ of the two crossing weights being nonzero, so it would hold for any pair of crossing weights with nonzero sum.
