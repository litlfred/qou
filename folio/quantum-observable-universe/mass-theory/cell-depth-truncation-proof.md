**Items 1–3.** Every trace on the split semisimple $A$ is $\tau=\sum_\mu y_\mu\chi_\mu$, and $\chi_\mu(e_\lambda x)=\delta_{\lambda\mu}\chi_\lambda(x)$, so $\tau(e_\lambda x)=y_\lambda\chi_\lambda(x)$. If $k>k_\star(x)$, every $\lambda$ with $a(\lambda)=k$ has $\chi_\lambda(x)=0$, so $\mathfrak m^{(k)}_\tau(x)=\sum_{a(\lambda)=k}y_\lambda\chi_\lambda(x)=0$. Item 2 follows because the terms with $k_\star<k\le K'$ vanish. For item 3, $\sum_kE_k=\sum_\lambda e_\lambda=1$, so $\sum_k\mathfrak m^{(k)}_\tau(x)=\tau(x)$, and the terms above $k_\star$ are $0$.

**Item 4.** The index character $T_s\mapsto q$ gives $\chi_{(n)}(T_w)=q^{\ell(w)}$ and, on a braid image, $q$ to the exponent sum. Both are units, so $(n)$ lies in the support. And $a(\lambda)=\sum_i\binom{\lambda_i}2\le\binom n2$ with equality only at $\lambda=(n)$.

**Item 5.** Put $P=\Phi_q^{[\le K']}(z)\ne0$ and $u=1-q^{-(K'+1)}z$. Then $\Phi_q^{[\le K'+1]}(z)=Pu^{-1}$, and $Pu^{-1}=P$ would force $u=1$, so $q^{-(K'+1)}z=0$, contradicting $q,z\ne0$. For the consequence apply this at $K'=k_0$. The hypotheses are satisfiable, for example $q=2$, $z=1$ over $\mathbb Q$. $\blacksquare$
