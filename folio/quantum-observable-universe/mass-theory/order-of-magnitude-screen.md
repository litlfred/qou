*Let $K$ be a linearly ordered field, $\lambda\in K$ a threshold and $t\in K$ with $t\ge0$ a target. An element $x\in K$ passes the $\lambda$-screen at $t$, written $\mathrm{Scr}_\lambda(t,x)$, if $t\le\lambda|x|$ and $|x|\le\lambda t$. The additive band is $\mathrm{Add}_\lambda(t,x)$: $|x-t|\le\lambda t$. Then:*

1. *(Order structure.) For $t\ge0$: $\lambda\le\lambda'$ and $\mathrm{Scr}_\lambda(t,x)$ imply $\mathrm{Scr}_{\lambda'}(t,x)$; $\lambda\ge1$ implies $\mathrm{Scr}_\lambda(t,t)$; $\mathrm{Scr}_\lambda(t,x)\iff\mathrm{Scr}_\lambda(t,|x|)\iff\mathrm{Scr}_\lambda(|x|,t)$; for $\lambda,\mu,b\ge0$, $\mathrm{Scr}_\lambda(a,b)$ and $\mathrm{Scr}_\mu(b,c)$ imply $\mathrm{Scr}_{\lambda\mu}(a,c)$; and for $a,b,c,d\ge0$, $\mathrm{Scr}_\lambda(a,b)$ and $\mathrm{Scr}_\mu(c,d)$ imply $\mathrm{Scr}_{\lambda\mu}(ac,bd)$.*
2. *(Conditional rejection.) Let $\mu\le\lambda$ and $t\ge0$. If $\mathrm{Scr}_\mu(t,x^\ast)$, then every $x$ with $\neg\mathrm{Scr}_\lambda(t,x)$ satisfies $x\ne x^\ast$.*
3. *(Finite ladder.) If $s$ is finite and $|a_k|\le\tfrac12$ for $k\in s$, then $\mathrm{Scr}_\Lambda\bigl(1,\prod_{k\in s}(1+a_k)\bigr)$ with $\Lambda=\prod_{k\in s}(1+2|a_k|)$.*
4. *(Direction obstruction, no threshold.) If $0\le z\le1$, $B\ge0$ and $B<t$, then $Bz^m<t$ for every $m\in\mathbb N$.*
5. *(Upward closure.) If $\lambda,B\ge0$, $0\le z\le1$, $n\le m$ and $\lambda Bz^n<t$, then $\neg\mathrm{Scr}_\lambda(t,Bz^m)$. If moreover $K$ is Archimedean, $0\le z<1$ and $t>0$, there is $n$ with $\neg\mathrm{Scr}_\lambda(t,Bz^m)$ for all $m\ge n$.*
6. *(The additive band is one-sided.) If $\lambda\ge1$, $t\ge0$ and $x\ge0$, then $\mathrm{Add}_\lambda(t,x)\iff x\le(\lambda+1)t$; in particular $\mathrm{Add}_\lambda(t,0)$ holds. For $x\ge0$, $\mathrm{Scr}_\lambda(t,x)$ implies $\mathrm{Add}_\lambda(t,x)$. For signed $x$ the test $|x-t|>\lambda t$ does not imply $\neg\mathrm{Scr}_\lambda(t,x)$: take $t=1$, $\lambda=10$, $x=-19/2$.*

These are the mathematical facts under the screening rule [$\mathfrak{Q}_\beta$ precision-ladder $\S 4(d)$ formalize-first gate](#rem:q-beta-direction-sanity-gate). The rule fixes $\lambda=10$ and a target built from a measured value; neither is part of this statement.
