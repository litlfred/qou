*Argument (not a proof).*
Over a field $R$ for which $H_n(R,q)$ is split semisimple (e.g. $q$ not a root of unity in characteristic $0$), the Wedderburn–Artin decomposition $H_n(R,q)\cong\bigoplus_{\lambda\vdash n}M_{d_\lambda}(R)$ identifies $Z(H_n(q))$ with the block scalars. Over a general commutative ring this decomposition does not hold. In the split semisimple case the algebra has a centre–cocentre splitting, and the central part of an element $x$ is $\sum_\lambda\frac{\chi^\lambda(x)}{d_\lambda}e_\lambda$ ([centre-cocentre-descent theorem](#thm:centre-cocentre-descent), item 6).

Applying the split directly to $\mathfrak m=\sum_i\ln|M_i|$ is invalid: that sum is a number, not an element of $H_n(q)$, so the split does not apply to it; defining its descended part as "terms that factor through the block's Casimir eigenvalue" is a definition, not a result. This argument establishes nothing beyond the theorem cited. It does not establish the matches with liquid-drop terms in the statement.
