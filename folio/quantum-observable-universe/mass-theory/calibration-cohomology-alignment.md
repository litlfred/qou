Bring's curve has first cohomology

$$
  H^1 = 2W, \qquad \mathrm{rank}\,H^1 = 8, \qquad \dim_{\mathbb{C}} W = 4,
$$

two copies of the irreducible $4$-dimensional $S_5$ representation $W$ ([A fixed-depth recurrence for the prolonged periods exists (Cayley–Hamilton)](#prop:prolonged-periods-fixed-depth)). Applying the archimedean realisation $\tau$ leaves exactly three real degrees of freedom in its polarised Hodge structure:

| cohomological datum | calibration | channel |
|---|---|---|
| **scale** of the rank-2 $W$-Hodge plane $= a_D^{(e)}$ | CAL-1 $m_e$ ([CAL-1 (m_e) is the n=0 base period of the prolongation tower](#prop:electron-period-base)) | $h^0$ prefactor |
| **modulus** of that plane $=$ SL$_2$ trace $\tau = 2\cosh\theta$ | CAL-2 $q_0$ ([Symplectic prolongation (det B = 1) gives a one-parameter recurrence](#prop:sl2-prolongation-recurrence)) | cosh / bar-even |
| **scale** of the $\mathrm{Vol}(\mathrm{Hopf})=0$ meson sector | CAL-3 $m_\pi$ | orthogonal class |

The rank-2 $W$-Hodge plane carries a scale and a modulus. The meson sector, having no geometric scale derivable from $(m_e, q_0)$ due to its zero hyperbolic volume, contributes an orthogonal scale. The count $2 + 1 = 3$ matches the three calibrations. These form the choice of units forced at the substrate-to-archimedean wall.
