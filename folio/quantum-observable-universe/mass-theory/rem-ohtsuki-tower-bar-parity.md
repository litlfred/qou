The identification of the bar-odd $S = \mathfrak{Q}_\beta / E_0$ with the decay projection $\mathrm{Im}(q)$ (candidate (iii); [imaginary-$q$ decay-projection remark](#rem:c3-s-imq-decay-projection)) has a clean structural counterpart at the level of the figure-eight complex volume $\mathfrak{V} = \mathrm{Vol} + i\,\mathrm{CS}$. The finite-$b$ (one-loop-plus-corrections) expansion is the Ohtsuki tower

$$
  \mathfrak{V}_b = \mathfrak{V}_0 + \sum_{n \geq 1} \kappa_n\,(2\pi b^2)^n,
$$

with **real** coefficients $\kappa_n \in \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{3})\cdot\pi^{\lceil n/2\rceil}$ (for instance $\kappa_1 = 11\sqrt{3}\,\pi/108$ and $\kappa_2 = 2\pi^2/27$). At the aether the fluctuation variable is **imaginary**: with $b = \sqrt{\log q_0 / 2\pi}\,e^{i\pi/4}$ one has $2\pi b^2 = i\,t$, where $t = \log q_0 \in \mathbb{R}$.

The imaginary aether direction splits the tower by parity, and the split is exactly the $\mathfrak{V} = \mathrm{Vol} + i\,\mathrm{CS}$ decomposition. Since $(i\,t)^2 = -t^2$ is real, pairing the imaginary factors gives:

- **even rungs are real**: $\mathrm{Im}\big((i\,t)^{2m}\,\kappa\big) = 0$, so the even sub-tower feeds $\mathrm{Re}\,\mathfrak{V} = \mathrm{Vol}$, the bar-even (mass) face;
- **odd rungs are imaginary**: $\mathrm{Re}\big((i\,t)^{2m+1}\,\kappa\big) = 0$, so the odd sub-tower feeds $\mathrm{Im}\,\mathfrak{V} = \mathrm{CS}$, the bar-odd $S$ (decay) face.

Both statements hold over $\mathbb{C}$, with the concrete leading rungs $\kappa_1$ (odd, imaginary) and $\kappa_2$ (even, real) as instances.

The consequence for the $c_3$ wall is that the bar-odd $S$ (and hence its anchor-dependent sign-flip) lives **entirely in the odd (imaginary) sub-tower**; the even sub-tower is the anchor-blind volume/mass channel. The odd sub-tower is factorially divergent (Gevrey-1), and its Borel–Padé resummation at the imaginary aether is single-valued: the Borel singularity sits at the real action $A = \mathrm{Vol}(4_1)/\pi \approx 0.646$ (the geometric-to-conjugate saddle gap), off the imaginary integration ray, so there is no Stokes ambiguity and no exponentially small trans-series. Resurgence therefore returns the exact smooth $\mathrm{Im}\,\mathfrak{V} \approx 0.055$ together with the action cross-check, but supplies no anchor-dependent piece. This confirms, from the resummation side, that the $S$ sign-flip is the $1/u^3$ ladder representation of a smooth object rather than a physical singularity (compare [imaginary-$q$ decay-projection remark](#rem:c3-s-imq-decay-projection)).

**A second, distinct instance of the same bar-parity principle: the deuteron binding.** The rung-parity split above is one realisation of "bar involution splits the object by parity"; the deuteron binding correction realises the *same principle on a different object*, namely the Frobenius-paired Markov trace $\mathrm{tr}_M(\beta_D) = \sum_{\lambda \vdash 6} w_\lambda(q)\,\chi^\lambda_q(\beta_D)$ over the eleven Wedderburn blocks of $H_6(q)$ (distinct from the figure-eight complex-volume tower). Here the bar involution $q \mapsto q^{-1}$ acts on the per-block term $T_\lambda(q) = w_\lambda(q)\,\chi^\lambda_q(\beta_D)$ by a **signed** partition conjugation $\lambda \mapsto \lambda'$ (Young-diagram transpose). Three exact facts pin it: the Markov weight is conjugation-invariant, $w_\lambda(q) = w_{\lambda'}(q)$ (the hook multiset is preserved under transpose); partition conjugation *is* the bar involution on characters, $\chi^{\lambda'}(\beta_D; q) = \chi^\lambda(\beta_D; q^{-1})$ (verified against the derived $q_0$/$q_0^{-1}$ caches); and the weight carries its own bar-sign $w_\lambda(q^{-1}) = (-1)^{n-1} w_\lambda(q)$ from the $(q-q^{-1})^{n-1}$ prefactor. Together these give $T_\lambda(q^{-1}) = (-1)^{n-1} T_{\lambda'}(q)$, and for the deuteron $n = 6$ the sign is $(-1)^5 = -1$. For $n = 6$ the eleven partitions form five conjugate pairs plus the single self-conjugate $(3,2,1)$, and (because of that $-1$) the self-conjugate block is **bar-odd**: $T_{(3,2,1)}(q^{-1}) = -T_{(3,2,1)}(q)$, so it contributes **only** to the bar-odd impurity $t$, never to the bar-even coefficient $g_2$. Equivalently, $g_2$ is carried by the conjugate-pair *asymmetries* $\Delta_\lambda - \Delta_{\lambda'}$ (which vanish on the self-conjugate), and $t$ by the pair-symmetric parts plus $(3,2,1)$. The parity backbone holds over a generic commutative ring; the derivation establishes the *structure* (signed parity) of the correction only, while the per-partition $\Delta_\lambda$ *magnitudes* remain the open Track-A gate.
