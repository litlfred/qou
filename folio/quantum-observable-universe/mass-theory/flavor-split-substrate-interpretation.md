**Remark (what the identity is about).**

Under the pair-Skein mass formula and the substrate ansatz for the per-quark split (Steps 1, 2 and 6 of the proof, all in the convention-C frame), the left-hand side of the identity is the substrate formula for the proton–neutron splitting $(m_n - m_p)/M_N$, with $M_N = (m_p + m_n)/2$. It needs no calibration to the $u$--$d$ mass difference: it depends only on $q$. Without the factor $1/3$, the right-hand side $\hbar_q^2 \cdot h_q \cdot [2]_q$ is the same splitting per quark, $(m_n - m_p)/\mu_{\text{avg}}$.

The factored form admits a categorical reading:

| Factor               | Categorical meaning                                                                   |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| $\hbar_q^2$          | Two quanta of substrate Planck action                                                 |
| $h_q$                | One Hecke crossing                                                                   |
| $[2]_q$              | Doublet quantum integer                                                              |

The flavor swap $\mathfrak{u} \leftrightarrow \mathfrak{d}$ acts via the Hecke generator $\sigma$.

**Remark (Bring-surface origin).**

The closed form emerges from per-sheet Picard–Lefschetz integration on the Bring color-tube cubic. The $1/3$ factor reflects the permutation average over three flavor placements.
