**Definition.** Let $G^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\mathrm{CS}}$ and $G^{q}_{\mathrm{CS}}$ be the $2 \times 2$ Gram matrices of the classical and the $q$-deformed Chern--Simons pairing on the rank-$2$ sublattice spanned by the force carriers $T_{2,9}$ and $T_{3,5}$ on Bring's surface ([Chern--Simons descent correction](#rem:em-coupling-chern-simons)), both written in one common basis, with $\det G^{q}_{\mathrm{CS}} \neq 0$. The **Gram-determinant ratio** is

$$
  \kappa \;:=\; \frac{\det G^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\mathrm{CS}}}{\det G^{q}_{\mathrm{CS}}}.
$$

A change of basis $B$ with $\det B \neq 0$ sends each Gram matrix $G$ to $B^{\top} G B$ and multiplies each determinant by $(\det B)^2$, so $\kappa$ does not depend on the basis chosen.
