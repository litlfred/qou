For the three nuclear crossing types (quarks as Borromean unknot components), the [transfer matrix](#def:transfer-matrix-operator) has the following crossing coefficients:

| Crossing | $K_1$ | $K_2$ | $c$ | $d$ | Hecke element |
|----------|-------|-------|-----|-----|---------------|
| $\mathfrak{p}$-$\mathfrak{p}$ | $u$ | $u$ | $1$ | $0$ | $\sigma$ |
| $\mathfrak{n}$-$\mathfrak{n}$ | $d$ | $d$ | $1$ | $-(q - q^{-1})$ | $\sigma^{-1}$ |
| mixed | $u$ | $d$ | $1$ | $-\frac{1}{2}(q - q^{-1})$ | $\frac{1}{2}(\sigma + \sigma^{-1})$ |
