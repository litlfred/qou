**Definition (Frobenius dual).**
*Let $G_{ij} = \mathrm{tr}_M(b_i \cdot b_j)$ be the Gram matrix of the Frobenius pairing on the NF basis $\{b_i\}$ of $H_3(q)$ ([Frobenius Gram matrix](#prop:frobenius-gram-matrix)), read under **convention D** on the quotient $H_3/\mathrm{rad}\,\mathrm{tr}_M \cong \mathrm{TL}_3$, where the induced pairing is non-degenerate. For a state $K = \sum_i c_i b_i$ in NF, the **Frobenius dual** is the unique element $K^\vee \in H_3(q)$ satisfying $\langle K^\vee, b_j \rangle_F = c_j$ for all $j$. Explicitly:*

$$
  K^\vee = \sum_{i,j} c_i (G^{-1})_{ij} b_j.
$$
