$$
  \frac{\mathfrak{m}_X}{\mathfrak{m}_\pi}
  \;=\; \chi^{(2,1)}(\sigma_1^7)\cdot\Bigl(\frac{V_S(T(2,7))}{V_S(T(2,3))}\Bigr)^2.
$$

Its value at $q_0$ and the identification with a measured meson are recorded in [this remark](#rem:gen3-meson-phi-hecke-seifert-identification).
