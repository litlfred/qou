Because the Frobenius collapse $T_\beta = S\cdot\mathbf{1}$ ([Min-Frobenius-norm collapse](#prop:t-beta-markov-frobenius-collapse)) reduces the leading $\beta$-decay amplitude on $H_3(q)$ to a scalar, the sub-leading precision coefficients $c_k$ of the $\mathfrak{Q}_\beta$ ladder (`sec:mass-algebraic-complex-volume-ladder`) must arise from a fibre-level Habiro kernel $F(q)$. Rather than searching unconstrained rational expressions for $c_k$, this section establishes the algebraic invariance gates that any candidate kernel $F(q)$ must satisfy over $\mathbb{Q}(q)$ prior to archimedean evaluation:

1. **Classical vanishing and shadow gate:** In the classical limit $q \to 1$, the ratio $\mathfrak{Q}_\beta/E_0$ vanishes while the normalized Taylor shadows reproduce $c_2 \to -1$ and $c_3 \to J_{\mathrm{Hopf}}(1) = -2$ ([$S(q) = \mathfrak{Q}_\beta(q)/E_0(q)$ vanishes at the classical point $q = 1$ — the SDP-constraint scalar is not the $c_k$ ladder normalization](#prop:q-beta-over-e0-classical-vanishing), [$\mathfrak{Q}_\beta$ precision-ladder $\S 4(c)$ formalize-first gate](#prop:q-beta-q-eq-one-shadow-gate)).
2. **Bar-even kernel invariance:** The kernel modifying the bar-odd carrier must itself be bar-even under $q \mapsto q^{-1}$ so that the expansion proceeds in integer powers of $u = b\,\hbar_q^2$ ([$\mathfrak{Q}_\beta$ precision-ladder $\bar{}$-even discriminant](#prop:q-beta-bar-even-kernel-invariant)).
3. **Habiro $q$-difference and Jackson $q$-Bessel recursion:** Any kernel in the Habiro ring satisfies a finite-order $q$-difference equation, inducing a $C$-finite contiguity recurrence on $c_k$ bridged to the per-block $z$-recursion ([$c_k$ precision-ladder recursion induced by the fibre-level Habiro $q$-difference equation — depth-$d$ linear recursion on $c_k$ from the $q$-shift kernel $R(q,\xi)$ acting on $F(\xi)$](#prop:q-beta-c-k-recursion-from-habiro-q-difference), [Bridge: $z$-Taylor recursion vs $c_k$ ladder recursion](#prop:qbeta-z-recursion-to-ck-recursion-bridge)).
4. **Radius-of-convergence and magnitude screens:** The kernel's singularity locus and sign pattern are constrained by the radius-of-convergence and direction-sanity gates ([$\mathfrak{Q}_\beta$ precision-ladder $\S 4(a)$ gate](#conj:q-beta-radius-of-convergence-gate), [The order-of-magnitude screen $\mathrm{Scr}_\lambda(t,x)$](#prop:order-of-magnitude-screen), [$\mathfrak{Q}_\beta$ precision-ladder $\S 4(d)$ formalize-first gate](#rem:q-beta-direction-sanity-gate)), packaged together with the leading bias-correction identity into the bar-even kernel typeclass ([$\mathfrak{Q}_\beta$ formalize-first unified typeclass](#prop:q-beta-bar-even-kernel-typeclass)).
