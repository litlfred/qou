Because the Frobenius collapse $T_\beta = S\cdot\mathbf{1}$ ([minimum-Frobenius-norm $T_\beta$ collapse](#prop:t-beta-markov-frobenius-collapse)) reduces the leading $\beta$-decay amplitude on $H_3(q)$ to a scalar, the sub-leading precision coefficients $c_k$ of the $\mathfrak{Q}_\beta$ ladder (the mass-algebraic-complex-volume-ladder section) must arise from a fibre-level Habiro kernel $F(q)$. Rather than searching unconstrained rational expressions for $c_k$, this section establishes the algebraic invariance gates that any candidate kernel $F(q)$ must satisfy over $\mathbb{Q}(q)$ prior to archimedean evaluation:

1. **Classical vanishing and shadow gate:** In the classical limit $q \to 1$, the ratio $\mathfrak{Q}_\beta/E_0$ vanishes while the normalized Taylor shadows reproduce $c_2 \to -1$ and $c_3 \to J_{\mathrm{Hopf}}(1) = -2$ ([classical $q = 1$ vanishing of $\mathfrak{Q}_\beta/E_0$](#prop:q-beta-over-e0-classical-vanishing), [classical $q = 1$ integer-shadow criterion](#prop:q-beta-q-eq-one-shadow-gate)).
2. **Bar-even kernel invariance:** The kernel modifying the bar-odd carrier must itself be bar-even under $q \mapsto q^{-1}$ so that the expansion proceeds in integer powers of $u = b\,\hbar_q^2$ ([bar-even ladder-kernel discriminant](#prop:q-beta-bar-even-kernel-invariant)).
3. **Habiro $q$-difference and Jackson $q$-Bessel recursion:** Any kernel in the Habiro ring satisfies a finite-order $q$-difference equation, inducing a $C$-finite contiguity recurrence on $c_k$ bridged to the per-block $z$-recursion ([Habiro $q$-difference $c_k$ recursion](#prop:q-beta-c-k-recursion-from-habiro-q-difference), [$z$-Taylor to $c_k$-ladder bridge](#prop:qbeta-z-recursion-to-ck-recursion-bridge)).
4. **Radius-of-convergence and magnitude screens:** The kernel's singularity locus and sign pattern are constrained by the radius-of-convergence and direction-sanity gates ([ladder radius-of-convergence conjecture](#conj:q-beta-radius-of-convergence-gate), [order-of-magnitude screen](#prop:order-of-magnitude-screen), [ladder magnitude-direction criterion](#rem:q-beta-direction-sanity-gate)), packaged together with the leading bias-correction identity into the bar-even kernel typeclass ([unified bar-even kernel criterion](#prop:q-beta-bar-even-kernel-typeclass)).
