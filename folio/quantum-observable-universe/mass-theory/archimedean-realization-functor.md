**Definition.**
*The **archimedean realization functor** $\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{top}}$ is the composite*

$$
  \mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{top}}
  \;:\;
  \widehat{\mathcal{M}}
  \;\xrightarrow{\;\;\chi_{\mathrm{top}}\;\;}\;
  \widehat{\mathcal{M}} \otimes_{K_0(\mathrm{Var})} \mathbb{Z}
  \;\xrightarrow{\;\;\pi_\infty\;\;}\;
  \mathbb{R}
$$

*sending the motivic class $[X]$ to its topological Euler characteristic $\chi_{\mathrm{top}}(X)$ followed by the **archimedean projection** $\pi_\infty \colon \mathbb{A} \to \mathbb{R}$.*
