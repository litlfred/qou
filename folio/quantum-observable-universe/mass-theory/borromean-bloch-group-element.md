**Conjecture (K-theoretic / Bloch-group closure).**
*The Borromean rings complement $\mathbf{S}^3 \setminus \mathcal{B}_3$
defines a non-trivial element $[\mathcal{B}_3] \in K_3(\mathbb{C})$
(equivalently, an element of the Bloch group $B(\mathbb{C})$) whose
**Beilinson regulator $r_2: K_3(\mathbb{C}) \to \mathbb{R}$ evaluates
to** $\mathrm{Vol}(\mathcal{B}_3) = 8G$.  The Markov-trace coefficient
$h^4 = -3/4$ of $\mathrm{tr}_M(\mathcal{B}_3, q)$ has a K-theoretic
shadow as the next-order regulator term: $r_2[\mathcal{B}_3] \cdot
(h^4\text{-coefficient}) = (8G)(-3/4) = -6G$.*

## The K-theoretic structure

The Bloch group $B(F)$ of a field $F$ is generated by symbols
$[z]$ for $z \in F \setminus \{0, 1\}$ modulo the **5-term
relation**

$$
  [x] + [y] + [1 - xy] + \bigl[\tfrac{1 - x}{1 - xy}\bigr] + \bigl[\tfrac{1 - y}{1 - xy}\bigr]
  \;=\;
  0,
$$

which IS the Bloch-Wigner / Rogers Pentagon identity for the
dilogarithm.  Bloch–Suslin showed $B(\mathbb{C}) \otimes \mathbb{Q}
\cong K_3^{\text{ind}}(\mathbb{C}) \otimes \mathbb{Q}$. The extended Bloch group $\widehat{B}(\mathbb{C})$ of Neumann, which refines $B(\mathbb{C})$ so that the complexified volume (volume plus $i$ times the Chern--Simons invariant, valued in $\mathbb{C}/4\pi^2\mathbb{Z}$) of a hyperbolic $3$-manifold is defined on it, is surveyed in \cite[\S3]{arxiv-2304.12545v1}.

The **Beilinson regulator** $r_2: B(\mathbb{C}) \to \mathbb{R}$ is
given by the Bloch-Wigner dilogarithm:

$$
  r_2([z]) \;=\; D(z)
  \;=\;
  \mathrm{Im}\,\mathrm{Li}_2(z) + \log|z| \cdot \arg(1 - z).
$$

For an ideal tetrahedron with cross-ratio $z = e^{i\theta}$ on the unit
circle, $\log|z| = 0$, so the regulator collapses to the **Clausen
function**

$$
  D(e^{i\theta}) \;=\; \mathrm{Im}\,\mathrm{Li}_2(e^{i\theta}) \;=\; \mathrm{Cl}_2(\theta),
  \qquad
  \Lambda(\theta) \;=\; \tfrac{1}{2}\mathrm{Cl}_2(2\theta),
$$

where $\Lambda$ is the **Lobachevsky function**.  So hyperbolic volume
IS a regulator evaluation, per
[$\mathrm{Vol}(K)$ as a motivic period: the Bloch--Wigner regulator reading of $\tau_{\mathrm{arch}}$](#rem:vol-as-motivic-period).  The two
normalisations differ by both an argument doubling and a factor $2$;
keeping one function's coefficient while writing the other's name is
a recurring slip.

## The Borromean Bloch-group element

Per
[Quantum Bloch group and the pentagon identity](#def:quantum-bloch-group) and the
classical Bloch-Suslin construction:

- The Borromean complement decomposes into **two** regular ideal
  octahedra (Thurston), each of volume $4G = 4\,\mathrm{Cl}_2(\pi/2)
  \approx 3.66386$.
- In the Bloch group each octahedron is $4 \cdot [i]$, so the
  cross-ratio carried by the class is the **fourth** root of unity
  $\zeta_4 = i = e^{i\pi/2}$ — not the sixth root $e^{i\pi/3}$, which
  is the figure-eight parameter.
- The total Bloch-group element is

$$
  [\mathcal{B}_3]_{B(\mathbb{C})}
  \;=\;
  8 \cdot [i]
  \;\in\;
  B(\mathbb{C}),
$$

with regulator $r_2[\mathcal{B}_3] = 8 \cdot D(i) = 8\,\mathrm{Cl}_2(\pi/2)
= 8G$ (Catalan's constant; see
[Binding energy scale $\alpha = m_e \times 8G$](#conj:binding-scale-catalan)).  This is
the $\zeta_4$ row of the
[transcendental-accounting table](../appendix-surreals/transcendental-accounting-table-data.md);
the figure-eight sits in the $\zeta_6$ row with
$\mathrm{Vol}(4_1) = 2\,\mathrm{Cl}_2(\pi/3)$.

## The conjectural content — $h^4$ as the next-order regulator term

The Beilinson regulator extends to higher orders via the formal
$q$-expansion of $\Phi_q$ around $q = 1$ (the archimedean limit per
[$(1{-}q)\,\log\Phi_n(x; q) \to \mathrm{Li}_2(x)$ under $\tau_{\mathrm{arch}}$](#conj:dilogarithm-archimedean-limit)):

$$
  \log \Phi_q(z)
  \;=\;
  \frac{1}{q - 1}
  \bigl(
    \mathrm{Li}_2(z) + h \cdot R_1(z) + h^2 \cdot R_2(z) + \cdots
  \bigr),
$$

where $R_k(z)$ are higher-Bloch regulators.  The conjecture is:

$$
  \frac{r_2^{(2)}[\mathcal{B}_3]}{r_2[\mathcal{B}_3]}
  \;=\;
  h^4 \text{ coefficient of } \mathrm{tr}_M(\mathcal{B}_3, q)
  \;=\;
  -\frac{3}{4},
$$

where $r_2^{(2)}$ is the order-$h^2$ correction to the leading
$r_2$ regulator.  Equivalently: $r_2^{(2)}[\mathcal{B}_3] = -6G$.

## Significance

Four candidate routes are open for deriving $h^4 = -3/4$
categorically:

1. **Direct Borromean Markov trace** — already done
2. **Pachner-Pentagon state-sum** —
   [Pachner $2$–$3$ move = Pentagon identity at Borromean ideal-tetrahedron decomposition](pachner-pentagon-borromean.md)
3. **K-theoretic / Bloch group regulator** (this conjecture)
4. **DIM-master Pentagon** — research-grade

Closing this conjecture ties $h^4$ to the K-theoretic structure of $K_3(\mathbb{C})$
— the deepest connection to motivic / arithmetic structure.  It
also closes the loop:

- $r_2$ at order $h^0$ = $8G$ (the bare hyperbolic volume = production
  $\mathrm{Vol}(\mathcal{B}_3)$ in the canonical formalism),
- $r_2^{(2)}$ at order $h^4$ = $-6G$ (the Markov-trace coefficient),
- $r_2^{(k)}$ at higher orders — the full Markov-trace closed form.

- **GB filtration level**: N/A — the Bloch group lives at the

  no Coxeter length attaches naturally.
- **Jet order**: $h^4$ (the load-bearing claim is at the next-order

- **Bar-symmetry status**: bar-symmetric.  The Beilinson regulator

  (per [the bar involution](../braids-and-knots/bar-involution.md), Table 2 — Rogers
  q-dilog is bar-symmetric, Faddeev's $\Phi_q$ alone is bar-anti-
  symmetric).

## What remains open

1. **Compute prototype**: at $q = q_0$, numerically evaluate both
   sides of the conjectural identity
   $r_2^{(2)}[\mathcal{B}_3] = -6G$.  The left side requires
   extending the Bloch-Wigner regulator to order $h^2$ via the
   $\Phi_q$ formal expansion; the right side is already in
   [the Borromean Markov-trace closed form](#prop:borromean-markov-trace-closed-form).
2. Closing the conjecture needs a proof of the regulator identities
   above ($[\mathcal{B}_3] = 8 \cdot [i]$, $r_2[\mathcal{B}_3] = 8G$,
   $r_2^{(2)}[\mathcal{B}_3] = -6G$) and of the resulting $h^4$
   coefficient $-3/4$ from the regulator, which would rest on the
   Bloch–Suslin theorem.
3. **Cross-validation.** The Pachner–Pentagon state-sum
   and the K-theoretic regulator should both give $h^4 = -3/4$ —
   a triple cross-validation of the topological coefficient.
