*Let $\mathfrak{Q}_\beta(q)$ and $E_0^{(N{=}0)}(q)$ be the substrate closed forms from [Symbolic $Q$-value of free neutron decay](../nuclear-interactions/neutron-qvalue-symbolic.md) and [Energy scale of the Hecke normal form: from dimensionless debt to MeV](../nuclear-interactions/beta-decay-energy-scale.md):*

$$
\mathfrak{Q}_\beta(q) \;=\; m_e \cdot \frac{(2q + 1 - q^2)(q+1)}{2q^3},
\qquad
E_0^{(N=0)}(q) \;=\; \frac{m_e}{q(q-1)}.
$$

*The ratio*

$$
S(q) \;\coloneqq\; \frac{\mathfrak{Q}_\beta(q)}{E_0^{(N{=}0)}(q)} \;=\; \frac{(2q + 1 - q^2)\,(q^2 - 1)}{2 q^2}
$$

*satisfies $S(q = 1) = 0$ as an algebraic identity over any commutative ring.*

What this means for the $c_k$ ladder, with the values at $q_0$, is recorded in [the ladder reading](#rem:q-beta-over-e0-classical-vanishing-ladder-reading).
