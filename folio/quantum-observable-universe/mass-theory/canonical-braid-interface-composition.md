*The canonical interface factor of a $(\mathfrak{p}, \mathfrak{n})$ generator is the colour-tube-weighted form $\sigma - h(1/2 - \delta/4)$, with $\delta$ the [substrate flavour split](#prop:flavor-split-substrate); the table entry $\sigma - h/2$ is its $\delta = 0$ convention. The **interface composition** of $\beta(Z, N)$ is the triple $(n_{pp}, n_{pn}, n_{nn})$ counting the interface generators of each of the three types.*
