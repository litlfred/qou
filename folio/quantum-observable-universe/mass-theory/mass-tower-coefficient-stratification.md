*Working over $A = H_n(R, q)$, every prefactor $\alpha_k$ in the unified mass formula*

$$
  \mathfrak{m}_{q, \mathcal{K}}(\mathcal{T}) = \sum_{k = 0}^{n-1} \alpha_k \cdot \mathfrak{m}^{(k)}_{q, \mathcal{K}}(\mathcal{T})
$$

*is the Plancherel-weighted projection onto a specific curvature-rank stratum $\Lambda_k$. No prefactor is a free fit. Each is the unique scalar making the depth-$k$ stratum reproduce its Bethe--Weizsäcker counterpart.*

**Coefficient-to-stratum mapping.**

See [Table 1](#tbl:mass-tower-coefficient-stratification-table1-data).

*Each row reads: the Bethe--Weizsäcker coefficient is the Plancherel-weighted Frobenius integral over partitions $\lambda$ in the indicated stratum $\Lambda_k$. The sum $\sum_{\lambda \in \Lambda_k} w_\lambda(1) \leq 1$. Thus each coefficient is automatically bounded. Neither truncation nor fitting is required.*
