*Let $\beta \in H_n(R, q)$ be the Hecke normal form of a closed braid with $\mathrm{Vol}_{q_0}(\beta) \neq 0$. The categorical mass admits the closed form:*

$$
  \mathfrak{m}_{\mathrm{alg}}(\beta)
  \;=\;
  \frac{\Phi_1(\beta)}{\hbar_q^{2}},
\qquad
  \Phi_1(\beta) \;=\; \tau_{\mathrm{arch}}\bigl(\mathrm{Vol}_q(\beta)\bigr)
  \;=\; \ln\!\bigl\lvert\mathrm{Vol}_{q_0}(\beta)\bigr\rvert.
$$

*Before $\tau_{\mathrm{arch}}$, the only object computed is $\mathrm{Vol}_q(\beta) = \mathrm{tr}_M(\beta)$, which lies in $\mathbb{Z}[q^{\pm 1}][(q^{2}+1)^{-1}] \subset \mathbb{Q}(q)$ under convention D. The absolute value and the logarithm are archimedean operations and are applied only after the specialisation $q \mapsto q_0$.*

The algebraic quantum volume is a polynomial in $z = q^{3}/(q^{2}+1)$ with coefficients in $\mathbb{Z}[h]$ ([cleared mass trace](#prop:full-pole-structure-mass-trace)). Computing it needs no complex-analytic limit. Identifying $\Phi_1$ with the hyperbolic volume $\mathrm{Vol}(K)$ is a separate step, and it rests on the volume conjecture ([topological mass formula](#conj:topological-mass-formula)).
