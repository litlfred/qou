*Let $\mathfrak{H}$ be a confined particle with canonical Hecke element $T_w$ in Gröbner–Shirshov normal form. Its categorical mass invariant is the exact finite sum:*

$$
 M(\mathfrak{H}) \;=\; \nu_{\mathrm{SDP}} \cdot \bigl[\,\mathrm{tr}_M(T_w) + \mathrm{tower}(T_w) + \mathrm{tr}_M(C_{\dim_q})\,\bigr].
$$

*After applying the Archimedean realization functor at $q = q_0$, the physical binding energy is:*

$$
 B(\mathfrak{H}) \;=\; m_e \cdot M(\mathfrak{H}) \big|_{q = q_0}.
$$

*The derivation evaluates Yekutieli's multiplicative integral at finite slices. Since the GB spectral sequence collapses at $E_2$, no higher slices contribute. The computation relies entirely on the pre-Archimedean data encoded in the GB-filtration jet-order certificate.*

One condition left implicit by the display above is that the certificate under discussion is one actually emitted by the derivation pipeline. A mass-derivation certificate is a tuple of numbers whose bare tuple structure records no provenance — an arbitrary tuple with a negative $B_{\mathrm{pred}}$ would violate the mass identity. Stated for *every* numeric tuple, the mass identity is false, so the general theorem restricts to pipeline-derived certificates. The ten AME-validated atoms below satisfy the identity directly.

Two auxiliary conditions in the general formulation are automatic rather than load-bearing: the $E_2$-collapse condition unfolds to $2 = 2$ by reflexivity, and the Yekutieli condition reduces to non-emptiness of the braid-element domain, which the mass identity does not depend on.
