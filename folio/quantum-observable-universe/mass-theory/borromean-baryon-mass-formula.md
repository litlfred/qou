$$
  \mathfrak{m}(B)
  \;=\;
  \mathfrak{m}_{\mathcal{B}_3}(q_0)
  \;+\;
  m_e \cdot S(B)
$$

*where $m_e$ is the electron mass, $S(B)$ is the Skein contribution of the orientation partition evaluated at $q_0$, and $\mathfrak{m}_{\mathcal{B}_3}(q_0)$ is a common Borromean term depending only on the Borromean complement $\mathcal{B}_3$ (with $\mathrm{Vol}(\mathcal{B}_3) \approx 7.327$) and $q_0$ — shared by all Borromean baryons.*

The canonical absolute normalisation of the common term is the muon-anchored Hecke energy; only the orientation-independent cancellation, not any particular closed form for $\mathfrak{m}_{\mathcal{B}_3}(q_0)$, is used here.
