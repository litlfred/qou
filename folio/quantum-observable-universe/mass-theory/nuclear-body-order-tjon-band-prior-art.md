**Remark (prior art: body order in nuclear effective field theory).**
Klein's thesis reviews the correlations that two-body interactions impose on few-nucleon binding \cite[\S 2.3, pp. 19--20]{klein-fewbody}. Two-body interactions fitted to the same two-body scattering data give triton and ${}^4\mathrm{He}$ binding energies that lie along a line, the Tjon line, which becomes a band once the effective-field-theory error is estimated (his Fig. 2.3). Where the triton falls on that line is not fixed by two-body data: a three-body force is needed to determine three-body physics. Once the triton is fixed, ${}^4\mathrm{He}$ follows without a four-body force.

The [body-order series](#conj:binding-manybody-eq-jet-filtration) has the same shape, $B = \sum_{n \ge 2} B_n$ with a separate $n$-body term, so this is independent support for the position that the series needs a genuine term at order three and not only pairwise terms. It does not support the identification of the body order with the jet degree, which is the content of the conjecture, and the band is an empirical comparison, not an input: it may be used to check light-nucleus binding values the framework derives, never to set one.
