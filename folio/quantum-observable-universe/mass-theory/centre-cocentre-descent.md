*Let $K$ be a commutative ring and $A$ a $K$-algebra. Write $Z(A)$ for its centre and $[A,A]$ for the $K$-span of all commutators $ab-ba$. Suppose $A = Z(A)\oplus[A,A]$ as $K$-modules (a centre–cocentre splitting), and let $\pi_Z : A\to A$ be the projection onto $Z(A)$ along $[A,A]$. Put $x^{\mathrm{desc}} := \pi_Z(x)$, $x^{\mathrm{trans}} := x-\pi_Z(x)$ and $\theta_Z := 2\pi_Z-\mathrm{id}_A$. Then:*

1. *$x = x^{\mathrm{desc}} + x^{\mathrm{trans}}$ with $x^{\mathrm{desc}}\in Z(A)$ and $x^{\mathrm{trans}}\in[A,A]$, and this is the only such decomposition of $x$.*
2. *$\pi_Z$ is idempotent and $Z(A)$-linear: $\pi_Z(zx) = z\,\pi_Z(x)$ for $z\in Z(A)$.*
3. *(Traces.) For every $K$-linear $\tau : A\to M$ with $\tau(ab)=\tau(ba)$: $\tau(x)=\tau(x^{\mathrm{desc}})$ and $\tau(x^{\mathrm{trans}})=0$.*
4. *(Symmetries.) $\pi_Z\circ\theta = \theta\circ\pi_Z$ for every $K$-algebra automorphism $\theta$ and for every $K$-linear anti-automorphism $\theta$.*
5. *$\theta_Z$ is an involution. It is the identity on $Z(A)$ and $-\mathrm{id}$ on $[A,A]$.*
6. *(Classical; cited, not proved here.) If $K$ is a field and $A\cong\bigoplus_\lambda M_{d_\lambda}(K)$ is split semisimple with every $d_\lambda$ invertible in $K$, then $A$ has a centre–cocentre splitting and $\pi_Z(x) = \sum_\lambda \frac{\chi^\lambda(x)}{d_\lambda}\,e_\lambda$, where $\chi^\lambda$ is the block trace and $e_\lambda$ the central idempotent of the block. This applies to $H_n(q)$ over $\mathbb{Q}(q)$. For the Markov trace $\mathrm{tr}_M=\sum_\lambda y_\lambda\chi^\lambda$ it gives $\mathrm{tr}_M(x)=\mathrm{tr}_M(\pi_Z x)$.*
7. *(The descent idempotent is not this projection.) Let $n\ge2$, let $\ell : H_n(q)\hookrightarrow H_{n+1}(q)$ be the standard inclusion and $\mathfrak d$ any $K$-linear retraction of it. Then the image of $\hat\tau_n = \ell\circ\mathfrak d$ of [Descent involution](#def:descent-involution) is not contained in $Z(H_{n+1}(q))$. (The proof exhibits $g_1$ in the image and checks non-centrality in an explicit matrix representation of $H_3(q)$; that the matrices satisfy the Hecke presentation is verified by hand.)*

Item 3 refines [Hochschild homology](#def:hochschild-homology): every trace factors through $HH_0 = A/[A,A]$, and under the splitting $Z(A)\to A/[A,A]$ is an isomorphism. Item 7 says that the descended part of the corpus's descent idempotent is not central in general, so "descended" in the sense of [QOU mass formula as descent decomposition of the mass endomorphism](#conj:mass-categorical-descent) must mean $\pi_Z$, not $\hat\tau_n$.
