Candidate (iii) of the conjecture reads $\mathrm{Im}(q)$ as the non-Hermitian, open-system parameter and notes it "needs a categorical formula for decay widths $\Gamma$." The bar-odd part $S = \mathfrak{Q}_\beta / E_0$ of the $\beta$-decay $Q$-value supplies exactly such an observable: it is the antisymmetric ($\sigma$-bar-odd, sinh) companion of the symmetric mass scale, and vanishes at the real substrate — $S = 0$ when $\mathrm{Im}(q) = 0$ — so it is the leading response of the framework to a nonzero $\mathrm{Im}(q)$.

Reading $S$ as the first-order response of the figure-eight complex volume $\mathrm{Im}\,\mathfrak{V}$ to a small imaginary part $\varepsilon = \mathrm{Im}(q)$, with the derived meridian map $u = 2\log q$ and leading Neumann–Zagier $\mathrm{Im}\,\mathfrak{V} \approx (\sqrt{3}/2)|u|^2\sin(2\arg u)$, gives $S \approx (4\sqrt{3}\,\log q_0 / q_0)\,\varepsilon$. Inverting for the required $\varepsilon$ at the two anchors — the input is the measured $S$, not a fitted formula — yields

$$
  \varepsilon(\mu) \approx +0.015 \;(+1.3\%\ \mathrm{of}\ \mathrm{Re}\,q),
  \qquad
  \varepsilon(\mathfrak{p}) \approx -0.061 \;(-5.5\%),
$$

which is small and **sign-flipped** between the anchors. Because the two real substrate values $q_0$ differ by only $\sim 10^{-8}$, the required $\varepsilon$ cannot be a function of $\mathrm{Re}(q_0)$ at this resolution — it is an independent degree of freedom, as candidate (iii) requires. A real $q_0$ can never flip the sign of $S$; a sign-flipped small $\mathrm{Im}(q)$ does so directly, which is what makes the near-singular sensitivity of the decay coefficient reside in the imaginary (decay) coordinate rather than in $\mathrm{Re}(q)$.

This is a structural identification, not a closure: the map $q_0 \mapsto \varepsilon(q_0)$ is the same open object as the wall phase $\arg(u(q_0))$, now read as the decay projection's imaginary part. The magnitude $|\varepsilon|$ depends on the choice of derived deformation map $u(q)$; the sign-flip is robust. There is no tension with the canonical convention $\mathrm{Im}(q_0) = 0$: the masses are the value at $\mathrm{Im}(q) = 0$, while $S$ is the $\partial/\partial\,\mathrm{Im}(q)$ response — the Im-sensitive measurement the conjecture flagged as pending.
