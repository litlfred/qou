## Substrate Cohomology and the Three Calibration Units

The categorical pipeline derives physical spectra from the geometry of Bring's curve and a single deformation parameter $q_0$. The first cohomology $H^1$ of Bring's genus-$4$ surface is eight-dimensional and decomposes under the $S_5$ automorphism group into two copies of the irreducible four-dimensional standard representation, $H^1 = 2W$. While the algebraic curve rigidly fixes the rational polarized Hodge structure on $2W$, passing across the archimedean realization $\tau_{\mathrm{arch}}$ to real-valued observables introduces exactly three real cohomological degrees of freedom:

* **$\mathrm{CAL\text{-}1}$ ($m_e$, Hodge scale):** The overall scale of the rank-two $W$-Hodge plane, fixed by the unprolonged ($n=0$) electron period $m_e$, which serves as the base unit of the mass recurrence.
* **$\mathrm{CAL\text{-}2}$ ($q_0$, Hodge modulus):** The modulus of the $W$-plane, determined by the $\mathrm{SL}_2$ monodromy trace $\tau = 2\cosh\theta$ with $\theta = \log q_0$, pinned numerically by the lepton mass ratio $m_\mu/m_e$.
* **$\mathrm{CAL\text{-}3}$ ($m_\pi$, orthogonal Seifert scale):** The scale of the cohomologically orthogonal meson sector, whose Hopf-link complement is Seifert-fibered with zero hyperbolic volume and therefore independent of $(m_e, q_0)$.

Crossing the archimedean wall simultaneously separates observables by bar-involution parity. A Hecke generator has eigenvalues $q$ and $-q^{-1}$; under $\lambda = \log q$, its symmetric and antisymmetric combinations split into $\cosh\lambda$ (bar-even under $q \mapsto q^{-1}$) and $\sinh\lambda$ (bar-odd). Both parities assemble into a single invariant, the **complex volume** $\mathfrak{V}$ ([`conj:binding-qbeta-complex-volume`](../mass-endomorphism/binding-qbeta-complex-volume.md)): its real, bar-even part $\operatorname{Re}\mathfrak{V} = \mathfrak{E}_{\mathrm{bind}}$ governs masses and nuclear binding energies, whereas its imaginary, bar-odd part $\operatorname{Im}\mathfrak{V} = \mathfrak{Q}_\beta$ governs $\beta$-decay $Q$-values and transition phases, forming the categorical counterpart of the Neumann–Zagier complex volume $\mathrm{Vol} + i\,\mathrm{CS}$.
