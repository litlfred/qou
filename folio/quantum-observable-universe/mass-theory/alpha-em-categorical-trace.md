Older isolated PSLQ-uniqueness guesses matching $q_0$ lack theoretical backing. These residual artifacts fail exact representation limits and smooth $q$ continuity. They exist only as historical diagnostic markers and are replaced by the exact Hopf 2-strand operation model.

On the archimedean period side, Quillen's holomorphic anomaly as analysed for torus and Jacobian determinants by Luis Alvarez-Gaumé, Gregory Moore, and Cumrun Vafa \cite[§2, §4, §5]{alvarez-gaume-moore-vafa1986} separates two distinct classes of modular quantities. Weight-$0$ holomorphic modular functions (such as $j(\tau)$ and the level-$5$ quotient $t_5(\tau) = (\eta(\tau)/\eta(5\tau))^6$) and holomorphic Hecke eigenvalues cancel the metric factor and take frame-independent algebraic or integer values, whereas regularised Laplacian determinant norms such as $\det'\Delta(E_\tau) = (\mathrm{Im}\,\tau)^2\lvert\eta(\tau)\rvert^4$ carry non-vanishing determinant-line curvature $\partial_\tau\partial_{\bar\tau}\log\det'\Delta(E_\tau) = -1/(2(\mathrm{Im}\,\tau)^2) \neq 0$. Point values of anomaly-carrying determinant norms therefore depend on the choice of period frame through the non-holomorphic Gaussian factor, while only curvature coefficients and weight-$0$ holomorphic ratios remain frame-invariant.
