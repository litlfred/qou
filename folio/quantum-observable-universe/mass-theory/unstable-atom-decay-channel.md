*Let $K$ be an atom-knot that is [unstable](#def:unstable-atom) at $q$, and let $\lambda^*$ be a candidate decay channel. Assume $q$ lies off the boundary variety, and assume the LP-duality selection input of [the shadow-price proposition](#prop:mass-coefficient-shadow-price): off the boundary variety, every partition that minimises the constraint-violation energy equals $\lambda^*$. Then:*

1. *any two energy-minimising partitions coincide, so the minimiser, when one exists, is unique;*
2. *if the energy $E(\lambda^*, \cdot)$ is continuous on $[q_1, q_2]$ with $E(\lambda^*, q_1) \le 0 \le E(\lambda^*, q_2)$, then $E(\lambda^*, q^*) = 0$ for some $q^* \in [q_1, q_2]$.*

*The existence of $\lambda^*$ is not part of this statement.*
