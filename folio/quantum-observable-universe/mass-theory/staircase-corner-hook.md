*Let $\rho_k = (k, k-1, \dots, 1)$ be the staircase partition, $k \geq 1$. Its corner cell $(0, 0)$ has $\mathrm{arm}(0,0) = k - 1$ (row $0$ has $k$ cells) and $\mathrm{leg}(0,0) = k - 1$ (there are $k$ rows). The corner hook is therefore*

$$
  h_{\mathrm{corner}}(\rho_k)
  \;=\; (k - 1) + (k - 1) + 1
  \;=\; 2k - 1.
$$

*The formal statement is the final arithmetic step, $(k-1) + (k-1) + 1 = 2k - 1$ in $\mathbb{N}$ for $k \geq 1$; the arm and leg values are read off the shape of $\rho_k$ and are not formalised as properties of a partition.*
