When a single nucleon strand is adjoined to an $A$-nucleon braid via the inclusion $H_A(q) \hookrightarrow H_{A+1}(q)$, the Gröbner–Shirshov normal form increments by a bounded Coset representative. This section proves the additive linearity of the per-nucleon shift across the spectral sequence, states the finite-dimensional LP-duality hypothesis class that formalizes strong duality in Lean, and proves that Frobenius self-duality on transpose partitions $\lambda \leftrightarrow \lambda'$ pairs conjugate bound-state channels while the exterior-square Borromean representation enforces three-strand colour confinement.
