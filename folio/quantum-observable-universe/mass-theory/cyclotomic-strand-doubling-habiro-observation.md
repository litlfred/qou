**Remark (observed in computed Habiro elements).** This remark records a computed observation that motivated the [identity $\Phi_8(X) = \Phi_4(X^2)$](#prop:cyclotomic-strand-doubling). It is an observation about computed examples rather than part of the identity.

**Universal atomic-step cyclotomic.**
The Habiro elements for successive atomic-step pairs—from deuteron to helium—universally extract a $\Phi_8^2$ cyclotomic prefactor. The appearance of $\Phi_8$ precedes the mechanical $m=8$ Pochhammer boundary, confirming it as a structural interference signature amongst the Habiro coefficients themselves.

The prior hypothesis interpreting $X \mapsto X^2$ as a categorical strand-doubling map failed empirical tests. The substitution does not generate a powers-of-two cyclotomic tower for heavier nuclei. The polynomial identity itself holds unconditionally, while a categorical mechanism explaining the recurring $\Phi_8^2$ factor across light isotopes is conjectural.
