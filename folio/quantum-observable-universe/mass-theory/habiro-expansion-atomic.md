*The algebraic quantum volume of a confined atom $\mathfrak{H}$ admits a $q$-Habiro expansion:*

$$
  \mathrm{Vol}_{\mathrm{alg}}(\mathfrak{H})
  \;=\;
  \sum_{n=0}^N c_n(q) \, (q; q)_n
$$

*where the truncation depth $N$ correlates with the number of valence nucleons.*

This proposition guarantees that the binding energy formula terminates algebraically when evaluated at a root of unity. The coefficients $c_n(q)$ determine the specific shell contributions. This structure bridges the categorical operator traces to the empirical magic-shell mass phenomenology.

For a knot, the expansion of this shape is Habiro's. The colored Jones polynomials are $J_n(q) = \sum_{k \ge 0} c_{n,k}(q)\,H_k(q)$ with $H_k(q) \in \mathbb{Z}[q^{\pm1}]$ and a knot-independent cyclotomic kernel $c_{n,k}(q) = q^{-kn}(q^{n+1};q)_k\,(q^{n-1};q^{-1})_k$ that vanishes for $k \ge n \ge 1$, so the sum terminates at each color; at $n = 0$ the same sum defines an element of the Habiro ring, and replacing $q^n$ by a variable gives an element of the colored Habiro ring \cite[\S2.2, eqs.~(2)--(4)]{garoufalidis-kashaev-descendants2023}. The same paper records a second realization of the colored Jones polynomials through R-matrices at roots of unity built from solutions of the pentagon identity \cite[Abstract and \S3]{garoufalidis-kashaev-descendants2023}. Both statements concern knot invariants; the application of the expansion to $\mathrm{Vol}_{\mathrm{alg}}(\mathfrak{H})$ and the reading of $N$ above are made here and are not in that source.
