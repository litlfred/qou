**Remark (topological mass evaluation).**
*This remark evaluates the two mass scales attached to the trefoil $\tau(2,3)$ (electron knot) and the figure-eight $4_1$ (Higgs knot).*

The trefoil is a non-hyperbolic torus knot: its complement is Seifert-fibered, so its hyperbolic volume — and with it any volume-route mass — vanishes ([torus-knot zero volume via Reeb](#rem:torus-knot-zero-volume-via-reeb)). The exclusion is not merely geometric. The colored Jones asymptotics of a torus knot contain no volume-type limit at all: the Kashaev-point growth is polynomial, and the deformed limit degenerates to the inverse Alexander polynomial on one side of the real axis and a pure Chern–Simons phase on the other \cite{murakami2004a}. The quantum-dilogarithm value

$$
  \frac{\mathrm{Li}_2(q^{-2})}{\hbar_q^{2}} \approx 112
$$

is therefore not an analytic continuation of the volume. It survives only as the natural-units rendering of the electron mass unit (CAL-1): the electron scale is a calibration input, not a trefoil volume prediction.

The figure-eight is hyperbolic, and its volume mass is genuine:

$$
  \mathfrak{m}(4_1) = \frac{\mathrm{Vol}(S^3 \setminus 4_1)}{\hbar_q^{2}} \approx 207.1.
$$

This equals the muon-to-electron mass ratio through the CAL-2 identity $m_\mu/m_e = \mathrm{Vol}(4_1)/\hbar_q^2$. The figure-eight is the Higgs knot, not the muon: torus-knot leptons carry no intrinsic volume mass, and the lepton mass scale $\sim 10^2$ lives in the figure-eight coupling, modulated by the generation index $T_{2,3}, T_{2,5}, T_{2,7}$. The knot topology dictates the mass scale through this coupling.
