A classic limitation of the uncolored Jones polynomial—and hence of the bare closure Markov trace—is its inability to distinguish mutant knots such as the $11$-crossing Conway and Kinoshita–Terasaka pair, which differ by a $180^\circ$ Conway-sphere involution yet have distinct hyperbolic volumes ($\mathrm{Vol}(\mathrm{KT}) \neq \mathrm{Vol}(\mathrm{Conway})$).

This section shows how the bi-filtered Hecke framework resolves mutant ambiguity. Whereas the level-zero closure trace collapses the four-strand Conway tangle to a symmetric scalar, the knot-indexed convolution grid and the non-commutative Wedderburn Fourier transform retain the off-diagonal representation matrix elements before closure. Pushing these representation invariants into the Bloch group $B(\mathbb{C})$ separates the Conway and Kinoshita–Terasaka complements, links the Pieri photon shift and JSJ tower bridge to the WKB Green's function of the quantum dilogarithm, and identifies the Borromean complement's class in the Bloch group via Poincaré–Lefschetz duality.
