**Definition (q-dimension decoration of the NF basis).**
*The :defterm[$\delta_q$-decoration]{#delta-q-decoration} maps an algebra element $b \in H_n(q)$ to the tuple of its $q$-characters across each irreducible representation $V_\lambda$:*

$$
  \delta_q(b)
  \;:=\;
  \bigl(\chi_\lambda(b)\bigr)_{\lambda \vdash n}
  \;\in\;
  R^{|\,\mathrm{Par}(n)\,|}.
$$

The decoration lands in the $q$-dimension layer $R^{|\mathrm{Par}(n)|}$ ([q dimension layer definition](#def:q-dimension-layer)), the per-irrep coordinate structure that the Markov trace normally collapses.
