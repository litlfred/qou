**Theorem (conditional on the [Bring magic-number conjecture](../observations/magic-numbers-bring.md),
the [gravity spectral prefactor](../observations/gravity-spectral-prefactor.md),
and the [diagrammatic doubly-magic Littlewood–Richardson fusion conjecture](../observations/diagrammatic-double-magic-lr-fusion.md)).**
*For a light nucleus $^A_Z\mathfrak{X}$ that is doubly magic
(both $Z \in \mathfrak{M}$ and $N \in \mathfrak{M}$, with
$\mathfrak{M} = \{2, 8, 20, 28, 50, 82, 126\}$), the binding energy
admits the closed form*

$$
  \mathfrak{B}(^A_Z\mathfrak{X}) \;=\; \rho_F \cdot \mathfrak{m}_e \cdot A \cdot 8G
$$

*where $G$ is Catalan's constant, $8G = \mathrm{Vol}(L6a4) =
\mathrm{Vol}(\mathrm{Borromean})$ is the universal residue prefactor
([gravity spectral prefactor](../observations/gravity-spectral-prefactor.md)),
and $\rho_F \in \mathbb{R}$ is the aether-determined structural
prefactor for the doubly-magic regime.*

*Specialised to* $^4_2\mathrm{He}$ *with* $\rho_F = 2$:

$$
  \mathfrak{B}(^4_2\mathrm{He})
    \;=\; 2 \cdot \mathfrak{m}_e \cdot 4 \cdot 8G
    \;\approx\; 29.956\;\mathrm{MeV}
$$

*compared to the experimental value $28.296\,\mathrm{MeV}$ — a relative
miss of $+5.9\%$. This is dramatically tighter than the alpha-shift
formula ([alpha-shift binding extension](binding-alpha-shift-extension.md))
which misses $^4_2\mathrm{He}$ by $-79\%$ ($5\times$ too small) because that
formula assumes a single hyperbolic force-carrier knot per nucleus —
an assumption that breaks for doubly-magic nuclei.*

The regime necessity, the derivation of the $\rho_F$ prefactor, and
the numerical comparison are collected in
[the doubly-magic binding context](#rem:magic-A-binding-formula-context). Because this expression scales the single-Borromean volume $\mathrm{Vol}(L6a4) = 8G$ by $4\rho_F$ rather than deriving the multi-nucleon link from first principles, it serves as an exploratory scaling comparison alongside the three-dimensional simplicial $\alpha$-cluster hierarchy ([$\alpha$-cluster activation gate](#prop:alpha-cluster-activation-gate)) and spin–orbit shell closures on the nucleon spin–isospin fiber.
