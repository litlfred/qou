*Let $D_w(\lambda)$ be the Demazure character of the irreducible $\mathrm{GL}_n$-module $V(\lambda)$ of highest weight $\lambda$ (Demazure characters are defined for highest-weight modules, not for $S_n$-modules). The Demazure-fiber functional is defined via principal specialisation $x_i \mapsto q^{i-1}$:*

$$
  \boxed{\;
    T^{\mathrm{Dem}}(\lambda;\, w)
    \;:=\;
    y_\lambda
    \cdot
    \frac{D_w(\lambda)}{D_{w_0}(\lambda)}.
  \;}
$$

*For the longest element $w_0$, this saturates to the Jones-Markov coefficient $y_\lambda$. For $w < w_0$, it yields the structural resonance width: the $q$-weighted share of $V(\lambda)$ carried by the Demazure sub-crystal cut by $w$. At $q = 1$ this is the dimension ratio $\dim V_w(\lambda) / \dim V(\lambda)$.*

This functional structurally bounds the LP/SDP costs. It maps heuristic cutoffs to explicit Bruhat-prefix Demazure sub-crystals.
