*For the basis torus knot $T_{2,k}$ ($k$ odd), the [top-channel decoration](#def:torus-knot-top-channel-decoration) evaluates exactly as:*

$$
  \delta^{\mathrm{top}}_q(T_{2,k}) \;=\; q^{k} - q^{-k} \;=\; h\,[k]_q.
$$

*The identity holds purely algebraically in $\mathbb{Z}[q^{\pm 1/2}, h^{-1}]$ with no archimedean approximation.*
