*Let $c_k$ be the coefficients of the $\mathfrak{Q}_\beta$ precision-ladder factorisation $\mathfrak{Q}_\beta = \mathfrak{Q}_{\text{base}} \prod_{k \ge 2} (1 + c_k u^k)$, and let $g(u) := \sum_{k \ge 2} c_k u^k$ be their log-correction generating function. Then $g$ has a unique dominant singularity, and it is a simple pole at $u = u_{\text{substrate}}$, the value $u = b\,\hbar_q^2 \approx 0.00852$ at $q_0$.*

**Scope.** The $c_k$ in this statement are the convention-C frame extraction: $c_2 = -1$ is structural, $c_3$ is a near-match, and $c_k$ for $k \ge 4$ are calibrated against the CODATA residual ([Closed-form conjectures for $c_4, c_5, c_6, c_7$ in the $\mathfrak{Q}_\beta$ precision ladder (convention-C frame)](../mass-endomorphism/conj-qbeta-c-k-closed-forms.md)). No ladder has yet been derived under convention D. The supporting evidence is the finite window $c_2, \ldots, c_{10}$, which is consistent with the conjecture but cannot establish it ([evidence](q-beta-radius-of-convergence-gate-proof.md)).

**Use as a test.** If the conjecture holds, a candidate Habiro element $F(q)$ whose generating function has its nearest pole $\tilde u_0 \ne u_{\text{substrate}}$ cannot reproduce the ladder coefficients for all large $k$. The operational test rejects a candidate when $|\tilde u_0 - u_{\text{substrate}}| / u_{\text{substrate}} > 10^{-6}$. The threshold is a chosen value, and a rejected candidate is inconsistent with this conjecture, not refuted outright.
