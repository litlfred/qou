*The symmetric-channel normalization $\mathcal{N}_{\rm sym}(q)$ evaluates the Plancherel-weighted character product across the electroweak symmetric channel:*

$$
  \mathcal{N}_{\rm sym}(q)
  \;=\;
  \frac{q^4 + q^6 + q^8}{3}
  \;=\;
  \frac{q^4 (1 + q^2 + q^4)}{3}.
$$

The non-degenerate trace uniformizes across the 1-dimensional sym/alt blocks. The resulting expression is a closed-form rational with no free parameters. 

However, the direct cell-content trace now supersedes this Plancherel-weighted formulation. The structural input is isolated to the bar-self-dual cell trace itself, bypassing Plancherel weights.

The polynomial and its factored form are unconditional algebra. Reading it as the electroweak symmetric-channel weight rests on two open premises: [`prop:weak-spectral-completeness`](#prop:weak-spectral-completeness), that the electroweak content is exhausted by the commutative subalgebra $H_2(q)^{\otimes 3} \subset H_6(q)$ generated by $\sigma_1, \sigma_3, \sigma_5$ (three commuting copies of the pair $e^+, e^-$), and [`conj:electroweak-higgs-torus-link-triplet`](#conj:electroweak-higgs-torus-link-triplet), that the carriers are exactly $T(2,4), T(2,6), T(2,8)$. That reading is not derived from these premises.

The symbol $\mathcal{N}_{\rm sym}$ also appears in [`conj:g-action-on-substrate`](#conj:g-action-on-substrate), where it denotes a different quantity, the Frobenius trace $\mathrm{tr}_F(e_{\rm sym}^{\rm EW} \cdot e_H)$, recorded there with three candidate values $[4]_q^2\,[6]_q$, $2\,[4]_q + [6]_q$ and $[4]_q\,[6]_q$, none adopted; it is not $(q^4 + q^6 + q^8)/3$.
