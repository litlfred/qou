*Argument (not a proof).*
The nuclear braid $\mathcal{N}(Z,N)$ is built by interleaving $Z$
proton NFs and $N$ neutron NFs with typed crossings (pp, nn, pn).
Each nucleon NF is the Borromean braid $\mathcal{B}_3$ dressed with
quark crossings.

For $A = 3$: the three nucleon NFs compose to give a braid whose
NF has alternating $\sigma_0, \sigma_1$ signs and dominant $L_+$
(Hopf link) content.  This is the NF signature of the figure-eight
knot $4_1$, whose braid word is $(\sigma_0\sigma_1^{-1})^2$. This
identification rests on a comparison of eigenvalue structure, not on a
proof that the closures are isotopic. (The word
$\sigma_0\sigma_1^{-1}\sigma_0^{-1}\sigma_1$ given here before closes
to the unknot: by cyclicity and $\sigma_1\sigma_0\sigma_1^{-1} =
\sigma_0^{-1}\sigma_1\sigma_0$ its trace equals
$\mathrm{tr}_M(\sigma_0^{-1}\sigma_1)$.)

For $A \geq 4$: the composition of four or more nucleon NFs
produces identity-dominated NF with small Hopf corrections — the
signature of the Borromean link. Frobenius positivity
$G \cdot x \geq 0$ at all six basis channels is consistent with this
reading but does not establish it.

The binding scale follows from the topological mass formula
([topological mass formula](#prop:topological-mass-formula)):
$\mathfrak{m}(K) = \mathrm{Vol}(K)/\hbar_q^2$.  The volume
determines the energy scale at which the nuclear braid binds.

Neither identification is proved, which is why the statement is a
conjecture.

**Comparison boundary.** At the aether parameter, the figure-eight
volume $\mathrm{Vol}(4_1) \approx 2.030$ is the volume through which
$q_0$ is pinned, via $\hbar_q^2 = \mathrm{Vol}(4_1)/(m_\mu/m_e)$ with

at the aether scale is a statement about that evaluation rather than part of
the argument above.
