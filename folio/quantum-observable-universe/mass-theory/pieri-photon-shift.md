**Statement.**
*Let $\lambda = (\lambda_1, \lambda_2) \vdash n$ be a partition with at most two rows (the shapes seen by $\mathrm{SU}_q(2)$ on $V_\square^{\otimes n}$), let $\gamma$ be a [photon](#def:photon) $\gamma(\varphi) = \mathbf{1} + \varphi_0\sigma_0 + \varphi_1\sigma_1 + \varphi_{01}\,\sigma_0\sigma_1^{-1}$, placed on three consecutive strands of the tower (its degree-one specialisation $\varphi_{01} = 0$ included), and let $\beta_m$ be supported purely on the $\lambda$-block. Write $\lambda + \square := (\lambda_1 + 1, \lambda_2)$ and $\lambda - \square := (\lambda_1, \lambda_2 + 1)$, both partitions of $n+1$ (the second only when it is a partition). The support of the product $\gamma \cdot \beta_m$, read in the tower $\bigoplus_m H_m(q)$, satisfies:*

$$
  \mathrm{supp}\bigl(\gamma \cdot \beta_m\bigr)
  \;\subseteq\;
  \{\lambda\} \;\cup\;
  \{\lambda + \square\} \;\cup\;
  \{\lambda - \square\}.
$$
