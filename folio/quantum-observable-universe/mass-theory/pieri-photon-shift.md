**Statement.**
*Let $q$ be nonzero and not a root of unity, let $\lambda \vdash m$, and let $\beta_m \in H_m(q)$ be supported on the $\lambda$-block: its seminormal representation vanishes on every partition of $m$ other than $\lambda$. Then:*

1. *For every $a \in H_m(q)$, the product $a \cdot \beta_m$ is again supported on $\lambda$.*
2. *For every $\gamma \in H_{m+1}(q)$, for example a degree-one element $1 + \sum_{i=1}^{m} \phi_i \sigma_i$, the product $\gamma \cdot \beta_m$, with $\beta_m$ included in $H_{m+1}(q)$, is supported on the partitions obtained from $\lambda$ by adding one box:*

$$
  \mathrm{supp}\bigl(\gamma \cdot \beta_m\bigr)
  \;\subseteq\;
  \{\lambda + \square\}
  := \{\mu \vdash m + 1 : \mu \text{ is } \lambda \text{ with one box added}\}.
$$

*No partition with a box removed occurs.*
