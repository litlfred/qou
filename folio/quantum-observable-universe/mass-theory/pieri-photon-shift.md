**Statement.**
*Let $\lambda = (\lambda_1, \lambda_2) \vdash n$ be a partition with at most two rows (the shapes seen by $\mathrm{SU}_q(2)$ on $V_\square^{\otimes n}$), and let $\beta_m \in H_n(q)$ be supported purely on the $\lambda$-block. Let $\gamma$ be a [photon](#def:photon) $\gamma(\varphi) = \mathbf{1} + \varphi_0\sigma_a + \varphi_1\sigma_b + \varphi_{01}\,\sigma_a\sigma_b^{-1}$ on consecutive generators $a$, $b = a + 1$ with $b \leq n$, so that at most one of its three strands lies beyond the $n$ strands of $\beta_m$ (its degree-one specialisation $\varphi_{01} = 0$ included).*

1. *If $b \leq n - 1$, then $\gamma\beta_m \in H_n(q)$ and $\mathrm{supp}(\gamma\beta_m) \subseteq \{\lambda\}$.*
2. *If $b = n$, read $\gamma\beta_m$ in $H_{n+1}(q)$. Its Wedderburn support is contained in the set of $\mu \vdash n + 1$ with $\mu \supset \lambda$, namely $\lambda + \square := (\lambda_1 + 1, \lambda_2)$, $\lambda - \square := (\lambda_1, \lambda_2 + 1)$ (when it is a partition) and $(\lambda_1, \lambda_2, 1)$. On $V_\square^{\otimes(n+1)}$ the three-row component acts by zero, so there*

$$
  \mathrm{supp}\bigl(\gamma \cdot \beta_m\bigr)
  \;\subseteq\;
  \{\lambda + \square\} \;\cup\; \{\lambda - \square\}.
$$

*The name $\lambda - \square$ comes from the $\mathrm{SU}_q(2)$ reading: a complete two-box column is the trivial weight, so $(\lambda_1, \lambda_2 + 1)$ has the $\mathrm{SU}_q(2)$ highest weight of $(\lambda_1 - 1, \lambda_2)$. As a partition it has one box more than $\lambda$, not one less.*

*The case $a = n$ (two strands beyond $\beta_m$) is not covered: there the product lies in $H_{n+2}(q)$ and the support can reach partitions with two more boxes.*
