**Remark (Option E isospin test).**
*The Option E Kashiwara crystal weight $c_\lambda(q)$ evaluates the principal $q$-specialisation of the Schur polynomial, providing a direct test of whether the canonical $\bowtie_K$-coupled words preserve structural isospin invariance.*

The mirror isobars $^3\mathrm{H}$ and $^3\mathrm{He}$ fail the invariance test under Option E. The weight factor $c_\lambda$ heavily breaks the transpose-partition symmetry required by the Hopf antipode. Only symmetric weights can preserve the structural isospin equality; a properly symmetrised variant is required to correct the non-Markov-trace deviation.
