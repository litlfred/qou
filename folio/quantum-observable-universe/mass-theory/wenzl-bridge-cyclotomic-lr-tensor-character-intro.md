When two composite nuclear sub-braids of strand counts $m$ and $n$ fuse into an $(m+n)$-strand configuration, the character of the induced representation on $H_{m+n}(q)$ decomposes via the Littlewood–Richardson (LR) coefficients $c^\lambda_{\mu\nu}$. Combining this LR tensor branching with the $q$-Murnaghan–Nakayama rule reduces multi-strand character evaluations to a fast recursive algorithm over border-strip tableaux.

This section proves the Littlewood–Richardson tensor character bridge, formulates depth-stratified Frobenius reciprocity, and establishes the memoised $q$-Murnaghan–Nakayama recursion. Applying these tools yields closed rational expressions in $q$ and $z_D$ for the explicit coefficients $\alpha_k$ on $H_3(q)$ and $H_4(q)$, proves the Wedderburn–Bratteli recursion and the Convention-D two-row non-negativity of $y_\lambda$, and tabulates the resulting higher-order operators for terms $2$ through $6$ of the mass formula.
