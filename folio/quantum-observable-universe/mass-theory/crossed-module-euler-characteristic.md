*Let $\beta_{\mathrm{Bor}} = (\sigma_1 \sigma_2^{-1})^3$ be the Borromean braid. Send its six letters to $2 \times 2$ quaternion matrices by $\sigma_1 \mapsto i$ and $\sigma_2^{-1} \mapsto j^{-1} = -j$, so the crossing images are $c_1, \dots, c_6 = i, -j, i, -j, i, -j$, with crossing signs $s_1, \dots, s_6 = +1, -1, +1, -1, +1, -1$. Then the $h^2$ coefficient of the multiplicative surface integral, traced over the $2 \times 2$ quaternion representation, is*

$$
  \mathrm{tr}\!\bigl(\textstyle\sum_{k < l}
  s_k s_l \, c_k c_l\bigr) = -12 .
$$

*This is a finite computation over $\mathbb{Z}[i]$. The integer $-12$ equals $2 \cdot (2 - 2 \cdot 4)$, that is, $\dim V \cdot \chi(\Sigma_4)$ for $V = \mathbb{C}^2$ and Bring's genus-4 surface $\Sigma_4$. That numerical agreement is recorded, not derived: no map from this trace to $\chi(\Sigma_4)$ is part of the statement.*
