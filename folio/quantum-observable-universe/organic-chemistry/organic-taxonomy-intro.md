The standard organic classes fall out of the Hopf-chain framework along two sorting parameters: the bond-order pattern $(k_1, \ldots, k_{N-1})$, and whether the structure is open or closed. Saturation reads as linking-site occupancy. The hydrogen capacity follows directly: $\mathrm{C}_N \mathrm{H}_{2N+2-2d}$ (open) versus $\mathrm{C}_N \mathrm{H}_{2N-2d}$ (closed), read off from the total degree of unsaturation. Aromatic rings appear as the case where the secondary trefoils close.
