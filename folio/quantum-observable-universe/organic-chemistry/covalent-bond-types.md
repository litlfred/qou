Let $\mathcal{A}_1$ and $\mathcal{A}_2$ be atoms with residual sub-links $R_1$ and $R_2$. A **covalent bond** of order $k$ is a shared sub-Hopf link

$$
  B_k(R_1, R_2)
  = 
  \underbrace{T_{2,3} \oplus \cdots \oplus T_{2,3}}_{k \text{ shared trefoils}}
$$

where each shared trefoil is simultaneously Hopf-linked to one open site in $R_1$ and one open site in $R_2$. The integer $k$ is the **bond order**. The **crossing energy** of the bond is

$$
  E_{\mathrm{bond}}(k)
  = 
  k \cdot \mathfrak{E}_{\mathrm{cross}}(T(2,2))
  + 
  \binom{k}{2}  \Delta E_{\mathrm{inter}},
$$

where $T(2,2)$ is the Hopf link and $\Delta E_{\mathrm{inter}}$ is the inter-link interaction within the shared sub-link.
