**Alexander polynomial.**
For even $N = 2m$ the torus link $T(2,2m)$ has two components and Conway-normalised Alexander polynomial

$$
  \Delta_{T(2,2m)}(t)
  = 
  \bigl(t^{1/2} - t^{-1/2}\bigr) \, \frac{t^m - t^{-m}}{t - t^{-1}}.
$$

Like every link with two or more components, it vanishes at $t = 1$.

**Reduced Alexander polynomial.**
Dividing out the factor $t^{1/2} - t^{-1/2}$ gives the reduced polynomial

$$
  \tilde\Delta_{T(2,2m)}(t)
  = 
  \frac{t^m - t^{-m}}{t - t^{-1}}
  = 
  [m]_t .
$$

For a two-component link, $\tilde\Delta(1)$ is the linking number of the two components. Here $\tilde\Delta_{T(2,2m)}(1) = m = N/2$.

Evaluated at the aether parameter $t = q$:

| Threading | $m$ | $\tilde\Delta(q)$ | $\tilde\Delta(1) = \mathrm{lk}$ |
|-----------|:---:|-------------|:---:|
| $T(2,2)$ (Hopf) | 1 | $1$ | 1 |
| $T(2,4)$ | 2 | $q + q^{-1}$ | 2 |
| $T(2,6)$ | 3 | $q^2 + 1 + q^{-2}$ | 3 |
| $T(2,8)$ | 4 | $q^3 + q + q^{-1} + q^{-3}$ | 4 |
