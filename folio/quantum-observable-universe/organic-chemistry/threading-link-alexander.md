**Alexander polynomial.**
The Alexander polynomial of $T(2,N)$ for even $N = 2m$ is

$$
  \Delta_{T(2,2m)}(t)
  = 
  \frac{t^m - t^{-m}}{t - t^{-1}}.
$$

Evaluated at the substrate parameter $t = q$:

| Threading | $m$ | $\Delta(q)$ |
|-----------|:---:|-------------|
| $T(2,2)$ (Hopf) | 1 | $1$ |
| $T(2,4)$ | 2 | $q + q^{-1}$ |
| $T(2,6)$ | 3 | $q^2 + 1 + q^{-2}$ |
| $T(2,8)$ | 4 | $q^3 + q + q^{-1} + q^{-3}$ |
