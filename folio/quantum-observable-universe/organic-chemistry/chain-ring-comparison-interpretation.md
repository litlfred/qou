The pairwise Hopf-link estimate of the cyclisation barrier is a factor of two above the threading-link estimate. The two estimates differ only in the linking number they assign to the ring: $N$ for $N$ Hopf links, $N/2$ for $T(2,N)$.

**Derivation chain.**
The reduced Alexander weights are topological: at $q = 1$ they are linking numbers and need no input. Two inputs are not derived. The proportionality between crossing energy and reduced Alexander weight is a hypothesis ([conj:ring-closure-barrier](#conj:ring-closure-barrier)). The molecular energy quantum $|q + q^{-1}|_{\mathrm{mol}} \sim 1\text{--}2$ eV is a chosen scale, not derived from the substrate parameter $q_0$, which is pinned by calibration CAL-2.

**The factor of $1/2$.**
The ratio of the two estimates is $\tilde\Delta_{T(2,6)}(1) / 6 = 3/6 = 1/2$, a ratio of linking numbers.
