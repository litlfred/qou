Evaluating a braid closure through the skein resolution tree expands $c$ crossings into $3^c$ resolution leaves, whereas evaluating the trace of a product of $c$ matrices of fixed dimension $d$ takes at most $d^3 c$ ring multiplications. This section develops the exact algebraic structures governing skein-tree bandwidth bounds, polynomial-depth skein subposets, Wedderburn block rigidity of derivations and automorphisms on $H_n(q)$ \cite{geckpfeiffer2000}, and the root-of-unity orders of the Jaeger--Vertigan--Welsh tractable set \cite{jaeger1990}, closing with the Gödel--Turing degree-$0$ horizon theorem.
