**Proof.** One obtains the factorisation directly from the split-link multiplicativity of the Markov trace. This is equivalent to the disjoint-union rule for the Kauffman bracket and the Jones polynomial. Associativity, commutativity, and the identity law follow from the corresponding ring identities for the product of trace values. All four properties hold over a generic commutative ring $R$. The factorisation is definitional. $\qquad\blacksquare$
