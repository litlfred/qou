Jaeger, Vertigan and Welsh show that exact evaluation of the Jones polynomial $V_L(t)$ is $\#\mathrm{P}$-hard except at the six points $t \in \{1, -1, i, -i, e^{2\pi i/3}, e^{-2\pi i/3}\}$ \cite{jaeger1990}; this is cited, not derived. These six points are primitive roots of unity of orders $1, 2, 4, 4, 3, 3$. None of them is a primitive $6$th root of unity, and $i$ has order $4$, which does not divide $6$. So with $t = q$ the orders of the tractable points form the set $\{1, 2, 3, 4\}$, not $\{n : n \mid 6\}$. With $t = q^{\pm 2}$ the orders of $q$ form $\{1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8\}$, which is not $\{n : n \mid 6\}$ either.
