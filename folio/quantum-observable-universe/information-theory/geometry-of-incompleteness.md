**Theorem (geometry of incompleteness).** *Given a QOU whose aether is Turing-complete, the jet horizon $\mathcal{H} = V(\mathfrak{h})$ is non-empty: there exists a degree-0 datum whose truth is inaccessible. Equivalently, the degree-0 sections vanish on a non-trivial region, $\Gamma(\mathcal{H}, A_0) = 0$.*

A **degree-0 datum** is an observable answer read off the aether: a decision result, a particle identity, or the truth value of a self-referential sentence. It is **inaccessible** (behind the horizon) when it fails either route to access:

- **formally**, it is unprovable (Gödel incompleteness, forced in any aether expressive enough to encode arithmetic, namely every $q \neq 1$ aether, whose $q$-Toffoli gates are universal); or
- **computationally**, extracting it is super-polynomial. For the boundary family a bandwidth-limited device must read the full $3^{3n}$-leaf surviving skein resolution tree, so by the Bekenstein bandwidth bound its step count grows faster than every polynomial.

These are **two witnesses of one phenomenon**: the vanishing of degree-0 sections. Gödel supplies a formally-inaccessible degree-0 datum; the resolution-tree information bound supplies a bandwidth-intractable one. Either establishes a non-empty horizon; the QOU exhibits both, so the horizon is doubly confirmed. In the framework's reading, this common cause is exactly what a black-hole event horizon *is*: the region across which degree-0 truth cannot pass while degree-$\geq 1$ data (witnesses, Hawking radiation) leaks. The event horizon is the geometry of incompleteness.

**Scope.** This is an **existence / necessity** statement about the aether's structure: a Turing-complete QOU *must* host an inaccessible degree-0 region. It is **not** a proof of $\mathrm{P} \neq \mathrm{NP}$. The step from "the observation trace is intractable to extract" to "$\mathrm{P} \neq \mathrm{NP}$" is a counting-to-decision transfer that no known result supplies and that a generic argument cannot (a $\#\mathrm{P}$-hard count can sit atop a poly-time decision, as for perfect matchings). The bandwidth-intractability leg is genuinely derived; the formal-incompleteness leg is Gödel's theorem; only the *identification* of this joint inaccessibility with the black-hole horizon is the framework's degree-0 reading.
