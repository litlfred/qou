Let $\mathrm{Bun}(\mathbb{P}(V))$ be the category of finite bundles over the projective line, whose morphisms are permutation-isotopy pairs. The **observable subcategory** $\mathrm{Bun}^{\circ}(\mathbb{P}(V))$ is the full subcategory on the objects whose degree-$0$ algebra is non-trivial:

$$
  \mathrm{Bun}^{\circ}(\mathbb{P}(V)) = \bigl\{ \mathcal{S} \in \mathrm{Bun}(\mathbb{P}(V)) : \Gamma(\mathcal{S}, A_0) \neq 0 \bigr\}.
$$
