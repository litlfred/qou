**Remark (Relation to Peña et al. and 't Hooft).**
Peña et al. construct a reversible quantum Game of Life using Toffoli-based adder circuits. Their Toffoli gate is the classical limit $q \to 1$ of the $q$-Toffoli, and their construction is recovered in that limit; away from it the two are not comparable, since the $q$-deformed generator is invertible but not unitary. Their unitary tick maps to $3$ Planck steps. The $q$-deformed amplitude $P_{7,6}(q)$ serves as the physical AND amplitude. In the canonical convention $z_D = q^3/(q^2+1)$ it has a single zero on $q > 0$, at $q = 1$, and the diagonal $P_{7,7}^{D}$ has none. The well-definedness condition (the diagonal positive and the q-AND non-vanishing) therefore holds on $q \neq 1$, which contains the aether parameter; the one excluded point is the classical limit itself, where the $q$-deformation contributes nothing. The three operator zeros once quoted here ($q_\star = 1/\varphi^{2}$, $q_\Phi \approx 0.4656$, and $q = 1$) belong to the excised convention $C$; only the last survives the change of convention.

The relation to 't Hooft's proposal (that quantum mechanics emerges from a deterministic Planck-scale cellular automaton) is more limited, and the limitation is structural rather than a matter of detail. The mechanism 't Hooft relies on is that a deterministic evolution *permutes* a distinguished basis of ontological states, so that the operator it induces on the span of that basis is automatically unitary and generates a Schrödinger flow. That step requires unitarity of the tick, and by the statement's item 4 the $q$-Toffoli is unitary only at $q = 1$.

Consequently the identification holds at $q = 1$ and is not established away from it. At $q = 1$ the tick is a permutation of basis states, the ontological states may be read as Reeb bundle fibres $\mathcal{S}_p$ and the tick as the Planck step $t_P(q)$, yet $q = 1$ is also where the $q$-Toffoli degenerates to the ordinary Toffoli gate, so what the classical limit realizes is the construction of Peña et al. rather than anything the $q$-deformation contributes. At $q \neq 1$ the tick remains invertible, and the automaton remains deterministic and reversible in the sense a cellular automaton requires, but the induced operator is not unitary and 't Hooft's embedding does not apply as stated. Whether a deterministic automaton with an invertible non-unitary tick can support the emergence 't Hooft argues for is not addressed here.

The reading of the Markov trace as a categorical coarse-graining replacing 't Hooft's information loss is likewise not established. It is a suggestion the degeneracy of the trace form makes worth examining (a degenerate form has a radical, and quotienting by a radical is a loss of information rather than an *ad hoc* postulate of one), though no such identification is derived in this paper. The amplitude is written $P_{7,6}$ rather than $\Phi$, which is retired as an object rather than re-derived ([Toffoli transfer matrix](#prop:toffoli-transfer-matrix)); the renaming changes no criterion and no number.
