The local Planck length $\ell_P^{\mathrm{step}}(p)$ defines the minimal quantisable size of an information unit at a point $p \in M$. It scales with the aether parameter as $\ell_P^{\mathrm{step}} \sim \hbar_q$ --- the fraction of one Reeb circuit traversed per Planck step, not the circuit itself (see [share versus total](#rem:planck-length-share-vs-total)).
