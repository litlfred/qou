The local per-step Reeb share $\hbar_q(p) = 1 - q(p)^{-1}$ sets the minimal quantisable grain of an information unit at a point $p \in M$. An elementary information unit at $p$ scales with the substrate parameter as $\hbar_q(p)$ --- the fraction of one Reeb circuit traversed per elementary connection step, distinct from the unit-normalised full circuit $\hbar_q \cdot t_P = 1$.
