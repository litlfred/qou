Let $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ be a quantum observable universe, and let $f_q(z) = z^2 + c(q)$ be the Julia set iteration on the exceptional divisor ([Julia set of Reeb flow](#rem:julia-set-reeb)). The **information-theoretic time** at substrate parameter $q$ is the cumulative Reeb operation count

$$
  \mathcal{T}(q)  :=  \sum_{n=0}^{N-1} 1
  =  N,
$$

where $N$ is the total number of iterations of $f_q$ applied since a reference configuration $\mathcal{S}_0$ ([bundle initial conditions](#def:bundle-initial-conditions)).
