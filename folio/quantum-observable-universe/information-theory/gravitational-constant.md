The **gravitational constant** at substrate parameter $q$ is

$$
  G_q := \frac{c_q^3 \cdot \hbar_q}{S_{\mathrm{cell}}(q)}
  = \frac{c_q^3 \cdot (2q - 1)}{q^2 \cdot \eta_q \cdot \hbar_q},
$$

where $c_q = 1/(q-1)$ is the [speed of light](#def:information-speed-of-light), $\hbar_q = (q-1)/q$, $\eta_q = (2q-1)/q^3$ is the [operational entropy](#def:operational-entropy), and $S_{\mathrm{cell}}(q) = (q-1)^2/q^3$ is the cumulative jet-tower entropy of a Planck cell.
