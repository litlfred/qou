At the local substrate parameter $q_0$ ([local substrate $q$](#prop:local-substrate-q)),
approximately $10$ iterations of the Julia set dynamics $f_q$ occur within each Planck
time. Each iteration:

1. Advances the phase of the Reeb vector by $\hbar_q \approx 0.099$ of a full circuit.
2. Applies the $q$-connection $\nabla_q$ once, correlating adjacent fibers.
3. Evaluates one $R$-matrix, resolving one braiding crossing on the exceptional divisor.

The link-length resolution within the exceptional divisor grows with each operation.
After $n$ steps, the divisor resolves knots up to crossing number $n$. Within a single
Planck time at $q = q_0$, the exceptional divisor completes $\sim 10$ braiding
evaluations. This provides ample capacity to resolve the full electron-positron sector
($c = 3$).
