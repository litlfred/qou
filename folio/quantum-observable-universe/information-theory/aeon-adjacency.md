Let $\mathcal{A}$ and $\mathcal{A}'$ be aeons with boundaries $\partial\mathcal{A}$ and $\partial\mathcal{A}'$. The aeons $\mathcal{A}$ and $\mathcal{A}'$ are **adjacent**, and stand in **aeon adjacency**, if their boundaries share a common object:
$$
  \partial\mathcal{A} \cap \partial\mathcal{A}' \neq \varnothing.
$$
