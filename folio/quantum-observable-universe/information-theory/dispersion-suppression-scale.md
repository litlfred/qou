On a [one-dimensional channel](#def:one-dim-qou-channel) with ultraviolet cutoff energy $\mathfrak{E}_*$, the symmetric bar-even $q$-phase $\phi_q(\mathfrak{E}_\gamma / \mathfrak{E}_*) = \frac{\sinh((\mathfrak{E}_\gamma / \mathfrak{E}_*)\ln q)}{\sinh(\ln q)}$ ([photon modes](#rem:photon-propagation-1d)) yields an infrared speed of light $c_{\mathrm{phys}}(q) = c_q\,\frac{\ln q}{\sinh(\ln q)}$ ([information speed of light](#def:information-speed-of-light)) with zero infrared dispersion, identically vanishing linear ($n = 1$) slope $\left.\partial_{\mathfrak{E}_\gamma} v_g\right|_0 = 0$, and proved quadratic enclosure
$$
  0
  \;\le\;
  \frac{v_g(\mathfrak{E}_\gamma) - c_{\mathrm{phys}}(q)}{c_{\mathrm{phys}}(q)}
  \;\le\;
  \frac{\cosh 1}{2}\left(\frac{\mathfrak{E}_\gamma}{\mathfrak{E}_*}\ln q\right)^2
  \;\le\;
  \left(\frac{\mathfrak{E}_\gamma}{\mathfrak{E}_*}\ln q\right)^2
  \qquad
  \left(\left|\frac{\mathfrak{E}_\gamma}{\mathfrak{E}_*}\ln q\right| \le 1\right).
$$

**Conjecture.** *Coupling the one-dimensional electromagnetic fibre to the gravitational horizon sector fixes the channel ultraviolet cutoff at or above the Planck energy, $\mathfrak{E}_* \ge E_{\mathrm{Pl}}$. Consequently, throughout the sub-Planckian regime $0 < \mathfrak{E}_\gamma \le E_{\mathrm{Pl}}$, the fractional photon group-velocity variation satisfies the quadratic Planck suppression bound $\frac{\Delta v_g(\mathfrak{E}_\gamma)}{c_{\mathrm{phys}}(q_0)} \le (\ln q_0)^2 (\mathfrak{E}_\gamma / E_{\mathrm{Pl}})^2$ and, a fortiori, satisfies the linear time-of-flight envelope $\frac{\Delta v_g(\mathfrak{E}_\gamma)}{c_{\mathrm{phys}}(q_0)} \le \mathfrak{E}_\gamma / \mathfrak{E}_{\mathrm{QG}}$ for an effective scale $\mathfrak{E}_{\mathrm{QG}} \ge E_{\mathrm{Pl}} / (\ln q_0)^2 \gg E_{\mathrm{Pl}}$.*

Because bar-invariance already eliminates both $O(\hbar_q)$ infrared dispersion and the $n = 1$ linear $\mathfrak{E}_\gamma / \mathfrak{E}_*$ term ([DSR and Planck-scale phenomenology](#rem:dsr-planck-scale-phenomenology)), compatibility with the Fermi-LAT GRB 090510 bound \cite{abdo2009} reduces to deriving $\mathfrak{E}_* \ge E_{\mathrm{Pl}}$ directly from the gravitational sector without inserting the Planck scale by hand.
