The incompleteness horizon of the previous section remains local to a single resolution tree; this section globalises it. The brane tower $A_0 \hookrightarrow A_1 \hookrightarrow A_2 \hookrightarrow \cdots$ stratifies every affine chart by jet order. A horizon forms as a codimension-$1$ locus where the lowest floor $A_0$ vanishes, allowing higher floors to leak through. This section defines these horizons, organises their connected components into aeons, and links the resulting cyclic structure to Penrose's Conformal Cyclic Cosmology.

The [jet horizon](#def:jet-horizon) (the degree-$0$ vanishing locus) establishes the horizon ideal $\mathfrak{h} \subset \Gamma(U, A_0)$, dictating that photons remain blocked at the horizon while degree $\geq 1$ Taylor coefficients escape. A [big bang state](#def:big-bang-state) reframes this degree-$0$ triviality condition as an initial datum, and the [CMB boundary datum](#def:cmb-boundary-datum) isolates the surviving degree-$1$ and degree-$2$ jet data. [Surviving photon color content](#rem:cmb-photon-color-content) interprets these boundary sections as polarization and color modes, while [Hawking radiation as higher-degree leakage](#rem:hawking-radiation) recovers the Bekenstein--Hawking entropy as the rank of $\Gamma(\mathcal{H}, A_1/A_0)$.

Categorical packaging follows. The [observable subcategory](#def:observable-subcategory) $\mathrm{Bun}^{\circ}(\mathbb{P}(V))$ isolates the locus where $A_0$ does not vanish, and an [aeon](#def:aeon) is defined as a connected component of that subcategory. The [horizon functor](#def:horizon-functor) and [CMB functor](#def:cmb-functor) map each aeon to its boundary and surviving jet data. [Aeon recycling](#rem:aeon-boundary-generates-chart) conjectures that $\Phi_1^q \circ \mathrm{CMB}$ lands in $\mathrm{Bun}^{\circ}$, regenerating a chart under one Planck step of the torsion flow. [Conformal invariance at the jet horizon](#prop:horizon-conformal-invariance) and [conformal cyclic structure of aeons](#prop:qou-is-ccc) align this geometry with Penrose's framework, detailed in [the CCC comparison](#rem:penrose-ccc). The next section, **Quantum Geometry Variants**, applies the aeon boundary and degree-$0$ vanishing to model alternative Euler-characteristic scenarios for the descended universe.
