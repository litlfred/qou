The quantum deformation of the commutator relations on Bring's surface is described here in outline. The classical commutator $[e_a, e_b] = \epsilon_{abc}\,e_c$ on $\mathfrak{su}(2)$ is replaced by the $q$-deformed commutator from $U_q(\mathfrak{su}(2))$. Bring's defining equations $\sum x_j = \sum x_j^2 = \sum x_j^3 = 0$ are interpreted as relations on the $q$-symmetric algebra of the aether parameter $q(p)$. This formalism relates the non-commutative geometry of the deformed surface directly to the aether parameter, recovering the classical Bring surface in the limit $q \to 1$.

The details are not developed here; this section fixes only the structural scope of the deformation.
