**Noether invariant.**
The quantum ideal gas law manifests identically as the Ward identity of a scale symmetry of the partition function. Under the $q$-deformed Noether dictionary, the strictly conserved quantity under simultaneous rescaling of $V_q$ and $\beta_q$ is the quantum free energy $F_q$.

One defines the thermodynamic Noether current:
$$
  \mathcal{J}_q^{\mathrm{thermo}} = V_q \,\frac{\partial}{\partial V_q} + \beta_q \,\frac{\partial}{\partial \beta_q}.
$$
The identity $\mathcal{J}_q^{\mathrm{thermo}}(\ln Z_q) = 0$ holds strictly at the equation-of-state surface. The quantum ideal gas law $P_q V_q = N T_q (1 + \cdots)$ is exactly the content of this vanishing. It structurally parallels the classical Euler relation $E = TS - PV + \mu N$.

**Classical limit.**
As $q \to 1$, $T_q = \hbar_q \to 0$. However, $N T_q = N \hbar_q$ remains finite in physical units via $k_B$. The correction vanishes identically as $\hbar_q^2 \to 0$. One recovers the classical ideal gas law $PV = Nk_BT$.
