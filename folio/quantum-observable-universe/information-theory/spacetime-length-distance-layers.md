## Layer I: Dimensionless $\mathbb{Q}(q)$ grain and atomic clock

Every rational-function scale in the algebraic substrate derives from the single primitive $\hbar_q = 1 - q^{-1} = (q-1)/q$, defined over any field $k$ at $q \in k^\times \setminus \{1\}$. That one element supplies the per-step Reeb share of $D_\nabla$ ([Reeb coefficient identity](#prop:reeb-coefficient-is-hbar-q)), the brane-fluid viscous cutoff scale $2\nu_q = \hbar_q$ opposing compression at short wavelengths, the reciprocal full-circuit tick $t_P(q) = \hbar_q^{-1} = q/(q-1)$ whose product with the share is the unit-normalised Reeb circuit $\hbar_q\, t_P(q) = 1$ ([discrete Planck step](#rem:discrete-planck-step); [share versus total](#rem:planck-length-share-vs-total)), and the internal gravitational identity $G_{\mathrm{Planck}} = G(q)(\hbar_q / c_q)^2 = 1$ ([gravitational constant](#def:gravitational-constant)). Retiring the auxiliary glyphs $\ell_P^{\mathrm{step}}$ and $\ell_q$ in favour of $\hbar_q$ keeps one symbol for one rational function across fluid dynamics and information theory.

On the Thomae--Jackson proper-time lattice $\tau_j = \tau_0 q^{2j}$, the ultraviolet partial sum satisfies the algebraic identity

$$
  \sum_{j=0}^{N-1} \bigl(q^{-1}\bigr)^j \;=\; \frac{1 - (q^{-1})^N}{1 - q^{-1}} \;=\; \bigl(1 - (q^{-1})^N\bigr)\, t_P(q)
$$

over any field $k$ with $q \in k^\times \setminus \{1\}$. Imposing a linear order with $q > 1$ (Tier 2) yields $(q^{-1})^N > 0$ and $1 - q^{-1} > 0$, bounding every finite ultraviolet sum strictly below the full-circuit tick $t_P(q) = \hbar_q^{-1}$. Under the atomic-step monoid premise --- that every substrate computation segment is an iterate of one indivisible step --- any two additive clocks on the step monoid differ by a single nonzero scalar, uniting Borel jet time, Thomae--Jackson proper time, and Reeb circuit counts up to scale.

## Layer II: Dimensionless state and 3-manifold moduli

Passing to an ordered archimedean ground field introduces two dimensionless geometric invariants that act on different spaces and carry distinct meanings. First, Leonid V. Kantorovich \cite{kantorovich1942}, Gennady Sh. Rubinstein \cite{rubinstein1957}, Alain Connes \cite{connes1989}, and Marc A. Rieffel \cite{rieffel1999,rieffel2004} define a state-space transport distance $d_L(\varphi,\psi)$ from a Lipschitz seminorm $L$ \cite{dandrea-martinetti2010}. Because non-scalar kernel elements send that supremum to infinity ([degeneracy obstruction](#prop:gram-degeneracy-blocks-connes)), $d_L$ lives on the faithful Temperley--Lieb quotient $TL_n(q) = H_n(q)/\mathrm{rad}$, where $D_\nabla$ weights horizontal gradient transport by $q^{-1}$ and Reeb contact transport by $\hbar_q$ ([Reeb-step-count qualification](#rem:connes-distance-not-reeb-step-count)). Second, the hyperbolic cusp 3-volume $\mathrm{Vol}(S^3 \setminus K)$ is a topological invariant of the knot complement entering the mass operator: the non-hyperbolic Hopf link has $\mathrm{Vol}(\mathrm{Hopf}) = 0$, and equal cusp volumes on the mirror nuclei ${}^3\mathrm{H}$ and ${}^3\mathrm{He}$ alongside distinct charge radii rule out treating $\mathrm{Vol}(S^3 \setminus K)^{1/3}$ as a Euclidean spatial radius.

## Layer III: Dimensionful unit-bridge functors

Physical units enter at the boundary where dimensionless ratios meet empirical standards. The first calibration input ($m_e$) defines the electroweak Compton functor $\lambda_{C,e} = \hbar / (m_e c)$, which converts dimensionless quantities into laboratory lengths and times including the Compton period $t_C$, the pion Yukawa scale $r_\pi$, and the deformed de Broglie wavelength $\lambda_q = q\,\lambda_{\mathrm{dB}}$. A separate empirical chart introduces Newton's constant $G_{\mathrm{Newton}}$ to form the SI Planck length $\ell_{\mathrm{Planck}}^{\mathrm{SI}} = \sqrt{\hbar G_{\mathrm{Newton}}/c^3}$, separated from $\lambda_{C,e}$ by the dimensionless electroweak--Planck ratio outside the three-mass calibration basis.

**Archimedean specialisation.** What follows sits on the archimedean side of the substrate wall: it invokes real- or complex-analytic constructs --- real-valued physical observables, real-analytic bounds, transcendental constants, or numerical evaluation at the substrate parameter $q_0$. Any generic categorical or $q$-deformation identities it draws on remain valid over an abstract coefficient ring; only their archimedean realisation is specialised here. At $q_0$, the $D_\nabla$ Lipschitz split places $q_0^{-1} \approx 90.09\,\%$ weight on horizontal gradient transport and $\hbar_{q_0} \approx 9.91\,\%$ on Reeb transport, with $t_P(q_0) \approx 10.09$, while in SI units the Compton--Planck ratio $\lambda_{C,e} / \ell_{\mathrm{Planck}}^{\mathrm{SI}}$ is $\approx 2.4 \times 10^{22}$.
