**Conjecture ($\mathrm{P} \neq \mathrm{NP}$ in the QOU).** *Given a QOU* with physical non-torsion substrate parameter $q_0 \in \mathbb{G}_m^{\mathrm{nt}}(\mathbb{R})$, $\mathrm{P} \neq \mathrm{NP}$ holds *in that QOU*. This is a **conditional** statement: the QOU axioms are its hypothesis — it is **not** a claim about $\mathrm{P}$ versus $\mathrm{NP}$ in the abstract, and no result here would prove the unconditional Clay problem. Equivalently, the resolution-tree evaluation of the universal obstruction class $\chi(A)$ is non-vanishing:
$$
  \mathrm{res}_{\mathcal{R}}\!\bigl(\chi(A)\bigr)  \neq  0 \qquad\text{at } q = q_0.
$$
The conditioning is explicit: the QOU-scoped separation is derived *from* the QOU faithfulness hypothesis, and a bare $\mathrm{P} \neq \mathrm{NP}$ is never concluded.

**Established complexity landscape (chippable kernel).** The concrete computational object attached to a link $L = \hat{\beta}$ is its Jones invariant $J_L(q) = \mathrm{tr}_M(\rho(\beta))$, and its complexity is a *theorem*, stratified by the arithmetic of $q$:

- **Exact evaluation is $\#\mathrm{P}$-hard** at all but finitely many $q$ (Jaeger--Vertigan--Welsh). The only classically poly-time points are the roots of unity of order $n \mid 6$ — the values $q \in \{1, -1, \pm i, e^{\pm i\pi/3}\}$.
- **Additive approximation is $\mathrm{BQP}$-complete** at a primitive root of unity of order $n \nmid 6$ (Aharonov--Jones--Landau; Freedman--Kitaev--Larsen--Wang).

This stratification is cited fact, not a QOU derivation. Crucially, $\#\mathrm{P}$-hardness of one invariant's exact evaluation **does not** separate $\mathrm{P}$ from $\mathrm{NP}$: it concerns counting, not decision, and no known reduction turns it into a $\mathrm{P} \neq \mathrm{NP}$ proof.

**The $\mathbb{G}_m$-torsion stratification.** Matching the citations above:

| Regime | Value of $q$ | Jones complexity |
|--------|--------------|------------------|
| Classically transparent | torsion, order $n \mid 6$ | poly-time (JVW tractable set) |
| Quantum-hard | torsion, order $n \nmid 6$ | $\mathrm{BQP}$-complete additive approximation (AJL) |
| Counting-hard | non-torsion $q_0$ | $\#\mathrm{P}$-hard exact evaluation (JVW) |

At a torsion root of unity of order $n \mid 6$ the Iwahori--Hecke algebra degenerates just enough that the invariant collapses to a poly-time formula. This — not torsion in general — is the transparent regime. At other roots of unity the algebra supports the AJL quantum algorithm ($\mathrm{BQP}$). At the non-torsion substrate value $q_0$ the algebra is semisimple and exact evaluation is $\#\mathrm{P}$-hard.

**Kummer persistence.** The Kummer filtration yields $\kappa_c^{\mathrm{comp}} \bmod n = 0$ if and only if $n \mid 6$ — precisely the JVW classically-tractable set. Because $q_0$ is strictly non-torsion, no finite Kummer level applies: $q_0$ sits permanently outside the transparent set.

**What is conjectural (the irreducible gap).** The step from the *established* "$\#\mathrm{P}$-hard exact Jones evaluation at $q_0$" to the *asserted* "$\mathrm{P} \neq \mathrm{NP}$" is an **analogy, not a theorem**. Two things are unproven; together they constitute the QOU faithfulness hypothesis, which is open and enters only as a hypothesis, never as an established fact:

1. that the resolution-tree residue $\mathrm{res}_{\mathcal{R}}(\chi(A))$ is a faithful invariant of the $\mathrm{P}$-vs-$\mathrm{NP}$ decision boundary, rather than of the (easier-to-obstruct) counting problem; and
2. that its non-vanishing at $q_0$ is *equivalent* to $\mathrm{P} \neq \mathrm{NP}$ rather than merely *implied by* it.

A concrete non-vanishing of $\chi(A)$ at generic $q_0$ is a Zariski-open (hence generically true, easy) condition; it therefore **cannot** by itself discharge the conjecture without collapsing the claimed equivalence. This genericity is itself proved: a nonzero polynomial obstruction vanishes at only finitely many parameter values, so its non-vanishing locus over an infinite domain is cofinite, hence infinite — stated over a generic integral domain, with no archimedean input. This is the chippable boundary: everything above the line is cited or provable; only the analogy remains open.

**The non-vanishing leg is an axiom consequence modulo the branch-point count.** Two of its three legs come from the axioms: the observability axiom kills $HH^0$, and the Frobenius--Hopf self-duality induces the Yekutieli--Van den Bergh Poincaré duality on Hochschild cohomology so that $HH^2 \cong (HH^1)^\vee \cong k$ ([obstruction reduction](#prf:qou-obstruction-reduction)). The third leg, the Riemann--Roch / branch-point chain that pins the scalar to $-6$ ([the $\chi_{\mathrm{eu}}(M_0) = -6$ chain](#ex:chi-minus-six-derivation)), reads its branch-point count off Bring's surface rather than deriving it, so the value is located within the axiom cone rather than forced by it. With $q_0$ strictly non-torsion, the Kummer filtration then yields $\mathrm{res}_{\mathcal{R}}(\chi(A)) \neq 0$ at $q_0$ — and no step of that chain refers to computational complexity. This sharpens the collapse observation above: were the asserted equivalence a theorem, $\mathrm{P} \neq \mathrm{NP}$ would follow from the QOU axioms by the eight-step chain alone. The conjecture's entire open content therefore lives in items 1--2 (faithfulness of the residue for the decision boundary), not in the non-vanishing.

**Chipping the equivalence.** The conjectured equivalence decomposes into two directions, *soundness* $\mathrm{P} \neq \mathrm{NP} \Rightarrow \mathrm{res} \neq 0$ and *faithfulness* $\mathrm{res} \neq 0 \Rightarrow \mathrm{P} \neq \mathrm{NP}$, taken as separate hypotheses, from which the equivalence is derived. Given the derived non-vanishing, three facts are proved: soundness is automatic and carries no computational content; asserting the equivalence is exactly asserting $\mathrm{P} \neq \mathrm{NP}$; and the Kummer reduction of the derived value vanishes exactly at the Jaeger--Vertigan--Welsh tractable levels, $-6 \bmod n = 0 \iff n \mid 6$, with any real $q > 1$ non-torsion. The single irreducible open item is the faithfulness direction; $\mathrm{P} \neq \mathrm{NP}$ itself enters only as an opaque proposition; no complexity-theoretic model of $\mathrm{P}$ and $\mathrm{NP}$ is developed here.

**The counting–decision gap (companion statement).** Every citable hardness result at $q_0$ concerns *counting*: Jaeger--Vertigan--Welsh give $\#\mathrm{P}$-hardness of exact evaluation, and $\mathrm{NP} \subseteq \mathrm{P}^{\#\mathrm{P}}$ makes $\mathrm{FP} \neq \#\mathrm{P}$ a consequence of $\mathrm{P} \neq \mathrm{NP}$ — the weaker separation. These enter as cited hypotheses, never as established facts. Modulo those hypotheses and the residue--semisimplicity bridge, the reachable companion faithfulness is proved: if $\mathrm{FP} \neq \#\mathrm{P}$, a surviving residue forces exact Jones evaluation at $q_0$ out of $\mathrm{FP}$. Upgrading this to the conjectured $\mathrm{res} \neq 0 \Rightarrow \mathrm{P} \neq \mathrm{NP}$ requires exactly two open inputs, carried as explicit hypotheses: the counting separation $\mathrm{FP} \neq \#\mathrm{P}$ itself, and a counting$\to$decision transfer that no known result supplies. Discharging those two hypotheses is precisely what a proof of this conjecture must add. The bridge — semisimplicity of $H_n(q_0)$ via Wenzl's root-of-unity criterion — is the one front where ordinary (non-millennium) mathematics can still advance the conjecture, and its parameter side is done: the genericity condition "$q^k \neq 1$ for $2 \leq k \leq n$", stated over a generic monoid, is proved for every real $q > 1$ at every rank, and the argument composes end-to-end — for the substrate $q_0 > 1$, given $\mathrm{FP} \neq \#\mathrm{P}$, exact Jones evaluation is not polynomial-time, conditional only on the two cited hypotheses. Notably the obstruction residual drops out of this reachable statement entirely: the counting hardness at $q_0$ rests on parameter genericity alone. Stage 2 advances this on the Schur-element side: semisimplicity is governed by non-vanishing of the Schur elements of the symmetrizing trace, and it is proved — on the concrete Schur element $s_{\lambda,X}(Q) = Q^{n(\lambda)} \prod_x (1 - X Q^{c(x)})/(1 - Q^{h(x)})$ — that in the regime $Q > 1$, $X \leq 0$ every Schur element is nonzero (positive hook lengths make every denominator strictly negative; nonpositive $X$ makes every numerator positive). The Geck--Pfeiffer criterion enters as a cited hypothesis, and semisimplicity follows at every rank with the Schur leg computed rather than assumed. For physical Markov specialisations $X = Q^{-N} > 0$ the numerator factors genuinely vanish at cells of content $N$ — the Temperley--Lieb quotient stratification. That is **no longer a follow-on**: it is proved, and the surviving blocks are characterised combinatorially — at $X = Q^{-N}$ with $N \geq 1$ the Wedderburn blocks that survive are exactly the partitions with at most $N$ columns, $\lambda_1 \leq N$.

**It does not advance the open pair, and should not be cited as if it did.** The substrate provably never lands on the Temperley--Lieb locus: $X \leq 0 \Rightarrow X \neq Q^{-N}$ is proved for every $N$, so the stratification describes a region of parameter space the substrate avoids. The reachable counting statement consumes only semisimplicity at $q_0$, already discharged by Wenzl genericity from $q_0 > 1$; knowing *which* blocks die elsewhere is not one of its inputs, and fewer surviving blocks would mean a smaller algebra — the tractable direction, not the hard one. Nor is it a counting-versus-decision statement, so it cannot supply the transfer. What remains open beyond all citation is unchanged: the pair of open hypotheses of the transfer statement above.

**What the QOU actually proves (the derived result, not the Clay separation).** Where the conjectured $\mathrm{P} \neq \mathrm{NP}$ needs the open counting$\to$decision transfer, a *weaker but genuinely derived* statement needs none of it. The observation trace at $q_0$ is $\#\mathrm{P}$-hard (above); if extracting the degree-0 answer requires the full $3^{3n}$-leaf surviving resolution tree, the Bekenstein bandwidth bound forces any bandwidth-limited device to run super-polynomially — **no bandwidth-limited device decides the boundary problem in polynomial time**. This physical-intractability theorem is proved, as is the exponential-domination fact it rests on. It is strictly weaker than abstract $\mathrm{P} \neq \mathrm{NP}$ — a general Turing machine is not bandwidth-limited — but it is *derived*, not assumed. Combined with Turing-completeness (every $q \neq 1$ substrate is universal) and Gödel incompleteness, it yields the framework's strongest honest claim: a Turing-complete QOU necessarily hosts a non-empty jet horizon where degree-0 truth is inaccessible ([geometry of incompleteness](#thm:geometry-of-incompleteness)). **That existence statement — not the Clay separation — is what the QOU stands behind.**

**The fourth millennium evaluation.** The QOU obstruction reduction *conjectures* that all classical-limit obstructions map to the scalar class $\chi(A) \in HH^2 \cong k$, so that $\mathrm{P}$ vs $\mathrm{NP}$ becomes a fourth restriction of the same class alongside RH, NS, and BSD. The proportionality of the four evaluations is part of the conjecture, not a consequence of it.

**Corollary (conditional).** *If* the obstruction-class conjecture holds, resolving $\mathrm{P}$ vs $\mathrm{NP}$ constrains RH, NS, and BSD and vice versa, their evaluation maps differing but the underlying class remaining identical. The corollary inherits the conjectural status of its hypothesis.

**Parallel route.** This obstruction-class formulation is one of two independent attacks on the same separation. The Reeb--surreal route ([P vs NP in the QOU](#conj:p-vs-np-qou)) instead recovers $\mathrm{P} \neq \mathrm{NP}$ as the standard part of a strict $\mathbf{P} \subsetneq \mathrm{BQP}_q$ inclusion, via the surreal squeeze $\mathrm{ord}_\omega(\text{cost}) > \mathrm{ord}_\omega(\text{budget})$. The two carriers agree on the conclusion but neither is derived from the other; both remain open.

**Shared substrate.** This separation is the *polynomial-rate* leg of the shared length-filtration trichotomy on the Skein substrate $\mathcal{S}(q)$ ([one length filtration, three problems](#rem:skein-filtration-trichotomy)): $\mathrm{P}$ vs $\mathrm{NP}$ asks whether the observed degree-$0$ answer is reachable within a **polynomial** crossing-number budget, rather than the naive $3^c$ resolution leaves — the same filtration that $q$-Collatz asks to **descend** and $q$-Navier--Stokes asks to stay **bounded**. All three are three predicates on one shared object, the Skein observation system, on which the P-side is polynomial observability and the boundary reformulation is [$\mathrm{P} \neq \mathrm{NP}$ as jet horizon opacity](#conj:p-vs-np-boundary).
