The Markov trace defining $\Gamma_{\mathrm{poly}}$ restricts to $\mathcal{R}_{p(c)}$. The poset $\mathcal{R}^{\circ}(L, q)$ forms the computational analogue of the physical [observable subcategory](#def:observable-subcategory) $\mathrm{Bun}^{\circ}(\mathbb{P}(V))$. It collects the nodes of the resolution tree from which the degree-$0$ answer $P_L(q)$ is polynomial-time accessible.
