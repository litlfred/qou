**Definition.**
The **speed of light** at aether parameter $q$ is the ratio of the spatial coupling weight $q^{-1}$ to the temporal coupling weight $\hbar_q = 1 - q^{-1}$ of the $q$-connection ([$q$-connection](#ex:q-connection)):

$$
  c_q  :=  \frac{q^{-1}}{\hbar_q}
  =  \frac{q^{-1}}{1 - q^{-1}}
  =  \frac{1}{q - 1}.
$$

It is the ratio of spatial to temporal information transfer within a light cone ([light cone](#rem:light-cone-affine)) — a $q$-dependent quantity, not a postulated constant set to $1$ by definition. Evaluated as the contact affine coordinate ([contact affine chart](#def:contact-affine-chart)) $Z = \alpha_H / (\iota_\xi\alpha)$ on the $q$-connection, it measures the ratio of horizontal spatial diffusion to vertical quantum spin. This is the Lieb--Robinson velocity of the causal cone: the number of spatial information fibres correlated per unit of Reeb phase advance in one discrete $f_q$-iteration.
