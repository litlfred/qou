Let $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ be a quantum observable universe. Let $\mathcal{S} \to M$ be the state bundle with Reeb bundle $S^3 \to \mathcal{S}_p$ at each brane point $p$. The **quantum volume** of a region $U \subset M$ is defined as
$$
  V_q(U) = \int_U \mathrm{Vol}_{S^3}(\mathcal{S}_p)\; \epsilon_q(p).
$$
Here $\epsilon_q$ is the categorical volume form on $\Lambda^2 V$, uniquely preserved by the rigid duality of $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$.

For a homogeneous gas exhibiting uniform $q$ throughout $U$, all fibres are strictly isometric. This yields
$$
  V_q(U) = |U| \cdot \mathrm{Vol}(S^3_q),
$$
where $|U|$ is the number of brane points in $U$. The term
$$
  \mathrm{Vol}(S^3_q) = \pi^2 \cdot [2]_q = \pi^2(q + q^{-1})
$$
is the explicitly evaluated volume of $S^3$ measured in the $q$-deformed metric. At $q = 1$, this trivially recovers the classical volume $\mathrm{Vol}(S^3) = 2\pi^2$.
