Let $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ be a quantum observable universe, and let $\mathcal{S} \to M$ be the state bundle with Reeb bundle $S^3 \to \mathcal{S}_p$ at each brane point $p$. The **quantum volume** of a region $U \subset M$ is
$$
  V_q(U) = \int_U \mathrm{Vol}_{S^3}(\mathcal{S}_p)\; \epsilon_q(p),
$$
where $\epsilon_q$ is the categorical volume form on $\Lambda^2 V$.
