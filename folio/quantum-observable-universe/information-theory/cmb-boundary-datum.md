Let $\mathcal{S}_0$ be a big bang state in an affine chart $U$, and let $A_0 \hookrightarrow A_1 \hookrightarrow A_2 \hookrightarrow \cdots$ be the associated brane tower. The **cosmic microwave background** (CMB) of $\mathcal{S}_0$ is the boundary datum

$$
  \mathrm{CMB}(\mathcal{S}_0)
  = 
  \bigl(
    \Gamma(U, A_1/A_0)|_{\mathcal{S}_0}, 
    \Gamma(U, A_2/A_0)|_{\mathcal{S}_0}
  \bigr).
$$
