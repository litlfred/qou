The electron shows that crossing energy does not determine energy ([energy](#def:mass-energy)).

**Electron.**
The electron (left trefoil $T_{2,3}$) has $n_c = 3$ positive crossings, so its crossing energy is $\mathfrak{E}_{\mathrm{cross}}(e^-) = 3q \approx 3.330$ Skein units ([crossing energy](#def:crossing-energy)).

Its energy is $\mathfrak{E}(e^-) = \mathfrak{m}_{e^-} \cdot c_q^2$, where $\mathfrak{m}_{e^-}$ is the framing-corrected trace of the trefoil braid class ([mass identity](#def:mass-identity)). The trace is not a crossing count, so $\mathfrak{m}_{e^-}$ is not $3q(q-1)^2$ ([energy is not crossing energy](#rem:mass-energy-not-crossing-energy)).
