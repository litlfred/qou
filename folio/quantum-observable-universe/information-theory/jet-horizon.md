Let $U \subset M_0$ be an open subset of the descended universe carrying an affine chart, and let $A_0 \hookrightarrow A_1 \hookrightarrow A_2 \hookrightarrow \cdots$ be the brane tower.

A **jet horizon** in $U$ is a closed codimension-$1$ subset $\mathcal{H} \subset U$ such that every section of $A_0$ extending continuously across $\mathcal{H}$ vanishes on $\mathcal{H}$:

$$
  \mathcal{H}
  = 
  V\!\bigl(\mathfrak{h}\bigr),
  \qquad
  \mathfrak{h}
  = 
  \bigl\{
    s \in \Gamma(U, A_0)
     : 
    s|_{\mathcal{H}} = 0
  \bigr\}.
$$

The ideal $\mathfrak{h} \subset \Gamma(U, A_0)$ is the **horizon ideal**.
