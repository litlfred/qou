**Proposition (conditional on the [braid-boost inertial equivalence](#conj:braid-boost-inertial-equivalence)).** Part 1 is unconditional; part 2 holds under that conjecture.

*Let $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ be an archimedean quantum observable universe over $\mathbb{R}$ with aether parameter $q > 1$ and causal-cone speed $c_q = q^{-1}/\hbar_q = 1/(q-1)$ ([speed of light](#def:information-speed-of-light)).*

1. *For a relativistic worldline of kinematic inertia $m_{\mathrm{in}} \in \mathbb{R}$ moving at sub-cone velocity $|v| < c_q$ with Lagrangian $L(v) = -m_{\mathrm{in}}\,c_q\sqrt{c_q^2 - v^2}$ and conjugate momentum $p(v) = \partial L/\partial v = m_{\mathrm{in}}\,c_q\,v / \sqrt{c_q^2 - v^2}$, the Legendre transform ([categorical Hamiltonian](#def:categorical-hamiltonian)) gives*
   $$
     E(v)
     = p(v)\,v - L(v)
     = \frac{m_{\mathrm{in}}\,c_q^2}{\sqrt{1 - v^2/c_q^2}},
     \qquad
     E(v)^2 - p(v)^2\,c_q^2 = \bigl(m_{\mathrm{in}}\,c_q^2\bigr)^2,
   $$
   *with rest-frame energy $E(0) = m_{\mathrm{in}}\,c_q^2 = m_{\mathrm{in}}/(q - 1)^2$ and classical Galilean divergence $c_q^2 \to \infty$ as $q \to 1$.*
2. *Conditional on [boost-inertial equivalence](#conj:braid-boost-inertial-equivalence) equating the kinematic worldline inertia $m_{\mathrm{in}}(\beta)$ of a braid class $\beta$ with its static Frobenius--Hopf holonomy mass $\mathfrak{m}(\beta)$ ([mass identity](#def:mass-identity)), the rest energy of $\beta$ is*
   $$
     \mathfrak{E}(\beta)
     = \mathfrak{m}(\beta) \cdot c_q^2
     = \frac{\mathfrak{m}(\beta)}{(q - 1)^2}.
   $$
