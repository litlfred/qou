**Definition.** *Let $(\mathbf{C}, \varTheta, G, \mathcal{S})$ be an archimedean quantum observable universe with aether parameter $q > 1$ and speed of light $c_q = 1/(q-1)$ ([speed of light](#def:information-speed-of-light)). Let $\mathfrak{m}(\beta)$ be the mass of a braid class $\beta$ ([mass identity](#def:mass-identity)). The **energy of a braid class** $\beta$ is*

$$
  \mathfrak{E}(\beta)
  =  \mathfrak{m}(\beta) \cdot c_q^2
  =  \frac{\mathfrak{m}(\beta)}{(q - 1)^2}.
$$

The bare symbol $\mathfrak{E}$ denotes this energy. Other energies carry a subscript, for example the crossing energy $\mathfrak{E}_{\mathrm{cross}}$ ([crossing energy](#def:crossing-energy)).
