Let $(\mathbf{C}, \varTheta, G, \mathcal{S})$ be an archimedean quantum observable universe with substrate parameter $q > 1$ and [speed of light](#def:information-speed-of-light) $c_q = 1/(q-1)$, and let $\mathfrak{m}(\beta)$ be the [mass](#def:mass-identity) of a braid class $\beta$. The **energy** of $\beta$ is

$$
  \mathfrak{E}(\beta)
  =  \mathfrak{m}(\beta) \cdot c_q^2
  =  \frac{\mathfrak{m}(\beta)}{(q - 1)^2}.
$$

The bare symbol $\mathfrak{E}$ denotes this energy; other energies carry a subscript, for example the [crossing energy](#def:crossing-energy) $\mathfrak{E}_{\mathrm{cross}}$.
