The energy $\mathfrak{E} = \mathfrak{m} \cdot c_q^2$ ([energy](#def:mass-energy)) restates the pair-production threshold.

**Pair creation reinterpreted.**
The pair production threshold ([pair creation energy](#rem:pair-creation-energy)) becomes:

$$
  \mathfrak{E}_{\gamma}  \geq 
  (\mathfrak{m}_{e^-} + \mathfrak{m}_{e^+}) \cdot c_q^2
  =  \frac{\mathfrak{m}_{e^-} + \mathfrak{m}_{e^+}}{(q-1)^2}.
$$

The crossing energies are a separate quantity ([energy is not crossing energy](#rem:mass-energy-not-crossing-energy)). The net crossing energy of the pair is $\mathfrak{E}_{\mathrm{cross}}(e^-) + \mathfrak{E}_{\mathrm{cross}}(e^+) = 3(q - q^{-1}) \approx 0.630$, and the absolute crossing energies sum to $3(q + q^{-1}) = 6\cosh(\ln q) \approx 6.033$ Skein units.
