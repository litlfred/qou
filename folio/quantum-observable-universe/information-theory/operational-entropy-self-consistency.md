The operational entropy $\eta_q = (2q-1)/q^3$ ([operational entropy](#def:operational-entropy)) is not an assumed form. Gravitational self-consistency forces it, with no free numerical input.

**Self-consistency.**
The gravitational self-consistency condition $G_{\mathrm{Planck}} = 1$ ([gravitational constant](#def:gravitational-constant)) forces this specific algebraic form. Writing the quadratic-suppression series for the in-progress braid entropy as $S_{\mathrm{cell}} = \eta_q \hbar_q^2 / (1 - \hbar_q^2)$, the condition $G_{\mathrm{Planck}} = 1$ reduces algebraically to $\eta_q = (2q-1)/q^3$.
