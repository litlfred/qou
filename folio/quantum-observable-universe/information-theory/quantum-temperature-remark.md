The Kashaev volume--density reading introduces the substrate density $\rho_q = 1/\hbar_q^{2}$. In these terms, the temperature and its inverse square are
$$
  T_q = \rho_q^{-1/2}, \qquad \beta_q^{2} = \rho_q.
$$
Temperature and substrate density form conjugate square roots. A state at high drag density (small $\hbar_q$) rests at low temperature, while the inverse-temperature squared matches the density itself. This relationship absorbs several dimensional-analysis identities.

The quantum temperature remains the thermodynamic conjugate of the quantum entropy $S_q(U)$:
$$
  \frac{1}{T_q} = \frac{\partial S_q}{\partial \mathfrak{E}_q}\bigg|_V = \frac{1}{\hbar_q}.
$$
