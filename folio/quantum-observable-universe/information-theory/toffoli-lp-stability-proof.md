*Proof.*
The Gram matrix $G$ over $H_3(q)$ has a characteristic polynomial whose eigenvalue crossings are controlled by the discriminant of the Hecke relation $(T - q)(T + q^{-1}) = 0$. The smallest eigenvalue $\lambda_{\min}(\mathrm{G}(q))$ is continuous in $q$. In the computed witness scan, it remains negative throughout the sampled domain — but that scan is the naive-length-rule matrix under the excised convention (see the open note below), so its sign is not yet a statement about the production form. On the **bilinear** pairing $\mathrm{tr}_M(b_i b_j)$ the corrected form does carry one negative eigenvalue, $\lambda_{\min}(q_0) = -0.75270211$ under convention D; on the **star** pairing $\mathrm{tr}_M(b_i b_j^*)$ it is positive semidefinite, $(5,0,1)$. Any sign claim here must name which pairing it means.

Write $T_q$ in the NF basis. The target-flip amplitude $P_{7,6}(q)$ determines the off-diagonal mass component. The diagonal $P_{7,7}(q)$ determines the Wedderburn weight of the standard channel ([Toffoli transfer matrix](#prop:toffoli-transfer-matrix)). Positivity of $G\vec{t}(q)$ therefore reduces to the positivity of the product $P_{7,7}(q) \cdot W_{\mathrm{std}}(q)$ with the appropriate sign from the off-diagonal amplitude.

**Open (2026-08-30) — the reduction's second half is underived.** The identification $P_{7,6}(q) = \Phi(q)$ is withdrawn: $\Phi$ is not in the image of the Toffoli construction under any indexing or convention, and the derived off-diagonal amplitude is
$$
  P_{7,6}(q) \;=\; -\frac{(q-1)(q+1)(q^6+q^4+2q^2+1)}{2q(q^2+1)^2}
$$
([Toffoli transfer matrix](#prop:toffoli-transfer-matrix)). Separately, the reduction above is asserted in a single clause and the part that now carries the weight — *"with the appropriate sign from"* the off-diagonal amplitude — is not derived anywhere in the corpus: nothing states what that sign must be, or why. The first factor is unaffected and survives, $P_{7,7}(q)\,W_{\mathrm{std}}(q) > 0$ for all $q > 0$; the second is open.

Solving $P_{7,7}(q) = 0$ on $(0,\infty)$: the numerator is $q^3(q^4+1)$, whose only real zero is $q = 0$, so the derived diagonal **has no positive zero**. The golden-ratio threshold $q_\star = 1/\varphi^2 = (3-\sqrt5)/2 \approx 0.382$ is the root of the convention-$C$ diagonal $(q + q^{1/2} - 1)/(q+1)$ — obtained from $x^2 + x - 1 = 0$ at $x = q^{1/2}$ — and is a property of the excised frame.

Solving $P_{7,6}(q) = 0$ on $(0,\infty)$: the factor $(q-1)$ gives $q = 1$; the factor $(q+1)$ has no positive root; and $q^6 + q^4 + 2q^2 + 1 > 0$ for every real $q$. So $q = 1$ is the **only** positive zero. The threshold $q_\Phi \approx 0.4656$ was the root of the retired $\Phi$'s cubic factor $(q^{3/2} + q^{1/2} - 1)$, and no longer corresponds to a zero of any amplitude the construction produces.

For $q > 1$: $P_{7,7}(q) > 0$ but $P_{7,6}(q) < 0$, since the sextic factor is positive and the sign is carried by $-(q-1)(q+1)$. The Wedderburn weights $W_{\mathrm{sym}} = W_{\mathrm{alt}} = q^2/(q^4 + 4q^2 + 1)$ and $W_{\mathrm{std}} = (q^2+1)^2/(q^4 + 4q^2 + 1)$ are strictly positive. So the sign pattern on the physical regime is **mixed**, not uniformly positive: the amplitudes of the derived family are not all of the correct sign. Whether feasibility survives a mixed pattern is exactly the open step above; it is not settled here in either direction.

For $0 < q < 1$: $P_{7,6}(q) > 0$. The sign reversal of the off-diagonal amplitude happens at $q = 1$ and nowhere else; in particular there is none at $q_\Phi \approx 0.466$.

The boundedness of shadow prices follows from LP duality ([LP-dual stability](#prop:lp-dual-stability)). The dual norm $|y^*|$ is bounded by the primal norm times the Gram condition number $\kappa(\mathrm{G}(q)) = \lambda_{\max}(G)/|\lambda_{\min}(G)|$. **Open (2026-08-09).** The finiteness step is withdrawn pending regeneration. The witness values previously quoted here, $\kappa(G(q_0)) \approx 3.03$ and $\lambda_{\min}(G(q_0)) \approx -0.956$, belong to a Gram matrix built with the retracted naive length rule $\mathrm{tr}_M(T_w) = z^{\ell(w)}$ under the excised convention C at a superseded $q_0$; they match no convention in the registry. Under the production convention D the form is singular, so the ratio $\lambda_{\max}/|\lambda_{\min}|$ is not a condition number and the duality bound does not close on the full NF span. What the proof still stands on is one positivity, not two: $P_{7,7}(q_0) \approx 0.6912 > 0$ under convention $D$. Its companion is gone — $\Phi$ is retired, and the derived $P_{7,6}(q_0) \approx -0.1438$ is negative. Nor is either positivity convention-independent: the convention-$C$ diagonal vanishes at $q_\star$ while the convention-$D$ diagonal has no positive zero at all, so the two frames disagree about the very sign structure this step invokes. The shadow-price bound awaits the radical-quotient ruling; the amplitude step awaits the reduction's open half. $\square$
