> **Scope caveat.** Only part of this formula is derived. The prefactor $\hbar_q^2 = (1-q^{-1})^2$ is derived as the coefficient of $\Delta_\xi$ in the three-term expansion of the $q$-Laplacian, whereas the eigenvalue of $\Delta_\xi$ on a Wedderburn block is not derived: identifying the Casimir value with $\sum [c][c+1]$ (after Jucys--Murphy and Ram) lacks a derivation showing that the Reeb-spin Laplacian $\Delta_\xi$ acts through a Jucys--Murphy central element. With symmetric quantum integers, $\sum_k [c_k][c_k+1]$ is a Laurent polynomial in the Jucys--Murphy eigenvalues $q^{2c_k}$: explicitly, the central element $C_n = q\sum_k L_k + q^{-1}\sum_k L_k^{-1} - (n+1)(q+q^{-1})$ acts on every Jucys--Murphy weight vector of shape $\lambda$ by $(q-q^{-1})^2\sum_{\square\in\lambda}[c][c+1]$ (conditional on the seminormal form). With Jackson integers $[k]_q = (1-q^k)/(1-q)$ it is not, because $q^{c_k}$ is required rather than $q^{2c_k}$. Moreover, $C_n$ cannot coincide with $\Delta_\xi$: $\Delta_\xi$ is independent of $q$ and hence has a fixed countable spectrum, whereas $\sum[c][c+1]$ varies continuously with $q$ (for instance $(q^2+1)/q$ for $\lambda=(2,2)$), so equality cannot hold across any interval of $q$. Evaluated at the substrate parameter $q_0$, the resulting deuteron correction is $-17.69$ MeV under the bare symmetric-integer sum without $\hbar_q^2$, $-1302.39$ MeV with $\hbar_q^2$ and symmetric integers, and $-1358.06$ MeV under the Jackson-integer formula as written, all opposite in sign to the empirical $+2.2246$ MeV.

*Proof.* Schur's lemma on the irreducible Wedderburn block $M_{d_\lambda(q)}$ forces $\Delta_\xi$ to act by a scalar. Evaluating the $q$-Casimir on a highest-weight vector determines this scalar. For $H_n(q)$, the Casimir value equals the sum of $[c(\square)]_q[c(\square) + 1]_q$ over cells $\square \in \lambda$ (a classical Jucys--Murphy result, $q$-deformed by Ram). Multiplying by $\hbar_q^2 = (1 - q^{-1})^2$ yields $\mu_\lambda(q)$.

The content-Casimir formula decomposes via the Jackson-integer shift identity:
$$[c]_q \cdot [c+1]_q = q \cdot [c]_q^2 + [c]_q,$$
yielding the equivalent form $\mu_\lambda(q) = \hbar_q^2 \bigl(q \sum_{{\square \in \lambda}} [c(\square)]_q^2 + \sum_{{\square \in \lambda}} [c(\square)]_q\bigr)$. The $q \cdot [c]_q^2$ term connects to the bar-symmetrised Jucys--Murphy sum $\Theta_n = \sum_k (L_k + L_k^{-1})$ whose block eigenvalue is $\sum_{c \in \lambda} (q^{2c} + q^{-2c})$, via the per-cell identity $q^{2c} + q^{-2c} = (q^c - q^{-c})^2 + 2$. The linear term $\sum [c]_q$ is the $q$-deformed first content moment, determined by $\lambda$ alone. $\square$
