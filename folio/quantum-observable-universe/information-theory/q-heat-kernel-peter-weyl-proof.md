> **Scope note (2026-11-16).** Corrected from the 2026-11-15 version, which used Jackson integers and the prefactor $\hbar_q^2$. Both changes are derived, not fitted. What is proved: the operator is central. Its Jucys--Murphy elements are relative full twists. Its eigenvalue on a Jucys--Murphy weight vector is $(q-q^{-1})^2\sum[c][c+1]$. The weight vectors exist for generic $q$ by the seminormal form. What is not proved: that $\Delta^2_k$ is the holonomy of the Reeb rotation loop of the cluster of the first $k+1$ points. That identification is Bridge A (the $n$-body base with $\xi$ the Euler field, plus Drinfeld--Kohno). The weight-$1$ argument for the Reeb part is written out below; unlike the statements listed as proved, it has not been independently checked. The binding computation already uses this $\mu_\lambda$, so no number moves. The deuteron term of the unified iterative correction at the proton $q_0$ is still $-17.69$ MeV against AME $+2.2246$ MeV, the wrong sign. That problem is downstream of $\mu_\lambda$ and is not addressed here.

*Proof.* Schur's lemma on the irreducible Wedderburn block forces any central element to act by a scalar. For $\Delta_\xi^{(q)}$ that scalar is computed on a Jucys--Murphy weight vector. There $H_k = L_k$ acts by $q^{2c_k}$, and each summand becomes $(q^{c} - q^{-c})(q^{c+1} - q^{-c-1}) = (q - q^{-1})^2[c][c+1]$. Summing over $k$ gives $\mu_\lambda(q)$. The value $[c][c+1]$ is unchanged under $c \mapsto -c-1$, so each cell contributes the $U_q(\mathfrak{sl}_2)$ Casimir value at spin $c$ or $-c-1$, whichever is non-negative.

The Reeb part carries no $\hbar_q^2$. Let $f$ be a mode with $df = (\xi f)\,\varTheta$. Then $d_q f = q^{-1}(\xi f)\varTheta + \hbar_q(\xi f)\varTheta = df$. For a $1$-form $g\varTheta$, adjointness alone gives $\delta(g\varTheta) = \mathcal{L}_\xi^* g$, and $\iota_\xi(g\varTheta) = g$, so $\delta_q(g\varTheta) = (q^{-1} + \hbar_q)\mathcal{L}_\xi^* g = \delta(g\varTheta)$. Hence $\Delta_q f = \mathcal{L}_\xi^*\mathcal{L}_\xi f$ with weight $1$. On Hecke blocks the Reeb Laplacian is realised by its finite-step form $\Delta_\xi^{(q)}$. $\square$
