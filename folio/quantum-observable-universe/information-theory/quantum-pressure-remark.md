The quantum pressure decomposes into bulk and boundary components:
$$
  P_q = P_q^{(\mathrm{bulk})} + P_q^{(\mathrm{boundary})}.
$$
The bulk term represents the volume derivative of $\ln Z_q$ at fixed boundary conditions. The boundary term arises directly from the $q$-anomaly ($d_q^2 \neq 0$) at $\partial U$:
$$
  P_q^{(\mathrm{boundary})} = \frac{\hbar_q^2}{V_q} \,\mathrm{tr}_{\mathrm{Ad}} \!\oint_{\partial U} \langle d_q K \,,\, d_q K \rangle_A,
$$
where $\langle\,\cdot\,,\,\cdot\,\rangle_A$ is the Frobenius pairing.

**Archimedean specialisation.** The pressure sits on the archimedean side of the substrate wall and invokes real- or complex-analytic constructs. Any categorical identities remain valid over an abstract coefficient ring; only their archimedean realisation is specialised here.
