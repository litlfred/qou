*Let $(\mathbf{C}, \varTheta, G, \mathcal{S})$ be a QOU. Let $U \subset M_0$ be an affine chart admitting a chain of adjacent aeons $\ldots, \mathcal{A}_{-1}, \mathcal{A}_0, \mathcal{A}_1, \ldots$ . This aeon chain satisfies the three defining properties of a conformal cyclic cosmology \cite{penrose2010}:*

1. *(CCC-1) **Conformal invariance at boundaries.** At each aeon boundary $\mathcal{S}_b$, the boundary datum*
$$
  \partial\mathcal{A}|_b = (A_1/A_0, A_2/A_0)|_{\mathcal{S}_b}
$$
*is invariant under rescaling of $A_0$, depending only on the conformal class of the horizon ideal $\mathfrak{h}$ (that is, on the vanishing locus rather than the specific generator).*

2. *(CCC-2) **Boundary data inheritance.** The CMB of aeon $\mathcal{A}_{k+1}$ precisely matches the terminal boundary datum of aeon $\mathcal{A}_k$:*
$$
  \mathrm{CMB}(\mathcal{S}_0^{(k+1)})
  = 
  \partial\mathcal{A}_k\big|_b.
$$
*The degree $\geq 1$ jet data that survives the terminal horizon of one aeon constitutes the seed of the next.*

3. *(CCC-3) **Only conformally invariant data crosses.** At each boundary, the degree-$0$ algebra vanishes ($A_0|_{\mathcal{S}_b} = 0$). By the observability axioms, the Frobenius pairing degenerates on $A_0$, so no degree-$0$ observable can detect a difference across the boundary. The only data that crosses is the degree $\geq 1$ quotient, which is exactly the conformal data.*
