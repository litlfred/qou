The speed of light $c_q$ is the ratio of spatial to temporal information transfer within a [light cone](#rem:light-cone-affine) — a $q$-dependent quantity, not a postulated constant set to $1$ by definition. Evaluated as the [contact affine coordinate](#def:contact-affine-chart) $Z = \alpha_H / (\iota_\xi\alpha)$ on the $q$-connection, it measures the ratio of horizontal spatial diffusion to vertical quantum spin. This is the Lieb--Robinson velocity of the causal cone: the number of spatial information fibres correlated per unit of Reeb phase advance in one discrete $f_q$-iteration.
