One places the quantum observable universe (QOU) within information-theoretic physics. The energy $\mathfrak{E} = \mathfrak{m}\,c_q^2$ ([energy](#def:mass-energy)) and the derived speed of light $c_q = 1/(q-1)$ connect the QOU to established information bounds.

**1. The light cone is the Lieb--Robinson cone.**
The causal boundary arises from the $q$-connection decomposition $A_q = q^{-1}A + \hbar_q\iota_\xi A\varTheta$. The boundary at $|Z| = c_q$ in the contact affine coordinate ([contact affine chart](#def:contact-affine-chart)) is the locus where horizontal spatial diffusion matches vertical Reeb advance. Outside this cone, the Lieb--Robinson bound enforces exponential suppression of correlations. Causality follows directly from the ratio of spatial to temporal information flow.

**2. The speed of light varies with $q$.**
The speed of light $c_q = 1/(q-1)$ depends on the substrate parameter. At the Big Bang ($q \to \infty$), $c \to 0$. As the universe classicalises ($q \to 1$), $c \to \infty$. At the present epoch $q = q_0$, $c \approx 9.093$ in Reeb units.

**3. The Margolus--Levitin bound and $c_q$.**
The Margolus--Levitin theorem bounds the operation rate by $2\mathfrak{E}/\pi\hbar$. One has the energy $\mathfrak{E} = \mathfrak{m}\,c_q^2 = \mathfrak{m}/(q-1)^2$ and the operation count $n_R(q) = 1/\hbar_q$. The bound is:

$$
  n_R(q)  \leq  \frac{2\mathfrak{m}}{\pi\hbar_q(q-1)^2}.
$$

The Reeb dynamics saturates this bound up to the factor $2/\pi$.

**4. Bremermann's limit and information bandwidth.**
Bremermann's limit states a system processes at most $mc^2/h$ bits per second. The bandwidth is:

$$
  \mathfrak{B}(q)
  =  \frac{q\eta_q}{(q-1)^2}
  \quad \text{($q$-bits per Planck time)}.
$$

At $q = q_0$, $\mathcal{B} \approx 82$ $q$-bits per Planck time.

**5. Pawlowski's information causality.**
Information causality restricts the accessible information Bob gains from Alice. In the QOU, the Lieb--Robinson bound ensures exponential decay outside the $|Z| = c_q$ cone.

**6. The Bekenstein bound and fibre entropy.**
The Bekenstein bound limits the entropy to $S \leq 2\pi RE / \hbar c$. One bounds the QOU fibre entropy by $\eta_q$ per operation:

$$
  S_{\mathrm{fibre}}  \leq  \eta_q
  =  \frac{2q - 1}{q^3}.
$$

**7. Summary.**
Every classical information bound above descends from two substrate quantities: the causal cone $|Z| = c_q$ with $c_q = 1/(q-1)$, and the per-operation fibre-entropy ceiling $\eta_q = (2q - 1)/q^3$. The cone fixes the Lieb--Robinson / information-causality light cone and the $q$-dependent speed of light; the entropy ceiling caps the Margolus--Levitin operation rate, the Bremermann bandwidth $\mathfrak{B}(q)$, and the Bekenstein entropy. Causality, processing rate, and entropy are thus one $q$-parametrised family.
