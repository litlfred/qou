**Legendre derivation and conditional boost bridge.**
The relation $\mathfrak{E} = \mathfrak{m} \cdot c_q^2$ ([mass--energy equivalence](#prop:mass-energy)) holds for a braid class only under the [braid-boost inertial equivalence](#conj:braid-boost-inertial-equivalence). Its unconditional part is the rest-frame value of the [categorical Hamiltonian](#def:categorical-hamiltonian) along the causal cone of the Dirac operator. The ratio of horizontal spatial weight $q^{-1}$ to Reeb temporal weight $\hbar_q = 1 - q^{-1}$ fixes the cone speed $c_q = 1/(q - 1)$, and the worldline Legendre transform fixes $E(0) = m_{\mathrm{in}}\,c_q^2$ and $E(v)^2 - p(v)^2 c_q^2 = (m_{\mathrm{in}}\,c_q^2)^2$ unconditionally. Identifying boosted kinematic inertia $m_{\mathrm{in}}(\beta)$ with the static Frobenius--Hopf holonomy mass $\mathfrak{m}(\beta)$ ([mass identity](#def:mass-identity)) enters as an explicit boost-invariance equivalence hypothesis. The resulting rest energy is distinct from the crossing energy ([energy is not crossing energy](#rem:mass-energy-not-crossing-energy)).

**Regime behaviour of $\mathfrak{E} = \mathfrak{m} \cdot c_q^2$.**

| Regime          | $q$            | $c_q^2$         | Physics                                                          |
| --------------- | -------------- | --------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Deep-quantum    | $q \to \infty$ | $\to 0$         | Energy $\to 0$ for any mass                                      |
| Golden threshold | $\varphi^2$    | $\approx 0.382$ | Sub-relativistic: mass dominates energy                          |
| The observed universe | $q_0$     | $\approx 83.2$  | $\mathfrak{E} \approx 83\mathfrak{m}$: large amplification       |
| Near-classical  | $1.01$         | $10{,}000$      | Extreme amplification: tiny mass $\to$ large energy              |
| Classical limit | $q \to 1^+$    | $\to \infty$    | $\mathfrak{E}/\mathfrak{m} \to \infty$: classical $E = mc^2$ with $c$ large |

At $q = q_0$, a braid class with mass $\mathfrak{m}$ has rest energy $\mathfrak{E} = \mathfrak{m}/(q-1)^2 \approx 83\mathfrak{m}$.

**Archimedean realisation.**
Over $\mathbb{C}$, Yekutieli's nonabelian multiplicative integral \cite{yekutieli2015} realises the holonomy mass ([mass over $\mathbb{C}$](#cor:mass-identity-C)), and $\mathfrak{E}$ rescales that norm by $c_q^2 = 1/(q-1)^2$.
