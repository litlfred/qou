**Content of the definition.**
The energy $\mathfrak{E} = \mathfrak{m} \cdot c_q^2$ ([energy](#def:mass-energy)) rescales the mass by the **square of the information speed**. The factor $c_q^2 = 1/(q-1)^2$ is the square of the ratio of spatial coupling to temporal coupling in the $q$-connection. The energy is not the crossing energy ([energy is not crossing energy](#rem:mass-energy-not-crossing-energy)).

**Regime behaviour of $\mathfrak{E} = \mathfrak{m} \cdot c_q^2$.**

| Regime          | $q$            | $c_q^2$         | Physics                                                          |
| --------------- | -------------- | --------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Deep-quantum    | $q \to \infty$ | $\to 0$         | Energy $\to 0$ for any mass                                      |
| Deep quantum    | $\varphi^2$    | $\approx 0.382$ | Sub-relativistic: mass dominates energy                          |
| The observed universe    | $q_0$          | $\approx 83.2$  | $\mathfrak{E} \approx 83\mathfrak{m}$: large amplification                  |
| Near-classical  | $1.01$         | $10{,}000$      | Extreme amplification: tiny mass $\to$ large energy              |
| Classical limit | $q \to 1^+$    | $\to \infty$    | $\mathfrak{E}/\mathfrak{m} \to \infty$: classical $E = mc^2$ with $c$ large |

At $q = q_0$, a braid class with mass $\mathfrak{m}$ has energy $\mathfrak{E} = \mathfrak{m}/(q-1)^2 \approx 83\mathfrak{m}$.

**Archimedean realisation.**
Over $\mathbb{C}$, Yekutieli's nonabelian multiplicative integral \cite{yekutieli2015} realises the mass ([mass over $\mathbb{C}$](#cor:mass-identity-C)), and $\mathfrak{E}$ is that norm divided by $(q-1)^2$.
