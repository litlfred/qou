The hypothesis of [`prop:incompleteness-implies-horizon`](#prop:incompleteness-implies-horizon) carries an implicit **arithmetic non-triviality condition**: the surjectivity of $e$ and the domain $\mathbb{N}$ of $\mathrm{eval}$ require $\mathbb{N}$ to embed into the system. Without this condition the diagonal argument is vacuous — a trivial category lacks self-referential capacity, rendering incompleteness inapplicable.
