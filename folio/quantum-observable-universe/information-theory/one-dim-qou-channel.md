Let $R$ be a commutative ring. A **one-dimensional channel** over $R$ is a bounded braid carrier along a line, conveying a braid word $\beta \in B_n$, whose transmitted datum is the Markov trace $\mathrm{tr}_M(\beta) \in R$; a channel is determined by its trace value. The **cladding** is a topological boundary condition that confines $\beta$ so that no strand crosses the boundary surface. The **vacuum channel** is the empty fibre, with $\beta = \mathbf{1}$ and $\mathrm{tr}_M(\mathbf{1}) = 1$. The **parallel placement** of two side-by-side fibres is

$$
  \mathcal{F}_A \parallel \mathcal{F}_B := \langle\, \mathrm{tr}_M(\mathcal{F}_A)\,\mathrm{tr}_M(\mathcal{F}_B) \,\rangle .
$$
