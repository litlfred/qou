**Definition.** Let $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ be a quantum observable universe with aether parameter $q$, and let $U \subset M$ be a region. The **quantum entropy** of $U$ is the non-abelian surface integral
$$
  S_q(U) = -\,\mathrm{tr}_{\mathrm{Ad}} \!\oint_{\partial U} \omega_{\mathcal{F}} \,\wedge\, *\eta_q.
$$
Here $\mathcal{F} = \mathrm{Ad}(A)$ is the adjoint bundle, $\omega_{\mathcal{F}}$ is the adjoint connection, and $\eta_q = (2q-1)/q^3$ is the operational entropy density. The operator $*$ is the Hodge star on $\partial U$.

The measurability conditions $c_1(\mathcal{F}) = 0$ and $c_2(\mathcal{F}) = 0$ ensure the surface integral is independent of the choice of bounding surface via the Yekutieli non-abelian Stokes theorem.
