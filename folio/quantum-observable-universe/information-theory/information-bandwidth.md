The **information bandwidth** per Planck time is the product of the [speed of light](#def:information-speed-of-light) $c_q$ and the [operational entropy](#def:operational-entropy) per fibre:

$$
  \mathfrak{B}(q)
  = c_q \cdot n_R(q) \cdot \eta_q
  = \frac{\eta_q}{\hbar_q \cdot (q-1)}
  = \frac{\eta_q}{q^{-1}(q-1)^2}
  = \frac{q \cdot \eta_q}{(q-1)^2}.
$$
