**Proposition (classical, $q = 1$; Cardona–Miranda–Peralta-Salas–Presas).**
On a closed Riemannian $3$-manifold, a conformal change of metric maps any non-vanishing steady Euler field to the Reeb vector field $R_\alpha$ of a contact form. There exists such a Reeb field whose first-return map simulates an arbitrary Turing machine. Consequently the trajectory (reachability) problem for its flow is **undecidable**: no algorithm decides whether the orbit through a given point ever enters a given region.

This is a *theorem* — the classical anchor. The categorical Reeb derivation $\xi_q$ recovers exactly this contact Reeb vector field at $q = 1$, which is why the classical result applies verbatim to the substrate's internal clock.
