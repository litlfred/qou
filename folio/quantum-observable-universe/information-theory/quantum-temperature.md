Let $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ be a quantum observable universe with substrate parameter $q$ and Planck time $t_P(q) = q/(q-1) = 1/\hbar_q$. The **quantum temperature** of a region $U \subset M$ is
$$
  T_q(U) = \hbar_q = \frac{q - 1}{q},
$$
and the **inverse temperature** is $\beta_q = 1/T_q = t_P(q)$.
