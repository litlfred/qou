The information-theoretic time inherits its direction from the braiding order $\prec_c$. The arrow of time is not re-derived in the continuous limit.

**Consistency with the arrow of time.**
The braiding-induced ordering $\prec_c$ on the Hecke basis ([ordering–jet compatibility](#prop:ordering-jet-compatibility)) propagates through the jet tower. This defines forward and backward time at every brane level. The information-theoretic time $\mathcal{T}$ is the counting measure on this well-ordering: $\mathcal{T}(a) < \mathcal{T}(b)$ iff $a \prec_c b$. The rescaling $t = \hbar_q \mathcal{T}$ preserves the order, ensuring the Reeb time coordinate inherits the same arrow.
