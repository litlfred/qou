Specialised to the archimedean ground field $k = \mathbb{C}$, the rigid axioms of a quantum observable universe structurally align with several consistency criteria of the **Swampland Program** \cite{vafa2005, palti2019}. This program separates effective field theories admitting a quantum-gravity completion from those that do not.

One records this correspondence as a dictionary, not a derivation. This paper does not model quantum gravity. One makes no claim to settle any Swampland conjecture. Three structures forced by the axioms serve as categorical counterparts to Swampland criteria:
1. The vanishing of the adjoint invariants $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathbf{C}}(\mathbf{1}, \mathrm{Ad}(A)) = 0$ corresponds to the absence of exact global symmetries.
2. The jet horizon ($A_0 \to 0$) at the foot of the brane tower corresponds to the tower of light states of the Distance Conjecture.
3. The rigidity of Bring's surface ($\chi_{\mathrm{eu}}(M_0) = -6$) corresponds to the selection of an isolated consistent point in moduli space.

The electromagnetic coupling $\alpha_{EM}^{-1} = q\,[9]_q\,[10]_q$ supplies a fourth entry, aligned with the Emergence Proposal.

In each case, the QOU realises algebraically, and at a single rigid point, what the Swampland Program states asymptotically across a continuous landscape. The categorical structures furnish concrete instances of Swampland pictures. They do not establish the conjectures themselves.
