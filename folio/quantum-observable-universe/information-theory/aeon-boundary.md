Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an aeon, a connected component of $\mathrm{Bun}^{\circ}(\mathbb{P}(V))$. The **boundary** $\partial\mathcal{A}$ of $\mathcal{A}$ is the set of objects $\mathcal{S} \in \mathrm{Bun}(\mathbb{P}(V))$ that are limits of diagrams in $\mathcal{A}$ but do not themselves lie in $\mathrm{Bun}^{\circ}$:
$$
  \partial\mathcal{A}
  = 
  \overline{\mathcal{A}} \setminus \mathcal{A}^{\circ}
  \subset
  \bigl\{
    \mathcal{S} : \Gamma(\mathcal{S}, A_0) = 0
  \bigr\}.
$$

The **boundary datum** at $\mathcal{S} \in \partial\mathcal{A}$ is the colimit of the degree $\geq 1$ data over all diagrams in $\mathcal{A}$ approaching $\mathcal{S}$.
