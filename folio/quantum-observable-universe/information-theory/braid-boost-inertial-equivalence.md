*Let $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ be an archimedean quantum observable universe over $\mathbb{R}$ with substrate parameter $q > 1$ and causal-cone speed $c_q = 1/(q-1)$ ([speed of light](#def:information-speed-of-light)). For every braid class $\beta$, the kinematic inertia $m_{\mathrm{in}}(\beta)$ that $\beta$ carries as a worldline under a boost along the causal cone equals its Frobenius--Hopf holonomy mass ([mass identity](#def:mass-identity)):*

$$
  m_{\mathrm{in}}(\beta) = \mathfrak{m}(\beta).
$$

The statement has content only once $m_{\mathrm{in}}(\beta)$ is defined without reference to $\mathfrak{m}(\beta)$, through an action of boosts on braid classes. No such action is defined in this work. Two routes are recorded.

1. **Reeb-clock kinetic coefficient.** Write $\hbar_q = 1 - q^{-1}$ and $\iota_\xi\nabla$ for the Reeb contraction of the connection in the [categorical Dirac operator](#def:categorical-dirac-operator). Scaling the Reeb contraction by a clock rate $\omega$ turns the traced [categorical Hamiltonian](#def:categorical-hamiltonian) Lagrangian into $a_2\,\omega^2 + a_1\,\omega + a_0$, and $m_{\mathrm{in}} = 2a_2 = 2\hbar_q^2\,\tau(\iota_\xi\nabla \circ \iota_\xi\nabla)$ is the natural kinetic coefficient. Under non-abelian Stokes descent the trace $\tau(\iota_\xi\nabla \circ \iota_\xi\nabla)$ is the Casimir-weighted, regular trace of the Hecke algebra, not the Markov trace. This candidate fails. In $H_2(q)$ and $H_3(q)$ the ratio $m_{\mathrm{in}}/\mathfrak{m}$ is a non-constant rational function that depends on $\beta$, and at $q = 1$ one has $m_{\mathrm{in}} = 0$ while $\mathfrak{m} = 2^{c(\beta)}$, with $c(\beta)$ the number of components of the closure. Any candidate of the form $\hbar_q^2$ times a trace that is finite at $q = 1$ fails for the same reason.
2. **Boost action on the affine causality cone.** On the [contact affine chart](#def:contact-affine-chart) with homogeneous coordinates $[\omega : u]$ (Reeb and horizontal components), the affine coordinate is $Z = u/\omega$ and the causality cone is $|Z| < c_q$ with null points $Z = \pm c_q$. A binary quadratic form vanishing at both null points is a multiple of $c_q^2\omega^2 - u^2$, so the cone alone fixes a Lorentzian form. The projective maps fixing both null points act on $Z$ as boosts $Z \mapsto (Z + v)/(1 + vZ/c_q^2)$. They compose by relativistic velocity addition and preserve the cone $|Z| < c_q$. Eliminating an einbein from the cone form gives the worldline Lagrangian $-m\,c_q\sqrt{c_q^2 - v^2}$ used in the [mass--energy equivalence](#prop:mass-energy). So the boost group and the worldline are derived from the cone rather than postulated. The parameter $m$ stays free: the conjecture now says that a braid class's traced Lagrangian along a worldline is boost-covariant in this sense, with coefficient $m = \mathfrak{m}(\beta)$. That remains open.

**What a proof needs.** Write the traced square of the Dirac operator $D = \Omega_H\,\nabla + \Omega_R\,\iota_\xi\nabla$ as $A\,\Omega_R^2 + B\,\Omega_R\Omega_H + C\,\Omega_H^2$, with $A = \tau\bigl((\iota_\xi\nabla)^2\bigr)$, $B = \tau(\nabla\,\iota_\xi\nabla + \iota_\xi\nabla\,\nabla)$ and $C = \tau(\nabla^2)$. This form is boost-covariant, that is, proportional to $c_q^2\Omega_R^2 - \Omega_H^2$, exactly when $B = 0$ and $A = -c_q^2\,C$. If $A$, $B$ and $C$ do not depend on $q$, covariance can hold at only one value of $q$ unless $A = C = 0$, since distinct $q > 1$ give distinct $c_q$. The concrete Dirac operator of the substrate (connection $\mathrm{ad}_\Omega$ with a $q$-independent Reeb map) has non-zero $A$, $B$ and $C$, with $C$ twice the discriminant of $\Omega$. For it, covariance would single out one value of $q$ from the substrate data. A proof of the conjecture therefore needs two steps, neither of which is constructed in this work:

1. a Dirac datum attached to each braid class $\beta$ whose traces scale as $A/C = -1/(q-1)^2$ for every $q > 1$, with $B = 0$;
2. the identification of the resulting boost-covariant coefficient with $\mathfrak{m}(\beta)$.

A natural candidate for the first step takes $\nabla$ and $\iota_\xi\nabla$ from the $q$-dependent Hecke representation of $\beta$, where the traces vary with $q$. Whether some such choice satisfies the scaling for every $q$, without selecting the operators to fit it, is open.

If the conjecture holds, the rest energy of a braid class is $\mathfrak{E}(\beta) = \mathfrak{m}(\beta)\,c_q^2$ ([mass--energy equivalence](#prop:mass-energy)).
