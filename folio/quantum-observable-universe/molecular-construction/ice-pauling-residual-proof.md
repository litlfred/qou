**Proof.** A water assigns its two donated hydrogens among its four bonds in $\binom{4}{2}=6$ ways; each of its two bonds is shared with a neighbour and, on the independent estimate, carries exactly one hydrogen with probability $\tfrac12$, giving $W_1 = 6\cdot(\tfrac12)^2 = \tfrac32$ over $\mathbb{Q}$. The entropy $S = Nk\ln W_1 = R\ln\tfrac32$ is the archimedean evaluation. $\qquad\blacksquare$
