Let $\lambda$ be a strand-partition of $N$ and $\mu \vdash N$ a Wedderburn block of $H_N(q)$. The **assembly projector** $e_{\lambda,\mu} \in H_N(q)$ is the canonical idempotent projecting the composite's module onto the irreducible block $\mu$. The distinguished case $\mu = (N)$, projection onto the one-row symmetric block, is called **delocalisation**.
