Iterating functorial column promotion produces a five-layer physical tower $\mathcal{L}_0 \to \mathcal{L}_1 \to \mathcal{L}_2 \to \mathcal{L}_3 \to \mathcal{L}_4$ governed at each step by a single dimensionless adiabatic ratio $\eta_k = \|V_{k+1}\| / \Delta_k$ evaluated from $(m_e, q_0)$ without fitted counterterms:

### Zero-counterterm algebraic renormalization versus continuum effective field theory

In Wilsonian renormalization \cite{wilson1971} and Weinberg chiral nuclear effective field theory \cite{weinberg1990}, integrating out ultraviolet momentum shells generates an infinite tower of non-renormalizable operators whose low-energy constants must be refitted at each scale. Here, passing from $\mathcal{L}_k$ to $\mathcal{L}_{k+1}$ restricts the ambient braid-Hecke state space onto the finite motif subspace via discrete disentangling projections analogous to entanglement renormalization \cite{vidal2007} and polygraphic normal-form coherence \cite{squier1987,lafont2003,guiraud-malbos2012,guiraud-malbos2018}. Whenever $\eta_k < 1$, [Theorem](#thm:adiabatic-confinement-gap-stability) guarantees that intra-motif fission channels remain strictly endothermic without introducing empirical counterterms.

### Cross-layer deconfinement thresholds ($\eta_k \to 1$)

Conversely, part (3) of [Theorem](#thm:adiabatic-confinement-gap-stability) predicts the exact threshold at which extreme thermodynamic potentials reopen a frozen lower layer. Across $\mathcal{L}_3 \to \mathcal{L}_2$, a degenerate relativistic electron sea supplies perturbation $\delta_V = -E_{F,e}$ to inverse beta capture $p + e^- \to n + \nu_e$, flipping $(m_n - m_p) - E_{F,e} < 0$ above the critical electron Fermi energy

$$
  E_{F,e}^* = m_n - m_p = 1.29333\,\mathrm{MeV}
  \qquad
  \bigl(p_{F,e}^* c = 1.18810\,\mathrm{MeV},\;\; n_e^* = 7.3719 \times 10^{30}\,\mathrm{cm}^{-3}\bigr),
$$

marking neutronization in white-dwarf cores and neutron-star outer crusts; at still higher baryon chemical potentials $\mu_B \sim \Delta_1$, $\eta_1 \to 1$ reopens $\mathcal{L}_2 \to \mathcal{L}_1$ nucleon deconfinement.
