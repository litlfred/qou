**Proposition (conditional on [Seven-term atomic mass formula with $q$-integer coefficients](#conj:q-integer-mass-formula)).**

$$
  \min_{\pi \in S_{26}} \; \Phi_1(\mathcal{M}(C, H_1, H_2, H_3, H_4; \pi))
$$

*The $S_4$ symmetry of the optimum means:*
1. *Each C--H bond has the same traced volume $\hat{V}_b$.*
2. *Each H--H repulsion has the same $\Phi_1$ penalty.*
3. *The total binding energy is exactly $4 \times \mathfrak{E}_{\mathrm{bind}}^{CH}$.*

*The LP dual shadow prices:*
- *$y_{CH}$: marginal value of one C--H bond = single-bond dissociation energy*

*The symmetry premium $y_{\mathrm{sym}} > 0$ is the braid-theoretic origin of the **regular tetrahedron**. Breaking $S_4$ symmetry (e.g., compressing one H--C--H angle) costs $\Phi_1$ through increased H--H repulsion.*
