*On the symmetric (trivial) representation of the braid monoid — the one-dimensional Wedderburn block that a molecule's $C_{nv}$ assembly projector selects (the $S_n$-symmetric channel of the [$C_{2v}$ assembly-projector remark](#rem:h2o-c2v-from-projector)) — every generator $\sigma_i$ acts as the scalar $q$. Hence an electron domain represented by a braid word $w$ of length $\ell$ has weight*

$$
  w_{\mathrm{domain}} = q^{\ell}.
$$

*In particular, the lone pair $e^- = \sigma_0\sigma_1\sigma_0$ (length $3$) and the $\sigma$-bond (length $1$) have weights $q^3$ and $q$, so the lone-pair to bond weight ratio is*

$$
  \frac{w_{\mathrm{lone}}}{w_{\mathrm{bond}}} = \frac{q^3}{q} = q^2 .
$$

This ratio follows directly as a ratio of Hecke eigenvalues on the symmetric channel, without central-atom input or fitting. Being a ratio of eigenvalues rather than an angle, it is independent of the repulsion kernel; the weight law $q^{\ell}$ and the identity $q^3 = q^2\cdot q$ hold identically in any commutative ring.
