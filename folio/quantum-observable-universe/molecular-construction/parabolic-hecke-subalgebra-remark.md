The cross-block generators are precisely the crossings created when the sub-units bond. Equivalently $H_\lambda(q)$ is the $q$-deformation of the Young subgroup $S_\lambda \subset S_N$. Its intra-unit generator count is $N - k$. The bonding degrees of freedom number $k-1$. For benzene's $\lambda = (2,2,2)$, there are 3 intra-unit generators and 2 bonding generators linking the three units into a ring, with the third join closing the ring.
