**Water** $H_2O$ is the **molecular braid** assembled from three sub-units — one oxygen $O(8,8)$ ($A+Z = 16+8 = 24$ strands, valence $2$ with two lone pairs) and two hydrogens $H_1, H_2$ ($2$ strands each) — in $H_{28}(q)$, by the [assembly projector](#def:molecular-assembly-projector) $e_{\lambda,\mu}$ over the parabolic subalgebra $H_\lambda(q)$ with [strand-partition](#def:molecular-strand-partition) $\lambda = (24, 2, 2)$.
