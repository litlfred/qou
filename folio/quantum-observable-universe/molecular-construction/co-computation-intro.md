Carbon monoxide is the first heteronuclear test of the template. Carbon monoxide $CO$ as a molecular braid joins the $(6,6)$ carbon braid to the $(8,8)$ oxygen braid with three shared electron pairs on a common strand set. The LP dual selects the triple bond as optimal for $CO$. We extract the $CO$ triple bond energy from the Wedderburn invariants. Heteronuclear bonding requires no new machinery, only the asymmetric strand counts of the two atomic factors.
