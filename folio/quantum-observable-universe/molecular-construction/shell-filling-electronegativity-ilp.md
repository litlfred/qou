Let $E(N)$ denote the optimal ground-state energy of a frozen nuclear core $(Z, N_{\mathrm{nuc}})$ dressed by $N \ge 1$ bound electrons in Layer $\mathcal{L}_3$, with vertical ionization potential $I(N) := E(N-1) - E(N)$ and electron affinity $A_{\mathrm{ea}}(N) := E(N) - E(N+1)$. Because the electron count $N \in \mathbb{N}$ is discrete, the LP dual variable $y_0$ governing marginal electron attachment splits at each integer $N$ into right and left discrete shadow slopes

$$
  \mu_+(N) := E(N+1) - E(N) = -A_{\mathrm{ea}}(N),
  \qquad
  \mu_-(N) := E(N) - E(N-1) = -I(N).
$$

Their negative midpoint recovers Mulliken absolute electronegativity $\chi_{\mathrm{M}}(N)$ \cite{mulliken1934}, while their jump across $N$ recovers the Parr–Pearson chemical hardness discontinuity $\eta_{\mathrm{PP}}(N)$ \cite{perdew-parr-levy-balduz1982,parr-pearson1983}:

$$
  2\,\chi_{\mathrm{M}}(N) = -\bigl(\mu_+(N) + \mu_-(N)\bigr) = I(N) + A_{\mathrm{ea}}(N),
  \qquad
  \eta_{\mathrm{PP}}(N) := I(N) - A_{\mathrm{ea}}(N) = \mu_+(N) - \mu_-(N) = E(N-1) + E(N+1) - 2E(N).
$$

(Parr and Pearson \cite{parr-pearson1983} also write $\frac{1}{2}(I - A_{\mathrm{ea}})$, differing by a conventional factor of $2$.) Consequently, $\eta_{\mathrm{PP}}(N) \ge 0$ holds if and only if the piecewise-linear Perdew–Parr–Levy–Balduz ground-state envelope satisfies discrete convexity $2E(N) \le E(N-1) + E(N+1)$ \cite{perdew-parr-levy-balduz1982}. Strict convexity $\eta_{\mathrm{PP}}(N) > 0$ makes charge disproportionation $2X(N) \to X(N-1) + X(N+1)$ strictly endothermic, with maximal shadow-slope kinks at the cumulative $2n^2$ Madelung period closures

$$
  Z_{\mathrm{noble}} \in \{2,\,10,\,18,\,36,\,54,\,86\}
  \qquad (\mathrm{He},\,\mathrm{Ne},\,\mathrm{Ar},\,\mathrm{Kr},\,\mathrm{Xe},\,\mathrm{Rn}),
$$

generated by period capacities $2\lceil (p+2)/2\rceil^2 \in (2, 8, 8, 18, 18, 32)$.

### ILP electron attachment and Hund generator alternation

Within an open shell, sequential integer-LP attachment selects at each step $N \mapsto N+1$ the crossing operator that minimizes $|\mathrm{tr}_M|$ subject to Frobenius positivity $G \cdot x \ge 0$; see [Table](#tbl:shell-filling-rule-table-data). The attachment sequence alternates generators ($\sigma_0^{-1}$ versus $\sigma_1^{-1}$), realizing Hund's rule in the molecular Hecke algebra: electrons $e_2$ and $e_6$ (and $e_7$ in $\mathrm{N}$ and $\mathrm{O}$) pair via $\sigma_0^{-1}$ rather than $\sigma_1^{-1}$ across the $2s$ and $2p$ subshells.
