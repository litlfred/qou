Assembling molecules first requires determining atomic bonding capacity. The electron shell translates atomic shell structure into the number of trefoils its braid admits. Minimising the dual cost $\Phi_1$ recovers the aufbau principle, yielding the standard valence electron count and the octet rule.

Two refinements sharpen this model. First, the normal-form term count in the quotient atom combinatorially detects closed shells. Second, the palindromicity of the normal-form histogram characterises $s, p, d, f$ orbital types. With these shell capacities fixed, the subsequent sections apply the construction to $H_2$, $CO$, $CO_2$, $CH_4$, and $C_6H_6$.
