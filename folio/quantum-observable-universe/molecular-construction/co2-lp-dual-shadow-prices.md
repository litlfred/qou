The LP dual of the [$CO_2$ LP-dual binding proposition](#prop:co2-lp-dual-binding) has three shadow prices:

- *$y_{\sigma}$: value of each $\sigma$-bond shared pair*
- *$y_{\pi}$: value of each $\pi$-bond shared pair*
- *$y_{\mathrm{sym}}$: value of symmetric bond placement (equal bonds to both oxygens)*

The symmetric double-bond configuration $(k_1, k_2) = (2, 2)$ wins over asymmetric alternatives $(3, 1)$ or $(1, 3)$ because:

- Each double bond contributes 4 attractive crossings.
- The symmetric arrangement minimises $O_1$-$O_2$ repulsion (protons are maximally separated).
- The asymmetric $(3, 1)$ configuration wastes one of oxygen's valence sites and places more electron density on one side, increasing $e$-$e$ repulsion.
