### Stage 1: Constituent atoms

- Carbon: $\mathcal{A}_C \in H_{18}(q)$, valence 4
- Oxygen 1: $\mathcal{A}_{O_1} \in H_{24}(q)$, valence 2
- Oxygen 2: $\mathcal{A}_{O_2} \in H_{24}(q)$, valence 2

### Stage 2: Molecular assembly in $H_{66}(q)$

Total strands: $18 + 24 + 24 = 66$. The two double bonds consume all 4 of carbon's valence electrons (2 per bond) and all 2 valence electrons of each oxygen (2 per bond):

- C=O$_1$ bond (order 2): 1 $\sigma$ pair + 1 $\pi$ pair
- C=O$_2$ bond (order 2): 1 $\sigma$ pair + 1 $\pi$ pair

Carbon's 4 valence electrons are split equally: 2 shared with $O_1$ and 2 shared with $O_2$. There are no lone pairs on carbon.

### Stage 3: Iterative construction

1. Start with $\mathcal{A}_C$ in NF.
2. Assemble $\mathcal{A}_{O_1}$: embed, apply inter-atomic crossings, apply 2 covalent bond operators (4 attractive $\sigma^{-1}$ crossings), reduce to NF.
3. Assemble $\mathcal{A}_{O_2}$: embed, apply inter-atomic crossings (including $O_1$-$O_2$ interactions), apply 2 covalent bond operators, reduce to NF.
