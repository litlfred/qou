Let $\lambda = (n_1,\dots,n_k)$ be a strand-partition with $\sum_i n_i = N$. The **parabolic Hecke subalgebra** is the tensor product

$$
  H_\lambda(q) \;=\; H_{n_1}(q) \otimes \cdots \otimes H_{n_k}(q) \;\hookrightarrow\; H_N(q),
$$

generated by the Hecke generators $\sigma_j$ that act within a single sub-unit block. The omitted generators, those joining two blocks, are the **cross-block generators**.
