Benzene closes the chapter with a genuinely new structural feature. Benzene $C_6H_6$ as a molecular braid arranges six carbons in a ring on twelve strands, with $\pi$-electrons spread across the cycle rather than localised on alternating bonds. The LP dual prefers the threading link $T(2,6)$ over any Kekulé alternation. The $D_6$ symmetry of this threading link expresses the experimental fact that all six $C{-}C$ bonds have equal length. Aromaticity here emerges as a forced output of the LP minimum on an $n$-membered ring rather than an extra postulate.
