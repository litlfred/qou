The $\Phi_1$-minimisation of the [shell-filling rule proposition](#prop:shell-filling-rule) reproduces the aufbau and Hund rules:

1. Inner shells ($n < n_{\mathrm{val}}$) fill completely because each additional electron in a partly filled inner shell reduces $\Phi_1$ more than any valence electron.

2. Within the valence shell, electrons distribute to maximise the number of singly occupied sites (Hund's rule). Parallel electron trefoils have lower $\Phi_1$ than antiparallel pairs, and the $ee$ crossing ($\sigma$, repulsive) is minimised when electrons occupy distinct spatial sites.
