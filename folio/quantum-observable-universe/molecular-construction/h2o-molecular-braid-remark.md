Of the $H_\lambda(q)$ generators, the $N - k = 25$ intra-block generators are internal to the three atoms. The $k - 1 = 2$ cross-block (bonding) generators are the two O–H $\sigma$-bonds. The assembly projector selects the Wedderburn block $\mu$ that pairs each hydrogen's electron with oxygen. The formalisation fixes the combinatorics: total $N = 28$, two bonding generators, and the hydrogen-exchange symmetry of the partition.

Two interpretations follow. First, the molecular point symmetry acts as the stabiliser of $e_{\lambda,\mu}$. Second, the lone pairs of oxygen force a bent geometry.
