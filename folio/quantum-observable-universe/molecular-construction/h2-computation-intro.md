The smallest covalent molecule provides the baseline test. The hydrogen molecule $H_2$ as a braid in $H_4(q)$ places two protons and two electrons on four strands and lets them share a single trefoil. The LP dual selects the optimal $H_2$ binding, determines the bond order, and provides the binding energy for comparison against the spectroscopic value. The covalent channel eigenvalue ratio yields the equilibrium internuclear separation. This sets the template for the heteronuclear and polyatomic cases.
