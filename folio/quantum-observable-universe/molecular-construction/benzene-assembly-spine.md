*Light initiates the assembly. The Breit–Wheeler process [$\gamma\gamma \to e^+e^-$](#rem:breit-wheeler-observation) seeds a lepton pair. That energy feeds [quark–antiquark production](#def:quark-antiquark-production), whose confinement closes into the [Borromean nucleon](#def:f-bor-borromean-observable) and, by [meson exchange](#rem:meson-to-proton-via-borromean), the proton. Twelve nucleons bind into a $^{12}\mathrm{C}$ atom (the $A \ge 3$ binding closure), and carbon's four octet channels assemble molecules.*

*The molecular layer is parameter-free. Each covalent bond order is the cardinality of the saturated orthogonal-channel set — the [bond order $=$ LP active set](#prop:co-lp-dual-binding) identity. The fractional $\pi$ of an aromatic ring is the lateral-$\pi$ overlap of the [embeddable knot operator](#def:embeddable-knot-operator), delocalised by the [assembly projector](#def:molecular-assembly-projector).*

### Validated predictions

*Running the spine end-to-end (witness `benzene-assembly-spine`) reproduces the observed structural bond orders with no fitted constant:*

| species | construction | predicted | observed |
|---|---|---|---|
| acetylene $\mathrm{C_2H_2}$ | $\sigma \perp \pi_x \perp \pi_y$ orthogonal triple | $3$ | $3$ |
| benzene $\mathrm{C_6H_6}$ | $\lambda=(2,2,2)\vdash 6$, $D_6$ ring | $\tfrac{3}{2}$ | $\tfrac{3}{2}$ |
| cyclohexane $\mathrm{C_6H_{12}}$ | saturated control | $1$ | $1$ |

*Acetylene's $\mathrm{C}\!\equiv\!\mathrm{C}$ fills all three orthogonal channels (one axial $\sigma$, two transverse $\pi$). The spatial-dimension bound caps it at three. Benzene's six $\mathrm{C\!-\!C}$ bonds come out identical. The $D_6$ symmetry of the assembly projector forces zero spread, so bond equalisation is a consequence of the construction rather than an assumption. Aromaticity then registers as a $+\tfrac{1}{2}$ bond-order increment per bond over the saturated ring. It consists of one localised $\sigma$ plus a half-share of the delocalised six-electron $\pi$ system.*
