**Definition.**
**Benzene** *$C_6H_6$ is the **molecular braid** of six carbon atoms and six hydrogen atoms, assembled iteratively from pre-computed atomic braids.*

### Strand count

- *6 carbon atoms: $6 \times 18 = 108$ strands*
- *6 hydrogen atoms: $6 \times 2 = 12$ strands*
- *Total: $N_{\mathrm{mol}} = 120$ strands, living in $H_{120}(q)$*

### Bond structure

*Each carbon has valence 4. In benzene:*
- *2 electrons shared with adjacent carbons (C--C bonds)*
- *1 electron shared with hydrogen (C--H bond)*
- *1 electron participates in $\pi$ delocalization*

*This gives:*
- *6 C--C $\sigma$ bonds (single, 1 shared pair each)*
- *6 C--H $\sigma$ bonds (single, 1 shared pair each)*
- *6 $\pi$ electrons delocalized around the ring*

### Iterative construction

1. *Build C$_1$--H$_1$ unit: $\mathcal{A}_{C_1}$ + covalent bond with $H_1$, reduce to NF in $H_{20}(q)$.*
2. *Add C$_2$--H$_2$: embed, apply C$_1$--C$_2$ $\sigma$ bond and C$_2$--H$_2$ bond, reduce in $H_{40}(q)$.*
3. *Continue for C$_3$--H$_3$ through C$_5$--H$_5$, reducing at each step.*
4. *Close the ring: apply C$_6$--H$_6$ and C$_6$--C$_1$ $\sigma$ bond to form the cyclic structure, reduce in $H_{120}(q)$.*
5. *Add $\pi$ electrons: 6 electrons form a threading link $T(2, 6)$ around the ring.*
