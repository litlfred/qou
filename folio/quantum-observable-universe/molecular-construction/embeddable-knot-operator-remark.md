Head-on overlap is maximal for an axial ($\sigma$-type) channel, and lateral overlap is smaller for a transverse ($\pi$-type) channel; this is why $w_\perp < 1$.

An order-$k$ bond is one axial $\sigma$ channel and $k-1$ transverse $\pi$ channels, with total binding $\mathfrak{E}_0\,\bigl(1 + (k-1)\,w_\perp\bigr)$.
