Let $e_1, \ldots, e_k$ be electrons of atom $i$, each a trefoil $T_{2,3} = \sigma_0\sigma_1\sigma_0$ in its local $H_3(q)$. A **covalent bond of order $k$** between atoms $i$ and $j$ is the Hopf linking of these $k$ electron trefoils across the two nuclei. In the molecular braid, the **covalent bond operator** for a bond $b$ of order $k_b$ connecting atoms $i$ and $j$ is

$$
  L_b = \prod_{e=1}^{k_b} \sigma_{g_e(\mathfrak{p}_j)}^{-1} \cdot \sigma_{g_e(\mathfrak{p}_i)}^{-1},
$$

where $g_e(\mathfrak{p}_j)$ is the generator index for the crossing between shared electron $e$ and proton $\mathfrak{p}_j$ of atom $j$.
