**Definition.**
*Let a molecule be assembled from $k$ sub-units carrying strand counts $n_1,\dots,n_k$. The **molecular strand-partition** is the composition*

$$
  \lambda = (n_1,\dots,n_k), \qquad |\lambda| = \sum_{i=1}^{k} n_i = N,
$$

*which records how the $N$ strands of the composite braid in $H_N(q)$ are distributed among the sub-units. Write $\ell(\lambda) = k$ for the number of sub-units.*

The strand-partition is fixed by what is joined to what. For three acetylene units, the carbon strand-partition is $\lambda = (2,2,2)$, with $N = 6$ and $k = 3$.
