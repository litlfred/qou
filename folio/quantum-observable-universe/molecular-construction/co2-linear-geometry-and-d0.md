*The geometric question is whether the LP dual produces the linear geometry O=C=O, or whether a bent geometry yields lower $\Phi_1$.*

*In the braid framework, linearity emerges when the optimal strand ordering places $O_1$ and $O_2$ on opposite sides of $C$. This maximises the distance (number of intervening strands) between the two oxygens' repulsive $\mathfrak{p}$-$\mathfrak{p}$ crossings. The LP dual selects:*

$$
  [\ldots O_1 \ldots, \ldots C \ldots, \ldots O_2 \ldots]
$$

*The interleaved covalent electrons mediate each C=O bond, rather than:*

$$
  [\ldots C \ldots, \ldots O_1 \ldots, \ldots O_2 \ldots]
$$

*which places the two oxygens adjacent (increasing repulsion).*

*The observed $CO_2$ structure is linear with $D_0 \approx 16.56 \;\mathrm{eV}$ (total of both C=O bonds, each $\approx {{ qou.computations.molecular-experimental-refs.data.d0_co2_double_ev | precision: 3 }}\;\mathrm{eV}$).*
