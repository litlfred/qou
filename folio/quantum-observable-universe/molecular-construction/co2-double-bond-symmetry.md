*The LP dual for $CO_2$ provides a categorical derivation of VSEPR (Valence Shell Electron Pair Repulsion) theory. The key correspondence is:*

| VSEPR concept | Braid framework |
|:-------------|:---------------|
| Electron pair repulsion | $ee$ crossing ($\sigma$, $q$-eigenvalue) increases $\Phi_1$ |
| Bond pair vs lone pair | Shared trefoil (2 attractive crossings) vs retained trefoil (1 attractive crossing) |
| Geometric arrangement | Strand ordering in $H_{N}(q)$ |
| Energy minimisation | $\Phi_1$ minimisation via LP dual |

*For $CO_2$:*
- *2 bond pairs (double bonds), 0 lone pairs on C*
- *VSEPR predicts: linear, 180$^\circ$ bond angle*
- *LP dual confirms: placing $O_1$ and $O_2$ on opposite sides of $C$ in the strand ordering minimises the $O$-$O$ proton repulsion crossings*

*For $H_2O$:*
- *2 bond pairs (single bonds), 2 lone pairs on O*
- *VSEPR predicts: bent, $\approx 104.5^\circ$*
- *LP dual should show: lone pairs force the two H's into a non-symmetric strand arrangement*

*The braid framework derives geometric preferences from the Hecke algebra structure rather than postulating repulsion rules. Bond angles emerge as properties of the optimal strand ordering rather than input parameters.*
