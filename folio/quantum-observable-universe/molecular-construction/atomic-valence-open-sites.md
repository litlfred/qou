Let $Z_{\mathrm{val}}$ be the number of valence electrons of an atom of atomic number $Z$ and $n_{\mathrm{val}}$ its outermost shell level. The **valence** of the atom, the number of open bonding sites available for covalent bonds, is

$$
  v(Z) = \min\bigl(Z_{\mathrm{val}},\; 2n_{\mathrm{val}}^2 - Z_{\mathrm{val}}\bigr).
$$

A **lone pair** is an electron pair whose $\Phi_1$ contribution is lower when bound to a single nucleus than when shared.
