*Retrosynthesis — asking which reactions produce a target — is famously non-unique. The forward categorical assembly is not. The [$\gamma\gamma$-spine](#rem:benzene-assembly-spine) has a **canonical** shortest path: the [Hecke normal form](#def:hecke-normal-form) of the assembly braid word. This is the shortest representative modulo the braid relations, selected by the [confinement min-action program](#prop:confinement-lp).*

*The path is unique only up to the molecular symmetry group. Benzene's $D_6$ permutes the six equivalent acetylene insertions, so the canonical word is a single orbit representative, not a single word. This is the assembly form of the Gröbner–Shirshov picture: many syntactic derivations collapse onto one normal form, and the min-action confinement program picks it. This is the categorical replacement for a standard synthetic route.*
