Let $A_{\mathrm{chem}} \in \mathbb{Z}^{E \times M}$ be the elemental stoichiometry matrix of $M$ molecular species over $E$ conserved Layer-$\mathcal{L}_2$ nuclear elements, and let $R$ be any commutative ring \cite{deloera-hemmecke-koeppe2013,sturmfels-thomas1997,craciun-dickenstein-shiu-sturmfels2009}.

1. **Diophantine kernel and lattice closure:** Unsigned reactant and product multiplicities $\nu_-, \nu_+ \in \mathbb{N}^M$ conserve every element ($\sum_{j=1}^M A_{\mathrm{chem},ej}\nu_{-,j} = \sum_{j=1}^M A_{\mathrm{chem},ej}\nu_{+,j}$ for all $e$) if and only if the signed stoichiometric vector $v := \nu_+ - \nu_-$ satisfies $A_{\mathrm{chem}} v = 0$. For all $v_1, v_2 \in \ker_{\mathbb{Z}}(A_{\mathrm{chem}})$ and $a, b \in \mathbb{Z}$, $a v_1 + b v_2 \in \ker_{\mathbb{Z}}(A_{\mathrm{chem}})$, reaction enthalpy $\Delta H(v) := h_f(v)$ satisfies $\Delta H(a v_1 + b v_2) = a\,\Delta H(v_1) + b\,\Delta H(v_2) = \bar{h}_f(y)(a v_1 + b v_2)$ for all elemental reference potentials $y \in R^E$, and every sequence of balanced reactions preserves $A_{\mathrm{chem}} n = b$.
2. **Toric binomial vanishing:** For every balanced reaction $\nu_+ - \nu_- \in \ker_{\mathbb{Z}}(A_{\mathrm{chem}})$ and every elemental fugacity tuple $t \in (R^\times)^E$, the monomial evaluation $x_j(t) := \prod_{e=1}^E t_e^{A_{\mathrm{chem},ej}}$ satisfies
   $$
     x(t)^{\nu_+} - x(t)^{\nu_-} = 0.
   $$
3. **Canonical rank-$1$ primitive circuits:** The integer kernels $\ker_{\mathbb{Z}}(A_{\mathrm{chem}})$ of water synthesis $(\mathrm{H}_2,\mathrm{O}_2,\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{O})$, methane combustion $(\mathrm{CH}_4,\mathrm{O}_2,\mathrm{CO}_2,\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{O})$, Haber–Bosch ammonia synthesis $(\mathrm{N}_2,\mathrm{H}_2,\mathrm{NH}_3)$, and glucose respiration $(\mathrm{C}_6\mathrm{H}_{12}\mathrm{O}_6,\mathrm{O}_2,\mathrm{CO}_2,\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{O})$ are free rank-$1$ $\mathbb{Z}$-modules generated respectively by the primitive circuits $(-2,-1,2)$, $(-1,-2,1,2)$, $(-1,-3,2)$, and $(-1,-6,6,6)$.
4. **Complete $\mathbb{Z}$-basis of the seven-species $\mathrm{C}\text{--}\mathrm{H}\text{--}\mathrm{O}$ network:** On $(\mathrm{C},\mathrm{H}_2,\mathrm{O}_2,\mathrm{CO},\mathrm{CO}_2,\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{O},\mathrm{CH}_4)$, $\ker_{\mathbb{Z}}(A_{\mathrm{CHO}}) \cong \mathbb{Z}^4$ is spanned over $\mathbb{Z}$ by water–gas shift $(0,1,0,-1,1,-1,0)$, Boudouard disproportionation $(1,0,0,-2,1,0,0)$, carbon-monoxide methanation $(0,-3,0,-1,0,1,1)$, and carbon oxidation $(-1,0,-1,0,1,0,0)$.
