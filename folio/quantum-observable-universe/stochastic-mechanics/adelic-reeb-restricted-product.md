*Let $\mathbb{A} = \mathbb{R} \times \prod_p' \mathbb{Q}_p$ be the rational adele ring. For each place $v$ of $\mathbb{Q}$, let $\varepsilon_v \in \mathrm{Der}(A_v)$ be the local Reeb infinitesimal on the aether's local completion $A_v$. Suppose every local $\omega$-order $\ordw_v$ takes values in one linearly ordered value group $\Gamma$, and that for any adelic observable $f = (f_v)_v$, the set of $v$ with $\ordw_v(f_v) \neq 0$ is finite. Then the global $\omega$-order is the finite sum of the local orders,*

$$
  \ordw_{\mathbb{A}}(f) \;=\; \sum_{v} \ordw_v(f_v),
$$

*and this assignment is additive over places and squeeze-coherent.*
