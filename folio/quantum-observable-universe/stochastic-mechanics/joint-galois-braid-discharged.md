The joint-action conjecture ([Joint motivic-Galois $\times$ braid-group action on the bi-filtration $\mathcal{H}_q^{(n, K)}$](#conj:joint-galois-braid-action)) predicted commutativity of the motivic-Galois action $G_{\mathrm{mot}}$ and the braid-group action $B_{n_K}$ on every $\mathcal{H}_q^{(n, K)}$ stratum. We discharge the conjecture in general. The argument is structural: the two actions touch disjoint factors of the bi-filtration's underlying tensor structure.

For every $(n, K)$, the actions $G_{\mathrm{mot}}$ and $B_{n_K}$ commute on $\mathcal{H}_q^{(n, K)}$:

$$
  g_{\mathrm{mot}}(\beta \cdot \alpha) \;=\; \beta \cdot g_{\mathrm{mot}}(\alpha)
  \quad\text{for } \alpha \in \mathcal{H}_q^{(n, K)},\;
  g_{\mathrm{mot}} \in G_{\mathrm{mot}},\;
  \beta \in B_{n_K}.
$$
