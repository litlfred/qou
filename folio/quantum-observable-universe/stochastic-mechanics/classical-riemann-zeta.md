The **classical Riemann zeta function** $\zeta(s)$ is the adelic global limit of the local $q$-geometries: as $q \to 1$, the local zeta functions $Z(\mathcal{B}, q^{-s})$ at each prime fiber assemble into the Euler product

$$
\zeta(s) = \prod_{p \text{ prime}} \frac{1}{1 - p^{-s}}
         = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n^s},
  \qquad \mathrm{Re}(s) > 1.
$$
