**Summary of surreal notation.** The full surreal-number formalism is developed in the [surreal-numbers appendix](#app:surreals). This summary records the aether-level uses of $\omega$ and $\varepsilon$; see the [surreal-notation reference table](#tbl:surreal-notation-cheat-sheet-table-data).

Wherever $\varepsilon$ appears in the aether-level definitions below, it denotes the positive infinitesimal $1/\omega$. Wherever a surreal-bound statement appears, only the standard-part map $\mathrm{st}$ and the asymptotic relation $a \sim b$ are required: the bound asserts that the inequality holds up to a $1 + \varepsilon$ factor, and applying $\mathrm{st}$ recovers the classical statement.
