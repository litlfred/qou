Let $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ be a quantum universe over a Noetherian ring $R$ with substrate parameter $q$ and classical limit functor $\mathcal{L} : \mathbf{C}_q \to \mathbf{C}_1$ ([classical limit functor](#def:classical-limit-functor)). The **classical limit obstruction** of the quantum universe is the Hochschild cohomology group

$$
\mathrm{Obs}(\mathbf{C}, q \to 1)
 := 
HH^2(\mathcal{U}_q(\mathfrak{g}), \mathrm{Ad})
$$

where $\mathcal{U}_q(\mathfrak{g})$ is the Lusztig integral form of the quantized enveloping algebra associated to $A$, and $\mathrm{Ad}$ denotes the adjoint bimodule.
