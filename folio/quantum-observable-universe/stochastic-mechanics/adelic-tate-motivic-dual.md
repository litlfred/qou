**Motivic dual.** The adelic decomposition of the global zeta integral has a geometric dual via the arc space $\mathcal{L}(X)$ and the Kontsevich--Denef--Loeser motivic measure. Motivic integration on $\mathcal{L}(X)$ factorises a class in the Grothendieck ring of varieties as a sum over jet-scheme cylinders.
