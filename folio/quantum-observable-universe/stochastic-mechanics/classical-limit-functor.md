Define the **classical limit** as a :refterm[monoidal functor]{#monoidal-functor} $\mathcal{L} : \mathbf{C}_q \longrightarrow \mathbf{C}_1$ from the :refterm[braided monoidal category]{#braided-monoidal-category} $\mathbf{C}_q$ (at deformation parameter $q$) to the :refterm[symmetric monoidal category]{#symmetric-monoidal-category} $\mathbf{C}_1$ (at $q = 1$). The functor $\mathcal{L}$ acts on the aether algebra by:

1. **Quantum gap collapse**: $\hbar_q = 1 - q^{-1} \to 0$, so that the non-commutativity vanishes.
2. **Differential smoothing**: The Jackson $q$-derivative $d_q f(z)$ converges to the ordinary derivative $\frac{df}{dz}$.
3. **Braiding trivialization**: The braiding $\sigma_{X,Y}$ specializes to the symmetric swap $\tau_{X,Y}$.

The functor $\mathcal{L}$ is exact but not full: quantum information is lost in the limit.
