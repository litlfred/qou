**Remark (the three millennium problems as specializations).** Each millennium problem is a property $\mathcal{P}$ that holds unconditionally at the $q$-scale, with obstruction class $[\mathcal{P}] \in \mathrm{Obs}(\mathbf{C}, q \to 1) \cong k$. All three are specializations of one class — though, as recorded below, sharing a class does not make the three verdicts agree.

All three obstructions are images of the same class $\chi(A) \in HH^2 \cong k$ under different evaluation maps:
- **RH**: evaluate $\chi(A)$ on the spectral localization map.
- **NS**: evaluate $\chi(A)$ on the collision restriction map.
- **BSD**: evaluate $\chi(A)$ on the elliptic restriction map.

**Proportionality is conjectural, not a consequence of one-dimensionality.** One-dimensionality of $HH^2$ does not by itself make these three evaluations proportional; rather, proportionality of the evaluations is part of the conjecture rather than a consequence of one-dimensionality ([$\mathrm{P} \neq \mathrm{NP}$ at non-torsion $q$](#conj:qou-p-neq-np)). One-dimensionality is a property of $HH^2 \cong k$, the *source* — the group the class lives in and the common domain of the three restriction maps. It gives that every class is a scalar multiple of a generator, hence that each restriction's image is determined *within its own target*. It does not make the three images proportional *to each other*: they land in three different groups ($H^2(\mathcal{S}, \mathbb{G}_m)$, $H^3_{\mathrm{loc}}(A, \varTheta)$, and the resolution-tree residue) with no canonical identification between them, so cross-target proportionality is not even well-typed, let alone implied. The three may vanish independently.

**The restrictions demonstrably disagree.** Vanishing is indexed by Kummer level — the obstruction vanishes at level $n$ exactly when $n \mid \chi(A)$ ([QOU obstruction reduction](#thm:qou-obstruction-reduction)(c)). At $\chi(A) = -6$ this makes level $2$ vanish since $2 \mid (-6)$, while the resolution-tree residue is non-zero at the strictly non-torsion $q_0$ because no finite Kummer level applies. A vanishing verdict and a non-vanishing verdict thus arise simultaneously from the same class, refuting any collapse of the three restrictions into a single verdict.

**Corollary (conditional).** *If* the proportionality conjecture holds, then resolving any one of these millennium problems constrains the others, the mechanism being shared and only the evaluation map differing. The corollary inherits the conjectural status of its hypothesis; absent that hypothesis, the shared class transfers nothing between the problems.
