The preceding sections established the finite-field Weil bound and its logarithmic exponent symmetry at fixed prime-power parameters. What remains is the algebraic degeneration $q \to 1$ of the $q$-deformed operators themselves: the limit collapses the quantum Planck parameter $\hbar_q \to 0$, sends the Jackson $q$-derivative toward the ordinary derivative, and specialises the braided monoidal structure to a symmetric one.
