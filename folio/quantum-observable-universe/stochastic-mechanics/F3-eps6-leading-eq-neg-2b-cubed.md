**Exact-arithmetic lemma**: the leading $\varepsilon^6$ coefficient of $(F_3 - 1)$ at $q = 1 + \varepsilon$ equals $-2 \cdot b^3(q=1) = -3\sqrt{3}/4$.

## Statement

For the $F_3 = 1 - 2 b^3 \hbar_q^6$ factor in the [categorical precision ladder](#prop:q-beta-categorical-precision-ladder), the leading $\varepsilon^6$ contribution of $(F_3 - 1)$ at $q = 1+\varepsilon$ is the exact algebraic value:

$$
\boxed{
  -2 \cdot b^3(q=1) \;=\; -2 \cdot \frac{3\sqrt{3}}{8} \;=\; -\frac{3\sqrt{3}}{4}
   \;\approx\; -1.29904 .
}
$$

## Proof

The Hoefsmit off-diagonal coupling is $b = \sqrt{q^4+q^2+1}/(q^2+1)$. At $q = 1$, $b^2 = 3/4$, hence $b = \sqrt{3}/2$. Thus $b^3 = 3\sqrt{3}/8$, and $-2 \cdot b^3 = -3\sqrt{3}/4$.

## Significance

This lemma isolates the purely arithmetic component of the $c_3 = -2$ identification in the [three-fold Markov--Hopf proposition](#prop:q-beta-c3-hopf-three-fold-markov): while the categorical factorisation remains conditional on the three-fold Markov--Hopf extraction hypothesis, its $\varepsilon^6$ arithmetic evaluation holds unconditionally over $\mathbb{R}$.

Combined with the [primitive Markov-axioms remark](#rem:markov-axioms-primitive), two auxiliary hypotheses reduce to unconditional algebraic and arithmetic identities, isolating the substantive open content to the categorical statement that the perturbative $\hbar_q^6$ piece of the $\mathrm{tr}_M$-expansion factors through Hopf-link Jones values.
