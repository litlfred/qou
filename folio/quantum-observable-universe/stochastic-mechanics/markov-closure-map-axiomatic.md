**Axiomatic Markov-closure map**, reducing the open content to two atomic axioms.

As set out in the [exact-arithmetic lemma for $F_3$](#rem:F3-eps6-leading-eq-neg-2b-cubed), the substantive open content of $c_3 = J_{\text{Hopf}}(q=1) = -2$ lives in the 3-fold Markov closure $\to$ Hopf-link substructure identification.

We factorise that content into two atomic axioms:

## The two atomic axioms

1. **Markov-closure of a 2-strand 2-crossing braid is the Hopf link**:
   $$\widehat{\sigma_1^2 \in B_2} \;=\; \text{Hopf link}$$
   This is the Alexander-Markov closure theorem applied to a specific case.

2. **Classical Jones value of the Hopf link**:
   $$J_{\text{Hopf}}(q{=}1) \;=\; -2$$
   Already proved, by direct evaluation.

## Result

Previously this content was a single broad hypothesis. Now it is split into two atomic axioms, each representing a recorded knot-theoretic result:

| atomic axiom | known result | status here |
|---|---|---|
| $\widehat{\sigma_1^2} = $ Hopf link | Alexander-Markov | assumed |
| $J_{\text{Hopf}}(q{=}1) = -2$ | Jones 1985 | proved |

Only the first atom therefore remains assumed; the second is already proved.
