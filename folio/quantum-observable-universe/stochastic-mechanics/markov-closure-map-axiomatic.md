**Axiomatic Markov-closure map**, reducing the open content to two atomic axioms.

As set out in [the exact-arithmetic lemma for the leading $\varepsilon^6$ coefficient of $F_3$](#rem:F3-eps6-leading-eq-neg-2b-cubed), the substantive open content of $c_3 = J_{\text{Hopf}}(q=1) = -2$ lives in the 3-fold Markov closure $\to$ Hopf-link substructure identification.

That content factorises into two atomic axioms:

## The two atomic axioms

1. **Markov-closure of a 2-strand 2-crossing braid is the Hopf link**:
   $$\widehat{\sigma_1^2 \in B_2} \;=\; \text{Hopf link}$$
   This is the Alexander-Markov closure theorem applied to a specific case.

2. **Classical Jones value of the Hopf link**:
   $$J_{\text{Hopf}}(q{=}1) \;=\; -2$$
   Already proved, by direct evaluation.

## Result

The three-fold Markov Hopf extraction was previously a single broad hypothesis. Now, that content is split into two atomic axioms, each representing a recorded knot-theoretic result:

| atomic axiom | known result | status |
|---|---|---|
| $\widehat{\sigma_1^2} =$ Hopf link | Alexander-Markov | assumed |
| $J_{\text{Hopf}}(q{=}1) = -2$ | Jones 1985 | proved |

A full proof therefore targets only the first atom; the second is already closed.
