**Adelic-assembly perspective (preferred reading).** Equivalently, $\mathcal{L}$ is the **archimedean component** of the realization tower $\{\rho_v\}_{v \in \mathrm{Pl}(\mathbb{Q})}$ of the [motivic aether](#def:motivic-substrate). Under this reading, $\mathbf{C}_q$ is the universal motive over $\widehat{\mathcal{M}}$. Each finite place $p$ of $\mathbb{Q}$ ([adelic aether](#def:adelic-substrate)) carries a local realization $\rho_p$ landing in $\mathbb{Q}_p$. The archimedean place $v = \infty$ carries $\rho_\infty = \mathcal{L}$, landing in $\mathbb{R}$. The $q \to 1$ limit is the vanishing of the local Reeb infinitesimal $\varepsilon_\infty$ at the archimedean place. The classical continuum is what remains after that single local $\varepsilon_v$ is collapsed.

**Non-fullness of $\mathcal{L}$: phase decoherence.** The full motivic class $[X] \in \widehat{\mathcal{M}}$ carries the relative $q$-phases between all finite places. The archimedean projection $\rho_\infty$ collapses across all those phases, retaining only the real-valued shadow. That collapse is the loss of quantum information: the classical continuum sees only the archimedean projection of the unglued adelic motives.
