Together, the graded Baer-$*$ structure on $\mathcal{H}_q$, the two-filtration bridge to Goncharov's period filtration, the Dirichlet-series limit on $\operatorname{Re}(s) > 1$ at $q \to 1$, and the adelic Tate squeeze fix the arithmetic consequences of the classical-limit functor. The final section of this chapter pairs that $q \to 1$ degeneration with the Lusztig canonical basis, the $\Phi_q$ / $A_J$ identification, and the dual reverse limit $q \to \infty$.
