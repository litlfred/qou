**Proposition (conditional on [Quantum Langlands duality — $H_n(q)$ ↔ $H_n(q^{-1})$ via Cartan folding (Type $A_{n-1}$ ↔ ${}^L\!A_{n-1}$)](../q-geometric-langlands/quantum-langlands-dual.md) + [Ding–Iohara–Miki master Pentagon as unified ancestor of Macdonald + $q$-dilog Pentagons](../mass-theory/dim-master-pentagon.md)).**

*Let $K$ be a hyperbolic knot or link with quantum A-polynomial $\hat{A}_K \in \mathcal{A}_q$ (the $q$-Weyl algebra over $\mathbb{Z}[q^{\pm 1}]$), defined from the recursion of the colored Jones function \cite[\S2.2]{garoufalidis2004}. The $\Phi_q$ (Faddeev) and $\mathrm{Li}_2^q$ (Rogers) asymptotic regulators appearing in the framework are specific saddle-point regimes of the AJ machinery*

$$
  \hat{A}_K(\hat{L}, \hat{M}; q) \cdot J_N(K; q) \;=\; 0 :
$$

*namely $\Phi_q$ is its root-of-unity saddle ($q = e^{2\pi i / N}$) and $\mathrm{Li}_2^q$ its real saddle ($q \in (0, 1)$). The identification is algebraic over $\mathbb{Z}[q^{\pm 1}]$ on both sides; the two contexts differ only by the choice of archimedean saddle.*

The full AJ-conjecture statement, the significance of the unified context-sorting, and the logical structure of the conditional proposition are recorded in the [details note](#rem:phi-q-aj-machinery-identification-details).

**Sources for the two readings.** The Chern--Simons reading of the root-of-unity saddle comes from Gukov, who identifies the zero locus of the A-polynomial with a Lagrangian submanifold of the $\mathrm{SL}(2,\mathbb{C})$ Chern--Simons phase space \cite[\S3.1]{arxiv-hep-th-0306165v1} and states the generalised volume conjecture: a one-parameter family of colored-Jones asymptotics $\mathrm{Vol}(l,m) + i\,2\pi^2\,\mathrm{CS}(l,m)$ on that zero locus, which contains the volume conjecture at $m = 1$ \cite[\S5.2, eq.~(5.12)]{arxiv-hep-th-0306165v1}; for $4_1$ the source checks the real part \cite[\S5.3]{arxiv-hep-th-0306165v1}. Dimofte and Gukov extend the leading term to the full expansion in $\hbar = i\pi/k$ \cite[\S2.2, eqs.~(2.15)--(2.17)]{dimofte-gukov2010} and treat $\hat A_K$ as the quantisation of the A-polynomial \cite[\S3.2]{dimofte-gukov2010}. Hikami assigns Faddeev's quantum dilogarithm to each ideal tetrahedron of a cusped hyperbolic manifold, shows that the classical limit of the resulting invariant is the Neumann--Zagier potential, and derives the A-polynomial from that potential \cite[Abstract and \S1]{hikami2006}. On the algebraic side, Takata proves that for every twist knot the A-polynomial is recovered from the $q = 1$ specialisation of the recurrences satisfied by the summand of Masbaum's colored-Jones formula \cite[Thm.~2.2]{takata2004}, and relates this to the AJ conjecture, recording a computer-algebra observation of the same for $5_2$ and $6_1$ \cite[\S4]{takata2004}. These sources give the setting of the conditional inputs; none of them discharges those inputs.
