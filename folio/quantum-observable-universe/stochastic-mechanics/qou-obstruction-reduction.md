**Theorem (conditional on the [Wigner axiom](#conj:wigner-axiom)).** Let $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ be a quantum observable universe with gauge group $G = \mathrm{Aut}^{\otimes}(\tau)$ of dimension $d = \dim G$, with archimedean base curve $M_0$, and assume the [Wigner axiom](#conj:wigner-axiom): the classical limit obstruction $\mathrm{Obs}(\mathbf{C}, q \to 1)$ ([classical limit obstruction](#def:classical-limit-obstruction)) is one-dimensional and spanned by a class $w(A)$ whose image under the tower comparison is the Euler class $e(\xi) \in H^2(M_0; \mathbb{Z})$ of the tangent bundle of $M_0$. Then:

**(a)** The obstruction is determined by a single integer, the Euler characteristic of the base:

$$
  \mathrm{Obs}(\mathbf{C}, q \to 1) \cong k, \qquad w(A) \longmapsto \langle e(\xi), [M_0] \rangle = \chi_{\mathrm{eu}}(M_0) = 2 - 2g(M_0).
$$

**(b)** The obstruction vanishes at Kummer level $n$ iff $n \mid \chi_{\mathrm{eu}}(M_0)$.

What the axioms of a quantum observable universe supply towards the Wigner axiom, and what they do not, is recorded in the proof.
