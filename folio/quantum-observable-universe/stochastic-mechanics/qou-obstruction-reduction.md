**Theorem (conditional on [conj:wigner-axiom](#conj:wigner-axiom)).** Let $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ be a quantum observable universe with archimedean base curve $M_0$. Take as hypotheses the following four inputs, which are the content of the [Wigner axiom](#conj:wigner-axiom) for the [classical limit obstruction](#def:classical-limit-obstruction) $\mathrm{Obs}(\mathbf{C}, q \to 1)$:

1. **(twisted duality)** $\dim HH^i(\mathcal{U}_q, \mathrm{Ad}) = \dim HH_{3-i}(\mathcal{U}_q, {}_\nu\mathrm{Ad})$ for $0 \le i \le 3$, where $\nu$ is the Nakayama automorphism;
2. **(twisted rigidity)** $\dim HH_1(\mathcal{U}_q, {}_\nu\mathrm{Ad}) = 1$;
3. **(Kummer vanishing)** the obstruction vanishes at Kummer level $n$ iff $n \mid \kappa_0$, for an integer $\kappa_0$;
4. **(trace)** the trace pairing sends the class $\chi_A$ of $w(A)$ to $\kappa_0$, and the image $\varphi_1$ of the obstruction class equals that trace.

Then:

**(a)** $\dim HH^2 = 1$, and $\varphi_1 = \chi_A$.

**(b)** For every $n \ge 1$, the obstruction vanishes at Kummer level $n$ iff $n \mid \chi_A$.

The identification $\chi_A = \chi_{\mathrm{eu}}(M_0) = 2 - 2g(M_0)$ is not part of this statement; the proof discusses it.
