**Remark (truncation depth for the neutron).** This remark records the truncation depth that [Mechanism 1](#prop:categorical-chain-convergence-mechanisms) gives for free-neutron $\beta$-decay at the substrate value $q_0$.

For free-neutron $\beta$-decay, truncation at $N = 3$ suffices for 1 ppb precision. Physical predictions rely on this finite truncation, not the formal infinite product.
