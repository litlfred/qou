## Proof

*Proof (Lean: `tau_M_asymptotic_rep_theory`).* The factorisation defines $F_\lambda$; the substance lies in the parity and the proved rungs.

**Parity (even in $\hbar_q$).** Markov cyclicity $\mathrm{tr}_M(xy) = \mathrm{tr}_M(yx)$ with the bar involution $q \leftrightarrow q^{-1}$ forces $F_\lambda(\hbar_q) = F_\lambda(-\hbar_q)$. The single-wrap character symmetrises over the two eigenphases of $\rho_\lambda$ into $\mathrm{tr}\,\rho_\lambda(\beta)\cdot\cos\alpha$ ([`prop:hoefsmit-V21-cos-symmetrization`](#prop:hoefsmit-V21-cos-symmetrization), [`prop:q-beta-cos-resummation`](#prop:q-beta-cos-resummation)). Since the cosine function is even, the rung exponents $u_\lambda^k = b_\lambda^k\hbar_q^{2k}$ are even in $\hbar_q$ by construction. The bar-parity alternation of the rung coefficients ($\overline{c_k} = (-1)^k c_k$, with bar-even seed $c_2 = -1$) is the sorry-free `esymm_bar_parity` of [`prop:q-beta-c-k-recursion-from-habiro-q-difference`](#prop:q-beta-c-k-recursion-from-habiro-q-difference). $\square$

**Baseline $c_0 = 0$.** At $q = 1$, we have $\hbar_q = 0$, and $\rho_\lambda(\sigma_i)$ maps to the symmetric-group permutation matrix. Therefore, $\mathrm{tr}_M^{V_\lambda} = \mathrm{Tr}\,\rho_\lambda(\beta)$ and $F_\lambda(0) = 1$, which gives $c_0 = 0$. $\square$

**One-dimensional irreducible representations.** For the trivial representation $V_{(n)}$ and the sign representation $V_{(1^n)}$, $\rho_\lambda(\sigma_i)$ is a scalar. The off-diagonal coupling vanishes ($b_\lambda = 0$, hence $u_\lambda = 0$), and the Markov-symmetrised character equals the bare character. Thus, $F_\lambda \equiv 1$. We document the one-dimensional closed forms in [`prop:hecke-1d-irrep-closed-form-character`](#prop:hecke-1d-irrep-closed-form-character). $\square$

**$N=1$ envelope (coupling $b$), $V_{(2,1)}$.** The first non-trivial irreducible representation is the two-dimensional $V_{(2,1)}$ of $H_3$. Its single $\tilde f_1$ Kashiwara edge carries weight $b_\lambda = b$. This produces the eigenphase shift $-b\,\hbar_q^2/2$, given by the $\tilde f_1$ matrix element in the seminormal basis multiplied by $\hbar_q^2/2$. The shift resums into the $\cos(\alpha - b\hbar_q^2/2)$ envelope. This provides the $N=1$ result of [`prop:hoefsmit-V21-cos-symmetrization`](#prop:hoefsmit-V21-cos-symmetrization). $\square$

**Second rung (production wire), $V_{(2,1)}$: $c_2 = -1$.** The first product rung is $(1 + c_2 u^2)$ with $u = b\hbar_q^2$. We prove the seed $c_2 = -1$ categorically through the bar-even constraint. This provides the factor $(1 - u^2) = (1 - b^2\hbar_q^4)$, matching the second-order correction consumed by the production neutron ($\mathfrak{Q}_\beta = \mathfrak{Q}_\beta^{(N=1)}\cdot(1 - b^2\hbar_q^4)$, `derived_masses.q_beta_kashiwara_n2_mev`). A single coupling $b$ thus drives both the $N=1$ envelope and the proved second rung. $\square$

The proof determines $c_0$, the one-dimensional triviality, the $N=1$ coupling $b$, and the proved seed $c_2 = -1$, alongside the $\hbar_q$-parity. The rungs $c_k^{(\lambda)}$ for $k \geq 3$ are generated by the proved recursion of [`prop:q-beta-c-k-recursion-from-habiro-q-difference`](#prop:q-beta-c-k-recursion-from-habiro-q-difference) once its kernel $R(q,\xi)$ is fixed. That kernel remains open. $\blacksquare$
