The Iwahori–Hecke algebra $H_n(R, q)$ is the unital associative algebra with generators $\sigma_0, \ldots, \sigma_{n-2}$ modulo three defining relations: the quadratic relation (R1), the far-commutation relation (R2), and the braid cubic relation (R3). Its full algebraic realization decomposes into four constructions: a free $R$-module on $B_n$, a multiplication structure, the quotient by the three relations, and the per-block Wedderburn decomposition (the $q$-deformed Schur–Weyl splitting $\sum_\lambda y_\lambda = 1$).

The axiomatic presentation packages the abstract generators, formal signed words, defining relations, and Plancherel completeness as structural hypotheses consumed by downstream trace and character identities.
