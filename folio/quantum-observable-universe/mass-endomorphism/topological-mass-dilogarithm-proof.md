**Sketch.** *Integrating the $q$-deformed expansion flow over the resolved defect $E_i$ in the spectral coordinates of the Briggs--Klein chart should give $\int_0^{z_i} \ln_q(1+t)/t\, dt$. The missing step is the evaluation of the non-abelian multiplicative integral in that chart. The second equality, $\int_0^{z} \ln_q(1+t)/t\, dt = -\mathrm{Li}_2^q(-z)$, is termwise integration of the series for $\ln_q$ (see the proof of the mass-endomorphism tower).*
