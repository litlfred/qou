Let $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ be a quantum observable universe ([quantum observable universe](#def:quantum-observable-universe)). Let $q$ denote the substrate parameter. Let $\varrho \colon \mathbf{1} \to A$ be a morphism (a "closed cycle" in $A$, [mass identity](#def:mass-identity)) whose categorical support is generated by $N$ consecutive extensions along the brane tower $\{A_n\}_{n \geq 0}$ ([recursive brane tower](#def:recursive-brane-tower)), applied to a base-level morphism $\varrho_0 \colon \mathbf{1} \to A_0$.

The mass $\mathfrak{m}(\varrho)$ ([mass identity](#def:mass-identity)) is conjectured to decompose exactly into descended and transient contributions via the :refterm[descent]{#descent} involution $\hat{\tau}_n$ ([:refterm[descent]{#descent} involution](#def:descent-involution)):

```tex
\begin{equation}\label{eq:topological-mass-decomp}
  \mathfrak{m}(\varrho)
  = 
  \bigl\lVert
  \mathrm{tr}_{\mathrm{desc}}(\varrho)
  \bigr\rVert
  + 
  \sum_{i=1}^{N}
  \bigl\lVert
  \mathrm{tr}_{\mathrm{trans}}(\varrho, p_i)
  \bigr\rVert,
\end{equation}
```

where:

1. $\mathrm{tr}_{\mathrm{desc}}(\varrho)$ evaluates the categorical trace of $(\int_A \otimes \mathrm{id}) \circ \Delta \circ \varrho$ restricted to the descended subalgebra $\mathcal{A}^{\mathrm{desc}} = \mathrm{im}\hat{\tau}_n$ ([:refterm[descent]{#descent} eigenspaces](#prop:descent-eigenspaces)). Since $\mathcal{A}^{\mathrm{desc}} \cong \mathrm{End}_{\mathbf{C}}(A_n)$ via the ascent embedding $\ell_n$, this equals the mass of the base-level cycle $\varrho_0$. It captures the baseline drag of the Frobenius--Hopf structure along the trivial core.
2. Each $\mathrm{tr}_{\mathrm{trans}}(\varrho, p_i)$ evaluates the categorical trace restricted to the component of the transient complement $\mathcal{A}^{\mathrm{trans}} = \ker\hat{\tau}_n$ localised at the $i$-th blow-up centre $p_i$ ([brane boundary](#def:brane-boundary)). The localisation remains well-defined because the kernels of $\mathfrak{d}_{n+1}$ at distinct blow-up centres form disjoint submodules of $\mathrm{End}_{\mathbf{C}}(A_{n+1})$.

The idempotent splitting $\mathrm{End}_{\mathbf{C}}(A_{n+1}) = \mathcal{A}^{\mathrm{desc}} \oplus \mathcal{A}^{\mathrm{trans}}$ ([:refterm[descent]{#descent} eigenspaces](#prop:descent-eigenspaces)) and the linearity of the categorical trace split the trace value additively, before norms are taken. The triangle inequality then gives the upper bound $\mathfrak{m}(\varrho) \le \lVert \mathrm{tr}_{\mathrm{desc}}(\varrho) \rVert + \sum_{i=1}^{N} \lVert \mathrm{tr}_{\mathrm{trans}}(\varrho, p_i) \rVert$ ([topological mass upper bound](#prop:topological-mass-upper-bound)). The conjecture is that equality holds. Equality does not follow from orthogonality of the splitting with respect to the Frobenius norm. Orthogonality gives $\lVert \alpha \rVert^2 = \lVert \hat{\tau}_n(\alpha) \rVert^2 + \lVert \alpha - \hat{\tau}_n(\alpha) \rVert^2$, an additivity of squared norms, not of norms.

For the archimedean form of the bound---where the descended trace yields the holonomy $\mathrm{Hol}_{\nabla_A}$ and each transient trace yields the quantum dilogarithm $\Phi_q(z_i)$---see [archimedean proof](#prf:topological-mass).

**Archimedean specialisation.** The categorical mass $\mathrm{categoricalMass} : R \to \mathbb{R}$ forms the post-$\tau$ archimedean realisation of the generic mass decomposition over a normed ground ring. It constitutes an $R\to\mathbb{R}$ norm evaluation at $q_0$ and is intrinsically wall-crossing.
