The mass $\mathfrak{m}(\varrho)$ of a vortex in any quantum observable universe ([mass identity](#def:mass-identity)) decomposes as a sum of a descended holonomy contribution and finitely many transient contributions localised at the exceptional divisors of the brane boundary ([brane boundary](#def:brane-boundary)):

$$
  \mathfrak{m}(\varrho)
  = 
  \bigl\lVert\mathrm{Hol}_{\nabla_A}(\varrho_0;\mathfrak{G})\bigr\rVert
  + 
  \sum_{i=1}^{N}
  \bigl\lVert\Phi_q(z_i)\bigr\rVert,
$$

where $\Phi_q(z_i)$ gives the quantum dilogarithm ([quantum dilogarithm](#cor:quantum-dilogarithm)) evaluated at the $i$-th blow-up centre $z_i$, and $\varrho_0$ forms the unknot core of $\varrho$.

## Relationship to the proved theorem

The analogous decomposition for morphisms supported along the recursive brane tower ([recursive brane tower](#def:recursive-brane-tower)) is established as [Topological Decomposition of Mass](#thm:topological-mass). That theorem uses the :refterm[descent]{#descent} involution $\hat\tau_n$ and Frobenius categorical traces to split $\mathfrak{m}(\varrho)$ into descended and transient parts.

The present conjecture concerns the vortex case: $\varrho$ forms a closed cycle whose support does not necessarily factor through a finite brane-tower extension. The holonomy $\mathrm{Hol}_{\nabla_A}$ along $\varrho_0$ (the flat gauge-field holonomy of the base-level connection) replaces the :refterm[descent]{#descent}-trace part. The quantum-dilogarithm sum captures the transient blow-up contributions at the exceptional divisors of the brane boundary $\partial A$.
