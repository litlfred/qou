## A single trace object

The embeddable strand counts dictate a single trace algebra $H_2(q) \oplus H_3(q)$ carrying $2+3=5$ irreducible representations ([Cohomological embedding](#prop:cohomological-embedding)). These match Bring's five homogeneous coordinates, partitioned into a rank-two spacetime block and a rank-three color block ([`brings-qou-4tuple`](../brings-surface/brings-qou-4tuple.md)). We record the color content of an embedded photon as an element of $H_3(q)$, where the generators $\sigma_0, \sigma_1$ serve as the color axes ([`knot-basis-color-content`](../brings-surface/knot-basis-color-content.md)). Color therefore provides a grading on the trace algebra itself, rather than a separate object.

The substrate symmetry $S_5$ acts on all five coordinates. Its block-diagonal subgroup $S_2 \times S_3$ permutes spacetime and color separately. The $S_3$ permutes the three color sheets while preserving spacetime ([`color-sector-coords`](../brings-surface/color-sector-coords.md), [Chirality and trefoil subgroups](#rem:chirality-trefoil)). The $S_2$ permutes the two spacetime coordinates while preserving color. Parts (1) and (2) formalize this. The product embeds faithfully, and each factor fixes the opposing sector pointwise. Color and spacetime function as independent coordinates on a single object. They couple only through $S_5$ elements outside $S_2 \times S_3$, which rotate spacetime indices into color indices.

## Discreteness

Part (3) enforces rigidity on the continuous sector. The connected group $\mathbb{R}$ is divisible, so any homomorphism to a finite group $G$ vanishes. For each $x$, $f(x) = f\big(|G| \cdot \tfrac{x}{|G|}\big) = |G| \cdot f(\tfrac{x}{|G|}) = 0$, because $|G| \cdot g = 0$ for every $g \in G$. A continuous winding parameter cannot carry a finite-group (e.g. $\mu_5$) character. Physically, this dictates that the continuous trace phase does not constitute the discrete color charge ([Mass is the modulus](#rem:mass-modulus-em-phase-color-grading)).
