In the archimedean realisation, the transient trace contribution $\mathrm{tr}_{\mathrm{trans}}(\varrho, p_i)$ at the blow-up centre $p_i$, as defined in the [topological mass upper bound](#prop:topological-mass-upper-bound), evaluates to the quantum dilogarithm $\Phi_q(z_i)$. Here, $z_i$ denotes the exact spectral coordinate of $p_i$ within the Briggs–Klein chart ([Briggs–Klein chart](#def:briggs-klein-chart)).
