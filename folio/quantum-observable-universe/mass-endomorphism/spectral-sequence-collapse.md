*Let $R$ be a field and $q \in R$ such that $A = H_n(R, q)$ is semisimple and separable over $R$. Then Hochschild cohomology vanishes in positive degrees, $\mathrm{HH}^k(A,\, A) = 0$ for $k \geq 1$. Hence the limit page of the GB filtration spectral sequence is concentrated in total degree $0$, with $E_\infty^{0,0} = \mathrm{HH}^0(A, A) = Z(A)$.*

*This does not say the sequence collapses at $E_1$: a zero abutment in positive degree is compatible with non-zero $E_1$ terms that are cancelled by differentials.*
