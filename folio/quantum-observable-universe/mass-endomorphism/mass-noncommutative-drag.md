When the aether parameter satisfies $q(p) \neq 1$ everywhere in the recursive brane tower, the comultiplication on the Frobenius--Hopf object $A$ ([quantum universe](#def:quantum-universe)) is non-cocommutative. Consequently, the algebra $\mathcal{A}_\infty$ ([direct limit algebra](#def:direct-limit-algebra)) does not reduce to a commutative direct limit. Moving a vortex through $J^1 \mathcal{S}_n$ then requires a continuous rearrangement of the $\mathrm{SU}_q(2)$ fibre data. This rearrangement generates the inertial resistance described in [mass geometric drag](#rem:mass-geometric-drag).
