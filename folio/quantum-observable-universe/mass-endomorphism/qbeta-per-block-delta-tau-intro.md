Applying the per-irrep character closed forms to the neutron–proton normal-form difference $\Delta_{T_\beta} = \mathcal{B}_{\mathfrak{n}} - \mathcal{B}_{\mathfrak{p}} \in H_3(q)$ isolates the algebraic structure of the $\beta$-decay trace increment $\Delta\mathrm{tr}_M$. Each irreducible character $\chi_\lambda(\Delta_{T_\beta})(q)$ vanishes at the classical symmetric point $q = 1$ ([per-block character closed forms](#prop:qbeta-per-block-chi-closed-forms)), so under any normalized family of Markov weights the weighted sum $\Delta\mathrm{tr}_M(q) = \sum_{\lambda \vdash 3} y_\lambda(q)\,\chi_\lambda(\Delta_{T_\beta})(q)$ factors through $(q^2 - 1)$ ([canonical $(q^2-1)$ factor of the Markov trace difference](#prop:qbeta-delta-tau-canonical-conditional-q2-minus-1-factor)). This section proves the $(q^2-1)$ factorization, records the Taylor expansion of $\Delta\mathrm{tr}_M$ around $q = 1$, and establishes the canonical Convention-D weight instance $y_{(3)} = [4]_q/[2]_q^3$, $y_{(2,1)} = 1/[2]_q^2$, $y_{(1^3)} = 0$.
