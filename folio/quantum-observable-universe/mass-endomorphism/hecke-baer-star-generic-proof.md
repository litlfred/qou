### Proof

By the canonical Wedderburn decomposition for $H_n(q)$ at generic $q$ ([canonical Wedderburn weight](#prop:wedderburn-weight-canonical), [Hoefsmit seminormal form](#prop:hoefsmit-seminormal-form)):

$$
  H_n(q) \;\cong\; \bigoplus_{\lambda \vdash n} M_{d_\lambda}(R),
$$

where $d_\lambda$ is the dimension of the $\lambda$-isotypic component ([Hoefsmit seminormal form](#prop:hoefsmit-seminormal-form)). Let $e_\lambda \in Z(H_n(q))$ be the central primitive idempotent projecting onto the block $M_{d_\lambda}(R)$.

**Step 1 (idempotents are bar-stable).** The bar involution preserves the Wedderburn block decomposition. The eigenvalues $q$ and $-q^{-1}$ of $\sigma_i$ are swapped by $* \colon q \mapsto q^{-1}$. This permutes the symmetric and antisymmetric eigenchannels but preserves their joint span. Hence $*(e_\lambda) = e_{\lambda'}$ where $\lambda'$ is the conjugate partition. For self-conjugate $\lambda = \lambda'$ (e.g. $(2,2)$ for $n = 4$), $e_\lambda$ is itself bar-fixed. For chiral pairs $(\lambda, \lambda')$, the sum $e_\lambda + e_{\lambda'}$ is bar-fixed.

**Step 2 (annihilators are projection-generated).** Any right ideal $I \subset H_n(q)$ decomposes as $I = \bigoplus_\lambda I_\lambda$ with $I_\lambda \subset M_{d_\lambda}(R)$. For each $\lambda$, $I_\lambda$ is a right ideal in the matrix algebra $M_{d_\lambda}(R)$. Thus $I_\lambda = p_\lambda M_{d_\lambda}(R)$ for some idempotent $p_\lambda \in M_{d_\lambda}(R)$. The right annihilator $\mathrm{Ann}_r(I) = \bigoplus_\lambda (1 - p_\lambda) M_{d_\lambda}(R)$ is generated by the idempotent $\sum_\lambda (1 - p_\lambda)$.

**Step 3 (idempotent self-adjointness).** Each block $M_{d_\lambda}(R)$ inherits a *-structure from $H_n(q)$. Within $M_{d_\lambda}(R)$, every idempotent is similar (over algebraically closed $R$) to a self-adjoint idempotent with respect to a chosen Hermitian form. The Hoefsmit basis is bar-orthogonal in the Frobenius pairing ([Frobenius Gram matrix](#prop:frobenius-gram-matrix)). Thus $p_\lambda$ can be chosen to be self-adjoint.

Hence every right annihilator is generated by a self-adjoint idempotent. The left annihilator case is symmetric. $\square$
