*Let $\alpha = (\nabla_A q, L \wedge F^+)$ form the integrand of the mass identity for a vortex $\varrho$. Let $\hat{\tau}_n$ be the :refterm[descent]{#descent} involution. Suppose the multiplicative integral factors over the splitting $\mathcal{A}_{n+1}^{\mathrm{desc}} \oplus \mathcal{A}_{n+1}^{\mathrm{trans}}$, so that $\mathrm{MI}\!\int_\Sigma \alpha = \mathrm{MI}\!\int_\Sigma \hat{\tau}_n(\alpha) \cdot \mathrm{MI}\!\int_\Sigma (\alpha - \hat{\tau}_n(\alpha))$. Then:*

$$
  \mathfrak{m}(\varrho)
  \le
  \bigl\lVert
  \mathrm{MI}\!\!\int_\Sigma \hat{\tau}_n(\alpha)
  \bigr\rVert +
  \bigl\lVert
  \mathrm{MI}\!\!\int_\Sigma
  \bigl(\alpha - \hat{\tau}_n(\alpha)\bigr)
  \bigr\rVert.
$$

Equality is not asserted. Killing-form orthogonality of the two parts does not supply it: orthogonality makes squared norms add, and two non-zero orthogonal vectors satisfy the triangle inequality strictly. Equality holds exactly when the two multiplicative integrals lie on a common ray, which is open.
