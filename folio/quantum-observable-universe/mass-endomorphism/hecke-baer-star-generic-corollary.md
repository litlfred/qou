## Corollary — complete system of bar-aware projections

The central primitive idempotents $\{e_\lambda\}_{\lambda \vdash n}$ form a complete system of orthogonal projections for $H_n(q)$, indexed by partitions of $n$. They are explicit ([explicit central idempotent](#prop:explicit-central-idempotent)) and bar-aware. Self-conjugate $\lambda$ yield bar-fixed projections (real period witnesses). Conjugate pairs yield bar-swapped projections (chirality-twisted witnesses).
