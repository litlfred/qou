In the recursive brane tower ([recursive brane tower](#def:recursive-brane-tower)), mass is strictly relational rather than an intrinsic or bare property of an isolated point-particle. Instead, it measures the topological resistance encountered by a stable vortex propagating through the $1$-jet bundle $J^1 \mathcal{S}_n$ ([jet prolongation](#prop:jet-prolongation)) relative to the trefoil calibration unit. The substrate parameter $q$ ([substrate parameter](#def:substrate-parameter)) varies from fibre to fibre. Translating the vortex along the base therefore forces a corresponding variation of the Frobenius--Hopf structure on each fibre, and the dimensionless mass ratio quantifies this variation with no intrinsic-mass parameter.
