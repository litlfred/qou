In the recursive brane tower ([recursive brane tower](#def:recursive-brane-tower)), mass is not an intrinsic property of a point-particle. Instead, it measures the resistance encountered by a stable vortex propagating through the $1$-jet bundle $J^1 \mathcal{S}_n$ ([jet prolongation](#prop:jet-prolongation)). The aether parameter $q$ ([aether parameter](#def:substrate-parameter)) varies from fibre to fibre. Translating the vortex along the base therefore forces a corresponding variation of the Frobenius--Hopf structure on each fibre. The mass quantifies this variation.
