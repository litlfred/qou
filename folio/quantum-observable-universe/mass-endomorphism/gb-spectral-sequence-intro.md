The descending Gröbner–Shirshov ideal filtration $J^\bullet(q)$ on the Iwahori–Hecke algebra $H_n(R, q)$ induces a spectral sequence whose successive pages isolate the six structural terms of the unified knot mass formula ([unified knot mass](#thm:knot-mass-formula-unified)), and whose $E_\infty$ page governs homological shell condensation and the motivic Galois coaction per cell.
