The mass
$$
  \mathfrak{m}(\varrho)
  \;=\;
  \textstyle\int_A \circ\, \bigl( (\int_A \otimes \mathrm{id}) \circ \Delta \bigr) \circ \varrho
$$
contains $\int_A$ twice. Let $c \in R$ be a unit and replace $\int_A$ by $c \int_A$. The comultiplication induced by the Frobenius form becomes $c^{-1} \Delta$, so the mass integrand $\Psi = (\int_A \otimes \mathrm{id}) \circ \Delta$ is unchanged. (If $\Delta$ is held fixed instead, $\Psi$ scales by $c$ and the mass by $c^2$; the statement uses the induced comultiplication.) Only the outer evaluation changes, and it multiplies $\mathfrak{m}(\varrho)$ by $c$, an identity in $R$. The QU/QOU axioms uniquely determine all other inputs ($\Delta$, $\varrho$). Hence fixing the normalisation of $\int_A$ (as part of the QU datum) uniquely fixes $\mathfrak{m}$. The absolute value $|\varphi(c)|$ appears only after a ring homomorphism $\varphi$ has evaluated the mass for comparison, where $|\cdot|$ is multiplicative.
