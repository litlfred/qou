*Each exceptional-divisor summand evaluates to the symmetric $q$-dilogarithm in the spectral coordinates of the Briggs--Klein chart at $E_i$:*

$$
  \bigl\lVert
  \mathrm{MI}\!\!\int_{E_i}
  \bigl(\nabla_A q, L \wedge F^+\bigr)
  \bigr\rVert
  =
  \int_0^{z_i} \frac{\ln_q(1+t)}{t}\, dt
  =
  -\mathrm{Li}_2^q(-z_i).
$$

*Here $\mathrm{Li}_2^q(z) = \sum_{n \ge 1} z^n/(n\,[n]_q)$ with the symmetric $q$-integer $[n]_q$. This is not the product $\Phi_q(z) = \prod_{k\ge1}(1-q^{-k}z)^{-1}$; see the [disambiguation](#def:quantum-dilogarithm-disambiguation).*
