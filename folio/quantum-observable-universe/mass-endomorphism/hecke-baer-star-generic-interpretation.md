### Co-location with the Volume Conjecture

The Kashaev–Murakami–Murakami volume conjecture ([Kashaev volume conjecture](#conj:kashaev-volume-conjecture)) sets $q = e^{2\pi i / N}$ for $N \to \infty$. Here $q$ runs over primitive $N$-th roots of unity. As $N$ grows past $n$, $q$ enters the failure locus $\mathrm{NonBaer}(H_n(q))$:

- For $N = 2k$ with $k \leq n$: $q = \zeta_{2k}$ sits in the failure locus. Baer-* fails, Wedderburn fails, and the asymptotic-$N$ regime activates.
- For $N \gg n$: $q = \zeta_N$ may fall outside the failure locus for $H_n(q)$ at fixed $n$. However, as $N \to \infty$, the relevant Hecke order $n$ grows with the colored-Jones level, eventually entering the failure locus.

Therefore, the Volume Conjecture's archimedean asymptotic regime exactly traces the trajectory through the Baer-*-failure locus. The loss of Baer-* projection-generation supplies the structural mechanism of the wall-crossing transition.

This co-location is the Hecke-algebra face of the phenomenon that forces hyperbolic $3$-manifold volumes to appear at depth $2$ in the period filtration. The radical at the failure locus produces the period-class-non-trivial elements upon archimedean specialisation. See [the volume conjecture as a depth-2 instance](#rem:vc-as-depth-2-instance) for the depth-$2$ reading.

### Significance for the depth correspondence

Combined with [the Grothendieck–Brieskorn depth–period correspondence](#conj:gb-depth-period-correspondence), the projections $\{e_\lambda\}$ become explicit witnesses for Goncharov period classes. Under $\tau_{\mathrm{arch}}$, $e_{\lambda_K} \cdot \mathrm{Li}_2^q(z_K)$ generates the period class $[\mathrm{Vol}(K)] \in \mathcal{P}_2 / \mathcal{P}_1$ for any hyperbolic knot $K$ with dominant cell $\lambda_K$ and shape parameter $z_K$. The figure-$8$ instance evaluates as $e_{(2,2)} \cdot \mathrm{Li}_2^q(\zeta_6)$.
