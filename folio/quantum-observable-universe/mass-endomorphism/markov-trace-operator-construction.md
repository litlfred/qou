The Jones–Wenzl Markov trace $\mathrm{tr}_M : \mathbf{B}_\infty \to \mathbb{R}$ on the inductive limit of braid groups is uniquely characterised by the M1–M4 axioms (Jones 1985 / Wenzl 1988). It is realised as a single operator on the von Neumann inductive-limit hyperfinite $\mathrm{II}_1$ factor in five stages: a per-$n$ Hilbert representation, an inductive embedding $\iota_n : V_n \hookrightarrow V_{n+1}$, the inductive limit $V_\infty = \varinjlim V_n$, the trace state on $\bigcup_n H_n$, and the bicommutant closure $\mathcal{M} := (\bigcup_n H_n)''$. On $\mathcal{M}$, the trace $\mathrm{tr}_M$ provides the unique faithful normal trace up to scaling.

The axiomatic construction packages the per-$n$ Hilbert representation, the inductive embedding, and the GNS bicommutant factor hosting $\mathrm{tr}_M$ as structural hypotheses; the GNS state and bicommutant closure then supply the concrete per-$n$ realisations.
