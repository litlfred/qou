**Macdonald coordinate $u = q^2$ substantive discharges for $H_3 / V_{(2,1)}$ scalar invariants.**

Many scalar invariants in the Hecke and Wenzl-bridge algebras are rational in the Macdonald coordinate $u = q^2$, even when their defining formulas involve square roots over $q$. This Macdonald substitution eliminates square roots, providing concrete, closed-form rational identities that algebraically verify the explicit-residual relations required in [the $c_k$ extraction from algebraic rules](#rem:ck-extraction-from-algebraic-rules) and related proofs.

Because the substitution $u = q^2$ removes square roots, every scalar identity in the table reduces to a pure rational function identity in $u$.

## Significance

The $u$-coordinate identities express the underlying scalars as rational functions in a single variable $u$, connecting the vanishing of each residual directly to aether arithmetic. For example, the Hecke quadratic residual on $H_3(q)$ vanishes because of the root-sum identity $q \cdot (q - q^{-1}) = u - 1$.
