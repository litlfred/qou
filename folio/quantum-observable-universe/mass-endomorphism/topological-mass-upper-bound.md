Let $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ be a quantum observable universe ([quantum observable universe](#def:quantum-observable-universe)). Let $\varrho \colon \mathbf{1} \to A$ be a closed cycle ([mass identity](#def:mass-identity)) whose categorical support is generated by $N$ consecutive extensions along the brane tower ([recursive brane tower](#def:recursive-brane-tower)) from a base-level morphism $\varrho_0 \colon \mathbf{1} \to A_0$. Let $\mathrm{tr}_{\mathrm{desc}}(\varrho)$ be the trace restricted to the descended subalgebra $\mathrm{im}\,\hat{\tau}_n$, and let $\mathrm{tr}_{\mathrm{trans}}(\varrho, p_i)$ be the trace restricted to the component of $\ker\hat{\tau}_n$ localised at the $i$-th blow-up centre $p_i$ ([brane boundary](#def:brane-boundary)). Then

$$
  \mathfrak{m}(\varrho)
  \le
  \bigl\lVert \mathrm{tr}_{\mathrm{desc}}(\varrho) \bigr\rVert +
  \sum_{i=1}^{N}
  \bigl\lVert \mathrm{tr}_{\mathrm{trans}}(\varrho, p_i) \bigr\rVert .
$$
