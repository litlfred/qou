Having decomposed the complex volume $\mathfrak{V}(K)$ into its bar-even binding part and bar-odd decay part, we return to the algebraic backbone of the real trace functional over $\mathbb{Z}[q, q^{-1}]$. Under the descent involution $\hat{\tau}$, the categorical mass endomorphism splits into a descended observable eigenspace and a transient correction, while its trace over $H_n(q)$ is canonically identified with the Jones–Markov trace $\mathrm{tr}_M$ ([categorical mass = Markov](#prop:categorical-mass-markov)). This section establishes the categorical eigenspace decomposition, connects the Hecke trace to the Knizhnik–Zamolodchikov connection and Gibbs free energy, and formulates the Gröbner–Shirshov normal-form expansion and Littlewood–Richardson branching rules across multi-nucleon tensor products.
