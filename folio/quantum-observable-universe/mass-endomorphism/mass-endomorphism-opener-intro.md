With the knot–particle dictionary ([*Knots, Particles, and Confinement*](#chap:knots-particles-confinement)) and its Eightfold Way multiplet structure ([*The Eightfold Way and Flavor Structure*](#chap:eightfold-way)) established, this chapter develops the algebraic engine that extracts a numerical mass invariant from each particle's closed braid. Three distinct mathematical objects participate in this construction ([`notation-collisions`](../notation/notation-collisions.md)):
1. The **mass endomorphism** $\mathcal{M}(K) \in \mathrm{End}_{\mathbf{C}}(X_K)$, a categorical morphism $X_K \to X_K$ on the Reshetikhin–Turaev long-knot object $X_K$.
2. The **Hecke mass element** $\Phi_{\mathrm{mass}}(\beta) \in H_n(R, q)$, the braid-group image of $\mathcal{M}(K)$ inside the Iwahori–Hecke algebra, acting on $V^{\otimes n}$ by quantum Schur–Weyl duality.
3. The **categorical mass functional** $\mathfrak{m}(\varrho) = |\mathrm{tr}_M(\rho(\beta))| \cdot \mathrm{VolDensity}(K, q_0)$, the scalar obtained by closing $\Phi_{\mathrm{mass}}(\beta)$ under the Jones–Markov trace $\mathrm{tr}_M$ and pairing it with the hyperbolic volume density.

This opening section states the mass endomorphism conjecture, establishes existence and uniqueness of the categorical mass identity ([mass identity](#def:mass-identity)), proves the upper bound of the four-part topological mass decomposition into eigenspace, holonomy, transient, and quantum-dilogarithm contributions ([upper bound](#prop:topological-mass-upper-bound)) and states equality as a conjecture ([topological mass](#conj:topological-mass)), and identifies the six-term unified mass formula with the $E_\infty$ page of the Gröbner–Shirshov spectral sequence. Subsequent sections introduce the complex volume $\mathfrak{V}$ ([the complex-volume section](#sec:complex-volume)), evaluate the Markov trace across Wedderburn blocks under Convention D ([the eigenspace section](#sec:categorical-mass-eigenspaces) and [the Markov-trace section](#sec:categorical-mass-markov-trace-endomorphism)), derive the per-irrep $\chi$-character closed forms ([the $u$-coordinate section](#sec:hecke-chi-closed-forms), [the cyclotomic $\chi$-character section](#sec:hecke-chi-closed-forms-chi-per-irrep), and [the $\Delta\mathrm{tr}_M$ section](#sec:hecke-chi-per-block-delta-tau-canonical-weights)), and close with the Hecke–Baer-$*$ chirality pairing ([the Baer-star section](#sec:hecke-baer-star-chirality)).
