Let $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ be a quantum observable universe ([quantum observable universe](#def:quantum-observable-universe)) and $\varrho \colon \mathbf{1} \to A$ a closed cycle. The inertial mass

$$
  \mathfrak{m}(\varrho)
  \;=\;
  \textstyle\int_A \circ\, \bigl( (\int_A \otimes \mathrm{id}) \circ \Delta \bigr) \circ \varrho
$$

of the [mass identity](#def:mass-identity) is a well-defined element of $R$ for every $\varrho$, by construction. Each step of the composite is a morphism of $\mathbf{C}$ that the definition already supplies:

1. $\Delta \colon A \to A \otimes A$ is part of the Frobenius--Hopf
   structure on $A$.
2. $\int_A \otimes \mathrm{id} \colon A \otimes A \to \mathbf{1} \otimes A \cong A$
   is a morphism by tensor functoriality.
3. $\int_A \colon A \to \mathbf{1}$ is the Frobenius form, and
   $\mathrm{End}_{\mathbf{C}}(\mathbf{1}) = R$.

Nothing here is a further claim; it records that the definition is total. The value is a ring element with no sign attached. A sign and a non-negative size appear only when the mass is compared with a measurement, by evaluating it and taking an absolute value.
