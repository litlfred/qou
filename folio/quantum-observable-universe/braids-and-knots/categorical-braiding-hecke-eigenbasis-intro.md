The group algebra of the braid group $B_n$ has infinite rank and cannot support a finite-dimensional trace functional directly. Imposing the quadratic skein relation $(\sigma_i - q)(\sigma_i + q^{-1}) = 0$ projects the braid action onto the finite-dimensional Iwahori–Hecke algebra $H_n(q)$ of rank $n!$ over $\mathbb{Z}[q, q^{-1}]$. Within $H_n(q)$, the Kazhdan–Lusztig bar involution $\overline{\sigma_i} = \sigma_i^{-1}$ separates self-dual and anti-self-dual classes, while the Jones–Markov trace $\mathrm{tr}_M$ collapses closed braids to a single Laurent rational invariant. Diagonalizing the central Jucys–Murphy operators in $H_n(q)$ produces the knot eigenbasis in $\mathrm{End}_{\mathbf{C}}(A)$, which serves as the algebraic basis for all subsequent mass and binding evaluations.
