The representation $\rho \colon B_3 \to \mathrm{SU}(2)$ specifies the exact holonomy of the symmetric group $S_3\cong D_3$ acting on the branched cover $\mathcal{S} \to \mathbb{P}^1(\mathbb{C})$. As the Galois group of the cubic constraint it permutes the cubic's three branches, and it determines how the spectral Lagrange resolvents $w_k$ are physically transported around the branch points.

The chiral sheets of the geometry are defined by these spectral coordinates. The conjugate pairs $(w_1, w_4)$ and $(w_2, w_3)$ tile the four non-trivial modes into a time-like pair $(w_2,w_3)$ and a space-like axis $w_1$, and the projective ratios $\eta_1=w_1/w_2$, $\eta_2=w_3/w_4$ coordinatize the three branches on which $S_3$ acts. The holonomy is built from two elementary operations:

1. **The rotation $\alpha$ (order $3$).** The $3$-cycle $\alpha=\sigma_1\sigma_2$ cyclically permutes the three branches. In the $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ embedding it is the generator $\alpha=\exp(\tfrac{2\pi}{3}e_3)$; physically it rotates the time-like resolvent pair $(w_2,w_3)$, carrying the dynamical phase of the Reeb flow and leaving the overall chirality unchanged.
2. **The reflection $\beta$ (order $2$).** A reflection swaps the conjugate pairs $(w_1,w_4)\leftrightarrow(w_2,w_3)$. In $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ it is the generator $\beta=\exp(\pi e_1)$, reflecting the space-like axis $w_1$ and implementing the geometric equivalent of parity inversion. The two braid generators $\sigma_1,\sigma_2$ ([braid twist–flip](#def:braid-twist-flip)) are reflections of this kind.

Together, the rotation and reflection generate $S_3\cong D_3$ with the dihedral relation $\beta\alpha\beta^{-1}=\alpha^{-1}$; the two braid reflections $\sigma_1,\sigma_2$ have product $\sigma_1\sigma_2=\alpha$, and $\alpha^3=\mathrm{id}$ on $\mathrm{SO}(3)$. The full $S_3$ holonomy is generated by these rotations and reflections of the chiral sheets.

Whenever $\rho$ appears in braid-group representations or knot invariants, it acts physically by rotating the time-like modes and reflecting the space-like axis of the chiral sheets.
