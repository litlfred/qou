The [$q$-Casimir element](#def:q-casimir) is the distinguished central element of $\mathcal{U}_q(\mathfrak{su}(2))$ whose eigenvalue on the spin-$j$ irreducible encodes the categorical $q$-angular momentum. Subtracting $(q + q^{-1})$ in the numerator ensures the eigenvalue vanishes on the trivial representation and has a finite classical limit. Equivalently,
$$
  C_q  =  E F  +  \frac{q^{-1}\,K + q\,K^{-1} - (q + q^{-1})}
                        {(q - q^{-1})^2}.
$$
By [existence](#prop:q-casimir-exists), $C_q$ is central and acts on the $(2j+1)$-dimensional highest-weight module $V_j$ as the scalar $[j]_q\,[j+1]_q$, where $[n]_q = (q^n - q^{-n})/(q - q^{-1})$. Centrality makes $C_q$ an endomorphism of the identity functor on $\mathbf{Rep}(\mathrm{SU}_q(2))$, the categorical counterpart of the angular-momentum-squared operator.
