The Wirtinger presentation of the knot-complement fundamental group $\pi_1(S^3 \setminus K)$ translates braid conjugacy Relations into geometric representation varieties $\mathrm{Hom}(\pi_1(S^3 \setminus K), \mathrm{SU}(2))$. By the theorem of Kronheimer and Mrowka \cite{kronheimer-mrowka2004}, every nontrivial knot admits an irreducible $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ representation, while the reducible locus factors through the abelianization $H_1(S^3 \setminus K) \cong \mathbb{Z}$ and isolates the Alexander polynomial at the boundary between abelian and non-abelian Hecke channels.
