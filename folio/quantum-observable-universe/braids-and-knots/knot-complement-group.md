For a knot $K\subset \mathbb{P}^1(\mathbb{C})$, the fundamental group $\pi_1(\mathbb{P}^1(\mathbb{C})\setminus K)$ encodes the topology of the complement. A representation
$$
  \rho\colon\pi_1(\mathbb{P}^1(\mathbb{C})\setminus K)
   \longrightarrow \mathrm{PSL}(2,\mathbb{C})
$$
maps each meridian generator to a Möbius transformation of the Riemann sphere. The image of $\rho$ characterises the geometric structure on the knot complement. A precise form of this for a hyperbolic knot complement $M = \mathbb{H}^3/\Gamma$: if a representation $\rho\colon \Gamma \to PSL(2,\mathbb{C})$ has $|\mathrm{vol}(\rho)| = \mathrm{vol}(M)$, then $\rho$ is conjugate to the inclusion, in particular discrete and faithful \cite[\S4]{francaviglia2003}.
