The pion decay $\pi \to \mu + \nu$ de-entangles $V_{(2,1)}$. Read through the [phase identity](#prop:pion-half-figure-eight), the decay inverts a half-Reeb-rotation: it releases the energy stored in the phase $\theta$. Two such decays would undo a full figure-eight rotation, which is how this reading connects meson decay to the lepton mass ratio. The connection is interpretive; the proposition itself states only the equality of phases.
