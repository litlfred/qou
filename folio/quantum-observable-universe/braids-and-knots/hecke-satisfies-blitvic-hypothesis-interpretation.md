The obstruction comes from the quadratic relation. A repeated generator does not reduce to the identity: $\sigma^{2} = h\sigma + 1$ leaves a term in $\sigma$ itself, and a non-zero linear term survives in every power. A $(q,t)$-Gaussian has a symmetric moment sequence. The centred Hecke generator has eigenvalues $q - z$ and $-q^{-1} - z$, which are not negatives of each other, so its distribution under $\mathrm{tr}$ is not symmetric.
