In the macroscopic $N^{-1/2}$-scaled central limit regime for $S_N = (N v)^{-1/2} \sum_{i=1}^N \tilde\sigma_i$, singletons vanish by the Markov stabilisation axiom and higher-cumulant blocks of size $\geq 3$ (such as the single-generator skewness $\mathrm{tr}_M(\tilde\sigma_i^3) = (h - 2z)v$) decay as $O(N^{-1/2})$, leaving only the pair-partition combinatorics of $P_2(k)$ in the $N \to \infty$ limit.
