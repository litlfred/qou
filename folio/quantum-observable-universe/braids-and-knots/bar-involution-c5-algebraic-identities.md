The parameter $h = q - q^{-1}$ factors into $h_{1/2} = q^{1/2} - q^{-1/2}$ and $q^{1/2} + q^{-1/2}$. The bar-anti-symmetry of $h$ stems entirely from $h_{1/2}$; the second factor is bar-invariant.

The second factor is **not** $1/z$. For the Markov parameter $z = q^3/(q^2+1)$, its reciprocal is $1/z = (q^2+1)/q^3 = [2]_q/q^2 = 1.632151991505175166202$ at $q_0$, whereas $q^{1/2} + q^{-1/2} = 2.0027\ldots$ is a Temperley–Lieb loop expression at the half-argument $q^{1/2}$, not the reciprocal of the Hecke generator's Markov trace. Moreover, $1/[2]_q^2$, the bar-even quantity from which the $c_5$ closed form below is built, equals the two-row Markov weight $y_{(2,1)}$ — the isospin-singlet weight $\mu_-$.

The Hoefsmit-Quantum-2 identity is $b^2(q) = 1 - 1/[2]_q^2$. From this, the $c_5$ closed form follows:
$$
c_5(q) \;=\; -\tfrac{3}{2} + \frac{1}{[2]_q^2} \;=\; -\tfrac{3}{2} + \frac{1}{h^2 + 4}.
$$
Both terms are strictly even functions of $h$. The constant $-3/2$ corresponds to half the dimension of the non-sign Wedderburn blocks of the symmetric group $S_3$. This identifies the $-1/2$ fingerprint in the empirical decomposition as the bar-symmetric contribution from the non-sign sector at the classical limit.
