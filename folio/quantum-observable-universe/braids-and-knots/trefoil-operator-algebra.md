Inside the binary dihedral group of [def:su2-generators](#def:su2-generators), the braid generators of $B_3$ are represented by the two transposition lifts:
$$
  s_1=\beta=\begin{pmatrix}0&i\\i&0\end{pmatrix},
  \qquad
  s_2=\alpha^2\beta=\begin{pmatrix}0&e^{7i\pi/6}\\ e^{-i\pi/6}&0\end{pmatrix}.
$$
They are conjugate, $s_2=\alpha s_1\alpha^{-1}$, with $\mathrm{tr}\,s_1=\mathrm{tr}\,s_2=0$ and $s_1^2=s_2^2=-I$, and they realise the trefoil braid relation $xyx=yxy$:
$$
  s_1s_2s_1
  = \begin{pmatrix}0&e^{5i\pi/6}\\ e^{i\pi/6}&0\end{pmatrix}
  = s_2s_1s_2.
$$
The generator $\alpha$ is the $3$-cycle lift; together with $\beta$ it obeys the dihedral relations $\alpha\beta\alpha=\beta$ and $\beta\alpha\beta^{-1}=\alpha^{-1}$.

The composite $s_1s_2$ satisfies $(s_1s_2)^3=-I$, so its eigenvalues are $e^{\pm i\pi/3}$ — the three-fold phase acquired when quarks exchange colour charges along the trefoil path.
