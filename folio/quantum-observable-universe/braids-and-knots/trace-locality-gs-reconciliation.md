Two computational operations seemingly contradict: single Hecke crossings obey Markov locality, yet composite braid evaluation via Gröbner–Shirshov reduction is non-local. Wenzl's product factorisation, recorded in [`prop:trace-locality-meets-gs-non-locality`](#prop:trace-locality-meets-gs-non-locality), reconciles them. The GS algorithm digests global entanglement into local coefficients; after digestion, the Markov trace factorises locally over the normal form. The process is therefore local after normalisation.
