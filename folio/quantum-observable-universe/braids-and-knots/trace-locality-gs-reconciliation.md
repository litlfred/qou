Two computational operations seemingly contradict: single Hecke crossings obey Markov locality, yet composite braid evaluation via Gröbner–Shirshov reduction is non-local. [Markov locality of single Hecke crossings](#prop:trace-locality-meets-gs-non-locality) separates them: the GS algorithm digests global entanglement into the coefficients of the normal form, and the trace is evaluated afterwards. Whether that evaluation is local over the normal form, a product of per-letter factors, is not yet proved; [the product-factorisation check](#rem:trace-locality-product-factorisation-check) records what has been verified.
