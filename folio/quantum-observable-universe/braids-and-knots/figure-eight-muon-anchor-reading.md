The muon-anchor identity $m_\mu / m_\mathfrak{e} = \mathrm{Vol}(4_1) / \hbar_q^2$ relies on the hyperbolic volume of the figure-eight complement, which the volume conjecture relates to the Kashaev limit of the [figure-eight rotation](#prop:figure-eight-reeb-rotation). This is the point at which the rotation of $V_{(2,1)}$ meets a measured quantity: the ratio $m_\mu/m_\mathfrak{e}$ is compared with experiment, and that comparison, not the proposition, carries the empirical claim.
