This chapter initiates Part II by passing from Step 1 of the five-step arc (the rigid braided monoidal category $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ of Part I) to Step 2: the algebraic mechanics of the braid group $B_n$ and its Iwahori–Hecke quotient $H_n(q)$. In a quantum observable universe whose base and fibres have finite cohomological rank, continuum path integrals $\int \mathcal{D}\phi$ are replaced by finite algebraic traces over braid operators acting on tensor powers $A^{\otimes n}$ of the Frobenius–Hopf object. Because the trace is evaluated over a finite-dimensional algebra over $\mathbb{Z}[q, q^{-1}]$, ultraviolet divergences do not arise.

The braiding isomorphism $c_{A,A} \colon A \otimes A \xrightarrow{\sim} A \otimes A$ induced by the universal $R$-matrix of $\mathcal{U}_q(\mathfrak{su}(2))$ satisfies the Yang–Baxter equation and generates a representation of Artin's braid group $B_n$. Factoring through the quadratic Hecke relation $(\sigma_i - q)(\sigma_i + q^{-1}) = 0$ yields the Iwahori–Hecke algebra $H_n(q)$, equipped with the Kazhdan–Lusztig bar involution and the Jones–Markov trace $\mathrm{tr}_M$. Under the production trace normalization (Convention D, $z_D = q^3/(q^2+1)$), the three-row Young weight $y_{(1^3)}$ vanishes identically, forcing the three-strand antisymmetric channel to be evaluated via the Geer–Patureau-Mirand–Turaev modified trace.
