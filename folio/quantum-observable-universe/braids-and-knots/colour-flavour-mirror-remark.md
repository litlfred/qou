The colour singlet $\varepsilon_q$ spans the column $(1^3)$ of $(K^3)^{\otimes 3}$. The proposition says that inside the $q$-exterior states its flavour partner lies in the row $(3)$: colour and flavour carry transposed partitions. This is the colour-singlet slice of quantum skew Howe duality, in which the $q$-exterior algebra of colour $\otimes$ flavour pairs each colour shape $\lambda$ with the flavour shape $\lambda'$. The transposition $\lambda \mapsto \lambda'$ is the Hecke involution $\sigma \mapsto -\sigma^{-1}$ that the $\mathfrak{gl}_6$ coideal route applies to the flavour generator.

The tensor route and the coideal route therefore describe one structure. In the tensor route colour is a separate symmetry commuting with the braid; in the coideal route the antisymmetry of the colour singlet is carried into the flavour generator. At $q = 1$, item 1 is the familiar statement that a totally antisymmetric three-strand state whose colour part is the singlet has a totally symmetric flavour part.
