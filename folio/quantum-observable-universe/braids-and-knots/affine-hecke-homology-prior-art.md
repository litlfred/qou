Let $\mathcal{R}$ be a based root datum with finite Weyl group $W$, let $T = \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(X, \mathbb{C}^\times)$, and let $\mathcal{H}(\mathcal{R}, q)$ be the associated affine Hecke algebra with positive, possibly unequal, parameters $q$. Maarten Solleveld \cite[Theorem 1(d), p.~3]{solleveld2022} (originally published in \cite{solleveld2013}) constructs families of representations $\pi(w, i, t, \epsilon)$ of $\mathcal{H}(\mathcal{R}, q^\epsilon)$ and proves that, for $\epsilon \notin \sqrt{-1}\,\mathbb{R}^\times$, two of them have the same trace if and only if they share $(w, i)$ and their torus parameters $t$ lie in one $Z_W(w)$-orbit. For the same $\epsilon$ the Hochschild homology is
$$
HH_*\big(\mathcal{H}(\mathcal{R}, q^\epsilon)\big) \cong \bigoplus_{w \in \langle W \rangle}\bigoplus_{i=1}^{c(w)} \Omega^*(T_i^w)^{Z_W(w)} \cong \Omega^*(\widetilde{T})^W,
$$
the $W$-invariant algebraic differential forms on the extended quotient $\widetilde{T}/W$; only the action of the centre $Z(\mathcal{H}(\mathcal{R}, q^\epsilon)) \cong \mathcal{O}(T)^W$ shifts with $q^\epsilon$ \cite[Theorem 2, pp.~3–4; §3.2]{solleveld2022}. A 2021 gap in the representation-family construction and its repair are discussed on pages 16, 18, 19 and 22 of \cite[Abstract]{solleveld2022}.

In degree zero, $HH_0\big(\mathcal{H}(\mathcal{R}, q^\epsilon)\big) \cong \mathcal{O}(\widetilde{T})^W$ is the cocentre $\mathcal{H}/[\mathcal{H}, \mathcal{H}]$, whose linear dual classifies all trace functionals on $\mathcal{H}(\mathcal{R}, q^\epsilon)$. Because $\mathcal{O}(\widetilde{T})^W$ does not move with $q^\epsilon$, any $q$-dependence of an affine Hecke trace value arises from the choice of functional or representation evaluation rather than from deformation of the cocentre.

This affine trace space contrasts sharply with the finite Iwahori–Hecke subalgebras $H_n(q) \subset \mathcal{H}(A_{n-1}, q)$ at generic $q$: $H_n(q)$ is finite-dimensional semisimple, so $\dim HH_0(H_n(q)) = p(n)$ is finite and spanned by the irreducible characters $\chi_\lambda$ ($\lambda \vdash n$) rather than by continuous torus algebras $\mathcal{O}(T_i^w)^{Z_W(w)}$. On the two-strand per-pair electromagnetic channel $H_2(q)$, $\dim HH_0(H_2(q)) = 2$ (spanned by $\chi_{(2)}$ and $\chi_{(1^2)}$), while across the strand-adding tower $H_n(q) \hookrightarrow H_{n+1}(q)$ the Markov stabilisation condition $\mathrm{tr}_M(x\,\sigma_n^{\pm 1}) = z\,\mathrm{tr}_M(x)$ cuts out a unique normalised one-parameter trace family. Moreover, the [inductive-limit trace](#rem:markov-trace-inductive-limit) on $H_\infty(q) = \bigcup_n H_n(q)$ is not covered directly by Solleveld's theorem: $H_\infty(q)$ is an increasing union across rank $n - 1 \to \infty$ rather than the affine Hecke algebra of a fixed finite-rank root datum $\mathcal{R}$, so passing to the limit requires an independent argument.
