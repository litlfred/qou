*Going once around a branch point of the colour tube over Bring's surface acts on the three-strand colour space $(K^3)^{\otimes 3}$ by a $K$-linear map that commutes with Jimbo's braidings $\check R_{12}$ and $\check R_{23}$. That map is not the bare permutation of the two colliding sheets. It is that permutation corrected by the braiding, in the manner of the quantum Weyl group of $U_q(\mathfrak{gl}_3)$ \cite{lusztig1993}.*

If the conjecture holds, [the commuting-singlet proposition](#prop:colour-braid-commuting-singlet) shows that the monodromy keeps the colour singlet $\varepsilon_q$ up to a scalar for every $q$ with $q^2 + 1 \neq 0$. The identification of the tube's sheets with an ordered colour basis would then hold globally, up to that correction. The expectation comes from the Drinfeld–Kohno theorem: the monodromy of a flat connection on configuration space with values in a representation is computed by the braiding \cite{kohno1987,drinfeld1990}. What is not shown is that the colour cubic on Bring's surface carries such a connection, and that its branch-point monodromy is the corrected swap.
