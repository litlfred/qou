Blitvić's theorem applies when joint moments $\mathrm{tr}_M(\sigma_{i_1} \sigma_{i_2} \cdots \sigma_{i_k})$ decompose as a sum over pair-partitions $\pi$ with weights depending only on $\pi$ and $q$. The Hecke relation $\sigma_i^2 = (q - q^{-1})\sigma_i + 1$ reduces every generator word to a combination of square-free products, and the Markov property then evaluates the trace as a sum over matchings — the mechanism realising the [Hecke pair-partition hypothesis](#prop:hecke-satisfies-blitvic-hypothesis).
