Blitvić's theorem applies when joint moments of centred variables vanish in odd degree and decompose as a sum over pair-partitions in even degree. For the Hecke generators under the Markov trace the first condition already fails: the third centred moment of a single generator is non-zero ([`prop:hecke-satisfies-blitvic-hypothesis`](#prop:hecke-satisfies-blitvic-hypothesis)). The quadratic relation reduces a repeated generator to a combination of $1$ and $\sigma_i$, not to a pairing, so the Markov-trace moments are not a pair-partition sum.
