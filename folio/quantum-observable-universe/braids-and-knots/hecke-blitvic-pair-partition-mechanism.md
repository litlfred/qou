Blitvić's non-commutative central limit theorem applies when macroscopic normalised moments $\mathrm{tr}_M(S_N^k)$ converge as $N \to \infty$ to a sum over pair-partitions $\pi \in P_2(k)$. In the Hecke algebra, the centred quadratic relation $\tilde\sigma_i^2 = (h - 2z)\tilde\sigma_i + v\cdot 1$ and the Markov stabilisation property eliminate every word with a singleton maximal strand index and suppress blocks of size $\geq 3$ by $O(N^{-1/2})$, realising the [Hecke pair-partition hypothesis](#prop:hecke-satisfies-blitvic-hypothesis).
