*Let $K$ be a field and $q \in K^\times$. Let $\varepsilon_q \in (K^3)^{\otimes 3}$ be the quantum colour singlet of Jimbo's braiding on $K^3$, normalised by $\varepsilon_q(0,1,2) = 1$. Let $s$ be the permutation of the colour labels exchanging $0$ and $1$, and let $(s\cdot w)(a,b,c) = w(s(a), s(b), s(c))$. Then $s \cdot \varepsilon_q$ is a scalar multiple of $\varepsilon_q$ if and only if $q^2 = 1$. When $q^2 = 1$ the scalar is $-q^{-1}$.*
