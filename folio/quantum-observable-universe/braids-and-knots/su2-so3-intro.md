The compact Lie group $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ and its adjoint quotient $\mathrm{SO}(3) \cong \mathrm{SU}(2)/\{\pm I\}$ govern the internal spin fibre and its spatial orientation frame. This section fixes the Pauli and half-quaternion bases for $\mathfrak{su}(2)$ and $\mathfrak{so}(3)$, computes the first-order Taylor expansion of the $q$-deformed generators around $q = 1$, shows how the double cover $\mathrm{Sp}(1) \twoheadrightarrow \mathrm{SO}(3)$ identifies spectral resolvents with $\mathfrak{su}(2)$-valued connection one-forms, and closes with the generators of the binary dihedral lift $\mathrm{Dic}_3 \subset \mathrm{SU}(2)$ of $S_3 \subset \mathrm{SO}(3)$.
