The Borromean observable takes the word $\beta_{\mathrm{Bor}}$ as input. Two results
fix it.

Zero pairwise linking forces writhe $0$. At writhe $0$ the crossing energy of a word is
its length times $(q - q^{-1})/2$, so for $q > 1$ minimising the crossing energy is
minimising length. By [the minimality theorem](#thm:borromean-word-minimal), the
words of least energy among non-trivial pure zero-linking three-braids are exactly the
conjugates of $\beta_{\mathrm{Bor}}$. Minimising crossing energy therefore selects
the word up to conjugacy.

The Wedderburn form of the Markov trace of a writhe-$0$ three-strand word depends on the
word only through $\chi_{(2,1)}$. By [the character theorem](#thm:borromean-character-fibre),
no other conjugacy class at writhe $0$, of any length, shares the value of
$\chi_{(2,1)}$ with $\beta_{\mathrm{Bor}}$ at $q = i$ and $q = 2$. So the trace
seen by the mass identity does not leave room for a second word.
