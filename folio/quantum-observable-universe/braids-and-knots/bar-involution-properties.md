The bar-involution satisfies $\overline{\overline x} = x$. Over a field of characteristic $\ne 2$, every $x \in H_n(q)$ decomposes uniquely into a bar-symmetric (even) part $P_{\mathrm{avg}}(x)$ and a bar-anti-symmetric (odd) part $P_{\mathrm{skew}}(x)$.

The framework asserts that $P_{\mathrm{avg}}$ is the canonical projection: the bar-symmetric part represents physical observables, while the bar-anti-symmetric part is projected away. This applies systematically across operations, retaining functions like $\cos$ and $\cosh$ while discarding $\sin$ and $\sinh$.
