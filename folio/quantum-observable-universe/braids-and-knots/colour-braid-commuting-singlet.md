*Let $K$ be a field and $q \in K^\times$ with $q^2 + 1 \neq 0$. Let $\check R_{12}, \check R_{23}$ be Jimbo's braidings on $(K^3)^{\otimes 3}$, and let $\Phi$ be a $K$-linear map of $(K^3)^{\otimes 3}$ with $\Phi \check R_{12} = \check R_{12} \Phi$ and $\Phi \check R_{23} = \check R_{23} \Phi$. Then $\Phi(\varepsilon_q)$ is a scalar multiple of $\varepsilon_q$. In particular, if the colour swap $s$ of [the colour-relabel proposition](#prop:colour-relabel-singlet) commuted with both braidings, then $q^2 = 1$.*
