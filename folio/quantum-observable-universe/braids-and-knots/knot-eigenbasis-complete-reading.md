Each braid closure $\hat\beta$ meets the summands $S_\lambda$ of [prop:knot-eigenbasis-complete](#prop:knot-eigenbasis-complete) through the components $y_\lambda\chi_\lambda(\rho_n(\beta))$ of its Markov trace. In this reading, knot types play the role of a basis for $\varinjlim V^{\otimes n}$, and the algebraic direct limit takes the place of Feynman's continuum integral. The reading is not a statement about eigenvectors: the summands are labelled by partitions, many knots share a summand, and one closure generally meets several.
