For item 1, direct matrix computation on $(K^3)^{\otimes 3}$ verifies that Jimbo's $\check R$ satisfies the quadratic Hecke relation, the braid relation $\check R_{12}\check R_{23}\check R_{12} = \check R_{23}\check R_{12}\check R_{23}$, and commutes with the three-fold coproducts of $E_i$, $F_i$, and $L_j$. For item 2, solving $E_i v = F_i v = 0$ and $L_j v = v$ in $(K^3)^{\otimes 3}$ under $q \neq 0$ and $q^2 + 1 \neq 0$ shows that the $U_q(\mathfrak{gl}_3)$-invariant subspace is one-dimensional and spanned by the $q$-antisymmetric vector $\varepsilon_q$, on which $\check R_{12}$ and $\check R_{23}$ each act by the scalar $-q^{-1}$. Item 3 follows immediately on $K\varepsilon_q \otimes W$: since $\check R_{\mathrm{colour}}$ acts as $-q^{-1}$, the tensor braiding $X = \check R_{\mathrm{colour}} \cdot b$ restricts to $-q^{-1} b$, and multiplying by $-q$ recovers $b$. For item 4, scaling every generator by a central scalar $c$ multiplies the evaluation of a braid word $w$ of exponent sum $e(w)$ by $c^{e(w)}$, and linearity of $\tau$ pulls $c^{e(w)}$ out of $\tau(w)$; setting $c = -q^{-1}$ gives the raw-generator factor, and setting $c = 1$ for $-qX$ gives exact agreement with the flavour trace.
