The [Skein resolution obstruction](#def:skein-resolution-obstruction) is a fourth evaluation of the universal obstruction class, and everything said of it is conditional on the [Wigner axiom](#conj:wigner-axiom), under which the archimedean image of $w(A)$ is the Euler class of the base, so that its integral over the base curve is $\chi_{\mathrm{eu}}(M_0)$. It measures whether the degree-$0$ link polynomial $P_L(q)$ can be extracted from the resolution tree in polynomial depth.

The class is proportional to the spectral, collision, and elliptic restrictions. Under the axiom a single integer, $\chi_{\mathrm{eu}}(M_0) = -6$, controls whether these computational problems persist through the classical limit. The Kummer filtration gives:
$$
  \kappa_c^{\mathrm{comp}} \bmod n = 0
  \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
  n \mid \chi_{\mathrm{eu}}(M_0).
$$
The obstruction vanishes for $n \mid 6$ and persists otherwise. At root-of-unity torsion where the obstruction vanishes, the Iwahori--Hecke algebra has a radical that prunes the resolution tree. At non-vanishing torsion, even quantum interference cannot collapse the tree. Over a finite field $\mathbb{F}_p$ for $p > 7$, the obstruction persists modulo $p - 1$, but computation terminates in bounded time because the field itself is finite. Over $\mathbb{R}$ at non-torsion $q_0 \approx 1.1099$, the algebra $H_n(q_0)$ is semisimple ($\mathrm{Rad} = 0$), the full $3^c$ tree survives, and the obstruction remains non-vanishing.
