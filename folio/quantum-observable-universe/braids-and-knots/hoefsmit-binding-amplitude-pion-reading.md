Read physically, through the [Hoefsmit identities](#prop:hoefsmit-binding-amplitude), the pion decay $\pi \to \mu + \nu$ de-entangles the meson into $V_{(2)} \oplus V_{(1,1)}$. It releases a fraction $1 - b^2 = 1/[2]_q^2$ of the pion's rest energy:
$$
  E_{\mathrm{bind}} = \frac{m_\pi}{[2]_q^2}.
$$
The residual energy $m_\pi \cdot b^2$ flows into the de-entangled lepton channel $V_{(2)}$.

The identification of $1 - b^2$ with a fraction of the pion's rest energy is an interpretation; the proposition proves only the two identities.
