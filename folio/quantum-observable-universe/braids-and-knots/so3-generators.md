Under the adjoint map to $\mathrm{SO}(3)$, the generators of $\mathrm{Dic}_3$ have images:
```tex
\begin{equation}\label{eq:so3-generators}
  R_\alpha
  =\begin{pmatrix}
    -\frac{1}{2}&-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}&0\\[3pt]
    \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}&-\frac{1}{2}&0\\[3pt]
    0&0&1
  \end{pmatrix},\qquad
  R_\beta
  =\begin{pmatrix}
    1&0&0\\[2pt]
    0&-1&0\\[2pt]
    0&0&-1
  \end{pmatrix}.
\end{equation}
```
