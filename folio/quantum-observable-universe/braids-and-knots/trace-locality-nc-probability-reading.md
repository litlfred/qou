In non-commutative probability terminology, Markov locality means the conditional expectation of a new random variable given the past is a fixed scalar. GS non-locality implies the joint distribution depends on the full word. A product factorisation over the normal form, if proved (see [the product-factorisation check](#rem:trace-locality-product-factorisation-check)), would say that the joint law decomposes as a product over states once the sequence is in normal form. GS reduction is thus the non-commutative analogue of finding the stationary representation of a Markov chain.
