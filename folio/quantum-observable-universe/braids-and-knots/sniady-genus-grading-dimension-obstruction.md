At the classical specialisation $q = 1$, where $H_k(1) \cong \mathbb{C}[S_k]$, the non-vanishing third moment of [the Blitvić-hypothesis proposition](#prop:hecke-satisfies-blitvic-hypothesis) has its symmetric-group counterpart in the three-cycle character observable on Young diagrams $\lambda \vdash k$. Let $\mathcal{A}$ denote the Kerov--Olshanski algebra of polynomial functions on Young diagrams, generated by the normalised conjugacy-class indicators $\Sigma_{k_1, \dots, k_t}$ (or equivalently by the transition-measure free cumulants $R_2, R_3, R_4, \dots$). For any set partition $\pi$ of an $n$-element set, Piotr Śniady proves that the partition-indexed indicator $\Sigma_\pi \in \mathcal{A}$ has filtration degree
$$
  \deg \Sigma_\pi = n - 2\,\mathrm{genus}_\pi
$$
\cite[§3.1, Prop.~3.4]{sniady2006asymptotics}, so non-crossing partitions ($\mathrm{genus}_\pi = 0$) govern the leading order and evercrossing partitions of genus $1$ govern the first subleading correction to normalised characters and Jucys--Murphy free cumulants \cite[§4.5, Thms.~4.13--4.14]{sniady2006asymptotics}. In particular, the normalised three-cycle central character
$$
  \omega_3(\lambda) = \frac{k(k-1)(k-2)\,\chi^\lambda((123))}{3\,f^\lambda} = \sum_{\square \in \lambda} c(\square)^2 - \binom{k}{2}
$$
and the fourth free cumulant $R_4(\lambda) = 3\,\omega_3(\lambda) - k$ both belong to $\mathcal{A}$ in filtration degree $4$, and under the Plancherel measure $\mathbb{P}_{\mathrm{Planch}}(\lambda) = (f^\lambda)^2 / k!$ their expectations satisfy $\mathbb{E}_{\mathrm{Planch}}[\omega_3] = 0$ and $\mathbb{E}_{\mathrm{Planch}}[R_4] = -k$.

By contrast, the dimension-cubed three-cycle sum
$$
  \sum_{\lambda \vdash k} (f^\lambda)^3\,\omega_3(\lambda)
  = \frac{k(k-1)(k-2)}{3} \sum_{\lambda \vdash k} (f^\lambda)^2\,\chi^\lambda((123))
  = k!\,\mathrm{Cov}_{\mathrm{Planch}}\bigl(f^\lambda,\, \omega_3(\lambda)\bigr)
  = \frac{k!}{3}\,\mathrm{Cov}_{\mathrm{Planch}}\bigl(f^\lambda,\, R_4(\lambda)\bigr)
$$
weights $\omega_3 \in \mathcal{A}$ by an additional factor of the hook-length dimension $f^\lambda = k! / \prod_{\square \in \lambda} h(\square)$. Because $f^\lambda$ grows on the scale $\sqrt{k!}$ across typical Plancherel shapes rather than polynomially in the free cumulants $(R_m)_{m \ge 2}$, neither $f^\lambda$ nor $\ln f^\lambda$ lies in $\mathcal{A}$. Proposition~3.4 of \cite{sniady2006asymptotics} grades Plancherel joint cumulants of elements within $\mathcal{A}$, so the genus filtration controls the $\omega_3$ factor in $\mathrm{Cov}_{\mathrm{Planch}}(f^\lambda, R_4)$ without assigning a finite filtration degree or leading genus term to the product $f^\lambda\,\omega_3(\lambda)$. Bounding the sign of $\mathrm{Cov}_{\mathrm{Planch}}(f^\lambda, R_4)$ therefore requires a direct functional representation of $f^\lambda$ or $\ln f^\lambda$ over Young diagrams rather than finite-genus character asymptotics inside $\mathcal{A}$.
