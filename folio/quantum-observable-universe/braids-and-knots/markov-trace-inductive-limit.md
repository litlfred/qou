The Markov trace extends to a single operator on the inductive limit of braid groups $\mathbf{B}_\infty = \bigcup_n \mathbf{B}_n$. The restrictions $\mathrm{tr}_M\big|_{\mathbf{B}_n}$ yield the per-$n$ trace. The inclusion $\iota_n : \mathbf{B}_n \hookrightarrow \mathbf{B}_{n+1}$ adjoins a non-interacting strand on the right. 

The existence of this global trace is taken as a single hypothesis, from which every per-$n$ trace is derived by restriction, so no separate per-$n$ assumption is needed. This treatment lies on the archimedean side of the substrate wall: it substitutes the substrate parameter $q_0$ and uses real-analytic bounds, while the generic categorical identities remain valid over an abstract coefficient ring.
