Write $\tau_1,\tau_2,\tau_3$ for the Pauli matrices. The **standard basis** of $\mathfrak{su}(2)$ is
```tex
\begin{equation}\label{eq:su2-basis}
  e_a=-\tfrac{i}{2}\tau_a,\qquad a=1,2,3.
\end{equation}
```
Its Lie bracket is
```tex
\begin{equation}\label{eq:su2-commutator}
  [e_a,e_b]=\epsilon_{abc}e_c.
\end{equation}
```
