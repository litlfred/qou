The off-diagonal element $b$ of the Hoefsmit seminormal matrix for the standard representation $(2,1)$ of $H_3(q)$ satisfies:
$$
  b^2 = \frac{[3]_q}{[2]_q^2},
  \qquad
  1 - b^2 = \frac{1}{[2]_q^2}.
$$
These identities hold for every $q$ with $q^4 \neq 1$, the condition under which the seminormal entries $a_{\pm 2} = h/(1 - q^{\mp 4})$ are defined; they do not depend on the substrate parameter. Written directly in terms of $[2]_q = q + q^{-1}$, the relation requires only that $[2]_q$ be invertible.
