Let $\mathbf{b}=(b_0,b_1,b_2)$ be the three branches of the cubic constraint — the sheets that the trefoil monodromy group $S_3\cong D_3$, the Galois group of the cubic, permutes. The **braid involutions** are the reflections **twist** $\sigma_1=(b_0\,b_1)$ and **flip** $\sigma_2=(b_1\,b_2)$, given by the $3\times 3$ permutation matrices
```tex
\begin{equation}\label{eq:perm-matrices}
  \sigma_1=\begin{pmatrix}
    0&1&0\\1&0&0\\0&0&1
  \end{pmatrix},\quad
  \sigma_2=\begin{pmatrix}
    1&0&0\\0&0&1\\0&1&0
  \end{pmatrix}.
\end{equation}
```
Their product is the **rotation**

$$
  \alpha=\sigma_1\sigma_2 .
$$
