Let $R$ be a commutative ring in which $2$ is invertible, and let $q \in R^\times$. Writing the aether Planck quantum $\hbar_q = (q - 1)/q$ gives:
$$
  1 + \hbar_q - \hbar_q^2/2
  \;=\;
  1 + \frac{1 - q^{-2}}{2}
  \;=\;
  \frac{3 - q^{-2}}{2}
  \;=\;
  \frac{3 q^2 - 1}{2 q^2}.
$$

**Scope of the rational-function identity.** The displayed chain is an exact identity of rational functions over $R$, and that is its entire content. It says that two spellings of one quantity agree. It does **not** identify that quantity with any physical correction, and it must not be cited as though it did.
