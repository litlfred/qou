The figure-eight knot $4_1$ (the Higgs mechanism) is amphichiral \cite{witten2011}. Under the bar-involution $\sigma\colon q \mapsto q^{-1}$, its colored Jones polynomial is fixed:
$$
  J_N(4_1; q) = J_N(4_1; q^{-1}) \qquad (\forall N).
$$
Consequently, its Neumann–Zagier complex volume $\mathbb{V}(4_1) = \mathrm{Vol}(4_1) + i\,\mathrm{CS}(4_1)$ is real:
$$
  \mathrm{CS}(4_1) = 0,
  \qquad
  \mathrm{Re}\,\mathbb{V}(4_1) = \mathrm{Vol}(4_1) \approx 2.0299.
$$
The bare Higgs knot contributes only the real volume (mass) face. Its imaginary (decay) face vanishes, proving that $\mathfrak{Q}_\beta$ is not a property of the lone Higgs knot.

For the amphichiral $4_1$, both archimedean saddles (hyperbolic and torus) collapse onto the real axis. This self-duality is an algebraic identity over any commutative ring with a bar-involution.
