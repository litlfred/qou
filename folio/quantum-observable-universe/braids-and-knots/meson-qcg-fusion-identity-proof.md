## Proof

Using $[n]_q = (q^n - q^{-n})/(q - q^{-1})$, multiply both sides by
$(q - q^{-1})^2$ to clear denominators. The identity becomes the
ring identity

$$
  (q^3 - q^{-3})(q^4 - q^{-4})
  \;=\;
  (q - q^{-1}) \cdot
  \bigl((q^2 - q^{-2}) + (q^4 - q^{-4}) + (q^6 - q^{-6})\bigr),
$$

which holds in any field with $q \ne 0$ by direct expansion. $\square$
