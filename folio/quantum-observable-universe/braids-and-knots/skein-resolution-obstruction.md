Assume the [Wigner axiom](#conj:wigner-axiom): the classical-limit obstruction space is $HH^2 \cong k$, spanned by the universal class $w(A)$. Let $L = \hat{\beta}$ be a link with $c$ crossings, and let $\mathcal{R}(L)$ be its Skein resolution tree. The **Skein resolution obstruction** is the restriction of the universal class to the resolution tree:
$$
  \kappa_c^{\mathrm{comp}}(L, q)
  \;=\; 
  \mathrm{res}_{\mathcal{R}}\!\bigl(w(A)\bigr)
   \;\in\; 
  H^2(\mathcal{R}, \mathbb{G}_m).
$$
