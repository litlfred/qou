The phase $\theta$ of [prop:figure-eight-reeb-rotation](figure-eight-reeb-rotation.md) is read as the accumulated $q$-Reeb rotation angle. Restricted to $H_2(q)$, the representation $V_{(2,1)}$ splits into the two one-dimensional channels $V_{(2)}$ and $V_{(1,1)}$, which are the two lines of the seminormal basis on which $\sigma_1$ acts by $q$ and $-q^{-1}$. In this reading these are the two lepton channels, and the $q$-Reeb flow rotates between them. The angle $\theta$ is then the natural invariant of this mixing.
