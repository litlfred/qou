The [twist and flip](#def:braid-twist-flip) are involutions, $\sigma_1^2=\sigma_2^2=\mathrm{id}$. They satisfy the **braid relation** $\sigma_1\sigma_2\sigma_1=\sigma_2\sigma_1\sigma_2$ — the defining relation of the trefoil-knot group $B_3$ — and the rotation $\alpha=\sigma_1\sigma_2$ has order three, $\alpha^3=\mathrm{id}$: it is the cyclic permutation of the three branches, and it is the $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ generator $\alpha$ of [the $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ generators](#def:su2-generators). The **dihedral relation** $\sigma_1\,\alpha\,\sigma_1=\alpha^{-1}$ exhibits $D_3\cong S_3$.
