To connect the finite-dimensional Iwahori–Hecke quotient $H_n(q)$ to the representation category of a quantum group, we construct the quantized universal enveloping algebra $\mathcal{U}_q(\mathfrak{su}(2))$ over $\mathbb{Q}(q)$. Its center is generated by a unique $q$-deformed Casimir operator $C_q$ that reduces to the classical quadratic Casimir as $q \to 1$, and its universal $R$-matrix induces the ribbon-category braiding on $\mathbf{Rep}(\mathcal{U}_q(\mathfrak{su}(2)))$ whose centralizer algebra on $V^{\otimes n}$ is $H_n(q)$ by quantum Schur–Weyl duality.
