At the aether parameter $q_0$, each confined particle and each neutral nuclide $(Z, N)$ of mass number $A = Z + N \geq 1$ is indexed by the Markov-equivalence class of the closure $\widehat{T_w}$ of a canonical Hecke element:

$$
[K]_M \;=\; \bigl[\,\widehat{T_w}\,\bigr], \qquad T_w \in H_{n_0}(q_0), \quad w \in B_{n_0},
$$

with confinement at $q_0$ certified by the [confinement SDP](#prop:confinement-sdp) and constituent generators fixed by the [confined-particle classification](#def:confined-particle). Across confinement levels $\ell \in \{0, 1, 2, 3\}$:

1. **Elementary braids and Borromean nucleons ($\ell = 0, 1$)**: Single-strand vacuum drag ($0_1$), four-component gauge identity ($\mathbf{1}_{B_4}$), trefoil leptons ($3_1 = \widehat{\sigma_1^3} \in B_2$), and flavored Borromean baryons ($\mathcal{B}_3 = \widehat{(\sigma_1\sigma_2^{-1})^3} \in B_3$) determine the parameter-free two-term nucleon mass ratios $m_{\mathfrak{p}}(q_{2\mathrm{t}})/m_e$, $m_{\mathfrak{n}}(q_{2\mathrm{t}})/m_e$, and neutron--proton splitting $\delta m_{\mathfrak{n}\mathfrak{p}}^{\mathrm{QOU}} = 1.29347\;\mathrm{MeV}$.
2. **Few-nucleon cells and the $3\alpha$ Hoyle threshold ($\ell = 2$)**: Closed-shell $H_4(q_0)$ full-twist Wigner-singlet evaluation resolves the $\alpha$-particle cell (${}^{4}\mathrm{He}$), while three-body Geer--Patureau-Mirand--Turaev (GPMT) cumulants separate $\alpha$-unbound ${}^{8}\mathrm{Be}$ ($\Delta B_{2\alpha} < 0$) from bound ${}^{12}\mathrm{C}$ and ${}^{16}\mathrm{O}$.
3. **Three-dimensional $\alpha$-simplicial nuclei ($\ell = 3$)**: Evaluating the close-packed three-dimensional nuclear shell ball with $H_4(q_0)$ GPMT cumulants, Mayer--Jensen spin--orbit splitting, Temperley--Lieb seniority pairing, and face-centered-cubic Coulomb repulsion across all $3{,}558$ evaluated nuclides in AME2020 ($Z = 0\text{--}118$, $A = 1\text{--}295$) yields $1.02\%$ median $|\Delta B/B|$ over all $3{,}556$ bound nuclides ($A \geq 2$) and locates $234$ of $235$ isobaric $\beta$-stability minima ($A = 4\text{--}238$) within $|\Delta Z| \leq 2$.
