The normal form of the Borromean braid $B_3$ encodes its gluon content:

$$
  B_3 = +0.998\,\mathbf{1} - 0.010\sigma_0 + 0.009\sigma_1 + 0.046L_+ - 0.044L_-
$$

The $L_+$ and $L_-$ coefficients measure the gluon content. Their signed sum is small but not zero: $+0.046 - 0.044 = +0.002$. The model reads a zero signed gluon sum as the color-singlet condition, so $B_3$ does not meet it exactly at $q_0$, and no tolerance is fixed.

The coefficient of the identity $\mathbf{1}$, $\approx +0.998$, places $B_3$ perturbatively close to the identity. The gluon terms thus constitute a small correction to free quarks, yielding net$(B_3) \approx 1$.
