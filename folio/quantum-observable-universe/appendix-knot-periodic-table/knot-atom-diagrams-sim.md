Interactive SVG visualization of composite atomic knot diagrams for the four lightest elements ($Z \leq 4$): hydrogen (${}^{1}\mathrm{H}$), helium (${}^{4}\mathrm{He}$), lithium (${}^{7}\mathrm{Li}$), and beryllium (${}^{9}\mathrm{Be}$).

Each diagram couples the nuclear sub-link of flavored Borromean nucleons to the electronic sub-link of trefoil fermions via Hopf linking:

- **Protons** $\mathfrak{p} = \mathcal{B}_3(\mathfrak{u}\mathfrak{u}\mathfrak{d})$: three-strand flavored Borromean baryons ([Borromean baryon](#def:borromean-baryon)).
- **Neutrons** $\mathfrak{n} = \mathcal{B}_3(\mathfrak{u}\mathfrak{d}\mathfrak{d})$: three-strand flavored Borromean baryons.
- **Electrons** $e^- = T_{2,3} = 3_1$: trefoil knots $\widehat{\sigma_1^3}$ in $B_2$ (mirror trefoil $T_{2,3}^{\sigma}$ for positrons).
- **Alpha clusters** $\alpha = 2\mathfrak{p} \oplus 2\mathfrak{n}$: tetrahedral $K_4$ four-nucleon closed sub-links.
- **Hopf links** $\mathcal{L}_H$: connect nuclear and electronic sub-links and pair closed electronic sub-shells (green border $= 1s^2$, blue border $= 2s^2$, brown border $=$ nuclear core).

Complete notation and pairwise transfer-matrix counts appear in [knot diagrams of light atoms](#rem:knot-atom-diagrams).
