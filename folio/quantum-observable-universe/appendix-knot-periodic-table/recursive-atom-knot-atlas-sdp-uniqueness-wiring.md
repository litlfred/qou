## Empty-constraint SDP uniqueness

**What is certified**: the *abstract framework demonstration*
of $T_w$ uniqueness at the empty-constraint SDP for a nucleus's
strand count. The **real per-nucleus SDP** (with non-empty
constraints from the mass formula and multi-term cost) is
per-nucleus substantive open work.

Within that scope, a single parameterised uniqueness theorem indexed
by strand count $n \in \mathbb{N}$ covers every atlas entry once $A$
(equivalently $n = 3A$) is fixed. Take $^4$He: with mass number
$A = 4$ the strand count is $n = 3A = 12$, and instantiation at
$3A = 12$ yields the uniqueness of the canonical $T_w$ knot operator
at the empty-constraint SDP demonstration level.

This parameterised result stratifies across four coverage layers —
from the ten tracked light nuclei, through per-element and
per-stable-isotope coverage, down to arbitrary strand count
$n \in \mathbb{N}$:

| Granularity | Coverage |
|---|---|
| Tracked nuclei | $^2$H, $^3$H, $^3$He, $^4$He, $^6$Li, $^9$Be, $^{11}$B, $^{12}$C, $^{14}$N, $^{16}$O |
| Per-element (AME2020 ref) | 118 elements H..Og |
| Per-stable-isotope | 252 stable nuclides H..Bi |
| Generic | Any $n \in \mathbb{N}$ |
