Each nucleon-pair crossing in the factored skein transfer matrix carries a baseline crossing weight $c$ and an isospin-dependent skein deformation coefficient $d \propto q_0 - q_0^{-1}$, distinguishing electromagnetic proton--proton repulsion from strong attraction.

| Nucleon pair | $c$ | $d$ | Channel character |
|:-------------|:---:|:---:|:------------------|
| $\mathfrak{p}$--$\mathfrak{p}$ | $1$ | $0$ | Electromagnetic repulsion |
| $\mathfrak{n}$--$\mathfrak{n}$ | $1$ | $-(q_0 - q_0^{-1})$ | Pure strong attraction |
| $\mathfrak{p}$--$\mathfrak{n}$ (mixed) | $1$ | $-\tfrac{1}{2}(q_0 - q_0^{-1})$ | Isospin-mixed attraction |
