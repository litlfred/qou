Varying the aether parameter continuously along $q \in [1, q_0]$ connects the classical undeformed symmetric-group limit ($q = 1$) to the physical Mayer--Jensen periodic structure at $q_0 \approx 1.10997858$:

1. **Classical isotropic limit ($q \to 1$)**: Both the Hecke step $h = q - q^{-1}$ and the spin--orbit coupling strength $\kappa_{\mathrm{SO}} = (q - 1)^2$ vanish as $q \to 1$. High-$j$ intruder orbitals remain degenerate with their parent harmonic-oscillator shell $N_{\mathrm{osc}}$, producing pure isotropic oscillator closures $(N_{\mathrm{osc}} + 1)(N_{\mathrm{osc}} + 2)$ at cumulative proton counts $2, 8, 20, 40, 70, 112, 168$ with zero spin--orbit magic splits at $28, 50, 82, 126$. Simultaneously, the photon--electron transfer-matrix root $\lambda_+(q) \to 1$ ([shell structure eigenvalues](#prop:shell-structure-eigenvalues)) collapses Rydberg shell separation.
2. **Onset of spin--orbit intruder splitting ($1 < q \leq q_0$)**: As $q$ increases from $1$ toward $q_0$, the $2\mathbf{l} \cdot \mathbf{s} = \ell$ aligned intruder orbital ($j = \ell + \tfrac{1}{2}$) descends across the $N_{\mathrm{osc}}$ oscillator gap ([shell-transition knot](#conj:shell-transition-knot)), detaching $1f_{7/2}$ to form the $Z = 28$ closure and lowering $1g_{9/2}$, $1h_{11/2}$, and $1i_{13/2}$ to establish the closures at $Z = 50, 82, 126$.
3. **Physical specialization ($q = q_0$)**: At $q = q_0$, the two-, three-, and four-body modified-trace cumulants $(b_2, w_3, w_4^{(H_4)})$, Temperley--Lieb pairing gap $\Delta_{\mathrm{TL}}$, Compton Fermi asymmetry $a_{\mathrm{sym}}$, and Bring--Schwinger fine-structure constant $\alpha_{\mathrm{EM}}(q_0)$ lock onto the values tabulated in [knot-periodic table of the elements](#prose:knot-periodic-table), while constituent knot diagrams follow [knot diagrams of light atoms](#rem:knot-atom-diagrams).
