With notation as in [exceptional divisor](#def:exceptional-divisor), fix the substrate parameter $q$ ([substrate parameter](#def:substrate-parameter)) and set $\phi \in [0,\pi]$, $\psi \in [0, 2\pi/3]$. We define the **color sector coordinates** — the **red**, **green**, and **blue** sector embeddings — by

```tex
\begin{align*}
  \mathfrak{r}(\phi,\psi)
  &=  O + R\bigl(\sin\phi \bigl(\cos(\psi + q\phi) T_1
         + \sin(\psi + q\phi) T_2\bigr) - \cos\phi N\bigr),\\
  \mathfrak{g}(\phi,\psi)
  &=  \mathfrak{r}\!\bigl(\phi,\psi + \tfrac{2\pi}{3}\bigr),\\
  \mathfrak{b}(\phi,\psi)
  &=  \mathfrak{r}\!\bigl(\phi,\psi + \tfrac{4\pi}{3}\bigr).
\end{align*}
```

The summand $q\phi$ is the **color twist**.
