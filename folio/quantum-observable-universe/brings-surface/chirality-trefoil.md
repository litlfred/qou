The chirality of the observable universe—manifest in the three colour charges of quantum chromodynamics—is encoded in the symmetry group of the descended surface $M_0$. [genus-maximal :refterm[descent]{#descent}](#prop:genus-maximal-descent) identifies $M_0$ as a genus-$4$ Riemann surface. The construction requires a genus-$4$ surface whose automorphism group contains three subgroups isomorphic to $S_3$. These are quotients of the trefoil knot group $\pi_1(S^3 \setminus K) \cong \langle x, y \mid x^2 = y^3 \rangle$, and they correspond to the three colour charges (red, green, blue). These $S_3$ subgroups generate a subgroup of the monodromy group. The remaining generators of the full automorphism group act by conjugation, permuting the three $S_3$ subgroups among themselves.
