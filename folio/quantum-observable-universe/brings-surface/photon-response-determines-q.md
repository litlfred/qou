*Let $K$ be a field, $q \in K$ with $q \neq 0$ and $q^2 + 1 \neq 0$, $h = q - q^{-1}$, and let $\mathrm{tr}_M$ be the Markov trace on $H_3(q)$ with parameter $z = q^3/(q^2+1)$. For every [photon](#def:photon),*

$$
  \mathrm{tr}_M\,\gamma(\varphi_0, \varphi_1, \varphi_{01}) = 1 + z\,(\varphi_0 + \varphi_1) + z\,(z - h)\,\varphi_{01}.
$$

*If $p \in K$ with $p \neq 0$, $p^2 + 1 \neq 0$ gives the same function of $(\varphi_0, \varphi_1, \varphi_{01})$, then $p = q$ or $q^4 + 1 = 0$.*

**Proof.** The Markov rules give $\mathrm{tr}_M(\sigma_i) = z$, and $\mathrm{tr}_M(\sigma_0\sigma_1^{-1}) = \mathrm{tr}_M\bigl(\sigma_0(\sigma_1 - h)\bigr) = z^2 - hz$. Equal functions have equal coefficients, so $p$ and $q$ give the same $z$ and the same $z(z - h)$, hence the same $h$. From $q - q^{-1} = p - p^{-1}$ follows $(q - p)(qp + 1) = 0$. If $p = -q^{-1}$, equality of $q^3/(q^2+1)$ and $p^3/(p^2+1)$ reduces to $(q^4 + 1)(q^2 + 1) = 0$. $\square$
