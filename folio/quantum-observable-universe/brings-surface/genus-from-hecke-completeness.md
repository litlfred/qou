Let $N(g)$ be the number of torus knots $T_{r,s}$ with $r \in \{2, 3\}$ whose Seifert genus $(r-1)(s-1)/2$ is at most $g$. Then $g = 4$ is the least genus at which $N(g) \geq 5$, the number of irreducibles of $H_2(q) \oplus H_3(q)$.

This is a statement about the *count* of available evaluations, and it is all that is proved here. It does **not** say that the evaluations available at $g = 4$ span the $5$-dimensional representation: $N(4) = 6$ vectors in a $5$-dimensional space need not span it, and here they provably do not.
