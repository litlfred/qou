Writing $\zeta_5 = e^{2\pi i/5}$ for a primitive fifth root of unity, the ramification locus of the double cover $\mathcal{X}_5 \to \mathbb{P}^1$ of the [hyperelliptic quintic](#def:hyperelliptic-quintic) consists of the six branch points

$$
  \{x_0, x_1, x_2, x_3, x_4, \infty\}  =
   \{1, \zeta_5, \zeta_5^2, \zeta_5^3, \zeta_5^4, \infty\}.
$$

Five distribute symmetrically on the unit circle; the last lies at infinity. By Riemann–Hurwitz, the genus of $\mathcal{X}_5$ is

$$
  g(\mathcal{X}_5)  =  \frac{6 - 2}{2}  =  2.
$$

Thus $\mathcal{X}_5$ is a hyperelliptic Riemann surface of genus $2$ in the Bolza family \cite{bolza1887}.

**Relation to Bring's surface.** The coordinate $x$ on $\mathcal{X}_5$ is a $\mathbb{Z}_5$-equivariant observable. We identify it with the first Lagrange resolvent $w_1$ of [the Lagrange resolvent transform](#prop:lagrange-resolvent-transform), restricted to the cyclic subgroup $\mathbb{Z}_5 \subset S_5$ of [Bring's surface](#def:brings-surface). Under this identification, the five branch points $\zeta_5^k$ are the cyclic images of a single $\mathbb{Z}_5$-orbit.
