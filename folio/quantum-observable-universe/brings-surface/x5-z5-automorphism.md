Let $\mathcal{X}_5 \colon y^2 = x^5 - 1$ be the hyperelliptic quintic of Definition [def:hyperelliptic-quintic](#def:hyperelliptic-quintic). The map

$$
  \phi_5 \colon \mathcal{X}_5 \longrightarrow \mathcal{X}_5,
  \qquad
  \phi_5(x, y)  =  (\zeta_5 x,\, y),
  \qquad
  \zeta_5  =  e^{2\pi i/5},
$$

with $\zeta_5$ a primitive fifth root of unity, is an automorphism of $\mathcal{X}_5$ of order $5$. It generates a cyclic group $\langle \phi_5 \rangle \cong \mathbb{Z}_5$ inside $\mathrm{Aut}(\mathcal{X}_5)$.
