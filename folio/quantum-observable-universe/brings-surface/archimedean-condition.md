A quantum observable universe $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ over $R$ ([quantum observable universe](#def:quantum-observable-universe), [QU-to-QOU lifting](#thm:qu-to-qou)) satisfies the **Descartes condition** if the base curve $M_0$ of its archimedean realisation has genus four,

$$
  g(M_0) = 4, \qquad \text{equivalently} \qquad \chi_{\mathrm{eu}}(M_0) = 2 - 2g(M_0) = -6 .
$$

This requires $6 \in R^{\times}$ (i.e. $\mathrm{char}(R) \nmid 6$). By [QU-to-QOU lifting](#thm:qu-to-qou)(3--4), this forces $\kappa_n \neq 0$ for all $n$ and produces $6$ branch points in the geometric :refterm[descent]{#descent} (with total ramification $R_{\mathrm{tot}} = 2d + 6$). A quantum observable universe satisfying this condition is called a *Descartes universe*.

The condition is a statement about the base, not about the substrate object. The number $\chi_{\mathrm{eu}}(M_0)$ is the topological Euler characteristic of a curve ([the three Euler characteristics](#rem:three-euler-characteristics)); the categorical trace $\mathrm{tr}(\mathrm{id}_A) = \dim_q(A)$ is a different quantity and does not enter the condition.
