*Let the darts be the $120$ elements of $S_5$, let $\sigma$ be right multiplication by $S = (1\,2\,3\,4)$ and $\alpha$ right multiplication by $A = (0\,1)$. Then $(\sigma, \alpha)$ is a connected combinatorial map, $S_5$ acts on it freely on darts by left multiplication, and*

$$
  \chi = \#\mathrm{cycles}(\sigma) - \#\mathrm{cycles}(\alpha) + \#\mathrm{cycles}(\sigma\alpha) = 30 - 60 + 24 = -6 .
$$

*Its genus is $4$, and it agrees with the topological value of [the Euler characteristic of Bring's surface](#lem:brings-euler-characteristic).*
