The **hyperelliptic quintic** $\mathcal{X}_5$ is the smooth projective completion of the affine curve

$$
  \mathcal{X}_5 \colon y^2 =  x^5 - 1
  \qquad
  \text{in } \mathbb{A}^2(\mathbb{C}),
$$

viewed as a double cover of $\mathbb{P}^1$ via the projection $(x, y) \mapsto x$. Its **hyperelliptic involution** is $\iota \colon (x, y) \mapsto (x, -y)$.
