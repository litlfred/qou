Under the identification of $x$ with the first Lagrange resolvent $w_1$ ([prop:lagrange-resolvent-transform](#prop:lagrange-resolvent-transform)), the automorphism $\phi_5$ is the diagonalised action of the cyclic generator $\sigma$ on $w_1$ via $\phi_5 \leftrightarrow \sigma$ with $\sigma(w_1) = \zeta_5^{-1} w_1$. The orbit $\{x, \zeta_5 x, \zeta_5^{2} x, \zeta_5^{3} x, \zeta_5^{4} x\}$ forms a single $\mathbb{Z}_5$-orbit of size five, matching the five finite branch points of the double cover.
