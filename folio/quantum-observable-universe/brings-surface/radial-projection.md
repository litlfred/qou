Restrict a Briggs--Klein affine chart ([Briggs--Klein chart](#def:briggs-klein-chart)) to the interval $r\in(0,1)$ and project Bring's surface onto this radial coordinate. This yields a fibration over the base manifold $M$ with trivial bundle $\mathcal{S}=\mathcal{S}_0$. At each $r$, the coordinate $w_1$ restricts to a circle of radius $\sqrt{r}$ in the complex plane $\mathbb{C}_{w_1}$. This circle constitutes the orbit of the $C_5$ symmetry: the rotation $w_1\mapsto\zeta w_1$ moves the point along this circle without altering $r$.
