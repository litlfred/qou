The Levi form $L$ must be preserved under $\mathrm{Sp}(1)$ conjugation. Since
$$
  \lVert\boldsymbol{\omega}'\rVert^2
  =\lVert\boldsymbol{\omega}\rVert^2
  =\omega_1\bar{\omega}_1+\omega_2\bar{\omega}_2+\omega_3\bar{\omega}_3,
$$
the quadric constraint remains satisfied and the horizontal self-duality $F_A|_H={*_H}F_A|_H$ is preserved. The mass of a stable vortex is recovered as the residue of the interaction between the growth profiles:
```tex
\begin{equation}\label{eq:mass-residue}
  m  \propto 
  \oint_{\varrho}
  \lVert\nabla_A q\rVert\cdot
  \lVert\Omega_1\wedge\Omega_{2,3}\rVert,
\end{equation}
```
where $\Omega_k$ is the curvature form restricted to the $\omega_k$ sector. When $q=1$, the $\omega_k$ commute and $m=0$. When $q\to 0$, maximal twist traps energy in the $\omega_1$ spatial filament.
