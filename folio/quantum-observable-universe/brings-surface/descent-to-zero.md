By the :refterm[descent]{#descent} theorem ([Descartes universe](#thm:descartes)), the study may be restricted to dimension-zero manifolds equipped with bundles $P$ and $\mathcal{S}$. At a point $p\in M_0$, the substrate parameter $q(p)= 1 + \lvert\langle L_\varTheta, F_A^{+}\rangle\rvert(p)$ ([substrate parameter](#def:substrate-parameter)) assigns a specific quantum constant $q\in[1,\infty)$ determined by $P$ and $\mathcal{S}$. Here $\mathcal{S}$ represents the totality of fermionic matter packed into the fibre at $p$. The trivial bundle $\mathcal{S}=\mathcal{S}_0$ corresponds to empty space, where every point's location is exactly known. A non-trivial bundle $\mathcal{S}$ fills spacetime with fermions, entering the regime of the quantum Bring's surface whose underlying geometry is non-commutative ([Section](#sec:quantum-brings)).
