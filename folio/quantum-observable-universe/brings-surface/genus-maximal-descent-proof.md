
The Descartes condition gives $\chi_{\mathrm{eu}}(M_0) = \chi(M_0, V) = -6$ ([the three Euler characteristics](#rem:three-euler-characteristics)). Under the fibre functor $\tau$ of the archimedean realisation, the fundamental object maps to the associated rank-$2$ vector bundle $V = P \times_{\mathrm{SU}(2)} K^2$ over $M_0$ with sheaf Euler characteristic $\chi(M_0, V) = -6$. Since $G = \mathrm{SU}(2)$, the determinant bundle $\det(V)$ is trivial, so $\deg(V) = 0$. By Riemann--Roch on the compact Riemann surface $M_0$, $\chi(M_0, V) = \deg(V) + \mathrm{rank}(V)(1-g) = 2(1-g) = \chi_{\mathrm{eu}}(M_0)$. Setting $2(1-g) = -6$ gives $g = 4$.
