Let $\lambda$ be the Frobenius form of $A$ and, for a point $p$ in the spectral fiber, let $\mathcal{L}_p : T_p^{1,0}A \times T_p^{0,1}A \to \mathbb{C}$ be the Levi form at $p$. The **matter locus** $\mathrm{Sing}(\lambda) \subset \mathrm{Spec}(A)$ is the set of points $p$ with $\mathcal{L}_p \neq 0$.
