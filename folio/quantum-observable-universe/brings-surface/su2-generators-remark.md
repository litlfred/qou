Every finite subgroup of $\mathrm{SO}(3)$ lifts via the double cover $\mathrm{SU}(2)\twoheadrightarrow\mathrm{SO}(3)$ to a binary subgroup of $\mathrm{SU}(2)$; for $S_3$ this lift is $\mathrm{Dic}_3$ ([generators of $\mathrm{Dic}_3$](#def:su2-generators)). The generators satisfy $\alpha^3=\beta^2=-I$, the dihedral relation $\beta\alpha\beta^{-1}=\alpha^{-1}$, and the commutator relation $[\beta,\alpha]=\alpha^{-2}$.
