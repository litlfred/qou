Over the smooth curve $\Sigma_4$, the cyclotomic resolvent basis $\{\omega_k\}$ carries a quaternionic $\mathrm{Sp}(1)$ structure and a tetraplectic form on the eight-dimensional real homology $H_1(\Sigma_4, \mathbb{R}) \cong \mathbb{H}^2$. This section constructs the quaternionic spectral resolvents, the Reeb quaternionic twist on the Bring cone, and the Seiberg–Witten abelianisation along the exceptional divisors, and concludes by extracting the Descartes elliptic curve $\mathcal{E}_q$ whose golden-ratio degeneration anchors the $\chi = -6$ condition.
