*Let $\pi \colon \Sigma_4 \to \mathbb{P}^1$ be a branched covering of degree $4$ from a genus-$4$ curve onto the projective line. Its total ramification is fixed by the Riemann--Hurwitz formula:*

$$
  R_{\mathrm{tot}}
  = 2g(\Sigma_4) - 2 - 4(2g(\mathbb{P}^1) - 2)
  = 6 + 8
  = 14.
$$

*The existence of such a covering, and the claim that its branch locus has exactly $6$ points, are hypotheses of this lemma, not consequences of it.*
