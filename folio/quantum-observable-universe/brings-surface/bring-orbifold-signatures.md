*Let $H$ run over the $19$ conjugacy classes of subgroups of $S_5$, and let $c$ be the central reflection of [the reflection parity](#prop:bring-reflection-parity).*

*(i) The quotient $\mathcal{B}/H$ is a closed orientable orbifold whose cone points are determined by the class distribution of $H$. If $k_{(2)}, k_{(22)}, k_{(4)}, k_{(5)}$ count the transpositions, double transpositions, $4$-cycles and $5$-cycles of $H$, it has*

$$
  \frac{4k_{(22)} - 2k_{(4)} + 12k_{(2)}}{|H|} \text{ cone points of order } 2, \qquad \frac{4k_{(4)}}{|H|} \text{ of order } 4, \qquad \frac{5k_{(5)}}{|H|} \text{ of order } 5 .
$$

*So $\mathcal{B}/S_5 = (0;2,4,5)$, $\mathcal{B}/A_5 = (0;2,5,5)$, $\mathcal{B}/S_4 = (0;2,2,2,4)$, $\mathcal{B}/F_{20} = (0;4,4,5)$, $\mathcal{B}/A_4 = (1;2)$, $\mathcal{B}/(S_3\times S_2) = (0;2^5)$ and $\mathcal{B}/S_3 = (0;2^6)$.*

*(ii) The quotient $\mathcal{B}/(H\times\langle c\rangle)$ has a mirror if and only if $H$ contains a transposition or a double transposition, and then its boundary is one curve. For the seven recovery subgroups it is a disc with corner reflectors $2,4,5$ ($S_5$), a disc with corners $2,5,5$ ($A_5$), a disc with one cone point $2$ and corners $2,4$ ($S_4$), a disc with cone $4$ and corner $5$ ($F_{20}$), a Möbius band with one corner $2$ ($A_4$), a disc with cone $2$ and corners $2,2,2$ ($S_3 \times S_2$), and a disc with cones $2,2,2$ ($S_3$).*

*(iii) Every one of the $38$ quotients satisfies the orbifold Euler characteristic formula $\chi(X) - \sum(1 - 1/m) - \tfrac12\sum(1 - 1/n) = \chi(\mathcal{B})/|K| = -6/|K|$.*
