1. **Lepton generations.** The electron is the braid word $\sigma_0\sigma_1\sigma_0 \in B_3$, whose closure is the Hopf link; the trefoil $T_{2,3}$ is kept as a backup electron. The torus-knot tower $T_{2,3}$, $T_{2,5}$, $T_{2,7}$ at Reeb degrees $1, 2, 3$ is the backup lepton family. Within that family only the first two are $S_5$-equivariant under the $\mathbb{Z}_n \hookrightarrow S_5$ criterion; the third embeds topologically but breaks its $\mathbb{Z}_7$ symmetry. $T_{2,9}$ saturates $g = 4$ (symmetry-broken), and $T_{2,11}$ is genus-excluded, precluding a fourth member.
2. **Quark sector.** The $(3,n)$-family generators $T_{3,4}$ and $T_{3,5}$ act as $H^{0,1}$ force carriers. $T_{3,4}$ encodes Hopf-squared color binding, while $T_{3,5}$ mediates the weak interaction. As a braid, $T_{3,5} = (\sigma_0\sigma_1)^5$ is the electron word cubed times one letter, $(\sigma_0\sigma_1\sigma_0)^3\sigma_1$ in $B_3$, and it is the only member of the family $T_{3,3m+2}$ within the genus bound that reaches $g = 4$. The three-strand meridional winding resonantly threads the three color tubes.
3. **Saturation.** The seventh generator $T_{2,9}$ at genus $4$ exactly saturates Bring's surface. New particle types would require composite construction by linking existing knots.
4. **Hyperbolic knots.** The figure-eight $4_1$ and three-twist $5_2$ are not generators. They arise from Skein crossing changes applied to linked torus-knot configurations, inheriting nonzero hyperbolic volume and bare mass from the linking process.

The bound $N(\chi) = 1 - \chi = 7$ on the number of generators equals the number of handles ($g = 4$) plus the three independent Seifert genus values available within them. The recursive brane tower does not increase the genus, making the generator count stable across all levels.
