Bring's curve $\Sigma_4 = \{\sum_{j=0}^4 x_j = \sum_{j=0}^4 x_j^2 = \sum_{j=0}^4 x_j^3 = 0\} \subset \mathbb{P}^4$ is the unique genus-four curve admitting a faithful action of the symmetric group $S_5$. This section verifies that $\Sigma_4$ equipped with its canonical spinor bundle and Reeb connection forms a quantum observable universe $(\mathbf{C}, \varTheta, G, \mathcal{S})$: we compute its Euler characteristic $\chi(\Sigma_4) = -6$ both via the intersection degree of the $(1,2,3)$ complete intersection in $\mathbb{P}^4$ and via the Riemann–Hurwitz formula on its পাঁচ-sheeted branched cover of $\mathbb{P}^1$, compare the topological, coherent, and categorical Euler characteristics, and construct the five-strand braid representation $B_5 \to \mathrm{Aut}(H_1(\Sigma_4, \mathbb{Z}))$.
