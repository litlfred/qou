Let $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ be the Descartes universe with $\chi(A) = -6$ and aether parameter $q$. Set $s = \sqrt{q}$. The color elliptic fiber ([color elliptic](#prop:color-elliptic)) of Bring's surface at spacetime ratio $z = s - s^{-1}$ is the elliptic curve

$$
  \mathcal{E}_q  : 
  y^2 =  x^3
  +  \frac{2s^4 + s^3 - 2s^2 - s + 2}{3s^2}x
  +  \frac{5(s^2 + s - 1)(4s^4 - s^3 - 4s^2 + s + 4)}{27s^3}
$$

with Weierstrass coefficients expressible directly in $q$:

$$
  p(q) = 
  \frac{2(q^2 - q + 1)}{3q}
  +  \frac{q - 1}{3\sqrt{q}},
$$

$$
  r(q) = 
  \frac{20(q - 1)^3}{27q^{3/2}}
  +  \frac{5(q - 1)^2}{9q}
  +  \frac{5(\sqrt{q} - 1/\sqrt{q})}{9}
  +  \frac{20}{27}.
$$
