This identification is categorical rather than a morphism of curves. By Riemann–Hurwitz, there is no non-constant curve morphism $\mathcal{X}_5 \to \mathcal{B}_{\mathrm{Br}}$ ($g = 2 < 4$). Furthermore, $\mathcal{B}_{\mathrm{Br}}$ has no genus-$2$ Galois quotient because $S_5$ has no subgroups of index $2$ or $3$ with the requisite ramification. The proposition instead asserts a compatibility of $\mathbb{Z}_5$-graded observables under the Lagrange ordering. The same categorical weight-$(-1)$ coordinate parametrises the single $\mathrm{U}(1)$ phase tube of [the single $\mathrm{U}(1)$ phase tube](#rem:u1-phase-tube) and the first Fourier mode of Bring's geometry.
