The four QOU data of [Bring's surface](#prop:brings-is-qou) are verified as follows.

1. *Quantum universe.*  Set $\mathbf{C} = \mathrm{Rep}(\mathrm{SU}_{q}(2))$ over $R$, $A = \mathcal{O}_q(\mathrm{SU}(2))$ with Frobenius form given by the Haar state, and $\tau$ the forgetful functor to $R$-$\mathrm{Mod}$. The differential calculus $\Omega^1(A)$ is the Woronowicz $3$-dimensional calculus.
2. *Observability.* $\mathrm{Hom}(\mathbf{1}, \mathrm{Ad}(A)) = 0$ by the quantum Schur lemma. $\mathrm{SU}_q(2)$ has no non-trivial invariants in the adjoint representation.
3. *Quantum lifting.*  The brane tower satisfies the quantum lifting condition ([quantum lifting](#prop:yekutieli-lifting)). By item 3 of [QU-to-QOU lifting](#thm:qu-to-qou), the Kummer obstruction $\chi(A) \bmod n$ with $\chi_{\mathrm{eu}}(M_0) = -6$ vanishes exactly for $n \in \{1, 2, 3, 6\}$ and persists for every $n \nmid 6$; the [motivic perpetual obstruction](#thm:motivic-perpetual-obstruction) theorem gives the matching statement at the places $p \nmid 6$.
4. *Euler characteristic.*  By [QU-to-QOU lifting](#thm:qu-to-qou), $\chi_{\mathrm{eu}}(M_0) = 2 - 2g = -6$ for the base curve; this is the topological Euler characteristic, not the categorical trace $\mathrm{tr}(\mathrm{id}_A)$ ([the three Euler characteristics](#rem:three-euler-characteristics)).
5. *Geometric :refterm[descent]{#descent}* (for $n = 1$, $\dim M_S = 3$):

   $$
     M_S \xrightarrow{ \mathrm{Reeb}} \Sigma_4
     \xrightarrow{\pi} \mathbb{P}(V)
     \xrightarrow{ \mathrm{branch}} \{6 \text{ points}\}.
   $$

   The degree-$4$ covering $\pi$ has $6$ branch points with total ramification $R_{\mathrm{tot}} = 14$ (Riemann–Hurwitz). The ramification profile is determined by the $S_5$-symmetry.
