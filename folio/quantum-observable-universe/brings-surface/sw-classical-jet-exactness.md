Let $X^{(n)}$ denote the $n$-th total $z$-derivative of the fibre coordinate $X$ on the colour spectral curve $\Sigma$ of the [MI--SW abelianisation](#prop:mi-sw-abelianisation), and let $\lambda_{\mathrm{SW}}^{(n)} = X^{(n)}(z)\,dz$ be the classical-jet prolongation of the Seiberg--Witten differential. For every $n \ge 1$,

$$
\lambda_{\mathrm{SW}}^{(n)} \;=\; d\bigl(X^{(n-1)}\bigr)
$$

is an exact meromorphic differential on $\Sigma$. Consequently $\mathrm{Res}_p\,\lambda_{\mathrm{SW}}^{(n)} = 0$ at every point $p \in \Sigma$, and $\oint_\gamma \lambda_{\mathrm{SW}}^{(n)} = 0$ over every closed cycle $\gamma$ avoiding the poles of $X^{(n-1)}$. In particular the residue-sum period of the [SW period framework](#prop:sw-period-residue-sum) vanishes identically at every vertical degree $n \ge 1$ under the classical-jet rule, so the classical-jet prolongation cannot realise the nonzero binding periods $a_D^{(m)} = \mathrm{lw}(m-1) = \frac{1}{m}\sum_{\lambda \vdash m} \dim_q(\lambda)$; the prolongation carrying the binding periods must therefore be non-classical (q-difference).

## Formal-residue structure

The load-bearing invariant holds on the formal-residue model over a generic commutative ring: a local Laurent expansion is its coefficient function, the residue is the coefficient of $t^{-1}$, and a termwise derivative has vanishing $t^{-1}$ coefficient. Composing this vanishing with the residue-sum shape of [SW period residue sum](#prop:sw-period-residue-sum) rules out the nonzero classical binding period $I(m)/m$ (involution numbers). The statement is also checked exactly at $z = \infty$ from the sheet expansions, for jets $n = 1\ldots4$.
