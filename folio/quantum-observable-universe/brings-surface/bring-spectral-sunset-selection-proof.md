$N(\rho,\sigma,\tau) = \langle \chi_\rho\chi_\sigma\chi_\tau, 1\rangle$, because the characters of $S_5$ are real. It is computed exactly from the character table, and the counts $200$ forbidden and $143$ allowed are checked by enumeration. The integral is invariant under $S_5 \times \langle c\rangle$, so $(u,v,w) \mapsto \int uvw$ is an invariant trilinear form. On $\rho_a \otimes \rho_b \otimes \rho_c$ it vanishes when $N = 0$, by Schur's lemma. Since $\varphi \circ c = \pm\varphi$, it also vanishes when the product of the three parities is $-1$. Splitting each of the three sums over the cut by type and parity gives the sum of partial sunsets. Each forbidden partial sunset is zero term by term. $\square$
