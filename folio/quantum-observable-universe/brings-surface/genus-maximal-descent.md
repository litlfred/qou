Let $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ be a quantum observable universe satisfying the Descartes condition $\chi_{\mathrm{eu}}(M_0) = \chi(M_0, V) = -6$ ([Descartes condition](#def:archimedean-condition)). If the geometric :refterm[descent]{#descent} produces a compact one-complex-dimensional base $M_0$, then $M_0$ is a Riemann surface of genus $g = 4$.

More precisely: the sheaf Euler characteristic $\chi(M_0, V) = -6$ of the rank-two degree-zero $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ state bundle $V \to M_0$, coinciding with the topological Euler characteristic $\chi_{\mathrm{eu}}(M_0) = 2 - 2g$ ([the three Euler characteristics](#rem:three-euler-characteristics)), determines the topological type of the base curve. Under the fibre functor $\tau$ of any archimedean realisation, Riemann--Roch on $M_0$ forces $2(1 - g) = -6$, hence $g = 4$. This is the maximal genus consistent with the Hurwitz bound $|\mathrm{Aut}(\Sigma)| \leq 84(g - 1) = 252$.
