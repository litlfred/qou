Let $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ be a quantum observable universe satisfying the Descartes condition $\chi_{\mathrm{eu}}(M_0) = -6$ ([Descartes condition](#def:archimedean-condition)). If the geometric :refterm[descent]{#descent} produces a compact one-complex-dimensional base $M_0$, then $M_0$ is a Riemann surface of genus $g = 4$.

More precisely: if the categorical Euler characteristic $\mathrm{tr}(\mathrm{id}_A) = -6$ equals the topological Euler characteristic $\chi_{\mathrm{eu}}(M_0)$ of the base curve, then Gauss--Bonnet on $M_0$ gives $2(1 - g) = -6$, hence $g = 4$. That identification is a hypothesis here, not a consequence: both Lean formalisations take $\chi = -6$ and $2(1 - g) = \chi$ as inputs and derive only $g = 4$ and the bound below. The Hurwitz bound $|\mathrm{Aut}(\Sigma)| \leq 84(g - 1)$ then gives $|\mathrm{Aut}(M_0)| \leq 252$. The bound holds for every genus $g \geq 2$, so it does not single out $g = 4$; the genus is fixed by the Euler characteristic alone. Bring's curve, the genus-4 curve used later, has automorphism group $S_5$ of order $120$, below the bound.
