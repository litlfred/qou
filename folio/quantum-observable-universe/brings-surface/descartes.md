Let $\mathbf{C}_{-6}$ denote the category of quantum observable universes over $R$ at a fixed value of the substrate parameter $q$ satisfying the Descartes condition $\chi_{\mathrm{eu}}(M_0) = -6$, with morphisms given by QOU maps that preserve the Euler characteristic. Bring's surface $\Sigma_4$ is a terminal object in $\mathbf{C}_{-6}$: for every Descartes universe $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ in $\mathbf{C}_{-6}$, there exists a unique morphism to the Bring's QOU.
