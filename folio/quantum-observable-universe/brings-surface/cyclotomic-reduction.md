Let $\mathfrak{p}$ be a prime (not to be confused with a point $p\in M$). Working over the cyclotomic ring

```tex
\begin{equation}\label{eq:cyclotomic-ring}
  \mathbb{Z}_{\zeta_{\mathfrak{p}}}
   := \mathbb{Z}[i,\zeta_{\mathfrak{p}}],
\end{equation}
```

where $\zeta_{\mathfrak{p}}=e^{2\pi i/\mathfrak{p}}$ is a primitive $\mathfrak{p}$-th root of unity, consider the sublocus

$$
  S_{\mathfrak{p}}
   := 
  \Sigma_4\cap\mathbb{Z}_{\zeta_{\mathfrak{p}}}^{5}.
$$

This defines the set of points on Bring's surface whose resolvent coordinates lie in $\mathbb{Z}_{\zeta_{\mathfrak{p}}}$. The cyclic subgroup $C_{\mathfrak{p}}\subset S_5$ generated by the $\mathfrak{p}$-cycle preserves $S_{\mathfrak{p}}$. For any $P\in S_{\mathfrak{p}}$, the image $\sigma^k(P)$ remains in $\mathbb{Z}_{\zeta_{\mathfrak{p}}}^{5}$ because $\sigma$ multiplies by $\zeta_{\mathfrak{p}}^{-k}$ in the resolvent basis ([Lagrange resolvent transform](#prop:lagrange-resolvent-transform)), and $\zeta_{\mathfrak{p}}^{-k}\in\mathbb{Z}_{\zeta_{\mathfrak{p}}}$. This arithmetic compatibility between the cyclic symmetry and the cyclotomic ring exhibits the descent of the $C_5$ action to finite characteristic.
