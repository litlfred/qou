Over an archimedean field $K$ (e.g. $\mathbb{Q}$, $\mathbb{R}$, or $\mathbb{C}$), Bring's surface is a compact Riemann surface of genus $4$. One explicit uniformization: it is the compactification of $\mathbb{H}/H_4(5)$, where $H_4(5)$ is the level-5 congruence subgroup of the Hecke group $H_4$, normal of index $120$; the induced regular map of type $\{5,4\}$ has $30$ vertices, $60$ edges and $24$ faces, so $2 - 2g = 30 - 60 + 24 = -6$ \cite[\S3]{kattan2024}. The unit circle bundle $M_S$ in $T^*\Sigma_4$ is a CR manifold of hypersurface type (real dimension $3$). It provides the archimedean realisation of the quantum observable universe ([$\mathbb{C}$-quantum universe](#ex:c-quantum-universe)).
