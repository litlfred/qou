Let $S$ denote Bring's surface. The CR geometry lives on the unit circle bundle $M_S \subset T^*S$: the Liouville $1$-form restricts to a contact form $\varTheta$ on $M_S$. The natural projection $\pi \colon S \to \mathbb{P}^1(\mathbb{C})$ lifts to a principal $\mathrm{SU}(2)$-bundle $P \to M_S$, yielding an archimedean QOU $(\mathbf{C}, \varTheta, G, \mathcal{S})$.
