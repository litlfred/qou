*On the darts $S_5$ of [the Bring map](#lem:bring-map-euler-characteristic), let $T = (2\,4)$, the unique element with $TST = S^{-1}$, $TAT = A$ and $T^2 = 1$. Then $c(x) = xT$ is an orientation-reversing automorphism of the map that commutes with $S_5$, and the full isometry group is $S_5 \times \langle c \rangle$, of order $240$.*

1. *$c$ fixes no vertex, edge or face, so it acts freely. $\mathcal{B}/c$ is a closed non-orientable surface with $\chi = -3$. This matches the curve: $c$ is complex conjugation, and $\sum x_i^2 = 0$ has no nonzero real solution.*
2. *The sides of the $(2,4,5)$ flag triangle are the mirrors of $(T, c)$, $(ST, c)$ and $(AT, c)$. Their pairwise products are $S^{-1}$, $A$ and $SA$, and $T$ is odd while $ST$ and $AT$ are even. So the four one-dimensional types are boundary problems on the triangle: $[5]^+$ is Neumann on every side, $[5]^-$ is Dirichlet on every side, $[1^5]^+$ is Dirichlet on the edge side and Neumann on the other two, and $[1^5]^-$ is the reverse.*
3. *Every type of [the classification by $S_5$ type](#prop:bring-harmonic-types) splits as $\rho^+ \oplus \rho^-$, and the fourteen types of $S_5 \times C_2$ are exhaustive. The harmonic $1$-cochains are $[2,1^3]^+ \oplus [2,1^3]^-$. The vertex functions are $[5]^+ + [4,1]^+ + [2,1^3]^- + [3,2]^+ + [2^2,1]^+ + [2^2,1]^- + [3,1^2]^-$.*
4. *For any eigenspace of the Laplacian, the fourteen multiplicities $m_\rho^\pm$ are integer combinations of its multiplicities on the seven quotients $\mathcal{B}/H$ of [the classification](#prop:bring-harmonic-types) and on the seven quotients $\mathcal{B}/(H \times \langle c \rangle)$.*
