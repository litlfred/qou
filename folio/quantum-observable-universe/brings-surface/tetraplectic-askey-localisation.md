*Fix the invariant measure of
[the tetraplectic structure](#def:bring-cone-tetraplectic-structure) and let*
$\mathcal{H} = L^2(M, \Omega) = \bigoplus_{\lambda \vdash 5} \mathcal{H}_\lambda$
*be its decomposition into $S_5$-isotypic components. Project those global
states into local coordinate patches near the singularities of
$C(\mathcal{B})$.*

*Conjecturally, the local profiles approximate standard orthogonal families of
the* **Askey scheme** \cite{askey1989qhermite}:

1. *in the radial regimes, Bessel-type wavefunctions;*
2. *in quadratic — harmonic — local approximations, Hermite polynomials.*

*The content of the conjecture is that the alignment is with the Askey
hierarchy specifically, so that the combinatorial index $\lambda$ and the
analytic local model are two views of one object rather than two unrelated
labellings.*

This is stated as framing. No local model has been computed at any singularity
of the cone, and nothing elsewhere in the chapter depends on the claim.
