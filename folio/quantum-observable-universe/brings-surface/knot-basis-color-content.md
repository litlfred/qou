An element of $H_3(q)$ of the form
$$
  \gamma
  = \mathbf{1} + \varphi_0 \sigma_0 + \varphi_1 \sigma_1 + \varphi_{01} \sigma_0\sigma_1^{-1}
$$
is called a photon with color content. Its fourth term is the chiral vertex $\sigma_0\sigma_1^{-1} = \sigma_0\sigma_1 - h\sigma_0$. Setting $\varphi_{01} = 0$ yields a 3-term linear specialization. Given two such specializations $\gamma_1$ and $\gamma_2$, the Gröbner--Shirshov normal form of their product factors into:
$$
  \mathrm{NF}(\gamma_1 \cdot \gamma_2)
  = (1 + \varphi_0\psi_0 + \varphi_1\psi_1) \mathbf{1}
  + (\varphi_0 + \psi_0 + \varphi_0\psi_0h) \sigma_0
  + (\varphi_1 + \psi_1 + \varphi_1\psi_1h) \sigma_1
  + \varphi_0\psi_1 \sigma_0\sigma_1
  + \varphi_1\psi_0 \sigma_1\sigma_0.
$$
The normal form decomposes into the first five entries of the knot basis. Hopf-link terms (word length 2) arise exclusively from cross-color products, while length-1 terms reflect both additive contributions and Hecke self-energy corrections.
