Let $\mathcal{S}_c \cong \mathbb{P}^1(\mathbb{C})$ be the exceptional divisor at a branch point $z_c$ ([Bring's surface smoothness](#rem:brings-smoothness)), and let $(\nabla_A q, L \wedge F^+)$ be the connection--curvature pair of the ([mass identity](#def:mass-identity)). For each vertical degree $n \geq 0$ in the Reeb decomposition, the restriction of the nonabelian multiplicative integral to $\mathcal{S}_c$ abelianises to the $n$-th prolonged Seiberg--Witten period:

```tex
\begin{equation}\label{eq:mi-sw-abelianisation}
  \bigl\lVert
  \mathrm{MI}\!\!\int_{\mathcal{S}_c}
  \bigl(\nabla_A q, L \wedge F^+\bigr)
  \bigr\rVert_{J^n}
  = 
  \bigl\lvert a_D^{(n)}\bigr\rvert
  = 
  \biggl\lvert\oint_{\mathcal{S}_c}\lambda_{\mathrm{SW}}^{(n)}\biggr\rvert,
\end{equation}
```

where $\lVert\cdot\rVert_{J^n}$ denotes the Killing-form norm projected onto the $n$-th jet component, and $\lambda_{\mathrm{SW}}^{(n)}$ is the $n$-th prolonged Seiberg--Witten differential. The unprolonged objects are those of Seiberg and Witten: for $\mathcal{N}=2$ $SU(2)$ gauge theory with matter hypermultiplets, the periods $(a, a_D)$ of a meromorphic differential on a family of elliptic curves determine the exact low-energy metric and the masses of the stable BPS states \cite{seiberg-witten1994b}. Mathematically, a Seiberg--Witten differential is a meromorphic $1$-form $\lambda$ on the total space of an algebraically integrable system with $d\lambda = \sigma$, the symplectic form, so that its periods are special coordinates on the base \cite[\S2]{donagi1997}.

**Classical abelianisation, and what transporting it would require.** In the Seiberg--Witten setting the abelianisation is a statement about spectral curves. For a curve $E$, an effective divisor $D$ and $n\geq 2$, the total space is the moduli of pairs $(V,\varphi)$ with $V$ a rank-$n$ bundle of trivial determinant and $\varphi\colon V\to V\otimes\omega_E(D)$ traceless; the map to the base sends $(V,\varphi)$ to the coefficients of $\det(t\cdot I-\varphi)$, whose zero set is a spectral curve $C_b$ in the total space $S$ of $\omega_E(D)$. For generic $(V,\varphi)$ the sheaf $\operatorname{coker}(\varphi-\lambda\cdot\mathrm{Id})$, with $\lambda$ the tautological $1$-form on $S$, is a line bundle on $C_b$, so the fibre maps to $\operatorname{Pic}(C_b)$ (to a translate of $\operatorname{Prym}(C_b/E)$ for $SL(n)$), and the Seiberg--Witten differential is induced from $\lambda$ \cite[\S3]{donagi1997}. A related abelianisation, on the side of flat connections, is the nonabelianisation map of spectral networks: for a nondegenerate spectral network subordinate to a branched cover $\Sigma\to C$, a twisted flat rank-$1$ connection on $\Sigma$ is carried to a twisted flat rank-$K$ connection on $C$ with flag data \cite[\S10.1, \S10.3]{arxiv-1204.4824v2}. For a single path, the transport of $Y'=A(t)Y$ with $Y(0)=I$ has a Magnus exponent $Y=\exp\Omega(t)$, and the Magnus series for $\Omega$ converges absolutely while $\int_0^t\lVert A(s)\rVert\,ds<\pi$ \cite[\S2.7]{blanes-casas-oteo-ros2009}. None of these three settings is the one of the displayed identity above. Transporting either abelianisation would require checking that the restriction to $\mathcal{S}_c$ produces a spectral cover (a curve, a meromorphic Higgs field on it, and the cover $C_b$), that the multiplicative-integral abelianisation on $\mathcal{S}_c$ is the same operation as the flat-connection one on a branched cover, and that a surface-ordered multiplicative integral reduces there to a path-ordered one, which is the only case the Magnus criterion covers. None of these hypotheses is established in this paper.

**Archimedean specialisation.** What follows sits on the archimedean side of the substrate wall. It invokes real- or complex-analytic constructs—real-valued physical observables, real-analytic bounds, transcendental constants, or numerical evaluation at the substrate parameter $q_0$. The generic categorical/$q$-deformation identities it relies upon remain valid over an abstract coefficient ring; only their archimedean realisation is specialised here.
