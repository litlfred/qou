Jacoby and Lawrence define the slope of a rational homology sphere $M$ as $\sigma(M) = \lim_{n\to\infty} \lambda_n(M)/(n\,\lambda_{n-1}(M))$, where $\lambda_n$ are the coefficients of its Ohtsuki series, and record from Lawrence–Rozansky that for a Seifert-fibred manifold the slope exists and equals $\sigma = \pm \prod_i P_i/\pi^2$ \cite[\S 4.3, p. 65]{jacoby-lawrence2004}. For an integer homology sphere $\pi^2\sigma \in \mathbb{Z}$. They also note that the WRT invariants of the mirror image satisfy $Z_K(\bar M)(q) = Z_K(M)(q^{-1})$ at all roots of unity, so the Ohtsuki series of $\bar M$ is obtained by $q \mapsto q^{-1}$ \cite[\S 4.2, p. 64]{jacoby-lawrence2004}.

For the Poincaré homology sphere $\Sigma(2,3,5)$, the Seifert fibration over the $S^2(2,3,5)$ orbifold in the [icosahedral bridge](#conj:klein-bring-icosahedral-bridge), this gives $|\pi^2\sigma| = 2\cdot 3\cdot 5 = 30$: the coefficients grow like $(30/\pi^2)^n\, n!$, so the series in $h$ has zero radius of convergence and any value away from $h = 0$ needs a resummation. The number $30$ is also the Coxeter number of $E_8$, but the paper does not mention $E_8$, and the agreement is not shown to be more than the arithmetic $2\cdot 3\cdot 5$. The mirror rule is the $q \mapsto q^{-1}$ bar involution stated for a 3-manifold invariant; it says nothing about the value of the series at the real substrate point.
