Let $\sigma$ denote the cyclic generator of $\mathbb{Z}_5$. Its induced action on regular functions is written on the left. On Bring's surface, the first Lagrange resolvent $w_1$ is an eigenvector of $\sigma$ with eigenvalue $\zeta_5^{-1}$ ([prop:lagrange-resolvent-transform](#prop:lagrange-resolvent-transform)). On $\mathcal{X}_5$, the coordinate $x$ is an eigenvector of $\sigma$ with the same eigenvalue ([prop:x5-z5-automorphism](#prop:x5-z5-automorphism)). Consequently, there is a canonical $\mathbb{Z}_5$-equivariant identification

$$
  \mathcal{O}(\mathcal{X}_5)_{\zeta_5^{-1}}
   \overset{\sim}{\longrightarrow}
   \mathcal{O}(\mathcal{B}_{\mathrm{Br}})_{\zeta_5^{-1}},
  \qquad
  x  \longmapsto  w_1,
$$

of the weight-$(-1)$ isotypical components of the coordinate rings. This identification extends to a $\mathbb{Z}_5$-equivariant map of graded rings on the entire $\mathbb{Z}_5$-isotypical decomposition by sending $x^{k} \mapsto w_1^{\,k}$ for $0 \leq k \leq 4$ (with $x^5$ and $w_1^{\,5}$ both invariant).
