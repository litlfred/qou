The cyclic symmetry $w_k \mapsto \zeta^{-k} w_k$ ([Lagrange resolvents](#rem:lagrange-resolvents)) becomes, in color tube coordinates:

```tex
\begin{align}
  \sigma & \longmapsto  \zeta^{-1}\sigma + (\zeta^{-1}-1)z,
    \label{eq:c5-sigma}\\[4pt]
  r & \longmapsto  \zeta^{-2}r + (\zeta^{-2}-1)z,
    \label{eq:c5-r}\\[4pt]
  g & \longmapsto  \zeta^{-3}g + (\zeta^{-3}-1)z,
    \label{eq:c5-g}\\[4pt]
  b & \longmapsto  \zeta^{-4}b + (\zeta^{-4}-1)z.
    \label{eq:c5-b}
\end{align}
```

The $C_5$ action is no longer diagonal: subtracting the affine coordinate $z$ from each resolvent breaks projective homogeneity and introduces an additive $(\zeta^{-k} - 1)z$ shift in the $k$-th channel.
