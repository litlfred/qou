Let $r=\lvert w_1\rvert^2$ denote the squared modulus of the first resolvent coordinate on Bring's surface ([Bring's surface](#def:brings-surface)). The **Briggs--Klein affine chart** is the coordinate system $(w_1,w_2)$ restricted to the open set $0<r<\infty$, equipped with the Hermitian metric inherited from the Fubini--Study metric on $\mathbb{P}^4$.
