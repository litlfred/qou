Let $S_3\cong D_3\subset\mathrm{SO}(3)$. The **binary dihedral group** $\mathrm{Dic}_3\subset\mathrm{SU}(2)$ is the lift of $S_3$ through the double cover $\mathrm{SU}(2)\twoheadrightarrow\mathrm{SO}(3)$, a group of order $12$. Its **generators** are

```tex
\begin{align}
  \alpha &= \exp\!\bigl(\tfrac{2\pi}{3}e_3\bigr)
  = \begin{pmatrix}e^{i\pi/3}&0\\[2pt]0&e^{-i\pi/3}\end{pmatrix},
  \label{eq:su2-3cycle}\\[4pt]
  \beta &= \exp(\pi e_1)
  = \begin{pmatrix}0&i\\[2pt]i&0\end{pmatrix}.
  \label{eq:su2-2cycle}
\end{align}
```
