Bring's surface $\mathcal{B}$ is the genus-$4$ curve cut out by the vanishing of the first three power sums, with maximal automorphism group $S_5$; its affine cone $M = C(\mathcal{B})$ inherits these discrete symmetries ([tetraplectic structure on the Bring cone](#def:bring-cone-tetraplectic-structure)).

Here $m = 1$: every $4$-form on a $4$-manifold is closed, so $d\Omega = 0$ is automatic and the tetraplectic requirement reduces to a nowhere-vanishing volume form. Averaging an arbitrary initial volume form $\omega_0$ over the $S_5 \times \mathbb{Z}_2$ action produces an $S_5$-invariant tetraplectic volume form $\Omega$.

Because $\Omega$ is $S_5$-invariant, $\mathcal{H} = L^2(M, \Omega)$ carries a unitary $S_5$
action and so decomposes into orthogonal isotypic components indexed by the
integer partitions of $5$. This is the entry point to the rest of the chapter:
the continuous geometry of the cone is resolved into the same Young-diagram
data that indexes the Wedderburn blocks elsewhere in the framework.
