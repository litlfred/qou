For any admissible Euler characteristic $\chi_{\mathrm{eu}}$, the category $\mathbf{C}_{\chi_{\mathrm{eu}}}$ of $\mathbb{C}$ quantum observable universes whose base curve satisfies $\chi_{\mathrm{eu}}(M_0) = \chi_{\mathrm{eu}}$ should admit a terminal object determined by the unique maximally symmetric Riemann surface of the corresponding genus. Bring's curve distinguishes the Descartes case $\chi_{\mathrm{eu}}(M_0) = -6$, but analogous universal objects are expected at other values of $\chi$.
