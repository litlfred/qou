Let $z = x_0/x_1 \in \mathbb{P}^1(\mathbb{C})$ be the spacetime ratio ([braid group action](#rem:braid-group-action)(i)) and let $w_k$, $k \in \{0,\dots,4\}$, be the Lagrange resolvents of Bring's surface ([Lagrange resolvent transform](#prop:lagrange-resolvent-transform)). The **color tube coordinates** are the shifted spectral coordinates

```tex
\begin{equation}\label{eq:color-tube-coords}
  c_r = w_2 - z, \quad c_g = w_3 - z, \quad c_b = w_4 - z,
\end{equation}
```

together with the space-like shift $\sigma = w_1 - z$. The *color vector* is $c = (c_r, c_g, c_b)$.
