To pass from the algebraic Iwahori–Hecke quotient $H_n(q)$ (Step 2 of the five-step arc) to a geometric manifold supporting hyperbolic knot complements and three-colour gauge fields (Step 3), we must fix the compact Riemann surface on which the categorical fibre functor $\tau$ is realized. Three empirical features of the observable universe constrain this choice:
1. **Three-colour symmetry.** The strong interaction carries a three-dimensional colour space whose cyclic and permutation symmetries embed into the automorphism group of the surface.
2. **Directed Reeb time-flow.** The Reeb vector field $\xi$ endows the unit-circle bundle over the surface with a preferred orientation, parametrized by the substrate deformation $q \neq 1$.
3. **Spatial isotropy.** Spatial rotations form the compact group $\mathrm{SO}(3)$, whose Lie algebra $\mathfrak{so}(3)$ is spanned by the three independent real Reeb degrees induced by the adjoint involution on the surface's weight decomposition.

These three conditions are satisfied simultaneously by a single algebraic curve: Bring's surface $\Sigma_4$, the smooth projective curve in $\mathbb{P}^4$ cut out by the vanishing of the first three power sums $\sum_{j=0}^4 x_j^k = 0$ ($k = 1, 2, 3$). Its genus is $g = 4$, its Euler characteristic is $\chi(\Sigma_4) = 2 - 2g = -6$, its first Betti number is $b_1(\Sigma_4) = 2g = 8$, its hyperbolic Gauss–Bonnet area under the $(2,4,5)$ triangle tiling is $\mathrm{Area}(\Sigma_4) = -2\pi\chi(\Sigma_4) = 12\pi$, and its full automorphism group is $\mathrm{Aut}(\Sigma_4) \cong S_5$. This chapter develops the geometry, cohomology, and torus-knot saturation of $\Sigma_4$.
