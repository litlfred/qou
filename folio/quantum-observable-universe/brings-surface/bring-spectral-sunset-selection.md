*Let $G = \sum_{\lambda \ne 0} \varphi_\lambda \otimes \varphi_\lambda / \lambda$ be the Green function of the Laplacian on $\mathcal{B}$, with a real orthonormal eigenbasis adapted to the fourteen types $\rho^\pm$ of [the reflection parity](#prop:bring-reflection-parity). The two-loop sunset is*

$$
  \iint G(x,y)^3 \, dx\, dy = \sum_{a,b,c} w_a w_b w_c\, C_{abc}^2, \qquad w = 1/\lambda, \qquad C_{abc} = \int \varphi_a \varphi_b \varphi_c .
$$

*Let $N(\rho,\sigma,\tau) = \dim \mathrm{Hom}_{S_5}(\rho\otimes\sigma\otimes\tau, 1)$. Then $C_{abc} = 0$ unless $N(\rho_a,\rho_b,\rho_c) \ne 0$ and an even number of $a, b, c$ are reflection-odd. On every energy-bounded cut of the spectrum, the sunset is therefore a sum of partial sunsets over the $572$ allowed typed triples out of $14^3 = 2744$. At the $S_5$ level, $143$ of the $343$ ordered triples of types are allowed, and $0 \le N \le 2$. In particular $N([2,1^3],[2,1^3],[2,1^3]) = 0$, so three modes of the holomorphic type never couple.*
