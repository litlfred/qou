Bring's curve $\Sigma_4$ is defined by the vanishing of the first three power sums of the homogeneous coordinates $x_0, \dots, x_4$, $\sum_{j=0}^{4} x_j = \sum_{j=0}^{4} x_j^2 = \sum_{j=0}^{4} x_j^3 = 0$, in $\mathbb{P}^4$. The $S_5$ action permutes the five coordinates. Each coordinate defines a sheet of the surface, glued together along the ramification locus. 

The five sheets meet at ramification points where two or more coordinates coincide. The regular pentagon inscribed in the unit circle links non-adjacent sheets via a "diagonal" connection ($T_{3,5}$ interaction) proportional to the golden ratio $\varphi \approx 1.618$. 

Along the substrate parameter $q$, the Skein amplitude $s = q^{1/2} - q^{-1/2}$ satisfies $s^2 + s - 1 = 0$ at the golden-ratio threshold $q = 1/\varphi^2 \approx 0.382$, where the Weierstrass coefficient of the elliptic fibre $\mathcal{E}_q$ degenerates the $5$-fold sheet coupling toward $3$-fold symmetry.

**Distinction from the Toffoli diagonal.** For the all-proton ordering, the Toffoli traced-volume product $\hat V_0 \hat V_1$ with $\hat V_i = z(q)\,a_i + b_i$ takes the form $P_{7,7}(q) = z + (q - q^{-1})z^2$. At the Markov parameter $z = q^3/(q^2+1)$ this evaluates to $P_{7,7}(q) = q^3(q^4+1)/(q^2+1)^2$, which is **strictly positive for every $q > 0$** (it vanishes at $q = 1/\varphi^2$ only under the auxiliary substitution $z \mapsto q^{1/2}/(q+1)$, where $z + (q - q^{-1})z^2 = (q + q^{1/2} - 1)/(q + 1)$). The golden-ratio degeneration at $q = 1/\varphi^2$ is therefore an intrinsic property of the Weierstrass locus $s^2 + s - 1 = 0$ of $\mathcal{E}_q$, independent of $z$.
