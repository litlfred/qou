Let $\mathcal{S}$ be the Bring's surface ([Bring's surface](#def:brings-surface)) with color projection $\pi \colon \mathcal{S} \to \mathbb{P}^1(\mathbb{C})$.

1. **Smoothness.** The three power-sum constraints are transverse in $\mathbb{P}^4$, so $\mathcal{S}$ is smooth.
2. **Intrinsic resolution.** At each branch point $z = z_c$ where two color sheets $x_i, x_j$ collide, the local coordinate $\delta = x_i - x_j$ satisfies $z - z_c \sim \delta^2$; the blow-up's exceptional divisor is already embedded in the color coordinates, so no external resolution is required.
