Choosing a well-ordered basis $\{x_i \mid i \in I\}$ for $\mathfrak{g}$, the
tensor algebra $T\mathfrak{g}$ has a multiplicative monomial basis of finite-length
words with the graded lexicographic ordering, and $S\mathfrak{g}$ has a basis of
non-decreasing words. The ideal $J$ is generated by:

$$G := \{g_{i,j} := x_i x_j - x_j x_i - [x_i, x_j]_{\mathfrak{g}} \mid x_i > x_j\}$$

The argument that $G$ is a Gröbner--Shirshov basis follows from
[Theorem 8](#thm:ugb-buchberger). As the leading terms $\LT(g_{i,j}) = x_i x_j$
are monic and the basis is multiplicative, $G$ is a unital Gröbner--Shirshov basis. The
corollary follows from [Theorem 2](#thm:ugb-main).
$\qquad\blacksquare$
