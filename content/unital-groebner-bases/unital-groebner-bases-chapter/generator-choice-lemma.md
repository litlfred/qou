Let $R$ be a commutative ring with unity, $A$ an $R$-algebra which
is free as an $R$-module, $(B, <)$ an
[admissible system](#def:admissible-system), and let $I$ be a
two-sided ideal of $A$ generated by a unital
[Gröbner--Shirshov basis](#def:groebner-basis)
$G = \{g_\gamma \mid \gamma \in \Gamma\}$.

Then in the [division algorithm](#def:division-algorithm), we may
choose $h_i \in I$ so that $h_i = g_{\gamma_i}$ for some
$\gamma_i \in \Gamma$.
