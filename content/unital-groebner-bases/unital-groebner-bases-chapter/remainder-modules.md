Let $R$ be a commutative ring with unity, $A$ an $R$-algebra which is free as an
$R$-module and without quasi-zeros, $(B, <)$ an admissible system on $A$, and
$I \subseteq A$ a subset. Define the $R$-submodule $O(I)$ of $A$ — the
**remainder module** — to be the $R$-submodule spanned by the set:

$$o(I) := \{\lambda\beta \mid \lambda \in R^*,\; \beta \in B,\; \forall h \in I \text{ we have } \LT(h) \neq \lambda\beta\}$$

We also define the $R$-module $\widetilde{O}(I)$ as the $R$-submodule spanned by
the set:

$$\widetilde{o}(I) := B \setminus \{\LM(h) \mid h \in I\}$$

$\widetilde{O}(I)$ is a free $R$-module.
