Let $A$ be the polynomial algebra $k[x_1, \ldots, x_n]$ and consider the ideal
$\mathfrak{m} = (x_1, \ldots, x_n)$. Taking a graded lexicographic ordering on $A$
with $x_i < x_j$ if $i < j$, the ideal $\mathfrak{m}^2$ has a Gröbner--Shirshov basis given by
$\{x_i x_j\}$. Thus the quotient $A/\mathfrak{m}^2$ is an $(n+1)$-dimensional
$k$-vector space with a basis:

$$A/\mathfrak{m}^2 = k\{1, x_1, \ldots, x_n\}$$

The fact that $k$ was a field is not important — one would have a similar
statement with the integers $\mathbb{Z}$, though not through classical Gröbner--Shirshov
basis techniques.
