A **monomial ordering** for a basis $B$ of an algebra $A$ is a well-ordering $<$ on
$B$ such that for $b, b', r, s \in B$:

1. If $b < b'$ then $r \cdot b \cdot s < r \cdot b' \cdot s$ whenever
   $r \cdot b \cdot s$ and $\LM(r \cdot b' \cdot s) \neq 0$.
2. If $b' = \LM(r \cdot b \cdot s) \neq 0$ with $r$ or $s \neq 1$ then $b < \LM(b')$.

If $B$ admits a monomial ordering $<$, then the pair $(B, <)$ is an **admissible
system**.
