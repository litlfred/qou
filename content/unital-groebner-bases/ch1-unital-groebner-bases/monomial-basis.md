Let $A$ be an $R$-algebra with multiplication $\cdot$ (and without quasi-zeros).
Choose a set of algebra generators $A = R\langle x_i \mid i \in \Lambda \rangle$ for some
index set $\Lambda$.

A **monomial** in $A$ is an ordered product
$x_\alpha := x_{\alpha_1} \cdot x_{\alpha_2} \cdots x_{\alpha_n}$
where $\alpha = \alpha_1 \alpha_2 \cdots \alpha_n$ is a finite-length word in $\Lambda$.

Suppose that $A$ is a free $R$-module. We say that $A$ has a **monomial basis**
$B$ subordinate to $\Lambda$ if $B$ is a subset of all words of finite length in
$\Lambda$ such that $A$ has an $R$-basis:

$$A = R\{x_\beta \mid \beta \in B\}$$
