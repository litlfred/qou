A **unit-root F-crystal** on a
[commutative formal group](#def:commutative-formal-group) $H$ is an
F-crystal whose Frobenius endomorphism acts invertibly (bijectively)
on the coordinate ring $\mathcal{O}(H)$.  The unit-root condition
means there is no nilpotent part in the Dieudonne--Manin decomposition.

Reference: \cite{hazewinkel1978}, Chapter V; \cite{zink2002}, Section 2.
