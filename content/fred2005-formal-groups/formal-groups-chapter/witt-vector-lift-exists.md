The [Witt vector lift](#def:witt-vector-lift) exists for any
[commutative formal group](#def:commutative-formal-group) equipped with a
[skewed-Poisson structure](#def:skewed-poisson-structure).  The proof
applies the [integration theorem](#thm:skew-poisson-integrates) to
obtain $G$, then wraps it in the Witt vector functor.

Reference: \cite{fred2005}, Main Theorem combined with Section 5.
