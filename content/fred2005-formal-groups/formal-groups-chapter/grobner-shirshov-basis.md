A **Groebner--Shirshov basis** for $\mathrm{Dist}(G)$ provides a
normal-form basis for the relations of the distribution algebra.  The
composition lemma guarantees that the leading monomials of the
$q$-Serre relations provide non-overlapping obstructions, yielding a
normal-form basis coinciding with the Lusztig divided-power basis.

The key property is **confluence**: a reduction map
$\mathrm{reduce} \colon \mathrm{Dist}(G) \to \mathrm{NF}$
satisfying $\mathrm{reduce} \circ \mathrm{embed} \circ \mathrm{reduce} = \mathrm{reduce}$, ensuring that all S-polynomials of leading
monomials reduce to zero.

Reference: \cite{fred2005}, Proposition 3.8.
