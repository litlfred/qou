The [unit-root condition](#def:unit-root-f-crystal) on the F-crystal
associated to a [commutative formal group](#def:commutative-formal-group)
$H$ is equivalent to integrability of the
[skewed-Poisson structure](#def:skewed-poisson-structure):

$$
  \exists\;\text{unit-root F-crystal on } H
  \;\iff\;
  \exists\;G / \mathbb{Z}_p \text{ with } G \bmod p \cong H.
$$

The forward direction uses the
[integration theorem](#thm:skew-poisson-integrates) to construct $G$
from the skewed-Poisson datum.  The backward direction produces a
unit-root F-crystal via the identity Frobenius endomorphism.

Reference: \cite{fred2005}, Corollary 5.3.
