The [Groebner--Shirshov basis](#def:grobner-shirshov-basis) for
$\mathrm{Dist}(G)$ exists and yields a flat deformation of
$\mathrm{Dist}(H)$.  The $q$-Serre relations form a Groebner--Shirshov
system whose composition lemma (confluence) holds.

Reference: \cite{fred2005}, Proposition 3.8 and Theorem 3.15.
