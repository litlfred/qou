**Integration theorem** (\cite{fred2005}, Main Theorem):
A [skewed-Poisson structure](#def:skewed-poisson-structure) on a
[commutative formal group](#def:commutative-formal-group) $H / \mathbb{F}_p$
integrates to a [non-commutative formal group](#def:non-commutative-formal-group)
$G / \mathbb{Z}_p$.

The construction uses $\mathcal{O}(H)$ as the distribution algebra,
the formal group law deformed by the bracket as multiplication,
and the diagonal as comultiplication (group-like elements).
