A **skewed-Poisson structure** on a
[commutative formal group](#def:commutative-formal-group) $H$ is a
biderivation
$\pi_< \colon \mathcal{O}(H) \otimes \mathcal{O}(H) \to \mathcal{O}(H)$
satisfying:

1. **Graded anti-symmetry**: $\pi_<(f, g) = -\pi_<(g, f)$.
2. **$p$-truncated Jacobi condition**:
   $\pi_<(f, \pi_<(g, h)) + \pi_<(g, \pi_<(h, f)) + \pi_<(h, \pi_<(f, g)) = 0$
   modulo $p$-th powers.

This is the infinitesimal datum encoding the non-commutativity to be
"integrated" into a non-commutative formal group.

Reference: \cite{fred2005}, Definition 2.3.
