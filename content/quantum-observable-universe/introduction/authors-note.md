Every proof in this paper possesses a corresponding Lean 4 formalisation, except where explicitly citing external references for large prior results. **735 of 746 (98.5%) provable claims** (theorems, lemmas, propositions, corollaries) carry **full Lean proofs** (sorry-free); the remainder bear the `sorry` marker against an explicit bibliographic reference. The paper poses **81 primary open conjectures** of its own—famous external open problems it connects to, and conjectures conditional on those primaries, are tallied separately—of which **71 (87.7%) are class-axiomatised in Lean** using the conditional-class convention. This allows downstream results to persist as fully verified conditional theorems. All computations—braid-word reductions, Gröbner–Shirshov normal forms, linear programs, hyperbolic volumes—execute symbolically in the substrate parameter $q$ and attach as witnesses to their supporting proofs. The Rust and Python implementations, alongside the Lean sources, reside at [https://github.com/litlfred/qou](https://github.com/litlfred/qou).
