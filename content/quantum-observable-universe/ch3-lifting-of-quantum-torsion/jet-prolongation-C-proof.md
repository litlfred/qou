
Apply [jet prolongation](#prop:jet-prolongation) to the categorical data
$(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ underlying the archimedean quantum
observable universe ([$\mathbb{C}$-quantum observable universe](#ex:c-quantum-observable-universe));
let $q$ denote the substrate parameter,
where $\mathbf{C} = \mathrm{Rep}(\mathrm{SU}_{q}(2))$,
$A = \mathcal{O}_q(\mathrm{SU}(2))$, and $\tau$ is the forgetful
functor.  The fibre functor $\tau$ converts the categorical jet object
$J^1(A)$ into the geometric first jet bundle $J^1 E$, the inherited
Frobenius--Hopf structure into the connection $A'$, and the
non-degeneracy and vanishing conditions into the analytic conditions of
[$\mathbb{C}$-quantum observable universe](#ex:c-quantum-observable-universe).
