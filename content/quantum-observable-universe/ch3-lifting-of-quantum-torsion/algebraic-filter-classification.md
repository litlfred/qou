**Proposition.**
Let $F_A$ be the curvature of the substrate parameter
([substrate parameter](#def:substrate-parameter)) and let $\alpha$ be a
harmonic $1$-form in the $q$-harmonic basis on branes
([q-harmonic basis](#rem:q-harmonic-basis-brane)).

Then the commutator $[F_A, \alpha]$ classifies $\alpha$ into exactly one of
two types:

1. **Fermionic (matter).** $[F_A, \alpha] \neq 0$.  The form $\alpha$ is
   entangled with the curvature, sees the $\mathrm{SO}(3)$ holonomy and the
   $-6$ index fixed points, and its geodesics are trapped or spiralling around
   the fundamental vortex filaments.

2. **Bosonic (radiation).** $[F_A, \alpha] = 0$.  The form $\alpha$ commutes
   with the curvature, corresponds to longitudinal modes that propagate
   through the bulk without deflection by the monodromy fixed points, and
   its geodesics are straight relative to the gauge-fluid flow.
