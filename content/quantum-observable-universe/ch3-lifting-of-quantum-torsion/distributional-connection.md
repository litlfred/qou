At the limit $n \to \infty$ one no longer looks at a single connection $A$,
but at a *distributional connection* represented by the series
$$
  A_\infty
  \;=\;
  \sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\;\sum_{i=1}^{b_1(\mathcal{S}_n)}
  c_{n,i}\,\alpha_{n,i}\,,
$$
where $c_{n,i} \in \mathbb{C}$ and $\{\alpha_{n,i}\}$ is the $q$-harmonic
basis of [$q$-harmonic basis on branes](#rem:q-harmonic-basis-brane).  This turns the gauge field
into a harmonic expansion across the infinite jet space $J^\infty \mathcal{S}$
([jet shell](#rem:jet-shell)).
