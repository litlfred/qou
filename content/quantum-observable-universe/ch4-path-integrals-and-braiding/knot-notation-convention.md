Throughout this paper, particles **are** their knots.  We write

$$
  \mathfrak{K}_x^{\pm}
$$

where $x$ is the traditional physics symbol for the particle and
$\pm$ is the chirality (orientation) of the knot.  The
superscript distinguishes particle from antiparticle: mirror
image = charge conjugation.

**Fundamental particles.**

| $\mathfrak{K}$ | Knot | Alexander–Briggs | Particle ($+$) | Antiparticle ($-$) |
|:-:|------|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| $\mathfrak{K}_\gamma$ | $\varnothing$ (empty) | — | photon $\gamma$ | (self) |
| $\mathfrak{K}_\nu$ | unknot $0_1$ | $0_1$ | neutrino $\nu$ | $\bar{\nu}$ |
| $\mathfrak{K}_g$ | Hopf link | $L2a1$ | gluon | (self) |
| $\mathfrak{K}_e$ | trefoil | $3_1$ | $e^-$ | $e^+$ |
| $\mathfrak{K}_\mu$ | figure-eight | $4_1$ | $\mu^\pm$ | (self) |
| $\mathfrak{K}_\tau$ | cinquefoil | $5_1$ | $\tau^-$ | $\tau^+$ |
| $\mathfrak{K}_c$ | $(2,7)$-torus knot | $7_1$ | charm $c$ | $\bar{c}$ |
| $\mathfrak{K}_t$ | $(3,4)$-torus knot | $8_{19}$ | top $t$ | $\bar{t}$ |

The sign convention: $\mathfrak{K}_e^- = T_{2,3}$ (left trefoil =
electron), $\mathfrak{K}_e^+ = \sigma(T_{2,3})$ (right trefoil =
positron).  For amphichiral knots ($\mathfrak{K}_\mu$,
$\mathfrak{K}_\gamma$), the sign is trivial: $K \cong \sigma(K)$.

The unknot $\mathfrak{K}_\nu$ is amphichiral as an unoriented knot,
but the neutrino $\nu$ and antineutrino $\bar{\nu}$ are
distinguished by **framing** (a choice of normal vector along the
knot), not by knot type.  This is the geometric origin of the
neutrino's chirality: left-handed $\nu_L$ and right-handed
$\bar{\nu}_R$ are the same unknot with opposite framings.

**Torus knots.**
When the torus parameters are relevant, write $T_{m,n}$ for the
$(m, n)$-torus knot.  The mirror image is $\sigma(T_{m,n}) =
T_{m,-n}$.

**Links.**

| Symbol | Link |
|--------|------|
| $\mathcal{L}_H$ | Hopf link |
| $\mathcal{B}_n$ | Borromean $n$-component link |

**Composites.**
The connected sum of knots is $\mathfrak{K}_1 \mathbin{\#}
\mathfrak{K}_2$.  A hadron ([hadron](#def:hadron)) is written
$[\mathfrak{K}_1, \mathfrak{K}_2, \mathfrak{K}_3]_p \bowtie
\mathcal{L}_H$, where the subscript $c$ indicates colour-tube
coupling and $\bowtie$ denotes the linking operation.  The proton:

$$
  \mathfrak{p} = [\mathfrak{K}_u, \mathfrak{K}_u, \mathfrak{K}_d]_p
  \bowtie \mathcal{L}_H
$$
