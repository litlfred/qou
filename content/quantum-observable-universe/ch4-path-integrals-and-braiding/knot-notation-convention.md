Throughout this paper we adopt the following notation for knots
and links.

**Knots.**
Every knot is denoted by a letter with a subscript indicating its
type:

| Symbol | Knot | Alexander--Briggs |
|--------|------|-------------------|
| $K_{0_1}$ | Unknot | $0_1$ |
| $K_{3_1}$ (or $T_{2,3}$) | Trefoil | $3_1$ |
| $K_{4_1}$ | Figure-eight | $4_1$ |
| $K_{5_1}$ (or $T_{2,5}$) | Cinquefoil | $5_1$ |

Torus knots are written $T_{m,n}$ when the torus parameters are
relevant.  The mirror image of a knot $K$ is $\sigma(K)$.

**Links.**

| Symbol | Link |
|--------|------|
| $\mathcal{L}_H$ | Hopf link |
| $\mathcal{B}_n$ | Borromean $n$-component link |

**Composites.**
The connected sum of knots is $K_1 \mathbin{\#} K_2$.
A hadron composite (Definition \ref{def:hadron}) is written
$[K_1, K_2, K_3]_c \bowtie \mathcal{L}_H$, where the subscript
$c$ indicates color-tube coupling and $\bowtie$ denotes the linking
operation.
