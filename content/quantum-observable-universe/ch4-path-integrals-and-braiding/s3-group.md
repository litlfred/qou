Imposing the involution relations $\sigma_i^2=\mathrm{id}$ on the braid
group $B_3$ ([Definition](#def:braid-group)) yields the
*symmetric group*

```tex
\begin{equation}\label{eq:s3-presentation}
  S_3
  =\bigl\langle s_1,s_2
    \;\big|\;s_1^2=s_2^2=(s_1 s_2)^3=\mathrm{id}\bigr\rangle.
\end{equation}
```
