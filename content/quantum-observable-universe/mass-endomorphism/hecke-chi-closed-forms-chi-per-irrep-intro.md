This sub-section assembles the $\chi$-character closed forms
per Hecke irrep at small partitions.  We first derive the
$\chi_{V_3}$ closed form (the substrate-level prototype), exhibit
the cyclotomic closed forms for $\chi$ per irrep with their
substrate-flow data, and isolate the per-block Hopf-Jones
absorption pattern that pins the $\mathfrak{Q}_\beta$ $c_k$ closed forms.
The closing blocks specialise to $\chi_{V_{111}}$ via sign
substitution, derive $\chi_{V_{21}}$ via the Hoefsmit matrix, and
state the $\phi_d$ SYT count conjecture that the categorical
chain convergence mechanisms close the section with.
