**Lemma.**
*Let $\alpha = (\nabla_A q, L \wedge F^+)$ form the integrand of the mass identity for a vortex $\varrho$. Let $\hat{\tau}_n$ be the :refterm[descent]{#descent} involution. The Killing-form orthogonality of $\mathcal{A}_{n+1}^{\mathrm{desc}} \oplus \mathcal{A}_{n+1}^{\mathrm{trans}}$ together with multiplicative-integral additivity over orthogonal direct sums yields:*

$$
  \mathfrak{m}(\varrho)
  =
  \bigl\lVert
  \mathrm{MI}\!\!\int_\Sigma \hat{\tau}_n(\alpha)
  \bigr\rVert
  +
  \bigl\lVert
  \mathrm{MI}\!\!\int_\Sigma
  \bigl(\alpha - \hat{\tau}_n(\alpha)\bigr)
  \bigr\rVert.
$$
