**Proof.** The transient component $\alpha - \hat{\tau}_n(\alpha)$ lies in $\mathcal{A}_{n+1}^{\mathrm{trans}} = \ker\hat{\tau}_n$. By the structure of the [brane boundary](#def:brane-boundary), the support of a transient endomorphism is contained in the union of the kernels $\ker(\mathfrak{d}_{n+1}|_{p_i})$ at the blow-up centres $p_1, \ldots, p_N$. These kernels are mutually disjoint submodules because each arises from a distinct extension step. The transient trace therefore decomposes as a sum:

$$
  \mathrm{tr}(\alpha - \hat{\tau}_n(\alpha))
  = 
  \sum_{i=1}^{N} \mathrm{tr}_{\mathrm{trans}}(\varrho, p_i).
$$

The disjointness of the supports ensures that the norm of the sum equals the sum of the norms. $\square$
