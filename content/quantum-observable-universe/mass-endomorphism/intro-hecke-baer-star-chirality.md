The Hecke algebra carries a canonical Baer-star involution descending from the bar-involution $q \mapsto q^{-1}$ on the braid-group representation. We record the foundational generic form of the Baer-star here. We use it to articulate the chirality pairing, which supplies the categorical distinction between a knot and its mirror. The chapters on particle interactions and observations consume this structure to separate matter from antimatter.
