The Hecke algebra carries a canonical Baer-star involution that
descends from the bar-involution $q \mapsto q^{-1}$ on the
braid-group representation.  This section records the
foundational generic form of the Baer-star and uses it to
articulate the chirality pairing — the categorical distinction
between a knot and its mirror — that the chapters on particle
interactions and observations consume to separate matter from
antimatter.
