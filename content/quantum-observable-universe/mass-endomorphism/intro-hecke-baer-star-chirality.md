The Hecke algebra carries a canonical Baer-star involution. It descends from the bar-involution $q \mapsto q^{-1}$ on the braid-group representation. This section records the foundational generic form of the Baer-star. We use it to articulate the chirality pairing (the categorical distinction between a knot and its mirror). The chapters on particle interactions and observations consume this structure to separate matter from antimatter.
