The $\chi$-character closed forms of the previous section gave the
Markov trace block by block; this section reads them through the
$T_\beta$ endomorphism. The per-block characters
$\chi_\lambda(\Delta_{T_\beta})(q)$ over $H_3(q)$ are written in closed
form and shown to vanish at the classical point $q = 1$
([`prop:qbeta-per-block-chi-closed-forms`](#prop:qbeta-per-block-chi-closed-forms)),
which is what forces the $\Delta\tau_M$ difference to carry an explicit
$(q^2-1)$ factor once the Markov weights are pinned to their canonical
values ([`prop:qbeta-delta-tau-canonical-conditional-q2-minus-1-factor`](#prop:qbeta-delta-tau-canonical-conditional-q2-minus-1-factor)),
a conditional statement carried as a class hypothesis (§3b-cond) and
inhabited by an explicit $\sqrt{q}$-bearing instance
([`prop:qbeta-canonical-markov-weights-explicit-instance`](#prop:qbeta-canonical-markov-weights-explicit-instance)).
The catalogued low-order Taylor data of the surrogate $\Delta\tau_M$
([`prop:qbeta-delta-tau-surrogate-taylor-coefficients`](#prop:qbeta-delta-tau-surrogate-taylor-coefficients))
is the per-block input the decay-side $c_k$ recursion consumes downstream;
the closed forms here are exact, while that downstream extraction remains
calibration-anchored.
