(not a root of unity, $q \neq 1$). Equip $H_n(q)$ with the bar

(extended antilinearly). Then $H_n(q)$ is a **Baer-*-ring**: every
The right (resp. left) annihilator ideal is generated by a *self-adjoint
idempotent* (a projection).*
