**Proposition (Iwahori–Hecke algebra is Baer-* at generic $q$).**

*Let $R$ be a field of characteristic 0 and $q \in R$ generic (not a root of unity, $q \neq 1$). Equip $H_n(q)$ with the bar involution $* \colon \sigma_i \mapsto \sigma_i^{-1}$, $q \mapsto q^{-1}$ (extended antilinearly). Then $H_n(q)$ is a **Baer-*-ring**. The right (resp. left) annihilator ideal is generated by a self-adjoint idempotent (a projection).*

### Corollary

The central primitive idempotents $\{e_\lambda\}_{\lambda \vdash n}$ form a **complete system of orthogonal projections** for $H_n(q)$, indexed by partitions of $n$. They are explicit (see [`prop:explicit-central-idempotent`](#prop:explicit-central-idempotent)) and bar-aware. Self-conjugate $\lambda$ give bar-fixed projections (real period witnesses). Conjugate pairs give bar-swapped projections (chirality-twisted witnesses).

### Failure locus — explicit characterisation

The Baer-* property fails on the exact locus where $H_n(q)$ ceases to be semisimple. Concretely:

$$
  H_n(q) \text{ is semisimple}
  \;\;\iff\;\;
  [k]_q \;\neq\; 0 \quad\text{for all } k \in \{2, 3, \ldots, n\},
$$

where $[k]_q := (q^k - q^{-k}) / (q - q^{-1})$ is the symmetric quantum integer. The failure locus is therefore:

$$
  \boxed{\;
    \mathrm{NonBaer}\bigl(H_n(q)\bigr)
    \;=\;
    \bigl\{\, q \in \overline{\mathbb{Q}}^\times \,:\,
      q^{2k} = 1 \text{ for some } k \in \{2, \ldots, n\} \,\bigr\}.
  \;}
$$

Equivalently, $q$ is a primitive $2k$-th root of unity for some $2 \leq k \leq n$. This means $q \in \{\zeta_4, \zeta_6, \zeta_8, \ldots, \zeta_{2n}\}$ (primitive forms only). At each such $q$, the Hoefsmit projector becomes singular and the Jacobson radical is non-trivial. The annihilator structure includes nilpotent elements not generated by self-adjoint idempotents.
