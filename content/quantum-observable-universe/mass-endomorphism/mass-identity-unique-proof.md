The mass formula
$$
  \mathfrak{m}(\varrho)
  \;=\;
  \bigl\lVert
    \mathrm{tr}\bigl( (\int_A \otimes \mathrm{id}) \circ \Delta \circ \varrho \bigr)
  \bigr\rVert
$$
is linear in $\int_A$ inside the norm. Scaling $\int_A \mapsto c \int_A$ scales the trace by $c$, and the norm transforms by $|c|$. The QU/QOU axioms uniquely determine all other inputs ($\Delta$, $\varrho$, $\mathrm{tr}$). Hence, fixing the normalisation of $\int_A$ (as part of the QU datum) uniquely fixes $\mathfrak{m}$.
