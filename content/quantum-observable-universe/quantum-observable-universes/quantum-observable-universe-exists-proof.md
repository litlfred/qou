Explicit construction: the category $\mathbf{C} = \mathbf{Rep}(\mathrm{SU}_q(2))$
with Frobenius--Hopf object $A$ the regular representation and fibre
functor $\tau$ the forgetful functor
([Example](#ex:qou-su2)) satisfies all four QOU axioms:

1. The Frobenius form $\int_A = $ Haar integral is non-degenerate
   (quantum Peter--Weyl).
2. Observability holds: there are no hidden symmetries of the
   adjoint representation beyond the Weyl group.
3. Measurability: the Chern classes of $\mathrm{Ad}(A)$ vanish because
   $\mathrm{SU}_q(2)$ is simply-connected.
4. The categorical volume constraint is the quantum determinant
   condition on $\mathrm{SU}_q(2)$.

A second example is the CR realisation
([Example](#ex:c-quantum-observable-universe)) over a pseudo-Hermitian
manifold.  Both establish existence; no universal-property uniqueness
holds (a QOU is a *structure*, not a universal object).
