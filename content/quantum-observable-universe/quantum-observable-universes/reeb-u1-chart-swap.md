**Remark (chart-swap $U(1)$ is the Reeb flow).**
The continuous family $\rho_\varTheta : z \mapsto e^{i\varTheta} z$
exhibited in
[affine-chart origin of $i$ and $q$](#rem:affine-chart-origin-of-i-and-q)
— whose square root of the antipodal involution
$\iota : z \mapsto z^{-1}$ generates the categorical coordinate
$i \in \mathrm{End}_{R[i]}(\mathbf{1})$ — coincides with the
$U(1)$ generated by the
[Reeb flow](#thm:categorical-reeb-exists) on the
[contact affine chart](#def:contact-affine-chart). The two
narratives describe the same circle from two angles.

**The identification.** Let $\xi$ be the
[categorical Reeb derivation](#def:categorical-reeb) on $A$ and
$\varTheta_A$ the dual contact $1$-form. On Bring's coordinate ring
$\mathcal{O}_q(\mathcal{B})$ the generator is
$\xi = e_3 \in \mathfrak{su}(2)$, the infinitesimal generator of
the maximal torus $T = \mathrm{U}(1) \subset \mathrm{SU}_q(2)$
([Constructive Reeb derivation on Bring's coordinate ring](#ex:categorical-reeb-brings)).
The flow $\phi_t = \exp(t\xi)$ acts on the contact affine chart
coordinate $Z = \alpha_H / \iota_\xi \alpha$
([contact affine chart](#def:contact-affine-chart)) as

$$
  \phi_t \cdot Z  =  e^{-2it}\, Z,
$$

since $\alpha_H \in \ker \xi$ (horizontal forms are weight-zero) and
$\iota_\xi \alpha$ sits in the weight-$1$ representation of $T$
under the Reeb decomposition
([Reeb spectrum on Bring](#ex:categorical-reeb-brings)). Setting
$\varTheta = -2t$, the action $\phi_t \cdot Z = e^{i\varTheta} Z$
is exactly the chart-swap family
$\rho_\varTheta$ of
[affine-chart origin of $i$ and $q$](#rem:affine-chart-origin-of-i-and-q).
The chart-swap $U(1)$ and the Reeb $U(1)$ are not analogous — they
are the same subgroup of $\mathrm{Aut}(\mathbb{P}(V))$.

**Three roles, one circle.** The identification organises five
disparate roles into a single $U(1)$:

| Role | Action on $Z$ | Subgroup / regime |
|------|---------------|-------------------|
| Chart-swap involution | $\iota : Z \mapsto Z^{-1}$ | $\mathbb{Z}_2$ at $\varTheta = \pi$ |
| Imaginary unit $i$ | $\rho_{\pi/2}$ (quarter-turn) | $\mathbb{Z}_4$ |
| Wick rotation | $\rho_\varTheta$, $\varTheta \in [0, \pi/2]$ | full $U(1)$, continuous |
| Reeb flow $\phi_t$ | $Z \mapsto e^{-2it} Z$ | generated by $\xi$ |
| $q$-deformation | $\rho_\varTheta$ off the unit circle | $U_q(1)$ |

The same one-parameter group plays all five roles; the
$\mathbb{Z}_2 \hookrightarrow \mathbb{Z}_4 \hookrightarrow U(1)
\hookrightarrow U_q(1)$ hierarchy of
[affine-chart origin of $i$ and $q$](#rem:affine-chart-origin-of-i-and-q)
is the same Reeb $U(1)$ viewed at successively finer chart-transition
resolution.

**The Wick-rotation reading.** The continuous metric deformation
$\rho_\varTheta$ from Euclidean ($\varTheta = 0$) to Minkowski
($\varTheta = \pi/2$) is therefore the Reeb flow running with
imaginary time parameter $t = i\varTheta/2$. The categorical
$\mathbb{Z}_2 \to S^1$ promotion of time reversal is not a separate
piece of structure: it is the same $\mathbb{Z}_2 \subset U(1)$
chart-swap subgroup that the Reeb circle has all along.
Bargmann–Hall–Wightman analytic continuation through the forward
tube is, in the categorical register, the Reeb $\phi_t$ flow
analytically continued in $t$.

**Knot operators as rotational actions.** Reeb orbits on Bring's
surface are closed circles (regular K-contact: the Reeb spectrum
is $\{-1, +1\}$ with finite multiplicity, so the flow is periodic).
The fibre $A \to A/\xi$ is therefore locally a $U(1)$-principal
bundle over the horizontal leaf space $H = \ker \varTheta_A$.
A torus knot $T_{m,n}$ embeds as the $(m,n)$-cable of a Reeb fibre:
$m$ twists around the Reeb direction, $n$ around a base cycle of
$\Sigma_4$. The categorical mass evaluation
$|\mathrm{tr}_M(\mathcal{B}_K)|$ on such a knot is the Frobenius
trace of the Reeb-$U(1)$ holonomy lifted to the gauge bundle:
knot operators are *not* arbitrary endomorphisms of $V^{\otimes n}$,
they are Wilson loops of the Reeb $U(1)$ decorated by the gauge-
theoretic representation labelling the cable. Framings are
exactly the integer ambiguity in the lift of the Reeb $U(1)$ to
the gauge bundle.

**The $\mathbb{Z}_2 \subset U(1)_{\text{Reeb}}$ as time-reversal.**
The antipodal involution $\varTheta \mapsto \varTheta + \pi$ on
the Reeb circle is the categorical realisation of time reversal:
it sends $\phi_t \mapsto \phi_{-t}$. The
[Frobenius pairing](#def:frobenius-hopf-substrate)
$\langle a, b \rangle_F = \int_A(a \cdot b)$ is by construction
symmetric under this $\mathbb{Z}_2$, since
$\Psi = S^2 = \mathrm{id}_A$. This $\mathbb{Z}_2$-symmetry is
the categorical datum that becomes Osterwalder–Schrader reflection
positivity in the Archimedean realisation $\tau$; the
reflection-positivity reading of the Frobenius pairing is taken
up in the appendix-surreals companion to
[Yang–Mills surreal bound](#rem:yang-mills-surreal-bound).

**Summary.** A single $U(1)$ — the maximal torus of
$\mathrm{SU}_q(2)$, equivalently the Reeb flow on
$\mathcal{O}_q(\mathcal{B})$, equivalently the chart-swap circle
on $\mathbb{P}(V)$ — supplies the imaginary unit $i$, the
continuous Wick rotation $\rho_\varTheta$, the time-reversal
$\mathbb{Z}_2$, the framing data of knot operators, and the
analytic-continuation parameter of the categorical Green's
function. The categorical infrastructure does not invent this
$U(1)$; it inherits it from the contact-affine chart of
$\mathbb{P}(V)$.
