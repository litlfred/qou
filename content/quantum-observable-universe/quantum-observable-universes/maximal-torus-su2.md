For the fundamental representation $V = \mathbb{C}^2$ of $\mathrm{SU}(2)$, the weight decomposition is $V = \mathbb{C} e_+ \oplus \mathbb{C} e_-$. The weight subcategory $\langle V \rangle$ consists of representations that decompose strictly into these weight spaces. The maximal torus is therefore the diagonal subgroup $\mathrm{U}(1) \subset \mathrm{SU}(2)$, which acts on $e_{±}$ by $e^{± i\varTheta}$.
