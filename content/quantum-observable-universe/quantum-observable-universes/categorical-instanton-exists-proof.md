The proof strategy proceeds by direct construction and deformation theory. We exhibit a specific connection and directly compute its curvature, demonstrating that it satisfies the $\star_q$-self-duality condition required for a categorical instanton.

The trivial connection $\nabla_0 = d$ has $F_{\nabla_0} = 0$, which
is by definition $\star_q$-self-dual; hence the zero instanton exists.
Non-trivial instantons exist on $\mathrm{SU}_q(2)$-bundles over the
CR realisation ([example](#ex\:c-quantum-observable-universe)) via
categorical :refterm[descent]{#descent} of the classical BPST instanton
([self-duality](#prop\:categorical-self-duality)).
