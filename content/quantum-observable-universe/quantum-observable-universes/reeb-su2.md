For $G = \mathrm{SU}(2)$, the maximal torus $T = \mathrm{U}(1)$ has the generator $\xi = \frac{1}{2}\begin{smallmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & -1 \end{smallmatrix} \in \mathfrak{su}(2)$. On $A = \mathcal{O}(\mathrm{SU}(2))$, $\xi$ acts as the left-invariant vector field. The canonical $1$-form is $\varTheta_A = \omega_3$, satisfying $\omega_3(\xi) = 1$. The horizontal forms are those with no $\omega_3$ component. In the archimedean realization on a CR manifold $(M, \varTheta)$, $\xi$ is the Reeb vector field with $\iota_\xi d\varTheta = 0$.
