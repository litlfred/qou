The maximal torus $T_q$ of the substrate $G_q$ plays five distinct roles:
1. Antipodal involution: $\rho_\pi : Z \mapsto -Z$ ($\mathbb{Z}_2$ at $\theta_q = \pi$).
2. Imaginary unit $i$: $\rho_{\pi/2}$ ($\mathbb{Z}_4$).
3. Wick rotation: $\rho_{\theta_q}$ (full $U(1)$).
4. Reeb flow $\phi_t$: $Z \mapsto e^{-t} Z$ (generated by $\xi$).
5. $q$-deformation: $\rho_{\theta_q}$ off the unit circle ($U_q(1)$).

The continuous Wick rotation is the analytic continuation of the Reeb-flow parameter. Knot operators function as Reeb-$U(1)$ Wilson loops on the contact bundle. The Osterwalder-Schrader reflection embodies the antipodal $\mathbb{Z}_2 \subset T_q$ subgroup.
