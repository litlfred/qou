The difference $e_1 - e_2$ is primitive (from linearising
$\Delta_q e_i = e_i \otimes e_i$) and satisfies $D_q(e_1 - e_2) = e_1 - e_2$.
The flow equation on primitives has only the trivial solution:
iterating $D_q$ on a primitive of bounded degree forces all
coefficients to vanish except the leading one, and the counit
condition $\epsilon(e_i) = 1$ kills the constant term of the
difference.  Hence $e_1 - e_2 = 0$.  Equivalently, $\exp_q$ is the
unique fixed point of $D_q$ on grouplikes with trivial counit — a
categorical fact holding over any base ring where the flow equation
has at least one solution
([existence of $\exp_q$](#prop:q-exponential-exists)).
