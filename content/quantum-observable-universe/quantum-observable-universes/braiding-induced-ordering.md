**Proposition.**
*Let $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ be a quantum observable universe
([QOU](#def:quantum-observable-universe)) whose braiding
$c_{V,V} \colon V \otimes V \to V \otimes V$ is non-symmetric
($c_{V,V}^2 \neq \mathrm{id}$).  Then $c_{V,V}$ determines
a canonical admissible well-ordering $\prec_c$ on the monomial
basis $B$ of the Iwahori--Hecke algebra $H_n(q)$ of $A$.*

**Construction.**  The monomial basis $B$ of $H_n(q)$ consists
of reduced positive braid monomials
$w = \sigma_{i_1}\sigma_{i_2}\cdots\sigma_{i_k}$ —
the images of the braiding $c_{V,V}$ under
$\rho_n(\sigma_i) = \mathrm{id}^{\otimes(i-1)} \otimes c_{V,V} \otimes \mathrm{id}^{\otimes(n-i-1)}$
([braid action on tensor powers](#prop:braid-action-tensor)).
The braiding $c_{V,V}$ (as opposed to its inverse
$c_{V,V}^{-1}$) defines "positive crossing" — forward time.

*Definition.* A **well-ordering** on a set $S$ is a total
order $\prec$ such that every non-empty subset of $S$ has a
least element.  Equivalently: there are no infinite strictly
descending chains $s_1 \succ s_2 \succ s_3 \succ \cdots$.

*Definition.* The **degree-lexicographic ordering** $\prec_c$
on $B$ is: $w \prec_c w'$ if and only if

$$
  \ell(w) < \ell(w'),
  \quad\text{or}\quad
  \ell(w) = \ell(w')  \text{and}
  (i_1, \ldots, i_k) <_{\mathrm{lex}} (i_1', \ldots, i_k')
$$

where $\ell(w) = k$ is the word length (number of crossings)
and $<_{\mathrm{lex}}$ is lexicographic order on the generator
indices $i_j \in \{1, \ldots, n-1\}$.

*Proof that $\prec_c$ is a well-ordering on $B$.*
Let $S \subseteq B$ be non-empty.

(a) **Degree is bounded below.**  Every $w \in B$ has
$\ell(w) \geq 0$.  Let $d = \min\{\ell(w) : w \in S\}$.
The set $S_d = \{w \in S : \ell(w) = d\}$ is non-empty.

(b) **Finite alphabet at each degree.**  Each $w \in S_d$
is a word of length $d$ over the finite alphabet
$\{1, \ldots, n-1\}$.  There are at most $(n-1)^d$ such
words — a finite set.  Every finite totally ordered set
has a minimum.  Let $w_0$ be the lex-minimum of $S_d$.

(c) **$w_0$ is the minimum of $S$.**  For any $w \in S$:
if $\ell(w) > d$ then $w_0 \prec_c w$ (shorter words
first); if $\ell(w) = d$ then $w_0 \leq_{\mathrm{lex}} w$
by construction.  $\square$

*Admissibility.*  The ordering $\prec_c$ is **admissible**
in the sense of
([admissible system](#unital-groebner-bases:def:admissible-system)):
left and right multiplication by basis elements preserves
the ordering ($w \prec_c w'$ implies
$u \cdot w \cdot v \prec_c u \cdot w' \cdot v$ whenever
both products are non-zero), because degree-lex is compatible
with any associative multiplication whose defining relations
(Hecke, Yang–Baxter) have leading terms of bounded degree —
the Hecke relation is quadratic.

**Two faces of the arrow of time.**  The braiding $c_{V,V}$
and the Reeb vector field $\xi$
([categorical Reeb](#def:categorical-reeb)) express the same
arrow of time from two complementary viewpoints:

- **Categorical arrow** ($c_{V,V}$): which strand crosses
  *over*.  A positive crossing $\sigma_i$ applies $c_{V,V}$;
  a negative crossing $\sigma_i^{-1}$ applies
  $\bar{c}_{V,V} := c_{V,V}^{-1}$.  The ordering $\prec_c$
  is the algebraic encoding: forward time = Gröbner--Shirshov reduction
  under $\prec_c$.

- **Geometric arrow** ($\xi$): which direction the Reeb flow
  runs.  The one-parameter subgroup $\phi_t^q$ generated by
  $\xi$ is the flow of forward time on the state bundle
  $\mathcal{S}$.  At each Planck step, one application of
  $c_{V,V}$ advances $\phi_t^q$ by one tick.

**Crossing reversal = time reversal.**  The crossing reversal
involution $\sigma \colon K \mapsto K^{\sigma}$ (mirror all
crossings) corresponds to switching the braiding:
$c_{V,V} \leftrightarrow \bar{c}_{V,V}$.  This flips the
ordering $\prec_c$ and reverses the Reeb flow
$\xi \mapsto -\xi$.  In the Feynman–Stueckelberg
interpretation, an antiparticle ($K^{\sigma}$) is its particle
($K$) with time reversed — precisely the opposite braiding.
CPT symmetry is the statement that the QOU axioms are
invariant under simultaneous
$c_{V,V} \leftrightarrow \bar{c}_{V,V}$ (T),
$K \mapsto K^{\sigma}$ (C), and
$\sigma_i \mapsto \sigma_{n-i}$ (P, strand reflection).

Although the underlying mathematical machinery is a standard degree-lexicographic well-foundedness construction, its physical interpretation—formally linking the categorical braid-over-under structure to the macroscopic thermodynamic arrow of time—is a novel conceptual contribution introduced by the Quantum Observable Universe.
