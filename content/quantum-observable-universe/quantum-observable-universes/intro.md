The remaining sections of this chapter collect the categorical,
algebraic, and analytic elaborations of the QOU axioms.  A reader
focussed on the main development may take the definitions above as
given and proceed to [Chapter 3](#chap:torsion); the elaboration
sections below fill in the TQFT context, the $q$-jacobiator, the
physical interpretation of the categorical action, and the
categorical lifting that prepares the Yekutieli tower.

The arc here is *expository* rather than *foundational*: each
elaboration block clarifies one aspect of the categorical
machinery (TQFT axiomatic stance, $q$-jacobiator identity,
physical reading of the categorical action, and the lifting
preliminaries) without introducing new structural axioms.
Readers who skipped to Chapter 3 can return to a specific
elaboration block when the corresponding construction appears in
later chapters; the blocks are independent of each other modulo
their shared dependence on the QOU axioms above.
