Let $R$ be the categorical ground ring and $\mathbb{P}(V)$ the projective line object. The **categorical-coordinate ring** of the chart-transition data of the deformation family $(\mathbb{P}_q(V))_{q \in R^\times}$ is

$$
  R[i, q^{± 1}] = R[q, q^{-1}][i] / (i^2 + 1).
$$

It acts as the smallest $R$-algebra extension wherein the chart-transition coefficient $q^{-1}$ is a unit and the tangent representation $\rho_p(\iota) = -1$ admits a square root. The chart-transition automorphism group factors as the semidirect product

$$
  \mathrm{Aut}_{R[i, q^{± 1}]}(U_0 \cap U_1, \mathbb{P}_q(V)) \cong \langle q \rangle \rtimes \langle \iota \rangle.
$$
