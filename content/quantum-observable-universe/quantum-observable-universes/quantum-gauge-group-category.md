The quantum gauge group operates fibrewise via the representation category $\mathrm{Rep}(\mathrm{SU}_{q(p)}(2))$. Tannaka--Krein duality reconstructs the Hopf algebra $\mathcal{O}_{q(p)}(\mathrm{SU}(2))$ through the forgetful fibre functor. The Hopf-algebra and representation-category viewpoints are equivalent across fibres.
