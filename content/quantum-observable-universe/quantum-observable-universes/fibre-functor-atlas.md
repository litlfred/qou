Let $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ be a quantum observable universe with gauge group $G$. For any two fibre functors $\tau', \tau'' \colon \mathbf{C} \to R\text{-}\mathbf{Mod}$, the space of monoidal natural isomorphisms $\tau' \Rightarrow \tau''$ forms a $G$-torsor, and functorial composition enforces the cocycle condition. Hence the category of all fibre functors of $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ forms a $G$-gerbe.
