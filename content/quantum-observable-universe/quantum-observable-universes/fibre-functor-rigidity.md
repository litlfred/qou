The fibre functor $\tau$ plays a rigid, passive role precisely because of the observability axiom. The condition $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathbf{C}}(\mathbf{1}, \mathrm{Ad}(A)) = 0$ is equivalent to the vanishing of the zeroth adjoint cohomology $H^0_G(\mathrm{Ad}(A)) = 0$. This kills all infinitesimal automorphisms of $\tau$. Tannaka--Krein reconstruction pins the functor uniquely up to natural isomorphism.
