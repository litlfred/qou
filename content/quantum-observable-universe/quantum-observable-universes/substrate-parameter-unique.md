Given a QOU $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$, the substrate parameter $q \in \mathrm{End}(\mathbf{1})$ is uniquely determined by the braiding spectrum and the positive-definite dagger.  Concretely, the spectral decomposition of $c_{V,V}$ pins the unordered pair $\{q, -q^{-1}\}$ (Step 2 of the proof), and positivity selects $q > 0$ (Step 3).  Since both steps are intrinsic, any two substrate parameters for the same QOU coincide.  Note: uniqueness is \emph{relative to the QOU data}; the value of $q$ may vary across $M$.
