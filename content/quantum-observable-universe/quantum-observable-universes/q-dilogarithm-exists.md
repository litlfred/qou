([existence of $\log_q$](#prop:q-logarithm-exists)), there exists a
level-2 iterated primitive $\mathrm{Li}_2^q \in \mathcal{H}_q^{\otimes 2}$
([$q$-dilogarithm](#def:q-dilogarithm)) satisfying the pentagon
identity.
