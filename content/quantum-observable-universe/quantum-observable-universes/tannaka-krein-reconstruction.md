By Tannaka--Krein duality, the coend $H = \mathrm{End}^{\otimes}(\tau) = \int^{X \in \mathbf{C}} \tau(X)^{\vee} \otimes_R \tau(X)$ carries a canonical Hopf algebra structure. This provides the monoidal equivalence $\mathbf{C} \simeq \mathrm{Rep}(H)$. For generic braiding, $H$ reconstructs the compact quantum group algebra $\mathcal{O}_q(G)$. The deformation parameter $q$ is determined by the braiding.
