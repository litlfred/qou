Self-duality means that the observer trace
$\Phi = \lambda \circ \mu$ and the observed state space are canonically
paired. The algebra $A$ does not require an external arena: it is
simultaneously the algebra of observables and the space on which those
observables act. In the language of rigid dualizing complex theory \cite{yekutieli2010}, the
Frobenius form $\lambda$ is the unique trace that makes $A$ its own
dualizing object.
