Maximal integral submanifolds of $H$ are Legendrian submanifolds. A $q$-torsion leaf is a Legendrian submanifold invariant under the $q$-deformed Reeb flow. For $q > 1$, the deformation generically breaks periodicity, demanding KAM-type persistence results. The failure of the Frobenius structure on $H$ forces the dimension bound and gives $q$-torsion leaves their rigidity.
