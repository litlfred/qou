We now develop the first-order differential structure on the Frobenius--Hopf object $A$. This machinery includes the categorical Reeb derivation, the projective-line object, the affine chart it carries, and the fibre-adjoint involution. Each of these structures is forced by the QOU axioms. We construct the categorical Reeb derivation $(\xi, \varTheta_A)$ from the weight decomposition and realize it physically within the left-covariant calculus. The function-algebra quotient then forms the projective line object, which carries an intrinsic contact affine chart. Finally, the fibre-adjoint involution embeds the projective line as a coadjoint orbit, establishing the observability axiom directly from the Frobenius pairing.
