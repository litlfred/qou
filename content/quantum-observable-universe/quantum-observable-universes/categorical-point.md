Let $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ be a
([quantum universe](#def:quantum-universe)).  A **categorical point**
of $A$ is a morphism

```tex
\begin{equation}\label{eq:categorical-point}
\begin{tikzcd}[column sep=3em]
  \mathbf{1} \ar[r, "p"] & A
\end{tikzcd}
\qquad\text{(categorical point)}
\end{equation}
```
