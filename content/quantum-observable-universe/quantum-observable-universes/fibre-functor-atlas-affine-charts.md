Every fibre functor $\tau' \colon \mathbf{C} \to R\text{-}\mathbf{Mod}$ serves as an affine chart, realizing the abstract categorical data over $R$; the canonical functor $\tau$ yields the contact affine chart on $\mathbb{P}(V)$. Under this reading each monoidal natural isomorphism acts as a transition function changing affine coordinates, so the $G$-gerbe of fibre functors is precisely the affine atlas of the quantum universe.
