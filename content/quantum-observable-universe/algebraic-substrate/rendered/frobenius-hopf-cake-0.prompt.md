# The Frobenius–Hopf substrate — three layers

F7.1 (P0). A three-layer cake stacked vertically. Bottom layer: the Hopf algebra structure $(m, \Delta, \eta, \varepsilon, S)$ on $A$, with the five string-diagram glyphs on the right (multiplication as a Y-shape, comultiplication as inverted Y, unit and counit as dots, antipode as a small loop). Middle layer: the Frobenius pairing $\int_A$, drawn as a coev / ev cup–cap pair, captioned "non-degenerate $(a,b) \mapsto \int_A ab$". Top layer: the Reeb derivation $\mathfrak{r}$, drawn as a single straight arrow, captioned "arrow of time on $A$". Each layer is a labelled rounded rectangle. The cake structure makes visible the layered axioms: the Hopf algebra is the substrate; the Frobenius pairing is the compatibility datum; the Reeb derivation is the dynamical step. Layout: 3 stacked rectangles, top → bottom = abstract → concrete (or future → past). Density: each layer carries a label, a tagline, and the relevant string-diagram glyphs. Style: schematic with string-diagram conventions on the right. Colour: monochrome.
