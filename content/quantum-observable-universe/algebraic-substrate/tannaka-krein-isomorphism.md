Tannaka--Krein duality establishes an equivalence between two perspectives
on the substrate:

$$
\mathbf{Rep}_{\mathrm{loc}}(A) \simeq \mathbf{Comod}(\mathcal{H}_\xi)
$$

where $\mathbf{Rep}_{\mathrm{loc}}(A)$ is the category of localized
$A$-module representations (the "monopoles" — modules supported at maximal
ideals) and $\mathbf{Comod}(\mathcal{H}_\xi)$ is the category of comodules over the
Hopf algebra $\mathcal{H}_\xi$ reconstructed from the Reeb spectrum. The fibre functor
$\tau$ underlying this equivalence sends each localized module to its fiber
at the unit defect.

This is a self-duality: the geometric side (localized representations at
defects) and the spectral side (global quantum group symmetries of the Reeb
flow) are equivalent descriptions of the same physical content. The
reconstruction theorem guarantees that $\mathcal{H}_\xi$ is uniquely determined by
the monoidal structure of
$\mathbf{Rep}_{\mathrm{loc}}(A)$ \cite{reshetikhin1991}.
