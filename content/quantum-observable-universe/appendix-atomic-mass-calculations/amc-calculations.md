Below, each element's atomic mass is computed step-by-step
from the topological mass formula.  The five steps are:

1. **Knot formula**: $Z\,\mathfrak{p} \bowtie N\,\mathfrak{n} \bowtie Z \cdot T_{2,3}$
2. **Free nucleon mass**: $M_{\mathrm{free}} = Z \cdot m_{\mathfrak{p}} + N \cdot m_{\mathfrak{n}}$
3. **Add electrons**: $M_{\mathrm{free+e}} = M_{\mathrm{free}} + Z \cdot m_e$
4. **Subtract binding**: $\mathcal{M}(Z,N) = M_{\mathrm{free+e}} - A \cdot (B/A)$
5. **Compare**: percentage deviation from AME2020 observed mass

$m_{\mathfrak{p}} = 938.27208943$ MeV,
$m_{\mathfrak{n}} = 939.56542194$ MeV,
$m_e = 0.51099895069$ MeV.
