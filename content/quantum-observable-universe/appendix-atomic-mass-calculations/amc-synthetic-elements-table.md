The QOU mass formula's bookkeeping for the 26 synthetic
elements ($Z = 93$ to $118$). All entries are marked with a dagger
($\dagger$) to indicate reactor- or accelerator-produced isotopes
without natural occurrence; the QOU formula treats them on the same
footing as naturally occurring elements once $(Z, N, B/A)$ is
supplied by AME2020.
