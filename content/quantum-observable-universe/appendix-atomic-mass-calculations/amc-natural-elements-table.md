prediction $M_q$, the observed mass $M_o$ (CODATA 2022 + AME2020),
the absolute deviation $|\Delta|$, and the relative deviation $\delta$
in percent. Because the formula recombines CODATA/AME2020 inputs
without an independent prediction (see the source classification
in [prose:amc-summary](#prose:amc-summary)), the deviations are
arithmetic-consistency checks, not predictive tests. Sub-keV
agreement across all 92 elements confirms the formula's bookkeeping.
