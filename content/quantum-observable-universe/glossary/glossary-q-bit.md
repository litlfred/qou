A **:defterm[$q$-bit]{#q-bit}** is the natural unit of information in a QOU
with substrate parameter $q$.  It is the information
generated by one application of the $q$-connection
$\nabla_q$ across a fibre — one crossing resolution in
the Iwahori--Hecke algebra $H_n(q)$
([skein generation](#prop:skein-generation)), equivalently
one iteration of the quadratic map $f_q(z) = z^2 + c(q)$
on the exceptional divisor.

**Definition.**  One $q$-bit $= \eta_q$ in classical
natural units, where

$$
  \eta_q =  \frac{2q - 1}{q^3}
$$

is the operational entropy
([operational entropy](#def:operational-entropy)).

**The two Hecke eigenvalues.**  The name "$q$-bit" reflects
the Iwahori--Hecke algebra's two eigenvalues $q$ (symmetric channel)
and $-q^{-1}$ (antisymmetric channel).  A classical bit
has eigenvalues $+1$ and $-1$; the $q$-bit deforms these
to $q$ and $-q^{-1}$.  At $q = 1$ the eigenvalues merge
to $\pm 1$ and one $q$-bit equals one classical nat.

**"It from $q$-bit."**  Wheeler's "it from bit" asserts
that physics emerges from information.  The QOU refines
this: **observable** physics emerges from $q$-deformed
information.  The observability axiom
($\mathrm{Hom}(\mathbf{1}, \mathrm{Ad}(A)) = 0$) forces
every observable quantity — mass, energy, coupling
constants, spacetime geometry — to be a rational function
of $q$, and each elementary process (crossing resolution,
braid evaluation, Reeb iteration) produces exactly one
$q$-bit.  The substrate parameter $q$ controls
non-commutativity, and the $q$-bit is the indivisible
unit of $q$-deformed information from which observable
"it" is built.
