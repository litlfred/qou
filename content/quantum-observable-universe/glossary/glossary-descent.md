Let $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ be a quantum observable universe and let
$\{A_n\}_{n \geq 0}$ be the
([recursive brane tower](#def\:recursive-brane-tower)).  The *:refterm[descent]{#descent}* is the
reverse traversal of the tower: starting from level $n$ one passes to
level $n{-}1$ by applying the restriction morphism
$r_n \colon A_n = J^1(A_{n-1}) \to A_{n-1}$ (the categorical retraction
of the jet embedding).  In the archimedean realisation, this restricts
the connection and bundle to the base manifold $M_{n-1}$.
By [QU-to-QOU lifting](#thm\:qu-to-qou)(1), the :refterm[descent]{#descent} terminates in finitely many
steps, bounded by $\mathrm{Krulldim} Z(\mathrm{End}_{\mathbf{C}}(A))$.
