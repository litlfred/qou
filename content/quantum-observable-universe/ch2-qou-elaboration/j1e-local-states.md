The jet bundle $J^1 E$ serves as the space of local states of the
$\mathbb{C}$ quantum observable universe. The classical gauge dynamics,
encoded by the *instanton connection* $A$
([Lemma](#lem:irreducible-connection)), are weighted by the substrate
parameter $q$ ([Definition](#def:substrate-parameter)) to define the
quantum field algebra $\mathrm{SU}_q(2)$
([Definition](#def:quantum-gauge-group)).
