**Proof.** The kernel collapse ([Frobenius–Hopf kernel](#lem:mobius-energy-frobenius-kernel)) forces $K_q(s,t) \to 1/r^2$ on the diagonal. Its coincidence residue defines the fundamental two-point contact $C(\varrho)$. By the Jones–Markov identity ([Jones–Markov trace](#thm:jones-markov-trace)), this is exactly the single-element Markov trace $\operatorname{tr}_M(\rho(\beta_K)) = J_K(q)$. The categorical Möbius energy is defined as the regularized two-point integral minus this contact—the O'Hara / Freedman–He–Wang Hadamard finite part. Rearranging the renormalization identity $E_{\mathrm{cat}} = T_{\mathrm{reg}} - C$ yields $C = T_{\mathrm{reg}} - E_{\mathrm{cat}}$, meaning $J_K = T_{\mathrm{reg}} - E_{\mathrm{cat}}$. The off-diagonal remainder strictly embeds the trace because it retains the fusion coefficients $N^\lambda_{\mu\nu}$ of the comultiplication, which the single-element trace cannot resolve. $\qquad\blacksquare$

**Surreal reading.** Under the surreal lift ([Möbius renormalization](#rem:mobius-surreal-renormalization)), the regularized two-point $I_1$ and the contact term $I_2$ are both infinite surreals of order $\operatorname{ord}_\omega = +1$. They share the same leading coefficient. This shared $\omega^{+1}$ divergence is precisely the contact/self-energy $J_K(q)$. The appreciable remainder is $E_{\mathrm{cat}} = \operatorname{std}(I_1 - I_2)$.

**Scope.** This establishes the fundamental ($N=2$) base of the embedding. The $N$-coloured contacts form the coloured-Markov-trace family $\operatorname{tr}_M^{(N)}(\rho(\beta_K))$. Its $N \to \infty$ logarithmic growth rate yields the hyperbolic volume ([Kashaev volume–density](#rem:kashaev-volume-density)). This lemma secures the $N=2$ base rung of that tower; it makes no claim about the limit itself.
