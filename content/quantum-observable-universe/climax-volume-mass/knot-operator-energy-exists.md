**Proposition.**
*Fix a partition $\lambda \vdash k$ with $k \leq k_{\max}$. Over the generic locus $\{q \in R : [k]_q! \neq 0 \;\forall k \leq k_{\max}\}$, the [knot operator energy](#def:knot-operator-energy) $E(\lambda, q) = |u_\lambda(q)|$ exists strictly within the absolute-value image $|R|$ of the base ring.*

*The shadow price $u_\lambda(q) = w_\lambda(q)/d_\lambda(q) - 1/[k]_q! \in R$ forms a rational function of $q$. Its denominator $[k]_q! \cdot d_\lambda(q)$ is strictly nonzero on the generic locus ([mass-coefficient-shadow-price](#prop:mass-coefficient-shadow-price)). The absolute value $|\cdot|: R \to |R|$ acts as follows:*

- *$R = \mathbb{R}$: uses the archimedean $|\cdot|_\infty$, landing in $\mathbb{R}_{\geq 0}$.*
- *$R = \mathbb{Q}_p$: uses the $p$-adic $|\cdot|_p$, landing in $\{p^{-n}\} \cup \{0\}$.*
- *$R = \mathbb{F}_p$: lacks an archimedean absolute value ($|R| = \emptyset$); the energy filtration collapses, and confinement defaults to ILP feasibility ([stable atom](#def:stable-atom)).*

*In every equipped base ring, $\lambda$ and $q$ uniquely determine $E(\lambda, q)$.*
