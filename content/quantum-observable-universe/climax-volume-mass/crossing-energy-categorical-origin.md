**Remark (crossing weights are Hecke eigenvalues, not postulates).**

The per-crossing weights $(q, -q^{-1})$ of the [crossing energy](#def:crossing-energy) are not energetic penalties added by hand. They are the exact eigenvalues of the Hecke generator $T = P \circ R \colon V \otimes V \to V \otimes V$ acting on the symmetric/antisymmetric decomposition. The rigid monoidal structure strictly dictates them:

1. **Rigidity fixes the $R$-matrix determinant.** The dualisability of $V$, combined with the categorical volume constraint ([volume constraint](#def:quantum-observable-universe)), forces the braiding $R = c_{V,V}$ to satisfy $\det(R|_{V \otimes V}) = 1$. Its eigenvalues on $S^{2} V \oplus \Lambda^{2} V$ are $(q, q^{-1})$.
2. **The Hecke generator carries the transposition sign.** The Hecke generator is $T = P \circ R$, where $P$ swaps the factors. Because $P|_{\Lambda^{2} V} = -\mathrm{id}$, the composition flips the sign on the antisymmetric summand. This yields $T$-eigenvalues $(q, -q^{-1})$ with $\det(T) = -1$, recovering the Hecke minimal polynomial $(T - q)(T + q^{-1}) = 0$ ([eigenvalue extraction](#prop:braiding-eigenvalue-extraction)).
3. **Crossing weights are eigenvalues.** Assigning weight $q$ to a positive crossing $L_{+}$ and weight $-q^{-1}$ to a negative crossing $L_{-}$ faithfully executes the action of $T$ on a symmetric channel and $T^{-1}$ on an antisymmetric channel. Thus, the sum $\mathfrak{E}_{\mathrm{cross}}(\mathfrak{H})$ acts as a trace of $T^{\pm 1}$-eigenvalues along the braid, compelled by the categorical structure.
4. **Gröbner--Shirshov resolution.** When reducing $\mathfrak{H}$ to Gröbner--Shirshov normal form ([nuclear normal form](#prop:nuclear-groebner-reduction)), each crossing contributes its $T$-eigenvalue precisely once. The net $\mathfrak{E}_{\mathrm{cross}}$ is the resulting Laurent polynomial in $q$, read directly off the normal form. Ignoring these weights violates duality and breaks the Hecke relation.

The $R$-matrix braiding ([R-matrix](#rem:r-matrix-braiding)) of $\mathcal{U}_q(\mathfrak{su}(2))$, acting on the tensor product of constituent representations, entirely determines this quantity.
