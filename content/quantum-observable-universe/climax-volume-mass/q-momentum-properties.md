**Proposition.**
The [$q$-momentum](#def:q-momentum) $p_q$ satisfies:

1. **Additivity under concatenation**:
   $p_q(\beta_1 \beta_2) = p_q(\beta_1) + p_q(\beta_2)$.
   This follows from the linearity of the trace on the Iwahori--Hecke algebra.

2. **Invariance under Markov I**:
   $p_q$ is conjugation-invariant. This follows from the cyclic property of the trace.

3. **Shift under Markov II**:
   Stabilization $\beta \mapsto \beta \sigma_n^{\pm 1}$ shifts $p_q$ by $\pm [1]_q$. We interpret this as adding one unit of momentum.

4. **Parity**:
   Under the mirror operation $\sigma: \beta \mapsto \beta^{\sigma}$ reversing all crossings, $p_q(\beta^{\sigma}) = -p_{q^{-1}}(\beta)$. At $q \neq 1$, $p_q(\beta^{\sigma}) \neq -p_q(\beta)$, indicating parity violation.
