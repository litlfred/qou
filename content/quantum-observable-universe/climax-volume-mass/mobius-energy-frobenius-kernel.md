**Lemma.**
*At $q = 1$, the $q$-Hodge star reduces to the classical Hodge star, and the Frobenius pairing $\int_A \circ \mu$ on $A \otimes A$ reduces to the standard $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ Killing form. The Frobenius--Hopf kernel collapses to the repulsive $1/r^2$:*

$$
  \mathrm{tr}\bigl(
    \Delta(\gamma(s)) \otimes \Delta(\gamma(t))
  \bigr)
  \xrightarrow{q \to 1}
  \frac{1}{\lvert \gamma(s) - \gamma(t) \rvert^2}.
$$
