**Proof.**
At $q = 1$, the $q$-Hodge star reduces to the classical Hodge star and the comultiplication $\Delta$ becomes cocommutative. The [Frobenius pairing](#def:frobenius-pairing) $\int_A \circ \mu$ on $A \otimes A$ reduces to the standard $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ Killing form. The integrand simplifies to

$$
  \mathrm{tr}\bigl(
    \Delta(\gamma(s)) \otimes \Delta(\gamma(t))
  \bigr)
  \xrightarrow{q \to 1}
  \frac{1}{\lvert \gamma(s) - \gamma(t) \rvert^2}.
$$

The repulsive kernel $1/r^2$ is the classical shadow of the resistance of the Frobenius--Hopf object $A$ to local deformations of the connection $\nabla$. The Frobenius form $\int_A$ assigns a diverging cost as two strands of $\gamma$ approach. $\square$
