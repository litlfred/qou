**Lemma.**
*For $q \neq 1$, the categorical mass of a vortex filament $\varrho$ inherits a kernel*

$$
  \frac{1}{\lvert \varrho(s) - \varrho(t) \rvert^2}
   \longmapsto 
  \frac{[2]_q}{\lvert \varrho(s) - \varrho(t) \rvert^2
  - \hbar_q^{2}},
$$

*where $[2]_q = q + q^{-1}$ and $\hbar_q = 1 - q^{-1}$. The kernel possesses a hard singularity at $\lvert \varrho(s) - \varrho(t) \rvert = \hbar_q$.*
