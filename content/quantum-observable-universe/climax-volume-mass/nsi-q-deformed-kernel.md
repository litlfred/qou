**Lemma.**
*For $q \neq 1$, the categorical mass of a vortex filament $\varrho$ inherits the kernel mapping:*

$$
  \frac{1}{\lvert \varrho(s) - \varrho(t) \rvert^2}
   \longmapsto 
  \frac{[2]_q}{\lvert \varrho(s) - \varrho(t) \rvert^2 - \hbar_q^{2}}.
$$

*Here $[2]_q = q + q^{-1}$ and $\hbar_q = 1 - q^{-1}$. The kernel produces a hard singularity at $\lvert \varrho(s) - \varrho(t) \rvert = \hbar_q$.*
