The mass operator relies on Hecke generators, but we must still specify their relations. We now use the rigid monoidal structure of the category to filter candidate braid configurations. 

Translating the quantum-universe axioms into polynomial relations on the Hecke generators gives an algebraic filter. Lifting these relations along the brane-tower jet bundle extends the filter globally. As an archimedean shadow, this process recovers the familiar Skein relations. The $(2,11)$ torus knot serves as the genus-bound test case detecting the ideal structure. We apply this relation algebra in the next section to factorise hadron composites.
