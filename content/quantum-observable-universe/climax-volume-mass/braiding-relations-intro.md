The mass operator of the previous section is built from Hecke generators, but we have not yet asked which Hecke relations the categorical axioms actually enforce. We now read those relations off the rigid monoidal structure and use them as a spectral filter on candidate braid configurations. The section pivots on **QU and QUO axioms as relations on Hecke generators** (prop), which translates the quantum-universe and quantum-universe-observation axioms into a finite list of polynomial relations on the generators, and on its prolongation **QU/QUO and cohomological relations on the jet bundle** (prop), which lifts those relations along the brane-tower jet bundle. The framing remark **Skein relations as an archimedean shadow** (rem) records that the familiar Skein relations are the archimedean image of the categorical identities, and the closing remark **Behaviour of $T_{2,11}$ on the jet bundle ideals** (rem) puts the filter to work, exhibiting the $(2,11)$ torus knot as the genus-bound test case that detects the ideal structure. The next section, **Hadron Algebra and Knot Factorisation**, takes the filtered relation algebra as fixed and uses it to define the hadron composite and its factorisation into constituent knots.
