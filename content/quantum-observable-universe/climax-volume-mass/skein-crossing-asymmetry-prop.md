The Skein relation weights positive crossings by $q$ and negative crossings by $q^{-1}$:

$$
  q  L_+ -  q^{-1} L_-
  =  (q^{1/2} - q^{-1/2}) L_0.
$$

For $q > 1$, the per-crossing repulsive Coulomb correction relative to a mixed-charge baseline is

$$
  \delta_{\mathrm{Coulomb}}
  =  q -  \tfrac{1}{2}(q + q^{-1})
  =  \tfrac{1}{2}(q - q^{-1})
   >  0,
$$

and the ratio of the antisymmetric (Coulomb) to symmetric (strong) Skein weights is

$$
  \frac{q - q^{-1}}{q + q^{-1}}
  =  \tanh(\ln q).
$$

At $q = 1$, the asymmetry vanishes. At $q \to \infty$, the ratio approaches $1$.
