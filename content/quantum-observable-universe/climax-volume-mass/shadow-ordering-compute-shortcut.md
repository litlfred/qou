**Remark (computational shortcut from local constancy).**
Per [unstable-atom](#def:unstable-atom) "Computational shortcut", expensive computations need exact evaluation only at points of the [phase-boundary variety](#prop:phase-boundary-variety) $\mathcal{B}$. These computations extract per-generator $\hat V_i^{\mathrm{full}}$ data in the tower formula [mass-endomorphism-tower](#prop:mass-endomorphism-tower). In the complement $Q \setminus \mathcal{B}$, all shadow prices, the filtration, and the per-generator data are locally constant. This follows from the [local constancy of confinement](#prop:confinement-local-constancy). We justify linear interpolation between nearest magic-number anchors mathematically.
