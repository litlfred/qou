**Remark (vanishing locus and cheap/expensive operators).**

**Vanishing locus.**
The vanishing locus $\{q \in Q : E(\lambda, q) = 0\}$ defines the LP boundary for the partition $\lambda$. At these exact $q$-values, the operator $\lambda$ becomes constraint-binding, triggering a switch in its basis inclusion. Any atom whose normal form incorporates $\lambda$ undergoes a categorical phase transition upon crossing this boundary.

**Cheap and expensive operators.**
The $1$-dimensional partitions $(k)$ and $(1^k)$ enforce $w_\lambda/d_\lambda = 1/[k]_q!$, identically setting $E(\lambda, q) = 0$. These operators demand no shadow-price weight and are perpetually available. Only mixing partitions ($d_\lambda \geq 2$) carry non-zero $E$ to populate the confinement filtration.
