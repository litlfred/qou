**Proposition.**
*The [confinement level](#def:confinement-level) $\ell(K, q)$ is locally constant on $Q \setminus \mathcal{B}$. For any $q \notin \mathcal{B}$, there exists an open neighbourhood $U \ni q$ such that $\ell(K, q') = \ell(K, q)$ for all $q' \in U$. The level jumps exclusively at the [phase-boundary variety](#prop:phase-boundary-variety) $\mathcal{B}$. These locus points define the categorical phase boundaries.*
