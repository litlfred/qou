**Proposition (Coulomb/strong ratio as a q-rational).**

*For every real $q > 0$ (the substrate-anchor regime, $\operatorname{Im}(q) = 0$ at the archimedean place),*

$$
  \tanh(\ln q) \;=\; \frac{q - q^{-1}}{q + q^{-1}} \;=\; \frac{q^2 - 1}{q^2 + 1}.
$$

*In particular, the Coulomb/strong ratio ($\operatorname{def:coulomb-charge}$), the analytic value $\tanh(\ln q_0)$, identically equals the q-rational expression $(q_0^2 - 1)/(q_0^2 + 1)$ at the substrate anchor. They form a single object, not two competing definitions.*

## Significance

By definition (`def:coulomb-charge`), the Coulomb-to-strong ratio is $\tanh(\ln q)$. This proposition verifies that its analytic and algebraic forms coincide identically over real $q > 0$. This eliminates any ambiguity regarding whether the framework deploys a "classical $\tanh$" or a "$q$-deformed $\tanh_q$" at the substrate anchor.

Because $\exp(\ln q) = q$ for $q > 0$, the equality follows directly:

$$
  \tanh(\ln q)
  \;=\; \frac{e^{\ln q} - e^{-\ln q}}{e^{\ln q} + e^{-\ln q}}
  \;=\; \frac{q - q^{-1}}{q + q^{-1}}
  \;=\; \frac{q^2 - 1}{q^2 + 1}.
$$
