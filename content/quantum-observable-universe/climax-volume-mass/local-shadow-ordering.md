**Definition.**
*Let $\lambda_1, \lambda_2$ be partitions with shadow prices $y_{\lambda_1}(q), y_{\lambda_2}(q) \in R$. The **local shadow-price ordering** at $q$ is:*

$$
  \lambda_1 \;\preceq_q\; \lambda_2
  \;\iff\;
  \exists\, U \ni q\;\text{open such that}\;
  |y_{\lambda_1}(q')| \;\leq\; |y_{\lambda_2}(q')|
  \;\;\forall q' \in U.
$$

*This asserts $\lambda_1$ carries at most the energy of $\lambda_2$ throughout a neighborhood of $q$.*
