Let $\varrho$ be a knot embedding with categorical Möbius energy $E_{\mathrm{cat}}(\varrho)$ as in [Proposition](#prop:mobius-energy), and let $q \neq 1$ be the substrate parameter ([Definition](#def:substrate-parameter)).

The non-cocommutativity of the Hopf structure at $q \neq 1$ provides a categorical obstruction to self-intersection. The energy $\mathfrak{m}(\varrho)$ diverges whenever two non-adjacent strands of $\varrho$ approach within the fibre $\tau(A)$.

Any generic categorical/$q$-deformation identities it relies upon remain valid over an abstract coefficient ring; only their archimedean realisation is specialised here.
