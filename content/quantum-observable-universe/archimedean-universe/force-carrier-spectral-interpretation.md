The denominator $[9]_q \cdot [10]_q$ in
([EM coupling projection](#prop:em-coupling-projection)) is forced by
three independent results:

1. The QOU existence axiom embeds the brane into a genus-$4$
   modular curve ([Bring's surface](#def:brings-surface)).
2. The genus-$4$ Diophantine classification
   ([genus-$4$ uniqueness](#prop:torus-knot-genus-four-unique))
   exhausts torus knots of that genus by the pair $\{T_{2,9},
   T_{3,5}\}$.
3. The categorical $q$-dimension assignment to torus simples is a
   fixed datum of $\mathbf{Rep}_q(\mathrm{SU}_q(2))$, not a choice.

The fine structure constant's denominator is therefore a topological
invariant of the Bring fibration, with no fitted parameter.

**Strong/weak labelling.**
The assignment $T_{2,9} \to$ strong, $T_{3,5} \to$ weak follows
from the lower crossing number being the dominant gauge boson
exchange ([force identification](#rem:force-knot-identification)).
This labelling does not affect the product $[9]_q \cdot [10]_q$ —
only the symbolic identification of which torus knot mediates which
sector.
