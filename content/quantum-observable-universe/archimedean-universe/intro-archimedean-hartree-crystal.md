The substrate-level constructions above sit on the categorical
side of the archimedean wall.  This section crosses the wall and
records the technical machinery that supports the crossing: a
Hartree-type self-consistent approximation that picks the
correct archimedean realisation, the Kashiwara crystal basis that
indexes the orthogonal decomposition compatible with that
approximation, and the Mathlib bridge that records which
classical-analytic identities (Clausen function, Bloch–Wigner
dilogarithm, Bloch-group quotients) the categorical construction
relies on.

The arc moves in three movements.  The first three blocks fix
the archimedean Hartree derivation and prove it unique (the
choice of fibre functor $\tau$ is forced by self-consistency).
The middle blocks promote the crystal-basis decomposition into a
resolution of the substrate-level mass-tower open problems: the
Type-B folded crystal makes the open-problem class
combinatorially tractable, and the Bloch-group quotient supplies
the cohomological obstruction that pins the Mathlib filler
content.  The closing blocks survey the deeper Mathlib fillers
needed and assess where the substrate-level statements end and
the archimedean specialisations begin.

A note on scope: this section's main consumers are
$q$-geometric-Langlands and the Lean blueprint; its open problems
are tracked as separate categorical conjectures that the next
chapter cycles attack.  Readers focused on substrate-level
statements can skim the Mathlib-filler blocks on first reading.
