The Borromean braid $\mathcal{B}_3 = (\sigma_0 \sigma_1^{-1})^3$ is a
concrete element of the braid group $B_3$, hence of the Hecke algebra
$H_3(q)$ via the standard representation.  Typed crossings for
$(\mathfrak{u}, \mathfrak{u}, \mathfrak{d})$ and the Hopf-link gluon
stabiliser $L_- = \sigma_1 \sigma_0$ are also elements of $H_3(q)$.
Their product reduces via Gröbner--Shirshov in $H_3(q)$ to a
canonical Hecke normal form, verified to satisfy
$G x[\gamma] \ge 0$ at every intermediate step by the LP-dual
feasibility check.  Hence $\mathfrak{p}$ exists as a well-defined,
LP-stable element.
