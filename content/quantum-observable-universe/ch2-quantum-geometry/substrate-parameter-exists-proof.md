
Categorically, one chooses $q_0 \in \mathrm{End}(\mathbf{1}) \cong k$
transcendental (hence not a root of unity). In the archimedean
realisation, the constant substrate field $q(p) = q_0 > 1$ for all $p$
satisfies the classicality bound $q \ge 1$ and positivity. Non-constant
substrate fields arise from the Reeb flow parameterisation of the
categorical $\mathrm{End}(\mathbf{1})$-valued coordinate.
