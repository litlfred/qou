The **neutron** is the baryon with $1 + 2$ crossing partition:
one up quark and two down quarks in the Borromean braid
$\mathcal{B}_3$ ([Borromean quark](#conj:borromean-quark)).

$$
  \mathfrak{n} = \mathcal{B}_3(\mathfrak{u},\mathfrak{d},\mathfrak{d})
$$

with one $\mathfrak{u}$ ([up quark](#def:up-quark)) and two
$\mathfrak{d}$ ([down quark](#def:down-quark)) threaded through the
Borromean braid. The normal-form construction, mass, transition to
the proton, and Coulomb charge are developed in the
[properties remark](#rem:categorical-neutron-properties).
