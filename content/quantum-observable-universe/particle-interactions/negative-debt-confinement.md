The NF$_+$ / NF$_-$ decomposition of the nucleon normal forms
reveals a quantitative signature of confinement: the neutron
carries significantly more internal cancellation than the proton.

## The negative coefficient debt

Each nucleon's Gröbner--Shirshov normal form in $H_3(q_0)$ splits
into positive and negative coefficient sums:

| | NF$_+$ | NF$_-$ | Net |
|:---:|---:|---:|---:|
| **Neutron** | $+1.0664$ | $-0.2555$ | $+0.8108$ |
| **Proton** | $+1.1420$ | $-0.1429$ | $+0.9991$ |
| **$\Delta$** | $-0.0756$ | $-0.1127$ | $-0.1883$ |

The neutron's negative coefficient debt $|\mathrm{NF}_-(\mathfrak{n})| =
0.2555$ exceeds the proton's by $|\Delta\mathrm{NF}_-| = 0.1127$.
This excess debt is **$11.3\%$** of the proton's net coefficient
$\mathrm{net}(\mathfrak{p}) = 0.9991$.

The dominant source is the $L_+$ sign flip: the $d$--$d$ typed
crossing makes the Hopf-link coefficient negative in the neutron
($-0.010$) while it is positive in the proton ($+0.099$).  This
single channel contributes $|\Delta(L_+)|/\mathrm{net}(\mathfrak{p})
\approx 10.9\%$ --- approximately the entire excess debt.

## Confinement interpretation

The internal cancellation ratio
$|\mathrm{NF}_-|/(|\mathrm{NF}_-| + \mathrm{NF}_+)$ measures
how much of the total coefficient magnitude is consumed by
destructive interference:

- **Neutron**: $19.3\%$ cancellation
- **Proton**: $11.1\%$ cancellation
- **Difference**: $8.2$ percentage points

The neutron's larger cancellation reflects its $[\mathfrak{u},
\mathfrak{d}, \mathfrak{d}]$ composition: the $d$--$d$ crossing
generates a $-\mathrm{HA} \cdot \mathbf{1}$ correction that
propagates through the Gröbner--Shirshov reduction, flipping the
$L_+$ coefficient and amplifying the $\gamma$ debt.  This is a
computable, algebraic manifestation of color confinement --- the
energy cost of holding three quarks in a color-singlet braid is
larger for the neutron because the $d$--$d$ interaction is more
confining than $u$--$u$.
