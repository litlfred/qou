**Proposition (far-commutation obstruction — the non-abelian
$\mathrm{SU}(2)_L$ structure is not carried by the slots).**
*Let $A$ be a ring, and let $\sigma_1, \sigma_3, \sigma_5 \in A$ be the
braid generators hosting the electroweak carriers on the disjoint
strand-pairs $1\text{--}2 \mid 3\text{--}4 \mid 5\text{--}6$ of
[`thm:hoefsmit-ew-cell-content`](#thm:hoefsmit-ew-cell-content), subject
to the braid far-commutation relation $\sigma_i \sigma_j = \sigma_j
\sigma_i$ for $|i - j| \ge 2$. Let $\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{slot}} \subseteq
A$ be the subalgebra they generate. If $J_1, J_2, J_3 \in A$ realise an
$\mathfrak{su}(2)_L$ adjoint structure relation $\lbrack J_1, J_2 \rbrack
= J_3$ with $J_3 \ne 0$, then $J_1$ and $J_2$ cannot both lie in
$\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{slot}}$. The non-abelian $\mathrm{SU}(2)_L$
structure therefore cannot be realised purely among the far-commuting
slots.*

### Statement (formal)

The slot indices $1, 3, 5$ are pairwise far apart,
$$
  |1 - 3| = 2, \qquad |1 - 5| = 4, \qquad |3 - 5| = 2,
$$
so the far-commutation relation applies to every pair: $\sigma_1, \sigma_3,
\sigma_5$ pairwise commute. Consequently the subalgebra
$\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{slot}} = \langle \sigma_1, \sigma_3, \sigma_5
\rangle$ is **commutative** — every commutator
$\lbrack x, y \rbrack = xy - yx$ vanishes for $x, y \in
\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{slot}}$. In particular
the three carriers $W \leftrightarrow \sigma_1^{4}$, $Z \leftrightarrow
\sigma_3^{6}$, $H \leftrightarrow \sigma_5^{8}$ pairwise commute.

The obstruction is the incompatibility of two facts:

1. **Commuting operators have vanishing bracket.** For $x, y \in
   \mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{slot}}$, $\lbrack x, y \rbrack = 0$.
2. **The $\mathfrak{su}(2)_L$ adjoint is non-abelian.** Its generators
   satisfy $\lbrack J_a, J_b \rbrack = \varepsilon_{abc} J_c \ne 0$; a
   nonzero bracket forbids commutation of the generators that carry it.

If both $J_1, J_2 \in \mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{slot}}$, then $\lbrack J_1, J_2
\rbrack = 0$ by (1), contradicting $J_3 \ne 0$ by (2). Hence at least one
$\mathfrak{su}(2)_L$ generator escapes the slot subalgebra.

### What this forces (localization, not closure)

The result is a **structural exclusion** (a proved impossibility, not a
numerical near-miss): the invariant that forbids the realisation is the
identical vanishing of the commutator on a commutative subalgebra. It
does **not** fit the gauge group into the framework by fiat, nor does it
supply the Weinberg angle. What it establishes is *where the missing
structure must live*.

The far-commutation relation covers only the pairs with $|i - j| \ge 2$.
The adjacent generators $\sigma_2, \sigma_4$ — bridging strand-pairs
$2\text{--}3$ and $4\text{--}5$ — have
$$
  |2 - 1| = |2 - 3| = |4 - 3| = |4 - 5| = 1 < 2,
$$
so they are exactly the generators the relation does not cover, and they
do **not** commute with the slots. The categorical linking operation
$\mathrm{Lk}_R^{(k)}$ of
[`conj:categorical-linking-operation`](#conj:categorical-linking-operation)
braids *between* strand-pairs through precisely these generators.
Therefore the non-abelian $\mathrm{SU}(2)_L$ structure must be carried by
the **inter-slot linking**, not by the individual slots.

This **narrows** the group-assignment postulate $\texttt{EWGroupAssignment}$
of
[`thm:ew-triplet-charge-spin-from-involution`](#thm:ew-triplet-charge-spin-from-involution).
The residual $\mathrm{SU}(2)_L \times \mathrm{U}(1)_Y$ input — which slot
is the neutral Cartan member $W^3 \to Z$ versus the charged root member
$W^\pm$ — is a property of the bracket-carrying linking data, not of the
mutually commuting slots. This is consistent with the prior finding that
no single-slot topological invariant forces the label
([`thm:ew-triplet-charge-spin-from-involution`](#thm:ew-triplet-charge-spin-from-involution),
`which_slot_is_W_free`): the label lives where the non-abelianness lives —
in the linking, not the slots.

### Lean structure

The formalisation is purely algebraic, over a generic ring (no
archimedean structure):

- `bracket_eq_zero_of_commute` — commuting elements have zero commutator.
- `nonabelian_bracket_forbids_commute` — the crux: $\lbrack x, y \rbrack =
  z \ne 0 \implies$ $x, y$ do not commute.
- `commute_of_mem_closure` — the submonoid generated by a pairwise-commuting
  set is commutative (via the centralizer submonoid).
- `slotSubmonoid_commutative`, `carriers_commute` — the slot subalgebra is
  mutually abelian; the carriers $\sigma_1^4, \sigma_3^6, \sigma_5^8$
  pairwise commute.
- `su2L_nonabelianness_not_in_slots`, `su2L_generator_escapes_slots` — the
  capstone obstruction and its positive form.
