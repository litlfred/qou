*Proof.*
*We construct the normal form (NF) $T_w \in H_{3(Z+N)}(q)$ explicitly.*

*First, we specify the intra-nucleon structure. Each Borromean baryon ([borromean baryon](#def:borromean-baryon))
possesses a canonical NF in $H_3(R, q)$ on $3$ strands. Let $T_p, T_n \in H_3(R, q)$ denote the proton and neutron NFs.*

*Second, we embed these into the many-body algebra. The disconnected tensor
$T_p^{\otimes Z} \otimes T_n^{\otimes N} \in H_3(R, q)^{\otimes (Z+N)}$ embeds into $H_{3(Z+N)}(q)$ via the
block-diagonal inclusion. This includes only the intra-nucleon strands.*

*Third, we connect the baryons. The inter-nucleon connector $w_{\mathrm{inter}} \in
S_{3(Z+N)}$ is the permutation that realizes the pp, nn, and pn
crossings between adjacent baryon blocks
([atomic braid](#def:atomic-braid) crossing table).*

*Fourth, we assemble the product. The product $T_w = T_{w_{q^Z}} \cdot T_{w_{\bar q^N}} \cdot
T_{w_{\mathrm{inter}}}$ is a well-defined element of
$H_{3(Z+N)}(q)$. Its Markov closure $\hat\beta = \hat T_w$
forms a closed braid in $S^3$.*

*The Diamond Lemma for the Hecke algebra
([hecke normal form](#def:hecke-normal-form)) guarantees that the NF
remains independent of the NF-rewriting order. Thus, the constructed atom-knot
is well-defined.* $\square$
