*Construct the NF $T_w \in H_{3(Z+N)}(q)$ explicitly:*

1. *Each Borromean baryon ([borromean baryon](#def:borromean-baryon))
   has a canonical NF in $H_3(R, q)$ on $3$ strands.  Let $T_p, T_n
   \in H_3(R, q)$ denote the proton and neutron NFs.*
2. *The disconnected tensor
   $T_p^{\otimes Z} \otimes T_n^{\otimes N} \in
   H_3(R, q)^{\otimes (Z+N)}$ embeds into $H_{3(Z+N)}(q)$ via the
   block-diagonal inclusion (intra-nucleon strands only).*
3. *The inter-nucleon connector $w_{\mathrm{inter}} \in
   S_{3(Z+N)}$ is the permutation realising the pp, nn, pn
   crossings between adjacent baryon blocks
   ([atomic braid](#def:atomic-braid) crossing table).*
4. *The product $T_w = T_{w_{q^Z}} \cdot T_{w_{\bar q^N}} \cdot
   T_{w_{\mathrm{inter}}}$ is a well-defined element of
   $H_{3(Z+N)}(q)$.  Its Markov closure $\hat\beta = \hat T_w$
   is a closed braid in $S^3$.*

*The Diamond Lemma for the Hecke algebra
([hecke normal form](#def:hecke-normal-form)) guarantees the NF
is independent of NF-rewriting order, so the constructed atom-knot
is well-defined.
$\qquad\blacksquare$
