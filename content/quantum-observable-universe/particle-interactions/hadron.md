A *hadron* is a **stable** composite of fundamental knots: one
whose crossing energy is negative, so that binding dominates
repulsion.

Let $K_1, \ldots, K_n$ be prime knot types in the representation
category $\mathbf{C}$, and let $\mathcal{L}$ be a link encoding
their internal entanglement.  A **hadron** is a composite

$$
  \mathfrak{H}_p =  [K_1, \ldots, K_n]_p  \oplus  \mathcal{L}
$$

satisfying the **stability condition**

$$
  \mathfrak{E}_{\mathrm{cross}}(\mathfrak{H}_p)  <  0
$$

where $\mathfrak{E}_{\mathrm{cross}}$ is the crossing energy
([crossing energy](#def:crossing-energy)).  The subscript $p$
is the localization point: it determines the colour sector of the
exceptional divisor $\mathcal{S}_p$ in which the constituents are coupled.
The colour-tube coupling is not an independent datum --- it is
inherited from $p$.  The symbol $\oplus$ denotes the linking
operation.


**Archimedean specialisation (§7c).** This block is stated over the real field $\mathbb{R}$ — the archimedean side of the substrate wall: its constructs are real-valued (physical observables, real-analytic bounds, or numerical evaluations at the substrate parameter $q_0$) rather than generic-ring $q$-deformation data. The generic categorical / $q$-deformation machinery it draws on is stated over an abstract coefficient ring in its own blocks; only the archimedean realisation is specialised here.
