**Remark (Jacobian-level parallel).**
The weight identification of
[prop:x5-bring-weight-identification](#prop:x5-bring-weight-identification)
has a companion statement at the level of Jacobians.
Writing $H^{1}(\,\cdot\,, \mathbb{Q})$ for rational first cohomology,
both $H^{1}(\mathcal{X}_5, \mathbb{Q})$ and $H^{1}(\mathcal{B}_{\mathrm{Br}}, \mathbb{Q})$
carry natural $\mathbb{Z}_5$-actions.  Under the cyclic action the
non-trivial $\mathbb{Z}_5$-isotypical decomposition is

$$
  H^{1}(\mathcal{X}_5, \mathbb{Q})_{\neq 0}
    =  \chi_1 \oplus \chi_2 \oplus \chi_3 \oplus \chi_4
    =  H^{1}(\mathcal{B}_{\mathrm{Br}}, \mathbb{Q})_{\neq 0}^{\mathrm{cyc}},
$$

where $\chi_k$ is the character $\sigma \mapsto \zeta_5^{\,-k}$ and the
The right-hand side is the non-trivial $\mathbb{Z}_5$-isotypical part of
Bring's rational $H^1$ (the remaining weight-$0$ summand is contained
in the $S_5$-standard isotype and does not see the cyclic structure).

**Not a Prym embedding.**  Because Bring has no genus-$2$ Galois
quotient as a curve, the standard Prym construction does not give a
direct embedding $J(\mathcal{X}_5) \hookrightarrow J(\mathcal{B}_{\mathrm{Br}})$.
What the character equality above records is a compatibility of
$\mathbb{Z}_5$-representations: both Jacobians carry the same
non-trivial $\mathbb{Z}_5$-structure, realised on abelian varieties of
different dimensions via different underlying curve geometries.

**Significance.**  Taken together with
[prop:x5-bring-weight-identification](#prop:x5-bring-weight-identification),
the coordinate and Jacobian compatibilities say that the cyclic
$\mathbb{Z}_5$-observable structure of the genus-$2$ substrate
$\mathcal{X}_5$ is a faithful shadow of the cyclic observable
structure of the full $S_5$-substrate (Bring).  Passing from Bring
to $\mathcal{X}_5$ discards the $A_5$-factor but preserves the
cyclic Sylow-$5$ residue exactly.
Edge \cite{edge1978} records the classical relation between Bring and
its hyperelliptic quotients; the present framing extracts the
categorical essential that both surfaces carry compatible
$\mathbb{Z}_5$-graded coordinate rings.
