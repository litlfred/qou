The Borromean braid $\mathcal{B}_3 = (\sigma_0 \sigma_1^{-1})^3$
is a **local** minimum of the categorical action, not a global
one.

## Local versus global structure of the confinement LP

### Global minimum

The [confinement LP](#prop:confinement-lp) with the corrected
Gram matrix (using $\mathrm{tr}_M(e^-) = z^3$) admits a
global minimum at $\mathrm{tr}_M = 0$ — a massless confined
state.  The Borromean solution $\mathcal{B}_3$ is a local
minimum at a vertex of the LP feasible polytope.

### Corrected Gram matrix

With the corrected Markov trace
([corrected trace](#prop:corrected-markov-trace)),
the $6 \times 6$ Gram matrix $G_{ij} = \mathrm{tr}_M(b_i
\cdot b_j)$ has a **negative eigenvalue** $\approx -0.956$.
The indefinite signature means the Frobenius form is not
positive definite — the LP feasible region is unbounded
in the direction of the negative eigenvector.

### Eigenvalue selection

The confinement LP selects $\mathcal{B}_3$ via the
$\min|\lambda|$ eigenvalue criterion (not $\max$).  The
minimum absolute eigenvalue of the Gram matrix restricted
to the net-constraint hyperplane picks out the Borromean
solution as the **least unstable** confined state — the
one closest to the boundary between confinement and
deconfinement.
