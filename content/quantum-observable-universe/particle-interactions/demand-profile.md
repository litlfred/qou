The **demand profile** of a derived atom
([derived atom](#def:derived-atom)) — the set of negative NF
coefficients — is the categorical diagnostic of stability.

**Definition.**
*For a derived atom $\mathcal{A}(Z, N) = \sum_w c_w \cdot w$ in
NF, the **demand profile** is:*

$$
  \mathrm{dem}(\mathcal{A})
  =
  \{(w, c_w) : c_w < 0\}
  \subset
  \mathrm{NF}(B_A) \times \mathbb{R}_{<0}.
$$

*The **demand magnitude** is $|\mathrm{dem}| = \sum_{c_w < 0} |c_w|$.*
