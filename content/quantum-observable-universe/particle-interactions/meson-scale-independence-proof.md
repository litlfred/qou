**Proof (representation-theoretic + topological).**

The baryon mass formula uses $\mathrm{Vol}(\mathcal{B}_3) / \hbar_q^2$ as the geometric normalization. $\mathrm{Vol}(\mathcal{B}_3) = 8G > 0$ is the hyperbolic volume of the Borromean complement. The meson's binding topology is the Hopf link $L_+$. Its complement is a solid torus: $\mathrm{Vol}(\text{Hopf complement}) = 0$. Without a non-zero hyperbolic volume, there is no geometric invariant to set the meson mass scale from $(m_\mathfrak{e}, q_0)$.

The Hecke character ratios under additional crossings are derived: $\chi^{(3)}(K)/\chi^{(3)}(\pi) = q^2$, $\chi^{(2,1)}(K)/\chi^{(2,1)}(\pi) = 1/(h^2+1)$, $\chi^{(1^3)}(K)/\chi^{(1^3)}(\pi) = q^{-2}$. These determine $m_K/m_\pi$ (given $m_\pi$) but not $m_\pi$ itself. $\square$
