The previous section produced a relation algebra on Hecke generators. We now use those relations to single out the configurations that bind into stable composites. A hadron is a knot composite whose Coulomb charge and crossing energy place it in the stability stratum of the relation algebra; an algebraic Gröbner reduction then factorises any closed strand uniquely into hadron constituents.

The section opens with Definition: Hadron (stable knot composite), which fixes a hadron as a knot composite whose Coulomb charge satisfies the stability inequality, and Remark: Stability condition and abstract character of the hadron collects the structural conditions that survive the abstract / categorical reading. The pair Proposition: Existence of hadrons and Proposition: Uniqueness of hadrons up to braid isotopy, with their proof blocks, secure the two halves of well-definedness: that the stability stratum is non-empty and that any two minimal-energy representatives differ by a braid isotopy. The remark Remark: Operations on hadrons and the supporting proposition (closure under the hadron operations) record the natural product, sum, and conjugation maps; Remark: Knot factorization through tensor powers of $V$ makes the connection to the Pawlowski--Leitner basis explicit. The section closes with Definition: Localized hadron, which adds a localisation datum to the hadron — a marked point at which the categorical mass concentrates — and the refinement Definition: Fibre-compatible hadron embedding, which packages the localisation as a fibre-compatible embedding ready for the Möbius-energy machinery upstream.
