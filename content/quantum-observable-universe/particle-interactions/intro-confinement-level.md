Confinement in the categorical setting is a stratification rather
than a binary property: each composite braid word carries a
*confinement level* $\ell \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}$ recording how
many Hecke-reduction steps separate it from a trivial closure.
The cemetery group is the categorical subgroup of braid words
quotient-trivial under the substrate flow — confinement level
infinity, the limit of the stratification.

We first define the confinement level via the Hecke-reduction
filtration, prove it is preserved under Markov moves, and
identify the cemetery group as the kernel of the substrate-flow
quotient.  The closing blocks compute the confinement level for
the standard particle braid words (electron, muon, proton, etc.)
and tabulate the cemetery group's generators.  The output is
the level function that the next section uses to organise the
confined-particle shells.
