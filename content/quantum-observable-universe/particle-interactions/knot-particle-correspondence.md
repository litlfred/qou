**Proposition.**
Let $V = V_{1/2}$ be the fundamental representation of
$\mathcal{U}_q(\mathfrak{su}(2))$ and let $B_n$ act faithfully on
$V^{\otimes n}$ via the $R$-matrix braiding
([braid action on tensor products](#prop:braid-action-tensor)).
By $q$-Schur--Weyl duality
([knot eigenbasis completeness](#prop:knot-eigenbasis-complete)),
$V^{\otimes n}$ decomposes into irreducible summands labelled by
partitions $\lambda \vdash n$.

Then the Markov trace on $H_n(R, q)$ assigns to each irreducible summand
a knot type $K_\lambda$ (the closure of any braid representative),
establishing a bijection between:

- Irreducible summands of $V^{\otimes n}$ under the Iwahori--Hecke algebra $H_n(R, q)$, and
- Knot/link types arising as closures of $n$-strand braids.

In particular, each localized knot ([localized knot](#def:localized-knot))
$(p, K)$ in a bundle corresponds to a unique irreducible summand, and
hadrons ([hadron](#def:hadron)) correspond to tensor products of such summands
bound by Hopf links.
