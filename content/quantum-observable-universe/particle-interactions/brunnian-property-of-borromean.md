**Conjecture (Brunnian property of $\mathcal{B}_3$).** *Let $\mathcal{B}_3 \subset \mathbb{R}^3$ be the standard Borromean link, presented as the closed braid $\overline{(\sigma_0\sigma_1^{-1})^3}$ in $B_3$. For each $i \in \{0, 1, 2\}$, let $\mathcal{B}_3 \setminus K_i$ denote the two-component sub-link obtained by removing the $i$-th component. Then $\mathcal{B}_3 \setminus K_i$ is topologically equivalent to the trivial two-component unlink $U_2$.*

This formalizes the confinement mechanism. Removing any single component dissolves the binding between the remaining two. This is the defining property of Brunnian links \cite{kawauchi1996}. Naive strand-deletion at the braid word level does not immediately yield the empty braid. Deleting the 0-th strand produces $(\sigma_0^{-1})^3 \in B_2$, whose closure is the negative trefoil $\overline{3_1}$. Topological equivalence to $U_2$ requires an explicit sequence of Reidemeister moves: two R2 moves and one R3 move.
