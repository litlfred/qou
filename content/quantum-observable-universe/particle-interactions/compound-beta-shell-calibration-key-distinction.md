**Non-magic vs magic β-decays (key distinction).**

A β-decay $(Z, N) \to (Z+1, N-1)$ in the Hecke algebra corresponds to
the operator transition tabulated in
[Table 1](#tbl:compound-beta-shell-calibration-table1-data).

For magic-crossing decays, the Q-value reflects the **difference of
shell-operator contributions** between parent and daughter, not the
shell magnitude itself. The parent contains $\sigma_{\mathrm{shell}}$
(via [shell self-duality](#prop:shell-schur-weyl-self-duality)) and the
daughter contains a **different** shell operator or a cross-shell
transition ([shell-transition knot](#conj:shell-transition-knot)); the
Q-value measures the **difference of shell-operator contributions**,
not either side individually.
