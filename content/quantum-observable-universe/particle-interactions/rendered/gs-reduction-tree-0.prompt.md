# The Gröbner–Shirshov reduction tree

F11.1 (P0). A rooted tree showing one full Gröbner–Shirshov reduction. Root at top: the unreduced Hecke word $T_w = \sigma_1 \sigma_2 \sigma_1 \sigma_2^{-1} \sigma_1 \sigma_2 \in H_3(q)$. Three downward branches, each annotated with a rewrite-rule label: *by braid* (σ₁σ₂σ₁ = σ₂σ₁σ₂), *by Skein* (σ²-relation), *by Markov* (σ_n^{±1} stabilisation). Each child node carries the resulting Hecke word; a second layer applies cancellations / collections / further braid moves; a third layer reaches the leaves — three normal-form monomials. The lex-smallest leaf is highlighted with a red border and a "★ chosen" marker, indicating the canonical normal form. Footer notes the Knuth–Bendix confluence that makes the choice well-defined, and mentions the GS engine's role in the LP/SDP operator selector (Ch 11 §3). Layout: rooted tree, 3 layers, 7 nodes total. Density: each node carries 1 Hecke word + 1 status label; edges carry the rule. Style: schematic boxes connected by arrows. Colour: monochrome plus red on the canonical leaf.
