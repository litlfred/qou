# Hecke spectral gap collapse: q → ±i ⇒ deconfinement

F12.4 (P1, sibling #633). Three miniatures of the complex-$q$ plane arranged left-to-right. Each miniature shows the unit circle, two red dots at $q = \pm i$ (Kauffman degeneracy points), and a shaded "feasible cone" wedge avoiding those points. Miniature 1 ($q \approx 1.1$): wide feasible cone, $q$ marked as a green dot well inside; caption "confined; wide feasible cone". Miniature 2 ($q$ closer to $\pm i$): narrower wedge, $q$ as orange dot; caption "wedge narrows". Miniature 3 ($q = \pm i$): wedge collapses to empty; caption "deconfinement; feasible region collapses". Annotation: $\Delta_\sigma(q) = |q + q^{-1}| \to 0$ as $q \to \pm i$. Footer: "spectral gap is the categorical signature of confinement". Layout: 3 mini complex planes in a row. Density: per panel — unit circle, ±i markers, feasible wedge, captions. Style: schematic. Colour: monochrome plus red on the Kauffman points and pink shading on the wedge.
