**Definition.**
*Fix the substrate parameter $q$.  Sort the Wedderburn-block knot
operators by energy
([knot operator energy](#def:knot-operator-energy)):*

$$
  0 \;<\; E_1(q) \;\leq\; E_2(q) \;\leq\; \cdots \;\leq\; E_m(q),
$$

*where $E_\ell(q)$ is the $\ell$-th distinct nonzero value of
$\{E(\lambda, q) : \lambda \vdash k,\; k \leq k_{\max}\}$.
Define the **confinement filtration** $J^\bullet(q)$ of $H_n(R, q)$
by:*

$$
  J^\ell(q)
  \;:=\;
  \text{subalgebra generated by}
  \;\{\lambda : E(\lambda, q) \leq E_\ell(q)\}.
$$

*Tied operators (those with $E(\lambda_i, q) = E(\lambda_j, q)$)
enter the filtration **together** at the level of their common
energy — the filtration is well-defined under degeneracy.*

*A closed braid $K$ with Hecke normal form
([Hecke normal form](#def:hecke-normal-form)) $T_w \in H_n(R, q)$
is **confined at level $\ell$ at $q$** iff $\ell$ is the smallest
integer with $T_w \in J^\ell(q)$.  Write
$\ell(K, q) := \min\{\ell : T_w \in J^\ell(q)\}$.*

**$q$-dependence.**
*The filtration is intrinsically $q$-dependent: the relative
ordering of $E(\lambda, q)$ shifts with $q$, so a knot may have
$\ell(K, q_1) \neq \ell(K, q_2)$ for $q_1 \neq q_2$.  Two regimes
of $q$-dependence:*

1. *Reordering: $E(\lambda_i, q)$ and $E(\lambda_j, q)$ swap
   relative position at some $q^* \in Q$ — the filtration
   re-indexes but the set of operators at each level is
   continuous.*

2. *Vanishing: $E(\lambda_\ell, q^*) = 0$ at the LP boundary
   ([knot operator energy](#def:knot-operator-energy)) — the
   operator drops out of the filtration entirely, $J^\ell$
   collapses, and the level structure rebuilds.  This is a
   categorical phase transition.*

**Per-atom truncation $k_{\max}(K, q)$ — witnessed, not chosen.**
*The truncation $k_{\max}$ is **not a global pragmatic parameter**.
It is determined per atom-knot $K$ at $q$ by the **interaction-
strand requirement**: $k_{\max}(K, q)$ is the smallest $k$ such
that the NF $T_w$ of $K$ does not require any partition of size
$> k$ to express its operator content.  Concretely:*

1. *Compute the NF $T_w \in H_{n(K)}(q)$.*
2. *Determine the maximal $k \leq n(K)$ such that the operator
   $T_w$ has a nonzero projection on some Wedderburn block
   $\lambda \vdash k$ with $d_\lambda \geq 2$ (mixing rep).*
3. *Set $k_{\max}(K, q)$ to this value.*

*Non-magic atoms typically have small $k_{\max}$ (few generators
needed for the interaction graph).  Magic configurations have
larger $k_{\max}$ because shell closure forces additional
operator content.  This is computed once per atom-knot and
**witnessed** as part of $K$'s NF data — not chosen externally.*

**Magic configurations.**
*A confined knot $K$ at $q$ is **deeply confined** at level $\ell$
iff $E_\ell(q) \ll E_{\ell+1}(q)$ — there is a large energy gap
to the next operator.  In nuclear contexts this corresponds to
**magic numbers** $A \in \{2, 8, 20, 28, 50, 82, 126\}$: the
nucleus sits at the bottom of an energy gap, far from the next
LP-boundary, and its $k_{\max}(K, q)$ jumps to a higher value
than non-magic neighbors (more strand interactions are required
to express the closed-shell structure).*

**Categorical interpretation.**
*Conjecturally the filtration $J^\bullet(q)$ coincides with the
$\mathbb{Z}$-grading filtration on the corresponding KLR module
under the Brundan–Kleshchev isomorphism — see
[confinement–grading correspondence](#conj:confinement-grading-correspondence).
This identifies the heuristic shadow-price ordering with a rigid
homological grading and supplies a structural cutoff for the
crystal-graph traversal that replaces S-polynomial overlap analysis.*
