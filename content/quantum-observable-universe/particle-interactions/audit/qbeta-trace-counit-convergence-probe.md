# Probe: Non-perturbative nature of the convergence

The trace-counit equality is not a small-correction limit. At the substrate the
single Markov-trace term already misses the target:
the $N=0$ contribution $\lvert\mathrm{tr}_M(\Delta)\rvert\cdot\mathfrak{E}_0
\approx 0.345$ MeV falls short of $\mathfrak{Q}_\beta \approx 0.78$ MeV by
$-56\%$. The Markov trace and the counit
differ at leading order by $\varepsilon(\Delta) - \mathrm{tr}_M(\Delta)
= h\,(z-1)$, which the substrate loop $z = 1/(\sqrt q + 1/\sqrt q)$ does
not render small. The counit form matches to $+0.17\%$ at
$N=0$ — the same residual that separates the derived energy scale
$\mathfrak{E}_0 \approx 4.186$ MeV from its calibration cross-check
$\approx 4.155$ MeV ([energy scale](#rem:energy-scale-planck)).

A perturbative expansion of the trace tower about its $N=0$ term does not establish the conjecture,
because the corrections exceed the leading term; a
closed evaluation of the tower-summed difference is required.
