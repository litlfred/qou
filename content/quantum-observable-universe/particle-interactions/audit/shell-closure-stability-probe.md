# Audit Probe: Shell Closure vs Empirical Noble Gases

**Target:** `prop:shell-closure-stability`

The categorical operator closures yield the sequence $Z \in \{2, 10, 28, 60, \ldots\}$ based on the pure $2n^2$ count of the $2\times 2$ transfer matrix $T_{\gamma e}$.

**Divergence:**
The empirical noble-gas numbers are $\mathcal{Z}_{\mathrm{noble}} = \{2, 10, 18, 36, 54, 86\}$. Connecting the categorical sequence $\{2, 10, 28, 60\}$ to the empirical noble gases is massive divergence and constitutes empirical fragility. It requires the Madelung sub-shell ordering which is not yet derived in the categorical framework. A high-precision categorical $\sigma_F$ dressing chain confirms $\sigma_F$ does not by itself reproduce the noble-gas peaks.

Similarly, identifying the theoretical marginal energy peaks with the observed peaks at noble-gas $Z$ and alkali troughs ($Z = 3, 11, 19, \ldots$) is conditional on this unproven derivation.

**Action Taken:**
This speculative correspondence has been removed from the core derivation `prop:shell-closure-stability` and its interpretation block to maintain mathematical formalism. Future extensions incorporating the Madelung ordering must be rigorously derived before physical claims can be made.
