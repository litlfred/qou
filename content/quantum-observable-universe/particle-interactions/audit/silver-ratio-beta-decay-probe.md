# Silver-ratio trace degeneracy probe

## Form A nulling

When treating the formal trace difference $\Delta(\mathrm{net})$ as a proxy for the physical $\beta$-decay $Q$-value (multiplying by $m_e$ and geometric factors), the resulting **Form A** vanishes precisely at the silver ratio:

$$
\mathfrak{Q}_\beta^A = m_e \cdot \frac{|q^2-2q-1|(q+1)}{2q^3} \;\xrightarrow{q \to 1+\sqrt{2}}\; 0
$$

This observation equates the categorical degeneracy condition ($h=2$) to a vanishing physical energy scale, motivating the choice of $q_0 \approx 1+\sqrt{2}$ as the physical substrate parameter where the free-neutron decay is marginally open. 

## SU(2)_L gauge connection

The $\beta$-decay process involves the SU(2)_L weak isospin doublet $(u, d)$. The $u \leftrightarrow d$ flavour change is one Weyl-group element. At the categorical level, the proton (uud) and neutron (udd) differ by exactly one such flip.

At the silver-ratio limit, the Hecke quotient $\sigma^2 = 2\sigma + 1$ recovers the **silver-mean self-similarity**: the two eigenvalues $1 + \sqrt{2}$ and $1 - \sqrt{2}$ are conjugate over $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{2})$ and are exactly the q-values at which the recursion $a_{n+1} = 2a_n + a_{n-1}$ (the **Pell recursion**) generates the Pell-Lucas / Pell number sequences.
