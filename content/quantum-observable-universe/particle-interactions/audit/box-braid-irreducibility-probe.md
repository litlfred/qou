**Audit Probe: Numerological time bound claims for $T_{3,5}$**

*Initial claims regarding the physical interpretation of the $T_{3,5}$ crossing number suggested that each crossing corresponds to one $R$-matrix application and therefore one Planck step.*

*Specifically, the following claims were flagged for numerology and overclaiming without formal categorical backing:*
1. *The process $\gamma\gamma \to \nu\nu^{\sigma}$ requires the $T_{3,5}$ torus knot topology (weak mediator, force–knot identification).*
2. *The process cannot occur before Planck step $10$.*
3. *No simpler mediator exists: $T_{3,5}$ is the unique cross-family torus knot on the genus-$4$ Bring's surface.*
4. *The box is the minimum-crossing topology for any $\gamma$–$\nu$ interaction.*

*These claims have been isolated from the formal categorical definitions pending rigorous derivation.*
