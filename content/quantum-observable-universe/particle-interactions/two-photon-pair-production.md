**Remark (concrete example: $\gamma\gamma \to e^- e^+$ in the symmetric crystal).**
*In the QOU topological ansatz the photon $\gamma$ is the empty
knot $\varnothing$ — the identity element on $n$ strands carrying
no crossings (distinct from the unknot $0_1$, which is the
neutrino).  The two-photon
state $\gamma\gamma$ therefore corresponds to the identity element
$T_e \in H_n(R, q)$ on $n = 2$ strands, with zero crossings, zero
graded Hecke energy, and strict bar-invariance: applying
$T_e \mapsto \overline{T_e}$ swaps over- and under-crossings of which
there are none, returning $T_e$ unchanged.  The state sits at the
highest-weight node of the trivial component of
$\mathrm{Cryst}^{\mathrm{sym}}(\mathfrak{so}_{2n+1})$.*

*Now place this state at the high-energy boundary of a heavy nucleus
(a fixed external boundary condition that supplies energy to the
crystal traversal).  The intense local energy density forces the
system to step down the crystal graph to absorb energy, but
[symmetric-crystal pair production](#conj:symmetric-crystal-pair-production)
forbids a unimodal lowering step
$\gamma\gamma \xrightarrow{\tilde f_i} v$ that would land on a single
chiral descendant.  The only admissible step is the paired operator*

$$
  \gamma\gamma
  \;\xrightarrow{\;(\tilde f_i,\tilde f_{\bar i})\;}\;
  e^-\,\sqcup\,e^+,
$$

*one operator twisting two strands into a left-handed trefoil — the
electron $e^-$ — and the bar-conjugate operator simultaneously
twisting two strands into the right-handed anti-trefoil — the positron
$e^+$.  The bar-invariance of the source state is conserved by the
bar-conjugate descendant pair, so charge ($-e + e = 0$) and chirality
($-\tfrac12 + \tfrac12 = 0$) are conserved structurally and not by an
imposed selection rule.*

**Falsification handle.**
*Any single-photon pair-production channel
$\gamma \to e^- e^+$ is forbidden by
[symmetric-crystal pair production](#conj:symmetric-crystal-pair-production)
without invoking energy–momentum conservation as a separate axiom: the
bar-invariant single-strand source state $\gamma$ carries no crossings
to fold into a paired operator, so the symmetric crystal has no
admissible step.  Empirical absence of $\gamma \to e^- e^+$ in vacuum
is consistent with the conjecture; observation of the channel without
a recoiling external boundary would falsify it.*
