**Proposition (compound β-decay calibration across magic shells).**

Q-value — the **$H^2$ calibration** of the
[three-calibration discipline](#prop:three-calibration-discipline) — and
let a β-decay $(Z, N) \to (Z+1, N-1)$ cross a magic anchor
$A_{\mathrm{magic}} \in \{2, 8, 20, 28, 50, 82, 126\}$, with the parent
containing $\sigma_{\mathrm{shell}}$ (via
[shell self-duality](#prop:shell-schur-weyl-self-duality)) and the
daughter containing a different shell operator or a cross-shell transition
([shell-transition knot](#conj:shell-transition-knot)). Then the Q-value
of the magic-crossing decay is:*

$$
  \mathfrak{Q}_\beta(A_{\mathrm{magic}}\text{-crossing})
  \;=\;
  E_0 \cdot
  \Bigl(
    \bigl|\mathrm{tr}_M^{\mathrm{parent}}\bigr|
    \;-\;
    \bigl|\mathrm{tr}_M^{\mathrm{daughter}}\bigr|
    \;-\;
    \bigl|\mathrm{tr}_M^{\mathrm{e^- + \bar\nu}}\bigr|
  \Bigr).
$$

*Equivalently, the shell-binding target $y_{\mathrm{target}}(A_{\mathrm{magic}})$
is structurally related to the cumulative β-decay chain reaching the magic
anchor via the categorical trace; to formalize the full binding scale, one
combines multiple β-decay Q-values along the isobar chain through the magic
anchor. (See [compound-beta-shell-calibration-probe](#audit:compound-beta-shell-calibration-probe) for empirical consistency checks.)*
