**Reading.** From the categorical origin of the crossing energy
([crossing energy](#def:crossing-energy)), feasibility of the
[confinement SDP](#prop:confinement-sdp) is equivalent to a
**Hecke spectral-gap condition**: the feasible cone is non-empty
if and only if

$$
  \Delta_{\sigma}(q) \;:=\; \lvert q + q^{-1} \rvert \;>\; 0,
$$

i.e. the Hecke generator $\sigma$ on $V \otimes V$ has
non-degenerate eigenvalues $(q, -q^{-1})$ on
$S^{2} V \oplus \Lambda^{2} V$.  The dual certificate at optimum
is the pair of Jones--Wenzl projectors onto the two isotypic
components; the SDP's complementary-slackness matrix is their
orthogonal decomposition.

**Deconfinement = spectral-gap closure.**
$\Delta_{\sigma}(q) \to 0$ at $q = \pm i$ (Kauffman-bracket
degeneracy) and the feasible cone collapses.  The SDP retains
its role as a numerical certificate at given $q$, but its
existence is guaranteed by the categorical volume constraint
and can be produced from the eigenvalue structure alone without
invoking continuous optimisation.
