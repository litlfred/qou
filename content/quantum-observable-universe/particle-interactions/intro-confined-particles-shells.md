With the confinement level defined in the previous section, we
now identify the *confined particles* — the categorical states
that survive confinement — and arrange them into shells.  The
shell structure is forced by the categorical Frobenius positivity
condition: a particle confined at level $\ell$ sits in the
$\ell$-th shell exactly when the Frobenius pairing on its braid
word is positive.

The arc records the confined-particle definition, the shell
indexing, and a small library of explicit shells (the substrate-
level analogues of the $1s$, $2s$, $2p$, $\dots$ atomic shells).
The closing blocks identify the shells of the lightest mesons
and nucleons and connect the indexing to the Hopf-fibration
torus-knot factorisation from the stable-nucleus section in the
Descartes chapter.
