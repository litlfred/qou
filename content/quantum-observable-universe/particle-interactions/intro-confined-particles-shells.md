We now identify the confined particles—the categorical states that survive confinement—and arrange them into shells. The shell structure is forced by the categorical Frobenius positivity condition. A particle confined at level $\ell$ sits in the $\ell$-th shell exactly when the Frobenius pairing on its braid word is positive.

This section defines the confined particles and indexes their shells. We build a small library of explicit shells (the substrate-level analogues of the atomic $1s, 2s, 2p, \dots$ shells). We identify the shells of the lightest mesons and nucleons. This connects the indexing to the Hopf-fibration torus-knot factorization from the stable-nucleus section.
