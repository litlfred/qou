*Proof.*
*The Ocneanu–Jones Markov trace $\mathrm{tr}_M: H_\bullet(q) \to \mathbb{C}(q)$ is a compatible family along the Hecke tower $H_n(R, q) \hookrightarrow H_{n+1}(R, q)$. It satisfies the Markov property $\mathrm{tr}_M \circ \iota = \mathrm{tr}_M$ ([Jones 1987](https://doi.org/10.2307/1971403)). Hypothesis $k_{\max}(P, q) \le n_0$ asserts that every Wedderburn block $\lambda \vdash k$ with $k > n_0$ has zero projection in $T_w^{\downarrow}$. Under the inclusion $\iota: H_{n_0}(R, q) \hookrightarrow H_{n_1}(R, q)$ this vanishing is preserved. The additional blocks $\lambda \vdash k$ with $n_0 < k \le n_1$ accessible at the fine level contribute zero to $T_w^{\uparrow} = \iota\,T_w^{\downarrow}$. Thus $\mathrm{tr}_M(T_w^{\uparrow}) = \mathrm{tr}_M(\iota\,T_w^{\downarrow}) = \mathrm{tr}_M(T_w^{\downarrow})$ by the Markov property. Functoriality of $\mathrm{tr}_M: \mathbf{Conf} \to \mathbb{C}(q)$ ([confinement category](#def:confinement-category)) extends this equality to every composite of unfoldings, establishing preservation along the full confinement chain.*

*For a general trace-class invariant $\mathcal{F}$ that factors through $\mathrm{tr}_M$, equality of traces implies equality of values: $\mathcal{F}(P^{\uparrow}) = \mathcal{F}(P)$. $\square$*

**Converse (unfolding necessity).**
*If $k_{\max}(P, q) > n_0$ — the witnessed partition content of $P$ exceeds the coarse strand count — then no representation of $T_w^{\downarrow}$ as an element of $H_{n_0}(q)$ captures all tight dual blocks of the [confinement SDP](#prop:confinement-sdp). Unfolding to the smallest level $n_1$ with $k_{\max}(P, q, n_1) \le n_1$ is strictly required. The unfolded trace $\mathrm{tr}_M(T_w^{\uparrow})$ differs from any coarse surrogate by contributions from the additional blocks. Magic shell closure and multi-operator binding configurations ($\mu(S, q) \ge 2$) are the principal triggers.*

**Corollary (default-coarse computability).**
*Every trace-class invariant $\mathcal{F}$ computed at the coarsest level satisfying $k_{\max} \le n$ is exact. Default-coarse computation is not a performance heuristic. It is the content of this proposition.*
