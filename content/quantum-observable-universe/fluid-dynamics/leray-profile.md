Near the blowup time $T^*$, the singular NS solution approaches a self-similar Leray profile $(V, P)$: a nontrivial solution of the time-independent Leray equations on $\mathbb{R}^3$ with $V \in L^2$. Decomposed by the Peter--Weyl theorem for $\mathrm{SU}_q(2)$ and truncated at the discrete vortex spectrum ($k_{\max} \approx 4$ active modes at $q \approx 1.110$), the classical infinite-dimensional Leray system reduces to a finite system of ODEs.
