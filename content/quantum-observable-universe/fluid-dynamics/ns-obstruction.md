**Definition.**
*Let $(A, \varTheta)$ be a quantum universe. The **NS obstruction class** is the element*

$$
  \mathrm{NS}(A) \;\in\; H^3_{\mathrm{loc}}(A, \varTheta)
$$

*obtained by evaluating the Yekutieli obstruction tower of the $q$-Navier–Stokes system at $q = 1$. Concretely, $\mathrm{NS}(A)$ is the obstruction to lifting the $q$-NS solution from brane level $n$ to level $n + 1$ in the limit $q \to 1$.*

*The NS obstruction depends on the Euler characteristic $\chi(A)$:*
- *We have $\mathrm{NS}(A) = 0$ if and only if the quantum universe is [NS-regular](#def:ns-regular-qou). In this case, the classical incompressible Navier–Stokes equation has global regularity.*
- *If $\mathrm{NS}(A) \neq 0$, the flow admits finite-time blowup of smooth solutions.*
