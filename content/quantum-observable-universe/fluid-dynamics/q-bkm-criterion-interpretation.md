**Remark (interpretation).**
The $q$-vorticity estimate translates the classical Beale--Kato--Majda criterion into algebra. The bound acts as a diagnostic limit check in the observable universe. The deformed Navier--Stokes system is unconditionally globally regular for $q > 1$, so the integral is finite in the bulk. The BKM condition thus shifts from a PDE survival test to an obstruction indicator at the $q \to 1$ substrate boundary.
