The [q-Kolmogorov spectrum](#prop:q-turbulence-spectrum) reduces to the standard $k^{-5/3}$ law as $q \to 1$. No existing turbulence measurement is contradicted. At the Planck scale, however, the discrete cascade predicts deviations from $k^{-5/3}$ at high wavenumbers in [BEC vortex tangles](#rem:bec-vortex-data). This is consistent with $q$-integer spacing.

Observations of quantum turbulence in ${}^{4}\mathrm{He}$ (Walmsley & Golov 2008) show precisely such deviations. These are conventionally attributed to vortex reconnection, but they potentially arise from the $q$-deformation of the energy cascade.
