**Mathematical novelty.** Computing local Euler classes via excision ($H^2_{\{p\}}$) and the Biot-Savart jet divergence are standard differential topology and fluid mechanics. The novelty is bridging them to show that the analytic and topological terms occupy complementary subgroups of the obstruction tower. This proves that adjustments in the topological background manifold cannot cancel the classical divergence bounds: the obstruction persists across all jet levels.
