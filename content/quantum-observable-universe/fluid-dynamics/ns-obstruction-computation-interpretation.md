Calculating local Euler classes via excision ($H^2_{\{p\}}$) and asserting Biot–Savart jet divergence are standard techniques. Combining them reveals that analytic and topological terms inhabit complementary subgroups of the Yekutieli obstruction tower. This establishes that classical divergence bounds cannot be cancelled by topological adjustments in the background manifold. The obstruction persists across all jet levels.
