The $q$-deformed hydrodynamics delivers a closed evolution equation on $\Omega^1(A)$. We prove that this evolution is globally regular whenever $q > 1$. We use the spectral theory of the $q$-Laplacian and the quantum Peter--Weyl decomposition of the brane.

The main theorem is "Theorem: Global regularity of the $q$-deformed Navier--Stokes equation": for every $q > 1$, smooth finite-energy initial data extends to a unique smooth solution for all time. We outline the argument in "Remark: Proof strategy". This names the UV damping, enstrophy bound, and uniqueness inputs. The first input is "Proposition: Spectral theory and Sobolev embedding for the $q$-Laplacian", which establishes compact resolvent and the embedding chain on the categorical Hodge complex. "Theorem: Quantum Peter--Weyl theorem" provides the completeness of the resulting eigenbasis. The nonlinear step is "Proposition: Nonlinear estimates and uniqueness for the $q$-Navier--Stokes system". Calculations appear in "Remark: Proof details". The subsequent section, **The NS obstruction**, takes this regularity theorem as the $q > 1$ starting point and determines what survives the $q = 1$ degeneration.
