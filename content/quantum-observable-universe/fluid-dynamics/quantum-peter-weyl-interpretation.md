The [spectral-Sobolev theory](#prop:q-spectral-sobolev) leaves a single gap: the completeness of the $\Delta_q$-eigenbasis. This theorem closes the gap by proving the quantum Peter--Weyl theorem for $\mathrm{SU}_q(2)$, using the categorical infrastructure already available.

**Connection to the Kelvin dispersion.**
The [Kelvin dispersion derivation](#prop:kelvin-dispersion-derivation) proved $\Delta_q(z^k) = -[k]_q^2z^k$ by explicit computation. The Peter--Weyl theorem provides the conceptual reason: $z^k$ is a matrix coefficient $D^{k/2}_{k/2, k/2}$ of the spin-$k/2$ representation. Its eigenvalue $[k/2]_q[k/2+1]_q$ reduces to $[k]_q^2$ under the appropriate normalisation on the circle.

The quantum Peter--Weyl theorem is a consequence of the QOU axioms (specifically the existence of the faithful exact monoidal fibre functor $\tau$). No external result (Woronowicz 1987) is needed. The categorical infrastructure is self-sufficient.
