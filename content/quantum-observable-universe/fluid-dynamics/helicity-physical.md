The [conservation of categorical helicity](#prop:helicity-conservation) expresses topological charge conservation in fluid dynamics. A vortex configuration cannot alter its total helicity through smooth evolution. Helicity redistributes among writhe, twist, and linking, as seen in [trefoil chirality flips](#rem:trefoil-vortex-chirality-flip).

The relation $\Gamma_i^{(q)} = [j_i]_q[j_i+1]_q$ ensures each term in the helicity sum remains individually quantised. Rather than a continuous conserved quantity, helicity occupies a discrete lattice determined by the $\mathrm{SU}_q(2)$ representation labels $\{j_i\}$.
