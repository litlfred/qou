The [discrete vortex spectrum](#prop:discrete-vortex-spectrum) has a direct physical interpretation. A stable vortex in the brane fluid does not smoothly increase its circulation, but jumps between discrete intensity states. The same $\mathrm{SU}_q(2)$ ladder operators that produce fermionic matter in the weight-space decomposition govern these transitions. The vortex intensity levels $E_j^{(q)}$ serve as the fluid-dynamical analogue of atomic energy levels.
