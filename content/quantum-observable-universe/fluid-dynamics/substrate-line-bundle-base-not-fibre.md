**Key property: the QOU structure is the base, not the fibre.**
The triple $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ together with the QOU axioms (non-degenerate integration, observability, measurability, volume constraint) govern the base of the family. The substrate parameter $q$ acts as a coordinate on the fibre $\mathbb{G}_m$.

In particular, the following objects do not depend on $q$:

- $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathbf{C}}(\mathbf{1}, \mathrm{Ad}(A))$.
- $c_1(\mathrm{Ad}(A))$ and $c_2(\mathrm{Ad}(A))$.
- $\chi(A) = \mathrm{tr}(\mathrm{id}_A)$.
- The Euler class $e(\xi)$ of the Reeb bundle.

The entire Yekutieli obstruction tower $\{\kappa_n \in H^2(\mathcal{S}, \mathcal{B})\}$ is a property of $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$, not of the section $q$.
