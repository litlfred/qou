*Proof.*
We compute the space $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathbf{C}}(\mathbf{1}, \mathrm{Ad}(A))$ within the category $\mathbf{C}$, which is defined independently of $q$. The substrate parameter $q$ determines only the differential $d_q$ on $\Omega^\bullet(A)$; it does not alter the objects, morphisms, tensor product, or braiding of $\mathbf{C}$. The adjoint object $\mathrm{Ad}(A)$ is defined via the conjugation action $a \mapsto S(a_{(1)})xa_{(2)}$. This action depends only on the Hopf structure $(\mu, \Delta, S)$, none of which depends on $q$.

The subscript $\mathbf{C}_q$ in $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathbf{C}_q}$ designates the category equipped with the differential $d_q$. However, $\mathrm{Hom}(\mathbf{1}, \mathrm{Ad}(A))$ involves no differential. It is a morphism space in the underlying :refterm[monoidal category]{#monoidal-category}, which remains invariant for all $q$.

**Corollary.** The QOU axioms (observability, measurability, volume constraint) constitute properties of the [substrate line bundle](#def:substrate-line-bundle) base $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$, not of any particular fibre $q \in \mathbb{G}_m$. They hold uniformly over $\mathbb{G}_m$, including at the torsion point $q = 1$ and at all roots of unity.
