**Linking and splitting in the divisor.**
The zero-locus $\{\psi = 0\}$ is a Weil divisor. Each blowup at a ramification point replaces a zero $z_i$ with an exceptional divisor $E_i \cong \mathbb{CP}^1$:

- **Splitting:** A single zero resolves into multiple components $E_{i_1}, \ldots, E_{i_k}$. The vortex filament bifurcates. The mass decomposes as $\sum_j \lambda_j|\Phi_q(z_{i_j})|$, recovering the [topological decomposition](#prop:topological-decomposition-mass).
- **Linking:** We have $E_i \cdot E_j \neq 0$ in homology if and only if the corresponding vortex filaments are Hopf-linked. The intersection pairing measures the strong-force binding energy from mutual velocity induction between the linked filaments.

The divisor structure of $\{\psi = 0\}$ is the correct object for tracking vortex topology and particle content. We do not use the probability density $|\psi|^2$ for this purpose.
