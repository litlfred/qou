The previous section established the $q$-deformed Navier–Stokes system on $\Omega^1(A)$ and its global regularity at substrate parameter $q > 1$. This section assembles the obstruction tower controlling how that regularity degenerates as $q \to 1$. We identify the modular tensor category structure on the limiting observables that survives the degeneration.

**The arc in two stages.**
The first six blocks build the Yekutieli obstruction tower on the collision locus of two anti-parallel vortex filaments. The boundary observability $\mathrm{Obs}_\partial(A)$ is the kernel of the restriction of the Yekutieli classes. This is computed explicitly via the Kummer specialisation and recovered as torus-NS-regular in the root-of-unity truncation. 

The remaining two blocks extract the observable modular tensor category and its braid-group action. This structure is the categorical residue of the fluid flow.

**Triple-vortex collision.**
The two-vortex amalgamation ([approach to exceptional divisor](#sec:approach-to-exceptional-divisor)) extends to three. At the triple-vortex collision, the merged fundamental group $\pi_1(U \setminus K_3)$ admits a three-strand braid representative. The categorical instanton acts via the $B_3$-representation $\rho_3$ of [prop:bn-mtc-action](#prop:bn-mtc-action) on $\mathbf{V}^{\otimes 3}$. The Brunnian (pairwise-unlinked, triple-linked) representative selected by semidefinite and linear programming minimisation is the Borromean braid $\mathcal{B}_3 = (\sigma_0\sigma_1^{-1})^3$ of [conj:borromean-quark](#conj:borromean-quark). Confinement corresponds to the non-vanishing triple Milnor invariant $\bar{\mu}_{123}$.
