# q-deformed Kolmogorov scaling E(k) ∝ k^{−5/3}

F10.4 (P2). Log-log plot of energy spectrum $E(k)$ on the brane. Two curves: a blue straight line representing the classical $k^{-5/3}$ Kolmogorov scaling, and a red broken-line that follows the classical slope until $k = k_q$ then bends to a steeper slope as $q$-deformation corrections dominate. Vertical dashed reference lines at $k_q$ (the q-deformation scale where the bend occurs) and $k_d$ (the dissipation scale). Footer: "classical: $-5/3$ power law down to dissipation scale $k_d$; q-deformed: bend at $k_q \ll k_d$ as quantum corrections dominate". Layout: log-log axes with two curves.
