While $q$-deforming operators is a standard procedure, applying the exact $q$-derivative $D_q$ to classical Kelvin-wave dispersion generates a novel result. It yields an analytically rigorous doubly-exponential ultraviolet suppression mechanism ($\tau_k \sim q^{-2k}$) in the fluid bulk. This exact suppression bypasses the need for ad-hoc phenomenological regularization or arbitrary cutoffs.
