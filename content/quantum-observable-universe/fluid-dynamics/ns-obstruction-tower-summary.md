We measure the NS obstruction consistently across every level of the brane tower by restricting $\kappa_n$ onto the collision locus $\Sigma$. It remains nonzero (confirming NS-singular blowup) if and only if the local cohomology $H^1_{\mathfrak{m}_p}(A)$ holds a nonzero value at collision points. This matches the exact condition where the Levi form evades degeneracy (maintaining a bounded core aspect ratio). The [Levi degeneracy conjecture](#conj:levi-degeneracy) isolates whether $\kappa_n|_\Sigma$ collapses or survives through the strict $q \to 1$ limit.
