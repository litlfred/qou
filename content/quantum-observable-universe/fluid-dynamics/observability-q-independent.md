Let $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ be a [QOU](#def:quantum-observable-universe) and let $\mathcal{Q}_M = M \times \mathbb{G}_m$ be the [substrate line bundle](#def:substrate-line-bundle). Let $q_1, q_2 \colon M \to \mathbb{G}_m$ be any two substrate sections.

We have:

$$
  \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathbf{C}_{q_1}}\!
  \bigl(\mathbf{1}, \mathrm{Ad}(A)\bigr)
  = 
  \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathbf{C}_{q_2}}\!
  \bigl(\mathbf{1}, \mathrm{Ad}(A)\bigr).
$$

If $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ satisfies the observability axiom for any substrate section $q > 1$, it satisfies it for every substrate section, including $q = 1$.

**Mathematical Novelty.** Hom-spaces over a fixed monoidal category do not depend on the differential layer $d_q$. This structural independence provides the formal mechanism permitting properties to propagate safely from the deformed observable phase space ($q > 1$) directly to the classical substrate ($q = 1$) without discontinuity.
