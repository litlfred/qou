As $q \to 1$, we have $[k]_q \to k$, $\Gamma_q \to j(j+1)$, and $\nu_q \to 0$. The [q-Kelvin dispersion](#prop:kelvin-dispersion-derivation) recovers the classical form $\omega_k = \Gamma k^2 / (4\pi a^2) \cdot (\ln(L/a) - c)$. The logarithmic factor arises from the Biot–Savart self-induction integral. The $q$-deformation absorbs this logarithm into the deformed core radius $a_q$.
