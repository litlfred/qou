The global regularity theorem for the $q$-deformed system isolates the classical Navier–Stokes question as a degeneration problem at $q = 1$. We construct a categorical obstruction $\mathrm{NS}(A) \in H^3_{\mathrm{loc}}(A, \varTheta)$ that controls whether finite-time blowup occurs, and we compute it on the Descartes quantum universe.

**Observability and the substrate.**
We first clarify the QOU observability axiom for the fluid setting. We provide independent motivation for why $\mathrm{Hom}(\mathbf{1}, \mathrm{Ad}(A)) = 0$ is not an arbitrary Navier–Stokes assumption. The substrate parameter is then promoted to a section of a line bundle, establishing a $\mathbb{G}_m$-family of differentials. The substrate parameter as a $\mathbb{G}_m$-section tracks torsion, specialisation, and the $q = 1$ boundary behaviour. Observability is a base property, remaining independent of the substrate section and surviving the family. 

**Vortex collisions and contact geometry.**
The geometric content at vortex collisions is captured by the core aspect ratio, which equals the Levi form condition number on the normal plane. We read the Navier–Stokes millennium problem as contact non-degeneracy at vortex collision.

**The Navier–Stokes obstruction.**
We define an NS-regular quantum observable universe and the NS obstruction class $\mathrm{NS}(A)$. We connect these to the classical regularity question for the Navier–Stokes equation. We outline the obstruction's cohomological structure in the Yekutieli tower and evaluate it concretely for the Descartes universe. The Kummer specialisation demonstrates that the NS obstruction survives the $q \to 1$ lift for $\gcd(\chi(A), n) \neq n$. The headline consequence is that the Descartes universe is NS-singular: Navier–Stokes admits finite-time blowup.

**Context and braid group action.**
We complement this blowup result with the torus quantum universe, where $\chi(A) = 0$ implies NS regularity. We place the result in a representation-theoretic context using truncation at roots of unity and the $q = 1$ accumulation. Finally, we establish the observable modular tensor category at $q = \zeta_N$. The braid group action on $\mathbf{V}^{\otimes n}$ does not factor through the symmetric group $S_n$. This non-permutation form of the braid action provides the foundation for subsequent open problems.
