The Einstein field equations emerge as the long-wavelength limit of the [q-Navier--Stokes equation](#def:q-navier-stokes). The stress-energy tensor $T_{\mu\nu}$ encodes the fluid momentum flux. Penrose--Hawking singularity theorems translate into the breakdown of smooth transonic flow. The classical singularity is the $q \to 1$ limit of a smooth $q$-deformed vortex core.
