# Carbon's half-filled valence shell

F14.1 (P0). Central carbon node "C" with eight equally-spaced linking sites around it on a circle, corresponding to the second-shell capacity $2n^2 = 8$. Four sites — north, east, south, west — are filled black (the closed inner shell $1s^2$). Four sites — northeast, southeast, southwest, northwest — are drawn as empty dashed rings (the residual sub-link $R$ of size 4, the bonding sites). Faint gray spokes connect each site to the centre. Annotation top-right: "$|R|/2n^2 = 4/8 = \tfrac{1}{2}$ — half-filled ⇒ maximal valence, symmetric energy landscape, unique chain-forming capacity". A two-item legend at the bottom keys the filled and open sites. This figure makes the topological origin of organic chemistry visible at a glance: carbon is uniquely positioned to share four electrons via covalent bonds. Layout: radial / 8-spoke wheel. Density: minimal labels. Style: schematic disks with filled vs dashed-open contrast. Colour: monochrome.
