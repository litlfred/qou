# Benzene aromaticity: Kekulé ↔ delocalised

F14.4 (P2). Two side-by-side hexagons of carbon atoms (the C₆ ring of benzene). Left: the Kekulé structure with alternating single and double bonds explicitly drawn (3 of each). Right: the delocalised aromatic structure with all six bonds equal and an inner red dashed circle representing the $T(2,6)$ thread that delocalises the bonds. Between them: a bidirectional $\mathbb{Z}/6$ rotation arrow indicating the symmetry. Captions: "alternating single / double bonds — 3 of each" and "equalised '1.5' bonds — $T(2,6)$ thread restores symmetry".
