# Chain vs ring — C₆ topology comparison

F14.3 (P1). Two panels side-by-side. Left: a linear C₆ chain — six C atoms in a horizontal row connected by five red shared-trefoil bond markers; caption "5 shared trefoils — open ends; no aromatic stabilisation"; energy label $E_{\mathrm{chain}}$. Right: a closed C₆ ring (benzene) — six C atoms on a regular hexagon with a single red threading-link ellipse passing through all six; caption "$T(2,6)$ threading link — closed; aromatic stabilisation, ½ crossing energy"; energy label $E_{\mathrm{ring}} < E_{\mathrm{chain}}$. A central arrow labelled "closure — ring formation; $\Delta E_{\mathrm{closure}}$ (positive barrier)" between the panels. Footer: "closing the chain into a ring lowers the energy by the aromatic stabilisation; barrier height $\Delta E_{\mathrm{closure}}$ matches experiment with no free parameters". Layout: 2 panels with arrow between. Density: 6 atoms each, 5 bonds (chain) / 1 thread (ring), captions. Style: schematic. Colour: monochrome plus red on bonds/threads.
