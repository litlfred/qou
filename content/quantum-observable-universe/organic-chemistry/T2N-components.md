**Statement.**
For every integer $N \ge 2$, the standard knot-theoretic invariants
of the torus link $T(2,N)$ have the following closed forms:

| Invariant | Formula | Even $N = 2m$ | Odd $N = 2m+1$ |
|-----------|---------|:--------------:|:--------------:|
| Components | $\gcd(2,N)$ | $2$ | $1$ |
| Braid-word crossings of $(\sigma_{1}\cdots\sigma_{N-1})^{2}$ | $2(N-1)$ | $2(N-1)$ | $2(N-1)$ |
| Minimal-diagram crossings | $N$ | $N$ | $N$ |
| Writhe (positive convention) | $+\,2(N-1)$ | $+\,2(N-1)$ | $+\,2(N-1)$ |
| Signature | $-(N-1)$ | $-(N-1)$ | $-(N-1)$ |

**Consequences cited in the chapter.**
The Components row recovers the table in
[threading link](#def:threading-link): odd-$N$ rings yield a single
torus knot, while even-$N$ rings (the case of benzene, $N = 6$) yield
a $2$-component link — the topological realisation of the two
delocalised $\pi$ electrons.
