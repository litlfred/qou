# The S₅ permutohedron — 120 vertices

F6.4 (P1). A 2D Schlegel-style projection of the order-5 permutohedron $\Pi_5$ — a 4-dimensional polytope whose 120 vertices are the permutations of $(1,2,3,4,5)$. The figure renders nested hexagonal facets (with quadrilateral connectors) reproducing the visible structure when viewed along a principal axis. Three of the outer vertices are highlighted in red as one $S_5$ orbit corresponding to a nuclear "magic number" class. Side annotations: top-left "$\mathrm{Aut}(\Sigma_4) = S_5, |\mathrm{Aut}| = 120$" and "120 permutations = 120 vertices"; top-right "red = magic-number orbit, stable nucleon classes". Footer notes the projection geometry: 24 hexagonal + 30 quadrilateral facets visible. Layout: nested polygons centred. Density: 120 vertices marked, 3 highlighted, side captions. Style: schematic projection. Colour: monochrome plus red on the highlighted orbit.
