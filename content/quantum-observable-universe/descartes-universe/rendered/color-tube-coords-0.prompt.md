# Colour-tube coordinates c_r, c_g, c_b on Σ₄

F6.1 (P0). The Bring coral from F5.1 with coordinate arrows added. Each of the four coloured handles has an arrow rising from its apex labelled with the corresponding shifted Lagrange resolvent: $c_r = w_2 - z$ (red), $c_g = w_3 - z$ (green), $c_b = w_4 - z$ (blue), and the time-like $\sigma = w_1 - z$ (black). Two small white-bordered cells at the upper corners: left, "spacetime ratio $z = x_0/x_1 \in \mathbb{P}^1(\mathbb{C})$"; right, "colour vector $c = (c_r, c_g, c_b)$". Footer notes the Newton power-sum constraint $c_r + c_g + c_b + \sigma = -4z$. This figure introduces the coordinate system used throughout Chapters 6 and 7. Layout: Bring coral with outgoing arrows + corner annotation cells. Density: 4 coordinate labels + 2 corner cells + 3-line footer. Style: schematic. Colour: full RGB triple plus black (the colour assignment is the figure's whole point).
