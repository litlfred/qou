The [categorical proton](#def:categorical-proton) $\mathfrak{p}$ is
uniquely determined up to braid isotopy by the triple
$(\mathfrak{u}, \mathfrak{u}, \mathfrak{d})$ and the Borromean
braiding pattern $\mathcal{B}_3$.  Its Hecke normal form in $H_3(q)$
is unique, and the $S_3$ permutation symmetry on the colour tubes
ensures the proton does not depend on which positions are assigned
to $(\mathfrak{u}, \mathfrak{u}, \mathfrak{d})$.
