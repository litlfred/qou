The Reeb vector field $\xi$ on the CR manifold $M$ picks out a
preferred vertical direction via $\theta(\xi) = 1$. We identify
left-handed and right-handed chiralities with the two orientations
of $\xi$ relative to the complex structure of $M$. For fermions
([fermion](#def:fermion-boson-decomposition)), the vertical component
$\alpha_V$ transforms with a definite sign when we reverse $\xi$,
so chirality sits in the $\pm$-eigenspaces of the Reeb involution
$\xi \mapsto -\xi$. Bosons
([boson](#def:fermion-boson-decomposition)), being purely horizontal,
are chirality-blind — consistent with the observed parity invariance
of the electromagnetic and strong interactions.
