Inherits from ([uniqueness of hadrons](#prop:hadron-unique)): the
Hecke NF is unique in $H_3(q)$.  The $S_3$-symmetry follows because
permuting the colour tubes $(r, g, b)$ acts by inner Hecke
automorphisms that fix the NF class.  Concretely, the three
assignments $(\mathfrak{u},\mathfrak{u},\mathfrak{d}),
(\mathfrak{u},\mathfrak{d},\mathfrak{u}),
(\mathfrak{d},\mathfrak{u},\mathfrak{u})$ produce Hecke elements
related by $S_3$-conjugation, hence the same NF.  The LP-optimal
gluon choice ($L_- = \sigma_1 \sigma_0$) is selected by the unique
minimum of $|\mathrm{tr}_M|$ among feasible stabilisers.
