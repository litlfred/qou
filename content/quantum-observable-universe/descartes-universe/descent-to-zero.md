By the :refterm[descent]{#descent} theorem ([Descartes universe](#thm\:descartes)), it is sufficient
to study dimension-zero manifolds equipped with their bundles $P$ and $\mathcal{S}$.
At a point $p\in M_0$, the substrate parameter
$q(p)= 1 + \lvert\langle L_\varTheta, F_A^{+}\rangle\rvert(p)$
([substrate parameter](#def\:substrate-parameter)) assigns a specific quantum
constant $q\in[1,\infty)$ determined by the principal bundle $P$ and the
state bundle $\mathcal{S}$.  Here $\mathcal{S}$ represents the totality of fermionic matter
packed into the fibre at $p$.  The trivial bundle $\mathcal{S}=\mathcal{S}_0$
corresponds to empty space, in which every point's location is exactly
known; a non-trivial bundle $\mathcal{S}$ begins to fill spacetime with fermions,
entering the regime of the quantum Bring's surface whose underlying geometry
is non-commutative ([Section](#sec\:quantum-brings)).
