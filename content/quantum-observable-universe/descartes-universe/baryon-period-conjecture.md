$m_p/m_e \approx 1836$ is a corollary of
([sw-period thimble equivalence](#prop:sw-period-thimble-equivalence))
applied at vertical degree $k = 3$, with the baryon channel
selected by the $\mathrm{Alt}^3$ projector

([Möbius limit from thimble](#conj:mobius-limit-from-thimble)).*

$$
  \frac{m_p}{m_e}
  \;=\;
  \frac{\mathrm{Vol}(\mathfrak{B}_3)}{\hbar_q^2} \cdot \mathcal{A}(q_0),
  \qquad
  \mathcal{A}(q_0)
  \;=\;
  \frac{\bigl|\sum_{p \in \mathcal{O}_{\mathrm{prin}}}
          \mathrm{Res}^{\mathrm{alg}}_p(\prod_{k=1}^{3} dc_k/dz)\bigr|}

          \mathrm{Res}^{\mathrm{alg}}_p(c_0 + z)\bigr|}.
$$

\approx 2.460$, $\mathrm{Vol}(\mathfrak{B}_3)/\hbar_q^2 \approx

mass derives from the substrate parameter $q_0$ and the Borromean
link volume alone.*
