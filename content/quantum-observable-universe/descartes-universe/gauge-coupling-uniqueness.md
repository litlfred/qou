**Corollary (uniqueness of the scalar gauge coupling).**
*Any $R$-linear, braid-invariant, scalar coupling
$A \otimes A \to \mathbf{1}$ is a multiple of
$\mathrm{tr}_\tau(e_-) \cdot e_-$.  Up to the global normalisation
$\lambda \in R$, there is exactly one non-trivial scalar coupling
on the two-strand sector of $\mathbf{C}$.*

*Proof.* A scalar coupling factors through projection onto a
singlet summand of $A \otimes A$.  The completeness theorem
([completeness](#thm:em-spectral-completeness)) provides exactly
one such summand ($V_- \cong \mathbf{1}$, of $R$-rank one), and
the projection onto it is $e_-$.  Any $R$-linear, braid-invariant
$\phi \colon E \to R$ vanishing on $V_+$ therefore equals
$\lambda \cdot \mathrm{tr}_\tau(e_-)$ on $e_-$, with
$\lambda = \phi(e_-)/\mathrm{tr}_\tau(e_-) \in R$.
$\square$
