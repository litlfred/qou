Tannaka--Krein duality establishes an equivalence between two perspectives on the substrate:

$$
\mathbf{Rep}_{\mathrm{loc}}(A) \simeq \mathbf{Comod}(\mathcal{H}_\xi).
$$

Here, $\mathbf{Rep}_{\mathrm{loc}}(A)$ is the category of localized $A$-module representations, supported at maximal ideals. The category $\mathbf{Comod}(\mathcal{H}_\xi)$ consists of comodules over the Hopf algebra $\mathcal{H}_\xi$ reconstructed from the Reeb spectrum. The fibre functor $\tau$ underlying this equivalence sends each localized module to its fiber at the unit defect.

This equivalence is a self-duality. The geometric side of localized representations at defects matches the spectral side of global quantum group symmetries of the Reeb flow. The reconstruction theorem guarantees that $\mathcal{H}_\xi$ is uniquely determined by the monoidal structure of $\mathbf{Rep}_{\mathrm{loc}}(A)$ \cite{reshetikhin1991}.
