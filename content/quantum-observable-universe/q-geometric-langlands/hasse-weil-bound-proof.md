**Proof sketch.** We apply the Castelnuovo--Severi inequality on the self-product $\mathcal{B} \times \mathcal{B}$. The diagonal $\Delta \subset \mathcal{B} \times \mathcal{B}$ defines a correspondence whose intersection number with the graph of $\mathrm{Fr}_q^n$ counts $|\mathcal{B}(\mathbb{F}_{q^n})|$. The Hodge index theorem on $\mathcal{B} \times \mathcal{B}$ supplies the inequality:

$$
\bigl(D \cdot D\bigr) \le 2g \cdot \bigl(D \cdot H\bigr)
$$

for any correspondence $D$ and ample divisor $H$. Setting $D = \mathrm{graph}(\mathrm{Fr}_q^n) - q^n \cdot \Delta$ yields $|\alpha_i|^2 \le q$. The functional equation $P(t) = q^g t^{2g} P(1/qt)$ then forces $|\alpha_i|^2 = q$.

This establishes the Riemann hypothesis for Bring's curve over $\mathbb{F}_q$. The result follows directly from Weil's framework for algebraic geometry over finite fields.
