Tannaka--Krein self-duality induces a discrete Fourier transform over the spectral-geometric space. The explicit formula equates the geometric sum over defects to the spectral trace over Reeb eigenvalues:

$$
\psi_q(X) = X - \sum_{\rho \in \mathrm{Spec}(\xi_q)} \frac{X^\rho}{[\rho]_q} - \ln_q(2\pi\Omega_q) - \frac{1}{2}\ln_q(1 - X^{-2})
$$

where $[\rho]_q = \frac{q^\rho - q^{-\rho}}{q - q^{-1}}$ is the $q$-integer, $\Omega_q$ is the Reeb period, and $\ln_q$ is the $q$-logarithm.

The leading term $X$ measures the smooth background growth of defect density. The sum over $\mathrm{Spec}(\xi_q)$ captures fluctuations driven by Reeb eigenvalues, where each $\rho$ contributes a wave of amplitude $X^\rho / [\rho]_q$. The constant and tail terms arise from trivial eigenvalues and functional equation symmetry. This formula provides the non-commutative analog of the explicit formula of prime number theory \cite{reshetikhin1991}.
