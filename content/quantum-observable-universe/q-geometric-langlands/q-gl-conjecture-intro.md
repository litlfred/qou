We state the central conjecture of this chapter and its surrounding structure. We define the $q$-Langlands dual group $\check{G}_q$. This deformation of the classical dual absorbs the substrate parameter into the spectral side. We then state the $q$-Geometric Langlands correspondence: an equivalence between $q$-$\mathcal{D}$-modules on $\mathrm{Bun}_{G_q}$ and quasi-coherent sheaves on $\mathrm{LocSys}_{\check{G}_q}$ over the non-torsion locus $\mathbb{G}_m^{\mathrm{nt}}$. We calibrate this conjecture by computing the Yekutieli obstruction classes $\kappa_n \in H^2(\mathcal{S}, \mathbb{G}_m)$. These classes measure the failure of $d_q^2 = 0$.

We organise the remaining structural conjectures around this obstruction. The Kummer filtration on $q$-local systems interprets the reduction $\kappa_0 \bmod n$ as a level-$n$ lifting obstruction. This pins the correspondence to the locus where $[n]_q \neq 0$. Finally, we assert that the rigid fibre functor $\tau$ implements the equivalence via Tannaka–Krein reconstruction. We outline the reconstruction argument, which we instantiate concretely for $q$-opers in the next section.
