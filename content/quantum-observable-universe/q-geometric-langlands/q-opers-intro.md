We turn to the spectral side of the conjecture and the canonical objects that realise it. We introduce the Definition: $q$-Oper. A $q$-oper is a $\check{G}_q$-bundle on $X$, equipped with a reduction to the Borel subgroup and a $q$-flat connection in oper position. This object provides the rigid spectral data dual to the automorphic $q$-Hecke eigensheaves.

The remaining blocks implement this duality. The Conjecture: $q$-Opers are $q$-Hecke eigensheaves states that the fibre functor maps every $q$-oper to a $q$-Hecke eigensheaf, with the eigenvalue determined by the oper coordinates. The Remark: Proof sketch: $q$-opers produce eigensheaves outlines the Frenkel–Reshetikhin construction that justifies this mapping. Together, they concretely realise the abstract Tannakian mediation conjectured in the previous section.
