The [fibre-functor mediation theorem](#conj:q-gl-fiber-functor)
connects the $q$-Geometric Langlands correspondence to the
observability axiom of the QOU
([quantum observable universe](#def:quantum-observable-universe)).

**Observability determines duality.**
The observability condition
$\mathrm{Hom}(\mathbf{1}, \mathrm{Ad}(A)) = 0$ constrains the
adjoint representation and thereby the fibre functor $\tau$.
Since $\tau$ uniquely determines $\mathbb{L}_q$, the
automorphic--spectral duality is a consequence of observability.
Relaxing observability reintroduces adjoint cohomology, and the
obstruction classes $\kappa_n$ interact with this
cohomology to produce additional obstructions to the equivalence
([fibre-functor rigidity](#rem:fibre-functor-rigidity)).

**Rigidity as uniqueness of the equivalence.**
The rigidity of $\tau$ ensures that the Langlands equivalence is
unique: there is exactly one way to match gauge configurations
with matter content, just as there is exactly one fibre functor
(up to unique isomorphism) on the representation category.  The
Kummer filtration on $\kappa_0$
([Kummer filtration](#rem:kummer-filtration-obstruction))
determines the arithmetic locus where $\mathbb{L}_q$ is
well-defined.

**The $q$-deformation as quantum correction.**
The passage from $\mathbb{L}_{\mathrm{BD}}$ to $\mathbb{L}_q$
is controlled by $(q-1)$, which plays the role of $\hbar_q$
([q-Planck constant](#def:quantum-planck-constant)).  The
$q$-Geometric Langlands correspondence is the fully
quantum-corrected gauge/matter duality, with the classical
Langlands programme recovered in the semiclassical limit
$\hbar_q \to 0$.  The perpetual obstruction ensures that
this quantum correction cannot be trivialised at cofinitely
many Kummer levels.
