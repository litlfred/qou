Let $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ be a quantum observable universe with
substrate parameter $q$.  If $\mathcal{M}$ is a $q$-Hecke
eigensheaf on $\mathrm{Bun}_{G_q}(X)$
([q-Hecke eigensheaf](#def:q-hecke-eigensheaf)), then its
eigenvalue
$\mathcal{L}_q \in \mathrm{LocSys}_{\check{G}_q}(X)$ is uniquely
determined by the action of $\tau$ on the Hecke eigenvalue data:

$$
  \mathcal{L}_q \simeq
  \mathrm{Aut}^\otimes(\tau)\text{-}\mathrm{equivariantisation of }
  \big\{ V_\lambda^{(q)}(\mathcal{L}_q)_x \big\}_{\lambda, x}.
$$

The rigidity of the fibre functor
([fibre-functor rigidity](#rem:fibre-functor-rigidity)) implies
that $\mathcal{L}_q$ is determined up to unique isomorphism by the
collection of Hecke eigenvalues.
