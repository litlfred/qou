The electron is the simplest nontrivial torus knot that embeds on $\Sigma_4$: the trefoil $\tau(2,3)$. Its Seifert genus is $(2-1)(3-1)/2 = 1 \leq 4$, so it embeds. The trefoil has 3 crossings (= 3 units of information content). Its Jones polynomial is:

$$
  J_{\tau(2,3)}(q) = -q^{-4} + q^{-3} + q^{-1}

$$

The mass is computed from the quantum dilogarithm at $q \approx 1.110$: $\mathfrak{m}_e = \mathrm{Li}_2(q^{-2})/\hbar_q^2$, which gives the electron mass as the fundamental mass unit. All other particle masses are ratios relative to this.
