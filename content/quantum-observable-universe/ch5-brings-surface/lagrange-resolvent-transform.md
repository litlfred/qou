**Proposition.**
Let $\zeta = e^{2\pi i/5}$ and let $\mathcal{S}$ be the Bring surface
([Bring's surface](#def:brings-surface)) with coordinates $x_0, \ldots, x_4$.
Define the Lagrange resolvents

$$
  w_k \;=\; \frac{1}{\sqrt{5}}\sum_{j=0}^{4}\zeta^{kj}\,x_j,
  \qquad k \in \{0,1,2,3,4\}.
$$

Then:

1. The three power-sum constraints on $\mathcal{S}$ transform to
   $w_0 = 0$, $w_1 w_4 + w_2 w_3 = 0$, and
   $w_1^2 w_3 + w_4^2 w_2 + w_2^2 w_1 + w_3^2 w_4 = 0$.

2. The cyclic permutation $\sigma \colon x_j \mapsto x_{j+1}$ acts
   diagonally: $\sigma(w_k) = \zeta^{-k} w_k$.
