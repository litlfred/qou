**(1) Localisation.**
The braiding of $\mathbf{C} = \mathbf{Rep}(\mathrm{SU}_q(2))$ is
given by the universal $R$-matrix
$R \in U_q(\mathfrak{su}(2))^{\otimes 2}$, which acts on
$V^{\otimes n}$ by permuting tensor factors with a $q$-dependent
phase.  The Hecke generator is $T_i = \tau_i \circ R_{i,i+1}$,
where $\tau_i$ is the transposition.  Under the fibre functor
$\tau$, the representation $V$ is a module over the coordinate ring
$\mathcal{O}(\mathrm{SU}_q(2))$, which is the structure sheaf of
the exceptional divisor $E_p \cong \mathbb{P}^1(\mathbb{C})$
(the quantum flag variety of $\mathrm{SU}_q(2)$ at $q$).  The
$R$-matrix acts fibrewise: it permutes sections of $V$ over $E_p$
without involving sections of the base algebra $A_0$.  Hence $T_i$
is supported on $E_p$.

**(2) Confinement.**
The Skein relation $L_+ - L_- = (q - q^{-1})\,L_0$ is the image
of the Hecke relation $(T_i - q)(T_i + q^{-1}) = 0$ under the
Markov trace.  Since $T_i$ acts within $E_p$ (part 1), and the
Markov trace is computed over the Reeb bundle $\mathcal{R}_p$
(the $U(1)$-bundle whose connection is $\xi_q$), every Skein
resolution is evaluated within $\mathcal{R}_p$.  The blow-down
map $\pi\colon \widetilde{M} \to M$ collapses $E_p$ to $p$,
projecting the resolved link invariant to the base level.

**(3) Completeness.**
The Hecke algebra $H_n(q)$ is generated by $\{T_1, \ldots, T_{n-1}\}$
subject to the Hecke and braid relations.  Every element of $H_n(q)$
is a finite linear combination of products of these generators
([Descartes braid structure](#cor:descartes-braid-structure)).
Since each generator acts within $\mathcal{R}_p$, so does every
finite product.  Crossing changes, pair creation (R-II moves), and
virtual exchange are all expressible as finite braid words in $B_n$,
whose Hecke images lie in $H_n(q)$.  No operation requires data
from outside the blow-up.
