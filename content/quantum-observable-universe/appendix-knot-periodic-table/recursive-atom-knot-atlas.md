**Atlas (recursive, $q_0$-indexed).**
*Each confined particle or atom is identified by the Markov-equivalence class of the closure $\widehat{T_w}$ of its canonical Hecke element:*

$$
[K]_M \;=\; \bigl[\,\widehat{T_w}\,\bigr], \qquad T_w \in H_{n_0}(q_0), \quad w \in B_{n_0},
$$

*with confinement at $q_0$ certified by the [confinement SDP](#prop:confinement-sdp) and each entry fixed by the [confinement source of truth](#def:confined-particle). The registry is recursive: composite entries are assembled from nucleon and $\alpha$-cluster constituents, giving uniform comparison against the AME 2020 reference.*

The methodology classification, notation legend, braid-closure gallery, the per-level atlas ($\ell = 0$ leptons/quarks through $\ell = 2$ $\alpha$-cluster atoms), and the per-element AME 2020 cross-reference ($Z = 1$ to $118$) are recorded in the [atlas registry](#rem:recursive-atom-knot-atlas-registry).
