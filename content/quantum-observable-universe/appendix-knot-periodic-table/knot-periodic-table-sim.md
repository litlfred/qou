Interactive periodic table organized by knot-theoretic nuclear structure.
Elements are arranged according to their nuclear knot invariants rather than
atomic number alone, revealing the shell structure predicted by the
topological mass formula and Borromean binding conditions.

Views include:

- **Knot-centric layout** with electron block coloring
- **Magic numbers** highlighted (shell closures from linking number constraints)
- **Binding energy gradient** ($B/A$ per nucleon from the topological mass formula)
- **Alpha sub-link count** ($k_\alpha$ from Borromean decomposition)
