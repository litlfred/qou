**Conjecture (super-exponential brane-tower growth; GAP-A-3).**
*The branched brane-tower weight $w_{\mathrm{std}}(k) = \mathbb{E}_{\mathrm{Planch}}\!\left[f^{\lambda/(2,1)}\right]$ admits a geometric lower bound*

$$
  c\, r^{k} \;\leq\; w_{\mathrm{std}}(k) \qquad (r > 1,\; c > 0)
$$

*for every jet level $k$.*

The geometric bound is a fortiori a consequence of the stronger **super-exponential** growth observed for the branched tower — successive ratios strictly increasing ($1.38 \to 2.88$) — which is all the logarithmic level-selection conclusion downstream requires. The base $r$ and constant $c$ are carried as parameters, so any fixed $r>1$ gives a valid (weaker) lower-bound instance.
