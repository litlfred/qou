**Step 1: $F$ generates the GB rewrite ideal.**
*The two-sided ideal $I \subset \mathbb{C}\langle \sigma_1, \ldots, \sigma_{n-1}\rangle$
generated by $\{R_H(i)\}_{i=1}^{n-1} \cup \{R_{YB}(i)\}_{i=1}^{n-2}$
is exactly the kernel of the surjection*

$$
  \mathbb{C}\langle \sigma_i\rangle \twoheadrightarrow H_n(R, q), \quad
  H_n(R, q) = \mathbb{C}\langle \sigma_i\rangle / I.
$$

*By construction $F$ takes values in $I$:
$F(\sigma_i, \sigma_j) \in I$ for every generator pair, and the
Leibniz extension preserves $I$ as an ideal.  Therefore
$F$ is the universal curvature 2-cocycle for the GB rewrite system.*

**Step 2: GB filtration is generated by powers of $I$.**
*The Bergman GB normal-form theorem
([Hecke normal form](#def:hecke-normal-form)) gives an explicit
basis of $H_n(R, q)$ as ascending products $\sigma_{i_1} \cdots
\sigma_{i_k}$ with $i_1 < i_2 < \cdots$ and bounded multiplicities.
Writing any $T_w$ as a free-algebra word, the GB rewriting
algorithm reduces $T_w$ to normal form by applying $R_H, R_{YB}$
repeatedly.  The filtration $J^\ell(q)$ collects elements requiring
at least $\ell$ applications of these relations to reach normal
form.  Equivalently, $J^\ell$ is the image of $I^\ell$ (the
$\ell$-th power of the rewrite ideal) under the quotient map.*

**Step 3: $F^{\,r}$ measures rewrite depth.**
*By the Leibniz rule and Step 1, $F^{\,r}(T_w) \in I^r$ but
$F^{\,r-1}(T_w) \not\in I^r$ when $T_w$ requires exactly $r$
GB reductions.  Combined with Step 2, $F^{\,r}(T_w) \in J^r$,
$F^{\,r-1}(T_w) \not\in J^r$, so the curvature-rank
$r(T_w)$ as defined matches the GB filtration depth.*

**Step 4: Spectral sequence pages stratify by curvature-rank.**
*The GB spectral sequence
([GB spectral sequence](#prop:gb-spectral-sequence)) has
$E_0^{\ell, *} = J^\ell / J^{\ell+1}$ as the associated graded.
By Step 3, this is exactly the curvature-rank-$\ell$ stratum.
The differentials $d_r$ propagate elements between adjacent
strata, and $E_\infty^{\ell, *}$ is the stable cohomology at depth
$\ell$, depending only on the curvature-rank-$\ell$ residues.*
$\qquad\blacksquare$
