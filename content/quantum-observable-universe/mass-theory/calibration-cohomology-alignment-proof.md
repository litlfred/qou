*The count.* $H^1(\text{Bring}) = 2W$ is a polarised weight-1 Hodge structure.
The $W$-isotypic part is the tensor $W \otimes \mathbb{C}^2$, with the $\mathbb{C}^2$
the Hodge multiplicity plane ($H^{1,0} \oplus H^{0,1}$). A polarised rank-2
weight-1 Hodge structure has two real moduli — an overall **scale** and a
**modulus** (period ratio) — and these are the only invariants of the
$S_5$-equivariant Gauss--Manin data on that plane (the equivariance forces
$M = \mathrm{id}_W \otimes B$, so all freedom lives in the $2\times 2$ block
$B$: its normalisation and its $\mathrm{SL}_2$ trace $\tau$, since
$\det B = 1$). That is $2$ data. The meson topology is the Hopf link, whose
complement has $\mathrm{Vol} = 0$ (meson scale-independence); it carries
no hyperbolic-volume invariant, so its mass scale is a third datum independent
of $(m_e, q_0)$. Total $2 + 1 = 3$ (`cohomology_data_count`).

*The alignment.* The scale of the $W$-plane is the $n=0$ period $a_D^{(e)} = m_e$
(CAL-1, [`prop:electron-period-base`](#prop:electron-period-base)); its modulus
is $\tau$, fixed by $q_0$ (CAL-2,
[`prop:sl2-prolongation-recurrence`](#prop:sl2-prolongation-recurrence)); the
meson-sector scale is $m_\pi$ (CAL-3). The calibration triple
$(m_e, q_0\text{-modulus}, m_\pi)$ therefore equals the cohomological triple
$(\text{scale}, \text{modulus}, \text{meson scale})$ term by term
([`QOU.Mass.calibrations_eq_cohomology`](#prop:calibration-cohomology-alignment)).
