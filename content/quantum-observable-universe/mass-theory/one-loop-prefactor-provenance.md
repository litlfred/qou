**Proposition (what is literature, what is measured, what is derived).** Write the sub-leading constant of the volume-conjecture asymptotic as a power law in the Dimofte–Garoufalidis 1-loop invariant,

$$
  c_\sigma \;=\; k \cdot |\tau_{\mathrm{DG}}|^{\,p}.
$$

Then the two factors have different provenance, and conflating them is the error this block exists to prevent.

- **The form is literature.** Dimofte–Gukov–Lenells–Zagier expand the perturbative $\mathrm{SL}(2,\mathbb{C})$ Chern–Simons partition function as $Z^{(\rho)}(M;\hbar) = \exp\big(S_0/\hbar - \tfrac{1}{2}\delta^{(\rho)}\log\hbar + \sum_{n\ge 0} S_{n+1}\hbar^n\big)$ and identify the one-loop coefficient with the Ray–Singer torsion of $M$ twisted by the flat bundle $E_\rho$: $S_1^{(\rho)} = \tfrac{1}{2}\log\big(T(M;E_\rho)/2\big)$. The statement is for a **general** $3$-manifold with a flat $G_\mathbb{C}$ bundle — no restriction to knots, to two-bridge knots, or to cusp number. So the dependence $c_\sigma \propto (\text{torsion})^{-1/2}$ is not an assumption of this corpus; it is a cited result. What it is *not* is a theorem: it is a one-loop saddle-point/Feynman-diagram derivation.

- **The exponent is normalisation-invariant, hence measurable.** Rescaling $c_\sigma \mapsto A\,c_\sigma$ by any nonzero constant leaves the two-point exponent $p_{ij} = (\log c_i - \log c_j)/(\log \tau_i - \log \tau_j)$ unchanged (`exponent_normalisation_invariant`). Every convention offset is such an $A$, so no change of convention can move $p$.

- **And $p = -1/2$ was already measured, by checks that were read as measuring something else.** For any *pair* of manifolds, $c_1\sqrt{\tau_1} = c_2\sqrt{\tau_2} \iff p_{12} = -1/2$ (`k_const_iff_exponent_neg_half`). The corpus validated the law by checking that $k_c := c_\sigma|\tau_{\mathrm{DG}}|^{1/2}$ agrees across manifolds and recorded the exponent as *assumed*; those are the same statement. The $17$-digit agreement $k_1 = k_2$ between $4_1$ and the Whitehead link **is** the measurement $p = -1/2$ to $17$ digits.

- **The constant cannot be lifted from DGLZ — but it need not be.** DGLZ carry at least three distinct additive offsets between conventions — the $/2$ inside their logarithm, a $+\log 2$ relating $S_1$ to the colored-Jones coefficient $s_1$, and a $\log\pi$ shift against Gukov–Murakami — and fix their integration constants "by comparison to the asymptotics of the colored Jones polynomial". They calibrate too. So $k = 2^{-1/2}$ cannot be read off their formula. It does not have to be: on the **link** side it is now derived directly from the colored Jones, with no convention to reconcile at all.

**The $N$-power is theorem-backed at two and three cusps.** The extraction $c_\sigma := \lim_N |J'_N|\,N^{-3/2}e^{-\mathrm{Vol}\cdot N/2\pi}$ uses the same power $3/2$ at every cusp number. Van der Veen proves, for the Whitehead-chain family $W_{a,b,c,d}$, that $J_N \sim \exp\big(\tfrac{1}{2\pi}[(\mathrm{Vol}+i\,\mathrm{CS})N + D\log N + E]\big)$ with $D = 3\pi$ whenever $b = 1$ — a value independent of $c$ and $d$, hence of the number of components. The Whitehead link is $W_{0,1,1,0}$ and the Borromean rings are $W_{0,1,1,1}$, both with $b = 1$, so both carry $N^{3/2}$. The cusp-independence of the exponent is therefore proved for exactly the links this corpus uses, and not merely fitted. The mechanism is Lemma 3's constant: $\tilde S_N \sim e^{4N\Lambda(\pi/4)/\pi}(2N)^{-1/2}$ contributes $N^{-(c+d)/2}$ against Lemma 4's $N^{(c+d+3)/2}$, and the cusp-dependence cancels exactly.

**On the link side the constant is derived, not calibrated.** Van der Veen's $E$ *is* the object measured here as $c_\sigma$, and his proof gives it explicitly as a $(c+d+1)$-dimensional Gaussian integral for the whole family; he evaluates it only far enough to show it is nonzero, and his closing paragraph defers the rest — citing the conjecture of Gukov–Murakami that $E$ "is determined by the Ray-Singer torsion of the complement twisted by the holonomy representation" — to a subsequent publication he never wrote. Evaluating that integral, after correcting a sign in his Lemma 2a and one in his Step-3 Gaussian coefficient (neither affecting his theorem), gives in closed form for every $b = 1$ chain

$$
  c_\sigma\big(W_{a,1,c,d}\big) \;=\; 2^{\,1/2 - n}\,\big(n^2 + m^2\big)^{-1/4},
  \qquad n := c+d,\quad m := 4a - c + d .
$$

Combined with the law, this forces $|\tau_{\mathrm{DG}}| = 2^{\,2n-2}\sqrt{n^2+m^2}$ (`vdv_c_sigma_iff_tau`). That is a prediction, not a fit: it reproduces the corpus's independently computed $|\tau_{\mathrm{DG}}|$ at the Whitehead link and the Borromean rings exactly, and it was **stated in advance** and then confirmed to $18$ digits at three further chains the corpus had never touched — $L8n5$ at three cusps, $L10n100$ and $L10n103$ at four. So $k = 2^{-1/2}$ survives a test at a cusp number it was never fitted to, against machinery that knows nothing about the colored Jones polynomial.

**What remains open, stated exactly.** Two things. First, the **knot** side: a $b=1$ Whitehead chain has $c+d+1 \ge 2$ components, so nothing above derives $c_\sigma(4_1) = 3^{-1/4}$, and the one-cusped leg still rests on Garoufalidis–Yoon together with the $4_1$ calibration. Second, the law itself: what is established is that a derived Jones-side constant and an independently computed $1$-loop invariant satisfy $c_\sigma \propto |\tau|^{-1/2}$ on five manifolds at three cusp numbers, which is a far stronger test than the corpus previously had and is still not a proof.

The distinction the proposition draws also fixes the shape of the residual risk. Any missing cusp-dependent step $g$ in the dictionary between the two normalisations satisfies $k_{\mathrm{meas}}(c) = k_{\mathrm{true}}\,g(c)$, so agreement of the measured constants at two cusp numbers forces $g$ to agree there (`normalisation_step_trivial_of_k_agree`) — which excludes every candidate with $g(1)\neq g(2)$, the retracted per-cusp factor $2^{(c-1)/2}$ among them. The tension lives in the exponent bookkeeping, not in the constant, at the cusp numbers tested.
