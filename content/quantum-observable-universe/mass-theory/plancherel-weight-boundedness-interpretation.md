**Remark (q-deformed alternative).**
The $q$-deformed weight $\widetilde{w}_\lambda = \dim_q^2 V_\lambda / [n]_q!$ sums to a finite value $\approx 1.51$ rather than exactly 1. It does not construct a pure probability measure at generic $q \neq 1$. However, the Plancherel boundedness property neutralizes matrix-dimension divergences inside log-pairings, suppressing combinatorial scale factors into average bounds. Without this boundedness, the deep-tower calculations would mathematically diverge.
