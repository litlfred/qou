**Proposition (fibre-level).** Let $J_\nu^{(1)}(x;q)$ and $J_\nu^{(2)}(x;q)$ be Jackson's first- and second-kind $q$-Bessel functions. Their power-series coefficients satisfy purely algebraic first-order contiguity recurrences in the order $\nu$. The recurrence is algebraic in the formal deformation variable $q$. The $q$-weights are adapted to the $SU_q(1,1)$ Casimir invariants. This fibre-level identity fixes the radial coefficient algebra without fixing the binding scale.
