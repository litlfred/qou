The exceptional divisor of the previous section carries a Reeb bundle and vertex operators, but we have not yet shown that this local structure is the unique source of all the operator content the chapter uses. We close that gap now. Every vertex operator factors through the exceptional divisor; the resulting factorisation matches the Markov trace term by term, and the two structures together identify a Feynman path integral with a categorical trace.

The section opens with **Vertex operators factor through the exceptional divisor** (prop), the structural pivot: a vertex operator $V$ acting on the categorical state space is the composition of restriction to $E_p$ with the Reeb-bundle action. The remark **Breit--Wheeler vertex** (rem) makes the photon-polarisation content visible — the two photon helicity states are Hecke generators on $E_p$ — and the companion literature alignment ties the picture to Bilson--Thompson preons and photonic hopfions. The theorem **Product factorization of the Markov trace** (thm) reads off the consequence on traces: $\mathrm{tr}_M(V_1 \cdots V_n)$ splits as a product over vertices, and **Hecke generator as stochastic transition operator** (prop) interprets each Hecke generator as the transition operator of a Markov chain on partitions, with **Knot operations on Young diagrams under the Markov chain** (rem) and **Error bounds for the Markov chain** (rem) recording the moves and fluctuations on the partition side.

The Feynman block is the heart of the section. **Eigenbasis decomposition for the Feynman amplitude** (lemma) reduces the amplitude to a sum over the Schur--Weyl eigenbasis; **Markov-weighted eigenbasis sum equals the Markov trace** (lemma) identifies the resulting sum with $\mathrm{tr}_M$ on $H_n(q)$; **Feynman amplitude collapses to the Markov trace** (lemma) carries out the collapse; and **Feynman transition amplitude as categorical trace** (prop) wraps the identification in QOU language. The dictionary **Dictionary: Feynman path integral $\leftrightarrow$ QOU categorical trace** (rem) writes out the correspondence in full, and **Classical limit: recovery of the symmetric path integral** (prop), together with its interpretation remark, closes the loop by showing that the $q \to 1$ degeneration recovers the ordinary path integral.
