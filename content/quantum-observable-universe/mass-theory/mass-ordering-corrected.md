With the corrected trefoil trace
$\mathrm{tr}_M(\mathfrak{e}^-) = hz^2 + z$
([corrected trace](#prop:corrected-markov-trace)) — not the earlier
$z^3$ — categorical mass is $\mathfrak{m} = |\mathrm{tr}_M|$ and the
nucleon (Borromean-baryon) traces carry the ordering

$$
  |\mathrm{tr}_M(\mathfrak{n})| > |\mathrm{tr}_M(\mathfrak{p})|
  \quad \Longrightarrow \quad
  \mathfrak{m}_{\mathfrak{n}} > \mathfrak{m}_{\mathfrak{p}} .
$$

The nucleon-trace magnitudes are computed on the 11-crossing
Borromean-baryon braid, independent of the single-knot trefoil trace
above; the numerical values quoted in earlier drafts were stated
alongside the withdrawn $z^3$ trefoil trace, and a committed producer
witness (the block currently cites only `codata-masses`) is needed to
regenerate them against the corrected $hz^2 + z$ convention before they
are treated as canonical.

This ordering matches the experimental fact
$m_n \approx :val[m_n_MeV]{precision=6, witness=computations/codata-masses.witness.json}$ MeV $> m_p \approx :val[m_p_MeV]{precision=6, witness=computations/codata-masses.witness.json}$ MeV.

The **negative signs** of both traces reflect the
fermionic (antisymmetric) character of the nucleon — the
Markov trace of a confined baryon is negative because the
Borromean braid $\mathcal{B}_3$ carries odd parity under
the sign representation of $S_3$.
