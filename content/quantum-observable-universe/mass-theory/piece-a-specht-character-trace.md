*Let $C_2 = \sum_{i} L_i^2$ be the sum of the squared Jucys–Murphy elements,
a central element of $\mathbb{C}[S_n]$. Then on the irreducible Specht module
$S^\mu$,*

$$
  \operatorname{Tr}\bigl(C_2 |_{S^\mu}\bigr)
  \;=\;
  \dim\bigl(S^\mu\bigr) \cdot \sum_{\square \in \mu} c(\square)^2 ,
$$

*and on the full regular representation of $S_n$,*

$$
  \operatorname{Tr}(C_2)
  \;=\;
  \sum_{\mu \vdash n}
    \Bigl( \sum_{\square \in \mu} c(\square)^2 \Bigr)
    \dim\bigl(S^\mu\bigr)^2 .
$$
