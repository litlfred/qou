The algebraic machinery developed in the previous sections —
Wedderburn decomposition, Frobenius pairing, Hecke characters —
now feeds into the concrete tools that evaluate the mass formula
on specific braids.

The transfer matrix
([categorical transfer matrix](#def:transfer-matrix-operator))
encodes how the mass integrand propagates across braid crossings;
the Skein mass lemma
([Skein mass from linking](#lem:skein-mass-from-linking)) reduces
the mass of a composite knot to linking energies plus volume
contributions; and the corrected Markov trace
([corrected Markov trace](#prop:corrected-markov-trace)) accounts
for the trefoil's crossing asymmetry that produces the observed
$m_n > m_p$ ordering.

The section also houses the Frobenius Gram matrix, the archimedean
functor uniqueness theorem, the $\mathfrak{Q}_\beta$ categorical precision
ladder, and the filtration bridges (Hecke bi-filtration,
Galois--braid joint action, adelic substrate) that connect the
algebraic mass machinery to the number-theoretic structure of
the substrate.

The arc widens further into the substrate-flow behaviour of the transfer matrix at higher levels and into the closing blocks that wire the substrate-level Markov-trace evaluation into the substrate-flow numerics consumed downstream. The output is the consolidated algebraic-mass closed form together with its substrate-flow data.
