Convention D does not annihilate the three-strand antisymmetriser by accident.
The row cap propagates up the whole tower, and what it leaves behind is a named
algebra.

Because the fundamental representation of $SU(2)$ is two-dimensional, no
$q$-antisymmetriser exists on three of its strands, and the Wedderburn weight of any
partition with three or more rows vanishes identically --- not numerically at $q_0$,
but as a rational function of $q$. The surviving blocks of $H_n(q)$ are therefore
exactly the two-row shapes, which is the stratum
[the two-row stratum](#def:two-row-stratum) isolates for the ${}^4\mathrm{He}$
fingerprint. Summing the squared Specht dimensions over those shapes gives, level by
level, the Catalan numbers:

| $n$ | surviving shapes | $\sum \dim^{2}$ | $\dim H_n(q)$ | $C_n$ |
|:---:|------------------|----------------:|--------------:|------:|
| 2 | $(2),\,(1,1)$ | 2 | 2 | 2 |
| 3 | $(3),\,(2,1)$ | 5 | 6 | 5 |
| 4 | $(4),\,(3,1),\,(2,2)$ | 14 | 24 | 14 |
| 5 | $(5),\,(4,1),\,(3,2)$ | 42 | 120 | 42 |
| 6 | $(6),\,(5,1),\,(4,2),\,(3,3)$ | 132 | 720 | 132 |
| 7 | $(7),\,(6,1),\,(5,2),\,(4,3)$ | 429 | 5040 | 429 |

The quotient of $H_n(q)$ by the radical of its Markov form thus has the dimension of
the Temperley--Lieb algebra $TL_n(q)$, and the blocks match shape by shape. At
$n = 4$ the bookkeeping closes exactly: $24 - 14 = 10 = 3^{2} + 1^{2}$, the discarded
$(2,1,1)$ and $(1^{4})$ blocks, so the quotient is the direct sum of the three
surviving matrix algebras and not a proper quotient of it.

## Why the quotient is not an amputation

The metric obstruction below asks for a quotient by the radical, and taken on its own
that reads as an ad hoc repair. It is not. The radical is the Temperley--Lieb ideal,
generated by the three-strand $q$-antisymmetriser, and the quotient is a
well-studied algebra with a diagrammatic calculus and a standard bilinear form.
"Quotient by the radical" and "work in $TL_n(q)$" are one instruction.

Stated the other way: the degeneracy is not a defect convention D introduces, it is
what convention D means. Under convention C the framework computes in the full Hecke
algebra; under convention D it has been computing in Temperley--Lieb throughout.

## What is measured and what is proved

The dimension count above is a **measurement, not a theorem of this paper**. The
weights were evaluated for every partition of $n = 2,\dots,7$ at $50$ decimal places,
with worst residual $1.34 \times 10^{-51}$ against the closed form
$y_{(n-k,k)} = [n-2k+1]_q/[2]_q^{\,n}$; no witness file records the run, and no
formalised statement of $H_n(q)/\mathrm{rad} \cong TL_n(q)$ exists. The Lean sibling
carries the arithmetic instances --- the Catalan counts at $n = 3,4,5$ checked against
the standard recursion, and the $n = 4$ radical split --- together with the branching
recursion that generates the pattern. The isomorphism is stated here as an
identification the counts support, and it is deliberately not a proposition.
