Let $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ be a
[quantum observable universe](#def:quantum-observable-universe) and
$\varrho \colon \mathbf{1} \to A$ a closed cycle.  Then the
inertial mass

$$
  \mathfrak{m}(\varrho)
  \;=\;
  \bigl\lVert
    \mathrm{tr}\bigl( (\int_A \otimes \mathrm{id}) \circ \Delta \circ \varrho \bigr)
  \bigr\rVert
$$

([mass identity](#def:mass-identity)) exists as an element of $R$ for
every $\varrho$.

Uniqueness is
([uniqueness of the mass identity](#prop:mass-identity-unique)).
