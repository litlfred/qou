**Proposition.**
*The coefficient of $\Phi_2$ in the Wedderburn mass formula is*

$$
  \beta_2 = -2[3]_q
          = -2 \cdot \frac{q^3 - q^{-3}}{q - q^{-1}}
          \approx -6.087
$$

*where $[3]_q$ is the quantum 3-integer.*
