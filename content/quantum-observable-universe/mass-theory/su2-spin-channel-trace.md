# SU(2) Spin-Channel Multiplicity and the Legacy Scaling Factor

The empirical legacy scaling factor previously attributed to the $H_4$ index set is exactly the multiplicity-weighted average of an isolated two-nucleon $SU(2)$ spin-channel interaction.

The two-nucleon tensor product $2 \otimes 2 \cong 3 \oplus 1$ decomposes into a spin-1 triplet and a spin-0 singlet. The multiplicity-weighted average of their quantum dimensions is:
$$ \frac{3 \cdot [3]_q + 1 \cdot [1]_q}{4} = \frac{3(q^2 + 1 + q^{-2}) + 1}{4} = \frac{3q^2 + 4 + 3q^{-2}}{4} $$

This algebraically matches the `Family A` legacy scalar exactly. The mass scale difference between $H_3$ and $H_4$ is precisely absorbed by this $SU(2)$ spin-channel trace scaling the underlying $H_3$ topological trace.
