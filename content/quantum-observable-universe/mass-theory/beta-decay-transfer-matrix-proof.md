Write $h = q - q^{-1}$.  Then:

$T_{pp} = T(1, 0)$: entries $[[h, 1], [1, 0]]$

$T_{nn} = T(1, -h)$: entries $[[0, 1], [1, -h]]$

$T_{\mathrm{mix}} = T(1, -h/2)$: entries $[[h/2, 1], [1, -h/2]]$

The differences $T_{\mathrm{mix}} - T_{nn}$ and
$T_{pp} - T_{\mathrm{mix}}$ both equal
$[[h/2, 0], [0, h/2]] = \frac{h}{2} \mathbb{I}$.
$\qquad\blacksquare$
