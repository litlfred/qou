Denef and Loeser's central insight: integration over definable
sets in Henselian valued fields produces elements of the
Grothendieck ring of varieties. The logical content of first-order
formulas — existential quantification, conjunction, disjunction —
translates into geometric operations: fibre products, disjoint
unions, complements. "Logical existence is a geometric quantity."

In the QOU framework, this principle manifests at two levels.

**At the foundational level** (QO $\to$ QOU): The QOU axioms are
first-order sentences in $\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{RMC}}$ whose truth
has a categorical measure. The [probability of QOU
existence](#def:qou-existence-probability)
$\mathrm{Prob}_q(\mathrm{QOU}) \in K_0(\mathbf{C})[L_q^{-1}]$
gives the existential statement "there exists a QOU" a definite
**volume** in the Grothendieck ring.

The [rigidity of the QOU locus](#prop:qou-motivic-euler)
(motivic Euler characteristic $= [\mathrm{pt}]$ in the
archimedean case) says: QOUs are rare but completely determined.
The universe exists with "probability zero yet necessarily" —
the locus is measure-zero inside $\mathfrak{M}_{\mathrm{QO}}$
but non-empty, and once realised, admits no continuous
deformations. The [axiom codimensions](#rem:qou-axiom-codimensions)
quantify exactly how each axiom contributes to this rigidity.

**At the representation-theoretic level** (q-GL motivic
structure): The [$q$-motivic zeta function](#def:q-motivic-zeta)
$Z_q(\mathcal{D}, s)$ encodes the depth structure of definable
subcategories of $\mathrm{Rep}(G_q)$ across the Kummer tower.
The perpetual obstruction at levels $n \nmid \chi(A)$ means the
logical content of a categorical sentence "thins out" ascending
the brane tower. The [$q$-motivic transfer
principle](#conj:q-motivic-transfer) connects $q$-scale
computation to classical geometry.

The two levels are linked by the Yekutieli class $\kappa_0$:
it simultaneously controls the rigidity of the QOU locus
(Part A) and the singularity structure of the motivic zeta
function (Part B). The arithmetic of $\chi(A) = -6$ — its
divisors $\{1, 2, 3, 6\}$, its prime factorisation $2 \cdot 3$
— determines both the surviving Kummer levels and the
factorisation of the existence probability.
