**Conjecture ($q$-Motivic Transfer Principle).** For any
definable sublocus $\mathcal{D}$ of $\mathfrak{M}_{\mathrm{QO}}$,
the [motivic integral](#def:qou-motivic-measure)
$\int_{\mathcal{D}} d\mu_q$ specialises under $q \to 1$ to the
classical motivic integral in
$K_0(\mathrm{Var}_k)[\mathbb{L}^{-1}]$:

1. **Filtered transfer.** The specialisation respects the
   Kummer filtration:
   $$
     \lim_{q \to 1} Z_q(\mathcal{D}, s)
     \;=\; Z_{\mathrm{mot}}(\mathcal{D}_1, s)
   $$
   where $Z_q$ is the [$q$-motivic zeta function](#def:q-motivic-zeta)
   and $Z_{\mathrm{mot}}$ is the classical Denef–Loeser motivic
   zeta function of the limit variety $\mathcal{D}_1$.

2. **Obstruction resolution.** At Kummer levels
   $n \nmid \chi(A)$, the perpetual obstruction contributes
   singularities to $Z_q$ that **resolve** in the $q \to 1$
   limit: the $q$-curvature $d_q^2 \to 0$ removes the
   intrinsic obstruction, and all Kummer levels become
   accessible.

3. **Langlands compatibility.** The transfer commutes with
   duality: the functional equation
   $Z_q(\mathcal{D}, s) = Z_{q^{-1}}(\check{\mathcal{D}}, d-s)$
   degenerates to the Poincaré duality functional equation of
   $Z_{\mathrm{mot}}$.

The conjecture asserts that the $q$-motivic measure is the
"universal" integration theory from which both classical motivic
integration and $p$-adic integration are obtained by
specialisation. The Kummer filtration controls which
specialisations are accessible: the [QOU existence
probability](#def:qou-existence-probability) at the $q$-scale
determines the classical motivic volume of the QOU locus.
