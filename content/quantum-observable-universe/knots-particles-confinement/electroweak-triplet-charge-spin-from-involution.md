**Theorem (conditional on the electroweak sector-selection postulate).**
*Fix the categorical coefficient ring $R$ (a field with $\tfrac{1}{2} \in R$) and the sector-selection postulate: each electroweak carrier is assigned to the spin-singlet sector $A_+$ ($\sigma$-fixed, bar-even) or the spin-doublet sector $A_-$ ($\sigma$-negated, bar-odd) of the spin involution $\sigma$, with $H$ the singlet and $W^\pm, Z$ the doublets, together with the isospin label map $T_3(W^\pm) = \pm 1$, $T_3(Z) = T_3(H) = 0$. Then the spin and charge of the triplet are determined:*

$$
  \mathrm{spin}(H) = 0 \ (\text{scalar, neutral}), \qquad
  \mathrm{spin}(W^\pm) = \mathrm{spin}(Z) = 1 \ (\text{vector}),
$$
$$
  T_3(W^\pm) = \pm 1 \ (\text{charged}), \qquad T_3(Z) = 0 \ (\text{neutral}).
$$

*Charge and spin are not independent open conjectures: both follow from the one physics selection postulate through the spin involution.*
