**Remark (K-theory ↔ depth filtration Rosetta — Beilinson regulator
matches the iterated-integral grading on $\mathcal{H}_q$).**

The graded-Mikusiński depth filtration
([`conj:gb-depth-period-correspondence`](#conj:gb-depth-period-correspondence))
on the substrate convolution algebra $\mathcal{H}_q$ admits an
**equivalent K-theoretic description**: the depth axis matches
the Beilinson–Borel regulator filtration on algebraic K-theory of
the algebraic-number-field-of-definition. This connection, while
not used elsewhere in the paper, is the standard period-theoretic
language and worth recording for cross-reference.

### The K-theoretic dictionary

| Depth $n$ | Iterated integral grouplike | Algebraic K-theory | Beilinson regulator |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | scalars, $\exp_q$ | $K_1(F) = F^\times$ | $K_1 \to F^\times \otimes \mathbb{R}$ (Dirichlet) |
| 1 | $\log_q$ | $K_1(F)$ via Bloch logs | Dirichlet regulator |
| 2 | $\mathrm{Li}_2^q$ | $K_3^{\mathrm{ind}}(F) \otimes \mathbb{Q}$ | Bloch–Wigner regulator $\to \mathbb{R} / \pi^2 \mathbb{Q}$ |
| 3 | $\mathrm{Li}_3^q$ | $K_5^{\mathrm{ind}}(F) \otimes \mathbb{Q}$ | Goncharov regulator $\to \mathbb{R} / \pi^4 \mathbb{Q}$ |
| $n$ | $\mathrm{Li}_n^q$ | $K_{2n-1}^{\mathrm{ind}}(F) \otimes \mathbb{Q}$ | Beilinson–Borel regulator $\to \mathbb{R} / \pi^{2(n-1)} \mathbb{Q}$ |

(Here $F = \overline{\mathbb{Q}}$ or a specific number field
$\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_n)$ for the cyclotomic-orbit-restricted version;
$K_*^{\mathrm{ind}}$ denotes the *indecomposable* part of higher
K-theory, modulo the image of products of lower K-groups —
equivalently, the part that detects "new" periods.)

### Why the dictionary is exact (heuristic)

The keystone ([`conj:gb-depth-period-correspondence`](#conj:gb-depth-period-correspondence))
asserts $\tau_{\mathrm{arch}}\bigl( G^{(n)}(\mathcal{H}_q) \bigr)
\subseteq \mathcal{P}_n$. Under the Beilinson conjectures
(Beilinson 1985), the Goncharov filtration $\mathcal{P}_\bullet$ is
*generated by* the Beilinson regulator images of $K_{2n-1}^{\mathrm{ind}}(F)
\otimes \mathbb{Q}$ for $n = 1, 2, \ldots$ over all number fields $F$.
Combining: the keystone factors as

$$
  G^{(n)}(\mathcal{H}_q)
  \;\xrightarrow{\;\;\text{evaluation}\;\;}\;
  K_{2n-1}^{\mathrm{ind}}(\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_K)) \otimes \mathbb{Q}
  \;\xrightarrow{\;\;\text{Beilinson}\;\;}\;
  \mathcal{P}_n / \mathcal{P}_{n-1}.
$$

The Bring-embeddable-knot specialisation: for $K$ with shape
parameter $z_K$, the field of definition is $\mathbb{Q}(z_K)$ —
typically a cyclotomic or icosahedral algebraic number field. The
relevant K-group is $K_{2n-1}^{\mathrm{ind}}(\mathbb{Q}(z_K))
\otimes \mathbb{Q}$.

### What this adds to the paper

K-theory was **not previously used** in the paper as a working
language. The transcendental-accounting ledger
([`rem:transcendental-accounting`](#rem:transcendental-accounting))
records each period under "Goncharov depth"; this remark provides
the equivalent K-theoretic label.

The two languages (Goncharov-Hopf-algebra vs Beilinson-K-theory)
are **fully equivalent** modulo Beilinson's conjectures (Bloch–Beilinson,
Goncharov–Manin). For computation and exposition, the paper
should freely use whichever is cleaner in context:

- **Hopf-algebra language** (used in the keystone) is cleaner for
  describing structural relations between grouplikes (multiplicative
  vs co-multiplicative, bar involution, joint actions).
- **K-theory language** (this remark) is cleaner for citing
  external results (Borel's regulator theorem, Beilinson's conjecture
  on $L$-values, Bloch's elliptic dilogarithm program).

### Significance for B1's discharge

The keystone's discharge targets reduce to:

1. **Inclusion target**: $\tau_{\mathrm{arch}}(G^{(n)}) \subseteq \mathcal{P}_n$.
   *K-theoretic version*: image of $G^{(n)}$ under evaluation lies
   in the Beilinson regulator's image. **This is essentially
   trivial** in the K-theory language (the regulator is
   surjective onto its image by definition); the content is the
   identification of $G^{(n)}$ with $K_{2n-1}^{\mathrm{ind}}$.

2. **Sharpness target**: image generates $\mathcal{P}_n / \mathcal{P}_{n-1}$
   non-trivially. *K-theoretic version*: $K_{2n-1}^{\mathrm{ind}}(F)
   \otimes \mathbb{Q}$ is non-zero for $n \geq 1$ when $F$ has
   appropriate signature (Borel's theorem). **This is a theorem**,
   not a conjecture, by Borel 1977. So the sharpness reduces to
   a Borel-rank computation per number field $F = \mathbb{Q}(z_K)$.

So switching to K-theoretic language **trivialises one keystone
discharge target** (sharpness via Borel) and reduces the other
(inclusion) to identifying the depth-$n$ grouplikes with
$K_{2n-1}^{\mathrm{ind}}$ generators. The Beilinson conjectures
then close the loop.
