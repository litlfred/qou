Item (4) of the [surreal rate calculus](#prop:surreal-rate-calculus) is the mechanism underlying every millennium-problem squeeze in Chapter~8 and Chapter~7. The cost of any standard-scale perturbation (a rogue zero off the critical line, a rogue vortex spike, a rogue Collatz trajectory, a rogue oracle separation) strictly exceeds the available mass-gap dissipation. The cost is transfinite. The dissipation is infinitesimal. No cancellation is possible in an ordered field.

This algebraic content replaces the informal "$O(\varepsilon^{-1})$ exceeds $O(\varepsilon^3)$ as $\varepsilon \to 0$". We do not need a limit. We only need an inequality inside $\mathbf{No}$.
