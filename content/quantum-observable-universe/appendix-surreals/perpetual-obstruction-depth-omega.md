The [motivic perpetual obstruction](#conj:motivic-perpetual-obstruction) asserts that the Yekutieli obstruction $\kappa_n$ is non-trivial at every finite tower level $n$. Under the surreal lift, this infinite family of non-vanishing statements is the shadow of a single graded coefficient.

We recall the substrate identification $\varepsilon = q - 1 \leftrightarrow 1/\omega$ ([surreal blow-up calculus](#def:surreal-blowup-calculus)), under which the level-$n$ contribution sits at $\varepsilon$-order $n$. The element

$$
  \varepsilon^{\omega} \;=\; \omega^{-\omega},
  \qquad
  0 \;<\; \varepsilon^{\omega} \;<\; \varepsilon^{n}
  \quad\text{for every finite } n,
$$

is the unique grade lying beneath the entire finite tower. This is the depth-$\omega$ slot of the [Conway normal form](#rem:conway-normal-form). It exists in $\mathbf{No}$ precisely because $\mathbf{No}$ admits Hahn expansions with surreal exponents. It has no counterpart in the archimedean Hahn line $\mathbb{R}((\varepsilon^{\mathbb{Q}}))$, whose exponents are finite.

**Interpretation.** Perpetuity of the obstruction is equivalent to the surreal-lifted obstruction $\widehat{\kappa}$ carrying a non-zero component at the depth-$\omega$ grade:

$$
  \kappa_n \neq 0 \ \ (\forall\, n \in \mathbb{N})
  \qquad\Longleftrightarrow\qquad
  [\varepsilon^{\omega}]\,\widehat{\kappa} \;\neq\; 0 .
$$

The universally-quantified family collapses to one non-vanishing graded coefficient. This is the cleanest service the surreal field renders the framework. An obstruction beyond every finite order acquires an explicit home as a single element of the value group, acting as a non-perturbative datum invisible to each $\varepsilon^{n}$ truncation.

**Scope.** The equivalence is a re-grading, not a new theorem. By Mostow rigidity the volume series $\mathrm{Vol} = \sum_n \Phi_n$ converges in $\mathbb{R}$ ([Chern–Simons evaluation faithfulness](#prop:cs-evaluation-faithfulness)). We recover the archimedean value by the standard part without surreal arithmetic. The depth-$\omega$ statement provides a single graded witness for perpetuity, narrowing the focus without archimedean obligations.
