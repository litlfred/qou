**Proposition (balanced-holonomy cycle exclusion, within a QOU).**
Let $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ be a quantum observable universe with substrate parameter $q$ and Skein module $\mathcal{S}(q) \cong \mathbb{Z}[q^{± 1}]$. The Skein-Collatz map $\mathcal{C}_q$ has no periodic orbit of balanced multiplicative holonomy — none with $A = \prod_i a_i = 1$ — other than the degenerate fixed point. Equivalently, $[3]_q^{k_{\mathrm{odd}}} = [2]_q^{k}$ has no solution with $k_{\mathrm{odd}} \geq 1$, by coprimality of $[2]_q$ and $[3]_q$.

Full nontrivial-cycle exclusion — the affine ($A \neq 1$) case — is part of the open [$q$-Collatz conjecture](#conj:q-collatz), not of this proposition; see the [proof](#prf:collatz-no-cycles) and [contact boundary](#prop:collatz-contact-boundary).
