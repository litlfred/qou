**Visualization aid.** We picture the substrate-parameter axis $q \in \mathbb{R}_{>0}$ ([substrate parameter](#def:substrate-parameter)) as the radial coordinate of a schematic cone. It has two named endpoints from [Big Bang / heat death](#conj:bigbang-heatdeath):

| endpoint | $q$ | structure constants | geometric reading |
|---|---|---|---|
| Big Bang | $q \to 1^+$ | $c_q = 1/(q-1) \to \infty$, $\hbar_q = (q-1)/q \to 0$ | "apex": all states causally connected, no time separation |
| heat death | $q \to \infty$ | $c_q \to 0$, $\hbar_q \to 1$ | "asymptotic base": classical and quantum scales of action saturate |

At every interior $q$, each cross-section of the cone carries a copy of the Reeb bundle $\mathcal{R}_p \cong S^3$ over the exceptional divisor $\mathcal{S}_p$ ([exceptional divisor](#def:exceptional-divisor)) at brane point $p$. The three color sectors $\mathfrak{r}, \mathfrak{g}, \mathfrak{b}$ ([color-sector coordinates](#def:color-sector-coords)) thread along the cone under the color twist $q\phi$. As $q$ varies, the embedding $(O, R, T_1, T_2, N)$ deforms continuously. It sweeps the sectors through their $q$-deformed positions while preserving the underlying $\mathcal{S}_p$-topology.

**Direction mnemonic.** The light speed $c_q$ diverges at the Big Bang ($q \to 1^+$). The parameter $\hbar_q$ saturates to $1$ at heat death ($q \to \infty$). We record the direction in [Big Bang / heat death](#conj:bigbang-heatdeath) and restate it here to prevent swapping the endpoints.

**Scope and limitations.** The cone acts as a schematic mnemonic for the $q$-parameter family of QOUs. It does not:

- Introduce a metric on the parameter axis (we do not claim $r = \sqrt{t}$ or any specific cone angle is physical).
- Commit to a cosmological-time reading of $q$ (parameter-time symmetry is preserved).
- Promote [Big Bang / heat death](#conj:bigbang-heatdeath) from conjecture to theorem (the endpoint functors remain speculative).

We include this visualization only to give a unifying picture of how the substrate parameter, the exceptional divisor, the Reeb bundle, and the color sectors fit on a single axis.
