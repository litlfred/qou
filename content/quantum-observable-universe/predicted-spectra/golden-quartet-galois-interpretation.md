**Remark (novelty).** While the Galois properties of $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{5})$ and the golden ratio are entirely standard, introducing the joint group $G = \mathrm{Gal} \times \langle \iota \rangle$ acting on the specific quartet $Q$ provides a novel structural refactoring for QOU models. Rather than relying on standard cyclotomic $\zeta_5$ bookkeeping which carries complex phase ambiguities, this formulation allows physical observables to be strictly bounded within the maximal real subfield. This geometric substitution of the cyclotomic Galois tracking with a pure real orbit $Q$ is conceptually novel in the context of Bring's curve reductions.
