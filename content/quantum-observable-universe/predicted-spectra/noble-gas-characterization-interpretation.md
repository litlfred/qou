**Remark (why "full curls").**

Each trefoil $T_{2,3}$ is a $(2,3)$-torus knot — a curve that
winds **2 times** around one axis and **3 times** around the
other.  The writhe (signed crossing number) encodes the
electron's angular state.  A closed sub-Hopf link at level $n$
is precisely a chain where every trefoil's curls are
**fully interlocked** with its neighbours: no crossing can be
added or removed by a Reidemeister I move without breaking the
sub-link.

In other words:

> **Full shell = full curls.**

A shell is "full" not because a count has been reached, but
because the trefoil chain has exhausted all available linking
sites at that writhe level.  The topological rigidity of the
closed sub-link is what makes noble gases chemically inert —
there is no crossing available for a new electron to hook into.

**Remark (mathematical novelty).** This full-shell-as-full-curls topological characterization breaks from standard heuristic chemistry, introducing a novel sub-Hopf link invariant to rigidly classify noble gas inertness and formally binding empirical ionization energy peaks to crossing-energy maxima.
