**Remark.** In the algebraic geometry of spectrahedra, pioneered by Sturmfels and Parrilo, the valid solution space of a semidefinite program (SDP) forms a highly structured convex domain. The phase collapse described above is structurally identical to the conformal bootstrap methodology of high-energy physics. Demanding unitarity isolates physical theories—such as the 3D Ising model "island"—strictly on the extreme geometric boundaries, or spectral extents, of SDP solution spaces. Similarly, the convex algebraic geometry of curvature operators dictates strict geometric boundaries.
