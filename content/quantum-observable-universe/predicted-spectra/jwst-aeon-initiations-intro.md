We scale the algebraic structure of the photon-electron transfer matrix $T_{\gamma e}$ to macroscopic observables. Recent observations from the James Webb Space Telescope (JWST) reveal a population of extremely compact, luminous objects in the early universe ($z \sim 4 - 10$), such as GLIMPSE-17775. 

These objects exhibit extreme Balmer breaks, broad H-$\alpha$ emission, and unexpected high-metallicity spikes without accompanying X-ray bremsstrahlung. We formalize these objects algebraically as localized aeon initiation events. Their anomalous physical observables derive directly from the convex algebraic geometry of the Hecke algebra's spectrahedron.
