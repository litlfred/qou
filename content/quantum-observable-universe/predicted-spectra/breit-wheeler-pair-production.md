**Definition (Breit--Wheeler pair production).**

The Breit--Wheeler process models categorical pair production, $\gamma\gamma \to e^- e^+$. In the Iwahori--Hecke algebra $H_3(q)$, this fusion of two photons into an electron--positron pair corresponds to the identity:

$$
  \gamma_1 \cdot \gamma_2
  = 
  \mathrm{NF}(e^-) + \mathrm{NF}(e^+)
  +  \text{binding energy}.
$$

The photon, electron, and positron are represented by the braid words:

$$
  \gamma = \sigma_0, \qquad
  e^- = \sigma_0\sigma_1\sigma_0  \text{ (trefoil)}, \qquad
  e^+ = \sigma_0^{-1}\sigma_1^{-1}\sigma_0^{-1}  \text{ (mirror trefoil)}.
$$
