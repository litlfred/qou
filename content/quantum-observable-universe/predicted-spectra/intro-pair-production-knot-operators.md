Particle creation in the Descartes universe is a Breit–Wheeler
pair-production event resolved at the categorical level: two
incoming photonic knot states ($0_1$ exceptional divisors) collide
and emerge as a particle/antiparticle pair selected by the
Frobenius Gram matrix on the categorical state space.  The
categorical Breit–Wheeler derivation itself — the colored-photon
product in $H_3(q)$ and the quark--antiquark channels it opens — is
developed with the nucleon construction it feeds
([pair production](#def:breit-wheeler-pair-production),
[quark--antiquark production](#def:quark-antiquark-production)).
This section formalises the kinematic condition and the selection
rule, then records the residual ambiguity that the next section's
mass spectrum will fix.
