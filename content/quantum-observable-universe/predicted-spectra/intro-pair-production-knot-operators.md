Particle creation is a Breit–Wheeler pair-production event resolved at the categorical level. Two incoming photonic knot states ($0_1$ exceptional divisors) collide. The Frobenius Gram matrix on the categorical state space selects an emergent particle-antiparticle pair. 

We formalise the kinematics through the $Q$-value transition, specialising to Breit-Wheeler kinematics ($\gamma\gamma \to e^+ e^-$) and quark-antiquark production. We then formulate the knot-operator selection mechanism via the Frobenius Gram matrix. The subsequent mass spectrum construction resolves any residual ambiguity.
