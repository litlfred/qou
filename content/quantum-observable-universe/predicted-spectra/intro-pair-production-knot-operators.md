Particle creation in the Descartes universe is a Breit–Wheeler
pair-production event resolved at the categorical level: two
incoming photonic knot states ($0_1$ exceptional divisors) collide
and emerge as a particle/antiparticle pair selected by the
Frobenius Gram matrix on the categorical state space.  This
section formalises the kinematics and the selection rule, then
records the residual ambiguity that the next section's mass
spectrum will fix.

The arc starts with the $Q$-value transition (the kinematic condition for pair production), specialises to Breit-Wheeler kinematics (the simplest $\gamma\gamma \to e^+ e^-$ channel) and quark-antiquark production, then closes with the knot-operator selection mechanism and the Frobenius Gram matrix that pins which channels survive.
