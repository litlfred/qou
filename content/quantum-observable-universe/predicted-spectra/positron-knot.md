The **positron** is the mirror trefoil $T_{2,3}^{\sigma}$ in the
lepton sector — the antiparticle of the electron.

$$
  e^+ =  T_{2,3}^{\sigma} \qquad \text{(mirror trefoil, outlined ○)}
$$

The positron is the same knot as the down quark, distinguished
by environment (orbital vs. color tube).  It carries reversed
crossing chirality: where the electron has Coulomb charge
$Q = -1$, the positron has $Q = +1$.

**Visual convention.** The positron is drawn as an **outlined**
trefoil (reversed time direction).

**Time reversal interpretation.**  The mirror operation
$\sigma$ reverses all crossings, which in the Reeb flow
corresponds to reversing the direction of time
([chirality remark](#rem:chirality-trefoil-electron-positron)).
The positron is an electron travelling backward in time
(Feynman--Stueckelberg interpretation).
