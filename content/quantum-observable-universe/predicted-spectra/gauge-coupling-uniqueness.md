**Corollary (uniqueness of the scalar gauge coupling).**
*Any $R$-linear, braid-invariant, scalar coupling
$A \otimes A \to \mathbf{1}$ is a multiple of
$\mathrm{tr}_\tau(e_-) \cdot e_-$.  Up to the global normalisation
$\lambda \in R$, there is exactly one non-trivial scalar coupling
on the two-strand sector of $\mathbf{C}$.*
