*Lean sibling: archimedean specialisation over $\mathbb{R}$ (AGENTS.md §7c). Underlying combinatorial content stays generic to any commutative ring with the same $q$-data.*

**Beta decay** is the crossing operation that inserts the weak-force
torus knot $T_{3,5}$ (braid word $\beta_{T_{3,5}} = (\sigma_1\sigma_2)^5$,
$10$ crossings, [force identification](#rem:force-knot-identification))
at the converting quark of a nuclear Gröbner--Shirshov normal form
$\mathcal{N}(Z, N)$ ([stable nucleus](#def:stable-nucleus)), followed by
re-reduction.

## Definition ($\beta^-$ decay)

*A $\beta^-$ transition inserts the $T_{3,5}$ braid at the
converting $\mathfrak{d}$-quark crossing of the Gröbner--Shirshov normal form
$\mathcal{N}(Z, N)$, followed by re-reduction:*

$$
  \mathcal{N}(Z, N) \cdot \beta_{T_{3,5}}
  \xrightarrow{ \text{reduce}}
  \mathcal{N}(Z + 1,  N - 1)
  \sqcup
  T_{2,3}
  \sqcup
  K_{0_1}
$$

## Definition ($\beta^+$ decay)

*A $\beta^+$ transition is the mirror operation, inserting the
conjugate $T_{3,5}^{\sigma}$ braid (with $q \leftrightarrow q^{-1}$):*

$$
  \mathcal{N}(Z, N) \cdot \beta_{T_{3,5}}^{\sigma}
  \xrightarrow{ \text{reduce}}
  \mathcal{N}(Z - 1,  N + 1)
  \sqcup
  T_{2,3}^{\sigma}
  \sqcup
  K_{0_1}
$$
