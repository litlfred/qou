**Proposition (conditional on [`conj:borromean-quark`](#conj:borromean-quark)).**
Let $T_{2,3}$ be the electron trefoil ([electron knot](#def:electron-knot))
Hopf-linked to a proton anchor, with writhe $w$ in its minimal diagram.

1. **Shell--writhe correspondence.** The principal quantum number $n$ is
   determined by the writhe level via $n = (w - 1)/2$, with ground state
   $w = 3$ ($n = 1$) and excited states $w = 2n + 1$ obtained by pairs of
   Reidemeister I moves at the exceptional divisor.

2. **Orbital counting.** At shell $n$, the resonance modes of the trefoil
   vibrating transversely in its normal bundle $N_{T_{2,3}}$ are labelled
   by $\ell = 0, 1, \ldots, n - 1$, each with $2\ell + 1$ orientations,
   giving $\sum_{\ell=0}^{n-1}(2\ell + 1) = n^2$ orbitals per shell.

3. **Energy scaling.** The Seifert surface of the Hopf link at level $n$
   has area $\propto n^2$.  The crossing energy per Reidemeister I curl
   scales as $1/n^2$, yielding $E_n = -E_{\mathrm{ion}} / n^2$.
