**Eigenvalue properties:**
- $\lambda_+ \cdot \lambda_- = \det T_{\gamma e} = -1$
  (so $\lambda_- = -1/\lambda_+$)
- $\lambda_+ + \lambda_- = \mathrm{tr} T_{\gamma e} = q - q^{-1}$
- $\lambda_+ > 1 > |\lambda_-|$ for all $q > 1$
- At $q = 1$: $\lambda_+ = 1$, $\lambda_- = -1$ (degenerate)
