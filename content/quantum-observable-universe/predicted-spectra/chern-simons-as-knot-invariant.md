Classical Chern--Simons does not survive as the bulk categorical
action ([comparison](#rem:categorical-action-vs-chern-simons)):
that role is filled by $\mathfrak{S}_q = \mathrm{tr}(\mathcal{L}_q)$,
whose archimedean image is Einstein--Hilbert + Yang--Mills + Dirac.
The Chern--Simons functional instead re-enters the framework as
one entry in a dictionary of *geometric knot invariants*, selected
by the commensurability class of the knot complement.

The mechanism is:

1. Trace the coupling endomorphism $P_{\mathrm{anti}}$ on a
   force-carrier knot configuration; at the $J^2$ jet layer this
   trace is a Gram pairing on pairs of knots.

2. For *hyperbolic* knots (generic case) the Gram entry is the
   hyperbolic volume $\mathrm{Vol}(S^3 \setminus K)$ of the knot
   complement.

3. For *non-hyperbolic* torus knots $T_{m,n}$ --- including the
   force carriers $T_{2,9}$ (strong) and $T_{3,5}$ (weak) --- the
   hyperbolic volume vanishes and is replaced by the classical
   Chern--Simons functional

   $$
     \mathrm{CS}(T_{m,n})
     =
     \frac{\pi^2\, (m^2 - 1)(n^2 - 1)}{12\, m\, n}.
   $$

4. The determinant ratio
   $\kappa = \det G_{\mathrm{CS}}^{\mathrm{cl}} / \det G_{\mathrm{CS}}^{q}$
   that appears in the fine-structure correction
   $1/\alpha = q \cdot [9]_q [10]_q (1 + \delta_{\mathrm{CS}} + \delta_{\mathrm{CS}}^2 \kappa)$
   is therefore exactly the classical-vs-$q$-deformed ratio of
   Chern--Simons pairings \cite{witten2011}.

Classical CS is not absent and not fundamental: it is the
geometric invariant the framework happens to call on whenever the
knot complement fails to be hyperbolic.  The analytic continuation
of Chern--Simons theory \cite{witten2011} provides the
$q$-deformed counterpart $G_{\mathrm{CS}}^{q}$ directly.
