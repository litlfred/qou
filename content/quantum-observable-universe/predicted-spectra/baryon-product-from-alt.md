**Proposition (Alt-isotype identification of the baryon integrand).**
*Let $F(z, c) \in R[z, c]$ be the color-tube cubic
([color-tube cubic](#rem:color-tube-cubic)) and let
$c_1(z), c_2(z), c_3(z)$ denote its three roots, viewed as
multi-valued algebraic functions of $z$ over the categorical base
ring $R$.  Let $\mathrm{S}_3$ act on the three color sheets by
permutation.  Then in the canonical Wedderburn decomposition*

$$
  R[\mathrm{S}_3]
  \;\cong\;
  R \oplus R \oplus R^{2 \times 2}
  \quad
  (\text{trivial} \;\oplus\; \text{sign} \;\oplus\; \text{standard}),
$$

*the integrand*

$$
  \lambda_{\mathrm{baryon}}(z)
  \;:=\;
  \prod_{k=1}^{3} \frac{dc_k}{dz}
$$

*is the projection onto the $\mathrm{Alt}^3$ (sign) isotype of the
$\mathrm{S}_3$-module $\bigotimes_{k=1}^3 \,T_{c_k}\mathbf{C}$
generated by the three colour-sheet velocities.  The corresponding
sum $\sum_k dc_k/dz$ is the projection onto the trivial (symmetric)
isotype, and $\sum_{k \neq l} (dc_k/dz)(c_l + z)$ projects onto the
$2$-dimensional standard isotype.*

*This is a purely algebraic statement: it lives over the categorical
base ring $R$ and makes no use of the archimedean fibre functor
$\tau \colon R \to \mathbb{R}$.  The quark-color content of the baryon
selects the $\mathrm{Alt}$ isotype, fixing the integrand uniquely
up to scalar.*

*

**Why "product, not sum".**
*The sum $\sum_k dc_k/dz$ is the projection onto $V_{(3)}$ (the
trivial isotype) and is the integrand for a putative $\mathrm{Sym}^3$
particle (a colour-singlet symmetric trion).  The baryon, by quark
content, sits in the antisymmetric colour singlet $\mathrm{Alt}^3$
and so its integrand is the wedge / product.  This selection is
purely group-theoretic — fixed by the Wedderburn decomposition of
$R[\mathrm{S}_3]$ above — and requires no input from the analytic
PL machinery.*

**Categorical status.**
*Both Vandermonde-cubed and the projector $\pi_{\mathrm{Alt}}$ are
defined entirely over $R$; the proposition is a statement in
$\mathrm{End}_{\mathbf{C}}(\Lambda^3 V) = R$ and does **not**
require the embedding $R \hookrightarrow \mathbb{C}$.  The
analytic / archimedean evaluation of $\int_{-a}^{a} \prod_k dc_k/dz \,dz$
(baryon period ratio) is a separate
step — see SW-period thimble equivalence —
which lifts this $R$-rational integrand to a Picard–Lefschetz
contour integral after applying $\tau$.*
