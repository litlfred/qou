Let $(\mathcal{C}, A, \tau)$ be a
([quantum observable universe](#def:quantum-observable-universe)) with
[weight decomposition](#def:weight-decomposition) $(\pi_H, \pi_V)$
and [categorical Reeb derivation](#def:categorical-reeb)
$(\xi, \theta_A)$. Then there exists a
[substrate parameter](#def:substrate-parameter)
$q \colon M \to \mathbb{R}_{>0}$ with $q(p) \ge 1$, not a root of
unity, such that the $q$-deformed differential

$$d_q = q^{-1}\, d + (1 - q^{-1})\, \pi_V \circ d$$

interpolates between the full differential $d$ (at $q = 1$) and the
vertical projection $\pi_V \circ d$ (as $q \to \infty$).

The construction proceeds by choosing $q_0 > 1$ transcendental (hence
not a root of unity) and setting $q(p) = q_0$ for all $p$. The
classicality bound $q \ge 1$ and positivity are immediate from
$q_0 > 1$. Non-constant substrate fields arise from the Reeb flow
parameterisation of the fibre coordinate.
