In the archimedean realisation, the categorical $d_q$
([Definition](#def:categorical-q-ext-deriv)) specialises to
$d_q \colon \Omega^k(M) \to \Omega^{k+1}(M)$:

$$
  d_q\,\alpha
  = q^{-1}\,d\alpha
        + (1 - q^{-1})\,\theta \wedge \mathcal{L}_\xi\,\alpha.
$$

The vertical projection $\pi_V \circ d$ becomes
$\theta \wedge \mathcal{L}_\xi$ via the Reeb decomposition
([Example](#def:reeb-decomposition)), and
$d_q^2 = q^{-1}(1-q^{-1})\,d\theta \wedge \mathcal{L}_\xi$
([Proposition](#prop:categorical-dq-squared)) specialises via
$\omega_V = d\theta$.
