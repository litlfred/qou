Let $(\mathcal{C}, A, \omega)$ be a
([quantum observable universe](#def:quantum-observable-universe)) with substrate
parameter $q_0$ ([substrate parameter](#def:substrate-parameter)).  The
*quantum gauge group* is
$G_{q_0} = \mathrm{SU}_{q_0}(2)$, the quantum group obtained by
deforming the Tannakian gauge group
$G = \mathrm{Aut}^{\otimes}(\omega) \cong \mathrm{SU}(2)$
([SU(2) emergence](#thm:su2-emergence)) at the parameter $q_0$.  When the
substrate parameter varies over a space $M$ (e.g. the archimedean
realisation), this yields a field of quantum groups
$\{G_{q(p)}\}_{p \in M}$.
