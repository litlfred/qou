In the rigid monoidal categories relevant to this paper, the unit
object $\mathbf{1}$ is *simple*: its endomorphism ring is the base
ring, $\mathrm{End}(\mathbf{1}) \cong R$. This forces the space of
morphisms $\mathrm{Hom}(\Lambda^2 V, \mathbf{1})$ to be
one-dimensional over $R$.

Consequently, if $\epsilon' \colon \Lambda^2 V \twoheadrightarrow
\mathbf{1}$ is any other non-degenerate epimorphism, then
$\epsilon' = c\,\epsilon$ for some unit $c \in R^{\times}$.
Physically, choosing a specific $\epsilon$ from this one-dimensional
line amounts to fixing a fundamental unit of volume. Once this
baseline scale is chosen, the gauge symmetries
$g \in \mathrm{Aut}^{\otimes}(\omega)$ lock it in place via the
commutativity constraint
([categorical volume constraint](#eq:categorical-volume-constraint)).

This structure anchors the topological invariants directly within
the categorical data $(\mathcal{C}, A, \omega)$: the volume
$\epsilon$ arises not as an external parameter but as a structural
morphism intrinsic to the observable universe.
