In the archimedean realisation
([Example](#def:c-quantum-universe)), the categorical weight
decomposition ([Definition](#def:weight-decomposition)) specialises
to the *Reeb decomposition*.  Let $\xi$ denote the *Reeb
vector field*, characterised by $\iota_\xi\,d\theta = 0$ and
$\theta(\xi) = 1$.  Every differential $k$-form $\alpha$ ($k \geq 1$)
on $M$ decomposes uniquely as

$$
  \alpha = \alpha_H + \theta \wedge \iota_\xi\,\alpha,
$$

where $\alpha_H := \alpha - \theta \wedge \iota_\xi\,\alpha$ is the
*horizontal component* ($\iota_\xi\,\alpha_H = 0$) and
$\alpha_V := \theta \wedge \iota_\xi\,\alpha$ is the *vertical
component*.  The Reeb vector $\xi$ generates the
$\mathrm{U}(1)$-action (the maximal torus,
[Definition](#def:maximal-torus)), and the weight-$0$ subspace is the
horizontal distribution $H = \ker\theta$.
