Let $\Gamma \subset \mathrm{SU}(2)$ be a finite subgroup and let $X$ be the
minimal resolution of $\mathbb{C}^2 / \Gamma$.  The link
$M = S^3 / \Gamma$ is a compact $3$-dimensional strictly pseudoconvex
CR manifold.  The Reeb vector field $\xi$ generates the Hopf-type
fibration $S^3/\Gamma \to S^2$.  Equipped with the induced principal
$\mathrm{SU}(2)$-bundle $P \to M$ and state bundle
$E = P \times_G \mathbb{P}^1(\mathbb{C})$, the tuple
$(M, \theta, G, P, E)$ provides an archimedean quantum observable universe.
