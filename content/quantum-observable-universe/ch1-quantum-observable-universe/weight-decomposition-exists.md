Let $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ be a
([quantum observable universe](#def:quantum-observable-universe)) with
[maximal torus](#def:maximal-torus) $T$ acting on
[higher forms](#def:higher-forms) $\Omega^k(A)$. Then there exists a
[weight decomposition](#def:weight-decomposition)
$(\pi_H, \pi_V)$ of $\Omega^k(A)$ satisfying:

1. **Complementarity:** $\pi_H + \pi_V = \mathrm{id}$.
2. **Idempotency:** $\pi_H^2 = \pi_H$ and $\pi_V^2 = \pi_V$.

```tex
\begin{equation}\label{eq:weight-decomposition-exists-diagram}
\begin{tikzcd}[row sep=2.5em, column sep=3em]
  \Omega^k(A) \ar[r, "\pi_H"] \ar[d, "\pi_V"']
  & \Omega^k_H(A) \\
  \Omega^k_V(A)
\end{tikzcd}
\qquad (\pi_H + \pi_V = \mathrm{id},\; \pi_H \pi_V = 0)
\end{equation}
```
