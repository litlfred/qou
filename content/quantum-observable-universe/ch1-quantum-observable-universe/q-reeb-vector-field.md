The categorical Reeb derivation $\xi$
([Definition](#def:categorical-reeb)) deforms in the archimedean
setting to $\xi_q$, acting on smooth functions $f \in C^\infty(M)$ by
$$
  \xi_q(f) \;=\; q^{-1}\,\xi(f) + (1 - q^{-1})\,[\xi, X_f]_q,
$$
where $X_f$ is the horizontal lift of $\mathrm{grad}_{g_\theta}\,f$ to
$H = \ker\theta$ and $[\,\cdot\,,\cdot\,]_q$ is the $q$-deformed Lie
bracket ([Example](#ex:q-lie-bracket)).
