In the archimedean realisation
([$\mathbb{C}$-quantum observable universe](#def:c-quantum-observable-universe)), an
([observation](#def:observation)) is realised as a continuous open map
from the spacetime base $M$ to the bundle of endomorphisms
$\operatorname{End}(E) \to M$, encoding the coordinate change in torsion
energy of the bundle $E$.  Because the substrate parameter $q(p)$
([substrate parameter](#def:substrate-parameter)) is perturbed by
condition (2), every observation shifts the non-commutative geometry:
observing the universe changes it.
