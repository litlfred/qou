The brane tower is built level by level, but the existence of each level depends on a cohomological obstruction vanishing. This section measures that obstruction, identifies it with a quantum characteristic class, and uses the result to upgrade a quantum universe to a quantum observable universe. We follow Yekutieli's framework for central extensions of gerbes throughout.

We open with **Categorical Lifting Condition** (def), which abstracts the gerbe-theoretic content: an $R$-linear rigid monoidal category $\mathbf{C}$ satisfies the condition with respect to a central extension $1 \to \mathcal{B} \to \mathfrak{G}' \to \mathfrak{G} \to 1$ when each object $x \in \mathfrak{G}$ carries a single cohomology class $\kappa(x) \in H^2(\mathcal{S}, \mathcal{B})$ obstructing its lift. **Quantum Characteristic Classes** (def) names this obstruction in the brane-tower setting, and the paired propositions **Quantum Characteristic Classes (existence)** (prop) and **Quantum Characteristic Classes (uniqueness)** (prop) verify that the class is well-defined. **Categorical lifting condition holds in the brane tower** (prop) is the structural payoff: the gerbe sequence arising from **Jet Prolongation** (prop) satisfies the Yekutieli condition, with $\mathcal{B} = \mathcal{O}_M^{\times}$ in the smooth case, so the level-$n$ to level-$(n+1)$ lift exists iff $\kappa_n = 0$.

The lifting machinery feeds **Quantum universe with observability and categorical lifting is a QOU** (thm), the main bridge result of the chapter: a quantum universe equipped with an observability datum and a categorical lift is automatically a QOU. **Fermionic sectors as twisted sectors** (rem), **The Kummer tower and perpetual obstruction** (rem), and **Hochschild--Kummer connection** (rem) interpret the obstruction class physically and connect it to the Kummer filtration. **Motivic Galois character $\chi^{\mathrm{mot}}_A$** (def) and **Quantum observable universe carries a motivic Galois character** (def) record the motivic refinement; **Motivic perpetual obstruction: $\chi^{\mathrm{mot}}_A$ is non-trivial at almost every finite place when $\chi(A) \neq 0$** (conj) is the conjectural endpoint.

The next section, *Direct Limit Algebra*, consumes the lifting result by passing to the colimit over the tower; the motivic conjecture is the form of obstruction it treats as given.
