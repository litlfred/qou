The Kummer filtration of $H^2(\mathcal{S}, \mathbb{G}_m)$
is related to the Hochschild cohomology of the
Frobenius--Hopf algebra $A$ via the
Hochschild--Kostant--Rosenberg comparison:

$$
  HH^2(\mathcal{U}_q, \mathrm{Ad})
  \xrightarrow{ \mathrm{HKR}}
  H^2(\mathcal{S}, \mathbb{G}_m).
$$

The deformation-theoretic obstruction (Hochschild) and the
gerbe-theoretic obstruction (Yekutieli) are two views of
the same class.  The Kummer filtration on the target
pulls back to a filtration on $HH^2$ that measures the
$n$-divisibility of deformation obstructions \cite{yekutieli2010}.
