**Motivic-Galois reading.**
The Kummer filtration on $H^2(\mathcal{S}, \mathbb{G}_m)$ is the restriction to the cyclotomic Galois subgroup $\mathrm{Gal}(\mathbb{Q}(\mu_\infty)/\mathbb{Q})$ of the **motivic-Galois filtration** on the [motivic substrate](#def:motivic-substrate). At each finite place $p \nmid \chi(A)$ of $\mathbb{Q}$ ([adelic substrate](#def:adelic-substrate)), the realization $\rho_p$ projects $\kappa_0$ to a non-trivial Frobenius character in $H^1_{\mathrm{Gal}}(\mathrm{Gal}(\bar{\mathbb{Q}}_p/\mathbb{Q}_p),\,\mu_n(1))$ through the cyclotomic ideal $\mathbb{Z}[q]/\Phi_n(q)$. The perpetual obstruction guarantees that the motivic-Galois character of the Frobenius–Hopf substrate is non-trivial in $\mathrm{Hom}(\chi_n, 1)$ for every $n$. This realises perpetuity at every cyclotomic place.

The Frobenius character of $\kappa_0$ is non-trivial at every finite place $p \nmid \chi(A)$. We establish this in the [motivic perpetual obstruction](#conj:motivic-perpetual-obstruction) (proved via Route A: prime-ideal collapse, $C = a_0(-1) + 2 \cdot a_1(-1)$). The divisibility properties of the [Kummer tower](#rem:kummer-tower) follow as corollaries at each adelic place.
