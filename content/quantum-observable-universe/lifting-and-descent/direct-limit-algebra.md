Let $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ be a
([quantum observable universe](#def:quantum-observable-universe)); let $q$ denote the substrate parameter.  Let
$\{A_n\}_{n \geq 0}$ be its
([recursive brane tower](#def:recursive-brane-tower)).  The
**direct limit algebra of the quantum observable universe** is the
colimit in the category of $R$-algebras:

$$
  \mathcal{A}_\infty
  = 
  \varinjlim_{n}
  \mathrm{End}_{\mathbf{C}}(A_n),
$$

taken over the tower of endomorphism algebras with the transition maps
induced by the canonical embeddings $A_n \hookrightarrow A_{n+1}$.

In the archimedean realisation
([$\mathbb{C}$-quantum observable universe](#ex:c-quantum-observable-universe)), applying the fibre
functor $\tau$ gives
$\mathcal{A}_\infty \cong \varinjlim\Gamma(J^1 \mathcal{S}_n, \mathrm{SU}_{q_n}(2))$.
