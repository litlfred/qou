The jet prolongation ([jet prolongation](#prop:jet-prolongation))
produces a central extension
$1 \to \mathcal{B} \to \mathfrak{G}_{n+1} \to \mathfrak{G}_n \to 1$
of gerbes banded by the sheaf of units $\mathcal{B}$.  By Giraud's
classification of gerbe extensions, every such central extension is
classified by a class in $H^2(\mathcal{S}, \mathcal{B})$; this class
is $\kappa_n$.

Concretely: choose local trivialisations
$\{U_\alpha \to \mathcal{S}\}$ of $\mathfrak{G}_n$ with transition
$2$-cocycles $\{g_{\alpha\beta\gamma}\}$ valued in $\mathcal{B}$.
The obstruction to extending these transitions to $\mathfrak{G}_{n+1}$
is the Čech cocycle $\kappa_n = [\{g_{\alpha\beta\gamma}\}] \in H^2$.
Independence of the trivialisation choice follows because different
trivialisations give cohomologous cocycles.
