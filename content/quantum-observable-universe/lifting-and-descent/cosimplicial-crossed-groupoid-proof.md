We verify the cosimplicial identities and the crossed-module axioms.

By
([cosimplicial identities](#lem\:ccg-cosimplicial-identities)),
the coface and codegeneracy maps satisfy the four cosimplicial
identities.  By
([crossed-module structure](#lem\:ccg-crossed-module-structure)),
the pair $(\mathrm{SU}_{q_n}(2), \mathcal{O}_M^{\times})$ is a crossed
module at each level $n$.

*Compatibility.*  The coface and codegeneracy maps respect the
crossed-module structure because the jet prolongation preserves the
Hopf-algebra multiplication
([jet prolongation](#prop\:jet-prolongation), Step 2) and the :refterm[descent]{#descent}
homomorphism is a $*$-homomorphism ([:refterm[descent]{#descent}](#lem\:descent), Step 4).
Hence the conjugation action of $g_{ij}^{(n)}$ on $a_{ij}^{(n)}$ is
compatible with the simplicial maps, making the total assignment a
cosimplicial crossed groupoid.  $\square$
