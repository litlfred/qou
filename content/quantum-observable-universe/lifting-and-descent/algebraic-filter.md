Let $\alpha$ be a harmonic $1$-form in the basis of [$q$-harmonic basis on branes](#rem:q-harmonic-basis-brane). Its *signature* is determined by its commutator with the curvature $F_A$ ([substrate parameter](#def:substrate-parameter)).

1. **Fermionic type (matter).** $[F_A, \alpha] \neq 0$. These forms are entangled with the curvature. They see the $\mathrm{SO}(3)$ holonomy and the $-6$ index fixed points, yielding geodesics trapped or spiralling around the three fundamental vortex filaments.

2. **Bosonic type (radiation).** $[F_A, \alpha] = 0$. These forms commute with the curvature. As longitudinal modes ([weight space decomposition](#rem:weight-space-decomposition)), they flow through the bulk undeflected by monodromy fixed points. Their geodesics are straight relative to the gauge-fluid flow.
