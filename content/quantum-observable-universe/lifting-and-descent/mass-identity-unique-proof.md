The mass formula

$$
  \mathfrak{m}(\varrho)
  \;=\;
  \bigl\lVert
    \mathrm{tr}\bigl( (\int_A \otimes \mathrm{id}) \circ \Delta \circ \varrho \bigr)
  \bigr\rVert
$$

is linear in $\int_A$ inside the norm: scaling
$\int_A \mapsto c \int_A$ produces
$\mathrm{tr}(\cdots) \mapsto c \cdot \mathrm{tr}(\cdots)$, and the
norm transforms by $|c|$.  All other inputs ($\Delta$, $\varrho$,
$\mathrm{tr}$) are uniquely determined by the QU/QOU axioms.  Hence
fixing the normalisation of $\int_A$ (as part of the QU datum) fixes
$\mathfrak{m}$ uniquely.
