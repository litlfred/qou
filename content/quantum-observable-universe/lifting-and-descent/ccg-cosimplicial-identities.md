**Definition.**
*The coface maps $\delta^i$ (composites of jet prolongation with Cech
face maps) and codegeneracy maps $\sigma^j$ (composites of :refterm[descent]{#descent} with
Cech degeneracy maps) satisfy the cosimplicial identities*

$$
  \delta^j \circ \delta^i = \delta^i \circ \delta^{j-1} \;(i < j),
  \quad
  \sigma^j \circ \sigma^i = \sigma^i \circ \sigma^{j+1} \;(i \leq j),
$$

*and the mixed identities, with each case reducing to disjoint-support
commutation or to the retraction
$\mathfrak{d}_{n+1} \circ \ell_n = \mathrm{id}$.*
