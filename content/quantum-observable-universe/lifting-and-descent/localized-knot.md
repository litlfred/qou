Let $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ be a quantum observable universe with
projective line object $\mathbb{P}(V) = G/T$
([Definition](#def:projective-line-object)).  A **localized knot** is a pair

$$
  (p, K)  \in  \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathbf{C}}(\mathbf{1}, A^{\mathrm{co}T})
   \times  \mathrm{Knot}
$$

where:

- $p \colon \mathbf{1} \to A^{\mathrm{co}T}$ is a categorical point of the
  projective line ([Definition](#def:categorical-point)), and
- $K$ is a knot type — an equivalence class of smooth embeddings
  $S^1 \hookrightarrow S^3$ under ambient isotopy.

We write $(p, K)$ to mean "the knot $K$ localized at the point $p$ on
the base manifold $\mathbb{P}(V)$."

Categorically, $(p, K)$ is a section of the *knot sheaf*
$\mathcal{K} \to \mathbb{P}(V)$: the functor that assigns to each open
$U \subseteq \mathbb{P}(V)$ the set of knot types embeddable in the fibre
$\mathcal{S}_p \cong \mathbb{CP}^1$ over any $p \in U$.  The fibre $\mathcal{S}_p = \pi^{-1}(p)$
carries the knot as a vortex line in the chiral sector decomposition
$(\mathfrak{r}, \mathfrak{g}, \mathfrak{b})$.

In the archimedean realisation with $G = \mathrm{SU}(2)$ and
$T \cong \mathrm{U}(1)$, the projective line is
$\mathbb{P}(V) \cong \mathbb{CP}^1 \cong S^2$, and a categorical point
$p$ is a point on the Riemann sphere.  The knot $K$ lives in the fibre
$\mathcal{S}_p \cong \mathbb{CP}^1$ — a topological $S^3$ neighborhood resolved by
the blowup.
