This chapter takes the quantum observable universe $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ and grows it: each level $A_n$ of a recursive tower is the jet prolongation of the previous one, and the whole edifice of lifting, descent, and mass decomposition rests on the tower being well-defined. This section sets up the tower.

We begin with **Jet Prolongation** (prop), which states that the jet object $J^1(A) = A \otimes \Omega^1(A)$ inherits a Frobenius--Hopf structure and a fibre functor so that $(\mathbf{C}, J^1(A), \tau', q)$ is again a QOU. The supporting **Categorical jet object $J^1(A)$ inherits the Frobenius--Hopf structure** (lemma) supplies the Hopf-side argument, and **Prolongation from the Frobenius--Hopf structure** (rem) records how the Kähler differential machinery feeds the prolongation. **Recursive Brane Tower** (def) iterates the construction to produce $\{A_n\}_{n \geq 0}$, with **Smooth model of the brane tower** (ex) giving the geometric model $J^1\mathcal{S}_n$, and **Arc space $\mathcal{L}(X)$ of the brane tower** (def) collecting the levels into a single ind-scheme. The next section, *Categorical Lifting*, uses the tower as the carrier on which torsion and transport are defined.
