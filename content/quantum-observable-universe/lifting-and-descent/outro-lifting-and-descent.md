This closes the categorical lifting-and-descent chapter.  The
brane tower has been built level by level, the descent
involution recovers the physical algebra in the classical limit,
and the cosimplicial crossed groupoid promotes the level-by-level
arithmetic to a higher-categorical structure.  The
topological-mass example at the close of the archimedean-lifting
section anchors the abstract machinery in $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ —
the categorical mass on the substrate side, the topological
$\mathrm{SU}(2)$ Casimir on the archimedean side.

The next chapter (braids and knots) consumes the brane tower as
the source of categorical braids: the level $A_n$ carries the
$n$-strand braid action that becomes the Hecke algebra at
$q \neq 1$, and the Yekutieli cocycle data introduced here
controls the global structure of the resulting knot category.
