Let $b_k(\mathcal{S}_n) = \dim H^k(\mathcal{S}_n, \mathbb{R})$ denote the $k$-th Betti number of $\mathcal{S}_n$. Since $\mathcal{S}_{n+1}$ is the bundle of orthonormal frames of $J^1 \mathcal{S}_n$, the Betti numbers of $\mathcal{S}_{n+1}$ are determined by those of $\mathcal{S}_n$ via the Leray--Serre spectral sequence of the fibration $\mathcal{S}_{n+1} \to \mathcal{S}_n$. The [quantum characteristic classes](#def:quantum-characteristic-classes) obstruct the collapse of this spectral sequence, controlling the growth of $b_k(\mathcal{S}_{n+1})$ relative to $b_k(\mathcal{S}_n)$.
