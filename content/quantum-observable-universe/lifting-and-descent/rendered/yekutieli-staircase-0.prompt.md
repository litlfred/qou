# Yekutieli obstruction along the brane tower

F3.2 (P1). A staircase rising left-to-right with five steps for $n = 0, 1, 2, 3, 4$. Each step represents the brane-tower level $A_n$. At each step (above the platform) a small red dot is the Yekutieli obstruction class $\kappa_n$. The final step is marked in green with $\kappa_4 = 0$ and labelled "tower stabilises (every higher $\kappa$ vanishes)" — the geometric meaning of the Descartes condition $\chi(A) = -6$. Beneath each step a small blue tick of growing height represents the depth of the Kummer filtration $K^\bullet$ at that level. Layout: 5-step staircase with annotations above and below. Density: minimal — 5 steps, 5 obstruction markers, 5 filtration ticks, 1 stabilisation annotation. Style: schematic.
