its conjecture extension. This sub-section unfolds the family of
variants — eigenspace-decomposition, holonomy, transient-
localisation, and dilogarithm — each with its own proof, plus the
SU(2) instance that anchors the family to a concrete category, and
the chapter's closing outro that gestures forward to the
braids-and-knots chapter (the categorical-side companion to this
