The preceding sections developed the lifting, descent, and mass machinery over a generic ring $R$. This section specialises everything to the case $R = \mathbb{R}$, where the brane tower becomes a tower of smooth jet bundles $J^1 \mathcal{S}_n$ and the categorical mass identity becomes a multiplicative integral. We collect here the corollaries and lemmas that ground the abstract framework in differential geometry.

The jet machinery descends first. **Smooth model of jet prolongation** (cor) and its proof identify $J^1\mathcal{S}_n$ as the geometric carrier of $A_n$. **Band of the gerbe extension** (rem) fixes the band $\mathcal{B} = \mathcal{O}_M^{\times}$, and the smooth **Proof of QU + observability + lifting $\Rightarrow$ QOU** (proof) instantiates the main theorem for $\mathrm{SU}_q(2)$ frame bundles. **Nonabelian multiplicative integration** (cor) interprets the categorical mass identity as a nonabelian multiplicative integral, and **Mass in the brane tower** (rem) reads the result physically as a non-commutativity invariant of the vortex on $\mathcal{S}$.

Two structural lemmas anchor the categorical-mass programme. **Smooth proof of Euler characteristic stability** (proof) verifies the smooth case of the $\chi(A_n)$-stability proposition, and **Structure of the defining boundary** (proof) gives the codimension and chirality data of the brane boundary. **Quantum Dilogarithm as Localized Torsion Volume** (cor) is the headline analytic identity: the quantum dilogarithm appears as the torsion volume of the localised Reeb flow.

The remainder is the proof package for the categorical mass conjecture. **Conjectural decomposition of mass by descent eigenspaces** (conj) splits the mass integrand by descent involution; **Eigenspace decomposition of the mass integrand** (lemma), **Descended term is the unknot-core holonomy** (lemma), **Transient terms localise to exceptional divisors** (lemma), and **Quantum dilogarithm evaluation of exceptional-divisor contributions** (lemma) supply the four steps, assembled by **Mass-decomposition proof** (proof). **Mass decomposition for $\mathrm{SU}(2)$** (ex) records the worked smooth case. Downstream chapters take these specialisations as the input to numerical predictions.
