With the brane tower in hand, we can ask what structure passes from $A_{n+1}$ back down to $A_n$. **Descent preserves the quantum observable universe** (lemma) shows that the restriction $r_n \colon A_n \to A_{n-1}$ induces a surjective algebra homomorphism $\mathfrak{d}_n$ on endomorphism algebras that intertwines the $G_q$-actions, so each level inherits the full QOU structure. **Descent involution** (def) then composes ascent and descent into the idempotent $\hat{\tau}_n = \ell_n \circ \mathfrak{d}_{n+1}$, whose image cuts out the descended subalgebra inside $\mathrm{End}_{\mathbf{C}}(A_{n+1})$. The $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ examples pin both constructions to the archimedean model. The Yekutieli-lifting section that follows takes the descent involution and the surjectivity of $\mathfrak{d}_n$ as inputs to the obstruction calculus.
