The two commutator classes of the
[fermion--boson filter](#prop:algebraic-filter-classification) carry a
geometric reading in the gauge-fluid flow.

1. **Fermionic (matter).** When $[F_A, \alpha] \neq 0$, the form
   $\alpha$ is entangled with the curvature, sees the $\mathrm{SO}(3)$
   holonomy and the $-6$ index fixed points, and its geodesics are
   trapped or spiralling around the fundamental vortex filaments.

2. **Bosonic (radiation).** When $[F_A, \alpha] = 0$, the form
   $\alpha$ commutes with the curvature, corresponds to longitudinal
   modes that propagate through the bulk without deflection by the
   monodromy fixed points, and its geodesics are straight relative to
   the gauge-fluid flow.
