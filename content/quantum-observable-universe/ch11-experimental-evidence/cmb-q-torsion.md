In the Descartes universe ([Example](#ex:descartes)), the negative Euler
characteristic $\chi(E) = -6$ forces a non-trivial distribution of angular
modes across the CR manifold ([Remark](#rem:descartes-cmb)). The
$q$-torsion leaves ([Definition](#def:q-torsion-leaf); here "leaves" is the
geometric plural of *leaf*) imprint a
characteristic pattern on the CMB that deviates from the statistically
isotropic Gaussian field predicted by $\Lambda$CDM. Specifically, the
$q$-deformed Reeb flow ([Definition](#def:q-reeb-flow)) introduces
*torsion* into the temperature and polarisation multipoles, producing
preferred spiral directions in the $B$-mode spectrum.
