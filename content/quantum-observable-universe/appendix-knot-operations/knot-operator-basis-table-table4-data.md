This table records, for each basis operator $T$, whether it creates an electron $e^-$ and whether the $\gamma$-conjugate $\gamma \cdot T \cdot \gamma$ retains that $e^-$. The braid words are written in the generators $\sigma_0, \sigma_1$, with $h$ the coefficient and $\gamma$ the identity operator of the Hecke relation $\sigma_0^2 = h\sigma_0 + \gamma$; the "Pairs with $e^- \neq 0$" column counts, out of $36$, the pairings for which $e^-$ is non-zero, so $36/36$ marks an operator that always produces an electron.

| Operator | Braid word | Pairs with $e^- \neq 0$ | $\gamma \cdot T \cdot \gamma$ has $e^-$? |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| $T_{1,0}$ | $\mathbf{1}$ | 19/36 | **No** |
| $T_{2,1}$ | $\sigma_0$ | 24/36 | **No** |
| $T_{2,2}$ | $\sigma_0^2 = h\sigma_0 + \gamma$ | 24/36 | **No** |
| $T_{2,3}$ | $\sigma_0\sigma_1\sigma_0 = e^-$ | **36/36** | **Yes** ($= 1$) |
| $T_{2,5}$ | $(\sigma_0\sigma_1)^2\sigma_0$ | **36/36** | **Yes** ($= h^2$) |
