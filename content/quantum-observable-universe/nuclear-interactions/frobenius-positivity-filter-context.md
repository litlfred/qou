For nuclei with even mass number $A$, the raw character sum is negative. The physical Q-value is obtained by restricting the sum to Frobenius-positive configurations.

## Connection to the confinement LP

The Frobenius positivity constraint provides the continuous analogue of the LP feasibility constraint $Gx \geq 0$. It acts as a configuration filter.

## Computational complexity

For the ${}^6\mathrm{He} \to {}^6\mathrm{Li}$ decay ($A = 6$), the 32 patterns with odd parity are excluded. The Frobenius-filtered sum yields $Q \approx 3.637$ MeV (experiment: $3.508$ MeV, error $+3.68\%$).
For large even $A$, the configurations require either enumeration or a closed-form simplification.
