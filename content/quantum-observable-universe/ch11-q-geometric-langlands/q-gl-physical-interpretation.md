The $q$-Geometric Langlands correspondence admits a physical
interpretation within the QOU framework:

1. **Automorphic side = gauge theory.** The category
   $\mathcal{D}_q\text{-}\mathrm{mod}(\mathrm{Bun}_{G_q})$
   encodes the $q$-deformed gauge theory on the brane: $G_q$-bundles
   are the field configurations, $q$-$\mathcal{D}$-modules are the
   quantum states, and $q$-Hecke operators implement modifications
   of the gauge field at a point (particle insertions).

2. **Spectral side = matter content.** The category
   $\mathrm{QCoh}(\mathrm{LocSys}_{\check{G}_q})$ encodes the
   $q$-deformed matter sector: $\check{G}_q$-local systems are
   parallel transport data for matter fields, and the Langlands dual
   $\check{G}_q$ controls the allowed representations (particle
   species).

3. **The equivalence $\mathbb{L}_q$ = gauge/matter duality.** The
   $q$-Geometric Langlands correspondence is the categorical
   statement that the gauge sector and matter sector carry equivalent
   information.  In the QOU, this is a consequence of the single
   substrate parameter $q$ controlling both the braiding (gauge) and
   the differential structure (matter).

4. **Hecke eigensheaves = particles.** A $q$-Hecke eigensheaf is a
   quantum state that responds to gauge field modifications by
   multiplication by a $q$-local system.  This is the categorical
   version of a particle carrying a definite charge under
   $\check{G}_q$.
