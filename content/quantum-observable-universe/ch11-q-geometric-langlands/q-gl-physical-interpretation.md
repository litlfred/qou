The $q$-Geometric Langlands correspondence admits a physical
reading within the QOU framework.

**The automorphic side as gauge theory.**
The category $\mathcal{D}_q\text{-}\mathrm{mod}(\mathrm{Bun}_{G_q})$
encodes $q$-deformed gauge theory on the brane: $G_q$-bundles are
field configurations, $q$-$\mathcal{D}$-modules are quantum states,
and $q$-Hecke operators implement modifications of the gauge field
at a point (particle insertions).  The $q$-curvature
$\mathcal{F}_q$ is the field strength, forced to be non-zero by
the $d_q^2 \neq 0$ obstruction.

**The spectral side as matter content.**
The category
$\mathrm{QCoh}(\mathrm{LocSys}_{\check{G}_q})$ encodes the
$q$-deformed matter sector: $\check{G}_q$-local systems carry
parallel transport data for matter fields, and the Langlands dual
$\check{G}_q$ controls the allowed representations.  The Kummer
filtration on $\mathrm{LocSys}_{\check{G}_q}$ stratifies the
matter sector by the divisibility of the Yekutieli obstruction.

**The equivalence as gauge/matter duality.**
The functor $\mathbb{L}_q$ is the categorical statement that
gauge and matter sectors carry equivalent information.  In the
QOU this follows from the single substrate parameter $q$
controlling both the braiding (gauge structure) and the
differential calculus (matter structure).

**Hecke eigensheaves as charged states.**
A $q$-Hecke eigensheaf is a quantum state that responds to gauge
field modifications by multiplication by a $q$-local system —
the categorical version of a particle carrying definite charge
under $\check{G}_q$.
