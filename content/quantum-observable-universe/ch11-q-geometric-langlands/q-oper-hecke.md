Every $q$-oper $(\mathcal{P}_{\check{G}_q}, \nabla_q) \in
\mathrm{Op}_{\check{G}_q}(X)$ determines a $q$-Hecke eigensheaf
$\mathcal{M}_{\nabla_q}$ on $\mathrm{Bun}_{G_q}(X)$ via the
$q$-Geometric Langlands equivalence:

$$
  \mathcal{M}_{\nabla_q} = \mathbb{L}_q^{-1}(\delta_{\nabla_q}),
$$

where $\delta_{\nabla_q}$ is the skyscraper sheaf at the point
$\nabla_q \in \mathrm{LocSys}_{\check{G}_q}(X)$.  The eigenvalue
$q$-local system is the $q$-oper connection itself:

$$
  \mathbf{T}_{x,\lambda}^{(q)}(\mathcal{M}_{\nabla_q})
  \;\simeq\;
  \mathcal{M}_{\nabla_q} \otimes
  V_\lambda^{(q)}(\nabla_q)_x.
$$

Moreover, the $q$-oper condition (transversality to the Borel)
ensures that the Hecke eigensheaf $\mathcal{M}_{\nabla_q}$ is
*irreducible* as a $q$-$\mathcal{D}$-module: the transversality
prevents $\mathcal{M}_{\nabla_q}$ from admitting non-trivial
sub-$q$-$\mathcal{D}$-modules.
