Let $(\mathbf{C}, \theta, G, \mathcal{S})$ be a QOU with fibre functor
$\tau$ and substrate parameter $q$.  If $\mathcal{M}$ is a $q$-Hecke
eigensheaf on $\mathrm{Bun}_{G_q}(X)$, then its eigenvalue
$\mathcal{L}_q \in \mathrm{LocSys}_{\check{G}_q}(X)$ is uniquely
determined by the action of $\tau$ on the Hecke eigenvalue data:

$$
  \mathcal{L}_q \;\simeq\;
  \mathrm{Aut}^\otimes(\tau)\text{-}\mathrm{equivariantisation of }
  \big\{ V_\lambda^{(q)}(\mathcal{L}_q)_x \big\}_{\lambda, x}.
$$

In particular, the rigidity of the fibre functor
(Proposition \ref{prop:fibre-functor-rigidity}) implies that the
$q$-local system $\mathcal{L}_q$ is determined up to unique
isomorphism by the collection of Hecke eigenvalues.
