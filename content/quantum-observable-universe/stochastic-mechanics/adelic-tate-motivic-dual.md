**Motivic dual.** The adelic decomposition of the global zeta integral has a geometric dual via the [arc space $\mathcal{L}(X)$](#def:arc-space) and the Kontsevich–Denef–Loeser motivic measure \cite{kontsevich1995, denef-loeser2001}. Tate factorises the global zeta as a restricted product of local $\mathbb{Q}_v$-integrals. Motivic integration on $\mathcal{L}(X)$ factorises a class in the Grothendieck ring of varieties as a sum over jet-scheme cylinders. For the QOU brane tower, the two factorisations agree under the change-of-variables formula applied to the Reeb flow. The leading-coefficient form of [BSD surreal bound](#rem:bsd-surreal-bound) assembles the prefactor $c$ from local $\varepsilon_p$-adic integrals. This is the motivic-measure interpretation of the same data on $\mathcal{L}(\mathcal{E}_q)$.
