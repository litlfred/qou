This chapter develops the stochastic-mechanics interpretation of the
QOU framework. The Markov trace $\mathrm{tr}_M$ — which packages
braid-word combinatorics into a class function and produces the
Jones polynomial in the Hecke quotient — also admits an interpretation
as a transition kernel of a stochastic dynamics on the categorical
state space.

In the classical limit $q \to 1$, this stochastic dynamics recovers
Brownian motion on the brane; at $q = q_0$ it produces the
Nelson-style stochastic mechanics that underlies the wave-particle
duality and the Madelung representation of the brane fluid.
