This chapter develops the stochastic-mechanics interpretation of the QOU framework. We package braid-word combinatorics into a class function using the Markov trace $\mathrm{tr}_M$. This yields the Jones polynomial in the Hecke quotient and provides a transition kernel for stochastic dynamics on the categorical state space.

In the classical limit $q \to 1$, these stochastic dynamics recover Brownian motion on the brane. At $q = q_0$, the dynamics produce Nelson-style stochastic mechanics. This structure supports wave-particle duality and the Madelung representation of the brane fluid.
