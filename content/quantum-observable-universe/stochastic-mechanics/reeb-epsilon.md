Let $(\mathbf{C}, A)$ be a quantum observable universe with categorical Reeb derivation $\xi \in \mathrm{Der}(A)$ ([categorical Reeb derivation](#def:categorical-reeb-derivation)). We denote the one-parameter Reeb flow generated by $\xi$ as $\Phi_t : A \to A$. This is the unique flow satisfying $\Phi_0 = \mathrm{id}_A$ and $d\Phi_t / dt = \xi \circ \Phi_t$.

The **Reeb infinitesimal** is the distinguished element

$$
\varepsilon := \left. \frac{d}{dt} \right|_{t = 0} \Phi_t
\;\in\; \mathrm{Der}(A)
$$

of the derivation algebra of the substrate object $A$.

**Primitivity.** The substrate parameter $q$ is a scalar attached to the braiding $R$-matrix. In contrast, $\varepsilon$ is intrinsic to the categorical structure of $A$. It is the tangent vector at the identity of the Reeb flow. We define $\varepsilon$ before choosing any deformation scale, independent of $q$. We recover the usual substrate parameter in the following section ([derivation of $q$ from $\varepsilon$](#def:q-from-epsilon)).

**Positivity and archimedean independence.** The contact-form normalization $\xi \circ \varTheta = \varTheta$ of the Reeb derivation ([categorical Reeb derivation](#def:categorical-reeb-derivation)) forces $\varepsilon$ to be a strictly positive element of $\mathrm{Der}(A)$ under the natural ordering induced by the Frobenius pairing. For every non-zero state $v \in \mathcal{S}_p$ in the state bundle, $\langle v,\, \varepsilon \cdot v \rangle > 0$. Furthermore, $\varepsilon$ is archimedean-smaller than every standard-real derivation $\lambda \cdot \mathrm{id}_A$ for $\lambda > 0$. The iterates $n \cdot \varepsilon$ for all $n \in \mathbb{N}$ remain dominated by $\lambda \cdot \mathrm{id}_A$ in the operator order on $\mathrm{End}(A)$.

These two properties designate $\varepsilon$ as a positive infinitesimal direction in the derivation algebra. We embed this infinitesimal into Conway's surreal field $\mathbf{No}$ and identify $\varepsilon \leftrightarrow 1 / \omega$ in the surreal-number appendix ([calculus up to $\varepsilon$](#rem:calculus-up-to-epsilon), [surreal blow-up calculus](#def:surreal-blowup-calculus)). The main text uses $\varepsilon$ strictly as a categorical primitive.

**Summary.**

| Object | Type | Categorical status |
|--------|------|--------------------|
| $\xi$ | derivation $A \to A$ | existing Reeb derivation |
| $\Phi_t$ | 1-parameter flow | generated by $\xi$ |
| $\varepsilon$ | tangent of $\Phi_t$ at $t = 0$ | **primitive** — intrinsic to $A$ |
| $q$ | substrate scalar | **derived** from $\varepsilon$ (next block) |
