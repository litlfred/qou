**Proposition (restricted-product structure of the Reeb $\omega$-order).**
*Let $\mathbb{A} = \mathbb{R} \times \prod_p' \mathbb{Q}_p$ be the rational adele ring. For each place $v$ of $\mathbb{Q}$, let $\varepsilon_v \in \mathrm{Der}(A_v)$ be the local Reeb infinitesimal on the local completion $A_v$. Suppose every local $\omega$-order $\ordw_v$ takes values in a common linearly ordered value group $\Gamma$. Assume the restricted-product condition: for any adelic observable $f = (f_v)_v$, the set of places $v$ with $\ordw_v(f_v) \neq 0$ is finite. Then the global $\omega$-order is the finite sum of the local orders,*

$$
  \ordw_{\mathbb{A}}(f) \;=\; \sum_{v} \ordw_v(f_v)
  \qquad (\text{finite sum}),
$$

*and this assignment is:*

1. **Additive over places:** $\ordw_{\mathbb{A}}(fg) = \ordw_{\mathbb{A}}(f) + \ordw_{\mathbb{A}}(g)$.
2. **Squeeze-coherent:** If $\ordw_v(g_v) \le \ordw_v(f_v)$ at every place $v$, and the inequality is strict at some place $v_0$, then $\ordw_{\mathbb{A}}(g) < \ordw_{\mathbb{A}}(f)$.
