Let $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ be a quantum universe over a Noetherian ring $R$. It carries a substrate parameter $q$ and a classical limit functor $\mathcal{L} : \mathbf{C}_q \to \mathbf{C}_1$ ([classical limit functor](#def:classical-limit-functor)).

The **classical limit obstruction** of the quantum universe is the Hochschild cohomology group

$$
\mathrm{Obs}(\mathbf{C}, q \to 1)
 := 
HH^2(\mathcal{U}_q(\mathfrak{g}), \mathrm{Ad})
$$

where $\mathcal{U}_q(\mathfrak{g})$ denotes the Lusztig integral form of the quantized enveloping algebra associated to $A$. The module $\mathrm{Ad}$ denotes the adjoint bimodule. Elements of $\mathrm{Obs}$ obstruct the extension of second-order deformations of the Frobenius--Hopf algebra $A_q$ across $q = 1$.
