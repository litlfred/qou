**BSD reduction.** The leading-coefficient form of BSD ([BSD surreal bound](#rem:bsd-surreal-bound)) requires identifying the standard-real prefactor $c$ in $L(\mathcal{E}_q, s) \asymp c \cdot (s - 1)^r$ with the product of the Tate–Shafarevich order, the regulator, and real or complex periods. The adelic Fourier transform gives the explicit formula for $c$ in terms of local $\varepsilon_p$-adic integrals. This reduces BSD to a local-squeeze check at each prime, combined with the archimedean regulator identification.
