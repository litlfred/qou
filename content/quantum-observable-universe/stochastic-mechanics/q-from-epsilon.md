With the Reeb infinitesimal $\varepsilon \in \mathrm{Der}(A)$ established ([Reeb infinitesimal](#def:reeb-epsilon)), the substrate parameter $q$ becomes a derived quantity rather than a primitive.

**Definition.** The substrate parameter associated to $\varepsilon$ is

$$
q := 1 + \varepsilon,
$$

interpreted inside the ordered field generated by $\varepsilon$ over $\mathbb{R}$. Equivalently, via the surreal appendix ([calculus up to $\varepsilon$](#rem:calculus-up-to-epsilon)), this is the element $q = 1 + \varepsilon \in \mathbf{No}$ under the identification $\varepsilon = 1 / \omega$ from [surreal blow-up calculus](#def:surreal-blowup-calculus). This block fixes only the main-text convention. The analytic content of the identification resides in the appendix.
