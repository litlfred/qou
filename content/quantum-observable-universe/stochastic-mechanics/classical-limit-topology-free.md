**Topology-free reformulation.** The [classical limit functor](#def:classical-limit-functor) uses a *topological* $q \to 1$ limit on the parameter space. This limit encounters obstructions ([classical limit obstruction](#def:classical-limit-obstruction)) and fails for surreal-valued $q$. We can replace it with a literal evaluation in Conway's surreal field at $q = 1 + \varepsilon$, followed by the standard-part map:

* At the **single-functor** level, $\mathcal{L} \cong \mathrm{St} \circ \mathrm{ev}_{1+\varepsilon}$ ([classical limit as standard-part](#conj:classical-limit-stpart)).
* At the **whole-tuple** level, the QOU $(\mathbf{C}, \varTheta, G, \mathcal{S})$ operates as the standard-part of an $\varepsilon$-relaxed surreal QOU ([archimedean QOU is standard-part of surreal](#conj:archimedean-stpart), with [proof strategy](#rem:archimedean-stpart-strategy)).

Both replacements are conjectural. If either holds, it eliminates the need for analytic or topological machinery on the parameter space.
