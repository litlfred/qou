Existence is immediate: $\mu$ and $\int_A$ are part of the quantum-
universe data, so the composite $\int_A \circ \mu$ is a morphism in
$\mathbf{C}$ by composition.  Non-degeneracy — that the adjoint map
$A \to A^\vee$ is an isomorphism — is equivalent to the snake
identities ([snake identity](#def:snake-identity)), which are part
of the rigid monoidal structure applied to the Frobenius object $A$.
Both components are part of the QU axioms.
