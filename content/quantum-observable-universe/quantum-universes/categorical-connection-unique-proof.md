The difference $\omega = \nabla_2 - \nabla_1$ satisfies
$\omega(ab) = \omega(a) b + a \omega(b) - (\nabla_2 - \nabla_1)$ (a
simple expansion), which shows $\omega$ is $A$-linear — i.e.
$\omega \in \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathbf{C}}(A, \Omega^1(A))^{\text{linear}}
  \cong \Omega^1(A) \otimes \mathrm{Ad}(A)$.
Connections therefore form an affine space over this module;
uniqueness is up to gauge translation within this affine structure.
