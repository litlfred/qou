Witness: the trivial connection $\nabla_0 = d$, where $d$ is the
de Rham differential of the Kähler module $\Omega^1(A)$.  The
Leibniz rule $d(ab) = (da)b + a(db)$ is part of the differential-
calculus axiom of the QU.  Non-trivial connections are obtained by
adding $\mathrm{Ad}(A)$-valued $1$-forms:
$\nabla = \nabla_0 + \omega$ with
$\omega \in \Omega^1(A) \otimes \mathrm{Ad}(A)$.  Such $\omega$
exist as elements of a free module.
