Witness: $\mathbf{C} = R\text{-}\mathrm{Mod}$ (finite-dimensional
$R$-modules) with $A = R$ as the trivial Frobenius--Hopf algebra,
$\tau = \mathrm{id}$, and $\Omega^1(R) = 0$.  All axioms are satisfied
trivially.  A richer witness is
$\mathbf{C} = \mathbf{Rep}(\mathrm{SU}_q(2))$
([Example](#ex:rigid-monoidal-vect)) with $A$ the regular
representation.  Both establish existence.
