# Rigidity: the zig-zag snake identity

F1.1 (P0). Left: a Penrose / string-diagram zig-zag composite. Top entry strand labelled $A$ enters; a cap labelled $\mathrm{coev}_A$ creates an $A \otimes A^\vee$ pair; three middle strands run vertically; a cup labelled $\mathrm{ev}_A$ closes the first two strands together; the third strand exits at the bottom labelled $A$. Caption: "snake on $A$ = $\mathrm{id}_A$, $(A \otimes \mathrm{coev}_A) \circ (\mathrm{ev}_A \otimes A) = \mathrm{id}_A$". Right: a single vertical strand labelled $A$ at top and bottom — the identity. A large $=$ between the two panels makes the rigidity equation concrete: the snake collapses to the identity. This is the standard graphical-calculus picture every category-theory text shows once; the paper invokes it dozens of times. Layout: 2 panels, snake left and identity strand right, with $=$ between. Density: minimal — labelled cup/cap, labelled strands. Style: string-diagram (cups as $\cup$, caps as $\cap$, identity strands as vertical lines). Colour: monochrome.
