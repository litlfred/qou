The defining identity
$\alpha \wedge \star \beta = \int_A(\alpha^* \cdot \beta) \mathrm{vol}$
must hold for *every* $\alpha \in \Omega^k(A)$.  By non-degeneracy of
the wedge pairing
$\Omega^k \otimes \Omega^{n-k} \to \Omega^n \xrightarrow{\int_A} \mathbf{1}$,
$\star \beta$ is uniquely determined as the element of $\Omega^{n-k}(A)$
representing the linear functional
$\alpha \mapsto \int_A(\alpha^* \cdot \beta) \mathrm{vol}$.  Hence
any two candidates for $\star$ agree on every $\beta$, so
$\star_1 = \star_2$.
