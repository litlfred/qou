With the QU axioms fixed and the Frobenius pairing in hand, we read off what they force. **Frobenius relation** (prop) shows that the comultiplication $\Delta_\mu$ dual to $\mu$ under the Frobenius pairing satisfies $(\mu \otimes \mathrm{id}) \circ (\mathrm{id} \otimes \Delta_\mu) = \Delta_\mu \circ \mu$, with **The Frobenius relation in $\mathrm{SU}(2)$** (ex) verifying the identity on $\mathcal{O}(\mathrm{SU}(2))$ via the group-multiplication rule for $\Delta$. **Involutive structure ($\ast$-structure)** (def) introduces the contravariant endofunctor $\dagger$ built from the antipode and Frobenius form, and **Frobenius dagger is positive-definite** (prop) shows that $f^{\dagger} \circ f$ is a positive element of $\mathrm{End}(\mathbf{1})$ for every non-zero $f$ — the categorical analogue of the $C^*$-axiom. **Positive-definite dagger in $\mathrm{SU}(2)$** (ex) recovers the conjugate-transpose adjoint in $\mathbf{Rep}(\mathrm{SU}(2))$ as a special case.

The section closes with the wider context. **Notation: $\dagger$, $\ast$, and the physicist's adjoint** (rem) disambiguates the Frobenius dagger from complex conjugation and the physicist's Hilbert-space adjoint, reserving each symbol for a distinct structural layer. **Tannaka--Krein duality: what the fibre functor gives us** (rem) records the four properties — reconstruction, equivalence, rigidity, constraint propagation — by which the fibre functor $\tau$ determines an affine group scheme $G = \mathrm{Aut}^{\otimes}(\tau)$ and identifies $\mathbf{C}$ with $\mathrm{Rep}(G)$, and locates the deformation parameter $q$ as the eigenvalue of $c_{V,V}$ on $S^2 V$. **Independence of the QU axioms** (rem) tracks which of the four QU axioms follow from the others over various base rings — symmetric versus braided, field versus ring — and where $q \neq 1$ forces each axiom to be assumed independently.
