Why is a rigid :refterm[monoidal category]{#monoidal-category} the starting point for quantum-geometric structure? Two lines of reasoning converge.

**Information-theoretic necessity.** Picture two subsystems side by side. The tensor product $\otimes$ joining them is the grammar of any physical system possessing inputs and outputs. A compositional language forces a :refterm[monoidal category]{#monoidal-category}. Rigidity is the further demand that processes be reversible at the level of types: a system of type $V$ bends around to become an input of type $V^{\vee}$, which is the content of the snake identities. That demand is separable from the first, and the paper keeps the two apart — a quantum universe is braided, a quantum observable universe is braided and rigid.

**Physical reconstruction from superselection.** Take an electric charge and its conjugate anticharge. Algebraic quantum field theory arrives at the same structure through superselection sectors. The category of superselection sectors is automatically rigid braided monoidal. The tensor product encodes charge composition. The braiding encodes exchange statistics. Rigidity encodes anticharges. Locality and covariance force the monoidal structure.
