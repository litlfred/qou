Given a fixed Frobenius form $\int_A$ and volume form
$\mathrm{vol}$, the
[categorical Hodge star](#def:categorical-hodge-star) is uniquely
determined: any other involution $\star'$ satisfying
$\alpha \wedge \star' \beta = \int_A(\alpha^* \cdot \beta) \mathrm{vol}$
for all $\alpha$ coincides with $\star$.
