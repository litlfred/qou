Each non-degenerate $\int_A^{(i)} \colon A \to \mathbf{1}$ factors
uniquely through the isomorphism $A \xrightarrow{\sim} A^\vee$ as a
morphism $A^\vee \to \mathbf{1}$.  The space
$\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathbf{C}}(A^\vee, \mathbf{1}) \cong
\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathbf{C}}(\mathbf{1}, A) \otimes
\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathbf{C}}(\mathbf{1}, A^\vee)$ is one-dimensional when
$A$ is simple ([simple objects V](#def:simple-objects-V)).  Hence any
two Frobenius forms differ by a unit scalar $c$, and the composite
$\int_A^{(2)} \circ \mu = c \cdot \int_A^{(1)} \circ \mu$ inherits
the same scaling.
