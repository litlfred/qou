The table below records which pieces of the Hecke-theoretic apparatus survive the passage from the characteristic-$0$ surreals $\mathbf{No}$ to the characteristic-$p$ substrate $\mathbf{On}_p$, and which break. The algebraic core carries over intact — the Hecke / quantum-group structure, its Frobenius–Hopf data, and the Markov trace all persist in modular form. Two failures drive the rest: at $q = \zeta_p$ Wedderburn semisimplicity **fails** (one gets cells rather than blocks), and there is **no** standard-part map back to $\mathbb{R}$, so the archimedean reduction available in characteristic $0$ is absent. The remaining rows track the technical adjustments those two failures force.

| Structure | Char 0 ($\mathbf{No}$) | Char $p$ ($\mathbf{On}_p$) |
|---|---|---|
| Algebraic Hecke / quantum group | survives | survives (Lusztig restricted form) |
| Frobenius / Hopf | survives | survives + Frobenius twist $a^p = a$ for nilpotents |
| Markov trace / Jones polynomial | survives | survives (modular Jones) |
| Wedderburn semisimplicity | survives | **fails** at $q = \zeta_p$ (cells, not blocks) |
| Tannaka reconstruction | survives | survives in modular Tannakian form |
| Standard part to $\mathbb{R}$ | survives | **none** — no archimedean reduction |
| Brauer characters | not needed | needed (replace ordinary characters) |
| Birthday grading | ordinal-valued | $p$-adic-ordinal-valued |
