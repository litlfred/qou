Irreducibility is what is at stake here: in the archimedean regime an
instanton cannot reduce to an abelian $\mathrm{U}(1)$ sub-bundle, so its
gauge orbit stays free. With that as the point,
[categorical irreducible](#lem:categorical-irreducible) specialises as follows:
every instanton connection $A$ on $P$ is irreducible.
The vanishing $H^0(M, \mathrm{Ad}(P)) = 0$
([$\mathbb{C}$-quantum observable universe](#ex:c-quantum-observable-universe)) is the archimedean
instance of $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathbf{C}}(\mathbf{1}, \mathrm{Ad}(A)) = 0$.  Concretely: a non-zero section
$s \in H^0(M, \mathrm{Ad}(P))$ would furnish an $\mathrm{Ad}$-invariant
direction in $\mathfrak{su}(2)$ at every point, reducing the connection
to $\mathrm{U}(1)$.  The QOU vanishing axiom forbids this, forcing the
gauge orbit of $A$ to be free.
