An instanton in the archimedean regime cannot reduce to an abelian $\mathrm{U}(1)$ sub-bundle. Its gauge orbit must remain free. This geometric fact specialises the categorical irreducibility axiom: every instanton connection $A$ on $P$ is irreducible. 

The vanishing cohomology $H^0(M, \mathrm{Ad}(P)) = 0$ provides the archimedean instance of $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathbf{C}}(\mathbf{1}, \mathrm{Ad}(A)) = 0$. Concretely, a non-zero section $s \in H^0(M, \mathrm{Ad}(P))$ furnishes an $\mathrm{Ad}$-invariant direction in $\mathfrak{su}(2)$ at every point. This reduces the connection to $\mathrm{U}(1)$. The QOU vanishing axiom forbids this, forcing the gauge orbit of $A$ to be free.
