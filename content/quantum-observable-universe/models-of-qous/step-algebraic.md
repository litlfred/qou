**Algebraic step.** We pass from braids to a trace-carrying quotient to turn the categorical action into a computable knot invariant. The braid group $B_n$ acts on $A^{\otimes n}$. Its quotient by the Hecke relation $(\sigma_i - q)(\sigma_i + q^{-1}) = 0$ is the Iwahori--Hecke algebra $H_n(q)$. This algebra controls the combinatorics of the categorical action. The Markov trace $\mathrm{tr}_M$ packages braid words into a class function. The evaluation on $\beta_K$ yields the Jones polynomial $J_K(q) = \mathrm{tr}_M(\rho(\beta_K))$. The Braids, Knots, and Hecke chapter develops braids, knot groups, the Hecke algebra, and the Skein-relation translation. The Stochastic Mechanics chapter (Part IV) presents the stochastic interpretation of $\mathrm{tr}_M$ as a transition kernel.
