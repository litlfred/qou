**Algebraic step.** The braid group $B_n$ acts on $A^{\otimes n}$. Its
quotient by the Hecke relation
$(\sigma_i - q)(\sigma_i + q^{-1}) = 0$ is the Iwahori--Hecke algebra
$H_n(q)$, which controls the combinatorics of the categorical action.
The Markov trace $\mathrm{tr}_M$ packages braid words into a class
function whose evaluation on $\beta_K$ is the Jones polynomial
$J_K(q) = \mathrm{tr}_M(\rho(\beta_K))$. Chapter 5 develops braids,
knot groups, the Hecke algebra, and the Skein-relation translation.
The stochastic interpretation of $\mathrm{tr}_M$ as a transition kernel
is deferred to Chapter 18 (Stochastic mechanics).
