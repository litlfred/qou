The observable gap and the categorical bound scale differently near the symmetric point $q = 1$. Expressing the gap in physical units divides the cubically-vanishing categorical bound by $\hbar_q^{\,2}$. This change in vanishing order — from cubic to linear — is the central feature.

**Proposition.** *Let $q \in \mathbb{R}$ with $q \neq 0, 1$, let
$\mathfrak{E}_\times(q) = \tfrac{1}{2}(q - q^{-1})$ be the
[crossing energy](#def:crossing-energy), and let
$\hbar_q = (q-1)/q$.  Then the crossing-energy gap bound
$\Delta_q = \mathfrak{E}_\times(q)\,(q-1)^2$ satisfies the exact
identity*

$$
  \frac{\Delta_q}{\hbar_q^{\,2}}
  \;=\;
  \frac{q\,(q-1)(q+1)}{2},
$$

*so the gap in physical units vanishes **linearly** in
$h = q - 1$ as $q \to 1$, while the categorical bound $\Delta_q$
itself vanishes cubically.  Both vanish identically at $q = 1$.*

**Archimedean specialisation (§7c).** We sit on the archimedean side of the substrate wall here. Our sole archimedean commitment is treating $q$ as a real physical scale near $q = 1$. Any generic categorical or $q$-deformation identities used remain valid over an abstract coefficient ring; we only specialise their archimedean realisation.
