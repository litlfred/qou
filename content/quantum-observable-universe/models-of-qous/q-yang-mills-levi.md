Let $(\mathbf{C}, \varTheta, G, \mathcal{S})$ be an archimedean quantum observable universe with instanton connection $A$ and substrate parameter $q$ ([substrate parameter](#def:substrate-parameter)). We couple the instanton energy to the substrate by weighting the self-dual curvature with the local Levi-form magnitude $q(p)$. This ensures that regions where $F_A^+$ overlaps the Levi form $L_\varTheta$ contribute more strongly. The **$q$-weighted Yang--Mills--Levi functional** is

$$
  \mathfrak{S}_q[A, \varTheta]
  =  \int_M q(p)\lVert F_A^+|_H \rVert^2
        \varTheta \wedge (d\varTheta)^n,
$$

where $q(p) = 1 + \lvert\langle L_\varTheta, F_A^+\rangle\rvert(p)$ ([$\mathbb{C}$-substrate via Levi form](#rem:c-substrate-via-levi)).
