**Geometric step.** The Reeb derivation lifts a braid word $\beta_K$ to
a Reeb flow whose complement $S^3 \setminus K$ is a $q$-hyperbolic
3-manifold. By a theorem of Yekutieli, this complement carries a
tessellation by ideal $q$-tetrahedra indexed by $k = 0, 1, 2, 3$ ---
the categorical $h^{2j}$ ladder with cell coefficients $(1, 4, 16, 64)$.
The volume $\mathrm{Vol}(K)$ of the complement is the central geometric
invariant. On the canonical surface --- **Bring's surface**, the genus-4
surface with $\chi = -6$, on which an $S_5$-action exchanges the five
quark--lepton families --- this volume realises the topological mass of
the knot.
