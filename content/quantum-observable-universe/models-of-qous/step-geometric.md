**Bring's surface.** The geometric step realizes the mass of every knot on a fixed surface. The canonical geometric realization is **Bring's surface** $\Sigma_4$. This genus-$4$ smooth projective curve has Euler characteristic $\chi = -6$. Its automorphism group $\mathrm{Aut}(\Sigma_4) \cong S_5$ exchanges the five quark--lepton families. It is the vanishing locus on $\mathbb{P}^4$ of the first three power sums:

$$
  \Sigma_4 = \mathrm{Proj}\!\left(
    R[x_0,\dots,x_4]\Big/\bigl(\textstyle\sum x_j,
    \textstyle\sum x_j^2, \textstyle\sum x_j^3\bigr)
  \right).
$$

We consume the following structural facts:

- **Topology.** $\Sigma_4$ has genus $g = 4$ and Euler characteristic $\chi(\Sigma_4) = 2 - 2g = -6$.
- **Symmetry.** The automorphism group $\mathrm{Aut}(\Sigma_4) \cong S_5$ has order 120. This is maximal for any genus-$4$ curve. The orientation-preserving subgroup $A_5 \subset S_5$ acts faithfully and supplies the symmetry group of the canonical particle assignment.
