A single set of QOU axioms admits realisations over strikingly
diverse ground fields and geometric settings.  The organising
question of this chapter is which features of those realisations are
universal — forced by the axioms themselves — and which are
model-dependent, fixed only by the chosen ground ring and fibre
geometry.

A concrete anchor for the survey is the Jones model arising from
$\mathbf{Rep}(\mathrm{SU}_q(2))$.  Around it we place the wider
landscape: archimedean realisations (Calabi--Yau, ALE spaces),
$p$-adic quantum groups via Drinfeld's theory, and finite-field and
Witt-vector models.  Each model instantiates the QOU axioms with
different fibre geometry and ground ring, illustrating the
flexibility of the categorical framework while exposing the
structural constraints that are universal.  The survey proceeds
through the physical interpretation of the $q$-weighted
Yang--Mills--Levi functional and the main commutative diagram
relating categorical and differential structures.
