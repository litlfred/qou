Let $A$ be an instanton connection on $P$
([instanton](#def:categorical-instanton)), $F_A^+$ its self-dual curvature
([self-dual curvature](#ex:self-dual-curvature)), and
$L_\varTheta(V, W) = -id\varTheta(V, \bar{W})$ the Levi form.  Since
$\Lambda^{1,1} H^* \subset \Lambda^+ H^*$ on the $4$-dimensional
distribution $H$, the Webster metric and Killing form yield a natural
contraction $\langle L_\varTheta, F_A^+\rangle_p \in \mathbb{R}$.  The
categorical ([substrate parameter](#def:substrate-parameter)) specialises
in the archimedean realisation to the smooth field

$$
  q(p) = 1 + \bigl\lvert\langle L_\varTheta, F_A^+ \rangle\bigr\rvert(p),
  \qquad p \in M,
$$

with $q = 1$ at classicality ($F_A^+ = 0$) and $q \to \infty$ in the
deep-quantum regime.  All downstream formulas ($\hbar_q$, commutators,
Yang--Mills) are algebraic in $q$, so the formula $1 + x$ may equally be
replaced by $\exp(x)$ without affecting the theory; the algebraic form is
preferred because it is meaningful over every base ring $R$.
Non-archimedean analogues appear in [Chapter](#chap:descartes-universe).
