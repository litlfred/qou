The abstract substrate parameter $q$ measures a connection's departure from classicality. We give its concrete archimedean realisation.

Let $A$ be an instanton connection on $P$ ([instanton](#def:categorical-instanton)). Let $F_A^+$ be its self-dual curvature ([self-dual curvature](#ex:self-dual-curvature)). Let $L_\varTheta(V, W) = -id\varTheta(V, \bar{W})$ be the Levi form. Because $\Lambda^{1,1} H^* \subset \Lambda^+ H^*$ on the 4-dimensional distribution $H$, the Webster metric and Killing form define a natural contraction $\langle L_\varTheta, F_A^+\rangle_p \in \mathbb{R}$. The categorical substrate parameter ([substrate parameter](#def:substrate-parameter)) over $\mathbb{C}$ or $\mathbb{R}$ evaluates to the smooth field

$$
  q(p) = 1 + \bigl\lvert\langle L_\varTheta, F_A^+ \rangle\bigr\rvert(p),
  \qquad p \in M.
$$

When the connection is classical ($F_A^+ = 0$), $q = 1$. In the deep-quantum regime, $q \to \infty$. 

Because all downstream quantities ($\hbar_q$, commutators, Yang–Mills action) are algebraic in $q$, replacing $1 + x$ with $\exp(x)$ does not change the structural theory. We prefer the algebraic formula $1+x$ because it remains well-defined over an arbitrary base ring $R$. Non-archimedean analogues appear in [Chapter](#chap:brings-surface).
