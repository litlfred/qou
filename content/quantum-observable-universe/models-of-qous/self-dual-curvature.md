We specialise [categorical self duality](#prop:categorical-self-duality) to recover the classical instanton picture. On a four-dimensional space, the Yang--Mills energy splits into a topological floor and a non-negative remainder. The bundle's second Chern number fixes the floor, while the remainder measures the anti-self-dual part of the curvature. A connection attains this minimum energy precisely when its curvature is self-dual.

In an archimedean quantum observable universe with $\dim_{\mathbb{R}} H = 4$, every instanton connection $A$ on $P$ satisfies $F_A|_H = {*_H}F_A|_H$, where $*_H$ denotes the Hodge star on $H$. The categorical Yang--Mills functional $\int_A(\star_q(F_\nabla) \cdot F_\nabla)$ — the integral of the $q$-Hodge pairing of the curvature with itself — becomes the ordinary Yang--Mills energy $\int_M \lVert F_A|_H \rVert^2\varTheta \wedge (d\varTheta)^2$ of the restricted curvature $F_A|_H$ on the base $M$.

The Bogomolny trick decomposes $\lVert F_A|_H \rVert^2 = \lVert F_A^+ \rVert^2 + \lVert F_A^- \rVert^2$. The self-dual part $\int \lVert F_A^+ \rVert^2$ computes a topological invariant, the second Chern number $c_2(P)$. This provides the energy floor. The anti-self-dual part carries energy but lacks a topological bound. Minimising the Yang--Mills functional therefore forces $F_A^-|_H = 0$.
