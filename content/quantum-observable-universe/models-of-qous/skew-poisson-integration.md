Let $H/\mathbb{F}_\pp$ be a smooth commutative formal group equipped with a skewed-Poisson structure $\pi_<$. There exists a (generally non-commutative) formal group $G/\mathbb{Z}_\pp$ such that:

1. $G \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}_\pp} \mathbb{F}_\pp  \cong  H$.

2. The distribution algebra $\mathrm{Dist}(G)$ deforms $\mathrm{Dist}(H)$. A Gröbner--Shirshov basis for the enveloping algebra relations computes this deformation.

3. The comultiplication on $\mathrm{Dist}(G)$ is strongly multiplicative. The Frobenius relation $(\mathrm{Id}\otimes m)\circ(\Delta\otimes\mathrm{Id}) = \Delta\circ m$ survives the deformation.
