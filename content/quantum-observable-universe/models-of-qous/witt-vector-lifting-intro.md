The $\mathbb{Q}_\pp$ realisation ([$p$-adic realisation](#ex:p-adic-realisation)) provides a QOU over characteristic $0$. The $\mathbb{F}_\pp$ realisation ([finite field realisation](#ex:finite-field-realisation)) provides one over characteristic $\pp$. The passage between them is mediated by the *Witt vector functor* $W$ and the theory of formal groups over $\pp$-adic rings.
