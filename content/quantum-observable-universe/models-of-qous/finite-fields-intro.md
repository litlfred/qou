We close the catalogue of general models with realisations over finite fields, where the substrate parameter $q$ ranges over $\mathbb{F}_\pp^{\times}$ (or a primitive $\ell$-th root of unity in $\overline{\mathbb{F}}_\pp$ with $\ell$ coprime to $\pp$) and the categorical data lives in a fusion category with finitely many simple objects. **$\mathbb{F}_\pp$ realisation** (ex) constructs $\mathrm{Rep}_{\mathbb{F}_\pp}(\mathrm{SU}_q(2))$ as a QOU and checks the Frobenius and observability axioms on the regular representation, supplying the finite-field counterpart of the Calabi--Yau and Bring's-surface witnesses of the archimedean section. **Outlook for finite-field models** (prose) lists the reductions to $\mathbb{F}_\pp$ from $\mathbb{Q}_\pp$, the finite-group representation theory, and the Betti-number comparison with characteristic zero. **Jones polynomials** (prose) sketches how the universal $R$-matrix of $U_q(\mathfrak{sl}_2)$ at a root of unity produces the Jones invariant $J_K(q)$ via the Markov trace, recovering the same knot-theoretic output from both the archimedean and finite-field models when both apply.
