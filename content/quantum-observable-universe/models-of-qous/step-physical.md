**Physical step.** The archimedean specialisation at $q_0$ on Bring's surface yields a particle spectrum. Three calibration inputs ($m_e$, $m_\mu/m_e$, $\mathfrak{Q}_\beta$) fix the scale. The Particles chapter develops the particle-generation mechanism via LP/SDP confinement and operator-valued folding/unfolding. The Statistics & Symmetrisation chapter derives Pauli exclusion and the fermion/boson split from the rigid-braided structure. The Predicted Spectra chapter catalogues these predictions (leptons, quarks, baryons, nuclei, $\mathfrak{Q}_\beta$, magnetic moments). The Observations chapter compares them to CODATA. Subsequent chapters extend the framework to atomic, molecular, and organic chemistry.

Part IV develops dynamics and information theory. This includes fluid dynamics of the brane, stochastic mechanics of the Markov trace, categorical information theory, quantum measurement as fibre-functor behaviour, and the $q$-geometric Langlands correspondence.
