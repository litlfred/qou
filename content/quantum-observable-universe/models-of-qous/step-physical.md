**Physical step.** Once the archimedean specialisation at $q_0$ is fixed
on Bring's surface, the framework predicts a particle spectrum from
three calibration inputs ($m_e$, $m_\mu/m_e$, $Q_\beta$). Chapter 9
develops the particle-generation mechanism (LP/SDP confinement,
operator-valued folding/unfolding); Chapter 8 derives Pauli exclusion
and the fermion/boson split from the rigid-braided structure. Chapter
13 catalogues the predicted spectra (leptons, quarks, baryons, nuclei,
$Q_\beta$, magnetic moments) and compares them to CODATA in Chapter 14.
Chapters 15--16 extend the framework to atomic, molecular, and organic
chemistry.

Part IV develops dynamical and information-theoretic deepenings: fluid
dynamics of the brane (Chapter 17), stochastic mechanics of the Markov
trace (Chapter 18), categorical information theory (Chapter 19),
quantum measurement as fibre-functor behaviour (Chapter 20), and the
$q$-geometric Langlands correspondence (Chapter 21).
