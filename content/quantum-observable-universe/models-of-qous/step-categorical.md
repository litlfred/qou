**Categorical step.** The substrate parameter $q$ deforms a classical structure. At $q = 1$, we have a classical symmetric tensor category. At $q \neq 1$, this resolves into a braided action. The substrate is a rigid braided monoidal category $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ equipped with a Frobenius--Hopf object $A$ and a fibre functor $\tau$. The parameter $q$ records the deformation of this categorical action. The Reeb derivation $\delta_q$ promotes $q$ to a connection on the categorical bundle. Its curvature is the Yekutieli MI tower. The Quantum Universes and Quantum Observable Universes chapters develop the axioms and derive the Reeb derivation. The Brane Tower chapter develops descent, jets, and the Gröbner--Shirshov filtration. These form the categorical infrastructure consumed throughout the paper.
