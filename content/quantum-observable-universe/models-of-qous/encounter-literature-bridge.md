**Reading.** The geometric encounter picture used in this
chapter is not an idiosyncratic QOU construction.  Each of its
ingredients has an established home in the
geometric-topology / mathematical-physics literature; this
remark records the matches.

* **Mostow rigidity (\cite{mostow1968}, \cite{thurston1978}).**
  The original rigidity theorem and Thurston's hyperbolisation
  programme together supply the geometric uniqueness that
  [Proposition `mostow-confinement`](#prop:mostow-confinement)
  invokes as a class.  Thurston's gluing equations are the
  Newton-iteration target of which the
  [Thurston relaxation](#sec:thurston-relaxation) section is the
  encounter-side reading.

* **Neumann--Zagier potential (\cite{neumann-zagier1985}).**
  The first-order behaviour of $\mathrm{Vol}_{\mathrm{hyp}}$
  near the rigid hyperbolic point is the critical behaviour of
  the Neumann--Zagier potential $\Phi_{\mathrm{NZ}}(z)$.  The
  cubic stability potential studied in
  [Chapter 9](#chap:descartes-universe) (colour-tube cubic;
  [Picard--Lefschetz thimble](#def:picard-lefschetz-thimble))
  is isogenous to $\Phi_{\mathrm{NZ}}$, with critical points
  at the "settled" configurations where the meridian and
  longitude holonomies are complete.

* **Witten's rigid phase (\cite{witten2011}).**  Analytic
  continuation of Chern--Simons theory in three dimensions
  exhibits a *rigid phase* in which the path integral
  concentrates exactly on the hyperbolic structures satisfying
  Mostow rigidity.  The encounter chapter's identification of
  the SDP optimum with this rigid phase makes the link explicit:
  the categorical Markov trace evaluated at $q_0$ is the
  Chern--Simons phase weight at the unique hyperbolic
  representation.

* **Yekutieli non-abelian Stokes (\cite{yekutieli2015}).**
  Licenses the use of Picard--Lefschetz thimbles to interpret
  the discrete Hecke normal-form reduction as a continuous
  steepest-descent flow.  Together with the
  [GB--PL thimble bijection](#conj:gb-reduction-pl-thimble-bijection)
  this is what justifies treating the SDP solver as a
  Newton iteration on the Thurston gluing equations.

* **Kashaev volume conjecture (\cite{kashaev1997}).**  The
  asymptotic of the coloured Jones polynomial at roots of unity
  realising the hyperbolic volume.  In QOU this is replaced by
  the
  [partial-product algebraic--geometric dilogarithm
  equivalence](#conj:algebraic-geometric-dilog-equivalence)
  --- the QOU-native, $q$-Pochhammer form of the same
  statement.

The point of recording these matches here is *not* to claim
new mathematics from established literature.  It is the
opposite: to make explicit that every step of the encounter
picture has a literature precedent in conventional
geometric topology, so that a reader can locate any single
ingredient in a standard reference and recover the QOU
treatment as a categorical reframing rather than a stand-alone
construction.
