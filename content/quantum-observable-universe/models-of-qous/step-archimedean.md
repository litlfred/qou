**Archimedean step.** The archimedean branch specializes at the substrate value $q_0$ to produce real-valued, dimensional quantities. This yields a dimensional mass. The generic categorical content extends over a commutative ring $R$ through $R \to R[i] \to R[i][q, q^{-1}]$. The substrate value $q = q_0$ is fixed by the $\chi = -6$ structure of Bring's surface. 

The mass endomorphism $\mathfrak{m}(K) = \mathrm{Vol}(K) \cdot c_q^{-2}$ closes the first half of the paper. This applies non-abelian Stokes integration of the Reeb flow against the categorical action. The climax chapter states this result. The Mass — Theory and Mass — Application chapters unpack the machinery needed to evaluate it (Wedderburn decomposition, partition channels $y_\lambda \chi^\lambda$, crystal bases, $\Delta_\lambda$ gating).

Section 7 sketches alternatives to the archimedean specialisation: $\pp$-adic ($R \to \mathbb{Q}_\pp$) and characteristic-$\pp$ ($R \to \mathbb{F}_\pp[T]$) substrate completions. These are non-archimedean and do not produce dimensional masses.
