**Archimedean step.** Categorical content stated over a generic
commutative ring $R$ specialises at the archimedean wall through a
chain of extensions: $R \to R[i] \to R[i][q, q^{-1}]$. The substrate
value $q = q_0 \approx 1.1097$ is fixed by the $\chi = -6$ structure
of Bring's surface; specialisation at $q_0$ crosses the wall and
produces archimedean (i.e., real-valued, dimensional) quantities.

The mass endomorphism
$\mathfrak{m}(K) = \mathrm{Vol}(K) \cdot c_q^{-2}$ ---
the non-abelian Stokes integration of the Reeb flow against the
categorical action --- is the statement that closes the first half
of the paper. Chapter 6 §7 states it; Chapters 10--11 unpack the
machinery (Wedderburn decomposition, partition channels $y_\lambda
\chi^\lambda$, crystal bases, $\Delta_\lambda$ gating) needed to
evaluate it.

Alternatives to the archimedean specialisation --- $p$-adic
($R \to \mathbb{Q}_p$) and characteristic-$p$ ($R \to \mathbb{F}_p[T]$)
substrate completions --- are sketched in §7 below. They are
non-archimedean and do not produce dimensional masses; we develop the
archimedean branch because it is the one that gives physics.
