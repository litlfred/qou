The [mass-gap conjecture](#conj:yang-mills-mass-gap) locates the vanishing of the gap on the $2$-torsion locus $\mu_2 = \{1, -1\} \subset \mathbb{G}_m$. This section examines the symmetric point $q = 1$ directly. We determine which structures of the mass tower $\mathrm{End}_{\mathbf{C}}(A_n)$ survive the specialisation, which degenerate, and at what order in $h = q - 1$ each lost structure returns. A uniform pattern emerges. The symmetric point deletes no spectrum. It only loses resolution. Distinctions maintained by braiding (chirality of crossings, inter-nucleon channel identity, accidental level coincidences) collapse at $q = 1$. They reopen at definite infinitesimal orders.
