In this model the metric is effectively replaced by the Levi form
$L_\varTheta$ and its coupling to the gauge curvature $F_A$.  Gravity is
expressed through the *Variational Bicomplex* — the double complex
$(\Omega^{p,q}(J^\infty E), d_H, d_V)$ on the infinite jet bundle,
where $d_H$ differentiates along the base $M$ and $d_V$ along the
fibre — as the back-reaction of the gauge fields on the CR manifold
$M$.

Variation of $\mathfrak{S}_q$ ([$q$-Yang--Mills--Levi functional](#def:q-yang-mills-levi)) with respect
to the Levi form $L_\varTheta$ yields field equations of the schematic form

$$
  \frac{\delta \mathfrak{S}_q}{\delta L_\varTheta}
  =  0
  \qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
  \mathrm{Ric}_{g_\varTheta} =  T^{(q)},
$$

where $\mathrm{Ric}_{g_\varTheta}$ is the Webster Ricci curvature and
$T^{(q)}$ is a $q$-weighted stress-energy $2$-tensor on $H$ (both
coordinate-free objects).  The geometry of $M$ is forced to curve to
accommodate the tension of the $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ instantons.
