Gravity in this model is the back-reaction of the gauge fields on the
CR manifold $M$: the geometry is forced to curve to accommodate the
tension of the $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ instantons.  Concretely, the metric is
effectively replaced by the Levi form $L_\varTheta$ and its coupling to
the gauge curvature $F_A$, and this coupling is organised by the
*Variational Bicomplex* on the infinite jet bundle.  That bicomplex is
the double complex $(\Omega^{p,q}(J^\infty E), d_H, d_V)$, whose
horizontal differential $d_H$ acts along the base $M$ and whose vertical
differential $d_V$ acts along the fibre.

Variation of $\mathfrak{S}_q$ ([$q$-Yang--Mills--Levi functional](#def:q-yang-mills-levi)) with respect
to the Levi form $L_\varTheta$ yields field equations of the schematic form

$$
  \frac{\delta \mathfrak{S}_q}{\delta L_\varTheta}
  =  0
  \qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
  \mathrm{Ric}_{g_\varTheta} =  T^{(q)},
$$

where $\mathrm{Ric}_{g_\varTheta}$ is the Webster Ricci curvature and
$T^{(q)}$ is a $q$-weighted stress-energy $2$-tensor on $H$ (both
coordinate-free objects).
