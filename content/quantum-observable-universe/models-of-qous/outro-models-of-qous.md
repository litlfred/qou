This concludes the survey of categorical models for a Quantum Observable Universe. An archimedean wall separates the substrate-level constructions from their archimedean realisations. The rigidity pairing at this wall — Mostow confinement on one side, and finite-field or $p$-adic obstructions on the other — determines which models survive the specialisation to numerical evaluation.

The subsequent chapter on the Descartes universe makes this wall concrete. We select the archimedean model in which the substrate parameter $q$ acquires a numerical value, and we realise the categorical objects as sections of bundles over Bring's surface. The remaining chapters trace the consequences of this choice through the physical-prediction layer.
