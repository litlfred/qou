The theorem on [skew poisson integration](#prop:skew-poisson-integration) drives the [jet prolongation](#prop:jet-prolongation) over the base field $\mathbb{Q}_\pp$:

1. The Frobenius--Hopf object $A$ in the [finite field realisation](#ex:finite-field-realisation) over $\mathbb{F}_\pp$ carries a skewed-Poisson structure. This is induced by the $q$-commutator $[\hat{X},\hat{P}]_q = i\hbar_q$ (see [Section](#sec:enveloping-algebras-models)).

2. The [Witt vector lift](#def:witt-vector-lift) $\widehat{G}$ is the non-commutative formal group whose distribution algebra deforms $\mathrm{Dist}(H)$. In the QOU language, this lifts the $\mathbb{F}_\pp$ fibre algebra to the $\mathbb{Q}_\pp$ fibre algebra.

3. The strong multiplicativity of [skew poisson integration](#prop:skew-poisson-integration) provides the Frobenius relation required in the jet prolongation proof. This ensures the lift preserves the comultiplication and the gauge connection.

4. The unit-root condition on the $F$-crystal ([$p$-adic realisation](#ex:p-adic-realisation)) corresponds to the integrability of the skewed-Poisson structure. The bivector $\pi_<$ integrates if and only if the associated $F$-crystal is unit-root.

This formal-groups integration theorem turns the $\pp$-adic lifting mechanism into a proven categorical lift.
