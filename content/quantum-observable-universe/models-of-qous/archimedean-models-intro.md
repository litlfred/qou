We turn to archimedean specialisations: realisations of the QOU axioms over $\mathbb{R}$ and $\mathbb{C}$ where the substrate parameter $q$ varies smoothly and the categorical data refines to differential geometry on a contact $3$-manifold. The thread set by the main commutative diagram of the preceding section is that every archimedean QOU arises as a $\mathbb{P}^1(\mathbb{C})$-bundle over a strictly pseudoconvex CR base with an $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ frame; the present section populates that thread with concrete fibre geometries.

Four explicit constructions anchor the catalogue. A real hypersurface in a Calabi--Yau $2$-fold draws its contact data from a K3 hypersurface and recovers the classical instanton moduli exactly; an ALE construction does the same for an $\mathrm{ADE}$ orbifold quotient via the McKay correspondence; the unit circle bundle over Bring's surface builds the genus-$4$ fibre $\chi(S) = -6$ that the next chapter selects; and a sweep of the cosmological limits of $q$ marks the deep-quantum and classical endpoints. Together they show that the archimedean axioms admit a range of geometric witnesses without preferring any one of them.

The structural results then read invariants off those witnesses along two axes. On one side is the gauge-theoretic data carried by the bundle: the instanton connection, its irreducibility, the self-dual curvature, and the Seiberg--Witten differential. On the other is the contact-geometric and uncertainty-relation content carried by the Reeb fibre: the Levi form of the $\mathbb{C}$-substrate, the Webster inner product, and the $q$-deformed Heisenberg commutator. In this setting the categorical observation functor lifts to a measurement of the Reeb flow.

Two bridge results close the section. A spatial-realisation functor packages this lift from categorical to differential data, and a categorical--geometric correspondence certifies that the functor preserves the QOU axioms, comparing the categorical action of the previous section against its classical Chern--Simons counterpart. The next section, **Non-Archimedean models**, takes the categorical reformulation as given and replaces $\mathbb{R}$ with $\mathbb{Q}_\pp$.
