We turn to archimedean specialisations: realisations of the QOU axioms over $\mathbb{R}$ and $\mathbb{C}$ where the substrate parameter $q$ varies smoothly and the categorical data refines to differential geometry on a contact $3$-manifold. The thread set by the main commutative diagram of the preceding section is that every archimedean QOU arises as a $\mathbb{P}^1(\mathbb{C})$-bundle over a strictly pseudoconvex CR base with an $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ frame; the present section populates that thread with concrete fibre geometries.

Four explicit constructions anchor the catalogue. Example: Real hypersurface in a Calabi--Yau $2$-fold draws the contact data from a K3 hypersurface and recovers the classical instanton moduli on the nose; Example: ALE links does the same for an $\mathrm{ADE}$ orbifold quotient via the McKay correspondence; Example: Bring's surface unit circle bundle builds the specific genus-$4$ fibre $\chi(S) = -6$ that the next chapter will later select; Example: Cosmological limits of $q$ sweeps the parameter range and marks the deep-quantum and classical endpoints. Together they show that the archimedean axioms admit a range of geometric witnesses without preferring any one of them.

The structural propositions then read off invariants from those witnesses. Example: Instanton connection, Example: Irreducibility of the connection, Remark: Geometric resolution and the Seiberg--Witten differential, Remark: Primordial vortex and the deep-quantum limit, Remark: Geometric drag, and Example: Self-dual curvature extract gauge-theoretic data from the bundle; Remark: $\mathbb{C}$-substrate via Levi form, Remark: Webster inner product, Proposition: $q$-deformed Heisenberg commutator from the Reeb fibre with its supporting Remark: Heisenberg commutator, and Example: Reeb decomposition extract the contact-geometric and uncertainty-relation content. Remark: Archimedean realisation of observation lifts the categorical observation functor to a measurement of the Reeb flow.

Two bridge results close the section. Definition: Spatial realization functor packages the lift from categorical to differential data, and Proposition: Categorical--geometric correspondence certifies that the functor preserves the QOU axioms; its Categorical--geometric correspondence proof supplies the diagram chase. Remark: Archimedean realization of the categorical action and Remark: Comparison with the classical Chern--Simons action match the categorical action of the previous section to its physical-physicist form. The next section, **Non-Archimedean models**, takes the categorical reformulation as given and replaces $\mathbb{R}$ with $\mathbb{Q}_\pp$.
