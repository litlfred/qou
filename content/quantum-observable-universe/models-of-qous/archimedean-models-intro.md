We specialise to archimedean fields. Over $\mathbb{R}$ and $\mathbb{C}$, the substrate parameter $q$ varies smoothly, and the categorical data becomes differential geometry on a contact 3-manifold. Every archimedean quantum observable universe is a $\mathbb{P}^1(\mathbb{C})$-bundle over a strictly pseudoconvex CR base with an $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ frame. We construct explicit geometries satisfying these conditions.

We present four models. First, a real hypersurface in a Calabi–Yau 2-fold extracts contact data from a K3 hypersurface to recover classical instanton moduli. Second, an ALE space yields an ADE orbifold quotient via the McKay correspondence. Third, the unit circle bundle over Bring's surface produces the genus-4 fibre ($\chi(S) = -6$). Finally, the cosmological limits of $q$ define the deep-quantum and classical endpoints. These examples demonstrate that the archimedean axioms support diverse geometric realisations.

We extract invariants from two sources. The bundle provides gauge-theoretic data: the instanton connection, irreducibility, self-dual curvature, and the Seiberg–Witten differential. The Reeb fibre provides contact-geometric data: the Levi form of the $\mathbb{C}$-substrate, the Webster inner product, and the $q$-deformed Heisenberg commutator. Over $\mathbb{C}$, the categorical observation functor becomes a measurement of the Reeb flow.

Two bridge results conclude the chapter. A spatial-realisation functor maps categorical data to differential data. A categorical–geometric correspondence confirms this functor preserves the axioms by comparing the categorical action against the classical Chern–Simons action. The subsequent section transports this categorical formulation to $\mathbb{Q}_p$.
