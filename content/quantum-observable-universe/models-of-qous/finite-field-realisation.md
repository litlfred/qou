This example exhibits a substrate parameter over a finite field, showing
that the Frobenius non-degeneracy and observability conditions are met with
no appeal to characteristic zero or archimedean structure.

Let $k = \mathbb{F}_\pp$ and let $\mathbf{C} = \mathrm{Rep}_{\mathbb{F}_\pp}(\mathrm{SU}_q(2))$, the category of
finite-dimensional representations of the quantum group
$\mathrm{SU}_q(2)$ over $\mathbb{F}_\pp$, where $q \in \mathbb{F}_\pp^{\times}$.  More generally, when $q$ is a primitive
$\ell$-th root of unity in $\overline{\mathbb{F}}_\pp$ (with $\ell$
coprime to $\pp$), $\mathbf{C}$ extends to a fusion category over the
extension field $\mathbb{F}_\pp(q)$ with finitely many simple objects.
The Frobenius--Hopf object $A$ is the regular representation, and
the fibre functor $\tau$ is the forgetful functor to
$\mathrm{Vect}_{\mathbb{F}_\pp}$.  The non-degeneracy of the
Frobenius form $\int_A$ follows from the semisimplicity of the fusion
category, and the observability condition
$\mathrm{Hom}(\mathbf{1}, \mathrm{Ad}(A)) = 0$ holds whenever $q \neq 1$
(since the trivial representation does not appear as a summand of the
adjoint representation for generic $q$).
