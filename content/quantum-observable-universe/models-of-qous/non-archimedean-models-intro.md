Archimedean models evaluate the substrate parameter $q$ as a real or complex number. This formulation omits the arithmetic of the $q$-commutation data, including its integral form and positive-characteristic behaviour. We capture this arithmetic structure using non-archimedean completions, where $q$ takes values in a $\pp$-adic field.

This translates to a lifting problem. We reconstruct the substrate from the algebra of its infinitesimal $q$-commutation relations. The germ of this lift is the Heisenberg uncertainty relation, written as the $q$-commutator $[\hat{X},\hat{P}]_q = i\hbar_q$. The subsequent sections detail this construction, proceeding from the `enveloping-algebras` through the Lusztig integral form and its $q$-deformed PBW basis to the $\pp$-adic and finite-field realisations.
