On the unit circle $q = e^{i\theta}$, the [crossing energy](#def:crossing-energy) is purely imaginary:

$$
  \mathfrak{E}_\times(e^{i\theta})
  \;=\; \tfrac{1}{2}\bigl(e^{i\theta} - e^{-i\theta}\bigr)
  \;=\; i \sin\theta .
$$

A crossing on $|q| = 1$ generates phase, not energy. The circular direction of $\mathbb{G}_m$ carries interference and transport (the electrodynamic, photonic sector). Real, positive crossing energy requires leaving the circle ($q$ real, $q > 1$). The two real points $\mu_2 = \{1, -1\}$ where the circle meets the real axis are doubly degenerate. There, both the phase resolution and the mass scale vanish.

The [categorical Dirac operator](#def:categorical-dirac-operator) realises this same split internally. Its deviation from the bare connection is the Reeb-direction coupling weighted by $(1 - q^{-1})$. This vanishes at $q = 1$ and is first-order in $h = q - 1$, matching the order at which the physically normalised gap opens. Mass is a first-order radial phenomenon on $\mathbb{G}_m$. Electrodynamics is the angular phase flow surviving on the circle.

At the fibre level, the deformation parameter is a formal variable. Archimedean evaluation selects a real $q_0 > 1$, establishing a massive sector. Massless (photonic) content lives on $|q| = 1$. Chiral or massive content requires $|q| \neq 1$.
