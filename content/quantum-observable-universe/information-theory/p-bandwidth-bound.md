**Proposition.** A decision problem encoded by a link $L = \hat{\beta}$ with $c = 3N$ crossings belongs to $\mathrm{P}$ if and only if its Skein polynomial $P_L(q)$ can be evaluated (its degree-$0$ answer extracted) within
$$
  t(c) \leq \mathfrak{B}(q)^{-1} \mathrm{poly}(c)
$$
Planck steps, where $\mathfrak{B}(q) = q\eta_q / (q-1)^2$ is the information bandwidth per Planck time.
