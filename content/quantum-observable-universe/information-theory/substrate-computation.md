Discrete time at the Planck scale
([discrete Planck step](#rem:discrete-planck-step)) gives
the universe discrete mathematics for free.  The iteration
$f_q(z) = z^2 + c(q)$ at each Planck step is a polynomial
map on a finite set — combinatorics, not analysis.  The
Toffoli braid $P_{7,6}(q)$
([Toffoli transfer matrix](#prop:toffoli-transfer-matrix))
is a rational function of the substrate parameter $q$ with
a factor $(q - 1)$ in its numerator.  This means:

* For all $q \neq 1$, the q-AND amplitude is **non-zero**.
* The combinatorial structure that makes reversible
  computation possible is present at every value of $q$.
* As the universe cools from the Big Bang ($q \to \infty$)
  to our epoch ($q = q_0$, [local substrate $q$](#prop:local-substrate-q)), the amplitude decreases but
  **never vanishes**.

This is why computers can be built from stones, from beads
on an abacus, from vacuum tubes, from silicon — from
anything.  The medium does not matter because the
combinatorics is not a property of the medium.  It is a
property of the substrate parameter.  The Hecke crossing
coefficients at $q = q_0$ still satisfy the same algebraic
relations as at $q = 10^{60}$; the transfer matrices still
multiply; the destructive interference that isolates the
q-AND from all other pathways still holds.  A computation
performed with pebbles in the Sahara and one performed with
photons in a superconducting circuit inhabit the same
Iwahori--Hecke algebra $H_n(q)$ at the same $q$.

**Consistency under :refterm[descent]{#descent}.**
The discrete combinatorics at the Planck scale must be
consistent with the continuous physics of the local universe.
This is guaranteed by the rationality of $P_{7,6}(q)$ in
$q^{1/2}$: the amplitude is a ratio of polynomials with
integer coefficients, valid over any field where $q \neq 1$.
The same polynomial that governs a $3$-strand braid at
the Big Bang governs it in our laboratory.  The passage
from discrete (Big Bang) to approximately continuous
(our epoch) does not break the logic gates — it only
reduces the amplitude $P_{7,6}$ and stretches the Planck
step $t_P(q) = q/(q-1)$.

At $q = q_0$
([local substrate $q$](#prop:local-substrate-q)):

$$
  P_{7,6}(q_0) = +0.0495, \qquad
  t_P(q_0) \approx 10.11
$$

The gate is slow and quiet, but it works.  That it works
is not a contingent fact about our universe — it is a
consequence of $q \neq 1$.

**Relation to 't Hooft's CA interpretation.**
The thesis that quantum mechanics emerges from a
deterministic Planck-scale cellular automaton \cite{thooft2016}
aligns structurally with the above: in both frameworks the
discrete combinatorics at the Planck scale is the ontological
primitive, and macroscopic quantum superposition is an
artifact of coarse-graining.  The QOU substrate replaces
't Hooft's information-loss mechanism with algebraic
coarse-graining by the Markov trace on $H_n(q)$, making the
passage from CA to QM functorial rather than statistical.
