The framework instantiates what Marolf calls *standard complementarity*
\cite{marolf2017}: a single Hilbert space carrying exact unitary
evolution, into which the physics of each observer is embedded by a
set of operators — per time slice — that approximately commute, while
the semiclassical interpretation of an operator differs from observer
to observer \cite{susskind1993, thooft2016}.

The dictionary is:

1. **One Hilbert space.**  The Markov-trace GNS space of
   $H_\infty(q)$
   ([GNS embedding](#prop:algebraic-gns-embedding)): a single tracial
   state in the Wenzl-positive window, a single separable
   $\mathcal{H}$, one faithful $*$-representation.

2. **Per-observer operator frames.**  An observer is a configuration
   in $\mathrm{Bun}^{\circ}$
   ([observable subcategory](#def:observable-subcategory)); its
   accessible operators are the subalgebra reachable by finite braid
   computation from its chart.  Spacelike-separated frames
   approximately commute inside the Lieb--Robinson cone $|Z| = c_q$.

3. **Observer-dependent interpretation.**  Data that is degree-$0$
   semantic content for one frame can lie beyond finite derivability
   for another.  Where the stretched-horizon picture makes the
   inside/outside split geometric \cite{susskind1993}, here it is
   arithmetic: a section behind the horizon exists in the full algebra
   but admits no finite derivation from the exterior chart
   ([information paradox](#rem:information-paradox-incompleteness)).

4. **Ontological substrate.**  The observer-independent layer is the
   beable algebra $\Gamma(\mathcal{S}, A_0)$
   ([beable correspondence](#rem:observable-beable-correspondence)),
   the 't Hooft ontological basis shared by all frames.

The framework is thus a standard-complementarity model rather than a
strong-complementarity one (no per-observer Hilbert spaces), with
Gödel-type underivability playing the role that horizon geometry plays
in the stretched-horizon account.
