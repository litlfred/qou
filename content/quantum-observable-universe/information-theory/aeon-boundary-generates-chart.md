The aeon-recycling conjecture is stated as
[`conj:aeon-recycling`](#conj:aeon-recycling): if the degree-$1$
component of $\mathrm{CMB}(\mathcal{A})$ satisfies the integrability
condition of the $q$-connection $\nabla_q$, then
$\Phi_1^q \circ \mathrm{CMB}$ — the first Planck step of the torsion
flow applied to the CMB of an aeon ([CMB functor](#def:cmb-functor))
— produces an object with $A_0 \neq 0$, landing back inside
$\mathrm{Bun}^{\circ}$. This remark interprets that conjecture as a
categorical recycling functor on the space of aeons.

If the conjecture holds for all aeons, then $\Phi_1^q \circ \mathrm{CMB}$
extends to a functor

$$
  \mathcal{R}
   \colon 
  \pi_0(\mathrm{Bun}^{\circ})
   \longrightarrow 
  \pi_0(\mathrm{Bun}^{\circ})
$$

— the **recycling functor** — that sends each aeon to its
successor.  A fixed point of $\mathcal{R}$ would be a
self-recycling aeon: one whose CMB regenerates itself. In this
reading the chart-generation map of an aeon boundary is the
particle-condensation half of the recycling functor: the degree-$1$
thermal transport data of the CMB, iterated once by the Reeb flow,
condenses into the degree-$0$ fibre data of the successor aeon's
chart.
