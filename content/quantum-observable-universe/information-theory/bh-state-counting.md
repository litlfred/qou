**Conjecture (black-hole state counting).**
*Let $\mathcal{H} = V(\mathfrak{h})$ be a jet horizon
([jet horizon](#def:jet-horizon)) in an archimedean quantum observable
universe at substrate parameter $q_0$, with mode rank
$A = \dim \Gamma(\mathcal{H}, A_1/A_0)$.  Let
$\mathcal{H}^{\mathrm{ext}}_{\mathrm{BH}}$ denote the GNS closure,
under the Markov trace, of the exterior horizon-mode algebra: the span
of trace-closed puncture labelings $(V_{j_1}, \ldots, V_{j_n})$ of
$\Gamma(\mathcal{H}, A_1/A_0)$ subject to the trace-closure constraint
$\sum_p m_p = 0$.  Then*

$$
\dim_q \mathcal{H}^{\mathrm{ext}}_{\mathrm{BH}}
= e^{s_\star A},
\qquad
\sum_j [2j+1]_{q_0}\, e^{-s_\star\, \tau(C_{q_0}(V_j))} = 1,
$$

*where $s_\star$ is the unique positive root of the puncture partition
relation ([partition pole](#conj:lqg-entropy-qdim-partition-pole)),
and with the calibration $\gamma = s_\star/2\pi$ the right-hand side
equals $\exp S(M)$ for the Bekenstein entropy \cite{bekenstein1973}
of the mass $M$ that a distant observer assigns to $\mathcal{H}$.*

The statement is the third postulate of black hole complementarity
\cite{susskind1993} — the dimension of the space of states describing
a black hole of mass $M$ is $\exp S(M)$ — restricted to the sector a
distant observer can witness.  The dimension counted is the quantum
dimension of the exterior (degree-$\geq 1$) horizon-mode space; the
degree-$0$ section space behind the horizon contributes no witnessable
states ([Hawking radiation](#rem:hawking-radiation)) and is not
counted.
