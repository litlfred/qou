The Boltzmann constant is the quantum Planck constant:

$$
  k_B \;=\; \hbar_q \;=\; \frac{q - 1}{q}.
$$

*Proof.*
The Boltzmann distribution over knot configurations is

$$
  \rho(K) \;=\; \frac{1}{Z_q}\,
  e^{-\beta_q \, S_q(K)},
$$

where $\beta_q = 1/\hbar_q = t_P(q) = q/(q-1)$ is the
inverse temperature.  The classical relation
$\beta = 1/(k_B T)$ with $T_q = \hbar_q$
([quantum temperature](#def:quantum-temperature)) gives

$$
  k_B \;=\; \frac{1}{\beta_q \, T_q}
  \;=\; \frac{1}{(1/\hbar_q) \cdot \hbar_q}
  \;=\; \hbar_q.  \qquad\square
$$
