At the fibre level, a mode of wavenumber $k$ on a one-dimensional channel ([channel](#def:one-dim-qou-channel)) advances with the $q$-integer phase $[k]_q$, so its group velocity carries fractional corrections of order $\hbar_q = 1 - q^{-1}$ relative to the substrate light speed $c_q = 1/(q-1)$ ([information speed of light](#def:information-speed-of-light)).

**Conjecture.** *The archimedean projection suppresses the fibre-level $q$-dispersion: the residual fractional variation of the archimedean photon group velocity with photon energy $\mathfrak{E}$ is bounded by $\mathfrak{E} / \mathfrak{E}_{\mathrm{QG}}$ for a suppression scale $\mathfrak{E}_{\mathrm{QG}}$ at or above the Planck energy.*

The bound is exactly what compatibility with the gamma-ray-burst time-of-flight limit \cite{abdo2009} requires; the observational context and the relation to doubly-special-relativity phenomenology are recorded in [the DSR remark](#rem:dsr-planck-scale-phenomenology). The conjectural content is the existence of the suppression and the location of $\mathfrak{E}_{\mathrm{QG}}$: the framework currently derives the fibre-level deformation but not the mechanism by which the substrate-to-archimedean projection removes its $O(\hbar_q)$ residue from macroscopic photon propagation.
