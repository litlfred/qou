This provides the QOU analogue to conformal invariance in Penrose's CCC \cite{penrose2010}. In CCC, massless particles are conformally invariant; they do not see the conformal factor $\Omega$. Here, the degree $\geq 1$ jet data ignores the "size" of $A_0$. Only its vanishing locus matters.

The QOU observability axioms ([observation](#def:observation)) require a non-degenerate Frobenius pairing to detect degree-$0$ data. At the horizon, $A_0 = 0$ and the Frobenius pairing degenerates on that component. The surviving observations act exclusively on $A_k/A_0$ — the higher-jet conformal data. Conformal invariance at the horizon emerges as a consequence of the observability axioms, rather than an additional assumption.
