*Proof.* (CCC-1) constitutes the content of [conformal invariance at the jet horizon](#prop:horizon-conformal-invariance).

(CCC-2) follows from the definition of aeon adjacency ([aeon](#def:aeon)). Adjacent aeons share the boundary $\mathcal{S}_b$. The CMB of the successor aeon is exactly the degree $\geq 1$ data at its past boundary, which is the shared locus $\mathcal{S}_b$.

(CCC-3) follows from the definition of a jet horizon ([jet horizon](#def:jet-horizon)). The defining condition is $A_0|_{\mathcal{H}} = 0$. The Frobenius non-degeneracy axiom requires $A_0 \neq 0$ for well-defined degree-$0$ observations. At the boundary where $A_0 = 0$, the pairing degenerates. Consequently, degree-$0$ data is unobservable. $\square$

**Limitations of the claim.** The proposition establishes that a QOU containing aeons is structurally a CCC. It does not claim that any particular affine chart admits aeons (a dynamical question). It does not claim that the aeon chain is infinite. Finally, it does not adopt Penrose's specific conformal metric rescaling $g \mapsto \Omega^2 g$; the QOU mechanism is ideal vanishing.

The CCC structure emerges as a theorem of the QOU axioms, not an independent postulate. Conformal invariance at boundaries, data inheritance, and the restriction to conformally invariant observables all follow directly from the observability axioms and the brane tower structure.
