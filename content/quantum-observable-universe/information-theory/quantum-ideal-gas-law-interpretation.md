**Noether invariant.**
The quantum ideal gas law is the Ward identity of a
**scale symmetry** of the partition function.  In the
$q$-deformed Noether dictionary
([Noether theorem](#rem:noether-theorem-callout)),
the conserved quantity under simultaneous rescaling of
$V_q$ and $\beta_q$ is the quantum free energy $F_q$.

Define the **thermodynamic Noether current**

$$
  \mathcal{J}_q^{\mathrm{thermo}}
  \;:=\;
  V_q \,\frac{\partial}{\partial V_q}
  \;+\;
  \beta_q \,\frac{\partial}{\partial \beta_q}.
$$

Then $\mathcal{J}_q^{\mathrm{thermo}}(\ln Z_q) = 0$
at the equation-of-state surface.  The quantum ideal
gas law $P_q V_q = N T_q (1 + \cdots)$ is the content
of this vanishing — the analogue of the classical Euler
relation $E = TS - PV + \mu N$.

**Classical limit.**
As $q \to 1$: $T_q = \hbar_q \to 0$, but
$N T_q = N \hbar_q$ remains finite in physical units
via $k_B$.  The correction vanishes as
$\hbar_q^2 \to 0$, and we recover $PV = Nk_BT$.
