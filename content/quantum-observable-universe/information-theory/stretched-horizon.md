The *stretched horizon* of black hole complementarity
\cite{susskind1993} — a timelike membrane just above the true horizon
which, in the exterior description, absorbs, thermalizes, and re-emits
what falls on it — has a direct realisation on a jet horizon
([jet horizon](#def:jet-horizon)): it is the degree-$1$ quotient sheaf
$\Gamma(\mathcal{H}, A_1/A_0)$.

The identification is functional, point for point:

1. **Absorbs.**  The horizon ideal $\mathfrak{h} \subset A_0$ blocks
   every degree-$0$ section: particle identity is captured at
   $\mathcal{H}$ and does not continue outward.

2. **Thermalizes.**  What survives restriction to the surface layer is
   degree-$\geq 1$ jet data — winding and linking modes whose
   statistics depend only on temperature-like quantities, not on
   individual identities
   ([Hawking radiation](#rem:hawking-radiation)).

3. **Re-emits.**  The quotients $A_k/A_0$ carry non-trivial sections
   across $\mathcal{H}$; the exterior observer detects them as the
   thermal Hawking flux.

4. **Real for the exterior, absent for the global description.**  The
   membrane's dynamics are the exterior chart's account of data it
   cannot finitely derive; in the full algebra there is no membrane,
   only the vanishing locus $V(\mathfrak{h})$.  This is the
   complementarity of descriptions defended by the gedanken analysis
   of \cite{susskindthorlacius1994}.
