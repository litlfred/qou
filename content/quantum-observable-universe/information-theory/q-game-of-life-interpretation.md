**Remark (Relation to Peña et al. and 't Hooft).**
Peña et al. construct a reversible quantum Game of Life using Toffoli-based adder circuits. Their Toffoli gate is the classical limit $q \to 1$ of the $q$-Toffoli, and their construction is recovered in that limit; away from it the two are not comparable, since the $q$-deformed generator is invertible but not unitary. Their unitary tick maps to $3$ Planck steps. We identify the $q$-deformed amplitude $\Phi(q)$ as the physical AND amplitude, with the stability region $q > 1$ and the three operator zeros $q_\star = 1/\varphi^2$ (where $P_{7,7}$ vanishes), $q_\Phi \approx 0.4656$ and $q = 1$ delimiting the sub-physical behaviour.

This construction realizes 't Hooft's proposal that quantum mechanics emerges from a deterministic Planck-scale cellular automaton. The QOU supplies the geometric mechanism. The ontological states are Reeb bundle fibres $\mathcal{S}_p$. The automaton tick is the Planck step $t_P(q)$. The $q$-deformation replaces ad hoc information loss with categorical coarse-graining via the Markov trace.
