Specialised to the archimedean ground field — the complex or real
continuum
([archimedean realization](#def:archimedean-realization-functor)) —
the rigid axioms of a quantum observable universe stand in structural
correspondence with several consistency criteria of the **Swampland
Program** \cite{vafa2005, palti2019}, the programme that separates
effective field theories admitting a quantum-gravity completion from
those that do not.

We record this correspondence as a dictionary, not a derivation. The
framework developed in this paper is not a model of quantum gravity,
and we make no claim to settle any Swampland conjecture. The remarks
that follow observe that three structures already forced by the
axioms — the vanishing of the adjoint invariants
([observability](#def:quantum-observable-universe)), the jet horizon
at the foot of the brane tower ([jet horizon](#def:jet-horizon)), and
the rigidity of Bring's surface
([Euler characteristic](#lem:brings-euler-characteristic)) — are
categorical counterparts of, respectively, the absence of exact global
symmetries, the tower of light states of the Distance Conjecture, and
the selection of an isolated consistent point in moduli space. The
electromagnetic coupling
([trace-ratio form](#prop:alpha-em-categorical-trace-ratio)) supplies a
fourth entry, aligned with the Emergence Proposal.

In each case the QOU realises algebraically, and at a single rigid
point, what the Swampland Program states asymptotically across a
continuous landscape. The direction of the dictionary is fixed: the
categorical structures furnish concrete instances of the Swampland
pictures; they do not establish the conjectures themselves.
