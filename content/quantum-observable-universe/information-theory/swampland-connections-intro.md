Specialised to the archimedean ground field ([archimedean realization](#def:archimedean-realization-functor)), the rigid axioms of a quantum observable universe structurally align with several consistency criteria of the **Swampland Program** \cite{vafa2005, palti2019}. This program separates effective field theories admitting a quantum-gravity completion from those that do not.

We record this correspondence as a dictionary, not a derivation. This paper does not model quantum gravity. We make no claim to settle any Swampland conjecture. Three structures forced by the axioms serve as categorical counterparts to Swampland criteria:
1. The vanishing of the adjoint invariants ([observability](#def:quantum-observable-universe)) corresponds to the absence of exact global symmetries.
2. The jet horizon at the foot of the brane tower ([jet horizon](#def:jet-horizon)) corresponds to the tower of light states of the Distance Conjecture.
3. The rigidity of Bring's surface ([Euler characteristic](#lem:brings-euler-characteristic)) corresponds to the selection of an isolated consistent point in moduli space.

The electromagnetic coupling ([trace-ratio form](#prop:alpha-em-categorical-trace-ratio)) supplies a fourth entry, aligned with the Emergence Proposal.

In each case, the QOU realises algebraically, and at a single rigid point, what the Swampland Program states asymptotically across a continuous landscape. The categorical structures furnish concrete instances of Swampland pictures. They do not establish the conjectures themselves.
