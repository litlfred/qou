**The Swampland statement.**
Gauge couplings and kinetic terms are not fundamental; they are
generated in the infrared by integrating out a tower of charged states,
and weak coupling emerges at large distance in moduli space
\cite{heidenreich2018, ooguri-vafa2007}.

**QOU translation.**
Here the coupling is generated by the discrete topological data of the
force-carrier knots rather than by a continuum loop sum, but the
conclusion is parallel: $\alpha_{EM}$ is a derived consequence of the
substrate, not an adjustable dial. Sending the coupling toward its
classical limit forces $q \to 1$, where the skein parameter
$h = q - q^{-1}$ vanishes, the braiding degenerates to a symmetric
monoidal structure, and the topological information distinguishing
charged states is erased — the QOU image of weak coupling at the
boundary of the parameter space.

**Scope.**
The trace-ratio identity and its combinatorial form
$\alpha_{EM}^{-1} = q\,[9]_q\,[10]_q$ are proved sorry-free. Agreement
with the measured value at the substrate parameter is leading-order;

still-open Chern–Simons correction
([open problems](#rem:alpha-em-derivation-open-problems)). The
emergence we describe is the structural fact that the coupling is fixed
by topology, not a claim that its measured value is fully derived.
