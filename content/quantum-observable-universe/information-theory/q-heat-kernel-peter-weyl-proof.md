*Proof.* Schur's lemma on the irreducible Wedderburn block $M_{d_\lambda(q)}$ forces $\Delta_\xi$ to act by a scalar. We compute this scalar by evaluating the $q$-Casimir on a highest-weight vector. For $H_n(q)$, the Casimir value equals the sum of $[c(\square)]_q[c(\square) + 1]_q$ over cells $\square \in \lambda$ (a classical Jucys--Murphy result, $q$-deformed by Ram). Multiplying by $\hbar_q^2 = (1 - q^{-1})^2$ yields $\mu_\lambda(q)$. $\square$
