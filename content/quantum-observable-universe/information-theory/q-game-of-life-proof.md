*Proof.* 
The operator $\mathcal{U}_{\mathrm{q\text{-}GoL}}$ is built from $q$-Toffoli generators. By [Toffoli transfer matrix](#prop:toffoli-transfer-matrix), these act as elements of $H_3(q) \otimes \cdots \otimes H_3(q)$ on the tensor product of Reeb bundles $\bigotimes_i \mathcal{S}_{p_i}$. Each generator is *invertible* in $H_n(q)$, and it is invertibility — not unitarity — that this argument establishes. The eigenvalues of $\sigma_i$ are $q$ and $-q^{-1}$, of modulus $\neq 1$ whenever $q \neq 1$, so $\sigma_i$ is not unitary with respect to any inner product away from the classical limit; and the Markov trace form is not available as one, being degenerate under the production trace convention. Reversibility therefore rests on invertibility alone: the quadratic relation $\sigma_i^2 = (q - q^{-1})\sigma_i + 1$ gives the explicit inverse
$$
  \sigma_i^{-1} = \sigma_i - (q - q^{-1})\cdot 1 ,
$$
so every $\sigma_i$ is a unit and the composite is invertible. The generators are *not* involutions at generic $q$: $\sigma_i^{-1} = \sigma_i$ holds precisely when $q = q^{-1}$, i.e. in the classical limit $q \to 1$, which is where the $q$-Toffoli reduces to the ordinary Toffoli gate.

Any reversible computation is simulated by Toffoli gates \cite{bennett1973, toffoli1980, fredkin1982}. That Toffoli together with Hadamard is universal for *quantum* computation is the theorem of Shi and of Aharonov \cite{shi2002, aharonov2003}, stated for the standard Toffoli gate; its extension to $\mathrm{Toff}_q$ at $q \neq 1$ is not established here.

The bandwidth bound follows by counting. Each $\mathrm{Toff}_q$ in the $3$-strand Hecke algebra requires three elementary crossings, taking three Planck steps. One generation applies one Toffoli gate per neighbour triple. This yields the stated bound.

The stability condition requires $P_{7,7}(q) > 0$ and $\Phi(q) > 0$. These are bounded by *different* thresholds, and [Toffoli LP-stability](#prop:toffoli-lp-stability) lists the three operator zeros separately: $P_{7,7}$ vanishes at $q_\star = 1/\varphi^2 \approx 0.3820$, while $\Phi$ reverses sign at $q_\Phi \approx 0.4656$ and vanishes again at $q = 1$. The golden-ratio zero therefore bounds $P_{7,7}$ only; $\Phi$ is negative on $(q_\Phi, 1)$, so $q > 1/\varphi^2$ is not sufficient. The common region on which both are strictly positive is $q > 1$, which is the regime asserted in the statement. $\square$
