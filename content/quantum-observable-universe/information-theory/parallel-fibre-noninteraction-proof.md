Split-link multiplicativity of the Markov trace — equivalently the disjoint-union rule for the Kauffman bracket and the Jones polynomial — gives the factorisation directly. Associativity, commutativity, and the identity law are then the corresponding ring identities for the product of trace values. The Lean sibling proves all four over a generic commutative ring $R$ (the factorisation is definitional; commutativity uses only $\mathrm{propext}$).
$\qquad\blacksquare$
