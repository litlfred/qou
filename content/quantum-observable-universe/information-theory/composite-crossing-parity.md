Let $\mathcal{A}(Z, N)$ be a stable atom with mass number
$A = Z + N$, constructed as
$\mathcal{N}(Z, N) \bowtie Z \cdot T_{2,3}$
([stable nucleus](#def:stable-nucleus)).

The composite crossing number
$n_K^{(\mathrm{diag})}(\mathcal{A})$ of the full atomic
link satisfies

$$
  n_K^{(\mathrm{diag})}(\mathcal{A})
  \;=\; A^2 + 7A + 5Z,
$$

from the crossing budget: $8A$ (nucleon internal) +
$A(A-1)$ (inter-nucleon Hopf links) + $3Z$ (electron
trefoils) + $2Z$ (electron--nucleus Hopf binding).

The Kauffman--Murasugi--Thistlethwaite theorem gives
$n_K = n_K^{(\mathrm{diag})}$ for alternating link
composites.  Since $A(A+1)$ is always even:

$$
  n_K(\mathcal{A}(Z, N))
  \;\equiv\; A^2 + 7A + 5Z
  \;\equiv\; A(A+1) + Z
  \;\equiv\; Z \pmod{2}.  \qquad\square
$$
