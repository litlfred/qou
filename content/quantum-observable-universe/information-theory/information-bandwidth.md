**Definition.**
The **information bandwidth** per Planck time is the total
information transferred across the causal boundary in one Planck
step: the product of the speed of light $c_q$
([speed of light](#def:information-speed-of-light)) (fibres
correlated per tick) and the operational entropy per fibre
([operational entropy](#def:operational-entropy)):

$$
  \mathfrak{B}(q)
  =  c_q \cdot n_R(q) \cdot \eta_q
  =  \frac{\eta_q}{\hbar_q \cdot (q-1)}
  =  \frac{\eta_q}{q^{-1}(q-1)^2}
  =  \frac{q \cdot \eta_q}{(q-1)^2}.
$$

At $q = q_0$:
$\mathfrak{B} = q\eta_q/(q-1)^2 \approx 82$ $q$-bits per Planck time.
This is the Bremermann-analog bit budget of the substrate: the
maximum number of distinguishable $q$-bits that one Planck step can
carry across the light-cone boundary.
