Let $\mathcal{A}$ and $\mathcal{A}'$ be aeons ([aeon](#def:aeon))
with boundaries $\partial\mathcal{A}$, $\partial\mathcal{A}'$
([boundary of an aeon](#def:aeon-boundary)).

**Definition.**
Two aeons $\mathcal{A}$ and $\mathcal{A}'$ are **adjacent** if
their boundaries share a common object:

$$
  \partial\mathcal{A} \cap \partial\mathcal{A}' \neq \varnothing.
$$

The shared boundary object is simultaneously the terminal horizon
of one aeon and the initial configuration of the next — the
categorical realisation of one cosmological cycle handing off to
the following one.
