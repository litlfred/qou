Let $\beta$ be an $n$-strand state of the gauge-action groupoid. A :defterm[strand twist datum]{#strand-twist-datum} on $\beta$ is a function

$$
  t \colon \{1, \dots, n\} \longrightarrow \mathbb{Z},
$$

assigning to each strand an integer ribbon twist, together with the total twist $T(t) = \sum_i t_i$. The pair $(\beta, t)$ is a framed state; the datum is the framing that the ribbon structure of the braided category carries in addition to the crossing data of $\beta$.

Three properties are proved in the Lean sibling. The residual $S_n$ strand-relabelling action of the gauge groupoid preserves both $T(t)$ and the full multiset $\{t_1, \dots, t_n\}$, so the datum descends to gauge orbits. The bar involution (orientation reversal, the conjugate state) negates the datum, $T(\bar t) = -T(t)$, so conjugate states carry opposite twist. Finally, the framing is genuinely independent of the crossing data: over any fixed braid word — hence any fixed writhe $w$ — there exist framings with distinct total twist. This last point is what qualifies the datum as a candidate for [the hypercharge invariant](#conj:sm-hypercharge-invariant): the factorization criterion there shows any hypercharge invariant must separate a writhe-degenerate pair of states, which no function of $w$ can do, and the twist datum supplies exactly such a separating coordinate.

The normalised candidate pairs the datum with the derived isospin: with $Q_{\mathrm{twist}} = T(t)/3$ (the colour-tube charge quantum of the quark picture, the helon-model normalisation \cite{bilson-thompson2005}) and $I_3$ the writhe-derived isospin, the candidate hypercharge is $Y_t = 2\,(Q_{\mathrm{twist}} - I_3)$. This makes the Gell-Mann–Nishijima relation hold by construction *if and only if* the two independent charge routes agree, $Q_{\mathrm{twist}} = Q$ against [the crossing-derived Coulomb charge](#def:coulomb-charge) — a falsifiable consistency between framing and crossing data, not a definition. That consistency, together with the first-generation spectrum and its $\mathbb{Z}_6$ compatibility \cite{baez-huerta2010}, is the battery any candidate must pass before wiring; it is exactly the open content of the hypercharge conjecture, and no numerical claim is made here.
