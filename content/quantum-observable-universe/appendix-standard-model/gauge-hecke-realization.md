We conjecture that the gauge symmetry of the knot-state groupoid realises faithfully inside the Hecke algebra: for every topological state $X$, there is an injective monoid homomorphism

$$
  \Phi_X \colon \mathrm{End}_{\mathbf{G}}(X) \longrightarrow H_n(q)
$$

from the gauge automorphisms of $X$ into the multiplicative monoid of $H_n(q)$.

The surjective part of this picture is standard and is not the conjectural content: the braid group $B_n$ maps onto $H_n(q)$ by imposing the Hecke relation $(\sigma_i - q)(\sigma_i + q^{-1}) = 0$ on the braid generators, as recorded in [the Hecke-algebra-from-braiding remark](#rem:hecke-algebra-from-braiding). The conjectural content is *faithfulness on gauge automorphisms*: the Hecke relation is a quotient, and a quotient can collapse symmetry. The conjecture asserts that no gauge transformation of a physical state is collapsed — gauge equivalence computed in the Hecke image coincides with gauge equivalence computed in $\mathbf{G}$, so that the Markov-trace evaluation of the derivation chain sees the full gauge structure rather than a proper quotient of it.

Consequences, contingent on the conjecture: gauge invariance of an observable becomes equivalent to topological invariance of the corresponding Hecke class, and the generators of the gauge symmetry acquire the concrete matrix realisations (Hoefsmit) already used by the compute layer. Neither consequence is available unconditionally.
