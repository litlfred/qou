Three of these deserve emphasis, because they are the ones a derivation of the Standard Model cannot do without. The hypercharge assignment requires a second crossing invariant independent of the writhe; the strand twist datum now supplies a gauge-invariant candidate with the falsifiable consistency $Q_{\mathrm{twist}} = Q$ against the crossing-derived charge, but deriving the per-fermion twist multisets from framework states — rather than enumerating them — remains open. The $\mathrm{SU}(3)$ factor now has its algebra: the octonion stabilizer with proved dimension gate and bracket closure; what it lacks is an action on the framework's own state space, which is the surviving open content of the colour conjecture. And the truncation mechanism — why the observable-scale symmetry should be a finite-dimensional Lie group rather than the full groupoid symmetry — has no derivation; every use of the phrase "freeze-out" in this appendix is a heuristic label for that gap, not an explanation.
