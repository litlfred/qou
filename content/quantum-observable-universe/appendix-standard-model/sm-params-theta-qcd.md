The strong-CP angle is entirely open: no block, computation, or audit in the corpus addresses $\theta_{\mathrm{QCD}}$ or the strong-CP problem. One speculative direction can be named without endorsement: the framework's framing phase $q^{w \cdot c}$ is a topological angle attached to crossings, which is the same *kind* of object as a $\theta$-term, and its per-crossing coupling is already structurally fixed at $c = 1/2$ by [the Coulomb-charge definition](#def:coulomb-charge). Whether the observed smallness of $\theta_{\mathrm{QCD}}$ could arise from the framing sector is a question, not a claim; nothing in the corpus develops it, and the framing phase's derived readings (charge and isospin) are already spoken for. A derivation would first require the $\mathrm{SU}(3)$ sector itself, which is open.
