**Figure (Bring chiral fibre along a real segment, intermediate regime).**

*Companion to [fig:bring-fibre-q0](#fig:bring-fibre-q0). Two-panel
illustration of the chiral fibre structure away from any branch
point, in the intermediate $q = \tfrac{1}{2}$ regime where all
three sheets are well-separated.*

The cubic and base are the same as in
[fig:bring-fibre-q0](#fig:bring-fibre-q0). Along the real segment
$z \in [-0.5, 1.5]$ — chosen because it lies well outside any
neighbourhood of the six branch points $z \approx (0.119, \pm 1.054)$,
$(0.106, \pm 0.937)$, $(-0.975, \pm 0.223)$ — the cubic has one real
root $X_{\text{real}}$ and a complex-conjugate pair $X_{\text{conj},1}$,
$X_{\text{conj},2} = \overline{X_{\text{conj},1}}$.

The right-hand stacked sub-plots show Re$(X)$ (top) and Im$(X)$
(bottom) versus $z$ along the segment. The real sheet (solid)
appears in Re-only, with Im $\equiv 0$; the conjugate pair
(dashed + dotted) have opposite imaginary parts in Im, and matching
real parts in Re.
