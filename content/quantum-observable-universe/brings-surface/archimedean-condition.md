*Lean sibling: archimedean specialisation over $\mathbb{R}$ (AGENTS.md §7c). Underlying combinatorial content stays generic to any commutative ring with the same $q$-data.*

A quantum observable universe $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ over $R$
([quantum observable universe](#def\:quantum-observable-universe),
[QU-to-QOU lifting](#thm\:qu-to-qou)) satisfies the *Descartes condition* if the
categorical Euler characteristic

$$
  \chi(A) =  \mathrm{tr}(\mathrm{id}_A) =  -6  \in  R.
$$

This requires $6 \in R^{\times}$ (i.e. $\mathrm{char}(R) \nmid 6$).  The
class $[A] \in K_0(\mathbf{C})$ has rank $-6$, which by
[QU-to-QOU lifting](#thm\:qu-to-qou)(3--4) forces $\kappa_n \neq 0$ for all $n$ and
produces $6$ branch points in the geometric :refterm[descent]{#descent} (with total ramification
$R_{\mathrm{tot}} = 2d + 6$).  A quantum observable universe satisfying the
Descartes condition is called a *Descartes universe*.
