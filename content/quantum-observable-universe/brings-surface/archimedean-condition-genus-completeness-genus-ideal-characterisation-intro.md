Continuing the previous derivation, we now characterise the genus ideal—the set of categorical genus invariants isolated by the archimedean condition—as a finitely generated ideal identified by the Yekutieli leading coefficient. The leading coefficient fixes the generator set, while the genus filtration interprets the ideal as a structured cover of the torus-embeddable subspace. We conclude by showing that every torus-embeddable categorical knot lies in the generation closure of the genus ideal, making the Descartes condition equivalent to a finite combinatorial classification.
