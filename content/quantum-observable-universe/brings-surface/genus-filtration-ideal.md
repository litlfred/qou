The genus of the moduli surface is fixed by the Iwahori--Hecke algebra rather than chosen: $g = 4$ is the least genus at which the embeddable torus knots $T_{r,s}$, $r \in \{2, 3\}$, number at least the five irreducibles of $H_2(q) \oplus H_3(q)$ ([genus from Hecke completeness](#lem:genus-from-hecke-completeness)). The embeddability count is proved; that the six evaluations available at $g = 4$ actually span the five-dimensional representation is verified numerically, so the value of $g$ rests on that rank condition. The genus-filtration ideal $\mathfrak{I}_4 = (u^5)$ serves as the algebraic obstruction that kills $T_{2,p}$ for all primes $p \geq 11$.
