The genus of the moduli surface is not assumed; it is determined by the Iwahori--Hecke algebra. The QOU axioms force $g = 4$ through a completeness condition on the irreducible representations of $H_2(q) \oplus H_3(q)$. The genus-filtration ideal $\mathfrak{I}_4 = (u^5)$ serves as the algebraic obstruction that kills $T_{2,p}$ for all primes $p \geq 11$.

## The genus-filtration ideal

Define the genus-$g$ filtration ideal $\mathfrak{I}_g \subset \mathbb{Z}[u]$ by:
$$
  \mathfrak{I}_g
  = \bigl\{ f(u) \in \mathbb{Z}[u] : \deg f > g \bigr\}
  = (u^{g+1}).
$$
A torus knot $T_{2,p}$ admits a localized knot structure on $\Sigma_g$ if and only if $a_p \notin \mathfrak{I}_g$. For $g = 4$, $\mathfrak{I}_4 = (u^5)$. Every $T_{2,p}$ with $p \geq 11$ has $a_p \in (u^5)$ because $\deg_u a_p \geq 5$.
