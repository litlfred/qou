At $g = 4$ the embeddable torus knots $T_{r,s}$, $r \in \{2, 3\}$, first number at least the five irreducibles of $H_2(q) \oplus H_3(q)$ ([genus from Hecke completeness](#lem:genus-from-hecke-completeness)). That count is proved. It does **not** fix the genus: the six evaluations available at $g = 4$ do not span the five-dimensional representation, because a $T_{r,s}$ evaluates only in the $H_r(q)$ columns and the resulting block-diagonal matrix has rank at most $2 + 2 = 4$ for every $q$ ([the block-rank remark](#rem:torus-evaluation-block-rank)). So $g = 4$ is taken as given below rather than derived from the algebra here. The genus-filtration ideal $\mathfrak{I}_4 = (u^5)$ serves as the algebraic obstruction that kills $T_{2,p}$ for all primes $p \geq 11$.
