The genus of the moduli surface is not assumed; it is determined by the Iwahori--Hecke algebra. The QOU axioms force $g = 4$ through a completeness condition on the irreducible representations of $H_2(q) \oplus H_3(q)$. The genus-filtration ideal $\mathfrak{I}_4 = (u^5)$ serves as the algebraic obstruction that kills $T_{2,p}$ for all primes $p \geq 11$.
