Let $\mathcal{B} \subset \mathbb{P}^4$ be
[Bring's surface](#def:brings-surface) — the genus-$4$ curve cut out by the
vanishing of the first three power sums, with maximal automorphism group
$S_5$. Its **affine cone** is the affine variety

$$
  C(\mathcal{B})
  \;=\;
  \Bigl\{\, x \in \mathbb{C}^5 \;:\;
    \textstyle\sum_j x_j = \sum_j x_j^2 = \sum_j x_j^3 = 0 \,\Bigr\}
  \;\subset\; \mathbb{C}^5,
$$

a complex surface, hence a real $4$-manifold. Write $M = C(\mathcal{B})$.

On a $4m$-dimensional manifold, a **tetraplectic structure** \cite{foth2002}
is a closed $4$-form $\Omega \in \Omega^4(M)$ whose $m$-th power $\Omega^m$ is
a non-degenerate volume form. Here $m = 1$: every $4$-form on a $4$-manifold is
closed, so $d\Omega = 0$ is automatic and the tetraplectic requirement reduces
to a nowhere-vanishing volume form.

$M$ inherits the discrete symmetries of $\mathcal{B}$ — an $S_5 \times
\mathbb{Z}_2$ action, with $\mathbb{Z}_2$ generated by an involution $\tau$.
Averaging an arbitrary initial volume form $\omega_0$ over that action,

$$
  \Omega
  \;=\;
  \frac{1}{|S_5|} \sum_{g \in S_5}
    \bigl( \omega_0 \pm \tau^{*}(g^{*}\omega_0) \bigr),
$$

produces an $S_5$-invariant tetraplectic volume form $\Omega$. Fix it, and set
the state space to be $\mathcal{H} = L^2(M, \Omega)$.

Because $\Omega$ is $S_5$-invariant, $\mathcal{H}$ carries a unitary $S_5$
action and so decomposes into orthogonal isotypic components indexed by the
integer partitions of $5$. This is the entry point to the rest of the chapter:
the continuous geometry of the cone is resolved into the same Young-diagram
data that indexes the Wedderburn blocks elsewhere in the framework.
