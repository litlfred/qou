Bring's curve $\Sigma_4$ is defined by the vanishing of the first three power sums of the homogeneous coordinates $x_0, \dots, x_4$, $\sum_{j=0}^{4} x_j = \sum_{j=0}^{4} x_j^2 = \sum_{j=0}^{4} x_j^3 = 0$, in $\mathbb{P}^4$. The $S_5$ action permutes the five coordinates. Each coordinate defines a sheet of the surface, glued together along the ramification locus. 

The five sheets meet at ramification points where two or more coordinates coincide. The regular pentagon inscribed in the unit circle links non-adjacent sheets via a "diagonal" connection ($T_{3,5}$ interaction) proportional to the golden ratio $\varphi \approx 1.618$. 

The Toffoli diagonal $P_{7,7}(q) = (q + q^{1/2} - 1)/(q + 1)$ has a zero at $q = 1/\varphi^2 \approx 0.382$. This golden ratio phase transition eliminates the diagonal $T_{3,5}$ connection, degenerating the $5$-fold symmetry down to $3$-fold. The zero belongs to the diagonal, not to the $q$-AND amplitude $P_{7,6} = \Phi$, whose own zero sits at $q \approx 0.4656$, the root of $q^{3/2} + q^{1/2} - 1$.

**Convention scope.** The closed form above is the traced-volume product $\hat V_0 \hat V_1$ with $\hat V_i = z(q)\,a_i + b_i$, evaluated at the *excised* Markov parameter $z_C = q^{1/2}/(q+1)$. Since $(a_i, b_i)$ are rational in $q$, the convention enters through $z$ alone, and the all-proton ordering gives the frame-independent $P_{7,7} = z + (q - q^{-1})z^2$. At the canonical $z_D = q^3/(q^2+1)$ this is $q^3(q^4+1)/(q^2+1)^2$, **strictly positive for every $q > 0$** — so the diagonal has no golden-ratio zero in the canonical frame. The golden threshold itself stands: it is independently the root of $s^2 + s - 1$ in the Weierstrass coefficient of $\mathcal{E}_q$, which carries no $z$. What the convention removes is the coincidence of the two.
