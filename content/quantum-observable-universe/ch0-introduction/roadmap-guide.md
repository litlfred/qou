**Reading guide.** Each chapter builds on the one before it:
Chapter 1 lays the categorical foundation (quantum universes),
Chapter 2 adds the geometric axioms that single out $\mathrm{SU}(2)$,
Chapters 3--4 develop the topological obstructions and braid calculus
that yield particle structure, Chapter 5 constructs the explicit model
on Bring's surface, Chapter 6 extracts the full particle spectrum,
Chapter 7 confronts predictions with experiment, and Chapter 8 derives
information-theoretic consequences.

The dashed line on the right marks the $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ running
example (`ex:*-su2` blocks): a single concrete instance that
accompanies each abstract definition from Chapter 1 through Chapter 5,
providing a worked example alongside the general theory at every step.
