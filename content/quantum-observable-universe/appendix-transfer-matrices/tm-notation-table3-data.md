This table assigns each Standard-Model fermion a torus knot
$T_{p,q}$ and lists the conjugate knot carried by its antiparticle.

| Particle | Knot | Antiparticle | Knot |
|----------|------|-------------|------|
| Neutrino $\nu$ | $T_{1,0}$ | $\nu^{\sigma}$ | $T_{1,0}^{\sigma} = T_{1,0}$ |
| Electron $e^-$ | $T_{2,3}$ | Positron $e^+$ | $T_{2,3}^{\sigma}$ |
| Up quark $\mathfrak{u}$ | $T_{2,3}$ | $\mathfrak{u}^{\sigma}$ | $T_{2,3}^{\sigma}$ |
| Down quark $\mathfrak{d}$ | $T_{2,3}^{\sigma}$ | $\mathfrak{d}^{\sigma}$ | $T_{2,3}$ |
| Charm quark $\mathfrak{c}$ | $T_{2,5}$ | $\mathfrak{c}^{\sigma}$ | $T_{2,5}^{\sigma}$ |
| Strange quark $\mathfrak{s}$ | $T_{2,5}^{\sigma}$ | $\mathfrak{s}^{\sigma}$ | $T_{2,5}$ |
| Bottom quark $\mathfrak{b}$ | $T_{2,7}^{\sigma}$ | $\mathfrak{b}^{\sigma}$ | $T_{2,7}$ |
| $T_{3,4}$ (Hopf${}^2$ binding) | $T_{3,4}$ | (self-conjugate in $H^{0,1}$) | $T_{3,4}^{\sigma}$ |

Here the pair $(p,q)$ are the standard torus-knot indices, and the
superscript $\sigma$ denotes the particle–antiparticle (bar)
conjugation: the antiparticle carries the knot $T_{p,q}^{\sigma}$,
and a knot satisfying $T_{p,q}^{\sigma} = T_{p,q}$ (such as the
neutrino's $T_{1,0}$) is self-conjugate. The grading $H^{0,1}$ is
where this self-conjugacy is registered.
