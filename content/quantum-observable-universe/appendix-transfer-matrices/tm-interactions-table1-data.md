The explicit $2 \times 2$ entries of the universal beta-decay interaction matrix $\Delta_\beta(q) = \tfrac{1}{2}(q - q^{-1})\,\mathbb{I}$: it is scalar, so only the two diagonal entries are nonzero.

| Entry | Formula |
|:-----:|---------|
| $(1,1)$ | $\frac{q - q^{-1}}{2}$ |
| $(1,2)$ | $0$ |
| $(2,1)$ | $0$ |
| $(2,2)$ | $\frac{q - q^{-1}}{2}$ |
