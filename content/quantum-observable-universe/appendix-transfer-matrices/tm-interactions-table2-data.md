These entries are the amplitudes of the $2\times 2$ Toffoli transfer matrix $\mathbf{M}(q)$, resolved by parity channel: index $6$ labels the even sector and index $7$ the odd sector. The diagonal amplitudes $P_{6,6}$ and $P_{7,7}$ are the **no-flip** (parity-preserving) amplitudes in the even and odd channels, while the off-diagonal $P_{7,6} = \Phi(q)$ is the **target-flip** amplitude — the $q$-AND channel that fires when the control–control pair carries unit crossing coefficient. Reversing the crossing negates it, so the flip channel is antisymmetric: $P_{6,7} = -P_{7,6} = -\Phi(q)$.

| Entry | Formula |
|:-----:|---------|
| $P_{6,6}$ (no flip, even) | $\dfrac{q^4 + q^{7/2} + 2q^{3/2} + q^{1/2} - 1}{2(q+1)^2}$ |
| $P_{7,6}$ (flip, q-AND) | $\Phi(q)$ |
| $P_{6,7}$ (reverse flip) | $-\Phi(q)$ |
| $P_{7,7}$ (no flip, odd) | $\dfrac{q + q^{1/2} - 1}{q + 1}$ |
