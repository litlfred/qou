Each row records the composition of a nucleon — proton $\mathfrak{p} = [\mathfrak{u}, \mathfrak{u}, \mathfrak{d}]$, neutron $\mathfrak{n} = [\mathfrak{u}, \mathfrak{d}, \mathfrak{d}]$ — together with the up/down-type character of its crossing with a partner. Read as a whole, the last column enumerates the three distinct partner-pairing channels ($\mathfrak{p}$-$\mathfrak{p}$, $\mathfrak{n}$-$\mathfrak{n}$, $\mathfrak{p}$-$\mathfrak{n}$), not a per-nucleon attribute: the two same-type channels pair like with like, while $\mathfrak{p}$-$\mathfrak{n}$ pairs an up-type with a down-type.

| Nucleon | Composition | Crossing types with a partner $\mathfrak{p}$ or $\mathfrak{n}$ |
|---------|------------|---------------------------------------------------------------|
| Proton $\mathfrak{p}$ | $[\mathfrak{u}, \mathfrak{u}, \mathfrak{d}]$ | $\mathfrak{p}$-$\mathfrak{p}$: both up-type |
| Neutron $\mathfrak{n}$ | $[\mathfrak{u}, \mathfrak{d}, \mathfrak{d}]$ | $\mathfrak{n}$-$\mathfrak{n}$: both down-type |
| Mixed | (one of each) | $\mathfrak{p}$-$\mathfrak{n}$: up meets down |
