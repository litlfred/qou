### Tetrahedral symmetry

*The LP dual selects a strand ordering with $S_4$ symmetry (permuting the four hydrogens). In the optimal ordering, the four H atoms are distributed **symmetrically** around carbon's strands. This maximises the inter-hydrogen distance (number of intervening strands). This provides the braid-theoretic origin of the tetrahedral geometry of methane.*

*The $H$-$H$ interactions are:*
- *$\mathfrak{p}_{H_i}\mathfrak{p}_{H_j}$: EM repulsion ($\sigma$)*
- *$e_{H_i} e_{H_j}$: EM repulsion ($\sigma$)*
- *$\mathfrak{p}_{H_i} e_{H_j}$: EM attraction ($\sigma^{-1}$) — a **non-bonding** attraction (no shared pair)*

*Minimising $\Phi_1$ over strand orderings distributes the 4 H atoms to maximise separation of repulsive crossings, producing the $S_4$-symmetric (tetrahedral) arrangement.*

*The observed C--H bond dissociation energy is $D_0 \approx 4.48 \;\mathrm{eV}$ per bond (total atomisation energy $\approx 17.9 \;\mathrm{eV}$).*
