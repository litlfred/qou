The covalent crossing eigenvalue ratio $q/q^{-1} = q^2 \approx 1.231$ is closer to unity than the dressed atomic ratio $q^5/q^{-5} = q^{10} \approx 2.826$. The covalent channel exhibits less destructive interference than intra-atomic channels. The shared electron reduces $\Phi_1$ relative to two isolated atoms.

### Strand orderings

The LP dual optimizes over all $4! = 24$ permutations of $[\mathfrak{p}_1, e_1, \mathfrak{p}_2, e_2]$. By the symmetry $(\mathfrak{p}_1, e_1) \leftrightarrow (\mathfrak{p}_2, e_2)$, there are 12 distinct orderings. The optimal ordering places the electron-proton pairs interleaved:

$$
  [\mathfrak{p}_1, e_2, \mathfrak{p}_2, e_1]
$$

Each electron is adjacent to the covalent partner proton. This maximizes the number of attractive $\sigma^{-1}$ crossings in adjacent positions and minimizes $\Phi_1$.
