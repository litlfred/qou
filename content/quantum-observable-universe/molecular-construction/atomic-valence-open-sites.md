**Definition.**
*The* **valence** *of an atom is the number of open bonding sites available for covalent bonds:*

$$
  v(Z) = \min\bigl(Z_{\mathrm{val}},\; 2n_{\mathrm{val}}^2 - Z_{\mathrm{val}}\bigr)
$$

*where $Z_{\mathrm{val}}$ is the number of valence electrons and $n_{\mathrm{val}}$ is the outermost shell level.*

| Atom | $Z$ | Config | $v(Z)$ | Open sites |
|:-----|:----|:-------|:-------|:-----------|
| H | 1 | $1s^1$ | 1 | 1 |
| C | 6 | $[He]\,2s^2 2p^2$ | 4 | 4 |
| N | 7 | $[He]\,2s^2 2p^3$ | 3 | 3 (+1 lone pair) |
| O | 8 | $[He]\,2s^2 2p^4$ | 2 | 2 (+2 lone pairs) |

*A* **lone pair** *is an electron pair whose $\Phi_1$ contribution is lower when bound to a single nucleus than when shared. The linear program (LP) dual assigns electrons to bonding versus lone-pair positions by comparing the $\Phi_1$ cost of sharing versus retaining.*
