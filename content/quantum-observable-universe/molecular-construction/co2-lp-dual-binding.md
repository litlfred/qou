**Proposition (conditional on [`conj:q-integer-mass-formula`](#conj:q-integer-mass-formula)).**
*The LP dual of the $CO_2$ molecular braid selects the symmetric linear arrangement $O{=}C{=}O$ with two double bonds as the ground state. It minimises $\Phi_1$ over all bond order assignments $(k_1, k_2)$ with $k_1 + k_2 \leq 4$ (carbon's valence), all electron-bond assignments, and all strand orderings (permutations of 66 strands).*

The LP dual generates three shadow prices: $y_{\sigma}$ (the value of each $\sigma$-bond shared pair), $y_{\pi}$ (the value of each $\pi$-bond shared pair), and $y_{\mathrm{sym}}$ (the value of symmetric bond placement). 

The symmetric double-bond configuration $(k_1, k_2) = (2, 2)$ dominates the asymmetric alternatives $(3, 1)$ and $(1, 3)$. Each double bond contributes four attractive crossings. The symmetric arrangement minimises $O_1$–$O_2$ repulsion by maximally separating the protons. The asymmetric $(3, 1)$ configuration increases $e$–$e$ repulsion by concentrating electron density on one side, while leaving one of oxygen's valence sites unoccupied.
