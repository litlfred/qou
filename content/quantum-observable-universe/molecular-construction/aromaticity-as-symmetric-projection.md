The [existence proposition](#prop:assembly-projector-exists) is
deliberately silent about energy.  Stability is a *separate*, three-step
chain, and keeping the steps distinct is what makes the aromatic-stability
claim falsifiable rather than circular.

1. **Existence** (algebra).  For any $\lambda$ the symmetric-block
   projector $e_{\lambda,(N)}$ exists — true of every molecule, stable or
   not.
2. **Optimality** (the physics).  Among admissible configurations the
   binding LP/SDP *selects* the one minimising the trace objective under
   Frobenius positivity.  Stability is this selection: the projected state
   is an energy minimum because it is the LP optimum, not because the
   projector exists.
3. **Energetics**.  The binding energy is the trace-weight *gap* between
   the selected block and the next configuration.  For the six-carbon ring
   the threaded $T(2,6)$ configuration carries Alexander weight
   $\approx 3$ while the open chain carries weight $5$; the gap $5 - 3 =
   2$, scaled by the molecular energy unit, is the ring-closure/resonance
   energy ([ring-closure barrier](#prop:ring-closure-barrier)).

**Why breaking the ring releases a large, concentrated energy.**  The
ring's projector $e_{(2,2,2),(6)}$ is *irreducible* over all six strands:
the delocalised $\pi$-system is a single object, not six separable bonds.
One cannot open the ring a single bond at a time — breaking it collapses
the entire symmetric block back to the localised chain, paying the whole
weight-gap at once (cooperatively).  An open chain breaks one localised
bond at a time, at small local cost; the ring resists as a unit, at large
concentrated cost.  This irreducibility of the global projector is the
categorical content of aromatic stability — benzene declining to behave as
three independent double bonds.

The account is testable, not retrofitted: step 2 requires the binding LP
to actually select $\mu = (N)$ for the ring geometry (computable with no
chemical foreknowledge), and step 3 requires the weight-gap times the
molecular scale to match the measured resonance energy — the
[$T(2,N)$ threading link](#def:threading-link) supplying the $N/2$ weight.
