Stability requires three steps:

1. **Existence**. For any $\lambda$ the symmetric-block projector $e_{\lambda,(N)}$ exists.
2. **Optimality**. Among admissible configurations, the binding LP/SDP selects the one minimising the trace objective under Frobenius positivity. Stability is this selection.
3. **Energetics**. The binding energy is the trace-weight gap between the selected block and the next configuration. For the six-carbon ring, the threaded $T(2,6)$ configuration carries Alexander weight $\approx 3$ while the open chain carries weight $5$. The gap $5 - 3 = 2$, scaled by the molecular energy unit, is the ring-closure resonance energy.

The ring's projector $e_{(2,2,2),(6)}$ is irreducible over all six strands. One cannot open the ring a single bond at a time. Breaking it collapses the entire symmetric block back to the localised chain, paying the whole weight-gap cooperatively. This irreducibility of the global projector is the categorical content of aromatic stability.
