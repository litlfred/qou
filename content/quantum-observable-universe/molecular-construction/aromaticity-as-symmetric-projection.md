We distinguish the existence of the symmetric projector from its energetic stability. The binding model separates this into three steps. This separation ensures the aromatic-stability claim is falsifiable rather than circular.

1. **Existence** (algebra). For any strand partition $\lambda$, the symmetric-block projector $e_{\lambda,(N)}$ exists. This holds for every molecule, stable or not.
2. **Optimality** (physics). Among admissible configurations, the binding linear program (LP) selects the configuration minimizing the trace objective under Frobenius positivity. Stability corresponds to this selection: the projected state is an energy minimum because it is the LP optimum.
3. **Energetics**. The binding energy is the trace-weight gap between the selected block and the next admissible configuration. For the six-carbon ring, the threaded $T(2,6)$ configuration carries Alexander weight $\approx 3$, while the open chain carries weight $5$. The gap $5 - 3 = 2$, scaled by the molecular energy unit, yields the ring-closure resonance energy ([ring-closure barrier](#prop:ring-closure-barrier)).

**Energy released by breaking the ring.** The ring's projector $e_{(2,2,2),(6)}$ is irreducible over all six strands. The delocalised $\pi$-system is a single mathematical object, not six separable bonds. We cannot open the ring a single bond at a time. Breaking the ring collapses the entire symmetric block back to the localised chain, requiring the entire weight gap to be paid cooperatively. An open chain breaks one localised bond at a time at small local cost. The ring resists as a unit at large concentrated cost. This irreducibility of the global projector supplies the categorical content of aromatic stability. Benzene does not behave as three independent double bonds.

This account is testable. Step 2 requires the binding LP to select $\mu = (N)$ for the ring geometry without chemical foreknowledge. Step 3 requires the weight gap, scaled by the molecular energy unit, to match the measured resonance energy. The [$T(2,N)$ threading link](#def:threading-link) supplies the $N/2$ weight.
