Methane raises the coordination number from two to four. As a molecular braid (definition), methane ($\mathrm{CH_4}$) joins a central carbon to four hydrogens via four shared trefoils on the molecular strand set. The $S_4$-symmetric strand ordering minimizes $\Phi_1$ (proposition). The linear program (LP) cost is minimized by configurations invariant under $S_4$ rather than lower-symmetry arrangements. The LP dual reads this $S_4$ symmetry (remark) as the $109.5^\circ$ tetrahedral bond angle. The same $\Phi_1$ minimization that dictates linear $\mathrm{CO_2}$ determines the tetrahedral geometry of $\mathrm{CH_4}$.
