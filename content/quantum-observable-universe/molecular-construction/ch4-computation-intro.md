Methane raises the coordination number from two to four. Methane $CH_4$ as a molecular braid joins a central carbon to four hydrogens by four shared trefoils on the molecular strand set. The $S_4$-symmetric strand ordering minimises $\Phi_1$ for $CH_4$. Among all ways of arranging the four bond strands, the LP cost is minimised by configurations invariant under $S_4$. We read this $S_4$ symmetry as the $109.5^\circ$ tetrahedral bond angle. The same $\Phi_1$ minimisation that produced linear $CO_2$ now selects the tetrahedral geometry of $CH_4$.
