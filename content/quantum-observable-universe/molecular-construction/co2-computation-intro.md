We test triatomic geometry on carbon dioxide. The $CO_2$ molecular braid couples a central carbon to two oxygens, each sharing an electron pair on the molecular Hecke strands. The LP dual selects the linear $O{=}C{=}O$ geometry by placing the two $C{=}O$ trefoils on opposite sides of the central strand. This configuration identifies $\Phi_1$ minimisation as the fundamental origin of VSEPR molecular geometry. The linear triatomic case isolates molecular geometry as a consequence of $\Phi_1$ minimisation, independent of bond order.
