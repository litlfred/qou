We turn to the smallest covalent molecule. The hydrogen molecule $H_2$ as a braid in $H_4(q)$ (def) places two protons and two electrons on four strands and lets them share a single trefoil. The LP dual gives optimal $H_2$ binding (prop) selects the bond order and reads off the binding energy, $H_2$ binding energy from the LP dual (rem) compares the prediction with the spectroscopic value, and $H_2$ bond length from the covalent channel eigenvalue ratio (rem) extracts the equilibrium internuclear separation from the ratio of LP eigenvalues. This sets the template for the heteronuclear and polyatomic cases that follow.
