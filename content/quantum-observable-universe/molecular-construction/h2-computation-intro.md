We turn to the smallest covalent molecule. The hydrogen molecule $H_2$ places two protons and two electrons on four strands in $H_4(q)$, sharing a single trefoil. The LP dual selects the bond order and determines the binding energy. We compare this predicted energy with spectroscopic values, and extract the equilibrium internuclear separation from the covalent channel eigenvalue ratio. This framework extends to heteronuclear and polyatomic cases.
