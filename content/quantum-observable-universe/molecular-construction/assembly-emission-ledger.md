*Assembling benzene from separated atoms releases the binding energy as a net emission:*

$$
  \mathfrak{E}_{\mathrm{emit}}
    = \bigl(\mathrm{NF}_{\text{final}} - \mathrm{NF}_{\text{initial}}\bigr)\,
      \mathfrak{E}_0 .
$$

*The change in normal-form crossing count is scaled by the per-crossing energy $\mathfrak{E}_0$. Ring closure delocalises six $\pi$ electrons, supplying a $+\tfrac{1}{2}$ bond-order increment on each of the six $\mathrm{C\!-\!C}$ bonds (witness `benzene-assembly-spine`). This releases three bond-orders of aromatic stabilisation on closure.*

*The emission splits into two channels. The **photon** channel is categorical. It corresponds to the [trivial-knot electromagnetic mode](#rem:trivial-knot-photon-interpretation), carrying the quantised normal-form change. The **phonon** channel is archimedean, describing lattice and thermal effects. The ledger is therefore partial. The normal-form crossing change dictates the robust cardinality of the photon channel. The absolute energy partition between photon and phonon awaits the lateral-$\pi$ overlap weight $w_\perp$ of the [embeddable knot operator](#def:embeddable-knot-operator). This cardinality-robust, energy-pending split matches the bond model.*
