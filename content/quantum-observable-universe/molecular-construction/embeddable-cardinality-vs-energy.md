Overlap weighting alters only the energy, never the channel count. Every channel with $w > 0$ is occupied. The active-set cardinality—the bond order of the $CO$ LP dual—is therefore independent of the weights (Lean: `multipleBond_length`). 

The equal-channel model ($w \equiv 1$) overbinds. For the $\sigma + 2\pi$ triple bond, it yields $1.42\times$ the observed $D_0(\mathrm{N_2})$. Introducing a lateral weight $w_\perp \in [0.3, 0.7]$ brackets the experimental value (witness `embeddable-knot-operator`).
