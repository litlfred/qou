# Molecular braid progression: H₂ → CO → CO₂ → CH₄ → C₂H₆ → C₆H₆

F13.2 (P0). A 2 × 3 grid of molecular cells. Each cell shows the closed braid representation of one molecule, with the constituent atoms drawn as black-stroked circles labelled C, H, or O, and the *shared sub-Hopf links* (covalent bonds) drawn as red circles bridging the atomic rings. Below each cell: the predicted binding energy in eV and a short note. Faint arrows between adjacent cells suggest the iterative construction.
