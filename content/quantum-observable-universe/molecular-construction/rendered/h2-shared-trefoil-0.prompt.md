# H₂ as a shared trefoil bond

F13.1 (P0). Two stylised hydrogen atoms drawn as trefoils ($3_1$) on left and right; between them a red shared circle representing a single sub-Hopf link bridging the two trefoils — the categorical realisation of a covalent bond. Each H atom carries the label "trefoil $3_1$"; the shared bond carries the label "covalent bond = shared sub-Hopf link" in red italic. Beneath the molecular picture: a horizontal axis with a black bar representing the LP-dual binding energy $E_{\mathrm{bind}}(\mathrm{H}_2) \approx 4.48$ eV (predicted ≈ experimental). Footer: "one shared trefoil binds two H atoms; LP optimisation finds the dual at the magic-number gap". This figure introduces the molecular-construction picture: covalent bonds are not new mathematics; they are shared sub-links between the atomic trefoils.
