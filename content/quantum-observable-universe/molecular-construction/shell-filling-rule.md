*The ground-state electron configuration of atom $(Z, N)$ is the assignment of $Z$ electron trefoils to bonding sites that minimises the total $\Phi_1$ of the atomic braid, reproducing the aufbau principle. The LP dual of the electron assignment problem is:*

$$
  \max_{y} \; Z \cdot y_0
  \quad\text{s.t.}\quad
  y_0 + \sum_{n} G_{n,s} \, y_{n,s} \leq \Phi_1^{(s)}
  \;\;\forall\, \text{sites } s
$$

*where $\Phi_1^{(s)}$ is the $\Phi_1$ contribution of placing an electron at site $s$, and $G_{n,s}$ encodes the Gram-matrix penalty for electron-electron repulsion at shell $n$.*
