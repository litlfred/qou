We test the heteronuclear template on carbon monoxide. The $CO$ molecular braid connects the $(6,6)$ carbon braid to the $(8,8)$ oxygen braid, sharing three electron pairs on a common strand set. The LP dual selects the triple bond as optimal. We extract the $CO$ triple bond energy from the dominant Wedderburn block weight. Heteronuclear bonding requires no new machinery beyond §3; it depends only on the asymmetric strand counts of the two atomic factors.
