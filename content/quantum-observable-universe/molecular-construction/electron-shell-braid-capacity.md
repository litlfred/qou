**Definition.**
*In the atomic braid $\mathcal{A}(Z, N)$, each of the $Z$ electrons forms a trefoil $T_{2,3} = \sigma_0\sigma_1\sigma_0$ in $H_3(q)$, Hopf-linked to the nuclear core. Each electron trefoil occupies a **bonding site**—a position in the atomic braid. The **shell capacity** of the atom defines the number of bonding sites available at each shell level. This capacity is determined by the electron arrangement that minimises $\Phi_1$.*

An atom with $Z$ electrons fills its shells in order of increasing $\Phi_1$ contribution.
