We extend the atomic constructor to multi-atom systems. The molecular braid $H_{N_{\mathrm{mol}}}(q)$ (def) joins pre-computed atomic braids within a single Hecke algebra. Inter-atomic crossing coefficients (rem) govern interactions between strands of distinct nuclei. 

Two atoms form a covalent bond by sharing a single electron trefoil (def). The ILP characterisation of the covalent bond (prop) identifies this shared trefoil as the inverse trefoil $T_{2,3}^{-1}$. This minimal-crossing knot operator provides positive binding energy. These structures allow us to construct arbitrary molecules by Hopf-linking atoms.
