We extend the atomic constructor to multi-atom systems. The molecular braid joins pre-computed atomic braids inside a single Hecke algebra. Inter-atomic crossing coefficients record how strands from different nuclei interact. Two atoms share a single electron trefoil to form a covalent bond. The ILP characterisation of the covalent bond identifies that shared trefoil as the inverse trefoil $T_{2,3}^{-1}$, the minimum-crossing knot operator with positive binding energy. With these four objects in hand, we compute a molecule by Hopf-linking atoms.
