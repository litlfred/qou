*Interpretation of the [H$_2$O molecular braid](#def:h2o-molecular-braid).*

The two hydrogen sub-units of water carry equal strand counts. The exchange $H_1 \leftrightarrow H_2$ fixes the strand-partition $\lambda = (24,2,2)$ and hence the assembly projector $e_{\lambda,\mu}$. That $S_2$ stabilizer establishes the $C_{2v}$ point symmetry of the molecule — a $C_2$ rotation about the bisector of the H–O–H angle, with oxygen fixed.

A structure's point symmetry is the stabilizer of its assembly projector. This mechanism gives benzene's three equal acetylene units the ring symmetry $S_3$ (via $e_{(2,2,2),(6)}$). The assembly projector acts as the structure-forming operator. Its invariance group defines the structure's symmetry. Water and benzene stand as the $S_2$ and $S_3$ instances of one categorical statement. This principle extends from molecular point groups to the symmetries of phases of matter when applied to the inter-molecular operator forming a condensed phase.
