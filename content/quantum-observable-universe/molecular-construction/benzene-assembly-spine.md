*Light initiates the assembly. The Breit–Wheeler process [$\gamma\gamma \to e^+e^-$](#rem:breit-wheeler-observation) seeds a lepton pair. That energy feeds [quark–antiquark production](#def:quark-antiquark-production), whose confinement closes into the [Borromean nucleon](#def:f-bor-borromean-observable) and, by [meson exchange](#rem:meson-to-proton-via-borromean), the proton. Twelve nucleons bind into a $^{12}\mathrm{C}$ atom (the $A \ge 3$ binding closure). Carbon's four octet channels then assemble molecules.*

*The molecular layer contains no free parameters. Each covalent bond order corresponds to the cardinality of the saturated orthogonal-channel set (the [bond order $=$ LP active set](#prop:co-lp-dual-binding) identity). The fractional $\pi$ of an aromatic ring corresponds to the lateral-$\pi$ overlap of the [embeddable knot operator](#def:embeddable-knot-operator), delocalised by the [assembly projector](#def:molecular-assembly-projector).*

### Validated predictions

*Executing the spine end-to-end (witness `benzene-assembly-spine`) reproduces the observed structural bond orders with no fitted constants:*

| Species | Construction | Predicted | Observed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Acetylene $\mathrm{C_2H_2}$ | $\sigma \perp \pi_x \perp \pi_y$ orthogonal triple | $3$ | $3$ |
| Benzene $\mathrm{C_6H_6}$ | $\lambda=(2,2,2)\vdash 6$, $D_{6h}$ ring | $\tfrac{3}{2}$ | $\tfrac{3}{2}$ |
| Cyclohexane $\mathrm{C_6H_{12}}$ | Saturated control | $1$ | $1$ |

*Acetylene's $\mathrm{C}\!\equiv\!\mathrm{C}$ triple bond fills all three orthogonal channels (one axial $\sigma$, two transverse $\pi$). The spatial-dimension bound caps the bond order at three. Benzene's six $\mathrm{C\!-\!C}$ bonds are identical. The $D_{6h}$ symmetry of the assembly projector forces zero spread. Bond equalisation is therefore a consequence of the construction, not an assumption. Aromaticity registers as a $+\tfrac{1}{2}$ bond-order increment per bond over the saturated ring, comprising one localised $\sigma$ plus a half-share of the delocalised six-electron $\pi$ system.*
