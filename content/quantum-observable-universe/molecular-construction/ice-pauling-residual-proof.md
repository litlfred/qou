**Proof.** A water molecule assigns its two donated hydrogens among its four bonds in $\binom{4}{2}=6$ ways. Each bond is shared with a neighbor. Based on the independent estimate, each bond carries exactly one hydrogen with probability $\tfrac12$. This yields $W_1 = 6\cdot(\tfrac12)^2 = \tfrac32$. The Lean sibling proves $\binom{4}{2}=6$ and $\binom{4}{2}(\tfrac12)^2 = \tfrac32$ exactly over $\mathbb{Q}$. The entropy $S = Nk\ln W_1 = R\ln\tfrac32$ provides the archimedean evaluation. $\qquad\blacksquare$
