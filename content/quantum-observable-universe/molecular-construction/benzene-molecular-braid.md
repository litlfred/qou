**Definition.**
*Benzene ($\mathrm{C_6H_6}$) is the molecular braid of six carbon atoms and six hydrogen atoms, assembled iteratively from pre-computed atomic braids.*

### Strand count

- *6 carbon atoms: $6 \times 18 = 108$ strands*
- *6 hydrogen atoms: $6 \times 2 = 12$ strands*
- *Total: $N_{\mathrm{mol}} = 120$ strands, inhabiting $H_{120}(q)$*

### Bond structure

*Each carbon possesses valence 4. In benzene, each carbon contributes:*
- *2 electrons shared with adjacent carbons ($\mathrm{C\!-\!C}$ bonds)*
- *1 electron shared with hydrogen ($\mathrm{C\!-\!H}$ bond)*
- *1 electron to $\pi$ delocalization*

*This yields:*
- *6 $\mathrm{C\!-\!C}$ $\sigma$ bonds (single, 1 shared pair each)*
- *6 $\mathrm{C\!-\!H}$ $\sigma$ bonds (single, 1 shared pair each)*
- *6 $\pi$ electrons delocalized around the ring*

### Iterative construction

1. *Build the $\mathrm{C_1\!-\!H_1}$ unit. Combine $\mathcal{A}_{C_1}$ and the covalent bond with $H_1$, reducing to normal form (NF) in $H_{20}(q)$.*
2. *Add $\mathrm{C_2\!-\!H_2}$. Embed the $\mathrm{C_1\!-\!C_2}$ $\sigma$ bond and the $\mathrm{C_2\!-\!H_2}$ bond, reducing in $H_{40}(q)$.*
3. *Iterate for $\mathrm{C_3\!-\!H_3}$ through $\mathrm{C_5\!-\!H_5}$, reducing at each step.*
4. *Close the ring. Add $\mathrm{C_6\!-\!H_6}$ and the $\mathrm{C_6\!-\!C_1}$ $\sigma$ bond to form the cyclic structure, reducing in $H_{120}(q)$.*
5. *Add $\pi$ electrons. The six electrons form a [threading link](#prop:benzene-threading-emergence) $T(2, 6)$ around the ring.*

*Step 5 is critical. The threading link is not postulated; it emerges from the LP dual as the optimal configuration for the remaining six electrons.*
