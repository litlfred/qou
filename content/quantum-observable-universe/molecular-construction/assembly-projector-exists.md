**Proposition.**
*For every [strand-partition](#def:molecular-strand-partition) $\lambda$ of $N$, the symmetric-block [assembly projector](#def:molecular-assembly-projector) $e_{\lambda,(N)} \in H_N(q)$ exists. This is the $q$-Young symmetriser onto the one-row block $\mu = (N)$. It is idempotent, $e_{\lambda,(N)}^2 = e_{\lambda,(N)}$, for generic $q$.*

This constitutes the $q$-deformation of the classical Young symmetriser. The idempotent is Wenzl's projector. It exists for all $N$ at generic $q$ \cite{wenzl1988,kassel1995}.
