**Definition.** Let $\lambda$ be a [strand-partition](#def:molecular-strand-partition) of $N$ and $\mu \vdash N$ a Wedderburn block of $H_N(q)$. The **assembly projector** $e_{\lambda,\mu} \in H_N(q)$ is the canonical idempotent ($e_{\lambda,\mu}^2 = e_{\lambda,\mu}$) projecting the composite's module — built over the [parabolic subalgebra](#def:parabolic-hecke-subalgebra) $H_\lambda(q)$ — onto the irreducible block $\mu$.

This operator is the $q$-Young symmetrizer (Wenzl projector) for the pair $(\lambda,\mu)$. It $q$-deforms the classical Young symmetrizer. The explicit $N=3$ instances are the [$H_3(q)$ idempotents](#rem:h3-hecke-idempotents). The distinguished case $\mu = (N)$ is projection onto the one-row symmetric block. This represents delocalization: the composite's shared content spreads symmetrically across all $N$ strands rather than localizing in pairwise sub-blocks.

For benzene, $e_{(2,2,2),(6)}$ projects the six $\pi$-electrons onto the symmetric block. This represents the categorical content of the aromatic ring. Geometrically, it realizes the $T(2,6)$ threading link. Reordering the three sub-units (via the $S_3$ permuting the acetylene units inside the $S_6$ of the carbon ring) leaves $e_{(2,2,2),(6)}$ invariant. That invariance is the algebraic expression of the ring's cyclic symmetry.
