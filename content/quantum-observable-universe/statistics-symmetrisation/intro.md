This chapter develops the categorical derivation of fermion/boson
statistics and Pauli exclusion from the rigid braided monoidal structure
of the substrate. The braided structure resolves the action of the
symmetric group $S_n$ into two distinct representations — the
antisymmetric one (fermions) and the symmetric one (trivial / bosons) —
and the rigid-duality constraint then forces antisymmetric states to
satisfy the exclusion principle.
