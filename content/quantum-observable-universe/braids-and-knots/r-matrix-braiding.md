To replace the classical twist/flip involutions ([braid twist-flip](#def:braid-twist-flip)), we deform the braid group representation. The universal $R$-matrix of $\mathcal{U}_q(\mathfrak{su}(2))$ ([quantum SU(2)](#def:quantum-su2)) is the element

$$
  \mathcal{R} \in 
  \mathcal{U}_q(\mathfrak{su}(2))\hat{\otimes}
  \mathcal{U}_q(\mathfrak{su}(2))
$$

that satisfies the Yang--Baxter equation $\mathcal{R}_{12}\mathcal{R}_{13}\mathcal{R}_{23} =\mathcal{R}_{23}\mathcal{R}_{13}\mathcal{R}_{12}$. Its action on a tensor product of representations gives the braid operator $\check{R}=P\circ\mathcal{R}$, where $P$ is the tensor swap.
