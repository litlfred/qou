**Corollary (shell-closed knot factor).**
*For $Z = N$ even-even nuclei, the Markov chain on the three
$H_3(q)$ partitions
([three-parameters Wedderburn](#prop:three-parameters-wedderburn))
returns to equilibrium after all inter-nucleon crossings: the
proton-proton and neutron-neutron character shifts cancel exactly.
In the spectral language, $\beta \cdot \beta^*$ projects entirely
onto the trivial idempotent $p_{(n)}$:*

$$
  c_\lambda(\beta \cdot \beta^*)
  \;=\;
  \delta_{\lambda, (n)} \cdot c_{(n)}(\mathbf{1})^2,
  \qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
  a_K \;=\; 1.
$$

*The result is exact algebraic cancellation, not approximation.*

*Proof.* Under the $Z = N$ even-even constraint, every neutron
crossing is matched by a proton crossing of opposite sign, and
the Wigner pairing
([Wigner $np$ pairing](#prop:wigner-np-pairing)) annihilates all
non-trivial $\lambda$-block contributions.  The only surviving
projection is onto the trivial irrep $p_{(n)}$, on which
$c_{(n)}(\beta \cdot \beta^*) = c_{(n)}(\mathbf{1})^2$.  The
ratio in
([knot factor spectral form](#prop:knot-factor-spectral-form))
is $1$.  $\square$

**Application.** ${}^4\mathrm{He}$, ${}^{12}\mathrm{C}$,
${}^{16}\mathrm{O}$, ${}^{40}\mathrm{Ca}$, ${}^{56}\mathrm{Fe}$,
${}^{208}\mathrm{Pb}$ all sit in this regime; each has $a_K = 1$
by Markov-chain equilibrium.
