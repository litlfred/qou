Kronheimer and Mrowka proved that the fundamental group of every non-trivial knot admits a non-trivial representation into $\mathrm{SU}(2)$, and hence into $\mathrm{PSL}(2,\mathbb{C})$. In the QOU context, this theorem guarantees that every non-trivial knot type contributing to the fibre bundle carries a non-abelian gauge holonomy. The restriction to traceless elements of $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ aligns with the category-theoretic setup of [Section](#sec:enveloping-algebras), where the representation factors through the adjoint form.
