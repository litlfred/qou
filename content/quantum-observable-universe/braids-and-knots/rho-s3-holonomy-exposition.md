The representation $\rho \colon B_3 \to \mathrm{SU}(2)$ specifies the exact holonomy of the symmetric group $S_3$ acting on the branched cover $\mathcal{S} \to \mathbb{P}^1(\mathbb{C})$. It determines how the spectral Lagrange resolvents $w_k$ are physically permuted when transported around the branch points.

The chiral sheets of the geometry are defined by these spectral coordinates. The conjugate pairs $(w_1, w_4)$ and $(w_2, w_3)$ span two-dimensional eigenspaces of the cyclic action. They tile the four non-trivial modes into time-like and space-like sectors. The representation $\rho$ acts through two elementary operations:

1. **The twist $\sigma_1$.** The generator $\sigma_1$ swaps the conjugate pairs. In the $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ embedding, this is a rotation by $T\varTheta$ around the $e_1$ axis. Physically, $\sigma_1$ rotates the time-like pairs carrying the dynamical phase of the Reeb flow, leaving the overall chirality unchanged.
2. **The flip $\sigma_2$.** The generator $\sigma_2$ reverses the full tuple. In $\mathrm{SU}(2)$, this is a rotation around $e_2$ that reflects the space-like axis. The flip exchanges the two chirality sectors, implementing the geometric equivalent of parity inversion.

Together, $\sigma_1$ and $\sigma_2$ generate the symmetric group $S_3\cong D_3$ acting on the resolvent tuple. Their composition $\sigma_1\sigma_2$ is the $3$-cycle $\alpha$ of [the $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ generators](#def:su2-generators), whose cube returns to the identity on $\mathrm{SO}(3)$. The full $S_3$ holonomy is generated by these rotations and reflections of the chiral sheets.

Whenever $\rho$ appears in braid-group representations or knot invariants, it acts physically by twisting the time-like modes and reflecting the space-like axis of the chiral sheets.
