The trefoil knot $K_{3,2}$ is the simplest non-trivial torus knot. Its complement has fundamental group

$$
  \pi_1(S^3\setminus K_{3,2})
  = \langle x,y\mid xyx=yxy\rangle
   \cong B_3.
$$

The trefoil-knot group is isomorphic to the braid group on three strands ([braid group](#def:braid-group)). The defining condition $xyx=yxy$ is the braid relation. It encodes the topological fact that the three-strand braid closes into the trefoil by identifying the top and bottom of the braid.
