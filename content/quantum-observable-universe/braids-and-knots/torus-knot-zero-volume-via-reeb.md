The two 3-cycle braid words $\sigma_1\sigma_2$ and

The resulting trajectory in $\mathcal{R}_p \cong S^3$ is a

**Torus-knot trajectory.**  Parametrising the Heegaard torus by
$(\alpha, \beta) \in (\mathbb{R}/2\pi\mathbb{Z})^2$ — with $\alpha$
the meridional and $\beta$ the longitudinal direction — the
3-cycle trajectory traces the torus knot $T_{2,3}$ (trefoil) when
the Reeb flow completes the $2\pi/3$ angular rotation across two
latitude windings; the chiral pair $\sigma_1\sigma_2$ vs
$\sigma_2\sigma_1$ corresponds to the right- and left-handed
trefoils respectively.

**JSJ verdict.**  By the Thurston geometric decomposition, the
complement $\mathcal{R}_p \setminus \nu(T_{m,n})$ of any torus
knot admits a Seifert fibration over the once-punctured disc
with two exceptional fibres of orders $m, n$.  The Seifert piece
is rigid and admits no hyperbolic structure, so
$V_{\mathrm{hyp}}(T_{m,n}) = 0$ identically — this is the
geometric meaning of
[Thurston volume additivity](#prop:thurston-volume-additivity)
in the torus regime, where every shape parameter $z_c$ collapses
to a real number and the Bloch–Wigner sum vanishes term-by-term.

**Why the 3-cycle is exactly the torus regime.**  Three features
of the global-vortex flow conspire to keep its complement
Seifert-fibered:

1. *Uniform angular rate* on all three sectors keeps the strands
   parallel inside $\mathcal{R}_p$; they never cross each other
   inside the bundle.
2. *Hopf-fibre alignment* — the global vortex's axis coincides
   with the fibre direction of the Hopf bundle, so the strands
   thread along a coherent Seifert family.
3. *Heegaard-torus capture* — the trajectory closes onto the
   Heegaard torus before reaching the foundry boundary, so the
   complement decomposes as two solid tori glued along $T^2$
   minus the torus-knot neighbourhood, all of which admits a
   Seifert structure.
