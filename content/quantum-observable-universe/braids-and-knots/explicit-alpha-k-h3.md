**Proposition (closed-form $\alpha_k$ at $H_3(R, q)$).**
*The four curvature-rank prefactors at $H_3(R, q)$ in the unified
mass formula
([unified mass formula](#prop:unified-curvature-rank-mass-formula))
are explicit $q$-algebraic expressions.  Setting $z = 1/(\sqrt q +
1/\sqrt q)$ (Markov parameter) and $h = q - q^{-1}$:*

| $k$ | partition stratum $\Lambda_k$ | $E_\infty^{0,0}$ representative | $\alpha_k = \langle \mathrm{tr}_M, r_k \rangle$ |
|---|---|---|---|
| $0$ | $\{(3)\}$ | $\mathbf{1}$ | $1$ |
| $1$ | $\{(2,1)\}$ | $\sigma_1 - \sigma_2$ | $0$ exactly |
| $2$ | $\{(2,1)\}$ | $(\sigma_1 \sigma_2 + \sigma_2 \sigma_1)/2$ | $z^2$ exactly |
| $3$ | $\{(1,1,1)\}$ | $\sigma_1 \sigma_2 \sigma_1$ | $h \cdot z^2 + z = z(1 + q^{1/2} - q^{-1/2})$ |

*All four are derived structurally — no fitting, no $\pi$, no
Catalan factors.  At $q_0 \approx 1.1097$: $\alpha_3 = 0.5514434$
verified numerically to better than $0.01\%$.*

**Derivation of $\alpha_1 = 0$.**
The depth-$1$ representative $\sigma_1 - \sigma_2$ is the unique (up
to scale) length-$1$ class function vanishing on the
trivial idempotent.  By cyclicity of $\mathrm{tr}_M$,
$\mathrm{tr}_M(\sigma_1) = \mathrm{tr}_M(\sigma_2) = z$, so
$\langle \mathrm{tr}_M, \sigma_1 - \sigma_2 \rangle = 0$ identically.
The vanishing is structural: depth-$1$ reflects a single-generator
parity that cancels under the symmetric Markov pairing.

**Derivation of $\alpha_2 = z^2$.**
The depth-$2$ symmetric combination $(\sigma_1 \sigma_2 + \sigma_2
\sigma_1)/2$ has Markov trace $\frac{1}{2}(z^2 + z^2) = z^2$ by the
length-$2$ pairing constraint.  No further $q$-correction at this
depth.

**Derivation of $\alpha_3 = h \cdot z^2 + z$.**
The longest reduced word $\sigma_1 \sigma_2 \sigma_1$ admits the
Hecke-relation simplification

$$
  \sigma_1 \sigma_2 \sigma_1 \;\stackrel{\text{cyclic}}{=}\;
  \sigma_1^2 \sigma_2 \;=\;
  (h \sigma_1 + 1)\, \sigma_2 \;=\;
  h \sigma_1 \sigma_2 + \sigma_2.
$$

Applying $\mathrm{tr}_M$ and using
$\mathrm{tr}_M(\sigma_1 \sigma_2) = z^2$,
$\mathrm{tr}_M(\sigma_2) = z$:

$$
  \alpha_3
  \;=\; h \cdot z^2 + z
  \;=\; z(1 + h \cdot z)
  \;=\; z(1 + q^{1/2} - q^{-1/2}).
$$

The last form uses $h \cdot z = (q - q^{-1})/(q^{1/2} + q^{-1/2}) =
q^{1/2} - q^{-1/2}$.
