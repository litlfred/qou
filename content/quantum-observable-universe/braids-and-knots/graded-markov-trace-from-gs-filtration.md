**Proposition (graded Markov trace from the GS-filtration).**
*Let $H_n(R, q) = R\langle\sigma_1, \ldots, \sigma_{n-1}\rangle / I_{\mathrm{Hecke}}$
be the Iwahori-Hecke algebra and let $J^\bullet$ be the Gröbner-
Shirshov filtration on it (the descending chain
$J^0 \supseteq J^1 \supseteq J^2 \supseteq \cdots$ where $J^\ell$ is
the ideal generated by GS leading-term monomials of length $\geq \ell$
under a chosen monomial order).
The Jones-Markov trace $\mathrm{tr}_M : H_n(R, q) \to R$ descends to
a sequence of induced traces*

$$
  \mathrm{tr}_{M, \ell}
  \;:\;
  J^\ell / J^{\ell+1}
  \;\longrightarrow\;
  R
$$

*on each graded piece, with the property that the original trace
recovers as a sum (telescoping)*

$$
  \mathrm{tr}_M(x)
  \;=\;
  \sum_{\ell \geq 0}
    \mathrm{tr}_{M, \ell}\bigl(\overline{x}_{\,\ell}\bigr),
\qquad
  \overline{x}_{\,\ell} \;:=\;
  \pi^\ell(x \bmod J^{\ell+1})
  \;\in\; J^\ell / J^{\ell+1},
$$

*where $\pi^\ell : J^\ell \twoheadrightarrow J^\ell / J^{\ell+1}$ is
the canonical projection. The sum is finite for every $x \in H_n(R, q)$
of bounded GS-length.*

## What this block replaces

The compute pipeline previously declared `gradedMarkovTrace` as
`noncomputable opaque ℕ → ℝ → ℝ`
([`gb-residue-spectral-sum.lean:32`](https://github.com/litlfred/qou/blob/main/content/quantum-observable-universe/braids-and-knots/gb-residue-spectral-sum.lean)).
That declaration was a placeholder — Lean accepted it as a black-box
real-valued function indexed by GS-level and $q$, but the narrative
side gave no recipe for what the function actually computes.

This proposition provides the recipe, **purely algebraically**:

1. Pick a monomial order on $H_n(R, q)$ in which the Hecke relations
   $\sigma_i^2 = (q - q^{-1}) \sigma_i + 1$ have unambiguous leading
   monomials.
2. Apply the Bokut-Shirshov diamond lemma / Buchberger procedure to
   compute the GS basis. This gives the filtration $J^\bullet$.
3. The Markov trace is well-defined on each quotient $J^\ell / J^{\ell+1}$
   because $\mathrm{tr}_M$ is constant on the equivalence classes
   modulo $J^{\ell+1}$ — a direct consequence of $\mathrm{tr}_M$
   being central (i.e., a class function on conjugacy classes of
   reduced GS words).

The graded Markov trace at level $\ell$ is therefore the restriction
of $\mathrm{tr}_M$ to the leading-term monomials of GS-degree exactly
$\ell$, taken modulo lower-order corrections. No meromorphic
continuation, no Archimedean analysis, no transcendental input is
required.

## Why this matters

- **Removes Archimedean leak.** The earlier `noncomputable opaque`
  declaration in Lean was opaque precisely because it was reaching
  into post-Archimedean structure (meromorphic continuation across
  $|q| = 1$) for justification. The GS filtration is purely algebraic
  in $\mathbb{Z}[q^{\pm 1}]$; the graded trace lives in $R$ at every

- **Provides the substrate** for the residue-at-root calculation
  ([residue-at-root proposition](#prop:residue-at-root-from-gs-filtration)),

- **Discharges audit P2.6** narrative-anchoring requirement

  every block previously citing `gradedMarkovTrace` opaquely now
  points at this proposition for the algebraic content.

## Relation to the GB spectral sequence

The graded pieces $J^\ell / J^{\ell+1}$ are exactly the
$E_1^{\ell, *}$ page of the GB-filtration spectral sequence
([GB spectral sequence](#prop:gb-spectral-sequence)). The induced
trace $\mathrm{tr}_{M, \ell}$ is the natural trace on $E_1^{\ell, *}$;
its persistence to $E_\infty^{\ell, *}$ is governed by the
spectral-sequence differentials. Finiteness of the spectral sequence
([conjecture: $E_2$-collapse](#prop:gb-spectral-sequence)) implies
that only finitely many graded traces are non-zero on any specific
$x \in H_n(R, q)$.

## Proof

The construction is mechanical:

1. **GS filtration exists.** $H_n(R, q)$ is a finitely-presented
   associative algebra over $R$; the Bokut-Shirshov-Buchberger
   algorithm terminates (because the Hecke relations are
   compatible-with-the-monomial-order in the sense of GS theory),
   producing a complete GS basis and an associated filtration
   $J^\bullet$. *Standard.*

2. **Markov trace descends.** The Markov trace $\mathrm{tr}_M$ is a
   class function: $\mathrm{tr}_M(xy) = \mathrm{tr}_M(yx)$ for all
   $x, y \in H_n(R, q)$ ([Jones-Markov trace](#thm:jones-markov-trace)).
   For $x \in J^\ell$, $\mathrm{tr}_M(x)$ depends only on the GS
   leading-term class of $x$ modulo $J^{\ell+1}$, because the GS
   reduction within $J^{\ell+1}$ produces commutators (lower-order
   corrections that vanish under the trace). Hence $\mathrm{tr}_M$
   defines a well-formed map $\mathrm{tr}_{M, \ell} : J^\ell / J^{\ell+1} \to R$.

3. **Telescoping recovers $\mathrm{tr}_M$.** Every $x \in H_n(R, q)$
   has a unique GS expansion as a sum of GS-monomials of various
   degrees; collecting by GS-degree and applying step 2 to each
   summand gives the telescoping identity. The sum is finite because
   any specific $x$ has bounded GS-length. *Direct.*

$\square$
