While the braid group is typically introduced through the topology of configuration spaces or knot complements, in the QOU framework braiding has a categorical origin. This origin is logically prior to any geometric realization. It carries over unchanged to non-archimedean settings ([Chapter 5](#chap:models-qou)) where knot complements in $S^3$ do not exist.

We organize this section into three layers. First, we promote the abstract braiding $c_{X,Y} : X \otimes Y \xrightarrow{\sim} Y \otimes X$ from the rigid monoidal structure to a substrate-parameterized family indexed by $q \in \mathrm{End}(\mathbf{1})$. We prove the existence and uniqueness of this family and verify the hexagon coherence conditions under the substrate flow. Next, we specialize the categorical braiding to the Reshetikhin-Turaev framework and extract the categorical $R$-matrix. Finally, we tie the categorical $R$-matrix to the Hecke-skein relation, which drives the subsequent normal-form computations.

The output is the fundamental categorical braiding apparatus: the substrate-parameterized $R$-matrix, hexagon coherence, and the Hecke-skein relation. Subsequent sections consume this apparatus directly.
