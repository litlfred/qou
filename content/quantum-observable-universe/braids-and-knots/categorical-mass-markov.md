The categorical mass definition
([mass identity](#def:mass-identity)) descends to the
Iwahori--Hecke algebra in exactly three steps, each
categorically clean.  The result is that **single-level
categorical mass equals the absolute value of the Markov
trace**.

## Statement

**Proposition.**
*Let $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ be a quantum observable universe
with Frobenius--Hopf object $A$, and let $\varrho \colon \mathbf{1} \to A$
be a closed cycle with braid representative $\beta \in B_n$.
Then the categorical mass
([mass identity](#def:mass-identity)) evaluated at a single
brane level satisfies*

$$
  \mathfrak{m}(\varrho)\big|_{\text{single level}}
  =
  \bigl|\mathrm{tr}_M\bigl(\rho(\beta)\bigr)\bigr|,
$$

*where $\mathrm{tr}_M$ is the Jones--Markov trace on $H_n(q)$.*

## Proof

The chain has three steps:

**Step 1 (Categorical trace → fibre functor).**
The categorical trace
$\mathrm{tr}(f) = \mathrm{ev}_A \circ (f \otimes \mathrm{id}_{A^\vee}) \circ \mathrm{coev}_A$
descends under the fibre functor $\tau$ to the Markov trace
$\mathrm{tr}_M$ on $H_n(q)$.  This is the identification
$\mathrm{tr} \mapsto \mathrm{tr}_M$ from the archimedean
descent
([RJ interpretation](#rem:rj-inner-product-interpretation)),
satisfying Markov properties I and II
([vertex algebra relations](#rem:vertex-algebra-relations),
QU-3).

**Step 2 (Nakayama automorphism = identity).**
The mass definition involves the endomorphism
$\Psi = (\int_A \otimes \mathrm{id}) \circ \Delta \colon A \to A$,
where $\Delta$ is the comultiplication and $\int_A$ the
Frobenius form.  This is the **Nakayama automorphism** of
the Frobenius algebra $A$.

For a Frobenius--Hopf algebra, the Nakayama automorphism
equals the square of the antipode: $\Psi = S^2$.  In $H_n(q)$,
the antipode is $S(T_i) = T_i^{-1}$ and therefore
$S^2(T_i) = T_i$.  Since $S^2 = \mathrm{id}$ on generators
and $H_n(q)$ is generated by $\{T_i\}$, we have
$\Psi = \mathrm{id}_A$.

**Step 3 (Mass = trace of cycle).**
Substituting $\Psi = \mathrm{id}$:

$$
  \mathfrak{m}(\varrho)
  =
  \bigl\lVert
  \mathrm{tr}\bigl(
    \underbrace{(\textstyle\int_A \otimes \mathrm{id}) \circ \Delta}_{= \,\mathrm{id}_A}
    \circ\, \varrho
  \bigr)
  \bigr\rVert
  =
  \bigl|\mathrm{tr}_M\bigl(\rho(\beta)\bigr)\bigr|.
$$

## Explicit values

Evaluating at $q_0 \approx 1.110$ with Markov parameter
$z = 1/(q^{1/2} + q^{-1/2}) \approx 0.499$ and
$h = q - q^{-1} \approx 0.209$:

| Particle | Braid $\beta$ | $\mathrm{tr}_M(\rho(\beta))$ | Symbolic |

| $\sigma_0$ (pol.) | $\sigma_0$ | $z \approx 0.499$ | $z$ |
| $L_+$ (gluon) | $\sigma_0\sigma_1$ | $z^2 \approx 0.249$ | $z^2$ |

| $4_1$ (figure-eight) | $(\sigma_0\sigma_1^{-1})^2$ | $\approx 0.182$ | (see witness) |

These are **dimensionless ratios** relative to the photon
($\mathfrak{m}(\gamma) = 1$).  To obtain physical masses in
MeV, multiply by a calibration constant $E_0$, determined
by matching one experimental value (e.g.\ the free neutron
beta-decay Q-value).

## Role in the unified mass formula

This proposition identifies $|\mathrm{tr}_M(\rho(\beta))|$ as
**Term 1** — the descended trace, equal to
$E_\infty^{0,*}$ of the GB spectral sequence — in the unified
six-term mass formula
([unified knot mass](#thm:knot-mass-formula-unified),
[$E_\infty$-page identifications](#prop:e-infinity-page-identifications)).

The Markov trace is **vortex-specific**: it differs for
$\varrho_{\mathfrak{p}}$ vs $\varrho_{\mathfrak{n}}$ and is
the source of fine isotope distinctions.  It is **not** the
constant offset of the formula; the algebra-level constant
(vortex-independent) is the Frobenius co-Casimir
$m_e \cdot \tau(C_{\dim_q})$
([Frobenius universal](#thm:frobenius-codim-universal)),
which appears as **Term 4**.

The remaining terms — single-generator and pair-generator
brane towers (Terms 2, 3), chirality (Term 5), and jet-prefix
residues (Term 6) — are derived in
([$E_\infty$-page identifications](#prop:e-infinity-page-identifications)).
The full mass is therefore

$$
  \mathfrak{m}(\varrho)
  \;=\;
  \sum_{p \geq 0} \bigl\langle \mathrm{tr}_M,\; E_\infty^{p,*}(T_w) \bigr\rangle
  \;+\; a_K \;+\; m_e \cdot \tau(C_{\dim_q}),
$$

with this proposition supplying the $p = 0$ piece.
