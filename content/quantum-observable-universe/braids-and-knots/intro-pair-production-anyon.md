The symmetric-crystal framework lifts pair production from a
fermionic process to a categorical anyon-collapse event: the
incoming pair of $0_1$ photonic states meets at a Type-B crystal
fixed point, and the outgoing particle/antiparticle pair is
selected by the crystal's involutive structure.

We conjecture the symmetric-crystal pair-production mechanism,
identify the anyon-collapse locus as a Demazure sub-crystal, and
exhibit the explicit collapse map on the first non-trivial
case (the $0_1 + 0_1 \to e^- + e^+$ Breit–Wheeler vertex).  The
closing blocks tie this categorical pair-production to the
Descartes-universe section on the same topic, identifying the
crystal-based picture as the substrate-level prototype for the
archimedean Breit–Wheeler kinematics.
