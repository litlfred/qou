**Proposition.**
*The binding energy scale in the atomic mass formula is*

$$
  \alpha = m_e \times \mathrm{Vol}(\mathcal{B}_3)
         = m_e \times 8G
         \approx 3.7445 \;\mathrm{MeV}
$$

*where $G = \sum_{k=0}^{\infty} (-1)^k/(2k+1)^2 \approx 0.9160$
is Catalan's constant and
$\mathrm{Vol}(\mathcal{B}_3) = 8G$ is the hyperbolic volume of
the Borromean rings complement.*

*Proof.*
The Borromean complement decomposes into two regular ideal
octahedra, each of volume $4G$ (where
$G = \mathrm{Cl}_2(\pi/4)$ is the Clausen function evaluated at
$\pi/4$).  The equality $\mathrm{Vol}(\mathcal{B}_3) = 8G$ is a
theorem of hyperbolic geometry, not a numerical approximation.
The volume is known to arbitrary precision as a mathematical
constant.

The scale $\alpha = m_e \times 8G$ connects:

- **Lepton sector** ($m_e$): the electron mass sets the overall
  energy scale.  This is the single measurement input.
- **Baryon topology** ($8G$): the Borromean link invariant
  $\mathrm{Vol}(\mathcal{B}_3)$ encodes the three-quark
  confinement geometry.

The same scale $\alpha$ appears at two levels:

1. **Nuclear binding**: $\mathcal{M}(Z, N) = M_{\mathrm{free}}
   + \alpha \cdot \Phi_{\mathrm{atom}}$, where $\Phi < 0$ for
   bound nuclei.
2. **Quark confinement**: $\Delta m = \alpha \cdot
   |\Delta\Phi_{\mathrm{quark}}|$, giving the neutron--proton
   mass splitting.

The error in $\alpha$ is dominated by the measurement uncertainty
of $m_e$ ($\pm 3 \times 10^{-8}$ relative).  The factor $8G$ has
zero uncertainty.
