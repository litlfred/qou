The Lie bracket captures the first-order behaviour of the group operation. For elements $A, B$ near the identity,

$$
  e^A e^B =  e^{A+B+\tfrac{1}{2}[A,B]+\cdots}.
$$

The commutator $[e_a,e_b]$ is the first-order Taylor approximation of the non-commutativity. Higher-order corrections (the full Baker–Campbell–Hausdorff series) encode the complete nonlinear group law. They reappear in the universal enveloping algebra ([Section](#sec:enveloping-algebras)).
