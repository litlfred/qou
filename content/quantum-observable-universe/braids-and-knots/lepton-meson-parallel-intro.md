The torus-knot dictionary assigns leptons to $T(2,k)$ braids. This assignment extends naturally to mesons. We develop this parallel here. Both sectors share the same Hecke algebra $H_3(q)$. Mesons, however, carry a bar-involution asymmetry (charge conjugation $\neq$ bar on non-zero-writhe braids). This distinguishes them from bar-symmetric baryons.
