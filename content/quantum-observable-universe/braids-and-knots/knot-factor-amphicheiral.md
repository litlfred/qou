**Corollary (amphicheiral knot factor).**
*Let $\beta \in B_n$ be amphicheiral — equivalently, $\beta^*
\cong \beta$ in $H_n(q)$ up to a sign on the alternating component.
The knot factor reduces to*

$$
  a_K \;=\;
  1 \,-\, w_{\mathrm{alt}}(q)\,
  \frac{c_{\mathrm{alt}}(\beta)^2}{c_{\mathrm{alt}}(\mathbf{1})^2}
  \;=\; 1 - h^2,
$$

*where $h := \chi_{\mathrm{alt}}(\beta) /
\chi_{\mathrm{alt}}(\mathbf{1})$ is the Hoefsmit eigenvalue ratio
on the alternating block.*

*Proof.* For amphicheiral $\beta$, the central-idempotent
coefficients satisfy $c_\lambda(\beta^*) = (-1)^{|\lambda \,\mathrm{anti}|}
c_\lambda(\beta)$; only the alternating block ($\lambda =
(1^n)$) carries a sign.  Substituting in
([knot factor spectral form](#prop:knot-factor-spectral-form))
and using the Plancherel identity $\sum_\lambda w_\lambda = 1$
yields the displayed expression.  $\square$

**Application.** ${}^3\mathrm{H}$, ${}^3\mathrm{He}$ are figure-eight
($4_1$) configurations; both are amphicheiral.  Their knot factor
$a = 1 - h^2$ is therefore the alternating-block projection formula,
not a fitted ansatz.
