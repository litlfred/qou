**Corollary (amphicheiral knot factor).**
*Let $\mathcal{K}$ be amphicheiral, so $\beta_\mathcal{K}^* \cong
\beta_\mathcal{K}$ in $H_n(R, q)$ up to a sign on the alternating
component.  The knot factor reduces to*

$$
  a_\mathcal{K} \;=\;
  1 \,-\, w_{(1^n)}\,
  \frac{\chi_{(1^n)}(\beta_\mathcal{K})^2}
       {\chi_{(1^n)}(\mathbf{1})^2}
  \;=\; 1 - h^2,
$$

*where $h := \chi_{(1^n)}(\beta_\mathcal{K})/\chi_{(1^n)}(\mathbf{1})$
is the Hoefsmit eigenvalue ratio on the alternating block
$\lambda = (1^n)$.*

*Proof.* Amphicheirality gives
$\chi_\lambda(\beta_\mathcal{K}^*) = (-1)^{[\lambda = (1^n)]}
\chi_\lambda(\beta_\mathcal{K})$; only the alternating block carries
the sign.  Substituting in
([knot factor](#prop:knot-factor-spectral-form)) and applying
$\sum_\lambda w_\lambda = 1$ produces the displayed expression.
$\square$

**Application.** ${}^3\mathrm{H}$ and ${}^3\mathrm{He}$ are
figure-eight ($4_1$) configurations; both are amphicheiral, so
$a = 1 - h^2$ is the alternating-block projection — not a fitted
ansatz.
