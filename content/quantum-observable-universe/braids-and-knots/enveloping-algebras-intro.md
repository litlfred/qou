The enveloping algebra $U(\mathfrak{su}(2))$ and its $q$-deformation $U_q(\mathfrak{su}(2))$ supply the algebraic substrate for the categorical braiding. This section assembles the universal enveloping algebra, defines the quantum group $\mathrm{SU}_q(2)$ via the $q$-Casimir, and exhibits the $R$-matrix. The $R$-matrix realises the categorical braiding on the representation category.

The section proceeds in three steps: defining the enveloping algebras and the $q$-Casimir; proving existence and uniqueness of the $q$-Casimir under the substrate-parametrised structure; and identifying the $R$-matrix as the canonical braiding morphism. We treat this morphism as a black box in the remainder of the chapter. Readers familiar with the classical Drinfeld–Jimbo construction can skim the existence proofs. The substrate-parametrised version directly reformulates the classical construction.
