The enveloping algebra $U(\mathfrak{su}(2))$ and its $q$-deformation $U_q(\mathfrak{su}(2))$ supply the algebraic substrate for categorical braiding. We assemble the universal enveloping algebra, define the quantum group $\mathrm{SU}_q(2)$ via the $q$-Casimir, and exhibit the $R$-matrix that realises the braiding on its representation category.
