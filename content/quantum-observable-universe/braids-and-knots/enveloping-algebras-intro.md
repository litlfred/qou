The enveloping algebra $U(\mathfrak{su}(2))$ and its
$q$-deformation $U_q(\mathfrak{su}(2))$ supply the algebraic
substrate for the categorical braiding introduced in the previous
section.  This section assembles the universal enveloping
algebra, defines the quantum group $\mathrm{SU}_q(2)$ via the
$q$-Casimir, and exhibits the $R$-matrix that realises the
categorical braiding on its representation category.

The arc proceeds in three movements: defining the enveloping
algebra and the quantum-$\mathrm{SU}(2)$ together with the
$q$-Casimir; proving existence and uniqueness of the $q$-Casimir
under the substrate-parametrised structure; and identifying the
$R$-matrix as the canonical braiding morphism that the rest of
the chapter treats as a black box.  Readers familiar with the
classical Drinfeld–Jimbo construction can skim the existence
proofs; the substrate-parametrised version is a direct
reformulation of the classical construction.
