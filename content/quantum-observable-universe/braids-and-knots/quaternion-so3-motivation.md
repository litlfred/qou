The structure group $G = \mathrm{SU}(2)$ of the principal bundle $P \to M_S$ ([Bring's QOU 4-tuple](#rem:brings-qou-4tuple)) rigorously dictates the quaternionic basis used in the spectral resolvents below.

The key identifications are:

1. **$\mathrm{Sp}(1) \cong \mathrm{SU}(2)$.**
   The group of unit quaternions $\mathrm{Sp}(1) = \{\mathbf{q} \in \mathbb{H} : \lvert\mathbf{q}\rvert = 1\}$ is isomorphic to $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ via:

   $$
     a + b\mathbf{i} + c\mathbf{j} + d\mathbf{k}
      \longmapsto 
     \begin{pmatrix} a + bi & -c + di \\ c + di & a - bi \end{pmatrix}.
   $$

   Under this mapping, the Pauli generators $e_a = -\frac{i}{2}\tau_a$ ([SU(2) generators](#def:su2-generators)) correspond to the half-quaternion basis $\frac{1}{2}\mathbf{i}, \frac{1}{2}\mathbf{j}, \frac{1}{2}\mathbf{k}$.

2. **$\mathrm{Im}\mathbb{H} \cong \mathfrak{su}(2)$ as Lie algebras.**
   The purely imaginary quaternions form a $3$-dimensional real Lie algebra under the commutator bracket $[p, q] = pq - qp$. In the half-quaternion basis $e_1 = \frac{1}{2}\mathbf{i}$, $e_2 = \frac{1}{2}\mathbf{j}$, $e_3 = \frac{1}{2}\mathbf{k}$, the structure constants are exactly $[e_a, e_b] = \epsilon_{abc}e_c$. For instance, $[\tfrac{1}{2}\mathbf{i}, \tfrac{1}{2}\mathbf{j}] = \tfrac{1}{4}(\mathbf{ij} - \mathbf{ji}) = \tfrac{1}{2}\mathbf{k} = e_3$.

3. **Adjoint action equals the double cover $\mathrm{SU}(2) \to \mathrm{SO}(3)$.**
   Conjugation by a unit quaternion $\mathbf{q}$ acts on $\mathrm{Im}\mathbb{H} \cong \mathbb{R}^3$ by orientation-preserving rotation:

   $$
     \mathrm{Ad}_{\mathbf{q}}\colon
     \mathbf{v}  \longmapsto  \mathbf{q}\mathbf{v}\mathbf{q}^{*},
     \qquad
     \mathbf{v} \in \mathrm{Im}\mathbb{H}.
   $$

   This defines the adjoint representation. The map $\mathbf{q} \mapsto \mathrm{Ad}_{\mathbf{q}}$ forms the double cover $\mathrm{Sp}(1) \twoheadrightarrow \mathrm{SO}(3)$. The $\mathrm{SO}(3)$ holonomy of the chiral sheets ([SO(3) generators](#def:so3-generators)) lifts directly to $\mathrm{Sp}(1)$ conjugation in the quaternion basis.

Writing the spectral resolvents in the $\{\mathbf{i}, \mathbf{j}, \mathbf{k}\}$ basis explicitly expands an $\mathfrak{su}(2)$-valued $1$-form along the generators of the structure group's Lie algebra. The space-like/time-like decomposition $(\omega_1 \mid \omega_2, \omega_3)$ corresponds directly to the split of $\mathfrak{su}(2) \cong \mathfrak{u}(1) \oplus \mathfrak{m}$ into the Cartan subalgebra (generated by $\mathbf{i}$) and its orthogonal complement.
