The [jet prolongation](#prop:jet-prolongation) preserves the braided monoidal structure. If $\mathbf{C}$ is braided, the jet object $J^1(A) = A \otimes \Omega^1(A)$ inherits a braiding from the tensor product of the braidings on $A$ and $\Omega^1(A)$. Consequently, the braid-group representations $\rho_n$ at each level of the brane tower are compatible with the transition maps. The direct-limit algebra $\mathcal{A}_\infty = \varinjlim\Gamma(J^1 \mathcal{S}_n, \mathrm{SU}_{q_n}(2))$ carries a well-defined $B_n$-action for every $n$.
