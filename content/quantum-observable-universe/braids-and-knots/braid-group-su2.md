The braid group $B_3$ acts on tensor products of the spin-$1/2$ representation $V = \mathbb{C}^2$ of $\mathcal{U}_q(\mathfrak{su}(2))$. We detail the action on $V \otimes V = \mathbb{C}^2 \otimes \mathbb{C}^2$.

**Basis and $R$-matrix.** 
Choose the standard basis $\{e_1, e_2\}$ for $V$ (spin up, spin down). The ordered basis for $V \otimes V$ is:
$$
  \{e_1 \otimes e_1, e_1 \otimes e_2,
    e_2 \otimes e_1, e_2 \otimes e_2\}.
$$
The braiding $\sigma \colon V \otimes V \to V \otimes V$ is the $R$-matrix of $\mathcal{U}_q(\mathfrak{su}(2))$, acting on basis vectors as:
$$
  \sigma(e_i \otimes e_j)
  = q^{\delta_{ij}} e_j \otimes e_i
  + (q - q^{-1})\delta_{i < j} e_i \otimes e_j.
$$

**Explicit matrix.** 
Evaluating on each basis element yields:
- $\sigma(e_1 \otimes e_1) = q e_1 \otimes e_1$,
- $\sigma(e_1 \otimes e_2) = e_2 \otimes e_1 + (q - q^{-1}) e_1 \otimes e_2$,
- $\sigma(e_2 \otimes e_1) = e_1 \otimes e_2$,
- $\sigma(e_2 \otimes e_2) = q e_2 \otimes e_2$.

In the ordered basis, the $4 \times 4$ matrix is:
$$
  R = \begin{pmatrix}
    q & 0 & 0 & 0 \\
    0 & q - q^{-1} & 1 & 0 \\
    0 & 1 & 0 & 0 \\
    0 & 0 & 0 & q
  \end{pmatrix}.
$$

**Eigenspace decomposition.** 
The tensor product $V \otimes V$ decomposes into irreducible $\mathcal{U}_q(\mathfrak{su}(2))$-modules:
$$
  V \otimes V =  \mathrm{Sym}^2_q(V)  \oplus  \Lambda^2_q(V),
$$
where $\mathrm{Sym}^2_q(V)$ is the $q$-symmetric (spin-1) part of dimension 3, spanned by
$$
  e_1 \otimes e_1, \qquad
  q^{1/2} e_1 \otimes e_2 + q^{-1/2} e_2 \otimes e_1, \qquad
  e_2 \otimes e_2,
$$
and $\Lambda^2_q(V)$ is the $q$-antisymmetric (spin-0) part of dimension 1, spanned by
$$
  q^{1/2} e_1 \otimes e_2 - q^{-1/2} e_2 \otimes e_1.
$$

On each summand, the braiding $\sigma$ acts as a scalar:
- **Symmetric part** ($\mathrm{Sym}^2_q$): $\sigma = q \cdot \mathrm{id}$,
- **Antisymmetric part** ($\Lambda^2_q$): $\sigma = -q^{-1} \cdot \mathrm{id}$.

These are exactly the two eigenvalues of the Iwahori-Hecke algebra generator $T = \sigma$, consistent with the Hecke relation $(T - q)(T + q^{-1}) = 0$. The symmetric channel corresponds to the positive eigenvalue $q$ (attractive/binding) and the antisymmetric channel to $-q^{-1}$ (repulsive).

**Braid group action.** 
The braid group $B_3 = \langle \sigma_1, \sigma_2 \mid \sigma_1 \sigma_2 \sigma_1 = \sigma_2 \sigma_1 \sigma_2 \rangle$ acts on $V^{\otimes 3}$ by letting $\sigma_i$ braid the $i$-th and $(i+1)$-th tensor factors using the $R$-matrix. On $V \otimes V$, the representation factors through the Hecke algebra $H_2(q)$, which is generated by the single element $T$ satisfying $T^2 = (q - q^{-1})T + 1$. Every braid word in $B_2$ reduces to a polynomial in $T$.
