The [$q$-Casimir element](#def:q-casimir) $C_q$ is the unique
central element of $\mathcal{U}_q(\mathfrak{su}(2))$ of the form
$FE + \phi(K, K^{-1})$ (Laurent polynomial in $K$) whose eigenvalue
on the spin-$j$ irrep is $[j]_q [j+1]_q$.  Any two such central
elements differ by a scalar multiple of the identity.


**Mathematical Novelty:** This is a standard quantum-group result regarding the Joseph-Letzter centre (Kassel VI.4.9). It is axiomatized here to support the framework's broader structural proofs; no novel mathematical argument is claimed for the uniqueness itself.
