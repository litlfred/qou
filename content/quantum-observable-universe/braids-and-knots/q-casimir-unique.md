**Proposition ($q$-Casimir uniqueness).**
*The [$q$-Casimir element](#def:q-casimir) $C_q$ is the unique central element of $\mathcal{U}_q(\mathfrak{su}(2))$ of the form $FE + \phi(K, K^{-1})$ (where $\phi$ is a Laurent polynomial in $K$) whose eigenvalue on the spin-$j$ irrep is $[j]_q [j+1]_q$. Any two such central elements differ by a scalar multiple of the identity.*

**Mathematical Novelty:** This uniqueness result follows standard Joseph–Letzter centre theory for quantum groups (Kassel VI.4.9). We axiomatize it to support the framework's broader structural proofs; the uniqueness argument itself is not novel.
