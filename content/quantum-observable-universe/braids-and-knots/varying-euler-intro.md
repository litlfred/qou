The Descartes universe fixes Bring's surface
($\chi = -6$) as the spacetime base, but the categorical
substrate is compatible with a *family* of base surfaces of
varying Euler characteristic.  This section surveys the
commutative-limit variants — the genus-2 quintic, the
hyperelliptic family, and the $\mathbb{Z}/5\mathbb{Z}$-Bring
descent — to identify the categorical features that survive a
choice other than Bring.

The arc has two movements.  The first half tracks the
commutative-limit reductions (Track A): the genus-2 quintic
intro fixes the formal setup, the hyperelliptic classification
catalogues the candidate base surfaces, and the
$x_5$-automorphism / Bring-descent / Prym-Jacobian blocks
identify Bring as the natural fixed point.  The second half
records the $\mathrm{U}(1)$-equivariant reduction that ties
the family back to the substrate-parametrised gauge group; this
half is the technical bridge to the next section's
representation-theoretic content.
