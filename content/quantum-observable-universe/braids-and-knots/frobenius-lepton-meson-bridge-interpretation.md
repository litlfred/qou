The underlying branching rule $V_{(2,1)} \downarrow V_{(2)} \oplus V_{(1,1)}$ is standard representation theory (derived from the Pieri or Littlewood–Richardson rule). The novelty lies in its formal topological application. We lift a strictly representation-theoretic branching into a geometric constraint on the observable mass spectrum. We demonstrate that the physical entanglement of lepton channels uniquely maps to the single mass-carrying partition of the meson sector.
