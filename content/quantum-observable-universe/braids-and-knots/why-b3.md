We now explain why the three-strand braid group $B_3$ is singled out.
The Descartes condition $\chi(A) = -6$ fixes the gauge group to a
quantum form of $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ (rank $2$), so the fundamental
representation $V$ is $2$-dimensional.  On $V^{\otimes 2}$ the braiding
gives us a single operator $c_{V,V}$; the first non-trivial braid group
action appears on $V^{\otimes 3}$, where the two generators $\sigma_1, \sigma_2$
of $B_3$ act via the categorical braid representation.
