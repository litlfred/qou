The volume axiom forces the gauge group to be a quantum form of $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ ([SU(2) emergence](#thm:su2-emergence)), independently of the Descartes condition; $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ has rank $1$. Consequently, the fundamental representation $V$ is $2$-dimensional. On $V^{\otimes 2}$, the braiding provides a single operator $c_{V,V}$. The first non-trivial braid group action occurs on $V^{\otimes 3}$, where the two generators $\sigma_1, \sigma_2$ of $B_3$ act via the categorical braid representation.
