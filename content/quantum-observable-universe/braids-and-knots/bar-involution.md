**Definition (bar-involution).**
*The bar-involution $\overline{(\cdot)} \colon H_n(q) \to H_n(q)$ is the ring anti-involution on the Iwahori–Hecke algebra defined on generators by*
$$ \overline{\sigma_i} \;=\; \sigma_i^{-1}, \qquad \overline{q} \;=\; q^{-1}. $$

*Equivalently, this involution acts on the parameter via $q \mapsto q^{-1}$ and on crossings by interchanging positive $L_+$ and negative $L_-$ (mirror reflection of the braid diagram).*

The bar-involution satisfies $\overline{\overline x} = x$. Over a field of characteristic distinct from 2, it splits every element $x \in H_n(q)$ into a bar-symmetric part $P_{\mathrm{avg}}(x)$ and a bar-anti-symmetric part $P_{\mathrm{skew}}(x)$. The canonical projection, classification by bar-type, key identities, and grading proofs appear in [bar-involution properties](#rem:bar-involution-properties).
