In the $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ representation ([SU(2) generators](#def:su2-generators)), the trefoil braid relation $xyx=yxy$ becomes the operator identity

$$
  \alpha\beta\alpha
  = \beta\alpha\beta
$$

for the generators $\alpha$ and $\beta$. Explicitly, using

$$
  \alpha=\begin{pmatrix}e^{i\pi/3}&0\\0&e^{-i\pi/3}\end{pmatrix},
  \qquad
  \beta=\begin{pmatrix}0&i\\i&0\end{pmatrix},
$$

both products evaluate to

$$
  \alpha\beta\alpha
  = \begin{pmatrix}0&ie^{2i\pi/3}\\ie^{-2i\pi/3}&0\end{pmatrix}
  = \beta\alpha\beta.
$$

The eigenvalues of the composite operator $\alpha\beta$ govern the phases acquired when quarks exchange colour charges along the trefoil path.
