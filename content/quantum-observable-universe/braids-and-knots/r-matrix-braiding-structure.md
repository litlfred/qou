To replace the classical twist/flip involutions ([braid twist-flip](#def:braid-twist-flip)), we require a braid group representation. The universal $R$-matrix
$\mathcal{R} \in \mathcal{U}_q(\mathfrak{su}(2)) \hat{\otimes} \mathcal{U}_q(\mathfrak{su}(2))$
([quantum SU(2)](#def:quantum-su2)) satisfies the Yang--Baxter equation:

$$
  \mathcal{R}_{12}\mathcal{R}_{13}\mathcal{R}_{23}
  = 
  \mathcal{R}_{23}\mathcal{R}_{13}\mathcal{R}_{12}.
$$

The braid operator $\check{R} = P \circ \mathcal{R}$, where $P$ is the tensor swap, furnishes this representation on any tensor product of finite-dimensional $\mathcal{U}_q(\mathfrak{su}(2))$-modules. The specific identification of the eigenvalues $q$ and $-q^{-1}$ (Hecke channels) connects this standard quantum-group structure to physical observables.
