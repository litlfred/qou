We establish the basis, the first-order Taylor relation between the generators, and the quaternionic picture of $\mathrm{SO}(3)$. These specify $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ and $\mathrm{SO}(3)$ as the natural gauge groups on the substrate side and the archimedean side, respectively. This drives the subsequent identification of the substrate-parametrised gauge group $\mathrm{SU}_q(2)$ as the deformation of $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ along the substrate flow.
