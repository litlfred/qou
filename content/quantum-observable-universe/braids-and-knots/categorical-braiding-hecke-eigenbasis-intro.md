Having established the categorical origin of braiding, we now identify the Hecke quotient of the braid action and show how its representation theory furnishes a basis of knot states.

The following blocks develop the Jones-Markov trace, the Hopf $\sigma_1^2$ closed form, the Hecke logarithmic decomposition, and the Hecke normal form. They detail the dark-vision strand bandwidth and the Markov-moves brane tower before constructing the knot eigenbasis and its Feynman counterpart.

The primary result is the **knot eigenbasis**. This basis of $\mathrm{End}_{\mathbf{C}}(A)$ is indexed by knot equivalence classes, diagonalizes the Hecke action, and is computed in closed form on the two-strand $B_3$ kernel. Subsequent sections use this basis—its eigenvalues, multiplicities, and Feynman identification—as the substrate for binding-energy and mass predictions. It serves as the structural pivot of the chapter.
