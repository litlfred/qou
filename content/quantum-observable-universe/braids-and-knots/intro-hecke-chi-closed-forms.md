The Hecke algebra carries two natural coordinate systems: the
Markov $u$-coordinate adapted to braid-trace identities and the
$\chi$-character coordinate adapted to representation-theoretic
decomposition.  This section derives the closed-form
transformation between the two, identifies the substrate
parameter as the deformation knob that interpolates the two
coordinate charts, and tabulates the explicit closed forms for
the characters that the chapter's binding-energy computations
need.

The arc proceeds in three movements.  We first reduce the
$u$-coordinate identities to a small set of generating
discharge moves (the $u$-coordinate discharges), then express
the $\chi$-character as a polynomial in $u$ and the substrate
parameter.  The middle blocks establish the closed-form
$\chi$ values at the small partitions ($\lambda = (1)$, $(2)$,
$(1,1)$, $(2,1)$, $\dots$) that drive the early-shell binding-
energy formulas.  The closing blocks tie the $\chi$-closed forms
to the Markov-axiom recursive ascent used downstream by the
Plancherel sum.

The output is the $\chi$-character table indexed by partitions,
which the next section's Markov-axioms-braid-link enumeration
consumes as its primary input.  Readers focused on the
binding-energy applications can take the closed forms on faith;
the substrate-parameter dependence is uniform across partitions
and does not interact with the enumeration.
