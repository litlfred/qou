The [braid action on tensor products](#prop:braid-action-tensor) requires only the braided monoidal structure of $\mathbf{C}$.

1. **Archimedean realization** ([$\mathbb{C}$-quantum observable universe](#ex:c-quantum-observable-universe)): The $R$-matrix of $\mathcal{U}_q(\mathfrak{su}(2))$ computes the braiding. The $B_n$-action recovers the skein relations of [Chapter 6](#chap:brings-surface) as traces on braid-group representations.
2. **$\mathfrak{p}$-adic realization** ([$p$-adic realization](#ex:p-adic-realisation)): The same braiding acts on $\mathrm{Rep}(\mathrm{SU}_{q(p)}(2))$ over $\mathbb{Q}_\mathfrak{p}$. The Yang-Baxter equation forms an identity in the endomorphism algebra valid over any base ring.
3. **Finite-field realization** ([finite field realization](#ex:finite-field-realisation)): The braiding persists on the fusion category $\mathrm{Rep}_{\mathbb{F}_\mathfrak{p}}(\mathrm{SU}_q(2))$.

The skein relations, knot invariants, and hyperbolic volumes from the archimedean setting ([Chapter 6](#chap:brings-surface)) are consequences of evaluating the categorical braiding via a specific fibre functor, rather than the origin of the braid-group structure itself.
