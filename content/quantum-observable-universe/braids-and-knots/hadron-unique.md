Two [hadrons](#def:hadron) with the same crossing partition,
localisation point $p$, and Hecke normal form coincide up to braid
isotopy.  The Hecke normal form
([Hecke normal form](#def:hecke-normal-form)) provides a canonical
representative, so "uniqueness" here means uniqueness of the Hecke
class in $H_n(R, q)$.
