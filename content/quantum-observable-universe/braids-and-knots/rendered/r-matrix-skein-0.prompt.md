# The Hecke skein triple

F4.2 (P0). Three side-by-side panels of local strand diagrams: a positive
crossing $\sigma_i$ (over-strand running from lower-left to upper-right), its
inverse $\sigma_i^{-1}$ (mirror), and the identity $1$ (two parallel strands).
Each panel is framed with a light dashed border. Time flows downward; arrow
markers at the bottom of each strand indicate orientation. Italic math-serif
labels $\sigma_i$, $\sigma_i^{-1}$, $1$ sit beneath each panel. The Hecke
defining relation $\sigma_i - \sigma_i^{-1} = (q-q^{-1})\cdot 1$ and the braid
relation $\sigma_i\sigma_{i+1}\sigma_i = \sigma_{i+1}\sigma_i\sigma_{i+1}$ live
in the surrounding prose, not inside the SVG.
