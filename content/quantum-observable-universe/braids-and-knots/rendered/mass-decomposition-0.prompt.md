# Brane-tower mass decomposition

F4.7 (P1). An exploded view: on the left, a stylised trefoil $K$ with its Hecke-word label $T_w$ underneath. An arrow labelled "decompose" points right to a stack of three rounded boxes, each carrying one term of the unified mass formula $m(K) = |\mathrm{tr}_M(T_w)| + \mathrm{tower}(T_w) + \mathrm{tr}_M(C_{\dim_q})$. Top box: descended Markov trace $|\mathrm{tr}_M(T_w)| = \sum_\lambda y_\lambda(q) \chi_\lambda(T_w)$ with a single red bar showing magnitude. Middle box: brane-tower contribution $\sum_i \Phi_q(|\hat V_i|) + z^2 \sum_{i<j} \Phi_q(|\hat V_i \hat V_j|)$ with four blue bars of decreasing height (one per stratification level). Bottom box: Frobenius co-Casimir $\sum_\lambda 1/\dim_q(\lambda)$ with a small green bar — the universal correction. Footer notes that the three terms are additive, finite, exact, and pre-Archimedean. Layout: knot left + 3 stacked term boxes right. Density: 3 boxes each with formula + magnitude bar. Style: schematic. Colour: monochrome line-art with one accent per term (red / blue / green).
