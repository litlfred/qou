# Markov closure: braid → link

F4.3 (P0). Three panels. Left: an open 3-strand braid $\beta = \sigma_1 \sigma_2^{-1} \sigma_1$ drawn vertically inside a faint dashed box, with three crossings (the gaps in the under-strands marking over/under). Centre: the *closure* $\hat\beta$ obtained by joining strand-$i$ at top to strand-$i$ at bottom via semicircular arcs that wrap around the left side of the box, producing an oriented link; a label notes the Markov trace decomposition $\mathrm{tr}_M = \sum_\lambda y_\lambda \chi_\lambda(\beta)$. Right: the two Markov moves shown as schematic equivalences. (M1) Conjugation $\beta \sim \alpha \beta \alpha^{-1}$ as an isotopy of the closure. (M2) Stabilisation $\beta \sim \beta \cdot \sigma_n^{\pm 1}$ as adding a strand and an extra crossing. Footer: "Markov's theorem: oriented links ⇄ braids modulo (M1) + (M2). The Markov trace descends from the braid to the link — backbone of the topological mass formula." Layout: 3-panel left-to-right (open braid, closure, moves). Density: minimal labels per panel. Style: schematic strand diagrams with iconic Markov-move boxes. Colour: monochrome.
