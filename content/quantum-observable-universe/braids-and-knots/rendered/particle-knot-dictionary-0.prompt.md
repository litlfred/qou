# The particle ↔ knot dictionary

F4.1 (P0). A 2-row grid mapping knot types to particle identities. Top row (5 cards): the four charged-lepton + one closure cell, with each card showing a stylised torus knot $T(2,2k+1)$ above its particle label. Cards left-to-right: unknot $0_1 \to \nu_e$ (a circle, trivial braiding); trefoil $3_1 = T(2,3) \to e^-$ (three overlapping circles); cinquefoil $5_1 = T(2,5) \to \mu^-$ (five-pointed star); $T(2,7) = 7_1 \to \tau^-$ (seven-pointed star); a "$\cdots$" closure card noting heavier leptons (if any) extend the brane tower. Bottom row (3 cards): the hadron family — three Borromean rings in the canonical RGB colouring representing the colour-confined quark generators ("remove any one and the other two fall apart"); a closed 3-strand braid for the proton $\mathfrak{p}$ with its Hecke word $\sigma_1 \sigma_2^{-1} \sigma_1$; a labelled card for the neutron $\mathfrak{n}$ with its mirror Hecke word $\sigma_1^{-1} \sigma_2 \sigma_1^{-1}$. Footer note: the icons are schematic; the QOU datum is the Hecke word in $H_n(q)$ up to Markov equivalence. Layout: 2-row × 5-col / 1-row × 3-col grid. Density: 3-line label per card. Style: stylised iconic knots (rose / star polygons; not isotopy-correct diagrams). Colour: monochrome plus RGB on the Borromean.
