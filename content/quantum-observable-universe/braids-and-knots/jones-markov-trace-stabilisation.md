Stabilisation by $\sigma_n^{-1}$ is forced by the Hecke quadratic relation: $\mathrm{tr}_M(x \sigma_n^{-1}) = z_{-}\,\mathrm{tr}_M(x)$ with $z_{-} = z - (q - q^{-1})$ ([Markov axioms](#rem:markov-axioms-primitive)). The two loop values are distinct, and invariance of the resulting link polynomial under both Markov moves is achieved by a writhe-dependent prefactor outside the trace, not by symmetry of the trace itself. The Jones link-invariant normalisation parameter $z_{\mathrm{J}} = (q-1)\,z^2$ that absorbs this prefactor is distinct from $z$ ([reconciliation of the Markov parameter $z$](#rem:markov-z-reconciliation)).
