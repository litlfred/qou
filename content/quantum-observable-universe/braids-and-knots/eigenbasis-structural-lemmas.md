The categorical-confinement proposition writes every physical observable as a sum of Wedderburn residues: $\mathrm{tr}_M = \sum_\lambda y_\lambda\,\chi_\lambda$. This provides one term per Wedderburn channel $V_\lambda$. The *content/twist eigenbasis* organises these channel characters $\chi_\lambda$ by the ribbon-twist eigenvalue $\theta_\lambda = q^{2c(\lambda)}$. This eigenvalue is the scalar by which the full twist $\varDelta^2 \in Z(H_n(q))$ acts on $V_\lambda$. The eigenbasis pins down three structural facts about each character $\chi_\lambda(\beta)$. All three are formalised in Lean (`QOU.BraidKnot.EigenbasisStructuralLemmas`):

1. **Order-parity equals word-length parity (L1).** For a braid word $\beta$ of length $L$, every Laurent exponent of $\chi_\lambda(\beta)(q)$ has parity $\equiv L \pmod 2$. Equivalently, $\chi_\lambda(\beta)(-q) = (-1)^L\, \chi_\lambda(\beta)(q)$. (Lean: `character_support_parity` and `character_qneg_parity`).

2. **Bar-involution equals conjugation duality (L2).** The bar involution $q \mapsto q^{-1}$ swaps the seminormal eigenvalues $q \leftrightarrow -q^{-1}$. It maps the intertwiner $V_\lambda \cong V_{\lambda'}$ to the conjugate channel. Therefore, $\chi_\lambda(q^{-1}) = \varepsilon_\lambda\,\chi_{\lambda'}(q)$ with $\varepsilon_\lambda \in \{\pm 1\}$. (Lean: `character_bar_conjugate`, with the duality constant derived as $\varepsilon_\lambda = s^L$).

3. **Framing generating function (L3).** Framing $\beta$ by $m$ full twists scales $\chi_\lambda$ by $\theta_\lambda^m$. The framing tower is therefore the single-pole rational $\chi_\lambda(\beta)/(1 - t\,\theta_\lambda)$, with its unique pole at $t = q^{-2c(\lambda)}$. (Lean: `framing_gf_closed_form`, `framing_gf_ribbon`, and `framing_gf_ribbon_class`).

These structural facts govern the per-channel characters summed by the confinement decomposition. The parity controls which crossing-counts contribute to a given channel. The conjugation duality provides the matter/antimatter symmetry of the channel weights. The framing rational fixes how reframing a confined braid redistributes weight across the tower. Each lemma is a rigorous conditional on a categorical input (the ribbon eigenvalue or the seminormal bar–conjugate relation), with the structural conclusion derived rather than assumed. The companion compute probe supplies exact-symbolic verification, while the Lean formalisation establishes the lemmas categorically.
