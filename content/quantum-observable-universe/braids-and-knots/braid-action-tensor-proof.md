The hexagon axioms of a :refterm[braided monoidal category]{#braided-monoidal-category} guarantee that the braiding $c_{V,V}$ satisfies the Yang-Baxter equation on $V^{\otimes 3}$:
$$
  (c \otimes \mathrm{id})
  \circ (\mathrm{id} \otimes c)
  \circ (c \otimes \mathrm{id})
  = 
  (\mathrm{id} \otimes c)
  \circ (c \otimes \mathrm{id})
  \circ (\mathrm{id} \otimes c).
$$
This coincides exactly with the braid relation for adjacent generators. The far-commutativity relation $\sigma_i\sigma_j = \sigma_j\sigma_i$ for $\lvert i-j\rvert \geq 2$ follows from the naturality of the braiding. Together, these verify the Artin presentation of $B_n$.
