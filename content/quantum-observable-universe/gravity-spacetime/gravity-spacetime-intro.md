This chapter develops the emergence of gravity and spacetime from the
QOU structural mechanism. The concrete phenomenon to keep in view is the
universal long-range attraction between masses — the pull that, in this
framework, is not imposed as a separate force law but falls out of the
flow of the brane fluid itself. That is the organizing claim: gravity
requires no additional axiom. The categorical substrate produces the
long-range gravitational interaction — through contact geometry on
divisors and geodesic flow in the Madelung representation of the brane
fluid — as a geometric consequence of the underlying categorical action,
rather than as an additional postulate.

The chapter closes Part II (Structural Mechanics): once the mass
endomorphism is established (Climax chapter) and unpacked via
Wedderburn and crystal-basis machinery (Mass — Theory, Mass — Application),
the long-range geometric content of the framework is gravity.
