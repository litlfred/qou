The fermion--boson split of
([fermion--boson decomposition](#def:fermion-boson-decomposition)) — bosons
purely horizontal ($\alpha_V = 0$), fermions with non-trivial vertical
projection — has a transparent geometric reading on the quantum
projective line.  By
([spectral--chart correspondence](#prop:spectral-chart-correspondence)),
the symmetric and antisymmetric eigenspaces $S^2 V \oplus \Lambda^2 V$
of the braiding $c_{V,V}$ are exactly the spectral data that pin the
chart-transition coefficient of $\mathbb{P}_q(V)$ to $\kappa = q^{-1}$.
The bosonic sector lives on $S^2 V$ with eigenvalue $\lambda_+ = q$, the
classical channel that degenerates to the symmetric monoidal
$\mathbb{P}(V)$ at $q = 1$; the fermionic sector lives on the unit
summand $\Lambda^2 V \cong \mathbf{1}$ with eigenvalue
$\lambda_- = -q^{-1}$, where the chart-glue is concentrated.  Reading
$\kappa = q^{-1} = \lambda_+^{-1} = -\lambda_-$ as the parameter
determined by the antisymmetric channel makes precise the colloquial
statement that $q$ calibrates how much vertical (fermionic) content
survives passage between charts: at $q = 1$ one recovers $\kappa = 1$
and the two sectors decouple into the classical inversion
$z \cdot w = 1$, while $q \neq 1$ deforms the glue and ties the
fermionic content to the substrate parameter via the same Hecke data
that fixes $c_{V,V}$ on $V \otimes V$.  This is the geometric face
of the action computed in
([observation fermion action](#rem:observation-fermion-action)): the
non-trivial chart-transition is what carries the fermionic backreaction
of an observation, and the substrate parameter is the chart-glue itself.
