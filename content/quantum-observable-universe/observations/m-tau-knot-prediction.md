The CAL-2 cohomological critique
([`rem:epistemic-status-of-calibrations`](#rem:epistemic-status-of-calibrations))
identifies a structural pattern in the paper's muon-mass identity
($m_\mu/m_e = \mathrm{Vol}(4_1)/\hbar_q^2$). The natural question is
whether $m_\tau/m_e$ admits an analogous cohomological reading
$m_\tau/m_e = \mathrm{Vol}(K_\tau)/\hbar_q^{k_\tau}$ for some
hyperbolic knot $K_\tau$ and small integer $k_\tau$.

**Refuted (specific evidence).** Direct search across 25 hyperbolic
knots + links through 10 crossings, $k_\tau \in \{2, 3, 4, 5\}$, plus
two-knot products at $k \le 7$, finds **no candidate within 1 %** of
the CODATA $m_\tau/m_e = 3477.23$. Best single-knot match is
$\mathrm{Vol}(8_1)/\hbar_q^3 = 3523.59$, off by $+1.33\%$ — about
$13\,000\times$ the experimental precision.
