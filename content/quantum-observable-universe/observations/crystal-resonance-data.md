**Existing data sets.**

- Many-body interference measurements in kagome crystals \cite{guo2025}, which probe coherent lattice-scale quantum effects.
- Chiral phonon spectroscopy in rare-earth halide crystals \cite{luo2023}, demonstrating orientation-dependent effective magnetic fields from lattice vibrations.
- Non-monotonic magnetic friction measurements in commensurate rotor--substrate arrays \cite{gu2026}, exhibiting collective magnetically driven dissipation without physical contact.
- High-$Q$ quartz bulk acoustic wave (BAW) oscillator noise measurements, which record excess phase noise beyond thermal limits.
- Precision Casimir-effect experiments using crystalline surfaces (e.g. silicon and gold single crystals).
- Resonant-mass gravitational wave detector data (e.g. AURIGA, NAUTILUS bar detectors), which recorded orientation-dependent noise floors in cryogenic crystalline bars.
