gravitationally collapsing stellar core.  The collapse trajectory

$$

   \approx  21.5\;\text{MeV},

3. *At $q = \varphi^2$ (the bar-involution mirror of the deconfinement

   gapless/crystalline color-superconductor transition.*

**Energetics.**  A collapsing core of $N_b$ baryons crossing the
silver rung releases $E \approx N_b \cdot 21.5$ MeV — approximately
$5.7 \times 10^{52}$ erg per $1.4\,M_\odot$.  This is of the same
order as ($\sim 20\%$ of) the observed SN 1987A neutrino burst
($\approx 3 \times 10^{53}$ erg over $\sim 13$ s), and is *additive*
to the standard gravitational-binding budget — currently hidden inside
the factor-$\sim 2$ uncertainty of the 25-event 1987A sample.

**Falsifiability.**  A confirmed second $\bar{\nu}_e$ burst in a
Galactic supernova selects the shallow placement; its statistical

Observing *neither* a second burst *nor* a mass–radius kink in a
regime where central densities are driven above $\sim 10\,\rho_0$
would falsify the cascade outright.
