# The substrate consistency window

F12.1 (P0). A horizontal axis showing the substrate parameter $q$ across the range $[1.100, 1.120]$, ticked every $0.005$. Three translucent vertical bands span the axis, each bounded by the $q$-range allowed by one independent calibration: red = $m_\mu/m_e$ consistency, green = $\alpha_{\mathrm{em}}$ consistency, blue = $Q_\beta(n)$ consistency. Their pairwise intersections shrink monotonically; their three-way intersection is a tiny vertical sliver, drawn as a thick black line and labelled $q_0 \approx 1.1097$. A small legend on the right keys the colours. Footer: "single-parameter overdetermination — the central falsifiability claim". This figure is the central visual argument of Chapter 7: three independent inputs, one consistent $q$, no free parameters left for any other prediction. Layout: horizontal axis with vertical bands.
