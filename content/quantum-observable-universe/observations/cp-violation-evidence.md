**Definition of CP violation.**
CP symmetry enforces perfect mirroring between matter and antimatter. CP violation fundamentally distinguishes them.

**Experimental evidence.**

Three systems establish CP violation:

1. **Neutral kaons** (1964). Cronin and Fitch observed $K_L \to \pi^+\pi^-$ at a rate of $\sim 2 \times 10^{-3}$ \cite{christenson1964}.
2. **$B$ mesons** (2001). BaBar \cite{aubert2001} and Belle measured CP-violating asymmetries in $B^0 \to J/\psi\, K_S$, confirming the complex CKM phase $\delta_{\mathrm{CKM}}$.
3. **$D$ mesons** (2019). LHCb measured CP violation in charm decays.

**The matter-antimatter asymmetry.**

Sakharov required C and CP violation for baryogenesis. The Standard Model CKM phase is quantitatively insufficient to explain the $n_B / n_\gamma \sim 6 \times 10^{-10}$ ratio.

**QOU interpretation.**

In the [complex chirality rotation](#conj:complex-chirality-rotation) framework, CP violation stems from a residual phase $\theta_{\mathrm{res}}$ of the complex parameter $q = |q|e^{i\theta}$:

- The observable sector projects $\theta \to 0$. An imperfect projection leaves $\theta_{\mathrm{res}}$.
- The discriminant scales as $\delta_{\mathrm{CP}} \sim \mathcal{O}(\theta_{\mathrm{res}}^2)$ for single-crossing systems ($e^-e^+$).
- For multi-crossing composites (kaons), phase accumulates linearly: $\delta_{\mathrm{CP}} \sim n_c\theta_{\mathrm{res}}$.
- This supplies a geometric origin for the CKM phase.

**Quantitative gap.**

The [crossing energy decomposition](#prop:crossing-energy-decomposition) puts the bar-odd-to-bar-even channel ratio at $\tanh(\ln q) \approx 0.104$ *for a positive braid closure*; a composite with $p$ positive and $m$ negative crossings carries the multiplicity factor $(p + m)/(p - m)$ on top of it. Kaon systems are the mixed-chirality case, so the bare constant is a lower bound on their ratio rather than its value, and the factor is unknown until the kaon braid words are fixed. Taking the bare constant, $\theta_{\mathrm{res}} \sim \tanh(\ln q) \cdot \alpha_{\mathrm{EM}}$ gives $\delta_{\mathrm{CP}} \sim 10^{-3}$, the observed kaon order; this does not explain baryogenesis, for which cosmological regimes must supply the enhancement.

Two caveats attach to that estimate and neither is closed here. The multiplicity factor above is not evaluated. Separately, reading the bar-odd channel as *the* electromagnetic one is not supplied by the decomposition — its coefficient $\tfrac{1}{2}(p + m)$ is a crossing count, hence sign-blind, while the Coulomb charge $Q = \tfrac{1}{2}(p - m)$ sits on the bar-even side ([Coulomb charge](#def:coulomb-charge)). The order-of-magnitude agreement stands as recorded; the channel identification behind it is open.
