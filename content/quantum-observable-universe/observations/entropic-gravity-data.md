**Existing data sets.**

- Cavendish-type torsion balance experiments (e.g. Eot-Wash group).
- Casimir force measurements at micrometre scales.
  See [`conj:casimir-epsilon-vacuum`](../appendix-surreals/casimir-epsilon-vacuum.md)
  for the QOU prediction: the textbook
  $F_{\text{Cas}}/A \approx -\pi^{2}\hbar c/(240\, d^{4})$ is the
  standard-part of an $\omega \cdot \varepsilon$ surreal product
  under the $\varepsilon$-relaxed vacuum axiom.
- Neutron interferometry (gravity resonance spectroscopy).

**Planned observations.**

- MICROSCOPE follow-up missions: improved equivalence principle tests in space.
- Atom interferometry experiments (MAGIS, AION, ZAIGA) probing gravitational decoherence.

**Potential new experiments.**

- Entanglement-assisted gravity experiments testing whether gravitational interactions preserve quantum coherence as predicted by the $q$-deformed field equations.
- High-precision measurements of Casimir forces in geometries designed to isolate contributions from $q$-deformed vacuum fluctuations.
  See [`conj:casimir-first-q-correction`](../appendix-surreals/casimir-first-q-correction.md):
  the leading $1/d^{4}$ force is substrate-independent, and the first
  $q$-dependent correction is carried by the figure-eight knot $4_1$ at
  the interacting (subleading) order. Its relative size
  $\sim \alpha\,\lambda_C/d$ is below present experimental reach, so this
  is a structural prediction rather than a near-term discriminator.
