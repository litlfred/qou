**Lemma.**
*Applying the pair Skein mass formula to the proton ($uud$) and
neutron ($udd$) Borromean braids gives*

$$
  m_p = \mathcal{S}_{\mathfrak{u}\mathfrak{u}}(q) + 2\mathcal{S}_{\mathfrak{u}\mathfrak{d}}(q),
  \qquad
  m_n = 2\mathcal{S}_{\mathfrak{u}\mathfrak{d}}(q) + \mathcal{S}_{\mathfrak{d}\mathfrak{d}}(q),
$$

*and the proton--neutron mass difference isolates the same-flavor
crossing pairs:*

$$
  m_n - m_p
  =
  \frac{q + q^{-1}}{q^{1/2}+q^{-1/2}}(\mu_{\mathfrak{d}} - \mu_{\mathfrak{u}}).
$$

*Substituting $q = q_0$ and the CODATA splitting
$m_n - m_p \approx 1.29333251\,\text{MeV}$ determines
$\mu_{\mathfrak{d}} - \mu_{\mathfrak{u}} \approx 1.288\,\text{MeV}$, with
$\mu_{\mathfrak{u}} \approx 311.10\,\text{MeV}$ and
$\mu_{\mathfrak{d}} \approx 312.39\,\text{MeV}$.*
