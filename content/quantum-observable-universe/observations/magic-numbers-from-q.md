**Scope (selection is open).** *What is established here is that $\delta^2\Phi$ is **elevated at** the magic $Z$. The stronger claim that it peaks **only** at magic numbers — the magic-number **selection** principle — is not shown here and remains open (`conj:homological-shell-condensation`); the underlying $\kappa = 0$ necessary condition holds for **all** $n$, not just magic ones, and the table above lists only magic $Z$ (the non-magic contrast needed to demonstrate selection is not included). The result is a necessary-condition **coincidence**, not a derivation of magic-number selection.*
