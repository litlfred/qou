The preceding sub-section established the mass-prediction
methodology: the stability cubic, the $q$-stability ranges, the
per-particle mass-ratio predictions, the mass-comparison table
that anchors the framework's outputs against AME2020/CODATA, and
the validation gates that detect when a prediction has drifted
outside its allowed band.

This sub-section walks the specific derivations: nucleon Borromean
topology and the pair-skein mass formula that builds nucleon
masses from braid-link data; flavor-split substrate and dressed-
quark numerology at $q_0$ for the quark sector; the deuteron
binding-energy derivation as a check on the two-nucleon
construction; the terminal-resolution closure that anchors the
categorical chain to the archimedean evaluation; and finally the
W-boson mass discrepancy, currently the largest residual gap
between QOU and Standard-Model expectation and a key open audit
target.
