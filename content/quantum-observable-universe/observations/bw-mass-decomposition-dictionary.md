The classical Bethe–Weizsäcker (BW) empirical mass formula decomposes nuclear binding into volume, surface, Coulomb, asymmetry, and pairing terms. In the categorical framework, this phenomenological liquid-drop model is **targeted for categorical replacement** — the program is to *derive* the decomposition from $q_0$ and $m_e$ rather than fit it. This is a program, **not a completed closure**: the per-isotope binding magnitude ($\Delta_\lambda$) is open, six ansätze have failed, and the BW coefficients are, as of now, still AME least-squares fits.

Instead of mapping empirical BW coefficients to specific algebraic strata (a numerological exercise), the exact macroscopic mass is governed by the $q$-Central Limit Theorem ($q$-CLT) and the Jaco-Shalen-Johannson (JSJ) volume addition.
