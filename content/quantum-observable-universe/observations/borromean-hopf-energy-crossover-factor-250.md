**Factor of $250$.** The ratio $746.4 / 3.016 \approx 247$ between Borromean and Hopf binding at $q = q_0$ ([energy crossover](#prop:borromean-hopf-energy-crossover)) shows why color superconductivity remains inaccessible at nuclear densities. The compression energy must supply roughly $750$ Skein units per baryon to close the gap. This demands densities many times $\rho_0$. This conclusion matches QCD estimates, which place the CFL onset at $\rho \gtrsim 5\text{--}10\rho_0$, deep inside neutron star cores.
