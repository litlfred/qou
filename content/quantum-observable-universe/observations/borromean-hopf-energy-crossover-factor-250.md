**The factor of $250$.** The enormous ratio
$749.2 / 3.016 \approx 249$ between Borromean and Hopf binding at
$q = q_0$
([energy crossover](#prop:borromean-hopf-energy-crossover))
explains why color superconductivity is inaccessible at nuclear
densities. The compression energy must supply $\sim 750$ Skein units
per baryon to close the gap — corresponding to densities many times
$\rho_0$. This is consistent with QCD estimates placing the CFL onset
at $\rho \gtrsim 5\text{--}10\rho_0$, deep inside neutron star cores.
