The non-commutative-probability section closed with a $q$-CLT that does not commit to whether the QOU volumes are algebraically independent. The present section catalogues the observable consequences of either answer. If the QOU volumes are algebraically independent, they would produce irrational ratios with no Diophantine relations. If they are dependent, they would produce specific rational identities. The simulations section that closes the chapter provides interactive tools for inspecting these signatures numerically.
