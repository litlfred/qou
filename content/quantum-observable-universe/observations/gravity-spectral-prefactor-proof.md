The unified mass formula
([unified mass formula](#prop:unified-curvature-rank-mass-formula))
decomposes mass as $\sum_k \alpha_k \mathfrak{m}^{(k)}_{q,\mathcal{K}}$.
For $k \geq 4$ no specific hyperbolic-knot ideal remains in the
centre $Z(A)$
([universal residue](#prop:plancherel-term-7-formulation)); the
surviving structure is the dualizing-complex residue, whose normalisation
is $\alpha = m_e \cdot 8 G$ by the Planck-unit convention.  Solving
for $G$ gives the displayed expression.
$\qquad\blacksquare$
