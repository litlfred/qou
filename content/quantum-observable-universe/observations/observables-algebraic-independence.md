The [algebraic independence
conjecture](#conj:hyperbolic-volume-transcendence) is falsifiable
against five classes of physical data.  Under independence
([mathematical and categorical consequences](#rem:implications-algebraic-independence)),
each class should behave as follows.

1. **Proton decay searches.** Non-collapse of the brane
   tower forbids any finite-order categorical channel for proton
   decay.  The predicted proton lifetime satisfies
   $\tau_{\mathfrak{p}} \gtrsim 10^{34}\;\mathrm{yr}$, consistent with
   null results from Super-Kamiokande and the projected reach of
   Hyper-Kamiokande.  Any observed decay at shorter timescales falsifies
   independence.

2. **Three-body nuclear force.** The Borromean baryon interpretation
   makes the three-nucleon contribution to $^{3}\mathrm{He}$ and
   $^{3}\mathrm{H}$ binding irreducible: no pairwise decomposition of
   the $NN$ potential should close the observed $\approx 8\%$ gap
   between two-body *ab initio* predictions and measured binding
   energies.  Chiral effective field theory calculations should
   continue to require an irreducible $3N$ term.

3. **Lattice-QCD hadron mass ratios.** The ratios
   $\mathfrak{m}_{\mathfrak{p}}/\mathfrak{m}_{\mathfrak{n}}$,
   $\mathfrak{m}_{\Lambda}/\mathfrak{m}_{\mathfrak{p}}$, and
   $\mathfrak{m}_{\Omega^-}/\mathfrak{m}_{\mathfrak{p}}$ carry no
   exact algebraic relation over $\mathbb{Q}(q_0)$.  Lattice QCD
   extrapolations should converge numerically without admitting a
   closed-form polynomial identity to any precision probed.

4. **Holographic information bounds.** The Bekenstein--Hawking and
   covariant entropy bounds are saturated by QOU predictions.
   Black-hole entropy measurements (via gravitational-wave ringdown
   or Hawking-analogue systems) should show no slack relative to the
   area law at the information-bandwidth scale $\mathcal{B}(q_0)$.

5. **Deconfinement temperature.** The lattice value
   $T_c/\Lambda_{\mathrm{QCD}}$ cannot be expressed as an algebraic
   number in $\mathbb{Q}(q_0)$.  Current lattice results
   ($T_c \approx 156\;\mathrm{MeV}$) should continue to resist
   closed-form algebraic fits as precision improves.

**Cleanest discriminator.** High-precision lattice-QCD hadron mass
ratios.  Current FLAG averages reach $\approx 10^{-4}$ relative
precision with no observed algebraic pattern --- a weak preliminary
indication in favour of independence.  A dedicated search at
$10^{-6}$ precision for polynomial relations among
$(\mathfrak{m}_{\mathfrak{p}}, \mathfrak{m}_{\mathfrak{n}},
\mathfrak{m}_{\Lambda}, \ldots)$ over $\mathbb{Q}(q_0)$ would constitute
a direct test.

**Null outcome.** No exact identity emerges at any probed precision
--- consistent with, though not conclusive evidence for, the full
conjecture.
