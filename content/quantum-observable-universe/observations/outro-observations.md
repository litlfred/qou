This closes the physical-observation chapter.  Every categorical
prediction from the previous chapters — fundamental constants
(speed of light $c_q$, Planck constant $\hbar_q$, Hausdorff
dimension $d_H$), particle masses, atomic-mass ratios, hyperbolic
volumes, $\beta$-decay $Q$-values — has been evaluated at the
substrate parameter $q_0 \approx 1.1097$ and compared against
CODATA / NIST measurements with the 100× per-observable accuracy
target spelled out in AGENTS.md §Precision goals.  The interactive
simulators distributed with the paper expose the same numerical
machinery in a browser, so a reader can perturb $q$ and watch the
predictions track the substrate parameter live.

The next chapter (molecular construction) restarts the building-up
process at the molecular scale: the categorical mass predictions
that close here are reused as the energy inputs for the
Hopf-chain construction of covalent bonds, so the precision
achieved here propagates into the chemistry chapters that follow.
