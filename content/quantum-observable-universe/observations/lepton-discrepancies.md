Lepton generations are distinguished by two structural criteria:
the Reeb-vertical degree of the knot's localisation
([rem:descartes-leptons](#rem:descartes-leptons)) and whether the
knot's cyclic symmetry embeds in $S_5$
([rem:symmetry-preserving-leptons](#rem:symmetry-preserving-leptons)).
Electron and muon satisfy both; tau only the first.  Anomalous
magnetic-moment tensions and lepton flavour universality violations
observed at the $B$-factories may reflect this structural asymmetry
rather than new heavy-particle loops --- especially where the
discrepancy tracks specifically with the tau channel.
