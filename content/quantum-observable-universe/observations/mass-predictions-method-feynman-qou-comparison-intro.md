This sub-section validates the mass-prediction methodology
against external comparators.  The arc starts with a Feynman /
QOU comparison that pins where the substrate-level mass
predictions agree with the Feynman amplitude formulation and
where they diverge; the middle blocks specialise to the
neutron–proton mass splitting (a substrate-level prediction
that admits direct comparison against CODATA) and to the
photon-channel Wedderburn chain that controls the leading mass
corrections.  The closing blocks aggregate the validation tables
and pin the methodology's empirical accuracy band.

The output is a validated mass-prediction methodology and an
explicit error bound against CODATA values; subsequent
sub-sections specialise the methodology to per-particle
derivations and to $\beta$-decay $Q$-value predictions.
