At $q = q_0$, the beta-stability formula ([beta-stability](#prop:beta-stability)) predicts a stable neutron number $n_{\mathrm{pred}}(p)$:

$$
  p^*(A) =  \frac{A}{2 + c \cdot A^{2/3}},
  \qquad c = \frac{a_C}{2a_A} = 0.01497.
$$
