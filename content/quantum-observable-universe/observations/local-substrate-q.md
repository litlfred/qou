*Lean sibling: archimedean specialisation over $\mathbb{R}$ (AGENTS.md §7c). Underlying combinatorial content stays generic to any commutative ring with the same $q$-data.*

In any sufficiently small neighbourhood $U \subset M$ of the observable
universe, the substrate parameter $q$
([substrate parameter](#def:substrate-parameter)) satisfies

$$
  q\big|_U =  1.1097 \pm 0.001.
$$

More precisely, $q|_U$ is the unique real root $q > 1$ of the equation

$$
  (1 - q^{-1})^{2}
  = 
  \frac{\mathrm{Vol}(4_1)}{m_\mu / m_e},
$$

where $\mathrm{Vol}(4_1) = 3\mathrm{Cl}_2(\pi/3) \approx :val[Vol_4_1]{precision=6}$ is the
hyperbolic volume of the figure-eight knot and $m_\mu / m_e \approx :val[m_mu_over_e]{precision=6}$
is the CODATA 2022 muon--electron mass ratio.
