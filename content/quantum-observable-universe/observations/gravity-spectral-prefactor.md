*Working over $A = H_n(R, q)$, the universal curvature-rank tail

mass formula
([universal residue](#prop:plancherel-term-7-formulation)) carries

$$
  \alpha \;=\; m_e \cdot 8\,G,
$$

*where $G$ is Newton's gravitational constant in Planck units
($G_{\mathrm{Planck}} = 1$).  Equivalently, the dimensionless
gravitational coupling at substrate parameter $q$ is*

$$
  G_q \;=\;
  \frac{\alpha(q)}{8\, m_e}
  \;=\;
  \frac{1}{8\, m_e}
  \cdot \lim_{k \to \infty} \frac{\mathfrak{m}^{\geq k}_{q,\mathcal{K}}(\mathcal{T})}

  \,\in\, R,

*the limiting ratio of the deep-tower mass to the Plancherel-weighted
character residue.*

prefactor — the same constant for every braid $\beta_\mathcal{K}$ —
extracted from the deep-tower Plancherel residue.  In QOU terms,
gravity is the macroscopic-stratum signature of the categorical mass
functional: at high curvature-rank no specific knot remains, only
the universal Frobenius co-Casimir tail, whose normalisation defines
$G$.

**Consequences.**

1. *No fitting.* $G$ is not an independent parameter; it is the
   prefactor $\alpha/8m_e$ from the spectral tail.  Its numerical
   value is fixed by Planck-unit normalisation.

   ([full pole structure](#prop:full-pole-structure-mass-trace))
   varies with $q$, so does $G_q$.  At $q_0$ the universal
   residue must reproduce the empirical Newton constant; this is a
   non-trivial cross-check between the cosmological and microscopic
   sectors.

3. *Falsifiability.* Any deviation of the deep-tower mass from
   $\alpha \cdot \sum_{k \geq 4} \Phi_k$ would imply either a new
   stratum prefactor or a violation of Plancherel completeness.
