The propositions of this section have utility well beyond settling
the CFS comparison. Whether or not Finster's Lagrangian
$\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{CFS}}$ matches QOU's Plancherel-weighted
character functional
([μ_arch continuum limit](#conj:mu-arch-continuum-limit)), the
following are *now established for QOU on their own terms*. We
collect them as a self-contained summary of what the non-commutative
probability lens contributes to the QOU framework.

**(1) The substrate is a rigorous Voiculescu non-commutative
probability space.** $(H_\infty(q), \mathrm{tr}_M)$ is a type II$_1$
factor with faithful normal tracial state. Every theorem about such
spaces — Speicher's free-cumulant theory, Voiculescu's free entropy,
asymptotic freeness of large random matrices, the Connes
classification of factors — applies to QOU directly. This is not a
metaphor; it is a structural fact provable from the Wenzl positivity
of the Markov trace
([algebraic GNS embedding](#prop:algebraic-gns-embedding)).

**(2) Knot invariants are unified as joint moments.** The Jones
polynomial $J_K(q)$, colored Jones $J_N(K)$, hyperbolic volume
$\mathrm{Vol}(S^3 \setminus K)$, and HOMFLY are unified under a
single rubric: they are joint moments of Hecke transfer matrices
under $\mathrm{tr}_M$, with the polynomial / asymptotic invariant
indexed by which moment or moment-generating-function-asymptotic
one extracts. Kashaev's volume conjecture is *literally* a
free-entropy statement in the non-commutative probability sense.

**(3) Macroscopic continuity has a discrete-substrate explanation.**
The q-CLT
([conj:q-clt-non-commutative](#conj:q-clt-non-commutative))
provides a derivation of why observers bounded by the operational
entropy limit register continuous q-Gaussian distributions for
nominally discrete observables
([archimedean macroscopic washout](#rem:archimedean-macroscopic-washout)).
This is the "bell curves from determinism" problem (issue #649 §2)
resolved via central-limit theory rather than Born-rule postulation.

**(4) Falsifiable q-Gaussian prediction.** The Tsallis–Marsh
attractor result implies that any precision measurement of a
macroscopic observable's distribution should show a q-Gaussian shape
with $q = q_0 \approx 1.110$ — distinguishable from a classical
Gaussian at $O(10^{-3})$ in the tails. This is a falsifiable
prediction that emerges from the discrete substrate, independent of
any external comparison.

**(5) Locality and non-locality are reconciled cleanly.** The
Markov property of single crossings and the Gröbner–Shirshov
non-locality of composite braids are sequential operations on the
same braid word, bridged by Wenzl's product factorisation
([prop:trace-locality-meets-gs-non-locality](#prop:trace-locality-meets-gs-non-locality)).
This resolves the operational tension in the QOU manuscript
between Markov-local trace evaluation and GS-global normal-form
reduction.

**(6) Brane-tower integration is finite and dominated by local
contributions.** The exponential decay of high-wavenumber q-Laplacian
modes gives an explicit geometric-series bound on the
$\hbar_q^{2n}$ tower sum
([prop:brane-tower-q-laplacian-decay](#prop:brane-tower-q-laplacian-decay)).
At $q_0$, the bound shows the single-jet contribution dominates at
the $\approx 99\%$ level, justifying the local-leading-term
truncations used elsewhere in mass-formula derivations.

**(7) Knot ↔ random-matrix bridge.** Through asymptotic freeness of
Wigner matrices (Voiculescu) and Biane's Jucys–Murphy free-probability
limit, the QOU non-commutative probability space is connected to
the large-$N$ limit of random matrix theory. Knot-theoretic
quantities (Jones polynomials of generic braids) have random-matrix
asymptotics; conversely, the spectrum of large random matrices has
a knot-theoretic interpretation. This opens an unexplored channel for
importing random-matrix universality results into the QOU framework
and exporting QOU's knot-topological predictions to random-matrix
data.

**(8) Free cumulants as cluster expansions.** Speicher's free
cumulants — extracted from joint moments via Möbius inversion on
non-crossing partitions — provide a non-commutative cluster
expansion for QOU observables. The $k$-th free cumulant
$\kappa_k^{(q)}(\sigma_{i_1}, \ldots, \sigma_{i_k})$ measures the
genuine $k$-body correlation of the substrate's quantum data,
isolated from lower-order combinatorial contributions. Free
cumulants of QOU operators are an unexplored set of physical
observables.

**What CFS may or may not add.** The
$\mu_{\mathrm{arch}}$ Lagrangian-universality conjecture asks one
specific question: does Finster's $\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{CFS}}$
equal QOU's $Z_{\mathrm{Pl}}$ on the free-probability spectral
limit? The answer to this question — yes or no — affects the
interpretation of the QOU↔CFS continuum lift but does *not* change
items (1)–(8) above. The non-commutative probability framework
delivers them regardless. The CFS comparison is a luxury of
external corroboration; the QOU framework's positive results stand
on their own.
