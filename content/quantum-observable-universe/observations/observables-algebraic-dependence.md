If the [algebraic independence
conjecture](#conj:hyperbolic-volume-transcendence) fails
([mathematical and categorical consequences](#rem:implications-algebraic-dependence)),
five classes of physical data should show characteristic deviations.

1. **Proton decay at a predicted scale.** Collapse of the Yekutieli
   tower at finite height $n$ opens a categorical decay channel with
   rate set by the collapse level: the predicted lifetime becomes
   $\tau_{\mathfrak{p}} \approx \tau_0 \cdot q_0^{-2n}$, where
   $\tau_0$ is fixed by the tower decomposition.  A detection at
   Hyper-Kamiokande in the range
   $10^{34}$--$10^{36}\;\mathrm{yr}$ would falsify independence and
   constrain $n$.

2. **Reducible three-body nuclear force.** A polynomial relation
   among volumes factors the Borromean contribution through Hopf
   (pairwise) pieces.  The $\approx 8\%$ three-body gap in
   $^{3}\mathrm{He}$ and $^{3}\mathrm{H}$ binding should close as a
   specific polynomial in the $NN$ coupling, in contrast to the
   pattern observed in chiral effective field theory.

3. **Exact mass-ratio identities.** The ratios
   $\mathfrak{m}_{\mathfrak{p}}/\mathfrak{m}_{\mathfrak{n}}$,
   $\mathfrak{m}_{\Lambda}/\mathfrak{m}_{\mathfrak{p}}$, and related
   combinations satisfy a polynomial identity over $\mathbb{Q}(q_0)$.
   Lattice-QCD extrapolations converge to a root of a low-degree
   polynomial to arbitrary precision.  No such identity has been
   observed in current FLAG averages.

4. **Slack in information bounds.** The Bekenstein--Hawking bound
   holds with strict inequality at the QOU information-bandwidth
   scale: black-hole entropy measurements should find a gap below
   the area law, or quantum-gravity analogue experiments should reveal
   computational shortcuts not present in the full-independence
   regime.

5. **Algebraic deconfinement ratio.** The lattice value
   $T_c/\Lambda_{\mathrm{QCD}}$ equals a specific algebraic number in
   $\mathbb{Q}(q_0)$.  Continued lattice-QCD refinement should
   converge to a root of an explicit low-degree polynomial, rather
   than to an apparently transcendental limit.

**Cleanest discriminator.** As for independence: high-precision
lattice-QCD hadron mass ratios.  A polynomial relation appearing at
$10^{-6}$ precision falsifies independence; the degree and
coefficients of the relation determine which weaker conjectures
(Borel--Zagier, single-volume irrationality) survive.

**Positive outcome.** A specific polynomial annihilates the
mass-ratio vector --- a direct detection of substrate
algebraicity.  The full QOU conjecture is refuted; the topological
mass formula and Gr\"obner--Shirshov predictions remain intact
([formal chain remark](#rem:formal-chain-transcendence)).
