**Proposition.**
Let $\mathcal{S}_N$ be a composite of $N$ baryon-number units of
strange quark matter, each containing one up, one down, and one
strange quark in a Borromean triple
$\mathcal{B}_3(\mathfrak{u}, \mathfrak{d}, \mathfrak{s})$ linked
by the Hopf link $\mathcal{L}_H$.  Then:

1. **Electromagnetic neutrality.**  The Coulomb charge
   ([Coulomb charge](#def:coulomb-charge)) of each baryon-number
   unit vanishes:

$$
  Q(\mathcal{B}_3(\mathfrak{u}, \mathfrak{d}, \mathfrak{s}))
  =  +\tfrac{2}{3} - \tfrac{1}{3} - \tfrac{1}{3}
  =  0.
$$

   The strangelet is **electromagnetically neutral** for all $q > 0$.
   This is a topological invariant — it depends only on the crossing
   orientation partition within the Borromean structure, not on $q$.

2. **Purely strong crossing energy.**  With $Q = 0$, the
   [crossing energy](#def:crossing-energy) reduces to

$$
  \mathfrak{E}_{\mathrm{cross}}(\mathcal{S}_N)
  = 
  N \cdot \tfrac{1}{2}(n_+ + n_-)(q + q^{-1})
$$

   with **no Coulomb correction**.  The entire crossing energy is
   in the symmetric (strong) channel
   ([decomposition](#prop:crossing-energy-decomposition)).

3. **Enhanced binding per baryon.**  The strange quark
   $\mathfrak{s} = T_{2,5}^{\sigma}$ contributes $5$ negative
   crossings to the Borromean braid word, versus $3$ for the down
   quark $\mathfrak{d} = T_{2,3}^{\sigma}$.  For each baryon unit:

$$
  n_-^{(\mathrm{uds})} = 3 + 5 = 8,
  \qquad
  n_-^{(\mathrm{udd})} = 3 + 3 = 6.
$$

   The binding component per baryon satisfies

$$
  \mathfrak{E}_{\mathrm{bind}}^{(\mathrm{uds})}
  =  8q^{-1}
   >  6q^{-1}
  =  \mathfrak{E}_{\mathrm{bind}}^{(\mathrm{udd})}
$$

   for all $q > 0$.  Strange matter has **strictly more
   strong binding per baryon** than neutron matter.
