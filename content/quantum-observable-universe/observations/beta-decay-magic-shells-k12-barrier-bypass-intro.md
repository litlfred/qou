This sub-section specialises the $\beta$-decay machinery to magic
shells.  The arc establishes the double-magic $\Delta I$ bound,
identifies the $k = 12$ barrier-bypass mechanism, derives the
magic numbers from the substrate parameter $q$, and exhibits the
canonical examples (non-magic ${}^6$Li and single-magic
${}^{18}$O) that test the substrate prediction.  The closing
blocks lift the substrate-level dictionary into the
Bethe–Weizsäcker mass decomposition and identify the Strutinsky
shell-correction term as the graded-trace residue.
