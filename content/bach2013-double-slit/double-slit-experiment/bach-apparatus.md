The apparatus consists of an electron beam with energy 600 eV
($\lambda_{\mathrm{dB}} = 50$ pm) generated by a thermionic tungsten
filament and electrostatic lenses.  The beam is collimated by a slit of
$2\;\mu\text{m}$ width and $10\;\mu\text{m}$ height placed at 16.5 cm.
The double-slit is located 30.5 cm from the collimation slit.

The resulting patterns are magnified by an electrostatic quadrupole lens
and imaged on a two-dimensional microchannel plate and phosphorus
screen, then recorded with a charge-coupled device camera.

The collimation slit, double-slit, and mask were fabricated by focused
ion beam (FIB) milling into 100 nm silicon-nitride membrane windows,
coated with approximately 2 nm of gold.
