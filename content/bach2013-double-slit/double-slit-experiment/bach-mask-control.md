The movable mask was positioned behind the double-slit and translated
from one side to the other.  Three regimes were observed:

1. **Single-slit $P_1$**: mask blocks slit 2, only slit 1 open.
   Single-slit diffraction observed with weak double-slit contribution
   visible in the negative first order (due to finite mask--slit
   separation of 230 $\mu$m).

2. **Double-slit $P_{12}$**: mask centered, both slits fully open.
   Full double-slit diffraction pattern with the central five orders
   visible.  At this position the mask edges are 2500 nm from center,
   affecting only diffraction orders $> 60$.

3. **Single-slit $P_2$**: mask blocks slit 1, only slit 2 open.
   Mirror image of $P_1$, with weak double-slit contribution in the
   positive first order.

The key quantum mechanical relation $P_{12} \neq P_1 + P_2$ was
directly verified: the double-slit pattern exhibits interference fringes
absent from either single-slit distribution.
