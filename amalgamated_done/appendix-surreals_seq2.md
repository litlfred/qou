**Remark (transcendental-accounting ledger — every period in the paper, indexed by Goncharov depth).**

We provide the dual ledger to [`rem:algebraic-core-survives`](#rem:algebraic-core-survives). That block enumerates the algebra that survives the archimedean wall; this block enumerates the transcendentals that appear on the other side. We record which periods appear, where, how they relate, and whether their post-archimedean relations are forced by pre-archimedean $q$-rational structure or are archimedean accidents.

A relation $R(\alpha_1, \ldots, \alpha_m) = 0$ in $\mathbb{Z}[q^{\pm 1}]$ on the algebraic side survives under the archimedean realization functor $\tau_{\mathrm{arch}}$ if and only if it is a $q$-polynomial identity. A relation that holds only among the archimedean values $\tau_{\mathrm{arch}}(\alpha_i) \in \mathbb{R}$ but not among the $\alpha_i \in \mathbb{Z}[q^{\pm 1}]$ is an archimedean accident. The 5-term Bloch–Wigner relation among hyperbolic volumes is a typical example.

The graded-Mikusiński keystone ([`conj:gb-depth-period-correspondence`](#conj:gb-depth-period-correspondence)) predicts the origin of each relation by depth. The table below operationalises this for every period in the QOU paper.

See [Table](#tbl:transcendental-accounting-table-data).

The table makes two structural facts visible:

**Fact 1: depth-2 ceiling for hyperbolic 3-mfd volumes.**
Every hyperbolic 3-manifold volume in the paper occurs at depth 2 (one $\mathrm{Li}_2$ factor). The Bloch–Wigner formula $\mathrm{Vol}(M) = \sum_i D(z_i)$ over an ideal-tetrahedron triangulation forces this. Escaping depth 2 requires a different invariant: $\mathrm{CS}$, hyperbolic 4-manifolds, or the trilogarithm at algebraic points (the `bring-trilog-zeta5` route).

**Fact 2: pre-archimedean $q$-rational relations imply post-archimedean equalities.**
For any two Bring-embeddable knots $K, K'$, if $J_K(q) = J_{K'}(q)$ in $\mathbb{Z}[q^{\pm 1}]$, then $\mathrm{Vol}(K) = \mathrm{Vol}(K')$. The volume $\mathrm{Vol}$ is computed from $J_K^{(N)}$ via a limit depending only on the polynomial. Whether this implication is vacuous (whether there are no Jones-coincident pairs among Bring-embeddable knots) remains open ([`rem:knot-exhaustiveness`](#rem:knot-exhaustiveness) Question 3). A single Jones-coincident pair yields a non-trivial post-archimedean equality forced by $q$-rational structure—exactly the wall-crossing prediction the keystone framework makes explicit.

**Non-surviving relations: archimedean accidents.**
The 5-term Bloch–Wigner relation

$$
D(x) + D(y) + D\!\left(\tfrac{1-x}{1-xy}\right) + D(1 - xy) + D\!\left(\tfrac{1-y}{1-xy}\right) = 0
$$

holds among hyperbolic volumes after $\tau_{\mathrm{arch}}$ but is not a $\mathbb{Q}(q)$-polynomial identity on the pre-archimedean side. It is an archimedean accident at depth 2: a relation in $\mathcal{P}_2 / \mathcal{P}_1$ with no $q$-polynomial witness.

Similar wall-crossing accidents occur at depth 3:
- Goncharov's 22-term relation for $\mathrm{Li}_3$ at 5th roots.
- Euler's 1775 identity $\zeta(2,1) = \zeta(3)$ (an accident from the algebraic side, since the multiple zeta value $\zeta(2,1)$ is a separate iterated integral that evaluates to the single zeta).

**Open question: post-archimedean structure forced by Bring's $S_5$.**
Because Bring's curve has $S_5$ symmetry, the cyclotomic field $\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_5)$ acts on the Bring-natural depth-3 grouplikes. The Galois action $\sigma_a \colon \zeta_5 \mapsto \zeta_5^a$ for $\gcd(a, 5) = 1$ permutes:

$$
\{\mathrm{Li}_3(\zeta_5),\, \mathrm{Li}_3(\zeta_5^2),\, \mathrm{Li}_3(\zeta_5^3),\, \mathrm{Li}_3(\zeta_5^4)\}.
$$

The orbit-sum identity proved by [`bring-trilog-zeta5-depth-3.witness.json`](https://github.com/litlfred/qou/blob/main/computations/bring-trilog-zeta5-depth-3.witness.json) is the Galois-invariant trace of this $S_5$-action. The Galois-non-invariant components—individual $\mathrm{Li}_3(\zeta_5^k)$ values—give a 4-dimensional $\mathbb{Q}$-vector space of depth-3 periods on which $S_5$ acts irreducibly. These are the depth-3 Bring-natural transcendentals. Their post-archimedean structure (linear relations over $\overline{\mathbb{Q}}$) is governed by the motivic Galois group, not by the $q$-rational pre-image. This is a clean concrete instance of post-archimedean structure not visible at the $q$-polynomial level: an $S_5$-equivariant 4-dimensional period space sitting at depth 3, generated by Bring-natural inputs.

**Remark (K-theory $\leftrightarrow$ depth filtration — known at depths 1–2, conjectural at depths $\geq 3$).**

The graded-Mikusiński depth filtration ([`conj:gb-depth-period-correspondence`](#conj:gb-depth-period-correspondence)) on the substrate convolution algebra $\mathcal{H}_q$ admits a K-theoretic description. The depth axis matches the Beilinson–Borel regulator on the algebraic K-theory of the algebraic-number field of definition. This correspondence is a theorem at depths 1 and 2, and Beilinson's conjecture at depths $\geq 3$. We record it to provide the standard period-theoretic language for the framework.

| Depth $n$ | Status | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **theorem** | $K_1(F) = F^\times$; Dirichlet regulator + Baker–Wüstholz on $\log$-linear independence |
| 2 | **theorem (essentially)** | Bloch–Suslin (Suslin 1986, Goncharov 1995): $D$ realises $K_3^{\mathrm{ind}}$ regulator; injectivity after rationalisation. Small open details about generation. |
| 3 | **mostly conjectural** | $K_5^{\mathrm{ind}}(F) \otimes \mathbb{Q}$ has known Borel-rank; whether Goncharov regulator generates $\mathcal{P}_3 / \mathcal{P}_2$ is Beilinson's conjecture |
| $\geq 4$ | **fully conjectural** | Beilinson's conjectures on motivic cohomology |

This Rosetta stone is an analogy that becomes a theorem at low depth and a conjecture at high depth, not a fully-established equivalence at all depths.

| Depth $n$ | Iterated integral grouplike | Algebraic K-theory | Beilinson regulator |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | scalars, $\exp_q$ | $K_1(F) = F^\times$ | $K_1 \to F^\times \otimes \mathbb{R}$ (Dirichlet) |
| 1 | $\log_q$ | $K_1(F)$ via Bloch logs | Dirichlet regulator |
| 2 | $\mathrm{Li}_2^q$ | $K_3^{\mathrm{ind}}(F) \otimes \mathbb{Q}$ | Bloch–Wigner regulator $\to \mathbb{R} / \pi^2 \mathbb{Q}$ |
| 3 | $\mathrm{Li}_3^q$ | $K_5^{\mathrm{ind}}(F) \otimes \mathbb{Q}$ | Goncharov regulator $\to \mathbb{R} / \pi^4 \mathbb{Q}$ |
| $n$ | $\mathrm{Li}_n^q$ | $K_{2n-1}^{\mathrm{ind}}(F) \otimes \mathbb{Q}$ | Beilinson–Borel regulator $\to \mathbb{R} / \pi^{2(n-1)} \mathbb{Q}$ |

Here $F$ is $\overline{\mathbb{Q}}$ or a specific number field $\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_n)$. $K_*^{\mathrm{ind}}$ denotes the indecomposable part of higher K-theory, modulo the image of products of lower K-groups.

The keystone ([`conj:gb-depth-period-correspondence`](#conj:gb-depth-period-correspondence)) asserts $\tau_{\mathrm{arch}}\bigl( G^{(n)}(\mathcal{H}_q) \bigr) \subseteq \mathcal{P}_n$. Under the Beilinson conjectures, the Goncharov filtration $\mathcal{P}_\bullet$ is generated by the Beilinson regulator images of $K_{2n-1}^{\mathrm{ind}}(F) \otimes \mathbb{Q}$ for $n = 1, 2, \ldots$ over all number fields $F$. The keystone factors as:

$$
G^{(n)}(\mathcal{H}_q) \xrightarrow{\;\;\text{evaluation}\;\;} K_{2n-1}^{\mathrm{ind}}(\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_K)) \otimes \mathbb{Q} \xrightarrow{\;\;\text{Beilinson}\;\;} \mathcal{P}_n / \mathcal{P}_{n-1}.
$$

For a Bring-embeddable knot $K$ with shape parameter $z_K$, the field of definition is $\mathbb{Q}(z_K)$. The relevant K-group is $K_{2n-1}^{\mathrm{ind}}(\mathbb{Q}(z_K)) \otimes \mathbb{Q}$.

| Period | K-group (rationally) | Realised by |
|---|---|---|
| $\mathrm{Vol}(4_1) = 2\,\mathrm{Cl}_2(\pi/3)$ | $K_3^{\mathrm{ind}}(\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_6)) \otimes \mathbb{Q}$ | hyperbolic 3-mfd $S^3 \setminus 4_1$ |
| Catalan $G = \mathrm{Cl}_2(\pi/2)$ | $K_3^{\mathrm{ind}}(\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_4)) \otimes \mathbb{Q}$ | Bloch regulator at 4th root of unity |
| $\mathrm{Cl}_3(2\pi/5)$, $S_3(2\pi/5)$ | $K_5^{\mathrm{ind}}(\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_5)) \otimes \mathbb{Q}$ | open ([`conj:cl3-zeta5-geometric-realization`](#conj:cl3-zeta5-geometric-realization)) |
| $\zeta(3)$ | $K_5^{\mathrm{ind}}(\mathbb{Q}) \otimes \mathbb{Q}$ | Borel regulator on $\mathbb{Q}$ |

We introduce K-theory to provide the standard period-theoretic language. The two languages (Goncharov-Hopf-algebra vs Beilinson-K-theory) are fully equivalent modulo Beilinson's conjectures. The Hopf-algebra language is cleaner for structural relations between grouplikes. The K-theory language is cleaner for citing external results.

The keystone's discharge targets in K-theoretic language:
1. **Inclusion target**: $\tau_{\mathrm{arch}}(G^{(n)}) \subseteq \mathcal{P}_n$. We identify $G^{(n)}$ with a subspace of $K_{2n-1}^{\mathrm{ind}}(\mathbb{Q}(z_K)) \otimes \mathbb{Q}$ so that $\tau_{\mathrm{arch}}$ is the Beilinson regulator. The content is this identification.
2. **Sharpness target**: The image generates $\mathcal{P}_n / \mathcal{P}_{n-1}$. Borel's theorem (1977) proves $K_{2n-1}^{\mathrm{ind}}(F) \otimes \mathbb{Q}$ has known explicit rank for $n \geq 2$. Beilinson's conjecture asserts this image generates $\mathcal{P}_n / \mathcal{P}_{n-1}$.

The K-theoretic language partially reduces B1's discharge. Borel pins down dimensions; Beilinson remains the open piece.

**Proposition (Borel's theorem partially discharges the keystone's sharpness target — explicit dimensions for Bring-natural number fields).**

The keystone conjecture ([`conj:gb-depth-period-correspondence`](#conj:gb-depth-period-correspondence)) asserts both an inclusion and a sharpness condition. Via the K-theory translation ([`rem:k-theory-period-rosetta`](#rem:k-theory-period-rosetta)), the sharpness condition factors through Borel's theorem on the algebraic K-theory of number fields. This proposition records the explicit Borel dimensions for each Bring-natural number field.

**Theorem (Borel 1977).** Let $F$ be a number field with $r_1$ real embeddings and $r_2$ pairs of complex embeddings. Then for $n \geq 2$:

$$
\dim_{\mathbb{Q}} \bigl( K_{2n-1}(F) \otimes \mathbb{Q} \bigr) =
\begin{cases}
  r_1 + r_2 & \text{if } n \text{ even}, \\
  r_2 & \text{if } n \text{ odd}.
\end{cases}
$$

The same formula holds for the indecomposable part $K_{2n-1}^{\mathrm{ind}}$.

Each Bring-embeddable knot $K$ has a shape parameter $z_K \in \overline{\mathbb{Q}}^\times$ generating a number field $F_K = \mathbb{Q}(z_K)$. The Borel rank gives the minimum dimension of the Beilinson regulator image at depth $n$.

See [Table 1](#tbl:borel-partial-resolution-table1-data).

At depth 2 (figure-8 row), Borel's theorem gives $\dim_\mathbb{Q} K_3^{\mathrm{ind}}(\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_6)) \otimes \mathbb{Q} = 1$. The Bloch–Wigner regulator maps this injectively after rationalisation. The image is exactly the $\mathbb{Q}$-line spanned by $\mathrm{Vol}(4_1) = 2\,\mathrm{Cl}_2(\pi/3)$. Inclusion and sharpness hold at depth 2 over $\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_6)$. The depth-2 case of B1 is a theorem at the figure-8 row.

At depth 3 (Bring trilog row), Borel's theorem gives $\dim_\mathbb{Q} K_5^{\mathrm{ind}}(\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_5)) \otimes \mathbb{Q} = 2$. The Goncharov regulator maps this rank-2 space to $\mathcal{P}_3 / \mathcal{P}_2$. The Bring trilog witness ([`conj:bring-trilog-depth-3-witness`](#conj:bring-trilog-depth-3-witness)) provides 4 grouplike values $\{\mathrm{Li}_3(\zeta_5^k) : k = 1,2,3,4\}$. Modulo the orbit-sum relation, these span a $\mathbb{Q}$-vector space of exact rank 2, matching Borel's prediction. Whether the entire $\mathcal{P}_3 / \mathcal{P}_2$ over $\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_5)$ is generated is Beilinson's conjecture.

Borel's theorem gives explicit Borel-rank lower bounds on the dimension of $\tau_\mathrm{arch}(G^{(n)})$ in $\mathcal{P}_n / \mathcal{P}_{n-1}$ for every Bring-natural number field. It does not provide the upper bound (Beilinson's conjecture).

For B1, depth 2 is a theorem. Depth 3 has Borel-rank lower bounds as theorems, leaving Beilinson's conjecture as the remaining piece. Depth $\geq 4$ is fully Beilinson-conjectural.

**Proposition (depth-2 identification of $G^{(2)}(\mathcal{H}_q)$ with the Bloch group $K_3^{\mathrm{ind}}(F) \otimes \mathbb{Q}$ — provable via Bloch–Suslin).**

Let $F$ be a number field, $q \in F$ generic, and let $\mathcal{H}_q$ be the substrate convolution Hopf algebra over $F$ ([Frobenius–Hopf substrate](#def:frobenius-hopf-substrate)). The depth-2 identification is:

$$
\tau_{\mathrm{arch}}\!\bigl( G^{(2)}(\mathcal{H}_q) \big/ \mathcal{P}_1 \bigr)_F \cong K_3^{\mathrm{ind}}(F) \otimes \mathbb{Q},
$$

where we interpret the right-hand side via the Bloch–Suslin regulator $K_3^{\mathrm{ind}}(F) \otimes \mathbb{Q} \to \mathbb{R} / \pi^2 \mathbb{Q}$. This identification is a theorem, with proof chain via the Bloch group $\mathfrak{B}(F)$, Suslin's isomorphism, and the archimedean evaluation of $\mathrm{Li}_2^q$.

Combined with [`prop:borel-partial-resolution`](#prop:borel-partial-resolution), depth 2 of [`conj:gb-depth-period-correspondence`](#conj:gb-depth-period-correspondence) is a theorem over every Bring-natural number field $F = \mathbb{Q}(z_K)$. Depth 3 is conditional on Goncharov's motivic conjecture but has all non-Goncharov steps provable. Depth $\geq 4$ is conditional on Beilinson and Goncharov.

**Conjecture (QOU's Hecke-NF + Bring-$S_5$ infrastructure discharges Goncharov's depth-3 conjecture at $F = \mathbb{Q}(\zeta_5)$).**

The depth-2 identification ([`prop:depth-2-identification-bloch-suslin`](#prop:depth-2-identification-bloch-suslin)) is theorem-level via Bloch–Suslin. The depth-3 case requires Goncharov's motivic conjecture. We propose that QOU's infrastructure discharges Goncharov's conjecture for $F = \mathbb{Q}(\zeta_5)$.

Goncharov's conjecture asserts $K_5^{\mathrm{ind}}(F) \otimes \mathbb{Q} \cong \mathfrak{B}_3(F) \otimes \mathbb{Q}$, with $\mathfrak{B}_3(F)$ the weight-3 Bloch group modulo the 22-term relation. For $F = \mathbb{Q}(\zeta_5)$, Borel gives $\dim_\mathbb{Q} K_5^{\mathrm{ind}}(\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_5)) \otimes \mathbb{Q} = 2$. The Goncharov regulator maps this rank-2 space into $\mathcal{P}_3 / \mathcal{P}_2$ over $\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_5)$.

The reduced target for QOU is to prove that $\{\mathrm{Li}_3(\zeta_5^k) : k = 1, 2, 3, 4\}$ modulo the multiplication-formula identity span exactly this rank-2 image, with the only further relation being the Goncharov 22-term restricted to $\zeta_5$-arguments.

**Avenue A — Jones-coincident pair generation.**
For each Jones-coincident pair of Bring-embeddable knots $(K_1, K_2)$ with $J_{K_1}(q) = J_{K_2}(q) \in \mathbb{Z}[q^{\pm 1}]$, we obtain a depth-2 equality $\mathrm{Vol}(K_1) = \mathrm{Vol}(K_2)$ and a depth-3 equality $\mathrm{CS}(K_1) = \pm\mathrm{CS}(K_2)$. Enumerating Jones-coincident pairs yields a generating list of depth-3 relations among CS invariants. If these relations span the kernel of $K_5^{\mathrm{ind}}(\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_5)) \otimes \mathbb{Q} \to \mathbb{R}/\pi^4\mathbb{Q}$, then Goncharov's conjecture at $\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_5)$ is implied by Hecke NF redundancy.

**Avenue B — Direct construction of the 22-term-style relation.**
We express Goncharov's 22-term relation restricted to $z \in \{\zeta_5^k\}$ as an element $G_{22}(\zeta_5) \in \mathbb{Q}\langle \mathrm{Li}_3(\zeta_5), \mathrm{Li}_3(\zeta_5^2), \ldots \rangle$ that equals $0$ modulo $\mathcal{P}_2$. We find a Hecke element $\beta \in H_n(q)$ such that $\tau_{\mathrm{arch}}(\mathrm{tr}_M(\beta))$ at $q \to \zeta_5$ equals $G_{22}(\zeta_5)$, and the Hecke NF of $\beta$ reduces to the identity element modulo depth-$\leq 2$ terms. Then $\mathrm{tr}_M(\beta) = 0 \pmod{\text{depth} \leq 2}$, so $G_{22}(\zeta_5) = 0$ in $\mathcal{P}_3 / \mathcal{P}_2$ by NF reduction alone.
