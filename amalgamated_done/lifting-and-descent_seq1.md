Let $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ be a quantum universe equipped with connection $\nabla$ ([categorical connection](#def:categorical-connection)). The space $J^1(A)$ ([categorical jet](#def:categorical-jet)) strictly inherits a Frobenius–Hopf structure from $A$. The quadruple $(\mathbf{C}, J^1(A), \tau', q)$, carrying the induced fibre functor $\tau'$, formally constitutes a quantum universe.

```tex
\begin{equation}\label{eq:jet-prolongation-diagram}
\begin{tikzcd}[row sep=2.5em, column sep=3em]
  (\mathbf{C}, A, \tau) \ar[r, "\nabla"]
  & (\mathbf{C}, J^1(A), \tau') \\
  A \ar[u, phantom, "\ni"] \ar[r, hook, "j^1"']
  & J^1(A) \ar[u, phantom, "\ni"]
\end{tikzcd}
\qquad\text{(jet prolongation)}
\end{equation}
```

## Proof (sketch)

The first jet $J^1(A)$ of a Frobenius–Hopf object $A$ inherits its Frobenius–Hopf structure via transport along the connection $\nabla$. This relies on the synthetic-differential-geometry construction of the jet bundle for an algebra object (\cite{kock2004}, §1).

1. **Frobenius–Hopf transport.** The jet inclusion $j^1 \colon A \hookrightarrow J^1(A)$ forms an exact algebra map, and $\nabla$ strictly splits $J^1(A) \cong A \oplus (\Omega^1 \otimes A)$. The multiplication, comultiplication, unit, counit, and antipode of $A$ naturally extend to $J^1(A)$ through the Leibniz rule for $\nabla$, establishing $J^1(A)$ as a complete Frobenius–Hopf object.
2. **Induced fibre functor.** Defining $\pi \colon J^1(A) \to A$ as the jet projection, the functor $\tau' = \tau \circ \pi$ remains strictly monoidal, faithful, and exact. Consequently, $(\mathbf{C}, J^1(A), \tau', q)$ precisely satisfies the four quantum-universe axioms with the identical braiding eigenvalue $q$. $\square$

<!-- FILE_START: categorical-q-reeb-flow.md -->
Let $\xi$ denote the Reeb derivation of $A$ ([categorical Reeb derivation](#def:categorical-reeb)) and let $[\cdot,\cdot]_q$ denote the $q$-Lie bracket ([categorical $q$-Lie bracket](#def:categorical-q-lie-bracket)). The *$q$-deformed Reeb derivation* $\xi_q$ on $A$ is precisely:

$$
  \xi_q(a) = q^{-1}\xi(a) + (1 - q^{-1})[\xi, X_a]_q,
$$

where $X_a$ represents the horizontal derivation associated to $a$, satisfying $\pi_H(da) = \iota_{X_a} \varTheta_A$. The **$q$-Reeb flow** defines the unique one-parameter family of automorphisms $\phi_t^q \in \mathrm{Aut}(A)$ identically generated by $\xi_q$.

```tex
\begin{equation}\label{eq:q-reeb-flow-diagram}
\begin{tikzcd}[row sep=2.5em, column sep=3em]
  A \ar[r, "\phi_t^q"] \ar[d, "\xi_q"']
  & A \ar[d, "\xi_q"] \\
  A \ar[r, "\phi_t^q"']
  & A
\end{tikzcd}
\qquad (\phi_0^q = \mathrm{id})
\end{equation}
```

<!-- FILE_START: reeb-primes-as-orbits.md -->
The $q$-Reeb flow $\phi_t^q$ dynamically reformulates the classical picture of primes. Classically, the natural logarithm $\ln x$ acts as the static clock for prime counting: the prime number theorem assigns roughly one prime per $\ln x$ integers, treating primes as discrete, isolated events.

The $q$-deformation strictly replaces this static counting with a continuous dynamical system. The derivation $\xi_q$ explicitly generates a continuous flow on the substrate algebra $A$. The closed orbits of this flow—the periodic trajectories of $\phi_t^q$—identically map to the prime ideals of the underlying geometry. A prime ceases to be a discrete point; it structurally forms a *closed orbit* winding through the brane tower ([recursive brane tower](#def:recursive-brane-tower)).

This dynamic shift forces three exact consequences:

1. **Counting becomes spectral.** Instead of summing $1/\ln p$ over primes $p \le x$ (the classical logarithmic integral), the framework analytically traces the eigenvalues of $\xi_q$. The discrete Reeb spectrum rigorously replaces the continuous logarithm.
2. **Holonomy encodes weight.** The contribution of each prime orbit is not a scalar weight but a precise holonomy matrix—the exact parallel transport of the adjoint representation around the closed orbit. This structurally promotes the abelian counting of classical number theory to a nonabelian, matrix-valued integral.
3. **Stability becomes geometric.** The Riemann hypothesis (the boundary on prime fluctuations) physically reduces to the condition that these holonomy matrices remain strictly unitary. The bound becomes an exact geometric constraint on the Reeb flow rather than an analytic property of a complex function.

The machinery of lifting and descent provides the exact algebraic infrastructure required to execute this mechanism.
