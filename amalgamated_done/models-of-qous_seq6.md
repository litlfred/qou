Archimedean models realise the substrate parameter $q$ as a smoothly varying real or complex number, so they see the substrate only through its differential-geometric shadow. This picture does not record the arithmetic of the $q$-commutation data itself—its integral form and its behaviour in positive characteristic. Recovering that structure requires completions carrying a non-archimedean valuation, in which $q$ is $p$-adic rather than continuous.

The concrete phenomenon these models expose is a lifting problem. We ask whether the substrate can be reconstructed from the algebra generated by its infinitesimal $q$-commutation relations. The Heisenberg uncertainty relation—read as the $q$-commutator $[\hat{X},\hat{P}]_q = i\hbar_q$—is the germ to be lifted. We trace this construction from the enveloping algebras onward, through the Lusztig integral form and its $q$-deformed PBW basis to the $p$-adic and finite-field realisations.

<!-- FILE_START: enveloping-algebras.md -->
The Heisenberg uncertainty relation, $[\hat{X},\hat{P}]_q = i\hbar_q$, provides a single physical $q$-commutator between position and momentum. The enveloping-algebra construction generalizes this germ to reconstruct the full substrate from its infinitesimal $q$-commutation data. 

We solve this lifting problem using the Lusztig integral form of $\mathcal{U}_q(\mathfrak{su}(2))$, its triangular decomposition, and the $q$-deformed Poincaré–Birkhoff–Witt (PBW) basis. The integral form and triangular decomposition factor the generators into component subalgebras. The $q$-deformed PBW basis provides an explicit ordered basis for the enveloping algebra. Together, these algebraic structures lift the infinitesimal $q$-commutation relations back to the substrate.

<!-- FILE_START: p-adic-realisation.md -->
To show that the substrate parameter and the surrounding categorical apparatus are not tied to an archimedean base field, we realise the archimedean tuple over a $p$-adic base. Let $k = \mathbb{Q}_p$ and let $\mathbf{C}$ be the category of $F$-isocrystals over $\mathbb{Q}_p$. This is a $\mathbb{Q}_p$-linear rigid monoidal category. The quantum group $\mathcal{O}_q(\mathrm{SU}(2))$ base-changed to $\mathbb{Q}_p$ provides a Frobenius–Hopf object $A$, where the Frobenius form $\int_A$ arises from the $p$-adic Haar measure. The Frobenius endomorphism $\varphi$ on $\mathbf{C}$ plays the role of the Levi-form contraction. The categorical substrate parameter specialises to $q = p^{-v_p(\det\varphi)} \in \mathrm{End}(\mathbf{1}) \cong \mathbb{Q}_p$. The fibre functor $\tau$ is the forgetful functor to $\mathrm{Vect}_{\mathbb{Q}_p}$. The observability condition $\mathrm{Hom}(\mathbf{1}, \mathrm{Ad}(A)) = 0$ amounts to the irreducibility of the associated $F$-crystal, generalising irreducible connection to the $p$-adic setting.

*Key structures.*

* The classical Frobenius integrability theorem constrains $q$-torsion leaves to be Legendrian. Over $\mathbb{Q}_p$, this is replaced by compatibility of the connection with the Frobenius lift. A $p$-adic instanton connection is integrable if and only if the associated $F$-crystal is unit-root.
* Crystal cohomology (de Rham–Witt complex) plays the role of de Rham cohomology.
* The base-field-independent quantum universe and quantum observable universe provide the categorical framework via Tannakian reconstruction.
