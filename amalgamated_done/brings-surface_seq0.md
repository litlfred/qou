We inhabit a universe in which three facts are immediately apparent:
1. **Three colors.** The strong interaction is mediated by three color charges (red, green, blue) corresponding to the three color tubes of Bring's surface.
2. **Time flow.** The Reeb vector field endows the Frobenius--Hopf object with a preferred flow direction. The substrate parameter $q$ distinguishes past from future, generating an arrow of time.
3. **Spatial isotropy.** Spatial rotations are governed by $\mathrm{SO}(3)$. In the categorical framework, this emerges from the fiber adjoint involution acting on the weight decomposition. The three real generators of $\mathfrak{so}(3)$ correspond to the three independent Reeb degrees on the genus-4 surface.

These three observations are not independent. They are consequences of a single topological constraint: the Euler characteristic $\chi(A) = -6$. Bring's surface, with genus $g = 4$ and $\chi = 2 - 2g = -6$, is the unique algebraic surface that realises all three simultaneously.
