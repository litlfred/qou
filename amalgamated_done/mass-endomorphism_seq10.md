**Proposition (Iwahori–Hecke algebra is Baer-* at generic $q$).**

*Let $R$ be a field of characteristic $0$ and let $q \in R$ be generic (not a root of unity, $q \neq 1$). Equip $H_n(q)$ with the bar involution $* \colon \sigma_i \mapsto \sigma_i^{-1}$, $q \mapsto q^{-1}$ (extended antilinearly). Then $H_n(q)$ is a Baer-*-ring. A self-adjoint idempotent (a projection) generates its right (resp. left) annihilator ideal.*

### Corollary

The central primitive idempotents $\{e_\lambda\}_{\lambda \vdash n}$ form a complete system of orthogonal projections for $H_n(q)$, indexed by partitions of $n$. They are explicit ([`prop:explicit-central-idempotent`](#prop:explicit-central-idempotent)) and bar-aware. Self-conjugate $\lambda$ yield bar-fixed projections (real period witnesses). Conjugate pairs yield bar-swapped projections (chirality-twisted witnesses).

### Failure locus — explicit characterisation

The Baer-* property fails exactly where $H_n(q)$ ceases to be semisimple:

$$
  H_n(q) \text{ is semisimple}
  \;\;\iff\;\;
  [k]_q \;\neq\; 0 \quad\text{for all } k \in \{2, 3, \ldots, n\},
$$

where $[k]_q := (q^k - q^{-k}) / (q - q^{-1})$ is the symmetric quantum integer. The failure locus is therefore:

$$
  \boxed{\;
    \mathrm{NonBaer}\bigl(H_n(q)\bigr)
    \;=\;
    \bigl\{\, q \in \overline{\mathbb{Q}}^\times \,:\,
      q^{2k} = 1 \text{ for some } k \in \{2, \ldots, n\} \,\bigr\}.
  \;}
$$

Equivalently, $q$ forms a primitive $2k$-th root of unity for some $2 \leq k \leq n$. This restricts $q \in \{\zeta_4, \zeta_6, \zeta_8, \ldots, \zeta_{2n}\}$ (primitive forms only). At each such $q$, the Hoefsmit projector becomes singular and the Jacobson radical becomes non-trivial. The annihilator structure then includes nilpotent elements not generated by self-adjoint idempotents.

### Co-location with the Volume Conjecture

The Kashaev–Murakami–Murakami volume conjecture ([`conj:kashaev-volume-conjecture`](#conj:kashaev-volume-conjecture)) sets $q = e^{2\pi i / N}$ for $N \to \infty$. Here $q$ runs over primitive $N$-th roots of unity. As $N$ grows past $n$, $q$ enters the failure locus $\mathrm{NonBaer}(H_n(q))$:

- For $N = 2k$ with $k \leq n$: $q = \zeta_{2k}$ sits in the failure locus. Baer-* fails, Wedderburn fails, and the asymptotic-$N$ regime activates.
- For $N \gg n$: $q = \zeta_N$ may fall outside the failure locus for $H_n(q)$ at fixed $n$. However, as $N \to \infty$, the relevant Hecke order $n$ grows with the colored-Jones level, eventually entering the failure locus.

Therefore, the Volume Conjecture's archimedean asymptotic regime exactly traces the trajectory through the Baer-*-failure locus. The loss of Baer-* projection-generation supplies the structural mechanism of the wall-crossing transition.

This co-location is not coincidental. It forms the Hecke-algebra face of the phenomenon that forces hyperbolic $3$-manifold volumes to appear at depth $2$ in the period filtration. The radical at the failure locus produces the period-class-non-trivial elements upon archimedean specialisation. See [`rem:vc-as-depth-2-instance`](#rem:vc-as-depth-2-instance) for the depth-$2$ reading.

### Significance for B1

Combined with [`conj:gb-depth-period-correspondence`](#conj:gb-depth-period-correspondence), the projections $\{e_\lambda\}$ become explicit witnesses for Goncharov period classes. Under $\tau_{\mathrm{arch}}$, $e_{\lambda_K} \cdot \mathrm{Li}_2^q(z_K)$ generates the period class $[\mathrm{Vol}(K)] \in \mathcal{P}_2 / \mathcal{P}_1$ for any hyperbolic knot $K$ with dominant cell $\lambda_K$ and shape parameter $z_K$. The figure-$8$ instance evaluates as $e_{(2,2)} \cdot \mathrm{Li}_2^q(\zeta_6)$.

**Proposition (Explicit formula for the central primitive idempotents $e_\lambda$ via Lagrange interpolation in Murphy elements).**

*Let $H_n(q)$ be the Iwahori–Hecke algebra over $R$ at generic $q$. The Jucys–Murphy elements $\{J_1, \ldots, J_n\} \subset H_n(q)$ ([`prop:jucys-murphy-central-elements-qou`](#prop:jucys-murphy-central-elements-qou)) commute pairwise and act diagonally on the Hoefsmit seminormal basis. For any standard Young tableau $T$ of shape $\lambda \vdash n$, we have $J_k \cdot v_T = c_T(k; q) \cdot v_T$, where*

$$
  c_T(k; q) \;=\; q^{\,2(j(k) - i(k))}
$$

*is the $q$-content of the box containing $k$ at row $i(k)$ and column $j(k)$ ([`prop:hoefsmit-seminormal-form`](#prop:hoefsmit-seminormal-form)).*

*The central primitive idempotent $e_\lambda \in Z(H_n(q))$ projecting onto the $\lambda$-Wedderburn block ([`prop:wedderburn-weight-canonical`](#prop:wedderburn-weight-canonical)) carries the explicit formula:*

$$
  \boxed{\;
    e_\lambda \;=\; \sum_{T \in \mathrm{SYT}(\lambda)}
    \prod_{k=1}^{n}
    \prod_{\substack{c \in \mathrm{C}(n) \\ c \neq c(T(k); q)}}
    \frac{J_k - c}{c(T(k); q) - c},
  \;}
$$

*where $\mathrm{C}(n) := \{c(\square; q) : \square \in \mu,\; \mu \vdash n\}$ comprises the distinct $q$-contents arising across every partition $\mu \vdash n$. The inner product excludes the eigenvalue $c(T(k); q)$. Each factor forms a Lagrange-interpolation projector onto the eigenspace where $J_k = c(T(k); q)$. This construction completely specifies $Z(H_n(q))$.*

## Cross-checks

1. **Sum-to-identity.** $\sum_{\lambda \vdash n} e_\lambda = 1 \in H_n(q)$, confirming the completeness of the partition decomposition.
2. **Orthogonality.** $e_\lambda e_\mu = \delta_{\lambda \mu} e_\lambda$ via Lagrange interpolation in commuting variables.
3. **Self-adjointness.** For a self-conjugate partition $\lambda = \lambda'$, every box $\square \in \lambda$ has a transposed partner $\square'$ with $c(\square'; q) = c(\square; q^{-1}) = *(c(\square; q))$. The product remains bar-fixed term-by-term: $*(e_\lambda) = e_\lambda$.
4. **Bar-pairing.** For a chiral partition $\lambda \neq \lambda'$, we have $*(e_\lambda) = e_{\lambda'}$. The sum $e_\lambda + e_{\lambda'}$ remains bar-fixed.

## Small $n$ examples

| $n$ | $\lambda$ | bar-class | $e_\lambda$ structure |
|---|---|---|---|
| $2$ | $(2)$ | self-conjugate | $\frac{L_2 - (-q^{-2})}{q^2 - (-q^{-2})} = \frac{q^{-1}\sigma_1 + 1}{q + q^{-1}}$ |
| $2$ | $(1,1)$ | self-conjugate | $\frac{L_2 - q^2}{(-q^{-2}) - q^2} = \frac{q\sigma_1^{-1} - 1}{-q - q^{-1}}$ |
| $3$ | $(3)$ | chiral pair w/ $(1,1,1)$ | $q$-deformed symmetriser |
| $3$ | $(2,1)$ | self-conjugate | standard 2D representation idempotent |
| $4$ | $(2,2)$ | self-conjugate | bar-fixed; witnesses $\mathrm{Vol}(4_1)$ via depth-2 pairing with $\mathrm{Li}_2^q(\zeta_6)$ |

## Significance

The element $e_\lambda$ serves as the explicit, computable generator of the period class assigned to cell $\lambda$. Under [`conj:gb-depth-period-correspondence`](#conj:gb-depth-period-correspondence) and [`prop:hecke-baer-star-generic`](#prop:hecke-baer-star-generic), the class

$$
  \tau_{\mathrm{arch}}\bigl( e_\lambda \cdot \mathrm{Li}_2^q(z) \bigr)
  \;\in\;
  \mathcal{P}_2 / \mathcal{P}_1
$$

forms a Bloch-regulator period computable to arbitrary precision. For the figure-$8$ knot ($\lambda = (2,2)$, $z = \zeta_6$):

$$
  \tau_{\mathrm{arch}}\bigl( e_{(2,2)} \cdot \mathrm{Li}_2^q(\zeta_6) \bigr)
  \;=\; 2\,\mathrm{Cl}_2(\pi/3)
  \;=\; \mathrm{Vol}(S^3 \setminus 4_1).
$$

This is numerically verified in [`conj:bring-figure-eight-depth-2-witness`](#conj:bring-figure-eight-depth-2-witness).

## Provenance

The Murphy-element diagonalization of $Z(H_n(q))$ originates with Murphy (1981, symmetric group) and Dipper–James (1986, Hecke algebra). The Lagrange-interpolation form offers the cleanest computable expression. It is standard in implementations like SageMath's `IwahoriHeckeAlgebra` and the local `pyhecke-native` module.

The declaration `explicit_central_idempotent_via_murphy` defines the Murphy-element construction of the central idempotent. Its proof remains deferred (`sorry`) awaiting formalization of Dipper–James.

**Remark (chirality structure on Goncharov period classes via the bar involution).**

The bar involution $* \colon q \mapsto q^{-1}$ on $H_n(q)$ (equivalently, the Hecke algebra antipode $*(\sigma_i) = \sigma_i^{-1}$) acts on the central primitive idempotents $e_\lambda$ by partition conjugation: $*(e_\lambda) = e_{\lambda'}$, where $\lambda'$ denotes the transposed Young diagram ([`prop:explicit-central-idempotent`](#prop:explicit-central-idempotent) Cross-check 3-4).

Under [`conj:gb-depth-period-correspondence`](#conj:gb-depth-period-correspondence) and [`prop:hecke-baer-star-generic`](#prop:hecke-baer-star-generic), this categorifies the chirality structure of hyperbolic $3$-manifold volumes:

| Cell-class | Bar action on $e_\lambda$ | Knot type | Period type |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Self-conjugate** ($\lambda = \lambda'$) | $*(e_\lambda) = e_\lambda$ | amphichiral knot | $\mathrm{Vol}(K) = \mathrm{Vol}(\overline{K})$ — real period in $\mathcal{P}_2(\mathbb{R})$ |
| **Chiral pair** ($\lambda \neq \lambda'$) | $*(e_\lambda) = e_{\lambda'}$ | chiral knot $K \neq \overline{K}$ | $\mathrm{Vol}(K) = \mathrm{Vol}(\overline{K})$, $\mathrm{CS}(K) = -\mathrm{CS}(\overline{K})$ — period with a Tate twist |

## The figure-8 row

The knot $4_1$ is amphichiral: $4_1 \cong \overline{4_1}$. Its dominant cell in $H_4(q)$ is $\lambda = (2, 2)$, the unique self-conjugate $4$-partition. The bar-fixed projection $e_{(2,2)}$ yields the real period $2\,\mathrm{Cl}_2(\pi/3) \in \mathbb{R}$ without a Tate twist.

## A chiral example: trefoil

The knot $3_1$ is chiral: $3_1 \neq \overline{3_1}$. Its dominant cell $\lambda = (3)$ pairs with $\lambda' = (1, 1, 1)$ under the bar involution: $*(e_{(3)}) = e_{(1, 1, 1)}$. The volume vanishes (the trefoil is not hyperbolic), but the Chern–Simons invariant $\mathrm{CS}(3_1) = \pm \pi^2 / 6$ flips sign under the bar involution. This yields the chirality-twisted depth-$3$ period in $\mathcal{P}_3 / \mathcal{P}_2$. It manifests the exact same chirality structure at depth $3$. See [`conj:bring-trilog-depth-3-witness`](#conj:bring-trilog-depth-3-witness) for a Bring-natural depth-$3$ witness.

## Compatibility with the depth filtration

The bar involution preserves the iterated-integral depth filtration: $* \colon G^{(n)}(\mathcal{H}_q) \to G^{(n)}(\mathcal{H}_q)$. Each depth $G^{(n)}$ decomposes into bar-eigenspaces:

$$
  G^{(n)}(\mathcal{H}_q) \;=\; G^{(n), +} \;\oplus\; G^{(n), -},
$$

where $G^{(n), \pm}$ are the $\pm 1$ eigenspaces of $*$. Under the archimedean completion $\tau_{\mathrm{arch}}$:

- $G^{(n), +} \to \mathcal{P}_n(\mathbb{R})$ produces real periods.
- $G^{(n), -} \to i \cdot \mathcal{P}_n(\mathbb{R})$ produces imaginary periods. Geometrically, these correspond to Chern–Simons-style invariants paired with a $(2\pi i)^n$-factor from the Tate motive.

Each Goncharov period class $\mathcal{P}_n / \mathcal{P}_{n-1}$ inherits a bar-grading. Hyperbolic volume resides in $\mathcal{P}_2^+$, Chern–Simons in $\mathcal{P}_3^-$, and multiple polylog values split according to the amphichirality of the underlying motive.

## Provenance

This categorifies the amphichirality construction introduced in the figure-$8$ block ([`rem:figure-8-archimedean-illustration`](#rem:figure-8-archimedean-illustration)). We recast it as a period-theoretic structure on $\mathcal{P}_\bullet$. The bar involution's role remains structural. It organises period classes by the orientation-reversal symmetry of the motivic substrate.

## Falsifiability

We propose a clean test using a chiral hyperbolic knot $K$ with known $\mathrm{CS}(K) \neq 0$. The simplest such knot is $5_2$, which has $\mathrm{Vol} \approx 2.8281$ and $\mathrm{CS} \approx -0.1530\,\pi^2 \pmod{\pi^2}$. We verify three points:

1. The dominant cell of $5_2$ in $H_4(q)$ (or $H_5(q)$) forms a chiral pair, rather than a self-conjugate cell.
2. The depth-$2$ pairing produces the real $\mathrm{Vol}(5_2)$.
3. The depth-$3$ pairing on the same cell produces the chirality-twisted $\mathrm{CS}(5_2)$.

Discharge remains open. The cell identification at $H_5(q)$ presents the primary technical obstacle.

**Remark (Bring-embeddable knot table).**

The QOU substrate selects the knots whose Seifert genus is bounded by Bring's surface genus ([`prop:knot-eigenbasis-complete`](#prop:knot-eigenbasis-complete), [`rem:knot-exhaustiveness`](#rem:knot-exhaustiveness)):

$$
  \mathrm{Knots}_{\mathrm{Bring}}
  \;:=\;
  \bigl\{ K \,:\, g(K) \;\leq\; 4 \bigr\}.
$$

By the [generation completeness theorem](#prop:knot-eigenbasis-complete), a finite list of torus-knot generators combined with skein crossing changes generates this class. The recursive atom-knot atlas lists the canonical low-genus representatives.

### Genus stratification and generators

The full enumeration of Bring-embeddable knots by genus appears in [Table: Bring-embeddable knot table](#tbl:bring-embeddable-knots-table-data). Every Bring-embeddable knot derives from seven torus-knot generators:

$$
  \{T(2, 3),\; T(2, 5),\; T(2, 7),\; T(2, 9),\; T(3, 4),\; T(3, 5),\; T(5, 2)\}
$$

via skein crossing changes. These generators satisfy $g(T(p, q)) = (p-1)(q-1)/2 \in [1, 4]$, and appropriate skein moves preserve the $g \leq 4$ bound.

### Falsifiability and depth-2 witnesses

For each Bring-embeddable knot $K$ with dominant cell $\lambda_K$ in $H_n(q)$ and shape parameter $z_K \in \overline{\mathbb{Q}}^\times$:

$$
  [\mathrm{Vol}(K)] \;=\; \tau_{\mathrm{arch}}\bigl( e_{\lambda_K}
  \cdot \mathrm{Li}_2^q(z_K) \bigr) \;\in\; \mathcal{P}_2 / \mathcal{P}_1.
$$

If [`conj:gb-depth-period-correspondence`](#conj:gb-depth-period-correspondence) holds, every entry in the principled atlas must satisfy:

$$
  \mathrm{Vol}(K) \;\stackrel{!}{=}\; \tau_{\mathrm{arch}}\bigl(
  e_{\lambda_K} \cdot \mathrm{Li}_2^q(z_K) \bigr).
$$

A single counter-example falsifies B1. We verified the figure-$8$ row ([`conj:bring-figure-eight-depth-2-witness`](#conj:bring-figure-eight-depth-2-witness)), but confirming nodes like $5_2$, $6_2$, and $6_3$ remains necessary. This verification entails identifying $\lambda_K$ and $z_K$, and computing the projection $e_{\lambda_K}$ as the explicit Goncharov-period witness ([`prop:explicit-central-idempotent`](#prop:explicit-central-idempotent)).
