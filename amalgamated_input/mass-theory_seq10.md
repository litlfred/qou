construction (operator-selection LP, universal braid branching,

The structural pattern unifying these blocks is the same one the
preceding sub-section established at the operator level: the
Wedderburn decomposition of $H_n(q)$ assigns each Young partition
$\lambda$ a weight $y_\lambda$, and only the small-rank rows
$(\lambda \in \{(n), (n-1,1), (n-2,2)\})$ carry non-negligible
Frobenius mass at $q_0$. Magic shells are then the integer $A$ at
which a row's contribution stabilises against further Hoefsmit
branching — the two-row stratum freezes when the SW-3-root
denominator $\prod_{i<j}(1 - q^{2(\lambda_i - \lambda_j + j - i)})$
saturates, and the $V_{21}$ cosine symmetrisation pins the
relative phase of the trivial against the standard 1d-irrep.
The Hecke closed-form character makes both vanishings explicit,
and the Markov-trace selection rule names which partition wins.

The output is the substrate-level magic-shell prediction tabulated
against the empirical noble-gas sequence; the next sub-section's
vertex-factorisation Feynman bridge consumes this prediction
wholesale.
