The [algebraic independence conjecture](#conj:hyperbolic-volume-transcendence) is falsifiable against five classes of physical data. Under independence ([mathematical and categorical consequences](#rem:implications-algebraic-independence)), each class behaves as follows.

1. **Proton decay searches.** Non-collapse of the brane tower forbids any finite-order categorical channel for proton decay. The predicted proton lifetime satisfies $\tau_{\mathfrak{p}} \gtrsim 10^{34}\;\mathrm{yr}$. Any observed decay at shorter timescales falsifies independence.

2. **Three-body nuclear force.** The Borromean baryon interpretation makes the three-nucleon contribution to $^{3}\mathrm{He}$ and $^{3}\mathrm{H}$ binding irreducible. No pairwise decomposition of the $NN$ potential closes the observed $\approx 8\%$ gap.

3. **Lattice-QCD hadron mass ratios.** The ratios $\mathfrak{m}_{\mathfrak{p}}/\mathfrak{m}_{\mathfrak{n}}$, $\mathfrak{m}_{\Lambda}/\mathfrak{m}_{\mathfrak{p}}$, and $\mathfrak{m}_{\Omega^-}/\mathfrak{m}_{\mathfrak{p}}$ carry no exact algebraic relation over $\mathbb{Q}(q_0)$.

4. **Holographic information bounds.** The Bekenstein--Hawking bounds are saturated. Black-hole entropy measurements show no slack relative to the area law.

5. **Deconfinement temperature.** The lattice value $T_c/\Lambda_{\mathrm{QCD}}$ cannot be expressed algebraically in $\mathbb{Q}(q_0)$. 

**Cleanest discriminator.** High-precision lattice-QCD hadron mass ratios reaching $\approx 10^{-4}$ relative precision show no algebraic pattern. This favors independence. A dedicated $10^{-6}$ precision search tests this directly.

**Null outcome.** No exact identity emerges at any probed precision. This is consistent with the conjecture.
