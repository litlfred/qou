<!-- FILE_START: glossary-quantum-flatness.md -->
Let $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ be a quantum observable universe with
gauge group $G_q = \mathrm{Aut}^{\otimes}(\tau)$
([quantum gauge group](#def:quantum-gauge-group)) and connection
$\nabla$ on $A$ ([categorical connection](#def:categorical-connection)).
Let $\mathrm{INN}(G_q)$ denote the inner-automorphism $2$-group of
$G_q$, with $2$-form datum $B \in \Omega^2(A) \otimes \mathrm{Ad}(A)$.

The connection $\nabla$ is *quantum flat* if the curvature $F_\nabla$
equals the $2$-form datum:

$$
  F_\nabla = B.
$$

This is not an independent axiom. It is a derived consequence of the
instanton condition ([categorical instanton](#def:categorical-instanton))
combined with the simplicity constraint
$B \in \Gamma(\Lambda^+ H^* \otimes \mathrm{Ad}(P))$:

```tex
\begin{equation}\label{eq:quantum-flatness-diagram}
\begin{tikzcd}[row sep=2.5em, column sep=3em]
  F_\nabla \ar[r, equal, "\text{instanton}"]
    \ar[dr, equal, "F_\nabla = B"']
  & F_\nabla^+ \ar[d, equal, "\text{simplicity}"] \\
  & B
\end{tikzcd}
\qquad\text{(quantum flatness)}
\end{equation}
```

1. The instanton equation $\star_q F_\nabla = F_\nabla$ forces
   $F_\nabla = F_\nabla^+$ (self-dual curvature).
2. The simplicity constraint restricts $B = F_\nabla^+$.
3. Combining: $F_\nabla = B$.

:refterm[Quantum flatness]{#quantum-flatness} therefore encodes the condition that the $2$-group
gauge theory of $\mathrm{INN}(G_q)$ admits no independent
$2$-connection — the $2$-form datum is fully determined by the
ordinary connection.
<!-- FILE_END: glossary-quantum-flatness.md -->

<!-- FILE_START: glossary-reeb-gauge-group.md -->
Let $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ be a quantum observable
universe over $R$ with maximal torus $T$
([maximal torus](#def:maximal-torus)) and Reeb derivation
$\xi$ ([categorical Reeb](#def:categorical-reeb)).  The
**:defterm[Reeb gauge group]{#reeb-gauge-group}** is the $R$-group scheme

$$
  U_1(R)  :=  T
  =  \mathrm{Aut}^{\otimes}(\tau|_{\langle V \rangle})
$$

— the maximal torus of the gauge group $G$, viewed as a
group scheme over $R$.  Over $\mathbb{C}$, this recovers
$U(1) = \{e^{i\varTheta}\}$.  Over a general commutative ring
$R$, it is the multiplicative group of norm-one elements
in the rank-one weight lattice.

The Reeb gauge group plays six roles simultaneously, all
arising from the single datum $T \subset G$:

1. **Gauge group of the Reeb fibre.**
   The projective line $\mathbb{P}(V) = G/T$
   ([projective line](#def:projective-line-object)) is a
   principal $U_1(R)$-bundle: the fibre over each point is
   a copy of $T$ acting by scalar multiplication on the
   weight spaces.  The Reeb derivation $\xi$ is the
   infinitesimal generator of this $U_1(R)$-action.

2. **Weight grading.**
   The $U_1(R)$-action on $\Omega^k(A)$ induces the weight
   decomposition $\alpha = \alpha_H + \alpha_V$
   ([weight decomposition](#def:weight-decomposition)):
   $\alpha_H \in \ker(\iota_\xi)$ (weight $0$, horizontal)
   and $\alpha_V$ (non-zero weight, vertical).  The
   integers labelling the weights are the characters of
   $U_1(R)$.

3. **Contact structure.**
   The contact affine chart $U_\xi \subset \mathbb{P}(V)$
   ([contact affine chart](#def:contact-affine-chart)) is
   the complement of the $U_1(R)$-fixed locus.  The
   contact form $\varTheta_A$ satisfies $\varTheta_A(\xi) = 1$
   and $\iota_\xid\varTheta_A = 0$ — these are the defining
   equations of a connection on the $U_1(R)$-bundle.

4. **Commutative observation direction.**
   In the adjoint bundle $\mathrm{Ad}(J^1\mathcal{S})$,
   the $U_1(R)$-direction is the Cartan subalgebra — the
   **one direction** in the colour fibre where intents
   commute: $[\mathfrak{O}_1, \mathfrak{O}_2] = 0$
   when both act along $\xi$.  The remaining two
   directions (the off-diagonal generators $E_{\pm}$ of
   $\mathfrak{su}(2)$) are where fermionic uncertainty
   lives.

5. **Longitudinal backbone.**
   In the harmonic decomposition of $H^1(\mathcal{S}_n, \mathrm{ad}\mathcal{S}_n)$, the $U_1(R)$-modes
   are the **longitudinal backbone**: steady-state flows
   aligned with the Reeb direction.  The off-diagonal
   modes ($E_{\pm}$) are the **ladder modes**: entangling
   vortices that braid neighbouring fibres.

6. **Arrow of time.**
   The Reeb flow $\phi_t^q$ is the one-parameter subgroup
   generated by $\xi$.  Under the braiding-induced ordering
   $\prec_c$
   ([temporal braiding](#def:quantum-observable-universe)),
   forward time is the direction of this flow.
   The $U_1(R)$-action on the function algebra
   $\mathcal{O}(\mathrm{Conf}_n(\mathcal{S}_p))$ of the
   configuration space
   ([categorical transfer matrix](#def:categorical-transfer-matrix))
   grades each insertion coordinate $z_i$ by Reeb degree —
   the number of vertical windings.

These six roles are not independent structures imposed
separately — they are the same $U_1(R)$ seen from different
angles.  The maximal torus $T$ determines all of them:
the gauge bundle, the grading, the contact geometry, the
commutative sector, the harmonic backbone, and the clock.
<!-- FILE_END: glossary-reeb-gauge-group.md -->

<!-- FILE_START: glossary-categorical-transport.md -->
Let $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ be a quantum observable universe with
instanton $\nabla$ ([categorical instanton](#def:categorical-instanton)) and
$q$-deformed exterior derivative $d_q$
([categorical $q$-exterior derivative](#def:categorical-q-ext-deriv)). The
:defterm[categorical transport equation]{#categorical-transport} for a section $\psi \in \tau(J^1(A))$
([categorical jet](#def:categorical-jet)) is

```tex
\begin{equation}\label{eq:categorical-transport}
  \hbar_q\pi_V(\nabla\psi) =  \hat{H}_q(\psi),
\end{equation}
```

where $\hat{H}_q \colon \tau(J^1(A)) \to \tau(J^1(A))$ is the
*$q$-Hamiltonian* endomorphism
$\hat{H}_q = -\tfrac{1}{2}\hbar_q\Delta_q + V_q$
(with $V_q$ the potential arising from the curvature $F_\nabla$).
The following diagram encodes the transport as the bottom row of the
main commutative diagram:

```tex
\begin{equation}\label{eq:transport-cd}
\begin{tikzcd}[row sep=2.5em, column sep=3em]
  \tau(J^1(A)) \ar[r, "\nabla"] \ar[d, "j^1"']
  & \tau(J^1(A)) \otimes \Omega^1 \ar[d, "\pi_V"] \\
  \tau(A) \ar[r, "\hat{H}_q"']
  & \tau(J^1(A))
\end{tikzcd}
\end{equation}
```

<!-- FILE_START: glossary-categorical-q-torsion-leaf.md -->
A :defterm[$q$-torsion leaf]{#categorical-q-torsion-leaf} is a maximal sub-object
$L \hookrightarrow A$ such that:

1. **Horizontality:** $L \subset \ker(\pi_V)$
   (all forms on $L$ are horizontal).
2. **Flow invariance:** $\phi_t^q(L) = L$ for all
   $t$ (the $q$-Reeb flow preserves $L$).

<!-- FILE_START: glossary-descent.md -->
Let $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ be a quantum observable universe and let
$\{A_n\}_{n \geq 0}$ be the
([recursive brane tower](#def:recursive-brane-tower)).  The *:refterm[descent]{#descent}* is the
reverse traversal of the tower: starting from level $n$ one passes to
level $n{-}1$ by applying the restriction morphism
$r_n \colon A_n = J^1(A_{n-1}) \to A_{n-1}$ (the categorical retraction
of the jet embedding).  In the archimedean realisation, this restricts
the connection and bundle to the base manifold $M_{n-1}$.
By [QU-to-QOU lifting](#thm:qu-to-qou)(1), the :refterm[descent]{#descent} terminates in finitely many
steps, bounded by $\mathrm{Krulldim} Z(\mathrm{End}_{\mathbf{C}}(A))$.
<!-- FILE_END: glossary-descent.md -->
