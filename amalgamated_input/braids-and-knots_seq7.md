<!-- FILE_START: su2-so3-intro.md -->
$\mathrm{SU}(2)$ and $\mathrm{SO}(3)$ are the natural gauge groups
on the substrate side and the archimedean side respectively.  This
short section fixes the basis, the first-order Taylor relation
between the generators, and the quaternionic picture of $\mathrm{SO}(3)$
that drives the chapter's later identification of the
substrate-parametrised gauge group $\mathrm{SU}_q(2)$ as the
deformation of $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ along the substrate flow.
<!-- FILE_END: su2-so3-intro.md -->

<!-- FILE_START: su2-basis.md -->
Write $\tau_1,\tau_2,\tau_3$ for the Pauli matrices and set

```tex
\begin{equation}\label{eq:su2-basis}
  e_a=-\tfrac{i}{2}\tau_a,\qquad a=1,2,3,
\end{equation}
```

so that the Lie bracket is

```tex
\begin{equation}\label{eq:su2-commutator}
  [e_a,e_b]=\epsilon_{abc}e_c.
\end{equation}
```
<!-- FILE_END: su2-basis.md -->

<!-- FILE_START: first-order-taylor.md -->
The Lie bracket captures the first-order
behaviour of the group operation: for elements $A,B$ near the identity,

$$
  e^A e^B =  e^{A+B+\tfrac{1}{2}[A,B]+\cdots},
$$

The commutator $[e_a,e_b]$ is the first-order Taylor polynomial of
the non-commutativity. Higher-order corrections (the full
Baker--Campbell--Hausdorff series) encode the complete nonlinear group
law and reappear accordingly in the universal enveloping algebra
([Section](#sec:enveloping-algebras)).
<!-- FILE_END: first-order-taylor.md -->

<!-- FILE_START: so3-generators.md -->
Under the adjoint map to $\mathrm{SO}(3)$ the generators of
$\mathrm{Dic}_3$ ([SU(2) generators](#def:su2-generators)) have images

```tex
\begin{equation}\label{eq:so3-generators}
  R_\alpha
  =\begin{pmatrix}
    -\frac{1}{2}&-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}&0\\[3pt]
    \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}&-\frac{1}{2}&0\\[3pt]
    0&0&1
  \end{pmatrix},\qquad
  R_\beta
  =\begin{pmatrix}
    1&0&0\\[2pt]
    0&-1&0\\[2pt]
    0&0&-1
  \end{pmatrix}:
\end{equation}
```

The rotation $R_\alpha$ is a rotation by $2\pi/3$ about the $e_3$-axis (3-cycle) and
$R_\beta$ is a $\pi$-rotation about $e_1$ (2-cycle).
<!-- FILE_END: so3-generators.md -->

<!-- FILE_START: quaternion-so3-motivation.md -->
The quaternionic basis appearing in the spectral resolvents below is not
an arbitrary choice: it is forced by the structure group
$G = \mathrm{SU}(2)$ of the principal bundle $P \to M_S$
([Bring's QOU 4-tuple](#rem:brings-qou-4tuple)).

The key identifications are:

1. **$\mathrm{Sp}(1) \cong \mathrm{SU}(2)$.**
   The group of unit quaternions
   $\mathrm{Sp}(1) = \{\mathbf{q} \in \mathbb{H} : \lvert\mathbf{q}\rvert = 1\}$
   is isomorphic to $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ via

   $$
     a + b\mathbf{i} + c\mathbf{j} + d\mathbf{k}
      \longmapsto 
     \begin{pmatrix} a + bi & -c + di \\ c + di & a - bi \end{pmatrix}.
   $$

   Under this map the Pauli generators
   $e_a = -\frac{i}{2}\tau_a$
   ([SU(2) generators](#def:su2-generators)) correspond to the half-quaternion
   basis $\frac{1}{2}\mathbf{i}, \frac{1}{2}\mathbf{j}, \frac{1}{2}\mathbf{k}$.

2. **$\mathrm{Im}\mathbb{H} \cong \mathfrak{su}(2)$ as Lie algebras.**
   The purely imaginary quaternions form a $3$-dimensional real Lie algebra under
   the commutator bracket
   $[p, q] = pq - qp$.  In the half-quaternion basis
   $e_1 = \frac{1}{2}\mathbf{i}$, $e_2 = \frac{1}{2}\mathbf{j}$,
   $e_3 = \frac{1}{2}\mathbf{k}$, the structure constants are exactly
   $[e_a, e_b] = \epsilon_{abc}e_c$
   (e.g.\ $[\tfrac{1}{2}\mathbf{i}, \tfrac{1}{2}\mathbf{j}] = \tfrac{1}{4}(\mathbf{ij} - \mathbf{ji}) = \tfrac{1}{2}\mathbf{k} = e_3$).

3. **Adjoint action $=$ double cover $\mathrm{SU}(2) \to \mathrm{SO}(3)$.**
   Conjugation by a unit quaternion $\mathbf{q}$ acts on
   $\mathrm{Im}\mathbb{H} \cong \mathbb{R}^3$ by orientation-preserving
   rotation:

   $$
     \mathrm{Ad}_{\mathbf{q}}\colon
     \mathbf{v}  \longmapsto  \mathbf{q}\mathbf{v}\mathbf{q}^{*},
     \qquad
     \mathbf{v} \in \mathrm{Im}\mathbb{H}.
   $$

   This is the adjoint representation, and the map
   $\mathbf{q} \mapsto \mathrm{Ad}_{\mathbf{q}}$ is the double cover
   $\mathrm{Sp}(1) \twoheadrightarrow \mathrm{SO}(3)$.  In particular, the
   $\mathrm{SO}(3)$ holonomy of the chiral sheets
   ([SO(3) generators](#def:so3-generators)) lifts to $\mathrm{Sp}(1)$
   conjugation in the quaternion basis.

Consequently, writing the spectral resolvents in the
$\{\mathbf{i}, \mathbf{j}, \mathbf{k}\}$ basis is the same as expanding
an $\mathfrak{su}(2)$-valued $1$-form along the generators of the Lie
algebra of the structure group.  The space-like/time-like decomposition
$(\omega_1 \mid \omega_2, \omega_3)$ then corresponds to the split of
$\mathfrak{su}(2) \cong \mathfrak{u}(1) \oplus \mathfrak{m}$ into the
Cartan subalgebra (generated by $\mathbf{i}$) and its orthogonal
complement.
<!-- FILE_END: quaternion-so3-motivation.md -->
