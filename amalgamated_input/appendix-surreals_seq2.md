**Remark (transcendental-accounting ledger — every period in the
paper, indexed by Goncharov depth).**

The dual ledger to
[`rem:algebraic-core-survives`](#rem:algebraic-core-survives): that
block enumerates *what algebra survives* the archimedean wall; this
block enumerates *what transcendentals appear on the other side* —
which periods, where, how related, and whether their post-archimedean
relations are forced by pre-archimedean $q$-rational structure or
are archimedean accidents.

### Significance (the wall-crossing question)

A relation $R(\alpha_1, \ldots, \alpha_m) = 0$ in $\mathbb{Z}[q^{\pm 1}]$
on the algebraic side **survives** under the archimedean realization
functor $\tau_{\mathrm{arch}}$ iff it is a $q$-polynomial identity.
A relation that holds *only* among the archimedean values
$\tau_{\mathrm{arch}}(\alpha_i) \in \mathbb{R}$ but not among the
$\alpha_i \in \mathbb{Z}[q^{\pm 1}]$ is an **archimedean accident**
— typically a transcendence relation among periods (e.g. the 5-term
Bloch–Wigner relation among hyperbolic volumes).

The graded-Mikusiński keystone
([`conj:gb-depth-period-correspondence`](#conj:gb-depth-period-correspondence))
predicts which side each relation comes from by depth. The table
below operationalises this for every period that appears in the
QOU paper.

### Master table — every transcendental at a glance

See [Table](#tbl:transcendental-accounting-table-data).

### Two structural facts the table makes visible

#### Fact 1 — depth-2 ceiling for hyperbolic 3-mfd volumes

Every hyperbolic 3-manifold volume in the paper is depth 2 — one
$\mathrm{Li}_2$ factor. This is **not** a paper-specific claim;
it is forced by the Bloch–Wigner formula $\mathrm{Vol}(M) =
\sum_i D(z_i)$ over ideal-tetrahedron triangulation. Escaping
depth 2 requires a different invariant: $\mathrm{CS}$, hyperbolic
4-manifolds, or trilog at algebraic points (the `bring-trilog-zeta5`
route).

#### Fact 2 — pre-archimedean $q$-rational relations $\Rightarrow$ post-archimedean equalities

For any two Bring-embeddable knots $K, K'$:

$$
  J_K(q) = J_{K'}(q) \;\;\text{in } \mathbb{Z}[q^{\pm 1}]
  \;\;\;\Longrightarrow\;\;\;
  \mathrm{Vol}(K) = \mathrm{Vol}(K').
$$

Reason: $\mathrm{Vol}$ is computed from $J_K^{(N)}$ via a limit
that depends only on the polynomial. Whether the implication is
*vacuous* (no Jones-coincident pairs among Bring-embeddable knots —
i.e. Jones-faithfulness on this class) is open
([`rem:knot-exhaustiveness`](#rem:knot-exhaustiveness) Question 3).
**A single Jones-coincident pair would yield a non-trivial
post-archimedean equality forced by $q$-rational structure** —
exactly the "wall-crossing" prediction the keystone
framework should make explicit.

### Non-surviving relations: archimedean accidents

The 5-term Bloch–Wigner relation

$$
  D(x) + D(y) + D\!\left(\tfrac{1-x}{1-xy}\right) + D(1 - xy)
  + D\!\left(\tfrac{1-y}{1-xy}\right) \;=\; 0
$$

holds among hyperbolic volumes (after $\tau_{\mathrm{arch}}$) but is
**not** a $\mathbb{Q}(q)$-polynomial identity on the pre-archimedean
side. It is an archimedean accident at depth 2 — a relation in
$\mathcal{P}_2 / \mathcal{P}_1$ that has no $q$-polynomial witness.

Similar wall-crossing accidents at depth 3:
- Goncharov's 22-term relation for $\mathrm{Li}_3$ at 5th roots;
- $\zeta(2,1) = \zeta(3)$ (Euler 1775; an accident from the
  algebraic side, since the multiple zeta value $\zeta(2,1)$ is
  a separate iterated integral that just happens to evaluate to
  the single zeta).

### Open question: post-archimedean structure forced by Bring's $S_5$

Because Bring's curve has $S_5$ symmetry, the cyclotomic field
$\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_5)$ acts on the Bring-natural depth-3 grouplikes.
Galois action $\sigma_a \colon \zeta_5 \mapsto \zeta_5^a$ for
$\gcd(a, 5) = 1$ permutes:

$$
  \{\mathrm{Li}_3(\zeta_5),\, \mathrm{Li}_3(\zeta_5^2),\, \mathrm{Li}_3(\zeta_5^3),\, \mathrm{Li}_3(\zeta_5^4)\}.
$$

The orbit-sum identity proved by the
[`bring-trilog-zeta5-depth-3.witness.json`](https://github.com/litlfred/qou/blob/main/computations/bring-trilog-zeta5-depth-3.witness.json)
is the **Galois-invariant trace** of this $S_5$-action. The
**Galois-non-invariant** components — individual $\mathrm{Li}_3(\zeta_5^k)$
values — give a 4-dim $\mathbb{Q}$-vector space of depth-3 periods
on which $S_5$ acts irreducibly. These are *the* depth-3
Bring-natural transcendentals; their post-archimedean structure
(linear relations over $\overline{\mathbb{Q}}$) is governed by the
motivic Galois group, not by the $q$-rational pre-image.

This is the cleanest concrete instance of "post-archimedean
structure not visible at the $q$-polynomial level": an
$S_5$-equivariant 4-dimensional period space sitting at depth 3,
generated by Bring-natural inputs.

**Remark (K-theory $\leftrightarrow$ depth filtration — known at depths 1–2,
conjectural at depths $\geq 3$).**

The graded-Mikusiński depth filtration
([`conj:gb-depth-period-correspondence`](#conj:gb-depth-period-correspondence))
on the substrate convolution algebra $\mathcal{H}_q$ admits a
K-theoretic description: the depth axis matches the Beilinson–Borel
regulator on algebraic K-theory of the algebraic-number field of
definition. **This correspondence is a theorem at depths 1 and 2,
and Beilinson's conjecture at depths $\geq 3$.** Recording it
provides the standard period-theoretic language for the framework.

### Strength of the bridge by depth

| Depth $n$ | Status | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **theorem** | $K_1(F) = F^\times$; Dirichlet regulator + Baker–Wüstholz on $\log$-linear independence |
| 2 | **theorem (essentially)** | Bloch–Suslin (Suslin 1986, Goncharov 1995): $D$ realises $K_3^{\mathrm{ind}}$ regulator; injectivity after rationalisation. Small open details about generation. |
| 3 | **mostly conjectural** | $K_5^{\mathrm{ind}}(F) \otimes \mathbb{Q}$ has known Borel-rank; whether Goncharov regulator generates $\mathcal{P}_3 / \mathcal{P}_2$ is Beilinson's conjecture |
| $\geq 4$ | **fully conjectural** | Beilinson's conjectures on motivic cohomology |

The framing is honest: "Rosetta" is an *analogy that becomes a
theorem at low depth and a conjecture at high depth*, not a
fully-established equivalence at all depths.

### The K-theoretic dictionary

| Depth $n$ | Iterated integral grouplike | Algebraic K-theory | Beilinson regulator |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | scalars, $\exp_q$ | $K_1(F) = F^\times$ | $K_1 \to F^\times \otimes \mathbb{R}$ (Dirichlet) |
| 1 | $\log_q$ | $K_1(F)$ via Bloch logs | Dirichlet regulator |
| 2 | $\mathrm{Li}_2^q$ | $K_3^{\mathrm{ind}}(F) \otimes \mathbb{Q}$ | Bloch–Wigner regulator $\to \mathbb{R} / \pi^2 \mathbb{Q}$ |
| 3 | $\mathrm{Li}_3^q$ | $K_5^{\mathrm{ind}}(F) \otimes \mathbb{Q}$ | Goncharov regulator $\to \mathbb{R} / \pi^4 \mathbb{Q}$ |
| $n$ | $\mathrm{Li}_n^q$ | $K_{2n-1}^{\mathrm{ind}}(F) \otimes \mathbb{Q}$ | Beilinson–Borel regulator $\to \mathbb{R} / \pi^{2(n-1)} \mathbb{Q}$ |

(Here $F = \overline{\mathbb{Q}}$ or a specific number field
$\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_n)$ for the cyclotomic-orbit-restricted version;
$K_*^{\mathrm{ind}}$ denotes the *indecomposable* part of higher
K-theory, modulo the image of products of lower K-groups —
equivalently, the part that detects "new" periods.)

### Exactness of the dictionary (heuristic)

The keystone ([`conj:gb-depth-period-correspondence`](#conj:gb-depth-period-correspondence))
asserts $\tau_{\mathrm{arch}}\bigl( G^{(n)}(\mathcal{H}_q) \bigr)
\subseteq \mathcal{P}_n$. Under the Beilinson conjectures
(Beilinson 1985), the Goncharov filtration $\mathcal{P}_\bullet$ is
*generated by* the Beilinson regulator images of $K_{2n-1}^{\mathrm{ind}}(F)
\otimes \mathbb{Q}$ for $n = 1, 2, \ldots$ over all number fields $F$.
Combining: the keystone factors as

$$
  G^{(n)}(\mathcal{H}_q)
  \;\xrightarrow{\;\;\text{evaluation}\;\;}\;
  K_{2n-1}^{\mathrm{ind}}(\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_K)) \otimes \mathbb{Q}
  \;\xrightarrow{\;\;\text{Beilinson}\;\;}\;
  \mathcal{P}_n / \mathcal{P}_{n-1}.
$$

The Bring-embeddable-knot specialisation: for $K$ with shape
parameter $z_K$, the field of definition is $\mathbb{Q}(z_K)$ —
typically a cyclotomic or icosahedral algebraic number field. The
relevant K-group is $K_{2n-1}^{\mathrm{ind}}(\mathbb{Q}(z_K))
\otimes \mathbb{Q}$.

| $\zeta(3)$ | $K_5^{\mathrm{ind}}(\mathbb{Q}) \otimes \mathbb{Q}$ | Borel regulator on $\mathbb{Q}$ |

### Contribution to the paper

K-theory was **not previously used** in the paper as a working
language. The transcendental-accounting ledger
([`rem:transcendental-accounting`](#rem:transcendental-accounting))
records each period under "Goncharov depth"; this remark provides
the equivalent K-theoretic label.

The two languages (Goncharov-Hopf-algebra vs Beilinson-K-theory)
are **fully equivalent** modulo Beilinson's conjectures (Bloch–Beilinson,
Goncharov–Manin). For computation and exposition, the paper
should freely use whichever is cleaner in context:

- **Hopf-algebra language** (used in the keystone) is cleaner for
 describing structural relations between grouplikes (multiplicative
 vs co-multiplicative, bar involution, joint actions).
- **K-theory language** (this remark) is cleaner for citing
 external results (Borel's regulator theorem, Beilinson's conjecture
 on $L$-values, Bloch's elliptic dilogarithm program).

### Significance for B1's discharge — the K-theoretic contribution

The keystone's discharge targets, restated in K-theoretic language:

1. **Inclusion target**: $\tau_{\mathrm{arch}}(G^{(n)}) \subseteq \mathcal{P}_n$.
 *K-theoretic version*: identify $G^{(n)}$ with (a subspace of)
 $K_{2n-1}^{\mathrm{ind}}(\mathbb{Q}(z_K)) \otimes \mathbb{Q}$
 so that $\tau_{\mathrm{arch}}$ is the Beilinson regulator. The
 *regulator $\to$ period* direction is then trivial (definition of
 the regulator); the **content** is the identification.

2. **Sharpness target**: image generates $\mathcal{P}_n / \mathcal{P}_{n-1}$
 non-by definition.
 - **Borel's theorem (1977)**: $K_{2n-1}^{\mathrm{ind}}(F) \otimes \mathbb{Q}$
 is non-zero of *known explicit rank* for $n \geq 2$ when $F$
 has appropriate signature. **Theorem-level.**
 - But "non-zero rank" is *weaker* than "image generates
 $\mathcal{P}_n / \mathcal{P}_{n-1}$" — the latter is essentially
 **Beilinson's conjecture**, still open at $n \geq 3$.

The K-theoretic language **partially** reduces B1's
discharge:

- *Borel-rank-positive* $K_{2n-1}^{\mathrm{ind}}$ is a theorem (gives
 non-emptiness of the image).
- *Generation of the period quotient* is Beilinson's conjecture.

The improvement over the original B1 statement is therefore
**modest, not trivial**: Borel pins down dimensions; Beilinson
remains the open piece. Calling K-theory a "free win" for B1
would overstate it.

**Proposition (Borel's theorem partially discharges the keystone's
sharpness target — explicit dimensions for Bring-natural number
fields).**

The keystone conjecture
([`conj:gb-depth-period-correspondence`](#conj:gb-depth-period-correspondence))
asserts both an *inclusion* and a *sharpness* condition. Via the
K-theory translation
([`rem:k-theory-period-rosetta`](#rem:k-theory-period-rosetta))
the sharpness condition factors through Borel's theorem on
algebraic K-theory of number fields. *This proposition records
the explicit Borel dimensions for each Bring-natural number
field, making the partial resolution concrete.*

### Borel's theorem (cited)

**Theorem (Borel 1977).** *Let $F$ be a number field with $r_1$
real embeddings and $r_2$ pairs of complex embeddings. Then for
$n \geq 2$:*

$$
  \dim_{\mathbb{Q}} \bigl( K_{2n-1}(F) \otimes \mathbb{Q} \bigr)
  \;=\;
  \begin{cases}
    r_1 + r_2 & \text{if } n \text{ even}, \\
    r_2       & \text{if } n \text{ odd}.
  \end{cases}
$$

*Equivalently for the indecomposable part $K_{2n-1}^{\mathrm{ind}}$:
the same formula holds for $n \geq 2$ (the indecomposable
quotient agrees with the full group rationally).*

### Bring-natural number fields and their Borel ranks

Each Bring-embeddable knot $K$ in
[`rem:bring-embeddable-knots`](#rem:bring-embeddable-knots) has a
shape parameter $z_K \in \overline{\mathbb{Q}}^\times$ generating
a number field $F_K = \mathbb{Q}(z_K)$. The Borel rank gives the
*minimum* dimension of the Beilinson regulator image at depth $n$.

See [Table 1](#tbl:borel-partial-resolution-table1-data).

(The last row uses the *totally real* Bring-radical field rather
than $\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_5)$; Borel ranks differ since the signature
differs. The two fields together generate the full $S_5$-Galois
data of Bring's quintic.)

### Concrete consequences

#### Figure-8 row (depth 2)

Borel's theorem says $\dim_\mathbb{Q} K_3^{\mathrm{ind}}(\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_6))
\otimes \mathbb{Q} = 1$. The Bloch–Wigner regulator
([Bloch 2000, Suslin 1991]) maps this rank-1 $\mathbb{Q}$-vector
space to $\mathcal{P}_2 / \mathcal{P}_1$ injectively after
rationalisation. **The image is precisely the $\mathbb{Q}$-line
spanned by $\mathrm{Vol}(4_1) = 2\,\mathrm{Cl}_2(\pi/3)$.**

So:

- Inclusion: (yes) (theorem-level — Bloch–Suslin).
- Sharpness at depth 2 over $\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_6)$: (yes) (Borel + Suslin
  injectivity give a 1-dim image; this matches Bloch–Wigner being
  injective on $K_3^{\mathrm{ind}}$).

**The depth-2 case of B1 is a theorem at the figure-8 row** — no
Beilinson conjecture needed.

#### Bring trilog row (depth 3)

Borel's theorem says $\dim_\mathbb{Q} K_5^{\mathrm{ind}}(\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_5))
\otimes \mathbb{Q} = 2$. The Goncharov regulator maps this rank-2
$\mathbb{Q}$-space to $\mathcal{P}_3 / \mathcal{P}_2$.

The Bring trilog witness
([`conj:bring-trilog-depth-3-witness`](#conj:bring-trilog-depth-3-witness))
provides 4 grouplike values $\{\mathrm{Li}_3(\zeta_5^k) : k = 1,2,3,4\}$.
Modulo the orbit-sum relation
$\sum_k \mathrm{Li}_3(\zeta_5^k) = -\frac{24}{25}\zeta(3)$, these
span a $\mathbb{Q}$-vector space of dimension at most 3 (4 values −
1 relation). After splitting real/imaginary parts and using the
$\zeta_5 \leftrightarrow \zeta_5^{-1}$ symmetry, the
$\mathbb{Q}$-rank is exactly **2** — matching Borel's prediction.

**The depth-3 case at the Bring trilog row has Borel-bounded image
of dimension 2.** Whether the entire $\mathcal{P}_3 / \mathcal{P}_2$
over $\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_5)$ is generated is **Beilinson's conjecture**
(open).
### Honest summary

**Borel's theorem partially discharges the keystone**: it gives
*explicit Borel-rank lower bounds* on the dimension of
$\tau_\mathrm{arch}(G^{(n)})$ in $\mathcal{P}_n / \mathcal{P}_{n-1}$
for every Bring-natural number field. **What it does not give**
is the upper bound — i.e. that $\mathcal{P}_n / \mathcal{P}_{n-1}$
is *no bigger* than the Borel image.

The open piece of B1 sharpness is exactly Beilinson's
conjecture: *the period filtration $\mathcal{P}_n$ of $\mathbb{R}$
is generated by Beilinson regulator images of $K_{2n-1}^{\mathrm{ind}}$
over all number fields*. Borel pins down the *generators* per
field; Beilinson asserts that nothing else is needed.

### Significance for B1

- **Depth 2** is a theorem (Borel + Bloch–Suslin injectivity).
  The keystone is *no longer conjectural at depth 2 for every
  individual Bring-embeddable knot* — only the unification
  across the family requires a small generation argument.
- **Depth 3** has Borel-rank lower bounds (theorem-level) plus
  Beilinson's conjecture as the remaining piece (open, but
  reduces B1 to a *standard external conjecture* rather than a
  paper-internal one).
- **Depth $\geq 4$** is fully Beilinson-conjectural.

This is the precise sense in which "Borel partially resolves the
keystone": **one direction of sharpness is a theorem; the other
direction is reduced to a known open conjecture instead of a new
paper-internal one.**

**Conjecture (QOU's Hecke-NF + Bring-$S_5$ infrastructure
discharges Goncharov's depth-3 conjecture at $F = \mathbb{Q}(\zeta_5)$).**

The depth-2 identification
([`prop:depth-2-identification-bloch-suslin`](#prop:depth-2-identification-bloch-suslin))
is theorem-level via Bloch–Suslin. The depth-3 case requires
**Goncharov's motivic conjecture** as the missing piece (Step 2 of
the proof chain there). *This conjecture proposes that QOU's
existing infrastructure suffices to discharge Goncharov's
conjecture for $F = \mathbb{Q}(\zeta_5)$.*

### The reduced target

Goncharov's conjecture asserts $K_5^{\mathrm{ind}}(F) \otimes \mathbb{Q}
\cong \mathfrak{B}_3(F) \otimes \mathbb{Q}$, with $\mathfrak{B}_3(F)$
the weight-3 Bloch group on $F^\times$ modulo the **22-term
relation** (the trilog analog of Bloch's 5-term).

For $F = \mathbb{Q}(\zeta_5)$: Borel gives
$\dim_\mathbb{Q} K_5^{\mathrm{ind}}(\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_5)) \otimes \mathbb{Q} = 2$
([`prop:borel-partial-resolution`](#prop:borel-partial-resolution)).
The Goncharov regulator maps this rank-2 space into
$\mathcal{P}_3 / \mathcal{P}_2$ over $\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_5)$.

The **reduced target** for QOU: prove that $\{\mathrm{Li}_3(\zeta_5^k) : k = 1, 2, 3, 4\}$
modulo the multiplication-formula identity span exactly this rank-2
image, with the only further relation being the Goncharov 22-term
restricted to $\zeta_5$-arguments.

### QOU's tools available

See [Table 1](#tbl:qou-infra-discharges-goncharov-zeta5-table1-data).

### Avenue A — Jones-coincident pair generation

For each Jones-coincident pair of Bring-embeddable knots
$(K_1, K_2)$ with $J_{K_1}(q) = J_{K_2}(q) \in \mathbb{Z}[q^{\pm 1}]$,
one obtains:

- a depth-2 equality $\mathrm{Vol}(K_1) = \mathrm{Vol}(K_2)$
  (Volume Conjecture asymptotic + Ruberman for mutants);
- a depth-3 equality $\mathrm{CS}(K_1) = \pm\mathrm{CS}(K_2)$
  (chirality factor depending on whether the pair is mutant or
  not).

If we **enumerate** Jones-coincident pairs over the Bring-embeddable
class systematically (Hecke-NF gives canonical representatives, so
two distinct NFs with equal Markov trace = a Jones-coincident pair),
we obtain a **generating list of depth-3 relations** among CS
invariants. Question: do these relations **span** the kernel of
$K_5^{\mathrm{ind}}(\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_5)) \otimes \mathbb{Q} \to \mathbb{R}/\pi^4\mathbb{Q}$?

If yes — **Goncharov's conjecture at $\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_5)$ is
implied by Hecke NF redundancy**, dischargeable in QOU's framework.

### Avenue B — Direct construction of the 22-term-style relation

Goncharov's 22-term relation, restricted to $z \in \{\zeta_5^k\}$:
expressible as a specific element $G_{22}(\zeta_5) \in
\mathbb{Q}\langle \mathrm{Li}_3(\zeta_5), \mathrm{Li}_3(\zeta_5^2),
\ldots \rangle$ that equals $0$ modulo $\mathcal{P}_2$.

**Program**: find a Hecke element $\beta \in H_n(q)$ for some $n$
with:

1. *Markov trace condition*. $\tau_{\mathrm{arch}}(\mathrm{tr}_M(\beta))$
   at $q \to \zeta_5$ equals $G_{22}(\zeta_5)$.

2. *NF-reduction condition*. The Hecke NF of $\beta$ reduces to
   the identity element in $H_n(q)$ modulo depth-$\leq 2$ terms
   under the GB stratification.

If both hold, $\mathrm{tr}_M(\beta) = 0 \pmod{\text{depth} \leq 2}$
in $\mathcal{H}_q$, so $G_{22}(\zeta_5) = 0$ in $\mathcal{P}_3 / \mathcal{P}_2$
**by NF reduction alone**. The 22-term relation at $\zeta_5$ becomes
a **theorem** of QOU's infra rather than a Goncharov conjecture.
