Let $(\mathbf{C}, A, \tau)$ be a
([quantum observable universe](#def:quantum-observable-universe)); let $q$
denote the substrate parameter.  Let
$\varrho \colon \mathbf{1} \to A$ be a morphism (a "closed cycle" in $A$,
[mass identity](#def:mass-identity)) whose categorical support is
generated by $N$ consecutive extensions along the brane tower
$\{A_n\}_{n \geq 0}$
([recursive brane tower](#def:recursive-brane-tower)), applied to a
base-level morphism $\varrho_0 \colon \mathbf{1} \to A_0$.

The mass $\mathfrak{m}(\varrho)$ ([mass identity](#def:mass-identity)) admits a
decomposition into descended and transient contributions via the
:refterm[descent]{#descent} involution $\hat{\tau}_n$
([:refterm[descent]{#descent} involution](#def:descent-involution)):

```tex
\begin{equation}\label{eq:topological-mass-decomp}
  \mathfrak{m}(\varrho)
  = 
  \bigl\lVert
  \mathrm{tr}_{\mathrm{desc}}(\varrho)
  \bigr\rVert
  + 
  \sum_{i=1}^{N}
  \bigl\lVert
  \mathrm{tr}_{\mathrm{trans}}(\varrho, p_i)
  \bigr\rVert,
\end{equation}
```

where:

1. $\mathrm{tr}_{\mathrm{desc}}(\varrho)$ is the categorical trace of
   $(\int_A \otimes \mathrm{id}) \circ \Delta \circ \varrho$ restricted
   to the descended subalgebra
   $\mathcal{A}^{\mathrm{desc}} = \mathrm{im}\hat{\tau}_n$
   ([:refterm[descent]{#descent} eigenspaces](#prop:descent-eigenspaces)).  Since
   $\mathcal{A}^{\mathrm{desc}} \cong \mathrm{End}_{\mathbf{C}}(A_n)$
   via the ascent embedding $\ell_n$, this equals the mass of the
   base-level cycle $\varrho_0$: it captures the baseline drag of
   the Frobenius--Hopf structure along the trivial core.
2. Each $\mathrm{tr}_{\mathrm{trans}}(\varrho, p_i)$ is the categorical
   trace restricted to the component of the transient complement
   $\mathcal{A}^{\mathrm{trans}} = \ker\hat{\tau}_n$ localised at the
   $i$-th blow-up centre $p_i$
   ([brane boundary](#def:brane-boundary)).  The localisation is
   well-defined because the kernels of $\mathfrak{d}_{n+1}$ at
   distinct blow-up centres are disjoint submodules of
   $\mathrm{End}_{\mathbf{C}}(A_{n+1})$.

The decomposition follows from the idempotent splitting
$\mathrm{End}_{\mathbf{C}}(A_{n+1}) = \mathcal{A}^{\mathrm{desc}} \oplus \mathcal{A}^{\mathrm{trans}}$
([:refterm[descent]{#descent} eigenspaces](#prop:descent-eigenspaces)) and the linearity of
the categorical trace.  When the splitting is orthogonal with respect to
the Frobenius norm, $\lVert \alpha \rVert^2 = \lVert \hat{\tau}_n(\alpha) \rVert^2 + \lVert \alpha - \hat{\tau}_n(\alpha) \rVert^2$, the mass decomposes additively
as stated.

For the archimedean realisation---where the descended trace becomes the
holonomy $\mathrm{Hol}_{\nabla_A}$ and each transient trace evaluates to
the quantum dilogarithm $\Phi_q(z_i)$---see
[archimedean proof](#prf:topological-mass).

**Archimedean specialisation (§7c).** The categorical mass $\mathrm{categoricalMass} : R \to \mathbb{R}$ is the post-$\tau$ archimedean realisation of the generic mass decomposition over a normed ground ring — an $R\to\mathbb{R}$ norm evaluation at $q_0$, intrinsically wall-crossing, acknowledged here.

<!-- FILE_START: topological-mass-conjecture.md -->
The mass $\mathfrak{m}(\varrho)$ of a vortex in any quantum observable universe
([mass identity](#def:mass-identity)) decomposes as a sum of a descended
holonomy contribution and finitely many transient contributions localised
at the exceptional divisors of the brane boundary
([brane boundary](#def:brane-boundary)):

$$
  \mathfrak{m}(\varrho)
  = 
  \bigl\lVert\mathrm{Hol}_{\nabla_A}(\varrho_0;\mathfrak{G})\bigr\rVert
  + 
  \sum_{i=1}^{N}
  \bigl\lVert\Phi_q(z_i)\bigr\rVert,
$$

where $\Phi_q(z_i)$ is the quantum dilogarithm
([quantum dilogarithm](#cor:quantum-dilogarithm)) evaluated at the
$i$-th blow-up centre $z_i$, and $\varrho_0$ is the unknot core of $\varrho$.

## Relationship to the proved theorem

The analogous decomposition for morphisms supported along the
*recursive brane tower*
([recursive brane tower](#def:recursive-brane-tower)) has been proved as
[Topological Decomposition of Mass](#thm:topological-mass). That theorem
uses the :refterm[descent]{#descent} involution $\hat\tau_n$ and Frobenius categorical traces
to split $\mathfrak{m}(\varrho)$ into descended and transient parts.

The present conjecture concerns the *vortex* case: $\varrho$ is a
**closed cycle** whose support does not necessarily factor through
a finite brane-tower extension. The holonomy $\mathrm{Hol}_{\nabla_A}$
along $\varrho_0$ (the flat gauge-field holonomy of the base-level connection)
replaces the :refterm[descent]{#descent}-trace part, while the quantum-dilogarithm sum
captures the transient blow-up contributions at the exceptional divisors of
the brane boundary $\partial A$.

**Archimedean specialisation (§7c).** This block sits on the archimedean side of the substrate wall: it invokes real- or complex-analytic constructs — real-valued physical observables, real-analytic bounds, transcendental constants, or numerical evaluation at the substrate parameter $q_0$. Any generic categorical / $q$-deformation identities it draws on remain valid over an abstract coefficient ring; only their archimedean realisation is specialised here.

**Lemma.**
*Let $\alpha = (\nabla_A q, L \wedge F^+)$ be the integrand of the
mass identity for a vortex $\varrho$, and let $\hat{\tau}_n$ be the
:refterm[descent]{#descent} involution.  The Killing-form orthogonality of
$\mathcal{A}_{n+1}^{\mathrm{desc}} \oplus \mathcal{A}_{n+1}^{\mathrm{trans}}$
together with multiplicative-integral additivity over orthogonal
direct sums gives*

$$
  \mathfrak{m}(\varrho)
  =
  \bigl\lVert
  \mathrm{MI}\!\!\int_\Sigma \hat{\tau}_n(\alpha)
  \bigr\rVert
  +
  \bigl\lVert
  \mathrm{MI}\!\!\int_\Sigma
  \bigl(\alpha - \hat{\tau}_n(\alpha)\bigr)
  \bigr\rVert.
$$

<!-- FILE_START: topological-mass-holonomy.md -->
**Lemma.** Consider the topological blow-up decomposition $\Sigma = \Sigma_0 \cup \bigcup_{i=1}^N D_i$, where $\Sigma_0$ is a disc spanning the unknot core $\varrho_0$ and each $D_i$ is the exceptional disc at the $i$-th blow-up. Under this decomposition, the descended term equals the unknot-core holonomy:

$$
  \mathrm{MI}\!\!\int_\Sigma \hat{\tau}_n(\alpha)
  =
  \mathrm{Hol}_{\nabla_A}\!\bigl(\varrho_0; \mathfrak{G}\bigr).
$$

<!-- FILE_START: topological-mass-transient-localisation.md -->
**Lemma.**
*The transient component $\alpha - \hat{\tau}_n(\alpha)$ is supported exclusively on disjoint discs $D_i$ centred at the blow-up points $p_1, \ldots, p_N$. The norm of its multiplicative integral reduces to a sum over the exceptional divisors $E_i$:*

$$
  \bigl\lVert
  \mathrm{MI}\!\!\int_\Sigma
  \bigl(\alpha - \hat{\tau}_n(\alpha)\bigr)
  \bigr\rVert
  =
  \sum_{i=1}^{N}
  \bigl\lVert
  \mathrm{MI}\!\!\int_{E_i}
  \bigl(\nabla_A q, L \wedge F^+\bigr)
  \bigr\rVert.
$$

<!-- FILE_START: topological-mass-dilogarithm.md -->
**Lemma.** Each exceptional-divisor summand evaluates to the quantum dilogarithm in the spectral coordinates of the Briggs--Klein chart at $E_i$:

$$
  \bigl\lVert
  \mathrm{MI}\!\!\int_{E_i}
  \bigl(\nabla_A q, L \wedge F^+\bigr)
  \bigr\rVert
  =
  \Phi_q(z_i)
  =
  \int_0^{z_i} \frac{\ln_q(1+t)}{t}\, dt.
$$
