<!-- FILE_START: trefoil-knot-intro.md -->
The trefoil $3_1$ is the simplest non-trivial knot. It serves as the canonical entry point to the categorical knot category. Its fundamental group and operator algebra exhibit the essential features—such as non-abelian first homology and two-dimensional irreducible representations—that heavier knots inherit.
<!-- FILE_END: trefoil-knot-intro.md -->

<!-- FILE_START: trefoil-knot-group.md -->
The trefoil knot $K_{3,2}$ is the simplest non-trivial torus knot. Its complement has fundamental group:
$$
  \pi_1(S^3\setminus K_{3,2})
  = \langle x,y\mid xyx=yxy\rangle
   \cong B_3.
$$
This trefoil-knot group is isomorphic to the braid group on three strands. The relation $xyx=yxy$ is the braid relation. It dictates that closing the three-strand braid by identifying its top and bottom yields the trefoil.
<!-- FILE_END: trefoil-knot-group.md -->

<!-- FILE_START: trefoil-operator-algebra.md -->
In the $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ representation, the trefoil braid relation $xyx=yxy$ becomes the operator identity:
$$
  \alpha\beta\alpha
  = \beta\alpha\beta.
$$
Using the explicit generators:
$$
  \alpha=\begin{pmatrix}e^{i\pi/3}&0\\0&e^{-i\pi/3}\end{pmatrix},
  \qquad
  \beta=\begin{pmatrix}0&i\\i&0\end{pmatrix},
$$
both products evaluate to:
$$
  \alpha\beta\alpha
  = \begin{pmatrix}0&ie^{2i\pi/3}\\ie^{-2i\pi/3}&0\end{pmatrix}
  = \beta\alpha\beta.
$$
The eigenvalues of the composite $\alpha\beta$ govern the phases acquired when quarks exchange colour charges along the trefoil path.
