<!-- FILE_START: non-archimedean-models-intro.md -->
Archimedean models realise the substrate parameter $q$ as a smoothly varying real or complex number, so they see the substrate only through its differential-geometric shadow. What that picture does not record is the arithmetic of the $q$-commutation data itself — its integral form and its behaviour in positive characteristic. Recovering that structure calls for completions carrying a non-archimedean valuation, in which $q$ is $\pp$-adic rather than continuous.

The concrete phenomenon these models expose is a lifting problem: one asks whether the substrate can be reconstructed from the algebra generated by its infinitesimal $q$-commutation relations, with the Heisenberg uncertainty relation — read as the $q$-commutator $[\hat{X},\hat{P}]_q = i\hbar_q$ — as the germ to be lifted. The blocks below trace this construction from the `enveloping-algebras` onward, through the Lusztig integral form and its $q$-deformed PBW basis to the $\pp$-adic and finite-field realisations.

<!-- FILE_START: enveloping-algebras.md -->
The Heisenberg uncertainty relation, $[\hat{X},\hat{P}]_q = i\hbar_q$, provides a single physical $q$-commutator between position and momentum. The enveloping-algebra construction generalizes this germ to reconstruct the full substrate from its infinitesimal $q$-commutation data. 

We solve this lifting problem using the Lusztig integral form of $\mathcal{U}_q(\mathfrak{su}(2))$, its triangular decomposition, and the $q$-deformed Poincaré–Birkhoff–Witt (PBW) basis. The integral form and triangular decomposition factor the generators into component subalgebras. The $q$-deformed PBW basis provides an explicit ordered basis for the enveloping algebra. Together, these algebraic structures lift the infinitesimal $q$-commutation relations back to the substrate (cf. [arXiv:math/0409565](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0409565)).

<!-- FILE_START: p-adic-realisation.md -->
To show that the substrate parameter and the surrounding categorical
apparatus are not tied to an archimedean base field, we realise the
archimedean tuple over a $\pp$-adic base.
Let $k = \mathbb{Q}_\pp$ and let $\mathbf{C}$ be the category of
$F$-isocrystals over $\mathbb{Q}_\pp$ (modules over the Dieudonne ring
$\mathbb{Q}_\pp\langle F, V \rangle / (FV - \pp)$ that are finite-dimensional
over $\mathbb{Q}_\pp$).
This is a $\mathbb{Q}_\pp$-linear rigid :refterm[monoidal category]{#monoidal-category}.
The quantum group $\mathcal{O}_q(\mathrm{SU}(2))$ base-changed
to $\mathbb{Q}_\pp$ provides a Frobenius--Hopf object $A$, where the
Frobenius form $\int_A$ arises from the $\pp$-adic Haar measure.
The Frobenius endomorphism $\varphi$ on $\mathbf{C}$ plays the
role of the Levi-form contraction: the categorical substrate parameter
([substrate parameter](#def:substrate-parameter)) specialises to
$q = \pp^{-v_\pp(\det\varphi)} \in \mathrm{End}(\mathbf{1}) \cong \mathbb{Q}_\pp$.  The fibre functor $\tau$
is the forgetful functor to $\mathrm{Vect}_{\mathbb{Q}_\pp}$.
The observability condition
$\mathrm{Hom}(\mathbf{1}, \mathrm{Ad}(A)) = 0$ amounts to the
irreducibility of the associated $F$-crystal, generalising
irreducible connection to the $\pp$-adic setting.

*Key structures.*

* The classical Frobenius integrability theorem constrains
  $q$-torsion leaves to be Legendrian
  ([$q$-torsion existence](#rem:q-torsion-existence)).  Over $\mathbb{Q}_\pp$,
  this is replaced by compatibility of the connection with the
  Frobenius lift: a $\pp$-adic instanton connection is
  "integrable" if and only if the associated $F$-crystal is
  unit-root.
* Crystal cohomology (de Rham--Witt complex) plays the role of
  de Rham cohomology.
* The base-field-independent
  [quantum universe](#def:quantum-universe)
  and [quantum observable universe](#def:quantum-observable-universe) provide the categorical
  framework via Tannakian reconstruction.
