The Schur–Weyl tower from the finite-rank Hecke algebra to its inverse limit houses the substrate-flow trace series. This section introduces the $\varphi_q$ resummation, which extracts an explicit closed form for the trace at the substrate parameter. By summing contributions from each tower level, the resummation bridges the categorical Hecke trace and the classical Markov trace.

The sequence establishes the $\varphi_q$ resummation as a generating function over Schur–Weyl levels, proves its convergence, and extracts the closed form for its leading terms. It then identifies the resummed series with the Plancherel-Markov trace. Finally, it tabulates the $\varphi_q$ values for small partitions, linking them to the Hecke-$\chi$-closed forms. These values serve as the combinatorial inputs for the downstream binding-energy formulas.
