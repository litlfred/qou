The Schur–Weyl tower from finite-rank Hecke to the inverse-limit
Hecke is the categorical home of the substrate-flow trace
series.  The $\varphi_q$ resummation extracts an explicit
closed form for the trace at the substrate parameter by summing
a contribution from each level of the tower; the result is the
canonical bridge between the categorical Hecke trace and the
classical Markov trace identities used downstream.

The arc proceeds in three movements.  The first blocks set up
the $\varphi_q$ resummation as a generating function over the
Schur–Weyl levels, prove the convergence under the substrate-
flow, and extract the closed form for the leading terms; the
middle blocks identify the resummed series with the
Plancherel-Markov trace and prove the identification both
substrate-level and archimedean; the closing blocks tabulate
the $\varphi_q$ values for small partitions and connect them
to the Hecke-$\chi$-closed forms from the earlier section.

The section's output is the explicit $\varphi_q$ values used in
the binding-energy substrate formula, together with the
Schur–Weyl-tower closed form they realise.  This is the
chapter's most technical section; readers can take the closed
forms from the closing tabulation if needed for downstream
applications.

The closing blocks tabulate the per-shell Hecke quandle data and exhibit the $\varphi_q$ trace series resummation with table data; this is the section's combinatorial engine for substrate-level binding-energy formulas.
