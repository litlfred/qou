§5  TOPOLOGICAL MASS THEOREM TRANSPARENCY
------------------------------------------------------------------------

The QOU mass derivation rests on the following proved theorem:

  theorem topological_mass_formula (tmf : TopologicalMassFormula) :
    tmf.mass = tmf.vol / tmf.h_q ^ 2

This theorem (Theorem 4.34 in the manuscript) encodes the
Topological Decomposition of Mass.  It was formerly stated as a
conjecture (axiom); it is now proved via the eigenspace decomposition
of the descent involution, additivity of the MI integral, and
localisation of transient modes to exceptional divisors.

The mass formula is instantiated for specific particles via
TopologicalMassFormula structures, with no axioms required.
