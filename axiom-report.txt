§5  TOPOLOGICAL MASS CONJECTURE TRANSPARENCY
------------------------------------------------------------------------

The QOU mass derivation depends on the following unproved axiom:

  axiom topological_mass_conjecture :
    ∀ (m vol h_q : ℝ), h_q ≠ 0 → m = vol / h_q ^ 2

This axiom (Conjecture 4.25 in the manuscript) encodes the
Topological Decomposition of Mass hypothesis.  All theorems in
QOU.MassDerivation that derive physical constants (substrate_q,
quantum_planck, etc.) depend transitively on this axiom.

Declarations depending on topological_mass_conjecture:
  - QOU.MassDerivation.derive_substrate_q
  - QOU.MassDerivation.mass_ratio_from_volume
