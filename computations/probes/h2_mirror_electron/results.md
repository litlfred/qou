## Outcome (i)

Every cell passes exactly {bare, V³@4, V³@5}, the same set as with Vᵏ electrons (h2_gg_electron). The mirror label
found in `gg_symmetry` (level-2 characters) does not change which bonds pass, and neither does going to the ninth power.

## What this means (scope a)

Under the owner's per-particle rule, H₂ is blind to which γγ word the electron is: any V^{3j} or V̄^{3j} gives the
same chemistry, bonded by exactly V³. H₂ cannot be used to select the electron. Another observable is needed.
