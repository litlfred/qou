## Claim

No construction in the searched class meets (a)–(f). One construction, K0, meets (a)–(e) and
fails only (f): there the colour index does not move with the strand.

The sharp result: within the class where colour acts strand-wise and commutes with the crossing,
**a Hecke braiding that carries colour and flavour together (q → 1 limit ±P on C⁶⊗C⁶) satisfies
the braid relation exactly when p² = 1 and q² = 1** (lex Gröbner basis of the 164 residual
numerators = {(p²−1)², (q²−1)²}). So, in this class, colour moves with the strand only at the
classical point. At generic q, every braided member keeps one factor (colour or flavour) at fixed
position.

K2 has no colour action in the searched class, so (d) and (e) are not tested there. Its Markov

## Evidence

- K0 (d): for every flavour basis vector e_g, σᵢ(ω ⊗ e_g) = ω ⊗ Ř_f,ᵢ e_g with ω = Σ sgn(π) e_π,
  checked symbolically. The Markov partial trace gives z = q³/(q²+1).
- K4 braid witnesses, p = 1, X = Ř_f, Y = −Ř_f⁻¹: entry (0,0,2) of σ₁σ₂σ₁ − σ₂σ₁σ₂ =
  (q−1)²(q+1)²(q²+1)/(8q³). At p = q it is 2q³(q−1)²(q+1)²/(q²+1)³.
- Consistent with `ColourBridge.q_eq_one_of_e01_mem` (a primitive gl₃ field commuting with the gl₃
  Jimbo Ř forces q = 1). The present result is the colour ⊗ flavour analogue, with a q-deformed
  colour parameter p allowed.

## Implications

1. The N = 2 vs N = 3 tension is not resolved by C⁶. A braiding that is Hecke and carries colour
   with the strand either has no colour symmetry (gl₆ Jimbo) or is classical (p² = q² = 1).
2. The only object meeting the stated acceptance test (a)–(e) is K0. Its colour is an external
   label on positions, which the braid does not permute. It is the "colour as separate tensor
   factor" construction of `computations/probes/qbeta_colour_factor/`. Whether that counts as
   framework-native colour is a modelling decision for the owner, not a computational result.
3. The scope of the negative is the K4 class: flavour operators in the algebra generated by Ř_f,
   with strand-wise colour symmetry (classical, or U_p(gl₃) Jimbo). This is a structural exclusion
   inside that class (Gröbner basis), and scope (a) outside it.

## Not established / not formalised

- Flavour operators X, Y outside {scalars, Ř_f, −Ř_f⁻¹}, for example non-U_q(gl₂)-invariant ones.
- Colour acting through a non-cocommutative structure other than the U_p(gl₃) Jimbo one: a twist,
  a coideal subalgebra of U_q(gl₆), or super/gl(m|n) gradings.
- Abstract colour actions inside End_{H₃}((C⁶)^⊗3) ≅ M₅₆ ⊕ M₇₀ ⊕ M₂₀ (they exist trivially, but no
  strand-wise structure fixes one).
- No Lean formalisation. The Gröbner result is on 216-dimensional operators and has not been
  reduced to a small identity.
- No gluon is identified or selected.

## What would close `qou-ni4b`

Either (i) the owner rules that K0 (colour as a position label, not transported) is the
framework's colour, after which a Lean proof of K0 (a)–(e) is mechanical; or (ii) a construction
outside the K4 class that meets (a)–(f) at generic q, for example a coideal / twisted colour symmetry
of the gl₆ Jimbo module whose singlet sector carries Ř_f. Neither exists in the corpus.
