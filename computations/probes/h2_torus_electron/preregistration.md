# Pre-registration: a single-crossing (torus-knot) electron, fallback (a)

Bean `qou-a1hh`, PR #7741. Owner 2026-11-15: "b, then a as fall back". Written and pushed **before** any
computation below.

## Why (a) runs

Fallback (b), the weak-vertex-dressed γγ electron, cannot supply chirality. W = σP_R + σ⁻¹P_L simplifies to
(σ + σ⁻¹)/(q[2]), which is invariant under σ ↔ σ⁻¹, and the mirror vertex W′ = q²·W. Both identities were
checked symbolically (sympy difference 0). So (b) changes the electron but not its handedness. Option (a)
is the remaining candidate the owner named: an electron generated by a single crossing σ, closing to the
torus knots T(2, m).

## Family

Electron e_{m,t} = σ₀^m σ₁^t on 3 strands, which is T(2, m) stabilised once (Markov). Here m is odd with
−9 ≤ m ≤ 9, and the stabilising sign is t ∈ {+1, −1}. That gives 20 electrons. m = ±1 closes to the
unknot. m = 3 with t = +1 is the trefoil already in `knot_convoluted_tensor_h2_probe.ELECTRONS`.
Nucleons: V³ and V⁶ (V = σ₀σ₁⁻¹), the two γγ nucleons that pass every level (`h2_gg_atom`). Reading 3,
levels from m = 1, convention D, proton pin, and mpmath at the dps the reused base code sets (40).

## Tests

- **T1 (electron alone):** reading 3 at levels 1–3 on its own three strands.
- **P1 (closed form):** the hydrogen atom p ⊗ e passes level 6 iff χ_(3)(e) + χ_(2,1)(p)·χ_(2,1)(e) ≥ 0.
  The sign is checked against the computed (3,3) value for every (p, e); a mismatch is a FAIL.
- **T3 (full H₂):** for every (p, e) whose atom passes levels 1–6, bare H₂ (atom 2 "pe" and "ep") at
  levels 1–12, plus bonds V^k at s ∈ {4, 5}, k ≤ 3, as in `h2_gg_atom`.
- **Chirality (reported):** for each m, whether e_{m,t} and its mirror e_{−m,−t} give different characters.

## Hand prediction, recorded before the run

With p = V³ and t = +1: χ_(3)(e) = q^{m+1} and χ_(2,1)(e) = −(q^{m−2} + q^{2−m})/[2] for odd m. At q₀ the
level-6 bracket q^{m+1} − 1.9942·(q^{m−2} + q^{2−m})/[2] is negative for m = 3 and m = 5 and positive for
m = 7 and m = 9, from a hand estimate. P1 will check this. If it holds, the level-6 obstruction for the
trefoil is a threshold in m, not a ban on torus electrons.

## Outcomes, fixed now

- **(i)** Some H₂ cell passes every level. Report **all** passing (m, t, nucleon, orientation, bond). Do
  not choose among them. In particular, a threshold in m is not a selection of T(2, 7): the passing set
  is the result, and picking one member of it would be selection against the target.
- **(ii)** Some atom passes but no H₂ cell does.
- **(iii)** No atom passes level 6.

Trace ratios are reported, never used to select. Nothing fitted, selected against data, or wired.
