# PRE-REGISTRATION: the (gl₆, gl₃⊕gl₃) coideal as colour on colour⊗flavour (ℂ³⊗ℂ²)^⊗n (bean `qou-ni4b`)

Written and pushed before any probe code exists. Date 2026-11-22. PR #9059.

## Question

`qcd_coideal_colour/RESULT.md` left one untested lead. The Letzter coideals of U_q(gl₃) are not
colour (their fixed algebras have dimension 3 and 4). But the symmetric pair (gl₆, gl₃⊕gl₃) acting
on ℂ⁶ = ℂ³⊗ℂ² has q → 1 fixed points that contain the diagonal sl₃, which acts as sl₃⊗1 there.
`qcd_colour_action/` K4 showed that a strand-wise Hopf colour (classical sl₃, or U_p(gl₃) by its
coproduct) carried with the strand together with flavour forces q² = 1. A coideal of U_q(gl₆) is
not a strand-wise Hopf colour. This probe asks whether it gives colour that moves with the strand
at generic q, and which of the acceptance tests (a)–(f) of `qcd_colour_action/` it meets.

## Setting (fixed in advance)

- V = ℂ⁶ with the Jimbo U_q(gl₆) action. Coproduct variant B (Kolb's), as in the sibling probes:
  Δ(E_i) = E_i⊗1 + K_i⊗E_i, Δ(F_i) = F_i⊗K_i⁻¹ + 1⊗F_i, Δ(K) = K⊗K.
- Ř = `jimbo_R(6, upper=True)`, the same function as `qcd_coideal_colour` (calibration: it commutes
  with Δ of every generator and satisfies Hecke (σ−q)(σ+q⁻¹) = 0).
- Exact arithmetic over ℚ(q, c₁, c₂, c₃) (sparse matrices with entries in the sympy fraction
  field). No float, q₀ not used.
- **Satake data.** Type AIII, quasi-split, n = 2p = 6: X = ∅, τ(i) = 6 − i (the diagram flip,
  τ(3) = 3). The classical involution is θ = Ad J with J the antidiagonal permutation, since
  J f_i J = e_{τ(i)}. Its fixed algebra is gl(V₊)⊕gl(V₋), V± = span(v_j ± v_{7−j}), so k ≅ gl₃⊕gl₃.
- **Generators.** B_i = F_i − c_i E_{τ(i)} K_i⁻¹, i = 1..5, s₃ = 0. Torus part: the θ-fixed
  T_j = K_{ε_j}K_{ε_{7−j}}, j = 1, 2, 3 (K_{ε_j} = q^{E_jj}).
- **Parameters.** c₁, c₂, c₃ free symbols. c₄ = c₂ and c₅ = c₁ is Kolb's admissibility condition
  (c_i = c_{τ(i)} when (α_i, Θα_i) = 0). It is not a choice against a target: at q = 1 it is the
  condition that f_i − c_i e_{τ(i)} generate a torus conjugate of k. The probe checks this: with
  c₄, c₅ free, the q = 1 Lie closure is predicted to be larger than 18. s₃ ≠ 0 is not tested
  (scope (a)).
- **Colour labelling (forced by τ).** Label v₁ v₂ v₃ v₄ v₅ v₆ = (1u)(2u)(3u)(3d)(2d)(1d). Then
  J = 1_c⊗σ_x, and the classical limits are f_i + e_{τ(i)} = E^c⊗1 for i = 1, 2, 4, 5 (colour
  raising/lowering on both flavours) while f₃ + e₃ = E^c₃₃⊗σ_x mixes flavour. The labelling is the
  τ-orbit structure; no other choice is made.
- **Colour candidate B^col.** The subalgebra of B generated by B₁, B₂, B₄, B₅ and T₁, T₂, T₃,
  i.e. B with the τ-fixed node 3 removed. It is the quantum symmetric pair coideal of the Levi
  U_q(gl₃×gl₃) for the diagonal pair (gl₃⊕gl₃, gl₃). It does not involve c₃. It is identified by its
  classical limit, not selected by any comparison with data.

## Tests

- **Q0 calibration.** Ř commutes with Δ(E_i), Δ(F_i), Δ(K) on V⊗V; Hecke and braid on V^⊗3.
- **Q1 coideal.** (Q1a) Δ(B_i) = B_i⊗K_i⁻¹ + 1⊗F_i − c_i K_{τ(i)}K_i⁻¹⊗E_{τ(i)}K_i⁻¹ on V⊗V with the
  first legs in the torus of B (and of B^col for i ≠ 3). (Q1b) Δⁿ(generator) commutes with every σ_i,
  n = 2, 3. (Q1c) Ř at q = 1 is P on ℂ⁶⊗ℂ⁶.
- **Q2 fixed algebra.** q → 1 Lie closure of B: predicted dimension 18 (gl₃⊕gl₃). With c₄, c₅ free:
  predicted larger. The closure of B^col's classical limit: predicted dimension 9 (gl₃), and the
  diagonal sl₃ realised as sl₃⊗1 after a flavour-block-diagonal colour rescaling on the d-block,
  diag(1, −c₁, c₁c₂).
- **Q3 relations of B^col at generic q**, on V^⊗n, n = 1, 2, 3, with e_i := B_{τ(i)}, f_i := B_i,
  k_1 = T₁T₂⁻¹, k_2 = T₂T₃⁻¹, i = 1, 2: k e k⁻¹ and k f k⁻¹ weights; [e_i, f_i] proportional to
  (k_i − k_i⁻¹)/(q − q⁻¹) with a constant fixed at n = 1 and checked at n = 2, 3; q-Serre among
  {f₁, f₂} and among {e₁, e₂}; [e₁, f₂] and [e₂, f₁]. **Prediction:** all hold with the
  proportionality constant −c_i, so B^col is a quotient of U_q(sl₃) on these modules. This is the
  least certain prediction: the mixed relations may hold only as q-commutators, and that would be
  reported as found.
- **Q4 K-matrix.** Commutant of B (full) on V: predicted dim 2, span{1, J_q}. Reflection equation
  Ř(J⊗1)Ř(J⊗1) = (J⊗1)Ř(J⊗1)Ř on the commutant pencil; solutions reported as found. Commutant of
  B^col on V: predicted dim 4 (the flavour matrices, conjugated).
- **Q5 singlets.** B^col-invariant vectors in V^⊗3 (B_i v = 0, k_i v = v). Predicted dim 8 (one
  colour singlet per flavour basis state). In V⊗V: predicted 0. The H₃ action on the singlet sector:
  multiplicities of S^(3), S^(2,1), S^(1³) from the characters. **Prediction:** S^(1³)×4 ⊕ S^(2,1)×2,
  i.e. the flavour model with σ ↦ −Ř_f⁻¹ (the image of Ř_f under σ ↦ −σ⁻¹), not σ ↦ Ř_f.
- **Q6 twists and z.** (i) Product twists T₁⊗g⊗g, g the gl₆ pivotal diag(q⁻¹⁰, …, q⁰), T₁ a
  B-invariant twist on V: predicted z₆ = q⁶/[6]_q identically (the Schur argument of the prior
  probe, with V irreducible for U_q(gl₆)). (ii) The colour-blind flavour twist G = 1_c⊗diag(a, b)
  (a, b free) commutes with B^col and with Ř, so x ↦ tr(G^⊗3|_S x|_S) on the singlet sector S is a
  trace on H₃. Its weights on the three blocks are reported as functions of (a, b). (iii) The
  flavour Markov parameter of the singlet sector's flavour model with K_f = diag(q, q⁻¹), computed as
  in `qcd_colour_action` K0: predicted z_D(−q⁻¹) = −q⁻¹/(q²+1), not z_D. Any (a, b) that would make a
  result equal z_D is reported only as a selection flag.

## Acceptance tests (a)–(f), verbatim from `qcd_colour_action/PREREGISTRATION-colour-action.md`

(a) injective colour action (su(3): 8 independent operators with the sl₃ brackets; U_q(sl₃):
Chevalley–Serre relations hold on the module and generators nonzero); (b) commutes with the H₃
action on W^⊗3; (c) Hecke and braid on W^⊗3; (d) on the colour-singlet sector the H₃ action equals
the flavour model σ_i ↦ Ř_f,i on (ℂ²)^⊗3 under an explicit flavour identification, and the
K_f = diag(q, q⁻¹) Markov trace there gives z_D = q³/(q²+1); (e) the colour-invariant subspace of the
colour part is one-dimensional; (f) transport: q → 1 limit of Ř is ±P on W⊗W.

Reading of (e) here: colour is not a tensor factor of the coideal action at q ≠ 1, so (e) is tested
as "B^col-invariants in V^⊗3 have dimension 8 = 1 × 2³" (one colour singlet per flavour state), the
same count as K0.

**Expected:** (a) ✓ if Q3 holds; (b) ✓ (B ⊂ U_q(gl₆)); (c) ✓; (d) ✗; (e) ✓ (dim 8); (f) ✓.

**Why (d) is expected to fail, stated before running.** At q = 1, (f) makes σ = P_c⊗P_f. The unique
sl₃-invariant of (ℂ³)^⊗3 is ε, on which P_c acts by −1. So on the singlet sector σ|_{q=1} = −P_f,
while (d) asks for Ř_f|_{q=1} = P_f. These are non-isomorphic S₃-representations (tr σ₁ = −4 versus
+4 on (ℂ²)^⊗3). H₃(q) is semisimple at generic q, so the multiplicities are constant on an open set
and cannot change between generic q and q → 1. If this holds, (d) and (f) are incompatible for
**every** construction whose q → 1 colour is sl₃⊗1 carried with the strand, not only this one. That
would be a scope-(b) exclusion with the S₃ sign character as the invariant. The probe checks the
character at generic q directly.

## The K4 question (what will be reported)

Whether the coideal colour moves with the strand without forcing q² = 1, and why. Predicted answer:
yes; it evades K4 because its coproduct is not strand-wise colour-only. On V⊗V,
Δ(B₁) = B₁⊗K₁⁻¹ + 1⊗F₁ − c₁K₅K₁⁻¹⊗E₅K₁⁻¹, and F₁ acts on the u-flavour while E₅ acts on the
d-flavour of the second strand, with different first legs. So the colour action depends on the
flavour content of other strands at q ≠ 1. K4's hypothesis (colour acts by Δ_H on the colour factors
⊗ 1_flavour) fails. The probe tests this: Δ²(B₁) does not commute with 1⊗(1_c⊗σ_x) on the second
strand, and it is not of the form X_c⊗1_f.

## Decision rule

A construction is a closure candidate for `qou-ni4b` only if it meets (a)–(f) exactly over
ℚ(q, c). Nothing is chosen by numerical comparison with a measured value. c stays free.

## Honesty clause

Exact arithmetic. Negatives are scope (a) unless an invariant is named. The bean stays open
whatever the outcome. No gluon is identified or selected.
