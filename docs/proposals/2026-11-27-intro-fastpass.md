## Proposal B — worked miniature box (`intro-structure-to-numbers.md`, after step 5)

Write $h = q - q^{-1}$, so the quadratic relation is $\sigma_i^2 = h\sigma_i + 1$
and $\sigma_i^{-1} = \sigma_i - h$; the Markov weight is $z = q^3/(q^2+1)$.

> **A worked miniature.** Take the three-strand word $V^3 = (\sigma_0\sigma_1^{-1})^3$,
> the core of the Borromean rings.
>
> 1. *Braid to algebra.* Replacing each $\sigma_1^{-1}$ by $\sigma_1 - h$ and
>    reducing with $\sigma_i^2 = h\sigma_i + 1$ leaves a combination of the six
>    basis elements $1, \sigma_0, \sigma_1, \sigma_0\sigma_1, \sigma_1\sigma_0,
>    \sigma_0\sigma_1\sigma_0$ with coefficients in $\mathbb{Z}[q, q^{-1}]$.
> 2. *Trace.* The Markov trace sends these six to
>    $1, z, z, z^2, z^2, z(hz+1)$, and the sum collapses to
>    $$\mathrm{tr}_M(V^3) = \frac{4 + h^2 - 3h^4 - h^6}{h^2 + 4}.$$
> 3. *Blocks.* The same number is $\sum_\lambda y_\lambda\chi_\lambda$ over
>    $\lambda = (3), (2,1), (1^3)$, with
>    $y_{(3)} = \frac{q^4+1}{(q^2+1)^2}$, $y_{(2,1)} = \frac{q^2}{(q^2+1)^2}$,
>    $y_{(1^3)} = 0$: the sign block carries no weight, which is the trace
>    radical of §0.1 seen in one line.
> 4. *Number.* At $q_0$ the value is $0.998562\ldots$. This rational function
>    is the one through which the proton ratio pins $q_0$.
>
> The figure-eight word $V^2$ gives $(h^4 + 3h^2 + 1)/(h^2+4) = 0.280196\ldots$
> by the same four steps.

## Critical view of the restructuring payoff (qou-private discussion, §"Manuscript Restructuring Payoff")

**What it proposes** (`uploads/discussions/2026-11-25-owner-gist-binding-photon-dec.md`
on qou-private branch `claude/clever-mccarthy-hngte7`, lines 699–712): make one
"Spine Theorem" the organising centre of the book, a chain

chapters visibly belong to one argument.

**Critical view (judgement, not a finding).**

1. *The motivation is right.* A reader of the current ToC cannot see why
   Navier–Stokes and nuclear binding share a book; one stated spine would fix
   that, and the Introduction's five-step chain is already half of it.
2. *The spine is not yet a theorem.* Only the first arrow (trace onto
   $TL_n$ via the trace radical) is corpus-proved (`ex:rieffel-quotient-temperley-lieb`).
   The second arrow and the "holographic loop" are conjectural in the gist
   itself. Reorganising 1,000 pages around an unproved statement puts the
   weakest link at the centre. Recommendation: state it in the Introduction
   as a *conjectured* spine with each arrow's status marked, and restructure
   only after the arrows are formalised.
3. *The pre-registration guardrail in the same gist is good and should be kept
   verbatim*: if the Bring period-norm minimum lands near the deuteron's
   2.22 MeV it counts only if no rescaling is chosen; any "clean factor"
   adjustment is a fit under the 2026-09-05 directive.
4. *Cost.* A spine restructure touches every chapter's opening and the
   chapter overview; it is not an Introduction fast-pass item.
