## Soft risks (recommend recast)

### `local-substrate-q-proof.md`

Uses `Vol(4_1) / ħ_q²` as the equation that determines $q_0$ from
the muon-electron mass ratio. The SnapPy witness verifies the
numerical value.

**Recommended recast**: write the derivation algebraically — the
muon and electron sit in the algebraic mass tower as
$\mathbb{Q}(q)$-elements; setting their ratio equal to CODATA
$m_\mu/m_e$ gives a $\mathbb{Q}(q)$-equation in $q$ alone. Solving
that equation algebraically determines $q_0$. The Vol(4_1)
identification is a τ-image cross-check, not a derivation input.

### `skein-mass-relation-proof.md`

Uses `Vol(3_1) = 0` and `Vol(4_1) ≈ 2.0299` to apply the
topological mass theorem and derive $m_\mu / m_e$.

**Recommended recast**: the Skein-functional / Markov-trace path
on the algebraic side already gives $m_\mu / m_e$ as a
$\mathbb{Q}(q_0)$-element. The Vol comparison should be moved to a
"τ-image consistency" remark rather than appearing as the proof's
key step.

Both recasts are **medium-effort** edits — the algebraic
derivation is already present in the paper (the Skein-functional
form); the proof blocks just need to lead with that form rather
than the Vol form.
