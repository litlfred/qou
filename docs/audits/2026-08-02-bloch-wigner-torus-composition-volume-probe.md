## Finding 1 (scope-(a) NEGATIVE) — the single-link volume is intensive

`V_hyp` is **flat** (≈ 7–8) across A = 2,3 while binding grows ≈ 3.8× (²H→³H).
So `B ∝ V_hyp(atom-link)` **saturates** — the naive Bloch-Wigner volume route is
**intensive**, exactly like the trace binding `E₀·Δtr_M` (2026-06-02) and every
other single-object route. The deuteron's +0.19% is **not** a closure: it is the
`vol_eff` substitution (Hopf `0 → 7.327`) landing near the deuteron binding, and it
does **not** extend — A=3 misses by −60%. **The naive charm/torus-composition volume,
as a direct `B ∝ V_hyp`, does not supply the `∝A` magnitude.** It joins the intensive
family; it is not, alone, the missing extensive term.

## Finding 2 — the owner's free-vs-bound distinction is *visible in the data*

The deuteron link is the **Hopf link, Vol = 0** (Seifert-fibred, torus-like) — the
"free charm has no volume" phenomenon at the nuclear level. The route only works by
substituting an **effective** volume `vol_eff = 7.327 = Vol(𝓑₃)·8G` (the **Borromean
3-body** volume). That `Vol=0 → Vol_eff` substitution **is** the bound-hyperbolization
the owner named — a vol=0 torus-like link acquiring a hyperbolic (Bloch-Wigner) volume
when bound. In the corpus it is a **substitution, not a derivation**: nothing derives
*why* the Hopf's effective volume is the Borromean 8G. Deriving it is exactly the
`b_i·β(T_{m,n})·b_j` composition-closure Bloch-Wigner computation
(`bring-hyperbolic-knot-bloch-decomposition.md`: the Bloch group is generated by the
torus operators under composition).

## Finding 3 — the sharpened surviving angle (extensive by construction)

The single-link volume is one object per atom (intensive). The **extensive** version
is a **sum over the `A−1` inter-nucleon arcs** (and the `C(A,3)` triples) of the
**per-arc/per-triple composition-closure** Bloch-Wigner volume — each arc a
`b_i·β(T_{m,n})·b_j` closure. This is `∝A` by construction and is **un-tested**.

Magnitude sanity (why even this must be super-pairwise): a *linear* per-arc sum gives
³H = 2×²H ≈ 4.4 MeV, still short of the 8.48 MeV AME (ratio 3.8×, not 2×). So the
closure needs the **3-body (triangle) volume** — and the corpus's own `vol_eff` for
the deuteron is *already* the **Borromean 3-body volume** (7.327). This is fully
consistent with the proved pairwise cap `ρ_A ≤ C(A,2)`
(`QOU.MassTheory.exchangeDet_pow_two_le_four`): the term must be irreducibly ≥3-body.
