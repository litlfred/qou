## §1. Specific mapping q_0 → X(K)

q_0 ∈ ℝ is **not** an element of X(K); X(K) is a 2-complex-dim algebraic variety. The connection is via the **meridian-trace** at the discrete-faithful representation.

For $K = \mathcal{B}_3$, the meridian holonomy $\rho_{\mathrm{DF}}(\mu_i) \in SL_2(\mathbb{C})$ has eigenvalues $(\mu, \mu^{-1})$, so $\mathrm{tr}(\rho_{\mathrm{DF}}(\mu_i)) = \mu + \mu^{-1}$. The Hecke parameter relation (framing-conventional):

$$q_0 + q_0^{-1} \;=\; [2]_{q_0} \;=\; \mathrm{tr}(\rho_{\mathrm{DF}}(\mu_i))$$

so

$$\boxed{q_0 \;=\; \frac{\mathrm{tr}(\rho_{\mathrm{DF}}(\mu)) + \sqrt{\mathrm{tr}(\rho_{\mathrm{DF}}(\mu))^2 - 4}}{2}}$$

**Numerical sanity check:** $q_0 + q_0^{-1} = 1.10978 + 0.90108 \approx 2.01086$. So $\mathrm{tr}(\rho_{\mathrm{DF}}(\mu)) \approx 2.01086$ — slightly above 2 (parabolic / cusp-flat case). This is **directly verifiable** against SnapPy/SnapPea for 𝓑_3.

## §2. Archimedean-explicit RHS

Standard formulation:

$$X(K) := \mathrm{Hom}(\pi_1(S^3 \setminus K),\, SL_2(\mathbb{C})) \,/\!\!/\, SL_2(\mathbb{C})$$

Factor through a $\mathbb{Z}$-scheme + explicit archimedean base-change:

$$\boxed{X(K) \;=\; \underbrace{X(K)_{\mathbb{Z}}}_{\text{algebraic core}} \;\otimes_{\mathbb{Z}}\; \underbrace{\mathbb{C}}_{\text{archimedean}}}$$

where $X(K)_{\mathbb{Z}} := \mathrm{Spec}\,\mathbb{Z}[X(K)]^{SL_2}$ is the GIT quotient over $\mathbb{Z}$.

**Generators:** Procesi (1976): invariants of SL_2(ℂ) acting on tuples of matrices are generated by **traces** $\tau_\gamma(\rho) := \mathrm{tr}(\rho(\gamma))$. So:

$$\mathbb{Z}[X(K)]^{SL_2} \;=\; \mathbb{Z}[\tau_{\gamma_1}, \tau_{\gamma_2}, \ldots] \,/\,(\text{trace identities})$$

**"Inside vs outside the archimedean evaluation":**

| Inside ⊗_ℤ ℂ (numerical, archimedean) | Outside (algebraic, char-0) |
|---|---|
| Specific 2×2 matrices $\rho(\gamma) \in SL_2(\mathbb{C})$ | Trace polynomial variables $\tau_\gamma$ |
| Eigenvalues $L, M \in \mathbb{C}$ at cusps | A-polynomial $A_K(L, M) \in \mathbb{Z}[L, M]$ |
| $q_0 \in \mathbb{R}$ (the substrate value) | Trace-algebra-defining polynomial relations |

The framework currently does everything in ℂ. **Most of the structure could be done in $\mathbb{Z}[\tau_\gamma]$ or $\overline{\mathbb{Q}}$.**

## §3. A_K(L, M) ↔ algebraic cusp coordinates ↔ Yekutieli boundary

The cusp of $S^3 \setminus K$ is a torus $T^2$ with generators $\mu$ (meridian) and $\lambda$ (longitude). Their SL_2(ℂ) images commute (since $\pi_1(T^2) = \mathbb{Z}^2$), so are simultaneously diagonalizable with eigenvalues $(M, M^{-1})$ and $(L, L^{-1})$. The A-polynomial:

$$A_K(L, M) = 0 \quad \text{cuts out the image of the cusp in } \mathbb{C}^* \times \mathbb{C}^*$$

**Yekutieli boundary connection:**

The Yekutieli MI integrates the connection over a spanning surface $\Sigma$ with $\partial \Sigma \subset K$. The 4^k cell refinement subdivides the bulk of $\Sigma$. The boundary cells (along ∂Σ = the knot K) inherit their holonomy from the **cusp coordinates** $(M, L)$:

| Yekutieli interior cells | bulk of Σ, abstract Hecke connection |
|---|---|
| Yekutieli boundary cells | $\partial \Sigma$ = knot K = cusp loop |
| Boundary holonomy | $\mu$, $\lambda$ matrices |
| Boundary holonomy eigenvalues | $(M, L)$ — constrained by $A_K(L, M) = 0$ |

So Yekutieli MI = (bulk integral) + (boundary holonomy on cusp), where the boundary part is COMPLETELY DETERMINED by $A_K$.

**For 𝓑_3 (3 cusps):** 3 simultaneous A-polynomial constraints $A_K(L_i, M_i) = 0$ for $i = 1, 2, 3$ (one per component). The bulk Yekutieli subdivision has 3 distinguished boundary-cell stacks, each algebraically described.

**Practical implication:** the Yekutieli "research-grade ~1 month" closure work CAN be split:
- Boundary contribution = algebraic, from $A_K$ (in literature for 𝓑_3 — Champanerkar 2008)
- Bulk contribution = the only remaining open piece

This is a significant scope reduction.

## §4. Minimal-archimedean R-categorical Hom

The cleanest categorical formulation:

$$\underline{X(K)} \;:\; \mathrm{CRing}_{\mathbb{Z}} \longrightarrow \mathrm{Set}, \quad R \mapsto \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Grp}/R}(\pi_1(S^3 \setminus K), \underline{SL_2}(R)) \,/\!\!/\, \underline{SL_2}(R)$$

This is **representable** by the $\mathbb{Z}$-scheme $X(K)_{\mathbb{Z}}$:

$$X(K)(R) \;=\; \underline{X(K)}(R) \;=\; \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}\text{-alg}}(\mathbb{Z}[X(K)]^{SL_2},\, R)$$

**Apply archimedean functor:** pick an embedding $\iota: \overline{\mathbb{Q}} \hookrightarrow \mathbb{C}$. Then:

$$X(K)(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}) \xrightarrow{\iota_*} X(K)(\mathbb{C})$$

**Discrete-faithful representation theorem** (Reid, Maclachlan-Reid):

For any finite-volume hyperbolic 3-manifold $M$, the discrete-faithful representation $\rho_{\mathrm{DF}}: \pi_1(M) \to PSL_2(\mathbb{C})$ has trace field $\mathbb{Q}(\mathrm{tr}\,\rho_{\mathrm{DF}})$ which is a **number field of finite degree over $\mathbb{Q}$**. The representation is thus **defined over $\overline{\mathbb{Q}}$**, not requiring transcendental data.

**For 𝓑_3 specifically:** The trace field is closely related to $\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_6) = \mathbb{Q}(i\sqrt{3})$ (cube roots of unity, reflecting the 3-fold rotational symmetry of the Borromean configuration). So:

$$\rho_{\mathrm{DF}} \in X(\mathcal{B}_3)(\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_6))$$

and consequently $q_0 \in \mathbb{Q}(\zeta_6)$ — an **algebraic number of degree ≤ 2 over ℚ** (assuming our identification is correct; precise degree needs SnapPy verification).

## §5. Char-0 assumption — physical observables are algebraic

**Profound observation from the author:** If we assume our universe is char 0 (no observed Frobenius failure at any energy scale), then:

1. The Hecke algebra $H_n(q)$ makes sense over **any commutative char-0 ring**
2. Physical observables can in principle be **algebraic numbers**, not just transcendental
3. The substrate $q_0$ is **an algebraic number** (specifically in $\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_6)$ or similar)
4. **"Different universes" = different Galois embeddings** $\iota: \overline{\mathbb{Q}} \hookrightarrow \mathbb{C}$, all equally valid algebraically

### Mass-predictions as algebraic identities

$$\frac{m_p}{m_e} \;=\; f(q_0, \text{Wedderburn-data}) \;\in\; \overline{\mathbb{Q}}$$

for some explicit algebraic function $f$. The observed real-number value is $\iota(f(q_0, \ldots))$ for our specific archimedean place. Same for $m_p, m_n, m_\mu, m_W, m_Z, m_H, Q_\beta, \ldots$

### Consequences for the framework

1. **State all QOU over $\overline{\mathbb{Q}}$.** ℂ-evaluation only at the end, when comparing to PDG values.
2. **Mostow-Prasad guarantees this is well-defined.** Geometric component of X(K) is defined over $\overline{\mathbb{Q}}$.
3. **1 ppb precision targets become rational-number identities** to verify exactly, not numerical approximations.
4. **CAL-2 reframes:** instead of "pin $q_0 \approx 1.1097$ to match $m_\mu/m_e$ empirically", state as "select the algebraic number $q_0 \in \mathbb{Q}(\zeta_6)$ whose ℂ-image $\iota(q_0)$ matches the empirical $m_\mu/m_e$ to experimental precision". The algebraic identity is the substrate; the empirical matching is the archimedean pin.
5. **Galois orbit gives "multiverse" structure** (purely categorically): the Galois group $\mathrm{Gal}(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}/\mathbb{Q})$ acts on $X(K)(\overline{\mathbb{Q}})$, giving conjugate "universes" — only ONE of which we observe (via our archimedean place).

### What this DOESN'T claim

This formulation does NOT predict physical observables are RATIONAL. They're ALGEBRAIC (in some specific number field, like $\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_6)$ or its quadratic extensions). The "rational" framing is a special case of "algebraic over $\mathbb{Q}$".

It also does NOT claim physical observables MUST be algebraic — only that they CAN be, and the framework naturally supports it.

## §9. AJ conjecture (Garoufalidis) crosses the archimedean wall

**Author follow-up question:** *"keep the AJ conjecture (Garoufalidis) across the archmedan wall. we have algbaric stament too? can we utilize?"*

**Yes — and this is the most actionable algebraic-side hook.** The AJ conjecture is purely algebraic in $\mathbb{Z}[q^{\pm 1}]$; no archimedean evaluation is needed to state or use it.

### The algebraic statement

Let $\mathcal{A}_q := \mathbb{Z}[q^{\pm 1}]\langle \hat{L}, \hat{M}\rangle \,/\, (\hat{L}\hat{M} - q\,\hat{M}\hat{L})$ be the **q-Weyl algebra** over $\mathbb{Z}[q^{\pm 1}]$. It's a non-commutative associative algebra. The operators act on functions $f(M)$ by:

$$\hat{M} f(M) = M \cdot f(M), \qquad \hat{L} f(M) = f(q M)$$

**Definition.** The **quantum A-polynomial** $\hat{A}_K \in \mathcal{A}_q$ of a knot $K$ is the generator of the **recurrence-ideal** of the colored Jones polynomial $J_N(K; q)$, where the recurrence ideal is:

$$I_K := \{P \in \mathcal{A}_q \,:\, P \cdot J_N(K; q) = 0 \;\text{for all }N\}$$

**AJ Conjecture (Garoufalidis 2004):** the classical limit $\hat{A}_K |_{q \to 1, \hat{L} \to L, \hat{M} \to M}$ equals the classical A-polynomial $A_K(L, M)$ up to multiplication by a polynomial in $\mathbb{Z}[M]$.

**Proved for:**
- 2-bridge knots (Lê 2006)
- (-2, 3, 2k+1) pretzel knots (Lê-Tran 2012)
- Most knots in the Rolfsen table (computational verification)
- Many torus and cable knots (multiple authors)
- Borromean rings 𝓑_3: status — likely proved or computable; needs literature check (Habiro? Hikami?)

### Why this matters for the framework

The colored Jones $J_N(K; q) \in \mathbb{Z}[q^{\pm 1}]$ is an **algebraic invariant**. For 𝓑_3:

$$J_1(\mathcal{B}_3; q) = \mathrm{Bracket} = (4 + h^2 - 3h^4 - h^6)/(h^2 + 4) \cdot [2]_q^? \quad \text{(framing-conventional)}$$

The framework already computes $J_N$ via the Wedderburn Markov trace (`alpha_k_jones_markov_h{3,4}.py`). What AJ adds:

**An algebraic RECURRENCE for the entire $J_N$ tower.** Instead of recomputing each $J_N$ via Hecke, AJ gives:

$$\hat{A}_K(\hat{L}, \hat{M}; q) \cdot J_N(K; q) = 0$$

as an EXACT identity in $\mathbb{Z}[q^{\pm 1}]$. Translating from operator form:

$$\sum_{(i, j)} c_{ij}(q^N, q) \cdot J_{N+j}(K; q) = 0$$

for specific coefficients $c_{ij}(q^N, q) \in \mathbb{Z}[q^{\pm 1}, q^N]$. This is a **finite-order q-difference recurrence** for $J_N$, computable from $\hat{A}_K$ alone.

**Key insight:** the AJ identity HOLDS on the algebraic side regardless of archimedean evaluation. It can be USED on the algebraic side to construct $J_{N+1}$ from $(J_N, J_{N-1}, \ldots, J_{N-d})$ purely symbolically. Only the FINAL numerical evaluation $q := q_0$ crosses the archimedean wall.

### How the framework can utilize

1. **Algebraic Jones tower:** rather than re-running the Hecke Wedderburn computation for each $N$, use $\hat{A}_K$ once to generate the entire $\{J_N\}_{N \ge 1}$ symbolically. Single algebraic recurrence replaces $O(N)$ Hecke evaluations.

2. **Volume conjecture via algebraic asymptotic:** the Kashaev-Murakami-Murakami Volume Conjecture
   $$\lim_{N \to \infty} \frac{2\pi}{N} \log |J_N(K; e^{2\pi i / N})| = \mathrm{Vol}(K)$$
   becomes the ASYMPTOTIC of the algebraic recurrence — the AJ operator's leading-order behavior determines the saddle-point of $J_N$. For 𝓑_3 with Vol = 8G, this is THE structural source of the Catalan constant (D16 Bloch K_3 hook).

3. **Categorical derivation of Markov-trace closed form (rescoped Path B from PR #1231 §11):** the closed form $(4+h^2-3h^4-h^6)/(h^2+4)$ is the $N=2$ instance of the algebraic Jones tower. The AJ recurrence GENERATES all higher-N values from lower ones — providing the categorical derivation of "why" the coefficients are what they are: they're determined by the structure of $\hat{A}_{\mathcal{B}_3}$, which in turn is determined by $\pi_1(S^3 \setminus \mathcal{B}_3)$.

4. **CAL-2 algebraic restatement:** the substrate $q_0$ is the algebraic number such that the AJ-recurrence-defined $J_N$ tower, evaluated at $q_0$, matches the observed $m_\mu / m_e$ ratio. This is a SINGLE algebraic equation defining $q_0 \in \overline{\mathbb{Q}}$ rather than a numerical optimization.
