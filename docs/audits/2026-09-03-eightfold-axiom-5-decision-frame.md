## 0. What is being decided, and why it needs the owner

Every route to a flavour `SU(3)` from the *single-fibre* category of a quantum
observable universe is now closed by a proved invariant:

| route | closed by | invariant |
|---|---|---|
| a summand of `V^{⊗n}` | `Sym2GateMultiplicity`, `Sym2GateFSTrichotomy` | Frobenius–Schur parity |
| re-tuning the Ocneanu trace | `OcneanuTwoThreeNotProportional` | trace parameter is the fibre quantum dimension |
| a different fibre functor `τ'` (PR #6458) | `FibreFunctorDimensionNotCategorical` | Clebsch–Gordan parity of `Rep(SL_q(2))` |
| identifying the octonion imaginaries with `𝔰𝔲(3)` | `OctonionicSu3RankObstruction` | rank |

What they have in common is the fusion rules of the category generated by `V`,
and those are fixed by **condition 5** of
[`def:quantum-observable-universe`](../../content/quantum-observable-universe/quantum-observable-universes/quantum-observable-universe.md):

> *(Categorical volume constraint)* … an **isomorphism** `ε : Λ²V ≅ 𝟙`
> preserved by all tensor automorphisms.

`Λ²V ≅ 𝟙` forces `dim V = 2` (`exteriorSquareDim_eq_one_iff`), hence
`Λ³V = 0`, hence no singlet in `V^{⊗3}`, hence no `𝟏𝟎 ⊕ 𝟖 ⊕ 𝟖 ⊕ 𝟏`. The
eightfold way needs an object whose *third* exterior power is the unit. So
the next move is not a search inside the present definition; it is a
decision about the definition, and that is the owner's to make.

The block itself already records that condition 5 has two readings of
unequal strength (its own table: epimorphism ⇒ `N ≥ 2`, isomorphism ⇒
`N = 2`) and chose the isomorphism because "the step to `det(g_V) = 1` — the
whole point of condition 5 — does not go through" under the weak one. That
choice is what this frame asks the owner to revisit, knowing what each
alternative costs.

## 1. The options

Each option is given the same four-part frame. "Consumers" are measured on
this branch: `thm:su2-emergence` has **10** `uses[]` consumers;
`exteriorSquareDim_eq_one_iff` / `VolumeConstraintForcesFibreTwo` is consumed
by `VolumeForm.lean`, `CategoricalFoundations.lean`,
`SU2EmergenceRankForced.lean`, `LightningPath.lean`, `volume-from-rigidity`,
`su2-emergence`, and by the convention-D forcing chain
(`markovWeights_eq_jones_of_volume_constraint`,
`ocneanu_eq_jones_of_volume_constraint`).

### A. Keep condition 5 as it is (`N = 2`) and add a **second fundamental object** `W` with `Λ³W ≅ 𝟙`

- **What it means.** The definition acquires a second generator: a quantum
  observable universe is `(𝐂, A, τ; V, W)` with `Λ²V ≅ 𝟙` (the present
  constraint, mass sector, `SU(2)`-type) and `Λ³W ≅ 𝟙` (an `SL(3)`-type volume
  form, flavour sector). `Λ³W ≅ 𝟙` gives `W^∨ ≅ Λ²W`, which is exactly
  `𝟑̄ ≅ Λ²𝟑` — the relation the Sym² gate needs (complex type, conjugate
  absent from `W` itself). Nothing about `V` changes.
- **What it costs.** A definition variant (new block, or a `structure`
  extending the present one), a Lean class for the second volume form, and a
  compatibility check against conditions 1–4 (rigidity and non-degenerate
  integration are per-object and unaffected; observability
  `Hom(𝟙, Ad(A)) = 0` and measurability are about `A`, not `V`, so
  unaffected on their face — to be verified, not assumed). Roughly a day of
  formalize-first work before any physics.
- **What it risks.** `W` is an **import** unless something in the substrate
  produces it. The corpus's own anti-laundering rule applies: adding `W` to the
  definition *postulates* the eightfold way's fundamental rather than deriving
  it. The honest label would be "flavour `SU(3)_f` is an axiom datum". The
  `SM` appendix's proved statement that `SU(3)` and `U(1)` "must come from
  symmetry visible only at the groupoid level — multi-strand structure" is
  consistent with a second object but does not supply one.
- **What it commits to.** Explaining why the substrate carries two
  fundamentals. `qou-dwsh`'s specification ("a groupoid with inequivalent
  fibre objects carrying a 3-dim `W` whose fundamental is not self-dual") is
  this option stated at the groupoid level.

### B. Weaken condition 5 to the **epimorphism** reading (`Λ²V ↠ 𝟙`, `N ≥ 2`)

- **What it means.** The block's own weaker row: `C(N,2) ≠ 0` only. `N = 3`
  becomes admissible for the *same* `V`.
- **What it costs.** The determinant constraint `det(g_V) = 1` is lost, and
  with it `thm:su2-emergence` (10 consumers), `VolumeForm.lean`,
  `SU2EmergenceRankForced`, and the convention-D forcing chain: under the weak
  reading the alternating weight need not vanish
  (`weak_volume_reading_admits_three`, `weightAlt_ne_zero_at_three`), so
  **convention D is no longer forced** and the 2026-08-15 R1 ruling's
  derivation has to be re-established some other way. Every mass and binding
  number downstream of convention D is on the blast radius.
- **What it risks.** Destabilising the mass sector to buy a flavour sector.
  It also does not *give* `N = 3`: it only stops forbidding it. The category
  generated by a 3-dimensional `V` with only an epimorphism `Λ²V ↠ 𝟙` is not
  `Rep(SL_q(3))` (that needs `Λ³V ≅ 𝟙`), so a further axiom would be needed
  anyway.
- **What it commits to.** Re-deriving convention D and re-running the
  precision dashboards. Not recommended.

### C. Replace condition 5 by a **top-exterior-power** form `Λ^N V ≅ 𝟙`, with `N` a datum of the universe

- **What it means.** The constraint becomes "the fundamental carries a volume
  form on its top exterior power", i.e. `SL(N)`-type for a chosen `N`. At
  `N = 2` this is literally the present definition, so nothing existing
  changes for the mass sector.
- **What it costs.** The definition gains a parameter; `su2-emergence`
  becomes "`SU(N)` emergence with `N` an input", and `SU2EmergenceRankForced`
  (which derives `N ≠ 3` from `Λ²V ≅ 𝟙`) becomes a statement *about the
  `N = 2` case* rather than a theorem about all universes. Lean: generalise
  `VolumeForm` to `N`; moderate.
- **What it risks.** One universe still has one `N`. Mass needs `N = 2`
  (convention D), flavour needs `N = 3`, so a single QOU cannot host both —
  this option forces the **multi-universe / groupoid** picture (T3g's original
  "inter-universe flavour", `rem:fivefold-affine-charts-generations`) as the
  only way to have both, and that picture is asserted in a `remark` with no
  Lean and no proof.
- **What it commits to.** Flavour living in a *different* universe from mass,
  with a transition structure between them that nothing in the corpus yet
  defines.

### D. Do nothing

- **What it means.** The chapter stays as it is: every flavour statement
  conditional on `N = 3`, the ledger row "the obstruction is proved; the
  construction is missing", now with the fibre-functor and reconstruction
  routes added to the closed list.
- **What it costs.** Nothing.
- **What it risks.** Nothing; the corpus is honest as it stands.
- **What it commits to.** Nothing. The eightfold chapter remains a
  classification the framework reproduces conditionally and does not derive.

## 2. Recommendation (judgement, not a finding)

**A, formalize-first, labelled as an axiom datum.** It is the only option that
leaves the mass sector untouched, it matches the specification the corpus
already reached independently (`qou-dwsh`), and the Sym² apparatus already
tells us what `W` must satisfy (complex type, `W^∨ ≅ Λ²W`). Its cost is
honesty about provenance: the flavour fundamental would be *postulated*.
B is not recommended (it breaks convention D). C is A with the two objects in
two universes and no bridge defined; it can be revisited if A's
compatibility check fails.

**Default if the owner says nothing: D.** Nothing changes; PR #6458 already
records the fibre-functor closure and the relocation of the missing object.

## 3. What a first step under A would look like (so the cost is concrete)

1. Lean: `class SecondFundamental (𝐂) [QOU 𝐂] where W : 𝐂; vol : Λ³W ≅ 𝟙;
   inv : ∀ g, Λ³ g = id` — a content-bearing class with **no instance**
   (an open primary conjecture, §3b-tax), never an `instance` fabricated to
   close it.
2. Prove from it, sorry-free: `W^∨ ≅ Λ²W`; `W` is complex type (so it passes
   the Sym² gate — `Sym2GateComplexType` already has the shape); `Λ³W ≠ 0`
   (so `V^{⊗3}`'s missing singlet is present in `W^{⊗3}`).
3. Only then the chapter: restate the octet/decuplet blocks as conditional on
   `SecondFundamental` instead of on the bare "`N = 3`", which is a strictly
   more precise hypothesis.

None of this derives the eightfold way. It moves the conditionality from a
number to a named categorical hypothesis, which is what the framework's
conditional-class discipline (§3b-cond) exists for.
