## Honest scope — (a), not (b); what stays OPEN

This derives the *factorisation* `b_a ∣ b_n`; it is scope-(a), not a
structural exclusion of any physical state.

   `σ₀ᵃ` — a suggestive but not rigorous "not-independent" statement. I
   have **no** rigorous derivation that this forbids an independent mass
