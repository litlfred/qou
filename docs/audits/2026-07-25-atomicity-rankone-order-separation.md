# Atomicity / rank-one — the order-theoretic separation (rank-2 independence test, order angle)

## The question

The atomicity upgrade asks whether the **rank-one** premise — the Tannaka dual
`G = Aut^⊗(τ)` of `(𝐂, τ)` is a rank-one group: a single fundamental object `V`
([`def:fundamental-object`](../../content/quantum-observable-universe/quantum-universes/fundamental-object.md),
symplectic self-dual, tensor-generating), every simple a power of `V` — can be
**derived** from the jet-tower ordering axioms rather than assumed. The decisive
independence test: *does a rank-2 model satisfy the jet-tower ordering axioms?*

## The order-theoretic core

The jet tower's simple objects are the powers of the fundamental generator(s),
ordered by **tensor generation** (divisibility in the Grothendieck monoid of
simples). For a category *freely* generated by `r` fundamental objects that
monoid is the free commutative monoid on `r` generators, and the
tensor-generation order is divisibility (componentwise). Then:

- **rank one** (single `V`; simples `Sym^k V` indexed by `k`): the order is the
  usual order on a one-generator monoid — **total**, a chain;
- **rank ≥ 2** (independent fundamentals): the order is componentwise on a
  ≥ 2-generator monoid — **not total**; two distinct fundamental weights are
  incomparable.

**Characterization (Lean, sorry-free — `freeGen_total_iff_subsingleton`):** the
free tensor-generation order on `S → ℕ` is **total** (the jet-tower chain axiom)
**iff** `S` is a subsingleton — **at most one fundamental object (rank ≤ 1)**.
The forward direction (`freeGen_total_forces_subsingleton`) is the *derivation*
direction for free models: chain axiom ⟹ rank-one, via the incomparable
fundamental pair. The converse (`subsingleton_forces_freeGen_total`) closes the
iff.

So the rank-2 independence test resolves, **for every free model**, as
**exclusion** — totality of the tensor-generation order is *exactly* rank-one
among free models. The excluding invariant is the incomparable fundamental pair.

## Honest scope (scope-(a), conditional)

- **Free models only.** Proved for the free tensor-generation (divisibility)
  order. A rank-2 weight lattice admits *other* total orders (e.g.
  lexicographic) that are not the tensor-generation order; those are not
  addressed, and non-free models (with relations among generators) are not
  addressed.
- **The corpus premise stays open.** This does **not** prove the corpus
  jet-tower ordering axioms *entail* totality of the tensor-generation order —
  that entailment is the remaining premise of `qou-ngwo`, and is precisely what
  the derivation route on #4266 targets.
- **Nothing wired; no fabricated instance.** The rank-one premise remains a
  premise (AGENTS.md §"Axiomatization is NOT a Proof"); the `JetTowerChain`
  class carries the totality content and has **no** rank-2 instance.

## Net — the narrowing

The atomicity question reduces to a single sharp premise:

> *Do the jet-tower ordering axioms force totality of the tensor-generation order?*

If yes, rank-one follows for free models by `freeGen_total_forces_subsingleton`;
`JetTowerChain` is the order-side hook to cite/verify against. This is the
forward, formalize-first complement to the Tannakian route on #4266.

## Reconnaissance — the corpus has no formalized jet-tower ordering axiom (2026-07-25, follow-up)

Attacking the open premise from the corpus side (does a jet-tower ordering axiom
already exist to connect to `freeGen_total_forces_subsingleton`?) returned a clear
negative:

- `FundamentalObject`
  ([`fundamental-object.lean`](../../content/quantum-observable-universe/quantum-universes/fundamental-object.lean))
  carries **no rank/dimension field** — `V`, `volume`, `coform`, and the two snake
  identities only. The dimension-2 / rank-2 of `V` is narrative (Deligne–Milne),
  **not formalised**.
- `KeystoneBridgeC1Binding.lean` concerns the normalizer recurrence
  `N(n) = (n−1)!`, not an ordering on simples.
- `JetOrderIndependence.lean` is a **different** object — the jet-Jacobian rank of
  the three coloured-Jones series (`4₁, 5₂, 𝓑₃`), not a tensor-generation order.

So the "jet-tower ordering axioms" of `qou-ngwo` are **genuinely unformalised**;
the route is a `premise → theorem` construction, not a re-proof of an existing
axiom.

**Deliverable — the minimal categorical→order bridge (Lean, sorry-free).** Added
to the same file: a conjectural §3b class `TensorGenerationFree` (the honest,
no-instance premise that the simple-object index is the free comm monoid on the
fundamental objects, via a monotone multiplicity map `ι` with
`ι (simple s) = fundamentalWeight s`), and the conditional theorem
`rankOne_of_chain_of_bridge`:

> `[TensorGenerationFree W S] → IsTotal W (· ≤ ·) → Subsingleton S`.

This is the atomicity upgrade's `premise → theorem` step, honestly delimited:
**rank-one is now derived from exactly two named premises** — the categorical
dictionary (`TensorGenerationFree`) and the jet-tower chain axiom (totality) — the
implication itself proved sorry-free, no bridge instance fabricated. The remaining
open content is exactly those two premises; the order-theoretic half (chain ⟹
rank-one, given free generation) is closed.
