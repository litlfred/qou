## 2. Model choice

Three candidate models:

- **(a) Pseudofunctor `(LocallyDiscrete Cᵒᵖ) ⥤ Cat` with groupoid fibres.** This is
  Yekutieli's definition verbatim (§2: "a prestack is a pseudofunctor
  `(Open X)ᵒᵖ → Cat`"). It is faithful. It is heavy because the Hom presheaf
  `Y ↦ Hom_{G(Y)}(f*x, f*y)` has restriction maps that need the composition
  isomorphisms `mapComp'`, and functoriality of that presheaf is a coherence argument.
- **(b) A category fibred in groupoids over `C`.** Equivalent to (a) by the
  Grothendieck construction. Mathlib has the pseudofunctor version
  (`Bicategory/Grothendieck.lean`), but the contravariant `∫ᶜ F` lacks a `Category`
  instance there, and there is no cleavage or fibration API. So (b) is a later
  convenience on top of (a), not a shortcut.
- **(c) A strict presheaf of groupoids `Cᵒᵖ ⥤ Grpd`.** Simplest. **Not faithful in
  general**: `Tors(G)` is not strictly functorial, because pullback of torsors is defined
  only up to unique isomorphism, and Yekutieli's twisted forms need the 2-cells. Under
  (c), Thm 4.17 can only be stated after a strictification, and the paper does not
  provide one.

**Decision: (a) is the target definition. The first milestone is placed where (a)
and the concrete model agree.** Write `G(U)` for the groupoid of `A`-equivariant
presheaves on `C/U`. Its Hom presheaf is a sub-presheaf of Mathlib's `presheafHom`,
which already handles restriction along `Over.map`. So the prestack (descent of
morphisms) axiom for this fibred category can be proved without building the
pseudofunctor first.

## 3. What is proved

File: `folio/quantum-observable-universe/lean/QOU/MathlibExt/StackGerbe.lean`.

- `PresheafAction A P`: an action of a presheaf of operators `A` on a presheaf `P`,
  compatible with restriction. For a presheaf of groups take `A = G ⋙ forget GrpCat`.
  The group axioms are deliberately not imposed, because the theorem does not use them.
- `equivariantHom αP αQ : Subpresheaf (presheafHom P Q)` gives the `A`-equivariant
  morphisms `P|_{C/X} ⟶ Q|_{C/X}`.
- **`isSheaf_equivariantHom`**: if `Q` is a sheaf, then `equivariantHom αP αQ` is a
  sheaf, for every presheaf `P`. Proof: `presheafHom P Q` is a sheaf (Mathlib), and
  equivariance is a local condition. It is checked on the pulled-back covering sieve,
  using separatedness of `Q` and naturality of `φ` along `Over.homMk k`.

This is the **descent-of-morphisms (prestack) axiom** for the fibred category of
`A`-sheaves, and hence for its full subcategory `Tors(G)`.

### Milestones 2 & 3 (2026-11-15 — `Hom = Iso` on `A`-torsors, `Groupoid` fibres, and `Tors(A)` gerbe axioms)

Added in `folio/quantum-observable-universe/lean/QOU/MathlibExt/StackGerbe.lean` (all
sorry-free, 0 warnings, standard axioms `[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]`):

1. **`Hom = Iso` on `A`-torsors (Milestone 2):**
   - `PresheafAction.IsPseudoTorsor`: sectionwise simply transitive action (`∀ U p q, ∃! a, αP.smul U a p = q`).
   - `localNonemptySieve P U` and `LocallyNonempty J P`: the sieve of `f : V ⟶ U` with `Nonempty (P.obj (op V))` belongs to `J U`.
   - `SheafTorsor J A P`: an `A`-sheaf on `(C, J)` that is a pseudo-torsor and locally non-empty in `J`.
   - `equivariantHom_injective` & `equivariantHom_surjective_of_nonempty`: sectionwise injectivity of every `A`-equivariant morphism between pseudo-torsors, and surjectivity wherever the source has a section.
   - `equivariantHom_surjective`: sectionwise surjectivity for any `A`-torsors `TP, TQ` on `(C, J)`, proved by gluing the unique local preimages along `localNonemptySieve P Y.left ∈ J Y.left` via `(hP' S hS).amalgamate` and checking agreement in `Q` via `(hQ' S hS).isSeparatedFor.ext`.
   - **`isIso_of_equivariantHom_torsor`** & **`inv_mem_equivariantHom`**: every `A`-equivariant morphism `φ : P|_{C/X} ⟶ Q|_{C/X}` between `A`-torsors is an isomorphism (`IsIso φ.1`) and its inverse `inv φ.1` is `A`-equivariant.
2. **Groupoid fibres and functorial pullback (Milestone 2/3):**
   - `TorsorFibre J A X`, `torsorFibreCategory`, and **`torsorFibreGroupoid`**: `Category` and `Groupoid` instances on the fibre of multiplicative `A`-torsors over `X : C`.
   - `torsorPullback J A f : TorsorFibre J A Y ⥤ TorsorFibre J A X`, **`torsorPullback_id`**, and **`torsorPullback_comp`**: contravariant base-change functors between groupoid fibres satisfying strict identity and composition laws.
3. **Band isomorphism, local connectedness, and `SiteGerbeData` instantiation (Milestone 3):**
   - `IsPresheafOfGroups A`, `regularAction A hA`, `isPseudoTorsor_regularAction`, and `trivialSheafTorsor J A hA hAsheaf`: the trivial `A`-torsor over `(C, J)`.
   - **`trivialTorsorEndEquiv`**: canonical bijection `(equivariantHom (regularAction A hA) (regularAction A hA)).obj (op X) ≃ A.obj (op X)` via evaluation at `1 ∈ A.obj (op X)` (the band axiom `Aut(trivial) ≃ A(X)`).
   - `equivariantHom_of_sections`, `localIsoSieve`, and **`torsor_locallyConnected`**: any two multiplicative `A`-torsors `TP, TQ` become `A`-equivariantly isomorphic on the `J`-covering intersection sieve `localNonemptySieve P U ⊓ localNonemptySieve Q U ∈ J U`.
   - `SiteGerbeData J A` and **`torsorSiteGerbe J A hA hAsheaf`**: Grothendieck-topology gerbe structure on `(C, J)` banded by `A`, instantiated sorry-free by `MulSheafTorsor J A`.

Still open for full D3:
1. effective descent of objects (gluing torsors along a cocycle on a covering sieve — the stack object-gluing axiom);
2. general abstract pseudofunctor-valued gerbes not embedded in presheaves on `C` and the stack-level Yekutieli Thm 4.17.

## 4. Cost estimate for full Thm 4.17 under D3

Here "session" means one agent session of about 2–4 h, with the private-olean
`lean`-direct recipe.

| Step | Content | Estimate |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | `Prestack` as `Pseudofunctor (LocallyDiscrete Cᵒᵖ) Cat` with groupoid fibres; the Hom presheaf `homPresheaf x y : (Over X)ᵒᵖ ⥤ Type` built from `mapComp'`, with functoriality proved | 1–2 sessions (the coherence is the hard part) |
| S2 | `IsPrestack` (Hom presheaves are sheaves), `IsStack` (plus effective descent for every covering sieve, phrased with descent data on sieves) | 1–2 sessions |
| S3 | Torsor stack: `Tors(G)` as a pseudofunctor; `Hom = Iso` on torsors; prestack (reuse `isSheaf_equivariantHom`); effective descent (glue torsors, which needs sheaf gluing for presheaves with action) | 2–4 sessions |
| S4 | Gerbe: locally nonempty and locally connected, stated with sieves; band / automorphism sheaf; neutral gerbe ≃ `Tors(G)` | 1–2 sessions |
| S5 | Yekutieli Def 3.21 (central extension of gerbes `1 → A → H → G`), the obstruction class in `H²(X, A)` of Thm 4.17. Mathlib has `H¹` but no nonabelian `H²` or Čech `H²` over sieves, so a Čech-2 layer is needed (or reuse of the cochain-style `PresheafCentralExtensionObstruction.lean`) | 3–6 sessions |
| S6 | Thm 4.17 proper (existence and uniqueness up to the obstruction class) | 2–4 sessions |

**Total: roughly 10–20 sessions.** The critical risk is S1: if Mathlib's
`Pseudofunctor` coherence lemmas for `LocallyDiscrete` (`mapComp'`, `mapId'`) are not
convenient, S1 alone can double. A cheaper faithful alternative for S1–S2 is to
restrict to stacks that are **full substacks of sheaf categories** (as here). That
covers `Tors(G)` and twisted forms but not abstract gerbes, so it cannot state
Thm 4.17 in full generality.

## 5. Honest scope

- The milestone is genuine (no `sorry`, no `True`, no hypothesis-as-field). It is
  one axiom of a prestack, for one concrete fibred category.
- It does **not** establish that D3 is cheap. The two expensive layers (pseudofunctor
  coherence, nonabelian `H²`) are untouched.
