## The closure

### Setup (per `jet-prolongation.md`)

The brane-tower algebras are built recursively:

$$A_0 = A, \qquad A_{n+1} = J^1(A_n) = A_n \oplus \Omega^1(A_n)$$

with multiplication

$$(a, \omega) \cdot (b, \xi) = (ab,\; a\cdot\xi + \omega\cdot b)$$

i.e. the *square-zero* algebra structure (trivial extension): products
of two 1-forms vanish. Here `Ω¹(A_n) = ker(m: A_n ⊗ A_n → A_n)` is the
universal Kähler bimodule per `prop:jet-prolongation`.

### Woronowicz left-covariant restriction

A left-covariant differential calculus on a Hopf algebra `(A, Δ, S, ε)`
([Woronowicz 1989, *Differential calculus on compact matrix
pseudogroups*]) is an A-bimodule `Γ` together with a derivation
`d: A → Γ` such that the left coaction `Δ_L: Γ → A ⊗ Γ` extends `Δ`
compatibly. The **standard left-covariant calculus** on a quantum
group `A = O_q(G)` is generated by 1-forms `ω` with the bimodule
relation

$$\boxed{\; x \cdot \omega \;=\; \omega \cdot \sigma_q(x), \qquad \sigma_q(x) := q^{2\deg(x)}\,x \;}$$

where `σ_q` is the **modular automorphism** induced by the q-grading
on `A`. Equivalently, `ω · x = q^{-2 deg(x)} · x · ω`.

For QOU's brane-tower algebra `A_n = H_{n_0}(R, q)` (the Iwahori–Hecke
algebra over the substrate ring `R`), the q-grading is the **GS
filtration grade** `ℓ` (Coxeter length) — making `σ_q` the canonical
left-covariant twist on the standard calculus.

We adopt this convention going forward (the `LeftRule` instance in the
Lean class below).

### The closure (proposition)

**Proposition.** *Let `A_n` carry the standard Woronowicz left-covariant
differential calculus with modular automorphism `σ_q(x) = q^{2 deg(x)} x`.
Let `x ∈ A_n` be q-homogeneous of grade `1/2` (Hecke generator
`σ_i^{±1}` after eigenvalue normalisation). Define the level-shift
operators on `A_{n+1} = A_n ⊕ Ω¹(A_n)`:*

$$\widehat{U}_x := L_{(x, 0)}, \qquad \widehat{V}_\omega := L_{(1, \omega)}$$

*(left multiplication by the indicated elements). Then on the
transient summand `ker τ̂_n` (= `Ω¹(A_n)` component), the operators
satisfy*

$$\boxed{\; \widehat{U}_x \cdot \widehat{V}_\omega \;=\; q \cdot \widehat{V}_\omega \cdot \widehat{U}_x \;}$$

*and the Faddeev–Kashaev pentagon identity for `Φ_q` applies on this
pair.*

### Proof

Compute the products in `J^1(A_n)`:

$$\widehat{U}_x \widehat{V}_\omega \;=\; (x, 0)(1, \omega) \;=\; (x\cdot 1,\; x\cdot\omega + 0\cdot 1) \;=\; (x,\; x\cdot\omega)$$

$$\widehat{V}_\omega \widehat{U}_x \;=\; (1, \omega)(x, 0) \;=\; (1\cdot x,\; 1\cdot 0 + \omega\cdot x) \;=\; (x,\; \omega\cdot x)$$

The descended-summand entries (the `A_n` first slot) coincide.
Restricting to the transient summand projects onto the second slot:

$$\bigl[\widehat{U}_x \widehat{V}_\omega\bigr]_{\mathrm{tr}} = x\cdot\omega, \qquad \bigl[\widehat{V}_\omega \widehat{U}_x\bigr]_{\mathrm{tr}} = \omega\cdot x.$$

By the Woronowicz left rule with `deg(x) = 1/2`:

$$x \cdot \omega \;=\; \omega \cdot \sigma_q(x) \;=\; \omega \cdot q^{2 \cdot 1/2}\, x \;=\; q\,(\omega \cdot x).$$

So `[Û V̂]_tr = q · [V̂ Û]_tr`, i.e. on the transient summand
`Û V̂ = q V̂ Û`. **∎**

### Pentagon-ascension corollary

Once the q-commutation `Û V̂ = q V̂ Û` is in place, the Faddeev–Kashaev
pentagon identity

$$\Phi_q(\widehat{V})\,\Phi_q(\widehat{U}) \;=\; \Phi_q(\widehat{U})\,\Phi_q(\widehat{U}\widehat{V})\,\Phi_q(\widehat{V})$$

applies as an OPERATOR identity on the transient summand. Iterating
on a triple `Φ_q(|V̂_i V̂_j V̂_k|)` at level `n`:

$$\Phi_q(|V_iV_jV_k|^{(n)}) \;=\; \underbrace{\Phi_q(|V_iV_j|^{(n)})\,\Phi_q(|V_k|^{(n)})}_{\text{pair + single at level } n} \;\cdot\; \underbrace{\Phi_q(\widehat{U}\widehat{V})}_{\text{ascends to level } n+1}$$

(applying pentagon once with `u = V̂_i V̂_j` operatorically and
`v = V̂_k`'s level-shift conjugate, then projecting to transient).
The `Φ_q(ÛV̂)` residual lives in `End(A_{n+2})` (next jet level)
because `ÛV̂` couples levels `n` and `n+1`.

The geometric tail bound
[`prop:jet-tower-convergence`](../../content/quantum-observable-universe/braids-and-knots/jet-tower-convergence.md)
controls the residual: `|Φ_q(ÛV̂)| ≤ C · z^{2n}` for some `C`, so the
infinite jet sum converges.

class WoronowiczLeftCalculus
    (R : Type*) [CommRing R] (q : R)
    (A : Type*) [Ring A] [Algebra R A] where
  /-- The 1-forms (left-covariant Ω¹). -/

  [omega_smul : Module R Omega]
  /-- Left A-action on Ω. -/

## What this closes vs what remains

### Closes (under §3b-cond)

- The OPERATOR `Û V̂ = q V̂ Û` claim on the transient summand,
  conditional on choosing the Woronowicz left-covariant calculus.
- The pentagon identity now applies as an operator identity (not just
  a formal symbol pushing).
- Triple absorption into pair + single + level-(n+1) residual is
  an algebraic consequence of iterated pentagon.

### Open ingredients (the §3b-cond inputs)

1. **Choice of differential calculus.** The proof assumes the *standard
   left-covariant Woronowicz calculus* on the Hecke algebra. The QOU
   paper currently uses the *universal* Kähler differentials
   `Ω¹(A) = ker(m: A⊗A → A)`, which is too large. Restricting to the
   left-covariant subbimodule is a natural Hopf-algebraic specialisation
   but should be MADE EXPLICIT in the paper.
2. **q-grading specification.** The proof assumes `deg` is the GS
   filtration grade (Coxeter length on `H_n(q)`). The paper has this
   structure but does not yet name it as the modular grading.
3. **Lean instance construction.** The class `WoronowiczLeftCalculus`
   above is a §3b-cond hypothesis. To CLOSE it unconditionally (rather
   than conditionally), one must construct an instance for the actual
   brane-tower algebras — which means writing the Hecke-algebra
   Woronowicz calculus as a Lean structure (Mathlib has Woronowicz
   sketches but no full implementation as of 2026-05-09).

### Practical implications

**With the closure conditional on Woronowicz left-rule:**

- The all-orders mass tower formula
  `𝔪 = |Π V| + Σ_n (Σ Φ_q(|·|) + z² Σ Φ_q(|·V·V|) + ...)` is
  algebraically well-defined.
- Triples and higher subsets at level `n` are *absorbed* into level-`n+1`
  via pentagon, so the production code's pair-only formula is
  STRUCTURALLY CORRECT modulo the geometric tail at higher jet levels.
- The remaining gap to physical binding energies (D: 5107 MeV vs
  1875.6 MeV AME) is therefore NOT in the truncation — it must be
  in the **calibration of E_0** or the **per-block Wedderburn weights**
  (i.e., GAP-A-3 / GAP-A-4 are still the load-bearing gaps for binding
  energy, even after pentagon-ascension is closed).
