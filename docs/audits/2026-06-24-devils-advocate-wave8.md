## Highlights

- **The Bucket-B canonical-chain set is uniformly defective at the formal-artifact level — 5/5 open.** The taxonomy of the defect splits cleanly: two **pure vacuous stubs** (`wenzl-bridge`, `y-lambda` — `: True` / `0=0` with no real library decl behind them), one **§0a shadow-stub over a too-weak real decl** (`confinement-topological`, `flavor-split`), and one **tautology + def-disguised-True audit-stub** (`q-beta-cos`).

- **Two findings dent the *mathematics framing*, not just the Lean wiring.** (i) `flavor-split-substrate`'s `no-fit` claim is **false** — the split is a fit-selected ansatz, a production-vs-exploratory-discipline violation the block's own deprecated probe admits ("the substrate-internal answer" after α_EM/α_S failed to match). (ii) `wenzl-bridge`'s **non-integer-rank non-sequitur** is a genuine structural gap: the Schur–Weyl backbone that licenses Wenzl's weight identity has no centraliser realisation at `r₀≈2.5995`, so applicability-at-q₀ is asserted, not established. Both are *limited*-scope (the formula still matches the Hoefsmit trace numerically), but they go beyond "the Lean is vacuous."

- **`y-lambda` is the cleanest illustration of the whole sweep's thesis.** Its witnesses reproduce the closed form to 28+ digits (`0.41539578633456658…`), the bridge level `r₀≈2.5995` cross-appears in independent SDP witnesses — and the Lean theorem is a literal `0 = 0`. The mathematics is sound; the durable formal artifact certifies nothing. Every open-objection in the sweep is this shape.
