## Question

Route A (PR #9530) showed the climbing family `e₀e₂Σ₁ᶜ` on four strands is never
whole-word neutral for `c ≥ 1`. The next question is what the selection rules
(term-by-term linking neutrality, rulings 2026-11-21 / 2026-11-25, plus PSD reading 3,
owner 2026-09-12) keep on **six** strands, the H₆ level of the H₆→H₃ ladder.

## Setup

- Photon: γ = 1 + φ₀σ_i + φ₁σ_{i+1} + φ₀₁·σ_iσ_{i+1}⁻¹, the chiral vertex `V_i = σ_iσ_{i+1}⁻¹`,
  for `i = 0..3` on six strands.
- A neutral term has writhe 0, so (as in the H₃ argument
  `GammaGammaNeutralSelectionAllN.eq_vertexPow_of_writhe_zero`) it is a word in the `V_i` only.
  **The six-strand version of that reduction is not formalised here**; the probe enumerates
  V-words directly.
- Enumerated: all V-words of length ≤ 6 (5460, all distinct after free reduction).
  Neutral test: every component's self-crossing count and every pairwise crossing count is zero.
- Reading 3: `χ_λ(w) ≥ 0` for every λ ⊢ 6 with `y_λ > 0` under convention D, i.e. the
  two-row shapes (6), (5,1), (4,2), (3,3). Evaluated exactly in the seminormal representations
  at `q₀` (rational pin from `gg_ntower_probe`) and at 11/10 and 23/20.

## Result

| stage | count |
|---|---|
| V-words enumerated | 5460 |
| neutral | 1906 |
| neutral with every `V_i` count ≡ 0 mod 3 | 86 |
| reading-3 survivors (all three q) | **58** |

1. **Every survivor uses each vertex a multiple of 3 times.** All 1820 neutral words with some
   count not divisible by 3 fail reading 3. No survivor lies outside the 86.
2. **The minimal survivors are exactly `V_i³`** (6 crossings), the Borromean core on one adjacent
   strand triple. There is no minimal survivor that spans triples.
3. **Every other survivor is a product of two cubes**, up to cyclic rotation and the commutation
   `V₀V₃ = V₃V₀` (disjoint triples): `V_i⁶`; `V_i³V_{i+1}³` and rotations (10 crossings, two cancel
   at the shared generator); `V_i³V_j³` for `|i−j| ≥ 2` and rotations (12 crossings).
4. **Interleaving fails when the triples overlap.** For `{0,2}` and `{1,3}` (strand-sharing but not
   commuting), every interleaved arrangement (e.g. `V₀V₂V₀V₂V₀V₂`) fails on (4,2) and (3,3);
   only the block arrangements survive. For `{0,3}` all interleavings survive, as they equal the
   block product.
5. All 58 are robust across the three q values; no survivor depends on the q₀ pin.

## Reading

Under the two selection rules, six-strand γγ words up to six vertices are generated by
three-vertex nucleon cores. That is a structural statement in the braid group algebra and
Hecke algebra; it involves no measured value and no magnitude. It says nothing yet about the
Q_β magnitude gap (F ≈ 2.4 on the V³ base); the H₆→H₃ residue of these words is the next step
(route C step 2).

## Scope and limits (not established)

- Length ≤ 6 vertices only. "Multiple of 3" is measured, not proved, beyond that bound.
- Words were deduplicated by free reduction only; braid-relation equalities (beyond the
  observed `V₀V₃` commutation) were not quotiented.
- The six-strand "neutral ⇒ V-word" reduction is assumed from the H₃ argument, not formalised.
- Classification is scope (a): a selection measured on an enumerated class, not an exclusion
  proved by an invariant.
