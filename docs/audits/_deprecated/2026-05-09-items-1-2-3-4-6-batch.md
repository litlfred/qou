concentration ratio."

Numerical results at q_0 (60 dps):

| A | n=3A | partitions | top-1 share | top-5 share |

| 2 | 6 | 1 | 1.000 | 1.000 |

of shell-correction: as the partition basis spreads, the

prediction. Witness migrated to `WitnessBuilder.wrap_legacy`
