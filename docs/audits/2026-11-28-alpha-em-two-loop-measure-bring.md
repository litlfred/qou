## Question

Is a second-loop period derivable from the model, so that the two-loop slot `c₂` of
`1/α_EM` can be computed rather than chosen?

## Answer

**Not from the corpus as it stands.** The corpus fixes a one-loop weight
`μ₁ = 1/(π b₁) = 1/(8π)` (itself a phase-4 identification, see the 2026-07-14 derivability
map) and uses the product rule `μ_L = μ₁^L` for higher loops (θ-graph grammar,
`2026-07-14-alpha-em-theta-graph-two-loop-candidate.md`). It defines no two-loop object
beyond that product.

### 1. The connected period is the only slot (core Lean, proved)

Write a loop measure as `(μ₁, μ₂)` and define its **connected two-loop period**
`κ₂ := μ₂ − μ₁²` (the second cumulant).

- `two_loop_split`: every two-loop coefficient is `θμ₂ = θμ₁² + θκ₂`.
- `factorizes_iff_connected_eq_zero`: the product rule is exactly `κ₂ = 0`.
- `factorized_ladder_mem`: under the product rule every ladder coefficient lies in the
  subring generated by the graph values (`ℤ[q, q⁻¹]`) and `μ₁`.
- `excess_lies_in_connected`: if a two-loop coefficient leaves that subring while `θ` and
  `μ₁` are in it, then `θκ₂` leaves it too. Any genuinely new period, `ζ(3)` included, is
  carried by `κ₂` and by nothing else.
- `connected_of_target`: a pre-registered slot `c` is realized iff `θκ₂ = c − θμ₁²`. For
  arm `C1` (`c₂ = [2]_q(ζ(3) − Li₃(q⁻²))/(8π)²`) this says the measure would have to supply
  `κ₂ = (ζ(3) − Li₃(q⁻²))·μ₁²`. The corpus contains no object with that value.

Scope (AGENTS.md §"Before declaring refuted"): this is a statement about **where** a new
period must sit, not an exclusion of `ζ(3)`. It does **not** say `ζ(3) ∉ ℚ[π, π⁻¹]`:
whether `ζ(3)/π³` is irrational is an open problem, so no such claim is made.

### 1b. The two corpus readings are two measures (with PR #9856)

PR #9856 (`EmThetaGraphSkeinSplit`) proved `[3]_q = 3 + h²` and `N₁ = 1` in both readings.
In the language above:

- **θ-graph:** slot `−(3 + h²)μ₁²`. The product measure, `κ₂ = 0` (`theta_factorizes`).
- **Form B:** slot `−h²[2]_q μ₁`. One measure factor: the measure `μ₂ = μ₁`
  (`formBMeasure`), whose connected part is `μ₁ − μ₁²` (`formB_connected`), nonzero unless
  `μ₁ ∈ {0, 1}`.

So the readings differ in the **measure**, not in the graph value: they are a factor
`[2]_q·8π` apart on the shared `h²` piece. `formB_theta_agree_iff`: the two slots agree iff
`μ₁((3 + h²)μ₁ − h²[2]_q) = 0`, which for `μ₁ ≠ 0` pins `μ₁` to a value in `ℚ(q)` and never
to `1/(8π)` by any corpus rule. `classical_limit_slots`: at `h = 0` Form B's slot is `0` and
the θ slot is `−3μ₁²`. The constant `3` is worked in the sibling thread
(`2026-11-28-alpha-em-two-loop-placement-count.md`).

### 2. A two-loop period that is `ζ(3)` by a theorem (Transcendental, cited)

The one construction found in which `ζ(3)` comes out of a two-loop vacuum graph by a
theorem, with no choice of constant:

> **Sunset identity** (D'Hoker–Green–Vanhove conjecture, proved by Zagier; JHEP 08 (2015)
> 041, arXiv:1502.06698). On a flat torus of modulus `τ`, with the scalar Green function
> normalized by `Im τ/π`, the two-loop sunset `C_{1,1,1}(τ)` and the one-loop
> three-propagator graph `E₃(τ)` satisfy `C_{1,1,1}(τ) = E₃(τ) + ζ(3)` for every `τ`.

This is carried in Lean as a hypothesis field (`FlatTorusSunset.sunset`), not proved.
From it, with no further input:

- `sunset_connected_const`: `C_{1,1,1} − E₃ = ζ(3)` for **every** modulus. So it does not
  matter which elliptic curve in the isogeny class of the factor `E` of `Jac(Bring) ~ E⁴` is
  used, and the open identification of `E` (candidate Cremona 50a1) is not needed.
- Bring is a branched cover of six torus quotients `B/H`, with
  `|H| ∈ {2, 4, 4, 6, 6, 12}` (`BringSymmetryQuotientGenus.quotient_genus_table`).
  `cover_scaled_period`: with the un-normalized pulled-back measure the period on Bring is
  `|H|² ζ(3)`. `genus_one_quotient_degrees_differ`: these factors differ, so that
  normalization needs a choice of quotient. With the probability-normalized pulled-back
  measure the factor is `1` for every quotient.
- `bring_sunset_loop_measure`: packaged as a loop measure, the connected part is `r ζ(3)`
  for the rational normalization `r` chosen.

### 3. What this candidate is not

- It gives a **bare** `ζ(3)`, independent of `q`. It is not arm `C1`, whose
  `ζ(3) − Li₃(q⁻²)` vanishes at `q = 1`. No route found here produces `Li₃(q⁻²)`.
- It uses the **flat** metric of a torus quotient pulled back to Bring (flat with cone
  points at the ramification points), not Bring's hyperbolic metric. On the hyperbolic
  metric the sunset is a sum over the Maass spectrum (sibling thread "Classify Maass
  harmonics on Bring's surface", bean `qou-u81q`), with no known closed form.
- The ratio of this period to the one-loop `1/(8π)` is not fixed by anything in the corpus.
  The torus Green function carries its own `1/π`, and the relative factor is a further
  modelling identification.

## Open, each a choice rather than a derivation

2. The normalization `r` (probability measure gives `r = 1`; un-normalized gives `|H|²`).
3. The relative factor against `μ₁ = 1/(8π)`.
4. The placement count `N₁` (owned by the sibling thread "Derive the two-loop α_EM
   coefficient").
