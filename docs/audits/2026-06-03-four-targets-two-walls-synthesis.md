gate** (six closure ansätze already failed on the deuteron;
[`2026-05-18-delta-lambda-gating-context.md`](2026-05-18-delta-lambda-gating-context.md)):

- **A=3 binding** needs the **GAP-A-3** brane-tower level selector — which

Neither is a fit; both are open categorical problems.
