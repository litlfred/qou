## 1. The route in one paragraph

1. `B ⊆ S`. This is already on `main` (G3.1–G3.3).
2. **Generation: `S ⊆ B`.** This is the Dipper–James / Du / Green content and nearly all of the cost.
3. Then `B = S = A'`, hence `B' = A''`.
4. **Bicommutant for a semisimple algebra: `A'' = A`.** The corpus already proves that `H_n(q)` is
   semisimple (`HeckeSemisimpleGeneral.isSemisimpleRing_hecke_generic`, over a field of
   characteristic 0 with `q` not a root of unity). So `V` is a semisimple `A`-module, and the
   Jacobson density argument gives `A'' = A`.
5. So `B' = A`.

**Answer to #7864's objection.** Stream 14 wrote that "the bicommutant only restates G3.4
(`B' = A ⟺ A' = B`)". That holds only if *both* bicommutant identities are taken for granted.
Here `A'' = A` is **not** G3.4: it follows from a semisimplicity theorem the corpus already proves
for `H`, independently of `U_q(gl_d)`. The real content of this route is step 2. Step 4 is generic
infrastructure.

## 2. A key literature finding: Doty–Giaquinto assumes generation

Doty–Giaquinto, *Presenting Schur algebras* (arXiv:math/0108046, now held at
`library/arxiv-math-0108046v3/`, key `doty-giaquinto-2002`) gives a presentation of the q-Schur
algebra and a basis `Y⁺ = ⋃_λ {e_A 1_λ f_C}` whose size equals `dim S`.

**It does not prove that `U → S` is surjective.** Its §9 closes `T ≅ S` by counting `|Y⁺| = dim S`
*given* surjectivity, which it cites to Du 1995 (`[Du]`, *A note on quantized Weyl reciprocity at
roots of unity*) and, at the `Q(v)` level, to Jimbo 1986. So the generation step is in **no held
source**. Under the `-- Ref:` rule it can be neither cited nor proved from a held text until one of
the items in §5 is uploaded.

Mathas 1999 (`library/hecke-alg/`, key `mathas1999`) Chapter 4 *is* held. It proves the
semistandard basis theorem for `S(d; n) = End_H(⊕_μ M^μ)`, i.e. the basis and triangularity
half, but not generation by `E`, `F`, `K`. Caveat: the held text layer is badly OCR'd (one token
per line, digits stripped). The PDF is held and should be re-extracted before quoting theorem
numbers.

**Field.** DJ0 and DJ5 hold over any field of characteristic 0 with `q` not a root of unity,
because that is the hypothesis of `isSemisimpleRing_hecke_generic`. No order on `K` is needed.
Stream 14's P1 (transpose-closure ⟹ complete reducibility) needs a linearly ordered field. This is
the one place where the DJ route proves strictly more.

## 4. A cheap corollary to weigh

DJ0 is generic. **Once stream 14 lands G3.4 (`B' = A`) over an ordered field, DJ0 applied to `B`
(semisimple by #7864's P1) gives `B = B'' = A' = S`.** That is the generation theorem, for free,
without DJ1–DJ3b.

So DJ1–DJ4 buy exactly two things over "DJ0 + stream 14":

- (a) **generation and G3.4 over non-ordered fields of characteristic 0**, for example `ℂ` or
  `ℚ(q)`;
- (b) **the standard basis of `S` itself**, which other q-Schur content would use.
