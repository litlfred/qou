## Highlights

- **A new, serious defect class: non-compiling content-block siblings.** `binding-alpha-shift-extension` (a `Prop`-class with a data field) and `bring-residue-resolvent` (4× `decide`-on-Rat) **do not elaborate** under the pinned toolchain, yet both wear `proved`+`sorry-free` labels and advertise "sorry-free Lean witnesses." They persist because they are **orphan siblings not in the QOU library import graph** — `lake build QOU` never compiles them, and `proof-build-green` is `n/a`. The C1 checker's literal "contains a `sorry`" premise is *imprecise* (the `sorry` tokens are in comments), but the real defect is **worse** than a clean cited `sorry`. Both are mechanically fixable (one-line tactic swap / `Prop`→`Type` or a derived `def`) — the **highest-value, lowest-effort** remediations the sweep has produced.

- **Another §0a sub-pattern: a stale sibling shadowing its own *completed* twin.** `qbeta-per-block-chi`'s sibling `.lean` carries a `sorry` that the **library twin already fixed**, but sibling-first resolution resolves to the stale sibling while the `.md` claims "Sorry tally: zero." This is the §0a no-shadowing rule cutting the *opposite* direction from waves 7–10 (there a vacuous sibling shadowed a real decl; here a stale sibling shadows a *fixed* one). Fix is a mechanical sibling↔twin sync.

- **The lens is now unambiguously the load-bearing layer.** Across all C1 blocks audited (waves 6–11), the literal "sorry-free-over-sorry" charge was a comment/stale false positive **~70% of the time** — yet the lens found a genuine open-objection underneath in the majority (compile failures, stale siblings, `Nat.zero_le` vacuity, gate-as-corroboration, missing scripts, post-hoc framing). A checker trusting the flag would mis-triage in *both* directions; only per-block lens adjudication is reliable on C1. (This validates the wave-9 queued fix to strip comments from the C1 heuristic — but even fixed, the checker is a prioritisation hint, not a verdict.)
