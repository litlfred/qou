# Torus-knot reference papers — usability analysis (2026-07-02)

**Status: IN PROGRESS — stub, analysis landing in follow-up commits on this branch.**

Two papers were uploaded to `uploads/` (commits `9e2c2d2a`, `1088902c`):

1. **Murakami, H.** *Asymptotic behaviors of the colored Jones polynomials of a
   torus knot* (arXiv:math/0405126, 2004) — `uploads/0405126v1.pdf`.
   Theorem 1.1: for the torus knot $T(a,b)$ and $r$ near $1$, $r \notin \mathbb{R}$,
   $\lim_{N\to\infty} J_N(T(a,b); e^{2\pi i r/N}) = 1/\Delta(T(a,b); e^{2\pi i r})$
   when $\operatorname{Im} r < 0$ (inverse Alexander polynomial), while for
   $\operatorname{Im} r > 0$ the log-limit is $(1 - \tfrac{1}{2abr} - \tfrac{abr}{2})\pi i$
   (exponential growth). The limit is discontinuous in $r$ — torus-knot colored
   Jones asymptotics do **not** produce volumes / Chern–Simons invariants of
   Dehn surgeries, unlike the figure-eight case.

2. **Avrin, J. S.** *Knots on a Torus: A Model of the Elementary Particles*
   (Symmetry 2012, 4(1), 39–115, doi:10.3390/sym4010039) —
   `uploads/symmetry-04-00039-v3.pdf`. Phenomenological particle model built
   from the unknot and trefoil as flattened Möbius strips; fusion algebra,
   boson matrix, three generations.

This audit maps both papers against the corpus's open derivation gaps and
records which gaps they can (and cannot) help close.
