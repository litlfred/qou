## P1. `thm:hoefsmit-ew-cell-content`: the statement claims more than its proof

**Decision.** The block is a `theorem`. Its `.md` statement says the electroweak
carriers W, Z, H *are realised* as the three disjoint-strand-pair projectors
`σ₁⁴, σ₃⁶, σ₅⁸` in `H₆(q)`. The Lean sibling now proves the algebra
underneath that statement. It does not prove that these projectors are W, Z and H.
The block's own `authorNotes` says that identification is open. So the
question is whether the statement should be cut back to what is proved, or the
block demoted.

**What is proved now** (`mass-theory/hoefsmit-ew-cell-content.lean`, sorry-free,
axioms `propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound`):

- the strand pairs `{1,2}, {3,4}, {5,6}` are pairwise disjoint and cover
  `{1,…,6}`;
- in the commutative algebra generated by three Hecke generators, the eight
  pattern projectors `E(a,b,c) = e^a(σ₁) e^b(σ₃) e^c(σ₅)` are idempotent,
  pairwise orthogonal, and sum to 1;
- the six single-flip and double-flip projectors sum to `1 − E(sss) − E(aaa)`.

**What is not proved:** that those six projectors are the W±, Z±, H± carriers.
That is a naming. The pattern multiplicities `(4,2,1,3,3,1,2,4)` and the total
of 20 pure tableaux are also not derived. They are copied from the witness
enumeration.

**A second defect, independent of the kind.** The `.md` opens by identifying the
carriers with the torus links `T(2,4), T(2,6), T(2,8)`. That identification is
`conj:electroweak-higgs-torus-link-triplet`. That conjecture depends on this block,
through `thm:ew-triplet-far-commuting-minimal`. So it cannot be added to `uses[]`
without making a cycle. Right now the first sentence of this theorem cites a
downstream conjecture.

### Option A — keep `theorem`, cut the statement to what is proved

- **What it means.** Rewrite the `.md` statement as the algebraic result: three
  disjoint strand pairs, and a complete orthogonal family of eight idempotents,
  with six called "physical". Move the sentence "W, Z, H are realised as …" and
  the torus-link sentence into the companion remark
  `rem:hoefsmit-ew-cell-content-context`, worded as a naming plus a pointer to
  `conj:categorical-higgs-mechanism`.
- **What it costs.** Two `.md` files, about 10 lines. No Lean change, since the
  Lean already states the cut-down content. No compute.
- **What it risks.** Low. Six blocks list this one in `uses[]`:
  `prop:weak-spectral-completeness`, `thm:ew-triplet-far-commuting-minimal`,
  `prop:electroweak-triplet-bring-embedding`,
  `thm:ew-triplet-charge-spin-from-involution`,
  `prop:ew-slot-far-commutation-obstruction` and `conj:g-action-on-substrate`.
  Each cites it for the disjoint-pair or idempotent structure, which stays
  in the theorem. `prop:weak-spectral-completeness` already says that "the
  boson-to-strand-pair identification itself … is an open input of
  `thm:hoefsmit-ew-cell-content`". That sentence would need to point at the remark
  or the conjecture instead.
- **What it commits to.** The physical identification is then stated nowhere
  as a theorem. It lives as a naming plus the open
  `conj:categorical-higgs-mechanism`.

### Option B — demote to `conjecture`

- **What it means.** Change the kind to `conjecture` and the label to
  `conj:hoefsmit-ew-cell-content`, keeping the current statement.
- **What it costs.** The label is renamed in the six downstream `uses[]` and in
  their `.md` links. Every downstream `theorem`/`proposition` then becomes a
  §3b conjectural-propagation violation and needs its own demotion or a
  conditional-class rewrite. `prop:weak-spectral-completeness` and
  `thm:ew-triplet-far-commuting-minimal` are the two affected.
- **What it risks.** High churn, and it throws away a correct proved result:
  the idempotent algebra is a theorem whatever the naming.
- **What it commits to.** A cascade of kind changes downstream.

### Option C — do nothing

The statement keeps claiming the identification under a `theorem` label. The
`authorNotes` record that the claim is open, and the Lean proves the algebra
only. A future `proof-statement-integrity` QA pass will flag the gap between the
Lean and the `.md` again.

**Default:** C. The branch changes only the Lean, `uses[]`, `lean.ref` and an
`authorNotes` entry.

**Recommendation (my judgement, not a finding):** A. It keeps a correct theorem
and moves the one unproved sentence to where unproved sentences belong. It
touches two prose files and nothing downstream needs a kind change.

## P2. `prop:knot-operator-lp-selection`: the `.md`'s `G⁻¹` does not exist under convention D

**Decision.** The `.md` writes the LP constraint as `G⁻¹ S x ≥ n_T`, with `G` the
Frobenius Gram matrix on `H_n(q)`. The sibling block `prop:knot-operator-selection`
(predicted-spectra) records that under convention D the Gram matrix on
`H₃(q)` has a radical, so it has no inverse. `pyhecke.gram.gram_inverse()`
deliberately raises. The code uses the Moore–Penrose pseudo-inverse on
`H₃(q)/rad` (`G_PINV`, `gram_quotient_inverse()`). The question is whether the
`.md` should say which matrix it means.

**What the branch did.** The Lean now states the `.md`'s LP literally. It uses
Mathlib's matrix inverse, which returns 0 when `det G` is not a unit. Weak
duality, the optimality certificate and complementary slackness are proved for
any `G`. A docstring and an `authorNotes` entry record that these results say
nothing when `G` is singular. The producer `wedderburn_pair_volumes.py` passes
the Gram matrix to the clarabel-MPFR solver. On this instance the LP is
degenerate: the objective is about 1e-43 and `x*` is about ±2e5. That was already
true before this branch.

### Option A — write the quotient pseudo-inverse into the `.md`

- **What it means.** Replace `G⁻¹` with `G⁺`, the inverse on `H_n(q)/rad`, and
  say so in one sentence. Restate the Lean `M` as `G⁺ S` with a hypothesis that
  `G⁺` is a pseudo-inverse. The proved LP lemmas do not depend on what `M` is,
  so they carry over unchanged.
- **What it costs.** One `.md` sentence and a small Lean edit, about 20 lines.
- **What it risks.** Low. No compute number moves. The producer's numbers are
  whatever the solver does with the matrix it is given, so a reader should check
  whether the producer passes `G` or `G⁺`. I did not change that.
- **What it commits to.** The block is then explicitly about the quotient
  algebra.

### Option B — keep `G⁻¹` and restrict the scope to non-degenerate `G`

- **What it means.** Add a hypothesis "for `q` at which `G` is non-degenerate"
  to the `.md` and the Lean.
- **What it costs.** About the same as A.
- **What it risks.** Under convention D at `q₀`, the canonical setting, the
  hypothesis fails. The block would then be true but would not apply where it is
  used.

### Option C — do nothing

The `.md` keeps `G⁻¹`, and the Lean caveat records that it degenerates.

**Default:** C.

**Recommendation (my judgement):** A. The corpus already settled on the quotient
pseudo-inverse in code (`G_PINV`). This makes the prose agree with that.
