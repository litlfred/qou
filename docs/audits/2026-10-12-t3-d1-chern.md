## Explicit hypotheses — not proved

- **Local real lifts exist** (`IsRealLift γ ξ` is a hypothesis). They exist when every
  overlap has a continuous logarithm, for example when overlaps are simply connected. That
  covering-space statement is not formalised.
- `exp : C(V,ℝ) → C(V,U(1))` is **not surjective** on sections, and nothing here claims it is.
  The extension is only "locally surjective". T2b's design already accounts for this.

## Gap list (what Mathlib would need)

1. **Good covers / local logarithms.** We need: every point has a neighbourhood
   basis on which `C(V,U(1)) → C(V,ℝ)`-lifts exist. Mathlib has
   `isLocalHomeomorph_circleExp` and covering-map lifting for simply connected
   spaces (`IsCoveringMap` lifting, partial), but there is no "good cover" notion
   on manifolds. That notion is what turns `IsRealLift` from a hypothesis into a theorem.
2. **Čech cohomology groups.** The results here are at cochain level, as "∃ β θ" statements.
   Mathlib's `NonabelianCohomology.H1` covers degree 1 only. An `H²` group, the
   colimit over refinements, and the comparison with sheaf cohomology `H²(X;ℤ)`
   are all absent, so `c₁ ∈ Ȟ²(X;ℤ)` cannot yet be stated as a class.
3. **Circle bundles ↔ U(1)-cocycles.** Mathlib has `FiberBundle` and
   `FiberBundleCore` (transition functions, the cocycle condition). It has neither a classification
   of principal `U(1)`-bundles by Ȟ¹(X; C(-,U(1))) nor an equivalence with
   `PresheafOfGroups.OneCocycle`.
4. **Euler class / χ / Poincaré–Hopf.** `c₁ = e = χ` for the Reeb bundle of a
   surface (thm:qu-to-qou item 2) needs: degree of maps `S¹ → S¹` (partially
   present via `Circle` lifting), oriented closed surfaces, the fundamental class
   pairing `⟨c₁,[Σ]⟩`, and Poincaré–Hopf. **None of this is in Mathlib.** Item 2's
   "= χ" and item 3's "χ mod n" are therefore **not** formalised. Only the
   cochain-level relation `obsₙ = c₁ mod n` is formalised, and it does not depend on what c₁ evaluates to.
