# 2026-07-03 — q-bar "unified norm" re-pitch assessment + operator-space Gram-norm probe

**Status: read-first record.** An external AI dialogue (Gemini-style, 2026-07)
pitched a "unified q-bar norm" ‖B‖²_q = tr_M(B·B̄) with B̄ = B⁻¹ as the fix for
the mass/binding gap, claiming it "cancels the 1.51 normalization artifact"
and yields exact meson/baryon masses. This document records (1) why that
specific proposal is vacuous, (2) what the corpus already has, and (3) the
result of the one non-trivial probe it suggested — the operator-space
Frobenius/Markov **Gram norm** `G(W) = tr_M(W·W^♯)`. Future re-pitches of
"a q-bar norm closes the mass gap" should hit this record first
(corpus-grep discipline).

## 1. The re-pitch is vacuous as stated

The dialogue's norm is `tr_M(B · B̄)` with `B̄ = B⁻¹` (its own worked example:
`B̄_π = B_π⁻¹`). Then `tr_M(B·B⁻¹) = tr_M(1)` for **every** braid word —
pion, proton, unknot alike. A functional constant on all knots carries zero
information; the claimed "cancellation of the 1.51 artifact" replaces the
observable with the constant 1, it does not renormalize anything.

Internal inconsistencies confirming confabulation rather than derivation:

- Claimed proton eigenvalues λ = q⁶, q⁻⁶ contradict the dialogue's own
  matrices: det ρ(σᵢ) = −q₀ ⇒ det ρ((σ₁σ₂σ₁)⁴) = q₀¹², not λ₁λ₂ = 1.
- `m_p/m_e` "derivation": exp(π/ln q₀) ≈ 1.27×10¹³ asserted to "truncate
  cleanly to 1836.15" via q-Lommel roots, with no mechanism — a
  13-orders-of-magnitude hand-wave (the fitted-rainbow pattern
  formalize-first exists to kill).
- Convention drift: its Hecke quadratic has eigenvalues (q, −1), not the
  repo's (q, −q⁻¹); it assigns the 4₁ braid to the *pion* whereas the corpus
  anchors Vol(4₁) to the **muon**.
- "Dehn invariant ≡ 0 blocks proton decay" and the writhe-conservation decay
  argument are invented.
- No engagement with the canonical-mass guardrail
  ([`docs/canonical-mass-formula-guardrail.md`](../canonical-mass-formula-guardrail.md))
  or the Δ_λ gate.

Structural answer to "is there a q-bar norm whose eigenbasis is the even/odd
channels": **yes, blockwise, and it is already proved.** Over the bar-fixed
subring (generated by q + q⁻¹) the bar involution is linear and involutive,
so H_n(q) = H⁺ ⊕ H⁻ (bar-even/meson ⊕ bar-odd/lepton) is its ±1
eigen-decomposition, and *any* pairing for which bar is self-adjoint makes
H⁺ ⊥ H⁻ automatically (⟨x,y⟩ = ⟨*x,y⟩ = ⟨x,*y⟩ = −⟨x,y⟩). Because
`*(e_λ) = e_λ′`, the Markov pairing is bar-self-adjoint **on the
self-conjugate stratum** (Σc(λ) = 0 — exactly where the Wenzl bridge
y_λ = w_λ holds); globally one must twist by partition conjugation
λ ↔ λ′. The obstruction to a *global untwisted* norm is precisely the
off-stratum q^{Σc} asymmetry — the same object as the ≈1.51 weight-sum
shift. The infinite-depth version is the open `graded-baer-star-Hq`.

## 3. The one non-trivial probe: operator-space Gram norm (scope-limited negative)

The only actionable kernel in the pitch: pair the word with its **reversal**
(the ♯ anti-involution, which fixes q and each letter c·σ_g + d·1) rather
than its inverse. `G(W) = tr_M(W·W^♯)` is a genuine quadratic invariant
(the linear trace is ♯-invariant — χ^λ(T_{w⁻¹}) = χ^λ(T_w), verified in the
witness — but the quadratic is not determined by it).

Probe: [`computations/probes/gram_norm_binding_probe.py`](../../computations/probes/gram_norm_binding_probe.py)
(float64 exploratory; linear trace cross-checked against the 50-dps
transfer engine, max rel diff < 1e-8). Witness:
`computations/probes/gram-norm-binding-probe.witness.json`.

Result (²H–⁴He; α = E₀, B_gram = (α/2)·ln(G_free/G_atom)):

| iso | B_AME | B_sym | B_gram | B_gram/B_sym | G/tr² atom | G/tr² free |
|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| ²H | 2.22 | 3.890 | −0.023 | −0.0059 | 34.7 | 5.35 |
| ³H | 8.48 | 9.068 | +0.108 | +0.0119 | 967.4 | 13.4 |
| ³He | 7.72 | 6.797 | −0.231 | −0.0339 | 327.9 | 11.4 |
| ⁴He | 28.30 | 11.975 | −0.100 | −0.0083 | 9152.1 | 28.6 |

**Verdict — scope (a), scope-limited negative:** the Gram-norm log-ratio,
*alone, as-is*, does not reproduce binding — |B_gram| ≤ 0.23 MeV across
²H–⁴He (vs B_AME up to 28.3) with no stable sign. This constrains the
magnitude of this construction as a standalone binding observable; it does
**not** structurally exclude ♯-pairing ingredients from a multi-term closure
(no invariant proved; per the (a)/(b) refutation discipline this is not a
(b)).

**Structural observation worth keeping — the Gram norm is (nearly)
conserved under binding.** Across all four isotopes ln(G_atom/G_free) ≈ 0
to within a few percent of the corresponding 2·B_sym/α. Binding suppresses
the *linear* trace (that is B_sym) but does not change the word's total
Frobenius length: the amplitude leaves the trace/scalar channel and
reappears in off-diagonal (cross-channel) components. The G/tr²
amplification column (6.5× → 320× bound-vs-free from ²H to ⁴He) is the
mirror image of the trace suppression under this near-conservation, i.e.
G/tr²|_atom ÷ G/tr²|_free ≈ exp(2·B_sym/α) — not an independent binding
observable. The *independent* finding is the conservation itself (the
≤ 0.23 MeV residual B_gram, sign-flipping under the ³H/³He mirror swap —
the pp vs nn interface asymmetry pushes the near-zero ratio through zero).
Any follow-up should target the per-λ decomposition of the redistribution
(which Wedderburn blocks receive the amplitude; does the self-conjugate
stratum dominate?), not the aggregate norm.

## 3b. Follow-ups: per-λ decomposition + bar-parity split of G (owner-requested)

Probe extended (same script, `--qbar` flag) with (i) the per-block
decomposition c_λ = y_λ·tr(ρ_λ(W)ρ_λ(W^♯)) and (ii) the genuine bar-parity
split G_± = ½(G(q₀) ± G(q₀⁻¹)) with word/weights/representation all
evaluated through the q-parameterised path on both legs.

**Per-λ decomposition (²H–⁴He):**

| iso | selfconj share atom | selfconj share free | chiral-anti share atom | chiral-anti share free | top Δc_λ receivers |
|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|:--|
| ²H | 0.136 | 0.129 | 0.435 | 0.439 | (5,1): +0.101; (3,2,1): +0.045; (4,2): −0.039 |
| ³H | 0.0034 | 0.0032 | 0.558 | 0.555 | (6,3): −0.224; (5,4): −0.173; (5,3,1): −0.084 |
| ³He | 0.0026 | 0.0029 | 0.547 | 0.551 | (6,3): +0.328; (7,2): +0.301; (5,3,1): +0.210 |
| ⁴He | 0.0107 | 0.0104 | 0.714 | 0.713 | (8,3,1): +0.229; (7,4,1): +0.191; (6,4,2): +0.189 |

- **The self-conjugate stratum does NOT dominate** — its share of G is
  small (13.6% at n = 6, ≲ 1% at n = 9, 12) and essentially unchanged by
  binding. The redistribution happens in the chiral pairs.
- Over half of G (up to 71% at n = 12) sits in the **block-level bar-odd
  content** (the antisymmetric ½|c_λ − c_λ′| over chiral pairs), again
  nearly binding-invariant in aggregate.
- **Mirror antisymmetry at block level:** ³H and ³He push amplitude
  through the *same* blocks in *opposite* directions — (6,3) receives
  −0.224 under ³H binding and +0.328 under ³He binding (likewise (5,3,1)).
  The pp↔nn interface swap acts as a sign flip on the block-level
  redistribution.

**Bar-parity split of G (²H–⁴He):**

| iso | odd/even atom | odd/even free | Δ under binding |
|---|--:|--:|:--|
| ²H | 0.34031 | 0.34033 | ≈ 0 (parity-neutral to 6e−5) |
| ³H | 0.3585 | 0.4426 | binding **suppresses** the odd channel |
| ³He | 0.5997 | 0.5284 | binding **enhances** the odd channel |
| ⁴He | 0.6128 | 0.6100 | ≈ 0 (parity-neutral to 3e−3) |

**Pattern: bar-parity loading under binding is an isospin-asymmetry
effect.** The N = Z isotopes (²H, ⁴He) bind bar-parity-neutrally — the
odd/even ratio of the Gram norm is unchanged by binding to 6e−5 (²H) /
3e−3 (⁴He) — while the N ≠ Z mirror pair splits antisymmetrically: ³H
(n-rich, β⁻ emitter) sheds bar-odd (decay-channel) weight under binding
and ³He (p-rich, stable) gains it. Consistent with the pp↔nn sign flip
in the per-λ table. Recorded as an observation only (no production wire,
no mechanism claim).

**§3c. Second test at the linear-trace level — generalization REFUTED
(scope (a)).** The dense seminormal sweep caps at n = 12, so ³H/³He is
the only Gram-reachable mirror pair. A second test via the 50-dps
transfer engine (linear trace, feasible to n = 21) checked the predicted
pattern on ⁷Li/⁷Be + a ⁶Li control
([`mirror_parity_linear_trace_probe.py`](../../computations/probes/mirror_parity_linear_trace_probe.py),
witness `mirror-parity-linear-trace-probe.witness.json`). Result: the
sign antisymmetry does **not** transfer — both mirror pairs shift with
the *same* sign, and the linear parity (tr(q₀)−tr(q₀⁻¹))/(tr(q₀)+tr(q₀⁻¹))
saturates → 1 with n (tr(q₀⁻¹)/tr(q₀) → 0 exponentially), which confounds
any cross-n class comparison. What survives at equal n: the A = 3
**magnitude** asymmetry (|Δ(³H)| ≈ 16×|Δ(³He)|). Calibrated conclusion:
the antisymmetric bar-parity loading is a property of the **quadratic
(operator-space Gram) observable** and is invisible to the linear trace;
the §3b observation stands at the Gram level for n ≤ 12 and is
**unconfirmed beyond it** — extending it needs a scalable quadratic-trace
method (e.g. a transfer-style evaluation of tr_M(W·W^♯)), not the dense
sweep.
