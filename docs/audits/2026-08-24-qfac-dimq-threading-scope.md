# Scoping the `qfac` / `dim_q` threading migration

**Bean:** `qou-wee5` (closed) → this is its sized follow-on · **Date:** 2026-08-24
· **Measured against** `main` @ `b134176d93`, and **re-verified unchanged** at
`80f16eb754` — 135 commits later, every figure below identical (191 / 47 / 144 /
11 / 133 / 67 / 114 / 19, and the 12 → 1 direct + 11 indirect split). The one
figure that did move is the `ideal_muon` population, 120 → 118, which this
document never asserted.

`qou-wee5` closed by threading an explicit `q` through **two** call sites in
`per_generator_V_hat_general`, which took the anchor-flip straddle gate from
3 of 8 to **clean=8 / STRADDLE=0** (PR #5646). The closing note recorded that
`qfac` / `dim_q` still have ~135 defaulted call sites, and that threading them
all is "a real migration, not a session task."

This document sizes that migration. **The headline is that the site count is
the wrong unit, and the real work is roughly 12 decisions rather than 133
edits.**

## CORRECTION 2026-08-24 (same day): there is a SECOND route, and this document missed it

Everything below was measured by matching the **names** `qfac` / `dim_q`. That
is the appearance-vs-role error this corpus keeps paying for, and it is in this
document too.

**41 of the 133 sites, in 29 files, call a LOCALLY-DEFINED `qfac`/`dim_q`, not
the anchor-bearing one.** They never touch `hecke_characters`. So the
library-level surface is **92 sites in 38 files**, not 133 in 67.

But the correction does **not** simply shrink the number, and this is the part
that matters. Classifying those 29 local definitions by whether they are
actually anchor-free:

| local definition | files | in scope? |
|---|---|---|
| symbolic (sympy `Symbol("q")`) | 4 | **no** — anchor-free by construction |
| takes an explicit `q` | 0 | — |
| **reads the shim itself** | **16** | **YES — still anchored, by a different route** |
| unclassified, needs triage | 8 | unknown |

So there are **~16 independent local re-implementations that each close over
`q_parameter`'s shim on their own.** Threading the library fixes **none** of
them. The migration is not one surface with 133 sites; it is **two surfaces** —
a library route (92 sites, fixed by Tranche B) and a copy-paste route (~16
files, fixed only by rewriting each).

Corroborating, from the corpus grep that found this: `docs/audits/scripts/`
already holds three hand-rolled q-explicit variants —
`qfact(q, n)`, `qfact(n, q)` and `q_int(k, q)` — **two different argument
orders**. Prior agents hit this exact problem and each solved it locally. That
is the copy-paste route being created in real time.

**Read the tranche plan below with that in mind:** Tranche B and C address the
library route only. A fourth tranche — consolidating the ~16 local
re-implementations onto the library — is neither scoped nor sized here.

## Why 133 is the wrong unit

The anchor is a property of the **running process**, not of the file holding the
call. `q_parameter` rebinds its `Q` to muon when `sys.argv[0]`'s stem is in
`q0_anchor_manifest.json::ideal_muon` (or `QOU_Q0_ANCHOR=ideal` is set).

So threading `Q0_PROTON` into a call site is **numerically inert wherever the
entry point already resolves proton** — which is **113 of the 114** producer
sites. Those edits change nothing today. Their only value is preventative: they
would stop a regression if that producer were later added to `ideal_muon`.

The cost therefore is not 133 edits. It is the number of **entry points that can
resolve muon and reach these functions**:

> **12 muon-listed producers reach a `qfac`/`dim_q`-hosting module.**

```
aoperator_a5_crosscheck                 -> mass_at_3A_proper
atomic_tr_m_mpfr_50dps                  -> mass_at_3A_proper
binding_via_interface_knot              -> mass_at_3A_proper
block_swap_binding                      -> mass_at_3A_proper
cross_check_trM_1ppb                    -> hecke_characters
gg_production_bond_probe                -> mass_at_3A_proper
gram_norm_binding_probe                 -> hecke_characters, mass_at_3A_proper
he3_tritium_cosh_asymmetry_diagnosis    -> mass_at_3A_proper
he4_stage2_ranking_seminormal_xcheck    -> mass_at_3A_proper
lever1_binding_habiro_single_element_test -> young_qdim
qbeta_hoefsmit_tbeta_matrix_build_probe -> hecke_characters
vhat_per_crossing_chain                 -> mass_at_3A_proper
```

**11 of those 12 reach `qfac`/`dim_q` indirectly**, through
`mass_at_3A_proper` / `hecke_characters`. Only
`qbeta_hoefsmit_tbeta_matrix_build_probe.py:124` calls `dim_q` directly.

That is the structural point: **call-site threading does not fix the 11.** Their
q has to be threaded *through the library API*, or the library's default has to
be correct for them. Editing 133 call sites would leave the 11 exactly as they
are.

## The known failure mode, already paid for once

Forcing proton on a deliberately-muon producer creates a **new** straddle. Measured
during PR #5634: passing the proton pin at `vhat_per_crossing_chain`'s seminormal
call, while the rest of the producer stayed on its own q, moved 17 fields and
flipped the producer's own internal consistency check
`scalar_vs_matrix_agree` from `True` to `False`. Both that producer and
`he3_tritium_cosh_asymmetry_diagnosis` were reverted to passing their own q, and
each then moved 0 fields.

Two of the 12 are therefore already known to be *deliberately* muon. The other
ten are unadjudicated.

## Options

**Option 1 — repoint the library default to proton (1 line).**
Change `hecke_characters`' module-level import from the shim to
`qou_substrate.constants`. Inert for 113 sites, instantly pins everything else
proton. **Rejected:** it silently re-values all 12 muon producers, which is
precisely the failure the `vhat` measurement above demonstrates, at 12× the
scale and with no signal at any call site.

**Option 2 — thread `q` through all 133 sites.**
Correct and complete, but it mis-targets: 113 of the edits are inert, and the 11
indirect cases are not addressed by call-site edits at all. The genuinely hard
part — deepening the `mass_at_3A_proper` / `hecke_characters` signatures so a q
can be forwarded — is not proportional to the 133.

**Option 3 — tranche it, risk first (recommended).**

- **Tranche A — the 12 decisions.** Per producer: is it *deliberately* muon
  (pass its own q explicitly, making the intent visible) or *accidentally* muon
  (migrate to proton and regenerate)? 14 committed witnesses are in scope. All
  the judgement in this migration lives here, and none of it is mechanical.
- **Tranche B — the forwarding API.** Give the `mass_at_3A_proper` /
  `hecke_characters` entry points an optional `q` that they forward, using the
  backwards-compatible shape already proven in #5646 (`qfac(n, q=None)`,
  `dim_q(shape, q=None)` — verified identical with and without). This is what
  makes Tranche A expressible for the 11 indirect cases.
- **Tranche C — the 113 inert sites.** Mechanical, verifiable by re-run + diff
  with an expected result of **0 fields moved**. Qualifies for the AGENTS.md
  batch-sweep exception (one bulk commit).

**Sequencing: B → A → C.** B first because A cannot be expressed without it. C
last, and optional.

## Honest note on Tranche C's value

**Tranche C buys no correctness today.** Every one of its 113 sites already
resolves proton; threading them changes no number in the corpus. Its entire
value is preventative — it stops a future regression if one of those 85 unlisted
or 28 proton producers is later moved into `ideal_muon`.

That may well be worth 113 mechanical edits and one bulk commit. It may equally
be better spent elsewhere. It is a judgement call about future-proofing, not a
defect being fixed, and it should be made as one.

## Cost signals

- **Unit cost is low where it is mechanical.** #5646 threaded 2 sites plus one
  optional-parameter addition, verified inert, in a single pass.
- **Verification dominates Tranche A.** Each of the 12 needs a ruling, a re-run
  and a witness diff. Producers in this area are slow — 400 s timeouts were hit
  repeatedly during the `qou-wee5` work.
- **Sequencing hazard.** PR #5500 is mid-migration on the muon anchor to CODATA
  2022 (audit red, 53 witnesses outstanding). Tranche A touches muon-pinned
  witnesses and **collides with it directly**; it should be sequenced behind
  #5500 and behind `qou-1c4y`, whose four constraints already govern this class
  of change.

## What is NOT claimed here

- No claim that the 133 sites are a live defect. **Nothing straddles today** —
  the straddle gate is green at clean=8 / STRADDLE=0.
- No claim about which of the 10 unadjudicated muon producers is deliberate. That
  is Tranche A's work, and reading their intent is exactly the step that must not
  be guessed.
