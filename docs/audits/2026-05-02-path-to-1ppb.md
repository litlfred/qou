## Theoretical floor (with current calibration discipline)

**~15 ppb** — set by the CODATA precision of $m_\mu/m_e$ used to pin $q_0$. To go below 15 ppb, must either:

- (a) **Tighten the input**: a $m_\mu/m_e$ measurement to ppb precision (the current PDG 2022 value is 206.7682830(46), ~22 ppb relative).
- (b) **Algebraically derive $q_0$**: close the substrate-uniqueness chain (`q-from-epsilon → substrate-parameter-unique → vacuum-substrate-compatibility`) so $q_0$ is not pinned to a CODATA ratio. **Research task P1.1**, multi-week.

## Practical work to reach the 15 ppb floor

### Phase A — Capture binding-energy structure (close ~10⁶ ppb)

The cal/m_e variation across A (0.992 → 0.981) IS the binding-energy correction. To convert this into a *first-principles prediction* (no per-atom anchor):

**A.1 — Full Schur–Weyl over all p(3A) partitions.** Replace `length_le_2_partitions_of(n_strands)` with full enumeration. Most of these have zero or small contribution under the 2-variable Schur, but the full Hecke character can be non-zero. Compute:

$$\mathfrak{m}(W) = \sum_{\lambda \vdash 3A} w_\lambda(q) \cdot \chi^\lambda_q(W)/\dim(\lambda)$$

where $w_\lambda = \dim_q(\lambda)^2 / [3A]_q!$ uses the FULL Schur (not the 2-variable specialization).

**A.2 — Per-braid Φ_q tower with q-deformation.** Current hybrid uses q=1 MN for the tower (Φ_q applied to q=1 traces). Switch to q-Hecke per-sub-braid traces with the right Φ_q normalisation. The P2.2c-norm research task: derive the correct coefficient (z² → ?) when sub-braid traces are q-Hecke, not q=1 MN.

**A.3 — Higher-level tower terms.** The current per-braid tower has level 0 (descended), 1 (per-nucleon), 2 (per-pair). Level 3 (triples) is absorbed via pentagon ascension. Verify pentagon at all levels; possibly add level-4 (four-body) for closer match.

**Estimated reduction**: 10⁶ → 10³ ppb (close 3 orders).

### Phase B — Compute completeness (close ~10³ ppb)

**B.1 — Geck–Pfeiffer character algorithm in Rust.** The pyhecke_native module has `chi_q_via_gp_hoefsmit` — bypasses Specht-module dimension explosion. Wire into per-braid path. Unlocks computation of ALL partitions of 3A (no dim_cap), not just small ones.

**B.2 — Caching across atoms.** Per-nucleon and per-pair sub-braid traces are reused across many atoms. Compute once, cache. Saves time for the ⁴⁰Ca test, which currently re-evaluates these.

**B.3 — Multi-level partition contribution.** Even at length-≤-2 within dim_cap, the LOST partitions (length>2 OR dim>5000) contribute. Compute their contribution via Geck–Pfeiffer to verify they're negligible OR include them.

**Estimated reduction**: 10³ → 10² ppb (close 1 order).

### Phase C — Numerical precision (close ~10² ppb)

**C.1 — Promote chi_q_atomic to mpfr.** Add `chi_q_atomic_mpfr(shape, factors, q, prec)` in Rust (uses the existing seminormal_mpfr machinery). Eliminates f64 round-off.

**C.2 — Use mpmath end-to-end.** All intermediate computations at 50dp. The witness already uses mpmath for the sum; ensure no float64 leak.

**Estimated reduction**: 10² → 15 ppb (close 1 order).

### Phase D — Algebraic q_0 (close to 1 ppb)

**D.1 — Substrate-uniqueness chain closure.** Land the algebraic derivation of q_0 from the categorical action. This removes the CODATA $m_\mu/m_e$ dependence and replaces it with an algebraic constraint. The remaining floor is then the m_e CODATA uncertainty (~0.4 ppb), ALMOST at 1 ppb.

**Estimated reduction**: 15 → 0.4 ppb (close to floor).

### Phase E — m_e to 1 ppb (require external)

**E.1 — Tighten m_e**. CODATA m_e is currently 0.51099895000(15) MeV, ~0.29 ppb relative. The framework's prediction CAN'T be more precise than the dimensional input.

If the user wants better than the m_e floor: **either accept the 0.4 ppb floor as physical** (it's the calibration discipline itself), or find a different dimensional input (e.g., the Compton wavelength).
