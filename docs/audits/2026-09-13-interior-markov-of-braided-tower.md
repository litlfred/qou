## 1. The gap as it stood

`QOU/Hecke/MarkovInteriorBond.lean` (2026-09-07) proved the general-`A` bond product for the
canonical non-monotone atom word,

    tr (bondWord) = (∏_k (z + d_k)) · tr (freeWord),

**conditionally on `InteriorMarkov`** — the rule `tr (a σ_g b) = z · tr (a b)` for `a` below
the bond `g` and `b` generated by the letters strictly above it — and showed by an explicit
counter-model (`cmTower` on `MvPolynomial (Fin 3) R`) that `InteriorMarkov` does not follow
from the `MarkovTower` axioms. The bean's stated next step: prove `InteriorMarkov` for a tower
that carries the Hecke relations.

## 2. What landed in between (`qou-301d`)

`HeckeSandwichReduces.disjointBlock_of_braided` (88c914836e): on any Ocneanu `MarkovTrace`
whose generators satisfy the three defining relations of the Iwahori–Hecke algebra
(`BraidedTower`: quadratic, far-commutation, braid), the **disjoint-block trace law**
`tr (a b) = tr a · tr b` holds for `a ∈ H n` and `b` generated by the letters at level `≥ n`.
Two instances: the presented Hecke tower `heckeTowerMarkovTrace h z hz` with `h ≠ 0`, and the
symmetric-group tower (`BraidedTowerInstances`, 9fe594af69).

## 3. The bridge (this tranche)

| `tr_bondWord_heckeTower`, `tr_bondWord_symTrace` | instantiated on both `BraidedTower` instances |

The proof of the first theorem is three applications of the block law and the Markov axiom at
`a = 1`: with `b` above the bond, `σ_g b` is generated at level `≥ g`, so
`tr (a σ_g b) = tr a · tr (σ_g b)`; `σ_g ∈ H (g+1)` and `b` is at level `≥ g+1`, so
`tr (σ_g b) = tr σ_g · tr b = z · tr b`; and `tr (a b) = tr a · tr b`. Everything else is
packaging between the two tower vocabularies. Generic `R`, `A`; no `ℝ`.

## 4. What this closes, and what it does not

**Closed.** The bespoke hypothesis `qou-gr3t` carried. `InteriorMarkov` is not an extra axiom
of the Hecke tower; it is a consequence of the braid relations, through the disjoint-block law.
The general-`A` bond product for the canonical word is now a theorem on **every** Markov trace
on **every** braided tower — in particular on the two the corpus instantiates.

**Not closed — one packaging gap, already named.** The trace the corpus computes with (Ocneanu
trace of `H_n(q)`, `z = q³/(q²+1)`) is constructed and proved unique in
`SchurWeyl/MarkovTraceInstance.lean` / `MarkovTraceUnique.lean`, but as a
`HeckeCoset.MarkovTraceFamily`; nothing in the tree packages that family as an
`OcneanuTrace.MarkovTrace` on the presented tower (PR #7258's correction; bean `qou-0fmb`). The
only `OcneanuTrace.MarkovTrace` on the presented tower in the tree, `heckeTowerMarkovTrace`,
factors through the rank-2 quotient and is not faithful. So the bond product holds for the
production trace once that bridge is built, and the bridge is a packaging statement rather than
new mathematics. Bean `qou-gr3t` stays open on exactly that item; no Lean statement here is
conditional on it.

The counter-model `cmTower` is unaffected: it is not braided (`σ_n = 1` for `n ≥ 3`, and
`X 2 X 1 X 2 ≠ X 1 X 2 X 1`), which is precisely why it could refute the rule.
