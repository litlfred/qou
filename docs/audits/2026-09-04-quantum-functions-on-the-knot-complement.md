# Is there a quantum algebra of functions on the knot complement?

**2026-09-04, owner question.** *"Is there a natural algebra of functions on the
knot complement? Is there a quantum analogue we can use alongside
`𝒪_q(Bring)`?"*

**Yes on both counts, and the corpus already has it.** It is the **quantum
torus**, and it is built out in nine Lean files with the figure-eight worked
sorry-free. The finding is not that it is missing — it is that **it has no
connection to the knot-operator layer the question came from.**

## 1. The answer: the quantum torus is that algebra

Classically, "functions on the knot complement" is the coordinate ring of the
`SL(2,ℂ)` **character variety** of `π₁(S³ ∖ K)`, and its boundary shadow is the
**A-polynomial** on the peripheral torus. The quantum analogue is the **quantum
torus**

$$
  T \;=\; R\langle L^{\pm}, M^{\pm}\rangle / (LM - q^{2}ML),
$$

with the knot's data appearing as the **recurrence ideal**
`A_K = { P ∈ T : P · J_K = 0 }` annihilating the coloured Jones tower — the AJ
conjecture. `T` is the quantized algebra of functions on the peripheral torus;
`A_K` is the complement's data inside it; the classical limit of a generator is
the A-polynomial.

## 2. The finding: the two layers are disconnected

**Forty-four files build the knot-convolution layer; nine build the
quantum-function layer; no file mentions both.** The convolution `⋈_K` is
described in `frobenius-su2.md` as *"a strict depth-0 convolution in the Hecke
algebra"*, and it carries the eightfold's own mechanism
(`conj:isospin-from-canonical-bowtie` — canonical `SU(2)_I` isospin from the
operator-valued `⋈_K` interface). The quantum torus carries the complement's.
Neither knows about the other.

That is the real gap the owner's question exposes, and it is not a missing
object — it is a missing **morphism**.

## 3. What this makes statable

The extension question (`qou-yb2b`) now has a concrete shape on each of the two
readings the owner asked for (*"try both skein and knots"*):

* **Knots reading.** Does the knot convolution `⋈_K` — which acts *on knots*,
  in the Hecke algebra — induce an operation on recurrence ideals? I.e. is there
  a map `A_{K₁} × A_{K₂} → A_{K₁ ⋈ K₂}` in `T`? This is the question that would
  make the operator basis and the quantum torus one structure rather than two.
* **Skein reading.** Does `Sk_q(𝐂)` map to or act on `Sk_q(S³ ∖ K)`
  compatibly with Markov closure? The Kauffman-bracket skein module of a
  complement is a **quantization of the SL(2,ℂ) character variety**
  (Bullock; Przytycki–Sikora), which is the same object the quantum torus
  quantizes on the boundary — so the two readings are two faces of one
  correspondence, not rivals.

**Both inherit the rank-1 ceiling** that `qou-52fu` found in the holonomy
(`ρ : π₁ → PSL(2,ℂ)`): the quantum torus and the KBSM both quantize `SL(2,ℂ)`
data. So this is a route to **volume**, not to flavour. The flavour content in
the corpus sits on the *other* side — `⋈_K` and
`conj:isospin-from-canonical-bowtie` — which is one more reason the missing
morphism is the interesting object.
