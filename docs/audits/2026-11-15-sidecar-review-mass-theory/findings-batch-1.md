## Cross-cutting findings

1. **tr_M(σ₀σ₁σ₀): the corpus disagrees with itself.** `prop:corrected-markov-trace` says z³ is correct and hz² + z is wrong. `thm:markov-trace-product`'s own caveat and `prop:explicit-alpha-k-h3` say the opposite, and sympy (R-matrix quantum trace, convention D) agrees with them: tr_M(σ₀σ₁σ₀) = hz² + z. Several blocks lean on the false vertex-product factorisation tr_M = ∏ V̂_i: corrected-markov-trace, hecke-markov-transition (iv), markov-trace-product, transfer-matrix-level-independence, and the wenzl-qmn 'tr_M(T_w) = z^{ℓ(w)}' check.
2. **'4_1 = σ₀σ₁⁻¹σ₀⁻¹σ₁' is not the figure-eight.** Its Markov trace equals that of σ₀σ₁⁻¹, whose closure is the unknot. It is used in figure-eight-branching-rule and nuclear-knot-classification.
3. **Convention D makes tr_M degenerate on H_n, n ≥ 3** (y_λ = 0 for ℓ(λ) ≥ 3). Blocks that divide by τ(p_λ) or assume tr_M is non-degenerate fail for that reason: frobenius-codim-universal (and through it knot-mass-formula-unified), t-beta-markov-frobenius-collapse.
4. **'q-difference ⇒ constant-coefficient recursion'** is false (index-dependent q^k factors): binding-genfun-qdifference, q-beta-c-k-recursion.
