## The mechanism (now Lean-formalized, sorry-free)

If a vector field `R` preserves a closed form `ω` (`L_R ω = 0`, `dω = 0`),
Cartan's homotopy formula `L_R = d ι_R + ι_R d` gives

$$
d(\iota_R \omega) \;=\; L_R \omega - \iota_R(d\omega) \;=\; 0 ,
$$

so `ι_R ω` is a **closed 3-form** and `[ι_R ω] ∈ H³_dR` is an invariant of the
flow. The algebraic step is formalized generically (hypothesis-carrying, per
