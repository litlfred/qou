## The mechanism (now Lean-formalized, sorry-free)

If a vector field `R` preserves a closed form `ω` (`L_R ω = 0`, `dω = 0`),
Cartan's homotopy formula `L_R = d ι_R + ι_R d` gives

$$
d(\iota_R \omega) \;=\; L_R \omega - \iota_R(d\omega) \;=\; 0 ,
$$

so `ι_R ω` is a **closed 3-form** and `[ι_R ω] ∈ H³_dR` is an invariant of the
flow. The algebraic step is formalized generically (hypothesis-carrying, per
§7c + "conditional-proved ≠ premise-established"; Mathlib has no manifold Lie
derivative, so Cartan's formula is an explicit hypothesis, never asserted):

  — `lie_apply_eq_d_iota_of_closed` (the Lie derivative of a closed form is

  `d_iota_eq_zero_of_closed_of_invariant` (the contraction is closed).
  Weakest typeclasses (`AddCommMonoid`; no subtraction needed).

   `S := c₃ + Vol(4₁)` fixes the split by definition, and the Φ_b
