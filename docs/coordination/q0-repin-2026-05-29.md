### Three findings worth carrying forward

**1. A staleness class that hash-based checks structurally cannot see.**
`sublemma_2_1_h12_he4_phase25` reported success for months while computing
nothing: its upstream was re-schema'd at #4619 and the loader read the old field
through a `.get(..., [])`, so a schema mismatch was indistinguishable from an
empty cache. Producer current, inputs current, `scriptHash` clean — and the
verification empty. **A consumer-side default that swallows upstream schema
drift is invisible to every staleness mechanism we have.** Fixed by reading both
shapes and raising on empty.

**2. float64 can manufacture a spurious NOISE FLOOR, not only spurious
agreement.** AGENTS.md warns about the 1e-16 case. `chi_sym_alt_closed_form` is
the inverse: `tolerance=1e-3` justified by a prose comment about "~0.05%
numerical noise" in the seminormal matrices. There is no such noise — it was the
producer's own complex128. Under mpmath the discrepancy is 5.1139e-9 and
*systematic*, agreeing to six significant figures across four atoms. **A
tolerance justified by prose rather than by measurement is a candidate for this
whole class**, and the drain is what falsifies the justification.

**3. `python -O` silently deletes 64 verifications.** The `'0'`-cluster
convention across the `ksel*` / `qborel` / `borel_propertime` / `rep_*` probe
suites does its verification in bare `assert` statements and then records the
outcome as `add_assertion("name", "0", "0")`. Sound today. Under `-O` the asserts
are compiled out and the witness still reports `passed: true`. Latent — nothing
here runs `-O` — but the evidence for those 64 lives in interpreter control flow
where neither a reader nor an audit can reach it.
